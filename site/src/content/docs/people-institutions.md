# People & Institutions

Family-law research takes place within institutions. This section will organize courts, government agencies, legal-aid organizations, clinics, advocates, scholars, and professional resources alongside their publicly available source material.

## Research guidance

Treat biographies and organizational summaries as context, not authority. Link each institution or person record to its official profile, published materials, and relevant primary sources.
