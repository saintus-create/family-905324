# Research Workbench

The research workbench organizes legal materials by source type, procedural role, and evidentiary strength. Begin with primary authority, then use cases, legislative history, forms, and empirical sources to test a proposition.

## Research sequence

1. Identify the governing statute, rule, or controlling decision.
2. Confirm the current text and effective date from the official source.
3. Trace related cases, amendments, forms, agency guidance, and procedural requirements.
4. Preserve the citation, source URL, access date, and any analytical assumptions.

## Source-first principle

Analytical summaries never replace the underlying authority. A research record should preserve the original source alongside every extraction, relationship, and inference.
