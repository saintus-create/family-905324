# California Rules of Court

The California Rules of Court supplement statutes with statewide procedural and administrative requirements. This section organizes the rules by title and will retain links to the original official sources.

## Research guidance

Identify the applicable court, proceeding, filing posture, and deadline before relying on a rule. Review local rules and current judicial council forms where they affect the same procedure.
