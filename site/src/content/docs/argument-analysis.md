# Analysis

Analysis in this corpus is source-grounded. It separates legal authority, factual assertions, evidence, and interpretive claims so that readers can inspect the support for each conclusion.

## Analytical standards

Record the proposition, the authority or evidence offered in support, known limitations, competing interpretations, and unresolved questions. Do not treat a summary or statistical observation as a substitute for law.
