## One connected research system

`TOPIC` → `LAW` → `LEGISLATION` → `CASE` → `HISTORY` → `EVIDENCE` → `ARGUMENT` → `ANALYSIS`

The interface stays simple while the underlying research graph becomes deeper. A user should be able to start with a citation, a topic, a case, a person, or a question and move through related sources without having to understand the database first.

<Callout intent="info">
This resource is for legal research and education. Primary authorities and source provenance remain distinct from explanatory material and analytical or AI-generated conclusions.
</Callout>
