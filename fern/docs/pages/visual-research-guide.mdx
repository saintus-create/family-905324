## The visual research path

`TOPIC` → `LAW` → `HISTORY` → `CASE` → `EVIDENCE` → `ARGUMENT` → `ANALYSIS`

Each layer should remain independently verifiable. Moving deeper should reveal more context, not replace the underlying source.

## Research without getting lost

Use the search bar for a known citation, case, person, or phrase. Use the major navigation areas for exploration. On individual records, follow related-authority links to move laterally through the research graph.

Long titles are intentionally shortened in navigation. The complete title belongs on the destination page, where there is enough room to actually read the thing. Humanity invented responsive design for a reason.

## What this project is becoming

The long-term system connects:

- California Family Code and Rules of Court
- legislation and legislative history
- judicial decisions and dissents
- federal and state civil-rights development
- LGBTQ family-law history
- empirical studies and statistics
- attorneys, universities, clinics, courts, and organizations
- legal argumentation and rhetoric
- computational dialectics
- source-grounded AI analysis

The goal is not to hide complexity. It is to make complexity navigable.
