# WIC § 5694.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Program Initiatives > Article: 4. Self-Help

**Citation:** WIC § 5694.5

---

## Text

The counties may utilize designated mental health funding pursuant to this part for establishing and maintaining any client self-help mental health projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 164. Effective June 30, 1991.)
