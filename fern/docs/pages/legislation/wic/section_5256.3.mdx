# WIC § 5256.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 4. Certification for Intensive Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5256.3

---

## Text

The person certified shall be present at the certification review hearing unless he or she, with the assistance of his or her attorney or advocate, waives his or her right to be present at a hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1598, Sec. 4.)
