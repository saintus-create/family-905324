# WIC § 3307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. NARCOTIC ADDICTS > Chapter: 2. California Rehabilitation Center

**Citation:** WIC § 3307

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections may establish and operate facilities to be known as community correctional centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1124.)
