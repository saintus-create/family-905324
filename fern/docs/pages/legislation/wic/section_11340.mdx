# WIC § 11340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 3.7. CalWORKs Educational Opportunity and Attainment Program

**Citation:** WIC § 11340

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the CalWORKs Educational Opportunity and Attainment Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 24, Sec. 30. (SB 89) Effective June 27, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 11346.)
