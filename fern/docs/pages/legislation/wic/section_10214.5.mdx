# WIC § 10214.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 10214.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, child care and development programs, as defined in Section 10213.5, shall include, but not be limited to, respite child care and development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
