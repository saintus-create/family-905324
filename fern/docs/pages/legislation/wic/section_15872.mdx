# WIC § 15872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 15872

---

## Text

The California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program is hereby established within, and shall be administered by, the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15872.5.)
