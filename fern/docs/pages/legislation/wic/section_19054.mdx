# WIC § 19054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1.5. Program Managers

**Citation:** WIC § 19054

---

## Text

The director shall appoint a Deaf Advisory Committee to advise the director on means to increase employment, enlarge economic opportunities, enhance independence and self-sufficiency, and otherwise improve services to persons who are deaf or hard of hearing. A majority of the committee members shall be deaf or hard of hearing, and other members shall have experience relating to services to the deaf or hard of hearing. The committee shall develop, in conjunction with stakeholders, an annual work plan to identify and address areas for improvement in services provided by the department to persons who are deaf or hard of hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1102, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
