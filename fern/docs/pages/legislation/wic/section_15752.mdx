# WIC § 15752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 13. Adult Protective Services

**Citation:** WIC § 15752

---

## Text

Each county shall establish and maintain a specialized entity within the county welfare department which shall have lead responsibility for the operation of the adult protective services program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 15751 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 54, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2003.)
