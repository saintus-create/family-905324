# WIC § 16901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 16901

---

## Text

“CMSP county” means a county that has elected to participate in the CMSP pursuant to Section 16809.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 348, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2007.)
