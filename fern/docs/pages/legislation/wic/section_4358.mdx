# WIC § 4358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 5. Persons With Acquired Traumatic Brain Injury

**Citation:** WIC § 4358

---

## Text

There is hereby created in the State Treasury the Traumatic Brain Injury Fund, the moneys in which may, upon appropriation by the Legislature, be expended for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 51. Effective June 30, 1991.)
