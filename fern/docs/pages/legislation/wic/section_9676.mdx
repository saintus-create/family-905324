# WIC § 9676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 10.5. Older Adult Wellness Program > Article: 3. Program for Injury Prevention in the Home Environment

**Citation:** WIC § 9676

---

## Text

The Program for Injury Prevention in the Home Environment is hereby established. The department, through the Senior Housing and Information Support Center in the department, shall provide grants to eligible local level entities for injury prevention information and education programs and services pursuant to this article for the purpose of increasing the awareness and prevention of injuries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 689, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
