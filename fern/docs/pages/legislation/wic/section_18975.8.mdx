# WIC § 18975.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12. The Maxine Waters Child Abuse Prevention Training Act of 1984 > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 18975.8

---

## Text

“Prevention training center” means a center defined in Article 3 (commencing with Section 18977).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 18975.9 by Stats. 1985, Ch. 134, Sec. 5. Effective July 1, 1985.)
