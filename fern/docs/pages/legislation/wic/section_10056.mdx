# WIC § 10056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 10056

---

## Text

“Board” means the State Social Services Advisory Board. Whenever any reference is made in any provision of law to the “State Benefits and Services Advisory Board”, it shall mean the State Social Services Advisory Board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
