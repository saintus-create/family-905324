# WIC § 14135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14135

---

## Text

To assure maximum federal financial participation under this chapter, the director shall establish an enrollment fee, premium or similar charge to the extent required by federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1240.)
