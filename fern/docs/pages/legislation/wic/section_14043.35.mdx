# WIC § 14043.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1.3. Provider Enrollment, Application, and Participation

**Citation:** WIC § 14043.35

---

## Text

Sections 14043.2, 14043.25, and 14043.3 shall not limit the authority granted the director and the rights granted providers in Section 14123. Action taken under the authority granted in Section 14123 shall be taken in accordance with that section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 146, Sec. 37. Effective July 22, 1999.)
