# WIC § 855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 23. Wards and Dependent Children—Juvenile Halls

**Citation:** WIC § 855

---

## Text

The probation officer shall keep a classified list of expenses for the operation of the juvenile hall and shall file a duplicate copy with the county board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1616.)
