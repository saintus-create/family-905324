# WIC § 16903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 16903

---

## Text

“Fund” means the Cigarette and Tobacco Products Surtax Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1331, Sec. 9. Effective October 2, 1989.)
