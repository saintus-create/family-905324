# WIC § 11002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11002

---

## Text

All aid given under a public assistance program shall be absolutely inalienable by any assignment, sale, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 182. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
