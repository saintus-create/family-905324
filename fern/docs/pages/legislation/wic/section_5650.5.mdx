# WIC § 5650.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. The County Performance Contract

**Citation:** WIC § 5650.5

---

## Text

Any other provision of law referring to the county Short-Doyle plan shall be construed as referring to the county mental health services performance contract described in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 113. Effective June 30, 1991.)
