# WIC § 4100.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4100.5

---

## Text

The department may contract with the State Department of Developmental Services to provide services to persons with mental disorders in state hospitals under the jurisdiction of the State Department of Developmental Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 429.)
