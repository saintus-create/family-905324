# WIC § 5875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Interagency System of Care > Article: 10. Application for State Regulation Waivers

**Citation:** WIC § 5875

---

## Text

The Secretary of California Health and Human Services shall require the State Department of Health Care Services to develop an administrative waiver process for counties that either propose to be, or are considered, system of care counties by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 438, Sec. 12. (AB 1468) Effective September 22, 2012.)
