# WIC § 9452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 4.5. Home Modifications for Seniors

**Citation:** WIC § 9452

## Text

The department shall distribute the information developed pursuant to Section 9451 to each area agency on aging and to other appropriate entities throughout the state.

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 797, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
