# WIC § 4453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4453

---

## Text

The state hospitals and the officers thereof shall make such financial statements to the Controller as the Controller requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
