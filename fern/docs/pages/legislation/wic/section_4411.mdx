# WIC § 4411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Part: 1. GENERAL ADMINISTRATION, POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE DEPARTMENT

**Citation:** WIC § 4411

---

## Text

The department may expend in accordance with law all money now or hereafter made available for its use, or for the administration of any statute administered by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
