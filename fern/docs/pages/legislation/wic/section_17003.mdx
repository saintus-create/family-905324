# WIC § 17003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 17003

---

## Text

Every county may give such emergency relief to dependent nonresidents as the respective boards of supervisors deem necessary.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
