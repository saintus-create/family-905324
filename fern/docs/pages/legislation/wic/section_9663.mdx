**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 10.5. Older Adult Wellness Program > Article: 2. Older Adult Wellness Program

## Text

This article shall be implemented only to the extent funds are made available for the purposes of this article in the annual Budget Act or another statute.
