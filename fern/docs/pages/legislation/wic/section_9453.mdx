# WIC § 9453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 4.5. Home Modifications for Seniors

**Citation:** WIC § 9453

---

## Text

The department, in cooperation with the entity contracting with the department pursuant to Section 9451, shall sponsor regional training sessions, and seminars, using the materials developed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 797, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
