# WIC § 14199.133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7.5. Protect Access to Health Care Act of 2024 > Article: 8. Stakeholder Advisory Committee

**Citation:** WIC § 14199.133

---

## Text

Compensation

Members of the committee shall serve without compensation, but shall receive reimbursement for necessary expenses, subject to approval by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2024, by initiative Proposition 35, Sec. 1. Effective December 18, 2024. Approved in Proposition 35 at the November 5, 2024, election. Operative January 1, 2025, pursuant to Section 14199.135.)
