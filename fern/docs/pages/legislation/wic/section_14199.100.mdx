# WIC § 14199.100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7.5. Protect Access to Health Care Act of 2024 > Article: 1. Title, Findings and Declarations, Statement of Purpose

**Citation:** WIC § 14199.100

## Text

Title

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Protect Access to Health Care Act of 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2024, by initiative Proposition 35, Sec. 1. Effective December 18, 2024. Approved in Proposition 35 at the November 5, 2024, election. Operative January 1, 2025, pursuant to Section 14199.135.)
