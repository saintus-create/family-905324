# WIC § 4123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4123

---

## Text

The Director of State Hospitals may authorize the transfer of persons from any institution within the department to any institution authorized by the federal government to receive the person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 24, Sec. 95. (AB 1470) Effective June 27, 2012.)
