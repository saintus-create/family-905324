# WIC § 4716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 7. Appeal Procedure > Article: 4. Services Pending Final Administrative Decision

**Citation:** WIC § 4716

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall presume the incompetence of any person with a developmental disability to participate in any of the appeals procedures established herein.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 506, Sec. 2.)
