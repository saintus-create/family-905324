# WIC § 10365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 20. Individualized County of San Mateo Child Care Subsidy Plan

**Citation:** WIC § 10365

---

## Text

A participating contractor shall receive any increase or decrease in funding that the contractor would have received if the contractor had not participated in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
