# WIC § 300.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 6. Dependent Children—Jurisdiction

**Citation:** WIC § 300.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (e) of Section 361 and Section 16507, family reunification services shall not be provided to a child adjudged a dependent pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 300.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1054, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
