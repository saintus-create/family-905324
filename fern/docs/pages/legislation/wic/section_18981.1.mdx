# WIC § 18981.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.5. Child Abuse Prevention Coordinating Council Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 18981.1

---

## Text

It is the intention of the Legislature to fund child abuse prevention coordinating councils in each county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 603, Sec. 1. Effective September 21, 1989.)
