# WIC § 14022.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14022.3

---

## Text

Long-term health care facilities shall reveal to applicants for admission, or their designated representatives, orally and in writing and prior to admission, whether the facility participates in the Medi-Cal program, and the circumstances under which the law permits a Medi-Cal recipient to be transferred involuntarily.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 11, Sec. 19. Effective March 6, 1985.)
