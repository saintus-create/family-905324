# WIC § 2014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act Program

**Citation:** WIC § 2014

---

## Text

A facility altered, acquired, renovated, constructed, or equipped using funds allocated under this chapter may not be used and may not be intended to be used for sectarian instruction or as a place for religious worship.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1535, Sec. 1.)
