# WIC § 5767

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Operation and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 5767

---

## Text

The department, in consultation with a statewide organization representing county mental health services, shall strengthen and ensure statewide application of managed care principles, building on existing county systems, to manage the Early Periodic Screening Diagnosis and Treatment Program benefit while ensuring access to eligible Medi-Cal recipients.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1161, Sec. 36. Effective September 30, 2002.)
