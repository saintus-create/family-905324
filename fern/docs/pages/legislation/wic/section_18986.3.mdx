# WIC § 18986.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.8. Interagency Children’s Services Act > Article: 1. General Provision and Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 18986.3

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “Children’s services” means any services provided by any state or local agency or private entity for the health, safety, or well-being of minors.

(b) “Council” means an interagency children’s services coordinating council established pursuant to Section 18986.10.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 347, Sec. 48. (SB 942) Effective January 1, 2012.)
