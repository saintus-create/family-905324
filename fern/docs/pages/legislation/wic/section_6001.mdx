# WIC § 6001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 1. Voluntary Admissions to Mental Hospitals and Institutions

**Citation:** WIC § 6001

---

## Text

Admissions to the Langley Porter Neuropsychiatric Institute or to the Neuropsychiatric Institute, U.C.L.A. Medical Center, may be on a voluntary basis after approval by the medical superintendent of the clinic or institute, as the case may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
