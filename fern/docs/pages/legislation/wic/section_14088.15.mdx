# WIC § 14088.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.9. Primary Care Provider Case Management

**Citation:** WIC § 14088.15

---

## Text

A plan shall not use false advertising or false statements to induce enrollment. No solicitation of enrollees shall include the granting or offering of any monetary or other valuable consideration for enrollment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1056, Sec. 3. Effective September 29, 1992.)
