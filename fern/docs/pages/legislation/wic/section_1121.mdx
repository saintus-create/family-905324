# WIC § 1121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 6. Conduct, Education, and Discipline

**Citation:** WIC § 1121

---

## Text

The chief of each such division of instruction shall be well trained in modern school administration.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 183.)
