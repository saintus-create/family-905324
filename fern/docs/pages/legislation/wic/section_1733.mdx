# WIC § 1733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 3. Commitments to Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1733

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter prevents a court from revoking or suspending any license issued to the defendant under any law of this State where such revocation or suspension is otherwise provided for.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 937.)
