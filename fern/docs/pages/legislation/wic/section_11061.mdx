# WIC § 11061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Applications

**Citation:** WIC § 11061

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each county shall file with the department a record of the action of such county in granting or refusing to grant aid to the potentially self-supporting blind to each applicant for such aid.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
