# WIC § 14455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 5. Standards for Prepaid Health Plans

**Citation:** WIC § 14455

---

## Text

The prepaid health plan shall maintain a complete unit medical record for each enrollee. Enrollee medical records shall also include records of all treatment received from subcontractors. Such records shall be maintained and preserved in a manner prescribed by the director and shall be available for review by the department and the United States Department of Health, Education, and Welfare.
