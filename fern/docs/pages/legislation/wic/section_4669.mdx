# WIC § 4669

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 3. Regional Center Board Meetings

**Citation:** WIC § 4669

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to the corporate affairs of the governing board of a regional center which have no relationship to the role and responsibility of a regional center set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 577, Sec. 2.)
