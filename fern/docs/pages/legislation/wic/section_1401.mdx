# WIC § 1401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 4. The Interstate Compact for Juveniles

**Citation:** WIC § 1401

---

## Text

The compact administrator shall be the Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, or his or her designee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 54, Sec. 19. (SB 1461) Effective January 1, 2015.)
