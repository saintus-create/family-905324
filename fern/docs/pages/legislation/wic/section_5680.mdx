# WIC § 5680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Program Initiatives > Article: 2. Community Support System for Persons Who are Homeless with a Mental Health Disability

**Citation:** WIC § 5680

---

## Text

To assist persons who are homeless with a mental health disability to secure, stabilize, and maintain safe and adequate living arrangements in the community, the Legislature hereby establishes the Community Support System for Persons who are Homeless with a Mental Health Disability.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 948, Sec. 23. (AB 2119) Effective January 1, 2025.)
