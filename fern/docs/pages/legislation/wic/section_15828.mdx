# WIC § 15828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Medi-Cal Access Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15828

---

## Text

The department shall coordinate with other state agencies, as appropriate, to help ensure continuity of health care services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 23, Sec. 68. (AB 82) Effective June 27, 2013.)
