# WIC § 14087.97

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.81. Managed Health Care System for Los Angeles County

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.97

---

## Text

The commission shall be deemed to be a public agency that is a local unit of government for purposes of all grant programs and other funding and loan guarantee programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 632, Sec. 2.5. Effective September 20, 1994.)
