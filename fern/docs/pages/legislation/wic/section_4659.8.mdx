# WIC § 4659.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 2.5. Interagency Dispute Resolution

**Citation:** WIC § 4659.8

---

## Text

The resolution under this article of whether a regional center or generic agency is the responsible party for providing the service in a particular matter shall not set a precedent for the resolution of any other matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 84, Sec. 1. (AB 140) Effective January 1, 2010.)
