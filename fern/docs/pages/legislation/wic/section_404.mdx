# WIC § 404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 13.5. Foster Care of Children

**Citation:** WIC § 404

---

## Text

Any school district that has a foster youth services program shall not be eligible to participate in the program authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 811, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
