# WIC § 14302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 14302

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 14490, the duration of initial contracts entered into pursuant to this chapter shall be for a maximum of one year and of renewed contracts for a maximum of five years.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1061.)
