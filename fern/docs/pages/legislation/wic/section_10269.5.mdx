# WIC § 10269.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 10269.5

---

## Text

Contractors operating or providing services pursuant to this chapter may use digital forms to allow families to apply for services, if those forms comply with state and federal standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
