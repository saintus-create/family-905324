# WIC § 1255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 10. The California Youth Training School

**Citation:** WIC § 1255

---

## Text

The Youth Authority shall construct and equip, in accordance with law, suitable buildings, structures, and facilities for the Heman G. Stark Youth Training School.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 555, Sec. 6.)
