# WIC § 19402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 5. Rehabilitation Facilities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 19402

---

## Text

The department may contract with qualified persons or firms for consultation in such technical fields as accounting, engineering and sales so that the directors and governing bodies of the community rehabilitation programs may be advised as to the desirability of any undertaking and the best methods of achieving their objectives.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 25. Effective October 8, 1993.)
