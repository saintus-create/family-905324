# WIC § 18975.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12. The Maxine Waters Child Abuse Prevention Training Act of 1984 > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 18975.6

---

## Text

“Child abuse” means those acts defined in Section 11165 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1638, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
