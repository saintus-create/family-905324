# WIC § 16215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Child Welfare Training > Article: 4. Funding

**Citation:** WIC § 16215

---

## Text

The appropriation in support of the Child Welfare Training Program shall be provided annually through the Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1310, Sec. 1.)
