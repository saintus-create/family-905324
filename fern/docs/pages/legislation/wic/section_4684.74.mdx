# WIC § 4684.74

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 6. Development and Support of Community Facilities and Programs > Article: 3.5. Adult Residential Facilities for Persons with Special Health Care Needs and Group Homes for Children with Special Health Care Needs

**Citation:** WIC § 4684.74

---

## Text

The State Department of Developmental Services shall only approve the development of ARFPSHNs or GHCSHNs that are directly associated with the identification of the need of regional center consumers residing in or at risk of placement in larger congregate settings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 76, Sec. 44. (AB 136) Effective July 16, 2021.)
