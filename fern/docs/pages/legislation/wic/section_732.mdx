# WIC § 732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 18. Wards—Judgments and Orders

**Citation:** WIC § 732

---

## Text

Before a minor is conveyed to any state or county institution pursuant to this article, it shall be ascertained from the superintendent thereof that such person can be received.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1616.)
