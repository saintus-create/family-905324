# WIC § 19625.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 6. Blind Persons > Article: 5. Business Enterprises for the Blind

**Citation:** WIC § 19625.5

---

## Text

The department shall support and encourage all participants in the Business Enterprises Program to be as successful at becoming self-supporting as possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1316, Sec. 1.5.)
