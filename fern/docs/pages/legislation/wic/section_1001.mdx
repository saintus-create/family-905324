# WIC § 1001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 1001

---

## Text

The general government and supervision of each such institution is vested in the Youth Authority.
