# WIC § 253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 4. The Juvenile Court

**Citation:** WIC § 253

---

## Text

A judge of the juvenile court may, on his own motion made within 20 judicial days of the hearing before a referee, order a rehearing of any matter heard before a referee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1068.)
