# WIC § 1905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 10. Youth Service Bureaus

**Citation:** WIC § 1905

---

## Text

Each youth service bureau funded under this article shall maintain accurate and complete case records, reports, statistics and other information necessary for the conduct of its programs; establish appropriate written policies and procedures to protect the confidentiality of individual client records; and submit monthly reports to the Department of the Youth Authority concerning services and activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1159.)
