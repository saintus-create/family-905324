# WIC § 1820.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 7.5. Juvenile Offender Local Prevention and Corrections Act

**Citation:** WIC § 1820.25

---

## Text

A county may apply to receive partnership funds under this article by submitting an application to the department in a manner and at a time determined by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 157, Sec. 2. Effective July 21, 1993.)
