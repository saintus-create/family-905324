# WIC § 19050.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1.5. Program Managers

**Citation:** WIC § 19050.5

---

## Text

The program manager for the blind and visually impaired programs and the program manager for the deaf and hard-of-hearing programs shall have demonstrated experience and sensitivity in working with these disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1102, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
