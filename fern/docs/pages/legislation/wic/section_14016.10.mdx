# WIC § 14016.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14016.10

---

## Text

The department shall implement the federal requirement under Section 4603 of the Omnibus Budget Reconciliation Act of 1990 (P.L. 101–508) which provides for the continuity of Medi-Cal coverage during pregnancy and the post partum period for pregnant women who were certified as Medi-Cal eligible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1062, Sec. 1. Effective October 14, 1991.)
