# WIC § 5212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 2. Court-Ordered Evaluation for Persons with a Mental Health Condition

**Citation:** WIC § 5212

---

## Text

Whenever possible, persons charged with service of orders and apprehension of persons pursuant to this article shall dress in plain clothes and travel in unmarked vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 722.)
