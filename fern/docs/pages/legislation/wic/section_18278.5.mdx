# WIC § 18278.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4.5. Child Sexual Abuse Prevention Training Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 18278.5

---

## Text

The office shall enter into contracts with the centers for the provision of services required by this chapter within four months of the effective date of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 542. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
