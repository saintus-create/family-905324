# WIC § 10601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. State Department of Social Services > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 10601

---

## Text

The department may expend, in accordance with law, all moneys made available for its use or for the administration of any statute administered by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
