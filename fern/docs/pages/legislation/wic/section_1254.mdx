# WIC § 1254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 10. The California Youth Training School

**Citation:** WIC § 1254

---

## Text

The Youth Authority shall appoint, subject to civil service, a superintendent for the Heman G. Stark Youth Training School, and such officers and employees as may be necessary, and shall fix their compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 555, Sec. 5.)
