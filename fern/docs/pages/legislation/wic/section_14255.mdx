# WIC § 14255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14255

---

## Text

“Specialist” means a physician who is board certified or board eligible in the specialty of medical care provided.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 983.)
