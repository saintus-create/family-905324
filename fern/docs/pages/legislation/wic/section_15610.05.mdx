# WIC § 15610.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.05

---

## Text

“Abandonment” means the desertion or willful forsaking of an elder or a dependent adult by anyone having care or custody of that person under circumstances in which a reasonable person would continue to provide care and custody.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 594, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
