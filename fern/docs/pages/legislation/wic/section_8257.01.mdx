# WIC § 8257.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 6.5. California Interagency Council on Homelessness

**Citation:** WIC § 8257.01

---

## Text

(a) The executive council shall leverage the programmatic and administrative expertise of relevant state departments and agencies.

(b) The executive council may also designate and reimburse a state agency or department to administer programs and related functions as it considers necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 28, Sec. 109. (SB 170) Effective June 29, 2026.)
