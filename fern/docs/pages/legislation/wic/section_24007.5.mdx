# WIC § 24007.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. STATE-ONLY FAMILY PLANNING PROGRAM

**Citation:** WIC § 24007.5

---

## Text

The program formulary shall include all federal Food and Drug Administration approved contraceptive drugs, devices, and supplies that are authorized by the Medi-Cal program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 146, Sec. 74. Effective July 22, 1999.)
