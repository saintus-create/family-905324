# WIC § 14052.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14052.1

---

## Text

“Cuban-Haitian entrant or refugee” means a person eligible under the Cuban-Haitian Entrant Program or Refugee Resettlement Program, as defined in federal regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1354, Sec. 19.)
