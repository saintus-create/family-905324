# WIC § 1736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 3. Commitments to Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1736

---

## Text

The juvenile court may in its discretion commit persons subject to its jurisdiction to the authority, and the authority may in its discretion accept such commitments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 937.)
