# WIC § 15501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 15501

---

## Text

“Supplemental food” means commodities specifically made available by the United States Department of Agriculture for persons in low-income groups vulnerable to malnutrition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1195.)
