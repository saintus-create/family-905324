# WIC § 5815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Part: 3. ADULT AND OLDER ADULT MENTAL HEALTH SYSTEM OF CARE ACT > Chapter: 4. Operation and Administration > Article: 4. Financial Participation

**Citation:** WIC § 5815

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services shall seek all available federal funding for mental health services for veterans.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 195. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
