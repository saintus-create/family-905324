# WIC § 11450.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11450.02

---

## Text

(a) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, commencing July 1, 2009, the maximum aid payments in effect September 1, 2007, as specified in paragraph (1) of subdivision (a) of Section 11450, shall be reduced by 4 percent.

(b) Notwithstanding any other law, the maximum aid payments in effect on July 1, 2009, as specified in subdivision (a), shall be reduced by 8 percent, effective on June 1, 2011, or on the first day of the first month following 90 days after the effective date of the act that added this subdivision, whichever is later.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 8, Sec. 21. (SB 72) Effective March 24, 2011.)
