# WIC § 1705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 1705

---

## Text

It is the intention of the Legislature that all persons in the custody of an institution under the supervision of the Department of the Youth Authority shall be afforded reasonable opportunities to exercise religious freedom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1349.)
