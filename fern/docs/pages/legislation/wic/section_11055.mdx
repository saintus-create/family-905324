# WIC § 11055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Applications

**Citation:** WIC § 11055

---

## Text

The county shall promptly investigate all applications for public assistance as prescribed by the regulations of the department.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
