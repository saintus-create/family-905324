# WIC § 9021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 9021

---

## Text

“Preventive services” means services that avoid dependency and assist older persons in maintaining their good health, well-being, and growth.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
