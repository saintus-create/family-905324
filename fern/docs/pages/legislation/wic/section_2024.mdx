# WIC § 2024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act Program

**Citation:** WIC § 2024

---

## Text

The department shall administer funds appropriated for youth centers and youth shelters as specified in subdivision (b) of Section 4496.12 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1130, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1989.)
