# WIC § 15673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 9. Paid Care Provider Requirements

**Citation:** WIC § 15673

---

## Text

Home health aides certified prior to July 1, 1998, shall, as a condition of renewal of their certificates, undergo a criminal background check pursuant to Section 1736.6 of the Health and Safety Code. Commencing July 1, 1998, pursuant to Section 1736.6 of the Health and Safety Code, home health aide applicants whose applications are submitted on or after July 1, 1998, shall undergo a criminal background check.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 558, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1998.)
