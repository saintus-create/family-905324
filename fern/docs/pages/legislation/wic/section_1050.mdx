# WIC § 1050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 3. Superintendents

**Citation:** WIC § 1050

---

## Text

The superintendent of the institutions under this chapter shall be persons of high moral character, specially qualified for the position.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1129.)
