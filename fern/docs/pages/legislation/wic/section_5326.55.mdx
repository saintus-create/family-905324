# WIC § 5326.55

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 7. Legal and Civil Rights of Persons Involuntarily Detained

**Citation:** WIC § 5326.55

---

## Text

Persons who serve on review committees shall not otherwise be personally involved in the treatment of the patient whose case they are reviewing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1109.)
