# WIC § 11409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 5. Aid to Families With Dependent Children—Foster Care

**Citation:** WIC § 11409

---

## Text

To the extent required by federal law, the department shall establish specific numeric goals for the number of children receiving AFDC-FC payments who have been in foster care two years or longer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 977, Sec. 10. Effective September 13, 1982. Operative October 1, 1982, by Sec. 36 of Ch. 977.)
