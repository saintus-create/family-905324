# WIC § 1251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 10. The California Youth Training School

**Citation:** WIC § 1251

---

## Text

The Heman G. Stark Youth Training School shall be an intermediate security type institution. Its primary purpose shall be to provide custody, care, industrial, vocational and other training, guidance and reformatory help for young men, too mature to be benefited by the programs of correctional schools for juveniles and too immature in crime for confinement in prisons.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 555, Sec. 2.)
