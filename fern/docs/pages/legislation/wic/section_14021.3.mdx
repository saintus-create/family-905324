# WIC § 14021.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14021.3

---

## Text

The state plan for medical assistance under Medicaid pursuant to Section 1915(g) of Title 19 of the federal Social Security Act, as amended by Public Law 99-272 (42 U.S.C. Section 1396n(g)), shall include targeted case management services as a benefit under the specialty mental health services Medi-Cal program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1385, Sec. 1) by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 222. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 254 of Ch. 34.)
