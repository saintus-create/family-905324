# WIC § 14512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.5. Family Planning

**Citation:** WIC § 14512

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that all grants for the provision of direct services entered into by the Office of Family Planning under this chapter shall be competitively awarded.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 641, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2003.)
