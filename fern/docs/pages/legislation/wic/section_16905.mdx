# WIC § 16905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 16905

---

## Text

“MISP county” means a county which administers, either directly or through contracts with selected providers, its own indigent health services program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1170, Sec. 16. Effective October 14, 1991.)
