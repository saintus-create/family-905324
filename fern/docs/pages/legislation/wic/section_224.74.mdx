# WIC § 224.74

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 1.5. Youth Bill of Rights

**Citation:** WIC § 224.74

---

## Text

The Office of Youth and Community Restoration, in consultation with youth, youth advocate and support groups, and groups representing children, families, children’s facilities, and other interested parties, shall develop, no later than July 1, 2023, standardized information explaining the rights specified in Section 224.71. The information developed shall be age-appropriate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 786, Sec. 5. (AB 2417) Effective January 1, 2023.)
