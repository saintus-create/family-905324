# WIC § 18459

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 18459

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, a person who is a resident of the state is a resident of the county in which he has resided continuously for one year immediately preceding his application for relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
