# WIC § 5153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 1. Detention of Persons with a Mental Health Condition for Evaluation and Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5153

---

## Text

Whenever possible, officers charged with apprehension of persons pursuant to this article shall dress in plain clothes and travel in unmarked vehicles.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 722.)
