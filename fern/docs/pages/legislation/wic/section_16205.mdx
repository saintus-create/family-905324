# WIC § 16205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Child Welfare Training > Article: 2. Child Welfare Training Program

**Citation:** WIC § 16205

---

## Text

The department shall select and award a grant to a private nonprofit or public entity for the purpose of establishing a statewide multipurpose child welfare training program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1310, Sec. 1.)
