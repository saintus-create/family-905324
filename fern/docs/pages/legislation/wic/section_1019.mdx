# WIC § 1019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 1019

---

## Text

When any personal property has been destroyed as provided in Section 1015 or 1016, no suit shall thereafter be maintained by any person against the State or any officer thereof for or on account of such property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1708.)
