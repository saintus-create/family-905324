# WIC § 4001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 4001

---

## Text

As used in this division “state hospital” means any hospital specified in Section 4100.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 24, Sec. 64. (AB 1470) Effective June 27, 2012.)
