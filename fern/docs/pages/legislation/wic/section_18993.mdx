# WIC § 18993

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 14. Community Challenge Grant Program

**Citation:** WIC § 18993

---

## Text

There is hereby created the Community Challenge Grant Program in the State Department of Health Services to provide community challenge grants to reduce the number of teenage and unwed pregnancies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 197, Sec. 51. Effective July 22, 1996.)
