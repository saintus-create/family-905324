# WIC § 18965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. The Office of Child Abuse Prevention > Article: 5. Children’s Trust Fund

**Citation:** WIC § 18965

---

## Text

A county board of supervisors may designate an existing local voluntary commission, board or council in order to carry out the purposes of this article. The commission shall either be a commission whose duties are primarily related to services for children, with special emphasis upon child abuse and neglect prevention and intervention services, or whose duties relate to human services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1399, Sec. 2.)
