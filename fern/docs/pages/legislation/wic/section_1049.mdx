# WIC § 1049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 3. Superintendents

**Citation:** WIC § 1049

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of law relating to the State civil service, the Youth Authority may appoint, define the duties, and fix the salary of the superintendent or executive officer of each institution under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 481.)
