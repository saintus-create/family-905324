# WIC § 16501.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. State Child Welfare Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16501.15

---

## Text

As used in subdivisions (b) and (c) of Section 16501.1, a home or setting that is “safe” means that the home or setting is free from abuse or neglect, as described in Section 11165.5 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 847, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2004.)
