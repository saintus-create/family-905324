# WIC § 3203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. NARCOTIC ADDICTS > Chapter: 1. Fentanyl Program Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 3203

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 64. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 1, commencing with Section 3200.)
