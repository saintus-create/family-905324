# WIC § 18994.61

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 14.1. California Families and Children Home Visit Program

**Citation:** WIC § 18994.61

---

## Text

Each grantee shall be required to provide matching funds with one dollar ($1) for every two dollars ($2) awarded. The match may be either in cash or through in-kind services or resources with comparable value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 329, Sec. 41. Effective August 21, 1998.)
