# WIC § 15610.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.15

---

## Text

“Division” means the Division of Medi-Cal Fraud and Elder Abuse within the office of the Attorney General.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 554, Sec. 13. (SB 823) Effective January 1, 2022.)
