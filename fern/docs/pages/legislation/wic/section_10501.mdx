# WIC § 10501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 10501

---

## Text

No person concerned with the administration of a public assistance program shall dictate how any recipient shall expend the aid granted to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
