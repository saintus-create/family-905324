# WIC § 5341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 8. Community Controlled Substances Treatment Services

**Citation:** WIC § 5341

---

## Text

As used in this article, “controlled substances” means those substances referred to in Division 10 (commencing with Section 11000) of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1635, Sec. 101.)
