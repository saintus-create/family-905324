# WIC § 5585.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 5585.21

---

## Text

The Director of Health Care Services may promulgate regulations as necessary to implement and clarify the provisions of this part as they relate to minors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 23, Sec. 40. (AB 82) Effective June 27, 2013.)
