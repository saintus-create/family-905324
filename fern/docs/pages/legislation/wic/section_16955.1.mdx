# WIC § 16955.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. California Healthcare for Indigents Program > Article: 3.5. Physician Services Account

**Citation:** WIC § 16955.1

---

## Text

This article shall not be applied or interpreted so as to prevent a physician from seeking payment from a patient or responsible third-party payor, or arranging a repayment schedule for the costs of services rendered prior to receiving payment under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 51, Sec. 42. Effective April 18, 1990.)
