# WIC § 1790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 5.5. Crime and Delinquency Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 1790

---

## Text

The purpose of this article is to reduce crime and delinquency by assisting the development, establishment and operation of comprehensive public and private community based programs for crime and delinquency prevention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1401.)
