# WIC § 19004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19004

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11150), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code apply to the director and the director is the head of a department within the meaning of the chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1107.)
