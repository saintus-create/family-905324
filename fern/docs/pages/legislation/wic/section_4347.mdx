# WIC § 4347

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Primary Intervention Program

**Citation:** WIC § 4347

---

## Text

School districts or publicly funded preschools receiving funds under this chapter shall demonstrate a capability for referral to appropriate public and private community services. The referrals shall be made through contacts with families in response to information regarding the need for referral arising from the child aide sessions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 858, Sec. 6. Effective October 14, 1991.)
