# WIC § 18475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18475

---

## Text

All income received by a person or family, regardless of source, shall be considered in determining the amount of relief to be provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
