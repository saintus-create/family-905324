# WIC § 17101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Eligibility

**Citation:** WIC § 17101

---

## Text

The residence is the place where one remains when not called elsewhere for labor or other special or temporary purpose, and to which he returns in seasons of repose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
