# WIC § 16916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. County Reporting Requirements

**Citation:** WIC § 16916

---

## Text

The department shall withhold payments to a county pursuant to this part if the county fails to provide the reports and data required by this chapter according to the schedule specified in subdivisions (a) and (b) of Section 16915.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1170, Sec. 18. Effective October 14, 1991.)
