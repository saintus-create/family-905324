# WIC § 18455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 18455

---

## Text

“Aid” means state financial assistance to counties for the direct cost of relief and the cost of administration thereof.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
