# WIC § 19200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 3. Deaf Persons

**Citation:** WIC § 19200

---

## Text

The department is authorized to pay for training and support services for deaf students in a public or private college or university approved by the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 938.)
