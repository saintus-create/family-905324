# WIC § 1950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1.5. Youthful Offender Block Grant Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 1950

---

## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to enhance the capacity of local communities to implement an effective continuum of response to juvenile crime and delinquency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 175, Sec. 30. Effective August 24, 2007. Operative September 1, 2007, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 175.)
