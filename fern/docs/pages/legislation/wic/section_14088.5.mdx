# WIC § 14088.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.9. Primary Care Provider Case Management

**Citation:** WIC § 14088.5

---

## Text

The beneficiary shall be permitted to disenroll from any contract entered into pursuant to this article upon request, except where prohibited under the provisions of any federal waivers obtained by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1212, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
