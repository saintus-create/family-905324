# WIC § 4463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4463

---

## Text

The Director of Developmental Services may authorize the transfer of persons from any institution within the department to any institution authorized by the federal government to receive such person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
