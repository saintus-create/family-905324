# WIC § 18375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6.5. Preventive Health Care for the Aging

**Citation:** WIC § 18375

---

## Text

The intent of this chapter is to assist city and county health agencies to provide preventive health care through public health nursing services to the aged.
