# WIC § 1861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 9. Youth Correctional Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 1861

---

## Text

The Department of the Youth Authority shall report to the Legislature no later than the fifth legislative day of the 1974 Regular Session on the experiences and the results under the provisions of this article. Pending review by the Legislature of such report, the state shall not participate financially in the establishment of more than four youth correctional centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1193.)
