# WIC § 6600.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 2. Commitment Classification > Article: 4. Sexually Violent Predators

**Citation:** WIC § 6600.1

---

## Text

If the victim of an underlying offense that is specified in subdivision (b) of Section 6600 is a child under the age of 14, the offense shall constitute a “sexually violent offense” for purposes of Section 6600.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 83, Sec. 25.)
