# WIC § 4834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 12. Community Living Continuums

**Citation:** WIC § 4834

---

## Text

The Director of the Department of Developmental Services may contract with a designated agency, pursuant to this chapter.
