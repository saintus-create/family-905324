# WIC § 9256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 3.5. Coordinated System of Care

**Citation:** WIC § 9256

---

## Text

The implementation of a care navigation program, as described in Section 9255, shall be subject to the enactment of legislation requiring implementation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 541, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2003.)
