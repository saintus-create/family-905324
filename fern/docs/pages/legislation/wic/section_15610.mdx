# WIC § 15610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

## Text

The definitions contained in this article shall govern the construction of this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise.
