# WIC § 14124.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3.2. Drug Medi-Cal Treatment Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14124.23

---

## Text

The department may enter into contracts for the procurement of services to assist the department in administering the Drug Medi-Cal Treatment Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 36, Sec. 80. (SB 1014) Effective June 27, 2012. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 36.)
