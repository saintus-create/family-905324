# WIC § 4351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Primary Intervention Program

**Citation:** WIC § 4351

---

## Text

The department shall provide for training of program personnel. Funds for this purpose may be appropriated under Section 11489 of the Health and Safety Code, through other special funds, or through the state budget. Training of program personnel may be contracted out to programs designated by the State Department of Mental Health appropriate to provide these services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 858, Sec. 11. Effective October 14, 1991.)
