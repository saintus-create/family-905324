# WIC § 14139.37

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.3. Long-Term Care Integration Pilot Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14139.37

---

## Text

The administrative action plan shall delineate specifically how the pooled funds will be used to deliver services to all eligible recipients in the geographic area covered by the pilot project site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
