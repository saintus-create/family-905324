# WIC § 14303.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 14303.3

---

## Text

The department shall renew a contract unless good cause is shown for nonrenewal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1073, Sec. 6.)
