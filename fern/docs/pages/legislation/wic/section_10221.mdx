# WIC § 10221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Resource and Referral Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 10221

---

## Text

All child care resource and referral services shall be provided in a manner which is responsive to the diverse cultural, linguistic, and economic needs of a defined geographic area of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
