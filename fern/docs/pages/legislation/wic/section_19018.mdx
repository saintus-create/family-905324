# WIC § 19018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19018

---

## Text

Financial need shall be considered in the furnishing or denial of services in accordance with the Federal Vocational Rehabilitation Act and regulations adopted thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 10. Effective October 8, 1993.)
