# WIC § 9159

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 2. California Department of Aging > Article: 5. Comprehensive Act for Families and Caregivers of Cognitively Impaired Adults

**Citation:** WIC § 9159

---

## Text

(a) The department shall do all of the following:

(1) Maintain or enter into contracts directly with nonprofit CRCs to provide direct services to caregivers throughout the state in the existing geographic service areas.

(2) Require the CRCs to maintain a CRC Operations Manual that defines CRC services and procedures and identifies CRC duties and responsibilities.

(b) The department may enter into any contracts under this chapter on a bid or noncompetitive bid basis. These contracts shall be exempt from Part 2 (commencing with Section 10100) of Division 2 of the Public Contract Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 50, Sec. 39. (SB 187) Effective June 30, 2022.)
