# WIC § 16519.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. State Child Welfare Services > Article: 2. Resource Family Approval Program

**Citation:** WIC § 16519.54

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, a resource family shall not be subject to civil penalties imposed pursuant to the Community Care Facilities Act (Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 1500) of Division 2 of the Health and Safety Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 773, Sec. 114. (AB 403) Effective January 1, 2016.)
