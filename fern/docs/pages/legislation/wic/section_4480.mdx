# WIC § 4480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WIC § 4480

---

## Text

As used in this article, “officers” of a state hospital means:

(a) Clinical director.

(b) Hospital administrator.

(c) Hospital director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
