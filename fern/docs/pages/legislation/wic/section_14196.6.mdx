# WIC § 14196.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 6.2. Short-Term Community Transitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14196.6

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 898, Sec. 4. (SB 281) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Art. 6.2, commencing with Section 14196.2.)
