# WIC § 19465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 5. Rehabilitation Facilities > Article: 2. Transportation and Assistive Technology Loan Guarantees

**Citation:** WIC § 19465

---

## Text

In the event that the amount of loans applied for under this article exceeds the amount of the loans that may be guaranteed pursuant to this article, the department may establish a system of priorities for the approval of loans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 810, Sec. 1.)
