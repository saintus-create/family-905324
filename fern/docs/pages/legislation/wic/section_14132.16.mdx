# WIC § 14132.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14132.16

---

## Text

Mammography for screening or diagnostic purposes upon the referral of a patient’s physician shall be covered under this chapter on or after January 1, 1988, to the extent required or permitted by federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 550, Sec. 4.)
