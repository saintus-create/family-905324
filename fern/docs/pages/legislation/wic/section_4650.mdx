# WIC § 4650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 2. Regional Center Responsibilities

**Citation:** WIC § 4650

---

## Text

Regional centers shall be responsible for developing an annual plan and program budget to be submitted to the director no later than September 1 of each fiscal year. An information copy shall be submitted to the state council by the same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 409, Sec. 48. (AB 1595) Effective January 1, 2015.)
