# WIC § 11367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 4.5. Kinship Guardianship Assistance Payment Program

**Citation:** WIC § 11367

---

## Text

The supplemental clothing allowance shall be paid pursuant to paragraph (5) of subdivision (f) of Section 11461.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 559, Sec. 34. (AB 12) Effective January 1, 2011. This section also became operative on January 1, 2011, pursuant to Section 11379.)
