# WIC § 14054.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14054.5

---

## Text

“Elective services” means any treatment service which generally can be postponed without seriously affecting the health of the person requiring the service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1242.)
