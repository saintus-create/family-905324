# WIC § 15504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 15504

---

## Text

Public social services as defined in Section 10051 also includes the supplemental food program provided in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1195.)
