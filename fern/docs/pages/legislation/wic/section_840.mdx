# WIC § 840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 22.5. Home Supervision

**Citation:** WIC § 840

---

## Text

There shall be in each county probation department a program of home supervision to which minors described by Section 628.1 shall be referred. Home supervision is a program in which persons who would otherwise be detained in the juvenile hall are permitted to remain in their homes pending court disposition of their cases, under the supervision of a deputy probation officer, probation aide, or probation volunteer.
