# WIC § 1757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1757

---

## Text

The director may inspect all public institutions and agencies whose facilities he or she is authorized to utilize and all private institutions and agencies whose facilities he or she is using. Every institution or agency, whether public or private, is required to afford the director reasonable opportunity to examine or consult with persons committed to the Youth Authority who are for the time being in the custody of the institution or agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 4, Sec. 31. Effective April 8, 2003. Operative January 1, 2004, by Sec. 52 of Ch. 4.)
