# WIC § 14001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14001

---

## Text

Health care as administered under this chapter shall be considered a component of public social services.
