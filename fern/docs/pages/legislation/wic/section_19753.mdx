# WIC § 19753

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 8.5. Independent Living Rehabilitation Services

**Citation:** WIC § 19753

---

## Text

Financial need shall be considered in the furnishing or denial of independent living services in accordance with the federal Rehabilitation Act of 1973 and any subsequent amendments thereto, and regulations adopted thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 34. Effective October 8, 1993.)
