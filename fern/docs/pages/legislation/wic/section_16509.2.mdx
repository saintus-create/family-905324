# WIC § 16509.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. State Child Welfare Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16509.2

---

## Text

The physical or mental incapacity, or both, in itself, of a parent or a child, shall not result in a presumption of need for child welfare services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 978, Sec. 63. Effective September 13, 1982.)
