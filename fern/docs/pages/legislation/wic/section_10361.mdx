# WIC § 10361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 20. Individualized County of San Mateo Child Care Subsidy Plan

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “county” means the County of San Mateo.
