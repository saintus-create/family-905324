# WIC § 14019.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14019.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as imposing any control over the management of any medical or health care facility, except that each such facility shall be required to comply with reasonable standards for certification to participate in the program provided by this chapter.
