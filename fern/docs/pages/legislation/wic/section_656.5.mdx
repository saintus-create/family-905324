# WIC § 656.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 16. Wards—Commencement of Proceedings

**Citation:** WIC § 656.5

---

## Text

Any petition filed in juvenile court to commence proceedings pursuant to this chapter that is not verified may be dismissed without prejudice by such court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 897.)
