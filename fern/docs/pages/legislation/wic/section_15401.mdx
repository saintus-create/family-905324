# WIC § 15401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 5. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15401

---

## Text

The project shall provide a single standard record for each participant, and shall cooperate with subcontractors and providers of noncovered services to maintain a complete unit record for each participant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 992.)
