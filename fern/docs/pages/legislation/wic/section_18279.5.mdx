# WIC § 18279.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4.5. Child Sexual Abuse Prevention Training Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 18279.5

---

## Text

The centers may seek, receive, and make use of any funds which may be made available from federal, voluntary, philanthropic, or other sources in order to augment any state funds appropriated for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1664, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
