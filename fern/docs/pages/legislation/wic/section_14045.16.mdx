# WIC § 14045.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1.4. Peer Support Specialist Certification Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14045.16

---

## Text

This article does not authorize an individual who is certified pursuant to this article to diagnose an illness, prescribe medication, or provide clinical services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 150, Sec. 2. (SB 803) Effective January 1, 2021.)
