# WIC § 7200.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Hospitals for the Mentally Disordered > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 7200.5

---

## Text

The children’s treatment center at Camarillo State Hospital shall be known as the Norbert I. Rieger Children’s Treatment Center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 80.)
