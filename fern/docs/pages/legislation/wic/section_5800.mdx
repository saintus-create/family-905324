# WIC § 5800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Part: 3. ADULT AND OLDER ADULT MENTAL HEALTH SYSTEM OF CARE ACT > Chapter: 4. Operation and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 5800

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Adult and Older Adult Mental Health System of Care Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 153, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
