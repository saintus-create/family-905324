# WIC § 254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 4. The Juvenile Court

**Citation:** WIC § 254

---

## Text

All rehearings of matters heard before a referee shall be before a judge of the juvenile court and shall be conducted de novo.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1068.)
