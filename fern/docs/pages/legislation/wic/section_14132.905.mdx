# WIC § 14132.905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14132.905

---

## Text

(a) Day care habilitative services, pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 14021, shall be provided only to alcohol- and drug-exposed pregnant women and women in the postpartum period, or as required by federal law.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 22, Sec. 106. (AB 75) Effective June 27, 2013. Adding action operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 110 of Ch. 22. Section operative July 1, 2013, by its own provisions.)
