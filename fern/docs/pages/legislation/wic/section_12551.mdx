# WIC § 12551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Emergency Payments and Special Circumstances for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 3. Special Circumstances

**Citation:** WIC § 12551

---

## Text

Special circumstances shall also include special needs as provided in Sections 11023 and 11023.1.

This section shall become operative on July 1, 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 606, Sec. 30. Effective October 3, 1997. Note: This section was inoperative from October 3, 1997, until July 1, 1998, pursuant to provisions from this amendment (by Stats. 1997, Ch. 606).)
