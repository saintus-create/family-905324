# WIC § 2019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act Program

**Citation:** WIC § 2019

---

## Text

The funds shall be given to applicants for youth shelters for abused and neglected children without regard to the priorities set forth in subdivision (a) of Section 2018.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1130, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1989.)
