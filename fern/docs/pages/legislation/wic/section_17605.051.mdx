# WIC § 17605.051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. State and Local Fund Allocations > Article: 6. Growth Account Allocations—Deposits

**Citation:** WIC § 17605.051

---

## Text

Upon request of the County Medical Services Program Governing Board, the Controller shall transfer amounts deposited into the County Medical Services Program Subaccount to the County Medical Services Program Governing Board for the purposes described in subdivision (f) of Section 16809.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 20, Sec. 40. (SB 79) Effective June 24, 2015.)
