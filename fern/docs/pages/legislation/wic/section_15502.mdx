# WIC § 15502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 15502

---

## Text

“Groups vulnerable to malnutrition” means women during and for 12 months after pregnancy, infants through 12 months, and preschool children 1 year through 5 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1195.)
