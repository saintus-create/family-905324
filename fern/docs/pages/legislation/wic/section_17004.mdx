# WIC § 17004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 17004

---

## Text

If no other funds are available for the purpose, a county may incur all necessary expenses in transporting a nonresident indigent to another state or county, when information at hand reasonably tends to show that the person has a legal residence in such state or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
