# WIC § 18312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.5. Green Light-Green Thumb Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 18312

---

## Text

The state shall supply the necessary in-kind equipment and materials for projects under which the Green Light-Green Thumb programs contract with a department or agency of the state. In projects under which the Green Light-Green Thumb programs contract directly with local government entities, such local governmental entities shall supply the necessary in-kind equipment and materials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1074.)
