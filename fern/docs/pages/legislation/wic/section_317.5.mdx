# WIC § 317.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 7. Dependent Children—Temporary Custody and Detention

**Citation:** WIC § 317.5

---

## Text

(a) All parties who are represented by counsel at dependency proceedings shall be entitled to competent counsel.

(b) Each minor who is the subject of a dependency proceeding is a party to that proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1073, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
