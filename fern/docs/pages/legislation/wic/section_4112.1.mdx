# WIC § 4112.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4112.1

---

## Text

Section 4112 does not apply to the funds known as the “sheltered workshop funds.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 722.)
