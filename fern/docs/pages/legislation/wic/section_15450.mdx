# WIC § 15450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 6. Implementation and Evaluation

**Citation:** WIC § 15450

---

## Text

The department shall request such waivers as are necessary for the use of health care moneys provided under Titles XVIII and XIX of the Social Security Act from the Department of Health, Education and Welfare for the purposes of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 373.)
