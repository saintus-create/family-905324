# WIC § 4349.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Primary Intervention Program

**Citation:** WIC § 4349.7

---

## Text

Proposals submitted to the department between April 1, 1992, and May 1, 1992, pursuant to Sections 4343 to 4350, inclusive, that received a passing score shall be funded pursuant to Part 4 (commencing with Section 4370). Those grants shall continue to be subject to this chapter, including the matching and eligibility requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 722, Sec. 21. Effective September 15, 1992.)
