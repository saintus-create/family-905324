# WIC § 826.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 22. Wards and Dependent Children—Records

**Citation:** WIC § 826.7

---

## Text

Juvenile case files that pertain to a child who died as the result of abuse or neglect shall be released by the custodian of records of the county welfare department or agency to the public pursuant to Section 10850.4 or an order issued pursuant to paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 827.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 468, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
