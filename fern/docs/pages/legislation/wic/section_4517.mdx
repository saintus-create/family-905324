# WIC § 4517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 1.6. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 4517

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prohibit the compilation and publication of statistical data for use by government or researchers and standards set by the Director of Developmental Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1141, Sec. 5.)
