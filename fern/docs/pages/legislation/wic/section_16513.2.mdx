# WIC § 16513.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. State Child Welfare Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16513.2

---

## Text

Funding of this chapter is subject to the provisions of Part 1.5 (commencing with Section 10100).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 16513 (as added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1235) by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 630. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
