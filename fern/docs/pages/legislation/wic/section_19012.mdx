# WIC § 19012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19012

---

## Text

In accepting the provisions and benefits of the act of Congress referred to in Section 19011, the people of the state agree to observe and comply with all of its requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1107.)
