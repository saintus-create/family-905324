# WIC § 14165.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 5.1. California Medical Assistance Commission

**Citation:** WIC § 14165.5

---

## Text

The executive director shall act under the authority of the commission and shall negotiate the terms, services, and costs consistent with funds available. The commission shall serve as a review body for all contracts negotiated by the executive director. The commission may reject any proposed contract within 20 days after approval by the executive director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 329, Sec. 11.)
