# WIC § 5840.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Early Intervention Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 5840.2

---

## Text

The department shall contract for the provision of services pursuant to this part with each county mental health program in the manner set forth in Section 5897.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 583. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016. Note: This section was added on Nov. 2, 2004, by initiative Prop. 63.)
