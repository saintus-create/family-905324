# WIC § 11172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 5. Recipients’ Property Tax

**Citation:** WIC § 11172

---

## Text

The receipt of funds shall be deemed as payments by the public assistance recipients and shall be accounted for as tax receipts which may be used for state, county, city, and district purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 504.)
