# WIC § 14452.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 5. Standards for Prepaid Health Plans

**Citation:** WIC § 14452.4

---

## Text

Where the prepaid health plan agrees to provide dental services such services shall be provided in a manner that does not require the enrollees to receive prior screening or authorization by nondental personnel.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1036.)
