# WIC § 991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2.5. Juvenile and Gang Violence Prevention, Detention, and Public Protection Act of 1998

**Citation:** WIC § 991

---

## Text

Moneys in the fund, up to twenty-five million dollars ($25,000,000), upon appropriation to the department, shall be available for allocation by the department in accordance with this chapter, for grants to nonprofit organizations for acquiring, renovating, or constructing youth centers. Of these moneys, an amount not to exceed 112 percent thereof shall be available to the department for administrative costs associated with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 499, Sec. 2. Effective September 15, 1998.)
