# WIC § 11206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 11206

---

## Text

In case of dispute, the application and supporting documents pertaining to his case on file in the department or on file in any county office shall be open to inspection at any time during business hours by the applicant or recipient or his attorney or agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
