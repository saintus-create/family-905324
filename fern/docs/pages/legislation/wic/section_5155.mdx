# WIC § 5155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 1. Detention of Persons with a Mental Health Condition for Evaluation and Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5155

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall be construed as granting authority to local entities to issue licenses supplementary to existing state and local licensing laws.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1374.)
