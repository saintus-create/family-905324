# WIC § 8051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Research Concerning Sexual Deviation and Sex Crimes

**Citation:** WIC § 8051

---

## Text

Upon the recommendation of the superintendent of the Langley Porter Clinic, the State Department of State Hospitals may enter into contracts with the Regents of the University of California for the conduct, by either for the other, of all or any portion of the research provided for in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 24, Sec. 200. (AB 1470) Effective June 27, 2012.)
