# WIC § 14005.275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14005.275

---

## Text

The department shall ensure coordination of covered services across all delivery systems of care in order to minimize disruption in services for children transitioning from the Healthy Families Program to Medi-Cal pursuant to Chapter 28 of the Statutes of 2012.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 23, Sec. 54.5. (AB 82) Effective June 27, 2013.)
