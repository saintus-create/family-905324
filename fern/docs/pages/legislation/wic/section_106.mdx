# WIC § 106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 1. Court-Appointed Special Advocates

**Citation:** WIC § 106

---

## Text

The CASA shall be notified of hearings and other proceedings concerning the case to which he or she has been appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 723, Sec. 5.)
