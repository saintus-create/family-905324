# WIC § 14261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14261

---

## Text

“Vendor” means any person who provides services or supplies to a prepaid health plan or a subcontractor of a prepaid health plan and who does not have a subcontract as defined by Section 14253 with either the prepaid health plan or its subcontractors.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1036.)
