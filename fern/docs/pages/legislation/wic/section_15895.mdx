# WIC § 15895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 7. Major Risk Medical Insurance Fund

**Citation:** WIC § 15895

---

## Text

Any moneys remaining in the fund at the end of any fiscal year may be carried forward to the next succeeding fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15872.5.)
