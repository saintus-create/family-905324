# WIC § 12201.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 5. Payment of Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 12201.04

---

## Text

On January 1, 1999, in addition to the changes in the payment schedules in Section 12200 required by Section 12201, the payment schedules in Section 12200 shall be increased by an additional 1 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 329, Sec. 24. Effective August 21, 1998.)
