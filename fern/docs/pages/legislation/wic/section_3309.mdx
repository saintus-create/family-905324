# WIC § 3309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. NARCOTIC ADDICTS > Chapter: 2. California Rehabilitation Center

**Citation:** WIC § 3309

---

## Text

Commencing July 1, 2005, the Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation shall make rules and regulations for the government of the community correctional centers in the management of their affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 10, Sec. 97. Effective May 10, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 99 of Ch. 10.)
