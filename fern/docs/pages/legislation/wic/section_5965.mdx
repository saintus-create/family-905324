# WIC § 5965

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Behavioral Health Infrastructure Bond Act of 2024.

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 789, Sec. 4. (AB 531) Effective March 6, 2024.)
