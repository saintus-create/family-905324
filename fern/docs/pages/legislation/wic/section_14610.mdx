# WIC § 14610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.8. Medi-Cal Management: Alternative Methods > Article: 2. Negotiated Hospital Rates

**Citation:** WIC § 14610

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Rate” means the rate of reimbursement per unit of service which is agreed to by the department and a hospital.

(b) “Unit of service” means an inpatient day, a case, a discharge or admission, an episode, or any other measure of service which is a basis for reimbursement agreed to by a hospital and the department.

(c) “Commission” means the California Health Facilities Commission.

(d) “Private entity” means any private organization with appropriate experience and expertise to negotiate Medi-Cal hospital reimbursement rates on behalf of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 102, Sec. 133. Effective June 28, 1981.)
