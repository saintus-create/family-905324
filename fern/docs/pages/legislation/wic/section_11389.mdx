# WIC § 11389

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 4.7. Kinship Guardianship Assistance Payments for Children

**Citation:** WIC § 11389

---

## Text

A child eligible for a Kin-GAP payment under this article is categorically eligible for Medi-Cal at no share of cost pursuant to Section 473(b)(3) of the federal Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 673(b)(3)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 559, Sec. 37. (AB 12) Effective January 1, 2011.)
