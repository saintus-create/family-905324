# WIC § 5328.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 7. Legal and Civil Rights of Persons Involuntarily Detained

**Citation:** WIC § 5328.5

---

## Text

Information and records described in Section 5328 may be disclosed in communications relating to the prevention, investigation, or treatment of elder abuse or dependent adult abuse pursuant to Chapter 11 (commencing with Section 15600) and Chapter 13 (commencing with Section 15750), of Part 3 of Division 9.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1166, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1987.)
