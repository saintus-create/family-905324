# WIC § 10431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 26. Direct Service Contract Procedure

**Citation:** WIC § 10431

---

## Text

The department shall develop and maintain a central distribution list for application announcements.
