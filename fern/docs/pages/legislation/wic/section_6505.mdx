# WIC § 6505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 2. Commitment Classification > Article: 2. Persons with Intellectual Disabilities

**Citation:** WIC § 6505

---

## Text

Whenever the court considers it necessary or advisable, it may cause an order to issue for the apprehension and delivery to the court of the person alleged to have a developmental disability, and may have the order executed by a peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 457, Sec. 56. (SB 1381) Effective January 1, 2013.)
