# WIC § 15857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. County Children’s Health Initiative Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15857

---

## Text

Each health care service plan, specialized health care service plan, and health insurer that contracts to provide health care benefits under this chapter shall be licensed by the Department of Managed Health Care or the Department of Insurance, or be a Medi-Cal managed care plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 89. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15864.)
