# WIC § 13750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Foster Care Social Security and Supplemental Security Income Assistance Program

**Citation:** WIC § 13750

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Foster Care Social Security and Supplemental Security Income Assistance Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 641, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
