# WIC § 270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 5. Probation Officers

**Citation:** WIC § 270

---

## Text

The chief probation officer shall be appointed and compensation for the position shall be determined as provided in Chapter 16 (commencing with Section 27770) of Part 3 of Division 2 of Title 3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 17, Sec. 57. (AB 103) Effective June 27, 2017.)
