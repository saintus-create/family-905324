# WIC § 5770.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Operation and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 5770.5

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services shall encourage county mental health programs to develop and support local programs designed to provide technical assistance to self-help groups for the purposes of maintaining existing groups, as well as to stimulate development of new self-help groups from locally defined needs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 169. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
