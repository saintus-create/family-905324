# WIC § 4098.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 6. Suicide Prevention Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 4098.1

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Suicide Prevention Act of 2000.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 159, Sec. 190. Effective January 1, 2002.)
