# WIC § 17737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Foster Care

**Citation:** WIC § 17737

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prevent children with special health care needs who have adoption as a case plan goal from receiving services under this program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1437, Sec. 1. Effective October 2, 1989.)
