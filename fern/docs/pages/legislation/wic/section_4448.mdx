# WIC § 4448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4448

---

## Text

The department shall participate with the City of Porterville in the construction of an interceptor sewer between the Porterville State Hospital facilities and the sewer facilities of the City of Porterville.

For the purpose of this section the state may expend from any available funds 20 percent of the bid for the construction of the project authorized pursuant to this section or sixty thousand dollars ($60,000), whichever is less.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 4108.2 by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
