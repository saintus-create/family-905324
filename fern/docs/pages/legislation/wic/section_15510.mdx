# WIC § 15510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15510

---

## Text

The director shall administer the provisions of this chapter and shall adopt such rules and regulations as are necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
