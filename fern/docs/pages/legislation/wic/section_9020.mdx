# WIC § 9020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 9020

---

## Text

“Planning and service area” means an area specified by the department as directed by the Older Americans Act of 1965, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
