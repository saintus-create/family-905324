# WIC § 18241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3.3. CalWORKs Demonstration Projects > Article: 5. Child Support Assurance Demonstration Project

**Citation:** WIC § 18241

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature, in implementing federal welfare reform, to create a Child Support Assurance Demonstration Project that is consistent with the Personal Responsibility and Work Opportunity Reconciliation Act of 1996 (P.L. 104-193) and that maximizes cost effectiveness while lifting children out of poverty. It is the intent of the Legislature that the program will secure financial stability for California’s children through a guaranteed minimum level of financial support for the children of participating families, while at the same time encouraging custodial parents to be employed and noncustodial parents to financially support their children.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 181. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998, by Sec. 183 of Ch. 270.)
