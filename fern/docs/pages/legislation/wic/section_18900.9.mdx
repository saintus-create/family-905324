# WIC § 18900.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. CalFresh

**Citation:** WIC § 18900.9

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code), the department may do both of the following:

(a) Implement all waivers approved by the United States Secretary of Agriculture for a period of less than 24 months through all-county letters or similar instructions.

(b) Implement all waivers approved by the United States Secretary of Agriculture for a period of 24 months or longer through all-county letters or similar instructions until regulations are adopted, which shall occur no later than 24 months after implementation occurs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 85, Sec. 81. (AB 135) Effective July 16, 2021.)
