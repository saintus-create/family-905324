# WIC § 5910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Procedures for the Transfer of Responsibility from the State to the Counties

**Citation:** WIC § 5910

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall preclude two or more counties from establishing a single agreement with a facility, or group of facilities, for the purchase of services for the counties as a single entity. When two or more counties enter into an agreement, a single county may act as the host county for the purpose of program management and administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 198. Effective June 30, 1991.)
