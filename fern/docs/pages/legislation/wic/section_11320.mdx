# WIC § 11320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 3.2. Welfare-to-Work Activities

**Citation:** WIC § 11320

---

## Text

Any reference to the Greater Avenues for Independence program or (GAIN) shall mean the welfare-to-work activities under the CalWORKs program provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 61. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998, by Sec. 183 of Ch. 270.)
