# WIC § 1761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1761

---

## Text

The Youth Authority shall establish policies for a background assessment of all persons committed to the Youth Authority in order to supplement the case history provided by the county which committed the person to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 612, Sec. 2.)
