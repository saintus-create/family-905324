# WIC § 14264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14264

---

## Text

“Marketing organization” means any subcontractor who agrees to provide marketing services for a prepaid health plan.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1036.)
