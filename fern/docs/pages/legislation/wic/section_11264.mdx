# WIC § 11264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2. Eligibility for Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 11264

---

## Text

No child maintained in an institution, for whom a bona fide offer of a proper home has been made, is eligible for further aid; but no institution shall be required to surrender a child to any person of religious faith different from that of the child or the parents of the child.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
