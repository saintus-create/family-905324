# WIC § 5522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Mental Health Advocacy > Article: 3. County Advocates

**Citation:** WIC § 5522

---

## Text

County patients’ rights advocates may conduct investigations if there is probable cause to believe that the rights of a past or present recipient of mental health services have been, may have been, or may be violated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 841, Sec. 7.)
