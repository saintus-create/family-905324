# WIC § 11481.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 7. Enforcement

**Citation:** WIC § 11481.5

---

## Text

The department shall evaluate the effectiveness of a 24-hour welfare fraud hotline pilot project, to assess greater public involvement and assistance in welfare fraud detection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1448, Sec. 3.5.)
