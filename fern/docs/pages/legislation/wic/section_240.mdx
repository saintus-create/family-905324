# WIC § 240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 3. Probation Commission

**Citation:** WIC § 240

---

## Text

In counties having a population in excess of 6,000,000 in lieu of a county juvenile justice commission, there shall be a probation commission consisting of not less than seven members who shall be appointed by the same authority as that authorized to appoint the probation officer in that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 228, Sec. 3.)
