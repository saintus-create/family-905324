# WIC § 14124.86

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3.5. Third Party Liability

**Citation:** WIC § 14124.86

---

## Text

The contractor shall retain its rights to compensation upon recovery for completed duties under the contract with respect to any claims or liens processed in whole or in part prior to the termination date of the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 52, Sec. 43. (SB 97) Effective July 10, 2017.)
