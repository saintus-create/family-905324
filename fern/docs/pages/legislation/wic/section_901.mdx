# WIC § 901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 25. Support of Wards and Dependent Children

**Citation:** WIC § 901

---

## Text

No order for payment shall be made in a sum in excess of the actual cost of supporting and maintaining the ward, dependent child or other minor person.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1616.)
