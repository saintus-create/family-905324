# WIC § 629.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 15. Wards—Temporary Custody and Detention

**Citation:** WIC § 629.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 628 or 628.1, whenever a minor who is 14 years of age or older is delivered to the custody of the probation officer pursuant to Section 626.6, the probation officer shall retain the minor in custody until such time that the minor can be brought before a judicial officer of the juvenile court pursuant to Section 632.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 843, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
