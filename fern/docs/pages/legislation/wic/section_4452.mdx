# WIC § 4452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4452

---

## Text

All money belonging to the state and received by state hospitals from any source, except appropriations, shall, at the end of each month, be deposited in the State Treasury, to the credit of the General Fund. This section shall not apply to the funds known as the industrial or amusement funds or the “sheltered workshop funds.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
