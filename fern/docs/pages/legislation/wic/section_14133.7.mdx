# WIC § 14133.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14133.7

---

## Text

The department shall not require emergency certification statements for hospital inpatient claims which have been reviewed and approved by the department for appropriateness of emergency admission or length of stay.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1164, Sec. 5.)
