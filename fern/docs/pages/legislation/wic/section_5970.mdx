# WIC § 5970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 5970

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Community Assistance, Recovery, and Empowerment (CARE) Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 283, Sec. 2. (SB 35) Effective September 30, 2023. Section conditionally operative as provided in Section 5970.5.)
