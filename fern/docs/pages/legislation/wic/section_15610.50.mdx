# WIC § 15610.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.50

---

## Text

“Long-term care ombudsman” means the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman, local ombudsman coordinators, and other persons currently certified as ombudsmen by the Department of Aging as described in Chapter 11 (commencing with Section 9700) of Division 8.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 54, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2003.)
