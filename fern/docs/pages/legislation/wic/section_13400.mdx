# WIC § 13400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.7. Rapid Response Program

**Citation:** WIC § 13400

---

## Text

The State Department of Social Services shall administer a rapid response program to award grants or contracts to entities that provide critical assistance to immigrants during times of need.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 27, Sec. 96. (SB 80) Effective June 27, 2019.)
