# WIC § 15864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. County Children’s Health Initiative Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15864

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on July 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 89. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 2014) for Chapter 3, commencing with Section 15850.)
