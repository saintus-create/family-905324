# WIC § 14000.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14000.1

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that health care services available under this chapter shall be at least equivalent to the level provided in 1970–71.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 577.)
