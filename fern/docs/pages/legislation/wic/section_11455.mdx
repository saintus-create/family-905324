# WIC § 11455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11455

---

## Text

If on the first day of the month a child is eligible for aid, aid for the entire month shall be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
