# WIC § 15610.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.65

---

## Text

“Reasonable suspicion” means an objectively reasonable suspicion that a person would entertain, based upon facts that could cause a reasonable person in a like position, drawing when appropriate upon his or her training and experience, to suspect abuse.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 594, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
