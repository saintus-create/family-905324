# WIC § 1760.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1760.46

---

## Text

(a) A ward or youth hand crew member placed at the Pine Grove Youth Conservation Camp pursuant to Section 730, Section 875, or Section 1760.45 shall be paid an hourly wage equal to seven dollars and twenty-five cents ($7.25) while assigned to an active fire incident. The wage rate shall be reviewed annually.

(b) The Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation shall maintain regulations regarding an administrative adjudication and remedy process for any disputes over sums owed pursuant to this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 681, Sec. 3. (AB 247) Effective October 13, 2025.)
