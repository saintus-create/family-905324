# WIC § 10103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Part: 1.5. FISCAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Electronic Benefits Transfer Act

**Citation:** WIC § 10103

---

## Text

All federal funds under Title IV-B of the Social Security Act, which are appropriated to the state shall be passed on to the counties, except for those reasonable funds needed to finance a required statewide information system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 66. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
