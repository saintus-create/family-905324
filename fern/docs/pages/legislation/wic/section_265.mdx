# WIC § 265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 4. The Juvenile Court

**Citation:** WIC § 265

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall establish rules governing practice and procedure in the juvenile court not inconsistent with law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1068.)
