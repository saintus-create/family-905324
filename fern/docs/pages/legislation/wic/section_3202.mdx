# WIC § 3202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. NARCOTIC ADDICTS > Chapter: 1. Fentanyl Program Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 3202

---

## Text

This chapter shall be implemented only to the extent that funds are appropriated for this purpose in the annual Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 783, Sec. 1. (AB 2365) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Sec. 3203.)
