# WIC § 6608.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 2. Commitment Classification > Article: 4. Sexually Violent Predators

**Citation:** WIC § 6608.1

---

## Text

A person who is released on outpatient status or granted conditional release pursuant to this article shall be monitored by a global positioning system (GPS) until the person is unconditionally discharged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 104, Sec. 1. (AB 1641) Effective January 1, 2023.)
