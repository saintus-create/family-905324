# WIC § 5965.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Behavioral Health Infrastructure Bond Act of 2024

**Citation:** WIC § 5965.16

---

## Text

The proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this chapter are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution, and the disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by that article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 789, Sec. 4. (AB 531) Effective March 6, 2024.)
