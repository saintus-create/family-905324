# WIC § 14681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.8. Medi-Cal Management: Alternative Methods > Article: 5. Mental Health Managed Care

**Citation:** WIC § 14681

---

## Text

The department shall ensure that all contracts for Medi-Cal managed care include a process for screening, referral, and coordination with any mental health plan established, of medically necessary specialty mental health care services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 237. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 254 of Ch. 34.)
