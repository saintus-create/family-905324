# WIC § 15351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 3. Scope of Services

**Citation:** WIC § 15351

---

## Text

Projects may offer day care centers providing necessary meals, personal care and therapeutic activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 992.)
