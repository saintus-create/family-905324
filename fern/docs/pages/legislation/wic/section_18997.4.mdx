# WIC § 18997.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 16. California Guaranteed Income Pilot Program

**Citation:** WIC § 18997.4

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on January 1, 2029, and, as of January 1, 2030, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 26, Sec. 37. (AB 152) Effective June 29, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 16, commencing with Section 18997.)
