# WIC § 4404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Part: 1. GENERAL ADMINISTRATION, POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE DEPARTMENT

**Citation:** WIC § 4404

---

## Text

The department is under the control of an executive officer known as the Director of Developmental Services.
