# WIC § 11208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 11208

---

## Text

Caseworker services shall be made available immediately to an applicant for aid under this chapter upon the filing of his application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
