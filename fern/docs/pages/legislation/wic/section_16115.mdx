# WIC § 16115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.1. Aid for Adoption of Children

**Citation:** WIC § 16115

---

## Text

Aid under this chapter shall be known as the Adoption Assistance Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 977, Sec. 18. Effective September 13, 1982. Operative October 1, 1982, by Sec. 36 of Ch. 977.)
