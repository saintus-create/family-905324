# WIC § 1799

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 5.5. Crime and Delinquency Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 1799

---

## Text

The director may, with the approval of the Director of General Services, enter into contracts with the federal government, other state governments, counties, cities, private foundations, private organizations, or any other group to accomplish the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1401.)
