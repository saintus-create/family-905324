# WIC § 10629

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.1. Public Social Services for Deaf Persons

**Citation:** WIC § 10629

---

## Text

The State Department of Social Services shall not expend over 5 percent of the sum appropriated for this chapter for the administrative costs of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1193, Sec. 2.)
