# WIC § 152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF WELFARE AND INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Unattended Collection Boxes

**Citation:** WIC § 152

---

## Text

A city, county, or city and county shall have the authority to declare a box that is in violation of this chapter to be a public nuisance and to abate that nuisance accordingly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 75, Sec. 1. (AB 918) Effective January 1, 2011.)
