# WIC § 5170.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 1.5. Detention of Individuals Under the Influence of Alcohol for Evaluation and Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5170.1

---

## Text

A 72-hour treatment and evaluation facility shall include one or more of the following:

(1) A screening, evaluation, and referral facility which may be accomplished by a mobile crisis unit, first aid station or ambulatory detoxification unit;

(2) A detoxification facility for alcoholic and acutely intoxicated persons.

(3) An alcohol recovery house.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1024.)
