# WIC § 19750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 8.5. Independent Living Rehabilitation Services

**Citation:** WIC § 19750

---

## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to establish program standards and authorize procedures utilized for subgranting funds, auditing records, and monitoring the delivery of services pursuant to the Independent Living Services Program created pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 32.5. Effective October 8, 1993.)
