# WIC § 19078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 2. Vocational Rehabilitation Federal Fund

**Citation:** WIC § 19078

---

## Text

Money in the Vocational Rehabilitation Federal Fund shall be expended as provided by this chapter, as requisitioned by the department in carrying out the provisions of this code and the federal act relating to vocational rehabilitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1107.)
