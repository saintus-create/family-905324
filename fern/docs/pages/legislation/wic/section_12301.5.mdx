# WIC § 12301.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 7. In-Home Supportive Services

**Citation:** WIC § 12301.5

---

## Text

The department may secure to the extent feasible such in-home supportive and other health services for persons eligible under this article to which they are entitled under the Medi-Cal Act (Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 14000) of this part).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
