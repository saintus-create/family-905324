# WIC § 4514.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 1.6. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 4514.3

---

## Text

(a) Notwithstanding Section 4514, information and records shall be disclosed to the protection and advocacy agency designated by the Governor in this state to fulfill the requirements and assurances of the federal Developmental Disabilities Assistance and Bill of Rights Act of 2000, contained in Chapter 144 (commencing with Section 15001) of Title 42 of the United States Code, for the protection and advocacy of the rights of persons with developmental disabilities, as defined in Section 15002(8) of Title 42 of the United States Code.

(b) Access to information and records to which subdivision (a) applies shall be in accord with Division 4.7 (commencing with Section 4900).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 878, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
