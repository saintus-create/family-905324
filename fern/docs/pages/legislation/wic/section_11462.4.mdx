# WIC § 11462.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11462.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 11342.610 of the Government Code, providers, as defined in Section 11466, shall be deemed small businesses and the department shall project the impact on group homes and foster family agencies of any new regulations which will affect those community care facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 732, Sec. 73. (AB 404) Effective January 1, 2018.)
