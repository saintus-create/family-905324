# WIC § 14552.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.7. Adult Day Health Care Programs > Article: 3. Services and Standards

**Citation:** WIC § 14552.5

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 14043.46, the department may implement a one-year moratorium on the certification and enrollment into the Medi-Cal program of new adult day health care centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 228, Sec. 26.1. Effective August 16, 2004.)
