# WIC § 10067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Electronic Benefits Transfer Act > Article: 2. Electronic Benefits Transfer Committee

**Citation:** WIC § 10067

---

## Text

The Electronic Benefits Transfer Committee is hereby created. The committee shall remain in existence until January 1, 2003.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 31. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative August 18, 1997, by Sec. 187 of Ch. 270.)
