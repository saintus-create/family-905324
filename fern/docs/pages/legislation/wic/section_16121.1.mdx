# WIC § 16121.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.1. Aid for Adoption of Children

**Citation:** WIC § 16121.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 11105, the residence of the adoptive parents at the time of or subsequent to adoptive placement shall not terminate the eligibility of a child who is otherwise eligible for adoptive assistance payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 977, Sec. 28.5. Effective September 13, 1982. Operative October 1, 1982, by Sec. 36 of Ch. 977.)
