# WIC § 18976.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12. The Maxine Waters Child Abuse Prevention Training Act of 1984 > Article: 2. Primary Prevention Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 18976.5

---

## Text

Parents shall be given notice of, and may refuse to have their children participate in, a primary prevention program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 134, Sec. 8. Effective July 1, 1985.)
