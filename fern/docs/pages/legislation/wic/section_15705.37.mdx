# WIC § 15705.37

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12. Protective Placements and Custody of Endangered Adults > Article: 3. Protective Services

**Citation:** WIC § 15705.37

---

## Text

This chapter shall be operative in a county only if the county board of supervisors has adopted a resolution to make this chapter operative in that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 913, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
