# WIC § 11259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2. Eligibility for Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 11259

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “personal property” shall not include the value of property belonging to an absent parent and not available to the family.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
