# WIC § 5686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Program Initiatives > Article: 2. Community Support System for Persons Who are Homeless with a Mental Health Disability

**Citation:** WIC § 5686

---

## Text

If a county believes that a person with a mental health disability may be unable to manage their SSI/SSP funds, the county mental health program shall advise the person that they may have a trusted family member, relative, or friend designated as their representative payee under the SSI/SSP program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 948, Sec. 28. (AB 2119) Effective January 1, 2025.)
