# WIC § 775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 20. Wards—Modification of Juvenile Court Judgments and Orders

**Citation:** WIC § 775

---

## Text

Any order made by the court in the case of any person subject to its jurisdiction may at any time be changed, modified, or set aside, as the judge deems meet and proper, subject to such procedural requirements as are imposed by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1616.)
