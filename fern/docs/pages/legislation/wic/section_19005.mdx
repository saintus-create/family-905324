# WIC § 19005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19005

---

## Text

The department is vested with all necessary powers and authority to cooperate with the government of the United States or any agency or agencies thereof in the administration of any act of Congress and rules and regulations lawfully adopted thereunder relating to individuals with disabilities or their vocational rehabilitation and independent living.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 3. Effective October 8, 1993.)
