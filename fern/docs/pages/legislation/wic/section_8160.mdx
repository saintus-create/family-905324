# WIC § 8160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 4.9. Better for Families Tax Refund

## Text

This chapter is known and may be cited as the “Better for Families Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 51, Sec. 6. (AB 192) Effective June 30, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 8165.)
