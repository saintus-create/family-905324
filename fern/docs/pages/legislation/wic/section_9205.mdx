# WIC § 9205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 3. California Commission on Aging

**Citation:** WIC § 9205

---

## Text

Members of the commission shall be reimbursed for their actual and necessary travel and other expenses incurred in the performance of their official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
