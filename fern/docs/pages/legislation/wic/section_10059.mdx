# WIC § 10059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 10059

---

## Text

“County director” means the director or other chief executive of the county department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
