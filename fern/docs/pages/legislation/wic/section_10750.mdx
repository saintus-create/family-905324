# WIC § 10750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3.5. State Administration of Health Care Services and Medical Assistance > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 10750

---

## Text

The functions of the department may include the administration and the supervision of the administration of health care services and medical assistance within this state as an agent of the federal government and acting as a service agency for the federal government in the field of health care services and medical assistance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
