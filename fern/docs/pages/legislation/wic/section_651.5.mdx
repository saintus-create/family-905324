# WIC § 651.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 16. Wards—Commencement of Proceedings

**Citation:** WIC § 651.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “community-based organization” means a public or private nonprofit organization of demonstrated effectiveness that is representative of a community or significant segments of a community and provides educational, physical, or mental health, recreational, arts, and other youth development or related services to individuals in the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 323, Sec. 9. (AB 901) Effective January 1, 2021.)
