# WIC § 11263.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2. Eligibility for Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 11263.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a child living with his or her parent, where the parent is a minor or a nonminor dependent, as described in subdivision (v) of Section 11400, and who also is a recipient of foster care pursuant to Article 5 (commencing with Section 11400), is not an eligible child within the meaning of this chapter for the purpose of payment of AFDC benefits other than AFDC-FC benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 846, Sec. 35. (AB 1712) Effective January 1, 2013.)
