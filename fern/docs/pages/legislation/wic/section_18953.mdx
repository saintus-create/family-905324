# WIC § 18953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. The Office of Child Abuse Prevention > Article: 3. Office of Child Abuse Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 18953

---

## Text

The office shall employ such personnel as is necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter, subject to civil service rules and regulations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1334.)
