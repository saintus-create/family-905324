# WIC § 9022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 9022

---

## Text

“Supportive services” means services that maintain individuals in home environments and avoid institutional care.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
