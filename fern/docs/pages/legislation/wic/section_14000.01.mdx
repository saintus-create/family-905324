# WIC § 14000.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14000.01

---

## Text

The department shall seek federal approval, if necessary, and shall issue all-plan letters or similar instructions to implement subdivision (d) of Section 1367.25 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 499, Sec. 5. (SB 999) Effective January 1, 2017.)
