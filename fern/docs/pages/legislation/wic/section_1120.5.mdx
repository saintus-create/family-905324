# WIC § 1120.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 6. Conduct, Education, and Discipline

**Citation:** WIC § 1120.5

---

## Text

At each institution under this chapter the Youth Authority shall organize and maintain a division of instruction and such other divisions as it deems necessary and advisable in the conduct of the school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1120 by Stats. 1979, Ch. 981.)
