# WIC § 14092.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.95. Primary Care Provider Enrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 14092.1

---

## Text

The department shall seek all federal waivers necessary to allow federal financial participation in expenditures under this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 722, Sec. 85. Effective September 15, 1992.)
