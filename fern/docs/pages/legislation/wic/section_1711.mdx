# WIC § 1711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 2. Department of the Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1711

---

## Text

Any reference to the Director of the Youth Authority shall be to the Director of the Division of Juvenile Justice in the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, unless otherwise expressly provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 337, Sec. 37. (SB 823) Effective September 30, 2020.)
