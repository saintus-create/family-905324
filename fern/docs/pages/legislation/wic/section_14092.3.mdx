# WIC § 14092.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.95. Primary Care Provider Enrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 14092.3

---

## Text

In implementing this article, the director has discretion to extend administrative or reimbursement flexibilities to participating primary care providers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 722, Sec. 85. Effective September 15, 1992.)
