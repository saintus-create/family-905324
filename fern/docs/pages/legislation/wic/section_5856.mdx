# WIC § 5856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Interagency System of Care > Article: 3. Target Client Population

**Citation:** WIC § 5856

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, “children with serious emotional disturbance” means those minors under 18 years of age as described in paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 5600.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 948, Sec. 51. (AB 2119) Effective January 1, 2025.)
