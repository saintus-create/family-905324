# WIC § 14087.9725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.81. Managed Health Care System for Los Angeles County

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.9725

---

## Text

(a) Nothing in this article shall be construed as amending the requirements of Section 17000.

(b) Nothing in this article shall be construed to preclude the department from expanding Medi-Cal managed care in ways other than those expressly provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 632, Sec. 2.5. Effective September 20, 1994.)
