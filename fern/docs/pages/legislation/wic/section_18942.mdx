# WIC § 18942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10.3. Cash Assistance Program for Aged, Blind, and Disabled Legal Immigrants

**Citation:** WIC § 18942

---

## Text

The department shall reimburse the counties for the cost of actual payments made pursuant to this chapter and for the administrative costs actually attributable to those payments. Any appropriation for this chapter may be transferred to pay for the costs of a contract entered into with the federal government to administer this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 329, Sec. 38. Effective August 21, 1998.)
