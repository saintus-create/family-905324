# WIC § 9654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 10.5. Older Adult Wellness Program > Article: 1. General

## Text

“Older adult wellness program” means the program established pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 9660).
