# WIC § 14094.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.985. Whole Child Model Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14094.19

---

## Text

This article is not intended, and shall not be interpreted, to permit any reduction in benefits or eligibility levels under the CCS program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 625, Sec. 7. (SB 586) Effective January 1, 2017.)
