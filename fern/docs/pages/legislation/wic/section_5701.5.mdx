# WIC § 5701.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 5701.5

---

## Text

City-operated Bronzan-McCorquodale programs paid by the state under Section 5615 shall be directly funded in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 174. Effective June 30, 1991.)
