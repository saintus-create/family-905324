# WIC § 5370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Conservatorship for Persons with a Grave Disability

**Citation:** WIC § 5370

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a conservatorship proceeding may be initiated pursuant to this chapter for any person who has been charged with an offense, regardless of whether action is pending or has been initiated pursuant to Section 1370 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1511.)
