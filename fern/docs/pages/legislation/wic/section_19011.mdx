# WIC § 19011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19011

---

## Text

The people of the state accept the provisions and benefits of the federal Workforce Innovation and Opportunity Act (Public Law 113-128) and any subsequent amendments thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 190, Sec. 3. (AB 2877) Effective January 1, 2017.)
