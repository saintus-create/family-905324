# WIC § 5513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Mental Health Advocacy > Article: 2. Patients’ Rights Program

**Citation:** WIC § 5513

---

## Text

The patients’ rights program shall serve as a liaison between county patients’ rights advocates and the State Department of Health Care Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 107. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
