# WIC § 12600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Emergency Payments and Special Circumstances for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 4. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 12600

---

## Text

The department shall reimburse the counties for the cost of actual payments made pursuant to this chapter and for the administrative costs actually attributable to such payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
