# WIC § 11275.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2.5. Needy Families’ Paperwork Reduction Act of 1990

**Citation:** WIC § 11275.15

---

## Text

No application for aid may be denied for failure to provide verification if the applicant is cooperating in obtaining the information requested, except as required by Section 11268.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1586, Sec. 3.)
