# WIC § 15511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15511

---

## Text

The county welfare department shall carry out the local administrative responsibilities of this chapter, subject to the supervision of the department and to rules and regulations adopted by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1195.)
