# WIC § 12254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 6. Services

**Citation:** WIC § 12254

## Text

Funding of this article is subject to the provisions of Part 1.5 (commencing with Section 10100) of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1235. Note: Repeal conditions in Sec. 14 of Ch. 1235 failed.)
