# WIC § 825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 22. Wards and Dependent Children—Records

**Citation:** WIC § 825

---

## Text

The order and findings of the superior court in each case under the provisions of this chapter shall be entered in a suitable book or other form of written record which shall be kept for that purpose and known as the “juvenile court record.”

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1616.)
