# WIC § 1985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1.6. Juvenile Reentry Grant

**Citation:** WIC § 1985

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on the 90th day after the enactment of the act adding this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 729, Sec. 23. (AB 1628) Effective October 19, 2010. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (Jan. 17, 2011) for Chapter 1.6, commencing with Section 1980.)
