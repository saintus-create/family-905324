# WIC § 14017.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14017.6

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, all references to “the Medi-Cal card,” identified in Section 14017.8, shall be deemed to also be a reference to the benefits identification card, identified in Section 14017.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 171, Sec. 36. Effective August 10, 2001.)
