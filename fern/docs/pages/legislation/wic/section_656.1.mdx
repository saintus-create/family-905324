# WIC § 656.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 16. Wards—Commencement of Proceedings

**Citation:** WIC § 656.1

---

## Text

Any petition alleging that the minor is a person described by Section 602 shall specify as to each count whether the crime charged is a felony or a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1071.)
