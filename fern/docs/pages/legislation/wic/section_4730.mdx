# WIC § 4730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 7. Appeal Procedure > Article: 5. Access to Records

**Citation:** WIC § 4730

---

## Text

Any person who willfully and knowingly violates the provisions of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 506, Sec. 2.)
