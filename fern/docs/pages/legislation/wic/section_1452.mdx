# WIC § 1452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 5. Youth Reinvestment Grant Program > Article: 2. Trauma-Informed Diversion Programs for Indian Children

**Citation:** WIC § 1452

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “Indian child” and “Indian tribe” shall have the same meaning as provided in Section 224.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 36, Sec. 33. (AB 1812) Effective June 27, 2018.)
