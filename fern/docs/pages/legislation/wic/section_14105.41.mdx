# WIC § 14105.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 14105.41

---

## Text

Moneys accruing to the department from contracts executed pursuant to Section 14105.33 shall be deposited in the Health Care Deposit Fund, and shall be subject to appropriation by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 93, Sec. 80) by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1161, Sec. 68. Effective September 30, 2002.)
