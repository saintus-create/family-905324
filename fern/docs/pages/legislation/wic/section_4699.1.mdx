# WIC § 4699.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 6.5. Developmental Services Workforce Stabilization

**Citation:** WIC § 4699.1

## Text

Implementation of this chapter shall be subject to an appropriation by the Legislature for the purpose of this chapter.
