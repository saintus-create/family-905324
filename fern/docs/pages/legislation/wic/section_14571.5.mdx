# WIC § 14571.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.7. Adult Day Health Care Programs > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 14571.5

---

## Text

Federally qualified health centers shall be reimbursed on a prospective payment system rate basis pursuant to Section 14132.100 for the provision of adult day health care services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 691, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2007.)
