# WIC § 19017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19017

---

## Text

The department may conduct research and compile statistics relating to the provision of services or the need of services by individuals with disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 937, Sec. 9. Effective October 8, 1993.)
