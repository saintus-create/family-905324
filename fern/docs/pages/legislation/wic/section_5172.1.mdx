# WIC § 5172.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 1.5. Detention of Individuals Under the Influence of Alcohol for Evaluation and Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5172.1

---

## Text

Any person who is a danger to others, or to himself, or gravely disabled as a result of inebriation, may voluntarily apply for admission to a 72-hour evaluation and detoxification treatment facility for inebriates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1581.)
