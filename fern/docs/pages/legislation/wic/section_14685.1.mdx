# WIC § 14685.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.8. Medi-Cal Management: Alternative Methods > Article: 5. Mental Health Managed Care

**Citation:** WIC § 14685.1

## Text

Section 14685 is hereby repealed on November 7, 2012, if Section 36 has been added to Article XIII of the California Constitution as of that date.

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 244. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
