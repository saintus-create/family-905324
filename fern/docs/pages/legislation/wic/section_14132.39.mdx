# WIC § 14132.39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14132.39

---

## Text

Midwifery services provided by a licensed midwife shall be covered under this chapter, to the extent that federal financial participation is available, and, subject to utilization controls.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1280, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
