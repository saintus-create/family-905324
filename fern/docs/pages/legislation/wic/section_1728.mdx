# WIC § 1728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 2.5. Board of Juvenile Hearings

**Citation:** WIC § 1728

---

## Text

The Governor may remove any member of the Board of Juvenile Hearings for misconduct, incompetency, or neglect of duty after a full hearing by the Board of State and Community Corrections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 33, Sec. 49. (SB 843) Effective June 27, 2016.)
