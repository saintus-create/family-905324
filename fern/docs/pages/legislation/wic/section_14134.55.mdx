# WIC § 14134.55

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14134.55

---

## Text

The department shall streamline and simplify existing Medi-Cal program procedures in order to improve access to lactation supports and breast pumps among Medi-Cal recipients.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 460, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2008.)
