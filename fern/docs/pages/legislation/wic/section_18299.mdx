# WIC § 18299

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. The Domestic Violence Shelter-Based Programs Act

**Citation:** WIC § 18299

---

## Text

A domestic violence shelter-based program shall maintain annual fiscal reports in a form to be prescribed by the Generally Accepted Accounting Principles (GAAP).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 857, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
