# WIC § 781.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 20. Wards—Modification of Juvenile Court Judgments and Orders

**Citation:** WIC § 781.1

---

## Text

A superior court or probation department shall not charge an applicant a fee for filing a petition to seal records under Section 781.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 582, Sec. 1. (AB 1394) Effective January 1, 2020.)
