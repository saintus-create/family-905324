# WIC § 1009.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 1009.4

---

## Text

If the Director of the Youth Authority finds that the amount of any refund is less than three dollars ($3), he may retain such amount, unless demand for the payment of such refund is made within six months after the determination that a refund is due. If such demand is made, the refund shall be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 60.)
