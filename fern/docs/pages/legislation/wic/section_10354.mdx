# WIC § 10354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 19. Child Care Subsidy Plan for the City and County of San Francisco

**Citation:** WIC § 10354

---

## Text

Any modifications to the plan shall be submitted in conformance with the procedures established in Chapter 18 (commencing with Section 10340).
