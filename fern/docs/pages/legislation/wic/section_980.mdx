# WIC § 980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 28. Adjustment Schools

**Citation:** WIC § 980

---

## Text

The governing board of any adjustment school shall cause the school to be conducted as may seem best calculated to carry out the intentions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 534.)
