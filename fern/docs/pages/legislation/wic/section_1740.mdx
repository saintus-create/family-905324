# WIC § 1740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 3. Commitments to Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1740

---

## Text

When a court commits a person to the Authority such court shall at once forward to the Authority a certified copy of the order of commitment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 937.)
