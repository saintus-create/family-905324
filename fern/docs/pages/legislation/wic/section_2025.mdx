# WIC § 2025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act Program

**Citation:** WIC § 2025

---

## Text

A county that is the recipient of a contract pursuant to this chapter may use funds received under the contract to provide grant awards to private nonprofit entities for the acquisition, renovation, construction, or purchase of equipment for a youth shelter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 493, Sec. 3. (SB 347) Effective January 1, 2014.)
