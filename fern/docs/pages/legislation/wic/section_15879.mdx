# WIC § 15879

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 3. Policies Issued by the Department

**Citation:** WIC § 15879

---

## Text

Except as provided in Article 3.5 (commencing with Section 14124.70) of Chapter 7 of Part 3, benefits received under this article or Article 4 (commencing with Section 15881) are in excess of and secondary to, any other form of health benefits coverage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15872.5.)
