# WIC § 18931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10.1. Food Assistance Program for Legal Immigrants

**Citation:** WIC § 18931

---

## Text

Any person who is eligible for federally funded Supplemental Nutrition Assistance Program benefits, administered in California as CalFresh benefits, shall not be eligible for assistance under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 227, Sec. 96. (AB 1400) Effective January 1, 2012.)
