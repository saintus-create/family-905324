# WIC § 10963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Hearings

**Citation:** WIC § 10963

---

## Text

The county director shall comply with and execute every decision of the director rendered pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
