# WIC § 18933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10.1. Food Assistance Program for Legal Immigrants

**Citation:** WIC § 18933

---

## Text

Benefits provided under this chapter shall be equivalent to the benefits provided under the federal Food Stamp Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 287, Sec. 1. Effective August 18, 1997.)
