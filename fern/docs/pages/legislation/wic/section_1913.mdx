# WIC § 1913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 11. Classification and Information System

**Citation:** WIC § 1913

---

## Text

The Department of the Youth Authority shall seek federal funds and funds from other sources to develop the classification system and to assess the feasibility of a statewide juvenile information system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 452, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
