# WIC § 4113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4113

---

## Text

The state hospitals and the officers thereof shall make such financial statements to the Controller as the Controller requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
