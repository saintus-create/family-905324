# WIC § 14088.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.9. Primary Care Provider Case Management

**Citation:** WIC § 14088.05

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “primary care case management plan” means a primary care provider or other entity who has contracted with the department pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 859, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
