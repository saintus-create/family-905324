# WIC § 11000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11000

---

## Text

The provisions of law relating to a public assistance program shall be fairly and equitably construed to effect the stated objects and purposes of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 409, Sec. 1.)
