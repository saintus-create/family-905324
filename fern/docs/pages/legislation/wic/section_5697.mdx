# WIC § 5697

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Program Initiatives > Article: 6. Regional Facilities for Wards with Serious Emotional Disturbance

**Citation:** WIC § 5697

---

## Text

The regional board of directors shall contract with the county in which the regional facility is located for the provision of a public education program which will meet the educational requirements and needs of the wards admitted to the facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (by Sec. 167) and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 170. Effective June 30, 1991.)
