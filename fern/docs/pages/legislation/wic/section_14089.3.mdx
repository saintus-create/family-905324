# WIC § 14089.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.91. Geographic Managed Care Pilot Project

**Citation:** WIC § 14089.3

---

## Text

The department shall not contract with insurance carriers, organized health systems, or provider organizations, that employ or subcontract with plans that employ providers under suspension from the Medicare or Medi-Cal program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1509, Sec. 4.)
