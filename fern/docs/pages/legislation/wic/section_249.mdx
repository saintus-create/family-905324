# WIC § 249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 4. The Juvenile Court

**Citation:** WIC § 249

---

## Text

No order of a referee removing a minor from his home shall become effective until expressly approved by a judge of the juvenile court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1068.)
