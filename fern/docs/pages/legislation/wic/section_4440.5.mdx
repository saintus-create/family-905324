# WIC § 4440.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4440.5

---

## Text

A state hospital under the jurisdiction of the department may also be known as a developmental center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 582, Sec. 2.)
