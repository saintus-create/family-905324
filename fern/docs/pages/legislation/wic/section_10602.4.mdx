# WIC § 10602.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. State Department of Social Services > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 10602.4

---

## Text

The Employment Development Department shall investigate, examine, and make reports upon the public officers who are in any way responsible for the administration of public funds used for services which are administered by the Employment Development Department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10602.5 by Stats. 1977, Ch. 579.)
