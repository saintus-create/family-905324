# WIC § 16006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Foster Care Placement

**Citation:** WIC § 16006

---

## Text

Children and nonminor dependents in out-of-home care shall be placed according to their gender identity, regardless of the gender or sex listed in their court or child welfare records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 805, Sec. 3. (SB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
