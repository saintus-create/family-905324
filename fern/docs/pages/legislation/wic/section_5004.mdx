# WIC § 5004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 5004

---

## Text

Persons with mental health disorders and persons with developmental disabilities shall receive protection from criminal acts equal to that provided any other resident in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 144, Sec. 85. (AB 1847) Effective January 1, 2015.)
