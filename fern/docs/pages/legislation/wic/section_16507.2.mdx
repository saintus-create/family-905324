# WIC § 16507.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. State Child Welfare Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16507.2

---

## Text

Prior to entering into a voluntary placement agreement with a parent or guardian, the social worker shall make every attempt to keep the family together by offering appropriate child welfare services except in the case of a voluntary placement pending relinquishment as provided for in subdivision (c) of Section 16507.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 978, Sec. 53. Effective September 13, 1982.)
