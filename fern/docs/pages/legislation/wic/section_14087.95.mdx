# WIC § 14087.95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.8. County Health Systems

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.95

---

## Text

Counties contracting with the department pursuant to this article shall be exempt from the provisions of Chapter 2.2 (commencing with Section 1340) of Division 2 of the Health and Safety Code for purposes of carrying out the contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1594, Sec. 48. Effective September 30, 1982.)
