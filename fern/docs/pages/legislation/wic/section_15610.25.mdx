# WIC § 15610.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.25

---

## Text

“Developmentally disabled person” means a person with a developmental disability specified by or as described in subdivision (a) of Section 4512.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 594, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
