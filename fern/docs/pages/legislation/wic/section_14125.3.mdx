# WIC § 14125.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3.7. Incontinence Medical Supplies

**Citation:** WIC § 14125.3

---

## Text

The department shall reduce the weighted average of the negotiated contract prices within each product category in effect on June 30, 1992, as follows:

(a) For all premium underpads, as defined by the department, by 24 percent.

(b) For all other incontinence medical supply products, as defined by the department, by 14 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1106, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
