# WIC § 15352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 3. Scope of Services

**Citation:** WIC § 15352

---

## Text

Supportive care organization services shall be made available as needed. “Need” determinations shall be made jointly by the participants and project’s staff. Any disagreements shall be appealable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 992.)
