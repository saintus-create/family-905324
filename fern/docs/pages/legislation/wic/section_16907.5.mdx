# WIC § 16907.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 16907.5

---

## Text

“Pediatric services” means all medical services rendered by any licensed physician to persons from birth to 21 years of age, and shall include attendance at labor and delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 51, Sec. 23. Effective April 18, 1990.)
