# WIC § 4415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Part: 1. GENERAL ADMINISTRATION, POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE DEPARTMENT

**Citation:** WIC § 4415

---

## Text

Except as in this chapter otherwise prescribed, the provisions of the Government Code relating to state officers and departments shall apply to the State Department of Developmental Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
