# WIC § 15810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Medi-Cal Access Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15810

---

## Text

(a) This chapter, formerly known as the AIM-Linked Infants Program, shall be known, and may be cited, as the Medi-Cal Access Program.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 66) and added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 67. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, by its own provisions.)
