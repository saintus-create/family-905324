# WIC § 14124.39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3.3. Protect Patients Now Act of 2024

**Citation:** WIC § 14124.39

---

## Text

Title

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Protect Patients Now Act of 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 5, 2024, by initiative Proposition 34, Sec. 1. Effective December 18, 2024. Approved in Proposition 34 at the November 5, 2024, election. Operative January 1, 2025, pursuant to Section 14124.52.)
