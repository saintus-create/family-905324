# WIC § 16804.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Part: 4.6. LOCAL HEALTH CARE BUDGETING > Chapter: 7. Family First Prevention Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16804.1

---

## Text

(a) No fee or charge shall be required of any person before a county renders medically necessary services to persons entitled to services pursuant to Section 17000.

(b) This section is declaratory of existing law and shall not be interpreted to effect a county’s authority to implement a reasonable sliding fee schedule based on ability to pay.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 200. Effective June 30, 1991.)
