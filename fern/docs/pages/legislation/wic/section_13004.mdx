# WIC § 13004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.1. Social Services

**Citation:** WIC § 13004

---

## Text

Counties, in expending the County Services Block Grant allocation shall provide protective services for adults pursuant to Section 12251.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 111, Sec. 44. Effective July 30, 2001.)
