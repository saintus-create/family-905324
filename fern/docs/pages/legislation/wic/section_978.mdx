# WIC § 978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 28. Adjustment Schools

**Citation:** WIC § 978

---

## Text

The juvenile court shall review the order of commitment at least once each year, and upon review the court may continue, terminate, or modify the order of commitment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 534.)
