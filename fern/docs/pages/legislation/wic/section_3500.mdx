# WIC § 3500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. CALIFORNIA HEALTH AND HUMAN SERVICES AGENCY DEPARTMENTS: PUBLIC NOTICE REQUIREMENTS

**Citation:** WIC § 3500

---

## Text

If a department within the California Health and Human Services Agency has received approval of an operational state plan by a federal agency, or has applied and has been approved for a waiver from a federal law or federal regulation, the department shall make any and all approved plans and waivers available to the public by publishing a hyperlink to that information on the homepage of the department’s Internet Web site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 296, Sec. 2. (AB 294) Effective January 1, 2016.)
