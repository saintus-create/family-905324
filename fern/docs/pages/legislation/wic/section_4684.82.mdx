# WIC § 4684.82

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 6. Development and Support of Community Facilities and Programs > Article: 3.6. Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes

**Citation:** WIC § 4684.82

---

## Text

The vendoring regional center shall, before placing any consumer into an enhanced behavioral supports home, ensure that the home has a license issued by the State Department of Social Services for not more than four individuals with developmental disabilities, is certified by the State Department of Developmental Services, and has a contract with the regional center that meets the contracting requirements established by the State Department of Developmental Services through regulations promulgated pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 11, Sec. 27. (AB 79) Effective June 29, 2020.)
