# WIC § 5352.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Conservatorship for Persons with a Grave Disability

**Citation:** WIC § 5352.2

---

## Text

Where the duly designated officer providing conservatorship investigation is a public guardian, his official oath and bond as public guardian are in lieu of any other bond or oath on the grant of temporary letters of conservatorship to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 566.)
