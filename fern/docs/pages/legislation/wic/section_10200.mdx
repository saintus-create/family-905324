# WIC § 10200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of Childcare Programs to the State Department of Social Services

**Citation:** WIC § 10200

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Early Childhood Development Act of 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 24, Sec. 86. (SB 98) Effective June 29, 2020. Operative July 1, 2021, pursuant to Section 10201.)
