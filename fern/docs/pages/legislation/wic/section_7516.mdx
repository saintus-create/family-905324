# WIC § 7516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Hospitals for the Developmentally Disabled

**Citation:** WIC § 7516

---

## Text

Nothing in this division contained interferes with or affects the status of such patients as are now in the Sonoma State Hospital under terms of life tenure.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
