# WIC § 15610.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.10

---

## Text

“Adult protective services” means those activities performed on behalf of elders and dependent adults who have come to the attention of the adult protective services agency due to potential abuse or neglect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 85, Sec. 63. (AB 135) Effective July 16, 2021.)
