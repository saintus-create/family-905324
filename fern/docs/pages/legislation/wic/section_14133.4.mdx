# WIC § 14133.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14133.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, utilization controls adopted by the State Department of Health Services shall not include prior authorization for portable X-ray services provided in nursing facilities and all categories of intermediate care facilities for the developmentally disabled, as defined in Section 1250 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1329, Sec. 30. Effective September 26, 1990.)
