# WIC § 14016.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14016.7

---

## Text

(a) Managed care contracts entered into by the department under the act adding this section shall include all of the following:

(1) Contractor requirements concerning eligibility and coverage verification.

(2) Utilization controls.

(3) Claims processing.

(b) The contract requirements shall include all of the following:

(1) Standards for prompt response to provider requests for information.

(2) Twenty-four hour response to emergency service authorization requests.

(3) Use of commonly accepted billing forms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 95, Sec. 4. Effective June 30, 1991.)
