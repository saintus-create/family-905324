# WIC § 898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 24.5. Regional Youth Educational Facilities

**Citation:** WIC § 898

---

## Text

The participating counties shall appoint a citizens advisory committee with a membership drawn from law enforcement, judiciary, probation, education, corrections, business, and the general public, whose function is to review the goals, objectives, and programs of each youth educational facility and provide input to the facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1455, Sec. 6. Effective September 26, 1984.)
