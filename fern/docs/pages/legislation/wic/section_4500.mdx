# WIC § 4500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 1. Title and Intent

**Citation:** WIC § 4500

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Lanterman Developmental Disabilities Services Act.
