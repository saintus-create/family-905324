# WIC § 18275.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4.5. Child Sexual Abuse Prevention Training Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 18275.5

---

## Text

Unless the context requires otherwise, for purposes of this chapter:

(a) “Director” means the Director of Emergency Services.

(b) “Office” means the Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 539. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
