# WIC § 5540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Mental Health Advocacy > Article: 5. Access to Records

**Citation:** WIC § 5540

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter or in other provisions of law, information about and records of recipients of mental health services shall be confidential in accordance with the provisions of Section 5328.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 841, Sec. 7.)
