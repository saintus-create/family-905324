# WIC § 1752.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1752.3

---

## Text

The director may, from any moneys made available for such purposes, allocate funds to local governmental and nongovernmental agencies to share in the cost of local correctional programs which are partially financed by federal grants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 816.)
