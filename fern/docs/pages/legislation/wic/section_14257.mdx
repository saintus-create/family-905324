# WIC § 14257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14257

---

## Text

Nothing in this act shall preclude the director from contracting with licensed specialized health care service plans which provide only dental, pharmaceutical, optometric, or psychological services in accordance with regulations issued by the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1036.)
