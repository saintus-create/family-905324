# WIC § 1801.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 6. Extended Detention of Dangerous Persons

**Citation:** WIC § 1801.6

---

## Text

When the venue of a proceeding under this chapter is changed, costs of the proceeding are chargeable as provided in Section 1037 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 235, Sec. 2.)
