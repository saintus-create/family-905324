# WIC § 7227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Hospitals for the Mentally Disordered > Article: 2. Admission

**Citation:** WIC § 7227

---

## Text

Prisoners who have mental health disorders and who are in the state prisons shall be admitted to the state hospitals in accordance with the provisions of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 144, Sec. 117. (AB 1847) Effective January 1, 2015.)
