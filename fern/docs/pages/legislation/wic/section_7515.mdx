# WIC § 7515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Hospitals for the Developmentally Disabled

**Citation:** WIC § 7515

---

## Text

The medical director may, with the approval of the department having jurisdiction, cause the peremptory discharge of any person who has been a patient for the period of one month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 699. Effective January 1, 2007.)
