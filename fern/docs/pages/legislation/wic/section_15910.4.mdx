# WIC § 15910.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Part: 3.6. Low Income Health Program > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15910.4

---

## Text

As a condition of participation in the voluntary program provided under this part, a LIHP shall comply with Section 14169.7.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 286, Sec. 9. (SB 335) Effective September 16, 2011.)
