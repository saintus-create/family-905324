# WIC § 14018.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14018.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, Section 3275 of the Civil Code does not apply to Medi-Cal reimbursement or prior authorization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 146, Sec. 36. Effective July 22, 1999.)
