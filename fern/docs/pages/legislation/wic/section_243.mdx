# WIC § 243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 3. Probation Commission

**Citation:** WIC § 243

---

## Text

The probation commission shall function in an advisory capacity to the probation officer.
