# WIC § 18473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18473

---

## Text

In determining and redetermining budgets and standards, the department may give consideration to living costs and may establish budgets and standards varying in monetary worth or amount, but providing a standard of living compatible with decency and health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
