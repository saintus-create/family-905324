# WIC § 1009.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 1009.2

---

## Text

The fiscal officer of the Department of the Youth Authority shall make payment of any refund pursuant to Section 1009.1 if the Director of the Youth Authority prepares a voucher which sets forth the facts which pertain to the refund and authorizes its payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 60.)
