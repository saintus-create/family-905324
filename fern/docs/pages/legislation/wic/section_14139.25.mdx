# WIC § 14139.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.3. Long-Term Care Integration Pilot Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14139.25

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, costs to the General Fund shall not exceed the amount that would have been expended in the absence of the pilot program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
