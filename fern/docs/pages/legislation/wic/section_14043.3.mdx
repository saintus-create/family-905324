# WIC § 14043.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1.3. Provider Enrollment, Application, and Participation

**Citation:** WIC § 14043.3

---

## Text

A provider shall be required to reimburse those Medi-Cal funds received during any period for which material information was not reported, or reported falsely, to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 146, Sec. 37. Effective July 22, 1999.)
