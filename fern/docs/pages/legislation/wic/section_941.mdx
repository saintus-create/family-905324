# WIC § 941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 27. 24-Hour Schools

**Citation:** WIC § 941

---

## Text

The 24-hour school or a nonresidential boot camp school program shall be under the management and control of the probation officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 72, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
