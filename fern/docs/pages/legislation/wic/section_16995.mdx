# WIC § 16995

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. California Healthcare for Indigents Program > Article: 10. Eligibility and Benefit Requirements

**Citation:** WIC § 16995

---

## Text

Funds received by a county pursuant to this chapter shall not be used to support health services provided to persons detained in a county or city jail or other correctional facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 719, Sec. 11. Effective September 15, 1992.)
