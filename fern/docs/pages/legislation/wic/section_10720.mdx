# WIC § 10720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3.5. State Administration of Health Care Services and Medical Assistance > Article: 1. Organization

**Citation:** WIC § 10720

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “department” means the State Department of Health Services, and “director” means the State Director of Health Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
