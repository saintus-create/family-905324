# WIC § 11011.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11011.2

---

## Text

For the 2021–22 fiscal year, upon order of the Director of Finance, the Controller shall transfer four hundred fifty million dollars ($450,000,000) from the General Fund to the Safety Net Reserve Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 85, Sec. 29. (AB 135) Effective July 16, 2021.)
