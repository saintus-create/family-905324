# WIC § 12053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 12053

---

## Text

An applicant’s share of his spouse’s community property income is defined as the income which is community property subject to the direction and control of the applicant, except for the earnings of his or her spouse.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
