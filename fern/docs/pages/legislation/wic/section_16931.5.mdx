# WIC § 16931.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Rural Health Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16931.5

---

## Text

The county may reimburse for emergency services provided by a physician in a standby emergency room in a hospital specified in Section 124840 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 491. Effective September 29, 1996.)
