# WIC § 1798

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 5.5. Crime and Delinquency Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 1798

---

## Text

As of July 1, 2005, the State Commission on Juvenile Justice, Crime and Delinquency Prevention is abolished.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 10, Sec. 89. Effective May 10, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 99 of Ch. 10.)
