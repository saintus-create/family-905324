# WIC § 9010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 9010

---

## Text

“Comprehensive and coordinated system” means a program of interrelated social and nutrition services designed to meet the needs of older individuals in a planning and service area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
