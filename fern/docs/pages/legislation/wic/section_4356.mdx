# WIC § 4356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 5. Persons With Acquired Traumatic Brain Injury

**Citation:** WIC § 4356

---

## Text

Using data collected consistent with requirements established pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 4355, the department shall monitor and evaluate the performance of service providers.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 439, Sec. 6. (AB 398) Effective January 1, 2010.)
