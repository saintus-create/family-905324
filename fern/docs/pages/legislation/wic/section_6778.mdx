# WIC § 6778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 6. Counselors in Mental Health

**Citation:** WIC § 6778

---

## Text

The counselor in mental health may perform such services as are designated by the county. Every counselor, assistant counselor, and deputy counselor in mental health shall have the powers of a peace officer.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1374.)
