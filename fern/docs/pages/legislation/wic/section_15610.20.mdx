# WIC § 15610.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Elder Abuse and Dependent Adult Civil Protection Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 15610.20

---

## Text

“Clients’ rights advocate” means the individual or individuals assigned by a regional center or state hospital developmental center to be responsible for clients’ rights assurance for persons with developmental disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 594, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
