# WIC § 10487

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 31. Local Planning Councils > Article: 2. Membership and Funding of Local Child Care Development Planning Councils

**Citation:** WIC § 10487

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that any additional conditions imposed upon local planning councils shall be funded from available federal funds to the greatest extent legally possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
