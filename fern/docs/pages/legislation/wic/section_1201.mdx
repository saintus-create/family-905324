# WIC § 1201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 9. Finances

**Citation:** WIC § 1201

---

## Text

For each person committed to any state school the county from which he was committed shall make payments to the state as provided in Section 911 of this code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 605.)
