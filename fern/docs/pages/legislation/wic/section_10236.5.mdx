# WIC § 10236.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. Migrant Child Care and Development Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 10236.5

---

## Text

For the purpose of enrollment in migrant child care and development programs, the department shall ensure that the self certification of income shall be consistent with other child care and development programs under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 459, Sec. 2. (SB 778) Effective January 1, 2026.)
