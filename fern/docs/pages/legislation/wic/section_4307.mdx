# WIC § 4307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WIC § 4307

---

## Text

The hospital director is the chief executive officer of the hospital and is responsible for all hospital operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 12, Sec. 43. (AB 80) Effective June 29, 2020.)
