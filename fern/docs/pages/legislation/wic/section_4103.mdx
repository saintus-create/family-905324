# WIC § 4103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4103

---

## Text

Each such corporation may acquire and hold in its corporate name by gift, grant, devise, or bequest property to be applied to the maintenance of the patients of the hospital and for the general use of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
