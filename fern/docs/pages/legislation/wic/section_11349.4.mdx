# WIC § 11349.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 3.9. Employment with Public Contractors

**Citation:** WIC § 11349.4

---

## Text

In the event that the State Department of Social Services determines that a waiver of any federal regulations or policies, or both, are required to fulfill the provisions of this article, the department shall seek those waivers. If the waivers necessary are not granted by March 1, 1985, the department shall report on the barriers to the waivers and the expected date of waiver approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1259, Sec. 3.)
