# WIC § 11200.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 11200.5

---

## Text

Funding for social services under this chapter is subject to the provisions of Part 1.5 (commencing with Section 10100) of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1235. Note: Repeal conditions in Sec. 14 of Ch. 1235 failed.)
