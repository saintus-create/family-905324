# WIC § 4052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 2. Planning, Research, Evaluation and Quality Assurance > Article: 3. Technical Assistance

**Citation:** WIC § 4052

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services shall, to the extent resources are available, provide training in performance standards, model programs, cultural competency, and program development.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 53. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
