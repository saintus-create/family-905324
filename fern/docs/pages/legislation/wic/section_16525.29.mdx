# WIC § 16525.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.3. Services for Children Who Are Exposed to Alcohol or Drugs or Who Are HIV Positive > Article: 2. Options for Recovery Program

**Citation:** WIC § 16525.29

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to prevent children who are alcohol- or drug-exposed or HIV positive who have adoption as a case plan goal from receiving services under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 296, Sec. 2. Effective August 2, 1993.)
