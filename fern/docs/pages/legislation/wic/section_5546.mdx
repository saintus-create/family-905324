# WIC § 5546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Mental Health Advocacy > Article: 5. Access to Records

**Citation:** WIC § 5546

---

## Text

The actual cost of copying any records or other materials authorized under this chapter, plus any additional reasonable clerical costs, incurred in locating and making the records and materials available, shall be borne by the advocate. The additional clerical costs shall be based on a computation of the time spent locating and making the records available multiplied by the employee’s hourly wage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 841, Sec. 7.)
