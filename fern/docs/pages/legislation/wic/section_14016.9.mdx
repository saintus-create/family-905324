# WIC § 14016.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14016.9

---

## Text

Where determined to be cost effective, the department shall utilize the earnings clearance system to verify the eligibility of persons who have applied for or are receiving benefits pursuant to Sections 14005.4 and 14005.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1354, Sec. 11.)
