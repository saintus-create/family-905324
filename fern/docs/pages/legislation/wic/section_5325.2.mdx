# WIC § 5325.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 7. Legal and Civil Rights of Persons Involuntarily Detained

**Citation:** WIC § 5325.2

---

## Text

Any person who is subject to detention pursuant to Section 5150, 5250, 5260, 5270.15, or 5270.70 shall have the right to refuse treatment with antipsychotic medication subject to provisions set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 643, Sec. 1. (SB 1184) Effective January 1, 2025.)
