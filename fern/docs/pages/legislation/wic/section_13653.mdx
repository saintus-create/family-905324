# WIC § 13653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.9. Enhanced Services for Asylees and Vulnerable Noncitizens

**Citation:** WIC § 13653

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that this chapter is a state law that may provide assistance and services for undocumented persons within the meaning of subsection (d) of Section 1621 of Title 8 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 85, Sec. 60. (AB 135) Effective July 16, 2021.)
