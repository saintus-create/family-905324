# WIC § 18325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.7. Nutrition Program for the Elderly

**Citation:** WIC § 18325

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the McCarthy-Kennick Nutrition Program for the Elderly Act of 1972.

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 918.)
