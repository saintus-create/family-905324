# WIC § 18983.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.5. Child Abuse Prevention Coordinating Council Act > Article: 3. Selection and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18983.6

---

## Text

Councils receiving funding under this chapter shall develop a protocol for interagency coordination and provide yearly reports to the county board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 603, Sec. 1. Effective September 21, 1989.)
