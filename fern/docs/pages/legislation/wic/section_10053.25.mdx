# WIC § 10053.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 10053.25

---

## Text

Funding of Sections 10053 and 14503 is subject to the provisions of Part 1.5 (commencing with Section 10100). The level of funding for Sections 14503 and 14504 shall not fall below the total amount appropriated for family planning services in the 1977–78 fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 676, Sec. 345. Note: Conditions in Stats. 1978, Ch. 1235, Sec. 14, for repeal of this section failed.)
