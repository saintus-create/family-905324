# WIC § 12301.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 7. In-Home Supportive Services

**Citation:** WIC § 12301.7

---

## Text

The annual administrative cost for any public authority or nonprofit consortium created pursuant to Section 12301.6, exclusive of any increase in provider wages or benefits or employer taxes when negotiated or agreed to by the public authority or nonprofit consortium, shall be shared by the state and the counties as prescribed in Section 12306.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 606, Sec. 27. Effective October 3, 1997.)
