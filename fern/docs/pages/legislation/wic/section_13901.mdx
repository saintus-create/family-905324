# WIC § 13901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6.5. Nonmedical Care Facilities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 13901

---

## Text

It is further the purpose of this chapter to give recognition to the fact that persons who require provision of an out-of-home living arrangement present a common set of personal problems, whether they are classified as aged, blind or disabled.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
