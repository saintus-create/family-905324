# WIC § 14089.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.91. Geographic Managed Care Pilot Project

**Citation:** WIC § 14089.2

---

## Text

In accordance with procedures required by Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 14200), each contract with a capitated health system shall provide for a grievance procedure under which Medi-Cal beneficiaries may submit their grievances. The department shall establish standards for these procedures to include appeals to an entity other than the capitated health system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1509, Sec. 3.)
