# WIC § 11263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2. Eligibility for Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 11263

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 11253 and 11455, no person over the age of 18 years is a needy child within the meaning of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 579.)
