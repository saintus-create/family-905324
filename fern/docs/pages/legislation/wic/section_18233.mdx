# WIC § 18233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3.3. CalWORKs Demonstration Projects > Article: 1. County Welfare-to-Work Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** WIC § 18233

---

## Text

Before authorizing a demonstration project, the director shall ensure that it is consistent with the intent of state CalWORKs law. At least 30 days prior to approving a demonstration project, the director shall provide written notice to the chairperson of the relevant policy committees of each house of the Legislature and the Chairperson of the Joint Legislative Budget Committee. The notice shall identify any increased state costs that will result from the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 181. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998, by Sec. 183 of Ch. 270.)
