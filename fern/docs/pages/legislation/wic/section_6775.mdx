# WIC § 6775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 6. Counselors in Mental Health

**Citation:** WIC § 6775

---

## Text

The office of counselor in mental health may be created in any county in this state by the board of supervisors thereof. The counselors in mental health to serve under the provisions of this chapter shall be nominated and appointed by the judge of the superior court by written order entered in the minutes of the court.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
