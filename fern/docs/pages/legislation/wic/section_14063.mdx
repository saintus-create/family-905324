# WIC § 14063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14063

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “Medi-Cal” means the California Medical Assistance Program.
