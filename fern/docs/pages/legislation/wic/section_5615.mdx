# WIC § 5615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 5615

---

## Text

If they so elect, cities that were operating independent public mental health programs on January 1, 1990, shall continue to receive direct payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 102. Effective June 30, 1991.)
