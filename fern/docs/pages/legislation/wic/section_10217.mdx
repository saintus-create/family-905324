# WIC § 10217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Resource and Referral Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 10217

---

## Text

Funds appropriated for the purpose of this chapter may be used for child care resource and referral programs which may be operated by public or private nonprofit entities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
