# WIC § 5350.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Conservatorship for Persons with a Grave Disability

**Citation:** WIC § 5350.1

---

## Text

The purpose of conservatorship, as provided for in this article, is to provide individualized treatment, supervision, and placement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1294.)
