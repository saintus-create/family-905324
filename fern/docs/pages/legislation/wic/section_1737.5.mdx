# WIC § 1737.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 3. Commitments to Youth Authority

**Citation:** WIC § 1737.5

---

## Text

A commitment to the Authority is a judgment within the meaning of Chapter 1 of Title 8 of Part 2 of the Penal Code, and is appealable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 898.)
