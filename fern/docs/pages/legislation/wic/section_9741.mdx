# WIC § 9741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 11. State Ombudsman > Article: 5. Advisory Council

**Citation:** WIC § 9741

---

## Text

At least 30 days prior to the designation of a new organization or agency as an approved organization, the department shall notify the advisory council for the purpose of soliciting comments regarding the designation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
