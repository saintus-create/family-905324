# WIC § 228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 2. Commissions and Committees

**Citation:** WIC § 228

---

## Text

A juvenile justice commission shall elect a chairman and vice chairman annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1068.)
