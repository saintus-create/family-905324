# WIC § 10304.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 13. Allocations and Expenditures

**Citation:** WIC § 10304.5

---

## Text

The state shall hold a security interest in all renovations and repairs funded pursuant to Section 10303.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
