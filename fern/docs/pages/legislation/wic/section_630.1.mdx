# WIC § 630.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 15. Wards—Temporary Custody and Detention

**Citation:** WIC § 630.1

---

## Text

Upon reasonable notification by counsel representing the minor, his parents or guardian, the clerk of the court shall notify such counsel of the hearings in the manner provided for notice to the parent or guardian of the minor under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 507.)
