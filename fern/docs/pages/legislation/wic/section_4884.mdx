# WIC § 4884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 15. Qualified ABLE Program

**Citation:** WIC § 4884

---

## Text

The board shall market this program to residents of the United States to the extent funds are available to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 470, Sec. 3. (AB 384) Effective January 1, 2018.)
