# WIC § 14252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14252

---

## Text

“Medi-Cal beneficiary” means a person who is eligible to receive benefits under Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 14000) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1366.)
