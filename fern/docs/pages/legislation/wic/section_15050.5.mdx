# WIC § 15050.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9. Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Social Welfare Federal Fund

**Citation:** WIC § 15050.5

---

## Text

All references in this division or in any other provision of law to the Social Welfare Federal Fund shall be deemed references to the Federal Trust Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 235, Sec. 4. Operative July 1, 1985, by Sec. 16 of Ch. 235.)
