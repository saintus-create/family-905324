# WIC § 7101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. County Psychiatric Hospitals

**Citation:** WIC § 7101

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “county psychiatric hospital” means the hospital, ward, or facility provided by the county pursuant to the provisions of Section 7100.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
