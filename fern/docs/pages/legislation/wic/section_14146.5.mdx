# WIC § 14146.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.6. Medi-Cal Medical Interpretation Services

**Citation:** WIC § 14146.5

---

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on July 1, 2026, and, as of January 1, 2027, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 105, Sec. 63. (AB 144) Effective September 17, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 4.6, commencing with Section 14146.)
