# WIC § 14110.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 14110.9

---

## Text

No nursing facility or any category of intermediate care facility for the developmentally disabled may require a security deposit from a Medi-Cal beneficiary who applies for admission to the facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1329, Sec. 21. Effective September 26, 1990.)
