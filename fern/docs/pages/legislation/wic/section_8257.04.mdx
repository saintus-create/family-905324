# WIC § 8257.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 6.5. California Interagency Council on Homelessness

**Citation:** WIC § 8257.04

---

## Text

The executive officer may employ employees that the executive officer may need to discharge, in a proper manner, the duties imposed upon them by law. The executive officer shall prescribe their duties and fix their compensation in accordance with classifications made by the State Personnel Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 28, Sec. 112. (SB 170) Effective June 29, 2026.)
