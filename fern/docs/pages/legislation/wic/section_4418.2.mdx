# WIC § 4418.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Part: 1. GENERAL ADMINISTRATION, POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE DEPARTMENT

**Citation:** WIC § 4418.2

---

## Text

The department shall support, utilizing regional resource development projects, the activities specified in Sections 4418.25, 4418.3, and 4418.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1161, Sec. 27. Effective September 30, 2002.)
