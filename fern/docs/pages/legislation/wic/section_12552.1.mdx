# WIC § 12552.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Emergency Payments and Special Circumstances for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 3. Special Circumstances

**Citation:** WIC § 12552.1

---

## Text

(a) A county may transfer funds received for the implementation of this chapter from its administrative allocation to its benefit allocation for the purposes of providing additional benefits to clients to the extent that administrative savings are achieved.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2001.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 111, Sec. 42. Effective July 30, 2001. Applicable from July 1, 2001, by its own provisions.)
