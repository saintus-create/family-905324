# WIC § 15876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 15876

---

## Text

Plan rates for major risk medical benefits approved for the program shall not be excessive, inadequate, or unfairly discriminatory, but shall be adequate to pay anticipated costs of claims or services and administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15872.5.)
