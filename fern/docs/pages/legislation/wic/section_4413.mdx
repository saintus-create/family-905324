# WIC § 4413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Part: 1. GENERAL ADMINISTRATION, POWERS AND DUTIES OF THE DEPARTMENT

**Citation:** WIC § 4413

---

## Text

The department may appoint and fix the compensation of such employees as it deems necessary, subject to the laws governing civil service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
