# WIC § 10616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. State Department of Social Services > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 10616

---

## Text

The department shall formulate plans for the recruitment, utilization, and training of volunteers to assist in performing services and other duties for the county public social services for the purpose of improving participation in the county public welfare programs. Such plans shall not become effective in a county until approved by a resolution adopted by the board of supervisors.
