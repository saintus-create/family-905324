# WIC § 10395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 23. Administrative Review

**Citation:** WIC § 10395

---

## Text

The determination of the hearing examiner shall be the final administrative determination to be afforded the local contracting agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
