# WIC § 14003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14003

---

## Text

The Governor may enter into and execute in behalf of the state all necessary agreements in connection with this chapter as may be required by the United States government.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 4.)
