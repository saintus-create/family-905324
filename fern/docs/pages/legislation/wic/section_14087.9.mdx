# WIC § 14087.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.8. County Health Systems

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.9

---

## Text

A combination of counties may contract with the department pursuant to this article for the provision of services.
