# WIC § 14087.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.8. County Health Systems

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.7

---

## Text

To the extent that a county chooses to contract with primary care providers pursuant to this article, the county shall ensure that designation forms and appropriate information concerning primary care providers are given to beneficiaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 328, Sec. 19. Effective June 30, 1982.)
