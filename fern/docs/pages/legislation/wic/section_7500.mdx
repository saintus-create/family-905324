# WIC § 7500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Hospitals for the Developmentally Disabled

**Citation:** WIC § 7500

---

## Text

There is established in the state, Porterville Developmental Center, in Tulare County, a state hospital for the care and treatment of persons with developmental disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 76, Sec. 58. (AB 136) Effective July 16, 2021.)
