# WIC § 18993.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 14. Community Challenge Grant Program

**Citation:** WIC § 18993.9

---

## Text

The program provided for under this chapter shall be implemented only to the extent that funds are appropriated in the Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 839, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
