# WIC § 11275.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2.5. Needy Families’ Paperwork Reduction Act of 1990

**Citation:** WIC § 11275.50

---

## Text

The State Department of Social Services shall consult with the County Welfare Directors Association when developing state budget estimates on the administrative funds necessary to implement this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1586, Sec. 3.)
