# WIC § 5911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Procedures for the Transfer of Responsibility from the State to the Counties

**Citation:** WIC § 5911

---

## Text

A county or group of counties, by agreement, may expand services into additional facilities utilizing any funds available to the county or counties for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 198. Effective June 30, 1991.)
