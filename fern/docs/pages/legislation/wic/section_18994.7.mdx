# WIC § 18994.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 14.1. California Families and Children Home Visit Program

**Citation:** WIC § 18994.7

---

## Text

The Office of Child Abuse Prevention shall provide administrative oversight for the California Families and Children Home Visit Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 329, Sec. 41. Effective August 21, 1998.)
