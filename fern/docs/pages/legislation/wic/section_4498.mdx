# WIC § 4498

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WIC § 4498

---

## Text

To assure a continuous level of competency for all state hospital treatment personnel under the jurisdiction of the State Department of Developmental Services, the department shall provide adequate in-service training programs for such state hospital treatment personnel.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 429.)
