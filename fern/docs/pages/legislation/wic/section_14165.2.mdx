# WIC § 14165.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 5.1. California Medical Assistance Commission

**Citation:** WIC § 14165.2

---

## Text

The Governor shall appoint three members of the commission, one of which shall be designated chairperson. The Speaker of the Assembly and the Senate Rules Committee shall each appoint two members of the commission. Of the members first appointed by the Governor, one shall serve for a term of three years, and two for a term of two years. Of the members appointed by the Speaker of the Assembly and the Senate Rules Committee, one appointed by the Speaker of the Assembly and one appointed by the Senate Rules Committee shall serve for a period of three years with the other appointees to serve for a period of two years. Thereafter, all appointments shall be for four-year terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 329, Sec. 11.)
