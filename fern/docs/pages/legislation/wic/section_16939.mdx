# WIC § 16939

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Rural Health Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16939

---

## Text

As a condition of receiving funds pursuant to this chapter, each county shall meet the requirements of Articles 6 (commencing with Section 16980), 7 (commencing with Section 16990) and 10 (commencing with Section 16995) of Chapter 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 430, Sec. 11. Effective July 26, 1990.)
