# WIC § 261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 4. The Juvenile Court

**Citation:** WIC § 261

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of Section 262, all orders of a juvenile hearing officer shall be immediately effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 679, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1998.)
