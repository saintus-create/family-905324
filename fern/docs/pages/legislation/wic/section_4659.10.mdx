# WIC § 4659.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 2.6. Third-Party Liability

**Citation:** WIC § 4659.10

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that this article shall be implemented consistent with the responsibilities of the department and the regional centers to provide services and supports pursuant to the requirements of this division and the California Early Intervention Program. It is further the intent of the Legislature that the department and the regional centers shall continue to be the payers of last resort consistent with the requirements of this division and the California Early Intervention Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 9, Sec. 14. (SB 74) Effective March 24, 2011.)
