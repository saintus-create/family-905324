# WIC § 4753

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 8. Evaluation

**Citation:** WIC § 4753

---

## Text

By January 1, 1979, the department shall implement the evaluation system for all programs under its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
