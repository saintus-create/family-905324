# WIC § 4628

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 1. Regional Center Contracts

**Citation:** WIC § 4628

---

## Text

If, for good reason, a contracting agency is unable to meet all the criteria for a governing board established in this chapter, the director may waive those criteria for a period of time, not to exceed one year, with the approval of the state council.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 409, Sec. 40. (AB 1595) Effective January 1, 2015.)
