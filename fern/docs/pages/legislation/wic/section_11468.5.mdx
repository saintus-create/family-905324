# WIC § 11468.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11468.5

---

## Text

The provider may request review of the final decision of the department made pursuant to Section 11468.4 in accordance with Section 1094.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure, within six months of the issuance of the director’s final decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 732, Sec. 90. (AB 404) Effective January 1, 2018.)
