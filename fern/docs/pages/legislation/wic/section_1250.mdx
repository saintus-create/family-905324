# WIC § 1250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 10. The California Youth Training School

**Citation:** WIC § 1250

---

## Text

There is hereby established an institution for the confinement of males under the custody of the Director of Corrections and the Youth Authority to be known as the Heman G. Stark Youth Training School.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 555, Sec. 1.)
