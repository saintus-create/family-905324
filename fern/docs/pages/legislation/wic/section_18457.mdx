# WIC § 18457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 18457

---

## Text

In accordance with the provisions of this chapter, every county shall provide relief to needy persons who are residents of this state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
