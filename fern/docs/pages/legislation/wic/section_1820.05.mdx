# WIC § 1820.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 7.5. Juvenile Offender Local Prevention and Corrections Act

**Citation:** WIC § 1820.05

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “partnership funds” means the state’s share of funding for county juvenile ranches, camps, and forestry camps.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 157, Sec. 2. Effective July 21, 1993.)
