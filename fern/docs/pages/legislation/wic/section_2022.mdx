# WIC § 2022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 2. Youth Center and Youth Shelter Bond Act Program

**Citation:** WIC § 2022

---

## Text

The committee, as defined in Section 4496.04 of the Penal Code, shall give priority to the issuance of bonds in order to carry out the actions specified in subdivision (b) of Section 4496.12 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1535, Sec. 1.)
