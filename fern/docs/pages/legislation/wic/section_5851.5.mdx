# WIC § 5851.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Interagency System of Care > Article: 1. Legislative Findings and Intent

**Citation:** WIC § 5851.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, a “system of care county” means a county which has been approved by the State Department of Health Care Services as having the capability to provide child- and family-centered services in a collaborative manner, resulting in quantitative outcome measures.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 196. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
