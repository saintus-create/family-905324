# WIC § 5152.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 1. Detention of Persons with a Mental Health Condition for Evaluation and Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5152.2

---

## Text

Each law enforcement agency within a county shall arrange with the county behavioral health director a method for giving prompt notification to peace officers pursuant to Section 5152.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 455, Sec. 20. (SB 804) Effective January 1, 2016.)
