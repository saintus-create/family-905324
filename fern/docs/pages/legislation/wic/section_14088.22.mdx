# WIC § 14088.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.9. Primary Care Provider Case Management

**Citation:** WIC § 14088.22

---

## Text

Sections 14408, 14409, 14410, and 14411 shall apply to primary care case management plans.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 859, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
