# WIC § 14265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 14265

---

## Text

“Marketing representative” means any person who engages in marketing activities on behalf of a marketing organization or the prepaid health plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 983.)
