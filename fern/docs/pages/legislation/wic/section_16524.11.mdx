# WIC § 16524.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.2. Commercially Sexually Exploited Children Program

**Citation:** WIC § 16524.11

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2015.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 29, Sec. 79. (SB 855) Effective June 20, 2014. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (January 1, 2015) for Chapter 5.2, commencing with Section 16524.6.)
