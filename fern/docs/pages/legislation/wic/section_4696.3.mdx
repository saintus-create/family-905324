# WIC § 4696.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 6. Development and Support of Community Facilities and Programs > Article: 7. Regional Center Clients Requiring Mental Health Services

**Citation:** WIC § 4696.3

---

## Text

A regional center shall notify the clients’ rights advocate, as described in Section 4433, of all consumers placed on an involuntary psychiatric hold or in a Lanterman-Petris-Short conservatorship pursuant to Section 5250, 5260, 5270.10, 5300, or 5350.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 28, Sec. 29. (SB 81) Effective June 27, 2019.)
