# WIC § 18378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6.5. Preventive Health Care for the Aging

**Citation:** WIC § 18378

---

## Text

The California Commission on Aging shall render such technical assistance and advice as is required by the department in carrying out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1058.)
