# WIC § 14017.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14017.1

---

## Text

The Joint Legislative Audit Committee shall conduct an audit of one or more county eligibility departments.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 247. Effective October 12, 2001.)
