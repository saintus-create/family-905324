# WIC § 18253.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. County Wraparound Services Program

**Citation:** WIC § 18253.5

---

## Text

Each county shall ensure that staff participating in the wraparound services program have completed training provided or approved by the department, on providing individualized wraparound services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 561, Sec. 5. (AB 1758) Effective January 1, 2011.)
