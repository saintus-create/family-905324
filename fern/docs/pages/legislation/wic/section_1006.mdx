# WIC § 1006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 1006

---

## Text

The land purchased for the site of Preston School of Industry shall be used exclusively for the occupancy and purposes of the school.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 369.)
