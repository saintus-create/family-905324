# WIC § 17030.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 17030.1

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 10000, 17000, or 17001 or any other provision of law shall require any county or city and county to provide or pay for a service reduced or eliminated from the Medi-Cal program, pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 14000) or Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 14200) of Part 3, to a person otherwise eligible for care pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 72, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
