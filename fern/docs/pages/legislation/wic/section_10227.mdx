# WIC § 10227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Alternative Payment Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 10227

---

## Text

Alternative payments may be made for services provided in licensed centers and family child care homes, for care provided in the child’s home, and for other types of care which conform to applicable law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
