# WIC § 14132.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14132.9

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (h) of Section 14132, any utilization controls imposed under such subdivision shall not include mandatory examination by any person not licensed as a dentist under the Dental Practice Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 958.)
