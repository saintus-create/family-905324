# WIC § 14013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14013

---

## Text

The department shall establish a system for investigation of a sufficient sample of applications and affirmations as shall be deemed necessary to assure the validity of such applications.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 4.)
