# WIC § 224.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 224.4

---

## Text

The Indian child’s tribe and Indian custodian, as defined in Section 224.1, have the right to intervene at any point in an Indian child custody proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 656, Sec. 5. (AB 81) Effective September 27, 2024.)
