# WIC § 14139.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.3. Long-Term Care Integration Pilot Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14139.24

---

## Text

The department shall seek all federal waivers necessary to allow for federal financial participation in the pilot program implemented pursuant to this article. This article shall not be implemented unless and until the director has executed a declaration that the approval of all necessary federal waivers has been obtained by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
