# WIC § 4032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 2. Planning, Research, Evaluation and Quality Assurance > Article: 1. Planning, Research, Evaluation, and Quality Assurance

**Citation:** WIC § 4032

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services shall, when appropriate, give and receive grants and contracts for research, evaluation, and quality assurance efforts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 48. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
