# WIC § 15872.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 15872.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on July 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 2014) for Chapter 4, comprising Sections 15870 to 15895.5.)
