# WIC § 4805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 10. Judicial Review

**Citation:** WIC § 4805

---

## Text

Objections to proposed transfers between state hospitals shall be resolved pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 4700).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 990, Sec. 4.)
