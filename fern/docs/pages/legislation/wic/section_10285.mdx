# WIC § 10285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Reimbursement Rates

**Citation:** WIC § 10285

---

## Text

The department shall adopt rules, regulations, and guidelines to facilitate the funding and reimbursement procedures required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
