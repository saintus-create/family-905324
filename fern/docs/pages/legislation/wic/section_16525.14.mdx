# WIC § 16525.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.3. Services for Children Who Are Exposed to Alcohol or Drugs or Who Are HIV Positive > Article: 2. Options for Recovery Program

**Citation:** WIC § 16525.14

---

## Text

When a child is identified by a physician, medical team, county social worker, or placement source as a child who may be eligible for services under this chapter, the county shall determine if the child is eligible for placement pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 296, Sec. 2. Effective August 2, 1993.)
