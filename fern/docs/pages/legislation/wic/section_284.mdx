# WIC § 284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 5. Probation Officers

**Citation:** WIC § 284

---

## Text

All probation officers shall make such special and periodic reports to the Youth Authority as the authority may require and upon forms furnished by the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1068.)
