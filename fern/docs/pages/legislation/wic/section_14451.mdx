# WIC § 14451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 5. Standards for Prepaid Health Plans

**Citation:** WIC § 14451

---

## Text

Services under a prepaid health plan contract shall be provided in accordance with the requirements of the Knox-Keene Health Care Service Plan Act of 1975.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 442, Sec. 39. (SB 1465) Effective September 18, 2014.)
