# WIC § 10286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Reimbursement Rates

**Citation:** WIC § 10286

---

## Text

The department shall support the coordination of resources available to state and local agencies serving children, youth, and their families.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
