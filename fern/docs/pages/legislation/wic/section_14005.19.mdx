# WIC § 14005.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14005.19

---

## Text

The receipt of respite care, as defined in Section 1418.1 of the Health and Safety Code, shall not affect the eligibility of any individual with respect to benefits under this chapter, except as subject to the limitations of subdivision (c) of Section 14124.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 339, Sec. 3. (SB 1354) Effective January 1, 2025.)
