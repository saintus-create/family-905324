# WIC § 11051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Applications

**Citation:** WIC § 11051

---

## Text

The department shall include questions that enable an applicant to disclose a disability, the need for accommodation due to disability, and any experience of domestic violence, in any amendment or revision adopted on or after January 1, 2019, to the standard form for initial applications described in Section 11050.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 485, Sec. 2. (AB 2030) Effective January 1, 2019.)
