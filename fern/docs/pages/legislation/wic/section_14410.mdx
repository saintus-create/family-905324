# WIC § 14410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 4. Enrollment and Disenrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 14410

---

## Text

No prepaid health plan or marketing representative shall adopt or utilize any procedure to identify prospective enrollees with medical or psychiatric problems in order to exclude them from enrollment in the prepaid health plan, other than medical conditions specifically excluded from coverage by the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 983.)
