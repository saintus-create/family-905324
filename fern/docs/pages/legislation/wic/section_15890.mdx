# WIC § 15890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 6. Plan Rates and Compensation from the Fund

**Citation:** WIC § 15890

---

## Text

Upon enrollment as a subscriber in the program, the subscriber shall be responsible for payment of the subscriber contribution. Termination of coverage by a participating health plan for nonpayment of the subscriber contribution shall be governed by the same laws and regulations by which the participating health plan is regulated as to all its subscribers and enrollees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15872.5.)
