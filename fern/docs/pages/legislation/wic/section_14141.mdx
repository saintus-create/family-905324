# WIC § 14141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.4. Dialysis, Parenteral Hyperalimentation, and Related Services

**Citation:** WIC § 14141

---

## Text

Net worth liability shall be determined as of the time of the initial dialysis or parenteral hyperalimentation treatment and shall be reevaluated each year by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1240, Sec. 2.)
