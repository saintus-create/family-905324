# WIC § 14148.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.7. Perinatal Services Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14148.6

---

## Text

The department shall engage in outreach activities in order to enhance participation in and access to perinatal services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1446, Sec. 4. Effective October 2, 1989.)
