# WIC § 5887.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Part: 4.1. Full-Service Partnership > Chapter: 3. Mental Health Student Services Act

**Citation:** WIC § 5887.1

---

## Text

This part shall become operative on July 1, 2026, if amendments to the Mental Health Services Act are approved by the voters at the March 5, 2024, statewide primary election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 790, Sec. 86. (SB 326) Effective April 17, 2024. Approved in Proposition 1 at the March 5, 2024, election. Operative July 1, 2026, by its own provisions.)
