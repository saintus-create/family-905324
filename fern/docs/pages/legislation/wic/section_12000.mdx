# WIC § 12000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 12000

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as The Burton-Moscone-Bagley Citizens’ Income Security Act for Aged, Blind and Disabled Californians.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
