# WIC § 634.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 15. Wards—Temporary Custody and Detention

**Citation:** WIC § 634.6

---

## Text

Any counsel upon entering an appearance on behalf of a minor shall continue to represent that minor unless relieved by the court upon the substitution of other counsel or for cause.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 205.)
