# WIC § 17609.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. State and Local Fund Allocations > Article: 10. Expenditure Limitations and Reports

**Citation:** WIC § 17609.01

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 17600.20, funds deposited in the health account may be expended only for public health and indigent health care services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 611, Sec. 108. Effective October 7, 1991.)
