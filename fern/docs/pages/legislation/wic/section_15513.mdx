# WIC § 15513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15513

---

## Text

The county welfare department shall contract with the local health department to determine the need of eligible persons for the nutrients in supplemental food and the local health department shall issue prescriptions for such food in specified amounts and varieties judged necessary for such persons health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1195.)
