# WIC § 9175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 2. California Department of Aging > Article: 6. Support for Older and Aging Immigrants Regardless of Immigration Status

**Citation:** WIC § 9175

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 364, Sec. 2. (AB 450) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6, commencing with Section 9170.)
