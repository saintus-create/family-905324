# WIC § 11401.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 5. Aid to Families With Dependent Children—Foster Care

**Citation:** WIC § 11401.4

---

## Text

A child living with his or her parent who is a minor or, on and after January 1, 2012, a nonminor dependent and a recipient of AFDC-FC benefits shall be deemed a child with respect to whom AFDC-FC payments are made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 559, Sec. 42. (AB 12) Effective January 1, 2011.)
