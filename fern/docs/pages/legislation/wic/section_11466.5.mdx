# WIC § 11466.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11466.5

---

## Text

The department shall collect cost data and monitor the cost of providing care and supervision, and social work services, to AFDC-FC recipients. These data shall include, but not be limited to, the costs incurred for employee wages and benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 773, Sec. 102. (AB 403) Effective January 1, 2016.)
