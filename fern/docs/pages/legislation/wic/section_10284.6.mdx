# WIC § 10284.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 11. Reimbursement Rates

**Citation:** WIC § 10284.6

---

## Text

The department and the State Controller shall establish the necessary plans to advance child care funds to contracting agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
