# WIC § 5270.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 4.7. Additional Intensive Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5270.40

---

## Text

Any individual who is knowingly and willfully responsible for detaining a person for more than 30 days in violation of the provisions of Section 5270.35 is liable to that person in civil damages.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1517, Sec. 10.)
