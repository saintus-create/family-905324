# WIC § 6003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. ADMISSIONS AND JUDICIAL COMMITMENTS > Chapter: 1. Voluntary Admissions to Mental Hospitals and Institutions

**Citation:** WIC § 6003

---

## Text

As used in this article, “county psychiatric hospital” means the hospital, ward, or facility provided by the county pursuant to the provisions of Section 7100.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
