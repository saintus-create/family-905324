# WIC § 14020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14020

---

## Text

All sections of this chapter shall remain in operation during such times as grants-in-aid are provided or made available to the state on the basis of a state plan approved by the federal government for medical assistance pursuant to provisions of the Federal Social Security Act, as amended.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 21.)
