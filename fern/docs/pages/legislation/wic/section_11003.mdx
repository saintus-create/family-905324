# WIC § 11003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11003

---

## Text

If the United States Department of Health, Education, and Welfare issues a formal ruling that any section of this code relating to public assistance cannot be given effect without causing this state’s plan to be out of conformity with federal requirements, the section shall become inoperative to the extent that it is not in conformity with federal requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
