# WIC § 971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 28. Adjustment Schools

**Citation:** WIC § 971

---

## Text

The governing board shall determine the number, title, duties, and terms of office of all other officers and employees and shall fix their salaries, and that of the superintendent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 534.)
