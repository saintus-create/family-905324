# WIC § 16995.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5. California Healthcare for Indigents Program > Article: 10. Eligibility and Benefit Requirements

**Citation:** WIC § 16995.1

---

## Text

Receipt of funding pursuant to this chapter shall not relieve a county of its obligation to provide indigent health care as required by Section 17000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1331, Sec. 9. Effective October 2, 1989.)
