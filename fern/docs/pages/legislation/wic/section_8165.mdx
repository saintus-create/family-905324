# WIC § 8165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 4.9. Better for Families Tax Refund

**Citation:** WIC § 8165

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 51, Sec. 6. (AB 192) Effective June 30, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 4.9, commencing with Section 8160.)
