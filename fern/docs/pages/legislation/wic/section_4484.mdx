# WIC § 4484

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. DEVELOPMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WIC § 4484

---

## Text

The primary purpose of a state hospital is the medical and nursing care of patients who are developmentally disabled. The efforts and direction of the officers and employees of each state hospital shall be directed to this end.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
