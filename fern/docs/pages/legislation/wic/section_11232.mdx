# WIC § 11232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 1.5. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 11232

## Text

“Hearing officer” means an administrative law judge designated by the director.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 46, Sec. 2. Effective April 10, 1990.)
