# WIC § 11011.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11011.1

---

## Text

For the 2018–19 fiscal year, upon order of the Director of Finance, the Controller shall transfer seven hundred million dollars ($700,000,000) from the General Fund to the Safety Net Reserve Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 29, Sec. 144. (SB 82) Effective June 27, 2019.)
