# WIC § 19469

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 5. Rehabilitation Facilities > Article: 2. Transportation and Assistive Technology Loan Guarantees

**Citation:** WIC § 19469

---

## Text

No loan in excess of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) shall be made to any eligible person pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 549, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
