# WIC § 1702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 1702

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply only to public offenses committed subsequently to the date upon which it becomes effective.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 937.)
