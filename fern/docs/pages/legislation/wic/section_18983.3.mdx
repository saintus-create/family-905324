# WIC § 18983.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.5. Child Abuse Prevention Coordinating Council Act > Article: 3. Selection and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18983.3

---

## Text

In the event that more than one council per county exists, the county board of supervisors shall develop a procedure for selecting a council for funding. More than one existing council may be funded in counties with geographically distinct population centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 603, Sec. 1. Effective September 21, 1989.)
