# WIC § 5653.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. The County Performance Contract

**Citation:** WIC § 5653.1

---

## Text

In conducting evaluation, planning, and research activities, counties may contract with public or private agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 125. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012.)
