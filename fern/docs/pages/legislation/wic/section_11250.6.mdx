# WIC § 11250.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2. Eligibility for Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 11250.6

---

## Text

Except where inconsistent with federal laws, the income of any person under a contract of employment on an annual basis but who works and receives income from such contract in fewer than 12 months, but more than eight months, shall be prorated over the period of the contract for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 3, Sec. 14. Effective February 17, 1982.)
