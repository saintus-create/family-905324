# WIC § 18983.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.5. Child Abuse Prevention Coordinating Council Act > Article: 3. Selection and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18983.4

---

## Text

For counties without existing councils the county board of supervisors shall make every effort to facilitate the formation and funding of a council in that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 603, Sec. 1. Effective September 21, 1989.)
