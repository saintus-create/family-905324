# WIC § 4552.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 2. State Council On Developmental Disabilities > Article: 7. State Council Costs and Support Services

**Citation:** WIC § 4552.5

---

## Text

The state council may request information, records, and documents from any other agency of state government, except for confidential patient records. These agencies shall comply with the reasonable requests of the state council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 676, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2003.)
