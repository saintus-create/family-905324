# WIC § 4116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 4116

---

## Text

The department may establish and supervise under its rules and regulations training schools or courses for employees of the department or of state institutions under its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
