# WIC § 14192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 6. Sterilizations

**Citation:** WIC § 14192

---

## Text

Willful failure on the part of a health facility to comply with the provisions of the regulations of the department under this article shall constitute cause for suspension as a provider of services under Medi-Cal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 220.)
