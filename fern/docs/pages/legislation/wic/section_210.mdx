# WIC § 210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 210

---

## Text

The Board of Corrections shall adopt minimum standards for the operation and maintenance of juvenile halls for the confinement of minors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 694, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
