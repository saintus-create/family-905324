# WIC § 14702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8.9. Transition of Community-Based Medi-Cal Mental Health

**Citation:** WIC § 14702

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “Department” means the State Department of Health Care Services.

(b) “Director” means the Director of Health Care Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 34, Sec. 245. (SB 1009) Effective June 27, 2012. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 14721.)
