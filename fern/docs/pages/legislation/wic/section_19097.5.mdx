# WIC § 19097.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 3. Division of Services for the Blind and Visually Impaired and the Deaf and Hard of Hearing

**Citation:** WIC § 19097.5

---

## Text

The department shall report annually in the fourth quarter of each calendar year to the Blind Advisory Committee on the amount of state and federal funds allocated to direct services governed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1102, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
