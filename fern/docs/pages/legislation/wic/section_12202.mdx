# WIC § 12202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 5. Payment of Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 12202

---

## Text

The policy shall be followed of granting aid to the recipient in his own home or in some other suitable home of his own choosing in preference to placing him in an institution.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
