# WIC § 4523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 2. State Council On Developmental Disabilities > Article: 1. Composition and Appointments

**Citation:** WIC § 4523

---

## Text

Persons appointed to membership on the state council shall have demonstrated interest and leadership in human service activities, including interest in Californians who have developmental disabilities, their families, services, and supports.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 676, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2003.)
