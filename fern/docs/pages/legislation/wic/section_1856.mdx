# WIC § 1856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 9. Youth Correctional Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 1856

---

## Text

When in the opinion of the chief probation officer an offender appears to be unamenable to the program of the youth correctional center, he shall be returned to the committing court for further disposition. The court shall then make an alternative disposition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1193.)
