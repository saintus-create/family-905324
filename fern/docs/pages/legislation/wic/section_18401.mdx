# WIC § 18401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Uniform Transfer of Dependents Act

**Citation:** WIC § 18401

---

## Text

This chapter shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the laws of those states which enact it.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
