# WIC § 14105.332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 14105.332

---

## Text

State and federal rebates that are owed to the state for drugs dispensed to Medi-Cal beneficiaries shall not be reduced to the state if a manufacturer reports, to the federal Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services or the department, a revised drug product’s average manufacturer price or best price as these terms are defined pursuant to Section 1927 of the federal Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 1396r-8) for any calendar quarter in which the rebate was due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 3, Sec. 96. (AB 97) Effective March 24, 2011.)
