# WIC § 11008.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11008.1

---

## Text

To the extent permitted by federal law, income, including but not limited to seven dollars and fifty cents ($7.50) of any income, received by a recipient of aid under Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 12000), Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 12500), or Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 13500) of this part shall not be considered income or resources of the recipient and shall not be deducted from the amount of aid to which the recipient would otherwise be entitled.

This section shall be known and may be cited as the Social Security Pass-On.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1520.)
