# WIC § 15834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Medi-Cal Access Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15834

---

## Text

A person shall not be eligible for covered services under the program if those services are covered through private health care coverage arrangements at the time of eligibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 23, Sec. 68. (AB 82) Effective June 27, 2013.)
