# WIC § 14087.9625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.81. Managed Health Care System for Los Angeles County

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.9625

---

## Text

(a) Members of the governing body of the commission shall serve four-year terms.

(b) Individuals shall be limited to serving on the governing body for two consecutive four-year terms or a maximum of 10 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 454, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
