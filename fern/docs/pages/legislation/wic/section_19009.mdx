# WIC § 19009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19009

---

## Text

No period of residence in this state is required for participation in any program administered by the department, except as required by federal law or regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1107.)
