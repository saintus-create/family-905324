# WIC § 7293

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Hospitals for the Mentally Disordered > Article: 4. Property and Support of Patients

**Citation:** WIC § 7293

---

## Text

The State Department of State Hospitals shall present to the county, not more frequently than monthly, a claim for the amount due the state under Section 7291 which the county shall process and pay pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 29700) of Division 3 of Title 3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 24, Sec. 183. (AB 1470) Effective June 27, 2012.)
