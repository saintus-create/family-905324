# WIC § 1000.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 3. Institutions for Delinquents > Article: 1. Establishment and General Government

**Citation:** WIC § 1000.5

---

## Text

Where in any law of this State the name “Whittier State School” appears it shall hereafter be understood to mean and shall be construed to refer to Fred C. Nelles School for Boys.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 155.5 by Stats. 1943, Ch. 481.)
