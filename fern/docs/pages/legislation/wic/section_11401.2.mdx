# WIC § 11401.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 5. Aid to Families With Dependent Children—Foster Care

**Citation:** WIC § 11401.2

---

## Text

AFDC-FC shall be paid to an otherwise eligible child in a voluntary placement in a demonstration county for a period not to exceed six months, with a maximum extension of an additional six months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1166, Sec. 13.)
