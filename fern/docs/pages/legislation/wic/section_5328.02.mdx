# WIC § 5328.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 7. Legal and Civil Rights of Persons Involuntarily Detained

**Citation:** WIC § 5328.02

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 5328, all information and records made confidential under the first paragraph of Section 5328 shall also be disclosed to the Youth Authority and Adult Correctional Agency or any component thereof, as necessary to the administration of justice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1117, Sec. 26.)
