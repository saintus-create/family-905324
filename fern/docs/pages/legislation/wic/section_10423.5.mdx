# WIC § 10423.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 25. Building a Better Early Care and Education System

**Citation:** WIC § 10423.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not alter the rights of families to select, direct, and terminate the services of family child care providers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
