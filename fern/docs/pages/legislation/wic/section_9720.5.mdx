# WIC § 9720.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 11. State Ombudsman > Article: 3. Investigation and Resolution of Complaints

**Citation:** WIC § 9720.5

---

## Text

The office shall give priority to investigations and complaint resolutions in 24-hour long-term care facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
