# WIC § 14407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 4. Enrollment and Disenrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 14407

---

## Text

Enrollment in a prepaid health plan shall be voluntary and a prepaid health plan shall not use false advertising or false statements to induce enrollment. No solicitation of enrollees shall include the granting or offering of any monetary or other valuable consideration for enrollment.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1036.)
