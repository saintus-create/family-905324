# WIC § 15512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15512

---

## Text

Application for supplemental food shall be made to the county welfare agency who shall determine program eligibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1195.)
