# WIC § 5511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Mental Health Advocacy > Article: 2. Patients’ Rights Program

**Citation:** WIC § 5511

---

## Text

The Director of State Hospitals or the executive director of each state hospital may contract with independent persons or agencies to perform patients’ rights advocacy services in state hospitals.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 144, Sec. 98. (AB 1847) Effective January 1, 2015.)
