# WIC § 19802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 9. Independent Living Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 19802

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, an “individual with a disability” is as defined pursuant to the federal Rehabilitation Act of 1973 (Section 705 of Title 29 of the United States Code), as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 190, Sec. 10. (AB 2877) Effective January 1, 2017.)
