# WIC § 17850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Part: 5.8. PUBLIC BENEFITS > Chapter: 4. Foster Care

**Citation:** WIC § 17850

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this part to affirm the ability of counties, cities, and hospital districts to provide health care and other services to all residents, if any of these entities has decided to do so at its own discretion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 801, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
