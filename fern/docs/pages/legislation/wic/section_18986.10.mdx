# WIC § 18986.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.8. Interagency Children’s Services Act > Article: 2. County Interagency Collaboration

**Citation:** WIC § 18986.10

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county or city and county may establish an interagency children’s services coordination council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1303, Sec. 1.)
