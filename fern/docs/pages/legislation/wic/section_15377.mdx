# WIC § 15377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 4. Eligibility and Enrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 15377

---

## Text

No copayment charges shall be imposed upon Medi-Cal enrollees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 992.)
