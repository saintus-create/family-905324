# WIC § 14193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 6. Sterilizations

**Citation:** WIC § 14193

---

## Text

The department shall transmit any data acquired by it regarding willful failure of a physician and surgeon to comply with the regulations of the department under this article to the Medical Board of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 886, Sec. 107.)
