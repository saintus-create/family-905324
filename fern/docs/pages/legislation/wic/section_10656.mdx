# WIC § 10656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Rehabilitation and Employment Coordination

**Citation:** WIC § 10656

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be implemented to the full extent of funds available for such purposes.
