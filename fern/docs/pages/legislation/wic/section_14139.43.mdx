# WIC § 14139.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.3. Long-Term Care Integration Pilot Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14139.43

---

## Text

This article shall not preclude a long-term care services agency from entering into additional agreements, separate from the pilot project, to serve additional individuals or populations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
