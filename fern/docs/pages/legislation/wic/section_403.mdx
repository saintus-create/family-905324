# WIC § 403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 13.5. Foster Care of Children

**Citation:** WIC § 403

---

## Text

An advocate shall not be assigned to assist any minor in foster care placement who has been appointed a surrogate parent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 811, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
