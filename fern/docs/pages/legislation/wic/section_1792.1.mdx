# WIC § 1792.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 5.5. Crime and Delinquency Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 1792.1

---

## Text

The director shall make annual allocations from funds made available to him for such purposes for administrative expenses to county delinquency prevention commissions established pursuant to Sections 233 and 235 not to exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000) per year for each commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 380.)
