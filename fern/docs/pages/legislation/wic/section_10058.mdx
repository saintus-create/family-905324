# WIC § 10058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 10058

---

## Text

“County department” means the county agency designated by the board of supervisors, or by such other authority as may be provided in the county charter, as responsible for the administration of public social services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
