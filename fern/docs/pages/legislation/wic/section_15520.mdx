# WIC § 15520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 10. Supplemental Food Program > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 15520

## Text

The department shall pay the nonfederal share of the administration of program pursuant to this chapter.
