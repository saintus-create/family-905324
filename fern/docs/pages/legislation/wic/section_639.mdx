# WIC § 639

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 15. Wards—Temporary Custody and Detention

**Citation:** WIC § 639

---

## Text

Upon any hearing or rehearing under the provisions of this article, the court may order such minor or any parent or guardian of such minor who is present in court to again appear before the court or the probation officer or the county financial evaluation officer at a time and place specified in said order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1485, Sec. 11.)
