# WIC § 15850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. County Children’s Health Initiative Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15850

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the County Children’s Health Initiative Program (CCHIP).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 266, Sec. 9. (AB 614) Effective January 1, 2024.)
