# WIC § 4865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 13. Habilitation Services For Persons With Developmental Disabilities

**Citation:** WIC § 4865

---

## Text

At the request of the Department of Rehabilitation, a work activity or supported employment program, or both, shall release state licensing reports and individual special incident reports, as required by law or regulations, in instances of suspected abuse.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 80, Sec. 46. (SB 163) Effective July 13, 2026.)
