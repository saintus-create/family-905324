# WIC § 15887.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. California Major Risk Medical Insurance Program > Article: 5. Subscriber Eligibility and Enrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 15887.5

---

## Text

A transfer of enrollment from one participating health plan to another may be made by a subscriber at times and under conditions as may be prescribed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 90. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15872.5.)
