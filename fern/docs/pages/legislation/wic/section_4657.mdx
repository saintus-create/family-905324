# WIC § 4657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 2. Regional Center Responsibilities

**Citation:** WIC § 4657

---

## Text

The State Department of Developmental Services shall, through the regional center contract, require that the following information is collected by each regional center for each new case and is also collected at each review of all regional center clients in out-of-home placement.

Information shall include:

(a) The birthday of the parents of the client.

(b) The disability status of the parents of the client.

(c) Whether the parents of the client are deceased or not.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 149, Sec. 1. (AB 445) Effective January 1, 2022.)
