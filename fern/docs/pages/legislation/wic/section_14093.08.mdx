# WIC § 14093.08

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.97. Managed Care Plan Contracts

**Citation:** WIC § 14093.08

---

## Text

Sections 1371 and 1371.35 of the Health and Safety Code apply to Medi-Cal managed care plan contracts entered into with the State Department of Health Care Services pursuant to this chapter or Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 14200).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 763, Sec. 10. (AB 3275) Effective January 1, 2025.)
