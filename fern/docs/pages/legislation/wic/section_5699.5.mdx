# WIC § 5699.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2.7. Case Management for Children With Serious Emotional Disturbance

**Citation:** WIC § 5699.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the use of state funds to provide services under this chapter or to enforce the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 5678.6 by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 141. Effective June 30, 1991.)
