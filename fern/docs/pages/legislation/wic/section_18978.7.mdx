# WIC § 18978.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12. The Maxine Waters Child Abuse Prevention Training Act of 1984 > Article: 4. The Office of Child Abuse Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 18978.7

---

## Text

The director shall be authorized to the extent necessary to adopt regulations, orders, or standards of general application to implement, interpret, or make specific the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1638, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
