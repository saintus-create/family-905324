# WIC § 829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 22. Wards and Dependent Children—Records

**Citation:** WIC § 829

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Board of Prison Terms, in order to evaluate the suitability for release of a person before the board, shall be entitled to review juvenile court records which have not been sealed, concerning the person before the board, if those records relate to a case in which the person was found to have committed an offense which brought the person within the jurisdiction of the juvenile court pursuant to Section 602.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 241, Sec. 1.)
