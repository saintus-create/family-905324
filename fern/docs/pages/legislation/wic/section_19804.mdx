# WIC § 19804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 9. Independent Living Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 19804

---

## Text

An independent living center shall not operate in conjunction with or be affiliated with a residential living center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 191.)
