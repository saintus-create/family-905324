# WIC § 11218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 11218

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 43.3 of the Civil Code, an applicant or recipient of aid under this chapter is entitled to breastfeed her child in any public area, or area where the mother and the child are authorized to be present, in a county welfare department or other county office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 563, Sec. 2. (SB 252) Effective January 1, 2014.)
