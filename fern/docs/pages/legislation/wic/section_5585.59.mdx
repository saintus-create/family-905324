# WIC § 5585.59

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Civil Commitment of Minors

**Citation:** WIC § 5585.59

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, legally emancipated minors requiring involuntary treatment shall be considered adults and this part shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1202, Sec. 2.)
