# WIC § 19463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 5. Rehabilitation Facilities > Article: 2. Transportation and Assistive Technology Loan Guarantees

**Citation:** WIC § 19463

---

## Text

State guaranteed loans made pursuant to this article shall be made without regard to race, religion, creed, or sex.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 810, Sec. 1.)
