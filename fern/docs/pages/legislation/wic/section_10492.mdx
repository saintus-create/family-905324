# WIC § 10492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 34. Whole Child Community Equity

**Citation:** WIC § 10492

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the End Racial and Economic Inequities in Childcare in California Initiative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 699, Sec. 2. (AB 2832) Effective January 1, 2023.)
