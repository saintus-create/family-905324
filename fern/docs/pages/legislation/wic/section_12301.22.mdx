# WIC § 12301.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 7. In-Home Supportive Services

**Citation:** WIC § 12301.22

---

## Text

On or before December 31, 2011, the department, in consultation with county welfare departments and other stakeholders, shall develop a process to ensure that a provider of services under this article receives a list specifying the approved duties to be performed for each recipient under the provider’s care and a complete list of supportive service tasks available under the IHSS program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 17, Sec. 2. (AB 19 4x) Effective October 23, 2009.)
