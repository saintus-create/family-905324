# WIC § 148.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF WELFARE AND INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1.8. Acquisition and Disposition of Salvageable Personal Property for Charitable Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 148.9

---

## Text

The enactment of this chapter shall in no way limit or infringe upon the powers of counties and cities to impose additional requirements for the privilege of soliciting and selling salvageable personal property within their jurisdictions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1410.)
