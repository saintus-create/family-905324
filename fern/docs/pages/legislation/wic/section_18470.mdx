# WIC § 18470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18470

---

## Text

The direct administration of this chapter shall be carried out by the boards of supervisors of the several counties, directly or through their authorized agents. The administration shall be supervised by the department.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
