# WIC § 1852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 9. Youth Correctional Centers

**Citation:** WIC § 1852

---

## Text

Complete operation and authority for administration of the youth correctional center shall be vested in the county. The board of supervisors shall place responsibility for internal management with the chief probation officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1193.)
