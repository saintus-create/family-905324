# WIC § 1716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 2.5. Board of Juvenile Hearings

**Citation:** WIC § 1716

---

## Text

Commencing July 1, 2016, any reference to the Youth Authority Board refers to the Board of Juvenile Hearings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 33, Sec. 40. (SB 843) Effective June 27, 2016.)
