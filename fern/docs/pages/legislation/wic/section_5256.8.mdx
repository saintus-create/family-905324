# WIC § 5256.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 4. Certification for Intensive Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5256.8

---

## Text

The requirement that there is a certification review hearing in accordance with this article shall apply only to persons certified for intensive treatment on or after January 1, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1598, Sec. 4.)
