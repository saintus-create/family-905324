# WIC § 5352.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Conservatorship for Persons with a Grave Disability

**Citation:** WIC § 5352.4

---

## Text

If a conservatee appeals the court’s decision to establish conservatorship, the conservatorship shall continue unless execution of judgment is stayed by the appellate court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 574.)
