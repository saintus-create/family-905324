# WIC § 10355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 19. Child Care Subsidy Plan for the City and County of San Francisco

**Citation:** WIC § 10355

---

## Text

A participating contractor shall receive any increase or decrease in funding that the contractor would have received if the contractor had not participated in the local subsidy plan established by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
