# WIC § 15400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 5. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15400

---

## Text

The supportive care organization shall make a concerted effort to employ persons over the age of 40 when such qualified personnel are available.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 992.)
