# WIC § 11008.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policies and Purposes

**Citation:** WIC § 11008.11

---

## Text

To the extent permitted by federal law, any stipends, meals, transportation, or other income received by a senior companion pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 9520) of Division 8.5 shall not be considered as income or resources of the recipient and shall not be deducted from the amount of any public assistance or aid to which the recipient would otherwise be entitled under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1121.)
