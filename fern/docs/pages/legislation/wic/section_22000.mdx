# WIC § 22000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA PARTNERSHIP FOR LONG-TERM CARE

**Citation:** WIC § 22000

---

## Text

The California Partnership for Long-Term Care Program is hereby established.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 802, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
