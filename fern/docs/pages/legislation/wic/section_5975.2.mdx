# WIC § 5975.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Process

**Citation:** WIC § 5975.2

---

## Text

CARE Act proceedings may be conducted by a superior court judge or by a court-appointed commissioner or other subordinate judicial officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 283, Sec. 9. (SB 35) Effective September 30, 2023. Section conditionally operative as provided in Section 5970.5.)
