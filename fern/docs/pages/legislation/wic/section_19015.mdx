# WIC § 19015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 19015

---

## Text

The department may enter into reciprocal agreements with other states to provide for the services authorized by this division to residents of the state concerned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1107.)
