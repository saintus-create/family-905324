# WIC § 16541.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.5. Child Welfare Leadership and Performance Accountability

**Citation:** WIC § 16541.5

---

## Text

The council shall meet no less frequently than each quarter of the state fiscal year and at the call of the cochairs, at a time and location convenient to the public as it may deem appropriate. All meetings of the council shall be open to the public. Members shall serve without compensation, with the exception of foster youth members, who shall be entitled to reimbursement for all actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 130, Sec. 252. Effective January 1, 2008.)
