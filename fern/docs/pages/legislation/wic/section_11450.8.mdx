# WIC § 11450.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11450.8

---

## Text

No payment of aid pursuant to Section 11450 shall be made to a family for a month in which the amount the family would receive is less than ten dollars ($10). The family shall be considered in receipt of aid for all other purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 3, Sec. 28. Effective February 17, 1982.)
