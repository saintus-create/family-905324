# WIC § 1794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. YOUTHS > Chapter: 1. The Youth Authority > Article: 5.5. Crime and Delinquency Prevention

**Citation:** WIC § 1794

---

## Text

Application for funds under Sections 1792, 1792.1, and 1792.2 shall be made to the Director of the Youth Authority in the manner and form prescribed by the department. The department shall prescribe the amounts, time, and manner of payments of assistance if granted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1401.)
