# WIC § 15204.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9. Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Appropriations for Public Assistance

**Citation:** WIC § 15204.9

---

## Text

(a) Prior to the 2011–12 fiscal year, the state shall pay 70 percent of the nonfederal administrative costs of administering the Aid to Families with Dependent Children Foster Care program under Article 5 (commencing with Section 11400) of Chapter 2.

(b) Notwithstanding subdivision (a), beginning in the 2011–12 fiscal year, and for each fiscal year thereafter, funding and expenditures for programs and activities under this section shall be in accordance with the requirements provided in Sections 30025 and 30026.5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 101. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
