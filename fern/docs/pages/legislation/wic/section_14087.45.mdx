# WIC § 14087.45

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 2.7. Contracts for Medi-Cal Services and Case Management

**Citation:** WIC § 14087.45

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not become operative until July 1, 1983, the date upon which the California Medical Assistance Commission, created pursuant to Assembly Bill 3480, assumes its full responsibilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 328, Sec. 18. Effective June 30, 1982. Section operative July 1, 1983, by its own provisions.)
