# WIC § 13007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 5.1. Social Services

**Citation:** WIC § 13007

---

## Text

Each county shall report to the State Department of Social Services all necessary information in order to comply with federal and state requirements for the social services block grant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 3, Sec. 40. Effective February 17, 1982.)
