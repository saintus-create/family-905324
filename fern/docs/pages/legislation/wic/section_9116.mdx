# WIC § 9116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 2. California Department of Aging > Article: 2. Senior Housing Information and Support Center

**Citation:** WIC § 9116

---

## Text

The Director of the California Department of Aging shall appoint all necessary staff to carry out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 797, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
