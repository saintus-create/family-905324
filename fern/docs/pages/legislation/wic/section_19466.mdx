# WIC § 19466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 5. Rehabilitation Facilities > Article: 2. Transportation and Assistive Technology Loan Guarantees

**Citation:** WIC § 19466

---

## Text

The State Treasurer shall invest, pursuant to statute, any surplus money in the Rehabilitation Revolving Loan Guarantee Fund. The interest or other accretions as a result of the investment of such money shall accrue to the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 810, Sec. 1.)
