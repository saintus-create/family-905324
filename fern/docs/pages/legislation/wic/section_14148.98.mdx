# WIC § 14148.98

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4.8. Perinatal Outreach, Coordination, and Expansion Services

**Citation:** WIC § 14148.98

---

## Text

No funds from the Health Education Account in the Cigarette and Tobacco Products Surtax Fund may be used in a manner that violates paragraph (1) of subdivision (b) of Section 30122 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1170, Sec. 13. Effective October 14, 1991.)
