# WIC § 12052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Supplementary Program for Aged, Blind and Disabled > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 12052

---

## Text

“Resources” means resources as defined on October 30, 1972 in Part A of Title XVI of the Social Security Act, except as otherwise specifically provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1216.)
