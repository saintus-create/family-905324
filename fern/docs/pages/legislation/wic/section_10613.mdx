# WIC § 10613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. State Department of Social Services > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WIC § 10613

---

## Text

The functions of the department may include the administration and the supervision of the administration of public social services, except health care services and medical assistance, within this state as an agent of the federal government and acting as a service agency for the federal government in the field of social service and welfare.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
