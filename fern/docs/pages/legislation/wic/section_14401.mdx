# WIC § 14401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 4. Enrollment and Disenrollment

**Citation:** WIC § 14401

---

## Text

No Medi-Cal beneficiary shall be enrolled in a prepaid health plan prior to the time a contract under this chapter is signed by the department and such prepaid health plan is approved by the appropriate state agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 983.)
