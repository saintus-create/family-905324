# WIC § 11275.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2.5. Needy Families’ Paperwork Reduction Act of 1990

**Citation:** WIC § 11275.40

---

## Text

This article applies to all applicants for, and recipients of, aid under this chapter, regardless of whether federal financial participation is available for the family.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1586, Sec. 3.)
