# WIC § 4667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 5. Regional Centers for Persons With Developmental Disabilities > Article: 3. Regional Center Board Meetings

**Citation:** WIC § 4667

---

## Text

All regional center board meetings shall be held in facilities accessible to persons with physical disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 577, Sec. 2.)
