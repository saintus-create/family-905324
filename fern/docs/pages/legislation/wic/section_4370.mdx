# WIC § 4370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** WIC § 4370

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the School-based Early Mental Health Intervention and Prevention Services for Children Act of 1991.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 757, Sec. 1. Effective October 9, 1991.)
