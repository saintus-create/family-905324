# WIC § 14133.225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 4. The Medi-Cal Benefits Program

**Citation:** WIC § 14133.225

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the department shall not provide or pay for any prescription drug or other therapy to treat erectile dysfunction for any person who is required to register pursuant to Section 290 of the Penal Code, except to the extent required under federal law. The department may require from the Department of Justice the information necessary to implement this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 469, Sec. 3. Effective October 4, 2005.)
