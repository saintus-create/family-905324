# WIC § 15405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9.5. Supportive Care Organizations > Article: 5. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 15405

---

## Text

A participant grievance procedure must be established by the project through which an aggrieved participant may submit his complaint to the project which will hear and take action on the complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 992.)
