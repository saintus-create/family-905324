# WIC § 9018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. MELLO-GRANLUND OLDER CALIFORNIANS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 9018

---

## Text

“Older individual” or “elderly” means a person 60 years of age or older, except where this provision is inconsistent with federal requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1097, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
