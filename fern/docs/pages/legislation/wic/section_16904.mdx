# WIC § 16904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** WIC § 16904

---

## Text

“Hospital services” means services provided by public and private hospitals licensed pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 1250 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1331, Sec. 9. Effective October 2, 1989.)
