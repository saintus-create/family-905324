# WIC § 16800.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Part: 4.6. LOCAL HEALTH CARE BUDGETING > Chapter: 7. Family First Prevention Services

**Citation:** WIC § 16800.5

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Services shall establish data collection and reporting requirements for counties to annually report health expenditures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 719, Sec. 5. Effective September 15, 1992.)
