# WIC § 18474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Relief Law of 1945 > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18474

---

## Text

Relief may be granted in cash or in kind, but relief may be granted in kind only after the method has been approved by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
