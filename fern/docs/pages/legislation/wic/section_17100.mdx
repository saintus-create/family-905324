# WIC § 17100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Eligibility

**Citation:** WIC § 17100

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, a person, in order to be entitled to aid, shall be a resident of the state and of the county wherein application is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
