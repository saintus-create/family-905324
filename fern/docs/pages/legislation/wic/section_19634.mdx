# WIC § 19634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 6. Blind Persons > Article: 5. Business Enterprises for the Blind

**Citation:** WIC § 19634

---

## Text

Blind persons who are authorized to operate vending facilities under this article may keep their guide dogs with them on the property while operating the vending facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1131.)
