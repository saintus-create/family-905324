# WIC § 25000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. HEALTH CARE COVERAGE

**Citation:** WIC § 25000

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to create a process by which the options for achieving universal health care coverage can be thoroughly examined.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 990, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
