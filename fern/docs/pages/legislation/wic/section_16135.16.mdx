# WIC § 16135.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.3. Adoption of Alcohol- and Drug-Exposed and HIV Positive Children

**Citation:** WIC § 16135.16

---

## Text

The requirements of this section may be met by the implementation of the TIES for Adoption program as defined in Subdivision (b) of Section 16135.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1014, Sec. 2) by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 118. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
