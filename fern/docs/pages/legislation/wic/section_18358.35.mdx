# WIC § 18358.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6.2. Intensive Foster Care Programs

**Citation:** WIC § 18358.35

---

## Text

Foster family agencies implementing intensive foster care programs shall under no circumstances have any interest in the properties occupied by the certified foster parents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1250, Sec. 3. Conditionally inoperative on or after July 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 18358.38. Repealed as of January 1 following the inoperative date.)
