# WIC § 10421.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 25. Building a Better Early Care and Education System

**Citation:** WIC § 10421.5

---

## Text

The state action antitrust exemption to the application of federal and state antitrust laws shall apply to the activities of family child care providers and their representatives authorized under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 116, Sec. 260. (AB 131) Effective July 23, 2021.)
