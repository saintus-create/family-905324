# WIC § 5697.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Program Initiatives > Article: 6. Regional Facilities for Wards with Serious Emotional Disturbance

**Citation:** WIC § 5697.5

---

## Text

The Director of the Youth Authority, in conjunction with the Director of Mental Health, shall adopt rules and regulations to establish, monitor, and enforce minimum standards for regional facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 170. Effective June 30, 1991.)
