# WIC § 14029.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 7. Basic Health Care > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 14029.8

---

## Text

Section 124260 of the Health and Safety Code shall not apply to the receipt of benefits under the Medi-Cal program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 503, Sec. 2. (SB 543) Effective January 1, 2011.)
