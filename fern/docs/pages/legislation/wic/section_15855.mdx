# WIC § 15855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. County Children’s Health Initiative Program

**Citation:** WIC § 15855

---

## Text

The department shall review each funding proposal submitted by an applicant in accordance with the criteria described in Section 15854 and based on that criteria, approve or reject the proposal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 31, Sec. 89. (SB 857) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, pursuant to Section 15864.)
