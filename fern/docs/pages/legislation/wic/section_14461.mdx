# WIC § 14461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. Prepaid Plans > Article: 5. Standards for Prepaid Health Plans

**Citation:** WIC § 14461

---

## Text

Upon request by the department, each prepaid health plan shall submit to the department a copy of any financial report submitted to any other public or private organization, if such report differs in content or format from any financial report already submitted to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1036.)
