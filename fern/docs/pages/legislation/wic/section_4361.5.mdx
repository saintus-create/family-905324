# WIC § 4361.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MENTAL HEALTH > Chapter: 6.7. State Department of State Hospitals: Contracting Facilities

**Citation:** WIC § 4361.5

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “department” means the State Department of State Hospitals.

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 353. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
