# WIC § 981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 28. Adjustment Schools

**Citation:** WIC § 981

---

## Text

There shall be organized a course of study, corresponding as far as practicable with the course of study in the public schools of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1452, Sec. 534.)
