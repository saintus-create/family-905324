# WIC § 22002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA PARTNERSHIP FOR LONG-TERM CARE

**Citation:** WIC § 22002

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services shall seek any federal waivers and approvals necessary to accomplish the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 487, Sec. 3. (SB 1384) Effective January 1, 2017.)
