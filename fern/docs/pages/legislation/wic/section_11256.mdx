# WIC § 11256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 2. Eligibility for Aid

**Citation:** WIC § 11256

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter only, the ownership of stock in a water company not appurtenant to the land shall be considered real property to the extent of and in the amount necessary to obtain water for agricultural purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
