# WIC § 19076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STATE DEPARTMENT OF REHABILITATION > Chapter: 2. Vocational Rehabilitation Federal Fund

**Citation:** WIC § 19076

---

## Text

The State Treasurer, as required by the federal act relating to vocational rehabilitation referred to in Section 19012 shall receive and provide for the proper custody of all funds apportioned to the state under that act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1107.)
