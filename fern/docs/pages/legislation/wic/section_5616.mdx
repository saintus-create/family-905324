# WIC § 5616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WIC § 5616

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall prevent any city or combination of cities from owning, financing, and operating a mental health program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 89, Sec. 104. Effective June 30, 1991.)
