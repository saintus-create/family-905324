# WIC § 18983.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12.5. Child Abuse Prevention Coordinating Council Act > Article: 3. Selection and Administration

**Citation:** WIC § 18983.8

---

## Text

Councils receiving funding under this chapter shall provide a local cash or in-kind match of 3313 percent. For councils unable to raise the full match for the maximum allocation, a partial grant shall be provided in the amount of three grant dollars to each match dollar.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 603, Sec. 1. Effective September 21, 1989.)
