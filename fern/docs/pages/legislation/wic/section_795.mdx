# WIC § 795

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CHILDREN > Chapter: 2. Juvenile Court Law > Article: 20.5. Deferred Entry of Judgment

**Citation:** WIC § 795

---

## Text

The county probation officer or a person designated by the county probation officer shall serve in each county as the program administrator for juveniles granted deferred entry of judgment and shall be responsible for developing, supervising, and monitoring treatment programs and otherwise overseeing the placement and supervision of minors granted probation pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added March 7, 2000, by initiative Proposition 21, Sec. 29.)
