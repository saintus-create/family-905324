# WIC § 11468.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. California Work Opportunity and Responsibility to Kids Act > Article: 6. Computation and Payment of Aid Grants

**Citation:** WIC § 11468.1

---

## Text

The administrative review procedure for rates established pursuant to Section 11468 shall include a protest within the department and a hearing conducted by a hearing officer appointed by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 732, Sec. 87. (AB 404) Effective January 1, 2018.)
