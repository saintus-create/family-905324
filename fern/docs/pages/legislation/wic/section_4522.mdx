# WIC § 4522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. SERVICES FOR THE DEVELOPMENTALLY DISABLED > Chapter: 2. State Council On Developmental Disabilities > Article: 1. Composition and Appointments

**Citation:** WIC § 4522

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prevent the reappointment or replacement of any individual presently serving on the existing state council if the reappointment or replacement is in conformity with all of the criteria established in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 676, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
