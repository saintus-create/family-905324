# WIC § 7105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MENTAL INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. County Psychiatric Hospitals

**Citation:** WIC § 7105

---

## Text

A superintendent or person in charge of the county psychiatric hospital may discharge any patient who is not a proper case for treatment therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1667.)
