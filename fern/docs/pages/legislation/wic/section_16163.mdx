# WIC § 16163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. PUBLIC SOCIAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. Foster Child Ombudsman Program

**Citation:** WIC § 16163

---

## Text

The department shall hire the necessary personnel to perform the functions of the office. Priority shall be given to former foster youth in hiring decisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 311, Sec. 66. Effective August 19, 1998.)
