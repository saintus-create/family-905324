# WIC § 5259.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. COMMUNITY MENTAL HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Involuntary Treatment > Article: 4. Certification for Intensive Treatment

**Citation:** WIC § 5259.1

---

## Text

Any individual who is knowingly and willfully responsible for detaining a person in violation of the provisions of this article is liable to that person in civil damages.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1598, Sec. 4.)
