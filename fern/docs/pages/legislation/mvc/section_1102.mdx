# MVC § 1102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Veterans’ Homes Bond Act of 2000 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** MVC § 1102

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the following terms have the following meaning:

(a) “Board” means the Department of Veterans Affairs designated in accordance with subdivision (b) of Section 1108.

(b) “Committee” means the Veterans’ Home Finance Committee created pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 1108.

(c) “Fund” means the Veterans’ Home Fund created pursuant to Section 1103.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 728, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 16 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
