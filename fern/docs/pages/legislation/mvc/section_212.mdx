# MVC § 212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 1. Membership

**Citation:** MVC § 212

---

## Text

The inactive National Guard shall consist of those organizations, officers, and enlisted persons as are authorized and prescribed by the laws of the United States and regulations issued thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 118, Sec. 4. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2019.)
