# MVC § 237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 2. Officers

**Citation:** MVC § 237

---

## Text

An officer may be dismissed from the service only by sentence of a general court-martial, which sentence is approved by the Governor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
