# MVC § 121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** MVC § 121

---

## Text

The unorganized militia consists of all persons liable to service in the militia, but not members of the National Guard, the State Guard, or the Naval Militia.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 97, Sec. 15. (AB 2193) Effective January 1, 2021.)
