# MVC § 975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 5. Veterans’ Affairs Commissions

**Citation:** MVC § 975

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may create and appoint members to a veterans’ affairs commission either as an independent agency or in conjunction with a California National Guard members’ commission or a veterans’ memorial commission, or in any combination of these commissions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 638, Sec. 1.)
