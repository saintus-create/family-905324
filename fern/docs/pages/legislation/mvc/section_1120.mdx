# MVC § 1120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 3. County Institutions

**Citation:** MVC § 1120

---

## Text

Any county may provide and maintain a home for veteran soldiers, sailors, airmen, guardians, and marines who have served the United States honorably in any of its wars.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 379, Sec. 17. (AB 1715) Effective January 1, 2023.)
