# MVC § 258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 3. Enlisted Persons

**Citation:** MVC § 258

---

## Text

In time of war or other emergency or imminent danger thereof, the Governor may detail retired enlisted persons to active duty and on conclusion of the emergency return them to the retired list.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 118, Sec. 12. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2019.)
