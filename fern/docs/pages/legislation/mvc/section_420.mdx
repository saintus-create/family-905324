# MVC § 420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 1. Funds, Arms, and Equipment

**Citation:** MVC § 420

---

## Text

No officer in charge of public property for military use shall transfer any portion thereof, either as a loan or permanently, without the authority of the Governor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
