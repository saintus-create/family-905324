# MVC § 409.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 7.5. Protections

**Citation:** MVC § 409.14

---

## Text

A service member or other person seeking to enforce rights pursuant to this chapter shall not be required to pay a filing fee or court costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 385, Sec. 11. (AB 2365) Effective January 1, 2011.)
