# MVC § 1011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Veterans’ Home of California

**Citation:** MVC § 1011

---

## Text

(a) There is in the department a Veterans’ Home of California system, as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 1010.

(b) There shall be an administrator for each home or homesite in that system, who shall be recommended by the Secretary of Veterans Affairs and appointed by the Governor, and shall be located at that home or homesite. The salary for each administrator shall be subject to the approval of the Department of Human Resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 28, Sec. 42. (SB 96) Effective June 27, 2017.)
