# MVC § 1222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 3.5. Construction and Alteration of Halls, Buildings and Meeting Places

**Citation:** MVC § 1222

---

## Text

No contract shall be let for the construction or alteration of any hall, building or meeting place unless such project can be completed and be ready for occupancy and use with the funds the district has on hand.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1067.)
