# MVC § 928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 1. Indigent Veterans

**Citation:** MVC § 928

---

## Text

The indigent and dependent surviving spouse or domestic partner, minor child, father, or mother of any indigent veteran may be granted relief by the organization out of the money available under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 129, Sec. 71. (SB 1097) Effective January 1, 2025.)
