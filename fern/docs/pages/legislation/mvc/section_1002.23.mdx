# MVC § 1002.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolutions of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 1002.23

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may include provisions requiring the preparation, filing and publication of financial statements with respect to the revenues, the funds securing the debentures and the expenses, properties and operations of the department, including provisions for examinations and reports by independent certified public accountants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
