# MVC § 417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 1. Funds, Arms, and Equipment

**Citation:** MVC § 417

---

## Text

The transportation of arms, equipment, and military stores issued to troops or received by the State and all other military transportation shall be contracted for by the Adjutant General.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 393.)
