# MVC § 505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 1. California Cadet Corps

**Citation:** MVC § 505

---

## Text

The Adjutant General may order officers, warrant officers, and noncommissioned officers of the State Guard, Naval Militia, or California National Guard to temporary state active duty to perform functions in support of the California Cadet Corps. Pay for those duties shall be disbursed from funds appropriated for the support and maintenance of the California Cadet Corps.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 67, Sec. 143. (AB 1170) Effective January 1, 2026.)
