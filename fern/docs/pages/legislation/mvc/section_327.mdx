# MVC § 327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 5. Compensation, Allowance, and Insurance > Article: 1. Pay and Allowances

**Citation:** MVC § 327

---

## Text

Officers, warrant officers, and enlisted persons on active duty in the service of the state shall be eligible for health care benefits 30 days after being called to active duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 118, Sec. 28. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2019.)
