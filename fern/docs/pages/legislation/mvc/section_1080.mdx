# MVC § 1080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Veterans Homes Planning Strategy

**Citation:** MVC § 1080

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be deemed to require or authorize the disclosure of personal or medical information otherwise prohibited or restricted from disclosure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 509, Sec. 1. (AB 1365) Effective January 1, 2018.)
