# MVC § 283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 4. The Naval Militia

**Citation:** MVC § 283

---

## Text

The organization of the Naval Militia shall conform generally to the provisions of the laws of the United States.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
