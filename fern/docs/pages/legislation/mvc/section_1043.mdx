# MVC § 1043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Veterans’ Home of California

**Citation:** MVC § 1043

---

## Text

With the exception of officers and employees and their families, no person shall be admitted to reside in a home who is not a member eligible under Section 1012.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 61, Sec. 6. (AB 240) Effective January 1, 2021.)
