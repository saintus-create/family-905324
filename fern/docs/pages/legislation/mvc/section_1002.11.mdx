# MVC § 1002.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolutions of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 1002.11

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may include a provision requiring the department to preserve and protect the security of the debentures and the rights of the holders thereof and to warrant and defend such rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
