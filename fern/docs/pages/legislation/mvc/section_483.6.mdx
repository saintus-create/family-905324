# MVC § 483.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 10. California National Guard Members’ Revenue Bond Act of 1978 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolution of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 483.6

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may include a clause providing the events of default and the terms and conditions upon which any or all of the revenue bonds then or thereafter issued may become or be declared due and payable prior to maturity, and the terms and conditions upon which such declaration and its consequences may be waived.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1274.)
