# MVC § 443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 3. Los Angeles Air Force Base Housing

**Citation:** MVC § 443

---

## Text

The Department of Housing and Community Development shall not bear the costs to implement or administer any housing program established pursuant to the joint powers agreement authorized by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1028, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1992.)
