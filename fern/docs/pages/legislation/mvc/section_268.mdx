# MVC § 268

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 3. Enlisted Persons

**Citation:** MVC § 268

---

## Text

All time lost while absent without leave or in desertion, in excess of twenty-four hours, shall be made good unless the enlisted person is sooner discharged by proper authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 118, Sec. 18. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2019.)
