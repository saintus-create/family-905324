# MVC § 1263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. County and City Buildings

**Citation:** MVC § 1263

---

## Text

Any money which is made available by the State to any political subdivision for the purposes of acquiring and constructing public buildings may be used for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1946, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 110.)
