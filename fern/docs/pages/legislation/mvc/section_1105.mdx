# MVC § 1105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Veterans’ Homes Bond Act of 2000 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** MVC § 1105

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the Veterans’ Home Fund, created by Section 1103.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 728, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 16 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
