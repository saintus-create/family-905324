# MVC § 282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 4. The Naval Militia

**Citation:** MVC § 282

---

## Text

The Naval Militia shall be located throughout the State at the discretion of the Governor.
