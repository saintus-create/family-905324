# MVC § 1259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** MVC § 1259

---

## Text

Territory forming a part of any existing district at the time of presentation of a petition for annexation, shall not be annexed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 34.)
