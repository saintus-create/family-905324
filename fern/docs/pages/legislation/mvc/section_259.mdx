# MVC § 259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 3. Enlisted Persons

**Citation:** MVC § 259

---

## Text

Separation from service of an enlisted person of the National Guard or the unorganized militia called into active service is effected by death or by discharge by proper authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 118, Sec. 13. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2019.)
