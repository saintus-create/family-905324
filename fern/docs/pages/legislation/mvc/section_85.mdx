# MVC § 85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF MILITARY AND VETERANS AFFAIRS > Chapter: 2. Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** MVC § 85

## Text

Records of the department which are records of contract purchasers, shall not be open to inspection by the public.
