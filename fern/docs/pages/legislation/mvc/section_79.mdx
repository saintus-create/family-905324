# MVC § 79

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF MILITARY AND VETERANS AFFAIRS > Chapter: 2. Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** MVC § 79

---

## Text

There shall be an Undersecretary of Veterans Affairs, who shall be a civil executive officer. The holder of this position shall be appointed and their salary shall be established by the secretary pursuant to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 129, Sec. 5. (SB 1097) Effective January 1, 2025.)
