# MVC § 1637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. NATIONAL DEFENSE > Chapter: 2. Sabotage Prevention Act of 1950 > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** MVC § 1637

---

## Text

If conduct prohibited by this chapter is also made unlawful by another law, the offender may be convicted for the violation of this chapter or of the other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 68.)
