# MVC § 1002.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolutions of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 1002.21

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may provide that the debentures issued thereunder and the interest thereon shall be secured by all or part of the revenues, and may establish a lien upon the revenues as the department, with the approval of the Veterans’ Debenture Finance Committee, shall determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
