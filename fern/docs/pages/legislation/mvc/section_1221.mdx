# MVC § 1221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 3.5. Construction and Alteration of Halls, Buildings and Meeting Places

**Citation:** MVC § 1221

---

## Text

No contract for the construction or alteration of any hall, building or meeting place shall be let until such time as professional estimates of the cost of such construction or alteration have been submitted to, considered, and approved by a majority of the board at an official meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1067.)
