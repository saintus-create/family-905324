# MVC § 1185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** MVC § 1185

---

## Text

A district may be formed in the manner prescribed by this article that is within an existing memorial district, if, in addition to the petition required by Section 1173, there is filed with the county elections official a verified copy of a resolution by the board of the existing district consenting to the formation of the new district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 76. Effective January 1, 2003.)
