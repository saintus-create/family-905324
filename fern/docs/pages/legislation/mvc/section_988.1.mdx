# MVC § 988.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.5. Funds

**Citation:** MVC § 988.1

---

## Text

The money paid by purchasers shall be deposited in the Veterans’ Farm and Home Building Fund of 1943 and be available for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 1046.)
