# MVC § 987.55

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.1. Veterans’ Farm and Home Purchase Act of 1974

**Citation:** MVC § 987.55

---

## Text

The department shall adopt rules and regulations in keeping with the purpose of this article to establish preferences in the granting of benefits conferred by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1477.)
