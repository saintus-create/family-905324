# MVC § 1044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Veterans’ Home of California

**Citation:** MVC § 1044

---

## Text

The secretary may adopt rules and regulations governing the admission of applicants and may prescribe the conditions upon which they may enter and remain with a home.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 28, Sec. 80. (SB 96) Effective June 27, 2017.)
