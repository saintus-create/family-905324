# MVC § 1651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. NATIONAL DEFENSE > Chapter: 2. Sabotage Prevention Act of 1950 > Article: 2. Protection of Property Used for Defense Preparation

**Citation:** MVC § 1651

---

## Text

Any person who without permission of the owner wilfully enters upon any premises posted pursuant to Section 1650 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 68.)
