# MVC § 450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 9. Military Courts

**Citation:** MVC § 450

---

## Text

The military courts of this state are: (a) general courts-martial; (b) special courts-martial; (c) summary courts-martial; (d) courts of inquiry; and (e) Courts-Martial Appellate Panel.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 322, Sec. 1. (AB 1410) Effective January 1, 2014.)
