# MVC § 1175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** MVC § 1175

---

## Text

The petition may be filed in sections, each of which shall fully comply with all of the requirements for a petition, except that each section need not contain the total number of signatures required for a petition.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
