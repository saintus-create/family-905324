# MVC § 943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 2. Burial of Veterans and Veterans’ Dependents

**Citation:** MVC § 943

---

## Text

Such burial shall not be made in any cemetery or burial ground, or any portion thereof, used exclusively for the burial of the pauper dead.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
