# MVC § 443.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 3. Los Angeles Air Force Base Housing

**Citation:** MVC § 443.5

---

## Text

No moneys received by or administered by the state under the federal HOME program which is part of the Cranston-Gonzalez National Affordable Housing Act (P.L. 101-625) shall be used for purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1028, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1992.)
