# MVC § 227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 2. Officers

**Citation:** MVC § 227

---

## Text

When an officer of the National Guard is sixty-four years of age, he shall be retired from active service and placed on the retired list.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
