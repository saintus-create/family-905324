# MVC § 160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 3. The Adjutant General

**Citation:** MVC § 160

---

## Text

The Adjutant General is chief of staff to the Governor, subordinate only to the Governor and is the commander of all state military forces.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 114, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
