# MVC § 1006.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** MVC § 1006.5

---

## Text

All debentures, subject to the provisions for registration contained therein, are negotiable instruments under the law merchant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
