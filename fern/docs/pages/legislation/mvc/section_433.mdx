# MVC § 433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 2. Armories

**Citation:** MVC § 433

---

## Text

The Adjutant General may lease on behalf of the State armories necessary for the use of the militia.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
