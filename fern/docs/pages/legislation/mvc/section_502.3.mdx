# MVC § 502.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 1. California Cadet Corps

**Citation:** MVC § 502.3

---

## Text

The Adjutant General may adopt rules and regulations providing for the personnel actions of officers, warrant officers, and noncommissioned officers of the California Cadet Corps.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 207, Sec. 11. (SB 1335) Effective January 1, 2025.)
