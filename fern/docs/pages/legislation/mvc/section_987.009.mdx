# MVC § 987.009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.2. The Veterans Housing and Homeless Prevention Act of 2014

**Citation:** MVC § 987.009

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13340 of the Government Code, the proceeds of bonds deposited in the Housing for Veterans Fund pursuant to Section 998.544 shall be subject to annual appropriation, as determined by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 727, Sec. 3. (AB 639) Effective October 10, 2013.)
