# MVC § 442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 3. Los Angeles Air Force Base Housing

**Citation:** MVC § 442

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, the power to provide a short-term solution to the problem of housing for military personnel stationed at Los Angeles Air Force Base shall be a power common to the contracting parties specified in this article, as required by Section 6502 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1028, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1992.)
