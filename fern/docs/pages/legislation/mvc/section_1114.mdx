# MVC § 1114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Veterans’ Homes Bond Act of 2000 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** MVC § 1114

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 728, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 16 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
