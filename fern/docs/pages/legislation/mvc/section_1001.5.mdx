# MVC § 1001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 2. Authorization and Issuance of Debentures

**Citation:** MVC § 1001.5

---

## Text

The validity of the authorization and issuance of any debentures shall not be dependent on or affected by the validity or regularity of any proceedings relating to the expenditure of the proceeds thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
