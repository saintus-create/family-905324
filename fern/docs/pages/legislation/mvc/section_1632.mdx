# MVC § 1632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. NATIONAL DEFENSE > Chapter: 2. Sabotage Prevention Act of 1950 > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** MVC § 1632

---

## Text

“Highway” includes any private or public street, way, or other place used for travel to or from property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 68.)
