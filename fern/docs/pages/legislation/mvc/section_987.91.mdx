# MVC § 987.91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.1. Veterans’ Farm and Home Purchase Act of 1974

**Citation:** MVC § 987.91

---

## Text

Pursuant to the provisions of Section 987.90, the Department of Veterans Affairs is authorized to issue to lenders or investors supplying funds for the program a participation contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1477.)
