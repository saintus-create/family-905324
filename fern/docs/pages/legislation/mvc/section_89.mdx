# MVC § 89

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF MILITARY AND VETERANS AFFAIRS > Chapter: 2. Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** MVC § 89

---

## Text

The department may apply to the California Debt Limit Allocation Committee for allocations of the state ceiling on qualified private activity bonds for qualified residential rental projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 332, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
