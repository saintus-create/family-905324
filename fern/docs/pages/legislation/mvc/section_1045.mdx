# MVC § 1045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Veterans’ Home of California

**Citation:** MVC § 1045

---

## Text

This chapter does not prevent the state from transferring the property and management of a home to the United States for a home of similar character.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 28, Sec. 82. (SB 96) Effective June 27, 2017.)
