# MVC § 998.210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 5v. Veterans’ Bond Act of 1996

**Citation:** MVC § 998.210

---

## Text

The committee may authorize the Treasurer to sell all or any part of the bonds authorized by this article at the time or times established by the Treasurer.

Whenever the committee deems it necessary for an effective sale of the bonds, the committee may authorize the Treasurer to sell any issue of bonds at less than their par value, notwithstanding Section 16754 of the Government Code. However, the discount on the bonds shall not exceed 3 percent of the par value thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 161, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 206 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
