# MVC § 989.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.8. Disaster Indemnity

**Citation:** MVC § 989.8

---

## Text

Eligible purchasers shall be indemnified pursuant to this article solely from moneys in the Indemnity Fund, and the state shall not be liable beyond the assets of the Indemnity Fund for any obligation in connection therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 362.)
