# MVC § 954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 2. Burial of Veterans and Veterans’ Dependents

**Citation:** MVC § 954

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each county is encouraged to designate personnel from a veterans’ remains organization, as defined in Section 951, to fulfill the role created in Section 942.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 189, Sec. 6. (AB 1644) Effective January 1, 2011.)
