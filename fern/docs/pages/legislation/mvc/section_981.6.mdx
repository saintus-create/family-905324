# MVC § 981.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 2. Educational Assistance

**Citation:** MVC § 981.6

---

## Text

No veteran who is receiving federal educational benefits shall be eligible to receive the educational benefits provided by this article during such time that he is receiving federal educational benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 354.)
