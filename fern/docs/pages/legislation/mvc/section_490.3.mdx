# MVC § 490.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 10. California National Guard Members’ Revenue Bond Act of 1978 > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** MVC § 490.3

---

## Text

Whenever any bonds of the State of California may by law be used as security for the performance of any act or the deposit of any public money, revenue bonds authorized under Section 481 may be so used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1274.)
