# MVC § 65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF MILITARY AND VETERANS AFFAIRS > Chapter: 2. Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** MVC § 65

---

## Text

The California Veterans Board shall consist of seven members who shall be appointed by the Governor subject to the confirmation of the Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 219, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
