# MVC § 998.614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 5z. The Veterans and Affordable Housing Bond Act of 2018

**Citation:** MVC § 998.614

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that, inasmuch as the proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this article are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution, the disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by Article XIIIB.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 365, Sec. 4. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 6, 2018, election; effective November 7, 2018.)
