# MVC § 490.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 10. California National Guard Members’ Revenue Bond Act of 1978 > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** MVC § 490.1

---

## Text

All revenue bonds shall be legal investments for all trust funds, for the funds of all insurance companies, banks (both commercial and savings), trust companies, state school funds, and any pension funds, public or private.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1274.)
