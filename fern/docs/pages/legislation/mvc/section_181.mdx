# MVC § 181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 3. The Adjutant General

**Citation:** MVC § 181

---

## Text

The Adjutant General shall superintend the preparation of all returns and reports required by the United States from the State on military matters.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
