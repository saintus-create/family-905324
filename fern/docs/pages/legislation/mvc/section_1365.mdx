# MVC § 1365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 8.7. Veterans Appreciation Week

**Citation:** MVC § 1365

---

## Text

In recognition of the efforts and sacrifices of veterans of the United States Armed Forces, the second full week in November is hereby annually designated as Veterans Appreciation Week.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 86, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
