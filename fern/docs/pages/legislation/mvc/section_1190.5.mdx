# MVC § 1190.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 2. Management

**Citation:** MVC § 1190.5

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2006.)
