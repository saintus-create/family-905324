# MVC § 987.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.1. Veterans’ Farm and Home Purchase Act of 1974

**Citation:** MVC § 987.51

---

## Text

The object of this article is to provide veterans with the opportunity to acquire farms and homes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1477.)
