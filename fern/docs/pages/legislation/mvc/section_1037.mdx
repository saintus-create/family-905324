# MVC § 1037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Veterans’ Home of California

**Citation:** MVC § 1037

---

## Text

The money belonging to a member and voluntarily deposited with a home may be withdrawn, in whole or in part, at the will of the member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 28, Sec. 72. (SB 96) Effective June 27, 2017.)
