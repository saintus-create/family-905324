# MVC § 214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 1. Membership

**Citation:** MVC § 214

---

## Text

During the absence of organizations of the National Guard in the service of the United States, their State or Federal designations shall not be given to new organizations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
