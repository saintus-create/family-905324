# MVC § 810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 3. The California Military Families Financial Relief Act

**Citation:** MVC § 810

---

## Text

In those instances where a financial obligation covered by this chapter is sold, any requirement to defer payments as specified in this chapter transfers to the purchaser of the obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 291, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
