# MVC § 484.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 10. California National Guard Members’ Revenue Bond Act of 1978 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolution of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 484.1

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may include provisions requiring the preparation, filing, and publication of financial statements with respect to the revenues, the funds securing the revenue bonds, and the expenses, properties, and operations of the department, including provisions for examinations and reports by independent certified public accountants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1274.)
