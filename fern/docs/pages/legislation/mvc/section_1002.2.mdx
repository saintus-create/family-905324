# MVC § 1002.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolutions of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 1002.2

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may provide for the dates to be borne by debentures, and by each series, issued thereunder and for the dates of maturity thereof. For any authorized issuance of debentures, the resolution may provide the department with discretion to determine the dates of the debentures and maturity dates of the debentures, subject to the maximum maturity date authorized in the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 287, Sec. 2. Effective September 25, 2008.)
