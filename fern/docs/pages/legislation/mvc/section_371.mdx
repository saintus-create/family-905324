# MVC § 371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 6. Discipline and Exercise

**Citation:** MVC § 371

---

## Text

Warning for duty may be given by any officer or noncommissioned officer or any other person authorized so to do.
