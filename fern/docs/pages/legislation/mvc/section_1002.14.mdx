# MVC § 1002.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 3. Provisions of Resolutions of Issuance and Supplemental Resolutions

**Citation:** MVC § 1002.14

---

## Text

A resolution of issuance may include a provision requiring the department to charge a uniform rate of interest on the unpaid balance of the purchase price of any and all properties acquired by the department and sold to veterans under this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
