# MVC § 1006.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 7. Veterans’ Revenue Debenture Act of 1970 > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** MVC § 1006.9

---

## Text

Warrants for the payments to be made on account of the debentures either as to principal, interest, premium, or otherwise, shall be duly drawn by the State Controller, upon the request of the State Treasurer, whenever the drawing of such warrants is required in order to make such payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1488.)
