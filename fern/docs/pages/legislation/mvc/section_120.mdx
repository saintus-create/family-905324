# MVC § 120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 1. Composition

**Citation:** MVC § 120

---

## Text

The militia of the State shall consist of the National Guard, State Guard and the Naval Militia—which constitute the active militia—and the unorganized militia.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 97, Sec. 14. (AB 2193) Effective January 1, 2021.)
