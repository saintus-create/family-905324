# MVC § 78.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF MILITARY AND VETERANS AFFAIRS > Chapter: 2. Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** MVC § 78.5

---

## Text

The secretary shall conduct audits as required by Section 13402 of the Government Code, on internal controls of the department, that shall be provided to the inspector general.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 977, Sec. 3. Effective September 27, 2002.)
