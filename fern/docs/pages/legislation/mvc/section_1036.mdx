# MVC § 1036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Veterans’ Home of California

**Citation:** MVC § 1036

---

## Text

The members may voluntarily deposit money with a home, which the home shall receive and keep without charge as a trust fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 28, Sec. 71. (SB 96) Effective June 27, 2017.)
