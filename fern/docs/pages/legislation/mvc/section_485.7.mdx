# MVC § 485.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 10. California National Guard Members’ Revenue Bond Act of 1978 > Article: 4. Appropriation, Security, Sale and Funds

**Citation:** MVC § 485.7

---

## Text

All revenues to be received by the department from purchase contracts shall be directly, concurrently, and exclusively pledged to the payment of revenue bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 920, Sec. 15.)
