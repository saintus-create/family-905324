# MVC § 981.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 2. Educational Assistance

**Citation:** MVC § 981.7

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as repealing the provisions of Chapter 579, Statutes of 1921, appropriating money for educational assistance to veterans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 627.)
