# MVC § 444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 8. Equipment and Accommodations > Article: 3. Los Angeles Air Force Base Housing

**Citation:** MVC § 444

---

## Text

A jurisdiction that is a party to the joint powers agreement authorized by this article shall comply with Article 10.6 (commencing with Section 65580) of Chapter 3 of Division 1 of Title 7 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1028, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1992.)
