# MVC § 987.881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.1. Veterans’ Farm and Home Purchase Act of 1974

**Citation:** MVC § 987.881

---

## Text

The insurance company or companies may provide life and disability insurance pursuant to Section 987.88 by one or more separate accounts.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1140, Sec. 1.)
