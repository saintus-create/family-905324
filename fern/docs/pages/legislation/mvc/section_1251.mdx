# MVC § 1251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** MVC § 1251

---

## Text

The boundaries of any district organized or existing under this chapter may be altered and new territory annexed to, and incorporated within, it pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 34.)
