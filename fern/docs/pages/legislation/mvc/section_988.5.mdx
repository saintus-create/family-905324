# MVC § 988.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 3.5. Funds

**Citation:** MVC § 988.5

---

## Text

All loans granted under this article shall be funded in accordance with federal laws governing the use of tax-exempt bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 465, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003.)
