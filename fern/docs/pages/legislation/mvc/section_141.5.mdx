# MVC § 141.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 2. Commander in Chief

**Citation:** MVC § 141.5

---

## Text

The state active duty force consists of service members in active state military service when ordered by the Governor in accordance with this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 355, Sec. 2. (SB 807) Effective January 1, 2013.)
