# MVC § 1194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 2. Management

## Text

The powers of the district, except as otherwise expressly provided, shall be exercised by the board of directors.
