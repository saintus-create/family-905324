# MVC § 286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 4. The Naval Militia

**Citation:** MVC § 286

---

## Text

The several divisions, companies of marines, and other organizations of the Naval Militia may be organized into battalions at the discretion of the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
