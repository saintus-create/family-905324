# MVC § 140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 2. Commander in Chief

**Citation:** MVC § 140

---

## Text

The Governor of the State, by virtue of his office, is the Commander in Chief of the Militia of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
