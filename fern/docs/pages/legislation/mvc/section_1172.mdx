# MVC § 1172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. Memorial Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** MVC § 1172

---

## Text

No district shall include territory not wholly in the same county. A district may include any incorporated territory of the county together with any contiguous unincorporated territory thereof, or may be formed entirely of contiguous incorporated territory or entirely of contiguous unincorporated territory.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
