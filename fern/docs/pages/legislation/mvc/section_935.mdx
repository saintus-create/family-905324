# MVC § 935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 1.5. Disabled Veterans

**Citation:** MVC § 935

---

## Text

The governing board of any county may grant financial assistance, relief, and support to a disabled veteran, as defined in Section 999.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 71, Sec. 113. (SB 1304) Effective January 1, 2015.)
