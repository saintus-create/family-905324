# MVC § 828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 3.3. California Military Families Financial Relief Act of 2005

**Citation:** MVC § 828

---

## Text

The Military Department shall, to the extent reasonable and feasible, inform all members of the militia of the benefits and protections provided by this act, and of similar benefits and protections provided by any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 345, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
