# MVC § 257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 3. Enlisted Persons

**Citation:** MVC § 257

---

## Text

When an enlisted person of the National Guard is 64 years of age, they shall be retired from active service or discharged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 67, Sec. 136. (AB 1170) Effective January 1, 2026.)
