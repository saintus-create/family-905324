# MVC § 1103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Veterans’ Homes Bond Act of 2000 > Article: 2. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** MVC § 1103

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Veterans’ Home Fund, which is hereby created in the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 728, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 16 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
