# MVC § 998.552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 5y. The Veterans Housing and Homeless Prevention Bond Act of 2014

**Citation:** MVC § 998.552

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold, in excess of any amount of premium used to pay costs of issuing the bonds, shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. (AB 639) Approved in Proposition 41 at the June 3, 2014, election.)
