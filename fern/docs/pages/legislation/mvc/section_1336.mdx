# MVC § 1336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. VETERANS BUILDINGS, MEMORIALS, AND CEMETERIES > Chapter: 7. California Mexican American Veterans’ Memorial Beautification and Enhancement

**Citation:** MVC § 1336

---

## Text

The design of the enhanced memorial shall be as politically neutral as possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1221, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994. Repealed on date prescribed in Section 1338.)
