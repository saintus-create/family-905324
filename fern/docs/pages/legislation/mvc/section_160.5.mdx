# MVC § 160.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 3. The Adjutant General

**Citation:** MVC § 160.5

---

## Text

Any statute of this state referring to the Commanding General of the State Military Forces is deemed to refer to the Adjutant General.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 517, Sec. 4.)
