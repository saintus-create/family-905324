# MVC § 144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 2. General Organization > Article: 2. Commander in Chief

**Citation:** MVC § 144

---

## Text

The Governor may at any time revoke a proclamation of insurrection or declare that it shall terminate at a time or in the manner that the Governor directs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 129, Sec. 10. (SB 1097) Effective January 1, 2025.)
