# MVC § 929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 1. Indigent Veterans

**Citation:** MVC § 929

---

## Text

The necessary expenses, not to exceed three hundred fifty dollars ($350), for burial or cremation of any indigent veteran may be paid out of the money available under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 380, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
