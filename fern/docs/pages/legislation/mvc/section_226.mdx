# MVC § 226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 2. Officers

**Citation:** MVC § 226

---

## Text

Every officer shall provide himself with the arms, uniforms, and equipment prescribed and approved by the Governor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 120.)
