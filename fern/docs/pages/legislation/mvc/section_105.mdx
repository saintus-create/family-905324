# MVC § 105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 1. Laws and Regulations of the United States

**Citation:** MVC § 105

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 11425.10 of the Government Code, Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 11400) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code does not apply to a hearing conducted by the Military Department under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 938, Sec. 79. Effective January 1, 1996. Operative July 1, 1997, by Sec. 98 of Ch. 938.)
