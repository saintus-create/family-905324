# MVC § 508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 1. California Cadet Corps

**Citation:** MVC § 508

---

## Text

The California Cadet Corps shall train in accordance with the appropriate manuals prescribed by the United States Army and by the rules and regulations adopted by the Adjutant General.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 207, Sec. 19. (SB 1335) Effective January 1, 2025.)
