# MVC § 265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 3. The National Guard > Article: 3. Enlisted Persons

**Citation:** MVC § 265

---

## Text

Lists of deserters shall be published by The Adjutant General in orders, from time to time as the Governor directs.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
