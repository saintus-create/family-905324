# MVC § 1691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. NATIONAL DEFENSE > Chapter: 3. Reinstatement of Public Officers After Military Service

**Citation:** MVC § 1691

---

## Text

Upon their return and reentry or their return to the office they shall have all of the rights and privileges in, connected with, or arising out of, the office which they would have enjoyed if they had not been absent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 129, Sec. 109. (SB 1097) Effective January 1, 2025.)
