# MVC § 1100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. VETERANS' INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Veterans’ Homes Bond Act of 2000 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** MVC § 1100

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Veterans’ Homes Bond Act of 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 728, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 16 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
