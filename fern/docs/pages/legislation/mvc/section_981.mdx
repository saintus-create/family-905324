# MVC § 981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 6. State Benefits for Veterans > Article: 2. Educational Assistance

**Citation:** MVC § 981

---

## Text

There is in the state government an educational institution known as the California Veterans’ Educational Institute, which is under the management and control of the Department of Veterans Affairs. The purpose of the institute is to provide opportunities for veterans to continue their education.
