# MVC § 822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 3.3. California Military Families Financial Relief Act of 2005

**Citation:** MVC § 822

---

## Text

No county recorder in this state may impose a fee for the recordation of a power of attorney to act as the agent for a service member.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 555, Sec. 23. (AB 3212) Effective January 1, 2019.)
