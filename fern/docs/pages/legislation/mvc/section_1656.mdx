# MVC § 1656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. NATIONAL DEFENSE > Chapter: 2. Sabotage Prevention Act of 1950 > Article: 2. Protection of Property Used for Defense Preparation

**Citation:** MVC § 1656

---

## Text

Appropriate notices in letters at least three inches high shall be posted conspicuously at each end of any highway so closed or restricted by such order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 68.)
