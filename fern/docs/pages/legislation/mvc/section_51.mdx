# MVC § 51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF MILITARY AND VETERANS AFFAIRS > Chapter: 1. The Military Department

**Citation:** MVC § 51

---

## Text

The Military Department includes the office of the Adjutant General, the California National Guard, the State Guard, the California Cadet Corps, and the Naval Militia.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 97, Sec. 10. (AB 2193) Effective January 1, 2021.)
