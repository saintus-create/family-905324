# MVC § 1821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VETERANS' WELFARE > Chapter: 2. Veterans’ Organizations and Insignia

**Citation:** MVC § 1821

---

## Text

A person who falsely represents themself in a manner specified in Section 532b of the Penal Code shall be guilty of a misdemeanor or infraction, as prescribed by Section 532b of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 129, Sec. 113. (SB 1097) Effective January 1, 2025.)
