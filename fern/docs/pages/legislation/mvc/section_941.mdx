# MVC § 941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. VETERANS' AID AND WELFARE > Chapter: 5. Local Aid > Article: 2. Burial of Veterans and Veterans’ Dependents

**Citation:** MVC § 941

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to soldiers, sailors, or marines who die in the national or State soldiers’ homes in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 389.)
