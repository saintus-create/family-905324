# MVC § 490.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. THE MILITARY FORCES OF THE STATE > Chapter: 10. California National Guard Members’ Revenue Bond Act of 1978 > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** MVC § 490.2

---

## Text

Any money or funds which may by law be invested in bonds of the State of California may be invested in revenue bonds authorized under Section 481.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1274.)
