# CCP § 484.330

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 4. Noticed Hearing Procedure for Obtaining Writ of Attachment > Article: 2. Noticed Hearing Procedure for Obtaining Additional Writs

**Citation:** CCP § 484.330

---

## Text

No writ of attachment shall be issued under this article except after a hearing. At least 15 days prior to the hearing, the defendant shall be served with both of the following:

(a) A notice of application and hearing.

(b) A copy of the application.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1090, Sec. 2.)
