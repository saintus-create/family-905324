# CCP § 583.140

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Dismissal for Delay in Prosecution > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 583.140

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter abrogates or otherwise affects the principles of waiver and estoppel.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1705, Sec. 5.)
