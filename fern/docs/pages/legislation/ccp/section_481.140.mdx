# CCP § 481.140

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.140

---

## Text

“Levying officer” means the sheriff or marshal who is directed to execute a writ or order issued under this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1997.)
