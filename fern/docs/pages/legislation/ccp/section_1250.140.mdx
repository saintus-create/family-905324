# CCP § 1250.140

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 5. Commencement of Proceeding > Article: 2. Commencement of Proceeding Generally

**Citation:** CCP § 1250.140

---

## Text

Where the state is a defendant, the summons and the complaint shall be served on the Attorney General.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
