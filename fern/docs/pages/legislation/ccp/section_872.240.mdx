# CCP § 872.240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2. Commencement of Action > Article: 1. Complaint and Lis Pendens

**Citation:** CCP § 872.240

---

## Text

Real and personal property may be partitioned in one action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
