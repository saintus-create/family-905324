# CCP § 405.36

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. RECORDING NOTICE OF CERTAIN ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Expungement and Other Relief

**Citation:** CCP § 405.36

---

## Text

Once a notice of pending action has been expunged, the claimant may not record another notice of pending action as to the affected property without leave of the court in which the action is pending.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 883, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
