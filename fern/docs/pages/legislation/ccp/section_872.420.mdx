# CCP § 872.420

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2. Commencement of Action > Article: 3. Answer

**Citation:** CCP § 872.420

---

## Text

Where the defendant has or claims a lien on the property, the answer shall set forth the date and character of the lien and the amount remaining due thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
