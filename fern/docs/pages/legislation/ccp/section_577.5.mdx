# CCP § 577.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Judgment in General

**Citation:** CCP § 577.5

---

## Text

In any judgment, or execution upon such judgment, the amount shall be computed and stated in dollars and cents, rejecting fractions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
