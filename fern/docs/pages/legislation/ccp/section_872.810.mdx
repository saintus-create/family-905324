# CCP § 872.810

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Trial > Article: 3. Determination of Manner of Partition

**Citation:** CCP § 872.810

---

## Text

The court shall order that the property be divided among the parties in accordance with their interests in the property as determined in the interlocutory judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
