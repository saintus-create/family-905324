# CCP § 873.530

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 6. Sale of the Property > Article: 1. Manner of Sale

**Citation:** CCP § 873.530

---

## Text

Part of the property may be sold at public auction and part at private sale if it appears that to do so will be more beneficial to the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
