# CCP § 481.180

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.180

---

## Text

“Plaintiff” means a person who files a complaint or cross-complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1516.)
