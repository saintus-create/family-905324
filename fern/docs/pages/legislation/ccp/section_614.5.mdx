# CCP § 614.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. Trial by Jury > Article: 2. Conduct of the Trial

**Citation:** CCP § 614.5

---

## Text

Except for good cause shown, the judge in his or her discretion need not be present in the court while testimony previously received in evidence is read to the jury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 88, Sec. 1. Effective July 2, 1987.)
