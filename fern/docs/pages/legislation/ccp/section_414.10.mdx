# CCP § 414.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. JURISDICTION AND SERVICE OF PROCESS > Chapter: 4. Service of Summons > Article: 2. Persons Who May Serve Summons

**Citation:** CCP § 414.10

---

## Text

A summons may be served by any person who is at least 18 years of age and not a party to the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1610.)
