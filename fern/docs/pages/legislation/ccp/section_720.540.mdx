# CCP § 720.540

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Creditor’s Demand for Third-Party Claim by Secured Party or Lienholder

**Citation:** CCP § 720.540

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, the levying officer may not release, sell, or otherwise dispose of the personal property described in the demand before the expiration of 30 days after service of the demand on the secured party or lienholder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
