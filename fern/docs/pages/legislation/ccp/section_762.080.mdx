# CCP § 762.080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4. Quiet Title > Article: 3. Defendants

**Citation:** CCP § 762.080

---

## Text

The court upon its own motion may, and upon motion of any party shall, make such orders for appointment of guardians ad litem as appear necessary to protect the interest of any party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 15.)
