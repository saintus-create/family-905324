# CCP § 1297.82

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 2. Arbitration Agreements and Judicial Measures in Aid of Arbitration > Article: 2. Stay of Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.82

---

## Text

A timely request for a stay of judicial proceedings made under Section 1297.81 shall be granted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
