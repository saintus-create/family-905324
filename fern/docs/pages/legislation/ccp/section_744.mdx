# CCP § 744

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3. Actions for the Recovery of Real Property, and Other Provisions Relating to Actions Concerning Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 744

---

## Text

A mortgage of real property shall not be deemed a conveyance, whatever its terms, so as to enable the owner of the mortgage to recover possession of the real property without a foreclosure and sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
