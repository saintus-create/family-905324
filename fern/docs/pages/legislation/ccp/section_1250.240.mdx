# CCP § 1250.240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 5. Commencement of Proceeding > Article: 3. Parties; Joinder of Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1250.240

---

## Text

The plaintiff may join in one complaint all property located within the same county which is sought to be acquired for the same project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
