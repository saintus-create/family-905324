# CCP § 1297.15

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 1. Application and Interpretation > Article: 1. Scope of Application

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.15

---

## Text

For the purposes of Section 1297.13, the states of the United States, including the District of Columbia, shall be considered one state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
