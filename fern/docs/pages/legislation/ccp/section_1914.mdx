# CCP § 1914

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE KINDS AND DEGREES OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Writings > Article: 2. Public Writings

**Citation:** CCP § 1914

---

## Text

The effect of the judicial record of a Court of admiralty of a foreign country is the same as if it were the record of a Court of admiralty of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
