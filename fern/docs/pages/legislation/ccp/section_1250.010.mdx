# CCP § 1250.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 5. Commencement of Proceeding > Article: 1. Jurisdiction and Venue

**Citation:** CCP § 1250.010

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 1230.060 and in Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 1273.010), all eminent domain proceedings shall be commenced and prosecuted in the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
