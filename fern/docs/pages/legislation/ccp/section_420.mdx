# CCP § 420

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. OF THE PLEADINGS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. The Pleadings in General

**Citation:** CCP § 420

---

## Text

The pleadings are the formal allegations by the parties of their respective claims and defenses, for the judgment of the Court.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
