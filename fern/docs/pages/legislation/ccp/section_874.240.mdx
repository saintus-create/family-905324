# CCP § 874.240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 9. Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 874.240

---

## Text

A conveyance or transfer pursuant to Sections 873.750 and 873.790 or Section 873.960 is binding and conclusive, in the same manner as a judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
