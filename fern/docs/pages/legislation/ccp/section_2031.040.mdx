# CCP § 2031.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 14. Inspection, Copying, Testing, Sampling, and Production of Documents, Electronically Stored Information, Tangible Things, Land, and Other Property > Article: 1. Inspection Demand

**Citation:** CCP § 2031.040

---

## Text

The party making a demand for inspection, copying, testing, or sampling shall serve a copy of the demand on the party to whom it is directed and on all other parties who have appeared in the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 5, Sec. 7. Effective June 29, 2009.)
