# CCP § 371

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF THE PARTIES TO CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Married Person

**Citation:** CCP § 371

---

## Text

If spouses are sued together, each may defend for his or her own right, but if one spouse neglects to defend, the other spouse may defend for that spouse’s right also.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 15. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
