# CCP § 1255.480

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 6. Deposit and Withdrawal of Probable Compensation; Possession Prior to Judgment > Article: 3. Possession Prior to Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 1255.480

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the right of a public entity to exercise its police power in emergency situations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
