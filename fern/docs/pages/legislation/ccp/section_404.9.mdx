# CCP § 404.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. OF THE PLACE OF TRIAL, RECLASSIFICATION, AND COORDINATION OF CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Coordination

**Citation:** CCP § 404.9

---

## Text

Any duties of the presiding judge specified in this chapter may be delegated by the presiding judge to another judge of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 59. Effective January 1, 2003.)
