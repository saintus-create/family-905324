# CCP § 1293.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 5. General Provisions Relating toJudicial Proceedings > Article: 2. Venue, Jurisdiction and Costs

**Citation:** CCP § 1293.2

---

## Text

The court shall award costs upon any judicial proceeding under this title as provided in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 1021) of Title 14 of Part 2 of this code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
