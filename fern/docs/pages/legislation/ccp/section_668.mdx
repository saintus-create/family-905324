# CCP § 668

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 8. The Manner of Giving and Entering Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 668

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 668.5, the clerk of the superior court, must keep, with the records of the court, a book called the “judgment book,” in which judgments must be entered.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 66. Effective January 1, 2003.)
