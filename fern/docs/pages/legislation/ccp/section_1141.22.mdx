# CCP § 1141.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2.5. Judicial Arbitration

**Citation:** CCP § 1141.22

---

## Text

The Judicial Council rules shall specify the grounds upon which the arbitrator or the court, or both, may correct, modify or vacate an award.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 743.)
