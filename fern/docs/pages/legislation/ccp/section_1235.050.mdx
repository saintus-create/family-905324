# CCP § 1235.050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 1. Construction

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.050

---

## Text

The present tense includes the past and future tenses; and the future, the present.
