# CCP § 167

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. JUDICIAL OFFICERS > Chapter: 2. Powers of Judges at Chambers

**Citation:** CCP § 167

---

## Text

Any act required or permitted to be performed by the clerk of a court may be performed by a judge thereof.
