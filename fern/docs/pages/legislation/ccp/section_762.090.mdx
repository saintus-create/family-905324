# CCP § 762.090

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4. Quiet Title > Article: 3. Defendants

**Citation:** CCP § 762.090

---

## Text

(a) The state may be joined as a party to an action under this chapter.

(b) This section does not constitute a change in, but is declaratory of, existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 15.)
