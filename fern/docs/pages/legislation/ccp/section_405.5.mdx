# CCP § 405.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. RECORDING NOTICE OF CERTAIN ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 405.5

---

## Text

This title applies to an action pending in any United States District Court in the same manner that it applies to an action pending in the courts of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 883, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
