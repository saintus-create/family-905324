# CCP § 1275

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. CHANGE OF NAMES

**Citation:** CCP § 1275

---

## Text

Applications for change of names must be determined by the Superior Courts.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 486, Sec. 1.)
