# CCP § 88

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.1. Limited Civil Cases > Article: 1. Jurisdiction in Limited Civil Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 88

---

## Text

A civil action or proceeding other than a limited civil case may be referred to as an unlimited civil case.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 344, Sec. 4. Effective September 7, 1999.)
