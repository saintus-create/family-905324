# CCP § 417.30

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. JURISDICTION AND SERVICE OF PROCESS > Chapter: 4. Service of Summons > Article: 5. Proof of Service

**Citation:** CCP § 417.30

---

## Text

After a summons has been served on a person, proof of service of the summons as provided in Section 417.10 or 417.20 shall be filed, unless the defendant has previously made a general appearance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 300, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
