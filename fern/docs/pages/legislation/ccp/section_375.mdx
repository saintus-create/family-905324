# CCP § 375

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF THE PARTIES TO CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Disability of Party

**Citation:** CCP § 375

---

## Text

An action or proceeding does not abate by the disability of a party. The court, on motion, shall allow the action or proceeding to be continued by or against the party’s representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 178, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1993.)
