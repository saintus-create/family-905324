# CCP § 2018.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 4. Attorney Work Product

**Citation:** CCP § 2018.040

---

## Text

This chapter is intended to be a restatement of existing law relating to protection of work product. It is not intended to expand or reduce the extent to which work product is discoverable under existing law in any action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
