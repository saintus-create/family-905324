# CCP § 1016

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Notices, and Filing and Service of Papers

**Citation:** CCP § 1016

---

## Text

The foregoing provisions of this Chapter do not apply to the sevice of a summons or other process, or of any paper to bring a party into contempt.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
