# CCP § 630.05

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4.5. Voluntary Expedited Jury Trials

**Citation:** CCP § 630.05

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is intended to preclude a jury from deliberating as long as needed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 674, Sec. 2. (AB 2284) Effective January 1, 2011.)
