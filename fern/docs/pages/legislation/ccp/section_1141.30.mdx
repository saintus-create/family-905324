# CCP § 1141.30

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2.5. Judicial Arbitration

**Citation:** CCP § 1141.30

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be construed in derogation of Title 9 (commencing with Section 1280) of Part 3, and, to that extent, this chapter and that title are mutually exclusive and independent of each other.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 200, Sec. 10. (AB 1521) Effective January 1, 2026.)
