# CCP § 1297.12

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 1. Application and Interpretation > Article: 1. Scope of Application

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.12

---

## Text

This title, except Article 2 (commencing with Section 1297.81) of Chapter 2 and Article 3 (commencing with Section 1297.90) of Chapter 2, applies only if the place of arbitration or conciliation is in the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 90, Sec. 1. (AB 1903) Effective January 1, 2025.)
