# CCP § 428.40

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. OF THE PLEADINGS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Pleadings Demanding Relief > Article: 4. Cross-Complaints

**Citation:** CCP § 428.40

## Text

The cross-complaint shall be a separate document.
