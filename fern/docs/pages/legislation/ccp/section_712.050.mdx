# CCP § 712.050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 712.050

---

## Text

The return of a writ of possession or sale is governed by Section 699.560 (return of writ of execution).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
