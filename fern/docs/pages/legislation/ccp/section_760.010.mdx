# CCP § 760.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4. Quiet Title > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 760.010

---

## Text

As used in this chapter:

(a) “Claim” includes a legal or equitable right, title, estate, lien, or interest in property or cloud upon title.

(b) “Property” includes real property, and to the extent applicable, personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 15.)
