# CCP § 481.190

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.190

---

## Text

A claim has “probable validity” where it is more likely than not that the plaintiff will obtain a judgment against the defendant on that claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1516.)
