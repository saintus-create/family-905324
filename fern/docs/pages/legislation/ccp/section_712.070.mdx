# CCP § 712.070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 712.070

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 695.050, a judgment against a public entity is enforceable under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
