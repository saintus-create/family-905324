# CCP § 1297.72

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 2. Arbitration Agreements and Judicial Measures in Aid of Arbitration > Article: 1. Definition and Form of Arbitration Agreements

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.72

---

## Text

An arbitration agreement shall be in writing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 90, Sec. 3. (AB 1903) Effective January 1, 2025.)
