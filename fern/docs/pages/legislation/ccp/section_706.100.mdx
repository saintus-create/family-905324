# CCP § 706.100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Wage Garnishment > Article: 5. Procedure for Earnings Withholding Orders and Exemption Claims

**Citation:** CCP § 706.100

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Judicial Council may provide by rule for the practice and procedure in proceedings under this chapter except for the state’s administrative hearings provided by Article 4 (commencing with Section 706.070).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
