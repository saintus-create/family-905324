# CCP § 577

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Judgment in General

**Citation:** CCP § 577

---

## Text

A judgment is the final determination of the rights of the parties in an action or proceeding.
