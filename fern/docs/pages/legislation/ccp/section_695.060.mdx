# CCP § 695.060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Property Subject to Enforcement of Money Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 695.060

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 708.630, a license issued by a public entity to engage in any business, profession, or activity is not subject to enforcement of a money judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
