# CCP § 357

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Provisions as to the Time of Commencing Actions

**Citation:** CCP § 357

---

## Text

No person can avail himself of a disability, unless it existed when his right of action accrued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
