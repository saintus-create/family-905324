# CCP § 689.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 9. Enforcement of Support Judgments

**Citation:** CCP § 689.010

---

## Text

For the purpose of the remedies provided under this chapter, jurisdiction is conferred upon the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 957, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1997.)
