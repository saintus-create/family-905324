# CCP § 1077

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 1. Writ of Review [[1067.] - 1077.]

**Citation:** CCP § 1077

---

## Text

A copy of the judgment, signed by the Clerk, entered upon or attached to the writ and return, constitute the judgment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
