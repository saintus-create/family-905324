# CCP § 708.190

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Creditors’ Remedies > Article: 2. Examination Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 708.190

---

## Text

The court may permit a person claiming an interest in the property or debt sought to be applied in an examination proceeding to intervene in the proceeding and may determine the person’s rights in the property or debt pursuant to Section 708.180.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
