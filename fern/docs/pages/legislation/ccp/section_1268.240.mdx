# CCP § 1268.240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 11. Postjudgment Procedure > Article: 3. Possession After Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 1268.240

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the right of a public entity to exercise its police power in emergency situations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
