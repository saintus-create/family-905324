# CCP § 116.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.5. Small Claims Court > Article: 2. Small Claims Court

**Citation:** CCP § 116.210

---

## Text

In each superior court there shall be a small claims division. The small claims division may be known as the small claims court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 2003.)
