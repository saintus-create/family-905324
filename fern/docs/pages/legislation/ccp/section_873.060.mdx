# CCP § 873.060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Referees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 873.060

---

## Text

The referee may perform any acts necessary to exercise the authority conferred by this title or by order of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
