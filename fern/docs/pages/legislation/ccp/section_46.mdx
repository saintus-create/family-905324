# CCP § 46

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 3. Supreme Court

**Citation:** CCP § 46

---

## Text

Appeals in actions brought under Section 1094.9 shall be given preference in scheduling for hearing in the courts of appeal, and in the Supreme Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 527, Sec. 1. (SB 808) Effective January 1, 2026.)
