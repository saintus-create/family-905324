# CCP § 422.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. OF THE PLEADINGS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. The Pleadings in General

**Citation:** CCP § 422.10

---

## Text

The pleadings allowed in civil actions are complaints, demurrers, answers, and cross-complaints.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 244.)
