# CCP § 1265.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 10. Divided Interests > Article: 3. Encumbrances

**Citation:** CCP § 1265.210

---

## Text

As used in this article, “lien” means a mortgage, deed of trust, or other security interest in property whether arising from contract, statute, common law, or equity.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
