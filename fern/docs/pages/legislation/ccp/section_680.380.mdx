# CCP § 680.380

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.380

---

## Text

“Writ” includes a writ of execution, a writ of possession of personal property, a writ of possession of real property, and a writ of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
