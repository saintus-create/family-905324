# CCP § 1933

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE KINDS AND DEGREES OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Writings > Article: 3. Private Writings

**Citation:** CCP § 1933

---

## Text

The execution of an instrument is the subscribing and delivering it, with or without affixing a seal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
