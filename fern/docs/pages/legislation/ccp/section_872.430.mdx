# CCP § 872.430

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2. Commencement of Action > Article: 3. Answer

**Citation:** CCP § 872.430

---

## Text

The answer may set forth any claim the defendant has for contribution or other compensatory adjustment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
