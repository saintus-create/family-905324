# CCP § 432.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. OF THE PLEADINGS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Objections to Pleadings; Denials and Defenses > Article: 3. Time to Respond to Cross-Complaint

**Citation:** CCP § 432.10

---

## Text

A party served with a cross-complaint may within 30 days after service move, demur, or otherwise plead to the cross-complaint in the same manner as to an original complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 244.)
