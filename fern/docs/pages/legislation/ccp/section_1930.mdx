# CCP § 1930

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE KINDS AND DEGREES OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Writings > Article: 3. Private Writings

**Citation:** CCP § 1930

---

## Text

A seal is a particular sign, made to attest, in the most formal manner, the execution of an instrument.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
