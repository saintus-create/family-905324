# CCP § 873.910

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 7. Partition by Appraisal

**Citation:** CCP § 873.910

---

## Text

When the interests of all parties are undisputed or have been adjudicated, the parties may agree upon a partition by appraisal pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
