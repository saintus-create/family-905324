# CCP § 1297.181

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 5. Manner and Conduct of Arbitration > Article: 1. Equal Treatment of Parties

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.181

---

## Text

The parties shall be treated with equality and each party shall be given a full opportunity to present his or her case.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
