# CCP § 1170.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Summary Proceedings for Obtaining Possession of Real Property in Certain Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 1170.9

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall adopt rules, not inconsistent with statute, prescribing the time for filing and serving opposition and reply papers, if any, relating to a motion under Section 1167.4, 1170.7, or 1170.8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 113, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
