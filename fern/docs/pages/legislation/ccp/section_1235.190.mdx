# CCP § 1235.190

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 2. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.190

---

## Text

“Public entity” includes the state, a county, city, district, public authority, public agency, and any other political subdivision in the state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
