# CCP § 41

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 3. Supreme Court

**Citation:** CCP § 41

---

## Text

The Supreme Court and the courts of appeal may transact business at any time.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 61.4 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 17.)
