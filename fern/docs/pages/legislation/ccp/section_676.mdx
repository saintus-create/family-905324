# CCP § 676

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8.5. UNIFORM FOREIGN-MONEY CLAIMS ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 676

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Uniform Foreign-Money Claims Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 932, Sec. 1.)
