# CCP § 1132

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Confession of Judgment Without Action

**Citation:** CCP § 1132

---

## Text

(a) A judgment by confession is unenforceable and may not be entered in any superior court.

(b) This section does not apply to a judgment by confession obtained or entered before January 1, 2023.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 851, Sec. 1. (SB 688) Effective January 1, 2023.)
