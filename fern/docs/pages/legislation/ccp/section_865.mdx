# CCP § 865

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 9. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 865

---

## Text

If more than one action is pending concerning similar contests which may be brought under this chapter, they shall be consolidated for trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1479.)
