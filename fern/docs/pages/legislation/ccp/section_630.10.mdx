# CCP § 630.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4.5. Voluntary Expedited Jury Trials

**Citation:** CCP § 630.10

---

## Text

All statutes and rules governing costs and attorney’s fees shall apply in expedited jury trials, unless the parties agree otherwise in the consent order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 674, Sec. 2. (AB 2284) Effective January 1, 2011.)
