# CCP § 230

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PERSONS SPECIALLY INVESTED WITH POWERS OF A JUDICIAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Trial Jury Selection and Management Act

**Citation:** CCP § 230

---

## Text

Challenges for cause shall be tried by the court. The juror challenged and any other person may be examined as a witness in the trial of the challenge, and shall truthfully answer all questions propounded to them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1245, Sec. 2.)
