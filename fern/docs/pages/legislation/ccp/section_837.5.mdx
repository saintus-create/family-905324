# CCP § 837.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 7. Actions Relating to Groundwater Rights > Article: 4. Intervention

**Citation:** CCP § 837.5

---

## Text

(a) The state may intervene in a comprehensive adjudication conducted pursuant to this chapter.

(b) This section does not affect substantive law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 676, Sec. 1. (SB 226) Effective January 1, 2016.)
