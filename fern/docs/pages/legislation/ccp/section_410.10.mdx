# CCP § 410.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. JURISDICTION AND SERVICE OF PROCESS > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and Forum > Article: 1. Jurisdiction

**Citation:** CCP § 410.10

---

## Text

A court of this state may exercise jurisdiction on any basis not inconsistent with the Constitution of this state or of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1610.)
