# CCP § 511.090

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 511.090

---

## Text

A claim has “probable validity” where it is more likely than not that the plaintiff will obtain a judgment against the defendant on that claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 526.)
