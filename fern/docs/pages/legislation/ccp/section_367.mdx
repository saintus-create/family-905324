# CCP § 367

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF THE PARTIES TO CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 367

---

## Text

Every action must be prosecuted in the name of the real party in interest, except as otherwise provided by statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993.)
