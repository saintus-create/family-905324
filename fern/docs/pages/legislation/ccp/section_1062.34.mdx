# CCP § 1062.34

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 11. Foster Family Agency Accountability

**Citation:** CCP § 1062.34

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 403, Sec. 1. (AB 2496) Effective September 22, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 11, commencing with Section 1062.30.)
