# CCP § 872.640

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Trial > Article: 1. Determination of Interests of Parties

**Citation:** CCP § 872.640

---

## Text

Where two or more parties are unknown, the court may consider their interests together in the action and not as between each other.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
