# CCP § 1614

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 8. Property in Custody of Federal Officers, Agencies, and Departments

**Citation:** CCP § 1614

---

## Text

Property received under this chapter shall be deposited or sold by the State Controller as though received under Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 1500) of this title. Property received under this chapter shall not be subject to claim within two years following the date upon which it is paid to or received by the state. Thereafter, claims shall be made in the manner provided in Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 1500) of this title.
