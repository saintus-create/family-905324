# CCP § 74

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 4. Superior Courts

**Citation:** CCP § 74

---

## Text

Adjournments from day to day, or from time to time, are to be construed as recesses in the sessions, and shall not prevent the Court from sitting at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Code Amendments 1880, Ch. 35.)
