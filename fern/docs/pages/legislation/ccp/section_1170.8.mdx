# CCP § 1170.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Summary Proceedings for Obtaining Possession of Real Property in Certain Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 1170.8

---

## Text

In any action under this chapter, a discovery motion may be made at any time upon giving five days’ notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 113, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
