# CCP § 1297.165

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 4. Jurisdiction of Arbitral Tribunals > Article: 1. Competence of an Arbitral Tribunal to Rule on its Jurisdiction

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.165

---

## Text

The arbitral tribunal may rule on a plea referred to in Sections 1297.162 and 1297.163 either as a preliminary question or in an award on the merits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
