# CCP § 872.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 872.030

---

## Text

The statutes and rules governing practice in civil actions generally apply to actions under this title except where they are inconsistent with the provisions of this title.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
