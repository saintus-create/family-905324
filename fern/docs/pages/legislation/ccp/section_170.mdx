# CCP § 170

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. JUDICIAL OFFICERS > Chapter: 3. Disqualifications of Judges

**Citation:** CCP § 170

---

## Text

A judge has a duty to decide any proceeding in which he or she is not disqualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1555, Sec. 2.)
