# CCP § 995.370

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 3. Execution and Filing

**Citation:** CCP § 995.370

---

## Text

At the time a bond is given, the principal shall serve a copy of the bond on the beneficiary. An affidavit of service shall be given and filed with the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
