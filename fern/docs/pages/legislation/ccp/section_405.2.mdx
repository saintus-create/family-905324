# CCP § 405.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. RECORDING NOTICE OF CERTAIN ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 405.2

---

## Text

“Notice of pendency of action” or “notice” means a notice of the pendency of an action in which a real property claim is alleged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 883, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
