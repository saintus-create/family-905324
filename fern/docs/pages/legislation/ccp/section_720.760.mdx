# CCP § 720.760

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 7. Undertakings

**Citation:** CCP § 720.760

---

## Text

A copy of a notice of motion objecting to an undertaking shall be filed with the levying officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 18, Sec. 14. Effective April 21, 1983. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 40 of Ch. 18.)
