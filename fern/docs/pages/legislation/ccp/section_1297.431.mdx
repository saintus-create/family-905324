# CCP § 1297.431

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 7. Conciliation > Article: 10. Immunity of Conciliators and Parties

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.431

---

## Text

Neither the conciliator or conciliators, the parties, nor their representatives shall be subject to service of process on any civil matter while they are present in this state for the purpose of arranging for or participating in conciliation pursuant to this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
