# CCP § 32.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 32.5

---

## Text

The “jurisdictional classification” of a case means its classification as a limited civil case or an unlimited civil case.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2003.)
