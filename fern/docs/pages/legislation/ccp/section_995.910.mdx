# CCP § 995.910

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 9. Objections to Bonds

**Citation:** CCP § 995.910

---

## Text

This article governs objections to a bond given in an action or proceeding.
