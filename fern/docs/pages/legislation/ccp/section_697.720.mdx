# CCP § 697.720

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 2. Liens > Article: 4. Execution Lien

**Citation:** CCP § 697.720

---

## Text

Subject to Section 701.630, if an interest in real property subject to an execution lien is transferred or encumbered, the interest transferred or encumbered remains subject to the lien after the transfer or encumbrance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
