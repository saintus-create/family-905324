# CCP § 133

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 6. General Provisions Respecting Courts of Justice > Article: 3. Judicial Holidays

**Citation:** CCP § 133

---

## Text

Courts of justice may be held and judicial business transacted on any day, except as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1450, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1989, by Sec. 6 of Ch. 1450.)
