# CCP § 704.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 3. Exempt Property

**Citation:** CCP § 704.040

---

## Text

Jewelry, heirlooms, and works of art are exempt to the extent that the aggregate equity therein does not exceed eight thousand seven hundred twenty-five dollars ($8,725).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 81, Sec. 4. (SB 898) Effective January 1, 2021.)
