# CCP § 704.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 3. Exempt Property

**Citation:** CCP § 704.210

---

## Text

Property that is not subject to enforcement of a money judgment is exempt without making a claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
