# CCP § 708.910

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Creditors’ Remedies > Article: 9. Enforcement Against Franchise

**Citation:** CCP § 708.910

---

## Text

As used in this article, “franchise” means a franchise granted by a public entity and all the rights and privileges thereof, other than the franchise of being a corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
