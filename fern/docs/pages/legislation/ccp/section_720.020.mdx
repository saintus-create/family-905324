# CCP § 720.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 720.020

---

## Text

“Creditor” means the judgment creditor or, in the case of a levy under a writ of attachment or prejudgment writ of possession of personal property, the plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
