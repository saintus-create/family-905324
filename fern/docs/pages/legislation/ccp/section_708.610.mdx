# CCP § 708.610

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Creditors’ Remedies > Article: 7. Receiver to Enforce Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 708.610

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 564) and Chapter 5a (commencing with Section 571) of Title 7 govern the appointment, qualifications, powers, rights, and duties of a receiver appointed under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
