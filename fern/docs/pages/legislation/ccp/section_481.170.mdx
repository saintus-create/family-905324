# CCP § 481.170

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.170

---

## Text

“Person” includes a natural person, a corporation, a partnership or other unincorporated association, a limited liability company, and a public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 1995.)
