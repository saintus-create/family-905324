# CCP § 1235.130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 2. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.130

---

## Text

“Judgment” means the judgment determining the right to take the property by eminent domain and fixing the amount of compensation to be paid by the plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
