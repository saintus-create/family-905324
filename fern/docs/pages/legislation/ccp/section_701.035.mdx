# CCP § 701.035

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 3. Execution > Article: 5. Duties and Liabilities of Third Persons After Levy

**Citation:** CCP § 701.035

---

## Text

A third person who gives a garnishee’s memorandum pursuant to this title is not liable to any person for the disclosure in the garnishee’s memorandum of any information contained in the garnishee’s memorandum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 155, Sec. 14.6. Effective June 30, 1983. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 32 of Ch. 155.)
