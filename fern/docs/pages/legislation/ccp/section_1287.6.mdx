# CCP § 1287.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 4. Enforcement of the Award > Article: 1. Confirmation, Correction or Vacation of the Award

**Citation:** CCP § 1287.6

---

## Text

An award that has not been confirmed or vacated has the same force and effect as a contract in writing between the parties to the arbitration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
