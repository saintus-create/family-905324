# CCP § 642

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 6. Of References and Trials by Referees

**Citation:** CCP § 642

---

## Text

Objections, if any, to a reference or to the referee or referees appointed by the court shall be made in writing, and must be heard and disposed of by the court, not by the referee.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 644, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
