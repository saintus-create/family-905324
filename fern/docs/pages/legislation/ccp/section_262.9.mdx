# CCP § 262.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. MINISTERIAL OFFICERS OF COURTS OF JUSTICE > Chapter: 1. Of Ministerial Officers Generally

**Citation:** CCP § 262.9

---

## Text

When process is delivered to an elisor, he shall execute and return it in the same manner as the sheriff is required to execute similar process.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
