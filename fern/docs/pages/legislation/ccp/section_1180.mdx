# CCP § 1180

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. OF THE ENFORCEMENT OF LIENS > Chapter: 1. Liens in General

**Citation:** CCP § 1180

---

## Text

A lien is a charge imposed upon specific property, by which it is made security for the performance of an act.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
