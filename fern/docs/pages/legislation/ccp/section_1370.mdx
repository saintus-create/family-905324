# CCP § 1370

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Management of Unclaimed Property > Article: 3. Sale or Disposal of Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1370

---

## Text

The Controller may sell or lease personal property at any time, and in any manner, and may execute those leases on behalf and in the name of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 13. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
