# CCP § 100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.1. Limited Civil Cases > Article: 2. Economic Litigation for Limited Civil Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 100

---

## Text

Any party shall have the right to appeal any judgment or final order consistent with the law governing appeals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1581, Sec. 1.)
