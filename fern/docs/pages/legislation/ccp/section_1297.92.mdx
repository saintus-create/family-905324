# CCP § 1297.92

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 2. Arbitration Agreements and Judicial Measures in Aid of Arbitration > Article: 3. Court-Ordered Interim Measures

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.92

---

## Text

Any party to an arbitration governed by this title may request the superior court to enforce an interim measure of protection granted by an arbitral tribunal pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 1297.171) of Chapter 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 90, Sec. 8. (AB 1903) Effective January 1, 2025.)
