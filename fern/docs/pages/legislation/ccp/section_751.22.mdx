# CCP § 751.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3.5. Actions to Re-establish Destroyed Land Records

**Citation:** CCP § 751.22

---

## Text

The remedies provided for by this chapter are cumulative and in addition to any other remedy provided by law for quieting or establishing title to real property.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 52.)
