# CCP § 873.600

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 6. Sale of the Property > Article: 2. Sales Procedures

**Citation:** CCP § 873.600

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this title, the court shall order sale by such methods and upon such terms as are expressly agreed to in writing by all the parties to the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
