# CCP § 904.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. APPEALS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Appeals in General

**Citation:** CCP § 904.5

---

## Text

Appeals from the small claims division of a superior court shall be governed by the Small Claims Act (Chapter 5.5 (commencing with Section 116.110) of Title 1 of Part 1).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 70. Effective January 1, 2003.)
