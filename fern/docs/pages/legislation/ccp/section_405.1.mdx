# CCP § 405.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. RECORDING NOTICE OF CERTAIN ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 405.1

---

## Text

“Claimant” means a party to an action who asserts a real property claim and records a notice of the pendency of the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 883, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
