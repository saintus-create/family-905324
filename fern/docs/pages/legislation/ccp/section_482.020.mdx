# CCP § 482.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 482.020

---

## Text

Nothing in this title precludes the granting of relief pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 525) of Title 7.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1516.)
