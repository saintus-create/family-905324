# CCP § 511.050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 511.050

---

## Text

“Inventory” means tangible personal property in the possession of a defendant who holds it for sale or lease or to be furnished under contracts of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 526.)
