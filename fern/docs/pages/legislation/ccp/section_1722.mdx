# CCP § 1722

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. MONEY JUDGMENTS OF OTHER JURISDICTIONS > Chapter: 2. Foreign-Country Money Judgments

**Citation:** CCP § 1722

---

## Text

In applying and construing this uniform act, consideration shall be given to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among states that enact it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 212, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
