# CCP § 995.230

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 995.230

---

## Text

The beneficiary of a bond given in an action or proceeding may in writing consent to the bond in an amount less than the amount required by statute or may waive the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
