# CCP § 1245.360

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 4. Precondemnation Activities > Article: 3. Resolution Consenting to Eminent Domain Proceeding by Quasi-Public Entity

**Citation:** CCP § 1245.360

---

## Text

The resolution required by this article shall be adopted by a vote of two-thirds of all the members of the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
