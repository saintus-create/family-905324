# CCP § 430.60

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. OF THE PLEADINGS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Objections to Pleadings; Denials and Defenses > Article: 1. Objections to Pleadings

**Citation:** CCP § 430.60

---

## Text

A demurrer shall distinctly specify the grounds upon which any of the objections to the complaint, cross-complaint, or answer are taken. Unless it does so, it may be disregarded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 244.)
