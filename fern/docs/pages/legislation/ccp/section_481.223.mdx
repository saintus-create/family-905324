# CCP § 481.223

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.223

---

## Text

“Security interest” means “security interest” as defined in Section 1201 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 20. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
