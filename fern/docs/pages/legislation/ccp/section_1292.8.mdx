# CCP § 1292.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 5. General Provisions Relating toJudicial Proceedings > Article: 2. Venue, Jurisdiction and Costs

**Citation:** CCP § 1292.8

---

## Text

A motion for a stay of an action on the ground that an issue therein is subject to arbitration shall be made in the court where the action is pending.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
