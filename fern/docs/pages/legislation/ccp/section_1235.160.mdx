# CCP § 1235.160

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 2. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.160

---

## Text

“Person” includes any public entity, individual, association, organization, partnership, trust, limited liability company, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 63. Effective January 1, 1995.)
