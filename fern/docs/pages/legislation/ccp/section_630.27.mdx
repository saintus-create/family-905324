# CCP § 630.27

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4.6. Mandatory Expedited Jury Trials in Limited Civil Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 630.27

---

## Text

All statutes and rules governing costs and attorney’s fees shall apply in limited civil cases that are conducted as mandatory expedited jury trials, unless the parties stipulate otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 330, Sec. 5. (AB 555) Effective January 1, 2016. Section operative July 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 630.29.)
