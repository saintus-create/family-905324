# CCP § 690.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 10. Enforcement of Judgments by Labor Commissioner

**Citation:** CCP § 690.020

---

## Text

For the purpose of the remedies provided under this chapter, jurisdiction is conferred upon the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 803, Sec. 1. (SB 588) Effective January 1, 2016.)
