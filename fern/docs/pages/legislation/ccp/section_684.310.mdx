# CCP § 684.310

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Manner of Service of Writs, Notices, and Other Papers > Article: 4. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** CCP § 684.310

---

## Text

Except for Sections 684.130 and 684.140, the provisions of Article 1 (commencing with Section 684.010) and Article 2 (commencing with Section 684.110) do not apply to service under Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 706.010) of Division 2 (wage garnishment).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
