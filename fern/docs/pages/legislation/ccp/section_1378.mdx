# CCP § 1378

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Management of Unclaimed Property > Article: 3. Sale or Disposal of Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1378

---

## Text

No suit shall be maintained by any person against the State or any officer thereof, for or on account of any transaction entered into by the Controller pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1708.)
