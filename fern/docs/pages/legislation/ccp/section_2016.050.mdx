# CCP § 2016.050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 2016.050

---

## Text

Sections 1011 and 1013 apply to any method of discovery or service of a motion provided for in this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 64, Sec. 2. (SB 543) Effective January 1, 2018.)
