# CCP § 720.420

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Hearing on Third-Party Claim

**Citation:** CCP § 720.420

---

## Text

An appeal may be taken from a judgment given pursuant to Section 720.390.
