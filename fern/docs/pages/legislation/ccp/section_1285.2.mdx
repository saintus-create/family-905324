# CCP § 1285.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 4. Enforcement of the Award > Article: 1. Confirmation, Correction or Vacation of the Award

**Citation:** CCP § 1285.2

---

## Text

A response to a petition under this chapter may request the court to dismiss the petition or to confirm, correct or vacate the award.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
