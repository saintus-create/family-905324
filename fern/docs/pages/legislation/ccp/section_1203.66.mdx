# CCP § 1203.66

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. OF THE ENFORCEMENT OF LIENS > Chapter: 2.5. Oil and Gas Liens

**Citation:** CCP § 1203.66

---

## Text

This chapter shall be given liberal construction in favor of all persons entitled to any lien under it.
