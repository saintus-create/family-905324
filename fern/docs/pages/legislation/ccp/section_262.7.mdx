# CCP § 262.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. MINISTERIAL OFFICERS OF COURTS OF JUSTICE > Chapter: 1. Of Ministerial Officers Generally

**Citation:** CCP § 262.7

---

## Text

If an action is begun against a sheriff, all process and orders may be served by any person in the manner provided in this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 470, Sec. 1. (AB 2256) Effective January 1, 2015.)
