# CCP § 1297.333

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 6. Making of Arbitral Award and Termination of Proceedings > Article: 6. Correction and Interpretation of Awards and Additional Awards

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.333

---

## Text

The arbitral tribunal may correct any error of the type referred to in subdivision (a) of Section 1297.331, on its own initiative, within 30 days after the date of the arbitral award.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
