# CCP § 686.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 6. Enforcement After Death of Judgment Creditor or Judgment Debtor

**Citation:** CCP § 686.010

---

## Text

After the death of the judgment creditor, the judgment may be enforced as provided in this title by the judgment creditor’s executor or administrator or successor in interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
