# CCP § 751.19

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3.5. Actions to Re-establish Destroyed Land Records

**Citation:** CCP § 751.19

---

## Text

The clerk shall number all actions authorized by this chapter consecutively in a distinct series and shall keep an index and register devoted exclusively to such actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 52.)
