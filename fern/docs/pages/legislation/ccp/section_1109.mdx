# CCP § 1109

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 5. Rules of Practice and Appeals

**Citation:** CCP § 1109

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this Title, the provisions of Part II of the Code are applicable to and constitute the rules of practice in the proceedings mentioned in this Title.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
