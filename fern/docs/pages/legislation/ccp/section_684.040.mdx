# CCP § 684.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Manner of Service of Writs, Notices, and Other Papers > Article: 1. Service on Attorney of Creditor or Debtor

**Citation:** CCP § 684.040

---

## Text

If service on an attorney is required under this article, service on the attorney shall be made in any of the following ways:

(a) By personal delivery to the attorney.

(b) By service in the manner provided in subdivision (1) of Section 1011.

(c) By mail in the manner provided in Section 684.120.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
