# CCP § 1896

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE KINDS AND DEGREES OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Writings > Article: 2. Public Writings

**Citation:** CCP § 1896

---

## Text

A written law is that which is promulgated in writing, and of which a record is in existence.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
