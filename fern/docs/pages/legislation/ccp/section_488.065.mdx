# CCP § 488.065

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 8. Levy Procedures; Lien of Attachment; Management and Disposition of Attached Property > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 488.065

---

## Text

A copy of the original notice of attachment which has been served upon a third party holding the property sought to be attached, if served upon the defendant or any other party, shall suffice as the notice of attachment to that person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 759, Sec. 1.)
