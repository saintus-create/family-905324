# CCP § 859

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 8. Actions Against Coowners of Mines

**Citation:** CCP § 859

---

## Text

A certified copy of the decree may be recorded in the office of the recorder of each county in which any part of the mine or mining claim is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 856 by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 38. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
