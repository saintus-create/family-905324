# CCP § 348.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. The Time of Commencing Actions Other Than for the Recovery of Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 348.5

---

## Text

An action upon any bonds or coupons issued by the State of California shall have no limitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 719, Sec. 8. (SB 856) Effective October 19, 2010.)
