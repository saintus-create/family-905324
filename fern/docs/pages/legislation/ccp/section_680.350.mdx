# CCP § 680.350

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.350

---

## Text

“Security agreement” means “security agreement” as defined in paragraph (74) of subdivision (a) of Section 9102 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 531, Sec. 5. (AB 502) Effective January 1, 2014. Operative July 1, 2014, by Sec. 28 of Ch. 531.)
