# CCP § 1240.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 3. The Right to Take > Article: 1. General Limitations on Exercise of Power of Eminent Domain

**Citation:** CCP § 1240.040

---

## Text

A public entity may exercise the power of eminent domain only if it has adopted a resolution of necessity that meets the requirements of Article 2 (commencing with Section 1245.210) of Chapter 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
