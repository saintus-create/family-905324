# CCP § 700.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 3. Execution > Article: 4. Methods of Levy

**Citation:** CCP § 700.030

---

## Text

Unless another method of levy is provided by this article, to levy upon tangible personal property in the possession or under the control of the judgment debtor, the levying officer shall take the property into custody.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
