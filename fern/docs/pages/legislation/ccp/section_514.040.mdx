# CCP § 514.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 4. Levy and Custody

**Citation:** CCP § 514.040

---

## Text

The levying officer shall return the writ of possession, with his proceedings thereon, to the court in which the action is pending within 30 days after levy but in no event more than 60 days after the writ is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 526.)
