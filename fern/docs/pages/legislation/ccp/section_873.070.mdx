# CCP § 873.070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Referees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 873.070

---

## Text

The referee or any party may, on noticed motion, petition the court for instructions concerning the referee’s duties under this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
