# CCP § 1053

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 1053

---

## Text

When there are three referees all must meet, but two of them may do any act which might be done by all.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
