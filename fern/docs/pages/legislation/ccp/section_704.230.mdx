# CCP § 704.230

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 3. Exempt Property

**Citation:** CCP § 704.230

---

## Text

Money provided to the judgment debtor by the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) is exempt without making a claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 552, Sec. 10. (SB 616) Effective January 1, 2020.)
