# CCP § 684.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Manner of Service of Writs, Notices, and Other Papers > Article: 3. Proof of Service

**Citation:** CCP § 684.210

---

## Text

If service of notice of a court hearing is required under this title, proof of service of the notice shall be made at or before the hearing to the satisfaction of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
