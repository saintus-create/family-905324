# CCP § 470

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. OF THE PLEADINGS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 8. Variance—Mistakes in Pleadings and Amendments

**Citation:** CCP § 470

---

## Text

Where the variance is not material, as provided in Section 469 the court may direct the fact to be found according to the evidence, or may order an immediate amendment, without costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 540, Sec. 4.)
