# CCP § 1235.200

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 2. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.200

---

## Text

“State” means the State of California and includes the Regents of the University of California.
