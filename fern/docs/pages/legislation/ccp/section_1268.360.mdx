# CCP § 1268.360

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 11. Postjudgment Procedure > Article: 4. Interest

**Citation:** CCP § 1268.360

---

## Text

The interest payable for each calendar quarter shall draw interest, computed as prescribed by Section 1268.350, in each succeeding calendar quarter for which interest is due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 311, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
