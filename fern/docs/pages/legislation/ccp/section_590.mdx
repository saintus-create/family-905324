# CCP § 590

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Issues—The Mode of Trial and Postponements

**Citation:** CCP § 590

---

## Text

An issue of fact arises:

1. Upon a material allegation in the complaint controverted by the answer; and,

2. Upon new matters in the answer, except an issue of law is joined thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
