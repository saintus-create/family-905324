# CCP § 871.12

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 11. Actions to Recover COVID-19 Rental Debt

**Citation:** CCP § 871.12

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect until October 1, 2027, and as of the date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 27, Sec. 10. (AB 832) Effective June 28, 2021. Repealed as of October 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 11, commencing with Section 871.10.)
