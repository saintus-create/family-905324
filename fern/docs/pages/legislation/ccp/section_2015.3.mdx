# CCP § 2015.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF THE PRODUCTION OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Manner of Production > Article: 2. Affidavits

**Citation:** CCP § 2015.3

---

## Text

The certificate of a sheriff, marshal, or the clerk of the superior court, has the same force and effect as his or her affidavit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 88. Effective January 1, 2003.)
