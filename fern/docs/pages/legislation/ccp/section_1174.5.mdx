# CCP § 1174.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Summary Proceedings for Obtaining Possession of Real Property in Certain Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 1174.5

---

## Text

A judgment in unlawful detainer declaring the forfeiture of the lease or agreement under which real property is held shall not relieve the lessee from liability pursuant to Section 1951.2 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 488, Sec. 1.)
