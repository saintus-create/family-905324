# CCP § 699.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 3. Execution > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 699.010

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, this chapter governs enforcement of a money judgment by a writ of execution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
