# CCP § 186

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. JUDICIAL OFFICERS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions Respecting Courts of Justice

**Citation:** CCP § 186

---

## Text

Such abbreviations as are in common use may be used, and numbers may be expressed by figures or numerals in the customary manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Code Amendments 1880, Ch. 35.)
