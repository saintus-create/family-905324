# CCP § 481.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.210

---

## Text

“Security” means a “security” as defined by Section 8102 of the Commercial Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1516.)
