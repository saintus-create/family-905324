# CCP § 697.910

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 2. Liens > Article: 5. Other Liens Created by Enforcement Process

**Citation:** CCP § 697.910

---

## Text

This article applies to liens created by any of the following:

(a) An examination proceeding as provided in Section 708.110, 708.120, or 708.205.

(b) A creditor’s suit as provided in Section 708.250.

(c) A charging order as provided in Section 708.320.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
