# CCP § 1170.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Summary Proceedings for Obtaining Possession of Real Property in Certain Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 1170.7

---

## Text

A motion for summary judgment may be made at any time after the answer is filed upon giving five days notice. Summary judgment shall be granted or denied on the same basis as a motion under Section 437c.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1620, Sec. 3.)
