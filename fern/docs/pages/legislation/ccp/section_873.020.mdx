# CCP § 873.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Referees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 873.020

---

## Text

The court in its discretion may appoint a referee for sale and a referee for division, or may appoint a single referee for both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
