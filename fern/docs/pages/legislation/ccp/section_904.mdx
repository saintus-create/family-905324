# CCP § 904

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. APPEALS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Appeals in General

**Citation:** CCP § 904

---

## Text

An appeal may be taken in a civil action or proceeding as provided in Sections 904.1, 904.2, 904.3, and 904.5.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 263, Sec. 9.5. Effective January 1, 2008.)
