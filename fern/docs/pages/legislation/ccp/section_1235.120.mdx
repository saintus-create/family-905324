# CCP § 1235.120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 2. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.120

---

## Text

“Final judgment” means a judgment with respect to which all possibility of direct attack by way of appeal, motion for a new trial, or motion under Section 663 to vacate the judgment has been exhausted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
