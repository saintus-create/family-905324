# CCP § 343

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. The Time of Commencing Actions Other Than for the Recovery of Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 343

---

## Text

An action for relief not hereinbefore provided for must be commenced within four years after the cause of action shall have accrued.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
