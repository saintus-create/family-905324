# CCP § 704.830

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 4. Homestead Exemption

**Citation:** CCP § 704.830

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 703.590 and 703.600 apply to proceedings under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
