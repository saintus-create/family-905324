# CCP § 873.270

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 5. Division of the Property

**Citation:** CCP § 873.270

---

## Text

Where the court has determined the combined interests of two or more unknown parties, the entire portion of the property allocated to such parties shall remain undivided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
