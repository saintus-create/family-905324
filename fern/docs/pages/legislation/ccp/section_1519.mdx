# CCP § 1519

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 7. Unclaimed Property Law > Article: 2. Escheat of Unclaimed Personal Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1519

---

## Text

All tangible personal property located in this state, and, subject to Section 1510, all intangible personal property, held for the owner by any government or governmental subdivision or agency, that has remained unclaimed by the owner for more than three years escheats to this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 450, Sec. 10. Effective July 31, 1990.)
