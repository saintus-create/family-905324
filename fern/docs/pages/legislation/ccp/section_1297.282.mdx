# CCP § 1297.282

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 6. Making of Arbitral Award and Termination of Proceedings > Article: 1. Rules Applicable to Substance of Dispute

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.282

---

## Text

Any designation by the parties of the law or legal system of a given state shall be construed, unless otherwise expressed, as directly referring to the substantive law of that state and not to its conflict of laws rules.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
