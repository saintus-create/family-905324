# CCP § 481.175

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.175

---

## Text

“Personal property” includes both tangible and intangible personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 15. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
