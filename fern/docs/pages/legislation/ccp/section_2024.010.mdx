# CCP § 2024.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 8. Time for Completion of Discovery

**Citation:** CCP § 2024.010

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, discovery is considered completed on the day a response is due or on the day a deposition begins.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
