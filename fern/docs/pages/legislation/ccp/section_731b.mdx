# CCP § 731b

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 2. Actions for Nuisance, Waste,and Willful Trespass, in Certain Cases,on Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 731b

---

## Text

In any action or proceeding to abate the use of an airport or an airpark, proof that the airport or airpark has been in existence for three years constitutes a rebuttable presumption which shall be prima facie evidence that the operation of the airport or airpark does not constitute a nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 52.)
