# CCP § 995.920

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 9. Objections to Bonds

**Citation:** CCP § 995.920

---

## Text

The beneficiary may object to a bond on any of the following grounds:

(a) The sureties are insufficient.

(b) The amount of the bond is insufficient.

(c) The bond, from any other cause, is insufficient.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
