# CCP § 701.630

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 3. Execution > Article: 6. Sale and Collection

**Citation:** CCP § 701.630

---

## Text

If property is sold pursuant to this article, the lien under which it is sold, any liens subordinate thereto, and any state tax lien (as defined in Section 7162 of the Government Code) on the property sold are extinguished.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
