# CCP § 872.140

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Powers of Court

**Citation:** CCP § 872.140

---

## Text

The court may, in all cases, order allowance, accounting, contribution, or other compensatory adjustment among the parties according to the principles of equity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
