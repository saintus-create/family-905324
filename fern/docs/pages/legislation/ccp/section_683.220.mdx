# CCP § 683.220

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 3. Period for Enforcement and Renewal of Judgments > Article: 2. Renewal of Judgments

**Citation:** CCP § 683.220

---

## Text

If a judgment is renewed pursuant to this article, the date of the filing of the application for renewal shall be deemed to be the date that the period for commencing an action on the renewed judgment commences to run under Section 337.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
