# CCP § 724.080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Satisfaction of Judgment

**Citation:** CCP § 724.080

---

## Text

In an action or proceeding maintained pursuant to this chapter, the court shall award reasonable attorney’s fees to the prevailing party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
