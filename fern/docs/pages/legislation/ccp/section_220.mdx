# CCP § 220

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PERSONS SPECIALLY INVESTED WITH POWERS OF A JUDICIAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Trial Jury Selection and Management Act

**Citation:** CCP § 220

---

## Text

A trial jury shall consist of 12 persons, except that in civil actions and cases of misdemeanor, it may consist of 12 or any number less than 12, upon which the parties may agree.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1245, Sec. 2.)
