# CCP § 871.28

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 12. Actions for Restitution for or Replacement of Certain Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** CCP § 871.28

---

## Text

The duties and obligations imposed by this chapter are cumulative with duties or obligations imposed under any other law and shall not be construed to relieve any party from any duties or obligations imposed under any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 938, Sec. 1. (AB 1755) Effective January 1, 2025.)
