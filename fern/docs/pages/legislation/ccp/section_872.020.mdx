# CCP § 872.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 872.020

---

## Text

This title governs actions for partition of real property and, except to the extent not applicable, actions for partition of personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 82, Sec. 1. (AB 2245) Effective January 1, 2023.)
