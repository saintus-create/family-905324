# CCP § 1268.311

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 11. Postjudgment Procedure > Article: 4. Interest

**Citation:** CCP § 1268.311

---

## Text

In any inverse condemnation proceeding in which interest is awarded, the interest shall be computed as prescribed by Section 1268.350.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1372, Sec. 2.)
