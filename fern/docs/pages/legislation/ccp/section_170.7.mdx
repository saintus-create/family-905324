# CCP § 170.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. JUDICIAL OFFICERS > Chapter: 3. Disqualifications of Judges

**Citation:** CCP § 170.7

---

## Text

Section 170.6 does not apply to a judge designated or assigned to serve on the appellate division of a superior court in the judge’s capacity as a judge of that division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 48. Effective September 28, 1998.)
