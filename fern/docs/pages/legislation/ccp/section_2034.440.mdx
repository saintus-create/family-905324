# CCP § 2034.440

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 18. Simultaneous Exchange of Expert Witness Information > Article: 3. Deposition of Expert Witness

**Citation:** CCP § 2034.440

---

## Text

The party designating an expert is responsible for any fee charged by the expert for preparing for a deposition and for traveling to the place of the deposition, as well as for any travel expenses of the expert.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
