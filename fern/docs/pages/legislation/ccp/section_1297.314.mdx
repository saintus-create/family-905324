# CCP § 1297.314

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 6. Making of Arbitral Award and Termination of Proceedings > Article: 4. Form and Content of Arbitral Award

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.314

---

## Text

The arbitral award shall state its date and the place of arbitration as determined in accordance with Article 3 (commencing with Section 1297.201) of Chapter 5 and the award shall be deemed to have been made at that place.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
