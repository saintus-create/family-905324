# CCP § 493.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 13. Effect of Bankruptcy Proceedings and General Assignments for the Benefit of Creditors

**Citation:** CCP § 493.020

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this title, the defendant may make a general assignment for the benefit of creditors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 499.)
