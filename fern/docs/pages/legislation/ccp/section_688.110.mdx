# CCP § 688.110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 8. Enforcement of State Tax Liability > Article: 2. Enforcement of Judgment for Taxes

**Citation:** CCP § 688.110

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, if a judgment is entered on a claim for taxes by a public entity, the judgment is enforceable pursuant to this title in the same manner as any other money judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
