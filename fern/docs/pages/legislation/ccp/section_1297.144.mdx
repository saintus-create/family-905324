# CCP § 1297.144

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 3. Composition of Arbitral Tribunals > Article: 5. Failure or Impossibility to Act

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.144

---

## Text

If, under this section or Section 1297.132, an arbitrator withdraws from office or a party agrees to the termination of the mandate of an arbitrator, this does not imply acceptance of the validity of any ground referred to in Section 1297.132.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
