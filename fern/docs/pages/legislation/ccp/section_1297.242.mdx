# CCP § 1297.242

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 5. Manner and Conduct of Arbitration > Article: 7. Hearings and Written Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.242

---

## Text

Unless the parties have agreed that no oral hearings shall be held, the arbitral tribunal shall hold oral hearings at an appropriate state of the proceedings, if so requested by a party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
