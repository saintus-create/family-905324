# CCP § 1260.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 8. Procedures for Determining Right to Take and Compensation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 1260.010

---

## Text

Proceedings under this title take precedence over all other civil actions in the matter of setting the same for hearing or trial in order that such proceedings shall be quickly heard and determined.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
