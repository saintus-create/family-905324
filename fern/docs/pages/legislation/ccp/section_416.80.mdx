# CCP § 416.80

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. JURISDICTION AND SERVICE OF PROCESS > Chapter: 4. Service of Summons > Article: 4. Persons Upon Whom Summons May Be Served

**Citation:** CCP § 416.80

---

## Text

When authorized by Section 12 of the Elections Code, a summons may be served as provided by that section.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 140, Sec. 38. (AB 1164) Effective January 1, 2010.)
