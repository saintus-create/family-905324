# CCP § 1297.101

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 3. Composition of Arbitral Tribunals > Article: 1. Number of Arbitrators

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.101

---

## Text

The parties may agree on the number of arbitrators. Otherwise, there shall be one arbitrator.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
