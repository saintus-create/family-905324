# CCP § 872.610

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Trial > Article: 1. Determination of Interests of Parties

**Citation:** CCP § 872.610

---

## Text

The interests of the parties, plaintiff as well as defendant, may be put in issue, tried, and determined in the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
