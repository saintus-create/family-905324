# CCP § 491.200

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 11. Attaching Plaintiff’s Miscellaneous Remedies > Article: 1. Examination of Third Person

**Citation:** CCP § 491.200

---

## Text

A lien created under this article continues on property subject to the lien notwithstanding the transfer or encumbrance of the property subject to the lien unless the transfer or encumbrance is made to a person listed in Section 697.740.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 57. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
