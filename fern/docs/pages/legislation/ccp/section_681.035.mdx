# CCP § 681.035

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 681.035

---

## Text

The California Law Revision Commission is authorized to maintain a continuing review of and submit recommendations to the Governor and the Legislature concerning enforcement of judgments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 15, Sec. 1. (AB 567) Effective January 1, 2014.)
