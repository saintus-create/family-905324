# CCP § 481.090

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.090

---

## Text

“Document of title” means “document” as defined in paragraph (30) of subdivision (a) of Section 9102 of the Commercial Code. A document of title is negotiable if it is negotiable within the meaning of Section 7104 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 12.3. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
