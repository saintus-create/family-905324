# CCP § 873.745

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 6. Sale of the Property > Article: 3. Consummation of Sale

**Citation:** CCP § 873.745

---

## Text

The amount of agents’ commissions on the sale, if any, shall be fixed by the court and divided or limited in the manner provided for private sales of real property in decedents’ estates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
