# CCP § 358

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Provisions as to the Time of Commencing Actions

**Citation:** CCP § 358

---

## Text

When two or more disabilities coexist at the time the right of action accrues, the limitation does not attach until they are removed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
