# CCP § 801.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4.6. Actions Concerning Real Property Titles Affected by Public Improvement Assessments

**Citation:** CCP § 801.7

---

## Text

Within thirty days after the issuance of the summons, the plaintiff shall post, or cause to be posted, a copy thereof in a conspicuous place on the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1108.)
