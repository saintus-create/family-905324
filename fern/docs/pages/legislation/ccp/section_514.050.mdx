# CCP § 514.050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 4. Levy and Custody

**Citation:** CCP § 514.050

---

## Text

Where the property taken is claimed by a third person, the rules and proceedings applicable in cases of third-party claims under Division 4 (commencing with Section 720.010) of Title 9 apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 33. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
