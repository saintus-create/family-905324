# CCP § 706.082

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Wage Garnishment > Article: 4. Earnings Withholding Order for Taxes

**Citation:** CCP § 706.082

---

## Text

No review of the taxpayer’s tax liability shall be permitted in any court proceedings under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
