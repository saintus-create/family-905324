# CCP § 1245.220

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 4. Precondemnation Activities > Article: 2. Resolution of Necessity

**Citation:** CCP § 1245.220

---

## Text

A public entity may not commence an eminent domain proceeding until its governing body has adopted a resolution of necessity that meets the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
