# CCP § 405.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. RECORDING NOTICE OF CERTAIN ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 405.3

---

## Text

“Probable validity,” with respect to a real property claim, means that it is more likely than not that the claimant will obtain a judgment against the defendant on the claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 883, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
