# CCP § 1297.11

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 1. Application and Interpretation > Article: 1. Scope of Application

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.11

---

## Text

This title applies to international commercial arbitration and conciliation, subject to any agreement which is in force between the United States and any other state or states.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
