# CCP § 403.090

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. OF THE PLACE OF TRIAL, RECLASSIFICATION, AND COORDINATION OF CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Reclassification of Civil Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 403.090

---

## Text

The Judicial Council may prescribe rules, not inconsistent with statute, governing the procedure for reclassification of civil actions and proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 344, Sec. 11. Effective September 7, 1999.)
