# CCP § 1092

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 2. Writ of Mandate [[1084.] - [1097.]]

**Citation:** CCP § 1092

## Text

The motion for new trial must be made in the Court in which the issue of fact is tried.
