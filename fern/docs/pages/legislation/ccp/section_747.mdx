# CCP § 747

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3. Actions for the Recovery of Real Property, and Other Provisions Relating to Actions Concerning Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 747

---

## Text

An action for the recovery of real property against a person in possession cannot be prejudiced by any alienation made by such person, either before or after the commencement of the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
