# CCP § 335.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. The Time of Commencing Actions Other Than for the Recovery of Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 335.1

---

## Text

Within two years: An action for assault, battery, or injury to, or for the death of, an individual caused by the wrongful act or neglect of another.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 448, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
