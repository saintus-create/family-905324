# CCP § 1069.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 1. Writ of Review [[1067.] - 1077.]

**Citation:** CCP § 1069.1

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 1089 as to a return by demurrer or answer apply to a proceeding pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1475.)
