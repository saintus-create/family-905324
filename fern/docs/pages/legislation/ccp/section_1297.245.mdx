# CCP § 1297.245

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 5. Manner and Conduct of Arbitration > Article: 7. Hearings and Written Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.245

---

## Text

Unless otherwise agreed by the parties, all oral hearings and meetings in arbitral proceedings shall be held in camera.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
