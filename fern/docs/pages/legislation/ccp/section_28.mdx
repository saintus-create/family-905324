# CCP § 28

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 28

---

## Text

An injury to property consists in depriving its owner of the benefit of it, which is done by taking, withholding, deteriorating, or destroying it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
