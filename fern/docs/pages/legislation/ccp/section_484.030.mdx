# CCP § 484.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 4. Noticed Hearing Procedure for Obtaining Writ of Attachment > Article: 1. Right to Attach Order; Issuance of Writ of Attachment

**Citation:** CCP § 484.030

---

## Text

The application shall be supported by an affidavit showing that the plaintiff on the facts presented would be entitled to a judgment on the claim upon which the attachment is based.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1516.)
