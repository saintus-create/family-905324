# CCP § 902

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. APPEALS IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 1. Appeals in General

**Citation:** CCP § 902

---

## Text

Any party aggrieved may appeal in the cases prescribed in this title. A party appealing is known as an appellant, and an adverse party as a respondent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 385.)
