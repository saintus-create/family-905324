# CCP § 588

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Issues—The Mode of Trial and Postponements

**Citation:** CCP § 588

---

## Text

Issues arise upon the pleadings when a fact or a conclusion of law is maintained by the one party and is controverted by the other. They are of two kinds:

1. Of law; and,

2. Of fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
