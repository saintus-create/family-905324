# CCP § 2033.070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 16. Requests for Admission > Article: 1. Requests For Admission

**Citation:** CCP § 2033.070

---

## Text

The party requesting admissions shall serve a copy of them on the party to whom they are directed and on all other parties who have appeared in the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
