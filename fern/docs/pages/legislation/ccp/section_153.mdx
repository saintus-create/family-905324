# CCP § 153

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 6. General Provisions Respecting Courts of Justice > Article: 6. Seals of Courts

**Citation:** CCP § 153

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided by law, the seal of a court need not be affixed to any proceeding therein, or to any document, except to the following:

(a) A writ.

(b) A summons.

(c) A warrant of arrest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1199, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1989, by Sec. 119 of Ch. 1199.)
