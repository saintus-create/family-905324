# CCP § 680.280

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.280

---

## Text

“Person” includes a natural person, a corporation, a partnership or other unincorporated association, a general partner of a partnership, a limited liability company, and a public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 60. Effective January 1, 1995.)
