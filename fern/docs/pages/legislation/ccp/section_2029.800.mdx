# CCP § 2029.800

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 12. Discovery in Action Pending Outside California > Article: 1. Interstate and International Depositions and Discovery Act

**Citation:** CCP § 2029.800

---

## Text

This article applies to requests for discovery in cases pending on or after the operative date of this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 231, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
