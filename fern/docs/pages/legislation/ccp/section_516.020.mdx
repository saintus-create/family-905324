# CCP § 516.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 516.020

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall prescribe the form of the applications, notices, orders, and other documents required by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 526.)
