# CCP § 2035.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 19. Perpetuation of Testimony or Preservation  of Evidence Before Filing Action

**Citation:** CCP § 2035.020

---

## Text

The methods available for discovery conducted for the purposes set forth in Section 2035.010 are all of the following:

(a) Oral and written depositions.

(b) Inspections of documents, things, and places.

(c) Physical and mental examinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
