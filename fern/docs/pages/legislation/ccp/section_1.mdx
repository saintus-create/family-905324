# CCP § 1

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 1

---

## Text

This act shall be known as the Code of Civil Procedure, and is divided into four parts, as follows: Part I. Of Courts of Justice. II. Of Civil Actions. III. Of Special Proceedings of a Civil Nature. IV. Miscellaneous Provisions.
