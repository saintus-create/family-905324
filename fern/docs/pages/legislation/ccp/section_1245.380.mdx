# CCP § 1245.380

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 4. Precondemnation Activities > Article: 3. Resolution Consenting to Eminent Domain Proceeding by Quasi-Public Entity

**Citation:** CCP § 1245.380

---

## Text

The requirement of this article is in addition to any other requirements imposed by law. Nothing in this article relieves the quasi-public entity from satisfying the requirements of Section 1240.030 or any other requirements imposed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
