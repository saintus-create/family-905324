# CCP § 1167.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Summary Proceedings for Obtaining Possession of Real Property in Certain Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 1167.1

---

## Text

If proof of service of the summons has not been filed within 60 days of the complaint’s filing, the court may dismiss the action without prejudice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 336, Sec. 4. (AB 2819) Effective January 1, 2017.)
