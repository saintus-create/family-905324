# CCP § 1723

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. MONEY JUDGMENTS OF OTHER JURISDICTIONS > Chapter: 2. Foreign-Country Money Judgments

**Citation:** CCP § 1723

---

## Text

This chapter does not prevent the recognition under principles of comity or otherwise of a foreign-country judgment not within the scope of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 212, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
