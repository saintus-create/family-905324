# CCP § 762.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4. Quiet Title > Article: 3. Defendants

**Citation:** CCP § 762.010

---

## Text

The plaintiff shall name as defendants in the action the persons having adverse claims to the title of the plaintiff against which a determination is sought.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 15.)
