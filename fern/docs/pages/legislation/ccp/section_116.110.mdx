# CCP § 116.110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.5. Small Claims Court > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 116.110

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as “The Small Claims Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1305, Sec. 3. Note: Prior to 1991, this subject matter was in Chapter 5A, comprising Sections 116 to 117.24.)
