# CCP § 404.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. OF THE PLACE OF TRIAL, RECLASSIFICATION, AND COORDINATION OF CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Coordination

**Citation:** CCP § 404.8

---

## Text

Expenses of the assigned judge, other necessary judicial officers and employees, and facilities for cases coordinated under Section 404 shall be paid or reimbursed by the state from funds appropriated to the Judicial Council.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 713, Sec. 7. Effective September 23, 1996.)
