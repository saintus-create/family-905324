# CCP § 1265.110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 10. Divided Interests > Article: 2. Leases

**Citation:** CCP § 1265.110

---

## Text

Where all the property subject to a lease is acquired for public use, the lease terminates.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
