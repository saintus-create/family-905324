# CCP § 680.145

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.145

---

## Text

“Child support” includes family support.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 163, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 161 of Ch. 163.)
