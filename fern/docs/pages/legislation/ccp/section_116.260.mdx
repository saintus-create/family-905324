# CCP § 116.260

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.5. Small Claims Court > Article: 2. Small Claims Court

**Citation:** CCP § 116.260

---

## Text

In each county, individual assistance shall be made available to advise small claims litigants and potential litigants without charge as provided in Section 116.940 and by rules adopted by the Judicial Council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1305, Sec. 3. Note: Prior to 1991, this subject matter was in Chapter 5A, comprising Sections 116 to 117.24.)
