# CCP § 686.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 6. Enforcement After Death of Judgment Creditor or Judgment Debtor

**Citation:** CCP § 686.020

---

## Text

After the death of the judgment debtor, enforcement of a judgment against property in the judgment debtor’s estate is governed by the Probate Code, and not by this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1416, Sec. 21.)
