# CCP § 631.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 5. Trial by the Court

**Citation:** CCP § 631.7

---

## Text

Ordinarily, unless the court otherwise directs, the trial of a civil action tried by the court without a jury shall proceed in the order specified in Section 607.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
