# CCP § 1310

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 2. Receipt and Expenditure of Funds > Article: 1. Deposit of Unclaimed Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1310

---

## Text

Whenever, under the provisions of this title or under any other provision of law, unclaimed money or other unclaimed property is payable into the State Treasury, the person responsible for making such payment shall, if it is cash, transmit it to the Treasurer, and if it is personal property other than cash, transmit it to the Controller for deposit in the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1708.)
