# CCP § 1375

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Management of Unclaimed Property > Article: 3. Sale or Disposal of Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1375

---

## Text

Any real property may be sold or leased by the Controller at private sale without published notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 15. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
