# CCP § 1297.131

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 3. Composition of Arbitral Tribunals > Article: 4. Challenge Procedure

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.131

---

## Text

The parties may agree on a procedure for challenging an arbitrator and the decision reached pursuant to that procedure shall be final.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
