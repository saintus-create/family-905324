# CCP § 1076

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 1. Writ of Review [[1067.] - 1077.]

**Citation:** CCP § 1076

---

## Text

A copy of the judgment, signed by the Clerk, must be transmitted to the inferior tribunal, Board, or officer having the custody of the record or proceeding certified up.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
