# CCP § 486.080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 6. Temporary Protective Order

**Citation:** CCP § 486.080

---

## Text

The temporary protective order shall be personally served on the defendant together with the documents referred to in Section 484.040.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 44. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
