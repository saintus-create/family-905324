# CCP § 761.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4. Quiet Title > Article: 2. Commencement of Action

**Citation:** CCP § 761.010

---

## Text

(a) An action under this chapter is commenced by filing a complaint with the court.

(b) Immediately upon commencement of the action, the plaintiff shall file a notice of the pendency of the action in the office of the county recorder of each county in which any real property described in the complaint is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 15.)
