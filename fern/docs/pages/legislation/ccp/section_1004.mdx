# CCP § 1004

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Motions and Orders

**Citation:** CCP § 1004

---

## Text

Except as provided in section 166 of this code, motions must be made in the court in which the action is pending.
