# CCP § 511.070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 511.070

---

## Text

“Person” includes an individual, a corporation, a partnership or other unincorporated association, a limited liability company, and a public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 59. Effective January 1, 1995.)
