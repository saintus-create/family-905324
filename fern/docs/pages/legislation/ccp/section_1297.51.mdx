# CCP § 1297.51

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 1. Application and Interpretation > Article: 5. Extent of Judicial Intervention

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.51

---

## Text

In matters governed by this title, no court shall intervene except where so provided in this title, or applicable federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
