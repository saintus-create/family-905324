# CCP § 2020.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 6. Nonparty Discovery > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 2020.030

---

## Text

Except as modified in this chapter, the provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 1985) of Title 3 of Part 4 of this code, and of Article 4 (commencing with Section 1560) of Chapter 2 of Division 11 of the Evidence Code, apply to a deposition subpoena.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
