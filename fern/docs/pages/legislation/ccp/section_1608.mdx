# CCP § 1608

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 8. Property in Custody of Federal Officers, Agencies, and Departments

**Citation:** CCP § 1608

---

## Text

The expiration of any period of time specified by statute or court order, during which an action or proceeding may be commenced or enforced to obtain payment of a claim for funds or delivery of property shall not affect the right of this State to acquire possession of unclaimed property in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1801.)
