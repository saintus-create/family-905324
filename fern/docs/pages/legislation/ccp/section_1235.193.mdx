# CCP § 1235.193

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 2. Principles of Construction; Definitions > Article: 2. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 1235.193

---

## Text

“Electric, gas, or water public utility property” means property appropriated to a public use by a public utility, as defined in Section 218, 222, or 241 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 812, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
