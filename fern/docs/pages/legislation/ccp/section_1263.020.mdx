# CCP § 1263.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 9. Compensation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 1263.020

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the right to compensation shall be deemed to have accrued at the date of filing the complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
