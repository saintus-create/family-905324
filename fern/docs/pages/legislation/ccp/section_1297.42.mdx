# CCP § 1297.42

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 1. Application and Interpretation > Article: 4. Waiver of Right to Object

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.42

---

## Text

For purposes of Section 1297.41, “any provision of this title” means any provision of this title in respect of which the parties may otherwise agree.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
