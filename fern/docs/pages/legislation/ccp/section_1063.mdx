# CCP § 1063

**Hierarchy:** Part: 3. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CIVIL NATURE > Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS

**Citation:** CCP § 1063

---

## Text

The party prosecuting a special proceeding may be known as the plaintiff, and the adverse party as the defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
