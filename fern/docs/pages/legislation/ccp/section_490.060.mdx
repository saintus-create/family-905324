# CCP § 490.060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 10. Liability for Wrongful Attachment

**Citation:** CCP § 490.060

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the right to recover for damages caused by an attachment or protective order on any common law theory of recovery.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1516.)
