# CCP § 2016.070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 2016.070

---

## Text

This title applies to discovery in aid of enforcement of a money judgment only to the extent provided in Article 1 (commencing with Section 708.010) of Chapter 6 of Title 9 of Part 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
