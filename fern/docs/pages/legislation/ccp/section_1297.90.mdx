# CCP § 1297.90

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 2. Arbitration Agreements and Judicial Measures in Aid of Arbitration > Article: 3. Court-Ordered Interim Measures

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.90

---

## Text

It is not incompatible with an arbitration agreement for a party to request from a superior court, before or during arbitral proceedings, an interim measure of protection, or for the court to grant such a measure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1297.91 by Stats. 2024, Ch. 90, Sec. 6. (AB 1903) Effective January 1, 2025.)
