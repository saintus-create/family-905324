# CCP § 706.152

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Wage Garnishment > Article: 7. Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** CCP § 706.152

---

## Text

If an employer withholds earnings pursuant to this chapter and, with the intent to defraud either the judgment creditor or the judgment debtor, fails to pay such withheld earnings over to the levying officer, the employer is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
