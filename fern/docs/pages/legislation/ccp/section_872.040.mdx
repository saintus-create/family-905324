# CCP § 872.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 872.040

---

## Text

Nothing in this title excuses compliance with any applicable laws, regulations, or ordinances governing the division, sale, or transfer of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
