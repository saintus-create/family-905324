# CCP § 680.170

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.170

---

## Text

“Deposit account” means “deposit account” as defined in paragraph (29) of subdivision (a) of Section 9102 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 16.2. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
