# CCP § 1935

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE KINDS AND DEGREES OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Writings > Article: 3. Private Writings

**Citation:** CCP § 1935

---

## Text

A subscribing witness is one who sees a writing executed or hears it acknowledged, and at the request of the party thereupon signs his name as a witness.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
