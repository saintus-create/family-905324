# CCP § 116.270

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.5. Small Claims Court > Article: 2. Small Claims Court

**Citation:** CCP § 116.270

---

## Text

Any small claims division may use law clerks to assist the judge with legal research of small claims cases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1305, Sec. 3. Note: Prior to 1991, this subject matter was in Chapter 5A, comprising Sections 116 to 117.24.)
