# CCP § 1258.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 7. Discovery; Exchange of Valuation Data > Article: 1. Discovery

**Citation:** CCP § 1258.010

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter supplement but do not replace, restrict, or prevent the use of discovery procedures or limit the matters that are discoverable in eminent domain proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
