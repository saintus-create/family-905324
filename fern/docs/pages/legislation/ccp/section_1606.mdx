# CCP § 1606

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 8. Property in Custody of Federal Officers, Agencies, and Departments

**Citation:** CCP § 1606

---

## Text

On the thirtieth day of June next following the date of certification by the Governor, and annually thereafter, the Controller shall request the Comptroller General or other proper officer of the United States to report all previously unreported information relating to unclaimed property as determined by that officer pursuant to federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1801.)
