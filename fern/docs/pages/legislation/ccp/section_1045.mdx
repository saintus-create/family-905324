# CCP § 1045

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 1045

---

## Text

If an original pleading or paper be lost, the Court may authorize a copy thereof to be filed and used instead of the original.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
