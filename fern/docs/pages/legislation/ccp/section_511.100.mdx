# CCP § 511.100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 511.100

---

## Text

“Public entity” includes the state, the Regents of the University of California, a county, city, district, public authority, public agency, and any other political subdivision or public corporation in the state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 526.)
