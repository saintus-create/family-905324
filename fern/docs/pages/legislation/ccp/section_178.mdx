# CCP § 178

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. JUDICIAL OFFICERS > Chapter: 4. Incidental Powers and Duties of Judicial Officers

**Citation:** CCP § 178

---

## Text

For the effectual exercise of the powers conferred by the last section, a judicial officer may punish for contempt in the cases provided in this Code.

(History: Repealed and added by Code Amendments 1880, Ch. 35.)
