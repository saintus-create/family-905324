# CCP § 1230.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 1230.020

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specifically provided by statute, the power of eminent domain may be exercised only as provided in this title.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
