# CCP § 1250.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 5. Commencement of Proceeding > Article: 3. Parties; Joinder of Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1250.210

---

## Text

Each person seeking to take property by eminent domain shall be named as a plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
