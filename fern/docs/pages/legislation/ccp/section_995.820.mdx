# CCP § 995.820

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 8. Bonds to the State of California

**Citation:** CCP § 995.820

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, a bond given by an officer of the court for the faithful discharge of the officer’s duties and obedience to the orders of the court shall be to the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
