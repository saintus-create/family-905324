# CCP § 712.060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 712.060

---

## Text

The court may appoint a receiver pursuant to Article 7 (commencing with Section 708.610) of Chapter 6 of Division 2 to enforce a judgment for possession or sale of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
