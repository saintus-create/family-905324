# CCP § 720.360

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Hearing on Third-Party Claim

**Citation:** CCP § 720.360

---

## Text

At a hearing on a third-party claim, the third person has the burden of proof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
