# CCP § 1263.120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 9. Compensation > Article: 2. Date of Valuation

**Citation:** CCP § 1263.120

---

## Text

If the issue of compensation is brought to trial within one year after commencement of the proceeding, the date of valuation is the date of commencement of the proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
