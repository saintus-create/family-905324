# CCP § 1216

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. OF CONTEMPTS

**Citation:** CCP § 1216

---

## Text

The officer must return the warrant of arrest and undertaking, if any, received by him from the person arrested, by the return day specified therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
