# CCP § 1105

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 3. Writ of Prohibition

**Citation:** CCP § 1105

---

## Text

The provisions of the preceding Chapter, except of the first four sections thereof, apply to this proceeding.
