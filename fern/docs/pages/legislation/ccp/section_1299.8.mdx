# CCP § 1299.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.5. ARBITRATION OF FIREFIGHTER AND LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICER LABOR DISPUTES

**Citation:** CCP § 1299.8

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in this title, Title 9 (commencing with Section 1280) shall be applicable to any arbitration proceeding undertaken pursuant to this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 906, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
