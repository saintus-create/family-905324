# CCP § 770.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4.5. Special Actions and Proceedings to Clear Title > Article: 1. Identity of Person in Chain of Title

**Citation:** CCP § 770.030

---

## Text

The proceeding shall be brought in the superior court of the county in which the property or any part thereof is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 16.)
