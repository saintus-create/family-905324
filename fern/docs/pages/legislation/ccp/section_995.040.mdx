# CCP § 995.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 995.040

---

## Text

An affidavit made under this chapter shall conform to the standards prescribed for an affidavit made pursuant to Section 437c.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
