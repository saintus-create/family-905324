# CCP § 1297.91

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 2. Arbitration Agreements and Judicial Measures in Aid of Arbitration > Article: 3. Court-Ordered Interim Measures

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.91

---

## Text

A superior court shall have the same power to issue an interim measure of protection in relation to arbitration proceedings, irrespective of whether the place of arbitration is in California, as it has in relation to proceedings filed in the first instance in the superior courts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 90, Sec. 7. (AB 1903) Effective January 1, 2025.)
