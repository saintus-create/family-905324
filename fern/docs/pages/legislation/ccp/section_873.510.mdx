# CCP § 873.510

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 6. Sale of the Property > Article: 1. Manner of Sale

**Citation:** CCP § 873.510

---

## Text

The referee appointed by the court to make a sale of the property shall sell the property in the manner and following the procedures provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
