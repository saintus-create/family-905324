# CCP § 874.120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 8. Costs of Partition > Article: 2. Payment of Costs of Partition

**Citation:** CCP § 874.120

---

## Text

(a) The costs shall be a lien on the share of the party specified.

(b) A lien imposed by this section has priority over any other lien on the share except those imposed under this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
