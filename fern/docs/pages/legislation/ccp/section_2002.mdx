# CCP § 2002

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF THE PRODUCTION OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Manner of Production > Article: 1. Mode of Taking the Testimony of Witnesses

**Citation:** CCP § 2002

---

## Text

The testimony of witnesses is taken in three modes:

1. By affidavit;

2. By deposition;

3. By oral examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
