# CCP § 751.14

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3.5. Actions to Re-establish Destroyed Land Records

**Citation:** CCP § 751.14

---

## Text

Judgment in any such action shall not be given by default, but the court must require proof of the facts alleged in the complaint and other pleadings.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 52.)
