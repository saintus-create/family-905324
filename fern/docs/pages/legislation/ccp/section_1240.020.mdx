# CCP § 1240.020

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 3. The Right to Take > Article: 1. General Limitations on Exercise of Power of Eminent Domain

**Citation:** CCP § 1240.020

---

## Text

The power of eminent domain may be exercised to acquire property for a particular use only by a person authorized by statute to exercise the power of eminent domain to acquire such property for that use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
