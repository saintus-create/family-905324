# CCP § 1297.362

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 7. Conciliation > Article: 3. Report of Conciliators

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.362

---

## Text

No party may be required to accept any settlement proposed by the conciliator or conciliators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
