# CCP § 491.120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 11. Attaching Plaintiff’s Miscellaneous Remedies > Article: 1. Examination of Third Person

**Citation:** CCP § 491.120

---

## Text

In any proceeding for the examination of a third person under this article, witnesses, including the defendant, may be required to appear and testify before the court or referee in the same manner as upon the trial of an issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 57. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
