# CCP § 363

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Provisions as to the Time of Commencing Actions

**Citation:** CCP § 363

---

## Text

The word “action” as used in this Title is to be construed, whenever it is necessary so to do, as including a special proceeding of a civil nature.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
