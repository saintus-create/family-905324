# CCP § 90

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.1. Limited Civil Cases > Article: 2. Economic Litigation for Limited Civil Cases

**Citation:** CCP § 90

---

## Text

Except where changed by the provisions of this article, all provisions of law applicable to civil actions generally apply to actions subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
