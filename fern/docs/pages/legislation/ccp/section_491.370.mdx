# CCP § 491.370

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 11. Attaching Plaintiff’s Miscellaneous Remedies > Article: 2. Creditor’s Suit

**Citation:** CCP § 491.370

---

## Text

Costs incurred by or taxed against the plaintiff in an action under this article may not be recovered from the defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 57. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
