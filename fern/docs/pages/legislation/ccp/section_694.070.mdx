# CCP § 694.070

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 20. Transitional Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 694.070

---

## Text

(a) A third-party claim filed prior to the operative date is governed by prior law.

(b) A demand for a third-party claim served on a secured party prior to the operative date is governed by prior law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
