# CCP § 995.840

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 8. Bonds to the State of California

**Citation:** CCP § 995.840

---

## Text

If a bond under this article is given in an action or proceeding:

(a) The bond shall be approved by the court.

(b) Any party for whose benefit the bond is given may object to the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
