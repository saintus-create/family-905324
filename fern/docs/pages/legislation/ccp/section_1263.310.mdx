# CCP § 1263.310

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 9. Compensation > Article: 4. Measure of Compensation for Property Taken

**Citation:** CCP § 1263.310

---

## Text

Compensation shall be awarded for the property taken. The measure of this compensation is the fair market value of the property taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
