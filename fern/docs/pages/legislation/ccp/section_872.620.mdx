# CCP § 872.620

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 3. Trial > Article: 1. Determination of Interests of Parties

**Citation:** CCP § 872.620

---

## Text

To the extent necessary to grant the relief sought or other appropriate relief, the court shall upon adequate proof ascertain the state of the title to the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
