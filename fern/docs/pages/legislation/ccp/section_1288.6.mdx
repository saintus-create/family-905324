# CCP § 1288.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 4. Enforcement of the Award > Article: 2. Limitations of Time

**Citation:** CCP § 1288.6

---

## Text

If an application is made to the arbitrators for correction of the award, a petition may not be served and filed under this chapter until the determination of that application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
