# CCP § 680.320

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.320

---

## Text

“Real property” includes any right in real property, including but not limited to a leasehold interest in real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
