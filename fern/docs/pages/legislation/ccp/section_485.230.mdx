# CCP § 485.230

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 5. Ex Parte Hearing Procedure for Obtaining Writ of Attachment > Article: 2. Order Determining Right to Attach; Issuance of Writ of Attachment

**Citation:** CCP § 485.230

---

## Text

Where a right to attach order has been issued by the court, a plaintiff may discover, through any means provided for by, and subject to the protections included in, Title 4 (commencing with Section 2016.010) of Part 4, the identity, location, and value of property in which the defendant has an interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
