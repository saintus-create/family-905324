# CCP § 116.510

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.5. Small Claims Court > Article: 5. Hearing

**Citation:** CCP § 116.510

---

## Text

The hearing and disposition of the small claims action shall be informal, the object being to dispense justice promptly, fairly, and inexpensively.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 915, Sec. 13.)
