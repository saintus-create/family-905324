# CCP § 873.160

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Referees > Article: 2. Contracts of Referee

**Citation:** CCP § 873.160

---

## Text

The referee is not personally liable on contracts made, or for expenses incurred, except as such liability is expressly assumed by the referee in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
