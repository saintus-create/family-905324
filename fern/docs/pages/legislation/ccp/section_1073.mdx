# CCP § 1073

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF WRITS OF REVIEW, MANDATE, AND PROHIBITION [[1067.] - 1110b.] > Chapter: 1. Writ of Review [[1067.] - 1077.]

**Citation:** CCP § 1073

---

## Text

The writ must be served in the same manner as a summons in civil action, except when otherwise expressly directed by the Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
