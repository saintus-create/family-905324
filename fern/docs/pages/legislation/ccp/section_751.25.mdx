# CCP § 751.25

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3.5. Actions to Re-establish Destroyed Land Records

**Citation:** CCP § 751.25

---

## Text

Upon the filing of the notice for recordation the recorder shall record the notice in the same manner as provided in Section 409.
