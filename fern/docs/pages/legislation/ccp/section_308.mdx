# CCP § 308

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE FORM OF CIVIL ACTIONS

**Citation:** CCP § 308

---

## Text

In such action the party complaining is known as the plaintiff, and the adverse party as the defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
