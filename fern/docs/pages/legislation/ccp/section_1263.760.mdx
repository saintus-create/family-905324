# CCP § 1263.760

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 9. Compensation > Article: 8. Remediation of Hazardous Materials on Property to be Acquired by School Districts

**Citation:** CCP § 1263.760

---

## Text

An offer by the plaintiff to purchase the property subject to this article shall be deemed to satisfy the requirements of Section 7267.2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 247, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
