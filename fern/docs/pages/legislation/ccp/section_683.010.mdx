# CCP § 683.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 3. Period for Enforcement and Renewal of Judgments > Article: 1. Period for Enforcement of Judgments

**Citation:** CCP § 683.010

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute or in the judgment, a judgment is enforceable under this title upon entry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
