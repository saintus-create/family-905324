# CCP § 685.110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Interest and Costs

**Citation:** CCP § 685.110

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects the law relating to prejudgment interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
