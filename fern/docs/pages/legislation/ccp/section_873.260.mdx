# CCP § 873.260

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 5. Division of the Property

**Citation:** CCP § 873.260

---

## Text

Where a lien is on an undivided interest of a party, the lien shall, upon division of the property, become a charge only on the share allotted to that party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
