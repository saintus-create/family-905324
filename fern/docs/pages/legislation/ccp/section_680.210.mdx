# CCP § 680.210

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.210

---

## Text

“General intangibles” means “general intangibles,” as defined in paragraph (42) of subdivision (a) of Section 9102 of the Commercial Code, consisting of rights to payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
