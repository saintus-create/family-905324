# CCP § 761.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 4. Quiet Title > Article: 2. Commencement of Action

**Citation:** CCP § 761.040

---

## Text

(a) The defendant may by cross-complaint seek affirmative relief in the action.

(b) If the defendant seeks a determination of title as of a date other than the date specified in the complaint, the cross-complaint shall include the date and a statement of the reasons why a determination as of that date is sought.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 44, Sec. 15.)
