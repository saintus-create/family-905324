# CCP § 995.830

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 8. Bonds to the State of California

**Citation:** CCP § 995.830

---

## Text

If a statute or court order pursuant thereto providing for a bond does not specify the beneficiary of the bond, the bond shall be to the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
