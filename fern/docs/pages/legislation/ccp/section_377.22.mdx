# CCP § 377.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF THE PARTIES TO CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. Effect of Death > Article: 2. Survival and Continuation

**Citation:** CCP § 377.22

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as affecting the assignability of causes of action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 178, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1993.)
