# CCP § 866

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 9. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** CCP § 866

---

## Text

The court hearing the action shall disregard any error, irregularity, or omission which does not affect the substantial rights of the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1479.)
