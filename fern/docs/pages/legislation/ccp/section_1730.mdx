# CCP § 1730

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. MONEY JUDGMENTS OF OTHER JURISDICTIONS > Chapter: 3. Tribal Court Civil Money Judgment Act

**Citation:** CCP § 1730

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Tribal Court Civil Money Judgment Act.
