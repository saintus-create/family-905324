# CCP § 355

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE TIME OF COMMENCING CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Provisions as to the Time of Commencing Actions

**Citation:** CCP § 355

---

## Text

If an action is commenced within the time prescribed therefor, and a judgment therein for the plaintiff be reversed on appeal other than on the merits, a new action may be commenced within one year after the reversal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 178, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
