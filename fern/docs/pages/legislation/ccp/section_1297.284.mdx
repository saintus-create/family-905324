# CCP § 1297.284

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 6. Making of Arbitral Award and Termination of Proceedings > Article: 1. Rules Applicable to Substance of Dispute

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.284

---

## Text

The arbitral tribunal shall decide ex aequo et bono or as amiable compositeur, if the parties have expressly authorized it to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
