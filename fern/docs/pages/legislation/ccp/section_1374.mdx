# CCP § 1374

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. UNCLAIMED PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Management of Unclaimed Property > Article: 3. Sale or Disposal of Property

**Citation:** CCP § 1374

---

## Text

The Controller may reject any and all bids made at sales or public auctions held under the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1708.)
