# CCP § 1141.19

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2.5. Judicial Arbitration

**Citation:** CCP § 1141.19

---

## Text

Arbitrators approved pursuant to this chapter shall have the powers necessary to perform duties pursuant to this chapter as prescribed by the Judicial Council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 743.)
