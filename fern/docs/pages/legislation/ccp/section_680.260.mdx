# CCP § 680.260

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 680.260

---

## Text

“Levying officer” means the sheriff or marshal.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1997.)
