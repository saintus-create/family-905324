# CCP § 1297.33

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 1. Application and Interpretation > Article: 3. Receipt of Written Communications

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.33

---

## Text

This article does not apply to written communications in respect of court proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
