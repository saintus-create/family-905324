# CCP § 1250.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 5. Commencement of Proceeding > Article: 1. Jurisdiction and Venue

**Citation:** CCP § 1250.040

---

## Text

The provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure for the change of place of trial of actions apply to eminent domain proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
