# CCP § 1297.232

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 5. Manner and Conduct of Arbitration > Article: 6. Statements of Claim and Defense

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.232

---

## Text

The parties may submit with their statements all documents they consider to be relevant or may add a reference to the documents or other evidence they will submit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 23, Sec. 1. Effective March 7, 1988.)
