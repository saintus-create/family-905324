# CCP § 481.203

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. ATTACHMENT > Chapter: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 481.203

---

## Text

“Real property” includes any right in real property, including, but not limited to, a leasehold interest in real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1198, Sec. 17. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 70 of Ch. 1198.)
