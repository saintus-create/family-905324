# CCP § 1297.187

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 5. Manner and Conduct of Arbitration > Article: 1.5. Representation by Foreign and Out-of-State Attorneys

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.187

---

## Text

A qualified attorney rendering legal services pursuant to this article shall not appear in a court of this state unless he or she has applied for and received permission to appear as counsel pro hac vice pursuant to the California Rules of Court, as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 134, Sec. 1. (SB 766) Effective January 1, 2019.)
