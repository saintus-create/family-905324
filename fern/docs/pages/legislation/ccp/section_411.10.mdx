# CCP § 411.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. JURISDICTION AND SERVICE OF PROCESS > Chapter: 2. Commencing Civil Actions

**Citation:** CCP § 411.10

---

## Text

A civil action is commenced by filing a complaint with the court.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1610.)
