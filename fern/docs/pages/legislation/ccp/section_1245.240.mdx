# CCP § 1245.240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. EMINENT DOMAIN LAW > Chapter: 4. Precondemnation Activities > Article: 2. Resolution of Necessity

**Citation:** CCP § 1245.240

---

## Text

Unless a greater vote is required by statute, charter, or ordinance, the resolution shall be adopted by a vote of two-thirds of all the members of the governing body of the public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1275.)
