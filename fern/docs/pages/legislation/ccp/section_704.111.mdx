# CCP § 704.111

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 3. Exempt Property

**Citation:** CCP § 704.111

---

## Text

Alimony, support, and separate maintenance, to the extent reasonably necessary for the support of the debtor and any dependent of the debtor, are exempt.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 716, Sec. 5. (SB 1099) Effective January 1, 2023.)
