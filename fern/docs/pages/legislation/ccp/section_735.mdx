# CCP § 735

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 2. Actions for Nuisance, Waste,and Willful Trespass, in Certain Cases,on Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 735

---

## Text

If a person recover damages for a forcible or unlawlful entry in or upon, or detention of any building or any cultivated real property, judgment may be entered for three times the amount at which the actual damages are assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
