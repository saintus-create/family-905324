# CCP § 734

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 2. Actions for Nuisance, Waste,and Willful Trespass, in Certain Cases,on Real Property

**Citation:** CCP § 734

---

## Text

Nothing in the last section authorizes the recovery of more than the just value of the timber taken from uncultivated woodland for the repair of a public highway or bridge upon the land, or adjoining it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
