# CCP § 751.03

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 3.5. Actions to Re-establish Destroyed Land Records

**Citation:** CCP § 751.03

---

## Text

Any number of separate parcels of land claimed by the plaintiff may be included in the same action.
