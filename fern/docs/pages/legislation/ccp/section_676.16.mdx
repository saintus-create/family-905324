# CCP § 676.16

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8.5. UNIFORM FOREIGN-MONEY CLAIMS ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 676.16

---

## Text

This title applies to actions and distribution proceedings commenced on or after January 1, 1992.
