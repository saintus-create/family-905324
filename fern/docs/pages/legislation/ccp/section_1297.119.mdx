# CCP § 1297.119

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.3. ARBITRATION AND CONCILIATION OF INTERNATIONAL COMMERCIAL DISPUTES > Chapter: 3. Composition of Arbitral Tribunals > Article: 2. Appointment of Arbitrators

**Citation:** CCP § 1297.119

---

## Text

An arbitrator has the immunity of a judicial officer from civil liability when acting in the capacity of arbitrator under any statute or contract.

The immunity afforded by this section shall supplement, and not supplant, any otherwise applicable common law or statutory immunity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 228, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
