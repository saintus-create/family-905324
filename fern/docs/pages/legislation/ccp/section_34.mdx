# CCP § 34

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 34

---

## Text

An electronic signature, as defined in Section 17, by a court or judicial officer shall be as effective as an original signature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 32, Sec. 2. (AB 432) Effective January 1, 2016.)
