# CCP § 1298.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.4. REAL ESTATE CONTRACT ARBITRATION

**Citation:** CCP § 1298.8

---

## Text

This title shall become operative on July 1, 1989, and shall only apply to contracts or agreements entered into on or after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 881, Sec. 1. Note: This section delayed the initial operation of Title 9.3 (now numbered 9.4), commencing with Section 1298.)
