# CCP § 2016.030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. CIVIL DISCOVERY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 2016.030

---

## Text

Unless the court orders otherwise, the parties may by written stipulation modify the procedures provided by this title for any method of discovery permitted under Section 2019.010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 182, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 64 of Ch. 182.)
