# CCP § 26

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. ACT

**Citation:** CCP § 26

---

## Text

An obligation is a legal duty, by which one person is bound to do or not to do a certain thing, and arises from either of the following:

(a) Contract.

(b) Operation of law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 561, Sec. 21. (AB 1516) Effective January 1, 2018.)
