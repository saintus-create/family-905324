# CCP § 856

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. ACTIONS IN PARTICULAR CASES > Chapter: 8. Actions Against Coowners of Mines

**Citation:** CCP § 856

---

## Text

If the mine or mining claim is situated in more than one county, the petition may be filed in the superior court of either county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 853 by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 35. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
