# CCP § 681.040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 681.040

---

## Text

If a paper is required or permitted to be filed with a levying officer under this title, the paper is considered filed when it is actually received by the levying officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
