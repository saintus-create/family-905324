# CCP § 192

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. PERSONS SPECIALLY INVESTED WITH POWERS OF A JUDICIAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Trial Jury Selection and Management Act

**Citation:** CCP § 192

---

## Text

This chapter applies to the selection of jurors, and the formation of trial juries, for both civil and criminal cases, in all trial courts of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1245, Sec. 2.)
