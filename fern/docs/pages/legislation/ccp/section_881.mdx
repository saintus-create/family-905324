# CCP § 881

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CONTRIBUTION AMONG JOINT JUDGMENT DEBTORS > Chapter: 2. Contribution Among Other Judgment Debtors

**Citation:** CCP § 881

---

## Text

This chapter governs contribution among joint judgment debtors other than joint tortfeasors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 63. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
