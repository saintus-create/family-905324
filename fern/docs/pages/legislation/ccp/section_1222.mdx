# CCP § 1222

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. OF CONTEMPTS

**Citation:** CCP § 1222

---

## Text

The judgment and orders of the court or judge, made in cases of contempt, are final and conclusive.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1737.)
