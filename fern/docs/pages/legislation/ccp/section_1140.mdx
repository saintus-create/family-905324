# CCP § 1140

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. OF SUMMARY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2. Submitting a Controversy Without Action

**Citation:** CCP § 1140

---

## Text

The judgment may be enforced in the same manner as if it had been rendered in an action of the same jurisdictional classification in the same court, and is in the same manner subject to appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 115. Effective September 28, 1998.)
