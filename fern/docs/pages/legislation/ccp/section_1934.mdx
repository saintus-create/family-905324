# CCP § 1934

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF THE KINDS AND DEGREES OF EVIDENCE > Chapter: 3. Writings > Article: 3. Private Writings

**Citation:** CCP § 1934

---

## Text

An agreement, in writing, without a seal, for the compromise or settlement of a debt, is as obligatory as if a seal were affixed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
