# CCP § 706.109

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Wage Garnishment > Article: 5. Procedure for Earnings Withholding Orders and Exemption Claims

**Citation:** CCP § 706.109

---

## Text

An earnings withholding order may not be issued against the earnings of the spouse of the judgment debtor except by court order upon noticed motion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1671, Sec. 20.)
