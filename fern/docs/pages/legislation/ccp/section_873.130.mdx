# CCP § 873.130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Referees > Article: 2. Contracts of Referee

**Citation:** CCP § 873.130

---

## Text

The referee may, with the approval of the court pursuant to Section 873.110, employ a surveyor with the necessary assistants to aid in making a sale or division of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 73.)
