# CCP § 116.745

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. ORGANIZATION AND JURISDICTION > Chapter: 5.5. Small Claims Court > Article: 7. Motion to Vacate, Appeal, and Related Matters

**Citation:** CCP § 116.745

---

## Text

The clerk shall collect a fee of twenty dollars ($20) for the filing of a motion to vacate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 75, Sec. 22. Effective July 19, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 156 of Ch. 75.)
