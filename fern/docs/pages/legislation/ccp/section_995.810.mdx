# CCP § 995.810

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. OF MISCELLANEOUS PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Bonds and Undertakings > Article: 8. Bonds to the State of California

**Citation:** CCP § 995.810

---

## Text

The provisions of this article apply to a bond executed to, in favor of, in the name of, or payable to the State of California or the people of the state, including but not limited to an official bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 998, Sec. 1.)
