# CCP § 511.060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OTHER PROVISIONAL REMEDIES IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 2. Claim and Delivery of Personal Property > Article: 1. Words and Phrases Defined

**Citation:** CCP § 511.060

---

## Text

“Levying officer” means the sheriff or marshal who is directed to execute a writ of possession issued under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1997.)
