# CCP § 656

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF THE TRIAL AND JUDGMENT IN CIVIL ACTIONS > Chapter: 7. Provisions Relating to Trials in General > Article: 2. New Trials

**Citation:** CCP § 656

---

## Text

A new trial is a re-examination of an issue of fact in the same court after a trial and decision by a jury, court, or referee.
