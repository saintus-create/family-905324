# CCP § 1285.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 4. Enforcement of the Award > Article: 1. Confirmation, Correction or Vacation of the Award

**Citation:** CCP § 1285.8

---

## Text

A petition to correct or vacate an award, or a response requesting such relief, shall set forth the grounds on which the request for such relief is based.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 461.)
