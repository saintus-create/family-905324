# CCP § 694.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 20. Transitional Provisions

**Citation:** CCP § 694.010

---

## Text

As used in this chapter:

(a) “Operative date” means July 1, 1983.

(b) “Prior law” means the applicable law in effect on June 30, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
