# CCP § 874.140

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.5. PARTITION OF REAL AND PERSONAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 8. Costs of Partition > Article: 2. Payment of Costs of Partition

**Citation:** CCP § 874.140

---

## Text

A judgment for unpaid costs of partition may be enforced by the person entitled to the costs in the manner provided for enforcement of money judgments generally against the share of the party specified in the judgment or against other property of the party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 60. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
