# CCP § 706.010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. ENFORCEMENT OF JUDGMENTS > Chapter: 5. Wage Garnishment > Article: 1. Short Title; Definitions

**Citation:** CCP § 706.010

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Wage Garnishment Law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1364, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1364.)
