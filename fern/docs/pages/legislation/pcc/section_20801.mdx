# PCC § 20801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 52. Sanitary Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20801

---

## Text

“District project,” as used in this article, means any construction, reconstruction, alteration, enlargement, renewal, or replacement of sewer facilities which the district is authorized to do, including, but not limited to, the furnishing of supplies or materials for any such work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 95.)
