# PCC § 10245.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 7.2. Public Works Contract Arbitration Committee

**Citation:** PCC § 10245.3

---

## Text

The committee may establish standards and qualifications for the certification of arbitrators and certify as arbitrators persons meeting such standards and qualifications. The committee may remove persons from its list of certified arbitrators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 91.)
