# PCC § 10472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 12. Minority Business Participation

**Citation:** PCC § 10472

---

## Text

In awarding contracts to the lowest responsible bidder, the awarding department shall consider the responsiveness of a bidder to minority business enterprise and women business enterprise goals set forth in Section 10471.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 19, Sec. 4. Effective July 28, 2009.)
