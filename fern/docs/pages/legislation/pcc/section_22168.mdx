# PCC § 22168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Local Agency Design-Build Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 22168

---

## Text

Nothing in this article affects, expands, alters, or limits any rights or remedies otherwise available at law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 931, Sec. 14. (SB 785) Effective January 1, 2015. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 22169.)
