# PCC § 22016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 2. California Uniform Construction Cost Accounting Commission

**Citation:** PCC § 22016

---

## Text

The commission shall meet not less than once each year, at a time and place chosen by its membership.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 21106 by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1019, Sec. 46.)
