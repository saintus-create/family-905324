# PCC § 21492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 114. Kern County Water Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21492

---

## Text

In case of an emergency, if notice for bids to let contracts will not be given, the board shall comply with Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 22050).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 803, Sec. 68. Effective January 1, 1995.)
