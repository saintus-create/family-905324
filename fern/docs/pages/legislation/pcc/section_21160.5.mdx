# PCC § 21160.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 83. Santa Clara Valley Water District

**Citation:** PCC § 21160.5

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Board” means the board of directors of the Santa Clara Valley Water District.

(b) “District” means the Santa Clara Valley Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 264, Sec. 1. (AB 707) Effective January 1, 2020.)
