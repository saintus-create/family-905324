# PCC § 4112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Subletting and Subcontracting

**Citation:** PCC § 4112

---

## Text

The failure on the part of a contractor to comply with any provision of this chapter does not constitute a defense to the contractor in any action brought against the contractor by a subcontractor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 42.1.)
