# PCC § 22013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 2. California Uniform Construction Cost Accounting Commission

**Citation:** PCC § 22013

---

## Text

The commission members shall select a chairperson from among its membership. The chairperson shall serve as chair for a term of two years from the date of selection. In no event shall two consecutive chairpersons be appointees representing either the construction industry or public agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 110, Sec. 1. (AB 943) Effective January 1, 2012.)
