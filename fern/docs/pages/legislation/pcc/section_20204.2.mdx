# PCC § 20204.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 6.5. Public Utility Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20204.2

---

## Text

The person or corporation to whom the contract is awarded shall execute a bond, to be approved by the board, for the faithful performance of the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
