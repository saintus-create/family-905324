# PCC § 12305.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Recycled Product Procurement by the Legislature > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PCC § 12305.5

---

## Text

If a recycled product costs more than the same product made with virgin material, the Legislature shall purchase fewer of those more costly products or apply cost savings, if any, gained from buying other recycled products towards the purchase of those more costly products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 816, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
