# PCC § 10109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 1. Scope of Chapter and General Provisions

**Citation:** PCC § 10109

---

## Text

Any notice inviting bids on a project which specifies locations of possible materials, such as a borrow pit or gravel bed, for use in the proposed project which would be subject to Section 1602 of the Fish and Game Code shall include any conditions or modifications established pursuant to Section 1603.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 383, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2006.)
