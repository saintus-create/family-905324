# PCC § 12105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Acquisition of Information Technology Goods and Services

**Citation:** PCC § 12105

---

## Text

The Department of General Services and the Department of Technology shall coordinate in the development of policies and procedures that implement the intent of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 28, Sec. 64. (SB 71) Effective June 27, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 93 of Ch. 28.)
