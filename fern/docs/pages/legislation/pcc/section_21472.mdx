# PCC § 21472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 112. Mojave Water Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21472

---

## Text

In case of an emergency, if notice for bids to let contracts will not be given, the agency shall respond to the emergency pursuant to Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 22050).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 803, second Sec. 65. Effective January 1, 1995.)
