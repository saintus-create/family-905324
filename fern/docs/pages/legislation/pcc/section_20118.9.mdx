# PCC § 20118.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 3.1. Task Order Contracting for School Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20118.9

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2034, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 523, Sec. 3. (AB 439) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 3.1, commencing with Section 20118.5.)
