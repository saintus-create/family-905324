# PCC § 20320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 18. Sacramento Regional Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20320

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Sacramento Regional Transit District, as provided for in Part 14 (commencing with Section 102000) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
