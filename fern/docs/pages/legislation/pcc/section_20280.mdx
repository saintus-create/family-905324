# PCC § 20280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 14. Santa Barbara Metropolitan Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20280

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Santa Barbara Metropolitan Transit District, as provided for in Part 9 (commencing with Section 95000) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
