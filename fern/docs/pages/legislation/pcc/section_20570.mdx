# PCC § 20570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 35. Irrigation Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20570

---

## Text

The works shall be constructed under the direction and to the satisfaction of the engineer of the district and be subject to the approval of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
