# PCC § 2057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Certification of Minority and Women Business Enterprises

**Citation:** PCC § 2057

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any professional services contract subject to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 16850) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1329, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
