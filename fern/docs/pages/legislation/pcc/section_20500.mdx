# PCC § 20500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 30. City Councils—Street Lighting Act of 1919

**Citation:** PCC § 20500

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by a city council, as provided in the Street Lighting Act of 1919, Part 1 (commencing with Section 18000) of Division 14 of the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
