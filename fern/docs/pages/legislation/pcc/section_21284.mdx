# PCC § 21284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 95. Santa Cruz County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21284

---

## Text

The board shall cause an advertisement for bids for the performance of the work on the purchase of materials to be published in the county pursuant to Section 6061 of the Government Code, at least 10 days before the day set for opening bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
