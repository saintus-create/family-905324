# PCC § 10123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 2. Plans and Specifications

**Citation:** PCC § 10123

---

## Text

Contracts upon informal bids authorized under Section 10122 shall be let only to a holder of a valid state contractor’s license unless such work is exempt from such licensing requirement by any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
