# PCC § 10783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 5. Award of Contracts

**Citation:** PCC § 10783

---

## Text

The failure of the successful bidder to furnish any bond required by law, within the time fixed for execution of the contract, constitutes a failure to execute the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
