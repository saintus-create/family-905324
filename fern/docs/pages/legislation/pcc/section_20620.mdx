# PCC § 20620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 38. County Drainage Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20620

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by county drainage districts, as provided for in the County Drainage District Act, Division 17 (commencing with Section 56000) of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
