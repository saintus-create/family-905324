# PCC § 20260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 12. Marin County Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20260

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Marin County Transit District, as provided for in Part 7 (commencing with Section 70000) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
