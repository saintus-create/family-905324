# PCC § 10329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 3. Competitive Bidding and Other Acquisition Procedures

**Citation:** PCC § 10329

---

## Text

No person shall willfully split a single transaction into a series of transactions for the purpose of evading the bidding requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 537. Effective January 1, 2007.)
