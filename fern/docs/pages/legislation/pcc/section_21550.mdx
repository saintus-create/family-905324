# PCC § 21550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 120. San Diego County Flood Control District

**Citation:** PCC § 21550

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the San Diego County Flood Control District, as provided for in Chapter 55 of the Statutes of the 1966 First Extraordinary Session.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
