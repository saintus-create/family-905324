# PCC § 20106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 2. Schools—State School Building Aid Law of 1949

**Citation:** PCC § 20106

---

## Text

A school district shall not expend money apportioned under the State School Building Aid Law unless the contracts under which the funds are expended have been let after competitive bids pursuant to the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
