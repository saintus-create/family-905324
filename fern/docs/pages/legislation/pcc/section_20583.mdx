# PCC § 20583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 36. Water Storage Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20583

---

## Text

If less than the whole work provided for in the plans and specifications is to be done, the portion to be done must be particularly described in the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
