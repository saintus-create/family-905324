# PCC § 3504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Formation > Article: 5. Buy Clean California Act

**Citation:** PCC § 3504

---

## Text

In carrying out its duties under this article, an awarding authority shall strive to achieve a continuous reduction of emissions over time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 816, Sec. 3. (AB 262) Effective January 1, 2018.)
