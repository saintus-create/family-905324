# PCC § 10871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 9. Offenses

**Citation:** PCC § 10871

---

## Text

Any contractor or his or her agent or employee who corruptly permits the violation of any contract awarded under this chapter to the injury of the state is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
