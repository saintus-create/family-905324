# PCC § 20696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 44. County Harbor Commissions

**Citation:** PCC § 20696

---

## Text

All improvement, development or protection of any harbor done under this article shall be of a substantial and permanent character.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 87.)
