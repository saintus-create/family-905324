# PCC § 20470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 28. Separation of Grade Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20470

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by Separation of Grade Districts, as provided for in Part 2 (commencing with Section 8100) of Division 9 of the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
