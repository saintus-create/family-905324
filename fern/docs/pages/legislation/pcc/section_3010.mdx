# PCC § 3010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Formation > Article: 1. Roofing Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 3010

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to a school district operating in accordance with Section 20113 or a community college district operating in accordance with Section 20654.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 438, Sec. 1. (AB 635) Effective September 29, 2010.)
