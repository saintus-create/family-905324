# PCC § 21250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 92. San Benito County Water Conservation and Flood Control District

**Citation:** PCC § 21250

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the San Benito County Water Conservation and Flood Control District, as provided for in Chapter 1598 of the Statutes of 1953.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
