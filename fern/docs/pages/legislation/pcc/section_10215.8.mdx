# PCC § 10215.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 6.7. Progressive Design-Build Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 10215.8

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until December 31, 2033, and as of that date is repealed. The repeal of this article shall not affect the contracts entered into by the department, or the department’s authority to complete the design-build projects for which a design and preconstruction phase contract has been entered under this article, before December 31, 2033.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 58, Sec. 3. (SB 146) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of December 31, 2033, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6.7, commencing with Section 10215.)
