# PCC § 10348.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 4. Contracts for Services

**Citation:** PCC § 10348.5

---

## Text

Each state agency shall designate at least one currently existing person or position within the state agency as a contract manager. Every contract manager shall have knowledge of legal contractual arrangements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 759, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2001.)
