# PCC § 20411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 27. Legislative Bodies—Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20411

---

## Text

Before the legislative body awards any contract for doing any work or for furnishing materials, supplies and equipment or any portion thereof, authorized by this division, the legislative body shall pass a resolution ordering the work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
