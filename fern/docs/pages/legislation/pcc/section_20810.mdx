# PCC § 20810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 53. Fire Protection Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20810

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by fire protection districts as provided for in the Fire Protection District Law pursuant to Division 12 (commencing with Section 13000) of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 96.)
