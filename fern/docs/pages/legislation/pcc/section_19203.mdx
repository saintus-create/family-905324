# PCC § 19203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2.5. CONTRACTING BY JUDICIAL BRANCH ENTITIES

**Citation:** PCC § 19203

---

## Text

This part shall apply to all contracts initially entered into or amended by judicial branch entities on or after October 1, 2011.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 10, Sec. 21. (SB 78) Effective March 24, 2011.)
