# PCC § 3004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Formation > Article: 1. Roofing Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 3004

---

## Text

Specifications for any roof project shall be designed to promote competition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 438, Sec. 1. (AB 635) Effective September 29, 2010.)
