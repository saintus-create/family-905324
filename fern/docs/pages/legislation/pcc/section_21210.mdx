# PCC § 21210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 88. Sacramento County Water Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21210

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Sacramento County Water Agency, as provided for in Chapter 10 of the Statutes of the 1952 First Extraordinary Session.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
