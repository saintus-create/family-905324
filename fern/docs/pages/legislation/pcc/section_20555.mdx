# PCC § 20555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 34. Boulevard Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20555

---

## Text

If the cost of any construction work on the boulevard does not exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000) the commission may make contracts therefor without advertisement or it may do the work without letting any contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
