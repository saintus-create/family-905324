# PCC § 21230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 90. Marin County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21230

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Marin County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 666 of the Statutes of 1953.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
