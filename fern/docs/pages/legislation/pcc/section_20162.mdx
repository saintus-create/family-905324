# PCC § 20162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 4. Cities

**Citation:** PCC § 20162

---

## Text

When the expenditure required for a public project exceeds five thousand dollars ($5,000), it shall be contracted for and let to the lowest responsible bidder after notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
