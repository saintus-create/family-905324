# PCC § 10280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 9. Offenses

**Citation:** PCC § 10280

---

## Text

Any officer or employee of the department who corruptly performs any official act to the injury of the state, is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
