# PCC § 20350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 21. North San Diego County Transit Development Board

**Citation:** PCC § 20350

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the North San Diego County Transit Development Board, as provided for in Division 11.5 (commencing with Section 125000) of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
