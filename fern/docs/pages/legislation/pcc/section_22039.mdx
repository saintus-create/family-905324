# PCC § 22039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 3. Public Projects: Alternative Procedure

**Citation:** PCC § 22039

---

## Text

The governing body of the participating public agency or its designated representative shall adopt plans, specifications, and working details for all public projects exceeding the amount specified in subdivision (c) of Section 22032.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 953, Sec. 4. (AB 2192) Effective January 1, 2025.)
