# PCC § 10252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 8. Modifications; Performance; Payment

**Citation:** PCC § 10252

---

## Text

The director may grant such extensions of time for completion as he deems for the best interests of the state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
