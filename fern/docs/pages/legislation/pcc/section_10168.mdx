# PCC § 10168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 4. Bids and Bidders

**Citation:** PCC § 10168

---

## Text

Whether or not bids are opened exactly at the time fixed in the public notice for opening bids, a bid shall not be received after that time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
