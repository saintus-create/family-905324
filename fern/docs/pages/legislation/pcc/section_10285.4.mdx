# PCC § 10285.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 10. Ineligibility to Contract

**Citation:** PCC § 10285.4

---

## Text

In the event that any state agency has suspended any person under this article, that agency may terminate the suspension if it determines that termination is in the best interest of the agency. In the event of termination, any suspension by adoption pursuant to Section 10285.3 also shall be terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 376, Sec. 1.)
