# PCC § 1604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.6. Electronic Transmissions

**Citation:** PCC § 1604

---

## Text

Posting solicitations and receiving bids through an electronic procurement platform shall satisfy all statutory requirements for public advertising, bid submission, and document retention under this code, including the procurement platform requirements set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 26. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
