# PCC § 22173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.1. Local Agency Progressive Design-Build Contracting

**Citation:** PCC § 22173

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects, expands, alters, or limits any rights or remedies otherwise available at law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 243, Sec. 1. (SB 991) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 22174.)
