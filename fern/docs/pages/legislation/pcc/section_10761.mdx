# PCC § 10761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 4. Bids and Bidders

**Citation:** PCC § 10761

---

## Text

The trustees shall adopt and apply a uniform system of rating bidders, on the basis of the standard questionnaires and financial statements, in respect to the size of the contracts upon which each bidder is qualified to bid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
