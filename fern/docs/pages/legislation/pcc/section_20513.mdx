# PCC § 20513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 31. City Councils—Street Lighting Act of 1931

**Citation:** PCC § 20513

---

## Text

The contract shall specify a date certain when the improvement shall be started, the date to be not more than 30 days after the execution thereof, and that the contract shall continue in force for a period of one year from the effective date of the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
