# PCC § 10115.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 1.5. Minority and Women Business Participation Goals for State Contracts

**Citation:** PCC § 10115.4

---

## Text

In implementing this article, the awarding department shall utilize existing resources such as the Office of Small and Minority Business, the Minority Business Development Agency, and the Small Business Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 61, Sec. 3.)
