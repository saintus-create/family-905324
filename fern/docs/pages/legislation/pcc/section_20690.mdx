# PCC § 20690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 44. County Harbor Commissions

**Citation:** PCC § 20690

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by county harbor commissions as provided for in Division 7 (commencing with Section 4000) of the Harbors and Navigation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 87.)
