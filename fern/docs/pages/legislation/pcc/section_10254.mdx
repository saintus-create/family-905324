# PCC § 10254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 8. Modifications; Performance; Payment

**Citation:** PCC § 10254

---

## Text

If the defaults are not remedied within the time specified in the notice, the contractor’s control shall terminate as of the expiration of that time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
