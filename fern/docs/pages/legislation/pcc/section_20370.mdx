# PCC § 20370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 23. Fresno Metropolitan Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20370

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Fresno Metropolitan Transit District, as provided for in Chapter 1932 of the Statutes of 1961.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
