# PCC § 10423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 9. Remedies and Penalties

**Citation:** PCC § 10423

---

## Text

Any person contracting with the state by oral or written contract who corruptly permits the violation of any contract made under this chapter is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1231, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1983.)
