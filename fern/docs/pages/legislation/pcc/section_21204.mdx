# PCC § 21204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 87. Yolo County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21204

---

## Text

The provisions of this section have no application to a contract entered into with the United States under the authority of Section 3 of this act, or to a contract authorized by a vote of the electorate of the district or zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
