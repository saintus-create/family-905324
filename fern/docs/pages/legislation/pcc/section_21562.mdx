# PCC § 21562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 121. Metropolitan Water District

**Citation:** PCC § 21562

---

## Text

A district may join with one or more other public or private corporations (within or without this state) for the purpose of carrying out any of its powers, and for that purpose may contract with any such other public or private corporation to finance acquisitions, constructions, and operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
