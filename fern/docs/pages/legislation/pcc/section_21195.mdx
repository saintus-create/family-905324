# PCC § 21195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 86.5. Solano County Water Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21195

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Solano County Water Agency, as provided for in Chapter 573 of the Statutes of 1989.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 906, Sec. 14.4. Effective October 8, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 24 of Ch. 906.)
