# PCC § 20928.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 60.6. Surface Storage Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 20928.4

---

## Text

Any project constructed pursuant to this article shall be subject to Part 1 (commencing with Section 6000) of Division 3 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 760, Sec. 1. (AB 2551) Effective January 1, 2017.)
