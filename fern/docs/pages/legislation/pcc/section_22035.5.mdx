# PCC § 22035.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 3. Public Projects: Alternative Procedure

**Citation:** PCC § 22035.5

---

## Text

In counties that are under court order to relieve justice facility overcrowding, the procedures and restrictions specified in Section 20134 shall apply to all contracts issued under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 993, Sec. 1.)
