# PCC § 22153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Recycled Product Procurement Mandates Pertaining to Local Governments

**Citation:** PCC § 22153

---

## Text

All printing contracts made by any local public entity shall provide that the paper used shall meet the recycled content requirements of Section 12209.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 590, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2006.)
