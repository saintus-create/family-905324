# PCC § 22001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 1. Legislative Intent and Definitions

**Citation:** PCC § 22001.5

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2009, the Controller shall send a notice to all public agencies describing the provisions of this chapter and the benefits of using its provisions. This notice shall also be included in any notification issued by the Controller pursuant to Section 22020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 144, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
