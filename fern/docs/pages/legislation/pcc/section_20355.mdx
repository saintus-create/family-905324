# PCC § 20355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 21.5. Sonoma-Marin Area Rail Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20355

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Sonoma-Marin Area Rail Transit District, as provided for in Part 16 (commencing with Section 105000) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
