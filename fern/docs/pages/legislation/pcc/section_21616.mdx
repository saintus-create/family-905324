# PCC § 21616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 126. Tahoe-Truckee Sanitation Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21616

---

## Text

The board may establish the manner of calling for bids and letting contracts, and all contracts shall be entered into upon such terms and in such manner as the board may authorize.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
