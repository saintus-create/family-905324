# PCC § 20380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 24. West Bay Rapid Transit Authority

**Citation:** PCC § 20380

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the West Bay Rapid Transit Authority, as provided for in Chapter 104 of the Statutes of 1964, First Extraordinary Session.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
