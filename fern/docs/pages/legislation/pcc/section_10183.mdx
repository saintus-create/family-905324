# PCC § 10183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 5. Award of Contracts

**Citation:** PCC § 10183

---

## Text

The failure of the successful bidder to furnish any bond required of him by law, within the time fixed for his execution of the contract, constitutes a failure to execute the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
