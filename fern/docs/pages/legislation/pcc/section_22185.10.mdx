# PCC § 22185.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.7. Local Agency Design-Build Contracting

**Citation:** PCC § 22185.10

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects, expands, alters, or limits any rights or remedies otherwise available at law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 500, Sec. 1. (SB 706) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 22185.11.)
