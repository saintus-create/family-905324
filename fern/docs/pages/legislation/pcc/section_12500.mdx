# PCC § 12500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. The Federal Laboratory Contracting Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Federal Laboratory Contracting Act.
