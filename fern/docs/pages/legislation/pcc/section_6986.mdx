# PCC § 6986

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.8. High Road Jobs in Transportation-Related Public Contracts and Grants Pilot Program

**Citation:** PCC § 6986

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. (SB 674) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 6.8, commencing with Section 6980.)
