# PCC § 6956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.6. Alternative Project Delivery Program: Construction Manager/General Contractor Authority

**Citation:** PCC § 6956

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects, expands, alters, or limits any rights or remedies otherwise available at law.

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 1549) Effective January 1, 2013.)
