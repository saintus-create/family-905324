# PCC § 10195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 6. State Agency Design-Build Projects

## Text

Nothing in this article affects, expands, alters, or limits any rights or remedies otherwise available at law.
