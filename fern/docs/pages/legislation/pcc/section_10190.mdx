# PCC § 10190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 6. State Agency Design-Build Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 10190

---

## Text

The director shall notify the State Public Works Board regarding the method to be used for selecting the design-build entity, prior to advertising the design-build project.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 247, Sec. 3. (SB 626) Effective January 1, 2022.)
