# PCC § 10115.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 1.5. Minority and Women Business Participation Goals for State Contracts

**Citation:** PCC § 10115.9

---

## Text

A limited liability company may be certified as a disabled veteran business enterprise pursuant to this article if the limited liability company is wholly owned by one or more disabled veterans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 632, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
