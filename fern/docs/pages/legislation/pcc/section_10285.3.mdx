# PCC § 10285.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 10. Ineligibility to Contract

**Citation:** PCC § 10285.3

---

## Text

In the event that any state agency has suspended any person under this article, any other state agency shall adopt the terms of that suspension without further notice or hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 376, Sec. 1.)
