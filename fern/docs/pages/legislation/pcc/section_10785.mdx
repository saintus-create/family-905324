# PCC § 10785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 5. Award of Contracts

**Citation:** PCC § 10785

---

## Text

If the trustees deem the acceptance of the lowest responsible bid or bids is not for the best interests of the state, they may reject all bids and proceed by day’s labor or advertise for other bids in the manner required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
