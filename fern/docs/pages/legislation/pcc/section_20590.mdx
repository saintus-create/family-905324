# PCC § 20590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 36. Water Storage Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20590

## Text

All contracts and other documents executed by the board shall be signed by the president and by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
