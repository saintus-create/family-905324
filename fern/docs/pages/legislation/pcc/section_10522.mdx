# PCC § 10522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.1. University of California Competitive Bidding > Article: 5. Remedies and Penalties

**Citation:** PCC § 10522

---

## Text

Any officer or employee of the University of California who corruptly performs any official act under this chapter to the injury of the university is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1122, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003. Operative July 1, 2003, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1122.)
