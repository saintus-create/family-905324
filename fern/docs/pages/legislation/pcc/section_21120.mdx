# PCC § 21120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 79. Monterey County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21120

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Monterey County Water Resources Agency, as provided for in the Monterey County Water Resources Agency Act.
