# PCC § 22033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 3. Public Projects: Alternative Procedure

**Citation:** PCC § 22033

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful to split or separate into smaller work orders or projects any project for the purpose of evading the provisions of this article requiring work to be done by contract after competitive bidding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 21203 (as added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1054) by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1019, Sec. 54.)
