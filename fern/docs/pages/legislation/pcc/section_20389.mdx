# PCC § 20389

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 24.5. Job Order Contracting Procurement by the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PCC § 20389

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 87, Sec. 1. (AB 499) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 24.5, commencing with Section 20385.)
