# PCC § 12166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. State Agency Buy Recycled Campaign > Article: 2. Recycled Paper Products

**Citation:** PCC § 12166

---

## Text

The Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery may contract as necessary for the recycling of products which have been returned pursuant to Section 12165.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 72. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
