# PCC § 20497

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 29. Public Entities—Municipal Improvement Act of 1913

**Citation:** PCC § 20497

---

## Text

If in the opinion of the legislative body the public interest will not be served by allowing the property owners to take the contract it may so provide in the resolution of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
