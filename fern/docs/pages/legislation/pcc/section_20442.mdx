# PCC § 20442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 27. Legislative Bodies—Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20442

---

## Text

The contract shall contain express notice that, in no case, except where it is otherwise provided by law or by charter will the city, or any officer thereof, be liable for any portion of the expense, nor for any delinquency of persons or property assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
