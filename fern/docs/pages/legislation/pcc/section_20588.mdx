# PCC § 20588

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 36. Water Storage Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20588

---

## Text

Upon receipt of an offer for a less price than that specified in the lowest rejected bid, the board may enter into a contract for the furnishing of the materials with the person who has offered them at the lowest price.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
