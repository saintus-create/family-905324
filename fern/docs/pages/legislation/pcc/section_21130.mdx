# PCC § 21130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 80. Sonoma County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21130

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Sonoma County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 994 of the Statutes of 1949.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
