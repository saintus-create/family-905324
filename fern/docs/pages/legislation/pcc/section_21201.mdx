# PCC § 21201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 87. Yolo County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21201

---

## Text

All contracts for the construction of any unit of work, except as provided, estimated to cost in excess of ten thousand dollars ($10,000) shall be let to the lowest responsible bidder in the manner as provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 142, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1999.)
