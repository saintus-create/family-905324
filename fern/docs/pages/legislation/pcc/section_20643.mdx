# PCC § 20643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 40. Municipal Water Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20643

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 20642, contracts, in writing or otherwise, for the acquisition or disposal of any real or personal property may be let without calling for competitive bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
