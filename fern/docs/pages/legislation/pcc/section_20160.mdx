# PCC § 20160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 4. Cities

**Citation:** PCC § 20160

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts awarded by cities subject to Title 4 (commencing with Section 34000) of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
