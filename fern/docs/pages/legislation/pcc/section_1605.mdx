# PCC § 1605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.6. Electronic Transmissions

**Citation:** PCC § 1605

---

## Text

An electronic procurement platform authorized under this chapter shall satisfy all of the following requirements:

(a) Include time-stamped bid submissions and acknowledgments.

(b) Maintain automated audit trails.

(c) Comply with applicable state information security standards.

(d) Allow vendors to access their submission history and receipts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 27. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
