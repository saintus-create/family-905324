# PCC § 6827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Transportation Design-Build Program

**Citation:** PCC § 6827

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects, expands, alters, or limits any rights or remedies otherwise available at law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 586, Sec. 3. (AB 401) Effective January 1, 2014. Repealed conditionally on or before January 1, 2034, pursuant to Section 6829.)
