# PCC § 20603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 37. County Waterworks Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20603

---

## Text

The district shall have the right to require such bonds as it may deem best from the successful bidder, to insure the faithful performance of the contract, and shall also have the right to reject any and all bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
