# PCC § 20720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 46. Harbor Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20720

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by harbor districts as provided for in Part 3 (commencing with Section 6000) of Division 8 of the Harbors and Navigation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 89.)
