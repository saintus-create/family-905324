# PCC § 10424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 9. Remedies and Penalties

**Citation:** PCC § 10424

---

## Text

Persons convicted under Section 10422 or 10423 are also liable to the state for double the amount the state may have lost, or be liable to lose by reason of the acts made crimes by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1231, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1983.)
