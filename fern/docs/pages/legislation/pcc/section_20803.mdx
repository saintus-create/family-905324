# PCC § 20803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 52. Sanitary Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20803

---

## Text

When the expenditure required for a district project exceeds fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000), it shall be contracted for and let to the lowest responsible bidder after notice, subject to Section 20805.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 142, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1999.)
