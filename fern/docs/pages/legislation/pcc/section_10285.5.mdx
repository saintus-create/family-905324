# PCC § 10285.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 10. Ineligibility to Contract

**Citation:** PCC § 10285.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall require any state agency to contract with, or to permit the employment of a person as a subcontractor, or any person on any project funded in whole or part by federal funds, if the person has been suspended or debarred by the federal agency providing the funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 376, Sec. 1.)
