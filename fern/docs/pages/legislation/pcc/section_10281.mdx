# PCC § 10281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 9. Offenses

**Citation:** PCC § 10281

---

## Text

Any contractor or his agent or employee who corruptly permits the violation of any contract awarded under this part to the injury of the state, is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
