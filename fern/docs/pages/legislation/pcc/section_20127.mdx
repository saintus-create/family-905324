# PCC § 20127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 3.5. Counties

**Citation:** PCC § 20127

---

## Text

All bidders shall be afforded opportunity to examine the plans, specifications, strain sheets, and working details.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
