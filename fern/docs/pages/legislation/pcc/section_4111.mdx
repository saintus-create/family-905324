# PCC § 4111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Subletting and Subcontracting

**Citation:** PCC § 4111

---

## Text

Violation of this chapter by a licensee under Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 7000) of Division 3 of the Business and Professions Code constitutes grounds for disciplinary action by the Contractors State License Board, in addition to the penalties prescribed in Section 4110.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 42.1.)
