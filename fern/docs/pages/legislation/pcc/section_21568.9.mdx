# PCC § 21568.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 121.1. Metropolitan Water District of Southern California-Alternative Project Delivery Program

**Citation:** PCC § 21568.9

---

## Text

If the district elects to award a project pursuant to this article, retention proceeds withheld by the district from the design-build entity or Construction Manager/General Contractor shall not exceed 5 percent if a performance and payment bond, issued by an admitted surety insurer, is required in the solicitation of bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 275, Sec. 2. (AB 1845) Effective January 1, 2023.)
