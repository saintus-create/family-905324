# PCC § 20340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 20. Transit Development Boards

**Citation:** PCC § 20340

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the San Diego Metropolitan Transit Development Board, as provided for in Division 11 (commencing with Section 120000) of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 557, Sec. 1. Effective October 5, 2005.)
