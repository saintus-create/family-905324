# PCC § 10383.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 5.5. Federal Surplus Personal Property

**Citation:** PCC § 10383.4

---

## Text

The department shall develop a detailed state plan of operation in accordance with subdivision (j) of Section 203 of the Federal Property and Administrative Services Act of 1949, as amended, and the Governor shall certify and submit the plan to the Administrator of the Federal General Services Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 196, Sec. 8. Effective June 13, 1984. Operative July 1, 1984, by Sec. 8.5 of Ch. 196.)
