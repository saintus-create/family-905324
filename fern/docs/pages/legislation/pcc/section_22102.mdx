# PCC § 22102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Acquisition of Electronic Data Processing

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Southern California Rapid Transit District.
