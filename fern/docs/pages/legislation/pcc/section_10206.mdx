# PCC § 10206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 6.5. Department of Water Resources Design-Build Projects

**Citation:** PCC § 10206

---

## Text

For purposes of projects at the Salton Sea, the director shall notify the California Water Commission regarding the method to be used for selecting the design-build entry, prior to advertising the design-build project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 247, Sec. 4. (SB 626) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, pursuant to Section 10214.)
