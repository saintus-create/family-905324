# PCC § 21380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 103. San Mateo County Flood Control District

**Citation:** PCC § 21380

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the San Mateo County Flood Control District, as provided for in Chapter 2108 of the Statutes of 1959.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
