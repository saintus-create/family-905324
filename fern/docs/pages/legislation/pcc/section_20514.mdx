# PCC § 20514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 31. City Councils—Street Lighting Act of 1931

**Citation:** PCC § 20514

---

## Text

The city council may, by resolution passed prior to the notice inviting bids, impose such other reasonable conditions, including faithful performance bonds and materialmen’s bonds, as may in its discretion appear to be necessary and proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
