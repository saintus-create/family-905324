# PCC § 22036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 3. Public Projects: Alternative Procedure

**Citation:** PCC § 22036

---

## Text

The commission shall determine, on a county-by-county basis, the appropriate construction trade journals which shall receive mailed, faxed, or emailed notice of all informal and formal construction contracts being bid for work within the specified county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 269, Sec. 30. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2016.)
