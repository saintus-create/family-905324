# PCC § 20560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 35. Irrigation Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20560

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by irrigation districts, as provided for in the Irrigation District Law, Division 11 (commencing with Section 20500) of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
