# PCC § 20250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 11. San Joaquin Regional Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20250

---

## Text

This article applies to contracts by the San Joaquin Regional Transit District, as provided for in Part 5 (commencing with Section 50000) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 704, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1995.)
