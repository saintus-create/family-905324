# PCC § 20207.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 6.5. Public Utility Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20207.3

---

## Text

All bids shall be opened by the board at an hour and place to be stated in the advertisements for proposals, in the presence of all bidders who attend, and the bidders may inspect the bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
