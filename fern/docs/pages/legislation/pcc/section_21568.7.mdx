# PCC § 21568.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 121.1. Metropolitan Water District of Southern California-Alternative Project Delivery Program

**Citation:** PCC § 21568.7

---

## Text

(a) The district may not award a design-build-operate contract for any project pursuant to this article. A contract may, however, cover operations during a training or transition period.

(b) The district shall perform construction inspection services for all projects authorized and awarded pursuant to this article, and it shall use district employees to the fullest extent possible, and then consultants under contract with the district to perform these services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 275, Sec. 2. (AB 1845) Effective January 1, 2023.)
