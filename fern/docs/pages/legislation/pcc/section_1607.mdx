# PCC § 1607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.6. Electronic Transmissions

**Citation:** PCC § 1607

---

## Text

This chapter shall supersede any conflicting provisions in code with respect to electronic procurement activities by public entities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 29. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
