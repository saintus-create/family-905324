# PCC § 21280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 95. Santa Cruz County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21280

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Santa Cruz County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 1489 of the Statutes of 1955.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
