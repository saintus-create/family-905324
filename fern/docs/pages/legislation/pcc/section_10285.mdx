# PCC § 10285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 10. Ineligibility to Contract

**Citation:** PCC § 10285

---

## Text

The term “person,” as used in this article, means any individual, partnership, joint venture, or association or any other organization or any combination thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 376, Sec. 1.)
