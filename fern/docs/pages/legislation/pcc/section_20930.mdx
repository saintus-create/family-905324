# PCC § 20930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 61. Levee District No. 1 of Sutter County

**Citation:** PCC § 20930

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by Levee District No. 1 of Sutter County, as provided for in Chapter 349 of the Statutes of 1873–74.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
