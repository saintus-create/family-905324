# PCC § 20202.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 6.5. Public Utility Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20202.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by public utility districts as provided for in the Public Utility District Act pursuant to Division 7 (commencing with Section 15501) of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
