# PCC § 20105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 2. Schools—State School Building Aid Law of 1949

**Citation:** PCC § 20105

---

## Text

This article shall apply to contracts subject to the State School Building Aid Law of 1949 provided for in Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 15700) of Part 10 of the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 539. Effective January 1, 2007.)
