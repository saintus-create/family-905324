# PCC § 10262.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 8. Modifications; Performance; Payment

**Citation:** PCC § 10262.3

---

## Text

To facilitate enforcement of Section 10262, the Department of General Services shall publish in the California State Contracts Register notice of progress payments made to prime contractors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 374, Sec. 1.)
