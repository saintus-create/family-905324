# PCC § 22018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 2. California Uniform Construction Cost Accounting Commission

**Citation:** PCC § 22018

---

## Text

The Controller shall, upon receipt of the commission’s recommendations, review and evaluate the recommended procedures and either formally adopt or reject the recommended procedures within 90 days of submission by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 21108 by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1019, Sec. 48.)
