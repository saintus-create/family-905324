# PCC § 6980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.8. High Road Jobs in Transportation-Related Public Contracts and Grants Pilot Program

**Citation:** PCC § 6980

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the High Road Jobs in Transportation-Related Public Contracts and Grants Pilot Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. (SB 674) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 6986.)
