# PCC § 10821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 7. Contract Requirements

**Citation:** PCC § 10821

---

## Text

Every contract shall provide for the filing of separate performance and payment bonds by the contractor executed by an admitted surety insurer, subject to the approval of the trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
