# PCC § 10780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 5. Award of Contracts

**Citation:** PCC § 10780

---

## Text

On the day named in the public notice the trustees shall publicly open the sealed bids and award the contracts to the lowest responsible bidders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
