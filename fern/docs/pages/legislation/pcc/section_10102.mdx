# PCC § 10102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 1. Scope of Chapter and General Provisions

**Citation:** PCC § 10102

---

## Text

Improvements on the property of the state on the waterfront of the City and County of San Francisco under the jurisdiction of the San Francisco Port Commission are not subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 328, Sec. 186. (SB 1330) Effective January 1, 2011.)
