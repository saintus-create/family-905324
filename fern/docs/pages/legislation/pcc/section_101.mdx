# PCC § 101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PURPOSE AND PRELIMINARY MATTERS

**Citation:** PCC § 101

---

## Text

California public contract law should be efficient and the product of the best of modern practice and research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 42, Sec. 1.)
