# PCC § 20453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 27. Legislative Bodies—Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20453

---

## Text

Changes may exceed the 20-percent limitation in cost when the legislative body orders such changes. That portion of the added cost which exceeds the 20-percent limitation shall be paid by the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
