# PCC § 10224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 7. Contract Requirements

**Citation:** PCC § 10224

---

## Text

The performance bond shall guarantee the faithful performance of the contract by the contractor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
