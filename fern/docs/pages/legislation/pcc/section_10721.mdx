# PCC § 10721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 2. Plans and Specifications

**Citation:** PCC § 10721

---

## Text

The plans, specifications, and estimates of cost shall be approved by the trustees, and the original draft or a certified copy filed permanently in the office of the trustees before further action is taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
