# PCC § 21560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 121. Metropolitan Water District

**Citation:** PCC § 21560

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by a Metropolitan Water District, as provided for in Chapter 209 of the Statutes of 1969.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
