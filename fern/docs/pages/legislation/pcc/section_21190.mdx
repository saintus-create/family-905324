# PCC § 21190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 86. Contra Costa County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21190

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Contra Costa County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 1617 of the Statutes of 1951.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
