# PCC § 20791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 51. Sewer Maintenance Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20791

---

## Text

“Maintenance of sewers” as used in this chapter includes the extension and enlargement of sewers, within a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 94.)
