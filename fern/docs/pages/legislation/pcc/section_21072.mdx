# PCC § 21072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 74. Ventura County Watershed Protection District

**Citation:** PCC § 21072

---

## Text

(a) Emergency work may be done by negotiated contract without advertising for bids or requiring bonds.

(b) In case of an emergency, if notice for bids to let contracts will not be given, the board of supervisors shall comply with Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 22050).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 67, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
