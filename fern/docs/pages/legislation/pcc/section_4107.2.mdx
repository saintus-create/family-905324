# PCC § 4107.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Subletting and Subcontracting

**Citation:** PCC § 4107.2

---

## Text

No subcontractor listed by a prime contractor under Section 4104 as furnishing and installing carpeting, shall voluntarily sublet his or her subcontract with respect to any portion of the labor to be performed unless he or she specified the subcontractor in his or her bid for that subcontract to the prime contractor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 42.1.)
