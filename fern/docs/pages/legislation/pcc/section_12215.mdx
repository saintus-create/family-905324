# PCC § 12215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. State Agency Buy Recycled Campaign > Article: 4. Recycled Materials, Goods, and Supplies

**Citation:** PCC § 12215

---

## Text

Each state agency may, at the discretion of the individual agency director or their designee, print a statement on recycled products selected by the agency director. This statement shall be determined by the department, in consultation with CalRecycle, and shall be similar to the following: “Contains at least ____ percent postconsumer material.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 517, Sec. 11. (AB 661) Effective January 1, 2023.)
