# PCC § 20760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 49. River Port Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20760

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by river port districts as provided for in Part 6 (commencing with Section 6800) of Division 8 of the Harbors and Navigation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 92.)
