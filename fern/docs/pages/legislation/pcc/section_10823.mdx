# PCC § 10823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 7. Contract Requirements

**Citation:** PCC § 10823

---

## Text

The payment bond shall secure the payment of the claims of laborers, mechanics, or materialmen employed on the work under the contract and shall contain all other provisions required by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
