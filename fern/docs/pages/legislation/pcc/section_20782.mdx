# PCC § 20782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 50. Sanitation Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20782

---

## Text

A district may make and perform any agreement with any public or private corporation of any kind or any person for the joint construction, acquisition, disposition, or operation of any property or works of a kind which might be constructed, acquired, disposed of, or operated by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 93.)
