# PCC § 22175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.5. Civic Openness in Negotiations

**Citation:** PCC § 22175

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Civic Reporting Openness in Negotiations Efficiency Act, or CRONEY.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 714, Sec. 1. (SB 331) Effective January 1, 2016.)
