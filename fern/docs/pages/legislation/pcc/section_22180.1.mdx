# PCC § 22180.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.6. Transit Progressive Design-Build Contracting

**Citation:** PCC § 22180.1

---

## Text

A local agency may procure progressive design-build contracts and use the progressive design-build contracting process described in this chapter for up to 10 public works projects in excess of five million dollars ($5,000,000) for each project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 310, Sec. 1. (SB 617) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 22180.11)
