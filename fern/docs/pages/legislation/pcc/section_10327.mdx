# PCC § 10327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 3. Competitive Bidding and Other Acquisition Procedures

**Citation:** PCC § 10327

---

## Text

Except for motor vehicles described in Section 43805 of the Health and Safety Code, the provisions of Article 1 (commencing with Section 43800) of Chapter 4 of Part 5 of Division 26 of the Health and Safety Code shall govern the acquisition of all motor vehicles by the state to the extent that the department determines that these low-emission vehicles are reasonable to meet state needs pursuant to Section 43804 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 776, Sec. 31. Effective September 27, 2000.)
