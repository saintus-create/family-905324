# PCC § 10240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 7.1. Resolution of Contract Claims

**Citation:** PCC § 10240

---

## Text

The remedy for the resolution of claims arising under contracts made under the provisions of this chapter shall be arbitration pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 90.)
