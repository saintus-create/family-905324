# PCC § 20310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 17. Golden Empire Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20310

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Golden Empire Transit District, as provided for in Part 13 (commencing with Section 101000) of the Public Utilities Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 155, Sec. 2.)
