# PCC § 21612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 126. Tahoe-Truckee Sanitation Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21612

---

## Text

The agency may join with one or more public entities, private corporations, or other persons for the purpose of carrying out any of the powers of the agency, and for that purpose may contract with any such other public entity, private corporation, or person to finance acquisitions, constructions, and operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
