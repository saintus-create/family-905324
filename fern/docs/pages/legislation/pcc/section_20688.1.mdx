# PCC § 20688.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 43.1. Redevelopment Agencies

**Citation:** PCC § 20688.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts awarded by entities as provided in Part 1 (commencing with Section 33000) of Division 24 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 51.5.)
