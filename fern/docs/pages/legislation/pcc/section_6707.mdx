# PCC § 6707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.3. Construction Manager/General Contractor Authority: Department of Transportation

**Citation:** PCC § 6707

---

## Text

Contracts awarded pursuant to this chapter shall be valid until the project is completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 752, Sec. 1. (AB 2498) Effective January 1, 2013.)
