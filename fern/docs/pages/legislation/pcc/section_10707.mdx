# PCC § 10707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PCC § 10707

---

## Text

The trustees may enter into service contracts. Such contracts shall be subject only to Section 10820 and to such other provisions of this chapter, if any, as the trustees may from time to time designate as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
