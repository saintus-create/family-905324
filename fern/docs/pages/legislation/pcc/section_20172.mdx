# PCC § 20172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 4. Cities

**Citation:** PCC § 20172

---

## Text

If the successful bidder fails to execute the contract, the amount of the bidder’s security shall be forfeited to the city except as hereinafter provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
