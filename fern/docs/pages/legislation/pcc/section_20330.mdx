# PCC § 20330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 19. San Mateo County Transit District

**Citation:** PCC § 20330

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the San Mateo County Transit District, as provided for in Part 15 (commencing with Section 103000) of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
