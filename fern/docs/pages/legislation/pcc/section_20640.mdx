# PCC § 20640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 40. Municipal Water Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20640

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by municipal water districts, as provided for in the Municipal Water District Law of 1911, Division 20 (commencing with Section 71000) of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
