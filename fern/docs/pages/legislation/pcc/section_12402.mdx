# PCC § 12402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Environmentally Preferable Purchasing

**Citation:** PCC § 12402

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this chapter shall prohibit, limit, or supersede recycled content requirements pursuant to any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 575, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
