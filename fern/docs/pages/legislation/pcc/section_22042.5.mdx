# PCC § 22042.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 3. Public Projects: Alternative Procedure

**Citation:** PCC § 22042.5

---

## Text

The commission shall review practices of any participating public agency where an interested party presents evidence that the public agency is not in compliance with Section 22034 or 22037.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 953, Sec. 6. (AB 2192) Effective January 1, 2025.)
