# PCC § 21090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 76. Riverside County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21090

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Riverside County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 1122 of the Statutes of 1945.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
