# PCC § 20886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 58. Public Entities—Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20886

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be deemed to relieve any city or public agency, if the work or improvement is to be performed by the city or public agency, from any competitive bidding requirements imposed, as to work performed by the city or public agency, by any charter or statutory provision applicable to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 101.)
