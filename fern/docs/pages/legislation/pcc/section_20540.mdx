# PCC § 20540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 33. Joint Highway Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20540

---

## Text

All construction contracts shall provide that a maximum of eight hours shall constitute a day’s labor and that three dollars ($3) shall be the minimum amount paid for a day’s labor performed upon any construction work covered by the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
