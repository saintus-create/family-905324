# PCC § 22198

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.8. Port of Long Beach Pier Wind Project Contracting

**Citation:** PCC § 22198

---

## Text

All documents related to the project shall be subject to disclosure under the California Public Records Act (Division 10 (commencing with Section 7920.000) of Title 1 of the Government Code), except those exempted from disclosure under the act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 707, Sec. 1. (AB 2235) Effective January 1, 2025.)
