# PCC § 19208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2.5. CONTRACTING BY JUDICIAL BRANCH ENTITIES

**Citation:** PCC § 19208

---

## Text

Nothing in this part is intended, nor shall it be construed to permit, the application of provisions of this code that do not apply to state agencies and departments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 10, Sec. 21. (SB 78) Effective March 24, 2011.)
