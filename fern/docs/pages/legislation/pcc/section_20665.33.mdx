# PCC § 20665.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 41.5. Job Order Contracting for Community College Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20665.33

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 303, Sec. 2. (AB 846) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Termination date applies to Article 41.5, commencing with Section 20665.20.)
