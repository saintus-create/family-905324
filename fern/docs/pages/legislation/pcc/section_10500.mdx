# PCC § 10500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.1. University of California Competitive Bidding > Article: 1. Construction

**Citation:** PCC § 10500

---

## Text

As used in this article, “project” includes the erection, construction, alteration, repair, or improvement of any University of California structure, building, road, or other improvement that will exceed in cost, including labor and materials, a total of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 563, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
