# PCC § 21530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 118. Castaic Lake Water Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21530

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Santa Clarita Valley Water Agency.
