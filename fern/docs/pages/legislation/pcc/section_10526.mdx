# PCC § 10526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.1. University of California Competitive Bidding > Article: 5. Remedies and Penalties

**Citation:** PCC § 10526

---

## Text

Sections 10522, 10523, 10524, and 10525 of this article do not apply to violations of Article 2.5 (commencing with Section 10510.4) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 699, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
