# PCC § 10121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 2. Plans and Specifications

**Citation:** PCC § 10121

---

## Text

The original draft or a certified copy of the plans, specifications, and estimates of cost shall be filed permanently in the office of the department before further action is taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 857, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
