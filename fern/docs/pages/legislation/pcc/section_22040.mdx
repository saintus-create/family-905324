# PCC § 22040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 3. Public Projects: Alternative Procedure

**Citation:** PCC § 22040

---

## Text

Any person may examine the plans, specifications, or working details, or all of these, adopted by the public agency for any project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 21210 (as added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1054) by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1019, Sec. 62.)
