# PCC § 10388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 6. Purchase of Federal Surplus Property

**Citation:** PCC § 10388

---

## Text

Unless otherwise prescribed by the department, state agencies desiring to purchase or acquire federal property shall submit their estimates or requisitions therefor to the department, and shall pay for the property, in accordance with existing laws governing state purchases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1231, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1983.)
