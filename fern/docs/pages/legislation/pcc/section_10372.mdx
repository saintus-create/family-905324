# PCC § 10372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 4. Contracts for Services

**Citation:** PCC § 10372

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 2024, when seeking to award a contract for commercial rental car services, the department shall develop criteria to evaluate bidders, at least in part, based on the number of zero-emission vehicles or plug-in hybrid electric vehicles in their rental car fleet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 360, Sec. 2. (SB 1010) Effective January 1, 2023.)
