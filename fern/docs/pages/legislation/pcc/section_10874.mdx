# PCC § 10874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 9. Offenses

**Citation:** PCC § 10874

---

## Text

Such persons are also liable to the state for double the amount the state may have lost, or be liable to lose by reason of the acts made crimes by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
