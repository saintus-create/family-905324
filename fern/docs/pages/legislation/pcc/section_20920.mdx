# PCC § 20920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 60.5. Reclamation Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20920

---

## Text

This article applies to contracts by reclamation districts formed under special acts of the Legislature or under Division 15 (commencing with Section 50000) of the Water Code or any district which is otherwise authorized to act under that division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1042, Sec. 1.)
