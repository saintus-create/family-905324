# PCC § 20604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 37. County Waterworks Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20604

---

## Text

In case of an emergency, if notice for bids to let contracts will not be given, the district shall comply with Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 22050).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 803, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
