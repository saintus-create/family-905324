# PCC § 10820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 7. Contract Requirements

**Citation:** PCC § 10820

---

## Text

Every contract awarded under this chapter shall be submitted to the Attorney General or the attorney appointed according to law and authorized to represent the trustees. Such a contract is not binding on the state until the appropriate attorney finds it to be in accordance with the requirements of this chapter, and endorses such finding thereon.

A certified copy of each contract shall be filed with the Controller, but the failure to so file does not invalidate it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
