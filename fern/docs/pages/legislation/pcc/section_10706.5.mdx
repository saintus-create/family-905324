# PCC § 10706.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PCC § 10706.5

---

## Text

The trustees may enter into an agreement with an auxiliary organization, as defined in Section 89901 of the Education Code, under which the auxiliary organization may carry out any of the functions of the trustees under this chapter, upon terms that are mutually agreed upon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 346, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
