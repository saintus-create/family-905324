# PCC § 21220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 89. Vallejo Flood and Wastewater District

**Citation:** PCC § 21220

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Vallejo Flood and Wastewater District, as provided for in Chapter 17 of the Statutes of the 1952 First Extraordinary Session.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 387, Sec. 15. (SB 205) Effective January 1, 2018.)
