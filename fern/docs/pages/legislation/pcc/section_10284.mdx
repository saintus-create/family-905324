# PCC § 10284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 9. Offenses

**Citation:** PCC § 10284

---

## Text

Such persons are also liable to the state for double the amount the state may have lost, or be liable to lose by reason of the acts made crimes by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
