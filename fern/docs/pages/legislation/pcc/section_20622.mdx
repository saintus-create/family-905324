# PCC § 20622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 38. County Drainage Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20622

---

## Text

Any contract for the doing of the work and furnishing any or all of the material shall be let to the lowest responsible bidder submitting a sealed bid in response to a notice calling for bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
