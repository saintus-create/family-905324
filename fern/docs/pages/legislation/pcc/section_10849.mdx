# PCC § 10849

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 8. Modifications; Performance; Payment

**Citation:** PCC § 10849

---

## Text

On the completion of the contract, the original contractor is entitled to the return of all his or her unused materials, and his or her equipment, tools, and appliances, except that he or she shall have no claim on account of usual and ordinary depreciation, loss, and wear and tear.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 4.)
