# PCC § 21150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 82. Alameda County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21150

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Alameda County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 1275 of the Statutes of 1949.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
