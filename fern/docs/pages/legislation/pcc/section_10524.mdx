# PCC § 10524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.1. University of California Competitive Bidding > Article: 5. Remedies and Penalties

**Citation:** PCC § 10524

---

## Text

Persons convicted under Section 10522 or 10523 are also liable to the University of California for double the amount the university may have lost or be liable to lose by reason of the acts made crimes by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 62, Sec. 242.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
