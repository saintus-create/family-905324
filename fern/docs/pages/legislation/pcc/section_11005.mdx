# PCC § 11005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. California State University Contract Law > Article: 9. Offenses

**Citation:** PCC § 11005

---

## Text

“Spouse,” as used in this code, includes “registered domestic partner,” as required by Section 297.5 of the Family Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 91. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
