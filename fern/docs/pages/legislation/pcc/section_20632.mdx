# PCC § 20632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 39. Levee Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20632

---

## Text

The board shall give notice by publication, pursuant to Section 6063 of the Government Code, in calling for bids for the construction or repair of any of the levees or associated works of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
