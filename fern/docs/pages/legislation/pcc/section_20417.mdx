# PCC § 20417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 27. Legislative Bodies—Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20417

---

## Text

If the bids are rejected or no bids are received the legislative body may readvertise for proposals or bids for the performance of the work as in the first instance, without further proceedings, and thereafter proceed in the manner provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
