# PCC § 21200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 87. Yolo County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21200

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Yolo County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 1657 of the Statutes of 1951.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
