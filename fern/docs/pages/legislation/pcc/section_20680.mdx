# PCC § 20680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 43. Community Services Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20680

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by community services districts as provided for in the Community Services District Law pursuant to Division 3 (commencing with Section 61000) of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 86.)
