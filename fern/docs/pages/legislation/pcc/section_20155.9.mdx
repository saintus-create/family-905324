# PCC § 20155.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 3.7. Best Value Construction Contracting for Counties

**Citation:** PCC § 20155.9

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 58, Sec. 5. (AB 1957) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 3.7, commencing with Section 20155.)
