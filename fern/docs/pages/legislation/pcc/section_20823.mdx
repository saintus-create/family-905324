# PCC § 20823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 54. Public Entities—Community Facilities Law of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20823

---

## Text

Any contract for the construction of any improvement may be let and entered into as other contracts for similar work are let and entered into by the city. If the improvement includes the acquisition of any property, the city may acquire such property by purchase, gift, eminent domain, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 97.)
