# PCC § 21552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 120. San Diego County Flood Control District

**Citation:** PCC § 21552

---

## Text

In case of an emergency, if notice for bids to let contracts will not be given, the district shall comply with Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 22050).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 803, Sec. 74. Effective January 1, 1995.)
