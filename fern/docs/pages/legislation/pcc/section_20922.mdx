# PCC § 20922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 60.5. Reclamation Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20922

---

## Text

It is unlawful to split or separate into smaller work orders or projects for the purpose of evading the provisions of this article requiring work to be done by contract after competitive bidding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1042, Sec. 1.)
