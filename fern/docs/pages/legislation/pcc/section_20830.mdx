# PCC § 20830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 55. Highway Lighting Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20830

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by highway lighting districts as provided for in the Highway Lighting District Act pursuant to Part 4 (commencing with Section 19000) of Division 14 of the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 98.)
