# PCC § 10124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 2. Plans and Specifications

**Citation:** PCC § 10124

---

## Text

Bids may be received and contracts awarded on a unit basis, that is, the bids compared upon the basis of estimates of the quantities of the work to be done.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
