# PCC § 20585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 36. Water Storage Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20585

---

## Text

The board may either:

(a) Let the work to the lowest responsible bidder.

(b) Reject any or all bids and readvertise for proposals.

(c) Proceed to construct the work under its own superintendence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
