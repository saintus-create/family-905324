# PCC § 20150.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 3.6. Counties of 500,000 or Less Population

**Citation:** PCC § 20150.12

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of the county shall adopt plans, specifications, and working details for all public projects the expenditure for which exceeds ten thousand dollars ($10,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 82.)
