# PCC § 10223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. State Contract Act > Article: 7. Contract Requirements

**Citation:** PCC § 10223

---

## Text

The payment bond shall secure the payment of the claims of laborers, mechanics or materialmen employed on the work under the contract and shall contain all other provisions required by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1981, Ch. 306.)
