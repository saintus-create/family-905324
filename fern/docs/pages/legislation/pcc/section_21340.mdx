# PCC § 21340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 99. Shasta County Water Agency

**Citation:** PCC § 21340

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Shasta County Water Agency, as provided for in Chapter 1512 of the Statutes of 1957.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
