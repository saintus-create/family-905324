# PCC § 10302.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 3. Competitive Bidding and Other Acquisition Procedures

**Citation:** PCC § 10302.6

---

## Text

Product specifications as prepared by the department or any other state agency shall not be written with the intent of excluding goods manufactured, produced, grown, or otherwise originating in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 776, Sec. 12. Effective September 27, 2000.)
