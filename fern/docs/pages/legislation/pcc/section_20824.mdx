# PCC § 20824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 54. Public Entities—Community Facilities Law of 1911

**Citation:** PCC § 20824

---

## Text

Instead of letting contracts for the improvement, the city may itself construct or complete the improvement, and buy the necessary materials, and employ the necessary labor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 97.)
