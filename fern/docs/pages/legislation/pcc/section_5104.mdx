# PCC § 5104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATIVE PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Relief of Bidders

**Citation:** PCC § 5104

---

## Text

Other than the notice to the public entity, no claim is required to be filed before bringing the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 435, Sec. 2.)
