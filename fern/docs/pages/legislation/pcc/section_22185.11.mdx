# PCC § 22185.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.7. Local Agency Design-Build Contracting

**Citation:** PCC § 22185.11

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 500, Sec. 1. (SB 706) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 4.7, commencing with Section 22185.)
