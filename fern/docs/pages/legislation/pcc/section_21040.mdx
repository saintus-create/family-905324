# PCC § 21040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 71. Orange County Water District

**Citation:** PCC § 21040

---

## Text

This article shall apply to contracts by the Orange County Water District, as provided for in Chapter 924 of the Statutes of 1933.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 556. Effective January 1, 2007.)
