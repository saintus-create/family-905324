# PCC § 20568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 35. Irrigation Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20568

---

## Text

Contracts for the purchase of material to be furnished or used by the district in the works shall be awarded to the lowest responsible bidder or bidders after such notice as the board deems proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
