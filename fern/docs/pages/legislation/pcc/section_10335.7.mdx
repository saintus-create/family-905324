# PCC § 10335.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. CONTRACTING BY STATE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. State Acquisition of Goods and Services > Article: 4. Contracts for Services

**Citation:** PCC § 10335.7

---

## Text

“State agency,” as used in this article, means every state office, department, division, bureau, board, or commission, but does not include the Legislature, the courts, or any agency in the judicial branch of government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10357 by Stats. 2000, Ch. 759, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2001.)
