# PCC § 20188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 4.5. Local Agencies—Earthquake Relief

**Citation:** PCC § 20188

---

## Text

Each taker and subsequent holder of the bonds and attached or detached coupons has recourse to all of the provisions of the resolution and of this chapter and is bound by their terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 465, Sec. 11.)
