# PCC § 21330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 98. Tehama County Flood Control and Water Conservation District

**Citation:** PCC § 21330

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Tehama County Flood Control and Water Conservation District, as provided for in Chapter 1280 of the Statutes of 1957.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1128, Sec. 69.)
