# PCC § 21070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Water District Acts > Article: 74. Ventura County Watershed Protection District

**Citation:** PCC § 21070

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts by the Ventura County Watershed Protection District, as provided for in Chapter 44 of the Statutes of the 1944 Fourth Extraordinary Session.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 67, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
