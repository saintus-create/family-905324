# PCC § 20805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 52. Sanitary Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20805

---

## Text

In its discretion, the district board may reject any bids presented and readvertise. If two or more bids are the same and the lowest, the district board may accept the one it chooses. If no bids are received the district board may have the project done without further complying with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 95.)
