# PCC § 22012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Bidding on Public Contracts > Article: 2. California Uniform Construction Cost Accounting Commission

**Citation:** PCC § 22012

---

## Text

At least one commission member of the seven representing the construction industry and at least one of the seven representing public agencies shall have previous accounting experience.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 296, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2004.)
