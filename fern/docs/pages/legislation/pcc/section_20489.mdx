# PCC § 20489

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 29. Public Entities—Municipal Improvement Act of 1913

## Text

The provisions of the Improvement Act of 1911 relating to liquidated damages are incorporated in this division.
