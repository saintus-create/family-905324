# PCC § 20207.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 6.5. Public Utility Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20207.4

---

## Text

All contracts shall be made with the lowest responsible bidder. Bidders for construction contracts shall give bonds for the faithful performance of the construction contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1226, Sec. 17.)
