# PCC § 20207.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 6.5. Public Utility Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20207.6

---

## Text

No officer or employee of the district shall order any article or make any publication which is not expressly authorized by this division or by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 256, Sec. 83.)
