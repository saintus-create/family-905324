# PCC § 20200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTING BY LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Local Agency Public Construction Act > Article: 6. Public Utility Districts

**Citation:** PCC § 20200

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to contracts subject to the Public Utility District Act provided for in Division 7 (commencing with Section 15501) of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 269, Sec. 23. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2016.)
