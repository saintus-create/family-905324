# HNC § 493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 3. Actions Against Vessels

**Citation:** HNC § 493

---

## Text

The mate and seamen of a vessel have a general lien, independent of possession, upon the vessel and freightage, for their wages, which is superior to every other lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
