# HNC § 7219

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 4. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 7219

---

## Text

The bonds shall bear interest at a rate or rates not to exceed 6 percent per annum payable semiannually, except that interest for the first year shall be payable at the end of the year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
