# HNC § 6805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. RIVER PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Finances and Taxation > Article: 2. Claims and Demands

**Citation:** HNC § 6805

---

## Text

If a district is dissolved, all land granted to it by a municipal corporation, reverts to and is revested in the municipal corporation granting it to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
