# HNC § 7003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 7003

---

## Text

“District” means any small craft harbor district formed pursuant to this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
