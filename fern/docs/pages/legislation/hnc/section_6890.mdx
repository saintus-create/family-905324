# HNC § 6890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. District Powers > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 6890

---

## Text

Any district created pursuant to this part is a public corporation created for municipal purposes.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
