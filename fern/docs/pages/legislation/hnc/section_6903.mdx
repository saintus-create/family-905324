# HNC § 6903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. District Powers > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 6903

---

## Text

A district may provide for the opening and laying out of streets leading to the waterfront.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 27.)
