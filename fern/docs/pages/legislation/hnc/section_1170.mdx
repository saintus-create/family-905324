# HNC § 1170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 4. Licensing of Pilots > Article: 1. Issuance of License

**Citation:** HNC § 1170

---

## Text

The board shall appoint and license the number of pilots which is sufficient to carry out the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1422, Sec. 19.)
