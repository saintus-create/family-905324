# HNC § 70.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 3. Small Craft Harbors and Connecting Waterways

**Citation:** HNC § 70.3

---

## Text

“Harbor of safe refuge” means a port, harbor, inlet, or other body of water normally sheltered from heavy seas by land and in which a vessel can navigate and safely moor, as set forth in Section 70.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 282, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
