# HNC § 1168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 3. Pension Plan

**Citation:** HNC § 1168

---

## Text

The board may review the pension plan and make any recommendations it deems necessary for changes in the plan. Monthly pensions shall not be adjusted except as provided by Section 1167.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 639, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1993.)
