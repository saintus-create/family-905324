# HNC § 6070.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Commission > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 6070.4

---

## Text

Every person who violates any of the provisions of a district ordinance adopted pursuant to Sections 6070 and 6070.2 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 128.)
