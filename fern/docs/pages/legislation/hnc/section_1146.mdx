# HNC § 1146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 8. State Certified Pilotage

**Citation:** HNC § 1146

---

## Text

A pilot shall refuse a pilotage assignment if he or she is physically or mentally fatigued and has a reasonable belief that the assignment cannot be carried out in a competent and safe manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 794, Sec. 3. (SB 1408) Effective January 1, 2013.)
