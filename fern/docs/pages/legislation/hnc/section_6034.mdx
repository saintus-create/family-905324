# HNC § 6034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 3. Election for Formation and for Harbor Commissioners

**Citation:** HNC § 6034

---

## Text

The resolution shall invite the qualified voters residing in the district to vote upon the propositions of its formation and the election of a board.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
