# HNC § 4081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. COUNTY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Improvement Bonds > Article: 3. Improvement and Construction

**Citation:** HNC § 4081

---

## Text

The improvement, development, or protection of any harbor, and all work, labor, or service employed therein, shall be under the control and management of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 6.)
