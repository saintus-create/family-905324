# HNC § 63.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 63.4

---

## Text

The director may recommend to the Legislature such action as may be necessary to provide the finances required of local agencies as a condition for the participation of the United States in any project in which the department may act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
