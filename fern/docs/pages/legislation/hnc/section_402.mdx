# HNC § 402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 402

---

## Text

A vessel in a port of the country in which it is registered is called a domestic vessel, in another port it is called a foreign vessel.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
