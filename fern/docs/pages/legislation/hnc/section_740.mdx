# HNC § 740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 2. Yacht and Ship Brokers

**Citation:** HNC § 740

---

## Text

Any action taken in the enforcement of this article shall be commenced within three years of the department’s discovery of the facts constituting the grounds for that action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 561, Sec. 5.)
