# HNC § 6341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 6341

---

## Text

The issuance of said bonds shall be authorized by ordinance adopted by two-thirds of all the members of the board, to take effect upon its publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 70.)
