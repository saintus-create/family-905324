# HNC § 19

**Citation:** HNC § 19

---

## Text

“Person” means any person, firm, association, organization, partnership, limited liability company, business trust, corporation, or company.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 146. Effective January 1, 1995.)
