# HNC § 6061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Commission > Article: 2. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 6061

---

## Text

The board shall fix the compensation or salary of all subordinate officers or employees of the district.
