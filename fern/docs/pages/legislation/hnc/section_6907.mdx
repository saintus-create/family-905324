# HNC § 6907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. District Powers > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 6907

---

## Text

It may act as warehousemen, stevedores, lighterers, reconditioners, shippers and reshippers of properties of all kinds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
