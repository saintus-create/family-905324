# HNC § 6201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HNC § 6201

---

## Text

“Board,” as used in this part, means the board of port commissioners described in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 6240).
