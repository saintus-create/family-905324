# HNC § 6101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 6101

---

## Text

The issuance of said bonds shall be authorized by ordinance adopted by two-thirds of all the members of the board, to take effect upon its publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1131.)
