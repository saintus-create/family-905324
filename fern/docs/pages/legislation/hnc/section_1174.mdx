# HNC § 1174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 4. Licensing of Pilots > Article: 1. Issuance of License

**Citation:** HNC § 1174

---

## Text

A pilot’s license shall be issued in the name of the State of California and contain a designation of the waters for which it is intended. The license shall be signed by the president of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1256.)
