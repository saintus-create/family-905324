# HNC § 1695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 1. Findings and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 1695

---

## Text

“Infrastructure fund” means a port or harbor infrastructure fund established by an authority pursuant to Section 1700.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1235, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
