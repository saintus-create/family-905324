# HNC § 6309.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. District Powers > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 6309.4

---

## Text

Every person who violates any of the provisions of a district ordinance adopted pursuant to Sections 6309 and 6309.2 is guilty of an infraction and shall be subject to a fine not to exceed one hundred dollars ($100).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 143. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
