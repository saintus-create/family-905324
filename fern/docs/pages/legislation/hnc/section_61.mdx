# HNC § 61

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 61

---

## Text

The department may take such action as may be necessary to take advantage of any act of Congress heretofore or hereafter enacted which may be of assistance in carrying out the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
