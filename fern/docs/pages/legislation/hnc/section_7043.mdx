# HNC § 7043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** HNC § 7043

---

## Text

All vacancies occurring in the office of director shall be filled by appointment by the remaining directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
