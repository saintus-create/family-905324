# HNC § 411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 1.5. Vessel Repair

**Citation:** HNC § 411

---

## Text

This article applies only to work done on a vessel with an estimated cost of one hundred dollars ($100) or more.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 305, Sec. 1.)
