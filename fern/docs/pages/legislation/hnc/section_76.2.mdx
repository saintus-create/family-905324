# HNC § 76.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 5. Recreational Marinas

**Citation:** HNC § 76.2

---

## Text

“Recreational marina” means a marina owned by a private marina owner which is used by the public primarily for recreational purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1307, Sec. 2.)
