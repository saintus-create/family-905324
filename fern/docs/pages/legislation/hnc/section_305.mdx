# HNC § 305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NAVIGATION > Chapter: 4. Offenses

**Citation:** HNC § 305

---

## Text

A person, other than one described in Section 304 who is guilty of any act specified in that section is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 610, Sec. 23. (SB 717) Effective January 1, 2010.)
