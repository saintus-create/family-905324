# HNC § 7034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 3. Election on Formation

**Citation:** HNC § 7034

---

## Text

The vote shall be canvassed by the board of supervisors not more than 10 days after the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
