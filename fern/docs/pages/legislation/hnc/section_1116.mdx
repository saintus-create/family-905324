# HNC § 1116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 1116

---

## Text

“Vessel” means all vessels not exempted under provisions of this division.
