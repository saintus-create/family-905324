# HNC § 7083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections > Article: 1. Elections Generally

**Citation:** HNC § 7083

---

## Text

If there is but one person to be elected to an office, the candidate receiving the highest number of votes cast for the candidates for that office shall be declared elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
