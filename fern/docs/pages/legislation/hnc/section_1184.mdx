# HNC § 1184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 4. Licensing of Pilots > Article: 2. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** HNC § 1184

---

## Text

The board shall formally review the executive director with respect to the performance of his or her duties pursuant to this article at least once each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1197, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
