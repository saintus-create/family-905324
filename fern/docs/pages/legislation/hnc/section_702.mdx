# HNC § 702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 2. Yacht and Ship Brokers

**Citation:** HNC § 702

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, all records of the department relating to yacht brokers and yacht salesmen shall be open to inspection by the public during regular office hours.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 453.)
