# HNC § 7227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 4. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 7227

---

## Text

Any general obligation bonds issued by a district shall have the same force, value and use as bonds issued by any city and shall be exempt from all taxation within the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
