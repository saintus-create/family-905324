# HNC § 6273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Port Commissioners > Article: 2. Jurisdiction and Powers of the Board

**Citation:** HNC § 6273

---

## Text

The district may itself, without letting contracts therefor, do work and make improvements. The work shall be done under the direction of its officers or employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 17.)
