# HNC § 3942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 2. San Francisco Harbor

**Citation:** HNC § 3942

---

## Text

The authority shall make payments from the San Francisco Harbor Improvement Fund to the Harbor Bond Sinking Fund as required under Section 3918.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 103.)
