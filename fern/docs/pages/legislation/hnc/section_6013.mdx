# HNC § 6013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition

**Citation:** HNC § 6013

---

## Text

Each signer of the petition shall be a registered voter, resident and freeholder within the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
