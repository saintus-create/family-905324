# HNC § 7044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** HNC § 7044

---

## Text

An appointment to fill a vacancy in the office of director shall be for the unexpired term of the office in which the vacancy exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
