# HNC § 5900.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Management and Control

**Citation:** HNC § 5900.10

---

## Text

Every person who violates the provisions of any ordinance enacted by the board of supervisors pursuant to this part, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 844.)
