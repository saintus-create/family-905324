# HNC § 6800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. RIVER PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Finances and Taxation > Article: 2. Claims and Demands

**Citation:** HNC § 6800

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this part, means any river port district formed pursuant to this part.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
