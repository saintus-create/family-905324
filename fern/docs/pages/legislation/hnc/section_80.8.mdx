# HNC § 80.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Boating and Waterways Commission

**Citation:** HNC § 80.8

---

## Text

The members of the commission may be removed from office by the Governor for inefficiency, neglect of duty, misuse of office, or misfeasance in office, in the manner provided by law for the removal of other public officers for like causes.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 136, Sec. 2. (AB 737) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 136.)
