# HNC § 5900.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Management and Control

**Citation:** HNC § 5900.11

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2006.)
