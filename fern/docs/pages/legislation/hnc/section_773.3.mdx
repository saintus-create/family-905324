# HNC § 773.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 4. Charter Boat Safety

**Citation:** HNC § 773.3

---

## Text

No person shall take or offer to take seven or more passengers on a charter boat unless the vessel is licensed by the United States Coast Guard.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1049, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1983.)
