# HNC § 6937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 6937

---

## Text

The issuance of said bonds shall be authorized by ordinance adopted by five-sevenths of all the members of the board, to take effect upon its publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1195, Sec. 18.1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
