# HNC § 1693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 1. Findings and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 1693

---

## Text

“Bonds” means bonds, notes, bond anticipation notes, commercial paper, or other evidences of indebtedness or lease, installment sale, or other agreements or certificates of participation therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1235, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
