# HNC § 1132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 7. Duties of Pilots

**Citation:** HNC § 1132

---

## Text

Every pilot in charge of a vessel arriving in Monterey Bay and the Bay of San Francisco, San Pablo, or Suisun, shall safely moor the vessel in place and position as directed by the master of the vessel, consistent with safe navigation and not contrary to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 177, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2002.)
