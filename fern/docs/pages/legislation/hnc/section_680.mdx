# HNC § 680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 1.5. Anthony Farr and Stacy Beckett Boating Safety Act of 2004

**Citation:** HNC § 680

---

## Text

This act shall be known as the Anthony Farr and Stacy Beckett Boating Safety Act of 2004, and may be cited as Anthony and Stacy’s Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 565, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
