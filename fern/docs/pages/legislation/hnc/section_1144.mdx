# HNC § 1144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 8. State Certified Pilotage

**Citation:** HNC § 1144

---

## Text

Any continuing education program adopted by the board shall regularly inform pilots of the hazards of fatigue and of effective strategies to prevent fatigue while on duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 794, Sec. 2. (SB 1408) Effective January 1, 2013.)
