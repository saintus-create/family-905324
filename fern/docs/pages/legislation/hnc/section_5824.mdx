# HNC § 5824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition and Hearing

**Citation:** HNC § 5824

---

## Text

Each signer of the petition shall be a registered voter, resident, and freeholder within the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
