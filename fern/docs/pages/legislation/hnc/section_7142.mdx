# HNC § 7142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 7142

---

## Text

The district may sue and be sued in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
