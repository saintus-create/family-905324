# HNC § 471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 2. Respondentia

**Citation:** HNC § 471

---

## Text

The owner of cargo may hypothecate it upon respondentia, at any time and place, and for any purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
