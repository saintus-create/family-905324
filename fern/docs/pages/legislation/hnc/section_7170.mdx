# HNC § 7170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 7170

---

## Text

A district may borrow money, incur indebtedness, and issue bonds or other evidences of indebtedness as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
