# HNC § 762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 3. For-Hire Vessel Operators’ Licenses

**Citation:** HNC § 762

---

## Text

An applicant for an operator’s license shall submit his written verified application to the department. The application shall be in such form and contain such information as the department requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
