# HNC § 6864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Port Commission > Article: 2. Jurisdiction and Powers of the Board

**Citation:** HNC § 6864

---

## Text

The district, may itself, without letting contracts therefor, do such work and make such improvements as are necessary. The work shall be done under the supervision and direction of its officers or employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 23.)
