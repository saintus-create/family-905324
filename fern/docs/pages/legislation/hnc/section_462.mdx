# HNC § 462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 1. Bottomry

**Citation:** HNC § 462

---

## Text

Of two or more bottomry liens on the same subject, the latter in date has preference, if created out of necessity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
