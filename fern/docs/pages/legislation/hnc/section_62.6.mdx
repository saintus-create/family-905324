# HNC § 62.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 62.6

---

## Text

The department, with the approval of the Department of General Services, may procure insurance on vessels owned or operated by the department against the usual hazards in addition to the special hazards occasioned by the existence of a state of war.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
