# HNC § 426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 2. Liability, Generally

**Citation:** HNC § 426

## Text

So far as possible, a jettison shall begin with the most bulky and least valuable articles.
