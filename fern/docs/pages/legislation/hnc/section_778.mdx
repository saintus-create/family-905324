# HNC § 778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 6. Vessel Sanitation

**Citation:** HNC § 778

---

## Text

The state board shall adopt standards for the location, construction, operation, and maintenance of vessel pumpout facilities.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 842, Sec. 3.)
