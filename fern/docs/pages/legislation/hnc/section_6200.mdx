# HNC § 6200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HNC § 6200

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this part, refers to any port district formed pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
