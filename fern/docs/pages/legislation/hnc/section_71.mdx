# HNC § 71

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 3. Small Craft Harbors and Connecting Waterways

**Citation:** HNC § 71

---

## Text

The department may provide for the granting of concessions within the boundaries of harbors under the jurisdiction of the department in order to furnish the public with fuel, oil, food, and other facilities, and may grant easements, rights of way, and permits with respect to such harbors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
