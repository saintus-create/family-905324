# HNC § 7053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** HNC § 7053

---

## Text

At its organization meeting the board may transact any business of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
