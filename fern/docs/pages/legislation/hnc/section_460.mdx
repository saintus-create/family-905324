# HNC § 460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 1. Bottomry

**Citation:** HNC § 460

---

## Text

A bottomry lien is independent of possession, and is lost by omission to enforce it within a reasonable time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
