# HNC § 7071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HNC § 7071

---

## Text

A director shall not be treasurer. The secretary and treasurer shall receive the compensation determined by the board and shall serve at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
