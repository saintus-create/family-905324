# HNC § 1157.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 2. Board of Pilot Commissioners

**Citation:** HNC § 1157.4

---

## Text

Upon a request to the board by a federal, state, or local law enforcement agency, the executive director shall make available to the requesting agency any information contained in the board’s records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 567, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2009.)
