# HNC § 36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 36

---

## Text

“Navigable waters” means waters which come under the jurisdiction of the United States Corps of Engineers and any other waters within the state with the exception of those privately owned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
