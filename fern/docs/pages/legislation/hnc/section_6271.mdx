# HNC § 6271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Port Commissioners > Article: 2. Jurisdiction and Powers of the Board

**Citation:** HNC § 6271

---

## Text

A grant of a franchise or lease of property of the district shall not be made for a longer period than 50 years.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 21.)
