# HNC § 6896

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. District Powers > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 6896

---

## Text

It may exercise the right of eminent domain to take any property necessary or convenient to carry out any of its purposes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 582.)
