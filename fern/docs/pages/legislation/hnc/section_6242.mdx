# HNC § 6242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Port Commissioners > Article: 1. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 6242

---

## Text

A vacancy on the board shall be filled by the body which appointed the commissioner whose office is vacated, and the new appointee shall hold his office for the unexpired term.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
