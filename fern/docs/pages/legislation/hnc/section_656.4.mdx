# HNC § 656.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 1. Operation and Equipment

**Citation:** HNC § 656.4

---

## Text

The department is authorized to develop a program of public information and research in the interest of reducing loss of life and property in the operation of vessels covered by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 685.4 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 842.)
