# HNC § 405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 405

---

## Text

The registry, enrollment, and license of vessels are regulated by acts of Congress.
