# HNC § 6077.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Commission > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 6077.3

---

## Text

It may perform the functions of warehousemen, stevedores, lighterers, reconditioners, shippers and reshippers of properties of all kinds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 1031.)
