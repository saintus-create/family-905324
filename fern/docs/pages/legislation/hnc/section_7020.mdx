# HNC § 7020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** HNC § 7020

---

## Text

At the time and place specified in the notice the board of supervisors shall hold the hearing, consider the petition and may continue the hearing from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
