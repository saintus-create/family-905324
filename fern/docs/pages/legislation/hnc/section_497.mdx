# HNC § 497

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 3. Actions Against Vessels

**Citation:** HNC § 497

---

## Text

The notice of sale published by the sheriff or marshal must contain a statement of the measurement and tonnage of the vessel and a general description of her condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 98. Effective January 1, 1997.)
