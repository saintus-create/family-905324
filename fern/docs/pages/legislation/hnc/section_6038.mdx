# HNC § 6038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 3. Election for Formation and for Harbor Commissioners

**Citation:** HNC § 6038

---

## Text

On the day of any election held pursuant to this part, the polls at each of the polling places designated, shall be opened and closed as provided in Section 14212 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 1995.)
