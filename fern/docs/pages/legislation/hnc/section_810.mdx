# HNC § 810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 2. Ship Masters and Cargoes

**Citation:** HNC § 810

---

## Text

The master of a vessel is a general agent for its owner in all matters concerning the vessel.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
