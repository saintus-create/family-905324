# HNC § 470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 2. Respondentia

**Citation:** HNC § 470

---

## Text

Respondentia is a contract by which all or part of a cargo is hypothecated as security for a loan, the repayment of which is dependent on maritime risks.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
