# HNC § 6081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Commission > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 6081

---

## Text

By resolution, the board may provide for the creation and accumulation of a fund for capital outlays.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 905.)
