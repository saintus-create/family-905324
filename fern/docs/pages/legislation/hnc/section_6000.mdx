# HNC § 6000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. HARBOR DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Inland Parks and Recreation Areas

**Citation:** HNC § 6000

---

## Text

“Harbor,” as used in this part, includes any bay, harbor, inlet, river, channel, slough, or arm of the sea, in which the tides of the Pacific Ocean ebb and flow or in which tides are affected by the Pacific Ocean.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
