# HNC § 1178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 4. Licensing of Pilots > Article: 1. Issuance of License

**Citation:** HNC § 1178

---

## Text

Persons applying for an original license shall not receive a license unless they have proper federal endorsements allowing them to pilot on the high seas and all the waters of those bays pursuant to Section 1171.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 282, Sec. 9.)
