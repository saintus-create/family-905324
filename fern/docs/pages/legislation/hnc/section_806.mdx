# HNC § 806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 2. Ship Masters and Cargoes

**Citation:** HNC § 806

---

## Text

The master of a vessel shall not abandon it during the voyage, without the advice of the other officers.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
