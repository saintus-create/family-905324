# HNC § 403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 403

---

## Text

If a vessel belongs to several persons, not partners, and they differ as to its use or repair, the controversy may be determined by any court of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
