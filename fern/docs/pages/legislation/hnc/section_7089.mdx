# HNC § 7089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections > Article: 1. Elections Generally

**Citation:** HNC § 7089

---

## Text

No irregularities or informalities in conducting any district election shall invalidate the same, if the election shall have been otherwise fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
