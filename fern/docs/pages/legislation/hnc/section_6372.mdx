# HNC § 6372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Finances and Taxation > Article: 2. Claims and Demands

**Citation:** HNC § 6372

---

## Text

Wages and salaries shall be paid at such intervals as the board may direct, but at least once each month.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
