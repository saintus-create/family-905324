# HNC § 7042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** HNC § 7042

---

## Text

If a person elected fails to qualify, the office shall be filled as if there were a vacancy in the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
