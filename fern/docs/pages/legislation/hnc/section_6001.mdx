# HNC § 6001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. HARBOR DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Inland Parks and Recreation Areas

**Citation:** HNC § 6001

---

## Text

“Board,” as used in this part, means the commission or board having the management or control of the improvements, development, protection and maintenance of any harbor district established or formed pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
