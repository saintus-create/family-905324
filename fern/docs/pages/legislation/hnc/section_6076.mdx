# HNC § 6076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Commission > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 6076

---

## Text

It may exercise the right of eminent domain to take any property necessary or convenient to the exercise of its powers.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 582.)
