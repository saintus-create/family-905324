# HNC § 69.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 2.8. California Public Beach Restoration Act

**Citation:** HNC § 69.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Public Beach Restoration Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 798, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000. Pursuant to Sec. 3 of Ch. 798, operation of provisions is contingent upon funding in the annual budget act.)
