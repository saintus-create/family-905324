# HNC § 39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 39

---

## Text

Any construction or development authorized by this division that also constitutes a project within the definition of Section 10105 of the Public Contract Code, when performed by the state, shall be subject to the State Contract Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 597, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2002.)
