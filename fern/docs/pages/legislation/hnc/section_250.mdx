# HNC § 250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NAVIGATION > Chapter: 2. Penalties

**Citation:** HNC § 250

---

## Text

When steam vessels meet each shall turn to the right, so as to pass without interference.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
