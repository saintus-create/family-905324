# HNC § 7041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** HNC § 7041

---

## Text

The term of office of each director, other than directors first elected or directors appointed to fill an unexpired term, shall be four years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
