# HNC § 7280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Changes in Organization > Article: 1. Annexation

**Citation:** HNC § 7280

---

## Text

Land in the same county in which the district is located, whether contiguous or not or incorporated or not, which is not a part of the district may be annexed thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
