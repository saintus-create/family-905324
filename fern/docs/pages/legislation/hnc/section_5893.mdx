# HNC § 5893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 2. Payment of Bonds, Taxation

**Citation:** HNC § 5893

---

## Text

In no event shall the district levy a tax in excess of twenty cents ($0. 20) for each one hundred dollars ($100) of assessed valuation within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1356.)
