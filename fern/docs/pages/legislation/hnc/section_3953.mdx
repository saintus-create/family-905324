# HNC § 3953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 3. Small Craft Harbors

**Citation:** HNC § 3953

---

## Text

The commission shall make payments from the Small Craft Harbor Improvement Fund to the Harbor Bond Sinking Fund as required under Section 3918.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 103.)
