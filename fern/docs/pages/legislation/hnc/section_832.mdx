# HNC § 832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 2.5. Ship’s Managers

**Citation:** HNC § 832

---

## Text

A managing owner is presumed to be without right to compensation for his own services.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
