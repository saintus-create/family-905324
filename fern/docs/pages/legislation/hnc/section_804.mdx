# HNC § 804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 2. Ship Masters and Cargoes

**Citation:** HNC § 804

---

## Text

The master of a vessel may confine any person on board, during a voyage, for wilful disobedience to his lawful commands.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
