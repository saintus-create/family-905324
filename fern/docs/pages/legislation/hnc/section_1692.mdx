# HNC § 1692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 1. Findings and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 1692

---

## Text

“Authority” means a joint powers entity or nonprofit public benefit corporation created in accordance with Section 1700.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1197, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
