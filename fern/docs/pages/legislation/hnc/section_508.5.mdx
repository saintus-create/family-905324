# HNC § 508.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 4. Boaters Lien Law

**Citation:** HNC § 508.5

---

## Text

All forms required pursuant to this article shall be prescribed by the department. Language used in the notices and declarations shall be simple and nontechnical.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 941, Sec. 2.)
