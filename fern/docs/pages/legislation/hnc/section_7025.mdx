# HNC § 7025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** HNC § 7025

---

## Text

A finding of the board of supervisors of the genuineness and sufficiency of the petition and that notice shall be conclusive against all persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
