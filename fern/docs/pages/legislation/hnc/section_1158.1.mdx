# HNC § 1158.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 2. Board of Pilot Commissioners

**Citation:** HNC § 1158.1

---

## Text

The executive director shall not, during the term of his or her office, serve as a member of the board or as a pilot, or otherwise be concurrently employed in the maritime industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 324, Sec. 12. (AB 1025) Effective January 1, 2012.)
