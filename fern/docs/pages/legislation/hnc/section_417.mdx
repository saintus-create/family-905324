# HNC § 417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 1.5. Vessel Repair

**Citation:** HNC § 417

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 413, upon authorization from the customer as to a specific job, a repairperson may work on a vessel on a time and materials basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 305, Sec. 1.)
