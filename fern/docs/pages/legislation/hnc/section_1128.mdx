# HNC § 1128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 6. Exempt Vessels and Vessels Liable for Pilotage

**Citation:** HNC § 1128

---

## Text

Any nonself-propelled vessel in tow of a tug within Monterey Bay and the Bay of San Francisco, San Pablo, or Suisun, or between those bays, is exempt from pilotage charges unless a pilot is actually employed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 177, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2002.)
