# HNC § 6205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HNC § 6205

---

## Text

If the district is dissolved, lands which were granted to it by a municipal corporation revert to that municipal corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
