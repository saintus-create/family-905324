# HNC § 677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 1.3. Invasive Mussel Infestation Prevention Fee

**Citation:** HNC § 677

---

## Text

This article does not preempt a special district, city, county, or joint powers authority from adopting local regulations or ordinances related to the prevention and eradication of invasive aquatic species.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 485, Sec. 2. (AB 2443) Effective January 1, 2013.)
