# HNC § 1281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 6. Pilotage Tariff Determination and Procedures > Article: 6. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 1281

---

## Text

Pilots may submit to the board annually a copy of an audit of the financial record of pilotage operations for the prior year. The pilot audited financial document shall be a public record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 769, Sec. 16. (AB 2056) Effective September 29, 2022.)
