# HNC § 457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 1. Bottomry

**Citation:** HNC § 457

---

## Text

A stipulation in a contract of bottomry, imposing any liability for the loan independent of the maritime risks, is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
