# HNC § 6345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 6345

---

## Text

The lien of said bonds of the same issue shall be prior and superior to all revenue bonds subsequently issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 70.)
