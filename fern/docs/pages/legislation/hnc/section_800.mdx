# HNC § 800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 2. Ship Masters and Cargoes

**Citation:** HNC § 800

---

## Text

The master of a vessel is appointed by and holds his appointment at the pleasure of the owner.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
