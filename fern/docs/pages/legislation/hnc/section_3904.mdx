# HNC § 3904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 3904

---

## Text

The members of the committee shall not receive additional compensation for their services under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 103.)
