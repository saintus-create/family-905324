# HNC § 6836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Port Commission > Article: 1. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 6836

---

## Text

The members of the board shall serve without salary or compensation, but are entitled to necessary traveling expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
