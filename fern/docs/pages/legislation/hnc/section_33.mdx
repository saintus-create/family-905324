# HNC § 33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 33

---

## Text

“Director” or “deputy director” means the Deputy Director of Boating and Waterways.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 323. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
