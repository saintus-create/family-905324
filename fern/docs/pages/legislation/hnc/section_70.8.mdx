# HNC § 70.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 3. Small Craft Harbors and Connecting Waterways

**Citation:** HNC § 70.8

---

## Text

The department may contract with a federal agency, state agency, or city, county, or district, in the design, planning, construction, development, and improvement of small craft harbors pursuant to this chapter, or for the maintenance and operation of any small craft harbors under the jurisdiction of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 610, Sec. 4. (SB 717) Effective January 1, 2010.)
