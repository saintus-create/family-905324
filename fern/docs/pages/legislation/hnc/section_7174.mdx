# HNC § 7174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 7174

---

## Text

The treasurer shall install and maintain a system of auditing and accounting that shall completely and at all times show the financial condition of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
