# HNC § 7074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HNC § 7074

---

## Text

The treasurer shall receive and safely keep all moneys of the district. He shall comply with all provisions of law governing the deposit and securing of public funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
