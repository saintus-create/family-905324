# HNC § 1160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 3. Pension Plan

**Citation:** HNC § 1160

---

## Text

There is hereby established a San Francisco Bar Pilot Pension Plan which is continued in existence as the San Francisco Pilot Pension Plan.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1653, Sec. 30.)
