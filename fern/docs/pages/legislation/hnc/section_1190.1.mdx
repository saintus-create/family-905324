# HNC § 1190.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 5. Pilotage Rates

**Citation:** HNC § 1190.1

---

## Text

Every vessel that uses a pilot under this division while navigating the waters of Monterey Bay shall pay the rate provided by Section 1190.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 769, Sec. 6. (AB 2056) Effective September 29, 2022.)
