# HNC § 6052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Harbor Commission > Article: 1. Election and Terms

**Citation:** HNC § 6052

---

## Text

There shall be an election for harbor commissioners within each district on the first Tuesday after the first Monday of November of each even-numbered year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 540.)
