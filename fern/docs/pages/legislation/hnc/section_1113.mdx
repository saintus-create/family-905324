# HNC § 1113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HNC § 1113

---

## Text

“Inland pilot” means a person holding an inland pilot license prior to January 1, 2011.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 324, Sec. 1. (AB 1025) Effective January 1, 2012.)
