# HNC § 5801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. HARBOR IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS

**Citation:** HNC § 5801

---

## Text

“Harbor commission,” as used in this part, includes any commission, board or officer in which the improvement, development, protection, maintenance, management or control of any harbor or part of any harbor in this state is by law, ordinance, or other legal authority vested or exercised.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
