# HNC § 1711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 3. Seaport Infrastructure Financing Districts

**Citation:** HNC § 1711

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public financing authority” has the same meaning as provided in Section 53398.51 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 793, Sec. 11. (SB 63) Effective January 1, 2016.)
