# HNC § 1250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 6. Pilotage Tariff Determination and Procedures > Article: 1. Establishment of Pilotage Tariff

**Citation:** HNC § 1250

---

## Text

Pursuant to the requirements of this chapter, the board shall adopt, and cause to be published, a pilotage tariff that establishes fair, just, reasonable, and sufficient rates for the provision of a safe, competent, reliable, and efficient pilotage service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 769, Sec. 16. (AB 2056) Effective September 29, 2022.)
