# HNC § 665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 1. Operation and Equipment

**Citation:** HNC § 665

---

## Text

Any person willfully violating his written promise to appear in court is guilty of a misdemeanor regardless of the disposition of the charge upon which he was originally arrested.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1454.)
