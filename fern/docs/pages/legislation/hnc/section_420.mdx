# HNC § 420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 2. Liability, Generally

**Citation:** HNC § 420

---

## Text

A marine carrier is liable in like manner as an inland carrier, except for loss or injury caused by the perils of the sea or fire.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
