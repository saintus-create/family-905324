# HNC § 7035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 3. Election on Formation

**Citation:** HNC § 7035

---

## Text

If less than a majority of the votes cast upon the proposition are in favor of forming the district the board of supervisors shall declare the proceedings terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
