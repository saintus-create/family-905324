# HNC § 6253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Port Commissioners > Article: 1. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 6253

---

## Text

The mayor of the city within the district, or the chairman of the board of supervisors of the county in which the district is located, may call meetings of the board of election.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
