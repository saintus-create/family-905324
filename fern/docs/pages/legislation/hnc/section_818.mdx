# HNC § 818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 2. Ship Masters and Cargoes

**Citation:** HNC § 818

---

## Text

The power of the master of a vessel to bind its owner, or the owners of the cargo, ceases upon the abandonment of the vessel and freightage to insurers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
