# HNC § 81.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Boating and Waterways Commission

**Citation:** HNC § 81.6

---

## Text

The Department of General Services shall furnish suitable offices and equipment for the use of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 136, Sec. 2. (AB 737) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 136.)
