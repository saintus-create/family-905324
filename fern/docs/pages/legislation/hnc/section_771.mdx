# HNC § 771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 3. For-Hire Vessel Operators’ Licenses

**Citation:** HNC § 771

---

## Text

It is unlawful and constitutes a misdemeanor for any person to violate, or to fail to comply with, any provision of this article after April 1, 1964.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1337.)
