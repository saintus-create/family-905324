# HNC § 3914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. HARBORS AND PORTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 3914

---

## Text

Each bond shall contain a reference to this part and if subject to call or redemption prior to maturity, a recital to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 103.)
