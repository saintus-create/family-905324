# HNC § 7144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HNC § 7144

---

## Text

The district may make contracts for any and all purposes necessary or convenient for the full exercise of its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
