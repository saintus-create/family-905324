# HNC § 446.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 1. Vessels Generally > Article: 4. Vessel Traffic Service

**Citation:** HNC § 446.5

---

## Text

The Ports of Los Angeles and Long Beach may impose fees upon all covered vessels within the VTS area to pay the cost of operating the vessel traffic service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
