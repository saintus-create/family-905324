# HNC § 451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Civil Actions and Liens > Article: 1. Bottomry

**Citation:** HNC § 451

---

## Text

The owner of a vessel may hypothecate it or its freightage, upon bottomry, for any lawful purpose, and at any time and place.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
