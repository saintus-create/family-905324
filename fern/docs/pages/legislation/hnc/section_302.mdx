# HNC § 302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NAVIGATION > Chapter: 4. Offenses

**Citation:** HNC § 302

---

## Text

A person who willfully and maliciously sinks or sets adrift a vessel of ten gross tons and upwards that is the property of another is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 610, Sec. 21. (SB 717) Effective January 1, 2010.)
