# HNC § 50.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** HNC § 50.6

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11150), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code apply to the director. The director may appoint, in accordance with civil service, such deputies, officers, and other employees as may be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 61.)
