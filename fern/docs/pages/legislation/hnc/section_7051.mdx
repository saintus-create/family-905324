# HNC § 7051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** HNC § 7051

---

## Text

The powers of the district shall, except as otherwise provided in this part, be exercised by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
