# HNC § 1120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 4. Liability of Persons Using Pilot Services

**Citation:** HNC § 1120

---

## Text

Vessels which use pilotage services, and their tackle, apparel, furniture, and other appurtenances, and their owners, operators, and agents are jointly and severally liable for pilotage fees and for the surcharges established pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 468, Sec. 1. Effective August 8, 1990.)
