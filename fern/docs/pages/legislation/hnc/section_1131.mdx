# HNC § 1131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PILOTS FOR MONTEREY BAY AND THE BAYS OF SAN FRANCISCO, SAN PABLO, AND SUISUN > Chapter: 1. Pilots > Article: 7. Duties of Pilots

**Citation:** HNC § 1131

---

## Text

Pilots shall at all times keep, for their exclusive use, vessels of such description and condition as the board may direct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1256.)
