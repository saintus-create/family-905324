# HNC § 7173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HNC § 7173

---

## Text

Warrants shall be drawn by the treasurer and signed by the president, or a member of the board designated for the purpose and by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
