# HNC § 72.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Department of Boating and Waterways > Article: 3. Small Craft Harbors and Connecting Waterways

**Citation:** HNC § 72.6

---

## Text

Transfers pursuant to Section 70, loans pursuant to Section 71.4, and grants pursuant to Section 72.5 shall be made by the division with the advice of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 353, Sec. 113. (SB 820) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 129 of Ch. 353.)
