# HNC § 6843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Port Commission > Article: 1. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** HNC § 6843

---

## Text

The fiscal year of a district shall begin on the first day of July of each year and shall end on the thirtieth day of June of the following year.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
