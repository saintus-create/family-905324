# HNC § 81.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF BOATING AND WATERWAYS AND THE BOATING AND WATERWAYS COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Boating and Waterways Commission

**Citation:** HNC § 81.8

---

## Text

The deputy director shall act as the secretary of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 136, Sec. 2) by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 328. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
