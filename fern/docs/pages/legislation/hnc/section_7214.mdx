# HNC § 7214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 4. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HNC § 7214

---

## Text

No irregularities or informalities in conducting the election shall invalidate the same, if the election shall have been otherwise fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1598.)
