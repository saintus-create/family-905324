# HNC § 7053.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** HNC § 7053.5

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2006.)
