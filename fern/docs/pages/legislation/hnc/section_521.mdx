# HNC § 521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 3. Wrecks and Salvage > Article: 1. Wrecks and Wrecked Property

**Citation:** HNC § 521

---

## Text

The expense of publishing notices under the provisions of this article is a charge upon the property or proceeds to which it relates.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
