# HNC § 6963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. HARBOR AND PORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Finances and Taxation > Article: 2. Claims and Demands

**Citation:** HNC § 6963

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter I, Division II of the District Organization Act relating to notices shall govern and control this part.
