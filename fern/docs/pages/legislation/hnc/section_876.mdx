# HNC § 876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MASTERS, CREWS AND CARGOES > Chapter: 3. Mates and Seamen

**Citation:** HNC § 876

---

## Text

A mate or seaman may not, under any pretext, ship goods on his own account without permission from the master.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 368.)
