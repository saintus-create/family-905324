# HNC § 677.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 1.3. Invasive Mussel Infestation Prevention Fee

**Citation:** HNC § 677.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “invasive mussel” has the same meaning as Section 2303 of the Fish and Game Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 106, Sec. 10. (AB 149) Effective September 17, 2025.)
