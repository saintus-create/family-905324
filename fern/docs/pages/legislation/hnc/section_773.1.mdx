# HNC § 773.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VESSELS > Chapter: 5. Operation and Equipment of Vessels > Article: 4. Charter Boat Safety

**Citation:** HNC § 773.1

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Charter Boat Safety Act of 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1049, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1983.)
