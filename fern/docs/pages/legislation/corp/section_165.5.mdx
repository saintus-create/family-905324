# CORP § 165.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 165.5

---

## Text

“Disappearing limited partnership” means a constituent limited partnership which is not the surviving limited partnership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 543, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
