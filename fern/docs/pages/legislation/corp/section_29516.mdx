# CORP § 29516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29516

---

## Text

“Sale” or “sell” includes every sale, contract of sale, contract to sell, or disposition, for value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
