# CORP § 2311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 23. Transition Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 2311

---

## Text

Section 706 of the new law applies to agreements and voting trusts entered into after the effective date and prior law governs such agreements or trusts entered into prior thereto unless the agreement or trust is amended or extended thereafter, in which event the new law applies.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
