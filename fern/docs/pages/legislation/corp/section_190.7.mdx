# CORP § 190.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 190.7

---

## Text

“Surviving other business entity” means an other business entity into which one or more other business entities or one or more corporations are merged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 19. Effective September 30, 1994.)
