# CORP § 2312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 23. Transition Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 2312

---

## Text

Section 800 of the new law applies to actions commenced after the effective date and prior law governs actions pending on the effective date.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
