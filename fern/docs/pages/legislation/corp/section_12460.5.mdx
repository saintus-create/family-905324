# CORP § 12460.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Meetings and Voting > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 12460.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 12460, a collective board worker cooperative shall not be required to hold an annual meeting of members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 192, Sec. 20. (AB 816) Effective January 1, 2016.)
