# CORP § 28151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** CORP § 28151

---

## Text

No person other than a person who meets the definition of a licensee may be issued a license under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
