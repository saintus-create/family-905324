# CORP § 28712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 11. Enforcement

**Citation:** CORP § 28712

---

## Text

Any person whose license is suspended or revoked shall immediately deliver the license to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
