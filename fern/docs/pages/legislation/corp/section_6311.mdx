# CORP § 6311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 13. Records, Reports, and Rights of Inspection > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 6311

---

## Text

Any inspection under this chapter may be made in person or by agent or attorney and the right of inspection includes the right to copy and make extracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
