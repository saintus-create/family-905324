# CORP § 190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 190

---

## Text

“Surviving corporation” means a corporation into which one or more other corporations or one or more other business entities are merged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 18. Effective September 30, 1994.)
