# CORP § 31153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 31153

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this law, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
