# CORP § 28036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 28036

---

## Text

“Controlling person,” when used with respect to a specified person, means any person who controls the specified person, directly or indirectly, through one or more intermediaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
