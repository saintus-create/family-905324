# CORP § 28041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 28041

---

## Text

“Order” means any approval, consent, authorization, exemption, denial, prohibition, or requirement applicable to a specific case issued by the commissioner. “Order” includes any condition of a license and any agreement made by any person with the commissioner under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
