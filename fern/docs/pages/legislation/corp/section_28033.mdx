# CORP § 28033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 28033

---

## Text

“Commissioner” means the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation or their designee with respect to a particular matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 86. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
