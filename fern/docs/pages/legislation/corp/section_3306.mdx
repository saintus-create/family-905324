# CORP § 3306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 9. Conversions

**Citation:** CORP § 3306

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Secretary of State shall charge an entity a fee not to exceed one hundred fifty dollars ($150) for its conversion made under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 740, Sec. 12. (SB 201) Effective January 1, 2012.)
