# CORP § 25609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 7. ADMINISTRATION > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 25609

---

## Text

Every final order, decision, license, or other official act of the commissioner is subject to judicial review in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
