# CORP § 29554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 6. Crimes and Civil Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 29554

---

## Text

Every cause of action under this law survives the death of any person who might have been a plaintiff or defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
