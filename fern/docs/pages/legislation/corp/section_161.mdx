# CORP § 161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 161

---

## Text

“Constituent corporation” means a corporation which is merged with or into one or more other corporations or one or more other business entities and includes a surviving corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 9. Effective September 30, 1994.)
