# CORP § 18250

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Liability and Enforcement of Judgments

**Citation:** CORP § 18250

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, an unincorporated association is liable for its act or omission and for the act or omission of its director, officer, agent, or employee, acting within the scope of the office, agency, or employment, to the same extent as if the association were a natural person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
