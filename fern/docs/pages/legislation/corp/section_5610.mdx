# CORP § 5610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Voting of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 5610

---

## Text

Except as provided in a corporation’s articles or bylaws or Section 5616, each member shall be entitled to one vote on each matter submitted to a vote of the members. Single memberships in which two or more persons have an indivisible interest shall be voted as provided in Section 5612.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
