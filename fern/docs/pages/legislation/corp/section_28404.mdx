# CORP § 28404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 6. Transaction of Business

**Citation:** CORP § 28404

---

## Text

No licensee shall provide financing assistance to any small business firm for the purpose of evading the requirements of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 516, Sec. 12. (SB 1155) Effective January 1, 2011.)
