# CORP § 31512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 31512

---

## Text

Any condition, stipulation or provision purporting to bind any person acquiring any franchise to waive compliance with any provision of this law or any rule or order hereunder is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
