# CORP § 13352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 7. Marketing Contracts

**Citation:** CORP § 13352

---

## Text

The contract may provide that the association may sell or resell the fishery products delivered by its members, with or without taking title thereto; and pay over to its members the resale price, after deducting all necessary selling, overhead, and other costs and expenses, including interest on preferred stock, not exceeding 8 percent per annum, and reserves for retiring the stock, if any; and other proper reserves; and interest not exceeding 8 percent per annum upon common stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
