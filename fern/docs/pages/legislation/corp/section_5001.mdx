# CORP § 5001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5001

## Text

This division of the Nonprofit Corporation Law, or any part, chapter, article or section thereof, may at any time be amended or repealed.
