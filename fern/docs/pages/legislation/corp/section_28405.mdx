# CORP § 28405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 6. Transaction of Business

**Citation:** CORP § 28405

---

## Text

This section creates and authorizes an exempt class of persons pursuant to Section 1 of Article XV of the California Constitution. The restrictions upon rates of interest contained in Section 1 of Article XV of the California Constitution shall not apply to any evidence of indebtedness purchased by any licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
