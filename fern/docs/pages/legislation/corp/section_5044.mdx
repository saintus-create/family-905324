# CORP § 5044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5044

---

## Text

“Constituent corporation” means a corporation which is merged with one or more other corporations and includes the surviving corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
