# CORP § 5045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5045

---

## Text

“Control” means the possession, direct or indirect, of the power to direct or cause the direction of the management and policies of a corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
