# CORP § 25144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 5. Authority of the Commissioner

**Citation:** CORP § 25144

---

## Text

The commissioner may vacate or modify a stop order if he finds that the conditions which caused its entry have changed or that it is otherwise in the public interest to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
