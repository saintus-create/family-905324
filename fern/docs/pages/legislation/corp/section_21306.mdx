# CORP § 21306

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 4. Insignia

**Citation:** CORP § 21306

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall keep a properly indexed record of the registrations provided for by this chapter, which record shall also show any altered or canceled registration.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1038.)
