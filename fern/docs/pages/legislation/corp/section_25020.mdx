# CORP § 25020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 25020

---

## Text

“State” means any state, territory, or possession of the United States, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
