# CORP § 25000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 25000

---

## Text

This division may be known as the “Corporate Securities Law of 1968.”

References herein to “this law” refer to the applicable provisions of this division.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
