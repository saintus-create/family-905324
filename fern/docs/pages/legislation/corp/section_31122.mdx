# CORP § 31122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Disclosure

**Citation:** CORP § 31122

---

## Text

The registration renewal statement shall be in the form and content prescribed by the commissioner, and shall be accompanied by a proposed offering prospectus. Each such registration renewal statement shall be accompanied by the fee prescribed in Part 5 (commencing with Section 31500) of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
