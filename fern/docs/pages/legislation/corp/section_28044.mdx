# CORP § 28044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 28044

---

## Text

“Principal security holder,” means any person who owns, directly or indirectly, of record or beneficially, securities representing 10 percent or more of the voting power of the issuer of those securities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
