# CORP § 28650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 10. Voluntary Surrender of License

**Citation:** CORP § 28650

---

## Text

Any licensee may offer to surrender its license by filing with the commissioner the license and a report, which shall (a) be in a form, (b) contain information, and (c) be signed and verified in a manner, that the commissioner may by regulation or order require.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
