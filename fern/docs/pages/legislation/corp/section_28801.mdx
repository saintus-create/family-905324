# CORP § 28801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 12. Crimes and Criminal Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 28801

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person having custody of any of the books, accounts, or other records of a licensee willfully to refuse to allow the commissioner, upon request, to inspect or make copies of any of those books, accounts, or other records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
