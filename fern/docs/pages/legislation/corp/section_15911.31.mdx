# CORP § 15911.31

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 11.5. Dissenting Limited Partners’ Rights

**Citation:** CORP § 15911.31

---

## Text

If litigation is instituted to test the sufficient or regularity of the vote or consent of the limited partners in authorizing a reorganization, any proceedings under Sections 15911.25 and 15911.26 shall be suspended until final determination of that litigation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
