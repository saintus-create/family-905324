# CORP § 5058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5058

---

## Text

“Membership certificate,” as used in Part 3 (commencing with Section 7110), means a document evidencing a transferable property interest in a corporation.
