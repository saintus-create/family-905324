# CORP § 5064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5064

---

## Text

A “parent” of a specified corporation is an affiliate controlling such corporation directly or indirectly through one or more intermediaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
