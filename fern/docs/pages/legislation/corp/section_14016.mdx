# CORP § 14016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 5. Corporation Board

**Citation:** CORP § 14016

---

## Text

If any vacancy occurs in the elective membership of the board of directors through death, resignation, or otherwise, the remaining directors shall elect a person representing the appropriate category to fill the vacancy for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 537, Sec. 2. (AB 1247) Effective October 4, 2013.)
