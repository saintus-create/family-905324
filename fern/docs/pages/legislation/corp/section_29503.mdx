# CORP § 29503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29503

## Text

“Commissioner” means the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation.
