# CORP § 31120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Disclosure

**Citation:** CORP § 31120

---

## Text

A franchise offering shall be deemed duly registered for a period of one year from the effective date of the registration, unless the commissioner by order or rule specifies a different period.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
