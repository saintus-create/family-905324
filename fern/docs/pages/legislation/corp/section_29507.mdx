# CORP § 29507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29507

---

## Text

“Commodity Futures Trading Commission” means the independent regulatory agency established by Congress to administer the Commodity Exchange Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
