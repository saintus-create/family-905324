# CORP § 13331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Powers

**Citation:** CORP § 13331

---

## Text

An association may levy assessments in the manner and in the amount provided in its by-laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
