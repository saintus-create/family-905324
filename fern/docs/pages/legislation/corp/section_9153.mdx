# CORP § 9153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Organization and Bylaws > Article: 5. Bylaws

**Citation:** CORP § 9153

---

## Text

A corporation may provide in its bylaws for voting by its members or delegates on the basis of chapter or other organizational unit, or by region or other geographic grouping.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
