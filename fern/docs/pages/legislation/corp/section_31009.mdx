# CORP § 31009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 31009

---

## Text

A “subfranchisor” is a person to whom a subfranchise is granted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 562, Sec. 3.)
