# CORP § 12404.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 12404.5

---

## Text

(a) The worker-members of a worker cooperative shall have voting power as provided in subdivision (a) of Section 12253.

(b) Community investors have voting power only as provided in subdivision (c) of Section 12253.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 192, Sec. 15. (AB 816) Effective January 1, 2016.)
