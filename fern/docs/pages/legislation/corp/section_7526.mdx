# CORP § 7526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Meetings and Voting > Article: 2. Additional Provisions Relating to Election of Directors

**Citation:** CORP § 7526

---

## Text

Without authorization of the board, no corporation funds may be expended to support a nominee for director after there are more people nominated for director than can be elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
