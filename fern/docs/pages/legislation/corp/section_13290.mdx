# CORP § 13290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Officers > Article: 2. Removal of Officers

**Citation:** CORP § 13290

---

## Text

Any member may bring charges against an officer or director by filing them in writing with the secretary of the association, together with a petition signed by 5 percent of the members, requesting the removal of the officer or director in question.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
