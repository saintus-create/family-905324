# CORP § 25542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. Crimes

**Citation:** CORP § 25542

---

## Text

Nothing in this law limits the power of the state to punish any person for any conduct which constitutes a crime under any other statute.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
