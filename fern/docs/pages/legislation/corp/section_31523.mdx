# CORP § 31523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 7. FRANCHISE BROKERS > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 31523

---

## Text

A registered franchise broker offering a franchise for sale in this state shall keep and maintain a complete set of books, records, and accounts of that offer for a period of five calendar years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 518, Sec. 5. (SB 919) Effective January 1, 2025. Conditionally operative on or after July 1, 2026, pursuant to Sec. 31528)
