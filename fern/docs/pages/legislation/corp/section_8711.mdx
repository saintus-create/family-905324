# CORP § 8711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 17. General Provisions Relating to Dissolution

**Citation:** CORP § 8711

---

## Text

A vacancy on the board may be filled during a winding up proceeding in the manner provided in Section 7224.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
