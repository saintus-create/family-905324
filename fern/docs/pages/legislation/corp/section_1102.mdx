# CORP § 1102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 11. Merger

**Citation:** CORP § 1102

---

## Text

Each corporation shall sign the agreement by its chairperson of the board, president or a vice president and secretary or an assistant secretary acting on behalf of their respective corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 98, Sec. 9. (SB 351) Effective January 1, 2016.)
