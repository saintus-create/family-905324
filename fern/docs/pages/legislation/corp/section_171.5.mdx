# CORP § 171.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 171.5

---

## Text

“Limited partnership” means a partnership formed by two or more persons and having one or more general partners and one or more limited partners, or their equivalents under any name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 543, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
