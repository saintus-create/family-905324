# CORP § 21302

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 4. Insignia

**Citation:** CORP § 21302

---

## Text

An association shall not be permitted to register any name or insignia similar to or so nearly resembling another name or insignia already registered as may be likely to deceive.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1038.)
