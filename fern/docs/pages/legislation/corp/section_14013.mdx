# CORP § 14013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 5. Corporation Board

**Citation:** CORP § 14013

---

## Text

The corporate powers of a corporation shall be exercised by its board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 537, Sec. 2. (AB 1247) Effective October 4, 2013.)
