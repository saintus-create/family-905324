# CORP § 12404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 12404

---

## Text

Except as permitted in Sections 12314 and 12404.5, the voting power of members having voting rights shall be equal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 192, Sec. 14. (AB 816) Effective January 1, 2016.)
