# CORP § 18060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 2. Application of Title

**Citation:** CORP § 18060

---

## Text

If a statute specific to a particular type of unincorporated association is inconsistent with a general provision of this title, the specific statute prevails to the extent of the inconsistency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
