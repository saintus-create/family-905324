# CORP § 14024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 7. Conflict of Interest

## Text
