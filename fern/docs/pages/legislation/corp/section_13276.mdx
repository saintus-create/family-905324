# CORP § 13276

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Officers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** CORP § 13276

---

## Text

Meetings of the board of directors may be held at any place within or without the State fixed by a quorum thereof unless otherwise provided in the articles of incorporation or by-laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
