# CORP § 29563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 7. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 29563

---

## Text

Every final order, decision, certificate, registration, or other official act of the commissioner is subject to judicial review in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
