# CORP § 184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 184

---

## Text

“Shares” means the units into which the proprietary interests in a corporation are divided in the articles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
