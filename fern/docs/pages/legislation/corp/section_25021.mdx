# CORP § 25021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 25021

---

## Text

“Subdivided lands” and “subdivision” have the meanings prescribed in Sections 11000, 11004.5, and 11218 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 697, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2005.)
