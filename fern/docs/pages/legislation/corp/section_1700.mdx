# CORP § 1700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 17. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 1700

---

## Text

In addition to the provisions of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 413. 10) of Title 5 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure, process may be served upon domestic corporations as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
