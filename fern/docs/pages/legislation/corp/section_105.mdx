# CORP § 105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 105

---

## Text

A corporation or association may be sued as provided in the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
