# CORP § 167.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 167.5

---

## Text

“Domestic limited partnership” means any limited partnership formed under the laws of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
