# CORP § 31124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Disclosure

**Citation:** CORP § 31124

---

## Text

An amendment to an application filed after the effective date of the registration of the sale of franchises, if such amendment is approved by the commissioner, shall become effective on such date as the commissioner may determine, having due regard for the public interest and the protection of franchisees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
