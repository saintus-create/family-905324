# CORP § 18000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 18000

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions in this chapter govern the construction of this title.
