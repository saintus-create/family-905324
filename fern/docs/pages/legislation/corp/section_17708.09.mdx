# CORP § 17708.09

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CALIFORNIA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT > Article: 8. Foreign Limited Liability Companies

**Citation:** CORP § 17708.09

---

## Text

The Attorney General may maintain an action to enjoin a foreign limited liability company from transacting intrastate business in this state in violation of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 419, Sec. 20. (SB 323) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 32 of Ch. 419.)
