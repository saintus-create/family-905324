# CORP § 5331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 3. Types of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 5331

---

## Text

Except as provided in or authorized by the articles or bylaws, all memberships shall have the same rights, privileges, preferences, restrictions and conditions.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
