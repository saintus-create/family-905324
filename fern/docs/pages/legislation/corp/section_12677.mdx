# CORP § 12677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 18. Crimes and Penalties

**Citation:** CORP § 12677

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the power of the state to punish any person for any conduct which constitutes a crime under any other statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
