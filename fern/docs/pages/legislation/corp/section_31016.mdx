# CORP § 31016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 31016

---

## Text

“Publish” means publicly to issue or circulate by newspaper, mail, radio or television, or otherwise to disseminate to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
