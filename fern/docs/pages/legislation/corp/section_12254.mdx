# CORP § 12254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12254

---

## Text

“Written” or “in writing” includes facsimile, telegraphic, and other electronic communication as authorized by this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 254, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2005.)
