# CORP § 177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 177

---

## Text

“Proper county” means the county where the principal office of the corporation is located or, if the principal office of the corporation is not located in this state, or the corporation has no such office, the County of Sacramento.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 617, Sec. 5. (SB 1202) Effective January 1, 2023.)
