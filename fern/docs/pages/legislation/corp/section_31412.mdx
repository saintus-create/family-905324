# CORP § 31412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. Crimes

**Citation:** CORP § 31412

---

## Text

Nothing in this law limits the power of the state to punish any person for any conduct which constitutes a crime under any other statute.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
