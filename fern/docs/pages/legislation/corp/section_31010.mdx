# CORP § 31010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 31010

---

## Text

Where used in this law, unless specifically stated otherwise, “franchise” includes “area franchise” and “subfranchise.”
