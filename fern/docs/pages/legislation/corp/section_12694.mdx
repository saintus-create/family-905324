# CORP § 12694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 20. Transition Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 12694

---

## Text

Subdivision (a) of Section 12353 of the new law shall apply to subject corporations, but the treasurer of these corporations shall be deemed to be the chief financial officer unless otherwise provided in the articles or bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 631, Sec. 50. (AB 1233) Effective January 1, 2010.)
