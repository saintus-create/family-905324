# CORP § 13313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Stock

**Citation:** CORP § 13313

---

## Text

Except as to the matters and things stated in the articles of incorporation no distinction shall exist between classes of stock or the holders thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
