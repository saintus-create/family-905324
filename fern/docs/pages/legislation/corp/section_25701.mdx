# CORP § 25701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 25701

---

## Text

Any condition, stipulation or provision purporting to bind any person acquiring any security to waive compliance with any provision of this law or any rule or order hereunder is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
