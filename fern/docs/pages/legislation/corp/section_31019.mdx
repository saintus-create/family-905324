# CORP § 31019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 31019

---

## Text

“State” means any state, territory, or possession of the United States, the District of Columbia and Puerto Rico.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
