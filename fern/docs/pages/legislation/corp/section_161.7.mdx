# CORP § 161.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 161.7

---

## Text

“Constituent other business entity” means an other business entity that is merged with or into one or more corporations and includes the surviving other business entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 10. Effective September 30, 1994.)
