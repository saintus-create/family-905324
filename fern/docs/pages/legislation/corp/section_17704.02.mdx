# CORP § 17704.02

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CALIFORNIA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT > Article: 4. Relations of Members to Each Other and to the Limited Liability Company

**Citation:** CORP § 17704.02

---

## Text

A contribution may consist of tangible or intangible property or other benefit to a limited liability company, including money, services performed, promissory notes, other agreements to contribute money or property, and contracts for services to be performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 419, Sec. 20. (SB 323) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 32 of Ch. 419.)
