# CORP § 12230.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12230.5

---

## Text

“Collective board worker cooperative” means a worker cooperative in which there is only one class of members consisting of worker-members, all of whom are members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 192, Sec. 6. (AB 816) Effective January 1, 2016.)
