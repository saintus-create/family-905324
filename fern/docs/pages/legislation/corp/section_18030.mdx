# CORP § 18030

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 18030

---

## Text

“Person” includes a natural person, corporation, partnership, or other unincorporated organization, government, or governmental subdivision or agency, or any other entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
