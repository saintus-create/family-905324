# CORP § 156.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 156.1

---

## Text

“Certificated security” means a share (Section 184), as defined in paragraph (4) of subdivision (a) of Section 8102 of, or an obligation of the issuer as described in paragraph (15) of subdivision (a) of, the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 497, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1997.)
