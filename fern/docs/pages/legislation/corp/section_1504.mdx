# CORP § 1504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 15. Records and Reports

**Citation:** CORP § 1504

---

## Text

If a natural person who has been designated agent for service of process pursuant to Section 202, 1502, 2105, or 2117 dies or resigns or no longer resides in the state or if the corporate agent for such purpose resigns, dissolves, withdraws from the state, forfeits its right to transact intrastate business, has its corporate rights, powers and privileges suspended or ceases to exist, the corporation shall forthwith file a designation of a new agent conforming to the requirements of Section 1502 or 2117.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 764, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1986, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 764.)
