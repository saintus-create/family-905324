# CORP § 190.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 190.5

---

## Text

“Surviving limited partnership” means a limited partnership into which one or more other limited partnerships or one or more corporations are merged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 543, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1994.)
