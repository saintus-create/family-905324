# CORP § 25122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. Recapitalizations and Reorganizations

**Citation:** CORP § 25122

---

## Text

Qualification of securities under this chapter becomes effective upon the commissioner issuing a permit authorizing the issuance of such securities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
