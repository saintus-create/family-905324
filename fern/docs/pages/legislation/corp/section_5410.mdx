# CORP § 5410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Distributions > Article: 1. Limitations

**Citation:** CORP § 5410

---

## Text

No corporation shall make any distribution. This section shall not apply to the purchase of a membership in a limited-equity housing cooperative, as defined in Section 33007.5 of the Health and Safety Code, which is organized as a public benefit corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 250, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
