# CORP § 167.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 167.3

---

## Text

“Domestic limited liability company” means a limited liability company as defined in subdivision (t) of Section 17000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 11. Effective September 30, 1994.)
