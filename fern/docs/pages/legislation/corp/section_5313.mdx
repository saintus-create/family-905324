# CORP § 5313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 5313

---

## Text

Except as provided in its articles or bylaws, a corporation may admit any person to membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
