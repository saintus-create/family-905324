# CORP § 5006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5006

---

## Text

The fees of the Secretary of State for filing instruments by or on behalf of corporations are prescribed in Article 3 (commencing with Section 12180) of Chapter 3 of Part 2 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
