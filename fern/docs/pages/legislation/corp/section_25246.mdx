# CORP § 25246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 25246

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any agent or broker-dealer to require, as a condition to the purchase or sale of securities for and in the name of a married person, that the prior consent or authorization of the spouse of that person be obtained.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1120, Sec. 10. Effective October 2, 1981. Operative November 1, 1981, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1120.)
