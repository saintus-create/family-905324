# CORP § 25003.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 25003.5

---

## Text

“Business days” are all days other than every Saturday, every Sunday, and such other days as are specified or provided for as holidays in the Government Code of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 390.)
