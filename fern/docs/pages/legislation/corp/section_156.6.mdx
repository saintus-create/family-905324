# CORP § 156.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 156.6

---

## Text

All references in this division to “chairperson of the board” shall be deemed to refer to all permissible titles for the chairperson of the board, as permitted by Section 312.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 98, Sec. 1. (SB 351) Effective January 1, 2016.)
