# CORP § 12228.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12228.3

---

## Text

“Capital account cooperative” is a worker cooperative in which the entire net book value is reflected in member capital accounts, one for each member, and an unallocated capital account, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 192, Sec. 5. (AB 816) Effective January 1, 2016.)
