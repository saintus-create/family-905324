# CORP § 14011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 4. New Corporations

**Citation:** CORP § 14011

---

## Text

The Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law (Part 2 (commencing with Section 5110) of Division 2 of this title) applies to corporations formed under this chapter, except as to matters otherwise provided for in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 537, Sec. 2. (AB 1247) Effective October 4, 2013.)
