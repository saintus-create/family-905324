# CORP § 29562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 7. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 29562

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this law, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
