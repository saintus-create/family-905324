# CORP § 9634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 3. Sale of Assets

**Citation:** CORP § 9634

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 5914) of Chapter 9 of Part 2 apply to religious corporations to the extent provided therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 442, Sec. 26. (AB 1211) Effective January 1, 2012.)
