# CORP § 31006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 31006

---

## Text

A “franchisee” is a person to whom a franchise is granted.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
