# CORP § 12581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 13. Records, Reports and Rights of Inspection > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 12581

---

## Text

Any inspection under this chapter may be made in person or by agent or attorney and the right of inspection includes the right to copy and make extracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
