# CORP § 16107

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 5. Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 16107

---

## Text

A partnership governed by this chapter is subject to any amendment to or repeal of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1003, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
