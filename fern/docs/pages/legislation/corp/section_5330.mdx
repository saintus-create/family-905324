# CORP § 5330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 3. Types of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 5330

---

## Text

A corporation may issue memberships having different rights, privileges, preferences, restrictions or conditions, as authorized by its articles or bylaws.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
