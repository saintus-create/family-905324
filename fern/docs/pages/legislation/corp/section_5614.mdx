# CORP § 5614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Voting of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 5614

---

## Text

A voting agreement or voting trust agreement entered into by a member or members of a corporation shall not be enforced.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
