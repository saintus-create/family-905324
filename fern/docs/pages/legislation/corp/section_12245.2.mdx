# CORP § 12245.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12245.2

---

## Text

“Preferred memberships” means memberships that have a preference over any other memberships with respect to distribution of assets on liquidation or with respect to payment of distributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 538, Sec. 1. (AB 1255) Effective January 1, 2014.)
