# CORP § 7311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 7311

---

## Text

Subject to the articles or bylaws, memberships may be issued by a corporation for no consideration or for such consideration as is determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 587, Sec. 30.)
