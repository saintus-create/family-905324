# CORP § 14301.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Water Companies

**Citation:** CORP § 14301.2

---

## Text

Each board member of a mutual water company that operates a public water system shall comply with the training requirements set out in subdivision (a) of Section 116755 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 512, Sec. 5. (AB 54) Effective January 1, 2012.)
