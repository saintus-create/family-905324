# CORP § 29006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BUCKET SHOP LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29006

---

## Text

“Bucket shop” means any room, office, store, building, or other place where any bucketing or bucket shopping contract is made or offered to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 386.)
