# CORP § 5071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5071

---

## Text

“Shareholder,” as used in Part 3 (commencing with Section 7110), means one who is a holder of record of shares.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 101, Sec. 25.)
