# CORP § 28603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 9. Merger and Purchase or Sale of Business

**Citation:** CORP § 28603

---

## Text

No licensee shall sell all or substantially all of its business to any other person unless the other person is a licensee and the sale is first approved by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
