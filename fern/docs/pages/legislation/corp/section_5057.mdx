# CORP § 5057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5057

---

## Text

A “membership” refers to the rights a member has pursuant to a corporation’s articles, bylaws and this division.
