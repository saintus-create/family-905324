# CORP § 194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 194

---

## Text

“Vote” includes authorization by written consent, subject to the provisions of subdivision (b) of Section 307 and subdivision (d) of Section 603.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 128, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
