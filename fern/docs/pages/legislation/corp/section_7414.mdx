# CORP § 7414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Distributions > Article: 1. Limitations

**Citation:** CORP § 7414

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter prohibits additional restrictions upon the purchase or redemption of a membership by provision in a corporation’s articles or bylaws or agreement entered into by the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
