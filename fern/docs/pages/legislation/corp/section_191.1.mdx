# CORP § 191.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 191.1

---

## Text

“Uncertificated security” means a share (Section 184), or an obligation of the issuer, described in paragraphs (15) and (18) of subdivision (a) of Section 8102 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 497, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1997.)
