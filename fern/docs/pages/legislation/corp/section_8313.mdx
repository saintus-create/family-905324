# CORP § 8313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 13. Records, Reports, and Rights of Inspection > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 8313

---

## Text

The rights of members provided in this chapter may not be limited by contract or the articles or bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
