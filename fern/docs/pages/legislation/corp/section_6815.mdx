# CORP § 6815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 18. Crimes and Penalties

**Citation:** CORP § 6815

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the power of the state to punish any person for any conduct which constitutes a crime under any other statute.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
