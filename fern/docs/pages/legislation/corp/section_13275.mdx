# CORP § 13275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Officers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** CORP § 13275

---

## Text

The affairs of the association shall be managed by a board of not less than three directors, elected by the members from their own number.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
