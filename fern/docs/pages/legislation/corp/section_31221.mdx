# CORP § 31221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. Unfair Practices

**Citation:** CORP § 31221

---

## Text

It shall be a violation of this division for any franchisor, directly or indirectly, through any officer, agent, or employee, to violate the provisions of Section 31126.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 728, Sec. 10. (AB 676) Effective January 1, 2023.)
