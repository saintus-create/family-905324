# CORP § 25615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 7. ADMINISTRATION > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 25615

---

## Text

A document is filed when it is received by the commissioner; but no qualification of the sale of securities shall become effective until the appropriate fee has been paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
