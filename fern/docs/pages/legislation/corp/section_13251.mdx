# CORP § 13251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Formation, Articles, and By-laws > Article: 3. By-laws

**Citation:** CORP § 13251

---

## Text

The by-laws may provide for an executive committee and may allot to such committee all the functions and powers of the board of directors, subject to the general direction and control of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
