# CORP § 13314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Stock

**Citation:** CORP § 13314

---

## Text

If an association issues nonpar value stock the issuance of such stock shall be governed by the terms of the General Corporation Law covering the issuance of nonpar value stock in domestic corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
