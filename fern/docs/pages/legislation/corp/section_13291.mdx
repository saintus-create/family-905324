# CORP § 13291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Officers > Article: 2. Removal of Officers

**Citation:** CORP § 13291

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 13293, the removal shall be voted upon at the next regular or special meeting of the association and, by a vote of a majority of the members, the association may remove the officer or director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
