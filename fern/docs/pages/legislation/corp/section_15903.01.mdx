# CORP § 15903.01

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 3. Limited Partners

**Citation:** CORP § 15903.01

---

## Text

A person becomes a limited partner:

(a) as provided in the partnership agreement;

(b) as the result of a conversion or merger under Article 11 (commencing with Section 15911.01); or

(c) with the consent of all the partners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
