# CORP § 16501

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 5. Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 > Article: 5. Transferees and Creditors of Partner

**Citation:** CORP § 16501

---

## Text

A partner is not a coowner of partnership property and has no interest in partnership property that can be transferred, either voluntarily or involuntarily.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1003, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
