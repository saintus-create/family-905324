# CORP § 2319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 23. Transition Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 2319

---

## Text

If the corporate rights, privileges and powers of a corporation have been suspended and are still suspended immediately prior to the effective date pursuant to Sections 5700 through 5908 of the old law and provisions of law there referred to, said sections and provisions continue to apply to such a corporation until restoration by the Controller pursuant to said sections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
