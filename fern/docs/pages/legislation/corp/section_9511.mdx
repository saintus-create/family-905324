# CORP § 9511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Records, Reports and Rights of Inspection

**Citation:** CORP § 9511

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in the articles or bylaws, a member may inspect and copy the record of all the members’ names, addresses and voting rights, at reasonable times, upon five business days’ prior written demand upon the corporation for a purpose reasonably related to the member’s interest as a member.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
