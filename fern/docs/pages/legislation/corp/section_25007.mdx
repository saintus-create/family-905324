# CORP § 25007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 25007

---

## Text

“Guaranteed” means guaranteed as to payment of principal, interest, dividends, or call premium.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
