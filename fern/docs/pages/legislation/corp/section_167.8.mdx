# CORP § 167.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 167.8

---

## Text

“Disappearing other business entity” means a constituent other business entity that is not the surviving other business entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 13. Effective September 30, 1994.)
