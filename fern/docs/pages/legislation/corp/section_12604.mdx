# CORP § 12604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 13. Records, Reports and Rights of Inspection > Article: 3. Rights of Inspection

**Citation:** CORP § 12604

---

## Text

Every director shall have the absolute right at any reasonable time to inspect and copy all books, records and documents of every kind and to inspect the physical properties of the corporation of which such person is a director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
