# CORP § 180.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 180.5

---

## Text

“Redomestication” means the transfer of an insurer’s place of incorporation from another state to this state or from this state to another state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 702, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
