# CORP § 18260

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Liability and Enforcement of Judgments

**Citation:** CORP § 18260

---

## Text

A money judgment against an unincorporated association, whether organized for profit or not, may be enforced only against the property of the association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
