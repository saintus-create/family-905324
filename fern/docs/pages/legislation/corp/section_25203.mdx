# CORP § 25203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Exemptions

**Citation:** CORP § 25203

---

## Text

A person whose only clients are insurance companies shall be exempted from the provisions of Section 25230.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 391, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1998.)
