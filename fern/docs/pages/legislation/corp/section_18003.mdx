# CORP § 18003

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 18003

---

## Text

“Board” means the board of directors or other governing body of an unincorporated association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 116, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
