# CORP § 28881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 12. Crimes and Criminal Penalties > Article: 3. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** CORP § 28881

---

## Text

Nothing in this division limits the power of the state to punish any person for any act which constitutes a crime under any statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
