# CORP § 16917

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 5. Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 > Article: 9. Conversions and Mergers

**Citation:** CORP § 16917

---

## Text

This article is not exclusive. Partnerships, other than limited liability partnerships, may be converted or merged in any other manner provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1003, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
