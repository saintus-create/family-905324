# CORP § 2509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2509

---

## Text

“Social purpose corporation,” unless otherwise expressly provided, refers only to a corporation organized under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 25. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
