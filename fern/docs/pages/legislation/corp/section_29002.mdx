# CORP § 29002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BUCKET SHOP LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29002

---

## Text

(a) “Trustee” means a person executing a trust, as defined in this section.

(b) “Trust” means any voluntary trust expressly created by or declared in an instrument in writing, other than a will or a judicial writ, order, decree, or judgment, to carry on any business or to secure the payment or repayment of money.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 820, Sec. 23. Operative July 1, 1987, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 820.)
