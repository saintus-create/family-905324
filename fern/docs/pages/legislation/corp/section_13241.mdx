# CORP § 13241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Formation, Articles, and By-laws > Article: 3. By-laws

**Citation:** CORP § 13241

---

## Text

The by-laws shall prohibit the transfer of the common stock or membership certificates of the associations to persons not engaged in the production of the products handled by the association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
