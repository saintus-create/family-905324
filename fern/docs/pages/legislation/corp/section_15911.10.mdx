# CORP § 15911.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 11. Conversion and Merger

**Citation:** CORP § 15911.10

---

## Text

Mergers of limited partnerships shall be governed by Sections 15911.11 to 15911.19, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
