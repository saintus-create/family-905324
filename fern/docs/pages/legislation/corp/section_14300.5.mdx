# CORP § 14300.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Water Companies

**Citation:** CORP § 14300.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “public water system” shall have the same meaning as provided in Section 116275 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 512, Sec. 3. (AB 54) Effective January 1, 2012.)
