# CORP § 14602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 14602

---

## Text

A benefit corporation shall be formed in accordance with Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 200) of Division 1 except that the articles shall also state that the corporation is a benefit corporation and shall identify any specific public benefit adopted pursuant to Section 14610.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 728, Sec. 1. (AB 361) Effective January 1, 2012.)
