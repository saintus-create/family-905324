# CORP § 9320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 2. Transfer of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 9320

---

## Text

Subject to Section 9417:

(a) No member may transfer for value a membership or any right arising therefrom; and

(b) Unless otherwise provided in the corporation’s articles or bylaws, all rights of membership cease upon the member’s death or dissolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 587, Sec. 50.)
