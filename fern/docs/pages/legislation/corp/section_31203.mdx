# CORP § 31203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Fraudulent Practices

**Citation:** CORP § 31203

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to violate any order of the commissioner or condition to the effectiveness of the registration of the offer or sale of franchises.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
