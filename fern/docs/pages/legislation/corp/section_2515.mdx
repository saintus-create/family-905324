# CORP § 2515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2515

---

## Text

“Special purpose MD&A” means the management discussion and analysis required of a social purpose corporation pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 3500.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 32. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
