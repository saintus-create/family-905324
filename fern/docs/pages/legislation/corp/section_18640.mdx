# CORP § 18640

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 18640

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits application of the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act (Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 3439) of Title 2 of Part 2 of Division 4 of the Civil Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 44, Sec. 25. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2016.)
