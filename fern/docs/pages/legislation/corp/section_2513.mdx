# CORP § 2513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2513

---

## Text

“Special purpose” means the special purpose set forth in a social purpose corporation’s articles pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 2602.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 30. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
