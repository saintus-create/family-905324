# CORP § 2315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 23. Transition Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 2315

---

## Text

Chapters 19 (commencing with Section 1900) and 20 (commencing with Section 2000) of the new law apply to any voluntary dissolution proceeding initiated by the filing of an election after the effective date, but the prior law governs any such proceeding so initiated prior to the effective date.
