# CORP § 12242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12242

---

## Text

“On the certificate,” as used in this part means that a statement appears on the face of a certificate or on the reverse thereof with a reference thereto on the face.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
