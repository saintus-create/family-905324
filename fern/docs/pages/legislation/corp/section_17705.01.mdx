# CORP § 17705.01

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.6. CALIFORNIA REVISED UNIFORM LIMITED LIABILITY COMPANY ACT > Article: 5. Transferable Interests and Rights of Transferees and Creditors

**Citation:** CORP § 17705.01

---

## Text

A transferable interest is personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 419, Sec. 20. (SB 323) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 32 of Ch. 419.)
