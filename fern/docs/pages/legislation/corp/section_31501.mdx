# CORP § 31501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 5. ADMINISTRATION > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 31501

---

## Text

Every final order, decision, license, or other official act of the commissioner is subject to judicial review in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
