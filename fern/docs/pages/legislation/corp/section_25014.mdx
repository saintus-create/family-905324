# CORP § 25014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 25014

---

## Text

“Publish” means publicly to issue or circulate by newspaper, mail, radio or television, or otherwise to disseminate to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
