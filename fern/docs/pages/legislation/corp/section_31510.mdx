# CORP § 31510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 6. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 31510

---

## Text

The commissioner in his discretion may honor requests from interested persons for interpretive opinions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
