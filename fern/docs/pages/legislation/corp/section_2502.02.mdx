# CORP § 2502.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2502.02

---

## Text

Unless otherwise expressly provided, whenever reference is made in this division to any other state or federal statute, that reference is to that statute as it may be amended from time to time, whether before or after the enactment of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 740, Sec. 12. (SB 201) Effective January 1, 2012.)
