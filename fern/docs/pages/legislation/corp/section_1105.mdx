# CORP § 1105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 11. Merger

**Citation:** CORP § 1105

---

## Text

The board may, in its discretion, abandon a merger, subject to the contractual rights, if any, of third parties, including other constituent corporations, without further approval by the outstanding shares (Section 152), at any time before the merger is effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
