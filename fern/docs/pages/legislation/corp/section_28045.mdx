# CORP § 28045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 28045

---

## Text

“To provide financing assistance to a person” means to purchase securities issued by the person, either directly from the person, or indirectly through a securities underwriter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
