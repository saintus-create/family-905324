# CORP § 13326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Powers

**Citation:** CORP § 13326

---

## Text

An association may borrow without limitation as to amount of corporate indebtedness or liability and may make advances to members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
