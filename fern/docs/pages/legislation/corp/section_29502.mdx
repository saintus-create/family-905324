# CORP § 29502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29502

---

## Text

“Business days” are all days other than every Saturday, every Sunday and such other days as are specified or provided for as holidays in the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
