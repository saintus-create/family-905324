# CORP § 18105

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Property

**Citation:** CORP § 18105

---

## Text

An unincorporated association may, in its name, acquire, hold, manage, encumber, or transfer an interest in real or personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
