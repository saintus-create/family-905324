# CORP § 13303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Members

**Citation:** CORP § 13303

---

## Text

Meetings of members shall be held at the place as provided in the by-laws; and if no provision is made, in the city where the principal place of business is located at a place designated by the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
