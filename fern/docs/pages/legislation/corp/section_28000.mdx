# CORP § 28000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title and Construction

**Citation:** CORP § 28000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the “Capital Access Company Law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
