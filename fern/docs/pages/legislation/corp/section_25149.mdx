# CORP § 25149

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 5. Authority of the Commissioner

**Citation:** CORP § 25149

---

## Text

The commissioner may act as escrow holder for securities required to be deposited in escrow by his order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
