# CORP § 29007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BUCKET SHOP LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29007

---

## Text

“Keeper” means any person owning, keeping, managing, operating, or promoting a bucket shop, or assisting to keep, manage, operate, or promote a bucket shop.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 386.)
