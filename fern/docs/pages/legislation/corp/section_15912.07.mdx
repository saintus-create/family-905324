# CORP § 15912.07

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 12. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 15912.07

---

## Text

This chapter does not affect an action commenced, proceeding brought, or right accrued before this chapter becomes operative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
