# CORP § 14000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 1. Introduction

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Small Business Financial Development Corporation Law.
