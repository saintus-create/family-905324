# CORP § 29512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29512

---

## Text

“Good funds” means the verified receipt of immediately available funds according to the applicable requirements of federal Regulation CC.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
