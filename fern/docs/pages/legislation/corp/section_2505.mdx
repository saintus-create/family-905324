# CORP § 2505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2505

---

## Text

“Conversion” means a conversion pursuant to Chapter 11.5 (commencing with Section 1150) of Division 1 and Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 3300) of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 740, Sec. 12. (SB 201) Effective January 1, 2012.)
