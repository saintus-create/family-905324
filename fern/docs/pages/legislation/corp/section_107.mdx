# CORP § 107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 107

---

## Text

No corporation, social purpose corporation, association, or individual shall issue or put in circulation, as money, anything but the lawful money of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 1. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
