# CORP § 13279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Officers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** CORP § 13279

---

## Text

An association may provide a fair remuneration for the time actually spent by its officers and directors in its service and for the service of the members of its executive committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
