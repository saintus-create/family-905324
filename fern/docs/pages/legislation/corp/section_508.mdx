# CORP § 508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Dividends and Reacquisitions of Shares

**Citation:** CORP § 508

## Text

This chapter does not apply in connection with any proceeding for winding up and dissolution under Chapter 18 or 19.
