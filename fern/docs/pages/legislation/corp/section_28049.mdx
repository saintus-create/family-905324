# CORP § 28049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Voting power” has the meaning set forth in Section 194.5.
