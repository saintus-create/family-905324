# CORP § 13301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Members

**Citation:** CORP § 13301

---

## Text

When a member of an association established without shares of stock has paid his membership fee in full, he shall receive a certificate of membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
