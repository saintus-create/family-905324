# CORP § 170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 170

---

## Text

“Foreign association” means a business association organized as a trust under the laws of a foreign jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
