# CORP § 15912.04

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 12. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 15912.04

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2008.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 5.5 (later renumbered as Chapter 4.5), comprising Sections 15900 to 15912.07.)
