# CORP § 12400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 12400

---

## Text

Subject to the articles or bylaws, memberships may be issued by a corporation for no consideration or for such consideration as is determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
