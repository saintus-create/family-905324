# CORP § 171.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 171.03

---

## Text

“Foreign limited liability company” means a foreign limited liability company as defined in subdivision (j) of Section 17701.02.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 419, Sec. 5. (SB 323) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 32 of Ch. 419.)
