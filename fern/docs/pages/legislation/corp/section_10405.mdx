# CORP § 10405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 9. SOCIETIES FOR PREVENTION OF CRUELTY TO ANIMALS > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 10405

---

## Text

All magistrates, sheriffs, and officers of police shall, as occasion may require, aid any such corporation, its officers, members, and agents, in the enforcement of all laws relating to or affecting animals.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 652, Sec. 5. (SB 1417) Effective January 1, 2011.)
