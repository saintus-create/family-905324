# CORP § 14400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Part: 9. CABLE TELEVISION CORPORATIONS > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Mutual Water Companies Formed in Connection With Subdivided Lands

**Citation:** CORP § 14400

---

## Text

Any person who willfully and maliciously does any injury to any property of a cable television corporation is liable to the corporation for three times the amount of actual damages sustained thereby, to be recovered in any court of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 598, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1998.)
