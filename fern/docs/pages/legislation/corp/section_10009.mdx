# CORP § 10009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 6. CORPORATIONS SOLE > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 10009

---

## Text

Any judge of the superior court in the county in which a corporation sole has its principal office shall at all times have access to the books of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1038.)
