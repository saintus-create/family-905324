# CORP § 171.08

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 171.08

---

## Text

“Social purpose corporation” means any social purpose corporation formed under Division 1.5 (commencing with Section 2500).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 3. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
