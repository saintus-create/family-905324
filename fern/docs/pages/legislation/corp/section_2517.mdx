# CORP § 2517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2517

---

## Text

“Surviving social purpose corporation” means a social purpose corporation into which one or more other corporations or one or more other business entities is merged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 34. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
