# CORP § 18605

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 18605

---

## Text

A member, director, or agent of a nonprofit association is not liable for a debt, obligation, or liability of the association solely by reason of being a member, director, officer, or agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2005.)
