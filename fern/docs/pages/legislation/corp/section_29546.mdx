# CORP § 29546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 5. Powers of the Commissioner

**Citation:** CORP § 29546

---

## Text

The commissioner in his or her discretion may honor requests from interested parties for interpretive opinions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
