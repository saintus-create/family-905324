# CORP § 16201

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 5. Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 > Article: 2. Nature of Partnership

**Citation:** CORP § 16201

---

## Text

A partnership is an entity distinct from its partners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1003, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
