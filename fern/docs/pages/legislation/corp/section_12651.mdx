# CORP § 12651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 17. General Provisions Relating to Dissolution

**Citation:** CORP § 12651

---

## Text

A vacancy on the board may be filled during a winding up proceeding in the manner provided in Section 12364.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
