# CORP § 176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 176

---

## Text

“Preferred shares” means shares other than common shares.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
