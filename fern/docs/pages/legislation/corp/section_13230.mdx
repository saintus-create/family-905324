# CORP § 13230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Formation, Articles, and By-laws > Article: 2. Articles of Incorporation

**Citation:** CORP § 13230

---

## Text

The articles of incorporation of any association may be altered or amended in the manner and for the purposes prescribed by the General Corporation Law for domestic corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
