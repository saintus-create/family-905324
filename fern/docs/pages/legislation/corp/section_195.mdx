# CORP § 195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 195

---

## Text

“Written” or “in writing” includes facsimile, telegraphic, and other electronic communication when authorized by this code, including an electronic transmission by a corporation that satisfies the requirements of Section 20.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 254, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2005.)
