# CORP § 161.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 161.9

---

## Text

“Conversion” means a conversion pursuant to Chapter 11.5 (commencing with Section 1150).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 480, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
