# CORP § 5039.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5039.5

---

## Text

All references in this division to “chairperson of the board,” other than in Sections 5213, 7213, and 9213, shall be deemed to refer to all permissible titles for a chair of the board, as permitted by Sections 5213, 7213, and 9213.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 617, Sec. 44. (SB 1202) Effective January 1, 2023.)
