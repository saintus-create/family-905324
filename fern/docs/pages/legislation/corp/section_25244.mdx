# CORP § 25244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 25244

---

## Text

Any person whose certificate as a broker-dealer or investment adviser has been suspended or revoked shall immediately surrender such certificate to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1120, Sec. 9. Effective October 2, 1981. Operative November 1, 1981, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1120.)
