# CORP § 2503.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2503.1

---

## Text

“Close social purpose corporation” means a social purpose corporation that is also a close corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 21. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
