# CORP § 28101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** CORP § 28101

---

## Text

Whenever the commissioner issues an order or license under this division, the commissioner may impose any conditions that are in his or her opinion necessary to carry out the provisions and purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
