# CORP § 18005

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 18005

---

## Text

“Director” means a natural person serving as a member of the board or other governing body of the unincorporated association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 116, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
