# CORP § 14020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 6. Corporations, Miscellaneous

**Citation:** CORP § 14020

---

## Text

A corporation shall report to the program manager, or his or her designated representative, all statistical and other reports required by this chapter and Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 63088) of Division 1 of Title 6.7 of the Government Code, including responses to audit reports, budget requirements, and other information relating to the establishment, monitoring, and suspension or termination of a corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 537, Sec. 2. (AB 1247) Effective October 4, 2013.)
