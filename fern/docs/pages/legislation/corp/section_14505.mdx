# CORP § 14505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Part: 11. SOCIETIES FOR THE PREVENTION OF CRUELTY TO ANIMALS > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Mutual Water Companies Formed in Connection With Subdivided Lands

**Citation:** CORP § 14505

---

## Text

Any law enforcement agency that is requested to provide summary criminal history information pursuant to Section 13300 of the Penal Code may charge the humane society or society for the prevention of cruelty to animals a fee not to exceed the reasonable costs of preparing reports and costs to maintain certificates of compliance as required by Section 14502.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 652, Sec. 12. (SB 1417) Effective January 1, 2011.)
