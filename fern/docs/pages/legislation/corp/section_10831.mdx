# CORP § 10831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 11. NONPROFIT MEDICAL, HOSPITAL, OR LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATIONS > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 3. Nonprofit Corporation to Administer System of Defraying Cost of Professional Services of Attorneys

**Citation:** CORP § 10831

---

## Text

The provisions of this article apply to corporations formed on or after January 1, 1980, under Part 3 of this division and pursuant to this article and to corporations existing on December 31, 1979, and formed under Section 9201.2 of the Corporations Code then in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1305.)
