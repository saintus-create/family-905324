# CORP § 156.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 156.5

---

## Text

“Certificate of Redomestication” is the document by which the appropriate official of another state approves the redomestication of a California insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 702, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
