# CORP § 12228.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12228.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, all references to “chairperson of the board,” other than in Section 12353, shall be deemed to refer to all permissible titles for a chair of the board, as permitted by Section 12353.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 617, Sec. 74. (SB 1202) Effective January 1, 2023.)
