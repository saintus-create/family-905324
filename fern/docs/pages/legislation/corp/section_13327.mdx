# CORP § 13327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Powers

**Citation:** CORP § 13327

---

## Text

An association may act as the agent or representative of any member or members in any of the two next preceding sections.
