# CORP § 12451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Distributions > Article: 1. Limitations

**Citation:** CORP § 12451

---

## Text

Distributions (Section 12235) in any fiscal year shall not exceed 15 percent, multiplied by contributions (whether by membership fees, capital credits, or otherwise) to capital.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 792, Sec. 23.)
