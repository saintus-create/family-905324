# CORP § 27003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SECURITY OWNERS PROTECTION > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 27003

---

## Text

If any provision of this division, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, the remainder of this division, or the application of such provision to other persons or circumstances, shall not be affected thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 27007 by Stats. 1996, Ch. 458, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1997.)
