# CORP § 2100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 21. Foreign Corporations

**Citation:** CORP § 2100

---

## Text

This chapter applies only to foreign corporations transacting intrastate business, except as otherwise expressly provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
