# CORP § 27000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SECURITY OWNERS PROTECTION > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 27000

---

## Text

Unless the provision or the context otherwise indicates, and except as expressly otherwise provided in this chapter, words used in this division have the meanings prescribed in Part 1 (commencing with Section 25000) of the Corporate Securities Law.
