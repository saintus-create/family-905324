# CORP § 18110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Property

**Citation:** CORP § 18110

---

## Text

Property acquired by or for an unincorporated association is property of the unincorporated association and not of the members individually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
