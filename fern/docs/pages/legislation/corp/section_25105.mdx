# CORP § 25105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Exemptions and Certain Securities and Transactions Not Subject to Qualification

**Citation:** CORP § 25105

---

## Text

There shall be exempted from the provisions of Section 25110, 25120 or 25130 any other transaction which the commissioner by rule exempts as not being comprehended within the purposes of this law and the qualification of which he finds is not necessary or appropriate in the public interest or for the protection of investors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
