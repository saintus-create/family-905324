# CORP § 16402

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 5. Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 > Article: 4. Relations of Partners to Each Other and to Partnership

**Citation:** CORP § 16402

---

## Text

A partner has no right to receive, and may not be required to accept, a distribution in kind.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1003, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
