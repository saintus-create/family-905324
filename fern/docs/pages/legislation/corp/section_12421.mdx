# CORP § 12421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 3. Types of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 12421

---

## Text

Except as provided in the articles or bylaws, all memberships shall have the same rights, privileges, preferences, restrictions and conditions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
