# CORP § 15901.15

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 15901.15

---

## Text

Action requiring the consent of partners under this chapter may be taken without a meeting, and a partner may appoint a proxy to consent or otherwise act for the partner by signing an appointment record, either personally or by the partner’s attorney in fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
