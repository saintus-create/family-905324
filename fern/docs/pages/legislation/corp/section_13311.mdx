# CORP § 13311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Stock

**Citation:** CORP § 13311

---

## Text

One class of stock shall always be known as common stock and voting power may be restricted to holders of common stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
