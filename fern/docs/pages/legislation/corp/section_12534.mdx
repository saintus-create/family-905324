# CORP § 12534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 10. Mergers > Article: 1. Merger

**Citation:** CORP § 12534

---

## Text

Each constituent corporation shall sign the agreement by the chairperson of its board, president or a vice president and secretary or an assistant secretary acting on behalf of their respective corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 617, Sec. 83. (SB 1202) Effective January 1, 2023.)
