# CORP § 28715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 11. Enforcement

**Citation:** CORP § 28715

---

## Text

Sections 11041, 11042, and 11043 of the Government Code do not apply to the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 88. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
