# CORP § 28100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** CORP § 28100

---

## Text

The commissioner shall administer and enforce the provisions of this division in a manner that facilitates the legislative purposes set forth in Section 28004, consistent with protection of investors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 516, Sec. 4. (SB 1155) Effective January 1, 2011.)
