# CORP § 203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Organization and Bylaws

**Citation:** CORP § 203

---

## Text

Except as specified in the articles or in any shareholders’ agreement, no distinction shall exist between classes or series of shares or the holders thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
