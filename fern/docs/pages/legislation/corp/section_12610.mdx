# CORP § 12610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 14. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 12610

---

## Text

Service of process upon a corporation shall be governed by Chapter 17 (commencing with Section 1700) of Division 1 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
