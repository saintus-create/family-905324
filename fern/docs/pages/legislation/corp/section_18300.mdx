# CORP § 18300

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 6. Governance > Article: 1. [Reserved]

**Citation:** CORP § 18300

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to enact legislation relating to the governance of unincorporated associations.

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 116, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2006.)
