# CORP § 29506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29506

---

## Text

“Commodity Exchange Act” means the federal statute so named, as amended before or after January 1, 1991.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
