# CORP § 12452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Distributions > Article: 1. Limitations

**Citation:** CORP § 12452

---

## Text

A corporation may, subject to meeting the requirements of Section 12453 and any additional restrictions authorized by Section 12454, purchase or redeem memberships.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 378, Sec. 7.)
