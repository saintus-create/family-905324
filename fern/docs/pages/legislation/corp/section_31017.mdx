# CORP § 31017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

## Text

“Rule” means any published regulation or standard of general application issued by the commissioner.
