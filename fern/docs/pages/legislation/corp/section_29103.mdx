# CORP § 29103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BUCKET SHOP LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Prohibited Activities

**Citation:** CORP § 29103

---

## Text

The prosecution, conviction, and punishment of a corporation under any provision of this chapter shall not be deemed to be a prosecution, conviction, or punishment of any of its officers, directors, or shareholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 386.)
