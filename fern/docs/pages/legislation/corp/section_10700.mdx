# CORP § 10700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 10. PORT AND TERMINAL PROTECTION AND DEVELOPMENT CORPORATIONS > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 10700

---

## Text

As used in this part, “public agency” includes every port district, river port district, municipal port district, harbor district, harbor improvement district, joint harbor improvement district, Board of State Harbor Commissioners, board of harbor commissioners, city, county, and city and county in this state.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1305.)
