# CORP § 7131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Organization and Bylaws > Article: 3. Articles of Incorporation

**Citation:** CORP § 7131

---

## Text

The articles of incorporation may set forth a further statement limiting the purposes or powers of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
