# CORP § 29551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 6. Crimes and Civil Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 29551

---

## Text

Nothing in this law limits the power of the state to punish any person for any conduct which constitutes a crime under any other statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
