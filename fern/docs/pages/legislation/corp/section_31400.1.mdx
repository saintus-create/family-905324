# CORP § 31400.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Powers of the Commissioner

**Citation:** CORP § 31400.1

---

## Text

In any proceeding under Section 31400, the court may prohibit, conditionally or unconditionally, and permanently or for such period of time as it shall determine, any person who violated Section 31200, 31201, or 31202 from acting as an officer or director of any franchisor if the person’s conduct demonstrates unfitness to serve as an officer or director of the franchisor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 101, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2008.)
