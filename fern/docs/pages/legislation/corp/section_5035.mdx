# CORP § 5035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS GOVERNING PARTS 1 THROUGH 5 > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS

**Citation:** CORP § 5035

---

## Text

“Articles” includes the articles of incorporation, amendments thereto, amended articles, restated articles, and certificates of incorporation.
