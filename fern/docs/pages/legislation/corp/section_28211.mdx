# CORP § 28211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Organizational Matters > Article: 2. Directors

**Citation:** CORP § 28211

---

## Text

The board of directors, executive committee, or other policy body of each licensee shall hold a meeting not less frequently than once each calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
