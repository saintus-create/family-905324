# CORP § 31305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 31305

---

## Text

Every cause of action under this chapter survives the death of any person who might have been a plaintiff or defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
