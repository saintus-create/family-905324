# CORP § 14018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. California Small Business Financial Development Corporations > Article: 6. Corporations, Miscellaneous

**Citation:** CORP § 14018

---

## Text

Every corporation shall provide for, and maintain a central staff to perform, all administrative requirements of the corporation, including all those functions required of a corporation by the contract and this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 537, Sec. 2. (AB 1247) Effective October 4, 2013.)
