# CORP § 31003.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES

**Citation:** CORP § 31003.5

---

## Text

“Business days” are all days other than every Saturday, every Sunday, and such other days as are specified or provided for as holidays in the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 539.)
