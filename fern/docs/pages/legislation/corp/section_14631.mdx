# CORP § 14631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 4. Transparency

**Citation:** CORP § 14631

---

## Text

All certificates representing shares of a benefit corporation shall contain, in addition to any other statements required by the General Corporation Law (Division 1 (commencing with Section 100)), the following conspicuous language on the face of the certificate:

“This entity is a benefit corporation organized under Part 13 (commencing with Section 14600) of Division 3 of Title 1 of the California Corporations Code.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 728, Sec. 1. (AB 361) Effective January 1, 2012.)
