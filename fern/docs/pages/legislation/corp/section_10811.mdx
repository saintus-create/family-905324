# CORP § 10811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Part: 11. NONPROFIT MEDICAL, HOSPITAL, OR LEGAL SERVICES CORPORATIONS > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions > Article: 1. Nonprofit Corporations for Medical Services

**Citation:** CORP § 10811

---

## Text

Any nonprofit corporation described in Section 10810 which defrays or assumes some portion or all of the costs of refractions or eye appliances shall offer an equal opportunity to render professional services upon a uniform basis to all licentiates expressly authorized by law to render such services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1305.)
