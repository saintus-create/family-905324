# CORP § 31150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FRANCHISE INVESTMENT LAW > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 31150

---

## Text

Every franchisor or subfranchisor offering franchises for sale in this state shall at all times keep and maintain a complete set of books, records, and accounts of such sales.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1400.)
