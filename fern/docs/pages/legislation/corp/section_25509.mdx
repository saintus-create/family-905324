# CORP § 25509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 25509

---

## Text

Every cause of action under this chapter survives the death of any person who might have been a plaintiff or defendant.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
