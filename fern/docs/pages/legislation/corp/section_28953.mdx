# CORP § 28953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 14. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 28953

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this law, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 668, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 668.)
