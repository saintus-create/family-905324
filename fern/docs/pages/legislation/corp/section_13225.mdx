# CORP § 13225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Formation, Articles, and By-laws > Article: 2. Articles of Incorporation

**Citation:** CORP § 13225

---

## Text

Articles of incorporation shall be signed, acknowledged, and filed in the manner prescribed by the General Corporation Law for domestic corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 207.)
