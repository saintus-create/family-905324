# CORP § 7914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 9. Sales of Assets

**Citation:** CORP § 7914

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 5914) of Chapter 9 of Part 2 apply to mutual benefit corporations to the extent provided therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 442, Sec. 17. (AB 1211) Effective January 1, 2012.)
