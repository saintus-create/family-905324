# CORP § 28154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CAPITAL ACCESS COMPANIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** CORP § 28154

---

## Text

Except pursuant to Section 28551, no license shall be transferable or assignable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 516, Sec. 7. (SB 1155) Effective January 1, 2011.)
