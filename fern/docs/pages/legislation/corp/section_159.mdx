# CORP § 159

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 159

---

## Text

“Common shares” means shares which have no preference over any other shares with respect to distribution of assets on liquidation or with respect to payment of dividends.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
