# CORP § 25508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability

**Citation:** CORP § 25508

---

## Text

No action shall be maintained to enforce any right of indemnification or contribution created by Section 25505 unless brought before the expiration of one year after final judgment based upon the liability for which the right of indemnification or contribution exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
