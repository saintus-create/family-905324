# CORP § 5226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 2. Directors and Management > Article: 2. Selection, Removal and Resignation of Directors

**Citation:** CORP § 5226

---

## Text

Except upon notice to the Attorney General, no director may resign where the corporation would then be left without a duly elected director or directors in charge of its affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
