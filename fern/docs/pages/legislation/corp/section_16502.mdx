# CORP § 16502

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 5. Uniform Partnership Act of 1994 > Article: 5. Transferees and Creditors of Partner

**Citation:** CORP § 16502

---

## Text

The only transferable interest of a partner in the partnership is the partner’s share of the profits and losses of the partnership and the partner’s right to receive distributions. The interest is personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1003, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
