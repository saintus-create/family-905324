# CORP § 161.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 161.5

---

## Text

“Constituent limited partnership” means a limited partnership which is merged with one or more corporations and includes the surviving limited partnership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 543, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
