# CORP § 7315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 7315

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), or in its articles or bylaws, a corporation may admit any person to membership.

(b) A corporation may not admit its subsidiary (Section 5073) to membership.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 724.)
