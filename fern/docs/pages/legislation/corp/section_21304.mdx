# CORP § 21304

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 4. Insignia

**Citation:** CORP § 21304

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall charge and collect a fee as set forth in paragraph (2) of subdivision (b) of Section 12191 of the Government Code for each registration made under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 1000, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2000.)
