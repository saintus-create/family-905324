# CORP § 12256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Organization and Bylaws > Article: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 12256

---

## Text

A central organization is a corporation whose membership is composed, in whole or in part, of other corporations organized under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 792, Sec. 7.)
