# CORP § 15910.04

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. PARTNERSHIPS > Chapter: 4.5. Uniform Limited Partnership Act of 2008 > Article: 10. Actions by Partners

**Citation:** CORP § 15910.04

---

## Text

In a derivative action, the complaint must state with particularity:

(1) the date and content of plaintiff’s demand and the general partners’ response to the demand; or

(2) why demand is excused as futile.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 495, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Section operative January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 15912.04.)
