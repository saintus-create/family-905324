# CORP § 8410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. NONPROFIT CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 14. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 8410

---

## Text

Service of process upon a corporation shall be governed by Chapter 17 (commencing with Section 1700) of Division 1 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 567.)
