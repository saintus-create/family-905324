# CORP § 25163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions

**Citation:** CORP § 25163

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this law, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
