# CORP § 2502.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2502.03

---

## Text

A social purpose corporation may be sued in the same manner as a corporation as provided in the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 17. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
