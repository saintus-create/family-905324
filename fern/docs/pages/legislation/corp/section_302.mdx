# CORP § 302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Directors and Management

**Citation:** CORP § 302

---

## Text

The board may declare vacant the office of a director who has been declared of unsound mind by an order of court or convicted of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
