# CORP § 21305

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 4. Insignia

**Citation:** CORP § 21305

---

## Text

Upon the registration, the Secretary of State shall issue his certificate setting forth the fact of the registration.
