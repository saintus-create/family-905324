# CORP § 29513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. COMMODITIES > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 29513

---

## Text

“Offer” includes every offer to sell, offer to purchase, or offer to enter into a commodity contract or commodity option.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 969, Sec. 2.)
