# CORP § 2291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 22.5. Victims of Corporate Fraud Compensation Fund

**Citation:** CORP § 2291

---

## Text

Any sums received by the Secretary of State pursuant to any provisions of this chapter shall be deposited in the State Treasury and credited to the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 564, Sec. 4. (SB 1058) Effective January 1, 2013.)
