# CORP § 2510.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2510.1

---

## Text

“Social purpose corporation subject to the Insurance Code as an insurer” means a social purpose corporation that has met the requirements of Sections 201.5, 201.6, and 201.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 27. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
