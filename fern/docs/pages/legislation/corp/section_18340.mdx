# CORP § 18340

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 6. Governance > Article: 4. Amendment of Governing Documents

**Citation:** CORP § 18340

---

## Text

If an unincorporated association’s governing principles do not provide a procedure to amend the association’s governing documents, the governing documents may be amended by a vote of the members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 116, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2006.)
