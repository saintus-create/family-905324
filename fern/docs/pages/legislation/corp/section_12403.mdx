# CORP § 12403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 3. Members > Article: 1. Issuance of Memberships

**Citation:** CORP § 12403

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), or in its articles or bylaws, a corporation may admit any person to membership.

(b) A corporation may not admit its subsidiary to membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
