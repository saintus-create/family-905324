# CORP § 12678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 18. Crimes and Penalties

**Citation:** CORP § 12678

---

## Text

Any person may be enjoined from violating the provisions of Section 12311. Any corporation may be enjoined from carrying on business outside of the purpose for which it was formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1625, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1984.)
