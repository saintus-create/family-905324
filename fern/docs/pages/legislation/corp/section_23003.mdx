# CORP § 23003

**Hierarchy:** Part: 4. REAL ESTATE INVESTMENT TRUSTS > Title: 3. UNINCORPORATED ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Death Benefit Payments by Fraternal Societies

**Citation:** CORP § 23003

---

## Text

A real estate investment trust shall not issue any security redeemable at the option of the holder of the security.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 650.)
