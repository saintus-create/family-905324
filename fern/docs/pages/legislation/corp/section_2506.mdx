# CORP § 2506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. SOCIAL PURPOSE CORPORATIONS ACT > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** CORP § 2506

---

## Text

“Disappearing social purpose corporation” means a constituent social purpose corporation that is not the surviving entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 694, Sec. 23. (SB 1301) Effective January 1, 2015.)
