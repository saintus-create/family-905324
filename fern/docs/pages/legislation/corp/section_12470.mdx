# CORP § 12470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORPORATIONS FOR SPECIFIC PURPOSES > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 5. Meetings and Voting > Article: 2. Additional Provisions Relating to Election of Directors

**Citation:** CORP § 12470

---

## Text

As to directors elected by members, there shall be available to the members reasonable nomination and election procedures given the nature, size and operations of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 589, Sec. 52. Effective January 1, 1997.)
