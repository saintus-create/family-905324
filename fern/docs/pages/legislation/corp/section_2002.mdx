# CORP § 2002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL CORPORATION LAW > Title: 1. CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 20. General Provisions Relating to Dissolution

**Citation:** CORP § 2002

---

## Text

A vacancy on the board may be filled during a winding up proceeding in the manner provided in Section 305.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 682.)
