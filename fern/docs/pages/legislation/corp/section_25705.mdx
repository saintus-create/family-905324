# CORP § 25705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CORPORATE SECURITIES LAW OF 1968 > Part: 8. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. SECURITIES > Chapter: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** CORP § 25705

---

## Text

All references to “the Corporate Securities Law” in any code or statute of the State of California shall hereafter be deemed to be references to the Corporate Securities Law of 1968.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 88.)
