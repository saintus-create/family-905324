# EVID § 660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 4. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Proof

**Citation:** EVID § 660

---

## Text

The presumptions established by this article, and all other rebuttable presumptions established by law that fall within the criteria of Section 605, are presumptions affecting the burden of proof.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
