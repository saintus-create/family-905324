# EVID § 981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 5. Privilege for Confidential Marital Communications

**Citation:** EVID § 981

---

## Text

There is no privilege under this article if the communication was made, in whole or in part, to enable or aid anyone to commit or plan to commit a crime or a fraud.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
