# EVID § 1252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. HEARSAY EVIDENCE > Chapter: 2. Exceptions to the Hearsay Rule > Article: 5. Statements of Mental or Physical State

**Citation:** EVID § 1252

---

## Text

Evidence of a statement is inadmissible under this article if the statement was made under circumstances such as to indicate its lack of trustworthiness.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
