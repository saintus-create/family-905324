# EVID § 522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 1. Burden of Proof > Article: 2. Burden of Proof on Specific Issues

**Citation:** EVID § 522

---

## Text

The party claiming that any person, including himself, is or was insane has the burden of proof on that issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
