# EVID § 761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 5. Method and Scope of Examination > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** EVID § 761

---

## Text

“Cross-examination” is the examination of a witness by a party other than the direct examiner upon a matter that is within the scope of the direct examination of the witness.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
