# EVID § 762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 5. Method and Scope of Examination > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** EVID § 762

---

## Text

“Redirect examination” is an examination of a witness by the direct examiner subsequent to the cross-examination of the witness.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
