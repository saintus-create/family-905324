# EVID § 987

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 5. Privilege for Confidential Marital Communications

**Citation:** EVID § 987

---

## Text

There is no privilege under this article in a criminal proceeding in which the communication is offered in evidence by a defendant who is one of the spouses between whom the communication was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
