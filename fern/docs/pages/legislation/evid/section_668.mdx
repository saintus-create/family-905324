# EVID § 668

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 4. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Proof

**Citation:** EVID § 668

---

## Text

An unlawful intent is presumed from the doing of an unlawful act. This presumption is inapplicable in a criminal action to establish the specific intent of the defendant where specific intent is an element of the crime charged.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
