# EVID § 1035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8.5. Sexual Assault Counselor-Victim Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 1035

---

## Text

As used in this article, “victim” means a person who consults a sexual assault counselor for the purpose of securing advice or assistance concerning a mental, physical, or emotional condition caused by a sexual assault.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 689, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
