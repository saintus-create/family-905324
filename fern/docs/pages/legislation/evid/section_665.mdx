# EVID § 665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 4. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Proof

**Citation:** EVID § 665

---

## Text

A person is presumed to intend the ordinary consequences of his voluntary act. This presumption is inapplicable in a criminal action to establish the specific intent of the defendant where specific intent is an element of the crime charged.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
