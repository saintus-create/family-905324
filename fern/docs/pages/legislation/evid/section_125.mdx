# EVID § 125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 125

---

## Text

“Conduct” includes all active and passive behavior, both verbal and nonverbal.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
