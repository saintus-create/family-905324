# EVID § 1034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8. Clergy Penitent Privileges

**Citation:** EVID § 1034

---

## Text

Subject to Section 912, a member of the clergy, whether or not a party, has a privilege to refuse to disclose a penitential communication if he or she claims the privilege.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 806, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2003.)
