# EVID § 970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 4. Privilege Not to Testify Against Spouse

**Citation:** EVID § 970

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, a married person has a privilege not to testify against his spouse in any proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
