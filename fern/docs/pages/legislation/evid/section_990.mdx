# EVID § 990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 6. Physician-Patient Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 990

---

## Text

As used in this article, “physician” means a person authorized, or reasonably believed by the patient to be authorized, to practice medicine in any state or nation.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
