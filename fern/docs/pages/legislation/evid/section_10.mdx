# EVID § 10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND CONSTRUCTION

**Citation:** EVID § 10

---

## Text

The singular number includes the plural; and the plural, the singular.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
