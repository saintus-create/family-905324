# EVID § 180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 180

---

## Text

“Personal property” includes money, goods, chattels, things in action, and evidences of debt.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
