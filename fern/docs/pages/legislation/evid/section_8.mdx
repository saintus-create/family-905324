# EVID § 8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND CONSTRUCTION

**Citation:** EVID § 8

---

## Text

The present tense includes the past and future tenses; and the future, the present.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
