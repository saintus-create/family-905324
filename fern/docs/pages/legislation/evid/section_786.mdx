# EVID § 786

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 6. Credibility of Witnesses > Article: 2. Attacking or Supporting Credibility

**Citation:** EVID § 786

---

## Text

Evidence of traits of his character other than honesty or veracity, or their opposites, is inadmissible to attack or support the credibility of a witness.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
