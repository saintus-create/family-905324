# EVID § 145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 145

---

## Text

“The hearing” means the hearing at which a question under this code arises, and not some earlier or later hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
