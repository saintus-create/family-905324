# EVID § 967

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 3.5. Lawyer Referral Service-Client Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 967

---

## Text

A lawyer referral service that has received or made a communication subject to the privilege under this article shall claim the privilege if the communication is sought to be disclosed and the client has not consented to the disclosure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 123, Sec. 2. (AB 267) Effective January 1, 2014.)
