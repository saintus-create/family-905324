# EVID § 766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 5. Method and Scope of Examination > Article: 2. Examination of Witnesses

**Citation:** EVID § 766

---

## Text

A witness must give responsive answers to questions, and answers that are not responsive shall be stricken on motion of any party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
