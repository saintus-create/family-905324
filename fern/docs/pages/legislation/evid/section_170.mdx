# EVID § 170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 170

---

## Text

“Perceive” means to acquire knowledge through one’s senses.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
