# EVID § 630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 3. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Producing Evidence

**Citation:** EVID § 630

---

## Text

The presumptions established by this article, and all other rebuttable presumptions established by law that fall within the criteria of Section 603, are presumptions affecting the burden of producing evidence.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
