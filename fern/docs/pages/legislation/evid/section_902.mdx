# EVID § 902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** EVID § 902

---

## Text

“Civil proceeding” means any proceeding except a criminal proceeding.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
