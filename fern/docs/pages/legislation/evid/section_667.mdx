# EVID § 667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 4. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Proof

**Citation:** EVID § 667

---

## Text

A person not heard from in five years is presumed to be dead.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 201, Sec. 1.)
