# EVID § 642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 3. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Producing Evidence

**Citation:** EVID § 642

---

## Text

A trustee or other person, whose duty it was to convey real property to a particular person, is presumed to have actually conveyed to him when such presumption is necessary to perfect title of such person or his successor in interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
