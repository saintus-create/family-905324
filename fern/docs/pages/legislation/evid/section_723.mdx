# EVID § 723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 3. Expert Witnesses > Article: 1. Expert Witnesses Generally

**Citation:** EVID § 723

---

## Text

The court may, at any time before or during the trial of an action, limit the number of expert witnesses to be called by any party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
