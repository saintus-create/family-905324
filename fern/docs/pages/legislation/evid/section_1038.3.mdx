# EVID § 1038.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8.8. Human Trafficking Caseworker-Victim Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 1038.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as limiting any obligation to report instances of child abuse as required by Section 11166 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 197, Sec. 4. (AB 1735) Effective January 1, 2020.)
