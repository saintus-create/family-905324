# EVID § 1420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. WRITINGS > Chapter: 1. Authentication and Proof of Writings > Article: 2. Means of Authenticating and Proving Writings

**Citation:** EVID § 1420

---

## Text

A writing may be authenticated by evidence that the writing was received in response to a communication sent to the person who is claimed by the proponent of the evidence to be the author of the writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
