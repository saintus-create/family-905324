# EVID § 450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. JUDICIAL NOTICE

**Citation:** EVID § 450

---

## Text

Judicial notice may not be taken of any matter unless authorized or required by law.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
