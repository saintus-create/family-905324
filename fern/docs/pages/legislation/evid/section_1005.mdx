# EVID § 1005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 6. Physician-Patient Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 1005

---

## Text

There is no privilege under this article in a proceeding brought by or on behalf of the patient to establish his competence.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
