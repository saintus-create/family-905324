# EVID § 195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 195

---

## Text

“Public employee” means an officer, agent, or employee of a public entity.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
