# EVID § 1037.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8.7. Domestic Violence Counselor-Victim Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 1037.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit any obligation to report instances of child abuse as required by Section 11166 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 854, Sec. 1.)
