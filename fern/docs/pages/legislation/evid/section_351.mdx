# EVID § 351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Admitting and Excluding Evidence > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EVID § 351

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, all relevant evidence is admissible.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
