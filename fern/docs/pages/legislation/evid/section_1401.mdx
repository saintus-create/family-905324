# EVID § 1401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. WRITINGS > Chapter: 1. Authentication and Proof of Writings > Article: 1. Requirement of Authentication

**Citation:** EVID § 1401

---

## Text

(a) Authentication of a writing is required before it may be received in evidence.

(b) Authentication of a writing is required before secondary evidence of its content may be received in evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
