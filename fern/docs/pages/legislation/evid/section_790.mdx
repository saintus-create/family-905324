# EVID § 790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 6. Credibility of Witnesses > Article: 2. Attacking or Supporting Credibility

**Citation:** EVID § 790

---

## Text

Evidence of the good character of a witness is inadmissible to support his credibility unless evidence of his bad character has been admitted for the purpose of attacking his credibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
