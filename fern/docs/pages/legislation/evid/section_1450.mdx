# EVID § 1450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. WRITINGS > Chapter: 1. Authentication and Proof of Writings > Article: 3. Presumptions Affecting Acknowledged Writings and Official Writings

**Citation:** EVID § 1450

---

## Text

The presumptions established by this article are presumptions affecting the burden of producing evidence.
