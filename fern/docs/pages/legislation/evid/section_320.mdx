# EVID § 320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Order of Proof

**Citation:** EVID § 320

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the court in its discretion shall regulate the order of proof.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
