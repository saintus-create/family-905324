# EVID § 1520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. WRITINGS > Chapter: 2. Secondary Evidence of Writings > Article: 1. Proof of the Content of a Writing

**Citation:** EVID § 1520

---

## Text

The content of a writing may be proved by an otherwise admissible original.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 100, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
