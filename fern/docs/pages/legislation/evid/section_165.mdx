# EVID § 165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 165

---

## Text

“Oath” includes affirmation or declaration under penalty of perjury.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
