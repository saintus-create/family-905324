# EVID § 1105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. EVIDENCE AFFECTED OR EXCLUDED BY EXTRINSIC POLICIES > Chapter: 1. Evidence of Character, Habit, or Custom

**Citation:** EVID § 1105

---

## Text

Any otherwise admissible evidence of habit or custom is admissible to prove conduct on a specified occasion in conformity with the habit or custom.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
