# EVID § 606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** EVID § 606

---

## Text

The effect of a presumption affecting the burden of proof is to impose upon the party against whom it operates the burden of proof as to the nonexistence of the presumed fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
