# EVID § 1235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. HEARSAY EVIDENCE > Chapter: 2. Exceptions to the Hearsay Rule > Article: 3. Prior Statements of Witnesses

**Citation:** EVID § 1235

---

## Text

Evidence of a statement made by a witness is not made inadmissible by the hearsay rule if the statement is inconsistent with his testimony at the hearing and is offered in compliance with Section 770.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
