# EVID § 1205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. HEARSAY EVIDENCE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** EVID § 1205

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to repeal by implication any other statute relating to hearsay evidence.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
