# EVID § 622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 2. Conclusive Presumptions

**Citation:** EVID § 622

---

## Text

The facts recited in a written instrument are conclusively presumed to be true as between the parties thereto, or their successors in interest; but this rule does not apply to the recital of a consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
