# EVID § 1231.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. HEARSAY EVIDENCE > Chapter: 2. Exceptions to the Hearsay Rule > Article: 2.5. Sworn Statements Regarding Gang-Related Crimes

**Citation:** EVID § 1231.4

---

## Text

If evidence of a prior statement is introduced pursuant to this article, the jury may not be told that the declarant died from other than natural causes, but shall merely be told that the declarant is unavailable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 499, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
