# EVID § 920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 3. General Provisions Relating to Privileges

**Citation:** EVID § 920

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to repeal by implication any other statute relating to privileges.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
