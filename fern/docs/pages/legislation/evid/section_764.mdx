# EVID § 764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 5. Method and Scope of Examination > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** EVID § 764

---

## Text

A “leading question” is a question that suggests to the witness the answer that the examining party desires.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
