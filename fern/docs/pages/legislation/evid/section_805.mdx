# EVID § 805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. OPINION TESTIMONY AND SCIENTIFIC EVIDENCE > Chapter: 1. Expert and Other Opinion Testimony > Article: 1. Expert and Other Opinion Testimony Generally

**Citation:** EVID § 805

---

## Text

Testimony in the form of an opinion that is otherwise admissible is not objectionable because it embraces the ultimate issue to be decided by the trier of fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
