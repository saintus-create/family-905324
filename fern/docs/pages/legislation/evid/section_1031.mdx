# EVID § 1031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8. Clergy Penitent Privileges

**Citation:** EVID § 1031

---

## Text

As used in this article, “penitent” means a person who has made a penitential communication to a member of the clergy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 806, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2003.)
