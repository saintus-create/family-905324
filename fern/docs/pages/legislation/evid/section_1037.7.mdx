# EVID § 1037.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8.7. Domestic Violence Counselor-Victim Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 1037.7

---

## Text

As used in this article, “domestic violence” means “domestic violence” as defined in Section 6211 of the Family Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 77.4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
