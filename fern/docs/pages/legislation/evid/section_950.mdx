# EVID § 950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 3. Lawyer-Client Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 950

---

## Text

As used in this article, “lawyer” means a person authorized, or reasonably believed by the client to be authorized, to practice law in any state or nation.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
