# EVID § 750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 4. Interpreters and Translators

**Citation:** EVID § 750

---

## Text

A person who serves as an interpreter or translator in any action is subject to all the rules of law relating to witnesses.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
