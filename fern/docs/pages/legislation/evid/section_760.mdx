# EVID § 760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 5. Method and Scope of Examination > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** EVID § 760

---

## Text

“Direct examination” is the first examination of a witness upon a matter that is not within the scope of a previous examination of the witness.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
