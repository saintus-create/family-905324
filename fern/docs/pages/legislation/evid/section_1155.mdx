# EVID § 1155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. EVIDENCE AFFECTED OR EXCLUDED BY EXTRINSIC POLICIES > Chapter: 3. Other Evidence Affected or Excluded by Extrinsic Policies

**Citation:** EVID § 1155

---

## Text

Evidence that a person was, at the time a harm was suffered by another, insured wholly or partially against loss arising from liability for that harm is inadmissible to prove negligence or other wrongdoing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
