# EVID § 1411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. WRITINGS > Chapter: 1. Authentication and Proof of Writings > Article: 2. Means of Authenticating and Proving Writings

**Citation:** EVID § 1411

---

## Text

Except as provided by statute, the testimony of a subscribing witness is not required to authenticate a writing.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
