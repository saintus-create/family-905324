# EVID § 785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 6. Credibility of Witnesses > Article: 2. Attacking or Supporting Credibility

**Citation:** EVID § 785

---

## Text

The credibility of a witness may be attacked or supported by any party, including the party calling him.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
