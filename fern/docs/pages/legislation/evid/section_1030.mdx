# EVID § 1030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8. Clergy Penitent Privileges

**Citation:** EVID § 1030

---

## Text

As used in this article, a “member of the clergy” means a priest, minister, religious practitioner, or similar functionary of a church or of a religious denomination or religious organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 806, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2003.)
