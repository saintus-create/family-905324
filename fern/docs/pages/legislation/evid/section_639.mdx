# EVID § 639

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. BURDEN OF PROOF; BURDEN OF PRODUCING EVIDENCE; PRESUMPTIONS AND INFERENCES > Chapter: 3. Presumptions and Inferences > Article: 3. Presumptions Affecting the Burden of Producing Evidence

**Citation:** EVID § 639

---

## Text

A judgment, when not conclusive, is presumed to correctly determine or set forth the rights of the parties, but there is no presumption that the facts essential to the judgment have been correctly determined.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
