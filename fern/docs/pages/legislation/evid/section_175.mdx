# EVID § 175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** EVID § 175

---

## Text

“Person” includes a natural person, firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, corporation, limited liability company, or public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 103. Effective January 1, 1995.)
