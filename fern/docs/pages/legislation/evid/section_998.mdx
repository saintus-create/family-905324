# EVID § 998

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 6. Physician-Patient Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 998

---

## Text

There is no privilege under this article in a criminal proceeding.
