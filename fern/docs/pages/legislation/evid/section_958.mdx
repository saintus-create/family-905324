# EVID § 958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 3. Lawyer-Client Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 958

---

## Text

There is no privilege under this article as to a communication relevant to an issue of breach, by the lawyer or by the client, of a duty arising out of the lawyer-client relationship.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
