# EVID § 789

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITNESSES > Chapter: 6. Credibility of Witnesses > Article: 2. Attacking or Supporting Credibility

**Citation:** EVID § 789

---

## Text

Evidence of his religious belief or lack thereof is inadmissible to attack or support the credibility of a witness.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
