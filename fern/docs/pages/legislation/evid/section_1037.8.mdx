# EVID § 1037.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. PRIVILEGES > Chapter: 4. Particular Privileges > Article: 8.7. Domestic Violence Counselor-Victim Privilege

**Citation:** EVID § 1037.8

---

## Text

A domestic violence counselor shall inform a domestic violence victim of any applicable limitations on confidentiality of communications between the victim and the domestic violence counselor. This information may be given orally.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 629, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
