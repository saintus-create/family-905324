# VEH § 4014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 1. Vehicles Subject to Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4014

---

## Text

Any portable or collapsible dolly carried in a tow truck or in a truck used by an automobile dismantler and used upon a highway exclusively for towing disabled vehicles is exempt from registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 924, Sec. 3.)
