# VEH § 9858.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. REGISTRATION AND TRANSFER OF VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 9858.1

---

## Text

Any documentary preparation charge by an authorized agent of the department shall not exceed twenty dollars ($20).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1407, Sec. 2.)
