# VEH § 13359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 2. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses > Article: 3. Suspension and Revocation by Department

**Citation:** VEH § 13359

---

## Text

The department may suspend or revoke the privilege of any person to operate a motor vehicle upon any of the grounds which authorize the refusal to issue a license.
