# VEH § 38110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 4. Renewal of Identification

**Citation:** VEH § 38110

---

## Text

Certificates of ownership shall not be renewed but shall remain valid until suspended, revoked, or canceled by the department for cause or upon transfer of any interest shown therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1816.)
