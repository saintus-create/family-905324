# VEH § 28206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Connected Vehicle Service > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 28206

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to vehicles owned or operated by a rental company, as defined in Section 1939.01 of the Civil Code.
