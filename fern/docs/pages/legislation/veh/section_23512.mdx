# VEH § 23512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. SENTENCING FOR DRIVING WHILE UNDER THE INFLUENCE > Chapter: 1. Court-Imposed Penalties: Persons Less Than 21 Years Of Age > Article: 3. Youthful Drunk Driver Visitation Program

**Citation:** VEH § 23512

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “program” means the Youthful Drunk Driver Visitation Program prescribed in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 118, Sec. 84. Effective January 1, 1999. Section operative July 1, 1999, pursuant to Section 23675.)
