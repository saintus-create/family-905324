# VEH § 3085.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 6. Export and Sale-for-Resale Prohibition Hearings

**Citation:** VEH § 3085.10

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed, unless a later enacted statute, that is enacted before January 1, 2030, deletes or extends that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 796, Sec. 17. (AB 179) Effective January 1, 2020. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6, commencing with Section 3085.)
