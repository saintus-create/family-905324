# VEH § 40808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 3. Illegal Evidence > Article: 1. Prosecutions Under Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40808

---

## Text

Subdivision (d) of Section 28 of Article I of the California Constitution shall not be construed as abrogating the evidentiary provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 538, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
