# VEH § 9268

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 2. Registration Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9268

---

## Text

In addition to any other registration fee and notwithstanding Section 9559, an additional fee of one dollar ($1) shall be collected upon the original registration of a motorcycle pursuant to Section 4150.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 887.)
