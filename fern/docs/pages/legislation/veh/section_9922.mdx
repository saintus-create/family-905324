# VEH § 9922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. REGISTRATION AND TRANSFER OF VESSELS > Chapter: 3. Transfer of Title or Interest in Undocumented Vessel

**Citation:** VEH § 9922

---

## Text

The method provided in this chapter for perfecting a security interest in a vessel numbered under this code is exclusive.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1428.)
