# VEH § 3050.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Board

**Citation:** VEH § 3050.6

---

## Text

The board or its executive director may, in the event of a granting of a continuance of a scheduled matter, assess costs of the board upon the party receiving the continuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 451, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2004.)
