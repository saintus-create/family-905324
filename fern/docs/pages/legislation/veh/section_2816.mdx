# VEH § 2816

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 1. Lawful Orders and Inspections

**Citation:** VEH § 2816

---

## Text

It is unlawful to load or discharge children onto or from a youth bus upon a highway at any location where the children must cross the highway upon which the youth bus is stopped, unless traffic is controlled by a traffic officer or an official traffic control signal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 383, Sec. 4. Effective July 4, 1982. Operative on date (not sooner than October 1, 1982, or later than January 1, 1983) prescribed by Sec. 13 of Ch. 383.)
