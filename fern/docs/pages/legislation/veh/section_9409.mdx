# VEH § 9409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 3. Weight Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9409

---

## Text

Any forklift truck which is designed primarily for loading and unloading and for stacking materials and is operated or drawn along a highway unladen is exempt from the provisions of Section 9400.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1395.)
