# VEH § 2406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 2406

---

## Text

The commissioner may provide that any highway patrol vehicle shall be equipped with a stretcher and emergency first aid equipment for use in transporting injured persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
