# VEH § 38185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 7. Dismantling of Off-Highway Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 38185

---

## Text

No off-highway motor vehicle subject to identification which has been reported dismantled or sold as salvage may be subsequently identified until it has been inspected by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1816.)
