# VEH § 626.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 626.2

---

## Text

A “traffic violator school branch or classroom location” is any place where a traffic violator school conducts instruction or maintains records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 396, Sec. 2. Effective July 30, 1985.)
