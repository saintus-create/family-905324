# VEH § 36109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 2. Registration of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** VEH § 36109

---

## Text

“Farm trailers,” as defined in Section 36010, having a gross weight of 10,000 pounds or less, are exempt from registration except that Section 5014 shall apply to such trailers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 861, Sec. 57. Effective September 29, 2000. Operative December 31, 2001, pursuant to Sec. 67 of Ch. 861.)
