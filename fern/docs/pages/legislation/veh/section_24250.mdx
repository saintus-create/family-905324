# VEH § 24250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 24250

---

## Text

During darkness, a vehicle shall be equipped with lighted lighting equipment as required for the vehicle by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
