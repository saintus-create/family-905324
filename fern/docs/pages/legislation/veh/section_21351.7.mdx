# VEH § 21351.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 2. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings > Article: 1. Erection and Maintenance

**Citation:** VEH § 21351.7

---

## Text

Local authorities in their respective jurisdictions may place and maintain, or cause to be placed and maintained, appropriate signs along city streets or county roads which indicate that a deaf child is near.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 719, Sec. 1.)
