# VEH § 6852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 4. Permits to Nonresident Owners > Article: 2. Foreign Commercial Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 6852

---

## Text

The nonresident owner of a foreign vehicle shall be granted such privileges and freedom from registration and payment of fees imposed by this code, or Part 5 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code, commencing at Section 10701, as the foreign jurisdiction in which the foreign commercial vehicle is registered or licensed grants to like vehicles registered under this code or as provided in agreements, arrangements, or declarations made under Article 3, commencing at Section 8000.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
