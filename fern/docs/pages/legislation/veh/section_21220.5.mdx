**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 5. Operation of Motorized Scooters

## Text

For the purposes of this article, a motorized scooter is defined in Section 407.5.
