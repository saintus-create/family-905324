# VEH § 9551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 4. Payment of Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9551

---

## Text

All fees required to be paid by an automobile dismantler, in accordance with this code, for any license, or special plates, shall be paid at the time application is made to the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1214.)
