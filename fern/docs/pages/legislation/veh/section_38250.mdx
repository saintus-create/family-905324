# VEH § 38250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 9. Identification Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 38250

---

## Text

Whenever any person has received as transferee a properly endorsed certificate of ownership and the transfer fee has not been paid as required by this division within 10 days, the fee is delinquent.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1268, Sec. 23.)
