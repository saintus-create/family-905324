# VEH § 9982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. VEHICLE SALES > Chapter: 3. Engine Manufacturers

**Citation:** VEH § 9982

---

## Text

This chapter applies only to new passenger vehicles and to new motortrucks with an unladen weight under 6,000 pounds, except housecars.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
