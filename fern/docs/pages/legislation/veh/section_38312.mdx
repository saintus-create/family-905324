# VEH § 38312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Off-Highway Vehicle Operating Rules > Article: 4. Turning and Starting

**Citation:** VEH § 38312

---

## Text

No person shall place in motion an off-highway motor vehicle that is stopped, standing, or parked until such movement can be made with reasonable safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
