# VEH § 36705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 7. Other Requirements

**Citation:** VEH § 36705

---

## Text

No automatic bale wagon exceeding 96 inches in width or carrying a load in excess of 100 inches in width may be operated on any highway during darkness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 627.)
