# VEH § 2401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 2401

---

## Text

The commissioner shall make adequate provision for patrol of the highways at all times of the day and night.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
