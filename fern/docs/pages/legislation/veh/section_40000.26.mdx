# VEH § 40000.26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 1. Violation of Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40000.26

---

## Text

A violation of subdivision (g) of Section 34501.12 or subdivision (d) of Section 34501.14, relating to inspections, is a misdemeanor and not an infraction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1243, Sec. 107.1. Effective September 30, 1992. Operative January 1, 1993, by Sec. 117 of Ch. 1243.)
