# VEH § 8200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 4. Permits to Nonresident Owners > Article: 6. Enforcement of Liens on Apportioned Fleet Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 8200

---

## Text

“Registrant,” for purposes of this article, means any person issued apportioned fleet registration pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with Section 8050).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 124, Sec. 120. Effective January 1, 1997.)
