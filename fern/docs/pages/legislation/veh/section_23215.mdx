# VEH § 23215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 12. Public Offenses > Article: 2. Offenses Involving Alcohol and Drugs

**Citation:** VEH § 23215

---

## Text

The department may, but shall not be required to, provide patrol or enforce the provisions of Section 23152 for offenses which occur other than upon a highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 940, Sec. 32.)
