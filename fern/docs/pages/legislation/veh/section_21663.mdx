# VEH § 21663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 3. Driving, Overtaking, and Passing > Article: 1. Driving on Right Side

**Citation:** VEH § 21663

---

## Text

Except as expressly permitted pursuant to this code, including Sections 21100.4 and 21114.5, no person shall operate or move a motor vehicle upon a sidewalk except as may be necessary to enter or leave adjacent property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 124, Sec. 126. Effective January 1, 1997.)
