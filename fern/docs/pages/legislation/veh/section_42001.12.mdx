# VEH § 42001.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 1. Penalties > Article: 1. Public Offenses

**Citation:** VEH § 42001.12

---

## Text

Every person convicted of an infraction for a violation of Section 21806 shall be punished as follows:

(a) For a first conviction, by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

(b) For a second conviction within one year, by a fine of not less than one hundred fifty dollars ($150) nor more than five hundred dollars ($500).

(c) For a third or any subsequent conviction within three years, by a fine of not less than two hundred fifty dollars ($250) nor more than five hundred dollars ($500).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 13, Sec. 67. Effective February 13, 1991.)
