# VEH § 2474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 5. Renderers and Transporters of Inedible Kitchen Grease

**Citation:** VEH § 2474

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to steal, misappropriate, contaminate, or damage inedible kitchen grease, or containers thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 394, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
