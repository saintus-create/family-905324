# VEH § 12810.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 3. Issuance and Renewal of Licenses

**Citation:** VEH § 12810.2

---

## Text

(a) Notwithstanding subdivision (f) of Section 12810, a violation point count shall not be given for a conviction of a violation of Section 27315, 27318, or 27319.

(b) The amendments to this section as added by the act that added this subdivision shall be operative on July 1, 2018.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 593, Sec. 1.5. (SB 20) Effective January 1, 2018. Amended version operative July 1, 2018, by Stats. 2017, Ch. 593, Sec. 5, and as restated in its own provisions.)
