# VEH § 22108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 6. Turning and Stopping and Turning Signals

**Citation:** VEH § 22108

---

## Text

Any signal of intention to turn right or left shall be given continuously during the last 100 feet traveled by the vehicle before turning.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
