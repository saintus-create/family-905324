# VEH § 23675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. SENTENCING FOR DRIVING WHILE UNDER THE INFLUENCE > Chapter: 4. Procedures > Article: 11. Operative Date

**Citation:** VEH § 23675

---

## Text

This division shall become operative on July 1, 1999.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 118, Sec. 84. Effective January 1, 1999. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 1999) for Division 11.5, commencing with Section 23500.)
