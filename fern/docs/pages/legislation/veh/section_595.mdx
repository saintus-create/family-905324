# VEH § 595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 595

---

## Text

“Terminal” is a place where a vehicle of a type listed in Section 34500 is regularly garaged or maintained, or from which the vehicle is operated or dispatched.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 832, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
