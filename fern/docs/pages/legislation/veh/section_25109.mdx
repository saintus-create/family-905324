# VEH § 25109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 6. Side and Fender Lighting Equipment

**Citation:** VEH § 25109

---

## Text

Any motor vehicle may be equipped with two white or amber running lamps mounted on the front, one at each side, which shall not be lighted during darkness except while the motor vehicle is parked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 858.)
