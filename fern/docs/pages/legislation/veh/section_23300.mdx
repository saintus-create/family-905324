# VEH § 23300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 13. Vehicular Crossings and Toll Highways > Article: 3. Tolls and Other Charges

**Citation:** VEH § 23300

---

## Text

The Department of Transportation shall erect appropriate signs at each entrance to a vehicular crossing to notify traffic that it is entering upon a vehicular crossing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 545.)
