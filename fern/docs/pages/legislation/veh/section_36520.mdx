# VEH § 36520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 5. Equipment of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** VEH § 36520

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 26706 relating to windshield wipers shall not apply to implements of husbandry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2149.)
