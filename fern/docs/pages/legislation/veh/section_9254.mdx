# VEH § 9254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 2. Registration Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9254

---

## Text

A service fee of fifteen dollars ($15) shall be paid to the department for a certificate of ownership issued without registration of the vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 719, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2004.)
