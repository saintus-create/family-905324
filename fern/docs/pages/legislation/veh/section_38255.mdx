# VEH § 38255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 9. Identification Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 38255

---

## Text

Upon application for transfer of ownership or any interest of an owner, or legal owner in or to any off-highway motor vehicle identified under this division, there shall be paid the following fees: (a) For a transfer by the owner $15(b) For a transfer by the legal owner $15(c) When application is presented showing a transfer by both the owner and legal owner $15

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 719, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2004.)
