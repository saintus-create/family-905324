# VEH § 40200.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 3. Procedure on Parking Violations

**Citation:** VEH § 40200.1

---

## Text

A person shall not be subject to both a notice of parking violation and a notice to appear for the same violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. (AB 144) Effective January 1, 2010.)
