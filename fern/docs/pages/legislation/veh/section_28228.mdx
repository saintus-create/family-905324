# VEH § 28228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Connected Vehicle Service > Article: 2. Access to Connected Vehicle Service

**Citation:** VEH § 28228

---

## Text

The termination of connected vehicle service pursuant to this article shall not be deemed to impact legal ownership of a vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 655, Sec. 2. (SB 1394) Effective January 1, 2025.)
