# VEH § 410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 410

---

## Text

A “motor truck” or “motortruck” is a motor vehicle designed, used, or maintained primarily for the transportation of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 272, Sec. 11. Effective August 2, 1993.)
