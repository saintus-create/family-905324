# VEH § 8056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 4. Permits to Nonresident Owners > Article: 4. Apportioned Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 8056

---

## Text

Any application filed pursuant to this article which contains vehicles powered by diesel fuel shall include information concerning any diesel fuel tax permit issued by the Board of Equalization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 533, Sec. 8.)
