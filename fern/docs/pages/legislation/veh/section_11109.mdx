# VEH § 11109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Driving Schools and Driving Instructors

**Citation:** VEH § 11109

---

## Text

Every licensee under this chapter shall maintain all vehicles used in driver training in safe mechanical condition at all times.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
