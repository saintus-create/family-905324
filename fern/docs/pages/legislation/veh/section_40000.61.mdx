# VEH § 40000.61

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 1. Violation of Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40000.61

---

## Text

A violation of Section 1808.45, relating to unauthorized disclosure of department records, is a misdemeanor, and not an infraction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1213, Sec. 18.)
