# VEH § 11704.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1. Issuance of Licenses and Certificates to Manufacturers, Transporters, and Dealers

**Citation:** VEH § 11704.7

---

## Text

Every person who applies to the department to take or retake the examination required under Section 11704.5 shall pay to the department a fee of sixteen dollars ($16).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1008, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1997.)
