# VEH § 8053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 4. Permits to Nonresident Owners > Article: 4. Apportioned Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 8053

---

## Text

Provisions of this code which specify and govern application filing, fee assignment, penalty assessment, and issuance of license plates and registration certificates, shall be applicable to vehicles registered pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 533, Sec. 8.)
