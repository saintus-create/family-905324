# VEH § 530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 530

---

## Text

A “roadway” is that portion of a highway improved, designed, or ordinarily used for vehicular travel.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
