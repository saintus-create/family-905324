# VEH § 27453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 4. Tires

**Citation:** VEH § 27453

---

## Text

There shall not be an average difference greater than 18 inch between the outside diameters of each single tire composing a dual solid rubber tire.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
