# VEH § 11755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1.1. Consumer Automotive Recall Safety Act

**Citation:** VEH § 11755

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 1633.3 of the Civil Code and Section 9975 of this code, a new motor vehicle dealer may receive electronic authorization from consumers consistent with regulations adopted by the Bureau of Automotive Repair for any repair of a manufacturer recall.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 490, Sec. 2. (AB 596) Effective January 1, 2020.)
