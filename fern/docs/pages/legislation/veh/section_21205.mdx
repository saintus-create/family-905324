# VEH § 21205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 4. Operation of Bicycles

**Citation:** VEH § 21205

---

## Text

No person operating a bicycle shall carry any package, bundle or article which prevents the operator from keeping at least one hand upon the handlebars.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 479.)
