# VEH § 11503.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Automobile Dismantlers

**Citation:** VEH § 11503.1

---

## Text

Any of the causes specified in this chapter as a cause to suspend or revoke the license issued to an automobile dismantler is cause to refuse to issue a license to an automobile dismantler.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1563, Sec. 28.)
