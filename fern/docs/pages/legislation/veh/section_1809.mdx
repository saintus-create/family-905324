# VEH § 1809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. The Department of Motor Vehicles > Article: 3. Records of Department

**Citation:** VEH § 1809

---

## Text

The department may prepare and disseminate information relating to prevention of traffic accidents.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
