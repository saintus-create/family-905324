# VEH § 3079

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 5. Hearings on Recreational Vehicle Franchise Modification, Replacement, Termination, Refusal to Continue, Establishment, and Relocation, and Consumer Complaints

**Citation:** VEH § 3079

---

## Text

This article applies only to a recreational vehicle franchise entered into or renewed on or after January 1, 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 407, Sec. 12. (AB 759) Effective January 1, 2016.)
