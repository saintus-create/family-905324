# VEH § 11604.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Lessor-Retailers

**Citation:** VEH § 11604.1

---

## Text

Any cause specified in this chapter as a cause to suspend or revoke the license issued to a lessor-retailer is a cause to refuse to issue a license to a lessor-retailer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1563, Sec. 33.)
