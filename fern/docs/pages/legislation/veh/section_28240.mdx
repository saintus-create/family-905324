# VEH § 28240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Connected Vehicle Service > Article: 3. Disabling Connected Vehicle Location Access

**Citation:** VEH § 28240

---

## Text

If a vehicle includes connected vehicle location access, a covered provider shall provide a mechanism that can be used by a driver who is inside a vehicle to immediately disable connected vehicle location access.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 655, Sec. 2. (SB 1394) Effective January 1, 2025.)
