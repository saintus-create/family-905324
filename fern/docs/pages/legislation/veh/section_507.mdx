# VEH § 507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 507

---

## Text

The “relevant market area” is any area within a radius of 10 miles from the site of a potential new dealership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 996.)
