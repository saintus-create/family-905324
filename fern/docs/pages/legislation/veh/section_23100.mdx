# VEH § 23100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 12. Public Offenses > Article: 1. Driving Offenses

**Citation:** VEH § 23100

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply to vehicles upon the highways and elsewhere throughout the State unless expressly provided otherwise.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
