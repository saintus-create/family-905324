# VEH § 21709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 3. Driving, Overtaking, and Passing > Article: 2. Additional Driving Rules

**Citation:** VEH § 21709

---

## Text

No vehicle shall at any time be driven through or within a safety zone.
