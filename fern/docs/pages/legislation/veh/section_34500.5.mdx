# VEH § 34500.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.8. SAFETY REGULATIONS

**Citation:** VEH § 34500.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, the term “commercial motor vehicle” has the same meaning as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 15210, or any vehicle listed in Section 34500.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 451, Sec. 58. (SB 491) Effective January 1, 2016.)
