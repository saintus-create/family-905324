# VEH § 436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 436

---

## Text

An “off-highway motorcycle” means a motorcycle or motor-driven cycle which is subject to identification under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1584, Sec. 5.)
