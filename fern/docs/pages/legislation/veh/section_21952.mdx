# VEH § 21952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 5. Pedestrians’ Rights and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 21952

---

## Text

The driver of any motor vehicle, prior to driving over or upon any sidewalk, shall yield the right-of-way to any pedestrian approaching thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
