# VEH § 25452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 11. Acetylene Lamps

**Citation:** VEH § 25452

---

## Text

No acetylene lamp shall emit any glaring light.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
