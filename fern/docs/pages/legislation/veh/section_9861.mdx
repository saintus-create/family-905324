# VEH § 9861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. REGISTRATION AND TRANSFER OF VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 9861

---

## Text

All certificates of number expire on December 31 of every odd-numbered year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 473, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
