# VEH § 34686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.86. PRIVATE CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS REGISTRATION ACT

**Citation:** VEH § 34686

---

## Text

The State of California and its agencies and political subdivisions are exempt from the registration requirements of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 40. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018. Operative July 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 34693.)
