# VEH § 38346

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Equipment of Off-Highway Vehicles > Article: 2. Lighting Equipment

**Citation:** VEH § 38346

---

## Text

A person shall not display a flashing or steady burning red or blue warning light on an off-highway motor vehicle except as permitted by Section 21055 or when an extreme hazard exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 908, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 2005.)
