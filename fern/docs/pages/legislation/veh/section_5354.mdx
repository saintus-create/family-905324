# VEH § 5354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 10. Registration of Trailer Coaches

**Citation:** VEH § 5354

---

## Text

The registration of a foreign trailer coach owned by a nonresident shall be subject to and governed by Section 6700.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1021.)
