# VEH § 27152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 2. Exhaust Systems

**Citation:** VEH § 27152

---

## Text

The exhaust gases from a motor vehicle shall not be directed to the side of the vehicle between 2 feet and 11 feet above the ground.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
