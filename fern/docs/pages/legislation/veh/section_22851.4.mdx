# VEH § 22851.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 10. Removal of Parked and Abandoned Vehicles > Article: 2. Vehicle Disposition

**Citation:** VEH § 22851.4

---

## Text

If the vehicle is determined to have a value exceeding five hundred dollars ($500) pursuant to Section 22670, the lien shall be satisfied pursuant to Sections 3067 to 3074, inclusive, of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 650, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2005.)
