# VEH § 38325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Equipment of Off-Highway Vehicles > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 38325

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to all off-highway motor vehicles, as defined in Section 38006, when operated in areas in which this division has application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
