# VEH § 2525.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Licenses Issued by California Highway Patrol > Article: 3.5. Inspection and Maintenance Stations

**Citation:** VEH § 2525.8

---

## Text

The commissioner shall make and enforce regulations with respect to the issuance of stickers to be displayed upon vehicles owned or operated by a fleet owner which has complied with Section 2525.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1067.)
