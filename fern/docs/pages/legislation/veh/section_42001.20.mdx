# VEH § 42001.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 1. Penalties > Article: 1. Public Offenses

**Citation:** VEH § 42001.20

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a person who violates subdivision (b) or (c) of Section 27000 is punishable as follows:

(a) By a fine of one hundred fifty dollars ($150).

(b) For a second infraction occurring within one year of a prior infraction that resulted in a conviction, a fine not exceeding two hundred dollars ($200).

(c) For a third or any subsequent infraction occurring within one year of two or more prior infractions that resulted in convictions, a fine, not exceeding two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 166, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
