# VEH § 3068

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 4. Hearings on Franchise Modification, Replacement, Termination, Refusal to Continue, Delivery and Preparation Obligations, and Warranty Reimbursement

**Citation:** VEH § 3068

---

## Text

Either party may seek judicial review of final decisions of the board. Time for filing for the review shall not be more than 45 days from the date on which the final order of the board is made public and is delivered to the parties personally or is sent to them by certified mail.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 407, Sec. 5. (AB 759) Effective January 1, 2016.)
