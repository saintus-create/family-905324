# VEH § 626.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 626.8

---

## Text

A “traffic violator school owner” is any natural person, association, or corporation that owns a traffic violator school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 396, Sec. 5. Effective July 30, 1985.)
