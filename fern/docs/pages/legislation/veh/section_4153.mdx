# VEH § 4153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 2. Original Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4153

---

## Text

If the vehicle to be registered is a specially constructed or remanufactured vehicle, the application shall also state that fact and contain additional information as may reasonably be required by the department to enable it properly to register the vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1286, Sec. 18.)
