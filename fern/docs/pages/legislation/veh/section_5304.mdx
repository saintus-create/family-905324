# VEH § 5304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 9.5. Registration of Fleet Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 5304

---

## Text

Renewal fees shall be paid pursuant to a schedule established by the department. Submission of renewal fees by an association pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 5301 shall be specific as to each fleet and vehicle thereof covered by the renewal application.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 986, Sec. 2.)
