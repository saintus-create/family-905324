# VEH § 40007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 1. Violation of Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40007

---

## Text

Division 5 (commencing with Section 11100) does not preclude the application of any other statutory provision which is applicable to any act violating any provision of Division 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 93, Sec. 1.)
