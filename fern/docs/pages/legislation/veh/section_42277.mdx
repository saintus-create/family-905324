# VEH § 42277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 3. Motor Vehicle Account

**Citation:** VEH § 42277

---

## Text

No money in the Motor Vehicle Account in the State Transportation Fund may be expended for automobile driver training.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 94, Sec. 1.)
