# VEH § 38022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 1. Motor Vehicles Subject to Identification

**Citation:** VEH § 38022

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 4000, motorcycles issued a special transportation identification device pursuant to Section 38088 may be transported upon a highway to and from a closed course.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1050.)
