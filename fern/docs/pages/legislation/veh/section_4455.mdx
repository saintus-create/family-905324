# VEH § 4455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 4. Evidences of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4455

## Text

Any permit issued under Section 4004 shall be carried in the vehicle for which issued at all times while it is being operated in this State.
