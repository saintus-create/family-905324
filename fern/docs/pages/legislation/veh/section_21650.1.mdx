# VEH § 21650.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 3. Driving, Overtaking, and Passing > Article: 1. Driving on Right Side

**Citation:** VEH § 21650.1

---

## Text

A bicycle operated on a roadway, or the shoulder of a highway, shall be operated in the same direction as vehicles are required to be driven upon the roadway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 58, Sec. 2.)
