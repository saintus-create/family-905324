# VEH § 5162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 8.6. Specialized License Plates

**Citation:** VEH § 5162

---

## Text

The State Department of Public Health shall apply to the department to sponsor a kidney disease awareness license plate program pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 359, Sec. 1. (AB 2450) Effective January 1, 2015.)
