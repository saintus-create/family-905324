# VEH § 20017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCIDENTS AND ACCIDENT REPORTS > Chapter: 1. Accidents and Accident Reports

**Citation:** VEH § 20017

---

## Text

Any peace officer who knows, or has reasonable cause to believe, that a pesticide has been spilled or otherwise accidentally released, shall report the spill as required in Section 105215 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 425. Effective September 29, 1996.)
