# VEH § 102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 102

---

## Text

“Ability to respond in damages” means financial responsibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 95 by Stats. 1992, Ch. 974, Sec. 2. Effective September 28, 1992.)
