# VEH § 2500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Licenses Issued by California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 2500

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to all licenses issued by the Department of the California Highway Patrol unless the particular provisions applicable to each license otherwise provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1309.)
