# VEH § 35600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SIZE, WEIGHT, AND LOAD > Chapter: 5. Weight > Article: 2. Tire Limits

**Citation:** VEH § 35600

---

## Text

The gross weight upon a solid tire upon a vehicle shall not exceed 600 pounds upon any inch of the channel base width of such tire.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
