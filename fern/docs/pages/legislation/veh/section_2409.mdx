# VEH § 2409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 2409

---

## Text

All members of the California Highway Patrol have the powers of a peace officer as provided in Section 830.2 of the Penal Code.
