# VEH § 385.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 385.3

---

## Text

A “logging vehicle” is a vehicle used exclusively in the conduct of logging operations and not designed for the transportation of persons or property on a highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 523, Sec. 21. (SB 788) Effective January 1, 2014.)
