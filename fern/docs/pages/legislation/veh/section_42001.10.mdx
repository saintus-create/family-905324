# VEH § 42001.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 1. Penalties > Article: 1. Public Offenses

**Citation:** VEH § 42001.10

---

## Text

Every person convicted for a violation of Section 38020 shall be punished by a fine of not less than fifty dollars ($50) for a first offense, and not more than two hundred fifty dollars ($250) for every subsequent offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 42001.9 (as amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1027) by Stats. 1988, Ch. 160, Sec. 184.)
