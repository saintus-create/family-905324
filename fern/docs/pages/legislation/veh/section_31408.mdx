# VEH § 31408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. TOWING AND LOADING EQUIPMENT > Chapter: 5. Transporting Other Loads > Article: 2. Vehicles Transporting Workmen

**Citation:** VEH § 31408

---

## Text

No person may operate a farm labor vehicle on a highway unless both headlamps required under Section 24400 are lighted, regardless of the time of day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 556, Sec. 5. Effective September 29, 1999.)
