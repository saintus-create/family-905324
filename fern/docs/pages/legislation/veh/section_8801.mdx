# VEH § 8801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 5. Offenses Against Registration Laws and Suspension, Revocation, and Cancellation of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 8801

---

## Text

The department may suspend, cancel, revoke, or renew any permanent registration made under Section 4155 when the department determines that it is advisable to reissue the registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
