# VEH § 26704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 4. Windshields and Mirrors

**Citation:** VEH § 26704

---

## Text

Wherever the term “safety glazing material” is used in this article, it means safety glazing material of a type meeting requirements established by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 723.)
