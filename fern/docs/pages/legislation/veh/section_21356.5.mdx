# VEH § 21356.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 2. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings > Article: 1. Erection and Maintenance

**Citation:** VEH § 21356.5

---

## Text

Local authorities may place signs, mirrors, or other visual or audible devices at exits from alleys that are under their jurisdiction to warn drivers to watch for pedestrians and bicyclists on the sidewalk prior to exiting the alley.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 150, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
