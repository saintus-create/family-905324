# VEH § 12810.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 3. Issuance and Renewal of Licenses

**Citation:** VEH § 12810.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no violation point shall be given for a conviction of a violation of Section 22526.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 647, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
