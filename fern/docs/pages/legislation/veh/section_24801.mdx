# VEH § 24801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 4. Parking Lamps

**Citation:** VEH § 24801

---

## Text

Parking lamps are those lamps permitted by Section 25106, or any lamps mounted on the front of a vehicle, designed to be displayed primarily when the vehicle is parked.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
