# VEH § 21957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 5. Pedestrians’ Rights and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 21957

---

## Text

No person shall stand in a roadway for the purpose of soliciting a ride from the driver of any vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
