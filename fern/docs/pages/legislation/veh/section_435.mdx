# VEH § 435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 435

---

## Text

“Nonresident” is a person who is not a resident of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
