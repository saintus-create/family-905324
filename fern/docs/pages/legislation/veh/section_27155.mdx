# VEH § 27155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 2. Exhaust Systems

**Citation:** VEH § 27155

---

## Text

No motor vehicle shall be operated or parked upon any highway unless the filling spout for the fuel tank is closed by a cap or cover of noncombustible material.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 453.)
