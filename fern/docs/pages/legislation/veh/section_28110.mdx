# VEH § 28110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 16. Methanol or Ethanol Fueled Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 28110

---

## Text

As used in this article, “antisiphoning device” means a device which prevents the removal by suction of fuel from a motor vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1301, Sec. 1.)
