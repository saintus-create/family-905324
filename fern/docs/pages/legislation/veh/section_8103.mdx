# VEH § 8103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 4. Permits to Nonresident Owners > Article: 5. Federal Motor Vehicle Safety Program

**Citation:** VEH § 8103

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this code, a hearing shall not be provided when the suspension is based solely on notification by a federal agency that interstate operation is prohibited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 169, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
