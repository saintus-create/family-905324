# VEH § 35000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SIZE, WEIGHT, AND LOAD > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 35000

---

## Text

The provisions of this division refer exclusively to the size and weight of, and loads upon, vehicles when operated upon the highways.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
