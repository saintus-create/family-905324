# VEH § 29005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. TOWING AND LOADING EQUIPMENT > Chapter: 1. Towing Equipment

**Citation:** VEH § 29005

---

## Text

When one vehicle is towing another, the drawbar or other connection shall not exceed 15 feet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 58.)
