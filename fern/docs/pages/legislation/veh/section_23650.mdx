# VEH § 23650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. SENTENCING FOR DRIVING WHILE UNDER THE INFLUENCE > Chapter: 4. Procedures > Article: 6. Alcohol Assessment

**Citation:** VEH § 23650

---

## Text

The Office of Traffic Safety shall adopt rules and guidelines to implement Sections 23646 to 23649, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 22, Sec. 43. Effective May 26, 1999. Operative July 1, 1999, by Sec. 46 of Ch. 22.)
