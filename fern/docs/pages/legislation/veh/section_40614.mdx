# VEH § 40614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 2. Procedure on Arrests > Article: 4. Notice to Correct Violation

**Citation:** VEH § 40614

---

## Text

Any person who signs a notice to correct or a certificate of correction with a false or fictitious name is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1350.)
