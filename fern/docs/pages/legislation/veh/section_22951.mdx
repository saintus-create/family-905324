# VEH § 22951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 11. Parking Lots

**Citation:** VEH § 22951

---

## Text

No operator of any offstreet parking facility shall park the vehicle of a patron of the facility in any street or alley.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 22518 by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1041.)
