# VEH § 21250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 5.5. Operation of Low-Speed Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 21250

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, a low-speed vehicle means a vehicle as defined in Section 385.5. A “low-speed vehicle” is also known as a “neighborhood electric vehicle.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 422, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
