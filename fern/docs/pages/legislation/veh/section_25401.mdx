# VEH § 25401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 10. Diffused Lights

**Citation:** VEH § 25401

---

## Text

No diffused nonglaring light on a vehicle shall resemble any official traffic control device.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
