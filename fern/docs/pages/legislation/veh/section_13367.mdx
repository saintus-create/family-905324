# VEH § 13367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 2. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses > Article: 3. Suspension and Revocation by Department

**Citation:** VEH § 13367

---

## Text

For purposes of the suspension or revocation of any driver’s license issued to a minor, the department shall not provide any lighter penalty than would be given to an adult under similar circumstances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 562.)
