# VEH § 17457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability of Owners and Operators of Vehicles > Article: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** VEH § 17457

---

## Text

The court in which the action is pending may order such continuances as may be necessary to afford the defendant reasonable opportunity to defend the action.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
