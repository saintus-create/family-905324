# VEH § 15003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 6. Driver License Compact > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** VEH § 15003

---

## Text

As used in the compact, with reference to this State, the term “executive head” shall mean the Governor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 237.)
