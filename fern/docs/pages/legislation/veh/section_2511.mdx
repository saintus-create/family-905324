# VEH § 2511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Licenses Issued by California Highway Patrol > Article: 2. Privately Owned and Operated Ambulances and Armored Cars

**Citation:** VEH § 2511

---

## Text

Licenses for the operation of ambulances may be issued only to those persons or entities which operate ambulances designed and operated exclusively as such and which are used to respond to emergency calls.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1309.)
