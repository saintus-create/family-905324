# VEH § 40248

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 3.6. Procedure on Photographic Imaging of Parking Violations Occurring in Bicycle Lanes

**Citation:** VEH § 40248

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 432, Sec. 1. (AB 361) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Repeal affects Article 3.6, commencing with Section 40245.)
