# VEH § 23250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 13. Vehicular Crossings and Toll Highways > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 23250

---

## Text

All of the provisions of this code not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter shall be applicable to vehicular crossings and toll highways. This chapter shall control over any provision of this code inconsistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1154, Sec. 72. Effective September 30, 1996.)
