# VEH § 626.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 626.4

---

## Text

A “traffic violator school instructor” is any person who provides instruction to traffic violators on behalf of a traffic violator school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 396, Sec. 3. Effective July 30, 1985.)
