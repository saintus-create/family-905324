# VEH § 1812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. The Department of Motor Vehicles > Article: 3. Records of Department

**Citation:** VEH § 1812

---

## Text

The department shall not charge for copies of records or for information from its records given to any county, city, any transit operator as defined in Section 99210 of the Public Utilities Code, state department, or the United States government.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 726, Sec. 4. Operative July 1, 1988, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 726.)
