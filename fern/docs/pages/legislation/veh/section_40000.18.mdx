# VEH § 40000.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 1. Violation of Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40000.18

---

## Text

A violation of subdivision (b) of Section 31401 or Section 31402 or 31403, relating to farm labor vehicles, is a misdemeanor and not an infraction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 613, Sec. 2.)
