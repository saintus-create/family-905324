# VEH § 17303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability of Owners and Operators of Vehicles > Article: 3. Liability for Damage to Highway

**Citation:** VEH § 17303

---

## Text

Damages under Sections 17301 and 17302 may be recovered in a civil action brought by the authorities in control of the highway or bridge.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
