# VEH § 24401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 2. Headlamps and Auxiliary Lamps

**Citation:** VEH § 24401

---

## Text

Whenever any motor vehicle is parked or standing upon a highway any headlamp that is lighted shall be dimmed or on the lower beam.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
