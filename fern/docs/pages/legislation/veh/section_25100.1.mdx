# VEH § 25100.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 6. Side and Fender Lighting Equipment

**Citation:** VEH § 25100.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this code, an ambulance may be equipped with clearance and side-marker lamps.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 616.)
