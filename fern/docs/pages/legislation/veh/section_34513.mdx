# VEH § 34513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.8. SAFETY REGULATIONS

**Citation:** VEH § 34513

---

## Text

The department shall adopt rules and regulations relating to the equipment and maintenance of tour buses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1306, Sec. 19.)
