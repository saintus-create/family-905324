# VEH § 28102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 15. Pilot Cars

**Citation:** VEH § 28102

---

## Text

Pilot cars equipped with vertical clearance measuring devices shall comply with Section 35252.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 460, Sec. 4.)
