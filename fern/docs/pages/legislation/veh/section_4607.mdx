# VEH § 4607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 5. Renewal of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4607

---

## Text

The department, upon renewing a registration, shall issue a new registration card to the owner as upon an original registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
