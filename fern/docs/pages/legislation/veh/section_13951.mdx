# VEH § 13951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 3. Investigation and Hearing > Article: 2. Notice

**Citation:** VEH § 13951

---

## Text

Whenever the department proposes to refuse to issue or renew a driver’s license, it shall notify the applicant of such fact and give him an opportunity to be heard.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
