# VEH § 4000.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 1. Vehicles Subject to Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4000.5

---

## Text

(a) The department shall register an autoette, as defined in Section 175, as a motor vehicle.

(b) The owner of an autoette shall remove the license plates from the vehicle and return them to the department when the autoette is removed from a natural island, as described in Section 175.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 322, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
