# VEH § 38300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Off-Highway Vehicle Operating Rules > Article: 1. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings

**Citation:** VEH § 38300

---

## Text

It is unlawful for the driver of any vehicle to disobey any sign, signal, or traffic control device placed or maintained pursuant to Section 38280.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
