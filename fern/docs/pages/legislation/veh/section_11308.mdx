# VEH § 11308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Vehicle Verifiers

**Citation:** VEH § 11308

---

## Text

The department may adopt rules and regulations concerning the issuance, use, and renewal of a vehicle verifier’s permit, and for determining the competence of an applicant therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 700.)
