# VEH § 11740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1. Issuance of Licenses and Certificates to Manufacturers, Transporters, and Dealers

**Citation:** VEH § 11740

---

## Text

The remedies and penalties provided in this code for a violation of this article are cumulative to the remedies and penalties provided by other laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 407, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
