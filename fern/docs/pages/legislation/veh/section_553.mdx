# VEH § 553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 553

---

## Text

“Shade trailer” means a device designed and utilized to provide shade pursuant to Section 3395 of Title 8 of the California Code of Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 168, Sec. 2. (AB 2111) Effective January 1, 2013.)
