# VEH § 21212.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 4. Operation of Bicycles

**Citation:** VEH § 21212.5

---

## Text

(a) A person shall not sell a class 3 electric bicycle to a person under 16 years of age.

(b) A violation of this section is an infraction and shall be punishable by a fine not to exceed two hundred and fifty dollars ($250).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 65, Sec. 1. (AB 965) Effective January 1, 2026.)
