# VEH § 11713.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1. Issuance of Licenses and Certificates to Manufacturers, Transporters, and Dealers

**Citation:** VEH § 11713.10

---

## Text

It is unlawful and a violation of this code to sell a low-speed vehicle, as defined in Section 385.5, without disclosing to the buyer the vehicle’s maximum speed and the potential risks of driving a low-speed vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 140, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
