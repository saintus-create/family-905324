# VEH § 42031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 1. Penalties > Article: 2. Weight Violations

**Citation:** VEH § 42031

---

## Text

Whenever the gross weight and any axle or wheel weight of a vehicle are in excess of the limits prescribed in this code, the excess weights shall be deemed one offense in violation of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
