# VEH § 4308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 3. Registration of Foreign Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 4308

---

## Text

In the event the department refuses to grant an application for registration in this State of a vehicle previously registered in another state, the department shall immediately return to the applicant all documents submitted by the applicant with the application.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
