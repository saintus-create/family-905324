# VEH § 231.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 231.5

---

## Text

A “bicycle path” or “bike path” is a Class I bikeway, as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 890.4 of the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 200, Sec. 5. (SB 734) Effective January 1, 2010.)
