# VEH § 4750.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 6. Refusal of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4750.4

---

## Text

Information provided by an insurer to the department pursuant to Section 11580.10 of the Insurance Code and former Section 4750.2, as added by Chapter 946 of the Statutes of 1991, shall be made available only to law enforcement agencies for law enforcement purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 193, Sec. 194. Effective January 1, 2005.)
