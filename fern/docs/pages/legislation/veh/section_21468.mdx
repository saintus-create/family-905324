# VEH § 21468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 2. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings > Article: 3. Offenses Relating to Traffic Devices

**Citation:** VEH § 21468

---

## Text

This division does not modify or limit the authority of the Public Utilities Commission to erect or maintain, or cause to be erected and maintained, signs, signals or other traffic control devices as authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
