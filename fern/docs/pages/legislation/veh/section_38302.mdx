# VEH § 38302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Off-Highway Vehicle Operating Rules > Article: 1. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings

**Citation:** VEH § 38302

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to place or erect any sign, signal, or traffic control device for off-highway traffic upon public lands unless authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
