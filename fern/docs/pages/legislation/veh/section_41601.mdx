# VEH § 41601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 7. Arrest Quotas

**Citation:** VEH § 41601

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “citation” means a notice to appear, notice of violation, or notice of parking violation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1111.)
