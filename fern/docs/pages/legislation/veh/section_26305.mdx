# VEH § 26305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 3. Brakes > Article: 1. Brake Requirements

**Citation:** VEH § 26305

---

## Text

Any auxiliary dolly or tow dolly may be equipped with brakes.
