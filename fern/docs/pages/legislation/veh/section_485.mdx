# VEH § 485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 485

---

## Text

A “pneumatic tire” is a tire inflated or capable of inflation with compressed air.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
