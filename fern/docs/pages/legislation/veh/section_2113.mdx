# VEH § 2113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 2113

---

## Text

The chief administrative officer shall carry out and execute such duties with respect to the administrative affairs of the department as may be specified by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
