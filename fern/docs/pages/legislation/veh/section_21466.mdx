# VEH § 21466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 2. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings > Article: 3. Offenses Relating to Traffic Devices

**Citation:** VEH § 21466

---

## Text

No person shall place or maintain or display upon or in view of any highway any light in such position as to prevent the driver of a vehicle from readily recognizing any official traffic control device.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 968.)
