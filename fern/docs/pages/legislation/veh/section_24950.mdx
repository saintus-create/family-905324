# VEH § 24950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 5. Signal Lamps and Devices

**Citation:** VEH § 24950

---

## Text

Whenever any motor vehicle is towing a trailer coach or a camp trailer the combination of vehicles shall be equipped with a lamp-type turn signal system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1536.)
