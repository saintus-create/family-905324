# VEH § 28210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Connected Vehicle Service > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 28210

---

## Text

A covered provider or any officer, director, employee, vendor, or agent of a covered provider shall not be subject to liability for any claims deriving from an action taken in compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 655, Sec. 2. (SB 1394) Effective January 1, 2025. Operative July 1, 2025, pursuant to Sec. 28206.)
