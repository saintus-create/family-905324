# VEH § 5204.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 9. Display of Plates, Tabs, and Stickers

**Citation:** VEH § 5204.5

---

## Text

The department shall post on its Internet Web site, and any other appropriate online venue used by the department for public outreach, detailed instructions for motorists that describe how to prevent theft of the tabs described in Section 5204.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 776, Sec. 3. (SB 773) Effective January 1, 2017.)
