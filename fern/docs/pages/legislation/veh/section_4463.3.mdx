# VEH § 4463.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 4. Evidences of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4463.3

---

## Text

In addition to, or instead of, any fine imposed for conviction of a violation of subdivision (b) or (c) of Section 4463, the court may impose a civil penalty of not more than two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500) for each conviction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 215, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
