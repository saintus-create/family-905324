# VEH § 34600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.85. MOTOR CARRIERS OF PROPERTY PERMIT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** VEH § 34600

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Motor Carriers of Property Permit Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1042, Sec. 53. Effective September 29, 1996.)
