# VEH § 9263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 2. Registration Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9263

---

## Text

Any automobile dismantler who fails to comply with Section 11520, or any other person who fails to comply with Section 5500 or 11520, shall pay an investigation service fee of fifteen dollars ($15).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1022, Sec. 6.)
