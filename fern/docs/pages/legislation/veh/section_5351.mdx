# VEH § 5351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 10. Registration of Trailer Coaches

**Citation:** VEH § 5351

---

## Text

Sections 4452, 4604, 5904, 6052, and 9254, and subdivision (a) of Section 9552 do not apply to the registration or renewal of registration of any trailer coach.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
