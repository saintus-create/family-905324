# VEH § 35400.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SIZE, WEIGHT, AND LOAD > Chapter: 4. Length

**Citation:** VEH § 35400.5

---

## Text

Subdivision (a) of Section 35400 does not apply to a motortruck used solely as a cotton module mover and which does not exceed 48 feet in length.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 270, Sec. 4. Effective July 3, 1984.)
