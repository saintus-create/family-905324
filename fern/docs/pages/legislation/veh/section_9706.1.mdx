# VEH § 9706.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 5. Partial Year Payment of Weight Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9706.1

---

## Text

If the registration card or potential registration card is unavailable, payment of the fee prescribed by Section 9265 for the issuance of a duplicate registration shall not be required in addition to the other fees prescribed by subdivision (b) of Section 9706.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 13, Sec. 22. Effective February 13, 1991.)
