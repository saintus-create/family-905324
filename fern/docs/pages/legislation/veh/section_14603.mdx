# VEH § 14603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 4. Violation of License Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 14603

---

## Text

No person shall operate a vehicle in violation of the provisions of a restricted license issued to him.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
