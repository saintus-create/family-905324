# VEH § 32100.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.3. TRANSPORTATION OF INHALATION HAZARDS

**Citation:** VEH § 32100.5

---

## Text

This division applies to the transportation of inhalation hazards in bulk packaging, except when that packaging contains only residue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1384, Sec. 5.)
