# VEH § 3072.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 5. Hearings on Recreational Vehicle Franchise Modification, Replacement, Termination, Refusal to Continue, Establishment, and Relocation, and Consumer Complaints

**Citation:** VEH § 3072.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, a “recreational vehicle line-make” is a group or groups of recreational vehicles defined by the terms of a written agreement that complies with Section 331.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 407, Sec. 8. (AB 759) Effective January 1, 2016.)
