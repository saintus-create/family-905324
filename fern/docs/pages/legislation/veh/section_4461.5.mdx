# VEH § 4461.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 4. Evidences of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4461.5

---

## Text

In addition to, or instead of, any fine imposed for conviction of a violation of subdivision (c) or (d) of Section 4461, the court may impose a civil penalty of not more than one thousand five hundred dollars ($1,500) for each conviction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 215, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
