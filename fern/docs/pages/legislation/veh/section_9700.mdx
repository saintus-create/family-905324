# VEH § 9700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 5. Partial Year Payment of Weight Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9700

---

## Text

With respect to vehicles subject to additional registration fees under Section 9400 or 9400.1, a proportionate share of the additional fees may be paid for any partial period of one month or more, but less than 12 months, in an amount determined to be one-twelfth of the annual registration times the consecutive months, or fraction thereof, of the period of registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 826, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 2002.)
