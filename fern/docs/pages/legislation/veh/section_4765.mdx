# VEH § 4765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 6. Refusal of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4765

---

## Text

No exemption from the payment of any fee imposed by this code is an exemption from the obligation of a registered owner or lessee to pay the full amount of parking penalties and administrative fees pursuant to Section 4760.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 939, Sec. 13.)
