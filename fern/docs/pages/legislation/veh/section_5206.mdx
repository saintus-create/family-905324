# VEH § 5206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 9. Display of Plates, Tabs, and Stickers

**Citation:** VEH § 5206

---

## Text

Vehicles for which weight fees are paid on a partial year basis shall display a certificate or insignia issued by the department, which shall state the end of the period for which the vehicle is licensed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 636, Sec. 4.)
