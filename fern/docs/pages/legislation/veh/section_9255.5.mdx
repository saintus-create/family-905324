# VEH § 9255.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 2. Registration Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9255.5

---

## Text

Upon application for transfer of registration pursuant to Section 5911, a fee as specified in Section 9265 shall be paid to the department in addition to the regular transfer fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 497.)
