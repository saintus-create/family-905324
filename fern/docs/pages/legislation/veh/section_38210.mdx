# VEH § 38210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 8. Transfers of Title or Interest

**Citation:** VEH § 38210

---

## Text

When the transferee of an off-highway motor vehicle subject to identification is a dealer who holds such vehicle for resale, the dealer is not required to make application for transfer, but upon transferring his title or interest to another person he shall comply with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1816.)
