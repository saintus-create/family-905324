# VEH § 13360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 2. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses > Article: 3. Suspension and Revocation by Department

**Citation:** VEH § 13360

---

## Text

Upon receiving satisfactory evidence of any violation of the restrictions of a driver’s license, the department may suspend or revoke the same.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
