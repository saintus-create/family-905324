# VEH § 21711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 3. Driving, Overtaking, and Passing > Article: 2. Additional Driving Rules

**Citation:** VEH § 21711

---

## Text

No person shall operate a train of vehicles when any vehicle being towed whips or swerves from side to side or fails to follow substantially in the path of the towing vehicle.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 44.)
