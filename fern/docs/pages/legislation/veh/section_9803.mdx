# VEH § 9803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 6. Enforcement of Liens

**Citation:** VEH § 9803

---

## Text

Nothing in this code prevents a review or other action as may be permitted by the Constitution and laws of this state by a court of competent jurisdiction of any order of the department to seize and sell a vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1212, Sec. 2.)
