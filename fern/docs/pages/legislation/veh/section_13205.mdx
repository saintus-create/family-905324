# VEH § 13205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 2. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses > Article: 2. Suspension or Revocation by Court

**Citation:** VEH § 13205

---

## Text

The privileges of a nonresident to operate vehicles in this State may be suspended or revoked under the provisions of this chapter in the same manner and to the same extent as the privileges of a resident driver.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
