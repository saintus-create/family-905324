# VEH § 5152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 8.6. Specialized License Plates

**Citation:** VEH § 5152

---

## Text

A person described in Section 5101 may apply for a specialized license plate under this article, in lieu of regular license plates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 454, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
