# VEH § 505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 505

---

## Text

A “registered owner” is a person registered by the department as the owner of a vehicle.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
