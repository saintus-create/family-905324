# VEH § 3000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 1. Organization of Board

**Citation:** VEH § 3000

---

## Text

There is in the Department of Motor Vehicles a New Motor Vehicle Board, which consists of nine members.
