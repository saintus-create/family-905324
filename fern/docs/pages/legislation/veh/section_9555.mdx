# VEH § 9555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 4. Payment of Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9555

---

## Text

Whenever any trailer coach is in this State without the registration fee having first been paid as required by this code, the fee is delinquent.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
