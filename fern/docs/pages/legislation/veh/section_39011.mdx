# VEH § 39011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.7. REGISTRATION AND LICENSING OF BICYCLES

**Citation:** VEH § 39011

---

## Text

No fine imposed for any violation of an ordinance or resolution, which is adopted pursuant to this division, shall exceed ten dollars ($10).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 400. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
