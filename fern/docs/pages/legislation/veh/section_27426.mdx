# VEH § 27426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 3.6. Charter-Party Carrier Safety Equipment

**Citation:** VEH § 27426

---

## Text

No later than July 1, 2018, the department shall adopt standards and criteria for the implementation of the safety equipment requirements specified in Section 27425.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 705, Sec. 2. (SB 247) Effective January 1, 2017.)
