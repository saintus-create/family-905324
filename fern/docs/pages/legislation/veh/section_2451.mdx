# VEH § 2451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 4. Highway Spill Containment and Abatement of Hazardous Substances

**Citation:** VEH § 2451

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that a statewide program for the management of hazardous substances highway spills, under the jurisdiction of the California Highway Patrol, is necessary to protect the public health and environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 922, Sec. 1.)
