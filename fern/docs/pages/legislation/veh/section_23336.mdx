# VEH § 23336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 13. Vehicular Crossings and Toll Highways > Article: 4. Special Traffic Regulations

**Citation:** VEH § 23336

---

## Text

It is unlawful to violate any rules or regulations adopted under Section 23334, notice of which has been given either by a sign on a vehicular crossing or by publication as provided in Section 23335.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
