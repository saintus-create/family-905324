# VEH § 16455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY LAWS > Chapter: 3. Proof of Financial Responsibility > Article: 2. Insurance Policy

**Citation:** VEH § 16455

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 16451 shall not apply to vehicles in storage if the current license plates and registration cards are surrendered to the department in Sacramento.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
