# VEH § 11750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1.1. Consumer Automotive Recall Safety Act

**Citation:** VEH § 11750

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Consumer Automotive Recall Safety Act (CARS Act).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 682, Sec. 4. (AB 287) Effective January 1, 2017.)
