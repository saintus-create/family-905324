# VEH § 9867

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. REGISTRATION AND TRANSFER OF VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 9867

---

## Text

A fee of fifteen dollars ($15) shall be charged for a duplicate of certificate of number, certificate of ownership, or current year registration stickers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 951, Sec. 4. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 951.)
