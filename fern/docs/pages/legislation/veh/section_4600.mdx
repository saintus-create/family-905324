# VEH § 4600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 5. Renewal of Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4600

---

## Text

Certificates of ownership shall not be renewed annually but shall remain valid until suspended, revoked, or canceled by the department for cause or upon a transfer of any interest shown therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
