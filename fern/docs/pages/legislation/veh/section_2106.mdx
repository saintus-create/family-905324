# VEH § 2106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 2106

---

## Text

The department shall maintain its main office within 20 miles of Sacramento.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 9, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
