# VEH § 36700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 7. Other Requirements

**Citation:** VEH § 36700

---

## Text

All implements of husbandry, farm trailers or any other vehicles subject to the provisions of this division, and any owner, operator or driver of such vehicles, shall also comply with and be subject to all other provisions of this code which are not in conflict with the specific provisions contained in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2149.)
