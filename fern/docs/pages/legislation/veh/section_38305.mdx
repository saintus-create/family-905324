# VEH § 38305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Off-Highway Vehicle Operating Rules > Article: 3. Speed Laws

**Citation:** VEH § 38305

---

## Text

No person shall drive an off-highway motor vehicle at a speed greater than is reasonable or prudent and in no event at a speed which endangers the safety of other persons or property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
