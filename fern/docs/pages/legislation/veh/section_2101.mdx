# VEH § 2101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 2101

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “department” means the Department of the California Highway Patrol.
