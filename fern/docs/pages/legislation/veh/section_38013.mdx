# VEH § 38013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 1. Motor Vehicles Subject to Identification

**Citation:** VEH § 38013

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided, the terms “identification” and “identification certificate” shall have the same meaning as the terms “registration” and “registration card,” respectively, as used in Division 3 (commencing with Section 4000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1816.)
