# VEH § 17462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability of Owners and Operators of Vehicles > Article: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** VEH § 17462

---

## Text

In the event of service outside the State, the person so served shall have 60 days in which to appear in the action in which the summons is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
