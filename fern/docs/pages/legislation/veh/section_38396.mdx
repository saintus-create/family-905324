# VEH § 38396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 6. Equipment of Off-Highway Vehicles > Article: 5. Emission Control Equipment

**Citation:** VEH § 38396

---

## Text

The provisions of this article apply to off-highway motor vehicles of the United States or its agencies, to the extent authorized by federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
