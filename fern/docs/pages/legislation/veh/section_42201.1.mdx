# VEH § 42201.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 2. Disposition of Fees, Fines, and Forfeitures > Article: 1. Fines and Forfeitures

**Citation:** VEH § 42201.1

---

## Text

Fines and forfeitures received by a county under Section 1463 of the Penal Code may be used to reimburse the state for the construction of platform scales and vehicle inspection facilities in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective July 30, 1985.)
