# VEH § 38304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Off-Highway Vehicle Operating Rules > Article: 2. Operating Controls

**Citation:** VEH § 38304

---

## Text

The operator of an off-highway motor vehicle shall be able to reach and operate all controls necessary to safely operate the vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1093.)
