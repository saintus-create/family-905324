# VEH § 5205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 9. Display of Plates, Tabs, and Stickers

**Citation:** VEH § 5205

---

## Text

The department may make appropriate rules and regulations for the use and display of stickers or devices issued in lieu of license plates, and shall publish a summary thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
