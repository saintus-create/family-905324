# VEH § 22521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 9. Stopping, Standing, and Parking

**Citation:** VEH § 22521

---

## Text

No person shall park a vehicle upon any railroad track or within 712 feet of the nearest rail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 625.)
