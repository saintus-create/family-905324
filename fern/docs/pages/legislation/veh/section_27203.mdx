# VEH § 27203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 2.5. Noise Limits

**Citation:** VEH § 27203

---

## Text

For the purposes of Section 27200, the noise limit of 82 dbA shall apply to any snowmobile manufactured after 1972.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 83.)
