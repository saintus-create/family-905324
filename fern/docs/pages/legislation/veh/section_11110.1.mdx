# VEH § 11110.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Driving Schools and Driving Instructors

**Citation:** VEH § 11110.1

---

## Text

Any of the causes specified in this chapter as a cause for refusal to issue a license under this chapter is cause to suspend or revoke a license under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1563, Sec. 12.)
