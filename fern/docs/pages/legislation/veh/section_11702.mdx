# VEH § 11702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1. Issuance of Licenses and Certificates to Manufacturers, Transporters, and Dealers

**Citation:** VEH § 11702

---

## Text

The department may issue, or for reasonable cause shown, refuse to issue a license to any applicant applying for a manufacturer's, manufacturer’s branch, remanufacturer's, remanufacturer’s branch, distributor's, distributor’s branch, transporter's, or dealer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1286, Sec. 26.)
