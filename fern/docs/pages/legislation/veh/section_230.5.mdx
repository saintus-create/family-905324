# VEH § 230.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 230.5

---

## Text

A “B-train assembly” is a rigid frame extension attached to the rear frame of a semitrailer which allows for a fifth wheel connection point for a second semitrailer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 13, Sec. 13. Effective February 13, 1991.)
