# VEH § 21710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 3. Driving, Overtaking, and Passing > Article: 2. Additional Driving Rules

**Citation:** VEH § 21710

---

## Text

The driver of a motor vehicle when traveling on down grade upon any highway shall not coast with the gears of such vehicle in neutral.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
