# VEH § 25650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 13. Headlamps on Motorcycles and Motor-Driven Cycles

**Citation:** VEH § 25650

---

## Text

Every motorcycle during darkness shall be equipped with at least one and not more than two lighted headlamps which shall conform to the requirements and limitations of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
