# VEH § 21252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 5.5. Operation of Low-Speed Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 21252

---

## Text

A vehicle dealer, selling a low-speed vehicle, shall provide to the buyer a disclosure statement regarding the operation of the vehicle that is in compliance with existing provisions of the California Code of Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 140, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2000.)
