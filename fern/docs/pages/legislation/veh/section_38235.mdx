# VEH § 38235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 9. Identification Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 38235

---

## Text

All money collected by the department under Section 38230 shall be reported monthly to the Controller and at the same time be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the Off-Highway License Fee Fund, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 970, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
