# VEH § 2111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 2111

---

## Text

The assistant commissioner shall carry out and execute such duties, with respect to traffic law enforcement, as may be specified by the commissioner.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
