# VEH § 27315.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 3. Safety Belts and Inflatable Restraint Systems

**Citation:** VEH § 27315.1

---

## Text

Section 27315 applies to any person in a fully enclosed three-wheeled motor vehicle that is not less than seven feet in length and not less than four feet in width, and has an unladen weight of 900 pounds or more.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 710, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
