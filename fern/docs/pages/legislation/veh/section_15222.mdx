# VEH § 15222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 7. Commercial Motor Vehicle Safety Program > Article: 3. Driver Notification Requirements

**Citation:** VEH § 15222

---

## Text

Any driver of a commercial motor vehicle who is convicted of any offense involving the safe operation of a motor vehicle shall notify his or her employer of the conviction within 30 days of the date of conviction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 208, Sec. 12. (AB 2906) Effective January 1, 2017.)
