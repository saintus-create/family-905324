# VEH § 11804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 2. Vehicle Salespersons

**Citation:** VEH § 11804

---

## Text

The department may issue or, for reasonable cause shown, refuse to issue, a license to any applicant applying for a vehicle salesperson’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1563, Sec. 51.)
