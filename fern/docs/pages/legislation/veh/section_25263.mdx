# VEH § 25263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 7. Flashing and Colored Lights

**Citation:** VEH § 25263

---

## Text

Trucks actually engaged in the towing of houses or buildings upon any highway may display flashing amber warning lights to the front, sides or rear on the vehicle or load.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 653.)
