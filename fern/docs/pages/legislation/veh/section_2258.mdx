# VEH § 2258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 2. The California Highway Patrol

**Citation:** VEH § 2258

---

## Text

Neither the commissioner nor any other person shall issue a badge to any person who is not a duly appointed member of the California Highway Patrol.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
