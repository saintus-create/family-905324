# VEH § 5104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 8.5. Environmental License Plates

**Citation:** VEH § 5104

---

## Text

Environmental license plates shall be issued only to the registered owner or lessee of the vehicle on which they are to be displayed.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 265.)
