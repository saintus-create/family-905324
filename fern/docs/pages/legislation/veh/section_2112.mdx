# VEH § 2112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 2112

---

## Text

The chief administrative officer of the department shall be appointed by the commissioner subject to the approval of the Governor, pursuant to the provisions of Article XXIV of the State Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 268, Sec. 32.5. Effective June 30, 1984.)
