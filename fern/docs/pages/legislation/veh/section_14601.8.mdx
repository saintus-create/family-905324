# VEH § 14601.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 4. Violation of License Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 14601.8

---

## Text

The judge may, in his or her discretion, allow any person convicted of a violation of Section 14601 or 14601.1 to serve his or her sentence on a sufficient number of consecutive weekend days to complete the sentence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 14601.5 by Stats. 1992, Ch. 982, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
