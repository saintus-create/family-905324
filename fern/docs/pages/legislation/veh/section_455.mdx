# VEH § 455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 455

---

## Text

“Original driver’s license” means the first driver’s license issued a person under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
