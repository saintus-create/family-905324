# VEH § 9258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 2. Registration Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9258

---

## Text

A fee of fifteen dollars ($15) shall be paid to the department for each one-trip permit issued pursuant to Section 4003.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 719, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2004.)
