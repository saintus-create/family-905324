# VEH § 5151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 8.6. Specialized License Plates

**Citation:** VEH § 5151

---

## Text

(a) As used in this article, “state agency” means a state office, officer, department, division, bureau, board, or commission, or any other state body or agency.

(b) It is the intent of the Legislature that this article contain the authority for specialized license plates for state agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 454, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
