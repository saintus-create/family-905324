# VEH § 12217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 11. Consumer Recovery Fund

**Citation:** VEH § 12217

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is intended to limit or restrict actions, remedies, penalties, or procedures otherwise available pursuant to any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 437, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2008.)
