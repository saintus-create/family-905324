# VEH § 35001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SIZE, WEIGHT, AND LOAD > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 35001

---

## Text

The provisions of this division, except those requiring a permit for overweight loads, do not apply to motor trucks equipped with snow removal devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
