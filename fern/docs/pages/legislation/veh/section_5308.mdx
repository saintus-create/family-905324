# VEH § 5308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 9.5. Registration of Fleet Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 5308

---

## Text

The director shall adopt procedures for initial application, payment of fees, fleet additions or deletions, and for the cancellation of the distinguishing plates or decals and registration card issued to a vehicle of a fleet owner or lessee who does not comply with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 695, Sec. 2.)
