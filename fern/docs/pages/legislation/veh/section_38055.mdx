# VEH § 38055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 2. Original Identification

**Citation:** VEH § 38055

---

## Text

In the event the vehicle is no longer identified in this state and the currently valid certificate of ownership is surrendered to the department, the bond or undertaking shall be returned and surrendered at the end of three years or prior thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 517, Sec. 402.)
