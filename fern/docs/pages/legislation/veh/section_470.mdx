# VEH § 470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 470

---

## Text

“Person” includes a natural person, firm, copartnership, association, limited liability company, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 225. Effective January 1, 1995.)
