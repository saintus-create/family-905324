# VEH § 21363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 2. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings > Article: 1. Erection and Maintenance

**Citation:** VEH § 21363

---

## Text

Detour signs shall be erected at the nearest points of detour from that portion of a highway, or from any bridge, which is closed to traffic while under construction or repair.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
