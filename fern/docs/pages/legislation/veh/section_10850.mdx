# VEH § 10850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. SPECIAL ANTITHEFT LAWS > Chapter: 4. Theft and Injury of Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 10850

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply to vehicles upon the highways and elsewhere throughout the State.
