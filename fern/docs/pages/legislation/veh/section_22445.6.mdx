# VEH § 22445.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 7. Speed Laws > Article: 5. State Highway Work Zone Speed Safety Program

**Citation:** VEH § 22445.6

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2032, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 684, Sec. 3. (AB 289) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 5, commencing with Section 22445.)
