# VEH § 1656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. The Department of Motor Vehicles > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 1656

---

## Text

The department shall publish on its internet website a synopsis or summary of the laws regulating the operation of vehicles and the use of the highways.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 82, Sec. 12. (SB 169) Effective July 13, 2026.)
