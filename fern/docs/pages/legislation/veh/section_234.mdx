# VEH § 234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 234

---

## Text

A “business” includes a proprietorship, partnership, corporation, and any other form of commercial enterprise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1563, Sec. 1.)
