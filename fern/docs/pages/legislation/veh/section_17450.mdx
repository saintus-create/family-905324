# VEH § 17450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability of Owners and Operators of Vehicles > Article: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** VEH § 17450

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “nonresident” means a person who is not a resident of this State at the time the accident or collision occurs.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
