# VEH § 12516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 1. Persons Required to Be Licensed, Exemptions, and Age Limits

**Citation:** VEH § 12516

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person under the age of 18 years to drive a school bus transporting pupils to or from school.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
