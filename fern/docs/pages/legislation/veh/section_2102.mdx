# VEH § 2102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 2102

---

## Text

Wherever in any statute “California Highway Patrol” is used, it means the Department of the California Highway Patrol.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
