# VEH § 42001.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 1. Penalties > Article: 1. Public Offenses

**Citation:** VEH § 42001.8

---

## Text

Every person convicted of an infraction for a violation of Section 4000 shall be punished by a fine of not less than fifty dollars ($50) and not more than two hundred fifty dollars ($250).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1126, Sec. 8. Effective September 28, 1985. Operative April 1, 1986, by Sec. 12 of Ch. 1126.)
