# VEH § 13350.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 2. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses > Article: 3. Suspension and Revocation by Department

**Citation:** VEH § 13350.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13350, for the purposes of this article, conviction of a violation of subdivision (b) of Section 191.5 of the Penal Code is a conviction of a violation of Section 23153.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 747, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2008.)
