# VEH § 5030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 8.1. Motorized Bicycles

**Citation:** VEH § 5030

---

## Text

A motorized bicycle, as defined in Section 406, is required to display a special license plate issued by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1070, Sec. 1.)
