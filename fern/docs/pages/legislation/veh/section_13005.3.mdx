# VEH § 13005.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 5. Identification Cards

**Citation:** VEH § 13005.3

---

## Text

In addition to the requirements of Section 13005, any identification card issued pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 13000 shall contain the words “Senior Citizen”.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1042, Sec. 4.)
