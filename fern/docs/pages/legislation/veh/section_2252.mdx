# VEH § 2252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 2. The California Highway Patrol

**Citation:** VEH § 2252

---

## Text

Such specialized positions as shall be designated by the commissioner with the approval of the Personnel Board shall be filled pursuant to open competitive examinations held pursuant to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
