# VEH § 610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 610

---

## Text

“Tire tread” is that portion of the tire, consisting of the ribs and grooves, which comes in contact with the roadway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 216.)
