# VEH § 14610.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 4. Violation of License Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 14610.7

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person to knowingly assist in obtaining a driver’s license or identification card for any person whose presence in the United States is not authorized under federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 820, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
