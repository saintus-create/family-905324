# VEH § 40000.77

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 1. Violation of Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40000.77

---

## Text

A violation of Article 7 (commencing with Section 2570) of Chapter 2.5 of Division 2, relating to transportation of school pupils, is a misdemeanor, and not an infraction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1563, Sec. 62.)
