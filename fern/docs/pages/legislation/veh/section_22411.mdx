# VEH § 22411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 7. Speed Laws > Article: 2. Other Speed Laws

**Citation:** VEH § 22411

---

## Text

No person shall operate a motorized scooter at a speed in excess of 15 miles per hour.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 722, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2000.)
