# VEH § 12952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 4. Signature and Display of Licenses

**Citation:** VEH § 12952

---

## Text

A licensee shall display his driver’s license upon request of a magistrate or judge before whom he may be brought for violation of any traffic law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
