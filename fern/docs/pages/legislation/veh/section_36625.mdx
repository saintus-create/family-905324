# VEH § 36625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 6. Size, Weight and Load of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** VEH § 36625

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 21715 do not apply to any vehicle drawing or towing unladen implements of husbandry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2149.)
