# VEH § 23301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 13. Vehicular Crossings and Toll Highways > Article: 3. Tolls and Other Charges

**Citation:** VEH § 23301

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 23301.3 and 23301.5, a vehicle that enters into or upon a vehicular crossing is liable for those tolls and other charges prescribed by the California Transportation Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 871, Sec. 1. (AB 2949) Effective January 1, 2023.)
