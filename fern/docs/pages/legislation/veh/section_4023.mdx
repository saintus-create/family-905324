# VEH § 4023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 1. Vehicles Subject to Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4023

---

## Text

A low-speed vehicle operated pursuant to Section 21115 or 21115.1 is exempt from registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 140, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
