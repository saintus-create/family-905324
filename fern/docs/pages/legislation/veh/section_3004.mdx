# VEH § 3004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 1. Organization of Board

**Citation:** VEH § 3004

## Text

Members of the board shall take an oath of office as provided in the Constitution and the Government Code.
