# VEH § 21463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 2. Traffic Signs, Signals, and Markings > Article: 3. Offenses Relating to Traffic Devices

**Citation:** VEH § 21463

---

## Text

No person shall operate a manually or traffic actuated signal other than for the purpose of permitting a pedestrian or vehicle to cross a roadway.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
