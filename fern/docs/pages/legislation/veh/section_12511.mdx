# VEH § 12511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 1. Persons Required to Be Licensed, Exemptions, and Age Limits

**Citation:** VEH § 12511

---

## Text

No person shall have in his or her possession or otherwise under his or her control more than one driver’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1509, Sec. 4.)
