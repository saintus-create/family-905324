# VEH § 36800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 36800

---

## Text

The provisions of this division, unless otherwise provided, apply to all vehicles, whether publicly or privately owned, when upon the highways.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2149.)
