# VEH § 10904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. SPECIAL ANTITHEFT LAWS > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention

**Citation:** VEH § 10904

---

## Text

The commissioner may develop a public education campaign to deter participation in auto insurance fraud and to encourage reporting of fraudulent claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 867, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2001.)
