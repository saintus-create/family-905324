# VEH § 34004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.7. FLAMMABLE AND COMBUSTIBLE LIQUIDS > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** VEH § 34004

---

## Text

The provisions of this division shall apply equally to new as well as existing cargo tanks.
