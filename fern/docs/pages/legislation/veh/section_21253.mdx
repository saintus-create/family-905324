# VEH § 21253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 5.5. Operation of Low-Speed Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 21253

---

## Text

A low-speed vehicle operated or parked on the roadway shall at all times meet federal Motor Vehicle Safety Standards established for low-speed vehicles in Section 571.500 of Title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 140, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2000.)
