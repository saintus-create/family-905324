# VEH § 21655.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 3. Driving, Overtaking, and Passing > Article: 1. Driving on Right Side

**Citation:** VEH § 21655.7

---

## Text

A local authority, with respect to any highway under its jurisdiction, may authorize or permit a portion of the highway to be used exclusively for a public mass transit guideway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1055, Sec. 12.)
