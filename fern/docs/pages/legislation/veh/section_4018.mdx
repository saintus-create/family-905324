# VEH § 4018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 1. Vehicles Subject to Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4018

---

## Text

Any logging vehicle is exempt from registration.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 517.)
