# VEH § 166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 166

---

## Text

An “autobroker” or “auto buying service” is a dealer, as defined in Section 285, who engages in the business of brokering, as defined in Section 232.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1253, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
