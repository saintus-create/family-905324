# VEH § 15602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6.7. UNATTENDED CHILD IN MOTOR VEHICLE SAFETY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 15602

---

## Text

This division applies to motor vehicles upon the highways and elsewhere throughout the state unless expressly provided otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 855, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
