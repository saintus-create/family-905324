# VEH § 6105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 2. Transfers of Title or Interest > Article: 5. Transfers Through Wholesale Auctions

**Citation:** VEH § 6105

---

## Text

This article shall become operative on July 1, 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 745, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 1993) for Article 5, commencing with Section 6100.)
