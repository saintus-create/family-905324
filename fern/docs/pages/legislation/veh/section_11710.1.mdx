# VEH § 11710.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Manufacturers, Transporters, Dealers, and Salesmen > Article: 1. Issuance of Licenses and Certificates to Manufacturers, Transporters, and Dealers

**Citation:** VEH § 11710.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (b) of Section 11710, the bond amount of a dealer who sells vehicles on a wholesale basis only, and who sells fewer than 25 vehicles per year, shall be ten thousand dollars ($10,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1110, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
