# VEH § 2403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** VEH § 2403

---

## Text

The commissioner may create highway patrol districts for the efficient administration and enforcement of this code and the laws respecting the use of highways. The commissioner may establish branch offices where necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 106. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
