# VEH § 17458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability of Owners and Operators of Vehicles > Article: 4. Service of Process

**Citation:** VEH § 17458

---

## Text

The director shall keep a record of all process served upon him under this article which record shall show the day and hour of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
