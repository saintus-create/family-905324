# VEH § 4151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 2. Original Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 4151

---

## Text

The department shall not accept an application for the original registration of a vehicle in this State unless the vehicle at the time of application is within this State unless the provisions of Section 4152 are complied with.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
