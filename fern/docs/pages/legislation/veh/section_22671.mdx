# VEH § 22671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 10. Removal of Parked and Abandoned Vehicles > Article: 1. Authority to Remove Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 22671

---

## Text

A local authority may either issue a franchise or execute a contract for the removal of abandoned vehicles in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 22706 by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1111, Sec. 26.)
