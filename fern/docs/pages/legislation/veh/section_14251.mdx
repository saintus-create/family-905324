# VEH § 14251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 3. Investigation and Hearing > Article: 4. Probation

**Citation:** VEH § 14251

---

## Text

The department shall have authority to terminate or to modify the terms or conditions of any order of probation whenever good cause appears therefor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
