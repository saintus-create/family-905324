# VEH § 20006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCIDENTS AND ACCIDENT REPORTS > Chapter: 1. Accidents and Accident Reports

**Citation:** VEH § 20006

---

## Text

If the driver does not have his driver’s license in his possession, he shall exhibit other valid evidences of identification to the occupants of a vehicle with which he collided.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
