# VEH § 12512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 1. Persons Required to Be Licensed, Exemptions, and Age Limits

**Citation:** VEH § 12512

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 12513, 12514, and 12814.6, no license to drive shall be issued to a person under the age of 18 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 596, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
