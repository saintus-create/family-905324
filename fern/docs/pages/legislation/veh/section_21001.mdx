# VEH § 21001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** VEH § 21001

---

## Text

The provisions of this division refer exclusively to the operation of vehicles upon the highways, unless a different place is specifically referred to.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
