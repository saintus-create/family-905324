# VEH § 3050.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Board

**Citation:** VEH § 3050.5

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 3016, the board shall establish a fee for the initial filing by any party in regard to any protest or petition filed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 796, Sec. 7. (AB 179) Effective January 1, 2020.)
