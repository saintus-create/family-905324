# VEH § 9704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 5. Partial Year Payment of Weight Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9704

---

## Text

Every partial year vehicle registration and every certificate or insignia issued under this article shall expire at midnight on the last day of the period for which issued. The department may, upon payment of the proper fees, renew the registration of the vehicle for a period of months less than the remainder of the registration year or for the remainder of the registration year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 636, Sec. 8.)
