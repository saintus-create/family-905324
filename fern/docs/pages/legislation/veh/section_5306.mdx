# VEH § 5306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 9.5. Registration of Fleet Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 5306

---

## Text

Upon payment of appropriate fees, the license plate or decal and registration card issued pursuant to this article for the vehicle shall remain valid until the provisions of Section 5307 have been met.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 695, Sec. 2.)
