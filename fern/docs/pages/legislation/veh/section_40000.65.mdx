# VEH § 40000.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 1. Violation of Code

**Citation:** VEH § 40000.65

---

## Text

A violation of Section 2430.5 or 2432, relating to emergency road service, is a misdemeanor and not an infraction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 488, Sec. 9.)
