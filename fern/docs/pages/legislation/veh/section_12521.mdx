# VEH § 12521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. DRIVERS' LICENSES > Chapter: 1. Issuance of Licenses, Expiration, and Renewal > Article: 1. Persons Required to Be Licensed, Exemptions, and Age Limits

**Citation:** VEH § 12521

---

## Text

An operator of a tour bus shall, at all times when operating the tour bus, do all the following:

(a) Use a safety belt.

(b) Report any accidents involving the tour bus to the Department of the California Highway Patrol.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1360, Sec. 22.5.)
