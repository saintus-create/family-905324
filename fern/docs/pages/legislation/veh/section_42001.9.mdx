# VEH § 42001.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENALTIES AND DISPOSITION OF FEES, FINES, AND FORFEITURES > Chapter: 1. Penalties > Article: 1. Public Offenses

**Citation:** VEH § 42001.9

---

## Text

Every person convicted of an infraction for a violation of Section 23135 shall be punished by a fine of fifty dollars ($50).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 421.)
