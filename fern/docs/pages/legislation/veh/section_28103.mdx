# VEH § 28103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 5. Other Equipment > Article: 15. Pilot Cars

**Citation:** VEH § 28103

---

## Text

It is unlawful and an infraction for any person to violate any provision of this article or to fail to have any required equipment in good working order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 460, Sec. 4.)
