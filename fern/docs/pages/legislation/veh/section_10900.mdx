# VEH § 10900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. SPECIAL ANTITHEFT LAWS > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention

**Citation:** VEH § 10900

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Motor Vehicle Theft Prevention Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1248, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
