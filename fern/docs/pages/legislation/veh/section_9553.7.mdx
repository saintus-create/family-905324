# VEH § 9553.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 4. Payment of Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9553.7

---

## Text

The penalty for delinquency with respect to any transfer is fifteen dollars ($15) and applies only to the last transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 756, Sec. 21. Effective September 30, 2008.)
