# VEH § 289

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 289

---

## Text

“Declared gross vehicle weight” means weight that equals the total unladen weight of the vehicle plus the heaviest load that will be transported on the vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 861, Sec. 13. Effective September 29, 2000. Operative December 31, 2001, pursuant to Sec. 67 of Ch. 861.)
