# VEH § 25264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 7. Flashing and Colored Lights

**Citation:** VEH § 25264

---

## Text

Any motor vehicle operated by a coroner, or by a deputy coroner, and which is at the scene of any violent highway death, may display flashing amber warning lights to the front or rear.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 653.)
