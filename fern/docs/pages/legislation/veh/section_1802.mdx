# VEH § 1802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. The Department of Motor Vehicles > Article: 3. Records of Department

**Citation:** VEH § 1802

---

## Text

Every judge of a court not of record shall keep a full record of every case in which a person is charged with any violation of this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
