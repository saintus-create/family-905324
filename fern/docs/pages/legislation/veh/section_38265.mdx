# VEH § 38265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. OFF-HIGHWAY VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Registration of Off-Highway Vehicles; Original and Renewal of Identification; Issuance of Certificates of Ownership > Article: 9. Identification Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 38265

---

## Text

(a) The penalty for delinquency in respect to any transfer shall be fifteen dollars ($15), and shall apply only to the last transfer.

(b) The penalty for delinquency in respect to the fees imposed by Sections 38225 and 38230 shall be equal to one-half the fee after it has been computed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 719, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2004.)
