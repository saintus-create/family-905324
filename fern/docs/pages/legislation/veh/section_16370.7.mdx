# VEH § 16370.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY LAWS > Chapter: 2. Suspensions Following Unsatisfied Judgments > Article: 2. Suspension of Driving Privilege

**Citation:** VEH § 16370.7

---

## Text

Documents filed with the department pursuant to Section 16370 shall be accompanied by a fee of not to exceed twenty dollars ($20) to pay for processing the documents and issuing the suspension order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 395, Sec. 4. Effective August 11, 1988.)
