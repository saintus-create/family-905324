# VEH § 36100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. IMPLEMENTS OF HUSBANDRY > Chapter: 2. Registration of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** VEH § 36100

---

## Text

Implements of husbandry which are only incidentally operated or moved over a highway and implements of husbandry listed in Section 36005 or 36015 are exempt from registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2149.)
