# VEH § 17157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 1. Civil Liability of Owners and Operators of Vehicles > Article: 2. Private Owners

**Citation:** VEH § 17157

---

## Text

If a motor vehicle is gratuitously loaned to a school district, the bailee and not the bailor shall be deemed to be the owner within the provisions of this chapter notwithstanding the terms of any contract, until the bailor retakes possession of the motor vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 289.)
