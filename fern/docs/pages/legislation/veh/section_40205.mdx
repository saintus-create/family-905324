# VEH § 40205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 3. Procedure on Parking Violations

**Citation:** VEH § 40205

---

## Text

If a person contests the parking violation, the processing agency shall proceed in accordance with Section 40215.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1244, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 39 of Ch. 1244.)
