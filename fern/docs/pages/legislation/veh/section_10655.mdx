# VEH § 10655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. SPECIAL ANTITHEFT LAWS > Chapter: 2. Reports of Stored Vehicles

**Citation:** VEH § 10655

---

## Text

No person required to keep a record or make a report under this chapter shall wilfully fail, refuse, or neglect to comply with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
