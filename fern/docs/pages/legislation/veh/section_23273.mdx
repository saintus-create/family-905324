# VEH § 23273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 13. Vehicular Crossings and Toll Highways > Article: 2. Towing on Vehicular Crossings

**Citation:** VEH § 23273

---

## Text

Sections 24605, 25253, 27700, and 27907 do not apply to vehicles operated by the Department of Transportation pursuant to this article.
