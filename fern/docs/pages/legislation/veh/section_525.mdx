# VEH § 525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 525

---

## Text

“Right-of-way” is the privilege of the immediate use of the highway.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
