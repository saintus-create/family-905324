# VEH § 24800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 4. Parking Lamps

**Citation:** VEH § 24800

---

## Text

No vehicle shall be driven at any time with the parking lamps lighted except when the lamps are being used as turn signal lamps or when the headlamps are also lighted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 58.)
