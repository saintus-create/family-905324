# VEH § 34500.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.8. SAFETY REGULATIONS

**Citation:** VEH § 34500.1

---

## Text

In addition to the duties imposed by Section 34500, the department shall regulate the safe operation of tour buses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1306, Sec. 13.)
