# VEH § 11113.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Driving Schools and Driving Instructors

**Citation:** VEH § 11113.3

---

## Text

The rules and regulations adopted pursuant to Section 11113 regarding the curriculum shall include, but are not limited to, the rights and duties of a motorist as they relate to traffic laws and traffic safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 833, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
