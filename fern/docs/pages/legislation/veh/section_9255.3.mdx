# VEH § 9255.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 6. Registration and Weight Fees > Article: 2. Registration Fees

**Citation:** VEH § 9255.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 9255, any vehicle transferred pursuant to Section 14607.6 shall be subject to a title transfer fee equal to the department’s actual cost of processing that transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1133, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
