# VEH § 21103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 3. Local Regulation

**Citation:** VEH § 21103

---

## Text

No ordinance or resolution enacted under Section 21101 shall be effective until signs giving notice of the local traffic laws are posted at all entrances to the highway or part thereof affected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 671, Sec. 4.)
