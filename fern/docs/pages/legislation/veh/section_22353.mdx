# VEH § 22353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 7. Speed Laws > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** VEH § 22353

---

## Text

When conducting an engineering and traffic survey, the City of Norco, in addition to the factors set forth in Section 627, may also consider equestrian safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 186, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
