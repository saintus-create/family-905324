# VEH § 2545

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Licenses Issued by California Highway Patrol > Article: 5. Denial, Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** VEH § 2545

---

## Text

Upon the effective date of any order of suspension or revocation of any license governed by this chapter, the licensee shall surrender the license to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1309.)
