# VEH § 22516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 9. Stopping, Standing, and Parking

**Citation:** VEH § 22516

---

## Text

No person shall leave standing a locked vehicle in which there is any person who cannot readily escape therefrom.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1959, Ch. 3.)
