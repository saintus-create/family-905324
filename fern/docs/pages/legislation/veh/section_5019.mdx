# VEH § 5019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. REGISTRATION OF VEHICLES AND CERTIFICATES OF TITLE > Chapter: 1. Original and Renewal of Registration; Issuance of Certificates of Title > Article: 8. Special Plates

**Citation:** VEH § 5019

---

## Text

The authorizations for the issuance of special license plates referred to in Sections 5005 and 5008 do not apply to trailers.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 918, Sec. 3.)
