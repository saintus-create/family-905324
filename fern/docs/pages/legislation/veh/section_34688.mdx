# VEH § 34688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.86. PRIVATE CARRIERS OF PASSENGERS REGISTRATION ACT

**Citation:** VEH § 34688

---

## Text

A private carrier of passengers shall display the carrier identification number, as required by Section 34507.5, on the vehicles operated pursuant to the registration granted under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 40. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018. Operative July 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 34693.)
