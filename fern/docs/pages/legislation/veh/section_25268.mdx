# VEH § 25268

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 7. Flashing and Colored Lights

**Citation:** VEH § 25268

---

## Text

No person shall display a flashing amber warning light on a vehicle as permitted by this code except when an unusual traffic hazard exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 653.)
