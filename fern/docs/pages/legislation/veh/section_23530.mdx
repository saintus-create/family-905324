# VEH § 23530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. SENTENCING FOR DRIVING WHILE UNDER THE INFLUENCE > Chapter: 2. Court Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** VEH § 23530

---

## Text

This chapter applies to the imposition of penalties, sanctions, and probation upon persons convicted of violating driving while under the influence offenses that are set forth in Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 23100) of Division 11.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 118, Sec. 84. Effective January 1, 1999. Section operative July 1, 1999, pursuant to Section 23675.)
