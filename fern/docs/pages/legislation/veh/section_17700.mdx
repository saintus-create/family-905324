# VEH § 17700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CIVIL LIABILITY > Chapter: 2. Civil Liability of Persons Signing License Applications of Minors

**Citation:** VEH § 17700

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, all persons under 18 years of age are minors.
