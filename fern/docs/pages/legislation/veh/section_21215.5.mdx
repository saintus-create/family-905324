# VEH § 21215.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 4.5. Operation of Pedicabs

**Citation:** VEH § 21215.5

---

## Text

This article does not preclude a local authority from imposing more stringent operating or equipment requirements on a pedicab subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 496, Sec. 2. (SB 530) Effective January 1, 2016.)
