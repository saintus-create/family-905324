# VEH § 3085.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 6. Export and Sale-for-Resale Prohibition Hearings

**Citation:** VEH § 3085.8

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall be applicable to any association which is primarily owned by or comprised of new motor vehicle dealers and acts on behalf of its new motor vehicle franchisees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 796, Sec. 17. (AB 179) Effective January 1, 2020. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 3085.10.)
