# VEH § 2452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of the California Highway Patrol > Article: 4. Highway Spill Containment and Abatement of Hazardous Substances

**Citation:** VEH § 2452

---

## Text

“Hazardous substance” means any hazardous material defined in Section 353 and any toxic substance defined pursuant to Section 108145 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 424. Effective September 29, 1996.)
