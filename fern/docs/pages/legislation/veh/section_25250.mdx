# VEH § 25250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. EQUIPMENT OF VEHICLES > Chapter: 2. Lighting Equipment > Article: 7. Flashing and Colored Lights

**Citation:** VEH § 25250

---

## Text

Flashing lights are prohibited on vehicles except as otherwise permitted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 223.)
