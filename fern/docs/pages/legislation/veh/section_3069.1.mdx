# VEH § 3069.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. New Motor Vehicle Board > Article: 4. Hearings on Franchise Modification, Replacement, Termination, Refusal to Continue, Delivery and Preparation Obligations, and Warranty Reimbursement

**Citation:** VEH § 3069.1

---

## Text

Sections 3060 to 3065.4, inclusive, do not apply to a franchise authorizing a dealership, as defined in subdivision (d) of Section 3072.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 295, Sec. 7. (AB 2956) Effective January 1, 2023.)
