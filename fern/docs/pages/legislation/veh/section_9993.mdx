# VEH § 9993

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. VEHICLE SALES > Chapter: 4. Disclosure of Damage

**Citation:** VEH § 9993

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter permits any dealer to respond to the inquiry of a purchaser in any untrue or misleading manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1373, Sec. 1.)
