# VEH § 9862.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. REGISTRATION AND TRANSFER OF VESSELS > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** VEH § 9862.5

---

## Text

In computing any penalty imposed under this chapter, a fraction of a dollar shall be disregarded unless it equals or exceeds fifty cents ($0.50), in which case it shall be treated as one dollar ($1).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 825, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2002.)
