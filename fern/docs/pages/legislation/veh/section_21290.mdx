# VEH § 21290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 7. Operation of Electrically Motorized Boards

**Citation:** VEH § 21290

---

## Text

(a) For purposes of this article, “bikeway” is defined in Section 890.4 of the Streets and Highways Code.

(b) For purposes of this article, an “electrically motorized board” is defined in Section 313.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 777, Sec. 3. (AB 604) Effective January 1, 2016.)
