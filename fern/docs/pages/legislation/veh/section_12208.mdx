# VEH § 12208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 11. Consumer Recovery Fund

**Citation:** VEH § 12208

---

## Text

If the recovery corporation pays the claim, the amount of the payment shall be the total of the amount of the eligible claim, but in no event may the payment exceed thirty-five thousand dollars ($35,000) for a transaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 437, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2008.)
