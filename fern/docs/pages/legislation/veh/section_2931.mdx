# VEH § 2931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 5. California Traffic Safety Program > Article: 2. Motorcycle Safety

**Citation:** VEH § 2931

---

## Text

A motorcyclist safety program is hereby established in the Department of the California Highway Patrol, to be administered by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 547, Sec. 2.)
