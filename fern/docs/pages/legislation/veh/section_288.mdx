# VEH § 288

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. WORDS AND PHRASES DEFINED

**Citation:** VEH § 288

---

## Text

“Declared combined gross weight” equals the total unladen weight of the combination of vehicles plus the heaviest load that will be transported by that combination of vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 861, Sec. 12. Effective September 29, 2000. Operative December 31, 2001, pursuant to Sec. 67 of Ch. 861.)
