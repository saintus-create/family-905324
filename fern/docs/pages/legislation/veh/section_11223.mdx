# VEH § 11223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Traffic Violator Schools

**Citation:** VEH § 11223

---

## Text

A motorcyclist safety training program established pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 2930) of Chapter 5 of Division 2, that is licensed by the department as a traffic violator school, may administer a motorcyclist training course. In addition to the curriculum prescribed by the department pursuant to Section 11219, the motorcyclist training course may include instruction specific to the safe and lawful operation of motorcycles in accordance with Section 2932.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 561, Sec. 1. (AB 1932) Effective January 1, 2017.)
