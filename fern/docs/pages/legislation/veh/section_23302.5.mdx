# VEH § 23302.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 13. Vehicular Crossings and Toll Highways > Article: 3. Tolls and Other Charges

**Citation:** VEH § 23302.5

---

## Text

(a) No person shall evade or attempt to evade the payment of tolls or other charges on any vehicular crossing or toll highway.

(b) A violation of subdivision (a) is subject to civil penalties and is neither an infraction nor a public offense, as defined in Section 15 of the Penal Code. The enforcement of those civil penalties shall be governed by the civil administrative procedures set forth in Article 4 (commencing with Section 40250) of Chapter 1 of Division 17.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 739, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
