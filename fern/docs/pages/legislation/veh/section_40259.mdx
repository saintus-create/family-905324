# VEH § 40259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. OFFENSES AND PROSECUTION > Chapter: 1. Offenses > Article: 4. Procedure on Toll Evasion Violations

**Citation:** VEH § 40259

---

## Text

If the toll evasion penalty is received by the person authorized to receive the deposit of the toll evasion penalty and there is no contest as to that toll evasion violation, the proceedings under this article shall terminate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 739, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
