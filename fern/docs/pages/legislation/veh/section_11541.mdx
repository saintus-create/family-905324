# VEH § 11541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. OCCUPATIONAL LICENSING AND BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Automobile Dismantlers

**Citation:** VEH § 11541

---

## Text

The department shall administer and enforce all provisions of this code pertaining to salvage pools.

This section shall become operative on July 1, 1987.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 952, Sec. 5. Section operative July 1, 1987, by its own provisions.)
