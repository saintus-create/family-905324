# VEH § 21280.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RULES OF THE ROAD > Chapter: 1. Obedience to and Effect of Traffic Laws > Article: 6. Electric Personal Assistive Mobility Devices

**Citation:** VEH § 21280.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, an electric personal assistive mobility device is defined in Section 313.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 979, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative March 1, 2003, pursuant to Section 21283 (later repealed).)
