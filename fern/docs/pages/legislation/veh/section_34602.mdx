# VEH § 34602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.85. MOTOR CARRIERS OF PROPERTY PERMIT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** VEH § 34602

---

## Text

As used in this division, “fund” means the Motor Vehicle Account in the State Transportation Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 805, Sec. 22. Effective September 23, 2002. Operative July 1, 2003, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 805.)
