# FAC § 40574

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 1. Certification of Agricultural Products > Article: 4. Shipping Point Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 40574

---

## Text

The director shall appoint an alternate for each committee member. In making these appointments, the director shall use the same criteria in selecting alternates as the regular members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 996, Sec. 2.)
