# FAC § 29142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 10. Exportation

**Citation:** FAC § 29142

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged for certification required by any law, regulation, or requirement of the United States.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
