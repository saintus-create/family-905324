# FAC § 20665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 3. Applications

**Citation:** FAC § 20665

---

## Text

A mark may be recorded only with a brand. If a mark is sought to be recorded with a brand, the application shall also contain a diagram which shows the manner of marking.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
