# FAC § 74922.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 74922.5

---

## Text

For the purpose of voting in the referendum required in Section 74921, only a person who would be required to pay the assessment as a producer pursuant to Section 74952.5 shall be considered a producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 8, Sec. 10. Effective March 27, 1987.)
