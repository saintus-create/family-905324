# FAC § 59923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 11. Amendments to Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59923

---

## Text

Minor amendments may be made to an effective marketing program upon the recommendation of not less than 75 percent of the producer members of the marketing program committee, with the approval of the director, if the director finds that such amendments are in conformity with the applicable provisions and procedures of Article 5 (commencing with Section 59641) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
