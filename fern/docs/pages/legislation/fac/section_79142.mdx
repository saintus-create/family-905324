# FAC § 79142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25. Sea Urchins > Article: 7. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 79142

---

## Text

The process specified in Section 79140 shall be conducted by the commission every fifth year following the initial certification of the commission, unless a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to Section 79143. In that case, the hearing, and the referendum if required, shall be conducted every fifth year following the industry petitioned referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
