# FAC § 18841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 18841

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to slaughter any livestock or poultry, or prepare any livestock or poultry product which is capable of use as human food, at any establishment preparing such article solely for intrastate commerce, except in compliance with the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
