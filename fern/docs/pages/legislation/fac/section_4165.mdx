# FAC § 4165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 6. Provisions Regarding Particular Associations > Article: 3.7. 48th District Agricultural Association

**Citation:** FAC § 4165

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the directors of the 48th District Agricultural Association may not serve concurrently on another fair board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 559, Sec. 65. (AB 2143) Effective January 1, 2025.)
