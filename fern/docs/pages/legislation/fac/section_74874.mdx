# FAC § 74874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 3. California Winegrape Growers Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74874

## Text

The secretary or his or her representatives shall be notified and may attend meetings of the commission and any committee meetings of the commission.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 59. Effective January 1, 1996.)
