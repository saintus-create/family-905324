# FAC § 78908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24.5. Mendocino County Winegrape and Wine Commission > Article: 5. Referendum on Implementation of this Chapter

**Citation:** FAC § 78908

---

## Text

Subsequent to the first members and alternate members elected to the commission, persons to be elected and appointed to the commission shall be selected pursuant to the nomination and election procedures established by the commission with the concurrence of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 597, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
