# FAC § 56571.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 17. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 56571.5

---

## Text

The fees provided by Section 56571 are maximum fees. The secretary may fix those fees at a lesser amount, and may adjust those fees from time to time, whenever the secretary finds that the funds needed to administer this chapter must be adjusted for the sustainability of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 603, Sec. 9. (SB 1270) Effective January 1, 2025.)
