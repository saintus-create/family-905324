# FAC § 75036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75036

---

## Text

“Secondary handler” means any person who purchases from a handler, eggs or egg products that were previously subject to assessment under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 454, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
