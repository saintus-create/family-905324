# FAC § 34262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Weighing, Measuring, Sampling, and Loading of Milk > Article: 3. Milk Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 34262

---

## Text

Each test shall be legibly recorded with indelible pencil or ink in such a manner as to correctly identify the test obtained upon the milk, cream, or fluid derivative of milk or cream.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
