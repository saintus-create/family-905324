# FAC § 6078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Cotton Pests Abatement Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6078

---

## Text

The persons declared elected shall take the oath of office and file their official bonds with the county elections official on or before the first day of April following their election and shall take office at noon of that day.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2003.)
