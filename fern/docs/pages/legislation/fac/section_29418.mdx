# FAC § 29418

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29418

---

## Text

“Placard” means any sign, label, or designation, except an oral designation, which is used in connection with any honey as a description or identification of it.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
