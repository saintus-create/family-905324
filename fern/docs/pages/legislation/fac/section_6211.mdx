# FAC § 6211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6211

---

## Text

The petition may be filed in sections, each of which shall comply with all the requirements for a petition, except that a section need not contain the total number of signatures required for the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
