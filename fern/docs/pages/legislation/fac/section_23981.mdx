# FAC § 23981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 7. Transportation

**Citation:** FAC § 23981

---

## Text

A person shall not transport any sheep, unless the sheep are accompanied by a bill of lading which shows all of the following:

(a) The name of the owner of the sheep.

(b) The destination of the sheep.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
