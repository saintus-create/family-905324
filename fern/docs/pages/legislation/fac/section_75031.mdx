# FAC § 75031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75031

---

## Text

“Market” or “marketing” means to sell or otherwise cause to be distributed eggs or egg products into commercial channels of trade resulting in the sale of eggs or egg products in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 454, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
