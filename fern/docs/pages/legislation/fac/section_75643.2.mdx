# FAC § 75643.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 75643.2

---

## Text

It is not necessary for the commission to allege or prove that an adequate remedy at law does not exist in any action brought under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 59. Effective May 9, 1994.)
