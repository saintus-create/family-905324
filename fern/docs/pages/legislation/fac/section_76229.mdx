# FAC § 76229

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 76229

---

## Text

“Marketing year” or “fiscal year” means the period beginning April 1 of any year and ending March 31 of the following year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 397, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
