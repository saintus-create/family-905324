# FAC § 55432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 55432

---

## Text

The director may publish in pamphlet form as often as he thinks necessary a list of processors that are licensed pursuant to this chapter, together with all necessary regulations which concern the enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
