# FAC § 26554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Sale, Possession, and Transportation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 26554

---

## Text

“Poultry” includes chickens, ducks, geese, turkeys, and all other fowls or birds which are used for food or production purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
