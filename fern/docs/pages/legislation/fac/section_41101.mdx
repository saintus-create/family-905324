# FAC § 41101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 41101

---

## Text

The director shall have supervision and control over the commissioners and their deputies or inspectors in the performance of their duties under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
