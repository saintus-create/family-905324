# FAC § 54141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 6. Directors and Management

**Citation:** FAC § 54141

---

## Text

The affairs of the association shall be managed by a board of not less than three directors who are elected by the members or stockholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
