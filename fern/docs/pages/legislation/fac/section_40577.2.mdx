# FAC § 40577.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 1. Certification of Agricultural Products > Article: 4. Shipping Point Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 40577.2

---

## Text

The committee shall not request that the secretary utilize, and the secretary shall not utilize, an entity a majority of whose officers and employees are persons utilizing services provided by the department pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 542, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
