# FAC § 76116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 76116

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in this article, he or she shall so certify and declare that this chapter is not operative. The director may conduct another implementation referendum vote one year or more after the previous vote has been taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 58. Effective September 26, 1985.)
