# FAC § 19354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 19354

---

## Text

A dead animal hauler shall register each vehicle used to transport dead animals with the bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 187, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2005.)
