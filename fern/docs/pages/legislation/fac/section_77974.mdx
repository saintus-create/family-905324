# FAC § 77974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 77974

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 77971, the director shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote to all producers whose names and addresses are on file with the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
