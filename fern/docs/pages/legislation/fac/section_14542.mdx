# FAC § 14542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14542

---

## Text

“Labeling” means all written, printed, or graphic matter on, accompanying, or used in promoting the sale of any fertilizing material, including advertisements, brochures, posters, internet websites, and television and radio announcements.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 208, Sec. 7. (SB 1522) Effective January 1, 2025.)
