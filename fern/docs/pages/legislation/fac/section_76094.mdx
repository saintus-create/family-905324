# FAC § 76094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76094

---

## Text

The commission may publish and distribute, without charge, a bulletin or other communication for dissemination of information to producers, shippers, grower-handlers, and processors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 53. Effective September 26, 1985.)
