# FAC § 407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 407

---

## Text

The director may adopt such regulations as are reasonably necessary to carry out the provisions of this code which he is directed or authorized to administer or enforce.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
