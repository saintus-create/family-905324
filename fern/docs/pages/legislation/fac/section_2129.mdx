# FAC § 2129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 2. Appointment and Employment

**Citation:** FAC § 2129

---

## Text

If the position of inspector cannot be filled from the list, a temporary appointment not exceeding six months may be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
