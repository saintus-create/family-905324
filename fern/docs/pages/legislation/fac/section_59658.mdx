# FAC § 59658

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 5. Establishment of Proration Zone

**Citation:** FAC § 59658

---

## Text

In making any findings pursuant to Section 59657, the director shall base his findings upon the facts, testimony, and evidence which is received at the public hearing together with any other relevant facts which are available to him from official publications or institutions of recognized standing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
