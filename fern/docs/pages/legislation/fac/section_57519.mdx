# FAC § 57519

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 12. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien > Article: 2. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57519

---

## Text

For the purpose of the Secretary of State’s index pursuant to Section 9519 of the Commercial Code and for the purpose of the issuance of a certificate pursuant to Section 9519 or 9528 of the Commercial Code, the Secretary of State shall identify a notice pursuant to this article as a financing statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 43.9. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
