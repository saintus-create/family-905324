# FAC § 59641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 5. Establishment of Proration Zone

**Citation:** FAC § 59641

---

## Text

Ten or more producers of the variety or kind of commodity which is to be affected may file with the director a petition for the establishment of a proration zone and the institution of a marketing program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
