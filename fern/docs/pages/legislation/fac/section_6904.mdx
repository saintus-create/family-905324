# FAC § 6904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 1. Inspections and Standards of Cleanliness

**Citation:** FAC § 6904

---

## Text

The commissioner shall issue such orders as may reasonably be necessary to insure compliance with the standards of cleanliness.

Any interested person, upon request, is entitled to a hearing before the director to review any order which is issued by a commissioner pursuant to this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
