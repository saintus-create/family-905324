# FAC § 77550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 3. The California Forest Products Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77550

---

## Text

The commission shall be and is hereby declared to be a corporate body. It may sue and be sued, enter into contracts, adopt a seal, and have and possess all of the powers of a corporation. Copies of its proceedings, records, and acts when authenticated, shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of all statements therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 550, Sec. 1. Effective August 27, 1990.)
