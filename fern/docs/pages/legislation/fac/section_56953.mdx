# FAC § 56953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 6. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 56953

---

## Text

The scale of charges which is established shall be the standard charge for unloading any farm product in the market.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
