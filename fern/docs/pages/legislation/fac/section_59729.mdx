# FAC § 59729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 7. Marketing Program and Grading Committees

**Citation:** FAC § 59729

---

## Text

The members and alternate members of any marketing program committee or grading committee which are duly appointed by the director, including employees of the marketing program committee, are not responsible individually in any way whatsoever to any person for liability on any contract or agreement of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
