# FAC § 59034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 13. Major Amendments

**Citation:** FAC § 59034

---

## Text

An amendment is a major amendment if it adds to, or deletes from, a marketing order authority to increase an assessment rate beyond the maximum rate which is authorized by the marketing order then in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
