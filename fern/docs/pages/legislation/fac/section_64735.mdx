# FAC § 64735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 9. Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 64735

---

## Text

If upon a count of the ballots the director finds that less than 51 percent of the total number of producers voting have assented to such discontinuance and termination, he shall make a finding to that effect and the council and programs which are established and maintained pursuant to this chapter shall continue to remain in full force and effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
