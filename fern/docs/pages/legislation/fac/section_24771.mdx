# FAC § 24771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 5. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 24771

---

## Text

A person shall not do any of the following:

(a) Operate a poultry plant which is not sanitary.

(b) Operate a poultry plant in a manner or under conditions which are not sanitary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
