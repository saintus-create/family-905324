# FAC § 77372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 77372

---

## Text

Following a favorable referendum conducted prior to April 30, 1994, a referendum shall be conducted by the commission every fifth year thereafter unless a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to this article. In that case, the referendum shall be every fifth year following the industry petitioned referendum following the procedures provided in Section 77371.
