# FAC § 16303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16303

---

## Text

“Inspector” means a hide and brand inspector. It includes the persons who are specified in Section 20010.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
