# FAC § 59021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 13. Major Amendments

**Citation:** FAC § 59021

---

## Text

In making effective major amendments to a marketing order, the director shall follow the same procedures which are prescribed in this chapter for the institution of a marketing order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
