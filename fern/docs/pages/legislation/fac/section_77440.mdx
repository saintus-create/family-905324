# FAC § 77440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 3. The California Strawberry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77440

---

## Text

The term of office of all members of the commission and alternates, except any ex officio member, shall be two years from the date of their election and until their successors are elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
