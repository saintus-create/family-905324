# FAC § 76530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 76530

---

## Text

“Fisherman” means an individual licensed to commercially harvest fish pursuant to Sections 7850 to 7852, inclusive, of the Fish and Game Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 53, Sec. 2. Effective June 22, 1989.)
