# FAC § 8754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 8. Dissolution of Districts

## Text

Notice of such hearing shall be given by publication in a newspaper of general circulation published and circulated in the district.
