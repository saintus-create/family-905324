# FAC § 57407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 11. Dairy Cattle Supply Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57407

---

## Text

Except to the extent specifically set forth in this chapter, the lien created by this chapter shall be subject to Division 9 (commencing with Section 9101) of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 679, Sec. 1.)
