# FAC § 5632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 4. Expense of Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 5632

---

## Text

From the date of the recording, the land which is described in the notice is subject to a lien for any expense which is incurred by the county subsequent to the recording of the notice in the abatement of the nuisance which is referred to in the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
