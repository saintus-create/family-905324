# FAC § 9262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1.5. Commercial Blood Banks for Animals and Biologics > Article: 7. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 9262

---

## Text

The biologics prepared prior to July 1, 1975, in laboratories licensed pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 1600) of Division 2 of the Health and Safety Code, which have an expiration date of July 1, 1975, or later, are not subject to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 776.)
