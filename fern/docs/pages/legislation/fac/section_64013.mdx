# FAC § 64013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 64013

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, cooperative association, and any other business unit or organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1995.)
