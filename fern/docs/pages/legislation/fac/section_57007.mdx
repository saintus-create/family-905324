# FAC § 57007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 57007

---

## Text

“Farm product” means any fruit, nut, vegetable, berry, or aquacultural product.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 810, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1996.)
