# FAC § 34952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 34952

---

## Text

“Milk products plant license” means a license which is issued by the director pursuant to Section 35015 for a milk products plant.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 423, Sec. 5.)
