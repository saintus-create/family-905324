# FAC § 64306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 11. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 64306

---

## Text

To the amount of assessments that are unpaid when due, the secretary may add a penalty of 10 percent. In addition, the secretary may assess interest at a rate of 1 percent of the unpaid balance for each 30 days the assessment is unpaid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 105. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
