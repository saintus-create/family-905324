# FAC § 38512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 16. Milk Drink

**Citation:** FAC § 38512

---

## Text

No milk or milk products, except market milk, market skim milk, or market cream, shall be combined with, or added to, milk drink mix or UHT milk drink mix in the freezing device in the making of milk drinks.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 14. Effective September 15, 1987.)
