# FAC § 4501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 5. Department of Food and Agriculture Supervision

**Citation:** FAC § 4501

---

## Text

The department shall prescribe regulations for both of the following:

(a) The judging of exhibits.

(b) The maximum amount of premiums which may be offered and paid for all classes, sections, and types of exhibits.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
