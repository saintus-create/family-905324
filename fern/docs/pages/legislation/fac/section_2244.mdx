# FAC § 2244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 7. Offices, Equipment and Supplies

**Citation:** FAC § 2244

---

## Text

All expenses which are ordered by the board of supervisors for the office, furniture, supplies, effects, and equipment for the commissioner are a county charge. The board of supervisors shall allow and pay such expenses out of the general fund of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
