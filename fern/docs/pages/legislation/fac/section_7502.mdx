# FAC § 7502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pest Seeds > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 7502

---

## Text

The enforcement of this chapter is under the supervision of the director. He shall make such regulations as he may deem necessary to properly carry out the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
