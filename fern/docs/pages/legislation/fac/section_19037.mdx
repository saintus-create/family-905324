# FAC § 19037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 10. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 19037

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to violate any provision of the regulations promulgated by the director which are applicable to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1128, Sec. 1.)
