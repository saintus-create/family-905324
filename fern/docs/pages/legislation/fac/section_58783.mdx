# FAC § 58783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 6. Notice, Reports, and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 58783

---

## Text

At the hearing, the director shall receive, in addition to other necessary or relevant matters, evidence upon the matters which are set forth in Article 7 (commencing with Section 58811).
