# FAC § 24004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 8. Drugging of Horses

**Citation:** FAC § 24004

---

## Text

A horse shall not be shown in any class at an event if it has been administered in any manner a prohibited substance or permissible substance in violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 289, Sec. 9. (AB 3252) Effective January 1, 2019.)
