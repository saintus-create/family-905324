# FAC § 77487

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 77487

---

## Text

Any assessment that is levied as provided in this chapter is a personal debt of every producer, processor, or shipper so assessed. Failure of a shipper or processor to make payment of the collected producer assessment to the commission shall not relieve the producer of this obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
