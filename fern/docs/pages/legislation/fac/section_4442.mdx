# FAC § 4442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions

**Citation:** FAC § 4442

---

## Text

No fair for which a separate appropriation is made by the state shall participate in the apportionment of any money appropriated for the encouragement of county and district agricultural association fairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
