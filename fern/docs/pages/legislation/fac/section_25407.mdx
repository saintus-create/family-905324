# FAC § 25407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 25407

---

## Text

“New York dressed fowl” means fowl from which the feathers have been removed and the blood drawn and which is offered for sale as a whole fowl with the head, feet, and entrails intact.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
