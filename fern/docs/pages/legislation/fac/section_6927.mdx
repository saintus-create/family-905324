# FAC § 6927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 2. Shipment of Nursery Stock, Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 6927

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to alter any shipping permit or nursery stock certificate or to use it in any manner other than as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6926 by Stats. 1968, Ch. 288.)
