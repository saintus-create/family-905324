# FAC § 52295

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 2.5. Seed Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52295

---

## Text

Members of the board shall receive no salary but may be allowed per diem in accordance with Department of General Services rules for attendance at meetings and other board activities authorized by the board and approved by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 31. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
