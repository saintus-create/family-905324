# FAC § 36033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 11. Bottle Caps and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 36033

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell or use any milk bottle caps which are not packed by the manufacturer in single column in unopened, dustproof tubular packages.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
