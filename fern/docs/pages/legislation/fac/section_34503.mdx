# FAC § 34503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 34503

---

## Text

“Single service container” means a container which is used only once and is not refilled.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
