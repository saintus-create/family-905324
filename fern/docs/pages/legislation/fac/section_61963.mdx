# FAC § 61963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 6. Marketing Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 61963

---

## Text

If the director finds, after a public hearing in and for each particular marketing area under consideration for consolidation, that conditions of production and handling are reasonably uniform in two or more such marketing areas in which stabilization and marketing plans are in effect, he may consolidate the areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
