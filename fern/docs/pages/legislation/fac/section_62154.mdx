# FAC § 62154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 12. Handlers’ Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 62154

---

## Text

After any decision, which includes any conditional or probationary orders, if the respondent does not comply with any such orders, the director may suspend or revoke the license in accordance with the procedure which is provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
