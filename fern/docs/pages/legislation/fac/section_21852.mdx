# FAC § 21852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 9. Injury to, or Theft of, Cattle > Article: 1. Driving Cattle From Their Range

**Citation:** FAC § 21852

---

## Text

Any person that is not the owner or does not have the right of possession of any cattle, that is found driving such cattle off its usual range, without the consent of the owner, is guilty of grand theft.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
