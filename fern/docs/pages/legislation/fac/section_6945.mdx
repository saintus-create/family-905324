# FAC § 6945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 3. Citrus Nursery Stock Pest Cleanliness Program

**Citation:** FAC § 6945

---

## Text

This article shall be construed liberally to effectuate the Legislature’s intent that an effective citrus nursery stock pest cleanliness program in the department be established and administered by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 639, Sec. 2. (SB 140) Effective November 2, 2009.)
