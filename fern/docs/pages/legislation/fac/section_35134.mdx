# FAC § 35134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 7. Butter Permits and Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 35134

---

## Text

Every person who is in charge of cutting, wrapping, packaging, or distributing butter, or any supervisor of any such person who is responsible for the grading of butter, shall obtain and hold a butter grader’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
