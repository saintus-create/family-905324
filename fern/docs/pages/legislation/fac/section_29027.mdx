# FAC § 29027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 2. Apiary Board

**Citation:** FAC § 29027

---

## Text

The board shall meet at the call of the director or at the request of any three members of the board. It shall meet at least once a year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
