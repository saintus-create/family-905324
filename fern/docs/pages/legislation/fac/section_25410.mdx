# FAC § 25410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 25410

---

## Text

“Poultry plant” means any of the following:

(a) Any place where poultry is slaughtered, dressed, or drawn.

(b) Any place where poultry meat is cut up, recut, packed or repacked, or otherwise prepared for human food.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
