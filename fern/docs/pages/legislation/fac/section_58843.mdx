# FAC § 58843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 8. Advisory Boards and Committees

**Citation:** FAC § 58843

---

## Text

Upon the recommendation of the advisory board, the director may appoint one person who is neither a producer nor a handler to represent the department or the public generally.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
