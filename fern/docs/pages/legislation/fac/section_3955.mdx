# FAC § 3955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Organization and Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 3955

---

## Text

Claims against an association shall be presented to the Department of General Services in accordance with Part 3 (commencing with Section 900) and Part 4 (commencing with Section 940) of Division 3.6 of Title 1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 29. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
