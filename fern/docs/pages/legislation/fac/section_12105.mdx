# FAC § 12105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 7. Pest Control Dealer > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 12105

---

## Text

A penalty as prescribed by the director pursuant to Section 11502.5 shall be added to any fee that is not paid by the date of expiration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 56. Effective January 1, 2004.)
