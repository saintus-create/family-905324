# FAC § 32911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32911

---

## Text

A person shall not efface, erase, cancel, or remove any mark, statement, or label which is required by this division, with intent to mislead, deceive, or to violate any provision of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
