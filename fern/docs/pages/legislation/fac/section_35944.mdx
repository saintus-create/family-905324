# FAC § 35944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 8. Homogenized Market Milk and Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 35944

---

## Text

Homogenized market milk or homogenized market cream shall not be mixed with market milk or market cream which has not been homogenized.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
