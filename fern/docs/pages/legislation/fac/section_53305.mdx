# FAC § 53305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 53305

---

## Text

“Labeling” means all labels, and other written, printed, or graphic representations, in any form whatsoever, which accompanies and pertains to any nursery stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
