# FAC § 2274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 8. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 2274

---

## Text

The commissioner shall learn about all pests that may exist, or are likely to exist, in his county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
