# FAC § 11221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. ANIMALS DETRIMENTAL TO AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Predators > Article: 1. Hunters and Trappers

**Citation:** FAC § 11221

---

## Text

The director may employ hunters and trappers throughout the state to control or eradicate coyotes and other harmful predatory animals and to shoot or trap bears which are damaging livestock, agricultural crops, or standing timber.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
