# FAC § 24802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 6. Marks and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 24802

---

## Text

Poultry meat shall not be packed, repacked, cut up, recut, or sold unless each container or each carcass is marked in the manner which is required by this chapter and the regulations which are adopted pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
