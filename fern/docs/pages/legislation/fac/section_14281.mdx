# FAC § 14281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 14281

---

## Text

A person shall not sell any livestock drug in this state prior to receipt of a registration certificate pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 834, Sec. 1.)
