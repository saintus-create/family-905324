# FAC § 61407.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 5. Bonding and Payment for Manufacturing Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 61407.2

---

## Text

If the recovery upon the bond is not sufficient to pay all of the claims as finally determined and adjudged by the court, the amount recovered shall be divided pro rata among the producer-creditors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 156, Sec. 7. Effective July 10, 1987.)
