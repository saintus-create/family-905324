# FAC § 76060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 3. The California Cherry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76060

---

## Text

The public member or his or her alternate on the commission, shall have all the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member on the commission. The public member shall not have any financial interest in the cherry industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 41. Effective September 26, 1985.)
