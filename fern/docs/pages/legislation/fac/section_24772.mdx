# FAC § 24772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 5. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 24772

---

## Text

After each day’s operation, a poultry plant and its outer premises shall be promptly cleaned in the manner which is prescribed by the regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
