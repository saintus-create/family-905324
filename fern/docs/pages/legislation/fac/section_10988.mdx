# FAC § 10988

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 2. Garbage Control > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 10988

---

## Text

The proceedings for all hearings pursuant to this chapter shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2, of the Government Code. The director shall have all powers which are granted by such chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
