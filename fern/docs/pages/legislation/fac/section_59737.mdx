# FAC § 59737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 7. Marketing Program and Grading Committees

**Citation:** FAC § 59737

---

## Text

Upon recommendation of the marketing program committee which is concerned, the director may, consistent with this chapter, establish such administrative regulations as may be necessary to facilitate the administration and enforcement of each marketing program which is promulgated pursuant to this chapter. Upon the issuance and making effective of such regulations, notice of the issuance of the regulations shall be made in the same manner as provided in Article 16 (commencing with Section 59111), Chapter 1, Part 2 of this division for marketing orders.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
