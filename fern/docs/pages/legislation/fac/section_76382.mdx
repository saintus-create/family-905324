# FAC § 76382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 76382

---

## Text

If a petition is filed as provided in Section 76382.5, the secretary shall hold a hearing pursuant to Section 76381 prior to conducting the referendum authorized by Section 76382.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 575, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1999.)
