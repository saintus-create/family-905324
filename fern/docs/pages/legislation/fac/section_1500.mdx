# FAC § 1500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 4. CALIFORNIA AGRICULTURE DAY

**Citation:** FAC § 1500

---

## Text

The first day of spring in each year is hereby designated as California Agriculture Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 348, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
