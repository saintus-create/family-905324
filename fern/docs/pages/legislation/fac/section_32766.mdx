# FAC § 32766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 4. Condemnation

**Citation:** FAC § 32766

---

## Text

The director may destroy or mark for identification with a nontoxic substance, any condemned product of milk or cream or product resembling a milk product.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
