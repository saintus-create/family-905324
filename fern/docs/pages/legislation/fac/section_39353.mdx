# FAC § 39353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Margarine, Dairy Spread, and Spread > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 39353

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person knowingly to possess or have under his or her control any margarine, dairy spread, or spread, except for actual consumption by himself or herself or his or her family, unless it is labeled in conformity with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 512, Sec. 9.)
