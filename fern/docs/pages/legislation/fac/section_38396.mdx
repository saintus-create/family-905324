# FAC § 38396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 12. Extra Grade Edible Dry Whey, Dry Buttermilk, and Modified Dry Whey

**Citation:** FAC § 38396

---

## Text

Each container of extra grade edible dry whey shall be labeled with the full name of the product and with the word “pasteurized” when filled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 306.)
