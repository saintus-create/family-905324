# FAC § 27686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 15. Criminal Punishment and Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 27686

---

## Text

All shipped eggs shall be transported under refrigeration in compliance with California statutes and regulations.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 121. Effective January 1, 2005.)
