# FAC § 54463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 4. Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 54463

---

## Text

In addition to any other remedies provided under this article, the director may seek to obtain injunctive relief in the proper court to require any person subject to this chapter to comply with any applicable requirement in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1100, Sec. 15.)
