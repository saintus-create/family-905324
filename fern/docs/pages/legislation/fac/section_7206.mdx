# FAC § 7206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 1. Weeds > Article: 1. Weed-Free Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 7206

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell, distribute, or transport into, or within, any weed-free area any seed of any noxious weed of which the area has been declared to be practically free.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
