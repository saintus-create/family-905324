# FAC § 52878

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 3. San Joaquin Valley Cotton Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52878

---

## Text

The term of office for each member, alternate, and ex officio member of the board is four years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 433, Sec. 5. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
