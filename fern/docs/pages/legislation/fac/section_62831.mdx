# FAC § 62831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. Marketing of Edible Nuts > Article: 2. Records

**Citation:** FAC § 62831

---

## Text

The commissioner of each county, under the supervision of the director, shall enforce this article insofar as it relates to the keeping of the records which are required to be kept by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
