# FAC § 4531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 6. Fairs Allocation and Classification Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 4531

---

## Text

The Fairs Allocation and Classification Committee is continued in existence. It consists of seven Members of the Senate and seven Members of the Assembly.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
