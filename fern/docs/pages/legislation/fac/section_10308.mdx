# FAC § 10308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 1. Definitions

## Text

“Premises” means any place where cattle have been or are kept.
