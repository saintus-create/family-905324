# FAC § 58555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Foreign Marketing > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58555

---

## Text

“Constraint” means a condition in a particular country or region which needs to be addressed in order to develop, expand, or maintain agricultural commodity exports.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1189, Sec. 2.)
