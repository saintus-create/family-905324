# FAC § 77921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77921

---

## Text

“Market research” means any investigation, development, analysis, or use of information relating to the marketing, sales, or promotion of flower products in this state and nationally and internationally, including, but not limited to, trade practices, consumer trends, promotion, sales, and advertising.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
