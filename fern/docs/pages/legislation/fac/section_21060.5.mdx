# FAC § 21060.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 1. Requirements, Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 21060.5

---

## Text

Any calves not exempt from inspection pursuant to Section 21060, shall be inspected in accordance with the provisions of Sections 21051 and 21052.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1221.)
