# FAC § 59812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59812

---

## Text

The marketing program committee may pledge all of the commodity that is in any pool which is established pursuant to any provision of this article with a bank or other lending agency for the purpose of obtaining loans upon such commodity. The marketing program committee has title to all of the commodity which is in any such pools for the purpose of financing and handling.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
