# FAC § 57700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 14. Limitation on Nonconsensual Supplier Liens

**Citation:** FAC § 57700

---

## Text

No more than two liens filed pursuant to Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 57501) and Chapter 13 (commencing with Section 57551) shall be enforceable against any one lien debtor, even if filed by different lien claimants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 3.)
