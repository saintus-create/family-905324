# FAC § 72022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 72022

---

## Text

“Wheat” means any variety of wheat produced in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
