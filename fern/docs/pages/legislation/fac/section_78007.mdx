# FAC § 78007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 78007

---

## Text

On and after one year after the commission is suspended or terminated pursuant to this article, the secretary may conduct a vote to reconvene the commission consistent with Article 5 (commencing with Section 77970).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 267, Sec. 3. (AB 1205) Effective January 1, 2021.)
