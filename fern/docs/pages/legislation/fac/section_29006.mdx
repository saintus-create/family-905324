# FAC § 29006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29006

---

## Text

“Colony” means one hive and its contents, including bees, comb, and appliances.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
