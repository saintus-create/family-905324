# FAC § 58004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58004

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 58005, fees, charges, and costs that are collected under this chapter shall be paid into the State Treasury to the credit of the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 346, Sec. 1. Effective August 29, 1994.)
