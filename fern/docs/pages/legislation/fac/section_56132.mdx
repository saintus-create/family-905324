# FAC § 56132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 56132

---

## Text

The director may publish in pamphlet form as often as he thinks necessary a list of all licensees and agents that are licensed pursuant to this chapter together with all necessary regulations which concern the enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 915.)
