# FAC § 41006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 10. Processing Tomato Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41006

---

## Text

The committee shall meet at the call of its chairman or at the request to the director of any three members of the committee. It shall meet at least once each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 783.)
