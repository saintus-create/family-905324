# FAC § 4

**Citation:** FAC § 4

---

## Text

The provisions of this code, insofar as they are substantially the same as existing law, are restatements and continuations of existing law, and not new enactments. The enactment of this code shall not impair any privilege granted or right acquired under any of the laws of this state prior to the date it takes effect.
