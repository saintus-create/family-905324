# FAC § 14513.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14513.5

---

## Text

“Biochar” means materials derived from thermochemical conversion of biomass in an oxygen-limited environment containing at least 60 percent carbon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 331, Sec. 2. (AB 2511) Effective January 1, 2017.)
