# FAC § 59806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59806

---

## Text

The contents of a stabilization pool may be disposed of or may be marketed, from time to time, as the marketing program committee deems advisable, consistent with the maintenance of stabilized marketing conditions.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
