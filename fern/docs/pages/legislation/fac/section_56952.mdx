# FAC § 56952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 6. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 56952

---

## Text

Any scale of charges which is established pursuant to this article may be modified from time to time in accordance with the method and procedure which is prescribed for establishing such scale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
