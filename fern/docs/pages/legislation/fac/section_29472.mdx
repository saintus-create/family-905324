# FAC § 29472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 29472

## Text

The containers of the ungraded honey shall not bear any false or misleading statements or be deceptively packed
