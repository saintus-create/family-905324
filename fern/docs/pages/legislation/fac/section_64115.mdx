# FAC § 64115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 4. Dairy Council of California

**Citation:** FAC § 64115

---

## Text

No member of the council, except for a member of the executive committee, shall receive compensation for more than 10 days’ service per year. No member of the executive committee shall receive compensation for more than three days’ service per month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 83. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
