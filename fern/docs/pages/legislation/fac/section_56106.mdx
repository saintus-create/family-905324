# FAC § 56106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56106

---

## Text

“Consignor” includes any person that ships or delivers to any commission merchant or dealer any farm product for handling, sale, or resale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
