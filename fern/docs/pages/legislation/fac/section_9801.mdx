# FAC § 9801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 1. Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 9801

---

## Text

This part does not apply to animals which are used in research projects of the University of California.
