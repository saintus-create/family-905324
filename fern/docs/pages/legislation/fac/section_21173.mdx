# FAC § 21173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 5. Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 21173

---

## Text

If requested to do so, the person in charge of the animals shall furnish the inspector with a list of the brands and marks on them to the best of his ability.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
