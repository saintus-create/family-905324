# FAC § 77552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 3. The California Forest Products Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77552

---

## Text

The secretary or his or her representatives shall be notified and may attend each meeting of the commission and any committee meeting of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 122. Effective January 1, 1996.)
