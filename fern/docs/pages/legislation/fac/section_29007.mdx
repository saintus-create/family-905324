# FAC § 29007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29007

---

## Text

“Comb” includes all materials which are normally deposited into hives by bees. It does not include extracted honey or royal jelly, trapped pollen, and processed beeswax.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
