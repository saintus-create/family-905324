# FAC § 6133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 2. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 6133

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may receive and accept on behalf of the county, any contribution or donation of money from a person or from any agency of this state or of the United States for the purpose of enforcing this chapter. Any money which is so received shall be credited to the budget of the commissioner and shall be expended solely for such enforcement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
