# FAC § 56807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56807

---

## Text

“Market” means the places of business, collectively, of produce dealers that operate in a city, or the vicinity of a city, and that deal in any farm product which is intended, in whole or in part, for ultimate sale for consumption in a fresh state within the city and the vicinity of the city. This chapter applies only to markets in which five or more produce dealers operate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
