# FAC § 12757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12757

---

## Text

“Rodent” means all members of the order Rodentia and all rabbits and hares.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
