# FAC § 41207.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 5. Wine Grape Inspection Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41207.9

---

## Text

The entity shall keep an accurate record of expenses incurred in performing its responsibilities. Those records shall be available for audit during regular business hours upon request of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 319, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993.)
