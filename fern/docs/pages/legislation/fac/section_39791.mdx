# FAC § 39791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 10. UHT Sour Flavored Half-and-Half

**Citation:** FAC § 39791

---

## Text

UHT flavored reduced-fat sour cream is a product consisting of market milk, market cream, and market skim milk to which edible stabilizers, stabilizing salts, and acidified flavoring may be added. It shall be processed and packaged pursuant to Section 39701.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 50. Effective August 21, 1997.)
