# FAC § 66565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 3. The California Iceberg Lettuce Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 66565

---

## Text

Each of the five districts is represented by the following number of handlers:

(a) District 1 has one member.

(b) District 2 has three members.

(c) District 3 has three members.

(d) District 4 has five members.

(e) District 5 has two members.
