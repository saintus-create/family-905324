# FAC § 74796

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 74796

---

## Text

To dissolve the commission, a simple majority vote of the nine board members is required, or the vote of five of a quorum of members present and voting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
