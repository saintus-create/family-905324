# FAC § 6970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 4. Nursery Stock Certificates and Shipping Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 6970

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person, whose right to use any nursery stock certificate or shipping permit has been revoked or suspended, to refuse to surrender to the commissioner, upon demand, any unused certificate or shipping permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 288.)
