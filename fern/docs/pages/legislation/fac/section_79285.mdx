# FAC § 79285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 79285

---

## Text

The commission is not required to allege or prove that an adequate remedy at law does not exist in any action brought under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
