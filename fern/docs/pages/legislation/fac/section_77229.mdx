# FAC § 77229

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77229

---

## Text

“Marketing year” or “fiscal year” means the period beginning March 1 of any year and extending through the last day of February of the next year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 167, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
