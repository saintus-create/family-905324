# FAC § 77731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77731

---

## Text

“Marketing year” or “marketing season” means the period from October 1 of any year to September 30, inclusive, of the following year. The marketing year may be changed by a vote of 23 of the membership of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77728 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 19. Effective July 18, 1995.)
