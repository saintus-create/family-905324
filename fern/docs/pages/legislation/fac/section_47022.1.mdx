# FAC § 47022.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10.5. Direct Marketing > Article: 3.5. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 47022.1

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person when operating under the provisions of this chapter or the regulations adopted thereunder to deceptively prepare, pack, place, deliver for shipment, load, ship, transport, or sell any products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 373, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2002.)
