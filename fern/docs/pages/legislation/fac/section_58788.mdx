# FAC § 58788

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 6. Notice, Reports, and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 58788

---

## Text

If the director finds that a referendum shall be had, the director shall direct that a referendum be held in accordance with Sections 58991 to 58998, inclusive. If a referendum is conducted, the director is not required, with respect to the assent, approval, or favor, to make a finding pursuant to Section 58785 concerning, or to fix and limit, the period during which assents may be received by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 677, Sec. 4.)
