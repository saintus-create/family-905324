# FAC § 55408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55408

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person that is engaged in the business of growing or producing any farm product.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
