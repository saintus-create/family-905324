# FAC § 9156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3.5. Laboratory Services

**Citation:** FAC § 9156

---

## Text

Test results certified by the department are prima facie evidence of the facts stated in the results for purposes of an action pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 9157. This section does not apply to a criminal action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. (SB 703) Effective January 1, 2022.)
