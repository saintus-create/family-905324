# FAC § 19355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 19355

---

## Text

No licensee shall refuse to permit entry or inspection by a representative of the department, or to permit the taking of a sample of products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
