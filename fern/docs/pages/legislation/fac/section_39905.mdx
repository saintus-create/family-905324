# FAC § 39905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 10. Dairy Beverages

**Citation:** FAC § 39905

---

## Text

This article does not apply to any product regulated under Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 38901) as a product resembling a milk product or any dairy product for which a standard is established in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 47. Effective June 6, 1994.)
