# FAC § 25408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 25408

---

## Text

“Poultry” means domesticated fowl and domesticated rabbit which is intended for use for human food.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
