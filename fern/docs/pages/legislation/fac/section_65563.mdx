# FAC § 65563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 3. The California Table Grape Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 65563

---

## Text

Upon expiration of the voting period, the secretary shall tabulate all votes and appoint a commissioner for each district. If all nominees are unsatisfactory to the secretary, the commission shall continue to submit lists of nominees to the secretary until the secretary has made a selection.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 21. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
