# FAC § 17004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 17004

---

## Text

The director may take up estray animals whose owners are unknown and cannot be located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 630.)
