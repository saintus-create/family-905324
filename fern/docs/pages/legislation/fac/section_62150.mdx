# FAC § 62150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 12. Handlers’ Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 62150

---

## Text

After any decision in favor of the issuance or renewal of a license which includes any conditional or probationary orders, if the person to whom the license is issued does not comply with any such orders, the director may suspend or revoke the license in accordance with the procedure provided in Sections 62151, 62152, 62153, 62154, and 62155.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
