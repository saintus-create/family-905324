# FAC § 62189

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 13. Bonding and Payment for Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 62189

---

## Text

The failure of any handler that purchases market milk from producers to execute and deliver the bond as provided and required in this article is a violation of this chapter. The failure of any such handler to post any additional bond as may be required to comply with any provision of this chapter is also a violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
