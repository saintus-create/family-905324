# FAC § 77439

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 3. The California Strawberry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77439

---

## Text

The public member or his or her alternate on the commission shall have all the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member on the commission. The public member and his or her alternate shall not have any financial interest in the strawberry industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
