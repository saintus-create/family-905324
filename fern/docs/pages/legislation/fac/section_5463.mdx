# FAC § 5463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 3. Notice and Expense of Abatement on Tax-Deeded Property

**Citation:** FAC § 5463

---

## Text

If no action to foreclose is commenced within 120 days subsequent to the redemption or sale of the property, the lien ceases to exist.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
