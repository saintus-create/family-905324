# FAC § 71059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 9.5. California Rice Commission > Article: 3. California Rice Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 71059

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this section, the public member and his or her alternate member on the commission shall have all the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member or alternate, respectively, on the commission. The public member and his or her alternate member shall not have a vote on issues regarding the assessments established pursuant to Section 71120.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
