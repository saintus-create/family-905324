# FAC § 6295.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Napa County Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the District

**Citation:** FAC § 6295.6

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may charge the district for actual costs incurred by the county in connection with the proceedings for the formation of the district, and the district shall reimburse the county from assessments levied for those expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
