# FAC § 55021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 55021

---

## Text

All funds received from the assessments levied pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in banks that the commission may designate and be accounted for in a manner prescribed by the commission, and shall be disbursed by order of the commission through an agent or agents as it may designate for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2009.)
