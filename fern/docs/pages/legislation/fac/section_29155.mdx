# FAC § 29155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 11. Wax Salvage

**Citation:** FAC § 29155

---

## Text

A license is good for the calendar year within which it is issued and shall expire on December 31, of that year, unless it is revoked or suspended sooner.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
