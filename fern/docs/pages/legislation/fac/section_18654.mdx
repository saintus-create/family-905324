# FAC § 18654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18654

---

## Text

“Broker” means any person that is engaged in the business of buying or selling any livestock product or poultry product on commission, or otherwise negotiating any purchase or sale of any livestock product or poultry product, other than for his own account or as an employee of another person.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
