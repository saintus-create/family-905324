# FAC § 48002.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 11. Navel and Valencia Oranges

**Citation:** FAC § 48002.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Carton” means a unit equivalent to 40 pounds of citrus fruit.

(b) “Mandarin citrus” means mandarins, including tangerines and mandarin hybrids.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 365, Sec. 3. (AB 1795) Effective January 1, 2011.)
