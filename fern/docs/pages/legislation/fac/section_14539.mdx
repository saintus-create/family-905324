# FAC § 14539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14539

---

## Text

“Investigational allowance” means an allowance for variation inherent in the taking, preparation, and analysis of an official sample of fertilizing materials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 504, Sec. 2.)
