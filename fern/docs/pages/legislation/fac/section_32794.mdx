# FAC § 32794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 5. Reports, Scorecards and Statistics

**Citation:** FAC § 32794

---

## Text

In addition to the monthly report, the director may require annual, semiannual, or quarterly reports of the same general character as the monthly report.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
