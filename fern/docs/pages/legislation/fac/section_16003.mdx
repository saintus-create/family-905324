# FAC § 16003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16003

---

## Text

“Food stores” means fruits, vegetables, or animal products which are carried as stores of vessels and aircraft and includes fruits, vegetables, or animal products which are carried in passengers’ and crews’ quarters.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 41.)
