# FAC § 29674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 10. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 29674

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to place or pack any honey in any container or subcontainer which bears any markings, or any designation of brand, quality, grade, or other matter, unless all of such markings which do not properly and accurately apply to the product placed or packed in the container or subcontainer have been removed, erased, or obliterated.
