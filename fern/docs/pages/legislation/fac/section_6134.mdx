# FAC § 6134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 2. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 6134

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to willfully or otherwise interfere with the enforcement of any provision of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
