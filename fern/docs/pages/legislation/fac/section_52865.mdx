# FAC § 52865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52865

---

## Text

“District” means the “San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District” which is comprised of all the counties in the San Joaquin Valley where cotton is produced.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 730, Sec. 5. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
