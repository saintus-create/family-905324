# FAC § 55253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Grain and Seed Cleaners

**Citation:** FAC § 55253

---

## Text

If a request is made by the owner, the cleaner shall sack or otherwise segregate the cleanings and give written notice to the owner when the cleaning is completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
