# FAC § 27631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 12. Unlawful Acts, General

**Citation:** FAC § 27631

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to violate any provision of this chapter or any regulation adopted by the director pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
