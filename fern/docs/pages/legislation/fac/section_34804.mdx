# FAC § 34804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Transportation

**Citation:** FAC § 34804

---

## Text

Every milk or cream can or other shipping container for any product of milk shall be handled carefully and, while it contains milk, cream, or other product of milk, shall be kept right end up.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
