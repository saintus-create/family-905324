# FAC § 14977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 4. Feed Inspection Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 14977

---

## Text

The board shall meet at the call of its chairman or the director or at the request of any three members of the board. The board shall meet at least once a year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
