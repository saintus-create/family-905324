# FAC § 79660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 28. California Apiary Research Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79660

---

## Text

The commission may educate and instruct beekeepers, the general public, and government on all matters concerning the health and welfare of honeybees and the beekeeping industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 585, Sec. 1. (AB 1912) Effective January 1, 2011.)
