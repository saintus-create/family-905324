# FAC § 18382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 2. Packers and Stockyards > Article: 2. Ownership Against Public Policy

**Citation:** FAC § 18382

---

## Text

Such ownership or control is hereby declared to be contrary to public policy, as tending to create a monopoly and to interfere with trade and commerce.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
