# FAC § 57161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. Dairy Produce Exchanges > Article: 3. Arbitration

**Citation:** FAC § 57161

---

## Text

The director shall act as arbitrator in all cases of dispute or contention which concerns the maintenance or operation of any licensed dairy produce exchange, or the bylaws, rules, or regulations which pertain to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
