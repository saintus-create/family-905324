# FAC § 5348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Quarantine Regulations and InspectionStations > Article: 3. Plant Quarantine Inspection Stations

**Citation:** FAC § 5348

---

## Text

Plant quarantine officers, and officers of the California Highway Patrol, are authorized to cite persons for any violation of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 994, Sec. 3.)
