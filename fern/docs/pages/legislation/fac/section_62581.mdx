# FAC § 62581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund Coverage

**Citation:** FAC § 62581

---

## Text

Only shipments which occur during the first 35 days from the date of the earliest shipment for which a producer has not been paid are eligible.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 476, Sec. 1.)
