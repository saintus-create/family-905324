# FAC § 6465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. SHIPMENTS > Chapter: 2. Interstate Shipments > Article: 4. Abatement, Reshipment, or Treatment

**Citation:** FAC § 6465

---

## Text

If, after the treatment or processing, the inspecting officer determines that the pest has been exterminated, the shipment may be released.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
