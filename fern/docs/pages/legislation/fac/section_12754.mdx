# FAC § 12754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12754

---

## Text

“Insect” means any animal within the class of animals which are known as “Insecta” or any similar animal such as a centipede, spider, mite, tick, or louse.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
