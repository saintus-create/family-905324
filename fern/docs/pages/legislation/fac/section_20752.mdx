# FAC § 20752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 6. Fees and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 20752

---

## Text

The fee entitles the applicant to the recordation of one brand, one duplicate brand registration certificate, and the right to use the brand until the following April 1st. The fee for each additional duplicate is ten dollars ($10).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 267, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
