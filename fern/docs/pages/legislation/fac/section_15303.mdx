# FAC § 15303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 7.5. Structural Pest Control Devices > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 15303

---

## Text

The director may adopt, amend, repeal, and enforce regulations relating to the regulation of devices and the administration of this chapter. When adopting regulations pursuant to this chapter, the director shall consider the safe use of devices and safe working conditions for persons handling, storing, or using devices or working in and about device treated areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 651, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
