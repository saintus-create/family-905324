# FAC § 12255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 9. Pesticide Dealer Designated Agent

**Citation:** FAC § 12255

---

## Text

Every licensed pest control dealer designated agent who changes his or her address shall immediately notify the director of the new address.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 620, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 1994.)
