# FAC § 64010.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 64010.2

---

## Text

“All other usages” means usages other than class I for milk, including grade B milk received by handlers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 63. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
