# FAC § 10007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 5. Tuberculosis Control Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 10007

---

## Text

Cattle moving into a tuberculosis control area must meet the requirements specified by the State Veterinarian.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 37. Effective August 23, 2004.)
