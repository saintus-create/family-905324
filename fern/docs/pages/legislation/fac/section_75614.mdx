# FAC § 75614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 75614

---

## Text

The failure of an eligible producer to receive a ballot shall not invalidate a referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 43. Effective May 9, 1994.)
