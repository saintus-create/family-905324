# FAC § 53391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 4. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 53391

---

## Text

The director may adopt regulations which may be necessary to carry into effect the purposes of this chapter and each section of it, and may issue in relation to this chapter explanatory data and charts.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
