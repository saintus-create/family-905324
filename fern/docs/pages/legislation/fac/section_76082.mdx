# FAC § 76082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76082

---

## Text

The commission may adopt and from time to time alter, rescind, modify, and amend all proper and necessary bylaws, rules, regulations, operating procedures, and orders for carrying out the provisions of this chapter, including rules for appeals from any bylaw, rule, regulation, operating procedure, or order of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 45. Effective September 26, 1985.)
