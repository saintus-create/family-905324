# FAC § 57566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 13. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien > Article: 2. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57566

---

## Text

The notice of claim of lien shall be signed by the lien claimant or by a person authorized to sign documents of a similar kind on behalf of the claimant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 2.)
