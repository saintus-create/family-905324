# FAC § 59288

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 22. Inspection and Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 59288

---

## Text

If the owner of the lot fails or refuses to give such consent, or if the lot has not been reconditioned or the deficiencies otherwise corrected so as to bring it into compliance within the time which is specified in the notice, the enforcing officer shall proceed as provided in Section 59289.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
