# FAC § 10309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 10309

---

## Text

“Reactor” or “reacting bovine animal” means any bovine animal that reacts positively to a test for brucellosis which is approved by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
