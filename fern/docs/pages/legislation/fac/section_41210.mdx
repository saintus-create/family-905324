# FAC § 41210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 5. Wine Grape Inspection Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41210

---

## Text

Alternate members shall serve at committee meetings only in the absence of the member for whom they are designated as the alternate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 92, Sec. 2. Effective May 11, 1990.)
