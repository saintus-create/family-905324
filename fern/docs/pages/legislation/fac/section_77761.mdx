# FAC § 77761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 3. The California Date Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77761

---

## Text

The term of office of all members and alternate members of the commission, except any ex officio member, shall be three years from the date of their election and until qualified successors are elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 83, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
