# FAC § 29587

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 7. Comb Honey

**Citation:** FAC § 29587

---

## Text

Comb honey which has crystallized shall be conspicuously marked with the word “crystallized” on a placard which is used in connection with such honey that has reference to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
