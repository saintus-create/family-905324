# FAC § 76118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 76118

---

## Text

To be eligible for election to the commission, producer, grower-handler, and shipper nominees must present to the director a nomination petition with signatures of at least six eligible producers, grower-handlers, or shippers from the district from which the nominee is seeking election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
