# FAC § 42763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 5. Inspection and Certification by the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 42763

---

## Text

Any money which is received pursuant to this article shall be paid into the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund to be expended in carrying out this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 101, Sec. 55.)
