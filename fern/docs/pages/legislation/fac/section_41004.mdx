# FAC § 41004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 10. Processing Tomato Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41004

---

## Text

The committee member’s term of office shall be three years. Any vacancies which occur during an unexpired term shall be filled by appointment for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 783.)
