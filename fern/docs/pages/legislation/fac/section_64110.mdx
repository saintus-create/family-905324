# FAC § 64110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 4. Dairy Council of California

**Citation:** FAC § 64110

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the council shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of all business, including the election of officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
