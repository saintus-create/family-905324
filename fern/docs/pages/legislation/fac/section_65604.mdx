# FAC § 65604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 4. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 65604

---

## Text

All assessments imposed by this chapter shall be due and payable and shall be paid to the commission by each shipper. The commission shall, by rule or regulations, prescribe the time and method of payment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1467.)
