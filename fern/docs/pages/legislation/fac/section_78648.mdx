# FAC § 78648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24. California Tomato Commission > Article: 3. The California Tomato Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78648

---

## Text

Any producer member and his or her alternate on the commission shall be an individual producer or an employee or representative of a producer who has a financial interest in producing or causing to be produced, tomatoes for market. Qualifications of producer members and their alternates shall be maintained during their entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
