# FAC § 4051.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 4051.2

---

## Text

An association shall not enter into a settlement agreement for an amount greater than one hundred thousand dollars ($100,000) without the prior approval of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 342, Sec. 7. (AB 2490) Effective January 1, 2015.)
