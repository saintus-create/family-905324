# FAC § 17094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 4. Disposal and Sale

**Citation:** FAC § 17094

---

## Text

Any carcass or portion of a carcass may be sold by the director at any time at the market price which prevails at the time of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
