# FAC § 52091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Field Crops > Article: 4. Disposition of Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 52091

---

## Text

Any money which is received under this chapter shall be paid monthly into the State Treasury and placed to the credit of the Department of Agriculture Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
