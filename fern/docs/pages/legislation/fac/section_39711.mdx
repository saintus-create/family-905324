# FAC § 39711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 2. UHT Milk and Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 39711

---

## Text

UHT milk and UHT cream is market milk or market cream processed and packaged pursuant to Section 39701. A harmless edible stabilizer as specified in Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations may be added to UHT milk or UHT cream.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 840, Sec. 9.)
