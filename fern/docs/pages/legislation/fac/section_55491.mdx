# FAC § 55491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 4. Agent’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55491

---

## Text

The director may issue to each agent a separate license card for each processor represented by the agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
