# FAC § 16523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 3. Pedigree and Proof of Ownership > Article: 2. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 16523

---

## Text

Evidence of ownership of a carcass may include a bill of sale, sales tag, or sales memorandum which shows all of the following:

(a) The names of the seller and buyer.

(b) The date of sale.

(c) The quantity sold.
