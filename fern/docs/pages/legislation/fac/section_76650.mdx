# FAC § 76650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 76650

---

## Text

The secretary shall administer and enforce this chapter, and may exercise any of the administrative powers that are conferred by Sections 11180 to 11191, inclusive, of the Government Code upon a head of a department. In order to effectuate the declared purposes of this chapter, the secretary may contract to carry out the declared intent and purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 307, Sec. 21. (AB 958) Effective January 1, 2016.)
