# FAC § 75092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75092

---

## Text

The commission may enter into contracts to render services in formulating and conducting plans, programs, and any other contracts and agreements that the commission decides are necessary to carry out the activities authorized under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
