# FAC § 57305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 10. Private Produce Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 57305

---

## Text

The remedies or penalties in this chapter are cumulative to each other and to the remedies or penalties available under all other laws of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1039, Sec. 1.)
