# FAC § 56609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 18. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 56609

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the licensee was intentionally guilty of fraud or deception in the procurement of such license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
