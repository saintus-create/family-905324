# FAC § 24746

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 24746

---

## Text

Each license shall expire on the last day of the calendar year for which it is issued. The fee shall not be prorated.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 133, Sec. 36. (AB 120) Effective July 26, 2011.)
