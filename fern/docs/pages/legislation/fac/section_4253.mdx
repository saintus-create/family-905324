# FAC § 4253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 7. War Memorials

**Citation:** FAC § 4253

---

## Text

Any construction work which is done pursuant to an agreement shall be performed in accordance with, and subject to, Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 10100) of Part 2 of Division 2 of the Public Contract Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 36.)
