# FAC § 42515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 42515

---

## Text

“Overripe” means having reached an advanced state of maturity which causes the product to be undesirable for human consumption in a fresh state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
