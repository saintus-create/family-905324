# FAC § 41831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 41831

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to make delivery or for any person to accept delivery of garlic or onions for dehydration purposes, or to process any garlic or onions, which have not first been inspected and certified as provided in Article 4 (commencing with Section 41731) and Article 5 (commencing with Section 41751).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 887.)
