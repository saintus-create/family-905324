# FAC § 75507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 75507

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to deal with the broad fields of advertising, promotion, marketing research, and production research of apples, and any other activity authorized in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2.)
