# FAC § 9944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 3. Sale and Use of Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9944

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell or otherwise dispose of any tuberculosis test in the state that has not been produced under license of the Agricultural Research Service of the United States Department of Agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 16. Effective August 23, 2004.)
