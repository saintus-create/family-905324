# FAC § 54434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 2. Unfair Trade Practices

**Citation:** FAC § 54434

---

## Text

The provisions of subdivision (e) of Section 54431 shall not apply to cooperative associations in respect to business done with its own membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 510.)
