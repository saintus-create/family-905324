# FAC § 60015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 14. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 60015

---

## Text

Section 60014 does not apply to a common carrier which is operating over a regular route or between fixed termini if the shipment is made by the carrier in good faith and in accordance with its duties as a common carrier and a record of every such shipment within or from this state is kept by the carrier which shows the date of shipment, character and quality of shipment, origin and destination of the shipment, and the names of the consignor and the consignee. The record shall be open to inspection at all reasonable hours by or on the written order of the official or administrative authority that is charged with the enforcement of this chapter or any marketing program pusuant to this chapter.
