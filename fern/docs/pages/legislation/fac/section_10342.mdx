# FAC § 10342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 3. Vaccines

**Citation:** FAC § 10342

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person, except a federal, state, county, or municipal officer or employee, in his official capacity, or an accredited veterinarian, to buy, possess or use a vaccine or antigen which contains brucella micro-organisms, except pursuant to the regulations of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
