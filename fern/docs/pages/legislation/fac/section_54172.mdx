# FAC § 54172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** FAC § 54172

---

## Text

An association may borrow without limitation as to amount of corporate indebtedness or liability and may make advances to members.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
