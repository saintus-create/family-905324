# FAC § 15078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 12. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 15078

---

## Text

If the seized and held lot, as determined by the director’s analysis, is not in violation, the director shall immediately release the seized and held lot and remove the hold order or tag.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
