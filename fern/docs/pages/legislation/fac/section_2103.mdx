# FAC § 2103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 1. Qualifications

**Citation:** FAC § 2103

---

## Text

The director shall issue a license, for any county which has a charter that provides for civil service examination of county agricultural inspectors, to any person who presents a certificate which shows that the person has passed the examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 734, Sec. 8. Effective September 18, 1987.)
