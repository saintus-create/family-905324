# FAC § 6901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 1. Inspections and Standards of Cleanliness

**Citation:** FAC § 6901

---

## Text

The director by regulation shall provide for periodic inspections of nurseries and may prescribe standards of cleanliness for nursery stock which is produced or sold within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
