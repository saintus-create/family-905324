# FAC § 37101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 37101

---

## Text

As used in this chapter:

(a) “Package” includes cartons, wrappers, or other containers which are used for the sale of butter to the retail trade.

(b) When “numerals” appear following words which relate to scoring, the numerals represent the score of the butter, 100 being a perfect score.
