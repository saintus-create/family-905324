# FAC § 80071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 3. Regulated Native Plants

**Citation:** FAC § 80071

---

## Text

The botanical names of the plants referred to in this chapter shall, in all cases, govern in the interpretation of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70630 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 63.)
