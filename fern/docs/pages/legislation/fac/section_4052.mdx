# FAC § 4052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 4052

---

## Text

An association shall not lease its racetrack for running races of horses to any private person, firm, or corporation, except to a national or international exposition or its affiliated corporations or associations for the period of time which is now permitted by law for fairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1579.)
