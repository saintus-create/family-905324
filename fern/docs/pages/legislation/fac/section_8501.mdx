# FAC § 8501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 3. Organization of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8501

---

## Text

Upon the adoption of the order of organization, the board of supervisors shall immediately appoint a board of directors of five members to administer the affairs of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
