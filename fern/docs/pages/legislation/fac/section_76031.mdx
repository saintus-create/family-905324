# FAC § 76031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 76031

---

## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” means the period beginning on April 1 of any year and extending through March 31 of the next year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 36. Effective September 26, 1985.)
