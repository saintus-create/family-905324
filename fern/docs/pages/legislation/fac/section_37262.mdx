# FAC § 37262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 6. Imported Butter

**Citation:** FAC § 37262

---

## Text

Violation of this article is punishable by imprisonment in the county jail for not more than six months, or by a fine of not more than five hundred dollars ($500), or by both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 119. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
