# FAC § 18962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18962

---

## Text

The department may adopt, by regulation, provisions to examine applicants for the positions of licensed livestock meat inspector and licensed processing inspector.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 680, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
