# FAC § 56413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 12. Complaints by Producers

**Citation:** FAC § 56413

---

## Text

He shall have free and unimpeded access at all times to any building, yard, warehouse, or storage or transportation facility in which any farm product is kept, stored, handled, or transported.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
