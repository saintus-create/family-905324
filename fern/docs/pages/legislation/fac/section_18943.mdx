# FAC § 18943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18943

---

## Text

“Livestock” means any cattle, sheep, swine, and goat, and pursuant to regulations adopted by the Fish and Game Commission, for the purposes of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 18650) and this chapter, fallow deer (Dama dama) whether alive or dead.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 373, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
