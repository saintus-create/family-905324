# FAC § 56254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 6. Financing Charges and Financial Statements

**Citation:** FAC § 56254

---

## Text

Any financial statement submitted to the director pursuant to the provisions of this article is confidential and shall not be divulged except if necessary for the proper determination of any court proceedings or hearing before the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1326.)
