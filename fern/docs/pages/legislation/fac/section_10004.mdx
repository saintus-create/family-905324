# FAC § 10004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 5. Tuberculosis Control Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 10004

---

## Text

The State Veterinarian may authorize the testing of any cattle in a tuberculosis control area to determine which animals are affected with tuberculosis.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 33. Effective August 23, 2004.)
