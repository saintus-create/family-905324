# FAC § 74707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 74707

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to provide those engaged in the production and marketing of grape rootstock the opportunity to avail themselves of the benefits of collective action in the broad field of grape rootstock research necessary to achieve the purposes stated herein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 49.5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
