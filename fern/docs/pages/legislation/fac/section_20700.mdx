# FAC § 20700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20700

---

## Text

The record of any brand may be canceled at any time upon the written request of the person in whose name it is recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
