# FAC § 57131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. Dairy Produce Exchanges > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 57131

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to establish or operate a dairy produce exchange unless he has first obtained from the director a license to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
