# FAC § 77799

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77799

---

## Text

The commission shall annually prepare and submit to the secretary, for his or her concurrence, a statement of contemplated activities authorized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77795 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 55. Effective July 18, 1995.)
