# FAC § 59051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 14. Minor Amendments

**Citation:** FAC § 59051

---

## Text

The director may make minor amendments to any marketing order upon the recommendation of not less than 75 percent of the producer members or handler members of the advisory board, or not less than 75 percent of each group if both are represented upon the advisory board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
