# FAC § 6443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. SHIPMENTS > Chapter: 2. Interstate Shipments > Article: 3. Pest Findings

**Citation:** FAC § 6443

---

## Text

The director may designate certain plants that are not for planting, propagation, or ornamental purposes within this state, which are arriving from certain areas, to be plants that may be released without inspection, if he finds, upon investigation, that such plants from such areas are not liable to cause the introduction of pests into this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
