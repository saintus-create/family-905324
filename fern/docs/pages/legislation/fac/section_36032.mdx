# FAC § 36032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 11. Bottle Caps and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 36032

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person that sells or handles market milk to remove any cap or label from any milk container and attach such cap or label to another milk container.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
