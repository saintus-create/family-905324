# FAC § 57034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 5. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 57034

---

## Text

Each produce dealer shall post and maintain a legible copy of the effective scale of charges established under this article in a conspicuous place at his place of business where it is readily visible at all times during which any farm product is delivered to such place of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 585.)
