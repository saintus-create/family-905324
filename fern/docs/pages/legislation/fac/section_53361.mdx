# FAC § 53361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 53361

---

## Text

The director and the commissioners of each county of the state, their deputies and inspectors, under the supervision and control of the director, shall enforce this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
