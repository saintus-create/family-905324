# FAC § 39904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 10. Dairy Beverages

**Citation:** FAC § 39904

---

## Text

The product may be cultured with safe and suitable bacterial cultures following pasteurization, ultra-pasteurization, or UHT processing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 47. Effective June 6, 1994.)
