# FAC § 32813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 6. Suspension or Revocation of Licenses or Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 32813

---

## Text

The proceedings for the suspension or revocation of a license or permit shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code. The director shall have all the powers granted in such chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
