# FAC § 20407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 1. Bureau of Livestock Identification

**Citation:** FAC § 20407

---

## Text

Subject to the approval of the director, the chief may employ investigators to investigate reported thefts or losses of cattle.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
