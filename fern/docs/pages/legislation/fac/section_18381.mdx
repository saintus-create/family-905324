# FAC § 18381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 2. Packers and Stockyards > Article: 2. Ownership Against Public Policy

**Citation:** FAC § 18381

---

## Text

No packer shall own or control, directly or indirectly, through stock ownership or control or otherwise, by himself or through his agents or employees, any interest in a stockyard.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
