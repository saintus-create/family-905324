# FAC § 77882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 77882

---

## Text

If a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to Section 77883, the hearing pursuant to Section 77881 shall be conducted every sixth year following the industry petitioned referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 85. Effective July 18, 1995.)
