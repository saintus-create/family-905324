# FAC § 77865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 77865

---

## Text

The suspension or termination of this chapter does not affect or waive any right, duty, obligation, or liability that has arisen or that may thereafter arise in connection with this chapter, release or extinguish any violation of this chapter, or affect or impair any right or remedies of the commission with respect to any violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77864 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 81. Effective July 18, 1995.)
