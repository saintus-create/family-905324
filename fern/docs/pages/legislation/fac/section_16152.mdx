# FAC § 16152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 4. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 16152

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to retain or maintain garbage on any vessel, aircraft, or other vehicle within the state, except in tightly closed containers or receptacles and under such treatment as may be prescribed by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
