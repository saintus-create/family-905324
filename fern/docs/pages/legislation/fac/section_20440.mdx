# FAC § 20440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 2. Inspectors and Other Peace Officers

**Citation:** FAC § 20440

---

## Text

Any animal which is seized by or comes into the possession of an inspector pursuant to this division shall be disposed of as provided in Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 17001), Part 1, Division 9 of this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
