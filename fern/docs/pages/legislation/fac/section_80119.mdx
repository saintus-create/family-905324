# FAC § 80119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 5. Harvesting of Native Plants

**Citation:** FAC § 80119

---

## Text

Each county may enact ordinances not inconsistent with this division to control commercial harvesting in that county.
