# FAC § 17044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 2. Taker-Up of Estrays

**Citation:** FAC § 17044

---

## Text

The taker-up is entitled to the sum prescribed by Section 17095 for the keeping and care of the estray animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
