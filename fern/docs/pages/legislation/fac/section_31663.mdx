# FAC § 31663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 9. Potentially Dangerous and Vicious Dogs > Article: 4. Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 31663

---

## Text

All fines paid pursuant to this article shall be paid to the city or county in which the violation occurred for the purpose of defraying the cost of the implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 761, Sec. 1.)
