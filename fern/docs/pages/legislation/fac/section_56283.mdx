# FAC § 56283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 7. Commission Merchants

**Citation:** FAC § 56283

---

## Text

Every commission merchant who receives any farm product for sale on consignment shall exercise reasonable care and diligence in disposing of the product in a fair and reasonable manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 942, Sec. 12.)
