# FAC § 58352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Void Contracts and Restraint of Trade > Article: 1. Void Contracts

**Citation:** FAC § 58352

---

## Text

Advertising allotments or discounts by a seller to a buyer for bona fide advertising expenditures, however, are not included within such transactions which are against public policy and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
