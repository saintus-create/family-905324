# FAC § 64291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 10. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 64291

---

## Text

A person shall not use the same name, or a name so similar as to mislead the public into believing it is the same name, as the Dairy Council of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
