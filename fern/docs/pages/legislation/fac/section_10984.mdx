# FAC § 10984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 2. Garbage Control > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 10984

---

## Text

An application for renewal of a license, accompanied by the proper fee, shall be made on or before the last day of the calendar year for which the current license was issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
