# FAC § 49025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 14.5. California Farm to School Act

**Citation:** FAC § 49025

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Farm to School Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 6. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
