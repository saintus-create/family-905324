# FAC § 29054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 4. Registration and Identification of Apiaries

**Citation:** FAC § 29054

---

## Text

If ownership of branded equipment is transferred, the original brand shall not be defaced or obliterated. The brand, if any, of the new owner shall be placed below the original brand and as near it as possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
