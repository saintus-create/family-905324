# FAC § 9907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 9907

---

## Text

“State TB status” means an area so declared by the United States Department of Agriculture.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 5. Effective August 23, 2004.)
