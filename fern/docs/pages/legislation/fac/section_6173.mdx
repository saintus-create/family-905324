# FAC § 6173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 4. Caprifig Trees

**Citation:** FAC § 6173

---

## Text

Every commercial grower of calimyrna or other Smyrna-type fig may maintain at least one caprifig tree in connection with his planting.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
