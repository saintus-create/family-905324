# FAC § 33223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 7. Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 33223

---

## Text

If a permit is issued by an approved milk inspection service designated by the secretary to a producer of market milk or manufacturing milk, no other permit shall be required of the producer by any other approved milk inspection service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 39, Sec. 10. (SB 1018) Effective June 27, 2012.)
