# FAC § 16524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 3. Pedigree and Proof of Ownership > Article: 2. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 16524

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to refuse to turn over to an inspector any animal, hide, or carcass which is seized pursuant to any provision of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
