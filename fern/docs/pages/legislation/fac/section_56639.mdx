# FAC § 56639

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 19. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 56639

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person to fail to comply in every respect with the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
