# FAC § 21704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Sale and Gift of Cattle, Their Carcasses and Hides > Article: 1. Bills of Sale

**Citation:** FAC § 21704

---

## Text

The failure of the seller or donor to give a written bill of sale or written instrument to the buyer or donee has no effect upon the validity of any sale or contract of sale or gift of an animal nor upon the rights of either the buyer, donee, seller, or donor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
