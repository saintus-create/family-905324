# FAC § 6038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 7. Beet Leafhopper Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6038

---

## Text

The director may receive moneys from other sources for this program, which shall be deposited into the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund and used to carry out the purposes of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 454, Sec. 28.)
