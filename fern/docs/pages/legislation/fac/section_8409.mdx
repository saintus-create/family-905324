# FAC § 8409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 8409

---

## Text

“Owner” includes joint owner, coowner, guardian, executor, administrator, or any other person that holds property in a trust capacity under appointment of court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
