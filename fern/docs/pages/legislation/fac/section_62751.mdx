# FAC § 62751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3.5. Milk Pooling

**Citation:** FAC § 62751

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 62756, this chapter shall remain operative until the secretary certifies to the Secretary of State that producers have voted in a referendum to suspend the operation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 601, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
