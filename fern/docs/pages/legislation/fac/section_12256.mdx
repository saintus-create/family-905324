# FAC § 12256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 9. Pesticide Dealer Designated Agent

## Text

The director may adopt rules and regulations that are reasonably necessary to effectuate this chapter.
