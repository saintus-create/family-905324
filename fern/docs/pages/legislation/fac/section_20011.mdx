# FAC § 20011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20011

---

## Text

“Mark” means a design which is cut into or from the ear, dewlap, or other part of an animal.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
