# FAC § 65673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FAC § 65673

---

## Text

The commission shall reimburse to the secretary all expenses incurred by them in the supervision of elections.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 46. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
