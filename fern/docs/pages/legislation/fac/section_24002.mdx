# FAC § 24002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 8. Drugging of Horses

**Citation:** FAC § 24002

---

## Text

The secretary has jurisdiction of all events under this chapter and shall administer and enforce this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 262, Sec. 1. (AB 945) Effective January 1, 2010.)
