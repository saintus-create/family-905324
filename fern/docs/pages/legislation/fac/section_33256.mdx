# FAC § 33256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 8. Fees Payable to a County

**Citation:** FAC § 33256

## Text

The cost of the inspections may include all directly related costs.
