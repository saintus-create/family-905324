# FAC § 30807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 3. Dog Tags

**Citation:** FAC § 30807

---

## Text

Any licensed kennel may be exempted pursuant to regulation or ordinance from any requirement to obtain a license tag for each dog within the kennel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 361.)
