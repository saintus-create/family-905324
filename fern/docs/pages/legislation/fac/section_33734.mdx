# FAC § 33734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Milk Products Plants > Article: 2. New Construction and Repairs

**Citation:** FAC § 33734

---

## Text

Whenever and wherever the functions which are specified in Section 33732 are performed, such performance shall be with equipment which is determined by the director to be stationary by nature and permanently located.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
