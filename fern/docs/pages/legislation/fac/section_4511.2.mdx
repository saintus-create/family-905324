# FAC § 4511.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 5. Department of Food and Agriculture Supervision

**Citation:** FAC § 4511.2

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “carnival workers” are defined as employees of a carnival.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 36, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
