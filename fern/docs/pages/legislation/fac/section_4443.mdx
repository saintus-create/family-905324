# FAC § 4443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions

**Citation:** FAC § 4443

---

## Text

If a district agricultural association fair is conducted in any county of the state, no county fair, except a county fair which received apportionments prior to September 9, 1953, shall be paid any apportionment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
