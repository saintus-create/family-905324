# FAC § 5564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 1.5. Notice

**Citation:** FAC § 5564

---

## Text

This article shall not prevent the commissioner from proceeding under any other provision of law for the purpose of abating a nuisance that constitutes an immediate hazard to adjoining or nearby property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 164, Sec. 2. Effective April 26, 1982.)
