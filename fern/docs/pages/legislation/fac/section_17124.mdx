# FAC § 17124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 5. Grazing Counties

**Citation:** FAC § 17124

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may by ordinance declare that such county, or part of such county, is devoted chiefly to grazing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
