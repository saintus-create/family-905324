# FAC § 77084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77084

---

## Text

The commission may appoint its own officers, including a chairperson, one or more vice chairpersons, and any other officers as it deems necessary. The officers shall have the powers and duties delegated to them by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective April 30, 1987.)
