# FAC § 65559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 3. The California Table Grape Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 65559

---

## Text

The commission shall annually compile and maintain a list of eligible producers and certify the list to the secretary at least 30 days before the date set for a meeting to nominate commissioners to office. The list shall constitute the list of producers qualified to vote in the election of commissioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 16. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
