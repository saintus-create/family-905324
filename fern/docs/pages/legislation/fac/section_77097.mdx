# FAC § 77097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77097

---

## Text

The commission may collect information, including, but not limited to, industry crop statistics, and may publish and distribute without charge a bulletin or other communication for dissemination of information to persons subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 609, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2000.)
