# FAC § 13187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 17. Healthy Schools Act of 2000

**Citation:** FAC § 13187

---

## Text

Sections 13186 and 13186.5 shall not apply to any agency signatory to a cooperative agreement with the State Department of Public Health pursuant to Section 116180 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 848, Sec. 14. (SB 1405) Effective January 1, 2015.)
