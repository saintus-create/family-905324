# FAC § 10323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 10323

---

## Text

The department may use the services of official veterinarians or contract with accredited veterinarians to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
