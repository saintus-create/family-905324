# FAC § 20757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 6. Fees and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 20757

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), the fee for rerecording a forfeited or canceled brand shall be one hundred forty dollars ($140). This amount shall accompany the application to rerecord.

(b) When a penalty has been paid pursuant to Section 20222, within 30 days of the date the application to rerecord is received by the secretary, the fee to rerecord shall be seventy dollars ($70).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 406, Sec. 5. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2016.)
