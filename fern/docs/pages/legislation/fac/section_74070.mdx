# FAC § 74070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 3. Winegrowers of California Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74070

---

## Text

The public member and his or her alternate member shall have all the rights, privileges, and powers of any other member of the commission. The public member shall not have any financial interest in the wine or winegrape industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
