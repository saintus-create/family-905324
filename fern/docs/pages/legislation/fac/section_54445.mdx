# FAC § 54445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 3. Annual Report

**Citation:** FAC § 54445

---

## Text

The advisory committee shall prepare and transmit a report to the secretary at a time fixed by the secretary so as to meet his or her obligation under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 613, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
