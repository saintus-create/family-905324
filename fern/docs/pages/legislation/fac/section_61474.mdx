# FAC § 61474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 7. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 61474

---

## Text

The person complained of shall, at least three days prior to the date fixed for the hearing, serve upon the complaining party and file with the director a verified answer to all the allegations which are contained in the complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
