# FAC § 12033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 3. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12033

---

## Text

Registration shall be renewed annually by all agricultural pest control advisers.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1276.)
