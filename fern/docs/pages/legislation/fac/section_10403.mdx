# FAC § 10403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 6. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10403

---

## Text

Within 30 days after the appraisal of the animal, it shall be slaughtered under the supervision of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
