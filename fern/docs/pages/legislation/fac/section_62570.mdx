# FAC § 62570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 4. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 62570

---

## Text

All fund activities shall be subject to an audit at least once every two years by an auditing firm recommended by the board and selected by the secretary. A copy of the audit shall be delivered to the secretary within 30 days after completion.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 28. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
