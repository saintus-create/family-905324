# FAC § 604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 4. Buildings of the Department of Food and Agriculture > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 604

---

## Text

The building or improvement shall be administered and supervised by the department in accordance with rules and regulations which are established by the department and approved by the Department of General Services. Such rules and regulations shall be comparable to those for the administration and supervision of other state-owned buildings.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
