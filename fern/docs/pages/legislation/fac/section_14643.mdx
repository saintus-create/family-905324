# FAC § 14643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 9. Inspection, Sampling, and Analysis

**Citation:** FAC § 14643

---

## Text

In determining the percentage of component parts of any substance for the purpose of proper labeling, registration, or determining compliance with representations, all analyses shall be made according to a method determined by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2006.)
