# FAC § 55002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 1. Declarations and Legislative Intent

**Citation:** FAC § 55002

---

## Text

This chapter is intended to allow the rice industry to work cooperatively to maintain consumer confidence and the acceptance of rice produced, processed, and handled in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
