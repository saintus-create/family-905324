# FAC § 30853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 3.5. Guide Dogs, Signal Dogs,and Service Dogs

**Citation:** FAC § 30853

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit the access of any person in violation of the Americans with Disabilities Act of 1990 (Public Law 101-336).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 498, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1997.)
