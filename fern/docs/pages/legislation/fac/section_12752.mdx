# FAC § 12752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12752

---

## Text

“Defoliating” includes killing or artifically accelerating the drying of plant tissues, with or without causing abscission.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
