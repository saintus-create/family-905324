# FAC § 16302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16302

---

## Text

“Animal” includes any domestic bovine animal, horse, mule, burro, sheep, goat, or swine, or the hide, carcass, or portion of a carcass of any such animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
