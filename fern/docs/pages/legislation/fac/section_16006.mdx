# FAC § 16006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16006

---

## Text

“Vessel” means every description of craft or other contrivance which is used, or capable of being used, as a means of transportation in or on coastal, intercoastal, or foreign waters.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
