# FAC § 56352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 10. Claims of Licensees

**Citation:** FAC § 56352

---

## Text

The certificate is not valid as proof of a proper claim, credit, or offset unless it is issued within 24 hours of the receipt by the licensee of the farm product which is involved.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 915.)
