# FAC § 54232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** FAC § 54232

---

## Text

If a member of a nonstock association is other than a natural person, such member may be represented by any individual, associate, officer, or manager or member of it, who is duly authorized in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
