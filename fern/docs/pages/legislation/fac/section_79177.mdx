# FAC § 79177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25.5. California Spiny Lobster Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79177

---

## Text

The commission shall establish an assessment rate to defray the operating costs of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 503, Sec. 1. (AB 944) Effective January 1, 2018. Provisions operative or inoperative as prescribed in Sections 79180.5, 79181.5, and 79195 et. seq. Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 79182.5.)
