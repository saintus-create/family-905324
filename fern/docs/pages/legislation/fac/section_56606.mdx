# FAC § 56606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 18. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 56606

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the applicant, or licensee, has intentionally made any false or misleading statement as to the conditions of the market for any farm product.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
