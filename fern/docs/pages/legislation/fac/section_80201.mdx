# FAC § 80201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 8. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 80201

---

## Text

All fees or moneys collected under this division shall be paid into the general fund of the county in which the permits, tags and seals, and wood receipts were issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70740 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 94.)
