# FAC § 10401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 6. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10401

---

## Text

Any bovine animal in a brucellosis control area that reacts positively to a test for brucellosis shall immediately be identified pursuant to the regulations of the secretary. The animal shall be slaughtered within 30 days in accordance with the regulations of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 653, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1997.)
