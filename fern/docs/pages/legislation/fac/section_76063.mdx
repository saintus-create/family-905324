# FAC § 76063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 3. The California Cherry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76063

---

## Text

A quorum of the commission shall be any six voting commissioners. Except for Sections 76052 and 76182, the vote of the majority of members present at a meeting at which there is a quorum shall constitute the act of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
