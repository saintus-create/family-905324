# FAC § 29614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 8. Extracted Honey

**Citation:** FAC § 29614

---

## Text

If extracted honey is in crystallized form, a sample of the honey shall be liquefied and the honey graded on the liquid basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
