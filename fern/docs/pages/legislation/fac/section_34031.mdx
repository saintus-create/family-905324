# FAC § 34031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 2. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 34031

---

## Text

Any apparatus which is used for the pasteurization of milk or any milk product shall be kept in strictly clean and sanitary condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
