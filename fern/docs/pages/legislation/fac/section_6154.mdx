# FAC § 6154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 3. Caprifigs

**Citation:** FAC § 6154

---

## Text

After the notice, the commissioner shall require the consignee to properly treat the figs for the prevention or elimination of endosepsis before using them.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
