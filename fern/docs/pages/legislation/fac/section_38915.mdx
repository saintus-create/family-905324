# FAC § 38915

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 38915

---

## Text

“Nondairy product” means a product resembling a milk product, but which nondairy product contains no milk or milk solids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
