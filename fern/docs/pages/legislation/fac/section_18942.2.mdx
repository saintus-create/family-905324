# FAC § 18942.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18942.2

---

## Text

“Licensed processing inspector” means a person who is licensed by the department to perform inspection functions under this chapter in licensed meat processing establishments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 680, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
