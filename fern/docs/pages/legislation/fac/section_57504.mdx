# FAC § 57504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 12. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 57504

---

## Text

“Poultry” means domesticated fowl and domesticated rabbit which is intended for use for human food.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 1.)
