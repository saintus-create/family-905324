# FAC § 66681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FAC § 66681

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall apply to any order, rule, or regulation issued or issuable by the Public Utilities Commission or the Interstate Commerce Commission with respect to the operation of common carriers.
