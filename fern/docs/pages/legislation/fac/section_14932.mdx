# FAC § 14932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14932

---

## Text

“Licensee” means a person that has obtained a license pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
