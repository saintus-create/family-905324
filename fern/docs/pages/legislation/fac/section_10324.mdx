# FAC § 10324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 2. General Provisions

## Text

The director may make such regulations as are reasonably necessary to carry this chapter into effect. He may require such reports and records as are necessary.
