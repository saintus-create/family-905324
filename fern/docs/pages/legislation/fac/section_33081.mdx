# FAC § 33081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 33081

---

## Text

The director may authorize as many approved milk inspection services as may be necessary to properly carry out the provisions of this division and the regulations for its enforcement.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
