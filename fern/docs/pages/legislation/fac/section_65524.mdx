# FAC § 65524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 65524

---

## Text

“Producer” means a person engaged within this state in the business of producing or causing to be produced for market “fresh grapes.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
