# FAC § 77907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77907

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to deal with the broad field of production research, education, advertising, market research, promotion, and trade of flowers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
