# FAC § 20601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20601

---

## Text

It is the ultimate object of this division to provide for statewide recordation of brands with the entire state as one branding district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
