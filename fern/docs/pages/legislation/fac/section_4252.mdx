# FAC § 4252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 7. War Memorials

**Citation:** FAC § 4252

---

## Text

The war memorial may be constructed and managed under such terms and conditions as may be agreed upon by the board of the association, with the approval of the department, and the board of supervisors of the county. It shall be owned and used jointly by the association and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
