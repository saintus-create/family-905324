# FAC § 77732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77732

---

## Text

“Process” and “processing” are synonymous and mean the operations of receiving, cleaning, grading, freezing, drying, preserving, chopping, mashing, pitting, slicing, concentrating, macerating, extruding, or otherwise preparing dates for market in any form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 24. Effective July 18, 1995.)
