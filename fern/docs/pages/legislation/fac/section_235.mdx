# FAC § 235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 2.5. Agriculture Trust Fund

**Citation:** FAC § 235

---

## Text

Each agricultural program specified in subdivision (b) of Section 230 with funds contained in the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund shall participate in the trust fund unless an entity is designated pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 227 or Section 6005 or 45021.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 1995.)
