# FAC § 24104.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 24104.5

---

## Text

The Bureau of Livestock Identification shall compile a report on information received pursuant to Section 24104. The bureau shall distribute the report to all county sheriffs’ departments in a timely manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 431, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
