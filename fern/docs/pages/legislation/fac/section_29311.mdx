# FAC § 29311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 15. Enforcement and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 29311

---

## Text

The penalties prescribed by this chapter are exclusive and no other civil penalties may be assessed, except as specified in Section 12999.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 854, Sec. 3. (AB 1752) Effective January 1, 2024.)
