# FAC § 283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 5. Collection

**Citation:** FAC § 283

---

## Text

In lieu of collecting fees in advance pursuant to Section 282, the director may, if he deems it necessary, accept a bond which guarantees payment of the fees. The bond shall be satisfactory to the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
