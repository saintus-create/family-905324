# FAC § 77792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77792

---

## Text

The commission may present facts to, and negotiate with, state, federal, and foreign agencies on matters that affect the date industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 50. Effective July 18, 1995.)
