# FAC § 79502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 27. California Nursery Producers Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 79502

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to nursery products produced only for a producer’s home use unless the use shall constitute a transfer of title between separate business entities held by a single owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 605, Sec. 1. (AB 2695) Effective January 1, 2011.)
