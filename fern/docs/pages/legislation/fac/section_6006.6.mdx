# FAC § 6006.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 5. Cotton Pest Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6006.6

---

## Text

Any decision made by the Cotton Pest Control Board which results in an entity other than the department administering this article shall be made not later than May 1 of any year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 953, Sec. 3.5. Effective September 26, 1985.)
