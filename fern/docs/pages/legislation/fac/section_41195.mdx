# FAC § 41195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 4. Determination of Soluble Solids, Rot, and Foreign Material by the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 41195

---

## Text

Any money which is received pursuant to this article shall be paid into the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund to be expended in carrying out this article.
