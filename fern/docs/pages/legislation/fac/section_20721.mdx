# FAC § 20721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 5. Suspension and Forfeiture

**Citation:** FAC § 20721

---

## Text

The right to use a recorded brand is lost by the failure to pay the fees which are required by Article 6 (commencing with Section 20751) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
