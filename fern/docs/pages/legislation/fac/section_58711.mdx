# FAC § 58711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 58711

---

## Text

The director shall administer and enforce this chapter and may exercise any or all of the administrative powers which are conferred by Sections 11180 to 11191, inclusive, of the Government Code upon the head of a department of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
