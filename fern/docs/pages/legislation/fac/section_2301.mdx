# FAC § 2301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. County Veterinarian > Article: 1. Appointment

**Citation:** FAC § 2301

---

## Text

A county veterinarian may be appointed by the board of supervisors whenever the public welfare demands the services of such veterinarian.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
