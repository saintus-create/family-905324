# FAC § 56272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 7. Commission Merchants

**Citation:** FAC § 56272

---

## Text

When requested by his consignors, a commission merchant shall, before the close of the next business day following such request, transmit to the consignor a true written report of the quantity sold and the selling price.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 632.)
