# FAC § 11223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. ANIMALS DETRIMENTAL TO AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Predators > Article: 1. Hunters and Trappers

**Citation:** FAC § 11223

---

## Text

No iron or steel-jawed trap shall be used by any hunter or trapper who is engaged in controlling or eradicating any bears pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
