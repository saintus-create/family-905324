# FAC § 78650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24. California Tomato Commission > Article: 3. The California Tomato Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78650

---

## Text

The public member and his or her alternate member on the commission shall have all the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member or alternate, respectively, on the commission. The public member and his or her alternate member shall not have any financial interest in the tomato industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
