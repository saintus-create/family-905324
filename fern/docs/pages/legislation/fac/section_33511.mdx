# FAC § 33511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 3. Dairy Farm Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33511

---

## Text

If any provision of this article is violated by any dairy farm, the dairy farm is insanitary, deficient, and unsatisfactory.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
