# FAC § 62308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 17. Local and Regional Producer Advisory Boards

**Citation:** FAC § 62308

---

## Text

The director may appoint a member to fill any vacancy on a local or a regional advisory board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
