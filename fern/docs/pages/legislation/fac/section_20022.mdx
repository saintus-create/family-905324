# FAC § 20022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20022

---

## Text

“Full point of origin inspection area” means any area designated by the director, by regulation, pursuant to Sections 21141 and 21141.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 210, Sec. 2. Effective May 24, 1982.)
