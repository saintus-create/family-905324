# FAC § 12814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12814

---

## Text

Any county, state, or federal officer or employee who sells any pesticide at cost is not required to pay any fee that is imposed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 1997.)
