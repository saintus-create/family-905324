# FAC § 31151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 5. Killing and Seizure > Article: 2. Optional Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 31151

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 31102 and 31103 shall not apply in any area of a county in which the board of supervisors has provided, by resolution, that the provisions of this article apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
