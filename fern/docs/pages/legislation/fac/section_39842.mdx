# FAC § 39842

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 15. UHT Flavored Nonfat Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 39842

---

## Text

UHT flavored nonfat, skim, or fat-free milk shall contain not more than twenty hundredths of 1 percent of milk fat and not less than 8.25 percent of milk solids-not-fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 52. Effective August 21, 1997.)
