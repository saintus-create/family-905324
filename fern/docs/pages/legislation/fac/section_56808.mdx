# FAC § 56808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56808

---

## Text

“Produce dealer” includes “commission merchant,” as defined in Section 56105, and “dealer,” as defined in Section 56107. It does not, however, include a “broker,” as defined in Section 56103, nor “processor,” as defined in Section 55407.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
