# FAC § 18754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 4. Adulteration

**Citation:** FAC § 18754

---

## Text

A livestock or poultry product is adulterated if it has been prepared, packed, or held under unsanitary conditions by which it may have become contaminated with filth, or been rendered injurious to health.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
