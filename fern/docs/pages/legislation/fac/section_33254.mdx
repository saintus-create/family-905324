# FAC § 33254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 8. Fees Payable to a County

**Citation:** FAC § 33254

---

## Text

Any fees levied by an approved milk inspection service for dairy farm inspection shall not exceed the maximum fees provided for state established inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1275, Sec. 1.)
