# FAC § 25556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 25556

---

## Text

Any lot of poultry meat which does not comply in all respects with the provisions of this part and the regulations which are adopted pursuant to it is a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
