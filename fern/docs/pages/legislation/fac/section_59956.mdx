# FAC § 59956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 12. Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 59956

---

## Text

An agent or employee of the marketing program committee shall not have or receive any funds which are collected pursuant to this chapter until such agent or employee has filed with the director a bond in such form and in such penal sum as the director may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
