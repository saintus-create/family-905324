# FAC § 74573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 3. California Wine Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74573

---

## Text

The director or his or her representatives shall be notified and may attend meetings of the commission and any committee meetings of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
