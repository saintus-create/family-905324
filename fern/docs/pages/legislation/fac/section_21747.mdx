# FAC § 21747

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Sale and Gift of Cattle, Their Carcasses and Hides > Article: 2. Public Salesyards

**Citation:** FAC § 21747

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell cattle at a public salesyard or release cattle from public salesyards unless an inspector has made a brand inspection of animals which are consigned or shipped to such yard.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 730.)
