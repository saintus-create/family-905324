# FAC § 14326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 5. Retail Licenses for Restricted Drugs

**Citation:** FAC § 14326

---

## Text

A separate license is required for each place of business at which any restricted drug is kept for sale, and for each mobile unit in which any such drug is kept for sale.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1224.)
