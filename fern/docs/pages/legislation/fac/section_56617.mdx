# FAC § 56617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 18. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 56617

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the licensee without reasonable cause has failed or refused to execute or carry out a lawful contract with a producer or another licensee.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 871.)
