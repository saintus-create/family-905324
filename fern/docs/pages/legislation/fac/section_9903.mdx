# FAC § 9903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 9903

---

## Text

“Approved veterinarian” means a veterinarian who has been granted permission by the department to buy, possess, or use tuberculin.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
