# FAC § 6103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 6103

## Text

“Mamme” crop means the winter crop of caprifigs.
