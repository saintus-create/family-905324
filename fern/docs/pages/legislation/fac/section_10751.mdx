# FAC § 10751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 1. Importation and Sale > Article: 3. Sales Yards

**Citation:** FAC § 10751

---

## Text

Any person that operates a sales yard shall keep records of all swine which pass through the sales yard, including the name and address of every person who offers swine for sale and the name and address of every purchaser of swine from the yard.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
