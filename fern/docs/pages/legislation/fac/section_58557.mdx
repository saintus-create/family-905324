# FAC § 58557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Foreign Marketing > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58557

---

## Text

“Marketing” means the advertising, sale, and distribution of agricultural commodities, including private brands and trade names, in foreign markets.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1189, Sec. 2.)
