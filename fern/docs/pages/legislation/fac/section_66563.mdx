# FAC § 66563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 3. The California Iceberg Lettuce Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 66563

---

## Text

The commission may appoint a president, a treasurer, a secretary, and any other necessary personnel to carry out this chapter. The compensation of each officer shall be fixed by the commission. No officer shall be a member of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 555, Sec. 8.)
