# FAC § 11472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 11472

---

## Text

The director shall keep a separate record of the classes and sources of income that is credited to, and disbursed from, the Department of Pesticide Regulation Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1997.)
