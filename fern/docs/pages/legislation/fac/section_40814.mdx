# FAC § 40814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 5. Inspection and Certification by the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 40814

---

## Text

The director is not required to perform inspection at any place where adequate inspection facilities are not provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 783.)
