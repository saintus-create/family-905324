# FAC § 20692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20692

---

## Text

Any person, upon request to the bureau, shall be furnished information as to any recorded brand.
