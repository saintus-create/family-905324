# FAC § 14526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14526

---

## Text

“Composting” means the biological decomposition of organic matter which inhibits pathogens, viable weed seeds, and odors. “Composting” may be accomplished by mixing and piling in a way as to promote aerobic or anaerobic decay, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 504, Sec. 2.)
