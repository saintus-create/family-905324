# FAC § 38941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 5. Product Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 38941

---

## Text

Any person engaged in the manufacture of products resembling milk products, shall register the products with the department as provided by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
