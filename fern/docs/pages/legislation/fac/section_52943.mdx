# FAC § 52943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 9. Board and Enforcement Funding

**Citation:** FAC § 52943

---

## Text

The secretary, after investigation and due notice, may fix the annual assessment provided under Section 52941 at a lesser amount that would provide sufficient funds to defray the board and enforcement costs incurred in administering this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 136, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
