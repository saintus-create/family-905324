# FAC § 76318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 76318

---

## Text

To be eligible for election to the commission, producer nominees must present to the director a nomination petition with the signatures of at least 10 eligible producers from the district from which the nominee is seeking election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
