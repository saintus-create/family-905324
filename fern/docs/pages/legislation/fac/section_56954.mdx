# FAC § 56954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 6. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 56954

---

## Text

A person shall not charge or receive any payment for unloading any farm product in excess of the standard charge which is established for the market.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
