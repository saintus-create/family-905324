# FAC § 53563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 10. Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 53563

---

## Text

If the owner fails to comply with the order of the court within the time specified in the order, the court may order that such nursery stock be forthwith destroyed or the nuisance otherwise abated as set forth in such order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
