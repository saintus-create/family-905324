# FAC § 19404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 11. Records

**Citation:** FAC § 19404

---

## Text

Every dead animal hauler shall keep a record when and where each carcass is picked up and delivered. The record shall be kept for a period of one year, and open to inspection by any agent of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 187, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
