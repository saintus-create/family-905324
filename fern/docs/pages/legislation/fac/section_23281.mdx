# FAC § 23281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 2. Brands and Brand Records > Article: 2. Swine

**Citation:** FAC § 23281

---

## Text

Brands for swine may be recorded pursuant to the same provisions and procedures as are made applicable to the recording of brands for cattle in Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 20601) of Division 10 of this code, except Section 20668. Such recording, however, is not mandatory.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
