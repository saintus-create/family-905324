# FAC § 25851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 4. Poultry and Rabbit Meat Classes

**Citation:** FAC § 25851

## Text

The classes of the kinds of poultry meat are as established in this chapter and any regulations which are adopted by the director pursuant to Section 25857.
