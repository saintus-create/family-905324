# FAC § 38764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 29. Light Cream, Heavy Cream, Whipping Creams, and Whipped Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 38764

---

## Text

Heavy cream or heavy whipping cream is a food that complies with Section 131.150 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 339, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2008.)
