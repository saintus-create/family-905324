# FAC § 8808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 6. PEST CONTROL COMPACT

**Citation:** FAC § 8808

---

## Text

As used in the compact, with reference to this state, the term “executive head” means the Governor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
