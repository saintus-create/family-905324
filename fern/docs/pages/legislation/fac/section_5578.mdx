# FAC § 5578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 2. Institution of Proceedings

**Citation:** FAC § 5578

---

## Text

The service of the petition and citation may be personal, by delivery to the party on whom service is required to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
