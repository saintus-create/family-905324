# FAC § 58682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 3. Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 58682

---

## Text

This chapter is not applicable to any retailer of any commodity except to the extent that such retailer also engages in the production, processing or distribution of any commodity.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 702.)
