# FAC § 37224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 5. Bulk Packages

**Citation:** FAC § 37224

---

## Text

This article does not prohibit the use of a trademark or brand which has a geographical name if it was copyrighted or registered in the office of the Secretary of State of this state prior to Janaury 1, 1923.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
