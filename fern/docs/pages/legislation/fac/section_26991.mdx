# FAC § 26991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 4. Immersion of Rabbit Carcasses

**Citation:** FAC § 26991

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to immerse or soak the carcass of any slaughtered rabbit in water for a period longer than necessary to eliminate the natural animal heat in the carcass and in no event for a period longer than 212 hours.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
