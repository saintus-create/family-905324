# FAC § 38261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 6. Evaporated Skimmed Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38261

---

## Text

Evaporated skimmed milk is a food that complies with Section 131.132 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 28. Effective June 6, 1994.)
