# FAC § 29073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 5. Intrastate Movement

**Citation:** FAC § 29073

---

## Text

The inspector, in a summary manner, may destroy, where required, any and all colonies, bees, combs, or hives that contain pests that are unlawfully moved within the state wherever they may be found.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 683, Sec. 10. (AB 719) Effective January 1, 2023.)
