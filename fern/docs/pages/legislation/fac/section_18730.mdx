# FAC § 18730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 3. Inspections and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18730

---

## Text

The director may order any labeling or container to be withheld from use if he determines that the labeling is false or misleading or the container is of a misleading size or form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
