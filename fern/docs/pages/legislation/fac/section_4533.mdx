# FAC § 4533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 6. Fairs Allocation and Classification Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 4533

---

## Text

The committee shall, from time to time, elect its chairman and vice chairman. The committee may meet at any time, whether or not the Legislature is in session, and shall continue in existence until otherwise provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
