# FAC § 40934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 8. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 40934

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, any money which is so collected and not used for the purposes of this chapter shall be returned to the processors from whom it was received for distribution to the processors and producers from whom the money was originally collected, in proportion to their several interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 24.)
