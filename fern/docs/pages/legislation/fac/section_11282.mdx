# FAC § 11282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. ANIMALS DETRIMENTAL TO AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Predators > Article: 4. Management of Coyotes on Public Lands

**Citation:** FAC § 11282

---

## Text

Prior to implementation of any control program pursuant to this article, the director shall consult with and obtain the consent of the director of the department which has jurisdiction over the state land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1491, Sec. 1.)
