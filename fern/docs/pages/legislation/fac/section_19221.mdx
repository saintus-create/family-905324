# FAC § 19221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 19221

---

## Text

The application for a license or a renewal of a license shall be accompanied by the license fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
