# FAC § 2242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 7. Offices, Equipment and Supplies

**Citation:** FAC § 2242

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall provide a suitable office for the commissioner, and shall furnish and equip his office with all necessary furniture, supplies, and effects for the proper discharge of his duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
