# FAC § 6298

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Napa County Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 7. Consolidation

**Citation:** FAC § 6298

---

## Text

Any two or more districts organized or existing under this chapter may be consolidated, whether or not the boundaries are coterminous.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
