# FAC § 5744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 3. Prevention of Pest Dissemination by Movement of Appliances

**Citation:** FAC § 5744

---

## Text

The used appliances may be moved to a place which is designated by the commissioner for treatment under his supervision.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
