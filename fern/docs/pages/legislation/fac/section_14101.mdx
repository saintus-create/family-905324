# FAC § 14101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3.5. Environmentally Harmful Material > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14101

---

## Text

As used in this division, “environment” means the aggregate of all factors that influence the conditions of life in or about the state or within any portion thereof, and which are affected by the use of pesticides or related materials within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 97. Effective January 1, 1997.)
