# FAC § 42822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 7. Powers of Enforcing Officers

**Citation:** FAC § 42822

---

## Text

An enforcing officer shall cause the prosecution of any person whom he knows or has reason to believe is guilty of violating any provision of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
