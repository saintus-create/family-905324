# FAC § 35282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Penalties > Article: 1. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 35282

---

## Text

Offering physical resistance or bodily attack on authorized representatives of the department or of an approved milk inspection service, who are engaged in the proper conduct of their official duties, is a misdemeanor, punishable by imprisonment in the county jail for not less than 10 days, without the alternative of a fine in any case.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
