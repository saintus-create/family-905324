# FAC § 33485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 2. New Construction or Repairs

**Citation:** FAC § 33485

---

## Text

No authority is conferred by this article upon the director or any approved milk inspection service to require the rebuilding of any existing dairy farm building unless the owner of the building is making repairs to it to the extent of at least 75 percent of the value of such building.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
