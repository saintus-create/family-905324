# FAC § 62833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. Marketing of Edible Nuts > Article: 2. Records

**Citation:** FAC § 62833

---

## Text

The record and statement required to be made by this article shall be kept for one year from the date of purchase or delivery, whichever is later, and shall be made available for inspection upon request by any peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
