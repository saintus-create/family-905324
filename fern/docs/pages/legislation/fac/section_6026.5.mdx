# FAC § 6026.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 6.5. Vertebrate Pest Control Research

**Citation:** FAC § 6026.5

---

## Text

On or before December 31 of each year, the committee shall recommend to the secretary priorities for conducting various vertebrate pest control research projects and the amount of the assessment necessary to carry out those research projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 176, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2006. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, pursuant to Section 6029.)
