# FAC § 41865.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 8. Garlic and Onion Dehydrator Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41865.6

---

## Text

The secretary shall reimburse the government agency or private entity, upon receipt of an invoice approved by the secretary, for the actual and necessary expenses incurred by the agency or entity in performing the services contracted for pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 520, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1995.)
