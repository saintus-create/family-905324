# FAC § 19002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 7. Processing Inspector License—Meat Processing Establishment

**Citation:** FAC § 19002

---

## Text

The department may suspend or revoke the license of a licensed processing inspector for permitting the processing or labeling of products not meeting the requirements provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 680, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1999.)
