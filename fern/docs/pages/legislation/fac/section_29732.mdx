# FAC § 29732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 12. Public Nuisances

**Citation:** FAC § 29732

---

## Text

The enforcement officer may affix a warning tag or notice to the honey and its containers which are a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
