# FAC § 33764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Milk Products Plants > Article: 3. Milk Products Plant Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33764

---

## Text

The cans or other containers in which the milk or milk products are received, transported, or delivered shall be thoroughly washed, sterilized, and dried after emptying and before being sent out to be used again.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
