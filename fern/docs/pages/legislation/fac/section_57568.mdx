# FAC § 57568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 13. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien > Article: 2. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57568

---

## Text

The notice of claim of lien shall be filed, indexed, and marked in the office of the Secretary of State in the same manner as a financing statement is filed, indexed, and marked pursuant to Section 9519 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 44.4. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
