# FAC § 53483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 7. Labeling

**Citation:** FAC § 53483

---

## Text

Nursery stock on display for sale at retail may be labeled by a sign on any block of stock of the same kind and species. Turf shall be labeled by a sign showing the required correct name of the stock on display.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 622, Sec. 12.)
