# FAC § 74790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 74790

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to do any of the following:

(a) Refuse to render a report, statement, or record required by the commission.

(b) Furnish a false report, statement, or record required by the commission.

(c) Secrete, destroy, or alter records required to be kept under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
