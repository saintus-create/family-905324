# FAC § 19313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 6.5. Transporters of Inedible Kitchen Grease

**Citation:** FAC § 19313

---

## Text

Every vehicle used in the transportation of inedible kitchen grease shall conspicuously display the name of the owner of the vehicle in letters not less than two inches high.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 699, Sec. 7.)
