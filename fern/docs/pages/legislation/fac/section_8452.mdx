# FAC § 8452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8452

---

## Text

The petition may be filed in sections each of which shall comply with all the requirements for a petition, except that a section need not contain the total number of signatures required for the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
