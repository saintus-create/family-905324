# FAC § 19215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19215

---

## Text

“Transporter of inedible kitchen grease” means any person who transports inedible kitchen grease.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 535, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
