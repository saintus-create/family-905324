# FAC § 29080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 6. Notification Regions

**Citation:** FAC § 29080

---

## Text

As used in this article and in Article 7 (commencing with Section 29100) “director” means the Director of Pesticide Regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 166, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
