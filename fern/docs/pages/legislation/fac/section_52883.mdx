# FAC § 52883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 3. San Joaquin Valley Cotton Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52883

---

## Text

A quorum of the board shall be any six voting members or their alternates. The vote of a majority of members present at a meeting at which there is a quorum shall constitute the act of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
