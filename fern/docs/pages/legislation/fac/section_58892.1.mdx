# FAC § 58892.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 9. Terms of Marketing Orders

**Citation:** FAC § 58892.1

---

## Text

A marketing order may contain provisions for research studies concerning the health, food, nutritional, therapeutic, dietetic or such qualities of other food products, for the development of new food products, or for the development of new uses for agricultural products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 217.)
