# FAC § 72132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 8. Continuation, Suspension, and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 72132

---

## Text

Following a favorable referendum conducted prior to April 30, 1988, a referendum shall be conducted by the commission every fifth year thereafter between May 1 and April 30, following procedures provided by this chapter and Section 72131.5, unless a referendum is conducted as the result of an industry petition pursuant to Section 72133. In that case the referendum shall be every fifth year following the industry petitioned referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 26. Effective September 26, 1985.)
