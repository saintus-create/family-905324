# FAC § 12043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 4. Agricultural Pest Control Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 12043

---

## Text

The public member shall be appointed to the committee by the director from nominees recommended by the committee. The public member shall represent the interests of the general public in all matters coming before the committee and shall have the same voting and other rights and immunities as other members of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 459, Sec. 4.)
