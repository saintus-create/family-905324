# FAC § 21112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 3. Modified Point-of-Origin Inspection Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 21112

---

## Text

A modified point-of-origin inspection area may consist of one or more counties or geographical areas.
