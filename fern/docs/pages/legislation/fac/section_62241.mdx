# FAC § 62241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 15. Reports and Statistics

**Citation:** FAC § 62241

---

## Text

All handlers shall make and file with the director at least once each month such reports as the director may require to enable him to enforce the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
