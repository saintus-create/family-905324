# FAC § 38874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 36. Kefir Milk—A Cultured Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38874

---

## Text

Kefir, reduced-fat kefir, lowfat or light (lite) kefir, or nonfat, skim, or fat-free kefir, in liquid form, and with or without fruit added, shall be made from market milk and may be labeled as “Certified Raw,” “Certified Pasteurized,” or “Grade A.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 34. Effective August 21, 1997.)
