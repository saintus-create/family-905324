# FAC § 14011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 14011

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to apply any restricted material for which regulations have been adopted except as provided in the regulations which are adopted by the director.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1276.)
