# FAC § 55104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 9. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 55104

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to fail or refuse to pay any assessments levied pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 579, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
