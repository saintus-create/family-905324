# FAC § 2003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. County Department of Agriculture

**Citation:** FAC § 2003

---

## Text

The California Agricultural Commissioners and Sealers Association shall be recognized as the official representative body on behalf of county agricultural commissioners and sealers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 460, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
