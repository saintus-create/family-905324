# FAC § 58039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58039

---

## Text

The director may act on behalf of the consumers of any product in conserving and protecting their interests in every practicable way.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
