# FAC § 61312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61312

---

## Text

“Packaged milk,” “packaged cream,” or “packaged dairy product” means market milk, market cream, or any dairy product, respectively, which is packaged in cartons, bottles, dispenser cans, or other consumer packages for sale to wholesale customers or consumers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 751, Sec. 2.)
