# FAC § 14082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 4. Methyl Bromide and Chloropicrin

**Citation:** FAC § 14082

---

## Text

The director may prescribe the time when, and the conditions under which, methyl bromide and chloropicrin may be used in different areas of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 606, Sec. 2.)
