# FAC § 61824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61824

---

## Text

“Consumer” means any person that purchases market milk, or any dairy product for consumption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
