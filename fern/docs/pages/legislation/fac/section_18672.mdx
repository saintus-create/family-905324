# FAC § 18672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18672

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, association, or other business unit, and any officer, agent, or employee thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 128. Effective January 1, 1995.)
