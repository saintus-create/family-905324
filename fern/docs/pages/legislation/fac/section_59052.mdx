# FAC § 59052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 14. Minor Amendments

**Citation:** FAC § 59052

---

## Text

The director may require a public hearing upon minor amendments if in his opinion the substance of such minor amendments so warrant. The director is not, however, required to submit minor amendments for written assents or referendum approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
