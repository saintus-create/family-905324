# FAC § 74524.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74524.5

---

## Text

“Eligible volume” means the total tonnage of winegrapes processed in the preceding marketing season by vintners subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
