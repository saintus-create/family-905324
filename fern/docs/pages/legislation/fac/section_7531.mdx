# FAC § 7531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pest Seeds > Article: 2. Certificates

**Citation:** FAC § 7531

---

## Text

To prevent the dissemination of the seed of any pest through the medium of crop seed for planting, the commissioner of each county may issue certificates which state that the crop seed is free from the seed of any pest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
