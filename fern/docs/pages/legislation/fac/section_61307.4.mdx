# FAC § 61307.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61307.4

---

## Text

“Manufacturing milk plant” means any place, structure, or building where a handler receives manufacturing milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 61403 by Stats. 1996, Ch. 759, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1997.)
