# FAC § 5637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 4. Expense of Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 5637

---

## Text

The lien is superior to all encumbrances, existing and future, except liens for taxes and assessments, if a copy of the notice of lien is recorded and a copy is served upon or mailed to the holder of any encumbrance of record, at the last known address of the encumbrancer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
