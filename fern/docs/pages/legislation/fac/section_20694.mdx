# FAC § 20694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20694

---

## Text

A brand shall not be recorded for any person under 18 years of age unless the application is countersigned by his parent or guardian.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1748.)
