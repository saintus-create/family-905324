# FAC § 12602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 5. Seizure

**Citation:** FAC § 12602

---

## Text

If the director seizes any lot of produce, he shall issue to the owner or bailee a hold order or notice. He may affix to the lot a warning tag which states that the lot is so held.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
