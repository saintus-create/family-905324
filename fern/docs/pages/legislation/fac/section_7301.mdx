# FAC § 7301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 1. Weeds > Article: 2. Camelthorn

**Citation:** FAC § 7301

---

## Text

Camelthorn is a public nuisance. It may be abated by summary action or otherwise pursuant to the law which relates to the abatement of public nuisance.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 118.)
