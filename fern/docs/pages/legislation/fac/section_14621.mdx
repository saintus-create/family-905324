# FAC § 14621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 7. Tonnage Reports

**Citation:** FAC § 14621

---

## Text

The last licensee selling or distributing fertilizing material shall submit a tonnage report, on a form or a computer printout format approved by the secretary, containing information on shipments received or deliveries made during specified periods designated by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2006.)
