# FAC § 45031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 3. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 45031

---

## Text

Every person who is in possession of over 25 pounds of avocados that are produced in this state shall possess a record of proof of ownership of the avocados.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 487, Sec. 3. (AB 905) Effective January 1, 2010.)
