# FAC § 20171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 2. Regulation

**Citation:** FAC § 20171

---

## Text

The director may make such regulations as may be reasonably necessary to carry out this division and to protect the cattle of this state from theft and misappropriation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
