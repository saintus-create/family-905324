# FAC § 74094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74094

---

## Text

The commission may appoint its own officers, including a chairperson, one or more vice chairpersons, and other officers it deems necessary. The officers shall have the powers and duties delegated to them by the commission. The office of chairperson shall alternate annually between the producer members and the vintner members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
