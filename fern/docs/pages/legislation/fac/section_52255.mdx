# FAC § 52255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52255

---

## Text

“Labeling” means all labels, and other written, printed, or graphic representations, in any form whatsoever, which accompany and pertain to any seed whether the seed is in bulk or in containers, and it includes invoices.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
