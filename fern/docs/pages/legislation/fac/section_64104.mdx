# FAC § 64104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 4. Dairy Council of California

**Citation:** FAC § 64104

---

## Text

The term of office of a member of the council shall be three years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 78. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
