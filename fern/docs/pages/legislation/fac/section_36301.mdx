# FAC § 36301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 3. Restricted Use Market Milk and Manufacturing Milk and Cream > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 36301

---

## Text

Restricted use market milk, manufacturing milk, or manufacturing cream shall be sold only for manufacturing purposes and to be converted into some other form or product.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 759, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
