# FAC § 79252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79252

---

## Text

(a) To prevent unfair trade practices which are detrimental to California’s blueberry industry, including, but not limited to, deception and misinformation, the commission shall collect and disseminate to any and all interested persons, handler f.o.b. (free on board), market price information based on sales that have occurred.

(b) The identity of each handler reporting information and the information reported under this section shall be kept confidential and not made public under any circumstances. Information that gives industry totals, averages, and other similar data may be disclosed by the commission.

(c) The procedure for the collection and dissemination of the information pursuant to this section shall be approved by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
