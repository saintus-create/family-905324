# FAC § 14081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 4. Methyl Bromide and Chloropicrin

**Citation:** FAC § 14081

---

## Text

The director, after investigation and hearing, shall adopt regulations by April 1, 1989, which govern the use of methyl bromide and chloropicrin as field fumigants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 606, Sec. 2.)
