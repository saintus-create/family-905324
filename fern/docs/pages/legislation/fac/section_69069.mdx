# FAC § 69069

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 69069

---

## Text

Subsequent to the first election of commissioners under this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to nomination and election procedures that shall be established by the commission with the concurrence of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 41. Effective January 1, 1996.)
