# FAC § 11501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 11501.5

---

## Text

The director, and the commissioner of each county under the direction and supervision of the director, shall enforce this division and the regulations which are issued pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11501 by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1276.)
