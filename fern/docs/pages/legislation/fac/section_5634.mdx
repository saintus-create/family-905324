# FAC § 5634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 4. Expense of Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 5634

---

## Text

The expense of the removal or destruction is a county charge which is payable out of county funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
