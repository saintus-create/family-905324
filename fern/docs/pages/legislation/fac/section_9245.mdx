# FAC § 9245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1.5. Commercial Blood Banks for Animals and Biologics > Article: 5. Blood or Blood Component Product Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 9245

---

## Text

The secretary may impose such conditions on the production or use of blood or blood component products as he or she deems necessary to accomplish the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 235, Sec. 19. (AB 1709) Effective January 1, 2011. Became operative on January 1, 2013, pursuant to Sec. 28 of Ch. 235.)
