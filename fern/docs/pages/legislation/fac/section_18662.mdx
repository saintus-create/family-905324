# FAC § 18662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18662

---

## Text

“Label” means a display of written, printed, or graphic matter upon any article or the immediate container of any article. It does not, however, include package liners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
