# FAC § 79866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 79866

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in this article, he or she shall certify the vote and declare this chapter inoperative. The secretary may conduct another implementation referendum vote one or more years after the previous vote has been taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
