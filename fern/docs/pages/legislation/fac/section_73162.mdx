# FAC § 73162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 73162

---

## Text

The commission shall educate and instruct the wholesale and retail trade with respect to proper methods of handling and selling navel oranges, to make market surveys and analyses, and to present facts to, and negotiate with, private and public agencies on matters which affect the marketing of navel oranges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
