# FAC § 30804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 3. Dog Tags

**Citation:** FAC § 30804

---

## Text

The fee for the issuance of the dog license tag is fifty cents ($0.50). The board of supervisors may, however, increase the fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
