# FAC § 19210.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19210.5

---

## Text

“Meat byproducts” means the edible parts other than meat which have been derived from cattle, goats, sheep, swine, or poultry or any combination of them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
