# FAC § 58596.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. The Buy American Food Act

**Citation:** FAC § 58596.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 602, Sec. 2. (SB 490) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 8, commencing with Section 58596.1.)
