# FAC § 59983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 13. Termination of Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59983

---

## Text

In conducting the referendum, the director may require the majority expression to be that of producers in terms of volume of production in addition to numbers of producers voting.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
