# FAC § 14931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14931

---

## Text

“Label” means a display of written, printed, or graphic matter upon, or affixed to, the container in which a commerical feed is distributed, or on the invoice or delivery slip which accompanies a commercial feed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
