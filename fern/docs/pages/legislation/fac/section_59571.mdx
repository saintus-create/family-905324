# FAC § 59571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 3. Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 59571

---

## Text

By reason of the climatic and other conditions which relate to the production and marketing of figs for canning purposes, no marketing program pursuant to this chapter shall be established for figs for canning purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
