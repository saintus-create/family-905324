# FAC § 20282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 6. Collection and Disposition of Money

**Citation:** FAC § 20282

---

## Text

Any money in the Department of Agriculture Fund derived under any of the provisions of this division may be expended for the administration and enforcement of any of the provisions of this code stated in Section 16461, notwithstanding any other provision of law which limits the expenditure of any such money to some specific section, article, or chapter of this division.
