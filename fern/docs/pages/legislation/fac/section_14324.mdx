# FAC § 14324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 5. Retail Licenses for Restricted Drugs

**Citation:** FAC § 14324

---

## Text

If the license is issued, the application fee covers the license for the remainder of the current calendar year in which it is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
