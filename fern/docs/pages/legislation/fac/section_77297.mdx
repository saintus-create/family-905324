# FAC § 77297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77297

---

## Text

The commission shall submit to the secretary for his or her concurrence, an annual statement of contemplated activities authorized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 167, Sec. 8.5. Effective January 1, 2005.)
