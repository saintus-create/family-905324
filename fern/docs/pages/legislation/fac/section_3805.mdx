# FAC § 3805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 3805

---

## Text

“District” means an agricultural district which is formed pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
