# FAC § 59804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59804

---

## Text

In operating any stabilization or surplus pool, the marketing program committee may receive and account for the commodity on the basis of variety, grade, or size classifications, and may fix servicing charges to be assessed against the total quantity of any commodity which is received into the pools.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
