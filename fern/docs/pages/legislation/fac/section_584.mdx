# FAC § 584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 9. University of California Center for Pest Research

**Citation:** FAC § 584

---

## Text

If the center is established by the Regents of the University of California, it is the intent of the Legislature that the administrative costs of establishing the center shall be supported from existing resources of the university.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1642, Sec. 1.)
