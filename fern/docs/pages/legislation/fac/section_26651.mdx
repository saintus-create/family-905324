# FAC § 26651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Sale, Possession, and Transportation > Article: 5. Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 26651

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to brand, or to alter or deface the brand on, any poultry with intent to steal it or to prevent identification of the poultry by the true owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
