# FAC § 14034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 2. 2,4-D and Other Herbicides

**Citation:** FAC § 14034

---

## Text

This article does not relieve any person from liability for any damage to the property of another person which is caused by the use of any herbicide which is named in the regulations which are adopted by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
