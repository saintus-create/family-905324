# FAC § 33321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 10. Inspection of Dairy Farms Producing Manufacturing Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 33321

---

## Text

The director may authorize the inspection of dairy farms producing manufacturing milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 927.)
