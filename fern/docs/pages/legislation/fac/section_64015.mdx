# FAC § 64015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 64015

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person that produces milk and sells it, or delivers possession or control of it, to a handler, in the form of unprocessed milk or cream, or in any other unprocessed form.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 66. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
