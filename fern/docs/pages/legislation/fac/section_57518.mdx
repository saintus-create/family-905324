# FAC § 57518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 12. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien > Article: 2. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57518

---

## Text

The lien claimant shall provide written notice of the claim of lien to the lien debtor within 10 days of the date of the filing with the office of the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 1.)
