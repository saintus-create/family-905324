# FAC § 61316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 1. Definitions

## Text

“Restricted use market milk” has the meaning of that term as defined in Section 32516.5.
