# FAC § 30654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 2. Disposition of Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 30654

---

## Text

The affidavits shall fix the value of the livestock and establish the fact beyond reasonable doubt that the animal was killed by a dog.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
