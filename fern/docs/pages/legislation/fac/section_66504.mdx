# FAC § 66504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and Purposes

**Citation:** FAC § 66504

---

## Text

It is hereby declared to be the policy of this state to aid in preventing economic waste in the marketing of iceberg lettuce, to develop more efficient and equitable methods in marketing, and to maintain job security for workers in iceberg lettuce and related industries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 434.)
