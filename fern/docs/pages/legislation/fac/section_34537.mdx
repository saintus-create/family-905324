# FAC § 34537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 2. Brand Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 34537

---

## Text

If renewal of any certificate is not made within 60 days after the expiration of the certificate, such certificate becomes null and void.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
