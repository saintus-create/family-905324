# FAC § 12502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12502

---

## Text

“Food” means any article which is used for food or drink for man or any other animal, or for a component of any such article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
