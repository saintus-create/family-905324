# FAC § 20902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 5. Cattle Record Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 20902

---

## Text

A cattle record brand shall be used only for purposes of identification. It is not evidence of ownership.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
