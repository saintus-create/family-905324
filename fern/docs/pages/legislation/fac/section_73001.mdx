# FAC § 73001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 73001

---

## Text

The navel orange industry in this state constitutes one of California’s principal tree fruit crops, and thus provides an important source of employment for many people in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
