# FAC § 19204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19204

---

## Text

“Collection center” means a receiving area for the temporary storage of animal carcasses, packinghouse waste, or other products before transportation to a licensed rendering plant or pet food processor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 393, Sec. 2. (AB 2612) Effective January 1, 2011.)
