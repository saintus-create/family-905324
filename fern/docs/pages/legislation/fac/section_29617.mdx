# FAC § 29617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 8. Extracted Honey

**Citation:** FAC § 29617

---

## Text

If the color of extracted honey is designated on the container or subcontainer, or upon a placard which has reference to such honey, it shall conform to the color definitions provided in this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
