# FAC § 40702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 40702

---

## Text

“Processing purposes” means tomatoes intended for the commercial production of processed products such as sliced, diced, quartered, halved, and whole tomatoes, tomato paste, puree, pulp, catsup, sauce, tomatoes in oil, juice, powder, flakes, and similar manufactured tomato products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 2.)
