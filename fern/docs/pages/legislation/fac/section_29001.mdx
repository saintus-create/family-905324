# FAC § 29001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29001

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as, the “Apiary Protection Act.” Unless the context otherwise requires, the definitions in this article govern the construction of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
