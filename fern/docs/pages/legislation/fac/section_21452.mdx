# FAC § 21452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 21452

---

## Text

The chief may provide for the release of hides or carcasses from inspection in cases in which inspection would serve no useful purpose.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
