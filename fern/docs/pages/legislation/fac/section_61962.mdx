# FAC § 61962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 6. Marketing Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 61962

---

## Text

The director may establish additional areas, or modify areas previously established, if he deems the establishment or modification of such areas necessary or advisble to effectuate the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
