# FAC § 20699

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20699

---

## Text

A transferee of a brand shall not use the brand until the transfer has been recorded by the bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
