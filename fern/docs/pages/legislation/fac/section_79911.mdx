# FAC § 79911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 30. California Agave Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79911

---

## Text

The cultivation of agave for processing into agave products and the marketing of agave and agave products constitute an important industry of this state, which provides substantial and necessary revenues for the state and employment for its residents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. (AB 2606) Effective January 1, 2025.)
