# FAC § 52262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52262

---

## Text

“Farm” means a place of agricultural production which has annual sales of agricultural products of one thousand dollars ($1,000) or more.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 706, Sec. 1.)
