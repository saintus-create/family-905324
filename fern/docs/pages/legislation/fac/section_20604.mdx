# FAC § 20604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20604

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to brand any animal except in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
