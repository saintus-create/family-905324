# FAC § 52953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 9.5. Promotion and Research Funding

**Citation:** FAC § 52953

---

## Text

The board shall develop a procedure for collecting assessments under this article.

(a) Collection of the assessment may be required of cotton growers or cotton handlers.

(b) A penalty of 10 percent of the unpaid assessment fee due and payable may be imposed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
