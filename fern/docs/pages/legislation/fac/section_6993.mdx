# FAC § 6993

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 3. Nursery Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 6993

---

## Text

(a) The Nursery Advisory Board shall annually elect a chairperson from its membership.

(b) The advisory board may, from time to time as it deems appropriate, elect other officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 234, Sec. 1. (SB 335) Effective January 1, 2018.)
