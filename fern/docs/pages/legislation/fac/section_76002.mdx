# FAC § 76002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 76002

---

## Text

These conditions vitally concern the health, peace, safety, and general welfare of the people of this state. It is, therefore, in the public interest to protect and enhance the reputation of California cherries in intrastate, interstate, and foreign markets.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
