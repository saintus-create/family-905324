# FAC § 26681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Sale, Possession, and Transportation > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 26681

---

## Text

Any person that violates any provision of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor. Upon conviction, such person shall be punished by a fine not exceeding two hundred dollars ($200), or by imprisonment not exceeding six months in the county jail, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 115. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
