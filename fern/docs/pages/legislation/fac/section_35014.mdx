# FAC § 35014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 3. Licensing of Milk Products Plants and Other Places of Business

**Citation:** FAC § 35014

---

## Text

Upon receipt of an application for a milk products plant license, the director shall investigate the equipment and the sanitary condition of the milk products plant for which the application for a license has been made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
