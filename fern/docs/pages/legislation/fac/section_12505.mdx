# FAC § 12505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12505

---

## Text

“Pesticide residue” means any pesticide chemical which is added to produce.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 717, Sec. 2.)
