# FAC § 9696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 8. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9696

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to fail or refuse to dispose of any property destroyed pursuant to Section 9562 or 9569 in the manner prescribed by the State Veterinarian, when directed or required to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 503, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2002.)
