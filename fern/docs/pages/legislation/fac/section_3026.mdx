# FAC § 3026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Free Admissions > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 3026

---

## Text

The percentage of courtesy pass admissions to any state, county, district, or citrus fruit fair shall not exceed 4 percent of the gross paid admission to the fair in the preceding calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
