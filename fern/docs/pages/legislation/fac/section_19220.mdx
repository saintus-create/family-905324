# FAC § 19220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 19220

---

## Text

A license granted under this chapter shall expire on December 31 of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 393, Sec. 3. (AB 2612) Effective January 1, 2011.)
