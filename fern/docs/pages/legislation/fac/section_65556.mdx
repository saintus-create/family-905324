# FAC § 65556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 3. The California Table Grape Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 65556

---

## Text

Each commissioner for each district shall be elected by a plurality of votes cast by producers in the district. Each producer who has grown fresh grapes in a district in the year preceding an election shall be entitled to one vote in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 13. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
