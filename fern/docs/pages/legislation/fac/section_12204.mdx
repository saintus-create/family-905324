# FAC § 12204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 8. Qualified Applicator License

**Citation:** FAC § 12204

---

## Text

The director shall issue to each applicant who satisfies the requirements of this chapter a qualified applicator license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 503, Sec. 12.)
