# FAC § 27511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 27511

---

## Text

“Egg meats” means the white, yolk, or any part of eggs, in liquid, frozen, dried, or any other form, used, intended or held for use, in the preparation of, or to be a part of or mixed with, food or food products, for human consumption.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
