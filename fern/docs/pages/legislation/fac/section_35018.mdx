# FAC § 35018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 3. Licensing of Milk Products Plants and Other Places of Business

**Citation:** FAC § 35018

---

## Text

Every milk products plant license expires at the end of each calendar year, but shall remain in force during the month of January of the next succeeding year or such part of the month as may be necessary for the renewal of such license by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
