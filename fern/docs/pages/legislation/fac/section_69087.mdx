# FAC § 69087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 69087

---

## Text

Any assessment which is levied as provided for in this chapter, is a personal debt of every producer so assessed. Failure of a processor to make payment of the collected assessment to the commission shall not relieve the producer of this obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 602.)
