# FAC § 4103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 6. Provisions Regarding Particular Associations > Article: 1. California Science Center

**Citation:** FAC § 4103

---

## Text

The California Science Center, in consultation with the Exposition Park Manager, may establish an air and space center in its building at Exposition Park in the City of Los Angeles.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 37, Sec. 8. (AB 1817) Effective June 27, 2018.)
