# FAC § 20693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20693

---

## Text

A brand shall not be recorded if it consists of any letters or characters which are applied in more than one branding location on the animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
