# FAC § 62587

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund Coverage

**Citation:** FAC § 62587

---

## Text

The secretary may waive any of the provisions of this article if, after consultation with the board, the secretary finds that a hardship would be imposed on persons subject to this chapter that is not consistent with the intent of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 38. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
