# FAC § 79824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79824

---

## Text

“Toll processor” means to process or mill olives into olive oil on behalf of a producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 188, Sec. 19. (AB 1322) Effective January 1, 2026.)
