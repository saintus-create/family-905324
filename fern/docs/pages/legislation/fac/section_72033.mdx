# FAC § 72033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 72033

---

## Text

“Producer” or “grower” means any person who is engaged, within this state, in the business of producing wheat for market during any of the preceding three marketing seasons, and who shall, upon request, provide proof of commodity sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 19. Effective September 26, 1985.)
