# FAC § 78831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24.5. Mendocino County Winegrape and Wine Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 78831

---

## Text

“Market development” includes, but is not limited to, trade, promotion, public education, dissemination of information on health and social issues, and other matters, and activities for the prevention, modification, or removal of trade barriers that restrict the free flow of winegrapes, wine, or winegrape products to market and may include, but is not limited to, the presentation of facts to local, state, federal, or foreign governmental agencies on matters that affect this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 597, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
