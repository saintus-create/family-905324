# FAC § 74126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 74126

---

## Text

The director shall establish a period in which to conduct the referendum which shall not be less than 10 days nor more than 60 days in duration, and may prescribe additional procedures to conduct the referendum. If the initial period established is less than 60 days, the director may extend the period. However, the total referendum period may not exceed 60 days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
