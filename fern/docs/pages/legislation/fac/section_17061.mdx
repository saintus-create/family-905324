# FAC § 17061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 3. Possession by Inspector

**Citation:** FAC § 17061

---

## Text

Upon demand of any authorized inspector, the taker-up shall deliver possession of the animal to the inspector.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
