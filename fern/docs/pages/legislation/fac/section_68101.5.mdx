# FAC § 68101.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 68101.5

---

## Text

Assessments shall be based on the total income or other valuable consideration received from the sale of kiwifruit, in whole or in part, by every producer subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 606, Sec. 19.)
