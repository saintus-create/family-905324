# FAC § 35195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 9. Reports and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 35195

---

## Text

Every manufacturer of oleomargarine shall submit to the director at the end of each month a report of the amount of oleomargarine which such person sold or distributed.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
