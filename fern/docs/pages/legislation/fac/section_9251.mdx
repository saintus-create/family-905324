# FAC § 9251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1.5. Commercial Blood Banks for Animals and Biologics > Article: 6. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 9251

---

## Text

The secretary may adopt reasonably necessary rules and regulations for the administration and enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 235, Sec. 20. (AB 1709) Effective January 1, 2011. Became operative on January 1, 2013, pursuant to Sec. 28 of Ch. 235.)
