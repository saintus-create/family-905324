# FAC § 55521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 5. Processor’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55521

---

## Text

A person shall not act as a processor or a cash buying processor unless he has first obtained a license as provided in this chapter or in Section 56574.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 871.)
