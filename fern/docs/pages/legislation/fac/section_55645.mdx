# FAC § 55645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 9. Producer’s Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 55645

---

## Text

All claims in relation to payment shall have equal standing and payment shall be prorated if necessary among the claimants.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
