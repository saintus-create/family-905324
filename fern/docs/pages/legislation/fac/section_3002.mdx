# FAC § 3002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Free Admissions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 3002

---

## Text

“Courtesy pass admission” means any admission, without payment of the admission charge, to any state, county, district, or citrus fruit fair, except any of the following:

(a) Credential admission.

(b) Admission of any child under 12 years of age.

(c) Admission of any military personnel.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 206, Sec. 1. (SB 1186) Effective January 1, 2025.)
