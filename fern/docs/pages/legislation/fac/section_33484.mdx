# FAC § 33484

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 2. New Construction or Repairs

**Citation:** FAC § 33484

---

## Text

Any dairy farm building heretofore or hereafter constructed in accordance with the regulations, plans, and specifications heretofore or hereafter adopted by the director is not, however, required to be rebuilt to conform to any future change in the regulations, plans, or specifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
