# FAC § 26803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Containing Spoilage Retardant Compound > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 26803

---

## Text

“Spoilage retardant compound” means any compound, drug, or antibiotic which is added to poultry meat which retards or limits spoilage. Seasonings, spices, or flavorings, such as salt or smoke, are not chemical preservatives.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
