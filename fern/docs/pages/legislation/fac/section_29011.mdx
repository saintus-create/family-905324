# FAC § 29011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29011

---

## Text

“Hive” means any receptacle or container, or part of any receptacle or container, which is made or prepared for the use of bees, or which is inhabited by bees.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
