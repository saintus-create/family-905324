# FAC § 61473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 7. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 61473

---

## Text

The hearing shall be held in the city or place in which is situated the business location of the person that is complained of, or in which the transaction complained of is said to have occurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
