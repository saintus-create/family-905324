# FAC § 52935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 8. Referendum

**Citation:** FAC § 52935

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 52932, the secretary shall so certify and declare all provisions of this chapter inoperative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
