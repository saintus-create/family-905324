# FAC § 21563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 21563

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, the fee shall be paid at the point of inspection and is two dollars and ten cents ($2.10) for each carcass or hide that is inspected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 214, Sec. 7. (AB 2436) Effective January 1, 2025.)
