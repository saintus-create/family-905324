# FAC § 77861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 77861

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to do any of the following:

(a) Fail to render or furnish a report, statement, or record required by the commission.

(b) Willfully render or furnish a false report, statement, or record required by the commission.

(c) Secrete, destroy, or alter records required to be kept under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 79. Effective July 18, 1995.)
