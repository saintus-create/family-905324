# FAC § 75591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75591

---

## Text

The commission may make, in its name, contracts to receive or render services in formulating and conducting plans and programs and any other contracts or agreements that the commission deems necesary for carrying out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2.)
