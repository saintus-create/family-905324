# FAC § 18668

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18668

---

## Text

“Official device” means any device which is prescribed or authorized by the director for use in applying any official mark.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
