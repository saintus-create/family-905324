# FAC § 76711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 4. California Salmon Council

**Citation:** FAC § 76711

---

## Text

A majority of the voting members of the council shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of all business, including the election of officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1500, Sec. 1.)
