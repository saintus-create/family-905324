# FAC § 77784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77784

---

## Text

The commission may appoint its own officers, including a chairperson, one or more vice chairpersons, and other officers it deems necessary. The officers have the powers and duties delegated to them by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1089, Sec. 1.)
