# FAC § 10100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 9. Tuberculosis-Exposed Cattle

**Citation:** FAC § 10100

---

## Text

The State Veterinarian may impose a quarantine pursuant to Section 9562 when it has been determined that any bovine animal has been exposed to tuberculosis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 50. Effective August 23, 2004.)
