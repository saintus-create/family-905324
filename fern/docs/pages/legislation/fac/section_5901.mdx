# FAC § 5901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 1. Citrus White Fly Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 5901

---

## Text

To provide for the eradication of the citrus white fly, the director may by proclamation declare any portion of the state where the citrus white fly is known to exist to be a citrus white fly district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
