# FAC § 29181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 12. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 29181

---

## Text

No person shall place, in any combless package of bees or queen bees offered for sale or shipment, any food which contains honey.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
