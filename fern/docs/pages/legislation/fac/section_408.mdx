# FAC § 408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 408

---

## Text

The director may enter upon any premises to inspect the premises or any plant, appliance, or thing which is on such premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
