# FAC § 61897

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 61897

---

## Text

A full and accurate record of business or acts performed, or of testimony taken, by the director pursuant to this chapter shall be kept and placed on file in the office of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
