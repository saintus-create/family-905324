# FAC § 29561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 6. Emergency Standards, Containers, Color Classifications, and Markings

**Citation:** FAC § 29561

---

## Text

Any emergency standard may be amended, suspended, or rescinded upon notice and hearing pursuant to the same procedure as is provided for the establishment of such standard.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
