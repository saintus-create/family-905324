# FAC § 17002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 17002

---

## Text

Any animal that is seized by, or comes into the possession of, an inspector pursuant to this part shall be managed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 444, Sec. 2. (AB 1363) Effective January 1, 2016.)
