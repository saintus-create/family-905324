# FAC § 33262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 8. Fees Payable to a County

**Citation:** FAC § 33262

---

## Text

Ten percent of the producers within any approved inspection area may file with the secretary a written protest as to the reasonableness of any inspection fee that is levied and collected from the producer pursuant to Section 33252.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 130, Sec. 102. Effective January 1, 2008.)
