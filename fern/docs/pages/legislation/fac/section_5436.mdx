# FAC § 5436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 2. Notice and Expense of Abatement Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 5436

---

## Text

If no action to foreclose is commenced within 120 days subsequent to the recordation of the notice which is required by Section 5431, the lien ceases to exist.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
