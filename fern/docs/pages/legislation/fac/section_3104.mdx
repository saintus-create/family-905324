# FAC § 3104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Fairs and District Agricultural Associations

**Citation:** FAC § 3104

---

## Text

The citrus fruit fairs and their locations are as follows:

(a) The Cloverdale Citrus Fair, held in the City of Cloverdale.

(b) The National Orange Show, held in the City of San Bernardino.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 559, Sec. 35. (AB 2143) Effective January 1, 2025.)
