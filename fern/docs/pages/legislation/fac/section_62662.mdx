# FAC § 62662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 10. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 62662

---

## Text

The secretary may exercise any or all of the powers conferred by Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11150) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code upon the head of a department with respect to hearings and investigations under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 51. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
