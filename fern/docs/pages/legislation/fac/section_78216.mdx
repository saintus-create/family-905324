# FAC § 78216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 78216

---

## Text

“Market” or “marketing” means to sell asparagus.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1990.)
