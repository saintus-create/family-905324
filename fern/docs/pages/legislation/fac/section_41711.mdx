# FAC § 41711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 41711

---

## Text

The secretary shall initially adopt the American Dehydrated Onion and Garlic Association quality standards for garlic and onions suitable for dehydration purposes. Those standards shall be subject to subdivision (a) of Section 41865.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 674, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
