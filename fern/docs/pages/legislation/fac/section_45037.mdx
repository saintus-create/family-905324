# FAC § 45037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 3. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 45037

---

## Text

Upon probable cause to believe avocados regulated by this chapter are being unlawfully transported, any peace officer may stop the transporting vehicle and request a record of proof of ownership of the avocados.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1068, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1989.)
