# FAC § 21710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Sale and Gift of Cattle, Their Carcasses and Hides > Article: 1. Bills of Sale

**Citation:** FAC § 21710

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to falsify any of the bill of sale information required by Sections 21702 and 21703, or to present such falsified documents to an inspector or peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 938.)
