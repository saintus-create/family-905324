# FAC § 25406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 25406

---

## Text

“Mislabel” means the placing, or presence of any false, deceptive, or misleading mark, tag, brand, design, inscription, statement, billing, invoice, placard, sign or other descriptive designation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
