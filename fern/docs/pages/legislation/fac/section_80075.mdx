# FAC § 80075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 3. Regulated Native Plants

**Citation:** FAC § 80075

---

## Text

Any native plant that is declared to be a rare, endangered, or threatened species by federal or state law or regulations, including, but not limited to, the Fish and Game Code, is exempt from this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70634 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 67.)
