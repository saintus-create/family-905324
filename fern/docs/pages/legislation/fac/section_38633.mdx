# FAC § 38633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 22. Concentrated Skim Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38633

---

## Text

When concentrated skim milk is made entirely from grade A milk, its manufacturer may label it grade A.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 954.)
