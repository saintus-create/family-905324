# FAC § 35926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 7. Certified Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 35926

---

## Text

A county milk commission may establish such fees, and expend funds derived therefrom, as are reasonably necessary to defray the costs of certifying milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 7.)
