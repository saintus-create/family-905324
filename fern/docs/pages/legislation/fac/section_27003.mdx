# FAC § 27003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 5. Moisture Content of Poultry Meat > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 27003

---

## Text

“Poultry meat” means the carcass of poultry or any part of the carcass in the raw state. It does not include poultry meat food products.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
