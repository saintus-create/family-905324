# FAC § 38541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 19. Cultured Milks

**Citation:** FAC § 38541

---

## Text

Cultured milks are foods that comply with Section 131.112 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 22. Effective August 21, 1997.)
