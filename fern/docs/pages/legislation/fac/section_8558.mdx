# FAC § 8558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the District

**Citation:** FAC § 8558

---

## Text

After the final adoption of the plan, the board shall make or cause to be made an estimate of the cost of operating the plan for the next fiscal year beginning not sooner than 90 days thereafter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
