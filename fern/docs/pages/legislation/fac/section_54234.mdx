# FAC § 54234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** FAC § 54234

---

## Text

If a member of an association which is established without shares of stock has paid his membership fee in full, he shall receive a certificate of membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
