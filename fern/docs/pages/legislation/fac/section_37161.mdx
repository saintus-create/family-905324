# FAC § 37161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 3. Butter Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 37161

---

## Text

Butter is the product made by gathering the fat or fresh or ripened milk or cream into a mass, which also contains a small portion of other milk constituents.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
