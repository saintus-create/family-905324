# FAC § 54034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 54034

---

## Text

Any provisions of law which are in conflict with this chapter do not apply to any association which is provided for in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
