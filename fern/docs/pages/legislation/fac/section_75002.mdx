# FAC § 75002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 75002

---

## Text

The protection and enhancement of the reputation of eggs and egg products in intrastate, interstate, and foreign markets, and the elimination of unreasonable and unnecessary economic waste of the agricultural wealth of this state is an essential aspect of the activities authorized in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 243, Sec. 1. Effective July 23, 1987.)
