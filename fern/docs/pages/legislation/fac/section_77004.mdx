# FAC § 77004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77004

---

## Text

The production and handling of walnuts produced in this state is hereby declared to be affected with a public interest. This chapter is enacted in the exercise of the police power of this state for the purpose of protecting the health, peace, safety, and general welfare of the people of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 409, Sec. 3. Effective September 22, 2006.)
