# FAC § 58002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58002

---

## Text

“Organization of producers and distributors” includes all corporations, societies, associations, and organizations of producers, producers and distributors, or distributors, cooperative or otherwise, which are formed for the purpose of facilitating the marketing of any product.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
