# FAC § 8568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the District

**Citation:** FAC § 8568

---

## Text

For each fiscal year subsequent to the first year of operation of the district, the board shall adopt the final budget therefor in the same manner and at the same time that the budget for the first fiscal year was adopted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
