# FAC § 431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 2. Services

**Citation:** FAC § 431

---

## Text

The department shall collect and preserve books, pamphlets, periodicals, and other documents which contain information that relates to agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
