# FAC § 77500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 77500

---

## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter and of the commission, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers, processors, and shippers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77505 (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805) by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 62. Effective January 1, 1995.)
