# FAC § 47013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10.5. Direct Marketing > Article: 2. Certified Farmers’ Market Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 47013

---

## Text

The members of the committee and any alternate shall serve without compensation, but may be reimbursed by the department for travel expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 833, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2000.)
