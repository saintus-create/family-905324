# FAC § 77031.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77031.5

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, partnership, limited liability partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or other association or entity doing business in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 289, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
