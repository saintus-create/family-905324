# FAC § 77733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77733

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person in this state who grows dates of any variety, and who, upon request, provides proof of commodity sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77729 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 21. Effective July 18, 1995.)
