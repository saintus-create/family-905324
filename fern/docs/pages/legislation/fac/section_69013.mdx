# FAC § 69013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 69013

---

## Text

“Books and records” means books, records, contracts, documents, memoranda, papers, correspondence, or other written data pertaining to matters relating to the activities subject to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 602.)
