# FAC § 72112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 72112

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt regulations for the purpose of according individuals aggrieved by its actions or determinations an informal hearing before the commission, or before a committee of the commission designated for this purpose. Appeals from decisions the commission made after the hearing may be made to the director. The determination of the director shall be subject to judicial review upon petition filed with the appropriate court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
