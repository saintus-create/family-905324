# FAC § 79292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 79292

---

## Text

Following a hearing, and favorable referendum if required, the process specified in Section 79291 shall be conducted by the secretary every five years thereafter between October 1 and September 30, unless a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to Section 79293. In that case, the hearing, and referendum if required, shall be conducted every five years following the industry petitioned referendum.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 365, Sec. 9. (AB 1795) Effective January 1, 2011.)
