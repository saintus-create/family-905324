# FAC § 76292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76292

---

## Text

The commission may make, in its name, contracts to render service in formulating and conducting plans and programs and any other contracts or agreements that the commission may deem necessary for carrying out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
