# FAC § 24652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 24652

---

## Text

“Fowl” includes chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese, and other domesticated birds.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
