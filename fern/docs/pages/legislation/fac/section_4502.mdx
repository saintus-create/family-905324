# FAC § 4502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 5. Department of Food and Agriculture Supervision

**Citation:** FAC § 4502

---

## Text

The regulations are exempt from provisions of Section 11380 of the Government Code. They may, however, be filed with the Secretary of State pursuant to Section 11385 of the Government Code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
