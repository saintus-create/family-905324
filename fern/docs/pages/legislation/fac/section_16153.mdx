# FAC § 16153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 4. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 16153

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to remove food stores from any vessel, aircraft, or other vehicle except under a permit issued by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
