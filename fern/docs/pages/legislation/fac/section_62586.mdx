# FAC § 62586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund Coverage

**Citation:** FAC § 62586

---

## Text

The secretary shall, at least monthly, publish a current list of all handlers to whom shipments are not eligible for coverage under the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 37. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
