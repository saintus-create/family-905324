# FAC § 62563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 4. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 62563

---

## Text

The amount of any handler’s obligation attributable to the security charges established pursuant to Section 62561 shall be remitted by the secretary to the fund by the end of the month following the month in which payment for the milk is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 20. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
