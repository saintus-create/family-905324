# FAC § 863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 8. Agriculture Theft Prevention > Article: 2. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 863

---

## Text

A bill of lading, bill of sale, certified farmers certificate, data obtainable by electronic transmission which is accessible to a common carrier, or a similar type document shall be considered proof of ownership for purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 661, Sec. 1.)
