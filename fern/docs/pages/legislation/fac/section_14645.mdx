# FAC § 14645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 9. Inspection, Sampling, and Analysis

**Citation:** FAC § 14645

---

## Text

The secretary may take a sample for analysis from any lot of fertilizing material which is in the possession of any producer, manufacturer, importer, agent, dealer, or user. The sample shall be taken pursuant to regulations adopted by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2006.)
