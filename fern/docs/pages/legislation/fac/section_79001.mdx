# FAC § 79001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25. Sea Urchins > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79001

---

## Text

The production and marketing of seafood, including sea urchin, constitute an important industry of this state that provides substantial and necessary revenues for the state and employment for its citizens.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
