# FAC § 12822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12822

---

## Text

A supplemental application for registration of any additional pesticide may be submitted at any time without payment of the penalty required by Section 12818.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1997.)
