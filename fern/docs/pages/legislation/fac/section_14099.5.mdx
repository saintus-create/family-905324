# FAC § 14099.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3.4. Private Applicator Certification

**Citation:** FAC § 14099.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 1997.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 705, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 3.4, commencing with Section 14090.)
