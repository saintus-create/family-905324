# FAC § 12647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 6. Abatement as Public Nuisance

**Citation:** FAC § 12647

---

## Text

A proceeding pursuant to this article where the value of the property seized amounts to thirty-five thousand dollars ($35,000) or less is a limited civil case.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 861, Sec. 10. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2024.)
