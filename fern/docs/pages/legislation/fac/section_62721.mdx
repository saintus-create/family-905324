# FAC § 62721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Equalization Pools

**Citation:** FAC § 62721

---

## Text

No pooling plan shall control the production of fluid milk except insofar as may otherwise be specifically authorized in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 927.)
