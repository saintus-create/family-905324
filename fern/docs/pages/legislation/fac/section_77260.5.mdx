# FAC § 77260.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 3. The California Pepper Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77260.5

---

## Text

Any at-large member elected to the commission, and his or her alternate shall have a financial interest in the California pepper industry and shall have all the rights and privileges of any other member or alternate member, respectively, of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 167, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2005.)
