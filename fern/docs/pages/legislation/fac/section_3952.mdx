# FAC § 3952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Organization and Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 3952

## Text

Copies of the articles of association shall be filed with the Governor, the Secretary of State, and the department.
