# FAC § 38761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 29. Light Cream, Heavy Cream, Whipping Creams, and Whipped Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 38761

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article light cream, light whipping cream, heavy cream, and whipped cream are market cream products and shall contain not more than 20,000 bacteria per gram and not more than 10 coliform bacteria per gram.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 339, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2008.)
