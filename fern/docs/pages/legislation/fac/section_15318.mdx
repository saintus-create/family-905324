# FAC § 15318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 7.5. Structural Pest Control Devices > Article: 3. Device Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 15318

---

## Text

The director may cancel a certificate of device registration, or refuse to issue a certificate of device registration to any manufacturer, importer, or dealer in any device that repeatedly violates this chapter or the regulations of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 651, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
