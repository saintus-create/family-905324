# FAC § 56103.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56103.5

---

## Text

“Cash” means coin or currency of the United States, and does not include a check or money order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 871.)
