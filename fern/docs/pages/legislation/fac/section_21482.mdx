# FAC § 21482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 21482

---

## Text

If, upon inspection, there is found any carcass or hide which does not bear the recorded brand of the person that presents the carcass or hide for inspection, such person shall show the inspector a bill of sale, certificate of inspection, or other proof of ownership of such carcass or hide.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
