# FAC § 29703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 11. Criminal Punishment and Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 29703

---

## Text

Any evidence which is taken by any enforcing officer in any county may be admitted in evidence in any prosecution in any other county.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
