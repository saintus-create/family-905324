# FAC § 38945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 5. Product Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 38945

---

## Text

In addition to any other penalty, the department may revoke or suspend the registration of any product resembling milk products for any violation of this chapter or the regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 787, Sec. 17.)
