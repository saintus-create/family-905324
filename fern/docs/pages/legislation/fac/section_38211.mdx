# FAC § 38211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 3. Reduced-fat Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38211

---

## Text

Reduced-fat milk is a market milk product. It shall contain not less than 1.9 percent milk fat, not more than 2.1 percent milk fat, and not less than 10 percent of milk solids not fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 16. Effective August 21, 1997.)
