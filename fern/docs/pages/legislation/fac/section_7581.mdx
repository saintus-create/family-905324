# FAC § 7581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pest Seeds > Article: 3. Seed Screenings and Cleanings

**Citation:** FAC § 7581

---

## Text

A proceeding pursuant to this article where the value of the property seized amounts to thirty-five thousand dollars ($35,000) or less is a limited civil case.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 861, Sec. 9. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2024.)
