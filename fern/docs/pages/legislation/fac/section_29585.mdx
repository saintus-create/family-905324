# FAC § 29585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 7. Comb Honey

**Citation:** FAC § 29585

---

## Text

If comb honey is in crystallized form, a sample of the honey shall be liquefied and the honey graded on the liquid basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
