# FAC § 65527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 65527

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, firm, corporation, association, or any other business entity, and includes a state agency that engages in any of the commercial activities regulated pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 3. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
