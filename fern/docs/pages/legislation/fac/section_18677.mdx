# FAC § 18677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18677

---

## Text

“Renderer” means any person that is engaged in the business of rendering any livestock or poultry carcass, or any part or product of such a carcass, except rendering conducted under inspection or exemption under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
