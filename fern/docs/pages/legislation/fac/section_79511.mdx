# FAC § 79511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 27. California Nursery Producers Commission > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 79511

---

## Text

All violations of this chapter shall be prosecuted in accordance with the provisions of this article and, as appropriate, Sections 79509 and 79510.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 605, Sec. 1. (AB 2695) Effective January 1, 2011.)
