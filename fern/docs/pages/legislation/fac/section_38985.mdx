# FAC § 38985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 8. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 38985

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provisions of this chapter to the contrary, the director shall by regulation waive any of the provisions of this chapter as they apply to products resembling milk products manufactured for sale and distribution exclusively outside of this state, provided that the regulations contain provisions ensuring that the products will not be made available or sold to consumers in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
