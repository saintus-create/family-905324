# FAC § 30525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 1.5. Special Provisions Applicable to Counties With a Population of Less Than 100,000 Persons

**Citation:** FAC § 30525

---

## Text

Whenever a dog license tag is issued pursuant to this division, the tag shall be issued for one-half or less of the fee required for a dog, if a certificate is presented from a licensed veterinarian that the dog has been spayed or neutered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 747, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative January 1, 2000, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 747.)
