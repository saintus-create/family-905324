# FAC § 27661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 14. Registered Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 27661

---

## Text

Every egg handler shall register with the director each brand name which is intended for use by the handler on a master container of eggs, other than a container made of corrugated fiber.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 507, Sec. 14. Effective August 13, 1990.)
