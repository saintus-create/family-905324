# FAC § 54173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** FAC § 54173

---

## Text

An association may act as the agent or representative of any member or members in any of the activites which are mentioned in Section 54171 or 54172.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
