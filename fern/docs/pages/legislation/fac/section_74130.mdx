# FAC § 74130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 74130

---

## Text

Subsequent to the action taken pursuant to Section 74129, persons to be elected to the commission upon expiration of existing terms shall be selected pursuant to the nomination and election procedures established by the commission with the concurrence of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
