# FAC § 15077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 12. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 15077

---

## Text

Upon demand of the person having control of the seized feed and within 10 days of sampling by the director, a subsample shall be returned from the state laboratory to the person in control of the feed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 238, Sec. 18.)
