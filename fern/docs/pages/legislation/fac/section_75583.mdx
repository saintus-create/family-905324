# FAC § 75583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75583

---

## Text

The commission may administer and enforce this chapter and perform all acts and exercise all powers incidental to, or in connection with, or deemed reasonably necessary for, the proper or advisable implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2.)
