# FAC § 601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 4. Buildings of the Department of Food and Agriculture > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 601

---

## Text

The department may, with the approval of the Department of General Services, purchase or otherwise acquire real property. It may construct and equip buildings upon the property and make improvements to the property, buildings, and equipment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 602 by Stats. 2022, Ch. 198, Sec. 3. (SB 1499) Effective January 1, 2023.)
