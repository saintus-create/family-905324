# FAC § 27662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 14. Registered Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 27662

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a brand registrant or his authorized agent or employees to pack eggs into a master container which does not bear the registrant’s brand, or to transport or sell eggs in such container.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 550.)
