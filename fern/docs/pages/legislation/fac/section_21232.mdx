# FAC § 21232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 7. Duplicate Brand Inspection Certificates

**Citation:** FAC § 21232

---

## Text

The duplicate brand inspection certificate shall accompany to destination any cattle which are transported over a public thoroughfare.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
