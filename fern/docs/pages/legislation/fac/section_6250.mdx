# FAC § 6250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 5. Levy of Assessments for District Purposes

**Citation:** FAC § 6250

---

## Text

The county assessor, in making the annual assessment of property included in the district each and every year after the organization of the district, shall identify any parcel of real property with one acre or more of winegrape plants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
