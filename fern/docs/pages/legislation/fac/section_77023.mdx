# FAC § 77023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77023

---

## Text

“Books and records” means books, records, contracts, documents, memoranda, papers, correspondence, or other written data pertaining to matters relating to the activities subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective April 30, 1987.)
