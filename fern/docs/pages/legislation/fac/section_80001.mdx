# FAC § 80001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 80001

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Desert Native Plants Act.
