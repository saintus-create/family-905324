# FAC § 17001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 17001

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “taker-up” means any person that takes up an estray animal pursuant to Section 17041.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
