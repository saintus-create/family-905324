# FAC § 65061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2.5. California Cattle Council Law > Article: 5. Implementation and Suspension of Operations

**Citation:** FAC § 65061

---

## Text

This chapter, except as necessary to conduct an implementation referendum vote, shall not become operative until the secretary finds that a majority of producers participating in an initial referendum vote conducted by the secretary on or before June 1, 2020, or a second referendum vote conducted by the secretary pursuant to Section 65066, approve the referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 410, Sec. 2. (SB 965) Effective January 1, 2019. Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 65066.)
