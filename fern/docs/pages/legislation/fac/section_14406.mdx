# FAC § 14406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4.5. Livestock: Use of Antimicrobial Drugs

**Citation:** FAC § 14406

---

## Text

The department has the authority to request and receive copies of veterinary feed directives from the livestock owner, veterinarian, or distributor to fully implement the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 758, Sec. 1. (SB 27) Effective January 1, 2016.)
