# FAC § 19211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19211

---

## Text

“Pet” means any household animal including but not limited to cats or dogs and other carnivora, whether or not for public exhibition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
