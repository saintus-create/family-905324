# FAC § 6047.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.65

---

## Text

The petition may be filed in sections, each of which shall comply with all the requirements for a petition, except that a section need not contain the total number of signatures required for the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 244, Sec. 1. Effective August 29, 2003.)
