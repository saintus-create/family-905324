# FAC § 64304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 11. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 64304

---

## Text

The assessments established and assessed as prescribed in this chapter shall be billed monthly to the handler and shall be paid to the secretary no later than the 15th day of the second month following the month during which the milk was received.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 19. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
