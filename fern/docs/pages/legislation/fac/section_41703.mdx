# FAC § 41703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 41703

---

## Text

“Dehydration purposes” means the commercial dehydration of garlic and onions.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 887.)
