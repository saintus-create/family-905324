# FAC § 21457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 21457

---

## Text

A person shall not remove any evidence of inspection from a hide until after it has been partially tanned.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
