# FAC § 65025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2.5. California Cattle Council Law > Article: 3. California Cattle Council

**Citation:** FAC § 65025

---

## Text

The secretary or the secretary’s representative shall be notified and may attend each meeting of the council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 410, Sec. 2. (SB 965) Effective January 1, 2019. Provisions operative or inoperative as prescribed in Sections 65061, 65066, 65068, and 65069. Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 65066.)
