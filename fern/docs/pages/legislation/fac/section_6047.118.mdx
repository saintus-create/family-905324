# FAC § 6047.118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.118

---

## Text

If, at the hearing, a majority of the board of supervisors does not find a compelling reason to override the owners’ petition to dissolve the district, the board of supervisors shall by resolution dissolve the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 400, Sec. 26. Effective September 9, 2004.)
