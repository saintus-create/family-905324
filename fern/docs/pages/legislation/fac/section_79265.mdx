# FAC § 79265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 79265

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 79262, he or she shall so certify and give notice of the vote to all producers and handlers whose names and addresses are on file with the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
