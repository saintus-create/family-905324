# FAC § 33522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 3. Dairy Farm Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33522

---

## Text

No container, utensil, appliance, or equipment shall be used for any purpose except the handling of milk or products of milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
