# FAC § 64043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 64043

---

## Text

This chapter shall be liberally construed. If any section, sentence, clause, or part of this chapter is, for any reason, held to be unconstitutional or invalid, that decision does not affect the remaining portion of this chapter but the unconstitutional or invalid provision is separable from the valid parts of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 72. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
