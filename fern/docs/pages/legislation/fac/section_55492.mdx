# FAC § 55492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 4. Agent’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55492

---

## Text

Any agent that displays a void or expired license card is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
