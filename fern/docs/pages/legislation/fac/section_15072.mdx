# FAC § 15072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 12. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 15072

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to manufacture or distribute in this state any commercial feed without complying with the provisions of this chapter and the regulations which are adopted pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
