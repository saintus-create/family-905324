# FAC § 301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 6. Refunds

**Citation:** FAC § 301

---

## Text

As used in this article, “fee” includes, but is not limited to, any application fee, license fee, permit fee, inspection fee, certification fee, registration fee, identification fee, analysis fee, or certificate fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
