# FAC § 27665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 14. Registered Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 27665

---

## Text

The director shall issue licenses to persons engaged in the container exchange business for master containers of eggs pursuant to such regulations as the director deems necessary to protect the shell egg industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 550.)
