# FAC § 35601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 35601

---

## Text

Milk shall be produced from nonreacting tuberculin and brucellosis tested cows or goats and shall conform to the regulations adopted by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 771.)
