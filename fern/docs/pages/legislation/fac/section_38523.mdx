# FAC § 38523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 17. Milk, Reduced-Fat Milk, Lowfat Milk, or Light Milk, and Nonfat Milk, Skim Milk, or Fat-Free Milk with Lactobacillus Acidophilus Culture Added

**Citation:** FAC § 38523

---

## Text

The manufacture, labeling, and sale of acidophilus milk and milk, reduced-fat milk, lowfat or light (lite) milk, and nonfat or fat-free milk with lactobacillus acidophilus culture added shall be in accordance with regulations adopted by the secretary after a hearing that is duly called. Interested parties shall be notified of the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 21. Effective August 21, 1997.)
