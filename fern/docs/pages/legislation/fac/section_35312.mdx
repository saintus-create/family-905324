# FAC § 35312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Penalties > Article: 2. Civil Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 35312

---

## Text

To the amount of any fee, except a license fee, which is required by this division, that is not paid by the last day of the month following that in which due, there shall be added a penalty of 25 percent of the unpaid amount of such fee, or five dollars ($5), whichever is greater, for each month or fraction of a month until the entire fee and penalty are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
