# FAC § 57553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 13. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 57553

---

## Text

“Labor” means labor or service performed in the application, delivery, or preparation of an agricultural chemical or agricultural seed provided by the seller of the agricultural chemical or agricultural seed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 2.)
