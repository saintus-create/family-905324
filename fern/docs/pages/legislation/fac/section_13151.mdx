# FAC § 13151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 15. The Pesticide Contamination Prevention Act

**Citation:** FAC § 13151

---

## Text

Any pesticide identified pursuant to Section 13149 that fails to meet any of the conditions of Section 13150 shall be canceled.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 92. Effective January 1, 1997.)
