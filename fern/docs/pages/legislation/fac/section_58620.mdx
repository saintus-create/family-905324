# FAC § 58620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 58620

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person that is engaged within this state in the business of producing, or causing to be produced for market, any commodity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
