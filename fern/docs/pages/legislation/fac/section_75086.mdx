# FAC § 75086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

## Text

The commission shall fix the compensation for all employees of the commission.
