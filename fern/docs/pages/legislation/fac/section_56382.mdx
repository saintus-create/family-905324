# FAC § 56382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 11. Examinations and Audits

**Citation:** FAC § 56382

---

## Text

If the examination discloses evidence of any violation of this chapter, the department may issue a complaint detailing the charges and the discipline sought in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 696, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 1998.)
