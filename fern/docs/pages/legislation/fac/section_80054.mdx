# FAC § 80054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 80054

---

## Text

“Harvester” means a person who harvests a native plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70603 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 52.)
