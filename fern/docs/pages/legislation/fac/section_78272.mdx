# FAC § 78272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 78272

---

## Text

The secretary shall establish a period to conduct the referendum that shall not be less than 10 days nor more than 60 days in duration, and may prescribe additional procedures that may be necessary to conduct the referendum. If the initial period established is less than 60 days, the secretary may extend the period to not more than 60 days.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 163. Effective January 1, 1996.)
