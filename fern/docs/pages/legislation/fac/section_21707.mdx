# FAC § 21707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Sale and Gift of Cattle, Their Carcasses and Hides > Article: 1. Bills of Sale

**Citation:** FAC § 21707

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 21706, it is unlawful for any person to alter, deface or change any invoice of sale or bill of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
