# FAC § 79025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25. Sea Urchins > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79025

---

## Text

“Sea urchin” means the following species of fish:

(a) Red sea urchin (Strongylocentrotus franciscanus).

(b) Purple sea urchin (Strongylocentrotus purpuratus).

(c) Any other species of sea urchin authorized for commercial landing in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
