# FAC § 41000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 10. Processing Tomato Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41000

## Text

The director shall not enforce this article during the period that any marketing order or commission covering the same subject is in effect.
