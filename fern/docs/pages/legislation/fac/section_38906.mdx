# FAC § 38906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 38906

---

## Text

If a provision of this chapter and a provision of the Health and Safety Code are applicable to the same person and subject matter, the provisions of this chapter shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
