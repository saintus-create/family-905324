# FAC § 38937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 4. Plant Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 38937

---

## Text

Grounds for revocation or suspension of such license shall be the manufacture of products resembling milk products under unhealthful or insanitary conditions or which violate the provisions of Section 38924 or 38925.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
