# FAC § 25127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 6. Marks and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 25127

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell or mark as inspected for wholesomeness any poultry meat that has not been inspected and passed for wholesomeness pursuant to this chapter and regulations which are adopted pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
