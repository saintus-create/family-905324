# FAC § 64005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 64005

---

## Text

“Dairy products” includes milk and any derivative of milk that is in natural, processed, or manufactured form, or that constitutes a part of another article, commodity, or product.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 57. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
