# FAC § 41207.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 5. Wine Grape Inspection Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41207.8

---

## Text

The committee shall not recommend and the director shall not utilize, an entity a majority of whose officers and employees are persons regulated by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 319, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1993.)
