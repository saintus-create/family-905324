# FAC § 27632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 12. Unlawful Acts, General

**Citation:** FAC § 27632

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to mark any eggs which were not produced in this state “Produced in California” or represent them in any manner to be a product of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
