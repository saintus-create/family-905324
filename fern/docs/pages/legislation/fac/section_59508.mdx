# FAC § 59508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 59508

---

## Text

“Marketing program” means any program which contains provisions which are authorized by Article 8 (commencing with Section 59801) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
