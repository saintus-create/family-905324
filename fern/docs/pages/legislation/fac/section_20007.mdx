# FAC § 20007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20007

---

## Text

“Cattle record brand” means a brand used to classify an animal for the purpose of keeping records which relate to the animal.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
