# FAC § 79295

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 79295

---

## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter and of the commission, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers and handlers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
