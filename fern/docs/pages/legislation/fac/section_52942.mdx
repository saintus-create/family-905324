# FAC § 52942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 9. Board and Enforcement Funding

**Citation:** FAC § 52942

---

## Text

The assessment shall be used exclusively for enforcement of the provisions of this chapter, and the board functions including cotton variety testing and related administrative expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 592. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
