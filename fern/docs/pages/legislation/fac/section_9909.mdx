# FAC § 9909

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 9909

---

## Text

“Purebred animal” means an animal which is identified in the herd book of a breed record association that is recognized by the United States Department of Agriculture.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
