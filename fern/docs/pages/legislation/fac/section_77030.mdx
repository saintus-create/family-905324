# FAC § 77030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77030

---

## Text

“Marketing research” means any research relating to the marketing of walnuts in domestic or foreign markets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 609, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2000.)
