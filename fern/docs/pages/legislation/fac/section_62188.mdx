# FAC § 62188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 13. Bonding and Payment for Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 62188

---

## Text

If recovery upon the bond is not sufficient to pay all of the claims as finally determined and adjudged by the court, any such amount recovered shall be divided pro rata among the producer-creditors.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
