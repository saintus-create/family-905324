# FAC § 24110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 24110

---

## Text

An inspector shall seize any animal for which a valid bill of sale containing the information designated in Section 24106 is not provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 747, Sec. 2.)
