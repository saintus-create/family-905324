# FAC § 9902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 9902

---

## Text

“Accredited herd” means a herd of cattle for which the owner holds a valid certificate of accreditation issued to him by the United States Department of Agriculture and the department.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
