# FAC § 46027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46027

---

## Text

No food or product shall be advertised or labeled as “organic when available,” or “better than organic,” or use terminology that leaves in doubt whether the food or product being sold is organic.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 456, Sec. 14. (SB 1117) Effective January 1, 2025.)
