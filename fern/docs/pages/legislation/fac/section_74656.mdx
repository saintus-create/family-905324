# FAC § 74656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 74656

---

## Text

Any assessment levied by the commission, pursuant to this chapter, is a personal debt of every person so assessed and is due and payable in a time and manner prescribed by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
