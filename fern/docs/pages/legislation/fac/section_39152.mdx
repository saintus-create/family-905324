# FAC § 39152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Imitation Ice Cream and Ice Milk > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 39152

---

## Text

Imitation ice cream and imitation ice milk shall otherwise be manufactured, advertised, and sold pursuant to regulations which are adopted by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
