# FAC § 15331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 7.5. Structural Pest Control Devices > Article: 6. Device Usage

**Citation:** FAC § 15331

---

## Text

The use of any device shall not conflict with the label on the device as registered pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 651, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
