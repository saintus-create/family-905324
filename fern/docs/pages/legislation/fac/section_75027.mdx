# FAC § 75027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75027

---

## Text

“Eggs” means eggs from domesticated chickens handled in California for human consumption in shell egg form, except those eggs used for hatchery purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 121, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
