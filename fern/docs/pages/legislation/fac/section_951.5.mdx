# FAC § 951.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. THE STATE BOARD OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 951.5

---

## Text

The board shall make recommendations to the secretary regarding the project agreements to be funded pursuant to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 58551) of Part 1 of Division 21.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 982, Sec. 2. Effective September 27, 2002.)
