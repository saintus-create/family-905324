# FAC § 9503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 9503

---

## Text

“Poultry” includes all domesticated fowl and any wild fowl or bird which is reduced to captivity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
