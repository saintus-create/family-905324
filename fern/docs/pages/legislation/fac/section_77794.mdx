# FAC § 77794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77794

---

## Text

The commission may educate and instruct the wholesale and retail trade with respect to proper methods of handling and selling dates, and conduct market surveys and analyses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 54. Effective July 18, 1995.)
