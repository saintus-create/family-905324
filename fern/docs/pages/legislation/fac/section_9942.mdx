# FAC § 9942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 3. Sale and Use of Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9942

---

## Text

Any sale or disposition of tuberculosis tests within the state shall be reported to the department within five days after the sale or disposition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 14. Effective August 23, 2004.)
