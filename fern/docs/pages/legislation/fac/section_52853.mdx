# FAC § 52853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 52853

---

## Text

The Legislature declares that the development of varieties or species of cotton which meet the desired quality of Acala or Pima cotton should be encouraged, if reasonable restrictions are imposed to prevent contamination and preserve integrity of the high quality cotton produced within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 730, Sec. 3. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
