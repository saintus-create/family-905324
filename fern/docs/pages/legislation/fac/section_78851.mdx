# FAC § 78851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24.5. Mendocino County Winegrape and Wine Commission > Article: 3. The Mendocino Winegrape and Wine Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78851

---

## Text

(a) There is in the state government the Mendocino Winegrape and Wine Commission. The commission shall be composed of six producers who are not vintners, four vintners, and one public member.

(b) The public member and his or her alternate shall be appointed to the commission by the secretary from nominees recommended by the commission.

(c) The secretary shall be a nonvoting, ex officio member of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 597, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
