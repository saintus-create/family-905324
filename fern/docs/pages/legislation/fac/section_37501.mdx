# FAC § 37501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. Cheese > Article: 2. Cottage Cheese

**Citation:** FAC § 37501

---

## Text

Dry curd cottage cheese is a food that complies with Section 133.129 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 23. Effective June 6, 1994.)
