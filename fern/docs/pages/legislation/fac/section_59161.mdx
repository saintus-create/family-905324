# FAC § 59161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 18. Administrative Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 59161

---

## Text

Upon recommendation of the advisory board which is concerned, the director may, without prior notice and hearing, establish administrative rules and regulations for each marketing order or marketing agreement which is issued and made effective as may be necessary to facilitate the administration and enforcement of each such order or agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
