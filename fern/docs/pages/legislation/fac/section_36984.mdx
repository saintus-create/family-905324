# FAC § 36984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Frozen Dairy Products > Article: 6. Quiescently Frozen Confections

**Citation:** FAC § 36984

---

## Text

In the production of a quiescently frozen confection, no processing or mixing prior to the quiescent freezing shall be used that develops in the finished confection mix any physical expansion in excess of 10 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
