# FAC § 56279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 7. Commission Merchants

**Citation:** FAC § 56279

---

## Text

The burden of proof shall be upon the commission merchant to prove the correctness of any accounting required to be performed by the commission merchant pursuant to this chapter as to any transaction which may be questioned.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1081, Sec. 3.)
