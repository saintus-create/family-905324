# FAC § 69040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 3. The California Pistachio Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 69040

---

## Text

A quorum of the commission shall be any five voting commissioners. Except as provided in Sections 69019 and 69102, the vote of a majority of members present at a meeting at which there is quorum shall constitute the act of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 602.)
