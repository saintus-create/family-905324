# FAC § 38453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 14. Milk Drink Mix

**Citation:** FAC § 38453

---

## Text

Milk drink mix may contain sweetening and harmless flavoring and coloring.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
