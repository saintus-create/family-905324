# FAC § 954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. THE STATE BOARD OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 954

---

## Text

Special or adjourned meetings may be held at the office of the director in Sacramento, or at any other place in the state which the president may deem necessary. Special meetings of the board shall also be called by the president at any time upon the written request of four members of the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 325.)
