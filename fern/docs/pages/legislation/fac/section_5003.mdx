# FAC § 5003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 5003

---

## Text

“Crop seed” means the seed or seedlike fruit of grain, beans, flax, beets, onions, or any other crop, whether or not it is intended for planting purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
