# FAC § 77319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 77319

---

## Text

Subsequent to the first election of members of the commission pursuant to this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to nomination and election procedures that are established by the commission with the concurrence of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 167, Sec. 8.95. Effective January 1, 2005.)
