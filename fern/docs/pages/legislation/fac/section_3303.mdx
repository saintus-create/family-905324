# FAC § 3303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 2. CALIFORNIA EXPOSITION AND STATE FAIR > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 3303

---

## Text

As used in this part, “board” or “board of directors” means the Board of Directors of the California Exposition and State Fair.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1148.)
