# FAC § 5782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 5. Host-Free Periods and Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 5782

---

## Text

During the existence of a host-free period or host-free district which is established by regulations of the director, any host which is planted, growing, or being cultivated or maintained within the host-free period or district is a public nuisance and is subject to all the laws which relate to the abatement of the nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 721, Sec. 2.)
