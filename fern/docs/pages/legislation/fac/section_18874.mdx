# FAC § 18874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 8. Products in Violation

**Citation:** FAC § 18874

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 18873 shall in no way derogate from authority for condemnation or seizure conferred by other provisions of this chapter, or other laws.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
