# FAC § 11283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. ANIMALS DETRIMENTAL TO AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Predators > Article: 4. Management of Coyotes on Public Lands

**Citation:** FAC § 11283

---

## Text

The director shall submit an annual memorandum of understanding to the Director of Fish and Game for review and approval prior to implementing any coyote control program pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1491, Sec. 1.)
