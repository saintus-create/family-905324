# FAC § 5433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 2. Notice and Expense of Abatement Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 5433

---

## Text

If the sum which is secured by lien is not repaid to the county within 80 days from the recording of the amount of the lien, there shall be added to the sum a penalty of 15 percent. The penalty shall also be secured by the lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
