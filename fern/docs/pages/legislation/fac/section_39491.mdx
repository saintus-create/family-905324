# FAC § 39491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Margarine, Dairy Spread, and Spread > Article: 7. Colored Margarine, Dairy Spread, or Spread

**Citation:** FAC § 39491

---

## Text

Colored margarine, dairy spread, or spread is margarine, dairy spread, or spread which has a tint or shade that contains more than 1.6 degrees of yellow, or of yellow and red collectively, but with an excess of yellow over red, measured in terms of the Lovibond tintometer scale or its equivalent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 512, Sec. 20.)
