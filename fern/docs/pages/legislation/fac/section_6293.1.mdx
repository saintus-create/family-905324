# FAC § 6293.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Napa County Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6293.1

---

## Text

The petition may be filed in sections, each of which shall comply with all the requirements for a petition, except that a section need not contain the total number of signatures required for the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
