# FAC § 12994

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 11. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 12994

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to transport, destroy, or dispose of any quarantined pesticide, unless the person has received written permission from the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 79. Effective January 1, 1997.)
