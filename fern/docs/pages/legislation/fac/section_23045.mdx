# FAC § 23045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Investigations and Seizures

**Citation:** FAC § 23045

---

## Text

Any animal which is seized by, or comes into the possession of an inspector pursuant to this division shall be disposed of pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 17001), Part 1, Division 9 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
