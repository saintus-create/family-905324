# FAC § 12503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12503

---

## Text

“Pesticide chemical” means any substance that is used in the production, storage, or transportation of produce that is a pesticide as defined in Section 12753.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 1997.)
