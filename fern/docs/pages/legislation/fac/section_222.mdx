# FAC § 222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 2. Department of Food and Agriculture Fund

**Citation:** FAC § 222

---

## Text

The director shall keep a separate record of the classes and sources of income which are credited to, and disbursed from, the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 31.)
