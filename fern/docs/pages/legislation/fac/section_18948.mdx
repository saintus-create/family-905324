# FAC § 18948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18948

---

## Text

“Sanitary” means free from dirt, filth, and contamination and free from any other substance or organisms which are known to be injurious to human health or which would render the product adulterated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1128, Sec. 1.)
