# FAC § 19300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 6. Renderers

**Citation:** FAC § 19300

---

## Text

Every person engaged in the business of rendering shall obtain a license from the department for each rendering plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 329, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
