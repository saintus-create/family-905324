# FAC § 14520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14520

---

## Text

“Chelates” means compounds, usually organic, which can combine with a metal ion and form a ring structure between a portion of the chelating agent molecule and the metal.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 504, Sec. 2.)
