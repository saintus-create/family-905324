# FAC § 21201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 6. Certificate of Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 21201

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any inspector to issue a certificate of inspection unless he personally made the inspection between sunrise and sunset.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
