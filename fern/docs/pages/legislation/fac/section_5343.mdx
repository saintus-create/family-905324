# FAC § 5343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Quarantine Regulations and InspectionStations > Article: 3. Plant Quarantine Inspection Stations

**Citation:** FAC § 5343

---

## Text

The director shall cause conspicuous signs to be erected at or near each inspection station which disclose the existence of the station.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
