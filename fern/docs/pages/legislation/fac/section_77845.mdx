# FAC § 77845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 77845

---

## Text

Any assessment that is levied pursuant to this chapter is a personal debt of every handler so assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 76. Effective July 18, 1995.)
