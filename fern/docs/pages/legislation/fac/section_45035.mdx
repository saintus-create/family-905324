# FAC § 45035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 3. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 45035

---

## Text

A copy of the proof of ownership record shall be retained by the buyer and seller for a period of one year after sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 868, Sec. 1.3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
