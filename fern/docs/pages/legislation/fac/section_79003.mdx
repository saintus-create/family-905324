# FAC § 79003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25. Sea Urchins > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79003

---

## Text

The establishment of a commission is necessary for the efficient creation and management of a research program to develop improved harvesting and processing practices, an integrated approach to fishery management, and more efficient resource assessment, monitoring and protection tools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
