# FAC § 77606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 77606

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 77602, the secretary shall certify and declare this chapter inoperative. The secretary may conduct another implementation referendum vote one year or more after the previous referendum has been held.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 132. Effective January 1, 1996.)
