# FAC § 78694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24. California Tomato Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 78694

---

## Text

Nonreceipt of a ballot by an eligible person shall not invalidate a referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
