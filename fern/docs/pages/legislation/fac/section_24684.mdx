# FAC § 24684

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 24684

---

## Text

The director may bring an action to enjoin the violation or threatened violation of this chapter or any regulation which is adopted pursuant to it in the superior court in and for the county in which the violation occurred or is about to occur.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
