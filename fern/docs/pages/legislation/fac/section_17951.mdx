# FAC § 17951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 2. UNLAWFUL MARKING AND BRANDING > Chapter: 4. Civil Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** FAC § 17951

---

## Text

Any person that violates any provision of this part or the regulations which are issued pursuant to it is liable civilly for a penalty in an amount not to exceed a sum of five hundred dollars ($500) for each violation. Any money which is recovered under this section shall be paid into the Department of Agriculture Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
