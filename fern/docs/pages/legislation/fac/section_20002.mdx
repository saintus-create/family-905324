# FAC § 20002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20002

---

## Text

“Animal,” “calf,” and “cattle” mean only bovine animals.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
