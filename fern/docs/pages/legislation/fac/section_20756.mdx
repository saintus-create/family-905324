# FAC § 20756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 6. Fees and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 20756

---

## Text

If the right to use a brand is suspended for failure to pay the renewal fee, it may be reinstated within one year from the date of suspension upon the payment of the biennial renewal fee of seventy dollars ($70) plus a twenty-five dollar ($25) penalty fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 406, Sec. 4. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2016.)
