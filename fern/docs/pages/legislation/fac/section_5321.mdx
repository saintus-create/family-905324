# FAC § 5321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Quarantine Regulations and InspectionStations > Article: 2. Quarantine and Other Regulations for Pests Within the State

**Citation:** FAC § 5321

---

## Text

If the director receives information of the existence of any pest which is not generally distributed within this state, he shall thoroughly investigate the existence and probability of its spread, and the feasibility of its control or eradication.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
