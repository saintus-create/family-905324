# FAC § 14161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3.7. Carbon Monoxide Pest Control Devices

**Citation:** FAC § 14161

---

## Text

The director shall regulate the use of carbon monoxide pest control devices, and shall adopt and enforce regulations to provide for the proper, safe, and efficient use of these devices for the protection of public health and safety, and the environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (SB 1332) Effective January 1, 2015.)
