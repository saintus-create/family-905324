# FAC § 29407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29407

---

## Text

“Crystallized honey” means honey which has assumed a solid form due to the crystallization of one or more of the natural sugars in it.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
