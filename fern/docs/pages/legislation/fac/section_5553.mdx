# FAC § 5553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 5553

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to maintain any neglected or abandoned plant or crop which is a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
