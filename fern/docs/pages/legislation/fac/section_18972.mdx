# FAC § 18972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 4. Inspection Granting and Withdrawal

**Citation:** FAC § 18972

---

## Text

The director may inspect, reinspect, or order the reinspection of any livestock or poultry product which is slaughtered, processed, or stored in an establishment licensed pursuant to Article 8 (commencing with Section 19010).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1128, Sec. 1.)
