# FAC § 26981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Containing Spoilage Retardant Compound > Article: 4. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 26981

---

## Text

Any person that violates any provision of this chapter or any regulation which is adopted pursuant to this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
