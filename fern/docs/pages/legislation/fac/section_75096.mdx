# FAC § 75096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75096

---

## Text

(a) The commission shall prepare an annual budget according to generally accepted accounting practices.

(b) Except for disbursements to pay the compensation of the employees of the commission, no disbursement of funds shall be made unless the director concurs in the budget.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
