# FAC § 58561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Foreign Marketing > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58561

---

## Text

“Third party cooperator” means a foreign government or private organization which has entered into an agreement with a cooperator to join in the marketing of agricultural commodities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1189, Sec. 2.)
