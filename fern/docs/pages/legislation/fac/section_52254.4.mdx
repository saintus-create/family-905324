# FAC § 52254.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52254.4

---

## Text

“Certification” means to certify as to the variety, purity, quality, type, strain, or other genetic character of agricultural or vegetable seed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 715, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
