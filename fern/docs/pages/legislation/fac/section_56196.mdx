# FAC § 56196

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 56196

---

## Text

All indicia of the possession of a license are at all times the property of this state. Each licensee is entitled to the possession of them only for the duration of the license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
