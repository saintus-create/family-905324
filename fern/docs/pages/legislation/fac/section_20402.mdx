# FAC § 20402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 1. Bureau of Livestock Identification

**Citation:** FAC § 20402

---

## Text

The bureau is administered by a chief who is appointed by the director pursuant to the State Civil Service Act, Part 2 (commencing with Section 18500), Division 5, Title 2 of the Government Code.
