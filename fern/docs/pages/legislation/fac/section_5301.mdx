# FAC § 5301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Quarantine Regulations and InspectionStations > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 5301

---

## Text

The director may establish, maintain, and enforce such quarantine regulations as he deems necessary to protect the agricultural industry of this state from pests. The regulations may establish a quarantine at the boundaries of this state or elsewhere within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
