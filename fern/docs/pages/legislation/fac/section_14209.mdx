**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 1.5. Definitions

## Text

“Distribute” means to offer for sale, sell, exchange, or barter.
