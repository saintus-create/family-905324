# FAC § 37222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 5. Bulk Packages

**Citation:** FAC § 37222

---

## Text

The markings shall remain on the packages until the butter which is contained in the packages is cut and wrapped for the retail trade.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
