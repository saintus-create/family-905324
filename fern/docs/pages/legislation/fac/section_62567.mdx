# FAC § 62567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 4. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 62567

---

## Text

If any handler or producer-handler does not provide the information necessary to determine the amount due, when required, the secretary shall estimate the amount due from the records of the department or from any other source of information that is available.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 25. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
