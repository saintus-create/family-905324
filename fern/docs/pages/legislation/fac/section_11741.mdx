# FAC § 11741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 4. Pest Control > Article: 2. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 11741

---

## Text

Any person whose certificate has been revoked, suspended, or refused may appeal to the director within 10 days for a hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
