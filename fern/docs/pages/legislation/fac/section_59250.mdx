# FAC § 59250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 21. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 59250

---

## Text

Any such action may be commenced either in the county where defendant resides, or where any act or omission, or part of the act or omission, which is complained of occurred.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
