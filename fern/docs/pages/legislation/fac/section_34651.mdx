# FAC § 34651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 34651

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to use the words “Registered in California” or the abbreviation “Reg. Cal.” on any container, cabinet, or other equipment unless the brand which appears upon it is registered pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
