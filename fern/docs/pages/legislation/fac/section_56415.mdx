# FAC § 56415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 12. Complaints by Producers

**Citation:** FAC § 56415

---

## Text

All parties disobeying the orders or subpoenas of the director are guilty of contempt and shall be certified to the superior court of the state for punishment for such contempt.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
