# FAC § 55013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55013

---

## Text

“Producer” includes any person who produces rice, or causes rice to be produced.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2009.)
