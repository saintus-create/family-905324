# FAC § 62666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 10. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 62666

---

## Text

Any producer who recovers from the fund shall subrogate to the secretary all rights of recovery against any person or organization, and the producer shall execute and deliver to the secretary instruments and papers and perform any other acts necessary to carry out this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 54. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
