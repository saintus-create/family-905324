# FAC § 42516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 42516

---

## Text

“Pack,” “packing,” or “packed” means the regular compact arrangement of all or part of the fruit or vegetables in any container or subcontainer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
