# FAC § 12999.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 12. Penalties and Injunctive Relief

**Citation:** FAC § 12999.2

---

## Text

The remedies or penalties provided by this division are in addition to the remedies or penalties available under any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 651, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1999.)
