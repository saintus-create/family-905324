# FAC § 21321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 10. Records and Memorandums

**Citation:** FAC § 21321

---

## Text

The inspector shall make a memorandum which shows the number, sex, brands, or marks on each animal which is inspected, and the names of the owner or claimant, consignor, and consignee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
