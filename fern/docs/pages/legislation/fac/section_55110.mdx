# FAC § 55110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 10. Investigations and Actions by the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 55110

---

## Text

The commission may receive and investigate complaints regarding alleged violations of this chapter. The commission may refer cases to the department for action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2009.)
