# FAC § 58203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Agreements > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58203

---

## Text

The department is the proper state agency for such intergovernmental cooperation in those regulatory and service features contemplated by Public Law 733 (60 Stat. 966) and in relation to such other federal laws as may relate to the same and comparable activities.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
