# FAC § 26581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Sale, Possession, and Transportation > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 26581

---

## Text

This chapter only applies to the sale, purchase, or marketing of poultry in any county of this state and to the transportation of poultry in, through, or between any county or counties of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
