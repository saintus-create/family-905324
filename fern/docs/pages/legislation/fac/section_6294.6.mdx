# FAC § 6294.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Napa County Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 3. Organization of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6294.6

---

## Text

Upon the expiration of the term of any member of the district board, the board of supervisors shall appoint the successor. Vacancies shall be filled by the board of supervisors for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
