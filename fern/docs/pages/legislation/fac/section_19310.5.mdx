# FAC § 19310.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 6.5. Transporters of Inedible Kitchen Grease

**Citation:** FAC § 19310.5

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person who is not a registered transporter of inedible kitchen grease to transport that product from any place within this state to any place outside the borders of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 533, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
