# FAC § 59281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 22. Inspection and Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 59281

---

## Text

This article applies to any lot of any commodity which is regulated by a marketing order or marketing agreement wherever, or in whose possession, such lot may be in the marketing channels within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
