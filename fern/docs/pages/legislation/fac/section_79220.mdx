# FAC § 79220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79220

---

## Text

“Market” or “marketing” means to sell or otherwise distribute blueberries into commercial channels or trade resulting in the sale of blueberries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
