# FAC § 55523.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 5. Processor’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55523.5

---

## Text

Failure to file a financial statement as required by this chapter or to comply with the affidavit requirements of Section 55523.1 is a violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 620, Sec. 4.5. Effective January 1, 1997.)
