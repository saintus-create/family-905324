# FAC § 20465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 3. Livestock Identification Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 20465

---

## Text

The term of office of the members of the board is four years. Any vacancy which occurs during an unexpired term shall be filled by appointment for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
