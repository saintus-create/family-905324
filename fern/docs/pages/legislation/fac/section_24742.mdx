# FAC § 24742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 24742

---

## Text

Each person shall, before operating a poultry plant, file an application with the director for a license to operate the poultry plant. The application shall be in such form as the director may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
