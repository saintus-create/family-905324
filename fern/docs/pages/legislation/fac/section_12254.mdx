# FAC § 12254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 9. Pesticide Dealer Designated Agent

**Citation:** FAC § 12254

---

## Text

The director shall issue to each applicant who satisfies the requirements of this chapter a pest control dealer designated agent license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 620, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1994.)
