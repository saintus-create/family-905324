# FAC § 20667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 3. Applications

**Citation:** FAC § 20667

---

## Text

An application shall not be accepted for the recordation of a brand which consists only of a mark.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
