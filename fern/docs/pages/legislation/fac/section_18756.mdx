# FAC § 18756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 4. Adulteration

**Citation:** FAC § 18756

---

## Text

A livestock or poultry product is adulterated if its container is composed, in whole or in part, of any poisonous or deleterious substance which may render the contents of the livestock or poultry product injurious to health.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
