# FAC § 64072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 64072

---

## Text

The secretary shall, from records of the department or any other reliable source, maintain a list of producers and handlers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 9. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
