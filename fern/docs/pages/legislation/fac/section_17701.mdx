# FAC § 17701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 2. UNLAWFUL MARKING AND BRANDING > Chapter: 3. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 17701

---

## Text

A violation of Section 17551 shall be punished by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 of the Penal Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 118. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.)
