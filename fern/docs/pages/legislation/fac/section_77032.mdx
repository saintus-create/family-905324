# FAC § 77032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77032

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person in this state who grows walnuts for market and who, upon request, provides proof of commodity sale. “Producer” does not include any person who handles 2,000 pounds or less of walnuts during a market year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 409, Sec. 10. Effective September 22, 2006.)
