# FAC § 5429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 2. Notice and Expense of Abatement Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 5429

---

## Text

The expense of the abatement by the commissioner is a county charge which is payable out of county funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
