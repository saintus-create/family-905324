# FAC § 55121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 11. Investigations and Actions by the Department

**Citation:** FAC § 55121

---

## Text

The department may commence civil actions and utilize all remedies provided in law or equity for obtaining a writ of attachment, specific performance, or injunctive relief for violations of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 2009.)
