# FAC § 21323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 10. Records and Memorandums

**Citation:** FAC § 21323

---

## Text

The bureau shall maintain for a period of five years a file of brand inspection certificates in such manner as to disclose the number of cattle which have been inspected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
