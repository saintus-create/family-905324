# FAC § 54177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** FAC § 54177

---

## Text

An association may levy assessments in the manner and in the amount as may be provided in its bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
