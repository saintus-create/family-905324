# FAC § 24123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 24123

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any inspector to issue a certificate of inspection unless he or she personally makes the inspection between sunrise and sunset.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 747, Sec. 2.)
