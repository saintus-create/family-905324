# FAC § 74719

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74719

---

## Text

“Grape rootstock nursery” or “nursery” means any person in this state which operates under a California state nursery license and grows grape rootstock for commercial use or distribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 726, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1998.)
