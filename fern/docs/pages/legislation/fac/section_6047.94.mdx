# FAC § 6047.94

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.94

---

## Text

The district shall identify any parcel of real property with more than one acre of table grape plants that shall be subject to assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 400, Sec. 18. Effective September 9, 2004.)
