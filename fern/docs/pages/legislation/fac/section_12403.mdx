# FAC § 12403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 10. Pesticide Brokers

**Citation:** FAC § 12403

## Text

All licenses issued pursuant to this chapter may be renewed before their expiration through application to the director.
