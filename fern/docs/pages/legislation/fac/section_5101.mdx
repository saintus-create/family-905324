# FAC § 5101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. County Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 5101

---

## Text

Each commissioner is an enforcing officer of all laws and regulations which relate to the prevention of the introduction into, or the spread within, the state of pests. He is, as to such activities, under the supervision of the director.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
