# FAC § 73113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 3. The California Navel Orange Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 73113

---

## Text

Any producer member or his or her alternate shall be an individual, partner, or employee of a producer who has a financial interest in producing, or causing to be produced, navel oranges for market.

A producer member or alternate shall meet this requirement during the entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
