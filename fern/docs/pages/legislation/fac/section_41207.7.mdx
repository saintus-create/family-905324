# FAC § 41207.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 5. Wine Grape Inspection Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41207.7

---

## Text

The director may provide rules and procedures to be used by the entity in administering this chapter. The procedures may include:

(a) Employment, training, supervision, and compensation of inspectors.

(b) Use of existing, and acquisition of, additional equipment and supplies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 319, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1993.)
