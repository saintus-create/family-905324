# FAC § 865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 8. Agriculture Theft Prevention > Article: 2. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 865

---

## Text

A copy of the record shall be retained by the buyer and seller for a period of 60 days after delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 661, Sec. 1.)
