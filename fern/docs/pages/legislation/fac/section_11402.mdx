# FAC § 11402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 11402

---

## Text

“License” means pest control business license, agricultural pest control adviser’s license, or pest control dealer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 620, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
