# FAC § 19201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19201

---

## Text

“Animals” means burros, cattle, goats, horses, mules, sheep, swine and other large domesticated animals and poultry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
