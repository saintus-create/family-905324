# FAC § 59801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59801

---

## Text

The powers which are granted to a marketing program committee by a marketing program pursuant to this article may be exercised for the purpose of minimizing the effect of any surplus or other adverse market condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
