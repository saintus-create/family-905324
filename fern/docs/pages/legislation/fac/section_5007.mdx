# FAC § 5007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 5007

---

## Text

“Plant” includes any part of a plant, tree, plant product, shrub, vine, fruit, vegetable, seed, bulb, stolon, tuber, corm, pip, cutting, scion, bud, graft, or fruit pit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
