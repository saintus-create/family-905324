# FAC § 6171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 4. Caprifig Trees

**Citation:** FAC § 6171

---

## Text

Caprifig trees, unless properly controlled and regulated under supervision, constitute a menace because they are a source of infection and pests.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
