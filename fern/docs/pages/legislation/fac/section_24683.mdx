# FAC § 24683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 24683

---

## Text

The director shall furnish to each person that holds a license, or makes application for a license, a copy of the regulations which are adopted pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
