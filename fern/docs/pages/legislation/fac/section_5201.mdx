# FAC § 5201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Certification of Plant Shipments

**Citation:** FAC § 5201

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “certificate” means the certification by a commissioner, deputy commissioner, or inspector of the pest condition or treatment of any shipment of plants.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
