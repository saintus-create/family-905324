# FAC § 58784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 6. Notice, Reports, and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 58784

---

## Text

At any public hearing which is held in connection with the formulation or amendment of a marketing order or marketing agreement, the director shall present testimony with respect to the procedure used to develop the official producer or handler list, as the case may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 319, Sec. 3.)
