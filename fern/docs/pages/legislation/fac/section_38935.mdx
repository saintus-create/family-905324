# FAC § 38935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 4. Plant Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 38935

---

## Text

The license shall expire at the end of each calendar year, but shall remain in force during the month of January of the next succeeding year or such part of the month as may be necessary for the renewal of the license by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
