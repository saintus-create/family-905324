# FAC § 953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. THE STATE BOARD OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 953

---

## Text

The board shall hold meetings at least once a month in the office of the director at Sacramento or at any other location determined by the president of the board to be appropriate.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 325.)
