# FAC § 12563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 3. Permissible Tolerances

**Citation:** FAC § 12563

---

## Text

The director may establish the tolerance for any pesticide chemical on produce at zero if he finds that a greater tolerance is not justified.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
