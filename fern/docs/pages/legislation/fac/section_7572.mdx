# FAC § 7572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pest Seeds > Article: 3. Seed Screenings and Cleanings

**Citation:** FAC § 7572

---

## Text

The commissioner may permit the movement of seed screenings or cleanings for the purpose of destruction or processing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
