# FAC § 45016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 2. Avocado Inspection Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 45016

---

## Text

Committee members shall be compensated for all reasonable expenses incurred in the performance of their duties, as determined by the committee and concurred in by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 19. Effective March 31, 1982.)
