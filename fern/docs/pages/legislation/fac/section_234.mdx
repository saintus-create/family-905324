# FAC § 234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 2.5. Agriculture Trust Fund

**Citation:** FAC § 234

---

## Text

The trust fund is created for the exclusive purpose of implementing and continuing the agricultural programs for which the funds were collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 872, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
