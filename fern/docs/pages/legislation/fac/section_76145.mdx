# FAC § 76145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 76145

---

## Text

Assessments shall be upon the producer. The first shipper, grower-handler, or processor of cherries being assessed shall deduct the assessment from amounts paid by him or her to the producer, and shall be a trustee of the funds until they are paid to the commission at the time and in the manner prescribed by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 64. Effective September 26, 1985.)
