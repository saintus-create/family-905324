# FAC § 57016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 2. General Provisions and Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 57016

## Text

The director may make such regulations as he may deem necessary to carry out this chapter and to effectuate its purposes.
