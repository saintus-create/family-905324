# FAC § 12817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12817

---

## Text

Every registration expires on December 31st of each year except when renewal is applied for within one month thereafter in the manner which is provided for registration.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 717, Sec. 8.)
