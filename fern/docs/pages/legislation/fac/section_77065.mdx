# FAC § 77065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 3. The California Walnut Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77065

---

## Text

A quorum of the commission shall be eight voting members of the commission. Except as provided in Section 77027, a vote in favor of a motion by nine members present at a meeting shall constitute the act of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 409, Sec. 15. Effective September 22, 2006.)
