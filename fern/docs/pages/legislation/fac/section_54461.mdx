# FAC § 54461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 4. Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 54461

---

## Text

The willful violation of any provision of this chapter is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not less than five hundred dollars ($500) nor more than five thousand dollars ($5,000) for each and every violation. This section does not apply to Article 3.5 (commencing with Section 54451).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 510, Sec. 3.)
