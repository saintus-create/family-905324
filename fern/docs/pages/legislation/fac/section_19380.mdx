# FAC § 19380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 10. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 19380

---

## Text

The director may adopt rules or regulations necessary to carry out the purpose of this chapter.
