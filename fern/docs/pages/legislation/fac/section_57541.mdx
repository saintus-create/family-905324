# FAC § 57541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 12. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien > Article: 6. Assignment of Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57541

---

## Text

Except to the extent specifically set forth in this chapter, the lien created by this chapter is subject to Division 9 (commencing with Section 9101) of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 1.)
