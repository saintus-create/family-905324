# FAC § 5204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Certification of Plant Shipments

**Citation:** FAC § 5204

## Text

The schedule of fees for the certificates shall be based upon the approximate cost of the inspection.
