# FAC § 55530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 5. Processor’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55530

---

## Text

Each licensed processor shall post his license, or a copy of it, in his office or salesroom in plain view of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
