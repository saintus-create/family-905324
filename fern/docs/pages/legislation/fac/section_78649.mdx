# FAC § 78649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24. California Tomato Commission > Article: 3. The California Tomato Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78649

---

## Text

Any handler member and his or her alternate on the commission shall be an individual handler or an employee or representative of a handler who has a financial interest in handling tomatoes for market. Qualifications of handler members and their alternates shall be maintained during their entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
