# FAC § 5031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 3. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 5031

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to knowingly falsify, misrepresent, or cause to be falsified or misrepresented, any information in a record intended to show proof of ownership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 806, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
