# FAC § 21202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 6. Certificate of Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 21202

---

## Text

If satisfied that the person that offers the cattle for inspection is in lawful possession of them, the inspector shall issue a certificate of inspection.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
