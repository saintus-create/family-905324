# FAC § 29735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 12. Public Nuisances

**Citation:** FAC § 29735

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to fail to comply with the directions of any officer relating to the disposition of any honey which is a public nuisance, or with any order of court respecting it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
