# FAC § 75035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75035

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person who is engaged in the business of producing, or causing to be produced, eggs or egg products for market.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1093, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
