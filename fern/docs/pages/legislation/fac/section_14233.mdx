# FAC § 14233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 14233

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be controlling over those of any other provisions of law which are in conflict with them. No act or thing which is authorized or permitted by this chapter shall be in violation of any other provisions of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
