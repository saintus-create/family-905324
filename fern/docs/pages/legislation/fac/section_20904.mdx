# FAC § 20904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 5. Cattle Record Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 20904

---

## Text

A cattle record brand may be placed upon either loin in front of the hip and behind the ribs of the animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 653, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
