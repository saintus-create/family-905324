# FAC § 2104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 1. Qualifications

**Citation:** FAC § 2104

---

## Text

A commissioner, deputy commissioner, or county agricultural inspector need not be a resident of the county for which he may be chosen.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
