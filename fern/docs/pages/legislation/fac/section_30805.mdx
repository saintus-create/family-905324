# FAC § 30805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 3. Dog Tags

**Citation:** FAC § 30805

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall fix the compensation of the animal control department for issuing dog license tags.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 118, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2005.)
