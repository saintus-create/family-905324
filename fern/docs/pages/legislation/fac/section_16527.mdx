# FAC § 16527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 3. Pedigree and Proof of Ownership > Article: 2. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 16527

---

## Text

An animal, hide, or carcass seized pursuant to this chapter shall be disposed of pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 17001).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 680, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
