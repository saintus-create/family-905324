# FAC § 5494

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 4. Notice and Expense of Abatement on Other Public Property

**Citation:** FAC § 5494

---

## Text

If any pest is found to exist in or on any irrigation canal or ditch, including its lateral banks, which is not subject to the control of any district or political subdivision of the state, the notice of abatement shall be served upon all users of water from the irrigation canal or ditch that is described in the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
