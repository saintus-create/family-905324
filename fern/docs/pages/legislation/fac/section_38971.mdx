# FAC § 38971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 7. Advertising and Display

**Citation:** FAC § 38971

---

## Text

No product resembling a milk product shall be advertised, displayed for sale, or sold in any manner or under any circumstances or conditions that is likely to mislead, deceive, or confuse consumers into believing such products are milk products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
