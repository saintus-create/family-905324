# FAC § 20751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 6. Fees and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 20751

---

## Text

The fee for each application for recording a brand is seventy dollars ($70).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 406, Sec. 1. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2016.)
