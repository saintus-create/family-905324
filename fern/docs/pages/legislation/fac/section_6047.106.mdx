# FAC § 6047.106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.106

---

## Text

Any two or more districts organized or existing under this article may be consolidated, whether or not the boundaries are coterminous, and whether or not the districts are located in the same county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 244, Sec. 1. Effective August 29, 2003.)
