# FAC § 42824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 7. Powers of Enforcing Officers

**Citation:** FAC § 42824

---

## Text

Any prosecution for the violation of any provision of this division may be made in any county where any part of the offense occurred.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
