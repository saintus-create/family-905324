# FAC § 37162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 3. Butter Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 37162

---

## Text

Butter shall be clean and nonrancid and shall contain not less than 80 percent of milk fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
