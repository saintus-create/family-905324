# FAC § 52862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52862

---

## Text

“Industry members” means cottonseed oil crushers, handlers of raw cotton fiber, and cotton ginners or employees thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 592. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
