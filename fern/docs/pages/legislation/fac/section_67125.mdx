# FAC § 67125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 8. Implementation, Continuation, or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 67125

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that assent has not been given as provided in Section 67121, he or she shall so certify and declare all provisions of this chapter inoperative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 946, Sec. 62. Effective January 1, 2003.)
