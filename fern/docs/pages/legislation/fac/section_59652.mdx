# FAC § 59652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 5. Establishment of Proration Zone

**Citation:** FAC § 59652

---

## Text

The hearings may be adjourned from time to time and from place to place as the circumstances may require. For the purpose of procuring additional evidence, facts, and data, petitioners or opponents shall, upon proper motion, be granted a continuation of any hearing by the director for a period not exceeding five days. A transcript of the proceedings at all such hearings shall be made by the director and shall be open to inspection by any interested party.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
