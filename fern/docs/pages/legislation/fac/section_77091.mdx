# FAC § 77091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77091

---

## Text

The commission may educate and instruct the wholesale and retail trade in domestic and foreign markets with respect to proper methods of handling walnuts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 409, Sec. 16. Effective September 22, 2006.)
