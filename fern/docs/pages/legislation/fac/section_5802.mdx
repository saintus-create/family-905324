# FAC § 5802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 6. Grafts and Buds from Plants Generally Infected

**Citation:** FAC § 5802

---

## Text

If a source of any prohibited or restricted kind or variety of plant has been demonstrated to be free of dangerous or detrimental viruses or mycoplasma-like organisms, the director shall, in the regulation, permit use of such source.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 383.)
