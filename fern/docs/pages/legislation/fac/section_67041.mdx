# FAC § 67041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 2.5. Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 67041

---

## Text

The commission shall establish no fewer than three districts and no more than five districts within the state, each representing approximately the same percentage of avocado production in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 307, Sec. 2. (AB 958) Effective January 1, 2016.)
