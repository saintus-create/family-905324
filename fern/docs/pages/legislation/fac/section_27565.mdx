# FAC § 27565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 5. Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 27565

---

## Text

The director may enter into an agreement with any commissioner for the enforcement of this chapter or the regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
