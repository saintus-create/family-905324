# FAC § 52259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52259

---

## Text

“Vegetable seed” means the seed of any crop which is or may be grown in gardens or on truck farms and which is generally known and sold under the name of vegetable seed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
