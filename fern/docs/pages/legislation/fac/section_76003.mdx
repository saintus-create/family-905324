# FAC § 76003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 76003

---

## Text

The promotional, educational, economic, and marketing research, and any similar activities authorized by this chapter will serve to expand existing markets, create new markets for California cherries, and prevent economic waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
