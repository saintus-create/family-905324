# FAC § 34061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 3. Thermometers and Temperature Records

**Citation:** FAC § 34061

---

## Text

Each vat, tank, or other receptacle in which milk or any milk product is pasteurized shall be equipped with a recording thermometer device to accurately record the temperature to which, and the length of time for which, the pasteurized product has been heated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
