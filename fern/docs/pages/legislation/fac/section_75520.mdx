# FAC § 75520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75520

---

## Text

“Marketing year” means the period beginning July 1 of each year and extending through June 30 of the following year.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 8. Effective May 9, 1994.)
