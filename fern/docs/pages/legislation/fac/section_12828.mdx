# FAC § 12828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12828

---

## Text

Action by the director pursuant to Sections 12824, 12825, 12826, or 12827 is not a condition precedent to the institution of any action to prosecute a violation of the chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1169.)
