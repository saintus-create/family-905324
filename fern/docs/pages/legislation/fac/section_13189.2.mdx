# FAC § 13189.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 17.5. Pesticide Use Near Schoolsites

**Citation:** FAC § 13189.2

---

## Text

In evaluating a county’s pesticide use enforcement program, the director shall evaluate the county’s effectiveness in enforcing Sections 6690 to 6692, inclusive, of Title 3 of the California Code of Regulations and this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 552, Sec. 1. (AB 1864) Effective January 1, 2025.)
