# FAC § 23001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Application

**Citation:** FAC § 23001

---

## Text

The provisions of this division, including the payment of fees, are applicable to all state and county institutions which are engaging in livestock operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
