# FAC § 7203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 1. Weeds > Article: 1. Weed-Free Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 7203

---

## Text

The director may by similar proclamation change the boundaries of a weed-free area or declare the weed-free area free from any additional noxious weeds, naming the noxious weed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
