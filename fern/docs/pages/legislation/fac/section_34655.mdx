# FAC § 34655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 34655

---

## Text

A consignee shall not accept or receive any empty container, cabinet, or other equipment which he is not authorized to use. Nothing herein shall be construed to derogate from any powers or authority of the Public Utilities Commission of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
