# FAC § 69113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 9. Marketing Order Involvement

**Citation:** FAC § 69113

---

## Text

Any activity adopted pursuant to this article shall be implemented by the secretary at the beginning of the marketing season next succeeding the date on which the activity is approved by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 1996.)
