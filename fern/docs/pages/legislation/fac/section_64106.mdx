# FAC § 64106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 4. Dairy Council of California

**Citation:** FAC § 64106

---

## Text

Members of the council shall be nominated and appointed pursuant to procedures recommended by the council and approved by the secretary. The procedures may include, but are not limited to, nominations, preference voting, and district representation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 323, Sec. 10. (AB 2682) Effective January 1, 2013.)
