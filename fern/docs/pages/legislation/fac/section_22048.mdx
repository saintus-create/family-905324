# FAC § 22048

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 10. Slaughter of Cattle > Article: 2. Licensed Slaughterers

**Citation:** FAC § 22048

---

## Text

If a licensed slaughterer desires to change to another location, the person shall notify the bureau of the relocation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 828, Sec. 20.)
