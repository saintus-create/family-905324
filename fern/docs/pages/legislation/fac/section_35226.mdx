# FAC § 35226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 10. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 35226

---

## Text

The fee for issuing the license required by Section 35101 for any manufacturer or processor within this state is one hundred dollars ($100) annually.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 715.)
