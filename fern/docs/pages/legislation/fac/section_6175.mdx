# FAC § 6175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 4. Caprifig Trees

**Citation:** FAC § 6175

---

## Text

If the owner does not destroy or graft the fig tree within the time which is specified in the notice, the commissioner shall cause the grafting or destruction of the tree, at the expense of the owner, in a summary manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
