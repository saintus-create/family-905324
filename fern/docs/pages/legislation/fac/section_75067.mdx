# FAC § 75067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 3. The California Egg Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75067

---

## Text

When a decision of the commission requires the concurrence of the director, he or she shall indicate his or her response, in writing, to the commission within 15 working days from notification of the decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
