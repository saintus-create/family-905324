# FAC § 29410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29410

---

## Text

“Extracted honey” means honey which has been removed from the comb.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
