# FAC § 14154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3.6. Qualified Applicator Certificates

**Citation:** FAC § 14154

---

## Text

The director shall issue a qualified applicator certificate to each applicant who satisfies the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 503, Sec. 15.)
