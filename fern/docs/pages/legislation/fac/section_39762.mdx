# FAC § 39762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 7. UHT Flavored Reduced-Fat Milk and UHT Flavored Lowfat or Light Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 39762

---

## Text

UHT flavored reduced-fat milk shall contain not more than 2.1 percent milk fat and not less than 8.25 percent milk solids-not-fat. UHT flavored lowfat or light (lite) milk shall contain not more than 1.2 percent milk fat and not less than 8.25 percent solids-not-fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 232, Sec. 10. Effective August 4, 1998.)
