# FAC § 77920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77920

---

## Text

“Market development” means trade promotion, public information, dissemination of information on health and social issues and other matters, and activities related to the production of flowers in this state and nationally and internationally.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
