# FAC § 9121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Serum

**Citation:** FAC § 9121

---

## Text

Any person that is engaged in stockraising may purchase for injection in his stock any serum used for the cure or prevention of animal diseases.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
