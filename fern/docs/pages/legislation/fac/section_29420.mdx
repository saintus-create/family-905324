# FAC § 29420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29420

---

## Text

“Slack-filled” means that the contents of any container occupy less than 85 percent of the volume of the closed container.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
