# FAC § 80052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 80052

---

## Text

“Landowner” includes the public agency administering any public lands within the areas subject to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70601 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 50.)
