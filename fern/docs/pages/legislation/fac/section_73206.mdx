# FAC § 73206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 73206

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 73202, he or she shall so certify and declare this chapter to be inoperative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
