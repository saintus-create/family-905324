# FAC § 78580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 8. Implementation, Voting Procedure, and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 78580

---

## Text

The nonreceipt of a ballot by any fisherman or receiver shall not invalidate a referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
