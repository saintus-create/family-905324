# FAC § 10101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 9. Tuberculosis-Exposed Cattle

**Citation:** FAC § 10101

---

## Text

The State Veterinarian may impose tuberculosis testing requirements for tuberculosis-exposed cattle.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 52. Effective August 23, 2004.)
