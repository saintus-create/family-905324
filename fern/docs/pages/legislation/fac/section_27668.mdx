# FAC § 27668

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 14. Registered Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 27668

---

## Text

The director may suspend or revoke any license for failure to comply with the provisions of Sections 27664, 27666, or 27667 or regulations adopted by the director relating to activities which must be licensed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 550.)
