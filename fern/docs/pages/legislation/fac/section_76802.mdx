# FAC § 76802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 6. Education, Research, and Public Information Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 76802

---

## Text

The council shall not expend council funds for the advertising of brand name salmon products. The council may expend council funds to conduct cost-sharing advertising with nonbrand name salmon products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1500, Sec. 1.)
