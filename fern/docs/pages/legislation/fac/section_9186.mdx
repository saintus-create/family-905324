# FAC § 9186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 6. Collections

**Citation:** FAC § 9186

---

## Text

Any fees that are due and payable pursuant to Section 9185 which are not paid may be recovered by the secretary in accordance with the provisions of Section 281.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 133, Sec. 13. (AB 120) Effective July 26, 2011.)
