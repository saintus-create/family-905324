# FAC § 20724

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 5. Suspension and Forfeiture

**Citation:** FAC § 20724

---

## Text

The right to use any brand which conflicts with any brand which has a prior cattle brand registration certificate number pursuant to this chapter is forfeited. All recordations shall be on a statewide basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
