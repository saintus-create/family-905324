# FAC § 79802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79802

---

## Text

The maintenance of the industry in California is necessary to ensure a continuous supply of olives and the resulting olive oil and to ensure the needed levels of income are maintained for those engaged in the production of olives for processing into olive oil and the marketing of olive oil.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
