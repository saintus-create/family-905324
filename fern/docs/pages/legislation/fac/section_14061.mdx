# FAC § 14061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 3. Compound 1080

**Citation:** FAC § 14061

---

## Text

As used in this article, “Compound 1080” means sodium fluoroacetate or any preparation of sodium fluoroacetate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
