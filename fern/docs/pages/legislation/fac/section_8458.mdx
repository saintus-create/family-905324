# FAC § 8458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8458

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication in a newspaper of general circulation published and circulated in the district.
