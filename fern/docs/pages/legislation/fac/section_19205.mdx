# FAC § 19205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19205

---

## Text

“Dead animal hauler” means any person or company that engages in the business of transporting the carcasses of dead animals.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 187, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
