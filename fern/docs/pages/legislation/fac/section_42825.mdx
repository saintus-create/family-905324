# FAC § 42825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 7. Powers of Enforcing Officers

**Citation:** FAC § 42825

---

## Text

Any evidence which is taken by an enforcing officer in any county may be admitted in evidence in any prosecution in any other county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
