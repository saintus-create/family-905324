# FAC § 14062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 3. Compound 1080

**Citation:** FAC § 14062

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, it is unlawful for any person to sell, use, or possess any Compound 1080.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
