# FAC § 14554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14554

---

## Text

“Person” means individual, partnership, association, firm, limited liability company, or corporation who assumes responsibility for the product.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 126. Effective January 1, 1995.)
