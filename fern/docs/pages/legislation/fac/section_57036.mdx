# FAC § 57036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 5. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 57036

---

## Text

If a scale of charges is not established pursuant to bargaining as provided for in Section 57031, the union may propose a scale of charges for approval by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 424, Sec. 3.)
