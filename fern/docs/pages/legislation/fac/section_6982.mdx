# FAC § 6982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 5. Special Assessments

**Citation:** FAC § 6982

---

## Text

The assessment shall be due and payable to the secretary annually by March 10. Assessments not paid within 30 days of the due date shall be considered delinquent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 576, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
