# FAC § 34981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 34981

---

## Text

Every license which is issued pursuant to this chapter, while in force, shall be kept conspicuously displayed in the place of business of the party to whom it has been issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
