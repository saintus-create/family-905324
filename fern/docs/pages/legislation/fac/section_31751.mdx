# FAC § 31751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.5. REGULATION OF CATS > Chapter: 1. Regulation of Cats Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 31751

---

## Text

For the purposes of this division, each member of a litter of kittens, weaned or unweaned, shall be treated as an individual animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 253, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2005.)
