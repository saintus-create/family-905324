# FAC § 34232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Weighing, Measuring, Sampling, and Loading of Milk > Article: 2. Basis for Payment

**Citation:** FAC § 34232

---

## Text

If the director finds, after investigation by him, that facilities are not available for weighing and testing, upon request of the parties he may issue a written permit which authorizes the purchase and sale of milk, cream, or any fluid derivative of milk or cream upon a gallonage basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
