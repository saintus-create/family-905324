# FAC § 2343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. County Veterinarian > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 2343

---

## Text

The veterinarian may establish such quarantine, sanitary, and other regulations within his jurisdiction as he deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
