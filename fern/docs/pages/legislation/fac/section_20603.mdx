# FAC § 20603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20603

---

## Text

The chief shall, by regulation, prescribe the location on the animal where different types of brands, including recorded brands and cattle record brands, may be applied.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
