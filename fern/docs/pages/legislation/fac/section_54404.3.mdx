# FAC § 54404.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 54404.3

---

## Text

The procedure for holding the hearing pursuant to Section 54404.2 is identical to hearings held pursuant to Article 13 (commencing with Section 55781) of Chapter 6.

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1100, Sec. 6.)
