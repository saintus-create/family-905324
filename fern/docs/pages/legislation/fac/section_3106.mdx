# FAC § 3106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Fairs and District Agricultural Associations

**Citation:** FAC § 3106

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter, insofar as they are substantially the same as existing code provisions relating to the same subject matter, shall be construed as restatements and continuations thereof, and not as new enactments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 559, Sec. 35. (AB 2143) Effective January 1, 2025.)
