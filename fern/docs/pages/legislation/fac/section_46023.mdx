# FAC § 46023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46023

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to forge, falsify, fail to retain, fail to obtain, or fail to disclose records pursuant to Section 46028.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 2003.)
