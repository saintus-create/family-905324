# FAC § 241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 3. Interdepartmental Expenses

**Citation:** FAC § 241

---

## Text

The secretary may charge a bureau, division, board, or other agency of the department that is supported other than by the appropriations from the General Fund its proportionate share of the administrative expense of the department, or a share in an amount that is computed to reasonably compensate the department for the administrative services that are rendered by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 3. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
