# FAC § 21532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 4. Duplicate Brand Inspection Certificates

**Citation:** FAC § 21532

---

## Text

The chief may release, or provide for the release of, any carcass or hide without the inspection which is required by Section 21531. Inspection shall, however, be made en route or at point of destination.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
