# FAC § 32508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 32508

---

## Text

“Manufacturing cream” means cream which does not conform to the requirements of market cream.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
