# FAC § 34062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 3. Thermometers and Temperature Records

**Citation:** FAC § 34062

---

## Text

Each vat, tank, or other receptacle in which milk or any milk product is pasteurized shall also be equipped with an indicating thermometer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
