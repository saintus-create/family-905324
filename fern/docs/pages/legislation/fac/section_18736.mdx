# FAC § 18736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 3. Inspections and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18736

---

## Text

The director may appoint and prescribe the duties of such inspectors and other personnel as he deems necessary for the efficient execution of the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
