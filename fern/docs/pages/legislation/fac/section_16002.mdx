# FAC § 16002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16002

---

## Text

“Aircraft” means every description of craft or other contrivance which is used, or capable of being used, as a means of transportation through the air from origins in other states or territories or in foreign countries.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
