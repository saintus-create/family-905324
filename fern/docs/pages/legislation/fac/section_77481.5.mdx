# FAC § 77481.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 77481.5

---

## Text

An assessment established by the commission shall require a favorable vote of not less than two-thirds of the voting producer members, a majority of the voting shipper members, and a majority of the voting processor members on the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1048, Sec. 4. Effective September 29, 1994.)
