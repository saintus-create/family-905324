# FAC § 46011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46011

---

## Text

This act shall not apply to the term “natural” when used in the labeling or advertising of a product.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 12.5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
