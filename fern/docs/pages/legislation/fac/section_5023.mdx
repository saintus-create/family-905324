# FAC § 5023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 5023

---

## Text

The commissioner, whenever necessary, may enter and make an inspection of any premises, plant, conveyance, or thing in his jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
