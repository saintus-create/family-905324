# FAC § 79273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 79273

---

## Text

Every person who handles blueberries shall keep a complete and accurate record of all blueberries handled by him or her with the name of the producer whose blueberries were handled. A producer who delivers or markets blueberries to persons other than to a handler shall keep a complete and accurate record of all those blueberries. The records shall contain information required by the commission. The records shall be preserved by the producer or handler for a period of two years and shall be offered and submitted for inspection at any reasonable time upon written demand of the commission or its duly authorized agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
