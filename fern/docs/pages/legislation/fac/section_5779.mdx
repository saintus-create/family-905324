# FAC § 5779

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 4.5. Notice Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 5779

---

## Text

For pesticide applications other than by air, the procedures in this article may be followed subject to the discretion of the director as to their practicality.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1195, Sec. 1.)
