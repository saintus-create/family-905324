# FAC § 58091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 4. Collection and Dissemination of Information

**Citation:** FAC § 58091

---

## Text

The director shall provide for the collection, analysis, and dissemination of statistics and other information which relates to the production and marketing of farm products, including processed farm products, which are produced in this state, such as, but not limited to, crop and livestock estimating and market news services.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
