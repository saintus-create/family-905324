# FAC § 55722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 11. Examinations and Audits of the Books and Records of Licensees

**Citation:** FAC § 55722

---

## Text

If the examination discloses evidence of any violation of this chapter, the department may issue a complaint detailing the charges and the discipline sought in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 696, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 1998.)
