# FAC § 56604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 18. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 56604

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the applicant, or licensee, has made any false statement as to the condition, quality, or quantity of any farm product which is received, handled, sold, or stored by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
