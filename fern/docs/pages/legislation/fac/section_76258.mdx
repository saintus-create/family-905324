# FAC § 76258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 3. The California Sheep Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76258

---

## Text

The public member or his or her alternate on the commission, shall have all the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member on the commission. The public member shall not have any financial interest in the sheep or wool industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
