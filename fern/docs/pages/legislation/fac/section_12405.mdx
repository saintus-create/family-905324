# FAC § 12405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 10. Pesticide Brokers

**Citation:** FAC § 12405

---

## Text

Each licensed pesticide broker that changes the address of its place of business, or that of a branch location, shall immediately provide the director written notification of the change.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 695, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1998.)
