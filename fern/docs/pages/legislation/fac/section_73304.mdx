# FAC § 73304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 73304

---

## Text

Any action for any penalty or other remedy which is prescribed under this chapter shall be commenced within two years from the date of the alleged violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
