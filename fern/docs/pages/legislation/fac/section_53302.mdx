# FAC § 53302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 53302

---

## Text

“Advertisement” means any representation which relates to nursery stock, that is disseminated in any manner or by any means, except a representation which is on the label.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
