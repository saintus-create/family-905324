# FAC § 64114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 4. Dairy Council of California

**Citation:** FAC § 64114

---

## Text

The appointed members of the council shall receive one hundred dollars ($100) per day for each day spent in actual attendance at the meetings or on the business of the council and shall be reimbursed for necessary traveling and other expenses that are incurred in the performance of their official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 82. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
