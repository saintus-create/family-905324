# FAC § 58681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 3. Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 58681

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any order, rule, or regulation which is issued, or is issuable, by the Public Utilities Commission or the Interstate Commerce Commission with respect to the operation of common carriers.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
