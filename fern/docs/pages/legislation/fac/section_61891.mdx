# FAC § 61891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 61891

---

## Text

The director shall enforce the provisions of this chapter and of any stabilization and marketing plan initiated pursuant to the provisions of this chapter. The director shall adopt those regulations necessary for the proper administration and enforcement of the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
