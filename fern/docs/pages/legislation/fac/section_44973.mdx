# FAC § 44973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 1. Container Certification and Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 44973

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person, including a grower, packer, handler, or retailer to pack, handle, or sell any lot of avocados which are not in compliance with maturity and quality standards established through regulations of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (by Sec. 4) and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 3. Effective March 31, 1982.)
