# FAC § 79931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 30. California Agave Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79931

---

## Text

(a) “Processor” means a person licensed to commercially process agave and who processes agave in an amount of more than three tons in the state in the preceding marketing season.

(b) When the processor is a corporation or a limited liability company, all of the directors, officers, managers, and members of the corporation or limited liability company shall be treated as individuals when determining compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. (AB 2606) Effective January 1, 2025.)
