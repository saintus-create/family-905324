# FAC § 12758.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12758.5

---

## Text

“Use-dilution” means a dilution specified on the label or labeling that produces the concentration of the pesticide for a particular purpose or effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 1997.)
