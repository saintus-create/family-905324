# FAC § 29175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 12. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 29175

---

## Text

No person shall extract or render any honey, pollen, or wax from comb except in a building or enclosure which is so constructed as to prevent access by bees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
