# FAC § 5576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 2. Institution of Proceedings

**Citation:** FAC § 5576

---

## Text

A copy of the citation, together with a copy of the petition, may also be served upon any person that appears of record to be the owner of any encumbrance upon, or interest in, the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
