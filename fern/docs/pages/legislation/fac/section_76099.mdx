# FAC § 76099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76099

---

## Text

The commission may investigate and prosecute civil violations of this chapter and file complaints with appropriate law enforcement agencies or officers for suspected criminal violations of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
