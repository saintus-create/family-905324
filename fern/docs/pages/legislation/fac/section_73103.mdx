# FAC § 73103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 3. The California Navel Orange Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 73103

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 73104, two cooperative handler members shall be nominated and elected by and from cooperative handlers. Two independent handler members shall be nominated and elected by and from independent handlers. No handler may be represented on the commission by more than one handler member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
