# FAC § 29419

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29419

---

## Text

“Section box” means the wood or other frame in which bees have built a small comb of honey.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
