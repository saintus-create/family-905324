# FAC § 4504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 5. Department of Food and Agriculture Supervision

**Citation:** FAC § 4504

---

## Text

The department may at any time examine the books and records of any fair to determine the correctness of any statement or report which is filed with the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
