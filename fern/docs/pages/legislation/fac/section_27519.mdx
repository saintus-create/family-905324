# FAC § 27519

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 27519

---

## Text

“Shell eggs” means any in-shell eggs other than pasteurized in-shell eggs, as defined in Section 27519.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 11, Sec. 2. (AB 1414) Effective April 17, 2014.)
