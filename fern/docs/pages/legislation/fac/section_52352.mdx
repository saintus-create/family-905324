# FAC § 52352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 4.5. Registration and Assessment

**Citation:** FAC § 52352

---

## Text

Each application for an annual registration shall be accompanied by the payment of a fee in the amount of forty dollars ($40) for each fiscal year or portion of a fiscal year beginning July 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 919.)
