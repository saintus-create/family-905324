# FAC § 6986

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 5. Special Assessments

**Citation:** FAC § 6986

---

## Text

The secretary shall levy on all delinquent and unpaid assessments pursuant to this article a collection charge of 20 percent of the amount due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 576, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
