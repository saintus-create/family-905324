# FAC § 65601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 4. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 65601

---

## Text

A shipper shall keep a complete and accurate record of all fresh grapes shipped by the shipper and the producer. The records shall be in simple form and contain the information prescribed by the commission. The records shall be preserved for a period of two years and shall be offered and submitted for inspection at any reasonable time upon written demand of the commission or its duly authorized agent.
