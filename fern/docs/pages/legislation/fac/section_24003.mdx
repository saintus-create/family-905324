# FAC § 24003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 8. Drugging of Horses

**Citation:** FAC § 24003

---

## Text

Except as otherwise prohibited by law, the full use of modern therapeutic measures for the improvement and protection of the health of the horses is permitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 227, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
