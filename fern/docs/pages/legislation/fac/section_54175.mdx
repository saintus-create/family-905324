# FAC § 54175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** FAC § 54175

---

## Text

An association may establish reserves and invest the funds of the reserves in bonds or in such other property as may be provided in the bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
