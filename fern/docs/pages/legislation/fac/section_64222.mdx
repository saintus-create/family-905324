# FAC § 64222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 7. Records and Reports

**Citation:** FAC § 64222

---

## Text

Records kept pursuant to Section 64221 shall be all of the following:

(a) In a form, and containing all information, relevant to the purposes of this chapter, that the secretary may, by order or regulation, prescribe.

(b) Preserved for a period of two years.

(c) Open to inspection at any time on the request of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 96. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
