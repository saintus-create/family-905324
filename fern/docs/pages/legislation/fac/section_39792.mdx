# FAC § 39792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 10. UHT Sour Flavored Half-and-Half

**Citation:** FAC § 39792

---

## Text

UHT sour flavored half-and-half shall contain not less than 9.4 percent milk fat and not more than six-tenths of 1 percent stabilizer by weight.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
