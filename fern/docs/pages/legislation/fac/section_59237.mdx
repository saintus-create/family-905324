# FAC § 59237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 21. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 59237

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter for any person to willfully render or furnish a false or fraudulent report, statement, or record which is required by the director pursuant to any provision of this chapter, or any marketing order which is effective pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
