# FAC § 76709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 4. California Salmon Council

**Citation:** FAC § 76709

---

## Text

The nonvoting members of the council shall be representatives of the following agencies, as selected by the secretary:

(a) The department.

(b) The Department of Fish and Wildlife.

(c) The California Sea Grant Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 307, Sec. 31. (AB 958) Effective January 1, 2016.)
