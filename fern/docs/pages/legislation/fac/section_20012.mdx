# FAC § 20012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20012

---

## Text

“Vent” means a design which is permanently impressed on the hide of a branded animal for the purpose of voiding the prior brand.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
