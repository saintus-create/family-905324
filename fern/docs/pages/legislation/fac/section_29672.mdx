# FAC § 29672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 10. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 29672

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to prepare, pack, place, deliver for shipment, load, ship, transport, or sell a deceptive pack, lot, load, arrangement, or display of honey.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
