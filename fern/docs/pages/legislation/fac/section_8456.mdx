# FAC § 8456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8456

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall order the clerk of the board of supervisors to give notice of the time and place fixed for the hearing upon the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2003.)
