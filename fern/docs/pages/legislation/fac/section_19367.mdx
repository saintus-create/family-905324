# FAC § 19367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 9. Horsemeat

**Citation:** FAC § 19367

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this chapter shall prevent any city, city and county, or county from regulating or prohibiting by ordinance the sale of horsemeat in their respective communities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
