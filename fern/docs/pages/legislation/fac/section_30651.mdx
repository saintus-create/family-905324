# FAC § 30651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 2. Disposition of Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 30651

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “livestock” includes domestic fowls and rabbits.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
