# FAC § 77437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 3. The California Strawberry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77437

---

## Text

A shipper member or his or her alternate on the commission shall be an individual, partner, or employee of a shipper who has a financial interest in shipping, or causing to be shipped, strawberries for market. The shipper member or his or her alternate shall be so qualified during the entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
