# FAC § 78678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24. California Tomato Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78678

---

## Text

The commission shall submit to the secretary for his or her concurrence an annual statement of contemplated activities authorized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
