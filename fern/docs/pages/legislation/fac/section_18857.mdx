# FAC § 18857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 18857

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person required by this chapter to file any annual or special report to fail so to do within the time fixed by the director for filing the report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
