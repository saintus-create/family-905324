# FAC § 55523.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 5. Processor’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55523.6

---

## Text

Any financial statement submitted to the secretary pursuant to this chapter is confidential and shall not be divulged except at a hearing before the secretary or under subpoena, if necessary, for the proper determination of any court proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 620, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1997.)
