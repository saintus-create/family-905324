# FAC § 8762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5.5. OLIVE, STONE, AND POME FRUIT PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 8762

---

## Text

Any district formed pursuant to Part 5 (commencing with Section 8401) to control and eradicate olive, stone, and pome fruit pests shall continue in existence without further proceedings and shall be subject to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 179, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
