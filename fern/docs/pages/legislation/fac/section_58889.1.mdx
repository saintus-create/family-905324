# FAC § 58889.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 9. Terms of Marketing Orders

**Citation:** FAC § 58889.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 58889, any marketing order for processed fruits, nuts, or vegetables may contain in its advertising and sales promotion plan provisions to allocate funds to promote private brands or trade names.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 809, Sec. 1.)
