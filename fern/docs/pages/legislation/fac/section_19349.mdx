# FAC § 19349

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 19349

---

## Text

All trucks and every licensed premises of a dead animal hauler shall be inspected by the bureau at least once a year before the license is renewed and at other times as the secretary deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 187, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2005.)
