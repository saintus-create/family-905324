# FAC § 6047.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.5. Pierce’s Disease and the Glassy-winged Sharpshooter

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.6

---

## Text

The secretary shall accept the recommendations of the board unless he or she determines that the recommendations are not practicable or in the interest of the industry or the public. The secretary shall provide the board with the reasons for his or her decision within 15 days if the secretary does not accept a recommendation of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 741, Sec. 4. Effective September 20, 2002. Conditionally inoperative on March 1, 2026, as provided in Sections 6047.20 and 6047.27. Repealed as of March 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 6047.19.)
