# FAC § 3957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Organization and Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 3957

---

## Text

If two or more counties constitute a district, each county shall be represented on the board by at least two persons who are citizens and residents of such county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
