# FAC § 62601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 6. Discovery and Notification of Default in Payments

**Citation:** FAC § 62601

---

## Text

The secretary shall, upon the secretary’s own motion or upon information from a producer, producer representative, or any other person, immediately take whatever action is necessary to determine whether a payment default has occurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 40. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
