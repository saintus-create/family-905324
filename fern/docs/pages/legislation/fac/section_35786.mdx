# FAC § 35786

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 2. Market Milk Standards and Grades

**Citation:** FAC § 35786

---

## Text

Market goat milk may be standardized to a milk fat content of not less than 2.8 percent. Market goat milk at the time of delivery to the consumer shall contain not less than 8.0 percent of solids not fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 512, Sec. 1.)
