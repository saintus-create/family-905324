# FAC § 75520.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75520.5

---

## Text

“Member” means a person who serves on the commission’s board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 9. Effective May 9, 1994.)
