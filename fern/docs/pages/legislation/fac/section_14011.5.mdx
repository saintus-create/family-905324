# FAC § 14011.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 14011.5

---

## Text

Except as may be provided in regulations adopted by the director, a pesticide use report shall be submitted to the commissioner, on a form prescribed by the director, within seven days after each use of a restricted material.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 926. Effective September 18, 1980.)
