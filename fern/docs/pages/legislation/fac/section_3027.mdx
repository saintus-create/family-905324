# FAC § 3027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Free Admissions > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 3027

## Text

A courtesy pass admission is not transferable.
