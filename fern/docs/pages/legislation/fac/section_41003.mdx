# FAC § 41003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 10. Processing Tomato Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41003

---

## Text

In making selection of the membership of the committee, the director shall take into consideration the recommendations of organizations and associations of producers of tomatoes for processing and of processors of tomatoes for processing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 33.)
