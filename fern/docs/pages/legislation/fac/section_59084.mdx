# FAC § 59084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 15. Termination or Suspension

**Citation:** FAC § 59084

---

## Text

If the director finds that the marketing order is not reapproved, he shall declare such order terminated. An order shall be considered reapproved if it has been assented to or favored at a referendum in the same manner as is required for a new marketing order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
