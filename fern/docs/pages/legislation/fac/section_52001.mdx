# FAC § 52001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Field Crops > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 52001

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “field crop products” includes grain crops, dry bean crops, seeds, forage crops, fiber crops, and other field crops and the manufactured products and byproducts of such field crops.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
