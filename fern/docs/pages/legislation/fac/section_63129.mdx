# FAC § 63129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Marketing of Processing Strawberries > Article: 2. Marketing Orders

**Citation:** FAC § 63129

---

## Text

The director shall amend any existing marketing order exclusively affecting processing strawberries in a manner necessary to conform to this chapter without the necessity of complying with any provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 58601) of Part 2 including those sections relating to the amendment of a marketing order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 18, Sec. 1. Effective March 29, 1985.)
