# FAC § 34801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Transportation

**Citation:** FAC § 34801

---

## Text

Every carrier of milk, cream, or any product of milk, whether a producer, gratuitous private carrier, private carrier for hire, or common carrier, shall maintain every conveyance which it uses in transporting milk and cream in a clean and sanitary condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
