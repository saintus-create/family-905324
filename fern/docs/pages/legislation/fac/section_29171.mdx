# FAC § 29171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 12. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 29171

---

## Text

No person shall conceal or attempt to conceal the fact that disease exists within an apiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
