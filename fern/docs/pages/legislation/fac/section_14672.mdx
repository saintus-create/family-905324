# FAC § 14672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 11. Procedure for Prosecution

**Citation:** FAC § 14672

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter requires the secretary to report for prosecution or to institute injunctive proceedings for any minor violation of this chapter whenever the secretary believes that the public interest would be adequately served by a suitable written notice of warning and compliance with the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 36. Effective January 1, 2006.)
