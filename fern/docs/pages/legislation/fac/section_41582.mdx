# FAC § 41582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 4. Canned Foods > Article: 7. Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 41582

---

## Text

Canned fruits or vegetables, including olives, which are found to be mislabeled or misbranded within the meaning of this chapter may, by order of any court or judge, be seized and destroyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
