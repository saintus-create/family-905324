# FAC § 54003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 54003

---

## Text

“Member” includes members of associations without capital stock and holders of common stock in associations which are organized with shares of stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
