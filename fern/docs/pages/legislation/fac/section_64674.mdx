# FAC § 64674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 7. Referendum for Voluntary Fee Payment

**Citation:** FAC § 64674

---

## Text

If the director finds that the referendum did not receive the required votes in favor of a voluntary promotion and research program pursuant to Section 64672, he shall declare this result and shall instruct the council to carry on the current program.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 418.)
