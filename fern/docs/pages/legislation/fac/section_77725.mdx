# FAC § 77725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77725

---

## Text

“Date” means any variety of date produced in California, whether handled in whole or processed form.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 14. Effective July 18, 1995.)
