# FAC § 54233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** FAC § 54233

---

## Text

Any association may become a member or stockholder of any other association.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
