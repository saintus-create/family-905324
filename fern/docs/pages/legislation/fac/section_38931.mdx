# FAC § 38931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 4. Plant Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 38931

---

## Text

It is unlawful to engage in the manufacture of products resembling milk products, unless a license for the then current calendar year for each separate plant or place used for such business is issued by the director pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
