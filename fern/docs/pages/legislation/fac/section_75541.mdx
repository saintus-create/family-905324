# FAC § 75541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 3. The California Apple Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75541

---

## Text

The commission may sue and be sued, enter into contracts, and adopt a seal. Copies of its proceedings, records, and acts, when authenticated, shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of all statements therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 374, Sec. 10. (AB 623) Effective January 1, 2022.)
