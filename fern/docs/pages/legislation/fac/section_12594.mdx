# FAC § 12594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 4.5. Commercial Laboratories

**Citation:** FAC § 12594

---

## Text

Analysis data developed by an accredited laboratory shall be subject to confirmation by a laboratory of the department prior to an official regulatory action of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 782, Sec. 1.)
