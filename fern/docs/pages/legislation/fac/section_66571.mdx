# FAC § 66571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 3. The California Iceberg Lettuce Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 66571

---

## Text

Immediately after January 1, 1978, the director shall establish a list of handlers in each district. Such list of handlers may be established from lists of such persons whose names and addresses appear on file in the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 434.)
