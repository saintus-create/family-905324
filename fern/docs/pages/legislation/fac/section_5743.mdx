# FAC § 5743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 3. Prevention of Pest Dissemination by Movement of Appliances

**Citation:** FAC § 5743

---

## Text

The commissioner of the county of destination shall refuse entry of the used appliances until the proof required in Section 5742 is furnished.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
