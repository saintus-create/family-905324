# FAC § 73070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 73070

---

## Text

“Producer” or “grower” means any person who is engaged within this state in the business of producing, or causing to be produced, navel oranges for market.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
