# FAC § 62183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 13. Bonding and Payment for Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 62183

---

## Text

If any handler so increases his purchases of market milk during the license year that such purchases exceed the amount for which the handler is bonded, such handler shall forthwith post such additional bond as may be required to comply with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
