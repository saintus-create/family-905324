# FAC § 5105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. County Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 5105

---

## Text

Pending decision after appeal, action by the commissioner on the case under appeal shall be suspended by the director. The refusal of any commissioner to carry out the orders and directions of the director which are issued in connection with any appeal is neglect of duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
