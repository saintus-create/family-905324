# FAC § 35631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 35631

---

## Text

Cream shall be fresh and clean. It shall contain not less than 18 percent of milk fat.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 455.)
