# FAC § 24747

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 24747

---

## Text

Each person that holds a license for a poultry plant shall file an application for the renewal of the license on or before the date of the expiration of the license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
