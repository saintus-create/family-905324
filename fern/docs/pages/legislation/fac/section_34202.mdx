# FAC § 34202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Weighing, Measuring, Sampling, and Loading of Milk > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 34202

---

## Text

No tolerance in weights, measures, percentages of milk fat, moisture, or any other measure or standard is permitted, except where specific provisions are made for such a tolerance.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
