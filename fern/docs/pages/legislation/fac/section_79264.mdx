# FAC § 79264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

## Text
