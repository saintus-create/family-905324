# FAC § 77963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77963

---

## Text

The commission and the secretary shall keep confidential and shall not disclose, except when required by court order in a judicial proceeding, all lists of producers in their possession.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 514, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
