# FAC § 14503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 14503

---

## Text

Any money which is received by the director pursuant to this chapter shall be paid into the State Treasury to the credit of the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund, to be expended solely for the administration and enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 504, Sec. 2.)
