# FAC § 38791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 31. Eggnog

**Citation:** FAC § 38791

---

## Text

Eggnog is a food that complies with Section 131.170 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations. Eggnog shall contain not more than 20,000 bacteria per gram and not more than 10 coliform bacteria per gram.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 339, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2008.)
