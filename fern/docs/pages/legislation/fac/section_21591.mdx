# FAC § 21591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 6. Reports

**Citation:** FAC § 21591

---

## Text

The bureau shall maintain for a period of five years a file of brand inspection certificates in such manner as to disclose the number of carcasses or hides which have been inspected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
