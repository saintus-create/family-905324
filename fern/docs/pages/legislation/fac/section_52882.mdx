# FAC § 52882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 3. San Joaquin Valley Cotton Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52882

---

## Text

The board shall meet at least once a year, or at the call of the chairperson or the secretary, or at the request of any six voting members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
