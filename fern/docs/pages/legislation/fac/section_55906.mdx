# FAC § 55906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 18. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 55906

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person that is subject to this chapter to fail to comply with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
