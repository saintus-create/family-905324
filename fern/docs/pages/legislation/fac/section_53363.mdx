# FAC § 53363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 53363

---

## Text

If the director or the commissioner shall find that any person has violated any provision of this chapter, he may institute proceedings in a court of competent jurisdiction in the area in which the violation occurred, to have such person convicted of the violation, or he may file with the district attorney such evidence that may be deemed necessary.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
