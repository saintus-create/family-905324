# FAC § 6701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 1. Licenses > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 6701

---

## Text

The director may publish in pamphlet form, as often as he deems necessary, a list of all persons that are licensed pursuant to this chapter. The pamphlet may also contain such additional information as the director deems advisable concerning the enforcement of laws and regulations which pertain to nursery stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
