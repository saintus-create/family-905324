# FAC § 55012.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55012.5

---

## Text

“Process” means to harvest, dry, mill, transport, or store rice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2009.)
