# FAC § 58936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 10. Assessments and Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 58936

---

## Text

The director may adopt rules and regulations with respect to the assessment and collection of funds pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 425, Sec. 2. Effective July 12, 1984.)
