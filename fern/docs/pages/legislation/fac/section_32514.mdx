# FAC § 32514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 32514

---

## Text

“Milk products plant scorecard” means the card which is adopted by the director pursuant to Section 32791 for the official scoring of milk products plants.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
