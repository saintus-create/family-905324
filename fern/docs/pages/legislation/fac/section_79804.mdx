# FAC § 79804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79804

---

## Text

The commission form of administration created by this chapter is uniquely situated to provide those engaged in the activities specified in this chapter the opportunity to avail themselves of the benefits of collective action in the broad fields of olive oil research, grades and labeling standards, and public education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 144, Sec. 4. (AB 933) Effective January 1, 2018.)
