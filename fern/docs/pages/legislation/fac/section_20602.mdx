# FAC § 20602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20602

---

## Text

Brands for the purpose of establishing or indicating ownership of cattle may be recorded pursuant to this chapter. Brands for other purposes shall not be recorded.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
