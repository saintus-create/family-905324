# FAC § 8756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 8. Dissolution of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8756

---

## Text

If, at the hearing, a majority of the members of the board of supervisors find that the dissolution of the district will benefit the citrus industry of the county, it shall by resolution dissolve the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 904, Sec. 34.)
