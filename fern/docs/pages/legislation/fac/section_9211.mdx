# FAC § 9211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1.5. Commercial Blood Banks for Animals and Biologics > Article: 2. Animal Blood and Blood Component Products Production and Biologics Production

**Citation:** FAC § 9211

---

## Text

No person shall engage in the production of biologics except as permitted under federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 235, Sec. 10. (AB 1709) Effective January 1, 2011. Became operative on January 1, 2013, pursuant to Sec. 28 of Ch. 235.)
