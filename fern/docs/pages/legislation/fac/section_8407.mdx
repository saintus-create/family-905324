# FAC § 8407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 8407

---

## Text

“District” means a citrus pest control district organized pursuant to this part.
