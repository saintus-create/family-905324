# FAC § 74008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 74008

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to deal with the broad fields of market development, marketing research, and production and processing research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
