# FAC § 71103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 9.5. California Rice Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 71103

---

## Text

Nonreceipt of a ballot by an eligible handler or producer shall not invalidate a referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
