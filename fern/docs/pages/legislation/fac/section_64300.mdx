# FAC § 64300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 11. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 64300

---

## Text

This article provides for a comprehensive and continuing plan for financing the operations of the council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 380, Sec. 23. Effective July 4, 1982. Section operative January 1, 1983, pursuant to Section 64313.)
