# FAC § 6105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 6105

---

## Text

“Profichi crops” means the crop of caprifigs which matures in June and July and is used to caprify or pollinate the main crop of the Smyrna type of figs.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
