# FAC § 5011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 5011

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in this code, for the purposes of pest management, “crop” means a plant or animal, or a product derived from a plant or animal, that can be grown and harvested for profit or for the subsistence of humans or animals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 414, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
