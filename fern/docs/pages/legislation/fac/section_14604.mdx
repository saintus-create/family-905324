# FAC § 14604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 5. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 14604

---

## Text

The secretary may grant a provisional registration for a period not exceeding six months for a registered product undergoing renewal. All fees shall be paid before the issuance of any provisional registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 448, Sec. 4. (AB 1811) Effective January 1, 2017.)
