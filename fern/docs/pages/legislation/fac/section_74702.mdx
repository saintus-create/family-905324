# FAC § 74702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 74702

---

## Text

The maintenance of the grape rootstock industry of California is necessary to assure the consuming public of a continuous supply of good fruit for the table, raisins, and wine grapes, and establishes consistent and needed levels of income for those engaged in the industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
