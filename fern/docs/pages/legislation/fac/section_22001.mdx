# FAC § 22001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 10. Slaughter of Cattle > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 22001

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, a person shall not slaughter, or request or hire any person that is not a licensed slaughterer to slaughter, any bovine animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 828, Sec. 3.)
