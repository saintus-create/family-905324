# FAC § 24113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 24113

---

## Text

If the brands or marks are recorded, the director shall immediately, by letter, notify the person in whose name the brands or marks are recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 747, Sec. 2.)
