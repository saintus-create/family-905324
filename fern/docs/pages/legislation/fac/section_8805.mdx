# FAC § 8805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 6. PEST CONTROL COMPACT

**Citation:** FAC § 8805

---

## Text

Within the meaning of Article VI(b) or VIII(a), a request or application for assistance from the fund may be made by the Governor whenever in his judgment the conditions qualifying this state for such assistance exist and it would be in the interest of this state to make such request.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
