# FAC § 34653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 34653

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to do any of the following:

(a) Erase, obliterate, cover up, or conceal any brand without first notifying the director and receiving his approval.

(b) Sell, buy, give, take, or otherwise traffic in such containers, cabinets, or other dairy equipment, except as provided in Section 34562.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
