# FAC § 5953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 3. Sterile Mexican Fruit Fly Production Facility

**Citation:** FAC § 5953

---

## Text

Any contract entered into by the department to determine the appropriate location, select a suitable facility, or prepare preliminary plans and working drawings is exempt from the Public Contract Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 489, Sec. 1. Effective October 7, 1991.)
