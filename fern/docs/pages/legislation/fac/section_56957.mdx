# FAC § 56957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 6. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 56957

---

## Text

If the director determines that the scale of charges is not unreasonable, he shall approve the scale and thereafter the scale of charges shall be the standard charge for unloading any farm product in the market.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967. Ch. 15.)
