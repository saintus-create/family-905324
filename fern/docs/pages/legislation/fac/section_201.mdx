# FAC § 201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 201

---

## Text

The department may expend in accordance with law all money which is made available for its use.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
