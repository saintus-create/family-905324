# FAC § 32516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 32516

---

## Text

“Product of milk” means milk product as set forth in Section 32512 of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 9.)
