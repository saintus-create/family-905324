# FAC § 14929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14929

---

## Text

“Feed ingredient” means each of the constituent substances making up a formula feed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
