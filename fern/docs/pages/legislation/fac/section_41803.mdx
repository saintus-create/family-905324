# FAC § 41803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 6. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 41803

---

## Text

The cost of such inspection shall be borne equally by both the operator of a dehydrator and the grower.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 887.)
