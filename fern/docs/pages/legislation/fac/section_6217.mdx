# FAC § 6217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6217

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication in a newspaper of general circulation published and circulated in the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
