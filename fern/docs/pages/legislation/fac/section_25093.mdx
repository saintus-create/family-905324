# FAC § 25093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 5. Requirements for Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 25093

---

## Text

The poultry meat inspector shall apply or direct the application of the proper marks, as determined by regulation.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
