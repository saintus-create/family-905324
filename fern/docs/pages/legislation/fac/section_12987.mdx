# FAC § 12987

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10.5. Pesticides and Worker Safety

**Citation:** FAC § 12987

---

## Text

The director shall require registrants of pesticides to submit the data necessary to perform the director’s duties under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 274, Sec. 1.)
