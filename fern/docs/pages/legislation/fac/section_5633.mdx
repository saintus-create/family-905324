# FAC § 5633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 4. Expense of Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 5633

---

## Text

The commissioner shall keep an account of the cost of the removal or destruction and shall render an itemized report of the cost to the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
