# FAC § 78411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 78411

---

## Text

“Processor” means any person who is licensed or who works for any person who is licensed pursuant to Section 8034 of the Fish and Game Code and engaged in the California commercial fisheries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
