# FAC § 77795

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77795

---

## Text

The commission may accept contributions of, or match private, state, or federal funds and employ or make contributions of funds to other persons or state or federal agencies for purposes of promoting and maintaining the date industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 56. Effective July 18, 1995.)
