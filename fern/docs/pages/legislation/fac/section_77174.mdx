# FAC § 77174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 77174

---

## Text

Termination of this chapter shall not affect or waive any right, duty, obligation, or liability which shall have arisen or which may thereafter arise in connection with this chapter, release or extinguish any violation of this chapter, or affect or impair any right or remedies of the commission with respect to any violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective April 30, 1987.)
