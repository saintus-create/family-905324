# FAC § 9976

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 4. Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9976

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor which is punishable by imprisonment in the county jail for not less than 90 days, without the alternative of a fine in any case, for any person to defeat or interfere with or to attempt to defeat or interfere with a tuberculosis test.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 23. Effective August 23, 2004.)
