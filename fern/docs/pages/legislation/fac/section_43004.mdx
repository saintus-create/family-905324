# FAC § 43004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 13. Civil Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** FAC § 43004

---

## Text

Any action for any civil penalty or other civil remedy which is provided for under this division shall be commenced within three years from the date of the alleged violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 123, Sec. 1.)
