# FAC § 35013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 3. Licensing of Milk Products Plants and Other Places of Business

**Citation:** FAC § 35013

---

## Text

Each application shall be accompanied by a fee in an amount which is established for the particular license by Section 35221.
