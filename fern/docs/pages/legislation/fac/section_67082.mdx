# FAC § 67082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 4. Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 67082

---

## Text

Persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to nomination and election procedures adopted by the commission and concurred in by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 188, Sec. 12. (AB 1322) Effective January 1, 2026.)
