# FAC § 42506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 42506

---

## Text

“Container” means any box, crate, lug, chest, basket, carton, barrel, keg, drum, sack, or other receptacle.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
