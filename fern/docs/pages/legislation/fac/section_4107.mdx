# FAC § 4107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 6. Provisions Regarding Particular Associations > Article: 1. California Science Center

**Citation:** FAC § 4107

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a Member of the Legislature representing any district in Los Angeles County may be appointed as a director of the California Science Center.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 841, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1997.)
