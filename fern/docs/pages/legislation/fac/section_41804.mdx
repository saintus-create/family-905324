# FAC § 41804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 6. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 41804

---

## Text

Any money that is collected pursuant to this article shall be remitted to the secretary weekly during the garlic and onions dehydration season for deposit into the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund to be used in carrying out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 674, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1999.)
