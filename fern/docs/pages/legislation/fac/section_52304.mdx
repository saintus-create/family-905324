# FAC § 52304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 2.6. Genetically Engineered Plants

**Citation:** FAC § 52304

---

## Text

The results of any testing conducted pursuant to this article shall be sent by registered letter by the testing party to all parties involved in the investigation within 30 days after the results are reported from the testing laboratory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 424, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
