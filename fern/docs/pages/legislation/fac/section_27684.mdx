# FAC § 27684

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 15. Criminal Punishment and Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 27684

---

## Text

The department shall perform sufficient inspections of the records of out-of-state registrants to ensure that out-of-state registrants selling eggs into California pay the appropriate fees as required by Section 27551.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 283, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2004.)
