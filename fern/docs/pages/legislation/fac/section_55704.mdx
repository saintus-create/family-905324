# FAC § 55704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 10.5. Liens on Livestock Sold or Delivered to Meatpackers

**Citation:** FAC § 55704

---

## Text

Any rights or remedies granted to a person who sells livestock pursuant to this article, are in addition to any other rights or remedies provided by law for such seller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 497.)
