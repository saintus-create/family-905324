# FAC § 33486

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 2. New Construction or Repairs

**Citation:** FAC § 33486

---

## Text

All existing structures which are used in the production of market milk shall be kept in repair in accordance with the sanitary requirements and standards which are established by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
