# FAC § 57586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 13. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien > Article: 5. Enforcement of Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57586

---

## Text

The lien claimant shall foreclose on a lien created by this chapter only in an action to recover the reasonable or agreed charges. The final judgment shall be enforced pursuant to Title 9 (commencing with Section 680.010) of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 2.)
