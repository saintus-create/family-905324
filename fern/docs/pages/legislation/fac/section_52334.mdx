# FAC § 52334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 4. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 52334

---

## Text

The declaration of a plant, seed, nursery stock, or crop as invasive is a power reserved for the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 340, Sec. 13. (SB 839) Effective September 13, 2016.)
