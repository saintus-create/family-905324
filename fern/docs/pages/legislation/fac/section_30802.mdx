# FAC § 30802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 3. Dog Tags

**Citation:** FAC § 30802

---

## Text

Each application for a dog license tag shall state the age, sex, color, and breed of the dog for which the license is desired and the address of the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
