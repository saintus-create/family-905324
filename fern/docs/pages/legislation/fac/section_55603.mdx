# FAC § 55603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 8. Processors

**Citation:** FAC § 55603

---

## Text

If a specific grade or quality is a condition of a packout basis contract between the producer and the processor, such grade or quality shall be determined at the completion of the processing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 807.)
