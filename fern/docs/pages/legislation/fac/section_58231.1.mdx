# FAC § 58231.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Agreements > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58231.1

---

## Text

If a cooperative agreement related to federal-state cooperative market news services, as described in subdivision (b) of Section 58231, is not in effect in the state, the secretary may define grape-pricing districts in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 67, Sec. 81. (AB 1170) Effective January 1, 2026.)
