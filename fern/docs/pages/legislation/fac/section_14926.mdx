# FAC § 14926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14926

---

## Text

“Consumer-buyer” means any person not licensed under this chapter who purchases commerical feed from a manufacturer or distributor of such feed for use in feeding animals.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 871.)
