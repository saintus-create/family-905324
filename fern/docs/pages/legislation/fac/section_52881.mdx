# FAC § 52881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 3. San Joaquin Valley Cotton Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52881

---

## Text

The board may appoint committees composed of both members and nonmembers of the board to advise the board in carrying out this chapter. The board may retain outside counsel, with the approval of the Attorney General, when a possibility of conflict exists between the board and the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 136, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
