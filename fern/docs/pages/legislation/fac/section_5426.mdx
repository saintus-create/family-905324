# FAC § 5426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 2. Notice and Expense of Abatement Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 5426

---

## Text

If the address of the encumbrancer is unknown to the commissioner, he shall state in the copy of the notice that the address is unknown to him and shall mail the copy, addressed to the encumbrancer at the county seat of the county in which the property is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
