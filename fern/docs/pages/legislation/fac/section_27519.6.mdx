# FAC § 27519.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 27519.6

---

## Text

“Pasteurized in-shell eggs” means in-shell eggs that have been pasteurized by any method approved by the federal Food and Drug Administration or the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 11, Sec. 3. (AB 1414) Effective April 17, 2014.)
