# FAC § 2183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 4. Removal

**Citation:** FAC § 2183

---

## Text

At least 10 days prior to the date of the hearing the director shall give notice in writing to the commissioner of the time and place of the hearing and such information as to the nature of the charges as will enable the commissioner to present a defense to the charges.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
