# FAC § 65663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 6. Suspension of Operations

**Citation:** FAC § 65663

---

## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of the commission, a notice of the suspension shall be posted on the department’s website. The commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers and shippers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file with the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 44. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
