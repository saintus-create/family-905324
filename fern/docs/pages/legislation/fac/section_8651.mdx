# FAC § 8651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 6. Inclusion of Lands

**Citation:** FAC § 8651

---

## Text

Lands devoted exclusively to the growing of citrus fruits within a tract of land outside the district but in the county wherein the district is located may be annexed to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 904, Sec. 27.)
