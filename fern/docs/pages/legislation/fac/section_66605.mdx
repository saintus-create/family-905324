# FAC § 66605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 4. Voting Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 66605

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has not been given under Section 66601, he or she shall so certify to the commission and shall declare this chapter inoperative.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 555, Sec. 21.)
