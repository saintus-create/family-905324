# FAC § 52353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 4.5. Registration and Assessment

**Citation:** FAC § 52353

---

## Text

If a registration is not renewed within one calendar month after the beginning of the fiscal year, a penalty of 20 percent of the annual registration fee due shall be added to the fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 919.)
