# FAC § 9352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Scabies and Ticks > Article: 3. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9352

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to knowingly sell any sheep or cattle which are infected with scabies, or to expose them in such a manner as may infect other sheep or cattle which are not infected with scabies.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
