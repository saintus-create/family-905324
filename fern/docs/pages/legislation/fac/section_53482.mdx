# FAC § 53482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 7. Labeling

**Citation:** FAC § 53482

---

## Text

In order to identify nursery stock properly, whenever it is shipped, delivered, or transported to any purchaser, each plant shall be individually labeled as to the correct name. The director may create exceptions to this section by regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 622, Sec. 10.)
