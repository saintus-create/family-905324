# FAC § 64305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 11. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 64305

---

## Text

Assessments established and assessed under this chapter may be enforced and collected by the secretary through direct payment or through accounting procedures established by the secretary under Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 62750).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 104. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
