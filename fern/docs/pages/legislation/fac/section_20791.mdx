# FAC § 20791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 7. Similar Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 20791

---

## Text

If the brand records on file with the bureau disclose that two or more brands have been recorded which appear to be so similar as to be misleading or conflicting, the chief may, and upon request of the owner of one of such brands he shall, cause an investigation to be made and a hearing held upon the matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
