# FAC § 77464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

## Text

Nonreceipt of a ballot shall not invalidate a referendum.
