# FAC § 13123.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 14. Birth Defect Prevention

**Citation:** FAC § 13123.5

---

## Text

To the extent feasible, health effects studies shall be conducted in accordance with standards and protocols established pursuant to the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (7 U.S.C. Sec. 135 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 669, Sec. 1.)
