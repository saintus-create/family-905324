# FAC § 29474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 29474

---

## Text

A person who makes a sworn statement pursuant to Section 29473 shall, within a reasonable time after the demand and receipt by the officer of the statement, present a written receipt to the commissioner of the county which is specified in the statement as the destination of the honey.

The receipt shall contain a signed statement by the person receiving such honey, that the honey, giving an accurate description of it, has been received.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
