# FAC § 20633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 2. Venting Brand Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20633

---

## Text

Only the owner’s recorded brand may be used as a vent. Recordings shall not be issued which allow the use of any other brand as a vent.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
