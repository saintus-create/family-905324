# FAC § 65652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 5. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 65652

---

## Text

Venue for actions commenced by the commission may be established at the domicile or place of business of the defendant or in the county of the principal office of the commission. The commission may be sued only in the county of its principal office.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 39. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
