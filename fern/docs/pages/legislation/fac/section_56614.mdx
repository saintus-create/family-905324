# FAC § 56614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 18. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 56614

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the licensee has knowingly employed an agent, without causing the agent to comply with the licensing requirements of this chapter which are applicable to agents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
