# FAC § 16005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16005

---

## Text

“Territorial waters of California” means all navigable waters of this state including all portions of the sea within its jurisdiction which are used by vessels or aircraft.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
