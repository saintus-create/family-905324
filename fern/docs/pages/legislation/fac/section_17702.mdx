# FAC § 17702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 2. UNLAWFUL MARKING AND BRANDING > Chapter: 3. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 17702

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 17701, any violation of any provision of this part is a misdemeanor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
