# FAC § 20696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20696

---

## Text

The original cattle brand registration certificate shall be kept on file in the bureau. A duplicate certificate shall be issued to the person in whose name the brand is recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
