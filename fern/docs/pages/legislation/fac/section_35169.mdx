# FAC § 35169

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 8. Licensing of Testers, Samplers and Weighers, Technicians, and Pasteurizers

**Citation:** FAC § 35169

---

## Text

Every person who tests any milk or cream purchased, received, or sold on the basis of the nonfat milk solids contained in it shall hold a nonfat milk solids tester’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
