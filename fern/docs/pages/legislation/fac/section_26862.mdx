# FAC § 26862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Containing Spoilage Retardant Compound > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 26862

---

## Text

No whole carcass of poultry meat shall be sold unless it is conspicuously and accurately marked in accordance with the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
