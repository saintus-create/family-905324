# FAC § 5903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 1. Citrus White Fly Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 5903

## Text

The director shall print a copy of the proclamation in one or more papers of general circulation in the infested district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
