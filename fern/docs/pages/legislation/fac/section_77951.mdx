# FAC § 77951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77951

---

## Text

The commission may appoint committees composed of both members and nonmembers of the commission to advise the commission in carrying out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
