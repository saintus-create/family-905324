# FAC § 56615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 18. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 56615

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the applicant or licensee has in the handling of any farm product been guilty of fraud, deceit, or willful negligence.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
