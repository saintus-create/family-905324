# FAC § 55604.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 8. Processors

**Citation:** FAC § 55604.2

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 55581 of the Agricultural Code and Section 12713 of the Business and Professions Code do not apply to farm products purchased and accounted for on a packout basis, pursuant to this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 807.)
