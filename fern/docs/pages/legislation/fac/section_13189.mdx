# FAC § 13189

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 17.5. Pesticide Use Near Schoolsites

**Citation:** FAC § 13189

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “schoolsite” has the same meaning as defined in Section 6690 of Title 3 of the California Code of Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 552, Sec. 1. (AB 1864) Effective January 1, 2025.)
