# FAC § 72135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 8. Continuation, Suspension, and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 72135

---

## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter and of the commission, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers and handlers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
