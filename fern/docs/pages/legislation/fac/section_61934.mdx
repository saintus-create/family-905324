# FAC § 61934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 5. Classes of Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 61934

---

## Text

Class 3 comprises all market milk, market skim milk, or market cream used in the manufacture of frozen dairy products.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
