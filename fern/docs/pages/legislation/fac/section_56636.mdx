# FAC § 56636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 19. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 56636

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person to make any fictitious sale or be guilty of collusion to defraud a producer or licensee.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 871.)
