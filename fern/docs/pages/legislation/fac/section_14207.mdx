# FAC § 14207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 1.5. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14207

---

## Text

“Manufacturer” includes any person that is responsible for the placing of a livestock drug on the market in this state.
