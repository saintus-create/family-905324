# FAC § 24831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 24831

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to do any of the following:

(a) Violate any provision of this chapter or any regulation which is adopted pursuant to it.

(b) Operate a poultry plant without a license.

(c) Mislabel poultry meat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
