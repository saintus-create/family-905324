# FAC § 52961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 10. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 52961

---

## Text

The secretary, and the commissioner of each county acting under the secretary’s supervision, shall enforce this chapter and carry out its provisions and requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 136, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
