# FAC § 5202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Certification of Plant Shipments

**Citation:** FAC § 5202

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may establish a schedule of fees for any or all classes of certificates to be paid by shippers that request such certificates.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
