# FAC § 76146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 76146

---

## Text

Every processor, shipper, and grower-handler is personally liable for the payment of the collected assessments, and failure of the processor, shipper or grower-handler to collect the assessment from any producer shall not exempt any processor, shipper, or grower-handler from liability for these funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1176, Sec. 1.)
