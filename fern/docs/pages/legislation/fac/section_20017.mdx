# FAC § 20017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20017

---

## Text

“Modified point-of-origin inspection area” means any area designated by the director, by regulation, pursuant to Section 21111.
