# FAC § 74828.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74828.1

---

## Text

“Producer region,” for the purpose of establishing and operating a local commission, shall consist of one or more grape crush report districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 854, Sec. 3.)
