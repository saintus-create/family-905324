# FAC § 58934.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 10. Assessments and Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 58934.5

---

## Text

In order to provide funds for defraying expenditures authorized by the marketing order, the director or any advisory board may also receive and disburse contributions that are made by persons other than those directly affected by the marketing order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 210, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
