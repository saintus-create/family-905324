# FAC § 76315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 76315

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 76312, he or she shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote to all producers whose names and addresses may be on file with the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
