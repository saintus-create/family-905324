# FAC § 57024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 3. Registered Unloaders

**Citation:** FAC § 57024

---

## Text

All vehicles shall be unloaded in a timely manner and in the order in which they arrive at the market, when the vehicles are unloaded by registered unloaders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 424, Sec. 2.)
