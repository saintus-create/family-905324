# FAC § 42801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 6.5. Standardization Program

**Citation:** FAC § 42801

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, the director shall create an industry-funded standardization program for the purposes of implementing and enforcing this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 76, Sec. 1. Effective June 3, 1992.)
