# FAC § 55462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 3. Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 55462

---

## Text

For the purposes of trading in cattle, this chapter does not apply to or include any person who is bonded under the federal Packers and Stockyards Act, 1921 (7 U.S.C. Sec. 181, et seq.) who is engaged in the business of buying or selling cattle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 696, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
