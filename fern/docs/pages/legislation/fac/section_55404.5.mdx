# FAC § 55404.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55404.5

---

## Text

“Licensee” means any person licensed under the provisions of this chapter as a processor or cash buying processor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 871.)
