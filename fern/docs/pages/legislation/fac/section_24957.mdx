# FAC § 24957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 24957

---

## Text

“Poultry” means domesticated fowl or domesticated rabbit which is intended for use for human food.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
