# FAC § 78261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78261

---

## Text

The commission shall annually prepare and submit to the secretary, for his or her concurrence, a statement of contemplated activities authorized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 159. Effective January 1, 1996.)
