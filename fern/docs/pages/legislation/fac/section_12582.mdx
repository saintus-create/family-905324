# FAC § 12582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 4. Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 12582

---

## Text

The director shall immediately notify the State Director of Health Services by telephone, with immediate written confirmation, whenever a lot of produce destined for processing is found to be in violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 248, Sec. 45.)
