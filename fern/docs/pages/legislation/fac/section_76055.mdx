# FAC § 76055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 3. The California Cherry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76055

---

## Text

The commission or the director may bring an action for judicial relief from the director’s written notice, or from noncompliance by the commission with the written notice, as the case may be, in a court of competent jurisdiction, which may issue a temporary restraining order, permanent injunction, or other applicable relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 39. Effective September 26, 1985.)
