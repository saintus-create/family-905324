# FAC § 29174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 12. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 29174

---

## Text

No person shall expose to bees any comb or honey from a diseased colony of bees, except pursuant to Section 29074.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
