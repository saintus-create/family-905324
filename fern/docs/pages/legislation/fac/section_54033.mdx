# FAC § 54033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 54033

---

## Text

Associations which are organized pursuant to this chapter are “nonprofit,” since they are not organized to make profit for themselves, as such, or for their members, as such, but only for their members as producers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
