# FAC § 65651.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 5. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 65651.5

---

## Text

An action by the commission for a violation of this chapter shall be commenced within two years from the date of discovery of the alleged violation. An action against the commission by a person shall be commenced within two years from the date of the act of which the person complains.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 37. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
