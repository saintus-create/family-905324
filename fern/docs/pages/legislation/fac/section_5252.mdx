# FAC § 5252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Newly Discovered Pests

**Citation:** FAC § 5252

---

## Text

The director shall furnish to the commissioner a statement as to the best known means or methods for eradicating or controlling the discovered pest and advise him of the procedure or treatment to prevent the further spread of the pest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
