# FAC § 4433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions

**Citation:** FAC § 4433

---

## Text

Marin County may receive an apportionment although that county held no fair in the calendar year 1941 and the agricultural association of the agricultural district of which Marin County is a part received an apportionment during the calendar year 1941.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
