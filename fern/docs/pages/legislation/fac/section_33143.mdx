# FAC § 33143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 4. Designation and Assignment

**Citation:** FAC § 33143

---

## Text

No more than one approved milk inspection service may be designated by the director for any single dairy farm which produces market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 445, Sec. 9.)
