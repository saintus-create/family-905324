# FAC § 25126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 6. Marks and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 25126

---

## Text

Each poultry plant shall submit samples of the proposed method of use of the design of a mark to the director. The method shall be approved by the director before it is used.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
