# FAC § 24955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 24955

---

## Text

“Mislabel” means the placing, or presence of any false, deceptive, or misleading mark, tag, brand, design, inscription, statement, billing, invoice, placard, sign, or other descriptive designation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
