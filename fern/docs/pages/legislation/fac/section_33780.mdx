# FAC § 33780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Milk Products Plants > Article: 3. Milk Products Plant Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33780

---

## Text

No equipment or apparatus shall be installed or maintained in such manner so as to prevent thorough and adequate cleaning of walls and floors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
