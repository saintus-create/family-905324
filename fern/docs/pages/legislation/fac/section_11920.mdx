# FAC § 11920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 5. Aircraft Operation Regulation > Article: 1.5. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 11920

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to act as a pest control aircraft pilot in any county without first registering with the appropriate county agricultural commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 732.)
