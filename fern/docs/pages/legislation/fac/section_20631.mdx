# FAC § 20631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 2. Venting Brand Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20631

---

## Text

Venting a brand consists of rebranding a branded animal, by the owner of the animal, for the purpose of voiding his prior brand.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
