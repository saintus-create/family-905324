# FAC § 77527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77527

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, firm, company, or other entity doing business in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 133. Effective January 1, 1995.)
