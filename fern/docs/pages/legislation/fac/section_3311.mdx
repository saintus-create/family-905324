# FAC § 3311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 2. CALIFORNIA EXPOSITION AND STATE FAIR > Chapter: 2. Organization

**Citation:** FAC § 3311

---

## Text

The California Exposition and State Fair is hereby created as a separate independent entity in state government. The California Exposition and State Fair shall be governed by its board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 694, Sec. 1.6. Effective January 1, 1998.)
