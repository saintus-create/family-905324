# FAC § 77420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77420

---

## Text

“Process” and “processing” are synonymous terms and mean the operations of receiving, cleaning, grading, capping, slicing, canning, freezing, preserving, or otherwise changing the form of the strawberries in preparing them for market.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 113.3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
