# FAC § 17045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 2. Taker-Up of Estrays

**Citation:** FAC § 17045

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, it is unlawful for any person to remove any animal from the possession of the taker-up.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
