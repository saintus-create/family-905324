# FAC § 21565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 21565

---

## Text

A charge shall not be made for the inspection of any hide which has been previously inspected pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
