# FAC § 34532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 2. Brand Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 34532

---

## Text

There shall be included as a part of each brand the words, “Registered in California” or the abbreviation “Reg. Cal.”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
