# FAC § 38213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 3. Reduced-fat Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38213

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, reduced-fat milk shall meet all standards and requirements that are specified in this division for market milk. It shall be labeled “reduced-fat milk.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 17. Effective August 21, 1997.)
