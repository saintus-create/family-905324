# FAC § 5

**Citation:** FAC § 5

---

## Text

All persons who, at the time this code goes into effect, hold office under any of the laws that are repealed by this code, which offices are continued by this code, continue to hold the offices according to their former tenure.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
