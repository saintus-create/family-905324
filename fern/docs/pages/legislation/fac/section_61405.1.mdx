# FAC § 61405.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 5. Bonding and Payment for Manufacturing Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 61405.1

---

## Text

If any handler increases his or her purchases of manufacturing milk during the license year so that the purchases exceed the amount for which the handler is bonded, the handler shall immediately post an additional bond as may be required to comply with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 156, Sec. 5. Effective July 10, 1987.)
