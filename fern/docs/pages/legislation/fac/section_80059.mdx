# FAC § 80059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 80059

---

## Text

“Resale” means native plants harvested, possessed, or transported with the intent to sell the plants for the ultimate purpose of landscaping or decoration, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70608 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 57.)
