# FAC § 52031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Field Crops > Article: 2. Employees

**Citation:** FAC § 52031

---

## Text

The director shall issue to each employee who is authorized to grade, inspect, or weigh the products which are included under this chapter, except those products for which standards have been established pursuant to federal law, a certificate which shows such authority.
