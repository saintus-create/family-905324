# FAC § 38944.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 5. Product Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 38944.5

---

## Text

If renewal of any registration is not made within 60 days after expiration of the registration, the registration becomes null and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 787, Sec. 16.)
