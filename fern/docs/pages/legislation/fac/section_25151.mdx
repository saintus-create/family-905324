# FAC § 25151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 25151

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to violate any provision of this chapter, or any regulations which are adopted by the director pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
