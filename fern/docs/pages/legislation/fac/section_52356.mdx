# FAC § 52356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 4.5. Registration and Assessment

**Citation:** FAC § 52356

---

## Text

Total expenditures from funds derived from registration fees and dollar volume assessments under this chapter shall not exceed the department’s cost of carrying out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 133, Sec. 45. (AB 120) Effective July 26, 2011.)
