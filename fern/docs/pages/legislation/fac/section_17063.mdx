# FAC § 17063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 3. Possession by Inspector

**Citation:** FAC § 17063

---

## Text

If the animal which is seized or otherwise in the possession of the inspector is branded, the director shall cause a search to be made of the brand records to ascertain the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
