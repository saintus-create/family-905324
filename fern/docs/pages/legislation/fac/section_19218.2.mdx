# FAC § 19218.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1.5. Rendering Industry Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 19218.2

---

## Text

The board shall elect a chairperson, and from time to time any other officers as it may deem advisable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 337, Sec. 1. (SB 513) Effective January 1, 2012.)
