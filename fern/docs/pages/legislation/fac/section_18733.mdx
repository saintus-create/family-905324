# FAC § 18733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 3. Inspections and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18733

---

## Text

Equines shall be slaughtered and prepared in establishments separate from any establishment where any other livestock is slaughtered or any product of any other livestock is prepared.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
