# FAC § 304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 6. Refunds

**Citation:** FAC § 304

---

## Text

The fiscal officer of the department shall make payment of any refund pursuant to this article if the director prepares a voucher which sets forth the facts which pertain to the refund and authorizes its payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
