# FAC § 59088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 15. Termination or Suspension

**Citation:** FAC § 59088

---

## Text

No suspension or termination of any marketing order or marketing agreement pursuant to this article shall become effective until the expiration of the marketing season then current.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
