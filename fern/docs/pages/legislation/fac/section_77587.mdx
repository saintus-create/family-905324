# FAC § 77587

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77587

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt an annual budget according to generally accepted accounting practices. The secretary shall concur in the adoption of the budget prior to the encumbrance of funds, except for any obligations incurred pursuant to Section 77576.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 125. Effective January 1, 1996.)
