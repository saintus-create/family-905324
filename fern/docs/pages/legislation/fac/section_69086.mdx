# FAC § 69086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 69086

---

## Text

Every processor shall be personally liable for the payment of the collected assessments, and failure of the processor to collect the assessment from any producer shall not exempt any processor from such liability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 602.)
