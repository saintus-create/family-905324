# FAC § 75523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75523

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person who is engaged in the business of producing, or causing to be produced, apples for market, and who, upon request of the commission, provides proof of commodity sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 10. Effective May 9, 1994.)
