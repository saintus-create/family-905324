# FAC § 31251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 6. Fees for Impounding

**Citation:** FAC § 31251

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each county shall fix the fee for impounding any dog and the amount which is to be paid for keeping the dog.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
