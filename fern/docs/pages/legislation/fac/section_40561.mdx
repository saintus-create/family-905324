# FAC § 40561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 1. Certification of Agricultural Products > Article: 3. Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 40561

---

## Text

Any money which is collected pursuant to this chapter shall be paid into the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 101, Sec. 52.)
