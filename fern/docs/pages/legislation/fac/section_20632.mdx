# FAC § 20632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 2. Venting Brand Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 20632

---

## Text

A vent brand may be applied on the loin of an animal which corresponds to the side the owner’s registered brand.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
