# FAC § 59856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 9. Assents to Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59856

---

## Text

If the director makes a marketing program effective, he shall publish a notice of the effective date pursuant to Section 6061 of the Government Code in the capital of the state and in such other localities as the director may prescribe, not less than five days prior to the effective date.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
