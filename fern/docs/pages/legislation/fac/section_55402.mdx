# FAC § 55402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55402

---

## Text

“Agent” means any person that on behalf of any processor contracts for or solicits any farm product from a producer of the farm product, or that negotiates the purchase of any farm product on behalf of any processor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
