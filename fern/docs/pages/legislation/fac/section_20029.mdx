# FAC § 20029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20029

---

## Text

“Shipper” means any person who transfers cattle from one geographical location to another.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 558, Sec. 1.)
