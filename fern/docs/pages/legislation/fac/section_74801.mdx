# FAC § 74801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 74801

---

## Text

The production and distribution of winegrapes constitute an important industry of this state which provides substantial and necessary revenues for the state and employment for its citizens. It also furnishes an important food which benefits the public health and welfare.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 4.)
