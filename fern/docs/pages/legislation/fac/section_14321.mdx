# FAC § 14321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 5. Retail Licenses for Restricted Drugs

**Citation:** FAC § 14321

---

## Text

A person shall not sell any restricted drug in this state at retail unless he holds a license to do so issued pursuant to this chapter.
