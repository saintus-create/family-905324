# FAC § 77405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17.5. California Strawberry Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77405

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to deal with the broad fields of advertising, promotion, marketing research, and production research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 805, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 1993.)
