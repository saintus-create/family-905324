# FAC § 46015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46015

---

## Text

Materials acceptable in this state are those outlined in Sections 205.600 to 205.607, inclusive, of Title 7 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 21.5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
