# FAC § 47030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10.5. Direct Marketing > Article: 5. Other Direct Marketing Outlets and Distributions

**Citation:** FAC § 47030

---

## Text

Field retail stands are producer-owned and operated premises located at or near the point of production established in accordance with local ordinances and land use codes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 447, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2009.)
