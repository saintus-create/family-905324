# FAC § 76091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76091

---

## Text

The commission may educate and instruct the wholesale and retail trade with respect to proper methods of handling and selling cherries; make market surveys and analyses; present facts to, and negotiate with, state, federal, and foreign agencies on matters which affect the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 50. Effective September 26, 1985.)
