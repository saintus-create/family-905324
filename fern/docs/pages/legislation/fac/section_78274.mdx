# FAC § 78274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 78274

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 78271, the secretary shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote to all producers whose names and addresses are on file with the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 164. Effective January 1, 1996.)
