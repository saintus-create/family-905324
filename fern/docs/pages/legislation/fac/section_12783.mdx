# FAC § 12783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 12783

---

## Text

Any person who is charged with the enforcement or execution of this chapter shall not be directly or indirectly interested in the sale, manufacture, or distribution of any pesticide.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 1997.)
