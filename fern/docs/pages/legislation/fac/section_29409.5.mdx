# FAC § 29409.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29409.5

---

## Text

“Domestic honey” means comb or extracted honey produced within one of the states of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 587.)
