# FAC § 61892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 61892

---

## Text

The director shall have and may exercise any or all the powers conferred by the Government Code upon the head of a department of the state with respect to hearings and investigations under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
