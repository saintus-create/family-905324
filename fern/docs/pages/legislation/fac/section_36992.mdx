# FAC § 36992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Frozen Dairy Products > Article: 7. Frozen Yogurt

**Citation:** FAC § 36992

---

## Text

Frozen yogurt shall contain not less than 3.5 percent milkfat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 236, Sec. 4. Effective August 1, 2008.)
