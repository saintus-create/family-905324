# FAC § 74692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 74692

---

## Text

Following a favorable referendum conducted prior to June 30, 1990, a referendum shall be conducted by the commission every fifth year thereafter unless a referendum is conducted as a result of a petition pursuant to this article. In that case, the referendum shall be every fifth year following the industry petitioned referendum following procedures provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
