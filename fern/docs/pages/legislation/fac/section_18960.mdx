# FAC § 18960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18960

---

## Text

The director may adopt, by regulation, standards and requirements relating to inspection, sanitation, facilities, equipment, reinspection, preparation, processing, buying, selling, transporting, storing, identification, recordkeeping, registration and labeling, and marking for carrying out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1128, Sec. 1.)
