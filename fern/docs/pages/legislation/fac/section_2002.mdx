# FAC § 2002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. County Department of Agriculture

**Citation:** FAC § 2002

---

## Text

The county department of agriculture is under the control of the county agricultural commissioner.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
