# FAC § 64733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 9. Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 64733

---

## Text

The ballot form shall be substantially as follows: BALLOT California Beef Council Law Termination of Council and Program

Shall the existence of the California Beef Council and the programs established and maintained under the California Beef Council Law (Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 64501) of Division 22 of the Agricultural Code) be discontinued and terminated?YESNO Signature of Producers
