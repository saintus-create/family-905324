# FAC § 2107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 1. Qualifications

**Citation:** FAC § 2107

---

## Text

The director shall publish and make available a registry of persons holding a license pursuant to Section 2101, 2102, or 2103 and Section 12202 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 734, Sec. 10. Effective September 18, 1987.)
