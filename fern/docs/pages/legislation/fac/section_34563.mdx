# FAC § 34563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 3. Branded Containers

**Citation:** FAC § 34563

---

## Text

Any person that purchases any container, cabinet, or other dairy equipment which is marked with a brand registered pursuant to this chapter shall submit to the director a copy of the bill of sale or other documentary evidence which supports such purchase.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
