# FAC § 75539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 3. The California Apple Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75539

---

## Text

The term of office of each member, except ex officio members, is four years, commencing with the beginning of the marketing year following the member’s election and until a qualified successor is elected or appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 374, Sec. 9. (AB 623) Effective January 1, 2022.)
