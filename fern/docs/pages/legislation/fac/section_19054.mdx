# FAC § 19054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.5. Holding, Segregation, and Disposal of Animals Unfit for Human Food Purposes

**Citation:** FAC § 19054

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to fail to comply with any order of the director issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 844.)
