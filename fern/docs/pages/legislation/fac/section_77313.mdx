# FAC § 77313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 77313

---

## Text

The secretary shall use fresh weight in calculating the volume voted pursuant to Section 77312. For converting dry weight and seed weight to fresh equivalent weight the secretary shall use the following ratios: 5.3 to 1 for dry weight and 220 to 1 for seed weight.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 167, Sec. 8.9. Effective January 1, 2005.)
