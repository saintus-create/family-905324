# FAC § 5642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 4. Expense of Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 5642

---

## Text

If no action to foreclose is commenced within 120 days subsequent to the recordation of the notice of lien, the lien ceases to exist.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
