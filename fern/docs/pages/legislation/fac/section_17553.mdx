# FAC § 17553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 2. UNLAWFUL MARKING AND BRANDING > Chapter: 2. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 17553

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to mark any bovine animal by cutting off more than one-half of an ear of the animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
