# FAC § 40577.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 1. Certification of Agricultural Products > Article: 4. Shipping Point Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 40577.5

---

## Text

The secretary shall reimburse the entity for its actual and necessary expenses upon receipt of an invoice approved by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 542, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1995.)
