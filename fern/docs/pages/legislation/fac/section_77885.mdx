# FAC § 77885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 77885

---

## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all handlers whose names and addresses are on file with the commission and to the appropriate policy committees in both the Assembly and Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77886 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 89. Effective July 18, 1995.)
