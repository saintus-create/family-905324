# FAC § 20283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 6. Collection and Disposition of Money

**Citation:** FAC § 20283

---

## Text

A civil action may be brought by the department to recover any fee, penalty, or other money that becomes due pursuant to this division.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
