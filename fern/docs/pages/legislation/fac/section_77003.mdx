# FAC § 77003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77003

---

## Text

It is necessary for human nutrition and public benefit that the public be made aware of accurate and scientific information relative to the beneficial qualities of walnuts and of the production and distribution costs necessary to make walnuts available to the consuming public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective April 30, 1987.)
