# FAC § 19225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 19225

---

## Text

Subject to Section 19229, the annual fee for each license held is one hundred dollars ($100).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 134, Sec. 4. (AB 107) Effective July 26, 2011.)
