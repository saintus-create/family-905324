# FAC § 75525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75525

---

## Text

“Sale” means the point at which the producer transfers title of apples in exchange for financial compensation or other valuable consideration. A sale may occur even though the purchased apples are not placed in normal marketing channels.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2.)
