# FAC § 62202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 13. Bonding and Payment for Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 62202

---

## Text

If a handler fails to pay for market milk delivered to him or her at the time and in the manner specified in this chapter, the director may assess a penalty for each payment date that producers were not fully paid. Any penalty assessed shall be a minimum of one hundred dollars ($100) for each payment date. If the amount not properly paid is in excess of forty thousand dollars ($40,000), the penalty assessed will be five thousand dollars ($5,000) or one-fourth of 1 percent of the amount not properly paid for, whichever is less.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1074, Sec. 23. Effective September 24, 1987.)
