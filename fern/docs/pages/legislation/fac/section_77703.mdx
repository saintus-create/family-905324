# FAC § 77703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77703

---

## Text

The maintenance of the date industry in California is necessary to assure the public of a continuous supply of this vital product and the maintenance of needed levels of income for those engaged in the marketing of dates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 6. Effective July 18, 1995.)
