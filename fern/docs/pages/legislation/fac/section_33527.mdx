# FAC § 33527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 3. Dairy Farm Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33527

---

## Text

The milk or cream shall be cooled as provided in Section 35783 and so maintained until delivery to a milk products plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 339, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
