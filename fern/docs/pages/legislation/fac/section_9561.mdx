# FAC § 9561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 3. Establishment of Quarantine

**Citation:** FAC § 9561

---

## Text

The State Veterinarian may establish such quarantine, sanitary, and police regulations as may be necessary to prevent, circumscribe, or exterminate, any condition designated pursuant to Section 9562.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 503, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
