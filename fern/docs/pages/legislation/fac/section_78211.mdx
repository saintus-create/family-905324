# FAC § 78211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 78211

---

## Text

“Asparagus” means all types of vegetables grown from the plant family Liliaceae or Lily; Genus, Asparagus officinalis.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 383, Sec. 3. (AB 1389) Effective January 1, 2014.)
