# FAC § 46014.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46014.6

---

## Text

Only products that have been produced and handled in accordance with this act may be certified by a registered certification agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 403, Sec. 16. (AB 1826) Effective January 1, 2017.)
