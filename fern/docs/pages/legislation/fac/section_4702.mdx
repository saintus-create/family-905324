# FAC § 4702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 5. CITRUS FRUIT FAIRS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 4702

---

## Text

Upon dissolution of any such association, all of its property, after payment of outstanding debts, shall escheat to the state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
