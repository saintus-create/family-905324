# FAC § 10357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 4. Vaccinations

**Citation:** FAC § 10357

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to refuse to submit a dairy calf which is required to be vaccinated and identified pursuant to this chapter to such vaccination.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
