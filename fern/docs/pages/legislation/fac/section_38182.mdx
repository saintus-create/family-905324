# FAC § 38182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 2. Skim Milk, Nonfat Milk, or Fat-free Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38182

---

## Text

Skim milk, nonfat milk, or fat-free milk shall conform to the bacterial standards, coliform bacteria standards, and temperature standards of the grade or class of milk from which the skim milk is derived.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 8. Effective August 21, 1997.)
