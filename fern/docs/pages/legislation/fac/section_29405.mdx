# FAC § 29405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29405

---

## Text

“Comb honey” means honey which is in the comb.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
