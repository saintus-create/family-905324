# FAC § 74721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74721

---

## Text

“Invoicable unit” means any rootstock cutting, or rooted rootstock cutting, or grafted grape rootstock that is used or distributed by a nursery for commercial purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 726, Sec. 14.5. Effective January 1, 1998.)
