# FAC § 33120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 3. Employees

**Citation:** FAC § 33120

---

## Text

Any person who is employed by the department or any county or city shall be paid his salary or compensation while he is attending such a special refresher course.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
