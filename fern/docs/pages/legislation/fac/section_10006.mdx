# FAC § 10006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 5. Tuberculosis Control Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 10006

---

## Text

All owners of cattle within a tuberculosis control area that are subject to examination and tuberculosis testing shall, upon request of the department, provide necessary facilities for conducting tuberculosis tests, and render such assistance as may be required.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 35. Effective August 23, 2004.)
