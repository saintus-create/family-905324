# FAC § 62197

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 13. Bonding and Payment for Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 62197

---

## Text

This article applies regardless of the form in which market milk is received by the handler and regardless of the area of origin of the market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 156, Sec. 46. Effective July 10, 1987.)
