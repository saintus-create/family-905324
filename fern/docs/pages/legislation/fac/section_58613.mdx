# FAC § 58613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 58613

---

## Text

“Major amendments” mean amendments to any marketing order or agreement which are made pursuant to Article 13 (commencing with Section 59021) of this chapter. It does not include a seasonal marketing regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
