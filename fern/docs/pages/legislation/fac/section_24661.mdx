# FAC § 24661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 24661

---

## Text

“Sanitary” means free from unnecessary dirt, filth, and contamination and free from any other substance or organism which is known to be injurious to human health.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
