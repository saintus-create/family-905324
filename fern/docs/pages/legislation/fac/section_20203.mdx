# FAC § 20203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 3. Investigations

**Citation:** FAC § 20203

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to refuse to exhibit any records to any inspector or peace officer who is acting in accordance with the provisions of Section 20202.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1221.)
