# FAC § 14533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14533

---

## Text

“Fertilizing material” means any commercial fertilizer, agricultural mineral, beneficial substance, or organic input material.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 208, Sec. 6. (SB 1522) Effective January 1, 2025.)
