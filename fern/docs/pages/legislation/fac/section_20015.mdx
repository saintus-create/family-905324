# FAC § 20015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20015

---

## Text

“Registered feedlot” means a feedlot defined in Section 20013 that is registered with the bureau in accordance with Sections 21081 and 21082.
