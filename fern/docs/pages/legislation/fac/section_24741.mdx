# FAC § 24741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 24741

---

## Text

A person shall not operate a poultry plant unless he has obtained a license pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
