# FAC § 77265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 3. The California Pepper Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77265

---

## Text

The secretary or his or her representative shall be notified and may attend each meeting of the commission and any committee meeting of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 22, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 2006.)
