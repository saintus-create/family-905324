# FAC § 34005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 1. Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 34005

---

## Text

Market cream which has not been packaged for distribution to consumers may be repasteurized once.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
