# FAC § 20471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 3. Livestock Identification Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 20471

---

## Text

The meetings of the board shall be held in the offices of the department at Sacramento, or elsewhere within the state, if necessary for the proper performance of its duties.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
