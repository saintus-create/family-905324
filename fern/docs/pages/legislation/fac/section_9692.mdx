# FAC § 9692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 8. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9692

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to bring or cause to be brought any animal into a quarantined district, area, or premises, without written permission from the State Veterinarian or an authorized representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 503, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2002.)
