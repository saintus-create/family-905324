# FAC § 9980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 4. Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9980

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to knowingly apply tuberculosis tests that are not approved tests.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 27. Effective August 23, 2004.)
