# FAC § 67057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 3. The California Avocado Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 67057

---

## Text

The commission may appoint its own officers, including a chair, one or more vice chair, and other officers it deems necessary. The officers shall have the powers and duties as may be delegated to them by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 188, Sec. 9. (AB 1322) Effective January 1, 2026.)
