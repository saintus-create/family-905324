# FAC § 77927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 3. The California Cut Flower Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77927

---

## Text

The commission or the director may bring an action for judicial relief from the director’s written notice, or from noncompliance by the commission with the written notice, as the case may be, in a court of competent jurisdiction, which may issue a temporary restraining order, permanent injunction, or other appropriate relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
