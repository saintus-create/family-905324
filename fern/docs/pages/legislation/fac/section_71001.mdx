# FAC § 71001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 9.5. California Rice Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 71001

---

## Text

The maintenance of the rice industry of California is necessary to assure the public of a continuous supply of this vital product and the maintenance of needed levels of income for those engaged in the rice industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
