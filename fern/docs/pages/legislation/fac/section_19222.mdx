# FAC § 19222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 19222

---

## Text

A previous violation of any provision of this chapter is good and sufficient cause for denial of a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
