# FAC § 38926.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 3. Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 38926.5

---

## Text

Nondairy frozen dessert mix is a product in a final, dry, or concentrated form used in the manufacture of nondairy frozen dessert. It shall comply with all the requirements of nondairy frozen dessert. It shall have been pasteurized or otherwise subjected to a process that has been proven, to the satisfaction of the director, to be equally effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 440.)
