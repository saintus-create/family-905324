# FAC § 5208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Certification of Plant Shipments

**Citation:** FAC § 5208

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to alter, deface, or wrongfully use a certificate issued pursuant to any provision of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
