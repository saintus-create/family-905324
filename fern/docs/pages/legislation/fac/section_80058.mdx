# FAC § 80058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 80058

---

## Text

“Seal” means a metal, tamperproof clamp used to permanently affix the tag to a native plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70607 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 56.)
