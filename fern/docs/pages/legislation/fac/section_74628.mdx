# FAC § 74628

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 74628

---

## Text

Subsequent to the first election of commissioners under this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to the nomination and election procedures established by the commission with the concurrence of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
