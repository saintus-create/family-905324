# FAC § 61839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61839

---

## Text

“Wholesale customer” means any person except a distributor that buys packaged milk, cream, or any dairy product for resale to consumers or to other wholesale customers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 751, Sec. 27.)
