# FAC § 62152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 12. Handlers’ Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 62152

---

## Text

The director may also revoke or suspend any license of a handler if he is satisfied that the handler has not paid for any market milk delivered to him at the time and in the manner specified in the contract with the producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
