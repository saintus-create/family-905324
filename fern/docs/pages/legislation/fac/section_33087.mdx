# FAC § 33087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 33087

---

## Text

A member of the advisory committee shall not receive a salary. The director may authorize the reimbursement for any actual expenses incurred while engaged in performing the duties authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 927.)
