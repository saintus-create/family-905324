# FAC § 41752.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 5. Weighing, Inspection, and Certification

**Citation:** FAC § 41752.5

---

## Text

Upon inspection of each load or lot, the secretary shall issue a certificate that shows the weight or percentage of garlic or onions by defect in the sample that does not conform to the standards established pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 674, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1999.)
