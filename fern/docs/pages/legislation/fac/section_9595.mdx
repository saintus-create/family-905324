# FAC § 9595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 4. Compensable Destruction of Diseased Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 9595

---

## Text

Indemnity payments provided by this division shall not be paid for any animal or property which is taken, destroyed, or otherwise disposed of pursuant to any provision of this code that relates to the control, prevention, or eradication of disease, if the owner is in violation of any quarantine order issued pursuant to this division or any regulation adopted pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 599.)
