# FAC § 64676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 7. Referendum for Voluntary Fee Payment

**Citation:** FAC § 64676

---

## Text

No referendum shall be conducted pursuant to this article until after the expiration of a period of two years from the effective date of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 418.)
