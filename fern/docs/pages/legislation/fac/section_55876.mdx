# FAC § 55876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 17. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 55876

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter if the applicant, or licensee, has otherwise violated any provision of this chapter or Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 56101) of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
