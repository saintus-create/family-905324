# FAC § 75097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75097

---

## Text

The commission shall submit to the director, for his or her concurrence, an annual statement of contemplated activities authorized under this chapter, including advertising, promotion, and marketing research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
