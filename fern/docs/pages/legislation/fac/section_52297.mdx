# FAC § 52297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 2.5. Seed Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52297

---

## Text

The board shall annually elect a chairman from its membership, and from time to time such other officers as it may deem advisable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 919.)
