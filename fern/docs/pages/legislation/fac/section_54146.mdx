# FAC § 54146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 6. Directors and Management

**Citation:** FAC § 54146

---

## Text

The bylaws may provide for an executive committee and may allot to such committee all the functions and powers of the board of directors, subject to the general direction and control of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
