# FAC § 11502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 11502

---

## Text

The director shall adopt regulations which govern the conduct of the business of pest control.
