# FAC § 17064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 3. Possession by Inspector

**Citation:** FAC § 17064

---

## Text

If the brands or marks are recorded, the director shall immediately, by letter, notify the person in whose name the brands or marks are recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
