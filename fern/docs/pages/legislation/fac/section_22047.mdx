# FAC § 22047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 10. Slaughter of Cattle > Article: 2. Licensed Slaughterers

**Citation:** FAC § 22047

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a licensed slaughterer to allow anyone to slaughter cattle at the licensee’s plant, except a person that is licensed to slaughter there or an employee of such person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
