# FAC § 44978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 1. Container Certification and Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 44978

---

## Text

The director may enter into an agreement with any county agricultural commissioner for assistance in the enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 44977 by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 9. Effective March 31, 1982.)
