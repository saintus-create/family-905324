# FAC § 21701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Sale and Gift of Cattle, Their Carcasses and Hides > Article: 1. Bills of Sale

**Citation:** FAC § 21701

---

## Text

As used in this article, “animal” includes:

(a) A bovine animal.

(b) The carcass of a bovine animal from which the hide has not been removed.

(c) The hide of a bovine animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
