# FAC § 55631.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 9. Producer’s Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 55631.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 55461, a nonprofit cooperative association acting as a producer bargaining association may assert producer’s lien rights for, or on behalf of, its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1109, Sec. 2.)
