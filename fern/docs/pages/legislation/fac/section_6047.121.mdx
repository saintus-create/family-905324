# FAC § 6047.121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.121

---

## Text

The board of supervisors is ex officio the governing body of the dissolved district. It may levy assessments and perform other acts solely for the purpose and as may be necessary to wind up the affairs of the district and to raise money for the payment of any outstanding indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 244, Sec. 1. Effective August 29, 2003.)
