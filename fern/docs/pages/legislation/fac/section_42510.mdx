# FAC § 42510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 42510

---

## Text

“Fruits, nuts, or vegetables” means the food product of any tree, vine, or plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
