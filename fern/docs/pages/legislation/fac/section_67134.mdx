# FAC § 67134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 9. Continuation and Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 67134

---

## Text

Upon suspension of this chapter and the commission, a notice shall be issued by the secretary, and a copy of the notice shall be published in a newspaper of general circulation in each district. The commission shall provide a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers and handlers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 307, Sec. 17. (AB 958) Effective January 1, 2016.)
