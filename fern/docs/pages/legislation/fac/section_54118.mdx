# FAC § 54118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 5. Bylaws

**Citation:** FAC § 54118

---

## Text

The bylaws may prescribe the amount of entrance, organization, and membership fees, if any; the manner and method of collection of such fees; and the purposes for which they may be used.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
