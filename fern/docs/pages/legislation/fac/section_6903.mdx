# FAC § 6903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 1. Inspections and Standards of Cleanliness

**Citation:** FAC § 6903

---

## Text

The commissioner shall inspect all nurseries within his jurisdiction when and as required by the regulations of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
