# FAC § 32701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 1. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 32701

---

## Text

The director shall, and representatives of approved milk inspection services under his general supervision may, enforce this division.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
