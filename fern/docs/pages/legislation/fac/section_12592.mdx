# FAC § 12592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 4.5. Commercial Laboratories

**Citation:** FAC § 12592

---

## Text

The director may charge a fee for accreditation and quality control oversight in an amount sufficient to cover the reasonable costs of carrying out these programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 782, Sec. 1.)
