# FAC § 55611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 8. Processors

**Citation:** FAC § 55611

---

## Text

All records required to be kept under this chapter shall be kept for a period of four years.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 632.)
