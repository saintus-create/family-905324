# FAC § 23401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 3. Inspection of Animals > Article: 1. Horses, Mules, and Burros

**Citation:** FAC § 23401

---

## Text

Inspection of horses, mules, and burros for shipment out of the state may be made at the request of the owner in the same manner and for the same fees as are provided for bovine animals pursuant to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 21051) of Division 10. This section does not apply to inspections made pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 24101) of Division 11.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 217, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
