# FAC § 75092.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75092.5

---

## Text

The commission may accept contributions for the purpose of carrying out any activity consistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 965, Sec. 7. Effective September 10, 1984.)
