# FAC § 10064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 7. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10064

---

## Text

Animals shall be tested or slaughtered pursuant to this article even if indemnity funds are unavailable.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 44. Effective August 23, 2004.)
