# FAC § 44983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 1. Container Certification and Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 44983

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person to falsify or cause to be falsified the record or information required to be kept pursuant to Section 44982.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 14. Effective March 31, 1982.)
