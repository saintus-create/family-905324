# FAC § 38394

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 12. Extra Grade Edible Dry Whey, Dry Buttermilk, and Modified Dry Whey

**Citation:** FAC § 38394

---

## Text

The alkalinity of ash which is contained in extra grade edible dry whey shall not exceed 225 ml. 0.1 normal hydrochloric acid (0.1 N HCl) per 100 grams. The scorched particle content shall be not more than 15.0 mg. and the solubility index shall be not more than 1.25 ml.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
