# FAC § 76257.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 3. The California Sheep Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 76257.5

---

## Text

Any producer member or his or her alternate on the commission shall be an individual or an employee representing a producer who has a financial interest in producing, or causing to be produced, wool for market. The qualifications of a producer member and an alternate producer member shall be maintained during the entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
