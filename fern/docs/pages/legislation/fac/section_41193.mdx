# FAC § 41193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 4. Determination of Soluble Solids, Rot, and Foreign Material by the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 41193

---

## Text

When any transaction involves fresh grapes for wine and byproducts purposes, the purchaser shall notify the seller, in writing, prior to delivery, of the conditions relating to soluble solids, rot, and foreign materials affecting the purchase price to be paid for such grapes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1073.)
