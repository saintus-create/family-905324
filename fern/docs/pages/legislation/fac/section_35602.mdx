# FAC § 35602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 35602

---

## Text

Milk shall be obtained by the complete milking of healthy cows or goats which are properly fed and kept. Milk shall not be obtained or used for human consumption within 15 days prior to or 5 days after parturition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
