# FAC § 14151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3.6. Qualified Applicator Certificates

**Citation:** FAC § 14151

---

## Text

The director may, by regulation, specify the pesticide applications that shall be made by or under the supervision of a person holding a valid qualified applicator certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 503, Sec. 15.)
