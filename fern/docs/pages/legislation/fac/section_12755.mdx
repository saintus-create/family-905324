# FAC § 12755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12755

---

## Text

“Registrant” means a person that has registered a pesticide and has obtained a certificate of registration from the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 1997.)
