# FAC § 20666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 3. Applications

**Citation:** FAC § 20666

---

## Text

Upon receipt of an application, the bureau shall examine the application and compare the design applied for with brands that are already of record.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
