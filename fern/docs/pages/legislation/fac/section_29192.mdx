# FAC § 29192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 13. Colony Strength

**Citation:** FAC § 29192

---

## Text

The inspection and certification of colony strength of bees may be made by department employees, the commissioner or persons authorized by the commissioner, or persons appointed by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
