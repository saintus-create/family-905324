# FAC § 61829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61829

---

## Text

“Market skim milk” means skim milk, as defined in this code, that is derived from market milk and conforms to the health and sanitary regulations of the place where sold or disposed of for human consumption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
