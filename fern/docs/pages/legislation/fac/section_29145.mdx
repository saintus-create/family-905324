# FAC § 29145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 10. Exportation

**Citation:** FAC § 29145

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to alter, deface, or misuse any certificate issued pursuant to this article.
