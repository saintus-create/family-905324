# FAC § 38943.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 5. Product Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 38943.5

---

## Text

Each registration which has been approved expires on December 31st of the second year after it was approved and may be renewed for two years by payment of a renewal fee of ten dollars ($10). The renewal shall be made on forms which are supplied by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 787, Sec. 15.)
