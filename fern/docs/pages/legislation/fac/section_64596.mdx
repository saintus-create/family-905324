# FAC § 64596

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 4. California Beef Council

**Citation:** FAC § 64596

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the council shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of all business, including the election of officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
