# FAC § 53312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 53312

---

## Text

“Sod” means grassy surface soil nursery stock held together by matted roots of the grass.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 622, Sec. 5.)
