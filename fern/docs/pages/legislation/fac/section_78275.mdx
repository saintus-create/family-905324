# FAC § 78275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 78275

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 78271, the secretary shall certify and declare this chapter inoperative. The secretary may conduct another implementation referendum vote one or more years after the previous vote has been taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 165. Effective January 1, 1996.)
