# FAC § 74826.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74826.5

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, firm, corporation, association, or other entity doing business in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 689, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
