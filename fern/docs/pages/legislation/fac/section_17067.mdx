# FAC § 17067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 3. Possession by Inspector

**Citation:** FAC § 17067

---

## Text

The notice of the taking up of an animal shall describe the weight, sex, breed, color, marks, and brands, if any, of the animal and shall state the time and place of its proposed sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
