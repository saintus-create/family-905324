# FAC § 78479

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 4. California Seafood Council

**Citation:** FAC § 78479

---

## Text

The voting members of the council shall each be appointed to a three-year term, except that in the first year of the council, four members shall be appointed to one-year terms; four members shall be appointed to two-year terms; and three shall be appointed to three-year terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
