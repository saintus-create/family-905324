# FAC § 18974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 4. Inspection Granting and Withdrawal

**Citation:** FAC § 18974

---

## Text

The director may order any labeling or containers to be withheld from use if he or she determines that the labeling is false or misleading or the containers are of a misleading size or form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1128, Sec. 1.)
