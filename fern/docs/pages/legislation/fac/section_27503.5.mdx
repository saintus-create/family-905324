# FAC § 27503.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 27503.5

---

## Text

“California egg” means an egg produced in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 283, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
