# FAC § 79201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79201

---

## Text

The production and marketing of blueberries constitute an important industry of this state that provides substantial and necessary revenues for the state and employment for its citizens.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
