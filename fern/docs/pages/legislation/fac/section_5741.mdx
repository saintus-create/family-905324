# FAC § 5741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 3. Prevention of Pest Dissemination by Movement of Appliances

**Citation:** FAC § 5741

---

## Text

To prevent the dissemination of pests through the agency of appliances, the director may from time to time publish a list of such pests which may be carried through such agencies and designate any treatment which in his opinion would prevent their dissemination.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
