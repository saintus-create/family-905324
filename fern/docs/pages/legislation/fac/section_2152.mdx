# FAC § 2152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 3. Vacancies

**Citation:** FAC § 2152

---

## Text

If, within 60 days after the receipt of the list of persons who are licensed, the appointing power fails to appoint a commissioner from the list, the secretary shall appoint a commissioner from the list.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 402, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
