# FAC § 5604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 3. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** FAC § 5604

---

## Text

If the order is not complied with within the time which is specified in the order, the commissioner shall cause the removal or destruction of the neglected or abandoned plant or crop which is mentioned in the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
