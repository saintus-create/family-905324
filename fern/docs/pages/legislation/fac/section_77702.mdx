# FAC § 77702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77702

---

## Text

The production and marketing of dates constitutes an important industry that provides substantial and necessary revenues for the state and employment for its citizens.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 4. Effective July 18, 1995.)
