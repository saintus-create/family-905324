# FAC § 12609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 5. Seizure

## Text

The produce after reconditioning is subject to all the provisions of this chapter pending its final release by the director pursuant to Section 12606.
