# FAC § 12608.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 5. Seizure

**Citation:** FAC § 12608.5

---

## Text

Upon demand of the owner or person in rightful possession of the produce for permission to remove the produce destined for processing, the director shall release the produce to the custody of the State Director of Health Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 248, Sec. 46.)
