# FAC § 74040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74040

---

## Text

“Winegrape products” include, but are not limited to, must, grape juice, grape concentrate, wine, high proof, and brandy produced from winegrapes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
