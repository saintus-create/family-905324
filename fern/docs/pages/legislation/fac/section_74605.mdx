# FAC § 74605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74605

---

## Text

The commission may establish an annual budget according to accepted accounting practices. The budget shall be concurred in by the director prior to disbursement of funds, except for disbursements made pursuant to Section 74596.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
