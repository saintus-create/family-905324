# FAC § 74902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74902

---

## Text

The commission may undertake appropriate research relating to the production and processing of winegrapes, including the development of new winegrape products and uses for these products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 4.)
