# FAC § 5648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 5. Civil Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 5648

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2035, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 440, Sec. 1. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 5, commencing with Section 5646.)
