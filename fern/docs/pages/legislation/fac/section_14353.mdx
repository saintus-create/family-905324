# FAC § 14353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 14353

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to administer any registered livestock drug to any human being.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1224.)
