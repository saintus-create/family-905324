# FAC § 20701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20701

---

## Text

The records of brands which have been forfeited or canceled for more than five years may be removed from the files and destroyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
