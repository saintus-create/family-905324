# FAC § 9266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1.5. Commercial Blood Banks for Animals and Biologics > Article: 7. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 9266

---

## Text

The department, or humane officers under contract with the department, shall inspect commercial blood banks for animals licensed by the department at least once a year to ensure compliance with the protocols required by subdivision (e) of Section 9221.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 822, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
