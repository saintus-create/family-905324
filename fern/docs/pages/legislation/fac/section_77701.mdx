# FAC § 77701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 77701

---

## Text

The inability of the date industry to maintain or expand present markets for dates may result in unnecessary economic waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 2. Effective July 18, 1995.)
