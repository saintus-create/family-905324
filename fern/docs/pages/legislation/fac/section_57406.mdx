# FAC § 57406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 11. Dairy Cattle Supply Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57406

---

## Text

The priority of conflicting interests, agricultural liens, and the agricultural lien created under this chapter shall be governed by subdivisions (a) to (f), inclusive, of Section 9322 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 309, Sec. 5. (SB 592) Effective January 1, 2013.)
