# FAC § 14558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14558

---

## Text

“Registrant” means any person who has registered a packaged agricultural mineral, beneficial substance, organic input material, or specialty fertilizer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 208, Sec. 12. (SB 1522) Effective January 1, 2025.)
