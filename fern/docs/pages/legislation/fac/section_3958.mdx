# FAC § 3958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Organization and Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 3958

---

## Text

If, by reason of the formation of a new district, a director in one district becomes a resident of another district, his term of office as director expires 60 days after the formation of the new district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
