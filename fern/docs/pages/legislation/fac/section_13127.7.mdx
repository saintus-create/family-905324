# FAC § 13127.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 14. Birth Defect Prevention

**Citation:** FAC § 13127.7

---

## Text

All documentation relevant to a finding made pursuant to Sections 13127.3 and 13127.5 shall be available to the public, and the findings shall be a public record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1228, Sec. 7.)
