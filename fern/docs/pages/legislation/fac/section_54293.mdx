# FAC § 54293

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 11. Reorganization of Corporations Organized Pursuant to Other Laws

**Citation:** FAC § 54293

---

## Text

If the amended articles conform, as provided in Section 54292, provisions in the articles of incorporation that appeared in the original articles or some previous amended articles, are ineffective if, and to the extent that, they are inapplicable to, or inconsistent with, this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
