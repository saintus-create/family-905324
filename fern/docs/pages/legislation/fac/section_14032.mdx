# FAC § 14032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 2. 2,4-D and Other Herbicides

**Citation:** FAC § 14032

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article and in the regulations which are adopted by the director, it is unlawful for any person to use any form of 2,4-D or any other herbicide which the director finds and determines, after hearing, is injurious to any crop.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
