# FAC § 52856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 52856

---

## Text

The Legislature further declares that all cotton approved by the board for planting in the district is for the benefit of the cotton-growing industry and is not for the exclusive or limited use of any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
