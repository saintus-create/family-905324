# FAC § 7501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pest Seeds > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 7501

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to disseminate the seed of any pest within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
