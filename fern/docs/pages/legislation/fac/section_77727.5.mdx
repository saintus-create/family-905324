# FAC § 77727.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

---

## Text

“Handle” means to engage in the business of a handler.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 18. Effective July 18, 1995.)
