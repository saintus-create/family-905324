# FAC § 14330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 5. Retail Licenses for Restricted Drugs

**Citation:** FAC § 14330

---

## Text

The director shall revoke a restricted drug license if he finds that the holder of such license has failed to keep the required record of sales of such drugs, or is not properly handling or storing such drugs.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1224.)
