# FAC § 55123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 11. Investigations and Actions by the Department

**Citation:** FAC § 55123

---

## Text

(a) The department shall be entitled to receive reimbursement for any reasonable attorney’s fees and other related costs, including, but not limited to, investigative costs, involved in enforcement of this chapter.

(b) The department shall use all funds received pursuant to this chapter for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 2009.)
