# FAC § 14323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 5. Retail Licenses for Restricted Drugs

**Citation:** FAC § 14323

---

## Text

The application shall be accompanied by an application fee of fifty dollars ($50). The fee is not refundable if the license is refused.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 622, Sec. 1. (AB 1132) Effective January 1, 2014.)
