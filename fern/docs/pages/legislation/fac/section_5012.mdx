# FAC § 5012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 5012

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in this code, for the purposes of pest management, “forage” means food for domestic or other wild animals that is taken by browsing or grazing, or food that wild or domestic animals take for themselves.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 414, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
