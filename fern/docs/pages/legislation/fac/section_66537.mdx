# FAC § 66537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 66537

---

## Text

“Market” or ”marketing” means to sell, ship, or otherwise distribute iceberg lettuce in the commercial channels of trade in a manner consistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 555, Sec. 3.)
