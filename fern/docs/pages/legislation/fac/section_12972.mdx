# FAC § 12972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10. Recommendations and Usage

**Citation:** FAC § 12972

---

## Text

The use of any pesticide by any person shall be in such a manner as to prevent substantial drift to nontarget areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1049.)
