# FAC § 40732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 40732

---

## Text

Tomatoes for processing purposes, except hard green tomatoes for use in the manufacture of green tomato products, shall conform to the standards established under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 6.)
