# FAC § 12047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 4. Agricultural Pest Control Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 12047

---

## Text

The committee shall be advisory to the director in all matters concerning the licensing, certification, and regulation of persons and firms licensed or certified pursuant to this division, and shall meet on the call of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 459, Sec. 4.)
