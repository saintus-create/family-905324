# FAC § 11201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. ANIMALS DETRIMENTAL TO AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 1. Importation and Transportation

**Citation:** FAC § 11201

---

## Text

Sections 5025, 6304, and 6305 of this code are applicable to the importation into, or the shipment or transportation within, this state of animals which are detrimental to agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
