# FAC § 31751.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.5. REGULATION OF CATS > Chapter: 1. Regulation of Cats Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 31751.6

---

## Text

Any licensed cattery may be exempted pursuant to regulation or ordinance from any requirement to obtain a license tag for each cat within the cattery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 361.)
