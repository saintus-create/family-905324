# FAC § 40810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 5. Inspection and Certification by the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 40810

---

## Text

The director shall not enforce this article during the period that any marketing order or commission covering the same subject is in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 12.)
