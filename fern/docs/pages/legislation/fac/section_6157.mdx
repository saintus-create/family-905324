# FAC § 6157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 3. Caprifigs

**Citation:** FAC § 6157

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to pack, ship, deliver for shipment or sell in any container any caprifigs of the mamme crop, which contain more than 25 percent of spent figs, or more than 10 percent of blanks.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
