# FAC § 31101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 5. Killing and Seizure > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 31101

---

## Text

Any dog which is found running at large without the identification tag or dog license tag which is required pursuant to Section 30951 may be seized and impounded by any peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
