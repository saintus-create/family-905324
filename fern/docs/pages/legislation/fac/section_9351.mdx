# FAC § 9351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Scabies and Ticks > Article: 3. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9351

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to willfully sell, except for immediate slaughter, or expose in such a manner as may infect other livestock not so infected, any cattle which have or are infected with the Boophilus annulatus tick.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
