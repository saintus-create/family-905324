# FAC § 38361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 11. Dry Whole Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38361

---

## Text

Dry whole milk is a food that complies with Section 131.147 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 30. Effective June 6, 1994.)
