# FAC § 76115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 76115

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in this article, he or she shall certify and declare this chapter operative and give notice of the favorable vote to all producers whose names and addresses are on file with the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 57. Effective September 26, 1985.)
