# FAC § 55604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 8. Processors

**Citation:** FAC § 55604

---

## Text

Every contract between a processor and a producer which covers the purchase of any farm product on a packout basis shall designate the price to be paid for each grade purchased.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 807.)
