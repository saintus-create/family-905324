# FAC § 62062.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 9. Establishment of Minimum Prices and Provisions of Stabilization and Marketing Plans

**Citation:** FAC § 62062.5

---

## Text

The minimum prices otherwise established pursuant to this chapter shall be increased by any security charges that are imposed pursuant to Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 62500).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 156, Sec. 24. Effective July 10, 1987.)
