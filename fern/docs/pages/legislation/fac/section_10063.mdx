# FAC § 10063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 7. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10063

---

## Text

Within 30 days after the appraisal of the reacting bovine animal, it shall be slaughtered under the supervision of the State Veterinarian.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 43. Effective August 23, 2004.)
