# FAC § 59691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 6. Change of Proration Zone Boundaries

**Citation:** FAC § 59691

---

## Text

Any area within the state which is not already included in a proration zone that produces the same kind or variety of a commodity as is produced in a proration zone which is already established pursuant to this chapter, and regulated by a marketing program, may be added or annexed to the established proration zone pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
