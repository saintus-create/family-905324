# FAC § 71070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 9.5. California Rice Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 71070

## Text

The powers and duties of the commission shall include, but are not limited to, all of those contained in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 567, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
