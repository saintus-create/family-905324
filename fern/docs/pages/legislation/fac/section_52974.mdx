# FAC § 52974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 11. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 52974

---

## Text

Any seed cotton, cotton plant, or cottonseed that does not comply with this chapter is a public nuisance and is subject to seizure on complaint of the secretary or commissioner to a court of competent jurisdiction in the area in which the seed cotton, cotton plant, or cottonseed is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 136, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
