# FAC § 41421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 4. Canned Foods > Article: 4. Size Standards Generally for Canned Ripe Olives

**Citation:** FAC § 41421

---

## Text

Tolerances above or below the count per pound of a designated size of olives shall be established which conform to the tolerances adopted by the United States Department of Agriculture in its United States Standards for Grades of Canned Ripe Olives.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 639, Sec. 3.)
