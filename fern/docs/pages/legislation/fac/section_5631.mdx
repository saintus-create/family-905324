# FAC § 5631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 4. Expense of Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 5631

---

## Text

If the removal or destruction of any neglected or abandoned plant or crop which is a public nuisance as defined in Section 5551 or 5552, is undertaken by the commissioner, he shall cause a notice of lien which describes the land on which it exists to be recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
