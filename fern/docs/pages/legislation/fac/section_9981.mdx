# FAC § 9981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 4. Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9981

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to have any tuberculosis infected or exposed bovine animal at any livestock fair or show.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 28. Effective August 23, 2004.)
