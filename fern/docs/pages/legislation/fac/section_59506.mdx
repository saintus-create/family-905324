# FAC § 59506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 59506

---

## Text

“Distributor” means any person, except a retailer, that acquires and distributes any commodity at wholesale or retail.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
