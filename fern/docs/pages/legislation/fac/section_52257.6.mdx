# FAC § 52257.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 52257.6

---

## Text

“Process” means any modification of the form or nature of agricultural or vegetable seed, or any treatment of the seed, which renders it inviable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 790.)
