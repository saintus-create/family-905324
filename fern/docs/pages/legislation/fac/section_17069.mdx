# FAC § 17069

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 7. Estrays > Article: 3. Possession by Inspector

**Citation:** FAC § 17069

---

## Text

If ownership is proved to the satisfaction of the director, the animal shall be turned over to the owner upon payment by the owner of all expenses which were incurred in the handling of the animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
