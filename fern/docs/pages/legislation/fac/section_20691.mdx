# FAC § 20691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 4. Recordation

**Citation:** FAC § 20691

---

## Text

The bureau shall maintain a record of all brands that are recorded, except forfeited or canceled brands which are subject to Section 20701.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
