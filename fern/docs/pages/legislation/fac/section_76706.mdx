# FAC § 76706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 4. California Salmon Council

**Citation:** FAC § 76706

---

## Text

The secretary may remove any member of the council or alternate for cause, and, upon that removal, there is a vacancy that shall be filled in the manner as provided for the unexpired term in Section 76705.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 307, Sec. 28. (AB 958) Effective January 1, 2016.)
