# FAC § 5425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 2. Notice and Expense of Abatement Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 5425

---

## Text

The commissioner may record a copy of any notice that is served and may mail a copy of the notice to the person that appears of record to be the owner of any encumbrance on the property, at his last known address.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
