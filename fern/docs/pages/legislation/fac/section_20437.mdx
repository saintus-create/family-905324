# FAC § 20437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 2. Inspectors and Other Peace Officers

**Citation:** FAC § 20437

---

## Text

Any animal, hide, carcass, or portion of a carcass shall not be held pursuant to Section 20435 for more than 30 consecutive days, unless the notice of seizure is renewed by the inspector or peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 85. Effective January 1, 2003.)
