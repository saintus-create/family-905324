# FAC § 2153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 3. Vacancies

**Citation:** FAC § 2153

---

## Text

If there is no qualified person available for the office of commissioner, the appointing power may make a temporary appointment, for a period not exceeding three months, of a person who is recommended in writing by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
