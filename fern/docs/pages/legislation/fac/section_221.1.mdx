# FAC § 221.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 2. Department of Food and Agriculture Fund

**Citation:** FAC § 221.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 221, the department shall establish all permanent positions with the Controller’s office, pursuant to standard state administrative practices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 97, Sec. 1. (AB 222) Effective January 1, 2012.)
