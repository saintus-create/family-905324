# FAC § 58381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Void Contracts and Restraint of Trade > Article: 2. Destruction of Foodstuffs in Restraint of Trade

**Citation:** FAC § 58381

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to destroy, in restraint of trade, any fowl, animal, vegetable, aquacultural product, or other stuff, product, or article which is customary food, or which is proper for food, for human beings, and is in fit sanitary condition to be used as such.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 810, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1996.)
