# FAC § 33121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 3. Employees

**Citation:** FAC § 33121

---

## Text

This article does not apply to any clerical employee, or to any person who is employed in a laboratory.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
