# FAC § 67051.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 3. The California Avocado Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 67051.6

---

## Text

Either the commission or the secretary may bring an action for judicial relief in a court of competent jurisdiction, which may issue a temporary restraining order, permanent injunction, or other applicable relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 946, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2003.)
