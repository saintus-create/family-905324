# FAC § 8560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the District

**Citation:** FAC § 8560

---

## Text

Upon the adoption of the preliminary budget, the board shall fix a time and place for holding a hearing thereon.

The hearing shall not be less than 20 days, nor more than 40 days, after the adoption of the preliminary budget.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
