# FAC § 11922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 5. Aircraft Operation Regulation > Article: 1.5. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 11922

---

## Text

Registration shall be renewed annually by all pest control aircraft pilots.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 732.)
