# FAC § 38632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 22. Concentrated Skim Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38632

---

## Text

Concentrated skim milk shall contain not less than 24 percent of milk solids not fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
