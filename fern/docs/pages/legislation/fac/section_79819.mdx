# FAC § 79819

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79819

---

## Text

“Market” or “marketing” means to sell olive oil into commercial channels of trade.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
