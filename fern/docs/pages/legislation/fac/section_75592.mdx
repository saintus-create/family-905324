# FAC § 75592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75592

---

## Text

The commission may conduct, and contract with others to conduct, production research, including the study, analysis, accumulation, and dissemination of information obtained from that research or otherwise, regarding this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2.)
