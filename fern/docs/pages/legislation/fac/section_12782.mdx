# FAC § 12782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 12782

---

## Text

A copy of the regulations which are adopted by the director pursuant to this chapter shall be mailed to each registrant promptly upon promulgation of the regulation. The failure to receive such copy is no defense to a violation of any regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
