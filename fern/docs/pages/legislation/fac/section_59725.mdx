# FAC § 59725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 7. Marketing Program and Grading Committees

**Citation:** FAC § 59725

---

## Text

Each respective alternate shall be appointed from the same eligible list from which the member is appointed. Each district in the proration zone is entitled to at least one member and one alternate member of the committee.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
