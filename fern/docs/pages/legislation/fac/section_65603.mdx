# FAC § 65603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 4. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 65603

---

## Text

All information obtained by the commission from shippers shall be confidential and shall not be disclosed, except when required by a court order in a judicial proceeding involving this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 35. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
