# FAC § 12971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10. Recommendations and Usage

**Citation:** FAC § 12971

---

## Text

No recommendation shall be in conflict with the registered labeling for the product being recommended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12972 by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1049.)
