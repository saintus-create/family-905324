# FAC § 52934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 8. Referendum

**Citation:** FAC § 52934

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 52932, he or she shall so certify and give notice of that favorable vote to the cotton growers in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2009. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
