# FAC § 29404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29404

---

## Text

“Clean and sound containers” means containers which are virtually free from rust, stains, or leaks.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
