# FAC § 78235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 3. The California Asparagus Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78235

---

## Text

The term of office of all members and alternate members of the commission, except ex officio members, is three years commencing with the first day of December before the beginning of the marketing year in the year of their election and until qualified successors are elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 383, Sec. 7. (AB 1389) Effective January 1, 2014.)
