# FAC § 64221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 7. Records and Reports

**Citation:** FAC § 64221

---

## Text

Every handler shall keep complete and accurate records of all milk in unprocessed form that the handler purchases, or possession or control of which it acquires from any producer or other handler. Every handler that produces milk shall include its own production in these records. Every handler shall also keep complete and accurate records of all milk that it utilizes for processing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 13. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
