# FAC § 34261.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Weighing, Measuring, Sampling, and Loading of Milk > Article: 3. Milk Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 34261.5

---

## Text

The director shall, by regulation, adopt methods and procedures for the testing, weighing, and measuring of milk components and for the recording and reporting of such testing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 824.)
