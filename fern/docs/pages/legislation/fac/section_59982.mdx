# FAC § 59982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 13. Termination of Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59982

---

## Text

An expression by a majority of the producers that participate in the referendum shall govern the action of the director in declaring a program modified, continued, or suspended.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
