# FAC § 58035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58035

---

## Text

The director may foster and encourage the standardizing, grading, inspection, labeling, handling, storage, and sale of any product.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
