# FAC § 11508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 11508

---

## Text

The commissioner shall consider all relevant matter which is presented to him before he adopts any regulation.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
