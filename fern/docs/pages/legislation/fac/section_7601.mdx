# FAC § 7601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pest Seeds > Article: 4. Crop Seed Cleaning Business

**Citation:** FAC § 7601

---

## Text

Every person, before engaging for hire in the business of cleaning crop seed, shall obtain a permit from the commissioner to operate each separate portable seed cleaner which is being used for the first time in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
