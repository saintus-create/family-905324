# FAC § 19430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 12.5. Inspection Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 19430

---

## Text

Any person who is licensed pursuant to this chapter, who requests inspection at time which is considered as overtime by Section 19352 shall be liable for overtime inspection fees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
