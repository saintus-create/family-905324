# FAC § 40963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 9. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 40963

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to remove, deface, or destroy, any warning tag or notice which has been placed by a proper enforcing officer upon a rejected lot or load of tomatoes for processing purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 28.)
