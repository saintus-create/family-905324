# FAC § 9185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 6. Collections

**Citation:** FAC § 9185

---

## Text

Any fees that are provided for by any provision of this division and regulations promulgated pursuant to it shall be due and payable upon receipt of a statement from the department itemizing the kind and amount of those fees. The fees shall be paid to the secretary within 30 days of the receipt of the statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 133, Sec. 12. (AB 120) Effective July 26, 2011.)
