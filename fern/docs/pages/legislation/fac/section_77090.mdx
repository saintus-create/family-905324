# FAC § 77090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77090

---

## Text

The commission may promote the sale of walnuts by brand and generic advertising and other promotional means, including tie-in advertising, for the purpose of creating, maintaining, and expanding domestic and foreign markets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 609, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2000.)
