# FAC § 14978.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 4. Feed Inspection Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 14978.6

---

## Text

Any person licensed under this chapter may petition the director, in accordance with regulations adopted by the director, to review any action, order, or decision of the entity or entities established or designated pursuant to Section 14978.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1210, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
