# FAC § 9143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Disposal of Carcasses

**Citation:** FAC § 9143

---

## Text

An animal which has died from any contagious disease shall not be used for the food of any human being, domestic animal, or fowl.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
