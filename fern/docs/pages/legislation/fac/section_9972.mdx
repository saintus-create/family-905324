# FAC § 9972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 4. Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9972

---

## Text

The result of every tuberculosis test shall be reported to the State Veterinarian by the veterinarian who conducted the test within 48 hours after the completion of the test.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 19. Effective August 23, 2004.)
