# FAC § 59509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 59509

---

## Text

“Primary channel of trade” means that transaction in which the producer or a cooperative marketing association of producers transfers physical possession of the commodity to another person in the current of commercial trade.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
