# FAC § 14935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14935

---

## Text

“Medicated feed premixes” means a concentrated combination of one or more substances, at least one of which is a drug, which must be diluted through manufacturing into a medicated feed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
