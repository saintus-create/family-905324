# FAC § 59617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 59617

---

## Text

In carrying out his duties under this chapter, the director may utilize the facilities and personnel of the state and county departments of agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
