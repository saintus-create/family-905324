# FAC § 20470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Enforcement > Article: 3. Livestock Identification Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 20470

---

## Text

The board shall meet at the call of its chairperson. It shall meet at least twice in every calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 200, Sec. 1. (AB 2483) Effective January 1, 2017.)
