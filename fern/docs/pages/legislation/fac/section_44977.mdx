# FAC § 44977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 1. Container Certification and Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 44977

---

## Text

The director may adopt such regulations as he determines are reasonably necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 44976 by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 8. Effective March 31, 1982.)
