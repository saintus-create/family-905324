# FAC § 38631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 22. Concentrated Skim Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38631

---

## Text

Concentrated skim milk is skim milk which is derived from market milk that has been reduced to a semiliquid state by the removal of a portion of its water content by the aid of heat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
