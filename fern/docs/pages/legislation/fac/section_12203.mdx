# FAC § 12203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 8. Qualified Applicator License

**Citation:** FAC § 12203

---

## Text

Applicants shall be examined on the requirements of laws and regulations concerning pesticide use and shall elect to be examined for licensing in one or more of the categories established by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 60, Sec. 13. (AB 203) Effective June 30, 2022.)
