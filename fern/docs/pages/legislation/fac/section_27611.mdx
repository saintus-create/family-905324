# FAC § 27611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 10. Samples

**Citation:** FAC § 27611

---

## Text

Any sample which is taken in accordance with the prescribed sampling procedure is prima facie evidence of the true condition of the entire lot in the examination of which the sample was taken. The presumption established by this section is a presumption affecting the burden of proof.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
