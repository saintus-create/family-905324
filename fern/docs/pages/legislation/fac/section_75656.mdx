# FAC § 75656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 75656

---

## Text

Upon suspension of this chapter, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers and handlers whose names and addresses are on file with the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 60. Effective May 9, 1994.)
