# FAC § 81010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. INDUSTRIAL HEMP

**Citation:** FAC § 81010

---

## Text

This division, and Section 221 shall become operative on January 1, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 112. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017. Note: This section was amended on Nov. 8, 2016, by initiative Prop. 64, making Division 24 (commencing with Section 81000) operative on January 1, 2017.)
