# FAC § 2186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 4. Removal

**Citation:** FAC § 2186

---

## Text

If the order disqualifies the commissioner, the director shall revoke the commissioner’s license and declare his or her office vacant. A copy of the order shall be immediately transmitted by the director to the board of supervisors and the auditor of the county in which the commissioner held office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 734, Sec. 15. Effective September 18, 1987.)
