# FAC § 6035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 7. Beet Leafhopper Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6035

---

## Text

The secretary may establish the rate of assessment by district and crop and may adjust the assessment rate from time to time, whenever necessary as provided in Section 6036.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 508, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
