# FAC § 32102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.8. ANIMALS > Part: 2. California Veterinary Emergency Team Program

**Citation:** FAC § 32102

---

## Text

Funds made available for purposes of this part shall not be considered an offset to any other state funds appropriated to the University of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 144, Sec. 72. (AB 132) Effective July 27, 2021.)
