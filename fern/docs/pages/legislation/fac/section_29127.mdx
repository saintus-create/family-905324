# FAC § 29127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 9. Importation

**Citation:** FAC § 29127

---

## Text

Any American foulbrood disease or other disease found pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 29126 shall be abated pursuant to Article 14 (commencing with Section 29200) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
