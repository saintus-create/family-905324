# FAC § 36063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 12. Misrepresentation

**Citation:** FAC § 36063

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell, purchase, use, or represent as market milk or market cream any milk or cream which does not meet the requirements and standards of market milk or market cream.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
