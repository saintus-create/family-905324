# FAC § 59653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 5. Establishment of Proration Zone

**Citation:** FAC § 59653

---

## Text

All evidence and exhibits and all facts and data which are used directly or indirectly by the director, or introduced at a hearing shall, within a reasonable time after being used or introduced, be available at the office of the director to all interested parties.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
