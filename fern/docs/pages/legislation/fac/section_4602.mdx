# FAC § 4602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 5. CITRUS FRUIT FAIRS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 4602

---

## Text

“Association” means a nonprofit corporation organized and existing under the laws of this state for the purpose of and which engages in conducting and carrying on a citrus fruit fair.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
