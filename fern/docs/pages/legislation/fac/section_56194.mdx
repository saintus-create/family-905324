# FAC § 56194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 56194

---

## Text

The director shall issue to any individual or member of a partnership, a card which specifies that he or his firm is licensed. The licensee shall show such card upon the request of any interested person.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 632.)
