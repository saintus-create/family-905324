# FAC § 2243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 7. Offices, Equipment and Supplies

**Citation:** FAC § 2243

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may also provide the commissioner with all necessary field equipment for the proper discharge of the duties of his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
