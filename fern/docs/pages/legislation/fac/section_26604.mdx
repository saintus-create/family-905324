# FAC § 26604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Sale, Possession, and Transportation > Article: 3. Transportation

**Citation:** FAC § 26604

---

## Text

The carrier from whom the poultry is seized pursuant to Section 26603 is not liable or responsible to the owner, shipper, or consignee for the seizure.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
