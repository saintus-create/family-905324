# FAC § 78257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78257

---

## Text

The commission may accept contributions or matching private, state, or federal funds, and employ or make contributions of funds to other persons or state or federal agencies, for purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1990.)
