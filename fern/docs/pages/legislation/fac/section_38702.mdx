# FAC § 38702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 26. Sour Cream Dressing

**Citation:** FAC § 38702

---

## Text

Sour cream dressing shall be labeled with the term “sour cream dressing” and with the name and address of the distributor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
