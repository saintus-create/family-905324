# FAC § 10302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 1. Definitions

## Text

“Accredited veterinarian” means a veterinarian who is so designated by the United States Department of Agriculture and the department.
