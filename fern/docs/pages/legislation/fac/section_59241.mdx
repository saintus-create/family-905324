# FAC § 59241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 21. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 59241

---

## Text

If the matter is referred directly by the director to the Attorney General or any district attorney, such officer shall thereupon bring an appropriate action in a court of competent jurisdiction in this state, if, after examination of the complaint and evidence he believes that a violation has occurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
