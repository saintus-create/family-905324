# FAC § 39752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 6. UHT Flavored Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 39752

---

## Text

UHT flavored cream shall contain not less than 20 percent milk fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
