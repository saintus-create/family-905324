# FAC § 3954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Organization and Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 3954

---

## Text

Each association by its name has perpetual succession. It may have a seal. An association may sue and be sued, and may do any and all things necessary to carry out the powers and the objects and purposes for which the association is formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 342, Sec. 1. (AB 2490) Effective January 1, 2015.)
