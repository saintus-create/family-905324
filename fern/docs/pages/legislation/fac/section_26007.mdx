# FAC § 26007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 5. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 26007

---

## Text

If a class of any kind of poultry meat contains two or more designations, any combination of the designations may appear in the class marking which is required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
