# FAC § 25094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 5. Requirements for Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 25094

---

## Text

All poultry meat which is condemned for human food shall be disposed of pursuant to the regulations.
