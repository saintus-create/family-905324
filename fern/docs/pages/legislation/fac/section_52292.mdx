# FAC § 52292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 2.5. Seed Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52292

---

## Text

The term of office for each member of the board is three years. Vacancies shall be filled by the secretary for an unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 573, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
