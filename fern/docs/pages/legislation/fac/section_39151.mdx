# FAC § 39151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Imitation Ice Cream and Ice Milk > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 39151

---

## Text

The ingredients, except fruits, nuts, and flavors, which are used in the manufacture of imitation ice cream or imitation ice milk, shall be pasteurized.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
