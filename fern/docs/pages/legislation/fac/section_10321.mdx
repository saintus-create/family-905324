# FAC § 10321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 10321

---

## Text

The department may enter into any cooperative arrangement with the United States Department of Agriculture to undertake brucellosis control pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
