# FAC § 901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. THE STATE BOARD OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** FAC § 901

## Text

There is in the state government the State Board of Food and Agriculture which consists of 15 members.
