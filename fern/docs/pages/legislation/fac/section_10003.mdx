# FAC § 10003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 5. Tuberculosis Control Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 10003

---

## Text

When the department establishes a tuberculosis control area, the affected industry shall be notified.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 31. Effective August 23, 2004.)
