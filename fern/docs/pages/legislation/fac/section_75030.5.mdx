# FAC § 75030.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75030.5

---

## Text

“Out-of-state handler” means any person located in another state who handles eggs or egg products, as specified in Section 75030, in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 121, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1998.)
