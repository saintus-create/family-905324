# FAC § 25701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 25701

---

## Text

This part, except Section 26008, does not apply to poultry meat or a container of poultry meat which is marked with the official class as prescribed by the Consumer and Marketing Service of the United States Department of Agriculture; if such official class marking meets the requirements of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
