# FAC § 22007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 10. Slaughter of Cattle > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 22007

---

## Text

Any owner or producer of livestock may have them inspected and slaughtered by a licensed slaughterer. The owner or producer may, thereafter, sell the meat from those livestock.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 828, Sec. 6.)
