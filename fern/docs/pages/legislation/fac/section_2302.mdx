# FAC § 2302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. County Veterinarian > Article: 1. Appointment

**Citation:** FAC § 2302

---

## Text

The veterinarian shall serve at the pleasure of the board of supervisors.
