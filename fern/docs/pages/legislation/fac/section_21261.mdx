# FAC § 21261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 8. Shipping Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 21261

---

## Text

The chief may provide for the issuance of shipping permits for inspected cattle which are shipped by railroad, or for cattle that are covered by one certificate of inspection, which are transported in more than one conveyance.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
