# FAC § 9641.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 6. Importation

**Citation:** FAC § 9641.7

---

## Text

Any positive test for equine infectious anemia performed in a California laboratory shall be reported by telephone within 24 hours to the department by the laboratory making the test.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1030.)
