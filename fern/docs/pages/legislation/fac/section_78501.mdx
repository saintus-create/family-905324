# FAC § 78501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 78501

---

## Text

The council shall keep books, records, and accounts of all of its transactions, dealings, contracts, agreements, funds, and expenditures that it deems necessary. The books, records, and accounts shall at all times be open to inspection and audit by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
