# FAC § 60011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 14. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 60011

---

## Text

Any action for penalties or other remedies which is prescribed in this chapter shall be commenced within two years from the date of the alleged violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
