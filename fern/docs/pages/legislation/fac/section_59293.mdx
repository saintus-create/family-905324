# FAC § 59293

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 22. Inspection and Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 59293

---

## Text

Disposal of any lot or portion of any lot pursuant to the provisions of this article, whether such disposal be by arrangement with an enforcement officer or by court order, does not waive any of the penalty provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
