# FAC § 29446

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29446

---

## Text

An enforcing officer shall cause the prosecution of any person whom he knows or has reason to believe is guilty of violating any provision of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
