# FAC § 5251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Newly Discovered Pests

**Citation:** FAC § 5251

---

## Text

Upon the discovery of any pest, the director shall immediately report the discovery to the commissioner of the county in which the pest is found.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
