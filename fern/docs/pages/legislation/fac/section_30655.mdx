# FAC § 30655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 2. Disposition of Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 30655

---

## Text

If a claim is allowed, it shall be paid from the fund which is provided for in this chapter in the same manner as other claims against the county are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
