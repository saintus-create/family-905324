# FAC § 56701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7.2. Market Enforcement Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 56701

---

## Text

(a) The definitions set forth in Article 1 (commencing with Section 55401) of Chapter 6 apply to this chapter.

(b) For purposes of this chapter, “committee” means the Market Enforcement Advisory Committee established pursuant to Section 56702.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 603, Sec. 10. (SB 1270) Effective January 1, 2025.)
