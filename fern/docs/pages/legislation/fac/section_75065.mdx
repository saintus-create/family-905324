# FAC § 75065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 3. The California Egg Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75065

---

## Text

A quorum of the commission is five voting commissioners. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the commission may act by a vote of a majority of the members present at a meeting at which there is a quorum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
