# FAC § 6744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 1. Licenses > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 6744

---

## Text

Any person that sells cut Christmas trees is exempt from the license requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 42, Sec. 1.)
