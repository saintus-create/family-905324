# FAC § 38398

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 12. Extra Grade Edible Dry Whey, Dry Buttermilk, and Modified Dry Whey

**Citation:** FAC § 38398

---

## Text

Modified dry whey means the class of foods manufactured from fresh, pasteurized, cheese whey by various processes and procedures under conditions prescribed by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 790.)
