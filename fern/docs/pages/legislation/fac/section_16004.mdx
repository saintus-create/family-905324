# FAC § 16004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. VESSEL AND AIRCRAFT GARBAGE > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 16004

---

## Text

“Garbage” means waste material, such as food scraps, table refuse, galley refuse, and refuse from stores of vessels and aircraft, including such waste material in passengers’ and crews’ quarters, which is derived, in whole or in part, from fruits, vegetables, or animal products.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
