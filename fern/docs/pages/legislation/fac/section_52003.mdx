# FAC § 52003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Field Crops > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 52003

---

## Text

The director may enter any place where field crop products are stored, shipped, or sold, for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
