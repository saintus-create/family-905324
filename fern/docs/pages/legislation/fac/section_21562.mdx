# FAC § 21562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 21562

---

## Text

Any fees which are due and payable for brand inspections that are made which remain unpaid or are not paid as required by this article may be recovered by the director pursuant to Section 281. Inspection services may be refused for nonpayment of prior services which have been rendered.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
