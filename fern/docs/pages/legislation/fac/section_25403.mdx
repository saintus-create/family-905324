# FAC § 25403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 25403

---

## Text

“Fowl” includes chickens, turkeys, ducks, geese, and other domesticated birds.

(History: Enacted by stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
