# FAC § 9700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 8. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9700

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to violate any other provision of this chapter or any regulation or order which is made pursuant to it.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
