# FAC § 6047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8. Pierce’s Disease

**Citation:** FAC § 6047

---

## Text

The secretary may establish, maintain, and enforce regulations consistent with the intent of the Legislature as expressed in this article as may be necessary to interpret, clarify, or implement this article. This authority shall be liberally construed to effectuate the intent of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 21, Sec. 1. Effective May 19, 2000. Inoperative March 1, 2031. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 6046.)
