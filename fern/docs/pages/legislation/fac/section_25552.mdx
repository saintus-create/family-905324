# FAC § 25552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 25552

---

## Text

The director shall enforce this part where any poultry meat is packed, repacked, cut up, or recut.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
