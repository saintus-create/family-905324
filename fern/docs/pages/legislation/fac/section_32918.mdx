# FAC § 32918

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32918

---

## Text

Every vehicle from which any milk or milk product is regularly sold and marketed or peddled shall have the name of the distributor plainly lettered upon it in letters at least 3 inches high on both sides of such vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
