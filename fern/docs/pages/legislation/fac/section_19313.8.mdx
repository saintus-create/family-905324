# FAC § 19313.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 6.5. Transporters of Inedible Kitchen Grease

**Citation:** FAC § 19313.8

---

## Text

A registered transporter or any other person shall not take possession of inedible kitchen grease from an unregistered transporter, an unlicensed collection center, or an unlicensed renderer, or knowingly take possession of stolen inedible kitchen grease.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 595, Sec. 7. (AB 1566) Effective January 1, 2015.)
