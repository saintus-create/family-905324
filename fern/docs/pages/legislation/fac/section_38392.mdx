# FAC § 38392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 12. Extra Grade Edible Dry Whey, Dry Buttermilk, and Modified Dry Whey

**Citation:** FAC § 38392

---

## Text

Extra grade edible dry whey and its reliquefied form shall have a clean whey flavor, free from objectional and nonwhey flavors and odors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
