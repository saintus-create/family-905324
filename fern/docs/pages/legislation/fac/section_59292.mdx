# FAC § 59292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 22. Inspection and Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 59292

---

## Text

In the event of sale of any lot by order of court, the costs of storage, handling, and reconditioning or disposal shall be deducted from the proceeds of sale and the balance, if any, paid into court for the account of the owner of any such lot.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
