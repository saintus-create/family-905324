# FAC § 78674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 24. California Tomato Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78674

---

## Text

The commission may solicit and accept contributions of, or match private, state, or federal funds, and employ or make contributions of funds to other persons or state or federal agencies for purposes of promoting and maintaining the tomato industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 373, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2002.)
