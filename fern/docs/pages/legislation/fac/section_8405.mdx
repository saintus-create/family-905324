# FAC § 8405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 8405

---

## Text

“Citrus acreage” means any parcel, tract, or lot of land with 25 or more citrus trees of any age growing on it, except a parcel, tract, or lot which is used as a nursery.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 904, Sec. 4.)
