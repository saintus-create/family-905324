# FAC § 7302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 1. Weeds > Article: 2. Camelthorn

**Citation:** FAC § 7302

---

## Text

The director shall eradicate camelthorn wherever it exists in this state.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 118.)
