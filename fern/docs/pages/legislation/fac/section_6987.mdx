# FAC § 6987

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 5. Special Assessments

**Citation:** FAC § 6987

---

## Text

The secretary shall not renew a nursery license to any applicant who has failed to pay an assessment due pursuant to this article within 60 days of the due date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 576, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
