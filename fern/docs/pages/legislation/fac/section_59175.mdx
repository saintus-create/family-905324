# FAC § 59175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 19. Seasonal Marketing Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 59175

---

## Text

It is recognized that, with respect to some commodities, marketing, weather, and other conditions may change so rapidly as to require changes in seasonal marketing regulations from week to week or oftener.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
