# FAC § 76147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 76147

---

## Text

Any assessment which is levied as provided for in this chapter, is a personal debt of every producer so assessed. Failure of a processor, shipper, or grower-handler to collect the assessment does not relieve the producer of the obligation to pay the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 65. Effective September 26, 1985.)
