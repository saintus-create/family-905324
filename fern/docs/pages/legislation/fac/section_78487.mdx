# FAC § 78487

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 4. California Seafood Council

**Citation:** FAC § 78487

---

## Text

There is no fixed term for nonvoting members of the council. The agencies listed in Section 78486 may select an alternate for their representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
