# FAC § 9979

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 4. Tuberculosis Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 9979

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to apply a tuberculosis test to any bovine animal that has at any time been found by an approved veterinarian to be a reactor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 26. Effective August 23, 2004.)
