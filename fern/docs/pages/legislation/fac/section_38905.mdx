# FAC § 38905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 38905

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a restaurant or any other place where food is served to the public for consumption on the premises to misrepresent products resembling milk products as milk products.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 787, Sec. 4.2.)
