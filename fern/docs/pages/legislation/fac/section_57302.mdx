# FAC § 57302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 10. Private Produce Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 57302

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may, pursuant to this chapter, establish a registration fee in an amount necessary to cover the actual costs of the commissioner for that registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1039, Sec. 1.)
