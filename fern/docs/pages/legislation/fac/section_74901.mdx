# FAC § 74901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74901

---

## Text

The commission may accept contributions of, or match, private, local, state, or federal funds and employ or make contributions of funds to other persons or local, state or federal agencies for purposes of maintaining, promoting, and enhancing the winegrape industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 587, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
