# FAC § 39431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Margarine, Dairy Spread, and Spread > Article: 4. Misrepresentation

**Citation:** FAC § 39431

---

## Text

A person shall not sell or take orders for the future delivery of any margarine, dairy spread, or spread under the name of butter, or under the pretense that the substance is butter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 512, Sec. 14.)
