# FAC § 74730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 3. The California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74730

---

## Text

(a) There is in state government the California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission. The commission shall be composed of nine grape rootstock nursery owners or representatives who shall be called members.

(b) The director and other appropriate individuals, as determined by the commission, shall be ex officio members of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
