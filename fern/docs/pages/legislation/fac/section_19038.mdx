# FAC § 19038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 10. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 19038

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person knowingly to represent that an article has been examined by a licensed livestock meat inspector, licensed processing inspector, or department inspector or exempted under this chapter when in fact it has respectively not been so examined or exempted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 680, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1999.)
