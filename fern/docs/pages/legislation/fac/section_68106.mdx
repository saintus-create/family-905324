# FAC § 68106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 68106

---

## Text

Every handler shall be personally liable for the payment of the collected assessments, and failure of the handler to collect the assessment from any producer shall not exempt any handler from such liability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1027.)
