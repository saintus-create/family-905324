# FAC § 5573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Abatement of Neglected or Abandoned Crops > Article: 2. Institution of Proceedings

**Citation:** FAC § 5573

---

## Text

The petition shall set forth all of the following:

(a) A description of the property.

(b) The name of the owner or person that is in charge or possession of the property.

(c) The name of the pest, or other condition which is a menace to the agriculture of the county, district, or vicinity.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
