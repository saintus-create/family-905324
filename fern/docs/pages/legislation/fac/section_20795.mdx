# FAC § 20795

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 7. Similar Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 20795

---

## Text

The owner of a recorded brand may request the chief to make an investigation if there are cattle other than his own which bear a brand so similar to such owner’s brand as to be misleading or conflicting.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
