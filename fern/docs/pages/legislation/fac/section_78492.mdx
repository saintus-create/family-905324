# FAC § 78492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 4. California Seafood Council

**Citation:** FAC § 78492

---

## Text

No member of the council, except a member of the executive committee, shall receive per diem for more than seven days per month, except the chairperson, who may receive per diem for not more than 10 days per month.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
