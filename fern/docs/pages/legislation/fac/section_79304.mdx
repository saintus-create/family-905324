# FAC § 79304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 9. Quality Standards

## Text

The commission shall serve as the advisory body to the secretary on all matters pertaining to this article.
