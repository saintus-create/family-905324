# FAC § 12045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 4. Agricultural Pest Control Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 12045

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 12046, the term of office of the members of the committee is three years. Vacancies shall be filled for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 459, Sec. 4.)
