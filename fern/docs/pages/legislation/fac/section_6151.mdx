# FAC § 6151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 3. Caprifigs

**Citation:** FAC § 6151

---

## Text

Caprifigs, unless properly treated, contribute to, and are responsible for, the transmission of endosepsis and other plant diseases by the blastophaga which the figs contain.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
