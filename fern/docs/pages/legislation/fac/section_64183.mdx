# FAC § 64183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 6. Research, Advertising, Publicity, and Education Campaigns

**Citation:** FAC § 64183

---

## Text

An advertising or sales promotion program that is conducted or sponsored by the council shall not make any false or unwarranted claim, shall not disparage the quality, value, use, or sale of any commodity that is authorized by law to be marketed in this state, and shall not be conducted with reference to a brand or trade name.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 94. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
