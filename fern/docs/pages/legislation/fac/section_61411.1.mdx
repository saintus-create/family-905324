# FAC § 61411.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 5. Bonding and Payment for Manufacturing Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 61411.1

---

## Text

The production of manufacturing milk in excess of amounts to be purchased under contracts executed pursuant to Section 61411 shall be voluntary on the part of the producer and shall not be a condition, oral or written, of the execution or renewal of any contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 227, Sec. 4.)
