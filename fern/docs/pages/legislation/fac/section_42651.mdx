# FAC § 42651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 1. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 42651

---

## Text

The director and the commissioners of each county of the state, their deputies and inspectors, under the supervision and control of the director shall enforce this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
