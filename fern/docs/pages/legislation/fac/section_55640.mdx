# FAC § 55640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 9. Producer’s Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 55640

---

## Text

If all lawful claims of the producers have been paid in accordance with this article by any processor, any product which is released by such action may be sold, transported, or otherwise disposed of.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
