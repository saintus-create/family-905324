# FAC § 20901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 5. Cattle Record Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 20901

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, cattle may be branded pursuant to this chapter.
