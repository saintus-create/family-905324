# FAC § 64562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 64562

---

## Text

Upon recommendations by the council the director may establish consistent with this chapter such regulations which cover the administration and enforcement of this chapter as may be necessary to carry out the purposes and attain the objectives of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
