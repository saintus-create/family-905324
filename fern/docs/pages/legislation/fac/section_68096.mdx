# FAC § 68096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 68096

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 68092, the secretary shall so certify and declare all provisions of this chapter inoperative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1996.)
