# FAC § 79885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 79885

---

## Text

The commission shall not be required to allege or prove that an adequate remedy at law does not exist in any action brought under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
