# FAC § 27636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 12. Unlawful Acts, General

**Citation:** FAC § 27636

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell as “fresh eggs,” “ranch eggs,” or “farm eggs,” or to represent as being fresh, any eggs which are below the quality of grade A or which have been held in cold storage more than 30 days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
