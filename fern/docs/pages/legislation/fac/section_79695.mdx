# FAC § 79695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 28. California Apiary Research Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 79695

---

## Text

The commission is not required to allege or prove that an adequate remedy at law does not exist in any action brought under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 585, Sec. 1. (AB 1912) Effective January 1, 2011.)
