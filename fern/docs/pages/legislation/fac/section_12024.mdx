# FAC § 12024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 2. Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 12024

---

## Text

The director by regulation shall establish the minimum requirement for pest control adviser by education, experience, and examination to carry out the purposes of this division.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 676.)
