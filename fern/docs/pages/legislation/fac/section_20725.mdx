# FAC § 20725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 5. Suspension and Forfeiture

**Citation:** FAC § 20725

---

## Text

(a) Any person convicted of Section 487a of the Penal Code shall be prohibited from registering or owning, in whole or in part, a brand for a period of five years following the date of the conviction.

(b) Upon being notified of the conviction, the secretary shall terminate the registration of any brand registered to a person convicted of Section 487a of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 129, Sec. 1. (AB 1722) Effective January 1, 2015.)
