# FAC § 59945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 12. Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 59945

---

## Text

Upon the request of any marketing program committee, the director shall confer with the committee or its representatives prior to fixing the amount or proportion of any fees of the marketing program which are payable to the department for maintenance of the department in the performance of the duties which are required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
