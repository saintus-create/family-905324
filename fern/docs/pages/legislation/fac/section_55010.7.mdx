# FAC § 55010.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55010.7

---

## Text

“Handle” means to engage in the business of being a handler.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2009.)
