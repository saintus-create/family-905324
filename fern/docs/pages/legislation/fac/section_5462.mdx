# FAC § 5462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Abatement, Generally > Article: 3. Notice and Expense of Abatement on Tax-Deeded Property

**Citation:** FAC § 5462

---

## Text

If the expense of abatement becomes a lien on such property, as provided in Sections 5429 to 5432, inclusive, and the property is redeemed or sold to a private person at a tax sale, the action to foreclose the lien shall be commenced within 120 days after the redemption or sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
