# FAC § 29442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29442

---

## Text

The refusal of any officer who is authorized pursuant to this chapter to carry out the orders and directions of the director in the enforcement of this chapter is neglect of duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
