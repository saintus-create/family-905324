# FAC § 61834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61834

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, firm, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, incorporated or unincorporated association, nonprofit cooperative association, nonprofit corporation, or any other business unit or organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 130. Effective January 1, 1995.)
