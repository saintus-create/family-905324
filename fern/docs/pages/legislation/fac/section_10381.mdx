# FAC § 10381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 5. Brucellosis Control Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 10381

---

## Text

The department may, after hearing, establish and maintain brucellosis control areas within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
