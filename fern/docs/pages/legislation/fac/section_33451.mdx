# FAC § 33451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 33451

---

## Text

A dairy farm which is insanitary, deficient, or unsatisfactory is subject to the action provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
