# FAC § 8701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 7. Consolidation

**Citation:** FAC § 8701

---

## Text

Any two or more districts organized or existing under this part may be consolidated, whether or not the boundaries are coterminous and whether or not the districts are located in the same county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
