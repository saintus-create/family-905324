# FAC § 8408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 8408

---

## Text

“Number of trees per acre of average planting,” for the purpose of assessment pursuant to this part, shall be 100 trees per acre.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
