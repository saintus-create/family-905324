# FAC § 29584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 7. Comb Honey

**Citation:** FAC § 29584

---

## Text

Opaque containers of bulk or chunk comb honey shall be marked with the color the honey would be if extracted, unless it is in the unclassified grade.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
