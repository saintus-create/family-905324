# FAC § 41865.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 8. Garlic and Onion Dehydrator Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41865.3

---

## Text

The committee shall not recommend and the secretary shall not utilize, a private entity a majority of whose officers and employees are persons regulated by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 520, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
