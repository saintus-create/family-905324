# FAC § 69068.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 69068.5

---

## Text

To be eligible to sign a nomination petition pursuant to Section 69068, a person shall meet either of the following qualifications:

(a) Be a producer as provided in Section 69027.

(b) Be engaged within this state in the business of growing nonproducing pistachio trees for the purpose of becoming a producer as defined in Section 69027 and who is not otherwise exempt from this chapter pursuant to Section 69082.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 391, Sec. 4. Effective July 17, 1986.)
