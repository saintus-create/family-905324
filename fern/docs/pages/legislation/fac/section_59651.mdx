# FAC § 59651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 5. Establishment of Proration Zone

**Citation:** FAC § 59651

---

## Text

At the public hearing, the director shall receive evidence offered by the petitioners in support of the proposed proration zone and the proposed marketing program and evidence from any interested person in support of, or in opposition to, them. All testimony at the hearings shall be under oath.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
