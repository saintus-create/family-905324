# FAC § 58093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 4. Collection and Dissemination of Information

**Citation:** FAC § 58093

---

## Text

The director shall cooperate with the Secretary of Agriculture, and with the appropriate office of the State of Oregon, to establish a market news service in the Klamath Basin.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
