# FAC § 75596

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75596

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt an annual budget according to accepted practices. The secretary shall concur in the adoption of the budget prior to the encumbrance of funds, except for encumbrances necessary to pay the compensation of employees of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 34. Effective May 9, 1994.)
