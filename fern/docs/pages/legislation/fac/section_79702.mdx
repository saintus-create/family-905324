# FAC § 79702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 28. California Apiary Research Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 79702

---

## Text

Following a hearing, and favorable referendum if required, the process specified in Section 79701 shall be conducted by the secretary every fifth year thereafter, between January 1 and December 31, unless a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to Section 79703.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 296, Sec. 108. (AB 1023) Effective January 1, 2012.)
