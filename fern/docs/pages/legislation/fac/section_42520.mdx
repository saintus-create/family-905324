# FAC § 42520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 42520

---

## Text

“Subcontainer” means any container which is being used within another container.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
