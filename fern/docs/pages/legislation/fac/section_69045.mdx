# FAC § 69045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 3. The California Pistachio Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 69045

---

## Text

All proprietary information obtained by the commission or the secretary from producers, processors, or producer-suppliers shall be confidential and shall not be disclosed except when required by a court order after a hearing in a judicial proceeding involving this chapter. Information on volume shipments, crop value, and any other related information that is required for reports to governmental agencies, financial reports to the commission or aggregate sales and inventory information, and any other information that the processors or producer-suppliers request from the commission to receive in total, excluding individual processor or producer-supplier information, may be disclosed by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 69084 by Stats. 1997, Ch. 726, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1998.)
