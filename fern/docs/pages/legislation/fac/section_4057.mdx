# FAC § 4057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 4057

---

## Text

The state is not liable for any action, obligation, commitment, contract, or premium that is offered, or award that is made, by any association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 342, Sec. 9. (AB 2490) Effective January 1, 2015.)
