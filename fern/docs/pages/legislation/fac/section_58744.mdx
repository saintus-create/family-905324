# FAC § 58744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 5. Marketing Orders and Agreements

**Citation:** FAC § 58744

---

## Text

Any marketing order which is issued by the director pursuant to this chapter may be limited in its application by prescribing the marketing area or portion of the state in which it shall be effective. A marketing order shall not, however, be issued by the director unless it embraces all persons of a like class that are engaged in a specific and distinctive agricultural industry or trade within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
