# FAC § 38602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 21. Pasteurized Concentrated Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38602

---

## Text

Pasteurized concentrated milk shall be packaged in containers other than hermetically sealed containers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
