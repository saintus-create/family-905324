# FAC § 63003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Marketing of Avocados

**Citation:** FAC § 63003

---

## Text

The record and statement required to be made by this chapter shall be kept for one year from the date of purchase or delivery, whichever is later, and shall be made available for inspection upon request by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 98.)
