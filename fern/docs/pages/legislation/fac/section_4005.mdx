# FAC § 4005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 4. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 4005

---

## Text

The fiscal year for each association is from January 1 to December 31.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
