# FAC § 58782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 6. Notice, Reports, and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 58782

---

## Text

The hearing on a proposed marketing order or proposed amendments shall be public and all testimony shall be received under oath. A full and complete record of all proceedings at such hearing shall be made and maintained on file in the office of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
