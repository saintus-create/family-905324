# FAC § 47005.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10.5. Direct Marketing > Article: 1.5. Certified Farmers’ Markets

**Citation:** FAC § 47005.3

---

## Text

Any evidence that is seized under the authority of this chapter or the regulations adopted thereunder by an enforcing officer in any county may be admitted into evidence in any action taken by any other county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 373, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2002.)
