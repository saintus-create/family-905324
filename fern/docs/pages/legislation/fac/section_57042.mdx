# FAC § 57042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 6. Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 57042

---

## Text

The director may assess a fee not to exceed four hundred dollars ($400) per year payable by produce dealers for use exclusively in administering the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 470.)
