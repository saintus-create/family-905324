# FAC § 38887

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 39. Fromage Frais or Soft Fresh Cheese

**Citation:** FAC § 38887

---

## Text

Fromage frais or soft fresh cheese, without the addition of bulky flavors, shall contain not less than 3.5 percent of milk fat.

Fromage frais or soft fresh cheese made from goat milk, without the addition of bulky flavors, shall contain not less than 2.8 percent of milk fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 39. Effective August 21, 1997.)
