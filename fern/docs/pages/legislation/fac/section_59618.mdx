# FAC § 59618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 59618

---

## Text

A full and accurate record of business or acts which are performed, or of testimony which is taken pursuant to the provisions of this chapter, shall be kept and be placed on file in the office of the director.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 371.)
