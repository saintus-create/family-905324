# FAC § 79237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 3. The California Blueberry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79237

---

## Text

A producer member or his or her alternate on the commission shall be an individual, partner, or employee of a producer who has a financial interest in producing, or causing to be produced, blueberries for market. The producer member or his or her alternate shall be so qualified during the entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
