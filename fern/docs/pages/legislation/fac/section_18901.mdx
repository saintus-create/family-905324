# FAC § 18901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 9. Investigations

**Citation:** FAC § 18901

---

## Text

The director may gather and compile information, and investigate the organization, business, conduct, practices, management, and the relation to other persons of any person subject to this chapter engaged in intrastate commerce.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
