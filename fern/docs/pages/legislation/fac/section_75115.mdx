# FAC § 75115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 75115

---

## Text

If the director finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in Section 75112, he or she shall certify and give notice of the favorable vote, and that this chapter is operative, to all handlers whose names and addresses are on the list of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
