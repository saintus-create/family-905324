# FAC § 77029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16.5. California Walnut Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77029

---

## Text

“Pack” means to bleach, clean, grade, shell, or otherwise prepare walnuts for market as inshell or shelled walnuts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 409, Sec. 8. Effective September 22, 2006.)
