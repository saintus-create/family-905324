# FAC § 77336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 77336

---

## Text

Every handler shall be personally liable for the payment of collected assessments to the commission. Failure of a handler to collect the assessment shall not exempt the handler from liability and shall not relieve any person subject to this chapter from the obligation to pay the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 335, Sec. 1. Effective July 14, 1988.)
