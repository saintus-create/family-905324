# FAC § 58003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58003

---

## Text

“Product” means any agricultural, aquacultural, dairy, or farm product which is produced, grown, raised, manufactured, or processed within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 810, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1996.)
