# FAC § 64303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 11. Financing Plan

**Citation:** FAC § 64303

---

## Text

Each handler shall pay to the secretary the assessments established for all milk used in the plant of the handler in the processing and manufacture of milk and milk products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 18. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
