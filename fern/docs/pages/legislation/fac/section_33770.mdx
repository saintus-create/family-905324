# FAC § 33770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Milk Products Plants > Article: 3. Milk Products Plant Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33770

---

## Text

No species of animal life other than man shall be permitted in any building which is used in connection with any milk products plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
