# FAC § 19350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 19350

---

## Text

Vehicles used for dead animal hauling shall be completely unloaded, cleaned, and disinfected at the rendering plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 187, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2005.)
