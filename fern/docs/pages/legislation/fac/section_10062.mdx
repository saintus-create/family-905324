# FAC § 10062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 7. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10062

---

## Text

The value of the reacting bovine animal for which indemnity is paid shall be determined in accordance with the provisions in Sections 9592 and 9593.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 42. Effective August 23, 2004.)
