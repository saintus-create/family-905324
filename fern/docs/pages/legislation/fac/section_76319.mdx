# FAC § 76319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 76319

---

## Text

Subsequent to the first election of members of the commission pursuant to this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to nomination and election procedures as shall be established by the commission with the concurrence of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
