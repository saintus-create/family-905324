# FAC § 35755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 35755

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell any market milk other than graded milk as provided in this division in any city or county or any combination of cities and counties in which a milk inspection service has been established, either by or with the approval of the director.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
