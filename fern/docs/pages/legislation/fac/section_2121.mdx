# FAC § 2121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 2. Appointment and Employment

**Citation:** FAC § 2121

---

## Text

The commissioner shall be appointed by the board of supervisors of the county. Any chartered county may, however, prescribe a different method of appointment.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
