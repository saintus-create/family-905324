# FAC § 6297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Napa County Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 6. Inclusion of Lands

**Citation:** FAC § 6297

---

## Text

Lands devoted exclusively to the growing of winegrapes within a tract of land outside the district, but in the county in which the district is located, may be annexed to the district in the same manner provided in this chapter for the formation of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
