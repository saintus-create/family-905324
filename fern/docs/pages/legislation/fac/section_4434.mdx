# FAC § 4434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions

**Citation:** FAC § 4434

---

## Text

A district agricultural association of a district created subsequent to January 1, 1943, may receive an apportionment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
