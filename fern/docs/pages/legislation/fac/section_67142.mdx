# FAC § 67142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 10. Quality Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 67142

---

## Text

Any standards or activities recommended by the commission and concurred in by the secretary, shall not be operative until approved in the manner specified in Section 67121.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 946, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 2003.)
