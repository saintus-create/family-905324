# FAC § 15056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 10. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 15056

---

## Text

The penalty for the manufacture or distribution of a commercial feed without a valid license as specified in Section 15051 is a violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 683, Sec. 3. (SB 668) Effective January 1, 2019.)
