# FAC § 69033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 3. The California Pistachio Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 69033

---

## Text

The commission or the secretary may bring an action for judicial relief from the secretary’s written notice, or from noncompliance by the commission with the written notice, in a court of competent jurisdiction, which may issue a temporary restraining order, permanent injunction, or other applicable relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 1996.)
