# FAC § 10726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 1. Importation and Sale > Article: 2. Importation

**Citation:** FAC § 10726

---

## Text

Swine that are imported into this state for immediate slaughter shall have as their destination a recognized slaughtering establishment. They shall not be diverted for any other purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 119, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
