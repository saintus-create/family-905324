# FAC § 14532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14532

---

## Text

“Farm use” means that the fertilizing material is used primarily for application to crops produced for commercial value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 856) Effective January 1, 2010.)
