# FAC § 76384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 76384

---

## Text

Upon suspension of the operation of this chapter, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of suspension to all producers affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
