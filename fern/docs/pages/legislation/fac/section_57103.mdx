# FAC § 57103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. Dairy Produce Exchanges > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 57103

---

## Text

The director shall have access to all records of every dairy produce exchange which is licensed pursuant to this chapter, and may appoint a representative to sit with the board of directors or other governing body of such exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
