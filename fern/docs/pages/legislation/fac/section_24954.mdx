# FAC § 24954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 24954

---

## Text

“Marked” means plainly, legibly, and conspicuously labeled, tagged, stenciled, or branded pursuant to this chapter and any regulations which are adopted pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
