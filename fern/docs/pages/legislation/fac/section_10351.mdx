# FAC § 10351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 4. Vaccinations

**Citation:** FAC § 10351

---

## Text

All female dairy calves shall be vaccinated by an official veterinarian or an accredited veterinarian, who is authorized by the department, at such ages as are designated by the department. Male dairy calves and beef calves may, at the option of the owner, be vaccinated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
