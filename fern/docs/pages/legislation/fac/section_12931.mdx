# FAC § 12931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 8. Analysis

**Citation:** FAC § 12931

---

## Text

The director may take samples of pesticides, make analyses or examinations of them, and make such investigations as are necessary for the full enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 695, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 1998.)
