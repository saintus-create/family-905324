# FAC § 61308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61308

---

## Text

“Manufacturer” means any person that is engaged in the business of manufacturing any dairy product.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
