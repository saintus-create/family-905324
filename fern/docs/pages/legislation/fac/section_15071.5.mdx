# FAC § 15071.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 12. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 15071.5

---

## Text

In lieu of any other penalty provided by this chapter, the secretary may initiate a proceeding for the issuance of a civil penalty.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 683, Sec. 8. (SB 668) Effective January 1, 2019.)
