# FAC § 34032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 2. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 34032

---

## Text

Any vat, tank, or container which is used to store a milk byproduct shall be kept in a clean and sanitary condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
