# FAC § 6002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 5. Cotton Pest Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6002

---

## Text

The director, or an entity designated by the Cotton Pest Control Board, may contract with the United States Department of Agriculture for the purpose of controlling, suppressing, or eradicating cotton boll weevil or pink bollworm in this state, the State of Arizona, or the Republic of Mexico to prevent the entry and establishment of these pests in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 953, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1985.)
