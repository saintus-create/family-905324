# FAC § 35831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 4. Pasteurized and Raw Market Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 35831

---

## Text

Pasteurized market milk shall be cooled to a temperature of 50 degrees Fahrenheit or below immediately after pasteurization and shall be so maintained until it is delivered to the consumer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
