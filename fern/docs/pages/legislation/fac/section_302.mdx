# FAC § 302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 6. Refunds

**Citation:** FAC § 302

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the director may authorize the refund of any money which is received or collected by the department in the payment of any fee, assessment, or tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 519, Sec. 1.)
