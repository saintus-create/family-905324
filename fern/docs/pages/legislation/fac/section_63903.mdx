# FAC § 63903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 1. LEGISLATIVE DECLARATIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAC § 63903

---

## Text

In addition to the authority granted to any commission by Part 2 (commencing with Section 64001), those commissions may commence or participate in administrative and civil actions relative to the activities of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
