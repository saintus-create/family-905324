# FAC § 38454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 14. Milk Drink Mix

**Citation:** FAC § 38454

---

## Text

Milk drink mix shall be pasteurized and shall contain not more than 75,000 bacteria per gram.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 306.)
