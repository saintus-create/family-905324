# FAC § 33521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 3. Dairy Farm Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33521

---

## Text

The containers, appliances, or equipment, after sterilization, shall be adequately dried and protected from flies, dust, and other contamination.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
