# FAC § 78559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 7. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 78559

---

## Text

Money that is deposited pursuant to Section 78558 may be invested and reinvested by the council in any of the securities described in Article 1 (commencing with Section 16430) of Chapter 3 of Part 2 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 726, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1997.)
