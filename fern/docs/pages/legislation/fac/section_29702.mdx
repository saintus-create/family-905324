# FAC § 29702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 11. Criminal Punishment and Procedure

**Citation:** FAC § 29702

---

## Text

Any prosecution for the violation of any provision of this chapter may be made in any county where any part of the offense occurred.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
