# FAC § 29417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29417

---

## Text

“Pack,” or “packing” or “packed,” means the arrangement of all or a part of the subcontainers in any container.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
