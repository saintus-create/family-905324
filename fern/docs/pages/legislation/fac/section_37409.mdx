# FAC § 37409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. Cheese > Article: 1. Varieties of Cheese

**Citation:** FAC § 37409

---

## Text

Pasteurized neufchatel cheese spread with other foods is a food that complies with Section 133.178 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 23. Effective June 6, 1994.)
