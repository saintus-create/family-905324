# FAC § 56832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 2. General Provisions

## Text

The director may make such regulations as he may deem necessary to carry out this chapter and to effectuate its purposes.
