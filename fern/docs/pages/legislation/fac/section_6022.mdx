# FAC § 6022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 6. Field Rodents

**Citation:** FAC § 6022

---

## Text

The commissioner shall cooperate in suppressing field rodents and insects, or other associated vectors of rodent-borne diseases transmissible and injurious to humans.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 897.)
