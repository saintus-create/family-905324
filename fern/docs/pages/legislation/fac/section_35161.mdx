# FAC § 35161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 8. Licensing of Testers, Samplers and Weighers, Technicians, and Pasteurizers

**Citation:** FAC § 35161

---

## Text

Every person who tests milk, cream, or any fluid derivative of milk or cream, which is purchased, received, or sold on the basis of milk fat which is contained in it, shall hold a tester’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
