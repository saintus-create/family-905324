# FAC § 23021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 23021

---

## Text

The director shall enforce this division. He may make such regulations as are reasonably necessary for such enforcement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
