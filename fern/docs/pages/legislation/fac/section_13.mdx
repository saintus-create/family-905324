# FAC § 13

**Citation:** FAC § 13

---

## Text

Whenever any power or authority is given by this code to any person, it may be exercised by any deputy, inspector, or agent who is duly authorized by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
