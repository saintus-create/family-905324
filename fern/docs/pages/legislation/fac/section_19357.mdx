# FAC § 19357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 19357

## Text

All labels or other identification marks or methods for articles subject to this chapter shall be approved by the director in advance of their use.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
