# FAC § 32903.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32903.5

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell any milk, or any product of milk, from cows or goats that are reactors to a brucellosis test applied in accordance with the law and regulations of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 771.)
