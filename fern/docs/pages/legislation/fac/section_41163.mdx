# FAC § 41163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 3. Grapes for Wine and Byproducts > Article: 3. Determination of Soluble Solids Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 41163

---

## Text

Each commissioner shall provide for the taking of samples, making tests, and issuing the certificates which are required in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
