# FAC § 77266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 3. The California Pepper Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77266

---

## Text

No member of the commission or member of a committee established by the commission who is a nonmember of the commission shall receive a salary. Each member of the commission or each alternate member serving in place of a member, except ex officio government members, and each member of a committee established by the commission who is a nonmember of the commission, may receive a sum not to exceed one hundred dollars ($100) per day, as established by the commission. This per diem shall be paid to members for each day spent in actual attendance at, or in traveling to and from, meetings of the commission or committees of the commission, or on special assignment for the commission, as approved by the commission. Members shall also receive the necessary traveling expenses and meal allowances, as approved by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 335, Sec. 1. Effective July 14, 1988.)
