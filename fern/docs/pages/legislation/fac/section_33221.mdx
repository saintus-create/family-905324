# FAC § 33221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 7. Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 33221

---

## Text

As used in this article, “permit” means a permit that is issued pursuant to Section 33222.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 584, Sec. 1.8. Effective January 1, 2007.)
