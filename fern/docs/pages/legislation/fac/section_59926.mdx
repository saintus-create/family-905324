# FAC § 59926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 11. Amendments to Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59926

---

## Text

Any modification of the provisions of any marketing program in effect for the purpose of clarifying the meaning or application of the provisions or modifying administrative procedures for carrying out the provisions is a minor amendment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
