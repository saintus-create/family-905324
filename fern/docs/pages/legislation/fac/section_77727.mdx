# FAC § 77727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77727

---

## Text

“Ex officio member” means a nonvoting member of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77726 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 15. Effective July 18, 1995.)
