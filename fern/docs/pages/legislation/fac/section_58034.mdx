# FAC § 58034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58034

---

## Text

The director may foster and encourage cooperation between producers and distributors of any product, in the interest of the general public.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
