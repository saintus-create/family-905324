# FAC § 27638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 12. Unlawful Acts, General

**Citation:** FAC § 27638

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to fail to comply with any lawful order of an enforcement officer, or of any court, in any proceeding pursuant to this chapter or any regulation adopted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
