# FAC § 36670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 4. Sweeteners

**Citation:** FAC § 36670

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, sweeteners approved by the United States Food and Drug Administration for use in milk products may be added to milk products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 199, Sec. 2. Effective July 25, 1989.)
