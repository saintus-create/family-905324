# FAC § 80060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 80060

---

## Text

“Resale load” means native plants harvested, possessed, or transported for resale purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70609 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 58.)
