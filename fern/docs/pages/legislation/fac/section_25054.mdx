# FAC § 25054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 4. Meat Inspector Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 25054

---

## Text

Every license shall expire on the last day of the calendar year for which it is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
