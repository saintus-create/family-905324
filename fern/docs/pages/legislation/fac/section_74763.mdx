# FAC § 74763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74763

---

## Text

The commission may publish and distribute, without charge, bulletins or other communications for the dissemination of information to grape rootstock nurseries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
