# FAC § 55063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 6. Assessments

**Citation:** FAC § 55063

---

## Text

The commission shall publish an annual report of its activities including an accounting of the use of assessments collected pursuant to this chapter. The report, in aggregate form, shall be made available to any person upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 579, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
