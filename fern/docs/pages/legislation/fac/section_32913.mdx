# FAC § 32913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32913

---

## Text

A person shall not use the name of any such product, orally, printed, or written, in connection with the sale of any product or substance unless such product or substance conforms to the definitions and standards which are prescribed by this division.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
