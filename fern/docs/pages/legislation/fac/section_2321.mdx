# FAC § 2321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. County Veterinarian > Article: 2. Compensation

**Citation:** FAC § 2321

---

## Text

The veterinarian shall receive a salary which is fixed by the board of supervisors. It shall be payable at the same time, in the same manner, and from the same fund as other county officers are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
