# FAC § 12671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 12671

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to pack, ship, or sell any produce that carries pesticide residue in excess of the permissible tolerance which is established by the director pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 717, Sec. 12.)
