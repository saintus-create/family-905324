# FAC § 32920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32920

---

## Text

Any pasteurized milk or milk product, frozen dessert, cheese, or product resembling milk products shall be produced, distributed, or sold in accordance with the current good manufacturing practices set forth in Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 427, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
