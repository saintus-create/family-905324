# FAC § 21884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 9. Injury to, or Theft of, Cattle > Article: 2. Livestock Killed or Injured on Railroads

**Citation:** FAC § 21884

---

## Text

Reports by telephone shall be confirmed within 24 hours by telegraph or mail to the office of the sheriff. A copy shall be sent to the bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
