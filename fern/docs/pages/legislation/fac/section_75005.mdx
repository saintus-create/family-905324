# FAC § 75005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 75005

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to deal with the broad fields of advertising, promotion, education, economic research and marketing research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
