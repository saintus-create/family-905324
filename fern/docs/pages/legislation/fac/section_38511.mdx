# FAC § 38511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 16. Milk Drink

**Citation:** FAC § 38511

---

## Text

Milk drink is the product which is made from milk drink mix or UHT milk drink mix and which contains not less than 3.5 percent of milk fat nor more than 75,000 bacteria per gram.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 13. Effective September 15, 1987.)
