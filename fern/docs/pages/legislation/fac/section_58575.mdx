# FAC § 58575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Foreign Marketing > Article: 2. Project Agreements

**Citation:** FAC § 58575

---

## Text

Activities undertaken pursuant to this chapter shall be supplemental to, and not a substitute for, any market development activities undertaken by the federal government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1189, Sec. 2.)
