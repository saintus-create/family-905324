# FAC § 23405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 3. Inspection of Animals > Article: 1. Horses, Mules, and Burros

**Citation:** FAC § 23405

---

## Text

Every slaughterer of horses, mules, or burros shall have on file in his office for a period of one year after slaughter the original bill of sale of the animals purchased and slaughtered by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 630.)
