# FAC § 33528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 3. Dairy Farm Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33528

---

## Text

The interior of the milking barn or milk house or room shall be kept clean and free from accumulated dust and cobwebs.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
