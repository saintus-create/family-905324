# FAC § 53541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 9. Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 53541

---

## Text

The violation of any provision of this chapter is a misdemeanor and punishable by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by imprisonment in the county jail for not more than six months, or by both fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 122. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
