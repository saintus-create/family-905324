# FAC § 19240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 3. Pet Food and Horse Slaughterers

**Citation:** FAC § 19240

---

## Text

Every person engaged in the business of slaughtering animals for pet food or horses for human food purposes shall first obtain a license pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
