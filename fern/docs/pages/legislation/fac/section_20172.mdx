# FAC § 20172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 2. Regulation

**Citation:** FAC § 20172

---

## Text

The regulations shall be published in one or more stock journals of general circulation in the state before they become effective.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
