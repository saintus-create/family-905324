# FAC § 59885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 10. Proration Certificates

**Citation:** FAC § 59885

---

## Text

Primary or secondary certificates are not negotiable between producers, except with the approval of the marketing program committee and the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
