# FAC § 2344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. County Veterinarian > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 2344

---

## Text

The veterinarian shall attend such meetings as are deemed necessary and advisable by the board of supervisors.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
