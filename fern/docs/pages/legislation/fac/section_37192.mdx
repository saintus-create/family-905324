# FAC § 37192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 4. Retail Packages

**Citation:** FAC § 37192

---

## Text

Butter which is sold to the retail trade shall be labeled with the words “pasteurized” or “raw,” as the case may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 306.)
