# FAC § 73152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 73152

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt and, from time to time, alter, rescind, modify, and amend in a manner prescribed by the commission, all proper and necessary bylaws, rules and regulations, and orders for carrying out this chapter, including rules for appeals from any bylaw, rule and regulation, or order of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
