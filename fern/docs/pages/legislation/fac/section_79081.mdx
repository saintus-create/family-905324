# FAC § 79081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25. Sea Urchins > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79081

---

## Text

The commission may approve payment of a stipend to commission members of not more than one hundred twenty-five dollars ($125) for each day a member is on official commission business pursuant to Section 79055, not to exceed five hundred dollars ($500) in any single month.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
