# FAC § 72003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 72003

---

## Text

The establishment of a California Wheat Commission is necessary for the efficient development and management of an international sales promotion program, and is essential to ensure that the California wheat industry can compete successfully in the marketplace.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
