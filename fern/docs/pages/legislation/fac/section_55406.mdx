# FAC § 55406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 55406

---

## Text

“Packout basis” means that payment for the farm product is based on the grade or quality of the finished product, as determined at the completion of the processing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
