# FAC § 29012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 29012

---

## Text

“Inspector” means any person who has received a certificate issued by the department with curriculum approved by the secretary and who is authorized to enforce this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 683, Sec. 4. (AB 719) Effective January 1, 2023.)
