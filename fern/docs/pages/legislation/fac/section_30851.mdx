# FAC § 30851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 3.5. Guide Dogs, Signal Dogs,and Service Dogs

**Citation:** FAC § 30851

---

## Text

The owners of assistance dogs shall comply with all state and local ordinances regarding health and licensure requirements for dogs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1257, Sec. 10.5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
