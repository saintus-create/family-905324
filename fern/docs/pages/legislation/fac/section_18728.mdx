# FAC § 18728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 3. Inspections and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18728

---

## Text

The director may refuse to provide inspection service to any establishment that fails to destroy any condemned product as required pursuant to Section 18722 and any regulation promulgated thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
