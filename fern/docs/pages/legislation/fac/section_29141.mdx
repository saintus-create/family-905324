# FAC § 29141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 10. Exportation

**Citation:** FAC § 29141

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may estalish a schedule of fees for certificates for bees on comb to be paid by persons that request the certificates.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
