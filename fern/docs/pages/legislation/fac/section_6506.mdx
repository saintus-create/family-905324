# FAC § 6506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. SHIPMENTS > Chapter: 3. Intrastate Shipments > Article: 1. Notice of Arrival and Hold for Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 6506

---

## Text

Any shipment of seed from an origin inspected warehouse is exempt from the requirements of this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 288.)
