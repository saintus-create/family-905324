# FAC § 18501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 3. Warranties

**Citation:** FAC § 18501

---

## Text

In the absence of an express warranty, the mere sale of livestock shall not be construed to imply a warranty of such livestock for any particular purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
