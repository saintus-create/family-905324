# FAC § 31764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.5. REGULATION OF CATS > Chapter: 2. Special Provisions Applicable to Counties With a Population of Less Than 100,000 Persons

**Citation:** FAC § 31764

---

## Text

Local ordinances concerning the adoption or placement procedures of any animal shelter shall be at least as restrictive as this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 747, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1999. Operative January 1, 2000, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 747.)
