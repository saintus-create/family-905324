# FAC § 35103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 6. Oleomargarine and Imitation Ice Cream Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 35103

---

## Text

Every license which is issued pursuant to Section 35102 expires on June 30th of each year, and may be issued in periods of one year.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
