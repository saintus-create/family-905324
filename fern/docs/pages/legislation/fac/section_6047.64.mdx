# FAC § 6047.64

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.64

---

## Text

(a) Proceedings for the formation of a district within any county shall be commenced by a petition signed by the owners of 15 percent of the table grape acreage.

(b) The petition shall be addressed to, and filed with, the board of supervisors of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 400, Sec. 4. Effective September 9, 2004.)
