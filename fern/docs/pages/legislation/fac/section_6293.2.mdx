# FAC § 6293.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Napa County Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6293.2

---

## Text

Signatures to the petition may be withdrawn at any time before it has been acted upon by filing with the clerk of the board of supervisors a declaration signed by the petitioner that states that it is the intention of the petitioner to withdraw his or her signature from the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 442, Sec. 1. Effective October 3, 2001.)
