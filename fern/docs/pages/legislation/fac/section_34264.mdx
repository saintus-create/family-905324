# FAC § 34264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Weighing, Measuring, Sampling, and Loading of Milk > Article: 3. Milk Tests

**Citation:** FAC § 34264

---

## Text

The original record of each test shall be immediately delivered to the purchaser, receiver, or seller of the milk, cream, or fluid derivative of milk or cream, and shall be retained by him for at least three months.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
