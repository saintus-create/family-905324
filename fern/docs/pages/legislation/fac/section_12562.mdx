# FAC § 12562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 3. Permissible Tolerances

**Citation:** FAC § 12562

---

## Text

The director may exempt any pesticide chemical from the requirement of a tolerance if he finds that the pesticide chemical may safely be used without a tolerance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
