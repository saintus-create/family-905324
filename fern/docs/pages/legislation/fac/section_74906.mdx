# FAC § 74906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74906

---

## Text

The commission shall submit to the secretary, for his or her concurrence, an annual statement of contemplated activities authorized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 63. Effective January 1, 1996.)
