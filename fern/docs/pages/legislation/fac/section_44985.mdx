# FAC § 44985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 1. Container Certification and Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 44985

---

## Text

The director may authorize the issuance and use of, or revocation of, a certification stamp as provided for under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 16. Effective March 31, 1982.)
