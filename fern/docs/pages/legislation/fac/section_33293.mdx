# FAC § 33293

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 9. Fees Payable to the Secretary

**Citation:** FAC § 33293

---

## Text

The inspection fee is payable during the first week of January, April, July, and October of each year.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
