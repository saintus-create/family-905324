# FAC § 6003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 5. Cotton Pest Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6003

---

## Text

“First handler” means the first person who, as owner, agent, or broker, purchases, or otherwise acquires from a grower, possession or control of cotton.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 167, Sec. 1. Effective June 30, 1983.)
