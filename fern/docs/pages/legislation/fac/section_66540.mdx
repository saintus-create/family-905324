# FAC § 66540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 66540

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, firm, corporation, association, or other business unit, and shall include, for purposes of this chapter, any state agency which engages in any of the commercial activities regulated pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 434.)
