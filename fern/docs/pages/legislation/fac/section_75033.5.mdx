# FAC § 75033.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75033.5

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, sole proprietorship, partnership, corporation, cooperative, limited liability company, firm, or other business entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 384, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
