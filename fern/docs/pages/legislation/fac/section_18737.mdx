# FAC § 18737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 3. Inspections and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 18737

---

## Text

No inspection of any product placed in any container at any official establishment shall be deemed to be complete until the product is sealed or enclosed in the container under the supervision of an inspector.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
