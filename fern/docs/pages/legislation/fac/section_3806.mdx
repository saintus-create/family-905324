# FAC § 3806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 3806

---

## Text

For the purposes of Article 1 (commencing with Section 4101) of Chapter 6 of this part, “agency” means the Natural Resources Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 98. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
