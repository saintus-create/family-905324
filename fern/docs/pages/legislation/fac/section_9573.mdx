# FAC § 9573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 3. Establishment of Quarantine

**Citation:** FAC § 9573

---

## Text

A quarantine shall not be established by one county or city against another county or city on account of the existence of any disease or condition subject to the authority of the State Veterinarian pursuant to Section 9562 or 9569.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 503, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2002.)
