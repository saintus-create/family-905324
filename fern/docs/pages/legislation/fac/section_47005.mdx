# FAC § 47005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10.5. Direct Marketing > Article: 1.5. Certified Farmers’ Markets

**Citation:** FAC § 47005

---

## Text

An enforcing officer may enter and inspect any place or conveyance where products are produced, stored, packed, delivered for shipment, loaded, shipped, transported, or sold pertaining to a certified producer’s certificate over which they have jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 373, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
