# FAC § 19316.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 6.5. Transporters of Inedible Kitchen Grease

**Citation:** FAC § 19316.5

---

## Text

The department is authorized to establish a system for documenting and tracking the transportation of inedible kitchen grease in order to ensure the proper disposal or recycling of that material.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 533, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2006.)
