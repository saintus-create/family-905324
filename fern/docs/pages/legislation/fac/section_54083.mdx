# FAC § 54083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 4. Articles of Incorporation

**Citation:** FAC § 54083

---

## Text

The articles of incorporation of any association may be amended in the manner and for the purposes which are authorized by the General Corporation Law, Division 1 (commencing with Section 100), Title 1 of the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
