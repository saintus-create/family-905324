# FAC § 75595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75595

---

## Text

The commission may collect information, and publish and distribute without charge, bulletins and other communications for dissemination of information.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 32. Effective May 9, 1994.)
