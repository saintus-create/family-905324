# FAC § 27541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 3. Registration of Egg Handlers

**Citation:** FAC § 27541

---

## Text

Any person engaged in business in this state as an egg producer or egg handler, or any out-of-state egg handler or egg producer selling eggs into California, shall register with the secretary. A new registration shall be submitted if any current information changes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 283, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
