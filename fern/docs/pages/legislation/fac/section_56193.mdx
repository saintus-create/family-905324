# FAC § 56193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 56193

---

## Text

Each licensee shall post his license or a copy of it in his office or salesroom in plain view of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 915.)
