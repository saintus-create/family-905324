# FAC § 10785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 1. Importation and Sale > Article: 4. Swine Disease Control

**Citation:** FAC § 10785

---

## Text

If the secretary finds and determines that similar regulations are necessary to control the diseases described in Section 10781 while hogs or swine are in interstate movement, the secretary shall notify the Governor so that the Governor may act pursuant to Section 9570.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10782 by Stats. 1999, Ch. 447, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
