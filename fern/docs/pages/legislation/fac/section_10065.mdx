# FAC § 10065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 7. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10065

---

## Text

The slaughtering of any reacting bovine animal, pursuant to this article, shall be under the supervision of the department, or of the United States Department of Agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 45. Effective August 23, 2004.)
