# FAC § 11262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. ANIMALS DETRIMENTAL TO AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Predators > Article: 3. Donations

**Citation:** FAC § 11262

---

## Text

Money which is made available by any board of supervisors that is accepted pursuant to this article shall be expended solely within the boundaries of the county which makes the appropriation, unless otherwise authorized by the board of supervisors. All other donations shall be applied to the particular locality in which the donors are interested, unless they specifically consent to the use of their donations in other localities.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
