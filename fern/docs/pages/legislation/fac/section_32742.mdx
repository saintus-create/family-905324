# FAC § 32742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 3. Animals Carrying Pesticide Residues

**Citation:** FAC § 32742

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to move any such animal except under written permission of the director and in accordance with the conditions stated in such written permission, or until the hold order has been revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
