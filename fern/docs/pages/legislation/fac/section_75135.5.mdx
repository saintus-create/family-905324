# FAC § 75135.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 75135.5

---

## Text

Persons handling eggs or egg products in California which originated out of state, including persons shipping eggs or egg products into California, shall register with the commission in the manner prescribed by the commission, before handling eggs or egg products in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 121, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1998.)
