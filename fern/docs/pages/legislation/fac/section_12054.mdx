# FAC § 12054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 5. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 12054

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any pest control adviser to make recommendations in a category for which he is not certified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1276.)
