# FAC § 56574.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 17. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 56574.5

---

## Text

Any person who has applied for and obtained a license as agent, in the manner and upon the payment of the fee which is set forth in Section 56571, whose principal has applied for and obtained a conjunctive processor’s license, pursuant to Section 56574, shall be deemed to be licensed to represent his principal under Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 55401) of this division, and no other fee shall be required of such agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 667.)
