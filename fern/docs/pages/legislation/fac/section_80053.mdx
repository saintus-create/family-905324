# FAC § 80053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 80053

---

## Text

“Harvest” means to remove or cut and remove from the place where grown.
