# FAC § 33552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 4. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 33552

---

## Text

No notice for a subsequent violation of any provision of this chapter, for which a notice, as herein provided, has been issued is required if such subsequent violation occurs during the three-month period following issuance of the original notice.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
