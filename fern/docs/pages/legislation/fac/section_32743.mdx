# FAC § 32743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 3. Animals Carrying Pesticide Residues

**Citation:** FAC § 32743

---

## Text

The director shall notify the owner or agent of any animal which is ordered held, or for which he has specified conditions for moving, pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
