# FAC § 58609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 58609

---

## Text

“Fish” means every form of fish, either salt water or fresh water fish, which is used for human consumption, except fish for canning, retorting, reduction, or use in an extraction process.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
