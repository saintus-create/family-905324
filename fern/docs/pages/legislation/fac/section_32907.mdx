# FAC § 32907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32907

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell, give away, deliver, or knowingly purchase or receive any milk or any product of milk which has been produced or handled in or by a dairy farm, milk products plant, carrier, store, or depot, that is in an insanitary condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 9.)
