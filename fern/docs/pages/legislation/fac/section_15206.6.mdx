# FAC § 15206.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 7. Structural Pest Control

**Citation:** FAC § 15206.6

---

## Text

The director and the Structural Pest Control Board shall jointly develop and concur in the list of the types of violations that the commissioners shall refer to the board for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 766, Sec. 14.)
