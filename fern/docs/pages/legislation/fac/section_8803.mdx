# FAC § 8803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 6. PEST CONTROL COMPACT

**Citation:** FAC § 8803

---

## Text

Pursuant to Article IV(h) of the compact, copies of bylaws and amendments thereto shall be filed with the Office of Administrative Procedure.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
