# FAC § 57552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 13. Agricultural Chemical and Seed Lien > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 57552

---

## Text

“Agricultural seed” means the seed of any domesticated grass or cereal, of any vegetable, flower, or propagated plant, and of any legume or other plant which is grown as turf, cover crop, forage crop, fiber crop, or field crop, and mixtures of the seed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 2.)
