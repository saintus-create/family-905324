# FAC § 65062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2.5. California Cattle Council Law > Article: 5. Implementation and Suspension of Operations

**Citation:** FAC § 65062

---

## Text

An implementation referendum shall advertise the amount to be assessed and declare that the assessment shall be a mandatory fee. An implementation referendum shall also advertise that the council will accept petitions to refund the fee for any producer subject to this chapter pursuant to Section 65075.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 410, Sec. 2. (SB 965) Effective January 1, 2019. Provisions operative or inoperative as prescribed in Sections 65061, 65066, 65068, and 65069. Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 65066.)
