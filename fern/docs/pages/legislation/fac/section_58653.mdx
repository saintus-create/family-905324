# FAC § 58653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 58653

---

## Text

The marketing of commodities within this state is hereby declared to be affected with a public interest. The provisions of this chapter are enacted in the exercise of the police powers of this state for the purpose of protecting the health, peace, safety, and general welfare of the people of this state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
