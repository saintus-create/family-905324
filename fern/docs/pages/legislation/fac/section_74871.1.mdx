# FAC § 74871.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 3. California Winegrape Growers Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74871.1

---

## Text

Not less than one-half of the producer members of any local commission, including alternate members when acting in the place of the members, shall constitute a quorum of the local commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 854, Sec. 6.)
