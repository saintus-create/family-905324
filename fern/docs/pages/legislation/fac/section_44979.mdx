# FAC § 44979

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 1. Container Certification and Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 44979

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to fail to comply with the provisions of this chapter and the regulations of the director adopted pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 141, Sec. 10. Effective March 31, 1982.)
