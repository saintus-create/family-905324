# FAC § 16411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 2. Investigation

**Citation:** FAC § 16411

---

## Text

The director may make any and all necessary investigations relative to reported violations of this part pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 11180), Chapter 2, Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
