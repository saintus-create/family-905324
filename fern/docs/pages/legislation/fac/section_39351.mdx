# FAC § 39351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Margarine, Dairy Spread, and Spread > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 39351

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, the provisions of this code which relate to margarine, dairy spread, or spread are likewise applicable to colored margarine, colored dairy spread, or colored spread.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 512, Sec. 7.)
