# FAC § 36983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Frozen Dairy Products > Article: 6. Quiescently Frozen Confections

**Citation:** FAC § 36983

---

## Text

A quiescently frozen confection shall be manufactured in a form of servings, individually packaged, bagged, or otherwise wrapped, properly labeled, and purveyed to the consumer in their original factory-filled package.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
