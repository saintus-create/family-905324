# FAC § 10325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 10325

---

## Text

Every accredited veterinarian who is conducting work pursuant to this chapter shall submit reports to the department on forms that are furnished for that purpose.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
