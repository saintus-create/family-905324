# FAC § 58233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Agreements > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58233

---

## Text

The director may receive and pay out money for the purposes which are mentioned in this article.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
