# FAC § 65555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 3. The California Table Grape Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 65555

---

## Text

The regular terms of office of the commissioners shall be three years from the date of appointment by the secretary and until their successors are appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 12. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
