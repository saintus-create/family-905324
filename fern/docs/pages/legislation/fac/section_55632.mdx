# FAC § 55632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 9. Producer’s Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 55632

---

## Text

This producer’s lien attaches on all of such delivered product from the date of delivery of such farm product or any portion of it by a producer to any processor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
