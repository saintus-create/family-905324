# FAC § 79912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 30. California Agave Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79912

---

## Text

The cultivation of agave in California is consistent with and supportive of California’s environmentally sensitive policies relating to sustainable farming practices, including water conservation, through the cultivation of drought tolerant crops.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. (AB 2606) Effective January 1, 2025.)
