# FAC § 77815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 77815

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has been given as provided in this article, the secretary shall certify and declare this chapter operative and give notice of the favorable vote to all handlers whose names and addresses are on file with the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 64. Effective July 18, 1995.)
