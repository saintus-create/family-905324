# FAC § 14937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14937

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, corporation, partnership, limited liability company, trust, association, cooperative association, or any other business unit or organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 127. Effective January 1, 1995.)
