# FAC § 72098

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 10. California Wheat Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 72098

---

## Text

Subsequent to the first election of commissioners under this chapter, persons to be elected to the commission shall be selected pursuant to the nomination and election procedures which shall be established by the rules and regulations adopted by the commission with concurrence of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 891, Sec. 1.)
