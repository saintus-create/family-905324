# FAC § 59916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 11. Amendments to Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59916

---

## Text

An amendment is a major amendment if it adds to, or deletes from, a marketing program authority to create and disburse an equalization fund as provided for in Sections 59813 and 59814.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
