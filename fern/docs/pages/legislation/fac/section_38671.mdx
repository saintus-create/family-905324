# FAC § 38671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 25. Sour Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 38671

---

## Text

Sour cream is a food that complies with Section 131.160 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations. Sour cream shall contain not more than 10 coliform bacteria per gram.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 339, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2008.)
