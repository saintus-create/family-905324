# FAC § 8507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 3. Organization of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8507

---

## Text

Upon the expiration of the term of any member of the board, the board of supervisors shall appoint his successor.

Vacancies shall be filled by the board of supervisors for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
