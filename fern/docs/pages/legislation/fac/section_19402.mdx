# FAC § 19402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 11. Records

**Citation:** FAC § 19402

---

## Text

All horsemeat and animal food records shall be open at all times during the hours of operation to the inspection of the director, any health officer or sanitation inspector.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
