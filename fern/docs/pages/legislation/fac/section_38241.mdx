# FAC § 38241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 5. Evaporated Cream or Clotted Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 38241

---

## Text

Evaporated cream or clotted cream is cream from which a considerable portion of moisture has been evaporated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
