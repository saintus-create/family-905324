# FAC § 77763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 3. The California Date Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77763

---

## Text

A quorum of the commission is a majority of the voting members of the commission. Except as otherwise provided in subdivision (b) of Section 77841 and Sections 77731 and 77883, the vote of the majority of the members present at a meeting at which there is a quorum constitutes the act of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 40. Effective July 18, 1995.)
