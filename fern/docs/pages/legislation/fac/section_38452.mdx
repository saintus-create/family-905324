# FAC § 38452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 14. Milk Drink Mix

**Citation:** FAC § 38452

---

## Text

Milk drink mix shall contain no milk or milk products except market milk and milk products which are derived from market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
