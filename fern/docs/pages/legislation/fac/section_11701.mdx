# FAC § 11701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 4. Pest Control > Article: 1. Licenses and Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 11701

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a person to advertise, solicit, or operate as a pest control business, unless the person has a valid pest control business license issued by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 435, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
