# FAC § 68003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 68003

---

## Text

The establishment of a California Kiwifruit Commission is necessary for the efficient development and management of a national and international advertising program and essential to ensure that the California kiwifruit industry can compete successfully in the marketplace.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 606, Sec. 1.)
