# FAC § 57027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 4. Association of Produce Dealers

**Citation:** FAC § 57027

---

## Text

An association may provide for watchman service to protect and give a receipt for any farm product which is delivered to a market at times when the market is not otherwise in operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 585.)
