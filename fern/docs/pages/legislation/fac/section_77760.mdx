# FAC § 77760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 3. The California Date Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77760

---

## Text

The public member and his or her alternate member on the commission shall have all of the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member or alternate member, respectively, on the commission. The public member and his or her alternate member shall not have any financial interest in the date industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1089, Sec. 1.)
