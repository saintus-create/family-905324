# FAC § 59953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 12. Funds

**Citation:** FAC § 59953

---

## Text

Unless otherwise requested by the marketing program committee, the director is not required to issue refunds to any producer in an amount less than five dollars ($5).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1222.)
