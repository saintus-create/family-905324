# FAC § 58777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 6. Notice, Reports, and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 58777

---

## Text

The director shall mail a copy of such notice to file such report to every handler of such commodity whose name and address appears upon the lists on file in the department, of handlers that may be directly affected by the provisions of such proposed marketing order.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1222.)
