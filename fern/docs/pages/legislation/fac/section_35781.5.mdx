# FAC § 35781.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 2. Market Milk Standards and Grades

**Citation:** FAC § 35781.5

---

## Text

A milk producer delivering in cans on delivery to a plant receiving both graded market milk and ungraded market milk or manufacturing milk shall label each can containing market milk as to the producer and grade of such market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 191.)
