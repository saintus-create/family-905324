# FAC § 41865.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 8. Garlic and Onion Dehydrator Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41865.5

---

## Text

The secretary may require the government agency or private entity to correct or cease any activity or function that is determined by the secretary not to be in the public interest or is in violation of this chapter, and shall notify the government agency or private entity in writing of these specific acts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 520, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
