# FAC § 59037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 13. Major Amendments

**Citation:** FAC § 59037

---

## Text

The exercise by the director of any regulatory authority which is authorized in a marketing order or marketing agreement is not a major amendment, but is a seasonal marketing regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
