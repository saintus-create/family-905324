# FAC § 58061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 3. Investigations

**Citation:** FAC § 58061

---

## Text

The director may conduct investigations, make surveys, and assemble facts which are pertinent to marketing and to the formulation, administration, or amendment of any marketing order, agreement, or program which is authorized by any law now in effect or which may hereafter be enacted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
