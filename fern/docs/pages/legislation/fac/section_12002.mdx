# FAC § 12002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 6. Agricultural Pest Control Advisers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 12002

---

## Text

No person shall act, or offer to act, as an agricultural pest control adviser in any county wherein he makes any recommendation for agricultural use without first registering with the county agricultural commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 901.)
