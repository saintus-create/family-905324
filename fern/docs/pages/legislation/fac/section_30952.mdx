# FAC § 30952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 4. Regulation

**Citation:** FAC § 30952

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to attach a license tag to the collar of any dog except the dog which is described in the application for such license tag.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
