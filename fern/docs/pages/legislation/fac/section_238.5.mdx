# FAC § 238.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Duties and Powers > Article: 2.5. Agriculture Trust Fund

**Citation:** FAC § 238.5

---

## Text

The chairperson of each advisory body shall advise the director on the administration of the trust fund, including, but not limited to, the amount of the fund to be applied to program closures, unanticipated occurrences, and replenishment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 239 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 1995.)
