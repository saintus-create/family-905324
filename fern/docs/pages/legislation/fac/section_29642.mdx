# FAC § 29642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 9. Labeling of Honey as to Origin and Flavor

**Citation:** FAC § 29642

---

## Text

A container or subcontainer of comb or extracted honey shall not be marked or labeled with the name “California” unless the honey in the container or subcontainer was produced entirely within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
