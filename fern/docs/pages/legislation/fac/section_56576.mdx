# FAC § 56576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 17. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 56576

---

## Text

All license fees which are collected pursuant to this chapter shall be paid into the State Treasury monthly and shall be credited to the Department of Agriculture Fund and, except as otherwise provided in Section 56133, shall be expended in carrying out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
