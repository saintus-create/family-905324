# FAC § 36031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 11. Bottle Caps and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 36031

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person that sells or handles market milk, except the original bottler of the market milk, to have in his possession, or under his control, any milk bottle cap or label, except the caps or labels that are affixed to the filled containers at the time of delivery to the seller or handler.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
