# FAC § 56103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56103

---

## Text

“Broker” means any person that negotiates the purchase or sale of any farm product. A broker may not, however, handle either the farm product which is involved or the proceeds of a sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 915.)
