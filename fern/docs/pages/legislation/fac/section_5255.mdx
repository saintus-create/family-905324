# FAC § 5255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Newly Discovered Pests

**Citation:** FAC § 5255

---

## Text

In any proceeding which is commenced pursuant to Section 5254, any duty, jurisdiction, or authority which is conferred on the commissioner by this division is conferred or imposed upon and transferred to the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
