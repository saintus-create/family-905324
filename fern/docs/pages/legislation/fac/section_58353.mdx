# FAC § 58353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Void Contracts and Restraint of Trade > Article: 1. Void Contracts

**Citation:** FAC § 58353

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person to take any part in any transaction which is declared by this article to be against public policy and void whether as seller, buyer, buyer’s agent, employee, or subsidiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
