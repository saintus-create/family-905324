# FAC § 24714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 24714

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to a poultry plant where poultry is slaughtered, dressed, and sold upon the premises where it was produced for purposes other than resale, if the entire poultry meat output of the producer is so sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
