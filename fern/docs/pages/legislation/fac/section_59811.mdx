# FAC § 59811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59811

---

## Text

A marketing program may authorize the marketing program committee to create, establish, or otherwise obtain and operate any facility for the storing, financing, grading, packing, servicing, processing, preparing for market, selling, or disposal of the contents of any pools which are provided for in this article. A marketing program committee shall not, however, engage in public warehousing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
