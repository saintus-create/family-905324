# FAC § 9933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 9933

---

## Text

The department may enter into any cooperative agreement with the United States Department of Agriculture to undertake tuberculosis control pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 11. Effective August 23, 2004.)
