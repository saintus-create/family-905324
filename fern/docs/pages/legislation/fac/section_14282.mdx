# FAC § 14282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 14282

---

## Text

The manufacturer of any livestock drug shall apply to the director for registration of the livestock drug.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1224.)
