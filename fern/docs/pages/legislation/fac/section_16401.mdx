# FAC § 16401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 1. Application

**Citation:** FAC § 16401

---

## Text

This part, including the provisions regarding payment of fees, is applicable to all state and county institutions engaging in livestock operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
