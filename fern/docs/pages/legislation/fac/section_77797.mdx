# FAC § 77797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77797

---

## Text

The commission shall establish an assessment rate to defray the operating costs of the commission to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 77793 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 51. Effective July 18, 1995.)
