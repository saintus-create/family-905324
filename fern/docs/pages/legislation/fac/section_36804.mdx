# FAC § 36804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Frozen Dairy Products > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 36804

---

## Text

Every product which is defined in this chapter shall be advertised and labeled in accordance with regulations which are adopted by the director.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
