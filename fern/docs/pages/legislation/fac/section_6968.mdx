# FAC § 6968

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 2. Other Regulations > Article: 4. Nursery Stock Certificates and Shipping Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 6968

---

## Text

The commissioner may revoke or suspend the right to use any nursery stock certificate or shipping permit which is issued to any person that fails to comply with the provisions of this chapter, Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 53301), or any regulation issued pursuant thereto.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 288.)
