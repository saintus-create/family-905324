# FAC § 18946

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18946

---

## Text

“Custom livestock slaughterhouse” means a licensed establishment where:

(a) Cattle, sheep, swine, or goats are slaughtered and prepared for the owners of the livestock.

(b) Fallow deer are slaughtered and prepared for transportation or sale, or transportation and sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 373, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
