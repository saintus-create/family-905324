# FAC § 29176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 12. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 29176

---

## Text

No person shall possess any comb which is not occupied by a live bee colony unless the comb is tightly enclosed to prevent access to the comb by bees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
