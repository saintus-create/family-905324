# FAC § 40932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 8. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 40932

---

## Text

The cost of the inspection and certification shall be borne equally by both the processor and the producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 22.)
