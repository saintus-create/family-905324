# FAC § 32509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 32509

---

## Text

“Manufacturing milk” means milk which does not conform to the requirements of market milk.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
