# FAC § 59726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 7. Marketing Program and Grading Committees

**Citation:** FAC § 59726

---

## Text

The marketing program committee may at any time invite any handler or other person to its meetings to serve in an advisory capacity and may authorize, subject to the approval of the director, the payment of compensation and necessary expenses to any person that is serving in such advisory capacity at rates not to exceed those which are granted to marketing program committee members.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
