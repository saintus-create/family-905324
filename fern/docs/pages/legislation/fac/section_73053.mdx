# FAC § 73053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 73053

---

## Text

“Books and records” means books, records, contracts, documents, memoranda, papers, correspondence, or other written data pertaining to matters relating to the activities subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 209. Effective January 1, 2007.)
