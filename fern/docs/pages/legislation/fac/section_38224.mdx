# FAC § 38224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 3.5. High Nutrient Lowfat Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38224

---

## Text

High nutrient lowfat milk shall be labeled in accordance with statutes and regulations applicable to vitamin and mineral addition. The phrase “multiple vitamins and minerals added” shall appear with the product on the principal display panel or panels.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 878, Sec. 1.)
