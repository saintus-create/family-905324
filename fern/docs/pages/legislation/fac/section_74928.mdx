# FAC § 74928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 74928

---

## Text

Subsequent to the action taken pursuant to Section 74927, persons to be elected to the commission upon expiration of existing terms shall be selected pursuant to the nomination and election procedures established by the commission with the concurrence of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 73. Effective January 1, 1996.)
