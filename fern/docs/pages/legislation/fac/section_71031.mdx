# FAC § 71031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 9.5. California Rice Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 71031

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, partnership, limited liability partnership, corporation, limited liability company, firm, company, or other entity doing business in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 328, Sec. 75. (SB 1330) Effective January 1, 2011.)
