# FAC § 66624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 5. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 66624

---

## Text

Any information obtained by the commission from handlers shall be confidential and shall not be disclosed except when required in a judicial proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 434.)
