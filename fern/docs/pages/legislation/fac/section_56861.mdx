# FAC § 56861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 3. Exceptions

**Citation:** FAC § 56861

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any market in which less than five produce dealers operate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
