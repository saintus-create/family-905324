# FAC § 27203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 6. California Grown Poultry > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 27203

---

## Text

“Poultry meat” means the carcass of poultry or any part of the carcass. It does not include live poultry or manufactured products in which poultry is not the main ingredient.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 27202 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 223, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
