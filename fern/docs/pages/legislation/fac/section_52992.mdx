# FAC § 52992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 13. Operation

**Citation:** FAC § 52992

---

## Text

The following sections of this chapter shall remain operative and shall be administered by the secretary:

(a) Section 52893 as it relates to certified cottonseed.

(b) Section 52901 as it relates to certified cottonseed and colored cotton.

(c) Section 52981 as it relates to colored cotton.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 383, Sec. 2. (AB 1389) Effective January 1, 2014.)
