# FAC § 79821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79821

---

## Text

“Producer” means a person that produces or causes to be produced olives that are processed or toll processed into olive oil in the amount of 5,000 gallons or more during the marketing season and that shall, upon request of the commission, provide proof of commodity sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 188, Sec. 18. (AB 1322) Effective January 1, 2026.)
