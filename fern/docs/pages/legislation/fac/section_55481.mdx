# FAC § 55481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 4. Agent’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55481

---

## Text

A person shall not act as an agent unless such person has first obtained a license as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
