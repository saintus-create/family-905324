# FAC § 58503.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Donation of Food

**Citation:** FAC § 58503.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter restricts or limits the operation of any other nonprofit organizations which are engaged in the distribution of agricultural products to nonprofit charitable organizations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1453, Sec. 8. Effective September 26, 1984.)
