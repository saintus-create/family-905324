# FAC § 36861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Frozen Dairy Products > Article: 2. Ice Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 36861

---

## Text

Ice cream is a food that complies with Section 135.110 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations. Ice cream shall contain not more than 75,000 bacteria per gram.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 339, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2008.)
