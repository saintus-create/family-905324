# FAC § 38393

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 12. Extra Grade Edible Dry Whey, Dry Buttermilk, and Modified Dry Whey

**Citation:** FAC § 38393

---

## Text

Extra grade edible dry whey shall contain not more than 50,000 bacteria per gram, not more than 1.25 percent of milk fat, not more than 5.00 percent of moisture, and not more than 0.16 percent of titratable acidity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
