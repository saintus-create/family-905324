# FAC § 8406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 8406

---

## Text

“Citrus pest” includes any infectious, transmissible, or contagious disease, any form of animal life, or any form of vegetable life infesting citrus trees or citrus fruits.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 904, Sec. 5.)
