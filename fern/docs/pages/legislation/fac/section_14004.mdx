# FAC § 14004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 14004

---

## Text

The director, and the commissioner of each county under the direction and supervision of the director, shall enforce this chapter and the regulations issued pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
