# FAC § 24112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 24112

---

## Text

If the animal which is seized or otherwise in the possession of the inspector is branded, the director shall cause a search to be made of the brand records to ascertain the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 747, Sec. 2.)
