# FAC § 6047.105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 8.7. Table Grape Pest and Disease District

**Citation:** FAC § 6047.105

---

## Text

Acreage devoted exclusively to the growing of table grapes within a tract of land outside the district, but in the county in which the district is located, may be annexed to the district in the same manner provided in this article for the formation of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 400, Sec. 22. Effective September 9, 2004.)
