# FAC § 10311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 10311

---

## Text

“Vaccine” means any vaccine which is produced by a laboratory which is licensed by the Agricultural Research Service of the United States Department of Agriculture and recognized by the department for use in preventing or treating brucellosis in cattle.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
