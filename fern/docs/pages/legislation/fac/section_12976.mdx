# FAC § 12976

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10. Recommendations and Usage

**Citation:** FAC § 12976

---

## Text

The director may adopt regulations to govern the possession, sale, or use of any pesticide which the director finds necessary to carry out the purposes of Division 6 (commencing with Section 11401) or this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 806, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
