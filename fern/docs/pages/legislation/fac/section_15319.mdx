# FAC § 15319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 7.5. Structural Pest Control Devices > Article: 3. Device Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 15319

---

## Text

Action by the director pursuant to Section 15305, 15311, 15315, 15317, or 15318 is not a condition precedent to the institution of any action to prosecute or levy a civil penalty for a violation of this chapter or regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 651, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
