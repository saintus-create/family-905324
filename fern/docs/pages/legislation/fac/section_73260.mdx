# FAC § 73260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 11. California Navel Orange Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 73260

---

## Text

Any assessment which is levied pursuant to in this chapter is a personal debt of the producer so assessed. Failure of a handler to make payment of the collected assessment to the commission does not relieve the producer of this obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. Effective July 28, 1983.)
