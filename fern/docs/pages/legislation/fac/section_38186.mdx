# FAC § 38186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 2. Skim Milk, Nonfat Milk, or Fat-free Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38186

---

## Text

Every container of skim milk, nonfat milk, or fat-free milk, except a glass container, shall be labeled with the words “skim milk,” “nonfat milk,” or “fat-free milk” in letters not less than one-quarter inch in height and one-eighth inch in width.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 10. Effective August 21, 1997.)
