# FAC § 24124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

## Text

One copy of the certificate of inspection required by this chapter shall accompany the shipment of animals.
