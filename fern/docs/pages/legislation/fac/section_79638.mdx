# FAC § 79638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 28. California Apiary Research Commission > Article: 3. The California Apiary Research Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79638

---

## Text

The public member, or his or her alternate, shall have all the powers, rights, and privileges of any other member on the commission. The public member shall not have any financial interest in the beekeeping industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 585, Sec. 1. (AB 1912) Effective January 1, 2011.)
