# FAC § 59814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59814

---

## Text

No part of any funds which are raised for equalization fund purposes, as specified in Section 59813, shall be applied to the cost of maintenance of the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
