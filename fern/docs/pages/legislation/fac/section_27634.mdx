# FAC § 27634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 12. Unlawful Acts, General

**Citation:** FAC § 27634

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell or use any container or subcontainer of eggs which bears a name, a trademark, or a trade name unless the seller or user is entitled to use the name, trademark, or trade name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
