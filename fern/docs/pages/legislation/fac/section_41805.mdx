# FAC § 41805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 6. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 41805

---

## Text

Except as provided in this article, any money which is so collected and not used for the purposes of this chapter shall be returned to the operators of dehydrators from whom it was received for distribution to the operators of dehydrators and growers from whom the money was originally collected, in proportion to their several interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 887.)
