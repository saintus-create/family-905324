# FAC § 52514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 10. Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 52514

---

## Text

A proceeding pursuant to this article if the value of the property seized amounts to thirty-five thousand dollars ($35,000) or less is a limited civil case.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 861, Sec. 12. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2024.)
