# FAC § 69041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 3. The California Pistachio Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 69041

---

## Text

The secretary or the secretary’s representatives shall be notified and may attend each meeting of the commission, and any committee meeting of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 1996.)
