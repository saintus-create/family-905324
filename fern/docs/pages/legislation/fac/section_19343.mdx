# FAC § 19343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 8. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 19343

---

## Text

Every establishment where animals are slaughtered for pet food, or where fresh or frozen pet food is processed or prepared shall be maintained and operated in a clean and sanitary manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
