# FAC § 57410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 11. Dairy Cattle Supply Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57410

---

## Text

The Secretary of State may adopt any regulations necessary to carry out his or her duties pursuant to this chapter, including prescribing necessary forms.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 679, Sec. 1.)
