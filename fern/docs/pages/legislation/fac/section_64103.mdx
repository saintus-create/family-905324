# FAC § 64103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 4. Dairy Council of California

**Citation:** FAC § 64103

---

## Text

Officials or members, otherwise qualified, of corporations, associations, and other business units, that are actually engaged in business as producers or handlers are eligible to be members of the council.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 11. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
