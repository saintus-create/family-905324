# FAC § 55437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 55437

---

## Text

The rights, remedies, and penalties which are provided for in this chapter are in addition to any other rights, remedies, or penalties which are provided for by law, and any acts or parts of acts in conflict therewith are hereby repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
