# FAC § 58453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Assignment of Dues

**Citation:** FAC § 58453

---

## Text

An assignment of dues may not exceed 2 percent of the total value of the product which is delivered by the grower or producer to the dealer or processor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
