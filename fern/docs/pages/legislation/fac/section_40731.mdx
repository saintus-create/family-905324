# FAC § 40731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 40731

---

## Text

The standards which are established as authorized by this chapter are the only standards of quality for processing tomatoes that the director shall enforce.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 5.)
