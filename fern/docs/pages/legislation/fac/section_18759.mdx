# FAC § 18759

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 4. Adulteration

**Citation:** FAC § 18759

---

## Text

A livestock or poultry product is adulterated if it is margarine containing animal fat and any of the raw material used in it consisted, in whole or in part, of any filthy, putrid, or decomposed substance.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
