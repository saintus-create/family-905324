# FAC § 6153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Caprifigs and Caprifig Trees > Article: 3. Caprifigs

**Citation:** FAC § 6153

---

## Text

If any caprifigs of the mamme crop are to be shipped or used outside the county, the person that owns, raises, uses, packs, ships or delivers them for shipment shall notify the commissioner of the county within which the figs are to be used of all of the following:

(a) The shipment.

(b) The nature of the shipment.

(c) The name and residence of the consignee.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
