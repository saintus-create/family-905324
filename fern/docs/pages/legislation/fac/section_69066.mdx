# FAC § 69066

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 69066

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a favorable vote has not been given as provided in Section 69062, the secretary shall so certify and declare this chapter inoperative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1996.)
