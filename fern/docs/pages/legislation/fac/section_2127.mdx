# FAC § 2127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 2. Appointment and Employment

**Citation:** FAC § 2127

---

## Text

Deputy commissioners and inspectors shall be appointed from a list which is furnished by the director of persons who hold a license for the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 734, Sec. 12. Effective September 18, 1987.)
