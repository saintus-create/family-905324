# FAC § 29140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 10. Exportation

**Citation:** FAC § 29140

---

## Text

As used in this article, “certificate” means the certification by a commissioner, or an inspector of the condition of any apiary which is to be transported to another state or country.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
