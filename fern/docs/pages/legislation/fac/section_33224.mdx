# FAC § 33224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 7. Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 33224

---

## Text

Upon receipt of an application for a permit, the secretary or approved milk inspection service shall cause an investigation to be made of the dairy farm where milk is produced and of the herd that produces the milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 584, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
