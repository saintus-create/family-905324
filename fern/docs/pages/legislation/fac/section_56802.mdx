# FAC § 56802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56802

---

## Text

“Association” means an association of produce dealers which is organized to carry out the responsibilities of produce dealers which are imposed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
