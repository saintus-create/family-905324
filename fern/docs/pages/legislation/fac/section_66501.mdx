# FAC § 66501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and Purposes

**Citation:** FAC § 66501

---

## Text

The inability of lettuce shippers to maintain or expand present markets for iceberg lettuce results in an unreasonable and unnecessary economic waste and accompanying loss of employment to the California economy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 434.)
