# FAC § 74901.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 74901.5

---

## Text

The commission may present facts to, and negotiate with, local, state, federal, and foreign agencies on matters that affect the winegrape industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 587, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
