# FAC § 6037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 7. Beet Leafhopper Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6037

---

## Text

Any money which is received by the director pursuant to this article shall be deposited in the Department of Food and Agriculture Fund to be used for the administration and enforcement of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 454, Sec. 27.)
