# FAC § 77752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 3. The California Date Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77752

---

## Text

District 1 consists of all of Riverside County. Additional districts may be added pursuant to Section 77752.5, and shall be numbered consecutively beginning with “District 2,” as each new district becomes subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 30. Effective July 18, 1995.)
