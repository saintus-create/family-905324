# FAC § 14528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 14528

---

## Text

“Discontinued manufacturing” means a beneficial substance, packaged agricultural mineral, organic input material, and specialty fertilizer that is no longer manufactured, but is still offered for sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 208, Sec. 5. (SB 1522) Effective January 1, 2025.)
