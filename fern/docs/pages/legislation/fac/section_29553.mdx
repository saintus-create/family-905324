# FAC § 29553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 6. Emergency Standards, Containers, Color Classifications, and Markings

**Citation:** FAC § 29553

---

## Text

Emergency standards shall apply in lieu of the standards for honey, standard units of net weight, type of container, color classifications, or markings required upon any container set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
