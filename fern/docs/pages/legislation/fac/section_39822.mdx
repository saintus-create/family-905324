# FAC § 39822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 13. UHT Dairy Spread

**Citation:** FAC § 39822

---

## Text

UHT dairy spread shall contain not less than 30 percent milk fat and not more than six-tenths of 1 percent stabilizer and emulsifier combined by weight, unless otherwise stated on the label.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
