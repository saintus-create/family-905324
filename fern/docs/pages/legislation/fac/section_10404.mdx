# FAC § 10404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 6. Slaughter of Reacting Bovine Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 10404

---

## Text

Where it is necessary to prevent the spread within this state of bovine brucellosis, the director may provide for the slaughter of animals.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
