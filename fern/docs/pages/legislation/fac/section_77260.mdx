# FAC § 77260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 3. The California Pepper Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77260

---

## Text

Any handler member and his or her alternate member on the commission shall have a financial interest in handling peppers for market, or be an employee of a firm which handles peppers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 527, Sec. 4.)
