# FAC § 38886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 39. Fromage Frais or Soft Fresh Cheese

**Citation:** FAC § 38886

---

## Text

Fromage frais or soft fresh cheese shall have at least 70 percent of the lactose in the original milk solids removed through the whey. Fromage frais or soft fresh cheese shall have a maximum dry matter content of 24.5 percent prior to the addition of any bulky flavors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 800, Sec. 8. Effective September 15, 1986.)
