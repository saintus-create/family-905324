# FAC § 59817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 8. Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59817

---

## Text

Educational and trade stimulation efforts may include all of the following:

(a) Efforts to prevent, modify, or remove trade barriers which directly affect the commodity to which the marketing program applies.

(b) The presentation of facts to, and negotiations with, any state, federal, or foreign governmental agency on any matter which affects the marketing of the commodity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
