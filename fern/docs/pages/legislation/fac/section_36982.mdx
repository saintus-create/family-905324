# FAC § 36982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Frozen Dairy Products > Article: 6. Quiescently Frozen Confections

**Citation:** FAC § 36982

---

## Text

Quiescently frozen confections may be sold under trade names which are not misleading or deceptive.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
