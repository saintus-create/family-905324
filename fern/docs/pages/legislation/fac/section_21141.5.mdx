# FAC § 21141.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 4. Point of Origin Inspection Area

**Citation:** FAC § 21141.5

---

## Text

A full point-of-origin inspection area may consist of one or more counties or geographical areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 210, Sec. 21. Effective May 24, 1982.)
