# FAC § 12975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10. Recommendations and Usage

**Citation:** FAC § 12975

---

## Text

A person making a written recommendation does not incur an obligation to insure that the actual use follows his or her recommendation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12978 by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1049.)
