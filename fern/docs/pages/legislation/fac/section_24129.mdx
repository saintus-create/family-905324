# FAC § 24129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 9. Equine Protection: Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 24129

---

## Text

The department shall maintain inspection certificates for a period of five years in a manner that shall disclose the number of animals that have been inspected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 747, Sec. 2.)
