# FAC § 12673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 7. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 12673

---

## Text

The director or commissioner may prohibit the harvest of any produce that carries pesticide residue in excess of a permissible tolerance which is established by the director pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 717, Sec. 13.)
