# FAC § 4055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 4055

---

## Text

Associations, by majority vote of their respective boards, may cooperate in the holding of a fair in any of the districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
