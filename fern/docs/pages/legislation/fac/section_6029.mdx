# FAC § 6029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 6.5. Vertebrate Pest Control Research

**Citation:** FAC § 6029

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2035, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 198, Sec. 1. (AB 1505) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6.5, commencing with Section 6025.)
