# FAC § 9169

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 9169

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to defeat or interfere with or to attempt to defeat or interfere with any animal disease test authorized by any provision of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 646.)
