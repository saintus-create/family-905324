# FAC § 29558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 6. Emergency Standards, Containers, Color Classifications, and Markings

**Citation:** FAC § 29558

---

## Text

Notice of hearing shall be mailed not less than 10 days prior to the date of the hearing to all persons that have filed with the director a request for notice of such hearings.
