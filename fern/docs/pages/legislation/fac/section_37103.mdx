# FAC § 37103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3. Butter > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 37103

---

## Text

Quality designations shall be affixed on the package in a manner and fashion which is prescribed in the regulations which are adopted by the director.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
