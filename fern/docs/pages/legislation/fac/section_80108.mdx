# FAC § 80108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 4. Enforcement Powers and Administrative Responsibilities

**Citation:** FAC § 80108

---

## Text

Any county may adopt ordinances not in conflict with this division for the preservation of native plants specified in Sections 80072 and 80073.
