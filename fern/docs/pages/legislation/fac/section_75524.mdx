# FAC § 75524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 75524

---

## Text

“Production research” means any research related to pest control and to the production, harvest, and postharvest handling of apples, other than marketing research.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 69, Sec. 11. Effective May 9, 1994.)
