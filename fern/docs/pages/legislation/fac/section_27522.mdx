# FAC § 27522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 27522

---

## Text

“Eggs that are packaged for export” means those eggs destined for conveyance to any location outside the United States or its territories.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 197, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
