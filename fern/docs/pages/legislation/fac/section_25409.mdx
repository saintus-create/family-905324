# FAC § 25409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 25409

---

## Text

“Poultry meat” means the carcass of poultry or any part of the carcass, except the giblets. It does not include live poultry or processed poultry meat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
