# FAC § 43063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 15. County Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 43063

---

## Text

“Costs,” as used in this article, means direct costs only in the administration and enforcement of this division at places other than places operated by retail merchants, as that term is described in Section 56161.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 450, Sec. 1.)
