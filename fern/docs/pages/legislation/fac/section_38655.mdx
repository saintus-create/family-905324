# FAC § 38655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 23. Flavored Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38655

---

## Text

Flavored high nutrient lowfat milk shall contain not more than 1.2 percent milk fat, and shall otherwise meet all standards and requirements which are specified in this division for high nutrient lowfat milk, and which are specified in Section 38651.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 26. Effective August 21, 1997.)
