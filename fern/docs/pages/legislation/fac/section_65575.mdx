# FAC § 65575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 3. The California Table Grape Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 65575

---

## Text

Members of the shipper advisory committee shall not receive a salary. Members of the shipper advisory committee may receive necessary traveling expenses and meal allowances, as approved by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 29. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
