# FAC § 77782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77782

---

## Text

The commission may adopt, and from time to time alter, rescind, modify, and amend all necessary and proper bylaws, rules, regulations, and orders in accordance with commission procedures for purposes of carrying out this chapter, including rules for appeals from any bylaw, rule, regulation, operating procedure, or order of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 45. Effective July 18, 1995.)
