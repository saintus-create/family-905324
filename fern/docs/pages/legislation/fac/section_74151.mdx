# FAC § 74151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12. Winegrowers of California Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 74151

---

## Text

(a) Prior to the beginning of each marketing season or as soon thereafter as possible, the commission shall establish an assessment rate for the marketing season.

(b) The assessment rate shall be the same for producers and vintners. The assessment rate for producers shall not exceed 1 percent of the gross dollar value of winegrapes marketed by producers to vintners. The assessment rate for vintners shall not exceed 1 percent of the gross dollar value of winegrapes acquired by vintners from producers. If a producer sells winegrapes for a specified sum in a bona fide sale, the gross dollar amount of this sum is the gross dollar value of the winegrapes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1286, Sec. 1.)
