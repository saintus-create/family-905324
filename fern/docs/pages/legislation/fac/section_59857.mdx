# FAC § 59857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 9. Assents to Marketing Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 59857

---

## Text

A member of the marketing program committee, or the proration zone agent, shall not unduly influence producers in their choice either for or against the institution of a marketing program, or for or against the termination of such a marketing program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 59610 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
