# FAC § 34627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Containers > Article: 5. Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 34627

---

## Text

A single service container shall be made of sanitary material, in a sanitary manner, and shall be kept in a sanitary condition free from contamination until it is actually filled with milk, skim milk, buttermilk, or cultured buttermilk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
