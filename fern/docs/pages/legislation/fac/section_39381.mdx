# FAC § 39381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Margarine, Dairy Spread, and Spread > Article: 2. Labeling of Shipping Containers

**Citation:** FAC § 39381

---

## Text

Each person that manufactures any margarine, dairy spread, or spread shall comply with this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 512, Sec. 10.)
