# FAC § 20004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20004

---

## Text

“Brand” includes “brand and mark” if a mark is recorded or used with a brand.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
