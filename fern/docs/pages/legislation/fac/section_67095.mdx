# FAC § 67095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 5. Duties and Powers of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 67095

---

## Text

In order to avoid excessive administrative charges which adversely impact avocado producers, handlers, and consumers, the commission may decide, not later than February 1 of any year, to exercise its authority prescribed in Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 44971) of Division 17 during the next succeeding marketing season.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 953, Sec. 10. Effective September 26, 1985.)
