# FAC § 3029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Free Admissions > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 3029

---

## Text

Each fair shall make an annual report to the department, as prescribed by the department, of the total number of credential and courtesy pass admissions issued and honored at the fair.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
