# FAC § 33481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 2. New Construction or Repairs

**Citation:** FAC § 33481

---

## Text

The secretary shall establish regulations for the construction of sanitary milk barns and milk houses which are used in the production of market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 524, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
