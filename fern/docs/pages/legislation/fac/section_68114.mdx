# FAC § 68114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 7. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 68114

---

## Text

It is not necessary for the commission to allege or prove that an adequate remedy at law does not exist in any action brought under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 525, Sec. 8.)
