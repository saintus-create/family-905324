# FAC § 41553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 4. Canned Foods > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 41553

---

## Text

One-half of all fines which are collected by any court or judge, for the violations of the provisions of this chapter shall be paid to the State Treasurer for credit to the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
