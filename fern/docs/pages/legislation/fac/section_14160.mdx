# FAC § 14160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3.7. Carbon Monoxide Pest Control Devices

**Citation:** FAC § 14160

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “carbon monoxide pest control device” means any method or instrument using carbon monoxide to prevent, eliminate, destroy, or mitigate burrowing rodent pests.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (SB 1332) Effective January 1, 2015.)
