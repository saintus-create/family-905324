# FAC § 14026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 1.5. Pesticides

**Citation:** FAC § 14026

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit or expand the department’s authority regarding pesticides which are not determined to be toxic air contaminants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1047, Sec. 2.)
