# FAC § 13122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 14. Birth Defect Prevention

**Citation:** FAC § 13122

---

## Text

It is the purpose of the Legislature in enacting this chapter to prevent pesticide induced abortions, birth defects, and infertility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 669, Sec. 1.)
