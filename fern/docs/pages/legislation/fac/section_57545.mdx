# FAC § 57545

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 12. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FAC § 57545

## Text

The Secretary of State may adopt any regulations necessary to carry our his or her duties pursuant to this chapter, including prescribing necessary forms.
