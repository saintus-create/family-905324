# FAC § 38222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 3.5. High Nutrient Lowfat Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38222

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, high nutrient lowfat milk shall meet all standards and requirements which are specified in this division for market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 878, Sec. 1.)
