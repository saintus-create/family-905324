# FAC § 59176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 19. Seasonal Marketing Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 59176

---

## Text

It is intended that the provisions of this article be interpreted liberally so that the director may carry out the marketing regulations and procedures authorized in this article in a practical and effective manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
