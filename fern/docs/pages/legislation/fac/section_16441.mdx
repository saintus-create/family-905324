# FAC § 16441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 1. ANIMALS AT LARGE > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 4. Civil Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** FAC § 16441

---

## Text

Any person that violates any provision of this part or any regulations issued pursuant to it is liable civilly for a penalty in an amount not to exceed a sum of five hundred dollars ($500) for each violation. Any money recovered under this section shall be paid into the Department of Agriculture Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
