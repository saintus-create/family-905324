# FAC § 77572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77572

---

## Text

The commission may adopt and, from time to time, alter, rescind, modify, and amend bylaws, regulations, operating procedures, and orders for carrying out this chapter, including regulations for appeals from any bylaw, regulation, operating procedure, or order of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 550, Sec. 1. Effective August 27, 1990.)
