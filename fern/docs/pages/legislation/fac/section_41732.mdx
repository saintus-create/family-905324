# FAC § 41732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 4. Powers of the Secretary

**Citation:** FAC § 41732

---

## Text

The secretary may cause the prosecution of any person violating any provision of this chapter, and may seize and hold a portion of any lot or load of garlic or onions for dehydration purposes involved in a violation, that may in the secretary’s judgment be necessary as evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 674, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
