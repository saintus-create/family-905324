# FAC § 34063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 3. Thermometers and Temperature Records

**Citation:** FAC § 34063

---

## Text

Any recording thermometer device and any indicating thermometer which is used in pasteurization shall be of a type which is approved by, and its use is at all times subject to the approval of, the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
