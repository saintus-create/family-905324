# FAC § 31107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. REGULATION AND LICENSING OF DOGS > Chapter: 5. Killing and Seizure > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 31107

---

## Text

No dog which is impounded pursuant to this division shall be killed or otherwise disposed of without notice to the owner, if he is known.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
