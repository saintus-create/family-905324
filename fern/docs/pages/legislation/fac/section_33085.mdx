# FAC § 33085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Inspection Services > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 33085

---

## Text

An advisory committee shall be established to assist the director in the administration of this chapter. The members of the advisory committee shall be appointed by the director and may hold office at the pleasure of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 927.)
