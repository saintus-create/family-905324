# FAC § 18669

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 1. Definitions and Short Title

**Citation:** FAC § 18669

---

## Text

“Official establishment” means any establishment at which inspection of the slaughter of livestock or poultry, or the preparation of any livestock product or poultry product, is maintained under the authority of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
