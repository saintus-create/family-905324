# FAC § 68037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 68037

---

## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” means the period beginning October 1 of any year and extending through September 30 of the following year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 606, Sec. 7.)
