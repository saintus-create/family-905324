# FAC § 79801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 29. Olive Oil Commission of California > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79801

---

## Text

The establishment of the commission is necessary for the efficient creation and management of the activities authorized in this chapter. The commission is also necessary to enhance the competitiveness of the industry within the state, national, and international marketplace.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. (SB 250) Effective January 1, 2014.)
