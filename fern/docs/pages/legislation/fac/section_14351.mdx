# FAC § 14351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 14351

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell any livestock drug which is subject to any provision of this chapter unless the drug is registered pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1224.)
