# FAC § 59286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 22. Inspection and Abatement

**Citation:** FAC § 59286

---

## Text

The notice of noncompliance shall include all of the following:

(a) A description of the lot.

(b) The place where, and the reasons for which, the lot is held.

(c) A citation of the applicable marketing order or agreement or marketing regulation, or rule and regulation, and any section of it upon which the notice of noncompliance is based.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
