# FAC § 11706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 4. Pest Control > Article: 1. Licenses and Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 11706

---

## Text

The license may be renewed before its expiration through application in the form prescribed by the director and upon payment of the proper fee as prescribed by the director pursuant to Section 11502.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 60, Sec. 6. (AB 203) Effective June 30, 2022.)
