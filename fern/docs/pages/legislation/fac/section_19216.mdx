# FAC § 19216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 19216

---

## Text

“Inedible kitchen grease” means any fat or used cooking greases and oils obtained from any source.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 699, Sec. 5.)
