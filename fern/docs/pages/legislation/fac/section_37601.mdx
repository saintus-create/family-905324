# FAC § 37601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. Cheese > Article: 3. Labeling

**Citation:** FAC § 37601

---

## Text

Any cheese sold in this state shall be labeled in compliance with Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 23. Effective June 6, 1994.)
