# FAC § 32792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 5. Reports, Scorecards and Statistics

**Citation:** FAC § 32792

---

## Text

The director shall do all of the following:

(a) Collect, compile, and publish statistics relative to the dairy industry, oleomargarine, and products resembling milk products.

(b) Disseminate the statistics and other information useful to the general good and development of the dairy industry of the state.
