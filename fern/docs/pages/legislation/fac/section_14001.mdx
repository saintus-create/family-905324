# FAC § 14001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FAC § 14001

---

## Text

The director shall control and otherwise regulate the use of restricted materials found to meet the criteria of Section 14004.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1276.)
