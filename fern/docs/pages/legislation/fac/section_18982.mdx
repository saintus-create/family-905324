# FAC § 18982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 5. Livestock Meat Inspector and Processing Inspector Licenses—General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 18982

---

## Text

The department shall conduct periodic training for licensed livestock meat inspectors and licensed processing inspectors to maintain and increase their competence in the performance of their official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 680, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
