# FAC § 79966

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 30. California Agave Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 79966

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that a referendum vote in favor of establishing the commission has not occurred, as provided in this article, the secretary shall certify the vote and declare this chapter inoperative. The secretary may conduct another implementation referendum vote one or more years after the previous vote has been taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. (AB 2606) Effective January 1, 2025.)
