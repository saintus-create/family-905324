# FAC § 79197.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25.5. California Spiny Lobster Commission > Article: 7. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 79197.5

---

## Text

Upon the suspension of the operation of this chapter, the commission shall mail a copy of the notice of the suspension to all eligible persons affected by the suspension whose names and addresses are on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 503, Sec. 1. (AB 944) Effective January 1, 2018. Repealed conditionally pursuant to Section 79182.5.)
