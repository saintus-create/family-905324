# FAC § 4483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 4. Deposit and Expenditure

**Citation:** FAC § 4483

---

## Text

Any expenditure by a county for construction and improvements is subject to Sections 25450 to 25463, inclusive, of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
