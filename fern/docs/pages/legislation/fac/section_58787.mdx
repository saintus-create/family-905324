# FAC § 58787

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 6. Notice, Reports, and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 58787

---

## Text

Upon the conclusion of the hearing, the director shall make a finding which is based upon the evidence received, whether assent, approval, or favor shall be determined by written assents or by referendum.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 677, Sec. 3.)
