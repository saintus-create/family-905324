# FAC § 9591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 4. Compensable Destruction of Diseased Animals

**Citation:** FAC § 9591

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 9595, if any animal or property is destroyed pursuant to Section 9569, the owner of the animal or property shall be paid an amount of money as provided in Section 9593.
