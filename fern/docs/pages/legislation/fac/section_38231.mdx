# FAC § 38231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 4. Evaporated Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38231

---

## Text

Evaporated milk is a food that complies with Section 131.130 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 95, Sec. 26. Effective June 6, 1994.)
