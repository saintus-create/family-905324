# FAC § 6025.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 6.5. Vertebrate Pest Control Research

**Citation:** FAC § 6025.2

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “vertebrate pest” means any specie of mammal, bird, reptile, amphibian, or fish that causes damage to agricultural, natural, or industrial resources, or to any other resource, and to the public health or safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 176, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, pursuant to Section 6029.)
