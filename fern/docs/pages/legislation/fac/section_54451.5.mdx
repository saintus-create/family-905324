# FAC § 54451.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 3.5. Conciliation

**Citation:** FAC § 54451.5

---

## Text

A conciliation service shall be appointed by the department if the parties cannot agree on a conciliator.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 77, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2006.)
