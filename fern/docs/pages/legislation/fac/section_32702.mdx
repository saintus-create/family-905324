# FAC § 32702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 1. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 32702

---

## Text

The director may bring an action to enjoin any violation or threatened violation of any provision of this division, in the superior court in and for the county in which the violation occurred or is about to occur.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
