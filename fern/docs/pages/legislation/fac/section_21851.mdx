# FAC § 21851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 9. Injury to, or Theft of, Cattle > Article: 1. Driving Cattle From Their Range

**Citation:** FAC § 21851

---

## Text

As used in this article, “range” means the enclosed or unenclosed lands outside of cities, towns, and villages in this state, whether of the public domain or in private ownership, upon which by custom, license, or otherwise, cattle are kept or permitted to roam and feed.
