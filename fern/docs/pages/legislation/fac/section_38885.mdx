# FAC § 38885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 39. Fromage Frais or Soft Fresh Cheese

**Citation:** FAC § 38885

---

## Text

Nonfat market milk, with or without added market milk solids, which has been pasteurized and afterwards fermented by one or more strains of streptococcus lactis, streptococcus cremoris, streptococcus diacethylactis, or leuconostoc sp., and rennet with or without pasteurized market cream, flavoring, or seasoning may be sold as fromage frais or soft fresh cheese. These products may contain nutritive carbohydrate sweeteners. They shall contain no more than 10 coliform bacteria per gram.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 38. Effective August 21, 1997.)
