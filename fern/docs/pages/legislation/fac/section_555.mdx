# FAC § 555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 8. Sustainable Agriculture

**Citation:** FAC § 555

---

## Text

If the Sustainable Agriculture Research and Education Program is established by the regents, it shall be established from existing resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 200, Sec. 1. (AB 1891) Effective January 1, 2011.)
