# FAC § 38531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 18. Modified Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38531

---

## Text

Modified milk is market milk, evaporated milk, or powdered milk which has been altered in composition to conform to special nutritional requirements and which contains no fat or oil except milk fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
