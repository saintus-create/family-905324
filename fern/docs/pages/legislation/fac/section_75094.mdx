# FAC § 75094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75094

---

## Text

The commission may publish and distribute, without charge, bulletins or other communications for dissemination of information relating to commission activities and other related matters to producers and handlers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1093, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1995.)
