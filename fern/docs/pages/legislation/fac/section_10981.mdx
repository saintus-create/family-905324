# FAC § 10981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 2. Garbage Control > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** FAC § 10981

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to feed garbage to swine unless he has a valid annual license issued by the director for each separate premises where garbage is fed to swine.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
