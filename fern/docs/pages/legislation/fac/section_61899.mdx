# FAC § 61899

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 61899

---

## Text

Any order of the director made pursuant to this chapter which substantially affects the rights of any interested party may be reviewed by any court of competent jurisdiction. Any such action shall be commenced within 30 days after the effective date of the order complained of, or within 30 days after the injurious effect complained of becomes reasonably apparent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
