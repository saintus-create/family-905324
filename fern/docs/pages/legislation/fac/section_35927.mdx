# FAC § 35927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 2. MILK AND CREAM > Chapter: 2. Market Milk and Cream > Article: 7. Certified Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 35927

---

## Text

A county milk commission may conduct any tests that it finds are reasonably necessary to carry out the purposes of this article and may employ personnel to conduct such tests and for such other duties as the commission deems necessary.

All applicants for certification shall submit samples for testing when requested to do so by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 7.)
