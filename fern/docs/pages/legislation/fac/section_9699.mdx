# FAC § 9699

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 8. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9699

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to violate the conditions of any permit which authorizes the permittee to travel upon any road or highway within a quarantined district, area, or premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
