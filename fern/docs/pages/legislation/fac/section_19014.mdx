# FAC § 19014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4.1. California Meat and Poultry Supplemental Inspection Act > Article: 8. Licensed Meat Processing and Custom Livestock Slaughter Establishments

**Citation:** FAC § 19014

---

## Text

Plant sanitation, sanitary dressing procedures, processing procedures, vehicle equipment, facility standards, and sanitation, including transportation and storage of products, shall follow procedures which may be set forth in regulations or operations manuals adopted by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1128, Sec. 1.)
