# FAC § 38943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 5. Product Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 38943

---

## Text

The application shall be accompanied by a fee of twenty-five dollars ($25). The information required by Section 38942 shall be kept current and amended whenever any change is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 787, Sec. 14.)
