# FAC § 12820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12820

---

## Text

The payment of any renewal fee or penalty is not a bar to any prosecution for doing business without proper registry.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
