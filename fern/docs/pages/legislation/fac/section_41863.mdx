# FAC § 41863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 8. Garlic and Onion Dehydrator Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41863

---

## Text

In selecting the membership of the committee, the secretary shall take into consideration the recommendations of organizations and associations of producers of garlic and onions for dehydrating and of handlers of garlic or onions for dehydrating.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 674, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1999.)
