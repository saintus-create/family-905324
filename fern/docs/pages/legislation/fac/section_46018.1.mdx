# FAC § 46018.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46018.1

---

## Text

The secretary and the county agricultural commissioners may conduct a program of announced and unannounced spot inspections to determine compliance with this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 456, Sec. 10. (SB 1117) Effective January 1, 2025.)
