# FAC § 32762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Powers of Director > Article: 4. Condemnation

**Citation:** FAC § 32762

---

## Text

The director may mark any condemned milk or cream for identification.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
