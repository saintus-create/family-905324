# FAC § 64663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 6. Education, Research, or Public Information Programs

**Citation:** FAC § 64663

---

## Text

The council shall not expend beef council funds for the advertising of brand name beef products, unless the expenditures are approved by a two-thirds vote of the council with the concurrence of the secretary. The council may expend beef council funds to conduct cost-sharing advertising with nonbeef brand name products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 291, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2002.)
