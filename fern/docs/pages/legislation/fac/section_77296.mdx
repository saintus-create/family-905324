# FAC § 77296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77296

---

## Text

The commission shall establish an annual budget according to accepted accounting practices. The budget shall be concurred in by the secretary prior to disbursement of funds, except for disbursements made pursuant to Section 77286.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 167, Sec. 8.4. Effective January 1, 2005.)
