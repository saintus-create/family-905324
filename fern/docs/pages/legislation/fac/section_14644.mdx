# FAC § 14644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 9. Inspection, Sampling, and Analysis

**Citation:** FAC § 14644

---

## Text

The secretary shall publish, at least annually, the results of examinations or chemical analyses of official samples of commercial fertilizer and agricultural minerals, and any additional information the secretary deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2006.)
