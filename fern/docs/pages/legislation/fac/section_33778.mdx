# FAC § 33778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Milk Products Plants > Article: 3. Milk Products Plant Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33778

---

## Text

The bodies or wearing apparel of persons who are employed or come in contact with any milk or its products shall be kept clean and shall be washed from time to time with reasonable frequency.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
