# FAC § 38861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 35. Milk Products Made by Acidified Processes

**Citation:** FAC § 38861

---

## Text

Acidified milk products are foods that comply with Section 131.111 or 131.162 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 30. Effective August 21, 1997.)
