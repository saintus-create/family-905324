# FAC § 2154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 3. Vacancies

**Citation:** FAC § 2154

---

## Text

If the appointing power does not make a temporary appointment of a commissioner and no person can be appointed from the eligible list by the director, the director may make a temporary appointment of a person who is competent to carry on the duties of the office for a period not exceeding three months.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
