# FAC § 39183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Imitation Ice Cream and Ice Milk > Article: 2. Imitation Ice Cream and Ice Milk Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 39183

---

## Text

Imitation ice milk shall contain not less than 2 percent of edible oil or fat, and harmless edible stabilizer as specified in Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 840, Sec. 8.)
