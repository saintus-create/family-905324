# FAC § 11707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 4. Pest Control > Article: 1. Licenses and Permits

**Citation:** FAC § 11707

---

## Text

To any fee which is not paid by the date of expiration, there shall be added a penalty as prescribed by the director pursuant to Section 11502.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 2004.)
