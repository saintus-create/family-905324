# FAC § 27542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 3. Registration of Egg Handlers

**Citation:** FAC § 27542

---

## Text

The certificate of registration shall not be transferable to any person, or be applicable to any location other than the location for which originally issued, and shall be conspicuously displayed at such location.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1009.)
