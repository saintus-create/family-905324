# FAC § 49010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 13. Nutrition Incentive Matching Grant Program

**Citation:** FAC § 49010

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Nutrition Incentives Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 442, Sec. 2. (AB 1321) Effective January 1, 2016.)
