# FAC § 34352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Weighing, Measuring, Sampling, and Loading of Milk > Article: 6. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 34352

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 34351, the department shall charge fees for examining glassware and apparatus which is used in methods of testing which are sufficient to cover the actual cost of such examination.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 715.)
