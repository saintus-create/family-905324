# FAC § 2128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 2. Appointment and Employment

**Citation:** FAC § 2128

---

## Text

If the position of deputy commissioner cannot be filled from the list, a temporary appointment may be made for a period not exceeding six months upon the written recommendation of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
