# FAC § 29190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 13. Colony Strength

**Citation:** FAC § 29190

---

## Text

The director may establish a system for certifying colony strength for bees used in the pollination of agricultural crops.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
