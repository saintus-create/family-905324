# FAC § 20669

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 3. Applications

**Citation:** FAC § 20669

---

## Text

An application shall not be accepted for the recordation of a brand which is accompanied by a mark if the use of the mark involves cutting off more than one-half of an ear.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
