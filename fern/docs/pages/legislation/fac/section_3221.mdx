# FAC § 3221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Revenue and Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 3221

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the 1-A District Agricultural Association may, with the approval of the department, expend any money available for expenditure by the association, for construction, repairs, and equipment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 559, Sec. 58. (AB 2143) Effective January 1, 2025.)
