# FAC § 41007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 10. Processing Tomato Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41007

---

## Text

The meetings of the committee shall be held in the office of the department at Sacramento, or elsewhere within the state, if necessary for the proper performance of its duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 783.)
