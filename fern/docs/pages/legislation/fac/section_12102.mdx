# FAC § 12102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 7. Pest Control Dealer > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 12102

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 12101 shall not apply to any federal, state, or county agency which provides pesticide materials for agricultural use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1414.)
