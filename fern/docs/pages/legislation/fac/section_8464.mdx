# FAC § 8464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8464

---

## Text

Any district formed prior to March 2, 1961, in which the order of the board of supervisors adopted the description in general terms as it appears in the petition is hereby validated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
