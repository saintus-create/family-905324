# FAC § 11405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PEST CONTROL OPERATIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 11405

---

## Text

“Registrant” means any person that registers pursuant to Section 11732 or 12031.
