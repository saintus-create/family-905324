# FAC § 74661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 74661

---

## Text

Within 30 days, after disallowing any claim in whole or in part, the commission shall serve written notice of its action on the claimant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 147, Sec. 4. Effective July 9, 1987.)
