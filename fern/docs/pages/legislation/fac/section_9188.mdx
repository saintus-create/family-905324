# FAC § 9188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 6. Collections

**Citation:** FAC § 9188

---

## Text

The secretary shall have a lien upon any livestock and real property owned by the person owing any fees due under any provision of this division that are not paid pursuant to Section 9185.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 133, Sec. 15. (AB 120) Effective July 26, 2011.)
