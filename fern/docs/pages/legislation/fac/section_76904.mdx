# FAC § 76904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 16. California Salmon Council > Article: 8. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 76904

---

## Text

Sections 8058 to 8070, inclusive, of the Fish and Game Code apply to claims for overpayment of fees to the secretary. For purposes of this section, “department,” as used in those sections, means the Department of Food and Agriculture, and “landing fee” means the fees imposed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 39. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
