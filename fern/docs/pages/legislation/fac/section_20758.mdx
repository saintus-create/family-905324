# FAC § 20758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 6. Fees and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 20758

---

## Text

The fee for recording the transfer of a brand, including a new certificate, is seventy dollars ($70).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 406, Sec. 6. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2016.)
