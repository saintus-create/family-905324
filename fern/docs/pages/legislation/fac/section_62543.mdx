# FAC § 62543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 3. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund Board

**Citation:** FAC § 62543

---

## Text

The board may, by contract, with the approval of the secretary, engage the services of an accounting firm or other consultants or agencies that the board determines to be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 16. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
