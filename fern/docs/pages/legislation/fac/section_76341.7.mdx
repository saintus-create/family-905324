# FAC § 76341.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 76341.7

---

## Text

This article applies to wool that was removed from sheep after December 31, 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 29, Sec. 1. Effective May 26, 1999.)
