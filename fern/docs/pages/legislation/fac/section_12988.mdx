# FAC § 12988

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10.5. Pesticides and Worker Safety

**Citation:** FAC § 12988

---

## Text

No pesticide may be registered or reregistered unless the director determines that the registrant has complied with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 274, Sec. 2.)
