# FAC § 77938

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 3. The California Cut Flower Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77938

---

## Text

The director or his or her representatives shall be notified and may attend each meeting of the commission and any committee meeting of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. Effective August 10, 1990.)
