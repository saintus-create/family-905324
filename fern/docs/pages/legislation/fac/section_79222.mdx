# FAC § 79222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79222

---

## Text

“Marketing season” or “fiscal year” are synonymous terms and mean the period beginning March 1, 2010, to September 30, 2011, and, thereafter, October 1 of any year and extending through September 30 of the following year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 365, Sec. 7. (AB 1795) Effective January 1, 2011.)
