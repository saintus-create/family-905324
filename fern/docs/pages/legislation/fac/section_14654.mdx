# FAC § 14654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 10. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 14654

---

## Text

If the secretary seizes any lot of fertilizing material, he or she shall immediately issue a hold order to the person that has control of that material. The secretary may affix to that lot or package of the material a warning tag which states that the lot is subject to a hold order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2006.)
