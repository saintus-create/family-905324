# FAC § 8802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 6. PEST CONTROL COMPACT

**Citation:** FAC § 8802

---

## Text

Consistent with law and within available appropriations, the departments, agencies, and officers of this state may cooperate with the fund established by the Pest Control Compact.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
