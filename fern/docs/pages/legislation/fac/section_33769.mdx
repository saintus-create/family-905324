# FAC § 33769

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Milk Products Plants > Article: 3. Milk Products Plant Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33769

---

## Text

No milk, its products, or any filth shall be allowed to accumulate, ferment, or decay upon the floor or walls.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
