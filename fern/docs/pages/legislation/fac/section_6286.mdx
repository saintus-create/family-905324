# FAC § 6286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 8. Dissolution of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6286

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall cause a certified copy of the resolution to be recorded in the office of the county recorder and shall file a certified copy of the same with the Secretary of State. Thereupon, the district is dissolved for all purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
