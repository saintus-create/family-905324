# FAC § 9161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 9161

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to bring, cause to be brought, or aid in bringing into the state any domestic animal which such person knows is infected with any contagious disease.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
