# FAC § 55485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 4. Agent’s License

**Citation:** FAC § 55485

---

## Text

The applicant shall satisfy the department of the applicant’s character, responsibility, and good faith in seeking to carry on the business that is stated in the application.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 696, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1998.)
