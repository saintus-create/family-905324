# FAC § 29300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 15. Enforcement and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 29300

---

## Text

The director, and the commissioner of each county under the direction and supervision of the director shall enforce this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
