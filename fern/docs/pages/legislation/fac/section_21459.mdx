# FAC § 21459

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Inspection of Hides and Carcasses > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 21459

---

## Text

A person shall not obliterate, alter, or remove a brand which is on a hide, or burn or in any manner destroy the hide until it has been inspected or released.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
