# FAC § 3334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 2. CALIFORNIA EXPOSITION AND STATE FAIR > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 3334

---

## Text

All records of the California Exposition and State Fair for entries in any of its events are public records, except entries in events which are scheduled for future judging or overnight entries in races on which there is parimutuel wagering prior to such events, judging, or races.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1148.)
