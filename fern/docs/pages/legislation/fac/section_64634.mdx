# FAC § 64634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 64634

---

## Text

None of the funds which are collected pursuant to this chapter shall be used for political contributions.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
