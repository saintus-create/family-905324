# FAC § 12852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 5. Labeling and Warranty

**Citation:** FAC § 12852

---

## Text

The registrant of any pesticide that is sold or delivered to a consumer in this state shall furnish printed directions for use, and dilution if any, upon the label, or shall enclose the printed directions in each container or package of the pesticide.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 60. Effective January 1, 1997.)
