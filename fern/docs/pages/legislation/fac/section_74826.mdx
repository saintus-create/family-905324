# FAC § 74826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.7. California Winegrape Growers Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74826

---

## Text

“Grape juice” or “juice” means unfermented crushed grapes without solids containing the major portion of the original water content.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 4.)
