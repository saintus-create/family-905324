# FAC § 57306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 10. Private Produce Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 57306

---

## Text

If the commissioner finds that a registered inspector has repeatedly given inaccurate information or failed to maintain required records, the commissioner may, after a hearing, cancel or suspend the registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1039, Sec. 1.)
