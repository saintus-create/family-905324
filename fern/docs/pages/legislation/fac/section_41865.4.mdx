# FAC § 41865.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 8. Garlic and Onion Dehydrator Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41865.4

---

## Text

The government agency or private entity shall keep an accurate record of expenses incurred in performing its responsibilities. Those records shall be available for not less than three years for audit during regular business hours upon request of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 520, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
