# FAC § 23404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 3. Inspection of Animals > Article: 1. Horses, Mules, and Burros

**Citation:** FAC § 23404

---

## Text

A certificate of inspection shall not be issued unless a bill of sale or other proof of ownership is exhibited at the time of the inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
