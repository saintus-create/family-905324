# FAC § 61838

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61838

---

## Text

“Stabilization and marketing plan” means any plan formulated and made effective by the director within the legislative standards provided by this chapter. It includes, among other things, the establishing of prices to be paid by handlers for any or all of the various classes of market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
