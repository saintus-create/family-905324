# FAC § 62621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2.5. Milk Producers Security Trust Fund > Article: 7. Producer Claims

**Citation:** FAC § 62621

---

## Text

If a verified claim form is not filed as required by Section 62620, the secretary and the fund are relieved of any further duty or liability pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 304, Sec. 45. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2020.)
