# FAC § 21322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 10. Records and Memorandums

**Citation:** FAC § 21322

---

## Text

The memorandum of the inspection shall be retained for record purposes by the inspector for at least two years after the date of the inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
