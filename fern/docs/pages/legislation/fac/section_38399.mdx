# FAC § 38399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 12. Extra Grade Edible Dry Whey, Dry Buttermilk, and Modified Dry Whey

**Citation:** FAC § 38399

---

## Text

Safe and suitable substances, as defined in Section 130.3 of Title 21 of the Code of Federal Regulations, may be used in the chemical, physical, or microbial processing of whey during the manufacture of modified whey products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 790.)
