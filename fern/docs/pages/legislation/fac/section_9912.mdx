# FAC § 9912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 9912

---

## Text

“Tuberculosis test” means the use of an official test for diagnosing tuberculosis in cattle.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 8. Effective August 23, 2004.)
