# FAC § 6230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Winegrape Pest and Disease Control District Law > Article: 3. Organization of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 6230

---

## Text

Upon the adoption of the order of organization, the board of supervisors shall immediately appoint a board of directors of five members to administer the affairs of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1. Effective October 11, 1993.)
