# FAC § 3333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 2. CALIFORNIA EXPOSITION AND STATE FAIR > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 3333

---

## Text

The board shall submit a report to the Legislature and Governor on or before May 31st of each year with respect to the financial condition, present operations, and future planned activities of the California Exposition and State Fair.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 825, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 2006.)
