# FAC § 38191

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 2.5. Lowfat Milk or Light Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38191

---

## Text

Lowfat milk or light (lite) milk is a market milk product with added milk solids derived from market milk. Lowfat milk or light milk shall contain not less than 0.9 percent milk fat, not more than 1.1 percent milk fat, and not less than 11 percent of milk solids not fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 329, Sec. 12. Effective August 21, 1997.)
