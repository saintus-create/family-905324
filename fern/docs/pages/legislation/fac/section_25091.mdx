# FAC § 25091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 5. Requirements for Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 25091

---

## Text

A person shall not operate a poultry plant unless all the poultry meat in the poultry plant is inspected by a poultry meat inspector pursuant to the regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
