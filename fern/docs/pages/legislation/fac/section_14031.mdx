# FAC § 14031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 2. 2,4-D and Other Herbicides

**Citation:** FAC § 14031

---

## Text

As used in this article, “2,4-D” means any form of 2,4-dichlorophenoxyacetic acid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
