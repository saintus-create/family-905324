# FAC § 9693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 3. Animal Quarantine > Article: 8. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 9693

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to move or cause to be moved any animal from place to place within any quarantined district, area, or premises, without written permission from the State Veterinarian or an authorized representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 503, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2002.)
