# FAC § 68040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Process” means to engage in the business of a processor.
