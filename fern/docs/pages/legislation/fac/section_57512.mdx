# FAC § 57512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 12. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien > Article: 2. Poultry and Fish Supply Lien

**Citation:** FAC § 57512

---

## Text

The lien created by this chapter shall be perfected and shall be effective upon the filing of a notice of claim of lien with the Secretary of State pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1502, Sec. 1.)
