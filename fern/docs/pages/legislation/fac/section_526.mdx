# FAC § 526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 7. Poultry and Livestock Diagnostic Laboratories

**Citation:** FAC § 526

---

## Text

The contract shall provide that the university, in consultation with the department, shall establish a schedule of fees to help defray the cost of operation of the laboratories.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1536, Sec. 4.)
