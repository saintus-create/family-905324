# FAC § 38987

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Products Resembling Milk Products > Article: 8. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 38987

---

## Text

Any moneys which are received by the director or the department pursuant to this chapter shall be paid into the State Treasury to the credit of the Department of Agriculture Fund to be expended as provided by the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1250.)
