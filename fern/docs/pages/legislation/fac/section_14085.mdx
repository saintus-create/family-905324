# FAC § 14085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 3. Restricted Materials > Article: 5. Paraquat Dichloride

**Citation:** FAC § 14085

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “pesticide” has the same meaning as specified in Section 12753.

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 688, Sec. 1. (AB 1963) Effective January 1, 2025.)
