# FAC § 64321.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. The Dairy Council of California Law > Article: 12. Referendum

**Citation:** FAC § 64321.5

---

## Text

If the secretary finds that producers and handlers have not assented to the council program pursuant to Section 64321, the council program may be resubmitted to a referendum as prescribed in this article within a period of no less than 60 days and no more than 180 days after the secretary announces the plan was not approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 371, Sec. 23. (AB 425) Effective January 1, 2022.)
