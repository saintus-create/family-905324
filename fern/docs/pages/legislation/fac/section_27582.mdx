# FAC § 27582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 7. Civil Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** FAC § 27582

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 27601, the director shall commence any civil action based on a violation of this chapter, and the regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter, which shall be limited to the remedies provided for in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 750, Sec. 6.)
