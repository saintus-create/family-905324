# FAC § 77625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 18. California Forest Products Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 77625

---

## Text

Any assessment which is levied pursuant to this chapter is a personal debt of every producer so assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 550, Sec. 1. Effective August 27, 1990.)
