# FAC § 5905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 1. Citrus White Fly Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 5905

---

## Text

The existence of any known host plant of citrus white fly within the boundaries of the district is reasonable cause to believe the host plant is infested with the citrus white fly.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 262.)
