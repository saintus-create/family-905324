# FAC § 63103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Marketing of Processing Strawberries > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 63103

---

## Text

“Purchase price” means all price and price terms for processing strawberries, including any and all forms of compensation from processors to producers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 18, Sec. 1. Effective March 29, 1985.)
