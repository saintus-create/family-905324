# FAC § 33483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 2. New Construction or Repairs

**Citation:** FAC § 33483

---

## Text

Minimum construction standards for new market milk dairy farms and extensive repairs to existing market milk dairy farms shall not be less than those which are established by the director pursuant to the authority which is conferred upon him by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
