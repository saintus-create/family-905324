# FAC § 8752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 8. Dissolution of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8752

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall give notice of the time and place fixed for the hearing upon the petition for dissolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
