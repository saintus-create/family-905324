# FAC § 9152.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. DISEASED ANIMALS AND POULTRY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3.5. Laboratory Services

**Citation:** FAC § 9152.5

---

## Text

A laboratory approved, certified, or accredited by the state or the federal government to provide services equivalent to those described in subdivision (a) of Section 9152 is exempt from this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 495, Sec. 1. (SB 703) Effective January 1, 2022.)
