# FAC § 58036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58036

---

## Text

The director may act as a mediator or arbitrator, if invited by both parties, in any controversy or issue that may arise between producers and distributors of any product.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
