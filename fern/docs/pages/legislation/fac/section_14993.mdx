# FAC § 14993

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 6. Commercial Feed > Article: 5. Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 14993

---

## Text

Any person that manufactures, processes, or mixes any special mix for another person, shall label it in accordance with regulations as specified by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1275.)
