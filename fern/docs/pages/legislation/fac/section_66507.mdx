# FAC § 66507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. California Iceberg Lettuce Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and Purposes

**Citation:** FAC § 66507

---

## Text

A commission form of administration created by this chapter is designed to deal with the broad fields of advertising, promotion, and marketing research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 958, Sec. 1.)
