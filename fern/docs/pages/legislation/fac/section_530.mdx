# FAC § 530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 7. Poultry and Livestock Diagnostic Laboratories

**Citation:** FAC § 530

---

## Text

A member of the Veterinary Diagnostic Laboratory Board is entitled to be paid his or her actual expenses which are incurred while engaged in performing his or her duties that are authorized by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1536, Sec. 4.)
