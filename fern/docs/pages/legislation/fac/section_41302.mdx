# FAC § 41302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 4. Canned Foods > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 41302

---

## Text

Any act that is made unlawful by Part 5 (commencing with Section 109875) of Division 104 of the Health and Safety Code is not made lawful by reason of this part. This part does not limit the powers of the State Department of Health Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 62. Effective September 29, 1996.)
