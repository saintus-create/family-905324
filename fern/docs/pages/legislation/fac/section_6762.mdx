# FAC § 6762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 1. Licenses > Article: 4. Refusal, Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** FAC § 6762

---

## Text

Any proceedings shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code. The director shall have all the powers which are granted in such chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
