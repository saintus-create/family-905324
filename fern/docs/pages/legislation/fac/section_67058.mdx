# FAC § 67058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 3. The California Avocado Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 67058

---

## Text

The commission shall have the power to appoint committees composed of both members and nonmembers of the commission to advise the commission in carrying out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 946, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2003.)
