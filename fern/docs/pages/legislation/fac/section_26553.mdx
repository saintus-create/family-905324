# FAC § 26553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 3. POULTRY > Chapter: 2. Sale, Possession, and Transportation > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 26553

---

## Text

“Place of business” includes any place or building in which, or at which, the business of a poultry dealer is conducted. Any group of buildings used as an integral part of one business shall be considered as one place of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
