# FAC § 6033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Specific Pest Control and Abatement Provisions > Article: 7. Beet Leafhopper Control

**Citation:** FAC § 6033

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, the assessment collected from producers by handlers or due from producer-handlers shall be paid by the handlers or producer-handlers to the secretary as provided by regulations of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 508, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
