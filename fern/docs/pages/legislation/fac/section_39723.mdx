# FAC § 39723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 3. UHT Reduced-Fat Milk and UHT Lowfat or Light Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 39723

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, UHT lowfat milk shall meet all the standards and requirements which are specified in this division for market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
