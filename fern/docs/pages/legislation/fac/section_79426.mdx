# FAC § 79426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 27. California Nursery Producers Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 79426

---

## Text

“Market” or “marketing” means to sell nursery products to a person other than another producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 605, Sec. 1. (AB 2695) Effective January 1, 2011.)
