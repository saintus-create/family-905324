# FAC § 27664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 4. EGGS AND EGG PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Eggs > Article: 14. Registered Brands

**Citation:** FAC § 27664

---

## Text

Every person who operates a container exchange business for master containers of eggs shall obtain a license from the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 550.)
