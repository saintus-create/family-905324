# FAC § 75502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13.5. California Apple Commission > Article: 1. Declarations and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 75502

---

## Text

The maintenance of the apple industry of California is necessary to assure the public of a continuous supply of this vital food product and the maintenance of needed levels of income for those persons engaged in the industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2.)
