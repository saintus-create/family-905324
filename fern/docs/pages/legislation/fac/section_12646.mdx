# FAC § 12646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 6. Abatement as Public Nuisance

**Citation:** FAC § 12646

---

## Text

If the court orders the sale of any of the produce or containers which can be salvaged, the costs of disposal shall be deducted from the proceeds of sale and the balance paid into court for the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
