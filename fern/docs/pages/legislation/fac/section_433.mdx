# FAC § 433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 2. Services

**Citation:** FAC § 433

---

## Text

The department shall correspond with agricultural societies, colleges, schools, the commissioners, and with all other persons who are necessary to secure the best results to agriculture in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
