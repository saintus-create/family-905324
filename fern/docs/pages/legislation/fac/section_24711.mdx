# FAC § 24711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 2. Plant Sanitation > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 24711

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to a poultry plant which is operated under the sanitary regulations of the Consumer and Marketing Service of the United States Department of Agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
