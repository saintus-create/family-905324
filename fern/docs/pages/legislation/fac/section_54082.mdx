# FAC § 54082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 4. Articles of Incorporation

**Citation:** FAC § 54082

---

## Text

Articles of incorporation shall be signed, acknowledged, and filed in the manner which is prescribed by the general laws of this state for domestic corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
