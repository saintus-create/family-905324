# FAC § 14585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 5. Fertilizing Materials > Article: 3. Fertilizer Inspection Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 14585

---

## Text

The board shall meet at the call of the chairperson or the secretary, or at the request of any five members of the board. The board shall meet at least once a year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 490, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2006.)
