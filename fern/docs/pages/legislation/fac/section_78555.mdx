# FAC § 78555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 7. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 78555

---

## Text

Every claim or refund for credit for overpayment of fees shall be in writing and shall state the specific ground upon which the claim is based.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1.)
