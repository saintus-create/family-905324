# FAC § 56445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 13. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** FAC § 56445

---

## Text

The department also has jurisdiction over any complaint or dispute if the licensee has failed to perform in accordance with any alternative dispute procedure award that is made in accordance with the terms of the written contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 696, Sec. 76. Effective January 1, 1998.)
