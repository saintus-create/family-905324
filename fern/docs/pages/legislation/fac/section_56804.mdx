# FAC § 56804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8. Produce Markets > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 56804

---

## Text

“Commissioner,” with respect to any matter which relates to a particular market, means the deputy labor commissioner who is in charge of the office of the Labor Commissioner which is located nearest to that market.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
