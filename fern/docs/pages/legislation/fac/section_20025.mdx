# FAC § 20025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 20025

---

## Text

“Cattle movement” means the transportation of cattle by vehicle or herding, such as a drive of an animal herd.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 210, Sec. 5. Effective May 24, 1982.)
