# FAC § 57010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 57010

---

## Text

“Produce dealer” includes “commission merchant,” as defined in Section 56105, and “dealer,” as defined in Section 56107. It does not, however, include a “broker,” as defined in Section 56103, nor “processor,” as defined in Section 55407.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 585.)
