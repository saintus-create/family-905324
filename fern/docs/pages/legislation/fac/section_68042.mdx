# FAC § 68042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 68042

---

## Text

“Production research” means any research related to the production, harvest, and post harvest handling of kiwifruit, other than marketing research.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 606, Sec. 10.)
