# FAC § 38703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 26. Sour Cream Dressing

**Citation:** FAC § 38703

---

## Text

At the time of sale to consumers, sour cream dressing shall be in containers of at least 12-gallon or 4-pound capacity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 641, Sec. 5.)
