# FAC § 35131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 7. Butter Permits and Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 35131

---

## Text

A person shall not engage in the business of cutting and wrapping butter unless such person, in addition to the milk products plant license, has a butter cutting and wrapping permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
