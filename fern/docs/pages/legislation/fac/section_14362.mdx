# FAC § 14362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 4. Livestock Drugs > Article: 6. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 14362

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to manufacture, distribute, sell, or use any livestock drug without complying with the provisions of this chapter and the regulations which are adopted pursuant to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1224.)
