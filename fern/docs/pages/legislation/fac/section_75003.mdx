# FAC § 75003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 75003

---

## Text

It is hereby declared to be the policy of this state to aid in the handling of eggs and egg products, to develop more efficient and equitable methods in handling, and to maintain job security for workers in the egg industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 121, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
