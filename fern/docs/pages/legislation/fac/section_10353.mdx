# FAC § 10353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 3. Bovine Brucellosis > Article: 4. Vaccinations

**Citation:** FAC § 10353

---

## Text

The owner of any calf which is to be vaccinated pursuant to this chapter shall, upon request of the department, provide the necessary facilities and assistance for such vaccination.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
