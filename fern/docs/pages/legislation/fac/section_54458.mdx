# FAC § 54458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 3.5. Conciliation

**Citation:** FAC § 54458

---

## Text

Any person who violates any provision of this article is liable civilly for a penalty in an amount not to exceed the sum of ten thousand dollars ($10,000) for each and every violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 510, Sec. 5.)
