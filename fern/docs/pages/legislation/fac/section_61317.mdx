# FAC § 61317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 61317

---

## Text

“Wholesale customer” means any person other than a distributor that buys packaged milk, cream, or any dairy product for resale to consumers or to other wholesale customers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 751, Sec. 4.)
