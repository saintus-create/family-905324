# FAC § 23123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. HORSES, MULES, BURROS, SHEEP, AND SWINE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 6. Collection and Disposition of Money

**Citation:** FAC § 23123

---

## Text

A civil action may be brought by the director to recover any fee, penalty, or other money that may become due pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
