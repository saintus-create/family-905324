# FAC § 78246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78246

---

## Text

The commission may adopt, amend, and repeal regulations and operating procedures necessary to carry out this chapter, including regulations governing appeals from actions taken by the commission pursuant to any of its regulations or operating procedures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1990.)
