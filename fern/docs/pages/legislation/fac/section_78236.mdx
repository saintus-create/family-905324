# FAC § 78236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 21. California Asparagus Commission > Article: 3. The California Asparagus Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 78236

---

## Text

The commission may sue and be sued, and may enter into contracts. Copies of its proceedings, records, and acts, when authenticated, shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of all statements therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 383, Sec. 8. (AB 1389) Effective January 1, 2014.)
