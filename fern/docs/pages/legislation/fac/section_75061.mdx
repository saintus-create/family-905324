# FAC § 75061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 3. The California Egg Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75061

---

## Text

The public member or his or her alternate has all the powers, rights, responsibilities, and privileges of any other member on the commission.

The public member shall not have any financial interest in the egg industry during the entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
