# FAC § 53423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Nursery Stock Grades and Standards > Article: 5. Warning Tags or Notices

**Citation:** FAC § 53423

---

## Text

The owner or custodian of nursery stock which is held under a warning tag or notice shall, upon demand, have the right of a hearing before the director or the commissioner as to the justification of any such order, and may appeal to the director from any order of the commissioner which is made pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
