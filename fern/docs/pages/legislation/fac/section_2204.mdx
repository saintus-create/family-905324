# FAC § 2204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 5. Compensation

**Citation:** FAC § 2204

---

## Text

The compensation of a commissioner who is appointed by the director shall be paid in the same manner as if he were appointed by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
