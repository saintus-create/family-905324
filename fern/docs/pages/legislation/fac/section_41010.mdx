# FAC § 41010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 10. Processing Tomato Advisory Committee

**Citation:** FAC § 41010

---

## Text

Alternate members shall serve at committee meetings only in the absence of the member for whom they are designated as alternate.
