# FAC § 52298

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. California Seed Law > Article: 2.5. Seed Advisory Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52298

---

## Text

The board shall meet at the call of its chairman or the director, or at the request of any four members of the board. The board shall meet at least once a year to review budget proposals and fiscal matters related to such proposals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 919.)
