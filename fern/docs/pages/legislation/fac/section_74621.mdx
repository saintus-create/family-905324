# FAC § 74621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.5. California Wine Commission > Article: 5. Implementation and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** FAC § 74621

---

## Text

This chapter, except as necessary to conduct an implementation referendum, shall not become operative until the director finds, in a referendum conducted by him or her, or a person designated by the director, that a favorable vote has been given pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1287, Sec. 3.)
