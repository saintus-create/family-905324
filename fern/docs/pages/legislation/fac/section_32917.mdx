# FAC § 32917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. General Requirements

**Citation:** FAC § 32917

---

## Text

A person shall not render any statement or bill which shows the weight, grade, percentage of fat, amount of fat, solids not fat, bacteria count, price, or total amount paid, for milk or any milk product which is false, deceptive, or misleading in any particular.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
