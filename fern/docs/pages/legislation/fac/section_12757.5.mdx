# FAC § 12757.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 12757.5

---

## Text

“Service container” means any container, other than the original labeled container of a registered pesticide provided by the registrant, that is utilized to hold, store, or transport the pesticide or the use-dilution of the pesticide.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 361, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1997.)
