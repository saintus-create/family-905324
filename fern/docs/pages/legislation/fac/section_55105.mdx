# FAC § 55105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 4. California Rice Certification Act of 2000 > Article: 9. Violations

**Citation:** FAC § 55105

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to plant, produce, process, or handle rice, except in compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 414, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2009.)
