# FAC § 10

**Citation:** FAC § 10

---

## Text

Whenever any notice, report, statement, or record is required by this code, it shall be in writing unless it is expressly provided that it may be oral.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
