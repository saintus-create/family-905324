# FAC § 59231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 21. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 59231

---

## Text

Any action for any penalty or other remedy which is prescribed under any provision of this chapter shall be commenced within three years from the date of the alleged violation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1222.)
