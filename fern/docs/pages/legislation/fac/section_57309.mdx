# FAC § 57309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 10. Private Produce Inspection

**Citation:** FAC § 57309

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to misrepresent the quality of any commodity inspected or to fail to make and maintain the records required by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1039, Sec. 1.)
