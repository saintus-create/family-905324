# FAC § 59607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 59607

---

## Text

The director may appoint an attorney and shall provide for such other personnel as may be necessary and shall prescribe their duties.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
