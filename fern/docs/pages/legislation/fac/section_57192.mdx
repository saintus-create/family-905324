# FAC § 57192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. Dairy Produce Exchanges > Article: 4. Investigations, Hearings, and Suspension or Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 57192

---

## Text

The proceedings shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code, and the director shall have all the powers which are granted in that chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
