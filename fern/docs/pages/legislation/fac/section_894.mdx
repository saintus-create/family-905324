# FAC § 894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. STATE ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. THE DEPARTMENT OF FOOD AND AGRICULTURE > Chapter: 9. False, Deceptive, or Misleading Marketing

**Citation:** FAC § 894

---

## Text

An action brought by the state or a county pursuant to Section 891 or 892 for a violation of Section 890 shall preclude a concurrent proceeding by the state or a county for the same act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 579, Sec. 1. (AB 1871) Effective January 1, 2015.)
