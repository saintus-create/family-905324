# FAC § 29406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 2. Honey > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 29406

---

## Text

“Container” means any box, crate, chest, carton, barrel, keg, or other receptacle which contains honey.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
