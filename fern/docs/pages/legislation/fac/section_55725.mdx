# FAC § 55725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 6. Processors of Farm Products > Article: 11. Examinations and Audits of the Books and Records of Licensees

**Citation:** FAC § 55725

---

## Text

The failure or refusal of any licensee to produce and make available to the director any books or records requested pursuant to this article, or otherwise to obstruct the examination or audit, is sufficient ground to suspend or revoke the license issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 942, Sec. 2.)
