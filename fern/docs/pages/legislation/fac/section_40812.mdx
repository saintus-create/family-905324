# FAC § 40812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 5. Inspection and Certification by the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 40812

---

## Text

If the director finds that the tomatoes which are being delivered conform with the standards prescribed by regulations, the director shall issue a certificate to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 783.)
