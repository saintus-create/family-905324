# FAC § 8806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 6. PEST CONTROL COMPACT

**Citation:** FAC § 8806

---

## Text

The notice of any meeting at which an application for assistance from the fund is to be considered given pursuant to Article VI(d), shall also be given to the Speaker of the Assembly and the President pro Tempore of the Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
