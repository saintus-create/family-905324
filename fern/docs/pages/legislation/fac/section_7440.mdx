# FAC § 7440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 4. WEEDS AND PEST SEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Broomrape Program > Article: 7. Operation

**Citation:** FAC § 7440

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on July 1, 2028, and, as of January 1, 2029, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 651, Sec. 2. (AB 402) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 1.5, comprising Sections 7401 to 7441.)
