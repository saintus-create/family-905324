# FAC § 76341.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 15. California Sheep Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 76341.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to the wool produced only for the producer’s home use. However, the producer shall file an affidavit with the commission establishing that he or she has not marketed the wool. The commission shall then determine whether the affidavit should be approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1102, Sec. 1.)
