# FAC § 4251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 7. War Memorials

**Citation:** FAC § 4251

---

## Text

Any association and any county may jointly construct a building to constitute a war memorial. The war memorial may be used for any purpose for which real property of an association may be used.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
