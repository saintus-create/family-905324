# FAC § 2187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 4. Removal

**Citation:** FAC § 2187

---

## Text

The license of a deputy commissioner may be revoked in the same manner and for the same causes that a license of a commissioner may be revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 734, Sec. 16. Effective September 18, 1987.)
