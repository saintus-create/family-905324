# FAC § 19421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAC § 19421

---

## Text

The compliance and evaluation team’s primary duty shall be the enforcement of the provisions of this chapter and the regulations made pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 815.)
