# FAC § 46005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 10. Organic Foods

**Citation:** FAC § 46005

---

## Text

This act shall apply notwithstanding any other provision of law that is inconsistent with this act. Nothing in this act is intended to repeal any other provision of law not inconsistent with this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 7.5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
