# FAC § 5826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 7. Registries

**Citation:** FAC § 5826

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to alter, deface, or misuse any statement of registry, certificate, label, or tag which is issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
