# FAC § 5008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 5008

---

## Text

“Shipment” means any article or thing which is, may be, or has been transported from one place to another place.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
