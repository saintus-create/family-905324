# FAC § 77735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77735

---

## Text

“Production research” means any research related to the production, harvest, or post-harvest handling of dates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 26. Effective July 18, 1995.)
