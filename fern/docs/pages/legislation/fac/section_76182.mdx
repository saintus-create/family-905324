# FAC § 76182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 14. California Cherry Commission > Article: 8. Continuation or Suspension and Termination

**Citation:** FAC § 76182

---

## Text

If a referendum is conducted as the result of a petition pursuant to Section 76183, it shall be conducted every fifth year following the industry petitioned referendum.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1004, Sec. 69. Effective September 26, 1985.)
