# FAC § 62750.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 3.5. Milk Pooling

**Citation:** FAC § 62750.1

---

## Text

In calculating the pool value, the volume of milk that has been classified as restricted use market milk shall be credited to the handler at the class 4a or class 4b price, whichever is lower.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 759, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1997.)
