# FAC § 33516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Dairy Farms > Article: 3. Dairy Farm Sanitation

**Citation:** FAC § 33516

---

## Text

The water supply for drinking by livestock shall not be stagnant, polluted with manure, urine drainage, decaying vegetable or animal matter, or pathogenic bacteria of any source.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
