# FAC § 74723.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 74723.5

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, partnership, limited liability company, corporation, firm, company, or other entity doing business in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 727, Sec. 50.1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
