# FAC § 32511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 32511

---

## Text

“Milk” means the unadulterated lacteal secretion which is obtained from the udder of a cow, water buffalo, goat, sheep, or other hooved mammal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 460, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
