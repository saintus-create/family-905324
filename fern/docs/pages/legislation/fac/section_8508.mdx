# FAC § 8508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 5. CITRUS PEST DISTRICT CONTROL LAW > Chapter: 3. Organization of Districts

**Citation:** FAC § 8508

---

## Text

The members of the board are not entitled to any compensation for their services, but may be reimbursed for their actual and necessary expenses, when claims for such expenses have been approved by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
