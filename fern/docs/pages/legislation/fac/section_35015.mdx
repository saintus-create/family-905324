# FAC § 35015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Licenses, Records, and Reports > Article: 3. Licensing of Milk Products Plants and Other Places of Business

**Citation:** FAC § 35015

---

## Text

If the condition of the milk products plant is found to be satisfactory, a milk products plant license shall be issued by the director to the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
