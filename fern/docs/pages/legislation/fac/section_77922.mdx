# FAC § 77922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 20. California Cut Flower Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77922

---

## Text

“Shipping research, analysis, and development” means the investigation, development, analysis, or use of information relating to the shipping of flower products and the development of shipping opportunities, practices, and methods in this state, nationally, and internationally.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 124, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2008.)
