# FAC § 74787

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 12.6. California Grape Rootstock Improvement Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 74787

---

## Text

Any assessment which is levied pursuant to this chapter is a personal debt of every nursery so assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 595, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
