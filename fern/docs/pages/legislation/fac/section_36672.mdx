# FAC § 36672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 4. Sweeteners

**Citation:** FAC § 36672

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, a milk product containing any sweetener shall be in conformance with all provisions of the applicable product definition and the identity standard specified in this division for the product.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 199, Sec. 2. Effective July 25, 1989.)
