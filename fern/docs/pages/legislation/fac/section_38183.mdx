# FAC § 38183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Dairy Products > Article: 2. Skim Milk, Nonfat Milk, or Fat-free Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 38183

---

## Text

All fluid skim milk which is sold for human consumption as such shall be derived from market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
