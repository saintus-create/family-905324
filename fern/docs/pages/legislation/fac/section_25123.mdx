# FAC § 25123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 6. Marks and Labels

**Citation:** FAC § 25123

---

## Text

A poultry meat inspector shall place or cause to be placed a mark on each carcass or container of poultry meat before the poultry meat is removed from a poultry plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
