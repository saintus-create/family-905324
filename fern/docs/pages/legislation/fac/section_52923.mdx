# FAC § 52923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 7. Seed Distribution

**Citation:** FAC § 52923

---

## Text

FSSC shall develop criteria in consultation with the board for the allocation of foundation seed to qualified recipients. FSSC shall determine who is a qualified recipient and allocate foundation seed pursuant to that criteria.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 136, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative on January 1, 2014, as prescribed in Section 52991.)
