# FAC § 61411.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 5. Bonding and Payment for Manufacturing Milk

**Citation:** FAC § 61411.3

---

## Text

Section 61411 does not apply to manufacturing milk purchased under cash-on-delivery terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 156, Sec. 14. Effective July 10, 1987.)
