# FAC § 24959

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 1. POULTRY AND RABBITS > Chapter: 3. Poultry Meat Inspection for Wholesomeness > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 24959

---

## Text

“Poultry meat inspector” means a person who after examination and demonstration has been issued a license by the director to inspect poultry meat for wholesomeness.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
