# FAC § 4361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 9. State Race Track Leasing Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 4361

---

## Text

The commission is a “department” for the purposes of hearings pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 11180) of Chapter 2 of Part 1 of Division 3 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1579.)
