# FAC § 80105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. CALIFORNIA DESERT NATIVE PLANTS > Chapter: 4. Enforcement Powers and Administrative Responsibilities

**Citation:** FAC § 80105

---

## Text

The director may adopt necessary rules and regulations not in conflict with this division for the enforcement of its provisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 70654 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 72.)
