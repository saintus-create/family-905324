# FAC § 3314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 2. CALIFORNIA EXPOSITION AND STATE FAIR > Chapter: 2. Organization

**Citation:** FAC § 3314

---

## Text

The directors shall serve without compensation, but shall be reimbursed for all necessary expenses actually incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1148.)
