# FAC § 20661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Brands and Brand Records Generally > Article: 3. Applications

**Citation:** FAC § 20661

---

## Text

Any person that desires to use a brand in this state may acquire the right to do so pursuant to this chapter. Application forms for this purpose shall be furnished by the bureau upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
