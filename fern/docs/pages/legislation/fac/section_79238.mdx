# FAC § 79238

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 26. California Blueberry Commission > Article: 3. The California Blueberry Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79238

---

## Text

A handler member or his or her alternate on the commission shall be an individual, partner, or employee of a handler who has a financial interest in handling blueberries for market. The handler member or his or her alternate shall be so qualified during the entire term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (AB 606) Effective January 1, 2010.)
