# FAC § 12974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 10. Recommendations and Usage

**Citation:** FAC § 12974

---

## Text

Failure of a person using a pesticide to possess a written recommendation shall create a rebuttable presumption that he or she has assumed responsibility for the recommendation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1476, Sec. 1.)
