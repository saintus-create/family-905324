# FAC § 61490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Marketing of Milk and Other Dairy Products > Article: 8. Manufacturing Milk Handler Licenses

**Citation:** FAC § 61490

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, each subsidiary manufacturing milk plant, or branch manufacturing milk plant, whether under one ownership or not, shall be considered as an individual manufacturing milk handler.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 446, Sec. 4.)
