# FAC § 68033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. California Kiwifruit Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 68033

---

## Text

“Handler” means any person engaged, within this state, as a distributor in the business of distributing kiwifruit or any person engaged as a processor in the business of processing kiwifruit and employees thereof so engaged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1027.)
