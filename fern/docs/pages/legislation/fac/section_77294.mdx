# FAC § 77294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 17. California Pepper Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 77294

---

## Text

The commission may publish and distribute, without charge, a bulletin or other communication for dissemination of information to producers and handlers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 335, Sec. 1. Effective July 14, 1988.)
