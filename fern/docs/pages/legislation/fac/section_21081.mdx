# FAC § 21081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Inspection of Cattle > Article: 2. Feedlot Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 21081

---

## Text

The operator of a feedlot as defined in Section 20013 shall register each such feedlot with the director.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 385.)
