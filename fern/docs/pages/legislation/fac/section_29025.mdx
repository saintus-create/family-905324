# FAC § 29025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 2. Apiary Board

**Citation:** FAC § 29025

---

## Text

The secretary may appoint a department representative as the secretary to the board. In consultation with the board, the secretary may identify a representative from the California Agricultural Commissioners and Sealers Association and a member of the industry that represents pollinated crops to serve as subject matter experts at meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 683, Sec. 6. (AB 719) Effective January 1, 2023.)
