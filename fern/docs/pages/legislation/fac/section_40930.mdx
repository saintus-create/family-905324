# FAC § 40930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 2. Standards for Tomatoes > Article: 8. Fees

**Citation:** FAC § 40930

---

## Text

The director shall not enforce this article during the period that any marketing order or commission covering the same subject is in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 676, Sec. 20.)
