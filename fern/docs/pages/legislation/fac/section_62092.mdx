# FAC § 62092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 10. Unlawful Trade Practices

**Citation:** FAC § 62092

---

## Text

The payment, allowance, or acceptance of any secret rebate, secret refund, or unearned discount by any person, whether in the form of money or otherwise, is an unlawful trade practice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
