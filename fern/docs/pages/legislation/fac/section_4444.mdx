# FAC § 4444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 4. COUNTY AND DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL FAIRS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions

**Citation:** FAC § 4444

---

## Text

No apportionment shall be made to any fair held in Sacramento County after January 1, 1948, other than a fair conducted by the California State Fair and Exposition, the California State Exposition and Fair Executive Committee or by a district agricultural association in Agricultural District 52.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
