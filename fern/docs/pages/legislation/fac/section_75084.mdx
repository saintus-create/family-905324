# FAC § 75084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 13. California Egg Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 75084

---

## Text

The commission shall appoint its own officers, including a chairperson, one or more vice chairpersons, and other officers that it deems necessary.

The officers have the powers and duties delegated to them by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 531, Sec. 1.)
