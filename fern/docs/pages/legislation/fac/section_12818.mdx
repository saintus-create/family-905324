# FAC § 12818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12818

---

## Text

If renewal is not applied for within one calendar month after the expiration of a registration, a penalty as prescribed by the director pursuant to Section 12812 shall be added to the registration fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 64. Effective January 1, 2004.)
