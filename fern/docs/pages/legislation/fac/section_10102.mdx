# FAC § 10102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 2. DISEASED BOVINES > Chapter: 2. Bovine Tuberculosis > Article: 9. Tuberculosis-Exposed Cattle

**Citation:** FAC § 10102

---

## Text

Indemnity for reacting tuberculosis-exposed cattle may be available and shall be in accordance with the provision stated in Article 7 (commencing with Section 10061) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 246, Sec. 54. Effective August 23, 2004.)
