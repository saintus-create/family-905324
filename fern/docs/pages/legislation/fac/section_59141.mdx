# FAC § 59141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 17. General Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** FAC § 59141

---

## Text

The director may establish such general rules and regulations for uniform application to all marketing orders and marketing agreements which are issued pursuant to this chapter as may be necessary to facilitate the administration and enforcement of such marketing orders and agreements.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
