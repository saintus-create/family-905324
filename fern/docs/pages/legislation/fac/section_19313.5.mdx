# FAC § 19313.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 5. Horsemeat and Pet Food > Article: 6.5. Transporters of Inedible Kitchen Grease

**Citation:** FAC § 19313.5

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to steal, misappropriate, contaminate, or damage inedible kitchen grease, or containers thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1036, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
