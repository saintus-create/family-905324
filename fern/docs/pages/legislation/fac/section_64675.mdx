# FAC § 64675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 7. Referendum for Voluntary Fee Payment

**Citation:** FAC § 64675

---

## Text

If the voluntary promotion and research program does not receive the required favorable vote or if less than the required number of producers have voted, another referendum shall not be petitioned for until after the expiration of a period of two years from the date of the referendum which failed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 418.)
