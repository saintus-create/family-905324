# FAC § 59031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 1. California Marketing Act of 1937 > Article: 13. Major Amendments

**Citation:** FAC § 59031

---

## Text

An amendment is a major amendment if it adds to, or deletes from, a marketing order authority to prohibit unfair trade practices.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
