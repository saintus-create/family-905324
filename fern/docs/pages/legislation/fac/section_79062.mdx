# FAC § 79062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 25. Sea Urchins > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 79062

---

## Text

The commission may administer and enforce this chapter and do and perform all acts and exercise all powers incidental to, or in connection with, or deemed reasonably necessary, to promote and maintain the sea urchin commercial fishing industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 973, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
