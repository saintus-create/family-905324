# FAC § 67107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. California Avocado Commission > Article: 6. Assessments and Records

**Citation:** FAC § 67107

---

## Text

The commission shall reimburse the secretary for all expenditures incurred by the secretary carrying out his or her duties and responsibilities under this chapter, except for the expenses incurred for any action under Section 67051.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 946, Sec. 53. Effective January 1, 2003.)
