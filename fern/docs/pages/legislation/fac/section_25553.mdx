# FAC § 25553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. POULTRY, RABBITS, EGGS, AND EGG PRODUCTS > Part: 2. CLASSIFICATION OF POULTRY AND RABBIT MEAT > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 25553

---

## Text

The director and the commissioners of each county, under the supervision of the director, shall enforce this part where any poultry meat is offered for sale or sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
