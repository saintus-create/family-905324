# FAC § 61998

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 7. Formation and Adoption of Stabilization and Marketing Plans

**Citation:** FAC § 61998

---

## Text

No stabilization or marketing plan shall contain provisions the purposes of which are to establish limitations upon the production of market milk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
