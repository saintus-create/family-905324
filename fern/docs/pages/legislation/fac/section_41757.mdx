# FAC § 41757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. CERTIFICATION, PROCESSING AND CANNING, AND CANNED FOODS > Chapter: 5. Standards for Garlic and Onions forDehydration > Article: 5. Weighing, Inspection, and Certification

**Citation:** FAC § 41757

---

## Text

The secretary is not required to perform inspection at any place where adequate inspection facilities are not provided.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 674, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1999.)
