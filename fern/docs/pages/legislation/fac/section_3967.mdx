# FAC § 3967

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXPOSITIONS AND FAIRS > Part: 3. DISTRICT AGRICULTURAL ASSOCIATIONS > Chapter: 3. Organization and Administration

**Citation:** FAC § 3967

---

## Text

Any director who misses three consecutive regular meetings of the board without the permission of the board is deemed to have resigned as a director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 50.)
