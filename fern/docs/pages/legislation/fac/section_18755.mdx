# FAC § 18755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ANIMALS GENERALLY > Part: 3. SLAUGHTERED ANIMALS > Chapter: 4. Meat and Poultry Inspection > Article: 4. Adulteration

**Citation:** FAC § 18755

---

## Text

A livestock or poultry product is adulterated if it is, in whole or in part, the product of an animal, including any poultry, which has died otherwise than by slaughter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1385.)
