# FAC § 78486

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 23. California Seafood Council > Article: 4. California Seafood Council

**Citation:** FAC § 78486

---

## Text

One nonvoting member of the council may be appointed by the secretary to represent each of the following entities:

(a) The department.

(b) The Department of Fish and Game.

(c) The California Sea Grant Program.

(d) The State Department of Health Services.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 225, Sec. 13. Effective August 16, 2004.)
