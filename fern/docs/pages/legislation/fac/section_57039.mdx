# FAC § 57039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 8.5. Produce Markets in Alameda, San Francisco, and San Mateo Counties > Article: 5. Charges for Unloading Produce

**Citation:** FAC § 57039

---

## Text

The director may by regulation establish a fee in the amount necessary to cover the costs that are incurred by the Department of Industrial Relations in implementing this article. Each party participating in a hearing conducted pursuant to this article shall pay an equal share of the fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 424, Sec. 6.)
