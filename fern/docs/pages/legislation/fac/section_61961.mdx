# FAC § 61961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 3. MARKETING LAWS REGARDING PARTICULAR PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Stabilization and Marketing of Market Milk > Article: 6. Marketing Areas

**Citation:** FAC § 61961

---

## Text

The director shall designate marketing areas which he deems necessary or advisable to effectuate the purposes of this chapter, and in which he finds the conditions affecting the production, handling, and sale of market milk, are reasonably uniform.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1192.)
