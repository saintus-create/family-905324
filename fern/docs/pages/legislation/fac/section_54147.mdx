# FAC § 54147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 1. Nonprofit Cooperative Associations > Article: 6. Directors and Management

**Citation:** FAC § 54147

---

## Text

An association may provide a fair remuneration for the time which is actually spent by its officers and directors in its service and for the service of the members of its executive committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
