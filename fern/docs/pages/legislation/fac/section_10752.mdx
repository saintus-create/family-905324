# FAC § 10752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. ANIMAL AND POULTRY QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. DISEASED SWINE > Chapter: 1. Importation and Sale > Article: 3. Sales Yards

**Citation:** FAC § 10752

---

## Text

The sales yard records shall show definite information relative to description of animals, such as breed, weight, color, and marks. The records shall be kept on file for one year, during which time they shall be at the disposal of the director for inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
