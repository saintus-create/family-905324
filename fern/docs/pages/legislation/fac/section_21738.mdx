# FAC § 21738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Sale and Gift of Cattle, Their Carcasses and Hides > Article: 2. Public Salesyards

**Citation:** FAC § 21738

---

## Text

Each such license shall be renewed on or before the first day of each succeeding calendar year. The renewal fee is one hundred dollars ($100) for each salesyard.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
