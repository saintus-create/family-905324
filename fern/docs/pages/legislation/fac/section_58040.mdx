# FAC § 58040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. State Marketing Services > Article: 2. Powers of the Director

**Citation:** FAC § 58040

---

## Text

The director may improve, broaden, and extend in every practicable way, the distribution and sale of any product of this state throughout the markets of the world.

This section shall become operative January 1, 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 1) and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1480, Sec. 2. Section operative January 1, 1990, by its own provisions.)
