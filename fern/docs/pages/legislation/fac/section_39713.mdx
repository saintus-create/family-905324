# FAC § 39713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 3. MANUFACTURED PRODUCTS > Chapter: 9. UHT Milk Products > Article: 2. UHT Milk and Cream

**Citation:** FAC § 39713

---

## Text

UHT milk shall contain not less than 3.25 percent milk fat and not less than 8.25 percent milk solids-not-fat.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 646, Sec. 17. Effective September 15, 1987.)
