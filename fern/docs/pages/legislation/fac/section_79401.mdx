# FAC § 79401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 27. California Nursery Producers Commission > Article: 1. Declaration and General Provisions

**Citation:** FAC § 79401

---

## Text

The production of nursery products constitutes an important industry of this state that provides substantial and necessary revenues for the state, products that are a foundation for its residents’ food supply, and employment for its residents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 605, Sec. 1. (AB 2695) Effective January 1, 2011.)
