# FAC § 69027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. California Pistachio Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 69027

---

## Text

“Producer” or “grower” means any person who is engaged within this state in the business of producing, or causing to be produced for market, pistachios, and who, upon request, provides proof of commodity sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 391, Sec. 1. Effective July 17, 1986.)
