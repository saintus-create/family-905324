# FAC § 12643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 1.5. Produce Carrying Pesticide Residue > Article: 6. Abatement as Public Nuisance

**Citation:** FAC § 12643

---

## Text

The district attorney of the county in which the nuisance is found, at the request of the director, shall maintain, in the name of the people of the State of California, a civil action to abate and prevent the nuisance.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 26.)
