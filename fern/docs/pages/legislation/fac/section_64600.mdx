# FAC § 64600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. California Beef Council Law > Article: 4. California Beef Council

**Citation:** FAC § 64600

---

## Text

The members of the council shall be reimbursed for necessary traveling and other expenses incurred in the performance of their official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 291, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
