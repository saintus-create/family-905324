# FAC § 34004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. MILK AND MILK PRODUCTS ACT OF 1947 > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Pasteurization > Article: 1. Standards

**Citation:** FAC § 34004

---

## Text

Pasteurized skim milk, which is derived from market milk and which is used for standardizing pasteurized market milk, may be repasteurized once.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
