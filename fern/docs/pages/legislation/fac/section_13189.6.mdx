# FAC § 13189.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 17.5. Pesticide Use Near Schoolsites

**Citation:** FAC § 13189.6

---

## Text

This article does not restrict the department’s authority to amend Sections 6690 to 6692, inclusive, of Title 3 of the California Code of Regulations to the extent that those amendments are consistent with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 552, Sec. 1. (AB 1864) Effective January 1, 2025.)
