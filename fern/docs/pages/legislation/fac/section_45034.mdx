# FAC § 45034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. FRUIT, NUT, AND VEGETABLE STANDARDS > Chapter: 9. Avocados > Article: 3. Proof of Ownership

**Citation:** FAC § 45034

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to knowingly falsify or cause to be falsified any information in a record intended to show proof of ownership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1068, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1989.)
