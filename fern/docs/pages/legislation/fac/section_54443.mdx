# FAC § 54443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperative Bargaining Associations > Article: 3. Annual Report

**Citation:** FAC § 54443

---

## Text

The advisory committee shall study and report on all of the following issues:

(a) Unfair trade practices.

(b) Licensing.

(c) Funding.

(d) Investigation and hearing procedures.

(e) The need for a mechanism to resolve bargaining disputes.

(f) Any other issues relating to this chapter.

(g) Any recommended changes to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 613, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
