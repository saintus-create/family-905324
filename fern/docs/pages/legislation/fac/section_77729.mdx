# FAC § 77729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 19. California Date Commission > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FAC § 77729

---

## Text

“Market” or “marketing” means to sell, consign, or otherwise distribute dates into commercial channels of trade resulting in the sale of dates. Dates shall also be considered “marketed” when inspected and certified by any third party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 130, Sec. 20. Effective July 18, 1995.)
