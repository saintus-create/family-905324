# FAC § 2280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LOCAL ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. County Agricultural Commissioners and Their Employees > Article: 8. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** FAC § 2280

---

## Text

The director, when acting in person with a commissioner, has all the rights of such commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15. Note: See this section as modified on July 17, 1991, in Governor's Reorganization Plan No. 1 of 1991.)
