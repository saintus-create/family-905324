# FAC § 52893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. FIELD CROPS, SEEDS, SEED POTATOES, ONE-VARIETY COTTON DISTRICTS, AND NURSERY STOCK GRADES AND STANDARDS > Chapter: 4. San Joaquin Valley Quality Cotton District > Article: 4. Duties and Powers of the Board

**Citation:** FAC § 52893

---

## Text

The board may require that all cottonseed delinted for planting within the district be certified by a qualified seed certification agency as provided by subdivision (d) of Section 52482.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 189, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2009.)
