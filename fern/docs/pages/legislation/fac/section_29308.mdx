# FAC § 29308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 15. Enforcement and Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 29308

---

## Text

The director shall appoint a supervisor of apiary inspection and such qualified state apiary inspectors as may be necessary.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
