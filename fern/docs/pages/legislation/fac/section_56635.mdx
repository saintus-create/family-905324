# FAC § 56635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PROCESSORS, STORERS, DEALERS, AND DISTRIBUTORS OF AGRICULTURAL PRODUCTS > Chapter: 7. Produce Dealers > Article: 19. Criminal Penalties

**Citation:** FAC § 56635

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person to intentionally make any false or misleading statement as to market conditions.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
