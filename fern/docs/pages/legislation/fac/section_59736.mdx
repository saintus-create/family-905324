# FAC § 59736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. MARKETING > Part: 2. GENERAL MARKETING LAWS > Chapter: 2. The Agricultural Producers Marketing Law > Article: 7. Marketing Program and Grading Committees

**Citation:** FAC § 59736

---

## Text

The exercise of the powers which are granted to a marketing program committee in its administration of a marketing program which is made effective pursuant to this chapter is subject to the approval of the director.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1186.)
