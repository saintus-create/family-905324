# FAC § 29144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BEE MANAGEMENT AND HONEY PRODUCTION > Chapter: 1. Bees > Article: 10. Exportation

**Citation:** FAC § 29144

---

## Text

A commissioner shall make reasonable inspection as may be necessary to determine the facts which are required by the state or country of intended destination and shall issue a certificate that states the facts which are determined upon receipt of the scheduled fee for a certificate or, if no scheduled fee has been established, upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1404, Sec. 2.)
