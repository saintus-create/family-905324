# FAC § 5803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 1. GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Special Quarantine, Control, and Abatement Methods > Article: 6. Grafts and Buds from Plants Generally Infected

**Citation:** FAC § 5803

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to bud, graft, or otherwise propagate or grow any fruit or nut tree or vine in violation of any regulations which are adopted pursuant to this article or to sell as nursery stock any plant which is so produced.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
