# FAC § 6742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PLANT QUARANTINE AND PEST CONTROL > Part: 3. NURSERY STOCK > Chapter: 1. Licenses > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** FAC § 6742

---

## Text

Any person that sells seeds and does not sell or ship any other kinds of nursery stock is exempt from the license requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
