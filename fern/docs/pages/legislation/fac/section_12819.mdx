# FAC § 12819

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. AGRICULTURAL CHEMICALS, LIVESTOCK REMEDIES, AND COMMERCIAL FEEDS > Chapter: 2. Pesticides > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** FAC § 12819

---

## Text

A penalty shall not be collected if the person that makes application for renewal of registration makes an affidavit that no business was done during the period of nonregistration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 717, Sec. 10.)
