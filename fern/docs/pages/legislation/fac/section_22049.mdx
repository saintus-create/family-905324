# FAC § 22049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CATTLE PROTECTION > Chapter: 10. Slaughter of Cattle > Article: 2. Licensed Slaughterers

**Citation:** FAC § 22049

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a licensed slaughterer to slaughter cattle at any place other than a slaughterhouse at which he is licensed to slaughter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1967, Ch. 15.)
