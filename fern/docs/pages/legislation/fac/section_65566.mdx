# FAC § 65566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL AGENCY LAWS > Part: 2. MARKETING ADVISORY AND PROMOTIONAL COUNCILS AND COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. The California Table Grape Commission > Article: 3. The California Table Grape Commission

**Citation:** FAC § 65566

---

## Text

A person appointed to the office of commissioner by the secretary shall be selected pursuant to the nomination, election, and appointment procedures established by the commission in accordance with this chapter and approved by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 614, Sec. 23. (AB 482) Effective January 1, 2026.)
