# PRC § 49601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Burning Garbage > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 49601

---

## Text

No crematory described in Section 49600 shall be operated in this state except in a manner which will prevent the propagation of disease through contamination of the atmosphere of any city or town by the gases or fumes arising from the fires or ovens of the crematory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
