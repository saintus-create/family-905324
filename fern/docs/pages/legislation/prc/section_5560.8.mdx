# PRC § 5560.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5560.8

---

## Text

A district may enact the foregoing as a separate and distinct ordinance which shall apply to all or one or more of its ordinances.
