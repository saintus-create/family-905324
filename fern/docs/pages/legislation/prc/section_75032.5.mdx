# PRC § 75032.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 3. Flood Control

**Citation:** PRC § 75032.5

---

## Text

The sum of forty million dollars ($40,000,000) shall be available to the department for Flood Protection Corridor projects that are consistent with Water Code Section 79037.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 84.)
