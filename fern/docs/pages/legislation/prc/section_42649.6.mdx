# PRC § 42649.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 12.8. Recycling of Commercial Solid Waste

**Citation:** PRC § 42649.6

---

## Text

A local agency may charge and collect a fee from a commercial waste generator in order to recover the local agency’s costs incurred in complying with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 476, Sec. 12. (AB 341) Effective January 1, 2012.)
