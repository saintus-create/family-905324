# PRC § 3182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3.5. Natural Gas Storage Wells

**Citation:** PRC § 3182

---

## Text

On a weekly basis, the division shall post a list of notices received pursuant to Section 3203 on the division’s Internet Web site. Copies of any notice shall be provided to members of the public upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 673, Sec. 3. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2017.)
