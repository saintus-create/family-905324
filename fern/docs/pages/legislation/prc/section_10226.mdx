# PRC § 10226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 10226

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to overrule, rescind, or amend any of the requirements prescribed in Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 51200) of Division 1 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 931, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
