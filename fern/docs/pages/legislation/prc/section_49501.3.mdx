# PRC § 49501.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Solid Waste Enterprises > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 49501.3

---

## Text

“Lawfully provided” means the services of the solid waste enterprise are in substantial compliance with the terms and conditions of its franchise, contract, license, or permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 811, Sec. 2. Effective September 24, 1998.)
