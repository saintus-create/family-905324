# PRC § 14549.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 14549.4

---

## Text

The department shall study and develop a system or process to address the issue of glass contamination to improve the quality of glass material collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 977, Sec. 1. (SB 38) Effective January 1, 2023.)
