# PRC § 49115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 49115

---

## Text

At the final hearing the board of supervisors shall make those changes in the proposed boundaries that are advisable and shall define and establish the boundaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
