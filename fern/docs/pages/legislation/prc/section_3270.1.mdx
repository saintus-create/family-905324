# PRC § 3270.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 4.4. Regulation of Production Facilities

**Citation:** PRC § 3270.1

---

## Text

Within three months of its acquisition of a production facility or at the time of the initial production at its production facility, the facility operator shall file with the division a spill contingency plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 562, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2009.)
