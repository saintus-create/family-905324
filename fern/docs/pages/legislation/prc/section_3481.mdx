# PRC § 3481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Grants for Used Oil Collection Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 3481

---

## Text

The purpose of the used oil collection demonstration grant program is to encourage the establishment of public used oil collection projects and to provide capital outlay and other costs to provide households with the capability of collection used oil generated in those households and to encourage the collection, recycling, and proper disposal of used oil.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1657, Sec. 2.)
