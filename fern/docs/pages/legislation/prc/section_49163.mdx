# PRC § 49163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49163

---

## Text

An election board consisting of one inspector, one judge and one clerk shall be appointed by the district board for each precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
