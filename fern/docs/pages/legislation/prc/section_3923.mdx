# PRC § 3923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 1. Manner of Locating Mining Claims, Tunnel Rights, and Millsites

**Citation:** PRC § 3923

## Text

This chapter does not in any manner affect or abolish any mining district or the rules and regulations thereof within the state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
