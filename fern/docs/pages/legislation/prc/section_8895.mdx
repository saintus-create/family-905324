# PRC § 8895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 5. California Orthometric Heights

**Citation:** PRC § 8895

---

## Text

When a geoid model is used to determine derived COH88 values, it shall be the latest geoid model published by NGS.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 29.16. Effective January 1, 2006.)
