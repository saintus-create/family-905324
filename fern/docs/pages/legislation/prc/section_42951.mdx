# PRC § 42951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 19. Tire Hauler Registration > Article: 2. Registration and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42951

---

## Text

(a) Every person who engages in the transportation of waste or used tires shall hold a valid waste and used tire hauler registration, unless exempt as specified in Section 42954.

(b) A registered waste and used tire hauler shall only transport waste or used tires to a facility that is permitted, excluded, exempted, or otherwise authorized by the department, by statute, or by regulation, to accept waste and used tires, or to a facility that lawfully accepts waste or used tires for reuse or disposal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 542, Sec. 2. (SB 1181) Effective January 1, 2023.)
