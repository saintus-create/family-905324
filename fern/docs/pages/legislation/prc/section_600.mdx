# PRC § 600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of Conservation > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 600

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “department” means the Department of Conservation and “director” means the Director of Conservation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
