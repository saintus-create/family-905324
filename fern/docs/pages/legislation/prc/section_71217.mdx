# PRC § 71217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 36. MARINE INVASIVE SPECIES ACT > Chapter: 5. Civil and Criminal Penalties and Liability

**Citation:** PRC § 71217

---

## Text

A person who violates subdivision (c) of Section 71216 is guilty of a misdemeanor, and is punishable by imprisonment in the county jail for not more than one year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 491, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2004.)
