# PRC § 21080.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.6. General

**Citation:** PRC § 21080.01

---

## Text

This division shall not apply to any activity or approval necessary for the reopening and operation of the California Men’s Colony West Facility in San Luis Obispo County.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 958, Sec. 6.5. Effective September 20, 1983.)
