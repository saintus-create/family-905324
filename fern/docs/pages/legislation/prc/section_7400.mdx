# PRC § 7400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 1. School Lands and Indemnity Lands > Article: 2. Indemnity Land Selections

**Citation:** PRC § 7400

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that, as of January 1, 2020, the commission has acquired roughly 81,643 acres of indemnity school lands and is owed roughly 51,000 additional acres of indemnity school lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 311, Sec. 7. (SB 1472) Effective January 1, 2021.)
