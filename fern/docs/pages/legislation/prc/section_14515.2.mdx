# PRC § 14515.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14515.2

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, corporation, operation, or entity, whether or not certified or registered pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 597, Sec. 1. (AB 1846) Effective January 1, 2015.)
