# PRC § 5796

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 14. Lower San Gabriel River Recreation and Park District

**Citation:** PRC § 5796

---

## Text

(a) The Lower San Gabriel River Recreation and Park District may be established subject to this chapter and the Cortese-Knox-Hertzberg Local Government Reorganization Act of 2000 (Division 3 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 5 of the Government Code).

(b) A petition or resolution to establish the district pursuant to this article and Article 3 (commencing with Section 5782) shall not be submitted to the Los Angeles County Local Agency Formation Commission on or after January 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 761, Sec. 2. (SB 268) Effective January 1, 2022.)
