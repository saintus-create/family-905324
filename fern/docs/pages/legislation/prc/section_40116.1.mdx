# PRC § 40116.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40116.1

---

## Text

“Composting” means the controlled or uncontrolled biological decomposition of organic wastes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 500, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
