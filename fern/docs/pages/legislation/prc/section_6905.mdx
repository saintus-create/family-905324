# PRC § 6905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 5.5. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 6905

---

## Text

Permits and leases may be issued pursuant to this article only to those who qualify for permits and leases under Section 6801.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1139.)
