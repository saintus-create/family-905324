# PRC § 13036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 13036

---

## Text

All vacancies in the office of director shall be filled by appointment by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
