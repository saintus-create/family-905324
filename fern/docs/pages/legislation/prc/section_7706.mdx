# PRC § 7706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 1. Applications for Purchase of Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 7706

---

## Text

Each application for lands shall be accompanied by a reasonable filing fee, not in excess of one hundred dollars ($100), as prescribed by the commission by rule or regulation, and no application shall be filed, or noted in any way until the fee is paid.
