# PRC § 9404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 9. General Powers of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9404

---

## Text

The directors may execute all necessary contracts. They may employ such agents, officers, and employees as may be necessary, prescribe their duties, and fix their compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
