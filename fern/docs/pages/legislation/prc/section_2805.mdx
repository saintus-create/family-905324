# PRC § 2805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 11. Earthquake Education

**Citation:** PRC § 2805

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Earthquake Education Act of 1984.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1558, Sec. 1.)
