# PRC § 49162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49162

---

## Text

For purposes of the bond election, the district board may consolidate into one precinct several precincts established for general election purposes and describe the precinct by reference to the general election precincts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
