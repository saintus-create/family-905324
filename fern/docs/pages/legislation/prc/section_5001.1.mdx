# PRC § 5001.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 1. State Park System

**Citation:** PRC § 5001.1

---

## Text

As used in this division, “department” means the Department of Parks and Recreation and “director” means the Director of Parks and Recreation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
