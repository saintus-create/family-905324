# PRC § 13035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 13035

---

## Text

Exclusive of the supervisorial member, the directors shall be voters in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 140, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
