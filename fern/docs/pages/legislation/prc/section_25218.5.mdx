# PRC § 25218.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 25218.5

---

## Text

The provisions specifying any power or duty of the commission shall be liberally construed, in order to carry out the objectives of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
