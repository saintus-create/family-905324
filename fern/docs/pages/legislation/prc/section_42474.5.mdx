# PRC § 42474.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.5. Electronic Waste Recycling > Article: 5. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 42474.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, this chapter and all regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter may be enforced by DTSC pursuant to Chapter 6.5 (commencing with Section 25100) of Division 20 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 370, Sec. 21. (SB 1215) Effective January 1, 2023. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Sections 42485 and 42486.)
