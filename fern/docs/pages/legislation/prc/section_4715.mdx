# PRC § 4715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 5. Forest Insect and Plant Disease Control

**Citation:** PRC § 4715

---

## Text

The department, in accordance with policy established by the board, may enter into agreements with any owner and with any agency of government, including the federal government, for the purpose of controlling or eradicating forest insects or plant diseases damaging or threatening destruction to timber or forest growth, and it may make expenditures for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 408, Sec. 1.)
