# PRC § 25950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 11. Gas Appliances > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25950

---

## Text

“Gas appliance” means any new residential-type furnace, air conditioner, heater, refrigerator, stove, range, dishwasher, dryer, decorative fireplace log, or other similar device, except a water heater, which uses a gaseous fuel for operation and is automatically ignited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1411.)
