# PRC § 49000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Garbage Disposal Districts > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 49000

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this chapter, means a district formed pursuant to this chapter or pursuant to any law which it supersedes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
