# PRC § 35174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Funding

**Citation:** PRC § 35174

---

## Text

The authority may incur indebtedness to finance capital improvements pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 43600) of Division 4 of Title 4 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
