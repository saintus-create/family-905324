# PRC § 42019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Market Development Programs > Article: 3. Market Development Zone Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42019

---

## Text

In evaluating an application for the designation of a recycling market development zone, the department shall consider the amount of landfill capacity remaining in the jurisdiction where the zone would be located.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 258, Sec. 34. (SB 155) Effective September 23, 2021.)
