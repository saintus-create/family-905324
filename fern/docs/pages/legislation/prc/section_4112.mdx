# PRC § 4112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 4112

---

## Text

The department shall divide the state into a suitable and convenient number of administrative districts and shall appoint a supervising forest officer for each district.
