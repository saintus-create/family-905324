# PRC § 26570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Nature and Powers of the District > Article: 1. Nature of the District

**Citation:** PRC § 26570

---

## Text

A district is a political subdivision of the state. A district is not an agency or instrumentality of a local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
