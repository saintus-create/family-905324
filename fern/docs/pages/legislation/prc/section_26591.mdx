# PRC § 26591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Finances > Article: 2. Financial Assistance

**Citation:** PRC § 26591

---

## Text

A district may accept financial or other assistance from any public or private source and may expend any funds so accepted for any of the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
