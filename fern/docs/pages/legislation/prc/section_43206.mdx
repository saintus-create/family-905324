# PRC § 43206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 1. Local Enforcement Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 43206

## Text

A designation made pursuant to this article may be withdrawn in the same manner in which it was made.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
