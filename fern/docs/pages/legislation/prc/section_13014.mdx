# PRC § 13014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13014

---

## Text

“Board of supervisors” and “board” means the board of supervisors of the county.
