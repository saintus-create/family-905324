# PRC § 30315.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 2. Qualifications and Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 30315.1

---

## Text

Adoption of findings for any action taken by the commission requires a majority vote of the members from the prevailing side present at the meeting of the commission, with at least three of the prevailing members present and voting.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 590. Effective January 1, 2007.)
