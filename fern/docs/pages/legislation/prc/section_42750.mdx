# PRC § 42750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 15. Newsprint > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42750

---

## Text

“Consumer of newsprint” means a person who uses newsprint in a commercial printing operation or in a commercial publishing operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 23. Effective March 30, 1990.)
