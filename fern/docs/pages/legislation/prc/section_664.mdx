# PRC § 664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of Conservation > Article: 2. State Mining and Geology Board

**Citation:** PRC § 664

---

## Text

Each member of the board shall hold office for four years. Vacancies shall be immediately filled by the Governor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 257, Sec. 2.)
