# PRC § 8002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 10. State Maps and Surveys

**Citation:** PRC § 8002

---

## Text

The state base map shall consist of complementary planimetric, mosaic, and topographic maps prepared in units of convenient size and scale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1581.)
