# PRC § 25200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 25200

---

## Text

There is in the Resources Agency the State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission, consisting of five members appointed by the Governor subject to Section 25204.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
