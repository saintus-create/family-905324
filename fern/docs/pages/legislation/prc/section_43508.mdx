# PRC § 43508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 3. Closure Plans

**Citation:** PRC § 43508

---

## Text

The board or the enforcement agency may recover any costs incurred in meeting the requirements of this article by charging a fee pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 41900) of Part 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1293, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1993.)
