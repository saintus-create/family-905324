# PRC § 31010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31010

---

## Text

“Local public agency” includes, but is not limited to, a city, county, city and county, district, association of governments, or joint powers agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
