# PRC § 41794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Planning Requirements > Article: 2. Board Review

**Citation:** PRC § 41794

---

## Text

Any city may submit its city source reduction and recycling element or nondisposal facility element to the board for review before the dates in the schedule in Section 41791.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1169, Sec. 11. Effective October 11, 1993.)
