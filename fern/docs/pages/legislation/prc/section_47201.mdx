# PRC § 47201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Household Hazardous Substance Information and Collection > Article: 3.5. Household Hazardous Waste Grants

**Citation:** PRC § 47201

---

## Text

The board shall adopt regulations for implementation of this article, including, but not limited to, criteria for selecting grant recipients.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 37. Effective October 1, 1993.)
