# PRC § 5061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 5. Lease Contracts for Park and Recreational Areas

**Citation:** PRC § 5061

---

## Text

The term of such a lease shall not exceed twenty years nor be less than two years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
