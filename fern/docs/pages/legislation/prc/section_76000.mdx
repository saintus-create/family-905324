# PRC § 76000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. FLUORINATED REFRIGERANTS

**Citation:** PRC § 76000

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, “GWP” means global warming potential.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 375, Sec. 2. (SB 1013) Effective January 1, 2019.)
