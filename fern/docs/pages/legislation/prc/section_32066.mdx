# PRC § 32066

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. Funds: Security for Payment of Bonds: Accounts: Deposits: Investments: Interest and Other Increments

**Citation:** PRC § 32066

---

## Text

The Treasurer is the elected representative of the state to approve the issuance of bonds issued by or on behalf of the state pursuant to this chapter to the extent this approval is required by federal tax law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
