# PRC § 9201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Initial Board of Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 9201

---

## Text

The initial board of directors of a district formed on or after January 1, 1992, shall be determined pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 831, Sec. 7.)
