# PRC § 3708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3708

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, firm, association, corporation, or any other group or combination acting as a unit.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1483.)
