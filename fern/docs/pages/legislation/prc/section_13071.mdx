# PRC § 13071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 13071

---

## Text

The district may hold, use, enjoy, lease or dispose of any of its property.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 302.)
