# PRC § 26502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 26502

---

## Text

“Bonds” means bonds, notes, or other evidence of indebtedness issued by a district pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
