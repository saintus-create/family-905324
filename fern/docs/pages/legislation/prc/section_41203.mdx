# PRC § 41203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. City Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 5. Composting Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41203

---

## Text

The city composting component shall describe any new facilities, and expansion of existing facilities, which will be needed to implement the composting component.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
