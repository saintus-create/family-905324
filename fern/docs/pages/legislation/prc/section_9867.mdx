# PRC § 9867

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** PRC § 9867

---

## Text

The directors may provide for the maintenance and operation of the works of an improvement district from the funds of the resource conservation district in lieu of levying further improvement district assessments for such purposes.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
