# PRC § 14519.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14519.5

---

## Text

“Recycler” means a recycling center, dropoff or collection program, or curbside program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 815, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2000.)
