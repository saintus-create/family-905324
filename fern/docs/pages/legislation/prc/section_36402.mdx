# PRC § 36402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5. Responsibilities and Duties of the Task Force

**Citation:** PRC § 36402

---

## Text

The task force shall provide opportunity for public review and comment following release of a draft scoping document and also following release of a draft report and plan. Public comments shall be submitted with the report and plan to the Governor and the Legislature.
