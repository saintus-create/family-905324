# PRC § 10227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 10227

---

## Text

No local government shall, in any way, limit development on any land solely because of the land’s proximity to property that is protected by an agricultural conservation easement that is subject to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 931, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
