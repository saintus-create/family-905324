# PRC § 14515.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14515.1

---

## Text

“Out-of-state container” means a used beverage container or used beverage container component that is not subject to Section 14560, and that is brought into this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 731, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
