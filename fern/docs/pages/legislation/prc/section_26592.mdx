# PRC § 26592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Finances > Article: 2. Financial Assistance

**Citation:** PRC § 26592

---

## Text

Contributions by a local agency, the state, or any instrumentality or political subdivision thereof, are hereby declared to be for a public purpose.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
