# PRC § 9635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 4. Transferring Lands From One District to Another

**Citation:** PRC § 9635

---

## Text

One district may transfer land within its boundaries to a district contiguous thereto in accordance with the provisions of the District Reorganization Act of 1965, Division 1 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
