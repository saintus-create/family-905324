# PRC § 9152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Lands Included

**Citation:** PRC § 9152

---

## Text

The lands included in a district shall be those that further the purpose of this chapter, including, but not limited to, those purposes specified in Sections 9001 and 9151.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 585, Sec. 7. (AB 1902) Effective January 1, 2023.)
