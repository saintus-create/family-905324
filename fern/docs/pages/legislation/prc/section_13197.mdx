# PRC § 13197

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Alternative Depositary of District Money

**Citation:** PRC § 13197

---

## Text

The finance officer shall make annual or earlier written reports to the board, as it shall determine, as to the receipts and disbursements and balances in the several accounts under his control. The report shall be signed by him and filed with the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
