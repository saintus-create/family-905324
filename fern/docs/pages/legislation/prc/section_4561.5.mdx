# PRC § 4561.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 5. Resource Conservation Standards

**Citation:** PRC § 4561.5

---

## Text

The board may from time to time, after a public hearing, amend permanent stocking standards applicable to commercial timberland where the growing timber does not meet the acceptable stocking standards as enumerated in Section 4561.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 383, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2006.)
