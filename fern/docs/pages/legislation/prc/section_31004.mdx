# PRC § 31004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31004

---

## Text

“Commission” means the California Coastal Commission established under Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 30300) of Division 20.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1441.)
