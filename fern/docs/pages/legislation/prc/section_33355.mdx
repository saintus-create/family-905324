# PRC § 33355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 33355

---

## Text

The Sierra Nevada Conservancy Fund is hereby created in the State Treasury. Moneys in the fund shall be available, upon appropriation by the Legislature, only for the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
