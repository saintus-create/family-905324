# PRC § 3358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 6. Appeals and Review

**Citation:** PRC § 3358

---

## Text

Witnesses shall be entitled to receive the fees and mileage fixed by law in civil causes, payable from the Oil, Gas, and Geothermal Administrative Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 240, Sec. 15. Effective August 13, 2003.)
