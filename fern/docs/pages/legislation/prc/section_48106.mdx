# PRC § 48106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Farm and Ranch Solid Waste Cleanup and Abatement Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 48106

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is intended to relieve any party who is responsible for the generation or illegal deposition of the solid waste from liability for removal costs if the party can be identified. Farm or ranch property owners whose property is the subject of solid waste cleanup or abatement under this chapter and who are not responsible for the generation or deposition of the solid waste shall not be subject to any cost recovery action for cleanup or abatement costs borne by public entities or Native American tribes or the board under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 628, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
