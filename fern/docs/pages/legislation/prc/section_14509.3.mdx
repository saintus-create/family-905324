# PRC § 14509.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14509.3

---

## Text

“Cullet” means scrap glass that is derived from postfilled food, drink, or beverage container glass produced or imported for sale in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 290. Effective January 1, 2005.)
