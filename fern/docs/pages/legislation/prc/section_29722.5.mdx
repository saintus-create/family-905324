# PRC § 29722.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 29722.5

---

## Text

“Delta Plan” means the plan adopted by the Delta Stewardship Council pursuant to Section 85300 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 3. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
