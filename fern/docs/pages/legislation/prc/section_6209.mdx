# PRC § 6209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6209

---

## Text

When certificates of purchase or patents are issued, the fact shall be noted on the plats.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
