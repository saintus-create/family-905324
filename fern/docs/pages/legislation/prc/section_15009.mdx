# PRC § 15009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.2. PRODUCTS CONTAINING TOXIC METALS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 15009

---

## Text

“Mercuric oxide battery” means a battery containing mercuric oxide electrodes, except that mercuric oxide button cells are excluded from this definition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 817, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
