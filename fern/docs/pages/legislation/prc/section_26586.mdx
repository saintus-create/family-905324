# PRC § 26586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Nature and Powers of the District > Article: 4. Officers

**Citation:** PRC § 26586

---

## Text

The board of directors may appoint other officers of the district and delegate thereto such powers of the district as may be appropriate in the circumstances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
