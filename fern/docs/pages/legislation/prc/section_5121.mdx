# PRC § 5121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 2. County Museums, and Historical Records and Property

**Citation:** PRC § 5121

---

## Text

The board may acquire and manage real and personal property necessary properly to house and care for the exhibits and materials placed in the museum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
