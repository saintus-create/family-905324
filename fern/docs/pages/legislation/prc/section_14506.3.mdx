# PRC § 14506.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14506.3

---

## Text

“Bimetal container” means a beverage container which consists of one or more metals and which is composed primarily of steel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1266, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1992.)
