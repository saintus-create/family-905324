# PRC § 33214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. SANTA MONICA MOUNTAINS CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the Santa Monica Mountains Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 33214

---

## Text

The conservancy and any city, county, or recreation and park district, in undertaking any project pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 33204, shall be subject to the provisions of Division 24 (commencing with Section 33000) of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1087.)
