# PRC § 25125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25125

---

## Text

“Major natural gas producer” means any person who produces natural gas in amounts determined by the commission as having a major effect on energy supplies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1195.)
