# PRC § 26550.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. District Formation > Article: 3. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** PRC § 26550.5

---

## Text

Proceedings for the formation of a district may be initiated by either of the following methods:

(a) A petition signed by owners of not less than 10 percent of the real property to be included within the proposed district.

(b) By resolution of the legislative body.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
