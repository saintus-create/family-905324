# PRC § 9205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Initial Board of Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 9205

---

## Text

In the case of a district which includes only incorporated territory within a single city, the district board may be elected or appointed by the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 831, Sec. 7.)
