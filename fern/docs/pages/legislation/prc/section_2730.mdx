# PRC § 2730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 2730

---

## Text

“Mining waste” includes the residual of soil, rock, mineral, liquid, vegetation, equipment, machines, tools, or other materials or property directly resulting from, or displaced by, surface mining operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1131.)
