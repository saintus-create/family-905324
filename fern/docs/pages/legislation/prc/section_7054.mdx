# PRC § 7054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 5. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases by Public Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 7054

---

## Text

An order to lease made by the governing body shall authorize and direct the execution and delivery by the chair or other presiding officer of a lease to the lessee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 213, Sec. 13. (AB 2768) Effective January 1, 2011.)
