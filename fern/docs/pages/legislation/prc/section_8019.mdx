# PRC § 8019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 10. State Maps and Surveys

**Citation:** PRC § 8019

---

## Text

The department shall prepare and distribute to public officials, uniform specifications and regulations for aerial photographic mapping procedure and for the preparation of planimetric or topographic maps by photogrammetric methods.
