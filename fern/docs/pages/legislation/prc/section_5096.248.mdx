# PRC § 5096.248

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.691. California Park and Recreational Facilities Act of 1984 > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.248

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, acquisition may include gifts, purchases, leases, easements, the exercise of eminent domain if expressly authorized, the transfer or exchange of property for other property of like value, and purchases of development rights and other interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 18 at the June 5, 1984, election.)
