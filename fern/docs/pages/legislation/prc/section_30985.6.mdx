# PRC § 30985.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20.6.9. SEA LEVEL RISE PLANNING AND ADAPTATION

**Citation:** PRC § 30985.6

---

## Text

The operation of this division is contingent upon an appropriation for its purposes by the Legislature in the annual Budget Act or another statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 384, Sec. 2. (SB 272) Effective January 1, 2024.)
