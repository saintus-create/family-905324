# PRC § 5001.96

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 1. State Park System

**Citation:** PRC § 5001.96

---

## Text

Attendance at state park system units shall be held within limits established by carrying capacity determined in accordance with Section 5019.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1722.)
