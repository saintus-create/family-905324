# PRC § 30328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 2.5. Fairness and Due Process

**Citation:** PRC § 30328

---

## Text

If a violation of this article occurs and a commission decision may have been affected by the violation, an aggrieved person, as described in Section 30801, may seek a writ of mandate from a court requiring the commission to revoke its action and rehear the matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1114, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
