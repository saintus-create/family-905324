# PRC § 21159.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 4.5. Streamlined Environmental Review > Article: 6. Special Review of Housing Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 21159.27

---

## Text

A project may not be divided into smaller projects to qualify for one or more exemptions pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1039, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2003.)
