# PRC § 3944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. Mining Partnerships

**Citation:** PRC § 3944

---

## Text

The mining-ground owned and worked by partners in mining, whether purchased with partnership funds or not, is partnership property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
