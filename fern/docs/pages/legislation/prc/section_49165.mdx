# PRC § 49165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49165

---

## Text

A resolution calling the election shall be published once a week for three successive weeks in a newspaper having a general circulation in the district and designated by the district board. No other notice of the election is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
