# PRC § 33837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.6. SALTON SEA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 33837

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall limit or alter any party’s, including the state’s, responsibility under existing law or any party’s authority to carry out any responsibility under existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 771, Sec. 1. (SB 583) Effective January 1, 2025.)
