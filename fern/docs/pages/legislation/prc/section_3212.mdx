# PRC § 3212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 4. Regulation of Operations

**Citation:** PRC § 3212

---

## Text

The core record shall show the depth, character, and fluid content of cores obtained, so far as determined.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
