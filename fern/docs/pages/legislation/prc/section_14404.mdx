# PRC § 14404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA CONSERVATION CORPS > Chapter: 3.5. Nonresidential Program

**Citation:** PRC § 14404

---

## Text

The corps shall assist corpsmembers who desire to return to school to develop plans to accomplish this goal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1710, Sec. 5.)
