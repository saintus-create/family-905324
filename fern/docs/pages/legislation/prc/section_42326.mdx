# PRC § 42326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5.5. Plastic Packaging Containers > Article: 3. Penalties, Regulations, and Report

**Citation:** PRC § 42326

---

## Text

In developing the regulations required by Section 42325, the board shall consult with representatives of the manufacturers affected by this chapter, with representatives of environmental organizations, and other interested parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 561, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2005.)
