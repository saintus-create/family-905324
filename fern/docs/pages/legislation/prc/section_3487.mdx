# PRC § 3487

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Grants for Used Oil Collection Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 3487

---

## Text

The board shall evaluate each grant application for its potential to satisfy the requirements of this chapter and shall award the grant based on the evaluation. The board shall notify the applicant of the approval or denial of the grant application on or before January 1, 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3787 by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1091, Sec. 127.)
