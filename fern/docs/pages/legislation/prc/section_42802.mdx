# PRC § 42802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42802

---

## Text

“Fund” means the California Tire Recycling Management Fund created by subdivision (a) of Section 42885.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 24. Effective March 30, 1990.)
