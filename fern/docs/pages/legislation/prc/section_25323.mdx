# PRC § 25323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 4. Integrated Energy Policy Reporting

**Citation:** PRC § 25323

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall authorize the commission in the performance of its analytical, planning, siting, or certification responsibilities to mandate a specified supply plan for any utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 568, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
