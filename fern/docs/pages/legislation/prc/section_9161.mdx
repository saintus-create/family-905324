# PRC § 9161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 2. Initiation

**Citation:** PRC § 9161

---

## Text

(a) A new district may be formed pursuant to this chapter.

(b) A proposal to form a district may be made by a petition of registered voters or by the adoption of a resolution of application.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 831, Sec. 3.)
