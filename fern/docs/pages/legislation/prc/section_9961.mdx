# PRC § 9961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 12. Suisun Resource Conservation District

**Citation:** PRC § 9961

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided in this chapter, the organization, powers, and functions of the district shall be governed by the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
