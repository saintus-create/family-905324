# PRC § 5019.90

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 1.8. California State Park Stewardship Act of 2012

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 533, Sec. 1. (AB 1589) Effective January 1, 2013.)
