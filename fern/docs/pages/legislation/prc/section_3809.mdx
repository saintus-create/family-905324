# PRC § 3809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 6. Disposition of Geothermal Revenues > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3809

---

## Text

“Private entity” means any individual or organization engaged in the exploration and development of geothermal energy for profit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 644, Sec. 1.)
