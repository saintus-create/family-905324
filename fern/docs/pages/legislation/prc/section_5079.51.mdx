# PRC § 5079.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.1.5. California Heritage Fund > Article: 6. Historical Resource Management Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 5079.51

---

## Text

After the approval of a proposed historical resource management project, the office may expend funds to prepare a plan for the protection and enhancement of the historical resource.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 749, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
