# PRC § 21064.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21064.8

---

## Text

“Oil and gas infrastructure” means a facility used for the production, processing, transmission, storage, or distribution of petroleum or natural gas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 24, Sec. 4. (SB 131) Effective June 30, 2025.)
