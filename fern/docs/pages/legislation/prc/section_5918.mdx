# PRC § 5918

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5.8. CALIFORNIA WILDLIFE, COASTAL, AND PARK LAND CONSERVATION ACT > Chapter: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5918

---

## Text

Every expenditure pursuant to this division shall comply with the California Environmental Quality Act (Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 70.)
