# PRC § 14510.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Department” means the Division of Recycling in the Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery.
