# PRC § 26569.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Emergency Formation > Article: 3. Nature of the District

**Citation:** PRC § 26569.7

---

## Text

This chapter is applicable only in a city or county which has adopted an ordinance providing that the chapter is applicable in its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 555, Sec. 2. Effective July 18, 1984.)
