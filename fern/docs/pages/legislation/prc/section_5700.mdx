# PRC § 5700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.7. Community Parklands Act of 1986 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5700

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Community Parklands Act of 1986.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 43 at the June 3, 1986, election. Operative July 1, 1986, by Stats. 1986, Ch. 5, Sec. 2.)
