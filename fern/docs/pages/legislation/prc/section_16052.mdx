# PRC § 16052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.4. GREASE TRAP AND GREASE INTERCEPTOR WASTE

**Citation:** PRC § 16052

---

## Text

A violation of this division may only be enforced against a grease waste hauling company and shall not be enforced against an employee of the grease waste hauler.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 186, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
