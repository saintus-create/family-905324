# PRC § 4525.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4525.7

---

## Text

“Slash” means branches or limbs less than four inches in diameter, and bark and split products debris left on the ground as a result of timber operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 880.)
