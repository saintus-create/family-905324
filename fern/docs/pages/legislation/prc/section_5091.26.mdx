# PRC § 5091.26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.27. California Sno-Park Permit Program > Article: 5. Revenues

**Citation:** PRC § 5091.26

---

## Text

Eminent domain shall not be exercised to acquire any interest in property for a designated parking area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1560, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1984.)
