# PRC § 5555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5555

---

## Text

The directors of the district, before assuming the duties of their office, shall take and subscribe an official oath, and file it in the office of the board. The official oath shall be in the form provided by law for official oaths of county officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
