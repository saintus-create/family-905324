# PRC § 49121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 3. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 49121

---

## Text

Any governing body authorized by Section 49120 to appoint a member to the district board may make the appointment from its own members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
