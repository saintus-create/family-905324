# PRC § 14541.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 14541.5

---

## Text

Any certification or registration granted by the department is a privilege and not a vested right or interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 731, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
