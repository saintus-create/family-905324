# PRC § 32578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.7. BALDWIN HILLS AND URBAN WATERSHEDS CONSERVANCY ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32578

---

## Text

The conservancy shall have, and may exercise, all rights and powers, expressed or implied, necessary to carry out the purposes of this division, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
