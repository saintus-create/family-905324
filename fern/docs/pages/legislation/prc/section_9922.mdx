# PRC § 9922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 8. Dissolution of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 9922

---

## Text

A hearing on dissolution shall be had in the same manner and after the same notice as is required for the formation of an improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
