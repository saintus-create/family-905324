# PRC § 48003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Finances > Article: 2. Management of the Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 48003

---

## Text

The state board may not spend more than 12 percent of the total revenues deposited, or anticipated to be deposited, in the account during a fiscal year for the administration of this chapter during that fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 742, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2004.)
