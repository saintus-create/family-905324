# PRC § 41793

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Planning Requirements > Article: 2. Board Review

**Citation:** PRC § 41793

---

## Text

Each county or city shall hold at least one public hearing before approving its source reduction and recycling element, household hazardous waste element, and the countywide integrated waste management plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1406, Sec. 9.)
