# PRC § 13016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13016

---

## Text

“Land” means land in the district and does not include improvements or personal or utility property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
