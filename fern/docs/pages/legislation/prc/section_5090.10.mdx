# PRC § 5090.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.10

---

## Text

“Conservation” and “conserve” mean activities, practices, and programs that protect and sustain soils, plants, wildlife, habitats, and cultural resources in accordance with the standards adopted pursuant to Section 5090.35.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 459, Sec. 1. (SB 249) Effective January 1, 2018.)
