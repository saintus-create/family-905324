# PRC § 5400.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Preservation of Public Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5400.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “public park” includes only a park operated by a public agency.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1642.)
