# PRC § 30223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 3. Coastal Resources Planning and Management Policies > Article: 3. Recreation

**Citation:** PRC § 30223

---

## Text

Upland areas necessary to support coastal recreational uses shall be reserved for such uses, where feasible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
