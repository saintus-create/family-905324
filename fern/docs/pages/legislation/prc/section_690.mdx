# PRC § 690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of Conservation > Article: 3. Geologic Energy Management Division

**Citation:** PRC § 690

---

## Text

The Geologic Energy Management Division shall be in charge of a chief, known as the State Oil and Gas Supervisor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 771, Sec. 7. (AB 1057) Effective January 1, 2020.)
