# PRC § 3950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. Mining Partnerships

**Citation:** PRC § 3950

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any operations for the extraction of oil, gas, and other hydrocarbons.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
