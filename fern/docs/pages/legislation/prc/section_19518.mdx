# PRC § 19518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.9. FIBERGLASS RECYCLED CONTENT ACT OF 1991 > Chapter: 3. Cullet Specifications for Fiberglass Manufacturing

**Citation:** PRC § 19518

---

## Text

The department may change minimum quality specifications based upon the fiberglass industries’ ability to use cullet with higher levels of contamination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 706, Sec. 1.)
