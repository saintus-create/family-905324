# PRC § 45042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Judicial Review

**Citation:** PRC § 45042

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, Section 1094.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure shall govern proceedings pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 952, Sec. 35. Effective October 16, 1995.)
