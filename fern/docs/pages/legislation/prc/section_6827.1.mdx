# PRC § 6827.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 2. Provisions Relating to Oil and Gas Leases Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6827.1

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this chapter or any other law shall prevent or prohibit two or more persons who are individually eligible to hold a lease under this chapter from making a joint bid for any lease or leases offered under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1724.)
