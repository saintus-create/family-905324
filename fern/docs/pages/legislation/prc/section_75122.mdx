# PRC § 75122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 13. Strategic Growth Council and Climate Change Reduction

**Citation:** PRC § 75122

---

## Text

The members of the council shall elect a chair of the council every two years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 729, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008. Note: Pursuant to Stats. 2008, Ch. 729, Sec. 5, the provisions of Division 43 added on Nov. 7, 2006, by initiative Prop. 84 (Sections 75001 to 75090) prevail over any conflicting provision of Ch. 729.)
