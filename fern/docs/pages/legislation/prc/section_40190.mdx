# PRC § 40190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40190

---

## Text

“Segregated from other waste material” means any of the following:

(a) The placement of recyclable materials in separate containers.

(b) The binding of recyclable material separately from the other waste material.

(c) The physical separation of recyclable material from other waste material.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
