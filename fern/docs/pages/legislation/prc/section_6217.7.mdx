# PRC § 6217.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6217.7

---

## Text

All net revenues, moneys, and remittances from the sale of school lands and lieu lands shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the School Land Bank Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 879, Sec. 2.)
