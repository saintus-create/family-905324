# PRC § 42363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5.9. Plastic Microbeads Nuisance Prevention Law

**Citation:** PRC § 42363

---

## Text

Section 42362 shall not apply to a person that sells or offers for promotional purposes a personal care product containing plastic microbeads in an amount less than 1 part per million (ppm) by weight.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 594, Sec. 1. (AB 888) Effective January 1, 2016.)
