# PRC § 30222.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 3. Coastal Resources Planning and Management Policies > Article: 3. Recreation

**Citation:** PRC § 30222.5

---

## Text

Oceanfront land that is suitable for coastal dependent aquaculture shall be protected for that use, and proposals for aquaculture facilities located on those sites shall be given priority, except over other coastal dependent developments or uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 589. Effective January 1, 2007.)
