# PRC § 13102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13102

---

## Text

The copy of the resolution published shall be accompanied by a notice subscribed by the clerk that:

(a) The hearing referred to in the resolution will be had at the time and place specified in the resolution.

(b) At that time and place any person interested, including all persons owning property in the district, will be heard upon the question stated in the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
