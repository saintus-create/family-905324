# PRC § 41353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. County Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 3. Source Reduction Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41353

---

## Text

The county source reduction component shall describe new facilities, and of expansion of existing facilities, which will be needed to implement the source reduction component.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 145, Sec. 33. Effective June 19, 1990.)
