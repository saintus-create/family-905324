# PRC § 32576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.7. BALDWIN HILLS AND URBAN WATERSHEDS CONSERVANCY ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32576

---

## Text

The conservancy may recruit and coordinate volunteers and experts to assist with conservancy projects and the maintenance of conservancy lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
