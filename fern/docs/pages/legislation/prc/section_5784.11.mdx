# PRC § 5784.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 5. Boards of Directors and Officers

**Citation:** PRC § 5784.11

---

## Text

A board of directors shall meet at least once every three months. Meetings of the board of directors are subject to the provisions of the Ralph M. Brown Act, Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
