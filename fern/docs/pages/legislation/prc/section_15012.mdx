# PRC § 15012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.2. PRODUCTS CONTAINING TOXIC METALS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 15012

---

## Text

“Remanufactured product” means a rechargeable consumer product manufactured prior to July 1, 1994, which has been returned to the manufacturer for refurbishment and either returned to the consumer or resold.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 816, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
