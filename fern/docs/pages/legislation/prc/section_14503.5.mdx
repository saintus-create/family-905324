# PRC § 14503.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14503.5

---

## Text

“Average monthly volume” means the average number of empty beverage containers per month received by a certified recycling center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1342, Sec. 1.)
