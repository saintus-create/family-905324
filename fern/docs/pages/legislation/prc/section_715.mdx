# PRC § 715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 715

---

## Text

The Department of Forestry and Fire Protection, in cooperation with the Office of Emergency Services, shall develop a program to certify active duty military pilots to engage in firefighting in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 473. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
