# PRC § 3490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Grants for Used Oil Collection Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 3490

---

## Text

Storage containers, such as drums and tanks, used to store used oil shall be in good condition and shall meet any applicable design and construction standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1657, Sec. 2.)
