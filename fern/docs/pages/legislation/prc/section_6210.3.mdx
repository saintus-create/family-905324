# PRC § 6210.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6210.3

---

## Text

The commission may grant easements and rights-of-way to the Department of Transportation to or over any of the public lands of the state for the purposes of rights-of-way for highways and for use in protecting highways from damage or destruction by natural forces.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1205.)
