# PRC § 29736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 3. Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 29736

---

## Text

The appointed members of the commission shall serve at the pleasure of their appointing entities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 12. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
