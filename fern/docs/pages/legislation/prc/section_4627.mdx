# PRC § 4627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 9. Conversion

**Citation:** PRC § 4627

---

## Text

The board may, by regulation, delegate its authority and responsibilities under this article to the director if it determines that the director can more efficiently exercise such authority and carry out such responsibilities.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
