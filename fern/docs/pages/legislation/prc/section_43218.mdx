# PRC § 43218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 1. Local Enforcement Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 43218

---

## Text

Each enforcement agency shall inspect each solid waste facility within its jurisdiction at least one time each month and shall file, within 30 days of the inspection, a written report in a format prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
