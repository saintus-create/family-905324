# PRC § 31180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4.6. Santa Ana River Conservancy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 31180

---

## Text

The conservancy shall include information about the program in its report to the Governor and Legislature pursuant to Section 31108.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 562, Sec. 1. (SB 1390) Effective January 1, 2015.)
