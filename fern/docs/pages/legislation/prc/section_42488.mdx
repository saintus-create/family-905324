# PRC § 42488

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.6. Right to Repair Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Right to Repair Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 704, Sec. 1. (SB 244) Effective January 1, 2024.)
