# PRC § 5097.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.7. Archaeological, Paleontological, and Historical Sites

**Citation:** PRC § 5097.6

---

## Text

Expenditures to carry out the purposes of this chapter shall be made only pursuant to legislative appropriation for these purposes or by contract with other state agencies.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1136.)
