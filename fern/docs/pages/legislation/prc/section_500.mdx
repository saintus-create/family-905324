# PRC § 500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 500

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “department” means the Department of Parks and Recreation and “director” means the Director of Parks and Recreation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
