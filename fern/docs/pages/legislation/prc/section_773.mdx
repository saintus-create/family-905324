# PRC § 773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 3. Professional Foresters

**Citation:** PRC § 773

---

## Text

Licenses and specialty certificates issued pursuant to this article shall be valid for two years and shall expire on July 1 of each alternating year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 784, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1994.)
