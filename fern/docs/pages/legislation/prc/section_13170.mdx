# PRC § 13170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 10. Funds

**Citation:** PRC § 13170

---

## Text

In a fund called the “Resort Improvement District General Fund,” the treasurer shall place and keep the money levied by the board for that fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
