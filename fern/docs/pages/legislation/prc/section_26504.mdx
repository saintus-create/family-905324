# PRC § 26504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 26504

---

## Text

“Clerk”, where not otherwise modified, means the clerk of the district.
