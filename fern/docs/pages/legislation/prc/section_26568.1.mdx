# PRC § 26568.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Emergency Formation > Article: 1. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** PRC § 26568.1

---

## Text

Proceedings for the formation of a district for any of the work specified in Section 26525 may be initiated by a petition signed by two-thirds of the property owners of the real property to be included within the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 555, Sec. 2. Effective July 18, 1984.)
