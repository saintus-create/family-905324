# PRC § 4209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 12. Community Wildfire Preparedness and Mitigation

**Citation:** PRC § 4209

---

## Text

There is within the Office of the State Fire Marshal a Deputy Director of Community Wildfire Preparedness and Mitigation who shall be responsible for fire preparedness and mitigation missions of the Department of Forestry and Fire Protection, as provided in Section 4209.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 225, Sec. 17. (AB 9) Effective January 1, 2022.)
