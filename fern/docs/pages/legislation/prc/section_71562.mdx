# PRC § 71562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 37. CALIFORNIA ENDOWMENT FOR MARINE PRESERVATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Transactions and Audits

**Citation:** PRC § 71562

---

## Text

Funds held by the endowment shall revert to the state or to another public agency or nonprofit organization approved by the state if the endowment does any of the following:

(a) Ceases operations.

(b) Is dissolved.

(c) Becomes bankrupt or insolvent.

(d) Fails to perform its fiduciary duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 687, Sec. 2. (AB 2503) Effective January 1, 2011.)
