# PRC § 13116.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13116.5

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 68, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2007.)
