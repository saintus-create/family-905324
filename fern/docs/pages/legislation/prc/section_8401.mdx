# PRC § 8401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Conveyances by Counties for National Park Purposes

**Citation:** PRC § 8401

---

## Text

The boards of supervisors of the several counties may grant, transfer and convey, without consideration, any real property or interest therein now owned or hereafter acquired by any county, to the United States to be used for National park purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
