# PRC § 26579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Nature and Powers of the District > Article: 2. Powers of a District

**Citation:** PRC § 26579

---

## Text

The district may enter into contracts and agreements with the United States, any state or local unit of government, public agency, including any other geologic hazard abatement district or public district, private organization, or any person in furtherance of the purposes of the division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
