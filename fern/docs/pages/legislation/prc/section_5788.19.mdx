# PRC § 5788.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 9. Finance

**Citation:** PRC § 5788.19

---

## Text

A local agency may loan any of its available funds to a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
