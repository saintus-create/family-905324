# PRC § 3477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3477

---

## Text

“Administrative costs” means those costs directly associated with regulation of the implementation of a used oil collection project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1657, Sec. 2.)
