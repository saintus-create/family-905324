# PRC § 29109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. SUISUN MARSH PRESERVATION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 29109

---

## Text

“Local government” means the County of Solano and the Cities of Suisun City, Fairfield, and Benicia.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
