# PRC § 3407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 7. Assessment and Collection of Charges

**Citation:** PRC § 3407

---

## Text

The department may, for good cause shown, by order entered upon its records, extend for not exceeding thirty days, the time for filing any report required by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
