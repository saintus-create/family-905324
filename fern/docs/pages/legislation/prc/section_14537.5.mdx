# PRC § 14537.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 14537.5

---

## Text

The department shall provide on its internet website information that enables consumers to identify the geographic location of all points of redemption for beverage containers, to be updated at least once per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 574, Sec. 19. (AB 211) Effective September 27, 2022.)
