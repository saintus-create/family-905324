# PRC § 31106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the State Coastal Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 31106

---

## Text

The State Public Works Board may, pursuant to Section 31105, use the power of eminent domain for the purposes specified in this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1441.)
