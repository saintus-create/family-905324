# PRC § 41821.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Approval of Local Planning > Article: 3. Other Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 41821.9

---

## Text

To assist market development efforts by the department, local agencies, and the private sector, the department shall use existing data resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 41821.6 by Stats. 2015, Ch. 746, Sec. 2. (AB 901) Effective January 1, 2016.)
