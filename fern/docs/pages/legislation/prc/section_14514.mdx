# PRC § 14514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14514

---

## Text

“Managing employee” includes, but is not limited to, any person who manages the operation of a facility or is authorized by the certified operator to sign shipping reports.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 731, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
