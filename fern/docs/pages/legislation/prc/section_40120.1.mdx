# PRC § 40120.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40120.1

---

## Text

“Disposal” or “dispose” has the same meaning as “solid waste disposal” as defined in Section 40192.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 500, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
