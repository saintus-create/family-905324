# PRC § 768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 3. Professional Foresters

**Citation:** PRC § 768

---

## Text

The board shall require an applicant to demonstrate such degree of experience and such general knowledge of the profession of forestry as the board deems necessary for the protection of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
