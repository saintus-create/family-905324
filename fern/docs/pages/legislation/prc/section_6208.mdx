# PRC § 6208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6208

---

## Text

The commission shall also keep plats of such lands, upon which all approved locations and surveys shall be designated by their numbers.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
