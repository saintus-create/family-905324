# PRC § 9870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** PRC § 9870

---

## Text

On behalf of an improvement district, the directors may cooperate and contract with the United States, or with any officer, department, bureau, or agency thereof, to accomplish any of the purposes of the improvement district, or to exercise any of the powers of the directors in relation to such improvement districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
