# PRC § 21080.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.6. General

**Citation:** PRC § 21080.19

---

## Text

This division does not apply to a project for restriping of streets or highways to relieve traffic congestion.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 750, Sec. 1.)
