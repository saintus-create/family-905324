# PRC § 6240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3.4. California Coastal Sanctuary

**Citation:** PRC § 6240

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Coastal Sanctuary Act of 1994.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 970, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
