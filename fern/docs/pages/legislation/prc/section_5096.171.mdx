# PRC § 5096.171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.69. California Parklands Act of 1980 > Article: 5. Coastal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.171

---

## Text

Funds available pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 5096.151 shall be expended pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 250, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 1980, election. Operative December 1, 1980.)
