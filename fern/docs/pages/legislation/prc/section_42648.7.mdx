# PRC § 42648.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 12.7. Large Venue Recycling

**Citation:** PRC § 42648.7

---

## Text

A local agency may charge and collect a fee from an operator of a large venue or large event in order to recover the local agency’s estimated costs incurred in complying with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 879, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
