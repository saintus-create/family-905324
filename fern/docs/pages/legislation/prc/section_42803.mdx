# PRC § 42803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42803

---

## Text

“Local agency” means a county, city, special district, or other local governmental agency which provides or regulates solid waste handling services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 24. Effective March 30, 1990.)
