# PRC § 12231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. CALIFORNIA FOREST LEGACY PROGRAM ACT OF 2007 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 12231

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter grants any new authority to the department to affect local policy or land use decisionmaking.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 657, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
