# PRC § 49600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Burning Garbage > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 49600

---

## Text

No person shall operate in any city or town any crematory for the destruction by fire heat of garbage, ashes, offal, or other refuse matter, except as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
