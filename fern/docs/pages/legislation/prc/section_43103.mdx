# PRC § 43103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1.5. The Solid Waste Disposal Regulatory Reform Act of 1993

**Citation:** PRC § 43103

---

## Text

The board and the state water board shall adopt regulations for the implementation of the changes required by this chapter, and the act adding this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 12. Effective October 1, 1993.)
