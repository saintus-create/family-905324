# PRC § 4465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 7. Burning of Lands > Article: 1. Experimental Program for Wildland Fire Protection and Resources Management

**Citation:** PRC § 4465

---

## Text

The department shall conduct an experimental program of wildland resources management through prescribed burning and other methods in two areas of wildlands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1118.)
