# PRC § 42824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 3. Major Waste Tire Facility Permits

**Citation:** PRC § 42824

---

## Text

On and after September 1, 1994, it is unlawful to direct or transport waste tires to a major waste tire facility or to accept waste tires at a major waste tire facility unless the operator has obtained a major waste tire facility permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1293, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1993.)
