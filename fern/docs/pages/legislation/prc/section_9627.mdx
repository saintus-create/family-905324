# PRC § 9627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 3. Changing Name of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9627

---

## Text

On acknowledgment of the change of name by the Secretary of State, the name of the district shall be considered changed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
