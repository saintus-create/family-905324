# PRC § 30500.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 6. Implementation > Article: 1. Local Coastal Program

**Citation:** PRC § 30500.1

---

## Text

No local coastal program shall be required to include housing policies and programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1007, Sec. 3.)
