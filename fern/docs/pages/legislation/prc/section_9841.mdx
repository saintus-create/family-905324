# PRC § 9841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 2. Assessments

**Citation:** PRC § 9841

---

## Text

The procedure followed in making the supplemental levy shall be substantially the same as for making the original levy, except that no petition is required.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
