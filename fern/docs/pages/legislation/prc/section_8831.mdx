# PRC § 8831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 2. Topographical Mapping

**Citation:** PRC § 8831

---

## Text

It is the policy of the State of California to provide for basic topographic map coverage in aid of development and conservation of the natural and economic resources of the State.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 108.)
