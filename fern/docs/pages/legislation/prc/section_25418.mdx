# PRC § 25418

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.2. Energy Conservation Assistance

**Citation:** PRC § 25418

---

## Text

The Department of Finance, at its discretion, may audit the expenditure of any allocation made pursuant to this chapter or the computation of any payment made pursuant to Section 25415.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1124. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 25421.)
