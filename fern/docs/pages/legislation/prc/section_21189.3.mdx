# PRC § 21189.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 6.5. Jobs and Economic Improvement Through Environmental Leadership Act of 2021

**Citation:** PRC § 21189.3

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect until January 1, 2034, and as of that date is repealed unless a later enacted statute extends or repeals that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 60, Sec. 5. (SB 149) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 6.5, commencing with Section 21178.)
