# PRC § 6106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 2. The State Lands Commission and the Division of State Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6106

---

## Text

The commission may, by resolution, authorize any of its employees or officers to execute any instrument in the name of the State.
