# PRC § 5791.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 12. Zones

**Citation:** PRC § 5791.3

---

## Text

A board of directors may change the boundaries of a zone or dissolve a zone by following the procedures in Sections 5791 and 5791.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
