# PRC § 30315.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 2. Qualifications and Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 30315.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the commission shall make the notice of any public meeting or hearing of the commission available in both English and Spanish, and may also make the notice available in any other language.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 866, Sec. 1. (AB 288) Effective January 1, 2015.)
