# PRC § 30220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 3. Coastal Resources Planning and Management Policies > Article: 3. Recreation

**Citation:** PRC § 30220

---

## Text

Coastal areas suited for water-oriented recreational activities that cannot readily be provided at inland water areas shall be protected for such uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
