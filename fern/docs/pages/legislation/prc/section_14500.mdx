# PRC § 14500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 1. Findings

**Citation:** PRC § 14500

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Beverage Container Recycling and Litter Reduction Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1290, Sec. 2. Effective September 29, 1986.)
