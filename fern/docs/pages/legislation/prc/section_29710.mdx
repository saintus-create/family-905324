# PRC § 29710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** PRC § 29710

---

## Text

The Legislature further finds and declares that agricultural, recreational, and other uses of the delta can best be protected by implementing projects that protect wildlife habitat before conflicts arise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
