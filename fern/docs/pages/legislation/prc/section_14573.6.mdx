# PRC § 14573.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 6. Returns

**Citation:** PRC § 14573.6

---

## Text

No dropoff or collection program shall pay any refund value to the consumer, and a dropoff or collection program is not eligible to receive any sum paid pursuant to Section 14573 or 14573.5 for administrative costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1339, Sec. 37. Effective October 2, 1989.)
