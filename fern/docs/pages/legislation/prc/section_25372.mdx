# PRC § 25372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 4.6. Petroleum Market > Article: 3. Division of Petroleum Market Oversight

**Citation:** PRC § 25372

---

## Text

For purposes of this article and Article 4 (commencing with Section 25373), “division” means the Division of Petroleum Market Oversight.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 10. (SB 2 1x) Effective June 26, 2023.)
