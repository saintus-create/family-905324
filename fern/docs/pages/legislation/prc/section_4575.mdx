# PRC § 4575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 6. Licenses

**Citation:** PRC § 4575

## Text

A license is subject to future legislation and is not transferable.
