# PRC § 710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 710

---

## Text

The director shall have no power to amend or repeal any order, regulation, ruling, or directive of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
