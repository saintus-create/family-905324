# PRC § 6340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Administration and Control of Swamp, Overflowed, Tide, or Submerged Lands, and Structures Thereon > Article: 2.5. Survey and Mapping of Ungranted Tide and Submerged Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6340

---

## Text

The inventory required by this article shall not extend to, or have any bearing upon, the determination of the seaward boundary separating lands belonging to the state and lands belonging to the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 706.)
