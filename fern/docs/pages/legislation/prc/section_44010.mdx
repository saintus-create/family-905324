# PRC § 44010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Permit and Inspection Program > Article: 1. Solid Waste Facility Permits

**Citation:** PRC § 44010

---

## Text

The enforcement agency shall issue the permit only if it finds that the proposed solid waste facilities permit is consistent with the standards adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
