# PRC § 6370.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4.5. Development of State Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6370.1

---

## Text

The Office of Planning and Research shall, for purposes of Section 6370, define “significant environmental values”, which definition shall include, but not be limited to, the criteria developed pursuant to Section 65041 of the Government Code. Such definition shall be submitted to the commission for its approval and adoption at a public hearing. The commission shall submit the adopted definition, including reasons in support of the adopted definition, to the Legislature no later than January 15, 1974.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 688.)
