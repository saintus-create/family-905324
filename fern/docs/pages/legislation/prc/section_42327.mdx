# PRC § 42327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5.5. Plastic Packaging Containers > Article: 3. Penalties, Regulations, and Report

**Citation:** PRC § 42327

---

## Text

The board may expend funds from the Integrated Waste Management Account to implement this chapter, upon appropriation by the Legislature.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 769, Sec. 1.)
