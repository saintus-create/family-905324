# PRC § 3604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3. Spacing of Wells and Community Leases

**Citation:** PRC § 3604

---

## Text

Each day in which the drilling of any well is carried on, or on which it is permitted to produce oil or gas in violation of this chapter is a separate nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1559.)
