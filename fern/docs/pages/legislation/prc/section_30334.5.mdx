# PRC § 30334.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 30334.5

---

## Text

In addition to the authority granted by Section 30334, the commission may apply for and accept grants, appropriations, and contributions in any form.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1331.)
