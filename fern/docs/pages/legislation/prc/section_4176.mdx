# PRC § 4176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 7. Public Nuisances

**Citation:** PRC § 4176

---

## Text

If the director determines, at the conclusion of the hearing, that a public nuisance actually exists upon property owned by the property owner, the director shall order the property owner to abate the public nuisance within a specified time.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 62. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
