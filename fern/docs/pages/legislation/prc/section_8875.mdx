# PRC § 8875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 4. California Geodetic Coordinates

**Citation:** PRC § 8875

---

## Text

The survey that establishes a CGC83 value or values shall meet all of the following requirements:

(a) The survey shall be referenced to and shall have field-observed statistically independent connections to one or more appropriate reference stations that is one of the following:

(1) CSRN station.

(2) Geodetic control station located outside of the State of California that meets all the requirements for inclusion in the CSRN except that the station is outside California.

(3) Existing CGC83 station that:

(A) Is shown on a map filed with the applicable county surveyor by a public officer, subdivision map, corner record, or record of survey.

(B) Meets all the requirements for inclusion in the CSRN except that the station and its data are not published by NGS or CSRC.

(C) Has an accuracy, conforming to the applicable CSRN requirements, stated for the station’s value.

(4) Existing CGC83 station that is shown on a public map or document that:

(A) Is compiled and maintained by the applicable county surveyor.

(B) Meets all the requirements for inclusion in the CSRN except that the station and its data are not published by NGS or CSRC.

(C) Has an accuracy, conforming to the applicable CSRN requirements, stated for the station’s value.

(b) If an accuracy is to be claimed for the CGC83 value or values established, the claimed accuracy shall be an accuracy standard published by FGDC or FGCS.
