# PRC § 9807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 9807

---

## Text

A petition to form an improvement district shall be filed with the secretary of the district and may be inspected by all persons interested.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
