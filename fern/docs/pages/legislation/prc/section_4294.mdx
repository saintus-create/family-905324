# PRC § 4294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 3. Mountainous, Forest-, Brush- and Grass-Covered Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 4294

---

## Text

A clearing to obtain line clearance is not required if self-supporting aerial cable is used. Forked trees, leaning trees, and any other growth which may fall across the line and break it shall, however, be removed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
