# PRC § 5810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 7. Wetlands Preservation

**Citation:** PRC § 5810

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Keene-Nejedly California Wetlands Preservation Act.
