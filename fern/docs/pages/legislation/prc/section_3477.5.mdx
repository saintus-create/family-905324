# PRC § 3477.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3477.5

---

## Text

“Capital outlay” means those costs directly associated with the purchase of equipment necessary to implement a used oil collection project. The costs may include, but are not limited to, the cost of material and labor associated with the installation of that equipment. Capital outlay does not include land acquisition costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1657, Sec. 2.)
