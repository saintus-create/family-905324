# PRC § 32640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32640

---

## Text

All meetings of the governing board are subject to the Bagley-Keene Open Meeting Act (Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 574, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
