# PRC § 35022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. COASTAL RESOURCES AND ENERGY ASSISTANCE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 35022

---

## Text

“Coastal zone” means the coastal zone as defined in Section 30103.
