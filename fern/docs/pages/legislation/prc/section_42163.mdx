# PRC § 42163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3.5. Metallic Discards > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42163

---

## Text

“Recycling residue” means nonhazardous residue or residue treated to be nonhazardous that is a direct result of metals recovery operations for the express purposes of recycling.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 849, Sec. 2.)
