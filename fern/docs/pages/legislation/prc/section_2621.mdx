# PRC § 2621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 7.5. Earthquake Fault Zoning

**Citation:** PRC § 2621

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Alquist-Priolo Earthquake Fault Zoning Act.
