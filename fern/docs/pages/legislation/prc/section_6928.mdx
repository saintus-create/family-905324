# PRC § 6928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 6. Extraction of Sodium Chloride From Specified Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6928

---

## Text

The purchaser shall acquire no right to sell any such sodium chloride, but may extract it solely for public use in carrying out the public functions herein specifed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
