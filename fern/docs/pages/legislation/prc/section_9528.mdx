# PRC § 9528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 4. District Finance > Article: 2. District Fiscal Procedure

**Citation:** PRC § 9528

---

## Text

An annual audit of the books, accounts, records, papers, money, and securities shall be made as required by Section 26909 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
