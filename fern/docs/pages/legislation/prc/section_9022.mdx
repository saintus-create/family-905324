# PRC § 9022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 9022

---

## Text

“Public district” means a district established under the law of this state, other than a resource conservation district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
