# PRC § 35131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 35131

---

## Text

The governing board shall adopt rules for its proceedings consistent with state law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
