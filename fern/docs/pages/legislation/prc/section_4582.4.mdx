# PRC § 4582.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7. Timber Harvesting

**Citation:** PRC § 4582.4

---

## Text

Notice of the filing of timber harvesting plans shall be made by the department to any person who requests, in writing, such notification.
