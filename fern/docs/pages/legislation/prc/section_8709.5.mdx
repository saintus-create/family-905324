# PRC § 8709.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.7. SCHOOL LANDS > Chapter: 1. School Land Bank Act

**Citation:** PRC § 8709.5

---

## Text

Expenses attributable to management and remediation efforts on state school lands are payable from the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 485, Sec. 2. (AB 982) Effective January 1, 2012.)
