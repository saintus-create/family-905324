# PRC § 29715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** PRC § 29715

---

## Text

To the extent of any conflict or inconsistency between this division and any provision of the Water Code, the provisions of the Water Code shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
