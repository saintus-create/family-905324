# PRC § 71533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 37. CALIFORNIA ENDOWMENT FOR MARINE PRESERVATION > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Governance

**Citation:** PRC § 71533

---

## Text

Any vacancy on the board shall be filled by the appointing authority for the remainder of the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 687, Sec. 2. (AB 2503) Effective January 1, 2011.)
