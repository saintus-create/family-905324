# PRC § 5096.806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.699. Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 3. Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Fund of 2006

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.806

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Fund of 2006, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 33, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1E at the November 7, 2006, election.)
