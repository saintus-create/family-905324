# PRC § 3949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. Mining Partnerships

**Citation:** PRC § 3949

---

## Text

The decision of the members owning a majority of the shares or interests in a mining partnership binds it in the conduct of its business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
