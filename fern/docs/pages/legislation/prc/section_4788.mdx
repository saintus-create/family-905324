# PRC § 4788

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 11. Range and Forage Lands > Article: 2. Land Protection and Improvement

**Citation:** PRC § 4788

---

## Text

The department may accept contributions of money from any private source to carry out the powers and duties imposed upon it by this article.
