# PRC § 3637.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3.5. Unit Operation > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3637.2

---

## Text

“Royalty interest” means a right to or interest in oil and gas produced from any lands or in the proceeds of the first sale thereof other than a working interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1673.)
