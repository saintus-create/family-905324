# PRC § 2206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. The California Geological Survey

**Citation:** PRC § 2206

---

## Text

The State Geologist may prepare a special collection of ores and minerals of California to be sent to or used at any world’s fair or exposition in order to display the mineral wealth of the State.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 371.)
