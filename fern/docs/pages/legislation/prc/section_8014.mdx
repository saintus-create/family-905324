# PRC § 8014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 10. State Maps and Surveys

**Citation:** PRC § 8014

---

## Text

The department may enter into contracts with public and private agencies for mapping and surveys not now the direct responsibility of existing state agencies with the approval of the Director of General Services.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
