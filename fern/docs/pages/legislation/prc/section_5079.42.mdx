# PRC § 5079.42

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.1.5. California Heritage Fund > Article: 5. Historical Resources Preservation Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 5079.42

---

## Text

A public agency may enter into an agreement with a nonprofit organization for the purpose of carrying out a historical resource preservation project funded by a grant made pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 749, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
