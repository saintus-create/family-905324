# PRC § 13040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 13040

---

## Text

The district board by resolution, shall provide for the time and place of its meetings, the manner of calling special meetings and shall establish rules for its proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
