# PRC § 21060.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21060.4

---

## Text

“Distribution center” means a warehouse distribution center, as defined in Section 2100 of the Labor Code, that is 50,000 square feet or larger.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 24, Sec. 3. (SB 131) Effective June 30, 2025.)
