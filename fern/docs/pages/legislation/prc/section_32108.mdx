# PRC § 32108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Bonds and Notes > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PRC § 32108

---

## Text

The authority may, out of any funds available therefor, purchase its bonds or notes. The authority may hold, pledge, cancel, or resell the bonds, subject to and in accordance with agreements with bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
