# PRC § 42490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.7. Cell Phone Recycling Act of 2004 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42490

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited as, the Cell Phone Recycling Act of 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 891, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005. Inoperative September 30, 2027, pursuant to Section 42499.5. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 42499.5.)
