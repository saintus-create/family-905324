# PRC § 8650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.5. ARROYO SECO PARKLANDS

**Citation:** PRC § 8650

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Arroyo Seco Parklands Preservation Law of 1975.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 886.)
