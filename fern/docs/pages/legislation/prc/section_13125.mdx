# PRC § 13125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13125

---

## Text

Any bonds issued by the district may be made callable by resolution of the board adopted at or prior to the time of issuing the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
