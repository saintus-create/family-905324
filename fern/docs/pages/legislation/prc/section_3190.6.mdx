# PRC § 3190.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3.6. Gravity-Based Energy Storage Well Pilot Program

**Citation:** PRC § 3190.6

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, a gravity-based energy storage well shall meet all requirements applicable to a well specified in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 419, Sec. 1. (SB 567) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, pursuant to Section 3190.9.)
