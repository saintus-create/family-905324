# PRC § 3710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3710

---

## Text

“Owner” includes “operator” when any well is operated or has been operated or is about to be operated by any person other than the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1483.)
