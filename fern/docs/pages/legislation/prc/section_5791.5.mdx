# PRC § 5791.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 12. Zones

**Citation:** PRC § 5791.5

---

## Text

A local agency formation commission shall have no power or duty to review and approve or disapprove a proposal to form a zone, a proposal to change the boundaries of a zone, or a proposal to dissolve a zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
