# PRC § 9357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 8. General District Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 9357

---

## Text

Members of county boards of supervisors shall not be eligible to simultaneously hold office as a district director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 831, Sec. 16.)
