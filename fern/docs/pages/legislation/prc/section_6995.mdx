# PRC § 6995

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4.1. Leases for Taking Minerals From Waters and Certain Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6995

---

## Text

The commission shall prescribe such additional terms, covenants and conditions, consistent with the provisions of this chapter, of permits and leases issued under this article as will in its opinion effectually protect the interests of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1241.)
