# PRC § 30970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20.6.5. CALIFORNIA SEA LEVEL RISE MITIGATION AND ADAPTATION ACT OF 2021

**Citation:** PRC § 30970

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Sea Level Rise Mitigation and Adaptation Act of 2021.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 236, Sec. 5. (SB 1) Effective January 1, 2022.)
