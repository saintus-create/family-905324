# PRC § 33826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.6. SALTON SEA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 33826

---

## Text

The conservancy may engage in partnerships with tribes, nonprofit organizations, local public agencies, including the Salton Sea Authority, and landowners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 771, Sec. 1. (SB 583) Effective January 1, 2025.)
