# PRC § 25000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1. Title and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 25000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Warren-Alquist State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Act.
