# PRC § 9307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 7. District Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 9307

---

## Text

The directors shall appoint a secretary who shall serve at the pleasure of, and whose compensation shall be fixed by, the directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
