# PRC § 5090.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 3. Division of Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.30

---

## Text

There is in the department the Division of Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation. Whenever any reference is made to the Office of Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation, it shall be deemed to be a reference to, and to mean, the division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 459, Sec. 8. (SB 249) Effective January 1, 2018.)
