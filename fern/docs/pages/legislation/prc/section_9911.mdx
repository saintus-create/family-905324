# PRC § 9911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 7. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** PRC § 9911

---

## Text

All acts, proceedings, conclusions, and findings of fact, including the levy of an assessment, by the directors of a district concerning an improvement district therein shall be conclusive except in an action or proceeding instituted within six months after the acts, proceedings, conclusions, or findings were had or made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
