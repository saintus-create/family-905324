# PRC § 3316.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5.5. Subsidence

**Citation:** PRC § 3316.14

## Text

“Royalty interest owner” means a person owning a royalty interest.
