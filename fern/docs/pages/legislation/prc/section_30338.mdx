# PRC § 30338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 30338

---

## Text

By May 1, 1977, the commission, after full consultation with the State Water Resources Control Board, shall adopt regulations for the timing of its review of proposed treatment works pursuant to the provisions of subdivision (c) of Section 30412.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
