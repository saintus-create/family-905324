# PRC § 31005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31005

---

## Text

“Bay commission” means the San Francisco Bay Conservation and Development Commission, established under Title 7.2 (commencing with Section 66600) of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 967, Sec. 1.)
