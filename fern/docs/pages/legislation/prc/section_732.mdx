# PRC § 732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 2. State Board of Forestry and Fire Protection

**Citation:** PRC § 732

---

## Text

Each member of the board shall hold office for four years from the expiration of the term of his or her predecessor. Vacancies shall be immediately filled by the Governor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 257, Sec. 5.)
