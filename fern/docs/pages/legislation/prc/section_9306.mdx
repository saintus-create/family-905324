# PRC § 9306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 7. District Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 9306

---

## Text

After such classification the directors shall organize and elect a president from their number who shall serve as such at the pleasure of the directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
