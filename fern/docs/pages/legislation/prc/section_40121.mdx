# PRC § 40121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40121

---

## Text

“Disposal facility” or “facility” means a facility or location where disposal of solid waste occurs or an EMSW conversion facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 411, Sec. 1. (AB 1126) Effective January 1, 2014.)
