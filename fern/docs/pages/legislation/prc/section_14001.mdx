# PRC § 14001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA CONSERVATION CORPS > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations of Policy

**Citation:** PRC § 14001

---

## Text

In the Natural Resources Agency there is the California Conservation Corps, which shall be responsible for carrying out the purposes of, and implementing the findings and policies set forth in, this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 635, Sec. 1. (AB 2126) Effective January 1, 2019.)
