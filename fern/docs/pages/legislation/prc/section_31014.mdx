# PRC § 31014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31014

---

## Text

“Bay plan” means the San Francisco Bay Plan as adopted by the bay commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 967, Sec. 3.)
