# PRC § 32300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.3. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 32300

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta Conservancy Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 37. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
