# PRC § 35151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PRC § 35151

---

## Text

The authority may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
