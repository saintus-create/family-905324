# PRC § 5096.3075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.692. Safe Neighborhood Parks, Clean Water, Clean Air, and Coastal Protection Bond Act of 2000 (The Villaraigosa-Keeley Act) > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.3075

---

## Text

Upon a finding by the administering entity that a particular project for which funds have been allocated cannot be completed, or that the funds are in excess of the total needed, the Legislature may reallocate those funds for other high priority needs consistent with this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 461, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 12 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
