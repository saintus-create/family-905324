# PRC § 44015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Permit and Inspection Program > Article: 1. Solid Waste Facility Permits

**Citation:** PRC § 44015

---

## Text

A solid waste facilities permit issued or revised under this chapter shall be reviewed and, if necessary, revised at least once every five years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 448, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
