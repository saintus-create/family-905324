# PRC § 546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 2. State Park and Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 546

---

## Text

The director may authorize any employee of the department to exercise any power granted to, or to perform any duty imposed upon, the director.
