# PRC § 5096.800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.699. Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.800

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Act of 2006.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 33, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1E at the November 7, 2006, election.)
