# PRC § 10339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.4. RANGELAND, GRAZING LAND, AND GRASSLAND PROTECTION ACT

**Citation:** PRC § 10339

---

## Text

The board may require further information as is reasonably necessary to allow the board to evaluate the proposed acquisition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 984, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
