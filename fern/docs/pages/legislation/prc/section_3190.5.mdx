# PRC § 3190.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3.6. Gravity-Based Energy Storage Well Pilot Program

**Citation:** PRC § 3190.5

---

## Text

A gravity-based energy storage well that has lost its mechanical integrity shall be plugged and abandoned within one year or scheduled for plugging and abandonment in an approved idle well management plan or related plan approved by the supervisor unless the mechanical integrity of the well is restored.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 419, Sec. 1. (SB 567) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, pursuant to Section 3190.9.)
