# PRC § 10283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.3. AGRICULTURAL PROTECTION PLANNING GRANT PROGRAM

**Citation:** PRC § 10283

---

## Text

Eligible projects funded under this division with the proceeds from the sale of any bonds shall be consistent with the requirements of Section 16727 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 983, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
