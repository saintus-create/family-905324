# PRC § 14422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA CONSERVATION CORPS > Chapter: 5. Corpsmember Educational and Employment Outcome Reporting

**Citation:** PRC § 14422

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this chapter to evaluate how effectively the corps transitions corpsmembers into educational and employment opportunities upon completion of their service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 51, Sec. 32. (SB 854) Effective June 27, 2018.)
