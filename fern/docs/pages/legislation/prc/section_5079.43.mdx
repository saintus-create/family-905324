# PRC § 5079.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.1.5. California Heritage Fund > Article: 5. Historical Resources Preservation Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 5079.43

---

## Text

After completion of the historical resource preservation project, the grant recipient shall return to the office the amount of the grant that exceeds the eligible project costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 749, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
