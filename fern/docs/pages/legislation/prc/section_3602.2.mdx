# PRC § 3602.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3. Spacing of Wells and Community Leases

**Citation:** PRC § 3602.2

---

## Text

In determining the area of parcels of land for the purposes of this chapter, the area of the oil and gas mineral estate shall be used exclusively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 405.)
