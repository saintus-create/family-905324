# PRC § 40190.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40190.5

---

## Text

“Sharps waste” means waste generated by a household that includes a hypodermic needle, syringe, or lancet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 157, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
