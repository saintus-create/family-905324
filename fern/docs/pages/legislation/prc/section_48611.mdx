# PRC § 48611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48611

---

## Text

“Container” means a drum, can, or other receptacle used primarily for storage or transportation of oil. “Container” does not mean the equipment in which oil is used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
