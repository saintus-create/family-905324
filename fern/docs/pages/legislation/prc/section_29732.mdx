# PRC § 29732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 29732

---

## Text

“Sphere of influence line” refers to those boundaries of local governments as defined in Sections 56425 and 56426 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
