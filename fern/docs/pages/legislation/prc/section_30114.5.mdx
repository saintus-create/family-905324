# PRC § 30114.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30114.5

---

## Text

“Residential development project” means a multifamily housing project that consists exclusively of residential uses and includes four or more units.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 22, Sec. 62. (AB 130) Effective June 30, 2025.)
