# PRC § 50001.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. WASTE MANAGEMENT FACILITIES

**Citation:** PRC § 50001.2

---

## Text

Nothing in this division is intended to limit the ability of a city or county to enter into a joint exercise of powers agreement to establish procedures, plans, policies, and criteria to which solid waste facilities shall conform.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1291, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1993.)
