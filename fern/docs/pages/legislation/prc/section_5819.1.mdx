# PRC § 5819.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 7.5. Sierra Nevada-Cascade Conservation Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 5819.1

---

## Text

(a) This chapter establishes the Sierra Nevada-Cascade Conservation Grant Program in the Resources Agency.

(b) The secretary shall administer the program.

(c) The program applies only in the Sierra Nevada-Cascade Mountain Region.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 15. Effective August 16, 2004.)
