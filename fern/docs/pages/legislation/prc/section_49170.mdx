# PRC § 49170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49170

---

## Text

The bonds shall be signed by the chairperson of the district board and countersigned by the county auditor, and the seal of the district board shall be affixed. The interest coupons of the bonds shall be numbered consecutively and signed by the county auditor by his or her engraved or lithographed signature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
