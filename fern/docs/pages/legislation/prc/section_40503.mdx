# PRC § 40503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 40503

---

## Text

The board shall maintain its headquarters in the County of Sacramento, and may establish regional offices in any part of the state that the board deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
