# PRC § 5128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 2. County Museums, and Historical Records and Property

**Citation:** PRC § 5128

---

## Text

The board may publish documents pertaining to the work of the museum and may sell, exchange, or distribute them without charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
