# PRC § 13015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13015

---

## Text

“Charges” includes fees, tolls, rates, and rentals.
