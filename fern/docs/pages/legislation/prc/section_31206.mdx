# PRC § 31206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Coastal Restoration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 31206

---

## Text

In accordance with procedures adopted by the conservancy, public agencies and nonprofit organizations may submit proposed coastal restoration projects for consideration by the conservancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2003.)
