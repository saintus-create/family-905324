# PRC § 2690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 7.8. Seismic Hazards Mapping

**Citation:** PRC § 2690

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Seismic Hazards Mapping Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1168, Sec. 3. Section operative April 1, 1991, pursuant to Section 2599.6 (later renumbered as 2699.6).)
