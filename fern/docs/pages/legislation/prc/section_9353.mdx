# PRC § 9353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 8. General District Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 9353

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in the chapter, districts governed by this chapter are subject to the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
