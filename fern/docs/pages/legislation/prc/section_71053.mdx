# PRC § 71053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 71053

---

## Text

“Advisory committee” means the Environmental Data Management Advisory Committee established pursuant to Section 71064.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1112, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
