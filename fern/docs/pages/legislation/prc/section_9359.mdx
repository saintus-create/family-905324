# PRC § 9359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 8. General District Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 9359

---

## Text

Except as election of directors by division may be provided pursuant to Section 9356, all registered voters in a district shall be qualified electors and eligible to vote in district elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 831, Sec. 18.)
