# PRC § 13053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 13053

---

## Text

Notices of elections shall be given as nearly as practicable in accordance with the general laws regarding notices of county election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
