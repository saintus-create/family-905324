# PRC § 539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 2. State Park and Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 539

---

## Text

The commission shall establish general policies for the guidance of the director in the administration, protection and development of the state park system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1179.)
