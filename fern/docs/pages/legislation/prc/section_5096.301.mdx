# PRC § 5096.301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.692. Safe Neighborhood Parks, Clean Water, Clean Air, and Coastal Protection Bond Act of 2000 (The Villaraigosa-Keeley Act) > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.301

---

## Text

Responding to the recreational and open-space needs of a growing population and expanding urban communities, this act will revive state stewardship of natural resources by investing in neighborhood parks and state parks, clean water protection, and coastal beaches and scenic areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 461, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 12 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
