# PRC § 9806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 9806

---

## Text

The petition may consist of any number of separate instruments, which shall be duplicates except as to signatures.
