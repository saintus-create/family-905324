# PRC § 8708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.7. SCHOOL LANDS > Chapter: 1. School Land Bank Act

**Citation:** PRC § 8708

---

## Text

Until expended for acquisitions in accordance with this division, moneys in the fund shall be deposited in the Pooled Money Investment Fund and the interest deposited in the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 485, Sec. 2. (AB 982) Effective January 1, 2012.)
