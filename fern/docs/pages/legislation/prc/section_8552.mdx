# PRC § 8552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Grazing Lands > Article: 2. Cooperation with Federal Agencies in Relation to Grazing Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 8552

---

## Text

The State Lands Commission may cooperate with the Secretary of the Interior, and may, in the name of the State, exchange lands in the manner and under the conditions prescribed in Section 8 of the Act of Congress cited in Section 8551 of this code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1383.)
