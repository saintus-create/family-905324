# PRC § 33333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Sierra Nevada Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 33333

---

## Text

On or after January 1, 2006, the board shall post agendas for each board meeting on the Internet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
