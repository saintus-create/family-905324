# PRC § 5780.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5780.5

---

## Text

This chapter is necessary for the public health, safety, and welfare, and shall be liberally construed to effectuate its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
