# PRC § 15000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.2. PRODUCTS CONTAINING TOXIC METALS > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** PRC § 15000

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Dry Cell Battery Management Act.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 816, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
