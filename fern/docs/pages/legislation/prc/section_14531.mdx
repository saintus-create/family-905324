# PRC § 14531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 14531

---

## Text

A payment made pursuant to this division, including, but not limited to, a handling fee payment, refund value payment, processing fee payment, or processing payment, may be made electronically.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 715, Sec. 1. (AB 2493) Effective January 1, 2019.)
