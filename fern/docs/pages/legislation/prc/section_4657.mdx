# PRC § 4657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 3. State Forests

**Citation:** PRC § 4657

---

## Text

Insofar as the provisions of this chapter may be in conflict with any other provision of this division, the provision of this chapter shall control.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
