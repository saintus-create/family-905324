# PRC § 26400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. ENERGY AND RESOURCES FUND

**Citation:** PRC § 26400

---

## Text

There is in the State Treasury the Energy and Resources Fund, which fund is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 899, Sec. 8. Effective September 17, 1980.)
