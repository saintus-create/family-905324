# PRC § 5360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 6. Municipal Park Improvement District Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 5360

---

## Text

The legislative body shall, subject to the provisions of this article, prescribe the form of the bonds, and of the interest coupons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1796.)
