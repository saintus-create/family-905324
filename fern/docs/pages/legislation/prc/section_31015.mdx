# PRC § 31015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31015

---

## Text

“Suisun Marsh Protection Plan” means the plan approved as provided in Division 19 (commencing with Section 29000).
