# PRC § 30614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 7. Development Controls > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30614

---

## Text

(a) The commission shall take appropriate steps to ensure that coastal development permit conditions existing as of January 1, 2002, relating to affordable housing are enforced and do not expire during the term of the permit.

(b) Nothing in this section is intended to retroactively authorize the release of any housing unit for persons and families of low or moderate income from coastal development permit requirements except as provided in Section 30607.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 297, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
