# PRC § 37005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NATURAL HERITAGE PRESERVATION TAX CREDIT ACT OF 2000 > Chapter: 3. Natural Heritage Preservation Tax Credit Program

**Citation:** PRC § 37005

---

## Text

The Wildlife Conservation Board shall implement the program. The board may request staff services from any department that submits an application and a proposal for a donation of property to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 113) by Stats. 2000, Ch. 900, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
