# PRC § 49523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Solid Waste Enterprises > Article: 3. Continuation of Services

**Citation:** PRC § 49523

---

## Text

Any local agency or solid waste handling enterprise may contract, upon mutually satisfactory terms, for the termination of all or any part of the business of the solid waste enterprise before the expiration of the period specified in Section 49520.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
