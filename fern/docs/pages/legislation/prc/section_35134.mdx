# PRC § 35134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 35134

---

## Text

All meetings of the governing board shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
