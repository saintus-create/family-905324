# PRC § 21189.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 6.5. Jobs and Economic Improvement Through Environmental Leadership Act of 2021

**Citation:** PRC § 21189.1

---

## Text

If, before January 1, 2033, a lead agency fails to approve a project certified by the Governor under this chapter, then the certification expires and is no longer valid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 60, Sec. 4. (SB 149) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, pursuant to Section 21189.3.)
