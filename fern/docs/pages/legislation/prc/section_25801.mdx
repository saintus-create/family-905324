# PRC § 25801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Accounts

**Citation:** PRC § 25801

---

## Text

There is in the General Fund in the State Treasury the Energy Resources Programs Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1067, Sec. 2. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1067.)
