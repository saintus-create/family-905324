# PRC § 5096.264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.691. California Park and Recreational Facilities Act of 1984 > Article: 7. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.264

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund or the State Coastal Conservancy Fund of 1984 which is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in those depositories and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 18 at the June 5, 1984, election.)
