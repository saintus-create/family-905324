# PRC § 29733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 29733

---

## Text

“Unincorporated towns” means the communities of Walnut Grove, Clarksburg, Courtland, Hood, Locke, Knightsen, Collinsville, and Ryde.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 8. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
