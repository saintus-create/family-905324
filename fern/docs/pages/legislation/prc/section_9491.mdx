# PRC § 9491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 12. Dissolution

**Citation:** PRC § 9491

---

## Text

A district may be dissolved in accordance with the provisions of the District Reorganization Act of 1965, Division 1 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 6 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
