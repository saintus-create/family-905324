# PRC § 3483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Grants for Used Oil Collection Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 3483

---

## Text

The board shall establish criteria for the granting of funds for used oil collection projects conducted by local agencies, including, but not limited to, information relating to proposed project costs and capital outlay.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1657, Sec. 2.)
