# PRC § 48640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 5. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 48640

---

## Text

The board shall administer this chapter. For organizational purposes, the board may create a new division, bureau, office, or unit to administer this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
