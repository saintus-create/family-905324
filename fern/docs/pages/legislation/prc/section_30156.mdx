# PRC § 30156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2.5. Revisions to the Coastal Zone Boundary

**Citation:** PRC § 30156

---

## Text

In San Mateo County, within the Butano Creek watershed, the boundary is moved seaward to the five-mile limit described in Section 30103 and as specifically shown on map 5.
