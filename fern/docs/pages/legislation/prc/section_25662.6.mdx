# PRC § 25662.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.6. Clean Energy Programs > Article: 2. Industrial Decarbonization and Improvement of Grid Operations Program

**Citation:** PRC § 25662.6

---

## Text

An eligible project that receives a financial incentive pursuant to this article is ineligible for a financial incentive pursuant to the Food Production Investment Program (Article 3 (commencing with Section 25663)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 251, Sec. 12. (AB 209) Effective September 6, 2022.)
