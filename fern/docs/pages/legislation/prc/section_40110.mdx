# PRC § 40110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40110

---

## Text

“Board” means the Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery, or the Director of Resources Recycling and Recovery, as appropriate.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 21, Sec. 5. (SB 63) Effective January 1, 2010.)
