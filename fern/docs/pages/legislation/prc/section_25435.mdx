# PRC § 25435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.3. Energy Conservation Act of 2001 > Article: 3. Small Business Energy Efficient Refrigeration Loan Program

## Text

The commission shall administer the Small Business Energy Efficient Refrigeration Loan Program, as provided for in Section 25436.
