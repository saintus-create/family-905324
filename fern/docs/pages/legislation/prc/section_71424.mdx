# PRC § 71424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Part: 6. Water-Energy Nexus Registry > Chapter: 4. Environmental Protection Indicators for California

**Citation:** PRC § 71424

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 39712 of the Health and Safety Code, entities participating in the registry may qualify for financing opportunities that provide incentives to reduce greenhouse gas emissions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 596, Sec. 1. (SB 1425) Effective January 1, 2017.)
