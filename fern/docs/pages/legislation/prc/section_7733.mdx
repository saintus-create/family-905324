# PRC § 7733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 2. Payments, Certificates of Purchase, and Patents

**Citation:** PRC § 7733

---

## Text

Where a patent for lands is issued in the name of a deceased person, the title is vested in the heirs, devisees, or assignees of such person in the same manner as if the patent had issued to such person during life.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
