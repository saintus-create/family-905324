# PRC § 29200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. SUISUN MARSH PRESERVATION > Chapter: 3. Responsibilities of the Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 29200

---

## Text

Unless expressly provided otherwise in this division, the commission shall have the primary state responsibility for the implementation of the provisions of this division and the protection plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
