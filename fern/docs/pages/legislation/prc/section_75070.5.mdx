# PRC § 75070.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 10. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 75070.5

---

## Text

Not more than 5% of the funds allocated to any program in this division may be used to pay the costs incurred in the administration of that program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 84.)
