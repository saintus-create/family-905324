# PRC § 5001.95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 1. State Park System

**Citation:** PRC § 5001.95

---

## Text

No state park system unit, other than a state wilderness, a natural preserve, or a cultural preserve, shall be located within the boundaries of another state park system unit.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 615.)
