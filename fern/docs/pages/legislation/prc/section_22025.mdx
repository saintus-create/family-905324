# PRC § 22025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. THE VENTURA-LOS ANGELES MOUNTAIN AND COASTAL STUDY COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Organization and Membership

**Citation:** PRC § 22025

---

## Text

All vacancies in the commission membership shall be filled in the same manner in which original appointments were made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1556.)
