# PRC § 8504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Grazing Lands > Article: 1. Regulating Grazing on Federal Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 8504

---

## Text

This article shall not be construed to prohibit any such established user from continuing his grazing use, as established in accordance with such customs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
