# PRC § 33300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 33300

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Laird-Leslie Sierra Nevada Conservancy Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
