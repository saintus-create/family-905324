# PRC § 40505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 40505

---

## Text

In order to carry out its powers and duties under this chapter, the board may enter into any contracts that the board determines to be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
