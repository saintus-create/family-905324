# PRC § 33821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.6. SALTON SEA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. Governing Board

**Citation:** PRC § 33821

---

## Text

The board may enter into contracts with private entities and public agencies to procure consulting and other services necessary to achieve the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 771, Sec. 1. (SB 583) Effective January 1, 2025.)
