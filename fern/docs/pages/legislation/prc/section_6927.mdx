# PRC § 6927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 6. Extraction of Sodium Chloride From Specified Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6927

---

## Text

The terms so fixed shall be operative for 20 years from the date of the grant. Upon the expiration of such 20-year period and each succeeding period of 20 years the commission or other duly constituted authority, may prescribe for each ensuing period of 20 years a different royalty rate or rates to be paid as a condition of the continued exercise of the grant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
