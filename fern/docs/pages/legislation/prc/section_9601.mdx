# PRC § 9601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 1. Consolidation

**Citation:** PRC § 9601

---

## Text

Any two or more contiguous districts, or districts situated within the same geophysical area, organized under this division may consolidate in accordance with the provisions of the District Reorganization Act of 1965, Division 1 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
