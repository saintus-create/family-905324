# PRC § 3636.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3.5. Unit Operation > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3636.3

---

## Text

“Unit operator” means the person or persons designated by the working interest owners as operator or operators of the unit area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1673.)
