# PRC § 562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 3. City of Pismo Beach Seawall Repair

**Citation:** PRC § 562

---

## Text

The department, with the approval of the Director of Finance and on terms satisfactory to the department, may advance moneys appropriated for purposes of this article to the United States Army Corps of Engineers, the County of San Luis Obispo, or the City of Pismo Beach, or any or all of them, for the repair, replacement, or improvement of the facilities described in Section 561.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1199, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1980.)
