# PRC § 9912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 7. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** PRC § 9912

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of an assessment or of any warrants may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
