# PRC § 30101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30101

---

## Text

“Coastal-dependent development or use” means any development or use which requires a site on, or adjacent to, the sea to be able to function at all.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
