# PRC § 30234.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 3. Coastal Resources Planning and Management Policies > Article: 4. Marine Environment

**Citation:** PRC § 30234.5

---

## Text

The economic, commercial, and recreational importance of fishing activities shall be recognized and protected.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 802, Sec. 2.)
