# PRC § 41352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. County Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 3. Source Reduction Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41352

---

## Text

The county source reduction component shall describe the methods that the county will use to determine the categories of solid wastes to be diverted from disposal at a disposal facility through source reduction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1292, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1993.)
