# PRC § 25203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 25203

---

## Text

Each member of the commission shall represent the state at large and not any particular area thereof, and shall serve on a full-time basis.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
