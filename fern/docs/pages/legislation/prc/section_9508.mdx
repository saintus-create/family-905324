# PRC § 9508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 4. District Finance > Article: 1. Regular Assessments

**Citation:** PRC § 9508

---

## Text

If the board fails to levy the assessment the auditor of the county shall do so, providing the directors have requested the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
