# PRC § 33351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 33351

---

## Text

The conservancy may expend funds under this division to conduct research and monitoring in connection with development and implementation of the program administered under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
