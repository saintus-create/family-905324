# PRC § 3711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3711

---

## Text

“Operator” includes “owner” when any well is or has been or is about to be operated by or under the direction of the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1483.)
