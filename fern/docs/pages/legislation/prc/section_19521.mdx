# PRC § 19521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.9. FIBERGLASS RECYCLED CONTENT ACT OF 1991 > Chapter: 4. Certification of Use

**Citation:** PRC § 19521

---

## Text

Every fiberglass manufacturer who submits glass cullet content usage certification pursuant to Section 19520 may be subject to an audit by the department to ensure that the cullet was used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 706, Sec. 1.)
