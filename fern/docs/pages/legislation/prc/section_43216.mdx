# PRC § 43216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 1. Local Enforcement Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 43216

---

## Text

If the board withdraws its approval of the designation of an enforcement agency, another enforcement agency shall be designated pursuant to Section 43203 within 90 days and approved by the board. If no designation is made within 90 days, the board shall become the enforcement agency within the jurisdiction of the former enforcement agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
