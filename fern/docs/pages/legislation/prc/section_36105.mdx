# PRC § 36105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 36105

---

## Text

“Ocean” and “marine” mean those waters from the coastline into the high seas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1215, Sec. 1.)
