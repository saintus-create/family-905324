# PRC § 29601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. SUISUN MARSH PRESERVATION > Chapter: 7. Judicial Review, Enforcement, and Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 29601

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 66630) of Title 7. 2 of the Government Code relating to cease and desist orders and penalties for violations thereof shall apply to all development for which a marsh development permit from the commission, or the commission hearing an appeal, may be required under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
