# PRC § 42823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 3. Major Waste Tire Facility Permits

**Citation:** PRC § 42823

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 42823.5, no person shall establish a new major waste tire facility or expand an existing minor waste tire facility unless the person has obtained a major waste tire facility permit issued by the board pursuant to Section 42822.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 191, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
