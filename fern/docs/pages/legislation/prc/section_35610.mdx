# PRC § 35610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. CALIFORNIA OCEAN PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Ocean Protection Council

**Citation:** PRC § 35610

---

## Text

One Member of the Senate, appointed by the Senate Committee on Rules, and one Member of the Assembly, appointed by the Speaker of the Assembly, shall meet with the council as nonvoting, ex officio members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 719, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
