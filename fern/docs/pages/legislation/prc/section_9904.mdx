# PRC § 9904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 6. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** PRC § 9904

---

## Text

The directors may require warrants delivered to lessen or remove an improvement district assessment lien to be substantially of the average maturities of the issue of warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
