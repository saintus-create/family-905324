# PRC § 31104.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the State Coastal Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 31104.2

---

## Text

The conservancy is the designated agency in the state for planning and coordinating federal surplus land sales in the coastal zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 505, Sec. 1. Effective July 13, 1982.)
