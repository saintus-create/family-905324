# PRC § 42023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Market Development Programs > Article: 3. Market Development Zone Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42023

---

## Text

Nothing in this section shall be interpreted to limit the authority of local governments to make land use decisions within their jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 733, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
