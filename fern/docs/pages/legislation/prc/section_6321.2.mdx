# PRC § 6321.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Administration and Control of Swamp, Overflowed, Tide, or Submerged Lands, and Structures Thereon > Article: 2. Control of Structures

**Citation:** PRC § 6321.2

---

## Text

In addition to the fees provided in Section 6321, the commission may fix and collect reasonable charges or rentals for the use of lands upon which any of the structures authorized under Section 6321 are situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1241.)
