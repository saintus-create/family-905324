# PRC § 19519

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.9. FIBERGLASS RECYCLED CONTENT ACT OF 1991 > Chapter: 3. Cullet Specifications for Fiberglass Manufacturing

**Citation:** PRC § 19519

---

## Text

A fiberglass manufacturer shall comply with all applicable state and federal laws, regulations, and permits concerning the environment, health, or safety when using cullet in fiberglass manufacturing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 706, Sec. 1.)
