# PRC § 41071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. City Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 4. Recycling Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41071

---

## Text

The city recycling component shall describe the types of materials which will be recycled under the programs in Section 41070.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
