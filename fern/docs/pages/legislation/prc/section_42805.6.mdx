# PRC § 42805.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42805.6

---

## Text

“Scrap tire” means a worn, damaged, or defective tire that is not a repairable tire.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 838, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
