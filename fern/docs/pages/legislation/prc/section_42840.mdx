# PRC § 42840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 5. Renewal, Suspension, or Revocation

**Citation:** PRC § 42840

---

## Text

A waste tire facility permit issued pursuant to this chapter is valid for five years unless suspended or revoked. The permit shall be renewed prior to the expiration thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 24. Effective March 30, 1990.)
