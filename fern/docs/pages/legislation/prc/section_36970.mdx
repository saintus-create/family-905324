# PRC § 36970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8. The California Ocean Resources Stewardship Act of 2000 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 36970

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Ocean Resources Stewardship Act of 2000 (CORSA).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 516, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
