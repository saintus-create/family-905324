# PRC § 40977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Plan Preparation > Article: 3. Regional Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 40977

---

## Text

A regional agency may authorize one district, as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 41821.2, to be included as a member of the regional agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 740, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
