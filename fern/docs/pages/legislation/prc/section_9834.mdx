# PRC § 9834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 2. Assessments

**Citation:** PRC § 9834

---

## Text

Each annual installment of the improvement district assessments shall be collected by county officers in the same manner and at the same times as county taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
