# PRC § 5099.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.9. California Outdoor Recreation Resources Plan Act > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 5099.11

---

## Text

The director shall transmit a copy of rules and regulations which have been adopted, and shall annually transmit any adopted revisions thereof, to each Member of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1322.)
