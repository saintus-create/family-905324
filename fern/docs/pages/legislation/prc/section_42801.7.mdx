# PRC § 42801.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42801.7

---

## Text

“Crumb rubber” means rubber granules derived from a waste tire that are less than or equal to, one-quarter inch or six millimeters in size.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 838, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
