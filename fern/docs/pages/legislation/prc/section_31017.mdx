# PRC § 31017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31017

---

## Text

“Public agency” includes, but is not limited to, local public agencies, state agencies, federal agencies, colleges and universities, intergovernmental bodies, and federally recognized Indian tribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
