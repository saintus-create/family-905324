# PRC § 5096.965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.699. Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 17. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.965

---

## Text

Pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 16720) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code, the cost of bond issuance shall be paid out of the bond proceeds. These costs shall be shared proportionally by each program funded through this bond act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 33, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1E at the November 7, 2006, election.)
