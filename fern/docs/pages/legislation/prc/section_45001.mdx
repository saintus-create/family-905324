# PRC § 45001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Administrative Enforcement > Article: 1. Corrective Action Orders

**Citation:** PRC § 45001

---

## Text

Nothing in this division affects the authority of the state water board or a regional water board to issue enforcement orders or take corrective actions with regard to solid waste facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 952, Sec. 34. Effective October 16, 1995.)
