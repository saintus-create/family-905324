# PRC § 25127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25127

---

## Text

“Refiner” means any person who owns, operates, or controls the operations of one or more refineries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1195.)
