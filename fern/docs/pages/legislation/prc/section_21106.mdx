# PRC § 21106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 3. State Agencies, Boards and Commissions

**Citation:** PRC § 21106

---

## Text

All state agencies, boards, and commissions shall request in their budgets the funds necessary to protect the environment in relation to problems caused by their activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1433.)
