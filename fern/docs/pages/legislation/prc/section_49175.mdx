# PRC § 49175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49175

---

## Text

If the proposition of issuing bonds submitted at the bond election fails to receive the requisite number of votes, the district board may, after expiration of six months after that election, call or order another bond election, either for the same objects and purposes, or for any other object or purpose of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
