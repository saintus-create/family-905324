# PRC § 48675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 8. Reporting

**Citation:** PRC § 48675

---

## Text

The board shall establish procedures to protect any proprietary information concerning sales, purchases, and operations obtained while collecting information for carrying out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
