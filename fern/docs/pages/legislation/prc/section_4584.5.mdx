# PRC § 4584.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7. Timber Harvesting

**Citation:** PRC § 4584.5

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 4584 shall exempt the owner of any timber harvested from registering with the State Board of Equalization or from the payment of any applicable timber yield taxes imposed pursuant to Section 38115 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 746, Sec. 3. Effective September 22, 1994.)
