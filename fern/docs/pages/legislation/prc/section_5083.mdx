# PRC § 5083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.2. Operation and Management of State Park System Properties > Article: 4. Old Sacramento State Historic Park

**Citation:** PRC § 5083

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to a lease agreement, management agreement, or other agreement entered into on or after July 1, 2023, for the operation of a hotel on land owned or controlled by the state in the Old Sacramento State Historic Park.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 51, Sec. 21. (SB 122) Effective July 10, 2023.)
