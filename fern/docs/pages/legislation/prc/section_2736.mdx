# PRC § 2736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 2736

---

## Text

“Financial assurances” means a current approved financial assurance cost estimate and a financial assurance mechanism that is at least equal to the current approved financial assurance cost estimate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 7, Sec. 4. (AB 1142) Effective January 1, 2017.)
