# PRC § 2741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 3. District Committees

**Citation:** PRC § 2741

---

## Text

The members of the committee shall receive no compensation for their services, but shall be entitled to their actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1131.)
