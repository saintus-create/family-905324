# PRC § 5096.688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.697. Efficient Water Use in Parks and Recreation Areas Act

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.688

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to promote the conservation and efficient use of water in California’s parks and recreational areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 322, Sec. 2. Effective September 3, 2002.)
