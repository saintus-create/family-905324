# PRC § 48642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 5. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 48642

---

## Text

The board may prepare, publish, or issue printed pamphlets, which the board determines to be necessary, for the dissemination of information concerning the activities of the board pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
