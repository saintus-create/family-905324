# PRC § 13233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Portion of District Included in a County Water District

**Citation:** PRC § 13233

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to repeal or limit any power possessed by a county water district within which there is any part of this district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
