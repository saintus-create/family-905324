# PRC § 26576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Nature and Powers of the District > Article: 2. Powers of a District

**Citation:** PRC § 26576

---

## Text

Within the territorial limits of the district, or for the purposes set forth in this division, a district may acquire real property or any interest therein by eminent domain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
