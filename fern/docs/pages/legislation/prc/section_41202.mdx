# PRC § 41202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. City Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 5. Composting Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41202

---

## Text

The city composting component shall describe the methods the city will use to determine the categories of solid wastes to be diverted from disposal at a disposal facility through composting.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1292, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1993.)
