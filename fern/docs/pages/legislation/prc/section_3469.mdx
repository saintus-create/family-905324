# PRC § 3469

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 9. Used Oil Recycling Act

**Citation:** PRC § 3469

---

## Text

The board, and every state officer and employee, shall encourage the purchase of recycled oil products represented as substantially equivalent to products made from new oil in accordance with Section 3471.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1158.)
