# PRC § 2760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 4. State Policy for the Reclamation of Mined Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 2760

---

## Text

The board shall not adopt or revise the state policy, unless a public hearing is first held respecting its adoption or revision. At least 30 days prior to the hearing, the board shall give notice of the hearing by publication pursuant to Section 6061 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 344.)
