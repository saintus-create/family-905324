# PRC § 14530.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 14530.6

---

## Text

Upon the request of the department, the Attorney General shall represent the department and the state in litigation concerning affairs of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1290, Sec. 2. Effective September 29, 1986.)
