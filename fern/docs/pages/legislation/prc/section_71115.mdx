# PRC § 71115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Part: 3. ENVIRONMENTAL JUSTICE > Chapter: 4. Environmental Protection Indicators for California

**Citation:** PRC § 71115

---

## Text

The Secretary for Environmental Protection shall, not later than January 1, 2004, and every three years thereafter, prepare and submit to the Governor and the Legislature a report on the implementation of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 72004 by Stats. 2001, Ch. 765, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2002.)
