# PRC § 4528.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4528.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to a person who engages in activities regulated by this chapter, as an employee, with wages as his or her sole compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 584, Sec. 9. (AB 1414) Effective January 1, 2012.)
