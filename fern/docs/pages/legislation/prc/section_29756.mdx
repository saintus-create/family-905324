# PRC § 29756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 29756

---

## Text

The commission may promote, facilitate, and administer the acquisition of voluntary private and public wildlife and agricultural conservation easements in the delta.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
