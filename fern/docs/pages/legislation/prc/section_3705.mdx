# PRC § 3705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3705

---

## Text

“Division,” in reference to the government of this state, means the Geologic Energy Management Division in the Department of Conservation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 771, Sec. 16. (AB 1057) Effective January 1, 2020.)
