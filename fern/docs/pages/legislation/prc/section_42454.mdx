# PRC § 42454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.4. Rechargeable Battery Recycling Act of 2006 > Article: 3. Rechargeable Battery Recycling

**Citation:** PRC § 42454

---

## Text

On and after July 1, 2006, it is unlawful for a retailer to sell a rechargeable battery to a consumer unless the retailer complies with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 572, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006. Inoperative September 30, 2026, pursuant to Section 42457. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 42457.)
