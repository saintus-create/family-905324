# PRC § 5937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5.8. CALIFORNIA WILDLIFE, COASTAL, AND PARK LAND CONSERVATION ACT > Chapter: 4. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5937

---

## Text

All money derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 70.)
