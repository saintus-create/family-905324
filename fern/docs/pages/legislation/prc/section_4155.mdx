# PRC § 4155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 5. Firewardens and Firefighting Personnel

**Citation:** PRC § 4155

---

## Text

Every person who in obedience to such summons assists in extinguishing any forest fire shall be compensated at the same wage rate, excluding fringe benefits, as state employees performing like services.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 500.)
