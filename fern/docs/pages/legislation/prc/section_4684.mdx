# PRC § 4684

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 2. State Nurseries

**Citation:** PRC § 4684

---

## Text

Trees, plants, nursery stock, and seeds which are sold pursuant to this article shall be sold at prices that are established by the department and approved by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
