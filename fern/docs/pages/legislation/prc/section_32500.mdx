# PRC § 32500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.5. SAN JOAQUIN RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 32500

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the San Joaquin River Conservancy Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1012, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
