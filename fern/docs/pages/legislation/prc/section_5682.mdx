# PRC § 5682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.5. Presley Urban Fishing Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 5682

---

## Text

Grant funds shall be encumbered by the recipient within two years of disbursement. Any grant funds not encumbered within this period shall revert to the Resources Account in the Energy and Resources Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 128, Sec. 1. Effective March 25, 1982.)
