# PRC § 6210.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6210.7

---

## Text

The commission may amend or terminate any lease, easement, or contract under its jurisdiction, with the consent of the other party thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6210.6 by Stats. 1951, Ch. 332.)
