# PRC § 2734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 2. Definitions

## Text

“State policy” means the regulations adopted by the board pursuant to Section 2755.
