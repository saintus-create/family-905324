# PRC § 26580.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Nature and Powers of the District > Article: 2. Powers of a District

**Citation:** PRC § 26580.1

---

## Text

The district may make improvements to existing public or private structures where the board of directors determines that it is in the public interest to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 771, Sec. 4.)
