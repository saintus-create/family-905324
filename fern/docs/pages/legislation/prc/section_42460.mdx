# PRC § 42460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.5. Electronic Waste Recycling > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42460

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the Electronic Waste Recycling Act of 2003.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 526, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Sections 42485 and 42486.)
