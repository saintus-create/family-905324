# PRC § 42871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 17. California Tire Recycling Act > Article: 3. Tire Recycling

**Citation:** PRC § 42871

---

## Text

The board shall administer a tire recycling program that promotes and develops alternatives to the landfill disposal of used whole tires.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 192. Effective October 12, 2001.)
