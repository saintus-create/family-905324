# PRC § 42012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Market Development Programs > Article: 3. Market Development Zone Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42012

---

## Text

The local governing body, or any person through the local governing body, may apply to the department for designation as a recycling market development zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 258, Sec. 27. (SB 155) Effective September 23, 2021.)
