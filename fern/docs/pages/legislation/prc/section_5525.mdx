# PRC § 5525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5525

---

## Text

The board of supervisors calling the election shall make all provisions for holding the election throughout the entire proposed district.

The district shall pay the cost of the election, except that if it fails to be created and established, the county shall pay the cost of the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1743.)
