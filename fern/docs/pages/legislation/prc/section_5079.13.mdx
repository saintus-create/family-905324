# PRC § 5079.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.1.5. California Heritage Fund > Article: 2. California Heritage Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 5079.13

---

## Text

The office shall deposit the proceeds from any lease, rental, sale, exchange, or transfer of real property, or any interest therein or option thereon, made pursuant to Section 5079.23, in the fund, together with any other reimbursements, repayments, and income received by the office, unless otherwise provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 749, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
