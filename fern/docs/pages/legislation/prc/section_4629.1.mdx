# PRC § 4629.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 9.5. Timber Regulation and Forest Restoration Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 4629.1

---

## Text

The Legislature further finds that the state’s forest practice regulatory program needs to develop adequate performance measures to provide transparency for both the regulated community and other stakeholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 289, Sec. 3. (AB 1492) Effective September 11, 2012.)
