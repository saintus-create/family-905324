# PRC § 31265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 6. Coastal Resource Enhancement Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 31265

---

## Text

Prior to undertaking any resource enhancement project under the provisions of Section 31263, the project shall be included within, and funded under, the Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1441.)
