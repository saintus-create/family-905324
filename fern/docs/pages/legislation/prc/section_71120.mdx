# PRC § 71120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Part: 3.5. CALIFORNIA ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION AGENCY CONSOLIDATION > Chapter: 4. Environmental Protection Indicators for California

**Citation:** PRC § 71120

---

## Text

Unless the context requires otherwise, the following definitions govern this part:

(a) “Agency” means the California Environmental Protection Agency.

(b) “Secretary” means the Secretary for Environmental Protection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 17. Effective August 16, 2004.)
