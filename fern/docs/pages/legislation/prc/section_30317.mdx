# PRC § 30317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 2. Qualifications and Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 30317

---

## Text

The headquarters of the commission shall be in a coastal county, but it may meet and may exercise any or all of its powers in any part of the state. The commission may maintain regional offices, if it finds that accessibility to, and participation by, the public will be better served or that the provisions of this division can be implemented more efficiently through the maintenance of those offices.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 285, Sec. 13.)
