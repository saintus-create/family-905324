# PRC § 22057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. THE VENTURA-LOS ANGELES MOUNTAIN AND COASTAL STUDY COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 22057

---

## Text

The commission may appoint technical advisory committees, whose members shall serve without compensation, and prescribe their membership, powers, and duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1556.)
