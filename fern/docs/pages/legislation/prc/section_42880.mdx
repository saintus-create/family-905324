# PRC § 42880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 17. California Tire Recycling Act > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 42880

---

## Text

The board shall administer this chapter. For organizational purposes, the board may create a new division, bureau, office, or unit to administer this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 25. Effective March 30, 1990.)
