# PRC § 5064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 5. Lease Contracts for Park and Recreational Areas

**Citation:** PRC § 5064

---

## Text

The department for a consideration may assign such options to other governmental agencies or departments. The department may accept gifts of money or lands from individuals and from other governmental agencies for the purpose of acquiring, by purchase or exchange, the lands under option.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2164.)
