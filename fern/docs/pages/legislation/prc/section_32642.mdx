# PRC § 32642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32642

---

## Text

The conservancy may do any of the following to assist in the decisionmaking process for the purposes of this division:

(a) Establish advisory boards, panels, or committees.

(b) Engage in public outreach.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 738, Sec. 1. (SB 1367) Effective January 1, 2019.)
