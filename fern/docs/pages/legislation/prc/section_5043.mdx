# PRC § 5043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 3.5. Columbia Historic State Park

**Citation:** PRC § 5043

---

## Text

Columbia Historic State Park is a unit of the State Park System and shall be administered in accordance with the laws applying thereto subject to the provisions of this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 71.)
