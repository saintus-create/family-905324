# PRC § 42833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 4. Minor Waste Tire Facility Permits

**Citation:** PRC § 42833

---

## Text

The board shall issue minor waste tire facility permits pursuant to the regulations upon application therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 24. Effective March 30, 1990.)
