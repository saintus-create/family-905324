# PRC § 21085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.6. General

**Citation:** PRC § 21085

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, for residential projects, the effects of noise generated by project occupants and their guests on human beings is not a significant effect on the environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 160, Sec. 1. (AB 1307) Effective September 7, 2023.)
