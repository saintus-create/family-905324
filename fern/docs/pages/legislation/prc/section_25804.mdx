# PRC § 25804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Accounts

**Citation:** PRC § 25804

---

## Text

All references in this division or any other provision of law to the State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Special Account shall be deemed references to the Energy Resources Programs Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1067, Sec. 4. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1067.)
