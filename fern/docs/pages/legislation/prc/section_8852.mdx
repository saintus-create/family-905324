# PRC § 8852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 3. Geodetic Datums and the California Spatial Reference Network

**Citation:** PRC § 8852

---

## Text

The official geodetic datum to which horizontal positions and ellipsoid heights are referenced within the State of California shall be NAD83, NATRF2022, or PATRF2022.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 111, Sec. 11. (SB 566) Effective January 1, 2024.)
