# PRC § 3982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 3982

---

## Text

“Hydraulic mining,” as used in Section 3981, is mining by means of the application of water, under pressure, through a nozzle, against a natural bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
