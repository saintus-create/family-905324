# PRC § 30270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 3. Coastal Resources Planning and Management Policies > Article: 8. Sea Level Rise

**Citation:** PRC § 30270

---

## Text

The commission shall take into account the effects of sea level rise in coastal resources planning and management policies and activities in order to identify, assess, and, to the extent feasible, avoid and mitigate the adverse effects of sea level rise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 236, Sec. 2. (SB 1) Effective January 1, 2022.)
