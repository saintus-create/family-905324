# PRC § 4625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 9. Conversion

**Citation:** PRC § 4625

---

## Text

If the board finds the applicant does have a bona fide intention to convert the land, it shall approve the application, authorizing the applicant to cut and remove any and all trees, provided that the applicant otherwise complies with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 88. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
