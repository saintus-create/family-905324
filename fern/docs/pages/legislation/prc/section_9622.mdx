# PRC § 9622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 3. Changing Name of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9622

---

## Text

Whenever in the judgment of the board of directors it is for the best interest of a district that its name be changed to a stated name, it may pass a resolution reciting such fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
