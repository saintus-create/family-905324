# PRC § 4787

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 11. Range and Forage Lands > Article: 2. Land Protection and Improvement

**Citation:** PRC § 4787

## Text

The department, with the approval of the board, may make such regulations as are necessary to effectuate the purposes of this article.
