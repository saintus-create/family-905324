# PRC § 71554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 37. CALIFORNIA ENDOWMENT FOR MARINE PRESERVATION > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 71554

---

## Text

The endowment shall not contribute to, or otherwise support, any political party, candidate for elective public office, or ballot measure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 687, Sec. 2. (AB 2503) Effective January 1, 2011.)
