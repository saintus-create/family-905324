# PRC § 14522.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14522.5

---

## Text

“Redemption” and “redeem” means the return to a recycling center or location of an empty beverage container for a refund of at least the refund value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1339, Sec. 10. Effective October 2, 1989.)
