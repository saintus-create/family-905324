# PRC § 42164

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3.5. Metallic Discards > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42164

---

## Text

“Solid waste landfill” means a solid waste landfill, as defined in Section 40195.1.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1041, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1997.)
