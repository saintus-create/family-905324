# PRC § 7604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Sale of Inland Lake and Unsegregated Swamp and Overflowed Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 7604

---

## Text

The lands designated in this chapter shall be sold at the price fixed by the commission.
