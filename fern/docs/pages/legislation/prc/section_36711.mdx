# PRC § 36711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Marine Managed Areas Improvement Act > Article: 2. Classifications, Designations, Restrictions, and Allowable Uses

**Citation:** PRC § 36711

---

## Text

The classifications contained in Section 36710 may not be inconsistent with United States military activities deemed mission critical by the United States military.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 610, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2004.)
