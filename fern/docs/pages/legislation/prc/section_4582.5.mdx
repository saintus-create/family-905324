# PRC § 4582.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7. Timber Harvesting

**Citation:** PRC § 4582.5

---

## Text

Timber harvesting plans shall be applicable to a specific piece of property or properties and shall be based upon such characteristics of the property as vegetation type, soil stability, topography, geology, climate, and stream characteristics.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 880.)
