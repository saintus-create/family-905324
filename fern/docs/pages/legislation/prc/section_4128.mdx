# PRC § 4128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 3. Responsibility for Fire Protection

**Citation:** PRC § 4128

---

## Text

In establishing boundaries of state responsibility areas, the board may, for purposes of administrative convenience, designate roads, pipelines, streams, or other recognizable landmarks as arbitrary boundaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
