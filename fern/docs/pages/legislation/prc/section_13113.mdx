# PRC § 13113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13113

---

## Text

A two-thirds vote shall be required for the issuance of general obligation bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
