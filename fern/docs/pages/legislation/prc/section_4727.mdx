# PRC § 4727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 6. Preservation of Big Trees

**Citation:** PRC § 4727

---

## Text

Upon the arrest and conviction of any person for violation of Section 4726, the party informing is entitled to one-half of the fines collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
