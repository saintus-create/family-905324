# PRC § 8617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. KAPILOFF LAND BANK ACT > Chapter: 2. Land Bank Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 8617

---

## Text

The trustee is authorized to accept gifts of real property or money for the purposes of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1471, Sec. 1.)
