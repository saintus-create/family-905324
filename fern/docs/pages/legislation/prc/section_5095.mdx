# PRC § 5095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.55. State Urban Parks and Healthy Communities Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the State Urban Parks and Healthy Communities Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 877, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
