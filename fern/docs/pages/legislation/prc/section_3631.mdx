# PRC § 3631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3.5. Unit Operation > Article: 1. Declaration of Policy

**Citation:** PRC § 3631

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed in such a manner as to conflict with the provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 6826) of Chapter 3 of Part 2 of Division 6.
