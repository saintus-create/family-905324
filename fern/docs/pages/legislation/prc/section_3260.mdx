# PRC § 3260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 4.3. Oil and Gas Environmental Remediation Account

**Citation:** PRC § 3260

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “account” means the Oil and Gas Environmental Remediation Account established under Section 3261.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 274, Sec. 3. (AB 2756) Effective January 1, 2017.)
