# PRC § 9855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 3. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** PRC § 9855

---

## Text

If any conditions not contained in the petition for inclusion are prescribed by the directors the real property shall not be included until two-thirds in number of the petitioners approve the conditions in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
