# PRC § 29308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. SUISUN MARSH PRESERVATION > Chapter: 4. Responsibilities of Other State and Federal Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 29308

---

## Text

All federal agencies, to the extent permitted under federal law or regulations or the United States Constitution, shall comply with this division and the policies of the protection plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
