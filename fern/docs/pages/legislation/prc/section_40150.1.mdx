# PRC § 40150.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40150.1

---

## Text

“Multicounty regional agency” means a regional agency, as defined in Section 40181, that includes all of the jurisdictions that are located in at least two or more rural counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 343, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2009.)
