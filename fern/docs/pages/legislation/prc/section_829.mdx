# PRC § 829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Aquaculture Development

**Citation:** PRC § 829

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “director” means the Director of the Department of Fish and Game.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 187.)
