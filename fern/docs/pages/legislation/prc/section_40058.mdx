# PRC § 40058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 40058

---

## Text

The solid waste handling services shall be provided for by one or any combination of the following:

(a) The furnishing of the services by the local agency itself.

(b) The furnishing of the services by another local agency.

(c) The furnishing of the services by a solid waste enterprise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
