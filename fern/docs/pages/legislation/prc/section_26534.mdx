# PRC § 26534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. District Formation > Article: 2. Lands Included

**Citation:** PRC § 26534

---

## Text

All lands included within a district shall be specially benefitted by construction proposed in a plan of control approved by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
