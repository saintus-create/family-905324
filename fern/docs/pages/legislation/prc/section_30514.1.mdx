# PRC § 30514.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 6. Implementation > Article: 2. Procedure for Preparation, Approval, and Certification of Local Coastal Programs

**Citation:** PRC § 30514.1

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt the findings or provide a written explanation or written notice, as appropriate, required by Sections 30512, 30512.2, and 30513 to support its action no later than 60 days after the date on which action was taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 746, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2005.)
