# PRC § 7723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 2. Payments, Certificates of Purchase, and Patents

**Citation:** PRC § 7723

---

## Text

Certificates of purchase, and all rights acquired thereunder, are subject to sale, by deed or assignment, executed and acknowledged before any officer authorized by law to take acknowledgments of conveyances of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
