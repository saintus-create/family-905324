# PRC § 32658

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. San Diego River Conservancy Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 32658

---

## Text

Proceeds or income from any lease, rental, sale, exchange, transfer of land, or interest in real property shall be deposited in the fund and shall be available for expenditure for the purposes of this division, upon appropriation by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 574, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
