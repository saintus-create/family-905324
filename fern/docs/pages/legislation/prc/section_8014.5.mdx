# PRC § 8014.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 10. State Maps and Surveys

**Citation:** PRC § 8014.5

---

## Text

With the approval of the Director of General Services the department may enter into cooperative agreements with the federal government or any agency or agencies thereof for performance of map production work in accordance with the general plan and program adopted hereunder.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
