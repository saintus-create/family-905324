# PRC § 5090.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 2. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.16

---

## Text

In making appointments to the commission, the Governor, Senate Committee on Rules, and Speaker of the Assembly shall consider the places of residence of the members of the commission in order to ensure statewide representation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1027, Sec. 6.)
