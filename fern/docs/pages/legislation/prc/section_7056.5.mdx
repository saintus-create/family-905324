# PRC § 7056.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 5. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases by Public Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 7056.5

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 7051 to 7056, inclusive, shall apply to operating agreements and all other types of agreements entered into by the agencies mentioned in Section 7056 for the purposes described in Section 7051.

This section does not constitute a change in, but is declaratory of, the pre-existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 780.)
