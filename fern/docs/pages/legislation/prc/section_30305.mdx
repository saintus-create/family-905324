# PRC § 30305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 1. Creation, Membership of Commission and Regional Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 30305

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this division, the commission shall succeed to any and all obligations, powers, duties, responsibilities, benefits, or legal interests of regional coastal commissions which existed prior to July 1, 1981.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1173, Sec. 5.)
