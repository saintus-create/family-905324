# PRC § 5163.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 3. Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5163.2

---

## Text

The X-ray film may be taken by a competent and qualified X-ray technician if the X-ray film is subsequently interpreted by a licensed physician and surgeon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 638.)
