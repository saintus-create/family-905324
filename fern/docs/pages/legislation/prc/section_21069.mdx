# PRC § 21069

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21069

---

## Text

“Responsible agency” means a public agency, other than the lead agency, which has responsibility for carrying out or approving a project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1312.)
