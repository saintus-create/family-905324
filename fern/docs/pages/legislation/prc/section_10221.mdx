# PRC § 10221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 10221

---

## Text

“Nonprofit organization” means an organization described in subdivision (a) of Section 815.3 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 502, Sec. 5. (AB 2964) Effective January 1, 2023.)
