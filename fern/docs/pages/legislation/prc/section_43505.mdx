# PRC § 43505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 3. Closure Plans

**Citation:** PRC § 43505

---

## Text

The closure plan and the postclosure maintenance plan may be revised only upon the filing of a written application therefor by the owner or operator, and the approval, or amendment and approval, by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
