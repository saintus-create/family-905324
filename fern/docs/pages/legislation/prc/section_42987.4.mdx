# PRC § 42987.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 21. Used Mattress Recovery and Recycling Act > Article: 2. Mattress Recycling Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 42987.4

---

## Text

Within 90 days after approval or conditional approval by the department of the plan, the mattress recycling organization shall implement the approved plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 388, Sec. 1. (SB 254) Effective January 1, 2014.)
