# PRC § 5097.991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.75. Native American Historical, Cultural, and Sacred Sites

**Citation:** PRC § 5097.991

---

## Text

It is the policy of the state that Native American remains and associated grave artifacts shall be repatriated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 370, Sec. 2.)
