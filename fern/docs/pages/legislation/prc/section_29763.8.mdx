# PRC § 29763.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 5. Resource Management Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 29763.8

---

## Text

A local government shall adopt its proposed general plan amendments within 120 days after their approval by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1155, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1994.)
