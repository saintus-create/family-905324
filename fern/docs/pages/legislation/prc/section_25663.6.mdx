# PRC § 25663.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.6. Clean Energy Programs > Article: 3. Food Production Investment Program

**Citation:** PRC § 25663.6

---

## Text

An eligible project that receives a financial incentive pursuant to this article is ineligible for a financial incentive pursuant to the Industrial Grid Support and Decarbonization Program (Article 2 (commencing with Section 25662)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 251, Sec. 12. (AB 209) Effective September 6, 2022.)
