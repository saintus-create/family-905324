# PRC § 4525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4525

---

## Text

“Person” includes any private individual, organization, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, city, county, district, or the state or any department or agency thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 209. Effective January 1, 1995.)
