# PRC § 42035.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Pharmaceutical and Sharps Waste Stewardship > Article: 6. Enforcement

**Citation:** PRC § 42035.8

---

## Text

All handling, transport, and disposal undertaken as part of a stewardship program under this chapter shall comply with applicable state and federal laws, including, but not limited to, regulations adopted by the United States Drug Enforcement Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 1004, Sec. 1. (SB 212) Effective January 1, 2019.)
