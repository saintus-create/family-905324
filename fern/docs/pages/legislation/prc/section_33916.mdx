# PRC § 33916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.7. PRESERVATION OF FUTURE TRANSPORTATION OPTIONS

**Citation:** PRC § 33916

---

## Text

Nothing in this division limits the authority conferred on the department by Section 104.6 of the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 754, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
