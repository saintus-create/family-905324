# PRC § 71015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 71015

---

## Text

“Consolidated permit agency” means the environmental agency that has the greatest overall jurisdiction over a project, as determined pursuant to Section 71020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 419, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
