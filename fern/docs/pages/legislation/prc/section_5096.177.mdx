# PRC § 5096.177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.69. California Parklands Act of 1980 > Article: 5. Coastal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.177

---

## Text

No state grant funds may be disbursed until the applicant agrees that any property acquired or developed with such funds shall be used by the applicant only for the purpose for which the funds were requested and that no other use of the property shall be permitted except by specific act of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 250, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 1980, election. Operative December 1, 1980.)
