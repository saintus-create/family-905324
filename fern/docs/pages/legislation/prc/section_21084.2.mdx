# PRC § 21084.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.6. General

**Citation:** PRC § 21084.2

---

## Text

A project with an effect that may cause a substantial adverse change in the significance of a tribal cultural resource is a project that may have a significant effect on the environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 532, Sec. 9. (AB 52) Effective January 1, 2015.)
