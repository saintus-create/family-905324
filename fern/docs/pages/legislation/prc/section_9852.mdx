# PRC § 9852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 3. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** PRC § 9852

---

## Text

The inclusion petition shall describe the boundaries of the improvement district as enlarged by the proposed inclusions and give the names and addresses of the owners of the additional real property in substantially the same manner as in the original petition for forming an improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
