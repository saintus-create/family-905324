# PRC § 5096.163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.69. California Parklands Act of 1980 > Article: 4. State Park System

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.163

---

## Text

Acquisition for the state park system by purchase or by eminent domain shall be under the Property Acquisition Law (commencing with Section 15850 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 250, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 1980, election. Operative December 1, 1980.)
