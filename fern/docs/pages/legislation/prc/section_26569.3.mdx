# PRC § 26569.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Emergency Formation > Article: 2. Notice and Hearing

**Citation:** PRC § 26569.3

---

## Text

If it appears at the hearing that the owners of more than one-third of the real property to be included within the proposed district object to the formation thereof, the proceedings for the formation of the district shall be abandoned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 555, Sec. 2. Effective July 18, 1984.)
