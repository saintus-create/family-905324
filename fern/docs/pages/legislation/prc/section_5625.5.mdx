# PRC § 5625.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.2. Roberti-Z'berg-Harris Urban Open-Space and Recreation Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 5625.5

---

## Text

Grants made to cities, counties, and districts for each fiscal year pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 5625 shall be based on the jurisdictional boundaries of recipients as of July 1st.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1166.)
