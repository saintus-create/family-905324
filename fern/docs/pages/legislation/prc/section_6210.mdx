# PRC § 6210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6210

---

## Text

The commission shall represent the State in all contests between it and the United States in relation to public lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
