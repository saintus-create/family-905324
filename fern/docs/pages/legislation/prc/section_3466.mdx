# PRC § 3466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 9. Used Oil Recycling Act

**Citation:** PRC § 3466

---

## Text

(a) The board shall prescribe guidelines for providing safe and conveniently located facilities for the deposit of used oil by persons possessing not more than five gallons at one time at no cost to those persons.

(b) The improper disposal of used oil pursuant to Section 25250.5 of the Health and Safety Code is prohibited and is subject to penalties pursuant to Article 8 (commencing with Section 25180) of Chapter 6.5 of Division 20 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 871, Sec. 4.5.)
