# PRC § 3270.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 4.4. Regulation of Production Facilities

**Citation:** PRC § 3270.2

---

## Text

The division shall inspect production facilities to ensure compliance with the standards prescribed in the regulations promulgated pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 3270.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 562, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2009.)
