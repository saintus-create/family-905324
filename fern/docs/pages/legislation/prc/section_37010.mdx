# PRC § 37010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NATURAL HERITAGE PRESERVATION TAX CREDIT ACT OF 2000 > Chapter: 4. Procedures

**Citation:** PRC § 37010

---

## Text

Applications shall be submitted to the donee to which the donor proposes to contribute the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 113, Sec. 1. Effective July 10, 2000.)
