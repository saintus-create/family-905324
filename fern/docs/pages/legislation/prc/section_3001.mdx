# PRC § 3001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 3001

## Text

“Department,” in reference to the government of this state, means the Department of Conservation.
