# PRC § 3226.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 4. Regulation of Operations

**Citation:** PRC § 3226.3

---

## Text

The division shall annually provide to the State Water Resources Control Board and the California regional water quality control boards an inventory of all unlined oil and gas field sumps.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 561, Sec. 1. (SB 1281) Effective January 1, 2015.)
