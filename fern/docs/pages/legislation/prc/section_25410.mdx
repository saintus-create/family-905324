# PRC § 25410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.2. Energy Conservation Assistance

**Citation:** PRC § 25410

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Energy Conservation Assistance Act of 1979.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1124. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 25421.)
