# PRC § 22087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.5. BLUE RIBBON COMMITTEE FOR THE REHABILITATION OF CLEAR LAKE

**Citation:** PRC § 22087

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Agency” means the Natural Resources Agency.

(b) “Committee” means the Blue Ribbon Committee for the Rehabilitation of Clear Lake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 842, Sec. 1. (AB 707) Effective January 1, 2018.)
