# PRC § 5506.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5506.6

---

## Text

The governing body of a district formed pursuant to Section 5506.5 may, by resolution, change the legal name of the district to the “Marin County Open Space District” or the “Sonoma County Agricultural Preservation and Open Space District,” as appropriate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 186, Sec. 2. Effective June 29, 1990.)
