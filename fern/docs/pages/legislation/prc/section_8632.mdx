# PRC § 8632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. KAPILOFF LAND BANK ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 8632

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to impair the ability of any other state agency from acquiring lands for wetlands restoration and enhancement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1471, Sec. 1.)
