# PRC § 7533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 2. Sale of Swamp and Overflowed Lands > Article: 2. Swamp and Overflowed Lands Sold Prior to September 19, 1939

**Citation:** PRC § 7533

---

## Text

These reports shall be forwarded to the commission, and upon receipt thereof the commission shall enter the payment so reported to the credit of the purchaser, and shall notify the county treasurer of the receipt of his report, or if it is not correct return it for correction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
