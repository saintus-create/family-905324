**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7.5. Responsible Battery Recycling Act of 2022 > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Responsible Battery Recycling Act of 2022.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 351, Sec. 1. (AB 2440) Effective January 1, 2023.)
