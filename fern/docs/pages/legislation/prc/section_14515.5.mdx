# PRC § 14515.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14515.5

---

## Text

“PET container” means a plastic beverage container labeled with a “1” pursuant to Section 18015 and subject to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 815, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2000.)
