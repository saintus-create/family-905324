# PRC § 42441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8. Recycled Battery Programs > Article: 1. Lead-Acid Battery Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42441

---

## Text

“Recycled lead-acid battery” means any lead-acid battery which contains a minimum percentage of postconsumer recovered lead. The required minimum percentage of postconsumer recovered lead shall be determined by the board in consultation with the Market Development Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
