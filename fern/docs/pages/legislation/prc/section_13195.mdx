# PRC § 13195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Alternative Depositary of District Money

**Citation:** PRC § 13195

---

## Text

Warrants shall be drawn by the finance officer and signed by the president and secretary, or one of them and one member of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
