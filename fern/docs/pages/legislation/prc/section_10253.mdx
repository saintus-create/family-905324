# PRC § 10253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 3. Eligibility and Selection Criteria

**Citation:** PRC § 10253

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall grant any new authority to the department to affect local policy or land use decisionmaking.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 931, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
