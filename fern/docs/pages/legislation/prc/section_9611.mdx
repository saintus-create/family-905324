# PRC § 9611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 2. Partition

**Citation:** PRC § 9611

---

## Text

A partition of a district shall be made in accordance with the District Reorganization Act of 1965, Division 1 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
