# PRC § 6210.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6210.2

---

## Text

The commission may withdraw from sale any of the public lands belonging to the State and may restore any or all public lands so withdrawn, or other public lands, for sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1241.)
