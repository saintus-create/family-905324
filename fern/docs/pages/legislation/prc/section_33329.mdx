# PRC § 33329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Sierra Nevada Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 33329

---

## Text

The board may enter into contracts with private entities and public agencies to procure consulting and other services necessary to achieve the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
