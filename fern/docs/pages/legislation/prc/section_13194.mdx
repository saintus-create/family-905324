# PRC § 13194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Alternative Depositary of District Money

**Citation:** PRC § 13194

---

## Text

Bond principal and interest and salaries shall be paid when due. All other claims and demands shall be approved in writing or in open meeting by a majority of the members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
