# PRC § 25121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25121

---

## Text

“Fuel” means petroleum, crude oil, petroleum product, coal, natural gas, or any other substance used primarily for its energy content.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
