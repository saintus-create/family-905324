# PRC § 9032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 9032

---

## Text

“Assessment roll” means the entire assessment roll upon the basis of which real property is taxed for county purposes.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
