# PRC § 71201.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 36. MARINE INVASIVE SPECIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 71201.7

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt regulations necessary to implement this division, pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 491, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2004.)
