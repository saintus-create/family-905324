# PRC § 30108.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30108.2

---

## Text

“Fill” means earth or any other substance or material, including pilings placed for the purposes of erecting structures thereon, placed in a submerged area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
