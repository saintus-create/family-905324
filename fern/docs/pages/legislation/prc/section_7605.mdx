# PRC § 7605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Sale of Inland Lake and Unsegregated Swamp and Overflowed Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 7605

---

## Text

If any of the lands described in this chapter are suitable for cultivation without reclamation, they shall be sold only to actual settlers in tracts not exceeding 160 acres.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
