# PRC § 2200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. The California Geological Survey

**Citation:** PRC § 2200

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “mine” includes all mineral bearing properties of whatever kind or character, whether underground, or in a quarry or pit, or any other source from which any mineral substance is or may be obtained.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1131.)
