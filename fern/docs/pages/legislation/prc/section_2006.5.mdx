# PRC § 2006.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 2006.5

---

## Text

“Supervisor of Mine Reclamation” or “supervisor” means the individual directing the Division of Mine Reclamation established pursuant to subdivision (d) of Section 607.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 521, Sec. 13. (SB 809) Effective January 1, 2018.)
