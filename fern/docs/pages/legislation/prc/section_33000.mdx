# PRC § 33000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. SANTA MONICA MOUNTAINS CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 33000

---

## Text

This division shall be known as and may be cited as the Santa Monica Mountains Conservancy Act.
