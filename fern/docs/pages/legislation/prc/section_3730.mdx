# PRC § 3730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3730

---

## Text

The owner or operator of any well shall keep, or cause to be kept, a careful and accurate log, core record, and history of the drilling of the well.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1483.)
