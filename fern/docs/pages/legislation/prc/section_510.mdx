# PRC § 510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 510

---

## Text

The department may adopt, alter, change or amend any state master plan of shoreline development.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
