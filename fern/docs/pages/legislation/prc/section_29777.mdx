# PRC § 29777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 7. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 29777

---

## Text

The commission shall not incur costs in excess of the amount of funds available for expenditure by the commission in any fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 286, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2005.)
