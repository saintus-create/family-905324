# PRC § 42649.85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 12.9. Recycling of Organic Waste

**Citation:** PRC § 42649.85

---

## Text

A local governmental agency may charge and collect a fee from an organic waste generator to recover the local governmental agency’s costs incurred in complying with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. (AB 1826) Effective January 1, 2015.)
