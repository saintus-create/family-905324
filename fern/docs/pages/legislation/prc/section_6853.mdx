# PRC § 6853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 3. Oil and Gas Leases on Lands Other Than Tide and Submerged Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6853

---

## Text

The commission may divide the lands within the tract proposed to be leased into parcels of convenient size and shape and shall prepare a form of lease therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
