# PRC § 3709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3709

---

## Text

“Operator” means any person drilling, maintaining, operating, pumping, or in control of any well.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1483.)
