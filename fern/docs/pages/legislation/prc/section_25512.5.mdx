# PRC § 25512.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 6. Power Facility and Site Certification

**Citation:** PRC § 25512.5

---

## Text

Within 15 days of the publication of the summary and hearing order, a copy will be distributed to any person who requests such copy.
