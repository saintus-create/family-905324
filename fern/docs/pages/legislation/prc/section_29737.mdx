# PRC § 29737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 3. Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 29737

---

## Text

Members shall serve without compensation, but the expenses of each member incurred in connection with official duties shall be paid by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
