# PRC § 9034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 9034

---

## Text

“Assessor” means the assessing officer of a county by whatever title he may be known.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
