# PRC § 13070.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 13070.1

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “acquire” includes, but is not restricted to, taking by condemnation, purchase, or lease and receiving by donation or dedication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 582.)
