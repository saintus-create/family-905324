# PRC § 9816

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 9816

---

## Text

The directors may include in the plans and specifications such terms and conditions as to the respective parcels of real property in the improvement district with respect to tolls, charges, assessments, or the conservation or use of soil and water or any other matters as the directors deem necessary or proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
