# PRC § 4593.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7.5. Nonindustrial Timber Management Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 4593.5

---

## Text

The department shall provide notice of the filing of nonindustrial timber management plans to any person who requests, in writing, that notification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1290, Sec. 9. Operative January 1, 1991, or sooner, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1290.)
