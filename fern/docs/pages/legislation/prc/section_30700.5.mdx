# PRC § 30700.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 8. Ports > Article: 1. Findings and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30700.5

---

## Text

The definitions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 30100) and the provisions of Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 30800) and Section 30900 shall apply to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
