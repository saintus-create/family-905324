# PRC § 5129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 2. County Museums, and Historical Records and Property

**Citation:** PRC § 5129

---

## Text

The board may employ curators, attendants, or other persons necessary to conduct the museum and to carry out the powers granted by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
