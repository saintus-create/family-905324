# PRC § 41420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. County Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 6. Education and Public Information Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41420

---

## Text

The county education and public information component shall describe to the board how the county will educate and inform its citizens about the source reduction, recycling, and composting programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1355, Sec. 17. Effective September 27, 1990.)
