# PRC § 5781.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 2. Area and Boundaries

**Citation:** PRC § 5781.1

---

## Text

The Cortese-Knox-Hertzberg Local Government Reorganization Act of 2000, Division 3 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 5 of the Government Code, shall govern any change of organization or reorganization of a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
