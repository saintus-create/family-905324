# PRC § 5096.606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.696. The California Clean Water, Clean Air, Safe Neighborhood Parks, and Coastal Protection Act of 2002 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.606

---

## Text

Lands or interests in land acquired with funds allocated pursuant to this chapter shall be acquired from a willing seller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 40 at the March 5, 2002, election.)
