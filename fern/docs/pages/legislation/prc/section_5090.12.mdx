# PRC § 5090.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.12

---

## Text

“Grant program” means the local assistance grant program and the cooperative agreement program.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (by Sec. 6) and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 563, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
