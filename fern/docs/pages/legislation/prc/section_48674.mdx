# PRC § 48674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 8. Reporting

**Citation:** PRC § 48674

---

## Text

After receiving payments pursuant to paragraph (3) of subdivision (a) of Section 48653, each local government shall submit an annual report to the board, in the manner specified by the board, that includes any revision to the local used oil collection program adopted pursuant to Section 48690, a description of all measures taken to implement the program, and a description of how payments were expended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 504, Sec. 14. (SB 579) Effective January 1, 2011.)
