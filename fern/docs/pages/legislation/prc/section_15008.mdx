# PRC § 15008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.2. PRODUCTS CONTAINING TOXIC METALS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 15008

---

## Text

“Mercuric oxide button cell battery” means a battery which contains mercuric oxide electrodes, resembles buttons in size and shape, and is used in consumer products such as hearing aids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 817, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
