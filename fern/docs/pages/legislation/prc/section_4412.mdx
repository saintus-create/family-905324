# PRC § 4412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 6. Prohibited Activities > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 4412

---

## Text

“Incinerator” means any device constructed of nonflammable materials for the purpose of burning therein trash, debris, and other flammable materials, excluding those devices regulated by Section 4438 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
