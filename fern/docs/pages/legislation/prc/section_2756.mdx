# PRC § 2756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 4. State Policy for the Reclamation of Mined Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 2756

---

## Text

State policy shall apply to the conduct of surface mining operations and shall include, but shall not be limited to, measures to be employed by lead agencies in specifying grading, backfilling, resoiling, revegetation, soil compaction, and other reclamation requirements, and for soil erosion control, water quality and watershed control, waste disposal, and flood control.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 800, Sec. 3. Effective July 28, 1980.)
