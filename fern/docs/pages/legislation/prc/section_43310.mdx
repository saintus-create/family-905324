# PRC § 43310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** PRC § 43310

---

## Text

If the board becomes the enforcement agency, it may charge reasonable fees to the local governing body to recover operation costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
