# PRC § 5795.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 13. Lower Los Angeles River Recreation and Park District

**Citation:** PRC § 5795.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “district” means the Lower Los Angeles River Recreation and Park District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 486, Sec. 1. (SB 1374) Effective January 1, 2017.)
