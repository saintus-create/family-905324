# PRC § 30421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 5. State Agencies > Article: 2. State Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 30421

---

## Text

State and regional agencies shall identify, assess, and, to the extent feasible and consistent with their statutory authorities, avoid, minimize, and mitigate the impacts of sea level rise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 236, Sec. 3. (SB 1) Effective January 1, 2022.)
