# PRC § 30949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20.4. WATERSHED, CLEAN BEACHES, AND WATER QUALITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Integrated Clean Water Programs > Article: 5. Integrated Watershed Management Programs

**Citation:** PRC § 30949

---

## Text

No more than 50 percent of the funds made available to the board for the purposes of this article shall be used for the purposes of Section 30948.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
