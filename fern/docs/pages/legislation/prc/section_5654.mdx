# PRC § 5654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.3. The Statewide Park Development and Community Revitalization Act of 2008

**Citation:** PRC § 5654

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be implemented only in a fiscal year for which funding is provided for that purpose in the annual Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 876, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
