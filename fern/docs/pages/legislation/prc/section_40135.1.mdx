# PRC § 40135.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40135.1

---

## Text

“Account” means the Integrated Waste Management Account created in the fund pursuant to Section 48001.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 3. Effective October 1, 1993.)
