# PRC § 9105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 4. The State Resource Conservation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 9105

---

## Text

Five members of the commission shall constitute a quorum for any purpose, including organization.
