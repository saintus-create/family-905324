# PRC § 48625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48625

---

## Text

The following terms have the following meaning:

(a) “Used oil storage facility” has the same meaning as defined in subdivision (g) of Section 25250.1 of the Health and Safety Code.

(b) “Used oil transfer facility” has the same meaning as defined in subdivision (h) of Section 25250.1 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1147, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
