# PRC § 2732.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 2732.5

---

## Text

“Permit” means any authorization from, or approval by, a lead agency, the absence of which would preclude surface mining operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1131.)
