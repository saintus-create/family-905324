# PRC § 3473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 9. Used Oil Recycling Act

**Citation:** PRC § 3473

---

## Text

If any provision of this article or the application of it to any person or circumstance is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this article are severable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1158.)
