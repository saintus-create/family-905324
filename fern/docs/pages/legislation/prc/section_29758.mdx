# PRC § 29758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 29758

---

## Text

All members of the commission are subject to Title 9 (commencing with Section 85100) of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
