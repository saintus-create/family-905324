# PRC § 25794.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 8.9. Strategic Reliability Reserve > Article: 5. Certification of Facilities

**Citation:** PRC § 25794.3

---

## Text

Upon receipt of an application pursuant to this article, commission staff shall establish a docket for the proceeding and publish a notice of receipt and shall mail the notice to all property owners within 1,000 feet of the proposed site. The commission shall establish a notification system for interested parties to receive notice of applications filed pursuant to this article. The notice shall also be sent to all local, state, and regional agencies that would have had jurisdiction over the site in the absence of the commission’s exclusive jurisdiction under subdivision (b) of Section 25794.1, federal agencies with jurisdiction over the project, and any California Native American tribe that is traditionally and culturally affiliated with the geographic area of the proposed site.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 251, Sec. 17. (AB 209) Effective September 6, 2022.)
