# PRC § 71155.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Part: 3.7. Climate Change and Climate Adaptation > Chapter: 4. Environmental Protection Indicators for California

**Citation:** PRC § 71155.5

---

## Text

The Treasurer, and the financing authorities that the Treasurer chairs, may assist state agencies by leveraging public and private capital investment to help with loans and other incentives to attain the goals established pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 338, Sec. 7. (AB 1384) Effective January 1, 2023.)
