# PRC § 33838

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.6. SALTON SEA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 6. Contingent Operation

**Citation:** PRC § 33838

---

## Text

This division shall become operative only if the Safe Drinking Water, Wildfire Prevention, Drought Preparedness, and Clean Air Bond Act of 2024 (Chapter 83 of the Statutes of 2024) is approved by the voters at the November 5, 2024, statewide general election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 771, Sec. 1. (SB 583) Effective January 1, 2025.)
