# PRC § 13058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 13058

---

## Text

Each voter, as defined in Section 13019, shall have one vote.
