# PRC § 32112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Bonds and Notes > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PRC § 32112

---

## Text

All expenses incurred in carrying out the provisions of this trust agreement or resolution may be treated as a part of the cost of the operation of a project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
