# PRC § 9509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 4. District Finance > Article: 1. Regular Assessments

**Citation:** PRC § 9509

---

## Text

The assessment shall be computed and entered on the assessment roll by the auditor.
