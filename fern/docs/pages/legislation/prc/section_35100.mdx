# PRC § 35100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 35100

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Santa Clara Valley Open-Space Authority Act.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 99, Sec. 2. (SB 422) Effective January 1, 2016.)
