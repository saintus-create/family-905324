# PRC § 9109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 4. The State Resource Conservation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 9109

---

## Text

The commission shall determine and advise policies for the guidance of the chief of the division in the performance and exercise of his duties and powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
