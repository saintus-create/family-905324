# PRC § 5572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5572

---

## Text

The boundaries of any district may be altered and new contiguous territory annexed, incorporated, and included and made a part of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2043.)
