# PRC § 75087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 11. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 75087

---

## Text

All money derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 84.)
