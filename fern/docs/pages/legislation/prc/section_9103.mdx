# PRC § 9103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 4. The State Resource Conservation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 9103

---

## Text

Within 30 days after his appointment the appointed member shall take and file his oath of office as member of the commission.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
