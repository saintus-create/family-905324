# PRC § 6898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 5. Minerals Other Than Oil and Gas

**Citation:** PRC § 6898

---

## Text

Leases under this article shall be for terms not to exceed 20 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 120, Sec. 7. (AB 706) Effective January 1, 2024.)
