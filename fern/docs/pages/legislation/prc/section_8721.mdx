# PRC § 8721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.7. SCHOOL LANDS > Chapter: 2. Land Exchanges for Renewable Energy-Related Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 8721

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “California desert” means the California Desert Conservation Area as described in Section 1781 of Title 43 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 485, Sec. 2. (AB 982) Effective January 1, 2012.)
