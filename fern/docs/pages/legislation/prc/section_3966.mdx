# PRC § 3966

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 3. Placer Mining Water Pollution

**Citation:** PRC § 3966

---

## Text

Any person, firm, or corporation who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
