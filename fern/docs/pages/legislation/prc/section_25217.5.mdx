# PRC § 25217.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 25217.5

---

## Text

The chair of the commission shall direct the public advisor, the executive director, and other staff in the performance of their duties in conformance with the policies and guidelines established by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 353, Sec. 6. (AB 1533) Effective January 1, 2025.)
