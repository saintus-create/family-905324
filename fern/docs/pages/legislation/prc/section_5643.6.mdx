# PRC § 5643.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.3. The Statewide Park Development and Community Revitalization Act of 2008

**Citation:** PRC § 5643.6

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the local assistance program created by this chapter fund both neighborhood parks and regional parks and trails.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 623, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2009.)
