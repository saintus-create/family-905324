# PRC § 31170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4.6. Santa Ana River Conservancy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 31170

---

## Text

The Santa Ana River Conservancy Program is hereby established, to be administered by the conservancy, to address the resource and recreational goals of the Santa Ana River region, as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 562, Sec. 1. (SB 1390) Effective January 1, 2015.)
