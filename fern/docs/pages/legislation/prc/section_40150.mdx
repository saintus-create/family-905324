# PRC § 40150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40150

---

## Text

“Local governing body” means the legislative body of the city, county, or special district which has authority to provide solid waste handling services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
