# PRC § 4646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 3. State Forests

**Citation:** PRC § 4646

---

## Text

The director, acting in accordance with policies adopted by the board, shall administer this chapter. The director may exercise all powers necessary to accomplish its purposes and intent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 89. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
