# PRC § 48619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48619

---

## Text

“Oil manufacturer” means the first person or entity in the state to take title to lubricating or industrial oil for sale, use, or transfer in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1101, Sec. 4. Effective September 29, 1992.)
