# PRC § 6206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6206

---

## Text

The commission shall provide the necessary record books and cause all lists or patents for lands from the United States to be recorded therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
