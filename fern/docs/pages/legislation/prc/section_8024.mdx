# PRC § 8024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 10. State Maps and Surveys

**Citation:** PRC § 8024

---

## Text

All money received from the sale of maps or reports and data related thereto shall be deposited in the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1581.)
