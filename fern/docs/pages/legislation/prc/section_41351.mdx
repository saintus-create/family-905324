# PRC § 41351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. County Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 3. Source Reduction Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41351

---

## Text

The county source reduction component shall describe the types of materials which will be reduced under the programs in Section 41350.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
