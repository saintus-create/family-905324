# PRC § 30341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 30341

---

## Text

The commission may prepare and adopt any additional plans and maps and undertake any studies it determines to be necessary and appropriate to better accomplish the purposes, goals, and policies of this division; provided, however, that the plans and maps shall only be adopted after public hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 285, Sec. 22.)
