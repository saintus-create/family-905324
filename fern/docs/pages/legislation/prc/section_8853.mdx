# PRC § 8853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 3. Geodetic Datums and the California Spatial Reference Network

**Citation:** PRC § 8853

---

## Text

The official geodetic datum to which orthometric heights are referenced within the State of California shall be NAVD88 or NAPGD2022.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 111, Sec. 12. (SB 566) Effective January 1, 2024.)
