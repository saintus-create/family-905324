# PRC § 9808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 9808

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a petition to form an improvement district the directors shall cause a survey to be made of the proposed improvements, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
