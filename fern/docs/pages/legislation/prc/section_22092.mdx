# PRC § 22092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.5. BLUE RIBBON COMMITTEE FOR THE REHABILITATION OF CLEAR LAKE

**Citation:** PRC § 22092

---

## Text

(a) The committee may receive assistance and funds from public and private sources.

(b) Upon appropriation by the Legislature, the committee may expend funds and award grants to conduct research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 842, Sec. 1. (AB 707) Effective January 1, 2018.)
