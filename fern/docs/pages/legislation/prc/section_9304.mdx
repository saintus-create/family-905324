# PRC § 9304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 7. District Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 9304

---

## Text

No director or other officer of the district shall be interested directly or indirectly in the sale of equipment, materials, or services to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
