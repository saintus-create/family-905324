# PRC § 3421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 7. Assessment and Collection of Charges

**Citation:** PRC § 3421

---

## Text

Every payment on a delinquent charge shall be applied as follows:

(a) First, to any interest due on the charge.

(b) Second, to any penalty imposed by this part.

(c) The balance, if any, to the charge itself.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 987, Sec. 3.)
