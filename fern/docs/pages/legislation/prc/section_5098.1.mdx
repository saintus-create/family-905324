# PRC § 5098.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.8. Federal Grants for Acquisition and Development Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 5098.1

---

## Text

All federal grants which result from the expenditure of state funds for Department of Parks and Recreation acquisition and development projects shall be deposited in the fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1065.)
