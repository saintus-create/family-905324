# PRC § 9814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 9814

---

## Text

At the hearing the directors shall make any changes in reference to the matters set forth in Section 9813 as they consider proper. The directors may exclude any part of the real property described in the petition from the proposed improvement district and may include additional real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
