# PRC § 13050.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 13050.5

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this division, districts governed by this division are subject to the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 16.)
