# PRC § 42810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42810

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the authority of a local agency to regulate persons or businesses that store, stockpile, process, or dispose of waste tires.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 717, Sec. 22.)
