# PRC § 40172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40172

---

## Text

“Processing” means the reduction, separation, recovery, conversion, or recycling of solid waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
