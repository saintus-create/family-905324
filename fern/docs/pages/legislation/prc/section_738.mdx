# PRC § 738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 2. State Board of Forestry and Fire Protection

**Citation:** PRC § 738

---

## Text

The Governor shall designate the chair of the board from among the members of the board. The person designated as the chair shall hold the office at the pleasure of the Governor. The board shall annually elect a vice chair from among its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 213, Sec. 5. (AB 2768) Effective January 1, 2011.)
