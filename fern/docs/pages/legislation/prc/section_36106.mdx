# PRC § 36106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 36106

---

## Text

“Ocean resources” means all living and nonliving resources found in the Pacific Ocean and its contiguous saline or brackish bays and estuaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1215, Sec. 1.)
