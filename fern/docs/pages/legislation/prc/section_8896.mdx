# PRC § 8896

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 5. California Orthometric Heights

**Citation:** PRC § 8896

---

## Text

The accuracy of derived COH88 values may be improved by applying a “local orthometric height correction” to the geoid height determined from the latest, applicable geoid model published by NGS.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 29.16. Effective January 1, 2006.)
