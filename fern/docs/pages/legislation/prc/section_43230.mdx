# PRC § 43230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 1.5. Local Enforcement Agency Grants

**Citation:** PRC § 43230

---

## Text

The board shall expend funds from the account, upon appropriation by the Legislature, for the making of grants to local enforcement agencies to carry out the solid waste facilities permit and inspection program pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 44001). The total amount of grants made by the board pursuant to this section shall not exceed, in any one fiscal year, one million five hundred thousand dollars ($1,500,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 20. Effective October 1, 1993.)
