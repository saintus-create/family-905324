# PRC § 36103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 36103

---

## Text

“High Seas” means offshore waters beyond 200 nautical miles from the coastline.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1215, Sec. 1.)
