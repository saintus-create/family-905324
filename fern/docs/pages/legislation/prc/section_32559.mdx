# PRC § 32559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.7. BALDWIN HILLS AND URBAN WATERSHEDS CONSERVANCY ACT > Chapter: 3. Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 32559

---

## Text

The conservancy may employ an executive officer and other staff to perform those functions that cannot be provided by volunteers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
