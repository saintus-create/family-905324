# PRC § 5097.93

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.75. Native American Historical, Cultural, and Sacred Sites

**Citation:** PRC § 5097.93

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall serve without compensation but shall be reimbursed their actual and necessary expenses.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1332.)
