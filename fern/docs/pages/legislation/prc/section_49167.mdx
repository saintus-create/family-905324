# PRC § 49167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49167

---

## Text

The validity of the bonds after their issuance shall not be questioned in any court except on the ground that the provisions of this chapter authorizing their issuance are unconstitutional, or that the required hearing regarding the formation of the district was not regularly held or proper notice of it was not given.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
