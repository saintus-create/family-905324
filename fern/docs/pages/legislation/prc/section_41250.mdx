# PRC § 41250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. City Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 8. Special Waste Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41250

---

## Text

The city special waste component shall describe existing waste handling and disposal practices for special wastes, including, but not limited to, asbestos and sewage sludge which is not hazardous waste. The component shall identify current and proposed programs to ensure the proper handling, reuse, and long-term disposal of special wastes. The component shall address the disposition of sewage sludge generated in the jurisdiction of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 145, Sec. 24. Effective June 19, 1990.)
