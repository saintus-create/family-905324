# PRC § 12200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. CALIFORNIA FOREST LEGACY PROGRAM ACT OF 2007 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Title

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Forest Legacy Program Act of 2007.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 657, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
