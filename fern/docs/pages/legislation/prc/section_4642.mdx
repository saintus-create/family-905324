# PRC § 4642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4642

---

## Text

“Reforestation” includes reforestation by natural means from seed and artificially by seeding or planting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
