# PRC § 43306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** PRC § 43306

---

## Text

The board shall keep and maintain records of its inspection, enforcement, training, and regulatory programs and of any other official action in accordance with regulations adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
