# PRC § 5079.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.1.5. California Heritage Fund > Article: 3. Functions of the State Office of Historic Preservation

**Citation:** PRC § 5079.27

---

## Text

The office may select and contract with other state agencies and with private entities and individuals to provide services necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter and Section 5020.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 749, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
