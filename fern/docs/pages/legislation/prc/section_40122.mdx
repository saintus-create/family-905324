# PRC § 40122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40122

---

## Text

“Disposal site” or “site” means the place, location, tract of land, area, or premises in use, intended to be used, or which has been used, for the disposal of solid wastes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 500, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2009.)
