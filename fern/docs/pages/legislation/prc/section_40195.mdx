# PRC § 40195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40195

---

## Text

“Solid waste handling” or “handling” means the collection, transportation, storage, transfer, or processing of solid wastes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
