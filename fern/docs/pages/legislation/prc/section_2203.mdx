# PRC § 2203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. The California Geological Survey

**Citation:** PRC § 2203

---

## Text

The State Geologist shall provide all requested and recommended information to the director who shall prepare a report for transmission to the Governor on or before the 15th day of September of each year. The report shall include a section reviewing the status of measures taken in the state to counter geologic hazards and a section reviewing the economic utilization and conservation of the state’s mineral resources and problems related thereto pursuant to Chapter 7.6 (commencing with Section 2650).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 999, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
