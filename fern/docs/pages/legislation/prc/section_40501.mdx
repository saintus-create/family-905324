# PRC § 40501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 40501

---

## Text

The Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery may hold any hearings and conduct any investigations in any part of the state necessary to carry out its powers and duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 21, Sec. 15. (SB 63) Effective January 1, 2010.)
