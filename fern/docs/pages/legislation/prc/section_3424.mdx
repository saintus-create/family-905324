# PRC § 3424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 7. Assessment and Collection of Charges

**Citation:** PRC § 3424

---

## Text

All charges assessed and levied shall be paid to the State Treasurer upon the order of the Controller. The Controller shall record the payment of any charge.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 529.)
