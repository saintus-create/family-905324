# PRC § 49143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 5. Taxation

**Citation:** PRC § 49143

---

## Text

For purposes of the district, the board of supervisors shall levy a tax of not more than fifteen cents ($0.15) on each one hundred dollars ($100) of taxable property of the district in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
