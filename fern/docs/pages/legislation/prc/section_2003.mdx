# PRC § 2003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 2003

---

## Text

“Survey,” in reference to the government of this state, means the California Geological Survey in the Department of Conservation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 521, Sec. 12. (SB 809) Effective January 1, 2018.)
