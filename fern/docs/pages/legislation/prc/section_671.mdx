# PRC § 671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of Conservation > Article: 2. State Mining and Geology Board

**Citation:** PRC § 671

---

## Text

The director shall have no power to amend or repeal any order, ruling, or directive of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1131.)
