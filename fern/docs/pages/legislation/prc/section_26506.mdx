# PRC § 26506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 26506

---

## Text

“District” means a geologic hazard abatement district created pursuant to this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
