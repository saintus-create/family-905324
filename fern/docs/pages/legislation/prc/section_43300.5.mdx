# PRC § 43300.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** PRC § 43300.5

---

## Text

The enforcement policies of this division shall be applied equally and without distinction to publicly owned or operated, and to privately owned or operated, solid waste facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 732, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1997.)
