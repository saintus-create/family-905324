# PRC § 22024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. THE VENTURA-LOS ANGELES MOUNTAIN AND COASTAL STUDY COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Organization and Membership

**Citation:** PRC § 22024

---

## Text

The commission shall select from among its members a chair and a vice chair.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 213, Sec. 17. (AB 2768) Effective January 1, 2011.)
