# PRC § 10242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 2. California Farmland Conservancy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 10242

---

## Text

The director shall review, and approve or disapprove, all requests for funding under this division.
