# PRC § 4597.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7.7. Working Forest Management Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 4597.21

## Text

This article does not affect the applicability of county rules adopted pursuant to Section 4516.5 nor any court decision made by a court of competent jurisdiction.

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 648, Sec. 1. (AB 904) Effective January 1, 2014.)
