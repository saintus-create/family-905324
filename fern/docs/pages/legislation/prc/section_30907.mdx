# PRC § 30907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20.4. WATERSHED, CLEAN BEACHES, AND WATER QUALITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30907

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to require state agencies to encourage and support both of the following:

(a) The development of coordinated and complementary strategies and solutions for watershed management across land ownership and agency jurisdictional boundaries.

(b) Coordinated program delivery from state and federal agencies to fit the needs of individual watersheds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
