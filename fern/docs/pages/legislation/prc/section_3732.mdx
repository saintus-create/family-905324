# PRC § 3732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3732

---

## Text

The core record shall show the depth, character, and fluid content of cores obtained, so far as determined.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1483.)
