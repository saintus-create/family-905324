# PRC § 7529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 2. Sale of Swamp and Overflowed Lands > Article: 2. Swamp and Overflowed Lands Sold Prior to September 19, 1939

**Citation:** PRC § 7529

---

## Text

Upon the filing with the commission of a copy of the final judgment of the court, the commission shall, if the judgment of the court cancels the location or certificate of purchase, enter an order of cancellation accordingly. But if by the judgment it is decreed that the person desiring to purchase the land is entitled to purchase it, the commission shall approve the location, or issue the certificate of purchase or other evidence of title, in accordance with the judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
