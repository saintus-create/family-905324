# PRC § 12249.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. CALIFORNIA FOREST LEGACY PROGRAM ACT OF 2007 > Chapter: 2. California Forest Legacy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 12249.6

---

## Text

The department shall review, and approve or disapprove, applications from landowners for the acquisition of conservation easements on forest lands or woodlands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 657, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
