# PRC § 21181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 6.5. Jobs and Economic Improvement Through Environmental Leadership Act of 2021

**Citation:** PRC § 21181

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to a project if the Governor does not certify the project as an environmental leadership development project eligible for streamlining under this chapter before January 1, 2032.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 60, Sec. 2. (SB 149) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, pursuant to Section 21189.3.)
