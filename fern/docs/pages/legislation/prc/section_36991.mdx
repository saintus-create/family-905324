# PRC § 36991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8. The California Ocean Resources Stewardship Act of 2000 > Article: 4. California Ocean Science Trust

**Citation:** PRC § 36991

---

## Text

The trust shall be subject to the Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law, Part 2 (commencing with Section 5110) of Division 2 of Title 1 of the Corporations Code. To the extent of any conflict between this chapter and the Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law, this division shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 516, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
