# PRC § 41220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. City Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 6. Education and Public Information Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41220

---

## Text

The city education and public information component shall describe to the board how the city will increase public awareness of, and participation in, recycling, source reduction, and composting programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
