# PRC § 5097.999

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.78. California Indian Heritage Center

**Citation:** PRC § 5097.999

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the department develop a California Indian Heritage Center for cultural preservation, learning and exchange, land stewardship, and a place to engage all visitors in celebrating the living cultures of California tribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 51, Sec. 28. (SB 854) Effective June 27, 2018.)
