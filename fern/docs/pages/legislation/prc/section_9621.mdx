# PRC § 9621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 3. Changing Name of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9621

---

## Text

A district may change its name by action of the board of supervisors of the principal county as provided by this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
