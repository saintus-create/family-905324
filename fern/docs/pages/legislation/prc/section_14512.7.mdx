# PRC § 14512.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14512.7

---

## Text

“Fund” means the California Beverage Container Recycling Fund established pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 14580.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1339, Sec. 4. Effective October 2, 1989.)
