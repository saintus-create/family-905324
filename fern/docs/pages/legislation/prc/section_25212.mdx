# PRC § 25212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 25212

---

## Text

Every two years the Governor shall designate a chair and vice chair of the commission from among its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 213, Sec. 18. (AB 2768) Effective January 1, 2011.)
