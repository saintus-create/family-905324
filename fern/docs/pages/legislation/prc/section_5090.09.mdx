# PRC § 5090.09

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.09

---

## Text

“System” means the state vehicular recreation areas, the California Statewide Motorized Trail, areas and trails within the state park system, and areas supported by the grant program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 270, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
