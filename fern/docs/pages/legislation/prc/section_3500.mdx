# PRC § 3500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 2. Wasting of Natural Gas

**Citation:** PRC § 3500

---

## Text

All persons, firms, corporations, and associations are prohibited from wilfully permitting natural gas wastefully to escape into the atmosphere.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
