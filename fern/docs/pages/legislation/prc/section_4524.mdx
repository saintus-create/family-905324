# PRC § 4524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4524

---

## Text

“License” means a license to engage in timber operations, issued pursuant to Article 6 (commencing with Section 4571) of this chapter.
