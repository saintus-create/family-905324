# PRC § 3405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 7. Assessment and Collection of Charges

**Citation:** PRC § 3405

---

## Text

The department shall prescribe the form and contents of all reports for making the charge or for other purposes to carry out the intent and provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 1002, Sec. 8. (AB 218) Effective September 30, 2024.)
