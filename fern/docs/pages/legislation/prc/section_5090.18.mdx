# PRC § 5090.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 2. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.18

---

## Text

In case of any vacancy in the membership of the commission, the appointing authority of the vacating member shall appoint a successor member for the unexpired portion of the term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 994, Sec. 11.)
