# PRC § 31013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 31013

---

## Text

“Nonprofit organization” means any private, nonprofit organization that qualifies under Section 501(c)(3) of the United States Internal Revenue Code of 1986.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 469, Sec. 8. (SB 785) Effective January 1, 2020.)
