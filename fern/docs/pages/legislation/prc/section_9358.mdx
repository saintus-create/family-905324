# PRC § 9358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 8. General District Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 9358

---

## Text

Nomination of candidates shall be in writing and signed by at least five landowners of the district. Nominations shall be filed with the county elections official of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 811, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2004.)
