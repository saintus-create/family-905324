# PRC § 40178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40178

---

## Text

“Pyrolysis” means the thermal decomposition of material at elevated temperatures in the absence or near absence of oxygen.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 678, Sec. 2. (AB 70) Effective January 1, 2026.)
