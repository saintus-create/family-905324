# PRC § 30244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 3. Coastal Resources Planning and Management Policies > Article: 5. Land Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 30244

---

## Text

Where development would adversely impact archaeological or paleontological resources as identified by the State Historic Preservation Officer, reasonable mitigation measures shall be required.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
