# PRC § 42014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Market Development Programs > Article: 3. Market Development Zone Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42014

---

## Text

The department may designate or redesignate recycling market development zones for persons applying for that designation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 258, Sec. 29. (SB 155) Effective September 23, 2021.)
