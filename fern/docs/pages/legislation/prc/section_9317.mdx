# PRC § 9317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 7. District Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 9317

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a vacancy in the office of a director who has been elected shall be filled pursuant to Section 1780 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 9178.5 by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1079.)
