# PRC § 5819.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 7.5. Sierra Nevada-Cascade Conservation Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 5819.6

## Text

An acquisition made pursuant to this chapter shall be from a willing seller.
