# PRC § 42984.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 20.5. Responsible Textile Recovery Act of 2024 > Article: 4. Producer Responsibility Plan for Covered Products

**Citation:** PRC § 42984.9

---

## Text

The PRO shall fully implement the approved plan within 12 months of the department’s approval of the plan pursuant to Section 42984.8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 864, Sec. 1. (SB 707) Effective January 1, 2025.)
