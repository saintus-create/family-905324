# PRC § 29764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 5. Resource Management Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 29764

---

## Text

Land use authority granted to the commission by this division is limited to the primary zone, and shall not preempt local government general plans for lands within the secondary zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 31. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
