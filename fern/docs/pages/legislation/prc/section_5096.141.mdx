# PRC § 5096.141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.69. California Parklands Act of 1980 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.141

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Parklands Act of 1980.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 250, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 1980, election. Operative December 1, 1980.)
