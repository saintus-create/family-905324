# PRC § 31257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 6. Coastal Resource Enhancement Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 31257

---

## Text

In connection with proposed coastal resource enhancement projects, the conservancy may fund up to three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000) of the cost of preparing coastal resource enhancement plans.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2003.)
