# PRC § 32630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 32630

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the San Diego River Conservancy Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 574, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
