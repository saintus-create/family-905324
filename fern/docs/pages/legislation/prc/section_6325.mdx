# PRC § 6325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Administration and Control of Swamp, Overflowed, Tide, or Submerged Lands, and Structures Thereon > Article: 2. Control of Structures

**Citation:** PRC § 6325

---

## Text

The authority granted under this chapter does not obviate the necessity for the applicant to obtain permisssion from the proper federal agencies to construct, alter, or maintain the structures herein authorized.
