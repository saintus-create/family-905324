# PRC § 13062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 13062

---

## Text

A majority of the votes cast shall be required to approve a proposition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
