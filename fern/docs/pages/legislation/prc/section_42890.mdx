# PRC § 42890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 17. California Tire Recycling Act > Article: 6. Use of Recycled Tire Products by State Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 42890

---

## Text

“Recycled tire product” means a product with not less than 50 percent of its total content derived from recycled used tires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 25. Effective March 30, 1990.)
