# PRC § 9062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 9062

---

## Text

The chief shall assist in the formation, organization and operation of resource conservation districts.
