# PRC § 48650.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 6. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 48650.2

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the board may collect the fees pursuant to the Fee Collection Procedures Law (Part 30 (commencing with Section 55001) of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1101, Sec. 6. Effective September 29, 1992.)
