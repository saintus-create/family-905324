# PRC § 700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 700

---

## Text

As used in this chapter:

(a) “Board” means the State Board of Forestry and Fire Protection.

(b) “Department” means the Department of Forestry and Fire Protection.

(c) “Director” means the Director of Forestry and Fire Protection.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 972, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
