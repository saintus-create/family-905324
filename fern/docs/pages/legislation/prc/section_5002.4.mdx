# PRC § 5002.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 1. State Park System

**Citation:** PRC § 5002.4

---

## Text

The department shall furnish a copy of the general plan for any unit of the state park system for which a plan has been prepared to any Member of the Legislature, upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 439, Sec. 8.)
