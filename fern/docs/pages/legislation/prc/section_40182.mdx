# PRC § 40182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40182

---

## Text

“Regional water board” means a California regional water quality control board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
