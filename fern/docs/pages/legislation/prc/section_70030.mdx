# PRC § 70030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 33. GLASS CONTAMINATION > Chapter: 1. The Clean Glass Recycling Act of 1990 > Article: 3. Penalties

**Citation:** PRC § 70030

---

## Text

In addition to other applicable civil or criminal penalties, any person convicted of a violation of this chapter is guilty of an infraction, which is punishable by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) per day that the violation continues. Each day of violation is a separate offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 879, Sec. 2.)
