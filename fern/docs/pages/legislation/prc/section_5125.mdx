# PRC § 5125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 2. County Museums, and Historical Records and Property

**Citation:** PRC § 5125

---

## Text

The board may conduct or assist study, investigation, or research in any department established in the museum.
