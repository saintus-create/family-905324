# PRC § 47102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Household Hazardous Substance Information and Collection > Article: 3. Household Hazardous Waste Management

**Citation:** PRC § 47102

---

## Text

The board shall designate a household hazardous waste coordinator to advise and assist local governments and other agencies which offer programs for household hazardous waste management.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 47100 by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 58. Effective March 30, 1990.)
