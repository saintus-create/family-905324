# PRC § 42295

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5.4. Plastic Trash Bags

**Citation:** PRC § 42295

---

## Text

Each supplier, manufacturer, and wholesaler required to provide a certification or any information pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to audit by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1076, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
