# PRC § 3316.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5.5. Subsidence

**Citation:** PRC § 3316.15

---

## Text

“Unit operator” means the person or persons designated by the unit agreement or in accordance with subdivisions (g) and (j) of Section 3322 as operator or operators of the unitized area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 73.)
