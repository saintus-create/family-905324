# PRC § 7958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 7. Correction of Incorrect Descriptions of Patented Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 7958

---

## Text

Any number of land owners whose lands are contiguous or would be affected by the decree may unite in one petition under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
