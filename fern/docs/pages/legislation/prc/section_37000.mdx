# PRC § 37000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NATURAL HERITAGE PRESERVATION TAX CREDIT ACT OF 2000 > Chapter: 1. Intent

**Citation:** PRC § 37000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the “Natural Heritage Preservation Tax Credit Act of 2000.”

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 113, Sec. 1. Effective July 10, 2000.)
