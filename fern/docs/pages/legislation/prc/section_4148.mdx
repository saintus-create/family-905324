# PRC § 4148

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 4.5. Rapid Disaster Response Act of 2005

**Citation:** PRC § 4148

---

## Text

The director shall draw upon eligible federal funds to augment any state funds appropriated by the Legislature for the purpose of replacing the department’s aging helicopter fleet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 290, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
