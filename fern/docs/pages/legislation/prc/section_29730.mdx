# PRC § 29730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 29730

---

## Text

“Restoration” means actions which return a degraded or deteriorated area to a level of increased productivity, environmental quality, or beneficial values.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
