# PRC § 13123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13123

---

## Text

The face value of refunding bonds exchanged for original bonds shall not exceed the face value of the original bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
