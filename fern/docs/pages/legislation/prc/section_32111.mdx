# PRC § 32111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Bonds and Notes > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PRC § 32111

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Treasurer shall not be deemed to have a conflict of interest by reason of acting as trustee pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
