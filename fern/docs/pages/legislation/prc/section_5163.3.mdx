# PRC § 5163.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 3. Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5163.3

---

## Text

The city or county shall maintain a file containing an up-to-date certificate for each person covered by Section 5163.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 638.)
