# PRC § 4641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4641

---

## Text

“Purchase area” means an area of forest land within which forest lands of sufficient acreage may be available and can be consolidated to make state forest units.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
