# PRC § 8834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 2. Topographical Mapping

**Citation:** PRC § 8834

---

## Text

All map production work to be undertaken pursuant to this chapter shall be in cooperation with the federal government. With the approval of the Department of General Services, the department may enter into agreements or contracts with the federal government or any of its agencies for performance of map production work in accordance with the general plan and program.
