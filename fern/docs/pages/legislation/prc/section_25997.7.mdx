# PRC § 25997.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 19. Fusion Research and Development Innovation Initiative

**Citation:** PRC § 25997.7

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 334, Sec. 2. (SB 80) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally operative on date prescribed by Section 25997.5. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 19, commencing with Section 25997.)
