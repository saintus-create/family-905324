# PRC § 40131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40131

---

## Text

“Enforcement program” means the regulations and procedures adopted by the board pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 43200) of Part 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
