# PRC § 25711.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 8.1. Electric Program Investment Charge Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 25711.8

---

## Text

For a project related to the deployment of a microgrid, a recipient of moneys awarded pursuant to this chapter shall not expend those moneys for the purchase of diesel generators, including diesel backup generators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 476, Sec. 2. (AB 1400) Effective January 1, 2018.)
