# PRC § 5808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 6.5. California Watershed Protection and Restoration Act

**Citation:** PRC § 5808

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Watershed Protection and Restoration Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 693, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
