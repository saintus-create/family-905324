# PRC § 3005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 3005

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, firm, association, corporation, or any other group or combination acting as a unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
