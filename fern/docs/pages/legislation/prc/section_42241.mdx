# PRC § 42241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5. Compost Market Program > Article: 2. Compost Market Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42241

---

## Text

The Department of Transportation shall use compost in place of, or to supplement, petroleum-based commercial fertilizers in the state’s highway landscape maintenance program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 238, Sec. 2. (AB 2411) Effective January 1, 2019.)
