# PRC § 7537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 2. Sale of Swamp and Overflowed Lands > Article: 2. Swamp and Overflowed Lands Sold Prior to September 19, 1939

**Citation:** PRC § 7537

---

## Text

Sections 7535 and 7536 do not apply to districts upon which controller’s warrants are outstanding, until after all of the warrants are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 154.)
