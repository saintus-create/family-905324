# PRC § 5877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 14. Southeast Los Angeles Cultural Center

**Citation:** PRC § 5877

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on July 1, 2032, and, as of January 1, 2033, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 45, Sec. 44. (AB 127) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 14, commencing with Section 5875.)
