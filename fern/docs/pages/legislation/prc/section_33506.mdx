# PRC § 33506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.5. COACHELLA VALLEY MOUNTAINS CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. Establishment and Purposes

**Citation:** PRC § 33506

---

## Text

The voting members of the conservancy’s governing board shall elect a chairperson, vice chairperson, and other officers as determined to be necessary, from among the persons designated in paragraphs (1), (2), (3), and (4) of subdivision (a) of Section 33503, to serve for one-year terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 676, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, pursuant to Sec. 13 of Ch. 676, as amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1286, Sec. 6.)
