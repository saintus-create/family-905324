# PRC § 4721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 6. Preservation of Big Trees

**Citation:** PRC § 4721

---

## Text

It is the policy of the state to preserve as far as possible the species Sequoia gigantea in the interest of conservation, both of soil and of the species itself, and for the benefit of present and future generations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
