# PRC § 4180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 7. Public Nuisances

**Citation:** PRC § 4180

---

## Text

An action to foreclose the lien shall be commenced by the Attorney General in the name of the people of the State of California within six months after the lien is filed and recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
