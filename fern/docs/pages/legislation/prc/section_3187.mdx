# PRC § 3187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3.5. Natural Gas Storage Wells

**Citation:** PRC § 3187

---

## Text

All materials provided to the division and approved by the supervisor to comply with Sections 3181, 3184, and 3185 shall be posted and available to the public on the Internet Web site of the division in a timely manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 673, Sec. 3. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2017.)
