# PRC § 30607.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 7. Development Controls > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30607.7

---

## Text

(a) A coastal development permit for sand replenishment requires the project applicant to provide onsite monitoring and supervision during the implementation of the permit.

(b) A permit subject to subdivision (a) may not be issued until the project applicant provides the issuing agency with a plan for onsite monitoring and supervision during the implementation of the permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 285, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
