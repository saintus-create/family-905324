# PRC § 48610.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48610.5

---

## Text

“Bulk oil” means oil sold and delivered in a single transaction in an amount greater than 55 gallons regardless of the size of the container or containers in which the oil is delivered.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
