# PRC § 3316.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5.5. Subsidence

**Citation:** PRC § 3316.12

## Text

“Working interest owner” means a person owning a working interest.
