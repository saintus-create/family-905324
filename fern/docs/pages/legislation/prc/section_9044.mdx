# PRC § 9044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Applicability

**Citation:** PRC § 9044

---

## Text

The Imperial Irrigation District may exercise the powers of a resource conservation district under this division in any area within its boundaries in which there is no resource conservation district organized and operating.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 831, Sec. 1.)
