# PRC § 25794.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 8.9. Strategic Reliability Reserve > Article: 5. Certification of Facilities

**Citation:** PRC § 25794.7

---

## Text

The commission shall maintain on its internet website a list of all certifications granted pursuant to this article for as long as the certifications are in effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 61, Sec. 6. (AB 205) Effective June 30, 2022.)
