# PRC § 42414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Retreaded Tire Program > Article: 2. Retreaded Tire Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42414

---

## Text

The number of retreaded tires purchased annually by the Department of General Services during each fiscal year shall be tabulated and forwarded to the board by August 31 every year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1038, Sec. 20. Effective September 29, 1996.)
