# PRC § 4692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 3. Reforestation Studies and Plans

**Citation:** PRC § 4692

---

## Text

The department may make surveys and prepare maps setting forth the areas of nonrestocking forest lands.
