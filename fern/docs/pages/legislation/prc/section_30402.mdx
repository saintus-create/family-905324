# PRC § 30402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 5. State Agencies > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PRC § 30402

---

## Text

All state agencies shall carry out their duties and responsibilities in conformity with this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
