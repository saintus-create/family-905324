# PRC § 6220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6220

---

## Text

Whenever authority is not vested in another officer, agency or commission to accept quitclaim deeds on behalf of the State, the authority is vested in the State Lands Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 641.)
