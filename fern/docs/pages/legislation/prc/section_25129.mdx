# PRC § 25129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25129

---

## Text

“Foreign” means any area exclusive of the 50 states and the District of Columbia.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1195.)
