# PRC § 4664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 4. Soquel Demonstration State Forest

**Citation:** PRC § 4664

---

## Text

The duties and authority of the department pursuant to this article shall only arise if the state acquires the property comprising the Soquel Demonstration State Forest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1338, Sec. 1.)
