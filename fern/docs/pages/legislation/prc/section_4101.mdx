# PRC § 4101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4101

---

## Text

“Person” includes any agency of the state, county, city, district, or other local public agency, and any individual, firm, association, partnership, business trust, corporation, limited liability company, or company.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 207. Effective January 1, 1995.)
