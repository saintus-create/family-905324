# PRC § 3210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 4. Regulation of Operations

**Citation:** PRC § 3210

---

## Text

The owner or operator of any well shall keep, or cause to be kept, a careful and accurate log, core record, and history of the drilling of the well.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
