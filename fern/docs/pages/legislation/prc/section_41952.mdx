# PRC § 41952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 9. Unlawful Acts > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 41952

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the right of any person to donate, sell, or otherwise dispose of his or her recyclable materials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
