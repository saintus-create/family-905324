# PRC § 25493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.9. Energy Systems > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25493

---

## Text

On or after January 1, 1979, no governmental agency shall commence construction on any new structure unless the new structure complies with Title 24 Standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 773.)
