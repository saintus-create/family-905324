# PRC § 8855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 3. Geodetic Datums and the California Spatial Reference Network

**Citation:** PRC § 8855

---

## Text

The official geodetic reference network for use within the State of California shall be the CSRN as defined by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 29.14. Effective January 1, 2006.)
