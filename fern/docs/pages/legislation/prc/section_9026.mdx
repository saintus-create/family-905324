# PRC § 9026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 9026

---

## Text

“Principal district” means the district which has the greater land area of two districts proposed to be consolidated.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
