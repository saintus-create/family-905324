# PRC § 30517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 6. Implementation > Article: 2. Procedure for Preparation, Approval, and Certification of Local Coastal Programs

**Citation:** PRC § 30517

---

## Text

The commission may extend, for a period of not to exceed one year, any time limitation established by this chapter for good cause.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 102, Sec. 6. (SB 856) Effective January 1, 2026.)
