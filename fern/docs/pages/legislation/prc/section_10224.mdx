# PRC § 10224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 10224

---

## Text

“Resource conservation district” means a resource conservation district established pursuant to Division 9 (commencing with Section 9001).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 502, Sec. 7. (AB 2964) Effective January 1, 2023.)
