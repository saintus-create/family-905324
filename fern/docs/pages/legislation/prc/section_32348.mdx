# PRC § 32348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.3. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. Governing Board

**Citation:** PRC § 32348

---

## Text

The board shall conduct business in accordance with the Bagley-Keene Open Meeting Act (Article 9 (commencing with Section 11120) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 37. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
