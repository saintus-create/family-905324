# PRC § 9863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** PRC § 9863

---

## Text

The directors of a district in which an improvement district exists may allow on terms that may be agreed upon any person to carry water through any conduit for the improvement of which the improvement district was formed and may cancel the right in the event that payments are not made in accordance with the agreed terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
