# PRC § 42443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8. Recycled Battery Programs > Article: 1. Lead-Acid Battery Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42443

---

## Text

The number of recycled lead-acid batteries purchased each year by the Department of General Services shall be tabulated and forwarded to the board on or before March 31 of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1038, Sec. 22. Effective September 29, 1996.)
