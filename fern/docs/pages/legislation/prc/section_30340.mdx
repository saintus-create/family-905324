# PRC § 30340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 30340

---

## Text

The commission shall manage and budget any funds that may be appropriated, allocated, granted, or in any other way made available to the commission for expenditure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 589, Sec. 133. Effective January 1, 1994.)
