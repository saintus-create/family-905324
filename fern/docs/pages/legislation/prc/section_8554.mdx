# PRC § 8554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Grazing Lands > Article: 2. Cooperation with Federal Agencies in Relation to Grazing Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 8554

---

## Text

The Department of Fish and Game is hereby designated the “official state agency engaged in the conservation or propagation of wild life” referred to in Section 9 of the act of Congress cited in Section 8551 of this code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 51.)
