# PRC § 14520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14520

---

## Text

“Recycling center” means an operation which is certified by the department and which accepts from consumers, and pays or provides the refund value pursuant to Section 14572 for, empty beverage containers intended to be recycled.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1339, Sec. 7. Effective October 2, 1989.)
