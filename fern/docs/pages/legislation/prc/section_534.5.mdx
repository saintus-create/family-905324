# PRC § 534.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 2. State Park and Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 534.5

---

## Text

The Director of the Department of Parks and Recreation shall act as secretary of the commission and shall appoint such assistants as are necessary to carry out this function. Such assistants shall be appointed from among the employees of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1179.)
