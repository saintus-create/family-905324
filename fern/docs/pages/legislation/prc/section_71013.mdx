# PRC § 71013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 34. ENVIRONMENTAL PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 71013

---

## Text

“Project” means an activity, the conduct of which requires an environmental permit from two or more environmental agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 419, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
