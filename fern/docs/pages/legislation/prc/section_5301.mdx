# PRC § 5301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 5. Park and Boulevard Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 5301

---

## Text

Any city or city and county may acquire and hold land for public parks, or public boulevards, or both, by purchase, or by condemnation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1240.)
