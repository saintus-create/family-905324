# PRC § 6238

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3.2. Sea Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 6238

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to preclude the application for funding of any project that would be eligible for funding under the terms of the National Sea Grant College and Program Act of 1966.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 293, Sec. 5. Effective August 18, 1997.)
