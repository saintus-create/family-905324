# PRC § 30805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 9. Judicial Review, Enforcement, and Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30805

---

## Text

Any person may maintain an action for the recovery of civil penalties provided for in Section 30820 or 30821.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1199, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
