# PRC § 50001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. WASTE MANAGEMENT FACILITIES

**Citation:** PRC § 50001.5

---

## Text

At the request of the board or any local governmental entity, the Attorney General shall bring an action to enforce this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1291, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1993.)
