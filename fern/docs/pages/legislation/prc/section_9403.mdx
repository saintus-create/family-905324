# PRC § 9403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 9. General Powers of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9403

---

## Text

The directors may accept gifts and grants of money from any source whatsoever to carry out the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
