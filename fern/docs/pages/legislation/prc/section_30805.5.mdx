# PRC § 30805.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 9. Judicial Review, Enforcement, and Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30805.5

---

## Text

Any action pursuant to Sections 30805 or 30822 to recover civil fines or penalties under this chapter shall be commenced not later than three years from the date on which the cause of action for the recovery is known or should have been known.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1199, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
