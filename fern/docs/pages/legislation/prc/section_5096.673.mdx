# PRC § 5096.673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.696. The California Clean Water, Clean Air, Safe Neighborhood Parks, and Coastal Protection Act of 2002 > Article: 6. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.673

---

## Text

Pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 16720) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code, the cost of bond issuance shall be paid out of the bond proceeds. These costs shall be shared proportionally by each program funded through this bond act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 40 at the March 5, 2002, election.)
