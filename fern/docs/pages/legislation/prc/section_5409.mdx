# PRC § 5409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Preservation of Public Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5409

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize the acquisition of public park property by purchase, exchange, condemnation, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1642.)
