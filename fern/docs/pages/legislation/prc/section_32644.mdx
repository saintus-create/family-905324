# PRC § 32644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32644

---

## Text

The jurisdiction of the conservancy is limited to the watershed of the San Diego River.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 448, Sec. 3. (SB 1027) Effective January 1, 2023.)
