# PRC § 9067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 9067

---

## Text

The chief may employ such clerical, technical, or other assistants as he deems necessary.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
