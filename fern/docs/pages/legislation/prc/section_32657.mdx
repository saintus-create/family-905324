# PRC § 32657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. San Diego River Conservancy Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 32657

---

## Text

The San Diego River Conservancy Fund is hereby created. Moneys in the fund shall be available, upon appropriation, for the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 574, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
