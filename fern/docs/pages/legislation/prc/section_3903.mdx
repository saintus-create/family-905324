# PRC § 3903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 1. Manner of Locating Mining Claims, Tunnel Rights, and Millsites

**Citation:** PRC § 3903

---

## Text

The relocation of any lode or placer mining location which is subject to relocation shall be made as an original location is required to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 259, Sec. 11.)
