# PRC § 4154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 5. Firewardens and Firefighting Personnel

**Citation:** PRC § 4154

---

## Text

Any person who fails to obey a summons which is authorized by Section 4153 is guilty of a violation of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
