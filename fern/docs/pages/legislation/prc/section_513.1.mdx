# PRC § 513.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 513.1

---

## Text

The department may provide space and facilities for schools to use for environmental education purposes within units of the state park system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 939.)
