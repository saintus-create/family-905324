# PRC § 49181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49181

---

## Text

The principal of, and interest on, the bonds shall be paid by the treasurer of the county in the manner prescribed by law for the principal of, and interest on, the bonds of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
