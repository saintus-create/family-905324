# PRC § 32025.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 32025.5

---

## Text

“Metropolitan statistical area” means any area so defined by the federal Office of Management and Budget.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
