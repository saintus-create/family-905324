# PRC § 32055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 2. Organization of the Authority: General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32055

---

## Text

The authority may, in accordance with Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, adopt the necessary rules and regulations to carry out this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 665, Sec. 3. Effective August 17, 1984.)
