# PRC § 8861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 3. Geodetic Datums and the California Spatial Reference Network

**Citation:** PRC § 8861

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not be construed to prohibit the appropriate use of other datums, including ITRF, and other geodetic reference networks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 29.14. Effective January 1, 2006.)
