# PRC § 21189.91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 7. Infrastructure Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 21189.91

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2034, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 60, Sec. 6. (SB 149) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 7, commencing with Section 21189.80.)
