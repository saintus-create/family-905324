# PRC § 21068

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21068

---

## Text

“Significant effect on the environment” means a substantial, or potentially substantial, adverse change in the environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1312.)
