# PRC § 14516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14516

---

## Text

“Place of business of the dealer” means the location at which a dealer sells, or offers for sale, beverages in beverage containers to consumers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1290, Sec. 2. Effective September 29, 1986.)
