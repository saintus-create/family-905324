# PRC § 5510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5510

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of the county having the largest area within the proposed district shall then, by resolution, fix a time and place for a hearing upon the question whether the petition should be granted and further proceedings taken under this article, and whether the property described in the petition will be benefited and should be included in the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
