# PRC § 30113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30113

---

## Text

“Prime agricultural land” means those lands defined in paragraph (1), (2), (3), or (4) of subdivision (c) of Section 51201 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 43, Sec. 5.1. Effective February 17, 1982.)
