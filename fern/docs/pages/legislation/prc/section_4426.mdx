# PRC § 4426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 6. Prohibited Activities > Article: 2. Prohibited Activities

**Citation:** PRC § 4426

---

## Text

A person shall not set a backfire, or cause a backfire to be set, except under the direct supervision or permission of a state or federal forest officer, unless it can be established that the setting of such backfire was necessary for the purpose of saving life or valuable property.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
