# PRC § 44208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Permit and Inspection Program > Article: 4. Development of Solid Waste Management Facilities on Indian Country

**Citation:** PRC § 44208

---

## Text

A cooperative agreement executed pursuant to this article shall be executed for the express benefit of the citizens of this state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 805, Sec. 4.)
