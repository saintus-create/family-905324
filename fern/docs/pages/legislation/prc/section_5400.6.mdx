# PRC § 5400.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2.5. Preservation of Public Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5400.6

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “operating entity” means the entity owning the park land and the facilities thereon.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1642.)
