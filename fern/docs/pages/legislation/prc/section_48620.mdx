# PRC § 48620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48620

---

## Text

“Recycled oil” means recycled oil, as defined in Section 25250.1 of the Health and Safety Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 880, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1999.)
