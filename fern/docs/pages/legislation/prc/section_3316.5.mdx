# PRC § 3316.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5.5. Subsidence

**Citation:** PRC § 3316.5

---

## Text

“Subsidence” means sinking, lowering, collapsing, compaction or other movement of the land whether covered by water or not.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 73.)
