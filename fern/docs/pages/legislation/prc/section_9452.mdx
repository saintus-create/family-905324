# PRC § 9452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 10. Property of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9452

---

## Text

The directors are hereby authorized and empowered to hold, use, acquire, manage, occupy and possess property of any kind, and may lease or sell it as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
