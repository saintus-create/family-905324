# PRC § 33005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. SANTA MONICA MOUNTAINS CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 33005

## Text

Federal grant funds shall be used to accomplish the purposes of this division to the maximum extent possible.
