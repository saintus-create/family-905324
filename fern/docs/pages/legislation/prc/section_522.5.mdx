# PRC § 522.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 1.5. Park Support Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 522.5

---

## Text

The park support organization is not a state agency or state body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 540, Sec. 2. (SB 1111) Effective January 1, 2017.)
