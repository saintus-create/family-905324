# PRC § 31115.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the State Coastal Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 31115.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the conservancy may undertake projects in the City of San Juan Capistrano pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 31150) and Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 31350).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 505, Sec. 2. Effective July 13, 1982.)
