# PRC § 5054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 4. Hostel Facilities

**Citation:** PRC § 5054

---

## Text

The department may acquire land for, develop, and maintain recreational trails to and between units of the state park system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 265.)
