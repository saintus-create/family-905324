# PRC § 3316.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5.5. Subsidence

**Citation:** PRC § 3316.3

---

## Text

“Field” means the same general surface area which is underlaid or reasonably appears to be underlaid by one or more pools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 73.)
