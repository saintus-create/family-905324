# PRC § 22013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. THE VENTURA-LOS ANGELES MOUNTAIN AND COASTAL STUDY COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 22013

---

## Text

“Region” means the Greater Los Angeles Metropolitan Region, comprised of Los Angeles and Ventura Counties.
