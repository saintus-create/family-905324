# PRC § 36101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 36101

---

## Text

“Advisory committee” means the California Ocean Resources Advisory Committee established pursuant to Section 36302.
