# PRC § 42801.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42801.6

---

## Text

“Baled tire” means either a whole or an altered tire that has been compressed and then secured with a binding material for the purpose of reducing its volume.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 838, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
