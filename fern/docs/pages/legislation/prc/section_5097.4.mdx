# PRC § 5097.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.7. Archaeological, Paleontological, and Historical Sites

**Citation:** PRC § 5097.4

---

## Text

No archaelogical program conducted by the Department of Parks and Recreation shall impair, impede or delay any state construction project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1136.)
