# PRC § 4332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 4. Restricted Areas

**Citation:** PRC § 4332

---

## Text

Whenever it is necessary in the interests of public peace or safety, the director, with the consent of the Governor, may order closed to camping, hunting, trapping, or the use of firearms, any area in any state park or state forest. The director shall post and enforce such closure order in such area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
