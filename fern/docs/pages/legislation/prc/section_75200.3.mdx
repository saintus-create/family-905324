# PRC § 75200.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 44. TRANSIT, AFFORDABLE HOUSING, AND SUSTAINABLE COMMUNITIES PROGRAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 75200.3

---

## Text

Moneys appropriated to the program pursuant to Section 39719 or 39719.4 of the Health and Safety Code may be used by a state agency or department designated by a program administrator pursuant to Section 75200.2 for that state agency’s or department’s support and local assistance costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 68, Sec. 30. (AB 179) Effective July 13, 2026.)
