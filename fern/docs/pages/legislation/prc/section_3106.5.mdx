# PRC § 3106.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 3106.5

---

## Text

Acting with the approval of the director, the supervisor may annually expend, from the amount appropriated to the division, up to ten thousand dollars ($10,000) to support activities at the West Kern Oil Museum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 731, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
