# PRC § 48622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48622

---

## Text

“Used oil collection center” means a business, governmental entity, or nonprofit organization which accepts used lubricating oil from the public and which is exempt from hazardous waste facility permit requirements pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 25250.11 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
