# PRC § 35103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 35103

---

## Text

“Authority” means the Santa Clara Valley Open-Space Authority created pursuant to this division in the County of Santa Clara.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 99, Sec. 4. (SB 422) Effective January 1, 2016.)
