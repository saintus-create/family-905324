# PRC § 9406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 9. General Powers of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9406

---

## Text

The directors may take conveyances, leases, contracts, or other assurances for all property acquired by the district, in the name, and for the uses and purposes, of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
