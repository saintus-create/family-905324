# PRC § 4050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 3. CAL-FIRE Infrastructure Projects Revolving Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 4050

---

## Text

As used in this article, “fund” shall mean the CAL-FIRE Infrastructure Projects Revolving Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 31, Sec. 20. (SB 85) Effective June 27, 2019.)
