# PRC § 21061.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21061.2

---

## Text

“Land evaluation and site assessment” means a decisionmaking methodology for assessing the potential environmental impact of state and local projects on agricultural land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 812, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
