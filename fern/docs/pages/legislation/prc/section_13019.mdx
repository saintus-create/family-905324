# PRC § 13019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13019

---

## Text

“Voter” means a voter, as defined in Section 359 of the Elections Code, who resides within the boundaries of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 140, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
