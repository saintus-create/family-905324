# PRC § 25357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 4.5. Petroleum Supply and Pricing

**Citation:** PRC § 25357

---

## Text

The commission shall obtain and analyze monthly production reports prepared by the State Oil and Gas Supervisor pursuant to Section 3227.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1055, Sec. 4. Effective September 26, 1980.)
