# PRC § 3701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 4. Geothermal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 3701

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “geothermal resources” shall mean geothermal resources as defined in Section 6903 of this code.
