# PRC § 4695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 3. Reforestation Studies and Plans

**Citation:** PRC § 4695

---

## Text

The department may collect the additional seed required, increase the capacity of state nurseries, purchase the necessary equipment and employ the necessary personnel to effectuate the purposes of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
