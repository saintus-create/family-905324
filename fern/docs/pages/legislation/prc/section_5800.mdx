# PRC § 5800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 6. Watershed Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 5800

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “public entity” means any county, city, or public district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 127.)
