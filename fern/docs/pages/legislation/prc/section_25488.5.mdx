# PRC § 25488.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.9. Energy Systems > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25488.5

---

## Text

“Building standard” means a building standard as defined in Section 18909 of the Health and Safety Code which is adopted by the commission.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152.)
