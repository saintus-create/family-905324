# PRC § 5163.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 3. Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5163.4

---

## Text

Nothing in Sections 5163 to 5163.3, inclusive, shall prevent the city or county from requiring more extensive or more frequent examinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 638.)
