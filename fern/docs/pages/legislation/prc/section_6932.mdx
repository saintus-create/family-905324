# PRC § 6932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 6. Extraction of Sodium Chloride From Specified Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6932

---

## Text

Any right granted under this article to extract sodium chloride from State lands shall terminate upon the expiration of 99 years from the date of the issuance of the patent unless sooner terminated as herein otherwise provided.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
