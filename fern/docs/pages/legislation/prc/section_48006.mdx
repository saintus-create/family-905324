# PRC § 48006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Finances > Article: 2. Management of the Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 48006

---

## Text

The board may exempt from all fees any operator of a solid waste landfill that receives less than a monthly average of five tons per operating day of solid waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
