# PRC § 5593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Districts > Article: 3. Regional Park, Park and Open-Space, and Open-Space Districts

**Citation:** PRC § 5593

---

## Text

All matters and things necessary for the proper administration of the affairs of districts which are not provided for in this article shall be provided for by the board of directors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
