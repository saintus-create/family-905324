# PRC § 29400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. SUISUN MARSH PRESERVATION > Chapter: 5. Responsibilities of the Commission and Local Agencies > Article: 1. Local Protection Program

**Citation:** PRC § 29400

---

## Text

The county shall prepare the local protection program for the marsh. The local protection program shall be consistent with the provisions of this division and the policies of the protection plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
