# PRC § 2205.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 2. The California Geological Survey

**Citation:** PRC § 2205.1

---

## Text

Division 1 (commencing with Section 500) and this division shall not be construed as abridging the authorized geologic functions of other state agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 236, Sec. 5. (SB 831) Effective January 1, 2026.)
