# PRC § 48002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Finances > Article: 2. Management of the Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 48002

---

## Text

The state board shall adopt rules and regulations to carry out Section 48000, including, but not limited to, provisions governing collections, reporting, refunds, and appeals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
