# PRC § 5819.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 7.5. Sierra Nevada-Cascade Conservation Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 5819.7

---

## Text

All regulations, criteria, and procedural guides that the secretary adopts to implement this chapter are exempt from Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 15. Effective August 16, 2004.)
