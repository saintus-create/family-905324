# PRC § 5090.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.13

---

## Text

“Monitoring program” means a program adopted by the department that provides periodic evaluations of the condition of resources and informs adaptive management within state vehicular recreation areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 459, Sec. 3. (SB 249) Effective January 1, 2018.)
