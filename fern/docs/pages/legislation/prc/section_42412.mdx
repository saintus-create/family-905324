# PRC § 42412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Retreaded Tire Program > Article: 2. Retreaded Tire Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42412

---

## Text

On or before July 1, 1991, and to the extent that existing stock shall be utilized first, all tires for use on state vehicles issued for short-term use through Fleet Administration shall, at the next required installation of tires, be equipped with retreaded tires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
