# PRC § 43215.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 1. Local Enforcement Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 43215.1

---

## Text

The board may, upon the written request of an enforcement agency, provide legal counsel for purposes of compliance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 952, Sec. 11. Effective October 16, 1995.)
