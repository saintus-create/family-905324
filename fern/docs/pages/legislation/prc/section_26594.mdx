# PRC § 26594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Finances > Article: 2. Financial Assistance

**Citation:** PRC § 26594

## Text

A district may repay any financial assistance accepted pursuant to Section 26591.
