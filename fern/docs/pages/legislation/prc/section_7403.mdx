# PRC § 7403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 1. School Lands and Indemnity Lands > Article: 2. Indemnity Land Selections

**Citation:** PRC § 7403

---

## Text

The commission is the general agent of the state for the selection of indemnity lands in lieu of the losses sustained by the state to its school land grants.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 311, Sec. 7. (SB 1472) Effective January 1, 2021.)
