# PRC § 22055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. THE VENTURA-LOS ANGELES MOUNTAIN AND COASTAL STUDY COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 22055

---

## Text

The existence of the commission shall terminate on the 61st day after the final adjournment of the 1972 Regular Session of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1556.)
