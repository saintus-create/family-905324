# PRC § 41373

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. County Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 4. Recycling Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41373

---

## Text

The county recycling component shall describe new facilities, and expansion of existing facilities, which will be needed to implement the recycling component.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
