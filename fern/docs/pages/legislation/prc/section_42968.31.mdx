# PRC § 42968.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 19.5. Carpet Producer Responsibility Program > Article: 3. Producer Responsibility Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 42968.31

---

## Text

The producer responsibility organization shall implement the approved producer responsibility plan within 12 months of the department’s approval of the producer responsibility plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 675, Sec. 1. (AB 863) Effective January 1, 2025.)
