# PRC § 42465.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.5. Electronic Waste Recycling > Article: 4. Manufacturer Responsibility

**Citation:** PRC § 42465.3

---

## Text

A manufacturer of a covered electronic device, as defined in subparagraph (A) of paragraph (1) of subdivision (g) of Section 42463, shall comply with the notification requirements of subdivision (d) of Section 25214.10.1 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 370, Sec. 16. (SB 1215) Effective January 1, 2023. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Sections 42485 and 42486.)
