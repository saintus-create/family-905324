# PRC § 75032.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 3. Flood Control

**Citation:** PRC § 75032.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13340 of the Government Code, the funds allocated in Sections 75031 and 75032 are continuously appropriated to the department for the purposes of those sections.
