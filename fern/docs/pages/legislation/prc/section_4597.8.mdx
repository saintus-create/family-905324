# PRC § 4597.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7.7. Working Forest Management Plan

**Citation:** PRC § 4597.8

---

## Text

The working forest landowner may take actions that do not substantially deviate from the approved plan without the submission of an amendment, but those actions shall be subsequently reported to the department. The board shall specify, by regulation, those nonsubstantial deviations that may be taken. The board shall specify the requirements for reporting those deviations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 648, Sec. 1. (AB 904) Effective January 1, 2014.)
