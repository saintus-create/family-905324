# PRC § 10237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 2. California Farmland Conservancy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 10237

---

## Text

The director shall not disburse any grant funds for easement or fee title acquisitions unless the applicant, and in the case of an easement acquisition grant, the seller, agrees to restrict the use of the land in perpetuity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 502, Sec. 17. (AB 2964) Effective January 1, 2023.)
