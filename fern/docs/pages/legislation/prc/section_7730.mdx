# PRC § 7730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 2. Payments, Certificates of Purchase, and Patents

**Citation:** PRC § 7730

---

## Text

The patent or deed shall then be signed by the Governor, attested by the Secretary of State, sealed with the Great Seal of the State of California, and countersigned by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 742, Sec. 13. (SB 1493) Effective January 1, 2019.)
