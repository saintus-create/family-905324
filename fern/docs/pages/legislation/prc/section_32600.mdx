# PRC § 32600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.8. SAN GABRIEL AND LOWER LOS ANGELES RIVERS AND MOUNTAINS CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 32600

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the San Gabriel and Lower Los Angeles Rivers and Mountains Conservancy Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 789, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
