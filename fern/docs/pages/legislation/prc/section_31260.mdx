# PRC § 31260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 6. Coastal Resource Enhancement Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 31260

---

## Text

As part of an approved coastal resource enhancement project, the conservancy may fund the costs of land acquisition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2003.)
