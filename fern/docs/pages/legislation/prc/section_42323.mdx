# PRC § 42323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5.5. Plastic Packaging Containers > Article: 3. Penalties, Regulations, and Report

**Citation:** PRC § 42323

---

## Text

Proprietary information included in part of a report or certificate submitted to the board pursuant to this chapter shall not be made available to the general public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 769, Sec. 1.)
