# PRC § 7926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 6. Contests

**Citation:** PRC § 7926

---

## Text

Unless the party contestant commences his action within 60 days after the order of reference is made, his rights in the premises and under his application cease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
