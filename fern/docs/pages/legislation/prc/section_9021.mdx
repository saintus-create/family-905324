# PRC § 9021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 9021

---

## Text

“District” or “soil conservation district” means a resource conservation district.
