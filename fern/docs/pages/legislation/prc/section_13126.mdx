# PRC § 13126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13126

---

## Text

When bonds are made callable a statement to that effect shall be set forth on the face of the bond.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
