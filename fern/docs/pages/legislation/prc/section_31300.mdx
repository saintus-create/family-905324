# PRC § 31300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 7. Urban Waterfront Restoration

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Urban Waterfront Restoration Act of 1981“.
