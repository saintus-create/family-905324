# PRC § 33327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Sierra Nevada Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 33327

---

## Text

The board shall establish and maintain a headquarters office within the region. The conservancy may rent or own real and personal property and equipment pursuant to applicable statutes and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
