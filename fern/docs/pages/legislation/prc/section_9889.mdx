# PRC § 9889

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 5. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** PRC § 9889

---

## Text

Improvement district warrants shall be paid only out of the proceeds of an improvement district assessment levied upon and collected from the real property within the improvement district for improvement purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
