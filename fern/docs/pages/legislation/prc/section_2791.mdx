# PRC § 2791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 6. Areas of Statewide or Regional Significance

**Citation:** PRC § 2791

---

## Text

The board shall seek the recommendations of concerned federal, state, and local agencies, educational institutions, civic and public interest organizations, and private organizations and individuals in the identification of areas of statewide and regional significance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1131.)
