# PRC § 35000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. COASTAL RESOURCES AND ENERGY ASSISTANCE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Coastal Resources and Energy Assistance Act.
