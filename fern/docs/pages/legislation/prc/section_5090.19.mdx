# PRC § 5090.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 2. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.19

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall elect a chairperson from their number who shall serve as chairperson for one year and until his or her successor is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 563, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003.)
