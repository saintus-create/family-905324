# PRC § 25128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25128

---

## Text

“Refinery” means any industrial plant, regardless of capacity, processing crude oil feedstock and manufacturing oil products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1195.)
