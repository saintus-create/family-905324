# PRC § 7724

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 2. Payments, Certificates of Purchase, and Patents

**Citation:** PRC § 7724

---

## Text

All such sales shall, when the deed or assignment is recorded by the county recorder, be reported by him to the commission, to be entered in its books.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
