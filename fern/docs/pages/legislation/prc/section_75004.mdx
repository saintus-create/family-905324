# PRC § 75004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 75004

---

## Text

It is the intent of the people that investment of public funds pursuant to this division should result in public benefits.

(History: Added November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 84.)
