# PRC § 5096.178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.69. California Parklands Act of 1980 > Article: 5. Coastal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.178

---

## Text

(a) An amount, not to exceed nine hundred thousand dollars ($900,000) in the aggregate, shall be available for appropriation during the 1980–81, 1981–82, and 1982–83 fiscal years, in amounts to be determined in each annual appropriation, from funds available pursuant to category (2) of subdivision (c) of Section 5096.151, in the manner provided in Section 5096.206, to the State Coastal Conservancy for expenditure for the administration of Sections 5096.173 to 5096.177, inclusive; provided, however, that not more than three hundred fifty thousand dollars ($350,000) may be appropriated in any one such fiscal year.

(b) An amount, not to exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000) in the aggregate shall be available for appropriation commencing with the 1980–81 fiscal year from funds available pursuant to category (2) of subdivision (c) of Section 5096.151, in the manner provided in Section 5096.206, for projects in San Francisco Bay; and an amount, not to exceed five million dollars ($5,000,000) in the aggregate, shall be available, for projects in the Santa Monica Mountains Zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 250, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 1980, election. Operative December 1, 1980.)
