# PRC § 42647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 12.6. Schoolsite Source Reduction and Recycling Assistance Program > Article: 6. Grants

**Citation:** PRC § 42647

---

## Text

The board may enter into an interagency agreement with the State Department of Education or other state agencies to implement this chapter, Part 4 (commencing with Section 71300) of Division 34, and Sections 33541 and 51226.4 of the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 665, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2004.)
