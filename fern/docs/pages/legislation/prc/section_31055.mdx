# PRC § 31055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Declaration of State Policy and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 31055

---

## Text

No funds may be expended or granted under this division, unless and until such funds are appropriated by the Legislature.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1441.)
