# PRC § 42752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 15. Newsprint > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42752

---

## Text

“Deink” or “deinking old newspapers” means a process in which old newspaper is mixed with water, the paper fibers are separated to form a paper pulp, and the pulp is cleaned to remove contaminants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 23. Effective March 30, 1990.)
