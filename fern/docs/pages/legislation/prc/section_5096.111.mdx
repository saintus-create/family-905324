# PRC § 5096.111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.68. Nejedly-Hart State, Urban,and Coastal Park Bond Act of 1976

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.111

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Nejedly-Hart State, Urban, and Coastal Park Bond Act of 1976.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 259. Approved in Proposition 2 at the November 2, 1976, election.)
