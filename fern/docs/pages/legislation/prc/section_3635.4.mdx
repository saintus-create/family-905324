# PRC § 3635.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3.5. Unit Operation > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3635.4

---

## Text

“Field” means the same general surface area which is underlaid or reasonably appears to be underlaid by one or more pools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1673.)
