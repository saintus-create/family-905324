# PRC § 42811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 16. Waste Tires > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42811

---

## Text

The board may delegate specific powers and authority in this chapter to enforcement agencies, as defined in Section 40130, including any of the following:

(a) Review of operation plans submitted pursuant to regulations adopted under subdivision (a) of Section 42821.

(b) Inspection of permitted facilities.

(c) Enforcement of waste tire facility permits.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 717, Sec. 22.)
