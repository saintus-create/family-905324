# PRC § 3185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3.5. Natural Gas Storage Wells

**Citation:** PRC § 3185

---

## Text

The division shall perform unannounced random onsite inspections of some gas storage wells annually. The results shall be posted and available to the public on the division’s Internet Web site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 673, Sec. 3. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2017.)
