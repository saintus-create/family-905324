# PRC § 5096.964

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.699. Disaster Preparedness and Flood Prevention Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 17. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.964

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold pursuant to this chapter shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 33, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1E at the November 7, 2006, election.)
