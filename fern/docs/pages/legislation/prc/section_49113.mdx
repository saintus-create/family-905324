# PRC § 49113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 49113

---

## Text

The notice shall describe the territory, or shall specify the exterior boundaries of the territory, proposed to be organized into a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
