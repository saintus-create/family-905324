# PRC § 13190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Alternative Depositary of District Money

**Citation:** PRC § 13190

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are alternative or supplemental to those providing for the use of the county treasury.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
