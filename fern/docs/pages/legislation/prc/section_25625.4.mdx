# PRC § 25625.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.2. Climate Innovation Program

**Citation:** PRC § 25625.4

---

## Text

Recipients of a financial incentive awarded pursuant to this chapter shall be a California-headquartered company for the entire term of the financial incentive and for 10 years after the financial incentive ends.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 251, Sec. 11. (AB 209) Effective September 6, 2022.)
