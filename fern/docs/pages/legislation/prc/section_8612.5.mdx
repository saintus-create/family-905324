# PRC § 8612.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. KAPILOFF LAND BANK ACT > Chapter: 2. Land Bank Fund

**Citation:** PRC § 8612.5

---

## Text

The trustee is authorized to manage and improve real property for purposes of providing open space, habitat for plants and animals, and public access.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 286, Sec. 1.)
