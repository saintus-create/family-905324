# PRC § 48676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 8. Reporting

**Citation:** PRC § 48676

---

## Text

The board shall make available on its Internet Web site an annual report that includes the accumulated industrial and lubricating oil sales and used oil recycling rates.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 504, Sec. 15. (SB 579) Effective January 1, 2011.)
