# PRC § 29700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. DELTA PROTECTION ACT OF 1992 > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** PRC § 29700

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Johnston-Baker-Andal-Boatwright Delta Protection Act of 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 898, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
