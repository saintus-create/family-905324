# PRC § 13018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13018

---

## Text

“Owner” means the owner of land as shown on the last equalized county assessment roll.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
