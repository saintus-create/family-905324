# PRC § 3306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5. Unreasonable Waste of Gas

**Citation:** PRC § 3306

---

## Text

Upon the conclusion of the hearing, the supervisor shall determine whether or not there is an unreasonable waste of gas in the field, in existence or threatened, and shall also determine the extent to which the waste of gas, occurring or threatened, is unreasonable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
