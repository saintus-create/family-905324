# PRC § 9902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 6. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** PRC § 9902

---

## Text

Real property on which the amount of the improvement district assessment has been paid pursuant to Section 9901 shall not be subject to the annual installments of the assessments levied for the purposes of the improvement, but it shall be and remain liable for any assessments levied for operation and for any supplemental or additional improvement district assessments levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
