# PRC § 25952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 11. Gas Appliances > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25952

---

## Text

“Intermittent ignition device” means an ignition device which is actuated only when the gas appliance is in operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1411.)
