# PRC § 5640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.3. The Statewide Park Development and Community Revitalization Act of 2008

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Statewide Park Development and Community Revitalization Act of 2008.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 623, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
