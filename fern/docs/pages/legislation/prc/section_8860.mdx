# PRC § 8860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SURVEYING AND MAPPING > Chapter: 3. Geodetic Datums and the California Spatial Reference Network

**Citation:** PRC § 8860

---

## Text

The use of the NAD83, NATRF2022, PATRF2022, NAVD88, NAPGD2022, and CSRN by any person, firm, or governmental agency is optional.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 111, Sec. 17. (SB 566) Effective January 1, 2024.)
