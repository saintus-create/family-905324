# PRC § 25103.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25103.7

---

## Text

“Jurisdiction of the San Francisco Bay Conservation and Development Commission” means the area defined in Section 66610 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1155.)
