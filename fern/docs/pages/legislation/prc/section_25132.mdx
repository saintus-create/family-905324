# PRC § 25132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25132

---

## Text

“Load management” means any utility program or activity that is intended to reshape deliberately a utility’s load duration curve.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1375.)
