# PRC § 31178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4.6. Santa Ana River Conservancy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 31178

---

## Text

To the extent feasible, in carrying out the purposes of this chapter, the conservancy shall utilize the services of the California Conservation Corps and Community Conservation Corps, as defined in Section 14507.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 562, Sec. 1. (SB 1390) Effective January 1, 2015.)
