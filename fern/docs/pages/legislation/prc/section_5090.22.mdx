# PRC § 5090.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 2. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.22

---

## Text

The chairperson of the commission may appoint committees composed of members of the commission and prescribe the jurisdiction of each.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 563, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2003.)
