# PRC § 5090.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 2. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.17

---

## Text

(a) The terms of the members of the commission shall be four years.

(b) Appointments to the commission shall comply with the qualifications for membership specified in Sections 5090.15 and 5090.16.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 563, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2003.)
