# PRC § 42499.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.7. Cell Phone Recycling Act of 2004 > Article: 7. Repeal

**Citation:** PRC § 42499.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on September 30, 2027, and, as of January 1, 2028, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 351, Sec. 3. (AB 2440) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 8.7, commencing with Section 42490.)
