# PRC § 49524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Solid Waste Enterprises > Article: 3. Continuation of Services

**Citation:** PRC § 49524

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 49523, a solid waste enterprise may not waive the right to continue to provide solid waste handling services as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 811, Sec. 6. Effective September 24, 1998.)
