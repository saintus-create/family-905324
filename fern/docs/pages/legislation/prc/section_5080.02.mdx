# PRC § 5080.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.2. Operation and Management of State Park System Properties > Article: 1. Concessions

**Citation:** PRC § 5080.02

---

## Text

As used in this article, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

(a) “Board” means the State Public Works Board.

(b) “Commission” means the State Park and Recreation Commission.

(c) “Contract” means a contract for the construction, maintenance, and operation of a concession.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1487, Sec. 7. Effective September 28, 1982.)
