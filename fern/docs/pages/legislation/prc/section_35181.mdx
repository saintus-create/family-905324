# PRC § 35181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 6. Coyote Valley Conservation Program

**Citation:** PRC § 35181

---

## Text

The authority may establish and administer the Coyote Valley Conservation Program to address the resource and recreational goals of the Coyote Valley, as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 356, Sec. 1. (AB 948) Effective January 1, 2020.)
