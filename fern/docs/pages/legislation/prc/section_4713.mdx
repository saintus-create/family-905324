# PRC § 4713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 5. Forest Insect and Plant Disease Control

**Citation:** PRC § 4713

---

## Text

Pine beetles and other insect pests or plant diseases which are harmful, detrimental and injurious to timber and forest growth are a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
