# PRC § 4103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4103

---

## Text

“Forest fire” means a fire burning uncontrolled on lands covered wholly or in part by timber, brush, grass, grain, or other flammable vegetation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
