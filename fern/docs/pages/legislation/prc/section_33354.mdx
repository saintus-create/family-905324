# PRC § 33354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 33354

---

## Text

Proceeds from a lease, rental, sale, exchange, or transfer of an interest or option in real property, and all other income, shall be deposited in the fund for expenditure for the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
