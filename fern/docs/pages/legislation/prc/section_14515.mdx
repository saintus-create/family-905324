# PRC § 14515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14515

---

## Text

“Other beverage container” means a beverage container which has a body consisting of metal, glass, plastic, other materials, or a combination of these, but which is not an aluminum, bimetal, glass, or plastic beverage container.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1259, Sec. 2. Effective October 11, 1993.)
