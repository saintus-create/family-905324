# PRC § 4416

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 6. Prohibited Activities > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 4416

---

## Text

This chapter does not authorize a county firewarden, a firefighter, or a county officer to obligate the state for the payment of any money.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 72. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
