# PRC § 9081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 3. Funds and Expenditures

**Citation:** PRC § 9081

---

## Text

The commission may receive contributions from the United States, public districts, resource conservation districts, public agencies, or persons and may use such contributions for the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
