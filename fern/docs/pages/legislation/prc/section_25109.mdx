# PRC § 25109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25109

---

## Text

“Energy” means work or heat that is, or may be, produced from any fuel or source whatsoever.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
