# PRC § 42983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 20. Product Stewardship for Carpets

**Citation:** PRC § 42983

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to review any federal law that has the purpose of managing postconsumer carpet in a manner consistent with this chapter and to consider the extent to which the program created by that federal law will, at a minimum, achieve the same levels of landfill diversion and recycling of postconsumer carpet in California as this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 681, Sec. 2. (AB 2398) Effective January 1, 2011. Conditionally inoperative pursuant to Section 42983.5.)
