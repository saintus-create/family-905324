# PRC § 7732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 2. Payments, Certificates of Purchase, and Patents

**Citation:** PRC § 7732

---

## Text

The commission shall record all patents in books to be kept in its office for that purpose, and then deliver them to persons entitled thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
