# PRC § 42395.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6.6. Reusable or Refillable Propane Cylinders

**Citation:** PRC § 42395.1

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 2028, only reusable or refillable propane cylinders shall be sold or offered for sale in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 466, Sec. 1. (SB 1280) Effective January 1, 2025.)
