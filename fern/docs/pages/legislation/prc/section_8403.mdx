# PRC § 8403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Conveyances by Counties for National Park Purposes

**Citation:** PRC § 8403

---

## Text

The boards of supervisors of the several counties may do and perform all acts that may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
