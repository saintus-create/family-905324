# PRC § 6204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6204

---

## Text

The commission shall, when required, survey and mark the boundary lines of counties and cities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
