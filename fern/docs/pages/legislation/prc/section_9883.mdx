# PRC § 9883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 5. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** PRC § 9883

---

## Text

Improvement district warrants shall bear interest at the rate fixed at the time of the levy of the improvement district assessment, and the interest may be made payable semiannually.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
