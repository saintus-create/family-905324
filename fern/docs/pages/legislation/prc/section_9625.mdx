# PRC § 9625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 5. District Reorganization > Article: 3. Changing Name of District

**Citation:** PRC § 9625

---

## Text

If the action of the board of supervisors on this request is negative, they shall forward a copy of the resolution to the board of directors initiating the request.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
