# PRC § 4582.75

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7. Timber Harvesting

**Citation:** PRC § 4582.75

---

## Text

The rules adopted by the board and the provisions of this chapter shall be the only criteria employed by the director when reviewing timber harvesting plans pursuant to Section 4582.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 900, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2004.)
