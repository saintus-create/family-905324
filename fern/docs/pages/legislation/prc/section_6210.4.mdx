# PRC § 6210.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6210.4

---

## Text

No lands owned by the State which lands front upon or are near to any lake, navigable stream or other body of navigable water, convenient access to which is not provided by public road or roads, or otherwise, shall ever be sold, leased or rented, without reserving to the people of the State an easement across the lands for convenient access to such waters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6210.3 (as amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 887) by Stats. 1949, Ch. 269.)
