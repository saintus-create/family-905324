# PRC § 14410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA CONSERVATION CORPS > Chapter: 4. Forestry Corps Program

**Citation:** PRC § 14410

---

## Text

No later than July 1, 2019, the director shall establish a forestry corps program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 635, Sec. 2. (AB 2126) Effective January 1, 2019.)
