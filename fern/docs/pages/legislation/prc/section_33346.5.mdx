# PRC § 33346.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.3. SIERRA NEVADA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 33346.5

---

## Text

The conservancy may apply for and receive grants to carry out the purposes of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 726, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
