# PRC § 4656.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 3. State Forests

**Citation:** PRC § 4656.3

---

## Text

Any person who violates the rules and regulations pertaining to the state forests established by the board is guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction shall be punished by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 341. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
