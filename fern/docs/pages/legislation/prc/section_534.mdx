# PRC § 534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Department of Parks and Recreation > Article: 2. State Park and Recreation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 534

---

## Text

The commission shall elect a chair from its number who shall serve as chair for one year and until a successor is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 213, Sec. 2. (AB 2768) Effective January 1, 2011.)
