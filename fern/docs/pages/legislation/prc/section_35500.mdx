# PRC § 35500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.5. CALIFORNIA OCEAN PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 35500

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Ocean Protection Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 719, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
