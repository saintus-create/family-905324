# PRC § 49400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5. Garbage and Refuse Dumps

**Citation:** PRC § 49400

---

## Text

No city, county, district, or public or municipal corporation shall acquire and operate, or cause to be acquired and operated, a dump or site for the disposal of garbage or refuse, or a transfer station or collection point for garbage or refuse, within a city without the consent of the city council or within the unincorporated area of a county without the consent of the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
