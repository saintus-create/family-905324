# PRC § 3103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 3103

---

## Text

The chief deputy shall be a competent engineer or geologist, preferably licensed in the state, and experienced in the development and production of oil and gas.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 521, Sec. 53. (SB 809) Effective January 1, 2018.)
