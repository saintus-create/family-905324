# PRC § 5096.367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.692. Safe Neighborhood Parks, Clean Water, Clean Air, and Coastal Protection Bond Act of 2000 (The Villaraigosa-Keeley Act) > Article: 13. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.367

---

## Text

Pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 16720) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code, the cost of bond issuance shall be paid out of the bond proceeds. These costs shall be shared proportionally by each program funded through this bond act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 461, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 12 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
