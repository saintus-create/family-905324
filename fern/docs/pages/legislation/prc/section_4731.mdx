# PRC § 4731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 7. Protection and Development Corporations

**Citation:** PRC § 4731

---

## Text

Corporations may be formed under the general corporation laws of this state for the protection and development of forest and other renewable natural resources, except as the provisions of such laws are modified or extended by the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
