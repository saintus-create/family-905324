# PRC § 5150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 3. Parks

**Citation:** PRC § 5150

---

## Text

Any county or city may assist the State in acquiring any park, playground, recreational center, or beach to be used for recreational purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
