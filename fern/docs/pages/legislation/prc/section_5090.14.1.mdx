# PRC § 5090.14.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.14.1

---

## Text

“State vehicular recreation area” means a unit of the state park system established pursuant to Section 5090.43.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 459, Sec. 5. (SB 249) Effective January 1, 2018.)
