# PRC § 31207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Coastal Restoration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 31207

---

## Text

In connection with proposed coastal restoration projects, the conservancy may fund up to three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000) of the cost of preparing coastal restoration plans.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2003.)
