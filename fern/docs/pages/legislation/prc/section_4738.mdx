# PRC § 4738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 7. Protection and Development Corporations

**Citation:** PRC § 4738

---

## Text

The operation of any corporation formed pursuant to this article shall, in addition to the requirements of this article, be subject to any stipulations or provisions contained in any contract which may be entered into between the corporation and the corporation or agency established under the authority of the federal government which may have loaned or advanced funds for the operations of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
