# PRC § 49602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Burning Garbage > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 49602

---

## Text

Every person who burns by fire heat or destroys by cremation any garbage, ashes, offal, or other refuse matter in violation of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
