# PRC § 10262.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 4. Agricultural Conservation Easements and Fee Title

**Citation:** PRC § 10262.5

---

## Text

The granting of an agricultural conservation easement under this division shall not be interpreted to convey any rights of public access to the subject property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 616, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2003.)
