# PRC § 10222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. CALIFORNIA FARMLAND CONSERVANCY PROGRAM ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 10222

---

## Text

“Program” means the California Farmland Conservancy Program established under this division.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 503, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
