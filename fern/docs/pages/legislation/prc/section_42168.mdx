# PRC § 42168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3.5. Metallic Discards > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42168

## Text

“Solid waste facility” means a solid waste facility as defined in Section 40194.
