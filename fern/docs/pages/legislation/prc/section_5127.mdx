# PRC § 5127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 2. County Museums, and Historical Records and Property

**Citation:** PRC § 5127

---

## Text

The board may cooperate with other governmental agencies, with universities, colleges, technical schools, societies, or individuals in the advancement of learning in the arts and sciences.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
