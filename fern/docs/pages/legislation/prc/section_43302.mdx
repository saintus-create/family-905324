# PRC § 43302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** PRC § 43302

---

## Text

The board may request enforcement by appropriate federal, state, and local agencies of their respective laws governing solid waste storage, handling, and disposal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
