# PRC § 7401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 1. School Lands and Indemnity Lands > Article: 2. Indemnity Land Selections

**Citation:** PRC § 7401

---

## Text

The commission shall ascertain from time to time the number of acres of land to which the state is entitled as indemnity and shall keep on file a statement showing of what those bases consist.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 311, Sec. 7. (SB 1472) Effective January 1, 2021.)
