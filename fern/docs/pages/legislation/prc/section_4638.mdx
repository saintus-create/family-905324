# PRC § 4638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 4638

---

## Text

“Forest products” includes sawlogs, pilings, poles, split products, pulpwood, bolts, bark and other products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
