# PRC § 32558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.7. BALDWIN HILLS AND URBAN WATERSHEDS CONSERVANCY ACT > Chapter: 3. Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 32558

---

## Text

The chairperson and vice-chairperson of the board shall be selected by a majority of the voting members of the board for one-year terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
