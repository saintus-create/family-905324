# PRC § 75029.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 2. Safe Drinking Water and Water Quality Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 75029.5

---

## Text

The sum of fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000) shall be available to the state board for grants to public agencies and non-profit organizations for projects that reduce the discharge of pollutants from agricultural operations into surface waters of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 84.)
