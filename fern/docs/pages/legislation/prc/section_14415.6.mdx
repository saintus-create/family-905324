# PRC § 14415.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. CALIFORNIA CONSERVATION CORPS > Chapter: 4.5. The Education and Employment Reentry Program

**Citation:** PRC § 14415.6

---

## Text

Implementation of the program pursuant to this chapter is contingent upon an appropriation being made in the annual Budget Act or another statute for its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 587, Sec. 1. (AB 1668) Effective January 1, 2020.)
