# PRC § 2701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 8. Strong-Motion Instrumentation Program

**Citation:** PRC § 2701

## Text

The survey shall organize and monitor the program with the advice of the Seismic Safety Commission.
