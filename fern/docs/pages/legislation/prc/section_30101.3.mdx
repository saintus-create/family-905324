# PRC § 30101.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30101.3

---

## Text

“Coastal-related development” means any use that is dependent on a coastal-dependent development or use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1090.)
