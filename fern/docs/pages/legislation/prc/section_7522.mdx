# PRC § 7522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 2. Sale of Swamp and Overflowed Lands > Article: 2. Swamp and Overflowed Lands Sold Prior to September 19, 1939

**Citation:** PRC § 7522

---

## Text

The commission shall not approve any application nor issue evidence of title for swamp and overflowed land, until six months after the land has been segregated by authority of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
