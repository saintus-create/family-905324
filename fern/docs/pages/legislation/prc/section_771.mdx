# PRC § 771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 3. Professional Foresters

**Citation:** PRC § 771

---

## Text

An applicant failing in an examination may be examined again upon filing a new application and paying the application fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
