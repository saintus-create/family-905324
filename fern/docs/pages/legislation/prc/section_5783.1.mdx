# PRC § 5783.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 4. Selection of the Initial Board of Directors

**Citation:** PRC § 5783.1

---

## Text

In the case of a district that contains only unincorporated territory in a single county, the board of directors may be elected or may be appointed by the county board of supervisors which may appoint itself as the district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
