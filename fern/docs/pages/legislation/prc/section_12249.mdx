# PRC § 12249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. CALIFORNIA FOREST LEGACY PROGRAM ACT OF 2007 > Chapter: 2. California Forest Legacy Program

**Citation:** PRC § 12249

---

## Text

The board shall adopt rules and regulations for the implementation of this division, including the standards, criteria, and requirements necessary for acquiring conservation easements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 657, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
