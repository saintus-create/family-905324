# PRC § 6230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3.2. Sea Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 6230

---

## Text

An amount specified in the annual Budget Act shall be available for distribution for public and private higher education for use as up to two-thirds of the local matching share for projects under the National Sea Grant College and Program Act of 1966 (P.L. 89-688) approved, upon the recommendation of the advisory panel appointed pursuant to Section 6232, by the Secretary of the Resources Agency or the secretary’s designee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 177. Effective October 12, 2001.)
