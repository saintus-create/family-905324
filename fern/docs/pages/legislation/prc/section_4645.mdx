# PRC § 4645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 3. State Forests

**Citation:** PRC § 4645

---

## Text

The department, in accordance with plans approved by the board, may engage in the management, protection, and reforestation of state forests.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
