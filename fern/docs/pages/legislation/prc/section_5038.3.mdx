# PRC § 5038.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 3.4. El Pueblo de Los Angeles State Historic Park

**Citation:** PRC § 5038.3

---

## Text

“Concession,” as used in this article, does not include those premises at El Pueblo de Los Angeles State Historic Park which are primarily rented and used for offices, banking, or storage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1049, Sec. 2.)
