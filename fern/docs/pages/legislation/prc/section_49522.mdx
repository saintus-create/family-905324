# PRC § 49522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Solid Waste Enterprises > Article: 3. Continuation of Services

**Citation:** PRC § 49522

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects the right of a city following annexation to terminate for cause a franchise, contract, license, or permit held by a solid waste enterprise authorized by the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
