# PRC § 9106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 4. The State Resource Conservation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 9106

---

## Text

The commission shall elect a chair from its number who shall serve as chair for one year and until the chair’s successor is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 213, Sec. 15. (AB 2768) Effective January 1, 2011.)
