# PRC § 42771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 15. Newsprint > Article: 3. Certification of Use

**Citation:** PRC § 42771

---

## Text

Every consumer of newsprint who submits recycled-content newsprint usage certification pursuant to Section 42770 may be subject to an audit to ensure that the recycled-content newsprint was used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 23. Effective March 30, 1990.)
