# PRC § 32052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 2. Organization of the Authority: General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32052

---

## Text

This division shall be administered by the authority which shall have, and is hereby vested with, all powers reasonably necessary to carry out its powers and responsibilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1264, Sec. 1.)
