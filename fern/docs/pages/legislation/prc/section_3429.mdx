# PRC § 3429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 7. Assessment and Collection of Charges

**Citation:** PRC § 3429

---

## Text

Payment of the penalties and charges, or the amount of the judgment recovered in the action, shall be made to the State Treasurer.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
