# PRC § 32051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22. CALIFORNIA URBAN WATERFRONT AREA RESTORATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 2. Organization of the Authority: General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32051

## Text

All members shall serve thereon without compensation as members of the authority.
