# PRC § 4682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 2. State Nurseries

**Citation:** PRC § 4682

---

## Text

The department shall construct and maintain such buildings, improvements, and equipment, and shall employ and fix the compensation of such employees as may be necessary to carry out this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
