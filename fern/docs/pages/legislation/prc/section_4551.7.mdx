# PRC § 4551.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 4. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** PRC § 4551.7

---

## Text

The board shall, on or before November 1, 1988, adopt regulations pertaining to site preparation that involves disturbance of soil or burning of vegetation following timber harvesting activities conducted after January 1, 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 987, Sec. 3.)
