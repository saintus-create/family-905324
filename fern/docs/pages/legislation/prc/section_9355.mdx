# PRC § 9355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 8. General District Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 9355

---

## Text

The directors so elected and qualified shall take office at noon on the last Friday in November following their election.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
