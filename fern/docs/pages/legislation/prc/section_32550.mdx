# PRC § 32550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.7. BALDWIN HILLS AND URBAN WATERSHEDS CONSERVANCY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 32550

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Baldwin Hills and Urban Watersheds Conservancy Act.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 714, Sec. 2. (SB 1052) Effective January 1, 2023.)
