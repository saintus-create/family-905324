# PRC § 8553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Grazing Lands > Article: 2. Cooperation with Federal Agencies in Relation to Grazing Lands

## Text

The State Lands Commission is hereby designated the “State land officials” referred to in Section 9 of the Act of Congress cited in Section 8551 of this code.
