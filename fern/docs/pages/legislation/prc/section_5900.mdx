# PRC § 5900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5.8. CALIFORNIA WILDLIFE, COASTAL, AND PARK LAND CONSERVATION ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5900

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Wildlife, Coastal, and Park Land Conservation Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 70.)
