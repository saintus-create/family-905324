# PRC § 33818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.6. SALTON SEA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. Governing Board

**Citation:** PRC § 33818

---

## Text

The board may establish advisory boards or committees, hold community meetings, and engage in public outreach.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 771, Sec. 1. (SB 583) Effective January 1, 2025.)
