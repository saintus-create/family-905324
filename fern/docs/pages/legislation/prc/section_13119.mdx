# PRC § 13119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13119

---

## Text

The board may, by resolution, do the following:

(a) Submit to voters a measure to issue new bonds to refund any or all of the district bonds outstanding.

(b) Submit to the voters of any improvement area a measure to issue new bonds to refund any improvement area bonds outstanding.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
