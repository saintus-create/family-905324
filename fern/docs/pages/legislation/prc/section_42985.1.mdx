# PRC § 42985.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 21. Used Mattress Recovery and Recycling Act

**Citation:** PRC § 42985.1

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Used Mattress Recovery and Recycling Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 388, Sec. 1. (SB 254) Effective January 1, 2014.)
