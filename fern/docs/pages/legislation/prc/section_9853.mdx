# PRC § 9853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 3. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** PRC § 9853

---

## Text

The same proceedings shall be had on the improvement district inclusion petition as upon the original petition for the formation of an improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
