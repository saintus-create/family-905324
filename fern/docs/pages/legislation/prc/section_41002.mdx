# PRC § 41002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. City Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 1. Requirements

**Citation:** PRC § 41002

---

## Text

The city source reduction and recycling element shall place primary emphasis on implementation of all feasible source reduction, recycling, and composting programs while identifying the amount of landfill and transformation capacity that will be needed for solid waste which cannot be reduced at the source, recycled, or composted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
