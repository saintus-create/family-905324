# PRC § 5096.676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.696. The California Clean Water, Clean Air, Safe Neighborhood Parks, and Coastal Protection Act of 2002 > Article: 6. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.676

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 875, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 40 at the March 5, 2002, election.)
