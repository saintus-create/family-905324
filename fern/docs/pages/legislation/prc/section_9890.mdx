# PRC § 9890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 5. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** PRC § 9890

---

## Text

Improvement district warrants or their proceeds shall be used solely for making the improvements for which the improvement district was formed and the necessary incidental expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
