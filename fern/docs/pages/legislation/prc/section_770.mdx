# PRC § 770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 3. Professional Foresters

**Citation:** PRC § 770

---

## Text

(a) Examinations shall be given by the board as often as it is deemed necessary, but at least every six months.

(b) The examination may consist of both written and oral portions. A grade of 75 percent shall be necessary for successful completion of the examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
