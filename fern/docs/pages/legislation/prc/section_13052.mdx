# PRC § 13052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Elections

**Citation:** PRC § 13052

---

## Text

The hours during which the polls shall be opened at any special district election shall be as established by the board.
