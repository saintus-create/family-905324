# PRC § 25951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 11. Gas Appliances > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25951

---

## Text

“Pilot light” means any gas operated device that remains continually operated or lighted in order to ignite a gas appliance to begin normal operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1411.)
