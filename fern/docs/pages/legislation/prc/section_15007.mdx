# PRC § 15007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.2. PRODUCTS CONTAINING TOXIC METALS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 15007

---

## Text

“Manufacturer” means any person who manufactures dry cell batteries, rechargeable batteries or battery packs, or rechargeable consumer products.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 816, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
