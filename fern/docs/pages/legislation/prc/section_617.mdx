# PRC § 617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Department of Conservation > Article: 1. Organization and General Powers

**Citation:** PRC § 617

---

## Text

Upon an appropriation by the Legislature, or as other funds become available and are appropriated for these purposes, the department may provide grants and other forms of local assistance to resource conservation districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 585, Sec. 3. (AB 1902) Effective January 1, 2023.)
