# PRC § 5090.06

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.25. Off-Highway Motor Vehicle Recreation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5090.06

---

## Text

“Fund” means the Off-Highway Vehicle Trust Fund created by subdivision (c) of Section 38225 of the Vehicle Code.
