# PRC § 42756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 15. Newsprint > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42756

---

## Text

“Recycled-content newsprint” means newsprint in which not less than 40 percent of its fiber consists of post consumer waste paper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 23. Effective March 30, 1990.)
