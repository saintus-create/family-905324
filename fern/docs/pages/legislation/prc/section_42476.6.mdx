# PRC § 42476.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.5. Electronic Waste Recycling > Article: 6. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42476.6

---

## Text

Section 42476.5 does not apply to a component part of a covered electronic device that is exported to an authorized collector or recycler and that is reused or recycled into a new electronic component.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 526, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Sections 42485 and 42486.)
