# PRC § 40131.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40131.5

---

## Text

“Federal act” means the Resource Conservation and Recovery Act of 1976, as amended (42 U.S.C. Sec. 6901 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 922, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
