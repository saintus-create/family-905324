# PRC § 5797.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 5. Glen Helen Regional Park Property

**Citation:** PRC § 5797.8

---

## Text

If the county has not acquired replacement park property, as approved by the department pursuant to this chapter, on or before January 1, 2029, this chapter shall become inoperative on January 1, 2029.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 259, Sec. 1. (AB 385) Effective January 1, 2026.)
