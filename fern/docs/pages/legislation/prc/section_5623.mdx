# PRC § 5623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.2. Roberti-Z'berg-Harris Urban Open-Space and Recreation Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5623

---

## Text

Grants to cities, counties, and districts pursuant to this chapter are intended to supplement and not to supplant local expenditures for park and recreation areas and facilities or to diminish in any way then current efforts for providing parks and recreation services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 160.)
