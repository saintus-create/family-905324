# PRC § 5644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.3. The Statewide Park Development and Community Revitalization Act of 2008

**Citation:** PRC § 5644

---

## Text

Eligible applicants for grants pursuant to this chapter are cities, counties, regional park districts, districts, joint powers authorities, and nonprofit organizations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 623, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2009.)
