# PRC § 4750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 9. Sudden Oak Death Management Act of 2002

**Citation:** PRC § 4750

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Sudden Oak Death Management Act of 2002.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 854, Sec. 1. Effective September 25, 2002.)
