# PRC § 6234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3.2. Sea Grant Program

**Citation:** PRC § 6234

---

## Text

The Secretary of the Resources Agency, or the secretary’s designee shall be a member of the advisory panel and shall serve as chairperson of the advisory panel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 293, Sec. 5. Effective August 18, 1997.)
