# PRC § 26511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 26511

---

## Text

“State” means the State of California and, where the context requires, any agency or instrumentality thereof.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
