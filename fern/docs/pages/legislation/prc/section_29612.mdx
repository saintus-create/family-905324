# PRC § 29612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. SUISUN MARSH PRESERVATION > Chapter: 7. Judicial Review, Enforcement, and Penalties > Article: 2. Penalties

**Citation:** PRC § 29612

---

## Text

Any moneys recovered by the commission under this article shall be deposited in the Bay Fill Clean-up and Abatement Fund established pursuant to Section 66647 of the Government Code.
