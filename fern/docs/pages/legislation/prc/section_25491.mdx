# PRC § 25491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.9. Energy Systems > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25491

---

## Text

“Governmental agency” means any public agency, including any agency of the state, each county, city, district, association of governments, and joint power agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 773.)
