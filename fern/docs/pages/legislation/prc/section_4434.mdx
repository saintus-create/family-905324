# PRC § 4434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 6. Prohibited Activities > Article: 2. Prohibited Activities

**Citation:** PRC § 4434

---

## Text

The escape of any campfire from the control of any person who is maintaining the campfire is prima facie evidence that such person was negligent in maintaining the campfire.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
