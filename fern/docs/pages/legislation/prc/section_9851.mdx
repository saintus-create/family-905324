# PRC § 9851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 3. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** PRC § 9851

---

## Text

If at any time it is desired to include additional real property within an improvement district, a petition for inclusion signed by the owners of real property to be included may be filed with the directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
