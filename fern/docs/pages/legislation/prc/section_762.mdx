# PRC § 762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 3. Professional Foresters

**Citation:** PRC § 762

---

## Text

The board may by regulation provide for the issuance of certificates of specialization in such fields of specialization as the board may by regulation establish.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
