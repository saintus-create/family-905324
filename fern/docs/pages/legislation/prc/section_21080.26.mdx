# PRC § 21080.26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.6. General

**Citation:** PRC § 21080.26

---

## Text

This division does not apply to minor alterations to utilities made for the purposes of complying with Sections 116410 and 116415 of the Health and Safety Code or regulations adopted thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 327, Sec. 32. (AB 1438) Effective January 1, 2018.)
