# PRC § 21065.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21065.3

---

## Text

“Project-specific effect” means all the direct or indirect environmental effects of a project other than cumulative effects and growth-inducing effects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1039, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
