# PRC § 9153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Lands Included

**Citation:** PRC § 9153

---

## Text

The lands included in any one district need not be contiguous. No lands may be included in more than one district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 585, Sec. 8. (AB 1902) Effective January 1, 2023.)
