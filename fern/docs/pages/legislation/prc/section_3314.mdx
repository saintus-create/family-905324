# PRC § 3314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5. Unreasonable Waste of Gas

**Citation:** PRC § 3314

---

## Text

Proceedings to enjoin waste as contemplated by this chapter shall be special proceedings restricted to the single issue whether gas is being produced or is threatened to be produced in unreasonably wasteful quantities and the extent to which such production should be enjoined on behalf of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1670.)
