# PRC § 4694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 3. Reforestation Studies and Plans

**Citation:** PRC § 4694

## Text

Experimentation and studies shall be conducted with the advice and cooperation of the University of California School of Forestry.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 56, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1994.)
