# PRC § 13032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 13032

---

## Text

If deemed advisable by the board of supervisors a board of directors for the district may be formed.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
