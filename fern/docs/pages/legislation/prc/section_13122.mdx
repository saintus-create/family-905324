# PRC § 13122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 13122

---

## Text

The refunding bonds may, if the holders of the bonds of an original issue and the board so agree, be exchanged for original bonds.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
