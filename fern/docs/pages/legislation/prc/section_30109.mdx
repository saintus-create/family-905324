# PRC § 30109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30109

---

## Text

“Local government” means any chartered or general law city, chartered or general law county, or any city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
