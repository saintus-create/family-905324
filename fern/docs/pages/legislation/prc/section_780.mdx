# PRC § 780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 3. Professional Foresters

**Citation:** PRC § 780

---

## Text

The fees received pursuant to this article shall be deposited in the Professional Forester Registration Fund in the State Treasury, which fund is hereby created.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
