# PRC § 5755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.8. California River Parkways Act of 2004

**Citation:** PRC § 5755

---

## Text

The secretary shall report annually to the Legislature regarding the geographic distribution, types, and benefits of projects funded pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 14. Effective August 16, 2004.)
