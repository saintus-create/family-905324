# PRC § 25545.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 6.2. Certification of Nonfossil-Fueled Powerplants, Energy Storage Facilities, and Related Facilities

**Citation:** PRC § 25545.11

## Text

Sections 25532 to 25534.2, inclusive, apply to a certification issued pursuant to this chapter.
