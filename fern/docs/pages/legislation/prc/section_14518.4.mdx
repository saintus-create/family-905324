# PRC § 14518.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14518.4

---

## Text

“Processing fee” means the amount paid by beverage manufacturers to the department pursuant to Section 14575.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 624, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
