# PRC § 48621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 48621

---

## Text

“Used oil” means used oil, as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 25250.1 of the Health and Safety Code. Used oil does not include articles contaminated with de minimis quantities of used oil, such as used oil filters, oily rags, and scrap metal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
