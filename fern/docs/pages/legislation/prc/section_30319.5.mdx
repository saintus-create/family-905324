# PRC § 30319.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 2. Qualifications and Organization

**Citation:** PRC § 30319.5

---

## Text

An applicant whose permit is denied due to the applicant’s failure to comply with Section 30319 may not apply to the commission for approval of an identical or similar project for two years from the date of the permit denial.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 97, Sec. 8. (SB 1497) Effective January 1, 2023.)
