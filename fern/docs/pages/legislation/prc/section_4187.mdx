# PRC § 4187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 8. Clarke-McNary Act

**Citation:** PRC § 4187

---

## Text

A county which enacts an ordinance, as provided in Section 4129, is entitled to any allocation of moneys received by the state pursuant to the Clarke-McNary Act. Such reimbursement may be provided for by any statute of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
