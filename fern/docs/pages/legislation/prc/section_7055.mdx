# PRC § 7055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 5. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases by Public Agencies

**Citation:** PRC § 7055

---

## Text

Any money accruing from leases under this chapter shall be paid into the general fund of the county or other public or quasi public corporation, body or agency for the use of the county or other public or quasi public corporation, body or agency, as the case may be.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1945, Ch. 529.)
