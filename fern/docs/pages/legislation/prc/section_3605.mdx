# PRC § 3605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3. Spacing of Wells and Community Leases

**Citation:** PRC § 3605

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter do not apply to any field producing oil or gas on August 14, 1931.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1559.)
