# PRC § 26577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Nature and Powers of the District > Article: 2. Powers of a District

**Citation:** PRC § 26577

---

## Text

A district may purchase, lease, obtain an option upon, acquire by gift, grant, bequest, or devise, or otherwise acquire any property or any interest in property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1118.)
