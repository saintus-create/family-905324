# PRC § 32370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.3. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Powers, Duties, and Limitations

**Citation:** PRC § 32370

---

## Text

The conservancy shall not exercise the power of eminent domain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 37. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
