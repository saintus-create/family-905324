# PRC § 8628

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. KAPILOFF LAND BANK ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 8628

---

## Text

The commission shall accept the conveyances on the part of the state and shall authorize their acknowledgment and recordation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1471, Sec. 1.)
