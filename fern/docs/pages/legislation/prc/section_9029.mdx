# PRC § 9029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 9029

---

## Text

“Voter” means an elector who is registered to vote pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 2100) of Division 2 of the Elections Code, and residing within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 170. Effective January 1, 1995.)
