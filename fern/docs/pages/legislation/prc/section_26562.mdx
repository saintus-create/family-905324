# PRC § 26562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. District Formation > Article: 4. Notice and Hearing

**Citation:** PRC § 26562

---

## Text

A copy of the petition described in Section 26552 or the resolution described in Section 26558 shall be attached to the notice.
