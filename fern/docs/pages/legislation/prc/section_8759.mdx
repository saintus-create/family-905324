# PRC § 8759

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.8. OIL SPILL PREVENTION AND RESPONSE > Chapter: 2. Prevention, Inspection, Response, Containment, and Cleanup Programs

**Citation:** PRC § 8759

---

## Text

The commission shall be reimbursed from the Oil Spill Prevention and Administration Fund for reasonable expenses undertaken under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 19. Effective September 24, 1990.)
