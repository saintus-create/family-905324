# PRC § 745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2.5. Department of Forestry and Fire Protection > Article: 2. State Board of Forestry and Fire Protection

**Citation:** PRC § 745

---

## Text

The board shall implement a public information program on matters involving forest management and shall maintain an information file on forest management research and other pertinent matters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1300.)
