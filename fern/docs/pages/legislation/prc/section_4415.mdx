# PRC § 4415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 6. Prohibited Activities > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 4415

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to the setting of fire on lands within any municipal corporation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
