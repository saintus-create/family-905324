# PRC § 2728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 9. Surface Mining and Reclamation Act of 1975 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 2728

---

## Text

“Lead agency” means the city, county, San Francisco Bay Conservation and Development Commission, or the board which has the principal responsibility for approving a reclamation plan pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 869, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2007.)
