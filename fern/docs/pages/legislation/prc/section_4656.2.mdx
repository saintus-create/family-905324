# PRC § 4656.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 9. State Forest > Article: 3. State Forests

**Citation:** PRC § 4656.2

---

## Text

The department shall protect the state forests from damage and preserve the peace therein.
