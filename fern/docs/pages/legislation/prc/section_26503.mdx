# PRC § 26503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 26503

---

## Text

“Local agency” means a city, a city and county, or a county.
