# PRC § 75070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 43. THE SAFE DRINKING WATER, WATER QUALITY AND SUPPLY, FLOOD CONTROL, RIVER AND COASTAL PROTECTION BOND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 10. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 75070

---

## Text

Every proposed activity or project to be financed pursuant to this division shall be in compliance with the California Environmental Quality Act, Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2006, by initiative Proposition 84.)
