# PRC § 4581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 7. Timber Harvesting

**Citation:** PRC § 4581

---

## Text

No person shall conduct timber operations unless a timber harvesting plan prepared by a registered professional forester has been submitted for such operations to the department pursuant to this article. Such plan shall be required in addition to the license required in Section 4571.
