# PRC § 43605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Solid Waste Handling and Disposal > Article: 4. Financial Ability

**Citation:** PRC § 43605

---

## Text

Nothing in this division affects the authority of the State Water Resources Control Board to impose closure and postclosure maintenance requirements on disposal sites.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1062, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
