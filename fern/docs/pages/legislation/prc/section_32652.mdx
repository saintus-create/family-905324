# PRC § 32652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.9. SAN DIEGO RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 32652

---

## Text

The conservancy may receive any gift, donation, subvention, grant, rent, royalty, and other financial aid and funds from any private or public source, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 574, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
