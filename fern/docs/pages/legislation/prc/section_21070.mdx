# PRC § 21070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.5. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 21070

---

## Text

“Trustee agency” means a state agency that has jurisdiction by law over natural resources affected by a project, that are held in trust for the people of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 744, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
