# PRC § 5096.174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.69. California Parklands Act of 1980 > Article: 5. Coastal Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.174

---

## Text

(a) An application for a grant shall be submitted to the State Coastal Conservancy for preliminary evaluation, review of adequacy, and classification as a park, beach, coastal access, or other project necessary to preserve coastal resource values.

(b) The minimum amount that may be applied for any individual project is one thousand dollars ($1,000).

(c) Every application for a grant shall comply with the provisions of the California Environmental Quality Act (Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 250, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 1980, election. Operative December 1, 1980.)
