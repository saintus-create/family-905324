# PRC § 5786.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 7. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 5786.25

---

## Text

A board of directors may require any employee or officer to be bonded. The district shall pay the cost of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 15, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
