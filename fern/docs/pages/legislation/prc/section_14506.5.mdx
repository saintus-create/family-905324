# PRC § 14506.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14506.5

---

## Text

“Commingled” means a mix of empty beverage containers, as defined in Section 14512, and all other containers of the same material type.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1342, Sec. 2.)
