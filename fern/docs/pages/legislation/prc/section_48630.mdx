# PRC § 48630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. California Oil Recycling Enhancement > Article: 4. Used Oil Recycling

**Citation:** PRC § 48630

---

## Text

On or before October 1, 1992, the board shall adopt a used oil recycling program which promotes and develops alternatives to the illegal disposal of used oil.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 817, Sec. 5.)
