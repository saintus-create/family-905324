# PRC § 4789.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 12. Forest and Rangeland Resources Assessment and Policy Act of 1977

**Citation:** PRC § 4789.5

---

## Text

The board shall hold public hearings on the assessment and the proposed policy statement prepared pursuant to Sections 4789.3 and 4789.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 4805 by Stats. 1980, Ch. 676, Sec. 272.)
