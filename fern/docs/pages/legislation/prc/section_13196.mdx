# PRC § 13196

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Alternative Depositary of District Money

**Citation:** PRC § 13196

---

## Text

The finance officer shall install and maintain a system of auditing and accounting that shall completely and at all times show the financial condition of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
