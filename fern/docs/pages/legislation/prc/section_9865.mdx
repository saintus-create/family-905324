# PRC § 9865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** PRC § 9865

---

## Text

The directors may, in lieu in whole or in part of levying assessments for the operation of improvement district works, fix and collect reasonable charges for the use of water or for any other service furnished by means of the improvement district works.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
