# PRC § 13038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 13038

---

## Text

Once each year, the district board shall elect one of its members to serve as president and shall appoint a secretary, who may be a member of the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 302.)
