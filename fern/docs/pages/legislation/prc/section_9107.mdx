# PRC § 9107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 4. The State Resource Conservation Commission

**Citation:** PRC § 9107

---

## Text

The commission shall appoint a secretary. The secretary shall be a paid employee of the commission. The secretary shall be allowed his reasonable and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of his official duties as such secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
