# PRC § 14514.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14514.6

---

## Text

“Not for recycling” means that an empty beverage container has been received by a processor who does not have an arrangement whereby that container will actually be recycled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1290, Sec. 2. Effective September 29, 1986.)
