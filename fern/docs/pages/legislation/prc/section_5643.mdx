# PRC § 5643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.3. The Statewide Park Development and Community Revitalization Act of 2008

**Citation:** PRC § 5643

---

## Text

(a) The Department of Parks and Recreation shall establish a local assistance program to distribute grants to the most critically underserved communities across the state, on a competitive basis, to eligible cities, counties, joint powers authorities, districts, and nonprofit organizations for the acquisition or development, or both, of property for parks and recreation areas and facilities.

(b) The Department of Parks and Recreation shall encourage joint partnership projects, if available, between two or more agencies, including, but not limited to, school districts, nonprofit organizations, and local governmental agencies in order to enhance investment of public resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 623, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2009.)
