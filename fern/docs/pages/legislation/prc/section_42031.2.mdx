# PRC § 42031.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Pharmaceutical and Sharps Waste Stewardship > Article: 2. Covered Entities and Stewardship Organizations

**Citation:** PRC § 42031.2

---

## Text

(a) The department shall adopt regulations for the implementation of this chapter with an effective date of no later than January 1, 2021.

(b) The state board may adopt regulations for the administration of the portions of this chapter for which it has been given responsibilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 1004, Sec. 1. (SB 212) Effective January 1, 2019.)
