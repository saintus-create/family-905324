# PRC § 5796.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 14. Lower San Gabriel River Recreation and Park District

**Citation:** PRC § 5796.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “district” means the Lower San Gabriel River Recreation and Park District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 466, Sec. 1. (SB 693) Effective January 1, 2018.)
