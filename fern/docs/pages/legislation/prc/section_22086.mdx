# PRC § 22086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14.5. BLUE RIBBON COMMITTEE FOR THE REHABILITATION OF CLEAR LAKE

**Citation:** PRC § 22086

---

## Text

There is hereby established in the Natural Resources Agency the Blue Ribbon Committee for the Rehabilitation of Clear Lake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 842, Sec. 1. (AB 707) Effective January 1, 2018.)
