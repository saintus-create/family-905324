# PRC § 9818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Formation

**Citation:** PRC § 9818

---

## Text

If at the hearing the directors find that it would not be for the best interests of the district and the proposed improvement district to form the improvement district the directors shall order the proceedings dismissed without prejudice to their renewal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
