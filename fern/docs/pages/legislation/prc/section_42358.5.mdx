# PRC § 42358.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5.7. Products

**Citation:** PRC § 42358.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 594, Sec. 1. (SB 567) Effective January 1, 2012. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (January 1, 2013) for Chapter 5.7, commencing with Section 42355, as added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 594.)
