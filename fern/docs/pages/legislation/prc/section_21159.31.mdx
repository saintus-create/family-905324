# PRC § 21159.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 4.5. Streamlined Environmental Review > Article: 7. Program Environmental Impact Report for Clean Infrastructure Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 21159.31

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Energy Commission” means the State Energy Resources Conservation and Development Commission.

(b) “Facility” has the same meaning as set forth in subdivision (b) of Section 25545, except for paragraph (4).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 119, Sec. 17. (SB 254) Effective September 19, 2025.)
