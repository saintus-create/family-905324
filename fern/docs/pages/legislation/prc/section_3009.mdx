# PRC § 3009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 3009

---

## Text

“Operator” means a person who, by virtue of ownership, or under the authority of a lease or any other agreement, has the right to drill, operate, maintain, or control a well or production facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 562, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
