# PRC § 14523.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14523.5

---

## Text

“Redemption rate” means the proportion of empty beverage containers returned to processors measured in the manner prescribed in Section 14551.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1290, Sec. 2. Effective September 29, 1986.)
