# PRC § 3304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5. Unreasonable Waste of Gas

**Citation:** PRC § 3304

---

## Text

The place of hearing shall be in the county or in any of the counties in which the unreasonable waste of gas is alleged to be taking place or to be threatened.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 93.)
