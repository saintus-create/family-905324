# PRC § 7975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Public Lands Generally > Article: 8. Procedure on Improper Sales and Abandonment of Entries

**Citation:** PRC § 7975

---

## Text

Whenever a purchaser of land upon credit desires to abandon the location or entry made by him, he may do so by conveyance of his title to the State and surrender of the certificate of purchase, or, if it has been lost, by filing an affidavit of that fact with the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 609.)
