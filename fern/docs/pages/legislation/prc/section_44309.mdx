# PRC § 44309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. Denial, Suspension, or Revocation of Permits > Article: 2. Suspension or Revocation

**Citation:** PRC § 44309

---

## Text

All hearings conducted by the board acting as the enforcement agency pursuant to Section 43205 shall be conducted by a hearing panel of three board members appointed by the chairperson of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 448, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2005.)
