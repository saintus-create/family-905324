# PRC § 5096.225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1.691. California Park and Recreational Facilities Act of 1984 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5096.225

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Park and Recreational Facilities Act of 1984.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 18 at the June 5, 1984, election.)
