# PRC § 5186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Counties and Cities > Article: 4. Municipal Park Control

**Citation:** PRC § 5186

---

## Text

Each board shall govern, manage, and direct the parks, avenues, and grounds which are placed under its care and charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
