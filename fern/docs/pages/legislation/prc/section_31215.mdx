# PRC § 31215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Coastal Restoration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 31215

---

## Text

Prior to undertaking any restoration project under the provisions of Section 31213, the project shall be included within, and funded under, the Budget Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1441.)
