# PRC § 4171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 7. Public Nuisances

**Citation:** PRC § 4171

---

## Text

Any condition endangering public safety by creating a fire hazard and which exists upon any property which is included within any state reponsibility area is a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
