# PRC § 31111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the State Coastal Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 31111

---

## Text

In implementing this division, the conservancy may fund and do all of the following:

(a) Prepare plans and feasibility studies.

(b) Provide technical assistance.

(c) Award grants to public agencies and nonprofit organizations for the purposes of subdivisions (a) and (b).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 701, Sec. 1. (SB 367) Effective January 1, 2020.)
