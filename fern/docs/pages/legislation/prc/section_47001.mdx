# PRC § 47001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Household Hazardous Substance Information and Collection > Article: 1. Legislative Findings and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 47001

---

## Text

The Legislature also finds that the disposal of hazardous substances by households can be injurious to sanitation workers, the general public, and wildlife and domestic animals, and can pose a threat to the environment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 46. Effective March 30, 1990.)
