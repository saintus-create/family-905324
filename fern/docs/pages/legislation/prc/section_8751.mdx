# PRC § 8751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.8. OIL SPILL PREVENTION AND RESPONSE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 8751

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, this division shall be applicable to all terminals, pipelines, facilities, vessels, and activities in the state, whether on lands that have been legislatively granted to local governments or on lands which remain ungranted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 19. Effective September 24, 1990.)
