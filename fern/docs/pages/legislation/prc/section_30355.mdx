# PRC § 30355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 4. Creation, Membership, and Powers of Commission and Regional Commissions > Article: 4. Local Coastal Program Implementation Costs

**Citation:** PRC § 30355

---

## Text

As used in this article, “certified local coastal program” means any portion of a local coastal program that has been certified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 919.)
