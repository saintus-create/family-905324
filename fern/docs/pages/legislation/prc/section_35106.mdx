# PRC § 35106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 35106

---

## Text

“Governing board” means the governing body of the authority.

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
