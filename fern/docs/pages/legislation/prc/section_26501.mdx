# PRC § 26501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. GEOLOGIC HAZARD ABATEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 26501

---

## Text

“Board of directors” means the governing body of the district.
