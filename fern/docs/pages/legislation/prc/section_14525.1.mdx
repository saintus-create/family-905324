# PRC § 14525.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14525.1

---

## Text

“Reusable beverage container” means a glass beverage container with a refund value established pursuant to Section 14560 and that is processed by a processor for subsequent washing for refill and sale by a beverage manufacturer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 502, Sec. 1. (AB 962) Effective January 1, 2022.)
