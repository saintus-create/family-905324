# PRC § 4714.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 10. Protection of Forest and Lands > Article: 5. Forest Insect and Plant Disease Control

**Citation:** PRC § 4714.5

---

## Text

The movement of eucalyptus wood containing live Eucalyptus Longhorn Borers or their larvae in trucks or trailers is prohibited. A violation of this section is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by imprisonment in a county jail for not more than six months, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 225, Sec. 2.)
