# PRC § 9861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 4. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** PRC § 9861

---

## Text

In a district containing an improvement district the directors and all of the officers of the district each respectively has all the rights, powers, and privileges as to the improvement district, its real property, and the proceedings in relation to the improvement district that each respectively has for the district of which the improvement district is a part including the right of the district to acquire, own, and hold property.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
