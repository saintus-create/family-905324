# PRC § 41350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. County Source Reduction and Recycling Elements > Article: 3. Source Reduction Component

**Citation:** PRC § 41350

---

## Text

The county source reduction component shall include a program and implementation schedule which shows the methods by which the county will, in combination with the recycling and composting components, reduce a sufficient amount of solid waste disposed of within the unincorporated area of the county to comply with the diversion requirements of Section 41780.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1292, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 1993.)
