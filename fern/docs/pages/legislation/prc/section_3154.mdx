# PRC § 3154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3. Well Stimulation

**Citation:** PRC § 3154

---

## Text

“Proppants” means materials inserted or injected into the underground geologic formation that are intended to prevent fractures from closing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 313, Sec. 2. (SB 4) Effective January 1, 2014.)
