# PRC § 6831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 3. Oil and Gas and Mineral Leases > Article: 2. Provisions Relating to Oil and Gas Leases Generally

**Citation:** PRC § 6831

---

## Text

Rights of way through all State lands may be granted to any lessee by the commission under such regulations as to survey, location, application, and use as may be prescribed by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
