# PRC § 22014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. THE VENTURA-LOS ANGELES MOUNTAIN AND COASTAL STUDY COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 22014

---

## Text

“Irreversible modification” means any modification that affects the physical shape, function, or environment of any estuary, watershed, flood plain, or park and recreation area.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1556.)
