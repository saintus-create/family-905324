# PRC § 32577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.7. BALDWIN HILLS AND URBAN WATERSHEDS CONSERVANCY ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

## Text

The conservancy shall coordinate its actions with state and local public safety agencies.
