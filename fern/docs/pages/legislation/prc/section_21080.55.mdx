# PRC § 21080.55

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ENVIRONMENTAL QUALITY > Chapter: 2.6. General

**Citation:** PRC § 21080.55

---

## Text

This division does not apply to updates to the state’s climate adaptation strategy, known as the plan, adopted by the Natural Resources Agency pursuant to Section 71153.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 24, Sec. 13. (SB 131) Effective June 30, 2025.)
