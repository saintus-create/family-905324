# PRC § 5736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.7. Community Parklands Act of 1986 > Article: 4. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 5736

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund which is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 43 at the June 3, 1986, election. Operative July 1, 1986, by Stats. 1986, Ch. 5, Sec. 2.)
