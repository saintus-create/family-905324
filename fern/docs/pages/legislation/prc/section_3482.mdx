# PRC § 3482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1.5. Used Oil Collection Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Grants for Used Oil Collection Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** PRC § 3482

---

## Text

(a) A local agency shall not use more than 5 percent of any grant for administrative costs.

(b) The board shall not use more than 10 percent of funds made available for the grant program under this chapter for administrative costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1657, Sec. 2.)
