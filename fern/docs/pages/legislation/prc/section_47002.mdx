# PRC § 47002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Household Hazardous Substance Information and Collection > Article: 1. Legislative Findings and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 47002

---

## Text

The Legislature further finds and declares that each household in the state should have reasonable access to legal, convenient, and environmentally safe methods for the disposal of hazardous substances commonly found in and around homes.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 35, Sec. 48. Effective March 30, 1990.)
