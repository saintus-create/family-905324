# PRC § 3337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 5.5. Subsidence

**Citation:** PRC § 3337

---

## Text

The division shall exercise surveillance over all repressuring operations in the state.
