# PRC § 3155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 3. Well Stimulation

**Citation:** PRC § 3155

---

## Text

“Supplier” means an entity performing a well stimulation treatment or an entity supplying an additive or proppant directly to the operator for use in a well stimulation treatment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 313, Sec. 2. (SB 4) Effective January 1, 2014.)
