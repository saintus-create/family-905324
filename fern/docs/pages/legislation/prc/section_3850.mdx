# PRC § 3850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 7. Methane Gas Hazards Reduction > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 3850

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Methane Gas Hazards Reduction Act.
