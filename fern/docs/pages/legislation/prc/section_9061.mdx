# PRC § 9061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 2. The Division of Resource Conservation > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 9061

## Text

The chief shall be responsible to the director for properly carrying out his functions under this division.
