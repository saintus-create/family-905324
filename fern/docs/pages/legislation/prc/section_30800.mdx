# PRC § 30800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 9. Judicial Review, Enforcement, and Penalties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 30800

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be in addition to any other remedies available at law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
