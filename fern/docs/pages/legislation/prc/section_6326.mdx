# PRC § 6326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4. Administration and Control of Swamp, Overflowed, Tide, or Submerged Lands, and Structures Thereon > Article: 2. Control of Structures

**Citation:** PRC § 6326

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter abridges any right of the State to erect, maintain, or remove the protective structures herein mentioned, upon, across, or over any of the swamp, overflowed, marsh, tide or submerged lands of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 548.)
