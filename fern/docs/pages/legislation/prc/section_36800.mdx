# PRC § 36800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 7. Marine Managed Areas Improvement Act > Article: 2. Classifications, Designations, Restrictions, and Allowable Uses

**Citation:** PRC § 36800

---

## Text

The Secretary of the Resources Agency shall establish and chair the State Interagency Coordinating Committee, whose members are representatives from those state agencies, departments, boards, commissions, and conservancies with jurisdiction or management interests over marine managed areas, including, but not limited to, the Department of Fish and Game, Department of Parks and Recreation, California Coastal Commission, State Water Resources Control Board, and State Lands Commission. The Secretary of the Resources Agency shall designate additional members of the committee. The committee shall review proposals for new or amended MMAs to ensure that the minimum required information is included in the proposal, to determine those state agencies that should review the proposal, and to ensure consistency with other such designations in the state. The committee shall also serve to ensure the proper and timely routing of site proposals, review any proposed site-specific regulations for consistency with the state system as a whole, and conduct periodic reviews of the statewide system to evaluate whether it is meeting the mission and statement of objectives.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 385, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2001.)
