# PRC § 33810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.6. SALTON SEA CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 33810

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Salton Sea Conservancy Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 771, Sec. 1. (SB 583) Effective January 1, 2025.)
