# PRC § 5025.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 2. Historical Resources

**Citation:** PRC § 5025.2

---

## Text

It shall be the duty of the department to keep in repair, or cause to be kept in repair, all markers of such historical routes.
