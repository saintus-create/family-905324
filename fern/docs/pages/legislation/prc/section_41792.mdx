# PRC § 41792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Planning Requirements > Article: 2. Board Review

**Citation:** PRC § 41792

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature, in enacting this part, that cities and counties shall commence efforts to implement source reduction, recycling, or composting activities immediately upon enactment of this part, in order to achieve the deadlines specified under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
