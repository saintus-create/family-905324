# PRC § 2804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GEOLOGY, MINES AND MINING > Chapter: 10. Earthquakes

**Citation:** PRC § 2804

---

## Text

The department and the Seismic Safety Commission may solicit and receive gifts and grants from other public and private agencies for the state’s share of costs under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1198, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1985.)
