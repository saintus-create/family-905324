# PRC § 13013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13013

---

## Text

“County” means the county in which the district in located.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
