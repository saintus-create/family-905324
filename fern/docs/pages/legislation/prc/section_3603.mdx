# PRC § 3603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3. Spacing of Wells and Community Leases

**Citation:** PRC § 3603

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, an alley which intersects or lies within any block or other subdivision unit is not a public street or road.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1559.)
