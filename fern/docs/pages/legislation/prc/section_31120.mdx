# PRC § 31120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the State Coastal Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 31120

---

## Text

In awarding grants to, or entering into agreements with, a federally recognized Indian tribe, the conservancy shall recognize and respect the limited sovereignty of the tribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2003.)
