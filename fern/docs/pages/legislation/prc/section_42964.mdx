# PRC § 42964

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 19. Tire Hauler Registration > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** PRC § 42964

---

## Text

The department may expend funds from the California Tire Recycling Management Fund, upon appropriation by the Legislature, for purposes of implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 542, Sec. 11. (SB 1181) Effective January 1, 2023.)
