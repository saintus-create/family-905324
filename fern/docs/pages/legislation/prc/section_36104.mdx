# PRC § 36104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 36104

---

## Text

“Program” means the California Ocean Resources Management Program established by Section 36200.
