# PRC § 42561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 10. Office Paper Recovery Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42561

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 1991, the board shall initiate a high grade white office paper recovery assistance program for state and local agencies and private businesses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
