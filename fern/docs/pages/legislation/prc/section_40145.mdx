# PRC § 40145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40145

---

## Text

“Jurisdiction” means a city, county, or regional agency that is approved by the board pursuant to Section 40975.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 343, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
