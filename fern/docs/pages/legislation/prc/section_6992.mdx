# PRC § 6992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC LANDS > Chapter: 4.1. Leases for Taking Minerals From Waters and Certain Lands

**Citation:** PRC § 6992

---

## Text

Leases for any such lands and for the privilege of taking minerals from the waters of streams and lakes may be issued by the commission by competitive bidding to the highest responsible bidder, under general regulations adopted by the commission.
