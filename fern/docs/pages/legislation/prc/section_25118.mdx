# PRC § 25118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25118

---

## Text

“Service area” means any contiguous geographic area serviced by the same electric utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
