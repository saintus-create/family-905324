# PRC § 4173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control of Forest Fires > Article: 7. Public Nuisances

**Citation:** PRC § 4173

---

## Text

The board shall establish standards, based upon its determination of conditions which create an unreasonable hazard to life or property from fire, for what constitutes a hazardous condition in those instances not covered by state law. Such standards shall be established for each of the department’s administrative districts after public hearing for which ample publicity is given.
