# PRC § 5065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 1. State Parks and Monuments > Article: 5. Lease Contracts for Park and Recreational Areas

**Citation:** PRC § 5065

---

## Text

Lands leased shall be administered as a part of the California State park system, subject to existing laws affecting the operation and administration of State parks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 94.)
