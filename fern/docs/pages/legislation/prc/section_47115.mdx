# PRC § 47115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. Household Hazardous Substance Information and Collection > Article: 3.3. Home-Generated Sharps Waste Collection

**Citation:** PRC § 47115

---

## Text

A pharmaceutical manufacturer that sells or distributes a medication in California that is usually intended to be self-injected at home through the use of a hypodermic needle, pen needle, intravenous needle, or any other similar device, shall, on or before July 1, 2010, and annually thereafter, submit to the board, or its successor agency, a plan that describes how the manufacturer supports the safe collection and proper disposal of the waste devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 591, Sec. 1. (SB 486) Effective January 1, 2010.)
