# PRC § 25119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25119

---

## Text

“Site” means any location on which a facility is constructed or is proposed to be constructed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 276.)
