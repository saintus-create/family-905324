# PRC § 14526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14526

---

## Text

“Scrap value” means the price paid for container material types subject to this division, after shipping and handling costs are deducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1274, Sec. 4. Effective September 25, 1990.)
