# PRC § 25620.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.1. Public Interest Energy Research, Demonstration,and Development Program

**Citation:** PRC § 25620.9

---

## Text

For a project related to the deployment of a microgrid, a recipient of moneys awarded pursuant to this chapter shall not expend those moneys for the purchase of diesel generators, including diesel backup generators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 476, Sec. 1. (AB 1400) Effective January 1, 2018.)
