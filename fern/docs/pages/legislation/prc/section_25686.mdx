# PRC § 25686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.8. Carbon Removal Innovation Program > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25686

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Eligible projects” means projects that include, but are not limited to, technology research, development, and demonstrations and implementation of prototype and pilot research test centers.

(b) “Financial incentive” includes a contract, grant, loan, or other appropriate funding mechanism.

(c) “Program” means the Carbon Removal Innovation Program established pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 25688).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 251, Sec. 13. (AB 209) Effective September 6, 2022.)
