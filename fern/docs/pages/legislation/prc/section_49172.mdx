# PRC § 49172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Garbage and Refuse Disposal Districts > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PRC § 49172

---

## Text

The district board may issue and sell bonds of the district at not less than par value, and the proceeds shall be placed in the treasury of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1095, Sec. 22.)
