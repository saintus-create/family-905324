# PRC § 80202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 47. FUEL REDUCTION WORK CONTRACTS

**Citation:** PRC § 80202

---

## Text

This division only applies to contracts in excess of five hundred thousand dollars ($500,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 428, Sec. 1. (AB 338) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative July 1, 2026, pursuant to Section 80204.)
