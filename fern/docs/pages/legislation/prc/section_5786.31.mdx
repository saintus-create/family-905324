# PRC § 5786.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Recreation and Park Districts > Article: 7. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 5786.31

---

## Text

Whenever the boundaries of a district or a zone change, the district shall comply with Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 54900) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 29.1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
