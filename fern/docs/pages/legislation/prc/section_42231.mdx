# PRC § 42231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 5. Compost Market Program > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 42231

---

## Text

“Compost” means the product resulting from the controlled biological decomposition of organic wastes that are sources separated from the municipal solid waste stream.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
