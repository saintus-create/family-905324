# PRC § 35150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SANTA CLARA VALLEY OPEN-SPACE AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PRC § 35150

---

## Text

The authority may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
