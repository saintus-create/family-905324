# PRC § 14523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 14523

---

## Text

“Redemption payment” means the minimum amount paid by a distributor to the department for every beverage container sold or transferred to a dealer.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1339, Sec. 12. Effective October 2, 1989.)
