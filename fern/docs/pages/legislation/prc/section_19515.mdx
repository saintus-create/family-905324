# PRC § 19515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.9. FIBERGLASS RECYCLED CONTENT ACT OF 1991 > Chapter: 3. Cullet Specifications for Fiberglass Manufacturing

**Citation:** PRC § 19515

---

## Text

The quality specifications provided in this chapter are the minimum quality specifications which cullet is required to meet for purposes of the exemption specified in Section 19522.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 706, Sec. 1.)
