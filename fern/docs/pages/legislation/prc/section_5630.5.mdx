# PRC § 5630.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PARKS AND MONUMENTS > Chapter: 3.2. Roberti-Z'berg-Harris Urban Open-Space and Recreation Program > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 5630.5

---

## Text

Grants may be made to memorial districts only for indoor and outdoor park and recreation facilities that meet the requirements of this chapter and the criteria adopted pursuant to Section 5625.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 879, Sec. 4. Effective September 10, 1982.)
