# PRC § 40160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 40160

---

## Text

“Operator” means a person who operates a solid waste facility or operates a disposal site.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 500, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2009.)
