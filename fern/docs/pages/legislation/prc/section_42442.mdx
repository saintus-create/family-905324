# PRC § 42442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8. Recycled Battery Programs > Article: 1. Lead-Acid Battery Program

**Citation:** PRC § 42442

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 1991, all lead-acid batteries purchased by any state agency for, and, at the next required installation of a battery in, an automobile or light truck owned or operated by the state agency, the battery shall be a recycled lead-acid battery, to the extent that all existing stock of nonrecycled batteries have been utilized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2.)
