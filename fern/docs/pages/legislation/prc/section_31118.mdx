# PRC § 31118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Establishment and Functions of the State Coastal Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 31118

---

## Text

The conservancy may seek repayments of funds granted pursuant to this division on terms and conditions as it deems appropriate to carry out the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1551, Sec. 3.)
