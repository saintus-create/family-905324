# PRC § 42641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 12.6. Schoolsite Source Reduction and Recycling Assistance Program > Article: 5. Training, Assistance, and Information

**Citation:** PRC § 42641

---

## Text

The board shall provide training and ongoing technical and informational assistance to local agencies, offices, schools, and school districts on implementing waste reduction programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 926, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
