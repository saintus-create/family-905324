# PRC § 42495

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. WASTE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8.7. Cell Phone Recycling Act of 2004 > Article: 3. Cell Phone Recycling

**Citation:** PRC § 42495

---

## Text

On and after July 1, 2006, it is unlawful to sell a cell phone to a consumer in this state unless the retailer of that cell phone complies with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 891, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005. Inoperative September 30, 2027, pursuant to Section 42499.5. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 42499.5.)
