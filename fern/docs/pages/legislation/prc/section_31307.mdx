# PRC § 31307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE COASTAL CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 7. Urban Waterfront Restoration

**Citation:** PRC § 31307

---

## Text

The conservancy may award grants to public agencies and nonprofit organizations for the restoration of urban coastal waterfront areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 958, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2003.)
