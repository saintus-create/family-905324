# PRC § 30100.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. CALIFORNIA COASTAL ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 30100.5

---

## Text

“Coastal county” means a county or city and county which lies, in whole or in part, within the coastal zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1330.)
