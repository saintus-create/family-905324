# PRC § 4611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 8. Z'berg-Nejedly Forest Practice Act of 1973 > Article: 8. Penalties and Enforcement

**Citation:** PRC § 4611

---

## Text

Neither the board, the department, nor any person authorized by the board or the department to enter upon any lands for the purpose of taking any corrective action pursuant to this article is liable to civil action for trespass for any acts which are necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.
