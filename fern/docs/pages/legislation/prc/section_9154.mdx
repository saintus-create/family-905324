# PRC § 9154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 3. Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 1. Lands Included

**Citation:** PRC § 9154

---

## Text

The lands included in any one district may be situated in one or more counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
