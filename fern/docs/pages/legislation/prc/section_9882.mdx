# PRC § 9882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 10. Improvement Districts in Resource Conservation Districts > Article: 5. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** PRC § 9882

---

## Text

Improvement district warrants shall be made payable in amounts and at the times corresponding substantially to the amounts and times of payment of the installments of the improvement district assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 513.)
