# PRC § 14536.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.1. CALIFORNIA BEVERAGE CONTAINER RECYCLING AND LITTER REDUCTION ACT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PRC § 14536.3

---

## Text

A traffic officer, as defined in Section 625 of the Vehicle Code, or a peace officer, as specified in Section 830.1 of the Penal Code, may enforce this division as an authorized representative of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 51, Sec. 33. (SB 854) Effective June 27, 2018.)
