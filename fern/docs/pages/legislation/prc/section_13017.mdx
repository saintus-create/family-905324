# PRC § 13017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RESORT IMPROVEMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 13017

---

## Text

“Legal representative” means an officer or other person or persons appointed to serve as such by the board of directors of a corporation owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2069.)
