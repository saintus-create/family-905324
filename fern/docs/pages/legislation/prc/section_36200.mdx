# PRC § 36200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. OCEAN RESOURCES MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. California Ocean Resources Management Program

**Citation:** PRC § 36200

---

## Text

To ensure the conservation and development of ocean resources consistent with purposes of this division, a coordinated program of ocean resources planning and management is established.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1215, Sec. 1.)
