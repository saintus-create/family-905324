# PRC § 35020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. COASTAL RESOURCES AND ENERGY ASSISTANCE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 35020

---

## Text

“Coastal city” means a city or port district which lies, in whole or in part, within the coastal zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1027, Sec. 2.)
