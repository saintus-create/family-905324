# PRC § 32512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 22.5. SAN JOAQUIN RIVER CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. The San Joaquin River Conservancy

**Citation:** PRC § 32512

---

## Text

The area under the jurisdiction of the conservancy includes that area of the parkway which is acquired by the conservancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 610, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
