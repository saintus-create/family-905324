# PRC § 25106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. ENERGY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 25106

---

## Text

“Public advisor” means the public advisor employed by the commission pursuant to Section 25217.1.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 353, Sec. 2. (AB 1533) Effective January 1, 2025.)
