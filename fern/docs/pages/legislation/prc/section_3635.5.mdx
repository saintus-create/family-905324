# PRC § 3635.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 3.5. Unit Operation > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PRC § 3635.5

---

## Text

“Tracts of land” means land areas under separate ownership which are all of the following:

(a) Contiguous either on the surface or in the subsurface.

(b) Located within a field which has been producing for more than 20 years.

(c) Located within a field over 75 percent of which lies within incorporated areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1673.)
