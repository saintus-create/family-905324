# PRC § 8707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7.7. SCHOOL LANDS > Chapter: 1. School Land Bank Act

**Citation:** PRC § 8707

---

## Text

The commission shall accept the conveyances on the part of the state and shall authorize their acknowledgment and recordation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 485, Sec. 2. (AB 982) Effective January 1, 2012.)
