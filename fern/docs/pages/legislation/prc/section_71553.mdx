# PRC § 71553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 37. CALIFORNIA ENDOWMENT FOR MARINE PRESERVATION > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PRC § 71553

---

## Text

Members of the board and appropriate staff shall be available to testify before appropriate committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 687, Sec. 2. (AB 2503) Effective January 1, 2011.)
