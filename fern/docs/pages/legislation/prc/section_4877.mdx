# PRC § 4877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FORESTS, FORESTRY AND RANGE AND FORAGE LANDS > Chapter: 2. Marking Logs and Timber

**Citation:** PRC § 4877

---

## Text

Any log or timber which has any such recorded mark impressed on it shall be presumed to belong to the person, firm, or corporation in whose name the mark has been recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1144.)
