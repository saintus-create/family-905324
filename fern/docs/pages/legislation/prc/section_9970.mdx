# PRC § 9970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. RESOURCE CONSERVATION > Chapter: 13. Ventura County Resource Conservation District

**Citation:** PRC § 9970

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply only to the Ventura County Resource Conservation District. For the purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Ventura County Resource Conservation District.
