# PRC § 3432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OIL AND GAS > Chapter: 1. Oil and Gas Conservation > Article: 7. Assessment and Collection of Charges

**Citation:** PRC § 3432

---

## Text

(a) The Attorney General shall defend the action.

(b) The provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to pleadings, proofs, trials, and appeals are applicable to these proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 497, Sec. 215. (AB 991) Effective January 1, 2020.)
