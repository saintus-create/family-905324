# PEN § 31355

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Body Armor

**Citation:** PEN § 31355

---

## Text

The Department of Justice shall adopt and promulgate regulations for the fair and efficient enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
