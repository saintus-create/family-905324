# PEN § 597y

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. MALICIOUS MISCHIEF

**Citation:** PEN § 597y

---

## Text

A violation of Section 597u or 597v is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 296, Sec. 206. (AB 1023) Effective January 1, 2012.)
