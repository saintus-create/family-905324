# PEN § 2621.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 3. Civil Rights of Prisoners > Article: 2. Prisoners as Witnesses

**Citation:** PEN § 2621.5

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 2620 and 2621 which impose a charge upon the counties shall not apply to cases coming within the provisions of Section 4750.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1310, Sec. 4.)
