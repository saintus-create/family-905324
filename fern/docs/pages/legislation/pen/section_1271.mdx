# PEN § 1271

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 1. In What Cases the Defendant May Be Admitted to Bail

**Citation:** PEN § 1271

---

## Text

If the charge is for any other offense, he may be admitted to bail before conviction, as a matter of right.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
