# PEN § 2717.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 1.5. Joint Venture Program

**Citation:** PEN § 2717.9

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a prisoner who participates in a joint venture program is ineligible for unemployment benefits upon his or her release from prison based upon participation in that program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 440, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 194 at the March 26, 1996, election.)
