# PEN § 1299.05

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 5.5. Bail Fugitive Recovery Persons Act

**Citation:** PEN § 1299.05

---

## Text

In performing a bail fugitive apprehension, an individual authorized by Section 1299.02 to apprehend a bail fugitive shall comply with all laws applicable to that apprehension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 747, Sec. 1. (AB 2029) Effective January 1, 2013.)
