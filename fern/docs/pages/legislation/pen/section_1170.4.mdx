# PEN § 1170.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 4.5. Trial Court Sentencing > Article: 1. Initial Sentencing

**Citation:** PEN § 1170.4

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall collect and analyze relevant information relating to sentencing practices in this state and other jurisdictions. Such information shall be taken into consideration by the Judicial Council in the adoption of rules pursuant to Section 1170.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 909, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1994.)
