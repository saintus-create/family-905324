# PEN § 2045

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 5. Correctional Training Facility

**Citation:** PEN § 2045

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections with the approval of the Board of Corrections, is authorized to establish a State prison for the confinement of males under the custody of the Director of Corrections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 75.)
