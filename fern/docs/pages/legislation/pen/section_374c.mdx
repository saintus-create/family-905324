# PEN § 374c

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY

**Citation:** PEN § 374c

---

## Text

Every person who shoots any firearm from or upon a public road or highway is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1933, Ch. 203.)
