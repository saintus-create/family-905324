# PEN § 1503

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Writ of Habeas Corpus

**Citation:** PEN § 1503

---

## Text

All writs, warrants, process, and subpoenas authorized by the provisions of this Chapter must be issued by the Clerk of the Court, and, except subpoenas, must be sealed with the seal of such Court, and served and returned forthwith, unless the Court or Judge shall specify a particular time for any such return.
