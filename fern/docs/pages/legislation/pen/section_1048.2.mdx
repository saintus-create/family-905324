# PEN § 1048.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 8. Formation of the Trial Jury and theCalendar of Issues for Trial

**Citation:** PEN § 1048.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (b) of Section 1048, for good cause shown, the court may grant priority to an action for an alleged violation of Section 236.1 as the court, in its discretion, may determine to be appropriate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 644, Sec. 4. (AB 2498) Effective January 1, 2017.)
