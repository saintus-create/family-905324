# PEN § 336.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 10. Gaming

**Citation:** PEN § 336.5

---

## Text

Gaming chips may be used on the gaming floor by a patron of a gambling establishment, as defined in subdivision (o) of Section 19805 of the Business and Professions Code, to pay for food and beverage items that are served at the table.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 162, Sec. 123. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2013.)
