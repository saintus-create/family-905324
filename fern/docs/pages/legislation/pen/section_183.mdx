# PEN § 183

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 8. Conspiracy

**Citation:** PEN § 183

---

## Text

No conspiracies, other than those enumerated in the preceding section, are punishable criminally.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
