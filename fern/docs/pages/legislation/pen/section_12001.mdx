# PEN § 12001

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. SENTENCE ENHANCEMENTS

**Citation:** PEN § 12001

---

## Text

As used in this title, “firearm” has the meaning provided in subdivision (a) of Section 16520.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 5. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
