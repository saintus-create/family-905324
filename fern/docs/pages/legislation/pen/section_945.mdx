# PEN § 945

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. THE PLEADINGS > Chapter: 1. Finding and Presentment of the Indictment

**Citation:** PEN § 945

---

## Text

When an indictment is found against a defendant not in custody, the same proceedings must be had as are prescribed in Sections 979 to 984, inclusive, against a defendant who fails to appear for arraignment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
