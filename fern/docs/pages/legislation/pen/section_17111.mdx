# PEN § 17111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 17111

---

## Text

For purposes of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 29030) of Division 7 of Title 4, use of the term “secure facility” is governed by Sections 29141 and 29142.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
