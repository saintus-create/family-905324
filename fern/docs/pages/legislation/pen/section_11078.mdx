# PEN § 11078

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 2.5. Criminal Record Dissemination

**Citation:** PEN § 11078

---

## Text

Each agency holding or receiving criminal offender record information in a computerized system shall maintain, for such period as is found by the Attorney General to be appropriate, a listing of the agencies to which it has released or communicated such information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1437.)
