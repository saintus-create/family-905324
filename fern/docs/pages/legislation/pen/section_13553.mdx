# PEN § 13553

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 5. Local Law Enforcement Accreditation

**Citation:** PEN § 13553

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall prohibit a law enforcement agency from establishing standards that exceed the minimum accreditation standards set by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1249, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
