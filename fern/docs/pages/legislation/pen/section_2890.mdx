# PEN § 2890

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 6. Sale of Prison-Made Goods > Article: 2. Sale of Prison Goods Made Outside California

**Citation:** PEN § 2890

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to any goods, wares or merchandise manufactured in any penitentiary or prison of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
