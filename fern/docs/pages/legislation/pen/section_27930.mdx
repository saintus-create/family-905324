# PEN § 27930

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 6. Exceptions to the Requirement of Using a Dealer for a Private Party Firearms Transaction

**Citation:** PEN § 27930

---

## Text

Section 27545 does not apply to deliveries, transfers, or returns of firearms made pursuant to any of the following:

(a) Sections 18000 and 18005.

(b) Division 4 (commencing with Section 18250) of Title 2.

(c) Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 33850) of Division 11.

(d) Sections 34005 and 34010.

(e) Section 29810.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 63, Sec. 10.2.)
