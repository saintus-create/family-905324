# PEN § 3603

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. EXECUTION OF DEATH PENALTY > Chapter: 1. Executing Death Penalty

**Citation:** PEN § 3603

---

## Text

The judgment of death shall be executed within the walls of the California State Prison at San Quentin.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 558, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
