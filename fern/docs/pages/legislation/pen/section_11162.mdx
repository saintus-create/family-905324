# PEN § 11162

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 2. Reports of Injuries

**Citation:** PEN § 11162

---

## Text

A violation of this article is a misdemeanor, punishable by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding six months, or by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or by both that fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 992, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
