# PEN § 2767

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 4. Employment at Road Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 2767

---

## Text

No prisoner while engaged in such construction, maintenance and improvement of a state highway shall drive a motor truck or other vehicle or wagon outside of the limits established for the camp or construction work.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1380.)
