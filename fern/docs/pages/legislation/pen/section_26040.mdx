# PEN § 26040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Carrying a Loaded Firearm > Article: 4. Other Exemptions to the Crime of Carrying a Loaded Firearm in Public

**Citation:** PEN § 26040

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 25850 shall prevent any person from carrying a loaded firearm in an area within an incorporated city while engaged in hunting, provided that the hunting at that place and time is not prohibited by the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
