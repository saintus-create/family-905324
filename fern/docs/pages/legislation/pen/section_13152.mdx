# PEN § 13152

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 3. Reporting Information

**Citation:** PEN § 13152

---

## Text

Both admission and release from detention facilities shall be reported by the detention agency to the Department of Justice within 30 days of that action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 814, Sec. 1. (AB 2080) Effective January 1, 2019.)
