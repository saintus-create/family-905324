# PEN § 19910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. BB DEVICES > Title: 3. WEAPONS AND DEVICES OTHER THAN FIREARMS

**Citation:** PEN § 19910

---

## Text

Every person who sells any BB device to a minor is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
