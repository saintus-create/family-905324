# PEN § 22296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SAPS AND SIMILAR WEAPONS > Title: 3. WEAPONS AND DEVICES OTHER THAN FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Metal Knuckles

**Citation:** PEN § 22296

---

## Text

As used in this part, a “billy,” “blackjack,” or “slungshot” does not include a nunchaku.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 434, Sec. 27. (SB 827) Effective January 1, 2022.)
