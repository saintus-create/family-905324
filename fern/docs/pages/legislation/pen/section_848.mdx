# PEN § 848

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 5. Arrest, by Whom and How Made

**Citation:** PEN § 848

---

## Text

An officer making an arrest, in obedience to a warrant, must proceed with the person arrested as commanded by the warrant, or as provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
