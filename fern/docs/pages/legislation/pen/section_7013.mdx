# PEN § 7013

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 11. Master Plan Construction

**Citation:** PEN § 7013

---

## Text

The Department of Corrections shall contract, or make a good-faith effort to contract, with the Department of Water Resources or the Bureau of Reclamation, or both, to secure a water supply for the prison at Avenal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 931, Sec. 2.5. Effective September 25, 1985.)
