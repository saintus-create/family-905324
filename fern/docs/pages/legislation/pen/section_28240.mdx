# PEN § 28240

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Recordkeeping, Background Checks, and Fees Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 3. Submission of Fees and Firearm Purchaser Information to the Department of Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 28240

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specified, each fee prescribed in this article shall only be charged once for a single transaction on the same date for taking title or possession of any number of firearms.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 540, Sec. 14. (AB 3064) Effective January 1, 2025.)
