# PEN § 2022

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 2. San Quentin Rehabilitation Center

**Citation:** PEN § 2022

---

## Text

The primary purpose of the San Quentin Rehabilitation Center shall be to provide humane confinement, rehabilitation and education programming, restorative justice, industrial and other training, treatment, and care to persons confined therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 47, Sec. 17. (AB 134) Effective July 10, 2023.)
