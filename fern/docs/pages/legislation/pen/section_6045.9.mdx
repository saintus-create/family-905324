# PEN § 6045.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 5. The Corrections Standards Authority > Article: 4. Mentally Ill Offender Crime Reduction Grants

**Citation:** PEN § 6045.9

---

## Text

The board may use up to 5 percent of the funds appropriated for purposes of this article to administer this program, including technical assistance to counties and the development of the evaluation component.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 26, Sec. 32. (AB 1468) Effective June 20, 2014.)
