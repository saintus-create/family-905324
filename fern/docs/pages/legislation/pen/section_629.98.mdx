# PEN § 629.98

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. MISCELLANEOUS CRIMES > Chapter: 1.4. Interception of Wire, Electronic Digital Pager, or Electronic Cellular Telephone Communications

**Citation:** PEN § 629.98

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 488, Sec. 1. (SB 514) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 1.4, commencing with Section 629.50.)
