# PEN § 3200

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. IMPRISONMENT OF FEMALE PRISONERS IN STATE INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of Institution for Women

**Citation:** PEN § 3200

---

## Text

There is and shall continue to be within the State an institution for the punishment, treatment, supervision, custody and care of females convicted of felonies to be known as “The California Institution for Women.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
