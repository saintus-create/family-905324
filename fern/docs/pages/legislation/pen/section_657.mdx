# PEN § 657

**Hierarchy:** Title: 16. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 657

---

## Text

A criminal act is not the less punishable as a crime because it is also declared to be punishable as a contempt.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
