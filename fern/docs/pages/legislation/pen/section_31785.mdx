# PEN § 31785

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Handguns and Firearm Safety > Article: 3. Exceptions to Firearm Safety Certificate Requirement

**Citation:** PEN § 31785

---

## Text

Subdivision (a) of Section 31615 does not apply to the sale, delivery, or transfer of unloaded firearms to a wholesaler if the firearms are being returned to the wholesaler and are intended as merchandise in the wholesaler’s business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
