# PEN § 11107.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 3. Criminal Identification and Statistics

**Citation:** PEN § 11107.5

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall report annually to the Legislature concerning the information pertaining to the sexual abuse of children reported to the Department of Justice pursuant to Sections 11107 and 11169. No confidential information shall be released in the reports submitted to the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 592, Sec. 1.)
