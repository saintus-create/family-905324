# PEN § 199

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON > Chapter: 1. Homicide

**Citation:** PEN § 199

---

## Text

The homicide appearing to be justifiable or excusable, the person indicted must, upon his trial, be fully acquitted and discharged.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
