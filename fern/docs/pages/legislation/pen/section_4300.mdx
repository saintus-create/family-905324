# PEN § 4300

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 3. Advisory Committees for Adult Detention Facilities

**Citation:** PEN § 4300

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may establish in each county a county advisory committee on adult detention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 349.)
