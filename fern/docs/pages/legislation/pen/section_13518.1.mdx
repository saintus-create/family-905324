# PEN § 13518.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 2. Field Services, Standards, and Certification

**Citation:** PEN § 13518.1

---

## Text

In order to prevent the spread of communicable disease, a law enforcement agency employing peace officers described in subdivision (a) of Section 13518 may provide to each of these peace officers an appropriate portable manual mask and airway assembly for use when applying cardiopulmonary resuscitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 28, Sec. 46. (SB 71) Effective June 27, 2013.)
