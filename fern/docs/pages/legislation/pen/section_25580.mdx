# PEN § 25580

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Carrying a Concealed Firearm > Article: 3. Conditional Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 25580

---

## Text

Section 25400 does not apply to, or affect, the transportation of a firearm that is a curio or relic, as defined in Section 478.11 of Title 27 of the Code of Federal Regulations, by a person in order to comply with Section 27565 as it pertains to that firearm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
