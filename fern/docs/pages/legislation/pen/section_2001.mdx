# PEN § 2001

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 1. California Institution for Men

**Citation:** PEN § 2001

## Text

The California Institution for Men shall be located at Chino, San Bernardino County, California.
