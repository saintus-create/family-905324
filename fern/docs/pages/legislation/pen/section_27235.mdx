# PEN § 27235

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Gun Show or Event > Article: 1. Gun Show or Event

**Citation:** PEN § 27235

---

## Text

Every producer of a gun show or event shall have a written contract with each gun show vendor selling firearms or ammunition at the show or event.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 736, Sec. 5. (AB 1669) Effective January 1, 2020.)
