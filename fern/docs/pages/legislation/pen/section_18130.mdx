# PEN § 18130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Temporary Emergency Gun Violence Restraining Order

**Citation:** PEN § 18130

---

## Text

A temporary emergency gun violence restraining order is valid only if it is issued by a judicial officer after making the findings required by Section 18125 and pursuant to a specific request by a law enforcement officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 872, Sec. 3. (AB 1014) Effective January 1, 2015. Section operative January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 18122.)
