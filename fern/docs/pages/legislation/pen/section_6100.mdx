# PEN § 6100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 8. The Medical Facility

**Citation:** PEN § 6100

---

## Text

There is hereby established an institution under the jurisdiction of the Department of Corrections to be known as the Medical Facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1491.)
