# PEN § 11152

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 1. Release of Persons Convicted of Arson

**Citation:** PEN § 11152

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a notice as provided in Section 11150 or 11151, the State Fire Marshal shall notify all regularly organized fire departments in the county in which the person was convicted and, if known, in the county in which he is to reside.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 919, Sec. 3.)
