# PEN § 4060

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1.5. Joint County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4060

---

## Text

The members of the board of directors may enter into an agreement for and on behalf of the counties appointing them binding said counties to the joint enterprise provided for in this chapter and apportioning the cost of establishing and maintaining a joint county jail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1019.)
