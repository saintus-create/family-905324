# PEN § 2643

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 3. Civil Rights of Prisoners > Article: 3. Sexual Abuse in Detention

**Citation:** PEN § 2643

---

## Text

The provisions of this act are severable. If any provision of this act or its application is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 303, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
