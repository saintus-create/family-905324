# PEN § 11080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 2.5. Criminal Record Dissemination

**Citation:** PEN § 11080

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to affect the right of access of any person or public agency to individual criminal offender record information that is authorized by any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1437.)
