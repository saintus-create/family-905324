# PEN § 11073.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 2.45. Tribal Police Pilot Program

**Citation:** PEN § 11073.5

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2032, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 10, Sec. 21. (AB 134) Effective June 27, 2025. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 11073.6. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 2.45, comprising Sections 11073 to 11073.6.)
