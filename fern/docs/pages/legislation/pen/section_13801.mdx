# PEN § 13801

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. CALIFORNIA COUNCIL ON CRIMINAL JUSTICE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PEN § 13801

---

## Text

Nothing in this title shall be construed as authorizing the board, or the local boards to undertake direct operational criminal justice responsibilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 36, Sec. 55.5. (SB 92) Effective June 30, 2011.)
