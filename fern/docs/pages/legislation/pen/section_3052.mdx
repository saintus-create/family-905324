# PEN § 3052

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3. Paroles

**Citation:** PEN § 3052

---

## Text

The Board of Parole Hearings shall have the power to establish and enforce rules and regulations under which inmates committed to state prisons may be allowed to go upon parole outside the prison buildings and enclosures when eligible for parole.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 470, Sec. 12. (SB 230) Effective January 1, 2016.)
