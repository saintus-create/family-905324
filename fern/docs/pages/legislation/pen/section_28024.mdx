# PEN § 28024

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4.1. Registration and Assignment of Firearms by Private Patrol Operators

**Citation:** PEN § 28024

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on July 1, 2016.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 423, Sec. 6. (AB 2220) Effective January 1, 2015. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (July 1, 2016) for Chapter 4.1, commencing with Section 28010.)
