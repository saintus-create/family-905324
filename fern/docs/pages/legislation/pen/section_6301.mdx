# PEN § 6301

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10. Regional Jail Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 6301

---

## Text

The primary purpose of the camps shall be the confinement, treatment, and care of persons sentenced to long jail terms, including persons so imprisoned as a condition of probation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 828, Sec. 166.)
