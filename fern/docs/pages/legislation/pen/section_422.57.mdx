# PEN § 422.57

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11.6. CIVIL RIGHTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PEN § 422.57

---

## Text

For purposes this code, unless an explicit provision of law or the context clearly requires a different meaning, “gender” has the same meaning as in Section 422.56.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 700, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2005.)
