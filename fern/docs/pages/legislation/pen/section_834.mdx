# PEN § 834

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 5. Arrest, by Whom and How Made

**Citation:** PEN § 834

---

## Text

An arrest is taking a person into custody, in a case and in the manner authorized by law. An arrest may be made by a peace officer or by a private person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
