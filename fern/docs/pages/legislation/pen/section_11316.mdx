# PEN § 11316

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 3. Prevention and Abatement of Unlawful Activities > Article: 3. Control of Gambling Ships

**Citation:** PEN § 11316

---

## Text

Any person, firm, association or corporation, either as principal, agent, servant, employee or otherwise, who violates any of the provisions of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 35.)
