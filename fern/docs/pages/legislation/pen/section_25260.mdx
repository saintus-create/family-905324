# PEN § 25260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. LOST OR STOLEN FIREARMS > Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Storage of Firearm Where Child Obtains Access and Carries Firearm Off-Premises

**Citation:** PEN § 25260

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 11108.2, every sheriff or police chief shall submit a description of each firearm that has been reported lost or stolen directly into the Department of Justice Automated Firearms System.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 864, Sec. 6. (AB 2222) Effective January 1, 2019. Note: This section was added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 63.)
