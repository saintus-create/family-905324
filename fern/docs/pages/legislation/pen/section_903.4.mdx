# PEN § 903.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2. Formation of Grand Jury > Article: 3. Jury Commissioners

**Citation:** PEN § 903.4

---

## Text

The judges are not required to select any name from the list returned by the jury commissioner, but may, if in their judgment the due administration of justice requires, make every or any selection from among the body of persons in the county suitable and competent to serve as grand jurors regardless of the list returned by the jury commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 296, Sec. 214. (AB 1023) Effective January 1, 2012.)
