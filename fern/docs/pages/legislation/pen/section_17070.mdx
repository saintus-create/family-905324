# PEN § 17070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 17070

---

## Text

As used in this part, “responsible adult” means a person at least 21 years of age who is not prohibited by state or federal law from possessing, receiving, owning, or purchasing a firearm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
