# PEN § 422.78

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11.6. CIVIL RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Crimes and Penalties

**Citation:** PEN § 422.78

---

## Text

The prosecuting agency of each county shall have the primary responsibility for the enforcement of orders issued pursuant to this title or Section 52.1 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 700, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2005.)
