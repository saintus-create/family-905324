# PEN § 1554

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 4. Proceedings Against Fugitives From Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 1554

---

## Text

The Governor may recall his warrant of arrest or may issue another warrant whenever he deems it proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 554.)
