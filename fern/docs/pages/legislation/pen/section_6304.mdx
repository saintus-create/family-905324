# PEN § 6304

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10. Regional Jail Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 6304

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections may return to the committing authority any person committed transferred to a regional jail camp pursuant to this chapter when there is no suitable employment or when such person is guilty of any violation of rules and regulations of the regional jail camp.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1646.)
