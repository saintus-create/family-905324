# PEN § 11415

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 3. Prevention and Abatement of Unlawful Activities > Article: 4.6. The Hertzberg-Alarcon California Prevention of Terrorism Act

**Citation:** PEN § 11415

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Hertzberg-Alarcon California Prevention of Terrorism Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 563, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
