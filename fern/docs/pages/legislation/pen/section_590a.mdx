# PEN § 590a

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 15. Malicious Injuries to Railroad Bridges, Highways, Bridges, and Telegraphs

**Citation:** PEN § 590a

---

## Text

One-half of all fines imposed and collected under Section 590 shall be paid to the informer who first causes a complaint to be filed charging the defendant with the violation of Section 590.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 828, Sec. 35.)
