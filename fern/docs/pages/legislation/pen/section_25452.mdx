# PEN § 25452

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Carrying a Concealed Firearm > Article: 2. Peace Officer Exemption

**Citation:** PEN § 25452

---

## Text

A peace officer and an honorably retired peace officer shall, when leaving a handgun in an unattended vehicle, secure the handgun in the vehicle pursuant to Section 25140.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 651, Sec. 2. (SB 869) Effective January 1, 2017.)
