# PEN § 172.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 7. Other Offenses Against Public Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 172.8

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 172a shall not apply to the sale of alcoholic beverages for consumption on the premises, by a nonprofit organization at a municipally owned conference center located more than one but less than 112 miles from the California Institute of Technology in Pasadena.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 88.)
