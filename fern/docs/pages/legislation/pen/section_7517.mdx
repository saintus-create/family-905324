# PEN § 7517

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. MEDICAL TESTING OF PRISONERS > Chapter: 2. Procedures for Requiring HIV Testing

**Citation:** PEN § 7517

---

## Text

Except as otherwise permitted by this title or any provision of law, any records, including decisions of a chief medical officer or an appeals panel, compiled pursuant to this chapter shall be confidential.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1579, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1988.)
