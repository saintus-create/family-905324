# PEN § 27730

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 4. Exceptions to Restrictions on Delivery of a Firearm

**Citation:** PEN § 27730

---

## Text

Until January 1, 2014, Section 27540 does not apply to the sale, delivery, or transfer of an unloaded firearm, other than a handgun, by a dealer to himself or herself.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 745, Sec. 37. (AB 809) Effective January 1, 2012.)
