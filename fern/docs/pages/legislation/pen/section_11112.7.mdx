# PEN § 11112.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 3.5. Fingerprints and Photographs

**Citation:** PEN § 11112.7

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall provide an annual status report to the Legislature beginning January 1, 1987, with the final report due January 1, 1990. The report shall include the status of the project to date, funds expended, and need, if any, for revision to the master plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1263, Sec. 3.)
