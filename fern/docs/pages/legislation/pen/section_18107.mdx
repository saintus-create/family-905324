# PEN § 18107

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** PEN § 18107

---

## Text

A petition for a gun violence restraining order shall describe the number, types, and locations of any firearms and ammunition presently believed by the petitioner to be possessed or controlled by the subject of the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 872, Sec. 3. (AB 1014) Effective January 1, 2015. Section operative January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 18122.)
