# PEN § 838

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 5. Arrest, by Whom and How Made

**Citation:** PEN § 838

---

## Text

A magistrate may orally order a peace officer or private person to arrest any one committing or attempting to commit a public offense in the presence of such magistrate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
