# PEN § 2760

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 4. Employment at Road Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 2760

---

## Text

The Department of Transportation of the State of California may employ or cause to be employed, prisoners confined in the state prisons in the improvement and maintenance of any state highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1549, Sec. 19.)
