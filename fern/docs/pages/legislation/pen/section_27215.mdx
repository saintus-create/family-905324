# PEN § 27215

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Gun Show or Event > Article: 1. Gun Show or Event

**Citation:** PEN § 27215

---

## Text

The producer of a gun show or event shall be responsible for informing prospective gun show vendors of the requirements of this article and of Article 2 (commencing with Section 27300) that apply to vendors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
