# PEN § 32410

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 5. Large-Capacity Magazine > Article: 2. Exceptions Relating Specifically to Large-Capacity Magazines

**Citation:** PEN § 32410

---

## Text

Section 32310 does not apply to the sale, purchase, or possession of any large-capacity magazine to or by a person licensed pursuant to Sections 26700 to 26915, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 63, Sec. 6.5.)
