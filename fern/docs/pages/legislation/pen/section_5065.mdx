# PEN § 5065

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 2. The Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation

**Citation:** PEN § 5065

---

## Text

When any personal property has been destroyed as provided in Section 5061 or 5062, no suit shall thereafter be maintained by any person against the State or any officer thereof for or on account of such property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1708.)
