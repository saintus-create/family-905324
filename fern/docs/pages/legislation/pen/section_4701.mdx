# PEN § 4701

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. OFFENSES RELATING TO PRISONS AND PRISONERS > Chapter: 5. Trials of Prisoners

**Citation:** PEN § 4701

---

## Text

The jurisdiction of a criminal action for escaping from any State prison is in any county of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
