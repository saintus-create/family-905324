# PEN § 13775

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5.7. REPRODUCTIVE RIGHTS LAW ENFORCEMENT ACT

**Citation:** PEN § 13775

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Reproductive Rights Law Enforcement Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 899, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
