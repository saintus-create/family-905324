# PEN § 1334

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3a. Attendance of Witnesses Outside the State

**Citation:** PEN § 1334

---

## Text

This chapter may be cited as the Uniform Act to Secure the Attendance of Witnesses from without the State in Criminal Cases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 262.)
