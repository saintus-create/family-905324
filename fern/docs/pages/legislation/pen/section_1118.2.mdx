# PEN § 1118.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 2. The Trial

**Citation:** PEN § 1118.2

---

## Text

A judgment of acquittal entered pursuant to the provisions of Section 1118 or 1118.1 shall not be appealable and is a bar to any other prosecution for the same offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 256.)
