# PEN § 1042

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 7. The Mode of Trial

**Citation:** PEN § 1042

---

## Text

Issues of fact shall be tried in the manner provided in Article I, Section 16 of the Constitution of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 787, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2003.)
