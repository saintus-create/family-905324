# PEN § 685

**Hierarchy:** Part: 2. OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Title: 17. RIGHTS OF VICTIMS AND WITNESSES OF CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 685

---

## Text

The party prosecuted in a criminal action is designated in this Code as the defendant.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
