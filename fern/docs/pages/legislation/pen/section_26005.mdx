# PEN § 26005

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Carrying a Loaded Firearm > Article: 4. Other Exemptions to the Crime of Carrying a Loaded Firearm in Public

**Citation:** PEN § 26005

---

## Text

Section 25850 does not apply to either of the following:

(a) Persons who are using target ranges for the purpose of practice shooting with a firearm.

(b) Members of shooting clubs while hunting on the premises of those clubs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
