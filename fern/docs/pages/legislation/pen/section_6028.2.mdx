# PEN § 6028.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 5. The Corrections Standards Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 6028.2

---

## Text

The Secretary of the Youth and Adult Correctional Agency may furnish for the use of any such commission such facilities, supplies, and personnel as may be available therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1437, Sec. 5.)
