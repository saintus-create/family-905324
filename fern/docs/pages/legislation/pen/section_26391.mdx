# PEN § 26391

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Openly Carrying an Unloaded Handgun > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26391

---

## Text

Section 26350 does not apply to, or affect, the open carrying of an unloaded handgun when done in accordance with the provisions of subdivision (d) of Section 171.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 700, Sec. 11. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2013.)
