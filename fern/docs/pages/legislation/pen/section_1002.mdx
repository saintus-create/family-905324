# PEN § 1002

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 3. Demurrer and Amendment

**Citation:** PEN § 1002

---

## Text

The only pleading on the part of the defendant is either a demurrer or a plea.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
