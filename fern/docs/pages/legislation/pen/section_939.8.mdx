# PEN § 939.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Grand Jury > Article: 4. Conduct of Investigations

**Citation:** PEN § 939.8

---

## Text

The grand jury shall find an indictment when all the evidence before it, taken together, if unexplained or uncontradicted, would, in its judgment, warrant a conviction by a trial jury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 501.)
