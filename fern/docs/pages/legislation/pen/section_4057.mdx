# PEN § 4057

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1.5. Joint County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4057

---

## Text

All districts organized under this chapter shall be numbered in the order of their creation, the number to be assigned to said district forthwith upon the organization thereof by the Secretary of State, and the Secretary of State shall keep and maintain in his office a list and register showing the joint county jail districts organized under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1019.)
