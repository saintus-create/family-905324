# PEN § 25595

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Carrying a Concealed Firearm > Article: 3. Conditional Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 25595

---

## Text

This article does not prohibit or limit the otherwise lawful carrying or transportation of any handgun in accordance with the provisions listed in Section 16580.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 725, Sec. 12. (AB 144) Effective January 1, 2012.)
