# PEN § 2768

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 4. Employment at Road Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 2768

---

## Text

Said prisoners when employed under the provisions of this article shall not be used for the purpose of building any bridge or structure of like character which requires the employment of skilled labor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
