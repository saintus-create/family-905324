# PEN § 2889

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 6. Sale of Prison-Made Goods > Article: 2. Sale of Prison Goods Made Outside California

**Citation:** PEN § 2889

## Text

The enforcement of the provisions of this article shall be under the supervision of the State Superintendent of Weights and Measures.
