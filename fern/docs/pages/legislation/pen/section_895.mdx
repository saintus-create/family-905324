# PEN § 895

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2. Formation of Grand Jury > Article: 2. Listing and Selection of Grand Jurors

**Citation:** PEN § 895

---

## Text

During the month preceding the beginning of the fiscal year of the county, the superior court of each county shall make an order designating the estimated number of grand jurors that will, in the opinion of the court, be required for the transaction of the business of the court during the ensuing fiscal year as provided in Section 905.5.
