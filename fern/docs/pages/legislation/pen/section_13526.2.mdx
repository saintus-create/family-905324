# PEN § 13526.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 3. Peace Officers’ Training Fund and Allocations Therefrom

**Citation:** PEN § 13526.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13526, for the purposes of this chapter, the housing authority police departments of the City of Los Angeles and the City of Oakland shall be entitled to receive funding from the State Penalty Fund, pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 49. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
