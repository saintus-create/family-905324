# PEN § 27130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Issuance, Forfeiture, and Conditions of License to Sell, Lease, or Transfer Firearms at Retail > Article: 6. Other Exceptions

**Citation:** PEN § 27130

---

## Text

Until January 1, 2014, Article 1 (commencing with Section 26700) and Article 2 (commencing with Section 26800) do not apply to the sale, delivery, or transfer of an unloaded firearm, other than a handgun, by a dealer to himself or herself.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 745, Sec. 22. (AB 809) Effective January 1, 2012.)
