# PEN § 4490

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.7. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE BOND ACT OF 1986 > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 4490

---

## Text

The committee may authorize the Treasurer to sell all or any part of the bonds herein authorized at such time or times as may be fixed by the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 52 at the June 3, 1986, election.)
