# PEN § 2913

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 7. Execution of Sentences of Imprisonment > Article: 1.5. Transfer of Prisoners

**Citation:** PEN § 2913

---

## Text

A city shall give notice to, and consult with, the county prior to contracting with the state pursuant to Section 2910 of this code or Section 1753.3 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1450, Sec. 2.6.)
