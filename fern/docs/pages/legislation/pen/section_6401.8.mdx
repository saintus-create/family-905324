# PEN § 6401.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10.7. Prison Visitation

**Citation:** PEN § 6401.8

---

## Text

The operation of Sections 6401 and 6401.5 is contingent upon the appropriation of funds for these sections by express reference in the annual Budget Act or another statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 837, Sec. 4. (SB 1139) Effective January 1, 2023.)
