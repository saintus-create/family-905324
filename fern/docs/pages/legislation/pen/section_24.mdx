# PEN § 24

**Hierarchy:** Title: OF. THE ACT

**Citation:** PEN § 24

---

## Text

This Act, whenever cited, enumerated, referred to, or amended, may be designated simply as The Penal Code, adding, when necessary, the number of the section.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
