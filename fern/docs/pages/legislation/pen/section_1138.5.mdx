# PEN § 1138.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 3. Conduct of the Jury After the Cause Is Submitted to Them

**Citation:** PEN § 1138.5

---

## Text

Except for good cause shown, the judge in his of her discretion need not be present in the court while testimony previously received in evidence is read to the jury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 88, Sec. 2. Effective July 2, 1987.)
