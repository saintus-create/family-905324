# PEN § 983

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 1. Of the Arraignment of the Defendant

**Citation:** PEN § 983

---

## Text

The bench warrant may be served in any county in the same manner as a warrant of arrest.
