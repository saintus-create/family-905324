# PEN § 23500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Title: 4. FIREARMS

**Citation:** PEN § 23500

---

## Text

The provisions listed in Section 16580 shall be known and may be cited as “The Dangerous Weapons Control Law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
