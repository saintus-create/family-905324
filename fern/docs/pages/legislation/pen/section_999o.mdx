# PEN § 999o

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.3. Repeat Sexual Offenders

**Citation:** PEN § 999o

---

## Text

The characterization of a defendant as a “repeat sexual offender” as defined by this chapter shall not be communicated to the trier of fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1078, Sec. 1.)
