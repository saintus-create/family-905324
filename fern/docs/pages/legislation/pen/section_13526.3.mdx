# PEN § 13526.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 3. Peace Officers’ Training Fund and Allocations Therefrom

**Citation:** PEN § 13526.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13526, for the purposes of this chapter, joint powers agencies formed pursuant to Article 1 (commencing with Section 6500) of Chapter 5 of Division 7 of Title 1 of the Government Code shall be entitled to receive funding from the State Penalty Fund, pursuant to this article. This section is declaratory of existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 50. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
