# PEN § 6208

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9. Conservation Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6208

---

## Text

Any persons under the custody of the Director of Corrections may be transferred to the conservation centers in accordance with law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1431.)
