# PEN § 803.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 2. Time of Commencing Criminal Actions

**Citation:** PEN § 803.5

---

## Text

With respect to a violation of Section 115 or 530.5, a limitation of time prescribed in this chapter does not commence to run until the discovery of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 468, Sec. 10.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
