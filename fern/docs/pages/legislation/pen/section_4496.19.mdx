# PEN § 4496.19

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.8. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND YOUTH FACILITY BOND ACT OF 1988 > Chapter: 2. Program

**Citation:** PEN § 4496.19

---

## Text

Money in the fund may only be expended for projects specified in this chapter as allocated in appropriations made by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 264, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 86 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
