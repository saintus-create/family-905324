# PEN § 4011.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1. County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4011.2

---

## Text

A sheriff, chief or director of corrections, or chief of police shall not charge a fee for an inmate-initiated medical visit of an inmate confined in a county or city jail.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 570, Sec. 2. (AB 45) Effective January 1, 2020.)
