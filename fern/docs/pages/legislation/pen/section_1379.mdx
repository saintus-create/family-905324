# PEN § 1379

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 7. Compromising Certain Public Offenses by Leave of the Court

**Citation:** PEN § 1379

---

## Text

No public offense can be compromised, nor can any proceeding or prosecution for the punishment thereof upon a compromise be stayed, except as provided in this Chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
