# PEN § 13012.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 1. Department Of Justice > Article: 1. Duties of the Department

**Citation:** PEN § 13012.6

---

## Text

The data published by the department on the OpenJustice Web portal pursuant to Section 13010 shall include information concerning arrests for violations of Section 530.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 418, Sec. 8. (AB 2524) Effective January 1, 2017.)
