# PEN § 11102

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 3. Criminal Identification and Statistics

**Citation:** PEN § 11102

---

## Text

The department may use the following systems of identification: the Bertillon, the fingerprint system, and any system of measurement that may be adopted by law in the various penal institutions of the state.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1377.)
