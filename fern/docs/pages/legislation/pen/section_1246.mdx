# PEN § 1246

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 1. Appeals, When Allowed and How Taken, and the Effect Thereof

**Citation:** PEN § 1246

---

## Text

The record on appeal shall be made up and filed in such time and manner as shall be prescribed in rules adopted by the Judicial Council.
