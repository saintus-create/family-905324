# PEN § 681

**Hierarchy:** Part: 2. OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Title: 17. RIGHTS OF VICTIMS AND WITNESSES OF CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 681

---

## Text

No person can be punished for a public offense, except upon a legal conviction in a Court having jurisdiction thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
