# PEN § 33225

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 8. Short-Barreled Rifle or Short-Barreled Shotgun > Article: 1. Restrictions Relating to Short-Barreled Rifle or Short-Barreled Shotgun

**Citation:** PEN § 33225

---

## Text

Section 33215 does not apply to the manufacture, possession, transportation, or sale of a short-barreled rifle or short-barreled shotgun, when authorized by the Department of Justice pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 33300) and not in violation of federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
