# PEN § 6025.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 5. The Corrections Standards Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 6025.6

---

## Text

The Board of Corrections may delegate any ministerial authority or duty conferred or imposed upon the board to a subordinate officer subject to those conditions as it may choose to impose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1017, Sec. 2.)
