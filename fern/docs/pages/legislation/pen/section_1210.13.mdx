# PEN § 1210.13

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF JUDGMENT AND EXECUTION > Chapter: 1.4. Electronic Monitoring

**Citation:** PEN § 1210.13

---

## Text

A county chief probation officer may revoke, in his or her discretion, the continuous monitoring of any individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 484, Sec. 1. Effective October 4, 2005.)
