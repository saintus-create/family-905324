# PEN § 2085

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of State Prisons > Article: 2. Wardens

**Citation:** PEN § 2085

---

## Text

The department shall keep a correct account of all money and valuables upon the prisoner when delivered at the prison, and shall pay the amount, or the proceeds thereof, or return the same to the prisoner when discharged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1949, Ch. 890.)
