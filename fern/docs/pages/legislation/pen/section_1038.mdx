# PEN § 1038

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 6. Change of Venue

**Citation:** PEN § 1038

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall adopt rules of practice and procedure for the change of venue in criminal actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 449, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 2004.)
