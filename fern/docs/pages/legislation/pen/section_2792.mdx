# PEN § 2792

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 5. Employment in Public Parks, Forests, etc.

**Citation:** PEN § 2792

## Text

Camps may be established under this article for the employment of paroled prisoners.
