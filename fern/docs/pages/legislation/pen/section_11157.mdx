# PEN § 11157

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 1.5. Reports of Disposition of Inmates

**Citation:** PEN § 11157

---

## Text

The victims may be notified of the opportunity to receive the notices provided by this article by means of adding a paragraph to the information contained on subpoena forms which are used in subpoenaing victims as material witnesses to any court proceedings resulting from the perpetration of the crime in which the victim was involved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1048, Sec. 1.)
