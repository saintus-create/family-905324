# PEN § 4492

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.7. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE BOND ACT OF 1986 > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 4492

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 16305.7 of the Government Code, all interest or other increment resulting from the investment of moneys deposited in the fund shall be credited to the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 52 at the June 3, 1986, election.)
