# PEN § 503

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 6. Embezzlement

**Citation:** PEN § 503

---

## Text

Embezzlement is the fraudulent appropriation of property by a person to whom it has been intrusted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
