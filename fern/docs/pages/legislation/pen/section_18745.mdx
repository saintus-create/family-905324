# PEN § 18745

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Destructive Devices and Explosives Generally > Article: 1. Prohibited Acts

**Citation:** PEN § 18745

---

## Text

Every person who explodes, ignites, or attempts to explode or ignite any destructive device or any explosive with intent to commit murder is guilty of a felony, and shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for life with the possibility of parole.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
