# PEN § 14114.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. COMMUNITY VIOLENCE PREVENTION AND CONFLICT RESOLUTION

**Citation:** PEN § 14114.5

---

## Text

Other programs shall include subdivisions (a) and (f) of Section 14114 and may include public lectures or sponsoring of conferences, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1709, Sec. 1.)
