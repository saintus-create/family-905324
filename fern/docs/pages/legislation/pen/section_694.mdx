# PEN § 694

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 1. Of Lawful Resistance

**Citation:** PEN § 694

---

## Text

Any other person, in aid or defense of the person about to be injured, may make resistance sufficient to prevent the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
