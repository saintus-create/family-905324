# PEN § 3516

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.1. BIOMEDICAL AND BEHAVIORIAL RESEARCH > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PEN § 3516

---

## Text

No behavioral research shall be conducted on any prisoner in this state in the absence of a determination by the department consistent with this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1553, Sec. 16.)
