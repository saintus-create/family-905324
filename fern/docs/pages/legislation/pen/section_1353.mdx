# PEN § 1353

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 5. Examination of Witnesses on Commission

**Citation:** PEN § 1353

---

## Text

The application may be made to the Court, or a Judge thereof, and must be upon three days’ notice to the District Attorney.
