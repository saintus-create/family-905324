# PEN § 3202

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. IMPRISONMENT OF FEMALE PRISONERS IN STATE INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of Institution for Women

**Citation:** PEN § 3202

---

## Text

As used in the sections of this Part 3 of the Penal Code providing for penal offenses and punishments therefor, the term “State prison” or “prison” shall refer to and include the California Institution for Women.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
