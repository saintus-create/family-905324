# PEN § 708

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 3. Security to Keep the Peace

**Citation:** PEN § 708

---

## Text

If the person complained of is committed for not giving the undertaking required, he may be discharged by any magistrate, upon giving the same.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
