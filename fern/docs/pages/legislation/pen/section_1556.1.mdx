# PEN § 1556.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 4. Proceedings Against Fugitives From Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 1556.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purposes to make uniform the law of those states which enact legislation based upon the Uniform Criminal Extradition Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 554.)
