# PEN § 4222

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 2. Joint County Road Camp Act

**Citation:** PEN § 4222

---

## Text

The board of directors may make such rules as it deems proper for the government of camps and the conduct of prisoners therein and may fix a reasonable compensation, not to exceed seventy-five cents ($0.75) per day, for each prisoner performing labor in a camp.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
