# PEN § 14174

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11.5. CENTRAL VALLEY RURAL CRIME PREVENTION PROGRAM

**Citation:** PEN § 14174

---

## Text

Funds appropriated for the purposes of this title shall be allocated based on the counties’ compliance with paragraph (3) of subdivision (b) of Section 14171.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 14174.3 by Stats. 2005, Ch. 497, Sec. 6. Effective October 4, 2005.)
