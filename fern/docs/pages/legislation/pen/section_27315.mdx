# PEN § 27315

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Gun Show or Event > Article: 2. Gun Show Enforcement and Security Act of 2000

**Citation:** PEN § 27315

---

## Text

Sales of ammunition at a gun show or event shall comply with all applicable laws, including Sections 30347, 30348, 30350, 30352, and 30360.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 736, Sec. 9. (AB 1669) Effective January 1, 2020.)
