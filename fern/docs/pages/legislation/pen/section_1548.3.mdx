# PEN § 1548.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 4. Proceedings Against Fugitives From Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 1548.3

---

## Text

When a demand is made upon the Governor of this State by the executive authority of another State for the surrender of a person so charged with crime, the Governor may call upon the Attorney General or any district attorney in this State to investigate or assist in investigating the demand, and to report to him the situation and circumstances of the person so demanded, and whether he ought to be surrendered according to the provision of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 554.)
