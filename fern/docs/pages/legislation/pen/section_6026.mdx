# PEN § 6026

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 5. The Corrections Standards Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 6026

---

## Text

The Corrections Standards Authority shall be the means whereby the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation may correlate its individual programs for adults and youths under its jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 10, Sec. 58. Effective May 10, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 99 of Ch. 10.)
