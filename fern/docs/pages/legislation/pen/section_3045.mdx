# PEN § 3045

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3. Paroles

**Citation:** PEN § 3045

---

## Text

Any sentence based on conviction of crime of which the person was previously pardoned on the express ground that he was not guilty shall not be counted as a previous conviction.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 671.)
