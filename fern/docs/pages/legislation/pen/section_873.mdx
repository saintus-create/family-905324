# PEN § 873

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 873

---

## Text

If the offense is not bailable, the following words must be added to the indorsement: “And he is hereby committed to the Sheriff of the County of ____. ”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
