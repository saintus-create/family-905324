# PEN § 20490

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. WEAPONS AND DEVICES OTHER THAN FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Disguised or Misleading Appearance > Article: 2. Belt Buckle Knife

**Citation:** PEN § 20490

---

## Text

Except as provided in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 17700) of Division 2 of Title 2, any belt buckle knife is a nuisance and is subject to Section 18010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
