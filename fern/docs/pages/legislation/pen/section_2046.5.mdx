# PEN § 2046.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 6. California Men’s Colony

**Citation:** PEN § 2046.5

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall construct and equip in accordance with law, suitable buildings, structures, and facilities for the said prison.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 892.)
