# PEN § 6356

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10.5. Prison Visitor Services

**Citation:** PEN § 6356

---

## Text

The department shall cooperate with the Department of Transportation in the development of public transportation services to prisons, pursuant to Section 14035.9 of the Government Code and Section 99317.9 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 603, Sec. 2.)
