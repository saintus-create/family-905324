# PEN § 1564

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions Respecting Special Proceedings of a Criminal Nature

**Citation:** PEN § 1564

---

## Text

The Courts and magistrates before whom such proceedings are prosecuted may issue subpoenas for witnesses, and punish their disobedience in the same manner as in a criminal action.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
