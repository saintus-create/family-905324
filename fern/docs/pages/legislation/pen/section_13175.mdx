# PEN § 13175

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 4. Information Service

**Citation:** PEN § 13175

---

## Text

When a criminal justice agency supplies fingerprints, or a fingerprint identification number, or such other personal identifiers as the Department of Justice deems appropriate, to the Department of Justice, such agency shall, upon request, be provided with identification, arrest, and, where applicable, final disposition data relating to such person within 72 hours of receipt by the Department of Justice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 992.)
