# PEN § 13823.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. CALIFORNIA COUNCIL ON CRIMINAL JUSTICE > Chapter: 3. Criminal Justice Planning

**Citation:** PEN § 13823.3

---

## Text

The Office of Emergency Services may expend funds for local domestic violence programs, subject to the availability of funds therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 431. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
