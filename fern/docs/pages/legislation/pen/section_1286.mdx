# PEN § 1286

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 3. Bail Upon an Indictment Before Conviction

**Citation:** PEN § 1286

---

## Text

When the defendant is so delivered into custody he must be held by the Sheriff, unless admitted to bail on examination upon a writ of habeas corpus.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
