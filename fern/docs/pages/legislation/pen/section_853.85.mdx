# PEN § 853.85

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 5C. Citations for Misdemeanors

**Citation:** PEN § 853.85

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply in any case where a person is arrested for an offense declared to be a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1009, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
