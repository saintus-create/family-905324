# PEN § 2078

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of State Prisons > Article: 2. Wardens

**Citation:** PEN § 2078

---

## Text

It shall be the duty of the department to prosecute all suits, at law or in equity, that may be necessary to protect the rights of the State in matters of property connected with the prisons and their management, such suits to be prosecuted in the name of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1944, 3rd [1st] Ex. Sess., Ch. 2.)
