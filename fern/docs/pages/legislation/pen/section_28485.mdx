# PEN § 28485

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Recordkeeping, Background Checks, and Fees Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 6. Centralized List of Exempted Federal Firearms Licensees

**Citation:** PEN § 28485

---

## Text

The department may remove from the centralized list described in Section 28450 any person who violates a provision listed in Section 16575.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
