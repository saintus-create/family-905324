# PEN § 11156

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 1.5. Reports of Disposition of Inmates

**Citation:** PEN § 11156

---

## Text

The notice sent to the chief of police and county sheriff pursuant to Section 11155 shall include an actual glossy photograph no smaller than 318 x 318 inches in size, in conjunction with the Department of Justice, fingerprints of each inmate in the reentry or work furlough program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 600, Sec. 5.)
