# PEN § 13050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 1.5. Reports to the Bureau of Livestock Identification

**Citation:** PEN § 13050

---

## Text

Each sheriff or other officer to whom a complaint that relates to the loss or theft of any equine animal is made shall, in a timely manner, transmit to the Bureau of Livestock Identification a report pursuant to Section 24104 of the Food and Agricultural Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 431, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
