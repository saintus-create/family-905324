# PEN § 970

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. THE PLEADINGS > Chapter: 2. Rules of Pleading

**Citation:** PEN § 970

---

## Text

When several defendants are named in one accusatory pleading, any one or more may be convicted or acquitted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1674.)
