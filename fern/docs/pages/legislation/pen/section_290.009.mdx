# PEN § 290.009

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 5.5. Sex Offenders

**Citation:** PEN § 290.009

---

## Text

Any person required to register under the Act who is enrolled as a student or is an employee or carries on a vocation, with or without compensation, at an institution of higher learning in this state, shall register pursuant to the provisions of the Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 579, Sec. 17. Effective October 13, 2007.)
