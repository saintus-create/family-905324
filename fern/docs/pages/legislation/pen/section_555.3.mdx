# PEN § 555.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 12. Unlawful Interference With Property > Article: 1. Trespassing or Loitering Near Posted Industrial Property

**Citation:** PEN § 555.3

---

## Text

Violation of any of the provisions of this article is a misdemeanor.
