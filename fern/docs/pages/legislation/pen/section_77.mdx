# PEN § 77

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5. OF CRIMES BY AND AGAINST THE EXECUTIVE POWER OF THE STATE

**Citation:** PEN § 77

---

## Text

The various provisions of this title, except Section 76, apply to administrative and ministerial officers, in the same manner as if they were mentioned therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1405, Sec. 2.)
