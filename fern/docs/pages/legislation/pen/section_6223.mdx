# PEN § 6223

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9.2. Restitution Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6223

---

## Text

Restitution centers shall be located in areas which will maximize the employment opportunities of persons sentenced to the centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1520, Sec. 1.)
