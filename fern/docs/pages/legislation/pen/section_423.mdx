# PEN § 423

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11.7. CALIFORNIA FREEDOM OF ACCESS TO CLINIC AND CHURCH ENTRANCES ACT

**Citation:** PEN § 423

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the California Freedom of Access to Clinic and Church Entrances Act, or the California FACE Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 899, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
