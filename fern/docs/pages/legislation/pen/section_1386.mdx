# PEN § 1386

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 8. Dismissal of the Action for Want of Prosecution or Otherwise

**Citation:** PEN § 1386

---

## Text

The entry of a nolle prosequi is abolished, and neither the Attorney General nor the district attorney can discontinue or abandon a prosecution for a public offense, except as provided in Section 1385.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 828, Sec. 93.)
