# PEN § 629.53

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. MISCELLANEOUS CRIMES > Chapter: 1.4. Interception of Wire, Electronic Digital Pager, or Electronic Cellular Telephone Communications

**Citation:** PEN § 629.53

---

## Text

The Judicial Council may establish guidelines for judges to follow in granting an order authorizing the interception of any wire or electronic communications.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 707, Sec. 4. (SB 1428) Effective January 1, 2011. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 629.98.)
