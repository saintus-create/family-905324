# PEN § 2046.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 6. California Men’s Colony

**Citation:** PEN § 2046.6

---

## Text

The provisions of this part shall apply to the prison and to the persons confined therein insofar as those provisions may be applicable.
