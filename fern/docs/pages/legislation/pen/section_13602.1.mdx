# PEN § 13602.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.5. COMMISSION ON CORRECTIONAL PEACE OFFICER STANDARDS AND TRAINING

**Citation:** PEN § 13602.1

---

## Text

The Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation may establish a training academy for correctional peace officers in southern California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 26, Sec. 39. (SB 85) Effective June 24, 2015.)
