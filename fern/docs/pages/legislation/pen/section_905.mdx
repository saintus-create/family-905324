# PEN § 905

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2. Formation of Grand Jury > Article: 4. Impaneling of Grand Jury

**Citation:** PEN § 905

---

## Text

In all counties there shall be at least one grand jury drawn and impaneled in each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1408, Sec. 3.)
