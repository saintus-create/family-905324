# PEN § 11301

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 3. Prevention and Abatement of Unlawful Activities > Article: 3. Control of Gambling Ships

**Citation:** PEN § 11301

---

## Text

As used in this article “craft” includes every boat, ship, vessel, craft, barge, hulk, float or other thing capable of floating.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 35.)
