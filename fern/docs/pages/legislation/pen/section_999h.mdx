# PEN § 999h

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.2. Career Criminals

**Citation:** PEN § 999h

---

## Text

The characterization of a defendant as a “career criminal” as defined by this chapter may not be communicated to the trier of fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 42, Sec. 1. Effective February 17, 1982.)
