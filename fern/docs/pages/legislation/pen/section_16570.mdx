# PEN § 16570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 16570

---

## Text

As used in this part, “flechette dart” means a dart, capable of being fired from a firearm, that measures approximately one inch in length, with tail fins that take up approximately five-sixteenths of an inch of the body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
