# PEN § 11104

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 3. Criminal Identification and Statistics

**Citation:** PEN § 11104

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall file all measurements, information and descriptions received and shall make a complete and systematic record and index, providing a method of convenience, consultation, and comparison.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 196, Sec. 8.)
