# PEN § 653.26

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. MISCELLANEOUS CRIMES > Chapter: 2.5. Loitering for the Purpose of Engaging in a Prostitution Offense

## Text

A violation of any provision of this chapter is a misdemeanor.
