# PEN § 11116.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 4. Criminal Records

**Citation:** PEN § 11116.8

---

## Text

The certificate of disposition provided by Section 11116.7 shall describe the charge or charges set forth in the original and any amended accusatory pleading, together with the disposition of each charge in the original and any amended accusatory pleading.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 152.)
