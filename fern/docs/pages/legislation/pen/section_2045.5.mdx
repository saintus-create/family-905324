# PEN § 2045.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 5. Correctional Training Facility

**Citation:** PEN § 2045.5

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall construct and equip in accordance with law, suitable buildings, structures and facilities for said institution.
