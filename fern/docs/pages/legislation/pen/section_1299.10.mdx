# PEN § 1299.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 5.5. Bail Fugitive Recovery Persons Act

**Citation:** PEN § 1299.10

---

## Text

An individual authorized by Section 1299.02 to apprehend a bail fugitive shall not carry a firearm or other weapon unless in compliance with the laws of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 747, Sec. 1. (AB 2029) Effective January 1, 2013.)
