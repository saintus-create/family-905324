# PEN § 31790

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Handguns and Firearm Safety > Article: 3. Exceptions to Firearm Safety Certificate Requirement

**Citation:** PEN § 31790

---

## Text

Subdivision (a) of Section 31615 does not apply to the sale, delivery, or transfer of firearms if all of the following conditions are satisfied:

(a) The firearms are unloaded.

(b) The sale, delivery, or transfer is made by one dealer to another dealer, upon proof of compliance with the requirements of Section 27555.

(c) The firearms are intended as merchandise in the receiving dealer’s business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
