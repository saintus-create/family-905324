# PEN § 1001.34

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.8. Diversion of Defendants With Cognitive Developmental Disabilities

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.34

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the diversion-related individual program plan shall be fully implemented by the regional centers upon court order and approval of the diversion-related treatment and habilitation plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1253, Sec. 1.)
