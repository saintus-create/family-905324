# PEN § 242

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON > Chapter: 9. Assault and Battery

**Citation:** PEN § 242

---

## Text

A battery is any willful and unlawful use of force or violence upon the person of another.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
