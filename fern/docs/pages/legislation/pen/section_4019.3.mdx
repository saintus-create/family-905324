# PEN § 4019.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1. County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4019.3

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may credit each prisoner with a sum of money to be determined by the board if the prisoner is confined in or committed to a county jail and performs a work assignment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 252, Sec. 1. (AB 248) Effective January 1, 2026.)
