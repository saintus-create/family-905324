# PEN § 30425

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1.5. Firearm Precursor Parts > Article: 1. Restrictions Relating to Firearm Precursor Parts

**Citation:** PEN § 30425

---

## Text

This article does not prohibit the manufacture of firearm precursor parts under contracts approved by agencies of the state or federal government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 730, Sec. 6. (AB 879) Effective January 1, 2020.)
