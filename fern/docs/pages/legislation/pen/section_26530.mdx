# PEN § 26530

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. License Requirement for Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 1. License Requirement and Miscellaneous Exceptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26530

---

## Text

Section 26500 does not apply to sales, deliveries, or transfers of firearms between or to importers and manufacturers of firearms licensed to engage in that business pursuant to Chapter 44 (commencing with Section 921) of Title 18 of the United States Code and the regulations issued pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
