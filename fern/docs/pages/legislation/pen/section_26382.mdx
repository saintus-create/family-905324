# PEN § 26382

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Openly Carrying an Unloaded Handgun > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26382

---

## Text

Section 26350 does not apply to, or affect, the open carrying of an unloaded handgun incident to and at the request of a sheriff or chief or other head of a municipal police department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 725, Sec. 14. (AB 144) Effective January 1, 2012.)
