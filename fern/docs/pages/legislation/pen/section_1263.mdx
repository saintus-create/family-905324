# PEN § 1263

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 4. Judgment Upon Appeal

**Citation:** PEN § 1263

---

## Text

If a judgment against the defendant is affirmed, the original judgment must be enforced.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
