# PEN § 1562

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions Respecting Special Proceedings of a Criminal Nature

**Citation:** PEN § 1562

---

## Text

The party prosecuting a special proceeding of a criminal nature is designated in this Code as the complainant, and the adverse party as the defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
