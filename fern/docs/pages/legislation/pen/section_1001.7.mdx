# PEN § 1001.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.7. Misdemeanor Diversion

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.7

---

## Text

If the divertee has performed satisfactorily during the period of diversion, the criminal charges shall be dismissed at the end of the period of diversion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 42, Sec. 2. Effective February 17, 1982.)
