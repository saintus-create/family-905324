# PEN § 610

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. MALICIOUS MISCHIEF

**Citation:** PEN § 610

---

## Text

Every person who unlawfully masks, alters, or removes any light or signal, or willfully exhibits any light or signal, with intent to bring any vessel into danger, is punishable by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 416. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.)
