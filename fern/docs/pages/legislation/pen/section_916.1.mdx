# PEN § 916.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Grand Jury > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 916.1

---

## Text

If the foreman of a grand jury is absent from any meeting or if he is disqualified to act, the grand jury may select a member of that body to act as foreman pro tempore, who shall perform the duties, and have all the powers, of the regularly appointed foreman in his absence or disqualification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 501.)
