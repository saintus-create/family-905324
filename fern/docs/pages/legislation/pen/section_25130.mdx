# PEN § 25130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Storage of Firearm

**Citation:** PEN § 25130

---

## Text

Every person licensed under Sections 26700 to 26915, inclusive, shall post within the licensed premises the notice required by Section 26835, disclosing the duty imposed by this chapter upon any person who keeps a loaded firearm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
