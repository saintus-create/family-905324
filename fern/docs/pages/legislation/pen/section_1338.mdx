# PEN § 1338

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Examination of Witnesses Conditionally

**Citation:** PEN § 1338

---

## Text

The application may be made to the court or a judge thereof, and must be made upon three days’ notice to the opposite party.
