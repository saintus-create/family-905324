# PEN § 558.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 12. Unlawful Interference With Property > Article: 3. Trespass on Property Belonging to the University of California

**Citation:** PEN § 558.1

---

## Text

Every person who violates any of the provisions of Section 558 is guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof shall be punished by a fine of not more than six hundred dollars ($600) or by imprisonment for not more than 30 days, or by both such fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 304. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
