# PEN § 2056

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of State Prisons > Article: 1. Miscellaneous Powers and Duties of Department and Director of Corrections

**Citation:** PEN § 2056

---

## Text

If any of the shops or buildings in which convicts are employed require rebuilding or repair for any reason, they may be rebuilt or repaired immediately, under the direction of the California Correctional Training and Rehabilitation Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 241, Sec. 31. (SB 857) Effective January 1, 2026.)
