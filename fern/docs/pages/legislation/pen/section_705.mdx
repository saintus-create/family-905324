# PEN § 705

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 3. Security to Keep the Peace

**Citation:** PEN § 705

---

## Text

If it appears that there is no just reason to fear the commission of the offense alleged to have been threatened, the person complained of must be discharged.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
