# PEN § 273.87

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 2.5. Spousal Abusers

**Citation:** PEN § 273.87

---

## Text

The Department of Justice is encouraged to utilize Federal Victims of Crimes Act (VOCA) funds or any other federal funds that may become available in order to implement this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 599, Sec. 8. Effective September 16, 1994.)
