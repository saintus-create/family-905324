# PEN § 4066

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1.5. Joint County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4066

---

## Text

The board of directors may make rules and regulations for the government of a joint county jail not inconsistent with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1019.)
