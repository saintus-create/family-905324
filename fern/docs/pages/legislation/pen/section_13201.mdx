# PEN § 13201

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 5. Access to Information

**Citation:** PEN § 13201

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize access of any person or public agency to individual criminal offender record information unless such access is otherwise authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 992.)
