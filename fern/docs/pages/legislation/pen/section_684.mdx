# PEN § 684

**Hierarchy:** Part: 2. OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Title: 17. RIGHTS OF VICTIMS AND WITNESSES OF CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 684

---

## Text

A criminal action is prosecuted in the name of the people of the State of California, as a party, against the person charged with the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
