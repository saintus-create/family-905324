# PEN § 31100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Assault Weapons and .50 BMG Rifles > Article: 8. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 31100

---

## Text

Any individual may arrange in advance to relinquish an assault weapon or a .50 BMG rifle to a police or sheriff’s department. The assault weapon or .50 BMG rifle shall be transported in accordance with Sections 16850 and 25610.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
