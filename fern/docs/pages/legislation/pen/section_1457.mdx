# PEN § 1457

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. PROCEEDINGS IN MISDEMEANOR AND INFRACTION CASES AND APPEALS FROM SUCH CASES > Chapter: 1. Proceedings in Misdemeanor and Infraction Cases

**Citation:** PEN § 1457

---

## Text

Upon payment of the fine, the officer must discharge the defendant, if he is not detained for any other legal cause, and pay over the fine to the court which rendered the judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1517. Note: The condition in Sec. 5 of Ch. 1517 was satisfied by adoption of Prop. 3 on Nov. 7, 1950.)
