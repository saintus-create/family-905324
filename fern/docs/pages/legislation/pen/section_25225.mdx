# PEN § 25225

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Storage of Firearm Where Child Obtains Access and Carries Firearm Off-Premises

**Citation:** PEN § 25225

---

## Text

Every person licensed under Sections 26700 to 26915, inclusive, shall post within the licensed premises the notice required by Section 26835, disclosing the duty imposed by this chapter upon any person who keeps any firearm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
