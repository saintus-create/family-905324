# PEN § 1256

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 3. Argument of the Appeal

**Citation:** PEN § 1256

---

## Text

It shall be the duty of the district attorney to cooperate with and assist the attorney general in presenting all criminal matters on appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1927, Ch. 620.)
