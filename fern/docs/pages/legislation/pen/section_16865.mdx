# PEN § 16865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 16865

---

## Text

As used in Section 26860, “long gun” means any firearm that is not a handgun or a machinegun.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 761, Sec. 2. (SB 683) Effective January 1, 2014.)
