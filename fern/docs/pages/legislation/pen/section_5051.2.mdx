# PEN § 5051.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 2. The Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation

**Citation:** PEN § 5051.2

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall have wide and successful administrative experience in adult or youth correctional programs embodying rehabilitative concepts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1458.)
