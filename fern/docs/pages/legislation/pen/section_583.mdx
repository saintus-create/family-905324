# PEN § 583

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 14. Fraudulent Issue of Documents of Title to Merchandise

**Citation:** PEN § 583

---

## Text

Section 581 does not apply where property is demanded or sold by virtue of process of law.
