# PEN § 2901

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 7. Execution of Sentences of Imprisonment > Article: 1. Commencement of Term

**Citation:** PEN § 2901

---

## Text

It is hereby made the duty of the wardens of the State prisons to receive persons sentenced to imprisonment in a State prison, and such persons shall be imprisoned until duly released according to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
