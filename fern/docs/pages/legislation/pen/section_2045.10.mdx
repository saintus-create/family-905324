# PEN § 2045.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 5.5. California Correctional Institution in Monterey County

**Citation:** PEN § 2045.10

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections is authorized to construct and establish a state prison for the confinement of males under the custody of the Director of Corrections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 695, Sec. 11. Effective September 15, 1992.)
