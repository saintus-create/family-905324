# PEN § 170

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 7. Other Offenses Against Public Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 170

---

## Text

Every person who maliciously and without probable cause procures a search warrant or warrant of arrest to be issued and executed, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
