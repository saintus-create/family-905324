# PEN § 2695.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 4. Treatment of Prisoners > Article: 6. Veterans In State Prisons

**Citation:** PEN § 2695.3

---

## Text

The advocate shall coordinate with the United States Department of Veterans Affairs in order to provide each inmate who is a veteran with access to earned veterans’ benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 652, Sec. 4. (AB 2263) Effective January 1, 2015.)
