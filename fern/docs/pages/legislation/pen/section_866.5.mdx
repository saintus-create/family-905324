# PEN § 866.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 866.5

---

## Text

The defendant may not be examined at the examination, unless he is represented by counsel, or unless he waives his right to counsel after being advised at such examination of his right to aid of counsel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1482.)
