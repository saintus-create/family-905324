# PEN § 5088

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 3.5. The Robert Presley Center of Crime and Justice Studies

**Citation:** PEN § 5088

---

## Text

The university shall negotiate and approve terms, services, and costs of contracts and research projects for purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 778, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
