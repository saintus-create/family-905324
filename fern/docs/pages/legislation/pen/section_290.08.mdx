# PEN § 290.08

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 5.5. Sex Offenders

**Citation:** PEN § 290.08

---

## Text

Every district attorney’s office and the Department of Justice shall retain records relating to a person convicted of an offense for which registration is required pursuant to Section 290 for a period of 75 years after disposition of the case.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 337, Sec. 17. Effective September 20, 2006.)
