# PEN § 26387

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Openly Carrying an Unloaded Handgun > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26387

---

## Text

Section 26350 does not apply to, or affect, the open carrying of an unloaded handgun pursuant to subparagraph (F) of paragraph (1) subdivision (c) of Section 171.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 725, Sec. 14. (AB 144) Effective January 1, 2012.)
