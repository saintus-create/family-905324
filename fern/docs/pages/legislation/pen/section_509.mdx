# PEN § 509

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 6. Embezzlement

**Citation:** PEN § 509

---

## Text

A distinct act of taking is not necessary to constitute embezzlement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
