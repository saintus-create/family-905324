# PEN § 408

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PUBLIC PEACE

**Citation:** PEN § 408

---

## Text

Every person who participates in any rout or unlawful assembly is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
