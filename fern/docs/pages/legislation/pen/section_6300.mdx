# PEN § 6300

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10. Regional Jail Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 6300

---

## Text

The Department of Corrections is authorized to establish and operate regional jail camps.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1646.)
