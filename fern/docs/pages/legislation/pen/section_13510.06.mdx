# PEN § 13510.06

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 2. Field Services, Standards, and Certification

**Citation:** PEN § 13510.06

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, information that identifies the testing results of a particular student of a regular basic course of peace officer training is confidential and shall not be released to the public unless otherwise subject to disclosure under Section 832.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 172, Sec. 2. (AB 332) Effective January 1, 2020.)
