# PEN § 19190

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Explosive Substance Other Than Fixed Ammunition

**Citation:** PEN § 19190

---

## Text

The unlawful concealed carrying upon the person of any explosive substance other than fixed ammunition, as provided in Section 19100, is a nuisance and is subject to Sections 18000 and 18005.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
