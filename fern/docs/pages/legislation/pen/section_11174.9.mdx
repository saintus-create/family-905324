# PEN § 11174.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 2.7. Elder and Dependent Adult Death Review Teams

**Citation:** PEN § 11174.9

---

## Text

Information gathered by the elder death review team and any recommendations made by the team shall be used by the county to develop education, prevention, and if necessary, prosecution strategies that will lead to improved coordination of services for families and the elder population.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 301, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
