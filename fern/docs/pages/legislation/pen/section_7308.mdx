# PEN § 7308

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 14. New Prison Construction Bond Act of 1986

**Citation:** PEN § 7308

---

## Text

There is hereby appropriated from the General Fund in the State Treasury for the purpose of this chapter such an amount as will equal the following:

(a) That sum annually as will be necessary to pay the principal of and the interest on the bonds issued and sold pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

(b) That sum as is necessary to carry out the provisions of Section 7309, which sum is appropriated without regard to fiscal years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 409, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 54 at the November 4, 1986, election.)
