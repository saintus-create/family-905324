# PEN § 25000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 25000

---

## Text

As used in this division, “child” means a person under 18 years of age.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
