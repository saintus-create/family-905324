# PEN § 698

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 2. Of the Intervention of the Officers of Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 698

---

## Text

When the officers of justice are authorized to act in the prevention of public offenses, other persons, who, by their command, act in their aid, are justified in so doing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
