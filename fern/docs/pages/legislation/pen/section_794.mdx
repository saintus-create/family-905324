# PEN § 794

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Local Jurisdiction of Public Offenses

**Citation:** PEN § 794

---

## Text

Where an offense is within the jurisdiction of two or more courts, a conviction or acquittal thereof in one court is a bar to a prosecution therefor in another.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1674.)
