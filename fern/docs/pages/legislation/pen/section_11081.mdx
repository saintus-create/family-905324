# PEN § 11081

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 2.5. Criminal Record Dissemination

**Citation:** PEN § 11081

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to authorize access of any person or public agency to individual criminal offender record information unless such access is otherwise authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1437.)
