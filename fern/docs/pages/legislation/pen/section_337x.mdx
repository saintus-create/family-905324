# PEN § 337x

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 10. Gaming

**Citation:** PEN § 337x

---

## Text

It is unlawful to cheat at any gambling game in a gambling establishment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 624, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
