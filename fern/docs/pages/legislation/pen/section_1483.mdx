# PEN § 1483

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Writ of Habeas Corpus

**Citation:** PEN § 1483

---

## Text

The Court or Judge before whom the writ is returned must, immediately after the return, proceed to hear and examine the return, and such other matters as may be properly submitted to their hearing and consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
