# PEN § 14113

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. COMMUNITY VIOLENCE PREVENTION AND CONFLICT RESOLUTION

**Citation:** PEN § 14113

---

## Text

Unless otherwise required by context, as used in this title:

(a) “Agency” or “office” means the Office of Emergency Services.

(b) “Secretary” or “director” means the Director of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 466. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
