# PEN § 320

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 9. Lotteries

**Citation:** PEN § 320

---

## Text

Every person who contrives, prepares, sets up, proposes, or draws any lottery, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
