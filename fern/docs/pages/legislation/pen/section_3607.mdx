# PEN § 3607

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. EXECUTION OF DEATH PENALTY > Chapter: 1. Executing Death Penalty

**Citation:** PEN § 3607

---

## Text

After the execution, the warden must make a return upon the death warrant to the clerk of the court by which the judgment was rendered, showing the time, mode, and manner in which it was executed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 561. Effective January 1, 2003.)
