# PEN § 1288

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 3. Bail Upon an Indictment Before Conviction

**Citation:** PEN § 1288

---

## Text

The provisions contained in sections 1279, 1280, 1280a and 1281, in relation to bail before indictment, apply to bail after indictment.
