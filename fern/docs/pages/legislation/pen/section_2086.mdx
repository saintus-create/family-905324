# PEN § 2086

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of State Prisons > Article: 2. Wardens

**Citation:** PEN § 2086

---

## Text

The wardens may make temporary rules and regulations, in case of emergency, to remain in force until the department otherwise provides.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1944, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 2.)
