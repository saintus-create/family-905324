# PEN § 3201

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. IMPRISONMENT OF FEMALE PRISONERS IN STATE INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of Institution for Women

**Citation:** PEN § 3201

---

## Text

The purpose of said institution shall be to provide custody, care, protection, industrial, vocational, and other training, and reformatory help, for women confined therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
