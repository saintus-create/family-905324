# PEN § 6028.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 5. The Corrections Standards Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 6028.4

---

## Text

The Governor shall report to each regular session of the Legislature the names of any persons appointed under Section 6028 together with a statement of expenses incurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1181.)
