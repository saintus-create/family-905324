# PEN § 2021

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 2. San Quentin Rehabilitation Center

**Citation:** PEN § 2021

---

## Text

The San Quentin Rehabilitation Center shall be located at San Quentin in the County of Marin.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 47, Sec. 16. (AB 134) Effective July 10, 2023.)
