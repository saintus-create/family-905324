# PEN § 13778.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 5.7. REPRODUCTIVE RIGHTS LAW ENFORCEMENT ACT

**Citation:** PEN § 13778.1

---

## Text

Every law enforcement agency in this state shall develop, adopt, and implement written policies and standards for officers’ responses to anti-reproductive-rights calls by January 1, 2023.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 191, Sec. 12. (AB 1356) Effective January 1, 2022.)
