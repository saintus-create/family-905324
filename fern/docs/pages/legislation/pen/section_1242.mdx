# PEN § 1242

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 1. Appeals, When Allowed and How Taken, and the Effect Thereof

**Citation:** PEN § 1242

---

## Text

An appeal taken by the people in no case stays or affects the operation of a judgment in favor of the defendant, until judgment is reversed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
