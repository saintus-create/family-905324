# PEN § 5052

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 2. The Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation

**Citation:** PEN § 5052

---

## Text

Any officer or employee of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation designated in writing by the secretary, shall have the power of a head of a department pursuant to Article 2 (commencing at Section 11180) of Chapter 2, Part 1, Division 3, Title 2, of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 10, Sec. 39. Effective May 10, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 99 of Ch. 10.)
