# PEN § 6351

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10.5. Prison Visitor Services

**Citation:** PEN § 6351

---

## Text

The Department of Corrections shall contract with a private nonprofit agency or agencies to establish and operate a visitor center outside each state adult prison in California which has a population of more than 300 inmates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 17, Sec. 1.)
