# PEN § 999w

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.4. Child Abusers

**Citation:** PEN § 999w

---

## Text

The characterization of a defendant as a “child abuser” as defined by this chapter shall not be communicated to the trier of fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1097, Sec. 1.)
