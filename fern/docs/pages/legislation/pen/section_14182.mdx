# PEN § 14182

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11.7. CENTRAL COAST RURAL CRIME PREVENTION PROGRAM

**Citation:** PEN § 14182

---

## Text

Sources of funding for the program may include, but shall not be limited to, appropriations from local government and private contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 18, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
