# PEN § 6251

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9.5. Community Correctional Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6251

---

## Text

The primary purpose of such facilities is to provide housing, supervision, counseling, and other correctional programs for persons committed to the Department of Corrections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1931.)
