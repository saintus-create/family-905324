# PEN § 687

**Hierarchy:** Part: 2. OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Title: 17. RIGHTS OF VICTIMS AND WITNESSES OF CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 687

---

## Text

No person can be subjected to a second prosecution for a public offense for which he has once been prosecuted and convicted or acquitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
