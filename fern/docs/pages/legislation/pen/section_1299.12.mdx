# PEN § 1299.12

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 5.5. Bail Fugitive Recovery Persons Act

**Citation:** PEN § 1299.12

---

## Text

Nothing in this article is intended to exempt from licensure persons otherwise required to be licensed as private investigators pursuant to Chapter 11.3 (commencing with Section 7512) of Division 3 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 747, Sec. 1. (AB 2029) Effective January 1, 2013.)
