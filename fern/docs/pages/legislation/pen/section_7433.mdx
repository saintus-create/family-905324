# PEN § 7433

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 16. New Prison Construction Bond Act of 1990

**Citation:** PEN § 7433

---

## Text

Money in the fund may only be expended pursuant to appropriations by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 158.5. Effective October 12, 2001. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 5, and approved in Prop. 120 on June 5, 1990.)
