# PEN § 11116.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 4. Criminal Records

**Citation:** PEN § 11116.9

---

## Text

The clerk of the court in which the disposition is made shall provide the defendant or his counsel of record with additional certified copies of the disposition certificate upon the payment of the fees provided by law for certified copies of court records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1279.)
