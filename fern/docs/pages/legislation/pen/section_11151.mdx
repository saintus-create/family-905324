# PEN § 11151

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 1. Release of Persons Convicted of Arson

**Citation:** PEN § 11151

---

## Text

Within five days after release of a person convicted of arson from an institution under the jurisdiction of the State Department of State Hospitals, the Director of State Hospitals shall send the notice provided in Section 11150.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 144, Sec. 48. (AB 1847) Effective January 1, 2015.)
