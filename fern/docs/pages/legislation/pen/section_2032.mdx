# PEN § 2032

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 3. California State Prison at Folsom

**Citation:** PEN § 2032

---

## Text

The primary purpose of the California State Prison at Folsom shall be to provide confinement, industrial and other training, treatment, and care to persons confined therein.
