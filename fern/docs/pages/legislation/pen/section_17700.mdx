# PEN § 17700

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 17700

---

## Text

The provisions listed in Section 16590 do not apply to any antique firearm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
