# PEN § 2786.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 5. Employment in Public Parks, Forests, etc.

**Citation:** PEN § 2786.1

---

## Text

The secretary shall make weight training equipment available to inmates assigned to fire suppression efforts pursuant to this article. The weight training equipment shall be used in accordance with the provisions of Section 5010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 737, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
