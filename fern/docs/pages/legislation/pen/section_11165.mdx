# PEN § 11165

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 2.5. Child Abuse and Neglect Reporting Act

**Citation:** PEN § 11165

---

## Text

As used in this article “child” means a person under the age of 18 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1459, Sec. 2.)
