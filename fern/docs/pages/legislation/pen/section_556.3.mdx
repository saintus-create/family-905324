# PEN § 556.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 12. Unlawful Interference With Property > Article: 2. Unlawfully Placing Signs on Public and Private Property

**Citation:** PEN § 556.3

---

## Text

Any sign, picture, transparency, advertisement, or mechanical device placed on any property contrary to the provisions of Sections 556 and 556.1, is a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 32.)
