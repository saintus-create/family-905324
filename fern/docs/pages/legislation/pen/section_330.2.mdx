# PEN § 330.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 10. Gaming

**Citation:** PEN § 330.2

---

## Text

As used in Sections 330.1 to 330.5, inclusive, of this code a “thing of value” is defined to be any money, coin, currency, check, chip, allowance, token, credit, merchandise, property, or any representative of value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 18.)
