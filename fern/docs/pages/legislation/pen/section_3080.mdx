# PEN § 3080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3.5. County Boards of Parole Commissioners

**Citation:** PEN § 3080

---

## Text

If any paroled prisoner leaves the county in which he is imprisoned without permission from the board granting his parole, he shall be arrested as an escaped prisoner and held as such.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1384.)
