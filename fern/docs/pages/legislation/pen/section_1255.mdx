# PEN § 1255

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 3. Argument of the Appeal

**Citation:** PEN § 1255

---

## Text

The defendant need not personally appear in the appellate Court.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
