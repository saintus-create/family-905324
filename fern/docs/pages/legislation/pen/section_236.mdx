# PEN § 236

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON > Chapter: 8. False Imprisonment and Human Trafficking

**Citation:** PEN § 236

---

## Text

False imprisonment is the unlawful violation of the personal liberty of another.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
