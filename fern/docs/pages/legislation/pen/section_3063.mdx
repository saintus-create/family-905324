# PEN § 3063

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3. Paroles

**Citation:** PEN § 3063

---

## Text

No parole shall be suspended or revoked without cause, which cause must be stated in the order suspending or revoking the parole.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
