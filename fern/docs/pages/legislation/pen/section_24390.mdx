# PEN § 24390

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Camouflaging Firearm Container

**Citation:** PEN § 24390

---

## Text

Except as provided in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 17700) of Division 2 of Title 2, any camouflaging firearm container is a nuisance and is subject to Section 18010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
