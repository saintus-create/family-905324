# PEN § 219.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON > Chapter: 5. Attempts to Kill

**Citation:** PEN § 219.3

---

## Text

Any person who wilfully drops or throws any object or missile from any toll bridge is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1053.)
