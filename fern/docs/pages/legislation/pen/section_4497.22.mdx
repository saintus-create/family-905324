# PEN § 4497.22

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.85. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITIES CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND YOUTH FACILITY BOND ACT OF 1988 ALLOCATIONS > Chapter: 3. Juvenile Facilities

**Citation:** PEN § 4497.22

## Text

Funds appropriated to the Department of the Youth Authority for allocation under this chapter shall be allocated as provided by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1327, Sec. 5. Effective October 2, 1989.)
