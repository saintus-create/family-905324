# PEN § 18750

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Destructive Devices and Explosives Generally > Article: 1. Prohibited Acts

**Citation:** PEN § 18750

---

## Text

Every person who willfully and maliciously explodes or ignites any destructive device or any explosive that causes bodily injury to any person is guilty of a felony, and shall be punished by imprisonment in the state prison for a period of five, seven, or nine years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
