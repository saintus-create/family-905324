# PEN § 8050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. PUNISHMENT OPTIONS > Chapter: 2. Community-Based Punishment Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 8050

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Community-Based Punishment Act of 1994.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 41, Sec. 4. Effective November 30, 1994.)
