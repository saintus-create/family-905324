# PEN § 807

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 3. Complaints Before Magistrates

**Citation:** PEN § 807

---

## Text

A magistrate is an officer having power to issue a warrant for the arrest of a person charged with a public offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
