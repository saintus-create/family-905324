# PEN § 922

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Grand Jury > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 922

---

## Text

The powers and duties of the grand jury in connection with proceedings for the removal of district, county, or city officers are prescribed in Article 3 (commencing with Section 3060), Chapter 7, Division 4, Title 1, of the Government Code.
