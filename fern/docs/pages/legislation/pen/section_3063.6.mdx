# PEN § 3063.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3. Paroles

**Citation:** PEN § 3063.6

---

## Text

Parole revocation proceedings and parole revocation extension proceedings may be conducted by a panel of one person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 695, Sec. 19. Effective September 15, 1992.)
