# PEN § 4225

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 2. Joint County Road Camp Act

**Citation:** PEN § 4225

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county not included within any joint county road camp district, and having a population of 150,000 or more persons, may establish and maintain a county road camp as provided in this article, and may provide a board of directors thereof, by passing the resolution and receiving the certificate of organization provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
