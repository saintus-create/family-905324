# PEN § 31670

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Handguns and Firearm Safety > Article: 2. Firearm Safety Certificate

**Citation:** PEN § 31670

---

## Text

Except for the provisions of former Section 12804, former Article 8 (commencing with Section 12800) of Chapter 6 of Title 2 of Part 4, as added by Section 10 of Chapter 942 of the Statutes of 2001, became operative on January 1, 2003.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
