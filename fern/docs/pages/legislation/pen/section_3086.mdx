# PEN § 3086

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3.5. County Boards of Parole Commissioners

**Citation:** PEN § 3086

---

## Text

Each county board shall not require, when setting terms or discharge dates, an admission of guilt to any crime for which an inmate was committed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 833.)
