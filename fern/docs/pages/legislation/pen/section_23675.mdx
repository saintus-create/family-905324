# PEN § 23675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. FIREARM SAFETY DEVICES, GUN SAFES, AND RELATED WARNINGS > Title: 4. FIREARMS

**Citation:** PEN § 23675

---

## Text

Compliance with the requirements set forth in this division does not relieve any person from liability to any other person as may be imposed pursuant to common law, statutory law, or local ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
