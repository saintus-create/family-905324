# PEN § 1150

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 4. The Verdict or Finding

**Citation:** PEN § 1150

---

## Text

The jury must render a general verdict, except that in a felony case, when they are in doubt as to the legal effect of the facts proved, they may, except upon a trial for libel, find a special verdict.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 390. Effective September 28, 1998.)
