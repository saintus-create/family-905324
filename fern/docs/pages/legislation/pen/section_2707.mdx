# PEN § 2707

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 1. Employment of Prisoners Generally

**Citation:** PEN § 2707

---

## Text

The director is further authorized and empowered to purchase, install, and equip, such machinery, tools, supplies, materials, and equipment as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2256.)
