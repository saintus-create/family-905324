# PEN § 307

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 7. Of Crimes Against Religion and Conscience, and Other Offenses Against Good Morals

**Citation:** PEN § 307

---

## Text

Every person, firm, or corporation which sells or gives or in any way furnishes to another person, who is in fact under the age of 21 years, any candy, cake, cookie, or chewing gum which contains alcohol in excess of 12 of 1 percent by weight, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 934, Sec. 4.)
