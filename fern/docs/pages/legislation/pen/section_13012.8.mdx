# PEN § 13012.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 1. Department Of Justice > Article: 1. Duties of the Department

**Citation:** PEN § 13012.8

---

## Text

The annual report published by the department pursuant to Section 13010 shall include information concerning arrests for violations of Section 597.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 237, Sec. 1. (SB 1200) Effective January 1, 2017.)
