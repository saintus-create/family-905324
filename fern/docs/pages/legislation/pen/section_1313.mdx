# PEN § 1313

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 8. Recommitment of the Defendant, After Having Given Bail or Deposited Money Instead of Bail

**Citation:** PEN § 1313

---

## Text

If the order recites, as the ground upon which it is made, the failure of the defendant to appear for judgment upon conviction, the defendant must be committed according to the requirement of the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
