# PEN § 31665

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Handguns and Firearm Safety > Article: 2. Firearm Safety Certificate

**Citation:** PEN § 31665

---

## Text

The department shall be immune from any liability arising from implementing Sections 31630, 31635, 31640, and subdivision (a) of Section 31655.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
