# PEN § 1342

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Examination of Witnesses Conditionally

**Citation:** PEN § 1342

---

## Text

The attendance of the witness may be enforced by a subpoena, issued by the magistrate before whom the examination is to be taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
