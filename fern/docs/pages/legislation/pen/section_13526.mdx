# PEN § 13526

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 3. Peace Officers’ Training Fund and Allocations Therefrom

**Citation:** PEN § 13526

---

## Text

An allocation shall not be made from the State Penalty Fund, pursuant to this article, to a local government agency if the agency was not entitled to receive funding under any of the provisions of this article, as they read on December 31, 1989.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 47. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
