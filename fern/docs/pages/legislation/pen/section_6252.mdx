# PEN § 6252

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9.5. Community Correctional Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6252

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall make rules and regulations for the government of the community correctional centers in the management of their affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1931.)
