# PEN § 1508

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Writ of Habeas Corpus

**Citation:** PEN § 1508

---

## Text

(a) A writ of habeas corpus issued by the Supreme Court or a judge thereof may be made returnable before the issuing judge or his court, before any court of appeal or judge thereof, or before any superior court or judge thereof.

(b) A writ of habeas corpus issued by a court of appeal or a judge thereof may be made returnable before the issuing judge or his court or before any superior court or judge thereof located in that appellate district.

(c) A writ of habeas corpus issued by a superior court or a judge thereof may be made returnable before the issuing judge or his court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 38.)
