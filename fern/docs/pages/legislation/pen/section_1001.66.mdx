# PEN § 1001.66

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.9A. Bad Check Diversion

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.66

---

## Text

At no time shall a defendant be required to make an admission of guilt as a prerequisite for placement in a precomplaint diversion program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1059, Sec. 1.)
