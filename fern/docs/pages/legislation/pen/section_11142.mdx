# PEN § 11142

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 6. Unlawful Furnishing of State Summary Criminal History Information

**Citation:** PEN § 11142

---

## Text

Any person authorized by law to receive a record or information obtained from a record who knowingly furnishes the record or information to a person who is not authorized by law to receive the record or information is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 963.)
