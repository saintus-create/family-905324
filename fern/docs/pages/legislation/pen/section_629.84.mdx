# PEN § 629.84

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. MISCELLANEOUS CRIMES > Chapter: 1.4. Interception of Wire, Electronic Digital Pager, or Electronic Cellular Telephone Communications

**Citation:** PEN § 629.84

---

## Text

Any violation of this chapter is punishable by a fine not exceeding two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500), or by imprisonment in the county jail not exceeding one year, or by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 427. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 629.98.)
