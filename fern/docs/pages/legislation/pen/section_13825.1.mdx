# PEN § 13825.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. CALIFORNIA COUNCIL ON CRIMINAL JUSTICE > Chapter: 3.1. The California Gang, Crime, and Violence Prevention Partnership Program

**Citation:** PEN § 13825.1

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Gang, Crime, and Violence Prevention Partnership Program.

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 885, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
