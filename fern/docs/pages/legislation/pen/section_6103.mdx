# PEN § 6103

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 8. The Medical Facility

**Citation:** PEN § 6103

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall construct and equip, in accordance with law, suitable buildings, structures, and facilities for the Medical Facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1491.)
