# PEN § 6224.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9.2. Restitution Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6224.5

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections may commingle inmates who have been assigned to a restitution center pursuant to Section 6227 with inmates who are in transit for community correctional reentry center placement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 249, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
