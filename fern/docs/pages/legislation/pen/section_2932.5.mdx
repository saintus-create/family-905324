# PEN § 2932.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 7. Execution of Sentences of Imprisonment > Article: 2.5. Credit on Term of Imprisonment

**Citation:** PEN § 2932.5

---

## Text

A prisoner who is found by a trial court to be a vexatious litigant as defined by Section 391 of the Code of Civil Procedure, shall be denied or lose 30 days of work time credit awarded under Section 2933.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 852, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
