# PEN § 22625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. STUN GUN > Title: 3. WEAPONS AND DEVICES OTHER THAN FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Metal Knuckles

**Citation:** PEN § 22625

---

## Text

(a) Each stun gun sold in this state shall be accompanied by an instruction booklet.

(b) Violation of this section shall be a public offense punishable by a fifty-dollar ($50) fine for each weapon sold without the booklet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
