# PEN § 6126.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 8.2. Office of the Inspector General

**Citation:** PEN § 6126.2

---

## Text

The Inspector General shall not hire any person known to be considered a suspect or subject in an investigation being conducted by any federal, state, or local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 364, Sec. 13. (SB 112) Effective September 27, 2019.)
