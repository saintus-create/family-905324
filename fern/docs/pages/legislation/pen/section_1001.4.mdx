# PEN § 1001.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.7. Misdemeanor Diversion

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.4

---

## Text

A divertee is entitled to a hearing, as set forth by law, before his or her pretrial diversion can be terminated for cause.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 42, Sec. 2. Effective February 17, 1982.)
