# PEN § 4496.17

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.8. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND YOUTH FACILITY BOND ACT OF 1988 > Chapter: 2. Program

**Citation:** PEN § 4496.17

---

## Text

The Department of the Youth Authority shall administer funds appropriated for juvenile facilities as specified in paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 4496.12.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1130, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1989.)
