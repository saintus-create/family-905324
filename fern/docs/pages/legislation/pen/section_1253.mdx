# PEN § 1253

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 3. Argument of the Appeal

**Citation:** PEN § 1253

---

## Text

The judgment may be affirmed if the appellant fail to appear, but can be reversed only after argument, though the respondent fail to appear.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
