# PEN § 930

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Grand Jury > Article: 2. Investigation of County, City, and District Affairs

**Citation:** PEN § 930

---

## Text

If any grand jury shall, in the report above mentioned, comment upon any person or official who has not been indicted by such grand jury such comments shall not be deemed to be privileged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 501.)
