# PEN § 4852.05

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. REPRIEVES, PARDONS AND COMMUTATIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Procedure for Restoration of Rights and Application for Pardon

**Citation:** PEN § 4852.05

---

## Text

The person shall live an honest and upright life, shall conduct himself or herself with sobriety and industry, shall exhibit a good moral character, and shall conform to and obey the laws of the land.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 981, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1997.)
