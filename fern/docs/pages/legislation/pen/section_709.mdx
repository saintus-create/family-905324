# PEN § 709

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 3. Security to Keep the Peace

**Citation:** PEN § 709

---

## Text

The undertaking must be filed by the magistrate in the office of the Clerk of the county.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
