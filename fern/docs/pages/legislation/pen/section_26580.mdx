# PEN § 26580

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. License Requirement for Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 1. License Requirement and Miscellaneous Exceptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26580

---

## Text

Section 26500 does not apply to the loan of an unloaded firearm or the loan of a firearm loaded with blank cartridges for use solely as a prop for a motion picture, television, or video production or entertainment or theatrical event.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
