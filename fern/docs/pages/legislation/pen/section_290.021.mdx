# PEN § 290.021

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 5.5. Sex Offenders

**Citation:** PEN § 290.021

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the statements, photographs, and fingerprints required by the Act shall not be open to inspection by the public or by any person other than a regularly employed peace officer or other law enforcement officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 579, Sec. 29. Effective October 13, 2007.)
