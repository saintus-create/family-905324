# PEN § 28150

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Recordkeeping, Background Checks, and Fees Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 2. Form of the Register or the Record of Electronic Transfer

**Citation:** PEN § 28150

---

## Text

As used in this article, the following words have the following meanings:

(a) “Purchase” means the purchase, loan, or transfer of a firearm.

(b) “Purchaser” means the purchaser or transferee of a firearm or the person being loaned a firearm.

(c) “Sale” means the sale, loan, or transfer of a firearm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
