# PEN § 1498

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Writ of Habeas Corpus

**Citation:** PEN § 1498

---

## Text

The Court or Judge may also insert in such warrant a command for the apprehension of the person charged with such illegal detention and restraint.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
