# PEN § 626.91

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. MISCELLANEOUS CRIMES > Chapter: 1. Schools

**Citation:** PEN § 626.91

---

## Text

Possession of ammunition on school grounds is governed by Section 30310.

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 1. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
