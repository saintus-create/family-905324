# PEN § 6264

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9.6. Work Furlough Programs

**Citation:** PEN § 6264

---

## Text

The Department of Corrections shall review each inmate for work furlough consideration at least 120 days prior to his or her scheduled parole date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 596, Sec. 1.)
