# PEN § 863

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 863

---

## Text

The commitment for examination is made by an indorsement, signed by the magistrate on the warrant of arrest, to the following effect: “The within named A.B. having been brought before me under this warrant, is committed for examination to the Sheriff of ____.” If the Sheriff is not present, the defendant may be committed to the custody of a peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
