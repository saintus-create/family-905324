# PEN § 6355

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 10.5. Prison Visitor Services

**Citation:** PEN § 6355

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is intended to limit the department in developing additional programs or making all reasonable efforts to promote visits to prisoners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 17, Sec. 1.)
