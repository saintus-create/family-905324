# PEN § 3083

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3.5. County Boards of Parole Commissioners

**Citation:** PEN § 3083

---

## Text

Whenever the board designates deputies to serve as temporary commissioners in considering applications for parole of prisoners, such temporary commissioners or deputies may also exercise all the powers granted by this article relative to the unconditional release of prisoners who are not citizens or nationals of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 296, Sec. 47. (AB 1096) Effective January 1, 2022.)
