# PEN § 11174.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 2.7. Elder and Dependent Adult Death Review Teams

**Citation:** PEN § 11174.4

---

## Text

The following definitions shall govern the construction of this article, unless the context requires otherwise:

(a) “Elder” means any person who is 65 years of age or older.

(b) (1) “Abuse” means any of the conduct described in Article 2 (commencing with Section 15610) of Chapter 11 of Part 3 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

(2) Abuse does not include the use of any reasonable and necessary force that may result in an injury used by a peace officer acting within the course of his or her employment as a peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 301) by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 174. Effective January 1, 2003.)
