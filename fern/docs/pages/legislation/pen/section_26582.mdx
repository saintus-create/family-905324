# PEN § 26582

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. License Requirement for Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 1. License Requirement and Miscellaneous Exceptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26582

---

## Text

Section 26500 does not apply to delivery or transfer of a firearm to a law enforcement agency made in accordance with Section 27922.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 110, Sec. 6. (AB 1292) Effective January 1, 2020.)
