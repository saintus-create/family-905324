# PEN § 273.86

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 2.5. Spousal Abusers

**Citation:** PEN § 273.86

---

## Text

The characterization of a defendant as a “spousal abuser” as defined by this chapter shall not be communicated to the trier of fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1122, Sec. 1.)
