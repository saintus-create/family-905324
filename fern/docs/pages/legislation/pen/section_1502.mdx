# PEN § 1502

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Writ of Habeas Corpus

**Citation:** PEN § 1502

---

## Text

Any writ or process authorized by this Chapter may be issued and served on any day or at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
