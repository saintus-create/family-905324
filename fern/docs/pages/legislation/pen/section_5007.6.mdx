# PEN § 5007.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 1. The Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation

**Citation:** PEN § 5007.6

---

## Text

The Secretary of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation shall not charge a fee for an incarcerated person to request, review, or use their medical records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 837, Sec. 1. (SB 1139) Effective January 1, 2023.)
