# PEN § 487i

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 5. Larceny

**Citation:** PEN § 487i

---

## Text

Any person who defrauds a housing program of a public housing authority of more than four hundred dollars ($400) is guilty of grand theft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 105, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
