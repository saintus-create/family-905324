# PEN § 486

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 5. Larceny

**Citation:** PEN § 486

---

## Text

Theft is divided into two degrees, the first of which is termed grand theft; the second, petty theft.
