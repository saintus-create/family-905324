# PEN § 13352

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 8. Referral of Persons on Supervised Release

**Citation:** PEN § 13352

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to supervised persons under the jurisdiction of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation until July 1, 2021.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 650, Sec. 1. (SB 620) Effective January 1, 2020.)
