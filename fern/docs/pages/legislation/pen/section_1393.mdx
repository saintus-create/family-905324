# PEN § 1393

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 9. Proceedings Against Corporations

**Citation:** PEN § 1393

---

## Text

At the appointed time in the summons, the magistrate shall proceed with the charge in the same manner as in other cases.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1591.)
