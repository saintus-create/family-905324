# PEN § 4852.09

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. REPRIEVES, PARDONS AND COMMUTATIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Procedure for Restoration of Rights and Application for Pardon

**Citation:** PEN § 4852.09

---

## Text

No filing fee nor court fees of any kind shall be required of a petitioner in proceedings under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 400.)
