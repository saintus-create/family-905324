# PEN § 13104

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 1. Legislative Findings and Definitions

**Citation:** PEN § 13104

---

## Text

Any certified reproduction of any record stored on a nonerasable storage medium under the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed to be a certification of the original record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 257, Sec. 6.)
