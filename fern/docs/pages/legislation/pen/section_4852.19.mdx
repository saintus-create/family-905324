# PEN § 4852.19

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. REPRIEVES, PARDONS AND COMMUTATIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Procedure for Restoration of Rights and Application for Pardon

**Citation:** PEN § 4852.19

---

## Text

This chapter shall be construed as providing an additional, but not an exclusive, procedure for the restoration of rights and application for pardon. Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as repealing any other provision of law providing for restoration of rights or application for pardon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 400.)
