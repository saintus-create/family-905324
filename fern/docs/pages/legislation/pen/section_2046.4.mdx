# PEN § 2046.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 6. California Men’s Colony

**Citation:** PEN § 2046.4

---

## Text

A warden for the said prison shall be appointed pursuant to Section 6050, and the Director of Corrections shall apoint, subject to civil service, such other officials and employees as may be necessary therefor, and shall fix their compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 892.)
