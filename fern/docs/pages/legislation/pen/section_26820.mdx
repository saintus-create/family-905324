# PEN § 26820

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Issuance, Forfeiture, and Conditions of License to Sell, Lease, or Transfer Firearms at Retail > Article: 2. Grounds for Forfeiture of License

**Citation:** PEN § 26820

---

## Text

No handgun or imitation handgun, or placard advertising the sale or other transfer thereof, shall be displayed in any part of the premises where it can readily be seen from the outside.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 745, Sec. 8. (AB 809) Effective January 1, 2012.)
