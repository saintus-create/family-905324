# PEN § 1001.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.7. Misdemeanor Diversion

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.8

---

## Text

Any record filed with the Department of Justice shall indicate the disposition of those cases diverted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 42, Sec. 2. Effective February 17, 1982.)
