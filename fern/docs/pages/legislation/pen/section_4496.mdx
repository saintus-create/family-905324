# PEN § 4496

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.8. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND YOUTH FACILITY BOND ACT OF 1988 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 4496

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the County Correctional Facility Capital Expenditure and Youth Facility Bond Act of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 264, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 86 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
