# PEN § 2048.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 7. California Correctional Institution at Tehachapi

**Citation:** PEN § 2048.5

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall construct and equip, in accordance with law, suitable buildings, structures, and facilities for the California Correctional Institution at Tehachapi.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1451.)
