# PEN § 30910

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Assault Weapons and .50 BMG Rifles > Article: 5. Registration of Assault Weapons and .50 BMG Rifles and Related Rules

**Citation:** PEN § 30910

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 30925, no assault weapon possessed pursuant to this article may be sold or transferred on or after January 1, 1990, to anyone within this state other than to a licensed gun dealer or as provided in Section 31100.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
