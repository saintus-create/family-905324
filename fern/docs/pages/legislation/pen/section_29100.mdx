# PEN § 29100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Issuance, Forfeiture, and Conditions of License to Manufacture Firearms > Article: 3. Prohibitions and Requirements Applicable to Licensee

**Citation:** PEN § 29100

---

## Text

A licensee shall comply with the prohibitions and requirements described in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
