# PEN § 147

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 7. Other Offenses Against Public Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 147

---

## Text

Every officer who is guilty of willful inhumanity or oppression toward any prisoner under his care or in his custody, is punishable by fine not exceeding four thousand dollars ($4,000), and by removal from office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 240. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
