# PEN § 2080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of State Prisons > Article: 2. Wardens

**Citation:** PEN § 2080

---

## Text

A copy of the rules and regulations prescribing the duties and obligations of prisoners shall be furnished to each prisoner in a state prison or other facility under the jurisdiction of the Department of Corrections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2047 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 933.)
