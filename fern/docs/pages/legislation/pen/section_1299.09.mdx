# PEN § 1299.09

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 5.5. Bail Fugitive Recovery Persons Act

**Citation:** PEN § 1299.09

---

## Text

An individual, authorized by Section 1299.02 to apprehend a bail fugitive shall not forcibly enter a premises except as provided for in Section 844.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 747, Sec. 1. (AB 2029) Effective January 1, 2013.)
