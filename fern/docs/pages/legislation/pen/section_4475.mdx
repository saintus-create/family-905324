# PEN § 4475

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.7. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE BOND ACT OF 1986 > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** PEN § 4475

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the County Correctional Facility Capital Expenditure Bond Act of 1986.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 52 at the June 3, 1986, election.)
