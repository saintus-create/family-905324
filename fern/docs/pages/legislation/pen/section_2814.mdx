# PEN § 2814

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 6. Sale of Prison-Made Goods > Article: 1. Prison Industry Authority

**Citation:** PEN § 2814

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provision of this chapter, products and byproducts of agricultural and animal husbandry enterprises, except nursery stock, may be sold to private persons, at public or private sale, under rules prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1150, Sec. 3.)
