# PEN § 1170.16

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 4.5. Trial Court Sentencing > Article: 1. Initial Sentencing

**Citation:** PEN § 1170.16

---

## Text

In lieu of the term provided in Section 1170.1, a full, separate, and consecutive term may be imposed for each violation of subdivision (a) of Section 192, whether or not the offenses were committed during a single transaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 421, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
