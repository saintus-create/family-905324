# PEN § 1170.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 4.5. Trial Court Sentencing > Article: 1. Initial Sentencing

**Citation:** PEN § 1170.5

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall conduct annual sentencing institutes for trial court judges pursuant to Section 68551 of the Government Code, toward the end of assisting the judge in the imposition of appropriate sentences.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1139.)
