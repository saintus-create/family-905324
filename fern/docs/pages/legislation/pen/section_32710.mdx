# PEN § 32710

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Machineguns > Article: 4. Licenses to Sell Machineguns

**Citation:** PEN § 32710

---

## Text

(a) Applications and licenses under this article shall be uniform throughout the state, on forms prescribed by the Department of Justice.

(b) A license under this article shall be effective for not more than one year from the date of issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
