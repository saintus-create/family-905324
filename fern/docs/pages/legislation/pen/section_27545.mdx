# PEN § 27545

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 1. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms

**Citation:** PEN § 27545

---

## Text

Where neither party to the transaction holds a dealer’s license issued pursuant to Sections 26700 to 26915, inclusive, the parties to the transaction shall complete the sale, loan, or transfer of that firearm through a licensed firearms dealer pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 28050).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
