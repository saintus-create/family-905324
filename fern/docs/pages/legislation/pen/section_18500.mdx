# PEN § 18500

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Liability

**Citation:** PEN § 18500

---

## Text

The law enforcement agency, or the individual law enforcement officer, shall not be liable for any act in the good faith exercise of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
