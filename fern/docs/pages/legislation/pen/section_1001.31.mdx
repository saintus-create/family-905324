# PEN § 1001.31

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.8. Diversion of Defendants With Cognitive Developmental Disabilities

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.31

---

## Text

If the divertee has performed satisfactorily during the period of diversion, the criminal charges shall be dismissed at the end of the diversion period.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1253, Sec. 1.)
