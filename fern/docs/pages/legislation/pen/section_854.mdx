# PEN § 854

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 6. Retaking After an Escape or Rescue

**Citation:** PEN § 854

---

## Text

If a person arrested escape or is rescued, the person from whose custody he escaped or was rescued, may immediately pursue and retake him at any time and in any place within the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
