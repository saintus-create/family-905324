# PEN § 25800

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Carrying a Loaded Firearm > Article: 1. Armed Criminal Action

**Citation:** PEN § 25800

---

## Text

(a) Every person who carries a loaded firearm with the intent to commit a felony is guilty of armed criminal action.

(b) Armed criminal action is punishable by imprisonment in a county jail not exceeding one year, or in the state prison.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
