# PEN § 1334.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3a. Attendance of Witnesses Outside the State

**Citation:** PEN § 1334.6

---

## Text

This chapter shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law of the States which enact similar legislation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 262.)
