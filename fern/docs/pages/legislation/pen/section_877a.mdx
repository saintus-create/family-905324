# PEN § 877a

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 877a

---

## Text

When the commitment is made under the provisions of section 859a of this code, it must be made to the following effect:

County of ____ (as the case may be).

The people of the State of California to the sheriff of the county of ____.

A.B. having pleaded guilty to the offense of (stating briefly the nature of the offense, and giving as near as may be the time when and the place where the same was committed), you are commanded to receive him into your custody and detain him until he is legally discharged.

Dated this ____ day of ____ nineteen ____.

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 217.)
