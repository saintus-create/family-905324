# PEN § 688

**Hierarchy:** Part: 2. OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Title: 17. RIGHTS OF VICTIMS AND WITNESSES OF CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 688

---

## Text

No person charged with a public offense may be subjected, before conviction, to any more restraint than is necessary for his detention to answer the charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 299.)
