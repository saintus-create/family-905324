# PEN § 1389.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 8.5. Agreement on Detainers

**Citation:** PEN § 1389.8

---

## Text

It shall be the responsibility of the agent of the receiving state to return the prisoner to the sending state upon completion of the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1185.)
