# PEN § 692

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 1. Of Lawful Resistance

**Citation:** PEN § 692

---

## Text

Lawful resistance to the commission of a public offense may be made:

1. By the party about to be injured;

2. By other parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
