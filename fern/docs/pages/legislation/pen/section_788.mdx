# PEN § 788

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 1. Of the Local Jurisdiction of Public Offenses

**Citation:** PEN § 788

---

## Text

The jurisdiction of a criminal action for treason, when the overt act is committed out of the State, is in any county of the State.
