# PEN § 3010.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 2. Electronic Monitoring

**Citation:** PEN § 3010.6

---

## Text

A parole officer may revoke, in his or her discretion, the continuous monitoring of any individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 484, Sec. 2. Effective October 4, 2005.)
