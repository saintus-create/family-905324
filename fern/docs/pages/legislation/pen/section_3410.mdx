# PEN § 3410

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. IMPRISONMENT OF FEMALE PRISONERS IN STATE INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 4. Community Treatment Programs

**Citation:** PEN § 3410

---

## Text

The term “community” shall, for the purposes of this chapter, mean an environment away from the prison setting which is in an urban or suburban area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1054.)
