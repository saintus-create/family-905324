# PEN § 14130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10.2. California Violence Intervention and Prevention Grant Program

**Citation:** PEN § 14130

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Break the Cycle of Violence Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 735, Sec. 1. (AB 1603) Effective January 1, 2020.)
