# PEN § 11158

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 1.5. Reports of Disposition of Inmates

**Citation:** PEN § 11158

---

## Text

As used in this article, “victim” means any person alleged or found, upon the record, to have sustained physical or financial injury to person or property as a direct result of the crime charged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1048, Sec. 1.)
