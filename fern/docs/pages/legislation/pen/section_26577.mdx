# PEN § 26577

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. License Requirement for Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 1. License Requirement and Miscellaneous Exceptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26577

---

## Text

Section 26500 does not apply to a delivery or transfer of firearms made to a dealer pursuant to Section 26892 or 29830 for storage by that dealer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 251, Sec. 6. (SB 368) Effective January 1, 2024.)
