# PEN § 1280

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 2. Bail Upon Being Held to Answer Before Indictment

**Citation:** PEN § 1280

---

## Text

The bail must in all cases justify by affidavit taken before the magistrate, that they each possess the qualifications provided in the preceding section. The magistrate may further examine the bail upon oath concerning their sufficiency, in such manner as he may deem proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
