# PEN § 2674

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 4. Treatment of Prisoners > Article: 2. Organic Therapy

**Citation:** PEN § 2674

---

## Text

A written manifestation of informed consent shall be obtained in all cases by the attending physician and shall be preserved and available to the person, his attorney, his guardian, or his conservator.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1513.)
