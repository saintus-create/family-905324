# PEN § 3326

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. IMPRISONMENT OF FEMALE PRISONERS IN STATE INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of Institution > Article: 1. Administration of Institution for Women

**Citation:** PEN § 3326

---

## Text

The department is authorized to provide the necessary facilities, equipment, and personnel to operate a commissary at any institution under its jurisdiction for the sale of toilet articles, candy, gum, notions, and other sundries.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 798, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2005, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 798.)
