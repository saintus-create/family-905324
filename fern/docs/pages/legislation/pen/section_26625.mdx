# PEN § 26625

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. License Requirement for Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 2. Exceptions Relating to Law Enforcement

**Citation:** PEN § 26625

---

## Text

Section 26500 does not apply to the loan of a firearm if the loan of the firearm is to a person enrolled in the course of basic training prescribed by the Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training, or any other course certified by the commission, for purposes of participation in the course.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 783, Sec. 1. (AB 693) Effective October 14, 2017.)
