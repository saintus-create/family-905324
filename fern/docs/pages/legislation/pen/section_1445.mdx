# PEN § 1445

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. PROCEEDINGS IN MISDEMEANOR AND INFRACTION CASES AND APPEALS FROM SUCH CASES > Chapter: 1. Proceedings in Misdemeanor and Infraction Cases

**Citation:** PEN § 1445

---

## Text

When the defendant pleads guilty, or is convicted, either by the court, or by a jury, the court shall render judgment thereon of fine or imprisonment, or both, as the case may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1360, Sec. 117.)
