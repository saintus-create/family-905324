# PEN § 273.88

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 2.5. Spousal Abusers

**Citation:** PEN § 273.88

---

## Text

Administrative costs incurred by the Department of Justice pursuant to the Spousal Abuser Prosecution Program shall not exceed 5 percent of the total funds allocated for the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 599, Sec. 9. Effective September 16, 1994.)
