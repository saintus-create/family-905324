# PEN § 2695.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 4. Treatment of Prisoners > Article: 6. Veterans In State Prisons

**Citation:** PEN § 2695.1

---

## Text

At each facility that is under the jurisdiction of the Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation, a veterans service organization may volunteer to serve as a veterans service advocate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 652, Sec. 2. (AB 2263) Effective January 1, 2015.)
