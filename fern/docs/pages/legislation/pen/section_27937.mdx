# PEN § 27937

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 6. Exceptions to the Requirement of Using a Dealer for a Private Party Firearms Transaction

**Citation:** PEN § 27937

---

## Text

Section 27545 does not apply to sales, deliveries, or transfers of firearms made pursuant to Section 26556.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 738, Sec. 25. (SB 376) Effective January 1, 2020.)
