# PEN § 28155

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Recordkeeping, Background Checks, and Fees Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 2. Form of the Register or the Record of Electronic Transfer

**Citation:** PEN § 28155

---

## Text

The Department of Justice shall prescribe the form of the register and the record of electronic transfer pursuant to Section 28105.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
