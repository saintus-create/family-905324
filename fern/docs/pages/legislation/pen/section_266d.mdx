# PEN § 266d

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 1. Rape, Abduction, Carnal Abuse of Children, and Seduction

**Citation:** PEN § 266d

---

## Text

Any person who receives any money or other valuable thing for or on account of placing in custody any other person for the purpose of causing the other person to cohabit with any person to whom the other person is not married, is guilty of a felony.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 996.)
