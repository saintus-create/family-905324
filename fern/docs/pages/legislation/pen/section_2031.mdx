# PEN § 2031

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 3. California State Prison at Folsom

**Citation:** PEN § 2031

---

## Text

The California State Prison at Folsom shall be located at Folsom, in Sacramento County, California.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 106.)
