# PEN § 27945

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 6. Exceptions to the Requirement of Using a Dealer for a Private Party Firearms Transaction

**Citation:** PEN § 27945

---

## Text

Section 27545 does not apply to the loan of a firearm to a minor in compliance with the applicable exemptions set forth in Section 27505.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 250, Sec. 11. (SB 715) Effective January 1, 2022.)
