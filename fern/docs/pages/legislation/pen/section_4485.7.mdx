# PEN § 4485.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.7. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITY CAPITAL EXPENDITURE BOND ACT OF 1986 > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 4485.7

---

## Text

Moneys in the fund may be available for construction of joint-use correctional facilities housing county and state or federal prisoners or any combination thereof in proportion to the county’s benefit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 52 at the June 3, 1986, election.)
