# PEN § 17800

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 17800

---

## Text

For purposes of the provisions listed in Section 16590, a violation as to each firearm, weapon, or device enumerated in any of those provisions shall constitute a distinct and separate offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
