# PEN § 172c

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 7. Other Offenses Against Public Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 172c

---

## Text

Section 172a shall not apply to the sale at auction of alcoholic beverages by a nonprofit organization at the California Science Center premises located at Exposition Park, Los Angeles, California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 841, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1997.)
