# PEN § 839

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 5. Arrest, by Whom and How Made

**Citation:** PEN § 839

---

## Text

Any person making an arrest may orally summon as many persons as he deems necessary to aid him therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
