# PEN § 13514

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. STANDARDS AND TRAINING OF LOCAL LAW ENFORCEMENT OFFICERS > Chapter: 1. Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training > Article: 2. Field Services, Standards, and Certification

**Citation:** PEN § 13514

---

## Text

The commission shall prepare a course of instruction for the training of peace officers in the use of tear gas. Such course of instruction may be given, upon approval by the commission, by any agency or institution engaged in the training or instruction of peace officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1231.)
