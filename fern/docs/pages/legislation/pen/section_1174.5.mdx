# PEN § 1174.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 4.8. Pregnant and Parenting Women’s Alternative Sentencing Program Act

## Text

The department shall be responsible for the funding and monitoring of the progress, activities, and performance of each program.
