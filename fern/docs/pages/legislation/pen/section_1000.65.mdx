# PEN § 1000.65

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.5. Special Proceedings in Narcotics and Drug Abuse Cases

**Citation:** PEN § 1000.65

---

## Text

This chapter does not affect a pretrial diversion program provided pursuant to Chapter 2.7 (commencing with Section 1001).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 778, Sec. 8. (AB 208) Effective January 1, 2018.)
