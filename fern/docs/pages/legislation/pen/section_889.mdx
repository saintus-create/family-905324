# PEN § 889

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 889

---

## Text

An indictment is an accusation in writing, presented by the grand jury to a competent court, charging a person with a public offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 501.)
