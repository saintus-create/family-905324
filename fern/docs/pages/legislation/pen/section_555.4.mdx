# PEN § 555.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. OF CRIMES AGAINST PROPERTY > Chapter: 12. Unlawful Interference With Property > Article: 1. Trespassing or Loitering Near Posted Industrial Property

**Citation:** PEN § 555.4

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are applicable throughout the State in all counties and municipalities and no local authority shall enact or enforce any ordinance in conflict with such provisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 32.)
