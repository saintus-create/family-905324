# PEN § 2702

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 1. Employment of Prisoners Generally

**Citation:** PEN § 2702

---

## Text

No person imprisoned after conviction of a violation of Section 502 or of subdivision (b) of Section 502.7 shall be permitted to work on or have access to any computer system of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1357, Sec. 5.)
