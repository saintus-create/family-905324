# PEN § 1179

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 6. New Trials

**Citation:** PEN § 1179

---

## Text

A new trial is a reëxamination of the issue in the same Court, before another jury, after a verdict has been given.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
