# PEN § 4123

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 1. County Industrial Farms

**Citation:** PEN § 4123

---

## Text

Any person transferred from an industrial farm or camp to the county jail shall be maintained at the jail at the expense of the county as are other prisoners in such jail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
