# PEN § 2788

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 5. Employment in Public Parks, Forests, etc.

**Citation:** PEN § 2788

---

## Text

The director shall have full jurisdiction at all times over the discipline and control of the convicts performing work under this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2256.)
