# PEN § 11235

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 3. Prevention and Abatement of Unlawful Activities > Article: 2. Red Light Abatement Law

**Citation:** PEN § 11235

---

## Text

“Building” as used in this article means so much of any building or structure of any kind as is or may be entered through the same outside entrance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 35.)
