# PEN § 2910.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 7. Execution of Sentences of Imprisonment > Article: 1.5. Transfer of Prisoners

**Citation:** PEN § 2910.6

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections may enter into an agreement consistent with applicable law for a city, county, or city and county to construct and operate community corrections programs, restitution centers, halfway houses, work furlough programs, or other correctional programs authorized by state law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1450, Sec. 2.5.)
