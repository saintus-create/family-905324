# PEN § 27120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Issuance, Forfeiture, and Conditions of License to Sell, Lease, or Transfer Firearms at Retail > Article: 6. Other Exceptions

**Citation:** PEN § 27120

---

## Text

Article 1 (commencing with Section 26700) and Article 2 (commencing with Section 26800) do not apply to the sale, delivery, or transfer of unloaded firearms to a wholesaler if the firearms are being returned to the wholesaler and are intended as merchandise in the wholesaler’s business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
