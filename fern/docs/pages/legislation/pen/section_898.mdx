# PEN § 898

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2. Formation of Grand Jury > Article: 2. Listing and Selection of Grand Jurors

**Citation:** PEN § 898

---

## Text

The list of grand jurors made in a county having a population in excess of four million shall contain the number of persons which has been designated by the court in its order.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 259.)
