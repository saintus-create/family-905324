# PEN § 26370

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Openly Carrying an Unloaded Handgun > Article: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** PEN § 26370

---

## Text

Section 26350 does not apply to, or affect, the open carrying of an unloaded handgun within a school zone, as defined in Section 626.9, if that carrying is not prohibited by Section 626.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 779, Sec. 2. (AB 424) Effective January 1, 2018.)
