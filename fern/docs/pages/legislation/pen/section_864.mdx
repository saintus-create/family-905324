# PEN § 864

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 864

---

## Text

At the examination, the magistrate must first read to the defendant the depositions of the witnesses examined on taking the information.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1174.)
