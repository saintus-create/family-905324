# PEN § 17040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 17040

---

## Text

As used in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 26350) of Division 5 of Title 4, “public place” has the same meaning as in Section 25850.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 725, Sec. 8. (AB 144) Effective January 1, 2012.)
