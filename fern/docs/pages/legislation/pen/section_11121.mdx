# PEN § 11121

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 5. Examination of Records

**Citation:** PEN § 11121

---

## Text

It is the function and intent of this article to afford persons concerning whom a record is maintained in the files of the bureau an opportunity to obtain a copy of the record compiled from such files, and to refute any erroneous or inaccurate information contained therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 939, Sec. 1.)
