# PEN § 1001.32

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.8. Diversion of Defendants With Cognitive Developmental Disabilities

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.32

---

## Text

Any record filed with the State Department of Justice shall indicate the disposition of those cases diverted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1253, Sec. 1.)
