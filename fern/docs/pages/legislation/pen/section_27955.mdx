# PEN § 27955

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 6. Exceptions to the Requirement of Using a Dealer for a Private Party Firearms Transaction

**Citation:** PEN § 27955

---

## Text

Section 27545 does not apply to the loan of a firearm if all of the following requirements are satisfied:

(a) The loan is infrequent, as defined in Section 16730.

(b) The firearm is unloaded.

(c) The loan is made by a person who is neither a dealer nor a federal firearms licensee pursuant to Chapter 44 (commencing with Section 921) of Title 18 of the United States Code.

(d) The loan is made to a person 18 years of age or older.

(e) The loan is for use solely as a prop in a motion picture, television, video, theatrical, or other entertainment production or event.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
