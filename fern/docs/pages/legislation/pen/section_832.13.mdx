# PEN § 832.13

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 4.5. Peace Officers

**Citation:** PEN § 832.13

---

## Text

Every person employed as a peace officer shall immediately report all uses of force by the officer to the officer’s department or agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 402, Sec. 5. (SB 16) Effective January 1, 2022.)
