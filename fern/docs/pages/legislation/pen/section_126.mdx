# PEN § 126

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 5. Perjury and Subornation of Perjury

**Citation:** PEN § 126

---

## Text

Perjury is punishable by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 for two, three or four years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 249. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.)
