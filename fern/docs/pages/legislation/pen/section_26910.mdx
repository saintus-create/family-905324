# PEN § 26910

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Issuance, Forfeiture, and Conditions of License to Sell, Lease, or Transfer Firearms at Retail > Article: 2. Grounds for Forfeiture of License

**Citation:** PEN § 26910

---

## Text

A licensee shall forward, in a format prescribed by the Department of Justice, information as required by the department on any firearm that is not delivered within the time period set forth in Section 478.102(c) of Title 27 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
