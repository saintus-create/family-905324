# PEN § 3088

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3.5. County Boards of Parole Commissioners

**Citation:** PEN § 3088

---

## Text

A prisoner who is released on parole pursuant to this article shall be supervised by a county parole officer of the county board of parole commissioners.
