# PEN § 11179

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 3. Uniform Act for Out-of-State Parolee Supervision

**Citation:** PEN § 11179

---

## Text

This article and compacts made pursuant thereto shall be construed as separate and distinct from any act or acts of this State relating to the extradition of fugitives from justice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1384.)
