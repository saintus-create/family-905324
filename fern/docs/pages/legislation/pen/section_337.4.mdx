# PEN § 337.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 10.5. Horse Racing

**Citation:** PEN § 337.4

---

## Text

Any person who in the commission of touting obtains money in excess of nine hundred fifty dollars ($950) may, in addition to being prosecuted for the violation of any provision of this chapter, be prosecuted for the violation of Section 487 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 28, Sec. 8. (SB 18 3x) Effective January 25, 2010.)
