# PEN § 2048.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 7. California Correctional Institution at Tehachapi

**Citation:** PEN § 2048.2

---

## Text

Any person under the custody of the Director of Corrections may be transferred to the California Correctional Institution at Tehachapi in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1451.)
