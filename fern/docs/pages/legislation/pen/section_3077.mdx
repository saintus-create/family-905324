# PEN § 3077

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 8. Length of Term of Imprisonment and Paroles > Article: 3.5. County Boards of Parole Commissioners

**Citation:** PEN § 3077

---

## Text

Whenever a prisoner is sentenced in one county and incarcerated in another county, only the county in which he was sentenced shall have jurisdiction to grant parole.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 918.)
