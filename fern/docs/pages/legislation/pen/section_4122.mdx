# PEN § 4122

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 1. County Industrial Farms

**Citation:** PEN § 4122

---

## Text

The cost of transporting city prisoners to an industrial farm or camp shall be borne by the city from whose courts they were committed. All other transportation charges shall be borne by the county and paid out of the general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
