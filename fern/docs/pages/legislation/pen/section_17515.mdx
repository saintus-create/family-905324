# PEN § 17515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. MISCELLANEOUS RULES RELATING TO WEAPONS GENERALLY > Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY

**Citation:** PEN § 17515

---

## Text

Nothing in any provision listed in Section 16580 prohibits a police officer, special police officer, peace officer, or law enforcement officer from carrying any equipment authorized for the enforcement of law or ordinance in any city or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
