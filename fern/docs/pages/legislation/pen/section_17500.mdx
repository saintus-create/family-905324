# PEN § 17500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. MISCELLANEOUS RULES RELATING TO WEAPONS GENERALLY > Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY

**Citation:** PEN § 17500

---

## Text

Every person having upon the person any deadly weapon, with intent to assault another, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
