# PEN § 32720

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Machineguns > Article: 4. Licenses to Sell Machineguns

**Citation:** PEN § 32720

---

## Text

Upon breach of any of the conditions stated in Section 32700, a license under this article shall be revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
