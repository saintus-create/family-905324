# PEN § 1448

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. PROCEEDINGS IN MISDEMEANOR AND INFRACTION CASES AND APPEALS FROM SUCH CASES > Chapter: 1. Proceedings in Misdemeanor and Infraction Cases

**Citation:** PEN § 1448

---

## Text

If the complainant does not pay the costs, or give an undertaking therefor, the court may enter judgment against the complainant for the amount of the costs, which may be enforced in the manner provided for enforcement of money judgments generally.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 18, Sec. 25. Effective April 21, 1983.)
