# PEN § 2771

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 5. Employment of Prisoners > Article: 4. Employment at Road Camps

**Citation:** PEN § 2771

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall have full jurisdiction at all times over the discipline and control of the prisoners employed on said roads.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1380.)
