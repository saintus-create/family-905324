# PEN § 13848.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. CALIFORNIA COUNCIL ON CRIMINAL JUSTICE > Chapter: 5.7. High Technology Theft Apprehension and Prosecution Program

**Citation:** PEN § 13848.2

---

## Text

There is hereby established a program of financial and technical assistance for law enforcement and district attorneys’ offices, designated the High Technology Theft Apprehension and Prosecution Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 43, Sec. 75. (SB 1023) Effective June 27, 2012.)
