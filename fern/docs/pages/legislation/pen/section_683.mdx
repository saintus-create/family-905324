# PEN § 683

**Hierarchy:** Part: 2. OF CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Title: 17. RIGHTS OF VICTIMS AND WITNESSES OF CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 683

---

## Text

The proceeding by which a party charged with a public offense is accused and brought to trial and punishment, is known as a criminal action.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
