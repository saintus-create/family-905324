# PEN § 4906

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. REPRIEVES, PARDONS AND COMMUTATIONS > Chapter: 5. Indemnity for Persons Erroneously Convicted and Pardoned

**Citation:** PEN § 4906

---

## Text

The California Victim Compensation Board is hereby authorized to make all needful rules and regulations consistent with the law for the purpose of carrying into effect this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 255. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
