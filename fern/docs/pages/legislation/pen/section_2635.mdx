# PEN § 2635

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 3. Civil Rights of Prisoners > Article: 3. Sexual Abuse in Detention

**Citation:** PEN § 2635

---

## Text

2635.The Department of Corrections and Rehabilitation shall review informational handbooks regarding sexual abuse in detention published by outside organizations. Upon approving the content thereof, handbooks provided by one or more outside organizations shall be made available to inmates and wards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 303, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
