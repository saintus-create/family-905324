# PEN § 875

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 875

---

## Text

If the offense is bailable, and the defendant is admitted to bail, the following words must be added to the order, “and that he be admitted to bail in the sum of ____ dollars, and is committed to the Sheriff of the County of ____ until he gives such bail.”
