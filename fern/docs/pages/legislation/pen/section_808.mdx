# PEN § 808

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 3. Complaints Before Magistrates

**Citation:** PEN § 808

---

## Text

The following persons are magistrates:

(a) The judges of the Supreme Court.

(b) The judges of the courts of appeal.

(c) The judges of the superior courts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 62, Sec. 229. Effective January 1, 2004.)
