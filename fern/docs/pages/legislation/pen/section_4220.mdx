# PEN § 4220

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 2. Joint County Road Camp Act

**Citation:** PEN § 4220

---

## Text

All payments provided for in Section 4219 shall be made by warrants drawn on the proper fund in favor of “Joint County Road Camp District No. ________” (inserting the number assigned by the Secretary of State), and shall become a portion of the revolving fund provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
