# PEN § 4128

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 1. County Industrial Farms

**Citation:** PEN § 4128

---

## Text

Payments authorized under this article to be made to any person other than the prisoner may be made weekly on any day designated by the superintendent of the farm or camp.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
