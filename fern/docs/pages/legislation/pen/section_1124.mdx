# PEN § 1124

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 2. The Trial

**Citation:** PEN § 1124

---

## Text

The Court must decide all questions of law which arise in the course of a trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
