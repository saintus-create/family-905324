# PEN § 7516.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. MEDICAL TESTING OF PRISONERS > Chapter: 2. Procedures for Requiring HIV Testing

**Citation:** PEN § 7516.8

---

## Text

It shall be the responsibility of the chief medical officer to see that copies of the hearing decision are distributed in accordance with requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1579, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1988.)
