# PEN § 1054.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 10. Discovery

**Citation:** PEN § 1054.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as limiting any law enforcement or prosecuting agency from obtaining nontestimonial evidence to the extent permitted by law on the effective date of this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 115.)
