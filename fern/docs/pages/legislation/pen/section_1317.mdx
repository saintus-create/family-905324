# PEN § 1317

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 8. Recommitment of the Defendant, After Having Given Bail or Deposited Money Instead of Bail

**Citation:** PEN § 1317

---

## Text

The bail must possess the qualifications, and must be put in, in all respects, in the manner prescribed in Article II of this Chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
