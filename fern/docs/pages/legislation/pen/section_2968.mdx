# PEN § 2968

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 7. Execution of Sentences of Imprisonment > Article: 4. Disposition of Mentally Disordered Prisoners Upon Discharge

**Citation:** PEN § 2968

---

## Text

If the prisoner’s severe mental health disorder is put into remission during the parole period, and can be kept in remission, the Director of State Hospitals shall notify the Board of Parole Hearings and the State Department of State Hospitals shall discontinue treating the parolee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 9, Sec. 9. (AB 46) Effective January 1, 2020.)
