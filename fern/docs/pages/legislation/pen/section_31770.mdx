# PEN § 31770

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Handguns and Firearm Safety > Article: 3. Exceptions to Firearm Safety Certificate Requirement

**Citation:** PEN § 31770

---

## Text

Subdivision (a) of Section 31615 does not apply to deliveries, transfers, or returns of firearms made pursuant to any of the following:

(a) Sections 18000 and 18005.

(b) Division 4 (commencing with Section 18250) of Title 2.

(c) Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 33850) of Division 11.

(d) Sections 34005 and 34010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
