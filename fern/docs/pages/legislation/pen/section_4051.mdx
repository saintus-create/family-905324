# PEN § 4051

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1.5. Joint County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4051

---

## Text

Any two or more counties may form a district for the purpose of establishing and operating a joint county jail to serve such counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1019.)
