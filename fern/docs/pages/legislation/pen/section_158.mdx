# PEN § 158

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 7. Other Offenses Against Public Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 158

---

## Text

Common barratry is the practice of exciting groundless judicial proceedings, and is punishable by imprisonment in the county jail not exceeding six months and by fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 246. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
