# PEN § 30

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. OF PARTIES TO CRIME

**Citation:** PEN § 30

---

## Text

The parties to crimes are classified as:

1. Principals; and,

2. Accessories.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
