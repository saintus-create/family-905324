# PEN § 1001.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2.7. Misdemeanor Diversion

**Citation:** PEN § 1001.3

---

## Text

At no time shall a defendant be required to make an admission of guilt as a prerequisite for placement in a pretrial diversion program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 42, Sec. 2. Effective February 17, 1982.)
