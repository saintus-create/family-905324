# PEN § 3523

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.1. BIOMEDICAL AND BEHAVIORIAL RESEARCH > Chapter: 4. Prisoners’ Rights as Research Subjects

**Citation:** PEN § 3523

---

## Text

The amount of such remuneration shall be comparable to that which is paid to nonprisoner volunteers in similar research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1250.)
