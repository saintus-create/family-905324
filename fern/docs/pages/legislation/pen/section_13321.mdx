# PEN § 13321

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 7. Examinations of Local Records

**Citation:** PEN § 13321

---

## Text

Any person desiring to examine a record relating to himself shall make application to the agency maintaining the record in the form prescribed by that agency which may require the submission of fingerprints.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 849.)
