# PEN § 3519

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2.1. BIOMEDICAL AND BEHAVIORIAL RESEARCH > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PEN § 3519

---

## Text

The department shall evaluate the impact of research on human subjects approved and conducted pursuant to this title, including any adverse reactions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1553, Sec. 19.)
