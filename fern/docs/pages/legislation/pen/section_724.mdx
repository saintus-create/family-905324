# PEN § 724

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 5. Suppression of Riots

**Citation:** PEN § 724

---

## Text

The officer must certify to the Court from which the process issued the names of the persons resisting, and their aiders and abettors, to the end that they may be proceeded against for their contempt of Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
