# PEN § 711

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 3. Security to Keep the Peace

**Citation:** PEN § 711

---

## Text

Upon the conviction of the person informed against of a breach of the peace, the undertaking is broken.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
