# PEN § 1350

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 5. Examination of Witnesses on Commission

**Citation:** PEN § 1350

---

## Text

When a material witness for the defendant resides out of the State, the defendant may apply for an order that the witness be examined on a commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
