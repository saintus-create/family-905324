# PEN § 830.10

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 4.5. Peace Officers

**Citation:** PEN § 830.10

---

## Text

Any uniformed peace officer shall wear a badge, nameplate, or other device which bears clearly on its face the identification number or name of the officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1165, Sec. 38.)
