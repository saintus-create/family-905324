# PEN § 32315

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 5. Large-Capacity Magazine > Article: 1. Rules Governing Large-Capacity Magazines

**Citation:** PEN § 32315

---

## Text

Upon a showing that good cause exists, the Department of Justice may issue permits for the possession, transportation, or sale between a person licensed pursuant to Sections 26700 to 26915, inclusive, and an out-of-state client, of large-capacity magazines.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
