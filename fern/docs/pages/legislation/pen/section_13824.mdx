# PEN § 13824

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. CALIFORNIA COUNCIL ON CRIMINAL JUSTICE > Chapter: 3. Criminal Justice Planning

**Citation:** PEN § 13824

---

## Text

A brief description of all projects eligible for a commitment of council funds shall be made available to the public through a publication of the council having statewide circulation at least 30 days in advance of the meeting at which funds for such project can be committed by vote of the council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1047.)
