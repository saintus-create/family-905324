# PEN § 6227.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9.2. Restitution Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6227.5

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall provide information to sentencing courts to ensure that the judges responsible for sentencing are aware of the existence of the restitution center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 249, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
