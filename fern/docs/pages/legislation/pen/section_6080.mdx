# PEN § 6080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** PEN § 6080

---

## Text

As used in his part, the following terms have the meanings described below:

(a) “Department” refers to the Department of Corrections.

(b) “Director” refers to the Director of Corrections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1944, 3rd [1st] Ex. Sess., Ch. 2.)
