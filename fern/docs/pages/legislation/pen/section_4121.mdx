# PEN § 4121

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 1. County Industrial Farms

**Citation:** PEN § 4121

---

## Text

The cost of establishing and maintaining an industrial farm or industrial road camp formed under this article shall be paid out of the county general fund. Any revenue derived from such farm or camp, including that received from any city for the care of its prisoners on said farm, shall be paid into the county general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 69.)
