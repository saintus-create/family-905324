# PEN § 830.85

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 4.5. Peace Officers

**Citation:** PEN § 830.85

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement officers and United States Customs and Border Protection officers are not California peace officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 116, Sec. 2. (AB 1440) Effective January 1, 2018.)
