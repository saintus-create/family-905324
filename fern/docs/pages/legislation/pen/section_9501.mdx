# PEN § 9501

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9.5. PRIVATELY OWNED AND OPERATED DETENTION FACILITIES

**Citation:** PEN § 9501

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this title, a person shall not operate a private detention facility within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 739, Sec. 2. (AB 32) Effective January 1, 2020.)
