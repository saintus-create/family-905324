# PEN § 6104

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 8. The Medical Facility

**Citation:** PEN § 6104

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall make rules and regulations for the government of the Medical Facility and the management of its affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1491.)
