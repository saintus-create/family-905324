# PEN § 29650

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. Juvenile > Article: 2. Possession of Live Ammunition

**Citation:** PEN § 29650

---

## Text

A minor shall not possess live ammunition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
