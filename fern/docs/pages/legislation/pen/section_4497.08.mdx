# PEN § 4497.08

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4.85. COUNTY CORRECTIONAL FACILITIES CAPITAL EXPENDITURE AND YOUTH FACILITY BOND ACT OF 1988 ALLOCATIONS > Chapter: 2. County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4497.08

---

## Text

No state moneys shall be encumbered in contracts with a county, nor released to a county, for construction or renovation of a local jail facility pursuant to this chapter until the conditions of this chapter have been fulfilled by the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1327, Sec. 5. Effective October 2, 1989.)
