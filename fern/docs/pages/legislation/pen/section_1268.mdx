# PEN § 1268

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. Bail > Article: 1. In What Cases the Defendant May Be Admitted to Bail

**Citation:** PEN § 1268

---

## Text

Admission to bail is the order of a competent Court or magistrate that the defendant be discharged from actual custody upon bail.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
