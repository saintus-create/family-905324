# PEN § 13906

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. LOCAL CRIMINAL JUSTICE PLANNING

**Citation:** PEN § 13906

---

## Text

Planning boards may contract with other public or private entities for the performance of services, may appoint an executive officer and other employees, and may receive and expend funds in order to carry out planning and coordinating responsibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1047.)
