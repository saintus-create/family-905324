# PEN § 1261

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 4. Judgment Upon Appeal

**Citation:** PEN § 1261

---

## Text

When a new trial is ordered it must be directed to be had in the Court of the county from which the appeal was taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
