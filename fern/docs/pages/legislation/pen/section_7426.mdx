# PEN § 7426

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 16. New Prison Construction Bond Act of 1990

**Citation:** PEN § 7426

---

## Text

The moneys in the fund shall be used for the acquisition, construction, renovation, remodeling, and deferred maintenance of state youth and adult correctional facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 5, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 120 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
