# PEN § 28065

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 5. Procedure For a Private Party Firearms Transaction

**Citation:** PEN § 28065

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a dealer who does not sell, transfer, or keep an inventory of handguns is not required to process private party transfers of handguns.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
