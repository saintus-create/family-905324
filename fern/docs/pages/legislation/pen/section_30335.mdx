# PEN § 30335

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. Ammunition > Article: 2. Other Restrictions Relating to Ammunition

**Citation:** PEN § 30335

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall prohibit the possession, importation, sale, attempted sale, or transport of ammunition from which the propellant has been removed and the primer has been permanently deactivated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
