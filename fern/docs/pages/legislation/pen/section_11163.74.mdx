# PEN § 11163.74

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 2. Control of Crimes and Criminals > Article: 2.4. Homeless Death Review Committees

**Citation:** PEN § 11163.74

---

## Text

Information gathered by the homeless death review committee and any recommendations made by the committee shall be used by the county to develop education and prevention strategies that will lead to improved coordination of services for the homeless population.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. (AB 271) Effective January 1, 2024.)
