# PEN § 1563

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. OF SPECIAL PROCEEDINGS OF A CRIMINAL NATURE > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions Respecting Special Proceedings of a Criminal Nature

**Citation:** PEN § 1563

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 1401, in respect to entitling affidavits, are applicable to such proceedings.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
