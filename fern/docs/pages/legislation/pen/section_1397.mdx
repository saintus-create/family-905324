# PEN § 1397

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 9. Proceedings Against Corporations

**Citation:** PEN § 1397

---

## Text

When a fine is imposed upon a corporation on conviction, it may be collected by virtue of the order imposing it in the manner provided for enforcement of money judgments generally.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 497, Sec. 140. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 185 of Ch. 497.)
