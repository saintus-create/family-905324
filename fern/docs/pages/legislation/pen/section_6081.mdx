# PEN § 6081

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 7. Definitions

**Citation:** PEN § 6081

---

## Text

As used in this code, “prison” and “state prison” include the California Institution for Women.
