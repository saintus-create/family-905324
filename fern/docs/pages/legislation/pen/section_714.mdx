# PEN § 714

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. OF THE PREVENTION OF PUBLIC OFFENSES > Chapter: 3. Security to Keep the Peace

**Citation:** PEN § 714

---

## Text

Security to keep the peace, or be of good behavior, cannot be required except as prescribed in this Chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
