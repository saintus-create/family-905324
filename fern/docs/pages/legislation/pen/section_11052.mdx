# PEN § 11052

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 2. Criminal Investigation

**Citation:** PEN § 11052

---

## Text

For the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this chapter, the investigators shall have all the powers conferred by law upon any peace officer of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1385.)
