# PEN § 11141

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 6. Unlawful Furnishing of State Summary Criminal History Information

**Citation:** PEN § 11141

---

## Text

Any employee of the Department of Justice who knowingly furnishes a record or information obtained from a record to a person who is not authorized by law to receive the record or information is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 963.)
