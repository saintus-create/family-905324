# PEN § 11076

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. INVESTIGATION AND CONTROL OF CRIMES AND CRIMINALS > Chapter: 1. Investigation, Identification, and Information Responsibilities of the Department of Justice > Article: 2.5. Criminal Record Dissemination

**Citation:** PEN § 11076

---

## Text

Criminal offender record information shall be disseminated, whether directly or through any intermediary, only to such agencies as are, or may subsequently be, authorized access to such records by statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1437.)
