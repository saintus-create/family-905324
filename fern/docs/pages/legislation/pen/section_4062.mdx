# PEN § 4062

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1.5. Joint County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4062

---

## Text

The board of directors may establish the joint county jail provided for in this chapter and shall provide for the feeding, care, and treatment of prisoners therein, and must conform to such standards for construction, feeding, clothing, bedding and programming as are imposed pursuant to law on county jails.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1019.)
