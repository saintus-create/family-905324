# PEN § 17315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 17315

---

## Text

As used in Articles 2 through 5 of Chapter 1 of Division 10 of Title 4, “vendor” means an ammunition vendor.

(History: Amended November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 63, Sec. 8.4.)
