# PEN § 1154

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 4. The Verdict or Finding

**Citation:** PEN § 1154

---

## Text

The special verdict need not be in any particular form, but is sufficient if it presents intelligibly the facts found by the jury.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 828, Sec. 65.)
