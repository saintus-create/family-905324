# PEN § 19290

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. WEAPONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Handgrenades

**Citation:** PEN § 19290

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 19205 and in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 17700) of Division 2, any metal military practice handgrenade or metal replica handgrenade is a nuisance and is subject to Section 18010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
