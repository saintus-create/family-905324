# PEN § 880

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. ADDITIONAL PROVISIONS REGARDING CRIMINAL PROCEDURE > Chapter: 7. Examination of the Case, and Discharge of the Defendant, or Holding Him to Answer

**Citation:** PEN § 880

---

## Text

Infants who are material witnesses against the defendant may be required to procure sureties for their appearance, as provided in the last section.
