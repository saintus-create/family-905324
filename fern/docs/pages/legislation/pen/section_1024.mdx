# PEN § 1024

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 4. Plea

**Citation:** PEN § 1024

---

## Text

If the defendant refuses to answer the accusatory pleading, by demurrer or plea, a plea of not guilty must be entered.
