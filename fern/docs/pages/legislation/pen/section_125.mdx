# PEN § 125

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 5. Perjury and Subornation of Perjury

**Citation:** PEN § 125

---

## Text

An unqualified statement of that which one does not know to be true is equivalent to a statement of that which one knows to be false.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
