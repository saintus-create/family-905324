# PEN § 279.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON INVOLVING SEXUAL ASSAULT, AND CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC DECENCY AND GOOD MORALS > Chapter: 4. Child Abduction

**Citation:** PEN § 279.1

---

## Text

The offenses enumerated in Sections 278 and 278.5 are continuous in nature, and continue for as long as the minor child is concealed or detained.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 988, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1997.)
