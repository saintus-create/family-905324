# PEN § 30290

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. Ammunition > Article: 1. Flechette Dart Ammunition or Bullet Containing or Carrying an Explosive Agent

**Citation:** PEN § 30290

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 30215 and in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 17700) of Division 2 of Title 2, any ammunition that contains or consists of any flechette dart, or any bullet containing or carrying an explosive agent, is a nuisance and is subject to Section 18010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
