# PEN § 27345

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Gun Show or Event > Article: 2. Gun Show Enforcement and Security Act of 2000

**Citation:** PEN § 27345

---

## Text

Any person who possesses a firearm or ammunition at a gun show or event shall have government-issued photo identification in immediate possession, and shall display it upon request to any security officer or peace officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 736, Sec. 12. (AB 1669) Effective January 1, 2020.)
