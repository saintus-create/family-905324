# PEN § 996

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 2. Setting Aside the Indictment or Information

**Citation:** PEN § 996

---

## Text

If the motion to set aside the indictment or information is not made, the defendant is precluded from afterwards taking the objections mentioned in Section 995.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 138.)
