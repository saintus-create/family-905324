# PEN § 120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 5. Perjury and Subornation of Perjury

**Citation:** PEN § 120

---

## Text

So much of an oath of office as relates to the future performance of official duties is not such an oath as is intended by the two preceding sections.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
