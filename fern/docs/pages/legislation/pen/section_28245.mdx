# PEN § 28245

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 6. Recordkeeping, Background Checks, and Fees Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 3. Submission of Fees and Firearm Purchaser Information to the Department of Justice

**Citation:** PEN § 28245

---

## Text

Whenever the Department of Justice acts pursuant to this article as it pertains to firearms other than handguns, the department’s acts or omissions shall be deemed to be discretionary within the meaning of the Government Claims Act pursuant to Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 759, Sec. 8. (AB 2690) Effective January 1, 2013.)
