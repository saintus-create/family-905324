# PEN § 16000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 16000

---

## Text

This act recodifies the provisions of former Title 2 (commencing with Section 12000) of Part 4, which was entitled “Control of Deadly Weapons.” The act shall be known and may be cited as the “Deadly Weapons Recodification Act of 2010.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
