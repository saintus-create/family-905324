# PEN § 27720

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. Crimes Relating to Sale, Lease, or Transfer of Firearms > Article: 4. Exceptions to Restrictions on Delivery of a Firearm

**Citation:** PEN § 27720

---

## Text

Section 27540 does not apply to the sale, delivery, or transfer of unloaded firearms to a wholesaler if the firearms are being returned to the wholesaler and are intended as merchandise in the wholesaler’s business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
