# PEN § 30355

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 1. Ammunition > Article: 3. Ammunition Vendors

**Citation:** PEN § 30355

---

## Text

Commencing February 1, 2011, the records required by this article shall be maintained on the premises of the vendor for a period of not less than five years from the date of the recorded transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
