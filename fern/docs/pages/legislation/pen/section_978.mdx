# PEN § 978

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6. PLEADINGS AND PROCEEDINGS BEFORE TRIAL > Chapter: 1. Of the Arraignment of the Defendant

**Citation:** PEN § 978

---

## Text

When his personal appearance is necessary, if he is in custody, the Court may direct and the officer in whose custody he is must bring him before it to be arraigned.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
