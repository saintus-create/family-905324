# PEN § 14032

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. THE HERTZBERG-LESLIE WITNESS PROTECTION ACT

**Citation:** PEN § 14032

---

## Text

The administrative costs of the Attorney General for the purposes of administering this title shall be limited to 5 percent of all costs incurred pursuant to this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 507, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
