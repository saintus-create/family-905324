# PEN § 3325

**Hierarchy:** Title: 2. IMPRISONMENT OF FEMALE PRISONERS IN STATE INSTITUTIONS > Chapter: 2. Administration of Institution > Article: 1. Administration of Institution for Women

**Citation:** PEN § 3325

---

## Text

The warden described in this chapter shall, subject to the control of the director, have those powers, perform those duties and exercise those functions, respecting females convicted of felonies, as the wardens now exercise over male prisoners.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1420, Sec. 15.)
