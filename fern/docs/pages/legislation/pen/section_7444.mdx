# PEN § 7444

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 17. Children of Incarcerated Parents

**Citation:** PEN § 7444

## Text

The California Research Bureau shall convene an advisory group to assist in designing and administering the study.

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 965, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
