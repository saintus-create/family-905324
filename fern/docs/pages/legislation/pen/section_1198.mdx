# PEN § 1198

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF JUDGMENT AND EXECUTION > Chapter: 1. The Judgment

**Citation:** PEN § 1198

---

## Text

The bench warrant may be served in any county in the same manner as a warrant of arrest.
