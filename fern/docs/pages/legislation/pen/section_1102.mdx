# PEN § 1102

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF PROCEEDINGS AFTER THE COMMENCEMENT OF THE TRIAL AND BEFORE JUDGMENT > Chapter: 2. The Trial

**Citation:** PEN § 1102

---

## Text

The rules of evidence in civil actions are applicable also to criminal actions, except as otherwise provided in this Code.

(History: Enacted 1872.)
