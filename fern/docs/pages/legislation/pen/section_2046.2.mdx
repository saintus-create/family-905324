# PEN § 2046.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 6. California Men’s Colony

**Citation:** PEN § 2046.2

---

## Text

Any person under the custody of the Director of Corrections may be transferred to the said prison in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 892.)
