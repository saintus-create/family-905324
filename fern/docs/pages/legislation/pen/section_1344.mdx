# PEN § 1344

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. MISCELLANEOUS PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Examination of Witnesses Conditionally

**Citation:** PEN § 1344

---

## Text

The deposition taken must, by the magistrate, be sealed up and transmitted to the Clerk of the Court in which the action is pending or may come for trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
