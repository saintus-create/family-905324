# PEN § 6031.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 5. The Corrections Standards Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 6031.3

---

## Text

The Board of Corrections is authorized to apply for any funds that may be available from the federal government to further the purposes of Sections 6030 to 6031.2, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1789.)
