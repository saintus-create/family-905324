# PEN § 16270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PEN § 16270

---

## Text

As used in this part, “blowgun” means a hollow tube designed and intended to be used as a tube through which a dart is propelled by the force of the breath of the user.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
