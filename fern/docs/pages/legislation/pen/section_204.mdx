# PEN § 204

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. OF CRIMES AGAINST THE PERSON > Chapter: 2. Mayhem

**Citation:** PEN § 204

---

## Text

Mayhem is punishable by imprisonment in the state prison for two, four, or eight years.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1424, Sec. 1.)
