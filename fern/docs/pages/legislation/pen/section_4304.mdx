# PEN § 4304

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 2. County Industrial Farms and Road Camps > Article: 3. Advisory Committees for Adult Detention Facilities

**Citation:** PEN § 4304

---

## Text

The committee shall file a report within 90 days after the thirty-first day of December of the calendar year for which such report is made, copies of which shall be filed with the county board of supervisors, the presiding judge, the sheriff, the Board of Corrections, and the Attorney General.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 571. Effective January 1, 2003.)
