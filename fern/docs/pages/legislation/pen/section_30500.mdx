# PEN § 30500

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 2. Assault Weapons and .50 BMG Rifles > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 30500

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known as the Roberti-Roos Assault Weapons Control Act of 1989 and the .50 Caliber BMG Regulation Act of 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
