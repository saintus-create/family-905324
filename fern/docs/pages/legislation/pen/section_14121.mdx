# PEN § 14121

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. COMMUNITY VIOLENCE PREVENTION AND CONFLICT RESOLUTION

**Citation:** PEN § 14121

---

## Text

The Office of Emergency Services may hire support staff and utilize resources necessary to carry out the purposes of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 471. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
