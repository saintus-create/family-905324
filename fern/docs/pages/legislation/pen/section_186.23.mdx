# PEN § 186.23

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. OF CRIMES AGAINST PUBLIC JUSTICE > Chapter: 11. Street Terrorism Enforcement and Prevention Act

**Citation:** PEN § 186.23

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to employees engaged in concerted activities for their mutual aid and protection, or the activities of labor organizations or their members or agents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1256, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1988.)
