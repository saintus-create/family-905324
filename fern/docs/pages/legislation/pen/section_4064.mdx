# PEN § 4064

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. COUNTY JAILS, FARMS AND CAMPS > Chapter: 1.5. Joint County Jails

**Citation:** PEN § 4064

---

## Text

Convicted persons may be committed to a joint county jail from a county comprising the district the same as if the commitment were to a jail maintained by that county alone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1019.)
