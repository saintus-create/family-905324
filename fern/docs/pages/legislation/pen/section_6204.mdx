# PEN § 6204

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 9. Conservation Centers

**Citation:** PEN § 6204

---

## Text

The Director of Corrections shall make rules and regulations for the government of the conservation centers in the management of their affairs.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1431.)
