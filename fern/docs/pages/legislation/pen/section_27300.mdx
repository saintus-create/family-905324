# PEN § 27300

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 3. Gun Show or Event > Article: 2. Gun Show Enforcement and Security Act of 2000

**Citation:** PEN § 27300

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited as, the Gun Show Enforcement and Security Act of 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
