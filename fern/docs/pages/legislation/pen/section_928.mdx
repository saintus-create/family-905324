# PEN § 928

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Grand Jury > Article: 2. Investigation of County, City, and District Affairs

**Citation:** PEN § 928

---

## Text

Every grand jury may investigate and report upon the needs of all county officers in the county, including the abolition or creation of offices and the equipment for, or the method or system of performing the duties of, the several offices. Such investigation and report shall be conducted selectively each year. The grand jury shall cause a copy of such report to be transmitted to each member of the board of supervisors of the county.
