# PEN § 26160

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. FIREARMS > Chapter: 4. License to Carry A Pistol, Revolver, or Other Firearm Capable of Being Concealed Upon the Person

**Citation:** PEN § 26160

---

## Text

Each licensing authority shall publish and make available a written policy summarizing the provisions of Section 26150 and subdivisions (a) and (b) of Section 26155.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 711, Sec. 6. (SB 1080) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 711.)
