# PEN § 1236

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. APPEALS IN FELONY CASES > Chapter: 1. Appeals, When Allowed and How Taken, and the Effect Thereof

**Citation:** PEN § 1236

---

## Text

The party appealing is known as the appellant, and the adverse party as the respondent, but the title of the action is not changed in consequence of the appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted 1872.)
