# PEN § 11500

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1.5. STATEWIDE PROGRAMS OF EDUCATION, TRAINING, AND RESEARCH FOR LOCAL PUBLIC PROSECUTORS AND PUBLIC DEFENDERS

**Citation:** PEN § 11500

---

## Text

The purpose of this title is to improve the administration of criminal justice by providing funding for statewide programs of education, training, and research for local public prosecutors and public defenders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 116, Sec. 1. Effective March 16, 1982.)
