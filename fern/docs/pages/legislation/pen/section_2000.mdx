# PEN § 2000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 1. California Institution for Men

**Citation:** PEN § 2000

---

## Text

There is and shall continue to be a State prison to be known as the California Institution for Men.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1192.)
