# PEN § 13326

**Hierarchy:** Title: 3. CRIMINAL STATISTICS > Chapter: 2. Criminal Offender Record Information > Article: 7. Examinations of Local Records

**Citation:** PEN § 13326

---

## Text

No person shall require an employee or prospective employee to obtain a copy of a record or notification that a record exists as provided in Section 13323. A violation of this section is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 849.)
