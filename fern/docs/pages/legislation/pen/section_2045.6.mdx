# PEN § 2045.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 1. IMPRISONMENT OF MALE PRISONERS IN STATE PRISONS > Chapter: 1. Establishment of State Prisons > Article: 5. Correctional Training Facility

**Citation:** PEN § 2045.6

---

## Text

The provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 2000) apply to the institution and to the persons confined therein insofar as those provisions may be applicable.
