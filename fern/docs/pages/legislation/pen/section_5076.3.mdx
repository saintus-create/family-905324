# PEN § 5076.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF THE STATE CORRECTIONAL SYSTEM > Chapter: 3. The Board of Parole Hearings

**Citation:** PEN § 5076.3

---

## Text

The executive officer of the Board of Parole Hearings shall have the authority of a head of a department set forth in subdivision (e) of Section 11181 of the Government Code to issue subpoenas as provided in Article 2 (commencing with Section 11180) of Chapter 2 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code. The board shall adopt regulations on the policies and guidelines for the issuance of subpoenas.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 80, Sec. 19. (AB 145) Effective July 16, 2021.)
