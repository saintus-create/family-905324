# PEN § 924

**Hierarchy:** Title: 4. GRAND JURY PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Grand Jury > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PEN § 924

---

## Text

Every grand juror who willfully discloses the fact of an information or indictment having been made for a felony, until the defendant has been arrested, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 501.)
