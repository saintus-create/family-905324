# RTC § 41104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 41104

---

## Text

Within 30 days after disallowing any claim in whole or in part the department shall give notice of its action to the claimant in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 104. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
