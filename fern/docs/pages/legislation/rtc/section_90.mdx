# RTC § 90

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 5. Taxpayer Reporting

**Citation:** RTC § 90

---

## Text

Assessees shall report change in ownership information to the assessor as provided in Article 2.5 (commencing with Section 480) of Chapter 3 of Part 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 242.)
