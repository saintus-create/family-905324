# RTC § 24421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 3. Items Not Deductible

**Citation:** RTC § 24421

---

## Text

In computing “net income” of taxpayers under this part, no deduction shall be allowed for the items specified in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 938.)
