# RTC § 5741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 5741

---

## Text

Foals born to a racehorse mare during the current calendar year shall be exempt from the tax imposed by this part or by any other part of this code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
