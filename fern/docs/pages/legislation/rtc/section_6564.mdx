# RTC § 6564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 5. Determinations > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 6564

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination becomes final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
