# RTC § 6931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 6931

---

## Text

No injunction or writ of mandate or other legal or equitable process shall issue in any suit, action, or proceeding in any court against this State or against any officer of the State to prevent or enjoin the collection under this part of any tax or any amount of tax required to be collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
