# RTC § 24956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 3. Nontaxable Exchanges

**Citation:** RTC § 24956

---

## Text

(a) Section 1044 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to rollover of publicly traded securities gain into specialized small business investment companies, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(b) The provisions of Section 1044 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to rollover of publicly traded securities gain into specialized small business investment companies, shall not apply to any taxable year (or portion thereof) that those provisions (or similar provisions) are not applicable for federal income tax purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 862, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 2001.)
