# RTC § 5900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 14. PROPERTY TAXPAYERS' BILL OF RIGHTS

**Citation:** RTC § 5900

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as “The Morgan Property Taxpayers’ Bill of Rights.”
