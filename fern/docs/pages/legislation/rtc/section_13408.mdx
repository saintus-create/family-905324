# RTC § 13408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 13408

---

## Text

“Nonresident” or “nonresident decedent” means a decedent who was domiciled outside of California at his or her death.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
