# RTC § 17760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 9. Estates, Trusts, Beneficiaries, and Decedents

**Citation:** RTC § 17760

---

## Text

Section 684 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to recognition of gain on certain transfers to certain foreign trusts and estates, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 38. Effective October 7, 2005.)
