# RTC § 5088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 5. Cancellation of Taxes on Exempt Property

**Citation:** RTC § 5088

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, unpaid taxes, penalties, or costs shall not be transferred to the unsecured roll with respect to property that has become subject to a power of sale pursuant to Section 3691.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 316, Sec. 91.)
