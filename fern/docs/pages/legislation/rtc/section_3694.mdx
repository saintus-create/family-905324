# RTC § 3694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3694

---

## Text

A sale under this chapter shall take place only if approved by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 497, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1999.)
