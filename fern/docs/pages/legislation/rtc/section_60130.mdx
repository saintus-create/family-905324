# RTC § 60130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 4.5. International Fuel Tax Agreement

**Citation:** RTC § 60130

---

## Text

Effective on and after January 1, 1996, the provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 9405) of Part 3.5 shall apply to all interstate users.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 84. Effective January 1, 1996.)
