# RTC § 46253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 3. Determinations If No Return Made

**Citation:** RTC § 46253

---

## Text

The amount of the determination, exclusive of penalties, shall bear interest at the modified adjusted rate per month, or fraction thereof, established pursuant to Section 6591.5, from date the amount of the fee, or any portion thereof, became due and payable until the date of payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
