# RTC § 23800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Tax Treatment of S Corporations and Their Shareholders

**Citation:** RTC § 23800

---

## Text

Subchapter S of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the tax treatment of “S corporations” and their shareholders, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 268, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2004.)
