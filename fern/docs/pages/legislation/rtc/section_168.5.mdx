# RTC § 168.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 168.5

---

## Text

Any document required in this division to be acknowledged by the county clerk at no charge may be acknowledged by a notary public or other county official pursuant to Section 1181 of the Civil Code, at no charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 941, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2000.)
