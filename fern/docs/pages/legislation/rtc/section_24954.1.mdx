# RTC § 24954.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 3. Nontaxable Exchanges

**Citation:** RTC § 24954.1

---

## Text

Section 1042(g) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to application of section to sales of stock in agricultural refiners and processors to eligible farm cooperatives, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 322, Sec. 101. Effective August 20, 1998.)
