# RTC § 60443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 3. Lien of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 60443

---

## Text

The lien arising under Section 60441 shall not be removed until the tax, interest, and penalties are paid or the qualified motor vehicle or other property subject to the lien is sold in payment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
