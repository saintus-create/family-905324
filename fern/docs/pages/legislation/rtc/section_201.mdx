# RTC § 201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 1. Taxable and Exempt Property

**Citation:** RTC § 201

---

## Text

All property in this State, not exempt under the laws of the United States or of this State, is subject to taxation under this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
