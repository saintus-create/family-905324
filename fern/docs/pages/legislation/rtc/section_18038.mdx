# RTC § 18038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 13. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property

**Citation:** RTC § 18038

---

## Text

Section 1040 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to transfer of certain real property, does not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 185, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2004.)
