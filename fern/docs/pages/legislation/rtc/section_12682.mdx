# RTC § 12682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 5. Payment and Collection > Article: 3. Suits for Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 12682

---

## Text

Payment of the amount of the judgment recovered in the action shall be made to the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12683 by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
