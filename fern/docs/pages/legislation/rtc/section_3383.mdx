# RTC § 3383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 1. Publication of Delinquent List and Notice of Sale > Article: 1.8. Alternative Method of Publication

**Citation:** RTC § 3383

---

## Text

The contracts for the publications shall include the publication of the proper portion of the published list and all other items relating to that portion of the published list required by law to be published.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 894.)
