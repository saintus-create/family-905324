# RTC § 7070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 2. Tax Amnesty Program

**Citation:** RTC § 7070

---

## Text

The board shall develop and administer a tax amnesty program for taxpayers subject to Part 1 (commencing with Section 6001), as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 226, Sec. 6. Effective August 16, 2004.)
