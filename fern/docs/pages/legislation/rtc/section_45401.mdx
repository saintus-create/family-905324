# RTC § 45401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 4. Collection of Fee > Article: 1. Suit for Fee

**Citation:** RTC § 45401

---

## Text

The board may bring any legal action necessary to collect any deficiency in the fee required to be paid, and, upon the board’s request, the Attorney General shall bring the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
