# RTC § 17141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17141

---

## Text

Gross income does not include income derived from an obligation of a Community Energy Authority established under the provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 52000) of Division 1 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 140. Effective July 28, 1983. Operative January 1, 1985, pursuant to Sec. 220 of Ch. 498.)
