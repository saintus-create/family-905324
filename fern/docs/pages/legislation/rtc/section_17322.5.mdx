# RTC § 17322.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 4. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 17322.5

---

## Text

Section 381(c)(20) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to carryforward of disallowed business interest, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 47. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
