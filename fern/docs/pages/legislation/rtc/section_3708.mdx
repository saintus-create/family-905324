# RTC § 3708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3708

---

## Text

On receiving the full purchase price at any sale under this chapter, the tax collector shall, without charge, execute a deed to the purchaser.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 497, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1999.)
