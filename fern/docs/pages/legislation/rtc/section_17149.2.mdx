# RTC § 17149.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17149.2

---

## Text

Section 132(g)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to qualified moving expense reimbursement suspension for taxable years 2018 to 2025, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 23. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
