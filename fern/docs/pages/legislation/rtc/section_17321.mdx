# RTC § 17321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 4. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 17321

---

## Text

Subchapter C of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to corporate distributions and adjustments, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 873, Sec. 16. Effective October 6, 1993.)
