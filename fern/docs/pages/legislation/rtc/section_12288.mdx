# RTC § 12288

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 3.5. Retaliatory Taxes, Licenses and Fees

**Citation:** RTC § 12288

---

## Text

Deficiency assessments for retaliatory taxes may be made in the same manner as is provided by this part for other deficiency assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 44.)
