# RTC § 34021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14.5. Cannabis Tax > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 34021

---

## Text

The taxes imposed by this part shall be in addition to any other tax imposed by a city, county, or city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 64, Sec. 7.1.)
