# RTC § 12982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 7. Cancellations and Refunds > Article: 2. Refund or Credit for Collected Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 12982

---

## Text

If the claim for refund or credit is presented to the commissioner he shall forthwith transmit it to the board, together with a statement of any information he may have concerning the subject of the claim.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
