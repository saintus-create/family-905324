# RTC § 1255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 6. Assessor’s Office Equipment

**Citation:** RTC § 1255

---

## Text

The maps shall show the private lands owned or claimed in the county so as to provide a legal description of the lands.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
