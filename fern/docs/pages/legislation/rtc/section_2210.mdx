# RTC § 2210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2210

---

## Text

“Law enacted after January 1, 1973,” means any statute enacted by the Legislature after January 1, 1973.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 486.)
