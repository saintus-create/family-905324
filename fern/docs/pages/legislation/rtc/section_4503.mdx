# RTC § 4503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7.5. TAX CERTIFICATES > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 4503

---

## Text

“Delinquency date” means the date on which a delinquency penalty attaches to taxes pursuant to Section 75.52, 2617, 2618, 2704, 2705, 2759, 2760, 2761, or 2762, as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 189, Sec. 7. Effective July 24, 1995.)
