# RTC § 40051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 1. Due and Payable Dates

**Citation:** RTC § 40051

---

## Text

The surcharges imposed by this part and the amounts thereof required to be collected by electric utilities are due quarterly on or before the last day of the month next succeeding each calendar quarter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
