# RTC § 3698.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3698.8

---

## Text

The tax collector, upon the recommendation of county counsel, may remove a parcel from the tax sale if it is deemed the removal is in the best interest of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 352, Sec. 5. (SB 948) Effective January 1, 2012.)
