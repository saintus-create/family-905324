# RTC § 43507.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 43507.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the board’s authority to examine the books and records of a taxpayer under Section 43502.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 105, Sec. 34. (AB 2009) Effective January 1, 2015.)
