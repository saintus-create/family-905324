# RTC § 7336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7336

---

## Text

“Removal” means any physical transfer of motor vehicle fuel, and any use of motor vehicle fuel other than as a material in the production of motor vehicle fuel. However, motor vehicle fuel is not removed when it evaporates or is otherwise lost or destroyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
