# RTC § 3695.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3695.3

---

## Text

As used in Section 3695, “assessments” does not include assessments which were, at the time of the declaration of default or sale to the taxing agency, not included in the amount required to redeem the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 988, Sec. 54. Effective September 11, 1984.)
