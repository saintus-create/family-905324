# RTC § 1649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 2. Duties of Auditor

**Citation:** RTC § 1649

---

## Text

As soon as the valuation statements are prepared, the auditor shall transmit one to the controller and one to the board.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
