# RTC § 2812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 3. Collection of Part of an Assessment > Article: 2. Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 2812

---

## Text

The amount due on the remainder of the assessment shall be the difference between the amount due on the whole assessment and the amount paid to satisfy and remove the lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1293.)
