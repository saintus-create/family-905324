# RTC § 38804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 9. Violations and Res Judicata

**Citation:** RTC § 38804

---

## Text

Any prosecution for violation of any of the penal provisions of this part shall be instituted within three years after the commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
