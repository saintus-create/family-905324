# RTC § 38571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 6. Collection of Taxes > Article: 8. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 38571

---

## Text

The board shall report to the Controller the amount of collections under this part, and the Controller shall keep a record thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 38561 by Stats. 1985, Ch. 186, Sec. 17.)
