# RTC § 45503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 4. Collection of Fee > Article: 3. Warrant for Collection

**Citation:** RTC § 45503

---

## Text

The fees, commissions, and expenses are the obligation of the person required to pay any amount under this part and may be collected from him or her by virtue of the warrant or in any other manner provided in this part for the collection of the fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
