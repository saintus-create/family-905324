# RTC § 9183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2.5. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 9183

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall prosecute any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 9181, and the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to service of summons, pleadings, proofs, trials and appeals shall apply to the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1999.)
