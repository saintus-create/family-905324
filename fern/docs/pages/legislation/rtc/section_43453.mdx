# RTC § 43453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 43453

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of the overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
