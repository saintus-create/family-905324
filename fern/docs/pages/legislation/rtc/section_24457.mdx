# RTC § 24457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 24457

---

## Text

Section 312(k)(3)(B)(ii) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to special rule for real estate investment trusts, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 104. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
