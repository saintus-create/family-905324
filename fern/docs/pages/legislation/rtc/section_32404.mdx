# RTC § 32404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 8. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 32404

---

## Text

Within 30 days after disallowing any claim in whole or in part, the board shall serve written notice of its action on the claimant, such service to be made as provided by Section 32271.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
