# RTC § 17256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17256

---

## Text

Section 179A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to deduction for clean-fuel vehicles and certain refueling property, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 32. Effective October 7, 2005.)
