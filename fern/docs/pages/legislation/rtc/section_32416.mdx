# RTC § 32416

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 8. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 32416

---

## Text

If judgment is rendered for the plaintiff, the amount of the judgment shall first be credited on any taxes due from the plaintiff, and the balance shall be refunded to the plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
