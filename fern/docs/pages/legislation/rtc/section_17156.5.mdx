# RTC § 17156.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17156.5

---

## Text

Gross income does not include any amount received as reparation payments paid by the Canadian government for the purpose of redressing the injustice done to persons of Japanese ancestry who were interned in Canada during World War II.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 471, Sec. 2. Effective September 23, 1999.)
