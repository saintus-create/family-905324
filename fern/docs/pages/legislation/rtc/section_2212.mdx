# RTC § 2212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2212

---

## Text

“Percentage change in the cost of living” means the percentage change from April 1 of the prior year to April 1 of the current year in the California Consumer Price Index for all items, as determined by the California Department of Industrial Relations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 358.)
