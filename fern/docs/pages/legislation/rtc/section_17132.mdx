# RTC § 17132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17132

---

## Text

Section 114 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to extraterritorial income, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 34, Sec. 8. Effective May 8, 2002. See identical section added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 35.)
