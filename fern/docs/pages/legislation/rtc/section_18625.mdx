# RTC § 18625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 2. Returns > Article: 3. General Provisions Applicable to All Persons

**Citation:** RTC § 18625

---

## Text

An income tax return preparer shall furnish a copy of any state tax return to a taxpayer and retain information in accordance with Section 6107 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
