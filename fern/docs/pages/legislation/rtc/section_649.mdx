# RTC § 649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 7. Information to Other Taxing Agencies

**Citation:** RTC § 649

---

## Text

The assessor may charge other taxing agencies the actual cost for each copy of the secured roll or description from the unsecured roll furnished them.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 1008.)
