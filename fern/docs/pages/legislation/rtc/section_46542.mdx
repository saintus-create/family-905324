# RTC § 46542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 46542

---

## Text

In any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 46541, the court may, with the consent of the Attorney General, order a change in the place of trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 45. Effective January 1, 1999.)
