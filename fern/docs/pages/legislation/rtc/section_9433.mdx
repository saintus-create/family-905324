# RTC § 9433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3.5. FUEL TAX AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 2. The International Fuel Tax Agreement > Article: 6. Distribution of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 9433

---

## Text

The fees paid for licenses and decals issued under Section 9420 shall be deposited in a reserve account in the State Treasury and shall, upon appropriation by the Legislature, be used for administration of the IFTA program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1996.)
