# RTC § 24726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 6. Adjustments Required by Changes in Method

**Citation:** RTC § 24726

---

## Text

Section 483 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to interest on certain deferred payments, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 77. Effective October 6, 1993.)
