# RTC § 646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 7. Information to Other Taxing Agencies

**Citation:** RTC § 646

---

## Text

The records of the assessor are at all times, during office hours, open to the inspection of any person charged with the duty of assessing property in the county for any taxing agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
