# RTC § 9411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3.5. FUEL TAX AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 2. The International Fuel Tax Agreement > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 9411

---

## Text

“IFTA” means the International Fuel Tax Agreement. The International Fuel Tax Agreement consists of the Articles of Agreement, the Procedures Manual, the Audit Manual, as amended from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 519, Sec. 6. Effective October 4, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 25 of Ch. 519.)
