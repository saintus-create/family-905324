# RTC § 43011.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 43011.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 43011, for purposes of the fees administered under Sections 43056 and 43057, “director” means the State Director of Health Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 941, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000.)
