# RTC § 41091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 41091

---

## Text

Any notice required by this article shall be served in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 499, Sec. 76. (SB 1528) Effective January 1, 2025.)
