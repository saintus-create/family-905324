# RTC § 43012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 43012

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “taxpayer” means any person liable for the payment of a fee or tax administered pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 62, Sec. 8. (SB 166) Effective July 6, 2026.)
