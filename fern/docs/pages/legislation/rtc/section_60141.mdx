# RTC § 60141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. License for Exempt Bus Operator

**Citation:** RTC § 60141

---

## Text

Every person before becoming an exempt bus operator, shall apply for a license authorizing the person to engage in business as an exempt bus operator. A license shall be issued only to a person who is an exempt bus operator as defined in Section 60040. It is unlawful for any person to be an exempt bus operator without first securing a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
