# RTC § 7284.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 1.8. Keep Groceries Affordable Act of 2018

**Citation:** RTC § 7284.16

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on January 1, 2031, and shall be repealed as of that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 61, Sec. 1. (AB 1838) Effective June 28, 2018. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 1.8, commencing with Section 7284.8.)
