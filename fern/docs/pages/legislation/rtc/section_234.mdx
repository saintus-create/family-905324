# RTC § 234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 1. Taxable and Exempt Property

**Citation:** RTC § 234

---

## Text

Seed potatoes of a grower, which are personal property, held on the lien date for subsequent planting in field form and planted during the assessment year by the grower shall be exempt from taxation. This section does not apply to plant nurseries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 232 (as added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 14) by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 398.)
