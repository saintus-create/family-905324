# RTC § 17142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17142

---

## Text

Sections 111(b) and 111(c) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to credits and treatment of credit carryovers, shall be applicable with respect to credits allowable under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1465, Sec. 5. Effective September 28, 1988. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1988, by Sec. 77 of Ch. 1465.)
