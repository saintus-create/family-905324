# RTC § 18403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. General Application

**Citation:** RTC § 18403

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, any return, declaration, report, statement, or other document required to be made or filed under Part 10 (commencing with Section 17001) or Part 11 (commencing with Section 23001) shall be deemed to have been required to be made or filed under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
