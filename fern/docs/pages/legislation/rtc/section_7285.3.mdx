# RTC § 7285.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 2. Counties Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7285.3

---

## Text

The combined rate of all taxes imposed in any county pursuant to this chapter and pursuant to Part 1.6 (commencing with Section 7251) shall not exceed the rate specified in Section 7251.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 76, Sec. 184. (AB 383) Effective January 1, 2014.)
