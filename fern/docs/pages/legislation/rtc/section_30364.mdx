# RTC § 30364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 30364

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of the overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
