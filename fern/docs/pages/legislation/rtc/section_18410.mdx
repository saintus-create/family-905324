# RTC § 18410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. General Application

**Citation:** RTC § 18410

---

## Text

For purposes of Part 10 (commencing with Section 17001), Part 11 (commencing with Section 23001), and this part, a legal holiday includes a federal legal holiday recognized by the Internal Revenue Service under Section 7503 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 313, Sec. 1. (AB 318) Effective January 1, 2013.)
