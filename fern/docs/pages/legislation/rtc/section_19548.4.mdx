# RTC § 19548.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 7. Administration of Tax > Article: 2. Disclosure of Information

**Citation:** RTC § 19548.4

---

## Text

The Franchise Tax Board shall annually furnish the jury commissioner of each county with a list of resident state tax filers, as defined in Section 197 of the Code of Civil Procedure, for the purpose of expanding jury pools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 230, Sec. 3. (SB 592) Effective January 1, 2021.)
