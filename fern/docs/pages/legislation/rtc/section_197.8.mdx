# RTC § 197.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Earthquake and Fire Disaster Relief

**Citation:** RTC § 197.8

---

## Text

The allocation of funds to, and the repayment of funds by, counties made pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to review and audit by the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Effective November 16, 1987.)
