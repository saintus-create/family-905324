# RTC § 60183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 6. Revocation of License

**Citation:** RTC § 60183

---

## Text

The board may cancel any license issued under this part immediately upon surrender thereof, but before revoking a license, the board shall allow the person an opportunity to show cause as provided in Section 60182.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
