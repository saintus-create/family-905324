# RTC § 2827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 3. Collection of Part of an Assessment > Article: 3. Applications and Computations for Separate Assessments

**Citation:** RTC § 2827

---

## Text

The amount due on the remaining parcel shall be the difference between the amount due on the whole assessment and the amount due on the parcel separately assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1293.)
