# RTC § 13105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 8. Taxpayers’ Suits

**Citation:** RTC § 13105

---

## Text

Failure to bring a suit or an action within the time specified constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of an alleged overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
