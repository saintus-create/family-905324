# RTC § 30363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 30363

---

## Text

Every claim for refund or credit shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which it is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
