# RTC § 40003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

## Text

“Surcharge” means a tax levied by this state.
