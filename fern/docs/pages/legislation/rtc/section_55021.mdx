# RTC § 55021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 2. Fee

**Citation:** RTC § 55021

---

## Text

Every person who owes a fee which is subject to this part shall register with the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
