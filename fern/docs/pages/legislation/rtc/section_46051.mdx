# RTC § 46051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 2. Oil Spill Prevention and Administration Fee and Oil Spill Response Fee > Article: 1. Imposition of Fee

**Citation:** RTC § 46051

---

## Text

The fee imposed pursuant to Section 8670.40 of the Government Code shall be administered and collected by the board in accordance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
