# RTC § 50158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 7. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 50158

---

## Text

The money in the fund shall, upon order of the Controller, be drawn therefrom for refunds under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
