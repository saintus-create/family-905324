# RTC § 41008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

## Text

As used in this part, “month” shall mean a calendar month.
