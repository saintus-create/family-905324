# RTC § 38803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 9. Violations and Res Judicata

**Citation:** RTC § 38803

---

## Text

Any violation of this part by any person is a misdemeanor. Each offense shall be punished by a fine of not less than one thousand dollars ($1,000) and not more than five thousand dollars ($5,000), or imprisonment not exceeding one year in the county jail, or both the fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 498, Sec. 41.)
