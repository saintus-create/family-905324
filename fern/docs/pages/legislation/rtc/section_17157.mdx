# RTC § 17157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17157

---

## Text

Gross income shall not include any amount received in any taxable year by a claimant pursuant to Section 4904 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 630, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
