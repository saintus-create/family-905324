# RTC § 24424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 3. Items Not Deductible

**Citation:** RTC § 24424

---

## Text

Section 264 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to certain amounts paid in connection with insurance contracts, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 35, Sec. 52. Effective May 8, 2002.)
