# RTC § 17004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17004

---

## Text

“Taxpayer” includes any individual, fiduciary, estate, or trust subject to any tax imposed by this part or any partnership.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 952, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
