# RTC § 17158.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17158.4

---

## Text

Section 343 of the Protecting Americans from Tax Hikes Act of 2015 (Public Law 114-113), relating to exclusion from gross income of certain coal power grants to non-corporate taxpayers, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 25. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
