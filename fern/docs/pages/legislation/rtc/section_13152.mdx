# RTC § 13152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 9. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 13152

---

## Text

The money in the Insurance Tax Fund shall, upon order of the Controller, be drawn therefrom for refunds under this part or be transferred to the General Fund of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
