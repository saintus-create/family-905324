# RTC § 7330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7330

---

## Text

“Pipeline” means a fuel distribution system that moves motor vehicle fuel, in bulk, through a pipe, from a refinery to a terminal, from a terminal to another terminal, from a vessel to a terminal, or from a refinery or terminal to a vessel.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 14. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
