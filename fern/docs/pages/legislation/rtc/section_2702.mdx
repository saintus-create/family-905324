# RTC § 2702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 2.1. Collection in Equal Installments

**Citation:** RTC § 2702

---

## Text

The second half of taxes on real and personal property on the secured roll is due February 1st.
