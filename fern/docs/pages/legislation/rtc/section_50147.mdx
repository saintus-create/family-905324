# RTC § 50147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 50147

---

## Text

A person’s failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
