# RTC § 45305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 3. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 45305

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination shall become final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
