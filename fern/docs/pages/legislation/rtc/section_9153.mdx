# RTC § 9153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 9153

---

## Text

The claim shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which it is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 38.)
