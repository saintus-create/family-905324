# RTC § 43057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. The Hazardous Substances Tax > Article: 1. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 43057

---

## Text

The fee imposed pursuant to Section 105310 of the Health and Safety Code shall be administered and collected by the board in accordance with this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 417. Effective September 29, 1996.)
