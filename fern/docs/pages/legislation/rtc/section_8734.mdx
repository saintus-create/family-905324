# RTC § 8734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Collection of Tax by Vendor > Article: 1. Collection of Tax by Vendor

**Citation:** RTC § 8734

---

## Text

The board may require vendors to submit at such times as it shall designate copies of the receipts given pursuant to Section 8732.
