# RTC § 40025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 2. The Surcharge > Article: 1. Imposition of Surcharge

**Citation:** RTC § 40025

---

## Text

The rate fixed by the board pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 40016 shall apply with respect to billing periods beginning on or after July 1, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1067, Sec. 7. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1067.)
