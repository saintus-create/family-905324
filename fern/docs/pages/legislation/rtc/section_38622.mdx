# RTC § 38622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 38622

---

## Text

The action shall be tried in the County of Sacramento unless the court with the consent of the Attorney General orders a change of place of trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
