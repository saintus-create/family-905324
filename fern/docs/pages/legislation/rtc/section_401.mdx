# RTC § 401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1. General Requirements

**Citation:** RTC § 401

---

## Text

Every assessor shall assess all property subject to general property taxation at its full value.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1207.)
