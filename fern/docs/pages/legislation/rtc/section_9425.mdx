# RTC § 9425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3.5. FUEL TAX AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 2. The International Fuel Tax Agreement > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 9425

---

## Text

The exemptions in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 8651) of Part 3 do not apply to IFTA-required returns. However, the exempt use shall be refunded under the refund provisions in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 9151) of Part 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1996.)
