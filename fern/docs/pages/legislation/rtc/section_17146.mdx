# RTC § 17146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17146

---

## Text

The compensation of employees of a foreign country shall be determined in accordance with Section 893 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 26. Effective July 28, 1983.)
