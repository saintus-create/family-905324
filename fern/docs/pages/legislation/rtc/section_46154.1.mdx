# RTC § 46154.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 1. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 46154.1

---

## Text

If the information return pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 46151 is not filed within the time prescribed, a penalty of five hundred dollars ($500) shall be assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 923, Sec. 54.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
