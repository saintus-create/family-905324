# RTC § 24612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 12. Deferred Compensation, Etc. > Article: 2. Exceptions to Part I of Subchapter D of the Internal Revenue Code, Relating to Pension, Profit-Sharing, Stock Bonus Plans, etc.

**Citation:** RTC § 24612

---

## Text

Sections 413(b)(6) and 413(c)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to liability for funding tax, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 698, Sec. 22. Effective September 15, 1992.)
