# RTC § 17151.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17151.1

---

## Text

For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2026, Section 128 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to employer contributions to 530A accounts, added by Section 70204(b)(1) of Public Law 119-21, shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 85, Sec. 4. (SB 180) Effective July 13, 2026.)
