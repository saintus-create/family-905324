# RTC § 17509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 5. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** RTC § 17509

---

## Text

Sections 413(b)(6) and 413(c)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to liability for funding tax, do not apply.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 185, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2004.)
