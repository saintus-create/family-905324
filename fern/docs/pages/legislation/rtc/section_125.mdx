# RTC § 125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 125

---

## Text

“Current roll” means the roll containing the property on which current taxes are a lien.
