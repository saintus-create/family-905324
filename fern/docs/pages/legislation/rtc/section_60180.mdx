# RTC § 60180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 6. Revocation of License

**Citation:** RTC § 60180

---

## Text

The board may revoke the license of any person who refuses or neglects to comply with any provision of this part or any rule or regulation of the board prescribed and adopted under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
