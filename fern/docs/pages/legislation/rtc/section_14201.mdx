# RTC § 14201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 3. Hearings

**Citation:** RTC § 14201

---

## Text

The Controller may conduct a hearing at any time or place for the purpose of determining whether a tax is due under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
