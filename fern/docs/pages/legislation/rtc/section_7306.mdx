# RTC § 7306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7306

---

## Text

“Aviation gasoline” means all special grades of gasoline that are suitable for use in aviation reciprocating engines.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
