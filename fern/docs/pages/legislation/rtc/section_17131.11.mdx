# RTC § 17131.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17131.11

---

## Text

Section 4 of the Federal Disaster Tax Relief Act of 2023 (Public Law 118-148), relating to East Palestine disaster relief payments, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 18. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
