# RTC § 24689

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 4. Year of Deduction

**Citation:** RTC § 24689

---

## Text

Section 468 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to special rules for mining and solid waste reclamation and closing costs, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 71. Effective October 6, 1993.)
