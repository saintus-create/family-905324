# RTC § 41010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 41010

---

## Text

“Access line in this state” means a telephone line, as defined in Section 233 of the Public Utilities Code, associated with a billing address located in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 43. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
