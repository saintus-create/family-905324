# RTC § 4835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 2. Corrections > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4835

---

## Text

If the correction will decrease the amount of unpaid taxes, the consent of the board of supervisors is necessary to the correction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 411, Sec. 45. Effective July 11, 1980. Operative January 1, 1981, by Sec. 51 of Ch. 411.)
