# RTC § 18031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 13. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property

**Citation:** RTC § 18031

---

## Text

Subchapter O of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to gain or loss on disposition of property, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 873, Sec. 27. Effective October 6, 1993.)
