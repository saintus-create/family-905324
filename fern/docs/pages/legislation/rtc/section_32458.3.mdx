# RTC § 32458.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 32458.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the board’s authority to examine the books and records of a taxpayer under Section 32453.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 105, Sec. 19. (AB 2009) Effective January 1, 2015.)
