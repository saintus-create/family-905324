# RTC § 46025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46025

---

## Text

“Ship,” “shipment,” or “shipped” means any physical transfer of renewable fuel from a renewable fuel production facility. However, renewable fuel is not shipped when it evaporates or is otherwise lost or destroyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 115, Sec. 91. (AB 148) Effective July 22, 2021.)
