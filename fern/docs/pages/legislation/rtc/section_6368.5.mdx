# RTC § 6368.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 1. General Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 6368.5

---

## Text

There are exempted from the taxes imposed by this part the gross receipts from the sale of and the storage, use, or other consumption in this State of rail freight cars for use in interstate or foreign commerce.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 749.)
