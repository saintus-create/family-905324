# RTC § 20644.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 20644.5

---

## Text

No interest shall be allowed on any assistance or postponement payment made to a claimant pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 43.)
