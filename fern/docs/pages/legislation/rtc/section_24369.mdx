# RTC § 24369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24369

---

## Text

Section 175 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to soil and water conservation expenditures, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 49. Effective October 6, 1993.)
