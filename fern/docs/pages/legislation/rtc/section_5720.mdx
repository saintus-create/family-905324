# RTC § 5720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5720

---

## Text

“Stud fee” is the sum of money charged by a stallion owner for the mating of his stallion to a broodmare.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1759.)
