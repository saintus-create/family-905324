# RTC § 423.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.5. Valuation of Open-Space Land Subject to an Enforceable Restriction

**Citation:** RTC § 423.9

---

## Text

Land which is zoned as timberland production pursuant to Chapter 6.7 (commencing with Section 51100) of Part 1 of Division 1 of Title 5 of the Government Code and which is not under an open-space contract pursuant to Section 51240 of the Government Code shall be valued pursuant to Section 435.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 35.)
