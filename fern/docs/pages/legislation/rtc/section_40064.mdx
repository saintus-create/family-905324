# RTC § 40064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 2. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 40064

---

## Text

The person required to file the return shall deliver the return together with a remittance of the amount of the surcharge payable to the office of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
