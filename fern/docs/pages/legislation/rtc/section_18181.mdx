# RTC § 18181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 14. General Rules for Determining Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 18181

---

## Text

Part VI of Subchapter P of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to treatment of certain passive foreign investment companies, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 40.5. Effective October 7, 2005.)
