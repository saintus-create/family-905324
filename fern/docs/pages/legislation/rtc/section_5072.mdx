# RTC § 5072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 4. Cancellation of Tax Liens on Any Sixteenth or Thirty-sixth Section or Legal Subdivision Thereof

**Citation:** RTC § 5072

---

## Text

A certificate from the State Lands Commission certifying that any sixteenth or thirty-sixth section is to be used as the base for lieu selections is authority for the action of the board of supervisors in ordering the cancellation of liens for taxes on such lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 69.)
