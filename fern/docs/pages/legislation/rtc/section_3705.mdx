# RTC § 3705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3705

---

## Text

Any city or the State or any taxing agency or revenue district may bid on property.
