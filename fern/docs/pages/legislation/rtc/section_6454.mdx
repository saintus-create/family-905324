# RTC § 6454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 5. Determinations > Article: 1. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 6454

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 6479.3 and 6479.31, a person required to file the return shall deliver the return together with a remittance of the amount of the tax due to the office of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 865, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
