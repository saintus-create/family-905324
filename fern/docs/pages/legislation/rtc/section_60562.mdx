# RTC § 60562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Overpayment and Refunds > Article: 4. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 60562

---

## Text

In any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 60561, the court may, with the consent of the Attorney General, order a change in the place of trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 1999.)
