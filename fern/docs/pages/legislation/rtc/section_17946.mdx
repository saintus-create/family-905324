# RTC § 17946

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 10.6. Tax and Fees on Limited Liability Companies

**Citation:** RTC § 17946

---

## Text

A limited liability company shall not be subject to the taxes and fees imposed by this chapter if the limited liability company did no business in this state during the taxable year and the taxable year was 15 days or less.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 952, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1997.)
