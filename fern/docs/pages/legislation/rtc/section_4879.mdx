# RTC § 4879

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 2. Corrections > Article: 2. Errors on the Board Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 4879

---

## Text

The board shall transmit a statement of the correction of the assessment to the auditor of the county or city in which the property is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
