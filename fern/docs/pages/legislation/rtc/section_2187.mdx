# RTC § 2187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 2. Effect of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 2187

---

## Text

Every tax, penalty, or interest, including redemption penalty or interest, on real property is a lien against the property assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 206, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
