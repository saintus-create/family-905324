# RTC § 19295.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 8.1. Collection of Recaptured California Small Business and Nonprofit COVID-19 Supplemental Paid Sick Leave Relief Grants

**Citation:** RTC § 19295.2

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until December 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 736, Sec. 5. (AB 152) Effective September 29, 2022. Repealed as of December 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 8.1, commencing with Section 19295.1.)
