# RTC § 5086.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 5. Cancellation of Taxes on Exempt Property

**Citation:** RTC § 5086.1

---

## Text

The auditor shall cancel taxes on the date of apportionment provided in the notice required by Section 5082.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 650, Sec. 3.)
