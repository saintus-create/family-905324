# RTC § 1361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 7. Responsibility of Assessor

**Citation:** RTC § 1361

---

## Text

The assessor and his sureties are liable on his official bond for all taxes on property which is unassessed through his wilful failure or neglect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
