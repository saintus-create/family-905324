# RTC § 20639.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 3.3. Senior Citizens Manufactured Home Property Tax Postponement Law

**Citation:** RTC § 20639.7

---

## Text

The Controller shall prescribe the manner in which a claimant eligible under this chapter, who for any reason is incapacitated, may appoint his or her spouse or authorized agent, or have any such person appointed for the claimant, for all purposes of postponement of property taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 896, Sec. 15. (SB 1130) Effective January 1, 2019. Operative July 1, 2019, pursuant to 20639.13.)
