# RTC § 16752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 3. Payment of Tax > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 16752

---

## Text

The tax becomes delinquent from and after the last day allowed for filing a return for the generation skipping transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
