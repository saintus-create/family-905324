# RTC § 13556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 5. Payment of Tax > Article: 3. Delinquent Tax Bond

**Citation:** RTC § 13556

---

## Text

The bond shall be filed in the office of the clerk of the court, and a certified copy shall be immediately transmitted to the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
