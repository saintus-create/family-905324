# RTC § 16761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 3. Payment of Tax > Article: 2. Interest and Penalties

**Citation:** RTC § 16761

---

## Text

Every payment on the tax imposed by this part is applied, first, to any interest due on the tax, and then, if there is any balance, to the tax itself.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
