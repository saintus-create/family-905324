# RTC § 42111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 21.1. Local Prepaid Mobile Telephony Services Collection Act

**Citation:** RTC § 42111

---

## Text

This part shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2031, and as of that date is repealed, unless a later enacted statute, that is enacted before January 1, 2031, deletes or extends that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 553, Sec. 1. (AB 330) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Part 21.1, commencing with Section 42100.)
