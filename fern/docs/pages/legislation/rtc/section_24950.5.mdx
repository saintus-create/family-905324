# RTC § 24950.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 3. Nontaxable Exchanges

**Citation:** RTC § 24950.5

---

## Text

The amendments made by Section 844 of the Pension Protection Act of 2006 (Public Law 109-280) to Section 1035 of the Internal Revenue Code shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 79. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
