# RTC § 12004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 12004

---

## Text

“Marine insurer” means an insurer transacting ocean marine insurance.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 113.)
