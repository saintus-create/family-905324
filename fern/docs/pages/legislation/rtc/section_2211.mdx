# RTC § 2211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2211

---

## Text

“Local agency” means any city, county, special district, authority or other political subdivision of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 242, Sec. 8. Effective July 21, 1981.)
