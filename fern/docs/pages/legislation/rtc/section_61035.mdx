# RTC § 61035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 32. Individual Shared Responsibility Penalty

**Citation:** RTC § 61035

---

## Text

Beginning July 1, 2023, and on July 1 annually thereafter, moneys collected from the Individual Shared Responsibility Penalty shall be deposited into the Health Care Affordability Reserve Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 61. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
