# RTC § 11251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 2. Assessments > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 11251

---

## Text

Private railroad cars operated upon railroads into, out of, or through this state shall be assessed and taxed by the board as prescribed in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 220, Sec. 3. Effective July 31, 1995.)
