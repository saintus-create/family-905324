# RTC § 8511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 12. Metropolitan Transportation Commission

**Citation:** RTC § 8511

---

## Text

The commission’s regional transportation expenditure plan shall include a process of ensuring periodic public review of the progress of the regional transportation expenditure plan and citizen oversight.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 878, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
