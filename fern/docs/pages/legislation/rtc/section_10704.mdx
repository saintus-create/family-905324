# RTC § 10704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 10704

---

## Text

“Horseless carriage” means any vehicle within the provisions of Section 5004 of the Vehicle Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1437.)
