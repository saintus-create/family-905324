# RTC § 11554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 11554

---

## Text

The claim shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which it is founded.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 41.)
