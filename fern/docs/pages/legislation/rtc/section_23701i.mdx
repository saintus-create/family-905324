# RTC § 23701i

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Exempt Corporations > Article: 1. Exemptions From This Part

**Citation:** RTC § 23701i

---

## Text

A voluntary employees’ beneficiary association described in Section 501(c)(9) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 359, Sec. 29. (AB 154) Effective September 30, 2015. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2015, as provided in Sec. 41 of Stats. 2015, Ch. 359.)
