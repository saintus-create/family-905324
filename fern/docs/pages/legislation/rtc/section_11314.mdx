# RTC § 11314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 2. Assessments > Article: 4. Estimated and Escaped Assessments

**Citation:** RTC § 11314

---

## Text

If the board is dissatisfied with the report filed by any person, it may compute and determine the assessment upon the basis of any information available to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 41.)
