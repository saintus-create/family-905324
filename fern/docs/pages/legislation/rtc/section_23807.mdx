# RTC § 23807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Tax Treatment of S Corporations and Their Shareholders

**Citation:** RTC § 23807

---

## Text

Section 1372 of the Internal Revenue Code shall be modified so that references to partnership treatment shall be to Internal Revenue Code partnership provisions, as modified by Part 10 (commencing with Section 17001).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1139, Sec. 55. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 241 of Ch. 1139.)
