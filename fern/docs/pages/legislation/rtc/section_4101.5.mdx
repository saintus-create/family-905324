# RTC § 4101.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7. REDEMPTION > Chapter: 1. Redemption Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4101.5

---

## Text

The tax collector may provide notification of the tax defaulted status of the property to the property owner. This notice is in addition to the notification required by Section 2612.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 527, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1996.)
