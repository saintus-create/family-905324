# RTC § 18626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 2. Returns > Article: 3. General Provisions Applicable to All Persons

**Citation:** RTC § 18626

---

## Text

For purposes of Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 19701), which relates to criminal penalties in the case of fraudulent returns, the term “return” includes any return filed under this part using electronic technology pursuant to Section 18621.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 845, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
