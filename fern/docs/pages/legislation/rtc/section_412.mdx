# RTC § 412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.3. Assessment of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** RTC § 412

---

## Text

The assessor of the county in which the implement of husbandry is located shall assess the implement as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 973.)
