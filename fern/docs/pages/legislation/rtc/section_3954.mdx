# RTC § 3954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 10. Rights of Purchaser of Tax-Deeded Property, or Any Other Person Claiming Through Him, to Bring Action to Determine Adverse Claims to or Clouds Upon Tax-Deeded Property Purchased From the State

**Citation:** RTC § 3954

---

## Text

Unknown defendants owning or claiming an interest in a special assessment shall be described in the complaint, as follows: “The owner or any person claiming an interest in a special assessment lien (here describe said lien as it is described in the bond or assessment records of the district in which it was issued).”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 897.)
