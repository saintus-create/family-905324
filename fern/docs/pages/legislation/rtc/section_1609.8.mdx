# RTC § 1609.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 1609.8

---

## Text

When valuing property, a county board shall follow the provisions set forth in Section 402.5.
