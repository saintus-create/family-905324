# RTC § 434.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.7. Valuation of Timberland and Timber

**Citation:** RTC § 434.2

---

## Text

Within 30 days of the effective date of this section, the board shall appoint the timber advisory committee as defined in subdivision (c) of Section 431.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
