# RTC § 14301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 10. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 14301

---

## Text

All taxes and other money collected under this part and received by the Treasurer shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the Estate Tax Fund, which fund is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
