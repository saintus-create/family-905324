# RTC § 23113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. The Corporation Franchise Tax > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 23113

---

## Text

The tax imposed under this chapter shall attach whether a taxpayer has a taxable year of twelve months or less.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 557.)
