# RTC § 24379

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24379

---

## Text

Section 83 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to property transferred in connection with performance of services, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 52. Effective October 6, 1993.)
