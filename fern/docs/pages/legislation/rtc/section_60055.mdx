# RTC § 60055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 60055

---

## Text

Any person that produces blended diesel fuel outside the bulk transfer/terminal system (the blender) shall pay tax as provided in subdivision (d) of Section 60052.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
