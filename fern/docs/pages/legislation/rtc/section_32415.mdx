# RTC § 32415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 8. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 32415

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
