# RTC § 12072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 2. Ocean Marine Insurance > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 12072

---

## Text

“Current calendar year” means the calendar year for which underwriting profit is computed pursuant to Section 12073.
