# RTC § 60704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 11. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 60704

---

## Text

Any person who makes, issues or signs a false or fraudulent claim for refund under this part is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable as provided in Section 60706.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
