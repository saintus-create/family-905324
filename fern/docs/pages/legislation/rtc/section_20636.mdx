# RTC § 20636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 3. Senior Citizens Tenant-Stockholder Property Tax Postponement Law

**Citation:** RTC § 20636

---

## Text

If a surviving spouse becomes eligible to postpone property taxes pursuant to this chapter, any postponement payments to such person shall be added to the postponement amounts paid to the deceased spouse.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 43.)
