# RTC § 4802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 4802

---

## Text

As used in this part, “district attorney” means the civil legal adviser of the board of supervisors.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
