# RTC § 433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.7. Valuation of Timberland and Timber

**Citation:** RTC § 433

---

## Text

When land is zoned as timberland production a notation of such zoning shall be made on the assessment rolls by the words “Timberland Production Zone” or the initials “T.P.Z.”

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 36.)
