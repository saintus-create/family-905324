# RTC § 5840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 13. TAXATION OF MANUFACTURED HOMES > Chapter: 6. Administrative and Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 5840

---

## Text

The board shall make such rules and regulations and prepare such forms as are necessary for the administration of, and to carry out the intent and purposes of, this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 285, Sec. 7. Effective June 30, 1980. Operative July 1, 1980, by Sec. 23 of Ch. 285.)
