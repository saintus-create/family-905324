# RTC § 7300.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.93. Local Government Finance in the County of Ventura

**Citation:** RTC § 7300.1

---

## Text

If an ordinance proposing the transactions and use tax authorized by this chapter is not approved pursuant to Section 7300, this chapter shall be repealed on January 1, 2027.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 286, Sec. 1. (AB 2453) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed conditionally on January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.93, commencing with Section 7300.)
