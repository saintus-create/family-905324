# RTC § 45855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 45855

---

## Text

Any information regarding solid wastes which is available to the board shall be made available to the Department of Resources Recycling and Recovery.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 654, Sec. 24. (SB 1494) Effective January 1, 2011.)
