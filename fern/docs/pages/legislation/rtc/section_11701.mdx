# RTC § 11701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 7. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 11701

---

## Text

All money received by the board under this part shall be transmitted to the State Treasurer and shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 41.)
