# RTC § 2260.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 4. Maximum Property Tax Rates

**Citation:** RTC § 2260.5

---

## Text

For the purpose of establishing maximum property tax rates, whenever there is a comparison of rates between two or more years that have different assessment ratios or methods of expressing the tax rate, the rates and ratios shall be adjusted as provided in Section 135.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1207.)
