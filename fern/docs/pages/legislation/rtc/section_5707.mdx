# RTC § 5707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5707

---

## Text

“Previous calendar year” means the calendar year immediately preceding the calendar year for which the annual tax is imposed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
