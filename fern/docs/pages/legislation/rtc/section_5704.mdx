# RTC § 5704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5704

---

## Text

“Owner” means the owner of a racehorse or his agent. Any other person claiming, possessing or controlling a racehorse shall provide the name and address of the owner thereof or his agent upon the request of the assessor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1759.)
