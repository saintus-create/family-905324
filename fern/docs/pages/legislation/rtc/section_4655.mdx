# RTC § 4655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 8. DISTRIBUTION > Chapter: 1b. Unsecured Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 4655

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “taxes” includes all taxes and assessments charged on the current unsecured roll.
