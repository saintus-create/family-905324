# RTC § 17131.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17131.4

---

## Text

(a) Section 106(d) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to contributions to health savings accounts, shall not apply.

(b) Section 106(g) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to qualified small employer health reimbursement arrangement, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 16. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
