# RTC § 6388.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 2. Exemptions From Sales Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 6388.3

---

## Text

The State Board of Equalization shall prepare and distribute a standard form or forms for the purchaser affidavit required by Sections 6388 and 6388.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1152, Sec. 1.)
