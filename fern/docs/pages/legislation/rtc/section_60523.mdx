# RTC § 60523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Overpayment and Refunds > Article: 2. Claim for Refunds of Licensed Suppliers, Interstate Users, Exempt Bus Operators, Highway Vehicle Operators, and End Sellers

**Citation:** RTC § 60523

---

## Text

The claim shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which it is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
