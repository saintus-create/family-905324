# RTC § 2202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2202

---

## Text

“Ad valorem property taxation” means any source of revenue derived from applying a property tax rate to the assessed value of property.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 358.)
