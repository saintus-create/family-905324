# RTC § 40096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 40096

---

## Text

All determinations made by the board under Article 3 (commencing with Section 40071) or Article 4 (commencing with Section 40081) are due and payable at the time they become final. If they are not paid when due and payable, a penalty of 10 percent of the amount of the determination, exclusive of interest and penalties, shall be added thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 589, Sec. 163. Effective January 1, 1994.)
