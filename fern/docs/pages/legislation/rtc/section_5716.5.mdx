# RTC § 5716.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5716.5

---

## Text

“Foal” means a racehorse which is foaled during the current calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
