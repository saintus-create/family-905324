# RTC § 45984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 9. Disclosure of Information

**Citation:** RTC § 45984

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits or increases public access to information on any aspect of solid waste generation or disposal collected pursuant to other state or local laws, regulations, or ordinances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
