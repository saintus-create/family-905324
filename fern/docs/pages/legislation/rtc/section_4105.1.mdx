# RTC § 4105.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7. REDEMPTION > Chapter: 1. Redemption Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4105.1

---

## Text

The tax collector shall prepare an estimate of the amount necessary to redeem.
