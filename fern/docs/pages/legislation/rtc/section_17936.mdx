# RTC § 17936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 10.5. Tax on Limited Partnerships

**Citation:** RTC § 17936

---

## Text

A limited partnership shall not be subject to the taxes imposed by this chapter if the limited partnership did no business in this state during the taxable year and the taxable year was 15 days or less.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 604, Sec. 10. Effective October 3, 1997.)
