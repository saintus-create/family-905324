# RTC § 4651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 8. DISTRIBUTION > Chapter: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4651

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, as used in this part, “fund” includes:

(a) A revenue district.

(b) A taxing agency.

(c) Annual installments of assessments charged on the roll.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 430.)
