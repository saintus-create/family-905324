# RTC § 24951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 3. Nontaxable Exchanges

**Citation:** RTC § 24951

---

## Text

Section 1036 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to stock for stock of same corporation, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 83. Effective October 6, 1993.)
