# RTC § 1017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 2. Goods in Transit

**Citation:** RTC § 1017

---

## Text

As used in this article, “goods in transit” means personal property which, on the lien date, is in transit and is possessed or controlled and managed by an intrastate water carrier.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
