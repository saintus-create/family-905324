# RTC § 41133.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Regulations, Records and Reports

**Citation:** RTC § 41133.3

---

## Text

This article does not limit the department’s authority to inspect and audit all records and returns of a service supplier or seller under Section 41130.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 133. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
