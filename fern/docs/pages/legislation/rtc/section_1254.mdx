# RTC § 1254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 6. Assessor’s Office Equipment

**Citation:** RTC § 1254

---

## Text

The State Board of Equalization shall prescribe the forms for the books, blanks, and maps, and may require the map books to:

(a) Be indexed by owners’ names.

(b) Show improvements and assessed value.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
