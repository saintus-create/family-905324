# RTC § 17851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 10. Partners and Partnerships

**Citation:** RTC § 17851

---

## Text

Subchapter K of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to partners and partnerships, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 47. Effective October 10, 1999.)
