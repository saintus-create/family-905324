# RTC § 19850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.3. EARNED INCOME TAX CREDIT INFORMATION ACT

**Citation:** RTC § 19850

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Earned Income Tax Credit Information Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 606, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
