# RTC § 19351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 19351

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 19066, which are applicable in determining the period of limitation on credit or refund, shall be applicable in determining the date of payment for purposes of Sections 19340, 19341, 19363, and 19391.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
