# RTC § 6351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 1. General Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 6351

---

## Text

“Exempted from the taxes imposed by this part,” as used in this article, means, in case of the sales tax, exempted from the computation of the amount of tax imposed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
