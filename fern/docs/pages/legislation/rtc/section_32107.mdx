# RTC § 32107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 3. Registration and Bonds

**Citation:** RTC § 32107

---

## Text

Whenever a taxpayer’s bond is canceled, or becomes void or unenforceable for any reason, or whenever a taxpayer fails to pay any taxes or penalties due under this part, the board shall give written notice thereof to the Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
