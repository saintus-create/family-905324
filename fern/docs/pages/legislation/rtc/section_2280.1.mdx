# RTC § 2280.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 5. Additional Property Tax Rates

**Citation:** RTC § 2280.1

---

## Text

A city may levy, or have levied on its behalf, a rate in addition to the maximum tax rate established pursuant to this chapter in order to comply with the provisions of Section 43073 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 309.)
