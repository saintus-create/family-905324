# RTC § 24990.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 4.5. Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 24990.8

---

## Text

For taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 2010, specific reference to Section 1223(4) to (16), inclusive, of the Internal Revenue Code in this part shall instead be treated as a reference to Section 1223(3) to (15), inclusive, of the Internal Revenue Code, respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 84. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
