# RTC § 41130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Regulations, Records and Reports

**Citation:** RTC § 41130

---

## Text

Upon proper notification to the service supplier or seller, the department or its authorized representative shall have the right to inspect and audit all records and returns of the service supplier or seller at all reasonable times.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 54, Sec. 36. (SB 96) Effective July 1, 2019.)
