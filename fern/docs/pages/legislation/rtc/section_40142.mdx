# RTC § 40142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 6. Collection of Surcharge > Article: 1. Suit for Surcharge

**Citation:** RTC § 40142

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall prosecute the action, and the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to service of summons, pleadings, proof, trials, and appeals are applicable to the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
