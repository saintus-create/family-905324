# RTC § 13533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 5. Payment of Tax > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 13533

---

## Text

The tax imposed by this part shall be paid to the Controller by remittance to the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
