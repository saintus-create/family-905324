# RTC § 21001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.7. TAXPAYERS' BILL OF RIGHTS

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the “Katz-Harris Taxpayers’ Bill of Rights Act.”
