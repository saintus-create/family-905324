# RTC § 30012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 30012

---

## Text

“Dealer” includes every person, other than one holding a distributor’s or wholesaler’s license, who engages in this state in the sale of cigarettes or tobacco products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 634, Sec. 6. Effective September 21, 1989.)
