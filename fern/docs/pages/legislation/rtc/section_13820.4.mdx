# RTC § 13820.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 7. Uniform Act on Interstate Arbitration of Death Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 13820.4

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 13820.2 in respect of the issuance of subpoenas, all questions arising in the course of the proceeding shall be determined by majority vote of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
