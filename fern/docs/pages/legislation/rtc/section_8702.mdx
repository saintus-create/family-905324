# RTC § 8702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 1. User

**Citation:** RTC § 8702

## Text

Applications for permits shall be made to the board upon forms prescribed by the board.
