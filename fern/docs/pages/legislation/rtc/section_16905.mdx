# RTC § 16905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 16905

---

## Text

The Controller on his own motion may appear in behalf of the state in any and all generation skipping transfer tax matters before any court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
