# RTC § 11532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection of Tax > Article: 7. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 11532

## Text

In all proceedings under this chapter the board may act on behalf of the people of the State of California.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 498, Sec. 21.)
