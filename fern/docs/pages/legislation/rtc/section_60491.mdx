# RTC § 60491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 7. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 60491

---

## Text

The remedies of the state provided for in this chapter are cumulative, and no action taken by the board constitutes an election by the state to pursue any remedy to the exclusion of any other remedy for which provision is made in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
