# RTC § 40145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 6. Collection of Surcharge > Article: 1. Suit for Surcharge

**Citation:** RTC § 40145

## Text

In any action brought under this part process may be served according to the Code of Civil Procedure and the Civil Code of this state.
