# RTC § 43607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 43607

---

## Text

Any prosecution for violation of any of the penal provisions of this part shall be instituted within three years after the commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
