# RTC § 14104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 14104

## Text

The Controller shall, without charge, distribute a copy of this part to any person who requests it.
