# RTC § 4653.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 8. DISTRIBUTION > Chapter: 1a. Secured Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 4653.6

---

## Text

Amounts paid as delinquent penalties shall be distributed to the county general fund.
