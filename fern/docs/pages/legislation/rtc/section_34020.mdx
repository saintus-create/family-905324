# RTC § 34020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14.5. Cannabis Tax > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 34020

---

## Text

The Controller shall periodically audit the Tax Fund to ensure that those funds are used and accounted for in a manner consistent with this part and as otherwise required by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 64, Sec. 7.1.)
