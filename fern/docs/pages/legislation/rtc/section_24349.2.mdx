# RTC § 24349.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24349.2

---

## Text

Section 280G of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to golden parachute payments, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 67. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
