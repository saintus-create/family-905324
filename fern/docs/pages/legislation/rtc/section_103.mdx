# RTC § 103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 103

---

## Text

“Property” includes all matters and things, real, personal, and mixed, capable of private ownership.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
