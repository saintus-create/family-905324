# RTC § 5392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 4. Levy

**Citation:** RTC § 5392

---

## Text

The tax on aircraft subject to this part shall be collected in the same manner as all other personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 610, Sec. 4. Effective July 18, 1980.)
