# RTC § 16731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of the Tax > Article: 3. Deficiency Determination

**Citation:** RTC § 16731

---

## Text

In the case of a false or fraudulent return or failure to file a return, the Controller may determine the tax at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
