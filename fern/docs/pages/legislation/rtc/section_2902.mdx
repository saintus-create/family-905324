# RTC § 2902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 2902

---

## Text

The assessment of unsecured property shall be deemed complete for the purpose of enforcing the collection thereof when the assessor has made a record in writing of the assessment in such form as the board may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 689.)
