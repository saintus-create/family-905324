# RTC § 60654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 10. Distribution of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 60654

---

## Text

Any costs to the board to carry out any duties imposed upon it by this part shall be paid, upon appropriation by the Legislature, from the moneys deposited in the account under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
