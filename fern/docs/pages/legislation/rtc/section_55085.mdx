# RTC § 55085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 3. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 55085

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination shall become final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
