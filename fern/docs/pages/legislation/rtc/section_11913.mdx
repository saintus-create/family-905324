# RTC § 11913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6.7. DOCUMENTARY TRANSFER TAX ACT > Chapter: 2. Authorization for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 11913

---

## Text

The transfer of any mobilehome installed on a foundation system, pursuant to Section 18551 of the Health and Safety Code, and subject to local property taxation shall be subject to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1160.)
