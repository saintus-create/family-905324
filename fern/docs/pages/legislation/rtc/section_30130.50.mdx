# RTC § 30130.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax > Article: 2.5. California Healthcare, Research and Prevention Tobacco Tax Act of 2016

**Citation:** RTC § 30130.50

---

## Text

Definitions.

For the purposes of this article:

(a) “Cigarette” has the same meaning as that in Section 30003 as it read on January 1, 2015.

(b) “Tobacco products” has the same meaning as that in subdivision (b) of Section 30121, as amended by this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 56, Sec. 4.1.)
