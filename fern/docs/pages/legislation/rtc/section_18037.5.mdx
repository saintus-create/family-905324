# RTC § 18037.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 13. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property

**Citation:** RTC § 18037.5

---

## Text

The amendments made by Section 844 of the Pension Protection Act of 2006 (Public Law 109-280) to Section 1035 of the Internal Revenue Code, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 37. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
