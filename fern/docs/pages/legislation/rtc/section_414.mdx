# RTC § 414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.3. Assessment of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** RTC § 414

---

## Text

Upon request of the assessor of the county in which an implement of husbandry is located, the owner shall file with him a statement setting forth the make, model and year of manufacture of the implement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 973.)
