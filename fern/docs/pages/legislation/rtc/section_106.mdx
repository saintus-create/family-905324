# RTC § 106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 106

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in Section 83.5, “personal property” includes all property except real estate.

(b) This section shall go into effect on the date Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 83) of Part 0.5 goes into effect pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 88.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 5) and added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 58, Sec. 6. (SB 364) Effective September 9, 2020. Section conditionally operative by it own provisions.)
