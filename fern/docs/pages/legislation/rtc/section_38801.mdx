# RTC § 38801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 9. Violations and Res Judicata

**Citation:** RTC § 38801

---

## Text

Any timber owner or other person who fails or refuses to furnish any return required to be made, or who fails or refuses to furnish a supplemental return or other data required by the board, is guilty of a misdemeanor punishable as provided in Section 38803.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 498, Sec. 37.)
