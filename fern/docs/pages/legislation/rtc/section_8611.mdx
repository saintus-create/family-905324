# RTC § 8611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 8611

---

## Text

“Sell” includes any transfer of title or possession, exchange, or barter, in any manner or by any means whatsoever, for a consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1814.)
