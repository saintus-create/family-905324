# RTC § 3963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 10. Rights of Purchaser of Tax-Deeded Property, or Any Other Person Claiming Through Him, to Bring Action to Determine Adverse Claims to or Clouds Upon Tax-Deeded Property Purchased From the State

**Citation:** RTC § 3963

---

## Text

On the trial of the action, the court shall determine the rights of all the parties thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 897.)
