# RTC § 30439

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 7.5. Seizure and Forfeiture

**Citation:** RTC § 30439

---

## Text

If the board determines that the property was seized erroneously or illegally, it shall order the release of the property. If the board denies the petition for the release or recovery of the property, notice of the denial shall be mailed within five days to the petitioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 963.)
