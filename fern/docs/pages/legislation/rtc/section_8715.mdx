# RTC § 8715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 2. Vendor

**Citation:** RTC § 8715

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to operate as a vendor of fuel in this State without a permit or after a permit has been suspended or revoked.
