# RTC § 464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 2. Information From Taxpayer

## Text

All moneys recovered by the assessor under Section 463 shall be paid into the county treasury.
