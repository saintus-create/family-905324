# RTC § 38403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 5. Determinations > Article: 1. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 38403

---

## Text

The person required to file the return shall deliver the return together with a remittance of the amount of the tax due to the office of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
