# RTC § 2860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 3.3. Collection of Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 2860

---

## Text

If the tax collector refuses or neglects for five days to make payments or settlements as required in this division, he is liable for the full amount of taxes charged against him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 239.)
