# RTC § 55087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 3. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 55087

## Text

Any notice required by this article shall be served in the same manner as prescribed for service of notice by subdivision (d) of Section 55061.
