# RTC § 5149.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 2. Refund Actions by Taxpayers

**Citation:** RTC § 5149.5

---

## Text

Where the taxes sought to be recovered have been paid after delinquency, the amount of penalties, interest or costs recoverable in actions brought under this article shall be computed only on the taxes recovered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 499.)
