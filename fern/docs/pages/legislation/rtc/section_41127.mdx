# RTC § 41127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 6. Collection of Tax > Article: 3. Warrant for Collection

**Citation:** RTC § 41127

---

## Text

The fees, commissions, and expenses are the obligation of the person required to pay any amount under this part and may be collected from him by virtue of the warrant or in any other manner provided in this part for the collection of the surcharge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 443.)
