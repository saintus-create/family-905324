# RTC § 7252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.6. TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7252

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this part, means any city, county, city and county, or other governmental entity authorized, to impose transaction and use taxes pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 342, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2008.)
