# RTC § 14102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 14102

---

## Text

The Controller may employ those assistants, including attorneys, as may from time to time be necessary for the proper administration of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
