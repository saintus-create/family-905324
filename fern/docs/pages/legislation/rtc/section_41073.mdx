# RTC § 41073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 3. Deficiency Determinations

**Citation:** RTC § 41073

---

## Text

If any part of the deficiency for which a deficiency determination is made is due to negligence or intentional disregard of this part or authorized rules and regulations, a penalty of 10 percent of the amount of the determination shall be added thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 80. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
