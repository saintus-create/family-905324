# RTC § 17132.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17132.7

---

## Text

A payment under Section 103(c)(10) of the Ricky Ray Hemophilia Relief Fund Act of 1998 (Public Law 105-369) to an individual shall be treated for purposes of this part, Part 10.2 (commencing with Section 18401) and Part 11 (commencing with Section 23001) as damages described in Section 104(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 17132.6 (as added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 35) by Stats. 2002, Ch. 807, Sec. 4. Effective September 23, 2002.)
