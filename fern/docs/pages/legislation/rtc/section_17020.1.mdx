# RTC § 17020.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17020.1

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the term “substituted basis property” has the same meaning given that term by Section 7701(a)(42) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1138, Sec. 5. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 189 of Ch. 1138.)
