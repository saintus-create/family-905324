# RTC § 3104.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 6. Unsecured Roll Summary Judgment

**Citation:** RTC § 3104.5

---

## Text

In addition to any penalty or fee imposed pursuant to this part, a penalty equal to the amount of any bond premium posted, or other costs incurred to enforce the judgment entered pursuant to this chapter shall be imposed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1126.)
