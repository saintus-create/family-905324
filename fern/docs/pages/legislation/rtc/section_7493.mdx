# RTC § 7493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Licenses and Bonds > Article: 5. Denial of License

**Citation:** RTC § 7493

---

## Text

The notice shall be addressed to the applicant at his or her address as it appears in the records of the board, and shall be given in the manner prescribed in Section 7671 for giving notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
