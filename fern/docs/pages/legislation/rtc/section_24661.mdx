# RTC § 24661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 3. Year of Inclusion

**Citation:** RTC § 24661

---

## Text

Section 451 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the general rule for taxable year of inclusion, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1243, Sec. 66. Effective September 30, 1994.)
