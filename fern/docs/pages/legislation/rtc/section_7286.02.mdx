# RTC § 7286.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 2.4. County of Los Angeles Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7286.02

---

## Text

If, as of December 31, 2028, an ordinance proposing a transactions and use tax has not been approved in the County of Los Angeles as required by Section 7286.01, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 731, Sec. 1. (AB 1679) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed conditionally on December 31, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 2.4, commencing with Section 7286.01.)
