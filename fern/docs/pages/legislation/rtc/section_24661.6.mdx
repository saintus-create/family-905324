# RTC § 24661.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 3. Year of Inclusion

**Citation:** RTC § 24661.6

---

## Text

Section 451(k) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to special rule for sales or dispositions to implement Federal Energy Regulatory Commission or state electric restructuring policy, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 112. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
