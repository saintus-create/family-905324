# RTC § 43307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 4. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 43307

---

## Text

Any notice required by this article shall be served in the same manner as prescribed for service of notice by Section 43201 or 43201.01.
