# RTC § 17018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17018

---

## Text

“State” includes the District of Columbia, and the possessions of the United States.
