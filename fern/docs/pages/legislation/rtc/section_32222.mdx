# RTC § 32222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 5.5. Surtax on Beer, Wine,and Distilled Spirits > Article: 1. Imposition of the Surtax

**Citation:** RTC § 32222

---

## Text

The taxes imposed by this article are in addition to any other tax imposed upon beer, wine, sparkling hard cider, or distilled spirits by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 86, Sec. 1. Effective June 30, 1991.)
