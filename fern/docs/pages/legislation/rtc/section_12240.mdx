# RTC § 12240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 3. All Insurance Other Than Ocean Marine > Article: 4. Basis of Tax for Medi-Cal Managed Care Plans

**Citation:** RTC § 12240

---

## Text

In the case of a Medi-Cal managed care plan, the basis of the tax is, in respect to each year, total operating revenue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 33, Sec. 10. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2013. Section operative as of July 1, 2012, pursuant to Section 12242.)
