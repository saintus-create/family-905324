# RTC § 43805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22.1. HAZARDOUS SPILL PREVENTION FEE LAW

**Citation:** RTC § 43805

---

## Text

“Hazardous waste” has the same meaning as defined in Section 25117 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1223, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1995.)
