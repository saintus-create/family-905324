# RTC § 1645.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 1.9. Hearings Before Assessment Hearing Officers for Unitary Property Located in More Than One County

**Citation:** RTC § 1645.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the term “unitary property” shall mean one or more parcels of real property that are contiguous and are operated as an economic unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1480, Sec. 1. Applicable July 1, 1989, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1480.)
