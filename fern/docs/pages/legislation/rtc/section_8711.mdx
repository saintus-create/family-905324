# RTC § 8711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 2. Vendor

**Citation:** RTC § 8711

---

## Text

Every person desiring to become a vendor shall first secure a vendor use fuel tax permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1814.)
