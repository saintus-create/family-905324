# RTC § 9173.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 9173.6

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2129.)
