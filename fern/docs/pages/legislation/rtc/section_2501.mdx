# RTC § 2501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 1. Medium of Payment

**Citation:** RTC § 2501

---

## Text

Taxes shall be paid only in the mediums permitted by this chapter.
