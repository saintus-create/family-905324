# RTC § 410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.3. Assessment of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** RTC § 410

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this article to provide for a uniform system of assessment of all implements of husbandry in this state, regardless of where located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 973.)
