# RTC § 1650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 2. Duties of Auditor

**Citation:** RTC § 1650

---

## Text

Every auditor who fails to transmit the valuation statements as required forfeits one thousand dollars to the State, to be recovered in an action brought by the Attorney General in the name of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
