# RTC § 24452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 24452

---

## Text

Section 301(e)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to 20 percent corporate shareholders, is modified to refer to Section 24402 in lieu of Sections 243, 244, and 245 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 117, Sec. 82. Effective July 16, 1991.)
