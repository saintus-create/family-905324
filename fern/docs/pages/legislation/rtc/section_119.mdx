# RTC § 119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 119

---

## Text

“County board” means the county board of supervisors when sitting as the county board of equalization.
