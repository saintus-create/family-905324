# RTC § 8107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds on Certain Sales

**Citation:** RTC § 8107

---

## Text

No refund shall be granted for losses in handling, transporting, or storing motor vehicle fuel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 37.)
