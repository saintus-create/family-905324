# RTC § 617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 6. Assessment Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 617

---

## Text

As soon as the assessor completes the local roll, he shall deliver it to the auditor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 147.)
