# RTC § 41081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 4. Determinations if No Return Made

**Citation:** RTC § 41081

---

## Text

In making a determination the department may offset overpayments for a period or periods, together with interest on the overpayments, against underpayments for another period or periods, against penalties, and against the interest on the underpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 85. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
