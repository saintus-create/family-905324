# RTC § 19168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 4. Payments and Assessments > Article: 7. Penalties and Additions to Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19168

---

## Text

The following rules shall apply to any penalty imposed under Section 19166 or 19167:

(a) The penalties shall be in addition to any other penalties provided by law.

(b) Article 3 (commencing with Section 19031) of this chapter (relating to deficiency assessments) shall not be applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
