# RTC § 4990.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4990.3

---

## Text

An action may be brought at any time against this State or any county or city to quiet title against the lien of any taxes which have been canceled in accordance with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 530.)
