# RTC § 100.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 6. Allocation of Property Tax Revenue > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 100.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the allocations and apportionments made in a County of the Eighteenth Class of revenues generated by Sections 75 to 75.80, inclusive, for fiscal years to the 1999–2000 fiscal year, inclusive, are deemed to be correct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 611, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
