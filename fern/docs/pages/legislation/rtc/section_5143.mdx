# RTC § 5143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 2. Refund Actions by Taxpayers

**Citation:** RTC § 5143

---

## Text

If a claim for refund relates only to the validity of a portion of an assessment, an action may be brought under this article only as to that portion.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 499.)
