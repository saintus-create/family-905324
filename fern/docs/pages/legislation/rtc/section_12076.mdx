# RTC § 12076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 2. Ocean Marine Insurance > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 12076

---

## Text

“Expenses incurred” includes specific and general expenses incurred in the current calendar year on ocean marine contracts written within the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 113.)
