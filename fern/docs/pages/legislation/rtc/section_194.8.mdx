# RTC § 194.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Disaster Relief: Tax Deferral

**Citation:** RTC § 194.8

---

## Text

The allocation of funds to, and the repayment of funds by, counties made pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to review and audit by the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1507, Sec. 14.)
