# RTC § 5012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 1.5. Cancellation of Assessments on State-Assessed Property

## Text

The date and nature of the cancellation shall be entered in the records of the board.
