# RTC § 1138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 5. Vessels

**Citation:** RTC § 1138

---

## Text

Vessels documented outside of this State and plying in whole or in part in its waters, the owners of which reside in this State, shall be assessed in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
