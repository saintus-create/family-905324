# RTC § 46007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46007

---

## Text

“Barge” means a vessel carrying oil, petroleum products, or renewable fuel in commercial quantities as cargo but are not equipped with a means of self-propulsion.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 115, Sec. 84. (AB 148) Effective July 22, 2021.)
