# RTC § 13410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 13410

---

## Text

“Taxable estate” means the “taxable estate” as defined in Section 2051 of the United States Internal Revenue Code of 1954, as amended or renumbered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
