# RTC § 23049.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23049.2

---

## Text

Section 65 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the definition of ordinary loss, shall apply.
