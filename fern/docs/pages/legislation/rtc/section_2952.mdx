# RTC § 2952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 2. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 2952

---

## Text

A record shall be kept of the property seized and sold.
