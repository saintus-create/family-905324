# RTC § 38110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 38110

---

## Text

“Yield tax” means the dollar amount derived by multiplying the net volume of harvested timber by the appropriate immediate harvest value per unit and by the yield tax rate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
