# RTC § 17631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 7. Exempt Trusts and Common Trust Funds > Article: 1. General Rule

**Citation:** RTC § 17631

---

## Text

An organization described in Section 401(a) of the Internal Revenue Code shall be exempt from taxation under this part unless such exemption is denied under Sections 17635 to 17639, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 39. Effective July 28, 1983.)
