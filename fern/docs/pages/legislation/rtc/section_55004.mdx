# RTC § 55004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 55004

---

## Text

“Feepayer” means any person liable for the payment of the fees collected pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
