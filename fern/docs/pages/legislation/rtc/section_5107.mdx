# RTC § 5107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 5107

---

## Text

As used in this article, “tax” or “taxes” includes penalties, interest, and costs.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 664.)
