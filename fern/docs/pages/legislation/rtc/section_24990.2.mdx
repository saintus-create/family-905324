# RTC § 24990.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 4.5. Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 24990.2

---

## Text

Section 301 of Title III of Division A of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-343), relating to gain or loss from sale of certain preferred stock, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 82. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
