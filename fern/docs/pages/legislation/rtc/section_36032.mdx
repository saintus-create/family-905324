# RTC § 36032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 16. FIREARM, FIREARM PRECURSOR PART, AND AMMUNITION EXCISE TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection and Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 36032

---

## Text

The taxes imposed by this part are due and payable to the department quarterly on or before the last day of the month next succeeding each quarterly period of three months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 231, Sec. 7. (AB 28) Effective January 1, 2024.)
