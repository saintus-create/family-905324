# RTC § 1651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 2. Duties of Auditor

**Citation:** RTC § 1651

---

## Text

Immediately upon completion of his duties under Section 1646, the auditor shall transmit the unsecured roll to the tax collector.
