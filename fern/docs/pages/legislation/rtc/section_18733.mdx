# RTC § 18733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 3. Voluntary Contributions > Article: 3.6. California Senior Citizen Advocacy Voluntary Tax Contribution Fund

**Citation:** RTC § 18733

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (c) of Section 18873 and Section 18874, this article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2032, and is repealed as of December 1 of that year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 89, Sec. 2. (AB 559) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed on December 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Termination provisions affect Article 3.6, commencing with Section 18730.)
