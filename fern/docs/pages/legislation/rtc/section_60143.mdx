# RTC § 60143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. License for Exempt Bus Operator

**Citation:** RTC § 60143

---

## Text

Before granting a license authorizing a person to engage in business as an exempt bus operator, the board may require the person to file with the board security pursuant to Section 60401. The license issued to any exempt bus operator is not transferable and is valid until canceled or revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
