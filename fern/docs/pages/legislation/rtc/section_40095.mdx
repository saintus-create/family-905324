# RTC § 40095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 40095

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination becomes final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
