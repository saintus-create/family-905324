# RTC § 30105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax > Article: 1. Tax on Distributors

**Citation:** RTC § 30105

---

## Text

The taxes imposed by this part shall not apply to the sale of cigarettes or tobacco products by the original importer to a licensed distributor if the cigarettes or tobacco products are manufactured outside the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 634, Sec. 12. Effective September 21, 1989.)
