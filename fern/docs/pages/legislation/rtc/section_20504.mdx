# RTC § 20504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 1. Senior Citizens Homeowners and Renters Property Tax Assistance Law > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 20504

---

## Text

“Household income” means all income received by all persons of a household while members of such household. In the case of a nonresident claimant, “household income” also includes all income of the claimant during the year without regard to source.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1242.)
