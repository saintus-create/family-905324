# RTC § 1752.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1.5. Multijurisdictional Assessment Appeals Boards > Article: 3. Establishment and Procedure

**Citation:** RTC § 1752.1

---

## Text

A multijurisdictional assessment appeals board shall be established by enactment of an ordinance, which shall be operative for not less than four years, by each participating county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. (SB 447) Effective January 1, 2018. Repealed on January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 1756.)
