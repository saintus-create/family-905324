# RTC § 7288.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.5. Local Public Finance

**Citation:** RTC § 7288.6

---

## Text

No tax imposed pursuant to this chapter shall become operative before the later of the following dates:

(a) January 1, 1993.

(b) The date that Sections 6051.5 and 6201.5 cease to be operative pursuant to those sections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1024, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1992.)
