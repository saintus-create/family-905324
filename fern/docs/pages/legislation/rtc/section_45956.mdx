# RTC § 45956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 8. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 45956

---

## Text

Any prosecution for violation of any provision of this part shall be instituted within three years after the commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
