# RTC § 430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.5. Valuation of Open-Space Land Subject to an Enforceable Restriction

**Citation:** RTC § 430

---

## Text

There shall be a rebuttable presumption that the present use of open-space land which is enforceably restricted and devoted to agricultural use is its highest and best agricultural use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 431 by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
