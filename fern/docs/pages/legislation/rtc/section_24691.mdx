# RTC § 24691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 4. Year of Deduction

**Citation:** RTC § 24691

---

## Text

Section 465 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to limitations of deductions to the amount at risk, shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 11, Sec. 78. Effective February 19, 1988. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 95 of Ch. 78.)
