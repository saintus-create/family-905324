# RTC § 2214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2214

---

## Text

“Sales tax exemption” means any provision which causes a reduction in revenue to a city or county under Part 1.5 (commencing with Section 7200) of Division 2.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 358.)
