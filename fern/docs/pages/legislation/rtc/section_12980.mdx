# RTC § 12980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 7. Cancellations and Refunds > Article: 2. Refund or Credit for Collected Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 12980

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim for refund or credit within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of any demand against the State on account of overpayment.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
