# RTC § 17156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17156

---

## Text

(a) Gross income shall not include any amount received as compensation in any taxable year by a taxpayer pursuant to Assembly Bill 110 of the 1999–2000 Regular Session.

(b) This section shall apply to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1999.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 619, Sec. 2. Effective October 10, 1999. Note: The Assembly Bill 110 referred to in the text became Ch. 619.)
