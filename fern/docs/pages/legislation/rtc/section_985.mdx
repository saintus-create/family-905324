# RTC § 985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 985

---

## Text

Every toll bridge connecting two or more counties shall be assessed in equal proportions in the counties it connects.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
