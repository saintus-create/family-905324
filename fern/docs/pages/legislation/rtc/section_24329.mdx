# RTC § 24329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Gross Income > Article: 3. Other Exclusions

**Citation:** RTC § 24329

---

## Text

Section 139 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to disaster relief payments, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 66. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
