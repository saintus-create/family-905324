# RTC § 32230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 5.5. Surtax on Beer, Wine,and Distilled Spirits > Article: 2. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 32230

---

## Text

All surtaxes, interest, and penalties imposed and required to be paid under this chapter shall be made in remittances to the State Board of Equalization and shall be deposited in the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 86, Sec. 1. Effective June 30, 1991.)
