# RTC § 32385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 4. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** RTC § 32385

---

## Text

The remedies of the State provided for in this chapter are cumulative, and no action taken by the board or Attorney General constitutes an election by the State or any of its officers to pursue any remedy to the exclusion of any other remedy for which provision is made in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 32382 by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1397.)
