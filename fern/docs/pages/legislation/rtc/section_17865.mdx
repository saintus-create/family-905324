# RTC § 17865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 10. Partners and Partnerships

**Citation:** RTC § 17865

---

## Text

Part IV of Subchapter K of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code (commencing with Section 771 of the Internal Revenue Code), shall not apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 322, Sec. 37. Effective August 20, 1998.)
