# RTC § 24451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 24451

---

## Text

Subchapter C of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to corporate distributions and adjustments, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 873, Sec. 40. Effective October 6, 1993.)
