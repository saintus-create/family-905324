# RTC § 13830.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 8. Determination of Domicile by Arbitration: Alternative Method

**Citation:** RTC § 13830.7

---

## Text

Whenever practicable the board shall apply the rules of evidence then prevailing in the federal courts under the federal rules of civil procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
