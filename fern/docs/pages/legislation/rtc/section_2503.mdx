# RTC § 2503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 1. Medium of Payment

**Citation:** RTC § 2503

---

## Text

A tax levied for a special purpose shall be paid in such funds as may be directed.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
