# RTC § 17090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 2. Items Specifically Included in Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17090

---

## Text

Gross income includes cash allowances received by an employee under a parking cash-out program, except any portion used for a ridesharing purpose and excluded from gross income by Section 17149.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 554, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
