# RTC § 60602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 60602

---

## Text

The board may employ attorneys, accountants, auditors, investigators, and other expert and clerical assistance necessary for the efficient administration of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
