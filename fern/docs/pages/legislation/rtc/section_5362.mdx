# RTC § 5362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 3. Assessment

**Citation:** RTC § 5362

---

## Text

The assessor of the county in which the aircraft is habitually situated shall assess the aircraft at its market value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
