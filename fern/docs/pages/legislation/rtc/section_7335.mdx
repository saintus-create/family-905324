# RTC § 7335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7335

---

## Text

“Refinery” means a facility used to produce motor vehicle fuel from crude oil, unfinished oils, natural gas liquids, or other hydrocarbons, and from which motor vehicle fuel may be removed by pipeline, by vessel, or at a rack.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
