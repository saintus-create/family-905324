# RTC § 8713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 2. Vendor

**Citation:** RTC § 8713

---

## Text

On receipt of an application the board shall issue to the applicant a vendor use fuel tax permit authorizing him to become a vendor of fuel taxable under this part. The permit is valid until canceled, suspended, or revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1814.)
