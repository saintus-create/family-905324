# RTC § 827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 4. Assessment by State Board of Equalization Generally > Article: 5. Property Statements

**Citation:** RTC § 827

---

## Text

The statement shall show specifically any information required by the board in order to assess State assessed property.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
