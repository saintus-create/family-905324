# RTC § 45051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 2. The Integrated Waste Management Fee > Article: 1. Imposition of Fee

**Citation:** RTC § 45051

---

## Text

The fee imposed pursuant to Section 48000 of the Public Resources Code shall be administered and collected by the board in accordance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 46. Effective October 1, 1993.)
