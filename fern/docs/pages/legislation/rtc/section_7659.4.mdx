# RTC § 7659.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Determinations and Payments > Article: 2. Prepayments

**Citation:** RTC § 7659.4

---

## Text

In determining whether a person’s estimated tax liability averages nine hundred thousand dollars ($900,000) or more per month, the board may consider tax returns filed pursuant to this part as well as any information in the board’s possession or which may come into its possession.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
