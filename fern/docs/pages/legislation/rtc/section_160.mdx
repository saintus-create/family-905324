# RTC § 160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 160

---

## Text

In any action against the county to quiet title allowed under this division, service of process shall be made on the tax collector of the county where the real property is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1420, Sec. 7.)
