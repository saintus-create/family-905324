# RTC § 136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 136

---

## Text

Whenever any taxes or assessments are entered on the roll under any provision of law, such taxes or assessments shall, notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, be subject to all provions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 135 (as added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 242) by Stats. 1980, Ch. 411, Sec. 4. Effective July 11, 1980. Operative January 1, 1981, by Sec. 51 of Ch. 411.)
