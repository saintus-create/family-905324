# RTC § 19431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 5. Cancellations

**Citation:** RTC § 19431

---

## Text

If a tax has been illegally levied against a taxpayer, the Franchise Tax Board shall set forth on its records the reasons therefor and thereafter shall authorize the cancellation of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
