# RTC § 17026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17026

---

## Text

This part applies to the taxable income of taxpayers received or accrued on or after January 1, 1935.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 497, Sec. 258. (AB 991) Effective January 1, 2020.)
