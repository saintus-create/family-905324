# RTC § 46010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46010

---

## Text

“Crude oil” means petroleum in an unrefined or natural state, including condensate and natural gasoline, and including substances that enhance, cut, thin, or reduce viscosity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 162. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014.)
