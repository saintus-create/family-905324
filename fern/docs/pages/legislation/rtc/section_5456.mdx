# RTC § 5456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Distribution

**Citation:** RTC § 5456

---

## Text

The details of the method of distribution shall be supplied by the county auditor and shall fairly carry out the purpose of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 202, Sec. 3.)
