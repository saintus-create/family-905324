# RTC § 7286.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 2.66. Santa Fe Springs Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7286.28

---

## Text

If, as of December 31, 2022, an ordinance proposing a transactions and use tax has not been approved in the City of Santa Fe Springs as required by subdivision (b) of Section 7286.27, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 651, Sec. 1. (SB 703) Effective January 1, 2018. Repealed conditionally on December 31, 2022, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 2.66, commencing with Section 7286.27.)
