# RTC § 8172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 4. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 8172

---

## Text

In any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 8171, the court may, with the consent of the Attorney General, order a change in the place of trial.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1999.)
