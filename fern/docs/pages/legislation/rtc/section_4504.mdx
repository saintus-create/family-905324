# RTC § 4504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7.5. TAX CERTIFICATES > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 4504

---

## Text

“Secured roll property” means property that remains on the secured roll after taxes thereon have been declared in default.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 189, Sec. 7. Effective July 24, 1995.)
