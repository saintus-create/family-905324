# RTC § 10756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Fee > Article: 1. Computation of Fee

**Citation:** RTC § 10756

---

## Text

If any vehicle which is exempt under Section 10781 or 10782 ceases to be so exempt by reason of change of ownership, the application shall be deemed an application for original registration for the purposes of determining the expiration date of the registration and subsequent renewals thereof.
