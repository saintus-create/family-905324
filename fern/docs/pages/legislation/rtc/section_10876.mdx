# RTC § 10876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 3. Collections and Refunds > Article: 2. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 10876

---

## Text

Every license fee and any penalty added thereto, from the date on which the fee becomes due, shall constitute a lien upon the vehicle for which due and upon any other vehicle owned by the owner of that vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 759, Sec. 2.)
