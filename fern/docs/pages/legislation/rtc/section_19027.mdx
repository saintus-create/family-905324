# RTC § 19027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 4. Payments and Assessments > Article: 2. Banks and Corporations

**Citation:** RTC § 19027

---

## Text

The application of this article to taxable years of less than 12 months shall be in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Franchise Tax Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 862, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001.)
