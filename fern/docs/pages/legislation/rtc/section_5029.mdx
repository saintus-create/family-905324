# RTC § 5029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 2. Cancellation of Assessments on Public Lands on Application of State Lands Commission

**Citation:** RTC § 5029

---

## Text

This article does not apply to:

(a) Lands acquired by the State on a sale for taxes, when the deed is required to be filed with the Controller.

(b) Swamp and overflowed lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
