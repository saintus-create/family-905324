# RTC § 23251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. The Corporation Franchise Tax > Article: 5. Reorganized Corporations

**Citation:** RTC § 23251

---

## Text

The term “reorganization,” as used in this chapter, shall have the same meaning as that term is defined in Section 368 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 938, Sec. 26.2. Effective September 7, 1984.)
