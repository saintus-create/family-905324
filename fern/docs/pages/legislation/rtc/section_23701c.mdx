# RTC § 23701c

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Exempt Corporations > Article: 1. Exemptions From This Part

## Text

A cemetery company described in Section 501(c)(13) of the Internal Revenue Code.
