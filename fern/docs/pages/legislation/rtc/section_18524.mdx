# RTC § 18524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 2. Returns > Article: 1. Individuals and Fiduciaries

**Citation:** RTC § 18524

---

## Text

If a joint return is made under Section 18522 after the death of either spouse, the return with respect to the decedent may be made only by his or her executor or administrator.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
