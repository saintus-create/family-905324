# RTC § 8613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 8613

---

## Text

“Natural gas” means naturally occurring mixtures of hydrocarbon gases and vapors consisting principally of methane, whether in gaseous or liquid form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1509.)
