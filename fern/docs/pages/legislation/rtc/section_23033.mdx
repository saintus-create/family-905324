# RTC § 23033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23033

---

## Text

“Paid or incurred” and “paid or accrued” shall be construed according to the method of accounting upon the basis of which net income is computed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 557.)
