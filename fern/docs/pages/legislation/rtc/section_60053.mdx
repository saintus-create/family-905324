# RTC § 60053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 60053

---

## Text

Every refiner shall pay tax on the removal of diesel fuel from a refinery as provided in subdivision (a) of Section 60052.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
