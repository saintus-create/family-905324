# RTC § 6207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 3. The Use Tax > Article: 1. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 6207

---

## Text

Any person violating Sections 6203, 6205, or 6206 is guilty of a misdemeanor.
