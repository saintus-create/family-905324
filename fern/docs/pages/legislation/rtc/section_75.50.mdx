# RTC § 75.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 3.5. Change in Ownership and New Construction After the Lien Date > Article: 6. Collection of Supplemental Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 75.50

---

## Text

The auditor shall transmit supplemental assessments entered on the supplemental roll to the tax collector for preparation of supplemental tax bills, and charge the tax collector with the taxes extended thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 946, Sec. 16. Effective September 10, 1984.)
