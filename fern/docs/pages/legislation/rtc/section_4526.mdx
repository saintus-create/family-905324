# RTC § 4526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7.5. TAX CERTIFICATES > Chapter: 3. Sale of Tax Certificates

**Citation:** RTC § 4526

---

## Text

A tax certificate shall not be considered as having any situs in the county in which the real property is located for which the tax certificate is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 189, Sec. 7. Effective July 24, 1995.)
