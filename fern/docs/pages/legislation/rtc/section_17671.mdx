# RTC § 17671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 7. Exempt Trusts and Common Trust Funds > Article: 3. Common Trust Funds

**Citation:** RTC § 17671

---

## Text

Section 584 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to common trust funds, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 45. Effective October 10, 1999.)
