# RTC § 60015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60015

---

## Text

“Diesel fuel registrant” includes any enterer, positionholder, refiner, throughputter, or terminal operator, that is licensed as a supplier pursuant to Section 60131.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 38. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
