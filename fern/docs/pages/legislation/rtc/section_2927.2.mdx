# RTC § 2927.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 2927.2

---

## Text

Annually, after the taxes on the unsecured roll become delinquent, the collector shall insert in the abstract list or prepare a delinquent roll or an abstract list of, all unpaid items.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 489.)
