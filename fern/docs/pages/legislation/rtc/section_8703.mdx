# RTC § 8703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 1. User

**Citation:** RTC § 8703

---

## Text

On receipt of an application and after the deposit of such security as the board may require pursuant to Section 8951, the board shall issue to the applicant a use fuel tax permit authorizing the applicant to become a user of fuel taxable under this part. The permit is valid until canceled or revoked.
