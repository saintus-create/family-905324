# RTC § 43054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. The Hazardous Substances Tax > Article: 1. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 43054

---

## Text

The fees imposed pursuant to Section 25205.6 of the Health and Safety Code shall be administered and collected by the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration in accordance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 73, Sec. 89. (SB 158) Effective July 12, 2021.)
