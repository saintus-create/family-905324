# RTC § 24661.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 3. Year of Inclusion

**Citation:** RTC § 24661.5

---

## Text

Section 451(g)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to special election rule, is modified by substituting the phrase “subdivision (b) of Section 24949.1” in lieu of the phrase “section 1033(e)(2)” contained therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 111. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
