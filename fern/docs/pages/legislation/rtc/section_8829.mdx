# RTC § 8829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 4. Jeopardy Determinations

**Citation:** RTC § 8829

---

## Text

Any notice required by this article may be served in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 499, Sec. 27. (SB 1528) Effective January 1, 2025.)
