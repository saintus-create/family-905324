# RTC § 40113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 40113

---

## Text

Every claim shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which the claim is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
