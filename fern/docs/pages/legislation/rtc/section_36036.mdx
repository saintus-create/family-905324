# RTC § 36036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 16. FIREARM, FIREARM PRECURSOR PART, AND AMMUNITION EXCISE TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection and Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 36036

---

## Text

The department shall issue a certificate of registration to each eligible applicant that has properly registered pursuant to Section 36035. A certificate of registration issued pursuant to this section shall be valid until canceled or revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 231, Sec. 7. (AB 28) Effective January 1, 2024.)
