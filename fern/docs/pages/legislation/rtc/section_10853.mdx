# RTC § 10853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 3. Collections and Refunds > Article: 1. Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 10853

---

## Text

Whenever any vehicle is operated upon any highway of this State without the license fee having first been paid as required by this part, the fee is delinquent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 40.)
