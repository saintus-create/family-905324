# RTC § 98.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 6. Allocation of Property Tax Revenue > Article: 4. Tax Equity Allocations for Certain Cities

**Citation:** RTC § 98.03

---

## Text

For purposes of Section 98, the definition of qualifying city contained in subdivision (d) of that section shall not include the City of Foster City.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1167, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
