# RTC § 7287.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3. Graffiti Prevention Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7287.9

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 7287.10, to the extent feasible or practicable, Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 6451), Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 6701), Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 6901), and Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 7051) of Part 1, shall govern determinations, collection of tax, overpayments and refunds, and administration under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1411, Sec. 1.)
