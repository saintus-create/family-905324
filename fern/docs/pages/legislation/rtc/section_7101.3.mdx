# RTC § 7101.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 9. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 7101.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 7101, all revenues, less refunds, derived from the taxes imposed to Sections 6051.5 and 6201.5 shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the Fiscal Recovery Fund, as established pursuant to Section 99008 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 13, Sec. 7. Effective October 28, 2003.)
