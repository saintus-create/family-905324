# RTC § 7201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.5. UNIFORM LOCAL SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 7201

---

## Text

Any county may by action of its board of supervisors adopt a sales and use tax in accordance with the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1311.)
