# RTC § 30131.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax > Article: 3. California Children and Families Trust Fund Account

**Citation:** RTC § 30131.1

---

## Text

The following definitions apply for purposes of this article:

(a) “Cigarette” has the same meaning as in Section 30003, as it read on January 1, 1997.

(b) “Tobacco products” shall have the same meaning as in subdivision (b) of Section 30121, as amended by the California Healthcare, Research and Prevention Tobacco Tax Act of 2016.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 56, Sec. 3.2. Note: This section was added on Nov. 8, 1988, by initiative Prop. 10.)
