# RTC § 2279.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 5. Additional Property Tax Rates

**Citation:** RTC § 2279.1

---

## Text

A local agency may levy, or have levied on its behalf, a rate in addition to the maximum property tax rate established pursuant to this chapter for interest and redemption charges on amounts borrowed pursuant to Section 54902.1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2279 by Stats. 1979, Ch. 516.)
