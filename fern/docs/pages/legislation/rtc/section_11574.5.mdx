# RTC § 11574.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 11574.5

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 808.)
