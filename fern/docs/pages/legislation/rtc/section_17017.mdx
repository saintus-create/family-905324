# RTC § 17017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17017

---

## Text

“United States,” when used in a geographical sense, includes the states, the District of Columbia, and the possessions of the United States.
