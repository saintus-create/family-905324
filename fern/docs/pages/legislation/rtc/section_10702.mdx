# RTC § 10702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 10702

---

## Text

“Vehicle” means every vehicle subject to registration under the Vehicle Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 40.)
