# RTC § 4841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 2. Corrections > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4841

---

## Text

If any error or defect has been carried into any publication, the publication may be republished as amended, or notice of the correction may be given in a supplementary publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
