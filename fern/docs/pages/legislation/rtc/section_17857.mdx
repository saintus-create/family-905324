# RTC § 17857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 10. Partners and Partnerships

**Citation:** RTC § 17857

---

## Text

Section 751(e) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the limitation on tax attributable to deemed sales of Section 1248 stock, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 50. Effective October 10, 1999.)
