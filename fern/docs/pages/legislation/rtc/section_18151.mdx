# RTC § 18151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 14. General Rules for Determining Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 18151

---

## Text

Subchapter P of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to capital gains and losses, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 873, Sec. 29. Effective October 6, 1993.)
