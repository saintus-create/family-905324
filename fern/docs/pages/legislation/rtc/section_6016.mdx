# RTC § 6016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 6016

---

## Text

(a) “Tangible personal property” means either of the following:

(1) Personal property that can be seen, weighed, measured, felt, touched, or is in any other manner perceptible to the senses.

(2) A digital product and any copyright or patent interests associated therewith.

(b) The amendments made this section by the act adding this subdivision shall become operative on January 1, 2027.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 23, Sec. 9. (SB 122) Effective June 29, 2026. Operative January 1, 2027, by its own provisions.)
