# RTC § 43553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 43553

---

## Text

All taxes, interest, and penalties imposed on all amounts of tax required to be paid to the state pursuant to Section 43056 shall be paid to the board in the form of remittances payable to the State Board of Equalization. The board shall transmit the payments to the Treasurer to be deposited into the State Treasury to the credit of the Occupational Lead Poisoning Prevention Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 798, Sec. 8.)
