# RTC § 3691.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3691.5

---

## Text

The tax collector shall file the notice in his or her office and keep a record to show the subsequent disposition of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 497, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1999.)
