# RTC § 60522.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Overpayment and Refunds > Article: 2. Claim for Refunds of Licensed Suppliers, Interstate Users, Exempt Bus Operators, Highway Vehicle Operators, and End Sellers

**Citation:** RTC § 60522.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 60522, a refund of an overpayment of any tax, penalty, or interest collected by the board by means of levy, through the use of liens, or by other enforcement procedures, shall be approved if a claim for a refund is filed within three years of the date of an overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 364, Sec. 41. Effective January 1, 2007.)
