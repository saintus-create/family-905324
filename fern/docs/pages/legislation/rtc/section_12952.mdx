# RTC § 12952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 7. Cancellations and Refunds > Article: 1. Cancellations

**Citation:** RTC § 12952

---

## Text

If the commissioner discovers an amount assessed by the board which he believes to have been illegally assessed, he shall notify the board in writing of such fact, together with a statement of any information he may have concerning the correctness of the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
