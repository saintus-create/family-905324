# RTC § 7388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2.5. Aircraft Jet Fuel Tax > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7388

---

## Text

“Aircraft jet fuel dealer” means any person who sells to an aircraft jet fuel user, aircraft jet fuel delivered in this state into the fuel tanks of aircraft or into a storage facility from which the fuel is withdrawn for use in aircraft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
