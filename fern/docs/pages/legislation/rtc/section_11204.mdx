# RTC § 11204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 11204

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, firm, partnership, joint venture, limited liability company, association, corporation, estate, trust, business trust, receiver, syndicate, or any other group or combination acting as a unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 50. Effective September 30, 1994.)
