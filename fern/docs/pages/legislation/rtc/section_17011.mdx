# RTC § 17011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17011

---

## Text

“Fiscal year” means an accounting period of 12 months ending on the last day of any month other than December.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 939.)
