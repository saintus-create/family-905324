# RTC § 50157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 7. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 50157

---

## Text

All fees, interest, and penalties imposed, and all amounts of fees required to be paid to the state pursuant to Section 50108 shall be paid to the board in the form of remittances payable to the State Board of Equalization of the State of California. The board shall transmit the payments to the Treasurer to be deposited in the Underground Storage Tank Cleanup Fund in the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 350, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
