# RTC § 2858

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 3.3. Collection of Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 2858

---

## Text

The auditor shall foot the amount unpaid on the secured roll, credit the tax collector with the amount, and have a final settlement with him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 239.)
