# RTC § 17131.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17131.13

---

## Text

Gross income does not include any payment received by an individual pursuant to Section 8257 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 40, Sec. 21. (AB 129) Effective July 10, 2023.)
