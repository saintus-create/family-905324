# RTC § 17275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17275

---

## Text

In computing taxable income, no deduction shall be allowed for any of the following:

(a) Abandonment fees paid under Section 51061 or 51093 of the Government Code.

(b) Tax recoupment fees paid under Section 51142 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 29. Effective July 28, 1983.)
