# RTC § 23456.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 2.5. Alternative Minimum Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 23456.5

---

## Text

Section 56A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to adjusted financial statement income, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 82. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
