# RTC § 8129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 8129

---

## Text

The claim shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which it is founded.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 37.)
