# RTC § 17201.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17201.5

---

## Text

Section 181 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to treatment of certain qualified film and television productions, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 20. Effective October 7, 2005.)
