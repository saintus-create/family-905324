# RTC § 11492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection of Tax > Article: 3. Lien of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 11492

---

## Text

The lien upon personal property created by this part attaches as of 12:01 a.m. on the first day of January of each year with respect to taxes, together with the interest and penalties thereon, to be levied during the year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 939, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 939.)
