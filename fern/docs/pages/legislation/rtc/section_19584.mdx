# RTC § 19584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 7. Administration of Tax > Article: 3. Tax Forms

**Citation:** RTC § 19584

---

## Text

The Franchise Tax Board shall include a voter registration card with the Personal Income Tax filing forms that are mailed annually to California taxpayers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 412, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
