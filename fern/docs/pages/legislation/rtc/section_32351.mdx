# RTC § 32351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 1. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 32351

---

## Text

The board may bring such legal actions as are necessary to collect any deficiency in the tax required to be paid, and, upon the board’s request, the Attorney General shall bring the actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
