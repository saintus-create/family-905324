# RTC § 1721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 4. Equalization with Assistance of Appraisal Commission

**Citation:** RTC § 1721

---

## Text

Save assessments by the assessor or valuations of individual parcels by the county board during its authorized sessions, taxable property shall not be appraised for taxation under authority of any county except under this article.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
