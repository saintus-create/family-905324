# RTC § 45705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 45705

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
