# RTC § 32451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 32451

---

## Text

The board shall enforce the provisions of this part and may prescribe, adopt, and enforce rules and regulations relating to the administration and enforcement of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
