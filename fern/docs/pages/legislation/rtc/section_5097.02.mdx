# RTC § 5097.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 5097.02

---

## Text

The claim shall be in writing, specifying:

(a) Whether the whole assessment is claimed to be void or, if only a part, what portion.

(b) The grounds on which the claim is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 499.)
