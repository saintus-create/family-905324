# RTC § 75.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 3.5. Change in Ownership and New Construction After the Lien Date > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 75.8

---

## Text

“New base year value” means the full cash value of property on the date it changes ownership or of new construction on the date it is completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 498, Sec. 133. Effective July 28, 1983.)
