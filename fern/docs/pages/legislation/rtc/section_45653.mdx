# RTC § 45653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 45653

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of the overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
