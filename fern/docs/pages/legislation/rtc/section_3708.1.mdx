# RTC § 3708.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3708.1

---

## Text

Upon execution the tax collector shall immediately record the deed with the county recorder and pay the recording fees. Recording of the deed shall constitute delivery thereof to the grantee named in the deed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3708.5 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 705, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1995.)
