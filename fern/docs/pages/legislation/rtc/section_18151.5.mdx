# RTC § 18151.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 14. General Rules for Determining Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 18151.5

---

## Text

Section 301 of Title III of Division A of the Emergency Economic Stabilization Act of 2008 (Public Law 110-343), relating to gain or loss from sale of certain preferred stock, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 38. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
