# RTC § 6905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 6905

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of any demand against the State on account of overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
