# RTC § 32457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 32457

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 15619 of the Government Code, all information contained in the Vendor’s Report of Beer Shipments into California may be made public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1087, Sec. 47. Effective January 1, 1997.)
