# RTC § 8258.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 8258.3

---

## Text

Nothing in the article limits the board’s authority to examine the books and records of a taxpayer under Section 8253.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 105, Sec. 4. (AB 2009) Effective January 1, 2015.)
