# RTC § 23701z

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Exempt Corporations > Article: 1. Exemptions From This Part

**Citation:** RTC § 23701z

---

## Text

An organization established pursuant to Section 5005.1 of the Corporations Code by three or more corporations as an arrangement for the pooling of self-insured claims or losses of those corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 954, Sec. 41.1. Effective September 26, 1996.)
