# RTC § 8705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 1. User

**Citation:** RTC § 8705

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person whose permit has been canceled or revoked to operate as a user of fuel in this State until he has fully complied with the provisions of this part and has been issued a new permit or his permit has been reinstated.
