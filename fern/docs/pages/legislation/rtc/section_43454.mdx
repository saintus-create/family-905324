# RTC § 43454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 43454

---

## Text

Within 30 days after disallowing any claim, in whole or in part, the board shall serve written notice of its action on the claimant, such service to be made as provided by Section 43201.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
