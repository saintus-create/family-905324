# RTC § 5451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Distribution

**Citation:** RTC § 5451

---

## Text

The revenue derived from any tax levied pursuant to this part shall be distributed as prescribed in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
