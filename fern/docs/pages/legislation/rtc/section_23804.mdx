# RTC § 23804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Tax Treatment of S Corporations and Their Shareholders

**Citation:** RTC § 23804

---

## Text

Section 1367(b)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to adjustments in case of inherited stock, shall apply for decedents dying after December 31, 1996.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 268, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2004.)
