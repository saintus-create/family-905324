# RTC § 23045.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23045.6

---

## Text

(a) For the purposes of this part, except as otherwise provided, the determination of whether an individual is an employee shall be governed by of Article 1.5 (commencing with Section 2775) of Chapter 2 of Division 3 of the Labor Code.

(b) Section 7701(a)(20) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the definition of “employee,” shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 38, Sec. 6. (AB 2257) Effective September 4, 2020.)
