# RTC § 41007.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 41007.1

---

## Text

“Access line” shall mean any of the following:

(a) A wireline communications service line.

(b) A wireless communications service line.

(c) A VoIP service line, as defined by Section 41016.5 as added by the act adding this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 54, Sec. 5. (SB 96) Effective July 1, 2019.)
