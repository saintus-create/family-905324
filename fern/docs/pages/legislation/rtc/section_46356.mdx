# RTC § 46356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 46356

---

## Text

All amounts determined to be due by the board under Article 2 (commencing with Section 46201) or Article 3 (commencing with Section 46251) are due and payable at the time they become final, and, if not paid when due and payable, a penalty of 10 percent of the amount determined to be due shall be added to the amount due and payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
