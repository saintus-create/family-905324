# RTC § 17278.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17278.5

---

## Text

The deduction allowed by Section 194 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to amortization of reforestation expenditures, shall be available only with respect to qualified timber property located in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 611, Sec. 36. Effective October 3, 1997.)
