# RTC § 5013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 1.5. Cancellation of Assessments on State-Assessed Property

**Citation:** RTC § 5013

---

## Text

The board shall transmit a statement of the cancellation to the auditor of the county or city in which the property is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1329.)
