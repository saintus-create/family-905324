# RTC § 17215.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17215.1

---

## Text

Section 220(f)(5) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to rollover contributions, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 26.5. Effective October 7, 2005.)
