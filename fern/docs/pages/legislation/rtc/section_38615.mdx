# RTC § 38615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 38615

---

## Text

If judgment is rendered for the plaintiff, the amount of the judgment shall first be credited on any timber yield tax or timber reserve fund tax due and payable from the plaintiff. The balance of the judgment shall be refunded to the plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
