# RTC § 7300.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.95. Monterey-Salinas Transit District Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7300.6

---

## Text

If, as of January 1, 2035, an ordinance proposing a transactions and use tax is not approved pursuant to Section 7300.5, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 706, Sec. 2. (AB 761) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed conditionally on January 1, 2035, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.95, commencing with Section 7300.5.)
