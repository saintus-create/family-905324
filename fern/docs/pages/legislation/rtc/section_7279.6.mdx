# RTC § 7279.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.6. TRANSACTIONS AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 4. Refunds of Unconstitutional Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 7279.6

---

## Text

An arbitrary and capricious action of the board in implementing the provisions of this chapter shall be reviewable by writ.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1060, Sec. 2. Effective October 11, 1993.)
