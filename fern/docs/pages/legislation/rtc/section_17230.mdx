# RTC § 17230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17230

---

## Text

Payments made to the California Housing Finance Agency by the borrower pursuant to Section 52514 of the Health and Safety Code shall be considered payments of interest for purposes of Section 163 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 29. Effective July 28, 1983.)
