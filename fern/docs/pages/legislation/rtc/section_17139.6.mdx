# RTC § 17139.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17139.6

---

## Text

Section 139A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to federal subsidies for prescription drug plans, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 12. Effective October 7, 2005.)
