# RTC § 16821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection of Tax > Article: 3. Writ of Execution

**Citation:** RTC § 16821

---

## Text

The writ shall be executed against any property of any person liable for the tax, or against any property subject to the lien of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
