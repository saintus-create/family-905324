# RTC § 43810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22.1. HAZARDOUS SPILL PREVENTION FEE LAW

**Citation:** RTC § 43810

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 55381, for purposes of the fee administered under Section 43807, “department” means the Department of Toxic Substances Control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1223, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1995.)
