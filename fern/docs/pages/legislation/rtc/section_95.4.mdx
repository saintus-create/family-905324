# RTC § 95.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 6. Allocation of Property Tax Revenue > Article: 1. Definitions and Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 95.4

---

## Text

Amounts invoiced pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 95.2 or its predecessor shall not include the amount of any costs incurred by the county auditor pursuant to Section 33672.5 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1167, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
