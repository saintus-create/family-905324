# RTC § 60634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 2. The California Taxpayers’ Bill of Rights

**Citation:** RTC § 60634

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part only, the board shall not revoke or suspend a person’s license pursuant to Section 60180 or 60181 unless the board has mailed a notice preliminary to revocation or suspension that indicates that the taxpayer will be suspended by a date certain pursuant to that section. The notice preliminary to suspension shall be mailed to the taxpayer at least 60 days before the date certain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
