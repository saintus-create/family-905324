# RTC § 46443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 4. Collection of Fee > Article: 5. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 46443

---

## Text

At the sale, the property shall be sold by the board, or by its authorized agent, in accordance with law and the notice, and the board shall deliver to the purchaser a bill of sale for the personal property and a deed for any real property sold. The bill of sale or deed vests title in the purchaser subject to a right of redemption as prescribed in the Code of Civil Procedure upon sales of real property on execution. The unsold portion of any property seized may be left at the place of sale at the risk of the feepayer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
