# RTC § 9302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 8. Distribution of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 9302

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund under this part is hereby appropriated as follows:

(a) To pay the refunds authorized in this part.

(b) To the Highway Users Tax Account in the Transportation Tax Fund as provided in this chapter.
