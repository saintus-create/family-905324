# RTC § 12411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 4. Assessment and Effect of Tax > Article: 2. Initial Assessments

**Citation:** RTC § 12411

---

## Text

A duplicate copy of each tax return received by the commissioner shall be promptly forwarded to the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
