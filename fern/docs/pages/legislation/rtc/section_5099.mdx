# RTC § 5099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 5099

---

## Text

The refund ordered by the board of supervisors may include county taxes and taxes collected by county officers for a city or revenue district.
