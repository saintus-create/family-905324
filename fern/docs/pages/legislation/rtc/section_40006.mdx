# RTC § 40006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 40006

---

## Text

“In this state” means within the exterior limits of the State of Caliornia and includes all territory within those limits owned by or ceded to the United States of America.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
