# RTC § 55121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 4. Collection of Fee > Article: 1. Suit for Fee

**Citation:** RTC § 55121

---

## Text

The board may bring any legal action that is necessary to collect any deficiency in the fee required to be paid, and upon the board’s request, the Attorney General shall bring the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
