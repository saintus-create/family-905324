# RTC § 1252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 6. Assessor’s Office Equipment

**Citation:** RTC § 1252

---

## Text

If the board of supervisors fails to furnish the assessor with the necessary office equipment, then, on the assessor’s application, the State Board of Equalization shall furnish it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
