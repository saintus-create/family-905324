# RTC § 3436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 2. Sale by Operation of Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 3436

---

## Text

At 12:01 a.m. on July 1, the taxes, assessments, penalties, and costs on real property except tax-defaulted property and possessory interests, which have not been paid shall by operation of law be declared in default.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 523, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1993.)
