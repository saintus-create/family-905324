# RTC § 40085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 4. Determinations If No Return Made

**Citation:** RTC § 40085

---

## Text

Promptly after making its determination the department shall give to the person written notice of the estimate, determination, and penalty, the notice to be served in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 499, Sec. 69. (SB 1528) Effective January 1, 2025.)
