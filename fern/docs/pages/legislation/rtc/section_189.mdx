# RTC § 189

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Disaster Relief

**Citation:** RTC § 189

---

## Text

Each eligible county shall make every reasonable effort to inform eligible property owners of the benefits provided by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 16, Sec. 3. Effective March 11, 1986.)
