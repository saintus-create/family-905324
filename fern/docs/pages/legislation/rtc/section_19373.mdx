# RTC § 19373

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19373

---

## Text

In the action a writ of attachment may be issued in the manner provided by Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 485.010) of Title 6.5 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure without the showing required by Section 485.010 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
