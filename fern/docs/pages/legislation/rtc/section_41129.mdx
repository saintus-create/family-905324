# RTC § 41129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Regulations, Records and Reports

**Citation:** RTC § 41129

---

## Text

Every service supplier or seller in this state shall keep such records pertaining thereto in such form as the department may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 54, Sec. 35. (SB 96) Effective July 1, 2019.)
