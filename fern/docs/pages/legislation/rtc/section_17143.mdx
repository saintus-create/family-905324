# RTC § 17143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17143

---

## Text

Sections 103 and 141 to 150, inclusive, of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to interest on governmental obligations, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 25. Effective October 10, 1999.)
