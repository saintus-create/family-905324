# RTC § 9252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 9252

---

## Text

The board may employ accountants, auditors, investigators, assistants, and clerks necessary for the efficient administration of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 38.)
