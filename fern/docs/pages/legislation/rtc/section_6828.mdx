# RTC § 6828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 6. Collection of Tax > Article: 8. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 6828

## Text

In all proceedings under this chapter the board may act on behalf of the people of the State of California.
