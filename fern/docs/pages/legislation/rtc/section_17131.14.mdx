# RTC § 17131.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17131.14

---

## Text

Gross income does not include any payment received from the California Victim Compensation Board pursuant to Sections 13955 and 13970 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 119, Sec. 1. (AB 2979) Effective July 15, 2024.)
