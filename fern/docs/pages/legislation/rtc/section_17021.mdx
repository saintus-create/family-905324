# RTC § 17021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17021

---

## Text

As used in this part, if the spouses therein referred to are divorced or their registered domestic partnership has been terminated, wherever appropriate to the meaning of this part, the term “spouse” shall be read “former spouse.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 95. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
