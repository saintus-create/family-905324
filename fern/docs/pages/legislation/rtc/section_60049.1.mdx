# RTC § 60049.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60049.1

---

## Text

“Vessel operator” means any person that operates or otherwise controls a vessel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 48. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
