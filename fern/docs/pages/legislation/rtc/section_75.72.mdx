# RTC § 75.72

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 3.5. Change in Ownership and New Construction After the Lien Date > Article: 7. Disposition of Revenues

**Citation:** RTC § 75.72

---

## Text

Any penalties, costs, or other charges resulting from delinquency of supplemental taxes shall be distributed pursuant to Part 8 (commencing with Section 4651).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 106, Sec. 118.)
