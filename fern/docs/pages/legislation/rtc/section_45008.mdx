# RTC § 45008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 45008

---

## Text

“In this state” means within the exterior limits of the State of California and includes all territory within those limits owned by or ceded to the United States of America.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1319, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1987.)
