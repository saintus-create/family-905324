# RTC § 24355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24355

---

## Text

Section 167(f) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to treatment of property excluded from Section 197, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 611, Sec. 83. Effective October 3, 1997.)
