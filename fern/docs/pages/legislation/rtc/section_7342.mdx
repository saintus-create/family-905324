# RTC § 7342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7342

---

## Text

“Train operator” includes any person that owns, operates, or controls a train and is licensed as a railroad by a state or federal agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 306, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2009.)
