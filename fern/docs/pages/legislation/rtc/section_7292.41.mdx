# RTC § 7292.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.74. Local Government Finance in the County of Santa Clara

**Citation:** RTC § 7292.41

---

## Text

If, as of December 31, 2028, an ordinance proposing a transactions and use tax has not been approved in the County of Santa Clara as required by subdivision (a) of Section 7292.4, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 391, Sec. 1. (SB 335) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed conditionally on December 31, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.74, commencing with Section 7292.4.)
