# RTC § 30005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 30005

---

## Text

“Untaxed cigarette” means any cigarette which has not yet been distributed in such manner as to result in a tax liability under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
