# RTC § 6826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 6. Collection of Tax > Article: 8. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 6826

---

## Text

The board shall report to the Controller the amount of collections under this part, and he shall keep a record thereof.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
