# RTC § 67

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 2. Change in Ownership and Purchase

**Citation:** RTC § 67

---

## Text

“Purchased” or “purchase” means a change in ownership for consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 242.)
