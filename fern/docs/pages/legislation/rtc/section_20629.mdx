# RTC § 20629

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 3. Senior Citizens Tenant-Stockholder Property Tax Postponement Law

**Citation:** RTC § 20629

---

## Text

Property taxes means the amount representing the claimant’s proportionate share of real estate taxes for the fiscal year for which postponement is claimed, determined in accord with the method prescribed in Section 216(b) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 90.5. Effective July 28, 1983.)
