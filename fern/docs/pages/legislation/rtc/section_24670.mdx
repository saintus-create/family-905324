# RTC § 24670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 3. Year of Inclusion

**Citation:** RTC § 24670

---

## Text

The amendments to Section 453B(e) of the Internal Revenue Code as enacted by Section 13512(b)(1) of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, 2017 (Public Law 115-97), relating to the repeal of the small life insurance company deduction, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 113. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
