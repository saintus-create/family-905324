# RTC § 24913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 2. Basis for Computation of Gain or Loss

**Citation:** RTC § 24913

---

## Text

If the property should have been included in the last inventory, the basis shall be the last inventory value thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 938.)
