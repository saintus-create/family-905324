# RTC § 5452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Distribution

**Citation:** RTC § 5452

---

## Text

If the aircraft are habitually based in a city and any school districts, the proceeds shall be distributed one-third to the city, one-third to the school districts, and one-third to the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
