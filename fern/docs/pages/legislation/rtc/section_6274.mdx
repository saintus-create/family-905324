# RTC § 6274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 3.5. Vehicles, Vessels and Aircraft > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 6274

---

## Text

“Aircraft” means any contrivance designed for powered navigation in the air except a rocket or missile.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1589, Sec. 9.)
