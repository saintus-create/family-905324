# RTC § 25126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 17. Allocation of Income > Article: 2. Uniform Division of Income for Tax Purposes Act

**Citation:** RTC § 25126

---

## Text

Interest and dividends are allocable to this state if the taxpayer’s commercial domicile is in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, Ch. 2.)
