# RTC § 11429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Corrections

**Citation:** RTC § 11429

---

## Text

If the amount of the tax is increased the additional tax shall be paid to the board on or before December 10th following the levy of the tax, or on or before the fifteenth day following the date of mailing the statement, whichever is the later.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 994.)
