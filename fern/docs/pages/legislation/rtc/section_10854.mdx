# RTC § 10854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 3. Collections and Refunds > Article: 1. Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 10854

---

## Text

Penalties for failure to pay license fees before delinquency are as specified in Section 9554 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1126, Sec. 3. Effective September 28, 1985. Operative April 1, 1986, by Sec. 12 of Ch. 1126.)
