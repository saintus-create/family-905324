# RTC § 46706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 8. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 46706

---

## Text

Any prosecution for violation of any of the penal provisions of this part shall be instituted within three years after the commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
