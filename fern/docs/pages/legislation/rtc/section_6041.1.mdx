# RTC § 6041.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1.7. Marketplace Facilitator Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 6041.1

---

## Text

Newspapers, internet websites, and other entities that advertise tangible personal property for sale, refer purchasers to the seller by telephone, internet link, or other similar means to complete the sale, and do not participate further in the sale are not facilitating a sale under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 34, Sec. 2. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2019. Section operative October 1, 2019, pursuant to Sec. 6049.5.)
