# RTC § 60025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60025

---

## Text

“Gallon” means the United States gallon of 231 cubic inches or the volumetric gallon adjusted to 60 degrees Fahrenheit when the invoice and settlement are made on the temperature corrected gallonage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 40. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
