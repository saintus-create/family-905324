# RTC § 43502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 43502

---

## Text

The board may make such examinations of the books and records of any taxpayer as it may deem necessary in carrying out the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
