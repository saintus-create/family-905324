# RTC § 50152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 50152

---

## Text

The board shall enforce this part and may adopt, and enforce rules and regulations relating to the administration and enforcement of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
