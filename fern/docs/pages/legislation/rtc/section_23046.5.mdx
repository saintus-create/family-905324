# RTC § 23046.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23046.5

---

## Text

(a) Section 7701(n) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to convention or association of churches, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(b) The phrase “this part” shall be substituted for “this title” in Section 7701(n) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 56. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
