# RTC § 18008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 12. Credit for Taxes Paid

**Citation:** RTC § 18008

---

## Text

A tax equal to the credit allowed for the taxes credited or refunded by the other state is due and payable from the taxpayer upon notice and demand from the Franchise Tax Board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 215.)
