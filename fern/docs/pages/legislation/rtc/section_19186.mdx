# RTC § 19186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 4. Payments and Assessments > Article: 7. Penalties and Additions to Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19186

---

## Text

(a) Section 6720B of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the fraudulent identification of exempt use property, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(b) This section shall apply to identifications made after January 1, 2011.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 52. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
