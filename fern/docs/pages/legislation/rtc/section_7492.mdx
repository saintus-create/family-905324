# RTC § 7492

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Licenses and Bonds > Article: 5. Denial of License

**Citation:** RTC § 7492

---

## Text

Before the refusal, the board shall grant the applicant a hearing and shall give him or her at least 10 days’ written notice of the time and place thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
