# RTC § 10857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 3. Collections and Refunds > Article: 1. Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 10857

---

## Text

No penalty fee shall be assessed for the delinquent payment of a vehicle license fee, when subsequent to the date on which the fee became due, the vehicle is repossessed on behalf of any legal owner, if the license fee is paid within 60 days of taking possession.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 200, Sec. 1.)
