# RTC § 13621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 4. Writ of Execution

**Citation:** RTC § 13621

---

## Text

The writ shall be executed against any property of the person liable for payment of the tax, or against any property subject to the lien of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
