# RTC § 30176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Stamps and Meter Machines > Article: 4. Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 30176

---

## Text

The board shall, pursuant to regulations prescribed by it, refund or credit to a distributor the denominated values, less the discount given on their purchase of any unused stamps or meter register settings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 884.)
