# RTC § 7294.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.82. Local Government Finance in the Cities within the County of Solano

**Citation:** RTC § 7294.4

---

## Text

If, as of January 1, 2029, an ordinance or citizens’ initiative proposing a transactions and use tax is not approved by any city within the County of Solano, as required by subdivision (a) of Section 7294.3, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 852, Sec. 4. (AB 3259) Effective September 28, 2024. Conditionally repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.82, commencing with Section 7294.3.)
