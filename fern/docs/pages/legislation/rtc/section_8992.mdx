# RTC § 8992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 3. Lien of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 8992

---

## Text

The lien arising under Section 8991 attaches at the time a vehicle is operated in this state through the use of fuel taxable under this part.
