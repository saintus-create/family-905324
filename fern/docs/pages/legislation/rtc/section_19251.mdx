# RTC § 19251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 19251

---

## Text

The remedies of the state provided for in this chapter are cumulative, and no action taken by the Franchise Tax Board constitutes an election by the state to pursue any remedy to the exclusion of any other remedy for which provision is made in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
