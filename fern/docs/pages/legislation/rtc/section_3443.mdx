# RTC § 3443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 2. Sale by Operation of Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 3443

---

## Text

The tax collector shall transmit the list of property to the assessor who shall enter on his or her records the fact and date of the declaration of default.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 988, Sec. 26. Effective September 11, 1984.)
