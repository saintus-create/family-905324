# RTC § 46028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46028

---

## Text

“Tanker” means a self-propelled waterborne vessel, constructed or adapted for the carriage of oil in bulk or in commercial quantities as cargo.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 115, Sec. 92. (AB 148) Effective July 22, 2021.)
