# RTC § 30017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 30017

---

## Text

“Wholesale cost” means the cost of tobacco products to the distributor prior to any discounts or trade allowances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 634, Sec. 7. Effective September 21, 1989.)
