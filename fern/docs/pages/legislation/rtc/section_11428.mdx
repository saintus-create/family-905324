# RTC § 11428

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Corrections

**Citation:** RTC § 11428

---

## Text

The date and nature of the correction shall be entered in the records of the board and a statement of the correction of the assessment shall be mailed to the assessee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 994.)
