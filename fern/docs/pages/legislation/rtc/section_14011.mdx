# RTC § 14011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 8. Court Jurisdiction and Procedure Generally > Article: 2. Court Procedure Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 14011

---

## Text

Nothing in this article precludes the state from any relief provided for in this part which may be inconsistent with the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
