# RTC § 100.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 6. Allocation of Property Tax Revenue > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 100.2

---

## Text

Supplemental property tax revenues for 1985–86 and each year thereafter, generated by Sections 75 to 75.80, inclusive, shall be apportioned using the property tax apportionment factors for the current year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1167, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
