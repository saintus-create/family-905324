# RTC § 7075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 2. Tax Amnesty Program

**Citation:** RTC § 7075

---

## Text

Any taxpayer who has an existing installment payment agreement under Section 6832 as of the start of the amnesty program, and who does not participate in the amnesty program, may not be subject to the penalty imposed under Section 7074.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 226, Sec. 6. Effective August 16, 2004.)
