# RTC § 60340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Determinations and Payments > Article: 4. Jeopardy Determinations and Weekly Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 60340

---

## Text

Any notice required by this article may be served in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.
