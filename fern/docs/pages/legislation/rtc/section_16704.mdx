# RTC § 16704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 16704

---

## Text

“Federal generation-skipping transfer tax” means the tax imposed by Chapter 13 of Subtitle B of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1138, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1987.)
