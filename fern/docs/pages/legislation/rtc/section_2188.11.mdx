# RTC § 2188.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 2. Effect of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 2188.11

---

## Text

The assessor shall separately assess undivided interests in accordance with Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 2801) of Part 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1222, Sec. 13.5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
