# RTC § 7345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7345

---

## Text

“Tax-paid fuel” or “tax paid” means the gallons of motor vehicle fuel acquired on either a temperature corrected or volumetric basis on which the tax in Section 7360 has been imposed at the time of or prior to the acquisition by the supplier or person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 18. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
