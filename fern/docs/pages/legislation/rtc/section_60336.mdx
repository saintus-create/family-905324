# RTC § 60336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Determinations and Payments > Article: 4. Jeopardy Determinations and Weekly Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 60336

---

## Text

Whenever any supplier who is required to pay tax in weekly installments as provided by Section 60334 fails to make a weekly return or to pay the full amount in accordance with the terms and conditions prescribed by the board, the supplier’s license may be revoked forthwith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
