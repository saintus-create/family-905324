# RTC § 30222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 3. Determinations If No Report or Return Made

**Citation:** RTC § 30222

---

## Text

In making a determination the board may offset overpayments for a month or months against underpayments for another month or months and against interest and penalties on the underpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
