# RTC § 24456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 24456

---

## Text

Section 306(f) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to source of gain, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 117, Sec. 82. Effective July 16, 1991.)
