# RTC § 30165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Stamps and Meter Machines > Article: 1. Use of Stamps and Impressions

**Citation:** RTC § 30165

---

## Text

The board by regulation may provide that the tax imposed by this part with respect to cigarettes shall be paid without the use of stamps or meter impressions in connection with a particular type of transaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 884.)
