# RTC § 41117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 6. Collection of Tax > Article: 1. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 41117

---

## Text

In the action a writ of attachment may issue, and no affidavit previous to the issuing of the attachment is required.
