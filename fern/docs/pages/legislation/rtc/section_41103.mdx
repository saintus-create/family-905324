# RTC § 41103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 41103

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of any demand against the state on account of overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 443.)
