# RTC § 6358.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 1. General Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 6358.2

---

## Text

There are exempted from the taxes imposed by this part the gross receipts from the sale of and the storage, use, or other consumption in this state of wood shavings, sawdust, rice hulls, or other products that are used as litter in poultry and egg production and that are ultimately resold as, or incorporated into, fertilizer products.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 199. Effective January 1, 1995.)
