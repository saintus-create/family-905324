# RTC § 2783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 2.3. Return of Replicated Property Tax Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 2783

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not be construed as prohibiting a tax collector’s exercise of reasonable judgment in applying a payment for which the wrong payment stub has been returned by the payor or in any case in which no indication of intended application has been made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1201, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1986.)
