# RTC § 32403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 8. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 32403

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of the overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
