# RTC § 8751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 1. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 8751

---

## Text

The excise tax imposed by this part is due and payable quarterly on or before the last day of the calendar month next succeeding each quarterly period in which a taxable use of fuel occurs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1996.)
