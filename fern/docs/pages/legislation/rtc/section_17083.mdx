# RTC § 17083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 2. Items Specifically Included in Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17083

---

## Text

Section 85 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to unemployment compensation, shall not apply.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 14. Effective October 10, 1999.)
