# RTC § 1816

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 2. Equalization by State Board of Equalization > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 1816

---

## Text

When valuing property other than “state-assessed property” as defined in Section 108, the board shall be subject to the same valuation considerations and methods applicable to assessors as provided by this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 327, Sec. 146. Effective June 30, 1982.)
