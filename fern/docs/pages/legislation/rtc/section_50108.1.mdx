# RTC § 50108.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 2. The Underground Storage Tank Fee > Article: 2. Registration and Security

**Citation:** RTC § 50108.1

---

## Text

Every person who is an owner of an underground storage tank for which a permit is required pursuant to Section 25284 of the Health and Safety Code containing petroleum shall register with the board on forms provided by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 639, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 1996.)
