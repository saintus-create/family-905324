# RTC § 18632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 2. Returns > Article: 4. Information Returns

**Citation:** RTC § 18632

---

## Text

Division 6 (commencing with Section 13000) of the Unemployment Insurance Code sets forth provisions administered by the Employment Development Department relating to the reporting, collection, refunding to the employer, and enforcement of taxes required to be withheld from wages paid by employers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
