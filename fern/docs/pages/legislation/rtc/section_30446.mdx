# RTC § 30446

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 7.5. Seizure and Forfeiture

**Citation:** RTC § 30446

---

## Text

If a timely answer has been filed, the proceeding shall be set for hearing on a day within 30 days after the expiration of the period for filing of an answer. Notice of the hearing shall be given by the clerk of the court to each person filing an answer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 963.)
