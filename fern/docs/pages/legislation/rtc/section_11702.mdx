# RTC § 11702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 7. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 11702

---

## Text

The money so deposited, or so much thereof as may be necessary, is hereby appropriated for the purpose of making refunds under this part and shall, upon warrant of the Controller, be withdrawn from the State Treasury for such purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 41.)
