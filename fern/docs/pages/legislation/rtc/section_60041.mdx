# RTC § 60041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60041

---

## Text

“Train operator” includes any person that owns, operates, or controls a diesel-powered train and is licensed as a railroad by a state or federal agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 76, Sec. 10. Effective July 16, 1997.)
