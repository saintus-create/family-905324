# RTC § 19592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 7. Administration of Tax > Article: 4. Tax Service Fees

**Citation:** RTC § 19592

---

## Text

All fees received by the Franchise Tax Board under this article shall be treated as reimbursement for the board’s costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 226, Sec. 10. Effective August 16, 2004.)
