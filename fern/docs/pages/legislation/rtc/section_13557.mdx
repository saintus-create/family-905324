# RTC § 13557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 5. Payment of Tax > Article: 3. Delinquent Tax Bond

**Citation:** RTC § 13557

---

## Text

If the bond is not filed within 20 days after the date of the filing of the order requiring it, the letters of the personal representative affected shall be revoked upon motion of the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
