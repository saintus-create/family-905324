# RTC § 17856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 10. Partners and Partnerships

**Citation:** RTC § 17856

---

## Text

Section 751(d)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to appreciated inventory items subject to tax as a gain on foreign investment company stock, does not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 185, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2004.)
