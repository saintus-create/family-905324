# RTC § 24443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 3. Items Not Deductible

**Citation:** RTC § 24443

---

## Text

Section 274 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the disallowance of certain entertainment, gift, travel, etc., expenses, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 35, Sec. 53. Effective May 8, 2002.)
