# RTC § 12106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 2. Ocean Marine Insurance > Article: 2. Basis of Tax for Ocean Marine Insurance

**Citation:** RTC § 12106

---

## Text

In computing the tax of a marine insurer issuing participating policies, there shall not be included in underwriting profit the amounts refunded by the insurer on account of premiums previously paid by its policyholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
