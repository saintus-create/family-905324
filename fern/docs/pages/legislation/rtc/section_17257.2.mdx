# RTC § 17257.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17257.2

---

## Text

Section 179D of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to energy efficient commercial buildings deduction, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 14, Sec. 23. (SB 401) Effective January 1, 2011.)
