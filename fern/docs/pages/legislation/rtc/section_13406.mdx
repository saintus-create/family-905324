# RTC § 13406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 13406

---

## Text

“Transferee” means any person to whom a transfer is made, and includes any legatee, devisee, heir, next of kin, grantee, donee, vendee, assignee, successor, survivor, or beneficiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
