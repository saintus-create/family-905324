# RTC § 42107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 21.1. Local Prepaid Mobile Telephony Services Collection Act

**Citation:** RTC § 42107

---

## Text

A local jurisdiction or local agency shall pay to the department its pro rata share of the department’s cost of collection and administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 179, Sec. 13. (SB 1441) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 42111.)
