# RTC § 413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.3. Assessment of Implements of Husbandry

**Citation:** RTC § 413

---

## Text

In assessing the implement of husbandry, the county assessor shall determine the value of the implement in accordance with standards and guides to the full cash value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 973.)
