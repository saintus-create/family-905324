# RTC § 17020.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17020.9

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the term “domestic building and loan association” has the same meaning given that term by Section 7701(a)(19) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1138, Sec. 9. Effective September 25, 1987. Applicable to taxable years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 189 of Ch. 1138.)
