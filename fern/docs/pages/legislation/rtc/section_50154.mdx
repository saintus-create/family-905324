# RTC § 50154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 50154

---

## Text

The board may employ accountants, auditors, investigators, and other expert and clerical assistance necessary to enforce its powers and perform its duties under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
