# RTC § 32432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 8. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 32432

---

## Text

In any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 32431, the court may, with the consent of the Attorney General, order a change in the place of trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1999.)
