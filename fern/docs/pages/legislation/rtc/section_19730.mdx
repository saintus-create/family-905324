# RTC § 19730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 9.1. Tax Amnesty

**Citation:** RTC § 19730

---

## Text

The Franchise Tax Board shall administer a tax amnesty program for taxpayers subject to Part 10 (commencing with Section 17001) and Part 11 (commencing with Section 23001), as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 226, Sec. 11. Effective August 16, 2004.)
