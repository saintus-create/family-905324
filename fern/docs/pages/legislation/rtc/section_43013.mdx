# RTC § 43013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 43013

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “feepayer” has the same meaning as taxpayer, as defined in Section 43012.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1123, Sec. 15. Effective October 14, 1991.)
