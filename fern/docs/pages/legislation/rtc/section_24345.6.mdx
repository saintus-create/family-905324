# RTC § 24345.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24345.6

---

## Text

A deduction shall not be allowed for the excise tax imposed by Section 4501 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to repurchase of corporate stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 90. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
