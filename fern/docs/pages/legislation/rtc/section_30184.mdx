# RTC § 30184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 1. Reports and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 30184

---

## Text

The distributor shall submit with each report or return a remittance payable to the board for the amount of tax due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 634, Sec. 27. Effective September 21, 1989.)
