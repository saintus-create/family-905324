# RTC § 5718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5718

---

## Text

“Nonactive racehorse” means any racehorse which has not participated in any horseracing contest on which parimutuel wagering was permitted during the previous calendar year, except a racehorse three years of age or younger.
