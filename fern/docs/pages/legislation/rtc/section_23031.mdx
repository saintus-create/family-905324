# RTC § 23031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23031

---

## Text

“Franchise Tax Board” means the Franchise Tax Board described in Part 10 (commencing with Section 15700) of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

“Board” means the State Board of Equalization.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 193, Sec. 116.)
