# RTC § 3104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 6. Unsecured Roll Summary Judgment

**Citation:** RTC § 3104

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provisions of law relating to interest authorized or allowed as a result of any judgment duly entered, the additional penalty provided for in this division shall be imposed in lieu of any such judgment interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 911.)
