# RTC § 55041.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 1. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 55041.1

---

## Text

The board may require the payment of the amount due and the filing of returns for periods other than the period or periods set forth in the tax and fee laws administered under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 545, Sec. 5. (AB 1547) Effective January 1, 2010.)
