# RTC § 17056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 17056

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, the term “dependents” has the same meaning as that term is defined by Section 152 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 15. Effective July 28, 1983.)
