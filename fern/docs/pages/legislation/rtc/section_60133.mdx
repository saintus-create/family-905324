# RTC § 60133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 1. Licenses for Suppliers

**Citation:** RTC § 60133

---

## Text

Before granting a license authorizing a person to engage in business as a supplier, the board may require the person to file with the board security pursuant to Section 60401. The license issued to any supplier is not transferable and is valid until canceled or revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
