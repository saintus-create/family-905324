# RTC § 5903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 14. PROPERTY TAXPAYERS' BILL OF RIGHTS

**Citation:** RTC § 5903

---

## Text

“Advocate” as used in this part means the “Property Taxpayers’ Advocate” designated pursuant to Section 5904.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 387, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1994.)
