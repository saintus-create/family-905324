# RTC § 12425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 4. Assessment and Effect of Tax > Article: 3. Deficiency Assessments

**Citation:** RTC § 12425

---

## Text

One or more deficiency assessments may be proposed or made for the amount of tax due for one or for more than one calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
