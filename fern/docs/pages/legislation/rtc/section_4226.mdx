# RTC § 4226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7. REDEMPTION > Chapter: 3. Redemption After Payment of Delinquent Taxes in Installments > Article: 2. Permanent Installment Plan

## Text
