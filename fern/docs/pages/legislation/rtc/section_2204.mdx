# RTC § 2204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2204

---

## Text

“Classification of property” means any enumeration or grouping of property by a statute which results in it being treated differently from other property for purposes of taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 358.)
