# RTC § 460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 2. Information From Taxpayer

**Citation:** RTC § 460

---

## Text

If the owner or claimant of any property, not listed by another person, is absent or unknown, the assessor shall estimate its value.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
