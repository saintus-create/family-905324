# RTC § 680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 8.5. Assessment Analyst Certificates

**Citation:** RTC § 680

---

## Text

This article shall only apply in counties or cities and counties that have passed a resolution upon the recommendation of the assessor to require certification as provided by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 446, Sec. 3. (AB 1534) Effective January 1, 2016.)
