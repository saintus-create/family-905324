# RTC § 40009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 40009

---

## Text

“Consumer” means any person receiving for consumption electrical energy furnished by an electric utility and includes a person receiving electrical energy for redistribution for the use of his tenants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
