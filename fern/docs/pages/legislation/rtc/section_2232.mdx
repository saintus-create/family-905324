# RTC § 2232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 2232

---

## Text

Any funds received by a local agency or school district pursuant to the provisions of this chapter may be used for any public purpose.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1256, Sec. 9.)
