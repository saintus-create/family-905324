# RTC § 30481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 10. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 30481

---

## Text

Any prosecution for violation of any of the penal provisions of this part shall be instituted within six years after commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 890, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2004.)
