# RTC § 13404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 13404

---

## Text

“Transfer” means the inclusion of any property or other interest included in the gross estate of a decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 363, Sec. 4. Effective September 8, 2000.)
