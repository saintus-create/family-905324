# RTC § 2960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 2. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 2960

---

## Text

On payment of the price bid for property sold, the delivery of the property with a bill of sale vests title in the purchaser.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2918 by Stats. 1974, Ch. 908.)
