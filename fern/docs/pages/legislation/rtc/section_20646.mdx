# RTC § 20646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 20646

---

## Text

Unless otherwise specifically provided, the provisions of any law effecting changes in this part shall be applied with respect to claims filed for property tax assistance for fiscal years beginning after enactment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1242.)
