# RTC § 1753.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1.5. Multijurisdictional Assessment Appeals Boards > Article: 4. Organization

**Citation:** RTC § 1753.2

---

## Text

Members of the board of supervisors are ineligible to serve as members or alternates to the multijurisdictional assessment appeals board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. (SB 447) Effective January 1, 2018. Repealed on January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 1756.)
