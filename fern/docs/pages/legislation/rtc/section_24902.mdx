# RTC § 24902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 1. Computation of Gain or Loss

**Citation:** RTC § 24902

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this part, on the sale or exchange of property the entire amount of the gain or loss, determined under Section 24901, shall be recognized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 938.)
