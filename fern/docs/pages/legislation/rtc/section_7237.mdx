# RTC § 7237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.55. MOTOR CARRIERS > Chapter: 2. Motor Carrier Safety Improvement Fund

**Citation:** RTC § 7237

---

## Text

This chapter is enacted for the purpose of creating a special fund to cover the costs to the Department of the California Highway Patrol to deter commercial motor vehicle cargo thefts and provide security of highway carriers and cargoes throughout the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1042, Sec. 48. Effective September 29, 1996.)
