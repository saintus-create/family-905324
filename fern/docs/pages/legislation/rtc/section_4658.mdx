# RTC § 4658

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 8. DISTRIBUTION > Chapter: 1d. Delinquent Unsecured Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 4658

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “taxes” includes all taxes and assessments charged on the delinquent unsecured roll or abstract list of delinquent unsecured taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 96.)
