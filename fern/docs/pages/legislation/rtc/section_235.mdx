# RTC § 235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 1. Taxable and Exempt Property

**Citation:** RTC § 235

---

## Text

For the purposes of this division, the lessee of tangible personal property owned by a bank or financial corporation shall be conclusively presumed the owner of that property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1457, Sec. 8.)
