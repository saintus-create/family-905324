# RTC § 44001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22.5

**Citation:** RTC § 44001

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “department” means the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 156. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
