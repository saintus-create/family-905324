# RTC § 7300.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.94. Local Government Finance in the County of Humboldt

**Citation:** RTC § 7300.4

---

## Text

If an ordinance proposing the transactions and use tax authorized by this chapter is not approved pursuant to Section 7300.3, this chapter shall be repealed on January 1, 2027.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 572, Sec. 1. (AB 1256) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed conditionally on January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.94, commencing with Section 7300.3.)
