# RTC § 19150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 4. Payments and Assessments > Article: 7. Penalties and Additions to Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19150

---

## Text

The application of Sections 19142 to 19151, inclusive, to taxable years of less than 12 months shall be in accordance with regulations prescribed by the Franchise Tax Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 862, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2001.)
