# RTC § 1251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 6. Assessor’s Office Equipment

**Citation:** RTC § 1251

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall furnish the assessor with the necessary office equipment, consisting of proper books, blanks, maps, office room, furniture, and stationery.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
