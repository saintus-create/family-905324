# RTC § 43511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 2. The California Taxpayers’ Bill of Rights

## Text

The board shall administer this article. Unless the context indicates otherwise, the provisions of this article shall apply to this part.
