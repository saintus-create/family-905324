# RTC § 260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 2. Procedure to Claim Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 260

---

## Text

If any person, claiming any exemption named in this article, fails to follow the required procedure, the exemption is waived by the person.
