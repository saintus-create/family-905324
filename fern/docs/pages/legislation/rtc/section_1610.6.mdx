# RTC § 1610.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 1610.6

---

## Text

The county board shall neither raise nor lower the entire local roll.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1606 by Stats. 1974, Ch. 180.)
