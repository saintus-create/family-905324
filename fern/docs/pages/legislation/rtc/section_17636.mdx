# RTC § 17636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 7. Exempt Trusts and Common Trust Funds > Article: 1. General Rule

**Citation:** RTC § 17636

---

## Text

Sections 17635 to 17639, inclusive, apply to any organization described in Section 401(a) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 41. Effective July 28, 1983.)
