# RTC § 7391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2.5. Aircraft Jet Fuel Tax > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7391

---

## Text

“Use” means the placing of aircraft jet fuel into the fuel tank of an aircraft in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
