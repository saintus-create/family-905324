# RTC § 6356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 4. Exemptions > Article: 1. General Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 6356

---

## Text

There are exempted from the sales tax the gross receipts from sales of vessels of more than 1,000 tons burden by the builders thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 591, Sec. 4.)
