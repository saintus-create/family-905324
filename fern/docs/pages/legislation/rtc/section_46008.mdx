# RTC § 46008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46008

---

## Text

“Barrel” means 42 gallons of crude oil, petroleum products, or renewable fuel.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 115, Sec. 85. (AB 148) Effective July 22, 2021.)
