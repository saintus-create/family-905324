# RTC § 18505.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 2. Returns > Article: 1. Individuals and Fiduciaries

**Citation:** RTC § 18505.3

---

## Text

If an individual is deceased, the return of that individual required under Section 18501 shall be made by his or her executor, administrator, or other person charged with property of that decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 863, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
