# RTC § 24327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Gross Income > Article: 3. Other Exclusions

**Citation:** RTC § 24327

---

## Text

Section 892 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the tax treatment of foreign governments and international organizations, shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 952, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 1997.)
