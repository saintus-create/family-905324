# RTC § 7202.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.5. UNIFORM LOCAL SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 7202.1

---

## Text

(a) For the purposes of a tax adopted under this part, any retail sale of a digital product transferred electronically or accessed remotely is subject to the sourcing rule established pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 6010.5.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2027.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 23, Sec. 20. (SB 122) Effective June 29, 2026. Operative January 1, 2027, by its own provisions.)
