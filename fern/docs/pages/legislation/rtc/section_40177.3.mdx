# RTC § 40177.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Regulations, Records and Reports

**Citation:** RTC § 40177.3

---

## Text

Nothing in the article limits the board’s authority to examine the records, meters, and equipment of an electric utility under Section 40174.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 105, Sec. 24. (AB 2009) Effective January 1, 2015.)
