# RTC § 11934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6.7. DOCUMENTARY TRANSFER TAX ACT > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 11934

---

## Text

Claims for refunds of taxes imposed pursuant to this part shall be governed by the provisions of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 5096) of Part 9 of Division 1 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11935 by Stats. 1968, Ch. 17.)
