# RTC § 60

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 2. Change in Ownership and Purchase

**Citation:** RTC § 60

---

## Text

A “change in ownership” means a transfer of a present interest in real property, including the beneficial use thereof, the value of which is substantially equal to the value of the fee interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 242.)
