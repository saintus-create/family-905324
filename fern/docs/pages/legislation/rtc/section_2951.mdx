# RTC § 2951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 2. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 2951

---

## Text

Taxes due on unsecured property may be collected by seizure and sale of any of the following property belonging or assessed to the assessee:

(a) Personal property.

(b) Improvements.

(c) Possessory interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2914 by Stats. 1974, Ch. 908.)
