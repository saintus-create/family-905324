# RTC § 30006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 30006

---

## Text

“Sale” includes any transfer of title or possession for a consideration, exchange or barter, in any manner or by any means whatever.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
