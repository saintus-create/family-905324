# RTC § 30110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax > Article: 1. Tax on Distributors

**Citation:** RTC § 30110

---

## Text

Any claim for exemption from tax under this part shall be made to the board in such manner as the board shall prescribe.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
