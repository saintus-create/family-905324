# RTC § 7288.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.5. Local Public Finance

**Citation:** RTC § 7288.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, a “school district” includes a community college district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1024, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1992. See Section 7288.6 for restriction on operative date of tax.)
