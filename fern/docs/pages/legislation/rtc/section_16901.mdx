# RTC § 16901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 7. Administration > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 16901

---

## Text

The Controller may employ such assistants, including attorneys, as may from time to time be necessary for the proper administration of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
