# RTC § 12833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Suspension of Rights of Delinquent Insurers > Article: 2. Suspended Corporate Insurers

**Citation:** RTC § 12833

---

## Text

The county in which occurs any part of the attempted exercise of the powers or any part of the transaction of business has jurisdiction of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 113.)
