# RTC § 4880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 2. Corrections > Article: 2. Errors on the Board Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 4880

---

## Text

The auditor shall enter the correction on the roll of the county or city opposite the description of property, and shall file and preserve the statement of the correction as a public record. The auditor shall make any necessary changes in his account with the tax collector.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1943, Ch. 409.)
