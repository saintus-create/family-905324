# RTC § 3366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 1. Publication of Delinquent List and Notice of Sale > Article: 1.5. Deeds to the State

**Citation:** RTC § 3366

---

## Text

The mailed notice shall show the same information required for the published notice in Section 3362. A copy of the published notice may be mailed in lieu of a separate notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 988, Sec. 15. Effective September 11, 1984.)
