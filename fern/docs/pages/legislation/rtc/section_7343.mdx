# RTC § 7343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 7343

---

## Text

“Vessel” means a waterborne vessel used for transporting motor vehicle fuel.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 16. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
