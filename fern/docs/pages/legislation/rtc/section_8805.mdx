# RTC § 8805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 3. Determinations if No Return Made

**Citation:** RTC § 8805

---

## Text

Promptly after making its determination the department shall give to the user written notice of its estimate and determination, the notice to be served in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 499, Sec. 26. (SB 1528) Effective January 1, 2025.)
