# RTC § 9354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 9. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 9354

---

## Text

Any violation of the provisions of this part, except as otherwise provided, is a misdemeanor and is punishable as such.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 38.)
