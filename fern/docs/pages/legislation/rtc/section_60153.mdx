# RTC § 60153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 3. License for Ultimate Vendor

**Citation:** RTC § 60153

---

## Text

The board shall license an applicant as an ultimate vendor of diesel fuel only if the board determines that the applicant meets the necessary requirements and is satisfied with the filing, deposit, payment, and claim history for all state taxes of the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
