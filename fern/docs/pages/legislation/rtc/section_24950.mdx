# RTC § 24950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 3. Nontaxable Exchanges

**Citation:** RTC § 24950

---

## Text

Section 1035 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to certain exchanges of insurance policies, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 82. Effective October 6, 1993.)
