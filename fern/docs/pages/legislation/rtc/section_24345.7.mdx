# RTC § 24345.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24345.7

---

## Text

A deduction shall not be allowed for the excise tax imposed by Section 5000D of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to designated drugs during noncompliance periods.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 91. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
