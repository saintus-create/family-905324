# RTC § 38573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 6. Collection of Taxes > Article: 8. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 38573

---

## Text

In all proceedings under this chapter the board may act on behalf of the people of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 38563 by Stats. 1985, Ch. 186, Sec. 19.)
