# RTC § 1646.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 1. Equalization by County Board of Equalization > Article: 2. Duties of Auditor

**Citation:** RTC § 1646.1

---

## Text

Upon receiving a statement from the clerk prepared under Section 1614, the auditor shall forthwith correct the roll to reflect the changes made by the county board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 147.)
