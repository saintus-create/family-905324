# RTC § 18152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 14. General Rules for Determining Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 18152

---

## Text

Section 1202 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to 50-percent exclusion for gain from certain small business stock, does not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 92, Sec. 197. (SB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
