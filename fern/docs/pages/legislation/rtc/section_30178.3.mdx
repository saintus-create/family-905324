# RTC § 30178.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Stamps and Meter Machines > Article: 4. Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 30178.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 30178, a refund of an overpayment of any tax, penalty, or interest collected by the board by means of levy, through the use of liens, or by other enforcement procedures, shall be approved if a claim for a refund is filed within three years of the date of an overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 364, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2007.)
