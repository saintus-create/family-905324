# RTC § 8303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 9. Records

**Citation:** RTC § 8303

---

## Text

All records required by this chapter shall be available at all times for the inspection of the board or its representatives.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 43. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
