# RTC § 32552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 11. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 32552

---

## Text

Any person who wilfully evades or attempts in any manner to evade or defeat the payment of the excise tax imposed by this part is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
