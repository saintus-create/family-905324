# RTC § 7052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 7052

---

## Text

The board may employ accountants, auditors, investigators, assistants, and clerks necessary for the efficient administration of this part and may designate representatives to conduct hearings, prescribe regulations, or perform any other duties imposed by this part or other laws of this State upon the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
