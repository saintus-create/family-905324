# RTC § 13517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 4. Deficiency Determination

**Citation:** RTC § 13517

---

## Text

In the case of a false or fraudulent return or failure to file a return, the Controller may determine the tax at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
