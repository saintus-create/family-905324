# RTC § 60627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 2. The California Taxpayers’ Bill of Rights

**Citation:** RTC § 60627

---

## Text

The board shall develop and implement a program that will evaluate an individual employee’s or officer’s performance with respect to his or her contact with taxpayers. The development and implementation of the program shall be coordinated with the Taxpayers’ Rights Advocate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
