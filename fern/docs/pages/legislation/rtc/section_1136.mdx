# RTC § 1136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 5. Vessels

**Citation:** RTC § 1136

---

## Text

A ferry is a place where passengers and freight are regularly transported by water between two fixed termini under authority of law so to do.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
