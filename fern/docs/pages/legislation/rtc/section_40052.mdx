# RTC § 40052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 1. Due and Payable Dates

**Citation:** RTC § 40052

---

## Text

Amounts due under Section 40051 for which a billing for the electrical energy is issued by an electric utility to the consumer prior to the close of a calendar quarter are payable when due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
