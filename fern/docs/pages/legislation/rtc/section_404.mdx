# RTC § 404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1. General Requirements

**Citation:** RTC § 404

---

## Text

All taxable property, except State assessed property, shall be assessed by the assessing agency of the taxing agency where the property is situated.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
