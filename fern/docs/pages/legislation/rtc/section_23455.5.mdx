# RTC § 23455.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 2.5. Alternative Minimum Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 23455.5

---

## Text

Section 55(e) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to exemption for small corporations, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 322, Sec. 64. Effective August 20, 1998.)
