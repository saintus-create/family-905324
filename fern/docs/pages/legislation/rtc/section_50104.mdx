# RTC § 50104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 50104

---

## Text

The provisions of this part, insofar as they are substantially the same as existing provisions of law relating to the same subject matter, shall be construed as restatements and continuations and not as new enactments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
