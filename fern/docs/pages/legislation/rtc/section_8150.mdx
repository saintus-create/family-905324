# RTC § 8150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 8150

---

## Text

If judgment is rendered for the plaintiff, the amount of the judgment shall first be credited on any taxes due from the plaintiff under this part, Part 3 (commencing with Section 8601), and Part 31 (commencing with Section 60001), and the balance of the judgment shall be refunded to the plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
