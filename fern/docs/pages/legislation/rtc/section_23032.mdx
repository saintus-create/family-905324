# RTC § 23032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23032

---

## Text

“Fiscal year” means an accounting period of twelve months or less ending on the last day of any month other than December.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 557.)
