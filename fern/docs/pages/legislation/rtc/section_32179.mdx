# RTC § 32179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 4. Tax on Beer and Wine > Article: 2. Presumptions and Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 32179

---

## Text

Any claim for exemption from taxes under this article shall be made to the board in such manner as the board shall prescribe.
