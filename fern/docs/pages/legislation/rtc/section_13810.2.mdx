# RTC § 13810.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 6. Tax Compromise

**Citation:** RTC § 13810.2

---

## Text

This article shall be so interpreted and construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law of those states which enact it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
