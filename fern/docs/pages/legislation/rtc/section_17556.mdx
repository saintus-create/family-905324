# RTC § 17556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 6. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting

**Citation:** RTC § 17556

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 442 of the Internal Revenue Code, the estate may change its annual accounting period one time without the approval of the Franchise Tax Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1102, Sec. 22. Effective September 27, 1983.)
