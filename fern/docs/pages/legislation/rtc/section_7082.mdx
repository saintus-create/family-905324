# RTC § 7082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 3. The California Taxpayers’ Bill of Rights

## Text

The board shall administer this article. Unless the context indicates otherwise, the provisions of this article shall apply to this part.
