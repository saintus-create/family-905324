# RTC § 8101.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds on Certain Sales

**Citation:** RTC § 8101.5

---

## Text

No refund of any tax shall be granted on motor vehicle fuel used in propelling an aircraft in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1027, Sec. 8. Effective September 29, 1989. Operative January 1, 1990, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 1027.)
