# RTC § 7286.34

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 2.7. San Diego County Justice Facilities Financing Act

**Citation:** RTC § 7286.34

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall have sole discretion to determine the specific activities and projects financed with revenues generated by the tax. However, those activities and projects shall be limited to the provision, construction, and operation of justice-related facilities, the funding of law enforcement and crime prevention projects and activities, the funding of the costs incurred by the county to conduct the election authorized by Section 7286.32, and the cost of any legal actions incurred by the county related to the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective August 3, 1992.)
