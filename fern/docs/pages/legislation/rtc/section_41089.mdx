# RTC § 41089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 41089

---

## Text

The order or decision of the department upon a petition for redetermination becomes final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 91. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
