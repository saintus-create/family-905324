# RTC § 40035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 2. The Surcharge > Article: 3. Registration

**Citation:** RTC § 40035

---

## Text

Every electric utility selling electrical energy for consumption in this state shall register with the board upon a form prescribed by the board and shall set forth the name under which the utility transacts or intends to transact business and such other information as the board may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
