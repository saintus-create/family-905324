# RTC § 24998

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 5. Wash Sales of Stock or Securities

**Citation:** RTC § 24998

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 1091 and 1092 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to loss from wash sales of stock or securities, and straddles, respectively, shall be applicable for purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 11, Sec. 86. Effective February 19, 1988. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1987, by Sec. 95 of Ch. 11.)
