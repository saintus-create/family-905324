# RTC § 17734.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 9. Estates, Trusts, Beneficiaries, and Decedents

**Citation:** RTC § 17734.6

---

## Text

Section 646 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to tax treatment of electing Alaska Native Settlement Trusts, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 37.6. Effective October 7, 2005.)
