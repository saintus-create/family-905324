# RTC § 10877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 3. Collections and Refunds > Article: 2. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 10877

---

## Text

The department shall collect the fee and any penalty by seizure and sale of the vehicle as provided in Article 6 (commencing with Section 9800) of Chapter 6 of Division 3 of the Vehicle Code, or by appropriate civil action.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 759, Sec. 3.)
