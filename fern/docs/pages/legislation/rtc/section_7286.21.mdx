# RTC § 7286.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 2.6. Calexico Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7286.21

---

## Text

The net proceeds of the tax imposed by Section 7286.20 shall be used exclusively for the Heffernan Memorial Hospital District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 973, Sec. 1.)
