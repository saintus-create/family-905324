# RTC § 12435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 4. Assessment and Effect of Tax > Article: 3. Deficiency Assessments

**Citation:** RTC § 12435

---

## Text

A copy of each notice of a deficiency assessment made by the board shall be transmitted to the commissioner and the Controller. The Controller shall keep an appropriate record of all such assessments and any payments thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 740.)
