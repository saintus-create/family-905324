# RTC § 50126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 4. Collection of Fee > Article: 3. Warrant for Collection

**Citation:** RTC § 50126

---

## Text

The board may pay or advance to the sheriff or marshal, the same fees, commissions, or expenses for services as are provided by law for similar services pursuant to a writ of execution. The board, and not the court, shall approve the fees for publication in a newspaper.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 170. Effective January 1, 1997.)
