# RTC § 23187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. The Corporation Franchise Tax > Article: 3. Tax on Banks and Financial Corporations

**Citation:** RTC § 23187

---

## Text

Upon the request of a taxpayer under this chapter, the tax-collecting officer of a county, city, or other political subdivision of this State, shall furnish an official receipt for real and personal property taxes paid to him setting forth a description of such property, the assessed valuation thereof, the rate of tax, the amount of taxes paid, and the beginning and ending of the year for which the taxes are paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 557.)
