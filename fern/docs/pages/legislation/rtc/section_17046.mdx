# RTC § 17046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 17046

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “surviving spouse” has the same meaning as that term is defined by Section 2(a) of the Internal Revenue Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 106, Sec. 126.)
