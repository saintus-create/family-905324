# RTC § 614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 6. Assessment Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 614

---

## Text

After each assessment of tax-defaulted property the assessor shall enter on the roll the fact that it is tax defaulted and the date of the declaration of default.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 316, Sec. 10.)
