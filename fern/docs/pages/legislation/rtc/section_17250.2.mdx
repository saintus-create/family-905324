# RTC § 17250.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17250.2

---

## Text

Section 170(p) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to special rule for taxpayers who do not elect to itemize deductions, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 38. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
