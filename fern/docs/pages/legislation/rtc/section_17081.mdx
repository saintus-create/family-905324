# RTC § 17081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 2. Items Specifically Included in Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17081

---

## Text

Part II of Subchapter B of Chapter 1 of Subtitle A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to items that are specifically included in gross income, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 873, Sec. 5. Effective October 6, 1993.)
