# RTC § 32174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 4. Tax on Beer and Wine > Article: 2. Presumptions and Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 32174

---

## Text

No tax is imposed by this part upon any wine sold or delivered in internal revenue bond to another wine grower in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
