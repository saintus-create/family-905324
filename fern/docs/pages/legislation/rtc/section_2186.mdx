# RTC § 2186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 2. Effect of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 2186

---

## Text

Every tax has the effect of a judgment against the person.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
