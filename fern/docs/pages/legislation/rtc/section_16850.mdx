# RTC § 16850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 1. When Allowable

**Citation:** RTC § 16850

---

## Text

If the Controller finds that there has been an overpayment of tax by a taxpayer for any reason, the amount of the overpayment shall be refunded to the taxpayer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
