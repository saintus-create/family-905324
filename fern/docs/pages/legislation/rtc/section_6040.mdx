## Text

This chapter shall be known as and referred to as the Marketplace Facilitator Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 5, Sec. 2. (AB 147) Effective April 25, 2019. Section operative October 1, 2019, pursuant to Sec. 6049.5.)
