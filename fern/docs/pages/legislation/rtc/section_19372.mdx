# RTC § 19372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19372

---

## Text

The Attorney General or the counsel for the Franchise Tax Board shall prosecute the action. The action shall be tried in the County of Sacramento unless the court with the consent of the prosecutor orders a change of place of trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
