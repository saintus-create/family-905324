# RTC § 24688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 4. Year of Deduction

**Citation:** RTC § 24688

---

## Text

Section 467 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to certain payments for the use of property or services, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 70. Effective October 6, 1993.)
