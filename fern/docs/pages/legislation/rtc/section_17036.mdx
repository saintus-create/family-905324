# RTC § 17036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17036

## Text

Unless expressly otherwise provided in this part, any notice may be given by first class mail postage prepaid.
