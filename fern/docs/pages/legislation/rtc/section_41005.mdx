# RTC § 41005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 41005

---

## Text

“In this state” means within the exterior limits of the State of California and includes all territory within those limits owned by or ceded to the United States of America.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 443.)
