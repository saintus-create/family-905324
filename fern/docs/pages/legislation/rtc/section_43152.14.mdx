# RTC § 43152.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 1. Returns and Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 43152.14

---

## Text

The fee imposed pursuant to Section 105310 of the Health and Safety Code, that is collected and administered under Section 43057, is due and payable on or before April 1 of each year for the previous calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 527, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2005.)
