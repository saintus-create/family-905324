# RTC § 23808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Tax Treatment of S Corporations and Their Shareholders

**Citation:** RTC § 23808

---

## Text

Sections 1373 and 1379 of the Internal Revenue Code shall not be applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 11, Sec. 64. Effective February 19, 1988. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1988, by Sec. 93 of Ch. 11.)
