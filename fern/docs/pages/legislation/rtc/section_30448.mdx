# RTC § 30448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 7.5. Seizure and Forfeiture

**Citation:** RTC § 30448

---

## Text

The court shall determine whether the seizure of the property was in accordance with law and enter an appropriate order for the disposition of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 963.)
