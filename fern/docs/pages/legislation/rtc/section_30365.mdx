# RTC § 30365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 30365

---

## Text

Within 30 days after disallowing any claim in whole or in part, the board shall serve written notice of its action on the claimant in the manner prescribed for the service of a notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
