# RTC § 8522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 12. Metropolitan Transportation Commission

**Citation:** RTC § 8522

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 878, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
