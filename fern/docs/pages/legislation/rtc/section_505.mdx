# RTC § 505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 3. Arbitrary and Penal Assessments

**Citation:** RTC § 505

---

## Text

The assessor shall make an assessment subject to penalty by entering on the local roll the assessment and penalty in such form and manner as prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 147.)
