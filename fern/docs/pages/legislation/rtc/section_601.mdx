# RTC § 601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 6. Assessment Roll

**Citation:** RTC § 601

---

## Text

The assessor shall prepare an assessment roll, as directed by the board, in which shall be listed all property within the county which it is the assessor’s duty to assess.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
