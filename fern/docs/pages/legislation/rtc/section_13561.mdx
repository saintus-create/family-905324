# RTC § 13561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 6. Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 13561

---

## Text

An application for the refund shall be made to the Controller within one year after the date the federal estate tax has been finally determined.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
