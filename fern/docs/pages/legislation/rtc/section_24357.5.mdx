# RTC § 24357.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24357.5

---

## Text

No deduction shall be allowed under Section 24357 for a contribution to or for the use of an organization or trust described in Section 4948(c)(4) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 938, Sec. 29. Effective September 7, 1984.)
