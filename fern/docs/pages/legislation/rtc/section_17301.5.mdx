# RTC § 17301.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 9. Special Rules for Nonresidents and Part-Year Residents

**Citation:** RTC § 17301.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, in the case of a nonresident or part-year resident, the term “total taxable income” means taxable income for the entire year determined under Section 17073 regardless of source.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 920, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2002.)
