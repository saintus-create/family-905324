# RTC § 18177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 14. General Rules for Determining Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 18177

---

## Text

Section 1275(a)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the definition of tax-exempt obligations, does not apply but instead the term “tax-exempt obligation” means an obligation the interest on which is exempt from tax under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 185, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2004.)
