# RTC § 402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1. General Requirements

**Citation:** RTC § 402

---

## Text

Cultivated and uncultivated land of the same quality and similarly situated shall be assessed at the same value.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
