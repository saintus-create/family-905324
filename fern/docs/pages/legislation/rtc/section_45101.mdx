# RTC § 45101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 2. The Integrated Waste Management Fee > Article: 2. Registration and Security

**Citation:** RTC § 45101

---

## Text

Every person who operates a solid waste landfill required to have a solid waste facilities permit pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 44001) of Part 4 of the Public Resources Code shall register with the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 48. Effective October 1, 1993.)
