# RTC § 60030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60030

---

## Text

“Bulk transfer/terminal system” means the diesel fuel distribution system consisting of refineries, pipelines, vessels, and terminals. Diesel fuel in a refinery, pipeline, vessel, or terminal is in the bulk transfer/terminal system. Diesel fuel in the fuel tank of any engine, or in any railcar, trailer, truck, or other equipment suitable for ground transportation is not in the bulk transfer/terminal system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
