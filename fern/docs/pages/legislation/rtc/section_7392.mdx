# RTC § 7392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2.5. Aircraft Jet Fuel Tax > Article: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7392

---

## Text

For the privilege of using or selling aircraft jet fuel a tax is imposed upon every aircraft jet fuel dealer at the rate of two cents ($0.02) for each gallon of that fuel sold to an aircraft jet fuel user or used by the dealer as an aircraft jet fuel user.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
