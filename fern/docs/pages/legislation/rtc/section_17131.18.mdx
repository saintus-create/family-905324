# RTC § 17131.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17131.18

---

## Text

Gross income does not include any payment received by an individual pursuant to Section 15990 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 266, Sec. 5. (AB 1878) Effective January 1, 2025.)
