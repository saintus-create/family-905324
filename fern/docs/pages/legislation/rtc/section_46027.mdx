# RTC § 46027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46027

---

## Text

“State waters” or “waters of the state” means any surface water, including saline waters, marine waters, and freshwaters, within the boundaries of the state but does not include groundwater.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 177. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014.)
