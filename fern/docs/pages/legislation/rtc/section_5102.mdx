# RTC § 5102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 5102

---

## Text

If any payment may be refunded under this article and no claim is filed within the time allowed, the payment may be transferred to the county general fund on order of the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
