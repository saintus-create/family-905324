# RTC § 1253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 6. Assessor’s Office Equipment

**Citation:** RTC § 1253

---

## Text

In any event, the cost of furnishing the assessor’s necessary office equipment is a county charge, payable like other county charges from the county general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
