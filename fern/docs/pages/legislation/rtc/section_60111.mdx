# RTC § 60111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Interstate Users > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60111

---

## Text

“Interstate user” includes any person who uses diesel fuel in the operation of a qualified motor vehicle in this state and who operates the qualified motor vehicle within and without this state or the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 77. Effective January 1, 1996.)
