# RTC § 5331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 5331

---

## Text

Aircraft owned by the United States, by any foreign government, or by a consul or other official representative of any foreign government, are exempt from personal property taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
