# RTC § 322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 2. Legal Description of Lands for Assessment Purposes

**Citation:** RTC § 322

---

## Text

If surveyed under the authority of the United States, land may be described by township, range, section, and fractional section, with its acreage.
