# RTC § 43604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 43604

---

## Text

Any person who willfully evades or attempts in any manner to evade or defeat the payment of the tax imposed by this part is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
