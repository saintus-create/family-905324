# RTC § 18045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 13. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property

**Citation:** RTC § 18045

---

## Text

Section 1061 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to partnership interests held in connection with performance of services, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 61. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
