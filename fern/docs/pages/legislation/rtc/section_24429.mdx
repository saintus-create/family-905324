# RTC § 24429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 3. Items Not Deductible

**Citation:** RTC § 24429

---

## Text

Section 276 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to certain indirect contributions to political parties, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 611, Sec. 89. Effective October 3, 1997.)
