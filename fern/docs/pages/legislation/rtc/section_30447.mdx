# RTC § 30447

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 7.5. Seizure and Forfeiture

**Citation:** RTC § 30447

---

## Text

At the time set for the hearing any person having a timely answer on file may offer evidence to establish that the seizure and forfeiture of the property was erroneous or illegal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 963.)
