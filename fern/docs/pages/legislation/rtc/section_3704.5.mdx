# RTC § 3704.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3704.5

---

## Text

In addition to the published notice required by Section 3702, the tax collector may advertise the intended sale by any means authorized by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1053.)
