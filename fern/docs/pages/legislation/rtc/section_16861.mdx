# RTC § 16861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 16861

---

## Text

Process in the action directed to the state shall be served on the Controller.
