# RTC § 24373

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 1. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 24373

---

## Text

Section 178 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the amortization of cost of acquiring a lease, shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1352, Sec. 111. Effective October 2, 1989. Applicable to income years beginning on or after January 1, 1989, by Sec. 172 of Ch. 1352.)
