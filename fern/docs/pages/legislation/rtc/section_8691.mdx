# RTC § 8691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 2.5. International Fuel Tax Agreement

**Citation:** RTC § 8691

---

## Text

Effective on and after January 1, 1996, Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 9405) of Part 3.5 shall apply to all interstate users.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1996.)
