# RTC § 10768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Fee > Article: 1.5. Trailer Coaches

**Citation:** RTC § 10768

---

## Text

Sections 10853, 10854, 10855, and 10856 of this part do not apply to the license fee imposed with respect to trailer coaches.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1653.)
