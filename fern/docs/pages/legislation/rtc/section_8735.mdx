# RTC § 8735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3.5. Collection of Tax by Vendor > Article: 1. Collection of Tax by Vendor

**Citation:** RTC § 8735

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapters 4 to 9, inclusive, of this part shall apply to any vendor in the same manner as such provisions apply to any user.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1814.)
