# RTC § 5062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 3. Cancellation of Assessments on Public Lands on Application of Property Owner

**Citation:** RTC § 5062

---

## Text

If any public land is heretofore or hereafter assessed and the taxes are not paid, the board of supervisors shall order the auditor to cancel the assessment if:

(a) Verified application is made by the owner of the land, and

(b) The applicant files with the board of supervisors a certificate of the Register of the United States Lands Office, or of the State Lands Commission, showing that the assessee or his successor in interest never received a patent or otherwise acquired title to the land.
