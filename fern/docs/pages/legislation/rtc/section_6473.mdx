# RTC § 6473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 5. Determinations > Article: 1.1. Prepayment

**Citation:** RTC § 6473

---

## Text

The amount of the prepayment shall constitute a credit against the amount of the taxes due and payable for the quarterly period in which the prepayment became due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, Ch. 5.)
