# RTC § 115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 115

---

## Text

“Interest” in any property includes any legal or equitable interest.
