# RTC § 5712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5712

---

## Text

“Nonproducing broodmare” means a racehorse mare which has not produced a live foal during the previous calendar year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
