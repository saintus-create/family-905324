# RTC § 17042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 17042

---

## Text

Section 2(b) and (c) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to definitions of head of household and certain married individuals living apart, respectively, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 9. Effective October 6, 1993.)
