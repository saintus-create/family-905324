# RTC § 24694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 4. Year of Deduction

**Citation:** RTC § 24694

---

## Text

Section 470 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to limitation on deductions allocable to property used by governments or other tax-exempt entities, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 71. Effective October 7, 2005.)
