# RTC § 41056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 1. Due and Payable Dates

**Citation:** RTC § 41056

---

## Text

The service supplier and seller shall maintain records as may be necessary to determine the amount of surcharges collected under provisions of this part. Those records shall be maintained for a period of four years from the time the surcharge is due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 747, Sec. 24. (AB 988) Effective September 29, 2022.)
