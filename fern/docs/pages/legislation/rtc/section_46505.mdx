# RTC § 46505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 46505

---

## Text

Within 30 days after disallowing any claim, in whole or in part, the board shall serve written notice of its action on the claimant, the service to be made as provided by Section 46202.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
