# RTC § 4831.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 2. Corrections > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4831.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, corrections to the roll that relate to the disabled veterans’ exemption described in Section 205.5 may be corrected within eight years after the making of the assessment being corrected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 871, Sec. 2. (SB 1458) Effective September 30, 2016.)
