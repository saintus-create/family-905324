# RTC § 43305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 3. Determinations > Article: 4. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 43305

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination shall become final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
