# RTC § 443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 2. Information From Taxpayer

**Citation:** RTC § 443

---

## Text

The property statement shall also show:

(a) The county where the property is taxable.

(b) If taxable in the county where the statement is made, any city or revenue district where it is situated.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
