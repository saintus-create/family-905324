# RTC § 46543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 46543

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall prosecute any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 46541, and the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to service of summons, pleadings, proof, trials, and appeals shall apply to the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 46. Effective January 1, 1999.)
