# RTC § 43422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Collection of Tax > Article: 3. Warrant for Collection

**Citation:** RTC § 43422

---

## Text

The board may pay or advance to the sheriff or marshal, the same fees, commissions, and expenses for their services as are provided by law for similar services pursuant to a writ of execution. The board, and not the court, shall approve the fees for publication in a newspaper.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 164. Effective January 1, 1997.)
