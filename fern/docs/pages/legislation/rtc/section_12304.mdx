# RTC § 12304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 4. Assessment and Effect of Tax > Article: 1. Tax Returns and Initial Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 12304

---

## Text

(a) Blank forms of returns shall be furnished by the commissioner on application, but failure to secure the form shall not relieve any insurer from making or filing a timely return.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 39) and added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 33, Sec. 40. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2013. Section operative July 1, 2013, by its own provisions.)
