# RTC § 5455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Distribution

**Citation:** RTC § 5455

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this chapter to divide the proceeds allotted to school districts equally between districts supporting elementary schools and districts supporting high schools, excluding all other educational districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
