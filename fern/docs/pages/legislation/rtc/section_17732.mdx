# RTC § 17732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 9. Estates, Trusts, Beneficiaries, and Decedents

**Citation:** RTC § 17732

---

## Text

Section 642(b) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to deduction for personal exemption, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 46. Effective October 10, 1999.)
