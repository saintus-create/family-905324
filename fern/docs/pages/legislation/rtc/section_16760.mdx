# RTC § 16760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 3. Payment of Tax > Article: 2. Interest and Penalties

**Citation:** RTC § 16760

---

## Text

If the tax is not paid before it becomes delinquent, it bears interest thereafter and until it is paid at the same rate per annum as provided in Section 6621(a)(2) of the Internal Revenue Code, compounded daily.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 363, Sec. 8. Effective September 8, 2000. Operative January 1, 2001, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 363.)
