# RTC § 30477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 10. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 30477

---

## Text

Any violation of the provisions of this part, except as otherwise provided, is a misdemeanor and is punishable as such.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 30476 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 963.)
