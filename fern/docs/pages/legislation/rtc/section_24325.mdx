# RTC § 24325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Gross Income > Article: 3. Other Exclusions

**Citation:** RTC § 24325

---

## Text

Section 118 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to contributions to the capital of a corporation, shall apply to contributions made on or after January 1, 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 698, Sec. 20. Effective September 15, 1992.)
