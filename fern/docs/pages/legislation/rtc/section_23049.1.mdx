# RTC § 23049.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23049.1

---

## Text

Section 64 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the definition of ordinary income, shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 117, Sec. 43. Effective July 16, 1991.)
