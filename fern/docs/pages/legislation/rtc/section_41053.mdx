# RTC § 41053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 1. Due and Payable Dates

**Citation:** RTC § 41053

---

## Text

The person required to file the return shall deliver the return together with a remittance of the amount of the surcharges payable to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 747, Sec. 23. (AB 988) Effective September 29, 2022.)
