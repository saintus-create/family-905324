# RTC § 45009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 23. INTEGRATED WASTE MANAGEMENT FEE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 45009

---

## Text

“Fee payer” means any person who is liable for payment of the fee imposed pursuant to Section 48000 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 656, Sec. 44. Effective October 1, 1993.)
