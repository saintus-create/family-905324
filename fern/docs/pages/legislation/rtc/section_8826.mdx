# RTC § 8826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 4. Jeopardy Determinations

**Citation:** RTC § 8826

---

## Text

If the board believes that the collection of any amount of excise tax imposed under this part will be jeopardized by delay, it shall thereupon make a determination of the amount of tax, noting that fact upon the determination. The amount determined is immediately due and payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 38.)
