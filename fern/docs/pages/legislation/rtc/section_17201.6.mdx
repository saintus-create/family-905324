# RTC § 17201.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17201.6

---

## Text

Section 199A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to qualified business income, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 29. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
