# RTC § 16800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection of Tax > Article: 1. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 16800

---

## Text

The state may enforce its claim for any tax imposed by this part and enforce the lien of the tax by a civil action in any court of competent jurisdiction against any person liable for the tax or against any property subject to the lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
