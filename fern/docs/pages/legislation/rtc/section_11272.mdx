# RTC § 11272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 2. Assessments > Article: 2. Reports

**Citation:** RTC § 11272

---

## Text

The board for good cause may extend for not to exceed 30 days the time for making a report, provided a written request is filed with the board prior to the period for which the extension may be granted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 54.)
