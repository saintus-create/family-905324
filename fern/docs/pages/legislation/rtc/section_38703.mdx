# RTC § 38703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 8. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 38703

---

## Text

Every timber owner shall keep such records, receipts, invoices, and other pertinent papers in such form as the board may require by rules or regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
