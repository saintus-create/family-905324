# RTC § 102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Construction

**Citation:** RTC § 102

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to permit double taxation.
