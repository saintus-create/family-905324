# RTC § 17062.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 2.1. Alternative Minimum Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 17062.5

---

## Text

Section 55(b)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to maximum rate of tax on net capital gain of noncorporate taxpayers, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 322, Sec. 15. Effective August 20, 1998.)
