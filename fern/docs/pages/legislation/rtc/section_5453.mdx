# RTC § 5453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Distribution

**Citation:** RTC § 5453

---

## Text

If the aircraft are habitually based outside of a city, but in any school districts, the proceeds shall be distributed one-half to the school districts and one-half to the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
