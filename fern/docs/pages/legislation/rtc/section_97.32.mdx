# RTC § 97.32

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 0.5. IMPLEMENTATION OF ARTICLE XIII A OF THE CALIFORNIA CONSTITUTION > Chapter: 6. Allocation of Property Tax Revenue > Article: 3. Revenue Allocation Shifts for Education

**Citation:** RTC § 97.32

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 97.3, a special district does not include, for purposes of the reductions required by that section, a memorial district formed pursuant to Article 1 (commencing with Section 1170) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 6 of the Military and Veteran’s Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1167, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
