# RTC § 30019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 30019

---

## Text

“Importer” means any purchaser for resale in the United States of cigarettes or tobacco products manufactured outside of the United States for the purpose of making a first sale or distribution within the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 501, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2007.)
