# RTC § 32304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 6. Determinations > Article: 4. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 32304

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination shall become final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 881.)
