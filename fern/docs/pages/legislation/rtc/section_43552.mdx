# RTC § 43552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 43552

---

## Text

All taxes, interest, and penalties imposed and all amounts of tax required to be paid to the state pursuant to Section 43054 shall be paid to the board in the form of remittances payable to the State Board of Equalization of the State of California. The board shall transmit the payments to the Treasurer for deposit in the State Treasury to the credit of the Toxic Substances Control Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 882, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1999.)
