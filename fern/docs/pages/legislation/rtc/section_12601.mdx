# RTC § 12601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 5. Payment and Collection > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 12601

---

## Text

(a) Amounts of taxes, interest, and penalties not remitted to the commissioner with the original return of the insurer shall be payable to the Controller.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 90) and added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 33, Sec. 91. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2013. Section operative July 1, 2013, by its own provisions.)
