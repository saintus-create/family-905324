# RTC § 17287

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17287

---

## Text

Section 269A of the Internal Revenue Code is modified by substituting “California Personal Income Tax” for “Federal income tax.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 34. Effective October 10, 1999.)
