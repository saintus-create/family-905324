# RTC § 19704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 9. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 19704

---

## Text

Any action or prosecution under this chapter shall be instituted within six years after commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
