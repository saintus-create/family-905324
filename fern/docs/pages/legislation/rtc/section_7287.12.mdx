# RTC § 7287.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.10. City of Victorville Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7287.12

---

## Text

If, as of January 1, 2029, an ordinance or citizens’ initiative proposing a transactions and use tax has not been approved as required by Section 7287.11, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 961, Sec. 1. (AB 2443) Effective September 29, 2024. Conditionally repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.10, commencing with Section 7287.11.)
