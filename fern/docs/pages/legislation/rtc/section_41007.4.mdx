# RTC § 41007.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 41007.4

---

## Text

“Purchase” means any transfer of title or possession, exchange, or barter, conditional or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 54, Sec. 8. (SB 96) Effective July 1, 2019.)
