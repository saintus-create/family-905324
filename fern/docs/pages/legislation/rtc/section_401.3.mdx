# RTC § 401.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1. General Requirements

**Citation:** RTC § 401.3

---

## Text

The assessor shall assess all property subject to general property taxation on the lien date as provided in Articles XIII and XIIIA of the Constitution and any legislative authorization thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 608, Sec. 9.)
