# RTC § 8360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 10. Distribution of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 8360

---

## Text

The board may pay out of the appropriations made to it from the Motor Vehicle Fuel Account all expenses incurred in the prosecution before any court of this state of any person charged with the violation of this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 56, Sec. 163.)
