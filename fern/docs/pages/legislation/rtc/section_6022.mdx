# RTC § 6022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 6022

---

## Text

“Vehicle” and “motor vehicle,” as used in this part, shall have the meanings ascribed to them in Sections 415 and 670 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1858.)
