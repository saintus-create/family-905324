# RTC § 60003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60003

---

## Text

“Terminal” means a diesel fuel storage and distribution facility that is supplied by pipeline or vessel, and from which diesel fuel may be removed at a rack. “Terminal” includes a fuel production facility where diesel fuel is produced and stored and from which diesel fuel may be removed at a rack.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 545, Sec. 6. (AB 1547) Effective January 1, 2010.)
