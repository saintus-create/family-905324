# RTC § 324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 2. Legal Description of Lands for Assessment Purposes

**Citation:** RTC § 324

---

## Text

City lots may be described by naming the city and giving the number of the lot and block, according to the system of numbering in the city.
