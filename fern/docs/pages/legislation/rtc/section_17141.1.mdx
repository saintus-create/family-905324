# RTC § 17141.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17141.1

---

## Text

Gross income does not include any amounts received as a premium assistance subsidy under Title 25 (commencing with Section 100800) of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 38, Sec. 36. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2019.)
