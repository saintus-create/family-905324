# RTC § 40114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 40114

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of any demand against the state on account of overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
