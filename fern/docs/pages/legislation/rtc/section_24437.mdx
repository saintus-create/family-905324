# RTC § 24437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 3. Items Not Deductible

**Citation:** RTC § 24437

---

## Text

Section 277 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to deductions incurred by certain membership organizations in transactions with members, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 58. Effective October 6, 1993.)
