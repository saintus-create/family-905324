# RTC § 11921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6.7. DOCUMENTARY TRANSFER TAX ACT > Chapter: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 11921

---

## Text

Any tax imposed pursuant to this part shall not apply to any instrument in writing given to secure a debt.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1332.)
