# RTC § 17021.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17021.5

---

## Text

Section 7703 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to determination of marital status, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 7. Effective October 6, 1993.)
