# RTC § 50155.6.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 26. UNDERGROUND STORAGE TANK MAINTENANCE FEE LAW > Chapter: 6. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 50155.6.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the board’s authority to examine the books and records of a feepayer under Section 50153.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 105, Sec. 49. (AB 2009) Effective January 1, 2015.)
