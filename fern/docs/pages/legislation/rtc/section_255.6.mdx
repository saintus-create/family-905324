# RTC § 255.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 2. Procedure to Claim Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 255.6

---

## Text

The assessor shall verify the eligibility of each claimant who is receiving a homeowners’ exemption to continue to receive such an exemption in accordance with rules issued by the board to provide for a periodic audit and for the establishment of a control system for the homeowners’ exemption claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 60, Sec. 8) by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1107, Sec. 7. Amendment operative beginning with 1974-75 fiscal year, by Sec. 21 of Ch. 1107.)
