# RTC § 6013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 6013

---

## Text

“Business” includes any activity engaged in by any person or caused to be engaged in by him with the object of gain, benefit, or advantage, either direct or indirect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 36.)
