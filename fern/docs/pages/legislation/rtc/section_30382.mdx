# RTC § 30382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 30382

---

## Text

In any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 30381, the court may, with the consent of the Attorney General, order a change in the place of trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1999.)
