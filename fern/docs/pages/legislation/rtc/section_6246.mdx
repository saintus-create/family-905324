# RTC § 6246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 3. The Use Tax > Article: 3. Presumptions and Resale Certificates

**Citation:** RTC § 6246

---

## Text

It shall be further presumed that tangible personal property shipped or brought to this State by the purchaser was purchased from a retailer on or after July 1, 1935, for storage, use, or other consumption in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6245 by Stats. 1943, Ch. 699.)
