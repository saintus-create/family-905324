# RTC § 17012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17012

---

## Text

“Paid or incurred” and “paid or accrued” shall be construed according to the method of accounting upon the basis of which the taxable income is computed under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 939.)
