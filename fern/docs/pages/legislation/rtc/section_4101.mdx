# RTC § 4101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 7. REDEMPTION > Chapter: 1. Redemption Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4101

---

## Text

Tax-defaulted property may be redeemed until the right of redemption is terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 988, Sec. 69. Effective September 11, 1984.)
