# RTC § 8778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 2. Deficiency Determinations

**Citation:** RTC § 8778

---

## Text

In making a determination the board may offset overpayments for a month or months against underpayments for another month or months and against the interest and penalties on the underpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 38.)
