# RTC § 17149.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 3. Items Specifically Excluded from Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17149.1

---

## Text

Section 132(f)(8) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to suspension of qualified bicycle commuting reimbursement exclusion, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 22. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
