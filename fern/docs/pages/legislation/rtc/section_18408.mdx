# RTC § 18408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. General Application

**Citation:** RTC § 18408

---

## Text

The Franchise Tax Board is authorized to require that information with respect to persons subject to the taxes imposed by Article 5 (commencing with Section 18661) of Chapter 2 (relating to tax withheld at source) as is necessary or helpful in securing proper identification of those persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 18552 by Stats. 2000, Ch. 863, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2001.)
