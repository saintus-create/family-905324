# RTC § 32172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 4. Tax on Beer and Wine > Article: 2. Presumptions and Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 32172

---

## Text

Beer consumed by employees of a manufacturer upon the premises of the manufacturer is exempted from the tax under such rules as the board may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
