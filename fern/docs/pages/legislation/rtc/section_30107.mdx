# RTC § 30107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax > Article: 1. Tax on Distributors

**Citation:** RTC § 30107

---

## Text

The taxes resulting from a distribution of cigarettes or tobacco products within the meaning of subdivision (b) of Section 30008 shall be paid by the user or consumer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 634, Sec. 14. Effective September 21, 1989.)
