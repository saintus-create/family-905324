# RTC § 5097.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 5. Refunds > Article: 1. Refunds Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 5097.03

---

## Text

When a claim for refund of taxes is filed, the amount of tax computed on the portion of the assessment not in dispute shall not be impounded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 499.)
