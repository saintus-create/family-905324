# RTC § 19209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 1. Judgment for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19209

---

## Text

If the Franchise Tax Board records a certificate of release pursuant to Section 19206 or 19207, the cost of recording is an obligation of the taxpayer and may be collected from the taxpayer in any manner provided in this part for the collection of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
