# RTC § 60366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Determinations and Payments > Article: 6. Payment by Unlicensed Persons

**Citation:** RTC § 60366

---

## Text

No action taken pursuant to this article relieves the unlicensed person in any way from the penal provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
