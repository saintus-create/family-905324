# RTC § 24428

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Net Income > Article: 3. Items Not Deductible

**Citation:** RTC § 24428

---

## Text

Section 267A of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to certain related party amounts paid or accrued in hybrid transactions or with hybrid entities, shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 100. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
