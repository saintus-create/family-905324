# RTC § 46453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 4. Collection of Fee > Article: 6. Successor Withholding and Liability

**Citation:** RTC § 46453

---

## Text

The certificate may be issued after the payment of all amounts due under this part, according to the records of the board as of the date of the certificate, or after the payment of the amounts is secured to the satisfaction of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
