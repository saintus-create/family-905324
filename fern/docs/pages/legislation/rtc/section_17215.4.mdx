# RTC § 17215.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17215.4

---

## Text

Section 223 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to health savings accounts, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 691, Sec. 26.6. Effective October 7, 2005.)
