# RTC § 5762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 4. Collection

**Citation:** RTC § 5762

---

## Text

The tax imposed by this part shall become delinquent at 5 p.m. on the 15th day of February of the calendar year for which it is imposed.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
