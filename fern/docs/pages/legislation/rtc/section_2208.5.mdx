# RTC § 2208.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2208.5

---

## Text

“School district” means any school district, community college district, or county superintendent of schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1135.)
