# RTC § 60003.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60003.1

---

## Text

“Fuel production facility” means a facility, other than a refinery, in which diesel fuel is produced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 545, Sec. 7. (AB 1547) Effective January 1, 2010.)
