# RTC § 55224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 55224

---

## Text

Within 30 days after disallowing any claim, in whole or in part, the board shall serve written notice of its action on the claimant pursuant to Section 55061.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
