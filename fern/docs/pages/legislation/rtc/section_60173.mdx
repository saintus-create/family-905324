# RTC § 60173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 5. Denial of License

**Citation:** RTC § 60173

---

## Text

The notice specified in Section 60172 shall be addressed to the applicant at his or her address as it appears in the records of the board, and shall be given in the manner prescribed in Section 60311 for giving notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
