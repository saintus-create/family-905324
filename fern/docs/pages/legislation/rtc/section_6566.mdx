# RTC § 6566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 5. Determinations > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 6566

---

## Text

Any notice required by this article shall be served in the manner prescribed for service of notice of a deficiency determination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 499, Sec. 12. (SB 1528) Effective January 1, 2025.)
