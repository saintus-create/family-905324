# RTC § 17553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 6. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting

**Citation:** RTC § 17553

---

## Text

Section 454(c) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to matured United States Savings Bonds, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 40. Effective October 10, 1999.)
