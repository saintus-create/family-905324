# RTC § 41052.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 1. Due and Payable Dates

**Citation:** RTC § 41052.1

---

## Text

The department may require the payment of the amount due and the filing of returns for periods other than the periods set forth under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 69. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
