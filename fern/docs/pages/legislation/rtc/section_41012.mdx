# RTC § 41012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 41012

---

## Text

“Public telephone” means any coin-operated telephone provided by the serving telephone company accessible to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 443.)
