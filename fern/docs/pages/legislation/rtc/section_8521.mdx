# RTC § 8521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 12. Metropolitan Transportation Commission

**Citation:** RTC § 8521

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold as the commission determines by resolution, and the bonds may be sold at a price below par, whether by negotiated or public sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 878, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
