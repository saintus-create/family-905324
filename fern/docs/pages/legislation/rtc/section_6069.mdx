# RTC § 6069

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 2. The Sales Tax > Article: 2. Permits

**Citation:** RTC § 6069

---

## Text

A seller whose permit has been previously suspended or revoked shall pay the board a fee of one hundred dollars ($100) for the renewal or issuance of a permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 545, Sec. 1. (AB 1547) Effective January 1, 2010.)
