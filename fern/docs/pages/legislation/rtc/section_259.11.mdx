# RTC § 259.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 2. Procedure to Claim Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 259.11

---

## Text

The affidavit for the aircraft of historical significance exemption shall show that both the property and the owner meet all the requirements entitling the property to the exemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1271, Sec. 7. Effective September 26, 1988.)
