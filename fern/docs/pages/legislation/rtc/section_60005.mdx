# RTC § 60005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60005

---

## Text

“Approved terminal or refinery” means a terminal or refinery that is operated, respectively, by a diesel fuel registrant that is a terminal operator, or by a diesel fuel registrant that is a refiner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
