# RTC § 38908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 10. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 38908

---

## Text

Local governmental entities which are allocated funds pursuant to Section 38905 or 38905.1 may expend such funds without restriction.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1058, Sec. 9.)
