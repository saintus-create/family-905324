# RTC § 2235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 2235

---

## Text

Claims for direct and indirect costs filed pursuant to Sections 2229 and 2231 shall be filed in the manner prescribed by the State Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2234 (as amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 105) by Stats. 1977, Ch. 309.)
