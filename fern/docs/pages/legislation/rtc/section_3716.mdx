# RTC § 3716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 7. Sale to Private Parties After Deed to State

**Citation:** RTC § 3716

---

## Text

Within 30 days after the sale, the tax collector shall report to the assessor the following:

(a) The name of the purchaser.

(b) The date the property was sold.

(c) The amount for which the property was sold.

(d) The description of the property conveyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 607, Sec. 7. (SB 825) Effective January 1, 2014.)
