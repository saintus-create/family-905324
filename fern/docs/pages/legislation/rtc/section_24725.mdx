# RTC § 24725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 6. Adjustments Required by Changes in Method

**Citation:** RTC § 24725

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 482 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to allocation of income and deductions among taxpayers, shall be applicable, except as provided in Article 1.5 (commencing with Section 25110) of Chapter 17.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1295, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 1993.)
