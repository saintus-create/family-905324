# RTC § 17222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17222

---

## Text

No deduction shall be allowed for the tax deducted and withheld under Section 18662 and Section 13020 of the Unemployment Insurance Code either to the employer or to the recipient of the income in computing taxable income under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 8. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
