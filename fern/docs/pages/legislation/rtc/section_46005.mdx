# RTC § 46005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46005

---

## Text

Any action or proceeding commenced before this part takes effect, or any right accrued, is not affected by this part, but all procedures taken shall conform to the provisions on this part as far as possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
