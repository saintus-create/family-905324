# RTC § 60057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 60057

---

## Text

Every highway vehicle operator/fueler is liable for the backup tax imposed under Section 60058.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 429, Sec. 51. Effective October 2, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 78 of Ch. 429.)
