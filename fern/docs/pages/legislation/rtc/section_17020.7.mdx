# RTC § 17020.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17020.7

---

## Text

Section 7701(a)(46) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to determination of whether there is a collective bargaining agreement, shall apply, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 2. Effective October 6, 1993.)
