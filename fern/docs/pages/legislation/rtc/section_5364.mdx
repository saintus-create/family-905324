# RTC § 5364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 3. Assessment

**Citation:** RTC § 5364

---

## Text

The board shall establish standards and fix guides or, after a public hearing, shall review and approve commercially available guides, to be used by the county assessor in the assessment of aircraft at market value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1087, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1997.)
