# RTC § 4915

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 2. Corrections > Article: 3. Incorrect Application of Payments

**Citation:** RTC § 4915

---

## Text

If the amount paid is less than the amount due on the property intended, the balance of the amount due shall be paid before the transfer is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
