# RTC § 41102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 1. Claim for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 41102

---

## Text

Every claim shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which the claim is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 443.)
