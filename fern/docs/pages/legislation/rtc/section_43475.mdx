# RTC § 43475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 43475

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 756, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1981.)
