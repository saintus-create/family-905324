# RTC § 5706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5706

---

## Text

“Current calendar year” means the yearly period from the first day of January to the last day of December, inclusive for which the tax is imposed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
