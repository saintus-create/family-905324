# RTC § 989

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 989

---

## Text

Unredeemed pledged goods in the possession of a pawnbroker, but not owned by him to hold and dispose of as his property, shall not be assessed to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 420.)
