# RTC § 2859

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 3.3. Collection of Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 2859

---

## Text

If the roll is transferred from one collector to another, the auditor shall credit the one and charge the other with the amount outstanding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 239.)
