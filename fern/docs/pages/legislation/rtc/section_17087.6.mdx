# RTC § 17087.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 2. Items Specifically Included in Gross Income

**Citation:** RTC § 17087.6

---

## Text

If a limited liability company is classified as a partnership for California tax purposes, a person with a membership or economic interest shall take into account amounts required to be recognized under Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 17851).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 52. Effective September 30, 1994.)
