# RTC § 32454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 9. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 32454

---

## Text

The board may employ accountants, auditors, investigators and other expert and clerical assistance necessary to enforce its powers and perform its duties under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
