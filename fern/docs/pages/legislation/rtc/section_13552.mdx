# RTC § 13552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 5. Payment of Tax > Article: 2. Interest

**Citation:** RTC § 13552

---

## Text

All interest and penalties provided in this chapter shall be treated and collected in the same manner as taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 221, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
