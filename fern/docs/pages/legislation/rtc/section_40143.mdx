# RTC § 40143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 6. Collection of Surcharge > Article: 1. Suit for Surcharge

**Citation:** RTC § 40143

---

## Text

In the action a writ of attachment may issue, and no bond or affidavit previous to the issuing of the attachment is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
