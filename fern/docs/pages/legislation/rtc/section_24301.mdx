# RTC § 24301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 6. Gross Income > Article: 2. Exclusions

**Citation:** RTC § 24301

---

## Text

In computing the tax imposed under this part, “gross income” does not include any of the items specified in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 938.)
