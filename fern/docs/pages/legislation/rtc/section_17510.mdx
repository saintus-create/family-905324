# RTC § 17510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 5. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** RTC § 17510

---

## Text

Section 7701(j) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to Federal Thrift Savings Funds, applies, except as otherwise provided.
