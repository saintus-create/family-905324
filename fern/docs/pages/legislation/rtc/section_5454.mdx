# RTC § 5454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 6. Distribution

**Citation:** RTC § 5454

---

## Text

If the aircraft are habitually based in an elementary school district and a high school district, the proceeds allotted to school districts shall be divided equally between the elementary school district and the high school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
