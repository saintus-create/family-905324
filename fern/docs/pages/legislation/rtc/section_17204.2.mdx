# RTC § 17204.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 3. Computation of Taxable Income > Article: 6. Deductions

**Citation:** RTC § 17204.2

---

## Text

The amendments made by Section 11050 of the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act, 2017 (Public Law 115-97) to Section 165(d) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to wagering losses, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 31. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
