# RTC § 3443.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 6. TAX SALES > Chapter: 2. Sale by Operation of Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 3443.5

---

## Text

In lieu of the procedure specified in Section 3443, where a machine-prepared roll is used, the fact and date of the declaration of default may be entered upon the reproduced roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 988, Sec. 27. Effective September 11, 1984.)
