# RTC § 31000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13.6. Healthy Outcomes and Prevention Education (HOPE) Act

**Citation:** RTC § 31000

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the “Healthy Outcomes and Prevention Education (HOPE) Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 489, Sec. 3. (SB 395) Effective January 1, 2022.)
