# RTC § 31203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13.7. Property Tax Reimbursement Law

**Citation:** RTC § 31203

---

## Text

Any property tax reimbursement collected by a qualified heavy equipment renter shall conform to Section 1656.5 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 505, Sec. 2. (AB 1130) Effective January 1, 2018.)
