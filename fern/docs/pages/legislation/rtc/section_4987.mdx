# RTC § 4987

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 9. CORRECTIONS, CANCELLATIONS, AND REFUNDS > Chapter: 4. Cancellations > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4987

---

## Text

No cancellation shall be made of charges on tax exempt property if there has not been compliance with the statutory procedure for claiming the exemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
