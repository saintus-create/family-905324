# RTC § 60563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Overpayment and Refunds > Article: 4. Recovery of Erroneous Refunds

**Citation:** RTC § 60563

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall prosecute any action brought pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 60561, and the provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to service of summons, pleadings, proof, trials, and appeals shall apply to the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 609, Sec. 59. Effective January 1, 1999.)
