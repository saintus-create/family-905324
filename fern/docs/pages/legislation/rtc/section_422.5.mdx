# RTC § 422.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Assessment Generally > Article: 1.5. Valuation of Open-Space Land Subject to an Enforceable Restriction

**Citation:** RTC § 422.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, open-space land is “enforceably restricted” within the meaning of Section 8 of Article XIII of the California Constitution if it is subject to an agricultural conservation easement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 931, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
