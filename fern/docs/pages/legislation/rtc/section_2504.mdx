# RTC § 2504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 1. Medium of Payment

**Citation:** RTC § 2504

---

## Text

As used in this division, “negotiable paper” means checks, drafts, and money orders.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 340, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2008.)
