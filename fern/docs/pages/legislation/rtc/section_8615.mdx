# RTC § 8615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 8615

---

## Text

“Standard pressure and temperature” means 14.73 pounds of pressure per square inch at 60° Fahrenheit.
