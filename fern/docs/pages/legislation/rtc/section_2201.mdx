# RTC § 2201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 3. Reimbursement for Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 2201

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, the definitions contained in this article govern the construction of this chapter. The definition of a word applies to any variants thereof; the singular tense of a word includes the plural.
