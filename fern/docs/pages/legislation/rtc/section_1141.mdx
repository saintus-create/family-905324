# RTC § 1141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 5. Vessels

**Citation:** RTC § 1141

---

## Text

Vessels not required to be documented shall be assessed in the county where habitually moored when not in service.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
