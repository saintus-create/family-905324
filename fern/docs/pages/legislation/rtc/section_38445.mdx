# RTC § 38445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 5. Determinations > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 38445

---

## Text

The order or decision of the board upon a petition for redetermination becomes final 30 days after service upon the petitioner of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
