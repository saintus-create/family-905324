# RTC § 8252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 8252

---

## Text

The board may employ attorneys, accountants, auditors, investigators, and other expert and clerical assistance necessary to enforce its powers and perform its duties under this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1222.)
