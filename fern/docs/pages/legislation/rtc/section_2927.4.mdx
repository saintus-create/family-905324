# RTC § 2927.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 2927.4

---

## Text

Errors occurring in the delinquent roll or abstract list may be corrected under the same conditions, except as to time, and in the same manner as they would be corrected if they occurred on the original unsecured roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 489.)
