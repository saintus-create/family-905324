# RTC § 18038.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 13. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property

**Citation:** RTC § 18038.4

---

## Text

Section 1045 of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to rollover of gain from qualified small business stock to another qualified small business stock, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 322, Sec. 40. Effective August 20, 1998.)
