# RTC § 1021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 2. Goods in Transit

**Citation:** RTC § 1021

---

## Text

The copy of the bill of lading or manifest shall show the description, value, consignor and consignee of the goods.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
