# RTC § 25134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 17. Allocation of Income > Article: 2. Uniform Division of Income for Tax Purposes Act

**Citation:** RTC § 25134

---

## Text

The sales factor is a fraction, the numerator of which is the total sales of the taxpayer in this state during the taxable year, and the denominator of which is the total sales of the taxpayer everywhere during the taxable year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 862, Sec. 225. Effective January 1, 2001.)
