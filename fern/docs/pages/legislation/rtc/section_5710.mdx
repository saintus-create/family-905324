# RTC § 5710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 12. TAXATION OF RACEHORSES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 5710

---

## Text

“Stallion” means any racehorse which, during the two previous calendar years, has serviced three or more different broodmares for the purpose of producing a racehorse.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 665.)
