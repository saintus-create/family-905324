# RTC § 2192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 4. LEVY OF TAX > Chapter: 2. Effect of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 2192

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specifically provided, all tax liens attach annually as of 12:01 a.m. on the first day of January preceding the fiscal year for which the taxes are levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 499, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1996.)
