# RTC § 9175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 9175

---

## Text

A judgment shall not be rendered in favor of the plaintiff in any action brought against the board to recover any amount paid when the action is brought by or in the name of an assignee of the user.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 38.)
