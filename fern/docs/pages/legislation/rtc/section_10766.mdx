# RTC § 10766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 5. VEHICLE LICENSE FEE > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Fee > Article: 1.5. Trailer Coaches

**Citation:** RTC § 10766

---

## Text

For the purpose of this article, “trailer coach” shall have the same meaning that is provided in the Vehicle Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1653.)
