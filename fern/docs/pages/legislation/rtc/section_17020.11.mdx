# RTC § 17020.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17020.11

---

## Text

Section 7701(h) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to motor vehicle operating leases, applies, except as otherwise provided.
