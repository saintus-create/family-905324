# RTC § 17779

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 9. Estates, Trusts, Beneficiaries, and Decedents

**Citation:** RTC § 17779

---

## Text

Sections 665 to 668, inclusive, of the Internal Revenue Code shall not apply to distributions described in subdivision (b) of Section 17745.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 488, Sec. 59. Effective July 28, 1983.)
