# RTC § 7393

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 2.5. Aircraft Jet Fuel Tax > Article: 2. Imposition of Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7393

---

## Text

The aircraft jet fuel dealer shall make a return of the tax due under Section 7392 as required of suppliers under Section 7651. All of the provisions of this part relating to the collection of the tax shall be applicable to the collection of the tax imposed by Section 7392.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1053, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 53 of Ch. 1053.)
