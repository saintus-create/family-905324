# RTC § 19252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 19252

---

## Text

In all proceedings under this chapter the Franchise Tax Board may act on behalf of the people of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
