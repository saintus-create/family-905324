# RTC § 38513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 6. Collection of Taxes > Article: 2. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 38513

---

## Text

In the action a writ of attachment may issue, and no bond or affidavit previous to the issuing of the attachment is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
