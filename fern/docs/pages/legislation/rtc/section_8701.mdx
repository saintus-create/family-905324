# RTC § 8701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 3. Permits > Article: 1. User

**Citation:** RTC § 8701

---

## Text

Every person desiring to become a user of fuel within this state shall secure a use fuel tax permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1996.)
