# RTC § 11337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 2. Assessments > Article: 5. Reassessments

**Citation:** RTC § 11337

---

## Text

Between August 1 and August 21, the assessments made by the board shall be open for inspection by all persons interested.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1132, Sec. 9.)
