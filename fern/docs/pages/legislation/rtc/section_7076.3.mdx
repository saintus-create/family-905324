# RTC § 7076.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 8. Administration > Article: 2.5. Managed Audit Program

**Citation:** RTC § 7076.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the board’s authority to examine the books, papers, records, and equipment of a taxpayer under Section 7054.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 87, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
