# RTC § 13405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 13405

---

## Text

“Decedent” or “transferor” means any person whose death gives rise to a transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 363, Sec. 5. Effective September 8, 2000.)
