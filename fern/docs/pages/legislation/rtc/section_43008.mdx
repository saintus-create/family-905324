# RTC § 43008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 22. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCES TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 43008

---

## Text

Any fee administered and collected by the board pursuant to this part is a tax for purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1506, Sec. 15. Effective September 30, 1986.)
