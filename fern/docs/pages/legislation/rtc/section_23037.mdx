# RTC § 23037

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 23037

---

## Text

“Taxpayer” means any person subject to the tax imposed under Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 23101), Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 23400), or Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 23501).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 605, Sec. 46. Effective January 1, 1998.)
