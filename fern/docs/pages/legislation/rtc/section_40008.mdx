# RTC § 40008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 19. ENERGY RESOURCES SURCHARGE LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 40008

---

## Text

“Consumption” means the utilization or employment of electrical energy. The term does not include the receiving of electrical energy by an electric utility for resale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 991.)
