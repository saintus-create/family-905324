# RTC § 24679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 13. Accounting Periods and Methods of Accounting > Article: 3. Year of Inclusion

**Citation:** RTC § 24679

---

## Text

For purposes of Sections 24675 through 24678, a fractional part of a month shall be disregarded unless it amounts to more than half a month, in which case it should be considered as a month.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 846.)
