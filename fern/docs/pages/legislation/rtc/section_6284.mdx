# RTC § 6284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1. SALES AND USE TAXES > Chapter: 3.5. Vehicles, Vessels and Aircraft > Article: 2. Special Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 6284

---

## Text

If a person is engaged in the business of selling vehicles, mobilehomes, commercial coaches, vessels or aircraft he or she shall not be excused from the requirements of Article 2 (commencing with Section 6066) of Chapter 2 of this part, by reason of the exemptions provided in Sections 6282 and 6283.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1589, Sec. 11.)
