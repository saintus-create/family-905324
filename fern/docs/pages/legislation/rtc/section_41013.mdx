# RTC § 41013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 41013

---

## Text

“Surcharge” means a tax or taxes levied by this state. Commencing January 1, 2023, “surcharge,” or “surcharges” as used in this part, refers to two separate charges, one related to 911 service and one related to 988 service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 747, Sec. 7. (AB 988) Effective September 29, 2022.)
