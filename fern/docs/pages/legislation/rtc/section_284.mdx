# RTC § 284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 3. Audit of Veterans’ Exemption Claims

**Citation:** RTC § 284

---

## Text

Should the audit prescribed by this article indicate that a veteran’s exemption has been incorrectly allowed, the assessor shall be notified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 148.)
