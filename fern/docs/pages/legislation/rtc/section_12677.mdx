# RTC § 12677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 5. Payment and Collection > Article: 3. Suits for Taxes

**Citation:** RTC § 12677

---

## Text

The provisions of the Code of Civil Procedure relating to service of summons, pleadings, proofs, trials, and appeals are applicable to an action brought pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 113.)
