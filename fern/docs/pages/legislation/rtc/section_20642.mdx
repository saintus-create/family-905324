# RTC § 20642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** RTC § 20642

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided by this part, the Franchise Tax Board shall administer and enforce this part and the provisions of Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 19501) of Part 10.2 shall apply to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 374, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2003.)
