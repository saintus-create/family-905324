# RTC § 1137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Special Types of Property > Article: 5. Vessels

**Citation:** RTC § 1137

---

## Text

Where a ferry connects points in more than one county, the wharves, storehouses, and stationary property connected with it shall be assessed in the county where located, and the ferryboats shall be assessed in equal proportions in the counties it connects.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 154.)
