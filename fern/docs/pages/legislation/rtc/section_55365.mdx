# RTC § 55365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** RTC § 55365

---

## Text

Any prosecution for violation of any provision of this part shall be instituted within three years after the commission of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
