# RTC § 55245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 30. FEE COLLECTION PROCEDURES LAW > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 55245

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
