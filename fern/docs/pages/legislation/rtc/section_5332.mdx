# RTC § 5332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 10. AIRCRAFT ASSESSMENT AND TAXATION > Chapter: 2. Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 5332

---

## Text

Aircraft owned by this State or by any county, city and county, city, district, political subdivision, public corporation or authority of this State are exempt from personal property taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1703.)
