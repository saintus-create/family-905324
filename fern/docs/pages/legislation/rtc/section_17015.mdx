# RTC § 17015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10. PERSONAL INCOME TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 17015

---

## Text

“Nonresident” means every individual other than a resident.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 939.)
