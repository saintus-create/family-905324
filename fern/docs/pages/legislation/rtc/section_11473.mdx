# RTC § 11473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 4. Collection of Tax > Article: 2. Suit for Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 11473

---

## Text

In the action a writ of attachment may be issued in the manner provided by Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 485.010) of Title 6.5 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure without the showing required by Section 485.010 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11472 by Stats. 1998, Ch. 695, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1999.)
