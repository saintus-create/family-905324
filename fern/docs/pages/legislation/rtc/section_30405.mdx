# RTC § 30405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 6. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 30405

---

## Text

Failure to bring a suit or an action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of an alleged overpayment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1040.)
