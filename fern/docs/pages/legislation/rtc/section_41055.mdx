# RTC § 41055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 20. EMERGENCY TELEPHONE USERS SURCHARGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Surcharge > Article: 1. Due and Payable Dates

**Citation:** RTC § 41055

---

## Text

All amounts required to be paid to the state under this part shall be paid to the department in the form of remittances payable to the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 432, Sec. 72. (SB 824) Effective January 1, 2022.)
