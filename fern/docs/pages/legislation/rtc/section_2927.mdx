# RTC § 2927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** RTC § 2927

---

## Text

The collector of taxes on unsecured property shall prepare a delinquent roll or abstract list of unpaid items from the unsecured roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 489.)
