# RTC § 11571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 6. PRIVATE RAILROAD CAR TAX > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 11571

---

## Text

No injunction or writ of mandate or other legal or equitable process shall issue in any suit, action, or proceeding in any court against this State or against any officer of the State to prevent or enjoin under this part the assessment of property or the collection of any amount required to be collected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 41.)
