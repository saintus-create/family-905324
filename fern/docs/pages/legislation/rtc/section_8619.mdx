# RTC § 8619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 8619

---

## Text

“Interstate user” includes any person who uses fuel in the propulsion of a motor vehicle in this state and who operates the motor vehicle within and without the state or the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 555, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1996.)
