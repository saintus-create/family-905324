# RTC § 8853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 3. USE FUEL TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 5. Redeterminations

**Citation:** RTC § 8853

---

## Text

The order or decision of the department upon a petition for redetermination becomes final 30 days after serving of notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 499, Sec. 28. (SB 1528) Effective January 1, 2025.)
