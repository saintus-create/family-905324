# RTC § 46013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 46013

---

## Text

“Feepayer” means any person liable for the payment of a fee imposed by either Section 8670.40 or 8670.48 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 165. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014.)
