# RTC § 16750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 9.5. GENERATION SKIPPING TRANSFER TAX > Chapter: 3. Payment of Tax > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 16750

---

## Text

The person liable for payment of the federal generation skipping transfer tax shall be liable for the tax imposed by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1079.)
