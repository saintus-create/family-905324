# RTC § 46525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 24. OIL SPILL RESPONSE, PREVENTION, AND ADMINISTRATION FEES > Chapter: 5. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 2. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 46525

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the state on account of any alleged overpayments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. Effective August 1, 1991.)
