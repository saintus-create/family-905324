# RTC § 8149.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 2. MOTOR VEHICLE FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 7. Overpayments and Refunds > Article: 3. Suit for Refund

**Citation:** RTC § 8149.5

---

## Text

Failure to bring suit or action within the time specified in this article constitutes a waiver of all demands against the State on account of any alleged claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 508.)
