# RTC § 30156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 3. Licenses, Bonds and Registration > Article: 3. Wholesalers

**Citation:** RTC § 30156

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged nor shall a bond be required of any person for the issuance to him of a wholesaler’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1187.)
