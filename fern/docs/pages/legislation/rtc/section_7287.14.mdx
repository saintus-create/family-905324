# RTC § 7287.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.11. City of Lancaster Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 7287.14

---

## Text

If, as of January 1, 2029, an ordinance or citizens’ initiative proposing a transactions and use tax has not been approved as required by Section 7287.13, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 961, Sec. 2. (AB 2443) Effective September 29, 2024. Conditionally repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.11, commencing with Section 7287.13.)
