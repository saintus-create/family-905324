# RTC § 24471.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 8. Corporate Distributions and Adjustments

**Citation:** RTC § 24471.5

---

## Text

Section 381(c)(20) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to carryforward of disallowed business interest, shall not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 231, Sec. 108. (SB 711) Effective October 1, 2025.)
