# RTC § 12259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 7. INSURANCE TAXATION > Chapter: 3. All Insurance Other Than Ocean Marine > Article: 5. Prepayments

**Citation:** RTC § 12259

---

## Text

The provisions of this article apply to the State Compensation Insurance Fund as well as to private insurers.
