# RTC § 7292.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 1.7. ADDITIONAL LOCAL TAXES > Chapter: 3.75. Local Government Finance in the City of Alameda

**Citation:** RTC § 7292.6

---

## Text

If, as of January 1, 2025, an ordinance proposing a transactions and use tax pursuant to this chapter has not been approved as required by paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 7292.5, this chapter shall be repealed as of that same date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 502, Sec. 1. (AB 366) Effective January 1, 2017. Repealed conditionally on January 1, 2025, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 3.75, commencing with Section 7292.5.)
