# RTC § 255.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 2. ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Taxation Base > Article: 2. Procedure to Claim Exemptions

**Citation:** RTC § 255.7

---

## Text

Whenever a change of ownership is recorded in the county recorder’s office, the county recorder shall provide the assessor with a copy of the transfer of ownership document as soon as possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 60.)
