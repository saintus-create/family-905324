# RTC § 30103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 2. Imposition of Tax > Article: 1. Tax on Distributors

**Citation:** RTC § 30103

---

## Text

The taxes imposed by this part shall not apply to the sale of cigarettes or tobacco products by the manufacturer to a licensed distributor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 634, Sec. 10. Effective September 21, 1989.)
