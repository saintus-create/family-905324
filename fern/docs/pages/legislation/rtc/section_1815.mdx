# RTC § 1815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 3. EQUALIZATION > Chapter: 2. Equalization by State Board of Equalization > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 1815

## Text

When valuing property, the board shall follow the provisions set forth in Section 402.5.
