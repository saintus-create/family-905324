# RTC § 23186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 2. The Corporation Franchise Tax > Article: 3. Tax on Banks and Financial Corporations

**Citation:** RTC § 23186

---

## Text

For taxable years ending on or after December 31, 1995, the rate of tax on banks and financial corporations shall be the rate of tax specified in Section 23151, plus 2 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 862, Sec. 55. Effective January 1, 2001.)
