# RTC § 2962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 5. COLLECTION OF TAXES > Chapter: 4. Collection on the Unsecured Roll > Article: 2. Seizure and Sale

**Citation:** RTC § 2962

---

## Text

The unsold portion of any property may be left at the place of sale at the risk of the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2920 by Stats. 1974, Ch. 908.)
