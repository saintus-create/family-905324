# RTC § 20639

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.5. SENIOR CITIZENS PROPERTY TAX ASSISTANCE AND POSTPONEMENT LAW > Chapter: 3.3. Senior Citizens Manufactured Home Property Tax Postponement Law

**Citation:** RTC § 20639

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Senior Citizens Manufactured Home Property Tax Postponement Law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 896, Sec. 15. (SB 1130) Effective January 1, 2019. Operative July 1, 2019, pursuant to 20639.13.)
