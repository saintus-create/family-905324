# RTC § 23701j

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 4. Exempt Corporations > Article: 1. Exemptions From This Part

**Citation:** RTC § 23701j

## Text

A teacher’s retirement fund association described in Section 501(c)(11) of the Internal Revenue Code.
