# RTC § 24991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 11. CORPORATION TAX LAW > Chapter: 15. Gain or Loss on Disposition of Property > Article: 4.5. Capital Gains and Losses

**Citation:** RTC § 24991

---

## Text

Section 1275(a)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code, relating to the definition of tax-exempt obligation, does not apply but instead the term “tax-exempt obligation” means an obligation the interest on which is exempt from tax under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 185, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 2004.)
