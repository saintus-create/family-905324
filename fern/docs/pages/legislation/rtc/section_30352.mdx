# RTC § 30352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

## Text

In all proceedings under this chapter the board may act on behalf of the people of the State of California.
