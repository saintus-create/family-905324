# RTC § 4651.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 8. DISTRIBUTION > Chapter: 1. Generally

**Citation:** RTC § 4651.2

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided by law, any amount distributed to the State pursuant to this part shall be deposited in the State General Fund.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 430.)
