# RTC § 38901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 18.5. TIMBER YIELD TAX > Chapter: 10. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** RTC § 38901

---

## Text

All taxes, interest and penalties imposed and all amounts of tax required to be paid under this part shall be made in remittances to the State Board of Equalization and shall be deposited in the State Treasury as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
