# RTC § 4673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PROPERTY TAXATION > Part: 8. DISTRIBUTION > Chapter: 1.3. Distribution of Proceeds From Sale of Tax-Deeded Property

**Citation:** RTC § 4673

---

## Text

Amounts to reimburse the county for the cost of advertising sales of tax-defaulted property shall be distributed to the county general fund as provided in Section 3719.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 316, Sec. 68.)
