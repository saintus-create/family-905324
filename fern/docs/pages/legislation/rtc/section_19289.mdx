# RTC § 19289

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 5. Collection of Tax > Article: 6. Collection of Amounts Imposed by CalSavers Retirement Savings Board and Related Appeals

**Citation:** RTC § 19289

---

## Text

Amounts collected under this article shall be transmitted to the CalSavers Retirement Savings Board for deposit in the CalSavers Retirement Savings Trust pursuant to Title 21 (commencing with Section 100000) of the Government Code, or remitted by means of the Fi$Cal accounting system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 21, Sec. 15. (AB 102) Effective June 29, 2020.)
