# RTC § 32005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 14. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGE TAX > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 32005

---

## Text

“Taxpayer,” means a person liable for the payment of a tax pursuant to this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1842.)
