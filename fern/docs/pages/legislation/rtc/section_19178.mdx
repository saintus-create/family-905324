# RTC § 19178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 10.2. ADMINISTRATION OF FRANCHISE AND INCOME TAX LAWS > Chapter: 4. Payments and Assessments > Article: 7. Penalties and Additions to Tax

**Citation:** RTC § 19178

---

## Text

A penalty shall be imposed for aiding and abetting understatement of tax liability. The penalty shall be determined in accordance with Section 6701 of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 31, Sec. 26. Effective June 16, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 31.)
