# RTC § 13810.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 8. PROHIBITION OF GIFT AND DEATH TAXES > Chapter: 7. Collection of Tax > Article: 6. Tax Compromise

**Citation:** RTC § 13810.1

---

## Text

As used in this article, “state” means any state, territory, or possession of the United States, and the District of Columbia.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1535, Sec. 15.)
