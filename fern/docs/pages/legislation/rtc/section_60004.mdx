# RTC § 60004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 31. DIESEL FUEL TAX LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** RTC § 60004

---

## Text

“Refinery” means a facility used to produce diesel fuel from crude oil, unfinished oils, natural gas liquids, or other hydrocarbons and from which diesel fuel may be removed by pipeline, by vessel, or at a rack.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 912, Sec. 17. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative July 1, 1995, by Sec. 26 of Ch. 912.)
