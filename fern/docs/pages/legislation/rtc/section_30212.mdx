# RTC § 30212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OTHER TAXES > Part: 13. CIGARETTE TAX > Chapter: 4. Determinations > Article: 2.5. Payment by Unlicensed Persons

**Citation:** RTC § 30212

---

## Text

The board shall forthwith collect the tax, penalty, and interest due from the unlicensed person by seizure and sale of property in the manner prescribed for the collection of a delinquent monthly tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 890, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2004.)
