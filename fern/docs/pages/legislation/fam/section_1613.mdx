# FAM § 1613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 5. MARITAL AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 2. Uniform Premarital Agreement Act > Article: 2. Premarital Agreements

**Citation:** FAM § 1613

---

## Text

A premarital agreement becomes effective upon marriage.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
