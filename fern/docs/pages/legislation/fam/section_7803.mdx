# FAM § 7803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 4. FREEDOM FROM PARENTAL CUSTODY AND CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 7803

---

## Text

A declaration of freedom from parental custody and control pursuant to this part terminates all parental rights and responsibilities with regard to the child.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
