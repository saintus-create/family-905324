# FAM § 7632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship > Article: 1. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship

**Citation:** FAM § 7632

---

## Text

Regardless of its terms, an agreement between an alleged father or a presumed parent and the other parent or child does not bar an action under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 510, Sec. 9. (AB 1403) Effective January 1, 2014.)
