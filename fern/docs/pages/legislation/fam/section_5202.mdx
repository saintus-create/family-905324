# FAM § 5202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 8. Earnings Assignment Order > Article: 1. Definitions

## Text

“Assignment order” has the same meaning as “earnings assignment order for support.”
