# FAM § 270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 5. ATTORNEY'S FEES AND COSTS

**Citation:** FAM § 270

---

## Text

If a court orders a party to pay attorney’s fees or costs under this code, the court shall first determine that the party has or is reasonably likely to have the ability to pay.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 87. Effective January 1, 1994.)
