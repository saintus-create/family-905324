# FAM § 2300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 3. DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Chapter: 1. Effect of Dissolution

**Citation:** FAM § 2300

---

## Text

The effect of a judgment of dissolution of marriage when it becomes final is to restore the parties to the state of unmarried persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
