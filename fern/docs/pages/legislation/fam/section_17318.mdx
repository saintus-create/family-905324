# FAM § 17318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SUPPORT SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Department of Child Support Services > Article: 3. Director of Child Support Services

**Citation:** FAM § 17318

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided, Part 1 (commencing with Section 11000) of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, as it may be added to or amended from time to time, shall apply to the conduct of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 478, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
