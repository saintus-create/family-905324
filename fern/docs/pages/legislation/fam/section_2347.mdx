# FAM § 2347

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 3. DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Chapter: 4. General Procedural Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 2347

---

## Text

A judgment of legal separation of the parties does not bar a subsequent judgment of dissolution of the marriage granted pursuant to a petition for dissolution filed by either party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
