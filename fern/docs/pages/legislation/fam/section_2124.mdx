# FAM § 2124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Relief From Judgment

**Citation:** FAM § 2124

---

## Text

The negligence of an attorney shall not be imputed to a client to bar an order setting aside a judgment, unless the court finds that the client knew, or should have known, of the attorney’s negligence and unreasonably failed to self-protect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 21. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)
