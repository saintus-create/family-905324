# FAM § 5230.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 8. Earnings Assignment Order > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 5230.5

---

## Text

Any obligee alleging arrearages in child support shall specify the amount thereof under penalty of perjury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1140, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
