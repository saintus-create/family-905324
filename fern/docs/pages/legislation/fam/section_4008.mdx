# FAM § 4008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4008

---

## Text

The community property, the quasi-community property, and the separate property may be subjected to the support of the children in the proportions the court determines are just.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
