# FAM § 7602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 7602

---

## Text

The parent and child relationship extends equally to every child and to every parent, regardless of the marital status of the parents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
