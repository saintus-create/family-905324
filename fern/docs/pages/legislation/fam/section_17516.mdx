# FAM § 17516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SUPPORT SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Child Support Enforcement > Article: 2. Collections and Enforcement

**Citation:** FAM § 17516

---

## Text

In no event shall public social service benefits, as defined in Section 10051 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, or benefits paid pursuant to Title XVI of the Social Security Act be employed to satisfy a support obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 478, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
