# FAM § 3175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 11. Mediation of Custody and Visitation Issues > Article: 3. Mediation Proceedings

**Citation:** FAM § 3175

---

## Text

If a matter is set for mediation pursuant to this chapter, the mediation shall be set before or concurrent with the setting of the matter for hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.87. Effective January 1, 1994.)
