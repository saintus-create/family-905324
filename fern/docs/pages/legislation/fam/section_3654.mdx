# FAM § 3654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Modification,Termination, or Set Aside of Support Orders > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3654

---

## Text

At the request of either party, an order modifying, terminating, or setting aside a support order shall include a statement of decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 653, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative January 1, 1994.)
