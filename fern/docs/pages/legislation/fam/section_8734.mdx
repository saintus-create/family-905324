# FAM § 8734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 2.5. Adoptions by Relative Caregivers or Foster Parents

**Citation:** FAM § 8734

---

## Text

The department shall encourage adoption agencies to make adoption training programs available to prospective adoptive families.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 983, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
