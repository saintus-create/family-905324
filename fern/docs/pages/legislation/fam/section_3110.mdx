# FAM § 3110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 6. Custody Investigation and Report

**Citation:** FAM § 3110

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “court-appointed investigator” means a probation officer, domestic relations investigator, or court-appointed evaluator directed by the court to conduct an investigation pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.81. Effective January 1, 1994.)
