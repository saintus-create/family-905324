# FAM § 301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 1. VALIDITY OF MARRIAGE

**Citation:** FAM § 301

---

## Text

Two unmarried persons 18 years of age or older, who are not otherwise disqualified, are capable of consenting to and consummating marriage.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 3. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
