# FAM § 751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Property Rights During Marriage

**Citation:** FAM § 751

---

## Text

The respective interests of each spouse in community property during continuance of the marriage relation are present, existing, and equal interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 14. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
