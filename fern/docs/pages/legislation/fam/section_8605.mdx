# FAM § 8605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 8605

---

## Text

A child not having a presumed father under Section 7611 may not be adopted without the consent of the child’s mother, if living.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
