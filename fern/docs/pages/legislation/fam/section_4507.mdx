# FAM § 4507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4507

---

## Text

When a court orders a person to make payment for child support or family support, the court may order that individual to make that payment as provided in Section 1151.5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 176, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
