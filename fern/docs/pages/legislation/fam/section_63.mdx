# FAM § 63

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 63

---

## Text

“Community estate” includes both community property and quasi-community property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 79.3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
