# FAM § 7800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 4. FREEDOM FROM PARENTAL CUSTODY AND CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 7800

---

## Text

The purpose of this part is to serve the welfare and best interest of a child by providing the stability and security of an adoptive home when those conditions are otherwise missing from the child’s life.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
