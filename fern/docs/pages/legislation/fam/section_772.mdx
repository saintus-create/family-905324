# FAM § 772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 2. CHARACTERIZATION OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2. Separate Property

**Citation:** FAM § 772

---

## Text

After entry of a judgment of legal separation of the parties, the earnings or accumulations of each party are the separate property of the party acquiring the earnings or accumulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
