# FAM § 4402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 4. SUPPORT OF PARENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4402

---

## Text

The duty of support under this part is cumulative and not in substitution for any other duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
