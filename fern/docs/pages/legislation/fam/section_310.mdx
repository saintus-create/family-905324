# FAM § 310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 1. VALIDITY OF MARRIAGE

**Citation:** FAM § 310

---

## Text

Marriage is dissolved only by one of the following:

(a) The death of one of the parties.

(b) A judgment of dissolution of marriage.

(c) A judgment of nullity of marriage.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
