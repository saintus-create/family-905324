# FAM § 8711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 2. Agency Adoptions

**Citation:** FAM § 8711

---

## Text

Sections 8708 to 8710.4, inclusive, apply only in determining the placement of a child who has been relinquished for adoption or has been declared free from the custody and control of the birth parents.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1056, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1999.)
