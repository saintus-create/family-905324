# FAM § 7142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 6. EMANCIPATION OF MINORS LAW > Chapter: 3. Court Declaration of Emancipation > Article: 4. Identification Cards and Information

**Citation:** FAM § 7142

---

## Text

No public entity or employee is liable for any loss or injury resulting directly or indirectly from false or inaccurate information contained in the Department of Motor Vehicles records system or identification cards as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
