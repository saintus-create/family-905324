# FAM § 20014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PILOT PROJECTS > Part: 1. FAMILY LAW PILOT PROJECTS > Chapter: 2. San Mateo County Pilot Project

**Citation:** FAM § 20014

---

## Text

The clerk shall stamp all moving papers in which a party is not represented by counsel with a notice of a requirement to see the Family Law Evaluator. The unrepresented party shall serve the stamped pleadings on the other party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)
