# FAM § 2112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Disclosure of Assets and Liabilities

**Citation:** FAM § 2112

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall adopt appropriate forms and modify existing forms to effectuate the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 14. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)
