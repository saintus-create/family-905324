# FAM § 7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 7

---

## Text

Whenever a reference is made to a portion of this code or to another law, the reference applies to all amendments and additions regardless of when made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
