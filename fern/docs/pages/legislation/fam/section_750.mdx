# FAM § 750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Property Rights During Marriage

**Citation:** FAM § 750

---

## Text

Spouses may hold property as joint tenants or tenants in common, or as community property, or as community property with a right of survivorship.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 13. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
