# FAM § 17432.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SUPPORT SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Child Support Enforcement > Article: 1. Support Obligations

**Citation:** FAM § 17432.5

---

## Text

No later than September 1, 2024, the Judicial Council shall adopt and approve any forms necessary to implement Sections 17400, 17404.1, 17430, and 17432 as added by the act that added this section. Forms adopted pursuant to this section shall have an effective date of January 1, 2026.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 213, Sec. 22. (SB 343) Effective January 1, 2024.)
