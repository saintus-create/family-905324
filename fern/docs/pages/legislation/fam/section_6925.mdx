# FAM § 6925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 4. MEDICAL TREATMENT > Chapter: 3. Consent by Minor

**Citation:** FAM § 6925

---

## Text

(a) A minor may consent to medical care related to the prevention or treatment of pregnancy.

(b) This section does not authorize a minor to be sterilized without the consent of the minor’s parent or guardian.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 136, Sec. 12. (AB 260) Effective September 26, 2025.)
