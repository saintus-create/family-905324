# FAM § 5700.318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 3. Civil Provisions of General Application

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.318

---

## Text

A tribunal of this state may:

(1) request a tribunal outside this state to assist in obtaining discovery; and

(2) upon request, compel a person over which it has jurisdiction to respond to a discovery order issued by a tribunal outside this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
