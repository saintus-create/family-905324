# FAM § 7639

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship > Article: 1. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship

**Citation:** FAM § 7639

---

## Text

If the judgment or order of the court is at variance with the child’s birth certificate, the court shall order that a new birth certificate be issued as prescribed in Article 2 (commencing with Section 102725) of Chapter 5 of Part 1 of Division 102 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 48. Effective September 29, 1996.)
