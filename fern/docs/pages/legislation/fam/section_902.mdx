# FAM § 902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 3. LIABILITY OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 902

---

## Text

“Debt” means an obligation incurred by a married person before or during marriage, whether based on contract, tort, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
