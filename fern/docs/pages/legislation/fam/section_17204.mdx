# FAM § 17204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SUPPORT SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Department of Child Support Services > Article: 2. Organization

**Citation:** FAM § 17204

---

## Text

The department consists of the director and such division or other administrative units as the director may find necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 478, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
