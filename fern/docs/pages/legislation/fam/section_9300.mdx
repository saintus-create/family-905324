# FAM § 9300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 3. ADOPTION OF ADULTS AND MARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 9300

---

## Text

(a) An adult may be adopted by another adult, including a stepparent, as provided in this part.

(b) A married minor may be adopted in the same manner as an adult under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 266, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
