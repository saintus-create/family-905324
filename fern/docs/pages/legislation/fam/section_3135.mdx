# FAM § 3135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 8. Location of Missing Party or Child

**Citation:** FAM § 3135

---

## Text

Part 3 (commencing with Section 3400) does not limit the authority of a district attorney or arresting agency to act pursuant to this chapter, Section 279.6 of the Penal Code, or any other applicable law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
