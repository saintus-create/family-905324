# FAM § 3930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 1. Duty of Parent to Support Child > Article: 3. Support of Grandchild

**Citation:** FAM § 3930

---

## Text

A parent does not have the duty to support a child of the parent’s child.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 132. Effective January 1, 1994.)
