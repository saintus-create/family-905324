# FAM § 3457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 3. UNIFORM CHILD CUSTODY JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT ACT > Chapter: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** FAM § 3457

---

## Text

The court may assess all direct expenses and costs incurred by a district attorney under Section 3455 or 3456 pursuant to the provisions of Section 3134.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
