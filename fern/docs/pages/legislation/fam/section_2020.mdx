# FAM § 2020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Procedural Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 2020

---

## Text

A responsive pleading, if any, shall be filed and a copy served on the petitioner within 30 days of the date of the service on the respondent of a copy of the petition and summons.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 581, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
