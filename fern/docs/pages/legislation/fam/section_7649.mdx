# FAM § 7649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship > Article: 1.5. Setting Aside or Vacating Judgment of Parentage

**Citation:** FAM § 7649

---

## Text

This article does not limit the rights and remedies available under any other law with regard to setting aside or vacating a judgment of parentage.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 62. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)
