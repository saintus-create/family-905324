# FAM § 20036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PILOT PROJECTS > Part: 1. FAMILY LAW PILOT PROJECTS > Chapter: 3. Santa Clara County Pilot Project

**Citation:** FAM § 20036

---

## Text

Upon motion by either party or on the court’s own motion, any proceeding that would otherwise fall within this pilot project may by judicial order be exempted from its requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)
