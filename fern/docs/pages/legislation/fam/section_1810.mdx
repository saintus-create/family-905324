# FAM § 1810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CONCILIATION PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. FAMILY CONCILIATION COURT LAW > Chapter: 2. Family Conciliation Courts

**Citation:** FAM § 1810

---

## Text

Each superior court shall exercise the jurisdiction conferred by this part. While sitting in the exercise of this jurisdiction, the court shall be known and referred to as the “family conciliation court.”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
