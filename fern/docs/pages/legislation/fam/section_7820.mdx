# FAM § 7820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 4. FREEDOM FROM PARENTAL CUSTODY AND CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Circumstances Where Proceeding May Be Brought

**Citation:** FAM § 7820

---

## Text

A proceeding may be brought under this part for the purpose of having a child under the age of 18 years declared free from the custody and control of either or both parents if the child comes within any of the descriptions set out in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
