# FAM § 4501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4501

---

## Text

A family support order is enforceable in the same manner and to the same extent as a child support order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
