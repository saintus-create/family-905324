# FAM § 7852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 4. FREEDOM FROM PARENTAL CUSTODY AND CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Procedure > Article: 3. Investigation and Report

**Citation:** FAM § 7852

## Text

“Qualified court investigator,” as used in this article, has the meaning provided by Section 8543.

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 182. Effective January 1, 1994.)
