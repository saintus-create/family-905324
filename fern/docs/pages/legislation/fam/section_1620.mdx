# FAM § 1620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 5. MARITAL AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 3. Agreements Between Spouses

**Citation:** FAM § 1620

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, spouses cannot, by a contract with each other, alter their legal relations, except as to property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 21. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
