# FAM § 5700.703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 7. Support Proceeding Under Convention

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.703

---

## Text

The Department of Child Support Services is recognized as the agency designated by the United States central authority to perform specific functions under the Convention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
