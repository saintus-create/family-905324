# FAM § 2253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 2. JUDICIAL DETERMINATION OF VOID OR VOIDABLE MARRIAGE > Chapter: 3. Procedural Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 2253

---

## Text

In a proceeding under this part, custody of the children shall be determined according to Division 8 (commencing with Section 3000).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
