# FAM § 3554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3554

---

## Text

An appeal may be taken from an order or judgment under this division as in other civil actions.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
