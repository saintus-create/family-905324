# FAM § 508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 4. CONFIDENTIAL MARRIAGE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 508

---

## Text

Upon issuance of a confidential marriage license, parties shall be provided with an application to obtain a certified copy of the confidential marriage certificate from the county clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 816, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative January 1, 2008, by Sec. 56 of Ch. 816.)
