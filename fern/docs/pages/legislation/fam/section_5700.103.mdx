# FAM § 5700.103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.103

---

## Text

(a) The superior court is the tribunal of this state.

(b) The Department of Child Support Services is the support enforcement agency of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
