# FAM § 3515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 3515

---

## Text

“Separate property” does not include quasi-community property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
