# FAM § 7502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 1. RIGHTS OF PARENTS

**Citation:** FAM § 7502

---

## Text

The parent, as such, has no control over the property of the child.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 165. Effective January 1, 1994.)
