# FAM § 4205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 3. Payment to Court Designated County Officer; Enforcement by District Attorney

**Citation:** FAM § 4205

---

## Text

Any notice from the local child support agency requesting a meeting with the support obligor for any purpose authorized under this part shall contain a statement advising the support obligor of the obligor’s right to have an attorney present at the meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 48. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)
