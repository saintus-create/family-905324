# FAM § 7909

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 5. INTERSTATE COMPACT ON PLACEMENT OF CHILDREN

**Citation:** FAM § 7909

---

## Text

“Executive head” as used in Article 7 of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children means the Governor. The Governor shall appoint a compact administrator in accordance with the terms of Article 7 of the compact.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
