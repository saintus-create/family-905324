# FAM § 5700.601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 6. Registration, Enforcement, and Modification of Support Order > Article: 1. Registration for Enforcement of Support Order

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.601

---

## Text

A support order or income-withholding order issued in another state or a foreign support order may be registered in this state for enforcement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
