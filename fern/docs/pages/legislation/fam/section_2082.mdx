# FAM § 2082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. Restoration of Spouse’s Former Name

**Citation:** FAM § 2082

---

## Text

This code does not abrogate the common law right of any person to change one’s name.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 780, Sec. 4. (SB 1150) Effective January 1, 2025.)
