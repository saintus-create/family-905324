# FAM § 305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 1. VALIDITY OF MARRIAGE

**Citation:** FAM § 305

---

## Text

Consent to and solemnization of marriage may be proved under the same general rules of evidence as facts are proved in other cases.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
