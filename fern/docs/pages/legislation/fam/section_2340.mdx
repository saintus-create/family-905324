# FAM § 2340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 3. DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Chapter: 4. General Procedural Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 2340

---

## Text

A judgment of dissolution of marriage shall specify the date on which the judgment becomes finally effective for the purpose of terminating the marriage relationship of the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
