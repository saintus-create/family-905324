# FAM § 10000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. FAMILY LAW FACILITATOR ACT

**Citation:** FAM § 10000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Family Law Facilitator Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 957, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1997.)
