# FAM § 7907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 5. INTERSTATE COMPACT ON PLACEMENT OF CHILDREN

**Citation:** FAM § 7907

---

## Text

No provision of law restricting out-of-state placement of children for adoption shall apply to placements made pursuant to the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
