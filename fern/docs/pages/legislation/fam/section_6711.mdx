# FAM § 6711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 3. CONTRACTS > Chapter: 2. Disaffirmance of Contracts

**Citation:** FAM § 6711

---

## Text

A minor cannot disaffirm an obligation, otherwise valid, entered into by the minor under the express authority or direction of a statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
