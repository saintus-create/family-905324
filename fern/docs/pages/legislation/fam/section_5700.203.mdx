# FAM § 5700.203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 2. Jurisdiction

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.203

---

## Text

Under this part, a tribunal of this state may serve as an initiating tribunal to forward proceedings to a tribunal of another state, and as a responding tribunal for proceedings initiated in another state or a foreign country.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
