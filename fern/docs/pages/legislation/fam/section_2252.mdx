# FAM § 2252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 2. JUDICIAL DETERMINATION OF VOID OR VOIDABLE MARRIAGE > Chapter: 3. Procedural Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 2252

---

## Text

The property divided pursuant to Section 2251 is liable for debts of the parties to the same extent as if the property had been community property or quasi-community property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
