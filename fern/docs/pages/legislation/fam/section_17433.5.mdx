# FAM § 17433.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SUPPORT SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Child Support Enforcement > Article: 1. Support Obligations

**Citation:** FAM § 17433.5

---

## Text

In any action enforced pursuant to this article, no interest shall accrue on an obligation for current child, spousal, family, or medical support due in a given month until the first day of the following month.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 75, Sec. 7. Effective July 12, 2006.)
