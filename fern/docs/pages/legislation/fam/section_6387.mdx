# FAM § 6387

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 4. PROTECTIVE ORDERS AND OTHER DOMESTIC VIOLENCE PREVENTION ORDERS > Chapter: 3. Registration and Enforcement of Orders

**Citation:** FAM § 6387

---

## Text

The court shall order the clerk of the court to provide to a petitioner, without cost, up to three certified, stamped, and endorsed copies of any order issued under this part, and of an extension, modification, or termination of the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 572, Sec. 22. (AB 1596) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 28 of Ch. 572.)
