# FAM § 308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 1. VALIDITY OF MARRIAGE

**Citation:** FAM § 308

---

## Text

A marriage contracted outside this state that would be valid by laws of the jurisdiction in which the marriage was contracted is valid in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 474, Sec. 4. (AB 2882) Effective January 1, 2017.)
