# FAM § 5700.712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 7. Support Proceeding Under Convention

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.712

---

## Text

Personal information gathered or transmitted under this chapter may be used only for the purposes for which it was gathered or transmitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
