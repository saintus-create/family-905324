# FAM § 2000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Application of Part

**Citation:** FAM § 2000

---

## Text

This part applies to a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, for nullity of marriage, or for legal separation of the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
