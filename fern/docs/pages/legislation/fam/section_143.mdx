# FAM § 143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 143

---

## Text

“Spouse” includes “registered domestic partner,” as required by Section 297.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 35. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
