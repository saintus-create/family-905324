# FAM § 2553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DIVISION OF PROPERTY > Part: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 2553

---

## Text

The court may make any orders the court considers necessary to carry out the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
