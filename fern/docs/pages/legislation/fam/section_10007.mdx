# FAM § 10007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. FAMILY LAW FACILITATOR ACT

**Citation:** FAM § 10007

---

## Text

The court shall provide the family law facilitator at no cost to the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 957, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1997.)
