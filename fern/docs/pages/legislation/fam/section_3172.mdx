# FAM § 3172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 11. Mediation of Custody and Visitation Issues > Article: 2. Availability of Mediation

**Citation:** FAM § 3172

---

## Text

Mediation shall not be denied to the parties on the basis that paternity is at issue in a proceeding before the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.87. Effective January 1, 1994.)
