# FAM § 1601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 5. MARITAL AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 2. Uniform Premarital Agreement Act > Article: 1. Preliminary Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 1601

---

## Text

This chapter is effective on and after January 1, 1986, and applies to any premarital agreement executed on or after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
