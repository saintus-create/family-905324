# FAM § 6406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 5. UNIFORM INTERSTATE ENFORCEMENT OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE PROTECTION ORDERS ACT

**Citation:** FAM § 6406

---

## Text

A protected individual who pursues remedies under this part is not precluded from pursuing other legal or equitable remedies against the respondent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 816, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
