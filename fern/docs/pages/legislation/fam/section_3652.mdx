# FAM § 3652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Modification,Termination, or Set Aside of Support Orders > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3652

---

## Text

Except as against a governmental agency, an order modifying, terminating, or setting aside a support order may include an award of attorney’s fees and court costs to the prevailing party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 653, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
