# FAM § 5201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 8. Earnings Assignment Order > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 5201

---

## Text

“Arrearage” or “arrearages” is the amount necessary to satisfy a support judgment or order pursuant to Section 695.210 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 599, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1998.)
