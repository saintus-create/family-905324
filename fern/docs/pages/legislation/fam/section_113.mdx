# FAM § 113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 113

---

## Text

“Property” includes real and personal property and any interest therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 21. Effective September 28, 2000.)
