# FAM § 3692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Modification,Termination, or Set Aside of Support Orders > Article: 4. Relief From Orders

**Citation:** FAM § 3692

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, or any other law, a support order may not be set aside simply because the court finds that it was inequitable when made, nor simply because subsequent circumstances caused the support ordered to become excessive or inadequate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 653, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2000.)
