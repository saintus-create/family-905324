# FAM § 5243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 8. Earnings Assignment Order > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 5243

---

## Text

An assignment order for support has priority as against any attachment, execution, or other assignment as specified in Section 706.031 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 876, Sec. 26. Effective October 6, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 876.)
