# FAM § 7130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 6. EMANCIPATION OF MINORS LAW > Chapter: 3. Court Declaration of Emancipation > Article: 3. Voiding or Rescinding Declaration

**Citation:** FAM § 7130

---

## Text

(a) A declaration of emancipation obtained by fraud or by the withholding of material information is voidable.

(b) A declaration of emancipation of a minor who is indigent and has no means of support is subject to rescission.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
