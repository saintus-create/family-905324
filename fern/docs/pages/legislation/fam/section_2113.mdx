# FAM § 2113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Disclosure of Assets and Liabilities

**Citation:** FAM § 2113

---

## Text

This chapter applies to any proceeding commenced on or after January 1, 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2109 (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219) by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 10. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)
