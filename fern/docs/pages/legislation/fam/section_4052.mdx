# FAM § 4052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 2. Statewide Uniform Guideline

**Citation:** FAM § 4052

---

## Text

The court shall adhere to the statewide uniform guideline and may depart from the guideline only in the special circumstances set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.)
