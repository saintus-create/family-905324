# FAM § 3403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 3. UNIFORM CHILD CUSTODY JURISDICTION AND ENFORCEMENT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3403

---

## Text

This part does not govern an adoption proceeding or a proceeding pertaining to the authorization of emergency medical care for a child.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 867, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
