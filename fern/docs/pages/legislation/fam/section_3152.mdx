# FAM § 3152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 10. Appointment of Counsel to Represent Child

**Citation:** FAM § 3152

---

## Text

(a) The child’s counsel may, upon noticed motion to all parties and the local child protective services agency, request the court to authorize release of relevant reports or files, concerning the child represented by the counsel, of the relevant local child protective services agency.

(b) The court shall review the reports or files in camera in order to determine whether they are relevant to the pending action and whether and to what extent they should be released to the child’s counsel.

(c) Neither the review by the court nor the release to counsel shall constitute a waiver of the confidentiality of the reports and files. Counsel shall not disclose the contents or existence of the reports or files to anyone unless otherwise permitted by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
