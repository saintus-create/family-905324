# FAM § 3604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Spousal and Child Support During Pendency of Proceeding

**Citation:** FAM § 3604

---

## Text

An order made pursuant to this chapter does not prejudice the rights of the parties or the child with respect to any subsequent order which may be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
