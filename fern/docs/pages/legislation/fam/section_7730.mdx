# FAM § 7730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 6. Protective and Restraining Orders > Article: 4. Orders Included in Judgment

**Citation:** FAM § 7730

---

## Text

A judgment entered in a proceeding under this part may include a protective order as defined in Section 6218 and any other restraining order as provided in Article 3 (commencing with Section 6360) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of Division 10.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 179.6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
