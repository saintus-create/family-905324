# FAM § 20002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PILOT PROJECTS > Part: 1. FAMILY LAW PILOT PROJECTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 20002

## Text

The duration of the pilot projects shall be two years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)
