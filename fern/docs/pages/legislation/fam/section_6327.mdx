# FAM § 6327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 4. PROTECTIVE ORDERS AND OTHER DOMESTIC VIOLENCE PREVENTION ORDERS > Chapter: 2. Issuance of Orders > Article: 1. Ex Parte Orders

**Citation:** FAM § 6327

---

## Text

Part 4 (commencing with Section 240) of Division 2 applies to the issuance of any ex parte order under this article, other than an order under Section 6322.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 511, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
