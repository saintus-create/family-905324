# FAM § 9007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 5. Stepparent Adoptions

**Citation:** FAM § 9007

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 9000.5, the prospective adoptive parent and the child proposed to be adopted shall appear before the court pursuant to Sections 8612, 8613, and 8613.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 636, Sec. 7. (AB 2344) Effective January 1, 2015.)
