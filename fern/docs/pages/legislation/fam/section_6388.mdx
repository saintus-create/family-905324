# FAM § 6388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 4. PROTECTIVE ORDERS AND OTHER DOMESTIC VIOLENCE PREVENTION ORDERS > Chapter: 3. Registration and Enforcement of Orders

**Citation:** FAM § 6388

---

## Text

A willful and knowing violation of a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, is a crime punishable as provided by Section 273.6 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)
