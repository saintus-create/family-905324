# FAM § 6256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 3. EMERGENCY PROTECTIVE ORDERS > Chapter: 2. Issuance and Effect of Emergency Protective Order

**Citation:** FAM § 6256

---

## Text

An emergency protective order expires at the earlier of the following times:

(a) The close of judicial business on the fifth court day following the day of its issuance.

(b) The seventh calendar day following the day of its issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219) by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1229, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
