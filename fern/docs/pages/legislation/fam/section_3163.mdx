# FAM § 3163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 11. Mediation of Custody and Visitation Issues > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3163

---

## Text

Courts shall develop local rules to respond to requests for a change of mediators or to general problems relating to mediation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.87. Effective January 1, 1994.)
