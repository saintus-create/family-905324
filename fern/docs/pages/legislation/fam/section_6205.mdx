# FAM § 6205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 1. SHORT TITLE AND DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 6205

---

## Text

“Affinity,” when applied to the marriage relation, signifies the connection existing in consequence of marriage between each of the married persons and the blood relatives of the other.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)
