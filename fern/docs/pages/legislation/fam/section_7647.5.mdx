# FAM § 7647.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship > Article: 1.5. Setting Aside or Vacating Judgment of Parentage

**Citation:** FAM § 7647.5

---

## Text

A guardian ad litem may be appointed for the child to represent the best interests of the child in an action brought pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 849, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2005.)
