# FAM § 7670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 5. Termination of Parental Rights in Adoption Proceedings

**Citation:** FAM § 7670

## Text

There shall be no filing fee charged for a petition filed pursuant to Section 7662.
