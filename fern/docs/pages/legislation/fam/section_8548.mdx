# FAM § 8548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 8548

---

## Text

“Stepparent adoption” means an adoption of a child by a stepparent where one birth parent retains custody and control of the child.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
