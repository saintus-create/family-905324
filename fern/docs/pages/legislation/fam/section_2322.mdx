# FAM § 2322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 3. DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Chapter: 3. Residence Requirements

**Citation:** FAM § 2322

---

## Text

For the purpose of a proceeding for dissolution of marriage, each spouse may have a separate domicile or residence depending upon proof of the fact and not upon legal presumptions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 27. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
