# FAM § 6901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 4. MEDICAL TREATMENT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 6901

---

## Text

“Dental care” means X-ray examination, anesthetic, dental or surgical diagnosis or treatment, and hospital care by a dentist licensed under the Dental Practice Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
