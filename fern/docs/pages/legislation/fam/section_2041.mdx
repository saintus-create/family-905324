# FAM § 2041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Protective and Restraining Orders > Article: 1. Orders in Summons

**Citation:** FAM § 2041

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 2040 adversely affects the rights, title, and interest of a purchaser for value, encumbrancer for value, or lessee for value who is without actual knowledge of the restraining order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 106.7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
