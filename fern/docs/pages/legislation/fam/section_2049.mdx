# FAM § 2049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Protective and Restraining Orders > Article: 4. Orders Included in Judgment

**Citation:** FAM § 2049

---

## Text

A judgment may include a protective order, as defined in Section 6218, and any other restraining order as provided in Article 3 (commencing with Section 6360) of Chapter 2 of Part 4 of Division 10.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 106.7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
