# FAM § 8600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 8600

---

## Text

An unmarried minor may be adopted by an adult as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
