# FAM § 4074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 2. Statewide Uniform Guideline

**Citation:** FAM § 4074

---

## Text

This article applies to an award for the support of children, including those awards designated as “family support,” that contain provisions for the support of children as well as for the support of the spouse.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.)
