# FAM § 4720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 5. Civil Penalty for Child Support Delinquency

## Text

“Support” for the purposes of this chapter means support as defined in Section 150.
