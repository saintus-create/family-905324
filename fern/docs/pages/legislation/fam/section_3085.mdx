# FAM § 3085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 4. Joint Custody

**Citation:** FAM § 3085

---

## Text

In making an order for custody with respect to both parents, the court may grant joint legal custody without granting joint physical custody.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.72. Effective January 1, 1994.)
