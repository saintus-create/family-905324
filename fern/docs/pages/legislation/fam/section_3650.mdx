# FAM § 3650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Modification,Termination, or Set Aside of Support Orders > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3650

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, as used in this chapter, “support order” means a child, family, or spousal support order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 124.5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
