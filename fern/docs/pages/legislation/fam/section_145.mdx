# FAM § 145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 145

---

## Text

“State” means a state of the United States, the District of Columbia, or a commonwealth, territory, or insular possession subject to the jurisdiction of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 661, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative January 1, 1994.)
