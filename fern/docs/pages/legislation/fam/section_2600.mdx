# FAM § 2600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DIVISION OF PROPERTY > Part: 4. SPECIAL RULES FOR DIVISION OF COMMUNITY ESTATE

**Citation:** FAM § 2600

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 2550 to 2552, inclusive, the court may divide the community estate as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
