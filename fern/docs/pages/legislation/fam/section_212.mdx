# FAM § 212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. GENERAL PROCEDURAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 212

---

## Text

A petition, response, application, opposition, or other pleading filed with the court under this code shall be verified.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
