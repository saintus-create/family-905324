# FAM § 6254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 3. EMERGENCY PROTECTIVE ORDERS > Chapter: 2. Issuance and Effect of Emergency Protective Order

**Citation:** FAM § 6254

---

## Text

The fact that the endangered person has left the household to avoid abuse does not affect the availability of an emergency protective order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)
