# FAM § 9305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 3. ADOPTION OF ADULTS AND MARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 9305

---

## Text

After adoption, the adoptee and the adoptive parent or parents shall sustain towards each other the legal relationship of parent and child and have all the rights and are subject to all the duties of that relationship.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
