# FAM § 8719

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 2. Agency Adoptions

**Citation:** FAM § 8719

---

## Text

If the petitioners move to withdraw the adoption petition or to dismiss the proceeding, the court clerk shall immediately notify the department at Sacramento of the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
