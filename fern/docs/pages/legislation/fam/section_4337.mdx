# FAM § 4337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 3. SPOUSAL SUPPORT > Chapter: 3. Spousal Support Upon Dissolution or Legal Separation

**Citation:** FAM § 4337

---

## Text

Except as otherwise agreed by the parties in writing, the obligation of a party under an order for the support of the other party terminates upon the death of either party or the remarriage of the other party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
