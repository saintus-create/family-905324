# FAM § 2111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Disclosure of Assets and Liabilities

**Citation:** FAM § 2111

---

## Text

A disclosure required by this chapter does not abrogate the attorney work product privilege or impede the power of the court to issue protective orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 13. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)
