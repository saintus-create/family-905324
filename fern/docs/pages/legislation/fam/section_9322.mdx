# FAM § 9322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 3. ADOPTION OF ADULTS AND MARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 2. Procedure for Adult Adoption

**Citation:** FAM § 9322

---

## Text

When the petition for approval of the adoption agreement is filed, the court clerk shall set the matter for hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
