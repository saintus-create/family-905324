# FAM § 903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 3. LIABILITY OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 903

---

## Text

A debt is “incurred” at the following time:

(a) In the case of a contract, at the time the contract is made.

(b) In the case of a tort, at the time the tort occurs.

(c) In other cases, at the time the obligation arises.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
