# FAM § 2621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DIVISION OF PROPERTY > Part: 6. DEBTS AND LIABILITIES

**Citation:** FAM § 2621

---

## Text

Debts incurred by either spouse before the date of marriage shall be confirmed without offset to the spouse who incurred the debt.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
