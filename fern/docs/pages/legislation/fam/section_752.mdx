# FAM § 752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Property Rights During Marriage

**Citation:** FAM § 752

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, neither spouse has any interest in the separate property of the other.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 15. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
