# FAM § 6903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 4. MEDICAL TREATMENT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 6903

---

## Text

“Parent or guardian” means either parent if both parents have legal custody, or the parent or person having legal custody, or the guardian, of a minor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
