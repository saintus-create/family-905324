# FAM § 20022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PILOT PROJECTS > Part: 1. FAMILY LAW PILOT PROJECTS > Chapter: 2. San Mateo County Pilot Project

**Citation:** FAM § 20022

---

## Text

The tax return submitted pursuant to Section 20020 may be reviewed by the other party. A party may be examined by the other party as to the contents of the tax return.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)
