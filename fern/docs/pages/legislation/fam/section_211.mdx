# FAM § 211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 2. GENERAL PROCEDURAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 211

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Judicial Council may provide by rule for the practice and procedure in proceedings under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
