# FAM § 356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 2. MARRIAGE LICENSE

**Citation:** FAM § 356

---

## Text

A marriage license issued pursuant to this part expires 90 days after its issuance. The calendar date of expiration shall be clearly noted on the face of the license.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
