# FAM § 6501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 1. AGE OF MAJORITY

**Citation:** FAM § 6501

---

## Text

An adult is an individual who is 18 years of age or older.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
