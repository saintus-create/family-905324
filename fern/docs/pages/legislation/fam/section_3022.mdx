# FAM § 3022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3022

---

## Text

The court may, during the pendency of a proceeding or at any time thereafter, make an order for the custody of a child during minority that seems necessary or proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3021 by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.12. Effective January 1, 1994.)
