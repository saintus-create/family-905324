# FAM § 2212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 2. JUDICIAL DETERMINATION OF VOID OR VOIDABLE MARRIAGE > Chapter: 2. Voidable Marriage

**Citation:** FAM § 2212

---

## Text

(a) The effect of a judgment of nullity of marriage is to restore the parties to the status of unmarried persons.

(b) A judgment of nullity of marriage is conclusive only as to the parties to the proceeding and those claiming under them.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
