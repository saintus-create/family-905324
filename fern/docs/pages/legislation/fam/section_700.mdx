# FAM § 700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 700

---

## Text

For the purposes of this division, a leasehold interest in real property is real property, not personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
