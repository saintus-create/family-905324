# FAM § 8514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 8514

## Text

“Days” means calendar days, unless otherwise specified.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 585, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
