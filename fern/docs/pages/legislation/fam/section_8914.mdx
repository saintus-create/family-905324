# FAM § 8914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 4. Intercountry Adoptions

**Citation:** FAM § 8914

---

## Text

If the licensed adoption agency is a party to or joins in the adoption petition, it shall submit a full report of the facts of the case to the court. The department may also submit a report.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
