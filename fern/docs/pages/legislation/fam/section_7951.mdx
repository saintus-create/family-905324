# FAM § 7951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 6. FOSTER CARE PLACEMENT CONSIDERATIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 7951

---

## Text

This part does not apply in determining the foster care setting in which the child may be placed for a period not intended to exceed 30 days.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 884, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
