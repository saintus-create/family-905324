# FAM § 20011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. PILOT PROJECTS > Part: 1. FAMILY LAW PILOT PROJECTS > Chapter: 2. San Mateo County Pilot Project

**Citation:** FAM § 20011

---

## Text

Motions for temporary orders under this chapter shall be heard as soon as practicable, consistent with the rules governing other civil actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 210. Effective January 1, 1994.)
