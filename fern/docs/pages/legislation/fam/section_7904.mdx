# FAM § 7904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 5. INTERSTATE COMPACT ON PLACEMENT OF CHILDREN

**Citation:** FAM § 7904

---

## Text

The phrase “appropriate authority in receiving state” as used in paragraph (a) of Article 5 of the Interstate Compact on the Placement of Children, with reference to this state, means the State Department of Social Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
