# FAM § 4571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 2. Deposit of Money to Secure Future Child Support Payments > Article: 4. Use of Deposit to Make Delinquent Support Payment

**Citation:** FAM § 4571

---

## Text

The court shall cause a copy of the application, as well as its order to disburse and replenish funds, to be served upon the child support obligor, who shall be subject to contempt of court for failure to comply with the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
