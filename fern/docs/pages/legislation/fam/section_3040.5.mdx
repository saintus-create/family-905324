# FAM § 3040.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 2. Matters To Be Considered in Granting Custody

**Citation:** FAM § 3040.5

---

## Text

When making a determination of the best interests of a child pursuant to Section 3011, the court shall consider any relevant, admissible evidence that a parent has caused human trafficking of the child or other parent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 452, Sec. 1. (AB 1375) Effective January 1, 2026.)
