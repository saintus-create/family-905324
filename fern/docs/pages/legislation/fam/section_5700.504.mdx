# FAM § 5700.504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 6. Uniform Interstate Family Support Act > Chapter: 5. Enforcement of Support Order Without Registration

**Citation:** FAM § 5700.504

---

## Text

An employer that complies with an income-withholding order issued in another state in accordance with this chapter is not subject to civil liability to an individual or agency with regard to the employer’s withholding of child support from the obligor’s income.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 493, Sec. 5. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2016.)
