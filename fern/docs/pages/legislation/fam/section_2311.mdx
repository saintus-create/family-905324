# FAM § 2311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 3. DISSOLUTION OF MARRIAGE AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Chapter: 2. Grounds for Dissolution or Legal Separation

**Citation:** FAM § 2311

---

## Text

Irreconcilable differences are those grounds which are determined by the court to be substantial reasons for not continuing the marriage and which make it appear that the marriage should be dissolved.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
