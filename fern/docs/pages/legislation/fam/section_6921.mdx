# FAM § 6921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 4. MEDICAL TREATMENT > Chapter: 3. Consent by Minor

**Citation:** FAM § 6921

---

## Text

A consent given by a minor under this chapter is not subject to disaffirmance because of minority.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
