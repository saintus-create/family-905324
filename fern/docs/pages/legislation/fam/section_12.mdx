# FAM § 12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 12

---

## Text

“Shall” is mandatory and “may” is permissive. “Shall not” and “may not” are prohibitory.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
