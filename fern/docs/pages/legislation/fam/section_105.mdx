# FAM § 105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 105

---

## Text

“Person” includes a natural person, firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, corporation, limited liability company, or public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 107. Effective January 1, 1995.)
