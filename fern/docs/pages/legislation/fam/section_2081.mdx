# FAM § 2081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. Restoration of Spouse’s Former Name

**Citation:** FAM § 2081

---

## Text

The restoration of a former name or birth name requested under Section 2080 shall not be denied on the basis that the party has custody of a minor child who bears a different name, or for any other reason except fraud.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 780, Sec. 3. (SB 1150) Effective January 1, 2025.)
