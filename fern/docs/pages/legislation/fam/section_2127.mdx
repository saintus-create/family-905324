# FAM § 2127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. NULLITY, DISSOLUTION, AND LEGAL SEPARATION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Relief From Judgment

**Citation:** FAM § 2127

---

## Text

As to actions or motions filed under this chapter, if a timely request is made, the court shall render a statement of decision where the court has resolved controverted factual evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219) by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1101, Sec. 17. Effective October 11, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 18 of Ch. 1101.)
