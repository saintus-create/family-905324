# FAM § 8711.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 2. Agency Adoptions

**Citation:** FAM § 8711.5

---

## Text

The department shall adopt regulations to administer the provisions of Sections 8708 to 8711, inclusive.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 884, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
