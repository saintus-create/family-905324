# FAM § 7636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship > Article: 1. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship

**Citation:** FAM § 7636

---

## Text

The judgment or order of the court determining the existence or nonexistence of the parent and child relationship is determinative for all purposes except for actions brought pursuant to Section 270 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
