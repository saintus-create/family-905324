# FAM § 3806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Deferred Sale of Home Order

**Citation:** FAM § 3806

---

## Text

The court may make an order specifying the parties’ respective responsibilities for the payment of the costs of routine maintenance and capital improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
