# FAM § 6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 6

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the general provisions and rules of construction in this part govern the construction of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
