# FAM § 931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 3. LIABILITY OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 4. Transitional Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 931

---

## Text

The provisions of this part that govern reimbursement apply to all debts, regardless of whether satisfied before, on, or after January 1, 1985.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
