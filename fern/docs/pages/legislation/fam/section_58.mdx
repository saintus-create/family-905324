# FAM § 58

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 58

---

## Text

“Child for whom support may be ordered” means a minor child and a child for whom support is authorized under Section 3587, 3901, or 3910.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 79. Effective January 1, 1994.)
