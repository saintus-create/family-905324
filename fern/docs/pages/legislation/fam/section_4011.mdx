# FAM § 4011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4011

---

## Text

Payment of child support ordered by the court shall be made by the person owing the support payment before payment of any debts owed to creditors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
