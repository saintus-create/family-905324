# FAM § 3602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Spousal and Child Support During Pendency of Proceeding

**Citation:** FAM § 3602

---

## Text

Unless the order specifies otherwise, an order made pursuant to this chapter is not enforceable during any period in which the parties have reconciled and are living together.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
