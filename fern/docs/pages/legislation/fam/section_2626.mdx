# FAM § 2626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DIVISION OF PROPERTY > Part: 6. DEBTS AND LIABILITIES

**Citation:** FAM § 2626

---

## Text

The court has jurisdiction to order reimbursement in cases it deems appropriate for debts paid after separation but before trial.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
