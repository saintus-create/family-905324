# FAM § 8515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 8515

---

## Text

“Delegated county adoption agency” means a county adoption agency that has agreed to provide the services described in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 8800) of Part 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 5. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
