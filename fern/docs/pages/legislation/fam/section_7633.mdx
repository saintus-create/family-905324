# FAM § 7633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 3. UNIFORM PARENTAGE ACT > Chapter: 4. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship > Article: 1. Determination of Parent and Child Relationship

**Citation:** FAM § 7633

---

## Text

An action under this chapter may be brought, an order or judgment may be entered before the birth of the child, and enforcement of that order or judgment shall be stayed until the birth of the child.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 806, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
