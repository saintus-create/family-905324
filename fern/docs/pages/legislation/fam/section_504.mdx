# FAM § 504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 4. CONFIDENTIAL MARRIAGE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 504

---

## Text

A confidential marriage license is valid only for a period of 90 days after its issuance by the county clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 913, Sec. 16. (AB 2747) Effective January 1, 2015.)
