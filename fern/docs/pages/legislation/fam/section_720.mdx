# FAM § 720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Relation of Spouses

**Citation:** FAM § 720

---

## Text

Spouses contract toward each other obligations of mutual respect, fidelity, and support.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 11. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
