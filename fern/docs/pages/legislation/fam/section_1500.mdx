# FAM § 1500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 5. MARITAL AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 1500

---

## Text

The property rights of spouses prescribed by statute may be altered by a premarital agreement or other marital property agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 82, Sec. 19. (SB 1306) Effective January 1, 2015.)
