# FAM § 1835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CONCILIATION PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. FAMILY CONCILIATION COURT LAW > Chapter: 3. Proceedings for Conciliation

**Citation:** FAM § 1835

## Text

No fee shall be charged by any officer for filing the petition.
