# FAM § 6600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 2. RIGHTS AND LIABILITIES; CIVIL ACTIONS AND PROCEEDINGS

**Citation:** FAM § 6600

---

## Text

A minor is civilly liable for a wrong done by the minor, but is not liable in exemplary damages unless at the time of the act the minor was capable of knowing that the act was wrongful.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
