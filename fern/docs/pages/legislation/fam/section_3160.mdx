# FAM § 3160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 11. Mediation of Custody and Visitation Issues > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 3160

---

## Text

Each superior court shall make a mediator available. The court is not required to institute a family conciliation court in order to provide mediation services.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.87. Effective January 1, 1994.)
