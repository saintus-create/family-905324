# FAM § 6601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 2. RIGHTS AND LIABILITIES; CIVIL ACTIONS AND PROCEEDINGS

**Citation:** FAM § 6601

---

## Text

A minor may enforce the minor’s rights by civil action or other legal proceedings in the same manner as an adult, except that a guardian must conduct the action or proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
