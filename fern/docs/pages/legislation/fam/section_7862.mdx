# FAM § 7862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 4. FREEDOM FROM PARENTAL CUSTODY AND CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Procedure > Article: 4. Appointment of Counsel

**Citation:** FAM § 7862

---

## Text

If a parent appears without counsel and is unable to afford counsel, the court shall appoint counsel for the parent, unless that representation is knowingly and intelligently waived.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
