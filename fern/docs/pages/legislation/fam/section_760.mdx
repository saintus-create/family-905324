# FAM § 760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 2. CHARACTERIZATION OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 1. Community Property

**Citation:** FAM § 760

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute, all property, real or personal, wherever situated, acquired by a married person during the marriage while domiciled in this state is community property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
