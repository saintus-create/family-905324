# FAM § 3663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Modification,Termination, or Set Aside of Support Orders > Article: 2. Discovery Before Commencing Modification or Termination Proceeding

**Citation:** FAM § 3663

---

## Text

In the absence of a pending motion for modification or termination of a support order, a request for discovery pursuant to this article may be undertaken not more frequently than once every 12 months.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
