# FAM § 17309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SUPPORT SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Department of Child Support Services > Article: 3. Director of Child Support Services

**Citation:** FAM § 17309

---

## Text

Effective October 1, 1998, the state shall operate a State Disbursement Unit as required by federal law (42 U.S.C. Secs. 654 (27), 654a(g), and 654b).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 387, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2004.)
