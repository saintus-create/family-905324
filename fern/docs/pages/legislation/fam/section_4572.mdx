# FAM § 4572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 2. Deposit of Money to Secure Future Child Support Payments > Article: 4. Use of Deposit to Make Delinquent Support Payment

**Citation:** FAM § 4572

---

## Text

The court shall cause a copy of its order to disburse and replenish funds to be served upon the depository institution where the child support security deposit is maintained, and upon the child support agency with jurisdiction over the case.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 755, Sec. 6. Effective October 12, 2001.)
