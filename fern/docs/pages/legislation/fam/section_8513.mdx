# FAM § 8513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 8513

---

## Text

“County adoption agency” means an adoption agency operated by a county or consortium of counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 4. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
