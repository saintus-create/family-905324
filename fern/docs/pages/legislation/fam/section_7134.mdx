# FAM § 7134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 6. EMANCIPATION OF MINORS LAW > Chapter: 3. Court Declaration of Emancipation > Article: 3. Voiding or Rescinding Declaration

**Citation:** FAM § 7134

---

## Text

If the petition is sustained, the court shall forthwith issue an order voiding or rescinding the declaration of emancipation, which shall be filed by the clerk of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 108. Effective January 1, 2003.)
