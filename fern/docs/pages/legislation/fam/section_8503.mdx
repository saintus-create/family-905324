# FAM § 8503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 8503

---

## Text

“Adoptive parent” means a person who has obtained an order of adoption of a minor child or, in the case of an adult adoption, an adult.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
