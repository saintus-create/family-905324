# FAM § 7559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 2. PRESUMPTION CONCERNING CHILD OF MARRIAGE AND GENETIC TESTING TO DETERMINE PARENTAGE > Chapter: 2. Genetic Testing to Determine Parentage

**Citation:** FAM § 7559

---

## Text

Payment of the cost of initial genetic testing shall be made in advance when agreed to by the parties or ordered by the court pursuant to Section 7640.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 22. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)
