# FAM § 6270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 3. EMERGENCY PROTECTIVE ORDERS > Chapter: 3. Duties of Law Enforcement Officer

**Citation:** FAM § 6270

---

## Text

A law enforcement officer who requests an emergency protective order shall reduce the order to writing and sign it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)
