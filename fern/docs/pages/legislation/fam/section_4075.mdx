# FAM § 4075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 2. Statewide Uniform Guideline

**Citation:** FAM § 4075

---

## Text

This article shall not be construed to affect the treatment of spousal support and separate maintenance payments pursuant to Section 71 of the Internal Revenue Code of 1954 (26 U.S.C. Sec. 71).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.)
