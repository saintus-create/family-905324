# FAM § 4502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4502

---

## Text

The period for enforcement and procedure for renewal of a judgment or order for child, family, or spousal support is governed by Section 291.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 86, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2007.)
