# FAM § 7900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 5. INTERSTATE COMPACT ON PLACEMENT OF CHILDREN

**Citation:** FAM § 7900

---

## Text

The Interstate Compact on Placement of Children as set forth in Section 7901 is hereby adopted and entered into with all other jurisdictions joining therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
