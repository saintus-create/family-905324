# FAM § 3003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FAM § 3003

---

## Text

“Joint legal custody” means that both parents shall share the right and the responsibility to make the decisions relating to the health, education, and welfare of a child.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
