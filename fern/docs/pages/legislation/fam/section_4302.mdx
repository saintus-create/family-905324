# FAM § 4302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 3. SPOUSAL SUPPORT > Chapter: 1. Duty to Support Spouse

**Citation:** FAM § 4302

---

## Text

A person is not liable for support of the person’s spouse when the person is living separate from the spouse by agreement unless support is stipulated in the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
