# FAM § 4400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 4. SUPPORT OF PARENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4400

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, an adult child shall, to the extent of the adult child’s ability, support a parent who is in need and unable to self-maintain by work.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 115, Sec. 54. (AB 1817) Effective January 1, 2020.)
