# FAM § 8512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 8512

---

## Text

“Birth parent” means the biological parent or, in the case of a person previously adopted, the adoptive parent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
