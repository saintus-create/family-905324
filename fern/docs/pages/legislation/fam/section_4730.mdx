# FAM § 4730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 5. Civil Penalty for Child Support Delinquency

**Citation:** FAM § 4730

---

## Text

At any hearing to set or modify the amount payable for the support of a child, the court shall not consider any penalties imposed under this chapter in determining the amount of current support to be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 149. Effective January 1, 1994.)
