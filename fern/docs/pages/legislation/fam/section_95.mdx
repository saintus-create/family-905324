# FAM § 95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 95

---

## Text

“Income and expense declaration” means the form for an income and expense declaration in family law matters adopted by the Judicial Council.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
