# FAM § 351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 2. MARRIAGE LICENSE

**Citation:** FAM § 351

---

## Text

The marriage license shall show all of the following:

(a) The identity of the parties to the marriage.

(b) The parties’ full given names at birth or by court order, and mailing addresses.

(c) The parties’ dates of birth.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 816, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative January 1, 2008, by Sec. 56 of Ch. 816.)
