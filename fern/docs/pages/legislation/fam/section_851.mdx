# FAM § 851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 2. CHARACTERIZATION OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 5. Transmutation of Property

**Citation:** FAM § 851

---

## Text

A transmutation is subject to the laws governing fraudulent transfers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
