# FAM § 6655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 2.5. ONLINE PLATFORMS

**Citation:** FAM § 6655

---

## Text

(a) This part does not affect a right or remedy available under any other law of the state.

(b) This part does not have any effect on a minor who produces their own content.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 611, Sec. 1. (SB 764) Effective January 1, 2025.)
