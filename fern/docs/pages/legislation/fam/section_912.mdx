# FAM § 912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. RIGHTS AND OBLIGATIONS DURING MARRIAGE > Part: 3. LIABILITY OF MARITAL PROPERTY > Chapter: 2. General Rules of Liability

**Citation:** FAM § 912

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, quasi-community property is liable to the same extent, and shall be treated the same in all other respects, as community property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
