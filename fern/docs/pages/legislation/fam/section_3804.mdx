# FAM § 3804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8. Deferred Sale of Home Order

**Citation:** FAM § 3804

---

## Text

A deferred sale of home order may be recorded in the office of the county recorder of the county in which the real property is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
