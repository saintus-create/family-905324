# FAM § 1811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. CONCILIATION PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. FAMILY CONCILIATION COURT LAW > Chapter: 2. Family Conciliation Courts

**Citation:** FAM § 1811

---

## Text

The presiding judge of the superior court shall annually, in the month of January, designate at least one judge to hear all cases under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2004.)
