# FAM § 6209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 1. SHORT TITLE AND DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 6209

---

## Text

“Cohabitant” means a person who regularly resides in the household. “Former cohabitant” means a person who formerly regularly resided in the household.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)
