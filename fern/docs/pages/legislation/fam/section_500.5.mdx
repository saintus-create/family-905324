# FAM § 500.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. MARRIAGE > Part: 4. CONFIDENTIAL MARRIAGE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 500.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the document issued by the county clerk is a marriage license until it is registered with the county clerk, at which time the license becomes a marriage certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 816, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative January 1, 2008, by Sec. 56 of Ch. 816.)
