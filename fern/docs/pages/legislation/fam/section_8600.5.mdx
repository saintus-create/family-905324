# FAM § 8600.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. ADOPTION > Part: 2. ADOPTION OF UNMARRIED MINORS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 8600.5

---

## Text

Tribal customary adoption as defined in Section 366.24 of the Welfare and Institutions Code and as applied to Indian Children who are dependents of the court, does not apply to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 35, Sec. 9. (SB 1013) Effective June 27, 2012.)
