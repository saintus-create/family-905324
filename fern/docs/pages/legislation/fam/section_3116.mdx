# FAM § 3116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CUSTODY OF CHILDREN > Part: 2. RIGHT TO CUSTODY OF MINOR CHILD > Chapter: 6. Custody Investigation and Report

**Citation:** FAM § 3116

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the duty of a court-appointed investigator to assist the appointing court in the transaction of the business of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 116.81. Effective January 1, 1994.)
