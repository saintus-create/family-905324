# FAM § 6227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PREVENTION OF DOMESTIC VIOLENCE > Part: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** FAM § 6227

---

## Text

The remedies provided in this division are in addition to any other civil or criminal remedies that may be available to the petitioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 154. Effective January 1, 1994.)
