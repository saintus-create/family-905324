# FAM § 7562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PARENT AND CHILD RELATIONSHIP > Part: 2. PRESUMPTION CONCERNING CHILD OF MARRIAGE AND GENETIC TESTING TO DETERMINE PARENTAGE > Chapter: 2. Genetic Testing to Determine Parentage

**Citation:** FAM § 7562

---

## Text

If a person seeking genetic testing demonstrates good cause, the court may order genetic testing of a deceased person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 876, Sec. 24. (AB 2684) Effective January 1, 2019.)
