# FAM § 4632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 5. ENFORCEMENT OF SUPPORT ORDERS > Chapter: 3. Deposit of Assets to Secure Future Child Support Payments > Article: 4. Use or Sale of Assets to Make Support Payments

**Citation:** FAM § 4632

---

## Text

An obligor-parent alleged to be in arrears under this article may use any ground set forth in Section 4612 as a basis for filing a motion under Section 4631 to stop a sale or use of assets under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
