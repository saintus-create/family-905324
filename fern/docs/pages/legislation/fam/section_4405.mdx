# FAM § 4405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 4. SUPPORT OF PARENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FAM § 4405

---

## Text

The court retains jurisdiction to modify or terminate an order for support where justice requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
