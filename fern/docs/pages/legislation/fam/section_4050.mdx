# FAM § 4050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SUPPORT > Part: 2. CHILD SUPPORT > Chapter: 2. Court-Ordered Child Support > Article: 2. Statewide Uniform Guideline

**Citation:** FAM § 4050

---

## Text

In adopting the statewide uniform guideline provided in this article, it is the intention of the Legislature to ensure that this state remains in compliance with federal regulations for child support guidelines.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 219, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1994.)
