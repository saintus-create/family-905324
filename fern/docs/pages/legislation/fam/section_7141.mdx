# FAM § 7141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. MINORS > Part: 6. EMANCIPATION OF MINORS LAW > Chapter: 3. Court Declaration of Emancipation > Article: 4. Identification Cards and Information

**Citation:** FAM § 7141

---

## Text

A person who, in good faith, has examined a minor’s identification card and relies on a minor’s representation that the minor is emancipated, has the same rights and obligations as if the minor were in fact emancipated at the time of the representation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1992, Ch. 162, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1994.)
