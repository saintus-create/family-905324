# FGC § 8686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 5. Gill and Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8686

---

## Text

In Districts 112, 212, and in the Trinity and Klamath River District, it is unlawful to possess any gill or trammel net with meshes over 134 inches in length, except under regulations which may be prescribed by the commission.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
