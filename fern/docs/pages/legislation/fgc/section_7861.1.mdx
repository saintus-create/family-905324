# FGC § 7861.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 3.5. Commercial Salmon Trollers Enhancement and Restoration Program

**Citation:** FGC § 7861.1

---

## Text

Not more than 15 percent of the funds expended pursuant to Section 7861 may be used to pay the costs incurred in the administration of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 368, Sec. 4. (SB 197) Effective January 1, 2014. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 7863.)
