# FGC § 8340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 8. Clams and Other Mollusks

**Citation:** FGC § 8340

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, in Districts 8, 9, and 17, clams may be taken between September 1st and April 30th, and in other districts clams may be taken at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
