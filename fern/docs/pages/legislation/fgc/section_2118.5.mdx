# FGC § 2118.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Importation, Transportation, and Sheltering of Restricted Live Wild Animals > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 2118.5

---

## Text

The commission may designate wild animals which may be possessed without a permit.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1510.)
