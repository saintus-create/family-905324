# FGC § 13231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. EXPENDITURES

**Citation:** FGC § 13231

---

## Text

Consistent with Section 13203, the department shall maintain a cost accounting system that accounts for the costs of each activity or program engaged in pursuant to Section 13230 using funds from the subaccounts listed in that section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 251, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
