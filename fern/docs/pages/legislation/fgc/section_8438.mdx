# FGC § 8438

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 10. Fresh-water Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8438

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 200, the commission shall regulate the taking of freshwater clams for commercial purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8475 by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 160. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
