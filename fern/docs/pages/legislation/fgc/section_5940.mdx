# FGC § 5940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Dams, Conduits, and Screens > Article: 2. Dams and Obstructions

**Citation:** FGC § 5940

---

## Text

If the owner of the dam generates electricity at the place of the dam, he shall furnish sufficient light, without charge, for the use of the hatchery.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
