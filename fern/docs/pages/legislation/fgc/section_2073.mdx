# FGC § 2073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 2. Listing of Endangered Species

**Citation:** FGC § 2073

---

## Text

Within 10 days of the receipt of a petition from an interested person under Section 2072.3, the commission shall refer the petition to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1162, Sec. 6.)
