# FGC § 4371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. Deer > Article: 3. Archery Deer Hunting

**Citation:** FGC § 4371

---

## Text

The provisions of this code relating to deer shall be applicable to the taking of deer with bow and arrow.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
