# FGC § 98.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Marine Life Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 98.5

---

## Text

“Population” or “stock” means a species, subspecies, geographical grouping, or other category of fish capable of management as a unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1052, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
