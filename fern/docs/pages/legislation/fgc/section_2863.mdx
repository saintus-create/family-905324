# FGC § 2863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 10.5. Marine Life Protection Act

**Citation:** FGC § 2863

---

## Text

The department shall confer as necessary with the United States Navy regarding issues related to its activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 1015, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
