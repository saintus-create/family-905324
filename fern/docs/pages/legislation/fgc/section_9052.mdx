# FGC § 9052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 4. Other Means of Taking > Article: 3. Other Appliances

**Citation:** FGC § 9052

---

## Text

Slurp guns may be used to take fish for commercial purposes in fish and game districts 6, 7, 10, 17, 18, 19, 20, and 20A.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 742, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1993.)
