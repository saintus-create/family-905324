# FGC § 8843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 10. Trawl Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8843

---

## Text

It is unlawful to use any trawl net with cod-end mesh less than 712 inches in length and with a cod end less than 29 meshes long and a circumference of not less than 47 meshes in waters lying between one and three nautical miles from the mainland shore between a line running due west (270° true) from Point Arguello and a line running due south (180° true) from Point Mugu.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1370, Sec. 36. Effective October 27, 1992.)
