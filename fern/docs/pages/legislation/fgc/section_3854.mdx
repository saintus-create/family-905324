# FGC § 3854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. California Condor

**Citation:** FGC § 3854

---

## Text

Not more than 10 percent of the funds provided to the zoos under this chapter may be used for administrative costs of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 88, Sec. 1. Effective April 25, 1988.)
