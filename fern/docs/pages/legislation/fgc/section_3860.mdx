# FGC § 3860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 5. Avian Influenza Wildlife Surveillance Act

**Citation:** FGC § 3860

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Avian Influenza Wildlife Surveillance Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 708, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 2006.)
