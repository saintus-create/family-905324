# FGC § 5521.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FGC § 5521.6

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 5521 and 5521.5, a registered aquaculturist may collect abalone for broodstock, in accordance with subdivision (b) of Section 15301.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 483, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2000.)
