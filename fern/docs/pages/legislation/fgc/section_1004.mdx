# FGC § 1004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 1004

---

## Text

The department may capture and sell birds and mammals, at prices to be fixed by the commission, to persons engaged in the domestication and sale thereof in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
