# FGC § 3515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 3515

---

## Text

Exotic nonresident game birds may be released in this State only on prior approval of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 398.)
