# FGC § 16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 16

---

## Text

“Affix” means physically attached to or imprinting an electronic validation to a license document.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 112, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
