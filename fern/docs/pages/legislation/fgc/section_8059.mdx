# FGC § 8059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 7.5. Landing Fees

**Citation:** FGC § 8059

---

## Text

Every claim for refund or credit for overpayment of a landing fee shall be in writing and shall state the specific grounds upon which the claim is founded.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 23. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
