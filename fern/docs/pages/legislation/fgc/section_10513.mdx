# FGC § 10513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 1. Refuges and Other Protected Areas > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 10513

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as prohibiting or preventing a person from taking a bird, mammal, fish, amphibian, or reptile from or on navigable water in a state game refuge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 100. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
