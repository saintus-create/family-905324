# FGC § 1670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 6.7. Restoration Management Permit Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1670

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known as, and may be cited as, the Restoration Management Permit Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 681, Sec. 2. (AB 1581) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, pursuant to Section 1678.)
