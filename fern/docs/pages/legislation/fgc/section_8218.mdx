# FGC § 8218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 4. Salmon

**Citation:** FGC § 8218

---

## Text

It is unlawful to gaff, club, otherwise injure, or possess any king or silver salmon under the legal size.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 189.)
