# FGC § 1419

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4.3. Inland Wetlands Conservation Program > Article: 2. The Inland Wetlands Conservation Program

**Citation:** FGC § 1419

---

## Text

Any funds remaining after an eligible acquisition, restoration, or enhancement of any project under this article shall be returned to the board and shall be deposited in the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1645, Sec. 2.)
