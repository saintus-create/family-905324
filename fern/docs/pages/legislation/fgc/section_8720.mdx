# FGC § 8720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 6. Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8720

---

## Text

“Trammel net” includes entangling nets constructed of more than one wall of webbing.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
