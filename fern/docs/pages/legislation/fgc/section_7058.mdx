# FGC § 7058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Marine Fisheries Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 7058

---

## Text

Any fishery management regulation adopted by the commission shall, to the extent practicable, conform to the policies of Sections 7055 and 7056.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 559, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
