# FGC § 2349

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Importation and Transportation of Dead Birds, Mammals, Fish, Reptiles, and Amphibia > Article: 1. Dead Wild Birds, Mammals, Fish, Reptiles, and Amphibia

**Citation:** FGC § 2349

---

## Text

No bird, mammal, fish, reptile, or amphibian, except smoked, cured, or dried fish other than trout, may be shipped by parcel post.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 44. Effective January 1, 2008.)
