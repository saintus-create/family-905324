# FGC § 8576.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 16. Drift Gill Net Shark and Swordfish Fishery

**Citation:** FGC § 8576.5

---

## Text

Thresher shark taken with drift gill nets shall not have the pelvic fin severed from the carcass until after the shark is brought ashore.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 589, Sec. 2.)
