# FGC § 3702.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties > Article: 3. Migratory Birds

**Citation:** FGC § 3702.5

---

## Text

The department may permit individual artists to sell a limited number of prints of duck stamp related artwork or posters.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 74. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
