# FGC § 1351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4. Wildlife Conservation Law of 1947 > Article: 3. Powers and Purposes

**Citation:** FGC § 1351

---

## Text

Each recreational area shall be so managed and controlled that the public shall have access to and use of the area for all recreational purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
