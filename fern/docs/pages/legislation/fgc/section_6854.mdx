# FGC § 6854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 7. Amphibia > Article: 1. Frogs

**Citation:** FGC § 6854

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take frogs by the use of firearms of any caliber or type.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 697.)
