# FGC § 2089.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 3.7. California State Safe Harbor Agreement Program Act

**Citation:** FGC § 2089.7

---

## Text

The department may authorize the taking of the Owens pupfish in the Owens River watershed if the take is authorized under an agreement pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 515, Sec. 1. (AB 2001) Effective January 1, 2017.)
