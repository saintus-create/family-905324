# FGC § 4180.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 3. Nongame Mammals and Depredators > Article: 2. Depredators

**Citation:** FGC § 4180.2

---

## Text

As used in this article, “wild pigs” has the meaning set forth in Section 4650.

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 85. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
