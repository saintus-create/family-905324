# FGC § 80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 80

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, a provision of this code that applies to a whole animal also applies to a part of the animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 8. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
