# FGC § 2932.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Salton Sea Restoration Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 2932.5

---

## Text

Moneys deposited in the fund created pursuant to Section 2932 shall not be expended for mitigation except for mitigation undertaken by the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 614, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2005.)
