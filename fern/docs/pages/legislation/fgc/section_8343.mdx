# FGC § 8343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 8. Clams and Other Mollusks

**Citation:** FGC § 8343

---

## Text

No northern razor clams (Siliqua patula) may be sold; but if taken outside the State and brought within the State, they may be possessed, transported, and sold without restriction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
