# FGC § 10509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 1. Refuges and Other Protected Areas > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 10509

---

## Text

Any refuge designated as a “fish and game refuge” shall be considered, for the purposes of this division, as both a game refuge and a fish refuge.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
