# FGC § 1206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 6. Cooperative Salmon and Steelhead Rearing Facilities

**Citation:** FGC § 1206

---

## Text

Salmon and steelhead raised pursuant to this article shall be released in streams, rivers, or waters north of Point Conception and upon release shall have unimpeded access to the sea.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1173.)
