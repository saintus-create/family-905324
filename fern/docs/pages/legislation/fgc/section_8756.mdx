# FGC § 8756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 7. Round Haul Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8756

---

## Text

Salmon, steelhead, striped bass, or shad may not be taken with round haul nets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 171. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
