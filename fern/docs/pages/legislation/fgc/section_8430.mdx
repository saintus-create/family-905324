# FGC § 8430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 10. Fresh-water Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8430

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, it is unlawful to sell or purchase any species of trout.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
