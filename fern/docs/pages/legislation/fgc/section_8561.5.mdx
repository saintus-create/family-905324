# FGC § 8561.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 16. Drift Gill Net Shark and Swordfish Fishery

**Citation:** FGC § 8561.5

---

## Text

(a) Notwithstanding Section 8102 or any other law, a permit issued pursuant to Section 8561 may not be transferred.

(b) This section shall become operative on March 31, 2019.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 844, Sec. 5. (SB 1017) Effective January 1, 2019. Section operative March 31, 2019, by its own provisions.)
