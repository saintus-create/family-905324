# FGC § 2072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 2. Listing of Endangered Species

**Citation:** FGC § 2072

## Text

The petition shall be written, shall be clearly identified as a petition, and shall clearly indicate the administrative measure recommended.

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1162, Sec. 6.)
