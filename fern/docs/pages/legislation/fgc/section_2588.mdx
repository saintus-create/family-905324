# FGC § 2588

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 6.5. Control of Illegally Taken Fish and Wildlife

**Citation:** FGC § 2588

---

## Text

All administrative penalties and revenues from forfeitures collected pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Fish and Game Preservation Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 59. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
