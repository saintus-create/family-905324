# FGC § 5945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Dams, Conduits, and Screens > Article: 2. Dams and Obstructions

**Citation:** FGC § 5945

---

## Text

The department may sell, at cost, to the owner of a dam, young fish ordered to be planted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
