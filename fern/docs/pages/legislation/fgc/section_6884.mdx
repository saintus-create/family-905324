# FGC § 6884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 7. Amphibia > Article: 2. Frog-Jumping Contests

**Citation:** FGC § 6884

---

## Text

A frog which is not kept in a manner which is reasonable to preserve its life is not within the coverage of this article.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
