# FGC § 2071.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 2. Listing of Endangered Species

**Citation:** FGC § 2071.5

---

## Text

The department shall recommend, and the commission shall adopt, criteria for determining if a species is endangered or threatened.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1162, Sec. 6.)
