# FGC § 6912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 8. Salmon, Steelhead Trout, and Anadromous Fisheries Program Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 6912

---

## Text

“Program” means the program for protecting and increasing the naturally spawning salmon and steelhead trout of the state provided for in Article 3 (commencing with Section 6920).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1545, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1988.)
