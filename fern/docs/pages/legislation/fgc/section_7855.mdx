# FGC § 7855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 3. Commercial Fishing Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 7855

---

## Text

The proceedings to revoke or suspend a commercial fishing license shall be conducted at one of the commission’s regularly scheduled meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1244, Sec. 8.)
