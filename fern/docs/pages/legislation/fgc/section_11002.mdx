# FGC § 11002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Boundaries

**Citation:** FGC § 11002

---

## Text

The following constitutes Fish and Game District 138:

Those portions of the following counties not included in other districts: Alpine, El Dorado, Amador, Calaveras, Tuolumne and Mariposa.

Except as otherwise provided, all of the provisions of this code relating to District 1 shall apply to District 138.
