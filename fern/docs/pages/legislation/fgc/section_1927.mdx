# FGC § 1927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 11.5. Western Joshua Tree Conservation Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1927

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited as, the Western Joshua Tree Conservation Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 51, Sec. 5. (SB 122) Effective July 10, 2023. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 1927.2.)
