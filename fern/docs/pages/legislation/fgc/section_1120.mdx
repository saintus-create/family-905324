# FGC § 1120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 3. State Fish Hatcheries

**Citation:** FGC § 1120

---

## Text

The commission shall establish fish hatcheries for stocking the waters of this State with fish. The department shall maintain and operate such hatcheries.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
