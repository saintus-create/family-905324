# FGC § 8246.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 4.5. Commercial Salmon Vessel Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 8246.4

---

## Text

The commission, after notice and opportunity for hearing, shall revoke a commercial salmon vessel permit issued pursuant to this article if the vessel permit was obtained by fraudulent means.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 472, Sec. 17. (SB 262) Effective January 1, 2020.)
