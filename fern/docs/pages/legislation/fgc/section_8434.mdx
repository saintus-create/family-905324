# FGC § 8434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 10. Fresh-water Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8434

---

## Text

It is unlawful to sell or purchase any fresh, canned, or cured fish taken in the Klamath River District or in the waters of the Smith River.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
