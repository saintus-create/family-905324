# FGC § 2200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Importation, Transportation, and Sheltering of Restricted Live Wild Animals > Article: 4. Mammals Used for Hire

**Citation:** FGC § 2200

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “mammal” means any wild animal of the class Mammalia as specified in Article 1 (commencing with Section 2116) or regulations adopted pursuant thereto which affects commerce.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1231, Sec. 2.)
