# FGC § 6598

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 8. Ocean Fishery Research

**Citation:** FGC § 6598

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 345, Sec. 13. (AB 1949) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Art. 8, commencing with Sec. 6590.)
