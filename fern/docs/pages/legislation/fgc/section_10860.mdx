# FGC § 10860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 2. Specific Refuge Boundaries > Article: 4. Waterfowl Refuges

**Citation:** FGC § 10860

---

## Text

The area described in this article is a waterfowl refuge.
