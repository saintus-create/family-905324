# FGC § 2089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 3.5. Incidental Take Associated with Routine and Ongoing Activities

**Citation:** FGC § 2089

---

## Text

Routine and ongoing agricultural activities shall be defined by the department by regulation and shall not include the conversion of agricultural land to a nonagricultural use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 528, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
