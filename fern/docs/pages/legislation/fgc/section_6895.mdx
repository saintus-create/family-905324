# FGC § 6895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 7. Amphibia > Article: 3. Commercial Use of Amphibians Except Frogs

**Citation:** FGC § 6895

---

## Text

“Native amphibians” as used in this article means salamanders, toads, or any other member of the class amphibia native to California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 605.)
