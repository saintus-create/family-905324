# FGC § 8238.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 4.5. Commercial Salmon Vessel Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 8238.1

## Text

The vessel classification system shall be used by the department for issuance of new original vessel permits pursuant to Section 8243.
