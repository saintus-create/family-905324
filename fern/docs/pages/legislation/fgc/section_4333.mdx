# FGC § 4333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. Deer > Article: 2. License Tags

**Citation:** FGC § 4333

---

## Text

Tags are valid only during that portion of the current hunting license year in which deer may be taken or possessed in any area.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 86. Effective January 1, 2008.)
