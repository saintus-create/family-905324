# FGC § 1770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.5. Native Species Conservation and Enhancement > Article: 4. Endangered and Rare Fish, Wildlife, and Plant Species Conservation and Enhancement Account

**Citation:** FGC § 1770

---

## Text

The department shall maintain within the Fish and Game Preservation Fund an Endangered and Rare Fish, Wildlife, and Plant Species Conservation and Enhancement Account which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1058, Sec. 1.)
