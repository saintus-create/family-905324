# FGC § 2360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Importation and Transportation of Dead Birds, Mammals, Fish, Reptiles, and Amphibia > Article: 1. Dead Wild Birds, Mammals, Fish, Reptiles, and Amphibia

**Citation:** FGC § 2360

---

## Text

Black bass and spotted bass lawfully taken may be carried or transported into and possessed in an area where the season is closed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
