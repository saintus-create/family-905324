# FGC § 6750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 6. Kelp and Other Aquatic Plants > Article: 4. Noncommercial Use of Kelp

**Citation:** FGC § 6750

---

## Text

The commission may regulate the taking, collecting, harvesting, gathering, or possession of kelp for purposes other than profit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 468.)
