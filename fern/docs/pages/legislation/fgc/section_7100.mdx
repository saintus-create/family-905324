# FGC § 7100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 1. Application of Part

**Citation:** FGC § 7100

---

## Text

The provisions of this part apply to the taking and possession of fish for any purpose other than commercial.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
