# FGC § 265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Regulation of Take and Possession Generally > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** FGC § 265

---

## Text

A regulation governed by this article is not subject to the time periods for the adoption, amendment, or repeal of a regulation prescribed in Sections 11343.4, 11346.4, 11346.8, and 11347.1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 546, Sec. 18. (SB 1473) Effective January 1, 2017.)
