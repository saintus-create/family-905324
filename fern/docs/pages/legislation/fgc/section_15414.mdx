# FGC § 15414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. AQUACULTURE > Chapter: 5. Leasing of State Water Bottoms

**Citation:** FGC § 15414

---

## Text

A water bottom lease may require periodic reports that the commission deems necessary for the proper administration of the state’s water bottoms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 25.)
