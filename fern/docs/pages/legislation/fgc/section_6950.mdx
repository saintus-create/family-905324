# FGC § 6950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 9. California Monitoring Program

**Citation:** FGC § 6950

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Local agencies” means local agencies that have a role in the conservation, restoration, and management of salmonid species.

(b) “Program” means the California Monitoring Program established pursuant to this chapter.

(c) “Relevant agencies” means state and federal agencies that have a role in the conservation, restoration, and management of salmonid species.

(d) “Tribe” has the same meaning as defined in Section 1452.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 455, Sec. 2. (AB 809) Effective January 1, 2024.)
