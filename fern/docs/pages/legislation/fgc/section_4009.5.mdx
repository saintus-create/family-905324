# FGC § 4009.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Fur-Bearing Mammals > Article: 1. Trapping Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 4009.5

---

## Text

The commission may adopt regulations as it determines to be necessary to regulate the taking of fur-bearing mammals or nongame mammals taken under a trapping license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 216, Sec. 10. (AB 273) Effective January 1, 2020.)
