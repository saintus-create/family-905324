# FGC § 270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Regulation of Take and Possession Generally > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** FGC § 270

---

## Text

The adoption, amendment, or repeal of a regulation governed by this article shall become effective at the time specified in the regulation, but not sooner than the date of the filing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 546, Sec. 18. (SB 1473) Effective January 1, 2017.)
