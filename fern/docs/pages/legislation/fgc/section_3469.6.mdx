# FGC § 3469.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 7.5. The California Winter Rice Habitat Incentive Program

**Citation:** FGC § 3469.6

---

## Text

The director and the owner or lessee may mutually agree to modify the terms and conditions of a contract under this article as the director may determine to be desirable to carry out the purposes of, or to facilitate administration of, the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 649, Sec. 2. (AB 2348) Effective January 1, 2019.)
