# FGC § 8375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8375

---

## Text

No bluefin tuna weighing less than 712 pounds may be sold, purchased, or processed.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 850.)
