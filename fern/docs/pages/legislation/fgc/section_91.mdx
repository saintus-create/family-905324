# FGC § 91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Marine Life Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 91

---

## Text

“Discards” means fish that are taken in a fishery but are not retained because they are of an undesirable species, size, sex, or quality, or because they are required by law not to be retained.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1052, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
