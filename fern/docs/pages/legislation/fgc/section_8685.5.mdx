# FGC § 8685.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 5. Gill and Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8685.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, gill nets may not be used to take salmon, steelhead, or striped bass.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 393, Sec. 1.5. Effective July 11, 1980.)
