# FGC § 1372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4. Wildlife Conservation Law of 1947 > Article: 3.5. Oak Woodlands Conservation Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1372

---

## Text

Nothing in this article grants any new authority to the board or any other agency, office, or department to affect local policy or land use decisionmaking.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 588, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
