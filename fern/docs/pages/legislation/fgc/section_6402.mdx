# FGC § 6402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 6402

---

## Text

The department may plant fish in streams passing through, or in lakes on, land on which is located a youth camp for underprivileged children.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 424.)
