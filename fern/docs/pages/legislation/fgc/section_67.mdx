# FGC § 67

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 67

---

## Text

“Person” means any natural person or any partnership, corporation, limited liability company, trust, or other type of association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 121. Effective January 1, 1995.)
