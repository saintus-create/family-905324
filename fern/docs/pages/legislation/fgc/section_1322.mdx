# FGC § 1322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4. Wildlife Conservation Law of 1947 > Article: 2. The Wildlife Conservation Board

**Citation:** FGC § 1322

---

## Text

The members of the board shall receive their actual and necessary traveling expenses, which shall be paid from the Wildlife Restoration Fund.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
