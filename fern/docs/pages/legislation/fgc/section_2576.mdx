# FGC § 2576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Capture, Transport or Sale of Wild Rodents

**Citation:** FGC § 2576

---

## Text

It is unlawful to knowingly capture for sale, transport for sale or sell wild rodents, except as provided in Article 1 (commencing with Section 1000) of Chapter 3 of Division 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 52. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
