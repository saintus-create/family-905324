# FGC § 4752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 9. Bear

**Citation:** FGC § 4752

---

## Text

Bear tags are valid only during that portion of the current hunting license year in which bear may be taken or possessed in any district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 97. Effective January 1, 2008.)
