# FGC § 5947

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Dams, Conduits, and Screens > Article: 2. Dams and Obstructions

**Citation:** FGC § 5947

---

## Text

It is unlawful for the owner of a dam in District 412 to release water from the dam, or any facilities for the generation of hydroelectric energy operated in connection therewith, in varying flows in such a manner as to destroy fish life below such release.
