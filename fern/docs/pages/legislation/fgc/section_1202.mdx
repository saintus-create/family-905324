# FGC § 1202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 6. Cooperative Salmon and Steelhead Rearing Facilities

**Citation:** FGC § 1202

---

## Text

All fish handled or released under authority of this article are the property of the state and may be taken only after their release into the wild and under the authority of a sport or commercial fishing license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1173.)
