# FGC § 4331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. Deer > Article: 2. License Tags

**Citation:** FGC § 4331

---

## Text

The commission may determine the design and makeup of the deer tag and prescribe the procedures for issuance and use.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 84. Effective January 1, 2008.)
