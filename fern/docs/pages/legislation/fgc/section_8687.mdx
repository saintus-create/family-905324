# FGC § 8687

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 5. Gill and Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8687

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this code, drift gill nets may be used in Districts 6, 7, 8, 9, and 10.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 203, Sec. 8. Effective June 20, 1984.)
