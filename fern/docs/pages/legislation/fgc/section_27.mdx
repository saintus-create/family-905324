# FGC § 27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 27

---

## Text

“Chumming” means the placing in the water of fish, or other material upon which fish feed, for the purpose of attracting fish to a particular area in order that they may be taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 3. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
