# FGC § 2150.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Importation, Transportation, and Sheltering of Restricted Live Wild Animals > Article: 2. Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 2150.2

---

## Text

The department shall establish fees for permits, permit applications, and facility inspections in amounts sufficient to cover the costs of administering, implementing, and enforcing this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 789, Sec. 6.)
