# FGC § 8591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 18. Prawns and Shrimp

**Citation:** FGC § 8591

---

## Text

Prawns or shrimp may be taken for commercial purposes under the regulations of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1271, Sec. 18.)
