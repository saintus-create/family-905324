# FGC § 4002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Fur-Bearing Mammals > Article: 1. Trapping Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 4002

---

## Text

Fur-bearing mammals may be taken only with a trap, a firearm, bow and arrow, poison under a proper permit, or with the use of dogs.
