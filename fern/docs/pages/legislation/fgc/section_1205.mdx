# FGC § 1205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 6. Cooperative Salmon and Steelhead Rearing Facilities

**Citation:** FGC § 1205

---

## Text

The department shall, subject to the limitations of appropriate egg sources and funding, make available fish of appropriate size and species to persons or entities who enter into agreements pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1173.)
