# FGC § 5060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PROTECTED REPTILES AND AMPHIBIANS > Chapter: 3. Commercial Use of Reptiles

**Citation:** FGC § 5060

---

## Text

“Native reptiles” as used in this chapter means snakes, lizards, turtles, or any other members of the class reptilia native to California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 605.)
