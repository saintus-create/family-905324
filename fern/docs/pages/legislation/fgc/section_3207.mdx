# FGC § 3207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 1. Domesticated Game Breeding

**Citation:** FGC § 3207

## Text

The department shall collect three cents ($0.03) for each tag or seal.
