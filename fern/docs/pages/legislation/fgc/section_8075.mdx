# FGC § 8075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 8. Reduction Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 8075

---

## Text

The commission may grant a permit, subject to such regulations as it may prescribe, to take and use fish by a reduction or extraction process.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
