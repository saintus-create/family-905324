# FGC § 10510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 1. Refuges and Other Protected Areas > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 10510

---

## Text

No specification of an open season in any area authorizes the taking of a bird, mammal, fish, amphibian, or reptile from a refuge within that area from which the taking is prohibited by this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 99. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
