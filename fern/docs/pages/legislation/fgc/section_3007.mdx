# FGC § 3007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Methods of Taking

**Citation:** FGC § 3007

---

## Text

Except as provided in this code or regulations adopted pursuant to this code, it is unlawful to take a bird or mammal without a license or entitlement to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 62. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
