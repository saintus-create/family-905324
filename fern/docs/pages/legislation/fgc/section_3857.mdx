# FGC § 3857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. California Condor

**Citation:** FGC § 3857

---

## Text

To the extent possible, the department shall seek private sector funding and any federal funds which may be available to augment state funds for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 88, Sec. 1. Effective April 25, 1988.)
