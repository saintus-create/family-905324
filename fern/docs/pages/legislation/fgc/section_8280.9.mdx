# FGC § 8280.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 6. Crab

**Citation:** FGC § 8280.9

---

## Text

Dungeness crab vessel permits are valid only in state waters and in the Pacific Ocean in federal waters south of the border with Oregon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 297, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
