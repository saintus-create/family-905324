# FGC § 15401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. AQUACULTURE > Chapter: 5. Leasing of State Water Bottoms

**Citation:** FGC § 15401

---

## Text

Areas used by the public for digging clams shall not be leased. The department shall designate those areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 25.)
