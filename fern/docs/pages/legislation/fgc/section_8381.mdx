# FGC § 8381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8381

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take grunion (Leuresthes tenuis) except between June 1st and March 31st.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
