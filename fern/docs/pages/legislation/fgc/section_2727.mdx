# FGC § 2727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 7.5. Wildlife and Natural Areas Conservation Program > Article: 2. Habitat Conservation Program

**Citation:** FGC § 2727

---

## Text

No funds available for appropriation under this chapter may be encumbered for any purpose described in Section 1353 of the Fish and Game Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 70. Operative November 9, 1988, by Sec. 4 of Prop. 70.)
