# FGC § 4000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Fur-Bearing Mammals > Article: 1. Trapping Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 4000

---

## Text

The following are fur-bearing mammals: pine marten, fisher, mink, river otter, gray fox, red fox, kit fox, raccoon, beaver, badger, and muskrat.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 73. Effective January 1, 2008.)
