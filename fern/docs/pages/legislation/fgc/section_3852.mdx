# FGC § 3852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. California Condor

**Citation:** FGC § 3852

---

## Text

The department shall provide funds to the Zoological Society of San Diego and to the Los Angeles Zoo for a condor breeding program on the grounds of each zoo.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 88, Sec. 1. Effective April 25, 1988.)
