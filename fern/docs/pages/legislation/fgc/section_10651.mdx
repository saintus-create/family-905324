# FGC § 10651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 1. Refuges and Other Protected Areas > Article: 2. Special Provisions for Given Areas

**Citation:** FGC § 10651

---

## Text

In the Mt. Tamalpais Game Refuge firearms may be possessed and used by members of any high school militia while on the grounds of the high school at which time they may be enrolled.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
