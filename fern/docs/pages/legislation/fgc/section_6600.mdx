# FGC § 6600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5.5. California Marine Resources Legacy Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 6600

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Marine Resources Legacy Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 687, Sec. 1. (AB 2503) Effective January 1, 2011.)
