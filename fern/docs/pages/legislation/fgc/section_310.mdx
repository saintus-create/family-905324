# FGC § 310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Other Regulatory Powers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 310

---

## Text

The commission may designate salmon spawning areas. It is unlawful to take salmon in any such spawning area, or within 250 feet of any salmon spawning station.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
