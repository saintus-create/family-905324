# FGC § 8752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 7. Round Haul Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8752

---

## Text

In Districts 6, 7, 8, 9, 10, and 11, round haul nets may be used.
