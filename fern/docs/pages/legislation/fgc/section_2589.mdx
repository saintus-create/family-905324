# FGC § 2589

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 6.5. Control of Illegally Taken Fish and Wildlife

**Citation:** FGC § 2589

---

## Text

The commission and the department may adopt regulations as are necessary to carry out their responsibilities under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1059, Sec. 4.)
