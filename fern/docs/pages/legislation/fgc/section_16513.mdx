# FGC § 16513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13.5. STATE-TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN FISHING ON THE KLAMATH RIVER > Chapter: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 16513

---

## Text

“Traditional Indian fishing practice” means a mode, method, or way of taking fish that is recognized in the customs and traditions of the Klamath River Indian Tribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1186, Sec. 1.)
