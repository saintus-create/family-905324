# FGC § 1052.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 2. General License Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 1052.5

## Text

Any stamp issued pursuant to this article is not valid unless affixed to the appropriate license document.
