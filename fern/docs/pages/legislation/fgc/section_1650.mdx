# FGC § 1650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 6.5. Habitat Restoration and Enhancement Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1650

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Habitat Restoration and Enhancement Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 604, Sec. 2. (AB 2193) Effective January 1, 2015. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 1657.)
