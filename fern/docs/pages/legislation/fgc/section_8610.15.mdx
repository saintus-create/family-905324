# FGC § 8610.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 1.4. Marine Resources Protection Act of 1990

**Citation:** FGC § 8610.15

---

## Text

This article does not preempt or supersede any other closures to protect any other wildlife, including sea otters, whales, and shorebirds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 94, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
