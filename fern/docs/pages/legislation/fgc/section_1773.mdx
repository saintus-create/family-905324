# FGC § 1773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.5. Native Species Conservation and Enhancement > Article: 5. Native California Wildlife Rehabilitation Voluntary Tax Contribution Fund

**Citation:** FGC § 1773

---

## Text

The department shall maintain the Native California Wildlife Rehabilitation Voluntary Tax Contribution Fund established pursuant to Section 18749.1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 504, Sec. 1. (AB 1031) Effective January 1, 2018.)
