# FGC § 3504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 3504

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this code permitting the sale of domestically raised game birds, it is unlawful to sell or purchase a game bird or nongame bird.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 70. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
