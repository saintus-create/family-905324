# FGC § 876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 2. Deputies, and Other Employees; County Wardens > Article: 2. County Fish and Game Wardens

**Citation:** FGC § 876

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall fix the salary to be paid and the expenses to be allowed the county fish and game warden, which salary and expenses shall be paid from the county treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
