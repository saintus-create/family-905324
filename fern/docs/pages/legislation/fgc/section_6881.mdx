# FGC § 6881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 7. Amphibia > Article: 2. Frog-Jumping Contests

**Citation:** FGC § 6881

---

## Text

Frogs to be used in frog-jumping contests shall be governed by this article only. Frogs to be so used may be taken at any time and without a license or permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
