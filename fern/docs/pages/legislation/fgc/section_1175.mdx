# FGC § 1175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 5. Private Nonprofit Hatcheries

**Citation:** FGC § 1175

---

## Text

The state shall assume no responsibility for the operation of a hatchery pursuant to this article and shall not be in any manner liable for its operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 862.)
