# FGC § 11012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Boundaries

**Citation:** FGC § 11012

---

## Text

The following constitutes Fish and Game District 412:

Those portions of the Counties of Mono and Inyo not included in other districts.
