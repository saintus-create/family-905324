# FGC § 3502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 3502

---

## Text

It is unlawful to use any mammal (except a dog) or an imitation of a mammal as a blind in approaching or taking game birds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
