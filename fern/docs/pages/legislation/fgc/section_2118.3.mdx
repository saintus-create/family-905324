# FGC § 2118.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Importation, Transportation, and Sheltering of Restricted Live Wild Animals > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 2118.3

---

## Text

No part of any elk horn or antler shall be removed from any live elk for commercial purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1074.)
