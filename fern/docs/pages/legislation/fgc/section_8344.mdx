# FGC § 8344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 8. Clams and Other Mollusks

**Citation:** FGC § 8344

---

## Text

Mussels (Mytilus californianus) may be taken only in accordance with such regulations as the commission may prescribe.
