# FGC § 11013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Boundaries

**Citation:** FGC § 11013

---

## Text

The following constitutes Fish and Game District 434:

Those portions of the Counties of San Diego and Imperial not included in other districts.
