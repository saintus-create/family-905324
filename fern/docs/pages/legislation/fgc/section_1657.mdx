# FGC § 1657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 6.5. Habitat Restoration and Enhancement Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1657

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 735, Sec. 2. (SB 716) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 6.5, commencing with Section 1650.)
