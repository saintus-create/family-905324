# FGC § 2799.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. California Wildlife Protection Act of 1990 > Article: 2. California Wildlife Protection

**Citation:** FGC § 2799.5

---

## Text

Reasonable public access to lands acquired in fee with funds made available pursuant to this chapter shall be provided except when that access may interfere with habitat protection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 117.)
