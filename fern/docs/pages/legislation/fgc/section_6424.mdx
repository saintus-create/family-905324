# FGC § 6424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 2. Artificial Reefs

**Citation:** FGC § 6424

---

## Text

The amount allocated for the administration of the program in any fiscal year may not exceed the amount authorized by applicable state and federal policy guidelines.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1103, Sec. 1.)
