# FGC § 1412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4.3. Inland Wetlands Conservation Program > Article: 2. The Inland Wetlands Conservation Program

**Citation:** FGC § 1412

---

## Text

The board may apply for and accept federal grants and receive gifts, donations, subventions, rent, royalties, and other financial support from public and private sources for the purposes of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1645, Sec. 2.)
