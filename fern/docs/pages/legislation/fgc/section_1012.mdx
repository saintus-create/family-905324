# FGC § 1012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 1012

---

## Text

The department may procure insurance for its employees for injury or death against the liability of the owner or operator of any vessel boarded by an employee as an observer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 886, Sec. 1. Effective September 14, 1980.)
