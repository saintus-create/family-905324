# FGC § 8382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8382

---

## Text

Barracuda and yellowtail not less than 28 inches in length may be taken with hook and line at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1370, Sec. 22. Effective October 27, 1992.)
