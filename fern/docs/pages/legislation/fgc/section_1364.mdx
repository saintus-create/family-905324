# FGC § 1364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4. Wildlife Conservation Law of 1947 > Article: 3.5. Oak Woodlands Conservation Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1364

---

## Text

Moneys in the fund shall be available to local government entities, park and open-space districts, resource conservation districts, private landowners, and nonprofit organizations for the purposes set forth in subdivision (d) of Section 1363.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 588, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
