# FGC § 2760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 8. Fisheries Restoration

**Citation:** FGC § 2760

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Keene-Nielsen Fisheries Restoration Act of 1985.
