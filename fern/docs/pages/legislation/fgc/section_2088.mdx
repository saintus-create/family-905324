# FGC § 2088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 3.5. Incidental Take Associated with Routine and Ongoing Activities

**Citation:** FGC § 2088

---

## Text

This article does not authorize the take of fish species and does not apply to timber harvesting governed by the State Board of Forestry. “Fish species” as used in this section means a member of the class Osteichthyes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 528, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
