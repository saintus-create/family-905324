# FGC § 6596.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 8. Ocean Fishery Research

**Citation:** FGC § 6596.3

---

## Text

The director shall provide for the solicitation of input from every person who pays a fee pursuant to Section 6596.1 on the direction of the program. The director may contract with a public or private entity to undertake this solicitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 345, Sec. 10. (AB 1949) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Sec. 6598.)
