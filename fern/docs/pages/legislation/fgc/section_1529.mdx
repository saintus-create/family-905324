# FGC § 1529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 5. Fish and Game Management > Article: 2. Wildlife Management Areas and Game Farms

**Citation:** FGC § 1529

---

## Text

The output of any state game farm shall be distributed on public lands or where the department determines that the output will receive adequate protection and be most likely to thrive and multiply.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
