# FGC § 8594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 18. Prawns and Shrimp

**Citation:** FGC § 8594

---

## Text

From Point Conception south to the Mexican border, prawns or shrimp may be taken with prawn or shrimp traps only in waters 50 fathoms or greater in depth.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 935, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1995.)
