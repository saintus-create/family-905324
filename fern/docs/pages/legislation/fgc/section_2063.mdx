# FGC § 2063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

“Feasible” means feasible as defined in Section 21061.1 of the Public Resources Code.
