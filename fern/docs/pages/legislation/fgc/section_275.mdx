# FGC § 275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Regulation of Take and Possession Generally > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** FGC § 275

---

## Text

A regulation governed by this article shall remain in effect for the period specified in the regulation or until superseded by subsequent regulation of the commission or by statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 546, Sec. 18. (SB 1473) Effective January 1, 2017.)
