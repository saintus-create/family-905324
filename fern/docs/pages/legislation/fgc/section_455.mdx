# FGC § 455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 5. Management of Deer

**Citation:** FGC § 455

---

## Text

Deer herd management unit plans shall be reviewed annually and shall be the basis for department recommendations to the commission pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 839.)
