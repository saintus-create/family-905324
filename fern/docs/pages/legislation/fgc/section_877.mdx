# FGC § 877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 2. Deputies, and Other Employees; County Wardens > Article: 2. County Fish and Game Wardens

**Citation:** FGC § 877

---

## Text

The board may by a majority vote of its members remove the warden at any time.
