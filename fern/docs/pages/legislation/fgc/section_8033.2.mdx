# FGC § 8033.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 7. Commercial Fish Business Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 8033.2

---

## Text

The annual fee for the marine aquaria receiver’s license is one thousand three hundred seventy-three dollars ($1,373).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2004.)
