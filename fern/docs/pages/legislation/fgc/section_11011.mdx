# FGC § 11011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Boundaries

**Citation:** FGC § 11011

---

## Text

The following constitutes Fish and Game District 418:

All of Los Angeles County not included within other districts.

Except as otherwise provided, all of the provisions of this code applicable to District 4 apply to District 418.
