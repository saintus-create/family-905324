# FGC § 2725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 7.5. Wildlife and Natural Areas Conservation Program > Article: 2. Habitat Conservation Program

**Citation:** FGC § 2725

---

## Text

No funds available pursuant to this chapter shall be encumbered for any lands that, due to their degraded character, will not sustain plants or wildlife or will not afford protection to a natural community on a long-term basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 70. Operative November 9, 1988, by Sec. 4 of Prop. 70.)
