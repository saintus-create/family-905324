# FGC § 9056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 4. Other Means of Taking > Article: 3. Other Appliances

**Citation:** FGC § 9056

---

## Text

The department shall adopt regulations regarding the marking and color coding of lines used in state managed fisheries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 876, Sec. 18. (SB 500) Effective January 1, 2024.)
