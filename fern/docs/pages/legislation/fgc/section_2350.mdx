# FGC § 2350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Importation and Transportation of Dead Birds, Mammals, Fish, Reptiles, and Amphibia > Article: 1. Dead Wild Birds, Mammals, Fish, Reptiles, and Amphibia

**Citation:** FGC § 2350

---

## Text

It is unlawful to transport or carry a deer or game bird out of this state, except by the holder of a nonresident hunting license or under a written permit issued by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 53. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
