# FGC § 10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 10

---

## Text

The singular number includes the plural, and the plural, the singular.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
