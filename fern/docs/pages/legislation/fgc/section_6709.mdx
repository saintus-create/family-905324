# FGC § 6709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 6. Kelp and Other Aquatic Plants > Article: 3. Exclusive Leases

**Citation:** FGC § 6709

---

## Text

A lease, or any renewal thereof, shall be submitted to, and approved by, the Department of General Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1373, Sec. 2.)
