# FGC § 330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Other Regulatory Powers > Article: 2. Special Seasons

**Citation:** FGC § 330

---

## Text

Cooperative hunting areas, as described in Section 1575, may be established in connection with any area opened to hunting under the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 17. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
