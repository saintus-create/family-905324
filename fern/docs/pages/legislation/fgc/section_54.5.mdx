# FGC § 54.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 54.5

---

## Text

“Marine finfish aquaculture” means the propagation, cultivation, or maintenance of finfish species in the waters of the Pacific Ocean that are regulated by this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 36, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
