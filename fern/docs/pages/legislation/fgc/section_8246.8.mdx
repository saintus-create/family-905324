# FGC § 8246.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 4.5. Commercial Salmon Vessel Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 8246.8

---

## Text

The commission and the department may make and enforce regulations that may be necessary or convenient for carrying out any power, authority, or jurisdiction delegated to it under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1164, Sec. 2.)
