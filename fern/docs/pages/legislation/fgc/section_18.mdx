# FGC § 18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 18

---

## Text

“Bag limit” means the maximum limit, in number or amount, of birds, mammals, fish, reptiles, or amphibians that may lawfully be taken by any one person during a specified period of time.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
