# FGC § 5505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FGC § 5505

---

## Text

Mollusks, crustaceans, and amphibia may be used for bait or released in the same waters wherein taken.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
