# FGC § 1413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4.3. Inland Wetlands Conservation Program > Article: 2. The Inland Wetlands Conservation Program

**Citation:** FGC § 1413

---

## Text

The board may acquire or accept the gift or dedication of fee title, easements, leases, development rights, or other interests in lands in inland areas necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1645, Sec. 2.)
