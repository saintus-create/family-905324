# FGC § 7709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 7709

---

## Text

The commission may establish rules and regulations for the commercial taking of fish for educational and scientific purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 689.)
