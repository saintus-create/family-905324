# FGC § 14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 14

---

## Text

“Anadromous fish” means fish which spawn in fresh water and spend a portion of their lives in the ocean.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 1.5.)
