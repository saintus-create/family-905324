# FGC § 8432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 10. Fresh-water Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8432

---

## Text

Steelhead trout from without the State may not be sold or possessed in District 112 in excess of the daily bag limit on steelhead trout for that district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
