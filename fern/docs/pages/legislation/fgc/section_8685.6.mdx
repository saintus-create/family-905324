# FGC § 8685.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 5. Gill and Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8685.6

---

## Text

It is unlawful to sell or possess for sale any salmon, steelhead, or striped bass which were taken in California waters by the use of a gill net.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 393, Sec. 2. Effective July 11, 1980.)
