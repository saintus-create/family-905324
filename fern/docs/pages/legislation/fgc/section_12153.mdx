# FGC § 12153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. FINES AND PENALTIES > Chapter: 2. Forfeitures, Revocation, and Seizures

**Citation:** FGC § 12153

---

## Text

A commercial fishing license is forfeited for the violation of Sections 1050.1 to 1059, inclusive, or Section 2012, or of any of the provisions of this code relating to the use of nets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 221. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
