# FGC § 1170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 5. Private Nonprofit Hatcheries

**Citation:** FGC § 1170

---

## Text

The commission may issue a permit, subject to such restrictions and regulations as the commission deems desirable, to a nonprofit organization to construct and operate an anadromous fish hatchery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 862.)
