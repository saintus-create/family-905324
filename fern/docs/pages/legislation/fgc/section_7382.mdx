# FGC § 7382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 6. Steelhead Trout

**Citation:** FGC § 7382

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 72, Sec. 5. (SB 156) Effective July 2, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6, commencing with Section 7380.)
