# FGC § 22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 22

---

## Text

“Bird” means a wild bird or part of a wild bird.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 2. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
