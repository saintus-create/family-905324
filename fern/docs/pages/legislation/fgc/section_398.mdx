# FGC § 398

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Other Regulatory Powers > Article: 6. Falconry

**Citation:** FGC § 398

---

## Text

The base year for determining the inflationary index applied to the fee established by Section 396 shall be the 1984–85 fiscal year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 6. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
