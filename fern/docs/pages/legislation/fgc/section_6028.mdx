# FGC § 6028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Dams, Conduits, and Screens > Article: 4. Conduits and Screens: Diversions Less Than 250 Cubic Feet per Second

**Citation:** FGC § 6028

---

## Text

All money paid by the department to the owner of a conduit pursuant to this article shall be paid out of the Fish and Game Preservation Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
