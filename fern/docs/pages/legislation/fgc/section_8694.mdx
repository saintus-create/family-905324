# FGC § 8694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 5. Gill and Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8694

---

## Text

In District 19A, gill nets may not be used, nor may they be possessed on any boat.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
