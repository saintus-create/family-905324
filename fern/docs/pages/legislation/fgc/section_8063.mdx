# FGC § 8063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 7.5. Landing Fees

**Citation:** FGC § 8063

---

## Text

If the department determines that any overpayment of a landing fee has been made intentionally or by reason of carelessness, it shall not allow any interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 27. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
