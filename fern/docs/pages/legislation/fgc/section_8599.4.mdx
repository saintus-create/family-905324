# FGC § 8599.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 20. White Sharks and Basking Sharks

**Citation:** FGC § 8599.4

---

## Text

The commission may adopt regulations to manage basking sharks. A basking shark may not be taken commercially unless the commission adopts regulations for that activity and the taking is in accordance with those regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 483, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2000.)
