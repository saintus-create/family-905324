# FGC § 3965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1.5. Exotic Game Mammals

**Citation:** FGC § 3965

---

## Text

(a) Exotic game mammals include wild pigs, including feral pigs and European wild boar (genus Sus) described in Section 4650.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 469, Sec. 16. (SB 856) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative July 1, 2024, by its own provisions.)
