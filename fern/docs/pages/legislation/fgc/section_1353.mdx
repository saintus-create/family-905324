# FGC § 1353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4. Wildlife Conservation Law of 1947 > Article: 3. Powers and Purposes

**Citation:** FGC § 1353

---

## Text

The board may purchase such boats, aircraft, or equipment as it deems necessary for the use of the department for the improvement and protection of marine fisheries.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
