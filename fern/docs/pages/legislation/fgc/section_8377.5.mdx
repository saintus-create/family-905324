# FGC § 8377.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8377.5

---

## Text

Pacific bonito shall be measured from the tip of the lower jaw to the center of the fork of the tail fin.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 269, Sec. 5.)
