# FGC § 6451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 4. Grass Carp

**Citation:** FGC § 6451

---

## Text

All providers of triploid grass carp for use under this article shall provide certification acceptable to the department of triploidy and disease-free conditions for all fish introduced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 249, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
