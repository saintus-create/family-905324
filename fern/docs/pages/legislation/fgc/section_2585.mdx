# FGC § 2585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 6.5. Control of Illegally Taken Fish and Wildlife

**Citation:** FGC § 2585

---

## Text

The administrative penalties imposed under this chapter are in addition to any forfeiture of equipment pursuant to Section 12157 or forfeiture of birds, mammals, amphibia, reptiles, or fish pursuant to Section 12159.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 56. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
