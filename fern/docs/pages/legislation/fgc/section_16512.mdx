# FGC § 16512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13.5. STATE-TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN FISHING ON THE KLAMATH RIVER > Chapter: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 16512

---

## Text

“Take” means pursue, catch, capture, or kill, or attempt to pursue, catch, capture, or kill.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1186, Sec. 1.)
