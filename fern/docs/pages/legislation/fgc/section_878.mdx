# FGC § 878

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 2. Deputies, and Other Employees; County Wardens > Article: 2. County Fish and Game Wardens

**Citation:** FGC § 878

---

## Text

The county fish and game warden shall enforce the state laws relating to the protection of fish and game. The warden has the powers and authority conferred by law upon peace officers listed in Section 830.6 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 817, Sec. 3.)
