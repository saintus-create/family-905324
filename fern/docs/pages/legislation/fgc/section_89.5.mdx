# FGC § 89.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 89.5

---

## Text

“Wildlife” means and includes all wild animals, birds, plants, fish, amphibians, reptiles, and related ecological communities, including the habitat upon which the wildlife depends for its continued viability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 9. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
