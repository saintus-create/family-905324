# FGC § 3505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 3505

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take, sell, or purchase any aigrette or egret, osprey, bird of paradise, goura, numidi, or any part of such a bird.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
