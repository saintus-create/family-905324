# FGC § 6650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 6. Kelp and Other Aquatic Plants > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 6650

---

## Text

Every person engaged in harvesting kelp or other aquatic plants for profit in the waters of this State shall have a license for that purpose.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
