# FGC § 6653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 6. Kelp and Other Aquatic Plants > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 6653

---

## Text

The commission may make such regulations as may be necessary to insure the proper harvesting of kelp and other aquatic plants.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
