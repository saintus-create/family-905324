# FGC § 8433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 10. Fresh-water Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8433

---

## Text

Nothing in this article applies to trout grown pursuant to Division 12 (commencing with Section 15000).
