# FGC § 5669

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Pollution > Article: 2. Shellfish Area Contamination

**Citation:** FGC § 5669

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “shellfish” means any bivalve mollusk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 9.)
