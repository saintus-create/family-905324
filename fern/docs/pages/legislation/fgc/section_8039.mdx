# FGC § 8039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 7. Commercial Fish Business Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 8039

---

## Text

The fees specified in this article are applicable to the 2004 license year and shall be adjusted annually thereafter pursuant to Section 713.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 2004.)
