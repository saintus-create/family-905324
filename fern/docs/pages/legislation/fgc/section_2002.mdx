# FGC § 2002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Taking and Possessing in General

**Citation:** FGC § 2002

---

## Text

It is unlawful to possess a bird, mammal, fish, reptile, amphibian, or part of any of those animals, taken in violation of this code or a regulation adopted pursuant to this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 43. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
