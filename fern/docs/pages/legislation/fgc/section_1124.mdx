# FGC § 1124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 3. State Fish Hatcheries

**Citation:** FGC § 1124

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take any fish in any pond, reservoir, or other water-retaining structure belonging to or controlled by the department and used for propagating, raising, holding, protecting, or conserving fish.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2008.)
