# FGC § 57

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 57

---

## Text

“Nonresident” means a person who is not a resident as defined in Section 70.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 7. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
