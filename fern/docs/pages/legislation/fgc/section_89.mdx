# FGC § 89

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 89

---

## Text

For the purposes of this code, “recycled water” or “reclaimed water” has the same meaning as recycled water as defined in subdivision (n) of Section 13050 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 28, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
