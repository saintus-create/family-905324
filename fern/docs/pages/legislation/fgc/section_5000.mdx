# FGC § 5000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PROTECTED REPTILES AND AMPHIBIANS > Chapter: 1. Turtles > Article: 1. Desert Tortoises

**Citation:** FGC § 5000

---

## Text

It is unlawful to sell, purchase, harm, take, possess, transport, or shoot a projectile at, a tortoise (Gopherus). This section does not apply to the taking of a tortoise when authorized by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 77. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
