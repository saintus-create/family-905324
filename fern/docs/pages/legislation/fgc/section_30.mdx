# FGC § 30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 30

---

## Text

“Commission” means the Fish and Game Commission, and “commissioner” means a member of the Fish and Game Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
