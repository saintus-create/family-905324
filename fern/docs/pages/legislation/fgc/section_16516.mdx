# FGC § 16516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13.5. STATE-TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN FISHING ON THE KLAMATH RIVER > Chapter: 2. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 16516

---

## Text

“Subsistence purposes” means fish or game taken by qualified Indian tribal members of the Klamath River Indian Tribes for personal consumption by the tribal members or their immediate families.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1186, Sec. 1.)
