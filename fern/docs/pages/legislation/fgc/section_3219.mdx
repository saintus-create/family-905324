# FGC § 3219

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 1. Domesticated Game Breeding

**Citation:** FGC § 3219

---

## Text

Domesticated reindeer may be imported into this State and sold only in accordance with regulations which the commission may prescribe.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
