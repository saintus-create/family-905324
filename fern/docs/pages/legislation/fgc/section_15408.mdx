# FGC § 15408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. AQUACULTURE > Chapter: 5. Leasing of State Water Bottoms

**Citation:** FGC § 15408

---

## Text

The commission shall promulgate regulations governing the termination of leases due to failure to pay rent or improper use of the leasehold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 25.)
