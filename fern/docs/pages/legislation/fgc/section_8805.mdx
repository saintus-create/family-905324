# FGC § 8805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 9. Beach Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8805

---

## Text

In Districts 12 and 13, beach nets may not be possessed on any boat.
