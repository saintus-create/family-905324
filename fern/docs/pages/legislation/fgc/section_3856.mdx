# FGC § 3856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. California Condor

**Citation:** FGC § 3856

---

## Text

The department shall include copies of the biannual reports from the zoos in the annual report to the Legislature on the status of listed species required in Section 2079.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 88, Sec. 1. Effective April 25, 1988.)
