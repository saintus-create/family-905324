# FGC § 8250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 5. Lobster

**Citation:** FGC § 8250

---

## Text

As used in this code, “spiny lobster” refers to the species Panulirus interruptus.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
