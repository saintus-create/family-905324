# FGC § 2537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Hunting and Fishing Guides

**Citation:** FGC § 2537

---

## Text

A person operating under a commercial passenger fishing boat license issued pursuant to Section 7920 is not required to obtain a guide license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 750, Sec. 4.)
