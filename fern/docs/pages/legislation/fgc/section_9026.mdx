# FGC § 9026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 4. Other Means of Taking > Article: 2. Fishing Lines

**Citation:** FGC § 9026

---

## Text

Set lines may be used in Districts 6, 7, 10, 17, 18, and 19. It is unlawful to use set lines with hooks more than 100 feet above the anchor or ocean bottom.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 318, Sec. 8.)
