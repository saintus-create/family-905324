# FGC § 16004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. STATE-TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN FISHING > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 16004

---

## Text

“Traditional Indian fishing practice” means a mode, method, or way of taking fish that is recognized in the customs and traditions of the Covelo Indian Community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 691, Sec. 1.)
