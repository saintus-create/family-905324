# FGC § 5507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** FGC § 5507

---

## Text

(a) It is unlawful for any person to possess a fish spear or gaff within 300 feet of a lake or stream in this state, at a time when spearing is prohibited in that lake or stream.

(b) This section does not apply to either of the following:

(1) Possession of a gaff carried as an accessory while angling.

(2) Possession of a fish spear or gaff in one’s own home.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 102. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
