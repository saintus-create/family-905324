# FGC § 1401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4.3. Inland Wetlands Conservation Program > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 1401

---

## Text

“Fund” means the Inland Wetlands Conservation Fund, created in the Wildlife Restoration Fund by Section 1430.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1645, Sec. 2.)
