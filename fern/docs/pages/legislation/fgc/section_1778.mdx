# FGC § 1778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.8. Sacramento-San Joaquin Valley Wetlands Mitigation Bank Act of 1993 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 1778

---

## Text

“Operator” means the department, or a public or private person or entity approved by the department, to administer a wetlands mitigation bank site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1254, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
