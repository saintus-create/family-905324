# FGC § 7290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 1.5. Pismo Clams

**Citation:** FGC § 7290

---

## Text

No pismo clam not in the shell may be possessed, except when it is being prepared for immediate consumption.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
