# FGC § 2085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 3. Taking, Importation, Exportation, or Sale

**Citation:** FGC § 2085

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to any species designated as a candidate species under Section 2074.2 if notice has been given pursuant to Section 2074.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1162, Sec. 6.)
