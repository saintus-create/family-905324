# FGC § 8567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 16. Drift Gill Net Shark and Swordfish Fishery

**Citation:** FGC § 8567

---

## Text

The fee for a drift gill net shark and swordfish permit shall be three hundred thirty dollars ($330).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 870, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 1997.)
