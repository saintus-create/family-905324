# FGC § 4012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Fur-Bearing Mammals > Article: 1. Trapping Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 4012

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take any red fox for profitmaking purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 76. Effective January 1, 2008.)
