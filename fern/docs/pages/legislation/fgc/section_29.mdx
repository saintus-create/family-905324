# FGC § 29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 29

---

## Text

“Closed season” means that period of time during which the taking of birds, mammals, fish, amphibians, or reptiles is prohibited.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 4. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
