# FGC § 2600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Fish and Wildlife Habitat Enhancement Act of 1984 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 2600

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Fish and Wildlife Habitat Enhancement Act of 1984.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 19 at the June 5, 1984, election. Operative July 1, 1984, by Stats. 1984, Ch. 6, Sec. 3.)
