# FGC § 2700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 7.5. Wildlife and Natural Areas Conservation Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 2700

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Wildlife and Natural Areas Conservation Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 70. Operative November 9, 1988, by Sec. 4 of Prop. 70.)
