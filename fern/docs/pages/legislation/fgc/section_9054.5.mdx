# FGC § 9054.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 4. Other Means of Taking > Article: 3. Other Appliances

**Citation:** FGC § 9054.5

---

## Text

The commission shall adopt regulations related to commercial sea urchin diving permits, and the vessels used to commercially fish for sea urchin, in order to better manage the number of permits issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 876, Sec. 16. (SB 500) Effective January 1, 2024.)
