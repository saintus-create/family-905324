# FGC § 6456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 4. Grass Carp

**Citation:** FGC § 6456

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as restricting grass carp programs approved by the department on or before June 1, 1995.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 249, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
