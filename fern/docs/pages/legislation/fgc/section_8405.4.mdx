# FGC § 8405.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9.1. Sea Cucumbers

**Citation:** FGC § 8405.4

---

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on April 1, 2030, and as of January 1, 2031, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 472, Sec. 29. (SB 262) Effective January 1, 2020. Inoperative April 1, 2030. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, by its own provisions. Note: Termination clause affects Article 9.1, commencing with Section 8405.)
