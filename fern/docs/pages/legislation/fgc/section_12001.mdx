# FGC § 12001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. FINES AND PENALTIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 12001

---

## Text

Any person who takes any bird or mammal in violation of an order issued pursuant to Section 12150 of this code, is guilty of a felony.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2210.)
