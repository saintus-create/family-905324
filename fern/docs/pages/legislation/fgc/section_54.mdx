# FGC § 54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 54

---

## Text

“Mammal” means a wild or feral mammal or part of a wild or feral animal, but not a wild, feral, or undomesticated burro.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 6. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
