# FGC § 8610.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 1.4. Marine Resources Protection Act of 1990

**Citation:** FGC § 8610.1

---

## Text

The Marine Resources Protection Act of 1990 (Art. XB, Cal. Const.) was adopted as an initiative constitutional amendment at the November 6, 1990, general election. This article codifies and implements that initiative constitutional amendment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 94, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
