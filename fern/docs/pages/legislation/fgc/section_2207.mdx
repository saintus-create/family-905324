# FGC § 2207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Importation, Transportation, and Sheltering of Restricted Live Wild Animals > Article: 5. Circus Cruelty Prevention Act

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Circus Cruelty Prevention Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 768, Sec. 1. (SB 313) Effective January 1, 2020.)
