# FGC § 2000.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Taking and Possessing in General

**Citation:** FGC § 2000.3

---

## Text

(a) As used in this chapter, “roadway” has the same meaning as defined in Section 530 of the Vehicle Code.

(b) As used in this chapter, “vehicle” has the same meaning as defined in Section 670 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 869, Sec. 4. (SB 395) Effective January 1, 2020.)
