# FGC § 8386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8386

---

## Text

Barracuda and yellowtail shall be measured from the tip of the lower jaw to the end of the longer lobe of the tail.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1370, Sec. 26. Effective October 27, 1992.)
