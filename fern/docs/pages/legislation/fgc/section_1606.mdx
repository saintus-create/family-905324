# FGC § 1606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 6. Fish and Wildlife Protection and Conservation

**Citation:** FGC § 1606

---

## Text

The department shall not condition the issuance of an agreement on the receipt of another local, state, or federal permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 736, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
