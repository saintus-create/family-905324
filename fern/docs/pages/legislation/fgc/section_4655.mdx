# FGC § 4655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 7. Wild Pigs

**Citation:** FGC § 4655

---

## Text

(a) Wild pig validations are valid only during that portion of the current hunting license year in which wild pigs may be taken or possessed in any area of the state.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 33) and added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 469, Sec. 34. (SB 856) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative July 1, 2024, by its own provisions.)
