# FGC § 1455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 4.4. California Desert Conservation Program

**Citation:** FGC § 1455

---

## Text

The preservation and restoration of desert habitat shall be a primary concern of the board and the department, and of all state agencies whose activities impact desert habitat.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 380, Sec. 1. (AB 1183) Effective January 1, 2022.)
