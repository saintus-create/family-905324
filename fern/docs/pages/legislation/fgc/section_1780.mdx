# FGC § 1780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.8. Sacramento-San Joaquin Valley Wetlands Mitigation Bank Act of 1993 > Article: 2. Legislative Goals

**Citation:** FGC § 1780

---

## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to ensure that no net loss of wetland acreage or habitat values within the Sacramento-San Joaquin Valley occurs as a result of fill permit activities pursuant to Section 404 of the federal Clean Water Act (33 U.S.C. Sec. 1344 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1254, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
