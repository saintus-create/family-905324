# FGC § 3001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Methods of Taking

**Citation:** FGC § 3001

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take birds or mammals with firearms, BB devices as defined in Section 16250 of the Penal Code, crossbows, or with bow and arrow when intoxicated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 178, Sec. 29. (SB 1115) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 107 of Ch. 178.)
