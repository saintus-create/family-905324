# FGC § 1054.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 2. General License Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 1054.5

---

## Text

The department may issue and shall collect payment for any entitlement, document, or authorization for which a fee is authorized pursuant to this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1370, Sec. 5. Effective October 27, 1992.)
