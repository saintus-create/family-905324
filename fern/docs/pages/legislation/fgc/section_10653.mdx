# FGC § 10653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 1. Refuges and Other Protected Areas > Article: 2. Special Provisions for Given Areas

**Citation:** FGC § 10653

---

## Text

In the San Francisco Fish and Game Refuge, birds, mammals, fish, amphibians, and reptiles legally possessed may be carried openly by persons traveling through the refuge on public roads, between one-half hour before sunrise and one-half hour after sunset.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 180. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
