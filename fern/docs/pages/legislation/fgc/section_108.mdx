# FGC § 108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** FGC § 108

---

## Text

By July 1, 2013, the commission shall adopt rules to govern the business practices and processes of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 592, Sec. 5. (AB 2609) Effective January 1, 2013.)
