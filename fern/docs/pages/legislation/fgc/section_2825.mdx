# FGC § 2825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Natural Community Conservation Planning Act

**Citation:** FGC § 2825

---

## Text

The department may adopt regulations for the development and implementation of natural community conservation plans consistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 4, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
