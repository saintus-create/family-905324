# FGC § 6911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 8. Salmon, Steelhead Trout, and Anadromous Fisheries Program Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 6911

---

## Text

“Production” means the survival of fish to adulthood as measured by the abundance of the recreational and commercial catch together with the return of fish to the state’s spawning streams.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1545, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1988.)
