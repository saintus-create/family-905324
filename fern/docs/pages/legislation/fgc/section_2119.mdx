# FGC § 2119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Importation, Transportation, and Sheltering of Restricted Live Wild Animals > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 2119

---

## Text

The department shall publish from time to time as changes arise, a list of animals that may not be imported or transported into this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 51. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
