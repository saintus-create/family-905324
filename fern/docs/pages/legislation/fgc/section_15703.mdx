# FGC § 15703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. AQUACULTURE > Chapter: 8. Aquaculture Development Committee

**Citation:** FGC § 15703

---

## Text

The committee shall meet on the call of the director, but not less than twice each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 25.)
