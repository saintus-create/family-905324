# FGC § 7149.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 3. Sport Fishing Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 7149.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 7149.05, a sport fishing license is not required for a resident to take a rattlesnake (genus Crotalus or Sistrurus).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 683, Sec. 22. (SB 798) Effective January 1, 2016.)
