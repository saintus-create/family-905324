# FGC § 1768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.5. Native Species Conservation and Enhancement > Article: 3. Native Species Conservation and Enhancement Account

**Citation:** FGC § 1768

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the revenues from fees and sales under this article are related to the protection and propagation of fish and game within the meaning of Section 9 of Article XVI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1539, Sec. 10.)
