# FGC § 8399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8399

---

## Text

North of Point Conception, squid may be taken the year around; however, the commission may adopt regulations specifying the days of the week and the times of the day when squid may be taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 131, Sec. 16.5. Effective June 27, 1983.)
