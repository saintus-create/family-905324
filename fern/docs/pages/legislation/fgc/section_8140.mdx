# FGC § 8140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 1. General Season

**Citation:** FGC § 8140

---

## Text

All fish, the taking of which is not otherwise restricted for commercial purposes, by state or federal law or any regulations adopted pursuant to those laws, may be taken at any time for commercial purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1052, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1999.)
