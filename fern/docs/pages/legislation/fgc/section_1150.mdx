# FGC § 1150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 4. County Fish Hatcheries

**Citation:** FGC § 1150

---

## Text

The boards of supervisors of the several counties may establish and maintain fish hatcheries, and may purchase the spawn or ova of fish.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
