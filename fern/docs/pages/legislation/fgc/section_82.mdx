# FGC § 82

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 82

---

## Text

“Slurp gun” means a self-contained, hand-held device used to capture fish by rapidly drawing water containing fish into a closed chamber.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 742, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
