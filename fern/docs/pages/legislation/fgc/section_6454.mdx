# FGC § 6454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 5. Fish Planting and Propagation > Article: 4. Grass Carp

**Citation:** FGC § 6454

---

## Text

The department shall establish permit and inspection fees sufficient to recover, but not exceed, the initial and ongoing costs of the program under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 249, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
