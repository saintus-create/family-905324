# FGC § 2799.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. California Wildlife Protection Act of 1990 > Article: 2. California Wildlife Protection

**Citation:** FGC § 2799.6

---

## Text

Only those amounts of money which are transferred to the fund from the General Fund pursuant to Section 2796 may be reappropriated for purposes of this chapter by a two-thirds vote of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 117.)
