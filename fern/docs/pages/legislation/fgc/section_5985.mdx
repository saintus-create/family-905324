# FGC § 5985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Dams, Conduits, and Screens > Article: 3. Conduits and Screens: Diversions Over 250 Cubic Feet per Second

**Citation:** FGC § 5985

---

## Text

The owner of the conduit shall, within 10 days after completing the installation of the screen, return to the department any surplus money advanced by the department pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
