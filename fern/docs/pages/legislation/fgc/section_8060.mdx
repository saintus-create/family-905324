# FGC § 8060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 7.5. Landing Fees

**Citation:** FGC § 8060

---

## Text

Failure to file a claim for refund or credit within the time prescribed in this article constitutes a waiver of any demand against the state on account of overpayment of a landing fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 24. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
