# FGC § 2272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Importation and Transportation of Live Plants and Animals > Article: 3. Aquatic Plants and Animals

**Citation:** FGC § 2272

---

## Text

Each package containing any live aquatic plant or animal shall bear, in a conspicuous place, a tag on which shall be stated the name and address of the consignor, the name and address of the consignee, and the exact contents of the package.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 85, Sec. 5. Effective April 19, 1984.)
