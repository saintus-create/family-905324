# FGC § 8598.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 19. Marine Aquaria Pets

**Citation:** FGC § 8598.6

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 12000, a violation of this article or any regulation adopted thereunder is punishable by a fine of not less than two thousand dollars ($2,000) or more than five thousand dollars ($5,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 742, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1993.)
