# FGC § 7850.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 3. Commercial Fishing Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 7850.5

---

## Text

Section 7850 does not apply to any person who is employed by the fish receiver to unload fish or fish products, or to load or unload food and supplies, on or from a commercial fishing boat at a dock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 80, Sec. 1. Effective June 30, 1991.)
