# FGC § 301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. FISH AND GAME COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Other Regulatory Powers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 301

---

## Text

The commission may adopt regulations that it deems necessary for the disposition of birds or mammals that are killed accidentally.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 154, Sec. 13. (AB 1527) Effective January 1, 2016.)
