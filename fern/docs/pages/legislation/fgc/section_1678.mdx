# FGC § 1678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 6.7. Restoration Management Permit Act

**Citation:** FGC § 1678

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2035, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 681, Sec. 2. (AB 1581) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2035, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 6.7, commencing with Section 1670.)
