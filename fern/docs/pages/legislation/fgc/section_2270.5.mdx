# FGC § 2270.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Importation and Transportation of Live Plants and Animals > Article: 3. Aquatic Plants and Animals

**Citation:** FGC § 2270.5

---

## Text

Section 2270 does not apply to the importation of live aquatic plants or animals for aquaculture purposes by a registered aquaculturist if the importation has been approved by the department pursuant to Section 15600.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1686, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
