# FGC § 1766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.5. Native Species Conservation and Enhancement > Article: 3. Native Species Conservation and Enhancement Account

**Citation:** FGC § 1766

---

## Text

The department may also offer for sale a native species stamp, promotional materials, and nature study aids. The fee for a native species stamp is seven dollars and fifty cents ($7.50), as adjusted in the calendar years following 1989 in accordance with Section 713.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1539, Sec. 8.)
