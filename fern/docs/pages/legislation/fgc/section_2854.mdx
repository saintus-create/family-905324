# FGC § 2854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 10.5. Marine Life Protection Act

**Citation:** FGC § 2854

---

## Text

The State Interagency Marine Managed Areas Workgroup shall, after appropriate consultation with members of the public, determine future actions for implementing the recommendations of its final report.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 132, Sec. 60. (AB 1760) Effective January 1, 2024.)
