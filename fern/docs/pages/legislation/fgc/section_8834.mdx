# FGC § 8834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 10. Trawl Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8834

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take or possess more than 500 pounds of crabs on any boat on which any type of trawl or drag net is carried or operated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1300, Sec. 14.)
