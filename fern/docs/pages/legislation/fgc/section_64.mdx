# FGC § 64

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 64

---

## Text

“Order,” “rule,” and “regulation” are used interchangeably and each includes the others.
