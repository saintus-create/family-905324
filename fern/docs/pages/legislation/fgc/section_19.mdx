# FGC § 19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 19

---

## Text

“Possession limit” means the maximum, in number or amount, of birds, mammals, fish, reptiles, or amphibians that may be lawfully possessed by one person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
