# FGC § 6921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 8. Salmon, Steelhead Trout, and Anadromous Fisheries Program Act > Article: 3. Salmon, Steelhead Trout, and Anadromous Fisheries Program

**Citation:** FGC § 6921

## Text

The program shall identify the measures the department will carry out to achieve the policies set forth in Section 6902.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1545, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1988.)
