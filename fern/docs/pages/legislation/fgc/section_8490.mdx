# FGC § 8490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 12. Crayfish

**Citation:** FGC § 8490

---

## Text

The taking of crayfish shall be subject to regulations as prescribed by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8491 by Stats. 2013, Ch. 226, Sec. 2. (AB 165) Effective January 1, 2014.)
