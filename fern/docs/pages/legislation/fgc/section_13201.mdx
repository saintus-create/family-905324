# FGC § 13201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. REVENUE > Chapter: 3. Accounting

**Citation:** FGC § 13201

---

## Text

In establishing the appropriate programs or activities for this system, the department shall consider the following programs or activities:

(a) Freshwater fisheries activities.

(b) Marine fisheries activities.

(c) Wildlife management activities.

(d) Planning and environmental review.

(e) Law enforcement.

(f) Nongame and endangered species.

(g) General administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1442, Sec. 23.)
