# FGC § 3803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 3. Nongame Birds

**Citation:** FGC § 3803

---

## Text

The department may take any individual bird, or birds of any species, that, in its opinion, are unduly preying upon any species of bird, mammal, reptile, amphibian, or fish.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 2008.)
