# FGC § 2624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Fish and Wildlife Habitat Enhancement Act of 1984 > Article: 2. Habitat Enhancement Program

**Citation:** FGC § 2624

---

## Text

Funds available pursuant to subdivision (b) of Section 2620 may be encumbered only for lands constituting habitat that is subject to destruction, drastic modification, or severe curtailment of habitat values.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 19 at the June 5, 1984, election. Operative July 1, 1984, by Stats. 1984, Ch. 6, Sec. 3.)
