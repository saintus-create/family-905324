# FGC § 90.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Marine Life Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 90.5

---

## Text

“Bycatch” means fish or other marine life that are taken in a fishery but which are not the target of the fishery. “Bycatch” includes discards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1052, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
