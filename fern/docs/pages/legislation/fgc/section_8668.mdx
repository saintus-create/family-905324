# FGC § 8668

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 4. Nets Generally in Particular Districts

**Citation:** FGC § 8668

---

## Text

No commercial fishing under Section 8666, 8667, or 8669 shall be carried on within the boundaries of any state or federal game refuge.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
