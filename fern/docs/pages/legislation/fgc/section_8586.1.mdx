# FGC § 8586.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 17. Nearshore Fisheries Management Act

**Citation:** FGC § 8586.1

---

## Text

Funding to pay the costs of this article shall be made available from the revenues deposited in the Fish and Game Preservation Fund pursuant to Sections 8587, 8589.5, and 8589.7, and other funds appropriated for these purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1052, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1999.)
