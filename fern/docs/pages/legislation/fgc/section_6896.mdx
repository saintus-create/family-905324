# FGC § 6896

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 7. Amphibia > Article: 3. Commercial Use of Amphibians Except Frogs

**Citation:** FGC § 6896

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the commission shall establish rules for the commercial take, sale, transport, export, or import of native amphibians.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 605.)
