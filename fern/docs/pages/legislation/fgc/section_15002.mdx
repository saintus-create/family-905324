# FGC § 15002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. AQUACULTURE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** FGC § 15002

---

## Text

Any person who takes aquaculture products without lawful entitlement is subject to prosecution for theft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1486, Sec. 25.)
