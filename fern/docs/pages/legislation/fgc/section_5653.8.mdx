# FGC § 5653.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Pollution > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FGC § 5653.8

---

## Text

For purposes of Sections 5653 and 5653.3, “person” does not include a partnership, corporation, or other type of association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1109, Sec. 2. Effective September 29, 1994.)
