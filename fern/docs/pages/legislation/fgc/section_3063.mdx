# FGC § 3063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Special Colorado River Hunting Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 3063

---

## Text

The fee for a special Colorado River hunting license shall be two dollars ($2), and each such license shall be valid until the end of the calendar year in which it is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
