# FGC § 1800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 8. Conservation of Wildlife Resources > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 1800

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “wildlife” means birds, mammals, and reptiles not raised in captivity.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1190.)
