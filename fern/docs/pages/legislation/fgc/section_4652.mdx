# FGC § 4652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 7. Wild Pigs

**Citation:** FGC § 4652

---

## Text

(a) It is unlawful to take any wild pig, except as provided in Section 4181, without first procuring a validation authorizing the taking of that wild pig in accordance with this chapter.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 26) and added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 469, Sec. 27. (SB 856) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative July 1, 2024, by its own provisions.)
