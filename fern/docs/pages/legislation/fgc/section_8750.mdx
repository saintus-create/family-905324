# FGC § 8750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 7. Round Haul Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8750

---

## Text

As used in this article, “round haul nets” are circle seines, and include purse seines and ring or half ring, and lampara nets.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
