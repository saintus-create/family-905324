# FGC § 10770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 2. Specific Refuge Boundaries > Article: 1. Fish and Game Refuges

**Citation:** FGC § 10770

---

## Text

The area described in this article is a fish and game refuge.
