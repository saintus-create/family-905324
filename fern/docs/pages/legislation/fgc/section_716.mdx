# FGC § 716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 1.5. Wildlife Violator Compact > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

The Wildlife Violator Compact is hereby enacted into law and entered into with all other participating states.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 398, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002. Compact effective as prescribed in Section 716.9.)
