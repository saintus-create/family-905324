# FGC § 16007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. STATE-TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN FISHING > Chapter: 3. Negotiation and Approval of Agreement

**Citation:** FGC § 16007

---

## Text

Any agreement or compact entered into pursuant to Section 16006 shall be submitted by the department to the commission for review and approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 691, Sec. 1.)
