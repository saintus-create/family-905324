# FGC § 4330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 4. Deer > Article: 2. License Tags

**Citation:** FGC § 4330

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take any deer without first procuring a deer tag or permit authorizing the taking of that deer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 285, Sec. 83. Effective January 1, 2008.)
