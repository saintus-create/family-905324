# FGC § 8721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 6. Trammel Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8721

---

## Text

In Districts 1, 2, and 3, trammel nets may not be possessed on any boat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
