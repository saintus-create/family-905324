# FGC § 2953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Salton Sea Restoration Act > Article: 3. Lithium

**Citation:** FGC § 2953

## Text

For purposes of implementing this article, the agency may enter into agreements with other agencies, including the Department of Water Resources.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 63, Sec. 1. (SB 125) Effective June 30, 2022.)
