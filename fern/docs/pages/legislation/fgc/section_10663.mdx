# FGC § 10663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. REFUGES > Chapter: 1. Refuges and Other Protected Areas > Article: 2. Special Provisions for Given Areas

**Citation:** FGC § 10663

---

## Text

In Fish and Game Refuge 4-D the lawful occupant of privately owned lands or the employees of such occupants may possess firearms without a permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 973.)
