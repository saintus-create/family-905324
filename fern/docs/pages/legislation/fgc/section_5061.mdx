# FGC § 5061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PROTECTED REPTILES AND AMPHIBIANS > Chapter: 3. Commercial Use of Reptiles

**Citation:** FGC § 5061

---

## Text

The commission shall establish rules and regulations for the commercial take, sale, transport, export, or import of native reptiles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 605.)
