# FGC § 8383.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9. Salt-water and Anadromous Fish Generally

**Citation:** FGC § 8383.5

---

## Text

It is unlawful to take, possess, sell, or purchase any white sea bass less than 28 inches in length, measured from the tip of the lower jaw to the end of the longer lobe of the tail.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1370, Sec. 24. Effective October 27, 1992.)
