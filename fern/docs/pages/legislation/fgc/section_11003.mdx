# FGC § 11003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Boundaries

**Citation:** FGC § 11003

---

## Text

The following constitutes Fish and Game District 112:

Those portions of the Counties of Del Norte, Siskiyou, Trinity, and Humboldt not included in other districts.
