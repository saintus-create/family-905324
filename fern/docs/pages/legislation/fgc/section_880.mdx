# FGC § 880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 2. Deputies, and Other Employees; County Wardens > Article: 2. County Fish and Game Wardens

**Citation:** FGC § 880

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each county may, in its discretion, appoint a deputy fish and game warden, to serve at the pleasure of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
