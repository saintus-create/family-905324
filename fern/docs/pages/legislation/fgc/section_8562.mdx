# FGC § 8562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 16. Drift Gill Net Shark and Swordfish Fishery

**Citation:** FGC § 8562

---

## Text

Applications delivered to a department office after April 30, or if mailed, postmarked after April 30, shall not be accepted unless approved by the commission pursuant to Section 8569.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 525, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1999.)
