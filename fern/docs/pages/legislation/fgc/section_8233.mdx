# FGC § 8233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 4.5. Commercial Salmon Vessel Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 8233

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, a permit shall be renewed prior to expiration. Each permit issued by the department shall display the expiration date on the face of the permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 870, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1997.)
