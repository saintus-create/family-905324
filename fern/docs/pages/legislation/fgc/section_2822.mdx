# FGC § 2822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Natural Community Conservation Planning Act

**Citation:** FGC § 2822

---

## Text

The department may seek injunctive relief against any plan participant, person, or entity to enforce this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 4, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
