# FGC § 3452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 6. Management of Fish and Wildlife on Military Lands

**Citation:** FGC § 3452

---

## Text

The commission may adopt regulations and authorize the department to enter into agreements with the United States Department of Defense for the administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 591, Sec. 1.)
