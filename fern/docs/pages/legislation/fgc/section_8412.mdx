# FGC § 8412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 9.5. Pacific Mackerel

**Citation:** FGC § 8412

---

## Text

Pacific mackerel may be taken under a revocable nontransferable permit issued by the department to boat owners or operators under conditions prescribed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 388, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2001.)
