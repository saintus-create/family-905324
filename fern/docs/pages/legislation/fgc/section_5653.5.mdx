# FGC § 5653.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Pollution > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** FGC § 5653.5

---

## Text

For purposes of Section 5653, “river, stream, or lake” means the body of water at the current water level at the time of the dredging.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1037, Sec. 3.)
