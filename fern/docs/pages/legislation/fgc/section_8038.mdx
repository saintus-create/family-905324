# FGC § 8038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 7. Commercial Fish Business Licenses

**Citation:** FGC § 8038

---

## Text

A license issued under this article is valid from January 1 to December 31, inclusive, or, if issued after the beginning of that term, for the remainder thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1117, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
