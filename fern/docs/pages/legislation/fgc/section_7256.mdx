# FGC § 7256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 1. Spiny Lobster

**Citation:** FGC § 7256

---

## Text

Spiny lobster may not be taken under a sport fishing license except by use of a hoop net or by hand.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1315.)
