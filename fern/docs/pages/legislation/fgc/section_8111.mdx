# FGC § 8111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 10. Far Offshore Fishing

**Citation:** FGC § 8111

---

## Text

“Far offshore fishery” means a fishery that lies outside the United States 200-mile exclusive economic zone, as defined by paragraph (6) of Section 1802 of Title 16 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 619, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
