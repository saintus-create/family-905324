# FGC § 3311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 4. Licensed Domesticated Migratory Game Bird Shooting Areas

**Citation:** FGC § 3311

---

## Text

The licensee shall comply with all applicable federal laws or regulations relating to the releasing and shooting of domesticated migratory game birds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1312.)
