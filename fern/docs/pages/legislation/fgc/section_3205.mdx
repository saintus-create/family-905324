# FGC § 3205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 1. Domesticated Game Breeding

**Citation:** FGC § 3205

---

## Text

Carriers for hire may carry within the State live domesticated game birds and mammals upon such terms and conditions as the commission may prescribe.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
