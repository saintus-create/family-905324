# FGC § 3472.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 8. Management of Wildlife at Public Use Airports

**Citation:** FGC § 3472.1

---

## Text

Take is authorized pursuant to this article only to relieve or prevent injurious situations affecting public safety and shall only be performed as part of an integrated wildlife management program that emphasizes nonlethal management techniques.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 186, Sec. 1. (SB 481) Effective January 1, 2010.)
