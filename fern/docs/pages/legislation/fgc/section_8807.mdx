# FGC § 8807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 9. Beach Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8807

---

## Text

In District 19, beach nets, the meshes of which are at least 112 inches in length, may be used to take smelt between September 1st and January 31st.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
