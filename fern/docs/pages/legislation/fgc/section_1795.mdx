# FGC § 1795

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 7.8. Sacramento-San Joaquin Valley Wetlands Mitigation Bank Act of 1993 > Article: 5. Discharge into Wetlands

**Citation:** FGC § 1795

---

## Text

After payment to the operator pursuant to this article, the permittee has no further obligations with respect to the operation of the bank site to which payment was made, unless the permittee has an equity involvement in the bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1254, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
