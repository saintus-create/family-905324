# FGC § 8599.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 20. White Sharks and Basking Sharks

**Citation:** FGC § 8599.3

---

## Text

The department shall cooperate, to the extent that it determines feasible, with appropriate scientific institutions to facilitate data collection on white sharks taken incidentally by commercial fishing operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1174, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
