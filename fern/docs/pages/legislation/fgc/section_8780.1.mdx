# FGC § 8780.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Nets > Article: 8. Bait Nets

**Citation:** FGC § 8780.1

---

## Text

The commission may, upon the recommendation of the department, adopt regulations governing the use of bait nets.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 483, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2000.)
