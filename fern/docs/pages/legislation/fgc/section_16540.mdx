# FGC § 16540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13.5. STATE-TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN FISHING ON THE KLAMATH RIVER > Chapter: 4. Enforceability of an Agreement or Compact

**Citation:** FGC § 16540

---

## Text

Any agreement or compact entered into pursuant to this division shall be enforceable by the parties only to the extent and in the form or forms provided for under the terms of the agreement or compact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1186, Sec. 1.)
