# FGC § 97.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Marine Life Definitions

**Citation:** FGC § 97.5

---

## Text

“Overfished,” with regard to a marine fishery, means both of the following:

(a) A depressed fishery.

(b) A reduction of take in the fishery is the principal means for rebuilding the population.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1052, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
