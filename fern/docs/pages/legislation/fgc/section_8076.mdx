# FGC § 8076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 1. Generally > Article: 8. Reduction Permits

**Citation:** FGC § 8076

---

## Text

No reduction of fish shall be permitted which may tend to deplete the species, or result in waste or deterioration of fish.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
