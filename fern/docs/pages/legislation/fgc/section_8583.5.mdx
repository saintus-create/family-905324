# FGC § 8583.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 2. Particular Varieties of Fish > Article: 16. Drift Gill Net Shark and Swordfish Fishery

**Citation:** FGC § 8583.5

---

## Text

This article does not create or recognize a property right in fish expected to be caught using a permit issued pursuant to Section 8561.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 844, Sec. 8. (SB 1017) Effective January 1, 2019.)
