# FGC § 2089.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FISH AND GAME GENERALLY > Chapter: 1.5. Endangered Species > Article: 3.7. California State Safe Harbor Agreement Program Act

**Citation:** FGC § 2089.24

---

## Text

The department, for informational purposes, shall maintain a list of qualified persons who have worked with the department on an approved agreement, and persons, entities, and organizations serving as program administrators for approved agreements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 184, Sec. 1. (SB 448) Effective January 1, 2010.)
