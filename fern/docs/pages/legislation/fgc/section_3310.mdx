# FGC § 3310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BIRDS AND MAMMALS > Chapter: 2. Commercial Activities > Article: 4. Licensed Domesticated Migratory Game Bird Shooting Areas

**Citation:** FGC § 3310

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person to shoot domesticated migratory game birds on a licensed area without having a valid hunting license as provided by Section 3031.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1312.)
