# FGC § 1123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. DEPARTMENT OF FISH AND WILDLIFE > Chapter: 3. Other Powers and Duties > Article: 3. State Fish Hatcheries

**Citation:** FGC § 1123

---

## Text

The department may purchase and import spawn or ova of fish suitable for food, and stock with such spawn or ova the waters of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
