# FGC § 5930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. FISH > Chapter: 3. Dams, Conduits, and Screens > Article: 2. Dams and Obstructions

**Citation:** FGC § 5930

---

## Text

The department shall, from time to time, examine all dams in all rivers and streams in this State naturally frequented by fish.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1957, Ch. 456.)
