# HSC § 52514.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 6. CALIFORNIA FIRST-TIME HOME BUYERS > Chapter: 2. Buy-Down Mortgage Plan

**Citation:** HSC § 52514.5

---

## Text

All payments to the agency by the borrower on any note executed pursuant to Section 52514 shall be considered payments of interest for the purposes of Section 17230 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 987, Sec. 1. Effective October 10, 1999.)
