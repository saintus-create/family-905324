# HSC § 8331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 4. Records

**Citation:** HSC § 8331

---

## Text

A record shall be kept of the ownership of all plots in the cemetery which have been conveyed by the cemetery authority and of all transfers of plots in the cemetery. Transfer of any plot, heretofore or hereafter made, or any right of interment, shall be complete and effective when recorded on the books of the cemetery authority.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 176, Sec. 1.)
