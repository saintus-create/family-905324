# HSC § 1474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. County Medical Facilities > Article: 2. Liability for Cost of Care

**Citation:** HSC § 1474

---

## Text

In collecting charges for care rendered under this chapter, the board may exercise all powers provided in Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 17400) of Part 5 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, as enacted and as it may be amended thereafter.
