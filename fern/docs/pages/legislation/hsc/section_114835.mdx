# HSC § 114835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 5. Containment of Radioactive Materials > Article: 3. Transportation of Radioactive Materials

## Text

A violation of any regulation adopted by the department pursuant to Section 114820 is a misdemeanor.
