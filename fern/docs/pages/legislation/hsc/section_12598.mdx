# HSC § 12598

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. Issuance, Revocation and Renewal

**Citation:** HSC § 12598

---

## Text

Every licensee who fails to renew his or her license by the time the license expires shall surrender the license to the State Fire Marshal within 10 days after the license expires.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 622, Sec. 2.)
