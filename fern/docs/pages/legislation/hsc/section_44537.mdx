# HSC § 44537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 44537

---

## Text

The authority shall take all reasonable steps to publicize its programs so that eligible applicants may be aware of them.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 643, Sec. 19. (SB 832) Effective November 2, 2009.)
