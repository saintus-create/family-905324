# HSC § 101405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 4. Additional Administrative Provisions > Article: 3. Contracts for Local Health Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 101405

---

## Text

Whenever a contract has been duly entered into, the county health officer and his or her deputies shall exercise the same powers and duties in the city as are conferred upon city health officers by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 303. Effective September 29, 1996.)
