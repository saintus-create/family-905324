# HSC § 129840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 129840

---

## Text

Subsequent to the occurrence of any earthquake, the department may make, or cause to be made, studies of health facilities within the area involved.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 300. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
