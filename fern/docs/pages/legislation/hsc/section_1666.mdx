# HSC § 1666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Regulation of Use of Animals in Diagnostic Procedures and Medical Research > Article: 3. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** HSC § 1666

---

## Text

No person shall keep or use animals for diagnostic purposes, education or research unless approved by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1750.)
