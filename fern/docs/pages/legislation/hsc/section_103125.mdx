# HSC § 103125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 9. Marriage Registration > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 103125

---

## Text

The forms for the marriage license shall be prescribed by the State Registrar.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 816, Sec. 45. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative January 1, 2008, by Sec. 56 of Ch. 816.)
