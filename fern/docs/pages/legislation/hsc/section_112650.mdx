# HSC § 112650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 8. Canneries > Article: 1. Definitions and Scope

**Citation:** HSC § 112650

---

## Text

“State board,” or “State Board of Public Health,” as used in this chapter, means the State Department of Health Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
