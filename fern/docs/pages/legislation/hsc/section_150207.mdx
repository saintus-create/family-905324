# HSC § 150207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 116. SURPLUS MEDICATION COLLECTION AND DISTRIBUTION

**Citation:** HSC § 150207

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall affect disciplinary actions taken by licensing and regulatory agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 444, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
