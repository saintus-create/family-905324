# HSC § 25159.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 5.6. The Toxic Injection Well Control Act of 1985
