# HSC § 51641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 51641

---

## Text

The agency may create other accounts within the insurance fund as it deems are necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993.)
