# HSC § 131105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Powers of the Department > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 131105

---

## Text

The department shall examine and may prevent the pollution of sources of public domestic water and ice supply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 100205 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 241, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 241.)
