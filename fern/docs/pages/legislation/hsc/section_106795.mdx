# HSC § 106795

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 1. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERSONNEL > Chapter: 4. Professional Certification > Article: 2. Radon Certification

**Citation:** HSC § 106795

---

## Text

It is unlawful for an individual to provide radon services in violation of this article. A violation of this article is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine of not more than one thousand dollars ($1,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 327, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
