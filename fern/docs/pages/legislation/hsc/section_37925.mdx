# HSC § 37925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 13. RESIDENTIAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 37925

---

## Text

Any action challenging the legality of a comprehensive residential rehabilitation financing program, the selection of a residential rehabilitation area, the selection of residences for residential rehabilitation pursuant to Section 37922.1, or the adoption of a plan for public improvements for a residential rehabilitation area, shall be commenced within 60 days of the adoption of such program, selection of such area or residences, or adoption of such plan for public improvements.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 558.)
