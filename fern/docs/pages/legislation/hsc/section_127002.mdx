# HSC § 127002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 1. DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 127002

---

## Text

Any reference to the Office of Statewide Health Planning and Development shall be deemed a reference to the Department of Health Care Access and Information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 31. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
