# HSC § 110957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110957

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for a person to represent in advertising or labeling that the person or the products of the person are registered pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 2003.)
