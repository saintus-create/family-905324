# HSC § 40365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 4. Advisory Council

**Citation:** HSC § 40365

---

## Text

The council shall meet as frequently as the regional district board or the council deem necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
