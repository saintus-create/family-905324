# HSC § 50712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 8.5. Special Housing Program for Migratory Workers

**Citation:** HSC § 50712

---

## Text

Any county, city, or other local agency may enter into contracts of the nature described in Section 50710, to the extent that such an agency is otherwise authorized by law to engage in the activity which it contracts to undertake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1165.)
