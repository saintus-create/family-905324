# HSC § 43001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 43001

---

## Text

The provisions of this part shall not apply to:

(a) Racing vehicles.

(b) Motorcycles, except as otherwise provided in Section 43107.

This section shall become operative on January 1, 1989.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 467, Sec. 2) by Stats. 1984, Ch. 233, Sec. 2. Section operative January 1, 1989, by its own provisions.)
