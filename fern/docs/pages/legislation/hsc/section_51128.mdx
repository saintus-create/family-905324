# HSC § 51128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 5. Financing of Housing Developments and Residential Structures > Article: 2. Loans Through Intermediary Lenders and Mortgage Purchase and Sale

**Citation:** HSC § 51128

---

## Text

Sales of mortgage obligations and securities pursuant to this article may be made at public or private sale, with or without public bidding, whether directly or through a contract with a private marketing intermediary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
