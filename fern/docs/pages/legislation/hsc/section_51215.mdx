# HSC § 51215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 5. Financing of Housing Developments and Residential Structures > Article: 5.7. Elderly Congregate Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51215

---

## Text

The agency shall make loans to finance the construction or rehabilitation of congregate housing for the elderly in an aggregate amount of fifty million dollars ($50,000,000). The loans shall be made from the proceeds of bonds authorized pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 51350.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1249, Sec. 3.)
