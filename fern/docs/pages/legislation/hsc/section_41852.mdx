# HSC § 41852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 3. Agricultural Burning

**Citation:** HSC § 41852

---

## Text

No person knowingly shall set or permit agricultural burning unless he has a valid permit from the agency designated by the state board to issue such permits in the area where the agricultural burning is to take place.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1063.)
