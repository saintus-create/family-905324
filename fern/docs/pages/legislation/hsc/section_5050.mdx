# HSC § 5050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 7. Rates and Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 5050

---

## Text

The rates for any class of users or property served may be extended to cover any additional premises thereafter served which fall within the same class, without the necessity of hearing or notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
