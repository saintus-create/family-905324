# HSC § 117425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 13. GARBAGE AND ONSITE SEWAGE DISPOSAL > Chapter: 4. Waste and Waste Disposal > Article: 1. Septic Tanks

**Citation:** HSC § 117425

---

## Text

The health officer is required to act upon each application within thirty (30) days of the date of filing same.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
