# HSC § 50610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5.6. Stable Affordable Housing Act of 2023

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Stable Affordable Housing Act of 2023.

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 402, Sec. 1. (SB 555) Effective January 1, 2024.)
