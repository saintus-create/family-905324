# HSC § 18003.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18003.3

---

## Text

“Dwelling unit” means one or more habitable rooms which are designed to be occupied by one family with facilities for living, sleeping, cooking, eating, and sanitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 975, Sec. 3.)
