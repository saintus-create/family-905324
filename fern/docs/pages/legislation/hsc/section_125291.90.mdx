# HSC § 125291.90

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Stem Cell Research, Treatments, and Cures Bond Act of 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 2020, by initiative Proposition 14, Sec. 24. Effective on December 16, 2020.)
