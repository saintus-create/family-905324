# HSC § 113876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113876

---

## Text

“Prepackaged food” means any properly labeled processed food, prepackaged to prevent any direct human contact with the food product upon distribution from the manufacturer, a food facility, or other approved source.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 14. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
