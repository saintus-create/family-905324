# HSC § 1266.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1266.7

---

## Text

The annual Licensing and Certification Program fee for a congregate living health facility shall be set in accordance with Section 1266.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 74, Sec. 8. Effective July 12, 2006.)
