# HSC § 1221.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 2.5. Licensing of Clinics

**Citation:** HSC § 1221.13

---

## Text

All new applications submitted to the centralized applications unit shall be reviewed within two weeks for completeness. The centralized applications unit shall resolve minor issues directly with the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 525, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
