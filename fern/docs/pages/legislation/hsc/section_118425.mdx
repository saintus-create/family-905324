# HSC § 118425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 1. Articles of Common Use > Article: 3. Common Towels

**Citation:** HSC § 118425

---

## Text

No person conducting, operating, or having charge or control of, any hotel, restaurant, factory, store, barber shop, office building, school, public hall, railroad train, railway station, boat, or any other public place, room, or conveyance, shall maintain or keep in or about any such place any towel for common use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
