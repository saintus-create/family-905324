# HSC § 7150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Uniform Anatomical Gift Act

**Citation:** HSC § 7150

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Uniform Anatomical Gift Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 629, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
