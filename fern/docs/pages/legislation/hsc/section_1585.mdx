# HSC § 1585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 5. Additional Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 1585

---

## Text

No member of the governing board of an adult day health center, nor any member of the immediate family of that board member, may have any direct or indirect interest in any contract for supplying services to the adult day health center.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 7, Sec. 11. Effective May 5, 2003.)
