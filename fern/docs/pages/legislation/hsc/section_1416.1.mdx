# HSC § 1416.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.35. Nursing Home Administrator Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1416.1

---

## Text

There is hereby established in the State Department of Health Services a Nursing Home Administrator Program (NHAP), which shall license and regulate nursing home administrators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 687, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
