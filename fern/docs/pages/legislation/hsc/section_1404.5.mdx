# HSC § 1404.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Referral Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1404.5

---

## Text

A license application shall be submitted to the department whenever any of the following circumstances occur:

(a) Change of ownership of the referral agency.

(b) Change of name of the referral agency.

(c) Change of location of the referral agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 700, Sec. 10.)
