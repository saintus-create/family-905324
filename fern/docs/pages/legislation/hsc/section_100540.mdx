# HSC § 100540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Additional Administrative Provisions > Article: 7. Contract Uniformity

**Citation:** HSC § 100540

---

## Text

The department shall take the steps necessary to achieve uniformity among contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
