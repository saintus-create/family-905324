# HSC § 33796

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 4. Residential Construction

**Citation:** HSC § 33796

---

## Text

If the jurisdiction of the agency to order a proposed act is not affected, an omission of any officer or the agency in proceedings under this chapter or any other defect in the proceedings shall not invalidate the proceedings or revenue bonds issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 48.)
