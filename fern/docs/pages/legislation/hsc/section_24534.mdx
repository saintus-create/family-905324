# HSC § 24534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4.8. Bunk Beds

**Citation:** HSC § 24534

---

## Text

Any commercial user who willfully and knowingly violates Section 24533 is guilty of an infraction and shall be punished by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 6, Sec. 3. Effective March 28, 2000.)
