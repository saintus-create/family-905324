# HSC § 116155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 3. Rodent Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 116155

---

## Text

All sums so paid by the county or city are a lien on the property on which the work was done, and may be recovered in an action against the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
