# HSC § 33605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 33605

---

## Text

In connection with the issuance and sale of preliminary loan notes, secured by a requisition agreement with the United States of America, the agency may delegate to one or more of its agents or employees the powers or duties it deems proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 33604 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1242.)
