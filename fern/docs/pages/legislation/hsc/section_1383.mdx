# HSC § 1383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 6. Operation and Renewal Requirements and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 1383

---

## Text

Every plan that is a health maintenance organization qualified under Section 1310(d) of Title XIII of the federal Public Health Service Act, shall provide the department with a copy of the reports the plan files annually with the United States Department of Health, Education, and Welfare pursuant to Title XIII of the federal Public Health Service Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1083.)
