# HSC § 1336.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 8.5. Long-Term Care Facility Advance Notification Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 1336.4

---

## Text

Failure to comply with the requirements in Sections 1336 to 1336.3, inclusive, shall be subject to issuance of citations and imposition of civil penalties pursuant to Chapter 2.4 (commencing with Section 1417), and Sections 72701 and following of the California Administrative Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1251, Sec. 3.)
