# HSC § 116092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 6. Pilot Project for School Athletics Safety

**Citation:** HSC § 116092

---

## Text

(a) One of the pilot projects shall be in southern California.

(b) One of the pilot projects shall be in northern California.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 553, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
