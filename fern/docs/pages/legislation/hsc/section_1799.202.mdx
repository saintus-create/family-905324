# HSC § 1799.202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 12. Emergency Medical Services System for Children

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Emergency Medical Services for Children Act of 1996.

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 197, Sec. 3. Effective July 22, 1996.)
