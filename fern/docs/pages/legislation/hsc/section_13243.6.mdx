# HSC § 13243.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Propane Storage and Handling > Article: 1. General and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 13243.6

---

## Text

Any costs incurred by state agencies or departments pursuant to this article, that are not funded through fees pursuant to Section 13244.5, shall be funded from existing resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1293, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
