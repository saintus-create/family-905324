# HSC § 113700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 113700

---

## Text

These provisions shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Retail Food Code, hereafter referred to as “this part.”

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
