# HSC § 18211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18211

## Text

“Mobilehome” as used in this part shall have the same meaning as defined in Section 18008.
