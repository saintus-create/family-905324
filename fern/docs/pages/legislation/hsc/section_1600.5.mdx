# HSC § 1600.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Human Whole Blood, Human Whole Blood Derivatives, and Other Biologics > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1600.5

---

## Text

“Production” includes collection, preparation, testing, processing, storage, and distribution of biologics under a license issued by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1055.)
