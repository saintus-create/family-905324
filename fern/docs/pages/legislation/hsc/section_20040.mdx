# HSC § 20040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 2.5. Election on Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 20040

---

## Text

The board shall call and give notice of an election to be held in the proposed district to determine whether the district shall be formed, and to elect the commissioners who shall serve as the first district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 896.)
