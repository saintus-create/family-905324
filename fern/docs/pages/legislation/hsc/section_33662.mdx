# HSC § 33662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Agency Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 33662

---

## Text

The bonds are issued for an essential public and governmental purpose, and together with interest on them and income from them are exempt from all taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
