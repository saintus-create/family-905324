# HSC § 104562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 1.5. Comprehensive Perinatal Outreach Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104562

---

## Text

A county that contracts with the state department for the provision of public health services may contract with the state department for the services described in Section 104561.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 349.102 (as added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 194) by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 126. Effective September 29, 1996.)
