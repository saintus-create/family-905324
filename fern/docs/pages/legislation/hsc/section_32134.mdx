# HSC § 32134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 32134

---

## Text

The effective date of any contract entered into for the construction and leasing of any hospital building or facilities shall be the date of execution of said lease notwithstanding the fact that said lease may be later amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 539.)
