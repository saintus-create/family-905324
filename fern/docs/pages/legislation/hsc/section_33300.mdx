# HSC § 33300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 1. Community Prerequisites

**Citation:** HSC § 33300

---

## Text

Before any area is designated for redevelopment, the community authorized to undertake such development shall comply with the requirements of this article.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
