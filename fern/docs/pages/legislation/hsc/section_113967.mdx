# HSC § 113967

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 3. Management and Personnel > Article: 5. Personal Cleanliness

**Citation:** HSC § 113967

---

## Text

No employee shall commit any act that may cause the contamination or adulteration of food, food-contact surfaces, or utensils.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 17. Effective July 20, 2007.)
