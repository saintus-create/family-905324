# HSC § 40120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. County Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 40120

---

## Text

All county officers and employees shall be ex officio officers and employees, respectively, of the county district in the county by which they are employed.

Except as otherwise provided in this division, they shall perform, without additional compensation, for the county district such duties as they perform for the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
