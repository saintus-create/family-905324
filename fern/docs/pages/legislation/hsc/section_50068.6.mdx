# HSC § 50068.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50068.6

---

## Text

“First-Time Home Buyers Fund” is a fund in the General Fund, distinguished from the California Housing Finance Fund, which is created by Section 52504.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 320, Sec. 5. Effective June 29, 1982.)
