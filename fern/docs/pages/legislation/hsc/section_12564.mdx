# HSC § 12564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 4. Classification of Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 12564

---

## Text

All fireworks examined by the State Fire Marshal and determined by him to come within the definition of “exempt fireworks” in Section 12508 shall be classified as exempt fireworks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
