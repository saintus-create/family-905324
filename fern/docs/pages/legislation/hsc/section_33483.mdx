# HSC § 33483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 15. Merger of Redevelopment Project Areas in the Cities of Chula Vista, San Jose, and Santa Fe Springs

**Citation:** HSC § 33483

---

## Text

Not less than 20 percent of all taxes which are allocated to the redevelopment agency pursuant to Section 33670 for redevelopment projects merged pursuant to this article, irrespective of the date of adoption of the final redevelopment plans, shall be used for the purposes set forth in Section 33334.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 243.)
