# HSC § 101565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Monterey County Special Health Care Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 101565

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the board of directors of the authority, members of its community advisory board, members of its professional advisory board, and members of committees of those boards, shall be deemed members of a peer review committee within the meaning of Section 43.7 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 303.5. Effective September 29, 1996.)
