# HSC § 13178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 3. Certificates of Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 13178

---

## Text

Following receipt of the properly completed application and prescribed fee, and the completion of the required examination, the State Fire Marshal shall issue a certificate of registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 802.)
