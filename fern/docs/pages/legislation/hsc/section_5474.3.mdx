# HSC § 5474.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 4. Sanitation and Sewerage Systems

**Citation:** HSC § 5474.3

---

## Text

Upon the conclusion of the hearing, the legislative body may adopt, revise, change, reduce or modify the fees or charges or may overrule any or all objections and make its determination, which determination shall be final.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 578.)
