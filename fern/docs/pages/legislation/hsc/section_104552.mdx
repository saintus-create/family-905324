# HSC § 104552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 1. Tobacco Control > Article: 3. Cigar Labeling

**Citation:** HSC § 104552

---

## Text

To the extent this article conflicts with any federal provision enacted subsequent to the effective date of this article that requires cigar manufacturers and importers to provide warning labels on cigars, those federal provisions shall supersede the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 693, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
