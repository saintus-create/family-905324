# HSC § 11240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 5. Use of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Veterinarians

**Citation:** HSC § 11240

---

## Text

No veterinarian shall prescribe, administer, or furnish a controlled substance for himself or any other human being.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
