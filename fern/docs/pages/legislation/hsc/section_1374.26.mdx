# HSC § 1374.26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 5.5. Health Care Service Plan Coverage Contract Changes

**Citation:** HSC § 1374.26

---

## Text

The director may, as required by this article, or from time to time as conditions warrant, pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, adopt reasonable regulations, and amendments and additions thereto, as are necessary to administer this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 525, Sec. 112. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, or sooner, by Sec. 214 of Ch. 525.)
