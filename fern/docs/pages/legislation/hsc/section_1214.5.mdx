# HSC § 1214.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1214.5

---

## Text

Each application under this chapter for an initial license, renewal license, license upon change of ownership, or special permit for a psychology clinic shall be accompanied by a Licensing and Certification Program fee set in accordance with Section 1266.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 74, Sec. 4. Effective July 12, 2006.)
