# HSC § 11572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 11572

---

## Text

Unless filed by the district attorney, or the city attorney of an incorporated city, the complaint in the action shall be verified.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1076, Sec. 3.)
