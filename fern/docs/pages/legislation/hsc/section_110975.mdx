# HSC § 110975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 10. Ice

**Citation:** HSC § 110975

---

## Text

The following definitions apply to this article:

(a) “Ice” means the product obtained as the result of freezing water by natural, mechanical, or artificial means.

(b) “Natural ice” means the product obtained as the result of freezing water by natural means.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
