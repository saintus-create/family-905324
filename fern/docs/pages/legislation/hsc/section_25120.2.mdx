# HSC § 25120.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25120.2

---

## Text

“RCRA hazardous waste” means all waste identified as a hazardous waste in Part 261 (commencing with Section 261.1) of Subchapter I of Chapter 1 of Title 40 of the Code of Federal Regulations and appendixes thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1436, Sec. 5. Effective October 2, 1989.)
