# HSC § 105225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 5. ENVIRONMENTAL AND OCCUPATIONAL EPIDEMIOLOGY > Chapter: 3. Pesticide Poisoning

**Citation:** HSC § 105225

---

## Text

Each public employer of a public employee subject to Section 105215 shall post in one or more prominent places frequented by such employee a notice informing such employee of the responsibility imposed by Section 105215.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
