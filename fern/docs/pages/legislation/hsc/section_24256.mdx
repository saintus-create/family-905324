# HSC § 24256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Patient Access and Protection

**Citation:** HSC § 24256

---

## Text

Health care provider entities shall have 45 days from the effective date of this chapter to comply with the requirements contained herein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 123, Sec. 3. (SB 81) Effective September 20, 2025.)
