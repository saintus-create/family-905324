# HSC § 121605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 1. Rabies Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121605

---

## Text

Following the order of quarantine the department shall make or cause to be made a thorough investigation as to the extent of the disease, the probable number of persons and animals exposed, and the area found to be involved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
