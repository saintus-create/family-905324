# HSC § 12305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 1. HIGH EXPLOSIVES > Chapter: 7. Illegal Use or Possession

**Citation:** HSC § 12305

---

## Text

Every person not in the lawful possession of an explosive who knowingly has any explosive in his possession is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1497.)
