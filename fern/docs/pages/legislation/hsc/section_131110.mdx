# HSC § 131110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Powers of the Department > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 131110

---

## Text

(a) The department shall maintain a program of Environmental Management.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 126) and added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 127. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, by its own provisions.)
