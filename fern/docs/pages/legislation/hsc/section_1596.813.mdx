# HSC § 1596.813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.813

---

## Text

The department shall adopt regulations regarding immunization requirements for children enrolled in family day care homes in accordance with Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 120325) of Part 2 of Division 105.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 166. Effective September 29, 1996.)
