# HSC § 6701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6701

---

## Text

Taxes for the payment of the principal and interest of bonds of annexed territory shall be limited to the taxable property in the annexed territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1944, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 53.)
