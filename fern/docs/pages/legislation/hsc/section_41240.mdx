# HSC § 41240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41240

---

## Text

The Mojave Desert district may adopt a schedule of fees, levied on permitted and other sources of air pollution to recover its costs of implementing this chapter, pursuant to Section 42311 and Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 44220) of Part 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 642, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
