# HSC § 4783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 4783

---

## Text

An election board consisting of one inspector, one judge, and one clerk shall be appointed by the district board for each precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
