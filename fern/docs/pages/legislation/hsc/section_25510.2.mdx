# HSC § 25510.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.95. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory > Article: 1. Business and Area Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 25510.2

---

## Text

In order to carry out the purposes of this chapter, a unified program agency may train for, and respond to, the release, or threatened release, of a hazardous material.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 419, Sec. 3. (SB 483) Effective January 1, 2014.)
