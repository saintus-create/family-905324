# HSC § 103925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 2. POPULATION AND PUBLIC HEALTH SURVEILLANCE > Chapter: 4. Reyes Syndrome

**Citation:** HSC § 103925

---

## Text

(a) The Legislature finds and declares the following:

(1) The cause and the cure of Reyes Syndrome are unknown, and the number of incidences of this disease is also unknown.

(2) Where the scope of a medical problem is unknown, the amount of research funds is usually small. Therefore, it is necessary to define the extent of this medical problem.

(b) A physician and surgeon attending a patient diagnosed as having Reyes Syndrome shall report that condition, within seven days of the diagnosis, to the department on report forms prescribed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
