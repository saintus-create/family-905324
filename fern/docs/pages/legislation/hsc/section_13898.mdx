# HSC § 13898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 7. Finance

**Citation:** HSC § 13898

---

## Text

A district may accept any revenue, money, grants, goods, or services from any federal, state, regional, or local agency or from any person for any lawful purpose of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
