# HSC § 39627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3.2. Goods Movement Emission Reduction Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39627

---

## Text

The state board may seek reimbursement for program administration costs annually through an appropriation in the Budget Act from funds available pursuant to paragraph (2) of subdivision (c) of Section 8879.23 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 181, Sec. 2. Effective August 24, 2007.)
