# HSC § 118230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 8. Treatment

**Citation:** HSC § 118230

---

## Text

An operator of a hazardous waste incinerator permitted pursuant to Section 25200 may also accept medical waste for incineration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
