# HSC § 34189.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.85. DISSOLUTION OF REDEVELOPMENT AGENCIES AND DESIGNATION OF SUCCESSOR AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Effect of the Act Adding this Part on the Community Redevelopment Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34189.3

---

## Text

An action contesting any act taken or determinations or decisions made pursuant to this part or Part 1.8 (commencing with Section 34161) may be brought in superior court and shall be filed in the County of Sacramento.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 26, Sec. 34. (AB 1484) Effective June 27, 2012.)
