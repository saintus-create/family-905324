# HSC § 39664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3.5. Toxic Air Contaminants > Article: 3. Identification of Toxic Air Contaminants

**Citation:** HSC § 39664

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Services shall conduct an epidemiological study, over a period of up to 10 years, of possible long-term health effects related to the aerial application of pesticides in urban areas, including, but not limited to, cancer, birth defects, and respiratory illnesses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1678, Sec. 6.)
