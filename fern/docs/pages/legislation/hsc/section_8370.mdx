# HSC § 8370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 8. Hydrolysis Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 8370

---

## Text

All hydrolyzed human remains not disposed of in accordance with this chapter, within one year, shall be interred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 69. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, pursuant to Sec. 8382.)
