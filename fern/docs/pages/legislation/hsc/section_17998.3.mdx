# HSC § 17998.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 8. Code Enforcement Incentive Program

**Citation:** HSC § 17998.3

---

## Text

In implementing the programs governed by this chapter, the department has all the general powers granted to it by Division 31 (commencing with Section 50000).

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 664, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
