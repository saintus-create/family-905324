# HSC § 100110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Organization of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 100110

---

## Text

The director shall have the powers of a head of the department pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11150) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
