# HSC § 1798.104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. Facilities > Article: 1. Base Hospitals

**Citation:** HSC § 1798.104

---

## Text

The base hospital shall provide, or cause to be provided, EMS prehospital personnel training and continuing education in accordance with local EMS policies and procedures.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1391, Sec. 18.)
