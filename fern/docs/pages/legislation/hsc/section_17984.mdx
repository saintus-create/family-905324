# HSC § 17984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 17984

---

## Text

Neither an enforcement agency, any of its officers, nor any city or county for which an enforcement agency may act, is liable for costs in any action or proceeding that the enforcement agency may commence pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1844.)
