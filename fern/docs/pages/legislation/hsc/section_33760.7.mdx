# HSC § 33760.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 33760.7

---

## Text

The same notice requirements as specified in Section 65863.10 of the Government Code shall apply to multifamily rental housing that receives financial assistance pursuant to Section 33760 or 33760.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1563, Sec. 2.)
