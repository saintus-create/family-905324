# HSC § 118315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 9. Containment and Storage

**Citation:** HSC § 118315

---

## Text

A person shall not use a trash chute to transfer medical waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
