# HSC § 103443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 11. Amendment of Records > Article: 7. Revision of Birth and Marriage Records to Reflect Change of Gender and Sex Identifier

**Citation:** HSC § 103443

---

## Text

(a) The State Registrar shall transmit a certified copy of a birth certificate newly established under this article to the registrant without additional charge.

(b) The county clerk shall transmit a copy of a confidential marriage certificate newly established under this article to the registrant without additional charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 577, Sec. 8. (AB 218) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Section 103445.)
