# HSC § 34071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.6. NATURAL DISASTERS > Chapter: 2. Natural Disaster Emergency Shelter Program

**Citation:** HSC § 34071

---

## Text

“Eligible recipient” as used in this chapter means an agency of local government or a nonprofit organization which is able to provide or contract with recognized community organizations to provide, emergency or temporary shelter for the homeless.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 19, Sec. 5. Effective March 13, 1990.)
