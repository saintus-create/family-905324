# HSC § 8732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 2. Care of Active Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8732

---

## Text

Not more than one member of the board of trustees of an endowment care fund may have a proprietary interest in the cemetery authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 729.)
