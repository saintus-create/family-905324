# HSC § 19101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 1. Scope and Application

**Citation:** HSC § 19101

---

## Text

Any city, city and county, or county may establish by ordinance construction standards higher than those established by this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
