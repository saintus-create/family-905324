# HSC § 40314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 2. City Selection Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 40314

---

## Text

Members of a city selection committee may be allowed their actual expenses incurred in the discharge of their duties pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
