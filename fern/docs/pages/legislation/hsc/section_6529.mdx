# HSC § 6529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 1.5. Inclusion in County Sanitation District

**Citation:** HSC § 6529

---

## Text

Nothing herein shall prevent any territory within a county sanitation district from being formed into or annexed to any sanitary district, and such territory shall thereafter become subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1375.)
