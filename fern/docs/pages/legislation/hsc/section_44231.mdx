# HSC § 44231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 7. District Fees to Implement the California Clean Air Act

**Citation:** HSC § 44231

---

## Text

After consulting with the Department of Motor Vehicles on the feasibility thereof, a district board may exempt from all or part of the fee any category of low-emission motor vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1705, Sec. 1.)
