# HSC § 12109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 1. HIGH EXPLOSIVES > Chapter: 4. Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 12109

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 12111, permits shall be valid for the period of time specified thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1497.)
