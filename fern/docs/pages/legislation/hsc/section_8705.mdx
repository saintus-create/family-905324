# HSC § 8705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 1. Care of Old Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8705

---

## Text

There shall be presented with the petition a plat of the cemetery and the proposed replat which shall clearly indicate the proposed changes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
