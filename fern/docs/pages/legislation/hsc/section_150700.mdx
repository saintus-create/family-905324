# HSC § 150700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 118.5. VIRTUAL HEALTH HUB FOR RURAL COMMUNITIES PILOT PROGRAM

**Citation:** HSC § 150700

---

## Text

There is the Virtual Health Hub for Rural Communities Pilot Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 311, Sec. 2. (SB 338) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally inoperative December 31, 2030, pursuant to Section 150706.)
