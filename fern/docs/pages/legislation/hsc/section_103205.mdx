# HSC § 103205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 10. Final Decrees of Dissolution of Marriage,or Legal Separation

**Citation:** HSC § 103205

---

## Text

The State Registrar shall maintain a comprehensive and continuous index of all decrees received under Section 103200.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
