# HSC § 50744.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. Rental Housing Construction Program > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50744.5

---

## Text

The same notice requirements as specified in Section 65863.10 of the Government Code shall apply to rental housing developments that receive financial assistance pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1563, Sec. 4.)
