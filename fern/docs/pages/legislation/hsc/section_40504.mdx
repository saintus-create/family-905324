# HSC § 40504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 7. Variances and Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 40504

---

## Text

The south coast district shall work with those persons granted variances to reduce emissions of air contaminants from their operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1301, Sec. 18.)
