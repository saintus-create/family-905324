# HSC § 11833.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.3. Licensing and Certification Program Funding

**Citation:** HSC § 11833.01

---

## Text

This chapter applies to all programs, facilities, or services certified pursuant to Chapter 7.1 (commencing with Section 11832) or licensed pursuant to Chapter 7.5 (commencing with Section 11834.01), or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 36. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
