# HSC § 1596.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 7. Offenses

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.5

---

## Text

The district attorney of every county shall, upon application by the state department or its authorized representative institute and conduct the prosecution of any action for violation within his county of any provisions of this chapter or of regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1066.)
