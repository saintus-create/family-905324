# HSC § 53062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 7. SOLAR AND ENERGY CONSERVATION MORTGAGE CORPORATION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 53062

---

## Text

“Structural improvements” means alterations or additions to a designated building intended to enhance energy conservation and efficiency of building space utilization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1033.)
