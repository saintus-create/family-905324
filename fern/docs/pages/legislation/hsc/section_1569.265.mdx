# HSC § 1569.265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 2.5. Resident’s Bill of Rights

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.265

---

## Text

(a) Rights and liberties set forth in this article do not diminish a resident’s constitutional rights or any other rights set forth in other state or federal laws and regulations. Persons residing in residential care facilities for the elderly shall continue to enjoy all of their civil and legal rights.

(b) The provisions of this article apply only to privately operated residential care facilities for the elderly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 702, Sec. 1. (AB 2171) Effective January 1, 2015.)
