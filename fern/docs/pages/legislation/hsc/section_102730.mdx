# HSC § 102730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Birth Following Adoption, Legitimation, Court Determination of Parentage, and Acknowledgment > Article: 2. Adjudication of Facts of Parentage

**Citation:** HSC § 102730

---

## Text

All records and information specified in this article, other than the newly issued birth certificate, shall be available only upon order of a court of record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
