# HSC § 1596.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.35. Child Care Provider Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.65

---

## Text

(a) An employment agency, as defined in Section 1812.501 of the Civil Code, that refers a child care provider to parents or guardians who are not required to be a licensed child day care facility shall not make a placement of a child care provider who is not a trustline applicant or a registered child care provider.

(b) Any violation of this section is a misdemeanor and shall be punishable by a fine of one hundred dollars ($100).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 287, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
