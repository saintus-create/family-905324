# HSC § 6528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 1.5. Inclusion in County Sanitation District

**Citation:** HSC § 6528

---

## Text

Copies of the resolutions herein mentioned, duly certified by the clerk or secretary of the respective legislative bodies, shall be filed with the county clerk, in the respective files of such sanitary district and county sanitation district, and with the county assessor, and such resolutions shall not be effective until said copies are so filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1375.)
