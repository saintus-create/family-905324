# HSC § 79310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 5. Cleanup of Hazardous Substance Releases > Article: 15. Operation and Maintenance

**Citation:** HSC § 79310

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “small business” is a business that meets the requirements set forth in subdivision (d) of Section 14837 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
