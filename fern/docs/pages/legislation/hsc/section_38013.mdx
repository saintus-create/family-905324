# HSC § 38013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. HEALTH AND WELFARE AGENCY—DIRECT SERVICE CONTRACTS REFORM ACT > Chapter: 2. Contract Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 38013

---

## Text

The Department of Finance shall review and approve contracts in accordance with the provisions of Section 10336 of the Public Contract Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1231, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1983.)
