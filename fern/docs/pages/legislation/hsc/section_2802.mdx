# HSC § 2802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 2802

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this chapter, means any pest abatement district formed pursuant to this chapter or pursuant to any law which it supersedes.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
