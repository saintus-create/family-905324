# HSC § 51476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 10. Multifamily Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51476

---

## Text

The agency shall administer a program for the preservation of multifamily housing under authority of this chapter with funds made available by the California Housing Bond Act of 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 577, Sec. 1. Effective September 5, 1990.)
