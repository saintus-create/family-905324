# HSC § 1589.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 5.5. Grants-in-Aid Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1589.5

---

## Text

State administrative costs on grants issued pursuant to this article shall not exceed 10 percent of the amount of the grants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1589 by Stats. 2000, Ch. 108, Sec. 10. Effective July 10, 2000.)
