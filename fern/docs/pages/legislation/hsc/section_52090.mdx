# HSC § 52090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 5. LOCAL HOUSING FINANCE AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Multifamily Rental Housing > Article: 2. State Assistance

**Citation:** HSC § 52090

---

## Text

This article constitutes an alternative method for cities and counties to issue bonds for making construction loans and mortgage loans for multifamily rental housing developments pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 12, Sec. 1. Effective March 11, 2002.)
