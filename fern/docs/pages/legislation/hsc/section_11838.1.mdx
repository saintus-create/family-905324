# HSC § 11838.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 9. Services to Persons Convicted for Driving While Under the Influence of Alcohol and Other Drugs

**Citation:** HSC § 11838.1

---

## Text

The department, in cooperation with the county and the Department of Motor Vehicles, shall establish uniform statewide reporting procedures and forms for the submission of any appropriate documents or information from boards of supervisors, administrators of programs, county alcohol and drug program administrators, and program participants to assure effective implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 113. Effective January 1, 2005.)
