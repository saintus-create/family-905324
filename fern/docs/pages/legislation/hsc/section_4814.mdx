# HSC § 4814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 6. Finance and Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 4814

---

## Text

The principal and interest on the bonds shall be paid by the treasurer of the county in the manner now or hereafter provided by law for the payment of principal and interest on the bonds of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
