# HSC § 50550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3.8. Workforce Housing Reward Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50550

---

## Text

There is hereby established the Workforce Housing Reward Program, to be administered by the department for the purpose of providing local assistance to cities, counties, and cities and counties that provide land use approval to housing developments affordable to very low and low-income households.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 482, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
