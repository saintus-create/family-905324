# HSC § 110275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 3. Guarantees

**Citation:** HSC § 110275

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to give a guarantee or undertaking that is false.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
