# HSC § 33804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 1. Definitions

## Text

“Persons and families of low or moderate income” has the same meaning as defined in Section 50093.
