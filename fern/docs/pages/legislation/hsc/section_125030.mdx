# HSC § 125030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5. HEREDITARY DISEASES/CONGENITAL DEFECTS > Chapter: 1. Genetic Prevention Services > Article: 3. Sickle Cell Anemia

**Citation:** HSC § 125030

---

## Text

The department may require that a test be given for sickle cell anemia pursuant to Section 125025 to any identifiable segment of the population that the department determines is susceptible to sickle cell anemia at a disproportionately higher ratio than is the balance of the population.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
