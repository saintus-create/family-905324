# HSC § 33334.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 4. Preparation and Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33334.8

---

## Text

The same notice requirements as specified in Section 65863.10 of the Government Code shall apply to multifamily rental housing that receives financial assistance pursuant to Sections 33334.2, 33334.3, and 33334.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1563, Sec. 1.)
