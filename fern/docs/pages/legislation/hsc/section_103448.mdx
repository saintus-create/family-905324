# HSC § 103448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 11. Amendment of Records > Article: 8. Revision of Birth Records to Correct Gender Errors Made by Birthing Hospitals or Local Registrars

**Citation:** HSC § 103448

---

## Text

The new birth certificate established pursuant to this article shall in no way indicate that it is not the original birth certificate of the applicant. The new birth certificate shall supplant any birth certificate previously registered for the applicant, and shall be the only birth certificate open to public inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 780, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
