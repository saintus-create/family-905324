# HSC § 55107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 32. SEISMIC SAFETY BUILDING REHABILITATION LOANS > Part: 2. BONDS

**Citation:** HSC § 55107

---

## Text

Neither the members of the legislative body of the local agency, nor any official or employee thereof, nor any other person executing the bonds shall be subject to any personal liability or accountability by reason of the issuance thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1602, Sec. 1.)
