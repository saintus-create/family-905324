# HSC § 104324.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 1. CHRONIC DISEASE > Chapter: 8. Environmental Health Surveillance System

**Citation:** HSC § 104324.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall only apply to the University of California to the extent that the Regents of the University of California make it applicable by appropriate resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 538, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
