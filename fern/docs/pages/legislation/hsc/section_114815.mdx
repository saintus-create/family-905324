# HSC § 114815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 5. Containment of Radioactive Materials > Article: 3. Transportation of Radioactive Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 114815

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article the term “radioactive materials” shall include any material or combination of materials that spontaneously emits ionizing radiation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
