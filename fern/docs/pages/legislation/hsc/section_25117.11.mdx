# HSC § 25117.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25117.11

---

## Text

“Offsite facility” means a hazardous waste facility that is not an onsite facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1121, Sec. 2.)
