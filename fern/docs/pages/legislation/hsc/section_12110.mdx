# HSC § 12110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 1. HIGH EXPLOSIVES > Chapter: 4. Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 12110

---

## Text

No permit issued under the provisions of this chapter shall be transferable.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1497.)
