# HSC § 11839.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 1. Narcotic Treatment Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.4

---

## Text

The department shall impose a civil penalty of one hundred dollars ($100) per day for a program that fails to timely submit a corrective action plan, or to timely implement any corrective action when it has been found to not be in compliance with applicable laws and regulations as required in Section 11839.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2005.)
