# HSC § 36053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7. FARM LABOR CENTER LAW

**Citation:** HSC § 36053

---

## Text

“Governing body” means the city council in the case of a city and the board of supervisors in the case of a county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1515.)
