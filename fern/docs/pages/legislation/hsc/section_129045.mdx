# HSC § 129045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 129045

---

## Text

The department shall annually report to the Legislature the financial status of the program and its insured portfolio, including the status of all borrowers in each stage of default and the department’s efforts to collect from borrowers that have defaulted on their debt service payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 231. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
