# HSC § 1339.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 11. Payers’ Bill of Rights

**Citation:** HSC § 1339.52

---

## Text

A hospital may not condition acceptance of a contract with a health care service plan or health insurer upon the health care service plan or health insurer waiving any provision of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 582, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2004.)
