# HSC § 13886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 6. Elections

**Citation:** HSC § 13886

---

## Text

A district board may require that its election of district board members be held on the same day as the statewide general election pursuant to Section 10404 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 138. Effective January 1, 1995.)
