# HSC § 112360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 6. Cold Storage > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 112360

---

## Text

“Cold stored” means the keeping of articles of food in cold storage for a period exceeding ten days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
