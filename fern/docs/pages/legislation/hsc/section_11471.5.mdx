# HSC § 11471.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 8. Seizure and Disposition

**Citation:** HSC § 11471.5

---

## Text

A peace officer making a seizure pursuant to Section 11471 shall notify the Franchise Tax Board where there is reasonable cause to believe that the value of the seized property exceeds five thousand dollars ($5,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 924, Sec. 1.5. Effective September 22, 1987.)
