# HSC § 130010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 8. New State Responsibilities For Seismic Safety in Hospitals

**Citation:** HSC § 130010

---

## Text

The department is responsible for reviewing and approving seismic evaluation reports, compliance schedules and construction documents that are developed by hospital owners, and field review of construction for work done pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 324. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
