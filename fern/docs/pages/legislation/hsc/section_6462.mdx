# HSC § 6462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 3. Election on Formation and for Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6462

---

## Text

At least 15 days prior to the election, the board of supervisors shall select one, and may select two or more, polling places in the proposed district, and shall make suitable arrangements for the election.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1337.)
