# HSC § 9507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9507

---

## Text

“Uniform Building Code” means the 1991 Edition of the Uniform Building Code, with 1992 amendments, adopted and published by the International Conference of Building Officials.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
