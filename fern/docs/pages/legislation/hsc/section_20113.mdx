# HSC § 20113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20113

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall not receive any compensation for performing duties relating to the receipt and disbursement of money collected pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
