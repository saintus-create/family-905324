# HSC § 1596.844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.844

---

## Text

The department shall acknowledge in writing within 10 days of receipt, the request of a licensee to review notices of deficiency or penalty, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 301, Sec. 5.)
