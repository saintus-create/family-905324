# HSC § 104566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 1.5. Comprehensive Perinatal Outreach Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104566

---

## Text

Funds from the Cigarette and Tobacco Products Surtax Fund may be used in combination with funds from other sources if the services provided to each person are documented and there is an auditable connection to services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 349.106 (as added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 194) by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 130. Effective September 29, 1996.)
