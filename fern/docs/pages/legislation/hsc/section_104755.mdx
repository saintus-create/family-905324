# HSC § 104755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 3. Oral Health > Article: 2. State Oral Health Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104755

---

## Text

The director shall appoint a dentist licensed in the State of California to administer the dental program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
