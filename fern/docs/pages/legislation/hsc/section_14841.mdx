# HSC § 14841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 14841

---

## Text

The chief or ranking officer of every fire company shall inquire into the cause of, and keep a record of, every fire occurring in the town.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2009.)
