# HSC § 1385.09

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 6.2. Review of Rate Increases

**Citation:** HSC § 1385.09

---

## Text

A health care service plan contract subject to Section 1385.03 or 1385.04 shall file a separate schedule documenting the cost savings associated with Section 1367.016 and the impact on rates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 862, Sec. 4. (AB 290) Effective January 1, 2020.)
