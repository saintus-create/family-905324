# HSC § 40829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** HSC § 40829

---

## Text

Any member of a hearing board may administer oaths in any hearing in which he participates as a member of the hearing board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
