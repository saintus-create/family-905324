# HSC § 101420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 4. Additional Administrative Provisions > Article: 3. Contracts for Local Health Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 101420

---

## Text

Payment for the services specified in Section 101415 in the unincorporated territory shall be made by the county to the city treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
