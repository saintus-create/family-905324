# HSC § 50424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1.5. Statewide Housing Plan

**Citation:** HSC § 50424

---

## Text

The plan developed pursuant to Section 50421 shall provide a reference guide for local housing market studies and for local housing elements required by Section 65302 of the Government Code. It is also intended to provide a framework for local housing plans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 341, Sec. 2. (AB 68) Effective January 1, 2022.)
