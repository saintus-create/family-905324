# HSC § 5016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 5016

---

## Text

If the bids are rejected or if no bids are received, the governing body may readvertise for bids as in the first instance without further proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
