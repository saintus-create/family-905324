# HSC § 104130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 1. CHRONIC DISEASE > Chapter: 1. Cardiovascular Disease

**Citation:** HSC § 104130

---

## Text

Local community high blood pressure control programs funded pursuant to this chapter shall make maximum use of third party payments and other resources to support their efforts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
