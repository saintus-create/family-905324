# HSC § 14896

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 3. Notice to Destroy Weeds > Article: 3. Posting and Publishing Notice

**Citation:** HSC § 14896

---

## Text

As an alternative to posting and publication, notice in the form required in Section 14892 may be mailed to the property owners as their names and addresses appear from the last equalized assessment role, or as they are known to the clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 579.)
