# HSC § 33142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 4. Suspension and Dissolution of Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 33142

---

## Text

The legislative body of the community shall file with the Secretary of State a certified copy of any ordinance suspending or dissolving an agency pursuant to Section 33140 or 33141.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1561.)
