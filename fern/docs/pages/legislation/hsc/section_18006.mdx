# HSC § 18006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18006

---

## Text

“Licensee” means a dealer, dealer branch, manufacturer, distributor, or salesperson licensed pursuant to this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1162, Sec. 1.)
