# HSC § 25120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25120

---

## Text

“Producer” means any person who generates a waste material.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective July 12, 1982.)
