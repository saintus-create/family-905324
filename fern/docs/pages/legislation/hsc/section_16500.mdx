# HSC § 16500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 3.5. School Buildings

**Citation:** HSC § 16500

---

## Text

The State Architect shall adopt guidelines applicable to substandard conditions of school buildings, as defined in Section 17283 of the Education Code, which guidelines shall take into consideration the unique design, use, safety needs, and construction of the school buildings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 308. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
