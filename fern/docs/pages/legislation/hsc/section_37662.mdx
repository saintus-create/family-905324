# HSC § 37662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 10. HISTORICAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 4. Rehabilitation Loans

**Citation:** HSC § 37662

---

## Text

All moneys received pursuant to the provisions of this part, whether proceeds from the sale of bonds or revenues, shall be deemed to be trust funds to be held and applied solely as provided in this part. Any bank or trust company in which such moneys are deposited shall act as trustee of such moneys and shall hold and apply the same for the purposes specified in this part, subject to the terms of the resolution authorizing the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1345.)
