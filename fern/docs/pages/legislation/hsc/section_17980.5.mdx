# HSC § 17980.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 17980.5

---

## Text

The local enforcement agency may establish and maintain a special fund for the purpose of implementing Sections 17980.1 to 17980.4, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 192, Sec. 6.)
