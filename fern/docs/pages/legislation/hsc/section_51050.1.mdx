# HSC § 51050.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 4. General Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 51050.1

---

## Text

The agency may make loans to finance affordable housing, including residential structures, housing developments, multifamily rental housing, special needs housing, and other forms of housing permitted by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 748, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
