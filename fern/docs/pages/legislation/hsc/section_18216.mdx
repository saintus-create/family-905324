# HSC § 18216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18216

---

## Text

“Rent” is money or other consideration given for the right of use, possession, and occupation of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1056.)
