# HSC § 39052.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39052.5

---

## Text

“South coast district” means the South Coast Air Quality Management District created pursuant to Chapter 5.5 (commencing with Section 40400) of Part 3.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 324.)
