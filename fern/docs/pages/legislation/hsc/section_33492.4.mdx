# HSC § 33492.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.4

---

## Text

Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 33300) shall be applicable to any project area formed pursuant to this chapter, except to the extent that Chapter 4 is inconsistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 221, Sec. 4. Effective July 22, 1996.)
