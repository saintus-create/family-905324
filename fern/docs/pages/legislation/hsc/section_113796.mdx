# HSC § 113796

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113796

---

## Text

“Gleaner” means a person who legally gathers remnants of an agricultural crop or harvests part of, or all of, an agricultural crop made available by the owner of the agricultural crop.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 580, Sec. 4. (AB 1990) Effective January 1, 2015.)
