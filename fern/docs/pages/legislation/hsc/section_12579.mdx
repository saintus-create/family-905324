# HSC § 12579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 1. Types of Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 12579

---

## Text

All licensees may transport the class of fireworks for which they hold a valid license as provided in Section 12651.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
