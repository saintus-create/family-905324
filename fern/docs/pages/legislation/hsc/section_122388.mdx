# HSC § 122388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 11. Pet Boarding Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 122388

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 7 of Article XI of the California Constitution, a city, county, or city and county may adopt ordinances that establish additional standards and requirements for a pet boarding facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 364, Sec. 1. (SB 945) Effective January 1, 2017.)
