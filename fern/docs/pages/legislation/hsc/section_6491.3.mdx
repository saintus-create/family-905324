# HSC § 6491.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6491.3

---

## Text

Ordinances of the district may be enacted in the same manner as general regulations of the board and shall have the same force and effect as such general regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 406.)
