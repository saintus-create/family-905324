# HSC § 11853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 13. Narcotic and Alcohol and Other Drug Use Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11853

---

## Text

Counties are encouraged to contract with providers for the provision of alcohol and drug services. Counties shall comply with the regulations of the department for the management of contracts with community organizations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 81. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
