# HSC § 2871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 2871

---

## Text

The county auditor shall allocate to a district its share of property tax revenue pursuant to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 95) of Part 0.5 of Division 1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 66. Effective September 7, 1984.)
