# HSC § 18948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Appeals and Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 18948

---

## Text

The responsibility for the enforcement and administration of building standards shall remain in the state or local agency specified by other provisions of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152.)
