# HSC § 7526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Consent to Removal

**Citation:** HSC § 7526

---

## Text

If the required consent can not be obtained, permission by the superior court of the county where the cemetery is situated is sufficient.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
