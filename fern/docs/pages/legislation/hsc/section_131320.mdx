# HSC § 131320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. The Office of Suicide Prevention

**Citation:** HSC § 131320

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative only if funds are appropriated in the annual Budget Act or another statute for its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 142, Sec. 2. (AB 2112) Effective January 1, 2021.)
