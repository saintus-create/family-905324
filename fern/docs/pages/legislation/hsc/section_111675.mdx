# HSC § 111675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 7. Cosmetics > Article: 2. Adulterated Cosmetics

**Citation:** HSC § 111675

---

## Text

Section 111670 shall not apply to coal tar hair dye, that is conspicuously labeled as follows:

“Caution—this product contains ingredients that may cause skin irritation on certain individuals and a preliminary test according to accompanying directions should first be made. This product must not be used for dyeing the eyelashes or eyebrows; to do so may cause blindness.”

The labeling shall also bear adequate directions for such preliminary testing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
