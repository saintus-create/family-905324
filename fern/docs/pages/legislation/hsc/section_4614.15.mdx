# HSC § 4614.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 2a. Formation of Districts in Two or More Municipal Corporations and Also in Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** HSC § 4614.15

---

## Text

This chapter does not prevent two or more cities or the board of supervisors of any county from entering into co-operation agreements for the joint acquisition, construction, or use of joint sanitary sewage facilities, flood control works, and storm water drainage systems.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 756.)
