# HSC § 11839.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 2. Narcotic Treatment Program Body Fluids Testing

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.27

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Services shall annually publish a list of approved and licensed laboratories engaging in the performance of tests referred to in Section 11839.24.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2005.)
