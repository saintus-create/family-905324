# HSC § 105180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 5. ENVIRONMENTAL AND OCCUPATIONAL EPIDEMIOLOGY > Chapter: 2. Occupational Health and Disease Prevention > Article: 1. Occupational Health and Disease Prevention Program

**Citation:** HSC § 105180

---

## Text

In any situation where these activities may duplicate or overlap the activities of another state department or agency such as the Department of Industrial Relations or Division of Industrial Safety, the department shall avoid duplication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
