# HSC § 40327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40327

---

## Text

A majority of the members, or the members with a majority of the voting weight, of a regional district board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business and may act for the regional district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
