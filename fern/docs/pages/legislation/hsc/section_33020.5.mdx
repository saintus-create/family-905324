# HSC § 33020.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Redevelopment

**Citation:** HSC § 33020.5

---

## Text

In addition to Section 33020, “redevelopment” also means payments to school districts in the fiscal years specified in Sections 33690 and 33690.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 21, Sec. 2. (AB 26 4x) Effective October 23, 2009.)
