## Text

This chapter shall become operative on September 1, 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 887, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (Sept. 1, 2004) for Chapter 6, commencing with Section 122320.)
