# HSC § 131020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Organization of the State Department of Public Health

**Citation:** HSC § 131020

---

## Text

All officers or employees of the department employed after July 1, 2007, shall be appointed by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 241, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 241.)
