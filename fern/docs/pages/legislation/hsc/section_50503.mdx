# HSC § 50503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. Assistance Activities of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 50503

---

## Text

The department may adopt rules and regulations establishing a mutual self-help housing technical assistance program providing grants to nonprofit housing sponsors for carrying out programs of technical and supervisory assistance to aid persons and families of low or moderate income to develop mutual self-help housing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
