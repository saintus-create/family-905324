# HSC § 14895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 3. Notice to Destroy Weeds > Article: 3. Posting and Publishing Notice

**Citation:** HSC § 14895

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing prescribed in Section 14892 shall be published once in a newspaper of general circulation printed and published in the county, not less than 10 days prior to the date of the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 60.)
