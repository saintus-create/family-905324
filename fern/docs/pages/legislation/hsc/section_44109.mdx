# HSC § 44109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 10. Accelerated Light-Duty Vehicle Retirement Program

**Citation:** HSC § 44109

---

## Text

The program shall include appropriate means to solicit vehicle owners, including mass mailings, media advertising, news coverage, and direct mail to owners of candidate vehicles, and may include high-emitting vehicles based on smog check or remote sensing or high-emitter profile information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 929, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
