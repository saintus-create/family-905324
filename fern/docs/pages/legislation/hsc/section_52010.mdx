# HSC § 52010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 5. LOCAL HOUSING FINANCE AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 52010

---

## Text

Unless otherwise indicated by the context, the definitions contained in this chapter shall govern the construction of this part. The definitions contained in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 50050) of Part 1 of this division shall not be applicable to this part, except as expressly and otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1069.)
