# HSC § 439.900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1.6. HEALTH RESEARCH FAIRNESS

**Citation:** HSC § 439.900

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Health Research Fairness Act.
