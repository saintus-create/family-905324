# HSC § 6746

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 4. Levy of Tax

**Citation:** HSC § 6746

---

## Text

After the entry in the minutes of the resolution fixing the rate of the tax the board shall cause the district assessor to compute the amount of the tax upon each item of real and personal property, and enter the amount on the assessment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
