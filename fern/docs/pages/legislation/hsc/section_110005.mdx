# HSC § 110005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 110005

---

## Text

“Potentially hazardous food” means any food capable of supporting growth of infectious or toxigenic micro-organisms when held at temperatures above 45 degrees Fahrenheit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 915, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
