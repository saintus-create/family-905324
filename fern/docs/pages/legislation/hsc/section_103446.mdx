# HSC § 103446

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 11. Amendment of Records > Article: 8. Revision of Birth Records to Correct Gender Errors Made by Birthing Hospitals or Local Registrars

**Citation:** HSC § 103446

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that this article provide a remedy for the correction of birth certificates that contain gender errors made by the birthing hospital or local registrar when completing the original birth certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 780, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
