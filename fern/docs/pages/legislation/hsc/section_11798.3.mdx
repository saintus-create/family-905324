# HSC § 11798.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 4. State-County Partnership > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 11798.3

---

## Text

The department shall review each county’s contract for alcohol and other drug services to determine that the contract complies with this division and with the standards adopted under this division. The department shall approve a contract that is in compliance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 25. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
