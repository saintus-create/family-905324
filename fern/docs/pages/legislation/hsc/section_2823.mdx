# HSC § 2823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 2823

---

## Text

The petition may fix the maximum rate of assessments that may be levied by the district.
