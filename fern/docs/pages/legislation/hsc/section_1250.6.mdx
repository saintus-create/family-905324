# HSC § 1250.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 1250.6

---

## Text

Any requirement placed upon, or reference to, a corporation in this chapter, shall also apply to a limited liability company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 685, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
