# HSC § 122400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 7. HEPATITIS C > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 122400

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Hepatitis C Education, Screening, and Treatment Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
