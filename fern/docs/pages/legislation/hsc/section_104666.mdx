# HSC § 104666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 2. Nutrition > Article: 3. Eating for Health

**Citation:** HSC § 104666

---

## Text

This article does not prohibit a public entity from voluntarily enacting more stringent restrictions on ultraprocessed foods, restricted school foods, or ultraprocessed foods of concern.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 467, Sec. 8. (AB 1264) Effective January 1, 2026.)
