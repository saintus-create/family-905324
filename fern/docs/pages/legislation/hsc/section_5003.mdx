# HSC § 5003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 5003

---

## Text

The governing body shall have supervision and control over the construction, acquisition, and operation of the works, and the collection of rates for their use.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
