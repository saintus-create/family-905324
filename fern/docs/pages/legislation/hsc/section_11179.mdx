# HSC § 11179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11179

---

## Text

A person who fills a prescription shall keep it on file for at least three years from the date of filling it.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 896.)
