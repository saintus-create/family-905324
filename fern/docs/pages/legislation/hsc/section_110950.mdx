# HSC § 110950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110950

---

## Text

The director may adopt any regulations as are reasonably necessary to assist in the implementation of, or to make more specific, the provisions of, this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
