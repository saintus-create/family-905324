# HSC § 11029.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11029.5

---

## Text

“Security printer” means a person approved to produce controlled substance prescription forms pursuant to Section 11161.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 406, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
