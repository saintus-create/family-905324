# HSC § 34053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.6. NATURAL DISASTERS > Chapter: 1. Natural Disaster Community Assistance

**Citation:** HSC § 34053

---

## Text

For the purpose of providing disaster relief to farmworkers in communities subject to a natural disaster, the department shall give priority to awarding grants in communities participating in the Special Housing Program for Migratory Workers (Chapter 8.5 (commencing with Section 50710) of Part 2 of Division 33).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 225, Sec. 44. Effective August 16, 2004.)
