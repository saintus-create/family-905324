# HSC § 44025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 2. Program Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 44025

---

## Text

The department shall act as a clearinghouse to provide access to the vendors who possess service information generated by the vehicle manufacturers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 27, Sec. 26. Effective March 30, 1994.)
