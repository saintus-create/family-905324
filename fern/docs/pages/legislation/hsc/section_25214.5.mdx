# HSC § 25214.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.2. Motor Vehicle Switches

**Citation:** HSC § 25214.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “mercury-containing motor vehicle light switch” means any motor vehicle light switch found in the hood or trunk of a motor vehicle that contains mercury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 656, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
