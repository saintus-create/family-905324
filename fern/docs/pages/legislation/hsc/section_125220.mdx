# HSC § 125220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5. HEREDITARY DISEASES/CONGENITAL DEFECTS > Chapter: 2. Genetic Disease Services > Article: 2. Long-Term Care for Degenerative Genetic Disease

**Citation:** HSC § 125220

---

## Text

The actions undertaken pursuant to this article shall not impose additional state obligations or expenditures for the care of persons with chronic and degenerative conditions, as defined by this article, unless the Legislature enacts a statute specifically appropriating money for the additional obligations or expenditures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
