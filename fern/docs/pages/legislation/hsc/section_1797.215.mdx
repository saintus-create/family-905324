# HSC § 1797.215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 1. Local EMS Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.215

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, EMT-I's, EMT-II's, and EMT-P’s shall be required to renew their cardiopulmonary resuscitation certificate no more than once every two years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 774, Sec. 1.)
