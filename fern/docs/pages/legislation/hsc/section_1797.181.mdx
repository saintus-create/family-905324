# HSC § 1797.181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 5. Personnel

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.181

---

## Text

The authority may, by regulation, prescribe standardized insignias or emblems for patches which may be affixed to the clothing of an EMT-I, EMT-II, or EMT-P.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1246, Sec. 24.)
