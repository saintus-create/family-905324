# HSC § 13835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 3. Formation > Article: 3. Selection of the Initial Board of Directors

**Citation:** HSC § 13835

---

## Text

In the case of a district which contains only unincorporated territory in a single county, the district board may be elected or may be appointed by the county board of supervisors which may appoint itself as the district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
