# HSC § 44072.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 7. Denial, Suspension, and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 44072.8

---

## Text

When a license has been revoked or suspended following a hearing under this article, any additional license issued under this chapter in the name of the licensee may be likewise revoked or suspended by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 386, Sec. 11.)
