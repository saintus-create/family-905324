# HSC § 5414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 2. Sewage and Other Waste

**Citation:** HSC § 5414

---

## Text

With respect to any condition of contamination, the state department may accept the action of any state, county, or municipal officer or agency having jurisdiction over the matter as sufficient.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1550.)
