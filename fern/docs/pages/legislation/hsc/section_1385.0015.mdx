# HSC § 1385.0015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 6.1. Pharmacy Benefit Managers

**Citation:** HSC § 1385.0015

---

## Text

A pharmacy benefit manager license issued under this article shall remain in effect until revoked or suspended by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 21, Sec. 23. (AB 116) Effective June 30, 2025.)
