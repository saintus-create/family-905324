# HSC § 122390.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 12. Safe Transportation of Dogs and Cats

**Citation:** HSC § 122390.1

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “mobile or traveling housing facility” means a transporting vehicle, including, but not limited to, a car, truck, trailer, bus, or recreational vehicle used to transport animals by an entity described in Section 122390.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 80, Sec. 2. (AB 1781) Effective January 1, 2023.)
