# HSC § 34355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 34355

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold at public or private sale in such manner and upon such terms as may be provided in the resolution authorizing the bonds or by separate resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 478, Sec. 6. Effective July 28, 1983.)
