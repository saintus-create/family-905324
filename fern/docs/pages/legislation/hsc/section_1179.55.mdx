# HSC § 1179.55

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6.1. CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2008 > Chapter: 2. The Children’s Hospital Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.55

---

## Text

The authority is authorized to award grants to any children’s hospital for purposes of funding projects, as defined in subdivision (g) of Section 1179.51.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 4, 2008, by initiative Proposition 3.)
