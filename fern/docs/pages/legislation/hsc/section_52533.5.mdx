# HSC § 52533.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 6. CALIFORNIA FIRST-TIME HOME BUYERS > Chapter: 4. Repeal

**Citation:** HSC § 52533.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the agency shall have no authority to make mortgages or to incur debt pursuant to this part after the date of the approval of the California Housing and Jobs Investment Bond Act (Part 6.1 (commencing with Section 52534)) by the California electorate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 116, Sec. 2. Effective July 15, 1993. Note: Conditions in Section 52533.6 for repeal of Part 6 failed. Part 6.1 (Calif. Housing and Jobs Investment Bond Act), as proposed by Stats. 1993, Ch. 116, Sec. 3, was rejected as Prop. 173 on Nov. 2, 1993.)
