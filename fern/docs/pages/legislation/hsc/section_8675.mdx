# HSC § 8675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 4. Property Rights > Article: 4. Vested Right of Interment

**Citation:** HSC § 8675

---

## Text

A vested right of interment may be waived and is terminated upon the interment elsewhere of the remains of the person in whom vested.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
