# HSC § 1797.100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 1. The Emergency Medical Services Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.100

---

## Text

There is in the state government, in the California Health and Human Services Agency, the Emergency Medical Services Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 19. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
