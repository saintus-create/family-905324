# HSC § 1798.181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. Facilities > Article: 4. Poison Control Centers

**Citation:** HSC § 1798.181

---

## Text

The authority shall consolidate the number of poison control centers if it is determined by the authority that the consolidation will result in cost savings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1366, Sec. 1. Effective October 27, 1992.)
