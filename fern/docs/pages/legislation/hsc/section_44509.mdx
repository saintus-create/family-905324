# HSC § 44509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 44509

---

## Text

“Public agency” means any state agency, board, or commission, any county, city and county, city, regional agency, public district, or other political subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
