# HSC § 1586.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 5. Additional Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 1586.6

---

## Text

Adult day health care centers may not require family members to attend the center or assist the participant with activities of daily living while at the center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 105, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
