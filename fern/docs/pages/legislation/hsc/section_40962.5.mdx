# HSC § 40962.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 2. Creation of the Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District

**Citation:** HSC § 40962.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, as of July 1, 1996, Article 2 (commencing with Section 40120) of Chapter 2 shall not be applicable to the Sacramento district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 729, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001.)
