# HSC § 4645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 4645

---

## Text

The resolution, together with the names of the members of the governing body voting for and against it, shall be published, posted, and mailed as provided in Sections 4610 and 4610.5, except that in applying such sections the word “district” shall mean the territory proposed to be annexed.
