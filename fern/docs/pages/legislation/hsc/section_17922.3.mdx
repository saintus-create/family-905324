# HSC § 17922.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 2. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 17922.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a residential structure that is moved into, or within, the jurisdiction of a local agency or the department, shall not be treated as a new building or structure, but rather shall be treated, for the purposes of this part, as subject to Section 17958.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 714, Sec. 16. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2017.)
