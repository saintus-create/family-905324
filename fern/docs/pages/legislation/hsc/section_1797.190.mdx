# HSC § 1797.190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 5. Personnel

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.190

---

## Text

The authority may establish minimum standards for the training and use of automatic external defibrillators.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 718, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
