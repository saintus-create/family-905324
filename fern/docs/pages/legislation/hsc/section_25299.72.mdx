# HSC § 25299.72

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 7. Cost Recovery, Enforcement, and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.72

---

## Text

Upon motion and sufficient showing by any party, the court shall join to the action any person who may be liable for costs or expenditures of the type recoverable pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25299.62 by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1366, Sec. 27. Effective September 27, 1990. Inoperative January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 25299.81.)
