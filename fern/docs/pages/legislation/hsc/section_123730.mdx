# HSC § 123730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 3. Sudden Infant Death Syndrome

**Citation:** HSC § 123730

---

## Text

The department shall keep each county health officer advised of the most current knowledge relating to the nature and causes of sudden infant death syndrome.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
