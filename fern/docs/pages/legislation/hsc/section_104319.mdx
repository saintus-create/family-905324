# HSC § 104319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 1. CHRONIC DISEASE > Chapter: 6.5. Reduction of Asthma through Assessment, Intervention, and Evaluation

**Citation:** HSC § 104319

---

## Text

The department shall monitor the clinical and public interventions required by this chapter, and shall report successful and unsuccessful interventions in clinical and public health practice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 93, Sec. 24. Effective July 7, 2000.)
