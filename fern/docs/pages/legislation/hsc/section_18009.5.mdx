# HSC § 18009.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18009.5

---

## Text

“Registered owner” means a person registered by the department as the owner of a manufactured home, mobilehome, commercial coach, floating home, or truck camper.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 686, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
