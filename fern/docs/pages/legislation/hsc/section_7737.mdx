# HSC § 7737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 3. Notice of Intention

**Citation:** HSC § 7737

---

## Text

Copies of the notice shall within ten days after the first publication be posted in at least three conspicuous places in the cemetery or the portion from which removals are to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
