# HSC § 43823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous > Article: 2. Fuel System Evaporative Loss Control Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 43823

---

## Text

The installation of a certified fuel system evaporative loss control device on used motor vehicles shall not be mandated except by statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
