# HSC § 108630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Consumer Products > Article: 4. Bucket Labeling

**Citation:** HSC § 108630

---

## Text

Any person subject to the labeling requirements of this article is exempt from those requirements if the person has a label that is in substantial compliance with the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
