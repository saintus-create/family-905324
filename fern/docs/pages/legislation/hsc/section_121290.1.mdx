# HSC § 121290.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 4. HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS (HIV) > Chapter: 13.7. Statewide African-American Initiative

**Citation:** HSC § 121290.1

---

## Text

The initiative shall sponsor and conduct an annual Summit on African-Americans and HIV. The summit shall be funded solely by private funds. The summit shall do all of the following:

(a) Provide a report on the progress of the initiative.

(b) Offer technical assistance workshops.

(c) Provide an overview of local, regional, and national efforts concerning health disparities relating to African-Americans and HIV.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 403, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
