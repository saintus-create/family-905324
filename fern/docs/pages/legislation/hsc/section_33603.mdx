# HSC § 33603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 33603

---

## Text

An agency may invest any money held in reserves or sinking funds, or any money not required for immediate disbursement, in property or securities in which savings banks may legally invest money subject to their control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
