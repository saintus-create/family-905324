# HSC § 120525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 3. SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASE > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control

**Citation:** HSC § 120525

---

## Text

The department may provide medical, advisory, financial, or other assistance to organizations funded pursuant to Section 120511.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 38, Sec. 21. (SB 78) Effective June 27, 2019.)
