# HSC § 111035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 11. Local Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 111035

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, the health officer and his or her deputies shall have the same powers and authority as an inspector of the Bureau of Food and Drug of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
