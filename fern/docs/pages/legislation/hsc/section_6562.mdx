# HSC § 6562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 5. Elections > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6562

---

## Text

At an annexation election every qualified voter resident in the territory proposed to be annexed for the length of time necessary to enable him to vote at a general election may vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1949, Ch. 977.)
