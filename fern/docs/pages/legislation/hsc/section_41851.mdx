# HSC § 41851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 3. Agricultural Burning

**Citation:** HSC § 41851

---

## Text

Section 41800 shall not apply to burning regulated pursuant to this article.
