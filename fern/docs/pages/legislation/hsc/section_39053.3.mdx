# HSC § 39053.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39053.3

---

## Text

“Title V” means Title V of the federal Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 7661 et seq.).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1166, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
