# HSC § 39028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39028

---

## Text

“Exhaust device” means a motor vehicle pollution control device to reduce exhaust emissions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
