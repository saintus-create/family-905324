# HSC § 40489

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 6. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 40489

---

## Text

The south coast district may contract for such professional assistance as may be necessary or convenient for the exercise of duties imposed on the south coast district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 324.)
