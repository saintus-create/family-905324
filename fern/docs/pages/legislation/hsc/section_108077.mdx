# HSC § 108077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 3. Enforcement and Penalties > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 108077

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2029, the department shall adopt regulations to implement, interpret, enforce, or make specific this chapter and the covered PFAS restrictions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 932, Sec. 2. (AB 347) Effective January 1, 2025.)
