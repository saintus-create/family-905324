# HSC § 101860.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 6. Other > Article: 1. Applicability and Purpose

**Citation:** HSC § 101860.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the primary public benefit purpose of the corporation is as stated in its articles of incorporation or bylaws of the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 927, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
