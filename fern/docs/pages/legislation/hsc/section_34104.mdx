# HSC § 34104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 34104

---

## Text

“Legislative body” means the city council, board of supervisors, or other legislative body of the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
