# HSC § 108958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 13. Cleaning Product Right to Know Act of 2017

**Citation:** HSC § 108958

---

## Text

A designated product shall not be sold in the state unless the designated product and the manufacturer of the designated product comply with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 830, Sec. 1. (SB 258) Effective January 1, 2018.)
