# HSC § 1409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Referral Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1409

---

## Text

Separate licenses shall be required for referral agencies which are maintained on separate, noncontiguous premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 700, Sec. 16.)
