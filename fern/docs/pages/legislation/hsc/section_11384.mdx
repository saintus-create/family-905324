# HSC § 11384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 6. Offenses and Penalties > Article: 6. Precursors of Phencyclidine (PCP) and Methamphetamine

**Citation:** HSC § 11384

---

## Text

The Board of Pharmacy shall, by regulation, authorize such persons to possess any combinations of substance specified in subdivision (a) or (b) of Section 11383 as it determines need and will use such substance for a lawful purpose.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1116.)
