# HSC § 46024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NOISE CONTROL ACT > Chapter: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 46024

---

## Text

“Public agency” means and includes every state agency and every local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
