# HSC § 1364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 4. Solicitation and Enrollment

**Citation:** HSC § 1364

---

## Text

Where the director finds it necessary in the interest of full and fair disclosure, all advertising and other consumer information disseminated by a plan for the purpose of influencing persons to become members of a plan shall contain such supplemental disclosure information as the director may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 525, Sec. 90. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, or sooner, by Sec. 214 of Ch. 525.)
