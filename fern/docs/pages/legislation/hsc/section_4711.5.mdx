# HSC § 4711.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 4711.5

---

## Text

The land proposed to be formed into a district need not consist of contiguous parcels.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2130.)
