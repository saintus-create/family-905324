# HSC § 5745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 9. Joint Municipal Sewage Disposal District Act > Article: 5.5. Claims

**Citation:** HSC § 5745

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against the district are governed by Part 3 (commencing with Section 900) and Part 4 (commencing with Section 940) of Division 3.6 of Title 1 of the Government Code except as provided therein, or by other statutes or regulations expressly applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1727) by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715. Note: This article was added to Chapter 9 (formerly comm. with Section 5700), which was repealed by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1309.)
