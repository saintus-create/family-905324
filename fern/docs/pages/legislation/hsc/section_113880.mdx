# HSC § 113880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113880

---

## Text

“Producer” means a person or entity who produces shell eggs or edible plants by practice of the agricultural arts upon land that the person or entity controls.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 447, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2009.)
