# HSC § 18021.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 18021.6

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall be construed as precluding the application of any other provision of the penal laws of this state to any transaction involving fraud, misrepresentation, forgery, or deceit which violates this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1342, Sec. 5.5. Effective September 26, 1984. Operative January 1, 1985, by Sec. 39 of Ch. 1342.)
