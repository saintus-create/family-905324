# HSC § 113963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 3. Management and Personnel > Article: 4. Handwashing

**Citation:** HSC § 113963

---

## Text

Consistent with Section 113952, a food employee working in any food facility, as defined in Section 113789 of the Health and Safety Code, shall be permitted to wash their hands every 30 minutes and additionally as needed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 45, Sec. 2. (AB 1867) Effective September 9, 2020.)
