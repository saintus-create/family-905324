# HSC § 12681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12681

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell or transfer any safe and sane fireworks to a consumer or user thereof other than at a fixed place of business of a retailer for which a license and permit has been issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
