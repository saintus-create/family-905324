# HSC § 12674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12674

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to store or possess any fireworks for which a license is required and which has been revoked or surrendered or any license which has not been renewed and such storage or possession is held beyond the period provided for in Section 12604.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
