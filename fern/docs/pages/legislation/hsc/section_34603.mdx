# HSC § 34603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Housing Operations Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34603

---

## Text

“Federal government” means the United States or any agency or instrumentality, corporate or otherwise, of the United States of America.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 579.)
