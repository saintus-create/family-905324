# HSC § 34279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 34279

---

## Text

For the legal services it requires, an authority may call upon the chief law officer of the city or the county or may employ its own counsel and legal staff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
