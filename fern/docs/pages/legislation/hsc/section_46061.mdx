# HSC § 46061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NOISE CONTROL ACT > Chapter: 6. Assistance to Local Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 46061

---

## Text

The office shall provide technical assistance to local agencies in combating noise pollution. Such assistance shall include but not be limited to:

(a) Advice concerning methods of noise abatement and control.

(b) Advice on training of noise control personnel.

(c) Advice on selection and operation of noise abatement equipment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
