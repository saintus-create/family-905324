# HSC § 79745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 8. Cost Recovery > Article: 4. Timing

**Citation:** HSC § 79745

## Text

This article does not apply to a cost recovery action brought by a regional board under the Water Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
