# HSC § 25997.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Safety Glazing Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 25997.6

---

## Text

No liability under this chapter shall be created as to workmen who are employees of a contractor, subcontractor, or other employer responsible for compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 329.)
