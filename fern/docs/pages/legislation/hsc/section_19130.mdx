# HSC § 19130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 2a. Building Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 19130

---

## Text

No person shall construct a building subject to this chapter unless he has obtained a written permit for that purpose from the appropriate enforcement agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 301.)
