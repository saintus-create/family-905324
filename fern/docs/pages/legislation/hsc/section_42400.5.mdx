# HSC § 42400.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 3. Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 42400.5

---

## Text

In addition to the penalties, specified in Section 42400, the cost of putting out any unauthorized open outdoor fires may be imposed on any person violating Section 41800 or 41852.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1063.)
