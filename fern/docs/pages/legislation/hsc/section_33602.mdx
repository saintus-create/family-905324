# HSC § 33602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 33602

---

## Text

“Bonds” means any bonds, notes, interim certificates, debentures, or other obligations issued by an agency pursuant to Article 5 (commencing with Section 33640) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
