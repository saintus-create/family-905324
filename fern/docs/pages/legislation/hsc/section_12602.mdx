# HSC § 12602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. Issuance, Revocation and Renewal

**Citation:** HSC § 12602

---

## Text

A license shall not be required for the retail sale, use, or discharge of agricultural and wildlife fireworks, model rocket motors, or emergency signaling devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 106, Sec. 4. (AB 467) Effective January 1, 2016.)
