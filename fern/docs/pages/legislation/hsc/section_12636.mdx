# HSC § 12636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** HSC § 12636

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the State Fire Marshal shall charge a fee in the amount of five dollars ($5) for each certified copy of any record, document, or paper in his custody, or for certification of any document representing the content of any such record, document, or paper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
