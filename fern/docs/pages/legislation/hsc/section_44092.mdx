# HSC § 44092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 9. Repair or Removal of High Polluters

**Citation:** HSC § 44092

---

## Text

The high-polluter repair or removal program shall be designed to repair or remove motor vehicles registered in this state that are subject to an inspection and maintenance program and are producing high levels of emissions as a result of their use in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 929, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
