# HSC § 1596.880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 3. Remedies for Employer Discrimination

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.880

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article:

(a) “Employee” means employee of a licensee or employee of the agent of a licensee subject to this act.

(b) “Employer” means a licensee or agent of a licensee subject to this act.
