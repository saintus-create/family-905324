# HSC § 6550.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 4. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 6550.20

---

## Text

The hearing on the proposed annexation shall be held in the manner required by Section 6550.4, except that in applying such section the words, “improvement district” shall mean the territory proposed to be annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1055.)
