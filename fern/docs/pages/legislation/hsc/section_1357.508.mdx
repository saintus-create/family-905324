# HSC § 1357.508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.16. Nongrandfathered Small Employer Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.508

---

## Text

A small employer health care service plan contract shall provide to subscribers and enrollees at least all of the essential health benefits as defined by the state pursuant to Section 1302 of PPACA.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 3. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
