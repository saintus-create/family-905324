# HSC § 53500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 11. HOUSING AND EMERGENCY SHELTER TRUST FUND ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 53500

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Housing and Emergency Shelter Trust Fund Act of 2002.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 26, Sec. 8. Approved in Proposition 46 at the November 5, 2002, election.)
