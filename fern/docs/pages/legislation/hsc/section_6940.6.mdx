# HSC § 6940.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 2. Use of County Assessor’s Roll

**Citation:** HSC § 6940.6

---

## Text

Following the board’s election, the county auditor shall on or about the third Monday in August of each year transmit to the board a written statement showing the total value of all property in the district, which value shall be ascertained from the assessment roll used by the county for that year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 426.)
