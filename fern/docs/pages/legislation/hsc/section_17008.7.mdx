# HSC § 17008.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1. EMPLOYEE HOUSING ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 17008.7

---

## Text

“Fund,” as used in this part, means the Employee Housing Regulation Fund established by Section 17036.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 40, Sec. 10. (AB 129) Effective July 10, 2023.)
