# HSC § 111805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 7. Cosmetics > Article: 4. Voluntary Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 111805

---

## Text

Any person registered pursuant to this article shall immediately notify the department of any change in the information reported in the registration application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
