# HSC § 7726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 2. Declaration of Intention by Cemetery Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 7726

---

## Text

Any resolution or declaration of intention to disinter and remove human remains pursuant to this chapter adopted or declared by any cemetery authority shall specify and declare that at any time after the expiration of ten months from and after the first publication of the notice of the resolution or declaration, the human remains then remaining in all or any part of the cemetery will be removed by the cemetery authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
