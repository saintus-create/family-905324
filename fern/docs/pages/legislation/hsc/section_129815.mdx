# HSC § 129815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 129815

---

## Text

Any permit or authorization issued or provided pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 15374) of Part 6.7 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
