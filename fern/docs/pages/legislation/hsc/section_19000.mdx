# HSC § 19000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 1. Scope and Application

**Citation:** HSC § 19000

---

## Text

Any provision in this part which is inconsistent with any provision in the State Housing Law, or any rule or regulation promulgated pursuant thereto, is inapplicable to buildings subject to that law.
