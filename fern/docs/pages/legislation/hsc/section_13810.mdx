# HSC § 13810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 2. Area

**Citation:** HSC § 13810

---

## Text

Any territory, whether incorporated or unincorporated, whether contiguous or noncontiguous, may be included in a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
