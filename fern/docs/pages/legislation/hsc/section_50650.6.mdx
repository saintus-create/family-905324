# HSC § 50650.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 6. CalHome Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50650.6

---

## Text

The department may use up to 5 percent of the funds appropriated for the purposes of this chapter for its costs in administering the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 84, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
