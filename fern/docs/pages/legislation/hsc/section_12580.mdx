# HSC § 12580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. Issuance, Revocation and Renewal

**Citation:** HSC § 12580

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal may issue and renew licenses for the manufacture, import, export, sale, and use of all fireworks and pyrotechnic devices in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
