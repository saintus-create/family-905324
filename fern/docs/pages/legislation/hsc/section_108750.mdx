# HSC § 108750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 7. Children's Poison Prevention Packaging Act

**Citation:** HSC § 108750

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Children’s Poison Protection Act of 1990.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
