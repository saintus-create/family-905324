# HSC § 38594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25.5. CALIFORNIA GLOBAL WARMING SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2006 > Part: 7. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 38594

---

## Text

(a) Nothing in this division shall limit or expand the existing authority of any district, as defined in Section 39025.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2046.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 135, Sec. 13) by Stats. 2025, Ch. 117, Sec. 12. (AB 1207) Effective September 19, 2025. Section operative January 1, 2046, by its own provisions.)
