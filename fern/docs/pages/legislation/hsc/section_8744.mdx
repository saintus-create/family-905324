# HSC § 8744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 2. Care of Active Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8744

---

## Text

There shall be printed at the head of all contracts, agreements, statements, receipts, literature and other publications of nonendowment care cemeteries the following form:

“This institution is operated as a ‛nonendowment care’ interment property.”

The phrase “nonendowment care” shall be of a size and style to be approved by the Cemetery and Funeral Bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 256. Effective January 1, 2001.)
