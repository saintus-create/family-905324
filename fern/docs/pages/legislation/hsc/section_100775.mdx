# HSC § 100775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Laboratory Services > Article: 2. Licensing of Laboratories

**Citation:** HSC § 100775

---

## Text

Any ampoules and their contents employed in a breath alcohol analysis test may be destroyed by the law enforcement agency in possession of them one year after the date of collection of the breath sample.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
