# HSC § 117770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Tracking document” means the medical waste tracking document specified in Section 118040.
