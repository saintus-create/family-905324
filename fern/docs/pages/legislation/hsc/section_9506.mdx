# HSC § 9506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9506

---

## Text

“Columbarium” includes any building or structure, used or intended to be used, for the interment of cremated human remains.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
