# HSC § 1774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Continuing Care Contracts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1774

---

## Text

No arrangement allowed by a permit to accept deposits, a provisional certificate or authority, or a certificate of authority issued by the department under this chapter may be deemed a security for any purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 820, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2001.)
