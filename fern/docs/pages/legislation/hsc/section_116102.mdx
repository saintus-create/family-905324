# HSC § 116102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 116102

---

## Text

“Place,” as used in Article 3 (commencing with Section 116125) of Chapter 2 and Section 116250, includes land, place, building, structure, wharf, pier, dock, vessel, or water craft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
