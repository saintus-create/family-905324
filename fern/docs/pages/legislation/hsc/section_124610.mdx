# HSC § 124610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4. PRIMARY HEALTH CARE > Chapter: 5. Rural Health Services Development > Article: 1. Legislative Intent and General Provisions

## Text

The director shall administer this chapter and shall adopt any regulations and standards as are necessary to implement this chapter.
