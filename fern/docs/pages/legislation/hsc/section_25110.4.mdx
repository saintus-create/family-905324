# HSC § 25110.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25110.4

---

## Text

“Buffer zone” means an area of land that surrounds a hazardous waste facility and on which certain land uses and activities are restricted to protect the public health and safety and the environment from existing or potential hazards caused by the migration of hazardous waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25110.3 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 73, Sec. 3. (SB 158) Effective July 12, 2021.)
