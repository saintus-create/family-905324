# HSC § 12509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12509

---

## Text

“Exporter” means any person who sells, consigns, or delivers fireworks located within this state for delivery, use, or sale out of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
