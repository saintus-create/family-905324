# HSC § 114272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 9. Permanent Food Facilities > Article: 1. Floors, Walls, and Ceilings

**Citation:** HSC § 114272

---

## Text

Mats and duckboards shall be designed to be removable and easily cleanable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
