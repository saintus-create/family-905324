# HSC § 117530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 13. GARBAGE AND ONSITE SEWAGE DISPOSAL > Chapter: 4. Waste and Waste Disposal > Article: 5. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 117530

---

## Text

Violation of any provision of Article 2 (commencing with Section 117475), Article 3 (commencing with Section 117505), Article 4 (commencing with Section 117510), and Article 6 (commencing with Section 117550) is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
