# HSC § 18214.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18214.1

---

## Text

“Park” means any manufactured housing community or mobilehome park.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 434, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2002. Operative January 1, 2004, by Sec. 44 of Ch. 434, as amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1038.)
