# HSC § 39602.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 39602.6

---

## Text

The state board shall condition eligibility to participate in grant, loan, voucher, or other incentive programs to purchase new drayage and short-haul trucks on compliance with Chapter 3.6 (commencing with Section 39680), as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 748, Sec. 3. (AB 794) Effective January 1, 2022.)
