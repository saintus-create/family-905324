# HSC § 4646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 4646

---

## Text

On the day fixed for the hearing, or any day to which the hearing is continued, the governing body shall hear and consider any objections presented to the annexation of the territory to the district or to the inclusion of any territory proposed to be annexed. At the hearing the governing body shall exclude from the proposed annexation any territory which in its opinion will not be benefited by such annexation.
