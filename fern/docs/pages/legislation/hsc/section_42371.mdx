# HSC § 42371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 2.5. Product Variances

**Citation:** HSC § 42371

---

## Text

Sections 42350.5, 42351, 42351.5, 42352.5, 42354 to 42357, inclusive, 42359, and 42362 to 42364, inclusive, shall apply to the granting of product variances pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 443, Sec. 2. Effective September 7, 1994.)
