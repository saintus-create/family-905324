# HSC § 6696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6696

---

## Text

The board may prescribe the time and manner of assessing, levying, and collecting taxes for district purposes, except as otherwise provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
