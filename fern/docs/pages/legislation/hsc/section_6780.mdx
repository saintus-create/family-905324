# HSC § 6780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 6. Use of County Assessor’s Roll

**Citation:** HSC § 6780

---

## Text

The board may elect to avail itself of the assessment made by the assessor of the county in which the district is situated, and may take that assessment as the basis for district taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
