# HSC § 13885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 6. Elections

**Citation:** HSC § 13885

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this part, districts are subject to the Uniform District Election Law, Part 4 (commencing with Section 10500) of Division 10 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 137. Effective January 1, 1995.)
