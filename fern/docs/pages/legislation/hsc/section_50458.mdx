# HSC § 50458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Policy Activities of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 50458

---

## Text

The department shall assist and advise the Office of Planning and Research on the performance of functions specified in Section 65040.3 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
