# HSC § 13011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Liability in Relation to Fires

**Citation:** HSC § 13011

---

## Text

Both doors of any double doors designated as the public entrance to any place of business shall be kept unlocked during normal business hours.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 267, Sec. 1.)
