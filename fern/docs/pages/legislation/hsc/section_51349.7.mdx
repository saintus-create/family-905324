# HSC § 51349.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.9. Disaster Rebuilding Fund Act

**Citation:** HSC § 51349.7

---

## Text

The agency shall communicate with relevant public agencies, including local, state, and federal agencies, to avoid duplication of benefits and to support program alignment with disaster recovery efforts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 68, Sec. 24. (AB 179) Effective July 13, 2026.)
