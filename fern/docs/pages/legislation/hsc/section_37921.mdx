# HSC § 37921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 13. RESIDENTIAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 37921

---

## Text

All residential rehabilitation shall be constructed or completed subject to the rules and regulations of the local agency. A local agency may acquire by deed, purchase, lease, contract, gift, devise, or otherwise any real or personal property, structures, rights, rights-of-way, franchises, easements, and other interests in lands necessary or convenient for the financing of residential rehabilitation, upon such terms and conditions as it deems advisable, and may lease, sell, or dispose of the same in such manner as may be necessary or desirable to carry out the objectives and purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1201.)
