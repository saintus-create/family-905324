# HSC § 8129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 2. PUBLIC CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8129

---

## Text

The public cemeteries of cities, towns, or neighborhoods or of fraternal or beneficial associations or societies shall be inclosed and laid off into plots.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
