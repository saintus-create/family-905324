# HSC § 36105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7.5. MODEL CITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 36105

---

## Text

As used in this part:

(a) “Coordinator” means the Model Cities Coordinator.

(b) “Board” means a local Model Cities resources board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1462.)
