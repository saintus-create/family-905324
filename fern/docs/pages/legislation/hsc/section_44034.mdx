# HSC § 44034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 3. Quality Assurance

**Citation:** HSC § 44034

---

## Text

Annual license fees for smog check stations and biennial license fees for smog check technicians shall be imposed by the department, but shall not exceed the reasonable cost of administering the qualifications and licensing program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 27, Sec. 31. Effective March 30, 1994.)
