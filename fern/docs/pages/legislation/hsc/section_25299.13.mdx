# HSC § 25299.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.13

---

## Text

“Claim” means a submittal to the fund for the reimbursement of costs incurred due to an occurrence. A claim consists of several documents, including, but not limited to, the fund application, reimbursement requests, and verification documents.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 154, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
