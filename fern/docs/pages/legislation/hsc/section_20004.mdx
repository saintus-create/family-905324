# HSC § 20004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 20004

---

## Text

Any reference in this chapter to a county or county officer is a reference to the county or officer of the county in which a district is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
