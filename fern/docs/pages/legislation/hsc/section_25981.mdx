# HSC § 25981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13.4. Force Fed Birds

**Citation:** HSC § 25981

---

## Text

A person may not force feed a bird for the purpose of enlarging the bird’s liver beyond normal size, or hire another person to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 904, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005. Operative July 1, 2012, pursuant to Section 25984.)
