# HSC § 1596.888

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 4. Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.888

---

## Text

Any license, registration, or special permit suspended pursuant to this chapter, and any special permit revoked pursuant to this chapter, may be reinstated pursuant to Section 11522 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1615, Sec. 9.)
