# HSC § 7015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7015

---

## Text

(a) “Crypt” or “vault” means a space in a mausoleum of sufficient size, used or intended to be used, to entomb human remains that have been neither cremated nor hydrolyzed.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 43) and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 44. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, by its own provisions.)
