# HSC § 41015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 41015

---

## Text

This chapter does not constitute an infringement on the existing authority of local governments to plan or control land use, and nothing in this chapter provides or transfers new authority over such land use to the Sacramento district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 3.)
