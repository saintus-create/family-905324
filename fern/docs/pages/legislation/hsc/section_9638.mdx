# HSC § 9638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 2. Structural and Material Requirements of Community Mausoleums and Columbariums

**Citation:** HSC § 9638

---

## Text

Material for exterior trim, including exterior crypt and niche fronts, shall be travertine, serpentine marble, or grade A exterior type marble or granite, only.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1994.)
