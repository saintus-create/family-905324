# HSC § 8707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 1. Care of Old Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8707

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publishing a copy of the notice in a newspaper of general circulation near the cemetery in the county in which the property is situated, once a week for three consecutive weeks prior to the date of hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
