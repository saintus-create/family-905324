# HSC § 25180.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 25180.1

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “permit” includes matters deemed to be permits pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 25198.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 113, Sec. 1. Effective July 2, 1992.)
