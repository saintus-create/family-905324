# HSC § 124300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 3. FAMILY PLANNING

**Citation:** HSC § 124300

---

## Text

Within any county where 10 percent or more of the population, as determined by the Demographic Research Unit of the Department of Finance, speaks any one language other than English as its native language, every local health department shall make copies of circulars and pamphlets relating to family planning that are made available to the public also available in the other language.

The State Department of Health Care Services, upon request, shall make a translation available in other than English those family planning informational materials normally distributed to the general public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 29, Sec. 122. (SB 82) Effective June 27, 2019.)
