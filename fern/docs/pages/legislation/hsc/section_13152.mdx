# HSC § 13152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. State Fire Marshal > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 13152

---

## Text

The authority for the enforcement of the provisions of this article shall be in accordance with Sections 13145 and 13146, Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 13182 by Stats. 1969, Ch. 39.)
