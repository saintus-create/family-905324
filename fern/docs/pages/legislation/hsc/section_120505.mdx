# HSC § 120505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 3. SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASE > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control

**Citation:** HSC § 120505

---

## Text

The department shall develop and review plans and provide leadership and consultation for, and participate in, a program for the prevention and control of venereal disease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
