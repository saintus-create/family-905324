# HSC § 131080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Powers of the Department > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 131080

---

## Text

The department may advise all local health authorities, and, when in its judgment the public health is menaced, it shall control and regulate their action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 100180 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 241, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 241.)
