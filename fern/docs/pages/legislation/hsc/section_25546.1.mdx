# HSC § 25546.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.95. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory > Article: 4. California Toxic Release Inventory Program Act of 2007

**Citation:** HSC § 25546.1

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the “California Toxic Release Inventory Program Act of 2007.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 616, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
