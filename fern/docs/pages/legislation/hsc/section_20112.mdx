# HSC § 20112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20112

---

## Text

All money collected pursuant to this article shall be paid into the county treasury for the use of the district. The county treasurer shall pay it out on warrants of the county auditor drawn on the county treasurer upon orders of the district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
