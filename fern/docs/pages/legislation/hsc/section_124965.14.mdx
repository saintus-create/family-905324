# HSC § 124965.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4.6. Jacqueline Marie Zbur Rare Disease Advisory Council

**Citation:** HSC § 124965.14

---

## Text

This part shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 2613) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Part 4.6, commencing with Sec. 124965.)
