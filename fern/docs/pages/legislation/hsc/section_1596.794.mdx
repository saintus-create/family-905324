# HSC § 1596.794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.794

---

## Text

The department shall serve as the liaison to child day care facilities for the purposes of Sections 17608 to 17613, inclusive, of the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 865, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2007.)
