# HSC § 42365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 2.5. Product Variances

**Citation:** HSC § 42365

---

## Text

Any person who manufactures a product may petition the hearing board for a product variance from a rule or regulation of the district pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 443, Sec. 2. Effective September 7, 1994.)
