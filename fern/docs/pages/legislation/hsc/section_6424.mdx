# HSC § 6424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 1. Petition

**Citation:** HSC § 6424

---

## Text

The petition shall be published for at least two weeks preceding the hearing in a newspaper of general circulation published in each county in which the district is located.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1629.)
