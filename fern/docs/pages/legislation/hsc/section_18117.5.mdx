# HSC § 18117.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 8. Registration and Titling of Manufactured Homes, Mobilehomes, and Commercial Coaches > Article: 6. Fees and Taxes

**Citation:** HSC § 18117.5

---

## Text

The transfer fee is delinquent if not paid within 20 days of receipt by the transferee of a properly executed certificate of title for the manufactured home, mobilehome, commercial coach, truck camper, or floating home.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1467, Sec. 35. Effective October 2, 1985.)
