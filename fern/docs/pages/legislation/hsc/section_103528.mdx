# HSC § 103528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 14. Certified Copy and Verification of Records

**Citation:** HSC § 103528

---

## Text

The department may create an automated system for the purposes of implementing Sections 103525, 103525.5, 103526, and 103526.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 914, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
