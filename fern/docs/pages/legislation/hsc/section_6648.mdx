# HSC § 6648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6648

---

## Text

Each bond shall be signed by the president and countersigned by the secretary.

The bonds shall be numbered consecutively, beginning with number one, and shall have coupons attached referring to the number of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
