# HSC § 116150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 3. Rodent Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 116150

---

## Text

The governing body shall record in the office of the county recorder a notice of payment, claiming a lien on the property for the amount of the payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
