# HSC § 14830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 14830

---

## Text

Every fire company shall choose or elect a foreman, or president, who is the presiding officer, and a secretary and treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2009.)
