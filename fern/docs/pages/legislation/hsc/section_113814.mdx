# HSC § 113814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113814

---

## Text

“Injected” means manipulating meat to which a solution has been introduced into its interior by processes that are referred to as “injecting,” “pinning,” or “stitch pumping.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 195, Sec. 2. (SB 1067) Effective January 1, 2017.)
