# HSC § 11839.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 1. Narcotic Treatment Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.12

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 11839.16, proceedings for the suspension, revocation, or denial of a license or cessation of review of a renewal license under this article, except where there has been a failure to pay required fees, under this article shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code and the department shall have all the powers granted thereby. In the event of conflict between this article and the Administrative Procedure Act, the Administrative Procedure Act shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2005.)
