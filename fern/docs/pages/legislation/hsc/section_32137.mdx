# HSC § 32137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 32137

---

## Text

The board of directors may, by resolution, change the name of the district. The change in the name of the district shall be effective upon the filing of a verified copy of the resolution with the county clerk of the county or counties in which the hospital district lies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 829, Sec. 42. Effective January 1, 1999.)
