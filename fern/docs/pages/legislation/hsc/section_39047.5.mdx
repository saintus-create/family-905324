# HSC § 39047.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39047.5

---

## Text

“Qualifying facility” means a qualifying small power production facility as defined in Section 228.5 of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 978, Sec. 1.)
