# HSC § 7300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Embalming and Transportation > Article: 1. Embalming

**Citation:** HSC § 7300

---

## Text

No person shall embalm the body of any person who has died from an unknown cause, except with the permission of the coroner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 560.)
