# HSC § 117550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 13. GARBAGE AND ONSITE SEWAGE DISPOSAL > Chapter: 4. Waste and Waste Disposal > Article: 6. Prohibited Waste Disposal

**Citation:** HSC § 117550

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “solid waste” has the same meaning as that term is defined in Section 40191 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 416, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
