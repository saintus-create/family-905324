# HSC § 33492.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.7

---

## Text

(a) Paragraph (11) of subdivision (d) of Section 33367 shall not apply to the territory within the military base for any redevelopment project area adopted pursuant to this chapter.

(b) For any project area adopted pursuant to this chapter, Section 33492.11 may be used in lieu of Section 33031.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 944, Sec. 4. Effective October 8, 1993.)
