# HSC § 6645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6645

---

## Text

Bonds issued by the district under the provisions of this part shall be of such denomination or denominations as the board determines.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 736.)
