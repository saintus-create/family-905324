# HSC § 34373

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 34373

---

## Text

An authority which has issued bonds pursuant to this chapter or any trustee or custodian on behalf of the authority may invest any funds held by it as provided in the resolution authorizing the issuance of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1309, Sec. 19.)
