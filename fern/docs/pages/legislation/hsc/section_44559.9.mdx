# HSC § 44559.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 8. Capital Access Loan Program for Small Businesses

**Citation:** HSC § 44559.9

---

## Text

The authority shall expand the Capital Access Loan Program established by this article to include outreach to financial institutions that service agricultural interests in the state for the purpose of funding air pollution control measures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 479, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2004.)
