# HSC § 113794.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113794.3

---

## Text

“Fresh frozen” means that the food was quickly frozen while still fresh, including immediately after the food had been harvested or fish had been caught.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 615, Sec. 5. (AB 226) Effective January 1, 2016.)
