# HSC § 40468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Plan

**Citation:** HSC § 40468

---

## Text

The state board shall not require as a condition of approval of the plan or subsequent revisions, any indirect source review program or other land use control measures.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1085.)
