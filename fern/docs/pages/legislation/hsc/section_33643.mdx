# HSC § 33643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Agency Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 33643

---

## Text

Neither the members of an agency nor any persons executing the bonds are liable personally on the bonds by reason of their issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
