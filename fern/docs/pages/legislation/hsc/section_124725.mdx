# HSC § 124725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4. PRIMARY HEALTH CARE > Chapter: 5. Rural Health Services Development > Article: 3. Health Services and Development Projects

**Citation:** HSC § 124725

---

## Text

Project funding shall be for up to three years. Continuation of funding for a project shall depend on progress toward achieving the goals of the project. The director shall make the final decision to continue or discontinue a project. In evaluating the success of a project, the director shall take into account the number of additional persons who are receiving quality health care as a result of the operation of the project and the improvement in health status of the population served by the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 744, Sec. 7. Effective October 10, 1999. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 744.)
