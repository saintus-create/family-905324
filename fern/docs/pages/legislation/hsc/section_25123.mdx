# HSC § 25123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25123

---

## Text

“Storage” means the holding of hazardous wastes, for a temporary period.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1632, Sec. 9.)
