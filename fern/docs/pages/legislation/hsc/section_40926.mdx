# HSC § 40926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 10. District Plans to Attain State Ambient Air Quality Standards

**Citation:** HSC § 40926

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter restricts the authority of the state board or a district to adopt regulations to control suspended particulate matter, visibility reducing particles, lead, hydrogen sulfide, or sulfates, or their precursors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1568, Sec. 11.)
