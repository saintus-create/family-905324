# HSC § 124014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 5.5. Assistance To Children At Home Demonstration Project

**Citation:** HSC § 124014

---

## Text

In order to most effectively assist children enrolled in the demonstration project, the demonstration project may employ the use of clinic visits, home visits, school visits, inpatient visits, and multidisciplinary conferences, as well as other innovative care management techniques.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 93, Sec. 32. Effective July 7, 2000.)
