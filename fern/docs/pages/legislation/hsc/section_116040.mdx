# HSC § 116040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 5. Swimming Pool Sanitation and Safety

**Citation:** HSC § 116040

---

## Text

Every person operating or maintaining a public swimming pool must do so in a sanitary, healthful and safe manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
