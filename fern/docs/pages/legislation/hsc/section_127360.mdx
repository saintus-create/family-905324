# HSC § 127360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 2. Health Policy Research and Evaluation > Article: 2. Hospitals: Community Benefits

**Citation:** HSC § 127360

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be used to justify the tax-exempt status of a hospital under state law. Nothing in this article shall preclude the department from requiring hospitals to directly report their charity activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 82. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
