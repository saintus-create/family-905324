# HSC § 1596.869

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.869

---

## Text

Directors of combination child day care facilities shall be included in the teacher-child ratio during periods when they are actively supervising children and on the same basis that any other director of a day care center may be included in the teacher-child ratio.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 867, Sec. 5.)
