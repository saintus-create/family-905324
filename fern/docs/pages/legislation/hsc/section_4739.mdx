# HSC § 4739

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 4739

---

## Text

A county sanitation district may employ such sanitation experts, surveyors, counsel, and other persons as are needed to carry into effect any powers of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
