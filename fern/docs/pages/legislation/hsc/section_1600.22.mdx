# HSC § 1600.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Human Whole Blood, Human Whole Blood Derivatives, and Other Biologics > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1600.22

---

## Text

“Stationary” means a nonmobile room or building maintained at a fixed address.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 800, Sec. 3.)
