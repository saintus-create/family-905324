# HSC § 117665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 117665

---

## Text

“Highly communicable diseases” means diseases, such as those caused by organisms classified by the federal Centers for Disease Control and Prevention as risk group 3 organisms or higher.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 564, Sec. 8. (AB 333) Effective January 1, 2015.)
