# HSC § 114107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 5. Cleaning and Sanitizing of Equipment and Utensils

**Citation:** HSC § 114107

---

## Text

(a) Testing equipment and materials shall be provided to adequately measure the applicable sanitization method used during manual or mechanical warewashing.

(b) The concentration of the sanitizing solution shall be accurately determined to ensure proper dosage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
