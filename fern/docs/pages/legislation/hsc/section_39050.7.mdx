# HSC § 39050.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39050.7

---

## Text

“Sacramento district” means the Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District created pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 40950) of Part 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 1.)
