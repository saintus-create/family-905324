# HSC § 4871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 4871

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may determine by resolution that any portion of the unincorporated area of the county not already included in a district is in need of sewer maintenance and should be formed into a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
