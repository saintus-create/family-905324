# HSC § 116855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 5. Water Equipment and Control > Article: 3. Water Treatment Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 116855

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to residential self-regenerating water softeners, as defined in Section 13148 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 403, Sec. 15. (AB 119) Effective January 1, 2014.)
