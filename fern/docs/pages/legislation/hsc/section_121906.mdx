# HSC § 121906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 4. Animal Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121906

## Text

“Person” means any individual, partnership, firm, joint stock company, corporation, association, trust, estate, or other legal entity.

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 377, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
