# HSC § 18040.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 6. Multiple Listing Between Dealers of Manufactured Homes, Mobilehomes, or Commercial Coaches

**Citation:** HSC § 18040.5

---

## Text

The department, after notice and hearing, may suspend or revoke a dealer’s license upon determining that the dealership has committed any of the acts or omissions specified in Section 18062.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1528, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1984.)
