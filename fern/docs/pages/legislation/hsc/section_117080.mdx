# HSC § 117080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 7. Water Supply > Article: 2. Additional Water Supply Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 117080

---

## Text

“Governmental agency,” as used in this article, includes a city, city and county, and district, but does not include a chartered city or city and county.

“Body of water” means a reservoir or lake.

“Owned” means owned or controlled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
