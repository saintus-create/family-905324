# HSC § 25219.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.9. Battery Management: Federal Regulation

**Citation:** HSC § 25219.2

---

## Text

Except as provided in this article, batteries not subject to regulation pursuant to Section 25219.1 shall be managed in compliance with all other requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 575, Sec. 1. Effective September 17, 1996.)
