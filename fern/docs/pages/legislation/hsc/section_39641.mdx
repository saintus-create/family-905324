# HSC § 39641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3.4. Schoolbus Idling and Idling at Schools

**Citation:** HSC § 39641

---

## Text

The state board has adopted regulations establishing toxic control measures to limit schoolbus idling and idling at schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 561, Sec. 1. (SB 124) Effective January 1, 2010.)
