# HSC § 150206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 116. SURPLUS MEDICATION COLLECTION AND DISTRIBUTION

**Citation:** HSC § 150206

---

## Text

The immunities provided in Section 150205 shall not apply in cases of noncompliance with this division, bad faith, or gross negligence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 444, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
