# HSC § 25244.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 11.8. Hazardous Waste Reduction, Recycling, and Treatment

**Citation:** HSC § 25244.01

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), the department’s duty to implement this article is contingent upon, and limited to, the availability of funding.

(b) Subdivision (a) does not apply to Section 25244.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 39, Sec. 40. (SB 1018) Effective June 27, 2012.)
