# HSC § 1327.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 8. Management of Long-Term Health Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1327.2

---

## Text

In the event that the state department proceeds with a receivership petition, the state department shall hold an informational meeting in the affected community for residents, family members, and interested parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 940, Sec. 3.)
