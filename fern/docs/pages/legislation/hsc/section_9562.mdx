# HSC § 9562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 3. Permits and Plans > Article: 2. Application, Permit and Certificate of Occupancy

**Citation:** HSC § 9562

---

## Text

The department shall cause all plans, specifications, and statements to be examined, and, if they conform to the provisions of this part, shall issue a permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
