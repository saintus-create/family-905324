# HSC § 128480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5. Health Professions Education Programs > Article: 4. Vocational Nurse Education Program

**Citation:** HSC § 128480

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to accomplish the following:

(a) Assure an adequate supply of appropriately trained vocational nurses.

(b) Encourage persons from populations that are currently underrepresented in the profession of vocational nursing to enter that profession.

(c) Encourage vocational nurses to work in medically underserved areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 640, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2004. Section operative July 1, 2004, pursuant to Section 128501.)
