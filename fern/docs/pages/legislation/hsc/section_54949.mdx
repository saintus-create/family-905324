# HSC § 54949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 18. Housing Development and Finance > Chapter: 3. Housing Development and Finance Executive Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 54949

---

## Text

The executive committee shall evaluate project monitoring fees to support the long-term viability of the programs and the financial sustainability of projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 68, Sec. 25. (AB 179) Effective July 13, 2026.)
