# HSC § 40841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 3. Subpoenas

**Citation:** HSC § 40841

---

## Text

A subpoena to appear before a hearing board shall be served in the same manner as a subpoena in a civil action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
