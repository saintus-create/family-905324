# HSC § 41973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 6. Gasoline Cargo Tanks

**Citation:** HSC § 41973

---

## Text

Each day that a gasoline cargo tank, which is the subject of a notice to appear issued pursuant to this article, is operated without correction of such violation subsequent to the date of the notice shall constitute a separate offense subject to the penalties provided in Article 3 (commencing with Section 42400) of Chapter 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1134.)
