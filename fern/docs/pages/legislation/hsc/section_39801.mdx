# HSC § 39801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 5. Air Pollution Control Subvention Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39801

---

## Text

The state board shall administer, pursuant to this chapter, such funds as may be appropriated to it for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
