# HSC § 34081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.6. NATURAL DISASTERS > Chapter: 2. Natural Disaster Emergency Shelter Program

**Citation:** HSC § 34081

---

## Text

A local agency or organization receiving a deposit guarantee and grant contract may utilize up to 5 percent of the allocation for costs of administering and operating its security deposit guarantee and grant program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 19, Sec. 24. Effective March 13, 1990.)
