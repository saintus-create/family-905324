# HSC § 111791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 7. Cosmetics > Article: 3.5. Chronic Health Effects of Cosmetics

**Citation:** HSC § 111791

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Safe Cosmetics Act of 2005.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 729, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
