# HSC § 13140.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. State Fire Marshal > Article: 2. The State Board of Fire Services

**Citation:** HSC § 13140.7

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal shall act as chairperson of the board and provide necessary staff services. A vice chairperson shall be selected by majority vote of the members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 142, Sec. 4. (SB 817) Effective January 1, 2022.)
