# HSC § 39032.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39032.5

---

## Text

“Gross polluter” means a vehicle with excess hydrocarbon, carbon monoxide, or oxides of nitrogen emissions as established by the department in consultation with the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 27, Sec. 1. Effective March 30, 1994.)
