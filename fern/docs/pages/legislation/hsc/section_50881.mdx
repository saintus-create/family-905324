# HSC § 50881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 15. Family Housing Demonstration Program > Article: 1. Findings, Definitions, and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50881

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Families Moving to Work Program. The Families Moving to Work Program shall be operated as a component of the Multifamily Housing Program of the department (Chapter 6.7 (commencing with Section 50675)).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 637, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
