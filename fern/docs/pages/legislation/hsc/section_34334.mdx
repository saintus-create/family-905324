# HSC § 34334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Housing Authorities

**Citation:** HSC § 34334

---

## Text

No responsibility of a private landlord of leased housing, or duty of a housing authority to impose a responsibility upon a private landlord of leased housing, shall be implied from Section 34331 or 34332.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1112.)
