# HSC § 44540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 5. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** HSC § 44540

---

## Text

The authority is authorized to incur indebtedness and to issue securities of any kind or class, and to renew the same, provided that all such indebtedness, howsoever evidenced, shall be payable solely from revenues of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 839.)
