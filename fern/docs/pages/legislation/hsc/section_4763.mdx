# HSC § 4763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 4763

---

## Text

All powers of the district shall be exercised by the district board unless otherwise specified.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 596.)
