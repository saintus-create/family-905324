# HSC § 102938

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 6. Death Registration > Article: 6. Sexual Orientation and Gender Identity Data Collection Pilot Program

**Citation:** HSC § 102938

---

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on the date the State Public Health Officer certifies that the final year of data from the pilot program has been submitted to the California Violent Death Reporting System as required under subdivision (b) of Section 102937, and on the January 1 following that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 177, Sec. 1. (AB 1094) Effective January 1, 2022. Conditionally repealed as prescribed by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6, commencing with Section 102935.)
