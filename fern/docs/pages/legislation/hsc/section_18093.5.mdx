# HSC § 18093.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 8. Registration and Titling of Manufactured Homes, Mobilehomes, and Commercial Coaches > Article: 3. Applications for Original Registration and Title

**Citation:** HSC § 18093.5

---

## Text

The manufacturer of a truck camper, the owner or purchaser of which chooses to register it pursuant to Section 18075.7, shall prepare a certificate of origin containing all of the information required by Section 18093 and provide the purchaser with an original copy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1527, Sec. 33.)
