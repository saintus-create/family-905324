# HSC § 53150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 9. HOUSING AND HOMELESS BOND ACT OF 1988 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 53150

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Housing and Homeless Bond Act of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 48, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 84 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
