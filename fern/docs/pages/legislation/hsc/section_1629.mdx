# HSC § 1629

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Human Whole Blood, Human Whole Blood Derivatives, and Other Biologics > Article: 8. Umbilical Cord Blood Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1629

---

## Text

In implementing the program, the department shall make every effort to avoid duplication or conflicts with existing and ongoing programs and to leverage existing resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 516, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 1630.)
