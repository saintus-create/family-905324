# HSC § 8706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 1. Care of Old Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8706

---

## Text

The petition shall be filed with the clerk of the superior court, and the clerk shall fix the time for hearing not less then thirty nor more than sixty days from the date of filing.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
