# HSC § 33615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Community Redevelopment Agency Administrative Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 33615

---

## Text

Each such agency shall file with the legislative body a detailed report of all its transactions, including a statement of all revenues and expenditures, at monthly, quarterly, or annual intervals as the legislative body may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
