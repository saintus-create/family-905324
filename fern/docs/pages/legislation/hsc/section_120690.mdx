# HSC § 120690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 3. SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASE > Chapter: 2. Prenatal Syphilis Tests

**Citation:** HSC § 120690

---

## Text

The blood specimen thus obtained shall be submitted to an approved laboratory for a standard laboratory test for syphilis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
