# HSC § 12551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 12551

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal shall appoint deputies and employees as may be required to carry out the provisions of this part, subject to approval in the annual Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 563, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
