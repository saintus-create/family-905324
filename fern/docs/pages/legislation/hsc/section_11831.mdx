# HSC § 11831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7. Quality Assurance

**Citation:** HSC § 11831

---

## Text

To the maximum extent possible, a reasonable effort to refer a client to other programs, facilities, or services is encouraged for any program or facility which is unable to accept a client after initial assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 919, Sec. 4.)
