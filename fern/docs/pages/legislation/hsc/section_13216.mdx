# HSC § 13216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. High Rise Structures

**Citation:** HSC § 13216

---

## Text

The governing body of any city or county may impose greater restrictions with respect to high-rise structures than are imposed by the building standards published in the State Building Standards Code relating to fire or panic safety or the other regulations of the State Fire Marshal adopted pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152.)
