# HSC § 25299.79

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 7. Cost Recovery, Enforcement, and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.79

---

## Text

The costs specified in subdivision (d) of Section 25299.51 are not recoverable pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1574, Sec. 3. Inoperative January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 25299.81.)
