# HSC § 11817.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 4. State-County Partnership > Article: 4. County Alcohol and Other Drug Program

**Citation:** HSC § 11817.1

---

## Text

The department may reallocate among counties any unexpended federal funds that occur during the fiscal year in programs or services or any allocations either not applied for by a county or not in compliance with this part. Reallocations may be made to counties by amendment to their county contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 36, Sec. 38. (SB 1014) Effective June 27, 2012. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 36.)
