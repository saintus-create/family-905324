# HSC § 39049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39049

---

## Text

“Regional district” means a district created pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 40300) of Part 3.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
