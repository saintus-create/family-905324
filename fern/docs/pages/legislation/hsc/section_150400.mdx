# HSC § 150400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 117. CANCER MEDICATION RECYCLING ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 150400

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Cancer Medication Recycling Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 541, Sec. 2. (SB 310) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 150404.)
