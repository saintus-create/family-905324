# HSC § 41063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 6. Air Quality Improvement Strategy

**Citation:** HSC § 41063

---

## Text

The strategy shall provide for the implementation of all feasible measures to improve transportation system management and reduce or mitigate increases in motor vehicle use within the Sacramento Valley region.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 3.)
