# HSC § 1797.206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 1. Local EMS Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.206

---

## Text

The local EMS agency shall be responsible for implementation of advanced life support systems and limited advanced life support systems and for the monitoring of training programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1246, Sec. 27.)
