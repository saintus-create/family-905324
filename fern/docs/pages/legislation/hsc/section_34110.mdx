# HSC § 34110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Establishment, Creation, and Suspension of Commission > Article: 1. Declaration of Creation and Establishment

**Citation:** HSC § 34110

---

## Text

There is hereby created and established in each community a public body, corporate and politic, known as the community development commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
