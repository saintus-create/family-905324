# HSC § 33133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 3. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 33133

---

## Text

The agency may accept any other assistance from the state or federal government or any public or private source for any redevelopment project within its area of operation or for the agency’s activities, powers, and duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
