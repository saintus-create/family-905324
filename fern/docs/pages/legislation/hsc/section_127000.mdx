# HSC § 127000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 1. DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 127000

---

## Text

There is in the state government, in the Health and Welfare Agency, the Department of Health Care Access and Information.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 30. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
