# HSC § 120120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Chapter: 1.5. COVID-19-Related Public Health Orders or Mandatory Guidance

**Citation:** HSC § 120120

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “COVID-19” means the 2019 novel coronavirus disease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 487, Sec. 1. (SB 336) Effective October 4, 2021. Conditionally inoperative as prescribed by Section 120123. Repealed on January 1 following inoperative date pursuant to Section 120123.)
