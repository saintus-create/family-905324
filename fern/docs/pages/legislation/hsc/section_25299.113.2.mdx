# HSC § 25299.113.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.76. Loans for Replacing, Removing, or Upgrading Underground Storage Tanks

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.113.2

---

## Text

An action by the executive director of the board to impose civil liability under this chapter is subject to review by the board in the same manner as provided for the review by the board of actions of a regional board under Section 13320 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 296, Sec. 13. (AB 3220) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 25299.117.)
