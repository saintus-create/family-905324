# HSC § 114254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 8. Physical Facilities > Article: 3. Poisonous and Toxic Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 114254

---

## Text

Only those insecticides, rodenticides, and other pesticides that are necessary and specifically approved for use in a food facility may be used. The use shall be in accordance with the manufacturer’s instructions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2) by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 42. Effective July 20, 2007.)
