# HSC § 26304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 23. Battery Standards for Electric Bicycles, Powered Mobility Devices, and Storage Batteries

**Citation:** HSC § 26304

---

## Text

A manufacturer, importer, distributor, or retailer of an electric bicycle, powered mobility device, charging system, or storage battery subject to testing under this chapter shall provide, upon request, a true and accurate copy of the test report for the product issued by the accredited testing laboratory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 791, Sec. 1. (SB 1271) Effective January 1, 2025. Operative January 1, 2026, pursuant to Section 26305.)
