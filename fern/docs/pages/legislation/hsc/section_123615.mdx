# HSC § 123615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 2. Maternal Health > Article: 4.2. Maternal Mental Health

**Citation:** HSC § 123615

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Maternal Mental Health Conditions Education, Early Diagnosis, and Treatment Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 773, Sec. 1. (AB 3032) Effective January 1, 2019.)
