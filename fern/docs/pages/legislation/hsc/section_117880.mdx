# HSC § 117880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 117880

## Text

If the department becomes the enforcement agency, it may charge the fees specified in this part.
