# HSC § 1751.80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8.5. California Hospice Licensure Act of 1990 > Article: 2.3. New License Moratorium

**Citation:** HSC § 1751.80

---

## Text

This article shall not be deemed to modify the authority of the department to renew a license pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 1751.85.)
