# HSC § 50768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. Rental Housing Construction Program > Article: 4. Contracts for Development and Construction by Local Housing Authorities

**Citation:** HSC § 50768

---

## Text

The department shall monitor the operation at the rental housing development constructed pursuant to this article, to ensure compliance with grant conditions, contract obligations, and the provisions of this chapter and regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1043.)
