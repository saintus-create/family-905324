# HSC § 25250.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 13.5. Motor Vehicle Brake Friction Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 25250.52

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 2021, any motor vehicle brake friction materials exceeding 5 percent copper by weight shall not be sold in this state, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 307, Sec. 2. (SB 346) Effective January 1, 2011.)
