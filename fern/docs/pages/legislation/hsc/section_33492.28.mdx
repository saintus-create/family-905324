# HSC § 33492.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.28

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “fiscal year” means a year commencing on July 1 and ending on the next June 30.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 33492.69 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 124. Effective January 1, 1995.)
