# HSC § 53190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 10. HOUSING AND HOMELESS BOND ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 2. Home Building and Rehabilitation Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 53190

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this part shall be deposited in the Home Building and Rehabilitation Fund. Moneys in the fund shall be allocated and utilized in accordance with Part 8 (commencing with Section 53130), as added by Senate Bill 1692 of the 1987–88 Regular Session.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 48, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 107 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
