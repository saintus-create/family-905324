# HSC § 1599.68

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.93. Admission Contracts for Long-Term Health Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1599.68

---

## Text

Any long-term health care facility that imposes interest charges on delinquent accounts shall clearly state in the contract of admission the rate of interest so charged and the method of computation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 625, Sec. 1.)
