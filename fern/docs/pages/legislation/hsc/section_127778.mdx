# HSC § 127778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1. Health Professions Planning > Article: 2. Nursing Clinical Placements

**Citation:** HSC § 127778

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2032, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 680, Sec. 1. (AB 1577) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 2, commencing with Sec. 127775.)
