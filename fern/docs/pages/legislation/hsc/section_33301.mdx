# HSC § 33301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 1. Community Prerequisites

**Citation:** HSC § 33301

## Text

The community shall have a planning agency established pursuant to law or charter.
