# HSC § 11834.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.5. Licensing > Article: 3. Offenses

**Citation:** HSC § 11834.30

---

## Text

A person, firm, partnership, association, corporation, or local governmental entity shall not operate, establish, manage, conduct, or maintain an alcohol or other drug recovery or treatment facility to provide the services specified in subdivision (a) of Section 11834.26 within this state without first obtaining a current valid license issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 27, Sec. 31. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2026.)
