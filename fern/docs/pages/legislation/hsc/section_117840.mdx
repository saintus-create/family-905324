# HSC § 117840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 117840

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the program carried out pursuant to this part be fully supported from the fees received pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
