# HSC § 114172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 6. Equipment, Utensils, and Linens > Article: 3. Location and Installation

**Citation:** HSC § 114172

---

## Text

All pressurized cylinders shall be securely fastened to a rigid structure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
