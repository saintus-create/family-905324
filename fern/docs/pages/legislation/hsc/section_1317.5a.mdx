# HSC § 1317.5a

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 7. Other Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1317.5a

---

## Text

A health facility licensed under this chapter that maintains and operates an emergency department may post a notice in a conspicuous place in the emergency department stating substantially the following: WE WILL NOT TOLERATE any form of threatening or aggressive behavior toward our staff. Assaults and batteries against our staff are crimes and may result in a criminal conviction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 937, Sec. 1. (AB 977) Effective January 1, 2025.)
