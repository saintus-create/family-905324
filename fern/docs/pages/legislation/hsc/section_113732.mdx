# HSC § 113732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113732

---

## Text

“Adulterated” means either of the following:

(a) Food that bears or contains any poisonous or deleterious substance that may render the food impure or injurious to health.

(b) Food that is manufactured, prepared, or stored in a manner that deviates from a HACCP plan so as to pose a discernable increase in risk.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
