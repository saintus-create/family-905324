# HSC § 55104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 32. SEISMIC SAFETY BUILDING REHABILITATION LOANS > Part: 2. BONDS

**Citation:** HSC § 55104

---

## Text

Any resolution or resolutions authorizing any bonds or issue thereof shall specify the extent to which revenues resulting from financing provided with proceeds of the bonds so authorized are to be used to secure the bonds and the extent to which the revenues may be used for other purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1602, Sec. 1.)
