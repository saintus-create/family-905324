# HSC § 118440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 1. Articles of Common Use > Article: 3. Common Towels

**Citation:** HSC § 118440

---

## Text

Violation of any provision of this article is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine not exceeding fifty dollars ($50) for each offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
