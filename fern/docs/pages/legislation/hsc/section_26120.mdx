# HSC § 26120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. Toxic Mold > Article: 2. Guidelines for Identification of Molds

**Citation:** HSC § 26120

---

## Text

The department, in consultation with the task force convened pursuant to Section 26101.7, shall adopt mold identification guidelines for the recognition of mold, water damage, or microbial volatile organic compounds in indoor environments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 584, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
