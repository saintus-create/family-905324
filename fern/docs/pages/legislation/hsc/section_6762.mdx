# HSC § 6762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 5. Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 6762

---

## Text

All the provisions of the laws of the state as to the collection of taxes and delinquent taxes, and the enforcement of their payment, so far as applicable, apply to the collection of district taxes.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
