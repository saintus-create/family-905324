# HSC § 33830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 3. Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 33830

---

## Text

If no newspaper is published and circulated in the city, the notice shall be published in a newspaper published in any county in which the city is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
