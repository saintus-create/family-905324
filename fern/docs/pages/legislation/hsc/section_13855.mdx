# HSC § 13855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 4. Existing Board of Directors and Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 13855

---

## Text

A district board shall meet at least once every three months. Meetings of the board are subject to the provisions of the Ralph M. Brown Act, (Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
