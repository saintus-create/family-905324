# HSC § 18024.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 3. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 18024.2

---

## Text

A citation under Section 18024 shall be issued by the department within three years after the act or omission which is the basis for the citation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 752, Sec. 2.)
