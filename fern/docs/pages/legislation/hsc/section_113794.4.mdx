# HSC § 113794.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113794.4

---

## Text

“Frozen food” means a food maintained at a temperature at which all moisture therein is in a solid state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 8. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
