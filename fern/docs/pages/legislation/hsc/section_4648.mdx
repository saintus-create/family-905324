# HSC § 4648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 4648

---

## Text

Upon the recordation of such resolution the territory annexed is a part of the district and is subject to all the liabilities and entitled to all the benefits of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 756.)
