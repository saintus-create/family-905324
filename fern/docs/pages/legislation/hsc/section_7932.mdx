# HSC § 7932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 8. Use of Funds

**Citation:** HSC § 7932

---

## Text

Before any such transfer of funds is made, the cemetery corporation or association shall obtain an order authorizing the transfer from the superior court of the county where the cemetery or portion from which the remains were removed is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
