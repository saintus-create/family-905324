# HSC § 25997.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 15. Safety Glazing Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 25997.4

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful within the state to knowingly install, consent, or cause to be installed, glazing materials in any hazardous location other than as permitted by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 215, Sec. 4. Effective May 26, 1982.)
