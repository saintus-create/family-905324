# HSC § 41855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 3. Agricultural Burning

**Citation:** HSC § 41855

---

## Text

The state board shall determine and designate from meteorological data the days when agricultural burning shall be prohibited within each air basin.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
