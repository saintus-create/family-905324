# HSC § 1179.101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6.3. CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2018 > Chapter: 4. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.101

---

## Text

The people hereby find and declare that, inasmuch as the proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this part are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution, the disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by that article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 2018, by initiative Proposition 4, Sec. 1. Effective December 19, 2018.)
