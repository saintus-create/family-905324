# HSC § 25214.8.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.2.2. Mercury Thermostat Collection Act of 2021

**Citation:** HSC § 25214.8.16

---

## Text

A person who demolishes a building shall remove any mercury-added thermostats from the building before demolition in accordance with all applicable statutes and regulations, and take the out-of-service mercury-added thermostat to a location that is authorized to collect out-of-service mercury-added thermostats.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 703, Sec. 14. (AB 707) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, pursuant to Section 25214.8.19.)
