# HSC § 129761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 129761

---

## Text

The department shall use, to the extent possible, information technology to facilitate the timely performance of its duties and responsibilities under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 284. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
