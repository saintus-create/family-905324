# HSC § 38505.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25.5. CALIFORNIA GLOBAL WARMING SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2006 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 38505.5

---

## Text

(a) “District” has the same meaning as in Section 39025.

(b) This section shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2031, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 135, Sec. 3. (AB 398) Effective July 25, 2017. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, by its own provisions.)
