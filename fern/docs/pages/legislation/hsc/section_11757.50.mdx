# HSC § 11757.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 1. CREATION OF DUTIES > Chapter: 2. Alcohol and Drug Affected Mothers and Infants

**Citation:** HSC § 11757.50

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Alcohol and Drug Affected Mothers and Infants Act of 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1688, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1990.)
