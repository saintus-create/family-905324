# HSC § 18015.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Application and Scope

**Citation:** HSC § 18015.1

---

## Text

All statutory references to “commercial coach” and to “special purpose commercial coach” are hereby deemed to refer to “commercial modular” and to “special purpose commercial modular,” respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 98, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
