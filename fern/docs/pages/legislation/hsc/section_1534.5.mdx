# HSC § 1534.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1534.5

---

## Text

The state department shall provide the office, as defined in subdivision (c) of Section 9701 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, with copies of inspection reports for community care facilities upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1632, Sec. 4.)
