# HSC § 18865.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.3. SPECIAL OCCUPANCY PARKS ACT > Chapter: 4. Application and Scope

**Citation:** HSC § 18865.5

---

## Text

(a) This part does not apply to any apartment house, hotel, or dwelling that is subject to Part 1.5 (commencing with Section 17910).

(b) This part does not apply to electric, gas, or water facilities owned, operated, and maintained by a public utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 434, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2002. Operative January 1, 2004, by Sec. 44 of Ch. 434, as amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1038.)
