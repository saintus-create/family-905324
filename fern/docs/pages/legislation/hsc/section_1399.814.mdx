# HSC § 1399.814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 11.5. Individual Access to Contracts for Health Care Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1399.814

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to require a health benefit plan to offer a contract to an individual if the plan does not otherwise offer contracts to individuals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 810, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
