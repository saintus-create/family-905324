# HSC § 4905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 6. Exclusion

**Citation:** HSC § 4905

---

## Text

Any portion of a district that will not be benefited by remaining in the district may be excluded as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
