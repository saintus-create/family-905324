# HSC § 5001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 5001

---

## Text

It may acquire by gift, purchase, condemnation, or otherwise, all lands, rights of way, or other property necessary therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
