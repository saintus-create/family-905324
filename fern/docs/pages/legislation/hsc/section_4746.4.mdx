# HSC § 4746.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 4746.4

---

## Text

The district shall pay interest to the county on all funds borrowed pursuant to Section 4746.2 at the same rate that the county applies to funds of the district on deposit with the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 419.)
