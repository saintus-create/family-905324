# HSC § 117680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 117680

---

## Text

“Large quantity generator” means a medical waste generator, other than a trauma scene waste management practitioner, that generates 200 or more pounds of medical waste in any month of a 12-month period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 732, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
