# HSC § 6543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 3. Application of Other Statutes

**Citation:** HSC § 6543

---

## Text

The powers and duties conferred by those acts and supplementary acts upon boards, officers, and agents of cities shall be exercised by the respective boards, officers, and agents of the district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
