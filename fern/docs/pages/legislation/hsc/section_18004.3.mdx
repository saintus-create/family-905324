# HSC § 18004.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18004.3

---

## Text

“Franchisor” means any person who manufactures, assembles, or distributes new manufactured homes, mobilehomes, or commercial coaches and who grants a franchise.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1076, Sec. 7.)
