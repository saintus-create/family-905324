# HSC § 55003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 32. SEISMIC SAFETY BUILDING REHABILITATION LOANS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS

**Citation:** HSC § 55003

---

## Text

The local agency may contract with state or federally chartered banks or savings and loan associations for originating or servicing loans authorized by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1602, Sec. 1.)
