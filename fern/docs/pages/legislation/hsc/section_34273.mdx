# HSC § 34273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 34273

---

## Text

A certificate of the appointment or reappointment of any commissioner shall be filed with the clerk and the certificate is conclusive evidence of the due and proper appointment of the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
