# HSC § 52502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 6. CALIFORNIA FIRST-TIME HOME BUYERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 52502

---

## Text

The First-Time Home Buyers Policy Committee is hereby created within the agency for the purpose of exercising those powers and responsibilities of the agency as specifically set forth in this division and, in the exercise of those powers and responsibilities, the acts of the policy committee shall be the acts of the agency.

The members of the policy committee shall be subject to the provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 50900) in the same manner as members of the board of directors of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 320, Sec. 12. Effective June 29, 1982.)
