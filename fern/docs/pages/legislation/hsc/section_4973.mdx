# HSC § 4973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 3. Notice, Hearing, and Election

**Citation:** HSC § 4973

---

## Text

Both the posting and the publication shall be completed at least ten days before the time set for the hearing. Affidavits of publication and of posting shall be filed with the clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
