# HSC § 33439

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 11. Property Disposition, Rehabilitation and Development

**Citation:** HSC § 33439

---

## Text

The agency shall retain controls and establish restrictions or covenants running with land sold or leased for private use for such periods of time and under such conditions as are provided in the redevelopment plan. The establishment of such controls is a public purpose under the provisions of this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1665.)
