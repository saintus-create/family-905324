# HSC § 110010.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 110010.2

---

## Text

“Prescription drug” means any drug limited to prescription use under Section 111470.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 837, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2001.)
