# HSC § 1793.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Continuing Care Contracts > Article: 8. Appointment of Administrators

**Citation:** HSC § 1793.52

---

## Text

The court-appointed administrator shall immediately notify the residents of that appointment and of the status of the continuing care retirement community management.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 920, Sec. 66. Effective January 1, 1996.)
