# HSC § 18026.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 4. Standards

**Citation:** HSC § 18026.1

---

## Text

Units sold to the federal government for use on federal lands are exempt from the requirements of Sections 18025 and 18026.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 442, Sec. 3.)
