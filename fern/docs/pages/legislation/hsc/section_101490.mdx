# HSC § 101490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 4. Additional Administrative Provisions > Article: 5. Released Waste

**Citation:** HSC § 101490

---

## Text

A local officer may charge the responsible party a fee to recover the reasonable and necessary costs incurred in carrying out this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 303.3. Effective September 29, 1996.)
