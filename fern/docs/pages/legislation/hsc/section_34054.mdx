# HSC § 34054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.6. NATURAL DISASTERS > Chapter: 1. Natural Disaster Community Assistance

**Citation:** HSC § 34054

---

## Text

Funds appropriated for the purposes of Section 34052, shall only be expended for rehabilitation or reconstruction of housing and related infrastructure which has been damaged as a result of the natural disaster.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 6, Sec. 3. Effective November 7, 1989.)
