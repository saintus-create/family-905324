# HSC § 127500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 2.6. Health Care Affordability > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Health Care Quality and Affordability Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 47, Sec. 19. (SB 184) Effective June 30, 2022.)
