# HSC § 18906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18906

---

## Text

“Adoption” or “adopt” means, with respect to the procedure for promulgation of a building standard, the final act of a state agency that has the legislative authority and responsibility to take proposed building standards to public hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 897, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
