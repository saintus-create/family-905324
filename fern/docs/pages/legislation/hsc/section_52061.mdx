# HSC § 52061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 5. LOCAL HOUSING FINANCE AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. California Housing Finance Agency Assistance in Issuing City and County Revenue Bonds for Home Mortgages

**Citation:** HSC § 52061

---

## Text

The agency, and any city or county, or a city and a county, may enter into an agreement which provides that the agency sell bonds on behalf of any such city or county, or for such city and such county, for the purpose of providing funds for home mortgages.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1141.)
