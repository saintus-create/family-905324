# HSC § 57053.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 37.5. REPAIR OR MAINTENANCE PROJECTS > Chapter: 2. Repair or Maintenance Projects

**Citation:** HSC § 57053.8

---

## Text

If the consolidated permit applicant fails to provide information required for the processing of the component repair or maintenance project permit applications for a consolidated permit or for the designation of a consolidated permit agency, the time limits set pursuant to paragraph (4) of subdivision (a) of Section 57053.1 shall be tolled until such time as the information is provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 776, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
