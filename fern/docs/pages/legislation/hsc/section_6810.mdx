# HSC § 6810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 8. Reorganization

**Citation:** HSC § 6810

---

## Text

A district organized under Chapter 161, Statutes of 1891, or under the Sanitary District Act of 1919 may be reorganized as a district under this part.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
