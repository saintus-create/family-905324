# HSC § 9636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 2. Structural and Material Requirements of Community Mausoleums and Columbariums

**Citation:** HSC § 9636

---

## Text

All marble floors shall be constructed on a bed of mortar or mastic placed on the floor subslab, with an approved additive to retard efflorescence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1994.)
