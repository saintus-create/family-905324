# HSC § 33650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Agency Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 33650

---

## Text

In connection with the issuance of bonds, and in addition to its other powers, an agency has the powers prescribed in Sections 33651 to 33659, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
