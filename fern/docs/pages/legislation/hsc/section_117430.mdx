# HSC § 117430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 13. GARBAGE AND ONSITE SEWAGE DISPOSAL > Chapter: 4. Waste and Waste Disposal > Article: 1. Septic Tanks

**Citation:** HSC § 117430

---

## Text

Registration shall be only for the unexpired portion of the calendar year in which application is made, and at the end of the calendar year all registrations shall become void and of no effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
