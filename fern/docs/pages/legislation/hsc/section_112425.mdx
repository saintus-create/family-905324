# HSC § 112425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 6. Cold Storage > Article: 3. Licensee Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 112425

---

## Text

Every licensee shall keep an accurate record of receipts and withdrawals of articles of food, and the department shall have free access to these records at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
