# HSC § 2862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 3.5. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 2862

---

## Text

If the nuisance is not abated within the time specified in the notice or an order following the hearing, the district board may abate the nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 54. Effective September 7, 1984.)
