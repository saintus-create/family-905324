# HSC § 39500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 1. Findings, Declarations, and Intent

**Citation:** HSC § 39500

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the State Air Resources Board shall have the responsibility, except as otherwise provided in this division, for control of emissions from motor vehicles and shall coordinate, encourage, and review the efforts of all levels of government as they affect air quality.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
