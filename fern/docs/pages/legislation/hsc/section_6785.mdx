# HSC § 6785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 6. Use of County Assessor’s Roll

**Citation:** HSC § 6785

---

## Text

The board shall by September 1st of each year certify to the county auditor of the county in which the district is situated the rate of taxation fixed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 884, Sec. 11.)
