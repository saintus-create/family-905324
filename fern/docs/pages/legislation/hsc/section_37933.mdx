# HSC § 37933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 13. RESIDENTIAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 3. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** HSC § 37933

---

## Text

Neither the members of the governing board of the local agency nor any person executing the bonds or notes shall be liable personally on the bonds or notes or be subject to any personal liability or accountability by reason of the issuance thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1201.)
