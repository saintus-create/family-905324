# HSC § 19969.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 19969.3

---

## Text

“Design approval agency” is a private organization meeting the requirements of department regulations to perform evaluation of factory-built housing plans and specifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 304, Sec. 1.)
