# HSC § 12685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12685

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to conduct a public display without possessing a valid permit for this purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
