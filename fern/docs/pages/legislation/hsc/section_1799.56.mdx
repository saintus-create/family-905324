# HSC § 1799.56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. The Commission on Emergency Medical Services > Article: 2. Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 1799.56

---

## Text

The commission may utilize technical advisory panels established pursuant to the provisions of Section 1797.133 as are needed to assist in developing standards for emergency medical services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
