# HSC § 38012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. HEALTH AND WELFARE AGENCY—DIRECT SERVICE CONTRACTS REFORM ACT > Chapter: 2. Contract Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 38012

---

## Text

The Department of General Services shall review and approve contracts in accordance with Article 4 (commencing with Section 10335) of Chapter 2 of Part 2 of Division 2 of the Public Contract Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 397. Effective January 1, 2007.)
