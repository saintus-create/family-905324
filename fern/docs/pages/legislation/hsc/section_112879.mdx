# HSC § 112879

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 9. Olive Oil > Article: 1. Olive Oil Grades

**Citation:** HSC § 112879

---

## Text

“Imitation olive oil,” as used in this chapter, means the mixture of any edible oil artificially colored or flavored to resemble olive oil.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 694, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
