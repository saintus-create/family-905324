# HSC § 129851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 129851

---

## Text

Written rules and regulations by the department to clarify the application of the California Building Standards Code pursuant to this chapter shall be made available to the public upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 302. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
