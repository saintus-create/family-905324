# HSC § 25258.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 15. Menstrual Products

**Citation:** HSC § 25258.3

---

## Text

A person shall not manufacture, distribute, sell, or offer for sale in the state a menstrual product that contains regulated PFAS.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 1008, Sec. 2. (AB 2515) Effective January 1, 2025.)
