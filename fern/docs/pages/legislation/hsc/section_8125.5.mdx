# HSC § 8125.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 2. PUBLIC CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8125.5

---

## Text

The City of Simi Valley may survey, lay out, dedicate, own, and operate for burial purposes, or may purchase, or receive by gift or donation, five acres or more of public lands to be used as a public cemetery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 126, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
