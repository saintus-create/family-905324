# HSC § 33492.84

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 5. March Joint Powers Redevelopment Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.84

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the terms “redevelopment agency” and “agency” refer to the March Joint Powers Redevelopment Agency, which is hereby authorized to engage in the redevelopment activities included in and referenced by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1170, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
