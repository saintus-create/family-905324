# HSC § 33621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 3. Redevelopment Revolving Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 33621

---

## Text

For the purpose of raising money to be deposited in the redevelopment revolving fund, the legislative body may appropriate money or the community may issue and sell its general obligation bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
