# HSC § 18077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 8. Registration and Titling of Manufactured Homes, Mobilehomes, and Commercial Coaches > Article: 1. Application and Scope

**Citation:** HSC § 18077

---

## Text

All manufactured home, mobilehome, or commercial coach license fees collected by the department pursuant to Section 18115 shall be deposited in the State Treasury to the credit of the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 699, Sec. 2.5. Effective September 15, 1992.)
