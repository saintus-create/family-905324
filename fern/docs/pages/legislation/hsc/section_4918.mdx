# HSC § 4918

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 7. Dissolution

**Citation:** HSC § 4918

---

## Text

At least ten days prior to the time fixed, the board shall publish a notice of the hearing by one insertion in a newspaper circulated in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
