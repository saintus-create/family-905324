# HSC § 1339.81

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.15. Hospital and Other Provider Requirements for Dissemination of Information Relating to Reproductive Health Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1339.81

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “provider” means any professional person, organization, health facility, or other person or institution licensed by the state to deliver or furnish health care services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 347, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
