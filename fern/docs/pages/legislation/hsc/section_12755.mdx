# HSC § 12755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 3. FLAMETHROWING DEVICES > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 12755

---

## Text

No person shall use or possess a flamethrowing device without a valid flamethrowing device permit issued by the State Fire Marshal pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
