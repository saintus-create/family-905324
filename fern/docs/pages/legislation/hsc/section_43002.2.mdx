# HSC § 43002.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 43002.2

---

## Text

The state board shall waive the provisions of this division on a case-by-case basis for the purpose of allowing the importation of vehicles designed only for use for disabled persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 244, Sec. 1. Effective June 26, 1984.)
