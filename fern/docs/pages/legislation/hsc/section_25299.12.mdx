# HSC § 25299.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.12

---

## Text

“Bodily injury” has the same meaning as used in Subchapter IX (commencing with Section 6991) of Chapter 82 of Title 42 of the United States Code and the regulations adopted pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 5. Effective October 2, 1989.)
