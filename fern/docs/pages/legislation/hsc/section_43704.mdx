# HSC § 43704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Used Motor Vehicles > Article: 3. Heavy-Duty Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** HSC § 43704

---

## Text

Any person who violates Section 2485 of Title 13 of the California Code of Regulations is subject to a minimum civil penalty of three hundred dollars ($300).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 592, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008.)
