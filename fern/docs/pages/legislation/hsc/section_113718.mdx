# HSC § 113718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 113718

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 16350 of the Government Code, all moneys deposited in the Retail Food Safety and Defense Fund shall be transferred to the Food Safety Fund for appropriation and expenditure as specified by Section 110050.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 23, Sec. 29. (AB 1467) Effective June 27, 2012.)
