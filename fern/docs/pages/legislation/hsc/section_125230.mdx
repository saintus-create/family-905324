# HSC § 125230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5. HEREDITARY DISEASES/CONGENITAL DEFECTS > Chapter: 2. Genetic Disease Services > Article: 3. Huntington’s Disease Research and Workshop Grants

## Text

The director may establish any rules or criteria for grants under this article as the director deems necessary.
