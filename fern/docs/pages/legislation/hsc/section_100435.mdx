# HSC § 100435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 3. Additional Administrative Provisions > Article: 4. Fees or Charges for Issuance and Renewal of Documents

**Citation:** HSC § 100435

---

## Text

For the fee specified in Section 26840 of the Government Code, the adjustment authorized by Section 100430 shall apply only to the portion of the fee designated for the State Registrar of Vital Statistics. Any increase in this component of the fee shall be added to the total fee authorized by Section 26840 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
