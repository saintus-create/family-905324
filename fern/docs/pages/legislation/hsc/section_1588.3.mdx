# HSC § 1588.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 5.5. Grants-in-Aid Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1588.3

---

## Text

The grant amount available from funds appropriated through the Budget Act for the Adult Day Health Care Program shall not exceed one hundred twenty-five thousand dollars ($125,000) for a single project.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 108, Sec. 7. Effective July 10, 2000.)
