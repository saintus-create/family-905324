## Text

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 5) and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 4. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (January 1, 2014) for new Article 3.15, commencing with Section 1357.50.)
