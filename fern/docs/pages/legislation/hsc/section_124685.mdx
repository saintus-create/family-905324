# HSC § 124685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4. PRIMARY HEALTH CARE > Chapter: 5. Rural Health Services Development > Article: 2. California Health Services Corps

**Citation:** HSC § 124685

---

## Text

The director may, upon request, provide technical assistance to groups preparing applications for assignment of corps personnel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
