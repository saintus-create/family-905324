# HSC § 110920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110920

---

## Text

No fee established and collected pursuant to this article shall exceed the department’s costs of regulating and enforcing the provisions of this article related to the function for which the fee is established.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
