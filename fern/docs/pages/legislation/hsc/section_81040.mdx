# HSC § 81040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 12. Compensation > Article: 7. State Recovery from Party Liable for the Loss

**Citation:** HSC § 81040

---

## Text

Moneys recovered pursuant to this article shall be deposited in the state account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
