# HSC § 12688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12688

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a person to advertise to sell or transfer any class of fireworks, including agricultural and wildlife fireworks or model rocket motors, unless he or she possesses a valid license or permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 106, Sec. 8. (AB 467) Effective January 1, 2016.)
