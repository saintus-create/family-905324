# HSC § 109540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 4. DRUGS, DEVICES, AND COSMETICS > Chapter: 5. Imitation Controlled Substances > Article: 1. Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 109540

---

## Text

“Distribute” means the actual, constructive, or attempted transfer, delivery, or dispensing to another of an imitation controlled substance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
