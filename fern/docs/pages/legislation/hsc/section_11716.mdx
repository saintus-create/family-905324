# HSC § 11716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. DRUG DEALER LIABILITY ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 11716

---

## Text

No cause of action shall arise based on any act by a defendant which occurred prior to the effective date of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
