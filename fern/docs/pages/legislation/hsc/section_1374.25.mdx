# HSC § 1374.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 5.5. Health Care Service Plan Coverage Contract Changes

**Citation:** HSC § 1374.25

---

## Text

Proof of mailing a notice and the reason therefor to the appropriate entity or individual at the most current policy or plan address shall be sufficient proof of the notice required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 949, Sec. 1.)
