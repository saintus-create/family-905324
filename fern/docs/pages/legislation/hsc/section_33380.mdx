# HSC § 33380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 6. Owner Participation

**Citation:** HSC § 33380

---

## Text

An agency shall permit owner participation in the redevelopment of property in the project area in conformity with the redevelopment plan adopted by the legislative body for the area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
