# HSC § 16101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 3. Earthquake Hazard Mitigation Technologies

**Citation:** HSC § 16101

---

## Text

(a) The Seismic Safety Commission, in cooperation with the State Architect, shall develop a state policy on acceptable levels of earthquake risk for new and existing state-owned buildings and submit their policy to the Legislature for consideration by January 1, 1991.

(b) The State Architect, in conjunction with state agency owners and operators of buildings, shall identify activities carried out within state-owned buildings which are necessary for postearthquake operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 988, Sec. 1.)
