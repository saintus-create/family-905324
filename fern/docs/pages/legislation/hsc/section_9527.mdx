# HSC § 9527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 2. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 9527

---

## Text

Any city or county may, by ordinance, designate any department or officer to enforce any portion of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
