# HSC § 13812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 2. Area

**Citation:** HSC § 13812

---

## Text

The Cortese-Knox-Hertzberg Local Government Reorganization Act of 2000 (Division 3 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 5 of the Government Code) shall govern any change of organization or reorganization of a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 296, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2004.)
