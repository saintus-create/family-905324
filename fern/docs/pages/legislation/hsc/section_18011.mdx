# HSC § 18011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18011

---

## Text

“Rent” means money or other consideration given for the right of use, possession, and occupation of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 975, Sec. 3.)
