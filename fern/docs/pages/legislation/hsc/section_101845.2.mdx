# HSC § 101845.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. San Luis Obispo County Hospital Authority > Article: 2. Hospital Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 101845.2

---

## Text

The hospital authority, the county, or both, may engage in marketing, advertising, and promotion of the medical and health care services made available to the community at General Hospital and the Family Care Centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 899, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
