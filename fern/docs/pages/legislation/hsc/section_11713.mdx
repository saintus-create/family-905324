# HSC § 11713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. DRUG DEALER LIABILITY ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 11713

---

## Text

(a) A plaintiff under this division, subject to subdivision (c), may request an ex parte prejudgment attachment order from the court against all assets of a defendant sufficient to satisfy a potential award.

(b) Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 485.010) of Title 6.5 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure shall apply to any request under this subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
