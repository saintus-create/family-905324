# HSC § 20081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 20081

---

## Text

In addition to the duties specified in this chapter, the district board shall perform such other duties as are proper and necessary to carry out this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
