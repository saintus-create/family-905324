# HSC § 110810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110810

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited as, the California Organic Food and Farming Act.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 302, Sec. 9. (SB 406) Effective September 29, 2020.)
