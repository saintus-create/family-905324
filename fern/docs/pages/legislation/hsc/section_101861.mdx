# HSC § 101861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 6. Other > Article: 2. Public Meetings

**Citation:** HSC § 101861

---

## Text

Meetings of the corporation shall be open and public, and all persons shall be permitted to attend, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 927, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
