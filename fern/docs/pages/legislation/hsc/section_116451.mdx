# HSC § 116451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 4. California Safe Drinking Water Act > Article: 5. Public Notification

**Citation:** HSC § 116451

---

## Text

If user notification is required pursuant to Section 116450, the department shall make a reasonable effort to ensure that notification is given.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 298, Sec. 14. (AB 1540) Effective January 1, 2010.)
