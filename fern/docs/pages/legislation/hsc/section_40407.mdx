# HSC § 40407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40407

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “electric plant” means an electric plant as defined in Section 217 of the Public Utilities Code, whether publicly or privately owned or operated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 665, Sec. 1.)
