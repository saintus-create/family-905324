# HSC § 6690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 5. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 6690

---

## Text

The board may cause refunding bonds to be issued for the purpose of refunding any or all outstanding bonds of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 304.)
