# HSC § 111580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 6. Drugs and Devices > Article: 5. New Drugs or Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 111580

---

## Text

When the department finds that there is an imminent hazard to the public health, it may suspend the approval for the application immediately.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
