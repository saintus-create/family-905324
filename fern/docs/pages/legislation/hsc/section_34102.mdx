# HSC § 34102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 34102

---

## Text

“Redevelopment agency” means a redevelopment agency created pursuant to the Community Redevelopment Law, Part 1 (commencing with Section 33000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
