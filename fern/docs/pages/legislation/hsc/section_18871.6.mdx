# HSC § 18871.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.3. SPECIAL OCCUPANCY PARKS ACT > Chapter: 8. Regulations—General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 18871.6

---

## Text

The department shall adopt regulations to ensure adequate animal control within parks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 434, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2002. Operative January 1, 2004, by Sec. 44 of Ch. 434, as amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1038.)
