# HSC § 6445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** HSC § 6445

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may grant further continuances, by order entered in its minutes, to the end that a full hearing may be had.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
