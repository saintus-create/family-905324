# HSC § 101980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6. OTHER > Chapter: 1. Voluntary Health Facility Philanthropic Support Act

**Citation:** HSC § 101980

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Voluntary Health Facility and Clinic Philanthropic Support Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 101800 by Stats. 1999, Ch. 950, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
