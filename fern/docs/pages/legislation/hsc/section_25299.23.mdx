# HSC § 25299.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.23

---

## Text

“Property damage” has the same meaning as used in Subchapter IX (commencing with Section 6991) of Chapter 82 of Title 42 of the United States Code and the regulations adopted pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25299.22 by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1217, Sec. 9. Effective September 24, 1990.)
