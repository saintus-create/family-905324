# HSC § 5473.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 4. Sanitation and Sewerage Systems

**Citation:** HSC § 5473.6

---

## Text

The tax collector shall include the amount of the charges on bills for taxes levied against the respective lots and parcels of land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 5473.5 by Stats. 1953, Ch. 862.)
