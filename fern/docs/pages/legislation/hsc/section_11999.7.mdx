# HSC § 11999.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.8. SUBSTANCE ABUSE TREATMENT FUNDING

**Citation:** HSC § 11999.7

---

## Text

Local Government Authority to Control Location of Drug Treatment Programs

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no community drug treatment program may receive any funds from the Substance Abuse Treatment Trust Fund unless the program agrees to make its facilities subject to valid local government zoning ordinances and development agreements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2000, by initiative Proposition 36, Sec. 7. Operative July 1, 2001, pursuant to Sec. 8 of Prop. 36.)
