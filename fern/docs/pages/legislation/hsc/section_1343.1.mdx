# HSC § 1343.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 1343.1

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any program developed under the authority of Chapter 8.75 (commencing with Section 14591) of Part 3 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 367, Sec. 2. (AB 574) Effective January 1, 2012.)
