# HSC § 9511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9511

---

## Text

“Type I Construction” includes the type of construction designated and specified as Type I Building Construction in the Uniform Building Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
