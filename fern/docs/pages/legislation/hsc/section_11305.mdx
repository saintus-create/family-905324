# HSC § 11305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 5. Use of Controlled Substances > Article: 5. Controlled Substance Checking Services

**Citation:** HSC § 11305

---

## Text

Utilizing a controlled substance checking service shall not serve as the basis for, or a fact contributing to the existence of, reasonable suspicion or probable cause for a law enforcement officer to conduct a search or seizure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 701, Sec. 2. (AB 2136) Effective January 1, 2025.)
