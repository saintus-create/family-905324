# HSC § 1439.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.45. Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, and Transgender Long-Term Care Facility Residents’ Bill of Rights

**Citation:** HSC § 1439.54

---

## Text

A violation of this chapter shall be treated as a violation under Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 1250), Chapter 2.4 (commencing with Section 1417), or Chapter 3.2 (commencing with Section 1569).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 483, Sec. 3. (SB 219) Effective January 1, 2018.)
