# HSC § 4911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 6. Exclusion

**Citation:** HSC § 4911

---

## Text

Upon the exclusion of any territory from the district all property acquired for the district shall remain vested in the county and be used for the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
