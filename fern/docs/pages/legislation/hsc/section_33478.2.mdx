# HSC § 33478.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 14.5. Merger of Redevelopment Project Areas in the Cities of Richmond and Pittsburg

**Citation:** HSC § 33478.2

---

## Text

Prior to merging project areas pursuant to Section 33478, the redevelopment agency shall notify the department of its intention to merge its project areas, which shall occur no later than 30 days prior to adoption of the ordinance which provides for merger.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 512.)
