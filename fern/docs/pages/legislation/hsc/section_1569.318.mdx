# HSC § 1569.318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.318

---

## Text

Every residential care facility for the elderly shall abide by the provisions of the Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, and Transgender Long-Term Care Facility Residents’ Bill of Rights (Chapter 2.45 (commencing with Section 1439.50)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 483, Sec. 4. (SB 219) Effective January 1, 2018.)
