# HSC § 51661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Loan Insurance

**Citation:** HSC § 51661

---

## Text

The agency may provide for, and require attendance at, homeownership counseling and training courses as a condition to the insurance of loans for the purchase or refinancing of residential structures under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993.)
