# HSC § 125520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 7. CHRONIC DISEASE SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Kidney Diseases-Chronic Uremia

**Citation:** HSC § 125520

---

## Text

The dialysis centers may also receive and make use of any outside source of funds that may become available from federal, voluntary, philanthropic, or other sources in order to augment state funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
