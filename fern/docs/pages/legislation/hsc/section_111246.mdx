# HSC § 111246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 6. Drugs and Devices > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 111246

---

## Text

Commencing January 1, 2002, any product used for the treatment of lice or scabies in human beings that contains the pesticide Lindane shall not be used or sold in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 326, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
