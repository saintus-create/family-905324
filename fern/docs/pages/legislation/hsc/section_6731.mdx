# HSC § 6731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 3. Equalization of Assessments by District Assessor

**Citation:** HSC § 6731

---

## Text

If the district assessor has returned the assessment roll for the year the board shall proceed to equalize the assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
