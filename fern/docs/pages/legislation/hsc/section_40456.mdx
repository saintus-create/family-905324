# HSC § 40456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 40456

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 43845, the south coast district shall not require any employer to charge its employees for parking.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 335, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
