# HSC § 9591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 4. Inspection and Approval

**Citation:** HSC § 9591

---

## Text

The department shall inspect or cause the work to be inspected, and shall issue a certificate of occupancy if the work has been performed in accordance with the approved plans, specifications, and statements, and in conformity with the provisions of this part; and if not, it shall refuse to issue the certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
