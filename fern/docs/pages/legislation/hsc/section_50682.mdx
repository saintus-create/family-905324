# HSC § 50682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7. Special Housing Programs for the Developmentally Disabled, Mentally Disordered, and Physically Disabled

**Citation:** HSC § 50682

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “developmentally disabled” means affected by a disability specified in subdivision (a) of Section 4512 of the Welfare and Institutions Code rendering the affected person eligible to participate in programs of rehabilitation, education, or social services conducted by or on behalf of a public agency, and also means persons affected by such a disability.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1020, Sec. 6.)
