# HSC § 34213.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 34213.1

---

## Text

“Construction loan” means a short-term loan secured by real property, made for costs incurred in construction or rehabilitation of a housing project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1309, Sec. 2.)
