# HSC § 79950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 8. Cost Recovery > Article: 13. Liens

**Citation:** HSC § 79950

---

## Text

The department shall not be considered a responsible party for a hazardous substance release site because a claim and lien is imposed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
