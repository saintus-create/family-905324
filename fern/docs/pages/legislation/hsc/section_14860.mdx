# HSC § 14860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** HSC § 14860

---

## Text

Every officer of a fire company or department who willfully issues or causes to be issued any certificate of exemption to a person not entitled to it, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2009.)
