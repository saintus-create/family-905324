# HSC § 12760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 3. FLAMETHROWING DEVICES > Chapter: 3. Enforcement and Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 12760

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal shall seize any flamethrowing device in the possession of any person who does not have a valid flamethrowing device permit issued pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
