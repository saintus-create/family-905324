# HSC § 40461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Plan

**Citation:** HSC § 40461

---

## Text

The plan, as adopted and revised by the south coast district board, shall be in lieu of the basinwide air pollution control plan required pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 41600) of Part 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1085.)
