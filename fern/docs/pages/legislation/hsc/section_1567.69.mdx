# HSC § 1567.69

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 9.5. Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes

**Citation:** HSC § 1567.69

---

## Text

This article does not interfere with the authority of the State Department of Social Services to temporarily suspend or revoke the license of an enhanced behavioral supports home pursuant to Section 1550.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 277. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
