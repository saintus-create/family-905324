# HSC § 40311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 2. City Selection Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 40311

---

## Text

Where a regional district may transact business and exercise its powers only in a portion of a county, the membership of the city selection committee of such county, for purposes of this chapter, shall consist only of the representatives from those cities within that portion of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
