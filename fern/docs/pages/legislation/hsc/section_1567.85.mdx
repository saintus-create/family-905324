# HSC § 1567.85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 9.7. Community Crisis Home Licensure

**Citation:** HSC § 1567.85

---

## Text

If applicable, a community crisis home shall be in compliance with Section 1531.15 and the applicable regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 30, Sec. 7. (SB 856) Effective June 20, 2014.)
