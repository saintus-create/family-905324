# HSC § 14836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 14836

---

## Text

Every fire company regularly organized may adopt a seal, having upon it the arms of the State, and the name of the company to which it belongs.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
