# HSC § 116085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 6. Ocean Water-Contact Sports

**Citation:** HSC § 116085

---

## Text

Every person who violates any rule or regulation adopted pursuant to this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
