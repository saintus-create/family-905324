# HSC § 104569

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 1.5. Comprehensive Perinatal Outreach Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104569

---

## Text

Funds appropriated for purposes of this program for a fiscal year shall be available for expenditure without regard to fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 310, Sec. 21. Effective August 19, 1998.)
