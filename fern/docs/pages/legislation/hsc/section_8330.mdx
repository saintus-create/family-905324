# HSC § 8330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 4. Records

**Citation:** HSC § 8330

---

## Text

A record shall be kept of every interment showing the date the human remains were received, the date of interment, the name and age of the person interred, when these particulars can be conveniently obtained, and the plot in which interment was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
