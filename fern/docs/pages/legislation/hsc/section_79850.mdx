# HSC § 79850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 8. Cost Recovery > Article: 8. Liability of Operator for Household Hazardous Waste or Used Oil Collection Program

**Citation:** HSC § 79850

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 79845, this article does not affect or modify the obligations or liabilities of a person imposed pursuant to state or federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
