# HSC § 9046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 4. PUBLIC CEMETERY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 9046

---

## Text

A board of trustees may require any employee or officer to be bonded. The district shall pay the cost of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 57, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
