# HSC § 1527.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 2.5. Foster Home and Small Family Home Insurance Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 1527.35

---

## Text

The fund shall not be liable for any loss arising out of the dishonest, fraudulent, criminal, or intentional act of any person if the date of the loss is prior to July 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 494, Sec. 3. (SB 522) Effective January 1, 2014.)
