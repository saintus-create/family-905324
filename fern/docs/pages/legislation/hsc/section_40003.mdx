# HSC § 40003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40003

---

## Text

A county may be in two or more districts, but not in two or more county districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
