# HSC § 33381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 6. Owner Participation

**Citation:** HSC § 33381

---

## Text

If the redevelopment plan adopted provides for participation in the redevelopment of property in the area by the owners of such property, and the owners fail or refuse to enter into a binding agreement for participation in accordance with the rules adopted by the agency pursuant to Section 33339, the alternative provisions provided for in Section 33340 become effective as the official redevelopment plan of the project area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
