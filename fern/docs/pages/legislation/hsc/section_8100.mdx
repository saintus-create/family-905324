# HSC § 8100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 8100

---

## Text

The definitions set forth in Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 7000) of Part 1 of Division 7 shall be applicable to this division.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 436, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2002.)
