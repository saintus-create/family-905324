# HSC § 1207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1207

---

## Text

The state department shall inspect and license clinics, and shall inspect and approve clinics to offer special services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1147.)
