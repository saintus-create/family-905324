# HSC § 40803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40803

---

## Text

No officer or employee of the district, or of the county in the case of a county district, shall be a member of the district hearing board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
