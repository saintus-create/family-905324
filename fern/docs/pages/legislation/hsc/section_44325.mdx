# HSC § 44325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 6. AIR TOXICS “HOT SPOTS” INFORMATION AND ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 2. Facilities Subject to this Part

**Citation:** HSC § 44325

---

## Text

Any solid waste disposal facility in compliance with Section 41805.5 is in compliance with the emissions inventory requirements of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1252, Sec. 1. Section operative July 1, 1988, pursuant to Section 44384.)
