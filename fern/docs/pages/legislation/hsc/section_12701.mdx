# HSC § 12701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 8. Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 12701

---

## Text

A person is guilty of a separate offense for each day during which he or she commits, continues, or permits a violation of this part, or any order or regulation issued pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 371. Effective January 1, 2007.)
