# HSC § 127665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 7. University of California Assessment on Legislation Proposing Mandated Benefits or Services

**Citation:** HSC § 127665

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on July 1, 2033, and, as of January 1, 2034, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 318, Sec. 3. (SB 439) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2034, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 7, commencing with Section 127660.)
