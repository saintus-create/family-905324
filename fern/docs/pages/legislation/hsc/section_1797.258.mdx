# HSC § 1797.258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 2. Local Emergency Medical Services Planning

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.258

---

## Text

After the submission of an initial trauma care system plan, a local EMS agency which has implemented a trauma care system shall annually submit to the authority an updated plan which identifies all changes, if any, to be made in the trauma care system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1735, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1984.)
