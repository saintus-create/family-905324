# HSC § 6972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 6972

---

## Text

After the formation of the zone pursuant to this article, all taxes levied to carry out the purposes of the zone shall be levied exclusively upon the property taxable in the zone by the public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1125.)
