# HSC § 9509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9509

---

## Text

“National Electrical Code” means the 1990 Edition of the National Electrical Code, with 1992 amendments, adopted and published by the National Fire Protection Association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
