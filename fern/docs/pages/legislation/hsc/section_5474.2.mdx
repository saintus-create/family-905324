# HSC § 5474.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 4. Sanitation and Sewerage Systems

**Citation:** HSC § 5474.2

---

## Text

At the time stated in the notice the legislative body shall hear and consider all objections or protests, if any, to the imposition of the fees or charges as set forth in said notice and may continue the hearing from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 578.)
