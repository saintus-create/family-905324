# HSC § 19136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 2a. Building Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 19136

---

## Text

The work authorized by a permit shall be performed only in accordance with the application, plans, and specifications filed by the permittee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 301.)
