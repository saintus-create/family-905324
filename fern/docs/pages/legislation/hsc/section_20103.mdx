# HSC § 20103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20103

---

## Text

The notice shall specify the time and place of the election, and the amount required for the establishment and equipment of the police department.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
