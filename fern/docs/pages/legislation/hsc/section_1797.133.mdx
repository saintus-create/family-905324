# HSC § 1797.133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 3. Coordination With Other State Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.133

---

## Text

The director may appoint select resource committees of experts and may contract with special medical consultants for assistance in the implementation of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 248, Sec. 129.)
