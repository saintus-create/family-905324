# HSC § 101140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Local Health Officers and Local Health Departments > Article: 4. Dental Professionals

**Citation:** HSC § 101140

---

## Text

The dentist or dental hygienist shall attend to dental conditions of the county, as the board of supervisors may assign. Compensation for the dentist or dental hygienist shall be determined by that board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 302. Effective September 29, 1996.)
