# HSC § 40104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. County Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 40104

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a county may delegate air pollution rulemaking and enforcement duties to a duly created joint powers authority established for air pollution control purposes of which the county is a member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1201, Sec. 6. Conditionally operative by Sec. 1 of Ch. 1201, as amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 765.)
