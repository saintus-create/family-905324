# HSC § 18001.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18001.6

---

## Text

“Building” is any permanent structure built for the support, shelter, or enclosure of persons, animals, chattel, or property of any kind.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 975, Sec. 3.)
