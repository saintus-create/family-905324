# HSC § 50704.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 8. Housing-Related Parks Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50704.5

---

## Text

The department shall adopt guidelines for the operation of the program. The guidelines shall not be subject to the requirements of Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 641, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
