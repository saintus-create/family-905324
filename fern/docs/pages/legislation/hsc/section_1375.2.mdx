# HSC § 1375.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 6. Operation and Renewal Requirements and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 1375.2

---

## Text

On and after October 1, 1977, every plan operating under a transitional license shall have a fiscally sound operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 818.)
