# HSC § 34376

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 5.5. Loans to Tax-Exempt Organizations

**Citation:** HSC § 34376

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that it would be beneficial to empower housing authorities to issue tax-exempt revenue bonds for the purpose of lending the proceeds to nonprofit organizations exempt from federal income taxation pursuant to Section 501(c)(3) of the Internal Revenue Code of 1986, as amended (26 U.S.C. Sec. 501(c)(3)), for the housing purposes specified in Section 34377.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1610, Sec. 3.)
