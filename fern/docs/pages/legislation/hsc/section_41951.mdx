# HSC § 41951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 5. Gasoline Vapor Control

**Citation:** HSC § 41951

---

## Text

A “pressure tank” is a tank which maintains working pressure sufficient at all times to prevent hydrocarbon vapor or gas loss to the atmosphere.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
