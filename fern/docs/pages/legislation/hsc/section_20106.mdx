# HSC § 20106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20106

---

## Text

Within twenty-four hours after the election, the judges of the election shall report and certify to the district board the number of votes cast for and against the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
