# HSC § 19134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 2a. Building Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 19134

---

## Text

The enforcement agency may approve changes in any application, plans, or specifications previously approved by it.
