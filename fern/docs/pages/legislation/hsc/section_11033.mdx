# HSC § 11033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11033

---

## Text

As used in this division, except as otherwise defined, the term “isomer” includes optical and geometrical (diastereomeric) isomers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 21, Sec. 2. Effective April 2, 1985.)
