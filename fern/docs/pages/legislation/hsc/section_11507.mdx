# HSC § 11507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 9. Collection and Disposition of Fines

**Citation:** HSC § 11507

---

## Text

The official bond of any judge or magistrate is liable for his failure to transmit the fines or forfeitures imposed by him under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
