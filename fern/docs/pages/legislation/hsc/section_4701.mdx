# HSC § 4701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 4701

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this chapter, means any county sanitation district formed pursuant to this chapter or pursuant to any law which it supersedes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
