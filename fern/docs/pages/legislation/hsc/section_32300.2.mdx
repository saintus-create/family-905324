# HSC § 32300.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 32300.2

---

## Text

Bonds may be issued by a district for the purpose of refunding any or all of the outstanding bonds or other indebtedness of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 910.)
