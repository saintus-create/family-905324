# HSC § 129942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 5. Building Safety Board

**Citation:** HSC § 129942

---

## Text

(a) Only appointed members shall vote at board meetings.

(b) Appointed members, ex officio members, and others appointed to a committee, including an appeal committee, by the chair, may vote at committee meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
