# HSC § 1357.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.1. Small Employer Group Access to Contracts for Health Care Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.01

---

## Text

Every health care service plan offering plan contracts to small employer groups shall in addition to complying with the provisions of this chapter and the rules adopted thereunder comply with the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1128, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1128.)
