# HSC § 127375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 2. Health Policy Research and Evaluation > Article: 3. The Medical Equity Disclosure Act

**Citation:** HSC § 127375

---

## Text

The Department of Health Care Access and Information shall adopt any rules, regulations, or informal guidance necessary to further the objectives of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 751, Sec. 2. (AB 1204) Effective January 1, 2022.)
