# HSC § 104890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 4. Diethylstilbesterol (DES)

**Citation:** HSC § 104890

---

## Text

The department shall present to the Legislature, on or before December 1, 1982, information on the diethylstilbestrol program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
