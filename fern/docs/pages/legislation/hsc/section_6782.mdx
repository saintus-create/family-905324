# HSC § 6782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 6. Use of County Assessor’s Roll

**Citation:** HSC § 6782

---

## Text

Following the board’s election, the county auditor shall on or about the third Monday of August of each year transmit to the board a written statement showing the total value of all property in the district, which value shall be ascertained from the assessment roll used by the county for that year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 426.)
