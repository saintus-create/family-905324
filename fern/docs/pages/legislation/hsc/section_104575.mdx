# HSC § 104575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 2. Nutrition > Article: 1. California Nutrition Monitoring Development Act

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the “California Nutrition Monitoring Development Act of 1986.”
