# HSC § 33111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 2. Appointment, Compensation, and Removal of Agency Members

**Citation:** HSC § 33111

---

## Text

A member may not be an elective officer or an employee of the community, but, notwithstanding any other law, he may be a member, commissioner, or employee of any other agency or authority of, or created for, the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
