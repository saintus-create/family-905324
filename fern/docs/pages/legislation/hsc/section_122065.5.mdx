# HSC § 122065.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Sale of Dogs and Cats > Article: 1. Sale of Dogs by Breeders

**Citation:** HSC § 122065.5

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for a breeder to primarily house a dog on wire flooring.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 350, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
