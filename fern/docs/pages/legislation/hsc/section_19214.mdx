# HSC § 19214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 8. Water Heater Strapping and Installation

**Citation:** HSC § 19214

---

## Text

Any person who violates Section 19212 or 19213 shall be deemed to have violated a provision of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 17000) of Part 2 of Division 7 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 951, Sec. 1.)
