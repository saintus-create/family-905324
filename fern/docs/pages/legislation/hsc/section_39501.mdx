# HSC § 39501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 1. Findings, Declarations, and Intent

**Citation:** HSC § 39501

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the state board maximize economic cobenefits by conditioning eligibility to participate in grant, loan, voucher, or other incentive programs to purchase new drayage and short-haul trucks on compliance with the labor standards required by Chapter 3.6 (commencing with Section 39680).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 748, Sec. 2. (AB 794) Effective January 1, 2022.)
