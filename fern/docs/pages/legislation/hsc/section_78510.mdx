# HSC § 78510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 3. General Powers and Duties > Article: 7. Laboratories

**Citation:** HSC § 78510

---

## Text

The analysis of any material that is required to demonstrate compliance with this part shall be performed by a laboratory accredited by the state board pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 100825) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of Division 101.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
