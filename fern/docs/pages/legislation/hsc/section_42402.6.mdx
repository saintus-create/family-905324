# HSC § 42402.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 3. Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 42402.6

---

## Text

(a) Penalties prescribed pursuant to Sections 42402, 42402.1, 42402.2, 42402.3, 42402.4, and 42402.5 for violations may be multiplied by a factor of not more than three if both of the following occur:

(1) The emission is from a Title V source.

(2) The emission contains or includes one or more air contaminants, as identified by the state board pursuant to Section 39657, and as defined in Section 39013, or as listed in the Table of Standards pursuant to Section 70200 of Title 17 of the California Code of Regulations.

(b) For purposes of this section, “source” means an establishment that is located on one or more contiguous or adjacent properties.

(c) In assessing penalties, health impacts, community disruptions, the timeliness and accuracy of the notifications from the violator, and other circumstances related to the violation shall be considered, pursuant to Section 42403.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 300, Sec. 1. (AB 1465) Effective January 1, 2025.)
