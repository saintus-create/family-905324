# HSC § 11751.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 1. CREATION OF DUTIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 11751.4

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to ensure the integrity of state alcohol and drug programs.
