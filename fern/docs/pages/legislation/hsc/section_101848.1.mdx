# HSC § 101848.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. San Luis Obispo County Hospital Authority > Article: 2. Hospital Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 101848.1

---

## Text

Information maintained in the management information system or in other filing and records maintenance systems that is confidential and protected by law shall not be disclosed except as provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 899, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
