# HSC § 17921.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 2. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 17921.6

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 18930 and 18949.5, the department shall prepare and adopt minimum standards regulating the use and application of cellular concrete as it determines are reasonably necessary for the protection of life and property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 17921.5 by Stats. 2007, Ch. 499, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008.)
