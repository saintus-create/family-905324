# HSC § 11839.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 2. Narcotic Treatment Program Body Fluids Testing

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.24

---

## Text

Substance use testing for narcotic treatment programs operating in the state shall be performed only by a laboratory approved and licensed by the State Department of Public Health for the performance of those tests.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 63. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
