# HSC § 44560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 2. Supplemental Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 44560

---

## Text

This division, being necessary for the welfare of the state and its inhabitants, shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes hereof.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
