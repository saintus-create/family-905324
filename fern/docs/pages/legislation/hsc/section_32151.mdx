# HSC § 32151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 3. Hearing Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 32151

---

## Text

The secretary of the board of directors or the hospital administrator shall mail notice of the action or decision to the affected applicant or medical staff member within the time specified in the applicable bylaw or rule.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1358, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
