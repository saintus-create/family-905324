# HSC § 13223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Emergency Procedure Information

**Citation:** HSC § 13223

---

## Text

Any person who violates the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor and upon conviction, shall be punished by a fine of not to exceed ten thousand dollars ($10,000) or imprisonment in the county jail not to exceed six months, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 557.)
