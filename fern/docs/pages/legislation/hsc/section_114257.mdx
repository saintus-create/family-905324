# HSC § 114257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 8. Physical Facilities > Article: 5. Premises and Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 114257

---

## Text

All premises of a food facility shall be kept clean fully operative, and in good repair.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 43. Effective July 20, 2007.)
