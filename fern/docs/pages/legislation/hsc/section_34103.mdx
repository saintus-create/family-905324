# HSC § 34103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 34103

---

## Text

“Housing authority” means a housing authority created pursuant to the Housing Authorities Law, Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 34200) of Part 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
