# HSC § 34148

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 34148

---

## Text

The commission may accept any other assistance from the state or federal government or any public or private source for any of the commission’s activities, powers, and duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
