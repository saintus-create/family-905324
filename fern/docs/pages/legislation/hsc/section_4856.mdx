# HSC § 4856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 9. Special Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 4856

---

## Text

The district board shall thereafter in each year, prior to the time of fixing the county tax rate, furnish to the board of supervisors a written statement of the amount of tax revenue necessary to maintain and operate any work or improvements within the zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 794.)
