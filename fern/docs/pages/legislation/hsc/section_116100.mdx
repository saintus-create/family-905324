# HSC § 116100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 116100

---

## Text

As used in Article 5 (commencing with Section 116185) of Chapter 2, “department” means the State Department of Health Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
