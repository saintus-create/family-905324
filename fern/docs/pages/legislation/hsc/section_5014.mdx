# HSC § 5014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 5014

---

## Text

The bids shall be delivered to the clerk. The governing body shall, in open session, publicly open, examine, and declare them.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
