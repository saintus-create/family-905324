# HSC § 1179.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6. CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2004 > Chapter: 2. The Children’s Hospital Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.22

---

## Text

The authority is authorized to award grants to any children’s hospital for purposes of funding projects, as defined in subdivision (g) of Section 1179.11.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 2, 2004, by initiative Proposition 61.)
