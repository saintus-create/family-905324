# HSC § 102525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 4. Delayed Registration of Birth > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 102525

---

## Text

This chapter is not exclusive of Chapters 3 (commencing with Section 102400) and 12 (commencing with Section 103450), but offer an alternative method of establishing a record of birth.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
