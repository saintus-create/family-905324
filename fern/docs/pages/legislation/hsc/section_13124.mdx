# HSC § 13124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. State Fire Marshal > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 13124

---

## Text

The name of any chemical, chemical concern or flame-retardant application concern whose name has been removed from the approved list shall not be restored to the approved list for a period of 90 days from the date of the removal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 332, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1997.)
