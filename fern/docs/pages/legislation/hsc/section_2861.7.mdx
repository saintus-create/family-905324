# HSC § 2861.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 3.5. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 2861.7

---

## Text

Any recurrence of the nuisance may be deemed to be a continuation of the original nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 53. Effective September 7, 1984.)
