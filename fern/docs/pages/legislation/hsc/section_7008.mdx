# HSC § 7008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7008

---

## Text

“Crematory and columbarium” means a building or structure containing both a crematory and columbarium.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
