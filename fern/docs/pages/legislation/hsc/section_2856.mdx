# HSC § 2856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 3.5. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 2856

---

## Text

Any nuisance may be abated in any action or proceeding by any remedy provided by this article or any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 465.)
