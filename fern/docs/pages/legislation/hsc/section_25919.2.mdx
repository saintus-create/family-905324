# HSC § 25919.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10.4. Asbestos Notification

**Citation:** HSC § 25919.2

---

## Text

“Building,” as used in this chapter, means all or part of any “public and commercial building,” as defined in Section 2642 of Title 15 of the United States Code, as that section reads on January 1, 1989, except that “building” shall not mean residential dwellings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25920 (as amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 948) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 216, Sec. 69.)
