# HSC § 35803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 6. FINANCIAL DISCRIMINATION > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations of Purpose and Policy

**Citation:** HSC § 35803

---

## Text

This part shall be deemed an exercise of the police power of the state for the protection of the health, welfare, and peace of the people of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1140.)
