# HSC § 127673.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 8.5. Health Care Payments Data Program

**Citation:** HSC § 127673.6

---

## Text

The department shall develop an information security program that uses existing state standards and complies with applicable state and federal laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 100. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
