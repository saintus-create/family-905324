# HSC § 150404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 117. CANCER MEDICATION RECYCLING ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 150404

---

## Text

This division shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 541, Sec. 2. (SB 310) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Division 117, commencing with Section 150400.)
