# HSC § 34213.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 34213.6

---

## Text

“Mortgage loan” means a long-term loan which is secured by a mortgage and is made for permanent financing, including refinancing of existing mortgage obligations as authorized by a resolution of the authority of a housing project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1309, Sec. 4.)
