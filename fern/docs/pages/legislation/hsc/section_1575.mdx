# HSC § 1575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 3. Licensure

**Citation:** HSC § 1575

---

## Text

No person or public agency within this state shall provide adult day health care in this state, without first obtaining a license therefor as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1066.)
