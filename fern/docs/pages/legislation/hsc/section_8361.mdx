# HSC § 8361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 7. Restrictions on Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 8361

---

## Text

The office of any director or officer who acts or permits action contrary to this article immediately thereupon becomes vacant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
