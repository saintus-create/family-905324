# HSC § 1760.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8.6. Pediatric Day Health and Respite Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1760.5

---

## Text

The annual Licensing and Certification Program fee for a pediatric day health and respite care facility, as defined in Section 1760.2, shall be set in accordance with Section 1266.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 74, Sec. 26. Effective July 12, 2006.)
