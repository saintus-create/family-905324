# HSC § 1418.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.4. Quality of Long-Term Health Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1418.6

---

## Text

No long-term health care facility shall accept or retain any patient for whom it cannot provide adequate care.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 11, Sec. 7. Effective March 6, 1985.)
