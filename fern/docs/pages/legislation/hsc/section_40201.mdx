# HSC § 40201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 1. Jurisdiction

**Citation:** HSC § 40201

---

## Text

The bay district shall continue to transact business and exercise its powers under this division in the counties, and portions of counties, specified in Section 40200.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
