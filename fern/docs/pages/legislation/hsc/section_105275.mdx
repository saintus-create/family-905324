# HSC § 105275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 5. ENVIRONMENTAL AND OCCUPATIONAL EPIDEMIOLOGY > Chapter: 5. Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention

**Citation:** HSC § 105275

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited as, the Childhood Lead Poisoning Prevention Act of 1991.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
