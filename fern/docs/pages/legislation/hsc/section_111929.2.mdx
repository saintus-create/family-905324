# HSC § 111929.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 9. Industrial Hemp > Article: 10. Inhalable Products

**Citation:** HSC § 111929.2

---

## Text

An inhalable product shall not contain any of the following:

(a) Flavorings other than natural terpenes.

(b) Polyethylene glycol (PEG).

(c) Vitamin E acetate.

(d) Medium chain triglycerides (MCT oil).

(e) Squalene or squalane.

(f) Any other substance that the department finds to be a danger to public health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 576, Sec. 10. (AB 45) Effective October 6, 2021. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Section 111929.4. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 111929.5.)
