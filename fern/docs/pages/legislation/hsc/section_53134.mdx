# HSC § 53134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 8. ALLOCATION OF HOUSING BOND REVENUES

**Citation:** HSC § 53134

---

## Text

In addition to any other reports required by law, the department shall include within the annual report required by Section 50408 information on the utilization of bond proceeds authorized by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 30, Sec. 7. Effective March 14, 1988.)
