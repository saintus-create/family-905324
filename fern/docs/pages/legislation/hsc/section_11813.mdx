# HSC § 11813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 4. State-County Partnership > Article: 4. County Alcohol and Other Drug Program

**Citation:** HSC § 11813

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall prohibit a county from appropriating funds for alcohol and other drug programs and services in addition to the funds allocated by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 82. Effective January 1, 2005.)
