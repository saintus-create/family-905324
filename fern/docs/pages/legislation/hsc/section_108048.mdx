# HSC § 108048

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 2. Product Recall Safety and Protection Act

**Citation:** HSC § 108048

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter relieves a commercial dealer, manufacturer, importer, distributor, wholesaler, or retailer from compliance with stricter requirements that may be imposed by an agency of the federal government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 569, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
