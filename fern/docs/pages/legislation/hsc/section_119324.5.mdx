# HSC § 119324.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 7. Body Art > Article: 6. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 119324.5

---

## Text

The local fees imposed pursuant to this chapter shall not exceed the reasonable costs to a local government for issuing licenses and permits, performing investigations, inspections, and audits, enforcing orders, and the administrative enforcement and adjudication thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 638, Sec. 2. (AB 300) Effective January 1, 2012. Operative July 1, 2012, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 638.)
