# HSC § 13234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 5. Building Certification

**Citation:** HSC § 13234

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal may establish regulations for certification according to Section 13233, including the general form and contents of letters certifying conformance with high rise fire codes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 178, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
