# HSC § 129290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 7. Small Facility Loan Guarantee for Developmental Disability Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 129290

---

## Text

If any provision of this article or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this article that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this article are severable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
