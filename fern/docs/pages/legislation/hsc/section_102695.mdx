# HSC § 102695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Birth Following Adoption, Legitimation, Court Determination of Parentage, and Acknowledgment > Article: 1. Adoption

**Citation:** HSC § 102695

---

## Text

A court report of adoption received from any court of record in this State, wherein the birth occurred outside the United States, the Territories of the United States, or Canada shall constitute a court order delayed registration of birth; provided, the court report contains a statement of the date and place of birth.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
