# HSC § 6971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 6971

---

## Text

No public agency shall form a zone if such formation will permit other land uses which are not consistent with the general plans, zoning ordinances, or other land use regulations of any county or city within which the proposed zone is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1125.)
