# HSC § 17988

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 17988

---

## Text

In any action or proceeding brought pursuant to this article, service of summons is sufficient if served in the manner provided in the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1844.)
