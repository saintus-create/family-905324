# HSC § 33632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Community Appropriations and General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 33632

---

## Text

Any surplus existing in the general obligation bond redemption fund after payment of principal and interest shall be transferred to the general fund of the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
