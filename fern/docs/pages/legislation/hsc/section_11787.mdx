# HSC § 11787

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 3. Research

**Citation:** HSC § 11787

---

## Text

The department may coordinate task forces and committees of subject-matter experts to assess and document successful practical applications suggested by research.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1328, Sec. 21.)
