# HSC § 1599.86

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.95. Seniors Nursing Home Disclosure

**Citation:** HSC § 1599.86

---

## Text

“Contract of admission,” as used in this chapter, includes all documents which a resident or his or her representative must sign at the time of, or as a condition of, admission to a long-term care health facility as defined in Health and Safety Code Section 1326.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 1988, by initiative Proposition 105. Operative January 1, 1990. Invalidated in 1991 by court decision.)
