# HSC § 124174.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 10. Public School Health Center Support Program

**Citation:** HSC § 124174.5

---

## Text

The program, in collaboration with the State Department of Education, shall act as a liaison for school-based health centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 728, Sec. 110. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2013.)
