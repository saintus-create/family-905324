# HSC § 25929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10.6. Asbestos Abatement and Control

**Citation:** HSC § 25929

---

## Text

If any building standards are adopted pursuant to this chapter, the standards shall be placed in the appropriate sections of the State Building Standards Code, contained in Title 24 of the California Administrative Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 116, Sec. 1. Effective May 28, 1986.)
