# HSC § 33492.89

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 5. March Joint Powers Redevelopment Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.89

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the March Joint Powers Redevelopment Agency shall not expend any tax-increment funds allocated to it for expenses related to carrying out the project until and unless the City of Perris adopts a housing element, pursuant to Section 65585 of the Government Code, that substantially complies with the requirements of Article 10.6 (commencing with Section 65580) of Chapter 3 of Division 1 of Title 7 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1170, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
