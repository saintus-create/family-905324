# HSC § 40265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. Advisory Council

**Citation:** HSC § 40265

---

## Text

Any vacancy on the council shall be filled by appointment in the same manner as the vacating member was appointed, except that the member appointed to fill the vacancy shall only serve the unexpired term of the vacating member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
