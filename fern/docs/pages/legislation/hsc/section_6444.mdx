# HSC § 6444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** HSC § 6444

---

## Text

If the notice is given by personal service, it shall be given at least three days prior to the date fixed for the further hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
