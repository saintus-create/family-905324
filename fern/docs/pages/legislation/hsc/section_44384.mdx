# HSC § 44384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 6. AIR TOXICS “HOT SPOTS” INFORMATION AND ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Fees and Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 44384

---

## Text

Except for Section 44380 and this section, all provisions of this part shall become operative on July 1, 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1252, Sec. 1. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (except for Section 44380) for Part 6, commencing with Section 44300.)
