# HSC § 11847.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 13. Narcotic and Alcohol and Other Drug Use Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11847.4

---

## Text

The department shall provide technical assistance, guidance, and information to local governments and state agencies with respect to the creation and implementation of programs and procedures for dealing effectively with alcohol and other drug prevention, care, treatment, and rehabilitation. The department may charge a fee for these services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 75. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
