# HSC § 6968

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 6968

---

## Text

Any election conducted pursuant to the provisions of this chapter shall be conducted pursuant to the provisions of law pertaining to regular or special elections held in the public agency.
