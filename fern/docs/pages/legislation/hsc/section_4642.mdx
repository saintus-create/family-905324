# HSC § 4642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 4642

---

## Text

Whenever the governing body of the city determines and finds that additional territory will be benefited by annexation to the district, it may pass a resolution to that effect.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 756.)
