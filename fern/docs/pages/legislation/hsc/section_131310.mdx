# HSC § 131310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. The Office of Suicide Prevention

**Citation:** HSC § 131310

---

## Text

If established, the Office of Suicide Prevention may apply for and utilize federal, state, and foundation grants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 142, Sec. 2. (AB 2112) Effective January 1, 2021. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 131320.)
