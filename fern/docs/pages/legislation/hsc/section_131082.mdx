# HSC § 131082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Powers of the Department > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 131082

---

## Text

Every person charged with the performance of any duty under the laws of this state relating to the preservation of the public health, who willfully neglects or refuses to perform the same, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 100182 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 241, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 241.)
