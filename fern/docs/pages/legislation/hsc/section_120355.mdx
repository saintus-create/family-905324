# HSC § 120355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 2. IMMUNIZATIONS > Chapter: 1. Educational and Child Care Facility Immunization Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 120355

---

## Text

Any person or organization administering immunizations shall furnish each person immunized, or his or her parent or guardian, with a written record of immunization given in a form prescribed by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
