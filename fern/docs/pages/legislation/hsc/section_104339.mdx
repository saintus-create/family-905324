# HSC § 104339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 2. INJURY PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Roman Reed Spinal Cord Injury Research Act of 1999

**Citation:** HSC § 104339

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the University of California unless the Regents of the University of California, by appropriate resolution, make these provisions applicable.

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 777, Sec. 2. Effective September 27, 2000.)
