# HSC § 7754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 4. Special Notice to Relative or Friend

**Citation:** HSC § 7754

---

## Text

Whenever a request of notice is given by a relative or friend, the cemetery authority shall not disinter the remains referred to until the notice of the time of disinterment is given the relative or friend, as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
