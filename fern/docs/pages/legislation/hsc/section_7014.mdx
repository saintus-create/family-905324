# HSC § 7014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7014

---

## Text

“Grave” means a space of earth in a burial park, used, or intended to be used, for the disposition of human remains.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 436, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2002.)
