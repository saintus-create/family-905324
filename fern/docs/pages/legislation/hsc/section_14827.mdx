# HSC § 14827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 14827

---

## Text

The certificate shall be filed by February 1 of each year. The board of supervisors may, by ordinance, require an updated or second filing each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
