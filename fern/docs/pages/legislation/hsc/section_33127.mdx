# HSC § 33127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 3. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 33127

---

## Text

An agency may:

(a) Obtain, hire, purchase, or rent office space, equipment, supplies, insurance, or services.

(b) Authorize and pay the travel expenses of agency members, officers, agents, counsel, and employees on agency business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
