# HSC § 17990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 17990

---

## Text

The time to file a written pleading in response to a summons in an action brought pursuant to this article is 10 days.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 807.)
