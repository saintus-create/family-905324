# HSC § 11016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11016

---

## Text

“Furnish” has the same meaning as provided in Section 4048.5 of the Business and Professions Code.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
