# HSC § 1357.501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.16. Nongrandfathered Small Employer Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.501

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to nongrandfathered small employer health care service plan contracts and only with respect to plan years beginning on or after January 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 3. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
