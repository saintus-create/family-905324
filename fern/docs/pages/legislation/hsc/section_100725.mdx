# HSC § 100725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Laboratory Services > Article: 2. Licensing of Laboratories

**Citation:** HSC § 100725

---

## Text

On or after January 1, 1971, the department shall enforce this chapter and regulations adopted by the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 301.5. Effective September 29, 1996.)
