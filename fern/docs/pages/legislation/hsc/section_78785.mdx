# HSC § 78785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 4. Releases of Hazardous Substances > Article: 5. Listing of Hazardous Substance Release Sites

**Citation:** HSC § 78785

---

## Text

This article does not require the department to characterize every site listed pursuant to Section 78760 before the department begins response actions at those sites.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
