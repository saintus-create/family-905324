# HSC § 40425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40425

---

## Text

The south coast district board shall elect a chairperson every two years from its membership.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 664, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
