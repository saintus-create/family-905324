# HSC § 39005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 1. Findings, Declarations, and Intent

**Citation:** HSC § 39005

---

## Text

The reenactment of this division by the Legislature during the 1975–76 Regular Session of the Legislature shall have no effect on any order, rule, or regulation of any district or of the state board, unless such order, rule, or regulation, as the case may be, is not consistent with the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
