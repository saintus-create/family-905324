# HSC § 40427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40427

---

## Text

The south coast district board shall determine the location of its headquarters and may establish branch offices in each of the counties included, in whole or in part, within the south coast district, and in such other parts of the south coast district as it deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 40227 by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1063.)
