# HSC § 35821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 6. FINANCIAL DISCRIMINATION > Chapter: 4. Complaint Resolution

**Citation:** HSC § 35821

---

## Text

Immediately upon receipt of the complaint, the secretary shall endeavor to eliminate any alleged unlawful practice by conference, conciliation, or persuasion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1140.)
