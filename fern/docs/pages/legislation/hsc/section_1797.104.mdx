# HSC § 1797.104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 1. The Emergency Medical Services Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.104

---

## Text

The authority shall provide technical assistance to existing agencies, counties, and cities for the purpose of developing the components of emergency medical services systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
