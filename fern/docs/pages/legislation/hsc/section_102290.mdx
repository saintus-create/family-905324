# HSC § 102290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Local Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 102290

---

## Text

Each assistant or deputy of a local registrar may perform all of the duties of the local registrar in the name and place of his or her principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
