# HSC § 79035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 5. Cleanup of Hazardous Substance Releases > Article: 7. Orders to Potentially Responsible Parties

**Citation:** HSC § 79035

---

## Text

Any determination made by the department regarding the largest manageable number of potentially responsible parties or the identification of other persons as potentially responsible parties pursuant to this article is not subject to judicial review. This section does not affect the rights of any potentially responsible party or the department under any other provision of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
