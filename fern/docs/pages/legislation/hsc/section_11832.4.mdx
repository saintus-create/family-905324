# HSC § 11832.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.1. Certification of Alcohol and Other Drug Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11832.4

---

## Text

A person or entity applying for a certification pursuant to this chapter shall submit all of the following to the department:

(a) A complete written application.

(b) A certification fee, as established in accordance with Chapter 7.3 (commencing Section 11833.01).

(c) An initial application fee.

(d) Any other documentation specified by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 32. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
