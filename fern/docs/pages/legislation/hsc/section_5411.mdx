# HSC § 5411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 2. Sewage and Other Waste

**Citation:** HSC § 5411

---

## Text

No person shall discharge sewage or other waste, or the effluent of treated sewage or other waste, in any manner which will result in contamination, pollution or a nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1447.)
