# HSC § 14828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 14828

---

## Text

There shall not be in any one unincorporated town more than one company for each one thousand inhabitants, but one company may be allowed in any town where the population is less than one thousand.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
