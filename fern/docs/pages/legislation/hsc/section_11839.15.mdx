# HSC § 11839.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 1. Narcotic Treatment Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.15

---

## Text

The director may bring an action to enjoin the violation of Section 11839.7, or the violation of a departmental order issued pursuant to Section 11839.16, in the superior court in and for the county in which the violation occurred. Any proceeding under this section shall conform to the requirements of Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 525) of Title 7 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure. The rebuttable presumption set forth in paragraph (7) of subdivision (b) of Section 11839.9 shall be applicable. If the court finds the allegations to be true, it shall issue its order enjoining the narcotic treatment program from continuance of the violation.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2005.)
