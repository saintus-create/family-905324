# HSC § 110661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 6. Misbranded Food

**Citation:** HSC § 110661

---

## Text

Any food is misbranded if it is manufactured, packed, or held in this state in a food processing facility not duly registered as provided in this part, except for food from facilities exclusively storing, handling, or processing dry beans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 915, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2000.)
