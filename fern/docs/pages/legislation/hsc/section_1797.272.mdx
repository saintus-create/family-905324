# HSC § 1797.272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 3. Emergency Medical Care Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.272

---

## Text

The county board of supervisors shall prescribe the membership, and appoint the members, of the emergency medical care committee. If two or more adjacent counties establish a single committee, the county boards of supervisors shall jointly prescribe the membership, and appoint the members of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1246, Sec. 35.)
