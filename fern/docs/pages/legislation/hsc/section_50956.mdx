# HSC § 50956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Purposes and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50956

---

## Text

It shall be the policy of the agency to coordinate its activities with the department. It shall be the policy of the agency to conduct its operations so as to be fiscally self-sufficient and so as not to require appropriations from the General Fund for payment of its administrative costs or to service bonds of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
