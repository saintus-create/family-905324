# HSC § 44227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 7. District Fees to Implement the California Clean Air Act

**Citation:** HSC § 44227

---

## Text

Upon request of a district, the Department of Motor Vehicles shall collect the fees established pursuant to Sections 44223 and 44225 upon renewal of the registration of any motor vehicle subject to this part and registered in the district, except those vehicles which are expressly exempted under the Vehicle Code from the payment of registration fees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1705, Sec. 1.)
