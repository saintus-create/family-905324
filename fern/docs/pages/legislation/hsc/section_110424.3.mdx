# HSC § 110424.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 4.5. Prenatal Multivitamins

**Citation:** HSC § 110424.3

---

## Text

A person shall not sell in the state or manufacture, deliver, hold, or offer for sale in the state a prenatal multivitamin, including, without limitation, a prenatal multivitamin that is sold by a retailer or that is sold directly to consumers, that does not comply with the requirements described in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 602, Sec. 1. (SB 646) Effective January 1, 2026.)
