# HSC § 7001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7001

---

## Text

“Human remains” or “remains” means the body of a deceased person, regardless of its stage of decomposition, and includes cremated, reduced, or hydrolyzed human remains.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 399, Sec. 28. (AB 351) Effective January 1, 2023.)
