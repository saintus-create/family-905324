# HSC § 25207.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 9.4. Banned, Unregistered, or Outdated Agricultural Wastes

**Citation:** HSC § 25207.11

---

## Text

The collection program shall require, when economically feasible, that the banned, unregistered, or outdated agricultural wastes which are collected are recycled. If not recycled, the wastes shall be treated or disposed of in compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25207.10 by Stats. 1993, Ch. 989, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1994.)
