# HSC § 8732.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 2. Care of Active Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8732.1

---

## Text

Each individual trustee of an endowment care fund shall be a resident of this State, and a corporate trustee shall be qualified to do business in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 595.)
