# HSC § 13026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Fire Equipment > Article: 1. Standard Equipment

**Citation:** HSC § 13026

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal is authorized to make such changes as may be necessary to standardize all existing fire protective equipment throughout the state.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 118.)
