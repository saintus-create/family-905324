# HSC § 1569

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1127, Sec. 3.)
