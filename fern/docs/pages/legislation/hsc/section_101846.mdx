# HSC § 101846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. San Luis Obispo County Hospital Authority > Article: 2. Hospital Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 101846

---

## Text

The hospital authority shall not be a “person” subject to suit under the Cartwright Act (Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 16700) of Part 2 of Division 7 of the Business and Professions Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 899, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
