# HSC § 13260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 8. Carbon Monoxide Poisoning Prevention Act of 2010

**Citation:** HSC § 13260

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Carbon Monoxide Poisoning Prevention Act of 2010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 19, Sec. 3. (SB 183) Effective January 1, 2011.)
