# HSC § 8021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 5. California Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation > Article: 2. State Cultural Affiliation and Repatriation

**Citation:** HSC § 8021

---

## Text

The filing of an appeal by either party automatically stays an order of the commission or a designated mediator on repatriation of human remains and cultural items.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 818, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
