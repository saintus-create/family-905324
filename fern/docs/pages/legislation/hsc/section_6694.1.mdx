# HSC § 6694.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 5. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 6694.1

---

## Text

The maturity date of refunding bonds shall be fixed by the board but in no case shall the maturity of any such bonds be more than forty years from the date thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 304.)
