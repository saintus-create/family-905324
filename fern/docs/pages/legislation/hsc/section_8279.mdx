# HSC § 8279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8279

---

## Text

A cemetery authority shall comply with the Mausoleum and Columbarium Law (Part 5 (commencing with Section 9501)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 207, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
