# HSC § 118410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 1. Articles of Common Use > Article: 2. Infected Packing Material

**Citation:** HSC § 118410

---

## Text

The person having the material cleaned and disinfected shall pay the costs of the inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
