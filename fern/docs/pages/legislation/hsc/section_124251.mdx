# HSC § 124251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Adolescent Health > Article: 2.8. Youth Sports for All Act

**Citation:** HSC § 124251

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Centralized entity” means a department, commission, board, council, or their equivalent.

(b) “Commission” means the Blue Ribbon Commission on the Development of a California Department of Youth Sports or an Equivalent Centralized Entity established and convened pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 124252.

(c) “Department” means the State Department of Public Health.

(d) “Youth sports” has the same meaning as “youth sports organization” as defined in Section 124235.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 705, Sec. 2. (AB 749) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, pursuant to Section 124256.)
