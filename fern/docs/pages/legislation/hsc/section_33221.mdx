# HSC § 33221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 3. Other Entities Undertaking or Assisting Redevelopment > Article: 3. Aid, Assistance, and Co-operation

**Citation:** HSC § 33221

---

## Text

The bonds and obligations issued by an agency also may be purchased, invested in, or used for security as authorized in Section 33663.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
