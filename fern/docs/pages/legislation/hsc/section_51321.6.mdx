# HSC § 51321.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.5. Manufactured Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51321.6

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this part, the agency may insure or guarantee loans made for the purposes described in Section 51320.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1105, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1994.)
