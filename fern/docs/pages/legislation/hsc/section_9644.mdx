# HSC § 9644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 2. Structural and Material Requirements of Community Mausoleums and Columbariums

**Citation:** HSC § 9644

---

## Text

All exterior materials used for doors, window frames, skylights, gutters, downspouts, flashings or embellishment shall be of copper, copper base alloy, aluminum, stainless steel, or other corrosion resistant material of gauges structurally determined.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 1994.)
