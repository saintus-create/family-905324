# HSC § 129975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 6. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 129975

---

## Text

The director of the department may conduct studies relating to the implementation of this chapter to ensure that the implementation of its provisions results in the least amount of increases in costs, staffing, and regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 318. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
