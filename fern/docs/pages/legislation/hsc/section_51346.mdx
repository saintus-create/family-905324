# HSC § 51346

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.8. Home Purchase Assistance Program

**Citation:** HSC § 51346

---

## Text

The agency may execute a contract with the Department of Veterans Affairs to provide home purchase assistance to first-time veteran homebuyers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 30, Sec. 6. Effective March 14, 1988.)
