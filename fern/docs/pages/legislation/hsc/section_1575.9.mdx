# HSC § 1575.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 3. Licensure

**Citation:** HSC § 1575.9

---

## Text

Each application for a new license or renewal submitted to the state department shall be accompanied by an annual Licensing and Certification Program fee set in accordance with Section 1266.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 74, Sec. 17. Effective July 12, 2006.)
