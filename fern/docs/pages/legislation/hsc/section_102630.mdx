# HSC § 102630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Birth Following Adoption, Legitimation, Court Determination of Parentage, and Acknowledgment > Article: 1. Adoption

**Citation:** HSC § 102630

---

## Text

The court reports of adoption that are received by the State Registrar for births that occurred in another state, the District of Columbia, in any territory of the United States, or Canada shall be transmitted to the registration authority of the place of birth.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
