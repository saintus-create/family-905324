# HSC § 50765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. Rental Housing Construction Program > Article: 4. Contracts for Development and Construction by Local Housing Authorities

**Citation:** HSC § 50765

---

## Text

The department may contract with local housing authorities and provide funds which shall be used by such authorities to develop and construct rental housing developments to be operated by such housing authorities and to be rented to eligible households.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1043.)
