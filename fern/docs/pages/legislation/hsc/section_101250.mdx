# HSC § 101250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 3. State Aid for Local Health Administration > Article: 3. State Aid

**Citation:** HSC § 101250

---

## Text

After determining the total amounts available to each area, the department shall notify the governing body of each local health department of that amount, and of the conditions governing its availability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
