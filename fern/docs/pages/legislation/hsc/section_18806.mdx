# HSC § 18806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.2. Mobilehome Residency Law Protection Program

**Citation:** HSC § 18806

---

## Text

This part shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 736, Sec. 3. (AB 318) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Part 2.2, commencing with Section 18800.)
