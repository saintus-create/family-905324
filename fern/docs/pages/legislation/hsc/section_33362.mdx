# HSC § 33362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 5. Procedure for Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Legislative Body

**Citation:** HSC § 33362

---

## Text

At any time not later than the hour set for hearing objections to the proposed redevelopment plan, any person may file in writing with the clerk of the legislative body a statement of his objections to the proposed plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
