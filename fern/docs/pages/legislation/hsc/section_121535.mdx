# HSC § 121535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 5. TUBERCULOSIS > Chapter: 3. Tuberculosis Tests for Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 121535

---

## Text

The X-ray may be taken by a competent and qualified X-ray technician if the X-ray is subsequently interpreted by a licensed physician and surgeon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 329, Sec. 3. (AB 1667) Effective January 1, 2015.)
