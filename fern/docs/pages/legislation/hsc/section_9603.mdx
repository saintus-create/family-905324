# HSC § 9603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9603

---

## Text

Electrical work in all mausoleums or columbariums shall conform to the provisions of the National Electrical Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
