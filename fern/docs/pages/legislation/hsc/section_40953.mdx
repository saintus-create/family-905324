# HSC § 40953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

As used in this chapter, “strategy” means the Sacramento district air quality improvement strategy.
