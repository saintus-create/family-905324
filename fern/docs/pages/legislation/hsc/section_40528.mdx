# HSC § 40528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 8. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40528

---

## Text

The south coast district shall appoint a controller who shall be the accounting officer for the south coast district and who shall exercise general supervision over the accounting forms and methods of keeping the accounts of the south coast district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 705.)
