# HSC § 14866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4.5. Use of Privately Contracted Private Fire Prevention Resources

**Citation:** HSC § 14866

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Department” means the Department of Forestry and Fire Protection.

(b) “Office” means the Governor’s Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 636, Sec. 1. (AB 2380) Effective January 1, 2019.)
