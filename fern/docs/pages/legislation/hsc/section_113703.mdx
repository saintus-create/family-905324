# HSC § 113703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 113703

---

## Text

The purpose of this part is to safeguard public health and provide to consumers food that is safe, unadulterated, and honestly presented through adoption of science-based standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
