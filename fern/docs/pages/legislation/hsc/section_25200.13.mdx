# HSC § 25200.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 9. Permitting of Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 25200.13

---

## Text

For purposes of Sections 25200.11 and 25200.12, “offsite facility” means a facility that serves more than one generator of hazardous waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 719, Sec. 5.)
