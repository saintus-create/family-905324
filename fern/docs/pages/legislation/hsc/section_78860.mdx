# HSC § 78860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 5. Cleanup of Hazardous Substance Releases > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 78860

---

## Text

No response actions taken pursuant to this part by the department or regional or local agencies shall duplicate federal response actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
