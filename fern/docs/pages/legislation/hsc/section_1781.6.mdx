# HSC § 1781.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Continuing Care Contracts > Article: 3. Deposit Subscription Period

**Citation:** HSC § 1781.6

---

## Text

All changes to a deposit agreement or deposit escrow account agreement form shall be submitted to, and approved by, the department before use by the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 820, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2001.)
