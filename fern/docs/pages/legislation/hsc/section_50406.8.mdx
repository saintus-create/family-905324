# HSC § 50406.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1. Organization of the Department and General Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 50406.8

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the department may expend any funds it is authorized to set aside for curing or averting potential defaults for the purposes of curing or averting defaults on any deferred interest loan issued by the department for rental housing development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 37, Sec. 40. (AB 1817) Effective June 27, 2018.)
