# HSC § 80930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 12. Compensation > Article: 2. Claims for Compensation

**Citation:** HSC § 80930

---

## Text

No claim may be presented to the Department of General Services pursuant to this chapter later than three years from the date of discovery of the loss or from January 1, 1982, whichever is later.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
