# HSC § 40226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40226

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the bay district board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business and may act for the bay district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
