# HSC § 4841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 8. Joint Operation

**Citation:** HSC § 4841

---

## Text

The agreement shall specify the proportionate amount to be paid by each district toward the costs and expenses of the organization and the salaries, wages, or other compensation of all persons employed jointly by the districts.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
