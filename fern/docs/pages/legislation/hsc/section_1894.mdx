# HSC § 1894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.8. OFFICE OF RESPONSE AND RESILIENCE

**Citation:** HSC § 1894

---

## Text

Implementation of this division is contingent upon an appropriation of funds by the Legislature in the annual Budget Act for the express purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 50, Sec. 4. (SB 187) Effective June 30, 2022.)
