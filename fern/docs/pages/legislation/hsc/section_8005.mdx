# HSC § 8005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 12. Removal by Counties

**Citation:** HSC § 8005

---

## Text

After the removal of all human remains the property may be used, managed and controlled by the board of supervisors as other county property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 586.)
