# HSC § 11834.60

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.6. Licensing and Certification During Emergencies or Disasters

**Citation:** HSC § 11834.60

---

## Text

This chapter shall only apply to alcohol or other drug recovery or treatment facilities, as defined in Section 11834.02, and alcohol or other drug programs, as defined in Section 11832.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 546, Sec. 12. (SB 582) Effective January 1, 2026.)
