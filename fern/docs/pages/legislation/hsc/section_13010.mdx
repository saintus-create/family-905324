# HSC § 13010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Liability in Relation to Fires

**Citation:** HSC § 13010

---

## Text

Sections 13007, 13008, and 13009 of this code do not apply to nor affect any rights, duties, or causes of action in existence and accruing prior to August 14, 1931.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 48.)
