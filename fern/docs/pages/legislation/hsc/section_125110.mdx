# HSC § 125110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5. HEREDITARY DISEASES/CONGENITAL DEFECTS > Chapter: 1. Genetic Prevention Services > Article: 4. Prenatal Testing

**Citation:** HSC § 125110

---

## Text

The Maternal and Child Health Program Act (Section 27) shall not apply if the pregnant woman objects to the test required by that act on the ground that the test conflicts with her religious beliefs or practices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
