# HSC § 8703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 1. Care of Old Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8703

---

## Text

The application may be by the cemetery authority owning or operating the cemetery or if there is no cemetery authority operating the cemetery, by twenty or more plot owners.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
