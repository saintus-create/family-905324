# HSC § 44382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 6. AIR TOXICS “HOT SPOTS” INFORMATION AND ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 5. Fees and Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 44382

---

## Text

Every district shall, by regulation, adopt the requirements of this part as a condition of every permit issued pursuant to Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 42300) of Part 4 for all new and modified facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1252, Sec. 1. Section operative July 1, 1988, pursuant to Section 44384.)
