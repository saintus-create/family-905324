# HSC § 114368

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 11.7. Compact Mobile Food Operation

**Citation:** HSC § 114368

---

## Text

A compact mobile food operation, as defined in subdivision (c) of Section 113831, shall meet the applicable requirements of Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 114294), except as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 489, Sec. 4. (SB 972) Effective January 1, 2023.)
