# HSC § 34131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Appointment, Compensation, and Removal of Commissioners

**Citation:** HSC § 34131

---

## Text

A commissioner, unless the legislative body is the commission, may not be an elective officer or an employee of the community, but may be a member, commissioner, or employee of any other agency or authority in the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
