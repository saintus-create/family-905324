# HSC § 34506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Housing Cooperation Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34506

---

## Text

“State public body” means any city, county, borough, commission, district, authority, or other subdivision or public body of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
