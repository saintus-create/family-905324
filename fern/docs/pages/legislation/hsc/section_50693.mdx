# HSC § 50693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.5. Housing Advisory Service > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50693

---

## Text

There is hereby established, within the Division of Community Affairs of the department, a California Self-Help Housing Program.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1001, Sec. 4. Effective September 26, 1985.)
