# HSC § 36113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7.5. MODEL CITIES > Chapter: 2. Model Cities Coordinator

**Citation:** HSC § 36113

---

## Text

The coordinator shall serve as the principal liaison officer between state government and local communities for Model Cities programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1462.)
