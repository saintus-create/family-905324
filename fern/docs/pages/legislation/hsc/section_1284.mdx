# HSC § 1284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1284

---

## Text

A licensed inpatient mental health facility shall be subject to the provisions of Section 5622 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 835, Sec. 1.)
