# HSC § 25299.73

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 7. Cost Recovery, Enforcement, and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.73

---

## Text

The standard of liability for any costs of corrective action recoverable pursuant to this chapter is strict liability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25299.63 by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1366, Sec. 28. Effective September 27, 1990. Inoperative January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 25299.81.)
