# HSC § 5000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 5000

---

## Text

Any district may acquire, construct, and operate works within or without its limits.
