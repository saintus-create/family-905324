# HSC § 113945.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 3. Management and Personnel > Article: 1. Supervision

**Citation:** HSC § 113945.1

---

## Text

Except as specified in Section 113984.1, the person in charge shall ensure that persons unnecessary to the food facility operation shall not be allowed in the food preparation, food storage, or warewashing areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 8. Effective July 20, 2007.)
