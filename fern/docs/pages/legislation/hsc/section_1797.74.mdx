# HSC § 1797.74

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.74

---

## Text

“Emergency medical services area” or “EMS area” means the geographical area within the jurisdiction of the designated local EMS agency.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1391, Sec. 8.)
