# HSC § 124270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Adolescent Health > Article: 4. Impacts of Social Media on Mental Health

**Citation:** HSC § 124270

---

## Text

As used in this article, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Children and youth” means individuals up to 26 years of age.

(b) “Department” means the State Department of Public Health.

(c) “Social media” means a social media platform, as defined in Section 22675 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 807, Sec. 1. (AB 1282) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 124270.2.)
