# HSC § 14915

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 4. Expense of Abatement > Article: 3. Collection of Expenses

**Citation:** HSC § 14915

---

## Text

A copy of the report, as confirmed, shall be turned over to the auditor of the county, on or before the tenth day of August following such confirmation, and the auditor shall enter the amounts of the respective assessments against the respective parcels of land as they appear on the current assessment roll.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 354.)
