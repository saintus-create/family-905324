# HSC § 16023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 2. Essential Services Buildings > Article: 5. Offenses

**Citation:** HSC § 16023

---

## Text

Any person who willfully violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.
