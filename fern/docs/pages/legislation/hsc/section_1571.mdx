# HSC § 1571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1571

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall require any county to include adult day health care as a part of the services offered by the county hospital or to otherwise establish an adult day health center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1066.)
