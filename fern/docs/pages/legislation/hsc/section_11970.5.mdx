# HSC § 11970.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 3. STATE GOVERNMENT’S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE USE OF ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUGS > Chapter: 2. Community Alcohol and Other Drug Use Control > Article: 1. Comprehensive Drug Court Implementation Act of 1999

**Citation:** HSC § 11970.5

---

## Text

(a) This article shall be known and may be cited as the Drug Court Programs Act.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 22, Sec. 61. (AB 75) Effective June 27, 2013. Adding action operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 110 of Ch. 22. Section operative July 1, 2013, by its own provisions.)
