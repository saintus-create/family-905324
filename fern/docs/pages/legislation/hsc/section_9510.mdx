# HSC § 9510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9510

---

## Text

“Incombustible Material” means and includes any material having an ignition temperature higher than 1,000 degrees Fahrenheit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
