# HSC § 6953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 6953

---

## Text

“Zone” means an on-site wastewater disposal zone formed pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1125.)
