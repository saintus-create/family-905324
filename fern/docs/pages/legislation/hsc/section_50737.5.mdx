# HSC § 50737.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. Rental Housing Construction Program > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50737.5

---

## Text

Funds available for the purposes of this chapter shall be allocated by the department throughout the state in accordance with identified housing needs.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1043.)
