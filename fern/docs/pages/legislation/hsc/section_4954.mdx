# HSC § 4954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 4954

---

## Text

“Area,” as used in this chapter, means the area served, or proposed to be served, by the works, or proposed works.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
