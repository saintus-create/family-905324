# HSC § 39010.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39010.6

---

## Text

“Acid deposition precursor” means an air contaminant which may be transformed to an acid gas or particle in the atmosphere.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1473, Sec. 2.)
