# HSC § 6954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 6954

---

## Text

“Real property” means both land and improvements to land which benefit, directly or indirectly from, or on behalf of, the activities of the zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1125.)
