# HSC § 1798.150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. Facilities > Article: 2. Critical Care

**Citation:** HSC § 1798.150

---

## Text

The authority may establish, in cooperation with affected medical organizations, guidelines for hospital facilities according to critical care capabilities.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
