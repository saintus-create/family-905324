# HSC § 33442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 11. Property Disposition, Rehabilitation and Development

**Citation:** HSC § 33442

---

## Text

An agency may sell, lease, grant, or donate real property owned or acquired by the agency in a survey area to a housing authority or to any public agency for public housing projects.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1665.)
