# HSC § 104320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 1. CHRONIC DISEASE > Chapter: 6.5. Reduction of Asthma through Assessment, Intervention, and Evaluation

**Citation:** HSC § 104320

---

## Text

The department shall establish and maintain a surveillance and intervention program for the prevention of asthma.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 93, Sec. 24. Effective July 7, 2000.)
