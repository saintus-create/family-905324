# HSC § 100135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Organization of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 100135

---

## Text

The director may seek and grant waivers that the department determines are reasonably necessary for the implementation of the department’s proposed consolidations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
