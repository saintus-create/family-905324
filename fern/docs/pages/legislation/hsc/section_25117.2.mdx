# HSC § 25117.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25117.2

---

## Text

“Hazardous waste management” or “management” means the transportation, transfer, recycling, recovery, disposal, handling, processing, storage, and treatment of hazardous waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1632, Sec. 6.)
