# HSC § 7011.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7011.2

---

## Text

(a) “Placement” means the placing of a container holding cremated remains or hydrolyzed human remains in a crypt, vault, or niche.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 41) and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 42. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, by its own provisions.)
