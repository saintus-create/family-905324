# HSC § 25125.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2.1. Board of Environmental Safety

**Citation:** HSC § 25125.7

---

## Text

The board shall annually prepare and transmit to the Secretary for Environmental Protection an annual review of the department’s performance as compared to its objectives, including, but not limited to, the performance of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 73, Sec. 5. (SB 158) Effective July 12, 2021.)
