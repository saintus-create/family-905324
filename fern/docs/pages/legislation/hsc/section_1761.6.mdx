# HSC § 1761.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8.6. Pediatric Day Health and Respite Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1761.6

---

## Text

A pediatric day health and respite care facility shall conspicuously post the license, or a true copy thereof, in a location accessible to public view.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1227, Sec. 8. Effective September 24, 1990.)
