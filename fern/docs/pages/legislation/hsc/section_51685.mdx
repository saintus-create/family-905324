# HSC § 51685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. Loan Loss Guarantee Program

**Citation:** HSC § 51685

---

## Text

Moneys transferred to the insurance fund under this section may be used to create reserves for loan loss guarantees authorized by this chapter and for the initial administrative expenses of the program which are not offset by premium income.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 51687.)
