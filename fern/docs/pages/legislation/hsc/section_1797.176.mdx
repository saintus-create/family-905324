# HSC § 1797.176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 5. Personnel

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.176

---

## Text

The authority shall establish the minimum standards for the policies and procedures necessary for medical control of the EMS system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1390, Sec. 3.)
