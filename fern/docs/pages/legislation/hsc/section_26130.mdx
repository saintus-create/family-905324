# HSC § 26130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. Toxic Mold > Article: 3. Guidelines for Remediation

**Citation:** HSC § 26130

---

## Text

The department, in consultation with the task force convened pursuant to Section 26101.7, shall develop and disseminate remediation guidelines for molds in indoor environments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 584, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
