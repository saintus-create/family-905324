# HSC § 9592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 4. Inspection and Approval

**Citation:** HSC § 9592

---

## Text

When it is found that the building or structure is structurally complete, upon request, a temporary certificate of occupancy shall be issued by the department for the use of a portion or portions of a mausoleum or columbarium for interment of human remains prior to the completion of the entire building or structure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
