# HSC § 4703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

Districts may be formed, maintained, and governed in any county as provided in this chapter.
