# HSC § 50888

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 15. Family Housing Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Community Housing Developments

**Citation:** HSC § 50888

---

## Text

Each community housing development shall consist of three components: a housing component, a supportive services component, and a jobs and economic development component.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 30, Sec. 5. Effective March 14, 1988.)
