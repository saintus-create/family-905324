# HSC § 36112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7.5. MODEL CITIES > Chapter: 2. Model Cities Coordinator

**Citation:** HSC § 36112

---

## Text

The coordinator shall coordinate all state activities in Model Cities areas in the state and may constitute such interdepartmental task forces as are necessary to effectuate the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1462.)
