# HSC § 1523.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1523.5

---

## Text

Transitional shelter care facilities, as defined in Section 1502.3, shall be exempt from the fees imposed pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 1523.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 732, Sec. 22. (AB 404) Effective January 1, 2018.)
