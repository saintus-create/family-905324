# HSC § 39035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39035

---

## Text

“Light-duty” means having a manufacturer’s maximum gross vehicle weight rating of under 6,001 pounds.
