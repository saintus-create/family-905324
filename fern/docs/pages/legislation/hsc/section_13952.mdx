# HSC § 13952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 10. Service Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 13952

---

## Text

A local agency formation commission shall have no power or duty to review and approve or disapprove a proposal to create a service zone, a proposal to change the boundaries of a zone, or a proposal to dissolve a zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
