# HSC § 111921.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 9. Industrial Hemp > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 111921.6

---

## Text

Manufacture or sale of inhalable hemp products is prohibited. Manufacture of inhalable hemp products for the sole purpose of sale in other states is not prohibited.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 248, Sec. 35. (AB 8) Effective January 1, 2026.)
