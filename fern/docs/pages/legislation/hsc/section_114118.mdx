# HSC § 114118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 5. Cleaning and Sanitizing of Equipment and Utensils

**Citation:** HSC § 114118

---

## Text

Fabric implements shall be laundered and sanitized before or after use in direct contact with food.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 42. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
