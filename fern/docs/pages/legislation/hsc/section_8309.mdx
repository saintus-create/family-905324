# HSC § 8309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 2. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 8309

---

## Text

The rules and regulations shall be plainly printed or typewritten and maintained subject to inspection in the office of the cemetery authority or in such place or places within the cemetery as the cemetery authority may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
