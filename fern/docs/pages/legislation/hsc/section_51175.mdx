# HSC § 51175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 5. Financing of Housing Developments and Residential Structures > Article: 4. Lender of Last Resort

**Citation:** HSC § 51175

---

## Text

Except as provided in this article, the agency shall not make construction loans or first mortgage loans for the purpose of financing owner-occupied residential structures unless those loans are made through a qualified mortgage lender.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 82, Sec. 4. (AB 984) Effective August 12, 2013.)
