# HSC § 1796.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Home Care Services > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1796.10

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Home Care Services Consumer Protection Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 790, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2014. Provisions implemented as of January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 1796.61.)
