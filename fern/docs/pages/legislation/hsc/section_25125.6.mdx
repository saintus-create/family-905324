# HSC § 25125.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2.1. Board of Environmental Safety

**Citation:** HSC § 25125.6

---

## Text

The director, or a designee, shall present and respond to the board, if requested by the board, on any issue or item brought forward by a member of the public, the ombudsperson, or a board member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 73, Sec. 5. (SB 158) Effective July 12, 2021.)
