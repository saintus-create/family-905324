# HSC § 46025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NOISE CONTROL ACT > Chapter: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 46025

---

## Text

“State agency” means and includes every state office, officer, department, division, bureau, board, council, commission, or other state agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
