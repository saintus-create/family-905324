# HSC § 55112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 32. SEISMIC SAFETY BUILDING REHABILITATION LOANS > Part: 2. BONDS

**Citation:** HSC § 55112

---

## Text

The local agency may create one or more bond reserve accounts to secure payments of the principal of, and interest and sinking fund payments on, any bonds or any issuance thereof, as specified in the resolution authorizing the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1602, Sec. 1.)
