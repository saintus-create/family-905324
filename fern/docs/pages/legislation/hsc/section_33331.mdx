# HSC § 33331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 4. Preparation and Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33331

---

## Text

Every redevelopment plan shall be consistent with the community’s general plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 580, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1998.)
