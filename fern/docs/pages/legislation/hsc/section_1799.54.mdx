# HSC § 1799.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. The Commission on Emergency Medical Services > Article: 2. Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 1799.54

---

## Text

The commission shall review and comment upon the emergency medical services portion of the State Health Facilities and Service Plan developed pursuant to Section 127155.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 175. Effective September 29, 1996.)
