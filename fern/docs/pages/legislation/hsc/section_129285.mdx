# HSC § 129285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 7. Small Facility Loan Guarantee for Developmental Disability Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 129285

---

## Text

(a) Loans of under three hundred thousand dollars ($300,000) for any single facility for six or fewer developmentally disabled shall have priority for obtaining loan insurance.

(b) The total amount of loans that may be insured pursuant to this article shall not exceed one hundred million dollars ($100,000,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 730, Sec. 4. (AB 1629) Effective October 19, 2010.)
