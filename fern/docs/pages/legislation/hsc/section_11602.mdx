# HSC § 11602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 11. Educational Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11602

---

## Text

The Attorney General may enter into contracts for educational and research activities without performance bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
