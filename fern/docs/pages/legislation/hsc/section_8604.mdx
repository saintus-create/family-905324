# HSC § 8604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 4. Property Rights > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8604

---

## Text

Cemetery property passing to an individual by reason of the death of the owner is exempt from all inheritance taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
