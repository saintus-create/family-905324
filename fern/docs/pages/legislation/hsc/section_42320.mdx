# HSC § 42320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 1.3. Air Pollution Permit Streamlining Act

**Citation:** HSC § 42320

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Air Pollution Permit Streamlining Act of 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1096, Sec. 3. Effective September 29, 1992.)
