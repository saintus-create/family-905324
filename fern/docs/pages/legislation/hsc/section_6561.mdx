# HSC § 6561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 5. Elections > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6561

---

## Text

Every voter resident within the district or a proposed district for the period requisite to enable him to vote at a general election, is entitled to vote at district elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
