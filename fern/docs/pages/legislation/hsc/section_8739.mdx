# HSC § 8739

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 2. Care of Active Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8739

---

## Text

A nonendowment care cemetery is one that does not have deposited in an endowment care fund the minimum amounts required by law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 176.)
