# HSC § 1267.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1267.17

---

## Text

Each congregate living health facility shall conspicuously post the license, or a true copy thereof in a location accessible to public view.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1478, Sec. 12. Effective September 28, 1988.)
