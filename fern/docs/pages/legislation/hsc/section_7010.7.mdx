# HSC § 7010.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7010.7

---

## Text

(a) “Scattering” means the authorized dispersal of cremated remains or hydrolyzed human remains at sea, in other areas of the state, or commingling in a defined area within a dedicated cemetery, in accordance with this part.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 37) and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 38. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, by its own provisions.)
