# HSC § 12618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 4. Reports

**Citation:** HSC § 12618

---

## Text

Each bill of lading, manifest, and invoice issued to cover the sale and shipment of fireworks shall bear the license number of both the seller or shipper and buyer or receiver.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
