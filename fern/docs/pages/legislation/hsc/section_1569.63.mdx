# HSC § 1569.63

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 6. Other Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.63

---

## Text

The director shall insure that licensing personnel at the department have appropriate training to properly carry out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1127, Sec. 3.)
