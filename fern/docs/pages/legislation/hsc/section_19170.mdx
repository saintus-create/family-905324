# HSC § 19170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 5. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 19170

---

## Text

Any person who violates, or causes or permits another person to violate, any provision of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1941, Ch. 301.)
