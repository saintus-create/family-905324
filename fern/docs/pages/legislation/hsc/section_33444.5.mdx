# HSC § 33444.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 11. Property Disposition, Rehabilitation and Development

**Citation:** HSC § 33444.5

---

## Text

An agency may establish a program under which it loans funds to owners or tenants for the purpose of rehabilitating commercial buildings or structures within the project area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 942, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1994.)
