# HSC § 34320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Housing Authorities

**Citation:** HSC § 34320

---

## Text

No law concerning the acquisition, operation, or disposition of property by other public bodies is applicable to an authority unless the Legislature specifically so states.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
