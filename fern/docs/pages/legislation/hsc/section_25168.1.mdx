# HSC § 25168.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Haulers

**Citation:** HSC § 25168.1

---

## Text

The department shall adopt regulations for containers used to transport hazardous waste that are not subject to the federal regulations contained in Title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 945, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
