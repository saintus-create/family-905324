# HSC § 131500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 113. THE ADULT HEALTH COVERAGE EXPANSION PROGRAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 131500

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Adult Health Coverage Expansion Program.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 677, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
