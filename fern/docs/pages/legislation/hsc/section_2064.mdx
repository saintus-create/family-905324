# HSC § 2064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 1. Mosquito Abatement and Vector Control Districts > Article: 5. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 2064

---

## Text

A board of trustees may consider any recurrence of a public nuisance abated pursuant to Section 2061 to be a continuation of the original public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 395, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
