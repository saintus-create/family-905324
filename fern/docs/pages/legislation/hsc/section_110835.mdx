# HSC § 110835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110835

---

## Text

The director may adopt regulations allowing or prohibiting the use of substances in the processing of products that are exempt or excluded from certification under the NOP, and animal food and cosmetics sold as organic.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 2003.)
