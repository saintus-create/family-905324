# HSC § 41337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 14. Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41337

---

## Text

The Antelope Valley district board may, by resolution, cause to be drawn all warrants on the treasurer against all funds, except funds for debt service, of the Antelope Valley district in the treasury for the payment of salaries and expenses of the Antelope Valley district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 163, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
