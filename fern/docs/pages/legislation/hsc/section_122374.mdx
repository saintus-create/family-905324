## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2016.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 231, Sec. 1. (AB 339) Effective January 1, 2014. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (Jan. 1, 2016) for Chapter 10, commencing with Section 122370.)
