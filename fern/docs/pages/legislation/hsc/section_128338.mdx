# HSC § 128338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5. Health Professions Education Programs > Article: 1. Health Professions Education Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 128338

---

## Text

Effective with the dissolution of the Health Professions Education Foundation, any reference to the Health Professions Education Foundation, or the Foundation, in this chapter shall be deemed a reference to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 164. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
