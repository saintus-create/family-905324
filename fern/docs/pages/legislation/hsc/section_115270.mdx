# HSC § 115270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 8. Radiation Control Law > Article: 17. Southwestern Low Level Radioactive Waste Disposal Compact

**Citation:** HSC § 115270

---

## Text

The department shall adopt regulations specifying the modes of transportation which are most protective of public health and the environment which shall be used by generators to transport low-level radioactive waste within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
