# HSC § 1399.813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 11.5. Individual Access to Contracts for Health Care Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1399.813

---

## Text

In connection with the offering for sale of any plan contract to an individual, each plan shall make a reasonable disclosure, as part of its solicitation and sales materials, of all individual contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 810, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
