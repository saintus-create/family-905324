# HSC § 33600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 33600

---

## Text

An agency may accept financial or other assistance from any public or private source, for the agency’s activities, powers, and duties, and expend any funds so received for any of the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
