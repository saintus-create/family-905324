# HSC § 39632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3.3. Cruise Ships and Oceangoing Ships

**Citation:** HSC § 39632

---

## Text

Commencing on January 1, 2005, a cruise ship, and commencing on January 1, 2006, an oceangoing ship, shall not conduct onboard incineration while operating within three miles of the California coast, to the extent allowed by federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 588, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
