# HSC § 39047.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39047.2

---

## Text

“PM2.5” means particulate matter 2.5 microns and smaller in size.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 477, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
