# HSC § 40376

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40376

---

## Text

A regional district treasury shall be in the custody of the county treasurer of a county in the regional district designated by the regional district board, and such treasurer shall be the regional district treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
