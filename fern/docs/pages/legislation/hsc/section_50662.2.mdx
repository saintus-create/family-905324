# HSC § 50662.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 6.5. Deferred-Payment Rehabilitation Loans

**Citation:** HSC § 50662.2

---

## Text

Deferred-payment loans may be made to finance actual costs incurred to meet rehabilitation standards for rehabilitation of mobilehome parks, as defined in Section 18214, for occupancy by lower income households.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1174, Sec. 3.)
