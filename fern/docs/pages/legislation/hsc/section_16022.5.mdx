# HSC § 16022.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 2. Essential Services Buildings > Article: 4. Qualifications and Reporting

**Citation:** HSC § 16022.5

---

## Text

Following completion of construction of a state-owned or state-occupied essential services building, the State Fire Marshal may conduct regular inspections of those buildings for compliance with building standards relating to fire and panic safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 31, Sec. 13. (SB 85) Effective June 27, 2019.)
