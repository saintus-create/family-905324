# HSC § 37001.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 8. PUBLIC HOUSING ELECTION IMPLEMENTATION LAW

**Citation:** HSC § 37001.3

---

## Text

The maximum income of “persons of low income,” as determined by the state public body, developing, constructing, or acquiring the property, for purposes of Section 1 of Article XXXIV of the State Constitution, shall not exceed the maximum income of lower income households, as defined in Section 50079.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 692.)
