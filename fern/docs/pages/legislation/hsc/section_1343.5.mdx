# HSC § 1343.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 1343.5

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this chapter, the burden of proving an exemption or an exception from a definition is upon the person claiming it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 778.)
