# HSC § 42302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 1. Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 42302

---

## Text

An applicant for a permit that has been denied may request, within 30 days after receipt of the notice of the denial, the hearing board of the district to hold a hearing on whether the permit was properly denied.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 643, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2000.)
