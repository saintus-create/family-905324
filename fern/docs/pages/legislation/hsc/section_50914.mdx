# HSC § 50914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 50914

---

## Text

(a) The board shall authorize any sale of obligations or securities or other debt obligations and shall approve other major contractual agreements. Any other contractual agreements or debt obligations may be approved by the executive director pursuant to regulations of the board.

(b) Actions of the board may be taken only by a concurrence of a majority of the entire membership thereof, excepting nonvoting ex officio members.
