# HSC § 39681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3.6. Labor Standards > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39681

---

## Text

This chapter establishes labor standards as a cobenefit of incentive programs for the purchase of new drayage and short-haul trucks that are based on clean air standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 748, Sec. 4. (AB 794) Effective January 1, 2022.)
