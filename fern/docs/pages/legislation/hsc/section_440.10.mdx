# HSC § 440.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1.7. HEALTH FACILITIES DISCLOSURE ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 440.10

---

## Text

“Health facility,” as used in this chapter, means any general acute care hospital required to be licensed pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 1250) of Division 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1049, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
