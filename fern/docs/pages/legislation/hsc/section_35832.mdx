# HSC § 35832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 6. FINANCIAL DISCRIMINATION > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous

## Text

The provisions of this part shall be liberally construed in order to effectuate the purposes of this part.
