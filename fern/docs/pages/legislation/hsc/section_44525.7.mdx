# HSC § 44525.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 3. Projects

**Citation:** HSC § 44525.7

---

## Text

Commencing in 2002, and annually thereafter, the authority shall submit a report to the Legislature regarding the program described in subdivision (h) of Section 44526.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 915, Sec. 7.5) by Stats. 2009, Ch. 643, Sec. 11. (SB 832) Effective November 2, 2009. Note: This operative section is retained in the code because its purported repeal by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 325, was ineffective as outside the scope of Ch. 303. Sec. 325 of Ch. 303, which incorrectly identified this operative version, was intended by the Legislature to repeal the inoperative version added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 915, Sec. 7.)
