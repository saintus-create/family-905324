# HSC § 124270.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Adolescent Health > Article: 4. Impacts of Social Media on Mental Health

**Citation:** HSC § 124270.2

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 807, Sec. 1. (AB 1282) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 4, commencing with Sec. 124270.)
