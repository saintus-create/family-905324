# HSC § 151003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 120. SEXUAL HEALTH EDUCATION ACCOUNTABILITY ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 151003

---

## Text

This division shall apply only to grants that are funded pursuant to contracts entered into or amended on or after January 1, 2008.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 602, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
