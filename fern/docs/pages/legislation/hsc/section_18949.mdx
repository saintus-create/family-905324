# HSC § 18949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 5. Appeals and Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 18949

---

## Text

The commission shall establish a schedule of fees for appeals in an amount sufficient to pay its costs of administration and hearing appeals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1082.)
