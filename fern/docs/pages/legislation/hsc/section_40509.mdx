# HSC § 40509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 7. Variances and Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 40509

---

## Text

Any person may petition the south coast district board to hold a public hearing on any application to issue or renew a permit.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1301, Sec. 20.)
