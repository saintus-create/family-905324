# HSC § 123630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 2. Maternal Health > Article: 4.6. California Dignity in Pregnancy and Childbirth Act

**Citation:** HSC § 123630

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Dignity in Pregnancy and Childbirth Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 533, Sec. 3. (SB 464) Effective January 1, 2020.)
