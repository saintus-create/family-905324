# HSC § 1797.257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 2. Local Emergency Medical Services Planning

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.257

---

## Text

A local EMS agency which elects to implement a trauma care system on or after the effective date of the regulations adopted pursuant to Section 1798.161 shall develop and submit a plan for that trauma care system to the authority according to the requirements of the regulations prior to the implementation of that system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1735, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
