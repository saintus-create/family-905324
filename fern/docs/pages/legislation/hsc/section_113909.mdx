# HSC § 113909

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113909

---

## Text

“Shellfish control authority” means a state, federal, foreign, tribal, or other government entity legally responsible for administering a program that includes certification of molluscan shellfish harvesters and dealers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 7. Effective July 20, 2007.)
