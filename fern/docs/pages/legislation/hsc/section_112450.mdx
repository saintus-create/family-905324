# HSC § 112450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 6. Cold Storage > Article: 4. General Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 112450

---

## Text

The department shall inspect and supervise all cold storage or refrigerating warehouses, and make the inspection of the entry of articles of food therein as it deems necessary to secure the proper enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
