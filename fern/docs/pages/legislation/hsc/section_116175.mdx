# HSC § 116175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 4. Mosquito and Gnat Control

**Citation:** HSC § 116175

---

## Text

The department shall make studies and demonstrations as may be necessary to determine the areas of the state that have a high proportion of mosquito-borne diseases, including malaria and encephalitis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
