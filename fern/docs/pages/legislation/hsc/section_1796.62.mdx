# HSC § 1796.62

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Home Care Services > Article: 12. Operation

**Citation:** HSC § 1796.62

---

## Text

This chapter and any rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to this chapter shall only be implemented to the extent that funds are made available through an appropriation in the annual Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 790, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2014. Provisions implemented as of January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 1796.61.)
