# HSC § 1357.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.1. Small Employer Group Access to Contracts for Health Care Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.19

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to a health care service plan contract that is subject to Article 3.16 (commencing with Section 1357.500) or Article 3.17 (commencing with Section 1357.600), except as otherwise provided in those articles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 2. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
