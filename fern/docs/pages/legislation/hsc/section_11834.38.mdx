# HSC § 11834.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.5. Licensing > Article: 4. Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 11834.38

---

## Text

Any license suspended pursuant to this chapter may be reinstated pursuant to Section 11522 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11834.37 by Stats. 1993, Ch. 741, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1994.)
