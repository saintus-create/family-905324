# HSC § 101450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 4. Additional Administrative Provisions > Article: 4. City Health Ordinances, Boards, and Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 101450

---

## Text

The governing body of a city shall take measures necessary to preserve and protect the public health, including the regulation of sanitary matters in the city, and including if indicated, the adoption of ordinances, regulations and orders not in conflict with general laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
