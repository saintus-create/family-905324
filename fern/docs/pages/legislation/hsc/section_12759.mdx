# HSC § 12759

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 3. FLAMETHROWING DEVICES > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 12759

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal shall establish fees pursuant to this part that shall be deposited in the State Fire Marshal Licensing and Certification Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
