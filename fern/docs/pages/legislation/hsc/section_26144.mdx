# HSC § 26144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. Toxic Mold > Article: 4. Disclosures

**Citation:** HSC § 26144

---

## Text

The requirements of this article shall not apply to properties where the tenant is contractually responsible for maintenance of the property, including any remedial action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 584, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
