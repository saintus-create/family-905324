# HSC § 33492.129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 8. The Alameda Naval Air Station and the Fleet Industrial Supply Center

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.129

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 33492.9 or any other provision of law, the redevelopment agency shall make payments to affected taxing entities required by Section 33607.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added (by renumbering Section 33493.3) by Stats. 1997, Ch. 580, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1998. Note: The action line's renumbering of Article 7 to Article 8 implies that it amended and renumbered Section 33493.3.)
