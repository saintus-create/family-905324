# HSC § 128030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Personnel Recruitment and Education > Article: 4. Health Professions Planning Grants

**Citation:** HSC § 128030

---

## Text

The department, in cooperation with the California Postsecondary Education Commission, shall administer the program established pursuant to this article and shall for this purpose, adopt regulations as it determines are reasonably necessary to carry out this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 126. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
