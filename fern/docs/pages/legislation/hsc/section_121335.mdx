# HSC § 121335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 4. HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS (HIV) > Chapter: 14. Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) Clinical Trial Grant Award for the Prevention of Maternal Transmission of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Infection

**Citation:** HSC § 121335

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature for the department to make every effort possible to insure a comprehensive and diverse expert representation on the committee. It is the intent of the Legislature to ensure that expert members of the committee include, but are not limited to, ethnic minorities and women.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
