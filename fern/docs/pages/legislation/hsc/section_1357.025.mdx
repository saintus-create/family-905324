# HSC § 1357.025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.1. Small Employer Group Access to Contracts for Health Care Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.025

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to preclude the application of this chapter to either of the following:

(a) An association, trust, or other organization acting as a “health care service plan” as defined under Section 1345.

(b) An association, trust, or other organization or person presenting information regarding a health care service plan to persons who may be interested in subscribing or enrolling in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1146, Sec. 1.4. Effective October 11, 1993.)
