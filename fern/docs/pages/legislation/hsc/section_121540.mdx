# HSC § 121540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 5. TUBERCULOSIS > Chapter: 3. Tuberculosis Tests for Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 121540

---

## Text

The school shall maintain a file containing an up-to-date certificate for each person covered by this chapter. It shall be the duty of the county health officer of each county to insure that the provisions of this chapter are complied with.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
