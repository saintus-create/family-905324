# HSC § 12577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 1. Types of Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 12577

---

## Text

A public display license (limited) allows the performance of a single public display action of a single nature with dangerous fireworks at one location to be executed at one or more performances or exhibitions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
