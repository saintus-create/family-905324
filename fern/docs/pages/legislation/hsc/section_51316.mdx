# HSC § 51316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.3. Special Needs Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51316

---

## Text

Subject only to the limitations prescribed in this chapter, the agency, in addition to any other power conferred by this part, may purchase, or undertake, directly or indirectly through lending institutions, commitments to purchase, construction loans, mortgage loans, or other types of loans originated in accordance with a financing agreement with the agency to finance the acquisition, construction, rehabilitation, refinancing, or development of special needs housing, and for the provision of capital improvements in connection with, and determined necessary to, such housing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 748, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007.)
