# HSC § 51369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 7. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 51369

---

## Text

The agency shall create and establish such other accounts in the California Housing Finance Fund as may be necessary or desirable for its agency purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
