# HSC § 40861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 4. Decisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40861

---

## Text

A hearing board may rehear a decision if a party petitions for a rehearing within 10 days after a copy of the decision has been mailed to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
