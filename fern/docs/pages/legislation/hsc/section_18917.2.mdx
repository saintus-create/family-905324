# HSC § 18917.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18917.2

---

## Text

“Propose” refers, with respect to the procedure for promulgation of a building standard, to the state agency that has the legislative authority and responsibility to write proposed building standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 897, Sec. 10.5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
