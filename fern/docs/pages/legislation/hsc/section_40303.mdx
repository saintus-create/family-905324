# HSC § 40303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 1. Creation of Regional Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 40303

---

## Text

Upon adoption of a resolution pursuant to Section 40302, the board of supervisors shall file a certified copy of the resolution with the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
