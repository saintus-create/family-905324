# HSC § 33853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 5. Legal Actions, Exceptions

**Citation:** HSC § 33853

---

## Text

The Special Assessment Investigation, Limitation and Majority Protest Act of 1931, Division 4 (commencing with Section 2800) of the Streets and Highways Code, shall not apply to proceedings under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
