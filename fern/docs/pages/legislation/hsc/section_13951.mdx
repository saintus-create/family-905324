# HSC § 13951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 10. Service Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 13951

---

## Text

A district board may change the boundaries of a service zone or dissolve a zone by following the procedures in Section 13950.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
