# HSC § 14855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** HSC § 14855

---

## Text

The active volunteer firefighters of volunteer fire companies or departments regularly organized and recognized by the Fire and Rescue Operational Area Coordinator or the county board of supervisors are exempt from military duty, except in case of war, invasion, or insurrection.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2009.)
