# HSC § 110674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 6. Misbranded Food

**Citation:** HSC § 110674

---

## Text

Any food is misbranded if its labeling does not conform with the requirements for pasteurized in-shell egg labeling as set forth in Section 27644.5 of the Food and Agricultural Code, and the regulations adopted pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 11, Sec. 7. (AB 1414) Effective April 17, 2014.)
