# HSC § 34146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 34146

---

## Text

Any grants or loans of money appropriated by the legislative body of the community to the commission is not to be construed as making the commission a department of the community or placing the officers, agents, counsel, and employees under civil service of the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
