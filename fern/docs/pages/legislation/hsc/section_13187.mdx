# HSC § 13187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 4. Denial, Revocation, and Suspension

**Citation:** HSC § 13187

---

## Text

When a statement of issues has been so filed, the State Fire Marshal may order denial pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 802.)
