# HSC § 6734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 3. Equalization of Assessments by District Assessor

**Citation:** HSC § 6734

---

## Text

The board may hear complaints as to the proceedings of the district assessor and adjudicate and determine the controversy. It may of its own motion raise an assessment, after such reasonable notice to the party whose assessment is to be raised, as may be ordered by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
