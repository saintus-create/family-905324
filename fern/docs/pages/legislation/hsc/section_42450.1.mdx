# HSC § 42450.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 4. Orders for Abatements

**Citation:** HSC § 42450.1

---

## Text

This article applies to any order for abatement issued pursuant to a determination made under Section 42301.7.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1589, Sec. 12.)
