# HSC § 20107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20107

---

## Text

If the majority of the votes cast are in favor of the tax, the district board shall report to the board the amount of money authorized to be raised.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
