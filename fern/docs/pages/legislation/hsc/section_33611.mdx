# HSC § 33611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Community Redevelopment Agency Administrative Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 33611

---

## Text

Each agency transacting business and exercising powers under this part shall annually submit to the legislative body of the community a proposed budget of its administrative expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
