# HSC § 25270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.67. Aboveground Storage of Petroleum

**Citation:** HSC § 25270

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Aboveground Petroleum Storage Act.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 626, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
