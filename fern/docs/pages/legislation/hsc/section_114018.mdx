# HSC § 114018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 4. General Food Safety Requirements > Article: 2. Time and Temperature Relationships

**Citation:** HSC § 114018

---

## Text

Frozen foods shall be stored and displayed in their frozen state unless being thawed in accordance with Section 114020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
