# HSC § 19204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 7. Seismic Gas Shutoff Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 19204

---

## Text

This article is limited to the service connections of individual structures to main gas lines and to connections of appliances to gas lines and does not apply to devices within gas lines.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 152, Sec. 10. Effective July 12, 1996.)
