# HSC § 12687

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12687

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell, transfer, give, or deliver any special effects fireworks to any person not licensed as a pyrotechnic operator.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
