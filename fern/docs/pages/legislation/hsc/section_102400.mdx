# HSC § 102400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 3. Live Birth Registration > Article: 1. Duty of Registering Live Birth

**Citation:** HSC § 102400

---

## Text

Each live birth shall be registered with the local registrar of births and deaths for the district in which the birth occurred within 21 days following the date of the event.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 34, Sec. 1. (AB 2176) Effective January 1, 2023.)
