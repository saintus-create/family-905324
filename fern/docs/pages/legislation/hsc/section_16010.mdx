# HSC § 16010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 2. Essential Services Buildings > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 16010

---

## Text

Essential services buildings of one-story Type V and Type II N construction that are 2,000 square feet or less in floor area are exempt from the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
