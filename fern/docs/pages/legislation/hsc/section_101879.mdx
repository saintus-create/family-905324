# HSC § 101879

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 6. Other > Article: 6. Application

**Citation:** HSC § 101879

---

## Text

It is also the intent of the Legislature that the provisions of this chapter regarding the holding of meetings of a corporation shall apply prospectively, so that any meetings held by the corporation prior to the effective date hereof shall not be deemed to have been in contravention of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 927, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
