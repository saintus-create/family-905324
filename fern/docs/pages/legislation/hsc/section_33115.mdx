# HSC § 33115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 2. Appointment, Compensation, and Removal of Agency Members

**Citation:** HSC § 33115

---

## Text

For inefficiency, neglect of duty, or misconduct in office, a member may be removed by the appointing officer, but only after he has been given a copy of the charges at least 10 days prior to a public hearing on them and has had an opportunity to be heard in person or by counsel. If a member is removed, a record of the proceedings and the charges and findings shall be filed in the office of the clerk of the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
