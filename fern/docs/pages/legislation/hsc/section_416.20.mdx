# HSC § 416.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. STATE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 7.5. Conservatorship and Guardianship for Developmentally Disabled Persons

**Citation:** HSC § 416.20

---

## Text

The director shall receive such reasonable fees for his services as guardian or conservator of the estate as the court allows and such fees shall be paid into the General Fund of the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1099.)
