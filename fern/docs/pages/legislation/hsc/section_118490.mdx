# HSC § 118490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 1. Articles of Common Use > Article: 4. Wiping Rags

**Citation:** HSC § 118490

## Text

Every person who violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
