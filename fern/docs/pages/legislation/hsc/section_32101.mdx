# HSC § 32101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 1. Election and Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 32101

---

## Text

All registered voters residing within the territory comprising a district organized under this division are qualified electors.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 932.)
