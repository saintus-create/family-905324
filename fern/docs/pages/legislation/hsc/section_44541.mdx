# HSC § 44541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 5. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** HSC § 44541

---

## Text

At such times as the authority desires to issue bonds, as defined in Section 44542, it shall adopt a resolution specifying the total amount of such bonds proposed to be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 839.)
