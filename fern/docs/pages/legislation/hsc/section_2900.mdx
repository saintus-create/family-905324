# HSC § 2900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 2900

---

## Text

At any time after the incorporation of a district, land contiguous to it may be annexed, if the board of trustees finds that the annexation will benefit both the land to be annexed and the district.
