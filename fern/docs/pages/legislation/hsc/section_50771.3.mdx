# HSC § 50771.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. Rental Housing Construction Program > Article: 6. Program Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 50771.3

---

## Text

The department may adopt, amend, or repeal emergency regulations to implement this chapter with respect to loans made with funds allocated pursuant to Section 53130. The adoption, amendment, or repeal of these regulations shall be conclusively presumed to be necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety, or general welfare within the meaning or purposes of Section 11346.1 of the Government Code. This authority shall remain in effect only until June 30, 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 100, Sec. 4. Effective July 1, 1991.)
