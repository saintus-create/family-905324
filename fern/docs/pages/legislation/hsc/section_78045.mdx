# HSC § 78045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 78045

---

## Text

“Contract competitor” means any person competing for a state contract pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 78655.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
