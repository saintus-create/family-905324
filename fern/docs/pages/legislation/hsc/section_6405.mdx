# HSC § 6405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 6405

---

## Text

“Treasurer,” as used in this part, means the treasurer of the county or counties in which a district is located.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1629.)
