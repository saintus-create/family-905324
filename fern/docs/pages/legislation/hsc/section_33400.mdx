# HSC § 33400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 8. Property Management

**Citation:** HSC § 33400

---

## Text

Within the survey area or for purposes of redevelopment an agency may:

(a) Insure or provide for the insurance of any real or personal property of the agency against risks or hazards.

(b) Rent, maintain, manage, operate, repair, and clear such real property.
