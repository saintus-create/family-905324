# HSC § 11157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11157

---

## Text

No person shall issue a prescription that is false or fictitious in any respect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
