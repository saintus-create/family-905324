# HSC § 40263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. Advisory Council

**Citation:** HSC § 40263

---

## Text

Each council member shall hold office for a term of two years and until the appointment and qualification of his successor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
