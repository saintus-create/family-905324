# HSC § 12596

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. Issuance, Revocation and Renewal

**Citation:** HSC § 12596

---

## Text

Any person or organization may obtain any license required by this part between January 1, 1974, and June 30, 1974, to be effective for that period only.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
