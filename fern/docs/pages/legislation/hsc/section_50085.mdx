# HSC § 50085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50085

---

## Text

“Mortgage assistance area” means an area characterized by a deficiency of available mortgage financing but in which general deterioration of residential structures has not occurred, and which is designated by the agency pursuant to Section 51304.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
