# HSC § 13800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 13800

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Fire Protection District Law of 1987 or as the Bergeson Fire District Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1195, Sec. 20.5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
