# HSC § 19853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 10. Building Records

**Citation:** HSC § 19853

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any building containing a bank, other financial institution, or public utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 616.)
