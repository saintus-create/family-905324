# HSC § 25188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 25188

---

## Text

A person subject to an order issued pursuant to Section 25187 who does not comply with that order shall be subject to a civil penalty of not more than seventy thousand dollars ($70,000) for each day of noncompliance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 499, Sec. 1. (AB 245) Effective January 1, 2018.)
