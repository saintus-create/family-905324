# HSC § 124120.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 6.5. Newborn and Infant Hearing Screening, Tracking, and Intervention Program

**Citation:** HSC § 124120.5

---

## Text

A newborn hearing screening test shall not be performed without the written consent of the parent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 310, Sec. 23. Effective August 19, 1998.)
