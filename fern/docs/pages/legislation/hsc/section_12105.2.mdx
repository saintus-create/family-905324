# HSC § 12105.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 1. HIGH EXPLOSIVES > Chapter: 4. Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 12105.2

---

## Text

A copy of each permit issued shall be forwarded to the State Bureau of Criminal Identification and Investigation in Sacramento.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1425.)
