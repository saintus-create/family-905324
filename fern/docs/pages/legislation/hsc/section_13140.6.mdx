# HSC § 13140.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. State Fire Marshal > Article: 2. The State Board of Fire Services

**Citation:** HSC § 13140.6

---

## Text

A quorum of the board shall consist of not less than 10 members of the board. Except as provided in Section 13140.5, proxy representation shall not be permitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 133, Sec. 3. (SB 1215) Effective July 15, 2024.)
