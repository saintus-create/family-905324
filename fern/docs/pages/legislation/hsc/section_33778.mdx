# HSC § 33778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 3. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** HSC § 33778

---

## Text

Neither the members of the agency nor any person executing the revenue bonds shall be liable personally on the revenue bonds or be subject to any personal liability or accountability by reason of the issuance thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 48.)
