# HSC § 439.906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1.6. HEALTH RESEARCH FAIRNESS

**Citation:** HSC § 439.906

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “state agency” has the same meaning as defined in Section 11000 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 792, Sec. 1.)
