# HSC § 14906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 4. Expense of Abatement > Article: 1. Determination and Notice

**Citation:** HSC § 14906

---

## Text

Before the report is submitted to the board of supervisors, a copy of it shall be posted for at least three days on or near the chamber door of the board with a notice of the time when the report will be submitted to the board for confirmation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 60.)
