# HSC § 53058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 7. SOLAR AND ENERGY CONSERVATION MORTGAGE CORPORATION > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 53058

---

## Text

“Financing” means to lend money for the purpose of purchasing, leasing, installing, or constructing energy conservation measures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1033.)
