# HSC § 112220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 5. Sanitary Control of Shellfish > Article: 2. General Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 112220

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 112210 and 112215, with respect to labeling requirements, shall not apply to any of the following:

(a) Shellstock held in dry storage under refrigerated conditions not for shipment or sale.

(b) Shellstock sold on premises when the sale is the ultimate point of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
