# HSC § 108947

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 12.5. Juvenile Products

**Citation:** HSC § 108947

---

## Text

A manufacturer shall use the least toxic alternative when replacing PFAS chemicals in a juvenile product in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 500, Sec. 1. (AB 652) Effective January 1, 2022.)
