# HSC § 19996

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 5. Appeals

**Citation:** HSC § 19996

---

## Text

The department may promulgate informal hearing rules pertaining to the hearing of appeals under the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 610, Sec. 2.9. (AB 2762) Effective January 1, 2011.)
