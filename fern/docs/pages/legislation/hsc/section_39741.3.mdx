# HSC § 39741.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 4.3. Carbon Sequestration > Article: 2. Carbon Capture, Removal, Utilization, and Storage Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39741.3

---

## Text

In furtherance of the objectives in Section 39741.1, by January 1, 2025, the state board shall develop a centralized public database to track the deployment of CCUS and CDR technologies and the development of carbon dioxide capture, removal, or sequestration projects throughout the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 359, Sec. 2. (SB 905) Effective January 1, 2023.)
