# HSC § 111625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 6. Drugs and Devices > Article: 6. Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 111625

---

## Text

A license application shall be completed biennially and accompanied by an application fee as prescribed in Section 111630. This fee is not refundable if the license is refused.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 74, Sec. 35. Effective July 12, 2006.)
