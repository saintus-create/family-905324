# HSC § 124580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4. PRIMARY HEALTH CARE > Chapter: 4. American Indian Health Services

**Citation:** HSC § 124580

---

## Text

The department shall cooperate with local governmental agencies and contract with voluntary nonprofit organizations in connection with the development of local health programs for American Indians and their families.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
