# HSC § 6716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 2. Assessment by District Assessor

**Citation:** HSC § 6716

---

## Text

No mistake in the name of the owner of any property, or any informality in the description or in other parts of the assessment, shall invalidate the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
