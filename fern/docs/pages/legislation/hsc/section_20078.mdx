# HSC § 20078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 20078

---

## Text

The district board shall keep a correct record of all its acts and proceedings, and of all its receipts and disbursements. For that purpose, it shall procure all necessary books and blanks.

The books of the district board shall be open to public inspection at all times.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
