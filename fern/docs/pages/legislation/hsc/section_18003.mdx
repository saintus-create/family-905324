# HSC § 18003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18003

---

## Text

“Distributor” means any person other than a manufacturer who sells or distributes new manufactured homes, mobilehomes, or commercial coaches to dealers in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1076, Sec. 4.)
