# HSC § 1596.79

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.79

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, partnership, association, corporation, limited liability company, or governmental entity, such as the state, a county, city, special district, school district, community college district, chartered city, or chartered city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 155. Effective January 1, 1995.)
