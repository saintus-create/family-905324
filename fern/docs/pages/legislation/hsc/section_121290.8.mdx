# HSC § 121290.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 4. HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS (HIV) > Chapter: 13.7. Statewide African-American Initiative

**Citation:** HSC § 121290.8

---

## Text

There is hereby established the Statewide African-American HIV/AIDS Steering Committee. The committee shall be appointed by the Office of AIDS and shall initially consist of the current membership of the informally established Statewide African-American HIV/AIDS Steering Committee, which consists of leadership from service providers, researchers, educators, community-based organizations, and public sector agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 403, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
