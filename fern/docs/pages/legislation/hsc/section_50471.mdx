# HSC § 50471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Building Homes and Jobs Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50471

---

## Text

The department and the California Housing Finance Agency shall report on the expenditure of funds pursuant to this chapter in accordance with Sections 50408 and 51005, respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 364, Sec. 4. (SB 2) Effective September 29, 2017.)
