# HSC § 2067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 1. Mosquito Abatement and Vector Control Districts > Article: 5. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 2067

---

## Text

Any money collected by a county from a lien authorized pursuant to this article, other than the amounts authorized pursuant to Section 29304 of the Government Code, shall be paid to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 395, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
