# HSC § 17957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 4. Application and Scope

**Citation:** HSC § 17957

---

## Text

The governing body of any city, county, or city and county may enact an ordinance prescribing an alternate procedure which is equal to or more restrictive than the procedure specified in Sections 17953, 17954, and 17955.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1001.)
