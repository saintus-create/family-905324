# HSC § 52052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 5. LOCAL HOUSING FINANCE AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 52052

---

## Text

This part is necessary for the health, welfare and safety of the state, its counties and cities and its inhabitants. Therefore, it shall be liberally construed to effect its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1069.)
