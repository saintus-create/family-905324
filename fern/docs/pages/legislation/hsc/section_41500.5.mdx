# HSC § 41500.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41500.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, any plan required by this division shall be subject to Article 5.5 (commencing with Section 53098) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 890, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2001.)
