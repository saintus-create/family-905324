# HSC § 8625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 4. Property Rights > Article: 2. Joint Tenants

**Citation:** HSC § 8625

---

## Text

In a conveyance to two or more persons as joint tenants each joint tenant has a vested right of interment in the plot conveyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
