# HSC § 18913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18913

---

## Text

“Emergency standard” means a building standard or an order of repeal of a building standard filed for publication in the code by the commission pursuant to Section 11346.1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1124, Sec. 33. Effective September 30, 2002.)
