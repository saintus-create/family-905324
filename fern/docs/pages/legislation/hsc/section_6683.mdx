# HSC § 6683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 4. Exchange of Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 6683

---

## Text

After the exchange the old bonds shall be canceled by punching holes in the signatures, and shall be retained by the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
