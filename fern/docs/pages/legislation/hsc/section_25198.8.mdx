# HSC § 25198.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8.6. Development of Hazardous Waste Management Facilities on Indian Country

**Citation:** HSC § 25198.8

---

## Text

A cooperative agreement executed pursuant to this article shall be executed for the express benefit of the citizens of this state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 805, Sec. 3.)
