# HSC § 17972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 2. Inspection

**Citation:** HSC § 17972

---

## Text

No person authorized by this article to enter buildings shall enter any dwelling between the hours of 6 o'clock p.m. of any day and 8 o'clock a.m. of the succeeding day, without the consent of the owner or of the occupants of the dwelling, nor enter any dwelling in the absence of the occupants without a proper written order executed and issued by a court having jurisdiction to issue the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1844.)
