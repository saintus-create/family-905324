# HSC § 109590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 4. DRUGS, DEVICES, AND COSMETICS > Chapter: 5. Imitation Controlled Substances > Article: 2. Offenses and Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 109590

---

## Text

All imitation controlled substances shall be subject to forfeiture in accordance with the procedures set forth in Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 11470) of Division 10.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
