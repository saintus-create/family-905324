# HSC § 116015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 3. Life Saving Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 116015

---

## Text

No person shall maintain a resort unless safety ropes are stretched wherever necessary to show the line of eddies, pools, sunken obstructions, and other hidden dangers to bathers in the water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
