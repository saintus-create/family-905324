# HSC § 8102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Vandalism

**Citation:** HSC § 8102

---

## Text

Any person violating any provision of this chapter is liable, in a civil action by and in the name of the cemetery authority, to pay all damages occasioned by his unlawful acts. The sum recovered shall be applied in payment for the repair and restoration of the property injured or destroyed.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
