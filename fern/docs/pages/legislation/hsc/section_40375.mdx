# HSC § 40375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40375

---

## Text

The treasurers of the counties included, in whole or in part, within a regional district shall pay into the regional district treasury all funds held by them to the credit of the regional district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1063.)
