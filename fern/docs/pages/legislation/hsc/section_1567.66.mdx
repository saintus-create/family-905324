# HSC § 1567.66

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 9.5. Enhanced Behavioral Supports Homes

**Citation:** HSC § 1567.66

---

## Text

An enhanced behavioral supports home employing secured perimeters shall comply with Section 1531.15 and applicable regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 30, Sec. 6. (SB 856) Effective June 20, 2014.)
