# HSC § 6700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6700

---

## Text

The payment of the principal and interest of all bonds, within forty years from their issuance, is the obligation of the district; and, if necessary to accomplish that purpose, a special tax shall be levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
