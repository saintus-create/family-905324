# HSC § 33779

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 3. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** HSC § 33779

---

## Text

The agency shall have the power out of any funds available therefor to purchase its revenue bonds. The agency may hold, pledge, cancel, or resell such revenue bonds, subject to and in accordance with agreements with the bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 48.)
