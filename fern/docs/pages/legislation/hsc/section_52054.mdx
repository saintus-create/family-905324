# HSC § 52054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 5. LOCAL HOUSING FINANCE AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 52054

---

## Text

If any provision of this part or the application thereof to any person or circumstances is held invalid, such invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of the part which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to this end the provisions of this part are severable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1069.)
