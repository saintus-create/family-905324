# HSC § 1385.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 6.2. Review of Rate Increases

**Citation:** HSC § 1385.05

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provision in a contract between a health care service plan and a provider, the department may request from a health care service plan any information required under this article or PPACA.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 661, Sec. 4. (SB 1163) Effective January 1, 2011.)
