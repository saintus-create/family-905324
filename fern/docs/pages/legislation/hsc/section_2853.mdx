# HSC § 2853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 2853

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2006.)
