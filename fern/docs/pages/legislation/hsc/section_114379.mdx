# HSC § 114379

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 12.8. Children’s Meals

**Citation:** HSC § 114379

---

## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to support children’s health by setting nutritional standards for a restaurant’s children’s meals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 608, Sec. 2. (SB 1192) Effective January 1, 2019.)
