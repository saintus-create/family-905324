# HSC § 25123.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25123.4

---

## Text

“Transportable hazardous waste treatment unit” or “transportable treatment unit” means mobile equipment which performs treatment, is transported onto a facility to perform treatment, and is not permanently stationed at a single facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1151, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
