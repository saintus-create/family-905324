# HSC § 50866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 14.5. Building Equity and Growth in Neighborhoods Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50866

---

## Text

No more than 5 percent of the funds deposited in the BEGIN Fund may be used to pay the costs of administration of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 724, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
