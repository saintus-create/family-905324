# HSC § 114332.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 10.5. Nonprofit Charitable Temporary Food Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 114332.4

---

## Text

The enforcement officer may establish additional structural or operational requirements as necessary to ensure that food is of a safe and sanitary quality.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
