# HSC § 115985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 3. Life Saving Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 115985

---

## Text

The boat shall be fully equipped with oars, oarlocks, and not less than two life preservers, and two hundred feet of rope.

It shall be kept in good repair and near the resort.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
