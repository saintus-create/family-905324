# HSC § 39042.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39042.5

---

## Text

“New motor vehicle engine” means a new engine in a motor vehicle.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1206.)
