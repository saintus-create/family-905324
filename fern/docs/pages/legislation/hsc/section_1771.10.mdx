# HSC § 1771.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Continuing Care Contracts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1771.10

---

## Text

Each provider shall adopt a comprehensive disaster preparedness plan specifying policies for evacuation, relocation, continued services, reconstruction, organizational structure, insurance coverage, resident education, and plant replacement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1771.11 by Stats. 2000, Ch. 820, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2001.)
