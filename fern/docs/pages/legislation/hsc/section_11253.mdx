# HSC § 11253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 5. Use of Controlled Substances > Article: 4. Sale Without Prescription

**Citation:** HSC § 11253

---

## Text

The written orders or blank forms shall be preserved for at least three years after the date of the last entry made.
