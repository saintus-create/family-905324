# HSC § 14876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 14876

---

## Text

Weeds may be declared a public nuisance and may be abated as provided in this part.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
