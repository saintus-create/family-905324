# HSC § 440.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1.7. HEALTH FACILITIES DISCLOSURE ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 440.50

---

## Text

No information, other than the itemized billing set forth in Section 440.20, that is prohibited from being released by any other provision of law shall be made available pursuant to Section 440.20.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1049, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
