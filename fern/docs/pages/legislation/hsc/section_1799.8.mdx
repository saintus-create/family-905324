# HSC § 1799.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. The Commission on Emergency Medical Services > Article: 1. The Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 1799.8

---

## Text

The commission shall select a chairperson from its members and shall meet at least quarterly on the call of the director, the chairperson, or three members of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
