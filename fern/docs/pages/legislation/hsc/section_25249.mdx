# HSC § 25249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 12. Financial Responsibility and Closure and Maintenance of Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 25249

---

## Text

On the effective date of this article, any operator subject to former Division 7.5 (commencing with Section 14000) of the Water Code shall be subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 90, Sec. 3. Effective March 2, 1982.)
