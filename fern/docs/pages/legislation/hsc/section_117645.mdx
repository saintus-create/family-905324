# HSC § 117645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 117645

---

## Text

“Container” means the rigid container in which the medical waste is placed prior to transporting for purposes of storage or treatment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
