# HSC § 11760.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Coordination of Services

**Citation:** HSC § 11760.6

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the department encourage the development of high quality, cost-effective services. It is further the intent of the Legislature that poor quality, underutilized, duplicative, or marginal services be disapproved by the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 2005.)
