# HSC § 102769

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Birth Following Adoption, Legitimation, Court Determination of Parentage, and Acknowledgment > Article: 4. Voluntary Declaration of Parentage

**Citation:** HSC § 102769

---

## Text

The State Registrar shall furnish a certified copy of the new record of birth prepared under authority of this article to the registrant without additional cost.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1062, Sec. 19.5. Effective January 1, 1997.)
