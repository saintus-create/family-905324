# HSC § 33358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 4.5. Alternative Procedures for a Joint Public Hearing by the Agency and the Legislative Body

**Citation:** HSC § 33358

---

## Text

After receipt of the approval and any recommendations for changes as well as the report and recommendation of the planning commission of the recommended changes, the legislative body may proceed to act upon the plan pursuant to Sections 33363.5 to 33375, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1242.)
