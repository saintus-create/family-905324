# HSC § 116555.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 4. California Safe Drinking Water Act > Article: 7. Requirements and Compliance

**Citation:** HSC § 116555.5

---

## Text

A public water system shall implement a cross-connection control program that complies with applicable regulations and with standards adopted by the board pursuant to Section 116407.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 533, Sec. 2. (AB 1671) Effective January 1, 2018.)
