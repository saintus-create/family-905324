# HSC § 11839.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 2. Narcotic Treatment Program Body Fluids Testing

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.28

---

## Text

Every laboratory that has been approved and for which a license has been issued shall be periodically inspected by a duly authorized representative of the State Department of Health Services. Reports of this inspection shall be prepared by the representative conducting it upon forms prepared and furnished by the State Department of Health Services and shall be filed with that department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2005.)
