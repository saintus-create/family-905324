# HSC § 17003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1. EMPLOYEE HOUSING ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 17003

---

## Text

“Commission,” as used in this part, means the Commission of Housing and Community Development.
