# HSC § 11832.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.1. Certification of Alcohol and Other Drug Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11832.8

---

## Text

(a) An alcohol or other drug program shall adopt policies and procedures that are consistent with this chapter and any regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter.

(b) All policies and procedures shall be kept in an operation manual and address and include, at a minimum, all of the following:

(1) Admission and discharge.

(2) Client rights.

(3) Services.

(4) Medications.

(5) Staff and client code of conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 32. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
