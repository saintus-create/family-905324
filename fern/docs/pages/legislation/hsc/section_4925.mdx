# HSC § 4925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 7. Dissolution

**Citation:** HSC § 4925

---

## Text

When all territory in a district has been included in a city the district is thereupon, by reason of the inclusion, dissolved.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
