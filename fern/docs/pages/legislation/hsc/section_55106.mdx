# HSC § 55106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 32. SEISMIC SAFETY BUILDING REHABILITATION LOANS > Part: 2. BONDS

**Citation:** HSC § 55106

## Text

Bond underwriters and consultants may be selected by the local agency.
