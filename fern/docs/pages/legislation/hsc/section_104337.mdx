# HSC § 104337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 2. INJURY PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Roman Reed Spinal Cord Injury Research Act of 1999

**Citation:** HSC § 104337

---

## Text

Moneys in the fund established pursuant to Section 104336 may be expended by the University of California solely for spinal cord injury research programs and grants, and may be expended for, but not limited to, costs for peer review and grant administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 457, Sec. 2. (AB 1931) Effective January 1, 2011.)
