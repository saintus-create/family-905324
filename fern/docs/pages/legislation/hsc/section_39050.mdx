# HSC § 39050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39050

---

## Text

“Regional district board” means the governing body of a regional district.
