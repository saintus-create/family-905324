# HSC § 114379.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 12.8. Children’s Meals

**Citation:** HSC § 114379.40

---

## Text

The local enforcement agency, as set forth in Section 113713, shall implement, administer, and enforce this chapter. The department may issue rules and regulations as necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 608, Sec. 2. (SB 1192) Effective January 1, 2019.)
