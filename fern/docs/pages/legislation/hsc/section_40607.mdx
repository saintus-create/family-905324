# HSC § 40607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.7. San Joaquin Valley Unified Air Pollution Control District

**Citation:** HSC § 40607

---

## Text

The district shall install one or more monitors for monitoring airborne fine particles smaller than 2.5 microns in diameter (PM 2.5) in primarily low-income and underserved areas in the western region of the County of Fresno.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 569, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
