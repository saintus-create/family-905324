# HSC § 6481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6481

---

## Text

The board is the governing power of the district, and exercises all district powers, except the making of an assessment roll in the first instance.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
