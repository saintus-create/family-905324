# HSC § 108946

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 12.5. Juvenile Products

**Citation:** HSC § 108946

---

## Text

On and after July 1, 2023, a person, including, but not limited to, a manufacturer, shall not sell or distribute in commerce in this state any new, not previously owned, juvenile product that contains regulated PFAS chemicals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 500, Sec. 1. (AB 652) Effective January 1, 2022.)
