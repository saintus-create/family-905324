# HSC § 7302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Embalming and Transportation > Article: 1. Embalming

**Citation:** HSC § 7302

---

## Text

Every funeral director and embalmer shall immediately report to the local health officer every contagious case on which the funeral director or embalmer may be called.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
