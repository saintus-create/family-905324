# HSC § 114900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 7. Atomic Energy Development > Article: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** HSC § 114900

---

## Text

This chapter may be cited and shall be known as the California Atomic Energy Development Law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
