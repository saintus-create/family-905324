# HSC § 104187.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 1. CHRONIC DISEASE > Chapter: 2. Cancer > Article: 2. The Cancer Research Act of 1997

**Citation:** HSC § 104187.5

---

## Text

Peer review panels, in reviewing proposals for cancer research, may recommend the awarding of a grant to a cancer research proposal on the condition that a single adjustment or correction be made before the receipt of funding under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 751, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2000.)
