# HSC § 18910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18910

---

## Text

“Code” means the California Building Standards Code, including the triennial editions and supplements.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 897, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
