# HSC § 54901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 17. California Surplus Land Unit > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 54901

## Text

The California Surplus Land Unit is hereby created within the Department of Housing and Community Development.
