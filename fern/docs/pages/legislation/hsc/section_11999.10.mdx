# HSC § 11999.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.8. SUBSTANCE ABUSE TREATMENT FUNDING

**Citation:** HSC § 11999.10

---

## Text

The department shall allocate up to 0.5 percent of the fund’s total moneys each year to fund the costs of the studies required in Section 11999.9 by a public or private university.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 63, Sec. 4. Effective July 12, 2006. Note: This section was added on Nov. 7, 2000, by initiative Prop. 36.)
