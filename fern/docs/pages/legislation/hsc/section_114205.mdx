# HSC § 114205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 7. Water, Plumbing, and Waste > Article: 3. Mobile Water and Wastewater Tanks

**Citation:** HSC § 114205

---

## Text

(a) Nonpermanent food facilities that handle nonprepackaged food shall be equipped with potable water and wastewater tanks, unless approved temporary water and wastewater connections are provided.

(b) Permanent food facilities shall be in compliance with Sections 114190 to 114201, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
