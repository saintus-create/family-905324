# HSC § 53531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 11. HOUSING AND EMERGENCY SHELTER TRUST FUND ACT OF 2002 > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 53531

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund that is derived from premiums and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 26, Sec. 8. Approved in Proposition 46 at the November 5, 2002, election.)
