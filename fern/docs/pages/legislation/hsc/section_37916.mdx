# HSC § 37916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 13. RESIDENTIAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 37916

---

## Text

The local agency may issue bonds and bond anticipation notes of the local agency for the purpose of financing residential rehabilitation authorized by this part and for the purpose of funding or refunding such bonds or notes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1201.)
