# HSC § 4956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 4956

---

## Text

“Bonds,” as used in this chapter, means revenue bonds authorized by this chapter.
