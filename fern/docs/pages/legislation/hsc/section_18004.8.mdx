# HSC § 18004.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18004.8

---

## Text

“Good moral character” has the same meaning as specified in Division 1.5 (commencing with Section 475) of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 975, Sec. 3.)
