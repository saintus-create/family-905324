# HSC § 5049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 7. Rates and Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 5049

---

## Text

A copy of the schedule of the rates shall be kept on file in the office of the clerk, and shall be open to inspection by any interested person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
