# HSC § 8250.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8250.5

---

## Text

As used in Section 8250 of this code, a public cemetery is a cemetery owned and operated by a city, county, city and county, or public cemetery district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 386.)
