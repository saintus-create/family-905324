# HSC § 11829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 6. Evaluation > Article: 2. County Program Evaluation

**Citation:** HSC § 11829

---

## Text

The department may disseminate information about the evaluation projects to all counties and interested persons throughout the state in order to advance the knowledge of the field about these evaluation methodologies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1328, Sec. 61.)
