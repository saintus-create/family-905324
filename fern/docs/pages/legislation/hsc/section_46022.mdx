# HSC § 46022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NOISE CONTROL ACT > Chapter: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 46022

---

## Text

“Noise” means and includes excessive undesirable sound, including that produced by persons, pets and livestock, industrial equipment, construction, motor vehicles, boats, aircraft, home appliances, electric motors, combustion engines, and any other noise-producing objects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
