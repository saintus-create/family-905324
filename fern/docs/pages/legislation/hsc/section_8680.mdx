# HSC § 8680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 4. Property Rights > Article: 5. Voluntary Establishment of Inalienability

**Citation:** HSC § 8680

---

## Text

A cemetery authority may take and hold any plot conveyed or devised to it by the plot owner so that it will be inalienable, and interments shall be restricted to the persons designated in the conveyance or devise.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
