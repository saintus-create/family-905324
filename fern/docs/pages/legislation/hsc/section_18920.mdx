# HSC § 18920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. Organization > Article: 1. The California Building Standards Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 18920

---

## Text

There is continued in existence in the Government Operations Agency a California Building Standards Commission consisting of the Secretary of Government Operations and 10 members appointed by the Governor subject to confirmation by the Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 345. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
