# HSC § 109992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 109992

---

## Text

“Pasteurized in-shell eggs” means shell eggs that have been pasteurized by any method approved by the federal Food and Drug Administration, the Department of Food and Agriculture, or the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 11, Sec. 6. (AB 1414) Effective April 17, 2014.)
