# HSC § 1416.45

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.35. Nursing Home Administrator Program > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1416.45

---

## Text

A licensee may not engage in licensed activity while his or her license is suspended or revoked, or after it has expired.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 687, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
