# HSC § 50081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50081

---

## Text

“Market rent” means the monthly rent established by the agency as competitive according to its own regulations, except where federal regulations provide a required method of determining market rent. Determination of market rent may be reviewed annually upon application by the mortgagor, subject to applicable federal regulations, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
