# HSC § 1248.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.3. Outpatient Settings

**Citation:** HSC § 1248.65

---

## Text

It shall constitute unprofessional conduct for a physician and surgeon to willfully and knowingly violate this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1276, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
