# HSC § 2910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 5a. Consolidation

**Citation:** HSC § 2910

---

## Text

Two or more contiguous pest abatement districts may be consolidated.
