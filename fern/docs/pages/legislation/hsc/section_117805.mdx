# HSC § 117805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 117805

---

## Text

A local agency that elects to implement a medical waste management program shall notify the department of its intent to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 564, Sec. 25. (AB 333) Effective January 1, 2015.)
