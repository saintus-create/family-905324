# HSC § 6402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 6402

---

## Text

“Secretary,” as used in this part, means the secretary of a district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
