# HSC § 7013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7013

---

## Text

“Burial” means the process of placing human remains in a grave.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 436, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2002.)
