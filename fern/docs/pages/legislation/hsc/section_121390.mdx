# HSC § 121390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 5. TUBERCULOSIS > Chapter: 1. Tuberculosis Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121390

---

## Text

The department shall lease any facilities it deems necessary to care for persons afflicted with active contagious tuberculosis who violate the quarantine or isolation orders of the health officer as provided in Section 120280.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
