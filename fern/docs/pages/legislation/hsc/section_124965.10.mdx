# HSC § 124965.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4.6. Jacqueline Marie Zbur Rare Disease Advisory Council

**Citation:** HSC § 124965.10

---

## Text

The advisory council shall apply for, and accept, any grant of funds from the federal government, private foundations, or other sources that may be available for programs related to rare diseases. The advisory council shall not accept any funds from the employer of a currently seated council member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 2613) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 124965.14.)
