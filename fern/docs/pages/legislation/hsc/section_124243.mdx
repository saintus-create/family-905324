# HSC § 124243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 4. Adolescent Health > Article: 2.7. California Youth Football Act

**Citation:** HSC § 124243

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall prohibit any youth sports organization or youth tackle football league from adopting and enforcing rules intended to provide a higher standard of safety for youth tackle football participants than the requirements established under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 158, Sec. 2. (AB 1) Effective January 1, 2020.)
