# HSC § 39022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39022

---

## Text

“County district” means a district continued in existence pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 40100) of Part 3.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
