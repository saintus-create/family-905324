# HSC § 50082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50082

---

## Text

“Metropolitan area” means a standard metropolitan statistical area as established by the United States Office of Management and Budget.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
