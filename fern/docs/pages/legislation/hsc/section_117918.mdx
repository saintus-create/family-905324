# HSC § 117918

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 4. Small Quantity Generator Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 117918

---

## Text

Medical waste shall be treated using treatment technologies in accordance with Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 118215).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 564, Sec. 35. (AB 333) Effective January 1, 2015.)
