# HSC § 14838

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 14838

---

## Text

The secretary of every company having a seal shall take the constitutional oath of office and give a bond as the bylaws provide for the faithful performance of his or her duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2009.)
