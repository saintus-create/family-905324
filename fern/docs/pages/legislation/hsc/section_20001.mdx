# HSC § 20001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 20001

---

## Text

“Board,” as used in this chapter, means the board of supervisors of the county in which it is proposed to form a district, or in which a district has been formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
