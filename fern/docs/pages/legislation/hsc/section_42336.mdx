# HSC § 42336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 1.5. District Review of a Permit Applicant’s Compliance History

**Citation:** HSC § 42336

---

## Text

In addition to any other information required to be submitted, an applicant for a permit to construct or a permit to operate which involves a change of operator who has owned or operated a facility pursuant to a permit issued by any district shall provide a description of all emissions violations satisfying the criteria specified in paragraphs (1) to (3), inclusive, of subdivision (a) of Section 42333, under this division or any regulation adopted pursuant to this division, and the Clean Air Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 7401 and following) or any regulations adopted thereunder, which occurred at any facility permitted by any district and owned or operated by the applicant in the state in the three years prior to the date of application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1209, Sec. 3.)
