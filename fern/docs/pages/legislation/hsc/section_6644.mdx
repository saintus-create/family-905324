# HSC § 6644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6644

---

## Text

If, at the election, two-thirds of the votes cast are in favor of the issuance of bonds, the board may issue and dispose of the bonds as proposed in the order calling the election.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1874.)
