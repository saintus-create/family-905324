# HSC § 1399.76

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 11. Nonprofit Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 1399.76

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to a nonprofit health care service plan restructure or conversion that has been submitted as a material modification to the department for review and approval prior to May 16, 1995.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 792, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
