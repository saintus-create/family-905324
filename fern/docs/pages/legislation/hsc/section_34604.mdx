# HSC § 34604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Housing Operations Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34604

---

## Text

“Local housing authority” means a housing authority created pursuant to Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 34200), Part 2, Division 24 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 579.)
