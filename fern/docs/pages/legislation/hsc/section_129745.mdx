# HSC § 129745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 129745

---

## Text

“Structural engineer” means a person who is validly certified to use the title structural engineer under Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 6700) of Division 3 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
