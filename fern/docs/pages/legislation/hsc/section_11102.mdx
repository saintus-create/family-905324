# HSC § 11102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 3. Regulation and Control > Article: 1. Reporting

**Citation:** HSC § 11102

---

## Text

The Department of Justice may adopt all regulations necessary to carry out the provisions of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1072.)
