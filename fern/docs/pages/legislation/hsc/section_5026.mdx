# HSC § 5026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 6. Finances

**Citation:** HSC § 5026

---

## Text

All compensation of employees, and all other expenses, incurred in carrying out the provisions of this chapter shall be paid solely from funds provided under the authority of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
