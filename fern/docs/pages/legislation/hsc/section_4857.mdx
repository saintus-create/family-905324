# HSC § 4857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 9. Special Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 4857

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall fix a special tax rate and levy a special assessment tax each year upon the real property in such zone, based upon the last equalized assessment roll of the county, sufficient to pay such expenses or the portion thereof which must be paid by the zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 794.)
