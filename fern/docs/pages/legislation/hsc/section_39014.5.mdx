# HSC § 39014.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39014.5

---

## Text

“Antelope Valley district board” means the governing board of the Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 163, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
