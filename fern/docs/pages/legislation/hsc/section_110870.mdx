# HSC § 110870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110870

---

## Text

Only products that have been handled and processed in accordance with this article may be certified by a registered certification organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 533, Sec. 57. Effective January 1, 2003.)
