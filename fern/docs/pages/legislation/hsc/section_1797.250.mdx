# HSC § 1797.250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 2. Local Emergency Medical Services Planning

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.250

---

## Text

In each designated EMS area, the local EMS agency may develop and submit a plan to the authority for an emergency medical services system according to the guidelines prescribed pursuant to Section 1797.103.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
