# HSC § 14832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 14832

---

## Text

The city council of an incorporated city may, by ordinance, regulate the formation and continued existence of fire companies providing services within its city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2009.)
