# HSC § 127443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 2.5. Fair Pricing Policies > Article: 1. Hospital Fair Pricing Policies

**Citation:** HSC § 127443

---

## Text

The rights, remedies, and penalties established by this article are cumulative, and shall not supersede the rights, remedies, or penalties established under other laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 755, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
