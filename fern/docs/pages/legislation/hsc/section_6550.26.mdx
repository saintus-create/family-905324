# HSC § 6550.26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 4. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 6550.26

---

## Text

Territory annexed to an improvement district shall be subject to existing bond issues and indebtedness of the improvement district from and after the filing with the county assessor specified in Section 6550.25.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1055.)
