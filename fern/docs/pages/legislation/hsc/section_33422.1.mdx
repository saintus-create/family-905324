# HSC § 33422.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 10. Demolition, Clearance, Project Improvements, and Site Preparation

**Citation:** HSC § 33422.1

---

## Text

To the greatest extent feasible, contracts for work to be performed in connection with any redevelopment project shall be awarded to business concerns which are located in, or owned in the substantial part by persons residing in, the project area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1757.)
