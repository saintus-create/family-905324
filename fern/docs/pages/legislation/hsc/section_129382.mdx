# HSC § 129382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 4. Distressed Hospital Loan Program

**Citation:** HSC § 129382

---

## Text

(a) The department shall administer the program, subject to subdivision (d) of Section 129385, for purposes of this chapter.

(b) The department shall enter into an interagency agreement with the authority to implement this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 6, Sec. 3. (AB 112) Effective May 15, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 129387.)
