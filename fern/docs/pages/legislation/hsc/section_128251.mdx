# HSC § 128251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 4. Health Care Workforce Training Programs > Article: 2. California Health Workforce Education and Training Council

**Citation:** HSC § 128251

---

## Text

The members of the council, other than state employees, shall receive compensation of twenty-five dollars ($25) for each day’s attendance at a council meeting, in addition to actual and necessary travel expenses incurred in the course of attendance at a council meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 47, Sec. 33. (SB 184) Effective June 30, 2022.)
