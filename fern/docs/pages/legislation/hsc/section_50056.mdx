# HSC § 50056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50056

---

## Text

“Below-market interest” means a below-market interest rate adequate to return to the agency sufficient income to meet its obligations, reserve requirements, and expenses connected with a loan financed by the agency for which such interest rate is established as determined by the agency at the time of commitment of funds, for the permanent financing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
