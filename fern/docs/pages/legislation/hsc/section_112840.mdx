# HSC § 112840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 8. Canneries > Article: 6. Rules and Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 112840

---

## Text

The district attorney of the county in which any violation of this chapter occurs shall prosecute the person accused of the violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
