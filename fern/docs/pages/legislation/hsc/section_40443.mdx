# HSC § 40443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 40443

---

## Text

The south coast district board shall adopt revised and updated nonvehicular source emission limitations for inclusion in the state’s implementation plan.
