# HSC § 40391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 6. Withdrawal of County From Regional District

**Citation:** HSC § 40391

---

## Text

The withdrawal of a county, or portion thereof, shall not affect the functioning of other counties within the regional district, and such withdrawal shall not constitute a dissolution of the regional district.

The regional district shall continue to function in a manner not inconsistent with the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
