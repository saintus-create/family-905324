# HSC § 11836.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 9. Services to Persons Convicted for Driving While Under the Influence of Alcohol and Other Drugs

**Citation:** HSC § 11836.16

---

## Text

The State Department of Health Care Services shall adopt regulations for satellite offices of driving-under-the-influence programs. The regulations shall include, but not be limited to, any limitations on where a satellite office may be located and the minimum and maximum number of clients to whom a satellite office may provide services. When adopting regulations pursuant to this section, the department shall also consider an appropriate licensing procedure for these offices. For purposes of this section, a “satellite office” is an offsite location of an existing licensed driving-under-the-influence program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 22, Sec. 45. (AB 75) Effective June 27, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 110 of Ch. 22.)
