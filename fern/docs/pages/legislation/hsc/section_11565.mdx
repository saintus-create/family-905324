# HSC § 11565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 2. Substance Abuse Treatment Control Units

**Citation:** HSC § 11565

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “parole authority” has the same meaning as described in Section 3000 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 695, Sec. 8. Effective September 15, 1992.)
