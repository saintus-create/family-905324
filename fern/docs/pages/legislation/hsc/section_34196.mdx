# HSC § 34196

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.9. Alternative Voluntary Redevelopment Program > Chapter: 5. Auditor-Controller Fee

**Citation:** HSC § 34196

---

## Text

The auditor-controller may charge a city or county a fee that does not exceed the reasonable costs of the auditor-controller to implement the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 6, Sec. 2. (AB 27 1x) Effective June 29, 2011. Ruled unconstitutional per California Redevelopment Assn. v. Matosantos (2011) 53 Cal.4th 231.)
