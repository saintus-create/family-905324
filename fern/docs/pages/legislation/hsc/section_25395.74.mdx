# HSC § 25395.74

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.82. California Land Reuse and Revitalization Act of 2004 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25395.74

---

## Text

“Hazardous material” has the same meaning as defined in subdivision (d) of Section 25260.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 705, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005. Repealed as of January 1, 2037, pursuant to Section 25395.109, with continuing effect as provided in Section 25395.110.)
