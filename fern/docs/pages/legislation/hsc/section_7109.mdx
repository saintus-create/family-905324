# HSC § 7109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Custody, and Duty of Interment

**Citation:** HSC § 7109

---

## Text

The court shall allow costs and reasonable attorney’s fees to a prevailing plaintiff against all defendants, other than the coroner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 436, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2002.)
