# HSC § 18210.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

## Text

“Manufactured home” as used in this part shall have the same meaning as defined in Section 18007.
