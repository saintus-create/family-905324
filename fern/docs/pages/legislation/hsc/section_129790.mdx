# HSC § 129790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 129790

---

## Text

The department shall propose specific space, architectural, structural, mechanical, plumbing, and electrical standards for correctional treatment centers in cooperation with the Board of Corrections, the Department of Corrections, and the Department of the Youth Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 290. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
