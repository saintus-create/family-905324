# HSC § 104820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 3. Oral Health > Article: 3. Dental Disease Prevention Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 104820

---

## Text

The dental disease prevention program established by this article shall be funded according to customary budget procedures and shall only be implemented upon appropriation of funds by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 11. Effective July 28, 2009.)
