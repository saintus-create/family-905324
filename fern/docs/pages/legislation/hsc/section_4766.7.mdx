# HSC § 4766.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 4766.7

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 4766.5 (as added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 21) by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 299. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
