# HSC § 101035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Local Health Officers and Local Health Departments > Article: 1. County Health Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 101035

---

## Text

The county health officer shall advise on medical matters any board or body vested with the management of any county pension or retirement system and shall attend the meetings of the board or body when requested by the board or body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
