# HSC § 11491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 8. Seizure and Disposition

**Citation:** HSC § 11491

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to extend or change decisional law as it relates to the topic of search and seizure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11499 by Stats. 1983, Ch. 948, Sec. 30.)
