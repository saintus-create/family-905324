# HSC § 9629

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 2. Structural and Material Requirements of Community Mausoleums and Columbariums

**Citation:** HSC § 9629

---

## Text

Footings shall be designed and constructed to conform to the requirements of the Uniform Building Code or specifications of a licensed geotechnical engineer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1994.)
