# HSC § 113748

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113748

---

## Text

“Commingle” means:

(a) To combine shellstock harvested on different days or from different growing areas as identified on the tag or label.

(b) To combine shucked shellfish from containers with different container codes or different shucking dates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
