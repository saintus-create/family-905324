# HSC § 128458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5. Health Professions Education Programs > Article: 3.5. Licensed Mental Health Service Provider Education Program

**Citation:** HSC § 128458

---

## Text

There is hereby established in the State Treasury the Mental Health Practitioner Education Fund. The moneys in the fund, upon appropriation by the Legislature, shall be available for expenditure by the Department of Health Care Access and Information for purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 181. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
