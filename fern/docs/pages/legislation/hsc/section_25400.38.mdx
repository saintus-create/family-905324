# HSC § 25400.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.9.1. Methamphetamine or Fentanyl Contaminated Property Cleanup Act > Article: 6. Requirements for Property Assessment and Cleanup

**Citation:** HSC § 25400.38

---

## Text

The PSA work plan and PSA report shall be signed and notarized by the contractor responsible for the completion of the preliminary site assessment and by a certified industrial hygienist for sufficiency and completeness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 570, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
