# HSC § 44341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 6. AIR TOXICS “HOT SPOTS” INFORMATION AND ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 3. Air Toxics Emission Inventories

**Citation:** HSC § 44341

---

## Text

Within 180 days after approval of a plan by the district, the operator shall implement the plan and prepare and submit a report to the district in accordance with the plan. The district shall transmit all monitoring data contained in the approved report to the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1252, Sec. 1. Section operative July 1, 1988, pursuant to Section 44384.)
