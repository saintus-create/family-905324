# HSC § 4862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 4862

---

## Text

“Board,” as used in this chapter, means the board of supervisors of the county in which a district is formed, or in which it is proposed to form a district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
