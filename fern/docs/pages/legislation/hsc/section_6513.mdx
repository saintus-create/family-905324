# HSC § 6513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6513

---

## Text

It may permit the use of any property of the district by any county or municipality, or any other district or governmental agency.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1944, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 53.)
