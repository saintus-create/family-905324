# HSC § 12591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. Issuance, Revocation and Renewal

**Citation:** HSC § 12591

---

## Text

The State Fire Marshal may, upon three days notice, suspend any license for a period not exceeding 30 days pending investigation of any violation of the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
