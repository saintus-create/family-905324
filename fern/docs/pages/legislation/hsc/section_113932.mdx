# HSC § 113932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113932

---

## Text

“Transporter” means any vehicle used to transport food pursuant to a prior order from a manufacturer, distributor, retail food facility, or other approved source to a retail food facility or consumer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 18. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
