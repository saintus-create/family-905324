# HSC § 1404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Referral Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1404

---

## Text

No licensee under this chapter shall have a direct or indirect financial interest in any medical facility doing business with the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 924.)
