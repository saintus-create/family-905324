# HSC § 11241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 5. Use of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Veterinarians

**Citation:** HSC § 11241

---

## Text

A prescription written by a veterinarian shall state the kind of animal for which ordered and the name and address of the owner or person having custody of the animal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
