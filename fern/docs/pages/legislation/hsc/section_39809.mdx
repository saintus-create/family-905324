# HSC § 39809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 5. Air Pollution Control Subvention Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39809

---

## Text

The state board may allocate to itself sufficient moneys to administer the subvention program under this chapter and to conduct reviews authorized by Section 39808.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
