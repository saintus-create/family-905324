# HSC § 40805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40805

---

## Text

Within 30 days after a regional district begins to function and exercise its powers, the regional district board shall appoint a hearing board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
