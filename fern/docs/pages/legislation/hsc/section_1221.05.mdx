# HSC § 1221.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 2.5. Licensing of Clinics

**Citation:** HSC § 1221.05

---

## Text

Commencing July 1, 2002, all new applications for licenses for clinics shall be reviewed by the centralized applications unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 525, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
