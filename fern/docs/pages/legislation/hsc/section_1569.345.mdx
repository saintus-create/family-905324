# HSC § 1569.345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.345

---

## Text

Upon request, the department shall provide the Office of the State Long-Term Care Ombudsman and any approved organizations of the office with copies of inspection reports for residential care facilities for the elderly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1127, Sec. 3.)
