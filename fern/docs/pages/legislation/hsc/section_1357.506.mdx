# HSC § 1357.506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.16. Nongrandfathered Small Employer Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.506

---

## Text

A small employer health care service plan contract shall not impose a preexisting condition provision or a waiting or affiliation period upon any individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 195, Sec. 4. (SB 1034) Effective January 1, 2015.)
