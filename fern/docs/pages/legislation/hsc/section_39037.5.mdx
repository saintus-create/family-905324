# HSC § 39037.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39037.5

---

## Text

“Medium-duty” means a heavy-duty vehicle having a manufacturer’s gross vehicle weight rating under a limit established by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 370, Sec. 201. (SB 1371) Effective January 1, 2021.)
