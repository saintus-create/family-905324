# HSC § 44241.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 7. District Fees to Implement the California Clean Air Act

**Citation:** HSC § 44241.5

---

## Text

The bay district board shall hold an annual public hearing to review the expenditure of revenues received by the bay district pursuant to Section 44241 to determine their effectiveness in improving air quality.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 950, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
