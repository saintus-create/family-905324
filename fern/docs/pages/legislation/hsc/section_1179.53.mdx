# HSC § 1179.53

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6.1. CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2008 > Chapter: 2. The Children’s Hospital Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.53

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this part shall be deposited in the Children’s Hospital Bond Act Fund, which is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 4, 2008, by initiative Proposition 3.)
