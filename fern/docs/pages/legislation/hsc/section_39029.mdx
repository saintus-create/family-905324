# HSC § 39029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39029

---

## Text

“Exhaust emissions” means substances emitted to the atmosphere from any opening downstream from the exhaust port of a motor vehicle engine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
