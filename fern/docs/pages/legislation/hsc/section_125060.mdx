# HSC § 125060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5. HEREDITARY DISEASES/CONGENITAL DEFECTS > Chapter: 1. Genetic Prevention Services > Article: 4. Prenatal Testing

**Citation:** HSC § 125060

---

## Text

The participation by any individual in the prenatal testing program shall be wholly voluntary and shall not be a prerequisite to eligibility for, or receipt of, any other service or assistance from, or to participation in, any other program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
