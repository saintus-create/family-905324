# HSC § 40224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40224

---

## Text

If any member of the bay district board is recalled from his or her office as a supervisor, mayor, or city council member, pursuant to Division 11 (commencing with Section 11000) of the Elections Code, his or her office as member of the bay district board shall be vacant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 155. Effective January 1, 1995.)
