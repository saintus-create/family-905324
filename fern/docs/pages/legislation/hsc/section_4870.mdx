# HSC § 4870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 4870

---

## Text

Any portion of the territory of a county, whether incorporated or unincorporated, in which lateral or collecting sanitary sewers have been installed, for the maintenance and repair of which provision is not otherwise made, may be formed into a district, except that no portion of any city shall be included within such a district unless consent of the governing body of the city is first obtained.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 167.)
