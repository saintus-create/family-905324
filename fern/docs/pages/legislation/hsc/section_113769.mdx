# HSC § 113769

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113769

---

## Text

“Egg” means the shell egg of an avian species that includes chicken, duck, goose, guinea, quail, ratite, or turkey, except a balut and an egg product. “Egg” does not include the egg of a reptile species, including an alligator.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 4. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
