# HSC § 125525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 7. CHRONIC DISEASE SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Kidney Diseases-Chronic Uremia

**Citation:** HSC § 125525

---

## Text

No resident of this state shall be denied treatment in any of the regional dialysis centers because of his or her place of residence, so long as he or she is able to transport himself to the center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
