# HSC § 33125.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 3. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 33125.5

---

## Text

An agency shall keep a record of the proceedings of its meetings and those records shall be open to examination by the public to the extent required by law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 797.)
