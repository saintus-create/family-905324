# HSC § 33340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 4. Preparation and Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33340

---

## Text

Every redevelopment plan which contemplates property owner participation in the redevelopment of the project area shall contain alternative provisions for redevelopment of the property if the owners fail to participate in the redevelopment as agreed.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1665.)
