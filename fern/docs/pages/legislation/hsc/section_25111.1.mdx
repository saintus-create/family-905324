# HSC § 25111.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25111.1

---

## Text

“Designated local public officer” means a local public officer designated by the director pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 25180.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 343, Sec. 3.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
