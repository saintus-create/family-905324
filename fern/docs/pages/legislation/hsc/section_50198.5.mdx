# HSC § 50198.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Qualified Mortgage Bonds And Mortgage Credit Certificates > Article: 2. Mortgage Credit Certificates

**Citation:** HSC § 50198.5

---

## Text

An issuer shall develop and maintain a list of lenders that have entered into an agreement with the issuer who will make loans to qualified holders of mortgage credit certificates, as provided by the federal act, as defined by paragraph (2) of subdivision (g) of Section 50176.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1399, Sec. 7.5. Effective October 1, 1985.)
