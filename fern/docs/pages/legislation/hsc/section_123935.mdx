# HSC § 123935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 5. California Children’s Services

**Citation:** HSC § 123935

---

## Text

A handicapped child shall not be denied services pursuant to this article because he or she has an intellectual disability.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 457, Sec. 32. (SB 1381) Effective January 1, 2013.)
