# HSC § 33820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 3. Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 33820

---

## Text

Special assessment areas wholly within redevelopment project areas may be established and special assessments levied within such special assessment areas pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
