# HSC § 41247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41247

---

## Text

The Mojave Desert district board may, by resolution, cause to be drawn all warrants on the treasurer against all funds, except funds for debt service, of the Mojave Desert district in the treasury for the payment of salaries and expenses of the Mojave Desert district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 642, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
