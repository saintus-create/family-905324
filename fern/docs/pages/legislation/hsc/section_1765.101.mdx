# HSC § 1765.101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 9. Mobile Health Care Units

**Citation:** HSC § 1765.101

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited as, the Mobile Health Care Services Act.
