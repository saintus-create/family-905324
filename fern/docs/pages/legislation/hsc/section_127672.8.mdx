# HSC § 127672.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 8.5. Health Care Payments Data Program

**Citation:** HSC § 127672.8

---

## Text

The department shall ensure that the system can map to other datasets, including public health datasets on morbidity and mortality, and data regarding the social determinants of health.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 92. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
