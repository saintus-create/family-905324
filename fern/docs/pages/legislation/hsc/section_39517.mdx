# HSC § 39517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 39517

---

## Text

The district shall be given notice and the opportunity to act before any rule or regulation is adopted by the state board for the district pursuant to Section 41502.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 982.)
