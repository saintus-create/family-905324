# HSC § 1399.84

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 11.1. Consumer Operated and Oriented Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 1399.84

---

## Text

The director may request any documentation relating to a CO-OP’s start-up loan or solvency loan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 859, Sec. 2. (AB 1846) Effective January 1, 2013.)
