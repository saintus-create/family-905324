# HSC § 33765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 33765

---

## Text

In addition to all other powers specifically granted by this chapter, an agency may do all things necessary or convenient to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 48.)
