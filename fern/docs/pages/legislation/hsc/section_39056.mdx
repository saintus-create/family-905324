# HSC § 39056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39056

---

## Text

“Unified district” means a district created or continued in existence pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 40150) of Part 3.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
