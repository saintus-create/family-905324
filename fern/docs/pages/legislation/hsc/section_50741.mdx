# HSC § 50741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 9. Rental Housing Construction Program > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50741

---

## Text

Only rental housing developments on which construction is commenced on or after six months from the effective date of this chapter shall be eligible for assistance under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1043.)
