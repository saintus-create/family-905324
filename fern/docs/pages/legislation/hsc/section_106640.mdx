# HSC § 106640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 1. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERSONNEL > Chapter: 4. Professional Certification > Article: 1. Registered Environmental Health Specialists

**Citation:** HSC § 106640

---

## Text

Educational institutions requesting approval of their environmental health degree program shall first submit their program to the committee for review. If the program meets the prescribed curricula in Section 106635 it shall be submitted to the department for approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
