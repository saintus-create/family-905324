# HSC § 25207.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 9.4. Banned, Unregistered, or Outdated Agricultural Wastes

**Citation:** HSC § 25207.7

---

## Text

The county shall act as the operator of the designated site for the collection of the wastes and shall comply with the regulations adopted pursuant to Section 25160 as the operator of that facility, as specified in Section 25207.13.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 989, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1994.)
