# HSC § 114111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 5. Cleaning and Sanitizing of Equipment and Utensils

**Citation:** HSC § 114111

---

## Text

(a) If used, dry cleaning methods such as brushing, scraping, and vacuuming shall contact only surfaces that are soiled with dry nonpotentially hazardous food residues.

(b) Cleaning equipment used in dry cleaning food-contact surfaces shall not be used for any other purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
