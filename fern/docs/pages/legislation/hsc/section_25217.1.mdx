# HSC § 25217.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.7. Recyclable Latex Paint and Oil-Based Paint

**Citation:** HSC § 25217.1

---

## Text

No person shall dispose of, or attempt to dispose of, liquid latex paint or oil-based paint in the land or into the waters of the state unless authorized by applicable provisions of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 603, Sec. 6. (AB 408) Effective October 8, 2011.)
