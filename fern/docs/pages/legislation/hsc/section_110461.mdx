# HSC § 110461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 2. Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 110461

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to manufacture, pack, or hold processed food in this state unless in a food processing facility duly registered, as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 110780 by Stats. 1999, Ch. 915, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2000.)
