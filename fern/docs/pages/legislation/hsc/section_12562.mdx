# HSC § 12562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 4. Classification of Fireworks and Pyrotechnic Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 12562

---

## Text

All fireworks examined by the State Fire Marshal and determined by him to come within the definition of “safe and sane fireworks” in Section 12529 shall be classified as safe and sane fireworks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
