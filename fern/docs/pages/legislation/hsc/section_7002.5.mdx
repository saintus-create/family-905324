# HSC § 7002.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7002.5

---

## Text

(a) “Hydrolyzed human remains” means bone fragments of a human body that are left after hydrolysis in a hydrolysis facility. “Hydrolyzed human remains” does not include foreign materials, pacemakers, or prostheses.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 27. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, by its own provisions.)
