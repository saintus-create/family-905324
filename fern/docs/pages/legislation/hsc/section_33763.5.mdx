# HSC § 33763.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 33763.5

---

## Text

All loans made by a redevelopment agency shall be made according to a regulation that contains standards, qualifications, and criteria for the making and approval of loans and that has been adopted by the redevelopment agency at a public meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 565, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1998.)
