# HSC § 102310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Local Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 102310

---

## Text

The local registrar of marriages shall carefully examine each license before acceptance for registration and, if it is incomplete or unsatisfactory, he or she shall require any further information to be furnished as may be necessary to make the record satisfactory before acceptance for registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 816, Sec. 42. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative January 1, 2008, by Sec. 56 of Ch. 816.)
