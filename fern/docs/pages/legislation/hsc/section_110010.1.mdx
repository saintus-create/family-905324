# HSC § 110010.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 110010.1

---

## Text

“Prescription device” means any device limited to prescription use under Section 111470.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 837, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 2001.)
