# HSC § 4965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 2. Resolution

**Citation:** HSC § 4965

---

## Text

Before a district acquires or constructs any works under this chapter, its governing body shall adopt a resolution declaring its intention to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
