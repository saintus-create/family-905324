# HSC § 25147

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 4.5. State Regulation of Existing Hazardous Waste Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 25147

---

## Text

Except as expressly provided in Section 25149, it is not the intent of this article to preempt local land use regulation of existing hazardous waste facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 244.)
