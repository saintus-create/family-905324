# HSC § 18924

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. Organization > Article: 1. The California Building Standards Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 18924

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall serve without compensation. Members of the commission who are not state officers shall be paid their actual necessary travel expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152.)
