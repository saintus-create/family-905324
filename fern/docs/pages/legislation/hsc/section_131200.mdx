# HSC § 131200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Powers of the Department > Article: 2. Regulatory Authorization and Review

**Citation:** HSC § 131200

---

## Text

The department may adopt and enforce regulations for the execution of its duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 241, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 37 of Ch. 241.)
