# HSC § 124121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 6.6. Newborn Critical Congenital Heart Disease Screening Program

**Citation:** HSC § 124121

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “CCHD” means critical congenital heart disease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 336, Sec. 2. (AB 1731) Effective January 1, 2013.)
