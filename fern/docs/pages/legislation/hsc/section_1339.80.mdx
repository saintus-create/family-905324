# HSC § 1339.80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.15. Hospital and Other Provider Requirements for Dissemination of Information Relating to Reproductive Health Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1339.80

---

## Text

Hospitals and other providers are not required to post, send, deliver, or otherwise provide the statement described in paragraph (1) of subdivision (b) of Section 1363.02, paragraph (1) of subdivision (b) of Section 10604.1 of the Insurance Code, or paragraph (1) of subdivision (b) of Section 14016.8 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 347, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
