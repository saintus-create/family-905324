# HSC § 1346.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1346.1

---

## Text

The department shall maintain a database indicating for each county, the names of the health care service plans that operate in that particular county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 80, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
