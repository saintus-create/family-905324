# HSC § 25244.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 11.9. Pollution Prevention and Hazardous Waste Source Reduction and Management Review Act

**Citation:** HSC § 25244.12

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Pollution Prevention and Hazardous Waste Source Reduction and Management Review Act.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 39, Sec. 42. (SB 1018) Effective June 27, 2012.)
