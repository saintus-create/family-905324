# HSC § 114333.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 10.6. Limited Service Charitable Feeding Operation

**Citation:** HSC § 114333.1

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is intended to replace or supersede a permit for any food facility when required by the local enforcement agency pursuant to any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 489, Sec. 3. (AB 2178) Effective January 1, 2019.)
