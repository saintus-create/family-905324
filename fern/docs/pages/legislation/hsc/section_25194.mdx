# HSC § 25194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 25194

---

## Text

Any action brought pursuant to this chapter against a person shall not abate by reason of a sale or other transfer of ownership, except with the express written consent of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 639, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1996.)
