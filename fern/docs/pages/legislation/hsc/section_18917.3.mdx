# HSC § 18917.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18917.3

---

## Text

“Publication” or “publish” means to print and make available to the public the California Building Standards Code or administrative regulations that apply directly to the implementation or enforcement of building standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1194, Sec. 8.)
