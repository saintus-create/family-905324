# HSC § 11007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11007

---

## Text

“Controlled substance,” unless otherwise specified, means a drug, substance, or immediate precursor which is listed in any schedule in Section 11054, 11055, 11056, 11057, or 11058.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1174, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1987.)
