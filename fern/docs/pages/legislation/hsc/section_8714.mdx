# HSC § 8714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 1. Care of Old Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8714

---

## Text

In allowing any damages to any plot owner for such vacation, the court shall take into consideration the benefit to be received from endowment care.
