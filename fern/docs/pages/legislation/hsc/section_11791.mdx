# HSC § 11791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 3.5. Resources and Information

**Citation:** HSC § 11791

---

## Text

The department may develop and implement a mass media alcohol and other drug education program involving newspapers, radio, and television in order to provide community education, develop public awareness, and motivate community action in alcohol and other drug use prevention, treatment, and rehabilitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 18. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
