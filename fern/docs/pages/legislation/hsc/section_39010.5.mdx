# HSC § 39010.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39010.5

---

## Text

“Acid deposition” means the wet or dry deposition of acid chemical compounds from the atmosphere.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1473, Sec. 1.)
