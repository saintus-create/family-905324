# HSC § 24218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.6. Forced or Involuntary Sterilization Compensation Program

**Citation:** HSC § 24218

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on July 1, 2026, and, as of January 1, 2027, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 997, Sec. 15. (AB 179) Effective September 30, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions Note: Repeal affects Chapter 1.6, commencing with Section 24210.)
