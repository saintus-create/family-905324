# HSC § 9601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9601

---

## Text

All mausoleums or columbariums shall be of Type I Construction as specified in the Uniform Building Code, except as otherwise provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
