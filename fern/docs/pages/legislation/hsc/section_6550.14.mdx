# HSC § 6550.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 4. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 6550.14

---

## Text

The procedure for levying and collecting taxes in any improvement district shall be the same as is provided in Chapter 7 (commencing at Section 6695) of this part for taxes on property in districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1055.)
