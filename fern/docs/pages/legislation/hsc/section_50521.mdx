# HSC § 50521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3.3. Residential Hotel Rehabilitation

**Citation:** HSC § 50521

---

## Text

Relocation benefits pursuant to Section 50520 may be provided in the form of grants pursuant to Sections 50504 and 50510 for costs of developing replacement housing or of providing subsidies to assist displaced persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 60.)
