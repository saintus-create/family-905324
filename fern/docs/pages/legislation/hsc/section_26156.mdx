# HSC § 26156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. Toxic Mold > Article: 6. Implementation

**Citation:** HSC § 26156

---

## Text

This chapter shall be implemented only to the extent that the department determines that funds are available for the implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 584, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
