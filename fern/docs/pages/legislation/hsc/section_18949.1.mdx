# HSC § 18949.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 6. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 18949.1

---

## Text

Any responsibilities of the State Architect to adopt regulations relating to building standards are hereby transferred to the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 865, Sec. 24.)
