# HSC § 33438

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 11. Property Disposition, Rehabilitation and Development

**Citation:** HSC § 33438

---

## Text

The agency may provide in the contract that any of the obligations of the purchaser are covenants or conditions running with the land, the breach of which shall cause the fee to revert to the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
