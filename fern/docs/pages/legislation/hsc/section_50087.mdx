# HSC § 50087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50087

---

## Text

“Mutual self-help housing” means assisted housing for which persons and families of low or moderate income contribute their own labor in individual or group efforts to provide decent, safe, and sanitary housing for themselves, their families, and others occupying the housing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
