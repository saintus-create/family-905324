# HSC § 41342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 14. Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41342

---

## Text

The Antelope Valley district shall provide the officials of the Los Angeles County Employees Retirement Association, in the form prescribed by them, the data necessary to make retirement reports and maintain records required by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 163, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
