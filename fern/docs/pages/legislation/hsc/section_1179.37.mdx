# HSC § 1179.37

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6. CHILDREN'S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2004 > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.37

---

## Text

All money deposited in the fund that is derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved in the fund and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 2, 2004, by initiative Proposition 61.)
