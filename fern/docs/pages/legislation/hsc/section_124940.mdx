# HSC § 124940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4. PRIMARY HEALTH CARE > Chapter: 7. Grants in Aid for Clinics > Article: 2. Primary Care

**Citation:** HSC § 124940

---

## Text

The use of funds granted pursuant to this article for use by school-based clinics shall be limited to those school-based clinics that were licensed and in operation before January 1, 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
