# HSC § 8010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 5. California Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8010

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited as the California Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation Act of 2001.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 818, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
