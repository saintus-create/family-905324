# HSC § 39600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 39600

---

## Text

The state board shall do such acts as may be necessary for the proper execution of the powers and duties granted to, and imposed upon, the state board by this division and by any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
