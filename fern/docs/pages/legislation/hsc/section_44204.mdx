# HSC § 44204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Used Direct Import Vehicles

**Citation:** HSC § 44204

---

## Text

The state board may perform a confirmatory test of the vehicle’s emissions prior to issuance of a certificate of conformity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1138, Sec. 2.)
