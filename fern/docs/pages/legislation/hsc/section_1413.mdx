# HSC § 1413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Referral Agencies > Article: 2. Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 1413

---

## Text

Civil penalties collected pursuant to this article shall be used to administer the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 924.)
