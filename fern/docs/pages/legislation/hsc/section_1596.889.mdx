# HSC § 1596.889

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 4. Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.889

---

## Text

In all proceedings conducted in accordance with Section 1596.887, the preponderance of the evidence standard shall apply.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1528, Sec. 1.)
