# HSC § 33812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 2. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** HSC § 33812

## Text

The Legislature further finds and declares that such defaults and breach of contracts may result in lengthy and costly legal actions against public agencies for the impairment of contractual rights.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
