# HSC § 6767

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 5. Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 6767

---

## Text

The tax collector and the sureties on his official bond are responsible for the due performance of the duties imposed upon him by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
