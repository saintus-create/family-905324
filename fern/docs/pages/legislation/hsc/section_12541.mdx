# HSC § 12541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 2. Exceptions

**Citation:** HSC § 12541

---

## Text

Nothing in this part authorizes the sale, use, or discharge of fireworks in any city, county, or city and county in which the sale, use, or discharge is otherwise prohibited or regulated by law or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 202, Sec. 1. Effective June 19, 1984. Pursuant to Sec. 2 of Ch. 202, Section 12541.1 prevails in case of conflict.)
