# HSC § 50895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 15. Family Housing Demonstration Program > Article: 4. Program Operations

**Citation:** HSC § 50895

---

## Text

No rule, policy, or standard of general application employed by the department in implementing this chapter shall be subject to the requirements of Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (by Sec. 33) and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 67, Sec. 34. Effective July 6, 1999. Operative July 6, 1999, pursuant to Sec. 45 of Ch. 67.)
