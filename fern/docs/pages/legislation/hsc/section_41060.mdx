# HSC § 41060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 6. Air Quality Improvement Strategy

**Citation:** HSC § 41060

---

## Text

Not later than January 1, 1990, the Sacramento district shall adopt an air quality improvement strategy to reduce public exposure to air pollution and toxic air contaminants and to achieve and maintain state and federal ambient air quality standards by the earliest practicable date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 3.)
