# HSC § 109500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 4. DRUGS, DEVICES, AND COSMETICS > Chapter: 4. Treatment of Cancer and Other Serious Diseases > Article: 5. Labeling, Sale, and Use of DMSO

## Text

As used in this article, “DMSO” means dimethyl sulfoxide.
