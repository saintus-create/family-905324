# HSC § 1596.856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.856

---

## Text

If the department finds that the applicant is not in compliance with this act or the regulations promulgated under this act, the department shall deny the applicant a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1315, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 1993.)
