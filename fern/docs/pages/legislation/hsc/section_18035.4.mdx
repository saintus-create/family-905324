# HSC § 18035.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 5. Sales and Escrows

**Citation:** HSC § 18035.4

---

## Text

Sections 18035, 18035.1, and 18035.2 shall not apply to the sale of manufactured homes or mobilehomes to:

(a) The federal government.

(b) The state.

(c) Any agency or political subdivision of the state.

(d) Any city, county, or city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1339, Sec. 3.)
