# HSC § 6495

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6495

---

## Text

The assessor’s duties are fixed by this part and he shall perform such other duties as are ordered or required by the board.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
