# HSC § 39760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 4.7. Agricultural Biomass Utilization Account

**Citation:** HSC § 39760

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that the rice industry has led many other commodity groups in developing alternatives to open-field burning. In order to aid in the continuation of this role of leadership within the agricultural industry and to enable the transition to a free-market utilization of biomass, funds are needed to provide grants to persons that utilize agricultural biomass and rice straw.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1017, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
