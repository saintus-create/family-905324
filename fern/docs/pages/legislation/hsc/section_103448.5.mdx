# HSC § 103448.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 11. Amendment of Records > Article: 8. Revision of Birth Records to Correct Gender Errors Made by Birthing Hospitals or Local Registrars

**Citation:** HSC § 103448.5

---

## Text

The State Registrar shall transmit a certified copy of a birth certificate established pursuant to this article to the applicant, without additional charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 780, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
