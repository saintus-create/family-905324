# HSC § 117030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 7. Water Supply > Article: 1. Water Supply Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 117030

---

## Text

Violation of this article may be enjoined by any court of competent jurisdiction at the suit of any person whose supply of water for human or animal consumption or for domestic purposes is or may be affected, or by the state department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
