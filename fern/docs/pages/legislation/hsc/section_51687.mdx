# HSC § 51687

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. Loan Loss Guarantee Program

**Citation:** HSC § 51687

---

## Text

This chapter shall be inoperative if moneys are not transferred to the fund pursuant to Section 51685.5 for the purposes defined in Section 51680.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993. Note: Termination clause affects Chapter 6, commencing with Section 51680.)
