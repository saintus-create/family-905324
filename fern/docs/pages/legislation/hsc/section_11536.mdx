# HSC § 11536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 9.5. Loitering for Drug Activities

**Citation:** HSC § 11536

---

## Text

A violation of any provision of this chapter is a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 981, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
