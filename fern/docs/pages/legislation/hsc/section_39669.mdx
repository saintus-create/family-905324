# HSC § 39669

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 3.5. Toxic Air Contaminants > Article: 4. Control of Toxic Air Contaminants

**Citation:** HSC § 39669

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is a limitation on the authority of the state board or a district to implement and enforce an airborne toxic control measure adopted prior to January 1, 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1161, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1993.)
