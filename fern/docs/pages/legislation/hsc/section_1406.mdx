# HSC § 1406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.3. Referral Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1406

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any local public agency performing referral services without cost to recipients of public social services when otherwise authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 700, Sec. 13.)
