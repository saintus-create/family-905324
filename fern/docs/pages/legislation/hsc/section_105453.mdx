# HSC § 105453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 5. ENVIRONMENTAL AND OCCUPATIONAL EPIDEMIOLOGY > Chapter: 8. California Environmental Contaminant Biomonitoring Program > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 105453

---

## Text

Implementation of this chapter shall be contingent on a specific appropriation being provided for this purpose in the annual Budget Act or other measure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 599, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
