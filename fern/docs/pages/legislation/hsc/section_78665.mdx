# HSC § 78665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 4. Releases of Hazardous Substances > Article: 1. General Powers of Director

**Citation:** HSC § 78665

---

## Text

Any person who refuses or prevents, without sufficient cause, any activity authorized pursuant to this article or Section 78870 shall be liable for a civil penalty not to exceed twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000) for each separate violation, or for continuing violations, for each day during which that violation continues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
