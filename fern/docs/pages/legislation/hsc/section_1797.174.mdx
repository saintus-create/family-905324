# HSC § 1797.174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 5. Personnel

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.174

---

## Text

In consultation with the commission, the Emergency Medical Directors Association of California, and other affected constituencies, the authority shall develop statewide guidelines for continuing education courses and approval of continuing education courses for EMT-Ps and for quality improvement systems which monitor and promote improvement in the quality of care provided by EMT-Ps throughout the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 997, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
