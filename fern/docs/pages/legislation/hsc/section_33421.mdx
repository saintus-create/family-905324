# HSC § 33421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 10. Demolition, Clearance, Project Improvements, and Site Preparation

**Citation:** HSC § 33421

---

## Text

An agency may develop as a building site any real property owned or acquired by it. In connection with such development it may cause, provide or undertake or make provision with other agencies for the installation, or construction of streets, utilities, parks, playgrounds and other public improvements necessary for carrying out in the project area the redevelopment plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
