# HSC § 1496.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. County Medical Facilities > Article: 5. Continuing Education

**Citation:** HSC § 1496.5

---

## Text

Any county may conduct continuing education programs for non-county-employed licensed health workers or non-county-employed nonlicensed health workers as prescribed in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 613.)
