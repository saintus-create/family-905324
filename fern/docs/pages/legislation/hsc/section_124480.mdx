# HSC § 124480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 4. PRIMARY HEALTH CARE > Chapter: 2. Primary Clinic Revolving Fund > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 124480

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “clinic” means a primary care clinic as defined in Section 1200.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
