# HSC § 36131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7.5. MODEL CITIES > Chapter: 4. Local Model Cities Resources Board

**Citation:** HSC § 36131

---

## Text

The coordinator may constitute local Model Cities resources boards in each county with one or more Model Cities programs consisting of local liaison personnel designated pursuant to Section 36130 and of any other representatives.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1462.)
