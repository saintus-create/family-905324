# HSC § 116205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 5. Mosquito Control and Imported Tires

**Citation:** HSC § 116205

---

## Text

The department shall collect and account for all money received pursuant to this article and shall deposit it in the Vectorborne Disease Account provided for in Section 116112.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 38, Sec. 5. Effective May 7, 2004. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 116225.)
