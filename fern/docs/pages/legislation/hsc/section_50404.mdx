# HSC § 50404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1. Organization of the Department and General Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 50404

---

## Text

The work of the department shall be divided into the following three divisions:

(a) The Division of Codes and Standards.

(b) The Division of Housing Policy Development.

(c) The Division of Financial Assistance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 348, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
