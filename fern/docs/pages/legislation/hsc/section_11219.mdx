# HSC § 11219

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 5. Use of Controlled Substances > Article: 2. Treatment of Addicts for Addiction

**Citation:** HSC § 11219

---

## Text

After 15 days of treatment, the physician may not prescribe for or furnish to the person with substance use disorder more than any one of the following amounts of controlled substances during each day of the treatment:

(a) Four grains of opium.

(b) Two grains of morphine.

(c) Three grains of Pantopon.

(d) One-half grain of Dilaudid.

(e) Two hundred milligrams of isonipecaine (Demerol).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 21, Sec. 11. (AB 1130) Effective January 1, 2024.)
