# HSC § 4774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4.5. Application of Other Statutes

**Citation:** HSC § 4774

---

## Text

The improvements authorized to be constructed or acquired under this article are restricted to those permitted to be constructed or acquired by such districts under Article 4 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1072.)
