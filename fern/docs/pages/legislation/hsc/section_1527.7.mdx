# HSC § 1527.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 2.5. Foster Home and Small Family Home Insurance Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 1527.7

---

## Text

All processing of decisions and reports, payment of claims, and other administrative actions relating to the fund shall be conducted by the State Department of Social Services or its designated contract agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1330, Sec. 3. Effective September 29, 1986.)
