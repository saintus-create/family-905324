# HSC § 1244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 5. Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 1244

---

## Text

Any license or special permit revoked pursuant to this chapter may be reinstated pursuant to the provisions of Section 11522 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1147.)
