# HSC § 36054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7. FARM LABOR CENTER LAW

**Citation:** HSC § 36054

---

## Text

The area of operation for any housing authority means the area of operation prescribed by Sections 34208 and 34209.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1515.)
