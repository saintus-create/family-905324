# HSC § 114135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 6. Equipment, Utensils, and Linens > Article: 1. Design and Construction

**Citation:** HSC § 114135

---

## Text

Sponges shall not be used in contact with cleaned and sanitized or in-use food-contact surfaces.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
