# HSC § 40160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Unified Air Pollution Control Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 40160

---

## Text

By the agreement ratified pursuant to Section 40151 or by resolution, a county treasurer of a member county shall be designated and shall act as the unified district treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
