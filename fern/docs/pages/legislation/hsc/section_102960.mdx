# HSC § 102960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 7. Fetal Death Registration > Article: 1. Duty of Registering Fetal Death

**Citation:** HSC § 102960

---

## Text

A funeral director, or if there is no funeral director, the person acting in lieu thereof, shall obtain the required information other than medical and health section data from the person or source best qualified to supply this information.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 304.45. Effective September 29, 1996.)
