# HSC § 121765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 2. Avian Zoonosis Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121765

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply to all shell parakeets or budgerigars.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
