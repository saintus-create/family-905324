# HSC § 34601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Housing Operations Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34601

---

## Text

The definition and general provisions contained in Sections 34602 to 34606, both inclusive, govern the construction of this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 579.)
