# HSC § 40329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40329

---

## Text

A regional district board may appoint an executive secretary to perform such duties as may be assigned to the executive secretary by the regional district board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
