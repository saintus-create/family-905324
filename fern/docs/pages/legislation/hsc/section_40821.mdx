# HSC § 40821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** HSC § 40821

---

## Text

A hearing board, with not fewer than four members present, may, in its discretion, within 30 days of the effective date of the decision, rehear any matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1412, Sec. 8.)
