# HSC § 6694.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 5. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 6694.2

---

## Text

The rate of interest on refunding bonds shall not exceed 8 percent per annum payable semiannually.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
