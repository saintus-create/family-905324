# HSC § 122475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 7.7. Valley Fever

**Citation:** HSC § 122475

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Valley Fever Education, Early Diagnosis, and Treatment Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 338, Sec. 1. (AB 1790) Effective January 1, 2019.)
