# HSC § 131410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Hospital, Emergency Medical Services, and Behavioral Health Facilities Bed Capacity Data Solution > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 131410

---

## Text

(a) The department may contract for any purpose to implement this chapter.

(b) This chapter shall be implemented subject to an appropriation and the availability of funds for this purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 999, Sec. 3. (AB 177) Effective September 30, 2024.)
