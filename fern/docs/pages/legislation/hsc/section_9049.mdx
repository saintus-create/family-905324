# HSC § 9049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 4. PUBLIC CEMETERY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 9049

---

## Text

A district may sell interment rights in its cemeteries, columbariums, and mausoleums, subject to the limitations of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 57, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
