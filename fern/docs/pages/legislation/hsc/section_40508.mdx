# HSC § 40508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 7. Variances and Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 40508

---

## Text

The revenues from the schedule of fees for the filing of applications for permits shall be collected by the south coast district board at the time that the application is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 324.)
