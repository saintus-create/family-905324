# HSC § 101317.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 3. State Aid for Local Health Administration > Article: 6. Federal Funding for Public Health Emergency Preparedness and Response

**Citation:** HSC § 101317.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, moneys made available in the 2004–05 Budget Act for public health emergency preparedness and response shall be available for expenditure and encumbrance until August 30, 2006.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 52, Sec. 11. (SB 97) Effective July 10, 2017.)
