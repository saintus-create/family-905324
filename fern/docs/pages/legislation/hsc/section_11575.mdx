# HSC § 11575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 11575

---

## Text

The action shall have precedence over all other actions, except criminal proceedings, election contests, hearings on injunctions, and actions to forfeit vehicles under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
