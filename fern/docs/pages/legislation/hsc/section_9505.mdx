# HSC § 9505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9505

---

## Text

“Companion crypts” or “nest of crypts” means two or more crypts entered through a single crypt opening.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
