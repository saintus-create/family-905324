# HSC § 24170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1.3. Human Experimentation

**Citation:** HSC § 24170

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Protection of Human Subjects in Medical Experimentation Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 360.)
