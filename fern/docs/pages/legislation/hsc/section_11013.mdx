# HSC § 11013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11013

---

## Text

“Distributor” means a person who distributes. The term distributor also includes warehousemen handling or storing controlled substances and customs brokers.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
