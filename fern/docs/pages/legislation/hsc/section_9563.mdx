# HSC § 9563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 3. Permits and Plans > Article: 2. Application, Permit and Certificate of Occupancy

**Citation:** HSC § 9563

---

## Text

The department may, from time to time, approve changes in any plans, specifications, or statements, previously approved if the changes are in conformity with the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1349.)
