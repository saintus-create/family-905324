# HSC § 4641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 4641

---

## Text

Territory which is either within or without the boundaries of the city and which is not within the boundaries of any other improvement district formed pursuant to this chapter may be annexed to any existing improvement district by the governing body of the city.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 828.)
