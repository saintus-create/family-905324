# HSC § 5033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 6. Finances

**Citation:** HSC § 5033

---

## Text

The treasurer is custodian of the funds derived from income received from the works constructed or acquired under the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
