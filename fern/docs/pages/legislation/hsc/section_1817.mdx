# HSC § 1817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 13. Community Paramedicine or Triage to Alternate Destination > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1817

---

## Text

“Public agency” means a city, county, city and county, special district, or other political subdivision of the state that provides first response services, including emergency medical care.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 138, Sec. 4. (AB 1544) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 1857.)
