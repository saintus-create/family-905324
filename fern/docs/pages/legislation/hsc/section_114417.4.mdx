# HSC § 114417.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 13. Compliance and Enforcement > Article: 4. Variance

**Citation:** HSC § 114417.4

---

## Text

A variance letter shall be valid solely with respect to those food facilities, events, locations, and operations expressly set forth and only on the specific terms and conditions upon which the variance is granted. A variance granted by the department shall be binding on every local enforcement agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
