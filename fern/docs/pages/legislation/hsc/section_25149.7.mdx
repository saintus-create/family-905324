# HSC § 25149.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 4.5. State Regulation of Existing Hazardous Waste Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 25149.7

---

## Text

No provision of this article, or any ruling by the department or director, shall be construed as a limitation on the right of any person to maintain a civil action to enjoin or abate a nuisance pursuant to Section 731 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 244.)
