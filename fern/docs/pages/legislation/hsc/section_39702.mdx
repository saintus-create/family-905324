# HSC § 39702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 4. Research

**Citation:** HSC § 39702

---

## Text

The state board shall report to the Legislature whenever it deems necessary to provide information on problems relating to air quality management.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
