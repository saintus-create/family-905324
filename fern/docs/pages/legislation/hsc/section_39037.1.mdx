# HSC § 39037.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39037.1

---

## Text

“Marine vessel” means any tugboat, tanker, freighter, passenger ship, barge, or other boat, ship, or watercraft, except those used primarily for recreation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1130.)
