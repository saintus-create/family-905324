# HSC § 12084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 1. HIGH EXPLOSIVES > Chapter: 3. General

**Citation:** HSC § 12084

---

## Text

No person shall willfully discharge any firearm within 500 feet of any magazine or any explosive manufacturing plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1497.)
