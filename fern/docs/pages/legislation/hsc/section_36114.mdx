# HSC § 36114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7.5. MODEL CITIES > Chapter: 2. Model Cities Coordinator

**Citation:** HSC § 36114

---

## Text

Each department or agency of the state shall provide such information and assistance in carrying out the purposes of this part as the coordinator may request.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1462.)
