# HSC § 11483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 8. Seizure and Disposition

**Citation:** HSC § 11483

---

## Text

No provision of this division shall be construed to prohibit the establishment and effective operation of a narcotic treatment program licensed pursuant to Article 4 (commencing with Section 11885) of Chapter 1 of Part 3 of Division 10.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 455, Sec. 12. Effective September 5, 1995.)
