# HSC § 13929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 9. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 13929

---

## Text

The district board may provide for the issuance of bonds in any amounts, in any series, and on any terms, provided that they do not exceed the limits approved by the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
