# HSC § 50962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Purposes and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50962

---

## Text

The same notice requirements as specified in Section 65863.10 of the Government Code shall apply to rental housing developments that receive financial assistance pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1563, Sec. 5.)
