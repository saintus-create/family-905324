# HSC § 25154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 5. Standards

**Citation:** HSC § 25154

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person to manage any hazardous waste except as provided for in this chapter or regulations adopted by the department pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1631, Sec. 18.)
