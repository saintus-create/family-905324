# HSC § 39027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39027

---

## Text

“Emission standards” means specified limitations on the discharge of air contaminants into the atmosphere.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
