# HSC § 33120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 3. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 33120

---

## Text

The territorial jurisdiction of the agency of a county is the unincorporated territory in the county, and that of a city or city and county is the territory within its limits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
