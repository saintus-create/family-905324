# HSC § 11171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11171

---

## Text

No person shall prescribe, administer, or furnish a controlled substance except under the conditions and in the manner provided by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
