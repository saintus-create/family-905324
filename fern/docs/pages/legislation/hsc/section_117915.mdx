# HSC § 117915

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 4. Small Quantity Generator Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 117915

---

## Text

Containment and storage of medical waste shall be in accordance with Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 118275).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
