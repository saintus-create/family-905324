# HSC § 1179.85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6.3. CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2018 > Chapter: 2. The Children’s Hospital Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.85

---

## Text

The authority is authorized to award grants to any children’s hospital for purposes of funding projects, as defined in subdivision (g) of Section 1179.82, or to a hospital pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 1179.86.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 2018, by initiative Proposition 4, Sec. 1. Effective December 19, 2018.)
