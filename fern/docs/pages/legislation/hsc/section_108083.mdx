# HSC § 108083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 3. Enforcement and Penalties > Article: 5. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 108083

---

## Text

(a) The department may receive reports of alleged violations of covered PFAS restrictions, including analytical test results, from consumers, businesses, research institutions, persons, entities, and not-for-profit entities, and shall verify those alleged reports through its own independent testing, verification, or inspection.

(b) If the violation concerns a failure to disclose information that is required by law, the department may issue a notice of violation and assess an administrative penalty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 932, Sec. 2. (AB 347) Effective January 1, 2025.)
