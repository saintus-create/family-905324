# HSC § 129030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 129030

---

## Text

The proceeds of all loans insured pursuant to this chapter shall be disbursed only upon order of the department or its designated agent. The department shall make regulations to insure the security of these proceeds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 228. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
