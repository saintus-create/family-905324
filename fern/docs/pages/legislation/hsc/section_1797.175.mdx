# HSC § 1797.175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 3. State Administration > Article: 5. Personnel

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.175

---

## Text

The authority shall establish the standards for continuing education and shall designate the examinations for certification and recertification of all prehospital personnel.

The authority shall consider including training regarding the characteristics and method of assessment and treatment of acquired immune deficiency syndrome (AIDS).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1362, Sec. 6. Effective October 2, 1989.)
