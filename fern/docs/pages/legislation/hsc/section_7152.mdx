# HSC § 7152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.56. Altruistic Living Donor Registry Act

**Citation:** HSC § 7152

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Altruistic Living Donor Registry Act of 2010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 217, Sec. 1. (SB 1395) Effective January 1, 2011.)
