# HSC § 103685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 15. Fees of State and Local Registrars > Article: 3. Other Fees

**Citation:** HSC § 103685

---

## Text

An additional fee of three dollars ($3) shall be paid for the issuance of a permit for disposition, when the permit is required to be issued outside the regularly scheduled office hours of the local registrar of births and deaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
