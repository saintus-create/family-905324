# HSC § 129810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 3. General Requirements and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 129810

---

## Text

Before commencing any construction or alteration of any hospital building, the written approval of the necessary plans as to safety of design and construction, by the department, shall be obtained.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 294. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
