# HSC § 106610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 1. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERSONNEL > Chapter: 4. Professional Certification > Article: 1. Registered Environmental Health Specialists

**Citation:** HSC § 106610

---

## Text

The department may, upon recommendation of the Environmental Health Specialist Registration Committee appointed pursuant to Section 106675, adopt any regulations necessary to accomplish the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
