# HSC § 121890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 4. Animal Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121890

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “tracker dog” means a dog trained to work with a handler in searching facilities for burglary suspects and other intruders.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 377, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
