# HSC § 113747.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113747.1

---

## Text

“Cold water” means potable water that is not heated by an auxiliary method or source.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 3. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
