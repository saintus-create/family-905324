# HSC § 34111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Establishment, Creation, and Suspension of Commission > Article: 1. Declaration of Creation and Establishment

**Citation:** HSC § 34111

---

## Text

There is also in each community a public body, corporate and politic, known as the redevelopment agency of the community. There is also in each community a public body, corporate and politic, known as the housing authority of the community. Nothing in this part changes the existence of such entities as separate public bodies, corporate and politic.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
