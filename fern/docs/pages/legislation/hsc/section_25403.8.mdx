# HSC § 25403.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.10. Hazardous Material Release Cleanup

**Citation:** HSC § 25403.8

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that this chapter is the policy successor to the Polanco Redevelopment Act (Article 12.5 (commencing with Section 33459) of Part 1 of Chapter 4 of Division 24) and shall be interpreted and implemented consistent with that act. It is further the intent of the Legislature that any judicial construction or interpretation of the Polanco Redevelopment Act also apply to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 588, Sec. 1. (AB 440) Effective January 1, 2014.)
