# HSC § 17995.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 6. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 17995.5

---

## Text

Fines collected pursuant to this part in excess of five hundred dollars ($500) per violation shall be reimbursed to the enforcement agency which investigated the violations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1545, Sec. 10.)
