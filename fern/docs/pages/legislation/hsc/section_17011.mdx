# HSC § 17011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1. EMPLOYEE HOUSING ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 17011

---

## Text

“Sleeping place,” as used in this part, means a dwelling, bunkhouse, tent, mobilehome, or other structure or shelter in which employees are housed in any employee housing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1298, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993.)
