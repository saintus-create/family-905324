# HSC § 51321.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.5. Manufactured Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51321.3

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the agency shall have the power to issue its bonds to defray, in whole or in part, the costs of studies and surveys, insurance premiums, underwriting fees, legal, accounting, and marketing services incurred in connection with the issuance and sale of bonds, including bond and mortgage reserve accounts, trustee, custodian, and rating agency fees, and other costs reasonably related to the foregoing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1105, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1994.)
