# HSC § 4799

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 4799

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall affect the validity of, or the right to issue and sell, bonds voted prior to the date when this code goes into effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 596.)
