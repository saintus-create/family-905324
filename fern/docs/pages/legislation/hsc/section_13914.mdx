# HSC § 13914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 8. Alternative Revenues

**Citation:** HSC § 13914

---

## Text

A district may levy an assessment for fire suppression services pursuant to Article 3.6 (commencing with Section 50078) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 1 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
