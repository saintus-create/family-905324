# HSC § 6792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 7. Funds

**Citation:** HSC § 6792

---

## Text

In a fund called the “running expense fund of ____ Sanitary District” (naming it) the treasurer shall place and keep the money levied by the board for that fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
