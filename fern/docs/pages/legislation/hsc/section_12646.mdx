# HSC § 12646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 6. Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 12646

---

## Text

The governing body may grant or deny the permit, subject to such reasonable conditions, if any, as it shall prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
