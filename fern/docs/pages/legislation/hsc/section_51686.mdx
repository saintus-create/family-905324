# HSC § 51686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. Loan Loss Guarantee Program

**Citation:** HSC § 51686

---

## Text

In addition to the powers expressly granted by this chapter, the agency shall have all the powers granted under this part in administering this program which are not inconsistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 51687.)
