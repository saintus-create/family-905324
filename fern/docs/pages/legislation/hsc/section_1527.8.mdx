# HSC § 1527.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 2.5. Foster Home and Small Family Home Insurance Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 1527.8

---

## Text

The fund established pursuant to Section 1527.1 shall be maintained at an adequate level to meet anticipated liabilities.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 642, Sec. 2. (AB 2019) Effective January 1, 2013.)
