# HSC § 42305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 1. Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 42305

---

## Text

The air pollution control officer shall reinstate a suspended permit when furnished with all the requested information, analyses, plans, and specifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
