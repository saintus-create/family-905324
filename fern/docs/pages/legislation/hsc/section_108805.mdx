# HSC § 108805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 8. Label Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 108805

---

## Text

Unless a specific color is prescribed, the cautionary statements may be printed in any color, but preferably red, upon a plain and distinctly contrasting background.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
