# HSC § 108195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 4. California Hazardous Substances Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 108195

---

## Text

The term “immediate container” does not include package liners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
