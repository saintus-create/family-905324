# HSC § 112235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 5. Sanitary Control of Shellfish > Article: 3. Fees

**Citation:** HSC § 112235

---

## Text

The department shall charge and collect a fee for each certificate issued. The amount of the fee shall be established by regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
