# HSC § 17951.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 4. Application and Scope

**Citation:** HSC § 17951.5

---

## Text

(a) Notwithstanding Section 17951, each city, county, or city and county may waive or reduce all building permit fees for improvements to the home of a veteran with a qualifying disability that are made to accommodate that disability.

(b) “Qualifying disability” means a disability that is recognized as service connected by the United States Department of Veterans Affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 281, Sec. 1. (SB 330) Effective January 1, 2018.)
