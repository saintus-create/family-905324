# HSC § 32121.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 32121.1

---

## Text

By resolution, the board of directors of a local hospital district may delegate to its administrator the power to employ (subject to the pleasure of the board of directors), and discharge, such subordinate officers and employees as are necessary for the purpose of carrying on the normal functions of any hospital operated by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 640.)
