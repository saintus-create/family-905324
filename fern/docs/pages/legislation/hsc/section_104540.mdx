# HSC § 104540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 1. Tobacco Control > Article: 2. Cigarette and Tobacco Products Surtax Medical Research Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104540

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that no more than 5 percent of the Research Account be used for the purposes of the administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
