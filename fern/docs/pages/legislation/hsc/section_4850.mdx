# HSC § 4850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 9. Special Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 4850

---

## Text

The district board may, by resolution, establish zones within the district, or amend the boundaries of an existing zone by annexing property to or by withdrawing property from such zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 794.)
