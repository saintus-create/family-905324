# HSC § 112690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 8. Canneries > Article: 2. Cannery Inspection Board

**Citation:** HSC § 112690

---

## Text

Each appointed member holds office for a term of one year or until his or her successor is appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
