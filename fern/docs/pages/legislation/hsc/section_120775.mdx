# HSC § 120775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 4. HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS (HIV) > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 120775

---

## Text

As used in this code:

(a) “AIDS” means acquired immune deficiency syndrome.

(b) “Human immunodeficiency virus” or “HIV” means the etiologic virus of AIDS.

(c) “HIV test” means any clinical test, laboratory or otherwise, used to identify HIV, a component of HIV, or antibodies or antigens to HIV.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
