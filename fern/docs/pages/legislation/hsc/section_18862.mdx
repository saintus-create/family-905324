# HSC § 18862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.3. SPECIAL OCCUPANCY PARKS ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18862

---

## Text

“Accessory building or structure” is any awning, cabana, ramada, storage cabinet, storage building, private garage, carport, fence, windbreak or porch, or any residential building or structure established for the use of the occupant of a recreational vehicle on a lot.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 434, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2002. Operative January 1, 2004, by Sec. 44 of Ch. 434, as amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1038.)
