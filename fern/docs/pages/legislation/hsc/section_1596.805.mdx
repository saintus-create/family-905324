# HSC § 1596.805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.805

---

## Text

No person, firm, partnership, association, or corporation shall provide specialized services within a child day care facility in this state without first obtaining a special permit as provided in this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1615, Sec. 9.)
