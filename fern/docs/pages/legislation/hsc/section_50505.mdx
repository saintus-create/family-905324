# HSC § 50505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 3. Assistance Activities of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 50505

---

## Text

The department may enter into agreements to provide staffing to assist government agencies in the conduct of federal loan and grant programs for the provision of housing for veterans and persons and families of low or moderate income in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 727, Sec. 6. (AB 639) Effective October 10, 2013.)
