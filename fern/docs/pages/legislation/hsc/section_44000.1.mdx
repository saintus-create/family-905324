# HSC § 44000.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 44000.1

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the amendments made to this part by the act that added this section during the 1999–2000 Regular Session not negatively affect the ability of the state to achieve its emission reduction goals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 67, Sec. 12. Effective July 6, 1999.)
