# HSC § 25517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.95. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory > Article: 1. Business and Area Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 25517

---

## Text

The secretary may develop materials, including guidelines and informational pamphlets, to assist businesses to fulfill their obligations under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 115, Sec. 34. (AB 148) Effective July 22, 2021.)
