# HSC § 14894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 3. Notice to Destroy Weeds > Article: 3. Posting and Publishing Notice

**Citation:** HSC § 14894

---

## Text

The notices shall be posted not more than one hundred feet in distance apart, but at least one notice shall be posted on each lot or parcel.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
