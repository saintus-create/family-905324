# HSC § 115720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9.5. ABANDONED EXCAVATIONS

**Citation:** HSC § 115720

---

## Text

This part is not applicable to any abandoned mining shaft, pit, well, septic tank, cesspool, or other abandoned excavation that contains a surface area of more than one-half acre.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 340. Effective September 29, 1996.)
