# HSC § 13937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 9. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 13937

---

## Text

A district shall not incur a bonded indebtedness in excess of 10 percent of the assessed value of all taxable property within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
