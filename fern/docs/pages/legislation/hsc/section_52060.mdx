# HSC § 52060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 5. LOCAL HOUSING FINANCE AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. California Housing Finance Agency Assistance in Issuing City and County Revenue Bonds for Home Mortgages

**Citation:** HSC § 52060

---

## Text

This chapter constitutes an alternative method for cities and counties to issue revenue bonds for providing home mortgages pursuant to the provisions of Chapters 1 (commencing with Section 52000) to 5 (commencing with Section 52051), inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1141.)
