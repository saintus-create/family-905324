# HSC § 1599.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.9. Skilled Nursing and Intermediate Care Facility Patient’s Bill of Rights

**Citation:** HSC § 1599.4

---

## Text

In no event shall this chapter be construed or applied in a manner which imposes new or additional obligations or standards on skilled nursing, intermediate care facilities, or hospice facilities or their personnel, other than in regard to the notification and explanation of patient’s rights or unreasonable costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 673, Sec. 8. (SB 135) Effective January 1, 2013.)
