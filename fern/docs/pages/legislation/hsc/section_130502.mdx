# HSC § 130502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. CALIFORNIA DISCOUNT PRESCRIPTION DRUG PROGRAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 130502

---

## Text

The California Discount Prescription Drug Program is hereby established within the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 619, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Provisions operative and implemented on or after July 1, 2010, as prescribed in Section 130500. Conditionally inoperative on February 1, 2015. Repealed conditionally on January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 130500.)
