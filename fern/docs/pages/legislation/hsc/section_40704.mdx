# HSC § 40704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 40704

---

## Text

A district board shall file with the state board, within 30 days any rule or regulation the district board adopts, amends, or repeals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
