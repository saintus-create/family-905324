# HSC § 12683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12683

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell, use, or discharge any emergency signaling device not registered by the State Fire Marshal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
