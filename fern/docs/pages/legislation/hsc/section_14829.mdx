# HSC § 14829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 14829

---

## Text

An engine company may consist of not more than 65 certificate members; a hook-and-ladder company of not more than 65 certificate members; a hose company of not more than 25 certificate members; and a rescue squad company of not more than 25 certificate members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 517.)
