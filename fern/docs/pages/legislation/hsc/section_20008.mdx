# HSC § 20008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 20008

---

## Text

Any district in existence on January 1, 2008, in an unincorporated town may protect and safeguard life and property, and may equip and maintain a police department, including purchasing and maintaining ambulances, and otherwise securing police protection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 343, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2008.)
