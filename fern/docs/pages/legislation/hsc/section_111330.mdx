# HSC § 111330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 6. Drugs and Devices > Article: 3. Misbranded Drugs or Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 111330

---

## Text

Any drug or device is misbranded if its labeling is false or misleading in any particular.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 796, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
