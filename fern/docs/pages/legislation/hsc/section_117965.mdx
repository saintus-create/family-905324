# HSC § 117965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 5. Large Quantity Generator Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 117965

---

## Text

Large quantity generators shall be subject to at least annual inspection by the enforcement agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
