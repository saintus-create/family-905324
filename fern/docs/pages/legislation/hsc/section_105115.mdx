# HSC § 105115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 4. OLDER ADULTS > Chapter: 3. Geriatrics and Gerontology Programs > Article: 1. University of California Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 105115

---

## Text

The Regents of the University of California shall be the agency with the responsibility for making grants for the Academic Geriatric Resource Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1996.)
