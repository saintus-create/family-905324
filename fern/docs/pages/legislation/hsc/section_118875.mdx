# HSC § 118875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 4. Indoor Air Quality > Article: 1. California Indoor Clean Air Act of 1976

**Citation:** HSC § 118875

---

## Text

This article and Article 2 (commencing with Section 118920) shall be known and may be cited as the California Indoor Clean Air Act of 1976.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
