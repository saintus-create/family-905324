# HSC § 25117.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25117.8

---

## Text

“Natural resources” includes, but is not limited to, disposal site capacity and substances which are hazardous waste, or which are in hazardous waste, the reuse of which is technologically and economically feasible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 89, Sec. 6. Effective March 2, 1982.)
