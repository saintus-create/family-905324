# HSC § 51525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 12. California Dream for All Program

**Citation:** HSC § 51525

---

## Text

The state and the agency shall not be liable beyond the assets of the fund for any obligation in connection therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 70, Sec. 43. (SB 197) Effective June 30, 2022.)
