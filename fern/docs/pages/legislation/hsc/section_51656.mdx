# HSC § 51656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Loan Insurance

**Citation:** HSC § 51656

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, the agency may establish a program of insuring reverse mortgages as authorized by resolution of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993.)
