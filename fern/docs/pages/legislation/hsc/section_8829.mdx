# HSC § 8829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 7. Abandonment

**Citation:** HSC § 8829

---

## Text

Thereafter the city or county shall maintain said pioneer memorial park so that it will not endanger the health, safety, comfort, or welfare of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 862.)
