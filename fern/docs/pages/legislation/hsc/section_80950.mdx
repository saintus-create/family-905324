# HSC § 80950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 12. Compensation > Article: 3. Compensable Losses

**Citation:** HSC § 80950

---

## Text

No money shall be used for the payment of any claim authorized by this part, where the claim is the result of long-term exposure to ambient concentrations of air pollutants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
