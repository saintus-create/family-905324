# HSC § 50803.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 11.5. Emergency Housing and Assistance Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50803.5

---

## Text

Each designated local board shall provide a process for appeal of its decisions and comply with the requirements of this chapter and the regulations promulgated hereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1022, Sec. 2. Effective October 11, 1993.)
