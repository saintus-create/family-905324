# HSC § 42356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 2. Variances

**Citation:** HSC § 42356

---

## Text

The hearing board may modify or revoke, by written order, any order permitting a variance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
