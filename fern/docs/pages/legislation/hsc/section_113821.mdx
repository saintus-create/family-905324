# HSC § 113821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113821

---

## Text

“Major violation” means a violation of this part that may pose an imminent health hazard and warrants immediate closure or other corrective action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 11. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
