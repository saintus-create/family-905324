# HSC § 104560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 3. RISK REDUCTION > Chapter: 1.5. Comprehensive Perinatal Outreach Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104560

## Text

There is established in the state department a comprehensive perinatal outreach program.
