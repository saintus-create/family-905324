# HSC § 50899.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 19. California Emergency Solutions Grants Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50899.2

---

## Text

The California Emergency Solutions Grants Program and the Federal Emergency Solutions Grants Program shall be administered by the California Department of Housing and Community Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 70, Sec. 37. (SB 197) Effective June 30, 2022.)
