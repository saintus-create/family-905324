# HSC § 129387

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 4. Distressed Hospital Loan Program

**Citation:** HSC § 129387

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2032, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 6, Sec. 3. (AB 112) Effective May 15, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 4, commencing with Section 129380.)
