# HSC § 51102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 5. Financing of Housing Developments and Residential Structures > Article: 1. Loans for Housing Developments

**Citation:** HSC § 51102

---

## Text

The agency shall enter into regulatory contracts and other agreements with housing sponsors receiving loans under the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
