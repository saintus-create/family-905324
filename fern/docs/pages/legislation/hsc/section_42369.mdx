# HSC § 42369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 2.5. Product Variances

**Citation:** HSC § 42369

---

## Text

(a) No product variance shall be granted if the use of the product under the variance will result in a violation of Section 41700.

(b) No emergency product variance shall be granted pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 443, Sec. 2. Effective September 7, 1994.)
