# HSC § 1491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. County Medical Facilities > Article: 4. Victims of Sexual Offenses

**Citation:** HSC § 1491

---

## Text

In addition to any examination performed without charge to a victim of rape or other sexual assault pursuant to Section 13823.95 of the Penal Code, a county hospital shall, without charge, provide the victim of rape, or other sexual assault, with testing for venereal disease and pregnancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1575, Sec. 2.)
