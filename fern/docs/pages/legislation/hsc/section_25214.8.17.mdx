# HSC § 25214.8.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.2.2. Mercury Thermostat Collection Act of 2021

**Citation:** HSC § 25214.8.17

---

## Text

The department shall repeal Chapter 24 (commencing with Section 66274.1) of Division 4.5 of Title 22 of the California Code of Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 742, Sec. 5. (AB 732) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, pursuant to Section 25214.8.19.)
