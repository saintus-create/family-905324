# HSC § 114041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 4. General Food Safety Requirements > Article: 4. Receipt of Food

**Citation:** HSC § 114041

---

## Text

(a) Shell eggs shall be received clean and sound.

(b) Shell eggs shall not exceed the restricted egg tolerances for United States Consumer Grade B Standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 35. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
