# HSC § 51482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 10. Multifamily Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51482

---

## Text

The agency shall adopt policies by resolution of the board for the admission of tenants, termination of tenancies, and eligibility of developers of housing financed under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 577, Sec. 1. Effective September 5, 1990.)
