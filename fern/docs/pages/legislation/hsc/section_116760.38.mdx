# HSC § 116760.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 4.5. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund Law of 1997 > Article: 3. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 116760.38

---

## Text

Subject to all applicable constitutional restrictions, a city, county, or special district may borrow money and incur indebtedness pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 673, Sec. 14. (AB 1531) Effective January 1, 2016.)
