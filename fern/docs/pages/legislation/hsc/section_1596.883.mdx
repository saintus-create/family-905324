# HSC § 1596.883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 3. Remedies for Employer Discrimination

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.883

---

## Text

Any employer who willfully refuses to rehire, promote, or otherwise restore an employee or former employee who has been determined to be eligible for the rehiring or promotion by a grievance procedure, arbitration, or hearing authorized by law, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1615, Sec. 9.)
