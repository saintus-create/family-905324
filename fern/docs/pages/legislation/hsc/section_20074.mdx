# HSC § 20074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 20074

---

## Text

All real property for a police station acquired pursuant to this article shall be conveyed to and held in the name of the “Board of Police Commissioners of the Police Protection District ______” (naming the district).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
