# HSC § 44557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 6. Construction and Leases of Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 44557

---

## Text

Subject to Section 44562, the existence of the authority may be terminated at any time by the Legislature. Upon dissolution of the authority, the title to all properties owned by it shall vest in and become the property of the State of California and shall not inure to the benefit of any private party.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 839.)
