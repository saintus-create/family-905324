# HSC § 111060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 11. Local Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 111060

---

## Text

This article shall not affect any previous authorization by the department to a local health department of a county, city, or city and county to enforce this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
