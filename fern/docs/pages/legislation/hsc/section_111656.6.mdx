# HSC § 111656.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 6. Drugs and Devices > Article: 6. Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 111656.6

---

## Text

Home medical devices for rental purposes shall at all times while under the control of the home medical device retail facility, be maintained in a clean and sanitary condition and in good working order following, where available, manufacturer specifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 837, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2001.)
