# HSC § 33852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 5. Legal Actions, Exceptions

**Citation:** HSC § 33852

---

## Text

All proceedings and decisions undertaken or made pursuant to this chapter shall be exempt from the requirements of Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000) of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
