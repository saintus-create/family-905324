# HSC § 7012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7012

---

## Text

“Entombment” means the process of placing human remains in a crypt or vault.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 436, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2002.)
