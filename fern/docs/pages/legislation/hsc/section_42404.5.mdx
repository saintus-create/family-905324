# HSC § 42404.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 3. Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 42404.5

---

## Text

Any limitation of time applicable to actions brought pursuant to Section 42403 shall not commence to run until the offense has been discovered, or could reasonably have been discovered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 260, Sec. 1.)
