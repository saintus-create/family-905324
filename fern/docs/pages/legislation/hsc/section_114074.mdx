# HSC § 114074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 4. General Food Safety Requirements > Article: 7. Food Display and Service

**Citation:** HSC § 114074

---

## Text

If tableware is preset, exposed, and unused, extra settings shall either be removed when a consumer is seated or cleaned and sanitized before further use.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 33. Effective July 20, 2007.)
