# HSC § 110475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 2. Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 110475

---

## Text

Any person registered pursuant to this article shall immediately notify the department of any change in the information reported on the registration application.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 915, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2000.)
