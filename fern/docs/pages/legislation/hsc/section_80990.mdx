# HSC § 80990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 12. Compensation > Article: 5. Relationship to Other Remedies

**Citation:** HSC § 80990

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall require, or be deemed to require, pursuit of any claim against the Department of General Services as a condition precedent to any other remedy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
