# HSC § 101961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 5.5. LOS ANGELES COUNTY HEALTH CARE MASTER PLAN > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 101961

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “Board” means the Board of Supervisors of Los Angeles County.

(b) “County” means the County of Los Angeles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 514, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
