# HSC § 52552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 6.2. CALIFORNIA HOUSING PARTNERSHIP > Chapter: 3. Corporation

**Citation:** HSC § 52552

---

## Text

The initial board of directors shall take whatever actions are necessary or appropriate to establish the corporation, including, but not limited to, the filing of articles of incorporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1355, Sec. 1.)
