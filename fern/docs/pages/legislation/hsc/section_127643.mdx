# HSC § 127643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 6.1. Reproductive Health Protection

**Citation:** HSC § 127643

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on July 1, 2029, and, as of January 1, 2030, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 105, Sec. 42. (AB 144) Effective September 17, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 6.1, commencing with Section 127640.)
