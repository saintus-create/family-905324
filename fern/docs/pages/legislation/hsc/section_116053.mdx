# HSC § 116053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 5. Swimming Pool Sanitation and Safety

**Citation:** HSC § 116053

---

## Text

Every health officer shall enforce the building standards published in the State Building Standards Code relating to swimming pools and the other regulations adopted by the department pursuant to this article in his or her jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
