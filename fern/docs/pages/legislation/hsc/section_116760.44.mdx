# HSC § 116760.44

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 4.5. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund Law of 1997 > Article: 3. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 116760.44

---

## Text

(a) The board may deposit administrative fees and charges paid by public water systems and other available and necessary money into an account of the fund.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 79) and added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 80. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014. Section operative July 1, 2014, by its own provisions.)
