# HSC § 25207.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 9.4. Banned, Unregistered, or Outdated Agricultural Wastes

**Citation:** HSC § 25207.9

---

## Text

A report regarding any transportation accident involving banned, unregistered, or outdated agricultural wastes that are transported pursuant to a collection program shall be submitted to the department by the participating county within 10 days of the incident.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25207.8 by Stats. 1993, Ch. 989, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1994.)
