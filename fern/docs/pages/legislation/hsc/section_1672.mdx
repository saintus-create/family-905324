# HSC § 1672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Regulation of Use of Animals in Diagnostic Procedures and Medical Research > Article: 4. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** HSC § 1672

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to use animals for the purposes provided for in this chapter without the approval of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1750.)
