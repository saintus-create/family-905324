# HSC § 117760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 117760

---

## Text

“Small quantity generator” means a medical waste generator, other than a trauma scene waste management practitioner, that generates less than 200 pounds per month of medical waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 732, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1998.)
