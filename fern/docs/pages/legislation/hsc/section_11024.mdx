# HSC § 11024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11024

---

## Text

“Physician,” “dentist,” “podiatrist,” “pharmacist,” “veterinarian,” and “optometrist” means persons who are licensed to practice their respective professions in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 676, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
