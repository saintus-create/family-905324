# HSC § 6560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 5. Elections > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6560

---

## Text

The election on the question of formation of a district shall be conducted as nearly as practicable in accordance with the Uniform District Election Law.

Except as otherwise provided in this part, general district elections shall be conducted pursuant to the Uniform District Election Law.
