# HSC § 14898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 3. Notice to Destroy Weeds > Article: 4. Hearing on Notice

**Citation:** HSC § 14898

---

## Text

At the time stated in the notices, the board of supervisors shall hear and consider all objections or protests, if any, to the proposed removal of weeds, and may continue the hearing from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
