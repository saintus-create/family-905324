# HSC § 129240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 6. Community Mental Health Facilities Loan Insurance

**Citation:** HSC § 129240

---

## Text

The total amount of loans that may be insured pursuant to this article shall not exceed fifteen million dollars ($15,000,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
