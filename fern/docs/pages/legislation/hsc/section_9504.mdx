# HSC § 9504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9504

---

## Text

“Mausoleum” includes any building or structure, used or intended to be used, for the interment of human remains. A columbarium may be built within a mausoleum.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
