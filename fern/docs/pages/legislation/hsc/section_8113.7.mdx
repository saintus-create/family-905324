# HSC § 8113.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Requirements for Burials

**Citation:** HSC § 8113.7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the statute of limitations for any individual’s criminal violation of Section 8113.5 shall begin to run at the time the violation is discovered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 371, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
