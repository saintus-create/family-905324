# HSC § 19122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 2. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 19122

---

## Text

Any city or county may, by ordinance, designate any department or officer, other than a department or officer mentioned in this chapter, to enforce all or any part of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
