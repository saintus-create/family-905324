# HSC § 1569.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 2. Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.11

---

## Text

The department shall inspect and license residential care facilities for the elderly. A license is not transferable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1127, Sec. 3.)
