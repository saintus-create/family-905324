# HSC § 50698

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 7.5. Housing Advisory Service > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50698

---

## Text

The director’s last annual report to the Governor and the Legislature prior to January 1, 1982, shall include recommendations for improvement, continuation, or termination of the California Housing Advisory Service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1354.)
