# HSC § 80770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 11. California Financial Assurance and Insurance for Redevelopment Program > Article: 2. Environmental Insurance Products

**Citation:** HSC § 80770

---

## Text

(a) An insurance company selected to provide prenegotiated environmental insurance products pursuant to Section 80760 shall offer this prenegotiated package of insurance products to any interested recipient of a loan under the CLEAN Program.

(b) The insurance company shall also offer the environmental insurance products made available under this chapter to any other person who conducts a response action in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
