# HSC § 1673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Regulation of Use of Animals in Diagnostic Procedures and Medical Research > Article: 4. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** HSC § 1673

## Text

Any person who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.
