# HSC § 116250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 11. VECTORS > Chapter: 3. Enforcement and Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 116250

---

## Text

A violation of Article 3 (commencing with Section 116125) of Chapter 2 is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
