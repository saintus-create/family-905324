# HSC § 25918

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10.4. Asbestos Notification

**Citation:** HSC § 25918

---

## Text

“Asbestos,” as used in this chapter, has the same meaning as defined in Section 6501.7 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1502, Sec. 1.)
