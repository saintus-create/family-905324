# HSC § 1569.311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.311

---

## Text

Every residential care facility for the elderly shall have one or more carbon monoxide detectors in the facility that meet the standards established in Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 13260) of Part 2 of Division 12. The department shall account for the presence of these detectors during inspections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 503, Sec. 3. (AB 2386) Effective January 1, 2015.)
