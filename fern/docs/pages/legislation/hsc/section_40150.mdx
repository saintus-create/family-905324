# HSC § 40150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Unified Air Pollution Control Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 40150

---

## Text

Two or more contiguous counties, all or part of which are county districts, may merge those county districts into one unified district pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
