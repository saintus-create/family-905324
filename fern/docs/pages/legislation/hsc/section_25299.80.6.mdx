# HSC § 25299.80.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 7. Cost Recovery, Enforcement, and Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.80.6

---

## Text

An action by the executive director to impose civil liability under this chapter is subject to review by the board in the same manner as provided for the review by the State Water Resources Control Board of actions of a regional board under Section 13320 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 547, Sec. 24. (SB 445) Effective September 25, 2014. Inoperative January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 25299.81.)
