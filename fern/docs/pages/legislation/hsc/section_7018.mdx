# HSC § 7018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7018

---

## Text

“Cemetery authority” includes cemetery association, corporation sole, limited liability company, or other person owning or controlling cemetery lands or property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 114, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
