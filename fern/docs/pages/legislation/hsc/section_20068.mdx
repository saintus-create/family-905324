# HSC § 20068

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 20068

---

## Text

A vacancy occurring in the office of an elected commissioner shall be filled by an appointment of the board.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
