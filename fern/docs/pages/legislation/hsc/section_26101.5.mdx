# HSC § 26101.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 18. Toxic Mold > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 26101.5

---

## Text

All standards that the department develops pursuant to this chapter shall be in accordance with existing administrative law procedures applicable to the development of regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 584, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
