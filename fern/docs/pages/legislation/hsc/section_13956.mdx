# HSC § 13956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 10. Service Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 13956

---

## Text

To assist it in the operation of a service zone, the district board may appoint one or more advisory groups composed of persons who reside in or own property in the zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
