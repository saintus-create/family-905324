# HSC § 34402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1.5. Tax Exemption of Housing Authority Property

**Citation:** HSC § 34402

---

## Text

The bonds of an authority are issued for an essential public and governmental purpose, and, together with interest thereon and income therefrom, are exempt from all taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 93.)
