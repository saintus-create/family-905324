# HSC § 120399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 2. IMMUNIZATIONS > Chapter: 1.7. Meningococcal Immunization

**Citation:** HSC § 120399

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the University of California except to the extent that the Regents of the University of California, by appropriate resolution, make applicable the provision of the chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 372, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
