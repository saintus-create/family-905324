# HSC § 16110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 3. Earthquake Hazard Mitigation Technologies

**Citation:** HSC § 16110

---

## Text

The State Architect shall periodically inform the Seismic Safety Commission with respect to the implementation and administration of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 988, Sec. 1.)
