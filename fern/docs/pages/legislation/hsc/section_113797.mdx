# HSC § 113797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113797

---

## Text

“Grade A standards” means the requirements of the United States Public Health Service/FDA “Grade A Pasteurized Milk Ordinance” and “Grade A Condensed and Dry Milk Ordinance” with which certain fluid and dry milk and milk products comply.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
