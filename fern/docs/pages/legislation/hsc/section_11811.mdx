# HSC § 11811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 4. State-County Partnership > Article: 4. County Alcohol and Other Drug Program

**Citation:** HSC § 11811

---

## Text

Counties shall have broad discretion in the choice of services they utilize to alleviate the alcohol and other drug problems of specific population groups and the community. Those services shall include services for alcohol and other drug use prevention and treatment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 27. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
