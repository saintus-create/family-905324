# HSC § 102700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Birth Following Adoption, Legitimation, Court Determination of Parentage, and Acknowledgment > Article: 1. Adoption

**Citation:** HSC § 102700

---

## Text

The court report of adoption shall be filed with the original record of birth, that shall remain as a part of the records of the State Registrar.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
