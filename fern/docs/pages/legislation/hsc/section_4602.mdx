# HSC § 4602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 4602

---

## Text

“City” means the city in which the district is located. If a district is not entirely within the boundaries of a single city, “city” means the initiating city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 756.)
