# HSC § 11180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11180

---

## Text

No person shall obtain or possess a controlled substance obtained by a prescription that does not comply with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
