# HSC § 1797.98g

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2.5. The Maddy Emergency Medical Services Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.98g

---

## Text

The moneys contained in an Emergency Medical Services Fund, other than moneys contained in a Physician Services Account within the fund pursuant to Section 16952 of the Welfare and Institutions Code, shall not be subject to Article 3.5 (commencing with Section 16951) of Chapter 5 of Part 4.7 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1169, Sec. 4.)
