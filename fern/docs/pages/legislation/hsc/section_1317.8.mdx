# HSC § 1317.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 7. Other Services

**Citation:** HSC § 1317.8

---

## Text

If any provision of this article is declared unlawful or unconstitutional in any judicial action, the remaining provisions of this chapter shall remain in effect.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1240, Sec. 11.)
