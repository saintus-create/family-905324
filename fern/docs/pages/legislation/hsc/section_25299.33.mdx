# HSC § 25299.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 3. Financial Responsibility

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.33

---

## Text

(a) An owner and operator subject to Section 25299.30 may establish evidence of financial responsibility pursuant to this article by any one or more of the means specified in the federal act.

(b) An owner or operator shall submit evidence of financial responsibility on a prepared form to the local agency which has issued a permit for the operation of the tank pursuant to Section 25284.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1442, Sec. 5. Effective October 2, 1989. Inoperative January 1, 2036, pursuant to Section 25299.81.)
