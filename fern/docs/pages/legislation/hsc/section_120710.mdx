# HSC § 120710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 3. SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASE > Chapter: 2. Prenatal Syphilis Tests

**Citation:** HSC § 120710

---

## Text

In case of question concerning the accuracy of a test required by this chapter, it is mandatory upon the department to accept specimens for checking purposes from any district in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
