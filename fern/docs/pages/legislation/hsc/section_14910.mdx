# HSC § 14910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 4. Expense of Abatement > Article: 2. Hearing on Report

**Citation:** HSC § 14910

---

## Text

At the time fixed for receiving and considering the report, the board shall hear it and any objections of any of the property owners liable to be assessed for the work of abatement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
