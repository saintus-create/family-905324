# HSC § 13252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 7. Civil Actions to Abate Fire Hazards

**Citation:** HSC § 13252

---

## Text

Any civil action brought pursuant to this chapter shall be brought in the county in which the action occurs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 345.)
