# HSC § 6817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 8. Reorganization

**Citation:** HSC § 6817

---

## Text

A district reorganized under this part is for all purposes the district previously existing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
