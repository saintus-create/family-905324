# HSC § 7955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 9. New Land, Mausoleum or Columbarium

**Citation:** HSC § 7955

---

## Text

The map or plan shall be kept on file in the office of the cemetery authority and shall at all times be open to inspection by the relatives or friends of deceased persons whose remains are reinterred therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
