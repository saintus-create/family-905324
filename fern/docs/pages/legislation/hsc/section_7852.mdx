# HSC § 7852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 6. Removal by Cemetery Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 7852

---

## Text

The remains of each person reinterred shall be placed in a separate and suitable receptacle and decently and respectfully interred under rules and regulations adopted by the cemetery authority making the removal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
