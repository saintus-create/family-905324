# HSC § 11029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11029

---

## Text

“Production” includes the manufacture, planting, cultivation, growing, or harvesting of a controlled substance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
