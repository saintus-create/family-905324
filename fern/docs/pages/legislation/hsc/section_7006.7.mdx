# HSC § 7006.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7006.7

---

## Text

“Cremated remains container” means a receptacle in which cremated remains are placed after cremation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1232, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1994.)
