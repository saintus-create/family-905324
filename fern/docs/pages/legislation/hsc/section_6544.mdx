# HSC § 6544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 3. Application of Other Statutes

**Citation:** HSC § 6544

---

## Text

The improvements authorized to be constructed or acquired by this article are restricted to those permitted to be constructed or acquired by such districts under Article 1 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1072.)
