# HSC § 127995

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Personnel Recruitment and Education > Article: 3. Nursing Education Scholarships

**Citation:** HSC § 127995

---

## Text

The department shall administer the program of nursing education scholarships and shall for this purpose, adopt regulations as it determines are necessary to carry out this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 122. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
