# HSC § 117125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 7. Water Supply > Article: 2. Additional Water Supply Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 117125

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the Department of Fish and Wildlife may stock with fish any body of water opened to public fishing pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 673, Sec. 19. (AB 1531) Effective January 1, 2016.)
