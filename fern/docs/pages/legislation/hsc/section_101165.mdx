# HSC § 101165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Local Health Officers and Local Health Departments > Article: 5. Municipal and County Laboratories

**Citation:** HSC § 101165

---

## Text

Nothing in this article, or any other provision of law, shall be construed to restrict, limit, or prevent individuals certified under authority of this part or Article 1 (commencing with Section 106600) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of Division 104 from performing their duties for the protection of the public health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 302.1. Effective September 29, 1996.)
