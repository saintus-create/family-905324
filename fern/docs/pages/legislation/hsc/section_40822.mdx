# HSC § 40822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 2. Procedure

**Citation:** HSC § 40822

---

## Text

Any hearing conducted by a hearing board shall be held in a location readily accessible to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
