# HSC § 122385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 11. Pet Boarding Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 122385

---

## Text

A pet boarding facility shall maintain either of the following:

(a) A fire alarm system that is connected to a central reporting station that alerts the local fire department in case of fire.

(b) A fire suppression sprinkler system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 364, Sec. 1. (SB 945) Effective January 1, 2017.)
