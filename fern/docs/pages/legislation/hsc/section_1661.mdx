# HSC § 1661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Regulation of Use of Animals in Diagnostic Procedures and Medical Research > Article: 2. Administration and Regulation

**Citation:** HSC § 1661

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, shall be applicable to all the rules and regulations promulgated by the department under this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 101, Sec. 105.)
