# HSC § 32124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 32124

---

## Text

The board of directors may establish a nurses’ training school in connection with the hospital, prescribe a course of study for such training and after the completion of the course, provide for the issuance of diplomas to graduate nurses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 932.)
