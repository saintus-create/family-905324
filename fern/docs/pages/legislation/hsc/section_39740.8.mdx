# HSC § 39740.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 4.3. Carbon Sequestration > Article: 1. Natural and Working Lands

**Citation:** HSC § 39740.8

## Text

The Natural Resources Agency may contract with a third-party organization to develop and operate the registry.
