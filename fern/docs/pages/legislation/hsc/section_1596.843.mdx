# HSC § 1596.843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.843

---

## Text

(a) Whenever a facility visit and a complaint investigation are conducted at the same time by the department, a separate licensing report shall be used to document the complaint investigation.

(b) The department shall review a random sample of licensing reports to evaluate the consistency of the application of regulations by different licensing program analysts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 301, Sec. 4.)
