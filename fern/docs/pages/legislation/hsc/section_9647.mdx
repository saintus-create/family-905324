# HSC § 9647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 5. Construction > Article: 2. Structural and Material Requirements of Community Mausoleums and Columbariums

**Citation:** HSC § 9647

---

## Text

All skylight frames shall be fabricated in conformance with structural requirements, and shall contain wire glass, tempered glass, or plastic of comparable strength and durability.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1994.)
