# HSC § 12576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 1. Types of Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 12576

---

## Text

A public display license (general) allows the holding and conducting of public displays of dangerous fireworks at various locations and at various times.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
