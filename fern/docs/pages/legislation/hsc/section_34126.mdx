# HSC § 34126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Establishment, Creation, and Suspension of Commission > Article: 4. Suspension of Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 34126

---

## Text

The legislative body of the community shall file with the Secretary of State and with the Department of Housing and Community Development a certified copy of any ordinance suspending a commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
