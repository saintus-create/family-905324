# HSC § 53597

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 14.2. Housing for a Healthy California Program

**Citation:** HSC § 53597

---

## Text

The department shall reimburse the State Department of Health Care Services for the costs of collaborating in matching and providing relevant data.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 777, Sec. 2. (AB 74) Effective January 1, 2018.)
