# HSC § 6496

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6496

## Text

The assessor shall receive such compensation as shall be fixed by the board.
