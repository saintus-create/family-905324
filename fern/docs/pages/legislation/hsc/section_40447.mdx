# HSC § 40447

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 40447

---

## Text

The south coast district board may request the state board to investigate the emission reduction capabilities of any motor vehicle pollution control devices which have not been previously tested by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 324.)
