# HSC § 118200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 7. Medical Waste Treatment Facility Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 118200

---

## Text

The enforcement agency shall evaluate, inspect, and review the records of medical waste treatment facilities for compliance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
