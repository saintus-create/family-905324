# HSC § 25117.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25117.14

---

## Text

“Permit-by-rule” means a provision of the regulations adopted pursuant to this chapter stating that a facility or activity is deemed to have a hazardous waste facilities permit if it meets the requirements of that provision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1345, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
