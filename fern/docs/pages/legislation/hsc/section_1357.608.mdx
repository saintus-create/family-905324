# HSC § 1357.608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.17. Grandfathered Small Employer Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 1357.608

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as prohibiting a health care service plan from restricting enrollment of late enrollees to open enrollment periods consistent with federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 6. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
