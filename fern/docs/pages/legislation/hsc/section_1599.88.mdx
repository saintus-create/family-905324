# HSC § 1599.88

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.95. Seniors Nursing Home Disclosure

**Citation:** HSC § 1599.88

---

## Text

The Department shall issue regulations to implement this Chapter, including permitting one disclosure to satisfy the requirements of Section 1599.85 for all advertisements on the same page.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 1988, by initiative Proposition 105. Operative January 1, 1990. Invalidated in 1991 by court decision.)
