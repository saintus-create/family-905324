# HSC § 34505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Housing Cooperation Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34505

---

## Text

“Housing projects” means any work or undertaking of a housing authority pursuant to the Housing Authorities Law or any similar work or undertaking of the Federal Government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
