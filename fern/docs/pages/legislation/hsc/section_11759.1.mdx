# HSC § 11759.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 1. CREATION OF DUTIES > Chapter: 4. Alcohol and Drug Treatment for Adolescents

**Citation:** HSC § 11759.1

---

## Text

The department, in collaboration with counties and providers of alcohol and other drug services, shall establish community-based nonresidential and residential recovery programs to intervene and treat the problems of alcohol and other drug use among youth.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2005. Inoperative July 1, 2013, pursuant to Section 11759.5.)
