# HSC § 4738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 4738

---

## Text

A county sanitation district may sue and be sued by its own name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1000.)
