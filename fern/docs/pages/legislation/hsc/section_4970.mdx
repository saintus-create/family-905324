# HSC § 4970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 3. Notice, Hearing, and Election

**Citation:** HSC § 4970

---

## Text

The time set for the hearing shall be not less than twenty nor more than forty days after the adoption of the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
