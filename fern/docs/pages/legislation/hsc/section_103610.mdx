# HSC § 103610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 15. Fees of State and Local Registrars > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 103610

---

## Text

The money collected by the local registrar shall be paid by him or her into the county or city treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
