# HSC § 43155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 2. New Motor Vehicles > Article: 1.5. Prohibited Transactions

**Citation:** HSC § 43155

---

## Text

An action brought pursuant to Section 43154 to recover such civil penalties shall take special precedence over all other civil matters on the calendar of the court except those matters to which equal precedence on the calendar is granted by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1206.)
