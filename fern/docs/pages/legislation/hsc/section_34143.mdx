# HSC § 34143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 34143

---

## Text

A commission may:

(a) Sue and be sued.

(b) Have a seal.

(c) Make and execute contracts and other instruments necessary or convenient to the exercise of its powers.

(d) Make, amend, and repeal bylaws and regulations not inconsistent with, and to carry into effect the powers and purposes of this part and all other powers and purposes delegated to them by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
