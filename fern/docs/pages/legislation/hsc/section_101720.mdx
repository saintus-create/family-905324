# HSC § 101720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Santa Barbara County Special Health Care Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 101720

---

## Text

Any vacancy on the board shall be filled for the unexpired term by the board of supervisors of the county authorized by Section 101690 to make the appointment to that position.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 266, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2008.)
