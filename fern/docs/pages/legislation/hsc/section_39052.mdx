# HSC § 39052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39052

---

## Text

“Solid waste dump” means any accumulation for the purpose of disposal of any solid waste.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
