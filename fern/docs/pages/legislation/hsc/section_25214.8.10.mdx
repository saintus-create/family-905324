# HSC § 25214.8.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.2.2. Mercury Thermostat Collection Act of 2021

**Citation:** HSC § 25214.8.10

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Mercury Thermostat Collection Act of 2021.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 703, Sec. 3. (AB 707) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, pursuant to Section 25214.8.19.)
