# HSC § 13171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 2. Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 13171

---

## Text

No licensee shall conduct his licensed business or solicit business under a name other than a name or names which appears on his license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 802.)
