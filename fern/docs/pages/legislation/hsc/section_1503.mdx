# HSC § 1503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1503

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “license” means a basic permit to operate a community care facility.

A license shall not be transferable.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1203.)
