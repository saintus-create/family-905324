# HSC § 123630.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 2. Maternal Health > Article: 4.6. California Dignity in Pregnancy and Childbirth Act

**Citation:** HSC § 123630.6

---

## Text

The Attorney General may publish a report outlining compliance data related to this article on a biennial basis. The report may be posted on the Attorney General’s internet website.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 621, Sec. 4. (AB 2319) Effective January 1, 2025.)
