# HSC § 1596.76

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.76

---

## Text

“Day care center” means a child day care facility other than a family day care home, and includes infant centers, preschools, extended day care facilities, and schoolage child care centers, and includes child care centers licensed pursuant to Section 1596.951.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 574, Sec. 1. (AB 605) Effective January 1, 2019.)
