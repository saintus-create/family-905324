# HSC § 51627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 51627

---

## Text

The assets of the insurance fund shall be applicable to the payment of losses sustained on account of insurance and to the payment of the salaries and other expenses charged against it in accordance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993.)
