# HSC § 33829

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 3. Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 33829

---

## Text

No proceeding shall be held invalid for the failure to mail notice to any property owner or occupant or to post the notice if there has been substantial compliance with the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
