# HSC § 40361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 4. Advisory Council

**Citation:** HSC § 40361

---

## Text

A regional district board may appoint an air pollution control advisory council to advise and consult with the regional district board and regional district air pollution control officer in effectuating the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
