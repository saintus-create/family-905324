# HSC § 33371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 5. Procedure for Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Legislative Body

**Citation:** HSC § 33371

---

## Text

Before entering into any or certain types of contracts in connection with the redevelopment plan, the legislative body may require the agency to submit such contracts to the legislative body and obtain its approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
