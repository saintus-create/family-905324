# HSC § 40320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 40320

---

## Text

A regional district board is the governing body of the regional district and shall exercise all the powers of the regional district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
