# HSC § 19816

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 8. Inflammable or Explosive Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 19816

---

## Text

Any vendor who knowingly and wilfully violates any rule or regulation of the Fire Marshal relating to inflammable articles shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 728.)
