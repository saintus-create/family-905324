# HSC § 127695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 10. California Affordable Drug Manufacturing Act of 2020

**Citation:** HSC § 127695

---

## Text

(a) On or before December 31, 2022, CHHSA shall report to the Legislature on both of the following:

(1) A description of the status of all drugs targeted under this chapter.

(2) An analysis of how the activities of CHHSA may impact competition, access to targeted drugs, the costs of those drugs, and the costs of generic prescription drugs to public and private purchasers.

(b) This section shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 47, Sec. 23. (SB 184) Effective June 30, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions.)
