# HSC § 128705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 5. HEALTH DATA > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Data

**Citation:** HSC § 128705

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 1986, any reference in this code to the Advisory Health Council or the California Health Policy and Data Advisory Commission shall be deemed a reference to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 197. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
