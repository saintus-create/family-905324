# HSC § 1600.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Human Whole Blood, Human Whole Blood Derivatives, and Other Biologics > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1600.35

---

## Text

“Blood component” or “blood derivative” means any product produced from whole blood.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 800, Sec. 5.)
