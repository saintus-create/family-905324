# HSC § 50104.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50104.7

---

## Text

“Urban area” means any portion of a county or the state which is not a rural area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 50104.5 (as added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 96) by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1191.)
