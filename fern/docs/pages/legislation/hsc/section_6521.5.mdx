# HSC § 6521.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6521.5

---

## Text

Any district may exercise the power granted to sanitation districts by Section 4765 of this code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1018.)
