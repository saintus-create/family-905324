# HSC § 102470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 3. Live Birth Registration > Article: 2. Content of Certificate of Live Birth

**Citation:** HSC § 102470

---

## Text

The State Registrar shall publish within 30 days of receipt of recommendations by the Vital Statistics Advisory Committee, made pursuant to Section 102465, (1) a list of the recommendations adopted, and (2) a list of the recommendations not adopted, with reasons for the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
