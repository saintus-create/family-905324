# HSC § 44121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 10. Accelerated Light-Duty Vehicle Retirement Program

**Citation:** HSC § 44121

---

## Text

The state board shall develop standards for the certification and use of emission reduction credits to ensure that the credits are real, surplus, and quantifiable after accounting for program uncertainties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 929, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
