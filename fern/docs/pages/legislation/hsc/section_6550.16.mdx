# HSC § 6550.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 4. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 6550.16

---

## Text

For the purpose of any tax levied under this article, the properties within any improvement district shall be deemed to be equally benefited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1055.)
