# HSC § 108050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 2. Product Recall Safety and Protection Act

**Citation:** HSC § 108050

---

## Text

(a) Any violation of this chapter shall be subject to a civil penalty of up to one thousand dollars ($1,000) for each occurrence, up to a maximum of twenty thousand dollars ($20,000).

(b) The remedies under this section are in addition to, and do not supersede or limit, any and all other remedies, civil or criminal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 569, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
