# HSC § 41980.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 7. Incineration of Toxic Waste Materials

**Citation:** HSC § 41980.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “toxic waste” means hazardous waste, as defined in Section 25117.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1474, Sec. 1.)
