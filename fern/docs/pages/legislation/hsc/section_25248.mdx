# HSC § 25248

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 12. Financial Responsibility and Closure and Maintenance of Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 25248

---

## Text

The owner or operator of a facility for which closure and postclosure plans have been approved shall carry out the plans during the closure and postclosure period required by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1631, Sec. 42.)
