# HSC § 11578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 11578

---

## Text

If the action is brought by a citizen and the court finds there was no reasonable ground or cause for the action, the costs shall be taxed against him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
