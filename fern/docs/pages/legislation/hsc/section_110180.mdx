# HSC § 110180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3. Publicity

**Citation:** HSC § 110180

---

## Text

The department may collect, report, or illustrate the results of any investigation of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
