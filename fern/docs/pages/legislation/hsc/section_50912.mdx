# HSC § 50912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 50912

---

## Text

There shall be within the agency a director of financing appointed by the Governor and serving at the pleasure of the executive director of the agency.

The director of financing shall have responsibility for the financial operations of the agency and shall perform such other duties as may be required by the executive director.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1115.)
