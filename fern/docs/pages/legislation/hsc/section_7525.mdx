# HSC § 7525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Consent to Removal

**Citation:** HSC § 7525

---

## Text

The remains of a deceased person may be removed from a plot in a cemetery with the consent of the cemetery authority and the written consent of one of the following in the order named:

(a) The surviving spouse.

(b) The surviving children.

(c) The surviving parents.

(d) The surviving brothers or sisters.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
