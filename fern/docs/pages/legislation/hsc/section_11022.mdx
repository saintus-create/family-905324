# HSC § 11022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11022

---

## Text

“Person” means individual, corporation, government or governmental subdivision or agency, business trust, estate, trust, partnership, limited liability company, or association, or any other legal entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 159. Effective January 1, 1995.)
