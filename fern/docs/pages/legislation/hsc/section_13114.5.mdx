# HSC § 13114.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. State Fire Marshal > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 13114.5

---

## Text

The governing body of any city or county may enact ordinances or laws imposing restrictions greater than those imposed by Sections 13113 and 13114.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1480.)
