# HSC § 116023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 4. Personal Flotation Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 116023

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “wearable personal flotation device” means a lifejacket, puddle jumper, water wings, or other flotation device designed to be worn or attached to the body of an infant or child.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 307, Sec. 1. (AB 2146) Effective January 1, 2025.)
