# HSC § 11564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 2. Substance Abuse Treatment Control Units

**Citation:** HSC § 11564

---

## Text

The authority granted to the parole authority and to the Department of the Youth Authority in no way limits Sections 3060 and 3325 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 695, Sec. 7. Effective September 15, 1992.)
