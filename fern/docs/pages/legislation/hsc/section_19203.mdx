# HSC § 19203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 7. Seismic Gas Shutoff Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 19203

---

## Text

Any new seismic gas shutoff device sold by any person in this state shall, prior to sale, be certified by the State Architect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 152, Sec. 9. Effective July 12, 1996.)
