# HSC § 112620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 7. Frozen Foods

**Citation:** HSC § 112620

---

## Text

The liability of the owner or operator of lockers for loss of goods in lockers or in the owner’s or operator’s care shall be limited to negligence of the owner or operator or his or her employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
