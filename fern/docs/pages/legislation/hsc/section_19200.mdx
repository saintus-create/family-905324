# HSC § 19200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 7. Seismic Gas Shutoff Devices

**Citation:** HSC § 19200

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that existing law does not require that any new seismic gas shutoff valve sold by any person in this state shall, prior to sale, be certified by the State Architect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 268, Sec. 1.)
