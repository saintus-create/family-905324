# HSC § 44233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 7. District Fees to Implement the California Clean Air Act

**Citation:** HSC § 44233

---

## Text

Not more than 6.25 percent of the fees distributed to any district pursuant to Section 44229, or distributed by a district to any other public agency pursuant to this chapter, shall be used by the district or other public agency for administrative costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 610, Sec. 5. (SB 513) Effective January 1, 2016.)
