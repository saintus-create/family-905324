# HSC § 25215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 10.5. The Lead-Acid Battery Recycling Act of 2016

**Citation:** HSC § 25215

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Lead-Acid Battery Recycling Act of 2016.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 2) and added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 666, Sec. 1. (AB 2153) Effective September 26, 2016. Section operative January 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 25215.75.)
