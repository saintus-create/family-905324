# HSC § 11839.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 10. Narcotic Treatment Programs > Article: 1. Narcotic Treatment Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11839.19

---

## Text

(a) The department shall not license the establishment of a narcotic treatment program without a written application by the treatment facility that meets evaluative criteria required by the department.

(b) The department shall not require disclosure of the identity of patients or former patients or of any records containing identifying information except as provided in Section 11845.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2005.)
