# HSC § 18209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18209

---

## Text

“Lease” is an oral or written contract for the use, possession, and occupation of property. It includes rent.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1056.)
