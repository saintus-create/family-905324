# HSC § 25250.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 13. Management of Used Oil

**Citation:** HSC § 25250.21

---

## Text

Any person whose permit or registration has been revoked may not serve in the employ of a hazardous waste hauler or used oil recycler during the period of revocation of the permit or registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 871, Sec. 1.)
