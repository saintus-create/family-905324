# HSC § 34191.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.85. DISSOLUTION OF REDEVELOPMENT AGENCIES AND DESIGNATION OF SUCCESSOR AGENCIES > Chapter: 9. Postcompliance Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 34191.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to a successor agency upon that agency’s receipt of a finding of completion by the Department of Finance pursuant to Section 34179.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 26, Sec. 35. (AB 1484) Effective June 27, 2012.)
