# HSC § 112460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 6. Cold Storage > Article: 4. General Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 112460

---

## Text

The department may also appoint at the salary as it may designate, any person it deems qualified to make any inspection required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
