# HSC § 24505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4.7. Infant Safety > Article: 1. Infant Crib Safety Act

**Citation:** HSC § 24505

---

## Text

Remedies available under this article shall be in addition to any other remedies or procedures under any other provision of law that may be available to an aggrieved party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1176, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
