# HSC § 1179.90

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6.3. CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2018 > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.90

---

## Text

The bonds authorized by this part shall be prepared, executed, issued, sold, paid, and redeemed as provided in the State General Obligation Bond Law (Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 16720) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code), and all provisions of that law apply to the bonds and to this part and are hereby incorporated in this part as though set forth in full in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 2018, by initiative Proposition 4, Sec. 1. Effective December 19, 2018.)
