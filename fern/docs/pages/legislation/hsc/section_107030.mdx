# HSC § 107030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 1. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERSONNEL > Chapter: 4. Professional Certification > Article: 5. Radiological Technologists

**Citation:** HSC § 107030

---

## Text

Not less than two months prior to the date of each examination, the department shall cause a notice thereof to be published in two or more newspapers of general circulation, and at least one radiologic technologist magazine, all of which are published within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
