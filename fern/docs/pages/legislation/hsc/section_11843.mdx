# HSC § 11843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 12. Registration of Narcotic, Alcohol, and Other Drug Use Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11843

---

## Text

The county shall establish and maintain a registry of all narcotic and drug programs and alcohol and other drug programs within the county in order to promote a coordination of effort in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 67. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
