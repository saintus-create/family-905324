# HSC § 52525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 6. CALIFORNIA FIRST-TIME HOME BUYERS > Chapter: 3. First-Time Home Buyers Bond Act of 1982

**Citation:** HSC § 52525

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the First-Time Home Buyers Bond Act of 1982.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 320, Sec. 12. Approved in Proposition 5 at the November 2, 1982, election.)
