# HSC § 34512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Housing Cooperation Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34512

---

## Text

A state public body may furnish, dedicate, close, pave, install, grade, regrade, plan, or replan streets, roads, roadways, alleys, sidewalks, or other places which it is otherwise empowered to undertake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
