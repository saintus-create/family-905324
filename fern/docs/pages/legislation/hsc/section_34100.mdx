# HSC § 34100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 34100

---

## Text

“Community” means a city, county, city and county, or Indian tribe, band or group which is incorporated or which otherwise exercises some local governmental powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
