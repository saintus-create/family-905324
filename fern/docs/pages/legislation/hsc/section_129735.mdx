# HSC § 129735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 129735

---

## Text

“Light steel frame construction” means building construction using bearing walls composed of light gauge steel studs for its primary vertical support systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
