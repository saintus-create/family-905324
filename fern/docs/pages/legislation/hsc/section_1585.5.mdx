# HSC § 1585.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 5. Additional Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 1585.5

---

## Text

Adult day health care centers shall provide services to each participant pursuant to an individual plan of care designed to maintain or restore each participant’s optimal capacity for self-care.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 151, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
