# HSC § 25110.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25110.2

---

## Text

“Authorized local health officer” means a local health officer authorized by the department pursuant to Section 25187.7.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 886, Sec. 2.)
