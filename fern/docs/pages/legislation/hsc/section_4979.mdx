# HSC § 4979

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 3. Notice, Hearing, and Election

**Citation:** HSC § 4979

---

## Text

If the proposal is not rejected at a referendum election, the governing body acquires jurisdiction to proceed.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 910.)
