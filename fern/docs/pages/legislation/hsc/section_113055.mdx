# HSC § 113055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 10. Processed Pet Foods > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113055

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Pure Pet Food Act of 1969.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
