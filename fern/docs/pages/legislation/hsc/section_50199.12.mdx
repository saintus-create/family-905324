# HSC § 50199.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.6. Low-Income Housing Credit

**Citation:** HSC § 50199.12

---

## Text

The committee shall adopt and supply forms for eliciting information for purposes of this chapter from housing credit applicants. Housing credit applicants shall provide the committee with any information requested by the committee in performing its duties and responsibilities under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1164, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1995. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 50199.18.)
