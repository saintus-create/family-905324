# HSC § 114790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 5. Containment of Radioactive Materials > Article: 2. Radiation Monitoring Devices for Nuclear Power Plants

**Citation:** HSC § 114790

---

## Text

The information transmitted to the radiation monitoring displays in the technical support center or emergency operating facility of a nuclear powerplant shall be simultaneously transmitted to the Office of Emergency Services State Warning Center.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 390. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
