# HSC § 50887.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 15. Family Housing Demonstration Program > Article: 2. Community Housing Developments

**Citation:** HSC § 50887.5

---

## Text

The department is encouraged, as appropriate, to enter into interagency agreements with any state department or agency to ensure close coordination and cooperation in using the funds of the other departments or agencies for the jobs component, child care component, or other components of the housing developments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 225, Sec. 55. Effective August 16, 2004.)
