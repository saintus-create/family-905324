# HSC § 127990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Personnel Recruitment and Education > Article: 3. Nursing Education Scholarships

**Citation:** HSC § 127990

---

## Text

No person shall be awarded a scholarship under subdivision (c) of Section 127980 unless he or she satisfies the requirements prescribed by subdivisions (a), (b), (c), and (e) of Section 127985.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
