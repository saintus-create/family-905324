# HSC § 115705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9.5. ABANDONED EXCAVATIONS

**Citation:** HSC § 115705

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may order securely covered, filled, or fenced abandoned mining excavations on unoccupied public lands in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 340. Effective September 29, 1996.)
