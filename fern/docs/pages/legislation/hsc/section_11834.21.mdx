# HSC § 11834.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.5. Licensing > Article: 2. Local Regulation

**Citation:** HSC § 11834.21

---

## Text

(a) Any person licensed under this chapter who operates or proposes to operate an alcohol or other drug recovery or treatment facility, the department or other public agency authorized to license such a facility, or any public or private agency that uses or may use the services of the facility to place its clients, may invoke the provisions of this article.

(b) This section shall not be construed to prohibit any interested party from bringing suit to invoke the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 51. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
