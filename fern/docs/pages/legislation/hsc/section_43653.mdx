# HSC § 43653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Used Motor Vehicles > Article: 2. Certified Device Installation

**Citation:** HSC § 43653

---

## Text

Every 1966 or later model year motor vehicle, subject to registration and first sold and registered in this state, shall be equipped with a certified device to control its crankcase emissions and exhaust emissions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
