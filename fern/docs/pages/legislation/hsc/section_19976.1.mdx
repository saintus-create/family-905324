# HSC § 19976.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 19976.1

---

## Text

“Residential building” means any structure designed for dwelling occupancy, containing one or more dwelling units and structures accessory thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 129.)
