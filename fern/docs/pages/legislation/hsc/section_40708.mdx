# HSC § 40708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 40708

---

## Text

The Cortese-Knox-Hertzberg Local Government Reorganization Act of 2000, Division 3 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 5 of the Government Code, shall not be applicable to the districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 296, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2004.)
