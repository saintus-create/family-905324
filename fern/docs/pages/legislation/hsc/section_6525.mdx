# HSC § 6525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 1.5. Inclusion in County Sanitation District

**Citation:** HSC § 6525

---

## Text

A sanitary district which becomes a part of a county sanitation district as hereinabove provided for is not thereby dissolved, but may continue to function, except as herein otherwise provided, in the same manner as heretofore.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1375.)
