# HSC § 1857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 13. Community Paramedicine or Triage to Alternate Destination > Article: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** HSC § 1857

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2031, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 270, Sec. 7. (AB 767) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, by its own provisions Note: Repeal affects Chapter 13, commencing with Section 1800.)
