# HSC § 8117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance of Cemetery Grounds

**Citation:** HSC § 8117

---

## Text

Hose bibs are approved for use at cemeteries supplied with disinfected tertiary treated recycled water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 635, Sec. 3. (AB 803) Effective January 1, 2014.)
