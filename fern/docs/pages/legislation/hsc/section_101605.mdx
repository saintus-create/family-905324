# HSC § 101605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Monterey County Special Health Care Authority > Article: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** HSC § 101605

---

## Text

The bodies that originally nominated or appointed a member whose term has expired shall nominate or appoint the successor for a full term of four years, except that members who are nominated by the professional advisory board or by the community advisory board shall serve a one-year term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
