# HSC § 110825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 7. The California Organic Food and Farming Act

**Citation:** HSC § 110825

---

## Text

Materials acceptable in this state are those outlined by regulations promulgated by the NOP and the provisions of this article.
