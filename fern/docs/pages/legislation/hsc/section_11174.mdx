# HSC § 11174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11174

---

## Text

No person shall, in connection with the prescribing, furnishing, administering, or dispensing of a controlled substance, give a false name or false address.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
