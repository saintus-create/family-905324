# HSC § 18613.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 5. Regulations > Article: 2. Mobilehome and Special Occupancy Park Lots

**Citation:** HSC § 18613.1

---

## Text

The requirements for any installation of a manufactured home or mobilehome shall not exceed the requirements set forth in Sections 18613 and 18613.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 240, Sec. 1. Effective July 21, 1994.)
