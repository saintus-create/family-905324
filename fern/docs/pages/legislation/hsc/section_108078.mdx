# HSC § 108078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 3. Enforcement and Penalties > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

## Text

This chapter does not limit or restrict existing mandates, prohibitions, deadlines, enforcement authorities, or rights of action.
