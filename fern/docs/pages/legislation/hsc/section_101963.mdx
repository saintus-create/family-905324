# HSC § 101963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 5.5. LOS ANGELES COUNTY HEALTH CARE MASTER PLAN > Chapter: 3. Master Plan Authorization

**Citation:** HSC § 101963

---

## Text

The board may assemble a task force to develop a master plan for health care that is based on a long-range planning and policy analysis for the county department of health services, and report the plan to the board according to a schedule adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 514, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
