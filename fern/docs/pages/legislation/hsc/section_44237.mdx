# HSC § 44237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 7. District Fees to Implement the California Clean Air Act

**Citation:** HSC § 44237

---

## Text

A district may use fees established under Sections 44223 and 44225 to enter into an agreement with a council of governments, regional agency, or local agency to carry out Section 40717.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1705, Sec. 1.)
