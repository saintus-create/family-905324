# HSC § 13050.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Fire Equipment > Article: 2. Use of Fire Equipment

**Citation:** HSC § 13050.1

---

## Text

“Public entity” includes the state, a county, city, district, public authority, public agency, and any other political subdivision or public corporation in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1203.)
