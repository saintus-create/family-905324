# HSC § 12592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 2. Issuance, Revocation and Renewal

**Citation:** HSC § 12592

---

## Text

Any applicant who has been denied a license or renewal of a license, or any licensee who has had a license suspended, shall be entitled to a hearing in accordance with the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
