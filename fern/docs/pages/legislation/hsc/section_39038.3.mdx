# HSC § 39038.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39038.3

---

## Text

“Mojave Desert district” means the Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District created pursuant to Chapter 13 (commencing with Section 41200) of Part 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 642, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
