# HSC § 123470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 2. Maternal Health > Article: 2.7. Reproductive FACT Act

**Citation:** HSC § 123470

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Reproductive FACT (Freedom, Accountability, Comprehensive Care, and Transparency) Act or Reproductive FACT Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 700, Sec. 3. (AB 775) Effective January 1, 2016.)
