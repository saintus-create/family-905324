# HSC § 130304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 110. CALIFORNIA INITIATIVE TO ADVANCE PRECISION MEDICINE

**Citation:** HSC § 130304

---

## Text

This division shall remain in effect only until June 30, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 54. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024. Repealed as of June 30, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Division 110, commencing with Section 130300.)
