# HSC § 416.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. STATE DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 7.5. Conservatorship and Guardianship for Developmentally Disabled Persons

**Citation:** HSC § 416.23

---

## Text

This article does not authorize the care, treatment, or supervision or any control over any developmentally disabled person without the written consent of his parent or guardian or conservator.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 730.)
