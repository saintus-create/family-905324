# HSC § 39741.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 4.3. Carbon Sequestration > Article: 2. Carbon Capture, Removal, Utilization, and Storage Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39741.4

---

## Text

In furtherance of the objectives in Section 39741.1, by January 1, 2024, the state board may adopt protocols to support additional methods of utilization or storage of captured carbon dioxide, including carbon capture for use in products and in methods as identified by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 359, Sec. 2. (SB 905) Effective January 1, 2023.)
