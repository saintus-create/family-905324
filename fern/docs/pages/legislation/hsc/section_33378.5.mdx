# HSC § 33378.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 5.5. Referendums

**Citation:** HSC § 33378.5

---

## Text

The provisions of this part establishing a right of referendum shall not be applicable to a charter city in the County of Los Angeles containing a population of 1,000 or less until January 1, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 797.)
