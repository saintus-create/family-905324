# HSC § 1596.657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.35. Child Care Provider Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.657

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 3200.5 of the Family Code, a professional supervised visitation provider, as described in that section, shall, beginning January 1, 2021, be registered pursuant to Sections 1596.603 and 1596.605.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 823, Sec. 3. (AB 1165) Effective January 1, 2020.)
