# HSC § 8362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 7. Restrictions on Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 8362

---

## Text

Every director or officer authorizing or consenting to a loan, and the person who receives a loan, in violation of this article are severally guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
