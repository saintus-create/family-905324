# HSC § 40806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40806

---

## Text

A hearing board shall select a chairman from its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
