# HSC § 37603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 10. HISTORICAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

## Text

The local agency on request shall submit information regarding its programs to the State Historic Preservation Officer.
