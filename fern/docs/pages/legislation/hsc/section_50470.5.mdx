# HSC § 50470.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Building Homes and Jobs Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 50470.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter:

(a) “Department” means the Department of Housing and Community Development.

(b) “Local government” means any city, county, city and county, or the duly constituted governing body of an Indian reservation or rancheria.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 660, Sec. 5. (AB 1010) Effective January 1, 2020.)
