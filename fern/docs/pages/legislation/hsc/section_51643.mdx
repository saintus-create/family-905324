# HSC § 51643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 51643

---

## Text

The agency shall not be required to pay any tax or assessment on any property owned by the fund or upon the income therefrom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993.)
