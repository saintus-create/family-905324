# HSC § 40408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5.5. South Coast Air Quality Management District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40408

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “plan” means the south coast district air quality management plan.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 40405 (as added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1546) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 216, Sec. 75.)
