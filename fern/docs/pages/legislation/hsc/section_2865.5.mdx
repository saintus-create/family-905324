# HSC § 2865.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 3.5. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 2865.5

---

## Text

The county auditor shall enter each lien on the county tax roll opposite the parcel of land.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 61. Effective September 7, 1984.)
