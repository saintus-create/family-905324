# HSC § 128685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 5. HEALTH DATA > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Data

**Citation:** HSC § 128685

---

## Text

Intermediate care facilities/developmentally disabled-habilitative, as defined in subdivision (e) of Section 1250, are not subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
