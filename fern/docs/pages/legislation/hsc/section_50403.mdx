# HSC § 50403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1. Organization of the Department and General Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 50403

---

## Text

The Governor shall appoint, upon the recommendation of the director, a deputy director. The deputy director shall hold office at the pleasure of the director and shall receive a salary as shall be fixed by the director with the approval of the Department of Finance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
