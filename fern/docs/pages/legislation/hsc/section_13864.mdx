# HSC § 13864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 5. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 13864

---

## Text

A district may lease or rent any property from an employee, including but not limited to, vehicles or equipment.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
