# HSC § 33391.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 7. Property Acquisition

**Citation:** HSC § 33391.1

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 2012, an agency shall obtain an appraisal from a qualified independent appraiser to determine the fair market value of property before the agency acquires or purchases real property pursuant to Section 33391.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 299, Sec. 1. (AB 1338) Effective January 1, 2012.)
