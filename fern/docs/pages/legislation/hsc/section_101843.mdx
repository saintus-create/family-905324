# HSC § 101843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. San Luis Obispo County Hospital Authority > Article: 2. Hospital Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 101843

---

## Text

The hospital authority shall be a public agency subject to the Meyers-Milias-Brown Act (Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 3500) of Division 4 of Title 1 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 899, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
