# HSC § 118949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 4. Indoor Air Quality > Article: 2.5. Smoking in Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** HSC § 118949

---

## Text

A law enforcement officer shall not stop a vehicle for the sole purpose of determining whether the driver is in violation of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 425, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
