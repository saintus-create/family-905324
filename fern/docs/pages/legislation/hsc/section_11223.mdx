# HSC § 11223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 5. Use of Controlled Substances > Article: 2. Treatment of Addicts for Addiction

**Citation:** HSC § 11223

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a physician and surgeon who is registered with the federal Attorney General pursuant to Section 823(g) of Title 21 of the United States Code may provide treatment for addiction pursuant to this federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 93, Sec. 1. (AB 2268) Effective January 1, 2011.)
