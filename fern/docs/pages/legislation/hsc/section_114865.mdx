# HSC § 114865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 6. Radiologic Technology > Article: 3. Radiologic Technology Certification

**Citation:** HSC § 114865

---

## Text

Members of the committee shall serve without compensation but shall receive their actual and necessary expenses incurred in the performance of the duties of their office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
