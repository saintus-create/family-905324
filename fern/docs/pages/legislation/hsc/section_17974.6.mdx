# HSC § 17974.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 2.3. Inspection of Homeless Shelters

**Citation:** HSC § 17974.6

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that this article addresses a matter of statewide concern rather than a municipal affair as that term is used in Section 5 of Article XI of the California Constitution. Therefore, this article applies to all cities, including charter cities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 395, Sec. 1. (AB 362) Effective January 1, 2022.)
