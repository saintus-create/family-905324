# HSC § 39761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 4.7. Agricultural Biomass Utilization Account

**Citation:** HSC § 39761

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the following terms mean:

(a) “Department” means the Department of Food and Agriculture.

(b) “Secretary” means the Secretary of Food and Agriculture.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1017, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
