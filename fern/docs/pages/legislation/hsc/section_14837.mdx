# HSC § 14837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 14837

---

## Text

The seal shall be under the control of and for the use of the secretary, and be by him affixed to exempt certificates, certificates of active membership, and such other documents as the by-laws provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
