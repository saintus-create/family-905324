# HSC § 7006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7006

---

## Text

“Crematory” means a building or structure containing one or more furnaces for the reduction of bodies of deceased persons to cremated remains.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
