# HSC § 129945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 7. FACILITIES DESIGN REVIEW AND CONSTRUCTION > Chapter: 1. Health Facilities > Article: 5. Building Safety Board

**Citation:** HSC § 129945

---

## Text

The chair of the board shall be an appointive member and shall be elected by a majority of the appointive members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1996.)
