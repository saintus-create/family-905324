# HSC § 115273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 8. Radiation Control Law > Article: 19. Radioactive Waste Reduction

**Citation:** HSC § 115273

---

## Text

In implementing this chapter, the department, consistent with other requirements imposed by this chapter to protect public health and safety, shall promote the reduction of low-level radioactive waste generated, both in volume and radioactivity, by encouraging waste reduction practices, including, but not limited to, all of the following:

(a) The minimization of waste produced by employing best practices to reduce the amount of contaminated materials;

(b) The substitution and use of nonradioactive materials or radioactive materials with shorter radioactive half-lives; and

(c) The compaction of low-level radioactive waste to reduce the volume of waste that must be transported and disposed of in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 513, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
