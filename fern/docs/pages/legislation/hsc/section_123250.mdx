# HSC § 123250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Maternal, Child, and Adolescent Health

**Citation:** HSC § 123250

---

## Text

Upon request the department shall advise all public officers, organizations, and agencies interested in the health and welfare of mothers and children in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
