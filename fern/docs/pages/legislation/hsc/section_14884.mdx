# HSC § 14884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 2. Resolution

**Citation:** HSC § 14884

---

## Text

Any number of streets, highways, roads, or parcels of private property may be included in one resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
