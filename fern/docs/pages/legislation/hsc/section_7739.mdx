# HSC § 7739

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 3. Notice of Intention

**Citation:** HSC § 7739

---

## Text

The notice shall also be mailed to each known living heir at law of any person whose remains are interred in the cemetery, if his address is known.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
