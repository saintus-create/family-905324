# HSC § 5461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 3. Procedure for Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 5461

---

## Text

Any person who discharges sewage or other waste in any manner which results in contamination is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1447.)
