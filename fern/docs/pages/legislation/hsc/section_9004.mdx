# HSC § 9004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 4. PUBLIC CEMETERY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9004

---

## Text

This part is necessary to protect the public health, safety, and welfare, and shall be liberally construed to effectuate its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 57, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
