# HSC § 127501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 2.6. Health Care Affordability > Article: 2. Office of Health Care Affordability

**Citation:** HSC § 127501.5

---

## Text

The office shall, in a manner prescribed by the office, establish requirements for management services organizations to submit data and other information as necessary to carry out the functions of the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 641, Sec. 3. (AB 1415) Effective January 1, 2026.)
