# HSC § 13953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 10. Service Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 13953

---

## Text

As determined by the district board, a service zone may provide any service at any level within its boundaries which the district may provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
