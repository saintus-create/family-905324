# HSC § 121380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 5. TUBERCULOSIS > Chapter: 1. Tuberculosis Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121380

---

## Text

The department may advise officers of state educational, correctional, and medical institutions regarding the control of tuberculosis and the care of tuberculosis patients.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
