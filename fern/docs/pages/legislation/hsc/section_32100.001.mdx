# HSC § 32100.001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 1. Election and Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 32100.001

---

## Text

The board of hospital directors shall meet on the first Monday subsequent to 30 days after the completion of organization of the district and shall organize by the election of one of their members as chairperson or president and one as secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 32102 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 696, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
