# HSC § 8124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Veteran’s Commemorative Property

**Citation:** HSC § 8124

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be interpreted to prohibit a cemetery corporation or funeral establishment from selling new veteran’s commemorative property on either an at-need or pre-need basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 774, Sec. 1. (AB 1225) Effective January 1, 2013.)
