# HSC § 9000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 4. PUBLIC CEMETERY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 9000

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Public Cemetery District Law.
