# HSC § 33426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 10. Demolition, Clearance, Project Improvements, and Site Preparation

**Citation:** HSC § 33426

---

## Text

Each contractor and subcontractor shall keep an accurate record showing the name, occupation, and actual per diem wages paid to each workman employed by him in connection with the work. The record shall be kept open at all reasonable hours to the inspection of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
