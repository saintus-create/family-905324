# HSC § 40212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 2. City Selection Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 40212

---

## Text

With regard to the city selection committee appointment to the bay district board for the City and County of San Francisco, the mayor shall make the appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
