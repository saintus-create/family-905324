# HSC § 6590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 5. Elections > Article: 2.5. Central Contra Costa Sanitary District

**Citation:** HSC § 6590

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, this article shall apply only to the Central Contra Costa Sanitary District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 696, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
