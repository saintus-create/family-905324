# HSC § 6443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** HSC § 6443

---

## Text

The notice shall be given either by publication in the same manner as the original petition and for the same period, or by personal service on each nonappearing owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
