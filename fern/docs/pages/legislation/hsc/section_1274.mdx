# HSC § 1274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 2.5. Health Care Advisory Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 1274

---

## Text

The committee shall meet on call of the director but no less than one time per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1351, Sec. 2.)
