# HSC § 14901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 3. Notice to Destroy Weeds > Article: 5. Proceedings After Hearing on Notice

**Citation:** HSC § 14901

---

## Text

The officer, board or commission, and his or its assistants, deputies, employees, or contracting agents, or other representatives may enter upon private property for the purpose of removing the weeds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
