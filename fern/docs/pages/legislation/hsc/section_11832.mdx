# HSC § 11832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.1. Certification of Alcohol and Other Drug Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11832

---

## Text

(a) The department has the sole authority in state government to certify alcohol or other drug programs.

(b) In administering this chapter, the department shall issue certifications for a period of two years to those alcohol or other drug programs that meet the requirements set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 32. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
