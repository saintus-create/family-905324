# HSC § 6491.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6491.5

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2006.)
