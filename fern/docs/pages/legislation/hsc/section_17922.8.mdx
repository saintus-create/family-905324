# HSC § 17922.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 2. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 17922.8

---

## Text

The Office of Noise Control may appoint an advisory committee to assist the office in reviewing and revising the noise insulation standards previously adopted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 645, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1998.)
