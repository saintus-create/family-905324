# HSC § 114090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 4. General Food Safety Requirements > Article: 8. Consumer Information

**Citation:** HSC § 114090

---

## Text

(a) If required by law, consumer warnings shall be provided.

(b) Food facility’s or manufacturer’s dating information on foods may not be concealed or altered.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
