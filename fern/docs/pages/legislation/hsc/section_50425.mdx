# HSC § 50425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 1.5. Statewide Housing Plan

**Citation:** HSC § 50425

---

## Text

The goals and recommendations adopted for the plan shall be published once adopted, and shall be republished as revisions are adopted. The plan shall be published and made available to the public on the department’s internet website.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 341, Sec. 2. (AB 68) Effective January 1, 2022.)
