# HSC § 43601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Used Motor Vehicles > Article: 1. Device Certification

**Citation:** HSC § 43601

---

## Text

The state board shall certify exhaust devices for 1955 through 1965 model year motor vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
