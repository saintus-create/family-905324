# HSC § 6733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 3. Equalization of Assessments by District Assessor

**Citation:** HSC § 6733

---

## Text

When the assessment roll is returned by the district assessor, the board shall equalize the assessments, and the board shall continue in session as a board of equalization with reasonable intermissions until the roll has been examined, rectified, and equalized.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
