# HSC § 121880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 4. Animal Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121880

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “sentry dog” means a dog trained to work without supervision in a fenced facility and to deter or detain unauthorized persons found within the facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
