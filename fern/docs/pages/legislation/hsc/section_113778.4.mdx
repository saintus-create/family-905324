# HSC § 113778.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113778.4

---

## Text

“Fabric implement” means a cloth or fabric, including, but not limited to, burlap and cheesecloth, that is used as part of the food process and comes in direct contact with food that is subsequently cooked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 571, Sec. 5. (SB 241) Effective October 11, 2009.)
