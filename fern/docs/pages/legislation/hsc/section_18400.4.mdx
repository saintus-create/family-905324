# HSC § 18400.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 3. Enforcement, Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** HSC § 18400.4

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “mobilehome owner” or “mobilehome owners” means the occupant of the manufactured home or mobilehome, or the registered owner of the manufactured home or mobilehome, if different from the occupant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 520, Sec. 5. Effective September 27, 1999. Operative January 1, 2000, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 520.)
