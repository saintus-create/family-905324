# HSC § 2861.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 3.5. District Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 2861.5

---

## Text

Any judicial review of administrative procedure provided for in this chapter shall be pursuant to Section 1094.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 465.)
