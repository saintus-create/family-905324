# HSC § 7800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 5. Removals by Relatives or Friends

**Citation:** HSC § 7800

---

## Text

At any time prior to the removal by a cemetery authority of the remains of any person, any relative or friend of the decedent may voluntarily remove and dispose of the remains.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
