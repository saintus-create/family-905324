# HSC § 34140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.7. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 34140

---

## Text

The powers duties, and responsibilities of the commission, the redevelopment agency, and the housing authority are vested in the commissioners in office. Each commission exercises governmental functions and has the powers prescribed in this part. Each commission is performing a public function of the community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 642.)
