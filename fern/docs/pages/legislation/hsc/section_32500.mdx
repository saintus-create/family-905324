# HSC § 32500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23.5. ENDOWMENT HOSPITALS

**Citation:** HSC § 32500

---

## Text

The provisions of this division shall be liberally construed to effect its objects and promote its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 82.)
