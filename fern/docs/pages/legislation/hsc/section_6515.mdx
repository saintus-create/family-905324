# HSC § 6515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 4. District Powers > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6515

---

## Text

It may make and accept contracts, deeds, releases, and documents that, in the judgment of the board, are necessary or proper in the exercise of any of the powers of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
