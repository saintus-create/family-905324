# HSC § 112000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 4. Wholesale Food Processors > Article: 1. Food Processing Establishments

**Citation:** HSC § 112000

---

## Text

The floors of toilet-rooms shall be made of cement, or of tile laid in cement, wood, brick, or other nonabsorbent material, and shall be washed and scoured daily.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
