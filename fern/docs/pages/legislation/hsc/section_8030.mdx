# HSC § 8030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 5. California Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation > Article: 4. Penalties and Enforcement Procedures

**Citation:** HSC § 8030

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are severable. If any provision of this chapter or its application is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 818, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
