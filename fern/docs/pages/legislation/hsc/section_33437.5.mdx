# HSC § 33437.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 11. Property Disposition, Rehabilitation and Development

**Citation:** HSC § 33437.5

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that property acquired from a redevelopment agency pursuant to a redevelopment plan not be the subject of real estate speculation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 565, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1998.)
