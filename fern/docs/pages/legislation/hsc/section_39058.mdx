# HSC § 39058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39058

---

## Text

“Used motor vehicle” means any motor vehicle which is not a new motor vehicle.
