# HSC § 6641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6641

---

## Text

A district may issue bonds to raise money for any of the purposes stated in Section 6512 hereof.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1944, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 53.)
