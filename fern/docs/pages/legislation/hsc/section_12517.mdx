# HSC § 12517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12517

---

## Text

“Licensee” means any person 21 years of age or older holding a fireworks license issued pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 12570).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
