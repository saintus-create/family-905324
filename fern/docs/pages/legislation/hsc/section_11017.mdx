# HSC § 11017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11017

---

## Text

“Manufacturer” has the same meaning as provided in Section 4034 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
