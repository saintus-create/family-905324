# HSC § 50063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50063

---

## Text

“Construction loan” means a short-term loan secured by real property, made for development costs incurred in construction or rehabilitation of a housing development or residential structure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 312, Sec. 4. Effective June 28, 1982.)
