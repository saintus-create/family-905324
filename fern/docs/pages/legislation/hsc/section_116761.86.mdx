# HSC § 116761.86

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 4.5. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund Law of 1997 > Article: 7. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund Management

**Citation:** HSC § 116761.86

---

## Text

To the extent amounts in the fund are not required for current obligation or expenditure, those amounts shall be invested in interest bearing obligations, and the interest earned shall become part of the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 734, Sec. 15. Effective October 7, 1997.)
