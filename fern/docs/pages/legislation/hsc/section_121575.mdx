# HSC § 121575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 1. Rabies Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121575

---

## Text

“Rabies,” as used in this chapter, includes rabies, and any other animal disease dangerous to human beings that may be declared by the department as coming under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 351.5. Effective September 29, 1996.)
