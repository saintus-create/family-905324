# HSC § 25551.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.97. Hazardous Materials Information and Consulting Services

**Citation:** HSC § 25551.2

---

## Text

A county may contract with another county, or enter into a memorandum of agreement with one or more nearby counties, to provide consulting services for businesses within a multicounty region.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1432, Sec. 1.)
