# HSC § 113953.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 3. Management and Personnel > Article: 4. Handwashing

**Citation:** HSC § 113953.2

---

## Text

A handwashing facility shall be provided with the following in dispensers at, or adjacent to, each handwashing facility:

(a) Handwashing cleanser.

(b) Sanitary single-use towels or a heated-air hand drying device.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
