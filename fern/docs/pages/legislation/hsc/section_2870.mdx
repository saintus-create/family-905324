# HSC § 2870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 2870

---

## Text

The district board shall annually before July 10 prepare a written estimate of the amount of money necessary for the purposes of the district during the ensuing fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 64. Effective September 7, 1984.)
