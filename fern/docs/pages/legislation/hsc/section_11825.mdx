# HSC § 11825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 6. Evaluation > Article: 1. Statewide Evaluation

**Citation:** HSC § 11825

---

## Text

The department may establish reasonable criteria to evaluate the performance of programs and services that are described in the county contract for alcohol and other drug services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 33. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
