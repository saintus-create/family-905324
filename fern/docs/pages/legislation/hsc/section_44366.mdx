# HSC § 44366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 6. AIR TOXICS “HOT SPOTS” INFORMATION AND ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 4. Risk Assessment

**Citation:** HSC § 44366

---

## Text

In order to verify the accuracy of any information submitted by facilities pursuant to this part, a district or the state board may proceed in accordance with Section 41510.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 407. Effective January 1, 2007.)
