# HSC § 19975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 19975

---

## Text

“Local enforcement agency” means the city, county, or city and county in which factory-built housing is installed.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1422.)
