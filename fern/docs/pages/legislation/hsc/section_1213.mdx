# HSC § 1213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1213

---

## Text

A person, firm, association, partnership, corporation, or other legal entity desiring a license for a clinic shall be exempt from the requirements of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 16000) of Division 12.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 502, Sec. 3. (SB 442) Effective January 1, 2011.)
