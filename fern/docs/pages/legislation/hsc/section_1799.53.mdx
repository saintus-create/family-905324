# HSC § 1799.53

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. The Commission on Emergency Medical Services > Article: 2. Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 1799.53

---

## Text

The commission shall advise the director with regard to communications, medical equipment, training personnel, facilities, and other components of an emergency medical services system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
