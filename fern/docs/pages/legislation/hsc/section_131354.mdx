# HSC § 131354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 112. PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Childhood Drowning Data Collection Pilot Program

**Citation:** HSC § 131354

---

## Text

Based on the reports submitted pursuant to this chapter, the department shall develop both of the following:

(a) (1) A California Water Safety Action Plan for Children. The plan shall be a comprehensive, realistic, and executable plan patterned after the United States National Water Safety Action Plan, which aims to create water-safe communities and states.

(2) The department shall post the plan developed pursuant to this subdivision on its internet website on or before January 1, 2027.

(b) A standardized electronic form for counties to use in reporting drowning statistics developed in consultation with the state advisory group and the National Center for Fatality Review and Prevention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 817, Sec. 2. (SB 855) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Sec. 131355.)
