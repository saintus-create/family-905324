# HSC § 12514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12514

---

## Text

“Issuing authority” means any person who has the responsibility of evaluating the application for, and issuing, the permits required by Section 12640.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
