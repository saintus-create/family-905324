# HSC § 122361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 9. Pet Store Animal Care

**Citation:** HSC § 122361

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2009.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 703, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2008. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (Jan. 1, 2009) for Chapter 9, commencing with Section 122350.)
