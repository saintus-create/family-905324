# HSC § 38500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25.5. CALIFORNIA GLOBAL WARMING SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2006 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Title of Division

**Citation:** HSC § 38500

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Global Warming Solutions Act of 2006.

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 488, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
