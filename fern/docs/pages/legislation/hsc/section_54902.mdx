# HSC § 54902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 17. California Surplus Land Unit > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 54902

---

## Text

Implementation of this part shall be subject to an appropriation of funds by the Legislature for purposes of establishing and funding the activities of the unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 366, Sec. 1. (SB 791) Effective January 1, 2022.)
