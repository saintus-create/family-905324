# HSC § 1853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 13. Community Paramedicine or Triage to Alternate Destination > Article: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** HSC § 1853

---

## Text

A triage paramedic shall provide triage to alternate destination services only if the triage paramedic has been certified and accredited to perform those services by a local EMS agency and is working as an employee of an authorized triage to alternate destination provider.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 138, Sec. 4. (AB 1544) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 1857.)
