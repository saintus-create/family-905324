# HSC § 80685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 11. California Financial Assurance and Insurance for Redevelopment Program > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 80685

---

## Text

“CLEAN Program” means the Cleanup Loans and Environmental Assistance to Neighborhoods Program established pursuant to Section 80450.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
