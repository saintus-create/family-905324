# HSC § 118135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 7. Medical Waste Treatment Facility Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 118135

---

## Text

Each person operating an offsite medical waste treatment facility shall obtain a permit pursuant to this chapter from the department prior to commencement of the treatment facility’s operation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 564, Sec. 65. (AB 333) Effective January 1, 2015.)
