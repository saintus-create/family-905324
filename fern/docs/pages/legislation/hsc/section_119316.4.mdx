# HSC § 119316.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 15. MISCELLANEOUS REQUIREMENTS > Chapter: 7. Body Art > Article: 4.5. Mobile Body Art Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 119316.4

---

## Text

A mobile body art facility shall only be operated within 200 feet of an accessible restroom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 555, Sec. 14. (AB 1168) Effective January 1, 2014.)
