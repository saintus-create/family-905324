# HSC § 127010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 1. DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 127010

---

## Text

The director of the department shall have the powers of a head of the department pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11150) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 33. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
