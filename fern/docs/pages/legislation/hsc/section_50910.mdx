# HSC § 50910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** HSC § 50910

---

## Text

The executive director may from time to time employ technical experts and other employees as may, in his or her judgment, be necessary for the conduct of the business of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 1. Effective July 15, 1993.)
