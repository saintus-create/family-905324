# HSC § 101710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Santa Barbara County Special Health Care Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by motion, resolution or ordinance.
