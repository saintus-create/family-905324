# HSC § 4876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 4876

---

## Text

At any time prior to the time fixed for the hearing any interested person may file with the clerk written objections to the formation of the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
