# HSC § 117130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 12. DRINKING WATER > Chapter: 7. Water Supply > Article: 3. Punishment or Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 117130

---

## Text

Violation of any provision of this chapter is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
