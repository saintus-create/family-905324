# HSC § 19977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 19977

---

## Text

“Site” is the entire tract, subdivision or parcel of land on which factory-built housing is installed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1422.)
