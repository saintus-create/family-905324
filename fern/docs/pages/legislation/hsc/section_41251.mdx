# HSC § 41251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41251

---

## Text

Except as specified in this article, no county officer shall be responsible for producing reports, statements, and other data relating to or based upon payments of salaries or claims of the Mojave Desert district pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 642, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
