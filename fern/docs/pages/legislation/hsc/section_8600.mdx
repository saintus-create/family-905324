# HSC § 8600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 4. Property Rights > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 8600

---

## Text

All plots conveyed to individuals are presumed to be the sole and separate property of the owner named in the instrument of conveyance.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 339.)
