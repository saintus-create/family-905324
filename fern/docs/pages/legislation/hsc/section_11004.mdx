# HSC § 11004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11004

---

## Text

“Attorney General” means the Attorney General of the State of California.
