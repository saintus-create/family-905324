# HSC § 33492.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4.5. Military Base Conversion Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 33492.1

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that extraordinary measures must be taken to mitigate the effects of the federal government’s efforts to reduce the number of military bases throughout the country.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 944, Sec. 4. Effective October 8, 1993.)
