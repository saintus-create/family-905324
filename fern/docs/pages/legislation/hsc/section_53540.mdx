# HSC § 53540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 12. HOUSING AND EMERGENCY SHELTER TRUST FUND ACT OF 2006 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 53540

---

## Text

(a) This part shall be known as the Housing and Emergency Shelter Trust Fund Act of 2006.

(b) This part shall only become operative upon adoption by the voters at the November 7, 2006, statewide general election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 27, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 1C at the November 7, 2006, election.)
