# HSC § 33134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redevelopment Agencies > Article: 3. Nature, Jurisdiction, and General Powers of Agencies

**Citation:** HSC § 33134

---

## Text

Within the survey area or for purposes of redevelopment an agency may insure or provide for the insurance of any operations of the agency against risks or hazards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1665.)
