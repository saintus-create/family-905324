# HSC § 13173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 2. Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 13173

---

## Text

The holder of any license shall submit such license for inspection upon request of the State Fire Marshal, any of his properly authorized employees, or any local fire official.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 802.)
