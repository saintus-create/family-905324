# HSC § 43100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 2. New Motor Vehicles > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 43100

## Text

The state board may certify new motor vehicles and new motor vehicle engines pursuant to this article.
