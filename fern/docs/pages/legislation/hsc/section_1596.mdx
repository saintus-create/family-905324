# HSC § 1596

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.3. California Adult Day Health Care Act > Article: 7. Offenses

**Citation:** HSC § 1596

---

## Text

Any action brought by the director against an adult day health center shall not abate by reason of a sale or other transfer of ownership of the adult day health center which is a party to the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1066.)
