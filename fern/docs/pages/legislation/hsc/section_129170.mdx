# HSC § 129170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 3. Defaults

**Citation:** HSC § 129170

---

## Text

No lender or borrower shall have any right or interest in any property conveyed to the department or in any claim assigned to it, nor shall the department owe any duty to any lender or borrower with respect to the management or disposal of this property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 259. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
