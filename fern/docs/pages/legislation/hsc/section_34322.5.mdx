# HSC § 34322.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Housing Authorities

**Citation:** HSC § 34322.5

---

## Text

No authority shall require occupants of any project to be citizens of the United States, provided, however, that in the event there are insufficient housing units to accommodate the number of applicants for such units, the authority may give preference to citizens of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1583.)
