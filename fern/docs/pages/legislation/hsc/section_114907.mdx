# HSC § 114907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 7. Atomic Energy Development > Article: 2. Declaration of Policy

**Citation:** HSC § 114907

---

## Text

“Atomic energy” means all forms of energy released in the course of nuclear transformation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
