# HSC § 120260.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Chapter: 3.5. Communicable Diseases Exposure Notification Act

**Citation:** HSC § 120260.5

---

## Text

The communicable disease testing and notification procedures provided for in this chapter are in addition to the notification to which prehospital emergency medical care persons or personnel are entitled under Section 1797.188.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 102, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
