# HSC § 51340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.7. Multifamily Rental Housing > Article: 3. State Assistance

**Citation:** HSC § 51340

## Text

This chapter constitutes an alternative method to finance construction loans and mortgage loans for multifamily rental housing pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.
