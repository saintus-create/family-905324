# HSC § 41971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 6. Gasoline Cargo Tanks

**Citation:** HSC § 41971

---

## Text

Proof of correction shall consist either of a verification pursuant to Section 41972 or of a certification by an authorized representative of one of the following agencies that the alleged violation has been corrected:

(a) The state board.

(b) The State Fire Marshal.

(c) The district board.

(d) The Department of the California Highway Patrol.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1255, Sec. 2.3. Operative July 1, 1983, or sooner, by Sec. 27.5 of Ch. 1255.)
