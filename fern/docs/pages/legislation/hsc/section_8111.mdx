# HSC § 8111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Records

**Citation:** HSC § 8111

---

## Text

The records shall at all times be open to official inspection.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
