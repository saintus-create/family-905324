# HSC § 17975.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 5. Administration and Enforcement > Article: 2.5. Tenant Relocation Assistance

**Citation:** HSC § 17975.8

---

## Text

Any order by a local agency that requires a tenant’s displacement and is issued to an owner, designated agent, or tenant, shall be accompanied by a summary of the provisions of this article. Failure to provide a summary shall not relieve any person of the obligations imposed by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 473, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
