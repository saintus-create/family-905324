# HSC § 7006.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7006.8

---

## Text

(a) “Hydrolyzed human remains container” means a receptacle in which hydrolyzed human remains are placed after hydrolysis.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 33. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, by its own provisions.)
