# HSC § 12690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 7. Violations

**Citation:** HSC § 12690

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to perform any act, or transact or attempt to transact any business, with an expired license or an expired permit where a license or permit is required for the performance of such act or transaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
