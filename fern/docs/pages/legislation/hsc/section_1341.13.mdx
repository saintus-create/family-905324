# HSC § 1341.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 1341.13

---

## Text

All officers or employees of the department employed after the operative date of this section shall be appointed by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 525, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, or sooner, by Sec. 214 of Ch. 525.)
