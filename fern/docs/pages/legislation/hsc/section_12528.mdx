# HSC § 12528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12528

---

## Text

“Retailer” means any person who, at a fixed place of business, sells, transfers, or gives fireworks to a consumer or user.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
