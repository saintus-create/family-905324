# HSC § 51332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 6.7. Multifamily Rental Housing > Article: 2. Financing

**Citation:** HSC § 51332

---

## Text

Subject only to the limitations prescribed in this chapter, the agency, in addition to any other power conferred by this part, may purchase, or undertake, directly or indirectly through lending institutions, commitments to purchase, construction loans and mortgage loans originated in accordance with a financing agreement with the agency to finance the acquisition, construction, rehabilitation, refinancing, or development of multifamily rental housing and for the provision of capital improvements in connection with, and determined necessary to, such multifamily rental housing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 27, Sec. 7. Effective April 8, 1996.)
