# HSC § 18105.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 8. Registration and Titling of Manufactured Homes, Mobilehomes, and Commercial Coaches > Article: 4. Amendments, Transfers, and Transactions

**Citation:** HSC § 18105.5

---

## Text

A legal owner or junior lienholder may assign title or interest to a manufactured home, mobilehome, commercial coach, truck camper, or floating home subject to registration under this part without the consent, and without affecting the interest of the registered owner or other lienholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 891, Sec. 10.)
