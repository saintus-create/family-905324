# HSC § 102790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 6. Death Registration > Article: 1. Duty of Registering Death

**Citation:** HSC § 102790

---

## Text

The funeral director shall obtain the required information other than medical and health section data from the person or source best qualified to supply this information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
