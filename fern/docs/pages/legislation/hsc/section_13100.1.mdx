# HSC § 13100.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. State Fire Marshal > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 13100.1

---

## Text

The functions of the office shall be to foster, promote and develop ways and means of protecting life and property against fire and panic.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1173.)
