# HSC § 1643.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4.1. Tissue Banks > Article: 3. Suspension or Revocation of License

**Citation:** HSC § 1643.1

---

## Text

Proceedings for the suspension or revocation of licenses under this chapter shall be conducted in accordance with Section 100171.
