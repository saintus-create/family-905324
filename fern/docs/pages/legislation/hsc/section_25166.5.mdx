# HSC § 25166.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 6. Transportation

**Citation:** HSC § 25166.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the department may, by regulation, provide for the issuance and renewal of a hazardous waste transporter registration on a two-year basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 870, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1998. Operative July 1, 1998, by Sec. 54 of Ch. 870.)
