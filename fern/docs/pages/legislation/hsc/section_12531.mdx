# HSC § 12531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12531

---

## Text

“Sell” means any arrangement between two or more persons as a result of which there is a transfer of property for a consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
