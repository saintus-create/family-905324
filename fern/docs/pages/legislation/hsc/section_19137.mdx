# HSC § 19137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 3. MISCELLANEOUS > Chapter: 2. Earthquake Protection > Article: 2a. Building Permits

**Citation:** HSC § 19137

---

## Text

The issuance of a permit does not constitute approval of any violation of any provision of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 301.)
