# HSC § 51506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 11. California Homebuyer’s Downpayment Assistance Program

**Citation:** HSC § 51506

---

## Text

The downpayment assistance provided by this program shall be limited to first-time homebuyers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 81, Sec. 1. Effective July 5, 2000. Inoperative on July 1, 2016, as provided in Section 51511.)
