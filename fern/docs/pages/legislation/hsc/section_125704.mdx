# HSC § 125704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 8. ADULT HEALTH > Chapter: 1. California Osteoporosis Prevention and Education Act

**Citation:** HSC § 125704

---

## Text

In consultation with the advisory panel convened pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 125703, the department shall develop effective protocols for the prevention of falls and fractures and establish these protocols in community practice to improve the prevention and management of osteoporosis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 819, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
