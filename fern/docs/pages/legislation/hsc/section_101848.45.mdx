# HSC § 101848.45

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 4. SPECIAL HEALTH AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. San Luis Obispo County Hospital Authority > Article: 2. Hospital Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 101848.45

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as preventing the governing board from meeting in closed session as otherwise provided by law.
