# HSC § 1799.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. The Commission on Emergency Medical Services > Article: 1. The Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 1799.6

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall receive no compensation for their services, but shall be reimbursed for their actual, necessary, traveling and other expenses incurred in the discharge of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
