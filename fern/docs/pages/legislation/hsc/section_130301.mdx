# HSC § 130301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 110. CALIFORNIA INITIATIVE TO ADVANCE PRECISION MEDICINE

**Citation:** HSC § 130301

---

## Text

As used in this division, “California Initiative to Advance Precision Medicine” or “initiative” means the California Initiative to Advance Precision Medicine established in Section 130302.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 54. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024. Repealed as of June 30, 2029, pursuant to Sec. 130304.)
