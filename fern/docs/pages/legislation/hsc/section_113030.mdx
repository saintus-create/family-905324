# HSC § 113030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 10. Processed Pet Foods > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113030

---

## Text

“Pet” means any household animal including but not limited to cats or dogs and other carnivores whether or not for exhibition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
