# HSC § 28000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 25. Social Media Warning Law

**Citation:** HSC § 28000

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known as the Social Media Warning Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 671, Sec. 2. (AB 56) Effective January 1, 2026. Operative January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 28002.)
