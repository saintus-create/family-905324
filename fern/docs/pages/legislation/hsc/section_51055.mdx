# HSC § 51055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 4. General Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 51055

---

## Text

The agency may purchase and sell construction loans, mortgage loans, property improvement loans, obligations secured by these loans, insurance on these loans, and participation therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1034, Sec. 27.)
