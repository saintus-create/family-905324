# HSC § 4614.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 2a. Formation of Districts in Two or More Municipal Corporations and Also in Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** HSC § 4614.1

---

## Text

Districts may be formed pursuant to this article for the purpose of making any improvement which will be of benefit to territory partially within the initiating city and partially within any other city or within unincorporated area of the same county, or both.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 828.)
