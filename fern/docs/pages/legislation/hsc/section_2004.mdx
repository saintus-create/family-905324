# HSC § 2004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 1. Mosquito Abatement and Vector Control Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 2004

---

## Text

This chapter is necessary to protect the public health, safety, and welfare, and shall be liberally construed to effectuate its purposes.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 395, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
