# HSC § 36102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 7.5. MODEL CITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. Legislative Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** HSC § 36102

---

## Text

The Legislature further finds that, because of the state’s extensive involvement in the programs which directly affect Model Cities areas, the Model Cities program will not succeed in California without significant state interest and cooperation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1462.)
