# HSC § 25110.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25110.8

---

## Text

“Business concern” means any sole proprietorship, corporation, association, firm, partnership, trust, or other form of commercial organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1304, Sec. 3.)
