# HSC § 112695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 8. Canneries > Article: 2. Cannery Inspection Board

**Citation:** HSC § 112695

---

## Text

Members of the board serve without compensation. The board shall meet at least quarterly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
