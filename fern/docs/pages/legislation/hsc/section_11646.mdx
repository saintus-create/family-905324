# HSC § 11646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 12. Clandestine Laboratory Enforcement Program

**Citation:** HSC § 11646

## Text

The Attorney General shall adopt rules and regulations for the administration and enforcement of this chapter.
