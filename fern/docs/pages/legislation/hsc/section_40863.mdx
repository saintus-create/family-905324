# HSC § 40863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Hearing Boards > Article: 4. Decisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40863

## Text

The decision shall become effective upon filing, unless the hearing board orders otherwise.
