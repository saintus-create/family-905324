# HSC § 6440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** HSC § 6440

---

## Text

At the time designated the board of supervisors shall hear the petition, and may adjourn the hearing from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
