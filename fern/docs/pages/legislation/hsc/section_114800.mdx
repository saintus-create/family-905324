# HSC § 114800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 5. Containment of Radioactive Materials > Article: 2. Radiation Monitoring Devices for Nuclear Power Plants

**Citation:** HSC § 114800

---

## Text

In no event shall a plant operator be required to spend more than one million dollars ($1,000,000) in capital outlay for a nuclear powerplant site in complying with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
