# HSC § 1596.80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 2. Administration of Child Day Care Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.80

---

## Text

No person, firm, partnership, association, or corporation shall operate, establish, manage, conduct, or maintain a child day care facility in this state without a current valid license therefor as provided in this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1016, Sec. 7.)
