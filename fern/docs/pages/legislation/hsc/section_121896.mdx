# HSC § 121896

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 4. Animal Control

**Citation:** HSC § 121896

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “trainer” means any person who engages in the practice of training any attack, guard, or sentry dog.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 377, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
