# HSC § 25395.62

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.82. California Land Reuse and Revitalization Act of 2004 > Article: 1. Legislative Findings and Intent

**Citation:** HSC § 25395.62

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the “California Land Reuse and Revitalization Act of 2004.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 705, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005. Repealed as of January 1, 2037, pursuant to Section 25395.109, with continuing effect as provided in Section 25395.110.)
