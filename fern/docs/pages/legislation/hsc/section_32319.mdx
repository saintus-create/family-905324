# HSC § 32319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 32319

---

## Text

The board of directors may sell the bonds pursuant to the resolution as follows:

(a) By giving notice inviting sealed bids and selling to the highest responsible bidder.

(b) By private sale, pursuant to a vote of four-fifths of the entire board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1513, Sec. 17.)
