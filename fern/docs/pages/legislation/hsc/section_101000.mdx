# HSC § 101000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 1. Organization and Appointment of Health Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 101000

---

## Text

Each board of supervisors shall appoint a health officer who is a county officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
