# HSC § 6952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 6952

---

## Text

“On-site wastewater disposal system” means any of several works, facilities, devices, or other mechanisms used to collect, treat, recycle, or dispose of wastewater without the use of communitywide sanitary sewers or sewage systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 28, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
