# HSC § 114139

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 6. Equipment, Utensils, and Linens > Article: 1. Design and Construction

**Citation:** HSC § 114139

---

## Text

Cutting or piercing parts of can openers shall be readily removable for cleaning and for replacement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
