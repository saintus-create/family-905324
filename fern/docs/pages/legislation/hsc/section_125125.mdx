# HSC § 125125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5. HEREDITARY DISEASES/CONGENITAL DEFECTS > Chapter: 2. Genetic Disease Services > Article: 1. Genetically Handicapped Persons Program

**Citation:** HSC § 125125

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Holden-Moscone-Garamendi Genetically Handicapped Persons Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 353. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
