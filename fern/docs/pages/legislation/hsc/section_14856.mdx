# HSC § 14856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** HSC § 14856

---

## Text

The burden of providing proof of eligibility for the privileges and exemptions of Section 14855 shall be the responsibility of the volunteer firefighter with the reasonable cooperation of his or her department.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2009.)
