# HSC § 1569.356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.356

---

## Text

To the extent that the department’s computer system can electronically accommodate additional residential care facility for the elderly profile information, the department shall post on its Internet Web site the current name, business address, and telephone number of the licensee, the name of the owner of the residential care facility for the elderly, if not the same as the licensee, the name of any parent organization, the licensed capacity of the facility, including the capacity for nonambulatory residents, whether the facility is permitted to accept and retain residents receiving hospice care services, whether the facility has a special care unit or program for people with Alzheimer’s disease and other dementias and has a delayed egress or secured perimeter system in place, or both, and information required pursuant to subparagraph (B) of paragraph (3) of subdivision (a) of Section 1569.15.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 628, Sec. 4. (AB 601) Effective January 1, 2016.)
