# HSC § 25534.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.95. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory > Article: 2. Hazardous Materials Management

**Citation:** HSC § 25534.2

---

## Text

Any new or modified stationary source which is required to prepare an RMP pursuant to this article shall be subject to the requirements of Section 65850.2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 715, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1997.)
