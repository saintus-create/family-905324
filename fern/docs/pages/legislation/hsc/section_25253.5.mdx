# HSC § 25253.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 14. Green Chemistry

**Citation:** HSC § 25253.5

---

## Text

The department shall revise its 2015–17 Priority Product Work Plan to include lead acid batteries for consideration and evaluation as a potential priority product.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 340, Sec. 23. (SB 839) Effective September 13, 2016.)
