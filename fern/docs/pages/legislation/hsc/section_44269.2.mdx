# HSC § 44269.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 8.8. Electric Vehicles

**Citation:** HSC § 44269.2

---

## Text

(a) (1) The commission, in consultation with the board and the Public Utilities Commission, may require any weight class of battery electric vehicle to be bidirectional-capable if it determines there is a sufficiently compelling beneficial bidirectional-capable use case to the battery electric vehicle operator and electrical grid.

(2) In its analysis, the commission shall consider vehicle readiness and duty cycles required of vehicles operated by essential service providers.

(b) This section does not prohibit the board from crediting a manufacturer of a battery electric vehicle that voluntarily includes bidirectional capability for that battery electric vehicle weight class.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 765, Sec. 2. (SB 59) Effective January 1, 2025.)
