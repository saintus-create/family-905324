# HSC § 8028.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 5. California Native American Graves Protection and Repatriation > Article: 3. Conditions on the Use of State Funds for the Handling or Maintenance of Native American Human Remains and Cultural Items

**Citation:** HSC § 8028.5

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are severable. If any provision of this article or its application is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 823, Sec. 3. (AB 2836) Effective January 1, 2019.)
