# HSC § 100115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 1. CALIFORNIA DEPARTMENT OF HEALTH SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Organization of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 100115

---

## Text

There is in the department a Division of Rural Health. The division shall administer Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 124550) and Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 124575) of Part 4 of Division 106, Section 101300, and Article 1 (commencing with Section 124600) of Chapter 5 of Part 4 of Division 106.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
