# HSC § 1337.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Health Facilities > Article: 9. Training Programs in Skilled Nursing and Intermediate Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1337.4

---

## Text

Every skilled nursing or intermediate care facility shall designate a licensed nurse as a director of staff development who shall be responsible for the management of the approved training program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 825, Sec. 3.)
