# HSC § 40304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 1. Creation of Regional Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 40304

---

## Text

From and after the date of the filing of certified copies of resolutions from two or more boards of supervisors desiring to create a regional district, the regional district shall begin to function and may exercise its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
