# HSC § 112065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 4. Wholesale Food Processors > Article: 2. Food Containers

**Citation:** HSC § 112065

---

## Text

This article is not applicable to containers subject to Division 15 (commencing with Section 32501) of the Food and Agricultural Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
