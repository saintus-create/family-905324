# HSC § 39043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39043

---

## Text

“Nonvehicular sources” means all sources of air contaminants, including the loading of fuels into vehicles, except vehicular sources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
