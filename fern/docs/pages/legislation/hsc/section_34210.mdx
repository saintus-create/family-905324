# HSC § 34210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 34210

---

## Text

“Federal Government” means the United States, the Federal Emergency Administration of Public Works, or any other agency or instrumentality, corporate or otherwise, of the United States.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
