# HSC § 11857.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 14. Ethical Treatment for Persons with Substance Use Disorder

**Citation:** HSC § 11857.4

---

## Text

(a) Every treatment provider shall maintain records of referrals made to or from recovery residences, including, if available, information about where the client referred by a treatment provider ultimately elected to go.

(b) For purposes of this section, “recovery residence” has the same meaning as in Section 11833.05.

(c) For purposes of this section, “referral” means when a person or entity provides a client by any means the name, address, or other identifying information for a recovery residence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 15, Sec. 2. (SB 349) Effective January 1, 2023.)
