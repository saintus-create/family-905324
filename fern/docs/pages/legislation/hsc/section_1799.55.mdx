# HSC § 1799.55

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 8. The Commission on Emergency Medical Services > Article: 2. Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 1799.55

---

## Text

Based upon evaluations of the EMS systems in the state and their coordination, the commission shall make recommendations for further development and future directions of the emergency medical services in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
