# HSC § 1796.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Home Care Services > Article: 10. Complaints, Inspections, and Investigations

**Citation:** HSC § 1796.51

---

## Text

In order to carry out the provisions of this chapter, the department may establish procedures for the receipt, investigation, and resolution of complaints against home care organizations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 790, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2014. Provisions implemented as of January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 1796.61.)
