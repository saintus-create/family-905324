# HSC § 25299.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.75. Petroleum Underground Storage Tank Cleanup > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25299.15

---

## Text

“Environmental impairment liability insurance” means liability insurance against liability for bodily injury, as defined in Section 25299.12, and for property damage, as defined in Section 25299.23, arising from an occurrence, as defined in Section 25299.19.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 544, Sec. 7. (SB 1458) Effective January 1, 2015.)
