# HSC § 111822.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 7.5. Menstrual Products

**Citation:** HSC § 111822.8

---

## Text

A menstrual product shall not be sold in the state unless the menstrual product and the manufacturer of the menstrual product comply with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 272, Sec. 1. (AB 1989) Effective January 1, 2021.)
