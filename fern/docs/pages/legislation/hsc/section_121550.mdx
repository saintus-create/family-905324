# HSC § 121550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 5. TUBERCULOSIS > Chapter: 3. Tuberculosis Tests for Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 121550

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prevent the school from requiring more extensive or more frequent examinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
