# HSC § 50199.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.6. Low-Income Housing Credit

## Text

The committee may also contract with other entities, including the department and the agency, to aid in the processing and review of applications.
