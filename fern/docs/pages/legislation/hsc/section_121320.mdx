# HSC § 121320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 4. HUMAN IMMUNODEFICIENCY VIRUS (HIV) > Chapter: 14. Acquired Immune Deficiency Syndrome (AIDS) Clinical Trial Grant Award for the Prevention of Maternal Transmission of Human Immunodeficiency Virus (HIV) Infection

**Citation:** HSC § 121320

---

## Text

No person shall be denied the opportunity to be a research subject because of the inability to pay for medical treatment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
