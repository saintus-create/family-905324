# HSC § 110015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 110015

---

## Text

“Principal display panel” means that part of a label most likely to be displayed, presented, shown, or examined under normal and customary conditions of display for retail sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
