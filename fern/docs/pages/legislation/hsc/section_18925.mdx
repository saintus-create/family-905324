# HSC § 18925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 2. Organization > Article: 2. The Executive Director of the State Building Standards Commission

**Citation:** HSC § 18925

---

## Text

The commission shall appoint an Executive Director of the California Building Standards Commission, who shall hold office at the pleasure of the commission. The executive director shall make public the processes of the commission. The executive director shall appoint, in accordance with civil service and other provisions of law, officers and employees necessary to carry out the intent and purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 897, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1993.)
