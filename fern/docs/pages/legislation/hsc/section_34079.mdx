# HSC § 34079

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1.6. NATURAL DISASTERS > Chapter: 2. Natural Disaster Emergency Shelter Program

**Citation:** HSC § 34079

---

## Text

Individuals who are eligible to participate in a deposit guarantee or grant contract shall be limited to persons who have been rendered homeless as a result of the natural disaster. First priority for assistance shall be families with minor children.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 19, Sec. 20. Effective March 13, 1990.)
