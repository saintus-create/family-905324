# HSC § 18905.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18905.5

---

## Text

“Adopting agency” means a state agency responsible for the adoption of building standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 897, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
