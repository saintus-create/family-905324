# HSC § 13182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 3. Certificates of Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 13182

---

## Text

Every certificate of registration holder shall notify the State Fire Marshal at his Sacramento office in writing within 15 days of any change of his address.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 802.)
