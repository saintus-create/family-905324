# HSC § 101095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Local Health Officers and Local Health Departments > Article: 2. Local Health Emergencies

**Citation:** HSC § 101095

---

## Text

Any person failing or refusing to furnish technical, toxicological, or other information required pursuant to Section 101085, or falsifying any information provided pursuant to Section 101085 is guilty of a misdemeanor and is also subject to any other criminal or civil penalties provided by statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 301.9. Effective September 29, 1996.)
