# HSC § 25919.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10.4. Asbestos Notification

**Citation:** HSC § 25919.4

---

## Text

“Employee’s representative,” as used in this chapter, means an employee’s union representative, a member of the employee’s immediate family, a nonrelated member of the employee’s household, and an employee’s attorney or a person with power of attorney.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 25922 (as added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1502) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 216, Sec. 71.)
