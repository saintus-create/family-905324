# HSC § 33022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Redevelopment

**Citation:** HSC § 33022

---

## Text

Redevelopment does not exclude the continuance of existing buildings or uses whose demolition and rebuilding or change of use are not deemed essential to the redevelopment and rehabilitation of the area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
