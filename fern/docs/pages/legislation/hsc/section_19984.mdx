# HSC § 19984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 3. Regulation

**Citation:** HSC § 19984

---

## Text

All building standards shall be adopted and published in the State Building Standards Code pursuant to Part 2.5 (commencing with Section 18901) of this division, and all other rules and regulations promulgated by the commission under the authority of this part shall be adopted pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.
