# HSC § 33844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 9. Special Assessments > Article: 4. Collection of Assessments

**Citation:** HSC § 33844

---

## Text

All the proceeds of the assessment shall be placed in a separate fund of the legislative body and shall be transferred to the agency only for the payment of indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 29.)
