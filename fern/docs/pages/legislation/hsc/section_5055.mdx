# HSC § 5055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 7. Rates and Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 5055

---

## Text

Until the principal and interest of the bonds are fully paid any holder of any bond outstanding at any time may compel the use of any or all of the remedies provided in this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
