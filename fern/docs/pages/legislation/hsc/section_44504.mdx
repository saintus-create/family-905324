# HSC § 44504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 44504

---

## Text

“Authority” means the Capital Programs and Climate Financing Authority established pursuant to Section 44515 and any board, commission, department, or officer succeeding to the functions thereof or to whom the powers conferred upon the authority by this division shall be given by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 710, Sec. 9. (AB 786) Effective January 1, 2026.)
