# HSC § 38510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25.5. CALIFORNIA GLOBAL WARMING SOLUTIONS ACT OF 2006 > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Role of State Board

**Citation:** HSC § 38510

---

## Text

The State Air Resources Board is the state agency charged with monitoring and regulating sources of emissions of greenhouse gases that cause global warming in order to reduce emissions of greenhouse gases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 488, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2007.)
