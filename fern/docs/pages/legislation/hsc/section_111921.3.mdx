# HSC § 111921.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 9. Industrial Hemp > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 111921.3

---

## Text

The department may adopt regulations imposing an age requirement for the sale of certain industrial hemp products upon a finding of a threat to public health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 576, Sec. 10. (AB 45) Effective October 6, 2021.)
