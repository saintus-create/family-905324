# HSC § 1358.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.5. Additional Requirements for Medicare Supplement Contracts

**Citation:** HSC § 1358.19

---

## Text

An issuer shall provide a copy of any Medicare supplement advertisement intended for use in this state whether through written, radio, or television medium to the director for review or approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 706, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
