# HSC § 44308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 6. AIR TOXICS “HOT SPOTS” INFORMATION AND ASSESSMENT > Chapter: 1. Legislative Findings and Definitions

---

## Text

“Plan” means the emissions inventory plan which meets the conditions specified in Section 44342.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1252, Sec. 1. Section operative July 1, 1988, pursuant to Section 44384.)
