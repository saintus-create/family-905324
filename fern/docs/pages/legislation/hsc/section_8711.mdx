# HSC § 8711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 1. Care of Old Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8711

---

## Text

The cemetery authority or other person directed by the court shall accept the newly created plots and shall sell and convey them only for interment purposes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 79.)
