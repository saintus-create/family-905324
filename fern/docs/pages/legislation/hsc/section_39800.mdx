# HSC § 39800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 5. Air Pollution Control Subvention Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39800

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “dollars budgeted” means moneys derived from revenue sources within a district for use in its air pollution control programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
