# HSC § 20046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 2.5. Election on Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 20046

---

## Text

If a majority of votes cast are in favor of forming the district, the board of supervisors shall by an order entered on its minutes declare the district formed under the name designated for it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 896.)
