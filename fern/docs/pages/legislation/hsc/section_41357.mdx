# HSC § 41357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 14. Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 41357

---

## Text

The Antelope Valley district may contract for any professional assistance that may be necessary or convenient for the exercise of its powers and duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 163, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
