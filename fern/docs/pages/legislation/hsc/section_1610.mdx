# HSC § 1610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Human Whole Blood, Human Whole Blood Derivatives, and Other Biologics > Article: 3. Biologics Other Than Human Whole Blood and Human Whole Blood Derivatives

**Citation:** HSC § 1610

---

## Text

The department shall make rules and regulations governing the production of all biologics produced in establishments under subdivision (c) of Section 1609.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1055.)
