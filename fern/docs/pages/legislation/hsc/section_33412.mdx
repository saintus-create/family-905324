# HSC § 33412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 9. Relocation of Persons Displaced by Projects

**Citation:** HSC § 33412

---

## Text

Permanent housing facilities shall be made available within three years from the time occupants are displaced and that pending the development of such facilities there will be available to such displaced occupants adequate temporary housing facilities at rents comparable to those in the community at the time of their displacement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
