# HSC § 127400.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 2.5. Fair Pricing Policies > Article: 1. Hospital Fair Pricing Policies

**Citation:** HSC § 127400.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Charity care” means free care.

(b) “Discounted payment” or “discount payment” means any charge for care that is reduced but not free.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 511, Sec. 2. (AB 2297) Effective January 1, 2025.)
