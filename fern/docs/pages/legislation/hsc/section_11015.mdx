# HSC § 11015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 11015

---

## Text

“Federal bureau” means the Drug Enforcement Administration of the United States Department of Justice, or its successor agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 978, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
