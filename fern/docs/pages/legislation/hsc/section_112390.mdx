# HSC § 112390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 6. Cold Storage > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 112390

## Text

On receipt of the application the board shall examine into the sanitary condition of the plant.
