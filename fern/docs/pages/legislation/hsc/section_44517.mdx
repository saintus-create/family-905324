# HSC § 44517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. CALIFORNIA POLLUTION CONTROL FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. California Pollution Control Financing Authority > Article: 2. Organization of Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 44517

---

## Text

The authority shall have and is hereby vested with all powers reasonably necessary to carry out the powers and responsibilities expressly granted or imposed upon it under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 839.)
