# HSC § 4768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 3. County Sanitation Districts > Article: 4.1. District Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 4768

## Text

Section 19990 of the Government Code shall apply to employees of the district.
