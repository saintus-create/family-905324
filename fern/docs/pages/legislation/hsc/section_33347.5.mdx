# HSC § 33347.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 4. Preparation and Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33347.5

---

## Text

If there exists within the project area a project area committee, the redevelopment plan shall be submitted to such committee before it is submitted to the legislative body. The committee may, if it chooses, prepare a report and recommendation for submission to the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 614.)
