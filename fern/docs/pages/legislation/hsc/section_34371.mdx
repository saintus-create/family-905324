# HSC § 34371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 34371

---

## Text

Insofar as the provisions of Sections 34369 and 34370 are inconsistent with the provisions of any other law, the provisions of the sections are controlling.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 93.)
