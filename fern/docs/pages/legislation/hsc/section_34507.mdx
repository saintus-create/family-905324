# HSC § 34507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Housing Cooperation Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34507

---

## Text

“Governing body” means the council, board of supervisors, board of trustees, or other body having charge of the fiscal affairs of the state public body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
