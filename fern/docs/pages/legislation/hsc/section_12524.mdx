# HSC § 12524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12524

---

## Text

“Public display of fireworks” means an entertainment feature where the public or a private group is admitted or permitted to view the display or discharge of dangerous fireworks.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
