# HSC § 1600.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Human Whole Blood, Human Whole Blood Derivatives, and Other Biologics > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1600.25

---

## Text

“Mobile unit” means a transportable auxiliary to a blood bank designed, equipped, and staffed to procure human whole blood and to transport this blood to the bank for processing, storing, and distribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 800, Sec. 4.)
