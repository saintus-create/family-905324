# HSC § 1341.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** HSC § 1341.10

---

## Text

The department may use the unexpended balance of funds available for use in connection with the performance of the functions of the Department of Corporations to which the department succeeds pursuant to Section 1341.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 525, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, or sooner, by Sec. 214 of Ch. 525.)
