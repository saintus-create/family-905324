# HSC § 53180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 10. HOUSING AND HOMELESS BOND ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 53180

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Housing and Homeless Bond Act of 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 48, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 107 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
