# HSC § 7150.70

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.5. Uniform Anatomical Gift Act

**Citation:** HSC § 7150.70

---

## Text

Each hospital in this state shall enter into agreements or affiliations with procurement organizations for coordination of procurement and use of anatomical gifts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 629, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
