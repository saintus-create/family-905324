# HSC § 109290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 4. DRUGS, DEVICES, AND COSMETICS > Chapter: 4. Treatment of Cancer and Other Serious Diseases > Article: 1. Intent and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 109290

---

## Text

No person may undertake to treat or alleviate cancer by use of drugs, surgery, or radiation unless the person holds a license issued under a law of this state expressly authorizing the diagnosis and treatment of disease by use of drugs, surgery, or radiation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
