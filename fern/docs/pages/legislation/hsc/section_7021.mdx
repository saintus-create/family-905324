# HSC § 7021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7021

---

## Text

“Directors” or “governing body” means the board of directors, board of trustees, or other policymaking body of a cemetery association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 436, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2002.)
