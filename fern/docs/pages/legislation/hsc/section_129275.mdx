# HSC § 129275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 6. FACILITIES LOAN INSURANCE AND FINANCING > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Construction Loan Insurance > Article: 7. Small Facility Loan Guarantee for Developmental Disability Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 129275

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Small Facility Loan Guarantee for Programs Serving People with Developmental Disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 730, Sec. 2. (AB 1629) Effective October 19, 2010.)
