# HSC § 111923.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 9. Industrial Hemp > Article: 4. Registration and Fees

**Citation:** HSC § 111923.7

---

## Text

A hemp manufacturer located outside the state shall reimburse the department for travel and per diem required to perform necessary onsite inspections at the facility to ensure compliance with this chapter and related activities pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 576, Sec. 10. (AB 45) Effective October 6, 2021.)
