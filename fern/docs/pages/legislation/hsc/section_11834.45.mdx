# HSC § 11834.45

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.5. Licensing > Article: 4. Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 11834.45

---

## Text

The civil and administrative remedies available to the department pursuant to this chapter are not exclusive, and may be sought and employed in any combination deemed advisable by the department to enforce this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11834.33 by Stats. 1993, Ch. 741, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1994.)
