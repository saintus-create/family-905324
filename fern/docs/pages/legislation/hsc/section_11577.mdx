# HSC § 11577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 11577

---

## Text

In case of failure to prosecute the action with reasonable diligence, or at the request of the plaintiff, the court, in its discretion, may substitute any other citizen consenting thereto for the plaintiff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
