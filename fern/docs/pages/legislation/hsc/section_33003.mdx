# HSC § 33003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. General Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 33003

---

## Text

“Agency” means a redevelopment agency created by this part or its predecessor, or a legislative body which has elected to exercise the powers granted to an agency by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
