# HSC § 32123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 23. HOSPITAL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** HSC § 32123

---

## Text

The board of directors may purchase such real property, and erect or rent and equip such buildings or building, room or rooms as may be necessary for the hospital.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 932.)
