# HSC § 114810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 5. Containment of Radioactive Materials > Article: 2. Radiation Monitoring Devices for Nuclear Power Plants

**Citation:** HSC § 114810

---

## Text

Failure to comply with this article shall not constitute the basis for an action in a court of law or in an administrative proceeding to enjoin or prevent the operation or start-up of a nuclear facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
