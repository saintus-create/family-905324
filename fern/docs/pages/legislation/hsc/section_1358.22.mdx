# HSC § 1358.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 3.5. Additional Requirements for Medicare Supplement Contracts

**Citation:** HSC § 1358.22

---

## Text

(a) On or before March 1 of each year, an issuer shall report the following information for every individual resident of this state for which the issuer has in force more than one Medicare supplement contract:

(1) Contract number.

(2) Date of issuance.

(b) The items set forth above shall be grouped by enrollee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 706, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
