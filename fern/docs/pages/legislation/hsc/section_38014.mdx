# HSC § 38014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. HEALTH AND WELFARE AGENCY—DIRECT SERVICE CONTRACTS REFORM ACT > Chapter: 2. Contract Authority

**Citation:** HSC § 38014

---

## Text

The Controller shall approve payments based on the provisions of approved contracts.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 990.)
