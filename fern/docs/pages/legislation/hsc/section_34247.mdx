# HSC § 34247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 2. Creation of Housing Authorities

**Citation:** HSC § 34247

---

## Text

The area of operation of an authority created pursuant to Section 34246 shall be the combined possible areas of operation of participating cities and counties, as determined by Sections 34208 and 34209.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 917.)
