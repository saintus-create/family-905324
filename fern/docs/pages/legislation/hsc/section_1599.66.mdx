# HSC § 1599.66

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.93. Admission Contracts for Long-Term Health Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1599.66

---

## Text

Every contract of admission shall clearly and explicitly state whether the facility participates in the Medi-Cal program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 625, Sec. 1.)
