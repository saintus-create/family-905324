# HSC § 11836.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 9. Services to Persons Convicted for Driving While Under the Influence of Alcohol and Other Drugs

**Citation:** HSC § 11836.10

---

## Text

No person, firm, partnership, association, corporation, or local governmental entity shall operate, establish, manage, conduct, or maintain a driving-under-the-influence program in this state without a current and valid license issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1244, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
