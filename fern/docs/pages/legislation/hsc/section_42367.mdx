# HSC § 42367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 2.5. Product Variances

**Citation:** HSC § 42367

---

## Text

No product variance shall be granted pursuant to this article from a requirement for a permit to build, erect, alter, or replace any article, machine, equipment, or other contrivance pursuant to Section 42300.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 443, Sec. 2. Effective September 7, 1994.)
