# HSC § 6699

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 7. Finances and Taxation > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6699

---

## Text

If any portion of the interest or principal due for any year remains unpaid, it shall be added to the levy for the next year, and shall be collected and paid accordingly.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
