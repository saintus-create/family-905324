# HSC § 33459.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 12.5. Hazardous Substance Release Cleanup

**Citation:** HSC § 33459.5

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 33459.3, nothing in this article shall limit the powers of the State Water Resources Control Board or a California regional water quality control board to enforce Division 7 (commencing with Section 13000) of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1113, Sec. 2.)
