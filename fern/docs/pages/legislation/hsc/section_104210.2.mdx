# HSC § 104210.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 103. DISEASE PREVENTION AND HEALTH PROMOTION > Part: 1. CHRONIC DISEASE > Chapter: 2. Cancer > Article: 4. California Firefighter Cancer Prevention and Research Program

**Citation:** HSC § 104210.2

## Text

Implementation of this article shall be subject to a resolution adopted by the Regents of the University of California agreeing to undertake the duties outlined in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 268, Sec. 2. (AB 700) Effective January 1, 2024.)
