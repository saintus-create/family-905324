# HSC § 34277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 34277

---

## Text

The mayor of the city or the governing body of the county shall designate the first chairman from among the commissioners. The authority shall select his successor from among its commissioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
