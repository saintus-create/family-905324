# HSC § 1796.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Home Care Services > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 1796.21

---

## Text

A registered home care aide shall be 18 years of age or older.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 790, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2014. Provisions implemented as of January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 1796.61.)
