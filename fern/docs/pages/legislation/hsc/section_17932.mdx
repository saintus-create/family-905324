# HSC § 17932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 1.5. REGULATION OF BUILDINGS USED FOR HUMAN HABITATION > Chapter: 3. Appeals

**Citation:** HSC § 17932

---

## Text

A decision of the director or the director’s designee made pursuant to Section 17930 is final, except for such action as may be taken by a court as permitted or required by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1020, Sec. 3.)
