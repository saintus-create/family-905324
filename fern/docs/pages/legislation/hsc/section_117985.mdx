# HSC § 117985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 5. Large Quantity Generator Requirements

**Citation:** HSC § 117985

---

## Text

Medical waste shall be treated using treatment technologies approved in accordance with Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 118215).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 564, Sec. 54. (AB 333) Effective January 1, 2015.)
