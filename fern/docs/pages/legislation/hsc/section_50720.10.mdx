# HSC § 50720.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 8.6. Foreclosure Intervention Housing Preservation Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50720.10

---

## Text

A loan made pursuant to the program may be partially or fully converted to a grant.

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 111, Sec. 23. (AB 140) Effective July 19, 2021.)
