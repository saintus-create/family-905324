# HSC § 33402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 8. Property Management

**Citation:** HSC § 33402

---

## Text

Except as provided in Article 9 (commencing with Section 33410), this part does not authorize an agency to own or operate rental property acquired and rehabilitated in prospect of resale beyond a reasonable period necessary to effect such resale.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 955.)
