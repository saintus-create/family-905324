# HSC § 41340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 14. Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41340

---

## Text

The form of the warrants shall be prescribed by the Antelope Valley district board and approved by the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 163, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
