# HSC § 33370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 5. Procedure for Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Legislative Body

**Citation:** HSC § 33370

---

## Text

The legislative body at the time of, or in connection with, the adoption of the plan, shall declare its intention to undertake and complete any proceedings necessary to be carried out by the community under the provisions of the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
