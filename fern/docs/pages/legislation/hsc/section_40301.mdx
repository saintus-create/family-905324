# HSC § 40301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 1. Creation of Regional Districts

**Citation:** HSC § 40301

---

## Text

Prior to the public hearing, the board of supervisors shall give, not less than 15 days nor more than 45 days before the hearing, notice of the time and place of the hearing by publication pursuant to Section 6061 of the Government Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1063.)
