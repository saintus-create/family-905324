# HSC § 8341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 2. Operation and Management > Article: 5. Operation of Crematories

**Citation:** HSC § 8341

---

## Text

All cremated remains not disposed of in accordance with this chapter, within one year, shall be interred.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1232, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1994.)
