# HSC § 125702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 8. ADULT HEALTH > Chapter: 1. California Osteoporosis Prevention and Education Act

**Citation:** HSC § 125702

---

## Text

There is hereby created within the department the California Osteoporosis Prevention and Education Program. The target population for this program shall be persons of age 50 years or older.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 819, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
