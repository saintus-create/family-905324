# HSC § 18214.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.1. MOBILEHOME PARKS ACT > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18214.2

---

## Text

“Multi-unit manufactured housing,” for the purposes of this part, has the same meaning as in Section 18008.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 356, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
