# HSC § 41066

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 6. Air Quality Improvement Strategy

**Citation:** HSC § 41066

---

## Text

The strategy shall be consistent with any nonattainment area plan required by state or federal law, or any requirement imposed on a local agency with respect to the preparation or administration of a plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 3.)
