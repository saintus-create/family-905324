# HSC § 6464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 3. Election on Formation and for Officers

**Citation:** HSC § 6464

---

## Text

At the election there shall be elected an assessor and the members of the board who shall be resident electors of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 155.)
