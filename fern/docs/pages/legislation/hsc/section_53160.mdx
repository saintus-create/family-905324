# HSC § 53160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 9. HOUSING AND HOMELESS BOND ACT OF 1988 > Chapter: 2. Home Building and Rehabilitation Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 53160

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this part shall be deposited in the Roberti Affordable Housing Fund, which is hereby created. Moneys in the fund shall be allocated and utilized in accordance with Part 8 (commencing with Section 53130).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 577, Sec. 19. Effective September 5, 1990. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 48, and approved in Prop. 84 on Nov. 8, 1988.)
