# HSC § 13954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 10. Service Zones

**Citation:** HSC § 13954

---

## Text

As determined by the district board and pursuant to the requirements of this part, a service zone may exercise any fiscal powers within its boundaries that the district may exercise.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
