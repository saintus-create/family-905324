# HSC § 117845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 117845

---

## Text

The department shall implement this part so as to maximize the funds that may be received from the federal government.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
