# HSC § 13188.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 4. Denial, Revocation, and Suspension

**Citation:** HSC § 13188.2

---

## Text

Any applicant who has been denied a license or certificate of registration or who has had a license or certificate of registration suspended, shall be entitled to a hearing in accordance with the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 909, Sec. 3.)
