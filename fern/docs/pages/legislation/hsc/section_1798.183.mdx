# HSC § 1798.183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. Facilities > Article: 4. Poison Control Centers

**Citation:** HSC § 1798.183

---

## Text

The authority may authorize a poison control center to provide poison control services for fewer than 24 hours a day, as the authority deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 236, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
