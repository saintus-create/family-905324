# HSC § 120390.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 2. IMMUNIZATIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Immunization of College-Age Students

**Citation:** HSC § 120390.7

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the University of California except to the extent that the Regents of the University of California, by appropriate resolution, make that provision applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 146, Sec. 5.5. Effective July 22, 1999.)
