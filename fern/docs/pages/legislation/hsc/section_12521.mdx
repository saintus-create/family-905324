# HSC § 12521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 12521

---

## Text

“Package” includes any case, container, or receptacle, used for holding fireworks, which is closed or sealed by tape, cordage, or by any other means.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
