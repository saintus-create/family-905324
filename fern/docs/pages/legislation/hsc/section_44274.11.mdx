# HSC § 44274.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 8.9. California Alternative and Renewable Fuel, Vehicle Technology, Clean Air, and Carbon Reduction Act of 2007 > Article: 4. Medium- and Heavy-Duty Zero-Emission Vehicle Fleet Purchasing Assistance Program

**Citation:** HSC § 44274.11

---

## Text

The Medium- and Heavy-Duty Zero-Emission Vehicle Fleet Purchasing Assistance Program is hereby established within the state board’s Air Quality Improvement Program established pursuant to Section 44274 to make financing tools and nonfinancial supports available to the operators of medium- and heavy-duty vehicle fleets to enable those operators to transition their fleets to zero-emission vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 574, Sec. 12. (AB 211) Effective September 27, 2022.)
