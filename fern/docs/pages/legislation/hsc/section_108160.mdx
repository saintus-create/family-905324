# HSC § 108160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 4. California Hazardous Substances Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 108160

---

## Text

If the department finds that available data on human experience with any substance indicate results different from those obtained on animals with the dosages or concentrations stated in Section 108155, the human data shall take precedence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
