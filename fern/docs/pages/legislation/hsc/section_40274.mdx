# HSC § 40274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40274

---

## Text

The treasurers of the counties included, in whole or in part, within the bay district shall pay into the bay district treasury all funds held by them to the credit of the bay district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1063.)
