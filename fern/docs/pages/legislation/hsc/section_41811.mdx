# HSC § 41811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Emission Limitations > Article: 2. Nonagricultural Burning

**Citation:** HSC § 41811

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not supersede any rule or regulation of any district, which rule or regulation was in effect for five or more years prior to September 19, 1970.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
