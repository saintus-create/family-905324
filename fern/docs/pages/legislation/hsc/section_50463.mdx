# HSC § 50463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 2. Policy Activities of the Department

**Citation:** HSC § 50463

---

## Text

The department may certify local housing agents, and may periodically review, recertify, and decertify such local housing agents as provided in Section 51252.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 610.)
