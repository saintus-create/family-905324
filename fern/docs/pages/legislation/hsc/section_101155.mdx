# HSC § 101155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 3. LOCAL HEALTH DEPARTMENTS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties of Local Health Officers and Local Health Departments > Article: 5. Municipal and County Laboratories

**Citation:** HSC § 101155

---

## Text

The cost of establishment and maintenance of the public health laboratory is a legal expenditure from any city or county funds that are for disbursement under the direction of the city or county health officer to protect public health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 302.1. Effective September 29, 1996.)
