# HSC § 33326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 3. Selection of Project Area and Formulation of Preliminary Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 33326

---

## Text

Prior to publication of notice of the agency public hearing, the planning commission may change the boundaries of a project area with the approval of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1665.)
