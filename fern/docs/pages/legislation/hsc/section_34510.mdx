# HSC § 34510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Housing Cooperation Law

**Citation:** HSC § 34510

---

## Text

A state public body may dedicate, sell, convey, or lease any of its property to a housing authority or the Federal Government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
