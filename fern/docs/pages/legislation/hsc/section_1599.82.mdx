# HSC § 1599.82

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.93. Admission Contracts for Long-Term Health Care Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 1599.82

---

## Text

No contract of admission shall include a clause that purports to alter the statutory period for filing an action against a facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 625, Sec. 1.)
