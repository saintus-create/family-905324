# HSC § 114259.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 8. Physical Facilities > Article: 6. Vermin and Animals

**Citation:** HSC § 114259.1

---

## Text

The premises of each food facility shall be kept free of vermin.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 96, Sec. 44. Effective July 20, 2007.)
