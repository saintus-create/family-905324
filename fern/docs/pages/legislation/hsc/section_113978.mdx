# HSC § 113978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 3. Management and Personnel > Article: 6. Hygienic Practices

**Citation:** HSC § 113978

---

## Text

Food facilities shall have a “no smoking” sign posted in the food preparation, food storage, and warewashing areas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007. Operative July 1, 2007, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 23.)
