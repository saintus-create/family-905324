# HSC § 11819.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 4. State-County Partnership > Article: 4. County Alcohol and Other Drug Program

**Citation:** HSC § 11819.1

---

## Text

The Legislature recognizes the need for increased potential for coordination between county alcohol programs and local health system agencies established pursuant to Public Law 93-641. Therefore, the Legislature encourages a harmonious working relationship between local health system agencies and county alcohol programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1328, Sec. 55.5.)
