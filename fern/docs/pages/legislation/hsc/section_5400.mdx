# HSC § 5400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 6. General Provisions with Respect to Sewers > Article: 1. Rights of Way for Sewers and Drainage

**Citation:** HSC § 5400

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of a county may vacate or abandon easements for sewage or drainage purposes whenever it determines that they are no longer required for public use.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
