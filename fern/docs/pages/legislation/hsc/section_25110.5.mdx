# HSC § 25110.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25110.5

---

## Text

“Business” means the conduct of activity and is not limited to a commercial or proprietary activity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1039.)
