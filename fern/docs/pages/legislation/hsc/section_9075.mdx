# HSC § 9075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 4. PUBLIC CEMETERY DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Finances

**Citation:** HSC § 9075

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against a district are governed by Part 3 (commencing with Section 900) and Part 4 (commencing with Section 940) of Division 3.6 of Title 1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 57, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
