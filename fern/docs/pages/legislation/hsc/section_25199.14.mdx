# HSC § 25199.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8.7. Procedures for the Approval of New Facilities

**Citation:** HSC § 25199.14

---

## Text

The final decision of the appeal board concerning an appeal authorized pursuant to Section 25199.9 shall be deemed to be the final administrative action of the appeal board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1504, Sec. 8.)
