# HSC § 130410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 111. GOLDEN BEAR STATE PHARMACY ASSISTANCE PROGRAM

**Citation:** HSC § 130410

---

## Text

A contract executed for the purposes of this division is exempt from Part 2 (commencing with Section 10100) of Division 2 of the Public Contract Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 542, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2003.)
