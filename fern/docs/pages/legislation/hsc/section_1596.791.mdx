# HSC § 1596.791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.791

---

## Text

“Provider” means a person who operates a child day care facility and is licensed pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 1596.90) or 3.6 (commencing with Section 1597.30).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1615, Sec. 9.)
