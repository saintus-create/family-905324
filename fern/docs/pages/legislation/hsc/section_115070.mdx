# HSC § 115070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 9. RADIATION > Chapter: 8. Radiation Control Law > Article: 4. Licensing and Regulation of Sources of Ionizing Radiation

**Citation:** HSC § 115070

---

## Text

The frequency of inspections of radioactive materials shall be based on priorities established by the United States Nuclear Regulatory Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
