# HSC § 4922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 7. Dissolution

**Citation:** HSC § 4922

---

## Text

If all of the district is included in one city, the fund shall be paid to the treasurer of the city and administered by the governing body of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
