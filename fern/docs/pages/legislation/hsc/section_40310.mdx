# HSC § 40310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 2. City Selection Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 40310

---

## Text

The city selection committee organized in each county within a regional district pursuant to Article 11 (commencing with Section 50270), Chapter 1, Part 1, Division 1, Title 5 of the Government Code shall make the appointments to the regional district board as prescribed in Section 40322.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
