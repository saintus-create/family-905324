# HSC § 128337

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5. Health Professions Education Programs > Article: 1. Health Professions Education Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 128337

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, on or before October 1, 2021, the nonprofit public benefit corporation known as the Health Professions Education Foundation shall be dissolved. All assets, including records, and any liabilities shall be transferred to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 163. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
