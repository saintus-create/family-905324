# HSC § 1596.615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.35. Child Care Provider Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.615

---

## Text

All moneys collected by the department to implement this chapter shall, notwithstanding Section 13340 of the Government Code, be continuously appropriated to the department without regard to fiscal year for expenditure pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 843, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1998. Section operative July 1, 1998, pursuant to Section 1596.68.)
