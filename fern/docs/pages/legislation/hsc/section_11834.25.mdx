# HSC § 11834.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.5. Licensing > Article: 2. Local Regulation

**Citation:** HSC § 11834.25

---

## Text

For the purposes of any contract, deed, or covenant for the transfer of real property executed on or after January 1, 1979, an alcohol or other drug recovery or treatment facility which serves six or fewer persons shall be considered a residential use of property and a use of property by a single family, notwithstanding any disclaimers to the contrary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 55. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
