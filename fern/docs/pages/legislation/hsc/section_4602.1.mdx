# HSC § 4602.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 1. Community Facilities Law of 1911 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 4602.1

---

## Text

“District” means any district which is formed pursuant to this chapter or pursuant to any law which it supersedes.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 4600 by Stats. 1963, Ch. 756.)
