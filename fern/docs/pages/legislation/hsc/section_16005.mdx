# HSC § 16005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. BUILDINGS USED BY THE PUBLIC > Chapter: 2. Essential Services Buildings > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 16005

---

## Text

“Construction or alteration” includes any construction of, addition to, reconstruction of, or alteration to any essential services building.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
