# HSC § 11165.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11165.6

---

## Text

A prescriber shall be allowed to access the CURES database for a list of patients for whom that prescriber is listed as a prescriber in the CURES database.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 274, Sec. 1. (AB 2086) Effective January 1, 2019.)
