# HSC § 9580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 5. MAUSOLEUMS AND COLUMBARIUMS > Chapter: 3. Permits and Plans > Article: 4. Expiration of Permit

**Citation:** HSC § 9580

---

## Text

Every permit or approval under which no work is done within one year from the date of issuance expires by limitation and a new permit shall be obtained before the work may proceed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 350, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
