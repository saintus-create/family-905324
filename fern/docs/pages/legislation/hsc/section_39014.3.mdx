# HSC § 39014.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39014.3

---

## Text

“Antelope Valley district” means the Antelope Valley Air Quality Management District created pursuant to Chapter 14 (commencing with Section 41300) of Part 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 163, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
