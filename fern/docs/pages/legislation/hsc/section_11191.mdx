# HSC § 11191

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 2. Prescriber’s Record

**Citation:** HSC § 11191

---

## Text

The record shall be preserved for three years.

Every person who violates any provision of this section is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 896.)
