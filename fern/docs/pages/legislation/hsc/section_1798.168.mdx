# HSC § 1798.168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 6. Facilities > Article: 2.5. Regional Trauma Systems

**Citation:** HSC § 1798.168

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as changing the boundaries of any local emergency medical services agency in existence on January 1, 1984.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1067, Sec. 2.)
