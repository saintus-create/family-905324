# HSC § 118321.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 14. MEDICAL WASTE > Chapter: 9.5. Trauma Scene Waste Management

**Citation:** HSC § 118321.4

---

## Text

As specified in Section 117705, a trauma scene waste management practitioner who transports trauma scene waste shall be deemed the generator of the trauma scene waste for purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 732, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1998.)
