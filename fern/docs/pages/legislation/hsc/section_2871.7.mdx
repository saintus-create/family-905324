# HSC § 2871.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PEST ABATEMENT > Chapter: 8. Pest Abatement Districts > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 2871.7

---

## Text

After a public hearing, the district board shall determine the rate of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 911, Sec. 69. Effective September 7, 1984.)
