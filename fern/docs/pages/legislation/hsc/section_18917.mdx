# HSC § 18917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18917

---

## Text

“Occupancy” means the purpose for which a building, structure, or other improvement to property, or a part thereof, is used or intended to be used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1152.)
