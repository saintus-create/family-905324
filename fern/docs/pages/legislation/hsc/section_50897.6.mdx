# HSC § 50897.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 2. DEPARTMENT OF HOUSING AND COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 17. State Rental Assistance Program

**Citation:** HSC § 50897.6

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the state closely monitor the usage of funding pursuant to this chapter to ensure that the program is stabilizing households and preventing evictions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 2, Sec. 24. (SB 91) Effective January 29, 2021.)
