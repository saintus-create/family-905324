# HSC § 33345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 4. Preparation and Adoption of Redevelopment Plans by the Agency

**Citation:** HSC § 33345

---

## Text

With respect to each redevelopment project, each agency shall, within a reasonable time before its approval of the redevelopment plan adopt and make available for public inspection rules to implement the operation of owner participation in connection with the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1665.)
