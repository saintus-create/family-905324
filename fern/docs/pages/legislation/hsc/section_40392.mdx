# HSC § 40392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Regional Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 6. Withdrawal of County From Regional District

**Citation:** HSC § 40392

---

## Text

A board of supervisors shall give at least two months’ notice to the regional district board of its intention to withdraw the county, or portion thereof, from the regional district. A county, or portion thereof, shall not be withdrawn from a regional district during any fiscal year after the expiration of the first four months of the fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
