# HSC § 110465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 2. Registration

**Citation:** HSC § 110465

---

## Text

A separate registration is required for each place of manufacture, packing, or holding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
