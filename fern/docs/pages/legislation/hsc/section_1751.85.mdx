# HSC § 1751.85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 8.5. California Hospice Licensure Act of 1990 > Article: 2.3. New License Moratorium

**Citation:** HSC § 1751.85

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 494, Sec. 1. (SB 664) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 2.3, commencing with Section 1751.70.)
