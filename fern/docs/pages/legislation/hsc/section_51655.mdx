# HSC § 51655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 4. BOND AND LOAN INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Loan Insurance

**Citation:** HSC § 51655

---

## Text

Subject to this part, the agency may insure or guarantee loans made by qualified mortgage lenders for the purposes described in Section 51320.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 115, Sec. 4. Effective July 15, 1993.)
