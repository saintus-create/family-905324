# HSC § 20061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 20061

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, all district elections shall be called, held, and conducted in all respects as nearly as practicable in conformity with the Uniform District Election Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 29.)
