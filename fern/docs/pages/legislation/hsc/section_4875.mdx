# HSC § 4875

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 4875

---

## Text

The board shall also direct the clerk to post the notice in three public places in the proposed district at least 10 days prior to the date set for the hearing. The heading of each posted notice shall be in letters of not less than one inch in height.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
