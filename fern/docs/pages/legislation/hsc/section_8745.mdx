# HSC § 8745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 5. Endowment and Special Care > Article: 2. Care of Active Cemeteries

**Citation:** HSC § 8745

---

## Text

All the information appearing on the signs and report filed in the cemetery office shall be revised annually and verified by the president and secretary, or two officers authorized by the cemetery authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 339.)
