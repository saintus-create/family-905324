# HSC § 25515.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.95. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory > Article: 1. Business and Area Plans

**Citation:** HSC § 25515.4

---

## Text

A person who willfully prevents, interferes with, or attempts to impede the enforcement of this article by any authorized representative of a unified program agency is, upon conviction, guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 419, Sec. 3. (SB 483) Effective January 1, 2014.)
