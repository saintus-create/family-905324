# HSC § 1439.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.45. Lesbian, Gay, Bisexual, and Transgender Long-Term Care Facility Residents’ Bill of Rights

**Citation:** HSC § 1439.52

---

## Text

A facility shall employ procedures for recordkeeping, including, but not limited to, records generated at the time of admission, that include the gender identity, correct name, as indicated by the resident, and pronoun of each resident, as indicated by the resident.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 483, Sec. 3. (SB 219) Effective January 1, 2018.)
