# HSC § 40276

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bay Area Air Quality Management District > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 40276

---

## Text

The bay district board shall, in carrying out the provisions of this article, comply as nearly as possible with the provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 29000), Division 3, Title 3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
