# HSC § 18152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 9. Mobilehome Ombudsman

**Citation:** HSC § 18152

---

## Text

The Governor shall designate a deputy director in the Department of Housing and Community Development for the Mobilehome Assistance Center.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 957, Sec. 9. (SB 1078) Effective January 1, 2019.)
