# HSC § 50199.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.6. Low-Income Housing Credit

**Citation:** HSC § 50199.16

---

## Text

All acts and proceedings taken by the committee prior to the effective date of this chapter to allocate the housing credit ceiling for 1987 under the Governor’s proclamation dated February 27, 1987, are hereby confirmed, validated, and declared legally effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 658, Sec. 3. Effective September 15, 1987. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 50199.18.)
