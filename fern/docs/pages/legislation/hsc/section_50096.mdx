# HSC § 50096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 1. STATE HOUSING POLICY AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 50096

---

## Text

“Rehabilitation” means repairs and improvements to a substandard residential structure necessary to make it meet rehabilitation standards. As used in this section, “substandard residential structure” has the same meaning as the term “substandard building,” as defined in subdivision (f) of Section 17920.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 920.)
