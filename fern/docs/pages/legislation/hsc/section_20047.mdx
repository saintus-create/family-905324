# HSC § 20047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 2.5. Election on Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 20047

---

## Text

No person is entitled to vote on the question of formation of a district, or for the first board of commissioners, unless that person is a voter within the meaning of the Elections Code, and a resident within the boundaries of the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 143. Effective January 1, 1995.)
