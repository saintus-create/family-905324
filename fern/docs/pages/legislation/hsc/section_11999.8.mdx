# HSC § 11999.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.8. SUBSTANCE ABUSE TREATMENT FUNDING

**Citation:** HSC § 11999.8

---

## Text

Surplus Funds

Any funds remaining in the Substance Abuse Treatment Trust Fund at the end of a fiscal year may be utilized to pay for drug treatment programs to be carried out in the subsequent fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 7, 2000, by initiative Proposition 36, Sec. 7. Operative July 1, 2001, pursuant to Sec. 8 of Prop. 36.)
