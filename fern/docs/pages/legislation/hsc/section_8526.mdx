# HSC § 8526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 3. Acquisition, Dedication and Sale > Article: 2. Declaration of Intention

**Citation:** HSC § 8526

---

## Text

The declaration may be filed for record in the office of the recorder of the county in which the property is situated, and from the date of filing the declaration is constructive notice of the use for which the property is intended.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
