# HSC § 40122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. County Air Pollution Control Districts > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 40122

---

## Text

All officers and employees of a county district are entitled to the benefits of the County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 (Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 31450), Part 3, Division 4, Title 3 of the Government Code) to the same extent as employees of the county.

A county district is a district as defined in Section 31468 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
