# HSC § 1797.256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 4. Local Administration > Article: 2. Local Emergency Medical Services Planning

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.256

---

## Text

A local EMS agency may review applications for grants and contracts for federal, state, or private funds concerning emergency medical services or related activities in its EMS area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
