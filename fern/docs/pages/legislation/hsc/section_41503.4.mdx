# HSC § 41503.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 41503.4

---

## Text

All actions of the state board to approve, revise and approve, or disapprove a district’s attainment plan or plan revision shall be taken at a noticed public hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1568, Sec. 18.)
