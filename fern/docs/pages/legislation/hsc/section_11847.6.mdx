# HSC § 11847.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 13. Narcotic and Alcohol and Other Drug Use Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11847.6

---

## Text

The department shall, in the same manner and subject to the same conditions as other state agencies, develop and submit annually to the Department of Finance a program budget.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 862, Sec. 120. Effective January 1, 2005.)
