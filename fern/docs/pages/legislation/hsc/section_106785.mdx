# HSC § 106785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 1. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERSONNEL > Chapter: 4. Professional Certification > Article: 2. Radon Certification

**Citation:** HSC § 106785

---

## Text

The department shall maintain a list of persons that have submitted proof of certification by either the National Environmental Health Association or the National Radon Safety Board Certified Radon Professional Program. This list shall be made available to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 327, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
