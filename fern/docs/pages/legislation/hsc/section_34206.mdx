# HSC § 34206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 2. HOUSING AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. Housing Authorities Law > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 34206

---

## Text

“Mayor” means the mayor of the city or the officer charged with the duties customarily imposed on the executive head of the city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 710.)
