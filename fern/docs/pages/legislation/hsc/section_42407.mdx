# HSC § 42407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 3. Penalties

**Citation:** HSC § 42407

---

## Text

Except as provided in Chapter 3.4 (commencing with Section 39640) of Part 2 and Sections 40720 and 42403.5, this article is not applicable to vehicular sources.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 561, Sec. 2. (SB 124) Effective January 1, 2010.)
