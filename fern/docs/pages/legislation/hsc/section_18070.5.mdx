# HSC § 18070.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 7.5. Manufactured Home Recovery Fund

**Citation:** HSC § 18070.5

---

## Text

When the department has caused payment to be made from the fund to any person, the department shall be subrogated to the rights of that person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 236, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2005.)
