# HSC § 1800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 13. Community Paramedicine or Triage to Alternate Destination > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1800

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Community Paramedicine or Triage to Alternate Destination Act of 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 138, Sec. 4. (AB 1544) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 1857.)
