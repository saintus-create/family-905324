# HSC § 120585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 3. SEXUALLY TRANSMITTED DISEASE > Chapter: 1. Prevention and Control

**Citation:** HSC § 120585

---

## Text

Local health officers may inspect and quarantine any place or person when the procedure is necessary to enforce the regulations of the board or the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1996.)
