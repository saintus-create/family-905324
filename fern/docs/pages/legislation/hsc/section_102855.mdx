# HSC § 102855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 102. VITAL RECORDS AND HEALTH STATISTICS > Part: 1. VITAL RECORDS > Chapter: 6. Death Registration > Article: 3. Responsibility of Coroner

**Citation:** HSC § 102855

---

## Text

The coroner whose duty it is to investigate such deaths shall ascertain as many as possible of the facts required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
