# HSC § 42350.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Enforcement > Article: 2. Variances

**Citation:** HSC § 42350.5

---

## Text

Any form developed by a district board for use in filing an application for a variance shall contain a notice to small businesses of the availability of assistance in filling out the form and developing compliance schedules.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1126, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
