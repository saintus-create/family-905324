# HSC § 46020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NOISE CONTROL ACT > Chapter: 3. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 46020

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, the definitions set forth in this chapter govern the construction of the words used in this division.
