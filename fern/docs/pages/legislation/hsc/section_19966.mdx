# HSC § 19966

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 19966

---

## Text

“Approved” means conforming to the requirements, and obtaining the approval, of the Department of Housing and Community Development of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1422.)
