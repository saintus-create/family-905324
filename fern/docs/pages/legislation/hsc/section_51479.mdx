# HSC § 51479

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 3. CALIFORNIA HOUSING FINANCE AGENCY > Chapter: 10. Multifamily Housing

**Citation:** HSC § 51479

---

## Text

In administering a program for the preservation of multifamily housing hereunder, the agency may segregate funds available for these purposes into separate accounts as necessary to reflect the different types of assistance authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 26, Sec. 5. Effective April 22, 2002. Operative after November 5, 2002 (Prop. 46 was adopted) by Sec. 9 of Ch. 26.)
