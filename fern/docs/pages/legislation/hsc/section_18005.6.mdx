# HSC § 18005.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2. MANUFACTURED HOUSING > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18005.6

---

## Text

“Lease” means an oral or written contract for the use, possession, and occupation of property. “Lease” includes rent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 975, Sec. 3.)
