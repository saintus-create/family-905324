# HSC § 25181.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 25181.5

---

## Text

A registered waste transporter transporting medical waste who is not subject to Section 25097 shall be subject to penalties for violations pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 813, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1994.)
