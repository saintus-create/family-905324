# HSC § 1179.88

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 6.3. CHILDREN’S HOSPITAL BOND ACT OF 2018 > Chapter: 2. The Children’s Hospital Program

**Citation:** HSC § 1179.88

---

## Text

The California State Auditor may conduct periodic audits to ensure that bond proceeds are awarded in a timely fashion and in a manner consistent with the requirements of this part and that awardees of bond proceeds are using funds in compliance with applicable provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 2018, by initiative Proposition 4, Sec. 1. Effective December 19, 2018.)
