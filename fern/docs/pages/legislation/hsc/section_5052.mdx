# HSC § 5052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 7. Rates and Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 5052

---

## Text

If the rate is not paid when due, on the first day of each calendar month thereafter a penalty of ten per cent of the amount of the delinquent rate shall be added.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
