# HSC § 19976

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 6. FACTORY-BUILT HOUSING > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 19976

---

## Text

“Manufacture” is the process of making, fabricating, constructing, forming, or assembling a product from raw, unfinished, or semifinished materials.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1422.)
