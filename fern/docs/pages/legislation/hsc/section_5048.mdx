# HSC § 5048

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 5. Sewer Revenue Bonds > Article: 7. Rates and Collection

**Citation:** HSC § 5048

---

## Text

After the hearing the ordinance, resolution, or order establishing rates, either as originally introduced or as modified and amended, shall be passed and put into effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
