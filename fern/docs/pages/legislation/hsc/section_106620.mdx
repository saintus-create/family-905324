# HSC § 106620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 1. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH PERSONNEL > Chapter: 4. Professional Certification > Article: 1. Registered Environmental Health Specialists

**Citation:** HSC § 106620

---

## Text

Except for the design of onsite septic systems, nothing in this article shall authorize registered environmental health specialists to design any of the fixed works defined in Section 6731 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
