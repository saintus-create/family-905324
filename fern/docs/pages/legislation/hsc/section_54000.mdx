# HSC § 54000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 16. Veterans and Affordable Housing Bond Act of 2018 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 54000

---

## Text

Together with Article 5z (commencing with Section 998.600) of Chapter 6 of Division 4 of the Military and Veterans Code, this part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Veterans and Affordable Housing Bond Act of 2018.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 365, Sec. 3. Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 6, 2018, election; effective November 7, 2018.)
