# HSC § 33797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 8. Redevelopment Construction Loans > Article: 4. Residential Construction

**Citation:** HSC § 33797

---

## Text

This chapter is full authority for the issuance of bonds by an agency for the purpose of financing residential construction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 48.)
