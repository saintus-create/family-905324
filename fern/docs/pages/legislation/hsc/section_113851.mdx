# HSC § 113851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 7. CALIFORNIA RETAIL FOOD CODE > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 113851

---

## Text

(a) “Permit” means the document issued by the enforcement agency that authorizes a person to operate a food facility or cottage food operation.

(b) “Registration” shall have the same meaning as permit for purposes of implementation and enforcement of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 415, Sec. 9. (AB 1616) Effective January 1, 2013.)
