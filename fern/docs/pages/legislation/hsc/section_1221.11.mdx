# HSC § 1221.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Clinics > Article: 2.5. Licensing of Clinics

**Citation:** HSC § 1221.11

---

## Text

Commencing January 1, 2002, a telephone number shall be provided for applicants to verify receipt of their application by the Licensing and Certification Division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 525, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
