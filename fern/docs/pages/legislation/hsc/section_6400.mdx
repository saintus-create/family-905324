# HSC § 6400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 6400

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this part, means a district formed pursuant to this part or pursuant to any law which it supersedes.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
