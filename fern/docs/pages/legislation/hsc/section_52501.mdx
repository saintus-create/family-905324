# HSC § 52501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 6. CALIFORNIA FIRST-TIME HOME BUYERS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 52501

---

## Text

This part shall be administered by the California Housing Finance Agency and all of the provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 50900) which are not inconsistent with the provisions of this part shall apply to the agency and its administration of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 320, Sec. 12. Effective June 29, 1982.)
