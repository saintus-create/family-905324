# HSC § 20109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20109

---

## Text

The auditor of each county in which a district is located shall allocate to the district, its share of property tax revenue pursuant to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 95) of Part 0.5 of Division 1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 343, Sec. 15.3. Effective January 1, 2008.)
