# HSC § 1796.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 13. Home Care Services > Article: 2. Registry and Exemptions

**Citation:** HSC § 1796.15

---

## Text

This chapter shall not prohibit an individual from employing an individual not listed on the home care aide registry to provide home care services. The department shall have responsibility only for the maintenance of the home care aide registry regarding registered home care aides.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 790, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2014. Provisions implemented as of January 1, 2016, pursuant to Section 1796.61.)
