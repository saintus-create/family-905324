# HSC § 39054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39054

---

## Text

“Truck” means a motor truck as defined in Section 410 of the Vehicle Code.
