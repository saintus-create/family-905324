# HSC § 7951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 2. DISINTERMENT AND REMOVAL > Chapter: 4. Removal of All Remains: Cities and Cities and Counties Over 100,000 > Article: 9. New Land, Mausoleum or Columbarium

**Citation:** HSC § 7951

---

## Text

Plots, crypts, or niches may be sold to persons desiring to make reinterments.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
