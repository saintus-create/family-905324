# HSC § 14916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 5. ABATEMENT OF HAZARDOUS WEEDS AND RUBBISH > Chapter: 4. Expense of Abatement > Article: 3. Collection of Expenses

**Citation:** HSC § 14916

---

## Text

The tax collector shall include the amount of the assessment on bills for taxes levied against the respective lots and parcels of land.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
