# HSC § 112894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 9. Olive Oil > Article: 2. Olive Oil Manufacture and Marketing

**Citation:** HSC § 112894

---

## Text

Virgin olive oil not fit for human consumption, sometimes known as lampante virgin olive oil, shall be refined before consumption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 567, Sec. 8. (SB 818) Effective January 1, 2012.)
