# HSC § 115895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 10. RECREATIONAL SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Safe Recreational Water Use > Article: 2. Public Beaches

**Citation:** HSC § 115895

---

## Text

Any private person who violates any regulation adopted by the department pursuant to Section 115880 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 592, Sec. 6. (SB 482) Effective January 1, 2012.)
