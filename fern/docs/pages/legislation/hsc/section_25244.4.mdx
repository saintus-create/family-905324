# HSC § 25244.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 11.8. Hazardous Waste Reduction, Recycling, and Treatment

**Citation:** HSC § 25244.4

---

## Text

Every generator of hazardous waste shall submit a report to the department, at least once every two years, reporting the changes in volume and toxicity of waste achieved through waste reduction during the period for which the report is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1030, Sec. 2.)
