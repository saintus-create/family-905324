# HSC § 4866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 4866

---

## Text

“Sewers” as used in this chapter includes lateral and collecting sewers, septic tanks and all other means of handling, gathering and disposing of sewage in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 765.)
