# HSC § 12575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. EXPLOSIVES > Part: 2. FIREWORKS AND PYROTECHNIC DEVICES > Chapter: 5. Licenses > Article: 1. Types of Licenses

**Citation:** HSC § 12575

---

## Text

A public display (special) license allows the holding and conducting at various times of public displays of dangerous fireworks at a single location only.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1109.)
