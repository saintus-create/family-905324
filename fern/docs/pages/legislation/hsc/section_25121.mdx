# HSC § 25121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 25121

---

## Text

(a) “Recycled material” means a recyclable material which has been used or reused, or reclaimed.

(b) “Recycled material” does not include an intermediate manufacturing process stream.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 579, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
