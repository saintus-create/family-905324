# HSC § 1596.75

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.4. California Child Day Care Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1596.75

---

## Text

“Child” means a person who is under 18 years of age who is being provided care and supervision in a child day care facility, except where otherwise specified in this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1615, Sec. 9.)
