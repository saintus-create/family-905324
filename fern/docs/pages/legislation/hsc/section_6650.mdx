# HSC § 6650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 1. SANITARY DISTRICT ACT OF 1923 > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** HSC § 6650

---

## Text

The term of bonds issued shall not exceed forty years.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
