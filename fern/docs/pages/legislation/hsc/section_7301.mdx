# HSC § 7301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 5. Embalming and Transportation > Article: 1. Embalming

**Citation:** HSC § 7301

---

## Text

No embalmer shall embalm a dead human body when he has information reasonably indicating crime in connection with the death until permission of the coroner has been obtained.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1257.)
