# HSC § 110960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 5. Food > Article: 8. Potentially Hazardous Food

**Citation:** HSC § 110960

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 113995, it is unlawful for any person to transport, hold, or display any potentially hazardous refrigerated food at any temperature above 45 degrees Fahrenheit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 532, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
