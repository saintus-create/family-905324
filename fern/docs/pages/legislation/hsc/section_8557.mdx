# HSC § 8557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 3. Acquisition, Dedication and Sale > Article: 3. Dedication

**Citation:** HSC § 8557

---

## Text

The county recorder of the county in which a declaration of dedication is filed shall record it in the official records of his office and index it in the general index.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 954.)
