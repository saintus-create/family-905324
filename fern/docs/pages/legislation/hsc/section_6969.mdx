# HSC § 6969

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. SANITARY DISTRICTS > Part: 2. OTHER SANITARY DISTRICT ACTS > Chapter: 3. On-Site Wastewater Disposal Zones > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 6969

---

## Text

After the canvass of returns of any election on the question of forming a proposed zone, the board shall adopt a resolution ordering the formation of the zone if a majority of votes cast at such election are in favor of such formation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1125.)
