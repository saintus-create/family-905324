# HSC § 14843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 14843

---

## Text

The chief or ranking officer shall perform other duties as may be by proper authority imposed upon him or her.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2009.)
