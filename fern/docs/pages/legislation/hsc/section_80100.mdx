# HSC § 80100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 9. Orphan Share Reimbursement > Article: 6. Operative Date

**Citation:** HSC § 80100

---

## Text

(a) This chapter shall become operative on the operative date of the statute that does either, or both, of the following:

(1) Appropriates funds to the fund to implement this chapter.

(2) Establishes a revenue source for the fund.

(b) Notwithstanding subdivision (a), the operation of this chapter shall be suspended during any fiscal year in which both no funds are appropriated to the fund to implement this chapter and no revenue source for the fund is operative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
