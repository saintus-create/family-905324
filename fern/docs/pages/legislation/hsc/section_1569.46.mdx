# HSC § 1569.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3.2. Residential Care Facilities for the Elderly > Article: 4. Offenses

**Citation:** HSC § 1569.46

---

## Text

Operation of an unlicensed facility shall be an act of unfair competition and an unfair business practice within the meaning of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 17200) of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1115, Sec. 17.)
