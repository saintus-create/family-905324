# HSC § 18862.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.3. SPECIAL OCCUPANCY PARKS ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18862.13

---

## Text

“Conditional permit” means a construction, reconstruction, or operation permit issued by the enforcement agency which may prescribe conditions on the use or occupancy of a special occupancy park, subject to the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 434, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2002. Operative January 1, 2004, by Sec. 44 of Ch. 434, as amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1038.)
