# HSC § 13169

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2. FIRE PROTECTION > Chapter: 1.5. Portable Fire Extinguishers > Article: 2. Licensing

**Citation:** HSC § 13169

---

## Text

Every licensee shall notify the State Fire Marshal at his Sacramento office in writing within 15 days of any change of his address.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 802.)
