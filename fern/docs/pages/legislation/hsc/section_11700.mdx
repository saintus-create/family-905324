# HSC § 11700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.2. DRUG DEALER LIABILITY ACT

**Citation:** HSC § 11700

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Drug Dealer Liability Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 867, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
