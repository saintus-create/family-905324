# HSC § 1797.86

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. EMERGENCY MEDICAL SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 1797.86

---

## Text

“Health systems agency” means a health systems agency as defined in subsection (a) of Section 300(l)-1 of Title 42 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1260.)
