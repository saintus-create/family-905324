# HSC § 7006.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 7006.3

---

## Text

“Cremation chamber” means the enclosed space within which the cremation of human remains is performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1232, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
