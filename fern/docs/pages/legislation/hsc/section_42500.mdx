# HSC § 42500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 4. NONVEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 4.5. Protect California Air Act of 2003

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Protect California Air Act of 2003.

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 476, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
