# HSC § 128805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 5. HEALTH DATA > Chapter: 1. Health Facility Data

**Citation:** HSC § 128805

---

## Text

The department may enter into agreements and contracts with any person, department, agency, corporation, or legal entity as are necessary to carry out the functions vested in the department by this chapter or any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 220. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
