# HSC § 53181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 10. HOUSING AND HOMELESS BOND ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 53181

---

## Text

As used in this part, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Committee” means the Housing Committee created pursuant to Section 53202.

(b) “Fund” means the Home Building and Rehabilitation Fund created pursuant to Section 53190.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 48, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 107 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
