# HSC § 1537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. California Community Care Facilities Act > Article: 3. Regulations

**Citation:** HSC § 1537

---

## Text

The director shall have the authority to contract for personal services as required in order to perform inspections of, or consultation with, community care facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1203.)
