# HSC § 41264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District > Article: 6. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** HSC § 41264

---

## Text

All officers and employees of the Mojave Desert district, other than members of the Mojave Desert district board, are entitled to the benefits of the County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 (Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 31450) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 3 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 642, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
