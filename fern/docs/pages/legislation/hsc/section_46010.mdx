# HSC § 46010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. NOISE CONTROL ACT > Chapter: 2. Short Title

**Citation:** HSC § 46010

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Noise Control Act of 1973.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
