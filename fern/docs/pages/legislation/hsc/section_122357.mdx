# HSC § 122357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 105. COMMUNICABLE DISEASE PREVENTION AND CONTROL > Part: 6. VETERINARY PUBLIC HEALTH AND SAFETY > Chapter: 9. Pet Store Animal Care

**Citation:** HSC § 122357

---

## Text

A pet store operator who violates any provision of this chapter that is not specified in subdivision (a) of Section 122356 and is not proscribed by Section 122354.5 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 740, Sec. 3. (AB 485) Effective January 1, 2018.)
