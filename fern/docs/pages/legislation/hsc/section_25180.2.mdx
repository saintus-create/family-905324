# HSC § 25180.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Hazardous Waste Control > Article: 8. Enforcement

**Citation:** HSC § 25180.2

---

## Text

The department shall prioritize an enforcement action authorized by this chapter affecting communities that have been identified by the California Environmental Protection Agency as being the most impacted environmental justice communities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 598, Sec. 2. (AB 1329) Effective January 1, 2014.)
