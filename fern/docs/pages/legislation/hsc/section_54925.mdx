# HSC § 54925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. HOUSING AND HOME FINANCE > Part: 18. Housing Development and Finance > Chapter: 2. Housing Development and Finance Committee

**Citation:** HSC § 54925

---

## Text

There is hereby established the Housing Development and Finance Committee within the California Housing and Homelessness Agency.

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 68, Sec. 25. (AB 179) Effective July 13, 2026.)
