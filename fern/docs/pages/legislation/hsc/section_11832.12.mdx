# HSC § 11832.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 7.1. Certification of Alcohol and Other Drug Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 11832.12

---

## Text

(a) The department shall conduct onsite visits for compliance at least once during each certification period.

(b) The department may conduct announced or unannounced site visits at any time to any alcohol or other drug program certified pursuant to this chapter to determine compliance with applicable statutes and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 42, Sec. 32. (AB 118) Effective July 10, 2023.)
