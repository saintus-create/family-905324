# HSC § 1458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.5. County Medical Facilities > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** HSC § 1458

---

## Text

The board may provide a farm in connection with the county hospital or almshouse and may make regulations for working the same.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1784.)
