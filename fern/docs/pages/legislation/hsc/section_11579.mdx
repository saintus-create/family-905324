# HSC § 11579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 10. Control of Users of Controlled Substances > Article: 3. Abatement

**Citation:** HSC § 11579

---

## Text

If the existence of the nuisance is established in the action, an order of abatement shall be entered as part of the judgment in the case, and plaintiff’s costs in the action are a lien upon the building or place. The lien is enforceable and collectible by execution issued by order of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
