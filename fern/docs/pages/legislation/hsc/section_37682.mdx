# HSC § 37682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 10. HISTORICAL REHABILITATION > Chapter: 5. Construction and Effect

**Citation:** HSC § 37682

---

## Text

This part is full authority for the issuance of bonds by a local agency for the purpose of financing historical rehabilitation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1345.)
