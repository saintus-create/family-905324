# HSC § 11152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. UNIFORM CONTROLLED SUBSTANCES ACT > Chapter: 4. Prescriptions > Article: 1. Requirements of Prescriptions

**Citation:** HSC § 11152

---

## Text

No person shall write, issue, fill, compound, or dispense a prescription that does not conform to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1407.)
