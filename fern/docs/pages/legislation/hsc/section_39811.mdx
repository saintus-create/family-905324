# HSC § 39811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 2. STATE AIR RESOURCES BOARD > Chapter: 5. Air Pollution Control Subvention Program

**Citation:** HSC § 39811

---

## Text

Any moneys appropriated to the state board for expenditure under this chapter not allocated during the fiscal year shall revert to the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 957.)
