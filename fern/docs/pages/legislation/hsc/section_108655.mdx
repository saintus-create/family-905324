# HSC § 108655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 3. PRODUCT SAFETY > Chapter: 5. Consumer Products > Article: 5. Charcoal Hazards

**Citation:** HSC § 108655

---

## Text

The warning label required pursuant to Section 108650 shall be the same as the following: WARNING: Do Not Use for Indoor Heating or Cooking Unless Ventilation Is Provided for Exhausting Fumes to Outside. Toxic Fumes May Accumulate and Cause Death.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
