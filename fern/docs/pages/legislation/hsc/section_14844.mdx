# HSC § 14844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 4. FIRE COMPANIES IN UNINCORPORATED TOWNS > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 14844

---

## Text

Every chief, if any, shall attend all fires with his or her badge of office conspicuously displayed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 65, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2009.)
