# HSC § 8555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CEMETERIES > Part: 3. PRIVATE CEMETERIES > Chapter: 3. Acquisition, Dedication and Sale > Article: 3. Dedication

**Citation:** HSC § 8555

---

## Text

The filed map or plat and the recorded declaration are constructive notice to all persons of the dedication of the property to cemetery purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
