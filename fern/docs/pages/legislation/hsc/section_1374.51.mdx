# HSC § 1374.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2.2. Health Care Service Plans > Article: 5. Standards

**Citation:** HSC § 1374.51

---

## Text

No plan may utilize any information regarding whether an enrollee’s psychiatric inpatient admission was made on a voluntary or involuntary basis for the purpose of determining eligibility for claim reimbursement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 506, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
