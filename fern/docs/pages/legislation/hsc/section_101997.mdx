# HSC § 101997

**Hierarchy:** Division: 101. ADMINISTRATION OF PUBLIC HEALTH > Part: 7. California Cancer Clinical Trials Program

**Citation:** HSC § 101997

---

## Text

This part does not preclude the university from establishing or operating one or more similar programs to facilitate participation in any clinical trials, as defined in Section 300gg-8(d) of Title 42 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 661, Sec. 2. (AB 1823) Effective January 1, 2017.)
