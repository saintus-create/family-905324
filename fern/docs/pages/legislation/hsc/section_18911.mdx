# HSC § 18911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 18911

---

## Text

“Codification” or “codify” means to arrange building standards in the publication format of the code as determined by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 897, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
