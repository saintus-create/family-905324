# HSC § 127770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 11. Health Care Data

**Citation:** HSC § 127770

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter:

(a) “Department” means the Department of Health Care Access and Information.

(b) “State entities” means all state departments, agencies, boards, commissions, programs, and other organizational units of the executive branch of state government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 27, Sec. 49. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2026.)
