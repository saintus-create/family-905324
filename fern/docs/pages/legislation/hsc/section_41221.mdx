# HSC § 41221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 13. Mojave Desert Air Quality Management District > Article: 3. Governing Body

**Citation:** HSC § 41221

---

## Text

The Mojave Desert district board shall elect a chairperson every year from its membership.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 664, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008.)
