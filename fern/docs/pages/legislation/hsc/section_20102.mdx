# HSC § 20102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20102

---

## Text

The election shall be called by posting notices in three of the most public places in the district for not less than ten days; and if there is a newspaper printed and published in the district, by publishing a notice in at least two regular issues of the newspaper.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
