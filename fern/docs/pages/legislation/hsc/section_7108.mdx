# HSC § 7108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DEAD BODIES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Custody, and Duty of Interment

**Citation:** HSC § 7108

---

## Text

If the coroner is directed to make such interment he shall make it in the manner provided by law for the interment of the indigent dead.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 339.)
