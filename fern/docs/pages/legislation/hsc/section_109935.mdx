# HSC § 109935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 109935

---

## Text

“Food” means either of the following:

(a) Any article used or intended for use for food, drink, confection, condiment, or chewing gum by man or other animal.

(b) Any article used or intended for use as a component of any article designated in subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 641, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
