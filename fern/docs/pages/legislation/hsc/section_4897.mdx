# HSC § 4897

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 5. Annexation

**Citation:** HSC § 4897

---

## Text

The date set for the hearing on the proposed annexation shall be at least three weeks after the date of the adoption of the resolution setting the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
