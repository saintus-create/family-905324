# HSC § 41014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 3. AIR POLLUTION CONTROL DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Sacramento Metropolitan Air Quality Management District > Article: 4. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** HSC § 41014

---

## Text

The Sacramento district may conduct public education, marketing, demonstration, monitoring, research, and evaluation programs or projects with respect to transportation emission control measures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1541, Sec. 3.)
