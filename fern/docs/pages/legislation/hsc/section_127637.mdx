# HSC § 127637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 2. HEALTH POLICY AND PLANNING > Chapter: 6. Reproductive Health Equity

**Citation:** HSC § 127637

---

## Text

Funds awarded pursuant to this chapter shall be expended solely for the purpose for which the funds were awarded, in accordance with the approved application and budget, implementation guidance issued by the department, and the terms and conditions of the grant or continuation award.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 562, Sec. 2. (AB 2134) Effective January 1, 2023.)
