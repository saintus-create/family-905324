# HSC § 112891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 6. WHOLESALE FOOD > Chapter: 9. Olive Oil > Article: 2. Olive Oil Manufacture and Marketing

**Citation:** HSC § 112891

---

## Text

Any olive oil and olive-pomace oil labeled for sale shall be consistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 567, Sec. 7. (SB 818) Effective January 1, 2012.)
