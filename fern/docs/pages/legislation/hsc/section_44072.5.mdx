# HSC § 44072.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 5. VEHICULAR AIR POLLUTION CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Inspection Program > Article: 7. Denial, Suspension, and Revocation

**Citation:** HSC § 44072.5

---

## Text

Upon the effective date of any order of suspension or revocation of any license governed by this chapter, the licensee shall surrender the license to the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 386, Sec. 11.)
