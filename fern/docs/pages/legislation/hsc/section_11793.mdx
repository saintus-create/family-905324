# HSC § 11793

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10.5. ALCOHOL AND OTHER DRUG PROGRAMS > Part: 2. STATE GOVERNMENT'S ROLE TO ALLEVIATE PROBLEMS RELATED TO THE INAPPROPRIATE USE OF ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES AND OTHER DRUG USE > Chapter: 3.5. Resources and Information

**Citation:** HSC § 11793

---

## Text

The department may develop an objective program evaluation device or methodology and evaluate state-supported alcohol and other drug use prevention and treatment programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 847, Sec. 19. (AB 2995) Effective January 1, 2025.)
