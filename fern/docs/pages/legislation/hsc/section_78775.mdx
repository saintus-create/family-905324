# HSC § 78775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 45. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE RESPONSE > Part: 2. HAZARDOUS SUBSTANCE ACCOUNT > Chapter: 4. Releases of Hazardous Substances > Article: 5. Listing of Hazardous Substance Release Sites

**Citation:** HSC § 78775

---

## Text

Hazardous substance release sites listed by the department pursuant to Section 78760 are subject to this part and all actions carried out in response to hazardous substance releases or threatened releases at listed sites shall comply with the procedures, standards, and other requirements set forth in this part or established pursuant to the requirements of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 257, Sec. 2. (AB 2293) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Sec. 4 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 257.)
