# HSC § 1776

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LICENSING PROVISIONS > Chapter: 10. Continuing Care Contracts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** HSC § 1776

---

## Text

The department shall adopt, amend, or repeal, in accordance with Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, reasonable regulations as may be necessary or proper to carry out the purposes and intent of this chapter and to protect the rights of the elderly.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 875, Sec. 2.)
