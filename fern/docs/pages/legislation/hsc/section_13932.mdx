# HSC § 13932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. FIRES AND FIRE PROTECTION > Part: 2.7. FIRE PROTECTION DISTRICT LAW OF 1987 > Chapter: 9. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** HSC § 13932

---

## Text

The principal and interest of the bonds shall be payable in lawful money of the United States at the office of the district treasurer or any other place, at the option of the bondholder.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1013, Sec. 11.)
