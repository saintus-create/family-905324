# HSC § 18902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. HOUSING > Part: 2.5. STATE BUILDING STANDARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. Title

**Citation:** HSC § 18902

---

## Text

All references to the State Building Standards Code, Title 24 of the California Code of Regulations shall mean the California Building Standards Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 610, Sec. 2.1. (AB 2762) Effective January 1, 2011.)
