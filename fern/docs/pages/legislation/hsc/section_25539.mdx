# HSC § 25539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6.95. Hazardous Materials Release Response Plans and Inventory > Article: 2. Hazardous Materials Management

**Citation:** HSC § 25539

---

## Text

The agency and each unified program agency, in implementing this article, shall, upon request, involve and cooperate with local and state government officials, emergency planning committees, and professional associations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 115, Sec. 52. (AB 148) Effective July 22, 2021.)
