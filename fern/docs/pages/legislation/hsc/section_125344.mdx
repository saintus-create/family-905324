# HSC § 125344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 5.5. USE OF HUMAN CELLS > Chapter: 2. Procuring of Oocytes for Research

**Citation:** HSC § 125344

---

## Text

The physician and surgeon performing the AOP or any alternative method of oocyte retrieval shall not have a financial interest in the outcome of the research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 483, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2007.)
