# HSC § 123845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 106. PERSONAL HEALTH CARE (INCLUDING MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT) > Part: 2. MATERNAL, CHILD, AND ADOLESCENT HEALTH > Chapter: 3. Child Health > Article: 5. California Children’s Services

**Citation:** HSC § 123845

---

## Text

“California Children’s Services Program,” as used in this article, means the program of services established and operated pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 415, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
