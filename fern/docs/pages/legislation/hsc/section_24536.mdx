# HSC § 24536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MISCELLANEOUS HEALTH AND SAFETY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4.8. Bunk Beds

**Citation:** HSC § 24536

---

## Text

Remedies available under this article shall be in addition to any other remedies or procedures under any other provision of law that may be available to an aggrieved party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 920, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
