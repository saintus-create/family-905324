# HSC § 4874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SANITATION > Part: 3. COMMUNITY FACILITIES > Chapter: 4. Sewer Maintenance Districts > Article: 2. Formation

**Citation:** HSC § 4874

---

## Text

The board shall direct the clerk to publish the notice once a week for two successive weeks in the newspaper of general circulation circulated in the territory which it is proposed to organize into a district that the board deems most likely to give notice to the inhabitants of the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
