# HSC § 20105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. POLICE PROTECTION > Part: 1. POLICE PROTECTION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. In Unincorporated Towns > Article: 4. Taxation

**Citation:** HSC § 20105

---

## Text

The district board shall appoint three judges and two clerks to conduct the election. The election shall be conducted as far as practicable pursuant to the general election law; but neither a new register nor legal ballot paper is required, and the polls may be opened at eight o'clock a.m., and closed at five o'clock p.m. on the day of the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1939, Ch. 60.)
