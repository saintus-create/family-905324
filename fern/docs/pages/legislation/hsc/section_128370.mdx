# HSC § 128370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 107. HEALTH CARE ACCESS AND INFORMATION > Part: 3. HEALTH PROFESSIONS DEVELOPMENT > Chapter: 5. Health Professions Education Programs > Article: 1. Health Professions Education Programs

**Citation:** HSC § 128370

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the department may exempt from public disclosure any document in the possession of the department that pertains to a donation made pursuant to this article if the donor has requested anonymity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 143, Sec. 172. (AB 133) Effective July 27, 2021.)
