# HSC § 33395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. COMMUNITY DEVELOPMENT AND HOUSING > Part: 1. COMMUNITY REDEVELOPMENT LAW > Chapter: 4. Redevelopment Procedures and Activities > Article: 7. Property Acquisition

**Citation:** HSC § 33395

---

## Text

Property already devoted to a public use may be acquired by the agency through eminent domain, but property of a public body shall not be acquired without its consent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1812.)
