# HSC § 110422.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 104. ENVIRONMENTAL HEALTH > Part: 5. SHERMAN FOOD, DRUG, AND COSMETIC LAWS > Chapter: 4. Packaging, Labeling,and Advertising > Article: 4. Dietary Supplement Labeling and Advertising

**Citation:** HSC § 110422.5

---

## Text

Violation of this article by any person, as defined in Section 109995, shall constitute an infraction, punishable by a fine not to exceed the following:

(a) One thousand dollars ($1,000) for the first violation.

(b) Two thousand dollars ($2,000) for the second violation.

(c) Five thousand dollars ($5,000) for the third and each subsequent violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 110424 by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 194. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
