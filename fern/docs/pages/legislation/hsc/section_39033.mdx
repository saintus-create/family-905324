# HSC § 39033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. AIR RESOURCES > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** HSC § 39033

---

## Text

“Heavy-duty” means having a manufacturer’s maximum gross vehicle weight rating of 6,001 or more pounds.
