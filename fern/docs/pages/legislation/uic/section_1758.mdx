# UIC § 1758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 7. Collections > Article: 3. Notices of Levy

**Citation:** UIC § 1758

---

## Text

As used in this article “person” includes this State and any county, city and county, municipality, district or other political subdivision thereof.
