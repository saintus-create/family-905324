# UIC § 3654.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 3. EXTENDED UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Extended Duration Benefits > Article: 3. Filing, Determination, and Payment of Extended Duration Benefit Claims

**Citation:** UIC § 3654.3

---

## Text

If any employing unit fails to respond to a request for wage information within the period prescribed by Section 3654.1, the director shall make a determination based upon available information.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
