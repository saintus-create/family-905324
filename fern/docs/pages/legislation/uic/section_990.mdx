# UIC § 990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 3. Contribution Rates

**Citation:** UIC § 990

---

## Text

In the payment of any contributions, a fractional part of a cent shall be disregarded unless it amounts to one-half cent ($0.005) or more, in which case it shall be increased to one cent ($0.01).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
