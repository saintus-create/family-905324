# UIC § 125.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 125.3

---

## Text

“American aircraft” means an aircraft registered under the laws of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1107.)
