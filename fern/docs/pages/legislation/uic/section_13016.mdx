# UIC § 13016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13016

---

## Text

When necessary to make effective the provisions of this division, the name and address of the recipient of income shall be furnished upon demand of the person paying the income.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
