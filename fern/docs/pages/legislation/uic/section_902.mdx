# UIC § 902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 902

---

## Text

“Computation date” means the close of business on June 30th, of each calendar year for the purpose of establishing contribution rates for the next succeeding calendar year.
