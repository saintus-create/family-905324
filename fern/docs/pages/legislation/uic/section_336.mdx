# UIC § 336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. Employment Development Department

**Citation:** UIC § 336

---

## Text

The director shall establish procedures to identify the transfer or acquisition of a business that is undertaken for purposes of obtaining a lower unemployment insurance contribution rate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 827, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
