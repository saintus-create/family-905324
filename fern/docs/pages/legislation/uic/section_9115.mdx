# UIC § 9115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 9115

---

## Text

“Economically displaced persons” means those persons who have been subjected to an involuntary layoff or separation from their employment, and who have not quit voluntarily or been dismissed for disciplinary reasons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1563.)
