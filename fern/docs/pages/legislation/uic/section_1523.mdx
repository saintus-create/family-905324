# UIC § 1523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Unemployment Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 1523

---

## Text

Withdrawals by the director from the Unemployment Fund are exempted from the operation of Sections 925.4 and 925.6 of the Government Code.
