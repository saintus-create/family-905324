# UIC § 11006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 5. Employment Services for the Deaf and Hearing Impaired

**Citation:** UIC § 11006

---

## Text

The Employment Development Department shall not expend over 5 percent of the sum appropriated for this chapter for its administrative costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1157, Sec. 1. Effective September 17, 1984.)
