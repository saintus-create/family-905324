# UIC § 2603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 2603

---

## Text

Disability benefits paid pursuant to this part shall not be charged against an employer’s account maintained pursuant to Article 4 of Chapter 4, Part 1 of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
