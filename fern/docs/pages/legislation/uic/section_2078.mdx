# UIC § 2078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 9.5. Employment for Older Workers

**Citation:** UIC § 2078

---

## Text

The age limitations of the apprenticeship programs in which the State participates shall not be considered discriminatory within the meaning of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1623.)
