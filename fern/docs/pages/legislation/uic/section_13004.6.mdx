# UIC § 13004.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13004.6

---

## Text

“Employee” does not include any member of a limited liability company that is treated as a partnership for federal income tax purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 122, Sec. 1. (SB 1131) Effective January 1, 2015.)
