# UIC § 644.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 2. Excluded Services

**Citation:** UIC § 644.5

---

## Text

“Employment” does not include services performed in the employ of an international organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 494. See operational condition in Section 638.)
