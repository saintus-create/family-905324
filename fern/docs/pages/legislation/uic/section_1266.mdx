# UIC § 1266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Unemployment Compensation Benefits > Article: 1.5. California Training Benefits Program

**Citation:** UIC § 1266

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Training Benefits Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 591, Sec. 2. (AB 2058) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative on date (no later than July 1, 2011) prescribed in Section 1274.20.)
