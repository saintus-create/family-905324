# UIC § 2702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 4. Filing, Determination and Payment of Disability Benefit Claims

**Citation:** UIC § 2702

---

## Text

Minors who are eligible for disability benefits may be paid and receive such benefits in their own right and a receipt signed by a minor shall be valid and binding in all respects.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
