# UIC § 9101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 9101

## Text

“Department” means the Employment Development Department, which may also be referred to as the Department of Employment Development.
