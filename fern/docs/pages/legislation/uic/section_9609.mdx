# UIC § 9609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Employment Development Department > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** UIC § 9609

---

## Text

The department shall administer manpower service funds and shall provide, in a balanced and flexible manner, needed services as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1207.)
