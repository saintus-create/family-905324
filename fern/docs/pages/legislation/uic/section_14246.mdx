# UIC § 14246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. CALIFORNIA WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND OPPORTUNITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Local Service Delivery > Article: 4. The California Creative Workforce Act of 2021

**Citation:** UIC § 14246

## Text

This article shall become operative upon the appropriation by the Legislature of sufficient funds for its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 767, Sec. 3. (SB 628) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative as prescribed by its own provisions. See conditional termination clause in Section 14007.)
