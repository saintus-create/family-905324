# UIC § 4900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. AUTOMATION OF THE EMPLOYMENT DEVELOPMENT DEPARTMENT > Chapter: 1. Annual Reports

**Citation:** UIC § 4900

---

## Text

As used in this division:

(a) “Department” means the Employment Development Department.

(b) “Director” means the Director of Employment Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 799, Sec. 3. Effective September 15, 1986.)
