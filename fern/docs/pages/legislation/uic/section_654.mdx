# UIC § 654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 2. Excluded Services

**Citation:** UIC § 654

---

## Text

“Employment” does not include service performed by a free-lance jockey or exercise boy who is regularly licensed by the California Horse Racing Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 620.)
