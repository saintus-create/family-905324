# UIC § 2108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 10. Violations

**Citation:** UIC § 2108

---

## Text

It is a violation of this chapter for any person to willfully fail or refuse to make any contributions which are due under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 724, Sec. 10.)
