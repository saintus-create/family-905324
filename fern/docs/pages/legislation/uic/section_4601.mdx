# UIC § 4601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 4. FEDERAL-STATE EXTENDED COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Federal-State Extended Benefits > Article: 2. Computation (Amount and Duration)

**Citation:** UIC § 4601

---

## Text

The computation of the amount and duration of federal-state extended benefits shall be in accordance with Section 4004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1156.)
