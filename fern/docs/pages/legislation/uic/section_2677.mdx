# UIC § 2677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 3. Disqualifications

**Citation:** UIC § 2677

---

## Text

An individual who is otherwise eligible for benefits under this part shall not be disqualified from receiving such benefits because of a disqualification from receiving unemployment compensation benefits under Section 1262.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1052.)
