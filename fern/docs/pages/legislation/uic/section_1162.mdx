# UIC § 1162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 8.6. Motor Carrier Employer Amnesty Program

**Citation:** UIC § 1162

---

## Text

Commercial drivers who are classified as employees pursuant to a settlement agreement shall be eligible to receive a refund of elective coverage contributions pursuant to Section 708 and may submit a claim for refund pursuant to Section 1178.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 741, Sec. 2. (AB 621) Effective January 1, 2016.)
