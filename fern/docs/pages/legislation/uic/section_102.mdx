# UIC § 102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Policy and Interpretation

**Citation:** UIC § 102

---

## Text

All the rights, privileges or immunities conferred by this division or by acts deemed pursuant thereto shall exist subject to the power of the Legislature to amend or repeal this division at any time.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 449.)
