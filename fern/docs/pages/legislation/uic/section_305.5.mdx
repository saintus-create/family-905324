# UIC § 305.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. Employment Development Department

**Citation:** UIC § 305.5

---

## Text

All regulations heretofore adopted by the Director of the Department of Human Resources Development shall remain in effect and shall be fully enforceable unless and until readopted, amended or repealed by the Director of Employment Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1207.)
