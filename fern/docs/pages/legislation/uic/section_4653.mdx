# UIC § 4653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 4. FEDERAL-STATE EXTENDED COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Federal-State Extended Benefits > Article: 3. Filing, Determination, and Payment of Federal-State Extended Benefit Claims

**Citation:** UIC § 4653

---

## Text

The effective date of a valid application shall be determined in the same manner as the effective date of a new claim for normal benefits pursuant to Section 1326.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1156.)
