# UIC § 1274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Unemployment Compensation Benefits > Article: 1.5. California Training Benefits Program

**Citation:** UIC § 1274

---

## Text

The director may publish a list of high demand occupations in each labor market area of this state. If a demand occupation is limited to a particular industry, the director may identify the industry of the demand occupation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1274.5 by Stats. 1987, Ch. 956, Sec. 5. Effective September 22, 1987.)
