# UIC § 1872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 7. Collections > Article: 8. Offers in Compromise

**Citation:** UIC § 1872

---

## Text

A determination by the director that it would not be in the best interest of the state to accept partial payment in satisfaction of a tax liability shall not be subject to administrative appeal or judicial review.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 356, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
