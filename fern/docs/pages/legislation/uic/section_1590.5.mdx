# UIC § 1590.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Contingent Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 1590.5

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the Budget Act for each fiscal year shall appropriate the entire amount available in the Contingent Fund during the fiscal year for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1591 (as added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1146) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 216, Sec. 109.)
