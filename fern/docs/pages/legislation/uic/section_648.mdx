# UIC § 648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 2. Excluded Services

**Citation:** UIC § 648

---

## Text

“Employment” does not include service performed on or in connection with a vessel or aircraft not an American vessel or an American aircraft, if the employee is employed on and in connection with such vessel or aircraft when outside the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1107. Provisions operative as prescribed in Section 638.)
