# UIC § 17000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. CALWORKS PROGRAM:  JOB CREATION

**Citation:** UIC § 17000

---

## Text

As used in this division “department” means the Employment Development Department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 270, Sec. 29. Effective August 11, 1997. Operative January 1, 1998, by Sec. 183 of Ch. 270.)
