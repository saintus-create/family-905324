# UIC § 1035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 4. Reserve Accounts

**Citation:** UIC § 1035

## Text

The director shall give notice pursuant to Section 1206 to the employer of his or her action on a protest filed under Section 1034.
