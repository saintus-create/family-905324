# UIC § 10208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 3.5. Employment Training Panel

**Citation:** UIC § 10208

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to preclude any employer from contracting with any public or private training entity for services, subject to the approval of the panel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1074, Sec. 1.)
