# UIC § 4551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 4. FEDERAL-STATE EXTENDED COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Federal-State Extended Benefits > Article: 1. Eligibility and Disqualifications

**Citation:** UIC § 4551

---

## Text

Federal-state extended benefits are payable from the Unemployment Fund to unemployed individuals who are eligible under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1156.)
