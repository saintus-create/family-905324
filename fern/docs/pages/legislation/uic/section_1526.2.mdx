# UIC § 1526.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Unemployment Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 1526.2

---

## Text

All worker contributions and amounts payable to the Disability Fund after proper clearance shall be forwarded to the Treasurer who shall immediately deposit them in that fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
