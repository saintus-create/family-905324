# UIC § 976.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 3. Contribution Rates

**Citation:** UIC § 976.8

---

## Text

(a) Section 976.6 does not apply to any employer who has a negative reserve account balance on the computation date.

(b) Subdivision (a) does not apply to an employer assigned the maximum rate pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 977.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 827, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
