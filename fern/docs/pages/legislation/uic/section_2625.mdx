# UIC § 2625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 1. Eligibility

**Citation:** UIC § 2625

---

## Text

Unemployment compensation disability benefits are payable from the Disability Fund to individuals who are eligible to receive such benefit payments under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
