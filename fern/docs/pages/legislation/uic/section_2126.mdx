# UIC § 2126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 10. Violations

**Citation:** UIC § 2126

---

## Text

Any person or employing unit convicted under this chapter may be charged the costs of investigation and prosecution at the discretion of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 724, Sec. 30.)
