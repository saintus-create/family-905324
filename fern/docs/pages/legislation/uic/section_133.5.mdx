# UIC § 133.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 133.5

---

## Text

“Department of Benefit Payments” or “State Department of Benefit Payments” shall be construed to refer to and mean the Employment Development Department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
