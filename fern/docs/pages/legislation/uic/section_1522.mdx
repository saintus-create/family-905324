# UIC § 1522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Unemployment Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 1522

---

## Text

The Unemployment Fund shall be administered by the director exclusively for the purposes of this division without liability upon the part of the State beyond the amounts paid into and earned by the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 313.)
