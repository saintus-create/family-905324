# UIC § 3009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Disability Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 3009

---

## Text

Refunds, credits, or judgments, and interest thereon, payable for contributions erroneously collected under Sections 984 and 985 may be paid from the Disability Fund on warrants issued by the Controller under the direction of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1025, Sec. 22.)
