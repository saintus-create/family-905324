# UIC § 405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3. California Unemployment Insurance Appeals Board

**Citation:** UIC § 405

---

## Text

The appeals board shall appoint a chief administrative law judge who shall be a member in good standing of the State Bar of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 537, Sec. 3.)
