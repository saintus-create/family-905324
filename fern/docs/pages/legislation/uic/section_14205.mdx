# UIC § 14205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. CALIFORNIA WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND OPPORTUNITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Local Service Delivery > Article: 1. Local Workforce Development Board

**Citation:** UIC § 14205

---

## Text

The local board shall elect a chairperson for the local board from among the business representatives.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 630, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2007. See conditional termination clause in Section 14007.)
