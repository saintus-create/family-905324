# UIC § 137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 137

---

## Text

“Public employment office” means a free public employment office or branch thereof operated by this State or maintained as a part of a state-controlled system of public employment offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
