# UIC § 14038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. CALIFORNIA WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND OPPORTUNITY ACT > Chapter: 3. State Responsibilities > Article: 3. Removing Barriers to Employment Act

**Citation:** UIC § 14038

---

## Text

All criteria, guidelines, and policies developed by the California Workforce Development Board for the administration of the initiative shall be exempt from the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedures Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 53, Sec. 41. (SB 866) Effective June 27, 2018. See conditional termination clause in Section 14007.)
