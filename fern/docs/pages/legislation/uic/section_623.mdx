# UIC § 623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 1.5. Employee

**Citation:** UIC § 623

---

## Text

“Employee” does not include any member of a limited liability company that is treated as a partnership for federal income tax purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 522, Sec. 2. (SB 1244) Effective January 1, 2011.)
