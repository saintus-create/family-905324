# UIC § 13007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13007

---

## Text

“Miscellaneous payroll period” means a payroll period other than a daily, weekly, biweekly, semimonthly, monthly, quarterly, semiannual, or annual payroll period.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
