# UIC § 141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 141

---

## Text

“Unemployment insurance” wherever it appears in this division means “unemployment compensation.”
