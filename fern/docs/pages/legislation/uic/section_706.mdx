# UIC § 706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 4. Elective Coverage

**Citation:** UIC § 706

---

## Text

The director may for good cause waive the requirement of Section 705 that a written application for termination shall be filed on or before the thirty-first day of January.
