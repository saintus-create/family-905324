# UIC § 3653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 3. EXTENDED UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Extended Duration Benefits > Article: 3. Filing, Determination, and Payment of Extended Duration Benefit Claims

**Citation:** UIC § 3653

---

## Text

The effective date of a valid primary claim shall be determined in the same manner as the effective date of a new claim for normal benefits pursuant to Section 1326.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2035.)
