# UIC § 938.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 2. “Wages,” the Basis of the Contribution

**Citation:** UIC § 938.5

---

## Text

“Wages” does not include any payment received by a member of the National Guard or reserve component of the armed forces for inactive duty training, annual training, or emergency state active duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 218, Sec. 1.)
