# UIC § 3011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Disability Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 3011

---

## Text

Whenever any warrant is drawn on an account in the Disability Fund by the Controller, and the same remains unclaimed after one year, the amount thereof shall revert to that account in the Disability Fund from which the amount was payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 296, Sec. 298. (AB 1023) Effective January 1, 2012.)
