# UIC § 14230.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. CALIFORNIA WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND OPPORTUNITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Local Service Delivery > Article: 3. One-Stop Career Center System

**Citation:** UIC § 14230.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this division and any laws governing workforce development programs, and to the extent permitted under Chapter 32 of Title 29 of the United States Code and any related regulations, entrance into a registered apprenticeship program shall be considered placement into a job.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 94, Sec. 39. (AB 1270) Effective January 1, 2016. See conditional termination clause in Section 14007.)
