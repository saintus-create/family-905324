# UIC § 13008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13008

---

## Text

“Payroll period” means a period for which a payment of wages is ordinarily made to the employee by his or her employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
