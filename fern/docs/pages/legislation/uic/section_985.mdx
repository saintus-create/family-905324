# UIC § 985

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 3. Contribution Rates

**Citation:** UIC § 985

---

## Text

(a) Section 984 shall not apply to that part of the remuneration which, after remuneration with respect to employment equal to four times the maximum weekly benefit for each calendar year specified in Section 2655 multiplied by 13 and divided by 55 percent has been paid to an individual by an employer, is paid to the individual by the employer.

(b) This section shall not apply with respect to wages paid on or after January 1, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 196, Sec. 25. (SB 143) Effective September 13, 2023.)
