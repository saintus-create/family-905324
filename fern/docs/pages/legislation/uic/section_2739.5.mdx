# UIC § 2739.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 5. Overpayments

**Citation:** UIC § 2739.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, the Franchise Tax Board shall aid the director in the recovery of overpayments of disability benefits through the exchange of information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 226, Sec. 3.)
