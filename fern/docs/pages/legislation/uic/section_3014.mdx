# UIC § 3014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Disability Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 3014

---

## Text

Withdrawals by the director from the Disability Fund for the payment of refunds, credits, or judgments, and disability benefits are exempted from the operation of Section 925.6 of the Government Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1252.)
