# UIC § 1340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Unemployment Compensation Benefits > Article: 3. Filing, Determination, and Payment of Unemployment Compensation Benefit Claims

**Citation:** UIC § 1340

---

## Text

Minors who are eligible for benefits may be paid and receive benefits in their own right, and a receipt signed by a minor shall be valid and binding.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
