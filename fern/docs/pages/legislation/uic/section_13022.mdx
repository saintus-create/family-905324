# UIC § 13022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 2. Withholding and Payment of Tax

**Citation:** UIC § 13022

---

## Text

In determining the amount to be deducted and withheld under Section 13020, the wages may, at the election of the employer, be computed to the nearest dollar.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
