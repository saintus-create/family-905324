# UIC § 677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 3. Subject Employers

**Citation:** UIC § 677

---

## Text

“Employer” also means any employing unit for which service is performed in “employment” as defined by Section 605.
