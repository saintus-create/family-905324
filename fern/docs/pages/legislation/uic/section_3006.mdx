# UIC § 3006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Disability Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 3006

---

## Text

There shall be no further transfer of money from the Unemployment Trust Fund to the Disability Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 397.)
