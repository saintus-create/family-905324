# UIC § 317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. Employment Development Department

**Citation:** UIC § 317

---

## Text

The Director of Employment Development shall maintain a field investigating staff, whose function shall embrace investigation throughout the state of violations of this code, to the end that its provisions are more adequately and strictly enforced.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1212, Sec. 119.)
