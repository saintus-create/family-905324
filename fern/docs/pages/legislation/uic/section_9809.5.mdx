# UIC § 9809.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Employment Development Department > Article: 4. California YouthBuild Program

**Citation:** UIC § 9809.5

---

## Text

Each grant recipient shall report to the director on other participant outcomes as required by the Governor under Section 122(h) of the federal Workforce Investment Act of 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 829, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
