# UIC § 1131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 8. Assessments

**Citation:** UIC § 1131

---

## Text

The director shall give to the employing unit against whom an assessment is made a written notice of the assessment pursuant to Section 1206.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1082.)
