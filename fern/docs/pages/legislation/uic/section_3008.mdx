# UIC § 3008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Disability Fund

**Citation:** UIC § 3008

---

## Text

All money collected under Section 984 shall be deposited in the Disability Fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1025, Sec. 21.)
