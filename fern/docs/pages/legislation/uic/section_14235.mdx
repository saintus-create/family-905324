# UIC § 14235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. CALIFORNIA WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND OPPORTUNITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Local Service Delivery > Article: 3. One-Stop Career Center System

**Citation:** UIC § 14235

---

## Text

To the full extent permitted by federal law, required by federal law, or both, the Employment Development Department shall utilize its Wagner-Peyser funded activities and programs to support local one-stop career centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 94, Sec. 42. (AB 1270) Effective January 1, 2016. See conditional termination clause in Section 14007.)
