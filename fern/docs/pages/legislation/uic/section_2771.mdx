# UIC § 2771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 6. Rights of Trainees

**Citation:** UIC § 2771

---

## Text

No disqualification shall be applied to any trainee after the termination of his military service, by reason of any act or course of action on his part prior to the date of his entry into such service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1165.)
