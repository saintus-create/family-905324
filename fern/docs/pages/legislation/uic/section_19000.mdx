# UIC § 19000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. FAIR SHARE FROM BIG CORPORATIONS ACT

**Citation:** UIC § 19000

---

## Text

This Division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Fair Share from Big Corporations Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 64, Sec. 1. (SB 177) Effective July 6, 2026.)
