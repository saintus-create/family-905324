# UIC § 13010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13010

---

## Text

“Withholding agent” means any person required to deduct and withhold any tax under the provisions of Section 13020.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
