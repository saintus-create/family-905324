# UIC § 2778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 7. Rights of Industrially Disabled Persons

**Citation:** UIC § 2778

---

## Text

No disqualification shall be applied to any industrially disabled person after the termination of his industrial disability, by reason of any act or course of action on his part prior to the date on which his industrial disability commenced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1027.)
