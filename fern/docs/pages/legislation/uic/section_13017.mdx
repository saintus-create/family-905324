# UIC § 13017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13017

---

## Text

Unless otherwise specifically provided, the provisions of any law effecting changes in withholding under this division shall begin in the manner set forth by Section 18665 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 877, Sec. 96. Effective October 6, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 102 of Ch. 877.)
