# UIC § 1180.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 9. Refunds and Overpayments

**Citation:** UIC § 1180.1

---

## Text

No claim for refund of amounts paid pursuant to Section 1870 may be filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 356, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
