# UIC § 930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 2. “Wages,” the Basis of the Contribution

**Citation:** UIC § 930

---

## Text

“Wages” does not include remuneration in excess of seven thousand dollars ($7,000) paid to an individual by an employer during any calendar year, with respect to employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1275, Sec. 1. Effective September 19, 1984.)
