# UIC § 3602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 3. EXTENDED UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Extended Duration Benefits > Article: 2. Computation (Amount and Duration)

**Citation:** UIC § 3602

---

## Text

An exhaustee’s extended duration award during any one extended duration period shall be 13 times his weekly benefit amount or one-half of the maximum amount of normal benefits payable to him during his parent benefit year, whichever is the lesser.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2035.)
