# UIC § 9808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Employment Development Department > Article: 4. California YouthBuild Program

**Citation:** UIC § 9808

---

## Text

The term “YouthBuild” shall only be used in connection with a program funded pursuant to this article or by the United States Department of Labor, or if the program is an affiliate of YouthBuild U.S.A.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 95, Sec. 3. (AB 271) Effective January 1, 2010.)
