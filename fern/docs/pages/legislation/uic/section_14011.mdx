# UIC § 14011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. CALIFORNIA WORKFORCE INNOVATION AND OPPORTUNITY ACT > Chapter: 3. State Responsibilities > Article: 1. California Workforce Development Board

**Citation:** UIC § 14011

---

## Text

The board shall report, through its executive director, to the Secretary of the Labor and Workforce Development Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 630, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2007. See conditional termination clause in Section 14007.)
