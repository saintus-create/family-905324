# UIC § 9108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** UIC § 9108

---

## Text

“Unemployed person” means a person who lacks a bona fide employment, suffers economic deprivation because of lack of employment, and is employable or capable of being made employable through the services available under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1460.)
