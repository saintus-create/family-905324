# UIC § 2654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 2. Computation (Amount and Duration)

**Citation:** UIC § 2654

---

## Text

For the purpose of this article wages due to any individual but unpaid within the time limit provided by law, shall be deemed wages paid to such individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1371.)
