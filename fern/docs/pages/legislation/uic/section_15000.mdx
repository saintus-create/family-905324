# UIC § 15000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. CALIFORNIA GREEN COLLAR JOBS ACT OF 2008

**Citation:** UIC § 15000

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Green Collar Jobs Act of 2008.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 312, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
