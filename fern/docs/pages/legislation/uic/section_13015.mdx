# UIC § 13015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 13015

---

## Text

The department is authorized to require such information with respect to persons subject to the taxes imposed by this division as is necessary or helpful in securing proper identification of such persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
