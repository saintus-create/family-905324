# UIC § 9925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Employment Development Department > Article: 7. Displaced Oil and Gas Worker Pilot Program

**Citation:** UIC § 9925

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until July 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 67, Sec. 43. (SB 191) Effective June 30, 2022. Repealed as of July 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 7, commencing with Section 9920.)
