# UIC § 324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. Employment Development Department

**Citation:** UIC § 324

---

## Text

The expense of the administration of this division shall be paid out of the Unemployment Administration Fund, unless otherwise provided for in this division.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
