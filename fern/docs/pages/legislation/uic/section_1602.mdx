# UIC § 1602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Investments in or Expenditures for Property

**Citation:** UIC § 1602

---

## Text

The department shall comply with all federal regulations with regard to the sale of property in which the federal government has an equity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1036, Sec. 2.)
