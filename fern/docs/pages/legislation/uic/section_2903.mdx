# UIC § 2903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions

**Citation:** UIC § 2903

---

## Text

The time, procedure, manner of payment and collection of contributions under this part shall be in accordance with the provisions of Part 1 of this division.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
