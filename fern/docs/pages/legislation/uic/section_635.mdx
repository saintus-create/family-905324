# UIC § 635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 3. Scope or Coverage > Article: 2. Excluded Services

**Citation:** UIC § 635

---

## Text

“Employment” does not include service under any unemployment compensation system established by a law of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
