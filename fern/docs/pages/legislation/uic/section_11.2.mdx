# UIC § 11.2

**Citation:** UIC § 11.2

---

## Text

“Spouse” includes “registered domestic partner,” as required by Section 297.5 of the Family Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 50, Sec. 115. (SB 1005) Effective January 1, 2017.)
