# UIC § 9802.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Employment Development Department > Article: 4. California YouthBuild Program

**Citation:** UIC § 9802.5

---

## Text

The department may accept proposals for funding from applicants who establish their eligibility for funding under this article by submitting proof that they have been funded or designated as a federal YouthBuild program by the United States Department of Labor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 95, Sec. 2. (AB 271) Effective January 1, 2010.)
