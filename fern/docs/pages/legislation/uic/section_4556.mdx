# UIC § 4556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 4. FEDERAL-STATE EXTENDED COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Federal-State Extended Benefits > Article: 1. Eligibility and Disqualifications

**Citation:** UIC § 4556

---

## Text

When the department refers individuals who have applied for federal-state extended benefits to work, the referrals shall include suitable work, as defined by subdivision (a) of Section 4553, to which any part of subdivision (b) of Section 4553 would not apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 10, Sec. 5. Effective March 31, 1981. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 4558.)
