# UIC § 938.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 4. Contributions and Reports > Article: 2. “Wages,” the Basis of the Contribution

**Citation:** UIC § 938.7

---

## Text

“Wages,” does not include any payment made by an employer to a survivor, or the estate of a former employee, after the calendar year in which the employee died.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 37, Sec. 1. Effective May 7, 1985.)
