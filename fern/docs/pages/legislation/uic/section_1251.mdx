# UIC § 1251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 5. Unemployment Compensation Benefits > Article: 1. Eligibility and Disqualifications

**Citation:** UIC § 1251

---

## Text

Unemployment compensation benefits are payable from the Unemployment Fund to unemployed individuals who are eligible under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
