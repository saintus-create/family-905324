# UIC § 13073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WITHHOLDING TAX ON WAGES > Chapter: 5. Collections

**Citation:** UIC § 13073

---

## Text

Any employer or person failing to withhold the amount due from any taxpayer and to transmit the same to the department after service of a notice pursuant to Section 13072 is liable for such amounts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1007, Sec. 64. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 67 of Ch. 1007.)
