# UIC § 2705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 2. DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Disability Benefits > Article: 4. Filing, Determination and Payment of Disability Benefit Claims

**Citation:** UIC § 2705

---

## Text

Where an individual who would be eligible to receive disability benefits dies before making a claim therefor, the director may in accordance with authorized regulations allow the filing of a claim for such benefits by a person legally entitled thereto under Section 1341.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1953, Ch. 308.)
