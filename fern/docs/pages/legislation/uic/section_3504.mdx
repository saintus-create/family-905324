# UIC § 3504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 3. EXTENDED UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** UIC § 3504

---

## Text

The director shall during the week immediately preceding each calendar week compute the insured unemployment rate for that calendar week. The computation shall be a public record.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1073, Sec. 4.)
