# UIC § 2055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 9. Public Employment Offices

**Citation:** UIC § 2055

---

## Text

Free public employment offices shall be maintained in the Cities of San Francisco, Los Angeles, Oakland and Sacramento, and, whenever the director deems it necessary, in other cities.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1729.)
