# UIC § 2125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 10. Violations

**Citation:** UIC § 2125

---

## Text

Any action or prosecution under this chapter shall be instituted within four years after the discovery of the offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 724, Sec. 29.)
