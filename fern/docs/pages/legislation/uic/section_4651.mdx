# UIC § 4651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 4. FEDERAL-STATE EXTENDED COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Federal-State Extended Benefits > Article: 3. Filing, Determination, and Payment of Federal-State Extended Benefit Claims

## Text

Claims for federal-state extended benefits shall be made as provided in this article.
