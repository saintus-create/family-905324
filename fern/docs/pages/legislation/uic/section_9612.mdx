# UIC § 9612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT SERVICES PROGRAMS > Part: 1. EMPLOYMENT AND EMPLOYABILITY SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Employment Development Department > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** UIC § 9612

---

## Text

The employees of the department shall be subject to the State Civil Service Act, except for exempt appointees. Members of the California Commission on Aging shall continue to be appointed by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 819, Sec. 4.)
