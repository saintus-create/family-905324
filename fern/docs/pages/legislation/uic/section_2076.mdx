# UIC § 2076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 9.5. Employment for Older Workers

**Citation:** UIC § 2076

---

## Text

The department shall issue such publications and such results of research and other activities as in its judgment will tend to minimize or eliminate discrimination in employment on account of age.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1623.)
