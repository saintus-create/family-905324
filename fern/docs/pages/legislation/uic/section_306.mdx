# UIC § 306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT AND DISABILITY COMPENSATION > Part: 1. UNEMPLOYMENT COMPENSATION > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. Employment Development Department

**Citation:** UIC § 306

---

## Text

The Director of Employment Development may adopt, amend, or repeal such regulations as are reasonably necessary to enforce his functions under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1212, Sec. 93.)
