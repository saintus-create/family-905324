# LAB § 1160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 6. Prevention of Unfair Labor Practices and Judicial Review and Enforcement

**Citation:** LAB § 1160

---

## Text

The board is empowered, as provided in this chapter, to prevent any person from engaging in any unfair labor practice, as set forth in Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 1153) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
