# LAB § 7952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Tunnels and Mines

**Citation:** LAB § 7952

---

## Text

There shall be within the division a separate unit of safety engineers trained to inspect all tunnel construction and mine operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1430.)
