# LAB § 1698.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Farm Labor Contractors

**Citation:** LAB § 1698.6

---

## Text

No licensee shall knowingly permit any persons of bad character, prostitutes, gamblers, intoxicated persons, or procurers to frequent his premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1505.)
