# LAB § 1107.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5.1. Whistleblower Protections: Catastrophic Risks in AI Foundation Models

**Citation:** LAB § 1107.2

---

## Text

The loss of value of equity does not count as damage to or loss of property for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 138, Sec. 4. (SB 53) Effective January 1, 2026.)
