# LAB § 974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Solicitation of Employees by Misrepresentation

**Citation:** LAB § 974

---

## Text

Any person, or agent or officer thereof, who violates Section 973 is guilty of a misdemeanor.
