# LAB § 1026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3.7. Alcohol and Drug Rehabilitation

**Citation:** LAB § 1026

---

## Text

The employer shall make reasonable efforts to safeguard the privacy of the employee as to the fact that he or she has enrolled in an alcohol or drug rehabilitation program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 506, Sec. 3.)
