# LAB § 1104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5. Political Affiliations

**Citation:** LAB § 1104

---

## Text

In all prosecutions under this chapter, the employer is responsible for the acts of his managers, officers, agents, and employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
