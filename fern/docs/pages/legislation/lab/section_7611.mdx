# LAB § 7611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 5. SHIPS AND VESSELS > Chapter: 3. Underground Telephones

**Citation:** LAB § 7611

---

## Text

Nothing in the foregoing sections of this part shall limit the authority of the division to prescribe or enforce general or special safety orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 398.)
