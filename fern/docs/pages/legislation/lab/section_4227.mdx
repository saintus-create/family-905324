# LAB § 4227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Economic Opportunity Programs > Article: 3. Adjustment of Claims

**Citation:** LAB § 4227

---

## Text

If the United States government or any agent thereof furnishes medical treatment to an injured enrollee, the enrollee will have no right to receive the same or similar treatment under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1685.)
