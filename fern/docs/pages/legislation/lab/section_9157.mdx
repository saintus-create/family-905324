# LAB § 9157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 13. SAFETY IN MOTION PICTURE PRODUCTIONS > Chapter: 1. Working Warehouses

**Citation:** LAB § 9157

---

## Text

An employer shall comply with this part and any applicable safety standard.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 56, Sec. 3. (SB 132) Effective July 10, 2023. Operative January 1, 2025, pursuant to Section 9161.)
