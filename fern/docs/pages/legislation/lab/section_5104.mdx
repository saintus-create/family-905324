# LAB § 5104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Lump Sum Payments

**Citation:** LAB § 5104

---

## Text

In the appointment of the trustee, preference may be given to the choice of the injured employee or his dependents.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
