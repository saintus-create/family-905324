# LAB § 3851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Subrogation of Employer

**Citation:** LAB § 3851

---

## Text

The death of the employee or of any other person, does not abate any right of action established by this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
