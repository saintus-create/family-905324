# LAB § 6149

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE: STATE EMPLOYEES NOT OTHERWISE COVERED > Chapter: 4. Benefits and Procedure

**Citation:** LAB § 6149

---

## Text

Nothing shall preclude an employee from negotiating the agreement mentioned in Sections 6147 and 6148 prior to the occurrence of injury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 45.)
