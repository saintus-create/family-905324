# LAB § 251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Payment of Wages > Article: 2. Seasonal Labor

**Citation:** LAB § 251

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to wages earned by seamen or other persons, where payment is regulated by Federal statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
