# LAB § 180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Enforcement

**Citation:** LAB § 180

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public prosecutor” means the Attorney General, a district attorney, a city attorney, a county counsel, or any other city or county prosecutor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 659, Sec. 2. (AB 594) Effective January 1, 2024.)
