# LAB § 7154.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 2. Scaffolding

**Citation:** LAB § 7154.1

---

## Text

The use of lean-to scaffolds, sometimes known as jack scaffolds, as support for scaffolds is hereby prohibited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 257.)
