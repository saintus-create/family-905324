# LAB § 2859

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 3. Obligations of Employee

**Citation:** LAB § 2859

---

## Text

An employee is always bound to use such skill as he possesses, so far as the same is required, for the service specified.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
