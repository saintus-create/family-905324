# LAB § 7907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 8. AMUSEMENT RIDES SAFETY LAW > Chapter: 2. Process Safety Management Standards

**Citation:** LAB § 7907

---

## Text

If, after inspection, an amusement ride is found to comply with the rules and regulations of the division, the division or a public entity shall issue a permit to operate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1113.)
