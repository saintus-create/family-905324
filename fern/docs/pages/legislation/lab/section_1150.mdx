# LAB § 1150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Agricultural Labor Relations Board > Article: 1. Agricultural Labor Relations Board: Organization

**Citation:** LAB § 1150

---

## Text

Each member of the board and the general counsel of the board shall be eligible for reappointment, and shall not engage in any other business, vocation, or employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
