# LAB § 7107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 1. Floors and Walls

**Citation:** LAB § 7107

---

## Text

Planked floors on buildings shall be tightly laid together of proper thickness, grade and span to carry the working load; such working load to be assumed as at least 25 pounds per square foot.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1498.)
