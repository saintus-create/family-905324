# LAB § 6392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Hazardous Substances Information and Training > Article: 4. Duties

**Citation:** LAB § 6392

---

## Text

Provision of a federal Material Safety Data Sheet or equivalent shall constitute prima facie proof of compliance with Section 6390.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1214, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
