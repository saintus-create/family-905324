# LAB § 3603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Conditions of Compensation Liability

**Citation:** LAB § 3603

---

## Text

Payment of compensation in accordance with the order and direction of the appeals board shall discharge the employer from all claims therefor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 438.)
