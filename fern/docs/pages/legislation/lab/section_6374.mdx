# LAB § 6374

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Hazardous Substances Information and Training > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 6374

---

## Text

“MSDS” means a material safety data sheet prepared pursuant to Section 6390. A label in 8-point or larger type, prepared pursuant to Section 6390, shall constitute an MSDS for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 874.)
