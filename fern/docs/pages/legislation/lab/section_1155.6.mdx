# LAB § 1155.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Unfair Labor Practices and Regulation of Secondary Boycotts

**Citation:** LAB § 1155.6

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 1155.4 or 1155.5 shall apply to any matter set forth in subsection (c) of Section 186 of Title 29 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
