# LAB § 3210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 3210

---

## Text

“Person” includes an individual, firm, voluntary association, or a public, quasi public, or private corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
