# LAB § 18

**Citation:** LAB § 18

---

## Text

“Person” means any person, association, organization, partnership, business trust, limited liability company, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 178. Effective January 1, 1995.)
