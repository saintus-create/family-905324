# LAB § 1184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Wages, Hours and Working Conditions

**Citation:** LAB § 1184

---

## Text

Any action taken by the commission pursuant to Section 1182 shall be effective on the first day of the succeeding January or July and not less than 60 days from the date of publication pursuant to Section 1182.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 150, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
