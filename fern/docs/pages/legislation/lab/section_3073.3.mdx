# LAB § 3073.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Apprenticeship and Preapprenticeship > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** LAB § 3073.3

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the Department of Industrial Relations will encourage greater participation for women, ethnic minorities, and the disabled in programs administered pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 164, Sec. 2. (AB 1019) Effective January 1, 2020.)
