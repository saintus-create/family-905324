# LAB § 7726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 4. Inspection Fees

**Citation:** LAB § 7726

---

## Text

All inspection fees shall be paid before the issuance of a permit.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 7723 by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1530.)
