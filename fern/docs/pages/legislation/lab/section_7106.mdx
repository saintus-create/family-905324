# LAB § 7106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 1. Floors and Walls

**Citation:** LAB § 7106

---

## Text

Where a building is being constructed in sections each section constitutes a building for the purpose of this article.
