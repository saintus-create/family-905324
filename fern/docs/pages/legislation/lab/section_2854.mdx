# LAB § 2854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 3. Obligations of Employee

**Citation:** LAB § 2854

---

## Text

One who, for a good consideration, agrees to serve another, shall perform the service, and shall use ordinary care and diligence therein, so long as he is thus employed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
