# LAB § 1684.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Farm Labor Contractors

**Citation:** LAB § 1684.5

---

## Text

The Labor Commissioner shall quarterly submit to the Department of the California Highway Patrol a list of all licensees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 917, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
