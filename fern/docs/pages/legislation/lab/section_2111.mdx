# LAB § 2111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 8.6. Warehouse Distribution Centers > Chapter: 5. Reporting

**Citation:** LAB § 2111

---

## Text

This part does not preempt any city, county, or city and county ordinance that provides equal or greater protection to employees who are covered by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 197, Sec. 3. (AB 701) Effective January 1, 2022.)
