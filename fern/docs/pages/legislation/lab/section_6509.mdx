# LAB § 6509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 6. Permit Requirements

**Citation:** LAB § 6509

---

## Text

Any person, or agent or officer thereof, who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 33.)
