# LAB § 6384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Hazardous Substances Information and Training > Article: 3. Hazardous Substances

**Citation:** LAB § 6384

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to impurities which develop as intermediate materials during chemical processing but are not present in the final product, and to which employee exposure is unlikely.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 874.)
