# LAB § 2106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 8.6. Warehouse Distribution Centers > Chapter: 5. Reporting

**Citation:** LAB § 2106

---

## Text

Upon receiving a complaint regarding a violation of this part, a state or local enforcement entity may request or subpoena the records of warehouse distribution center quotas and employee work speed data.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 197, Sec. 3. (AB 701) Effective January 1, 2022.)
