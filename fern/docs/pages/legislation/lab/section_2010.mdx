# LAB § 2010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Extension of Public Works

**Citation:** LAB § 2010

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “State agency” means any department, division, board, bureau, or commission of the State.
