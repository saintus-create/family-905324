# LAB § 409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Privileges and Perquisites > Article: 2. Bonds and Photographs

**Citation:** LAB § 409

---

## Text

All fines imposed and collected under this article shall be paid into the State treasury and credited to the general fund.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
