# LAB § 9253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 14. Safety on Staging for Live Events > Chapter: 1. Working Warehouses

**Citation:** LAB § 9253

---

## Text

This part, or any related health and safety standard, does not prevent or limit an employer, contracting entity, or entertainment events vendor from adopting stricter safety standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 759, Sec. 2. (AB 1775) Effective January 1, 2023.)
