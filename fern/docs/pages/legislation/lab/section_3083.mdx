# LAB § 3083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Apprenticeship and Preapprenticeship > Article: 2. Apprenticeship Programs

**Citation:** LAB § 3083

---

## Text

All findings of fact in a final determination or decision issued pursuant to Section 3082 shall be conclusive if supported by substantial evidence, and all orders and decisions shall be prima facie lawful and reasonable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 704, Sec. 43. (AB 235) Effective September 22, 2018.)
