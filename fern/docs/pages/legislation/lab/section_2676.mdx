# LAB § 2676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** LAB § 2676

---

## Text

Any person engaged in the business of garment manufacturing who is not registered is guilty of a misdemeanor, except as provided in subdivision (d) of Section 2678.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 276, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
