# LAB § 1705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4.5. Fee-Related Talent Services > Article: 5. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 1705

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are not exclusive and do not relieve a person subject to this chapter from the duty to comply with all other laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 286, Sec. 3. (AB 1319) Effective January 1, 2010.)
