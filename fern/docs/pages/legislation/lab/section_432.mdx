# LAB § 432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Privileges and Perquisites > Article: 3. Contracts and Applications for Employment

**Citation:** LAB § 432

---

## Text

If an employee or applicant signs any instrument relating to the obtaining or holding of employment, he shall be given a copy of the instrument upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 714.)
