# LAB § 1138.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 10. Unlawful Acts During Labor Disputes

**Citation:** LAB § 1138.4

---

## Text

The term “labor dispute” as used in this chapter has the same meaning as set forth in clauses (i), (ii), and (iii) of paragraph (4) of subdivision (b) of Section 527.3 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 616, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
