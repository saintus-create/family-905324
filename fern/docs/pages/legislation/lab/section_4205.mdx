# LAB § 4205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Economic Opportunity Programs > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 4205

---

## Text

“Participating agency” means any agency, entity or institution, public or private, taking part in an economic opportunity program, other than a sponsoring agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1685.)
