# LAB § 7259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 4. Structural Steel Framed Buildings

**Citation:** LAB § 7259

---

## Text

Floor planks that are temporarily removed for any reason whatsoever shall be replaced as soon as work requiring their removal is completed or the open area shall be properly guarded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1498.)
