# LAB § 2352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Sanitary Conditions > Article: 3. Factories and Business Establishments

**Citation:** LAB § 2352

---

## Text

No place which the Labor Commissioner condemns as unhealthy and unsuitable, shall be used as a place of employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1943, Ch. 486.)
