# LAB § 4214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Economic Opportunity Programs > Article: 2. Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4214

---

## Text

In addition to death benefit in the event of fatal injury, the reasonable expenses of the enrollee’s burial shall be paid not to exceed six hundred dollars ($600).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1685.)
