# LAB § 1157.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5. Labor Representatives and Elections

**Citation:** LAB § 1157.3

---

## Text

Employers shall maintain accurate and current payroll lists containing the names and addresses of all their employees, and shall make such lists available to the board upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
