# LAB § 7928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 8.1. PERMANENT AMUSEMENT RIDE SAFETY INSPECTION PROGRAM > Chapter: 2. Process Safety Management Standards

**Citation:** LAB § 7928

---

## Text

The division shall adopt rules and regulations necessary for the administration of this part. The division may employ qualified safety inspectors as necessary for the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 585, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
