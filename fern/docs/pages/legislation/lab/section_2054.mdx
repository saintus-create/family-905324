# LAB § 2054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** LAB § 2054

---

## Text

Every employer shall register with the commissioner annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 825, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
