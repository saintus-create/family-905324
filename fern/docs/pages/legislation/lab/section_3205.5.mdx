# LAB § 3205.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 3205.5

---

## Text

“Appeals board” means the Workers’ Compensation Appeals Board of the Division of Workers’ Compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1097, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1995.)
