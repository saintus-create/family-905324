# LAB § 1702.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4.5. Fee-Related Talent Services > Article: 2. Advance-Fee Talent Representation Service

## Text

A person who violates Section 1702 is subject to the provisions of Article 4 (commencing with Section 1704).
