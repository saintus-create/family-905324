# LAB § 6372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Hazardous Substances Information and Training > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 6372

---

## Text

“Manufacturer” means a person who produces, synthesizes, extracts, or otherwise makes a hazardous substance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 874.)
