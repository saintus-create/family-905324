# LAB § 1698.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Farm Labor Contractors

**Citation:** LAB § 1698.8

---

## Text

No licensee shall divide fees with an employer, an agent or other employee of an employer or person to whom help is furnished.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1505.)
