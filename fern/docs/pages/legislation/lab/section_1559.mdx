# LAB § 1559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 5.6. WORKPLACE KNOW YOUR RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Domestic Work Employee Rights

**Citation:** LAB § 1559

---

## Text

This part does not preempt any city, county, or city and county ordinance that provides equal or greater protection to employees who are covered by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 667, Sec. 1. (SB 294) Effective January 1, 2026.)
