# LAB § 1703.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4.5. Fee-Related Talent Services > Article: 3. Other Talent Services

**Citation:** LAB § 1703.5

---

## Text

No talent scout shall use the same name as used by any other talent scout soliciting for the same talent service, and no talent service shall permit a talent scout to use the same name as used by any other talent scout soliciting for the talent service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 286, Sec. 3. (AB 1319) Effective January 1, 2010.)
