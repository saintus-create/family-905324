# LAB § 4854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 7. City Police and Firemen, Sheriffs, and Others

**Citation:** LAB § 4854

---

## Text

No disability indemnity shall be paid to any such officer or employee concurrently with wages or salary payments.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1378.)
