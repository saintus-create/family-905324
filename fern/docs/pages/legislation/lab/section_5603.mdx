# LAB § 5603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Attachments

**Citation:** LAB § 5603

---

## Text

In levying attachments preference shall be given to the real property of the employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
