# LAB § 3209.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 3209.4

---

## Text

The inclusion of optometrists in Section 3209.3 does not imply any right or entitle any optometrist to represent, advertise, or hold himself out as a physician.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1404.)
