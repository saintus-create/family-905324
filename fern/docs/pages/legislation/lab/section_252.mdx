# LAB § 252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Payment of Wages > Article: 2. Seasonal Labor

**Citation:** LAB § 252

---

## Text

Upon application of either the employer or the employee, the wages earned in seasonal labor shall be paid in the presence of the Labor Commissioner, or his deputy or agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
