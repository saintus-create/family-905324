# LAB § 1151.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Agricultural Labor Relations Board > Article: 2. Investigatory Powers

**Citation:** LAB § 1151.3

---

## Text

Any party shall have the right to appear at any hearing in person, by counsel, or by other representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
