# LAB § 4810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 6. Special Payments to Certain Persons

**Citation:** LAB § 4810

---

## Text

No disability indemnity shall be paid to such member of the University of California Police Department as temporary disability concurrently with wages or salary payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 918.)
