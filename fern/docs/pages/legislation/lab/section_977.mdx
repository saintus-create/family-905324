# LAB § 977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Solicitation of Employees by Misrepresentation

**Citation:** LAB § 977

---

## Text

Any person, or agent or officer thereof, who violates Section 976 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1583.)
