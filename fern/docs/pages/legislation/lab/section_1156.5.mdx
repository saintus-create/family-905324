# LAB § 1156.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5. Labor Representatives and Elections

**Citation:** LAB § 1156.5

---

## Text

(a) The board shall not direct an election in any bargaining unit where a valid election has been held in the immediately preceding 12-month period.

(b) This section shall be operative January 1, 2028.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 10) and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 7, Sec. 11. (AB 113) Effective May 15, 2023. Operative January 1, 2028, by its own provisions.)
