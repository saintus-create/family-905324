# LAB § 1010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Class of Labor Employed; Labor Union Insignia

**Citation:** LAB § 1010

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “label” includes label, imprint, trade-mark, tag, stamp, inscription, or other device.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
