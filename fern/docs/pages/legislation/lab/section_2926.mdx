# LAB § 2926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 4. Termination of Employment

**Citation:** LAB § 2926

---

## Text

An employee who is not employed for a specified term and who is dismissed by his employer is entitled to compensation for services rendered up to the time of such dismissal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
