# LAB § 880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 7. Emergency Ambulance Services > Article: 1. Title

**Citation:** LAB § 880

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the Emergency Ambulance Employee Safety and Preparedness Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 2018, by initiative Proposition 11, Sec. 1. Effective December 19, 2018.)
