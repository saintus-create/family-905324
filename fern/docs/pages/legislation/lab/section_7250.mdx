# LAB § 7250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 4. Structural Steel Framed Buildings

**Citation:** LAB § 7250

---

## Text

As used in this article “building” means any multifloor structural steel framed building more than two stories high in the course of construction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1498.)
