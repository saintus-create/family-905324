# LAB § 1695.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Farm Labor Contractors

**Citation:** LAB § 1695.6

---

## Text

No person shall knowingly enter into an agreement for the services of a farm labor contractor who is not licensed under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 803.)
