# LAB § 1500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 5. Civil Air Patrol > Chapter: 2. Domestic Work Employee Rights

**Citation:** LAB § 1500

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Civil Air Patrol Employment Protection Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 242, Sec. 1. (AB 485) Effective January 1, 2010.)
