# LAB § 452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Privileges and Perquisites > Article: 4. Purchases

**Citation:** LAB § 452

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall prohibit an employer from prescribing the weight, color, quality, texture, style, form and make of uniforms required to be worn by his employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
