# LAB § 6405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Responsibilities and Duties of Employers and Employees

**Citation:** LAB § 6405

---

## Text

No employer, owner, or lessee of any real property shall construct or cause to be constructed any place of employment that is not safe and healthful.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 993.)
