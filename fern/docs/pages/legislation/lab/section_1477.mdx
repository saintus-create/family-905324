# LAB § 1477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 4.5.5. Fast Food > Chapter: 2. Domestic Work Employee Rights

**Citation:** LAB § 1477

---

## Text

Sections 1474 to 1476, inclusive, shall become operative and shall take effect commencing January 1, 2024, only if Referendum No. 1939 (Attorney General No. 22-0005) has been withdrawn by its proponents by January 1, 2024. If that referendum has not been withdrawn by its proponents by January 1, 2024, Sections 1474 to 1476, inclusive, and this section shall become inoperative on January 1, 2024, and as of that date, are repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 262, Sec. 3. (AB 1228) Effective January 1, 2024.)
