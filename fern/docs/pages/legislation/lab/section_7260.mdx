# LAB § 7260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 4. Structural Steel Framed Buildings

**Citation:** LAB § 7260

---

## Text

Prior to removal of temporary floor plank, employees shall be instructed by assigned supervision the steps to be taken to perform the work safely and in proper sequence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1498.)
