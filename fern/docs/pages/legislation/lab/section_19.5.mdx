# LAB § 19.5

**Citation:** LAB § 19.5

---

## Text

“Secretary” means the Secretary of Labor and Workforce Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 859, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003.)
