# LAB § 88

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Division of Labor Standards Enforcement

**Citation:** LAB § 88

---

## Text

The personnel records of all employees transferred pursuant to Section 87 shall remain in the Department of Industrial Relations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 746.)
