# LAB § 1180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Wages, Hours and Working Conditions

**Citation:** LAB § 1180

---

## Text

The proceedings and deliberations of the wage board shall be made a matter of record for the use of the commission, and shall be admissible as evidence in any proceedings before the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1181 by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1454.)
