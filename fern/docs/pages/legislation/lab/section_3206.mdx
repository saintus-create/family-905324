# LAB § 3206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 3206

---

## Text

“Administrative director” means the Director of the Division of Workers’ Compensation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1097, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1995.)
