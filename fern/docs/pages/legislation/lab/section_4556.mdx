# LAB § 4556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 4556

---

## Text

The increases provided for by this article shall not be limited by the provisions of Chapter 1 of this part relating to maximum amounts in the computation of average earnings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 520.)
