# LAB § 1721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Public Works > Article: 1. Scope and Operation

**Citation:** LAB § 1721

---

## Text

“Political subdivision” includes any county, city, district, public housing authority, or public agency of the state, and assessment or improvement districts.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 239, Sec. 1.)
