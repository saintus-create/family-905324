# LAB § 1706.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5. Child Performer Services Permits

**Citation:** LAB § 1706.4

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are not exclusive and do not relieve any person subject to this chapter from the duty to comply with all other laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 634, Sec. 1. (AB 1660) Effective January 1, 2013.)
