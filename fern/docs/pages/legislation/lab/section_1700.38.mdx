# LAB § 1700.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Talent Agencies > Article: 3. Operation and Management

**Citation:** LAB § 1700.38

---

## Text

No talent agency shall knowingly secure employment for an artist in any place where a strike, lockout, or other labor trouble exists, without notifying the artist of such conditions.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1382.)
