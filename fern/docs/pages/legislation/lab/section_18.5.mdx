# LAB § 18.5

**Citation:** LAB § 18.5

---

## Text

“Agency” means the Labor and Workforce Development Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 859, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2003.)
