# LAB § 1115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 7. Jurisdictional Strikes

**Citation:** LAB § 1115

---

## Text

A jurisdictional strike as herein defined is hereby declared to be against the public policy of the State of California and is hereby declared to be unlawful.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1388.)
