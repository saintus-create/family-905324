# LAB § 3072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Apprenticeship and Preapprenticeship > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** LAB § 3072

---

## Text

The Director of Industrial Relations is ex officio the Administrator of Apprenticeship and is authorized to appoint assistants as necessary to effectuate the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 100. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012.)
