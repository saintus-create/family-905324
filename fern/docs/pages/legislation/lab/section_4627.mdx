# LAB § 4627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 2.5. Medical-Legal Expenses

**Citation:** LAB § 4627

---

## Text

The board and the administrative director may promulgate such reasonable rules and regulations as may be necessary to interpret this article and compel compliance with its provisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 596, Sec. 4. Effective July 19, 1984.)
