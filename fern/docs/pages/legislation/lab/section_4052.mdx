# LAB § 4052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Medical Examinations

**Citation:** LAB § 4052

---

## Text

The employee may employ at his own expense a physician, to be present at any examination required by his employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
