# LAB § 7153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 2. Scaffolding

**Citation:** LAB § 7153

---

## Text

Platforms or floors of such scaffolding shall be not less than 14 inches in width and shall be free from knots or fractures impairing their strength.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 700.)
