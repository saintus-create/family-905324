# LAB § 4415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Asbestos Workers’ Account > Article: 3. Collections

**Citation:** LAB § 4415

---

## Text

In any hearing or proceeding, the Director of Industrial Relations may use attorneys from within the department, or the Attorney General, to represent the director and the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1041. Provisions inoperative January 1, 1989, as prescribed in Section 4418.)
