# LAB § 1509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 5.5. ORGAN AND BONE MARROW DONATION > Chapter: 2. Domestic Work Employee Rights

**Citation:** LAB § 1509

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Employee” and “employee benefits” have the same meanings set forth in Section 1501.

(b) “Employer” means any person, partnership, corporation, association, or other business entity that employs 15 or more employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 296, Sec. 195. (AB 1023) Effective January 1, 2012.)
