# LAB § 2852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 3. Obligations of Employee

**Citation:** LAB § 2852

---

## Text

A gratuitous employee, who accepts a written power of attorney, shall act under it so long as it remains in force, or until he gives notice to his employer that he will not do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
