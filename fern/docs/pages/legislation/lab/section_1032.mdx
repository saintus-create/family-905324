# LAB § 1032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3.8. Lactation Accommodation

**Citation:** LAB § 1032

---

## Text

An employer is not required to provide break time under this chapter if to do so would seriously disrupt the operations of the employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 821, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
