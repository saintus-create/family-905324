# LAB § 138.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 5. Division of Workers’ Compensation

**Citation:** LAB § 138.1

---

## Text

The administrative director shall be appointed by the Governor with the advice and consent of the Senate and shall hold office at the pleasure of the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 83, Sec. 4. (SB 171) Effective July 13, 2026.)
