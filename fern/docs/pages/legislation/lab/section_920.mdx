# LAB § 920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Contracts Against Public Policy

**Citation:** LAB § 920

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise indicates, “promise” includes promise, undertaking, contract, or agreement, whether written or oral, express or implied.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
