# LAB § 4820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 6. Special Payments to Certain Persons

**Citation:** LAB § 4820

---

## Text

No disability indemnity shall be paid to a member of the California State University Police Department as temporary disability concurrently with wages or salary payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 50, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
