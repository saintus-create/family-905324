# LAB § 2863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 3. Obligations of Employee

**Citation:** LAB § 2863

---

## Text

An employee who has any business to transact on his own account, similar to that intrusted to him by his employer, shall always give the preference to the business of the employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
