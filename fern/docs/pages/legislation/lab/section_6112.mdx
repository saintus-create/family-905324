# LAB § 6112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE: STATE EMPLOYEES NOT OTHERWISE COVERED > Chapter: 2. Direct Payments

**Citation:** LAB § 6112

---

## Text

The master agreement shall provide for the rendition of services at a uniform rate to all State agencies.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 45.)
