# LAB § 7258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 4. Structural Steel Framed Buildings

**Citation:** LAB § 7258

---

## Text

Metal decking where used in lieu of wood planking shall be of equivalent strength and shall be laid tightly and secured to prevent movement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1498.)
