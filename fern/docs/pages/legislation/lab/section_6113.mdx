# LAB § 6113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE: STATE EMPLOYEES NOT OTHERWISE COVERED > Chapter: 2. Direct Payments

**Citation:** LAB § 6113

---

## Text

The fund may make all expenditures, including payments to claimants for medical care or for adjustment or settlement of claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 45.)
