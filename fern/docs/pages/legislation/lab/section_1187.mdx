# LAB § 1187

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Wages, Hours and Working Conditions

**Citation:** LAB § 1187

---

## Text

The findings of fact made by the commission are, in the absence of fraud, conclusive.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
