# LAB § 9007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 9007

---

## Text

“Standards” means standards and orders adopted by the standards board pursuant to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 140) of Division 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 947, Sec. 2.)
