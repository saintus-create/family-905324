# LAB § 3357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Employers, Employees, and Dependents > Article: 2. Employees

**Citation:** LAB § 3357

---

## Text

Any person rendering service for another, other than as an independent contractor, or unless expressly excluded herein, is presumed to be an employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
