# LAB § 221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Payment of Wages > Article: 1. General Occupations

**Citation:** LAB § 221

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any employer to collect or receive from an employee any part of wages theretofore paid by said employer to said employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 357.)
