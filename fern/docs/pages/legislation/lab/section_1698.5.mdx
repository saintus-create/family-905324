# LAB § 1698.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Farm Labor Contractors

**Citation:** LAB § 1698.5

---

## Text

No licensee shall send any minor to any saloon or place where intoxicating liquors are sold to be consumed on the premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 579.)
