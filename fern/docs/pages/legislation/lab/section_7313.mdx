# LAB § 7313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Elevators, Escalators, Platform and Stairway Chair Lifts, Dumbwaiters, Moving Walks, Automated People Movers, and Other Conveyances

**Citation:** LAB § 7313

---

## Text

Each conveyance inspector shall, within 21 days after he or she makes an inspection, forward to the division on forms provided by it, a report of the inspection. Failure to comply with this section shall be grounds for the division to cancel his or her certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 503, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2005.)
