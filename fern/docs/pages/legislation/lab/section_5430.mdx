# LAB § 5430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.3. Workers’ Compensation—Truth in Advertising

**Citation:** LAB § 5430

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Workers’ Compensation Truth in Advertising Act of 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 904, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
