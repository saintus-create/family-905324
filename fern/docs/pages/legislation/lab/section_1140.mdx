# LAB § 1140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 1140

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be referred to as the Alatorre-Zenovich-Dunlap-Berman Agricultural Labor Relations Act of 1975.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
