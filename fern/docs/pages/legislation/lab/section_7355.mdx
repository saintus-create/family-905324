# LAB § 7355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 4. Passenger Tramways

**Citation:** LAB § 7355

---

## Text

Nothing in the foregoing sections of this chapter shall limit the authority of the division to prescribe or enforce general or special safety orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1047.)
