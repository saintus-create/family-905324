# LAB § 4055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Medical Examinations

**Citation:** LAB § 4055

---

## Text

Any physician who makes or is present at any such examination may be required to report or testify as to the results thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
