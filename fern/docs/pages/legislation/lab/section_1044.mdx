# LAB § 1044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3.9. Employee Literacy Assistance

**Citation:** LAB § 1044

---

## Text

An employee who reveals a problem of illiteracy and who satisfactorily performs his or her work shall not be subject to termination of employment because of the disclosure of illiteracy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 339, Sec. 2.)
