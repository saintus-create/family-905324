# LAB § 1700.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Talent Agencies > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** LAB § 1700.15

---

## Text

A talent agency shall also deposit with the Labor Commissioner, prior to the issuance or renewal of a license, a surety bond in the penal sum of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 46, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
