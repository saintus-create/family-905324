# LAB § 1773.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Public Works > Article: 2. Wages

**Citation:** LAB § 1773.7

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 11250 of the Government Code shall not be applicable to Sections 1773, 1773.4, and 1773.6.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 281.)
