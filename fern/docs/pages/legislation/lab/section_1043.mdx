# LAB § 1043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3.9. Employee Literacy Assistance

**Citation:** LAB § 1043

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to require an employer to provide time off with pay for an employee to enroll and participate in an adult literacy education program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 339, Sec. 2.)
