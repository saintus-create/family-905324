# LAB § 7686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Operation of Tanks and Boilers

**Citation:** LAB § 7686

---

## Text

Upon good cause being shown therefor, and after notice and an opportunity to be heard, the division may revoke any permit.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1142.)
