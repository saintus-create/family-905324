# LAB § 6409.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Responsibilities and Duties of Employers and Employees

**Citation:** LAB § 6409.3

---

## Text

In no case shall the treatment administered for pesticide poisoning or a condition suspected as pesticide poisoning be deemed to be first aid treatment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 889.)
