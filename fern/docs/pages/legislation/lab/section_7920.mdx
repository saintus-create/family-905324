# LAB § 7920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 8.1. PERMANENT AMUSEMENT RIDE SAFETY INSPECTION PROGRAM > Chapter: 2. Process Safety Management Standards

**Citation:** LAB § 7920

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this part to create a state system for the inspection of permanent amusement rides. This part shall be known and may be cited as the Permanent Amusement Ride Safety Inspection Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 585, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
