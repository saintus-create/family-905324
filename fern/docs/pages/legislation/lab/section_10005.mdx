# LAB § 10005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. THE PROMOTE OWNERSHIP BY WORKERS FOR ECONOMIC RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 10005

---

## Text

For purposes of this division, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Association” means the Association of Cooperative Labor Contractors.

(b) “Secretary” means the Secretary of Labor and Workforce Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 808, Sec. 1. (AB 2849) Effective January 1, 2023.)
