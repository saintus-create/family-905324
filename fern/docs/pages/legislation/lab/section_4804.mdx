# LAB § 4804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 6. Special Payments to Certain Persons

**Citation:** LAB § 4804

---

## Text

No disability indemnity shall be paid to said member of the California Highway Patrol or harbor policeman as temporary disability concurrently with wages or salary payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1553.)
