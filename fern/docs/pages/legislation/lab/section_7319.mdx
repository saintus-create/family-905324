# LAB § 7319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Elevators, Escalators, Platform and Stairway Chair Lifts, Dumbwaiters, Moving Walks, Automated People Movers, and Other Conveyances

**Citation:** LAB § 7319

---

## Text

All elevators used for the carriage of passengers shall be provided with a suitable seat for the operator in charge. Failure to comply with this section is a misdemeanor punishable by a fine not exceeding fifty dollars ($50) for each offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 224. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
