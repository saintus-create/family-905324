# LAB § 4900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. Payment and Assignment

**Citation:** LAB § 4900

---

## Text

No claim for compensation, except as provided in Section 96, is assignable before payment, but this provision does not affect the survival thereof.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 555.)
