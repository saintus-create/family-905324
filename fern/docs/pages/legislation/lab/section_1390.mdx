# LAB § 1390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Working Hours

**Citation:** LAB § 1390

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise indicates:

(a) “Horticultural” includes the curing and drying but not the canning of all varieties of fruit.

(b) “Drama” or “play” includes the production of motion picture plays.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 561, Sec. 164. (AB 1516) Effective January 1, 2018.)
