# LAB § 6402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Responsibilities and Duties of Employers and Employees

**Citation:** LAB § 6402

---

## Text

No employer shall require, or permit any employee to go or be in any employment or place of employment which is not safe and healthful.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 993.)
