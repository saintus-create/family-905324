# LAB § 1423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 4.2. Property Service Workers Protection > Chapter: 4. Relocations, Terminations, and Mass Layoffs

**Citation:** LAB § 1423

---

## Text

Effective July 1, 2018, every employer shall register with the commissioner annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 373, Sec. 1. (AB 1978) Effective January 1, 2017.)
