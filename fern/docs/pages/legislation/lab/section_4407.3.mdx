# LAB § 4407.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Asbestos Workers’ Account > Article: 2. Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4407.3

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the death benefit shall be paid in installments in the same manner and amounts as temporary disability indemnity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1077, Sec. 6. Effective September 15, 1982. Provisions inoperative January 1, 1989, as prescribed in Section 4418.)
