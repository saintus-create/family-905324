# LAB § 80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Division of Labor Standards Enforcement

**Citation:** LAB § 80

---

## Text

The headquarters of the Division of Labor Standards Enforcement, hereafter in this chapter referred to as the division, shall be located in San Francisco.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 746.)
