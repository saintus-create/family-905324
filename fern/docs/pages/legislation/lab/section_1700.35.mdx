# LAB § 1700.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Talent Agencies > Article: 3. Operation and Management

**Citation:** LAB § 1700.35

---

## Text

No talent agency shall knowingly permit any persons of bad character, prostitutes, gamblers, intoxicated persons, or procurers to frequent, or be employed in, the place of business of the talent agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1382.)
