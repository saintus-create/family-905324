# LAB § 4727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 4.5. Public Official Death Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4727

---

## Text

Any person who is convicted of any crime in connection with the assassination of an elected public official shall not be eligible for any benefits pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 983.)
