# LAB § 1704.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4.5. Fee-Related Talent Services > Article: 4. Remedies

**Citation:** LAB § 1704.1

---

## Text

The Attorney General, a district attorney, or a city attorney may institute an action for a violation of this chapter, including an action to restrain and enjoin a violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 286, Sec. 3. (AB 1319) Effective January 1, 2010.)
