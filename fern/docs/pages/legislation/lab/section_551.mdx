# LAB § 551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** LAB § 551

---

## Text

Every person employed in any occupation of labor is entitled to one day’s rest therefrom in seven.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
