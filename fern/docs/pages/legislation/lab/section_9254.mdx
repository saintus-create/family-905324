# LAB § 9254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 14. Safety on Staging for Live Events > Chapter: 1. Working Warehouses

**Citation:** LAB § 9254

---

## Text

Nothing in this part relieves an employer from conducting any other training required under Title 8 of the California Code of Regulations and complying with any other occupational safety and health law or regulation, as applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 759, Sec. 2. (AB 1775) Effective January 1, 2023.)
