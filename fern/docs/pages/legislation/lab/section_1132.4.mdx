# LAB § 1132.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 8. Professional Strikebreakers > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 1132.4

---

## Text

“Employee” means any person who performs services for wages or salary under a contract of employment, express or implied, for an employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1079.)
