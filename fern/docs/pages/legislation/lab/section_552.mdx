# LAB § 552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** LAB § 552

---

## Text

No employer of labor shall cause his employees to work more than six days in seven.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
