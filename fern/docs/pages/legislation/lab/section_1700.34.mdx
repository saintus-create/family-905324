# LAB § 1700.34

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Talent Agencies > Article: 3. Operation and Management

**Citation:** LAB § 1700.34

---

## Text

No talent agency shall send any minor to any saloon or place where intoxicating liquors are sold to be consumed on the premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1382.)
