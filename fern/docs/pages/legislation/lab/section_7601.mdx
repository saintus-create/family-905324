# LAB § 7601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 5. SHIPS AND VESSELS > Chapter: 3. Underground Telephones

**Citation:** LAB § 7601

---

## Text

Handtrucks shall be maintained in a safe condition by the employer. Handles shall be maintained free of hazardous burrs, splinters, cracks or splits.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 398.)
