# LAB § 885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 7. Emergency Ambulance Services > Article: 4. Emergency Ambulance Employee Safety and Preparedness

**Citation:** LAB § 885

---

## Text

Meal and Rest Periods.

(a) All emergency ambulance employees are hereby entitled to meal and rest periods as prescribed elsewhere by the Industrial Welfare Commission.

(b) Emergency ambulance employees shall be compensated at their regular hourly rate of pay during meal and rest periods.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 2018, by initiative Proposition 11, Sec. 1. Effective December 19, 2018.)
