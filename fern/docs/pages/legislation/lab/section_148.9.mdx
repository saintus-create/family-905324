# LAB § 148.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Occupational Safety and Health Appeals Board

**Citation:** LAB § 148.9

---

## Text

Decisions of the appeals board shall be made by a majority of the appeals board, except as otherwise expressly provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 993.)
