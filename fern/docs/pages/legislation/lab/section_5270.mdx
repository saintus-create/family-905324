# LAB § 5270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Part: 3.5. ARBITRATION > Chapter: 3. Lump Sum Payments

**Citation:** LAB § 5270

---

## Text

This part shall not apply in cases where an injured employee or dependent is involved unless the employee or dependent is represented by an attorney.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1550, Sec. 49.)
