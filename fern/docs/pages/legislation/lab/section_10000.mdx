# LAB § 10000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. THE PROMOTE OWNERSHIP BY WORKERS FOR ECONOMIC RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 10000

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the Promote Ownership by Workers for Economic Recovery Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 808, Sec. 1. (AB 2849) Effective January 1, 2023.)
