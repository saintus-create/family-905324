# LAB § 7318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Elevators, Escalators, Platform and Stairway Chair Lifts, Dumbwaiters, Moving Walks, Automated People Movers, and Other Conveyances

**Citation:** LAB § 7318

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the authority of the division to prescribe or enforce general or special safety orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1149, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2003.)
