# LAB § 7377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 5. Cranes > Article: 2. Certification

**Citation:** LAB § 7377

---

## Text

Revocation of a license to certify may be appealed to the Director of Industrial Relations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1033, Sec. 1.)
