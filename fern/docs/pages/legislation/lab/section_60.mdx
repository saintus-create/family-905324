# LAB § 60

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** LAB § 60

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, the provisions of Divisions 4 and 4.5 of this code shall be administered and enforced by the Division of Workers’ Compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 137. Effective January 1, 1995.)
