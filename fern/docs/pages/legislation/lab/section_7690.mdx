# LAB § 7690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Operation of Tanks and Boilers

**Citation:** LAB § 7690

---

## Text

The order may be reheard by the division, or reviewed by the courts, in the manner specified by this code for safety orders, and not otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1142.)
