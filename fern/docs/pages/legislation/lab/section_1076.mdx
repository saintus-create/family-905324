# LAB § 1076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4.6. Public Transit Service Contracts and Contracts for the Collection and Transportation of Solid Waste

**Citation:** LAB § 1076

---

## Text

The amendments and additions to this chapter made by the act adding this section shall not apply to contracts awarded before January 1, 2017, or to contracts for which the bid process has been completed before January 1, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 874, Sec. 6. (AB 1669) Effective January 1, 2017.)
