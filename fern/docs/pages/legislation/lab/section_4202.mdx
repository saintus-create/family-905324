# LAB § 4202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Economic Opportunity Programs > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 4202

---

## Text

“Economic Opportunity Program” means any program adopted pursuant to Public Law 88-452, including, but not limited to, work training and work study.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1685.)
