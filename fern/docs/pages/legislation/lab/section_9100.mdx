# LAB § 9100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Working Warehouses

**Citation:** LAB § 9100

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “sales floor” means any area where the public is invited to shop, whether indoors or outdoors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 856, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
