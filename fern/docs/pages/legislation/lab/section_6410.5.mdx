# LAB § 6410.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Responsibilities and Duties of Employers and Employees

**Citation:** LAB § 6410.5

---

## Text

The reports required by subdivision (a) of Section 6409, subdivision (a) of Section 6409.1, and Section 6413 shall contain, prominently stated, the statement set forth in Section 5401.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 116, Sec. 34.)
