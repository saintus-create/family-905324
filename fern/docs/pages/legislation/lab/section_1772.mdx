# LAB § 1772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Public Works > Article: 2. Wages

**Citation:** LAB § 1772

---

## Text

Workers employed by contractors or subcontractors in the execution of any contract for public work are deemed to be employed upon public work.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1342, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
