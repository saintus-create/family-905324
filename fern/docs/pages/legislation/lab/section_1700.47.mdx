# LAB § 1700.47

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Talent Agencies > Article: 3. Operation and Management

**Citation:** LAB § 1700.47

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any licensee to refuse to represent any artist on account of that artist’s race, color, creed, sex, national origin, religion, or handicap.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 488, Sec. 18.)
