# LAB § 3077.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Apprenticeship and Preapprenticeship > Article: 2. Apprenticeship Programs

**Citation:** LAB § 3077.5

---

## Text

A program sponsor administering an apprenticeship program under this chapter shall not provide a maximum age for apprentices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 330, Sec. 7.)
