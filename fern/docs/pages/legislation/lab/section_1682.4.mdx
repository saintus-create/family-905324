# LAB § 1682.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Farm Labor Contractors

**Citation:** LAB § 1682.4

---

## Text

“Farm labor contractor” does not include a commercial packing house engaged in both the harvesting and the packing of citrus fruit or soft fruit for a client or customer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 662, Sec. 1.)
