# LAB § 2858

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 3. Obligations of Employee

**Citation:** LAB § 2858

---

## Text

An employee is bound to exercise a reasonable degree of skill, unless his employer has notice, before employing him, of his want of skill.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
