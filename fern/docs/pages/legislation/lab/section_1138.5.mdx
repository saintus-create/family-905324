# LAB § 1138.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 10. Unlawful Acts During Labor Disputes

**Citation:** LAB § 1138.5

---

## Text

Sections 1138.1, 1138.2, and 1138.3 shall not apply to any peace officer as defined in Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 830) of Title 3 of Part 2 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 616, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
