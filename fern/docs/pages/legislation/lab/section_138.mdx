# LAB § 138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 5. Division of Workers’ Compensation

**Citation:** LAB § 138

---

## Text

The administrative director may appoint a deputy to act when he or she is absent from the state due to official business, vacation, or illness.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 559, Sec. 8. (AB 1426) Effective October 7, 2011.)
