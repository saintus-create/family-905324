# LAB § 7602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 5. SHIPS AND VESSELS > Chapter: 3. Underground Telephones

**Citation:** LAB § 7602

---

## Text

Handtools shall be kept in good condition and be safely stored by the employer. Unsafe handtools shall not be used.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 398.)
