# LAB § 7653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** LAB § 7653

---

## Text

Upon good cause being shown therefor, the division may revoke a certificate of competency.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1142.)
