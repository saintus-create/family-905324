# LAB § 2518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 9.5. Grocery Workers > Chapter: 1. Sanitary Conditions

**Citation:** LAB § 2518

---

## Text

This part shall not be construed to limit an eligible grocery worker’s right to bring legal action for wrongful termination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 212, Sec. 1. (AB 359) Effective January 1, 2016.)
