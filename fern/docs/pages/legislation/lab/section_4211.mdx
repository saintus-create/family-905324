# LAB § 4211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Economic Opportunity Programs > Article: 2. Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4211

---

## Text

Where liability for compensation exists, such compensation shall be provided as limited by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1685.)
