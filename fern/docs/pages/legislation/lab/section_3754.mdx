# LAB § 3754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Compensation Insurance and Security > Article: 3. Insurance Rights and Privileges

**Citation:** LAB § 3754

---

## Text

Except as provided in paragraph (12) of subdivision (f) of Section 1202.4 of the Penal Code, payment, in whole or in part, of compensation by either the employer or the insurer shall, to the extent thereof, be a bar to recovery against each of them of the amount so paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 868, Sec. 2. (SB 1177) Effective January 1, 2013.)
