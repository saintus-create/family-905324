# LAB § 751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Smelters and Underground Workings

**Citation:** LAB § 751

---

## Text

In the case of an emergency where life or property is in imminent danger, the work shift may be extended during the continuance of the emergency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 903, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
