# LAB § 6359.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2.2. Artificial Stone; Occupational Exposure to Respirable Crystalline Silica

**Citation:** LAB § 6359.3

---

## Text

This part, or any related health and safety standard, does not prevent or limit an employer, contractor, or fabrication shop from adopting stricter safety standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 734, Sec. 3. (SB 20) Effective January 1, 2026.)
