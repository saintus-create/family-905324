# LAB § 6505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 6. Permit Requirements

**Citation:** LAB § 6505

---

## Text

The division may at any time, upon good cause being shown therefor, and after notice and an opportunity to be heard revoke any permit issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 993.)
