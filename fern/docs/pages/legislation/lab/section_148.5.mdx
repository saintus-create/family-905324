# LAB § 148.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Occupational Safety and Health Appeals Board

**Citation:** LAB § 148.5

---

## Text

A decision of the appeals board is final, except for any rehearing or judicial review as permitted by Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 6600) of Part 1 of Division 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 993.)
