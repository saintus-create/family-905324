# LAB § 4409.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Asbestos Workers’ Account > Article: 2. Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4409.5

---

## Text

The administrative director shall appoint workers’ compensation judges and support staff who shall give priority to the processing of the claims of asbestos workers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 326, Sec. 12. Provisions inoperative January 1, 1989, as prescribed in Section 4418.)
