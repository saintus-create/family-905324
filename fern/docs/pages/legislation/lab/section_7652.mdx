# LAB § 7652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** LAB § 7652

---

## Text

The division may determine by examination the competency of an applicant for a certificate of competency.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1142.)
