# LAB § 401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Privileges and Perquisites > Article: 2. Bonds and Photographs

**Citation:** LAB § 401

---

## Text

If a bond or photograph of an employee or applicant is required by any employer, the cost thereof shall be paid by the employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
