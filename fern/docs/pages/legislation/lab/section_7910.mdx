# LAB § 7910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 8. AMUSEMENT RIDES SAFETY LAW > Chapter: 2. Process Safety Management Standards

**Citation:** LAB § 7910

---

## Text

This part shall not be construed to prevent the use of any existing installation which upon inspection is found to be in a safe condition and in conformance with the rules and regulations of the division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1113.)
