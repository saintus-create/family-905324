# LAB § 4726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 4.5. Public Official Death Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4726

---

## Text

The Department of General Services and the Administrative Director of the Division of Workers’ Compensation shall jointly adopt rules and regulations as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 194. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
