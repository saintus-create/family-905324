# LAB § 7620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Scope of Chapter and General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 7620

## Text

“Division,” as used in this part, means the Division of Occupational Safety and Health.
