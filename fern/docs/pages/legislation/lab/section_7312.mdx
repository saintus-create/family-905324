# LAB § 7312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Elevators, Escalators, Platform and Stairway Chair Lifts, Dumbwaiters, Moving Walks, Automated People Movers, and Other Conveyances

**Citation:** LAB § 7312

---

## Text

The division may at any time, upon good cause being shown therefor, and after notice and an opportunity to be heard, revoke any permit to operate a conveyance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1149, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2003.)
