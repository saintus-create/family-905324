# LAB § 120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 5. Division of Workers’ Compensation

**Citation:** LAB § 120

---

## Text

The administrative director and the chairman of the appeals board may each respectively appoint a secretary and assistant secretaries to perform such services as shall be prescribed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1513.)
