# LAB § 1963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Firefighters

**Citation:** LAB § 1963

---

## Text

The enactment of this chapter shall not be construed as making the provisions of Section 923 of this code applicable to public employees.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 723.)
