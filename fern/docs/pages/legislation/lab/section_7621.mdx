# LAB § 7621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Scope of Chapter and General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 7621

---

## Text

“Boiler” as used in this part means any fired or unfired pressure vessel used to generate steam pressure by the application of heat subject to this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1530.)
