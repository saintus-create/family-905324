# LAB § 7976

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Gassy and Extrahazardous Tunnels

**Citation:** LAB § 7976

---

## Text

In any tunnel or underground mine classified as gassy, the division shall determine the number of fire extinguishers necessary and their locations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1430.)
