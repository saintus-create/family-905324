# LAB § 8002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Licensing and Penalties

**Citation:** LAB § 8002

---

## Text

All fees from applications shall be nonrefundable. Those fees shall be deposited into the Occupational Safety and Health Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 228. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
