# LAB § 7254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Buildings Under Construction or Repair > Article: 4. Structural Steel Framed Buildings

**Citation:** LAB § 7254

---

## Text

Temporary floors shall be wood planking of proper thickness, grade and span to carry the working load, but shall not be less than two inches thick, full size undressed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1498.)
