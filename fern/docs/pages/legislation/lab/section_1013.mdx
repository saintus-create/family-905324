# LAB § 1013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Class of Labor Employed; Labor Union Insignia

**Citation:** LAB § 1013

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “forge” means forge, reproduce, copy, imitate, or counterfeit.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
