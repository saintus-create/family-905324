# LAB § 4804.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 6. Special Payments to Certain Persons

**Citation:** LAB § 4804.5

---

## Text

No disability indemnity shall be paid to said member of a University of California fire department, specified in Section 4804.1, as temporary disability concurrently with wages or salary payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1149.)
