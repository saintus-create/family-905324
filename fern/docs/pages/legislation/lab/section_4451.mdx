# LAB § 4451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. Average Earnings

**Citation:** LAB § 4451

---

## Text

Average annual earnings shall be taken as fifty-two times the average weekly earnings referred to in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
