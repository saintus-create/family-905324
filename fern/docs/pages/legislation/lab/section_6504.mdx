# LAB § 6504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 6. Permit Requirements

**Citation:** LAB § 6504

---

## Text

Any employer issued a permit pursuant to this chapter shall post a copy or copies of the permit pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 6408.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 993.)
