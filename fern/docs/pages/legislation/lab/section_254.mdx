# LAB § 254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Payment of Wages > Article: 2. Seasonal Labor

**Citation:** LAB § 254

---

## Text

After a final hearing by the Labor Commissioner, he shall file in the office of his division a copy of the findings of fact and his award.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
