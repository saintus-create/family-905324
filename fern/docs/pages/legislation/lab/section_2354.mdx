# LAB § 2354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Sanitary Conditions > Article: 3. Factories and Business Establishments

**Citation:** LAB § 2354

---

## Text

Any person violating this article is guilty of a misdemeanor, punishable by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than six hundred dollars ($600), or by imprisonment in the county jail for not less than 30 days nor more than 90 days, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 216. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
