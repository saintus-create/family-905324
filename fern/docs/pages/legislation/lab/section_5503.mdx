# LAB § 5503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Applications and Answers

**Citation:** LAB § 5503

---

## Text

The person so applying shall be known as the applicant and the adverse party shall be known as the defendant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
