# LAB § 859

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 6. Agriculture

**Citation:** LAB § 859

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “employed in an agricultural occupation” has the same meaning as in Order No.14-2001 of the Industrial Welfare Commission (revised 07-2014).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 313, Sec. 2. (AB 1066) Effective January 1, 2017.)
