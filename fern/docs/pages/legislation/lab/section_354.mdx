# LAB § 354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Privileges and Perquisites > Article: 1. Gratuities

**Citation:** LAB § 354

---

## Text

Any employer who violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor, punishable by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000) or by imprisonment for not exceeding 60 days, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 190. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
