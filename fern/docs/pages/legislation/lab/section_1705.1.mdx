# LAB § 1705.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4.5. Fee-Related Talent Services > Article: 5. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 1705.1

---

## Text

The remedies provided in this chapter are not exclusive and shall be in addition to any other remedies or procedures provided in any other law, including Section 17500 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 286, Sec. 3. (AB 1319) Effective January 1, 2010.)
