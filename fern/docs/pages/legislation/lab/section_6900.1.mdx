# LAB § 6900.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Operation Personnel

**Citation:** LAB § 6900.1

---

## Text

This Act shall be known and cited as the Railroad Anti-Featherbedding Law of 1964.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 1964, by initiative Proposition 17. Note: Prop. 17 also includes Section 6900.5.)
