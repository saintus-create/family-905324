# LAB § 3758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Compensation Insurance and Security > Article: 3. Insurance Rights and Privileges

**Citation:** LAB § 3758

---

## Text

A proceeding to obtain compensation shall not abate on account of substitution of the insurer in place of the employer and on account of the dismissal of the employer, but shall be continued against such insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
