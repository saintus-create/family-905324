# LAB § 1154.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Unfair Labor Practices and Regulation of Secondary Boycotts

**Citation:** LAB § 1154.6

---

## Text

It shall be an unfair labor practice for an employer or labor organization, or their agents, willfully to arrange for persons to become employees for the primary purpose of voting in elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
