# LAB § 1706.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5. Child Performer Services Permits

**Citation:** LAB § 1706.5

---

## Text

The remedies provided in this chapter are not exclusive and are in addition to any other remedies or procedures provided in any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 634, Sec. 1. (AB 1660) Effective January 1, 2013.)
