# LAB § 7608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Part: 5. SHIPS AND VESSELS > Chapter: 3. Underground Telephones

**Citation:** LAB § 7608

## Text

Any person who violates any provisions of this part is guilty of a misdemeanor.
