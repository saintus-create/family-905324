# LAB § 1706.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 5. Child Performer Services Permits

**Citation:** LAB § 1706.2

---

## Text

The Attorney General, any district attorney, or any city attorney may institute an action for a violation of this chapter, including an action to restrain and enjoin a violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 634, Sec. 1. (AB 1660) Effective January 1, 2013.)
