# LAB § 4754.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 5. Subsequent Injuries Payments

**Citation:** LAB § 4754.3

---

## Text

(a) SIBTF shall not be liable for the payment of interest on any awards or order for payment of attorney’s fees.

(b) SIBTF shall not be liable for any penalties.

(c) The director, as administrator and trustee, shall pay the claimant only those benefits as allowed under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 83, Sec. 14. (SB 171) Effective July 13, 2026.)
