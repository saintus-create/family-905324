# LAB § 6145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE: STATE EMPLOYEES NOT OTHERWISE COVERED > Chapter: 4. Benefits and Procedure

**Citation:** LAB § 6145

---

## Text

The state, acting by or through any state agency, or when the consent of the opposing party is obtained, shall submit to the appeals board all controversies under this division susceptible of being arbitrated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1513.)
