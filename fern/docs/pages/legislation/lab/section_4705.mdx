# LAB § 4705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 4. Death Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4705

---

## Text

The person to whom the death benefit is paid for the use of the several beneficiaries shall apply it in compliance with the findings and directions of the appeals board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1513.)
