# LAB § 7626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 1. Scope of Chapter and General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 7626

---

## Text

This part does not limit the authority of the division to prescribe or enforce general or special safety orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1142.)
