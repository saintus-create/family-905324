# LAB § 1206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Wages, Hours and Working Conditions

**Citation:** LAB § 1206

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, this code establishes minimum penalties for failure to comply with wage-related statutes and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 655, Sec. 11. (AB 469) Effective January 1, 2012.)
