# LAB § 2800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. Employer and Employee > Article: 2. Obligations of Employer

**Citation:** LAB § 2800

---

## Text

An employer shall in all cases indemnify his employee for losses caused by the employer’s want of ordinary care.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
