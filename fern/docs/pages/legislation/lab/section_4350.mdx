# LAB § 4350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Disaster Service Workers

**Citation:** LAB § 4350

---

## Text

The Office of Emergency Services shall administer this chapter as it relates to volunteer disaster service workers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 401. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
