# LAB § 1194.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Wages, Hours and Working Conditions

**Citation:** LAB § 1194.3

---

## Text

An employee may recover attorney’s fees and costs incurred to enforce a court judgment for unpaid wages due pursuant to this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 655, Sec. 8. (AB 469) Effective January 1, 2012.)
