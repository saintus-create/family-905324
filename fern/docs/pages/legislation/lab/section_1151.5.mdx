# LAB § 1151.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Agricultural Labor Relations Board > Article: 2. Investigatory Powers

**Citation:** LAB § 1151.5

---

## Text

The several departments and agencies of the state upon request by the board, shall furnish the board all records, papers, and information in their possession, not otherwise privileged, relating to any matter before the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
