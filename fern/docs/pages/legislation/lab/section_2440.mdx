# LAB § 2440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Sanitary Conditions > Article: 5. General Health Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 2440

---

## Text

All employers shall comply with standards relating to the ready availability of medical services and first aid adopted by the Occupational Safety and Health Standards Board, pursuant to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 140) of Division 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 486, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1995.)
