# LAB § 7685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Operation of Tanks and Boilers

**Citation:** LAB § 7685

---

## Text

The division may issue and renew temporary permits for not to exceed 30 days each, pending the making of replacements or repairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1142.)
