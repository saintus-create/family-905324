# LAB § 7990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Licensing and Penalties

**Citation:** LAB § 7990

---

## Text

In any tunnel or mine under jurisdiction of the division, the use of explosives shall be limited to persons licensed by the division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1430.)
