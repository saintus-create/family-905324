# LAB § 1723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Public Works > Article: 1. Scope and Operation

**Citation:** LAB § 1723

---

## Text

“Worker” includes laborer, worker, or mechanic.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 954, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 21 of Ch. 954.)
