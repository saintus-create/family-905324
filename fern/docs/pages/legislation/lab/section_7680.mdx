# LAB § 7680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Operation of Tanks and Boilers

**Citation:** LAB § 7680

---

## Text

No tank or boiler shall be operated unless a permit for its operation has been issued by or in behalf of the division.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 467.)
