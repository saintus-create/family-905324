# LAB § 4616.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 2.3. Medical Provider Networks

**Citation:** LAB § 4616.6

---

## Text

No additional examinations shall be ordered by the appeals board and no other reports shall be admissable to resolve any controversy arising out of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 34, Sec. 27. Effective April 19, 2004.)
