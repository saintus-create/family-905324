# LAB § 4407.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Asbestos Workers’ Account > Article: 2. Benefits

**Citation:** LAB § 4407.5

---

## Text

Benefits provided by this chapter shall not be commuted into a lump-sum payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1077, Sec. 7. Effective September 15, 1982. Provisions inoperative January 1, 1989, as prescribed in Section 4418.)
