# LAB § 1901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Public Agencies > Article: 1. Municipal Employees

**Citation:** LAB § 1901

---

## Text

Any officer or agent of a city having supervision and control of employees covered by this article who violates any provision hereof is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
