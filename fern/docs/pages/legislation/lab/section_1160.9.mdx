# LAB § 1160.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 6. Prevention of Unfair Labor Practices and Judicial Review and Enforcement

**Citation:** LAB § 1160.9

---

## Text

The procedures set forth in this chapter shall be the exclusive method of redressing unfair labor practices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
