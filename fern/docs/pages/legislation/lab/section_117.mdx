# LAB § 117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF INDUSTRIAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 5. Division of Workers’ Compensation

**Citation:** LAB § 117

---

## Text

The administrative director may appoint an attorney licensed to practice law in the state as counsel to the division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 892, Sec. 13.)
