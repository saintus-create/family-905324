# LAB § 801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Lumber Industries

**Citation:** LAB § 801

---

## Text

Any person, or agent or officer thereof who violates any provision of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor, punishable by a fine of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than four hundred dollars ($400).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 195. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
