# LAB § 434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3. Privileges and Perquisites > Article: 3. Contracts and Applications for Employment

**Citation:** LAB § 434

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to applications for employment filed with common carriers by railroad subject to the act of Congress known as the Railway Labor Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
