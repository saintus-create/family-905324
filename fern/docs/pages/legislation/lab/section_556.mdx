# LAB § 556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** LAB § 556

---

## Text

Sections 551 and 552 shall not apply to any employer or employee when the total hours of employment do not exceed 30 hours in any week or six hours in any one day thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 134, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2000.)
