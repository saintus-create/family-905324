# LAB § 3747

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Compensation Insurance and Security > Article: 2.5. Self-Insurers’ Security Fund

## Text

This article shall be known and may be referred to as the “Young-La Follette Self-Insurers’ Security Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 252, Sec. 5. Effective June 27, 1984.)
