# LAB § 1960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Firefighters

**Citation:** LAB § 1960

---

## Text

Neither the State nor any county, political subdivision, incorporated city, town, nor any other municipal corporation shall prohibit, deny or obstruct the right of firefighters to join any bona fide labor organization of their own choice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 723.)
