# LAB § 1556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Part: 5.6. WORKPLACE KNOW YOUR RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Domestic Work Employee Rights

**Citation:** LAB § 1556

---

## Text

Parties subject to this part may provide, by collective bargaining agreement, that the agreement supersedes the requirements of this part, in whole or in part, if the waiver is explicitly set forth in the agreement in clear and unambiguous terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 667, Sec. 1. (SB 294) Effective January 1, 2026.)
