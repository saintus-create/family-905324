# LAB § 1700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Talent Agencies > Article: 1. Scope and Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 1700

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” means any individual, company, society, firm, partnership, association, corporation, limited liability company, manager, or their agents or employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 184. Effective January 1, 1995.)
