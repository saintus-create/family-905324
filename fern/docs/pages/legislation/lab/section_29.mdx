# LAB § 29

**Citation:** LAB § 29

---

## Text

“Medical director” means the physician appointed by the administrative director pursuant to Section 122.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 639, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
