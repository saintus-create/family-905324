# LAB § 1155.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 4. Unfair Labor Practices and Regulation of Secondary Boycotts

**Citation:** LAB § 1155.5

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person to request, demand, receive, or accept, or agree to receive or accept, any payment, loan, or delivery of any money or other thing of value prohibited by Section 1155.4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
