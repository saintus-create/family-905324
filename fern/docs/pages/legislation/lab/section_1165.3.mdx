# LAB § 1165.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 7. Suits Involving Employers and Labor Organizations

**Citation:** LAB § 1165.3

---

## Text

The service of summons, subpoena, or other legal process of any superior court upon an officer or agent of a labor organization, in his capacity as such, shall constitute service upon the labor organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1.)
