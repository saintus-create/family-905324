# LAB § 3089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EMPLOYMENT RELATIONS > Chapter: 4. Apprenticeship and Preapprenticeship > Article: 2. Apprenticeship Programs

**Citation:** LAB § 3089

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Shelley-Maloney Apprentice Labor Standards Act of 1939, as amended.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 704, Sec. 47. (AB 235) Effective September 22, 2018.)
