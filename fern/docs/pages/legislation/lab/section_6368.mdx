# LAB § 6368

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2.5. Hazardous Substances Information and Training > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** LAB § 6368

---

## Text

“Common name” means any designation or identification such as code name, code number, trade name, or brand name used to identify a substance other than by its chemical name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 874.)
