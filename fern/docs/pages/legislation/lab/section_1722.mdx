# LAB § 1722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Public Works > Article: 1. Scope and Operation

**Citation:** LAB § 1722

---

## Text

“Awarding body” or “body awarding the contract” means department, board, authority, officer or agent awarding a contract for public work.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
