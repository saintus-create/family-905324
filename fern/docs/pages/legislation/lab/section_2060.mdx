# LAB § 2060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 2. Registration

**Citation:** LAB § 2060

---

## Text

No employer may conduct any business without complying with the registration and bond requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 825, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2004.)
