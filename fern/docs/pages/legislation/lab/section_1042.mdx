# LAB § 1042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 3.9. Employee Literacy Assistance

**Citation:** LAB § 1042

---

## Text

The employer shall make reasonable efforts to safeguard the privacy of the employee as to the fact that he or she has a problem with illiteracy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 339, Sec. 2.)
