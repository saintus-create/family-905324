# LAB § 4209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Economic Opportunity Programs > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 4209

---

## Text

Insofar as not inconsistent with the provisions of this chapter, all of the provisions of this division shall pertain to enrollees and their dependents and the furnishing of compensation benefits thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1685.)
