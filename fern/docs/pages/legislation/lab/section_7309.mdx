# LAB § 7309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Elevators, Escalators, Platform and Stairway Chair Lifts, Dumbwaiters, Moving Walks, Automated People Movers, and Other Conveyances

**Citation:** LAB § 7309

---

## Text

The division may cause the inspection herein provided for to be made either by its safety inspectors or by any qualified elevator inspector employed by an insurance company.
