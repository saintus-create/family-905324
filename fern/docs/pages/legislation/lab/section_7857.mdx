# LAB § 7857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 2. Process Safety Management Standards

**Citation:** LAB § 7857

---

## Text

The process safety management standards shall include provisions dealing with the items prescribed by Sections 7858 to 7868, inclusive, of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1632, Sec. 1.)
