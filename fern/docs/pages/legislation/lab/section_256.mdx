# LAB § 256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. EMPLOYMENT REGULATION AND SUPERVISION > Chapter: 1. Payment of Wages > Article: 2. Seasonal Labor

**Citation:** LAB § 256

---

## Text

The Labor Commissioner shall impose a civil penalty in an amount not exceeding 30 days pay as waiting time under the terms of Section 203.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1096, Sec. 3.)
