# LAB § 4550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** LAB § 4550

---

## Text

Where liability for compensation exists under this division, such compensation shall be furnished or paid by the employer and shall be as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
