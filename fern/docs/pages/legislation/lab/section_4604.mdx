# LAB § 4604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WORKERS' COMPENSATION AND INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Compensation Schedules > Article: 2. Medical and Hospital Treatment

**Citation:** LAB § 4604

---

## Text

Controversies between employer and employee arising under this chapter shall be determined by the appeals board, upon the request of either party, except as otherwise provided by Section 4610.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 363, Sec. 40. (SB 863) Effective January 1, 2013.)
