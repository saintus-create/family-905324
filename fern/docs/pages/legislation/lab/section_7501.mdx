# LAB § 7501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. SAFETY IN EMPLOYMENT > Chapter: 3. Underground Telephones

**Citation:** LAB § 7501

---

## Text

The failure or refusal of any owner or lessee to install or maintain such telephone system is a misdemeanor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1937, Ch. 90.)
