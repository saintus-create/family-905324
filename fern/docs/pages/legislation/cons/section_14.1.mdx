# Cal. Const. art. I, § 14.1

**Hierarchy:** Article: I. DECLARATION OF RIGHTS

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. I, § 14.1

---

## Text

If a felony is prosecuted by indictment, there shall be no postindictment preliminary hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 14.1 added June 5, 1990, by Prop. 115. Initiative measure.)
