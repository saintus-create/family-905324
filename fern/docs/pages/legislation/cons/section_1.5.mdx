# Cal. Const. art. XX, § 1.5

**Hierarchy:** Article: XX. MISCELLANEOUS SUBJECTS

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. XX, § 1.5

---

## Text

The Legislature shall protect, by law, from forced sale a certain portion of the homestead and other property of all heads of families.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 1.5 added June 8, 1976, by Prop. 14. Res.Ch. 5, 1976, clause 38, as revised by Res.Ch. 24, Amdt. 7.)
