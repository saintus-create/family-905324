# Cal. Const. art. II, § 2.5

**Hierarchy:** Article: II. VOTING, INITIATIVE AND REFERENDUM, AND RECALL

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. II, § 2.5

---

## Text

A voter who casts a vote in an election in accordance with the laws of this State shall have that vote counted.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 2.5 added March 5, 2002, by Prop. 43. Res.Ch. 114, 2001.)
