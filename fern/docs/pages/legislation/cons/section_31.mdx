# Cal. Const. art. XIII, § 31

**Hierarchy:** Article: XIII. TAXATION

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. XIII, § 31

---

## Text

The power to tax may not be surrendered or suspended by grant or contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 31 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 70, 1974.)
