# Cal. Const. art. XIII, § 33

**Hierarchy:** Article: XIII. TAXATION

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. XIII, § 33

---

## Text

The Legislature shall pass all laws necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 33 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 70, 1974.)
