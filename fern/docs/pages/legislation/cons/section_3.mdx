# Cal. Const. art. XXXV, § 3

**Hierarchy:** Article: XXXV. MEDICAL RESEARCH

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. XXXV, § 3

---

## Text

No funds authorized for, or made available to, the institute shall be used for research involving human reproductive cloning.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 3 added Nov. 2, 2004, by Prop. 71. Initiative measure.)
