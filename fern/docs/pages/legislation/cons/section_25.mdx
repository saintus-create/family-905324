# Cal. Const. art. XIII, § 25

**Hierarchy:** Article: XIII. TAXATION

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. XIII, § 25

---

## Text

The Legislature shall provide, in the same fiscal year, reimbursements to each local government for revenue lost because of Section 3(k).

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 25 added Nov. 5, 1974, by Prop. 8. Res.Ch. 70, 1974.)
