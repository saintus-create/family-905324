# Cal. Const. art. XXXV, § 7

**Hierarchy:** Article: XXXV. MEDICAL RESEARCH

**Citation:** Cal. Const. art. XXXV, § 7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this Constitution, including Article VII, or any law, the institute and its employees are exempt from civil service.

## Legislative History

(History: Sec. 7 added Nov. 2, 2004, by Prop. 71. Initiative measure.)
