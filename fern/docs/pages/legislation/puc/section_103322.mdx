# PUC § 103322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 103322

---

## Text

The district shall not levy or collect a property tax unless a majority of the voters voting on the question at an election called for such purpose approves the levy of a property tax by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
