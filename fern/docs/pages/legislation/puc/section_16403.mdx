# PUC § 16403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 16403

---

## Text

A district may adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
