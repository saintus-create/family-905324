# PUC § 80018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Consolidation with Other Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 80018

---

## Text

Thereafter such consolidation shall be effected by such other district in the manner provided for annexation of counties in Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 29500), Part 2 of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
