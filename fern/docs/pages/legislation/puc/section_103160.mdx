# PUC § 103160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 3. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 103160

---

## Text

The officers of the district shall consist of the members of the board, the chairman of the board, a secretary, a general manager, a legal counsel, a controller, a treasurer, and such other officers, assistants and deputies as the board may deem necessary and provide for by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
