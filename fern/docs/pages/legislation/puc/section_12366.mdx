# PUC § 12366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** PUC § 12366

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 12364, the retirement board may contract with one or more qualified investment managers in connection with the investment program of the retirement board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1738, Sec. 37. Effective September 30, 1984.)
