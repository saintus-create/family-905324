# PUC § 30742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 9. Elections

**Citation:** PUC § 30742

---

## Text

No irregularities or informalities in conducting any election shall invalidate the same if the election shall have been otherwise fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
