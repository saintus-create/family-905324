# PUC § 16073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 16073

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances shall be:

“Be it enacted by the board of directors of _______ public utility district:”.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
