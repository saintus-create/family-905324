# PUC § 29580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 6. Contest of Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 29580

---

## Text

The validity of any proceedings for the annexation of any county to the district shall not be contested in any action unless the action is brought within three (3) months after the completion of the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
