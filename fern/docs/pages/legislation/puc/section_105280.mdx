# PUC § 105280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 105280

## Text

Territory may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
