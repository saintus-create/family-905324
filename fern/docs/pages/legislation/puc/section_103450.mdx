# PUC § 103450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 4. Other Benefits

**Citation:** PUC § 103450

---

## Text

The district shall take such steps as may be necessary to obtain coverage for the district and its employees under Subchapter II of the Federal Social Security Act, as amended, and the related provisions of the Federal Contributions Act, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
