# PUC § 180259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 180259

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
