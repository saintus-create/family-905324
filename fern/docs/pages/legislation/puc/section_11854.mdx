# PUC § 11854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2.5. Additional Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11854

---

## Text

The term of office of the additional directors elected pursuant to this article shall be four years and until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 337.)
