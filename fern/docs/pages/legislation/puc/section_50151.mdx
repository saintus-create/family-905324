# PUC § 50151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 3. Retirement Board

## Text

All members of the retirement board shall serve without pay.
