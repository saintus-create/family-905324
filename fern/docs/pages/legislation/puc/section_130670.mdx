# PUC § 130670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. Code of Conduct for the Board of the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 130670

---

## Text

The payment for, and reimbursement of, board travel shall be governed by applicable state law and MTA’s administrative code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 755, Sec. 19. (AB 3123) Effective January 1, 2025.)
