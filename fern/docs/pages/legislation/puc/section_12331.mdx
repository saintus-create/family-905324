# PUC § 12331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 12331

---

## Text

The board may prescribe the terms and conditions upon which the officers and employees of the district or their beneficiaries shall be entitled to benefits and the amounts thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
