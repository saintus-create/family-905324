# PUC § 21650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 2.5. Non-Public-Use Airports

**Citation:** PUC § 21650

---

## Text

“Airport” for the purposes of this article means an airport not open to the general public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1243, Sec. 17. Effective September 30, 1992.)
