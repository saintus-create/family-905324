# PUC § 101150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 7. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 101150

---

## Text

All matters and things necessary for the proper administration of the affairs of the district which are not provided for by this part shall be provided for by the board. The powers vested in the board by this part shall be deemed to be inclusive but not exclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
