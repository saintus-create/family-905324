# PUC § 892.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 10. Natural Gas Surcharge

**Citation:** PUC § 892.1

---

## Text

The surcharges imposed by this part and the amounts thereof required to be collected by public utility gas corporations are due quarterly on or before the last day of the month next succeeding each calendar quarter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 932, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
