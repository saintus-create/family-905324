# PUC § 28767.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28767.3

---

## Text

Not later than October 12, 1974, the board shall adopt an affirmative action program approved by the Office of Federal Contract Compliance of the Department of Labor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 199. Effective October 12, 2001.)
