# PUC § 150003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 150003

---

## Text

“Authority” means the Tuolumne County Traffic Authority created pursuant to this division in the County of Tuolumne.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
