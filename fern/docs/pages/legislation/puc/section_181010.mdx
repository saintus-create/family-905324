# PUC § 181010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.1. SONOMA COUNTY REGIONAL CLIMATE PROTECTION AUTHORITY

**Citation:** PUC § 181010

---

## Text

The members of the authority shall be compensated as determined by the authority and shall be reimbursed for necessary and reasonable expenses incurred in connection with performing authority duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 375, Sec. 1. (AB 881) Effective January 1, 2010.)
