# PUC § 25334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 25334

---

## Text

All members of the retirement system shall contribute in the manner and amount fixed by the board and such contributions may be collected by deducting the amounts thereof from the salary, wages, or compensation due such members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
