# PUC § 27415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 27415

---

## Text

The board may reduce or enlarge the boundaries of the proposed special transit service district, but the boundaries of the proposed special transit service district shall be within the boundaries of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
