# PUC § 3028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 3028

---

## Text

An association is not any of the following:

(a) A conspiracy, a combination in restraint of trade, or an illegal monopoly.

(b) An attempt to lessen competition, to fix prices arbitrarily, or to create a combination or pool in violation of any law of this state.

(c) A public utility for any purpose under Part 1 (commencing with Section 201).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
