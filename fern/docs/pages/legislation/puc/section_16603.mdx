# PUC § 16603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 7. Receipts, Accounts, and Audits

**Citation:** PUC § 16603

---

## Text

Whenever a reserve fund exceeds one-half of the payment for operating expenses in the preceding fiscal year, the board may appropriate such excess to the general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
