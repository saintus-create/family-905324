# PUC § 132351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 2. Organization and Governance

**Citation:** PUC § 132351

---

## Text

The consolidation of SANDAG and the transit boards will consolidate responsibilities under the organization and governance structure and with the powers, duties, functions, and authority as set forth herein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
