# PUC § 180103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 180103

---

## Text

The acts of the authority shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
