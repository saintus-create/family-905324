# PUC § 50027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 2. Request by Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 50027

---

## Text

Certified copies of the resolution shall be presented to the Board of Supervisors of San Joaquin County requesting the board of supervisors to call an election without delay for determining whether the district will be created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
