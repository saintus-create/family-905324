# PUC § 11531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 11531

---

## Text

A municipal utility district may be created as provided in this division and when so created may exercise the powers herein granted.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
