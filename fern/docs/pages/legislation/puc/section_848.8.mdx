# PUC § 848.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 5.6. Financing Utility Recovery

**Citation:** PUC § 848.8

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, regulations adopted to implement this article are not subject to the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 46, Sec. 5. Effective June 7, 2004.)
