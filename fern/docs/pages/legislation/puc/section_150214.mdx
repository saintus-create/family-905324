# PUC § 150214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 150214

---

## Text

The commission may not impose any tax other than the retail transactions and use tax imposed upon approval of the voters in accordance with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
