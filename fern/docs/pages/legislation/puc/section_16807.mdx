# PUC § 16807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 1. Petition to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16807

---

## Text

The board may apply to the Public Utilities Commission to ascertain the value of existing utility works for the purpose of submitting to the electors estimates of the cost of acquiring them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
