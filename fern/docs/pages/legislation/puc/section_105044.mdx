# PUC § 105044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 105044

---

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
