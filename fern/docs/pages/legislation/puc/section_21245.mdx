# PUC § 21245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 2. Department of Transportation and State Aeronautics Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 21245

---

## Text

The department shall keep on file with the Secretary of State, and at its principal office, a copy of all its rules for public inspection.
