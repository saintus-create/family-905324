# PUC § 16843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 2. Resolution to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16843

---

## Text

When the cost of the public utility works can be paid out of the revenues of the district derived from the operation of its public utilities, in addition to the other necessary expenses of the district, as soon after the filing of the plans and estimates as the board deems is for the best interests of the district, it shall by ordinance determine to acquire such public utility works.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
