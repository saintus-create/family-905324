# PUC § 132370.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 8. Bonds, Equipment, and Leases

**Citation:** PUC § 132370.6

---

## Text

The consolidated agency may borrow money in accordance with Article 7 (commencing with Section 53820), Article 7.6 (commencing with Section 53850), or Article 7.7 (commencing with Section 53859) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 62, Sec. 269. Effective January 1, 2004.)
