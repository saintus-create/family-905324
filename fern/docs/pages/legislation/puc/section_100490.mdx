# PUC § 100490

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 100490

---

## Text

The VTA may bring an action to determine the validity of any of its bonds, equipment trust certificates, warrants, notes, or other evidences of indebtedness pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 103. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
