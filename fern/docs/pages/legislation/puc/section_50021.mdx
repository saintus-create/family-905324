# PUC § 50021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 50021

---

## Text

The request for the formation of the Stockton Metropolitan Transit District may be made by resolution or by petition as set out in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
