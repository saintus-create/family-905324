# PUC § 80005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 80005

---

## Text

The board shall file a certified copy of the resolution with the Secretary of State and for record in the office of the county recorder.

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
