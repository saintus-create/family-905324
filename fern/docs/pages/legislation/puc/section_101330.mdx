# PUC § 101330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 6. Validation

**Citation:** PUC § 101330

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds, including refunding bonds, may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860), Title 10, Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
