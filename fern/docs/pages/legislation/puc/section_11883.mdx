# PUC § 11883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11883

## Text

The board is the legislative body of the district and determines all questions of policy.
