# PUC § 96752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 96752

---

## Text

The resolution shall be passed by a vote of two-thirds of the members of the board and be approved by the board of supervisors of the county in which the territory is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
