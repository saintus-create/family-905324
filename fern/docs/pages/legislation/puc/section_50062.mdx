# PUC § 50062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 1. Government

**Citation:** PUC § 50062

---

## Text

A vacancy on the board shall be filled by the body which appointed the director whose office is vacated and the new appointee shall hold his office for the unexpired term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
