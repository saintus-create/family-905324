# PUC § 916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 916

---

## Text

On or before November 30 of each year, the commission shall report to the Legislature on its rail safety activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 612, Sec. 50. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2016.)
