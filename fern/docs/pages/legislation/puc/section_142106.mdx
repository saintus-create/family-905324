# PUC § 142106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. FRESNO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 142106

---

## Text

The authority may hire an independent staff of its own or contract with any department or agency of the United States or with any public agency to implement this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 4. Effective July 14, 1986. Repealed on date prescribed in Section 142010.)
