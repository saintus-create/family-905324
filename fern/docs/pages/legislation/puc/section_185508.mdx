# PUC § 185508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. CALIFORNIA HIGH-SPEED RAIL SERVICE > Chapter: 5. Relocation of Utilities

**Citation:** PUC § 185508

---

## Text

A utility is entitled to a permit for such reasonable crossings of high-speed rail property as may be required for the proper discharge of the utility’s service to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 35, Sec. 7. (SB 85) Effective June 27, 2013.)
