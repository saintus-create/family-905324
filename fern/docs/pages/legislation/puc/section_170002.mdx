# PUC § 170002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SAN DIEGO COUNTY REGIONAL AIRPORT AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 170002

---

## Text

There is hereby established the San Diego County Regional Airport Authority, as a local governmental entity of regional government, with jurisdiction extending throughout the County of San Diego.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 946, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
