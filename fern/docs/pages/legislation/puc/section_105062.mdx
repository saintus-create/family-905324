# PUC § 105062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 105062

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against the district are governed by Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code, except as provided herein, or by other statutes or regulations expressly applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
