# PUC § 103074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 2. Formation Election

**Citation:** PUC § 103074

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 3 (commencing with Section 9160) of Chapter 2 of Division 9 of the Elections Code relating to ballot arguments and analyses shall be applicable to the formation election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 212. Effective January 1, 1995.)
