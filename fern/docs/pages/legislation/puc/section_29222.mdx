# PUC § 29222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29222

---

## Text

When any outstanding bonds, notes, or other evidences of indebtedness are surrendered and paid, the chief financial officer shall cancel them by endorsing on their faces the amount for which they are received, “Canceled,” and the date of cancellation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 106, Sec. 10. (AB 2325) Effective January 1, 2025.)
