# PUC § 141020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. COACHELLA VALLEY INTERMODAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY

**Citation:** PUC § 141020

---

## Text

All meetings of the board or the district boards shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 486, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
