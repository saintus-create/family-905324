# PUC § 13171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 11. Proceeds

**Citation:** PUC § 13171

---

## Text

All sums received as accrued interest on the sale of any issue of bonds issued pursuant to this chapter shall be applied to the payment of interest on or principal of bonds of that issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
