# PUC § 100171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 6. Public Grants, Loans and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 100171

---

## Text

The VTA may obtain temporary transfers of funds in accordance with the last paragraph of Section 6 of Article XVI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 65. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
