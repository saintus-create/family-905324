# PUC § 26286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 4. Refunding

**Citation:** PUC § 26286

---

## Text

In lieu of selling refunding bonds and using the proceeds to purchase or retire the bonds to be refunded, the board may exchange refunding bonds at not less than par and accrued interest for the bonds so refunded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
