# PUC § 30943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 3. Equipment Trust Certificates

**Citation:** PUC § 30943

---

## Text

The covenants, conditions and provisions of the agreements, leases, and equipment trust certificates shall not conflict with any of the provisions of any agreement securing the payment of bonds, notes or certificates of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
