# PUC § 106021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 17. Monterey-Salinas Transit District > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 106021

## Text

The board shall adopt bylaws for its proceedings consistent with the laws of the state.

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 460, Sec. 1. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2010.)
