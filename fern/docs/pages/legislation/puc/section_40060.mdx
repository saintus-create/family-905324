# PUC § 40060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 1. Government

**Citation:** PUC § 40060

---

## Text

The members of the Orange County Transportation Authority, comprised as specified by Section 130052, shall serve as the board of directors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 983, Sec. 1. Operative December 1, 1991, or sooner, as prescribed by Sec. 8 of Ch. 983.)
