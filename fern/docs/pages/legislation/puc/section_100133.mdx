# PUC § 100133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 3. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 100133

---

## Text

The VTA is entitled to the benefit of any reservation or grant, in all cases, where any right has been reserved or granted to any public agency to construct or maintain roads, highways, or other crossings over any public or private lands.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 49. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
