# PUC § 24410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 5. Declaration of Intent

**Citation:** PUC § 24410

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this part to establish minimum standards for aircraft financial responsibility, and nothing in this part shall be construed as preventing any local city or county ordinance from exceeding the minimum insurance requirements prescribed in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1452.)
