# PUC § 24887

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24887

---

## Text

The board may from time to time contract for or employ any professional service required by the district or for the performance of work or services which cannot satisfactorily be performed by the regular employees of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
