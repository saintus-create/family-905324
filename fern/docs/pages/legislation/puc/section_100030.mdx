# PUC § 100030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 100030

---

## Text

The Legislature recognizes the formation of the Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority and it may exercise the powers herein granted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 11. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
