# PUC § 22401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 22401

---

## Text

A board of five directors is the governing body of the district. Each director shall be a resident of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 398.)
