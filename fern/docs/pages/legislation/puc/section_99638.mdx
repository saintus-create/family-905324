# PUC § 99638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99638

---

## Text

Seventeen million dollars ($17,000,000) shall be allocated to the Monterey County Transportation Commission for the following:

(a) Extension of CalTrain service.

(b) Other rail projects within Monterey County.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
