# PUC § 99568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 7. Transit Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 9. Impasse Procedures

**Citation:** PUC § 99568

---

## Text

The impasse procedures contained in Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 1137) of Part 3 of Division 2 of the Labor Code shall govern any impasse proceedings under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 833, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
