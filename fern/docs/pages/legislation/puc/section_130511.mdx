# PUC § 130511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 130511

---

## Text

“Bonds” means indebtedness and securities of any kind or class, including bonds, notes, bond anticipation notes, commercial paper, and other obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
