# PUC § 11884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11884

---

## Text

All matters and things necessary for the proper administration of the affairs of the district which are not provided for in this division shall be provided for by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
