# PUC § 340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 2. Oversight Board

**Citation:** PUC § 340

---

## Text

The Oversight Board shall take the steps that are necessary to ensure the earliest possible incorporation of the Independent System Operator and the Power Exchange as separately incorporated public benefit, nonprofit corporations under the Corporations Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 854, Sec. 10. Effective September 24, 1996.)
