# PUC § 130201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 130201

---

## Text

The commission may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
