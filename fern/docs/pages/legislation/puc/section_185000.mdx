# PUC § 185000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. CALIFORNIA HIGH-SPEED RAIL SERVICE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions,Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 185000

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the California High-Speed Rail Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 796, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
