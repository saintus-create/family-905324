# PUC § 15708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** PUC § 15708

---

## Text

After the election for the incorporation of the proposed district, the sufficiency of the petition in any respects shall not be subject to judicial review or otherwise questioned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
