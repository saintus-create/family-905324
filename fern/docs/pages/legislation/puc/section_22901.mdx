# PUC § 22901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 22901

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of the principal county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
