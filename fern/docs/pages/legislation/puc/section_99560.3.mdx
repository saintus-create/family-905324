# PUC § 99560.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 7. Transit Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 99560.3

---

## Text

This chapter shall only apply to supervisory employees of the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 833, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
