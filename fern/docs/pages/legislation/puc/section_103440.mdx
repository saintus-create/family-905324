# PUC § 103440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 3. Pension Plan

**Citation:** PUC § 103440

---

## Text

The adoption, terms, and conditions of a pension plan covering employees of the district in a bargaining unit represented by a labor organization shall be pursuant to a collective bargaining agreement between such organization and the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
