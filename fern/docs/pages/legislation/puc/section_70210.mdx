# PUC § 70210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 70210

---

## Text

The district may levy, and collect or cause to be collected, taxes for any lawful purpose subject to a maximum limit of five cents ($0.05) per one hundred dollars ($100) of assessed valuation within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
