# PUC § 16033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 16033

---

## Text

The president shall sign all contracts on behalf of the district and perform such other duties as may be imposed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
