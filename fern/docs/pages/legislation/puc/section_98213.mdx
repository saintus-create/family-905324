# PUC § 98213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 98213

---

## Text

No action in eminent domain to acquire property or interests therein within any incorporated city or any county shall be commenced unless the legislative body of the affected city or county has consented to such acquisition by resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
