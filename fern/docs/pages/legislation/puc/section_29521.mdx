# PUC § 29521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 29521

---

## Text

The ordinance, together with a notice fixing the time and place for hearing thereon, shall be published once in a newspaper of general circulation published in the district. The time fixed for the hearing shall be not less than thirty (30) nor more than sixty (60) days from the date of the publication of the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
