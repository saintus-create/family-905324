# PUC § 13135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 7. Obligation to Bondholders

**Citation:** PUC § 13135

---

## Text

No indebtedness payable out of revenues shall be created in contravention of the provisions of any resolutions heretofore adopted by the board in connection with the authorization of any bonds payable out of revenues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
