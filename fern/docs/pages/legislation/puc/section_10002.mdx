# PUC § 10002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 1. Acquisition and Operation

**Citation:** PUC § 10002

---

## Text

Any municipal corporation may acquire, construct, own, operate, or lease any public utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
