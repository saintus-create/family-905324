# PUC § 100114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 100114

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this part, VTA elections shall be called, held, and conducted as provided by the Uniform District Election Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 37. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
