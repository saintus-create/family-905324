# PUC § 103105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 103105

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business, and all official acts of the board shall require the affirmative vote of a majority of the members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
