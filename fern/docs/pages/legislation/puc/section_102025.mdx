# PUC § 102025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 102025

---

## Text

“Member entity” means a city or county that is within the boundaries of the district as defined in Section 102052.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 335, Sec. 1. (AB 738) Effective January 1, 2016.)
