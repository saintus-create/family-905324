# PUC § 40616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Consolidation with Other Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 40616

---

## Text

The validity of any consolidating proceedings shall not be contested in any action unless the action is brought within three (3) months after the completion of the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1899.)
