# PUC § 24501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24501.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, except Section 24826, elections for the Alameda-Contra Costa Transit District shall be conducted in accordance with the Uniform District Election Law (Part 4 (commencing with Section 10500) of Division 10 of the Elections Code). Any costs incurred by county officials shall be paid by the transit district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 191. Effective January 1, 1995.)
