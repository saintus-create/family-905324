# PUC § 22733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 2. Bond Election

**Citation:** PUC § 22733

---

## Text

For the purpose of the bond election, the board may divide the district into election precincts and may change the precincts as often as occasion requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
