# PUC § 101102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PUC § 101102

---

## Text

Except for the first members of the board, the term of office of each director shall be four years commencing at noon on the first Monday following the first day of January and until the appointment and qualification of his successor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
