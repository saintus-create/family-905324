# PUC § 101242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Audits and Financial Report

**Citation:** PUC § 101242

---

## Text

Within 90 days from and after the close of each fiscal year, the general manager shall prepare and file with the secretary of the district a financial report showing the result of the operations of the district for the preceding fiscal year and the financial status of the district on the last day thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
