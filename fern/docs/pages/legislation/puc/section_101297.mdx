# PUC § 101297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 101297

---

## Text

The votes of a majority of all the voters voting on the proposition at the election are required to authorize the issuance of bonds under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
