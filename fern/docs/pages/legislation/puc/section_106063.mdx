# PUC § 106063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 17. Monterey-Salinas Transit District > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 5. Financing

**Citation:** PUC § 106063

---

## Text

The district may advocate and act on behalf of all district member jurisdictions with their concurrence to further Monterey County transportation system interests, funding, projects, and priorities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 460, Sec. 1. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2010.)
