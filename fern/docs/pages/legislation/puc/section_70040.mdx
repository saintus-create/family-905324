# PUC § 70040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 70040

---

## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto the following: Shall the “Marin County Transit District” be created and established?YESNO
