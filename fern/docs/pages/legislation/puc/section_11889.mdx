# PUC § 11889

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11889

---

## Text

The board shall employ an expert who shall examine and report, at least annually, upon the system of accounts kept by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
