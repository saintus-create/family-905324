# PUC § 70272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 70272

## Text

The resolution shall be passed by a vote of two-thirds of the members of the board.
