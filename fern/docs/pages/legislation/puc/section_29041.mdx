# PUC § 29041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Rapid Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 29041

---

## Text

At the time fixed for any hearing before the board any board of supervisors or city council eligible to file a request for hearing, not a party to the original request for hearing, may intervene and shall be entitled to be heard and to introduce evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
