# PUC § 150151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 150151

---

## Text

The authority may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all court tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
