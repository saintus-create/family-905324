# PUC § 24830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24830

---

## Text

Candidates for the office of director shall be voted upon and elected one from each ward.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 66, Sec. 4. (AB 1141) Effective January 1, 2026.)
