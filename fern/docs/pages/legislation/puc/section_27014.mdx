# PUC § 27014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 3. Approval by Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 27014

---

## Text

If upon a canvass of the election it is found that a majority of all votes cast on the proposition at the election were cast in favor of the annexation, the proposition and all of the terms and conditions set forth in the ordinance shall be deemed carried and approved by the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
