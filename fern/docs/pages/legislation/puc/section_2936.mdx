# PUC § 2936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 3. SURRENDER OF MUNICIPAL CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Surrender of Control by Municipal Corporations > Article: 2. Petition for Election

**Citation:** PUC § 2936

---

## Text

If, by the certificate of the clerk, the petition is shown to be insufficient, it may be amended by filing a supplemental petition within 10 days from the date of the certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
