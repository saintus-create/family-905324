# PUC § 92004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 92004

---

## Text

No other notice of the election other than that provided for in this chapter need be given and no sample ballots need be sent to the voters.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
