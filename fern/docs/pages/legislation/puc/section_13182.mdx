# PUC § 13182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 12. Short Term Borrowing

**Citation:** PUC § 13182

---

## Text

All notes or other evidences of indebtedness issued under the authority of this article shall contain upon their face a recital that they are so issued and shall be payable in not to exceed three years from their date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
