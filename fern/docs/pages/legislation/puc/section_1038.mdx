# PUC § 1038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Public Convenience and Necessity > Article: 2. Passenger Stage Corporations

**Citation:** PUC § 1038

---

## Text

The interior of every passenger stage operated for hire in this State shall be maintained in a clean and sanitary condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1007.)
