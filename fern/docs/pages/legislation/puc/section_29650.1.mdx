# PUC § 29650.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Withdrawal From District

**Citation:** PUC § 29650.1

---

## Text

In addition to the right of withdrawal of a county as provided in Section 29650, any county may withdraw from the district at any time prior to the date of adoption by the board of a resolution calling a special election to submit to the qualified voters of the district a proposition of incurring a bonded indebtedness as provided in Section 29158, provided the board of directors of the district elects to accept for filing and approves a resolution of withdrawal adopted by the board of supervisors of the county seeking withdrawal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1962, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 8.)
