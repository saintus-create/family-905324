# PUC § 15701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** PUC § 15701

---

## Text

A district may be incorporated and managed in unincorporated territory pursuant to this division and may exercise the powers expressly granted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
