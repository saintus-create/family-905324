# PUC § 16152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. District Elections

**Citation:** PUC § 16152

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this division, districts governed by this division are subject to the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.
