# PUC § 130544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 4. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** PUC § 130544

---

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to Section 130542 are subject to this chapter in the same manner and to the same extent as other bonds issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
