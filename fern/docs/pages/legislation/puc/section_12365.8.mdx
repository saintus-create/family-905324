# PUC § 12365.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** PUC § 12365.8

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, the retirement system may invest in any and all investments authorized by Section 1372 of the Financial Code and Section 12871 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 484, Sec. 3.)
