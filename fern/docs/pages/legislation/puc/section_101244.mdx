# PUC § 101244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Audits and Financial Report

**Citation:** PUC § 101244

---

## Text

Within 15 days after the filing of the financial report, the general manager shall publish a notice of such filing and shall state the fact of such availability for public inspection, together with the address of the office of the general manager. Such publication shall be in accord with Section 6061 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
