# PUC § 100500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 8. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 100500

---

## Text

The VTA may be dissolved pursuant to the Cortese-Knox-Hertzberg Local Government Reorganization Act of 2000 (Division 3 (commencing with Section 56000) of Title 5 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 106. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
