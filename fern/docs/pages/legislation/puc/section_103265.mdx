# PUC § 103265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Transit Planning

**Citation:** PUC § 103265

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 5 (commencing with Section 53090), Chapter 1, Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code shall not be applicable to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
