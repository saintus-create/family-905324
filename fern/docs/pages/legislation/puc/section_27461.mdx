# PUC § 27461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 27461

---

## Text

Any public agency or portion thereof within the boundaries of a district, hereafter referred to collectively as “territory,” not included within the boundaries of a special transit service district, may be annexed thereto in the manner provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
