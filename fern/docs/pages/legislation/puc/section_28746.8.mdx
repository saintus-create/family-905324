# PUC § 28746.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 2.2. District Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28746.8

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, candidates for the board shall be nominated, the election held and conducted, and the ballots canvassed in accordance with the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 521.)
