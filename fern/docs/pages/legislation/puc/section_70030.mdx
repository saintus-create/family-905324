# PUC § 70030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 3. Request by Petition

**Citation:** PUC § 70030

---

## Text

Instead of a resolution, a petition may be presented to the board of supervisors signed by voters within Marin County equal in number to at least 25 percent of the total vote cast at the last general election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
