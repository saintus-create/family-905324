# PUC § 28602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Creation of District

**Citation:** PUC § 28602

---

## Text

Through compliance with the provisions for annexation set forth in Chapter 9 of this part, the territory of all or any of the Counties of Napa, Santa Clara, Solano, or Sonoma may be included within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
