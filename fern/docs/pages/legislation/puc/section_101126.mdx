# PUC § 101126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 3. Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** PUC § 101126

---

## Text

In any particular not otherwise provided for by this part, the board, by resolution or ordinance, may prescribe any regulation which it may deem appropriate for the proper conduct of the internal functions of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
