# PUC § 99233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 3. Local Transportation Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99233

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 99233.11, the fund shall be allocated by the designated transportation planning agency for the purposes specified in Sections 99233.1 to 99233.9, inclusive, in the sequence provided in those sections.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 45, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
