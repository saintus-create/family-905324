# PUC § 17364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17364

---

## Text

The petition shall be addressed to the board of directors of the annexing district and filed with the secretary of the annexing district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
