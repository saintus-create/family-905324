# PUC § 8329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 2. Decommissioning of Nuclear Facilities

**Citation:** PUC § 8329

---

## Text

The commission or the board shall, for purposes of establishing rates or charges, review and approve the estimated service life and estimated retirement date of all nuclear facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1560, Sec. 5.)
