# PUC § 887

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 9. Telephone Prepaid Debit Cards

## Text

The commission may enforce the standards and requirements of Section 17538.9 of the Business and Professions Code.
