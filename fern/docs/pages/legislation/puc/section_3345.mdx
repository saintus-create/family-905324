# PUC § 3345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER POWER AND CONSERVATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. The California Consumer Power and Conservation Financing Authority > Article: 4. Powers of the Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 3345

---

## Text

The authority’s operating budget shall be subject to review and appropriation in the annual Budget Act. For purposes of this section, the authority’s operating budget shall include the costs of personnel, administration, and overhead.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 10, Sec. 1. Effective August 13, 2001.)
