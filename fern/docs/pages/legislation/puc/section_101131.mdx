# PUC § 101131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 4. Compensation and Expenses

**Citation:** PUC § 101131

---

## Text

In addition to the compensation provided for in Section 101130, each member of the board shall receive reimbursement for his actual and necessary traveling expenses incurred in the discharge of his official duties when such travel is beyond the territory of the district and when such travel is first approved by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
