# PUC § 103333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 103333

---

## Text

In lieu of, or in addition to, any taxes which may be levied by the district pursuant to this article, any city council within the district or the board of supervisors may enter into agreement with the district to make annual contributions to the district from its general funds or from any other source of funds legally available to it for such purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
