# PUC § 2781

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 6. Home Insulation Assistance and Financing

**Citation:** PUC § 2781

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the term “electrical corporation” shall have the same meaning as prescribed in Section 218, and the term “gas corporation” shall have the same meaning as prescribed in Section 222.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1202.)
