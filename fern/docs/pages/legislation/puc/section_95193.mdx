# PUC § 95193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 95193

---

## Text

The election shall be held not less than 60 days nor more than 90 days from the date of the adoption of the resolution prescribed by Section 95192.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
