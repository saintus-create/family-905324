# PUC § 29524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 29524

---

## Text

Any hearing on the agreement may be adjourned from time to time by the board of directors of the district without further notice other than an order to be entered upon the minutes of the meeting fixing the time and place of adjournment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
