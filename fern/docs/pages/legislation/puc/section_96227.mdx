# PUC § 96227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 96227

---

## Text

The board shall immediately after fixing the rate of taxes as above provided transmit to the county auditor of the county a statement of the rate of taxes fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
