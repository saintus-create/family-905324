# PUC § 170035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SAN DIEGO COUNTY REGIONAL AIRPORT AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 170035

---

## Text

The authority is a local agency for purposes of the California Disaster Assistance Act (Chapter 7.5 (commencing with Section 8680) of Division 1 of Title 1 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 620, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2009.)
