# PUC § 30656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5.5. Citizens’ Advisory Committees

**Citation:** PUC § 30656

---

## Text

Every meeting of a citizens’ advisory committee is open to the general public. Minutes of the proceedings shall be kept and shall be available for inspection at the district office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 725, Sec. 1.)
