# PUC § 24239

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24239

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, firm, copartnership, association or corporation, public or private, including his or its successors, assignees or legal representatives.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1452.)
