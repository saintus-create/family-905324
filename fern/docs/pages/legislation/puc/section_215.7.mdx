# PUC § 215.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 215.7

---

## Text

“Nonfood product” means any article, material, substance, or product which is not food.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1685, Sec. 2.5.)
