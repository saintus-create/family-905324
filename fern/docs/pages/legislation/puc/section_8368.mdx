# PUC § 8368

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 4. Smart Grid Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 8368

---

## Text

The commission may modify or adjust the requirements of this chapter for any electrical corporation with fewer than 100,000 service connections, as individual circumstances merit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 327, Sec. 1. (SB 17) Effective January 1, 2010.)
