# PUC § 15954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 1. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 15954

---

## Text

Candidates for the office of director to be elected by a unit shall be nominated from each such territorial unit, and the remaining number of directors shall be nominated from the district at large.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
