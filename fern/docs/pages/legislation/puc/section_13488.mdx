# PUC § 13488

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 13488

---

## Text

If no protests are filed, or if the protests filed are overruled and denied by the board, the board shall thereupon by resolution approve the creation of the special district either as originally described or as revised by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
