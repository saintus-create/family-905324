# PUC § 16081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 16081

---

## Text

An ordinance is suspended from going into operation until the examination, verification, and certification are completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
