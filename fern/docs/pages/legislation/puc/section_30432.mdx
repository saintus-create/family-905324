# PUC § 30432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 30432

---

## Text

Contributions shall be in amounts which will accumulate at retirement a fund sufficient to carry out the promise to pay benefits to the individual on account of his service as a member of the system, without further contributions from any source.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
