# PUC § 120482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 11. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 120482

---

## Text

(a) The county shall conduct an election called by the board pursuant to Section 120480.

(b) The election shall be called and conducted in the same manner as provided by law for the conduct of elections by a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 658, Sec. 7. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2018.)
