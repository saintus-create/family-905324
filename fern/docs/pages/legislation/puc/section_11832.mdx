# PUC § 11832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11832

---

## Text

The secretary of the district shall issue certificates of election, signed by him and duly authenticated, immediately following the determination of the result of the election by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
