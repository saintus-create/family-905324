# PUC § 99317.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 6.5. Transportation Planning and Development Account

**Citation:** PUC § 99317.9

---

## Text

The department and the commission shall give reasonable priority to allocations pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 99317 to station projects that improve access for visitors to state prisons.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 597, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2002.)
