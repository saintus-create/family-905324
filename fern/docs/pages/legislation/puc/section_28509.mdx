# PUC § 28509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 28509

---

## Text

“Public agency,” as used in this part, includes the State of California, and any county, city and county, city, district, or other political subdivision or public entity of, or organized under the laws of, this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
