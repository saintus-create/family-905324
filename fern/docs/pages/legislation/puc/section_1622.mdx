# PUC § 1622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 8.7. School Energy Efficiency Stimulus Program > Article: 3. School Reopening Ventilation and Energy Efficiency Verification and Repair Program

**Citation:** PUC § 1622

---

## Text

As conditions for receiving a grant pursuant to this article, a local educational agency shall comply with the requirements of Sections 1623 to 1627, inclusive, for all air-handling units, rooftop units and unitary and single zone equipment in that facility’s HVAC system or systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 372, Sec. 5. (AB 841) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 1640.)
