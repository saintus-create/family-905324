# PUC § 13151.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 9. Form and Content

**Citation:** PUC § 13151.1

---

## Text

The board may provide for such bonds being in such amounts, of such denominations, payable at such times, and in such form as the board may determine.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
