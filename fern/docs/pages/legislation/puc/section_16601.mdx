# PUC § 16601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 7. Receipts, Accounts, and Audits

**Citation:** PUC § 16601

---

## Text

Receipts from the operation of a public utility shall be paid daily into the treasury of the district in a special fund set aside for such public utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
