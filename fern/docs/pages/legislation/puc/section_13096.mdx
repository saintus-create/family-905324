# PUC § 13096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 3. General Authorization

**Citation:** PUC § 13096

---

## Text

The board shall have power to determine all the terms and conditions of the issuance and sale of bonds pursuant to this chapter, excepting only as such power is limited by express provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
