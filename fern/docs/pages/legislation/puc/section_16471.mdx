# PUC § 16471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 3. Utility Works and Services

**Citation:** PUC § 16471

---

## Text

If the assessment is divided and made payable in two installments, the unpaid charges may be added to and become a part of the first installment of the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
