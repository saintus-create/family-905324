# PUC § 102027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 102027

---

## Text

“Voting entity” means a member entity or a participating entity. “Voting entities” means all member entities and participating entities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 272, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2007.)
