# PUC § 99666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 4. Applications for Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99666

---

## Text

The eligibility of an applicant agency for a grant pursuant to a section of Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 99620) does not make it ineligible to apply for and receive a grant pursuant to any other provision of that chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
