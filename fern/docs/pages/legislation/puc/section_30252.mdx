# PUC § 30252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30252

---

## Text

The board of directors is the legislative body of the district and, consistent with the provisions of this part, shall determine all questions of district policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
