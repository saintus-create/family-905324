# PUC § 102015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 102015

---

## Text

“City” means, individually, the Cities of Citrus Heights, Davis, Elk Grove, Folsom, Rancho Cordova, Roseville, Sacramento, West Sacramento, and Woodland, and any other city that is annexed to the district as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 620, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
