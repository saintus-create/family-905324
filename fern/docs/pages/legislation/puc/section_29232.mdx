# PUC § 29232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 5. Temporary Borrowing

**Citation:** PUC § 29232

---

## Text

The treasurers of the counties within the district shall pay into the treasury of the district all funds held by them to the credit of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
