# PUC § 130381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 8. Transit Coordination and Service Program

**Citation:** PUC § 130381

---

## Text

The Los Angeles County Transportation Commission shall adopt by eight affirmative votes of the voting members, or designated alternates, a transit coordination and service program not later than January 10, 1981, after a public hearing held at least 30 days prior to its adoption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 579.)
