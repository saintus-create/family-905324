# PUC § 1402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 8. Determination of Just Compensation for Acquisition of Utility Properties

**Citation:** PUC § 1402

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “political subdivision” means a county, city and county, city, municipal water district, county water district, irrigation district, public utility district, or any other public corporation.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
