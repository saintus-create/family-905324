# PUC § 95162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 3. Request by Petition

**Citation:** PUC § 95162

---

## Text

The petition may be on separate papers, but each paper shall contain the affidavit of the person who circulated it certifying that each name signed thereto is a true signature of the person whose name it purports to be.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
