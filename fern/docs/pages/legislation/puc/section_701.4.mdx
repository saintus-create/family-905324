# PUC § 701.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** PUC § 701.4

---

## Text

It is the policy of the state and the intent of the Legislature that state and municipal electric resource acquisition programs recognize and include a value for the resource diversity provided by renewable resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1225, Sec. 3.)
