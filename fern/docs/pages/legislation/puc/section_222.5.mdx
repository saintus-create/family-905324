# PUC § 222.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 222.5

---

## Text

“Golden State Energy” means the nonprofit public benefit corporation that is incorporated and operating pursuant to Division 1.7 (commencing with Section 3400).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 5. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
