# PUC § 103570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Improvement Acts and Special Benefit Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 103570

---

## Text

The Improvement Act of 1911, the Municipal Improvement Act of 1913, and the Improvement Bond Act of 1915 are applicable to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
