# PUC § 30503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 30503

---

## Text

The district may exercise the right of eminent domain within the boundaries of the district to take any property necessary, incidental, or convenient to the exercise of the powers granted in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1322, Sec. 2.)
