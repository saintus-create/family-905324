# PUC § 40263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 5. Validation

**Citation:** PUC § 40263

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds, including refunding bonds, may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1899.)
