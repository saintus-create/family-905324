# PUC § 13375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 7.5. Short-Term Borrowing > Article: 1. Proceedings for Incurring Short-Term Indebtedness

**Citation:** PUC § 13375

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the term “revenues of the district” shall have the same meaning as is provided in Section 54315 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1117.)
