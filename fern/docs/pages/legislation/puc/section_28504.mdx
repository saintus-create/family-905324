# PUC § 28504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 28504

---

## Text

“San Francisco Bay area,” as used in this part, means the Counties of San Francisco, Marin, Sonoma, Napa, Solano, Contra Costa, Alameda, Santa Clara and San Mateo.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
