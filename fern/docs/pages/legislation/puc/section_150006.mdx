# PUC § 150006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 150006

---

## Text

“County of Tuolumne” and “Tuolumne County” mean the entire territory of the County of Tuolumne, including the territory of incorporated cities within the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
