# PUC § 103050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 103050

---

## Text

The San Mateo County Transit District may be formed as provided in this chapter and, when so formed, may exercise the powers herein granted.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
