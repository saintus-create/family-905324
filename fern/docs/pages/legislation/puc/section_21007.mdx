# PUC § 21007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21007

---

## Text

Whenever the term “California Aeronautics Commission,” “Division of Aeronautics,” or “Department of Aeronautics” is used in any other law, it means the Department of Transportation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 725, Sec. 4.)
