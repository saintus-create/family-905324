# PUC § 1099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 5. Certificates of Public Convenience and Necessity > Article: 5. Electrical and Gas Corporations

**Citation:** PUC § 1099

---

## Text

Upon completion of the project and commencement of operations, the construction project board of consultants shall submit a final report of its findings and recommendations to the commission and the corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1253, Sec. 5.)
