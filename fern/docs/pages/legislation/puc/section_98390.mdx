# PUC § 98390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 98390

---

## Text

Territory may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in this chapter. Territory to be annexed need not be contiguous to the present district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
