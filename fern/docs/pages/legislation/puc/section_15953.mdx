# PUC § 15953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 1. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 15953

---

## Text

All unincorporated territories situated in the same county and included within the district shall be regarded as an entirety and as a territorial unit of the district. Each unit having a population of at least 5,000 is entitled to one director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
