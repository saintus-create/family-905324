# PUC § 28795

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 28795

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances shall be as follows: “Be it enacted by the Board of Directors of the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District:”

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
