# PUC § 3367.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER POWER AND CONSERVATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. The California Consumer Power and Conservation Financing Authority > Article: 6. Renewable Energy and Conservation

**Citation:** PUC § 3367.5

---

## Text

The authority may require that a participating party utilize a consumer protection plan for screening qualified contractors who serve consumers under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 10, Sec. 1. Effective August 13, 2001.)
