# PUC § 50210.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 50210.5

---

## Text

The district may only levy and collect taxes pursuant to this article within the Stockton Metropolitan Area, as defined in Section 50007.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 704, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
