# PUC § 120200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 120200

---

## Text

The board has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 294.)
