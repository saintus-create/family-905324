# PUC § 2729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 3. Private and Mutual Water Service Law

**Citation:** PUC § 2729

---

## Text

A mutual water company may exercise the power of eminent domain for water, water rights, canals, ditches, dams, poundings, flumes, aqueducts, and pipes for irrigation of lands furnished with water by such company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1240.)
