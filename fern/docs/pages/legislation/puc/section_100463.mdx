# PUC § 100463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 3. Equipment Trust Certificates

**Citation:** PUC § 100463

---

## Text

The covenants, conditions, and provisions of the agreements, leases, and equipment trust certificates shall not conflict with any of the provisions of any trust agreement securing the payment of bonds, notes, or certificates of the VTA.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 98. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
