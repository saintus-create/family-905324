# PUC § 50272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 50272

---

## Text

The resolution shall be passed by a vote of two-thirds of the members of the board and be approved by the board of supervisors of the county in which the territory is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
