# PUC § 16077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 16077

---

## Text

All tax levies for a purpose other than interest or sinking fund payments shall be first authorized by the board by ordinance when the amount proposed to be raised by a tax levy exceeds fifty thousand dollars ($50,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 709, Sec. 2.)
