# PUC § 125488

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 9. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 125488

---

## Text

The board has no power to impose any tax other than the transactions and use tax imposed upon approval of the voters in accordance with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 658, Sec. 10. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2018.)
