# PUC § 99268.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 4. Claims for Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99268.18

---

## Text

The exclusions contained in Sections 99268.10, 99268.16, and 99268.17 shall not be applicable for purposes of determining an operator’s compliance with Section 99268.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 800, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
