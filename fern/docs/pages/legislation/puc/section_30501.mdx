# PUC § 30501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 30501

---

## Text

The district and its officers may sue and be sued in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
