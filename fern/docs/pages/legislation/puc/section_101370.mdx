# PUC § 101370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 12. Change of Organization

**Citation:** PUC § 101370

---

## Text

As used in this article the terms “change of organization” and “reorganization” shall have the meanings defined in Section 56021 and Section 56073, respectively, of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 85. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
