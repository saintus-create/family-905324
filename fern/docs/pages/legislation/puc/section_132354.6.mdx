# PUC § 132354.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 5. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 132354.6

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the consolidated agency shall not have any authority over local land use decisions affecting permitting or zoning of public or private development projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
