# PUC § 6296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 2. Gas, Oil, Electric, and Water Franchises by Municipalities > Article: 4. Duties and Liabilities of Grantee

**Citation:** PUC § 6296

---

## Text

The grantee shall indemnify and hold harmless the municipality and its officers from all liability for damages proximately resulting from any operations under the franchise.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
