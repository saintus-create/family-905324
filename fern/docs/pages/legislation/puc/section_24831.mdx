# PUC § 24831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24831

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the returns of the canvass by the respective boards of supervisors the board shall meet and determine results of the election and declare the candidate or candidates elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
