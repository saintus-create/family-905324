# PUC § 30433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 30433

---

## Text

Nothing in any pension or retirement system or plan shall prevent the board from, at any time, amending, changing, modifying or terminating any provision for benefits, participation, or contributions thereto or thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
