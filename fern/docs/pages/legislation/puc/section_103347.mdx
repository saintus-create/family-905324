# PUC § 103347

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 9. Taxation for Bond Redemption Purposes

**Citation:** PUC § 103347

---

## Text

Whenever any real property has been sold for taxes and has been redeemed, the money paid for redemption shall be apportioned and paid to the district by the county controller pursuant to provisions of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
