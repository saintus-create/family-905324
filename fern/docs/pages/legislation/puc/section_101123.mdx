# PUC § 101123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 3. Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** PUC § 101123

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business. The adoption of any minute order, resolution, or ordinance shall require the affirmative votes of at least three members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
