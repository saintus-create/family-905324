# PUC § 181012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.1. SONOMA COUNTY REGIONAL CLIMATE PROTECTION AUTHORITY

**Citation:** PUC § 181012

---

## Text

The authority may sue and be sued, except as provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 375, Sec. 1. (AB 881) Effective January 1, 2010.)
