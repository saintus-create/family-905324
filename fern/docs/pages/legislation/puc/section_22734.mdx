# PUC § 22734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 2. Bond Election

**Citation:** PUC § 22734

---

## Text

The board shall appoint one inspector, one judge, and two clerks of election in each precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
