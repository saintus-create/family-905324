# PUC § 105233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Bonds and Other Evidence of Indebtedness > Article: 3. Equipment Trust Certificates

**Citation:** PUC § 105233

---

## Text

The covenants, conditions, and provisions of the agreements, leases, and equipment trust certificates shall not conflict with any of the provisions of any trust agreement securing the payment of bonds, notes, or certificates of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
