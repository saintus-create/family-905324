# PUC § 101011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 101011

---

## Text

“Transit” means the transportation of passengers by any means and includes the transportation of the incidental baggage of passengers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
