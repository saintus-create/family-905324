# PUC § 101124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 3. Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** PUC § 101124

---

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by minute order, resolution, or ordinance. District ordinances shall be adopted by the board in substantially the same manner as prescribed for the adoption of county ordinances by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
