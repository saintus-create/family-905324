# PUC § 103017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 103017

---

## Text

“System” means all transit works and transit facilities owned or held, or to be owned or held, by the district for transit purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
