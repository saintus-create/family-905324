# PUC § 130354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 6. Transactions and Use Taxes

**Citation:** PUC § 130354

---

## Text

The revenues received by the Los Angeles County Transportation Commission from the imposition of the transactions and use taxes shall be used for public transit purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
