# PUC § 132405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. Pasadena Metro Blue Line Construction Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 132405

---

## Text

The authority is hereby created for the purpose of awarding and overseeing all design and construction contracts for completion of the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1021, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
