# PUC § 17378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17378

---

## Text

After the hearing, the board of directors of the annexing district may adopt a resolution ordering and approving the annexation.
