# PUC § 17370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17370

---

## Text

The resolution of the board of directors of the annexing district shall describe the territory proposed to be annexed and set forth the terms and conditions at length upon which the annexation is proposed to be made, and declare the board’s intention of approving such annexation upon the terms and conditions therein specified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
