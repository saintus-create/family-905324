# PUC § 16196

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 7. Employees

**Citation:** PUC § 16196

---

## Text

The manager, engineer, clerk, accountant, and treasurer shall not be included within the classified civil service of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
