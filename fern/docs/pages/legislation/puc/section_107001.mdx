# PUC § 107001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 18. GOLD COAST TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 107001

---

## Text

The purpose of the transit district is to develop, provide, operate, and administer public transportation and to protect the public interest, health, and welfare.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 503, Sec. 1. (AB 664) Effective January 1, 2014.)
