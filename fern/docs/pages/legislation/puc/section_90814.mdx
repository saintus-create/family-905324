# PUC § 90814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation of Cities > Article: 3. Approval by City

**Citation:** PUC § 90814

## Text

If the proposition fails to carry, the result shall be entered upon the minutes of the governing body of the city.
