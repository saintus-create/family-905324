# PUC § 105075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 105075

---

## Text

The district is a local agency for purposes of the Natural Disaster Assistance Act, Chapter 7.5 (commencing with Section 8680) of Division 1 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
