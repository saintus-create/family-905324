# PUC § 773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 3. Equipment, Practices, and Facilities

**Citation:** PUC § 773

---

## Text

Section 4200 of the Government Code shall not apply to a public utility under the jurisdiction of the Public Utilities Commission of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2128.)
