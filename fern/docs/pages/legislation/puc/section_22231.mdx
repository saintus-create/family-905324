# PUC § 22231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 22231

---

## Text

The canvass of the returns of the election shall be made at the time, in the manner, and by the officers prescribed by law for special elections in each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
