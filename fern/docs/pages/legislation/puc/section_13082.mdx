# PUC § 13082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 13082

---

## Text

“Bonds” includes the meaning of “notes, certificates and other evidences of indebtedness” and in every case refers only to bonds issued under this chapter, whether so specified or not.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
