# PUC § 28502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 28502

---

## Text

The part shall be liberally construed to carry out the objects and purposes and the declared policy of the State of California as in this part set forth.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
