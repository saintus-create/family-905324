# PUC § 22403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 22403

---

## Text

Within 30 days after notice of election or appointment each member of the board shall qualify by taking the official oath of office and filing it with the clerk of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
