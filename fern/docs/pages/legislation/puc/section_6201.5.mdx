# PUC § 6201.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 2. Gas, Oil, Electric, and Water Franchises by Municipalities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 6201.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, municipality includes counties, but no county shall grant a franchise pursuant to this chapter in any incorporated area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 422.)
