# PUC § 3355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER POWER AND CONSERVATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. The California Consumer Power and Conservation Financing Authority > Article: 5. Generation Facilities

**Citation:** PUC § 3355

---

## Text

The authority may not invest in any nuclear facilities or develop additional hydroelectric facilities without first receiving specific statutory authorization to do so on a project-by-project basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 10, Sec. 1. Effective August 13, 2001.)
