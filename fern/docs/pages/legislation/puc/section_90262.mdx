# PUC § 90262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

## Text

The general manager need not be a resident of this state at the time of his appointment.
