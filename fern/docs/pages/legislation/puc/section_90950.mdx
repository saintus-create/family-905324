# PUC § 90950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 90950

---

## Text

The board shall by ordinance setting forth the terms and conditions at length declare its intention of causing the agreement to be approved by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
