# PUC § 27427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 27427

---

## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto the following: Shall the “(giving name of special transit service district)” be created and established in “(giving name of public agency or portion thereof)”?YESNO

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
