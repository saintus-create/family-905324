# PUC § 16607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 7. Receipts, Accounts, and Audits

**Citation:** PUC § 16607

---

## Text

The expense of employing an expert shall not exceed fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000) at any one time, to be paid out of the income and revenues of the district, as the board prescribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 83, Sec. 2.)
