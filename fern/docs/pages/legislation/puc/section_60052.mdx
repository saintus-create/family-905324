# PUC § 60052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. YOLO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 60052

---

## Text

The district shall act as a countywide forum for the coordination of transportation system planning, programming, and prioritization of significant projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 457, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
