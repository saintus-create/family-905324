# PUC § 29154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 1. Authorization of General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29154

---

## Text

After adoption by the board of the reports made pursuant to Section 29152 the board shall refer such reports to the boards of supervisors of the counties comprising the district for their approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
