# PUC § 21018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21018

---

## Text

“Airway” means a route in the navigable air space over the land or waters of this State, designated by proper authority as a route suitable for air navigation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
