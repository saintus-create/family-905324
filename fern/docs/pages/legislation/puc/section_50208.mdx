# PUC § 50208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Investments

**Citation:** PUC § 50208

---

## Text

Sales of any investments purchased and held by the district shall from time to time be made in season so that the proceeds may be applied to the purposes for which the money with which the investments were originally purchased was placed in the treasury of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 216, Sec. 5. (AB 1853) Effective January 1, 2025.)
