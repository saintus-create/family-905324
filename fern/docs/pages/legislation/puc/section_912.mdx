# PUC § 912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 912

---

## Text

A summary of the audits conducted by the commission pursuant to Section 314.5 shall be provided annually to the Legislature. The commission may provide this information as part of its annual report delivered pursuant to Section 910.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 612, Sec. 34. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2016.)
