# PUC § 100169

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 100169

---

## Text

The VTA and any one or more school districts located within its boundaries may enter into agreements pursuant to which school transportation equipment may be used for transit purposes within the VTA’s boundaries during any time that the equipment is not actually required for school purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 63. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
