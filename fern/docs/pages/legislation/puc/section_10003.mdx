# PUC § 10003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 1. Acquisition and Operation

**Citation:** PUC § 10003

---

## Text

The power to acquire and operate a public utility includes the power to complete, reconstruct, extend, change, enlarge, and repair a public utility acquired, constructed, owned, or operated by a municipality.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
