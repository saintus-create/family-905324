# PUC § 12103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Civil Service System > Article: 2. Appointments

**Citation:** PUC § 12103

---

## Text

The general manager may make appointments to higher positions exclusively promotional by tests from lower ranks.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
