# PUC § 25304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** PUC § 25304

---

## Text

The board may adopt all ordinances and resolutions and perform all acts necessary or convenient to the initiation, maintenance, and administration of the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
