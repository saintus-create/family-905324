# PUC § 132314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 5. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 132314

---

## Text

The commission has no power to impose any tax other than the transactions and use tax imposed upon approval of the voters in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
