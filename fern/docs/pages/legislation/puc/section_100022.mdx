# PUC § 100022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 100022

---

## Text

“Transportation works” or “transportation facilities” means any or all real and personal property, rights, or interests owned or to be acquired by the VTA to facilitate the passage of vehicles or pedestrians exclusive of transit works and transit facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 10. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
