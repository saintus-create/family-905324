# PUC § 102018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 102018

---

## Text

“System” means all transit works and transit facilities owned or held, or to be owned or held, or used, by the district for transit purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 522, Sec. 2. (AB 709) Effective January 1, 2019.)
