# PUC § 130512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 130512

---

## Text

“Commission” means the Los Angeles County Transportation Commission created pursuant to Section 130050 and any board, commission, department, or officer succeeding to the functions thereof or to whom the powers conferred upon the commission by this division is granted by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
