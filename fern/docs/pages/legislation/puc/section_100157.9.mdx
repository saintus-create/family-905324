# PUC § 100157.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 4.5. Job Order Contracting

**Citation:** PUC § 100157.9

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 803, Sec. 2. (AB 2763) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 4.5 commencing with Sec. 100157.)
