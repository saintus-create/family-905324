# PUC § 98132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 98132

---

## Text

The board shall establish rules for its proceedings and board members shall be allowed necessary traveling and personal expenses incurred in performance of duties authorized by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
