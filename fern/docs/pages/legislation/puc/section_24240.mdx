# PUC § 24240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24240

---

## Text

“Notification” means notice in writing served upon a person by either:

(a) Actual delivery or offer of delivery to such person by any adult individual whose rights are not affected by the proceeding involved; or

(b) Registered mail addressed to the person at the last address known to the division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1452.)
