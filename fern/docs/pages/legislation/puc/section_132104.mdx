# PUC § 132104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 132104

---

## Text

The commission shall do all of the following:

(a) Adopt an annual budget.

(b) Cause a postaudit of the financial transactions and records of the commission and of all revenues expended pursuant to this chapter to be made at least annually by a certified public accountant.

(c) Do any and all things necessary to carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
