# PUC § 125001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 125001

---

## Text

As used in this division, “district” means the North County Transit District.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 150, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
