# PUC § 7936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 3.5. New Area Codes

**Citation:** PUC § 7936

---

## Text

The commission shall direct the North American Numbering Plan Administrator to obtain utilization data for any area code for which a relief plan is proposed, prior to adopting a plan for, or setting a date for, relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 809, Sec. 4. Effective October 10, 1999.)
