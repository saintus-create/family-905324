# PUC § 24865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Terms of Office of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24865

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 1780 of the Government Code, the board shall fill all vacancies on the board, including those caused by the death or resignation of a member. If a vacancy exists for 90 days, the Governor shall fill the vacancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 200, Sec. 6. (AB 784) Effective January 1, 2022.)
