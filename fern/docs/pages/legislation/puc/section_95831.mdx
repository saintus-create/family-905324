# PUC § 95831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 95831

---

## Text

The retirement allowance may be predicated in part upon service rendered the district by a member prior to the establishment of the retirement system, which service is known as “prior service.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
