# PUC § 99204.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 99204.5

---

## Text

“Consolidated transportation service agency” means an agency designated pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 15975 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1120.)
