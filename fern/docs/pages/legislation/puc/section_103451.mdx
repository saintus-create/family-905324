# PUC § 103451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 4. Other Benefits

**Citation:** PUC § 103451

---

## Text

The district shall take such steps as may be necessary to obtain coverage for the district and its employees under the workmen’s compensation, unemployment compensation, and disability and unemployment insurance laws of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
