# PUC § 100061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Government of VTA > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 100061

---

## Text

The board of directors shall annually elect a chairperson who shall preside at all meetings. The board of directors shall also annually elect a vice chairperson, who, in the event of the chairperson’s absence or inability to act, shall act as chairperson, and, while so acting, shall have all of the power and obligations of the chairperson.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 23. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
