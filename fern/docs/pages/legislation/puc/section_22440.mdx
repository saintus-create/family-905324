# PUC § 22440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

## Text

The general manager shall report to the board in accordance with rules and regulations adopted by the board.
