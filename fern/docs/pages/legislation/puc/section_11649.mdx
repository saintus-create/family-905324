# PUC § 11649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11649

---

## Text

No person shall be entitled to vote at the election unless he is a voter of the territory included in the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
