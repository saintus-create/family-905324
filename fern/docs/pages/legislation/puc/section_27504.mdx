# PUC § 27504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 11. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 27504

---

## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and in addition the following: Shall the “(giving the name thereof) transit district” be dissolved?YESNO
