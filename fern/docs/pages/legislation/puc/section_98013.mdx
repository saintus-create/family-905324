# PUC § 98013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 98013

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this part all ordinances and notices which are required to be published shall be published within the district pursuant to Section 6066 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
