# PUC § 95521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 95521

---

## Text

All other things being equal, the board shall appoint as general manager, a person who has had experience in the construction or management of transit facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
