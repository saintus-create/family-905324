# PUC § 180002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 180002

---

## Text

“Authority” means a local transportation authority created or designated pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
