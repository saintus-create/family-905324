# PUC § 105305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Service Zones

**Citation:** PUC § 105305

---

## Text

The board may change the boundaries of a service zone or dissolve a zone by following the procedures in Section 105301.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
