# PUC § 98011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 98011

## Text

The Santa Cruz Metropolitan Transit System may be created as provided in this part and when so created may exercise the powers herein granted.
