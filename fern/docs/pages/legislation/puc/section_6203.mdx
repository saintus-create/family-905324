# PUC § 6203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 2. Gas, Oil, Electric, and Water Franchises by Municipalities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 6203

---

## Text

The legislative body may in such a franchise impose such other and additional terms and conditions not in conflict with this chapter, whether governmental or contractual in character, as in the judgment of the legislative body are to the public interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
