# PUC § 100491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 100491

---

## Text

All bonds and other evidences of indebtedness issued by the VTA under the provisions of this part, and the interest thereon, are free and exempt from all taxation within the State of California, except for transfer, franchise, inheritance, and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 104. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
