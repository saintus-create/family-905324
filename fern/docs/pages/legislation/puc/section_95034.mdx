# PUC § 95034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 95034

---

## Text

Whenever the signature of any officer or employee of a district or of any member of the retirement board or of any officer or employee of the retirement system is authorized or required under the provisions of this act, except in the single instance provided in Section 96443, the signature may be made by the use of a plate bearing facsimiles of such signatures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
