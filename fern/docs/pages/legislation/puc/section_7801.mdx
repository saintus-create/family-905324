# PUC § 7801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 2. Street Railroads

**Citation:** PUC § 7801

---

## Text

Street railroads are governed by Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 7501), so far as it is applicable, unless street railroads are therein specially excepted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 172.)
