# PUC § 29672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 29672

---

## Text

The time fixed for the hearing shall be not less than ten (10) nor more than thirty (30) days from the date of the publication of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1495.)
