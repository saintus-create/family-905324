# PUC § 3180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** PUC § 3180

---

## Text

No public utility, corporation holding a controlling interest in a public utility, or subsidiary or affiliate of a public utility may be a member of an association organized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
