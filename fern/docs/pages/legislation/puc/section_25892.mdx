# PUC § 25892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 25892

---

## Text

If, in the opinion of the board, the revenues will not be sufficient for any and all lawful purposes the board shall levy a tax for such purpose or purposes and fix the amount of money necessary to be raised therefor by taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
