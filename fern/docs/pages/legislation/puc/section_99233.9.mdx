# PUC § 99233.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 3. Local Transportation Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99233.9

---

## Text

There shall be allocated to counties and cities such money as is approved by the transportation planning agency for claims presented pursuant to Article 8 (commencing with Section 99400) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1348.)
