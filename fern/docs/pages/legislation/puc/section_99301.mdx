# PUC § 99301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 99301

---

## Text

Interest earned on funds allocated pursuant to this chapter shall be expended only for those purposes for which the funds were allocated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1348.)
