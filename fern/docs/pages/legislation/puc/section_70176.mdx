# PUC § 70176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 70176

---

## Text

Exercise of the powers provided under Section 70175 shall be undertaken in the same manner and pursuant to the same procedures as in the exercise of like powers by the County of Marin, except as may otherwise be expressly provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
