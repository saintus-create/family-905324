# PUC § 21669.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 3. Regulation of Airports

**Citation:** PUC § 21669.3

---

## Text

Any regulations designed to establish a noise monitoring program at an airport entering service after November 30, 1971, shall go into effect on the date the airport enters service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 725, Sec. 8.)
