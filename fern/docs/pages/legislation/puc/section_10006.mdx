# PUC § 10006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 1. Acquisition and Operation

**Citation:** PUC § 10006

---

## Text

No lease of a public utility is valid for a period of more than 15 years, and all such leases shall be let to the highest bidder at public auction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
