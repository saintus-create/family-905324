# PUC § 2815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 7. Private Energy Producers > Article: 2. Interconnection of Facilities

**Citation:** PUC § 2815

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall require that any private energy producer perform any service or deliver any commodity to the public or any portion thereof, for compensation or otherwise, except as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 915.)
