# PUC § 40612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Consolidation with Other Districts

## Text

If the proposition fails to carry, the result shall be entered upon the minutes of the Board of Supervisors of Orange County.
