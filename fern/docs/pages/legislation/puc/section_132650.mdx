# PUC § 132650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. Exposition Metro Line Construction Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 132650

---

## Text

The authority shall be dissolved upon completion of construction of the light rail project. The LACMTA shall assume responsibility for operating the project upon completion of the project or any of its phases.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 808, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2007.)
