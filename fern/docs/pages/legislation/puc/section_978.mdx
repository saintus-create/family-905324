# PUC § 978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4.5. Gas Pipeline Safety > Article: 3. Methane Leakage Abatement

**Citation:** PUC § 978

---

## Text

Except as expressly so provided, this article does not expand or in any manner alter the commission’s jurisdiction over the regulation of emissions of greenhouse gases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 525, Sec. 2. (SB 1371) Effective January 1, 2015.)
