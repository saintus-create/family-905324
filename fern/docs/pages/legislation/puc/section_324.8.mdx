# PUC § 324.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2. The Public Utilities Commission: Organization

**Citation:** PUC § 324.8

---

## Text

The commission shall make available on its Internet Web site audits conducted by the Department of General Services of the commission’s contracting practices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 815, Sec. 3. (AB 1651) Effective January 1, 2017.)
