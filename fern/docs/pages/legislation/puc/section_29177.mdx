# PUC § 29177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 2. Form and Terms of General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29177

---

## Text

Bonds may be serial or sinking fund bonds, or in part serially and in part sinking fund bonds, with such maturities and in such amounts annually, semiannually or otherwise, as the district may determine at the time of authorizing the issuance thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
