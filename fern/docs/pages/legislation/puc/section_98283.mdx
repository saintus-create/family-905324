# PUC § 98283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 98283

---

## Text

The board shall avail itself of the assessments made by the assessor of the county and of the assessments made by the State Board of Equalization for the county and shall take such assessments as the basis for the district taxation and have its taxes collected by the tax collector of the county, as part of the annual tax bill.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
