# PUC § 103290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 103290

---

## Text

No interconnection shall be made into fixed right-of-way facilities of the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District without the approval of the voters of the district at an election called for the purpose of securing such approval. Any fixed right-of-way facilities shall be in compliance with Section 66515.5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
