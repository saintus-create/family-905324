# PUC § 27505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 11. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 27505

---

## Text

No other notice of the election other than that provided for in this chapter need be given and no sample ballots need be sent to the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
