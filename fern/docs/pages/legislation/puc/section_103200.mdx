# PUC § 103200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 103200

---

## Text

The district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
