# PUC § 132003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 132003

---

## Text

“Commission” means the San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission created pursuant to this chapter in the County of San Diego.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
