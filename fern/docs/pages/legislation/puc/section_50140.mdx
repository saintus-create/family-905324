# PUC § 50140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 50140

---

## Text

The board may prescribe the terms and conditions upon which the officers and employees of the district or their beneficiaries shall be entitled to benefits and the amounts thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
