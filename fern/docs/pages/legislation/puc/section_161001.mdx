# PUC § 161001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. STATE TRANSPORTATION CORRIDOR PRESERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 161001

---

## Text

As used in this division, “commission” means the California Transportation Commission.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 781, Sec. 1.)
