# PUC § 11857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2.7. Sacramento Municipal Utility District

**Citation:** PUC § 11857

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, in the Sacramento Municipal Utility District, the number of directors on the district board shall be increased from five to seven as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 845, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
