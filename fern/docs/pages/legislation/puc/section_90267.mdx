# PUC § 90267

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 90267

---

## Text

The secretary and such assistants as the board may determine, and shall have the power to administer all oaths or affirmations required by this act, including the oath of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
