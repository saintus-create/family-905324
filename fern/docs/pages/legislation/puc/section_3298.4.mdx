# PUC § 3298.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 6. Wildfire > Chapter: 4. The Continuation Account

**Citation:** PUC § 3298.4

---

## Text

The Director of Finance may, at any time, examine the books and records of the council and the administrator relating to the operation, management, and administration of the account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 119, Sec. 45. (SB 254) Effective September 19, 2025. Conditionally inoperative pursuant to Section 3298.6. Repealed as of January 1 following inoperative date.)
