# PUC § 30533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 30533

## Text

The district may insure against any accident to or destruction of the system or any part thereof.

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
