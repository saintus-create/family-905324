# PUC § 99267

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 4. Claims for Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99267

---

## Text

If an operator ceases operations, any capital acquisitions made by that operator from funds allocated to it pursuant to this article may be transferred to any claimant to provide transportation services under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 630, Sec. 7.)
