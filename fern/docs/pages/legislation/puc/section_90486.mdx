# PUC § 90486

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 90486

---

## Text

The rates and charges for service furnished pursuant to this act shall be fixed by the board and shall be reasonable.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
