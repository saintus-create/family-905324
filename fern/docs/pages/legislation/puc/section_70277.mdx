# PUC § 70277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 70277

---

## Text

Whenever any territory is annexed to the district it shall thereupon become a part of the district subject to all the liabilities and entitled to all the benefits of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
