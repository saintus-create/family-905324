# PUC § 6452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 3. Resettlement of Street, Suburban, and Interurban Railroad Franchises

**Citation:** PUC § 6452

---

## Text

The resettlement franchise shall be granted after such publication and upon such notice as the governing or legislative body shall by resolution determine, or failing such determination, after such publication and upon such notice as is prescribed by law for the enactment of ordinances by the governing or legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
