# PUC § 100120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 100120

---

## Text

The VTA may make contracts and enter into stipulations of any nature whatsoever, either in connection with eminent domain proceedings or otherwise, including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, contracts and stipulations to indemnify and save harmless, to employ labor, and to do all acts necessary and convenient for the full exercise of the powers granted in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 40. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
