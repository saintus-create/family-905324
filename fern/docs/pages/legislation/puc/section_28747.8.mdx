# PUC § 28747.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 2.2. District Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28747.8

---

## Text

The candidate from each election district receiving the highest number of votes cast for the board in that election district shall be declared elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 521.)
