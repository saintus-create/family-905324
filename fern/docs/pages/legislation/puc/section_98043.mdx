# PUC § 98043

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 3. Request by Petition

**Citation:** PUC § 98043

---

## Text

The County Elections Official of the County of Santa Cruz shall compare the signatures to the petition with the affidavits of registration and certify to their sufficiency or insufficiency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2003.)
