# PUC § 50270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 50270

---

## Text

Territory may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in this chapter.
