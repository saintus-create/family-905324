# PUC § 10051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 2. Sale and Disposal

**Citation:** PUC § 10051

---

## Text

Any municipal corporation incorporated under the laws of this State may as provided in this article sell and dispose of any public utility that it owns.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
