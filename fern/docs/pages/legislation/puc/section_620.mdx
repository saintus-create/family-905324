# PUC § 620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 3. Rights and Obligations of Public Utilities > Article: 7. Eminent Domain

**Citation:** PUC § 620

---

## Text

A common carrier, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 211, may condemn any property necessary for the construction and maintenance of facilities for its transportation of persons or property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1240.)
