# PUC § 29714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 29714

---

## Text

The secretary of the district shall compare the signatures with the affidavits of registration on file with the county elections official, and shall certify to the board as to the sufficiency or insufficiency of the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 104. Effective January 1, 2003.)
