# PUC § 99299

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 5. Relationships Between Operators

**Citation:** PUC § 99299

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall control over the provisions of any other act or law applicable to a transit district to the extent of any conflict with such provisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 99285 by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1408.)
