# PUC § 101355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Retirement

**Citation:** PUC § 101355

---

## Text

Subject to Sections 101356 and 101357, the board shall provide for retirement benefits for the officers and employees of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 372, Sec. 1.)
