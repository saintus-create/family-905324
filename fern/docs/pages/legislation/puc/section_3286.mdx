# PUC § 3286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 6. Wildfire > Chapter: 2. The Wildfire Fund

**Citation:** PUC § 3286

---

## Text

The Director of Finance may, at any time, examine the books and records of the council and the administrator relating to the operation, management, and administration of the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 79, Sec. 16. (AB 1054) Effective July 12, 2019.)
