# PUC § 28762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28762

---

## Text

The board of directors is the legislative body of the district and, consistent with the provisions of this part, shall determine all questions of district policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
