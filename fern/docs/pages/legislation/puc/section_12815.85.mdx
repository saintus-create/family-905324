# PUC § 12815.85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Utility Works and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 12815.85

---

## Text

The secretary shall attach to the petition, a certificate showing the result of the signature examination, and shall notify the proponents of either the sufficiency or insufficiency of the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1098.)
