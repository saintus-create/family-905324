# PUC § 4462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. REGULATION OF RELATED BUSINESSES BY THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION > Chapter: 4.1. Enforcement of Federal Pipeline Safety Standards for Propane Operators

**Citation:** PUC § 4462

---

## Text

(a) This chapter hereby creates a trust fund known as the propane safety inspection and enforcement program trust fund. The trust fund shall be administered by the commission.

(b) The trust fund shall be used to partially support the funding requirements of the propane safety inspection and enforcement program of the commission pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 651, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1997.)
