# PUC § 29225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29225

---

## Text

Refunding of revenue bonds of the district may be accomplished in the manner provided by the Revenue Bond Law of 1941, all of the provisions of which are hereby made applicable to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
