# PUC § 2778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 5. Electrical Cooperatives

**Citation:** PUC § 2778

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specified in this chapter, every electrical cooperative is subject to Part 1 (commencing with Section 201).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 170.)
