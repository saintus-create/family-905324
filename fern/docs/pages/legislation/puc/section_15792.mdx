# PUC § 15792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Establishment of the District

**Citation:** PUC § 15792

---

## Text

If it appears from the certificates that a majority of the electors voting at the election has voted against the formation of the district, the proceedings shall fail entirely.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
