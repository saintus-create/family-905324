# PUC § 12702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 12702

---

## Text

A district may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
