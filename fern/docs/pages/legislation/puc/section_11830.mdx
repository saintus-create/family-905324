# PUC § 11830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11830

---

## Text

Candidates for the office of director shall be voted upon at large, and every voter in the district may vote for all of the directors to be elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
