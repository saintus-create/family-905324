# PUC § 100110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 100110

---

## Text

The VTA has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 33. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
