# PUC § 3101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 6. Directors and Management

**Citation:** PUC § 3101

---

## Text

The affairs of the association shall be managed by a board of not less than three directors who are elected by the members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
