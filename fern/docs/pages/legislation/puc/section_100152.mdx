# PUC § 100152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 4. Construction Manager/General Contractor Project Delivery Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 100152

---

## Text

Any public works project that is contracted for pursuant to this article shall be subject to the requirements of Section 1771.4 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 85. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
