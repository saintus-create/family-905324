# PUC § 17370.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17370.2

---

## Text

After said resolution has been filed with the executive officer, proceedings thereon shall be taken by the local agency formation commission in the manner and subject to the provisions of Part 4 (commencing at Section 56250, Government Code) of the District Reorganization Act of 1965. If the resolution and proposed annexation are approved by the commission, with or without amendment, wholly, partially or conditionally, it shall be mandatory for the board of directors of the annexing district to complete the proceedings for such annexation, subject to compliance with the commission’s resolution making determinations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2043.)
