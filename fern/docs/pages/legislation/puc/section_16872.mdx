# PUC § 16872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 3. Joint Acquisition and Use of Sewage Facilities and Water Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16872

---

## Text

As used in this article, “sewage disposal facilities” includes sewers or other works or facilities for the handling, treatment, or disposal of sewage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
