# PUC § 3417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. GOLDEN STATE ENERGY ACT > Part: 1. General Provisions > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 3417

---

## Text

“Pacific Gas and Electric Company’s service territory” means the boundaries and specifications of the company’s service territory on the date of the closing of the acquisition of Pacific Gas and Electric Company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 12. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
