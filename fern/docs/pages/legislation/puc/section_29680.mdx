# PUC § 29680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 29680

---

## Text

At the time of the approval of the creation of a special service district, the board shall call an election in the territory proposed to be included in the special service district for the purpose of submitting to the voters in such territory the question of whether the special service district shall be created and established.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1495.)
