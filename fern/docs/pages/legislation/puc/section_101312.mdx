# PUC § 101312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 3. Issue and Sale

**Citation:** PUC § 101312

---

## Text

In lieu of the immediate levy of a tax to pay the interest, or any part thereof, on any bonded indebtedness incurred in accordance with this chapter, the board may, in the estimate of the amount of money necessary to be raised by the bonds, include a sum sufficient to pay interest on all of the bonds, or part thereof, during the period of acquisition, construction, or completion, but for no period in excess of five years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
