# PUC § 29540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 3. Approval by County

**Citation:** PUC § 29540

---

## Text

At any time after the board of directors of the district has finally approved the agreement of annexation the board of supervisors of the county to be annexed shall cause an election to be held in the county to determine whether the county will be annexed to the district upon the terms and conditions stated in the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
