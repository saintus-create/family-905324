# PUC § 99691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99691

---

## Text

The proceeds of bonds and notes issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Clean Air and Transportation Improvement Fund created by Section 99610.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
