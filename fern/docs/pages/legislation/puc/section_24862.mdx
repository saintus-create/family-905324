# PUC § 24862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Terms of Office of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24862

---

## Text

At each biennial general election, a number of directors corresponding to the number whose terms of office expire shall be elected for the term of four years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 66, Sec. 6. (AB 1141) Effective January 1, 2026.)
