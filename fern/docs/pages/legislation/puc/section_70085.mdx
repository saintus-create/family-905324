# PUC § 70085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 70085

---

## Text

All meetings of the board shall be conducted in a manner prescribed by the Ralph M. Brown Act, Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950), Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
