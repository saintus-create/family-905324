# PUC § 33019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 12. Special Benefit Assessment Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 33019

---

## Text

In the event of conflict with any other law, the provisions of this chapter shall prevail with respect to benefit districts within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1322, Sec. 4.)
