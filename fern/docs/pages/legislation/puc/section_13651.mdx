# PUC § 13651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 13651

---

## Text

Any public agency or portion thereof or unincorporated territory within the boundaries of a district (hereinafter referred to collectively as “territory”) not included within the boundaries of a special district may be annexed thereto in the manner provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
