# PUC § 28506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 28506

---

## Text

For the purpose of establishing the bonded debt limit of the district, “taxable property,” as used in this part, shall not include solvent credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
