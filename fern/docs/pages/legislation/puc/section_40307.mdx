# PUC § 40307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Taxation > Article: 1. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 40307

---

## Text

The board shall immediately, after fixing the rate of taxes as provided in Section 40306, transmit to the county auditor of the county a statement of the rate of taxes fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 508.)
