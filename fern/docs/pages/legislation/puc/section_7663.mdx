# PUC § 7663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 7. Operation

**Citation:** PUC § 7663

---

## Text

Whenever the Department of the California Highway Patrol or a designated local public safety agency responds to a railroad accident, the accident shall be reported to the Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 500. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
