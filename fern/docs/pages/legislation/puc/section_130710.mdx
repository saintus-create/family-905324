# PUC § 130710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. Code of Conduct for the Board of the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 130710

---

## Text

The MTA shall not contract with any of the following:

(a) Board members or their staff.

(b) Any profit-making firm or business in which a board member or member of their staff is an officer, principal, partner, or shareholder who owns more than 10 percent of the firm or business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 755, Sec. 26. (AB 3123) Effective January 1, 2025.)
