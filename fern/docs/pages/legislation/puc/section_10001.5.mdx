# PUC § 10001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 1. Acquisition and Operation

**Citation:** PUC § 10001.5

---

## Text

A municipal corporation that provides broadband Internet access services shall comply with the requirements of Article 12 (commencing with Section 53167) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 963, Sec. 6. (AB 1999) Effective January 1, 2019.)
