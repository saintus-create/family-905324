# PUC § 16113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 16113

---

## Text

When the district acquires, constructs, owns, or operates two or more public utilities, a general manager may be appointed and employed for each public utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
