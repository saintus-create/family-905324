# PUC § 142151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. FRESNO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 142151

---

## Text

The authority may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 4. Effective July 14, 1986. Repealed on date prescribed in Section 142010.)
