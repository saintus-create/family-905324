# PUC § 50207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Investments

**Citation:** PUC § 50207

---

## Text

Any investments purchased and held by the district may from time to time be sold and the proceeds reinvested in investments as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 216, Sec. 4. (AB 1853) Effective January 1, 2025.)
