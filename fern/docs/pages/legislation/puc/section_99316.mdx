# PUC § 99316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 6.5. Transportation Planning and Development Account

**Citation:** PUC § 99316

---

## Text

Funds made available pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 99315 shall be appropriated to the department for allocation, as directed by the commission, for purposes of bus and passenger rail services pursuant to Sections 14035, 14035.5, and 14038 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 105, Sec. 29. Effective July 10, 1989.)
