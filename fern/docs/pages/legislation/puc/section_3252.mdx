# PUC § 3252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 2. Gas Producer Acquisition of Easement Rights > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 3252

---

## Text

“Natural gas” means all gas produced in this state, natural or manufactured, except propane, for light, heat, or power.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 839, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
