# PUC § 2806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 7. Private Energy Producers > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 2806

---

## Text

“Fossil fuel” means a mixture of hydrocarbons including coal, petroleum, or natural gas, occurring in and extracted from underground deposits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 915.)
