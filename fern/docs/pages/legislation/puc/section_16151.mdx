# PUC § 16151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. District Elections

**Citation:** PUC § 16151

---

## Text

The biennial election for the election of directors shall be held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November of odd-numbered years. This election is the general district election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 14.)
