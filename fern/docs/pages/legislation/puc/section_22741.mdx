# PUC § 22741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 2. Bond Election

**Citation:** PUC § 22741

---

## Text

The board shall provide a booth or private room in which the voter may prepare his ballot in secret.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
