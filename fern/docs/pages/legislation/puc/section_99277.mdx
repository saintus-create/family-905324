# PUC § 99277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 4.5. Claims for Community Transit Services

**Citation:** PUC § 99277

---

## Text

Claimants may contract on the basis of competitive bidding to provide community transit services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 988, Sec. 13.)
