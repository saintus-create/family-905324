# PUC § 150203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 150203

---

## Text

(a) The county shall conduct the elections called pursuant to Section 150201.

(b) The elections shall be called and conducted in the same manner as provided by law for the conduct of elections by a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
