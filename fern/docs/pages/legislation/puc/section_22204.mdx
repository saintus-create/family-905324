# PUC § 22204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 3. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 22204

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each affected county may exclude any territory within the county that in its opinion will not be benefited by inclusion in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
