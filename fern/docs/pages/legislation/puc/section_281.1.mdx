# PUC § 281.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1.5. Advisory Boards

**Citation:** PUC § 281.1

---

## Text

Beginning January 1, 2022, the commission may impose the surcharge pursuant to paragraph (4) of subdivision (d) of Section 281 to fund the California Advanced Services Fund pursuant to Section 281 until December 31, 2032.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 658, Sec. 2. (AB 14) Effective October 8, 2021.)
