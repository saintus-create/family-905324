# PUC § 150007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 150007

---

## Text

“Electors” means the electors of the city with respect to a city ordinance, or the electors of the county with respect to a county ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
