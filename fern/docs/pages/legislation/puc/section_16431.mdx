# PUC § 16431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 2. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 16431

---

## Text

A district may take by grant, purchase, gift, devise, or lease, or otherwise acquire, hold and enjoy, and lease or dispose of, real and personal property of every kind within or without the district, when in the judgment of the board it is for the best interests of the district so to do.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
