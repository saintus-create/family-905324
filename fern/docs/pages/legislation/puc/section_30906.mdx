# PUC § 30906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 1. Authorization and Issuance of Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 30906

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the State, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
