# PUC § 103112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 103112

---

## Text

Any vacancy on the board shall be filled for the unexpired term by appointment by the body which originally appointed the member whose office has become vacant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
