# PUC § 70079

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 70079

---

## Text

Payment of demands against the district and disbursement of district funds shall be in the manner provided for demands against a county and disbursements of funds of a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
