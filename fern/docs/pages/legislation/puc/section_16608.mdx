# PUC § 16608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 7. Receipts, Accounts, and Audits

**Citation:** PUC § 16608

---

## Text

Experts shall be selected by the Public Utilities Commission, and their names and addresses certified to the board of directors of the district. Several experts may be so selected and certified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
