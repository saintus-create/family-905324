# PUC § 748.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 2. Rates

**Citation:** PUC § 748.1

---

## Text

Except for Golden State Energy, an electrical corporation or gas corporation shall not recover, through a rate approved by the commission, a fine or penalty.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 7. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
