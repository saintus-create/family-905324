# PUC § 132354.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 5. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 132354.4

---

## Text

The consolidated agency shall be excluded from the requirements of a “local agency” set forth in Section 53091 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
