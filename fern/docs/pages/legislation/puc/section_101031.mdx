# PUC § 101031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 101031

---

## Text

The territory of such district shall be composed of the entire City of Bakersfield and such portions of unincorporated territory of the county contiguous thereto as may be benefited by inclusion in the district.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 590.)
