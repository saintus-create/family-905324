# PUC § 132360.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 6.5. Adoption and Administration of a Regional Comprehensive Plan

**Citation:** PUC § 132360.4

---

## Text

Each member agency should review the actions that the consolidated agency makes on state and federally regulated or mandated items and report these actions to their respective jurisdiction for review.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 508, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
