# PUC § 28832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 6. General Manager

**Citation:** PUC § 28832

---

## Text

The general manager need not be a resident of this State at the time of his appointment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
