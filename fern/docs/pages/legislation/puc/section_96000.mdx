# PUC § 96000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 96000

---

## Text

The district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
