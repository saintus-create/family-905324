# PUC § 26532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 8. Annexation of Cities > Article: 6. Effect of Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 26532

---

## Text

No annexation of a city to a district shall operate to dissolve or terminate the legal existence of the city annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
