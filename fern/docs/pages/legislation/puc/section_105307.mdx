# PUC § 105307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Service Zones

**Citation:** PUC § 105307

---

## Text

As determined by the board, a service zone may provide any service at any level within its boundaries that the district may provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
