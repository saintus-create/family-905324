# PUC § 11927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 11927

---

## Text

All other things being equal, the board shall appoint as general manager some person who has had experience in municipal engineering or in the construction or management of public utilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
