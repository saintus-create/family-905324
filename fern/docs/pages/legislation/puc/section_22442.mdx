# PUC § 22442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** PUC § 22442

---

## Text

The board shall designate depositories for the custody of the funds of the district. A depository shall give security sufficient to secure the district against possible loss and shall pay the warrants drawn by the auditor for demands against the district under such rules as the board may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
