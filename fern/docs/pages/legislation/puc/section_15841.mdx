# PUC § 15841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 6. Verification Deputies

**Citation:** PUC § 15841

---

## Text

Verification deputies shall be qualified electors residing within the territory of the district or the proposed district for which they are appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
