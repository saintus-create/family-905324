# PUC § 28752.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 2.6. Board Selection Election

**Citation:** PUC § 28752.4

---

## Text

If a majority of the votes cast in the district on the proposition are in favor of the proposition, the members of the board of directors from that county shall be elected and shall serve as provided in Article 2.2 (commencing with Section 28745).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 521.)
