# PUC § 131116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 3. Retail Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 131116

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold as the agency imposing the retail transactions and use tax determines by resolution, and the bonds may be sold at a price below par, whether by negotiated or public sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
