# PUC § 26421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 8. Annexation of Cities > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 26421

---

## Text

The agreement shall become effective and be binding upon the district and the city when approved in the manner set forth in this chapter.

The board shall by ordinance setting forth the agreement at length declare its intention of causing it to be executed by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
