# PUC § 24506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24506

---

## Text

“Transit works” or “transit facilities” means all real and personal property, equipment, rights, or interests owned or to be acquired by the district for transit service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
