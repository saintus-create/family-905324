# PUC § 105104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 4. Rail Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 105104

---

## Text

The district shall work with the Federal Railroad Administration and any of its successor agencies to achieve safe, efficient, and compatible operations of both passenger rail and freight service along the rail line in the Counties of Sonoma and Marin.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 866, Sec. 5. (SB 904) Effective January 1, 2025.)
