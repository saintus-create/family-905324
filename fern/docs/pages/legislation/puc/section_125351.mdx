# PUC § 125351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 6. Transportation Funding

**Citation:** PUC § 125351

---

## Text

The district shall take all action necessary to obtain the maximum amount of funding available pursuant to Section 1602 of Title 49 of the United States Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 150, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 2006.)
