# PUC § 40222.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6.5. Existing Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 40222.5

---

## Text

The district shall not establish the proposed service or system, or maintain and operate the service or system until it has completed the purchase of the existing system or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1899.)
