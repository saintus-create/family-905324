# PUC § 130539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 4. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** PUC § 130539

---

## Text

All expenses incurred in carrying out the provisions of the trust agreement or resolution may be treated as a part of the cost of the operation of a project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
