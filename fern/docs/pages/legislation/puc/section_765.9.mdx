# PUC § 765.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 3. Equipment, Practices, and Facilities

**Citation:** PUC § 765.9

---

## Text

Federal funds available to the commission for rail safety inspection and enforcement purposes shall be allocated to eligible passenger and freight rail activities on a proportionate basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 767, Sec. 7.)
