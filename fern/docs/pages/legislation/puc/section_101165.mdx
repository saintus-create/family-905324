# PUC § 101165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 8. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 101165

---

## Text

The board shall appoint a secretary and an attorney to serve as legal counsel to the district. These officers shall serve at the pleasure of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
