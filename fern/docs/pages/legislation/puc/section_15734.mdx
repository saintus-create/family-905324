# PUC § 15734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 15734

---

## Text

If the signatures attached to the petition are sufficient, no defect in the contents of the petition or in the title to or form of the notice shall vitiate any proceedings thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
