# PUC § 90216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 90216

---

## Text

The board may from time to time contract for or employ any professional service required by the district or for the performance of work or service which cannot satisfactorily be performed by the regular employees of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
