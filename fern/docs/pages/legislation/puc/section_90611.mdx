# PUC § 90611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 90611

---

## Text

The votes of 60 percent of all the voters voting on the proposition at the election are required to authorize the issuance of general obligation bonds under this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 102.)
