# PUC § 100162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 100162

---

## Text

The VTA may lease or contract for the use of its transit facilities, or any portion thereof, to any operator, and may provide for subleases by an operator upon terms and conditions as it deems in the public interest. The word “operator” as used in this section means any public agency or any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 57. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
