# PUC § 16613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 7. Receipts, Accounts, and Audits

**Citation:** PUC § 16613

---

## Text

The expert shall enter upon his duties at least 90 days before the next impending biennial district election, and shall complete his examination and file his report at least 30 days before the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
