# PUC § 30630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Rapid Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 30630

---

## Text

The district may provide a rapid transit system for the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
