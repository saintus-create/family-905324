# PUC § 102120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 102120

---

## Text

The board of directors is the legislative body of the district and shall determine all questions of district policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
