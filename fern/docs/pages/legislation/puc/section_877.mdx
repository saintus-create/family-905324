# PUC § 877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 8. Universal Telephone Service

**Citation:** PUC § 877

---

## Text

Nothing in this article precludes the commission from changing any rate established pursuant to Section 873, either specifically or pursuant to any general restructuring of all telephone rates, charges, and classifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 163, Sec. 2. Effective July 16, 1987.)
