# PUC § 105060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 105060

---

## Text

The district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
