# PUC § 131000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Bay Area County Traffic and Transportation Funding Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
