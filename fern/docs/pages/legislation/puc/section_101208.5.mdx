# PUC § 101208.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. General Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 6. Existing Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 101208.5

---

## Text

Section 851 shall not apply to any contract for sale or sale of an existing system, or any portion thereof, pursuant to this article, and the Public Utilities Commission shall have no jurisdiction with respect thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
