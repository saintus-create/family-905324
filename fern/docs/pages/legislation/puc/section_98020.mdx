# PUC § 98020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 98020

## Text

The County of Santa Cruz may organize and incorporate the Santa Cruz Metropolitan Transit District.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
