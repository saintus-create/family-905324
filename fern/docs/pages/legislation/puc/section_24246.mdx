# PUC § 24246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24246

## Text

A discharge in bankruptcy shall not relieve any person from the requirements of this part.
