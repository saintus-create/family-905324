# PUC § 13485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 13485

---

## Text

The board may reduce or enlarge the boundaries of the proposed special district, but the boundaries as enlarged shall be within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
