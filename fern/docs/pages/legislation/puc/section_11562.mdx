# PUC § 11562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 11562

## Text

A request for the formation of a district may be made by resolution or by petition as set out in this chapter.
