# PUC § 8002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 5. Electrical Line Construction > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 8002

---

## Text

“Person” includes any commission, officer, agent, or employee of this State, or of any county, city, city and county, or other political subdivision thereof, and any other person, firm, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
