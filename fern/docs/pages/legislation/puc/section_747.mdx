# PUC § 747

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 2. Rates

**Citation:** PUC § 747

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the commission reduce rates for electricity and natural gas to the lowest amount possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 612, Sec. 18. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2016.)
