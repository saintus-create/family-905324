# PUC § 29125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Taxation > Article: 1. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 29125

---

## Text

Each county auditor shall, on or before the third Monday in August of each year, transmit to the board a statement in writing showing the total value of all property within the district as equalized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
