# PUC § 103202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 103202

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against the district are governed by Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code except as provided therein, or by other statutes or regulations expressly applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
