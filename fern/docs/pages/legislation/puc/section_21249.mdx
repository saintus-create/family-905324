# PUC § 21249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 2. Department of Transportation and State Aeronautics Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 21249

---

## Text

The department may confer or hold joint hearings with any federal agency in connection with any matter arising under this part or relating to the sound development of aeronautics.
