# PUC § 30335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 6. General Manager

**Citation:** PUC § 30335

---

## Text

The general manager shall attend meetings of the board and be entitled to participate in the deliberations of the board, but shall not have a vote as to any matter before the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
