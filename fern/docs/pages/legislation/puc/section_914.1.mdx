# PUC § 914.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 914.1

---

## Text

The commission shall annually report to the Legislature, in a document that can be made public, information relative to the actions undertaken by the commission implementing the lifeline telecommunications universal service program pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 873.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 612, Sec. 42. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2016.)
