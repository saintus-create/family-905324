# PUC § 22551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 22551

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this part, the powers of the district shall be exercised by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
