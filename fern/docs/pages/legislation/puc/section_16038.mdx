# PUC § 16038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 16038

---

## Text

The board may provide for an assistant to any officer of the district who may perform the acts his principal may perform, when authorized so to do by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
