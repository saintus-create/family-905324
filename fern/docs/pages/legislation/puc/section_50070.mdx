# PUC § 50070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 50070

---

## Text

The board shall annually, in January, select one of its members as chairperson to serve at the pleasure of the board. The chairperson shall preside at all meetings of the board and shall vote on the propositions passed upon by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 995, Sec. 4.)
