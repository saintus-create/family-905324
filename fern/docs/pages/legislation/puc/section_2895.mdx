# PUC § 2895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 10. Telephone Corporations > Article: 4. Telecommunication Services

**Citation:** PUC § 2895

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Telecommunications Customer Service Act of 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1233, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
