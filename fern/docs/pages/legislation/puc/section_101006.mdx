# PUC § 101006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 101006

---

## Text

“District” means the Golden Empire Transit District formed under the provisions of this part.
