# PUC § 27016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 3. Approval by Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 27016

---

## Text

If the proposition is defeated by the voters, no annexation proceeding shall be commenced involving the territory or any part thereof until after the expiration of one year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
