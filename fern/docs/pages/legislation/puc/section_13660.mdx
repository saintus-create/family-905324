# PUC § 13660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 13660

---

## Text

No annexation to a special district shall operate to dissolve or terminate the legal existence of any public agency within or partly within the territory annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
