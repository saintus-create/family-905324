# PUC § 70042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 70042

---

## Text

The election may be held on the same day as any other state, county, or city election, and be consolidated therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
