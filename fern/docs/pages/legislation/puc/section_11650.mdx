# PUC § 11650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11650

---

## Text

The election may be held on the same day as any other state, county, or city election, and be consolidated therewith.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
