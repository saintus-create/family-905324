# PUC § 100020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 100020

---

## Text

“Establish” includes establish, construct, complete, acquire, extend, or reroute. It does not, however, include the maintenance and operation of any existing system acquired by the VTA.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 9. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
