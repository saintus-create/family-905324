# PUC § 22014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 22014

---

## Text

“Bond election” means an election provided for in Article 2 (commencing with Section 22731) of Chapter 5.
