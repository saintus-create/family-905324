# PUC § 3400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. GOLDEN STATE ENERGY ACT > Part: 1. General Provisions > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Matters

**Citation:** PUC § 3400

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Golden State Energy Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 12. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
