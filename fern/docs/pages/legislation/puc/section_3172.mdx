# PUC § 3172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** PUC § 3172

---

## Text

If a member of a nonstock association is other than a natural person, that member may be represented by any individual, associate, officer, manager, or member of it who is authorized in writing to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
