# PUC § 101245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Audits and Financial Report

**Citation:** PUC § 101245

---

## Text

Within 15 days after the filing of the financial report, the general manager shall transmit a copy thereof to the board of supervisors and to the city council of each city within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
