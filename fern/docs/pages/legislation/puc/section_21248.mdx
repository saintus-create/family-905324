# PUC § 21248

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 2. Department of Transportation and State Aeronautics Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 21248

---

## Text

The department may exercise any of its powers under this part jointly with any political subdivision, state agency, other states or their political subdivisions, or the United States.
