# PUC § 6297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 2. Gas, Oil, Electric, and Water Franchises by Municipalities > Article: 4. Duties and Liabilities of Grantee

**Citation:** PUC § 6297

---

## Text

The grantee shall remove or relocate without expense to the municipality any facilities installed, used, and maintained under the franchise if and when made necessary by any lawful change of grade, alignment, or width of any public street, way, alley, or place, including the construction of any subway or viaduct, by the municipality.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
