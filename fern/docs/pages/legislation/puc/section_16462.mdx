# PUC § 16462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 3. Utility Works and Services

**Citation:** PUC § 16462

---

## Text

A district may also purchase any of such commodities or services from any other utility district, municipality, person, or private company, and distribute them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
