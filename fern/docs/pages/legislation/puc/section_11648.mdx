# PUC § 11648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11648

---

## Text

Candidates for the office of director shall be voted upon at large, and every voter in the proposed district may vote for all of the directors to be elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
