# PUC § 103311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Public Grants, Loans, and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 103311

---

## Text

The district shall have the power to obtain temporary transfers of funds in accordance with the last paragraph of Section 6 of Article XVI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 140, Sec. 166. (AB 1164) Effective January 1, 2010.)
