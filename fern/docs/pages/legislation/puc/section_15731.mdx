# PUC § 15731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 15731

---

## Text

By resolution each board of supervisors to which a petition is presented shall fix a time for a hearing, which shall be within 15 days after final publication of notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
