# PUC § 17361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17361

---

## Text

As used in this article, “annexing district” means any public utility district to which land is to be annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
