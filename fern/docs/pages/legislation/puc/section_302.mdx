# PUC § 302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2. The Public Utilities Commission: Organization

**Citation:** PUC § 302

---

## Text

Each commissioner shall, before entering upon the duties of his office, take and subscribe the constitutional oath of office.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
