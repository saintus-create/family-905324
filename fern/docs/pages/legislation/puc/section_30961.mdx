# PUC § 30961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 5. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 30961

---

## Text

Notice of said hearing shall be given by publishing a copy of the resolution declaring the necessity pursuant to Section 6066 of the Government Code prior to the time fixed for the hearing in at least one newspaper published in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
