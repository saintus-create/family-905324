# PUC § 7672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 7.5. Hazardous Materials Transportation by Rail

**Citation:** PUC § 7672

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “hazardous material” means either of the following:

(a) A hazardous material, as defined in Section 171.8 of Title 49 of the Code of Federal Regulations.

(b) A hazardous material, as defined in Section 25501 of the Health and Safety Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 644, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2005.)
