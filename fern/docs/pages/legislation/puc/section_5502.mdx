# PUC § 5502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. REGULATION OF RELATED BUSINESSES BY THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION > Chapter: 9. Commercial Air Carriers > Article: 1. Accident Liability Protection

**Citation:** PUC § 5502

---

## Text

As used in this article, “person” means any individual, firm, partnership, private, municipal or public corporation, limited liability company, company, association, joint stock association, trustee, receiver, assignee, or other similar representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 219. Effective January 1, 1995.)
