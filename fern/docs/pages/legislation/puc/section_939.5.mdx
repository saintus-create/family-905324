# PUC § 939.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 14.5. Powering Up Californians

**Citation:** PUC § 939.5

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to an electrical cooperative, as defined in Section 2776.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 394, Sec. 1. (SB 410) Effective January 1, 2024.)
