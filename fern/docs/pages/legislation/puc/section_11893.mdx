# PUC § 11893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11893

---

## Text

If a legal holiday falls on a Saturday, the board may provide by resolution that the Friday preceding is a holiday within the district for the purpose of closing its offices and excusing its employees from work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 401.)
