# PUC § 98006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 98006

---

## Text

“Transit works” or “transit facilities” means all real and personal property equipment, rights, or interests owned or to be acquired by the district for transit service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
