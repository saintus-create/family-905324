# PUC § 50085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 50085

---

## Text

All meetings of the board shall be conducted in a manner prescribed by the Ralph M. Brown Act, Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950), Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
