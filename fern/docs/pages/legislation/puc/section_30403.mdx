# PUC § 30403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** PUC § 30403

---

## Text

The board may adopt all ordinances and resolutions and perform all acts necessary or convenient to the initiation, maintenance, and administration of the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
