# PUC § 101110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 2. First Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 101110

---

## Text

The appointment of the first members of the board of directors shall be made within 30 days after the date of the formation of the district. The members shall take office immediately upon their appointment and qualification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
