# PUC § 22851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 22851

---

## Text

Revenue bonds under this article shall be authorized, issued and sold under the provisions of the Revenue Bond Law of 1941 (Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 54300), Part 1, Division 2, Title 5, Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 760.)
