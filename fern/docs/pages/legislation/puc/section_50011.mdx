# PUC § 50011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 50011

---

## Text

The San Joaquin Regional Transit District may be created as provided in this part and when so created may exercise the powers herein granted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 704, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1995.)
