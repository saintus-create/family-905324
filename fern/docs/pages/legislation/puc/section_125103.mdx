# PUC § 125103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 125103

---

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.
