# PUC § 30308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 5. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 30308

---

## Text

The district may designate the treasurer and the auditor of the County of Los Angeles as treasurer and auditor of the district and the treasury of that county may be designated the depositary for district funds for such time as the board may determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
