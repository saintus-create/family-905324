# PUC § 3090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 5. Bylaws

**Citation:** PUC § 3090

---

## Text

The bylaws may prescribe the amount of any dividends which may be declared on the stock or membership capital, which shall not exceed 8 percent per annum, shall be in the nature of interest, and shall not affect the nonprofit character of any association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
