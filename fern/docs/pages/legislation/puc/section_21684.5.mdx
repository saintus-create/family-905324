# PUC § 21684.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 4. Aeronautics Fund

**Citation:** PUC § 21684.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of this article inasmuch as they require matching funds the department may allocate funds to public agencies for the construction of airports.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1566.)
