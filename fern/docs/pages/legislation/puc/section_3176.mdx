# PUC § 3176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** PUC § 3176

---

## Text

An association, in its bylaws, may limit the amount of common stock which any member may own.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
