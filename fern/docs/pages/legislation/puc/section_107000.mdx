# PUC § 107000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 18. GOLD COAST TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 107000

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Gold Coast Transit District Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 503, Sec. 1. (AB 664) Effective January 1, 2014.)
