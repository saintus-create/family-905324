# PUC § 2932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 3. SURRENDER OF MUNICIPAL CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Surrender of Control by Municipal Corporations > Article: 2. Petition for Election

**Citation:** PUC § 2932

---

## Text

The ordinance of intention or the petition, as the case may be, shall contain the propositions proposed to be so submitted, as set forth in Section 2965.
