# PUC § 17002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 17002

---

## Text

The bonds are payable, principal and interest, in lawful money of the United States.
