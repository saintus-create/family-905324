# PUC § 13341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 6. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** PUC § 13341

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1520.)
