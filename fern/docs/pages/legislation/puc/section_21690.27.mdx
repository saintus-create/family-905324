# PUC § 21690.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 5. Los Angeles International Airport Relocation and Development

**Citation:** PUC § 21690.27

---

## Text

The Board of Airport Commissioners of the City of Los Angeles is directed to pay any sum awarded by the board pursuant to Section 21690.26.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 942.)
