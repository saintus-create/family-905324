# PUC § 236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 236

---

## Text

“Telegraph corporation” includes every corporation or person owning, controlling, operating, or managing any telegraph line for compensation within this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
