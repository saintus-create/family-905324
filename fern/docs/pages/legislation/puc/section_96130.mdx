# PUC § 96130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 96130

---

## Text

Within 30 days after submission of the case, the board shall render its decision and its decision shall be final.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
