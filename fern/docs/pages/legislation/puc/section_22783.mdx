# PUC § 22783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 22783

---

## Text

The bonds shall be sold at the times and in the amounts prescribed by the board of supervisors, but for not less than par.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
