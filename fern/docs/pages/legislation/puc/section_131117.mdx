# PUC § 131117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 3. Retail Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 131117

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
