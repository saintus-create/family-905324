# PUC § 181000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.1. SONOMA COUNTY REGIONAL CLIMATE PROTECTION AUTHORITY

**Citation:** PUC § 181000

---

## Text

The Sonoma County Regional Climate Protection Authority is hereby created. For the purposes of this division, “authority” means the Sonoma County Regional Climate Protection Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 375, Sec. 1. (AB 881) Effective January 1, 2010.)
