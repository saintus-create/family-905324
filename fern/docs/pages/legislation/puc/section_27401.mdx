# PUC § 27401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 1. Proposal of Formation

**Citation:** PUC § 27401

---

## Text

One or more special transit service districts may be created within the boundaries of a district pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
