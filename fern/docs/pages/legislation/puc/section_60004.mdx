# PUC § 60004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. YOLO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 60004

---

## Text

There is hereby created the Yolo County Transportation District. The jurisdiction of the district extends throughout the county, including all of the incorporated and unincorporated territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 457, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
