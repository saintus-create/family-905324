# PUC § 99233.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 3. Local Transportation Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99233.4

---

## Text

Allocations shall be made for rail passenger service operations and capital improvements pursuant to Section 99234.5, 99234.7, or 99234.9.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 995, Sec. 6.)
