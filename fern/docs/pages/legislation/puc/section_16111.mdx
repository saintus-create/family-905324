# PUC § 16111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 16111

---

## Text

The officers of the district, other than the board of directors, are:

(a) A clerk, who is also ex officio secretary of the board.

(b) An accountant.

(c) A treasurer.

(d) A general manager.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
