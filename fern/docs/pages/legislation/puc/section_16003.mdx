# PUC § 16003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Terms and Compensation of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 16003

## Text

A vacancy in the office of director shall be filled pursuant to Section 1780 of the Government Code.
