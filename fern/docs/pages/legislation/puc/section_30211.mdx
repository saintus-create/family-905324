# PUC § 30211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30211

---

## Text

Any vacancy in the city selection committee shall be filled by appointment by the body which originally made the appointment to the office in which the vacancy exists, in the manner provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
