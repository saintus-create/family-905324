# PUC § 5371.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. REGULATION OF RELATED BUSINESSES BY THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION > Chapter: 8. Charter-Party Carriers of Passengers > Article: 2. Authorization to Operate as a Charter-Party Carrier of Passengers

**Citation:** PUC § 5371.3

---

## Text

A class C certificate holder is one who provides transportation services incidental to commercial balloon operations, commercial river rafting, or skiing. This section does not apply to certificates issued under Section 5371.1 or 5371.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 125, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
