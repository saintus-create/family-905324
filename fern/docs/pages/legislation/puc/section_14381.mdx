# PUC § 14381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 11. Exclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Exclusion of Incorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 14381

---

## Text

If the city to which any territory included in any district has been annexed already serves electricity or water to the territory annexed, the city council or other governing body of the city may propose an agreement for the exclusion of such territory from the district.
