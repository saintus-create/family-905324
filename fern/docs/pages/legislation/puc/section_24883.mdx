# PUC § 24883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24883

## Text

The board is the legislative body of the district and determines all questions of policy.
