# PUC § 120103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 120103

---

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 294.)
