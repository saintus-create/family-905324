# PUC § 125486

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 9. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 125486

---

## Text

The board, as part of the ballot proposition to approve the imposition of a retail transactions and use tax, may seek authorization to issue bonds payable from the proceeds of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 658, Sec. 10. (AB 805) Effective January 1, 2018.)
