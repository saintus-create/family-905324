# PUC § 378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 6. Requirements for the Public Utilities Commission

**Citation:** PUC § 378

---

## Text

The commission shall authorize new optional rate schedules and tariffs, including new service offerings, that accurately reflect the loads, locations, conditions of service, cost of service, and market opportunities of customer classes and subclasses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 854, Sec. 10. Effective September 24, 1996.)
