# PUC § 99695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99695

---

## Text

All money derived from premium and accrued interest on bonds sold shall be reserved and shall be available for transfer to the General Fund as a credit to expenditures for bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
