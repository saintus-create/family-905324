# PUC § 13071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 1. Application

**Citation:** PUC § 13071

---

## Text

This chapter is complete authority for the issuance of bonds hereunder, and no action or proceeding not required by this chapter shall be necessary for the valid authorization and issuance of such bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
