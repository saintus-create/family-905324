# PUC § 29650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Withdrawal From District

**Citation:** PUC § 29650

---

## Text

Any county, the board of supervisors of which does not approve the reports of the board of directors of the district submitted pursuant to Section 29154 or 29157, may be withdrawn from the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
