# PUC § 22176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 2. Investigation and Termination by Majority Protest

**Citation:** PUC § 22176

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, the District Investigation Law of 1933 applies to districts formed under this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
