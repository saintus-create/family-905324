# PUC § 16656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 16656

---

## Text

The boards of supervisors may provide extra help for their county offices or officers as in their judgment is necessary for the proper performance of their duties under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
