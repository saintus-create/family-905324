# PUC § 132201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 132201

---

## Text

The commission may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all court and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
