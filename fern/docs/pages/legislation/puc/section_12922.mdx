# PUC § 12922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 9. Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** PUC § 12922

---

## Text

The powers and duties conferred by the Improvement Act of 1911 on boards, officers and agents of cities shall be exercised by the respective boards, officers and agents of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1206.)
