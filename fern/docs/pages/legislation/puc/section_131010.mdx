# PUC § 131010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 131010

---

## Text

“Sponsoring agency” means a governmental agency, including a county transportation authority, that has transportation responsibilities in the county in which a retail transactions and use tax ordinance has been approved pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 83, Sec. 1. Effective July 19, 2005.)
