# PUC § 22438

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** PUC § 22438

---

## Text

The secretary shall countersign all contracts on behalf of the district and perform such other duties as may be imposed by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
