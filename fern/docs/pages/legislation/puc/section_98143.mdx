# PUC § 98143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 4. Other Offices

## Text

Each appointive officer shall give such bond and in such amount as the board may require.
