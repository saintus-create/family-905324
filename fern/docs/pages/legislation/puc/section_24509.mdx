# PUC § 24509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24509

---

## Text

“Public agency” includes a city, city and county, a county, the State of California, or any public district organized under the laws of the State of California or any agency or authority of any thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
