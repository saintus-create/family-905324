# PUC § 31409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation and Exclusion

**Citation:** PUC § 31409

---

## Text

The validity of any proceedings resulting in the annexation of territory to the district shall not be contested in any action unless such action shall have been brought within three months after the completion of such annexation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
