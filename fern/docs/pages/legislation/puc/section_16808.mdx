# PUC § 16808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 1. Petition to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16808

---

## Text

Upon application by the board the commission without delay shall make a valuation in accordance with Chapter 8, Part 1 of Division 1 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
