# PUC § 26205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 26205

---

## Text

If the required number of signatures is found to be genuine, the secretary shall transmit to the board an authentic copy of the petition without the signatures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
