# PUC § 105333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 105333

---

## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and, in addition, the following: Shall the Sonoma-Marin Area Rail Transit District be dissolved?YESNO

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
