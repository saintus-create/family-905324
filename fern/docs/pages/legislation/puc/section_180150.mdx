# PUC § 180150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 180150

---

## Text

The authority may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
