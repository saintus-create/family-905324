# PUC § 25891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 25891

---

## Text

A district may levy, and collect or cause to be collected, taxes for any lawful purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
