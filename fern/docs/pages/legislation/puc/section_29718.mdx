# PUC § 29718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 29718

---

## Text

At or before the hearing, any person interested may file with the secretary of the district written objections to the annexation of the territory to the special service district or to the inclusion of his property. Upon the hearing the board shall hear and determine all protests and objections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1495.)
