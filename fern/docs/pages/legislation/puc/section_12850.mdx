# PUC § 12850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 6a. Electric, Water, Communications, or Sewage Disposal System Improvements

**Citation:** PUC § 12850

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to districts which have owned and operated an electric distribution, water distribution, or sewage disposal system for at least eight years and which have a population of 250,000 or more.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 399.)
