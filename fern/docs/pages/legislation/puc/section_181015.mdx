# PUC § 181015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.1. SONOMA COUNTY REGIONAL CLIMATE PROTECTION AUTHORITY

**Citation:** PUC § 181015

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall supersede or interfere with activities, plans, or actions of other local agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 375, Sec. 1. (AB 881) Effective January 1, 2010.)
