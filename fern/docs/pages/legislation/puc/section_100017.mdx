# PUC § 100017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 100017

---

## Text

“System” means all transit works and transit facilities owned or held or to be owned or held by the VTA for transit purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 7. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
