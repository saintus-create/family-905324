# PUC § 11645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11645

---

## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto the following:Shall the “ (giving name thereof) municipal utility district” be created and established?YESNO
