# PUC § 30641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Rapid Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 30641

---

## Text

At the time fixed for any hearing before the board any board of supervisors or city governing body eligible to file a request for hearing, not a party to the original request for hearing, may intervene and shall be entitled to be heard and to introduce evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
