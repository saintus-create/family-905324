# PUC § 105241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Bonds and Other Evidence of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Improvement Acts and Special Benefit Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 105241

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 99000) of Part 11 of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code are applicable to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
