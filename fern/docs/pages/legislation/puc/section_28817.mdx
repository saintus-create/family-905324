# PUC § 28817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 5. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 28817

---

## Text

The chief financial officer shall be the custodian of the funds of the district and shall make payments only upon warrants duly and regularly signed by the general manager. The chief financial officer shall keep an account of all receipts and disbursements.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 106, Sec. 3. (AB 2325) Effective January 1, 2025.)
