# PUC § 50504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 50504

---

## Text

The board shall canvass the vote. If a majority of the votes favor dissolution, the board shall by resolution dissolve the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
