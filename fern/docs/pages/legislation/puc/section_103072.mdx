# PUC § 103072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 2. Formation Election

**Citation:** PUC § 103072

---

## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and, in addition thereto, the following: Shall the “San Mateo County Transit District” be formed?YESNO
