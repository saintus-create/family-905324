# PUC § 130454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 10. Regional Transit Services Program

**Citation:** PUC § 130454

---

## Text

The adopted program shall serve as the basis for a coordinated application submitted by the commissions for funds allocated by the California Transportation Commission. Nothing in this article precludes a commission from applying for funding prior to the adoption of the initial program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 114, Sec. 1. Effective May 25, 1990. Section operative on date prescribed in Section 130455.)
