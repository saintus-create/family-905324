# PUC § 24361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 3. Security Following Accident > Article: 3. Financial Responsibility

**Citation:** PUC § 24361

---

## Text

No insurance policy meeting the requirements of Section 24350 shall be canceled unless 30 days’ prior notice is given to the department by either the insured or the insurance company.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 138.)
