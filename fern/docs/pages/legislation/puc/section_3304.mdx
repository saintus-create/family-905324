# PUC § 3304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER POWER AND CONSERVATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 3304

---

## Text

Any action taken pursuant to this division is exempt from the Administrative Procedure Act, as defined in Section 11370 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 10, Sec. 1. Effective August 13, 2001.)
