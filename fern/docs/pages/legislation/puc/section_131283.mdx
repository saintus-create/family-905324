# PUC § 131283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 5. County Transportation Authority > Article: 3. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 131283

---

## Text

A county transportation authority may make contracts and enter into stipulations of any nature to indemnify and hold harmless, to employ labor, and to do all acts necessary and convenient for the full exercise of the powers granted in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
