# PUC § 100163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 100163

---

## Text

The board may contract with any public agency or person to provide transit or transportation facilities and services for the VTA.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 58. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
