# PUC § 11911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 5. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 11911

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances shall be as follows:

“Be it enacted by the board of directors of ________ municipal utility district:”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
