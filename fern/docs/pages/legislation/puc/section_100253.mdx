# PUC § 100253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 9. Retail Transaction and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 100253

---

## Text

Prior to the operative date of the transaction and use tax ordinance, the VTA shall contract with the State Board of Equalization to perform all functions incident to the administration and operation of the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 68. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
