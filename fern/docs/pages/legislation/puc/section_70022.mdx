# PUC § 70022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 70022

---

## Text

The district may include incorporated or unincorporated territory within the County of Marin.

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
