# PUC § 24821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24821

---

## Text

The first directors are elected at the formation election as provided in Chapter 2. All elections of directors subsequent to the first shall be held at the same time as the general election in the manner provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
