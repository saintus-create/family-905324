# PUC § 29173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 2. Form and Terms of General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29173

---

## Text

Bonds shall bear interest at a rate of not to exceed 8 percent per annum, payable semiannually, except that interest for the first year or any fraction thereof may be paid in one installment.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
