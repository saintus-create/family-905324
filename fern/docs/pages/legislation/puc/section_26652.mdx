# PUC § 26652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 1. Annexation Agreement

**Citation:** PUC § 26652

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory not contiguous to a district may not be annexed if the district does not possess facilities for supplying transit service to that territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
