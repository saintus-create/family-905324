# PUC § 60050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. YOLO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 60050

---

## Text

The district may advocate and act on behalf of all district member jurisdictions with their concurrence to further Yolo County transportation system interests, funding, projects, and priorities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 457, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
