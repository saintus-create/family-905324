# PUC § 100012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 100012

---

## Text

“Transit” means the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage by any means, and includes rapid transit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 373, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
