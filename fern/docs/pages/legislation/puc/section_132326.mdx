# PUC § 132326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 6. Extension, Expansion, or Increase of Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 132326

---

## Text

(a) The county shall conduct an election called by the commission pursuant to Section 132322.

(b) The election shall be called and conducted in the same manner as provided by law for the conduct of elections by a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 297, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
