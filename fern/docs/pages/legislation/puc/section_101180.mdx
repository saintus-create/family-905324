# PUC § 101180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. General Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 101180

---

## Text

The district may make contracts and enter into stipulations of any nature whatsoever, either in connection with eminent domain proceedings or otherwise, including, without limiting the generality of the foregoing, contracts and stipulations to indemnify and save harmless, to engage labor or services, and to do all acts necessary and convenient for the full exercise of the powers granted in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
