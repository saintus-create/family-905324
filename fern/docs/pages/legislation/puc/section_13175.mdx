# PUC § 13175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 11. Proceeds

**Citation:** PUC § 13175

---

## Text

Money in any such separate fund shall be drawn therefrom only upon demands authenticated by the signature of the accountant of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
