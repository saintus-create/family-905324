# PUC § 90970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 3. Approval by Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 90970

---

## Text

Within 90 days after the board has finally approved the terms and conditions of annexation, the board shall cause an election to be held in the territory proposed to be annexed to determine whether the territory shall be annexed to the district upon the terms and conditions stated in the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
