# PUC § 22004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 22004

---

## Text

Airport districts may be created, maintained, and managed as provided in this part and may exercise the powers expressly granted or necessarily implied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
