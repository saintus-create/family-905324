# PUC § 101167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 8. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 101167

---

## Text

No member of the board of directors, and no county or city officer or employee of the county or a city located within the county, shall be eligible for appointment to any of the offices provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
