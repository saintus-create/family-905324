# PUC § 130052.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. Creation of Commissions

**Citation:** PUC § 130052.1

---

## Text

The members of the Orange County Transportation Authority, comprised as specified by Section 130052, shall serve as the governing body of the Orange County Service Authority for Freeway Emergencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 983, Sec. 5. Operative December 1, 1991, or sooner, as prescribed by Sec. 8 of Ch. 983.)
