# PUC § 381.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 7. Research, Environmental, and Low-Income Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 381.4

---

## Text

The commission shall require an electrical or gas corporation to revise a ratepayer-funded energy efficiency program identified pursuant to Section 913.9 as necessary to ensure that the program complements and does not duplicate a program administered by a state agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 425, Sec. 1. (SB 385) Effective January 1, 2018.)
