# PUC § 26683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 26683

---

## Text

At the hearing any person interested may file with the board written objections to the approval of the terms and conditions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
