# PUC § 99233.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 3. Local Transportation Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99233.5

---

## Text

Up to 10 percent of the remaining money for the area under the jurisdiction of a transit development board created pursuant to Division 11 (commencing with Section 120000) shall be allocated to the transit development board and the transportation planning agency to carry out administrative and planning powers, duties, and functions and construction and acquisition programs in accordance with Article 4 (commencing with Section 132353) of Chapter 3 of Division 12.7.

Thereafter, the remaining money for allocation in that area shall be allocated by the transportation planning agency to claimants.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
