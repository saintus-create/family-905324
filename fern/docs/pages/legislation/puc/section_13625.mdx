# PUC § 13625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 13625

---

## Text

Any proceeding denying the validity of the creation of any special district, or of any bonds authorized by the voters thereof pursuant to this division, shall be brought within three months after the date upon which the applicable proposition is approved by the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
