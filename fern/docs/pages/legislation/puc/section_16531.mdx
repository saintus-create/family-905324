# PUC § 16531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 5. Purchase of Supplies and Advertising

**Citation:** PUC § 16531

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, the board shall determine annually what goods, merchandise, stores, subsistence, materials, and other supplies will be needed by the district for the ensuing year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
