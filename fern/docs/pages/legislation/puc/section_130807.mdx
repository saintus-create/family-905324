# PUC § 130807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. San Bernardino County Transportation Authority Consolidation Act of 2017

**Citation:** PUC § 130807

---

## Text

The San Bernardino County Transportation Commission shall be known as the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 130054 by Stats. 2016, Ch. 216, Sec. 2. (SB 1305) Effective January 1, 2017.)
