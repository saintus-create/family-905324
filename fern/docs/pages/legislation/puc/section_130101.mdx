# PUC § 130101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 130101

---

## Text

The commission shall establish rules for its proceedings consistent with the law of the State of California.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
