# PUC § 22736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 2. Bond Election

**Citation:** PUC § 22736

---

## Text

The board may fix the compensation of the election officers but not exceeding seven dollars ($7) for each election officer.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
