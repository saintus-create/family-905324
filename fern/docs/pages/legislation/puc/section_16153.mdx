# PUC § 16153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. District Elections

**Citation:** PUC § 16153

---

## Text

No person may vote at any district election unless he possesses all the requirements of an elector under the general laws and is a qualified elector residing within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
