# PUC § 29291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 11. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 29291

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds or other evidences of indebtedness may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2117.)
