# PUC § 181008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.1. SONOMA COUNTY REGIONAL CLIMATE PROTECTION AUTHORITY

**Citation:** PUC § 181008

---

## Text

All meetings of the authority shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 375, Sec. 1. (AB 881) Effective January 1, 2010.)
