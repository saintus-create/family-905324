# PUC § 33002.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 12. Special Benefit Assessment Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 33002.10

---

## Text

Notice of each hearing upon the petition for exclusion or reduction shall be given in accordance with subdivisions (d) and (e) of Section 33001.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1322, Sec. 4.)
