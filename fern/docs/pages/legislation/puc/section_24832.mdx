# PUC § 24832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24832

---

## Text

The secretary of the district shall issue certificates of election, signed by him and duly authenticated, immediately following the determination of the result of the election by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
