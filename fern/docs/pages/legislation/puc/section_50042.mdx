# PUC § 50042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 50042

---

## Text

The election may be held on the same day as any other state, county, or city election, and be consolidated therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
