# PUC § 25362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 3. Retirement Board

## Text

All members of the retirement board shall serve without pay.
