# PUC § 33002.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 12. Special Benefit Assessment Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 33002.2

---

## Text

An election shall be held if the board finds that a petition requesting that the proposal be submitted to confirmation by the voters has been signed by the owners of at least 25 percent of the assessed value of real property within the benefit district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1322, Sec. 4.)
