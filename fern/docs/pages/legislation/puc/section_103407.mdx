# PUC § 103407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 1. Employee Relations

**Citation:** PUC § 103407

---

## Text

In the event an exclusive collective bargaining representative is selected pursuant to Section 103401, the provisions of Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 3500), Division 4, Title 1 of the Government Code are not applicable to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
