# PUC § 24932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 24932

---

## Text

The general counsel shall be admitted to practice law in the Supreme Court of the State, and shall have been actively engaged in the practice of law for not less than five years next preceding appointment pursuant to Section 24931.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 200, Sec. 8. (AB 784) Effective January 1, 2022.)
