# PUC § 102162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 4. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 102162

---

## Text

Article 4 (commencing with Section 1090) and Article 4.6 (commencing with Section 1120), Chapter 1, Division 4, Title 1 of the Government Code shall apply to all officers, employees, and contracts of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
