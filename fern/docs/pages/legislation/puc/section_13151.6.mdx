# PUC § 13151.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 9. Form and Content

**Citation:** PUC § 13151.6

---

## Text

The board may provide for the issuance by the district of a duplicate, in the manner and on such terms and conditions as the board may determine, in the event any bond, temporary bond, coupon or interim receipt of any such issue is lost, destroyed or mutilated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
