# PUC § 142272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. FRESNO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 6. Transactions and Use Taxes

**Citation:** PUC § 142272

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 4. Effective July 14, 1986. Repealed on date prescribed in Section 142010.)
