# PUC § 99354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 7. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99354

---

## Text

The county shall preserve and protect the security of the bonds and the rights of the bondholders and warrant and defend their rights against all claims and demands of all persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1400.)
