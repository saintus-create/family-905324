# PUC § 130050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. Creation of Commissions

**Citation:** PUC § 130050

---

## Text

There is hereby created a commission in Los Angeles County, in Orange County, in Riverside County, and in San Bernardino County.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
