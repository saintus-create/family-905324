# PUC § 12772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 12772

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6 of the Government Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2006.)
