# PUC § 897

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 10. Natural Gas Surcharge

**Citation:** PUC § 897

---

## Text

Nothing in this article impairs the rights and obligations of parties to contracts approved by the commission, as the rights and obligations were interpreted as of January 1, 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 932, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
