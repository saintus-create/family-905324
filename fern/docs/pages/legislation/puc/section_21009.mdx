# PUC § 21009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21009

---

## Text

“Person” means any individual, firm, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, company, association, joint stock association, or body politic; and includes any trustee, receiver, assignee, or other similar representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 220. Effective January 1, 1995.)
