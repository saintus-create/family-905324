# PUC § 7818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 2. Street Railroads

**Citation:** PUC § 7818

---

## Text

Any person operating or driving a street car who becomes or is intoxicated while so engaged in operating or driving such street car is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
