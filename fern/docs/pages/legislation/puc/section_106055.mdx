# PUC § 106055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 17. Monterey-Salinas Transit District > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 4. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 106055

---

## Text

The district may make contracts and enter into stipulations of any nature whatsoever, either in connection with eminent domain proceedings or otherwise, including, but not limited to, contracts and stipulations to indemnify and hold harmless, to employ labor, and to perform all acts necessary and convenient for the full exercise of the powers granted in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 460, Sec. 1. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2010.)
