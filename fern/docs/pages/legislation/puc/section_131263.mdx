# PUC § 131263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 5. County Transportation Authority > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 131263

---

## Text

The acts of a county transportation authority shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
