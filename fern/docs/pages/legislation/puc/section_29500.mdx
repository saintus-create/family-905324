# PUC § 29500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 1. Annexation Agreement

**Citation:** PUC § 29500

---

## Text

Any county not included within the boundaries of the district, including a county which has withdrawn from the district pursuant to Chapter 10 of this part, may be annexed thereto in the manner provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
