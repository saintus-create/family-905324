# PUC § 21650.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 2.5. Non-Public-Use Airports

**Citation:** PUC § 21650.2

---

## Text

Nothing shall prevent an airport from applying for and receiving a permit pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 21661) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1243, Sec. 19. Effective September 30, 1992.)
