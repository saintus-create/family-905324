# PUC § 50133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** PUC § 50133

---

## Text

The board may adopt all ordinances and resolutions and perform all acts necessary or convenient to the initiation, maintenance, and administration of the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
