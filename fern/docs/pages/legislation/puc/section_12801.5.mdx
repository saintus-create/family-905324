# PUC § 12801.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Utility Works and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 12801.5

---

## Text

A district that provides broadband Internet access services shall comply with the requirements of Article 12 (commencing with Section 53167) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 963, Sec. 7. (AB 1999) Effective January 1, 2019.)
