# PUC § 96411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 96411

---

## Text

The votes of two-thirds (23) of all the voters voting on the proposition at the election are required to authorize the issuance of bonds under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
