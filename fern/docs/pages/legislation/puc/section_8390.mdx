# PUC § 8390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 7. Designated Water System Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 8390

---

## Text

A privately owned public utility may serve as an administrator for purposes of Section 116686 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 120, Sec. 10. (SB 200) Effective July 24, 2019.)
