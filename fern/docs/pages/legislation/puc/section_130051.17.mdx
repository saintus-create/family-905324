# PUC § 130051.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. Creation of Commissions

**Citation:** PUC § 130051.17

---

## Text

Any gifts received by members of the board or employees of the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority shall be governed by applicable state law and the authority’s administrative code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 755, Sec. 2. (AB 3123) Effective January 1, 2025.)
