# PUC § 16871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 3. Joint Acquisition and Use of Sewage Facilities and Water Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16871

---

## Text

As used in this article, “public agency” includes the Federal Government or any of its branches, and any county, city, district, or other public corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
