# PUC § 93012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 11. Consolidation With Other Districts

## Text

If the proposition fails to carry, the result shall be entered upon the minutes of the Board of Supervisors of San Diego County.
