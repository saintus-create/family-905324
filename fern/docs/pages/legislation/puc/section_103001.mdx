# PUC § 103001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. Title of Act and Policy

**Citation:** PUC § 103001

---

## Text

It is necessary that a transit district be established in the County of San Mateo in order to meet the public transit problems of that county. Because there is no general law under which such a district could be formed, the adoption of a special act and the formation of a special district is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
