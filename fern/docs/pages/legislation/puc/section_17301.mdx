# PUC § 17301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 1. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17301

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory contiguous or noncontiguous to a district may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in the District Reorganization Act of 1965 (commencing with Section 56000 of the Government Code).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 413.)
