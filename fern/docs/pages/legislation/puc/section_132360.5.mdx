# PUC § 132360.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 6.5. Adoption and Administration of a Regional Comprehensive Plan

**Citation:** PUC § 132360.5

---

## Text

All documents created in compliance with this article shall be made available and ready for public review in compliance with the California Public Records Act (Division 10 (commencing with Section 7920.000) of Title 1 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 408. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Section 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
