# PUC § 17370.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17370.1

---

## Text

After the board of directors of the annexing district shall have adopted a resolution declaring its intention of approving an annexation the secretary of such district shall file a certified copy of such resolution with the executive officer of the local agency formation commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2043.)
