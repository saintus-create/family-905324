# PUC § 22408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 22408

---

## Text

The board shall hold its first meeting in the meeting room of the board of supervisors of the principal county at 10 a.m. on the first Monday after the formation of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
