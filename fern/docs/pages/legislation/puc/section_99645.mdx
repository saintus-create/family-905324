# PUC § 99645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99645

---

## Text

One hundred twentyfive million dollars ($125,000,000) shall be allocated to the City of Irvine for construction of a guideway demonstration project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
