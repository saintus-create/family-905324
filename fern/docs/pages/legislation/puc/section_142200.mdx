# PUC § 142200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. FRESNO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Transportation Coordination

**Citation:** PUC § 142200

---

## Text

The authority shall consult with, and coordinate its actions to secure funding for the completion and improvement of the priority regional transportation improvements with the cities in the county, the board of supervisors, the Council of Fresno County Governments, and the Department of Transportation for the purpose of integrating its planned improvements with the other transportation improvement plans and operations of other transportation agencies impacting the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 474, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2002. Repealed on date prescribed in Section 142010.)
