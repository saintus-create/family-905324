# PUC § 11647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11647

---

## Text

Any person may be nominated for the office of director upon written petition of at least 50 voters of the ward in which such person resides.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
