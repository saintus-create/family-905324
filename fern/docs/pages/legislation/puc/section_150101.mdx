# PUC § 150101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 150101

---

## Text

Meetings of the authority shall be held at least annually and as often as determined necessary by the authority.

All meetings of the authority shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
