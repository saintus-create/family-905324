# PUC § 99629

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99629

---

## Text

Two hundred two million dollars ($202,000,000) shall be allocated to the Los Angeles-San Diego Rail Corridor Agency for intercity and commuter rail projects for expenditure as follows:

(a) In San Diego County, fortyfive million dollars ($45,000,000).

(b) In Orange County, eightytwo million dollars ($82,000,000).

(c) In Los Angeles County, seventyfive million dollars ($75,000,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
