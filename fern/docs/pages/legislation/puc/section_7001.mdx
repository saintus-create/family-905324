# PUC § 7001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.5. UTILITY RELOCATION AGREEMENTS > Chapter: 1. Relocation for Transit or Transportation Capital Improvement Projects

**Citation:** PUC § 7001

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “authority” means the Santa Clara Valley Transportation Authority, as defined in Section 100011.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 655, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
