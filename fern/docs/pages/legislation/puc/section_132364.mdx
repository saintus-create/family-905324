# PUC § 132364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 7. Voter Approval

**Citation:** PUC § 132364

---

## Text

The county shall conduct an election, including an advisory election, called by the consolidated agency in the same manner as provided by law for the conduct of elections by a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 132634 by Stats. 2003, Ch. 62, Sec. 271. Effective January 1, 2004.)
