# PUC § 100602.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 9. Benefit Assessment Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 100602.12

---

## Text

The expenses of giving the notice provided for herein and of the hearing on the exclusion or reduction petition shall be paid by the persons filing the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
