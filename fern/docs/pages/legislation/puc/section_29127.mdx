# PUC § 29127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Taxation > Article: 1. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 29127

---

## Text

The board shall immediately after fixing the rate of taxes as above provided transmit to the county auditors of the counties in which the district is situated a statement of the rate of taxes fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
