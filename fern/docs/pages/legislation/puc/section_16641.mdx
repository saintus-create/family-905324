# PUC § 16641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 16641

---

## Text

A district may levy and collect, or cause to be levied and collected, taxes for the purpose of carrying on its operations and paying its obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
