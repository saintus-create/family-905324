# PUC § 345.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 3. Independent System Operator

**Citation:** PUC § 345.1

---

## Text

The chair of the board of governors and the chief executive officer of the Independent System Operator shall appear annually before the appropriate policy committees of the Assembly and Senate to present the report required pursuant to Section 345.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 116, Sec. 2. (AB 825) Effective January 1, 2026.)
