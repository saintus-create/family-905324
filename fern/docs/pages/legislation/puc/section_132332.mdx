# PUC § 132332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 6. Extension, Expansion, or Increase of Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 132332

---

## Text

The commission has no power to extend, expand, or increase any tax other than the transactions and use tax extended, expanded, or increased upon approval of the voters in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 83, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2009.)
