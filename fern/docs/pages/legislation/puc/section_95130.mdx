# PUC § 95130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 2. Request by Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 95130

---

## Text

The legislative body, the City Council of the City of Santa Barbara, may pass a resolution declaring that in its opinion public interest or necessity demands the creation and maintenance of the Santa Barbara Metropolitan Transit District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
