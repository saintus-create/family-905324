# PUC § 102335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 102335

## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto, the following:Shall the Sacramento Regional Transit District be authorized to operate and levy a property tax at an annual rate which shall not exceed _______ cents ($0.__) on each one hundred dollars ($100) of assessed valuation within (describe city or unincorporated area)?YES◻NO ◻
