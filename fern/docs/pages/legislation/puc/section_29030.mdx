# PUC § 29030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Rapid Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 29030

---

## Text

The district may provide a rapid transit system for the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
