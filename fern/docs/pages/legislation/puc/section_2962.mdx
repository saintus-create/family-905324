# PUC § 2962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 3. SURRENDER OF MUNICIPAL CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Surrender of Control by Municipal Corporations > Article: 3. Elections

**Citation:** PUC § 2962

---

## Text

A special election shall be held on the next established election date not less than 74 days after the adoption of the ordinance of intention, or the presentation of the petition to the legislative body.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1146.)
