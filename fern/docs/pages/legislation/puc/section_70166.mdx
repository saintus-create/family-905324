# PUC § 70166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 70166

---

## Text

No officer or employee of the district shall in any manner be interested, directly or indirectly, in any contract awarded or to be awarded by the board, or in the profits to be derived therefrom contrary to the provisions of Article 4 (commencing with Section 1090), Chapter 1, Division 4, Title 1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
