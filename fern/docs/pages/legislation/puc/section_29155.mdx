# PUC § 29155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 1. Authorization of General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29155

---

## Text

After receiving the reports, each board of supervisors shall fix a time and place for a public hearing to consider the reports. The time fixed for the hearing shall not be less than 30 days nor more than 60 days from the date the reports were received from the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 177.)
