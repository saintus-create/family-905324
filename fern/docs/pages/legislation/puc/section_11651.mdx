# PUC § 11651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11651

---

## Text

The board of supervisors which called the election shall meet on Monday next succeeding the day of the election and canvass the votes cast thereat.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
