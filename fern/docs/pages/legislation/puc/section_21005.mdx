# PUC § 21005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21005

---

## Text

This part shall not be construed as limiting any power of the state or a political subdivision to regulate airport hazards by zoning.
