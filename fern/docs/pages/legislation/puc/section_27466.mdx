# PUC § 27466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 27466

---

## Text

Notice fixing the time and place for hearing on the question of the annexation of territory to a special transit service district shall be published once in a newspaper of general circulation published in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
