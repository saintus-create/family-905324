# PUC § 101281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 101281

---

## Text

The district shall not incur a total bonded indebtedness which exceeds 5 percent of the assessed value of all the taxable real and personal property within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
