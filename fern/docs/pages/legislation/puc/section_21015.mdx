# PUC § 21015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21015

---

## Text

“Operation of aircraft” or “operate aircraft” means the use, navigation, or piloting of aircraft in the air space over this State or upon any airport within this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
