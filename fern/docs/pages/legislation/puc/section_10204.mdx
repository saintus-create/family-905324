# PUC § 10204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 5. Permitted Use of Improvements

**Citation:** PUC § 10204

---

## Text

“District,” as used in this article, includes any district for which the legislative body of any county or city may make contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
