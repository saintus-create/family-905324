# PUC § 15967

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 1. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 15967

---

## Text

A certificate of election shall be issued by the board of supervisors to each person elected and declared elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
