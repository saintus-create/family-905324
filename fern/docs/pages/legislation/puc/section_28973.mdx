# PUC § 28973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 28973

## Text

The district may insure against any accident to or destruction of the system or any part thereof.
