# PUC § 22010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 22010

---

## Text

“Affected county” means any county in which the territory of the district is situated.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
