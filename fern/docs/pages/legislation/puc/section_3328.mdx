# PUC § 3328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER POWER AND CONSERVATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. The California Consumer Power and Conservation Financing Authority > Article: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 3328

---

## Text

The California Public Records Act (Division 10 (commencing with Section 7920.000) of Title 1 of the Government Code) applies to all records of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 399. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Section 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
