# PUC § 130516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 130516

---

## Text

“Public agency” means any state agency, department, board, or commission, any county, city, regional agency, district, or other political subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
