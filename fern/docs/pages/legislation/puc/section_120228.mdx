# PUC § 120228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 120228

---

## Text

Subject to Section 120260, the board may enter into joint powers agreements to operate exclusive public mass transit guideways.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 294.)
