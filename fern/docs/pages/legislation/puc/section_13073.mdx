# PUC § 13073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 1. Application

**Citation:** PUC § 13073

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply only to districts which have owned and operated an electric distribution system for at least 10 years and which have a population of 250,000 or more.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
