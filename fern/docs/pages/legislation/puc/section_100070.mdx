# PUC § 100070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Government of VTA > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 100070

---

## Text

The board of directors is the legislative body of the VTA and shall determine all questions of VTA policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 26. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
