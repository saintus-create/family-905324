# PUC § 103353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 10. Retail Transaction and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 103353

---

## Text

Prior to the operative date of the transaction and use tax ordinance, the district shall contract with the State Board of Equalization to perform all functions incident to the administration and operation of the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
