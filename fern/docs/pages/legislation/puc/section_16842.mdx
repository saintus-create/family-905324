# PUC § 16842

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 2. Resolution to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16842

---

## Text

The board shall procure plans and estimates and negotiate for offers for the sale to the district of existing public utility works in the same manner as specified in Article 1 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
