# PUC § 70217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 70217

---

## Text

The board shall immediately after fixing the rate of taxes as above provided transmit to the county auditor of the county a statement of the rate of taxes fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
