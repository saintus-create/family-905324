# PUC § 13103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 4. Proceedings for Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 13103

---

## Text

When bonds are issued under this chapter, the preliminary resolution of the board adopted pursuant to this article shall take effect upon its adoption by the board subject to the right of referendum herein provided for.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1962, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 6.)
