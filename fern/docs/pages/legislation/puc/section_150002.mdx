# PUC § 150002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 150002

---

## Text

“Bonds” means indebtedness and securities of any kind or class, including bonds, notes, bond anticipation notes, and commercial paper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
