# PUC § 16001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Terms and Compensation of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 16001

---

## Text

On the first Tuesday after their election and qualification, the directors shall meet and classify themselves by lot, so that the largest possible minority shall hold office for two years, and a majority for four years. Thereafter at each biennial district election their successors shall be elected for the term of four years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
