# PUC § 22404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 22404

---

## Text

The terms of office of the directors elected at the formation election are governed by the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.
