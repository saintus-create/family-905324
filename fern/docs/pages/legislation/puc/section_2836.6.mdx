# PUC § 2836.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 7.7. Energy Storage Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 2836.6

---

## Text

All procurement of energy storage systems by a load-serving entity or local publicly owned electric utility shall be cost effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 469, Sec. 2. (AB 2514) Effective January 1, 2011.)
