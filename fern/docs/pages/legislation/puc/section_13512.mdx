# PUC § 13512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 13512

---

## Text

No person is entitled to vote at the election unless he is a voter of the territory included in the proposed special district. The election may be held on the same day as any other state, county, or city election and may be consolidated therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
