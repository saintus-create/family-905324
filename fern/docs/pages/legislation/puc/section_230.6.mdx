# PUC § 230.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 230.6

---

## Text

“Sewer system corporation” includes every corporation or person owning, controlling, operating, or managing any sewer system for compensation within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1110.)
