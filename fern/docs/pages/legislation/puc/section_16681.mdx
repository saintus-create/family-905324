# PUC § 16681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 9. Payment of Claims Against Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 16681

---

## Text

No claims shall be paid until allowed by the board, and only upon a warrant signed by any two officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
