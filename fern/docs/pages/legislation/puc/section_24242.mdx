# PUC § 24242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24242

---

## Text

“Claimants” means any person having a claim for damages as the result of an accident within this state involving an aircraft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1452.)
