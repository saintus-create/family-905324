# PUC § 27282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10. Exclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Exclusion of Incorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 27282

---

## Text

The petition shall contain the information prescribed for petitions for exclusion under Article 1 of this chapter, a deposit for expenses shall be made as therein provided, and thereafter all proceedings shall be had as provided therein for such petitions after filing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
