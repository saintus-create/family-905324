# PUC § 402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.5. Public Utilities Commission Reimbursement Fees > Article: 1. Collection and Disposition of Fees

**Citation:** PUC § 402

---

## Text

The Public Utilities Commission Utilities Reimbursement Account is hereby continued in existence. All fees collected by the commission pursuant to Section 431 shall be transmitted to the Treasurer at least quarterly for deposit in the account.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 323, Sec. 71. Effective July 1, 1983.)
