# PUC § 132354.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 5. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 132354.2

---

## Text

This chapter is necessary for the public health, safety, and welfare, and shall be liberally construed to carry out the objects and purposes of this chapter and the declared policy of the state as set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
