# PUC § 132310.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 5. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 132310.5

---

## Text

The commission may provide for the bonds to bear a variable interest rate, for the manner and intervals in which the rate shall vary, and for the dates on which the interest shall be payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
