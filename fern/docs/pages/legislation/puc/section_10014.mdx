# PUC § 10014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 1. Acquisition and Operation

**Citation:** PUC § 10014

---

## Text

The commission may enter into a contract with a local agency to provide any technical assistance needed to comply with Section 10013, if the contract includes provisions for the commission to be reimbursed for its estimated reasonable costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1430, Sec. 3.)
