# PUC § 11907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 5. Meetings and Legislation

## Text

A majority of the board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business.
