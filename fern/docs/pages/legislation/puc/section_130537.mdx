# PUC § 130537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 4. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** PUC § 130537

---

## Text

The commission may, from any funds available therefor, purchase its bonds. The commission may hold, pledge, cancel, or resell the bonds, subject to, and in accordance with, agreements with the bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
