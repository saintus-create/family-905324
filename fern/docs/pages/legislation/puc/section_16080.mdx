# PUC § 16080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 16080

---

## Text

Within 10 days after the filing of a supplemental petition, the clerk shall examine it and certify to the result of his examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
