# PUC § 28600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Creation of District

**Citation:** PUC § 28600

---

## Text

There is hereby created the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District, comprising the territory lying within the boundaries of the Counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, San Francisco, and San Mateo.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
