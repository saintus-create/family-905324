# PUC § 230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 230

---

## Text

“Railroad corporation” includes every corporation or person owning, controlling, operating, or managing any railroad for compensation within this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
