# PUC § 7665.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 7.3. Local Community Rail Security Act of 2006

**Citation:** PUC § 7665.1

---

## Text

Unless the context requires otherwise, for purposes of this article:

(a) “Agency” or “office” means the Office of Emergency Services.

(b) “Secretary” or “director” means the Director of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 501. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
