# PUC § 14051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 10. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 14051

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory may be annexed to a district in the manner provided in the Cortese-Knox-Hertzberg Local Government Reorganization Act of 2000 (Division 3 (commencing with Section 56000) of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 347, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2006.)
