# PUC § 30651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5.5. Citizens’ Advisory Committees

**Citation:** PUC § 30651

---

## Text

The citizens’ advisory committee may advise the district of the prevalent sentiments of its area and shall advise the board on any and all related transit issues currently under the jurisdiction of, or contemplated by, the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 725, Sec. 1.)
