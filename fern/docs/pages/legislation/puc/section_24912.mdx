# PUC § 24912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 5. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 24912

---

## Text

All ordinances shall be signed by the president of the board or the vice president, and attested by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
