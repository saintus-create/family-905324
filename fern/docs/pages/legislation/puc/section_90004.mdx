# PUC § 90004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 90004

## Text

“Voter” means any elector who is registered under the Elections Code.
