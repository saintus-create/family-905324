# PUC § 26455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 8. Annexation of Cities > Article: 3. Approval by City

**Citation:** PUC § 26455

---

## Text

If the proposition fails to carry, the result shall be entered upon the minutes of the governing body of the city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
