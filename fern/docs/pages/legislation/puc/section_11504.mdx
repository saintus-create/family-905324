# PUC § 11504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 11504

---

## Text

“Public agency” includes a city, county water district, county sanitation district, or sanitary district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
