# PUC § 50064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 1. Government

**Citation:** PUC § 50064

---

## Text

A person shall not be appointed to or be a member of the board unless he is a resident of the district and has resided within the district at least one year immediately prior to his appointment to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
