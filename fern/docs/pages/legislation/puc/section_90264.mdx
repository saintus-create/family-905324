# PUC § 90264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 90264

---

## Text

Notwithstanding this article, until such time as the district has operated, controlled, or used facilities or parts of facilities for providing the inhabitants within the boundaries of the district with transit service for a period of six months, the board may, but is not required to, appoint a general manager. A general manager appointed during such time holds office at the pleasure of the majority of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
