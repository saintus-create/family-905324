# PUC § 15796

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Establishment of the District

**Citation:** PUC § 15796

---

## Text

No charge shall be made by either the Secretary of State or any county recorder or county elections official for the services required of him or her under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 92. Effective January 1, 2003.)
