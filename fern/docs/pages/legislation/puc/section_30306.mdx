# PUC § 30306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 5. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 30306

---

## Text

Article 4 (commencing with Section 1090), Chapter 1, Division 4, Title 1, of the Government Code shall apply to all officers and contracts of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
