# PUC § 101154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 7. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 101154

---

## Text

The board may, from time to time, contract for or employ any professional service required by the district and may, from time to time, contract for or employ any other person necessary to perform any other work or service which cannot be satisfactorily performed by the regular employees of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
