# PUC § 99211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 99211

---

## Text

“Public transportation system” means any system of an operator which provides transportation services to the general public by any vehicle which operates on land or water, regardless of whether operated separated from or in conjunction with other vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1408.)
