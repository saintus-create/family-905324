# PUC § 16882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 3. Joint Acquisition and Use of Sewage Facilities and Water Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16882

---

## Text

A contract may provide for the joint use of any water works or other facilities upon such terms and conditions as may be agreed upon by the parties to the contract for such area for each of the parties as may be described in the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
