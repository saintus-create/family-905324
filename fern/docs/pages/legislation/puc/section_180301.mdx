# PUC § 180301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 7. Sacramento Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 180301

---

## Text

Unless expressly stated in this chapter, the provisions set forth in this division apply to the authority. This chapter is not intended to limit or otherwise conflict with the other provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 724, Sec. 1. (AB 1223) Effective January 1, 2026.)
