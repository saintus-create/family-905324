# PUC § 16461.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 3. Utility Works and Services

**Citation:** PUC § 16461.10

---

## Text

A district that provides broadband Internet access services shall comply with the requirements of Article 12 (commencing with Section 53167) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 963, Sec. 8. (AB 1999) Effective January 1, 2019.)
