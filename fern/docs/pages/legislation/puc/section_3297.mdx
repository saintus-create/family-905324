# PUC § 3297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 6. Wildfire > Chapter: 3. Operation of the Fund

**Citation:** PUC § 3297

---

## Text

Fund earnings are tax exempt and fund contributions by electrical corporations are tax deductible for state tax purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 396, Sec. 48. (AB 1513) Effective January 1, 2020.)
