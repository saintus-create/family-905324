# PUC § 847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 5.5. Financing of Transition Costs

**Citation:** PUC § 847

---

## Text

Regulations adopted to implement this article shall not be subject to the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 854, Sec. 11. Effective September 24, 1996.)
