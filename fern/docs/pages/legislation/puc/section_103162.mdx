# PUC § 103162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 3. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 103162

---

## Text

Article 4 (commencing with Section 1090), Chapter 1, Division 4, and Division 4.5 (commencing with Section 3600), of Title 1 of the Government Code shall apply to all officers, employees, and contracts of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
