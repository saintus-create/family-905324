# PUC § 15507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 15507

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, a firm or a copartnership.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1271.)
