# PUC § 17013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Adoption of Improvement Acts

**Citation:** PUC § 17013

---

## Text

The improvements authorized to be constructed or acquired by this chapter are restricted to those permitted to be constructed or acquired pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 226.)
