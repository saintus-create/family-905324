# PUC § 50213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 50213

---

## Text

The board may provide for the assessment, levy, and collection of taxes by the district, including the sale of property to the district for delinquent taxes, with penalties, interest, and cost.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
