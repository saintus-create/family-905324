# PUC § 50236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 50236

---

## Text

The votes of a majority of all the voters voting on the proposition at the election are required to authorize the issuance of bonds under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
