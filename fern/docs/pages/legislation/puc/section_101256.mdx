# PUC § 101256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Budget

**Citation:** PUC § 101256

---

## Text

A copy of the budget as finally adopted by the board shall be filed with the county auditor pursuant to Section 53901 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
