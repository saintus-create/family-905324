# PUC § 90190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 2. Terms of Office

**Citation:** PUC § 90190

---

## Text

The term of each director shall be for four years, except as provided in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
