# PUC § 353.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 3.5. Distributed Energy Resources

**Citation:** PUC § 353.5

---

## Text

Each electrical corporation, as part of its distribution planning process, shall consider nonutility owned distributed energy resources as a possible alternative to investments in its distribution system in order to ensure reliable electric service at the lowest possible cost.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 12, Sec. 11. Effective May 22, 2001.)
