# PUC § 12167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Civil Service System > Article: 4. Discipline

**Citation:** PUC § 12167

---

## Text

Nothing in the civil service provisions of this division deprives any person of his rights and remedies in a court of competent jurisdiction.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
