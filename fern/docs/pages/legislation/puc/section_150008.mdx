# PUC § 150008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 150008

---

## Text

“Ordinance” means either a city ordinance or a county ordinance, adopted pursuant to Section 150201, which imposes a retail transactions and use tax within the city or the county, respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
