# PUC § 131262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 5. County Transportation Authority > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 131262

---

## Text

The majority of the members of a county transportation authority constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business, and all official acts of the authority require the affirmative vote of a majority of the members of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
