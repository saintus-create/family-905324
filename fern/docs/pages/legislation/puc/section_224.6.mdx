# PUC § 224.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 224.6

---

## Text

“Motor carrier of property” means a motor carrier of property as defined in Section 34601 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1042, Sec. 10. Effective September 29, 1996.)
