# PUC § 26211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 26211

---

## Text

The votes of two-thirds of all the voters voting on the proposition at the election are required to authorize the issuance of bonds under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
