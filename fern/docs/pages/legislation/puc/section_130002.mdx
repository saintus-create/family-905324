# PUC § 130002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 130002

---

## Text

As used in this division, “commission” means a county transportation commission created pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 130050).
