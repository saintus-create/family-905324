# PUC § 98134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 98134

---

## Text

All ordinances shall be printed after passage, and maintained in the district’s offices.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
