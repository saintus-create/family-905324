# PUC § 13151.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 9. Form and Content

**Citation:** PUC § 13151.7

---

## Text

The board may provide for the appointment and payment of fiscal, paying, sinking fund or other agents, or of trustees or registrars.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
