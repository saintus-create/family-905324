# PUC § 916.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 916.5

---

## Text

By January 1 of each year, the commission shall submit to the Legislature a detailed budget for the expenditure of railroad corporation fees for the ensuing budget year, consistent with Section 421.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 425, Sec. 8. (SB 385) Effective January 1, 2018.)
