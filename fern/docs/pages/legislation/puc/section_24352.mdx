# PUC § 24352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 3. Security Following Accident > Article: 3. Financial Responsibility

**Citation:** PUC § 24352

---

## Text

Any person may at any time apply to the department for a certificate of self-insurance, whether or not there has occurred an accident as a result of which he might be affected by some other provision of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 138.)
