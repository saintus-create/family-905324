# PUC § 90021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 90021

## Text

The San Diego County Transit District may be created as provided in this part and when so created may exercise the powers herein granted.
