# PUC § 101171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 8. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 101171

---

## Text

The secretary shall maintain and preserve a permanent written record of all of the official acts of the board as evidenced by minute order, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
