# PUC § 24932.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 24932.5

---

## Text

The secretary, and such assistants as the board may determine, shall have the power to administer all oaths or affirmations required by this part, including the oath of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 744.)
