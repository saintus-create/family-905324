# PUC § 98396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 98396

---

## Text

Whenever any territory is annexed to the district it shall thereupon become a part of the district subject to all the liabilities and entitled to all the benefits of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
