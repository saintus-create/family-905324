# PUC § 11857.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2.7. Sacramento Municipal Utility District

**Citation:** PUC § 11857.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall affect the term of office of district directors in office on the effective date of this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 845, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
