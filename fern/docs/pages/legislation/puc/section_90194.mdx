# PUC § 90194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 2. Terms of Office

**Citation:** PUC § 90194

---

## Text

Every director is subject to recall by voters of the district, in accordance with the recall provisions of the general laws of the state applicable to county officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
