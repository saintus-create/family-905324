# PUC § 30471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 4. Other Benefits

**Citation:** PUC § 30471

---

## Text

The district shall take such steps as may be necessary to obtain coverage for the district and its employees under the state laws relating to workers’ compensation and insurance, unemployment compensation, and disability compensation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1454.)
