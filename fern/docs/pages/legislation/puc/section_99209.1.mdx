# PUC § 99209.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 99209.1

---

## Text

“Municipal operator” also means any county which is located in part within a transit district and which operates a public transportation system in the unincorporated area of the county not within the area of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1002.)
