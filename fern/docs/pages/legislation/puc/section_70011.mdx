# PUC § 70011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 70011

---

## Text

The Marin County Transit District may be created as provided in this part and when so created may exercise the powers herein granted.

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
