# PUC § 101240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Audits and Financial Report

**Citation:** PUC § 101240

---

## Text

The general manager shall cause to be installed and maintained a system of auditing and accounting which shall completely and at all times show the financial condition of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
