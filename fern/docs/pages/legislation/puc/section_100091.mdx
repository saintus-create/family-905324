# PUC § 100091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Government of VTA > Article: 4. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 100091

---

## Text

The secretary and those assistants that the board designates may administer all oaths or affirmations required by law, including the oath of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 373, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1998.)
