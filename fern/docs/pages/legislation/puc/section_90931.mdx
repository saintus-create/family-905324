# PUC § 90931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 1. Annexation Agreement

**Citation:** PUC § 90931

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory not contiguous to the district may not be annexed if the district does not possess facilities for supplying transit service to that territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
