# PUC § 22151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 1. Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 22151

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of each county desiring to form, or join in forming, a district shall adopt a resolution of intention to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
