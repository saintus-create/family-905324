# PUC § 70161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 70161

---

## Text

The district may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
