# PUC § 95404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 1. Government

**Citation:** PUC § 95404

---

## Text

A person shall not be appointed to or be a member of the board unless he is a resident of the district and has resided within the district at least one year immediately prior to his appointment to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
