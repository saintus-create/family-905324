# PUC § 17377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17377

---

## Text

Any person who is interested in any land located within the annexing district or the territory to be annexed and who does not file a written protest is deemed to have assented to the annexation of the territory set forth in the petition of the petitioning district, and upon the terms and conditions set forth therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
