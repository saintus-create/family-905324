# PUC § 13211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 13211

---

## Text

The votes of two-thirds of all the voters voting on the proposition at the election are required to authorize the issuance of bonds under this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 761.)
