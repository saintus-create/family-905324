# PUC § 15705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** PUC § 15705

---

## Text

The county elections official shall within 30 days examine and verify the signatures to the petition and certify the result of the examination to the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 89. Effective January 1, 2003.)
