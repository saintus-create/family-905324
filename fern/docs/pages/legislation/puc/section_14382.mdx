# PUC § 14382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 11. Exclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Exclusion of Incorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 14382

---

## Text

The agreement for exclusion shall be authorized and executed and proceedings for such exclusion taken in the same manner as provided in Chapter 9 (commencing at Section 13801) for the annexation of a public agency to a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2043.)
