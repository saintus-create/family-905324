# PUC § 3415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. GOLDEN STATE ENERGY ACT > Part: 1. General Provisions > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 3415

---

## Text

“Indebtedness” means bonds, notes, commercial paper, variable rate and variable maturity securities, other obligations, and any other evidences of indebtedness issued by Golden State Energy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 12. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
