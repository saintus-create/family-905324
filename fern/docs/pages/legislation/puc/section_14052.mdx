# PUC § 14052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 10. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 14052

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory not contiguous to a district may not be annexed if the district does not possess facilities for supplying utility service to that territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
