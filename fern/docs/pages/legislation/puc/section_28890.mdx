# PUC § 28890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 28890

---

## Text

The board may prescribe the terms and conditions upon which the officers and employees of the district or their beneficiaries shall be entitled to benefits and the amounts thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
