# PUC § 409.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.5. Public Utilities Commission Reimbursement Fees > Article: 1. Collection and Disposition of Fees

**Citation:** PUC § 409.5

---

## Text

The commission shall maintain those records as are necessary to account separately for all fees and charges, including the fees authorized by Section 431, received from each class of utility subject to Article 3 (commencing with Section 431) and remitted pursuant to Section 409.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1035, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
