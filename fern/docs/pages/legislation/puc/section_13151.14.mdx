# PUC § 13151.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 9. Form and Content

**Citation:** PUC § 13151.14

---

## Text

The board may provide for restrictions on future transfers out of the revenues out of which the bonds then authorized are to be payable.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
