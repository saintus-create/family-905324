# PUC § 728.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 2. Rates

**Citation:** PUC § 728.4

---

## Text

A telephone corporation shall list a telephone number as the number for a facsimile machine in its alphabetical or classified directory only if requested to do so by the subscriber.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 973, Sec. 2.)
