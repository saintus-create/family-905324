# PUC § 103078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 2. Formation Election

**Citation:** PUC § 103078

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall make all provisions for the holding of the election throughout the district as proposed, and the cost of the election shall be a charge against the general funds of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
