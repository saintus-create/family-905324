# PUC § 70271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 70271

---

## Text

Whenever the board finds and determines that additional territory will be benefited by annexation to the district, it shall pass a resolution to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
