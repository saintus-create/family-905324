# PUC § 60159

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. YOLO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 60159

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 457, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
