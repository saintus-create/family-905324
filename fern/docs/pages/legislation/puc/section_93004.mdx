# PUC § 93004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 11. Consolidation With Other Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 93004

---

## Text

At the hearing any interested person may file with the board written objections to the approval of the terms and conditions of the proposed contract or the consolidation, or both the proposed contract and the consolidation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 102.)
