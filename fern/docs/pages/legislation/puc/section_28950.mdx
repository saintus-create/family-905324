# PUC § 28950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 28950

---

## Text

The district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
