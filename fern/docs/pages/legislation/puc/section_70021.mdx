# PUC § 70021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 70021

---

## Text

The request for the formation of the Marin County Transit District may be made by resolution or by petition as set out in this chapter.
