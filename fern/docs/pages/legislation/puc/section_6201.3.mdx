# PUC § 6201.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 2. Gas, Oil, Electric, and Water Franchises by Municipalities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 6201.3

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “industrial gas” means any substance which is in a gaseous state at ambient conditions of temperature and pressure used for commercial, industrial, or scientific purposes, but does not include any gaseous hydrocarbon used for light, heat, power, or feedstock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 197, Sec. 1.)
