# PUC § 103076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 2. Formation Election

**Citation:** PUC § 103076

---

## Text

No person shall be entitled to vote at the election unless he is a registered voter of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
