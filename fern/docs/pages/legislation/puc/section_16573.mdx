# PUC § 16573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 6. Indebtedness and Financing

**Citation:** PUC § 16573

---

## Text

No district shall incur any funded indebtedness which in the aggregate exceeds 20 percent of the assessed valuation of all real and personal property situated within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
