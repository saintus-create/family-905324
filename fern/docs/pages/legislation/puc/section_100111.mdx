# PUC § 100111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 100111

---

## Text

The VTA may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 34. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
