# PUC § 98294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8.5. Retail Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 98294

---

## Text

If the district shall not have contracted with the State Board of Equalization prior to the operative date of its transactions and use tax ordinance, it shall nevertheless so contract, and, in such case, the operative date shall be the first day of the first calendar quarter following the execution of the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 672.)
