# PUC § 13623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 13623

---

## Text

The favorable vote of two-thirds of all the voters within the special district voting on the proposition is required to authorize the issuance of the bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 761.)
