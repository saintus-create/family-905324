# PUC § 3294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 6. Wildfire > Chapter: 3. Operation of the Fund

**Citation:** PUC § 3294

---

## Text

Costs and expenses of administration of the fund shall be paid from Wildfire Fund assets.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 79, Sec. 16. (AB 1054) Effective July 12, 2019.)
