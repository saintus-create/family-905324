# PUC § 21690.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 4.5. Airport Facilities and Concessions

**Citation:** PUC § 21690.6

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to any airport owned or operated by a political subdivision, including a charter city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. Effective September 8, 1982.)
