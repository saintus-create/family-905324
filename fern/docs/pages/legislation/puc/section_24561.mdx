# PUC § 24561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 2. Alameda-Contra Costa Transit District

**Citation:** PUC § 24561

---

## Text

The Alameda-Contra Costa Transit District formed pursuant to this section, as it read on January 1, 2021, shall continue in existence unless dissolved pursuant to Chapter 11 (commencing with Section 27501) and is hereby designated a rapid transit district.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 200, Sec. 3. (AB 784) Effective January 1, 2022.)
