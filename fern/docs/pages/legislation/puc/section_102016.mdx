# PUC § 102016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 102016

---

## Text

“County” means, individually, the Counties of Sacramento and Yolo, and any other county which is annexed, in whole or in part, to the district as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
