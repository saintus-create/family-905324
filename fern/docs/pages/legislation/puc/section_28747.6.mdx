# PUC § 28747.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 2.2. District Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28747.6

---

## Text

Members of the board shall be residents and voters of the district and of the geographical area making up the election district from which they are elected. The office shall be deemed to be vacant if a member ceases to meet these requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 521.)
