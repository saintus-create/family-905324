# PUC § 29720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 6. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 29720

---

## Text

From and after the date of filing of the resolution with the secretary of the district, the annexation of the territory, or portion thereof, to the special service district is complete.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1495.)
