# PUC § 22203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 3. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 22203

## Text

Each board of supervisors may overrule any protests or objections filed with it.
