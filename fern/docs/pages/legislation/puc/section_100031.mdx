# PUC § 100031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 100031

---

## Text

The boundaries of the VTA shall include all incorporated and unincorporated territory lying within the County of Santa Clara.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 12. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
