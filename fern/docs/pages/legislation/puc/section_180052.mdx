# PUC § 180052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Creation of Local Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 180052

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), members of an authority which is an entirely new entity shall serve for a term of not more than four years.

(b) At the first meeting of an authority which is an entirely new entity convened pursuant to Section 180112 the members shall be selected by lot to serve staggered terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
