# PUC § 106023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 17. Monterey-Salinas Transit District > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 106023

## Text

The board of directors shall adopt priorities reflecting the district’s goals.
