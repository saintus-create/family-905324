# PUC § 24866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Terms of Office of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24866

---

## Text

The person appointed to fill any vacancy on the board shall hold office for the remainder of the unexpired term of his predecessor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
