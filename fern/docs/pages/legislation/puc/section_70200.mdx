# PUC § 70200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 6. Indebtedness

**Citation:** PUC § 70200

---

## Text

The district shall have the power to obtain temporary transfers of funds in accordance with the last paragraph of Section 31, Article IV of the State Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
