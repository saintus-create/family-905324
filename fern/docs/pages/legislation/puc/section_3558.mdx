# PUC § 3558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. GOLDEN STATE ENERGY ACT > Part: 5. Other Matters

**Citation:** PUC § 3558

---

## Text

Nothing in this act shall be construed as diminishing or enlarging any valid existing rights under any license or franchise previously issued pursuant to federal or state law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 12. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
