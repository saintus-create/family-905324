# PUC § 24534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 24534

---

## Text

Whenever in this part publication is required and there is no newspaper of general circulation published within the district, the publication may be made in a newspaper of general circulation published in any county where the district is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 603.)
