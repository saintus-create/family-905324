# PUC § 30101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Creation of District

**Citation:** PUC § 30101

---

## Text

The district created in accordance with the provisions of this part is a public corporation created for the purposes set forth in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
