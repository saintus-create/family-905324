# PUC § 98291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8.5. Retail Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 98291

---

## Text

Any transactions and use tax ordinance adopted shall be operative on the first day of the first calendar quarter commencing not less than 180 days after adoption of the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 672.)
