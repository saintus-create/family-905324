# PUC § 98111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 98111

---

## Text

All matters and things necessary for the proper administration of the affairs of the district which are not provided for in this part shall be provided for by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
