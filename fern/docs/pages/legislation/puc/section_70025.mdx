# PUC § 70025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 2. Request by Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 70025

---

## Text

The Board of Supervisors of the County of Marin may pass a resolution declaring that in its opinion public interest or necessity demands the creation and maintenance of the Marin County Transit District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
