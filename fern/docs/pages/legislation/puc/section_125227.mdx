# PUC § 125227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 125227

---

## Text

The district may contract for the services of independent contractors.
