# PUC § 142002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. FRESNO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 142002

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that funds generated pursuant to this division not replace property tax revenues which would otherwise be expended for roads and transportation purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 4. Effective July 14, 1986. Repealed on date prescribed in Section 142010.)
