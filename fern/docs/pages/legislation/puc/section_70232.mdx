# PUC § 70232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 70232

---

## Text

Propositions for incurring indebtedness for more than one object or purpose may be submitted at the same election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
