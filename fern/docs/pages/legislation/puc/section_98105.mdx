# PUC § 98105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 1. Government

**Citation:** PUC § 98105

---

## Text

The first meeting of the board shall be held within 10 days of the appointment of the last director by a legislative body. The board shall make its own rules of procedure and determine the place and time of its meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
