# PUC § 106041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 17. Monterey-Salinas Transit District > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 1. Personnel

**Citation:** PUC § 106041

---

## Text

The board of directors shall fix the compensation of the district’s officers and employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 460, Sec. 1. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2010.)
