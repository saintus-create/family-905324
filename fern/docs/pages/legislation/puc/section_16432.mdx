# PUC § 16432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 2. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 16432

---

## Text

The legal title to all property acquired under this division vests immediately and by operation of law in the district, and is held by the district in trust, and is dedicated and set apart to the uses and purposes set forth in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
