# PUC § 13932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Public Agencies > Article: 6. Effect of Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 13932

---

## Text

No annexation of a public agency to a district shall operate to dissolve or terminate the legal existence of the public agency annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
