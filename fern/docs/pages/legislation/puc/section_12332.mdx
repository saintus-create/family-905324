# PUC § 12332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 12332

---

## Text

The retirement allowance may be predicated in part upon service rendered the district by a member prior to the establishment of the retirement system, which service is known as “prior service.”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
