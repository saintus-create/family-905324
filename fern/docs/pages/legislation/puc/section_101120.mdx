# PUC § 101120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 3. Organization and Procedure

**Citation:** PUC § 101120

---

## Text

Immediately following the appointment and qualification of the first board of directors, and thereafter at the first meeting of the board following the first Monday after the first day of January of each year, the board shall elect a chairman and a vice chairman.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
