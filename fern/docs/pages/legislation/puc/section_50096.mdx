# PUC § 50096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 50096

---

## Text

All other things being equal, the board shall appoint as general manager, a person who has had experience in the construction or management of transit facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
