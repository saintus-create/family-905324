# PUC § 30215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30215

---

## Text

The presence of members of the city selection committee representing more than fifty (50) percent of the total number of votes of all the members of the city selection committee shall constitute a quorum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
