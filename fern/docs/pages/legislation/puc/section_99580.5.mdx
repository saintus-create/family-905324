# PUC § 99580.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 8. Administrative Enforcement for Fare Evasion and Prohibited Conducts

**Citation:** PUC § 99580.5

---

## Text

A transit district’s ordinance may be enforced outside of the transit district’s jurisdiction only where the local jurisdiction has adopted the transit district’s ordinance by reference as authorized by the local jurisdictions’ governing body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 534, Sec. 4. (AB 1337) Effective January 1, 2022.)
