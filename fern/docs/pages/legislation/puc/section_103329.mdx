# PUC § 103329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 103329

---

## Text

The board shall, immediately after fixing the rate or rates of taxes, transmit to the county auditor a statement of the rate of taxes fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
