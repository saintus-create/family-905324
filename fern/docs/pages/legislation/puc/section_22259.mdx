# PUC § 22259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation > Article: 5. Establishment of District

## Text

Upon the filing with the Secretary of State, the formation of the district is complete.
