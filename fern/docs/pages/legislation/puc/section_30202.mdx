# PUC § 30202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30202

---

## Text

At its first regular meeting after the effective date of this part, the Board of Supervisors of the County of Los Angeles shall appoint as members of the first board of directors of the district the number to be appointed by it under Section 30201.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
