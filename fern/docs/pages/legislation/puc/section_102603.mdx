# PUC § 102603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 102603

---

## Text

The district shall not incur a total indebtedness under the provisions of this chapter which exceeds 15 percent of the assessed value of all taxable property within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
