# PUC § 29218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29218

---

## Text

The taxes shall be levied and collected as other district taxes, and are in addition to all other taxes. They shall be used only for the payment of the bonds and interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
