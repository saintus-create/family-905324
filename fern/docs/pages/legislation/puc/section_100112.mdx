# PUC § 100112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 100112

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against the VTA are governed by Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code except as provided therein, or by other statutes or regulations expressly applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 35. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
