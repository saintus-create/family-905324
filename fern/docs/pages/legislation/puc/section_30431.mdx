# PUC § 30431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 30431

---

## Text

Any pension or retirement system adopted shall be on a sound actuarial basis and provide for contributions by both the district and the employee members of the system which shall be based on percentage of pay roll to be changed only by adjustments on account of experience under the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
