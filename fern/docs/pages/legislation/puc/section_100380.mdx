# PUC § 100380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 4. Other Benefits

**Citation:** PUC § 100380

---

## Text

The VTA shall take the steps as may be necessary to obtain coverage for the VTA and its employees under Title 2 of the Federal Social Security Act, as amended, and the related provisions of the Federal Contributions Act, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 83. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
