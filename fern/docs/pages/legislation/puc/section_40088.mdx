# PUC § 40088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 40088

---

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by motion, resolution or ordinance. No ordinance, resolution or motion shall have any validity or effect unless passed by the affirmative votes of a majority of the directors. District ordinances shall be adopted in the same manner as provided for adoption of ordinances by a county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1899.)
