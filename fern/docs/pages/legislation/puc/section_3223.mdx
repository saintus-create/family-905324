# PUC § 3223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 11. Reorganization of Corporations Organized Pursuant to Other Laws

**Citation:** PUC § 3223

---

## Text

If the amended articles conform, as provided in Section 3222, provisions in the articles of incorporation that appeared in the original articles or some previous amended articles are ineffective if, and to the extent that, they are inapplicable to, or inconsistent with, this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
