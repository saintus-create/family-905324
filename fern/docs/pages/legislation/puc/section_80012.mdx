# PUC § 80012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Consolidation with Other Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 80012

---

## Text

The board shall thereafter, by resolution setting forth the terms and conditions of the proposed contract at length, declare its intention to cause the agreement to be approved and effect consolidation of district with such other transit district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
