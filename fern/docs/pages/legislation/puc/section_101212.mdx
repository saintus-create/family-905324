# PUC § 101212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. Deposit and Withdrawal of Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 101212

---

## Text

The board may designate the county treasury as the depositary, and in such case the county treasurer shall have custody of all, or any portion, of the moneys of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
