# PUC § 216.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 216.4

---

## Text

“Cable television corporation” shall mean any corporation or firm which transmits television programs by cable to subscribers for a fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 215.5 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 198, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2007.)
