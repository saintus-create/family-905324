# PUC § 7723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 10. Railroad Safety and Emergency Planning and Response

**Citation:** PUC § 7723

---

## Text

The civil and criminal penalties provided in this article are separate and in addition to, and do not supersede or limit, any other civil or criminal remedy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 766, Sec. 7.)
