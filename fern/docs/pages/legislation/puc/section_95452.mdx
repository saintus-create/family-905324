# PUC § 95452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 95452

---

## Text

The board shall select one of its members vice chairman, who shall preside in the absence of its chairman. The board shall provide for and select such officers as it deems necessary to conduct the affairs of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
