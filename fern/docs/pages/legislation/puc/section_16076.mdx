# PUC § 16076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 16076

---

## Text

An order entered in the minutes of the board that an ordinance has been duly posted and published is prima facie proof of such posting and publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
