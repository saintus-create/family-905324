# PUC § 665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 3. Rights and Obligations of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Neighborhood Decarbonization Zone Pilot Projects

**Citation:** PUC § 665

---

## Text

In a new or existing proceeding, the commission shall evaluate the costs and benefits of thermal energy networks and identify potential implementation barriers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 602, Sec. 3. (SB 1221) Effective January 1, 2025. Conditionally repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 666.)
