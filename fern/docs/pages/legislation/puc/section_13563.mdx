# PUC § 13563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 5. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 13563

---

## Text

Upon completion of sewage disposal facilities for any special district all sewage and industrial waste originating within the special district shall be disposed of through the sewage disposal facilities of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
