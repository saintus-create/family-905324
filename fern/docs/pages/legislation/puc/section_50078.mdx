# PUC § 50078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 50078

---

## Text

The board shall have an annual audit made of all books and accounts of the district by a certified public accountant or public accountant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
