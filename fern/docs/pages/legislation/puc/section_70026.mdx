# PUC § 70026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 2. Request by Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 70026

---

## Text

The resolution may state the transit facilities proposed to be first acquired, but failure to acquire such transit facilities shall not affect the validity of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
