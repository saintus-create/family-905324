# PUC § 29504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 1. Annexation Agreement

**Citation:** PUC § 29504

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a petition pursuant to Section 29503, the board of supervisors shall communicate with the board and negotiate an annexation agreement and hold an election as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
