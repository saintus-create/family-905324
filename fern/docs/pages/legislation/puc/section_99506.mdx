# PUC § 99506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Fuel Tax

## Text

The State Board of Equalization shall adopt the necessary rules and regulations to administer the tax.
