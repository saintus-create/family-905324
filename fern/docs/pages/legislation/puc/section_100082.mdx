# PUC § 100082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Government of VTA > Article: 3. Advisory Commission

**Citation:** PUC § 100082

---

## Text

The advisory committees shall provide advice to the board of directors on matters of VTA policy and shall have additional duties as provided by the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 28. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
