# PUC § 125201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 125201

---

## Text

The district may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 150, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2006.)
