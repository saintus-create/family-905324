# PUC § 125350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 6. Transportation Funding

**Citation:** PUC § 125350

---

## Text

The district shall be deemed a provider of services within the area of its jurisdiction for purposes of Section 1604 of Title 49 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 150, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2006.)
