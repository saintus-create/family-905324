# PUC § 11928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

## Text

The general manager need not be a resident of this State at the time of his appointment.
