# PUC § 914.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 914.6

---

## Text

The commission shall report to the Legislature and the Governor annually on the effectiveness of the program administered pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 280.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 612, Sec. 47. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2016.)
