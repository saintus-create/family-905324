# PUC § 29682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 29682

---

## Text

The ballot for the election shall contain such instructions as are required by law to be printed thereon and in addition thereto the following: Shall the “San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District, Special Service District No. _____ (here insert number)” be created and established?YESNO

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1495.)
