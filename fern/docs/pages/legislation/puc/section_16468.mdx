# PUC § 16468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 3. Utility Works and Services

**Citation:** PUC § 16468

---

## Text

Charges fixed by the board for commodities or services furnished may be made payable in advance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
