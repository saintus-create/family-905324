# PUC § 21690.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 4.5. Airport Facilities and Concessions

**Citation:** PUC § 21690.10

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall excuse any local agency from complying with applicable state or local requirements for competitive bidding or public hearings which may be required prior to the awarding or entering into of any bid, agreement or lease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. Effective September 8, 1982.)
