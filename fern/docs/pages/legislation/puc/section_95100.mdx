# PUC § 95100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 95100

---

## Text

The City of Santa Barbara, together with unincorporated territory, may organize and incorporate as the Santa Barbara Metropolitan Transit District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
