# PUC § 13532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 4. Establishment

**Citation:** PUC § 13532

---

## Text

A special district has no separate corporate existence but shall be deemed to be a taxing subdivision of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
