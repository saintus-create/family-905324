# PUC § 5510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. REGULATION OF RELATED BUSINESSES BY THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION > Chapter: 9. Commercial Air Carriers > Article: 1. Accident Liability Protection

**Citation:** PUC § 5510

---

## Text

Any commercial air operator who knowingly refuses or fails to procure protection against liability, as required by Section 5503, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 581, Sec. 3.)
