# PUC § 240003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. RIVERSIDE COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 240003

---

## Text

“Commission” means the Riverside County Transportation Commission created pursuant to Section 130054.

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 270, Sec. 2. Effective July 28, 1987.)
