# PUC § 131202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 4. Metropolitan Transportation Commission Administration of Adopted County Transportation Expenditure Plan

**Citation:** PUC § 131202

---

## Text

The commission shall prepare and adopt an annual report by January 1 of each year on the progress to achieve the objectives of completion of the projects in each county transportation expenditure plan that is administered by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
