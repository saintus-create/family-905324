# PUC § 13561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 5. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 13561

---

## Text

Connections of all sewers within the special district to intercepting sewers of the district shall be made at such points and in such manner and under such rules, regulations, terms, charges, and conditions as the board may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
