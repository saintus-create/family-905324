# PUC § 102264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Transit Planning

**Citation:** PUC § 102264

---

## Text

The adoption of the general plan, or an element or amendment thereof, shall be by resolution of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
