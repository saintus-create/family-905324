# PUC § 11681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 5. Establishment of the District

**Citation:** PUC § 11681

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall cause a certified copy of the order declaring the result of the election to be filed in the Office of the Secretary of State, from and after which the establishment of the district shall be deemed complete.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
