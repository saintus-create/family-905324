# PUC § 846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 5.5. Financing of Transition Costs

**Citation:** PUC § 846

---

## Text

The authority of the commission to issue financing orders pursuant to Section 841 shall expire on June 30, 2022. The expiration of the authority shall have no effect upon financing orders adopted by the commission pursuant to this article or any transition property arising therefrom, or upon the charges authorized to be levied thereunder, or the rights, interests, and obligations of the electrical corporation or a financing entity or holders of transition bonds pursuant to the financing order, or the authority of the commission to monitor, supervise, or take further action with respect to the order in accordance with the terms of this article and of the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 842, Sec. 17. (SB 1222) Effective January 1, 2017.)
