# PUC § 15764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 15764

---

## Text

The proposition submitted at the election shall be stated upon the ballot substantially as follows: “Shall ________ public utility district (naming it) be organized pursuant to Division 7 of the Public Utilities Code.Yes ◻No ◻”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
