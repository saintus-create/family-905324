# PUC § 22705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PUC § 22705

---

## Text

The order calling the bond election shall be signed by a majority of the directors.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
