# PUC § 29753

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 11. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 29753

---

## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and in addition the following: Shall the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District be dissolved?YESNO
