# PUC § 913.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 913.21

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 10231.5 of the Government Code, the commission shall annually submit a report to the Governor and the Legislature on the types, amounts, and costs to ratepayers of eligible energy resources procured pursuant to Section 454.52.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 367, Sec. 9. (AB 1373) Effective October 7, 2023.)
