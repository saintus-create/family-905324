# PUC § 101208.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. General Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 6. Existing Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 101208.2

---

## Text

The district shall not establish the proposed service or system, or maintain and operate the service or system, until it has completed the purchase of the existing system or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
