# PUC § 161024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. STATE TRANSPORTATION CORRIDOR PRESERVATION > Chapter: 2. Duties and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 161024

---

## Text

(a) The department may sell, rent, lease, exchange, or otherwise transfer any land or interest therein acquired under this division pursuant to an implementation plan adopted by the department. Any lease entered into under this division shall be for not more than 20 years.

(b) Except as otherwise provided by law or by agreement, the proceeds of the sale, rental, lease, exchange, or transfer of land or interest therein, including any rent or other income from land held by the department, shall be deposited in the State Highway Account in the State Transportation Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 781, Sec. 1.)
