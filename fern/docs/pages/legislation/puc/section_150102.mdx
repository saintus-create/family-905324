# PUC § 150102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 150102

---

## Text

The acts of the authority shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
