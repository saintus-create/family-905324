# PUC § 106010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 17. Monterey-Salinas Transit District > Chapter: 2. Formation of District

**Citation:** PUC § 106010

---

## Text

There is hereby created the Monterey-Salinas Transit District. The jurisdiction of the district extends throughout the county, including all of the incorporated and unincorporated territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 460, Sec. 1. (AB 644) Effective January 1, 2010.)
