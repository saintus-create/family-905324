# PUC § 10056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 2. Sale and Disposal

**Citation:** PUC § 10056

---

## Text

The resolution and ordinance may provide for the sale of more than one public utility, but in such case the question of selling each public utility shall be separately stated in the ordinance and upon the election ballots.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
