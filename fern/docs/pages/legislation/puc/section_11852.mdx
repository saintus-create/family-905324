# PUC § 11852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2.5. Additional Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11852

---

## Text

The two additional directors provided for by this article shall be nominated and elected in the manner and at the election called and conducted within the district for the election of district directors pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 11821) of this chapter and held at the same time as the 1974 statewide general election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 337.)
