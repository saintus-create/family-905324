# PUC § 102415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 1. Employee Relations

**Citation:** PUC § 102415

---

## Text

The district shall take steps as may be necessary to obtain coverage for the district and its employees under Subchapter II of the Federal Social Security Act, as amended, and the related provisions of the Federal Insurance Contributions Act, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 492, Sec. 25. (SB 598) Effective January 1, 2022.)
