# PUC § 99348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 7. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99348

---

## Text

The transportation planning agency may provide for any other acts and things necessary, convenient or desirable to secure the bonds or tending to make them more marketable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1400.)
