# PUC § 130635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. Code of Conduct for the Board of the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 130635

---

## Text

The rules of conduct at board meetings shall be governed by the board’s adopted rules and procedures. Board members shall treat MTA employees and colleagues on the board with respect and courtesy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 755, Sec. 13. (AB 3123) Effective January 1, 2025.)
