# PUC § 99264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 4. Claims for Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99264

---

## Text

An operator shall not be eligible for allocation under this article if it routinely staffs with two or more persons a vehicle for public transportation purposes designed to be operated by one person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 99266.7 by Stats. 1979, Ch. 161.)
