# PUC § 160000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. PENINSULA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 160000

---

## Text

The Peninsula Corridor Study Joint Powers Board, created pursuant to Section 1 of Chapter 1328 of the Statutes of 1987, is hereby redesignated the Peninsula Rail Transit District and, as such, has the additional powers provided for in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 160005).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1434, Sec. 4. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1434, Sec. 11.)
