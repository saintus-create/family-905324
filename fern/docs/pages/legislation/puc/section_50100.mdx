# PUC § 50100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 50100

---

## Text

The board shall appoint a secretary and an attorney, who shall hold office during the pleasure of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
