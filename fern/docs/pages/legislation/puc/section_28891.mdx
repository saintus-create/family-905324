# PUC § 28891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 2. Benefits and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 28891

---

## Text

The retirement allowance may be predicated in part upon service rendered the district by a member prior to the establishment of the retirement system, which service is known as “prior service.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
