# PUC § 911.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 11. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** PUC § 911.2

---

## Text

The commission shall report the findings of any risk assessment required by Section 1103 to the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 425, Sec. 6. (SB 385) Effective January 1, 2018.)
