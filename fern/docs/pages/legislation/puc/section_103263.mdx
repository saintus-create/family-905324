# PUC § 103263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Transit Planning

**Citation:** PUC § 103263

---

## Text

The adoption of the general plan, or an element or amendment thereof, including any changes or additions, shall be by resolution of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
