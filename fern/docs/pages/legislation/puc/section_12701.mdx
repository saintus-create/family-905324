# PUC § 12701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 12701

---

## Text

A district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
