# PUC § 96900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Exclusion

**Citation:** PUC § 96900

---

## Text

Territory within the district may be detached from the district by a four-fifths vote of the board of directors.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 595.)
