# PUC § 22779

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bonds > Article: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 22779

---

## Text

The total amount of bonds issued shall not exceed 15 percent of the taxable property of the district as shown by the last equalized assessment books of the affected counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
