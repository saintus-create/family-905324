# PUC § 16645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 16645

---

## Text

The board shall state the purposes for which taxes are necessary, and shall fix by ordinance the amount of money necessary to be raised by taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
