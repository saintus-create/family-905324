# PUC § 28812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 5. Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 28812

---

## Text

The compensation of all district officers and employees, except as otherwise provided in this part, shall be fixed by ordinance or resolution of the directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
