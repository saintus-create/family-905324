# PUC § 103107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 103107

---

## Text

All meetings of the board shall be conducted in the manner prescribed by the Ralph M. Brown Act (Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950), Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
