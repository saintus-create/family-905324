# PUC § 30214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30214

## Text

All meetings of the city selection committee shall be conducted pursuant to the Ralph M. Brown Act, Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
