# PUC § 22437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** PUC § 22437

---

## Text

At its first meeting or as soon thereafter as practicable, by a majority vote the board shall appoint a general manager, a secretary, a district counsel, and an auditor. These officers shall serve at the pleasure of the board. The board shall fix their compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
