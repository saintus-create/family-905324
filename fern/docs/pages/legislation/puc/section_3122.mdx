# PUC § 3122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 3122

---

## Text

An association may borrow without limitation as to amount of corporate indebtedness or liability and may make advances to members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
