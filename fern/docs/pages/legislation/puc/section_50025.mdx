# PUC § 50025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 2. Request by Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 50025

---

## Text

The legislative body of the City of Stockton may pass a resolution declaring that in its opinion public interest or necessity demands the creation and maintenance of the Stockton Metropolitan Transit District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
