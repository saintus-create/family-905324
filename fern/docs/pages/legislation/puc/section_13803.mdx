# PUC § 13803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Public Agencies > Article: 1. Annexation Agreement

**Citation:** PUC § 13803

---

## Text

After the legislative body of the public agency and the board have concurred upon the proposed terms and conditions, the secretary or clerk of the public agency to be annexed shall file a certified copy of the proposed agreement with the executive officer of the local agency formation commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2043.)
