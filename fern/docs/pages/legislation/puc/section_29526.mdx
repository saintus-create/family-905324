# PUC § 29526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 29526

---

## Text

When executed by the district the agreement shall be dated and an executed copy filed with the secretary of the district. An executed copy shall also be filed with the clerk of the county to be annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
