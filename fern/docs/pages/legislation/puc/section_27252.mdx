# PUC § 27252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10. Exclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Exclusion of Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 27252

---

## Text

The petition shall describe the territory sought to be excluded and shall set forth that the territory is not benefited in any manner by the district or by its continued inclusion therein, and shall pray that the territory be excluded from the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
