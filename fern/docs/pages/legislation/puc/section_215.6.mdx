# PUC § 215.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 215.6

---

## Text

“Food products for human consumption” means articles used for human food or drink and articles used as components of any of those articles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1685, Sec. 2.)
