# PUC § 90261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 90261

---

## Text

All other things being equal, the board shall appoint as general manager some person who has had experience in the construction or management of transit facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
