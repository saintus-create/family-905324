# PUC § 29754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 11. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 29754

---

## Text

No other notice of the election other than that provided for in this article need be given and no sample ballots need be sent to the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
