# PUC § 3452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.7. GOLDEN STATE ENERGY ACT > Part: 5. Other Matters

**Citation:** PUC § 3452

---

## Text

This division, being necessary for the prosperity of the state and its residents, shall be liberally construed to effect its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 27, Sec. 12. (SB 350) Effective January 1, 2021.)
