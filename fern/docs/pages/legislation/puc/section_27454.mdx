# PUC § 27454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** PUC § 27454

---

## Text

The favorable vote of a majority of all the voters voting on the proposition to authorize the issuance of bonds at the election or elections held pursuant to Sections 27451 or 27452 within the special transit service district as finally established is required to authorize the issuance of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
