# PUC § 120355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 6. Transportation Funding

**Citation:** PUC § 120355

---

## Text

The board may take all action necessary to obtain funding available pursuant to Chapter 53 (commencing with Section 5301) of Subtitle III of Title 49 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 557, Sec. 25. Effective October 5, 2005.)
