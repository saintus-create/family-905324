# PUC § 7701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 9. Rail Service Assistance

**Citation:** PUC § 7701

---

## Text

The Department of Transportation, in cooperation with the commission and other affected state and local agencies, shall be responsible for the preparation and periodic update of the state rail plan required by the act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 819.)
