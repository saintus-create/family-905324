# PUC § 105000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 105000

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Sonoma-Marin Area Rail Transit District Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
