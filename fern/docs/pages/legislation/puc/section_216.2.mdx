# PUC § 216.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 216.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 216, “public utility” does not include a motor carrier of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 216.5 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 198, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2007.)
