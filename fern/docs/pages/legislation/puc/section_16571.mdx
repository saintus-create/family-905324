# PUC § 16571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 6. Indebtedness and Financing

**Citation:** PUC § 16571

---

## Text

A district may borrow money and incur or assume indebtedness, and issue bonds or other evidences of indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
