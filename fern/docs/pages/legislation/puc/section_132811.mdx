# PUC § 132811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.8. IMPERIAL COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 132811

---

## Text

The board shall establish rules for its proceedings consistent with the law of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 56, Sec. 1. (SB 607) Effective January 1, 2010.)
