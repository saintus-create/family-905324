# PUC § 30224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30224

---

## Text

Members of the board of directors shall serve until their respective successors are appointed and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
