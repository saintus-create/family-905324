# PUC § 13851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Public Agencies > Article: 3. Approval by Public Agency

**Citation:** PUC § 13851

---

## Text

At any time after the board has finally approved the agreement of annexation the legislative body of the public agency to be annexed shall cause an election to be held in the public agency to determine whether the public agency will be annexed to the district upon the terms and conditions stated in the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
