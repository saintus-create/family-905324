# PUC § 91010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 5. Annexation Without Election

**Citation:** PUC § 91010

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory may be annexed to a district without an election in the manner provided in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
