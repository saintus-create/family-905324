# PUC § 7671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 7.5. Hazardous Materials Transportation by Rail

**Citation:** PUC § 7671

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the purpose of this article is to protect the health and safety of the public and to improve the safety of transporting hazardous materials by rail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1662, Sec. 11.)
