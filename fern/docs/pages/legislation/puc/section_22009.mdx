# PUC § 22009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 22009

---

## Text

“Principal county” means the county in which all of the territory of the district is situated, or if the territory is situated in more than one county, the county in which the greatest portion of the territory is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
