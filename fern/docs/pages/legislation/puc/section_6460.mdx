# PUC § 6460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 3. Resettlement of Street, Suburban, and Interurban Railroad Franchises

**Citation:** PUC § 6460

---

## Text

The resettlement franchise, permit, or privilege shall be granted for an indeterminate period, subject always to the right of the city or city and county to acquire and possess the property of the grantee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
