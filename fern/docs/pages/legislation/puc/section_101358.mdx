# PUC § 101358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Retirement

**Citation:** PUC § 101358

---

## Text

The retirement system provided for by this chapter shall not include the members of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
