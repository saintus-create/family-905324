# PUC § 99637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99637

---

## Text

Ten million dollars ($10,000,000) shall be allocated to the San Mateo County Transit District for expenditure for extensions of the San Francisco Bay Area Rapid Transit District within San Mateo County.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
