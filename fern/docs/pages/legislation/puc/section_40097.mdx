# PUC § 40097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 4. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 40097

---

## Text

The treasurer shall be the custodian of the funds of the district and shall make payments only upon warrants duly and regularly signed by the chairman of the board or any other person so authorized by the board. The treasurer shall keep an account of all receipts and disbursements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 546.)
