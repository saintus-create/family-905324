# PUC § 15532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 15532

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed as repealing or modifying the provisions of any other law relating to public utility districts, except insofar as the provisions of such other law are inconsistent with the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
