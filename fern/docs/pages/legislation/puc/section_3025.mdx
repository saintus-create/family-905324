# PUC § 3025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 3025

---

## Text

Any exemption under any existing law which applies to gas in the possession, or under the control, of the individual producer, applies equally to gas in the possession, or under the control, of an association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
