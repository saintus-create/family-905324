# PUC § 90930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 1. Annexation Agreement

## Text

Unincorporated territory may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in this chapter.
