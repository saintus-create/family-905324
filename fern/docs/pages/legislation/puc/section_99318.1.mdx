# PUC § 99318.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 6.5. Transportation Planning and Development Account

**Citation:** PUC § 99318.1

---

## Text

An intercity rail project nominated by the department shall be eligible to compete for funding pursuant to Section 99317 if it is recommended in the passenger rail element of the State Rail Plan prepared pursuant to Section 14036 of the Government Code, or an update to that plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 597, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2002.)
