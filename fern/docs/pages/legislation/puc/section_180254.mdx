# PUC § 180254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 180254

---

## Text

The bonds shall bear interest at a rate or rates not exceeding the maximum allowable by law, payable at intervals determined by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
