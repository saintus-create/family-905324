# PUC § 90266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 90266

---

## Text

The attorney shall be admitted to practice law in the Supreme Court of the state, and shall have been actively engaged in the practice of his profession for not less than five years next preceding his appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
