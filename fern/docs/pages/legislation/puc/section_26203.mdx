# PUC § 26203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 26203

---

## Text

In lieu of a resolution passed by the board, proceedings for the issuance of bonds for the purposes provided in this chapter may be initiated by petition of the voters of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
