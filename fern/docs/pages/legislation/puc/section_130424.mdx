# PUC § 130424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 9. Transaction and Use Taxes in the County of Orange

**Citation:** PUC § 130424

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1320, Sec. 1.)
