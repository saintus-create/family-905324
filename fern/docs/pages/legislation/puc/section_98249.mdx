# PUC § 98249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 10. SANTA CRUZ METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 98249

---

## Text

At the time fixed for any hearing before the board any board of supervisors or city council eligible to file a request for hearing, not a party to the original request for hearing, may intervene and shall be entitled to be heard and to introduce evidence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 978.)
