# PUC § 95491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

## Text

A majority of the board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business.
