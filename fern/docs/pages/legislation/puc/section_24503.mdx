# PUC § 24503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24503

---

## Text

“District” means a transit district formed under this part; “board” means the board of directors of a district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 603.)
