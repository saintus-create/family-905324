# PUC § 16801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 1. Petition to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16801

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “acquisition of public utility works” includes construction, completion, or acquisition of public utilities or public utility works.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
