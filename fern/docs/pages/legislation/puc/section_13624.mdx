# PUC § 13624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 13624

---

## Text

Only the property in the special district shall be taxable for the payment of the principal and interest on special district bonds. Until the bonds are paid, taxes shall be levied in substantial compliance with Section 12893.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
