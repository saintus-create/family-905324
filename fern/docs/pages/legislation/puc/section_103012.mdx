# PUC § 103012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 103012

---

## Text

“Transit” or “transit service” means the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage and parcels by any means.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
