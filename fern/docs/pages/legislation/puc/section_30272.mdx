# PUC § 30272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 30272

---

## Text

The board shall adopt rules for the general conduct of its proceedings including, without limitation, penalties for unexcused absence from meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
