# PUC § 13208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 13208

---

## Text

Propositions for incurring indebtedness for more than one object or purpose may be submitted at the same election.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
