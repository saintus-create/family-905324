# PUC § 95458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 95458

---

## Text

The board shall have an annual audit made of all books and accounts of the district by a certified public accountant or public accountant. The general manager shall cause copies of such audit to be made available to the public at the cost of printing only.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 363.)
