# PUC § 15797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Establishment of the District

**Citation:** PUC § 15797

---

## Text

From and after the date the duplicate roll is filed with the Secretary of State, the district is incorporated as a public utility district, with all the rights, privileges, and powers set forth in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
