# PUC § 99503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 5. Motor Vehicle Fuel Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 99503

---

## Text

A special election for a transit development board or a transit district shall be conducted pursuant to the Uniform District Election Law (Part 4 (commencing with Section 10500) of Division 10 of the Elections Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 207. Effective January 1, 1995.)
