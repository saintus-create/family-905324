# PUC § 1401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 8. Determination of Just Compensation for Acquisition of Utility Properties

**Citation:** PUC § 1401

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “lands, property, and rights” includes a part or portion thereof.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
