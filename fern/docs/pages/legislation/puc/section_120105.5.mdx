# PUC § 120105.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 120105.5

---

## Text

The board shall appoint a general manager or chief executive officer with experience in the management, planning, and development of urban mass transportation systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 615, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2005.)
