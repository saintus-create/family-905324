# PUC § 150051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Creation of the Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 150051

---

## Text

The appointed Commissioners of the Tuolumne County LTC shall serve as the Tuolumne County Traffic Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
