# PUC § 161003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. STATE TRANSPORTATION CORRIDOR PRESERVATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 161003

---

## Text

As used in this division, “secretary” means the Secretary of Transportation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 510. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
