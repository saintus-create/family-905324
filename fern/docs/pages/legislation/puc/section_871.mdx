# PUC § 871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4. Regulation of Public Utilities > Article: 8. Universal Telephone Service

**Citation:** PUC § 871

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Moore Universal Telephone Service Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 163, Sec. 2. Effective July 16, 1987.)
