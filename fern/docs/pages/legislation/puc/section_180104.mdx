# PUC § 180104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 180104

---

## Text

All meetings of the authority shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
