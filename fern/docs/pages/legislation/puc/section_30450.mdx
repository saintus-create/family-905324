# PUC § 30450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 3. Employees Under Bargaining Agreements

**Citation:** PUC § 30450

---

## Text

Articles 1 (commencing with Section 30400) and 2 (commencing with Section 30430) do not apply to any employees of the district in a bargaining unit which is represented by a labor organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 193, Sec. 107.)
