# PUC § 99570.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 7. Transit Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 11. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 99570.1

---

## Text

No memorandum of understanding shall contravene any federal or state law, including rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to such laws, prohibiting discrimination in employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 833, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
