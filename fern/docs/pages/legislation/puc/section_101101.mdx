# PUC § 101101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PUC § 101101

---

## Text

Each director shall be a resident of the district. Members of the board of supervisors and members of the City Council of the City of Bakersfield shall not be eligible for appointment as members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
