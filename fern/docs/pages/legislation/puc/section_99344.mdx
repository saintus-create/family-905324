# PUC § 99344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 7. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99344

---

## Text

The transportation planning agency may provide for the terms upon which the bondholders or any percentage of them may enforce covenants or duties imposed by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1400.)
