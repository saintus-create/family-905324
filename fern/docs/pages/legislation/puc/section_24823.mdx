# PUC § 24823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 2. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 24823

---

## Text

The board shall by resolution or ordinance fix the boundaries of the wards for the purpose of electing directors therefrom. The board shall adjust the boundaries of the wards for the purpose of electing directors therefrom pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 22000) of Division 21 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 435, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
