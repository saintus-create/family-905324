# PUC § 103020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 103020

---

## Text

“Establish” includes establish, construct, complete, acquire, extend, or reroute. It does not, however, include the maintenance and operation of any existing system acquired by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
