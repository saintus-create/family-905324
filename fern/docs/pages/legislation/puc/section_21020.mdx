# PUC § 21020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21020

---

## Text

“Land” includes tide and submerged lands or other lands subject to the public trust for commerce, navigation, or fisheries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 534, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
