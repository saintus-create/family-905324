# PUC § 131302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 5. County Transportation Authority > Article: 4. Administration of Adopted County Transportation Plan

**Citation:** PUC § 131302

---

## Text

A county transportation authority shall consult with, and coordinate its actions with, other transportation funding agencies when allocating the net revenues derived from the retail transactions and use tax to the sponsoring agencies of the projects listed in the adopted county transportation expenditure plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
