# PUC § 8371.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 4.5. Microgrids

**Citation:** PUC § 8371.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall discourage or prohibit the development or ownership of a microgrid by an electrical corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 566, Sec. 2. (SB 1339) Effective January 1, 2019.)
