# PUC § 50041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 50041

---

## Text

No person shall be entitled to vote at the election unless he is a voter of the territory included in the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
