# PUC § 240103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. RIVERSIDE COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 2. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 240103

---

## Text

The commission may approve a transaction and use tax ordinance and ballot proposition and call an election in accordance with Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 240300).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 270, Sec. 2. Effective July 28, 1987.)
