# PUC § 2822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 7. Private Energy Producers > Article: 3. Rights, Obligations, and Charges

**Citation:** PUC § 2822

---

## Text

The commission shall approve and establish standby charges for electrical corporations. The commission may act in this regard on its own motion or on application of an electrical corporation or a private energy producer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 373, Sec. 3.)
