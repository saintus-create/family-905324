# PUC § 13655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 13655

---

## Text

Notice fixing the time and place for hearing on the question of the annexation of territory to a special district shall be published once in a newspaper of general circulation published in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
