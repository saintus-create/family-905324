# PUC § 240316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. RIVERSIDE COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 240316

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold as the commission determines by resolution, and the bonds may be sold at a price below par, whether by negotiated or public sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 270, Sec. 2. Effective July 28, 1987.)
