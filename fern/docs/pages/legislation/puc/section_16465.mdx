# PUC § 16465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 3. Utility Works and Services

**Citation:** PUC § 16465

---

## Text

A district may construct its works across any stream of water or watercourse.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 136.)
