# PUC § 31003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Provisions Relating to the Los Angeles Metropolitan Transit Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 31003

---

## Text

On said merger date, all employees of the authority shall become employees of the district with all the same rights, privileges and compensation they had as employees of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
