# PUC § 40503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 40503

---

## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and in addition the following: Shall the Orange County Transit District be dissolved?YESNO
