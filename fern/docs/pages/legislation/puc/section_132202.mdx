# PUC § 132202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 4. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 132202

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against the commission are governed by Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code except as provided therein, or by other statutes or regulations expressly applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
