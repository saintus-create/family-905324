# PUC § 99210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 99210

---

## Text

“Operator” means any transit district, included transit district, municipal operator, included municipal operator, or transit development board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1188.)
