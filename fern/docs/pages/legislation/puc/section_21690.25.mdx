# PUC § 21690.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 4. Airports and Air Navigation Facilities > Article: 5. Los Angeles International Airport Relocation and Development

**Citation:** PUC § 21690.25

---

## Text

Upon establishment by the department of a program for such additional compensation, the mayor shall appoint, subject to the approval of the city council, five persons who shall constitute the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 942.)
