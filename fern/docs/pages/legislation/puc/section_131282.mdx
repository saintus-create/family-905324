# PUC § 131282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.5. COUNTY TRAFFIC AND TRANSPORTATION FUNDING IN THE NINE-COUNTY SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA > Chapter: 5. County Transportation Authority > Article: 3. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 131282

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against a county transportation authority are governed by Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code except as provided therein, or by other statutes or regulations expressly applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 301, Sec. 3. Effective July 14, 1986.)
