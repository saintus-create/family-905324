# PUC § 11655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 11655

---

## Text

The board of supervisors calling the election shall make all provision for the holding thereof throughout the entire district as proposed, and shall pay the cost thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
