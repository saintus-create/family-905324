# PUC § 99635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99635

---

## Text

Thirtyfive million dollars ($35,000,000) shall be allocated to the Public Utilities Commission of the City and County of San Francisco, for expenditure for rail projects within the City and County of San Francisco.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
