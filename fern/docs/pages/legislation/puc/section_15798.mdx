# PUC § 15798

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 4. Establishment of the District

**Citation:** PUC § 15798

---

## Text

The board may change the name of the district by adoption of an ordinance changing the name of the district and by giving notification of the name change to the State Controller, the State Board of Equalization, and the board of supervisors of each county which has land within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 446.)
