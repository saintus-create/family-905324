# PUC § 16642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 16642

---

## Text

Farm products and crops which are stored within the exterior boundaries of the district are not subject to taxation by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
