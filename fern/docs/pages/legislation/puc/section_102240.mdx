# PUC § 102240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 3. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 102240

---

## Text

The district may take by grant, purchase, devise, or lease, or condemn in proceedings under eminent domain, or otherwise acquire, and hold and enjoy, real and personal property of every kind within or without the district necessary to the full or convenient exercise of its powers. The board may lease, mortgage, sell, or otherwise dispose of any real or personal property within or without the district necessary to the full or convenient exercise of its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
