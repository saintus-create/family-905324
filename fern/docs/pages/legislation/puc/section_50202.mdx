# PUC § 50202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 6. Indebtedness

**Citation:** PUC § 50202

---

## Text

The district shall not incur an indebtedness under Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 50225) of this part which in the aggregate exceeds two (2) percent of the assessed value of all the real and personal property within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
