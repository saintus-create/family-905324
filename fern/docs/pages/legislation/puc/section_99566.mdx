# PUC § 99566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 7. Transit Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 7. Organizational Security

**Citation:** PUC § 99566

---

## Text

Subject to the limitations set forth in this chapter, organizational security shall be within the scope of representation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 833, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
