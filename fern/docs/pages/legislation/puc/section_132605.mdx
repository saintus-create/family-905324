# PUC § 132605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. Exposition Metro Line Construction Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 132605

---

## Text

The authority is hereby created for the purpose of awarding and overseeing final design and construction contracts for completion of the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 827, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
