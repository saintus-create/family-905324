# PUC § 407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.5. Public Utilities Commission Reimbursement Fees > Article: 1. Collection and Disposition of Fees

**Citation:** PUC § 407

---

## Text

The commission shall authorize refunds of the fees provided for in this chapter when the fees were collected in error.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 323, Sec. 71. Effective July 1, 1983.)
