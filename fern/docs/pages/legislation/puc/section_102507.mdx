# PUC § 102507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 1. Authorization and Issuance of General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 102507

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
