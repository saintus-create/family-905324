# PUC § 99340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 7. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99340

---

## Text

The transportation planning agency may provide for limitations on:

(a) The purpose to which the proceeds of sale of any issue of bonds may be applied.

(b) The issuance of additional bonds for the same purpose and the lien of additional bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1400.)
