# PUC § 11931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 11931

---

## Text

The board may appoint an accountant, a secretary, a treasurer, and an attorney, who shall hold office during the pleasure of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
