# PUC § 103109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 103109

---

## Text

The expiration date of the term of office of each member shall be the 31st day of December in the year in which his term is to expire.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
