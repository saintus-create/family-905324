# PUC § 95198

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 95198

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall meet on the Tuesday next succeeding the day of the election and canvass the returns.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
