# PUC § 102101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 102101

---

## Text

A member of a city council or board of supervisors may serve as a member of the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
