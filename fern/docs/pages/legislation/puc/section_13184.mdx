# PUC § 13184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 12. Short Term Borrowing

**Citation:** PUC § 13184

---

## Text

All of the provisions of this chapter not inconsistent with the terms of this article, and not by their terms made inapplicable thereto, shall apply to all notes, or other evidences, issued under the authority of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
