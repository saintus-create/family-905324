# PUC § 28746.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 2.2. District Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28746.2

---

## Text

All cities, counties, and cities and counties within the district shall provide such information, services, and facilities as may be required in apportioning the district into the nine election districts.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 184.)
