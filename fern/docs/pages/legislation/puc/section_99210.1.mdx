# PUC § 99210.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 99210.1

## Text

“Operator” also means the San Joaquin Regional Rail Commission for operation of commuter rail services.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 615, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2005.)
