# PUC § 103140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 103140

---

## Text

The board is the legislative body of the district and shall determine all questions of district policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
