# PUC § 100160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 100160

---

## Text

The VTA may provide transit service for the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage by any means.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 52. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
