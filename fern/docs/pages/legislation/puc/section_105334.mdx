# PUC § 105334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 105334

---

## Text

The board shall canvass the vote. If a majority of the votes favor dissolution, the board shall by resolution dissolve the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
