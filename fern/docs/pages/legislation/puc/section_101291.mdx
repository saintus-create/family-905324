# PUC § 101291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 101291

---

## Text

Propositions for incurring indebtedness for more than one object or purpose, or propositions proposed by resolution or petition, may be submitted at the same election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
