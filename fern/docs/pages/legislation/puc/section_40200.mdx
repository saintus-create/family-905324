# PUC § 40200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 6. Indebtedness

**Citation:** PUC § 40200

---

## Text

The district shall have the power to obtain temporary transfers of funds in accordance with the last paragraph of Section 31, Article IV of the State Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1899.)
