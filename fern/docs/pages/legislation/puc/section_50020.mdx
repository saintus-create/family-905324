# PUC § 50020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 50020

---

## Text

The City of Stockton, together with unincorporated territory, may organize and incorporate as the Stockton Metropolitan Transit District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
