# PUC § 101141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 6. Removal From Office

**Citation:** PUC § 101141

## Text

Any such removal from office shall create a vacancy to be filled in the manner prescribed by Sections 101135 and 101136.
