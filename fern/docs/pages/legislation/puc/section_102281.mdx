# PUC § 102281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 102281

---

## Text

The district may provide charter bus service, special school service, and other service to the extent permitted under federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 522, Sec. 10. (AB 709) Effective January 1, 2019.)
