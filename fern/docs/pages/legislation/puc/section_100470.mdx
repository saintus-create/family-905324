# PUC § 100470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Improvement Acts and Special Benefit Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 100470

---

## Text

The Improvement Act of 1911, the Municipal Improvement Act of 1913, and the Improvement Bond Act of 1915 are applicable to the VTA.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 99. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
