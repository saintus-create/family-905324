# PUC § 50090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 50090

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances shall be as follows:

“Be it enacted by the board of directors of San Joaquin Regional Transit District:” All ordinances shall be signed by the chairperson of the board or the vice chairperson and attested by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 704, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1995.)
