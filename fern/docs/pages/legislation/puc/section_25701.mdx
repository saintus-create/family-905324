# PUC § 25701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 25701

---

## Text

A district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
