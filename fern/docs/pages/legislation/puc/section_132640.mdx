# PUC § 132640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 7. Exposition Metro Line Construction Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 132640

---

## Text

The authority shall not encumber any future farebox revenue anticipated from the operation of the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 827, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
