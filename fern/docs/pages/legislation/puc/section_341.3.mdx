# PUC § 341.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 2. Oversight Board

**Citation:** PUC § 341.3

---

## Text

Voting members of the Oversight Board shall be required to file financial disclosure statements with the Fair Political Practices Commission. The appointing authority for voting members shall avoid appointing persons with conflicts of interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 261, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1998.)
