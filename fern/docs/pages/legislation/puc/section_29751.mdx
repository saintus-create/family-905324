# PUC § 29751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 11. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 29751

---

## Text

The election, for the purpose of submitting to the voters of the district the question of whether or not the district shall be dissolved, shall be held within 60 days following the date on which it is ordered by the board or on which the petition is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 181.)
