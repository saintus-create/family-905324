# PUC § 90420.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 90420.5

---

## Text

The district further shall be empowered to furnish contractual service outside of the district’s boundaries.
