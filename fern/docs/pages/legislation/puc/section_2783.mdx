# PUC § 2783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 6. Home Insulation Assistance and Financing

**Citation:** PUC § 2783

---

## Text

A home insulation assistance and financing program shall meet the requirements specified in Sections 2784 to 2786, inclusive, and such other requirements as the commission may impose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1202.)
