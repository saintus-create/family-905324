# PUC § 103392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 11. Investments

**Citation:** PUC § 103392

---

## Text

Any bonds, treasury notes, or obligations purchased and held as investments by the district may, from time to time, be sold and the proceeds reinvested in bonds, treasury notes, or obligations as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
