# PUC § 12898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 12898

---

## Text

In such case the county auditor shall, on or before August 15th of each year, transmit to the board a statement in writing showing the total value of all property within the district, ascertained from the assessments referred to in Section 12897 as equalized.
