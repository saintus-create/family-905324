# PUC § 97004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 97004

---

## Text

The board shall canvass the vote. If a majority of the votes favor dissolution, the board shall by resolution dissolve the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
