# PUC § 21010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 21010

---

## Text

“Political subdivision” means any county, city, city and county, public corporation, district or other political entity or public corporation of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
