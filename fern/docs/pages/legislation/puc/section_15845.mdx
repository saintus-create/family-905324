# PUC § 15845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 6. Verification Deputies

**Citation:** PUC § 15845

---

## Text

Verification deputies shall not be paid, in whole or in part, directly or indirectly, out of the county treasury or the treasury of a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
