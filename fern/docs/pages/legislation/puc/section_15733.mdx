# PUC § 15733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 2. Formation of Districts > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 15733

---

## Text

The hearing may be adjourned from time to time for the determination of facts, not exceeding two weeks in all.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
