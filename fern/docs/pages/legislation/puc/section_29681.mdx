# PUC § 29681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 29681

---

## Text

Such election shall be held on the next established election date not less than 74 days after the board’s approval of the creation of the special service district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1146.)
