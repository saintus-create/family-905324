# PUC § 161031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16.5. STATE TRANSPORTATION CORRIDOR PRESERVATION > Chapter: 2. Duties and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 161031

---

## Text

The department shall comply with applicable environmental protection laws and regulations when taking any action authorized by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 781, Sec. 1.)
