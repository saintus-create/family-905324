# PUC § 30405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Retirement System and Employment Benefits > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** PUC § 30405

---

## Text

The board may also contract with the Board of Administration of the State Employees’ Retirement System for participation in the Federal Social Security Act and may perform all acts necessary or convenient for such participation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
