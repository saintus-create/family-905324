# PUC § 13562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 5. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 13562

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall require the district to construct any sewers other than intercepting and outfall sewers. The determination by the board of what are intercepting and outfall sewers within the meaning of this division is final and conclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
