# PUC § 13593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 6. Financing

**Citation:** PUC § 13593

---

## Text

Nothing in this article limits the general powers of a district with reference to sewage disposal or solid waste resource recovery.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 128.)
