# PUC § 17369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Annexation > Article: 2. Annexation of Noncontiguous Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 17369

---

## Text

The terms and conditions shall become effective and binding upon the annexing district and upon the territory to be annexed and the petitioning district when approved in the manner provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
