# PUC § 102012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 102012

---

## Text

“Transit” means the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage by any means.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
