# PUC § 240303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. RIVERSIDE COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 240303

---

## Text

(a) The county shall conduct an election called by the board of supervisors pursuant to Section 240301.

(b) The election shall be called and conducted in the same manner as provided by law for the conduct of elections by a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 270, Sec. 2. Effective July 28, 1987.)
