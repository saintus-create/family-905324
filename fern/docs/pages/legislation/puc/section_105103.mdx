# PUC § 105103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 4. Rail Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 105103

---

## Text

The rates and charges for rail transit service furnished pursuant to this part shall be fixed by the board and shall be reasonable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
