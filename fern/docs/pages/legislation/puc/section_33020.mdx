# PUC § 33020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 12. Special Benefit Assessment Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 33020

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the district shall not pledge any portion of its general fund revenues to pay any part of any bonded indebtedness incurred under this chapter unless required by provisions of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1322, Sec. 4.)
