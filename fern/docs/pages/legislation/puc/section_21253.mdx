# PUC § 21253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 1. STATE AERONAUTICS ACT > Chapter: 2. Department of Transportation and State Aeronautics Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 21253

---

## Text

In the name of the state, the department may enforce this part and rules and orders issued under this part by injunction or other legal process in the courts of this state.
