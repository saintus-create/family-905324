# PUC § 16646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 16646

---

## Text

By ordinance, the provisions of which conform to general law, the board may provide the manner of assessing and of correcting and equalizing assessments upon the taxable property situated within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
