# PUC § 30254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30254

---

## Text

The board shall determine what transit facilities should be acquired, constructed, developed, jointly developed, leased, or disposed of, by means including, but not limited to, lease, sale, purchase, option, gift, devise, condemnation, grant, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 497, Sec. 1.5.)
