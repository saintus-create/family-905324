# PUC § 30502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 30502

---

## Text

The district may levy, and collect or cause to be collected, taxes for any lawful purpose, as provided in Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 30800) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
