# PUC § 132370.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Transportation Consolidation For San Diego > Article: 8. Bonds, Equipment, and Leases

**Citation:** PUC § 132370.2

---

## Text

The Improvement Act of 1911 (Division 7 (commencing with Section 5000) of the Streets and Highways Code), the Improvement Bond Act of 1915 (Division 10 (commencing with Section 8500) of the Streets and Highways Code), and the Municipal Improvement Act of 1913 (Division 12 (commencing with Section 10000) of the Streets and Highways Code), are applicable to the consolidated agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 743, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
