# PUC § 8361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 4. Smart Grid Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 8361

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “ISO” means the Independent System Operator operating pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 345) of Chapter 2.3 of Part 1 of Division 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 327, Sec. 1. (SB 17) Effective January 1, 2010.)
