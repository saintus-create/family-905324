# PUC § 100381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 6. Personnel > Article: 4. Other Benefits

**Citation:** PUC § 100381

---

## Text

The VTA shall take the steps as may be necessary to obtain coverage for the VTA and its employees under the workers’ compensation, unemployment compensation disability, and unemployment insurance laws of the State of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 381, Sec. 84. (AB 2196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
