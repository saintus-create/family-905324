# PUC § 16082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 16082

---

## Text

If a petition, or petition as amended, is certified to be sufficient the ordinance is suspended from going into operation and the board shall reconsider it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
