# PUC § 28951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 28951

---

## Text

The district may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
