# PUC § 90071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 90071

---

## Text

Each signer of a petition within a particular city shall be a registered voter and resident of that city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
