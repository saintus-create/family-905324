# PUC § 30002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 30002

---

## Text

The part shall be liberally construed to carry out the objects and purposes and the declared policy of the State of California as in this part set forth.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
