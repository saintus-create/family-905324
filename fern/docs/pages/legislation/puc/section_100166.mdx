# PUC § 100166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of VTA > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 100166

---

## Text

The rates and charges, if any, for transit service furnished pursuant to this part shall be fixed by the board and shall be reasonable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 180.)
