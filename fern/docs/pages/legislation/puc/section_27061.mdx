# PUC § 27061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 5. Annexation Without Election

**Citation:** PUC § 27061

---

## Text

Unincorporated territory may be annexed to a district without an election in the manner provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
