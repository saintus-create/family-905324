# PUC § 102700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 102700

---

## Text

The district may be dissolved pursuant to the provisions of the District Reorganization Act of 1965 (Division 1 (commencing with Section 56000), Title 6 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1374.)
