# PUC § 170034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SAN DIEGO COUNTY REGIONAL AIRPORT AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 170034

---

## Text

All the provisions of Section 120242 are applicable to the authority, and the authority may exercise those provisions within its area of jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 946, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
