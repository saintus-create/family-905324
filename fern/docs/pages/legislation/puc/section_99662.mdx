# PUC § 99662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 4. Applications for Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99662

---

## Text

Two or more applicant agencies may join in applying for a grant for a project in which each of the agencies will participate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
