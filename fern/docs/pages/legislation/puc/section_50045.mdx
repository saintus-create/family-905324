# PUC § 50045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Formation of District > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 50045

---

## Text

If a special election is held exclusively on the proposition of organizing the district, the expenditure therefor shall be reimbursed to the county by means of a tax on all the taxable property within the territory which was proposed to be included in the district, and this tax shall be added to the next county tax bills by the proper official of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
