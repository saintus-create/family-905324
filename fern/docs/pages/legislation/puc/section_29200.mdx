# PUC § 29200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 3. Issuance and Sale of General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 29200

---

## Text

The district may by resolution at any time, or from time to time, provide for the authorization and issuance of any bonds authorized pursuant to this part and for the sale of the whole or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
