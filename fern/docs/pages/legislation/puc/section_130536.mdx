# PUC § 130536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 5. Los Angeles County Transportation Commission Revenue Bond Act > Article: 4. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** PUC § 130536

---

## Text

Neither the members of the commission, nor any person executing the bonds, are liable personally on the bonds, or are subject to any personal liability or accountability by reason of the issuance thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1646, Sec. 2.)
