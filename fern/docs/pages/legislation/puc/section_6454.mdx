# PUC § 6454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 3. Resettlement of Street, Suburban, and Interurban Railroad Franchises

**Citation:** PUC § 6454

---

## Text

No resettlement franchise shall go into effect until it has received the approval of a majority of the electors voting thereon and until it has been accepted in writing by the grantee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
