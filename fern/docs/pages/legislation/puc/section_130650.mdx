# PUC § 130650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 6. Code of Conduct for the Board of the Los Angeles County Metropolitan Transportation Authority

**Citation:** PUC § 130650

---

## Text

Committee chairs shall present items from their committee meetings and the recommendation of their committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 900, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1998.)
