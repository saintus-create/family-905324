# PUC § 7704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 1. Railroad Corporations > Article: 9. Rail Service Assistance

**Citation:** PUC § 7704

---

## Text

To the maximum extent permitted by federal law, rules, and regulations, the Department of Transportation shall recover the costs of administering this chapter from the federal funds received pursuant to the act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 819.)
