# PUC § 5372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. REGULATION OF RELATED BUSINESSES BY THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION > Chapter: 8. Charter-Party Carriers of Passengers > Article: 2. Authorization to Operate as a Charter-Party Carrier of Passengers

**Citation:** PUC § 5372

---

## Text

Applications for permits and certificates shall be in writing, verified under oath, and shall be in such form, contain such information, and be accompanied by such proof of service upon interested parties as the commission may require.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 234.)
