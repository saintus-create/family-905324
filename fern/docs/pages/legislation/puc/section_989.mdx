# PUC § 989

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4.7. Core Transport Agent

**Citation:** PUC § 989

---

## Text

Unclaimed refunds ordered by the commission, and any accrued interest, may be used by the commission to fund additional consumer protection efforts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 604, Sec. 4. (SB 656) Effective January 1, 2014.)
