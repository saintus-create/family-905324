# PUC § 22015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 22015

---

## Text

“Formation election” means an election provided for in Article 4 (commencing with Section 22226) of Chapter 2.
