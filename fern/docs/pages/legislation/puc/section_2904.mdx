# PUC § 2904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 3. SURRENDER OF MUNICIPAL CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Surrender of Control by Municipal Corporations > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 2904

---

## Text

“Municipal corporation” means a city and county or incorporated city.
