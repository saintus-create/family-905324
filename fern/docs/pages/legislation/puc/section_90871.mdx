# PUC § 90871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation of Cities > Article: 6. Effect of Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 90871

---

## Text

No annexation of a city to a district shall operate to dissolve or terminate the legal existence of the city annexed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
