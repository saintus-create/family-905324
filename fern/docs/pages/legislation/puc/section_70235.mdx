# PUC § 70235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** PUC § 70235

---

## Text

The board shall comply with Article 3 (commencing with Section 9160) of Chapter 2 of Division 9 of the Elections Code, the provisions of which are applicable to any bond election held pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 202. Effective January 1, 1995.)
