# PUC § 17005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 17005

---

## Text

District bonds have the same force, value, and use as bonds issued by a municipality.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
