# PUC § 130352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 6. Transactions and Use Taxes

**Citation:** PUC § 130352

---

## Text

Any transactions and use tax ordinance adopted shall be operative on the first day of the first calendar quarter commencing not less than 180 days after adoption of the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
