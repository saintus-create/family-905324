# PUC § 12827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Utility Works and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 12827

---

## Text

The board of a district that has owned and operated an electric distribution system for at least eight years and has a population of 250,000 or more may engage in programs to encourage economic development that benefits its ratepayers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 53, Sec. 2. Effective April 26, 1994.)
