# PUC § 101254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 5. Budget

**Citation:** PUC § 101254

---

## Text

The board may continue the hearing from day to day; provided the hearing shall be concluded not later than July 15 first following its commencement.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
