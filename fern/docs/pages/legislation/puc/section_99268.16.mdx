# PUC § 99268.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 4. Claims for Funds

**Citation:** PUC § 99268.16

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (a) of Section 99247, the costs of providing ridesharing services are excluded from operating costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1143, Sec. 2.)
