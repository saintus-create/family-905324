# PUC § 28601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 2. Creation of District

**Citation:** PUC § 28601

---

## Text

Through compliance with the provisions for withdrawal set forth in Chapter 10 of this part, the territory of any of the Counties of Alameda, Contra Costa, Marin, San Francisco, and San Mateo may be withdrawn from the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
