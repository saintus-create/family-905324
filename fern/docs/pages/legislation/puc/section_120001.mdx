# PUC § 120001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 120001

---

## Text

As used in this division, “board” means a county transit development board created pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 120050).

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 294.)
