# PUC § 6464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PUBLIC UTILITY FRANCHISES BY LOCAL GOVERNMENTS > Chapter: 3. Resettlement of Street, Suburban, and Interurban Railroad Franchises

**Citation:** PUC § 6464

---

## Text

The resettlement franchise shall provide that the grantee, its successors or assigns, shall never claim before any court or other public authority in any proceeding of any character any value for the resettlement franchise, permit, or privilege in excess of the amount originally paid for it by the grantee to the granting authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
