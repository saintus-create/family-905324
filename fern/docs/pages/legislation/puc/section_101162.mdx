# PUC § 101162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 8. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 101162

---

## Text

All other things being equal, the board shall appoint as general manager a person who has had experience in the acquisition, construction, or management of transit facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
