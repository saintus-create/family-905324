# PUC § 454.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 3. Rights and Obligations of Public Utilities > Article: 1. Rates

**Citation:** PUC § 454.7

---

## Text

The commission shall, to the extent permitted by federal law and consistent with Section 2771, provide cogeneration technology projects with the highest possible priority for the purchase of natural gas.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 922.)
