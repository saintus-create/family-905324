# PUC § 103226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 103226

---

## Text

The district may contract for the services of independent contractors.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
