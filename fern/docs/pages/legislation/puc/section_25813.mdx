# PUC § 25813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 25813

---

## Text

The general counsel for the district shall be present at the hearing and shall advise the board on matters of law, and shall render other assistance that may be requested by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 200, Sec. 16. (AB 784) Effective January 1, 2022.)
