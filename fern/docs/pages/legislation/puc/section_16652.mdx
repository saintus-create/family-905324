# PUC § 16652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 16652

---

## Text

Immediately after fixing the rate of taxes the board shall transmit to the county auditors of the counties in which the district is situated a statement of the rate so fixed by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
