# PUC § 185510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19.5. CALIFORNIA HIGH-SPEED RAIL SERVICE > Chapter: 5. Relocation of Utilities

**Citation:** PUC § 185510

---

## Text

The authority, in acting upon applications for utility permits, shall consider both the interests of the traveling public upon the high-speed rail property and the needs of consumers for utility services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 35, Sec. 7. (SB 85) Effective June 27, 2013.)
