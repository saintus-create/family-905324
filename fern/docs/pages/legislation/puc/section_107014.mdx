# PUC § 107014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 18. GOLD COAST TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Meetings

**Citation:** PUC § 107014

---

## Text

The acts of the board shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 503, Sec. 1. (AB 664) Effective January 1, 2014.)
