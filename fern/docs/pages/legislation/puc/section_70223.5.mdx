# PUC § 70223.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Retail Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 70223.5

---

## Text

Repeal of the transactions and use tax ordinance shall not be operative earlier than the first day of the first calendar quarter following the adoption of the ordinance of repeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 672.)
