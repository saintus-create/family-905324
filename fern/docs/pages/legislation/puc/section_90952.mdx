# PUC § 90952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 90952

---

## Text

At the hearing any person interested may file with the board written objections to the approval of the terms and conditions.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
