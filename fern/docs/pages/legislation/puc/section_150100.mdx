# PUC § 150100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15.5. TUOLUMNE COUNTY TRAFFIC AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 150100

---

## Text

The organization and procedures manual of the Tuolumne County LTC shall be the established rules for the proceedings and administration of the authority except as provided otherwise in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1521, Sec. 1.)
