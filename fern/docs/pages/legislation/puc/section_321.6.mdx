# PUC § 321.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2. The Public Utilities Commission: Organization

**Citation:** PUC § 321.6

---

## Text

The president of the commission shall annually appear before the appropriate policy committees of the Senate and Assembly to present the annual report of the commission required pursuant to Section 910.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 612, Sec. 6. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2016.)
