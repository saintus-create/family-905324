# PUC § 25897

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 25897

---

## Text

In such case the county auditor shall, on or before the third Monday in August of each year, transmit to the board a statement in writing showing the total value of all property within the district, ascertained from the assessments referred to in Section 25896 as equalized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
