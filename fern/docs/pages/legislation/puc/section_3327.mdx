# PUC § 3327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. CALIFORNIA CONSUMER POWER AND CONSERVATION FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. The California Consumer Power and Conservation Financing Authority > Article: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 3327

---

## Text

Meetings of the board shall be open to the public and shall be conducted in accordance with the Bagley-Keene Open Meeting Act (Article 9 (commencing with Section 11120) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 10, Sec. 1. Effective August 13, 2001.)
