# PUC § 15503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 15503

---

## Text

“District” means a public utility district formed pursuant to this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
