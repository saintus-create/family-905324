# PUC § 7802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 2. Street Railroads

**Citation:** PUC § 7802

---

## Text

When a street railroad is constructed, owned, or operated by any natural person, this chapter is applicable to such person in like manner as it is applicable to corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
