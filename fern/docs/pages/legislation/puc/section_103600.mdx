# PUC § 103600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 103600

---

## Text

The district may bring an action to determine the validity of any of its bonds, equipment trust certificates, warrants, notes, or other evidences of indebtedness pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860), Title 10, Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
