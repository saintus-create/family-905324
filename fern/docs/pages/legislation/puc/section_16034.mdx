# PUC § 16034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 16034

---

## Text

The board may from time to time contract for or employ any professional services required by the district, the board, or any officers of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
