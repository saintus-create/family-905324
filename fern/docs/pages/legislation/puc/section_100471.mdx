# PUC § 100471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Improvement Acts and Special Benefit Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 100471

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 99000) of Part 11 of Division 10 of the Public Utilities Code are applicable to the VTA.
