# PUC § 3023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 4. PRODUCTION AND TRANSMISSION OF PUBLIC UTILITY COMMODITIES AND SERVICES > Chapter: 1. Gas Producers’ Cooperative Associations > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 3023

---

## Text

Associations are “nonprofit,” since they are not organized to make profit for themselves, as such, or for their members, as such, but only for their members as producers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 680, Sec. 1.)
