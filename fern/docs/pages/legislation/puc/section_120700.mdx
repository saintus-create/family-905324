# PUC § 120700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 6. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** PUC § 120700

---

## Text

The board may bring an action to determine the validity of any of its bonds, equipment trust certificates, warrants, notes, or other evidences of indebtedness pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 599, Sec. 2.)
