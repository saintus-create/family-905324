# PUC § 16815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 1. Petition to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16815

---

## Text

The affirmative vote of a majority of the electors voting upon the proposition is necessary to accept a proposition submitted under Section 16811.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
