# PUC § 99213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 99213

---

## Text

“Transit district” means a public district organized pursuant to state law and designated in the enabling legislation as a transit district or a rapid transit district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1400.)
