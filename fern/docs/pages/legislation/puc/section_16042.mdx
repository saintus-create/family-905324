# PUC § 16042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 16042

---

## Text

A neglect or refusal of the board to comply with Sections 16601 to 16614, inclusive, 16644 to 16659, inclusive, and 16467 is cause for removal from office.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
