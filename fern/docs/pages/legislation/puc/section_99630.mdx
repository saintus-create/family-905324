# PUC § 99630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99630

---

## Text

Two hundred twentynine million dollars ($229,000,000) shall be allocated to the Los Angeles County Transportation Commission, for expenditure on rail projects within Los Angeles County as follows:

(a) Not less than eighty million dollars ($80,000,000) thereof shall be allocated for Metro Rail.

(b) Not less than eighty million dollars ($80,000,000) thereof shall be allocated for rail projects other than Metro Rail within Los Angeles County.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
