# PUC § 100608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 12. SANTA CLARA VALLEY TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 9. Benefit Assessment Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 100608

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds issued under this chapter may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 727, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
