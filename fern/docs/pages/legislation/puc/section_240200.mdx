# PUC § 240200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. RIVERSIDE COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. Powers and Functions

**Citation:** PUC § 240200

---

## Text

The commission may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 270, Sec. 2. Effective July 28, 1987.)
