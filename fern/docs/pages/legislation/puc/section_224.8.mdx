# PUC § 224.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 224.8

---

## Text

“Network railroad transportation” means railroad transportation that is subject to the jurisdiction of the federal Surface Transportation Board pursuant to subsection (a) or (b) of Section 10501 of Title 49 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 1005, Sec. 4.5. Effective January 1, 2000.)
