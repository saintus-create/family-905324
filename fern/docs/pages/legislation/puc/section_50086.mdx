# PUC § 50086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 50086

## Text

A majority of the board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business.
