# PUC § 102265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Transit Planning

**Citation:** PUC § 102265

---

## Text

The Sacramento Area Council of Governments shall be the long-range planning agency advising the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 272, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2007.)
