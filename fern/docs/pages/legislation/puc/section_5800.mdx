# PUC § 5800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2.5. THE DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE AND VIDEO COMPETITION ACT OF 2006

**Citation:** PUC § 5800

---

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Digital Infrastructure and Video Competition Act of 2006.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 700, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
