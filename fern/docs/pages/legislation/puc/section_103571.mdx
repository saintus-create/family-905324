# PUC § 103571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 7. Bonds and Other Evidences of Indebtedness > Article: 4. Improvement Acts and Special Benefit Districts

**Citation:** PUC § 103571

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 99000) of Part 11 of this division are applicable to the district.
