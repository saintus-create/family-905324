# PUC § 24931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 24931

---

## Text

The board may appoint a secretary and a general counsel who shall hold office during the pleasure of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 200, Sec. 7. (AB 784) Effective January 1, 2022.)
