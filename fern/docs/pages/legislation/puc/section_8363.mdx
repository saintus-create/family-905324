# PUC § 8363

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 4. Smart Grid Systems

**Citation:** PUC § 8363

---

## Text

This chapter shall be implemented in a manner that does not compromise customer or worker safety or the integrity or reliability of the electrical transmission and distribution system in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 327, Sec. 1. (SB 17) Effective January 1, 2010.)
