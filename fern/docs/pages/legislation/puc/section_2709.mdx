# PUC § 2709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 2. Water Companies

**Citation:** PUC § 2709

---

## Text

The commission may require any water corporation to file with the commission a statement in writing defining and describing the lands and territory to be supplied by the corporation with water.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
