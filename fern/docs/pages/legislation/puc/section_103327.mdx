# PUC § 103327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 103327

---

## Text

The county auditor shall, on or before the third Monday in August of each year, transmit to the board a statement in writing showing the total value of all property within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
