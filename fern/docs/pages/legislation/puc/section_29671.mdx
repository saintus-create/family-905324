# PUC § 29671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10.5. Special Service Districts > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** PUC § 29671

---

## Text

The notice of hearing shall be published once in a newspaper of general circulation published in the district. The board, in its discretion, may give such additional notice as it deems desirable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1495.)
