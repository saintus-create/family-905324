# PUC § 101140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 6. Removal From Office

**Citation:** PUC § 101140

---

## Text

Any member of the board may be removed at any time and without cause by the body which is authorized, pursuant to Section 101103, to make the appointment of the particular class of director who is removed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
