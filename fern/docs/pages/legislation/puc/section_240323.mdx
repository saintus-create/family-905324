# PUC § 240323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 25. RIVERSIDE COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Transactions and Use Tax

**Citation:** PUC § 240323

---

## Text

The commission has no power to impose any tax other than the transactions and use tax imposed upon approval of the voters in accordance with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 270, Sec. 2. Effective July 28, 1987.)
