# PUC § 101135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of the District > Article: 5. Vacancies

**Citation:** PUC § 101135

---

## Text

Any vacancy in the membership of the board shall be filled for the unexpired term of the member whose office has become vacant, and shall be made by appointment by the body which is authorized, pursuant to Section 101103, to make the appointment of the particular class of director whose office has become vacant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
