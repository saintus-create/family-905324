# PUC § 120201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. TRANSIT DEVELOPMENT BOARDS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 120201

---

## Text

The board may sue and be sued, except as otherwise provided by law, in all actions and proceedings, in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 294.)
