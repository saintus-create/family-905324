# PUC § 422.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.5. Public Utilities Commission Reimbursement Fees > Article: 2. Common Carriers and Related Businesses

**Citation:** PUC § 422.1

---

## Text

In addition to the requirements of Section 422, the commission may establish a uniform annual fee to be paid by every charter-party carrier of passengers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1369, Sec. 2.)
