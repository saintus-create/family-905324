# PUC § 70210.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 70210.5

---

## Text

The maximum tax rate set forth in Section 70210 may be increased to an amount approved by a majority of the electors of the district voting at a special election called for such purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 930.)
