# PUC § 170038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. SAN DIEGO COUNTY REGIONAL AIRPORT AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** PUC § 170038

---

## Text

The authority may take by grant, purchase, devise, or lease or otherwise acquire, hold, enjoy, lease, and dispose of, real and personal property within or outside its area of jurisdiction in order to further its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 978, Sec. 7. Effective September 27, 2002.)
