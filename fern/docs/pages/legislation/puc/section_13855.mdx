# PUC § 13855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Public Agencies > Article: 3. Approval by Public Agency

**Citation:** PUC § 13855

## Text

If the proposition fails to carry, the result shall be entered upon the minutes of the governing body of the public agency.
