# PUC § 99647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11.5. CLEAN AIR AND TRANSPORTATION IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 1990 > Chapter: 3. Grants

**Citation:** PUC § 99647

---

## Text

Seven million dollars ($7,000,000) shall be allocated to the City of South Lake Tahoe for expenditure on acquisition of rights-ofway, construction of an intermodal station, and related facilities for an exclusive public mass transit guideway project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1990, by initiative Proposition 116.)
