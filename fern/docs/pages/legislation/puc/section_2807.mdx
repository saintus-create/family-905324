# PUC § 2807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 7. Private Energy Producers > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 2807

---

## Text

“Standby charge” means a charge by an electrical corporation for providing standby generation, transmission, and distribution facilities to a private energy producer employing other than a conventional power source for the generation of electricity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 373, Sec. 1.)
