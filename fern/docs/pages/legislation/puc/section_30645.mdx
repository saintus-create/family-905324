# PUC § 30645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Rapid Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 30645

---

## Text

Within 30 days after the conclusion of the hearing, the board shall render its decision, in writing, with written findings of fact. Copies of the findings and decision shall be sent immediately to the petitioners and intervenors by certified mail, postage prepaid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 184.)
