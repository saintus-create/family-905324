# PUC § 180258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 180258

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold as the authority determines by resolution, and the bonds may be sold at a price below par, whether by negotiated or public sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
