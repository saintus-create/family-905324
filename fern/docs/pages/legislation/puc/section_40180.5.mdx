# PUC § 40180.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 40180.5

---

## Text

The district may make bulk purchases of passenger tickets for scheduled passenger rail services provided by the National Railroad Passenger Corporation during peak hours, as defined by subdivision (a) of Section 99260.2, on Monday through Friday, inclusive.

The district may resell to residents of the district the tickets at less than the cost to the district.
