# PUC § 90954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. SAN DIEGO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 9. Annexation of Unincorporated Territory > Article: 2. Approval by District

**Citation:** PUC § 90954

---

## Text

If no protests are filed or the protests filed are overruled and denied by the board, the board shall by resolution finally approve the terms and conditions and proceed with the annexation in the manner provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2039.)
