# PUC § 50176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 5. SAN JOAQUIN REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 4. Property

**Citation:** PUC § 50176

---

## Text

Whenever the board by resolution determines that any record, map, book, or paper in the possession of the district or any officer or employee thereof is of no further value to the district, the board may authorize its sale, destruction, or other disposition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 839.)
