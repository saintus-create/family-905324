# PUC § 103221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 2. Contracts

**Citation:** PUC § 103221

---

## Text

The district may contract with any department or agency of the United States of America, with any public agency, or with any person upon such terms and conditions as the board finds is for the best interest of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
