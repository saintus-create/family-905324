# PUC § 22443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 2. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** PUC § 22443

---

## Text

The general manager, secretary, auditor, and all other employees or assistants of the district who may be required to do so by the board, shall give bonds to the district conditioned for the faithful performance of their duties as the board may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
