# PUC § 22017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 2. AIRPORT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 22017

---

## Text

“District tax” means the tax levied pursuant to Chapter 6 for the payment of expenses of, and claims against the district, other than the bonded indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 151.)
