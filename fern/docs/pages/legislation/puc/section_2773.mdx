# PUC § 2773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 2. SPECIFIC PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 4.5. Electrical and Gas Corporations

**Citation:** PUC § 2773

---

## Text

The commission may establish as many priorities of use for a customer as that customer has uses of gas or electricity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 757.)
