# PUC § 99367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 11. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO ALL PUBLIC TRANSIT > Chapter: 4. Transportation Development > Article: 7. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** PUC § 99367

---

## Text

The bonds may be refunded in the manner now or as may hereafter be provided in Article 11 (commencing with Section 54660), Chapter 6, Part 1, Division 2, Title 5 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1400.)
