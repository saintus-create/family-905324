# PUC § 24241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. AVIATION > Part: 5. AIRCRAFT FINANCIAL RESPONSIBILITY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 24241

---

## Text

“State” means any state, the District of Columbia, any territory or possession of the United States and the Commonwealth of Puerto Rico.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1452.)
