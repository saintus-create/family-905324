# PUC § 70270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 7. MARIN COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 70270

---

## Text

Territory within Marin County may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 92.)
