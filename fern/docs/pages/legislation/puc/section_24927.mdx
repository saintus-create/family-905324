# PUC § 24927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. Other Officers

**Citation:** PUC § 24927

---

## Text

All other things being equal, the board shall appoint as general manager some person who has had experience in the construction or management of transit facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
