# PUC § 125200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11.5. NORTH COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 125200

---

## Text

The district has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 150, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2006.)
