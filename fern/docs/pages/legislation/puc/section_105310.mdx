# PUC § 105310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 16. SONOMA-MARIN AREA RAIL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Service Zones

**Citation:** PUC § 105310

---

## Text

To assist in the operation of a service zone, the board may appoint one or more advisory groups composed of persons who reside in or own property in the zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 341, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
