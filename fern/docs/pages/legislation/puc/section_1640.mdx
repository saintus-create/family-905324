# PUC § 1640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 8.7. School Energy Efficiency Stimulus Program > Article: 5. Repealer

**Citation:** PUC § 1640

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 372, Sec. 5. (AB 841) Effective January 1, 2021. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 8.7, commencing with Section 1600.)
