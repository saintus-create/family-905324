# PUC § 11861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 3. Terms of Office of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 11861

---

## Text

The directors elected at the formation election shall hold their respective offices only until the first day of January next following the next general election and until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 985, Sec. 24.)
