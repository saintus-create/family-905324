# PUC § 16162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 6. District Elections

**Citation:** PUC § 16162

---

## Text

Whenever a special election is called pursuant to Articles 1 or 2 of Chapter 5 for the purpose of submitting to the electors a proposition, the board shall pass an ordinance calling the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
