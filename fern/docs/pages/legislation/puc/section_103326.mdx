# PUC § 103326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 8. Property Taxation

**Citation:** PUC § 103326

---

## Text

The board shall avail itself of the assessments made by the county assessor and of the assessments made by the State Board of Equalization for the county, and shall take such assessments as the basis for district property taxation and have its property taxes collected by the county tax collector.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 92, Sec. 180. (SB 1289) Effective January 1, 2019.)
