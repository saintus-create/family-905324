# PUC § 96750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 96750

---

## Text

Territory may be annexed to the district in the manner provided in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
