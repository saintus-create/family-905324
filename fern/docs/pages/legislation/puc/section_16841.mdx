# PUC § 16841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Acquisition of Works > Article: 2. Resolution to Acquire Works

**Citation:** PUC § 16841

---

## Text

Whenever the board determines that the public interest or necessity of the district demands the acquisition of any public utility works, it shall specifically declare such determination by a resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
