# PUC § 1808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 9. Hearings and Judicial Review > Article: 5. Intervenor’s Fees and Expenses

**Citation:** PUC § 1808

---

## Text

The commission shall deny any award to any customer or eligible local government entity that attempts to delay or obstruct the orderly and timely fulfillment of the commission’s responsibilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 808, Sec. 13. (SB 512) Effective January 1, 2017.)
