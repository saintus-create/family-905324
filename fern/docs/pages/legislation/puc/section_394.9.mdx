# PUC § 394.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.3. Electrical Restructuring > Article: 12. Consumer Protection

**Citation:** PUC § 394.9

---

## Text

Unclaimed refunds ordered by the commission, and any accrued interest, may be used by the commission to fund additional consumer protection efforts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 275, Sec. 24. Effective August 15, 1997.)
