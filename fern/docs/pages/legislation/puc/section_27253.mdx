# PUC § 27253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10. Exclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Exclusion of Unincorporated Territory

**Citation:** PUC § 27253

---

## Text

The petition shall be filed with the secretary of the district and shall be accompanied by a deposit with the secretary of the sum of one hundred dollars ($100) to meet the expenses of advertising and other costs incident to the proceedings for the exclusion of the territory, including the cost of recording a certified copy of the order provided for in this chapter. Any unconsumed balance of the deposit shall be returned to the petitioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
