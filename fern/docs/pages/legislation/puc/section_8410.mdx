# PUC § 8410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.1. PROVISIONS APPLICABLE TO PRIVATELY OWNED AND PUBLICLY OWNED PUBLIC UTILITIES > Chapter: 10. Information Sharing

**Citation:** PUC § 8410

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, all of the following definitions apply:

(a) “Electrical corporation” has the same meaning as defined in Section 218.

(b) “Local publicly owned electric utility” has the same meaning as defined in Section 224.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 709, Sec. 1. (AB 777) Effective January 1, 2026.)
