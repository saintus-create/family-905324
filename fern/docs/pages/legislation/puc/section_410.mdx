# PUC § 410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 2.5. Public Utilities Commission Reimbursement Fees > Article: 1. Collection and Disposition of Fees

**Citation:** PUC § 410

## Text

The commission may establish rules and regulations that it deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 323, Sec. 71. Effective July 1, 1983.)
