# PUC § 13576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 5.5. Waste Water Control

**Citation:** PUC § 13576

---

## Text

Any person who intentionally discharges waste water in any manner, in violation of any order issued by the district, which results in contamination, pollution or a nuisance, as defined in this article, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 733.)
