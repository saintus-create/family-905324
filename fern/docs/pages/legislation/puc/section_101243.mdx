# PUC § 101243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Audits and Financial Report

**Citation:** PUC § 101243

---

## Text

A copy of the financial report shall be kept in the office of the general manager and shall be available to public inspection at all times during the regular office hours of his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
