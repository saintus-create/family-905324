# PUC § 10154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. UTILITIES OWNED BY MUNICIPAL CORPORATIONS > Chapter: 1. Acquisition, Operation, and Sale of Utilities > Article: 4. Rights in Public Lands and Waters

**Citation:** PUC § 10154

---

## Text

If the route or location of any works or adjuncts is changed so as not to cover or cross the lands selected under this article, or the use of the land selected is abandoned, the selected lands revert, and the title thereto is reinvested in the State or its grantees free from all such uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
