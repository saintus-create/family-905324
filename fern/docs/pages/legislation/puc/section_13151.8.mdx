# PUC § 13151.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 9. Form and Content

**Citation:** PUC § 13151.8

---

## Text

The board may provide for the appointment of the treasurer of the district to act as such fiscal, paying, sinking fund or other agent.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
