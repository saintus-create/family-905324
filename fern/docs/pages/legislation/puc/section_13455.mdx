# PUC § 13455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 8. Special District for Sewage Disposal or Solid Waste Resource Recovery > Article: 1. Proposal of Formation

**Citation:** PUC § 13455

---

## Text

The secretary of the district shall compare the signatures with the affidavits of registration and shall certify to the sufficiency or insufficiency of the petition.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
