# PUC § 95451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 95451

---

## Text

The first meeting of the board shall be held within 10 days of the appointment of its chairman. The board may make its own rules of procedure and determine the place and time of its meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1835.)
