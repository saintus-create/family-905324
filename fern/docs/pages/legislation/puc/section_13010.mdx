# PUC § 13010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 11. Municipal Improvement Act of 1913

**Citation:** PUC § 13010

---

## Text

The Municipal Improvement Act of 1913 may be used by a district formed pursuant to this division for any purpose or purposes which a district may carry out.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 210.)
