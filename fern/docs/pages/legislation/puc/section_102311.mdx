# PUC § 102311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 6. Public Grants, Loans and Contributions

**Citation:** PUC § 102311

---

## Text

The district shall have the power to obtain temporary transfers of funds in accordance with the last paragraph of Section 6 of Article XVI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 272, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 2007.)
