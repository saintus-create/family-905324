# PUC § 103280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 15. SAN MATEO COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 103280

---

## Text

The district may provide transit service within or without the district for the transportation of passengers and their incidental baggage and parcels by any means.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 502.)
