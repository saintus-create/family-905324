# PUC § 130200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 1. Corporate Powers

**Citation:** PUC § 130200

---

## Text

The commission has perpetual succession and may adopt a seal and alter it at its pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
