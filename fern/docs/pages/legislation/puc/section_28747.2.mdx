# PUC § 28747.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 2.2. District Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28747.2

---

## Text

A candidate for election from any of the election districts shall be nominated and elected by the voters residing within the election district from which he is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 521.)
