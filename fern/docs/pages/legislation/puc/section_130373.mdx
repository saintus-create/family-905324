# PUC § 130373

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions > Article: 7. Transit Service Dispute Resolution

**Citation:** PUC § 130373

---

## Text

Prior to January 1, 1981, only a transit service dispute submitted by a transit operator in the County of Los Angeles may be resolved by the Los Angeles County Transportation Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 579.)
