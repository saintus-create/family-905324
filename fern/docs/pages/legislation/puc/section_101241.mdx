# PUC § 101241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 6. General Financial Provisions > Article: 4. Audits and Financial Report

**Citation:** PUC § 101241

---

## Text

The fiscal affairs of the district shall be predicated upon a fiscal year commencing on the first day of July of each calendar year and terminating on the 30th day of June of the following calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
