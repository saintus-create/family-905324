# PUC § 101302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 2. Form and Content

**Citation:** PUC § 101302

---

## Text

The bonds shall be issued in such denominations as the board shall determine and shall be payable on the day and at the place or places fixed in the bonds, and with interest at the rate specified therein, payable semiannually or annually the first year and semiannually thereafter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
