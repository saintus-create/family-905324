# PUC § 13261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 7. Bonds > Article: 3. Issue and Sale

**Citation:** PUC § 13261

---

## Text

The bonds may be issued and sold for not less than their par value, but otherwise as the board determines.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
