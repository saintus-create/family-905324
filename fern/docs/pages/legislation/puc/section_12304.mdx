# PUC § 12304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 1. Establishment

**Citation:** PUC § 12304

---

## Text

The board may adopt all ordinances and resolutions and perform all acts necessary or convenient to the initiation, maintenance, and administration of the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1951, Ch. 764.)
