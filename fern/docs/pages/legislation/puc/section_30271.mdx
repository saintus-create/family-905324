# PUC § 30271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 4. Meetings and Legislation

**Citation:** PUC § 30271

---

## Text

The affirmative votes of a majority of the members of the board shall be necessary and, except as otherwise provided in this part, shall be sufficient to carry any order, resolution, or ordinance coming before the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
