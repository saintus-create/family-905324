# PUC § 30259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 30259

---

## Text

The board shall have an annual audit made of all books and accounts of the district by an independent certified public accountant or public accountant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 62.)
