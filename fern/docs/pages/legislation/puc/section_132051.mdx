# PUC § 132051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12.7. COUNTY AND REGIONAL TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 2. San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission > Article: 2. Creation of the Commission

**Citation:** PUC § 132051

---

## Text

The Board of Directors of the San Diego Association of Governments shall serve as the San Diego County Regional Transportation Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1576, Sec. 23.)
