# PUC § 5353.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. REGULATION OF RELATED BUSINESSES BY THE PUBLIC UTILITIES COMMISSION > Chapter: 8. Charter-Party Carriers of Passengers > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 5353.5

---

## Text

On and after July 1, 1989, this chapter does not apply to transportation service, other than transportation service furnished in a limousine for hire, rendered wholly within the corporate limits of a single city or city and county and licensed or regulated by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1105, Sec. 2.)
