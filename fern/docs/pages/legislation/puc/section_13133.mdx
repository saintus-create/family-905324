# PUC § 13133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6.5. Electric System Improvements > Article: 7. Obligation to Bondholders

**Citation:** PUC § 13133

---

## Text

Bonds issued under this chapter shall be issued without any priority with respect to payment of principal or interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2237.)
