# PUC § 7810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. LAWS RELATING TO UTILITY CORPORATIONS AND THEIR EMPLOYEES > Chapter: 2. Street Railroads

**Citation:** PUC § 7810

---

## Text

Street railway cars shall be of the most approved construction for the comfort and convenience of passengers, and provided with brakes to stop them, when required. A violation of this section subjects the corporation to a fine of two hundred dollars ($200) for each offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 360. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
