# PUC § 25815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 5. Transit Facilities and Service

**Citation:** PUC § 25815

---

## Text

Within 30 days after submission of the case the board shall render its decision in writing together with written findings of fact. Copies of the findings and decision shall be sent forthwith to the petitioners and intervenors by registered mail, postage prepaid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1036.)
