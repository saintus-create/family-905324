# PUC § 950.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. REGULATION OF PUBLIC UTILITIES > Part: 1. PUBLIC UTILITIES ACT > Chapter: 4.5. Gas Pipeline Safety > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** PUC § 950.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to gas pipeline facilities owned and operated by a local publicly owned utility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 520, Sec. 1. (SB 44) Effective January 1, 2012.)
