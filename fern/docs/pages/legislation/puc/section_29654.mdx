# PUC § 29654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Withdrawal From District

**Citation:** PUC § 29654

---

## Text

Any county which is withdrawn from the district pursuant to this chapter may be subsequently annexed thereto on compliance with Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 29500).
