# PUC § 40070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 4. ORANGE COUNTY TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of District > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 40070

---

## Text

The chairman is the presiding officer of the board and he shall vote on the propositions passed upon by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1899.)
