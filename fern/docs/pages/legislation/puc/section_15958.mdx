# PUC § 15958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization of Districts > Article: 1. Election of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 15958

---

## Text

If only one unit of unincorporated territory is contained in the district, it may be designated as “the unincorporated territorial unit.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 72.)
