# PUC § 130103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 130103

---

## Text

The acts of the commission shall be expressed by motion, resolution, or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
