# PUC § 28765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Government of District > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 28765

---

## Text

The board may determine what transit facilities should be acquired or constructed:

(a) For the common benefit of the district as a whole; or

(b) For the benefit of two or more zones; or

(c) For the benefit of a single zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1056.)
