# PUC § 27429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 1. TRANSIT DISTRICTS IN COUNTIES OF ALAMEDA OR CONTRA COSTA > Chapter: 10.5. Special Transit Service Districts > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** PUC § 27429

---

## Text

The costs of the election required by this article shall be borne equally by the public agency and the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 116.)
