# PUC § 102100.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 4. Government of District > Article: 1. Board of Directors

**Citation:** PUC § 102100.6

---

## Text

An action by the board shall not be void or voidable if it is determined, subsequent to an action in which a member representing a participating entity casts a vote, that the agreement for that participating entity did not comply with subdivision (b) of Section 102100.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 92, Sec. 10. (AB 1924) Effective January 1, 2025.)
