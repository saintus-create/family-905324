# PUC § 30700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 3. SOUTHERN CALIFORNIA RAPID TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 7. Indebtedness

**Citation:** PUC § 30700

---

## Text

The district may issue any bonds, borrow money and incur indebtedness as authorized by law or in this part provided.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1215.)
