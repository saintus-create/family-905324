# PUC § 12857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. MUNICIPAL UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 6. Powers and Functions of District > Article: 6a. Electric, Water, Communications, or Sewage Disposal System Improvements

**Citation:** PUC § 12857

---

## Text

The authority herein contained shall be in addition to the authority conferred by Chapter 7 of this division and indebtedness incurred in accordance with the authority herein contained shall not be included in ascertaining the aggregate indebtedness specified in Section 12842.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12852 by Stats. 1965, Ch. 916.)
