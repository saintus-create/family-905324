# PUC § 95861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. Retirement System > Article: 3. Retirement Board

## Text

All members of the retirement board shall serve without pay.
