# PUC § 60022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 6. YOLO COUNTY TRANSPORTATION DISTRICT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PUC § 60022

## Text

All meetings of the district shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 457, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
