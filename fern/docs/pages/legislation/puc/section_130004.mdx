# PUC § 130004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY TRANSPORTATION COMMISSIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Findings, and Definitions

**Citation:** PUC § 130004

---

## Text

As used in this division, “multicounty designated transportation planning agency” means the Southern California Association of Governments.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1333.)
