# PUC § 180101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LOCAL TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Administration

**Citation:** PUC § 180101

---

## Text

The authority shall adopt rules for its proceedings consistent with the laws of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 786, Sec. 1.)
