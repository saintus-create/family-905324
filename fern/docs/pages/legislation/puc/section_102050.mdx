# PUC § 102050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 14. SACRAMENTO REGIONAL TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 3. Creation of District and Annexation

**Citation:** PUC § 102050

---

## Text

The Sacramento Regional Transit District may be formed pursuant to this chapter to carry out the purposes of this part. The district is hereby designated a rapid transit district. The district shall be a public corporation for those purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 522, Sec. 3. (AB 709) Effective January 1, 2019.)
