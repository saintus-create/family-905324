# PUC § 97003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 9. THE SANTA BARBARA METROPOLITAN TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 10. Dissolution

**Citation:** PUC § 97003

---

## Text

The ballots for the election shall contain substantially the instructions required to be printed on ballots for use at general state and county elections and in addition the following:Shall the Santa Barbara Metropolitan Transit District be dissolved?YESNO
