# PUC § 16466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. PUBLIC UTILITY DISTRICT ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Districts > Article: 3. Utility Works and Services

**Citation:** PUC § 16466

---

## Text

A district using a street or highway shall restore it to its former state as nearly as possible and shall not unnecessarily impair its usefulness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 195, Sec. 137.)
