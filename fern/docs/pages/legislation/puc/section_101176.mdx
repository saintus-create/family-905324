# PUC § 101176

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. TRANSIT DISTRICTS > Part: 13. GOLDEN EMPIRE TRANSIT DISTRICT > Chapter: 5. General Powers and Functions of the District > Article: 1. Corporate Power

**Citation:** PUC § 101176

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the district may sue and be sued in all courts and tribunals of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1161.)
