# GOV § 56887

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Commission Decision > Article: 2. Terms and Conditions

**Citation:** GOV § 56887

---

## Text

Any change of organization or reorganization may be conditionally approved by a local agency formation commission subject to the certification by the California Coastal Commission of an amendment to the local coastal program of a city or a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 211. Effective January 1, 2001.)
