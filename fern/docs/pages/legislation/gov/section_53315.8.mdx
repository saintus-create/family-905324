# GOV § 53315.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 53315.8

---

## Text

A county may not form a district within the territorial jurisdiction of a city without the consent of the legislative body of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1440, Sec. 3.1.)
