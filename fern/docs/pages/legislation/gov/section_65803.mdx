# GOV § 65803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Zoning Regulations > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 65803

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, this chapter shall not apply to a charter city, except to the extent that the same may be adopted by charter or ordinance of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 190, Sec. 1.5. Effective June 25, 1986.)
