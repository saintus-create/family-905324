# GOV § 9350.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 9350.10

---

## Text

“Net earnings” means the earnings of the retirement fund less the administrative costs specified in Section 9354 and transfers to the reserve against deficiencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 168, Sec. 1.)
