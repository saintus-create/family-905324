# GOV § 25204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 25204

---

## Text

The board may assume and discharge such municipal functions of the cities within the county as are authorized by any county charter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
