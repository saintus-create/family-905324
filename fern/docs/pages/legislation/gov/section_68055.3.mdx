# GOV § 68055.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.97. RECYCLING, RESOURCE RECOVERY, AND LITTER PREVENTION

**Citation:** GOV § 68055.3

---

## Text

Placement of litter receptacles shall be in conformance with laws, ordinances, resolutions, and regulations pertaining to fire, safety, public health, or welfare.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 364.)
