# GOV § 92103

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 2. Organization of Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 92103

---

## Text

All members of the commission shall serve thereon without compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
