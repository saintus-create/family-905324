# GOV § 3118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8.5. Volunteers > Article: 3. Volunteers

**Citation:** GOV § 3118

---

## Text

All volunteers shall comply with applicable department and other state policy and regulations. It shall be the responsibility of the state department in which a volunteer participates to insure this compliance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1195.)
