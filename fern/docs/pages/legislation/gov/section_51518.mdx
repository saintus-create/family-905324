# GOV § 51518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 51518

---

## Text

The county auditor shall apportion to the city the money paid for redemption in the proportion which the tax due the city bears to the total tax for which the real property was sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
