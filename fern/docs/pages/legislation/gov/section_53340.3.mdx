# GOV § 53340.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 4. Procedures for Levying

**Citation:** GOV § 53340.3

---

## Text

At the request of the legislative body, the tax collector may set forth on the tax bill descriptive information provided by the legislative body to identify each public entity receiving portions of the revenue from the special tax levied pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1440, Sec. 10.)
