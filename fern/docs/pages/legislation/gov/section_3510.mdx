# GOV § 3510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10. Local Public Employee Organizations

**Citation:** GOV § 3510

---

## Text

(a) The provisions of this chapter shall be interpreted and applied by the board in a manner consistent with and in accordance with judicial interpretations of this chapter.

(b) The enactment of this chapter shall not be construed as making the provisions of Section 923 of the Labor Code applicable to public employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3509 by Stats. 2000, Ch. 901, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
