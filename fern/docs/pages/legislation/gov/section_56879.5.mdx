# GOV § 56879.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 6. Inactive Special Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 56879.5

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to a special district formed by special legislation that is required by its enabling statute to obtain funding within a specified period of time or be dissolved. That district shall not be subject to this article during that specified period of time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 56880 (as added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 334, Sec. 7) by Stats. 2018, Ch. 86, Sec. 11. (AB 3254) Effective January 1, 2019.)
