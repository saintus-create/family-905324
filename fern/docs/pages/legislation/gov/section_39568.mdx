# GOV § 39568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 13. Weed and Rubbish Abatement > Article: 2. Alternative Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 39568

---

## Text

At the time stated in the notices, the legislative body shall hear and consider all objections to the proposed removal of weeds, rubbish, refuse, and dirt. It may continue the hearing from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
