# GOV § 65089.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2.65. Management of Traffic Congestion and Stormwater Pollution in San Mateo County

**Citation:** GOV § 65089.15

## Text

The City/County Association of Governments of San Mateo County shall provide a report to the Legislature on the program by July 1, 2006.
