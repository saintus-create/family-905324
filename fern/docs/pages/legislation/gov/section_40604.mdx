# GOV § 40604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Mayor

**Citation:** GOV § 40604

---

## Text

The mayor may acknowledge the execution of all instruments executed by the city and required to be acknowledged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 41. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
