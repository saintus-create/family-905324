# GOV § 9350.56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 9350.56

---

## Text

“State service,” within the meaning of Sections 9350.55, 9356.15, and 9356.16 means employment with the Legislature or either house thereof as an officer or employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1074, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1998.)
