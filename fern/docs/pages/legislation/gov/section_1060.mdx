# GOV § 1060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Residence Restrictions and Absence from State

**Citation:** GOV § 1060

---

## Text

The following officers shall keep their offices in the City of Sacramento:

(a) The Governor.

(b) Secretary of State.

(c) Controller.

(d) Treasurer.

(e) Attorney General.

(f) Superintendent of Public Instruction.

(g) Justices of the Supreme Court.

(h) Clerk and reporters of the Supreme Court.

(i) Adjutant General.
