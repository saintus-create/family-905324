# GOV § 40517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 40517

---

## Text

“Property” means any real property acquired pursuant to the Federal Property and Administration Services Act of 1949, or other federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
