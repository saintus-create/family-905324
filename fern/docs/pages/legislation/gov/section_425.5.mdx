# GOV § 425.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.5

---

## Text

The California gray whale (Eschrichtius robustus) is the official State Marine Mammal.
