# GOV § 23518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23518

---

## Text

If the clerk certifies a petition to be sufficient, the clerk shall immediately transmit a copy of the certification to the board of supervisors of each county and to each of the chief petitioners, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
