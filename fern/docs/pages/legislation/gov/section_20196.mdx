# GOV § 20196

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 6. Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 20196

---

## Text

The board may contract with the Department of General Services or any other state department for assistance and supervision in the acquisition of real property and the construction thereon of buildings and improvements authorized in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
