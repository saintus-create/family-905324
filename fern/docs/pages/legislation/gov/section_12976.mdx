# GOV § 12976

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Enforcement and Hearing Procedures > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices

**Citation:** GOV § 12976

---

## Text

Any person who willfully violates Section 12946 concerning recordkeeping is guilty of a misdemeanor, punishable by imprisonment in a county jail, not exceeding six months, or by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 134. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
