# GOV § 65454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8. Specific Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 65454

---

## Text

No specific plan may be adopted or amended unless the proposed plan or amendment is consistent with the general plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1009, Sec. 18.)
