# GOV § 29852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 3. Duplicate Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 29852

---

## Text

If the duplicate warrant is not presented for payment to the county treasurer within the time within which the original warrant could have been presented for payment it is void.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 585.)
