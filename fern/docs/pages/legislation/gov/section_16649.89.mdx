# GOV § 16649.89

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Prohibited Business Arrangements

**Citation:** GOV § 16649.89

---

## Text

State moneys shall not be deposited with financial institutions that, following January 1, 1994, engage in discriminatory business practices in furtherance of or in compliance with the Arab League’s economic boycott of Israel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1351, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
