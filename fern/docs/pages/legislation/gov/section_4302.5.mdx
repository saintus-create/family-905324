# GOV § 4302.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Preference for Materials > Article: 1. American-Made Materials

**Citation:** GOV § 4302.5

---

## Text

The provisions of this article do not apply to the purchase of sewing machines, regardless of the place of their manufacture or the source of the materials from which such machines were manufactured.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1829.)
