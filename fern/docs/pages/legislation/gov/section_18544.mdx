# GOV § 18544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18544

---

## Text

“Duration employment” means an employment during time of war or during an emergency in connection with the national defense, which employment is subject to termination and other conditions as prescribed by Section 19200 and by rules.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 12. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
