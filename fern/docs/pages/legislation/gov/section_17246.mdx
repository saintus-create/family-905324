# GOV § 17246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 3. Reimbursement and Refunding Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17246

---

## Text

Notice of public sale of registered reimbursement warrants shall be given by the Controller by publication, not less than three days prior to sale, at least once in a newspaper published in the City of Sacramento.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 336, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008.)
