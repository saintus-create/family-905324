# GOV § 27284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 3. Documents to be Recorded

**Citation:** GOV § 27284

---

## Text

Affidavits showing work or posting of notices upon mining claims may be recorded in the recorder’s office of the county where the mining claims are situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
