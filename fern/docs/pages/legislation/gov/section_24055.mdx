# GOV § 24055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 24055

---

## Text

Any clerk or sheriff who receives any fine or forfeiture and refuses or neglects to pay it over according to law and within 30 days after its receipt is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 192. Effective September 28, 1998.)
