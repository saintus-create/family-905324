# GOV § 9221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees of the Legislature > Article: 4.5. Speaker of the Assembly

**Citation:** GOV § 9221

---

## Text

Every member, officer, and employee of the Assembly shall, upon request, assist the Speaker in carrying out the duties imposed upon him under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1247.)
