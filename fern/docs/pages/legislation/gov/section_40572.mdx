# GOV § 40572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 6. Co-operation

**Citation:** GOV § 40572

---

## Text

The authority may obligate itself to pay the purchase price or installments thereof, in the manner and to the extent required under federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
