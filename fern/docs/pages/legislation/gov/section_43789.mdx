# GOV § 43789

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 43789

---

## Text

If the entire issue of bonds is to mature at one time, the bonds may be called for redemption in numerical order at par and accrued interest on any interest payment date prior to their fixed maturity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
