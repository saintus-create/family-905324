# GOV § 17252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 3. Reimbursement and Refunding Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17252

---

## Text

It is not essential to the validity of the sale of any reimbursement warrant that it be actually registered prior to sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
