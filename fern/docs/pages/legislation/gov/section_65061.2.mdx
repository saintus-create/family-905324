# GOV § 65061.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 2. Creation of Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 65061.2

---

## Text

The boundaries of every regional planning district shall be coextensive with the boundaries of the region within which it is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
