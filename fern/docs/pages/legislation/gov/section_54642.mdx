# GOV § 54642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 10. Remedies of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 54642

---

## Text

By mandamus or other suit or proceeding at law or in equity a bondholder or his trustee may enforce his rights against the local agency, its legislative body, and any of its officers, agents, and employees and compel them to perform and carry out their duties under this chapter and their agreements with bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
