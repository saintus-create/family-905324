# GOV § 14529.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 14529.10

---

## Text

The department shall recommend, and the commission shall adopt, guidelines and procedures to implement Sections 14529.8 and 14529.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1475, Sec. 4.)
