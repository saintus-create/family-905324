# GOV § 14200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. State Employee Telecommuting Program

**Citation:** GOV § 14200

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “telecommuting” means the partial or total substitution of computers or telecommunication technologies, or both, for the commute to work by employees residing in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1389, Sec. 1.)
