# GOV § 54517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 6. Duties of Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 54517

---

## Text

The local agency shall preserve and protect the security of the bonds and the rights of the bondholders and warrant and defend their rights against all claims and demands of all persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
