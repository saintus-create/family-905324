# GOV § 51131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 4. Immediate Rezoning

**Citation:** GOV § 51131

---

## Text

A timberland production zone may not be immediately rezoned except pursuant to a request by a landowner, and as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 19.)
