# GOV § 50142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6. Reimbursement for Use of Drainage Structures

**Citation:** GOV § 50142

---

## Text

The word “person” as used in this article in reference to a person liable to pay a charge includes districts formed for the purpose of constructing or maintaining sewers. Charges imposed on such districts pursuant to this article shall be included in assessments levied for district purposes and in any bonds which may be issued by such districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 831.)
