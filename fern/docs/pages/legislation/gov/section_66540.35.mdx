# GOV § 66540.35

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 3. Facilities, Equipment, and Services

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.35

---

## Text

The authority may acquire, own, lease, construct, and operate water transportation vessels and equipment, including, but not limited to, real and personal property, equipment, and any facilities of the authority, except those facilities providing access to national parks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
