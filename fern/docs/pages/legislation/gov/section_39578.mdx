# GOV § 39578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 13. Weed and Rubbish Abatement > Article: 2. Alternative Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 39578

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 39577, after confirmation of the report, a copy shall be given to the county auditor, who shall add the amount of the assessment to the next regular tax bill levied against the parcel for municipal purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 643, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2007.)
