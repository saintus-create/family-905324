# GOV § 38782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 6.5. Reports of Residential Building Records

**Citation:** GOV § 38782

---

## Text

The ordinance may require payment of a reasonable fee by the owner, or the owner’s authorized agent, for the issuance of the reports.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 30. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
