# GOV § 59593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 16. Acquisition and Cancellation of Bonds of Nonconsenting Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 59593

---

## Text

If the legislative body makes all or part of such advancement it may reimburse the local agency of which it is the legislative body, entirely or partially by taking all or part of such new bonds at par.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
