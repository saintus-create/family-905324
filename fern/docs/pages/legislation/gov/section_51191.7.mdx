# GOV § 51191.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.9. Solar-Use Easement > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51191.7

---

## Text

The parcel or parcels subject to a solar-use easement shall be assessed pursuant to Section 402.1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code during the term of the easement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 596, Sec. 8. (SB 618) Effective January 1, 2012.)
