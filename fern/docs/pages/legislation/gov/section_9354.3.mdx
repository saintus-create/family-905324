# GOV § 9354.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 9354.3

---

## Text

As soon as practicable after the close of each fiscal year, the board shall file with the Governor a report of its work for such fiscal year. Not later than March 15th next following the close of the fiscal year, the board shall transmit a copy of such report to each house of the Legislature and may transmit a copy to every member and beneficiary of the system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1213.)
