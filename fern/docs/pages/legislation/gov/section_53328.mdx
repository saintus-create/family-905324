# GOV § 53328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 2. Proceedings to Create a Community Facilities District

**Citation:** GOV § 53328

---

## Text

After the canvass of returns of any election pursuant to Section 53326, the legislative body may, pursuant to Section 53340, levy any special tax as specified in the resolution of formation adopted pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 53325.1 within the territory of the district if two-thirds of the votes cast upon the question of levying the tax are in favor of levying that tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 670, Sec. 76. Effective January 1, 2008.)
