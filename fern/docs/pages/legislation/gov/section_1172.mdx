# GOV § 1172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6.5. Withholding Tax Deductions

**Citation:** GOV § 1172

---

## Text

They may pay the withheld sums at the times and in the manner provided by the law and make the records, reports, and returns required by the law or the regulations promulgated under it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 83.)
