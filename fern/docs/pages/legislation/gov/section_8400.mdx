# GOV § 8400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.6. Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement > Article: 1. Establishment and Functions of the Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement

**Citation:** GOV § 8400

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, except as otherwise provided:

(a) “Director” means the Director of the Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement.

(b) “Office” means the Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement established in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 30. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024.)
