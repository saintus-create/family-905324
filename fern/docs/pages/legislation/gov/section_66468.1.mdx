# GOV § 66468.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Procedure > Article: 6. Filing Maps With County Recorder

**Citation:** GOV § 66468.1

---

## Text

Whenever separate documents are to be recorded concurrently with the final or parcel map pursuant to Section 66435.1 or 66445, the county recorder shall complete the cross-reference to such concurrently recorded separate documents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 87, Sec. 19. Effective March 1, 1982.)
