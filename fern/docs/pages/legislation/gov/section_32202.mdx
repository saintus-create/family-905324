# GOV § 32202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 32202

---

## Text

This chapter shall be given a liberal interpretation with a view of carrying out its purpose and shall not be construed as a local measure or one intended as a benefit to particular persons or places.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
