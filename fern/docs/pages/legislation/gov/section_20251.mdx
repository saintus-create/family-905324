# GOV § 20251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 8. Subrogation

## Text

As used in this article, “state fund” means the State Compensation Insurance Fund.
