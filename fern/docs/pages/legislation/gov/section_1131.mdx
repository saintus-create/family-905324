# GOV § 1131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Withholding Salary on Contest of Title

**Citation:** GOV § 1131

---

## Text

As soon as the proceedings are instituted, the clerk of the court in which they are pending shall certify the facts to the officers whose duty it would otherwise be to draw the warrant or pay the salary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
