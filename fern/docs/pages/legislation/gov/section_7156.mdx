# GOV § 7156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14. State Tax Liens > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 7156

## Text

“Holder in due course” has the same meaning as defined in Section 3302 of the Commercial Code.
