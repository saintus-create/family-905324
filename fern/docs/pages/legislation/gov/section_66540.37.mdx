# GOV § 66540.37

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 3. Facilities, Equipment, and Services

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.37

---

## Text

The authority may accept, through purchase of fee, conveyance of title, long-term lease, or other means deemed appropriate, the vessels, terminals, maintenance and support facilities, and other assets of public water transportation services providers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
