# GOV § 23374.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 4.5. Election to Select County Officers and Location of County Seat in the Approved County

**Citation:** GOV § 23374.18

---

## Text

All the officers elected at the election shall hold their offices until the times provided by general law for the election and qualification of such officers, and until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 370.)
