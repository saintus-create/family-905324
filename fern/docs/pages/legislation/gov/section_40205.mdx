# GOV § 40205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 40205

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the verified census, the clerk shall make a certified copy. Thereafter it is the official census of the city.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 806.)
