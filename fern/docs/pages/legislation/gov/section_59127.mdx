# GOV § 59127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 59127

---

## Text

When any notice or resolution is required to be published, publication shall be made in any newspaper published and circulated within the local agency in which the legislative body has jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
