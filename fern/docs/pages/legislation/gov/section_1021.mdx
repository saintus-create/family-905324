# GOV § 1021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Disqualifications for Office or Employment

**Citation:** GOV § 1021

---

## Text

A person is disqualified from holding any office upon conviction of designated crimes as specified in the Constitution and laws of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
