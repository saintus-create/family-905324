# GOV § 21090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 3. Second Tier Retirement—Contracting Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 21090

---

## Text

(a) The governing body of a contracting agency may establish a two-tiered retirement system developed by the board .

(b) It is the intent of the Legislature to make a two-tiered retirement system a matter for bargaining pursuant to provisions of law relating to employer-employee relations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 133. Effective January 1, 1997.)
