# GOV § 64731

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 4. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 64731

---

## Text

The board shall provide for annual financial reports. The board shall make copies of the annual financial reports available to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
