# GOV § 7489

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 20. Governmental Access to Financial Records > Article: 5. Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** GOV § 7489

---

## Text

Evidence obtained in violation of this chapter is inadmissible in any proceeding except a proceeding to enforce the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1320.)
