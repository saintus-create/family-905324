# GOV § 11890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. The Financial Information System for California (FISCal) > Article: 5. Department of FISCal

**Citation:** GOV § 11890

---

## Text

(a) There is in state government the Department of FISCal.

(b) The Department of FISCal shall be within the Government Operations Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 569, Sec. 27. (AB 156) Effective September 27, 2022.)
