# GOV § 12476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 5. Uniform State Pay Roll System

**Citation:** GOV § 12476

---

## Text

The Controller may audit the uniform state pay roll system, the State Pay Roll Revolving Fund, and related records of state agencies within the uniform state pay roll system, in such manner as the Controller may determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1146.)
