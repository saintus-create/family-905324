# GOV § 17554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Commission Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17554

---

## Text

With the agreement of all parties to the claim, the commission may waive the application of any procedural requirement imposed by this chapter or pursuant to Section 17553. The authority granted by this section includes the consolidation of claims and the shortening of time periods.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 890, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2005.)
