# GOV § 57111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Resolution for Order Subject to Election

**Citation:** GOV § 57111

---

## Text

In any reorganization proceeding where the component changes of organization would not individually require a confirmation election, no confirmation election shall be required to approve the reorganization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 57089 by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 245.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
