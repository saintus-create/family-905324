# GOV § 54783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.6. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54783

---

## Text

Interest earned on any bond issued by the county shall at all times be free from state personal income tax and corporate income or franchise tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 91, Sec. 54. Effective January 1, 1996.)
