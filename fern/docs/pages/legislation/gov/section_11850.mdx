# GOV § 11850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. The Financial Information System for California (FISCal) > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 11850

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Financial Information System for California (FISCal) Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 9. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
