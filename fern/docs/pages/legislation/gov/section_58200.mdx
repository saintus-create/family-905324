# GOV § 58200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 7. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 58200

---

## Text

An action to determine the legality of the existence of the district may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1552.)
