# GOV § 57179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Confirmation of Election Results

**Citation:** GOV § 57179

---

## Text

The commission shall execute a certificate of termination of proceedings if the votes cast in favor of the change of organization or reorganization do not constitute a majority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 114, Sec. 18. (AB 1532) Effective January 1, 2016.)
