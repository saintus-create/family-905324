# GOV § 53369.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.6. Infrastructure and Revitalization Financing Districts > Article: 2. Preparation and Adoption of Infrastructure Revitalization Financing District Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 53369.23

---

## Text

After the canvass of returns of any election pursuant to Section 53369.20, the legislative body may, by ordinance, adopt the infrastructure financing plan and create the district with full force and effect of law, if two-thirds of the votes upon the question of creating the district are in favor of creating the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 775, Sec. 1. (AB 229) Effective January 1, 2015.)
