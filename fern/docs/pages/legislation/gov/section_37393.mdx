# GOV § 37393

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 2. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 37393

---

## Text

A lease of an interest in real property of a city shall be recorded in the office of the recorder of the county in which the property is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
