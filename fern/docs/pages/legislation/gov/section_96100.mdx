# GOV § 96100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 15. OFFICE OF CHILD DEVELOPMENT AND EDUCATION > Chapter: 3. Academic Volunteers and Mentors > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 96100

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the “California Academic Volunteer and Mentor Service Act of 1992.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 901, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
