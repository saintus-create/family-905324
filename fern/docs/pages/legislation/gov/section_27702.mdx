# GOV § 27702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 13. Public Defender

**Citation:** GOV § 27702

---

## Text

At the time of establishing the office the board of supervisors shall determine whether the public defender is to be appointed or elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
