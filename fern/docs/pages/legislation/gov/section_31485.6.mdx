# GOV § 31485.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31485.6

---

## Text

“Treasurer” as used in Sections 31595.9, 31625, 31625.1, 31629, and 31706 means the county treasurer or any other entity authorized by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 584, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
