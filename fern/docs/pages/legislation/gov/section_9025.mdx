# GOV § 9025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 2. Meeting and Organization of the Legislature

**Citation:** GOV § 9025

---

## Text

An entry of the oath taken by members of the Legislature shall be made on the journals of the proper house.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
