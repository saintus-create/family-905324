# GOV § 26910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Auditor > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 26910

---

## Text

The auditor may at any reasonable time and place examine the books and records of any special purpose assessing or taxing district located wholly in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1461.)
