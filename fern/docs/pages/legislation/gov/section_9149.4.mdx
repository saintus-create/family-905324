# GOV § 9149.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 9. The Historic State Capitol Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.4

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall serve without compensation, but shall receive reimbursement for travel and living expenses in connection with their official duties, at rates established by the Department of Human Resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 46. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
