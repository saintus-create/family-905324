# GOV § 53312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

Any provision in this chapter which conflicts with any other provision of law shall prevail over the other provision of law.
