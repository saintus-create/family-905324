# GOV § 25690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 5. Water System

**Citation:** GOV § 25690

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may acquire, develop, distribute, and sell water to the county and its inhabitants for domestic, irrigation, agricultural, and other beneficial uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1176.)
