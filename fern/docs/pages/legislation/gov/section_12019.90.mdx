# GOV § 12019.90

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 2.3. Tribal Nation Grant Fund Program

**Citation:** GOV § 12019.90

---

## Text

Actions taken under this article shall be consistent with the provisions of tribal-state gaming compacts and class III gaming secretarial procedures.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 129, Sec. 11. (AB 221) Effective January 1, 2026.)
