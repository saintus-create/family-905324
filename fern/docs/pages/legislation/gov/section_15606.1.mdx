# GOV § 15606.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15606.1

---

## Text

The duties, rules, regulations, and instructions as specified in Section 15606 shall include provisions for mobilehomes and floating homes which are subject to local property taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 366, Sec. 9. (SB 974) Effective January 1, 2017.)
