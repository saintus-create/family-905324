# GOV § 12164

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12164

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall record all conveyances made to the state, except (1) evidences of title acquired for state highway purposes and retained by the Department of Transportation, and (2) conveyances of tax-sold property.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 41.)
