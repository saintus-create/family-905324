# GOV § 69600

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 3. Number of Judges

**Citation:** GOV § 69600

---

## Text

In the County of Santa Clara there are 77 judges of the superior court.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 17, Sec. 21. (AB 103) Effective June 27, 2017.)
