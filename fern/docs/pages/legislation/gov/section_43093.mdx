# GOV § 43093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 2. Alternative Methods

**Citation:** GOV § 43093

---

## Text

Annually on or before the third Monday in August, the county auditor shall transmit to the legislative body of each city within the county a written statement showing the total value of all property within the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
