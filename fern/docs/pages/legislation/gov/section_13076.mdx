# GOV § 13076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13076

---

## Text

The director shall visit or cause to be visited from time to time every public institution maintained in whole or in part by State appropriations, to ascertain their condition, and their wants and requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 112.)
