# GOV § 14828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. California State Contracts Register

**Citation:** GOV § 14828

---

## Text

All governmental contracting entities shall provide to the Department of General Services which shall maintain, for review by those desiring the information, all of the following:

(a) Names of contractors to whom specific contracts have been awarded.

(b) Contract procedures and requirements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 823, Sec. 3.5.)
