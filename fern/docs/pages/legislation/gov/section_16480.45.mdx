# GOV § 16480.45

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 4.5. Treasury Pooled Money Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 16480.45

---

## Text

In addition to any other investment authorized by this article, the Treasurer may invest in Property Assessed Clean Energy (PACE) bonds, as defined in Section 26054 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 396, Sec. 8. (AB 1513) Effective January 1, 2020.)
