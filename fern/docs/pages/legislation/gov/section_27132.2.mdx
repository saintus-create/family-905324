# GOV § 27132.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 6. County Treasury Oversight Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 27132.2

---

## Text

A member may not directly or indirectly raise money for a candidate for local treasurer or a member of the governing board of any local agency that has deposited funds in the county treasury while a member of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 156, Sec. 4. Effective July 12, 1996.)
