# GOV § 13989

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. State-Funded Research

**Citation:** GOV § 13989

---

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the California Taxpayer Access to Publicly Funded Research Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 789, Sec. 1. (AB 609) Effective January 1, 2015.)
