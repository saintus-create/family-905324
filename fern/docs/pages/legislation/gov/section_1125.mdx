# GOV § 1125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4.7. Incompatible Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 1125

---

## Text

“Local agency,” as used in this article, means a county, city, city and county, political subdivision, district, or municipal corporation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 633.)
