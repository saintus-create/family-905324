# GOV § 31203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31203

---

## Text

If a member of a county retirement system becomes a member of another county retirement system, his membership in the first system ceases.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
