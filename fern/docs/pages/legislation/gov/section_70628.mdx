# GOV § 70628

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.8. Superior Court Fees > Article: 1. Civil Fees Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 70628

---

## Text

For an exemplification of a record or other paper on file, the fee is fifty dollars ($50), in addition to the charges allowed for copying or comparing each page of the record or other paper.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 31, Sec. 7. (SB 75) Effective June 27, 2013.)
