# GOV § 14827.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. California State Contracts Register

**Citation:** GOV § 14827.1

---

## Text

No state agency shall award a contract unless notice thereof has first been published in the California State Contracts Register.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 823, Sec. 3.5.)
