# GOV § 92310

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 6. Construction and Lease of Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 92310

---

## Text

Subject to Section 92352, the existence of the commission may be terminated at any time by the Legislature. Upon dissolution of the commission, the title to all properties owned by it shall vest in and become the property of the State of California and shall not inure to the benefit of any private party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
