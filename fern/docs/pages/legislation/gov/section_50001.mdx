# GOV § 50001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 50001

---

## Text

“Local agency” as used in this division means county, city, or city and county, unless the context otherwise requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
