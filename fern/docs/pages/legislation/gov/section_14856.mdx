# GOV § 14856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14856

---

## Text

Appropriations from the General Fund shall not be expended for the printing or publishing of any book, pamphlet, report of activities, or compilation or synopsis of laws, rules or regulations, or other document concerning any of those activities of any state agency that is supported from special funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
