# GOV § 66499.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICIAL MAPS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE

**Citation:** GOV § 66499.50

---

## Text

This division applies to all counties and, whether incorporated or not, to all cities, towns and villages in this state.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 24.)
