# GOV § 29926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29926

---

## Text

The tax shall be levied and collected as a part of the state tax and paid into the State Treasury and passed to the special credit of the county as a bond tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
