# GOV § 51191.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.9. Solar-Use Easement > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51191.1

---

## Text

Any county or city may enter into an agreement with a landowner pursuant to Section 51255.1 to use lands determined to be eligible pursuant to Section 51191 in a solar-use easement in the manner provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 596, Sec. 8. (SB 618) Effective January 1, 2012.)
