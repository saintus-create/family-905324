# GOV § 16489

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 5. Interfund Purchases, Sales and Transfers of Bond Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 16489

---

## Text

Any procedure established under this article shall provide that the market value of the bonds shall be regarded, for the purposes thereof, as the equivalent of cash in that amount, in any purchase, sale or transfer of bonds in lieu of cash thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 208.)
