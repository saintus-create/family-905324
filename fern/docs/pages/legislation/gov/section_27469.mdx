# GOV § 27469

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27469

---

## Text

In any action or proceeding in which the sheriff is a party, the coroner shall discharge the duties of sheriff.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
