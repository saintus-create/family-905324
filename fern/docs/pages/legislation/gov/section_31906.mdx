# GOV § 31906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31906

---

## Text

“Pensioner” means a retired peace officer who is entitled, because of his former service as a member, to receive a pension pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
