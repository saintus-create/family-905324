# GOV § 53900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 10. Local Filing of Financial Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 53900

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means city and any district other than school districts required by the Advisory Committee and the Controller to furnish financial reports pursuant to Section 12463.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1170.)
