# GOV § 32001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 5. Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 32001

---

## Text

All transfers or payments to the fund and all withdrawals and other cash transactions shall be accounted upon the books of the auditor and treasurer in and out of this fund in the same manner as county transactions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
