# GOV § 12096.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 12096.1

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter:

(a) “Office” means the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development.

(b) “Director” means the Director of the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 475, Sec. 2. (AB 29) Effective January 1, 2012.)
