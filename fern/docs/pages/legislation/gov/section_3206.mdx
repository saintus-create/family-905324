# GOV § 3206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.5. Political Activities of Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 3206

---

## Text

No officer or employee of a local agency shall participate in political activities of any kind while in uniform.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1422.)
