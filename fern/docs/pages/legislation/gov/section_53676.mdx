# GOV § 53676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53676

---

## Text

The treasurer is not responsible for securities delivered to and receipted for by any bank, savings and loan association, credit union, federally insured industrial loan company, or trust company.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1004, Sec. 6. Operative January 1, 1989, by Sec. 8 of Ch. 1004.)
