# GOV § 16752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. State General Obligation Bonds > Article: 4. Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 16752

---

## Text

The Treasurer may from time to time, by electronic means or by public announcement at the place and at or before the time fixed for a competitive sale of bonds, continue the sale to the time and place the Treasurer may select.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 205, Sec. 7. (SB 826) Effective January 1, 2010.)
