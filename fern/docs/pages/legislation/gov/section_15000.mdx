# GOV § 15000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15000

---

## Text

There is in the State Government a Department of Justice. The department is under the direction and control of the Attorney General.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 118.)
