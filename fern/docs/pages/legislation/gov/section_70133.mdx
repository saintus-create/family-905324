# GOV § 70133

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 12.7. Official Reporters in Counties with a Population of 140,000 to 147,000

**Citation:** GOV § 70133

---

## Text

Nothing in any county ordinance or in any state law disqualifying employees at any age from further employment, shall affect any reporter employed on the effective date of this article, or for a period of 10 years thereafter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 100.)
