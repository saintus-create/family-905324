# GOV § 12168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12168

---

## Text

On demand, the Secretary of State shall furnish, to a person paying the fees therefor, a certified copy of all or any part of a law, record, or other instrument filed, deposited, or recorded in the Secretary’s office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 44. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
