# GOV § 22943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 11. Prefunding Plan for Health Care Coverage for Annuitants

**Citation:** GOV § 22943

---

## Text

An employer authorized by the board may elect to participate in the prefunding plan established by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 22942 by Stats. 2007, Ch. 318, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
