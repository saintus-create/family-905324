# GOV § 59597

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 16. Acquisition and Cancellation of Bonds of Nonconsenting Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 59597

---

## Text

If the proposals or contract with the bondholders so provides, the legislative body may sell all of the new bonds for cash and pay the bondholders in cash at a price to be fixed in the proposals or contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
