# GOV § 1191

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Deputies and Subordinates

**Citation:** GOV § 1191

---

## Text

When not otherwise provided for, the appointment of deputies and other subordinate officers shall be made in writing and filed in the office of the appointing power or the office of its clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
