# GOV § 26644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 2. Prisoners’ Property

**Citation:** GOV § 26644

---

## Text

Any money and valuables of a prisoner and the accounts are subject to audit by the county auditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
