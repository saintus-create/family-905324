# GOV § 38402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38402

---

## Text

As used in this article, “interested person” means any person owning an interest in the park or the land embraced within it as reversioner, remainderman, abutting property owner, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
