# GOV § 8680.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8680.2

---

## Text

“Local agency” means any city, city and county, county, county office of education, community college district, school district, or special district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 33, Sec. 1. Effective December 1, 1990.)
