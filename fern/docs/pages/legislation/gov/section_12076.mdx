# GOV § 12076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 7. Commission on the Governorship

**Citation:** GOV § 12076

---

## Text

The commission shall have the power of a head of a department to make investigations to determine whether or not a petition should be filed pursuant to this article. Every state agency shall, on request of the commission, make available to the commission the facilities and personnel of the agency to enable the commission to perform its functions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 162.)
