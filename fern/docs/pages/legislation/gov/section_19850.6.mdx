# GOV § 19850.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of Salaries > Article: 5. Uniform and Equipment Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 19850.6

---

## Text

The state department in which an employee is employed may pay the cost of replacing personal tools or other equipment required in the employee’s work when stolen from the jobsite without fault of the employee. The department shall adopt rules setting forth the criteria under which payment may be made, including the method of payment, determination of cost, and conditions which constitute absence of fault by the employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
