# GOV § 15563.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Deputy Secretary for Climate

**Citation:** GOV § 15563.2

---

## Text

Upon appropriation by the Legislature, the position of Deputy Secretary for Climate is hereby established within the Labor and Workforce Development Agency for the purpose of assisting in the oversight of California’s workforce transition to a sustainable and equitable carbon neutral economy. The deputy secretary shall be appointed by the Governor, subject to confirmation by the Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 348, Sec. 2. (AB 2204) Effective January 1, 2023. Conditionally operative pursuant to Sec. 15563.6. Repealed as of January 1, 2046, pursuant to Sec. 15563.6.)
