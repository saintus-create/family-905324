# GOV § 58101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58101

---

## Text

At the hearing any owner of land in the proposed district may present to the supervising authority a written request for exclusion of all or part of such land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
