# GOV § 77012

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 13. State Funding of Trial Courts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 77012

## Text

As used in this chapter, “county” includes a city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 945, Sec. 10. Effective September 16, 1988. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 77400.)
