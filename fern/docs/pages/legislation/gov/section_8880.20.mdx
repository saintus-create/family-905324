# GOV § 8880.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 2. California State Lottery Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.20

---

## Text

Meetings

Meetings of the Commission shall be open and public in accordance with the Bagley-Keene Open Meeting Act (Article 9 (commencing with Section 11120) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 3).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 917, Sec. 2. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
