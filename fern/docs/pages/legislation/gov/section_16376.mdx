# GOV § 16376

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 2. Special Deposit Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16376

---

## Text

All funds on deposit in trust in the State Treasury for the use and benefit of the California State Historical Association are hereby transferred to the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 557.)
