# GOV § 54703.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.4. Benefit Assessment Act of 1982 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 54703.3

---

## Text

The purpose of this chapter is to recodify and supplant previously enacted provisions of law which authorize the imposition of benefit assessments and to provide a uniform procedure for the enactment of benefit assessments for services by local jurisdictions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 54703 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 151.)
