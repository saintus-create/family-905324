# GOV § 965.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Payment of Claims and Judgments Against the State

**Citation:** GOV § 965.1

---

## Text

The Director of General Services may allow a claim filed pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 905.2 if the settlement amount of that claim does not exceed fifty thousand dollars ($50,000), or to reject any claim as so described.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 54. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
