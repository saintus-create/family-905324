# GOV § 66905.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 66905.7

---

## Text

“Governing body” means the governing board of the California Tahoe Conservancy.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 4. Effective September 17, 1984.)
