# GOV § 45317

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Retirement > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 45317

---

## Text

Any regularly established fire or police protection district may adopt a retirement system for its employees pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
