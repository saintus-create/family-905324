# GOV § 11335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 5. Sale or Exchange of Products

**Citation:** GOV § 11335

---

## Text

This article does not limit or prevent sales pursuant to any other law relating to the sale of personal property belonging to the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
