# GOV § 59672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 19. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 59672

---

## Text

In such validation action all findings, conclusions, and determinations of the legislative body which conducted the proceedings are conclusive in the absence of actual fraud.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1553.)
