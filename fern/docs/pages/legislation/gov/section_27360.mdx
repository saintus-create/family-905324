# GOV § 27360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27360

---

## Text

For services performed by the recorder’s office, the county recorder shall charge and collect the fees fixed in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 269, Sec. 15. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2016.)
