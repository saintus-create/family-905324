# GOV § 12945.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Discrimination Prohibited > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices, Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 12945.1

## Text

Sections 12945.2 and 19702.3 shall be known, and may be cited, as the Moore-Brown-Roberti Family Rights Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 580, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
