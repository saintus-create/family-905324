# GOV § 53852.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 7.6. Temporary Borrowing

**Citation:** GOV § 53852.5

---

## Text

Proceeds of sales or funds set aside for the repayment of any notes issued pursuant to this article shall not be invested for a term that exceeds the term of the notes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 784, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1996.)
