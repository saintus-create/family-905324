# GOV § 57008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 57008

---

## Text

For any proposal initiated by the commission pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 56375, the commission shall hold a public protest hearing in the affected territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 220. Effective January 1, 2001.)
