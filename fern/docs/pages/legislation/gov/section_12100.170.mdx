# GOV § 12100.170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 16. Building and Reinforcing Inclusive, Diverse, Gender-Supportive Equity Project

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.170

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Building and Reinforcing Inclusive, Diverse, Gender-Supportive Equity Project (BRIDGE Project).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 199, Sec. 3. (SB 447) Effective September 13, 2023.)
