# GOV § 53603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 1. Investment of Surplus

**Citation:** GOV § 53603

---

## Text

The legislative body may make the investment by direct purchase of any issue of eligible securities at their original sale or after they have been issued.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 537.)
