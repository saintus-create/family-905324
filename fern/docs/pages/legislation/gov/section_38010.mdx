# GOV § 38010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 7. Park and Playground Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 38010

---

## Text

When the public interest or convenience requires, the legislative body may acquire by condemnation land situated in the city for improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 426.)
