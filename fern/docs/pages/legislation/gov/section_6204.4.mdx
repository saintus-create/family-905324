# GOV § 6204.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3.01. Public Records Protection and Recovery

**Citation:** GOV § 6204.4

---

## Text

In an action brought pursuant to this chapter, the court may award reasonable attorney’s fees and costs to the prevailing party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 519, Sec. 1. (AB 1245) Effective January 1, 2010.)
