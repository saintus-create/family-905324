# GOV § 55642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 3. Rescue and Resuscitator Services

**Citation:** GOV § 55642

---

## Text

The contract between the county and the state may provide for the duties, directions, and salaries of personnel performing rescue and resuscitator services, including volunteer firefighters, upon the terms agreed upon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 658.)
