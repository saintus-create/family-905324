# GOV § 16505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16505

---

## Text

Deposits in any bank shall not exceed the total of its net worth.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 509, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
