# GOV § 37105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37105

---

## Text

Subpenas shall be signed by the mayor and attested by the city clerk. They may be served as subpenas are served in civil actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
