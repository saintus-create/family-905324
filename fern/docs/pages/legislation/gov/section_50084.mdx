# GOV § 50084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 50084

---

## Text

Each local agency’s hiring practices and promotional practices shall conform to the Federal Civil Rights Act of 1964.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 915.)
