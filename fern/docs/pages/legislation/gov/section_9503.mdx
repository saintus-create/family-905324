# GOV § 9503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Enactment of Statutes and Adoption of Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9503

---

## Text

The clerk shall deliver the bills and documents without delay, in the order of their receipt, to the State Printer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
