# GOV § 23683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Seats > Article: 2. Removal of County Seats

**Citation:** GOV § 23683

---

## Text

The election shall be held and conducted, and the returns made in all respects as prescribed by law for elections of county officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
