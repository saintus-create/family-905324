# GOV § 31955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** GOV § 31955

---

## Text

The board shall organize by choosing one of its number as chairman. Thereafter the officers shall be chosen biennially by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
