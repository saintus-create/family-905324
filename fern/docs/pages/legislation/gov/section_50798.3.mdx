# GOV § 50798.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 8. Definition and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 50798.3

---

## Text

“Board” as used in this chapter means the board of supervisors in the case of a county or city and county, and city council or board of trustees in the case of a city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
