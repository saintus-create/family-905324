# GOV § 9003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 1. Numbering and Election of Legislators

**Citation:** GOV § 9003

---

## Text

At the general election in every even-numbered year a Member of the Assembly shall be elected in each of the Assembly districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
