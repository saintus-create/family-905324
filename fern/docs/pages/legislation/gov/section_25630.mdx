# GOV § 25630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25630

---

## Text

Whenever the members of the county planning commission have been designated as the county board of forestry, it shall not employ or appoint a county forester, but the employment and appointment may be made by the board of supervisors. In such case the bond of the forester shall be furnished to the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
