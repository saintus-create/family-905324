# GOV § 38635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 2. Police Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 38635

---

## Text

Leaves of absence shall be so arranged as not to interfere with the police protection of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
