# GOV § 8695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.6. Economic Disaster Act of 1984

**Citation:** GOV § 8695

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Farr Economic Disaster Act of 1984.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 18, Sec. 1.5. Effective March 26, 1992.)
