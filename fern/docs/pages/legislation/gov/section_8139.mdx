# GOV § 8139

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.6. California-Nevada Interstate Compact Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8139

---

## Text

Members and employees of the commission may travel outside of the state and may hold hearings and conduct investigations within and outside the state, whenever necessary to carry out the purposes of this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1365.)
