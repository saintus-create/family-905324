# GOV § 14978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Purchase of Prescription Drugs for Government Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 14978

---

## Text

The department, in consultation with the agencies listed in subdivision (a) of Section 14977.5, may investigate and implement other options and strategies to achieve the greatest savings on prescription drugs with prescription drug manufacturers and wholesalers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 483, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
