# GOV § 11342.535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 11342.535

---

## Text

“Cost impact” means the amount of reasonable range of direct costs, or a description of the type and extent of direct costs, that a representative private person or business necessarily incurs in reasonable compliance with the proposed action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1059, Sec. 6.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
