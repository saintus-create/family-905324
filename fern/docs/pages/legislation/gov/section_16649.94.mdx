# GOV § 16649.94

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Prohibited Business Arrangements

**Citation:** GOV § 16649.94

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any Internal Revenue Code Section 457, 401(k), or 403(b) defined contribution plan administered by the Department of Human Resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 53. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
