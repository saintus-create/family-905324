# GOV § 8899.71

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 16. California Catastrophe Response Council

**Citation:** GOV § 8899.71

---

## Text

For purposes of conducting the business of the council, a quorum shall be five members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 81, Sec. 1. (AB 111) Effective July 12, 2019.)
