# GOV § 13290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13290

## Text

The fiscal year shall commence on the first day of July.
