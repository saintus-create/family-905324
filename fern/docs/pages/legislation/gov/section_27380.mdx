# GOV § 27380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27380

---

## Text

The fee for filing, indexing, and keeping each paper not required by law to be recorded is three dollars ($3).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 59.)
