# GOV § 14999.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Commission for Economic Development

**Citation:** GOV § 14999.2

---

## Text

The commission may appoint task forces to study and report on specific issues which relate to the purposes of this chapter. The commission may contract for studies and other special services for purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 54, Sec. 3.)
