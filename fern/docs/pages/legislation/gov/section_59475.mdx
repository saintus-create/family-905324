# GOV § 59475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 12. Recording of Assessment

**Citation:** GOV § 59475

---

## Text

The lien has priority over all special assessment liens created against the same property subsequent to the date of recordation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
