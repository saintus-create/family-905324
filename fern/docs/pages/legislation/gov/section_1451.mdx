# GOV § 1451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 1451

---

## Text

The approval of every official bond shall be indorsed thereon and signed by the officer approving the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
