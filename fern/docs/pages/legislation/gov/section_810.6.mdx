# GOV § 810.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL

**Citation:** GOV § 810.6

## Text

“Enactment” means a constitutional provision, statute, charter provision, ordinance or regulation.
