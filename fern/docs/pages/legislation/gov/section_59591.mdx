# GOV § 59591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 16. Acquisition and Cancellation of Bonds of Nonconsenting Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 59591

---

## Text

Any local agency authorized by this chapter to appropriate money to aid in refunding the original bonds of the district may make an advancement, contribution, or an additional contribution to provide money to pay or retire bonds of the nonconsenting holders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
