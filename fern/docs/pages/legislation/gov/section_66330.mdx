# GOV § 66330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 13. Accessory Dwelling Units > Article: 2. Accessory Dwelling Unit Approvals

**Citation:** GOV § 66330

---

## Text

A local agency may count an accessory dwelling unit for purposes of identifying adequate sites for housing, as specified in subdivision (a) of Section 65583.1, subject to authorization by the department and compliance with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 7, Sec. 20. (SB 477) Effective March 25, 2024.)
