# GOV § 31698.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.9. Vision Care

**Citation:** GOV § 31698.4

---

## Text

The sponsor of the vision care program may contract with a third-party administrator to provide vision care to the retired member, his or her survivors, and his or her eligible dependents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 331, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
