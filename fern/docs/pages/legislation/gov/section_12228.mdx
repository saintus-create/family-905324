# GOV § 12228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12228

---

## Text

The Chief of Archives shall give an appropriate receipt for all material received by the Chief of Archives as a part of the archives.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 55. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
