# GOV § 65654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 11. Supportive Housing

**Citation:** GOV § 65654

---

## Text

If the supportive housing development is located within one-half mile of a public transit stop, the local government shall not impose any minimum parking requirements for the units occupied by supportive housing residents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 753, Sec. 3. (AB 2162) Effective January 1, 2019.)
