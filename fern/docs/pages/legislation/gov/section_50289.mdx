# GOV § 50289

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 12. Historical Property Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 50289

---

## Text

In the event that property restricted by a contract with a county under this article is annexed to a city, the city shall succeed to all rights, duties, and powers of the county under such contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1442.)
