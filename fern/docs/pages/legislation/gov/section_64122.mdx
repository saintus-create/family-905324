# GOV § 64122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CALIFORNIA TRANSPORTATION FINANCING AUTHORITY > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE

**Citation:** GOV § 64122

---

## Text

Any bonds issued under this division, their transfer, and the income therefrom shall at all times be free from taxation of every kind by the state and by all political subdivisions in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 474, Sec. 1. (AB 798) Effective January 1, 2010.)
