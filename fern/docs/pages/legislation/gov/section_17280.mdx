# GOV § 17280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 4. Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 17280

---

## Text

If at any time it is necessary to register warrants for the payment of principal and interest on State bonds, warrants so registered have a prior lien in the order of their issuance on any money thereafter received into the General Fund, and shall be paid before any other warrants regardless of the prior issuance of the latter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
