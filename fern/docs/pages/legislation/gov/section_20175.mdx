# GOV § 20175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 5. The Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 20175

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, funds in the reserve against deficiencies shall not be used to pay any employers’ contribution required by this chapter to be paid by the state, any school employer, or any contracting agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
