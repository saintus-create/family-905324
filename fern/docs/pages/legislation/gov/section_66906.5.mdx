# GOV § 66906.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 3. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 66906.5

---

## Text

The conservancy shall establish and maintain an office within the region. The conservancy may rent or own real and personal property and equipment pursuant to applicable statutes and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 66906.4 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 13. Effective September 17, 1984.)
