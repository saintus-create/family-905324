# GOV § 14998.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Film Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 14998.11

---

## Text

The commission shall perform the following functions and activities:

(a) Provide staff to the board.

(b) Implement the Cooperative Motion Picture Marketing Plan.

(c) Provide services necessary to increase the amount of filming within California.

(d) Create a navigational link on its Internet Web site labeled “Independent Films.” The navigational link shall contain information explaining the qualified motion picture tax credits available to independent films pursuant to Sections 17053.95 and 23695 of the Revenue and Taxation Code. The navigational link shall also contain information relating to the application process and shall highlight that the commission is required to allocate 5 percent of the aggregate amount of credits to independent films.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 302, Sec. 12. (AB 3256) Effective January 1, 2019.)
