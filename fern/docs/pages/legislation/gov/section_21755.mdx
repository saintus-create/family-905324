# GOV § 21755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Part: 3.4. INTERNAL REVENUE CODE COMPLIANCE AND REPLACEMENT BENEFIT PLAN > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 19. California Employers’ Pension Prefunding Trust Program

**Citation:** GOV § 21755

---

## Text

Internal Revenue Service Procedure 92-42 shall apply to all changes in benefit structure adopted by any employer regardless of whether the change was adopted before August 3, 1992, or on or after August 3, 1992. Internal Revenue Service Notice 89-45 shall not be applied to any changes in benefit structure adopted by any employer.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative as prescribed in Section 21763.)
