# GOV § 31852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 15.5. Survivors’ Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31852

---

## Text

The amounts deducted pursuant to Section 31851 shall not be considered to be a portion of a member’s accumulated contributions and shall not be refunded to a member upon termination of service, or for any other reason.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1591.)
