# GOV § 23724

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Charters > Article: 2. Charter Amendment and Repeal

**Citation:** GOV § 23724

---

## Text

Two copies of the amendment or amendments shall be authenticated, certified, recorded, submitted, filed and published in the same manner as provided in Sections 23713 and 23714 for the charter, and with like force and effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 238.)
