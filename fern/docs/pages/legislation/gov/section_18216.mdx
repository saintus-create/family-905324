# GOV § 18216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Administrative Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 18216

---

## Text

Regulations concerning contracting out shall be subject to the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 935, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
