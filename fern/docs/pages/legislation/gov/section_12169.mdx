# GOV § 12169

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12169

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall file in the Secretary’s office descriptions of seals in use by the different state officers and furnish those officers with new seals whenever required.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 46. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
