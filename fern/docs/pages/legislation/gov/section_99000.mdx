# GOV § 99000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 17. CALIFORNIA FISCAL RECOVERY FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 99000

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the California Fiscal Recovery Financing Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 13, Sec. 2. Effective October 28, 2003.)
