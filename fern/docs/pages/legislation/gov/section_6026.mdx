# GOV § 6026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 2. Establishing Standing as Newspaper of General Circulation

**Citation:** GOV § 6026

---

## Text

An appeal may be taken to the supreme court from any final decision or judgment, or from any final order vacating, modifying or setting aside a decision or judgment previously entered.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
