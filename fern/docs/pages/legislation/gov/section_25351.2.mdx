# GOV § 25351.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25351.2

---

## Text

In the operation of a county art institute, the board may charge fees necessary to defray the cost of instruction and may issue certificates evidencing the completion of courses of instruction to the satisfaction of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 586.)
