# GOV § 27338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 4. Recording

**Citation:** GOV § 27338

---

## Text

A deed, instrument, or other document related to a conveyance that is subject to Section 8560 of the Public Resources Code shall not be recorded without a certificate from the State Lands Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 51, Sec. 9. (SB 854) Effective June 27, 2018.)
