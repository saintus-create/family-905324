# GOV § 66641.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66641.8

---

## Text

No person shall be subject to both civil liability imposed under Section 66641.6 and civil liability imposed by the superior court under Section 66641.5 for the same act or failure to act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 519, Sec. 10.)
