# GOV § 40538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40538

---

## Text

The bond of the treasurer of the local agency shall cover the treasurer’s duties as ex officio treasurer of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 38. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
