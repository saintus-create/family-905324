# GOV § 7922.630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Agency Regulations, Guidelines, Systems, and Similar Matters > Article: 1. Agency Regulations and Guidelines

**Citation:** GOV § 7922.630

---

## Text

Every agency may adopt regulations in accordance with this article stating the procedures to be followed when making its records available.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
