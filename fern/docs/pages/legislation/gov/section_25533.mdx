# GOV § 25533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 8. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25533

---

## Text

The final acceptance by the board of supervisors may be made either at the same session or at any adjourned session of the same meeting held within the 10 days next following.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1800.)
