# GOV § 19996.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations from Service > Article: 1.6. Reduced Worktime Act

**Citation:** GOV § 19996.28

---

## Text

The Trustees of the California State University shall adopt the appropriate rules and guidelines relating to the implementation of the reduced worktime program for the employees of the California State University system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 128.)
