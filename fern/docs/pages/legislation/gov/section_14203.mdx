# GOV § 14203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. State Employee Telecommuting Program

**Citation:** GOV § 14203

---

## Text

Each state agency shall evaluate its telecommuting program. The Department of General Services shall establish criteria for evaluating the state’s telecommuting program and recommend modifications, if necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1389, Sec. 1.)
