# GOV § 65060.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65060.6

---

## Text

“Region,” as used in this chapter, means the area included within a district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
