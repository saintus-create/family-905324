# GOV § 27723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Hearing Officer

**Citation:** GOV § 27723

---

## Text

The local body may, but need not be present during the proceeding.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 480.)
