# GOV § 9744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 3. Legislative Manual

**Citation:** GOV § 9744

---

## Text

The remainder shall be sold at a price to be fixed by the Joint Rules Committee. The price shall be sufficient to cover the cost of printing and binding the manual.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 7.)
