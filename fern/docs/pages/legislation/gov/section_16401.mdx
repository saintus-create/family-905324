# GOV § 16401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 5. Revolving Funds and Advances

**Citation:** GOV § 16401

---

## Text

Any revolving fund drawn under the provisions of Section 16400 may only be used in accordance with law for payment of compensation earned, traveling expenses, traveling expense advances, or where immediate payment is otherwise necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 726, Sec. 18. Effective September 22, 1994.)
