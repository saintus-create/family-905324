# GOV § 7307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.7. Open-Space Land Dedication

**Citation:** GOV § 7307

---

## Text

Land accepted by the state pursuant to this chapter shall be maintained and preserved in perpetuity as open-space land, except as provided in Section 7308.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1144.)
