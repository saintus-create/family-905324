# GOV § 29061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 3. Recommended Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29061

---

## Text

The board shall designate either the administrative officer or auditor to review the budget requests and prepare a recommended budget. Any differences may be described in the written recommendations or comments, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 25. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
