# GOV § 34409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Special Charter Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34409

---

## Text

From the date of the entry, the city is organized with the powers of general law cities.
