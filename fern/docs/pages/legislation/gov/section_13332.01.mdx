# GOV § 13332.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2.5. Provisions Applicable to Appropriations of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13332.01

---

## Text

Any state agency which collects funds from the federal government shall include in the collections, amounts to offset federally allowed statewide indirect costs, as determined by the Department of Finance, except where prohibited by federal statutes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 323, Sec. 44. Effective July 1, 1983.)
