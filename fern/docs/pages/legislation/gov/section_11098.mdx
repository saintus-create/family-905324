# GOV § 11098

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 6.5. Distribution of State Publications

**Citation:** GOV § 11098

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, when funding for any legislatively mandated publication is discontinued in the Budget Act, the statutory mandate for that publication shall be automatically suspended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1632, Sec. 1. Effective October 1, 1982.)
