# GOV § 41205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. City Assessor

**Citation:** GOV § 41205

---

## Text

The assessor and the assessor’s deputies may administer oaths and affirmations necessary in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 48. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
