# GOV § 9378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 8. Survivor’s Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 9378

---

## Text

A person who in any month is an eligible survivor of more than one member, shall receive only one allowance under this article, which shall be the largest of the monthly allowances to which he or she would otherwise be entitled.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 93.5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
