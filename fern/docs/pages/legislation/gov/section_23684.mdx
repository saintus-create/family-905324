# GOV § 23684

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Seats > Article: 2. Removal of County Seats

**Citation:** GOV § 23684

---

## Text

Each elector shall vote for the place in the county which he prefers as the seat of justice, plainly designating it in his ballot.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
