# GOV § 20930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 2. Prior Service Credit

**Citation:** GOV § 20930

---

## Text

Credit for prior service shall be granted to each member who was employed by the university at the time of becoming a member .

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 108. Effective January 1, 1997.)
