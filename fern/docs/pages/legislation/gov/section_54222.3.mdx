# GOV § 54222.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 8. Surplus Land

**Citation:** GOV § 54222.3

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to the disposal of exempt surplus land as defined in Section 54221 by an agency of the state or any local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 664, Sec. 4. (AB 1486) Effective January 1, 2020.)
