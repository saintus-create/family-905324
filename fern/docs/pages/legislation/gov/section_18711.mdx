# GOV § 18711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 18711

---

## Text

The board and the Department of Human Resources may join associations of personnel agencies having as their purpose the interchanging or supplying of information relating to the technique of personnel administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 60. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
