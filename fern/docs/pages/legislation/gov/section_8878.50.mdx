# GOV § 8878.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.47. Earthquake Safety and Public Buildings Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1990 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8878.50

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Earthquake Safety and Public Buildings Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 122 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
