# GOV § 8880.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 2. California State Lottery Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.21

---

## Text

Quorum; Voting

A quorum shall consist of three members of the Commission. All decisions of the Commission shall be made by a majority vote of the Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1517, Sec. 2. Effective October 2, 1985. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
