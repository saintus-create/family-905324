# GOV § 31598

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 31598

---

## Text

The annual statement shall be prepared in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles on the basis of pronouncements of the Government Accounting Standards Board or its successor organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 680, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1997.)
