# GOV § 12513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 2. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12513

---

## Text

After judgment in any of the causes referred to in Section 12512, the Attorney General shall direct the issuing of such process as may be necessary to carry the judgment into execution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
