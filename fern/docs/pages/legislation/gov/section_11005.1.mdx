# GOV § 11005.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11005.1

---

## Text

The Director of Finance may accept on behalf of the State any gift of real or personal property whenever he deems such gift and the terms and conditions thereof to be in the best interest of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1023.)
