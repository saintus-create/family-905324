# GOV § 21683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 16. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** GOV § 21683

---

## Text

This system may require an investment manager or recordkeeper under contract with, or appointed by, this system be subject to the duties set forth in Section 21679.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 639, Sec. 19. (SB 1139) Effective January 1, 2011.)
