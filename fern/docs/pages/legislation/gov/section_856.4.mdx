# GOV § 856.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Medical, Hospital and Public Health Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 856.4

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 815.6, neither a public entity nor a public employee acting in the scope of his employment is liable for an injury resulting from the failure to admit a person to a public medical facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
