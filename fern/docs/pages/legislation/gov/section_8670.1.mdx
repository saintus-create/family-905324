# GOV § 8670.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.1

---

## Text

This chapter, Article 3.5 (commencing with Section 8574.1) of Chapter 7 of the Government Code, and Division 7.8 (commencing with Section 8750) of the Public Resources Code shall be known, and may be cited as, the Lempert-Keene-Seastrand Oil Spill Prevention and Response Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 17. Effective September 24, 1990.)
