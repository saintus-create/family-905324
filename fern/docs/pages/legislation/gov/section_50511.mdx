# GOV § 50511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50511

---

## Text

The notice shall be published once a week for two successive weeks in a daily, semiweekly, or weekly newspaper published or circulated in the local agency or assessment district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
