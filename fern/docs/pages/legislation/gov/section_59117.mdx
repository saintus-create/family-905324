# GOV § 59117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 59117

---

## Text

“Proceedings” means proceedings pursuant to this chapter to refund the indebtedness of a district and to levy a reassessment for such indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
