# GOV § 8590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 5.7. Firefighting Thermal Imaging Equipment Act of 2001

**Citation:** GOV § 8590

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Firefighting Thermal Imaging Equipment Act of 2001.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 837, Sec. 2. Effective October 13, 2001.)
