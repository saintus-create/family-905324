# GOV § 93100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.3. SISKIYOU RAILROAD AUTHORITY

**Citation:** GOV § 93100

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Siskiyou Railroad Authority Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
