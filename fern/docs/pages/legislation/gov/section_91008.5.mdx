# GOV § 91008.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 11. Enforcement

**Citation:** GOV § 91008.5

---

## Text

No civil action may be filed under Section 91004, 91005, or 91005.5 with regard to any person for any violations of this title after the commission has issued an order pursuant to Section 83116 against that person for the same violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 670, Sec. 4.)
