# GOV § 53388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 5. Senior Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53388

---

## Text

The legislative body shall submit the proposal to issue the bonds to the voters who reside within the district. Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, the election shall be conducted in the same manner as other city or county elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
