# GOV § 53343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 4. Procedures for Levying

**Citation:** GOV § 53343

---

## Text

Any special taxes collected pursuant to this chapter may only be used for facilities and services authorized by this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 670, Sec. 95. Effective January 1, 2008.)
