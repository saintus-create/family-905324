# GOV § 12075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 7. Commission on the Governorship

**Citation:** GOV § 12075

---

## Text

The commission may file a petition pursuant to Sections 12071, 12072, or 12073 only if a majority of the members of the commission vote to take such action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 162.)
