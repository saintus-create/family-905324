# GOV § 29324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 2. Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29324

---

## Text

Upon the filing of the required bond the auditor shall draw his warrant in favor of the officer for whose benefit the revolving fund is created, and the treasurer shall pay the warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
