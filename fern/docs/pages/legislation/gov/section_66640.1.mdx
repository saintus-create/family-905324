# GOV § 66640.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66640.1

---

## Text

The commission shall create and implement all of the following by the end of the 2020–21 fiscal year:

(a) A procedure to ensure that managers perform a documented review of staff decisions in enforcement cases.

(b) Timelines for resolving enforcement cases.

(c) A penalty matrix for assessing fines and civil penalties.

(d) A method for assessing civil penalties in cases involving multiple violations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 220, Sec. 1. (AB 2809) Effective January 1, 2021.)
