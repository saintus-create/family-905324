# GOV § 1221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 1221

---

## Text

Where not otherwise provided, the seals of office of the various executive officers are those in use by such officers on January 1, 1873.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
