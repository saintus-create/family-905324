# GOV § 54205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 7. Purchases of Supplies and Equipment by Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 54205

---

## Text

Any local agency may request the Department of General Services to make purchases of materials, equipment, or supplies on its behalf in accordance with Section 10298 of the Public Contract Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 176, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2002.)
