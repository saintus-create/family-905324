# GOV § 20534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Contract Members of System > Article: 3. Contracting Agency Financial Obligations

**Citation:** GOV § 20534

---

## Text

The board may make arrangements with any contracting agency for the payment of the prior service liability on terms that its financial condition will permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
