# GOV § 18551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18551

---

## Text

A “part-time” position or appointment is a position or appointment in which the employee is to work a specific fraction of the full-time work schedule.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 387, Sec. 2.)
