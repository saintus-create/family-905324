# GOV § 26502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. District Attorney > Article: 1. Duties as Public Prosecutor

**Citation:** GOV § 26502

---

## Text

The district attorney shall draw all indictments and informations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
