# GOV § 51297.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 7. Farmland Security Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51297.1

---

## Text

All of the provisions of Article 6 (commencing with Section 51290) shall apply to farmland security zones created pursuant to this article except as specifically provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 506, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2001.)
