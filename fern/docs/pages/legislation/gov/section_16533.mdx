# GOV § 16533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 2. Security for Deposits

**Citation:** GOV § 16533

---

## Text

If at any time the security deposited with the Treasurer is not deemed satisfactory by the Treasurer, he may require such additional security as is satisfactory to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1542.)
