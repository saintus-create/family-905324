# GOV § 421.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 421.7

---

## Text

“I Love You, California,” a song published in 1913 with lyrics by F.B. Silverwood and music by A.F. Frankenstein, is an official state song.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 421.5 (as added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1198) by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1360, Sec. 47.)
