# GOV § 5102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Protection of Bond Guarantors

**Citation:** GOV § 5102

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “guarantor” means the person or persons guaranteeing the payment of bonds, the repurchase thereof, or the payment or repurchase of any interest therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
