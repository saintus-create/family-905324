# GOV § 12773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Community Services Block Grant Program > Article: 8. American Indian Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 12773

---

## Text

American Indian entities funded by the department and operating under authority of this chapter in the prior program year shall have the same protections against defunding, as defined in subdivision (e) of Section 12736.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 46, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2008. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 12790.)
