# GOV § 75527

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11.5. Judges’ Retirement System II > Article: 2. Early Retirement and Normal Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 75527

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the benefits payable to any person shall be subject to the limitations set forth in the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 879, Sec. 11. Effective September 27, 1994. Operative November 9, 1994, by Sec. 16 of Ch. 879.)
