# GOV § 31898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 18. Adjustment to Fit Other Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31898

---

## Text

A retirement allowance or other benefit payable to a beneficiary pursuant to this chapter shall not be reduced pursuant to this article if such reduction would impair the constitutional rights of any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 511.)
