# GOV § 23002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 23002

---

## Text

The several existing counties of the State and such other counties as are hereafter organized are legal subdivisions of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
