# GOV § 27583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 2. Survey Markers

**Citation:** GOV § 27583

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall furnish all necessary pipes or stakes for monuments in the county on demand and without cost.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
