# GOV § 17274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 4. Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 17274

---

## Text

The notice of redemption shall read substantially as follows: “NOTICE TO HOLDERS OF STATE OF CALIFORNIA WARRANTSState Controller’s warrants number______to number______, inclusive, drawn against the General Fund in the state treasury will be payable on (redemption date) upon presentation to the State Treasurer.(Name of State Treasurer)State Treasurer.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 766, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
