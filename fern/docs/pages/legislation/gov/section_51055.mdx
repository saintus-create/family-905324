# GOV § 51055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Open-Space Easements

**Citation:** GOV § 51055

---

## Text

An instrument described in subdivision (a) of Section 51051 shall not be effective until it has been accepted by resolution of the governing body of the city or county and its acceptance endorsed thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 875, Sec. 5. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2013.)
