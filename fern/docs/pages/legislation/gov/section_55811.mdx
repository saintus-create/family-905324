# GOV § 55811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Counties Transaction and Use Tax

**Citation:** GOV § 55811

---

## Text

Delivery of any bonds issued pursuant to this chapter may be made at any place either inside or outside the state, and the purchase price may be received in cash or bank credits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 384, Sec. 1. Effective September 9, 1991.)
