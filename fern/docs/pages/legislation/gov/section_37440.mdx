# GOV § 37440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 3.5. Sale and Leases of Airports

**Citation:** GOV § 37440

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are alternative to Sections 37350 and 37351 of this code and any other authority granted by law to cities to dispose of city-owned property.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1552.)
