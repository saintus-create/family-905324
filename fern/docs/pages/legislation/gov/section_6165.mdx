# GOV § 6165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2.6. State Payment Card Act

**Citation:** GOV § 6165

---

## Text

The Department of General Services and state agencies shall enter into interagency agreements to reimburse the Department of General Services for its costs in entering into contracts pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 926, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
