# GOV § 15562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 15562

---

## Text

Whenever a power is granted to the secretary, the power may be exercised by an officer or employee within the agency as designated in writing by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 859, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
