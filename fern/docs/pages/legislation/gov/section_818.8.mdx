# GOV § 818.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions Relating to Liability > Article: 2. Liability of Public Entities

**Citation:** GOV § 818.8

---

## Text

A public entity is not liable for an injury caused by misrepresentation by an employee of the public entity, whether or not such misrepresentation be negligent or intentional.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
