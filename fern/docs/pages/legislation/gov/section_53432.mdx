# GOV § 53432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Mutilated or Defaced Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53432

---

## Text

The duplicate shall have the same time to run, bear like interest, and have the same number, as the mutilated or defaced bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
