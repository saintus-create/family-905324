# GOV § 29634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Charges > Article: 2. Victims of Crime

**Citation:** GOV § 29634

---

## Text

In establishing a program pursuant to this article the legislative body may provide for the procedures to determine whether or not a claimant is entitled to reimbursement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1627.)
