# GOV § 26665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 3. Process and Notices

**Citation:** GOV § 26665

---

## Text

All writs, notices, or other process issued by superior courts in civil actions or proceedings may be served by any duly qualified and acting marshal or sheriff of any county in the state, subject to the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 171. Effective January 1, 2003.)
