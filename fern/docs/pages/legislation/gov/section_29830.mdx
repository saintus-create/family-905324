# GOV § 29830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 2.3. Alternative Payment and Registration Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 29830

---

## Text

In lieu of the method in Article 2 (commencing with Section 29820) for the registration of county warrants, the provisions of this article may be followed upon adoption of a resolution by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1191, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1992.)
