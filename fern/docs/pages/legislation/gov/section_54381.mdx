# GOV § 54381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 3. Authorization of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54381

## Text

By a majority of all members, the legislative body may adopt the resolution at a regular or special meeting and at the same meeting at which it is introduced.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
