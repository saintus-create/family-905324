# GOV § 16582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.3. The Accounts Receivable Management Act

**Citation:** GOV § 16582

---

## Text

Within the uniform state payroll system, the Controller may, for each participant, disregard errors of twenty-five dollars ($25) or less in individual accounts receivable, if he or she has determined that time and expense will be saved in doing so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 520, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2005.)
