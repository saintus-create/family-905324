# GOV § 22960.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.29

---

## Text

“State peace officers and firefighters” means those persons included in the definition of “state peace officer/firefighter member” pursuant to Article 3 (commencing with Section 20390) of Chapter 4 of Part 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
