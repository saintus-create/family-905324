# GOV § 34851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 1. City Manager

**Citation:** GOV § 34851

---

## Text

An ordinance establishing a city manager form of government may be:

(a) Enacted by the legislative body; or

(b) Submitted to the electors by the legislative body at any municipal or special election; or

(c) Submitted by the people as an initiative measure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
