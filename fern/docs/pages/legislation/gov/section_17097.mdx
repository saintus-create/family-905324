# GOV § 17097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Lost or Destroyed Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17097

---

## Text

Any loss incurred in connection with the issuance of a replacement warrant shall be charged against the account from which the payment was derived.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 47. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
