# GOV § 38412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38412

---

## Text

The notice shall be published for at least two weeks before the meeting in a newspaper published in the city, or if there is none, it shall be posted in three public places in the city for two weeks before the meeting.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
