# GOV § 23560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** GOV § 23560

---

## Text

The clerk shall also prepare and mail to the qualified electors of each affected county a sample ballot. The sample ballot shall be mailed with and at the same time as the ballot pamphlet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
