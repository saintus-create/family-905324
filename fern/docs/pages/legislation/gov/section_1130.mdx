# GOV § 1130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Withholding Salary on Contest of Title

**Citation:** GOV § 1130

---

## Text

When the title of the incumbent of any office in this State is contested by proceedings instituted in any court for that purpose, no warrant shall thereafter be drawn or paid for any part of his salary until such proceedings have been finally determined.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
