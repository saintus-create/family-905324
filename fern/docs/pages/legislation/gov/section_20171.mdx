# GOV § 20171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 5. The Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 20171

---

## Text

The board has the exclusive control of the administration and investment of the retirement fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
