# GOV § 9355.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 3. Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 9355.3

---

## Text

Any member may resign from this system at any time prior to retirement, by written resignation filed with the Board of Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 879.)
