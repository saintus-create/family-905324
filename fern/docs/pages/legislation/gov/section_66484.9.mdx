# GOV § 66484.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Requirements > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 66484.9

---

## Text

The authorizations granted by this article are additional to all other authority granted by law to local agencies relating to subdivisions and shall in no way be construed as a limitation on or diminution of that authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 228, Sec. 2. (AB 147) Effective January 1, 2012.)
