# GOV § 40574

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 6. Co-operation

**Citation:** GOV § 40574

---

## Text

The authority may enter into leases or contracts of sale of said properties or any part thereof for such periods of time as it deems will beneficially serve any requirements of the contract of purchase, not to exceed 99 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
