# GOV § 14552.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Federal Highway Grant Anticipation Notes > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 14552.6

---

## Text

A “note” is a federal highway grant anticipation note issued by the Treasurer under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 862, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
