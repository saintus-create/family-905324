# GOV § 34903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 3. Elective Mayor

**Citation:** GOV § 34903

---

## Text

The mayor is a member of the city council and has all of the powers and duties of a member of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1571.)
