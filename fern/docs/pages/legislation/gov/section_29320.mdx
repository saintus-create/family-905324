# GOV § 29320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 2. Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29320

---

## Text

As used in this article, “officer of the county” includes any elective or appointive officer of a county and any person in charge of any office, department, service, or institution of the county, or a division or branch thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 470, Sec. 33. (AB 1529) Effective January 1, 2013.)
