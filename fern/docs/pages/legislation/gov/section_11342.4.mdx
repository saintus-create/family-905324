# GOV § 11342.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11342.4

---

## Text

The office shall adopt, amend, or repeal regulations for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11344.6 by Stats. 1983, Ch. 797, Sec. 12.)
