# GOV § 59649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59649

---

## Text

Bond Plan A bonds shall also have semiannual interest coupons attached to them and payable as in the case of bonds issued pursuant to the Improvement Act of 1911.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
