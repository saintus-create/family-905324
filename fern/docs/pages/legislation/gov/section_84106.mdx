# GOV § 84106

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 1. Organization of Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 84106

---

## Text

(a) Whenever identification of a sponsored committee is required by this title, the identification shall include the full name of the committee as required in its committee registration.

(b) A sponsored committee shall use only one name in its committee registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 278, Sec. 18. (AB 808) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally operative pursuant to Sec. 78 of Stats. 2025, Ch. 278.)
