# GOV § 5601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Denominations

**Citation:** GOV § 5601

---

## Text

“Bonds” as used in this chapter means bonds, warrants, notes and other evidences of indebtedness issued by or on behalf of any public body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 736.)
