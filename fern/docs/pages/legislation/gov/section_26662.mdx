# GOV § 26662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 3. Process and Notices

**Citation:** GOV § 26662

---

## Text

The return of the sheriff upon process or notices is prima facie evidence of the facts stated in the return.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
