# GOV § 27361.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27361.5

---

## Text

(a) As used in Section 27361, a page shall be one printed side of a single piece of paper being 812 inches by 11 inches.

(b) A sheet shall be one printed side of a single piece of paper which is not exactly 812 inches by 11 inches but not greater than 812 inches by 14 inches.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 87, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1994, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 87.)
