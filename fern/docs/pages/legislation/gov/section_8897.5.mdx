# GOV § 8897.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13.8. Residential Strengthening—Seismic Safety > Article: 2. Earthquake Evaluations

**Citation:** GOV § 8897.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the duty of the real estate licensee shall be limited to providing to the seller a copy of the Homeowner’s Guide to Earthquake Safety for delivery to the prospective transferee pursuant to Section 2079.8 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 307, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
