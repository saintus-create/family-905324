# GOV § 50022.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Legislative Bodies

**Citation:** GOV § 50022.5

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this article shall be deemed to relieve any local agency from the requirement of publishing in full the ordinance that adopts any code, and all provisions applicable to the publication shall be fully carried out.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 124, Sec. 43. Effective January 1, 1997.)
