# GOV § 54430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54430

---

## Text

The holder of the bonds or coupons shall not compel the exercise of the taxing power by the local agency or the forfeiture of its property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
