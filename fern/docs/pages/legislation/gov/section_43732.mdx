# GOV § 43732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43732

---

## Text

The taxes shall be levied and collected as other city taxes, and are in addition to all other taxes. They shall be used only for the payment of the bonds and interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
