# GOV § 23361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 4. Election on Creation of the Proposed County

**Citation:** GOV § 23361

---

## Text

The law relating to the preparation, printing, and distribution of sample ballots and primary elections does not apply to any election held pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
