# GOV § 27375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27375

---

## Text

The fee for taking an acknowledgment of any instrument is seventy-five cents ($0.75).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 869.)
