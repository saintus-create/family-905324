# GOV § 1061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Residence Restrictions and Absence from State

**Citation:** GOV § 1061

---

## Text

The director of each department who is a member of the Governor’s council shall reside at Sacramento.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
