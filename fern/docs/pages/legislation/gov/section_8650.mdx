# GOV § 8650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 16. General Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8650

---

## Text

Any funds received by state agencies as reimbursement for services or supplies furnished under the authority of this chapter shall be deposited to the credit of the appropriation or appropriations from which the expenditures were made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
