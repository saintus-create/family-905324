# GOV § 12071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 7. Commission on the Governorship

**Citation:** GOV § 12071

---

## Text

The commission shall have exclusive authority to petition the Supreme Court to determine any questions that arise relating to vacancies in and succession to the office of Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 162.)
