# GOV § 98000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 16. STATE–TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN GAMING > Chapter: 1. The Tribal Government Gaming and Economic Self-Sufficiency Act of 1998

**Citation:** GOV § 98000

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as “The Tribal Government Gaming and Economic Self-Sufficiency Act of 1998.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 1998, by initiative Proposition 5. Note: The title of Chapter 1 (the Tribal-Government Gaming and Economic Self-Sufficiency Act of 1998) implies a short title for Prop. 5)
