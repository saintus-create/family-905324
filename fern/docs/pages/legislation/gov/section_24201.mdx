# GOV § 24201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Election

**Citation:** GOV § 24201

---

## Text

All officers elected pursuant to this chapter shall hold office until their successors are elected or appointed and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
