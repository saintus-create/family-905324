# GOV § 1320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Appointments, Nominations, Commissions, and Oaths > Article: 2. Nominations by Governor

**Citation:** GOV § 1320

---

## Text

Nominations made by the Governor to the Senate shall be in writing, designating the residence of the nominee and the office for which he is nominated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
