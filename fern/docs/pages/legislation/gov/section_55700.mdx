# GOV § 55700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Tax Sharing > Article: 1. Bradley-Burns Revenues

**Citation:** GOV § 55700

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means a county, city and county, and city, both chartered and general law.
