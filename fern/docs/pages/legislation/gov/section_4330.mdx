# GOV § 4330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Preference for Materials > Article: 2. California-Made Materials

**Citation:** GOV § 4330

---

## Text

As used in this article, “supplies” includes goods, wares, merchandise, and manufactures.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 785, Sec. 3. (AB 822) Effective January 1, 2018.)
