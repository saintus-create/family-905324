# GOV § 18930.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Employment Lists > Article: 2. Examinations

**Citation:** GOV § 18930.1

---

## Text

The department shall establish best practices for each aspect of the design, announcement, and administration of examinations for the purpose of increasing diversity of applicant pools on employment lists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 313, Sec. 4. (AB 1604) Effective January 1, 2023.)
