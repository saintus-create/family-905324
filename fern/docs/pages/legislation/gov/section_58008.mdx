# GOV § 58008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 58008

---

## Text

Proof of publication may be by affidavit of the owner, publisher, printer, or clerk of the newspaper. The affidavit is prima facie evidence of the facts stated in it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
