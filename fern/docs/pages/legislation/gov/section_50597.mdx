# GOV § 50597

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. Open Space Maintenance Districts > Article: 2. Formation of District

**Citation:** GOV § 50597

---

## Text

The ordinance of intention shall be published as provided in Section 6061 and at least 15 days before the date set for hearing protests or objections.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1503.)
