# GOV § 70508

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 9. Conveyance: Court Facility Property: County of San Diego

**Citation:** GOV § 70508

---

## Text

Any legal challenge that is brought against the County of San Diego with regard to the demolition project or the development project shall not result in a reconveyance of the San Diego property to the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 17, Sec. 23. (AB 103) Effective June 27, 2017.)
