# GOV § 22970.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.1

---

## Text

The benefits provided under the Supplemental Contributions Program shall supplement the benefits provided under Part 3 (commencing with Section 20000) and Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 9350) of Part 1 of Division 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
