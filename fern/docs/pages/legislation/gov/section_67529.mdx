# GOV § 67529

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 2. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 67529

---

## Text

The authority may either employ an executive director, a secretary, an attorney, and such other officers, or employees, or consultants as are necessary to enable it to perform the duties imposed upon it by this title or it may contract for any or all necessary services with a qualified public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1373.)
