# GOV § 53932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 13. Priority of Special Assessment Liens

**Citation:** GOV § 53932

---

## Text

From and after the time for filing or recording the assessment, as provided in the law pursuant to which it is levied, it shall impart notice to all persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1465.)
