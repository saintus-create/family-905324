# GOV § 14555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Federal Highway Grant Anticipation Notes > Article: 4. Issuance of Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 14555

---

## Text

Upon request of the commission, the Treasurer may issue refunding notes to refund any outstanding notes, and to pay costs associated with that refunding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 862, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
