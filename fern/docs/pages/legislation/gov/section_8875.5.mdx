# GOV § 8875.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.2. Building Earthquake Safety

**Citation:** GOV § 8875.5

---

## Text

The Seismic Safety Commission shall coordinate the earthquake-related responsibilities of government agencies imposed by this chapter to ensure compliance with the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8879.5 by Stats. 1987, Ch. 56, Sec. 66.)
