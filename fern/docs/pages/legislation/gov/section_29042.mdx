# GOV § 29042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 2. Budget Request

**Citation:** GOV § 29042

---

## Text

The requests shall be submitted as prescribed by the administrative officer or the auditor, as designated by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 19. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
