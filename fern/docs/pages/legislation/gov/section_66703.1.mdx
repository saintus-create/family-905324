# GOV § 66703.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.25. SAN FRANCISCO BAY RESTORATION AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 66703.1

---

## Text

The members of the board are subject to the Political Reform Act of 1974 (Title 9 (commencing with Section 81000)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 690, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009. Repealed as of January 1, 2049, pursuant to Section 66706.)
