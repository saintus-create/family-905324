# GOV § 23005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 23005

---

## Text

A county may exercise its powers only through the board of supervisors or through agents and officers acting under authority of the board or authority conferred by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
