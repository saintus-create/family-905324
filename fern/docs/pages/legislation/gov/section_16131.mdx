# GOV § 16131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. Transfers to the State School Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16131

---

## Text

The funds appropriated by Section 16100 shall be used for any advances authorized by Section 17401.5 of the Education Code.
