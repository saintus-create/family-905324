# GOV § 12419.3.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12419.3.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, the payments authorized pursuant to Chapter 16.1 (commencing with Section 18997.5) of Part 6 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code shall not be used to offset any delinquent accounts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 1010, Sec. 3. (SB 242) Effective January 1, 2025.)
