# GOV § 63025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 2. California Infrastructure and Economic Development Bank > Article: 1. Creation of the Bank

**Citation:** GOV § 63025

---

## Text

The bank board shall do the following:

(a) Adopt bylaws for the regulation of its affairs and the conduct of its business.

(b) Adopt an official seal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 94) by Stats. 1994, Ch. 749, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1995.)
