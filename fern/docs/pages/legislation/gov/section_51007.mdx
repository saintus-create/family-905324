# GOV § 51007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 51007

---

## Text

The court shall determine the location of the boundary line by courses and distances or by giving such other definite description as is necessary or desirable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
