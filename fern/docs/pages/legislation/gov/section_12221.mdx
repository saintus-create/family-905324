# GOV § 12221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12221

---

## Text

The Secretary of State is the custodian of the public archives of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
