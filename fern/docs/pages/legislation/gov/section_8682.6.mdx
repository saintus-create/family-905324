# GOV § 8682.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 8682.6

---

## Text

The project proposal executed between a local agency and the director pursuant to Section 8685.6 shall contain a provision under which the local agency agrees to hold the state harmless from damages due to the work for which funds are allocated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 188. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
