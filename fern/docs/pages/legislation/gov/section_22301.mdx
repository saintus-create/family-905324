# GOV § 22301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Agreements for Coverage > Article: 2. Retirement System Referendums

**Citation:** GOV § 22301

---

## Text

In the case of employees in positions covered by a retirement system as set forth in subdivision (i) of Section 22009.1, such request may be made by such officials as the board deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this part as they relate to such system.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 662.)
