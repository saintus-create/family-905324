# GOV § 51188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.8. Moderate, High, and Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51188

---

## Text

In the instance of conflict between this chapter and any provision of state law that allows a regional planning agency to regulate very high fire hazard severity zones, this chapter shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1188, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
