# GOV § 65040.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 65040.5

---

## Text

(a) The office shall notify a city or county with a general plan that has not been revised within eight years.

(b) The office shall notify the Attorney General if a general plan of a city or county has not been revised within ten years.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 437, Sec. 2. Effective September 24, 1993.)
