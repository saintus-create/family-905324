# GOV § 11291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 3. Special Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 11291

---

## Text

If a state agency refuses to pay the charges fixed by the Department of General Services pursuant to this article, the department may file a claim for such charges against any appropriations made for the support or maintenance of all or any part of the work and affairs of the state agency. The Controller shall draw his warrant in accordance with law upon the claim in favor of the Department of General Services.
