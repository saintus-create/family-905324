# GOV § 20131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 20131

---

## Text

The board shall keep in convenient form data necessary for the actuarial valuation of this system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
