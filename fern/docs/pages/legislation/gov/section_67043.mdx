# GOV § 67043

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 3. California Tahoe Regional Planning Agency > Article: 1. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 67043

---

## Text

The term of office of the members of the governing body shall be at the pleasure of the appointing authority in each case.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
