# GOV § 66499.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 6. Reversions and Exclusions > Article: 2. Exclusions

**Citation:** GOV § 66499.27

---

## Text

The exclusion of any real property or the alteration or vacation of any recorded map pursuant to this article shall not affect or vacate the whole or any part of any public street or highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
