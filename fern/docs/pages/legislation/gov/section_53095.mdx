# GOV § 53095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Regulation of Local Agencies by Counties and Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 53095

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall prevail over Sections 17215 and 81035 of the Education Code and over Section 65402 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1058, Sec. 119. Effective January 1, 2001.)
