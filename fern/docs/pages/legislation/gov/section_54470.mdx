# GOV § 54470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54470

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for the rights, liabilities, powers, and duties arising upon the local agency’s breach of any covenants, conditions, or obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
