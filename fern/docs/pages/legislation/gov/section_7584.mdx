# GOV § 7584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 26.5. Interagency Responsibilities for Providing Services to Children with Disabilities

**Citation:** GOV § 7584

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “disabled youth,” “child,” or “pupil” means individuals with exceptional needs as defined in Section 56026 of the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 759, Sec. 79. Effective September 21, 1992.)
