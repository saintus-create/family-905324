# GOV § 54732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Contracts of Sanitation or Sewerage Enterprises

**Citation:** GOV § 54732

---

## Text

The duration and terms of the contracts shall be those which the legislative body determines to be for the best interests of the local agency in each case.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1249.)
