# GOV § 6718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6718

---

## Text

The Governor shall proclaim the month of February as Black History Month each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 256, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
