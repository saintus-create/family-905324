# GOV § 40200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 40200

---

## Text

Pursuant to this article, the legislative body may take a census of the city between the years of taking the federal census. That census shall be validated by the Demographic Research Unit of the Department of Finance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 29, Sec. 113. (SB 82) Effective June 27, 2019.)
