# GOV § 31106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 2. CIVIL SERVICE > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31106

---

## Text

The ordinance creating the civil service system shall designate the appointive officers and employees to be placed in the system.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
