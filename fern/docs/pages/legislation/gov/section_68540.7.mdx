# GOV § 68540.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 2. Assignment of Judges

**Citation:** GOV § 68540.7

---

## Text

Any judge assigned to a court wherein a judge’s compensation is greater than his or her own shall receive while sitting therein the compensation of a judge thereof. The extra compensation shall be paid in such manner as may be provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 187, Sec. 3. Effective June 29, 1990. Operative July 1, 1990, by Sec. 12 of Ch. 187.)
