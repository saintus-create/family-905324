# GOV § 13904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13904

---

## Text

The board shall keep a record of all its proceedings and any member may cause his dissent to the action of the majority upon any matter to be entered upon such record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 112.)
