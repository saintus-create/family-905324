# GOV § 93102

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.3. SISKIYOU RAILROAD AUTHORITY

**Citation:** GOV § 93102

---

## Text

As used in this title, “authority” means the Siskiyou Railroad Authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
