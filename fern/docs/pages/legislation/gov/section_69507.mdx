# GOV § 69507

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 69507

---

## Text

The presiding judge of each superior court may designate a judge of the court to be available on days other than a Saturday, Sunday or legal holiday to perform a marriage without fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1589.)
