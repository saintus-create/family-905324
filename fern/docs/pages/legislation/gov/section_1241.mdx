# GOV § 1241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 1241

---

## Text

Whenever a section of the California Constitution uses both the terms “salary” and “compensation”, with respect to a public officer or employee, the terms shall be construed to apply only to salary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1356, Sec. 1.)
