# GOV § 31776.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 11.5. Deferred Retirement Option Program

**Citation:** GOV § 31776.5

---

## Text

The final compensation calculated under Section 31776.2 shall be the member’s final compensation for purposes of calculating any reciprocal benefits due the member from another retirement system pursuant to Article 15 (commencing with Section 31830).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 897, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
