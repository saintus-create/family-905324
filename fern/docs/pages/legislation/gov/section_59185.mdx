# GOV § 59185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 2. Authority to Conduct Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 59185

---

## Text

When consent has been given, the legislative body to which it was granted has complete authority to conduct proceedings pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
