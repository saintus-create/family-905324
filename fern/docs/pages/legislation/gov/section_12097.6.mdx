# GOV § 12097.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 4.7. Office of Regional Economic Development Initiatives

**Citation:** GOV § 12097.6

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, notwithstanding Section 12096.1, the following definitions shall apply:

(a) “GO-Biz” means the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development.

(b) “Office” means the Office of Regional Economic Development Initiatives established pursuant to Section 12097.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 84, Sec. 14. (SB 172) Effective July 13, 2026.)
