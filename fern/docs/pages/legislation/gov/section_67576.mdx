# GOV § 67576

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67576

---

## Text

The commission may provide for the payment of the principal and interest of bonds at any place or places within or without the State of California, and in any specified lawful coin or currency of the United States of America.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1079.)
