# GOV § 9704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 9704

---

## Text

Proceeds from sales of all documents, the cost of publication of which is or has been paid from any appropriation for legislative printing, shall be credited to the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 870.)
