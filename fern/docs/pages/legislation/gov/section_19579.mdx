# GOV § 19579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations From Service > Article: 1. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 19579

---

## Text

Failure of either party (the employee, the employer, or their representatives) to proceed at the hearing shall be deemed a withdrawal of the action or appeal, unless the hearing is continued by mutual agreement of the parties, or upon showing of good cause.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1195, Sec. 4.5.)
