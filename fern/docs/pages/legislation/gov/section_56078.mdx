# GOV § 56078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56078

---

## Text

“Subsidiary district” means a district in which a city council is designated as, and empowered to act as, the ex officio board of directors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 59. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
