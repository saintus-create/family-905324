# GOV § 50705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 1. Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 50705

---

## Text

The board has full charge of the acquisition, construction and completion of all projects authorized by it and may proceed with such work subject to the availability of funds therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
