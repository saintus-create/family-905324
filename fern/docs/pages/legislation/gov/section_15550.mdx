# GOV § 15550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 15550

---

## Text

As used in this part, “agency” and “secretary” refer to the Labor and Workforce Development Agency and the Secretary of Labor and Workforce Development, respectively, unless the context otherwise requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 859, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
