# GOV § 12012.495

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12012.495

---

## Text

The memorandum of agreement entered into between the State of California and the Pechanga Band of Luiseño Indians, executed on June 27, 2007, is hereby approved.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 42, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2008.)
