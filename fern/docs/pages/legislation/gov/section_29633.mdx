# GOV § 29633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Charges > Article: 2. Victims of Crime

**Citation:** GOV § 29633

---

## Text

The legislative body may impose such other restrictions and conditions as it finds advisable and which do not impair the constitutional rights of any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1627.)
