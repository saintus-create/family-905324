# GOV § 8295

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8295

---

## Text

The commission and the committee shall confer and cooperate with any legislative committee on revision of the law and may contract with any other committee for the rendition of service, by either for the other, in the work of revision.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 16. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
