# GOV § 13332.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2.5. Provisions Applicable to Appropriations of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13332.12

---

## Text

(a) Any acquisition of land or other real property authorized in any appropriation, except an appropriation from the California Water Fund or an appropriation to the Department of Transportation or the High-Speed Rail Authority for capital outlay purposes, shall be subject to the provisions of the Property Acquisition Law (Part 11 (commencing with Section 15850)). Nothing in this section shall be construed as exempting the California Coastal Commission from this section.

(b) All property acquisitions, including those exempted pursuant to subdivision (a), shall be reported to the State Public Works Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 790, Sec. 5. (SB 1172) Effective January 1, 2019.)
