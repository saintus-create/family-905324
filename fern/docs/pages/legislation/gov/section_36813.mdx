# GOV § 36813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 36813

---

## Text

The council may establish rules for the conduct of its proceedings. It may punish a member or other person for disorderly behavior at a meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
