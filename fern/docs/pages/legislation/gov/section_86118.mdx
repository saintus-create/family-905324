# GOV § 86118

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 6. Lobbyists > Article: 1. Registration and Reporting

**Citation:** GOV § 86118

---

## Text

(a) Reports required by Sections 86114 and 86116 shall be filed online or electronically with the Secretary of State.

(b) Any form, registration, or report requiring paper filing pursuant to this chapter may be verified by the filer with an electronic signature.
