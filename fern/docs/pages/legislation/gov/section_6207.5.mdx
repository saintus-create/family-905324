# GOV § 6207.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3.1. Address Confidentiality for Victims of Domestic Violence, Sexual Assault, Stalking, Human Trafficking, Child Abduction, and Elder or Dependent Adult Abuse

**Citation:** GOV § 6207.5

---

## Text

A program participant who is otherwise qualified to vote may seek to register and vote in a confidential manner pursuant to Section 2166.5 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1005, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
