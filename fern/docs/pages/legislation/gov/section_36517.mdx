# GOV § 36517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36517

---

## Text

The city clerk and the city treasurer shall receive, at stated times, a compensation fixed by ordinance or resolution.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 275.)
