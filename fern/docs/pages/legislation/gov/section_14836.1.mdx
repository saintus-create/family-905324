# GOV § 14836.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. Small Business Procurement and Contract Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 14836.1

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that this chapter is a state law that provides assistance and services for persons regardless of immigration status within the meaning of Section 1621(d) of Title 8 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. (AB 2543) Effective January 1, 2025.)
