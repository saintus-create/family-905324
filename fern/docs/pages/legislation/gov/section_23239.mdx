# GOV § 23239

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23239

---

## Text

A petition may designate not more than three persons as chief petitioners setting forth their names and mailing addresses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
