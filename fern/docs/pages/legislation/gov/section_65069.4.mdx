# GOV § 65069.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 8. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 65069.4

---

## Text

The treasurers of the several counties within the district shall pay into the district treasury all funds held by them to the credit of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
