# GOV § 8955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 3. Joint Legislative Ethics Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8955

---

## Text

The committee may render advisory opinions to Members of the Legislature with respect to the provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 8920) and their application and construction. The committee may secure an opinion from the Legislative Counsel for this purpose or issue its own opinion.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 60.)
