# GOV § 25701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 6. Reclamation of Water

**Citation:** GOV § 25701

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may enter into contracts with municipalities, sanitation districts, sanitary districts or other incorporated bodies within the county, providing for the delivery to the county of sewage, or other waste water, or both, produced by or coming from such incorporated bodies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 28.)
