# GOV § 27711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 13. Public Defender

**Citation:** GOV § 27711

---

## Text

The annual salary of each public defender shall be such as has been or may be fixed by the county or counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1288.)
