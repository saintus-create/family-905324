# GOV § 23077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 1. Terms Defined

**Citation:** GOV § 23077

---

## Text

The use of a county name specified in this division, without further description, is a reference to the county of that name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
