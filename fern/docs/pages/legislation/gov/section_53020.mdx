# GOV § 53020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Emergency Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 53020

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means public agency.
