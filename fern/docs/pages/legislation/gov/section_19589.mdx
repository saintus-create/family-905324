# GOV § 19589

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations From Service > Article: 1. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 19589

---

## Text

Letters of reprimand shall be removed from the personnel file of the state employee and destroyed not later than three years from the date the letters were issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 618, Sec. 1.)
