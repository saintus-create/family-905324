# GOV § 11121.95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 11121.95

---

## Text

Any person appointed or elected to serve as a member of a state body who has not yet assumed the duties of office shall conform his or her conduct to the requirements of this article and shall be treated for purposes of this article as if he or she has already assumed office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 949, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
