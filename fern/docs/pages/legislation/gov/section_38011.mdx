# GOV § 38011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 7. Park and Playground Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 38011

---

## Text

The Improvement Act of 1911, the Municipal Improvement Act of 1913, and the Improvement Bond Act of 1915 may be used by a city for any improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 426.)
