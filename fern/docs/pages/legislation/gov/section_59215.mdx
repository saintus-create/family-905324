# GOV § 59215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 3. Resolution of Intention

**Citation:** GOV § 59215

---

## Text

No property within the new district shall be charged in the new assessment with any of the debt of a district of which it was not formerly a part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
