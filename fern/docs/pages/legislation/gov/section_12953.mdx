# GOV § 12953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Discrimination Prohibited > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices, Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 12953

---

## Text

It is an unlawful employment practice for an employer to violate Section 432.6 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. (AB 51) Effective January 1, 2020.)
