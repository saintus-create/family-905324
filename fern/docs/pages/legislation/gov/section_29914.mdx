# GOV § 29914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29914

## Text

The bonds may be issued in such denomination or denominations as the board of supervisors may prescribe.
