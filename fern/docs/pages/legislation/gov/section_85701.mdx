# GOV § 85701

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 5. Limitations on Contributions > Article: 7. Additional Contribution Requirements

**Citation:** GOV § 85701

---

## Text

Any candidate or committee that receives a contribution in violation of Section 84301 shall pay to the General Fund of the state the amount of the contribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 102, Sec. 66. Approved in Proposition 34 at the November 7, 2000, election. Operative January 1, 2001, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 102, as amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 241, Sec. 18.)
