# GOV § 34407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Special Charter Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34407

---

## Text

The legislative body shall immediately call a special election, to be held within six weeks, for the election of officers required to be elected.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 624.)
