# GOV § 53836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 7.4. Securitized Limited Obligations Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 53836

---

## Text

The powers conferred by this article are in addition to and alternative to any powers conferred by any other law for borrowing by a special district and any amount borrowed pursuant to this article shall not be considered in any limitation on the amount that may be borrowed by a special district under any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 114, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
