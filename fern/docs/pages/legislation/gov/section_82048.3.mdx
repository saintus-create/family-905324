# GOV § 82048.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82048.3

---

## Text

“Slate mailer” means a mass mailing which supports or opposes a total of four or more candidates or ballot measures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 905, Sec. 1.)
