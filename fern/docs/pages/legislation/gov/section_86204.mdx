# GOV § 86204

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 6. Lobbyists > Article: 2. Prohibitions

**Citation:** GOV § 86204

---

## Text

It shall be unlawful for any person knowingly to receive any gift which is made unlawful by Section 86203.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 161, Sec. 7. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
