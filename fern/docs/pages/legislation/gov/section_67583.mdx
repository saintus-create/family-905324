# GOV § 67583

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67583

---

## Text

All bonds, and the interest thereon, are exempt from all taxation by the State of California or any public corporation other than gift, inheritance and franchise taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1373.)
