# GOV § 6723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6723

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim February 6 as Ronald Reagan Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 114, Sec. 10. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2011.)
