# GOV § 9792

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 5. Distribution of Statutes and Legislative Publications

**Citation:** GOV § 9792

---

## Text

All other copies of the laws, resolutions and journals shall be sold by the Department of General Services at such price as it may fix.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
