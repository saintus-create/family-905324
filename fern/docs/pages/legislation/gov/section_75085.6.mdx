# GOV § 75085.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 4.5. Extended Service Incentive Program

**Citation:** GOV § 75085.6

---

## Text

“Extended service calculation date” means the later of (a) January 1, 2001, or (b) the date the judge first becomes eligible to participate in the program pursuant to Section 75086.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 961, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
