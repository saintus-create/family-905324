# GOV § 62119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. Community Revitalization and Investment Authorities > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Replacement and Location

**Citation:** GOV § 62119

---

## Text

Permanent housing facilities shall be made available within two years from the time occupants are displaced and that pending the development of such facilities there will be available to such displaced occupants adequate temporary housing facilities at rents comparable to the units from which the displaced occupants were displaced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 319, Sec. 2. (AB 2) Effective January 1, 2016.)
