# GOV § 50083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 50083

---

## Text

No local agency or district shall require that its employees be residents of such local agency or district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1418.)
