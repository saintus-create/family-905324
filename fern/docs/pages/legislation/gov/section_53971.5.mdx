# GOV § 53971.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 16. Police and Fire Protection Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53971.5

---

## Text

As used in this article, “ordinance” includes “resolution” where the local agency is authorized to act only by resolution.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 397.)
