# GOV § 54404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54404

---

## Text

The bonds are not subject to call or redemption prior to their fixed maturity date unless the right to exercise a call is expressly stated on the face of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
