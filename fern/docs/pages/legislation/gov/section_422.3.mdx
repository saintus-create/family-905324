# GOV § 422.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 422.3

---

## Text

The almond (Prunus dulcis, Prunus amygdalus), walnut, pistachio, and pecan are each the official state nut.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 49, Sec. 2. (AB 1067) Effective January 1, 2018.)
