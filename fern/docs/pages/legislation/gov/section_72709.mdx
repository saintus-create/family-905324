# GOV § 72709

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 9. Los Angeles County

**Citation:** GOV § 72709

---

## Text

The salaries and benefits of official reporters shall be paid from the Reporters’ Salary Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 398. Effective January 1, 2003.)
