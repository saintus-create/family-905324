# GOV § 14928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8.5. Guaranteed Return Trip Demonstration Project

**Citation:** GOV § 14928

---

## Text

The department shall develop standards and guidelines for the demonstration project, including standards for eligibility for and utilization of services provided under the demonstration project. The services shall be available to all state employees who regularly commute to work by use of mass transit or high-occupancy vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1653, Sec. 3.)
