# GOV § 26741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26741

---

## Text

The fee for executing and delivering a certificate or deed of sale is nineteen dollars ($19).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 29, Sec. 16. (SB 564) Effective January 1, 2024.)
