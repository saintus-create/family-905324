# GOV § 8696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.6. Economic Disaster Act of 1984

**Citation:** GOV § 8696

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this chapter to institutionalize the planning and response of state agencies to disasters in order to reduce economic hardship stemming from these disasters to business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1410, Sec. 2.)
