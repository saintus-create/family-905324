# GOV § 26222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 13. Miscellaneous Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 26222

---

## Text

The assignee, through an attorney duly licensed to practice in this State, may invoke legal process in the collection of obligations assigned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 865.)
