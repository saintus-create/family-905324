# GOV § 7274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 16. Relocation Assistance

**Citation:** GOV § 7274

---

## Text

Sections 7267 to 7267.7, inclusive, create no rights or liabilities and shall not affect the validity of any property acquisitions by purchase or condemnation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1574.)
