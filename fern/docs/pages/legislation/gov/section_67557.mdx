# GOV § 67557

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67557

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause requiring the department to continuously operate the regional transit terminal and other facilities in an efficient and economical manner.
