# GOV § 69846

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 7. Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 69846

---

## Text

The clerk of the superior court shall safely keep or dispose of according to law all papers and records filed or deposited in any action or proceeding before the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 206.)
