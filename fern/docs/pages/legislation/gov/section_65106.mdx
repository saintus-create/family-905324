# GOV § 65106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 1. Local Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 65106

---

## Text

Upon request all public officials shall furnish to the planning agency within a reasonable time any available information as may be required for the work of the planning agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 617, Sec. 26.)
