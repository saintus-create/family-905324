# GOV § 3000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 3000

---

## Text

An officer forfeits his office upon conviction of designated crimes as specified in the Constitution and laws of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
