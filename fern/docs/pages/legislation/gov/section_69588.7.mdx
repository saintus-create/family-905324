# GOV § 69588.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 3. Number of Judges

**Citation:** GOV § 69588.7

---

## Text

In the County of Mendocino there are eight judges of the superior court.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 250. Effective January 1, 2003.)
