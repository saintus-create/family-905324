# GOV § 23721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Charters > Article: 2. Charter Amendment and Repeal

**Citation:** GOV § 23721

---

## Text

The petition shall forthwith be examined and certified by the county elections official as provided in Article 1 (commencing with Section 9100) of Chapter 2 of Division 9 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 51. Effective January 1, 1995.)
