# GOV § 11518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Administrative Adjudication: Formal Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 11518

---

## Text

Copies of the decision shall be delivered to the parties personally or sent to them by registered mail.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 938, Sec. 43. Effective January 1, 1996. Operative July 1, 1997, by Sec. 98 of Ch. 938.)
