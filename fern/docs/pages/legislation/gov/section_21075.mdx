# GOV § 21075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 2. Second Tier Retirement—State

**Citation:** GOV § 21075

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 20340, a person who is subject to Section 21076, 21076.5, or Section 21077 ceases to be a member if he or she has less than 10 years of service credit and no accumulated contributions in the retirement fund at the time of termination of service, except a member who had five years of credited service prior to January 1, 1985.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 237, Sec. 5. (AB 2472) Effective January 1, 2015.)
