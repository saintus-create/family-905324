# GOV § 27134

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 6. County Treasury Oversight Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 27134

---

## Text

The county treasury oversight committee shall cause an annual audit to be conducted to determine the county treasury’s compliance with this article. The audit may include issues relating to the structure of the investment portfolio and risk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 784, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1996.)
