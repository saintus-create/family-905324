# GOV § 50752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50752

---

## Text

All bonds issued under the provisions of this chapter are negotiable instruments, except when registered in the name of a registered owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
