# GOV § 17280.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 4. Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 17280.2

---

## Text

In the event a tax liability is paid, in whole or in part, with a registered warrant which is redeemable at the time the tax liability is paid, interest as specified in this article, shall be credited to the taxpayer’s account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1211, Sec. 4.)
