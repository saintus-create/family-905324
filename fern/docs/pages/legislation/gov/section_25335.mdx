# GOV § 25335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4.5. Provision of County Services > Article: 1. Optional Services and Enhanced Services Provided to the Public

**Citation:** GOV § 25335

---

## Text

Any contract entered into pursuant to this chapter shall be authorized by a resolution of the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 787, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
