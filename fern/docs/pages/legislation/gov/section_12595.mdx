# GOV § 12595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 7. Supervision of Trustees and Fundraisers for Charitable Purposes Act

**Citation:** GOV § 12595

---

## Text

This act shall be so construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law of those states which enact it.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1258.)
