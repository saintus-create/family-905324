# GOV § 38734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5. Water

**Citation:** GOV § 38734

---

## Text

If the legislative bodies approve the plans and terms reported by the commissioners, they shall declare their approval by resolution and submit them to the qualified voters of their respective cities for approval or rejection at the next city election or at a special election called for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
