# GOV § 53035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2.5. Disposal of Coastal Property

**Citation:** GOV § 53035

---

## Text

It is the policy of this state to protect public access to beaches and coastal lands.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1099.)
