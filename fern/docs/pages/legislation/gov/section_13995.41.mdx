# GOV § 13995.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 4. Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.41

---

## Text

The purpose of the commission is to increase the number of persons traveling to and within California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 229, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
