# GOV § 8876.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.3. Center for Earthquake Engineering Research

**Citation:** GOV § 8876.10

---

## Text

No provision of this chapter shall apply to the Regents of the University of California, unless the regents adopt a resolution making that provision applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 966, Sec. 2. Effective September 27, 1996.)
