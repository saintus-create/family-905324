# GOV § 29749

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Claims > Article: 2. Allowance of Claims by Auditor

**Citation:** GOV § 29749

---

## Text

The auditor shall require the certificate of the requisitioning, inspection, or receiving officers that the articles and services have been received or furnished or contracted for.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 109.)
