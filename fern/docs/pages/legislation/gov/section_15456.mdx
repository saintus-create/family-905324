# GOV § 15456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 7.2. HEALTH FACILITIES FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15456

---

## Text

To the extent that the provisions of this part are inconsistent with any other provisions of any general statute or special act or parts thereof, the provisions of this part shall be deemed controlling.

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1033.)
