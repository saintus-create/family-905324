# GOV § 65870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Zoning Regulations > Article: 2.7. Covenants for Easement

**Citation:** GOV § 65870

---

## Text

Any city or county may adopt an ordinance for the imposition of covenants pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 996, Sec. 1.)
