# GOV § 43723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43723

---

## Text

The rate of interest during the entire term of the bonds need not be the same but different rates may be fixed for one or more interest payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
