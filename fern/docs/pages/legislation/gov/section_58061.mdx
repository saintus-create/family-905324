# GOV § 58061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 3. Preliminary Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58061

---

## Text

If the principal act or any other law applicable to the formation of the district requires the making of an investigation of and a report on the project, the hearing may be continued from time to time to enable the making of such investigation and report. Such continuance shall not exceed three months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
