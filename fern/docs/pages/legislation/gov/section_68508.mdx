# GOV § 68508

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68508

---

## Text

No act of the Judicial Council shall be valid unless concurred in by a majority of its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 161.)
