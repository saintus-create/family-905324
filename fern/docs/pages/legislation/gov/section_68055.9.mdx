# GOV § 68055.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.97. RECYCLING, RESOURCE RECOVERY, AND LITTER PREVENTION

**Citation:** GOV § 68055.9

---

## Text

The penalties which may be levied for littering in this state shall be prominently posted throughout the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 364.)
