# GOV § 41203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. City Assessor

**Citation:** GOV § 41203

---

## Text

The list shall:

(a) Describe the property assessed.

(b) Give the value of the property.

(c) Contain other information required in county assessors’ lists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
