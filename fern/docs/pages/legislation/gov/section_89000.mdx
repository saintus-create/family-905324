# GOV § 89000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 9. Incumbency

**Citation:** GOV § 89000

---

## Text

Any provision of law to the contrary notwithstanding, the order of names of candidates on the ballot in every election shall be determined without regard to whether the candidate is an incumbent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
