# GOV § 11155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Departments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11155

---

## Text

The head of a department has no authority to obligate the State for salaries in excess of money available by law for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
