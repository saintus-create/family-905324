# GOV § 19995.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. The Governor’s Awards

**Citation:** GOV § 19995.6

---

## Text

Employees shall be nominated for the award by the directors of the various state departments and agencies and the Governor shall select from the persons so nominated the persons to whom he or she shall make the award.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
