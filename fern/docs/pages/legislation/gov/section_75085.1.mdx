# GOV § 75085.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 4.5. Extended Service Incentive Program

**Citation:** GOV § 75085.1

---

## Text

The design and administration of the Extended Service Incentive Program shall conform to the applicable provisions of Title 26 of the United States Code and the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 961, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
