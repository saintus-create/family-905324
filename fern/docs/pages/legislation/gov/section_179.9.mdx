# GOV § 179.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Sovereignty of the State > Article: 3.7. Emergency Management Assistance Compact

**Citation:** GOV § 179.9

---

## Text

This article shall become inoperative on March 1, 2038, and, as of January 1, 2039, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 402, Sec. 1. (AB 2469) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2039, by its own provisions. Note: Termination clause affects Article 3.7, commencing with Section 179.)
