# GOV § 54956.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 54956.6

---

## Text

No fees may be charged by the legislative body of a local agency for carrying out any provision of this chapter, except as specifically authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1284.)
