# GOV § 40232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 2. Special Census

**Citation:** GOV § 40232

---

## Text

The ordinance shall prescribe the rules and regulations for taking the census to insure an accurate count of the inhabitants of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
