# GOV § 54063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 3. Coliseum and Stadium Parking

**Citation:** GOV § 54063

---

## Text

For the use of such property and facilities by the public, the local agency may make and collect a charge fixed by its legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
