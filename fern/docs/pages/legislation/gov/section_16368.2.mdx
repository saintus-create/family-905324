# GOV § 16368.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 1.8. Special Account for Capital Outlay

**Citation:** GOV § 16368.2

---

## Text

This article shall become operative upon the date that Article 1.8 (commencing with Section 16368) of Chapter 2 of Part 2 of Division 4 of Title 2 of the Government Code, as added by Section 5 of the act that added this article to the code, is repealed unless a later enacted statute changes the requirements of this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 5) and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 899, Sec. 5.5. Effective September 17, 1980. Note: This section prescribes conditional operation for Article 1.8 (commencing with Section 18368) as added by Sec. 5.5 of Ch. 899.)
