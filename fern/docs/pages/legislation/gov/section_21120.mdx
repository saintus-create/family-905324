# GOV § 21120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 4. Reduced Worktime for Partial Service Retirement

## Text

Section 21546 shall apply to any member while in state service in partial service retirement.
