# GOV § 50572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 10. Surplus Real Property

**Citation:** GOV § 50572

---

## Text

Property may be transferred under this article only after a public hearing, but without regard to other provisions of this code concerning leases of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1737.)
