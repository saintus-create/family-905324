# GOV § 31685.96

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.4. Community Property

**Citation:** GOV § 31685.96

---

## Text

(a) The age factor applicable to the nonmember shall be based on the age of the nonmember at the time of his or her retirement.

(b) The board shall adopt age factors as recommended by the actuary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 670, Sec. 2 (1st text). Effective January 1, 1995.)
