# GOV § 53581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 11. Provisions Applicable to All Refunding Bonds of All Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 53581

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of any other law, the provisions of this article shall apply to all refunding bonds of any local agency, regardless of the authority for their issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 897.)
