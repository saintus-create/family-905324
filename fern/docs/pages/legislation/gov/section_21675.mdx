# GOV § 21675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 16. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** GOV § 21675

---

## Text

All development and administration costs of tax-preferred retirement savings programs shall be paid by employers and plan participants.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 639, Sec. 12. (SB 1139) Effective January 1, 2011.)
