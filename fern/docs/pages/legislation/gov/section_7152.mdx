# GOV § 7152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14. State Tax Liens > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 7152

---

## Text

“Buyer in ordinary course of business” has the same meaning as defined in paragraph (9) of subdivision (b) of Section 1201 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 254, Sec. 78. Effective January 1, 2007.)
