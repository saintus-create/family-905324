# GOV § 11275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 2. General Administrative Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 11275

---

## Text

In the event a fund has an insufficient fund balance for the payment of the administrative costs, the Controller shall request that the Department of Finance provide direction on effecting the transfer and its timing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 76. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
