# GOV § 73640

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 10. County-Specific Provisions > Article: 9. El Cajon Judicial District

**Citation:** GOV § 73640

---

## Text

This article applies to the municipal court established in a district embracing the Judicial District of El Cajon.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 416. Effective January 1, 2003. Repealed as of January 1, 2074, pursuant to Section 73643.)
