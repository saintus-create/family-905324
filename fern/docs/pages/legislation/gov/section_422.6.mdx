# GOV § 422.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 422.6

---

## Text

The giant garter snake (Thamnophis gigas) is the official state snake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 482, Sec. 2. (SB 765) Effective January 1, 2026.)
