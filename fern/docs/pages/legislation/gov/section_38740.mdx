# GOV § 38740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5. Water

**Citation:** GOV § 38740

---

## Text

The plans and terms or other agreement for acquiring the water supply may be modified by agreement between the cities, as declared by the legislative bodies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
