# GOV § 13974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Indemnification of Citizens Benefiting the Public

**Citation:** GOV § 13974

---

## Text

The California Victim Compensation Board is hereby authorized to make all needful rules and regulations consistent with the law for the purpose of carrying into effect this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 121. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
