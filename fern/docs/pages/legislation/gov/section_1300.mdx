# GOV § 1300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Appointments, Nominations, Commissions, and Oaths > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 1300

---

## Text

Every officer, the mode of whose appointment is not prescribed by law, shall be appointed by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
