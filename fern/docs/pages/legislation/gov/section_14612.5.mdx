# GOV § 14612.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 14612.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, for state printing procurement purposes, printing is not considered a personal service contract as defined in Section 19130.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1124, Sec. 17. Effective September 30, 2002.)
