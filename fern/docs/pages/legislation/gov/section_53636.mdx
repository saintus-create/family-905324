# GOV § 53636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53636

---

## Text

Money so deposited is deemed to be in the treasury of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
