# GOV § 67613

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 6. Rents, Rates, Fees, and Charges

**Citation:** GOV § 67613

---

## Text

The commission may provide in an indenture that the rates, rents, fees, and charges established are minimum rates, rents, fees, and charges and subject to increase or decrease in accordance only with the terms of the indenture.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1079.)
