# GOV § 11565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Statutory Salary Equalization Plan > Article: 2. Application of Salary Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 11565

---

## Text

If the salary specified in Article 1 (commencing with Section 11550) for any particular position is greater than the salary which the incumbent is receiving on the date when this chapter takes effect, he or she shall receive the higher amount from and after the first day of the month immediately following such effective date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 100.)
