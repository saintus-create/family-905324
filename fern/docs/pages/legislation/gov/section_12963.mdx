# GOV § 12963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Enforcement and Hearing Procedures > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices

**Citation:** GOV § 12963

---

## Text

After the filing of any complaint alleging facts sufficient to constitute a violation of any of the provisions of this part, the department shall make prompt investigation in connection therewith.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1023.)
