# GOV § 31965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** GOV § 31965

---

## Text

The board has charge and control of the funds of the system, and shall administer them and order payments therefrom in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
