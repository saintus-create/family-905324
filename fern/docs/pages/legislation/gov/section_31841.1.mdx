# GOV § 31841.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 15.5. Survivors’ Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31841.1

---

## Text

This article shall not be applicable to any member who because of his county service is subject to the federal old age and survivors insurance provisions of the Federal Social Security Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1591.)
