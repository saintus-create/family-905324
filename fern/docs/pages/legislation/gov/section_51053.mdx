# GOV § 51053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Open-Space Easements

**Citation:** GOV § 51053

---

## Text

The execution and acceptance of an instrument described in subdivision (a) of Section 51051 shall constitute a dedication to the public of the open-space character of the lands for the term specified. Any such easement and covenant shall run for a term of not less than 20 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 875, Sec. 3. (SB 1501) Effective January 1, 2013.)
