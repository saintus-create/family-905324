# GOV § 26728.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26728.1

---

## Text

The fee for preparing and posting additionally required notices of personal property sales is nineteen dollars ($19) each.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 29, Sec. 8. (SB 564) Effective January 1, 2024.)
