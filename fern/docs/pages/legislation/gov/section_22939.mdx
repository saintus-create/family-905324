# GOV § 22939

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 10. Contracting with Public Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 22939

---

## Text

The board may terminate the participation of a contracting agency if it fails for three months after a demand to perform any act required by this part or by board rules or regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
