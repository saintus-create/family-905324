# GOV § 13982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 13982

---

## Text

Whenever a power is granted to the secretary, the power may be exercised by such officer or employee within the agency as designated in writing by the secretary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 138.)
