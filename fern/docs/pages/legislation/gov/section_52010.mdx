# GOV § 52010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 52010

---

## Text

“Bond” means any bonds, notes, certificates of participation, interim certificates, debentures, or other obligations issued by an authority pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
