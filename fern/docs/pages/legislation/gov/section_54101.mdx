# GOV § 54101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4.5. Reconstruction of Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 54101

---

## Text

The board may insure all buildings replaced or reconstructed in a local agency by it until the total of the agreed rentals has been paid by the legislative body of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
