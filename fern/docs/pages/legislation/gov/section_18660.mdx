# GOV § 18660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1.5. Policy and Audit

**Citation:** GOV § 18660

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 3 of Article VII of the California Constitution, the board shall establish rules implementing and enforcing the merit principle in the state civil service system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 17. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
