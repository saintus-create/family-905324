# GOV § 8880.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.3

---

## Text

Prohibition on Use of State Funds

No appropriations, loans, or other transfer of State funds shall be made to the California State Lottery Commission except for a temporary line of credit for initial start-up costs as provided in this Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
