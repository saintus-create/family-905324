# GOV § 63048.91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 2. California Infrastructure and Economic Development Bank > Article: 6.7. Climate Catalyst Revolving Fund Act of 2020

**Citation:** GOV § 63048.91

---

## Text

(a) This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Climate Catalyst Revolving Fund Act of 2020.

(b) Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, this article does not apply to any other activities, powers, and duties of the Infrastructure and Economic Development Bank under this division.

(c) The bank shall administer the Climate Catalyst Revolving Fund to provide financial assistance for climate catalyst projects, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 63048.92.

(d) Financial assistance for climate catalyst projects through the Climate Catalyst Revolving Fund Program shall be provided at low-interest rates and at low-cost as determined by the bank, to support the projects directly and to attract additional third-party capital.

## Legislative History
