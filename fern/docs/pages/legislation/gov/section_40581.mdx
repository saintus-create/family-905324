# GOV § 40581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 40581

---

## Text

Pursuant to this chapter an authority may create a revenue bond indebtedness for the acquisition and construction of any improvements or property or facilities contained within its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
