# GOV § 21070.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 2. Second Tier Retirement—State

**Citation:** GOV § 21070.7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, Sections 21076, 21076.5, and 21077 shall not apply to service with the California National Guard or service as a National Guard member regardless of any prior membership status or previous election made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 526, Sec. 21. (SB 220) Effective January 1, 2014.)
