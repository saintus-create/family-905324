# GOV § 7920.000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Preliminary Provisions > Article: 1. Short Titles

**Citation:** GOV § 7920.000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the California Public Records Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
