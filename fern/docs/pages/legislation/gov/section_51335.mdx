# GOV § 51335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of City Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51335

---

## Text

If the city charter so authorizes, other functions may be transferred and performed as provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
