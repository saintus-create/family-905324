# GOV § 7927.700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 13. Private Records, Privileged Materials, and Other Records Protected by Law From Disclosure

**Citation:** GOV § 7927.700

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 7924.510, 7924.700, and 7929.610, this division does not require disclosure of personnel, medical, or similar files, the disclosure of which would constitute an unwarranted invasion of personal privacy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
