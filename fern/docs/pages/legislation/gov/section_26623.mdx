# GOV § 26623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1.1. Sheriff as Ex Officio Director of Emergency Services

**Citation:** GOV § 26623

---

## Text

In the event the board of supervisors elects to require that the sheriff perform the additional services authorized by this article it shall have the authority and it shall be its duty to provide by proper appropriations for any additional personnel, equipment, supplies or expenses made necessary thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1555.)
