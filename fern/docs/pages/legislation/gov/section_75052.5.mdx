# GOV § 75052.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 2.5. Community Property

**Citation:** GOV § 75052.5

---

## Text

If the nonmember withdraws accumulated contributions in accordance with Section 75052, the member may redeposit those contributions pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 892, Sec. 10. Effective October 14, 1991.)
