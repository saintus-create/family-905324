# GOV § 8682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 8682

---

## Text

The director shall administer this chapter. The director may delegate any power or duty vested in him or her under this chapter to a state agency or to any other officer or employee of the Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 186. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
