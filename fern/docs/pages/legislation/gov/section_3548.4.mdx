# GOV § 3548.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.7. Meeting and Negotiating in Public Educational Employment > Article: 9. Impasse Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 3548.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to prohibit the mediator appointed pursuant to Section 3548 from continuing mediation efforts on the basis of the findings of fact and recommended terms of settlement made pursuant to Section 3548.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 961.)
