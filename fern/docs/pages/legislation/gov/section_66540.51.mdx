# GOV § 66540.51

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 4. Funding

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.51

---

## Text

The authority may insure against any accident to or destruction of the public transportation ferry system or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
