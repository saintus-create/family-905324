# GOV § 12192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 3. Business Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 12192

---

## Text

The filing fees for a nonprofit mutual benefit ridesharing corporation are the following:

(a) Nonprofit mutual benefit corporation having as its sole purpose the operation of a single ridesharing vanpool vehicle designed for transporting at least seven persons, including the driver, under an arrangement in which ridesharing is incidental to another purpose of the driver: No fee.

(b) For purposes of this section ridesharing shall have the meaning specified in Section 522 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 1000, Sec. 54. Effective January 1, 2000.)
