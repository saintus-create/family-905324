# GOV § 14971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Emergencies

**Citation:** GOV § 14971

---

## Text

The department may perform any work required or take any remedial measures necessary to avert, alleviate, repair, or restore damage or destruction to property as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
