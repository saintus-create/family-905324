# GOV § 12011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12011

---

## Text

The Governor shall see that all offices are filled and their duties performed. If default occurs, the Governor shall apply such remedy as the law allows. If the remedy is imperfect, the Governor shall so advise the Legislature at its next session.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 5. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
