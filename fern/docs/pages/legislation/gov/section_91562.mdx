# GOV § 91562

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. CALIFORNIA INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 1. California Industrial Development Financing > Article: 3. Small Business Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 91562

---

## Text

A small business loan issued pursuant to this chapter, shall be for no less than seventy-five thousand dollars ($75,000), nor more than five hundred thousand ($500,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1166, Sec. 6.)
