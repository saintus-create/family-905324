# GOV § 14854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14854

---

## Text

The department shall decide upon the style and manner of printing all laws and other state documents except those printed for the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
