# GOV § 16360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 1.5. Federal Trust Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16360

---

## Text

The Federal Trust Fund is hereby created in the State Treasury. It consists of money which is paid into it in trust pursuant to law. Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, all money received by the state from the United States, the expenditure of which is administered through or under the direction of any state agency, shall, under such conditions as the Director of Finance may prescribe and on order of the State Controller, be deposited in the Federal Trust Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1284.)
