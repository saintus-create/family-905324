# GOV § 75610

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11.5. Judges’ Retirement System II > Article: 8. Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 75610

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, all expenses of administration of this article shall be paid by appropriation from the Judges’ Retirement System II Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 879, Sec. 11. Effective September 27, 1994. Operative November 9, 1994, by Sec. 16 of Ch. 879.)
