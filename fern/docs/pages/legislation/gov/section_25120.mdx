# GOV § 25120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 7. Ordinances

**Citation:** GOV § 25120

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances of the board of supervisors shall be as follows: “The Board of Supervisors of the County of ____ ordains as follows.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 985, Sec. 2.5.)
