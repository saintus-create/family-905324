# GOV § 6081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1.1. Publication of Notice in Public Notice Districts > Article: 1. Public Notice Districts, Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 6081

---

## Text

A notice published or posted in a manner that is contrary to the provisions of this chapter shall not be invalidated as a consequence of a reasonable, good faith error as to the applicable public notice district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 703, Sec. 13. (AB 2881) Effective January 1, 2017.)
