# GOV § 8952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 3. Joint Legislative Ethics Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8952

---

## Text

The filing of a complaint with the committee pursuant to this article suspends the running of the statute of limitations applicable to any violation of the provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 8920) while the complaint is pending.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 466, Sec. 59.)
