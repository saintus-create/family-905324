# GOV § 22960.66

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Participant Accounts

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.66

---

## Text

In the case of a contribution that is made under a mistake of fact, nothing in this part shall prohibit the return of that contribution within one year after discovery of the mistake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
