# GOV § 25383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 2. Jails in Other Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 25383

---

## Text

The consent of the board of supervisors of the county in which the jail is to be situated shall be first obtained and filed with the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
