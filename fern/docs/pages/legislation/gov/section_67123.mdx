# GOV § 67123

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 5. Finances

**Citation:** GOV § 67123

---

## Text

The agency is authorized to receive gifts, donations, subventions, grants, and other financial aids and funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
