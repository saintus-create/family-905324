# GOV § 54476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54476

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for the purchase out of any available funds of any outstanding notes, bonds, or other obligations, and the price at which, and the manner in which, the purchases may be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
