# GOV § 25021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 2. Chairman

**Citation:** GOV § 25021

## Text

The chairman of the board shall perform such duties as are prescribed by law or by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
