# GOV § 59380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 9. Hearing on Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 59380

---

## Text

As used in this article, “interested person” means an owner or any other person interested in any of the following:

(a) The proposed acquisition and cancellation of original bonds.

(b) The diagram.

(c) The assessment.

(d) The bondholders’ proposal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
