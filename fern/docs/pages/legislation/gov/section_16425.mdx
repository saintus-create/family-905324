# GOV § 16425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 9. Litigation Deposits Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16425

---

## Text

There is in the State Treasury the Litigation Deposits Fund, hereafter referred to as “the fund,” which is hereby appropriated for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1148.)
