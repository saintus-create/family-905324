# GOV § 68645.15

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 7. Ability to Pay Program

**Citation:** GOV § 68645.15

---

## Text

A defendant’s request for an ability-to-pay determination through the online tool shall not impact their eligibility to attend traffic violator school pursuant to Section 42005 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 191, Sec. 1. (SB 1096) Effective January 1, 2023.)
