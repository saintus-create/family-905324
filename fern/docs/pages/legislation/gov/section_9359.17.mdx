# GOV § 9359.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 6. Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 9359.17

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 9359, a legislative statutory officer who has attained the age of 55 shall be retired upon his or her written application therefor to the Board of Administration, and shall thereafter receive for life the retirement allowance provided by Section 9359.10.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 91.8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
