# GOV § 67473

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.75. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY, CHANNEL ISLANDS SITE AUTHORITY ACT

**Citation:** GOV § 67473

---

## Text

(a) The California State University, Channel Islands Site Authority is hereby established.

(b) The purpose of the authority shall be to provide a specific reuse plan for and to finance and support the transition of the property known as Camarillo State Hospital from its former use to a new state university campus and compatible uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 861, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Stats. 1998, Ch. 861, Sec. 5.)
