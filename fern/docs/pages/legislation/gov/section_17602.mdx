# GOV § 17602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 3. Reports to the Legislature

**Citation:** GOV § 17602

---

## Text

On or before January 15, 2007, and on or before each January 15 thereafter, the commission shall report to the Legislature the number of individual and consolidated incorrect reduction claims decided during the preceding calendar year and whether and why the reduction was upheld or overturned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 168, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
