# GOV § 18542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18542

---

## Text

“Rehabilitation” as used within this part shall mean a restoration of veterans declared to be 10 per cent or more disabled, either physically or mentally.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 45.)
