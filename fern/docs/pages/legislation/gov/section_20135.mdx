# GOV § 20135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 20135

---

## Text

To the extent possible, the board shall resolve conflicts between retirement systems applicable to state hospital employees transferred to county or local mental health programs as a result of state hospital closures or scheduled state hospital closures.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
