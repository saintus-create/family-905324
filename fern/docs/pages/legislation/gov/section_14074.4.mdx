# GOV § 14074.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5.4. San Joaquin Corridor

**Citation:** GOV § 14074.4

---

## Text

The authority shall be created only if the agencies that would be represented on the board enter into a joint exercise of powers agreement to form the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 801, Sec. 8. (AB 1779) Effective January 1, 2013.)
