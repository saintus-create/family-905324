# GOV § 12472.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 5. Uniform State Pay Roll System

**Citation:** GOV § 12472.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, on and after January 1, 2025, payments to employees made through the uniform state payroll system for master payroll paid on June 30 of each year shall be issue dated on July 1, provided that employees shall, in any event, be paid promptly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 52, Sec. 6. (AB 171) Effective July 2, 2024.)
