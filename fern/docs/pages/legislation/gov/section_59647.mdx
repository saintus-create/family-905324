# GOV § 59647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59647

---

## Text

Bond Plan A bonds shall be for the term and bear interest at the rate specified in the resolution of final confirmation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
