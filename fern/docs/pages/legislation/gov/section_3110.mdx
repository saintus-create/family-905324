# GOV § 3110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8.5. Volunteers > Article: 1. General

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California State Government Volunteers Act.
