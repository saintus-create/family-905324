# GOV § 13292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13292

---

## Text

When necessary, the department may require special reports from any such State or public agency. These special reports shall be filed with the department without delay.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 112.)
