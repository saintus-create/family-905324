# GOV § 22803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 4. Eligibility

**Citation:** GOV § 22803

---

## Text

An out-of-state employee shall be eligible for enrollment, in accordance with reasonable rules as the board may prescribe, to receive the benefits provided by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
