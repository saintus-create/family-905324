# GOV § 55641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 3. Rescue and Resuscitator Services

**Citation:** GOV § 55641

---

## Text

When a contract has been made, the Director of Forestry and Fire Protection may exercise the same powers and duties within the county for the provision of rescue and resuscitator services which by state or local law are conferred upon officers of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 427, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 1993.)
