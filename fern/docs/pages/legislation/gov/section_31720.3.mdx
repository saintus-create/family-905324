# GOV § 31720.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 10. Disability Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 31720.3

---

## Text

In determining whether a member is eligible to retire for disability, the board shall not consider medical opinion unless it is deemed competent and shall not use disability retirement as a substitute for the employer’s disciplinary process.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 370, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2009.)
