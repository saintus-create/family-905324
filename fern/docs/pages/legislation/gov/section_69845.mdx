# GOV § 69845

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 7. Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 69845

---

## Text

The clerk of the superior court may keep a register of actions in which shall be entered the title of each cause, with the date of its commencement and a memorandum of every subsequent proceeding in the action with its date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1030, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1995.)
