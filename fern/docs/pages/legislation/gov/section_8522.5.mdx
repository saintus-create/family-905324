# GOV § 8522.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. The Milton Marks Commission on California State Government Organization and Economy > Article: 2. Purposes and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8522.5

---

## Text

The commission shall review final reports and make recommendations to the Legislature and the Governor concerning the results of state audits and the revenues and expenditures of the state, its departments, subdivisions, agencies, and other public entities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 12, Sec. 6. Effective May 7, 1993.)
