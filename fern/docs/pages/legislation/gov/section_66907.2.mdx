# GOV § 66907.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Conservancy’s Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 66907.2

---

## Text

The conservancy is authorized to accept and hold real property or any interest therein acquired through gift, exchange, donation, or dedication.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 17. Effective September 17, 1984.)
