# GOV § 51030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Massage

**Citation:** GOV § 51030

---

## Text

The legislative body of a city for incorporated areas or county for unincorporated areas may enact an ordinance which provides for the licensing for regulation of the business of massage when carried on within the city or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1352.)
