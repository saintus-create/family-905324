# GOV § 53389

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 5. Senior Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53389

---

## Text

The legislative body shall notify the county auditor if the voters approve the issuance of the bonds pursuant to the election conducted in accordance with Section 53388.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
