# GOV § 50561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 9. Sale of Improvements Financed by Special Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 50561

---

## Text

The legislative body shall sell the improvement to the highest responsible bidder or may reject any or all bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1923.)
