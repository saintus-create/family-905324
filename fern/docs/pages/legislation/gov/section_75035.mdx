# GOV § 75035

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 2. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 75035

---

## Text

“Resignation,” as used in this article, does not include a resignation, express or implied, which is for the purpose of accepting an elective or appointive public office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 853.)
