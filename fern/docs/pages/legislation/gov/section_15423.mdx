# GOV § 15423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15423

---

## Text

The State Public Defender is authorized to appear as a friend of the court and may appear in a legislative, administrative or other similar proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1125.)
