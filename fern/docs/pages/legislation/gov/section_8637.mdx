# GOV § 8637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 15. Preservation of Local Government

**Citation:** GOV § 8637

---

## Text

Each political subdivision may provide for the succession of officers who head departments having duties in the maintenance of law and order or in the furnishing of public services relating to health and safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
