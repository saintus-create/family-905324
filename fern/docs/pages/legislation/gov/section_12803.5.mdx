# GOV § 12803.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 12803.5

---

## Text

The Governor, upon recommendation of the Secretary of the Health and Welfare Agency, may appoint not to exceed two deputies for the secretary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 432.)
