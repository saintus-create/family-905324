# GOV § 22151.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 2.5. Division of Retirement Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 22151.1

---

## Text

A county superintendent of schools whose employees are deemed county employees may request a division and give notice of a division in anticipation of a finding that his office is a separate public agency. Upon such request, the county superintendent’s office shall be deemed to be a public agency for purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 316.)
