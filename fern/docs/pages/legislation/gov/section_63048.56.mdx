# GOV § 63048.56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 2. California Infrastructure and Economic Development Bank > Article: 6.3. Safe Drinking Water State Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 63048.56

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, Article 3 (commencing with Section 63040), Article 4 (commencing with Section 63042), and Article 5 (commencing with Section 63043) shall not apply to any financing provided by the bank to, or at the request of, the department in connection with the revolving fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 518, Sec. 3. (AB 1292) Effective January 1, 2012.)
