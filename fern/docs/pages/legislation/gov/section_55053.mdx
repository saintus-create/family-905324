# GOV § 55053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 3. Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 55053

---

## Text

The legislative body shall require such bonds as it deems best from the successful bidder to insure the faithful performance of the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
