# GOV § 6512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6512

---

## Text

The agreement shall provide that after the completion of its purpose, any surplus money on hand shall be returned in proportion to the contributions made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 84.)
