# GOV § 38754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5.5. Transfer of City Waterworks Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 38754

---

## Text

The ordinance shall be published weekly for at least four weeks in a newspaper published in the city, or if there is no such newspaper it shall be posted in three public places in the city for three weeks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 329.)
