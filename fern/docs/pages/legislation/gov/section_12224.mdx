# GOV § 12224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12224

---

## Text

The Secretary of State may receive into the archives an item that the Secretary of State deems to be of historical value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 53. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
