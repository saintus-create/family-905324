# GOV § 41801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 7. City Attorney

**Citation:** GOV § 41801

---

## Text

The city attorney shall advise the city officials in all legal matters pertaining to city business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
