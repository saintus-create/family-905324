# GOV § 22970.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. The Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.43

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 13340, all moneys in the fund are continuously appropriated, without regard to fiscal years or plan years, to the board to carry out the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
