# GOV § 30029.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6.3. Local Revenue Fund 2011

**Citation:** GOV § 30029.12

---

## Text

Any schedule developed by the Department of Finance pursuant to this chapter and transmitted to the Controller shall also be transmitted by the Department of Finance to the appropriate legislative fiscal committees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 40, Sec. 28. (SB 1020) Effective June 27, 2012.)
