# GOV § 12988

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Enforcement and Hearing Procedures > Article: 2. Housing Discrimination

**Citation:** GOV § 12988

---

## Text

The department may engage in affirmative actions with owners in furtherance of the purpose of this part as expressed in Section 12920.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 62. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012. Operative January 1, 2013, by Sec. 140 of Ch. 46.)
