# GOV § 55111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 5. Sewage Treatment and Disposal Plants

**Citation:** GOV § 55111

---

## Text

The contracts may provide for the maintenance and operation of the plant or works and the percentages of the maintenance and operation cost to be paid by the local agencies entering into the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
