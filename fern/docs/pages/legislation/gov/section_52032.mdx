# GOV § 52032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 2. Creation of Community Energy Authorities

**Citation:** GOV § 52032

---

## Text

The area of operation of an authority created pursuant to Section 52031 shall be the combined possible areas of operation of participating cities and counties, as determined by Section 52007.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
