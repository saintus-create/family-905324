# GOV § 82026

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82026

---

## Text

“Filer” means the person filing or required to file any statement or report under this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
