# GOV § 54090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4. Beach Access

**Citation:** GOV § 54090

---

## Text

As used in this article “public beach” means any beach area used for recreational purposes which is owned, operated or controlled by the State, any state agency or any local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1595.)
