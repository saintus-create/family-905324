# GOV § 56020.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56020.6

---

## Text

“Certificate of filing” means the document issued by the executive officer that confirms an application for a change of organization or reorganization has met submission requirements and is accepted for filing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 12. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
