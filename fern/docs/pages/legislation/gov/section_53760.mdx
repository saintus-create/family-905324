# GOV § 53760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 5. Bankruptcy

**Citation:** GOV § 53760

---

## Text

A local public entity in this state may file a petition and exercise powers pursuant to applicable federal bankruptcy law if either of the following apply:(a) The local public entity has participated in a neutral evaluation process pursuant to Section 53760.3.

(b) The local public entity declares a fiscal emergency and adopts a resolution by a majority vote of the governing board pursuant to Section 53760.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 675, Sec. 2. (AB 506) Effective January 1, 2012.)
