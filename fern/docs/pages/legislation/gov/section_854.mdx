# GOV § 854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Medical, Hospital and Public Health Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 854

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, unless the context otherwise requires, “medical facility” includes a hospital, infirmary, clinic, dispensary, mental institution, or similar facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
