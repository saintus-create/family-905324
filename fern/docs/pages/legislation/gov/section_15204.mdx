# GOV § 15204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Administration of Justice

## Text

The State Controller may establish rules and regulations to carry out the purposes of this chapter.
