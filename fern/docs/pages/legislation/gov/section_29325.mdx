# GOV § 29325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 2. Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29325

---

## Text

The officer may be authorized to use the fund for making change, when necessary in carrying on his official work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
