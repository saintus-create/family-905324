# GOV § 24100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Deputies

**Citation:** GOV § 24100

---

## Text

Whenever the official name of any principal officer is used in any law conferring power or imposing duties or liabilities, it includes deputies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
