# GOV § 25549.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 10. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** GOV § 25549.4

---

## Text

Sections 25521 to 25535, inclusive, shall not apply to leases or agreements made pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 341.)
