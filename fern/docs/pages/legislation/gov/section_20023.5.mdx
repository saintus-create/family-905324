# GOV § 20023.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 20023.5

---

## Text

“County retirement system” means a retirement system established under Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 31450) of Part 3 of Division 4 of Title 3, the County Employees Retirement Law of 1937.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1997.)
