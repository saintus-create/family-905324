# GOV § 23330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23330

---

## Text

Upon certification of a petition, the board of the principal county shall forthwith transmit a copy of the petition certification to the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
