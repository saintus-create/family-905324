# GOV § 54060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 3. Coliseum and Stadium Parking

**Citation:** GOV § 54060

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means city, county, or district agricultural association.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
