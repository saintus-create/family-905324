# GOV § 37107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37107

---

## Text

The judge shall issue an attachment directed to the sheriff of the county where the witness was required to appear, commanding the sheriff to attach the person, and forthwith bring the person before the judge.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 22. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
