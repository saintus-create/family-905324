# GOV § 68932

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 3. The Supreme Court > Article: 4. Fees in the Supreme Court and Courts of Appeal

**Citation:** GOV § 68932

---

## Text

For all other services not specified in this article the fee shall be the same as that prescribed by law for similar services by notaries public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 873.)
