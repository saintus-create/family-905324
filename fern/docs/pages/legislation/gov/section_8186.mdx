# GOV § 8186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 3. Capitol Area Redevelopment

**Citation:** GOV § 8186

---

## Text

The City of Sacramento shall not adopt a plan containing the provisions of Section 33670 of the Health and Safety Code with respect to any portion of the project area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 953.)
