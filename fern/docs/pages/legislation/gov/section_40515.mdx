# GOV § 40515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 40515

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, firm, copartnership, association, corporation, trust, business trust or receiver or trustee or conservator for any thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
