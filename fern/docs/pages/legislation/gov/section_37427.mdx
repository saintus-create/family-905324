# GOV § 37427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 3. Sale of Buildings and Sites

**Citation:** GOV § 37427

---

## Text

The legislative body may adopt an ordinance calling and fixing the date of a special election at which the proposition of sale of the property will be submitted to the city electors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
