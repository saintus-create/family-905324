# GOV § 11548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.6. Department of Technology

**Citation:** GOV § 11548

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the State Compensation Insurance Fund, the Legislature, or the Legislative Data Center in the Legislative Counsel Bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 183, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2008.)
