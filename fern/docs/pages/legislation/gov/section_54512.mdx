# GOV § 54512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 6. Duties of Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 54512

---

## Text

The local agency shall pay or cause to be paid the principal and interest of the bonds on the date, at the place, and in the manner mentioned in the bonds and coupons and in accordance with the resolution authorizing their issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
