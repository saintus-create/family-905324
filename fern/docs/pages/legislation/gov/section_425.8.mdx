# GOV § 425.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.8

## Text

The Chipped Stone Bear (bear-shaped eccentric) is the official state prehistoric artifact.
