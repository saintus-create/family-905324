# GOV § 58032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 2. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** GOV § 58032

---

## Text

The petition may be filed in sections. Each section shall comply with all the requirements for a petition, except that it need not contain all the signatures required for the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
