# GOV § 68500.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68500.3

---

## Text

Any reference to the Administrative Office of the Courts in state law means the Judicial Council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 36, Sec. 8. (AB 452) Effective January 1, 2018.)
