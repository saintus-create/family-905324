# GOV § 31485.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31485.5

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that counties that are considering the adoption of defined contribution plans, also consider having those plans administered by their county retirement systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1108, Sec. 3.5. Effective October 14, 1991.)
