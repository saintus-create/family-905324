# GOV § 22960.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.20

---

## Text

“Employer contribution rate” means the percentage of the participant’s compensation to be contributed by the employer to the participant’s account in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
