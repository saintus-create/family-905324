# GOV § 19050.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19050.3

---

## Text

Transfer of an employee from a position under one appointing power to a position under another appointing power may be made, subject to board rule.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19994.5 by Stats. 1987, Ch. 316, Sec. 6.)
