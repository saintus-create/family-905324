# GOV § 52101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 52101

---

## Text

An authority may, subject to the approval of the governing body, do all of the following:

(a) Sue and be sued.

(b) Have a seal and alter it.

(c) Have a perpetual succession.

(d) Make and execute contracts and other instruments necessary or convenient to the exercise of its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
