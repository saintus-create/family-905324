# GOV § 29710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Claims > Article: 1. Filing and Approval

**Citation:** GOV § 29710

---

## Text

No fee or charge shall be made or collected by any officer for verifying or receiving any claim against the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 29721 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1725.)
