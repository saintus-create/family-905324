# GOV § 66905.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 66905.6

## Text

“Conservancy” means the California Tahoe Conservancy.
