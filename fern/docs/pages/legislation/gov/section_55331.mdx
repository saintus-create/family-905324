# GOV § 55331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Joint Projects for Sanitation and Electrical Lines > Article: 2. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Conduits and Electrical Lines

**Citation:** GOV § 55331

---

## Text

The legislative body of each local agency may bind or obligate it to pay a proportionate part of the construction and maintenance costs of the conduits or lines at the times and in the installments so approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
