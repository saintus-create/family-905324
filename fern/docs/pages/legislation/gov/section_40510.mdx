# GOV § 40510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 40510

---

## Text

“Contract” means a contract entered into by the authority for the purchase or other acquisition of surplus real property under the Federal Property and Administration Services Act of 1949 or other federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
