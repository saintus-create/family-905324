# GOV § 3208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.5. Political Activities of Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 3208

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 19990, the limitations set forth in this chapter shall be the only restrictions on the political activities of state employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 142, Sec. 25.)
