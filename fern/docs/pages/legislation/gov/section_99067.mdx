# GOV § 99067

**Hierarchy:** Title: 18. THE ECONOMIC RECOVERY BOND ACT > Chapter: 4. Bond Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 99067

---

## Text

For purposes of this title, the Department of Finance is designated the “board” as that term is used in the State General Obligation Bond Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, 5th Ex. Sess., Ch. 2, Sec. 3. Approved in Proposition 57 at the March 2, 2004, election.)
