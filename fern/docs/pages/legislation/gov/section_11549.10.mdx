# GOV § 11549.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.7. Office of Information Security and Office of Privacy Protection > Article: 2. Office of Privacy Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 11549.10

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the State Compensation Insurance Fund, the Legislature, or the Legislative Data Center in the Legislative Counsel Bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11549.6 by Stats. 2010, Ch. 404, Sec. 32. (AB 2408) Effective January 1, 2011.)
