# GOV § 50668.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50668.4

---

## Text

All pledged revenue shall be deposited in the special fund and payments shall be made therefrom in the order specified in subdivisions (a) to (c), inclusive, of Section 50665.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1199, Sec. 1.)
