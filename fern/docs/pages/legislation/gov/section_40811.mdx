# GOV § 40811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. City Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 40811

---

## Text

The city clerk is the custodian of the city seal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
