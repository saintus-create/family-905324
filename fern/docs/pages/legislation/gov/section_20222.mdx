# GOV § 20222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 7. Records and Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 20222

---

## Text

The comptroller of the university, or any other official who the university may designate, shall furnish monthly reports to the board showing changes in the status of all members employed by the university during the preceding month, and shall furnish any additional information concerning any members that the board may require in the administration of this system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
