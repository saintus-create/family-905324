# GOV § 18538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18538

---

## Text

“Part” means this part, Part 2.5 (commencing with Section 19800), and Part 2.6 (commencing with Section 19815) and those portions of Part 1 (commencing with Section 18000) that confer powers or impose duties on the board or the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 14. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
