# GOV § 53992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 18. Local Tax Allocation Agreements to Implement Community Facilities Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 53992

---

## Text

A local agency and state agency may enter into an agreement to coordinate the priority or allocation of state funds to the local agency or state construction projects in order to implement the financial component of the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 396, Sec. 2. Effective July 27, 1983.)
