# GOV § 36936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Ordinances > Article: 2. Enactment

**Citation:** GOV § 36936

---

## Text

Resolutions, orders for the payment of money, and all ordinances require a recorded majority vote of the total membership of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 454, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2003.)
