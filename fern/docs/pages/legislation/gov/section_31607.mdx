# GOV § 31607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 31607

---

## Text

To assist in carrying out its investment powers and duties the board may employ an attorney in private practice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1738, Sec. 26. Effective September 30, 1984.)
