# GOV § 14625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 14625

---

## Text

The director may require any person who has charge of, handles or has access to any state property to file an official bond in an amount to be fixed by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 13075 by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
