# GOV § 29065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 3. Recommended Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29065

---

## Text

On or before September 8 of each year, as the board directs, the recommended budget shall be made available to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 29. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
