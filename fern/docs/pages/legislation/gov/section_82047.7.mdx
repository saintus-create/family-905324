# GOV § 82047.7

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82047.7

---

## Text

“Proponent of a state ballot measure” means “proponent” as defined in Section 9001 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 317, Sec. 1. (AB 1590) Effective January 1, 2022.)
