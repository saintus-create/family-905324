# GOV § 71824

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 7.5. Trial Court Interpreter Employment and Labor Relations Act

**Citation:** GOV § 71824

---

## Text

A court interpreter may authorize, and the trial court employer shall honor, a dues deduction from his or her salary or wages as provided in Section 1152, 1153, 1157.1, 1157.2, 1157.3, 1157.4, 1157.5, or 1157.7.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 53, Sec. 38. (SB 866) Effective June 27, 2018.)
