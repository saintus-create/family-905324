# GOV § 53355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53355

---

## Text

A two-thirds vote shall be required for the issuance of bonds under authority of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 670, Sec. 102. Effective January 1, 2008.)
