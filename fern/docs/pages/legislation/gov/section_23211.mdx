# GOV § 23211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4. Minor Boundary Changes

**Citation:** GOV § 23211

---

## Text

Each county shall continue to provide services to the territory within its own boundaries until the effective date of the boundary change as specified in the ordinances adopted pursuant to Section 23210.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 702, Sec. 2.)
