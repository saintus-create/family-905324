# GOV § 53378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 3. Appointment of Board Members

**Citation:** GOV § 53378

---

## Text

If the legislative body adopts an ordinance to establish a district pursuant to Section 53375, the legislative body shall serve as ex officio directors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
