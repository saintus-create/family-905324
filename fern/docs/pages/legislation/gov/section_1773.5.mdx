# GOV § 1773.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Resignations and Vacancies > Article: 2. Vacancies

**Citation:** GOV § 1773.5

---

## Text

In addition to any other applicable provision of law, a vacancy occurs in the office of Representative in Congress in the event of his or her disappearance, as defined in subdivision (c) of Section 10731 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 658, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
