# GOV § 16371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 2. Special Deposit Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16371

---

## Text

Trust funds which have come into the possession of any agency of the State may be paid into the Special Deposit Fund in trust, subject to the right of recovery to fulfill the purposes of the trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 121.)
