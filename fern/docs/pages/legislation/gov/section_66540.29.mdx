# GOV § 66540.29

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.29

---

## Text

The authority may take by grant, purchase, devise, or lease or otherwise acquire, hold, enjoy, lease, and dispose of, real and personal property within or outside its area of jurisdiction in order to further its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
