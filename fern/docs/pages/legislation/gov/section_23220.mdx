# GOV § 23220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4. Minor Boundary Changes

**Citation:** GOV § 23220

---

## Text

On and after the effective date of the boundary change, the superior court in each affected county shall retain jurisdiction in all cases pending in a session of that court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 133. Effective January 1, 2003.)
