# GOV § 965.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Payment of Claims and Judgments Against the State

**Citation:** GOV § 965.4

---

## Text

The Governor shall report to the Legislature, at each session, all judgments or settlements against the State not theretofore reported.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
