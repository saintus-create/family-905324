# GOV § 399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Great Seal of the State

**Citation:** GOV § 399

---

## Text

(a) There shall be a seal of this state, which shall be called “the Great Seal of the State of California.”

(b) The Great Seal of the state shall be used by the Governor officially.
