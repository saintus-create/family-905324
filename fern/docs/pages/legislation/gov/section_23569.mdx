# GOV § 23569

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 5. Establishment of Consolidated Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 23569

---

## Text

All the officers elected at the election shall hold their offices until the time provided by general law for the election and qualification of such officers, and until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
