# GOV § 75108

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 6. Judges’ Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 75108

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision, all expenses of administration of this article shall be paid by appropriation from the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 639, Sec. 9. Effective September 1, 1983.)
