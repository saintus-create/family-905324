# GOV § 82000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82000

---

## Text

Unless the contrary is stated or clearly appears from the context, the definitions set forth in this chapter shall govern the interpretation of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
