# GOV § 29929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29929

---

## Text

The board may contract a bonded indebtedness for county purposes only as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
