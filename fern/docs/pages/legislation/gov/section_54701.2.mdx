# GOV § 54701.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 54701.2

---

## Text

A local agency may issue bonds pursuant to this chapter for the purpose of financing the construction of rental housing for employees and for the purpose of funding or refunding previously issued bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
