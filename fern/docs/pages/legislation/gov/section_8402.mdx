# GOV § 8402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.6. Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement > Article: 1. Establishment and Functions of the Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement

**Citation:** GOV § 8402

---

## Text

The work of the office shall be organized within the following offices or programs:

(a) CaliforniaVolunteers.

(b) The Office of Community Partnerships and Strategic Communications.

(c) The California Youth Empowerment Commission established by the California Youth Empowerment Act (Chapter 3.4 (commencing with Section 8261)).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 30. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024.)
