# GOV § 8231.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Notaries Public > Article: 2. Online Notarization

**Citation:** GOV § 8231.19

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this article is contrary to public policy and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 291, Sec. 8. (SB 696) Effective January 1, 2024.)
