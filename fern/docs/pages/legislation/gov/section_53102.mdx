# GOV § 53102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6. Local Emergency Telephone Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 53102

---

## Text

“Public safety agency,” as used in this article, means a functional division of a public agency which provides firefighting, police, medical, or other emergency services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1005.)
