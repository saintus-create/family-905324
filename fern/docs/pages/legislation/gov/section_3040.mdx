# GOV § 3040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 2. Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3040

---

## Text

If the offense for which the defendant is convicted on impeachment is also the subject of an indictment or information, the indictment or information is not barred thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
