# GOV § 425.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.10

---

## Text

Purple needlegrass, or Nassella pulchra, is the official State Grass.

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 243, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
