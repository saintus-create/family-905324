# GOV § 59107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 59107

---

## Text

“Lot” means land, lot, part of a lot, parcel, or piece of property and includes property owned or controlled by any person as a railroad right of way or as a street or interurban railroad right of way.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
