# GOV § 59642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59642

---

## Text

Under Bond Plan B or C bonds shall be issued to represent and be secured by the unpaid assessments upon the reassessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
