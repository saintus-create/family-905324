# GOV § 65071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2.3. Long-Range Transportation Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 65071

---

## Text

The department shall update the California Transportation Plan every five years consistent with this chapter. The second update shall be completed by December 31, 2020.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 605, Sec. 3. (AB 285) Effective January 1, 2020.)
