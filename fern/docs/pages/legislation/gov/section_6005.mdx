# GOV § 6005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 6005

---

## Text

“Printed” and “published” are not synonymous. Each relates to separate acts or functions necessary to constitute a newspaper of general circulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
