# GOV § 67559

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67559

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause requiring the commission to preserve and protect the security of the bonds and the rights of the holders thereof and to warrant and defend such rights.
