# GOV § 5923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Public Finance Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 5923

---

## Text

(a) To the extent that this chapter is inconsistent with any other general statute or special act or parts thereof, now or hereafter enacted, this chapter is controlling.

(b) This chapter shall be liberally construed to effect its purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 846, Sec. 1.)
