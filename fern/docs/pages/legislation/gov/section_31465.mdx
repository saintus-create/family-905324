# GOV § 31465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31465

---

## Text

“Additional contributions” means contributions made by members in addition to normal contributions under Section 31627.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 275, Sec. 30. (AB 2101) Effective January 1, 2021.)
