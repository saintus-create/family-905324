# GOV § 11900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. California Education Interagency Council

**Citation:** GOV § 11900

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Education Interagency Council Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 446, Sec. 1. (AB 1098) Effective January 1, 2026.)
