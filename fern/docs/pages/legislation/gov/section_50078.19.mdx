# GOV § 50078.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.6. Fire Suppression Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 50078.19

---

## Text

This article does not limit or prohibit the levy or collection of any other fee, charge, assessment, or tax for fire suppression services authorized by any other provisions of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1396, Sec. 8.5.)
