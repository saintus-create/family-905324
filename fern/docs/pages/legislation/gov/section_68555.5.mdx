# GOV § 68555.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 3. Coordinated Educational Programs for the Judiciary

**Citation:** GOV § 68555.5

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall report to the Legislature and the relevant policy committees, on or before January 1, 2025, and each January thereafter, on the trainings for judicial officers provided pursuant to Section 68555. The report shall include both of the following:

(a) The titles of the training courses being offered by Judicial Council.

(b) The number of judicial officers that participated in each training.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 865, Sec. 5. (SB 331) Effective January 1, 2024.)
