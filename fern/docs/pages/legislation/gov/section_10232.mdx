# GOV § 10232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 10232

---

## Text

Upon request, the Legislative Counsel shall advise any State agency as to the preparation of measures to be submitted to the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
