# GOV § 12500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 12500

---

## Text

The annual salary of the Attorney General includes all services rendered ex officio as member of any board or commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 142, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 2008.)
