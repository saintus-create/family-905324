# GOV § 55300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Joint Projects for Sanitation and Electrical Lines > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 55300

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “local agency” means county, city, and sanitary district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
