# GOV § 31881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 17. Integration with Federal Old Age, Survivors, and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31881

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided in this article, all of the provisions of Article 13 of this chapter (commencing with Section 31800) apply to, and are incorporated by reference in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2090.)
