# GOV § 65028.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65028.1

## Text

“Council,” as used in this chapter, means the Planning Advisory and Assistance Council established pursuant to subdivision (a) of Section 65040.6.
