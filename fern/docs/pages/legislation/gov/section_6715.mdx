# GOV § 6715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6715

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim March 30 as Welcome Home Vietnam Veterans Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 158, Sec. 3. (AB 717) Effective January 1, 2010.)
