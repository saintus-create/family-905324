# GOV § 68500.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68500.1

---

## Text

The Judicial Council may prescribe the methods, means, and standards for electronic collection of data related to court administration, practice, and procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 387, Sec. 2.)
