# GOV § 33004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. County Peace Officers and Fire Service Retirement, Disability and Death Benefit Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 33004

---

## Text

Subject only to the limitations of this chapter, any county may, by ordinance adopted by a four-fifths (45) vote of the board of supervisors, or by a majority vote of the qualified electors of said county, establish and maintain a retirement, disability and death benefit plan for county peace officers, foresters, fire wardens, and firemen.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1479.)
