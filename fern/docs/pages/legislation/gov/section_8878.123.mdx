# GOV § 8878.123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.47. Earthquake Safety and Public Buildings Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1990 > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8878.123

---

## Text

The administrative provisions of Article 3 (commencing with Section 8878.60) and Article 4 (commencing with Section 8878.95) may be amended by statute without submission to the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 122 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
