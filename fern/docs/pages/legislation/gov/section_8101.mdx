# GOV § 8101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Nevada Compact

**Citation:** GOV § 8101

---

## Text

No compact or agreement is binding on the State of California until it has been approved by the Legislature of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
