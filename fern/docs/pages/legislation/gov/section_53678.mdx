# GOV § 53678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53678

---

## Text

The charges for the handling and safekeeping of any such securities are not a charge against the treasurer but shall be paid by the depository owning the securities.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 349.)
