# GOV § 14829.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. California State Contracts Register

**Citation:** GOV § 14829.1

---

## Text

The Department of General Services may adopt rules and regulations consistent with the law for the purpose of carrying into effect this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 823, Sec. 3.5.)
