# GOV § 25633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25633

---

## Text

In the exercise of its powers and the performance of its duties, the board of forestry shall not interfere with the jurisdiction of the board of supervisors over and in the improvement, care, and general control of the roads, highways, grounds, and property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
