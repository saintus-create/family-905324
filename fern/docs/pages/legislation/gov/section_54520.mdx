# GOV § 54520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 6. Duties of Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 54520

---

## Text

The local agency shall keep proper books of record and accounts of the enterprise, separate from all other records and accounts, in which complete and correct entries shall be made of all transactions relating to the enterprise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
