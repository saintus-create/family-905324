# GOV § 26777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 8. Transportation of Prisoners

**Citation:** GOV § 26777

---

## Text

In any case in which a sheriff, acting pursuant to a contract entered into under this article, takes a prisoner into custody and transports him, such action shall be deemed lawful to the same extent as if he were the sheriff for whom he is performing such service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1138.)
