# GOV § 72708

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 9. Los Angeles County

**Citation:** GOV § 72708

---

## Text

This chapter applies to proceedings in the Los Angeles County Superior Court that would have been within the jurisdiction of the former Municipal Court of the Los Angeles Judicial District as of January 21, 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 398. Effective January 1, 2003.)
