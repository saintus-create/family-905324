# GOV § 8687.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 4. Allocations to Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 8687.9

---

## Text

Funding and financial assistance provided to local governments in response to an emergency, as that term is used in Section 8558, is not subject to the eligibility restrictions of Section 1782 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 2, Sec. 4. (AB 92) Effective March 27, 2015.)
