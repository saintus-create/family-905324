# GOV § 62461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. Downtown Revitalization and Economic Recovery Financing Districts > Title: 6. DISTRICTS

**Citation:** GOV § 62461

---

## Text

All costs incurred by the local government in connection with the division of taxes pursuant to this division for the district shall be paid by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 642, Sec. 12. (AB 1445) Effective January 1, 2026.)
