# GOV § 45344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Retirement > Article: 2. Pension and Retirement Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 45344

---

## Text

Benefits based on service rendered prior to membership in the system shall be met by additional contributions of the employer. Such prior service liability may be funded over a fixed period of years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
