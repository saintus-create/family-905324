# GOV § 940.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 940.4

---

## Text

“Local public entity” includes a county, city, district, public authority, public agency, and any other political subdivision or public corporation in the State, but does not include the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
