# GOV § 59352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 8. Notice of Assessment Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 59352

---

## Text

The notice shall:

(a) Recite that a bondholders’ proposal has been filed.

(b) Briefly indicate its terms.

(c) Refer to the resolution of intention, the bondholders’ proposal, the diagram, and assessment on file for further particulars.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
