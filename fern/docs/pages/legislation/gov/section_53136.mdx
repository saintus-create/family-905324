# GOV § 53136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Federally Mandated Audits of Block Grant Funds Allocated to Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 53136

---

## Text

The Controller shall assure that audits conducted in accordance with this article meet the federal audit requirements provided pursuant to block grant laws and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1343, Sec. 10.)
