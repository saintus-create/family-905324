# GOV § 58030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 2. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** GOV § 58030

---

## Text

When the principal act authorizes or requires a petition, formation proceedings shall be commenced by petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
