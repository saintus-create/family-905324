# GOV § 56076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56076

---

## Text

“Sphere of influence” means a plan for the probable physical boundaries and service area of a local agency, as determined by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1307, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
