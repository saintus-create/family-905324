# GOV § 59118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 59118

---

## Text

“New district” means the assessment district proposed to be created, or created, pursuant to this chapter, and which is, or will be, assessed to pay the cost of the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
