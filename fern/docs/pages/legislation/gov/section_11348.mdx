# GOV § 11348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 5. Public Participation: Procedure for Adoption of Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 11348

---

## Text

Each agency subject to this chapter shall keep its rulemaking records on all of that agency’s pending rulemaking actions, in which the notice has been published in the California Regulatory Notice Register, current and in one central location.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1059, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2001.)
