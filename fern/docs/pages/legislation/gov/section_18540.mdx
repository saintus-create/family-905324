# GOV § 18540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18540

---

## Text

“Armed forces” means the United States Air Force, Army, Navy, Marine Corps, Space Force, and Coast Guard.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 379, Sec. 9. (AB 1715) Effective January 1, 2023.)
