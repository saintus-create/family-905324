# GOV § 9760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 4. Preparation and Printing of Statutes

**Citation:** GOV § 9760

---

## Text

Whenever a law is signed by the Governor, official notice of the fact shall be forwarded, in writing, to the State Printing Office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
