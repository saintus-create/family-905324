# GOV § 202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Sovereignty of the State > Article: 5. Rights Over Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 202

---

## Text

The state may imprison or confine for the protection of the public peace or health or of individual life or safety.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 20.)
