# GOV § 977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Payment of Judgments Against Local Public Entities > Article: 2. Funding Judgments With Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 977

---

## Text

The board shall prescribe the form of the bonds. The bonds may be issued in denominations not to exceed one thousand dollars ($1,000) and not less than one hundred dollars ($100). The board shall fix, and designate in the bonds, a time and place for payment of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
