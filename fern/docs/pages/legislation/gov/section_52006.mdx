# GOV § 52006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 52006

---

## Text

“Governing body” means the city council in the case of a city or the board of supervisors in the case of a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
