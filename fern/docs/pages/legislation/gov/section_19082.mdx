# GOV § 19082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 2. Limited Term

**Citation:** GOV § 19082

---

## Text

The department may provide for the establishment, maintenance, and use of preferred limited-term lists.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 57. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
