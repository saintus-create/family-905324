# GOV § 1462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 1462

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged by any officer to file or record any official bond of any official of this state or any political subdivision of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1105.)
