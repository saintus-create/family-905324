# GOV § 22013.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22013.2

---

## Text

“Policeman” as used in this part also includes members of the Department of the California Highway Patrol who are designated as peace officers under subdivision (a) of Section 2250.1 of the Vehicle Code and whose principal duties consist of active law enforcement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 305, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 1997.)
