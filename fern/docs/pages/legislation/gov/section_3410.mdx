# GOV § 3410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.9. California Golden Shield Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Golden Shield Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 462, Sec. 1. (AB 671) Effective January 1, 2010.)
