# GOV § 10230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 10230

---

## Text

The Legislative Counsel shall be in attendance upon all regular and special sessions of the Legislature.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 7.)
