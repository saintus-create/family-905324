# GOV § 6575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 6575

---

## Text

All bonds and the interest thereon or income therefrom are exempt from all taxation in this State other than gift, inheritance and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 329.)
