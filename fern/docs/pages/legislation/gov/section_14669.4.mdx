# GOV § 14669.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. State Property

**Citation:** GOV § 14669.4

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14669, the director may enter into a lease-purchase agreement, or a lease with an option to purchase, with an initial option purchase price which may exceed two million dollars ($2,000,000), to provide for a California Conservation Corps facility and office space in the City of Fortuna in Humboldt County.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1003, Sec. 1. Effective September 29, 1989.)
