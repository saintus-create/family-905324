# GOV § 5901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 11. Public Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 5901

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that state and local governments be provided with the powers and flexibility necessary and appropriate for them to access the market for bonds which bear interest subject to federal income taxation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1389, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1986.)
