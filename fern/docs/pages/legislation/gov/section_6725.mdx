# GOV § 6725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6725

---

## Text

The Governor annually shall proclaim October 25 as Larry Itliong Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 29, Sec. 3. (AB 7) Effective January 1, 2016.)
