# GOV § 54462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54462

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for the amount and kind of insurance on the enterprise including insurance against:

(a) Accident to or destruction of any enterprise from any or all risks.

(b) Loss of revenues from an enterprise.

(c) Public liability or property damage and workmen’s compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
