# GOV § 56024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56024

---

## Text

“City council” means the legislative body or governing board of a city.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 16. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
