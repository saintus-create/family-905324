# GOV § 17213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17213

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Controller is the agent for sale for registered warrants, reimbursement warrants, and refunding warrants issued pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 12, Sec. 2. Effective June 2, 2003.)
