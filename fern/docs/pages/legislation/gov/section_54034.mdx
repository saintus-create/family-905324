# GOV § 54034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 2. Automobile Parking Areas

**Citation:** GOV § 54034

---

## Text

In the manner other public property is leased or sublet, a local agency may lease or sublet any such property for the purpose of operating or authorizing the operation of a parking station for the parking of automobiles by the public.

It may also grant franchises authorizing such use of the property for parking by the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
