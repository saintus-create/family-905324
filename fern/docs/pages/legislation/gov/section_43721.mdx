# GOV § 43721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43721

---

## Text

By a two-thirds vote of its number, the legislative body may fund or refund the indebtedness at, after, or before maturity and issue bonds of the city for the indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
