# GOV § 4300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Preference for Materials > Article: 1. American-Made Materials

**Citation:** GOV § 4300

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “United States” means the United States of America, and includes any Territory or insular possession of the United States.

(b) “Produced” includes mined and manufactured.

(c) “Materials” includes articles and supplies.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
