# GOV § 51285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 5. Cancellation

**Citation:** GOV § 51285

---

## Text

The owner of any property located in the county or city in which the agricultural preserve is situated may protest such cancellation to the city or county conducting the hearing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1372.)
