# GOV § 8270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.4. California Youth Empowerment Act > Article: 4. Purposes, Duties, and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 8270

---

## Text

The commission shall conduct full commission meetings at least every other month, with the first meeting on or before September 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 25. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 8276.)
