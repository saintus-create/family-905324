# GOV § 23395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 7. Courts

**Citation:** GOV § 23395

---

## Text

In a proposed county there is one judge of the superior court who shall be selected as provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
