# GOV § 53238.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 2.4.6. Fiscal and Financial Training

**Citation:** GOV § 53238.4

---

## Text

A local agency official who is in compliance with Section 26945, 26945.1, 27000.7, 27000.8, or 27000.9 is exempt from the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 661, Sec. 4. (SB 827) Effective January 1, 2026.)
