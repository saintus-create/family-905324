# GOV § 6721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6721

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim May 22 as Harvey Milk Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 626, Sec. 3. (SB 572) Effective January 1, 2010.)
