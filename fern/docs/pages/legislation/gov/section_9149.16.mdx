# GOV § 9149.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 9. The Historic State Capitol Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.16

---

## Text

The commission annually shall report to the Legislature on its activities. The commission shall propose to the Legislature such recommendations for legislation in connection with the historic State Capitol as the commission determines to be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1757, Sec. 8. Effective October 1, 1984.)
