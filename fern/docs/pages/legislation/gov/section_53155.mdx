# GOV § 53155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 8. Costs of Emergency Response

**Citation:** GOV § 53155

---

## Text

In no event shall a person’s liability under this article for the expense of an emergency response exceed twelve thousand dollars ($12,000) for a particular incident.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 51, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
