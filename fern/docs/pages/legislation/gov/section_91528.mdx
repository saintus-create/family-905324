# GOV § 91528

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. CALIFORNIA INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 1. California Industrial Development Financing > Article: 2. Industrial Development Authorities

**Citation:** GOV § 91528

---

## Text

An authority shall not have the power to operate any facility as a business, except in the event of a default on any bonds, through a trustee, for the minimum reasonable time needed to sell or otherwise dispose of the facility in accordance with the resolution or indenture.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1358.)
