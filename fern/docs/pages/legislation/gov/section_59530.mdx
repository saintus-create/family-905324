# GOV § 59530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 14. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 59530

---

## Text

To aid in refunding the indebtedness of a district, entirely or partly within a city or unincorporated county territory, any city in which all or part of the district lies and the county in which it lies may:

(a) Appropriate money from any available fund.

(b) Contribute money owed to it for delinquent taxes upon property within the district by canceling such taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
