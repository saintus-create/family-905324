# GOV § 43770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 43770

---

## Text

Taxes for the payment of the bonds shall be levied pursuant to Article 1 except where any issue of bonds pursuant to this article is to mature at one time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
