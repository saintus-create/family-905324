# GOV § 40591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 8. Financial

**Citation:** GOV § 40591

---

## Text

It is the intention of this chapter that the authority shall pay all of the costs that it may incur from revenues derived from the sale, leasing and operation of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
