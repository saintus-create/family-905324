# GOV § 18577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 18577

---

## Text

Whenever this part refers to “board rules,” “rules of the board,” “rules,” or makes a similar reference, that reference authorizes the board to make rules concerning the subject matter for which that reference is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 53, Sec. 20. (SB 866) Effective June 27, 2018.)
