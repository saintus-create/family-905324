# GOV § 51561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51561

---

## Text

The compensation paid the county for the services shall be filed by agreement between the board of supervisors and the city legislative body. Not more than 1 percent shall be charged for collecting the first twenty-five thousand dollars ($25,000), and not more than one-fourth of 1 percent for all sums over that amount. The compensation shall be paid into the county salary fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1994.)
