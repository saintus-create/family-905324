# GOV § 20060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Retirement” means the granting of a retirement allowance under this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
