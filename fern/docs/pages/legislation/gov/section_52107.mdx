# GOV § 52107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 52107

---

## Text

All energy projects shall be consistent with all state, regional, and local planning, zoning, sanitary, safety, and building laws, ordinances, and regulations applicable to the jurisdiction in which the project is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
