# GOV § 50079.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.8. School Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 50079.5

---

## Text

This article shall not be construed to affect the authority of any community facilities district to levy any special tax or other charge under Chapter 2.5 (commencing with Section 53311) of Division 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1365, Sec. 3.)
