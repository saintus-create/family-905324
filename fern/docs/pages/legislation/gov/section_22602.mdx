# GOV § 22602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Administration of Old Age and Survivors’ Insurance Program > Article: 3. OASI Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 22602

---

## Text

With the exception of penalties and interest collected pursuant to Section 22556, and except as provided in subdivision (b) of Section 22600, moneys received by the board from public agencies under the provisions of this part may be deposited in the Old Age and Survivors’ Insurance Revolving Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 24, Sec. 23. (SB 83) Effective June 27, 2019.)
