# GOV § 82050

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82050

---

## Text

“State candidate” means a candidate who seeks nomination or election to any elective state office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
