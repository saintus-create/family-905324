# GOV § 12993.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 12993.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 12993, nothing contained in this part shall be construed, in any manner or way, to limit or restrict the application of Section 51.7 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 911, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1993.)
