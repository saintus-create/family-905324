# GOV § 53859.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 7.7. Grant Anticipation Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 53859.01

---

## Text

The powers conferred by this article are in addition to and alternative to any powers conferred by any other law for borrowing by a local agency and any amount borrowed hereunder shall not be considered in any limitation on the amount which may be borrowed by any such local agency under any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 552.)
