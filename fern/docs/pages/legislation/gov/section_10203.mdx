# GOV § 10203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 10203

---

## Text

The Legislative Counsel shall be chosen without reference to party affiliations and solely on the ground of fitness to perform the duties of his or her office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 62, Sec. 114. Effective January 1, 2004.)
