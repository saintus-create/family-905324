# GOV § 51191.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.9. Solar-Use Easement > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51191.4

---

## Text

No deed or other instrument described in subdivision (c) of Section 51190 shall be effective until it has been accepted or approved by resolution of the governing body of the county or city and its acceptance endorsed thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 596, Sec. 8. (SB 618) Effective January 1, 2012.)
