# GOV § 29943

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 2. Destruction of Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29943

---

## Text

No other issue of bonds in place of those destroyed shall be made unless again authorized by a vote of the people.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
