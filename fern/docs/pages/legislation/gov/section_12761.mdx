# GOV § 12761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Community Services Block Grant Program > Article: 6. Community Action Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 12761

---

## Text

A community action agency or eligible entity shall not use any funds received under this article to replace discontinued state or local funding.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 46, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2008. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 12790.)
