# GOV § 54731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Contracts of Sanitation or Sewerage Enterprises

**Citation:** GOV § 54731

---

## Text

All consideration paid under the contract is payable solely from the revenues of the enterprise and the contracts shall expressly provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1249.)
