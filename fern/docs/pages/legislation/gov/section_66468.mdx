# GOV § 66468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Procedure > Article: 6. Filing Maps With County Recorder

**Citation:** GOV § 66468

---

## Text

The filing for record of a final or parcel map by the county recorder shall automatically and finally determine the validity of such map and when recorded shall impart cconstructive notice thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
