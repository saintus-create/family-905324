# GOV § 4479

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Purchases

**Citation:** GOV § 4479

---

## Text

Each state agency shall exercise due diligence in determining whether or not one or more persons have divided a contract to avoid the limitation of Section 4478.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 27, Sec. 2. Effective May 28, 1987.)
