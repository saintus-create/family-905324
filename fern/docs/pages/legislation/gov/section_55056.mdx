# GOV § 55056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 3. Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 55056

---

## Text

In any city operating under a charter providing for a board of public works, all things required in this article to be performed by its legislative body shall be performed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
