# GOV § 64722

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 3. Powers of the Los Angeles County Affordable Housing Solutions Agency

**Citation:** GOV § 64722

---

## Text

The board and the agency shall not do either of the following:

(a) Regulate or enforce local land use decisions.

(b) Acquire property by eminent domain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
