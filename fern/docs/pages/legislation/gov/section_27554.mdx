# GOV § 27554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27554

---

## Text

When required the surveyor shall aid and assist the State Lands Commission in making surveys within the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
