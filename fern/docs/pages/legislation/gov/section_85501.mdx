# GOV § 85501

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 5. Limitations on Contributions > Article: 5. Independent Expenditures

**Citation:** GOV § 85501

---

## Text

A controlled committee of a candidate may not make independent expenditures and may not contribute funds to another committee for the purpose of making independent expenditures to support or oppose other candidates.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 241, Sec. 13. Effective September 4, 2001. Note: This section was added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 102, and approved in Prop. 34 on Nov. 7, 2000.)
