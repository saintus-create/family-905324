# GOV § 22970.32

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.32

---

## Text

With regard to the plan, the board shall not engage in any transaction prohibited by subsection (b) of Section 503 of Title 26 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
