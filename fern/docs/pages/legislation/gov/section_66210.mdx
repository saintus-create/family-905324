# GOV § 66210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 11. Housing Sustainability Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 66210

---

## Text

If a city, county, or city and county reduces the density of sites within the district from the levels required pursuant to paragraph (3) of subdivision (b) of Section 66201, the city, county, or city and county shall return the full amount of zoning incentive payments it has received under this chapter to the department. Amounts repaid pursuant to this section shall be used for further incentive payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 371, Sec. 2. (AB 73) Effective January 1, 2018. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Stats. 2017, Ch. 371, Sec. 4.)
