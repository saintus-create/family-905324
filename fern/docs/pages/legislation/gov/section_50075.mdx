# GOV § 50075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.5. Voter-Approved Special Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 50075

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to provide all cities, counties, and districts with the authority to impose special taxes, pursuant to the provisions of Article XIIIA of the California Constitution.
