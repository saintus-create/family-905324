# GOV § 429.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 429.9

## Text

The California Vietnam Veterans Memorial is the official state Vietnam veterans war memorial.

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 191, Sec. 2. (AB 1289) Effective January 1, 2014.)
