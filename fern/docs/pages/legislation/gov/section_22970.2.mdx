# GOV § 22970.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.2

---

## Text

The design and administration of the Supplemental Contributions Program shall conform with the applicable provisions of Title 26 of the United States Code and the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307) by Stats. 1999, Ch. 785, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2000.)
