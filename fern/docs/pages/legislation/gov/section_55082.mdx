# GOV § 55082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 4. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 55082

---

## Text

The agreement shall provide for the joint payment of the cost of the joint use, benefit, and maintenance of all the outfall sewers and conduits upon the terms and under the regulations approved by the legislative bodies of all the local agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
