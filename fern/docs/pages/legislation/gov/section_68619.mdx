# GOV § 68619

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 5. The Trial Court Delay Reduction Act

**Citation:** GOV § 68619

---

## Text

(a) The Judicial Council shall review all local delay reduction rules adopted by courts and any revisions or new rules adopted by them and make recommendations to the courts of revisions so that these rules are consistent, to the extent desirable, with other court rules.

(b) The Judicial Council shall adopt uniform delay reduction rules which shall apply to any court that adopts a delay reduction program pursuant to Section 68618.

This subdivision shall cease to be operative on July 1, 1992.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1232, Sec. 3.)
