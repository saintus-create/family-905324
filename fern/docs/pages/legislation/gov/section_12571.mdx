# GOV § 12571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 6. Special Agents and Investigators

**Citation:** GOV § 12571

---

## Text

When serving under the direction of the Attorney General each special agent or investigator is a peace officer of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
