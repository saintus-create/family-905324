# GOV § 40520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 3. Surplus Property Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 40520

---

## Text

There is, in each city, city and county, or county of the State, a public corporation known as the “surplus property authority” of the city, city and county, or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
