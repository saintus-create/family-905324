# GOV § 55027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 2. Request by Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 55027

---

## Text

If the legislative body grants permission for the construction and maintenance of sewers, it may require as a condition that it may connect its sewers with those constructed under permission and use them in connection with its sewer system upon paying a proportionate part of the cost of constructing and maintaining them to the local agency constructing them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
