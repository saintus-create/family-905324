# GOV § 18906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Employment Lists > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 18906

---

## Text

Any name, after a period of five consecutive years, shall be removed from the reemployment lists for the class unless the period is extended by action of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 25. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
