# GOV § 53264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 3.5. Employment Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 53264

---

## Text

This article shall apply prospectively and shall affect any contract originally approved by the governing body, or any existing contract that is extended by the governing body, after the effective date of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 962, Sec. 6. Effective September 28, 1992.)
