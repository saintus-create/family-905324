# GOV § 25080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 5. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 25080

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by state law, all meetings of the board of supervisors shall be public.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1019, Sec. 1.5.)
