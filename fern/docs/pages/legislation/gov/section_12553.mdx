# GOV § 12553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 4. Supervision of District Attorneys

**Citation:** GOV § 12553

---

## Text

If a district attorney is disqualified to conduct any criminal prosecution within the county, the Attorney General may employ special counsel to conduct the prosecution. The attorney’s fee in such case is a legal charge against the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
