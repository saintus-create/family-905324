# GOV § 54471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54471

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for the vesting in a trustee of the right to enforce covenants to secure payment of or in relation to the bonds and the trustee’s powers, duties, and the limitation of his liabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
