# GOV § 8548.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. California State Auditor > Article: 3.5. Whistleblower Information

**Citation:** GOV § 8548.3

---

## Text

On July 1, 2002, and annually thereafter, every state agency shall send the information contained in the notice by electronic mail to its employees who have authorized access to electronic mail from the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 883, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
