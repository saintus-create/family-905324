# GOV § 7604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SECURITIES OWNED BY STATE AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Investment of Securities Owned by State Agencies > Article: 2. Security Loans

**Citation:** GOV § 7604

---

## Text

In the event of a loss in the reacquisition of loaned securities, the responsible state agency shall make a written report of the loss to the Legislature and the State Auditor as soon as possible, but not later than 30 days after the incurrence of that loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 107, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2004.)
