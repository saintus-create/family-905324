# GOV § 20411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Membership Classifications > Article: 3. Safety Member Classification—State

**Citation:** GOV § 20411

---

## Text

“State safety member” also includes members employed in the positions with the classification of Vocational Instructor (Barber Shop Practices) (Correctional Facility) (Class Code 2441).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 1997.)
