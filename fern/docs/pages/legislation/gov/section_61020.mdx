# GOV § 61020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Initial Board of Directors

**Citation:** GOV § 61020

---

## Text

The initial board of directors of a district formed on or after January 1, 2006, shall be determined pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
