# GOV § 23638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Seats > Article: 1. Location of County Seats

## Text

The county seat of San Francisco City and County is San Francisco.
