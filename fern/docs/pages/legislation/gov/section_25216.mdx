# GOV § 25216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2.5. County Service Areas > Article: 7. Capital Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 25216

---

## Text

Whenever the board determines that the amount of revenue available to a county service area is inadequate to acquire, construct, improve, rehabilitate, or replace the facilities authorized by this chapter, or to fund or to refund any outstanding indebtedness, the board may incur debt and raise revenues pursuant to this article or any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 158, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
