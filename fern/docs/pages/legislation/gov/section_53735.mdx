# GOV § 53735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4. Capital Outlay Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53735

---

## Text

At any time after the creation of the fund the legislative body may transfer to the fund any unencumbered surplus funds remaining on hand at the end of a fiscal year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 373.)
