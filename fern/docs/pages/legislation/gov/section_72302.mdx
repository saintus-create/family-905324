# GOV § 72302

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 8. Superior Courts > Article: 8. When Open for Business

**Citation:** GOV § 72302

---

## Text

If a defendant has been arrested for felony upon a warrant issued by a judge of that court, the clerk may, under like conditions, accept bail in the amount fixed in the warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 329. Effective September 28, 1998.)
