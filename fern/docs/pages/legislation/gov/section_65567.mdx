# GOV § 65567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 10.5. Open-Space Lands

**Citation:** GOV § 65567

---

## Text

No building permit may be issued, no subdivision map approved, and no open-space zoning ordinance adopted, unless the proposed construction, subdivision or ordinance is consistent with the local open-space plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1590.)
