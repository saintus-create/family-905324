# GOV § 43002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43002

---

## Text

Tax liens attach as of 12:01 a.m. on the first day of January of each year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 296, Sec. 12.1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
