# GOV § 55091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 4. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 55091

---

## Text

The local agencies entering into the agreements may use the streets within such local agencies for the construction and maintenance of outfall sewers and conduits pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
