# GOV § 32303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 4. Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 32303

---

## Text

Payments provided for in this chapter shall be made monthly upon proper vouchers.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
