# GOV § 52033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 2. Creation of Community Energy Authorities

**Citation:** GOV § 52033

---

## Text

As an alternative to Section 52032, two or more governing bodies may jointly exercise any power under this chapter pursuant to a joint powers agreement entered into pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 6500) of Division 7 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
