# GOV § 65065.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 5. Powers and Duties of District

**Citation:** GOV § 65065.3

---

## Text

The district shall have power to contract with any person, firm, association, or corporation, or to contract for any other types of services judged by the board to be necessary or convenient for carrying out the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
