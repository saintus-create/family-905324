# GOV § 34855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 1. City Manager

**Citation:** GOV § 34855

---

## Text

Within sixty days after the effective date of the ordinance, the legislative body shall appoint a city manager, who need not be a resident of the city at the time of appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 18. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
