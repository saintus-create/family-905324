# GOV § 50876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 3. Pensions and Death Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 50876

---

## Text

The board may at any time summon before it a member retired for disability and require examination of his fitness for duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
