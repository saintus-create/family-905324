# GOV § 8942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 3. Joint Legislative Ethics Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8942

---

## Text

Funds for the support of the committee shall be provided from the Contingent Funds of the Assembly and the Senate in the same manner that such funds are made available to other joint committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 163.)
