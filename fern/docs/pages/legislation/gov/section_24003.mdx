# GOV § 24003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. County Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 24003

---

## Text

The county veterinarian shall at the time of his or her appointment be a qualified veterinary surgeon having a certificate issued to him or her by the Veterinary Medical Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 118, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2005.)
