# GOV § 3304.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.7. Public Safety Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 3304.5

---

## Text

An administrative appeal instituted by a public safety officer under this chapter shall be conducted in conformance with rules and procedures adopted by the local public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 263, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
