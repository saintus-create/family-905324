# GOV § 5301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Signatures

**Citation:** GOV § 5301

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public body” means any county, city, district, or any other political subdivision of the State.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1252.)
