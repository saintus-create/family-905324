# GOV § 50666.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50666.7

---

## Text

The general credit or taxing power of the local agency, other than the revenues pledged, is not liable for the payment of the bonds or their interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
