# GOV § 11151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Departments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11151

---

## Text

Each department shall maintain an office and the director of each department who is a member of the Governor’s council shall reside at Sacramento. Each department shall adopt and keep an official seal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
