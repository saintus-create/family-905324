# GOV § 93005

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. GREAT REDWOOD TRAIL AGENCY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 93005

---

## Text

(a) The agency is a local agency for purposes of the Disaster Assistance Act (Chapter 7.5 (commencing with Section 8680) of Division 1 of Title 2).

(b) This section shall become operative on March 1, 2022.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 8) and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 423, Sec. 9. (SB 69) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative March 1, 2022, by its own provisions.)
