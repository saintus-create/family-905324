# GOV § 20194.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 6. Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 20194.5

---

## Text

In addition to the other investments authorized by this article, the board may invest in Property Assessed Clean Energy (PACE) bonds, as defined in Section 26054 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 396, Sec. 9. (AB 1513) Effective January 1, 2020.)
