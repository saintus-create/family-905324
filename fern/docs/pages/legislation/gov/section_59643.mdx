# GOV § 59643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59643

---

## Text

Bond Plan B bonds shall be:

(a) Issued in the manner and form provided in the Improvement Bond Act of 1915.

(b) Entitled “Refunding Bond. Bond Plan B.”

(c) Substantially in the form of the street improvement bonds provided in such act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
