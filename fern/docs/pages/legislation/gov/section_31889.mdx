# GOV § 31889

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 17. Integration with Federal Old Age, Survivors, and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31889

---

## Text

The division shall be conducted in accordance with Section 218(d)(7) of the Social Security Act and applicable federal and board rules and regulations, by the pension board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2090.)
