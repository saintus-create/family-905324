# GOV § 10200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 10200

---

## Text

There is in the State Government the Legislative Counsel Bureau. The bureau is in charge of a chief, who is a civil executive officer and who shall be known as the Legislative Counsel of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
