# GOV § 19231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 9. Hiring of Disabled Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 19231

---

## Text

As used in this article, “individual with a disability” means any individual who has a physical or mental disability as defined in Section 12926.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1049, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2001.)
