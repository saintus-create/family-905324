# GOV § 19055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19055

---

## Text

The department may, consistent with board rules, provide for certification of names from appropriate employment lists of the same or higher level in the event an employment list is not available for the class to which a position belongs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 40. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
