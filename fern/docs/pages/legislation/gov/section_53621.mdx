# GOV § 53621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 1.5. Health Fund Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 53621

---

## Text

The authority of the governing body to invest or to reinvest funds intended for the payment of employee retiree health benefits, or to sell or exchange securities purchased for that purpose, may be delegated by the governing body to designated officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 996, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1999.)
