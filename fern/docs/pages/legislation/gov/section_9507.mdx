# GOV § 9507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Enactment of Statutes and Adoption of Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9507

---

## Text

If the enrolled copy of a bill or other document is found to be correct, the committee shall present it to the proper officers for their signatures. When the officers sign their names thereon, as required by law, it is enrolled.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
