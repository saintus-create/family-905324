# GOV § 77007

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 13. State Funding of Trial Courts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 77007

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “trial court” means a superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 506. Effective January 1, 2003. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 77400.)
