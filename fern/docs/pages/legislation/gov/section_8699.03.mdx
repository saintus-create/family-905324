# GOV § 8699.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.9. Flexible Assistance for Survivors (FAS) Pilot Grant Program

**Citation:** GOV § 8699.03

---

## Text

This chapter shall become inoperative on July 1, 2028, and, as of January 1, 2029, is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 997, Sec. 9. (AB 179) Effective September 30, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 7.9, commencing with Section 8699.)
