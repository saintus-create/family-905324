# GOV § 65954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 5. Approval of Development Permits

**Citation:** GOV § 65954

---

## Text

The time limits established by this article shall not apply in the event that federal statutes or regulations require time schedules which exceed such time limits.
