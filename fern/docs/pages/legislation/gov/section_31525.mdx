# GOV § 31525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** GOV § 31525

---

## Text

The board may make regulations not inconsistent with this chapter. The regulations become effective when approved by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
