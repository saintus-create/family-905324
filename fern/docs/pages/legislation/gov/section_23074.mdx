# GOV § 23074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 1. Terms Defined

**Citation:** GOV § 23074

---

## Text

“To,” “by,” “along,” “with,” “in,” “up,” or “down” a creek, river, slough, strait, or bay, mean the middle of the main channel thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
