# GOV § 26723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26723

---

## Text

The fee for opening a safe-deposit box pursuant to Sections 488.460 and 700.150 of the Code of Civil Procedure is one hundred seventy dollars ($170).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 29, Sec. 4. (SB 564) Effective January 1, 2024.)
