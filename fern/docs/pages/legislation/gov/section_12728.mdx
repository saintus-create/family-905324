# GOV § 12728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Community Services Block Grant Program > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 12728

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the provisions of this chapter shall supersede and prevail over any provisions of law relating to or in any way dealing with the subject matter of this chapter or federal economic opportunity programs which were repealed by federal Public Law 97-35, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1436, Sec. 1.2. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 12790.)
