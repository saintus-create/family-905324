# GOV § 31899.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.9. Internal Revenue Code County Compliance and Replacement Benefits Program

**Citation:** GOV § 31899.1

---

## Text

(a) The definitions in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 31450) of this part shall apply to this chapter.

(b) The term “Internal Revenue Code” includes all regulations, revenue rulings, notices, and revenue procedures issued by the Internal Revenue Service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 520, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2004.)
