# GOV § 14121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 2. Emergencies

**Citation:** GOV § 14121

---

## Text

The department may perform any work required or take any remedial measures necessary to avert, alleviate, repair, or restore damage or destruction to property as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 118.)
