# GOV § 811.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL

**Citation:** GOV § 811.8

---

## Text

“Statute” means an act adopted by the Legislature of this State or by the Congress of the United States, or a statewide initiative act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
