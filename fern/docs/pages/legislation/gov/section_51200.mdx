# GOV § 51200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This chapter shall be known as the California Land Conservation Act of 1965 or as the Williamson Act.
