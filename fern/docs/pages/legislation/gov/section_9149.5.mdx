# GOV § 9149.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 9. The Historic State Capitol Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.5

---

## Text

The Joint Rules Committee shall appoint a Subcommittee on the Capitol Restoration Project, comprised of three members from the Senate and three from the Assembly, to monitor the commission. The legislators so appointed shall have no vote in commission proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1757, Sec. 8. Effective October 1, 1984.)
