# GOV § 31696.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.8. Long-Term Care Group Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31696.5

---

## Text

Subdivision (b) of Section 31696.1 shall not be operative in any county until the board of supervisors shall, by resolution adopted by a majority vote, make that subdivision applicable in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 491, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
