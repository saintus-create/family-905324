# GOV § 15457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 7.2. HEALTH FACILITIES FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15457

---

## Text

Any net earnings of the authority beyond that necessary for retirement of any obligations issued by the authority or to implement the purposes of this chapter may inure to the benefit only of the State of California or the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1033.)
