# GOV § 11342.545

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 11342.545

---

## Text

“Emergency” means a situation that calls for immediate action to avoid serious harm to the public peace, health, safety, or general welfare.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 713, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2007.)
