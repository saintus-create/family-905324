# GOV § 19826.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of Salaries > Article: 3. Salary Classification

**Citation:** GOV § 19826.5

---

## Text

Whenever the department finds that pay data was furnished to the department on the basis that the source remain confidential, the source shall not be open to the public or admissible as evidence in any action or special proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 684, Sec. 3. Effective September 23, 1981.)
