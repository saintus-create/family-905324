# GOV § 58098

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58098

---

## Text

Any protest to the regularity or sufficiency of the proceedings shall be written and shall clearly set forth the defects to which objection is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
