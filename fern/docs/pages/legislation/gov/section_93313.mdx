# GOV § 93313

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.5. CENTRAL CALIFORNIA RAILROAD AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of the Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 93313

---

## Text

The authority may acquire, own, lease, and operate railroad lines and equipment, including, but not limited to, real and personal property, tracks, rights-of-way, equipment, and facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 234, Sec. 1. (SB 325) Effective January 1, 2012.)
