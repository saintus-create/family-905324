# GOV § 8878.121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.47. Earthquake Safety and Public Buildings Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1990 > Article: 5. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8878.121

---

## Text

Notwithstanding anything in the State General Obligation Bond Law, the maximum maturity of any bonds authorized by this chapter shall not exceed 20 years from the date of each respective series. The maturity of each series shall be calculated from the date of each series.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 122 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
