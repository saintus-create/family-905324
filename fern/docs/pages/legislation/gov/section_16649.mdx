# GOV § 16649

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Prohibition on Use of State Funds and Facilities to Assist, Promote, or Deter Union Organizing

**Citation:** GOV § 16649

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are severable. If any section or portion of this chapter, or any application thereof, is held invalid, in whole or in part, that invalidity shall not effect any other section, portion, or application that can be given effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 872, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
