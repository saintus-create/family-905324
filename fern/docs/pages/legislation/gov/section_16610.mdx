# GOV § 16610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Savings and Loan Association Deposits > Article: 2. Security for Deposits

**Citation:** GOV § 16610

---

## Text

Security shall not be required for that portion of any deposit that is insured under any law of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 349.)
