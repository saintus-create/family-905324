# GOV § 53216.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 1.5. Pension Trusts

**Citation:** GOV § 53216.3

---

## Text

The legislative body shall employ investment counsel or trust companies or trust departments of banks to render service in connection with its investment program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1069.)
