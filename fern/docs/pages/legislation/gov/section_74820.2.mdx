# GOV § 74820.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 10. County-Specific Provisions > Article: 32.3. San Joaquin County Court Security and Civil Process

**Citation:** GOV § 74820.2

---

## Text

There is a court services division within the San Joaquin County Sheriff’s Department to provide security within the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 468. Effective January 1, 2003.)
