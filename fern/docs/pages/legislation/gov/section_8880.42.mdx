# GOV § 8880.42

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Director

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.42

---

## Text

The director shall provide a monthly cumulative sales report to the commission and the Controller within 15 days after the end of each month. The report shall include cumulative lottery sales by product, compared to the commission-approved budget and the estimated administrative expenses for the current fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 531, Sec. 3. Effective October 4, 1995. Note: Section 8880.42 originally was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
