# GOV § 29743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Claims > Article: 2. Allowance of Claims by Auditor

**Citation:** GOV § 29743

---

## Text

If the auditor finds that any claim presented is a proper county charge, but is greater in amount than is justly due, he may allow the claim in part and issue his warrant for the portion allowed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
