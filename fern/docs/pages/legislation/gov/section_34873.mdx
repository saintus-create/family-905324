# GOV § 34873

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 2. Election of Legislative Body By or From Districts in Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34873

---

## Text

An ordinance enacted pursuant to this article may be amended or repealed in the same manner; provided, the term of office of any council member elected shall not be affected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 7. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
