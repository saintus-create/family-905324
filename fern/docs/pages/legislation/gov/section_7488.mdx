# GOV § 7488

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 20. Governmental Access to Financial Records > Article: 5. Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** GOV § 7488

---

## Text

An action to enforce any provision of this chapter must be commenced within three years after the date on which the violation occurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1320.)
