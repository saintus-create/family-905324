# GOV § 53642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53642

---

## Text

The money deposited may be drawn out by check or order of the treasurer or other official authorized to make such deposit.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 670.)
