# GOV § 54460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54460

---

## Text

In the resolution authorizing the bonds, the legislative body may insert any of the provisions authorized by this article, which shall become a part of the contract with the bond holders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
