# GOV § 19784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Military Service > Article: 4. Reinstatement After Military Service

**Citation:** GOV § 19784

---

## Text

Upon reinstatement of a person under the provisions of Sections 19780 and 19783, any necessary separations shall be effected under the provisions of this part governing layoff and demotion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 446.)
