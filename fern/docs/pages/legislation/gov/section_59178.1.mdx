# GOV § 59178.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 2. Authority to Conduct Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 59178.1

---

## Text

If the district lies entirely within the incorporated territory of a public district, the public district legislative body has complete authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 256.)
