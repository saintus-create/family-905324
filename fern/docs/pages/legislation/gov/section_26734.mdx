# GOV § 26734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26734

---

## Text

The fee for making a levy on personal property already in possession of the officer who is holding it under attachment in the same action shall be the amount described in Section 26720.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 629, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001.)
