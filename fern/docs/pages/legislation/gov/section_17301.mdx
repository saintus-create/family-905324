# GOV § 17301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Part: 5. STATE OF CALIFORNIA NOTES > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 6. Warrant Payment Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 17301

---

## Text

Notes authorized to be issued may be sold by the Treasurer from time to time on a negotiated or a competitive bid basis as the Treasurer shall deem advisable. The Treasurer shall pay the notes and the interest thereon from moneys in the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1648, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1984.)
