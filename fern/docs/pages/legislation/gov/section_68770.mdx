# GOV § 68770

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.5. Commission on Judicial Performance > Article: 4. Committee to Review the Operations and Structure of the Commission on Judicial Performance

**Citation:** GOV § 68770

---

## Text

The Committee to Review the Operations and Structure of the Commission on Judicial Performance is hereby created in state government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 79, Sec. 16. (AB 143) Effective July 16, 2021.)
