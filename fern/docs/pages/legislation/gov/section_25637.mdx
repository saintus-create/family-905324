# GOV § 25637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25637

---

## Text

Whenever this article is availed of, the board of supervisors shall appropriate money for the use of the county board of forestry sufficient to pay the compensation of the county forester and for the necessary expenses of the county board of forestry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
