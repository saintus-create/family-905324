# GOV § 59531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 14. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 59531

---

## Text

The legislative body making any contribution of public funds to aid in the refunding shall determine that the public interest, convenience, or necessity requires it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
