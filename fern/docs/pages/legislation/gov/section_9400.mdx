# GOV § 9400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Witnesses

**Citation:** GOV § 9400

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “committee” means a committee of either the Senate or Assembly, a joint committee of both houses, or, when any of the foregoing committees is authorized to create subcommittees, a subcommittee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
