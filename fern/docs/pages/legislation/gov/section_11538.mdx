# GOV § 11538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Technology > Article: 2. Office of Technology Services

**Citation:** GOV § 11538

---

## Text

The Chief of the Office of Technology Services shall be appointed by, and serve at the pleasure of, the Governor, subject to Senate confirmation. The chief shall report to the Director of Technology.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 353, Sec. 72. (SB 820) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 129 of Ch. 353.)
