# GOV § 822.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions Relating to Liability > Article: 3. Liability of Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 822.2

---

## Text

A public employee acting in the scope of his employment is not liable for an injury caused by his misrepresentation, whether or not such misrepresentation be negligent or intentional, unless he is guilty of actual fraud, corruption or actual malice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
