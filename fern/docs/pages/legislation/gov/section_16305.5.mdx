# GOV § 16305.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16305.5

---

## Text

Money in treasury trust accounts shall be deposited, invested and reinvested in the same manner and to the same extent as if the money in trust accounts were money in the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 687.)
