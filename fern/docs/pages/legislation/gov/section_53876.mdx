# GOV § 53876

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53876

---

## Text

A local agency may provide and expend a fund or expend money from the general fund to purchase or acquire an accrued lien or property required to be sold for the nonpayment of an assessment or a delinquency incurred by the nonpayment of an assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
