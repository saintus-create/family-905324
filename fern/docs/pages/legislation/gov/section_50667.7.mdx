# GOV § 50667.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50667.7

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for the terms upon which the bondholders or any percentage of the bondholders may enforce covenants or duties imposed by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
