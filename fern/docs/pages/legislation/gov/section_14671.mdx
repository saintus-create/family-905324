# GOV § 14671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. State Property

**Citation:** GOV § 14671

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14670, the Director of General Services, with the consent of the state agency concerned, may let for any period of time any real or personal property which belongs to the state, for radio or television purposes where he deems such letting is in the best interests of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
