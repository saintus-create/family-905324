# GOV § 425.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.6

---

## Text

The garibaldi (Hypsypops rubicundus) is the official state marine fish.

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 948, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
