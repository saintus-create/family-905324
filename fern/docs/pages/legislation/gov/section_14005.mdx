# GOV § 14005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 14005

---

## Text

The director shall perform all duties, exercise all powers and jurisdiction, assume and discharge all responsibilities, and carry out and effect all purposes vested by law in the department, except as otherwise expressly provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1253.)
