# GOV § 40536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40536

---

## Text

The commission may appoint as its attorney, engineer or other officer, the attorney, engineer, or other officer of the local agency, or other competent person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
