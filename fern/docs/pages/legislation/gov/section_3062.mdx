# GOV § 3062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 3. Removal Other Than by Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3062

---

## Text

The accusation shall be delivered by the foreman of the grand jury to the district attorney of the county, unless he is the officer accused.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
