# GOV § 66004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 5. Fees for Development Projects

## Text

The establishment or increase of any fee pursuant to this chapter shall be subject to the requirements of Section 66018.
