# GOV § 62578

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 2. Commercial Linkage Fee

**Citation:** GOV § 62578

---

## Text

Any action by the authority board or interested person under this article shall be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2025.)
