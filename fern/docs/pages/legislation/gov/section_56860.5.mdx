# GOV § 56860.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 3. Formation

**Citation:** GOV § 56860.5

---

## Text

If a petition is for consolidation of districts or formation of a new district, the petition may propose a name for the new or consolidated district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 208. Effective January 1, 2001.)
