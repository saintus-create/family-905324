# GOV § 53226.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 1.7. Local District Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53226.2

---

## Text

As an alternative or supplementary to the bonds required by this article, the legislative body may establish a master bond pursuant to Section 1481.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 330, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
