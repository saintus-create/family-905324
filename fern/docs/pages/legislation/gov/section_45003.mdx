# GOV § 45003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Service > Article: 1. The System

**Citation:** GOV § 45003

---

## Text

The inclusion of any class of officers and employees made prior to September 19, 1947, is operative from the inception of the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
