# GOV § 56074.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56074.5

---

## Text

“Service review” means an analysis conducted by the commission documenting and analyzing the services in a particular geographic region or jurisdictional area pursuant to the requirements of Section 56430.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 20, Sec. 2. (AB 1822) Effective January 1, 2020.)
