# GOV § 53731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4. Capital Outlay Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53731

---

## Text

By ordinance, the legislative body of a local agency may provide for the levy and collection of assessments or taxes for the creation and accumulation of a fund for capital outlays.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
