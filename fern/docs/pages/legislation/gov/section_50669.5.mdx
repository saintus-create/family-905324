# GOV § 50669.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50669.5

---

## Text

A fiscal or paying agent may be appointed as now, or hereafter, provided in Article 7 (commencing with Section 54550) of Chapter 6 of Division 2 of Title 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
