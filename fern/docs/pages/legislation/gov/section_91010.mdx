# GOV § 91010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 11. Enforcement

**Citation:** GOV § 91010

---

## Text

No request to the civil prosecutor pursuant to Section 91007 shall be made or filed in connection with a report required by Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 84100) until the time when an audit and investigation could be begun under subdivision (c) of Section 90002.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 278, Sec. 72. (AB 808) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally operative pursuant to Sec. 78 of Stats. 2025, Ch. 278. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
