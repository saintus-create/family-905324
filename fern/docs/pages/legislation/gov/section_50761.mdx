# GOV § 50761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 3. Rental Rates, Charges and Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 50761

---

## Text

The board may lease concessions for a period of time not exceeding 10 years as may be agreed upon by the contracting parties with or without advertising for bids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
