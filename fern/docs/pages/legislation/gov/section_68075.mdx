# GOV § 68075

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68075

---

## Text

The seal used by the Supreme Court abolished by the Constitution of 1879 shall be the seal of the Supreme Court provided for in this title. The Supreme Court may direct its clerk to provide two duplicates of the seal, each of which shall have the same effect as the original.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 206.)
