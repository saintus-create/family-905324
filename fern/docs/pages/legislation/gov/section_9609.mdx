# GOV § 9609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Operation of Statutes and Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9609

---

## Text

A statute amending a section of a repealed statute is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
