# GOV § 18592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4. Scope

**Citation:** GOV § 18592

---

## Text

The “state civil service” shall include all fire protection personnel employed at the Armed Forces Reserve Center in Los Alamitos.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 310, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Secs. 2 and 3 of Ch. 310.)
