# GOV § 63082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 63082

---

## Text

Notwithstanding anything herein to the contrary, this act shall be supplemental to, and not in lieu of, the right of any sponsor to issue general obligation bonds or bonds that it is otherwise lawfully authorized to issue or cause to be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 94, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995. Conditionally operative as prescribed by Sec. 17 of Ch. 94.)
