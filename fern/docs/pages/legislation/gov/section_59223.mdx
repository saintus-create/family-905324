# GOV § 59223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 3. Resolution of Intention

**Citation:** GOV § 59223

---

## Text

The resolution of intention shall be published twice in a newspaper designated in the resolution. The first publication shall be at least 10 days before the date set in the resolution for hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
