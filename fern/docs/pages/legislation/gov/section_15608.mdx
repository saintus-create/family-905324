# GOV § 15608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15608

---

## Text

The board shall instruct, advise, and direct assessors as to their duties under the laws. It may obtain the opinion of the Attorney General upon any questions of law relating to such duties in such cases as it deems necessary.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1369.)
