# GOV § 12225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12225

---

## Text

The Secretary of State may at any time return to the state agency from which it was received an item in the archives which the Secretary of State does not deem to be of historical value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 54. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
