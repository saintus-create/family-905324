# GOV § 33009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. County Peace Officers and Fire Service Retirement, Disability and Death Benefit Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 33009

---

## Text

Any retirement plan established pursuant to the provisions of this chapter, shall supersede any existing retirement system for county peace officers or county foresters, fire wardens and firemen, and all money, property and investments standing to the credit of the fund of the superseded system shall be transferred to the county peace officers and fire service retirement fund created by this chapter, whether such money and investments were derived from member contributions, county contributions, earnings, accumulations or profits from investments, or otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1479.)
