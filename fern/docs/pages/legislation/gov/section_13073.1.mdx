# GOV § 13073.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13073.1

---

## Text

Whenever the department issues reports containing population breakdowns or analyses based on race or ethnicity, it shall report Asian-Pacific Islanders as a distinct category, and not combine Asian-Pacific Islanders into any other category.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1013, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
