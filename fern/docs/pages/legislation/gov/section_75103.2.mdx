# GOV § 75103.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 6. Judges’ Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 75103.2

---

## Text

The Legislature reserves the right to reduce any benefits applicable to any person who becomes a judge on and after January 1, 1980.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 709.)
