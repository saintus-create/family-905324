# GOV § 12610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 8. Environmental Actions

**Citation:** GOV § 12610

---

## Text

In granting temporary and permanent equitable relief, the court may impose such conditions upon the defendant as are required to protect the natural resources of the state from pollution, impairment, or destruction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1518.)
