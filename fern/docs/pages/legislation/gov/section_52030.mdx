# GOV § 52030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 2. Creation of Community Energy Authorities

**Citation:** GOV § 52030

---

## Text

The governing body of any county or city may, by resolution or ordinance, create a community energy authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
