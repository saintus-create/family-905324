# GOV § 12328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Treasurer > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12328

---

## Text

The Treasurer shall report daily to the Controller the amounts disbursed during the preceding day and the funds out of which the disbursements were paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1288.)
