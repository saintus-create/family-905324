# GOV § 54238.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 8.5. Surplus Residential Property

**Citation:** GOV § 54238.5

---

## Text

Failure to comply with the provisions of this article shall not invalidate the transfer, sale, or conveyance to a bona fide purchaser for value or an encumbrancer for value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1116.)
