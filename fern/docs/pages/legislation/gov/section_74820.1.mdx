# GOV § 74820.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 10. County-Specific Provisions > Article: 32.3. San Joaquin County Court Security and Civil Process

**Citation:** GOV § 74820.1

---

## Text

This article applies to the court security functions and service of process and notice functions in the sheriff’s office.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 210, Sec. 48. (AB 1984) Effective January 1, 2021.)
