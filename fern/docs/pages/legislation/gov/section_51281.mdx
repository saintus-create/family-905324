# GOV § 51281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 5. Cancellation

**Citation:** GOV § 51281

---

## Text

A contract may not be canceled except pursuant to a request by the landowner, and as provided in this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1372.)
