# GOV § 56860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 3. Formation

**Citation:** GOV § 56860

---

## Text

If a proposal for formation of a new district is made by petition, the petition shall comply with the signature requirements and content of a petition for formation of the district as set forth in the principal act under which the new district is proposed to be formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 208. Effective January 1, 2001.)
