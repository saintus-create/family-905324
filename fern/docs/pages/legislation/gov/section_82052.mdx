# GOV § 82052

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82052

---

## Text

“Statewide candidate” means a candidate who seeks election to any statewide elective office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
