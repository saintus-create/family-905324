# GOV § 29103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 5. Tax Levy

**Citation:** GOV § 29103

---

## Text

It shall be the responsibility of the auditor to calculate the several tax rates for the board’s action thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 751, Sec. 30.)
