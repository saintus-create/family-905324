# GOV § 31685.97

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.4. Community Property

**Citation:** GOV § 31685.97

---

## Text

This article shall not be operative in any county until the board of supervisors shall, by resolution adopted by a majority vote, make this article applicable in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31685.96 (as added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 670, Sec. 2, 2nd text) by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 224. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
