# GOV § 53197.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 5. Confirmation and Collection of Levy

**Citation:** GOV § 53197.5

---

## Text

The local agency may bring an action in any court of competent jurisdiction against the owners of any property to collect delinquent contingent assessments and penalties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
