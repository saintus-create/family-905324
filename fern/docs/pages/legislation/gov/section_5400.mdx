# GOV § 5400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Tax or Assessment Levy for Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 5400

---

## Text

“Public body” as used in this chapter means any county, city and county, city, public district, public authority or other public corporation which is authorized to issue bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 333.)
