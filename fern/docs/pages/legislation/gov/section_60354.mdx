# GOV § 60354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.5. California Special District Consolidation Assistance Program

**Citation:** GOV § 60354

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall develop the procedure to be followed by special districts to submit applications and the criteria for evaluating those loan applications and awarding loans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1392, Sec. 1.)
