# GOV § 11128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 11128

---

## Text

Each closed session of a state body shall be held only during a regular or special meeting of the body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 968, Sec. 18.)
