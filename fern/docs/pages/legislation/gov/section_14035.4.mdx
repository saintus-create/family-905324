# GOV § 14035.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 14035.4

---

## Text

The department shall request the involvement and cooperation of affected local agencies and public and private passenger carriers in decisions relative to the acquisition, development, and operation of intermodal passenger facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 14035.6 (as added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 568) by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1220, Sec. 7. Effective September 30, 1994.)
