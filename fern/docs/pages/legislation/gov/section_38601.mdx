# GOV § 38601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 1. Fire Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 38601

---

## Text

The legislative body of a city may:

(a) Establish and maintain fire limits.

(b) Regulate building and construction and removal of buildings within the city.
