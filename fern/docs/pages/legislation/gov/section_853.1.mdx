# GOV § 853.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4.5. Unmanned Aircraft

**Citation:** GOV § 853.1

---

## Text

The immunity provided by this chapter is in addition to any other immunity provided to a local public entity or public employee of a local public entity under law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 834, Sec. 2. (SB 807) Effective January 1, 2017.)
