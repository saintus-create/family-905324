# GOV § 37650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 8. Hospitals Managed by City Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 37650

---

## Text

This article applies only to cities where a municipal hospital has not been established.
