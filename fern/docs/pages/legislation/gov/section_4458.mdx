# GOV § 4458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Access to Public Buildings by Physically Handicapped Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 4458

---

## Text

The district attorney, the city attorney, the county counsel if the district attorney does not bring an action, or the Attorney General may bring an action to enjoin a violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 872, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
