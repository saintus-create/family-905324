# GOV § 29301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29301

---

## Text

The general fund consists of money received into the treasury and not specially appropriated to any other fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
