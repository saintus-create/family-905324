# GOV § 9404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Witnesses

**Citation:** GOV § 9404

---

## Text

(a) The members of any committee may administer oaths to witnesses in any matter under examination.

(b) Anyone authorized to administer oaths who is designated by the chairman of a committee may administer oaths to witnesses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 575.)
