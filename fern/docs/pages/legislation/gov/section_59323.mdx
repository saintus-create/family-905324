# GOV § 59323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 7. Reassessment

**Citation:** GOV § 59323

---

## Text

Tax deeded lands are subject to reassessment in refunding proceedings pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
