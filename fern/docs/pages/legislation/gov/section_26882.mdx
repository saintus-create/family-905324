# GOV § 26882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Controller

**Citation:** GOV § 26882

---

## Text

In addition to the accounts required by law the auditor-controller shall, upon order of the board of supervisors, maintain such accounts and statistics and prepare such reports therefrom as the board may deem necessary for its information and use in the management and control of the operations of the county and of those districts whose funds are kept in the county treasury.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 321.)
