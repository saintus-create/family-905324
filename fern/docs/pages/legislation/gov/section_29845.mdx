# GOV § 29845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 2.5. Optional Method—Registering Claims or Orders

**Citation:** GOV § 29845

---

## Text

Actual costs incurred for claim or order registration, record maintenance, notification, interest calculation, and payment processing may be recovered from the issuing entity as administrative costs by the county auditor-controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 523, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1993.)
