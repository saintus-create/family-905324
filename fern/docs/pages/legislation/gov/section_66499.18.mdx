# GOV § 66499.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 6. Reversions and Exclusions > Article: 1. Reversion to Acreage

**Citation:** GOV § 66499.18

---

## Text

Reversion shall be effective upon the final map being filed for record by the county recorder, and thereupon all dedications and offers of dedication not shown thereon shall be of no further force or effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
