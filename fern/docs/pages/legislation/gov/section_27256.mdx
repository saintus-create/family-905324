# GOV § 27256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 2. Books

**Citation:** GOV § 27256

---

## Text

The recorder shall keep such other indices as are required in the performance of official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 269, Sec. 10. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2016.)
