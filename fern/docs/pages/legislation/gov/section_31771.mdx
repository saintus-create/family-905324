# GOV § 31771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 11.5. Deferred Retirement Option Program

**Citation:** GOV § 31771

---

## Text

The provisions of this section to Section 31776.5, inclusive, shall be referred to collectively as the “forward DROP provisions.”

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 897, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
