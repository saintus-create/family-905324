# GOV § 9407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Witnesses

**Citation:** GOV § 9407

---

## Text

If the contempt is committed before a committee during the session of the Legislature, the committee shall report the contempt to the Senate or Assembly, as the case may be, for such action as may be deemed necessary by the Senate or Assembly.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
