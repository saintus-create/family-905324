# GOV § 62105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. Community Revitalization and Investment Authorities > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Housing for Persons of Low and Moderate Income

**Citation:** GOV § 62105

---

## Text

The same notice requirements as specified in Section 65863.10 shall apply to multifamily rental housing that receives financial assistance pursuant to Sections 62100 and 62101.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 319, Sec. 2. (AB 2) Effective January 1, 2016.)
