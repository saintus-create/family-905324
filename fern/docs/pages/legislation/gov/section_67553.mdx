# GOV § 67553

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67553

---

## Text

The commission shall determine the time, form, and manner of the issuance of bonds. The bonds shall be payable only out of specified revenues as the commission shall determine at the time of their issuance.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1079.)
