# GOV § 6514.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6514.5

---

## Text

Any public agency may enter into agreements with other state agencies pursuant to the provisions of Section 11256.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 729, Sec. 1.)
