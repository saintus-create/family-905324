# GOV § 7306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.7. Open-Space Land Dedication

**Citation:** GOV § 7306

---

## Text

Upon acceptance of an offer of land pursuant to this chapter, the secretary shall forward appropriate certification of such acceptance to the Controller.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 220, Sec. 3.)
