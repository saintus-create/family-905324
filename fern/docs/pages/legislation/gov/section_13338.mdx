# GOV § 13338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 3. Revised Budgeting and Accounting System for Fiscal Affairs of State Government

**Citation:** GOV § 13338

---

## Text

(a) The Budget Bill shall utilize a coding scheme compatible with the Governor’s Budget and with the records of the Controller.

(b) The Budget Bill shall provide for the appropriation of federal funds received by the state and deposited in the State Treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1286, Sec. 11.)
