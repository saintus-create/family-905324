# GOV § 43423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Street Work Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 43423

---

## Text

The legislative body may appropriate any sums deemed necessary out of any such revolving fund and expend the money for any of the purposes enumerated and incidental expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
