# GOV § 67072

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 3. California Tahoe Regional Planning Agency > Article: 3. Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 67072

---

## Text

All provisions of the Tahoe regional general plan shall be enforced by the agency and by the counties and cities in the region.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
