# GOV § 14877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 3. Operation of the Office of State Printing

**Citation:** GOV § 14877

---

## Text

There shall be kept in the Office of State Printing, a time book, containing the name of every employee connected with the Office of State Printing, the time employed, the rate of wages, and the amount paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 371.)
