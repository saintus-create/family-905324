# GOV § 65912.105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.1. Affordable Housing and High Road Jobs Act of 2022 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 65912.105

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2033, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 647, Sec. 3. (AB 2011) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative July 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7 of Stats. 2022, Ch. 647. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 4.1, comprising sections 65912.100 to 65912.140.)
