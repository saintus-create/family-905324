# GOV § 57380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Effect of Incorporation

**Citation:** GOV § 57380

---

## Text

Courts shall take judicial notice of the organization and existence of cities incorporated pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 541, Sec. 3. Effective September 9, 1985. Operative January 1, 1986, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 541.)
