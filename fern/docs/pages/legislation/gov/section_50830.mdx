# GOV § 50830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 2. Organization, Powers, and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 50830

---

## Text

The board for each fund consists of the board of commissioners for the department. If there is no board of commissioners, the chairman of the legislative body of the local agency, the treasurer of the local agency, and the chief of the department constitute the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
