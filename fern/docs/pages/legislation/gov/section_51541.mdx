# GOV § 51541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51541

---

## Text

This article provides an alternative method by which the duties of city officers designated in this article may be performed by officers of the county in which the city is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
