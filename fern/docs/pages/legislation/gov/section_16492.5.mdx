# GOV § 16492.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 6. Local Agency Emergency Loan Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16492.5

---

## Text

It is the purpose of the Local Agency Emergency Loan Fund to provide short-term loans to local agencies for operational purposes so that vital services are not interrupted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 292.)
