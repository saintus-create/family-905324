# GOV § 25382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 2. Jails in Other Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 25382

---

## Text

The board may construct, lease, build, rebuild, furnish, refurnish, or repair the jail, and provide all necessary offices, employees, attendants, and supplies for its proper maintenance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
