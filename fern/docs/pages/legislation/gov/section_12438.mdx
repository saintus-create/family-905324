# GOV § 12438

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 2.5. Discharge of State Entity from Duty to Collect

**Citation:** GOV § 12438

---

## Text

A state agency is not required to collect taxes, licenses, fees, or money owing to the state for any reason if the amount to be collected is five hundred dollars ($500) or less. Nothing contained in this section shall be construed as releasing any person from the payment of any money due the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 13943.2 by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 115. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
