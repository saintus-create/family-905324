# GOV § 11549.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.7. Office of Information Security and Office of Privacy Protection > Article: 2. Office of Privacy Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 11549.7

---

## Text

The Office of Privacy Protection shall be under the direction of a director who shall report to the Secretary of State and Consumer Services and lead the Office of Privacy Protection in carrying out its mission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 404, Sec. 33. (AB 2408) Effective January 1, 2011.)
