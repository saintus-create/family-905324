# GOV § 50667.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50667.1

---

## Text

The bonds and, any interest on, or income from, the bonds are exempt from taxation in this state, except from bank and corporation franchise, gift, inheritance, and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
