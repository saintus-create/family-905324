# GOV § 19818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Department of Human Resources > Article: 2.5. Personnel Classification Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 19818

---

## Text

Subject to Article VII of the California Constitution, the department succeeds to and is vested with the duties, purposes, responsibilities, and jurisdiction exercised by the State Personnel Board with respect to the administration of the Personnel Classification Plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1015, Sec. 20. Effective September 26, 1985.)
