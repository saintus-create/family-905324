# GOV § 69846.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 7. Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 69846.5

---

## Text

The clerk of the superior court shall endorse on each paper filed with the court the day, month, and year it is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1610.)
