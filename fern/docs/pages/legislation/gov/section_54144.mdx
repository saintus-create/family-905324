# GOV § 54144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 6. Federal Surplus Property

**Citation:** GOV § 54144

---

## Text

Any statute, charter, ordinance, or resolution inconsistent with this article is suspended to the extent of such inconsistency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
