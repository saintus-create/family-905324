# GOV § 17000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Form, Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17000

---

## Text

Every warrant shall be drawn by the Controller upon the fund out of which it is payable, and such fund shall be designated thereon.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 261.)
