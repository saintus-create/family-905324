# GOV § 59651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59651

---

## Text

Bond Plan A bonds may be issued for a period of not to exceed 29 years from the January 2d succeeding the October 15th following their date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
