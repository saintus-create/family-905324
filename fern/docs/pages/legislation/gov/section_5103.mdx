# GOV § 5103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Protection of Bond Guarantors

**Citation:** GOV § 5103

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “issuing body” means the State, any subdivision thereof, municipality, public corporation, or district.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
