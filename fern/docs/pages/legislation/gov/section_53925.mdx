# GOV § 53925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 12. Proceeds of Sale for Taxes or Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 53925

---

## Text

This article shall govern the distribution of proceeds of any sale by a local agency of real property for taxes or special assessments, except where the sale is conducted and the proceeds are accounted for as provided in Division 1 (commencing with Section 101) of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 648.)
