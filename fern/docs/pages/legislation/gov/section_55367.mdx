# GOV § 55367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Joint Projects for Sanitation and Electrical Lines > Article: 3. Refusal to Grant Permission

**Citation:** GOV § 55367

---

## Text

In the judgment the Public Utilities Commission may prescribe the terms upon which the local agency making the application may use the streets and public places.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
