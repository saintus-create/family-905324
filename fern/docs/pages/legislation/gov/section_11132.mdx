# GOV § 11132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 11132

---

## Text

Except as expressly authorized by this article, no closed session may be held by any state body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1320, Sec. 4.)
