# GOV § 68080

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68080

---

## Text

The clerk of the court shall keep the seal of the court.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1417, Sec. 6.)
