# GOV § 54701.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 54701.7

---

## Text

Construction of housing for employees pursuant to this chapter shall be exempt from the provisions of Section 30213 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
