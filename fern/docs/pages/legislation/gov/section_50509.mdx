# GOV § 50509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50509

---

## Text

The legislative body shall cause a notice to be posted conspicuously for five days on or near the legislative body’s chamber door.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
