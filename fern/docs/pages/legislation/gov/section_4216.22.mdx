# GOV § 4216.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3.1. Protection of Underground Infrastructure > Article: 2. Regional Notification Center System

**Citation:** GOV § 4216.22

---

## Text

Consistent with all laws of this state, the board may prescribe rules and regulations as may be necessary or proper to carry out the purposes and intent of this act and to exercise the powers and duties conferred upon it by this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 809, Sec. 20. (SB 661) Effective January 1, 2017.)
