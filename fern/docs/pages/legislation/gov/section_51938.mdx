# GOV § 51938

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 3. Reorganization Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 51938

---

## Text

A copy of the record of the canvass, with a statement of the proposition or propositions submitted at the election shall be filed with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 972.)
