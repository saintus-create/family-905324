# GOV § 5003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Registration of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 5003

---

## Text

After any bond is registered, the principal and interest of the bond are payable to the registered owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
