# GOV § 65460.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8.5. Transit Village Development Planning Act of 1994

**Citation:** GOV § 65460.3

---

## Text

To increase transit ridership and to reduce vehicle traffic on the highways, local, regional, and state plans should direct new development close to the transit stations. These entities should provide financial incentives to implement these plans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 780, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
