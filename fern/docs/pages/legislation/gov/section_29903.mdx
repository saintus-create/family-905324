# GOV § 29903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29903

---

## Text

To the right of or below each bond proposition on the ballot, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines, with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 15. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
