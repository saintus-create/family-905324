# GOV § 54906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 8. Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 54906

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any zones within a permanent road division created pursuant to Section 1162.6 of the Streets and Highways Code, where the zones are formed for a specific permanent road project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 269, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
