# GOV § 41603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 6. Chief of Police

**Citation:** GOV § 41603

---

## Text

The chief of police shall execute and return all process issued and directed to the chief of police by legal authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 50. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
