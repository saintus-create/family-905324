# GOV § 43100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 2. Alternative Methods

**Citation:** GOV § 43100

---

## Text

When collected, the net amount shall be paid to the treasurer of the city to which it belongs, under the general requirements and penalties provided by law for the settlement of other taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
