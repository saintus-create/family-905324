# GOV § 70322

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 3. Agreements Transferring Responsibility for Court Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 70322

---

## Text

Agreements for the transfer of responsibility for court facilities from the county to the Judicial Council may include multiple buildings within the county, and need not require a separate agreement for each building.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 9, Sec. 3. Effective April 23, 2008.)
