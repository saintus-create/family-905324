# GOV § 29329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 2. Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29329

---

## Text

Upon demand of the auditor or board of supervisors, the officer entrusted with the fund shall give an account of the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
