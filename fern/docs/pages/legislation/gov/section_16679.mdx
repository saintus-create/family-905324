# GOV § 16679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Fiscal Agent

**Citation:** GOV § 16679

---

## Text

Express charges and postage are a proper charge against the State.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
