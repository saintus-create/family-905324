# GOV § 8224.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Notaries Public > Article: 1. Notaries Public

**Citation:** GOV § 8224.1

---

## Text

A notary public shall not take the acknowledgment or proof of instruments of writing executed by the notary public nor shall depositions or affidavits of the notary public be taken by the notary public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1009.)
