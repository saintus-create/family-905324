# GOV § 50720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50720

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause relating to the bonds issued thereunder requiring the board to continuously operate all improvements acquired, constructed, or completed, in whole or in part, from the proceeds of the bonds in an efficient and economical manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
