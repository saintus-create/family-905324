# GOV § 26225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 13. Miscellaneous Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 26225

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of one or more counties may authorize studies of the feasibility of a high-speed transit system, to operate in one or more counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 328.)
