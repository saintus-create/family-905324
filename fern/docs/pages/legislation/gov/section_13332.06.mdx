# GOV § 13332.06

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2.5. Provisions Applicable to Appropriations of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13332.06

---

## Text

The California Coastal Commission, without regard to fiscal year, shall not be subject to the Statewide Cost Allocation Plan for statewide indirect costs established pursuant to Sections 13332.01 and 13332.02.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 228, Sec. 14. Effective August 11, 2003.)
