# GOV § 66950

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.43. LAKE TAHOE ACQUISITIONS BOND ACT > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** GOV § 66950

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Lake Tahoe Acquisitions Bond Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 305, Sec. 1. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 305.)
