# GOV § 29371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 4. County Officers’ Cash Difference Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29371

---

## Text

If the board elects to establish a cash difference fund, it shall by the same resolution also establish an overage fund for the use of each county officer or administrative head of a county department affected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 470, Sec. 36. (AB 1529) Effective January 1, 2013.)
