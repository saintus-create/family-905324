# GOV § 67590

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67590

---

## Text

Subject to any applicable provisions which may be contained in an indenture, the commission may invest any available funds in bonds issued by it and may hold, cancel, or resell such bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1079.)
