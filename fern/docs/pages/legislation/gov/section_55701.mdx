# GOV § 55701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Tax Sharing > Article: 1. Bradley-Burns Revenues

**Citation:** GOV § 55701

---

## Text

As used in this article, “legislative body” means the board of supervisors in the case of a county or city and county and the city council or board of trustees in the case of a city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 991.)
