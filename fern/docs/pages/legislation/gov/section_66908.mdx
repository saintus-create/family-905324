# GOV § 66908

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 6. Finances

**Citation:** GOV § 66908

---

## Text

The conservancy may receive gifts, donations, subventions, grants, rents, royalties, and other financial aids and funds from private and public sources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 19. Effective September 17, 1984.)
