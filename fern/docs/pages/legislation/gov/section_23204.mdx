# GOV § 23204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4. Minor Boundary Changes

**Citation:** GOV § 23204

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of the initiating county shall file its resolution with the board of supervisors of the other affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 702, Sec. 2.)
