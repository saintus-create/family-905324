# GOV § 7299.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.5. Use of a Foreign Language in Public Services

**Citation:** GOV § 7299.1

---

## Text

State agencies may, utilizing existing funds, contract for telephone-based interpretation services in addition to employing qualified bilingual persons in public contact positions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 259, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2008.)
