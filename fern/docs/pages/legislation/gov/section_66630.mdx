# GOV § 66630

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66630

---

## Text

The commission shall make a continuing review of all the matters referred to in Section 66603 and Section 66651.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 413, Sec. 2.5.)
