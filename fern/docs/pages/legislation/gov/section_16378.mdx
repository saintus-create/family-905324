# GOV § 16378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 2.5. State Motor Vehicle Insurance Account

**Citation:** GOV § 16378

---

## Text

The State Motor Vehicle Insurance Account is hereby created as a special account in the General Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1079. See same-numbered section added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 390.)
