# GOV § 11527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Administrative Adjudication: Formal Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 11527

---

## Text

Any sums authorized to be expended under this chapter by any agency shall be a legal charge against the funds of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 867.)
