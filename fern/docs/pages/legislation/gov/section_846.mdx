# GOV § 846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Police and Correctional Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 846

---

## Text

Neither a public entity nor a public employee is liable for injury caused by the failure to make an arrest or by the failure to retain an arrested person in custody.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
