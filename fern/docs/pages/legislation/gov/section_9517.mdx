# GOV § 9517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Enactment of Statutes and Adoption of Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9517

---

## Text

The Legislature shall finish its actions on the budget required by the Constitution by June 15th of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 980.)
