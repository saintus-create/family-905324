# GOV § 9406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Witnesses

**Citation:** GOV § 9406

---

## Text

If the contempt is committed before the Senate or Assembly, the Senate or Assembly may commit him for contempt by resolution entered on the Senate or Assembly Journal, as the case may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
