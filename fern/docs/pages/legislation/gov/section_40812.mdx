# GOV § 40812

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. City Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 40812

---

## Text

The city clerk shall perform any additional duties as are prescribed by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 44. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
