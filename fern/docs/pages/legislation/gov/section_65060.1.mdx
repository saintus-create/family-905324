# GOV § 65060.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65060.1

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the people of California have a fundamental interest in the orderly development of the urban regions of the State in which large segments of the State’s population are concentrated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
