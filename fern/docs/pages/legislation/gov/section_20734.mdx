# GOV § 20734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Member Contributions > Article: 4. Return of Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 20734

---

## Text

The payment of accumulated contributions to a former member shall include current year interest through the date in which the claim is filed with the office of the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 87. Effective January 1, 1997.)
