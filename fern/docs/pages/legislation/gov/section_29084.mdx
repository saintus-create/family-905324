# GOV § 29084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 4. Adopted Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29084

---

## Text

The budget may contain an appropriation or appropriations for contingencies in such amounts as the board deems sufficient.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 38. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
