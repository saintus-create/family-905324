# GOV § 68725

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.5. Commission on Judicial Performance > Article: 2. Co-operation of Public Officers and Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 68725

---

## Text

State and local public bodies and departments, officers and employees thereof, and officials and attaches of the courts of this State shall co-operate with and give reasonable assistance and information to the commission and any authorized representative thereof, in connection with any investigations or proceedings within the jurisdiction of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 564.)
