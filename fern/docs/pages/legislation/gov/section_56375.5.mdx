# GOV § 56375.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 56375.5

---

## Text

Every determination made by a commission regarding the matters provided for by subdivisions (a), (m), and (n) of Section 56375 and by subdivision (a) of Section 56375.3 shall be consistent with the spheres of influence of the local agencies affected by those determinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 355, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2005.)
