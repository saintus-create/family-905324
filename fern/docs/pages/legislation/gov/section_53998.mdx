# GOV § 53998

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.5. Sale of Proposition 1A Receivables

**Citation:** GOV § 53998

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “Proposition 1A receivable” means the right to payment of moneys due or to become due to a local agency pursuant to clause (iii) of subparagraph (B) of paragraph (1) of subdivision (a) of Section 25.5 of Article XIII of the California Constitution and Section 100.06 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 634, Sec. 6. (SB 67) Effective October 19, 2009.)
