# GOV § 53866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53866

---

## Text

If after public notice the lien or property cannot be sold for that total, the legislative body may by a four-fifths vote sell the lien or property for the best price obtainable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
