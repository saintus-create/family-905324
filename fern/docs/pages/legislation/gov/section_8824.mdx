# GOV § 8824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8824

---

## Text

The commission may apply for, receive, and distribute federal funds, state funds, and other public or private funds from any source for all or any purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
