# GOV § 8878.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.45. California Earthquake Safety and Housing Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8878.15

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Earthquake Safety and Housing Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 27, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 77 at the June 7, 1988, election.)
