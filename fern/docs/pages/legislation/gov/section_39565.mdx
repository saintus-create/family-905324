# GOV § 39565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 13. Weed and Rubbish Abatement > Article: 2. Alternative Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 39565

---

## Text

The heading of the notices shall be “Notice to destroy weeds and remove rubbish, refuse, and dirt” in letters not less than one inch in height.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
