# GOV § 22960.82

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Beneficiary Designation

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.82

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in the beneficiary designation form, each designated beneficiary shall be entitled to equal shares of the lump-sum distribution that may be payable from the participant’s account upon the death of the participant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
