# GOV § 56331.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Formation of Commission and Selection of Commissioners

**Citation:** GOV § 56331.3

---

## Text

If two or more members are absent or disqualify themselves from participating in a meeting of the commission, any alternate member who is authorized to serve and vote in the place of a member shall only have one vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1327, Sec. 4.)
