# GOV § 26730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26730

---

## Text

The fee for conducting or postponing the sale of real or personal property as required by law or the litigant is one hundred ten dollars ($110).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 29, Sec. 10. (SB 564) Effective January 1, 2024.)
