# GOV § 8897.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13.8. Residential Strengthening—Seismic Safety > Article: 2. Earthquake Evaluations

**Citation:** GOV § 8897.4

---

## Text

No transfer of title shall be invalidated on the basis of a failure to comply with this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 699, Sec. 1.)
