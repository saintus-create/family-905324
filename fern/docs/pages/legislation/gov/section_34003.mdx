# GOV § 34003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES GENERALLY > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 34003

---

## Text

The adoption of this title does not affect the organization of any city created or organized pursuant to any law repealed or superseded by this code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
