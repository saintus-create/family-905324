# GOV § 55608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 1. Performance of Fire Protection Functions by County Firewarden or State Forester

**Citation:** GOV § 55608

---

## Text

When a contract has been made, the Director of Forestry and Fire Protection or the county fire warden may exercise the same powers and duties within the county for the prevention and suppression of fires which by state or local law is conferred upon those officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 427, Sec. 70. Effective January 1, 1993.)
