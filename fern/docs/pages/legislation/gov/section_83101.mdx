# GOV § 83101

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 3. Fair Political Practices Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 83101

---

## Text

The chair and one additional member of the Commission shall be appointed by the Governor. The Governor’s appointees shall not be members of the same political party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 148. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
