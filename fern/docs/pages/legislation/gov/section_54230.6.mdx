# GOV § 54230.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 8. Surplus Land

**Citation:** GOV § 54230.6

---

## Text

The failure by a local agency to comply with this article shall not invalidate the transfer or conveyance of real property to a purchaser or encumbrancer for value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 664, Sec. 10. (AB 1486) Effective January 1, 2020.)
