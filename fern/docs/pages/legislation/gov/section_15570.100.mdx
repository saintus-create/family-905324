# GOV § 15570.100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Operative Date

**Citation:** GOV § 15570.100

---

## Text

This part shall become operative on July 1, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 5. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Note: Operative date provision applies to Part 8.7, commencing with Section 15570.)
