# GOV § 11252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11252

---

## Text

Such adjustments shall be made in accordance with the approved plan by filing a financial statement with the Controller, and requesting the Controller to make the necessary transfers or other entries upon his books. Upon receipt of such a request, the Controller shall make such transfers or other entries.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 222.)
