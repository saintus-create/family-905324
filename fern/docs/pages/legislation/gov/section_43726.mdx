# GOV § 43726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43726

---

## Text

The bonds shall be payable in such money and at such places as the legislative body designates in the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
