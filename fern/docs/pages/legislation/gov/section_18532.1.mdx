# GOV § 18532.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18532.1

---

## Text

“Preferred limited term list” means a list of persons who have served under limited-term appointment and who, in accordance with rule, are granted eligibility for additional limited-term appointments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 8. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
