# GOV § 51304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of City Functions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 51304

---

## Text

The city may provide in the contract for the payment to the county of a consideration agreed upon, which shall be paid to the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
