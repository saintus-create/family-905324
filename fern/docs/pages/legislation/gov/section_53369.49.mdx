# GOV § 53369.49

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.6. Infrastructure and Revitalization Financing Districts > Article: 4. Tax Increment Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53369.49

---

## Text

If any member of the legislative body whose signature appears on bonds ceases to be a member of the legislative body before delivery of the bonds, his or her signature is as effective as if he or she had remained in office. Bonds issued pursuant to this chapter are fully negotiable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 775, Sec. 1. (AB 229) Effective January 1, 2015.)
