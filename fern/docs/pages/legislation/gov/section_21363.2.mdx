# GOV § 21363.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13. Retirement Benefits > Article: 4. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 21363.2

---

## Text

(a) This section shall apply only to patrol members in State Bargaining Unit 5.

(b) Patrol members who were previously classified as peace officer/firefighter members shall have their past service under Section 21363 credited, at no cost to the member, under Section 21363.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 778, Sec. 3. Effective October 10, 1999.)
