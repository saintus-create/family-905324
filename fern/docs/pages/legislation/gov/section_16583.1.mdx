# GOV § 16583.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.3. The Accounts Receivable Management Act

**Citation:** GOV § 16583.1

---

## Text

A participant may impose a reasonable fee, not to exceed the actual costs, to recover the participant’s collection costs on a past due account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 23, Sec. 13. Effective July 28, 2009.)
