# GOV § 31899.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.9. Internal Revenue Code County Compliance and Replacement Benefits Program

**Citation:** GOV § 31899.9

---

## Text

The Legislature reserves the power and right to amend this chapter, as needed to effect its purposes. This chapter shall be controlling over any memorandum of understanding reached between employers and employees pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 3500) of Division 4 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31899.10 by Stats. 2003, Ch. 520, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2004.)
