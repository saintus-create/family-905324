# GOV § 3106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Oath or Affirmation of Allegiance for Disaster Service Workers and Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 3106

---

## Text

Compliance with this chapter shall, as to state employees, be deemed full compliance with Chapter 4, Part 1, Division 5, Title 2 of this code, requiring taking of oaths by state employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 7.)
