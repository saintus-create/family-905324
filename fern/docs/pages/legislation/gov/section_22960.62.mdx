# GOV § 22960.62

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.62

---

## Text

Pursuant to terms and conditions established by the board, a participant may be permitted to transfer funds from an eligible retirement plan into the plan to the extent that the transfers are allowable under applicable federal and state laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
