# GOV § 36931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Ordinances > Article: 2. Enactment

**Citation:** GOV § 36931

---

## Text

The enacting clause of ordinances shall be: “The city council of the City of ____ does ordain as follows:”.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
