# GOV § 58007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 58007

---

## Text

Unless otherwise expressly provided, each notice shall be published once a week for three successive weeks prior to the date set for the event of which notice is given.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
