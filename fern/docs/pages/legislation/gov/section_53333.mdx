# GOV § 53333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 3. Extension of Authorized Facilities and Services and Changes in Special Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53333

---

## Text

The petition shall request the legislative body to commence proceedings to make specified changes to a named community facilities district. The petition may consist of any number of separate instruments each of which shall comply with all the requirements of a petition except as to the number of signatures.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1102, Sec. 26. Effective September 24, 1986.)
