# GOV § 29143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 29143

---

## Text

Any unencumbered balance remaining to the credit of any appropriation shall lapse at the end of the fiscal year and shall revert to the available balance of the fund from which appropriated.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1209.)
