# GOV § 53594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 12. Financial Advisers and Bond Counsel

**Citation:** GOV § 53594

---

## Text

Injunctive relief shall be available, subject to judicial discretion, to prohibit or enjoin any violation of this article, but no violation shall affect the authority, validity, or enforceability of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1033, Sec. 21. Effective September 27, 1985.)
