# GOV § 20615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. School Employers

**Citation:** GOV § 20615

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, all of the provisions of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 20460) apply to contracts made pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
