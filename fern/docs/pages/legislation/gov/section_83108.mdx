# GOV § 83108

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 3. Fair Political Practices Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 83108

---

## Text

The Commission may delegate authority to the chair or the executive director to act in the name of the Commission between meetings of the Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 154. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
