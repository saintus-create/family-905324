# GOV § 23557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** GOV § 23557

---

## Text

The election shall be governed and controlled by the general election laws of the state so far as applicable, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
