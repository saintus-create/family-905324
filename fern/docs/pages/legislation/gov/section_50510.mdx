# GOV § 50510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50510

---

## Text

The notice shall contain the terms of the petition and an invitation for sealed bids for the leasing of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
