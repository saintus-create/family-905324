# GOV § 15845.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 15845.2

---

## Text

(a) There is hereby created within the Public Buildings Construction Fund an Expense Account into which will be deposited amounts received by the board as additional rental under any of its leases and any other money received by the board, other than proceeds of certificates or revenue bonds, as directed by the board.

(b) Notwithstanding Section 13340, there is hereby continuously appropriated, without regard to fiscal years, from the Expense Account to the board the amount necessary to pay for the administrative expenses and costs associated with the implementation of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 21, Sec. 9. (AB 119) Effective June 27, 2017.)
