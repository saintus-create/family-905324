# GOV § 15675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 9.5. Office of Tax Appeals > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Access to Public Records

**Citation:** GOV § 15675

---

## Text

The office shall publish a written opinion for each appeal decided by each tax appeals panel. The written opinion shall be published within 100 days after the date upon which a tax appeals panel’s decision becomes final.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 13. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Operative July 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 15680.)
