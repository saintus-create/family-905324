# GOV § 53098.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5.5. Regulation of the Environmental Management Plan by State Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 53098.1

---

## Text

After adoption or the annual revision of the Environmental Management Plan by the General Assembly of the Association of Bay Area Governments, any state agency that is required, pursuant to federal law, to review, certify, approve, or otherwise act upon such plan shall transmit such plan without change to the federal Environmental Protection Agency, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 934.)
