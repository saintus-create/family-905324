# GOV § 36521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36521

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, all laws relating to the official bonds of officers apply to bonds required by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
