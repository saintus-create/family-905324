# GOV § 18213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Administrative Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 18213

---

## Text

A regulation concerning the following may be adopted without public notice or comment:

(a) Selection and examinations. However, all of these rules shall be reasonably available to all interested parties.

(b) Classification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 935, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
