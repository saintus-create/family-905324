# GOV § 6085.470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1.1. Publication of Notice in Public Notice Districts > Article: 2. Public Notice Districts by County

**Citation:** GOV § 6085.470

---

## Text

The City and County of San Francisco contains a single, countywide public notice district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 703, Sec. 13. (AB 2881) Effective January 1, 2017.)
