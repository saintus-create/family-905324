# GOV § 22970.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.17

---

## Text

“Employee contribution” means the amount contributed by the participating employee to his or her account in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
