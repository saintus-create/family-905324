# GOV § 18702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 18702

---

## Text

The board shall create and adjust classes of positions in the state civil service in accordance with Article VII of the Constitution and this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 794, Sec. 15.)
