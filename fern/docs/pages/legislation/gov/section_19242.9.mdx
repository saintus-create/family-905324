# GOV § 19242.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Limited Examination and Appointment Program > Article: 2. Examinations and Appointments

**Citation:** GOV § 19242.9

---

## Text

Upon failure of the appointing power to terminate the appointment of the candidate within 30 days following the end of the job examination period, it shall be presumed that the candidate has qualified in the examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 738, Sec. 2.)
