# GOV § 19877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. Days and Hours of Work > Article: 4. Industrial Disability Leave

**Citation:** GOV § 19877

---

## Text

The department shall adopt any rules and regulations necessary for the administration of this article.

The appointing power of any officer or employee not a member of the civil service shall adopt any rules and regulations necessary for the administration of this article for such officers or employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 670, Sec. 7.)
