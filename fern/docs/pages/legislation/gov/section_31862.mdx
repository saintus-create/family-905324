# GOV § 31862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 16. Alternate Survivors’ Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31862

---

## Text

In counties to which this article applies, this article provides an alternative system of survivors’ allowance applicable only to the members who elect to participate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1591.)
