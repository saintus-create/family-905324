# GOV § 11349.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 6. Review of Proposed Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 11349.2

---

## Text

An agency may add material to a rulemaking file that has been submitted to the office for review pursuant to this article if addition of the material does not violate other requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1060, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 2001.)
