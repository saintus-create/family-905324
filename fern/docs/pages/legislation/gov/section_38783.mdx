# GOV § 38783

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 6.5. Reports of Residential Building Records

**Citation:** GOV § 38783

---

## Text

The ordinance shall provide that it shall be unlawful for the owner of a residential building to sell or exchange same without first having obtained and delivered to the buyer a report of residential building record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 894.)
