# GOV § 38462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 2. Parks Dedicated by City

**Citation:** GOV § 38462

---

## Text

Except as permitted by general laws allowing diversions of bond funds, money so transferred shall be devoted only to the purposes for which the bonds were authorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
