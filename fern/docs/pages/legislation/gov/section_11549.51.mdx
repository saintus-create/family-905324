# GOV § 11549.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.8. Office of Broadband and Digital Literacy

**Citation:** GOV § 11549.51

---

## Text

There is in state government, within the department, the Office of Broadband and Digital Literacy.

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 112, Sec. 3. (SB 156) Effective July 20, 2021.)
