# GOV § 9070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 3.5. Legislative Open Records Act

**Citation:** GOV § 9070

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that access to information concerning the conduct of the people’s business by the Legislature is a fundamental and necessary right of every citizen in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1246.)
