# GOV § 9403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Witnesses

**Citation:** GOV § 9403

---

## Text

The service of a subpena may be made by any person over 18 years of age.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
