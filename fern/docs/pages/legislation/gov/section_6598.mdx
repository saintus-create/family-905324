# GOV § 6598

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 4. Local Bond Pooling

**Citation:** GOV § 6598

---

## Text

Interest earned on any bonds issued by the authority shall at all times be free from state personal income tax and corporate income tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 868, Sec. 6. Effective September 23, 1985.)
