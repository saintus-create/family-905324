# GOV § 20198

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 6. Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 20198

---

## Text

Any building or improvement constructed by the board under this article shall be subject to the supervision of the board in accordance with rules and regulations established by the board with the assistance of the Department of General Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
