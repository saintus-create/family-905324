# GOV § 37621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 7. Hospitals Managed by Board of Hospital Trustees

**Citation:** GOV § 37621

---

## Text

The board may issue warrants for payments from the fund after due audit. The president and secretary of the board shall sign the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
