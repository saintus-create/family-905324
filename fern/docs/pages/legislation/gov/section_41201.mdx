# GOV § 41201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. City Assessor

**Citation:** GOV § 41201

---

## Text

Annually between the first Monday in March and July 1st, the assessor shall make out a true list of all taxable property within the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
