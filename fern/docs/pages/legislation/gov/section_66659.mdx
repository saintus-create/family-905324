# GOV § 66659

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 5. The San Francisco Bay Plan and Further Reports of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66659

---

## Text

The commission shall continue in existence until such time as the Legislature provides for the termination of the existence of the commission or for the transfer of the commission’s functions and duties to some other permanent agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 713.)
