# GOV § 40576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 6. Co-operation

**Citation:** GOV § 40576

---

## Text

The action provided in Section 40575 shall be brought against all persons, pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
