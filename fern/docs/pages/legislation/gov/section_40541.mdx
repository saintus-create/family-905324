# GOV § 40541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40541

---

## Text

Within 30 days after the effective date of the ordinance provided in Section 40521, the commissioners shall meet and organize as a board of commissioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
