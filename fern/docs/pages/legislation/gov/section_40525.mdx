# GOV § 40525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 3. Surplus Property Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 40525

---

## Text

Quarterly, semiannually, or annually, as the legislative body prescribes, the authority shall file with the legislative body a detailed report of all its transactions, including a statement of all revenues and expenditures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
