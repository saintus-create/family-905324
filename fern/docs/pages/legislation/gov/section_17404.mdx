# GOV § 17404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17404

---

## Text

Upon notification from the State Treasurer that a forged or erroneously endorsed state warrant has been charged back to the presenting financial institution, the State Controller may process a claim schedule directing payment to the original payee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 920, Sec. 9. Effective October 12, 1997.)
