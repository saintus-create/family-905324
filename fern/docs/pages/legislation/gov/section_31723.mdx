# GOV § 31723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 10. Disability Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 31723

---

## Text

The board may require such proof, including a medical examination at the expense of the member, as it deems necessary or the board upon its own motion may order a medical examination to determine the existence of the disability.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
