# GOV § 17002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Form, Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17002

---

## Text

Before delivering a warrant to the payee, the Controller shall, upon request, permit the Treasurer to endorse upon or attach to the warrant an order designating the place where it may be paid. At the option of the Treasurer, warrants may be made payable at his office or at some bank at which money of the State is deposited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
