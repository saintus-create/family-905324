# GOV § 18501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Purpose

**Citation:** GOV § 18501

## Text

This part and Part 2.6 shall be known as the State Civil Service Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 9. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
