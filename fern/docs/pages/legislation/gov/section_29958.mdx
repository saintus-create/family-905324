# GOV § 29958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 3. Special Improvement Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29958

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall keep the money arising from the sale of bonds issued pursuant to this article separate and distinct from all other county money in a fund called “General improvement fund.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
