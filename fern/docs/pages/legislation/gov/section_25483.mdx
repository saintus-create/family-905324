# GOV § 25483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 6. Purchase of Personal Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25483

---

## Text

All supplies furnished the county or any county officer shall be at a price no greater than is specified in the bid which is accepted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
