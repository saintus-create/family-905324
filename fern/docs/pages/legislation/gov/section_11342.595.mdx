# GOV § 11342.595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 11342.595

---

## Text

“Proposed action” means the regulatory action, notice of which is submitted to the office for publication in the California Regulatory Notice Register.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 59, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
