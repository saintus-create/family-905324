# GOV § 53395.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.8. Infrastructure Financing Districts > Article: 2. Preparation and Adoption of Infrastructure Financing Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 53395.13

---

## Text

After adopting the resolution pursuant to Section 53395.10, the legislative body shall designate and direct the city or county engineer or other appropriate official to prepare an infrastructure plan pursuant to Section 53395.14.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 210, Sec. 11. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2014.)
