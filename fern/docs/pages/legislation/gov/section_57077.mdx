# GOV § 57077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Resolution of Conducting Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 57077

---

## Text

If a change of organization consists of an incorporation, disincorporation, or consolidation of two or more cities, or if a reorganization includes an incorporation, disincorporation, or consolidation of two or more cities, the commission shall order the change of organization or reorganization subject to confirmation of the voters. A protest proceeding shall not be conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 20, Sec. 5. (AB 1822) Effective January 1, 2020.)
