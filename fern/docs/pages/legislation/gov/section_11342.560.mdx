# GOV § 11342.560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 11342.560

---

## Text

“Order of repeal” means any resolution, order, or other official act of a state agency that expressly repeals a regulation in whole or in part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1060, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2001.)
