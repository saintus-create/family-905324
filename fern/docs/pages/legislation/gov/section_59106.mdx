# GOV § 59106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 59106

---

## Text

“Bonds” includes:

(a) Bonds, their coupons, and accrued interest on matured bonds.

(b) Cash assessments and accrued interest and penalties where no bonds are issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
