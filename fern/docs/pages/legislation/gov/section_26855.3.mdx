# GOV § 26855.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26855.3

---

## Text

The fee for issuing a certificate pursuant to Section 995.640 of the Code of Civil Procedure is three dollars and fifty cents ($3.50).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 18, Sec. 23. Effective April 21, 1983.)
