# GOV § 18973.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Employment Lists > Article: 4. Veterans’ Preference

**Citation:** GOV § 18973.5

---

## Text

(a) For purposes of this article, an entrance examination is any open competitive examination.

(b) No veterans’ preference under this article shall be awarded to permanent civil service employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 75, Sec. 8. (AB 372) Effective January 1, 2014.)
