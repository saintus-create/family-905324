# GOV § 21111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 4. Reduced Worktime for Partial Service Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 21111

---

## Text

Any public agency employee, who is a local member of this system working standard hours on a full-time basis, and who is eligible to retire pursuant to Section 21118, may elect to participate in reduced worktime for partial service retirement pursuant to Sections 21110 through 21115.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
