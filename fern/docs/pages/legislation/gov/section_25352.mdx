# GOV § 25352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25352

---

## Text

The board may purchase, acquire, construct, equip, and maintain all necessary tanks, reservoirs, pumps, apparatus, motor vehicles, and other machinery necessary or proper to facilitate the performance of the work in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
