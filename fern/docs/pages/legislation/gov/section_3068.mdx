# GOV § 3068

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 3. Removal Other Than by Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3068

---

## Text

If an objection to the sufficiency of the accusation is not sustained, the defendant shall answer thereto forthwith.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
