# GOV § 45054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Service > Article: 3. Age Limits

**Citation:** GOV § 45054

---

## Text

This article does not prevent the legislative body from fixing minimum or maximum age limits for the employment of policemen or firemen.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
