# GOV § 53098

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5.5. Regulation of the Environmental Management Plan by State Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 53098

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, review, certification, and approval of any and all provisions of the San Francisco Bay Regional Environmental Management Plan, and any subsequent revision or amendments thereto by the Association of Bay Area Governments, shall be subject to the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 934.)
