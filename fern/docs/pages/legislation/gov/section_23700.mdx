# GOV § 23700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Charters > Article: 1. Enacting a Charter

**Citation:** GOV § 23700

---

## Text

Counties may enact charters or revise existing charters according to the provisions of this chapter.
