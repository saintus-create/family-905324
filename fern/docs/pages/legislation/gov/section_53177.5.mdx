# GOV § 53177.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 53177.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to the construction of any interchange which serves to connect two interstate freeways.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
