# GOV § 12523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 2. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12523

---

## Text

The Attorney General may appear for and represent the Indians of the State of California before the Indian Claims Commission created by an act of Congress approved August 13, 1946 (Public Law 726).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 84.)
