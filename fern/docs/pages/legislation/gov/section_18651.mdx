# GOV § 18651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. The State Personnel Board

**Citation:** GOV § 18651

---

## Text

The board may appoint and fix the compensation of a secretary and such other personnel as is necessary to carry out and perform the powers, duties, purposes, functions and jurisdiction of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
