# GOV § 12933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties > Article: 1. The Department

**Citation:** GOV § 12933

---

## Text

The department shall maintain liaison with the human relations commissions of cities, counties, and any city and county, and shall provide any information not designated by law as confidential to such commissions on request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 992.)
