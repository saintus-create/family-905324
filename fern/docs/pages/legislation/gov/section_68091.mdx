# GOV § 68091

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68091

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the board of supervisors in each county may specify by ordinance the compensation and mileage for members of the grand jury in that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1361.)
