# GOV § 8661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 18. Political Activity

**Citation:** GOV § 8661

---

## Text

No organization established under the authority of this chapter shall participate in any form of political activity nor shall it be employed directly or indirectly for political purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
