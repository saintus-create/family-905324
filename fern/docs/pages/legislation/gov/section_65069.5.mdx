# GOV § 65069.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 8. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 65069.5

---

## Text

The district board shall, in carrying out the provisions of this article, comply as nearly as possible with the provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 29000) of Division 3 of Title 3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
