# GOV § 84606

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4.6. Online Disclosure Act of 1997

**Citation:** GOV § 84606

---

## Text

The date that a filer transmits an online or electronic report shall be the date the filed report is received by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 662, Sec. 26. (SB 1239) Effective January 1, 2019. Conditionally operative on date prescribed by Stats. 2018, Ch. 662, Sec. 44.)
