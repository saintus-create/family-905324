# GOV § 54400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54400

---

## Text

The legislative body may determine and provide in the resolution for:

(a) The number of series in which the bonds are to be issued.

(b) The date the bonds are to bear.

(c) The maturity dates of the bonds, not exceeding forty years from their respective dates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
