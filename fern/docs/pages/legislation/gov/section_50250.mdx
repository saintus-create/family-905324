# GOV § 50250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 9. Abandoned Excavations

**Citation:** GOV § 50250

## Text

The county auditor shall enter each assessment on the county tax roll opposite the parcel of land.
