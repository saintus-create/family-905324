# GOV § 50253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 9. Abandoned Excavations

**Citation:** GOV § 50253

---

## Text

Laws relating to the levy, collection, and enforcement of county taxes apply to such special assessment taxes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1142.)
