# GOV § 1158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6. Salary and Wage Deductions

**Citation:** GOV § 1158

---

## Text

No provision of law prohibiting, restricting or limiting the assignment or order for wages or salaries in any way prohibits, restricts or limits the powers conferred in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
