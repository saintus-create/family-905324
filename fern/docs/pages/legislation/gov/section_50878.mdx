# GOV § 50878

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 3. Pensions and Death Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 50878

---

## Text

During great public emergency the chief of the department may assign members retired from that department to such duty as he directs. They have no claim against the local agency for payment for duty so performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
