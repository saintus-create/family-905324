# GOV § 50369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 3. Issuance of Bonds for the Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** GOV § 50369

---

## Text

The legislative body may insert in the deeds any conditions subsequent necessary to insure the use of the lands by the United States for the purposes mentioned in, and to carry out the provisions of, this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
