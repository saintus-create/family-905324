# GOV § 3599.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12.5. Legislature Employer-Employee Relations

**Citation:** GOV § 3599.50

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Legislature Employer-Employee Relations Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 313, Sec. 1. (AB 1) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative July 1, 2026, pursuant to Sec. 3599.84.)
