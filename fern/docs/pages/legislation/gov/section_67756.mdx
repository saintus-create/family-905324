# GOV § 67756

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.85. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL PUBLIC TRANSIT FINANCE > Chapter: 3. Regional Public Transit Revenue Measure Expenditures

**Citation:** GOV § 67756

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the allocation of revenues prescribed in this chapter is not intended to set any precedent for future funding relationships or agreements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 740, Sec. 4. (SB 63) Effective January 1, 2026.)
