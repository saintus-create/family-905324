# GOV § 26833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26833

---

## Text

The fee for certifying to a copy of any paper, record, or proceeding on file in the office of the county clerk is one dollar and seventy-five cents ($1.75).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 327, Sec. 74. Effective June 30, 1982.)
