# GOV § 37382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 2. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 37382

---

## Text

A city may lease city land used for agricultural or horticultural purposes upon which sewage or waste water is discharged for not to exceed twenty-five years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
