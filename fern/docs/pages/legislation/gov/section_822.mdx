# GOV § 822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions Relating to Liability > Article: 3. Liability of Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 822

---

## Text

A public employee is not liable for money stolen from his official custody. Nothing in this section exonerates a public employee from liability if the loss was sustained as a result of his own negligent or wrongful act or omission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
