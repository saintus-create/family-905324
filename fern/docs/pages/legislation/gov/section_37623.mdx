# GOV § 37623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 7. Hospitals Managed by Board of Hospital Trustees

**Citation:** GOV § 37623

---

## Text

Unless inconsistent with the terms of its acquisition, the title to property acquired for hospital purposes vests in the city. Actions involving the property shall be brought and defended in the name of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
