# GOV § 17200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17200

---

## Text

All provisions of this chapter referring to registered warrants are applicable to “reimbursement warrants” and “refunding warrants,” unless the context otherwise requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
