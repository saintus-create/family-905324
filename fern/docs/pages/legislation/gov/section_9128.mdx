# GOV § 9128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 6. Legislative Funds and Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 9128

---

## Text

Any money appropriated for legislative printing shall be disbursed under or pursuant to the direction of the Senate or Assembly as provided in the rules, orders, and resolutions of the Senate or Assembly or their joint rules and resolutions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 870.)
