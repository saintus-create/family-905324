# GOV § 11507.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Administrative Adjudication: Formal Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 11507.5

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 11507.6 provide the exclusive right to and method of discovery as to any proceeding governed by this chapter.
