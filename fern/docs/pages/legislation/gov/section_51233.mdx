# GOV § 51233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 2.5. Agricultural Preserves

**Citation:** GOV § 51233

---

## Text

When a county proposes to establish, disestablish, or alter the boundary of an agricultural preserve it shall give written notice at least two weeks before the hearing to the local agency formation commission and to every city within the county within one mile of the exterior boundaries of the preserve.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1120.)
