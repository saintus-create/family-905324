# GOV § 6733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6733

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim November 20 as “Transgender Day of Remembrance.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 41, Sec. 1. (AB 1741) Effective January 1, 2023.)
