# GOV § 13920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13920

---

## Text

The board may adopt regulations pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 governing any matter over which it has jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 37, Sec. 25. (AB 1817) Effective June 27, 2018.)
