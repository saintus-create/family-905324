# GOV § 66905.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 66905.8

---

## Text

“Local public agency” means a city, county, district, or joint powers agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 6. Effective September 17, 1984.)
