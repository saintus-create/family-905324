# GOV § 16703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Duplicate Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 16703

---

## Text

A copy of the petition for the order to show cause shall be served upon the Treasurer not less than 10 days prior to the time set for the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
