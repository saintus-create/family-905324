# GOV § 12033.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 3.2. Open Central Registry of Appointive Offices

**Citation:** GOV § 12033.2

---

## Text

The Governor shall make copies of all information on the State Appointments List and updated materials available to any organization or person for a reasonable fee not to exceed actual cost.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 669, Sec. 2.)
