# GOV § 31471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31471

---

## Text

“Pension” means payments for life derived from contributions made from funds controlled by the board of supervisors, or from funds of a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
