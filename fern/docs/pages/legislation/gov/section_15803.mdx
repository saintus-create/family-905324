# GOV § 15803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15803

---

## Text

This part provides an additional and alternative method for doing the things authorized herein and shall be regarded as supplemental and additional to powers conferred by other laws and shall not be regarded as in derogation of any powers now existing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1686.)
