# GOV § 22959.91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Part: 6.7. RETIRED PUBLIC EMPLOYEES VISION CARE PROGRAM > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Recovery of Medical Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 22959.91

---

## Text

The Retired Public Employees Vision Care Program shall be administered by the board or its designees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 265, Sec. 1. (AB 65) Effective January 1, 2010.)
