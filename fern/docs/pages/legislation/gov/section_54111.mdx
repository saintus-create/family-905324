# GOV § 54111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4.5. Reconstruction of Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 54111

---

## Text

When the legislative body has paid the board the total of the agreed rentals, the board shall convey to the local agency in the name of the State the building mentioned in the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
