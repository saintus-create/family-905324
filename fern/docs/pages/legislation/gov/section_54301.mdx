# GOV § 54301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 54301

---

## Text

This chapter is full authority for the issuance of bonds and the acquisition, construction, or improvement of any enterprise pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
