# GOV § 65044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 5. Statewide Environmental Goals and Policy Report

**Citation:** GOV § 65044

---

## Text

Upon completion of the State Environmental Goals and Policy Report, the Governor, prior to approval, shall seek the advice of the Legislature and for this purpose shall transmit the report to the Speaker of the Assembly and to the Senate Rules Committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1534.)
