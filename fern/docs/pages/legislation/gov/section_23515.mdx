# GOV § 23515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23515

---

## Text

All petitions shall be filed with the clerk of the principal county. All counterparts of a petition shall be filed at the same time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
