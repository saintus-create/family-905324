# GOV § 51930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 3. Reorganization Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 51930

---

## Text

If, voting on a proposition presented pursuant to Section 51929, an elector marks the voting target next to the word “Yes,” the elector’s vote shall be counted for reorganization and charter adoption, and if the elector marks the voting target next to the word “No,” the elector’s vote shall be counted against reorganization and charter adoption.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 18. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
