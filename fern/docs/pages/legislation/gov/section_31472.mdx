# GOV § 31472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31472

---

## Text

“Regular interest” means interest at 212 percent a year until otherwise determined by the board compounded semiannually on June 30th and December 31st.
