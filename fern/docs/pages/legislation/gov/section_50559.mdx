# GOV § 50559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 9. Sale of Improvements Financed by Special Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 50559

---

## Text

The notice inviting sealed proposals shall contain a description of the improvement, the terms and conditions of the sale, and the time and place when and where sealed proposals will be received.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1923.)
