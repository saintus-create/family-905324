# GOV § 25627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25627

---

## Text

The county board of forestry may employ a suitable and competent person as county forester to serve during the pleasure of the board and prescribe his duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
