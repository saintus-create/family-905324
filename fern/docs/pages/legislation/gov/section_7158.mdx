# GOV § 7158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14. State Tax Liens > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 7158

## Text

“Personal property” includes both tangible and intangible personal property.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 600.)
