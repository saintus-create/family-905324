# GOV § 43606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43606

---

## Text

An indebtedness is incurred within the meaning of this article to the extent of the principal amount of bonds sold and delivered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
