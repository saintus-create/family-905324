# GOV § 89522

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 9.5. Ethics > Article: 4. Campaign Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 89522

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be construed to permit an expenditure of campaign funds prohibited by Section 18680 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 108. Effective January 1, 1995.)
