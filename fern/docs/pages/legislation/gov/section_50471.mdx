# GOV § 50471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 6. Airports

**Citation:** GOV § 50471

---

## Text

All or any portion of land acquired by a governmental agency for airport purposes may be used for park or recreation purposes until actually needed for airport development.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 332.)
