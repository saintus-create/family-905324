# GOV § 40571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 6. Co-operation

**Citation:** GOV § 40571

---

## Text

The authority may enter into a contract for the purchase of said property over a period of years, not to exceed 40.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
