# GOV § 82051

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82051

---

## Text

“State measure” means any measure which is submitted or is intended to be submitted to the voters of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
