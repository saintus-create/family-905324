# GOV § 273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. People of the State > Article: 2. Political Rights and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 273

---

## Text

A citizen of the United States who is not a citizen of the State, has the same rights and duties as a citizen of the State not an elector.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
