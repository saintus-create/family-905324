# GOV § 65959.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 5.6. Environmental Permits

**Citation:** GOV § 65959.3

---

## Text

This article does not confer any new or additional authority over the issuance of environmental permits on the California Environmental Protection Agency or diminish in any way the existing authority of any other state or local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 952, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
