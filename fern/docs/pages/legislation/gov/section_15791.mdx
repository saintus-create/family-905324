# GOV § 15791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 15791

---

## Text

The board may authorize the purchase of property with improvements in lieu of purchasing sites and constructing new improvements when in its opinion it is for the best interest of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 170.)
