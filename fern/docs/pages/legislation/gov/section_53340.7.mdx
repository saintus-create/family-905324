# GOV § 53340.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 4. Procedures for Levying

**Citation:** GOV § 53340.7

---

## Text

If the legislative body supplies to the tax collector each year a listing of the specific amount due from each parcel within the district, and the tax collector bills for and collects the tax without reference to tax rate areas, then Sections 54900 to 54916.5, inclusive, are not applicable to the formation of, change in the boundaries of, annexation to, or existence of, community facilities districts created pursuant to this chapter and there is no requirement that the statement and map described in these sections be filed with the State Board of Equalization or the county assessor. This section shall not prevent the voluntary filing of such a statement or map. This section does not constitute a change in, but is declaratory of, the existing law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1440, Sec. 11.)
