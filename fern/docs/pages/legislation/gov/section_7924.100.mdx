# GOV § 7924.100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Election Materials and Petitions > Article: 2. Initiative, Referendum, Recall, and Other Petitions and Related Materials

**Citation:** GOV § 7924.100

---

## Text

As used in this article, “petition” means any petition to which a registered voter has affixed the voter’s own signature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
