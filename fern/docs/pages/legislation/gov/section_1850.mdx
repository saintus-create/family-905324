# GOV § 1850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Obtaining Possession of Office

**Citation:** GOV § 1850

---

## Text

Every officer is entitled to the possession of all books and papers pertaining to his office, or in the custody of a former incumbent by virtue of his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
