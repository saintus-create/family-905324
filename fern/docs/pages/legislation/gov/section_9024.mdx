# GOV § 9024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 2. Meeting and Organization of the Legislature

**Citation:** GOV § 9024

---

## Text

Members of the Legislature who did not take the oath of office at the assembling of the Legislature may take the oath at any time during the term for which they were elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
