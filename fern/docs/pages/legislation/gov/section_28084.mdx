# GOV § 28084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Classification of Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 28084

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of a newly created county shall fix the numerical population of the county which figure shall be validated by the Demographic Research Unit of the Department of Finance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 29, Sec. 111. (SB 82) Effective June 27, 2019.)
