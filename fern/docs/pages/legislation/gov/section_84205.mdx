# GOV § 84205

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 2. Filing of Campaign Statements

**Citation:** GOV § 84205

---

## Text

The commission may, by regulation or written advice, permit candidates and committees to combine campaign reports when filing as required by this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 278, Sec. 29. (AB 808) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally operative pursuant to Sec. 78 of Stats. 2025, Ch. 278. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
