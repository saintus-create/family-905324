# GOV § 13103.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. Infrastructure Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 13103.5

---

## Text

The department may perform audits, as it deems necessary, of the allocations or expenditures made in accordance with Article XIXB of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 728, Sec. 61. (SB 71) Effective January 1, 2013.)
