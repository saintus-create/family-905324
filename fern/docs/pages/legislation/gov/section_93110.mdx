# GOV § 93110

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.3. SISKIYOU RAILROAD AUTHORITY

**Citation:** GOV § 93110

---

## Text

The state is not liable for any contracts, debts, or other obligations of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
