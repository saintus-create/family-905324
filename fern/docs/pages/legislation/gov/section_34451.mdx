# GOV § 34451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** GOV § 34451

---

## Text

The charter may be proposed by a charter commission chosen by the voters of the city or city and county, at any general or special election, but no person shall be eligible as a candidate for the commission unless he or she is a registered voter of the city or city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 357, Sec. 10.)
