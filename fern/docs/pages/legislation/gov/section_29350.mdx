# GOV § 29350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 3. Salary Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29350

---

## Text

All fees directed to be paid into the county treasury shall be set apart as a separate salary fund, and shall be applied to the payment of salaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
