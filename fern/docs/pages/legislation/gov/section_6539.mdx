# GOV § 6539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6539

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the composition of the Board of Directors of the Orange County Fire Authority shall not include alternate members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 504, Sec. 1. (AB 1217) Effective January 1, 2017.)
