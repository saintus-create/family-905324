# GOV § 50965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.5. Volunteer Firefighters Length of Service Award Act > Article: 4. Contract Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 50965

---

## Text

When the governing body of a department desires to consider participation in the award system, the governing body shall ask the board for a quotation of the approximate contribution to the award system that would be required by the department for participation in the award system.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 651, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008. Operative March 1, 2008, by Section 50979.)
