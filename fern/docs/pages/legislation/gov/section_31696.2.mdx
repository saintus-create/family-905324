# GOV § 31696.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.8. Long-Term Care Group Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31696.2

---

## Text

(a) The full cost of enrollment in a long-term care insurance plan shall be paid by the enrollees.

(b) The long-term care insurance plan shall not become part of, or subject to, the retirement or health benefits programs administered by the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 491, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
