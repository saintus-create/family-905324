# GOV § 53586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 11. Provisions Applicable to All Refunding Bonds of All Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 53586

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 53583, the outstanding bonds of the project areas of the redevelopment agency of the City of San Bernardino, which were merged into one and designated Central City by Chapter 1227 of the Statutes of 1983, may be refunded on the first date on which the bonds may legally be called, or on any date or dates thereafter, or at the maturity date or dates of the outstanding bonds without regard to the dates on which the outstanding bonds may legally be called.

Nothing in this section shall be construed as modifying the requirements for low- and moderate-income housing set forth in Section 33471.5 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1291, Sec. 1.)
