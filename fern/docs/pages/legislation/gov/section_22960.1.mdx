# GOV § 22960.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.1

---

## Text

The State Peace Officers’ and Firefighters’ Defined Contribution Plan shall supplement the benefits provided under Part 3 (commencing with Section 20000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
