# GOV § 64700

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 64700

---

## Text

This title shall be known, and may be cited, as the Los Angeles County Regional Housing Finance Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
