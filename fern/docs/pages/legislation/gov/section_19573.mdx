# GOV § 19573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations From Service > Article: 1. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 19573

---

## Text

The Department of Human Resources may establish disciplinary criteria applicable to adverse actions taken by appointing bodies pursuant to the causes of discipline set forth in Section 19572.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 71. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
