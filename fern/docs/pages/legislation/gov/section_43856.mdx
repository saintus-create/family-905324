# GOV § 43856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 6. Compromise

**Citation:** GOV § 43856

---

## Text

The rights of any nonconsenting holder of bonds specified in the notice are not affected by the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
