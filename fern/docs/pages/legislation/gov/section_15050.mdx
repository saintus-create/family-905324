# GOV § 15050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. Western States Organized Crime Compact

**Citation:** GOV § 15050

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall study, in conjunction with representatives of the States of Nevada, Arizona, and Oregon, the necessity and desirability of an interstate compact relative to the control and suppression of organized crime.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1795.)
