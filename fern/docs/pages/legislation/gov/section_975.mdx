# GOV § 975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Payment of Judgments Against Local Public Entities > Article: 2. Funding Judgments With Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 975

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Board” means the governing body of a local taxing entity.

(b) “Local taxing entity” means a local public entity that has the power to levy ad valorem taxes, or ad valorem assessments, upon property within the territory of the entity.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
