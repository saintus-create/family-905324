# GOV § 66499.56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICIAL MAPS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE

**Citation:** GOV § 66499.56

---

## Text

The map shall become an official map for all the purposes of this division when certified, and filed, but not before.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 427, Sec. 20. (SB 1489) Effective January 1, 2023.)
