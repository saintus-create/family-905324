# GOV § 43064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43064

---

## Text

Every tax is conclusively presumed to have been paid after thirty years from the time it became a lien, unless the property has been sold pursuant to law for the payment of the tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
