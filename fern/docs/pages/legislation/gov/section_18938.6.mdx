# GOV § 18938.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Employment Lists > Article: 2. Examinations

**Citation:** GOV § 18938.6

---

## Text

The department or designated appointing power shall provide for the inspection of examination papers for all written test competitors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 38. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
