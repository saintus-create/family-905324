# GOV § 53595.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 13. Collateralized Borrowing

**Citation:** GOV § 53595.10

---

## Text

A local agency may pledge, sell, transfer, assign, or otherwise dispose of tax increment revenues for any purpose for which the general funds of the local agency may be expended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 902, Sec. 1. Effective October 8, 1993.)
