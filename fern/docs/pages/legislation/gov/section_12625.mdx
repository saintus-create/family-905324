# GOV § 12625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 8.2. International Student Exchange Visitor Placement Organizations

**Citation:** GOV § 12625

---

## Text

Subject to reasonable rules and regulations adopted by the Attorney General, the registry and copies of instruments and the documents filed with the Attorney General pursuant to this article shall be open to public inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 825, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
