# GOV § 25662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 3. Fish

**Citation:** GOV § 25662

---

## Text

If the right of passage cannot be purchased at a satisfactory price, the board may authorize condemnation proceedings to be commenced to procure the right.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
