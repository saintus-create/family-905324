# GOV § 949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Actions Against Public Entities

**Citation:** GOV § 949

---

## Text

The governing body of a local public entity may compromise, or may delegate the authority to its attorney or an employee to compromise, any pending action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
