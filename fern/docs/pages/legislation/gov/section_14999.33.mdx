# GOV § 14999.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Uniform Film Permit

**Citation:** GOV § 14999.33

---

## Text

(a) If a county, city, or city and county requires a permit for media production, it is encouraged to use the uniform film permit application form when processing film permit applications.

(b) A completed uniform film permit application which has been approved by the county, city, or city and county shall constitute the local film permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 302, Sec. 19. (AB 3256) Effective January 1, 2019.)
