# GOV § 68656

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.2. California Access to Justice Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 68656

---

## Text

The commission shall be subject to the Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law (Part 2 (commencing with Section 5110) of Division 2 of Title 1 of the Corporations Code). To the extent of any conflict between this chapter and the Nonprofit Public Benefit Corporation Law, this chapter shall prevail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 34, Sec. 7. (SB 133) Effective June 30, 2023.)
