# GOV § 423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 423

---

## Text

The California valley quail (Lophortyx californica) is the official bird and avifaunal emblem of the State.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
