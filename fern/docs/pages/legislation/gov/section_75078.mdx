# GOV § 75078

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 3.6. Benefits Payable

**Citation:** GOV § 75078

---

## Text

The allowance to the judge and to his surviving spouse under this article shall be paid by the State at the times and in the manner provided for the payment of salaries of justices of the Supreme Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1363.)
