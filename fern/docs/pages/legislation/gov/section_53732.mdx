# GOV § 53732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4. Capital Outlay Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53732

---

## Text

In making a levy, the legislative body shall not exceed any limitation upon its right to impose taxes prescribed by law except as authorized by law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 808.)
