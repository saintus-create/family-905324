# GOV § 65359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 6. Preparation, Adoption, and Amendment of the General Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 65359

---

## Text

Any specific plan or other plan of the city or county that is applicable to the same areas or matters affected by a general plan amendment shall be reviewed and amended as necessary to make the specific or other plan consistent with the general plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1009, Sec. 13.5.)
