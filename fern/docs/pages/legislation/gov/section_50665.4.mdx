# GOV § 50665.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50665.4

## Text

Proceedings are initiated when the legislative body passes an initiating resolution by a majority vote of all its members.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
