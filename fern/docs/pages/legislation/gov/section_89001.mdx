# GOV § 89001

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 9. Incumbency

**Citation:** GOV § 89001

---

## Text

No newsletter or other mass mailing shall be sent at public expense.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended June 7, 1988, by initiative Proposition 73, Sec. 3. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
