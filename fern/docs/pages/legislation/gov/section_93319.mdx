# GOV § 93319

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.5. CENTRAL CALIFORNIA RAILROAD AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of the Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 93319

---

## Text

The authority may not utilize or be a claimant for Transportation Development Act funds and shall not be entitled to receive funds from the Public Transportation Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 234, Sec. 1. (SB 325) Effective January 1, 2012.)
