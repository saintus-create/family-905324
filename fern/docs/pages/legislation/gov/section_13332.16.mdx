# GOV § 13332.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2.5. Provisions Applicable to Appropriations of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13332.16

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to appropriations to the Legislature, the Legislative Counsel Bureau, the Bureau of State Audits, the California Commission on Uniform State Laws, or the California Law Revision Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 12, Sec. 17. Effective May 7, 1993.)
