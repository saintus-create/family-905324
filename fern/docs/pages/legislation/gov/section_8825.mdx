# GOV § 8825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8825

---

## Text

The commission may conduct, through grants or contracts, research and demonstration activities in matters relating to public broadcasting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
