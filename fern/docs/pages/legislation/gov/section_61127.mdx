# GOV § 61127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Capital Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 61127

---

## Text

A board of directors may finance any enterprise and issue revenue bonds pursuant to the Revenue Bond Law of 1941, Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 54300) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
