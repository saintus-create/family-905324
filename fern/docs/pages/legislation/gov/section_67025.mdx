# GOV § 67025

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 67025

---

## Text

“Interim plan” shall mean the interim regional plan adopted pending the adoption of the regional plan.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
