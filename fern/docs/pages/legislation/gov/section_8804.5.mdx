# GOV § 8804.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8804.5

---

## Text

“Instructional programming” means public broadcast programming which is curriculum related, whether for credit or not.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
