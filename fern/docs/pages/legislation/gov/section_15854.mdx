# GOV § 15854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 11. PROPERTY ACQUISITION LAW > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 15854

---

## Text

Property may be acquired pursuant to this part by condemnation in the manner provided for in Title 7 (commencing with Section 1230.010) of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 164, Sec. 1.)
