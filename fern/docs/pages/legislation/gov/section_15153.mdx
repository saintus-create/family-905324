# GOV § 15153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. California Law Enforcement Telecommunications System

**Citation:** GOV § 15153

---

## Text

The system shall be under the direction of the Attorney General, and shall be used exclusively for the official business of the state, and the official business of any city, county, city and county, or other public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1595.)
