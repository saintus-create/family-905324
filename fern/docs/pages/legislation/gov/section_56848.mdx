# GOV § 56848

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 2. Reorganization

**Citation:** GOV § 56848

---

## Text

Approval by a reorganization committee of the report and recommendation shall require the affirmative vote of more than one-half of the subject districts represented on the reorganization committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 187. Effective January 1, 2001.)
