# GOV § 61119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Finance

**Citation:** GOV § 61119

---

## Text

All claims for money or damages against a district are governed by Part 3 (commencing with Section 900) and Part 4 (commencing with Section 940) of Division 3.6 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
