# GOV § 50804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 50804

---

## Text

“Member” means a person duly appointed or selected and sworn as a member of the regularly constituted police department if the local agency has established a police relief and pension fund pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
