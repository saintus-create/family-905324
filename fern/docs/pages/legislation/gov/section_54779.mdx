# GOV § 54779

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.6. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54779

---

## Text

The county may, out of any funds available therefor, purchase its bonds. The county may hold, pledge, cancel, or resell the bonds, subject to, and in accordance with, agreements with bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 293, Sec. 2. Effective July 21, 1994.)
