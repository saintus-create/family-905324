# GOV § 6023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 2. Establishing Standing as Newspaper of General Circulation

**Citation:** GOV § 6023

---

## Text

The court shall hear the proofs of the petitioner and contestant, if there be any. Within 10 days thereafter it shall render its decision and judgment and the clerk shall enter it in the records of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
