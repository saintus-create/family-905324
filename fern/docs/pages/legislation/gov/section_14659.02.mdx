# GOV § 14659.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 1.1. Government Claims Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 14659.02

---

## Text

The Department of General Services may assign any matter related to the statutory powers and duties of the Department of General Services described in Section 14659 to the Office of Risk and Insurance Management or to any other state office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 130. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
