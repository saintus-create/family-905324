# GOV § 3250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.6. Firefighters

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Firefighters Procedural Bill of Rights Act.
