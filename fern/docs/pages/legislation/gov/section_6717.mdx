# GOV § 6717

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6717

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim March 31 as “Farmworkers Day.”

(History: Amended by Stats. 2026, Ch. 7, Sec. 2. (AB 2156) Effective March 26, 2026.)
