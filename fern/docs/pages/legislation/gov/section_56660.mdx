# GOV § 56660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 56660

---

## Text

The executive officer shall give notice of any hearing by the commission by publication, as provided in Sections 56153 and 56154, and by posting, as provided in Sections 56158 and 56159.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 91. Effective January 1, 2001.)
