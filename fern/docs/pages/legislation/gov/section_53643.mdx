# GOV § 53643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53643

---

## Text

The treasurer may deposit any part of the money as agreed upon between the treasurer and the depository.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 248, Sec. 94.)
