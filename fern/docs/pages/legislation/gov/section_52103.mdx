# GOV § 52103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 52103

---

## Text

An authority may, subject to approval by the governing body, contract for the furnishing by any person, business, public utility, or agency, public or private, including community-based organizations, of services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
