# GOV § 68650

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.1. Civil Legal Representation

**Citation:** GOV § 68650

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Sargent Shriver Civil Counsel Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 457, Sec. 6. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2010.)
