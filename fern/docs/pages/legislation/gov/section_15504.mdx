# GOV § 15504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 8. LOCAL AGENCY ALLOCATION LAW > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15504

---

## Text

The Director of General Services shall provide the board with the assistance it may require in order to carry out the provisions of this part.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
