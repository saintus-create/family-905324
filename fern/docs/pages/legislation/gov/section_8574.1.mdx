# GOV § 8574.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 3.5. Oil Spills

**Citation:** GOV § 8574.1

---

## Text

In addition to any other authority conferred upon the Governor by this chapter, the Governor shall establish a California oil spill contingency plan pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 796, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
