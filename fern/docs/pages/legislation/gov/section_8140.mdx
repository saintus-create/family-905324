# GOV § 8140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.6. California-Nevada Interstate Compact Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8140

---

## Text

The commission shall be abolished upon the earlier occurrence of either the effective date of the compact, or January 1, 1977.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 301.)
