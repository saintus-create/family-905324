# GOV § 66540.50

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 4. Funding

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.50

---

## Text

The authority may deposit or invest any moneys of the authority in banks or financial institutions in the state in accordance with state law.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
