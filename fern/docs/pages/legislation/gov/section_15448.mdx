# GOV § 15448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 7.2. HEALTH FACILITIES FINANCING AUTHORITY ACT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15448

---

## Text

Any bonds issued under the provisions of this part, their transfer, and the income therefrom shall at all times be free from taxation of every kind by the state and by all political subdivisions in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1033.)
