# GOV § 10504.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Legislative Audit Bureau > Article: 1. Joint Legislative Audit Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 10504.1

---

## Text

At least one deputy auditor general shall be a certified public accountant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 833, Sec. 2. Effective September 19, 1985.)
