# GOV § 8880.47

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 5. Lottery Game Retailers

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.47

---

## Text

The commission shall promulgate regulations specifying the terms and conditions for contracting with lottery game retailers so as to provide adequate and convenient availability of tickets or shares to prospective buyers of lottery games.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 500, Sec. 7. Effective August 17, 1992. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
