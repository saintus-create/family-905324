# GOV § 6565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 6565

---

## Text

The indenture may provide for events of default and the terms upon which the bonds may be declared due before maturity and the terms upon which that declaration and its consequences may be waived.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 329.)
