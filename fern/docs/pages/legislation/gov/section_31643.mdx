# GOV § 31643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 7. Service

**Citation:** GOV § 31643

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in the regulations of the board, “prior service” means service prior to the date of entry of a member into the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
