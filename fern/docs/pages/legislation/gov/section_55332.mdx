# GOV § 55332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Joint Projects for Sanitation and Electrical Lines > Article: 2. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Conduits and Electrical Lines

**Citation:** GOV § 55332

---

## Text

All contracts for construction of conduits or lines pursuant to this article shall be made and entered into by one of the local agencies designated by the legislative bodies of all the local agencies and in the manner provided by Section 55308.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
