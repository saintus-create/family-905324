# GOV § 8880.53

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 5. Lottery Game Retailers

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.53

---

## Text

No lottery tickets or shares shall be sold by a lottery game retailer unless the retailer has a certificate of authority, issued by the lottery, to sell lottery tickets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 377, Sec. 3. Effective August 29, 1994. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
