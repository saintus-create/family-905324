# GOV § 6592.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 4. Local Bond Pooling

**Citation:** GOV § 6592.1

---

## Text

A resolution authorizing bonds or any issuance of bonds or accepting the benefit of any bonds or the proceeds of bonds shall be adopted by an authority only during a regular meeting held pursuant to Section 54954.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 557, Sec. 5. (SB 99) Effective January 1, 2010.)
