# GOV § 17400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17400

---

## Text

The State Treasurer may promulgate regulations as to the redemption of state warrants and agency checks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 622.)
