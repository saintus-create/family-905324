# GOV § 27600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 3. Expenses and Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27600

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall provide for the use of the surveyor a suitable office, office furniture, heat, light, and care for the office. It shall provide office and record books, and other necessary material. The board shall pay all necessary expenses and transportation on work performed in the field.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
