# GOV § 16113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. Special Supplemental Subventions

**Citation:** GOV § 16113

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “multicounty special district” has the same meaning as that term is defined in subdivisions (c) and (d) of Section 16271.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 447, Sec. 3. Effective July 16, 1984.)
