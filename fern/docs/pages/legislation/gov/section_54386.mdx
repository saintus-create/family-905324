# GOV § 54386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 3. Authorization of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54386

---

## Text

The votes of a majority of all the voters voting on the proposition are required to authorize the issuance of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
