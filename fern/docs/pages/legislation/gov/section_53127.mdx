# GOV § 53127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6.6. State Nonemergency Telephone System

**Citation:** GOV § 53127

---

## Text

The Public Safety Communications Division is authorized to aid local public agencies in the formulation of concepts, methods, and procedures that will improve the operation of systems authorized by this article and increase cooperation among public agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 404, Sec. 56. (AB 2408) Effective January 1, 2011.)
