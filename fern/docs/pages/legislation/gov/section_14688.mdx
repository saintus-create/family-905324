# GOV § 14688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 3. Buildings and Grounds

**Citation:** GOV § 14688

---

## Text

The department shall consider all matters of city planning affecting the future needs of the state and the relation of the state plans to those of the capital city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
