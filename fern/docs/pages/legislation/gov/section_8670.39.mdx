# GOV § 8670.39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 6. The Oil Spill Prevention and Administration Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.39

---

## Text

(a) The administrator shall administer the fund in accordance with this article.

(b) The administrator may develop and adopt any rules, regulations, and guidelines determined to be necessary to carry out and enforce this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 17. Effective September 24, 1990.)
