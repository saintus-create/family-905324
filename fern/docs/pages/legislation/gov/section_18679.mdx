# GOV § 18679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** GOV § 18679

---

## Text

The board and any person authorized by it to conduct a hearing or investigation is the head of a department within the meaning of Article 2, Chapter 2, Part 1, Division 3, Title 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
