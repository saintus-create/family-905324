# GOV § 50506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50506

---

## Text

If the legislative body has denied the protests, or if no protests have been made, it shall by resolution order the property leased pursuant to the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
