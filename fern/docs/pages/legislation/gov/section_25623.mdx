# GOV § 25623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25623

---

## Text

Whenever the board of supervisors desires to avail itself of this article, it shall by resolution or ordinance so declare.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
