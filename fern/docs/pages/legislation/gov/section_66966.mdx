# GOV § 66966

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.43. LAKE TAHOE ACQUISITIONS BOND ACT > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66966

---

## Text

The agency designated by the Tahoe Area Land Acquisition Commission, or if none is so designated, the California Tahoe Conservancy Agency, shall be deemed the “board” for purposes of Section 16722.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 305, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 4 at the November 2, 1982, election. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 305.)
