# GOV § 905.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 905.9

---

## Text

All claims against the California State University for money or damages based upon an express contract or for an injury for which the Trustees of the California State University is liable shall be presented in accordance with Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 900) and Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 910) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 636, Sec. 3. (SB 1046) Effective January 1, 2011.)
