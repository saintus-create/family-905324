# GOV § 34500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Corporate Name

**Citation:** GOV § 34500

## Text

The provisions of this chapter do not apply to chartered cities.
