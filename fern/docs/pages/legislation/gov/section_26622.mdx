# GOV § 26622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1.1. Sheriff as Ex Officio Director of Emergency Services

**Citation:** GOV § 26622

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall become operative in any county only upon their adoption by a resolution passed by unanimous vote of the board of supervisors thereof at a regular meeting at which all members are present. Such resolution may be repealed by the board of supervisors at any time by a three-fifths vote.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1555.)
