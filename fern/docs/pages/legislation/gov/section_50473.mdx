# GOV § 50473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 6. Airports

**Citation:** GOV § 50473

---

## Text

A local agency may levy taxes to raise funds to acquire land for the purposes of this article and to pay the principal and interest of bonds issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
