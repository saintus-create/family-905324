# GOV § 31685.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.4. Community Property

**Citation:** GOV § 31685.9

---

## Text

If a member becomes disabled, the combined benefit payments to both the member and nonmember shall not exceed the amount that would otherwise be paid to the member alone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 670, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
