# GOV § 4360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Preference for Materials > Article: 3. Services and Supplies of Agricultural Aircraft Operators

**Citation:** GOV § 4360

---

## Text

As used in this article

(a) “Agricultural aircraft operator” means any person who applies, from an aircraft, chemicals, seeds, or fertilizers.

(b) “Services” means the application, from an aircraft, of chemicals, seeds, or fertilizers.

(c) “Supplies” means supplies used by an agricultural aircraft operator in performing services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1107.)
