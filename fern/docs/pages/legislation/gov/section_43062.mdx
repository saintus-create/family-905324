# GOV § 43062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43062

---

## Text

Every tax has the effect of a judgment against the person, and every tax lien has the effect of an execution levied against all property of the delinquent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
