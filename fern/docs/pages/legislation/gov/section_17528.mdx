# GOV § 17528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Commission on State Mandates

**Citation:** GOV § 17528

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall elect a chairperson and a vice chairperson of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
