# GOV § 24152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 24152

---

## Text

Before qualifying the tax collector shall give an additional bond as license collector in such sum as is fixed by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
