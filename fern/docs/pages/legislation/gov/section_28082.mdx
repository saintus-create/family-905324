# GOV § 28082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Classification of Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 28082

---

## Text

The salary of any county officer for the term for which he was elected and qualified shall not be affected by reason of any division of the county or order of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
