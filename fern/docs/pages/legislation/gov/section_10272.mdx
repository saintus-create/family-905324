# GOV § 10272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 3. Commission on Uniform State Laws

**Citation:** GOV § 10272

---

## Text

Each appointed member of the commission shall be any of the following:

(a) A member in good standing of the State Bar of California.

(b) A person admitted to practice before the highest court of any other state of the United States.

(c) A judge of a court of record in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 17. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012.)
