# GOV § 22970.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. The Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.40

---

## Text

The Supplemental Contributions Program Fund is hereby established as a special trust fund in the State Treasury to accept participant contributions to the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
