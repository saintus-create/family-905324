# GOV § 43773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 43773

---

## Text

At such times as the legislative body determines, it may sell any of the bonds purchased by it. The bonds shall not be sold at a price less than that paid for them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
