# GOV § 50748

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50748

---

## Text

All bonds, and the interest or income therefrom, are exempt from all taxation in this State other than gift, inheritance and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
