# GOV § 53176.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 53176.5

---

## Text

The inadvertent failure of any person to receive a notice, resolution, order, or other matter shall not affect in any way whatsoever the validity of any proceedings taken under this chapter, or prevent the legislative body from proceeding with any hearing so noticed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
