# GOV § 19808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 19808

---

## Text

Local agencies shall provide such information and reports relating to merit system administration as are required by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 101. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
