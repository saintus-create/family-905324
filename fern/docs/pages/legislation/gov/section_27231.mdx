# GOV § 27231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 2. Books

**Citation:** GOV § 27231

---

## Text

The recorder has the custody of, and shall keep all books, records, maps, and papers deposited in the recorder’s office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 269, Sec. 8. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2016.)
