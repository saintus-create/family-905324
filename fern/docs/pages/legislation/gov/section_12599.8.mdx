# GOV § 12599.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 7. Supervision of Trustees and Fundraisers for Charitable Purposes Act

**Citation:** GOV § 12599.8

---

## Text

For any year that the balance sheet of a charitable organization shows that it holds restricted net assets, while reporting negative unrestricted net assets, the organization shall provide an explanation of its compliance with its charitable trust responsibilities and proof of directors’ and officers’ liability insurance coverage to the Attorney General’s Registry of Charities and Fundraisers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 478, Sec. 35. (AB 1756) Effective January 1, 2024.)
