# GOV § 4216.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3.1. Protection of Underground Infrastructure > Article: 2. Regional Notification Center System

**Citation:** GOV § 4216.11

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2020, the board shall adopt regulations to establish minimum elements for the onsite meeting and minimum elements for the mutually agreed-upon plan described in paragraph (1) of subdivision (c) of Section 4616.10 for managing an area of continual excavation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 51. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
