# GOV § 53123.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6.3. Miles Hall Lifeline and Suicide Prevention Act

**Citation:** GOV § 53123.1

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the “Miles Hall Lifeline and Suicide Prevention Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 747, Sec. 2. (AB 988) Effective September 29, 2022.)
