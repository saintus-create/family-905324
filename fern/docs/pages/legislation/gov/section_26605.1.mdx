# GOV § 26605.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 26605.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no deputy sheriff shall be required to become a custodial or other officer involuntarily.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1236, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
