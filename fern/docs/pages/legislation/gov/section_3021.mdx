# GOV § 3021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 2. Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3021

---

## Text

All impeachments shall be by resolution adopted, originated in, and conducted by managers elected by the Assembly.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
