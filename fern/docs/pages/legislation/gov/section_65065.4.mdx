# GOV § 65065.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 5. Powers and Duties of District

**Citation:** GOV § 65065.4

---

## Text

The board, acting with the advice of the director, shall determine the compensation, number, and general duties of personnel employed by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
