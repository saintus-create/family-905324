# GOV § 53804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 6.5. Building Certificates

## Text

The preparation of plans and specifications of any public building constructed pursuant to this article shall be the responsibility of the board.
