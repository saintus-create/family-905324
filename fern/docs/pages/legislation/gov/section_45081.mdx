# GOV § 45081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Service > Article: 4. Emergency Appointments

**Citation:** GOV § 45081

---

## Text

An emergency appointee does not acquire civil service or other permanent status because of the emergency appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
