# GOV § 24255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Offices and Office Hours

**Citation:** GOV § 24255

---

## Text

All offices required by this chapter shall be provided by the county through the board of supervisors.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
