# GOV § 31685.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.4. Community Property

**Citation:** GOV § 31685.01

---

## Text

Benefits enumerated in this article shall be determined in accordance with the provisions of this chapter or the California Public Employees’ Pension Reform Act of 2013, whichever is applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 247, Sec. 42. (AB 1380) Effective January 1, 2014.)
