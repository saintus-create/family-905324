# GOV § 65098.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2.8. Warehouse Design and Build Standards

**Citation:** GOV § 65098.7

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to supersede mitigation measures required by the California Environmental Quality Act (Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000) of the Public Resources Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 931, Sec. 1. (AB 98) Effective January 1, 2025.)
