# GOV § 15163

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. California Law Enforcement Telecommunications System

**Citation:** GOV § 15163

---

## Text

The system shall provide service to any law enforcement agency qualified by the committee which, at its own expense, desires connection through the county terminal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1595.)
