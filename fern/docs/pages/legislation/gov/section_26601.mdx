# GOV § 26601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 26601

---

## Text

The sheriff shall arrest and take before the nearest magistrate for examination all persons who attempt to commit or who have committed a public offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
