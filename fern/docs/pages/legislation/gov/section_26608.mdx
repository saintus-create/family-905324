# GOV § 26608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 26608

---

## Text

The sheriff shall serve all process and notices in the manner prescribed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
