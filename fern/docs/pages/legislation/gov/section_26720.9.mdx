# GOV § 26720.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26720.9

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the amounts set forth in Sections 26721, 26721.1, 26725, 26728, 26734, 26742, and 26743 shall be fifty dollars ($50).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 29, Sec. 1. (SB 564) Effective January 1, 2024.)
