# GOV § 6511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6511

---

## Text

The agreement shall provide for the disposition, division, or distribution of any property acquired as the result of the joint exercise of powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 84.)
