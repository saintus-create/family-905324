# GOV § 100008

**Hierarchy:** Title: 21. THE CALSAVERS RETIREMENT SAVINGS TRUST ACT

**Citation:** GOV § 100008

---

## Text

The CalSavers Retirement Savings Program shall include, as determined by the board, one or more payroll deduction IRA arrangements.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 37, Sec. 32. (AB 1817) Effective June 27, 2018.)
