# GOV § 11346.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 5. Public Participation: Procedure for Adoption of Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 11346.7

---

## Text

The office shall maintain a link on its website to the website maintained by the Small Business Advocate that also includes the telephone number of the Small Business Advocate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1059, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2001.)
