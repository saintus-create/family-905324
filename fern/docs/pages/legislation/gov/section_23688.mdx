# GOV § 23688

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Seats > Article: 2. Removal of County Seats

**Citation:** GOV § 23688

---

## Text

The board shall transmit a certified copy of the notice of the selection of a county seat to the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
