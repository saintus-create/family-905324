# GOV § 7921.500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. General Rules Governing Disclosure > Article: 2. Voluntary Disclosure

**Citation:** GOV § 7921.500

---

## Text

Unless disclosure is otherwise prohibited by law, the provisions listed in Section 7920.505 do not prevent any agency from opening its records concerning the administration of the agency to public inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
