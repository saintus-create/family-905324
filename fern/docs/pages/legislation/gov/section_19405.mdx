# GOV § 19405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Service > Article: 2. Upward Mobility

**Citation:** GOV § 19405

---

## Text

The department shall annually submit a report to the Legislature on the performance of each appointing authority and agency in state government in meeting its obligations under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 72. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
