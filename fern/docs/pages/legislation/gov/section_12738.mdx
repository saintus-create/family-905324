# GOV § 12738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Community Services Block Grant Program > Article: 3. State Application and Assurances

**Citation:** GOV § 12738

---

## Text

In addition to the general powers vested in the Department of Community Services and Development by Section 12087, the department may promulgate regulations, make grants, and enter into contracts as necessary and appropriate to carry out its responsibilities under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 46, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2008. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 12790.)
