# GOV § 14039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 14039

---

## Text

The department shall have no authority to operate railroads.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 161.)
