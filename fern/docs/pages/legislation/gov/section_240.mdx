# GOV § 240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. People of the State > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 240

---

## Text

The people, as a political body, consist of:

(a) Citizens who are electors.

(b) Citizens not electors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
