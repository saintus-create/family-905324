# GOV § 38409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38409

---

## Text

Upon ordering the park abandoned, the legislative body shall appoint and fix the compensation of three appraisers to assess the damages to abutting property owners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
