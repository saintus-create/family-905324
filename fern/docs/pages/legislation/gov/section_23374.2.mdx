# GOV § 23374.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 4.5. Election to Select County Officers and Location of County Seat in the Approved County

**Citation:** GOV § 23374.2

---

## Text

The proclamation and notice of election provided for pursuant to this article shall be published at least once a week for three weeks commencing not less than 70 days prior to the date of the election in a newspaper of general circulation in the approved county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 370.)
