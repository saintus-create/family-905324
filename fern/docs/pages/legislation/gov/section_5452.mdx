# GOV § 5452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5.5. Collateral Security for Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 5452

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize new or increased authority to issue bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 966, Sec. 1.)
