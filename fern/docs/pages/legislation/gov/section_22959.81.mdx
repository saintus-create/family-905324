# GOV § 22959.81

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Part: 6.5. CALIFORNIA STATE UNIVERSITY ANNUITANT VISION CARE PROGRAM > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Recovery of Medical Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 22959.81

---

## Text

The California State University Annuitant Vision Care Program shall be administered by the Office of the Chancellor of the California State University.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 344, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
