# GOV § 12227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12227

---

## Text

The Chief of Archives is responsible for the preservation and description of material deposited in the State Archives and shall make the material readily available for use.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 145, Sec. 3. (AB 1759) Effective January 1, 2024.)
