# GOV § 50788

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 6. Contributions and Cooperation

**Citation:** GOV § 50788

---

## Text

The board may cooperate and contract with the United States under the War Mobilization and Reconversion Act of 1944, approved October 3, 1944, and any acts amendatory or supplemental thereto or any other act of Congress heretofore or hereafter enacted, authorizing or permitting such cooperation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
