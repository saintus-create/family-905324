# GOV § 11810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8.1. State Government Strategic Planning and Performance Review Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 11810

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the State Government Strategic Planning and Performance and Review Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 779, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1994.)
