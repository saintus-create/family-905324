# GOV § 27307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 3.5. Social Security Number Truncation Program

**Citation:** GOV § 27307

---

## Text

A county recorder is authorized to take all actions required by this article notwithstanding subdivision (d) of Section 27203 or any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 627, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2008.)
