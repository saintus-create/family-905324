# GOV § 31612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 5.5. Alternative Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 31612

---

## Text

“Actuarial rate” means the interest assumption rate established by the most recent actuarial survey recommended by the board of retirement and adopted by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31510.2 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 193, Sec. 56.)
