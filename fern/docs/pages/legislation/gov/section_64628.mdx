# GOV § 64628

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 2. Commercial Linkage Fee

**Citation:** GOV § 64628

---

## Text

Any action by the executive board and the authority board or interested person under this article shall be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 598, Sec. 1. (AB 1487) Effective January 1, 2020.)
