# GOV § 8503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. The Milton Marks Commission on California State Government Organization and Economy > Article: 1. Creation and Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 8503

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall serve without compensation, but shall be reimbursed for all necessary expenses actually incurred in the performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8502 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1625.)
