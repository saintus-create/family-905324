# GOV § 68755

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.5. Commission on Judicial Performance > Article: 3. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** GOV § 68755

---

## Text

No award of costs shall be made in any proceeding before the commission, masters, or Supreme Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 564.)
