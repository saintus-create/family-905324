# GOV § 38758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5.5. Transfer of City Waterworks Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 38758

---

## Text

If the transaction is a sale, either for cash or after the completion of installment payments, the deed conveys to the municipal water district the full title to the property described.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 329.)
