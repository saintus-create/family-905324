# GOV § 422.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 422.7

---

## Text

The California red-legged frog (Rana draytonii) is the official state amphibian.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 77, Sec. 2. (AB 2364) Effective January 1, 2015.)
