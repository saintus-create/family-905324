# GOV § 69894.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 8. Officers, Attaches, and Employees Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 69894.5

---

## Text

The court may by rule employ and assign officers or attachés to perform the duties outlined in Section 26806 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 769.)
