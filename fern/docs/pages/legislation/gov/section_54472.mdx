# GOV § 54472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54472

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for the terms upon which the bondholders or any percentage of them may enforce covenants or duties imposed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
