# GOV § 29637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Charges > Article: 2. Victims of Crime

**Citation:** GOV § 29637

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, the legislative body of a county or a city may by resolution determine that a resident may be reimbursed for any losses not otherwise covered by insurance.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 313.)
