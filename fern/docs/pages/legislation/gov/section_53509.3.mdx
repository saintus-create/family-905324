# GOV § 53509.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4.5. General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53509.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall limit the authority of the legislative body to enter into any contract in connection with the issuance of the bonds which it is permitted by Section 5922 to enter into.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 841, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
