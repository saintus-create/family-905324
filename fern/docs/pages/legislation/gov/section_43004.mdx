# GOV § 43004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43004

---

## Text

Deeds made upon any sale of property for taxes or special assessments have the same effect in evidence as deeds for property sold for nonpayment of county taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
