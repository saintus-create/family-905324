# GOV § 54551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 7. Fiscal Agent

**Citation:** GOV § 54551

---

## Text

The local agency may:

(a) Provide for the powers, duties, functions, and compensation of the fiscal agent.

(b) Limit the liabilities of the fiscal agent.

(c) Prescribe a method for his resignation and removal, and the merger or consolidation of fiscal agents.

(d) Prescribe a method for the appointment of a successor fiscal agent and the transfer of rights and properties to the successor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
