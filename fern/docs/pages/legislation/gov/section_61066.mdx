# GOV § 61066

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 61066

---

## Text

A board of directors may require any employee or officer to be bonded. The district shall pay the cost of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
