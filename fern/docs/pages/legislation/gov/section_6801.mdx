# GOV § 6801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Computation of Time

**Citation:** GOV § 6801

---

## Text

Time is computed according to the Gregorian or new style; and January 1st, in every year, after 1752 is reckoned as the first day of the year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
