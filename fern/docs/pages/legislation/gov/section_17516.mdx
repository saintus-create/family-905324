# GOV § 17516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 17516

---

## Text

“Executive order” means an order, plan, requirement, rule, or regulation issued by any of the following:

(a) The Governor.

(b) An officer or official serving at the pleasure of the Governor.

(c) An agency, department, board, or commission of state government.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 288, Sec. 1. (SB 1169) Effective January 1, 2011.)
