# GOV § 34872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 2. Election of Legislative Body By or From Districts in Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34872

---

## Text

An ordinance shall state the number of legislative districts and whether members of the legislative body shall be elected by districts, from districts, by districts with an elective mayor, or from districts with an elective mayor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 736, Sec. 1. (AB 278) Effective January 1, 2017.)
