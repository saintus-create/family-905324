# GOV § 11081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 5. Advertisements

**Citation:** GOV § 11081

---

## Text

After approval of the notice, advertisement or publication, the Department of General Services shall cause it to be published in the newspapers or other medium required by law. The Department of General Services has exclusive control of the publication of all such advertisements, publications, and notices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
