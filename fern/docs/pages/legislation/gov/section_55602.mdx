# GOV § 55602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 1. Performance of Fire Protection Functions by County Firewarden or State Forester

**Citation:** GOV § 55602

---

## Text

As used in this article, “forest fire” means any fire burning uncontrolled on any lands partially or wholly covered by timber, brush, grass, grain, or other inflammable vegetation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
