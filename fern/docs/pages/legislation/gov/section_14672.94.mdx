# GOV § 14672.94

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. State Property

**Citation:** GOV § 14672.94

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14670, the Director of General Services may let to Solano Community College, for a period not to exceed 25 years, for the purpose of establishing a fire science training center, real property located within the grounds of the California Medical Facility in Vacaville.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1042, Sec. 1. Effective September 12, 1984.)
