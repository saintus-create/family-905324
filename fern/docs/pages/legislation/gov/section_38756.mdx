# GOV § 38756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5.5. Transfer of City Waterworks Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 38756

---

## Text

The sale, lease, or transfer on terms shall be for a valuable consideration, which shall be agreed upon between the legislative body of the city and the board of directors of the municipal water district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 329.)
