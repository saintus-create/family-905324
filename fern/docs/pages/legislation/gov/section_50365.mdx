# GOV § 50365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 3. Issuance of Bonds for the Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** GOV § 50365

---

## Text

Except as modified in this article, Article 1, Chapter 4, Division 4, Title 4 is applicable to bonds issued by a city pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
