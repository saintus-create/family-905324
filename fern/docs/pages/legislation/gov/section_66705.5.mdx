# GOV § 66705.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.25. SAN FRANCISCO BAY RESTORATION AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 6. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66705.5

---

## Text

The authority shall be funded through gifts, donations, grants, state or local bonds, assessments, other appropriate funding sources, and other types of financial assistance from public and private sources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 690, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009. Repealed as of January 1, 2049, pursuant to Section 66706.)
