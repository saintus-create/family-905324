# GOV § 17201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17201

---

## Text

The Department of General Services may make rules and regulations governing the issuance and sale of registered warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 143. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
