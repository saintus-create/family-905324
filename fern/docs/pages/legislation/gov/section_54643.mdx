# GOV § 54643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 10. Remedies of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 54643

---

## Text

By a suit in equity a bondholder or his trustee may require the local agency and its legislative body to account as the trustee of an express trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
