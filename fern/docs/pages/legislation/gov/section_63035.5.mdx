# GOV § 63035.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 2. California Infrastructure and Economic Development Bank > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 63035.5

---

## Text

The report required by Section 63035 shall be submitted to the Governor and the Joint Legislative Budget Committee on a quarterly basis during the 1999–2000 fiscal year and the 2000–01 fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 84, Sec. 3. Effective July 12, 1999.)
