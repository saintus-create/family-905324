# GOV § 65926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65926

---

## Text

“Air pollution control district” means any district created or continued in existence pursuant to the provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 40000) of Division 26 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1200.)
