# GOV § 21038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 5. Credit for Public Service

**Citation:** GOV § 21038

---

## Text

All contributions of a member under this article shall be considered to be and shall be administered as normal contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 906, Sec. 128. Effective January 1, 1997.)
