# GOV § 56023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56023

---

## Text

“City” means any incorporated chartered or general law city, including any city the name of which includes the word “town.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 15. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
