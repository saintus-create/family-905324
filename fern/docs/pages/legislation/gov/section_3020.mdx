# GOV § 3020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 2. Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3020

---

## Text

State officers elected on a statewide basis, members of the State Board of Equalization, and judges of state courts are subject to impeachment for misconduct in office.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 312.)
