# GOV § 38794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 38794

---

## Text

The legislative body of a city may contract for ambulance service to serve the residents of the city as convenience requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 182.)
