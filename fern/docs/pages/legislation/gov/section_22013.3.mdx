# GOV § 22013.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22013.3

---

## Text

“Policeman” as used in this part also includes persons employed in positions set forth in Section 20403 for the purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 678, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1999.)
