# GOV § 14703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 4. State Burial Grounds

**Citation:** GOV § 14703

---

## Text

The department may employ such employees as are necessary to perform its duties concerning the state burial grounds in the City of Sacramento and the Union Cemetery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
