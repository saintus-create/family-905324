# GOV § 53745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4.5. Tort Liability Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53745

---

## Text

At any time after the creation of the fund the legislative body may transfer to the fund any unencumbered surplus funds remaining on hand at the end of the fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1059.)
