# GOV § 50660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 3. Negotiability

**Citation:** GOV § 50660

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” includes any board or department of the local agency.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
