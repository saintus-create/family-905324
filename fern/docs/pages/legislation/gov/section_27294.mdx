# GOV § 27294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 3. Documents to be Recorded

**Citation:** GOV § 27294

---

## Text

In all cases the recorder shall endorse the amount of the fee for recordation on the instrument, paper, or notice recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 7. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
