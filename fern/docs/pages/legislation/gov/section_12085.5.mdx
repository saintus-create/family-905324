# GOV § 12085.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 8. Department of Community Services and Development

**Citation:** GOV § 12085.5

---

## Text

Any reference in any provision of law or regulation to the State Office of Economic Opportunity or the Department of Economic Opportunity shall be deemed to refer to the Department of Community Services and Development.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 46, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
