# GOV § 53389.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 5. Senior Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53389.7

---

## Text

If any proposition submitted to the voters pursuant to this chapter is defeated by the voters, the legislative body shall not submit, or cause to be submitted, a similar proposition to the voters for at least six months after the first election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
