# GOV § 9124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 5.5. Legislative Office Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 9124

---

## Text

(a) The determination of the Senate Committee on Rules and the Assembly Committee on Rules as to the needs of the Legislature shall be subject to change only by action of those committees or by concurrent resolution.

(b) The Department of General Services shall provide maintenance and operation services in connection with the legislative office facilities as requested by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1366, Sec. 2. Effective October 2, 1989.)
