# GOV § 26022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Transportation and Airports > Article: 2. Airports

**Citation:** GOV § 26022

---

## Text

The board by resolution shall specify the general nature of the improvement proposed, the nature of the aid to be furnished by the county, and any funds from which the aid is to be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
