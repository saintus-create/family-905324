# GOV § 32260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 3. Board of Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 32260

---

## Text

The board shall organize by choosing one of its number as chairman. Thereafter the officers shall be chosen biennially by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
