# GOV § 67047

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 3. California Tahoe Regional Planning Agency > Article: 1. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 67047

---

## Text

The governing body shall elect from its own members a chairman and vice chairman, whose terms of office shall be two years, and who shall be subject to reelections, provided that a new chairman or vice chairman may be elected by the agency before the expiration of the two-year term for the fulfillment of that two-year term.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
