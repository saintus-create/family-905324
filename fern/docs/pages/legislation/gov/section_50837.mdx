# GOV § 50837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 2. Organization, Powers, and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 50837

---

## Text

The board shall make an annual report in June to the legislative body of the local agency. The report shall state:

(a) The condition of the fund.

(b) The receipts and disbursements on account of the fund.

(c) A list of the beneficiaries of the fund and the amounts paid them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
