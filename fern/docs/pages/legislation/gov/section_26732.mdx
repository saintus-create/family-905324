# GOV § 26732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26732

---

## Text

The fee for publication of a notice in a newspaper is the reasonable cost of the publication.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1114.)
