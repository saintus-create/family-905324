# GOV § 37655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 8. Hospitals Managed by City Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 37655

---

## Text

The legislative body may prescribe rules for the administration of the hospital and the terms upon which patients may be admitted to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
