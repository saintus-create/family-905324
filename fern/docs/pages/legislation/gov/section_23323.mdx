# GOV § 23323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23323

---

## Text

A petition may designate not more than three persons as chief petitioners setting forth their names and mailing addresses.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
