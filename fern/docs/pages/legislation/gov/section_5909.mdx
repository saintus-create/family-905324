# GOV § 5909

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 11. Public Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 5909

---

## Text

To the extent that the provisions of this chapter are inconsistent with any other provision of general law or special act or any part thereof, now or hereafter enacted, the provisions of this chapter shall be controlling.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 845, Sec. 4. Effective September 21, 1987.)
