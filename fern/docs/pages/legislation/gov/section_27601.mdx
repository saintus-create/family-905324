# GOV § 27601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 3. Expenses and Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27601

---

## Text

The surveyor shall charge and collect such fees as are allowed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
