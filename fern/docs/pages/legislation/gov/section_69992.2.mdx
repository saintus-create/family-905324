# GOV § 69992.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 10.1. Official Reporters in Counties with a Population of 750,000 to 1,070,000

**Citation:** GOV § 69992.2

---

## Text

The fee required by Section 69992.1 shall be taxed as costs in favor of any party paying the same and to whom costs are awarded by the judgment of the court. Such fee shall not be subject to the provisions of Section 6103 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 601, Sec. 1. Effective July 19, 1984.)
