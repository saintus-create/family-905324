# GOV § 14501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14501

---

## Text

As used in this part, unless the context requires otherwise:

(a) “Commission” means the California Transportation Commission.

(b) “Department” means the Department of Transportation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1106.)
