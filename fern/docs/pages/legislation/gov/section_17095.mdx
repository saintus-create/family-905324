# GOV § 17095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Lost or Destroyed Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17095

---

## Text

The Treasurer shall pay a replacement warrant as though it were the original.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 45. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
