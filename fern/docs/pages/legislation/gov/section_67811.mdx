# GOV § 67811

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 67811

---

## Text

Counties and cities may establish an authority with the powers and duties set forth in this title upon the adoption of resolutions pursuant to Section 67820.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165) by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1166, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
