# GOV § 50499

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50499

---

## Text

At the time for hearing protests, the legislative body shall hear and pass upon all protests and objections and its decision is final.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
