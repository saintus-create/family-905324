# GOV § 65107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 1. Local Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 65107

---

## Text

Any action brought in the superior court relating to this chapter may be subject to a mediation proceeding conducted pursuant to Chapter 9.3 (commencing with Section 66030).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 20. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
