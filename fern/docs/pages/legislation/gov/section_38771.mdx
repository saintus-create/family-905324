# GOV § 38771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 6. Nuisances

**Citation:** GOV § 38771

---

## Text

By ordinance the city legislative body may declare what constitutes a nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
