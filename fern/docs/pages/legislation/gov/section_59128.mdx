# GOV § 59128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 59128

---

## Text

When a notice is required to be mailed to property owners, upon the completion of the mailing of the notice, the clerk shall file with the legislative body an affidavit setting forth the time and manner of the compliance with the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
