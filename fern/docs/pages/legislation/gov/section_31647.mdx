# GOV § 31647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 7. Service

**Citation:** GOV § 31647

---

## Text

A person is not entitled to credit as prior service for time during which he was not in service as defined in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
