# GOV § 23310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 23310

---

## Text

Proceedings under this chapter shall not be subject to the provisions of Chapter 6.6 (commencing with Section 54773) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5, relating to local agency formation commissions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
