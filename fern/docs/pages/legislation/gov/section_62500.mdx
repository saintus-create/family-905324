# GOV § 62500

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 62500

---

## Text

This title shall be known, and may be cited, as the Regional Housing Finance Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2025.)
