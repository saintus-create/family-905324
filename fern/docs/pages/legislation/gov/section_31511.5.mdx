# GOV § 31511.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 2.6. Alternative Plan for Counties of the 13th Class

**Citation:** GOV § 31511.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter to the contrary, no surviving spouse benefit shall be paid to a surviving spouse of a member who at the time of death had not met the minimum age and service credit requirements for service retirement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1480, Sec. 1.)
