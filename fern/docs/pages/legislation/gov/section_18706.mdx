# GOV § 18706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 18706

---

## Text

The board may make agreements with personnel agencies in other jurisdictions, political subdivisions, and state agencies excepted from the state civil service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 57. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
