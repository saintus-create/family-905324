# GOV § 58097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58097

---

## Text

Oral or written protest may be made at the hearing by any person interested in the formation of the proposed district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
