# GOV § 67800

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. Title and Declarations

**Citation:** GOV § 67800

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the Military Base Reuse Authority Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
