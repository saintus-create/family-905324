# GOV § 54477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54477

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for any other acts and things necessary, convenient, or desirable to secure the bonds or tending to make them more marketable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
