# GOV § 16405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 5. Revolving Funds and Advances

**Citation:** GOV § 16405

---

## Text

No state agency or person shall use or disburse any money withdrawn under the provisions of this article for any purpose whatever, unless authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 255.)
