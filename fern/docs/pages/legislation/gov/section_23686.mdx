# GOV § 23686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Seats > Article: 2. Removal of County Seats

**Citation:** GOV § 23686

---

## Text

The notice shall declare the place selected to be the county seat of the county from a day specified, not more than 90 days after the election. After the day named the place chosen is the county seat.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
