# GOV § 53361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53361

---

## Text

Any bonds issued by a district organized under the provisions of this chapter are hereby given the same force, value and use as bonds issued by any municipality and shall be exempt from all taxation within the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1451, Sec. 1.)
