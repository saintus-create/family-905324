# GOV § 50665.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50665.10

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in the initiating resolution, any election required under this article shall be conducted as other elections of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
