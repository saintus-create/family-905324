# GOV § 54708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.4. Benefit Assessment Act of 1982 > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 54708

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “service” includes the cost of maintaining any facility used to provide any service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1449, Sec. 2.)
