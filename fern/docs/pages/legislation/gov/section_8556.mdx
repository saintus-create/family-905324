# GOV § 8556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 2. General Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8556

---

## Text

“Governor” means the Governor or the person upon whom the powers and duties of the office of Governor have devolved pursuant to Section 10 of Article V of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
