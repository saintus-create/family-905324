# GOV § 8261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.4. California Youth Empowerment Act > Article: 1. Title

**Citation:** GOV § 8261

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Youth Empowerment Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 660, Sec. 2. (AB 46) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 8276.)
