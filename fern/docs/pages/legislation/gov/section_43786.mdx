# GOV § 43786

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 43786

---

## Text

The legislative body may determine that any issue of bonds pursuant to this article shall mature at one time, not to exceed twenty years from the date of issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
