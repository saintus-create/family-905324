# GOV § 31892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 17. Integration with Federal Old Age, Survivors, and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31892

---

## Text

When federal benefits are to be extended to any firemen’s or policemen’s position pursuant to this part it shall be done only by a vote of such firemen or policemen as provided in Section 31801.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2090.)
