# GOV § 53908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 10.5. Local Agency Internet Posting of Financial Information

**Citation:** GOV § 53908

---

## Text

(a) If a local agency, that is required to report to the Controller under Section 53891, maintains an Internet Web site, it shall post, in a conspicuous location on its Internet Web site, information on the annual compensation of its elected officials, officers, and employees that is submitted to the Controller under Section 53891.

(b) A local agency may comply with subdivision (a) by posting, in a conspicuous location on its Internet Web site, a link to the Controller’s Government Compensation in California Internet Web site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 894, Sec. 3. (AB 2040) Effective January 1, 2015.)
