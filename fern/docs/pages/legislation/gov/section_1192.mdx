# GOV § 1192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Deputies and Subordinates

**Citation:** GOV § 1192

---

## Text

When not otherwise provided for, within 10 days after receiving notice of their appointment, deputies and other subordinate officers shall take and file an oath in the manner required of their principals.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
