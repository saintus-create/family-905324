# GOV § 25306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 25306

---

## Text

The board may rent or lease office space for the members of the board and employ a secretary at such office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1270.)
