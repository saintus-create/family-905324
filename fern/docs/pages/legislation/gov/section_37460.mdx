# GOV § 37460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37460

---

## Text

As used in this article, “veteran” means a soldier, sailor, airman, guardian, or marine who has served the United States honorably in any of its wars.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 379, Sec. 11. (AB 1715) Effective January 1, 2023.)
