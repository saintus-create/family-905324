# GOV § 15811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15811

---

## Text

The board has no power at any time or in any manner to pledge the credit or taxing power of the State or any of its local agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1686.)
