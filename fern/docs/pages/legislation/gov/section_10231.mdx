# GOV § 10231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 10231

---

## Text

The Legislative Counsel shall prepare and assist in the preparation, amendment and consideration of legislative measures when requested or upon suggestion as herein provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
