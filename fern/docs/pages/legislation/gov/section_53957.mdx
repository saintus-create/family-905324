# GOV § 53957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 15. District Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53957

---

## Text

Any expenditure in excess of one dollar ($1) shall not be made unless a receipt is obtained, setting forth the date, purpose of expenditure and amount expended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 333.)
