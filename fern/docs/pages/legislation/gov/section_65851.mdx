# GOV § 65851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Zoning Regulations > Article: 2. Adoption of Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 65851

---

## Text

For such purposes the legislative body may divide a county, a city, or portions thereof, into zones of the number, shape and area it deems best suited to carry out the purpose of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1880.)
