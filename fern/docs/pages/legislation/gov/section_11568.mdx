# GOV § 11568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Statutory Salary Equalization Plan > Article: 2. Application of Salary Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 11568

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not be superseded or modified by any subsequent legislation except to the extent that such legislation shall do so expressly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1613.)
