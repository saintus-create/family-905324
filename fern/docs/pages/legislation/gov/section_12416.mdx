# GOV § 12416

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12416

---

## Text

The Controller shall require all persons who have received any money belonging to the State and have not accounted for it to settle their accounts and may inspect the books of any person charged with the receipt, safekeeping, or disbursement of public money.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
