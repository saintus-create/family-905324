# GOV § 68511.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68511.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Judicial Council shall provide by rule for the photographic, microphotographic, mechanical, or electronic entry, storage, and retrieval of court records.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 110. Effective October 12, 2001.)
