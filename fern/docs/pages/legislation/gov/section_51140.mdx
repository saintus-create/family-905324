# GOV § 51140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 5. Removal from Zone

**Citation:** GOV § 51140

---

## Text

Upon rezoning, the board or council shall certify the rezoning indicating the new zone and its effective date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
