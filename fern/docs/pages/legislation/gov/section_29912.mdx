# GOV § 29912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29912

---

## Text

Notice of redemption shall be published at such time and in such manner as the board may provide in the resolution providing for the issuance of the bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 141.)
