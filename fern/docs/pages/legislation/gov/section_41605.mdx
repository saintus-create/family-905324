# GOV § 41605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 6. Chief of Police

**Citation:** GOV § 41605

---

## Text

The chief of police has charge of the prisoners and of any city jail established by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 51. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
