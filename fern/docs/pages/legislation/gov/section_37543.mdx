# GOV § 37543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 6. Museums

**Citation:** GOV § 37543

---

## Text

With the consent of the legislative body, the mayor shall appoint a board of five museum trustees to manage the museum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
