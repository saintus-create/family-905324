# GOV § 22960.23

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.23

---

## Text

“Normal retirement age” means the age at which the participant is eligible for a retirement benefit without special qualifications and is the age of 50 years under this plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
