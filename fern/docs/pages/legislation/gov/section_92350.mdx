# GOV § 92350

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 7. Supplemental Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 92350

---

## Text

This title, being necessary for the welfare of the state and its inhabitants, shall be liberally construed to effect the purposes hereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
