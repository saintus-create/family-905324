# GOV § 31463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31463

---

## Text

“Normal contributions” means contributions by a member at the normal rates of contributions, but does not include additional contributions by a member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
