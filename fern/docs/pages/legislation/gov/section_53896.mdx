# GOV § 53896

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 9. Financial Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 53896

---

## Text

An officer of a local agency wilfully refusing to make such a report is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1521.)
