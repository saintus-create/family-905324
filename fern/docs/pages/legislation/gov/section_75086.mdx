# GOV § 75086

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 4.5. Extended Service Incentive Program

**Citation:** GOV § 75086

---

## Text

A judge shall be eligible to participate in the program if the judge has performed at least 20 years of creditable service and is at least 60 years of age.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 961, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
