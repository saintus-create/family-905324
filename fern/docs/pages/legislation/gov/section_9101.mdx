# GOV § 9101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 4. Legislative Offices

**Citation:** GOV § 9101

---

## Text

The legislative offices provided for in this article shall be under the management and control of the Senate Committee on Rules.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1968, Ch. 312.)
