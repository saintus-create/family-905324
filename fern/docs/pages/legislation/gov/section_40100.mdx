# GOV § 40100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 16. Advertising

**Citation:** GOV § 40100

---

## Text

By ordinance the legislative body may appropriate a sum for a city publicity or advertising fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
