# GOV § 53986

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 17. Community Facilities Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 53986

---

## Text

The local agency shall give written notice of the adoption of the plan, and submit copies for delivery, to those state agencies which may be affected as determined by the State Clearinghouse in the Office of Planning and Research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 396, Sec. 1. Effective July 27, 1983.)
