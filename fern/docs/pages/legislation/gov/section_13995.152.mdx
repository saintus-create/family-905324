# GOV § 13995.152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. California Welcome Centers

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.152

---

## Text

The Department of Transportation shall, to the maximum extent feasible, install and maintain California Welcome Center highway signs when requested by the Office of Tourism.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 296, Sec. 1. Effective August 25, 2004.)
