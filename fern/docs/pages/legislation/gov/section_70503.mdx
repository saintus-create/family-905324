# GOV § 70503

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 9. Conveyance: Court Facility Property: County of San Diego

**Citation:** GOV § 70503

---

## Text

The Board of Supervisors of the County of San Diego is authorized to approve a lease for any or all of the San Diego property and the county property, which actions shall not be subject to Article 8 (commencing with Section 25520) of Chapter 5 of Part 2 of Division 2 of Title 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 17, Sec. 23. (AB 103) Effective June 27, 2017.)
