# GOV § 15820.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.6. Research Facilities in the University of California > Article: 5. Funding of Research Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 15820.22

---

## Text

No provision of this article shall be applicable to the Regents of the University of the California unless the regents adopt a resolution to make the provisions applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 209, Sec. 5. Effective July 11, 1990.)
