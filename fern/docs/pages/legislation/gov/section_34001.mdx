# GOV § 34001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES GENERALLY > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 34001

---

## Text

As used in this title, unless otherwise specifically provided, “board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of the county within which the city or territory is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
