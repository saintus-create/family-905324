# GOV § 13995.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 4. Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.46

---

## Text

Commissioners and employees of the commission are not responsible individually in any way whatsoever to any person for liability for any good faith activity of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 229, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
