# GOV § 31110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 2. CIVIL SERVICE > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31110

---

## Text

Upon the adoption of the civil service system, the board of supervisors shall appoint a civil service commission to assist in administering the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
