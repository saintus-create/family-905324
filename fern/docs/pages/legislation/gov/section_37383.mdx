# GOV § 37383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 2. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 37383

---

## Text

A city may lease city property not acquired for park purposes for not to exceed thirty-five years for the production of minerals, oil, gas, or other hydrocarbon substances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
