# GOV § 50718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50718

---

## Text

An indenture pursuant to which bonds are issued may include such covenants and agreements on the part of the board as the board deems necessary or advisible for the better security of the bonds issued thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
