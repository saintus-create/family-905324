# GOV § 15677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 9.5. Office of Tax Appeals > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Access to Public Records

**Citation:** GOV § 15677

---

## Text

If a person that sought relief from a tax appeals panel disagrees with its decision, the person may bring an action in superior court in accordance with the law imposing the tax or fee for a trial de novo.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 252, Sec. 14. (AB 131) Effective September 16, 2017.)
