# GOV § 5107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Protection of Bond Guarantors

**Citation:** GOV § 5107

---

## Text

The relief of guarantors is limited to the extent of defaults in principal, interest, or both principal and interest of the bonds guaranteed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
