# GOV § 40603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Mayor

**Citation:** GOV § 40603

---

## Text

The mayor may administer oaths and affirmations, take affidavits, and certify them.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 40. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
