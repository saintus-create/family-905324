# GOV § 40577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 6. Co-operation

**Citation:** GOV § 40577

## Text

No contest of any thing or matter provided in this article shall be made other than in the time and manner herein specified.
