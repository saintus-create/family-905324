# GOV § 38449

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 2. Parks Dedicated by City

**Citation:** GOV § 38449

## Text

At the time set for the hearing or to which it is postponed, the legislative body shall hear and pass upon all such protests.
