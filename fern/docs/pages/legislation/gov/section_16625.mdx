# GOV § 16625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Savings and Loan Association Deposits > Article: 3. Custody of Securities

**Citation:** GOV § 16625

---

## Text

As used in this article, “qualified trust company” means the trust department of any state or national bank in this state or a trust company authorized to act as such in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 349.)
