# GOV § 43763

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to this article shall be issued substantially in the manner prescribed by Article 1 of this chapter.
