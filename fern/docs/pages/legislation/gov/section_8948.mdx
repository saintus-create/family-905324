# GOV § 8948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 3. Joint Legislative Ethics Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8948

---

## Text

At any hearing held by the committee:

(a) Oral evidence shall be taken only on oath or affirmation.

(b) Each party shall have these rights: to be represented by legal counsel; to call and examine witnesses; to introduce exhibits; and to cross-examine opposing witnesses.

(c) The hearing shall be open to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 163.)
