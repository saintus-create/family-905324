# GOV § 17004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Form, Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17004

---

## Text

Unless otherwise requested, the Controller may mail a warrant to the last known address of the claimant, and the signature of the payee on the warrant is a sufficient receipt.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
