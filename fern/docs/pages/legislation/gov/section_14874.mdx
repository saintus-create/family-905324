# GOV § 14874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 3. Operation of the Office of State Printing

**Citation:** GOV § 14874

---

## Text

There shall be retained and filed in the Office of State Printing, with the job ticket, one copy or sample of the printing produced on each completed printing order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
