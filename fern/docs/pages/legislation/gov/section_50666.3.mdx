# GOV § 50666.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50666.3

---

## Text

So long as any bonds or interest thereon are unpaid following their maturity, the revenues and interest thereon shall not be used for any other purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
