# GOV § 55022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 2. Request by Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 55022

---

## Text

The resolution shall be accompanied by a written request that permission be granted to the local agency to construct and maintain the conduits described in the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
