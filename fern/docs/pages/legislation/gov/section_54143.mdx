# GOV § 54143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 6. Federal Surplus Property

**Citation:** GOV § 54143

---

## Text

The legislative body of any such local agency may authorize any of its officers to make a down payment or payment in full required in the bidding or sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
