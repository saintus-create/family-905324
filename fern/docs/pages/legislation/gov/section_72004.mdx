# GOV § 72004

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 8. Superior Courts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 72004

---

## Text

Sections 24350 to 24356, inclusive, and Sections 29350 and 29351 apply to officers of superior courts and to the disposition of fees collected by those officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 362. Effective January 1, 2003.)
