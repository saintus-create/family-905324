# GOV § 10238

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 10238

---

## Text

Every suggestion of a judge shall set forth the substance of the provisions desired or which may be needed with the reasons therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
