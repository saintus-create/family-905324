# GOV § 27726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Hearing Officer

## Text

Such contracting entity is authorized to conduct its hearings in accordance with the provisions of this chapter.
