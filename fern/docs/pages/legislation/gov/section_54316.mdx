# GOV § 54316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 54316

---

## Text

“Holder of bonds” or “bondholder” means:

(a) The bearer of any outstanding bond registered to bearer or not registered.

(b) The registered owner of an outstanding bond registered other than to bearer at the time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
