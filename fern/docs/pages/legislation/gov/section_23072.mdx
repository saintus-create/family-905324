# GOV § 23072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 1. Terms Defined

**Citation:** GOV § 23072

---

## Text

“Northerly,” “southerly,” “easterly,” and “westerly,” mean due north, due south, due east, and due west, unless controlled by other words, or by lines, monuments, or natural objects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
