# GOV § 54413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54413

---

## Text

Any resolution authorizing bonds may provide that the bonds may recite that it is issued pursuant to this chapter and bonds containing such recital are conclusively deemed valid and issued in conformity with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
