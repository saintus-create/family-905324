# GOV § 34102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES GENERALLY > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Classification

**Citation:** GOV § 34102

---

## Text

Cities organized under the general law shall be “general law cities.”

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 624.)
