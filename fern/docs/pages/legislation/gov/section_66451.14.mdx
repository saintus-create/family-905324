# GOV § 66451.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Procedure > Article: 1.5. Merger of Parcels

**Citation:** GOV § 66451.14

---

## Text

At any time within 30 days after recording of the notice of intention to determine status, the owner of the affected property may file with the local agency a request for a hearing on determination of status.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 102, Sec. 1.4. Effective April 30, 1984.)
