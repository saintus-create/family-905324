# GOV § 40601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Mayor

**Citation:** GOV § 40601

---

## Text

In the absence of the mayor, the mayor pro tempore shall exercise the powers granted in this chapter.
