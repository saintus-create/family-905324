# GOV § 11342.590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 11342.590

---

## Text

“Prescriptive standard” means a regulation that specifies the sole means of compliance with a performance standard by specific actions, measurements, or other quantifiable means.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1060, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2001.)
