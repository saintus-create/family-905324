# GOV § 8631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 14. Local Emergency

**Citation:** GOV § 8631

---

## Text

In periods of local emergency, political subdivisions have full power to provide mutual aid to any affected area in accordance with local ordinances, resolutions, emergency plans, or agreements therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
