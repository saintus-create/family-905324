# GOV § 37462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37462

---

## Text

The legislative body may acquire real or personal property necessary for such buildings, by condemnation or otherwise. It may improve, manage, and control such property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
