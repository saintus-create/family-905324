# GOV § 66907.6

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 5. Conservancy’s Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 66907.6

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, the conservancy may enter into an option to purchase lands in fee or lesser interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 17. Effective September 17, 1984.)
