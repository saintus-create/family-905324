# GOV § 75073

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 3.5. Payment of Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 75073

---

## Text

A judge who elects to receive optional settlement two or three in Section 75071 may concurrently and irrevocably elect to waive the provision for an increase to his or her allowance, as specified in subdivisions (b) and (c) of Section 75071, and shall, instead, have his or her allowance based upon the waiver of this benefit.

This section shall apply to any judge who retires on or before December 31, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 199, Sec. 44. (AB 2404) Effective January 1, 2017.)
