# GOV § 51118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 2. Timberland Production Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51118

---

## Text

Land zoned as timberland production under this chapter shall be enforceably restricted within the meaning of Section 3(j) of Article XIII of the Constitution and the restriction shall be enforced and administered by the city or county in a manner to accomplish the purposes of that section and this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 14.)
