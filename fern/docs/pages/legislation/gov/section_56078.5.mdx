# GOV § 56078.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56078.5

---

## Text

“Successor Agency” means the local agency the commission designates to wind up the affairs of a dissolved district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 37, Sec. 1. (AB 2957) Effective January 1, 2023.)
