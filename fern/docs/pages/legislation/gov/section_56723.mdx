# GOV § 56723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Proceedings for Cities > Article: 1. Incorporation

**Citation:** GOV § 56723

---

## Text

If the petition is for incorporation, it may also include provisions for appointment of a city manager and appointment of elective city officials, except city council members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 2001.)
