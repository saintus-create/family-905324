# GOV § 50553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 9. Sale of Improvements Financed by Special Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 50553

---

## Text

The clerk of the legislative body shall cause the resolution of intention to be published once a week for two successive weeks in a daily, semiweekly or weekly newspaper of general circulation in the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1923.)
