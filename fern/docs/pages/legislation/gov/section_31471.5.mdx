# GOV § 31471.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31471.5

---

## Text

“Recognized retiree organization” means an organization in which a majority of the members of the organization are retired members of the system and which the board, upon request, has approved recognition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 178, Sec. 2. (SB 1382) Effective January 1, 2013.)
