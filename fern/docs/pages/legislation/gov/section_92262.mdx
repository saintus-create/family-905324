# GOV § 92262

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 5. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 92262

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the Treasurer shall not be deemed to have a conflict of interest by reason of acting as trustee pursuant to this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
