# GOV § 58107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58107

## Text

The supervising authority may make such inclusion upon the owner’s application or upon giving him notice of and hearing on the inclusion.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
