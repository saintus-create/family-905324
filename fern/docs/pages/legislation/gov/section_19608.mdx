# GOV § 19608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** GOV § 19608

---

## Text

Any demonstration project implemented under this chapter shall not include the adoption or waiver of regulations or statutes that are administered or enforced by the Department of Human Resources without the express approval of the Department of Human Resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 91, Sec. 24) by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 77. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
