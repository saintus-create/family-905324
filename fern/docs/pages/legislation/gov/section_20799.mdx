# GOV § 20799

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Employer Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 20799

---

## Text

The board shall credit all contributions of the state, school employer, and contracting agencies in the retirement fund with interest at the current net earnings rate compounded at each June 30.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
