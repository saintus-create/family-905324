# GOV § 72116

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 8. Superior Courts > Article: 3. Marshal

**Citation:** GOV § 72116

---

## Text

Court-related services are provided by the marshal’s office in Shasta County.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 210, Sec. 27. (AB 1984) Effective January 1, 2021.)
