# GOV § 41202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. City Assessor

**Citation:** GOV § 41202

---

## Text

The mode of making out the list and proceedings relating thereto shall conform with the laws regulating county assessors except as provided by law or ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
