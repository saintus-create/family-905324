# GOV § 1190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Deputies and Subordinates

**Citation:** GOV § 1190

---

## Text

All assistants, deputies, and other subordinate officers, whose appointments are not otherwise provided for, shall be appointed by the officer or body to whom they are respectively subordinate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
