# GOV § 50666.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50666.8

---

## Text

The holder of the bonds shall not compel the exercise of the taxing power by the local agency, other than the revenues pledged, or the forfeiture of its property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
