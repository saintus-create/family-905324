# GOV § 34402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Special Charter Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34402

## Text

The notice shall be given for at least the four weeks prior to the election, and be made a part of the general election notice.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
