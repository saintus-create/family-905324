# GOV § 67106

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 4. Agency’s Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 67106

---

## Text

Violation of any ordinance of the agency is a misdemeanor.
