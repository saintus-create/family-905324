# GOV § 45300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Retirement > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 45300

---

## Text

It is the intent of this article to enable any city to adopt such a retirement system as is adaptable to its size and type.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
