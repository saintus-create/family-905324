# GOV § 14456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. California Transportation Research and Innovation Program

**Citation:** GOV § 14456

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 14453, the department may enter into a joint powers agreement with other entities for development and operation of the center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 352, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
