# GOV § 13995.80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 8. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.80

---

## Text

Any action for any penalty or other remedy that is prescribed under any provision of this chapter shall be commenced within three years from the date of the alleged violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 229, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
