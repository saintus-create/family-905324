# GOV § 8566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 3. Powers of the Governor

**Citation:** GOV § 8566

---

## Text

The Governor is empowered to expend any appropriation for support of the California Emergency Services Act to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
