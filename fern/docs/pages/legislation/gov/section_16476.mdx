# GOV § 16476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 4. Special Fund Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 16476

---

## Text

If any provision of this article, or the application thereof to the money in any fund referred to herein, is held invalid, the remaining provisions of this article shall not be affected thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 991.)
