# GOV § 52182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 8. Tax Exemption of Community Energy Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 52182

---

## Text

The bonds of an authority are issued for an essential public and governmental purpose, and, together with interest thereon and income therefrom, are exempt from all taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
