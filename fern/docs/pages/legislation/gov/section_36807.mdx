# GOV § 36807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 36807

---

## Text

At any special meeting the city council shall act only on matters referred to in the notice or consent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
