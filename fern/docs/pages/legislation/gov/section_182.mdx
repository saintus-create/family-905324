# GOV § 182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Sovereignty of the State > Article: 4. Rights Over Property

**Citation:** GOV § 182

---

## Text

All property within the limits of the State, which does not belong to any person, belongs to the people. Whenever the title to any property fails for want of heirs or next of kin, it reverts to the people.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
