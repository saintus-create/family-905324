# GOV § 65915.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.3. Density Bonuses and Other Incentives

**Citation:** GOV § 65915.1

---

## Text

For purposes of Section 65915, affordable housing impact fees, including inclusionary zoning fees and in-lieu fees, shall not be imposed on a housing development’s affordable units.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 346, Sec. 1. (AB 571) Effective January 1, 2022.)
