# GOV § 15167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. California Law Enforcement Telecommunications System

**Citation:** GOV § 15167

---

## Text

In the case of a state agency, the charge shall be paid from the money available by law for the support of the state agency using the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1595.)
