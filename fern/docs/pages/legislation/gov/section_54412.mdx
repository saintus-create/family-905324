# GOV § 54412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54412

---

## Text

The validity of the authorization and issuance of the bonds is not affected by proceedings for the acquisition, construction, or improving of the enterprise or by contracts in connection therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
