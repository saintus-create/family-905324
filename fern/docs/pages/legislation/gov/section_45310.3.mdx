# GOV § 45310.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Retirement > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 45310.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a person ceases to be a member for any portion of his or her service as an elected public officer that is forfeited pursuant to Section 1243.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 322, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2006.)
