# GOV § 64714

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 2. The Los Angeles County Affordable Housing Solutions Agency and Governing Board

**Citation:** GOV § 64714

---

## Text

A member of the board shall exercise independent judgment on behalf of the interests of the residents, the property owners, and the public of Los Angeles County in furthering the intent and purposes of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
