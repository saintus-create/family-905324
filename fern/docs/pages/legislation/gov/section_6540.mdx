# GOV § 6540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 6540

---

## Text

As used in this article “bonds” means revenue bonds, notes or other evidences of indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 329.)
