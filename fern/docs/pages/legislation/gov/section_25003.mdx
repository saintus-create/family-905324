# GOV § 25003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25003

---

## Text

The board may make and enforce rules and regulations necessary for the government of the board, the preservation of order, and the transaction of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
