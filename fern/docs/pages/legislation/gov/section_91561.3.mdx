# GOV § 91561.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. CALIFORNIA INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 1. California Industrial Development Financing > Article: 3. Small Business Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 91561.3

---

## Text

Each local authority may enter into joint powers agreements with other local authorities to issue pooled bonds in response to small business loan requests.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 645, Sec. 9. (AB 1547) Effective September 21, 2018.)
