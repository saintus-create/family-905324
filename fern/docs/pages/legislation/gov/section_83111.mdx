# GOV § 83111

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 3. Fair Political Practices Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 83111

---

## Text

The Commission has primary responsibility for the impartial, effective administration and implementation of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
