# GOV § 6599.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5.5. Local Agency Self-Insurance Authority > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 6599.05

---

## Text

The authority is not a state agency and has no power at any time or in any manner to pledge the credit of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1327, Sec. 1.)
