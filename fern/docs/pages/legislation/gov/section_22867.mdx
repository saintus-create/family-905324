# GOV § 22867

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 6. Health Benefit Plans and Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 22867

---

## Text

The provisions of this article do not supersede, modify, or in any manner alter or impair the effect of any provision of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 2000) of Division 2 of the Business and Professions Code or any provision of the Insurance Code. This article shall be interpreted and applied in a manner consistent with those provisions of the Business and Professions Code and the Insurance Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
