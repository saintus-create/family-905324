# GOV § 8548.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. California State Auditor > Article: 4. Omnibus Audit Accountability Act of 2006

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Omnibus Audit Accountability Act of 2006.

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 452, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
