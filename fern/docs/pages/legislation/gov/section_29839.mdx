# GOV § 29839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 2.3. Alternative Payment and Registration Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 29839

---

## Text

When the treasurer redeems any warrant upon which any interest is due, the amount of interest shall be entered in the account distinct from principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1191, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1992.)
