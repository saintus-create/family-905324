# GOV § 58908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Notice of Formation or Change in Boundaries of Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 58908

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not be superseded or modified by any subsequent legislation except to the extent that such legislation shall do so expressly.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 846.)
