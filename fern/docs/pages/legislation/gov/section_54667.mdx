# GOV § 54667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 11.5. Revenue Bond Anticipation Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 54667

---

## Text

Notes may be issued pursuant to this article in anticipation of the sale of bonds authorized either before or after the effective date of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 27.)
