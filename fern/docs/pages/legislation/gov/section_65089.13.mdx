# GOV § 65089.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2.65. Management of Traffic Congestion and Stormwater Pollution in San Mateo County

**Citation:** GOV § 65089.13

---

## Text

Prior to the imposition of the fee by the City/County Association of Governments of San Mateo County, a specific program with performance measures and a budget shall first be developed and adopted by the association at a noticed public hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 931, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
