# GOV § 23296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23296

---

## Text

Any action to determine the validity of any change, alteration, or reformation to the boundaries of a county pursuant to this article shall be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 4.3. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
