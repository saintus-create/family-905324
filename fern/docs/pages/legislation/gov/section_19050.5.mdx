# GOV § 19050.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19050.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 3517.6, an appointing power may transfer any employee under his or her jurisdiction to a position in the same class or to another position in a different class pursuant to board rule.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 38. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
