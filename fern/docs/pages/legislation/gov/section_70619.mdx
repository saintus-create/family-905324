# GOV § 70619

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.8. Superior Court Fees > Article: 1. Civil Fees Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 70619

---

## Text

The fee for reclassification of a case from a limited civil case to an unlimited civil case under Section 403.060 of the Code of Civil Procedure is one hundred forty dollars ($140).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 75, Sec. 121. Effective July 19, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 156 of Ch. 75.)
