# GOV § 38406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38406

---

## Text

At the time fixed, the legislative body shall meet and hear and pass on protests to abandonment of the park. Any interested person may protest at the meeting. Failure of an interested person to protest is conclusive evidence that the person consents to the abandonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 27. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
