# GOV § 59652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59652

---

## Text

Bond Plan B or C bonds may be issued for not to exceed 29 years after July 2d following the first 10 months after their date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
