# GOV § 50581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. Open Space Maintenance Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 50581

---

## Text

“Open space maintenance district” or “district” means any district created pursuant to the provisions of this chapter to pay the assessments for the maintenance work to be done hereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1503.)
