# GOV § 54701.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 54701.8

---

## Text

A local agency may accept financial or other assistance from any public or private source, and may expend any funds so accepted for any of the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
