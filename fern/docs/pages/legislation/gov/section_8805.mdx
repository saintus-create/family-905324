# GOV § 8805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8805

---

## Text

“Interconnection” means the use of apparatus or equipment for the electronic transmission and distribution of public broadcast programming to public broadcasting stations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
