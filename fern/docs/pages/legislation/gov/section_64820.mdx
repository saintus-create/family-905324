# GOV § 64820

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 2. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 64820

---

## Text

The board may, by majority vote, initiate proceedings to issue bonds pursuant to this chapter by adopting a resolution or ordinance stating its intent to issue the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
