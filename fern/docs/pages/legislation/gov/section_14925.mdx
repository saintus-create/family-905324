# GOV § 14925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8.5. Guaranteed Return Trip Demonstration Project

**Citation:** GOV § 14925

---

## Text

This chapter and Article 4 (commencing with Section 14170) of Chapter 2 of Part 5 shall be known and may be cited as the Guaranteed Return Trip Demonstration Project.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1653, Sec. 3.)
