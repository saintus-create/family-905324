# GOV § 29461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 7. Public Administrator’s and Public Guardian’s Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29461

---

## Text

The fees, charges, and expenses incurred in connection with any estate required to be administered by the public administrator or guardian in which the assets of the estate prove insufficient are county charges.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 892.)
