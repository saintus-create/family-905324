# GOV § 68639

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 6. Waiver of Court Fees and Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 68639

---

## Text

An initial fee waiver shall expire 60 days after the judgment, dismissal, or other final disposition of the case.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 462, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009. Operative July 1, 2009, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 462.)
