# GOV § 53185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 3. Purposes and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 53185

---

## Text

A district may be established to assist in financing any work which may be financed pursuant to a financing act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
