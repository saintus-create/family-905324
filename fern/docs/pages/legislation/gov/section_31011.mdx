# GOV § 31011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31011

---

## Text

Every employee has the right to inspect personnel records pursuant to Section 1198.5 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 886, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
