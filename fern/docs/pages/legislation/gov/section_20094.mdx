# GOV § 20094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 1. The Board of Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 20094

---

## Text

The counsel to the board shall notify each new member of the board upon his or her assumption of office and each member of the board annually that he or she is subject to the gift provisions of Chapter 9.5 (commencing with Section 89500) of Title 9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 291. Effective January 1, 2007.)
