# GOV § 12223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12223

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall receive into the archives an item that is required by law to be delivered to or filed with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 52. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
