# GOV § 12521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 2. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12521

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall account for and pay over to the proper officer all money which may come into the Attorney General’s possession belonging to the state or to any county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 96. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
