# GOV § 22013.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22013.11

---

## Text

“Policeman” or “fireman,” as used in this part, also includes persons employed in positions set forth in Sections 20409 and 20410 for the purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 418(d)(5)(A)).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 678, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 1999.)
