# GOV § 54701.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 2. Powers and Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 54701.10

---

## Text

Bonds issued under the provisions of this chapter shall not be deemed to constitute a debt or liability of the local agency for which is pledged the faith and credit of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
