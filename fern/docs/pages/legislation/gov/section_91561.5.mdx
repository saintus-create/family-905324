# GOV § 91561.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. CALIFORNIA INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 1. California Industrial Development Financing > Article: 3. Small Business Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 91561.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “small business” means small business as defined in Section 14837.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 645, Sec. 10. (AB 1547) Effective September 21, 2018.)
