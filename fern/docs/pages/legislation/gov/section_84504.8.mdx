# GOV § 84504.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 5. Disclosure in Advertisements

**Citation:** GOV § 84504.8

---

## Text

If a disclosure statement required by a local ordinance is substantially similar to a disclosure statement required pursuant to this article, the two disclosure statements may be merged into a single statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 887, Sec. 14. (SB 1360) Effective January 1, 2023.)
