# GOV § 25004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25004

---

## Text

It may adopt a seal. A description and impression of the seal shall be filed in the office of the county clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 829, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1999.)
