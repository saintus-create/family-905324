# GOV § 43614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43614

---

## Text

If two-thirds of the electors voting on the proposition vote for it, the bonds may be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
