# GOV § 98009

**Hierarchy:** Title: 16. STATE–TRIBAL AGREEMENTS GOVERNING INDIAN GAMING > Chapter: 1. The Tribal Government Gaming and Economic Self-Sufficiency Act of 1998

**Citation:** GOV § 98009

---

## Text

The provisions of the Gaming Compact set forth in Section 98004 are hereby incorporated into state law, and all gaming activities, including but not limited to gaming devices, authorized therein are expressly declared to be permitted as a matter of state law to any Indian tribe entering into the Gaming Compact in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 1998, by initiative Proposition 5.)
