# GOV § 31980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 4. Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 31980

---

## Text

The system established under this chapter or its predecessor is exclusive, and persons entitled to membership in this system shall not participate in any other system provided for officers or employees of a county or judicial district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1553.)
