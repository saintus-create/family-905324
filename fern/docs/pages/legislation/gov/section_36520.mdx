# GOV § 36520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36520

---

## Text

The city council shall approve all bonds. When approved, the clerk’s bond shall be filed with the mayor, and other bonds shall be filed with the city clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
