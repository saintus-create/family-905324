# GOV § 15130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State’s Teletype System > Article: 2. Leases of Teletype Facilities and Equipment

**Citation:** GOV § 15130

---

## Text

The Attorney General may arrange for the connection of the State Teletype System with that of any adjacent state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 84.)
