# GOV § 68502

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68502

---

## Text

The committees may assemble information and make recommendations to the Judicial Council, but shall not exercise any of the powers vested in the council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 206.)
