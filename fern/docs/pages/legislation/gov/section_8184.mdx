# GOV § 8184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 3. Capitol Area Redevelopment

**Citation:** GOV § 8184

---

## Text

For the purpose of allocating taxes pursuant to Section 8183, state subvention payments made with respect to property within the project area are deemed to be taxes levied upon such property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 953.)
