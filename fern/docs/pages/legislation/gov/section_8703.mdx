# GOV § 8703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Office of California-Mexico Affairs

**Citation:** GOV § 8703

---

## Text

The members of the Southwest Border Regional Conference shall be the Governors of the four American border states. The California member of the conference is the Governor of California or his designee.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1197, Sec. 3.)
