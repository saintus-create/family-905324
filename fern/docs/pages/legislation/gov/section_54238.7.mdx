# GOV § 54238.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 8.5. Surplus Residential Property

**Citation:** GOV § 54238.7

---

## Text

Except those properties the Department of Transportation has in escrow as of August 15, 1997, to sell, the Department of Transportation shall not dispose of any surplus property in the City of South Pasadena prior to January 31, 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 105. Effective October 12, 2001.)
