# GOV § 25404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 3. Cement Manufacturing Plants

**Citation:** GOV § 25404

---

## Text

The State and any municipal or public corporation of the State shall have a preferred right to purchase the products of the cement manufacturing plant at the same price as they are offered to private persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
