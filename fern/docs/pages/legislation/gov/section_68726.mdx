# GOV § 68726

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.5. Commission on Judicial Performance > Article: 2. Co-operation of Public Officers and Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 68726

---

## Text

It shall be the duty of the sheriffs and marshals in the several counties, upon request of the commission or its authorized representative, to serve process and execute all lawful orders of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 1997.)
