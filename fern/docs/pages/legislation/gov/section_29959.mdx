# GOV § 29959

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 3. Special Improvement Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29959

---

## Text

The board shall invest and reinvest the money in the general improvement fund in bonds issued by the county or bonds issued for highway, sewer, drainage, or other improvements within the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
