# GOV § 84307

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 3. Prohibitions

**Citation:** GOV § 84307

---

## Text

No contribution shall be commingled with the personal funds of the recipient or any other person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 779.)
