# GOV § 66965

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.43. LAKE TAHOE ACQUISITIONS BOND ACT > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66965

---

## Text

All proposed appropriations for the programs specified in this title shall be included in a section in the Budget Bill for the 1983–84 and each succeeding fiscal year, for consideration by the Legislature. All appropriations shall be subject to all limitations enacted in the Budget Act and to all fiscal procedures prescribed by law with respect to the expenditures of state funds, unless expressly exempted from such laws by a statute enacted by the Legislature. No funds derived from the bonds authorized by this title may be expended pursuant to an appropriation not contained in such section of the Budget Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 305, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 4 at the November 2, 1982, election. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 2 of Ch. 305.)
