# GOV § 12100.160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 15. California Civic Media Act

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.160

---

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Civic Media Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 649, Sec. 1. (SB 155) Effective October 11, 2025.)
