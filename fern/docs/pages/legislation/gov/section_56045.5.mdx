# GOV § 56045.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56045.5

---

## Text

“Indirect cost” has the same meaning as used in paragraph (2) of subdivision (b) of Section 33338 of the Education Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 304, Sec. 2. (AB 851) Effective January 1, 2016.)
