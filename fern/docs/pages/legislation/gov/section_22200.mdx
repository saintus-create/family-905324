# GOV § 22200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Agreements for Coverage > Article: 1. Applications for Coverage

**Citation:** GOV § 22200

---

## Text

The board is hereby authorized on behalf of the state to administer and to maintain in full force and effect the agreement entered into between the state and the Federal Security Administrator on March 9, 1951, and all modifications thereof heretofore made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 83, Sec. 81. Effective January 1, 2000.)
