# GOV § 66481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Requirements > Article: 4. Reservations

**Citation:** GOV § 66481

---

## Text

If the public agency for whose benefit an area has been reserved does not enter into such a binding agreement, the reservation of such area shall automatically terminate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
