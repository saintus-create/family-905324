# GOV § 12412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12412

---

## Text

The Controller shall keep and state all accounts in which the State is interested and keep a separate account under the head of each specific appropriation, showing at all times the undisbursed balance of the appropriation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1298.)
