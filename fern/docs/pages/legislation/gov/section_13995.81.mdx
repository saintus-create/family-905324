# GOV § 13995.81

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 8. Actions and Penalties

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.81

---

## Text

Any person who files false information concerning an assessment is civilly liable in an amount of not more than ten thousand dollars ($10,000), in addition to any amount owed as the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 229, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
