# GOV § 65460.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8.5. Transit Village Development Planning Act of 1994

**Citation:** GOV § 65460.5

---

## Text

A city or county establishing a district and preparing a plan pursuant to this article shall be eligible for available transportation funding.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 78, Sec. 3. (AB 2605) Effective January 1, 2017.)
