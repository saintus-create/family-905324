# GOV § 43429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Street Work Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 43429

---

## Text

The amount of the reimbursement shall not exceed the amount paid out of or advanced from the revolving fund for the improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
