# GOV § 57103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Resolution for Order Subject to Election

**Citation:** GOV § 57103

---

## Text

Any order in any resolution adopted by the commission on or after January 1, 1986, ordering the dissolution of a local hospital district, organized pursuant to Division 23 (commencing with Section 32000) of the Health and Safety Code, is subject to confirmation by the voters unless dissolution of the local hospital district meets the requirements set forth in subdivision (c) of Section 57077.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 20, Sec. 6. (AB 1822) Effective January 1, 2020.)
