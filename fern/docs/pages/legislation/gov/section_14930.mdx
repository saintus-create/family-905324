# GOV § 14930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Golden State Financial Marketplace Program (GS $Mart Program)

**Citation:** GOV § 14930

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Golden State Financial Marketplace Program or GS $Mart Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 28, Sec. 16. (SB 71) Effective June 27, 2013.)
