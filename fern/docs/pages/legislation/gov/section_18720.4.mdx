# GOV § 18720.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3.5. Employment Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 18720.4

---

## Text

State agencies shall exhaust existing supplies of forms which conform with federal and state law before using any new form approved by the department or the Civil Rights Department pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 48, Sec. 50. (SB 189) Effective June 30, 2022.)
