# GOV § 66540.33

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 2. Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.33

---

## Text

The authority shall refer for recommendation the plans of routes, rights of way, terminals, yards, and related facilities and improvements to the city councils and boards of supervisors within the jurisdiction of which those facilities and improvements lie and to other state, regional, and local agencies and commissions as may be deemed appropriate by the authority. The authority shall give due consideration to all recommendations submitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 66540.34 (1st of two) by Stats. 2008, Ch. 179, Sec. 121. Effective January 1, 2009.)
