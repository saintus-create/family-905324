# GOV § 1171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6.5. Withholding Tax Deductions

**Citation:** GOV § 1171

---

## Text

The State, its agencies and political subdivisions, cities, and all other public agencies may withhold from any wages or salaries paid by them that portion required to be withheld by any law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 83.)
