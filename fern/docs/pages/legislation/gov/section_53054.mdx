# GOV § 53054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.5. Cable Television and Video Provider Customer Service and Information Act

**Citation:** GOV § 53054

---

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Cable Television and Video Provider Customer Service and Information Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 262, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
