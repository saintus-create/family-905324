# GOV § 26331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Revenue Bonds for County Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26331

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause designating the rights, limitations, powers and duties arising upon breach by the board of any of the covenants, conditions, or obligations contained in any indenture.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 815.)
