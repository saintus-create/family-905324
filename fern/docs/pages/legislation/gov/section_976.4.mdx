# GOV § 976.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Payment of Judgments Against Local Public Entities > Article: 2. Funding Judgments With Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 976.4

---

## Text

The board shall provide for holding the election in the same manner as provided by law in respect to general elections of the local taxing entity so far as applicable, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
