# GOV § 51332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of City Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51332

---

## Text

The transfer may be rescinded:

(a) At any time by the joint action of the city and the county.

(b) By separate action of either with one year’s notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
