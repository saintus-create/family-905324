# GOV § 67631

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 9. Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 67631

---

## Text

The proceeds of the sale of bonds shall be deposited and disbursed as provided in the indenture authorizing the issuance of such bonds.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1373.)
