# GOV § 3262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.6. Firefighters

**Citation:** GOV § 3262

---

## Text

The rights and protections described in this chapter shall only apply to a firefighter during events and circumstances involving the performance of his or her official duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 591, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
