# GOV § 19242.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Limited Examination and Appointment Program > Article: 2. Examinations and Appointments

**Citation:** GOV § 19242.1

---

## Text

An appointment to a position for the purpose of completing a job performance evaluation shall be known as an examination appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 478, Sec. 12.)
