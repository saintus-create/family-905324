# GOV § 71265

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 6. Provisions Relating to Trial Courts > Article: 7. Marshal

**Citation:** GOV § 71265

---

## Text

All provisions of Sections 26600 to 26604, inclusive, 26607 to 26608.1, inclusive, 26609, 26611, 26660 to 26664, inclusive, and 26680 of the Government Code, and Sections 262, 262.1, 262.2, 262.3, 262.4, and 262.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure, apply to marshals and govern their powers, duties and liabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 353. Effective January 1, 2003.)
