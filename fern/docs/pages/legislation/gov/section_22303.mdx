# GOV § 22303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Agreements for Coverage > Article: 2. Retirement System Referendums

**Citation:** GOV § 22303

---

## Text

In the case of employees in positions covered by the retirement systems set forth in subdivision (f) of Section 22009.1, the board shall conduct each such referendum; provided, that each such referendum is authorized by the Legislature.
