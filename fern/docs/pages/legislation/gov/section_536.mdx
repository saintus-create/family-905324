# GOV § 536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. SEAT OF GOVERNMENT, POLITICAL DIVISIONS, AND LEGAL DISTANCES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 6. Legal Distances

**Citation:** GOV § 536

---

## Text

From the county seat of San Benito County to Sacramento it is one hundred and seventy-three (173) miles.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
