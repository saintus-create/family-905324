# GOV § 25506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 7. Purchasing Agents

**Citation:** GOV § 25506

---

## Text

Notices of sales shall be posted for not less than five business days preceding the day of sale in the courthouse of the county and in the office of the purchasing agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
