# GOV § 6712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6712

---

## Text

The Governor shall proclaim annually the fourth Friday in September to be “Native American Day.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 637, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
