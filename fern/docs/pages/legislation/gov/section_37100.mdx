# GOV § 37100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37100

---

## Text

The legislative body may pass ordinances not in conflict with the Constitution and laws of the State or the United States.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
