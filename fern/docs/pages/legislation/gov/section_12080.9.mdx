# GOV § 12080.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 7.5. Executive Reorganization

**Citation:** GOV § 12080.9

---

## Text

Each reorganization plan which takes effect shall be printed in the same volume as the acts of the session of the Legislature to which it was submitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12079 by Stats. 1968, Ch. 449.)
