# GOV § 27460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27460

---

## Text

If an inquest is held by the coroner and no other person takes charge of the body of the deceased, he shall cause it to be interred decently.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
