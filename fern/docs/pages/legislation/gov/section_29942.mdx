# GOV § 29942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 2. Destruction of Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29942

---

## Text

At the time and place specified and in accordance with the terms of the notice, the board shall publicly destroy the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
