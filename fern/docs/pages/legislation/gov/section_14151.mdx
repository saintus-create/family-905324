# GOV § 14151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 3. Contracts for Services With the United States

**Citation:** GOV § 14151

---

## Text

Any architectural or construction work on buildings shall be limited to projects on property owned or controlled by the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 84.)
