# GOV § 43902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 8. Destruction of Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 43902

---

## Text

The notice shall state:

(a) The time and place of the destruction.

(b) The reason for the destruction.

(c) A general description of the character and amount of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
