# GOV § 26625.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1.2. Court Security in Contra Costa County

**Citation:** GOV § 26625.9

---

## Text

All sworn permanent employees assigned to the court security bureau shall be required to meet those requirements of the California Commission on Peace Officer Standards and Training imposed on all marshal’s departments in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 210, Sec. 14. (AB 1984) Effective January 1, 2021.)
