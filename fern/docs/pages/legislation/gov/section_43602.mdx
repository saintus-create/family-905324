# GOV § 43602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43602

---

## Text

Pursuant to this article, a city may incur indebtedness for any municipal improvement requiring an expenditure greater than the amount allowed for it by the annual tax levy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
