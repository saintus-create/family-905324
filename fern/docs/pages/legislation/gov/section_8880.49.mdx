# GOV § 8880.49

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 5. Lottery Game Retailers

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.49

---

## Text

A contract with a lottery game retailer shall not be assignable or transferable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 500, Sec. 9. Effective August 17, 1992. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
