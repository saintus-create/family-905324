# GOV § 14708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 5. State Land Settlement

**Citation:** GOV § 14708

---

## Text

All personal property received from the state land settlement shall be held by the department for use by it, for transfer to and for use by other state agencies which receive their support from the General Fund, or it may be sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
