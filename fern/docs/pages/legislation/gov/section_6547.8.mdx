# GOV § 6547.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 6547.8

---

## Text

No member of the governing body of the authority shall be personally liable on the bonds or be subject to any personal liability or accountability by reason of the issuance of bonds pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 920, Sec. 1. Effective October 12, 1997.)
