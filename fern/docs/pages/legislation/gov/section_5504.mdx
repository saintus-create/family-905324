# GOV § 5504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 6. Uniform Facsimile Signatures of Public Officials Act

**Citation:** GOV § 5504

---

## Text

This act shall be so construed as to effectuate its general purpose to make uniform the law of states which enact it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1061.)
