# GOV § 15837.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 15837.6

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 10295 and Sections 10335 to 10382, inclusive, of the Public Contract Code shall not apply to agreements entered into by the board or Treasurer in connection with the obtaining of financing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 932, Sec. 3. Effective September 25, 1985.)
