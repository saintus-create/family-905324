# GOV § 6

**Citation:** GOV § 6

---

## Text

Title, division, part, chapter, article, and section headings do not in any manner affect the scope, meaning, or intent of the provisions of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
