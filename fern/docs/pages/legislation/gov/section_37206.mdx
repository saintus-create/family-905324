# GOV § 37206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37206

---

## Text

By ordinance or resolution, the legislative body shall prescribe the time and method of paying salaries and wages of officers and employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
