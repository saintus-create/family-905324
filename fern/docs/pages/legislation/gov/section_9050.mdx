# GOV § 9050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 3. Crimes Against the Legislative Power

**Citation:** GOV § 9050

---

## Text

Every person who wilfully, and by force or fraud, prevents the Legislature, either of the houses composing it, or any of the members thereof from meeting or organizing is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
