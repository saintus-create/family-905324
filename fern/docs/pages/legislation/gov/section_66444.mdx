# GOV § 66444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Maps > Article: 3. Parcel Maps

**Citation:** GOV § 66444

---

## Text

The content and form of parcel maps shall be governed by the provisions of this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
