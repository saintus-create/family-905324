# GOV § 3128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9.1. Public Transit Employer Obligations

**Citation:** GOV § 3128

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be construed as creating any labor requirements that are less protective of employees than any labor requirements created pursuant to statute or a collective bargaining agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 419, Sec. 1. (AB 96) Effective January 1, 2024.)
