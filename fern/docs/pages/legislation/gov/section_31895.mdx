# GOV § 31895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 18. Adjustment to Fit Other Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31895

---

## Text

As used in this article “public agency” includes the federal government or any federal department or agency, this state, any other state or any state department or agency, a county, city, public corporation, or public district of this state or any other state. Section 31478 does not apply to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 511.)
