# GOV § 54958

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 54958

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply to the legislative body of every local agency notwithstanding the conflicting provisions of any other state law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1588.)
