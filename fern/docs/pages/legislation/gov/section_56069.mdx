# GOV § 56069

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56069

---

## Text

“Proposal” means a desired change of organization or reorganization initiated by a petition or by resolution of application of a legislative body or school district for which a certificate of filing has been issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 52. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
