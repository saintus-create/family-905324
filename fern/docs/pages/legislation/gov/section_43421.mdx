# GOV § 43421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Street Work Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 43421

---

## Text

The revolving fund may be created and used when the cost of the work or improvement is to be paid for:

(a) By special assessment.

(b) Out of any general or special city fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
