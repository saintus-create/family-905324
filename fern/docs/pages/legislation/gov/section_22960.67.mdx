# GOV § 22960.67

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Participant Accounts

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.67

---

## Text

The net earnings of the fund shall be allocated to the participant’s account as of each valuation date in the ratio that the participant’s account balance bears to the aggregate of all participants’ account balances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
