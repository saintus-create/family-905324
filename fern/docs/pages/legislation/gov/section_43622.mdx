# GOV § 43622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

## Text

The bonds shall be issued in such denomination or denominations as the legislative body may prescribe.
