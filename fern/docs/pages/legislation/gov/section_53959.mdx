# GOV § 53959

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 15. District Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53959

---

## Text

Upon demand of the county auditor or the governing board of the district, the officer entrusted with the fund shall give an account of the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 333.)
