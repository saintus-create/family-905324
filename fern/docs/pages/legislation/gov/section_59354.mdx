# GOV § 59354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 8. Notice of Assessment Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 59354

---

## Text

The notice shall state that any such owner may file his written consent to the refunding at any time prior to the adoption of the resolution of final confirmation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
