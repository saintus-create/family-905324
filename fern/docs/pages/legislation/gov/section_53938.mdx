# GOV § 53938

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 13.5. Payment and Apportionment of Special Assessment Liens

## Text

As used in this article, “transaction” means negotiated purchase and sale, gift, or devise.
