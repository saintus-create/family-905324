# GOV § 32002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 5. Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 32002

---

## Text

All warrants drawn on the fund shall be signed by the treasurer and at least one other member of the board, who shall be designated by the board. No warrant is valid until it is countersigned, numbered, and recorded by the county auditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
