# GOV § 26729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26729

## Text

The fee for furnishing a notice for publication is nineteen dollars ($19).
