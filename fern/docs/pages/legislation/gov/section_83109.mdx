# GOV § 83109

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 3. Fair Political Practices Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 83109

---

## Text

For purposes of Section 19818.6, a nonclerical position under the Commission shall not be included in the same class in the civil service classification plan with any position of any other department or agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 654, Sec. 2. (AB 1418) Effective January 1, 2014. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
