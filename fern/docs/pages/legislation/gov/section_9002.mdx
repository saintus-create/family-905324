# GOV § 9002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 1. Numbering and Election of Legislators

**Citation:** GOV § 9002

---

## Text

At the general election in every fourth year after 1908 a Senator shall be elected in each odd-numbered senatorial district. At the general election in every fourth year after 1910 a Senator shall be elected in each even-numbered district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
