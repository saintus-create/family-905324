# GOV § 16480.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 4.5. Treasury Pooled Money Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 16480.3

---

## Text

Immediately after designations described in Section 16480.1, the Treasurer shall invest or make deposits in banks and savings and loan associations in accordance with the designations.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 349.)
