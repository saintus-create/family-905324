# GOV § 16563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 4. Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 16563

---

## Text

The contracts shall be executed by the depositaries in triplicate. The Treasurer shall file one copy of each contract with the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
