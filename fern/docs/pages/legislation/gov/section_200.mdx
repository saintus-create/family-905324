# GOV § 200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Sovereignty of the State > Article: 5. Rights Over Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 200

---

## Text

The State has the rights prescribed in this article over persons within its limits, to be exercised in the cases and in the manner provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
