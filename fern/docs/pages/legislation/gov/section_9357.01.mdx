# GOV § 9357.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 5. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 9357.01

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 9357 or 9357.05, the rate of contribution for a member of the system first elected after the date this section becomes operative shall be 8 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1820.)
