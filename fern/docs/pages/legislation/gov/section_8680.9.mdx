# GOV § 8680.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8680.9

---

## Text

“Local emergency” means a condition of extreme peril to persons or property proclaimed as such by the governing body of the local agency affected, in accordance with Section 8630.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
