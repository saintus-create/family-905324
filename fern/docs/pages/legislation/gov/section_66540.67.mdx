# GOV § 66540.67

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 5. Employee Benefits and Retirement System

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.67

---

## Text

Nothing in any pension or retirement system or plan shall prevent the board from, at any time, amending, changing, modifying or terminating any provision for benefits, participation, or contributions thereto or thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
