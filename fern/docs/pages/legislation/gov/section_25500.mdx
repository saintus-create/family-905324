# GOV § 25500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 7. Purchasing Agents

**Citation:** GOV § 25500

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may employ a purchasing agent and such assistants as are necessary for him properly to fulfill his duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
