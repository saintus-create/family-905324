# GOV § 31699.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.11. Dental Care

**Citation:** GOV § 31699.21

---

## Text

A retired member of a county retirement system covered by this chapter may enroll in a dental care program offered pursuant to this article, subject to meeting the eligibility requirements established for the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 59, Sec. 4. (AB 2664) Effective January 1, 2013.)
