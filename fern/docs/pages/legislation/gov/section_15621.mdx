# GOV § 15621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15621

---

## Text

The board may hire or lease upon the written approval of the Department of General Services any property, real or personal, for its occupancy or use in the performance of its duties.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
