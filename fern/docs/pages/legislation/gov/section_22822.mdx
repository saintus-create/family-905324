# GOV § 22822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 4. Eligibility

**Citation:** GOV § 22822

---

## Text

No person is eligible for enrollment in a health benefit plan pursuant to this part as a family member if he or she becomes a family member of a surviving spouse of a deceased member of the system after the date of the member’s death.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
