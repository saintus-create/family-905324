# GOV § 1100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4.5. Sales of Public Securities

**Citation:** GOV § 1100

---

## Text

As used in this article, “public securities” means any issue of bonds, notes, warrants, or other evidences of indebtedness and the interest coupons, if any, attached thereto, issued by any public body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 53.)
