# GOV § 52051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 52051

---

## Text

The powers and functions of an authority are vested in its commissioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
