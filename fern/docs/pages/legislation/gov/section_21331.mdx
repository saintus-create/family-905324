# GOV § 21331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13. Retirement Benefits > Article: 3. Cost-of-Living Adjustments

**Citation:** GOV § 21331

---

## Text

An adjustment in a monthly allowance under this article shall be derived from or credited to contributions of the employer, and if the monthly allowance is a retirement allowance the adjustment shall be applied as an increase or decrease, as the case may be, in the current and prior service pensions.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
