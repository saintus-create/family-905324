# GOV § 11373.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Office of Administrative Hearings > Article: 2. Medical Quality Hearing Panel

**Citation:** GOV § 11373.3

---

## Text

The Office of Administrative Hearings shall provide facilities and support personnel for the review committee panel and shall assess the Medical Board of California for facilities and personnel, where used to adjudicate cases involving the Medical Board of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1091, Sec. 44.)
