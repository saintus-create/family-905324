# GOV § 4450.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Access to Public Buildings by Physically Handicapped Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 4450.5

---

## Text

The State Architect’s regulations adopted pursuant to Section 4450 shall require that all parking spaces reserved for the handicapped be identified as prescribed by Sections 22511.7 and 22511.8 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 484, Sec. 1.)
