# GOV § 13474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Purchase and Sale of Bonds > Article: 2. State School Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13474

---

## Text

All bonds purchased under this article shall be delivered to the Treasurer, who shall credit the interest to the State School Fund, when collected.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 544.)
