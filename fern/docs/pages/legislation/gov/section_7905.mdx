# GOV § 7905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. EXPENDITURE LIMITATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL

**Citation:** GOV § 7905

---

## Text

Revenues to a local jurisdiction from regulatory licenses, user charges, or user fees may be aggregated if they are reasonably related.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1205.)
