# GOV § 38752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5.5. Transfer of City Waterworks Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 38752

---

## Text

The ordinance shall specify the price, the yearly or monthly rental, or if the transfer and control of the waterworks system is to be made upon terms, the ordinance shall briefly state the terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 329.)
