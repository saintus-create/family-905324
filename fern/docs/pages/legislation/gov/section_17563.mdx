# GOV § 17563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Commission Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17563

---

## Text

Any funds received by a local agency or school district pursuant to the provisions of this chapter may be used for any public purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 879, Sec. 8.)
