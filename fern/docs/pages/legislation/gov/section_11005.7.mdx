# GOV § 11005.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11005.7

---

## Text

The Department of General Services on or before July 1, 1991, shall develop procedures and policies to expedite normal procedures for purchasing, leasing, and contracting during a business interruption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1210, Sec. 5.)
