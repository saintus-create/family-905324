# GOV § 53463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 3. Lost or Destroyed Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53463

---

## Text

The application shall be by petition a copy of which shall be served upon the legislative body not later than ten days prior to the time set for the hearing.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
