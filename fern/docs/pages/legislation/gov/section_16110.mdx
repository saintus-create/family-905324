# GOV § 16110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. Special Supplemental Subventions

**Citation:** GOV § 16110

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this chapter to provide special supplemental subventions to certain cities, multicounty special districts, and redevelopment agencies which, without these subventions, would lose substantial revenue because of the repeal of the former personal property tax subvention programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 447, Sec. 3. Effective July 16, 1984.)
