# GOV § 19816.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Department of Human Resources > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 19816.8

---

## Text

The department may expend, in accordance with law, all moneys made available for its use or for the administration of any statute administered by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19819 by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1015, Sec. 16. Effective September 26, 1985.)
