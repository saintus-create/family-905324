# GOV § 53870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53870

---

## Text

The notice shall specify:

(a) The time and place of sale.

(b) The office of the legislative body or clerk thereof where a description of the property may be ascertained.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
