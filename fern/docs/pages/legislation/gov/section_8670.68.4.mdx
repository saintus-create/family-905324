# GOV § 8670.68.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 9. Enforcement

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.68.4

---

## Text

Any operator of a small craft refueling dock who fails to register in compliance with subdivision (c) of Section 8670.36.1 shall be subject to a civil penalty in the amount of one hundred dollars ($100). The administrator may levy the penalty administratively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1313, Sec. 9. Effective September 30, 1992.)
