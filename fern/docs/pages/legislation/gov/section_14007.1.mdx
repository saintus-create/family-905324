# GOV § 14007.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 14007.1

---

## Text

(a) There is in the Department of Transportation the Division of Rail, which is responsible for the development of a comprehensive rail passenger system and the preparation of the rail passenger development plan required pursuant to Section 14036.

(b) An undersecretary of the agency shall be assigned to give attention to rail matters to ensure that the rail passenger system and plan are carried out.

(c) It is the intent of the Legislature that the commission give high priority to the implementation of the rail passenger system and plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1490, Sec. 2.)
