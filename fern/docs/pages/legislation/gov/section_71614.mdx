# GOV § 71614

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 7. Trial Court Employment Protection and Governance Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 71614

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as affecting the interpretation or operation of Sections 70210 to 70218, inclusive, for purposes of unification of the trial courts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 117, Sec. 8. (AB 414) Effective January 1, 2022.)
