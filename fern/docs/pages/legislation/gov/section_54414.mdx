# GOV § 54414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54414

---

## Text

Reference on the face of the bonds to the resolution by its date of adoption incorporates the provisions of the resolution and of this chapter into the bonds and their coupons.
