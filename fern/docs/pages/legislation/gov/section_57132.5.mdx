# GOV § 57132.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Elections

**Citation:** GOV § 57132.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 57132, the election on the question of a special reorganization shall be called pursuant to Section 57000 and held at the next regular primary or general election occurring in an even-numbered year at least 88 days after the date on which the resolution calling the election was adopted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 35, Sec. 3. (AB 419) Effective January 1, 2011.)
