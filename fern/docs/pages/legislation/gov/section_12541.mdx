# GOV § 12541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 3. Escheated Property

**Citation:** GOV § 12541

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall commence and prosecute actions on behalf of the State pursuant to Title 10, Part 3, of the Code of Civil Procedure, for the purpose of having it adjudged that title to real or personal property, to which the State has become entitled by escheat, is vested in the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 597.)
