# GOV § 53398.76

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.99. Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District > Article: 3. Division of Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53398.76

---

## Text

All costs incurred by a county in connection with the division of taxes pursuant to Section 53398.75 for a district shall be paid by that district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 785, Sec. 1. (SB 628) Effective January 1, 2015.)
