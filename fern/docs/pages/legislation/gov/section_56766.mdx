# GOV § 56766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Proceedings for Cities > Article: 4. Initiation by Petition

**Citation:** GOV § 56766

---

## Text

A petition for the consolidation of two or more cities shall be signed by not less than 5 percent of the registered voters of each affected city as shown on the county register of voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 2001.)
