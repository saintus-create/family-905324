# GOV § 19849.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of Salaries > Article: 4. Miscellaneous Compensation

**Citation:** GOV § 19849.2

---

## Text

Any state agency may, subject to rules and regulations of the department, insure its employees against injury or death incurred while flying on state business in any except regularly scheduled passenger aircraft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
