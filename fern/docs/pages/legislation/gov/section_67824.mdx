# GOV § 67824

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 67824

---

## Text

The secretary of the board shall maintain minutes of the meetings of the board and, as soon as possible after each meeting, shall cause a copy of the minutes to be forwarded to each member of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
