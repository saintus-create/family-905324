# GOV § 17511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 17511

---

## Text

“City” means any city whether general law or charter, except a city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
