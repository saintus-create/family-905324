# GOV § 45315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Retirement > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 45315

---

## Text

The determination of the retirement board is final and shall not be modified or set aside except for fraud or abuse of discretion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
