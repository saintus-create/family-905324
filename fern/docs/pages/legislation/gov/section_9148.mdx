# GOV § 9148

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 8. Legislative Oversight of State Board Formation and Licensed Professional Practice

**Citation:** GOV § 9148

---

## Text

Any state board or category of licensed professional proposed for creation by the Legislature shall be subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 315, Sec. 3. (AB 28) Effective January 1, 2012.)
