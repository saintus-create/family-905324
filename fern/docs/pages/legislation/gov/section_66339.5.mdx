# GOV § 66339.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 13. Accessory Dwelling Units > Article: 3. Junior Accessory Dwelling Units

**Citation:** GOV § 66339.5

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), this article shall supersede a conflicting local ordinance.

(b) This article does not limit the authority of local agencies to adopt less restrictive requirements for the creation of a junior accessory dwelling unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 520, Sec. 13. (SB 543) Effective January 1, 2026.)
