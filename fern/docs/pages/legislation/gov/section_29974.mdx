# GOV § 29974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 3. Special Improvement Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29974

---

## Text

Upon the date fixed for redemption, the bonds called shall be redeemed at par and accrued interest to that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
