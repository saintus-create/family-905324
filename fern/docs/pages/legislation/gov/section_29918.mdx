# GOV § 29918

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29918

---

## Text

The bonds shall be sold at the times, in the amounts, and in the manner prescribed by the board, but for not less than par.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
