# GOV § 8670.56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 8. Oil Spill Technical Advisory Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.56

---

## Text

The administrator may expend from the Oil Spill Prevention and Administration Fund any amounts necessary for the purposes of carrying out this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 17. Effective September 24, 1990.)
