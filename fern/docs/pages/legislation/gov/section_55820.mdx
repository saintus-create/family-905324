# GOV § 55820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Counties Transaction and Use Tax

**Citation:** GOV § 55820

---

## Text

The authority has no power to impose any tax other than the transactions and use tax imposed upon approval of the voters in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 384, Sec. 1. Effective September 9, 1991.)
