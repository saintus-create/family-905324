# GOV § 25350.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25350.5

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may acquire by eminent domain any property necessary to carry out any of the powers or functions of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1240.)
