# GOV § 7283.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.2. Standards for Participation in United States Immigration and Customs Enforcement Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 7283.2

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to provide, expand, or ratify the legal authority of any state or local law enforcement agency to detain an individual based upon an ICE hold request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 768, Sec. 3. (AB 2792) Effective January 1, 2017.)
