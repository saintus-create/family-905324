# GOV § 14672.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. State Property

**Citation:** GOV § 14672.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14670, the Director of General Services, with the consent of the Department of Mental Hygiene, may let to the City of Costa Mesa for a public purpose real property which belongs to the state and which is retained by the state primarily to provide a peripheral buffer area, or zone, between real property upon which is located a state hospital and adjacent real property, when the director deems such letting is in the best interests of the state.

The lease shall be for a period not to exceed 20 years but the lease may provide for the renewing of the lease for an additional period not to exceed 20 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1951.)
