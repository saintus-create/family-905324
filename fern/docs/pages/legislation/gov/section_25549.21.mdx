# GOV § 25549.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 11. Condominium Occupancy

**Citation:** GOV § 25549.21

---

## Text

The site in which the condominium interest is to be acquired may, prior to acquisition of such interest, be owned by a private person, firm, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 158, Sec. 1.)
