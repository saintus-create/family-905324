# GOV § 8405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.6. Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement > Article: 2. CaliforniaVolunteers

**Citation:** GOV § 8405

---

## Text

CaliforniaVolunteers, as established by Executive Order S-24-06, is hereby established in the Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 30. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024.)
