# GOV § 60355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.5. California Special District Consolidation Assistance Program

**Citation:** GOV § 60355

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall determine after public hearing which applications by districts for loans shall be approved based on the criteria established pursuant to Section 60354.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1392, Sec. 1.)
