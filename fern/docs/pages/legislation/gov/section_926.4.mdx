# GOV § 926.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Presentation of Claims to State Controller

**Citation:** GOV § 926.4

---

## Text

Any person who is aggrieved by the disapproval of a claim by the Controller may appeal to the department. If the department finds that facts are presented justifying such action, the Controller shall reconsider his or her rejection of the claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 47. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
