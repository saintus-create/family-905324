# GOV § 29617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Charges > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29617

---

## Text

Every sum directed by law to be raised for any county purpose under the direction of the board of supervisors or declared to be such is a county charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
