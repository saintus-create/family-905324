# GOV § 31458

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31458

---

## Text

“Beneficiary” means any person in receipt of a pension, annuity, retirement allowance, death benefit, or any other benefit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
