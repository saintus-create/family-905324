# GOV § 6061.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 4. Manner of Publication

**Citation:** GOV § 6061.3

---

## Text

Publication of notice pursuant to this section shall be for three successive times.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 357.)
