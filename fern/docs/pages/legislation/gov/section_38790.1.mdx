# GOV § 38790.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 38790.1

---

## Text

Any city collecting garbage fees or charges may collect delinquent fees or charges in the manner provided in Section 25831 for counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 175.)
