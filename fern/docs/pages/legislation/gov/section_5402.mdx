# GOV § 5402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Tax or Assessment Levy for Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 5402

---

## Text

“Governing body” as used in this chapter means the board of supervisors, city council, board of directors or other legislative or governing body which has the power to fix the annual tax or assessment levy for a public body.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 333.)
