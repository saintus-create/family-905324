# GOV § 8506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. The Milton Marks Commission on California State Government Organization and Economy > Article: 1. Creation and Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 8506

---

## Text

All vacancies in the commission membership shall be filled in the same manner in which original appointments were made.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8507 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1625.)
