# GOV § 17550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Commission Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17550

---

## Text

Reimbursement of local agencies and school districts for costs mandated by the state shall be provided pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
