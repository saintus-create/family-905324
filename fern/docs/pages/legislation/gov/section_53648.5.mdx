# GOV § 53648.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53648.5

---

## Text

Upon the removal by federal law of the conflicting federal law or rule the agreement between the treasurer or other authorized official and a depository may be terminated by either party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1132, Sec. 11.)
