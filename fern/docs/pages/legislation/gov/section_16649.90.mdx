# GOV § 16649.90

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Prohibited Business Arrangements

**Citation:** GOV § 16649.90

---

## Text

The prohibitions contained in Sections 16649.83, 16649.87, 16649.88, and 16649.89, shall not apply to any loan or extension of credit for which an agreement is entered into before the date of enactment of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1351, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
