# GOV § 82054

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82054

---

## Text

“Statewide petition” means a petition to qualify a proposed state measure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
