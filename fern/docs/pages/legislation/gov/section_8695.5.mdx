# GOV § 8695.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.6. Economic Disaster Act of 1984

**Citation:** GOV § 8695.5

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the disaster response of state agencies does not adequately focus on the economic impact of a natural disaster on the business community.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1410, Sec. 2.)
