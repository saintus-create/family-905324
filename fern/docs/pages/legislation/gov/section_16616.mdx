# GOV § 16616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Savings and Loan Association Deposits > Article: 2. Security for Deposits

**Citation:** GOV § 16616

---

## Text

On demand of the Treasurer, the Insurance Commissioner shall issue a certificate showing the qualifications of any admitted surety insurer as surety for deposits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 349.)
