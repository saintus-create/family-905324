# GOV § 18525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18525

---

## Text

“Appointment” means the offer to and acceptance by a person of a position in the State civil service in accordance with this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
