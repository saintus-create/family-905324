# GOV § 38400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38400

---

## Text

As used in this article, “original dedicator” includes any person, or that person’s successors and assigns, who has dedicated any land in the city for use as a public park.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 26. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
