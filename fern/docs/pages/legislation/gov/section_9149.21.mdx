# GOV § 9149.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 10. Whistleblower Protection Act

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.21

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that state employees and other persons should disclose, to the extent not expressly prohibited by law, improper governmental activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 156, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
