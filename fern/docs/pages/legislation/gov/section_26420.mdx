# GOV § 26420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 15. Revenue Bonds for County Incinerators > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26420

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause relating to the bonds issued thereunder requiring the board to continuously operate all projects acquired, constructed, or completed, in whole or in part, from the proceeds of the bonds in an efficient and economical manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 822.)
