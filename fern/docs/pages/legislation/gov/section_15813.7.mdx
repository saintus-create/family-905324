# GOV § 15813.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.1. Art in Public Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 15813.7

---

## Text

If a work of art acquired pursuant to this chapter is to be sold by the state, such sale shall be made to the highest bidder, conditioned on the work of art first being offered to the artist at the bid price.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 513.)
