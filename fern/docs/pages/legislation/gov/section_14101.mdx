# GOV § 14101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14101

---

## Text

The department shall contract with qualified architects and engineers for the performance of work when it is determined by the Director of Transportation, with the approval of the Director of Finance, that the obtainable staff is unable to perform the particular work within the time the public interest requires such work to be done.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 212.)
