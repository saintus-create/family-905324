# GOV § 14865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 2. Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 14865

---

## Text

All printed matter for all state agencies and the Regents of the University of California shall be prepared at the expense of their respective funds or appropriations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
