# GOV § 69951

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 9. Official Reporters Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 69951

---

## Text

For transcription, the reporter may charge an additional 50 percent for special daily copy service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 257, Sec. 13. (AB 177) Effective September 23, 2021.)
