# GOV § 6710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6710

---

## Text

The Governor shall proclaim March 7, to be known hereafter as “Arbor Day.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 189.)
