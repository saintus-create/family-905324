# GOV § 65568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 10.5. Open-Space Lands

**Citation:** GOV § 65568

---

## Text

If any provision of this article or the application thereof to any person is held invalid, the remainder of the article and the application of such provision to other persons shall not be affected thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1590.)
