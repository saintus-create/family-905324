# GOV § 16380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 3. General Cash Revolving Fund

## Text

This article does not apply to the Unemployment Fund or the Unemployment Administration Fund created by the Unemployment Insurance Act.
