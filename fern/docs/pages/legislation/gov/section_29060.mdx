# GOV § 29060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 3. Recommended Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29060

---

## Text

The administrative officer or auditor, as designated by the board, shall compile the budget requests.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 24. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
