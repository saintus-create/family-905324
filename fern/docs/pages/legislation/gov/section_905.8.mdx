# GOV § 905.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 905.8

---

## Text

Nothing in this part imposes liability upon a public entity unless such liability otherwise exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
