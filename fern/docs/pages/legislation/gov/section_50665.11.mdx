# GOV § 50665.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50665.11

---

## Text

When two or more propositions for incurring indebtedness are submitted at the same election, the votes cast for and against each proposition shall be counted separately.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
