# GOV § 65852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Zoning Regulations > Article: 2. Adoption of Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 65852

---

## Text

All such regulations shall be uniform for each class or kind of building or use of land throughout each zone, but the regulation in one type of zone may differ from those in other types of zones.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1880.)
