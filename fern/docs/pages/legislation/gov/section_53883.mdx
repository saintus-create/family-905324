# GOV § 53883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8.5. Notification of Subordinate Interests

**Citation:** GOV § 53883

---

## Text

This article applies only to requirements to give notice to third parties and not to existing statutory requirements to give notice to the owner of the real property as shown on the most recent assessment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 97, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
