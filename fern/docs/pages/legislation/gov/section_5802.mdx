# GOV § 5802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10. Sale of Local Securities

**Citation:** GOV § 5802

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “parking authority” means any entity created pursuant to Division 18 (commencing with Section 31500) of the Streets and Highways Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 292.)
