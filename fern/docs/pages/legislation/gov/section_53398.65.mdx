# GOV § 53398.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.99. Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District > Article: 2. Preparation and Adoption of Infrastructure Financing Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 53398.65

---

## Text

The designated official shall consult with each affected taxing entity, and, at the request of any affected taxing entity, shall meet with representatives of an affected taxing entity. Any affected taxing entity may suggest revisions to the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 785, Sec. 1. (SB 628) Effective January 1, 2015.)
