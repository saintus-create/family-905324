# GOV § 13323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2. Fiscal Year Budgets

**Citation:** GOV § 13323

---

## Text

Upon request of a State agency or court at any time during the fiscal year, the department may authorize transfers between its budget allotments, including reserves.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 112.)
