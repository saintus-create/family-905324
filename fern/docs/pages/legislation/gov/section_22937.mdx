# GOV § 22937

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 10. Contracting with Public Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 22937

---

## Text

A contracting agency may elect, by amending its contract with the board, to participate in a Medicare reimbursement program for its employees, annuitants, or family members who are enrolled in a Medicare health benefit plan under this part, as prescribed by board regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
