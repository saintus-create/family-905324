# GOV § 29534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 11. Transportation Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29534

---

## Text

The county auditor shall keep records and make reports concerning the local transportation fund as the Director of Transportation or the Controller shall prescribe.

This section shall become operative on July 1, 1987.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 2) and added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 988, Sec. 3. Section operative July 1, 1987, by its own provisions.)
