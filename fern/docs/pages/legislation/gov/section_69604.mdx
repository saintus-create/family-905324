# GOV § 69604

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 3. Number of Judges

## Text

In the County of Stanislaus there are 17 judges of the superior court.
