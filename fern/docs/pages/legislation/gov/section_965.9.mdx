# GOV § 965.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Payment of Claims and Judgments Against the State

**Citation:** GOV § 965.9

## Text

This chapter does not apply to claims, settlements, and judgments against the Regents of the University of California.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 215.)
