# GOV § 16531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 2. Security for Deposits

**Citation:** GOV § 16531

---

## Text

That portion of any security for deposit that is in excess of the requirements of this article may be withdrawn or released on the written consent of the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1542.)
