# GOV § 22550.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Administration of Old Age and Survivors’ Insurance Program > Article: 2. Administrative Cost Assessment and Financial Obligations

**Citation:** GOV § 22550.5

---

## Text

A school district whose employees in positions covered under a retirement system, as defined in subdivision (c) of Section 22009.1, are included in the agreement, shall be subject to all obligations and liabilities imposed on a public agency under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 728, Sec. 9.)
