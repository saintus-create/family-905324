# GOV § 8413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.6. Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement > Article: 3. CaliforniaVolunteers Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8413

---

## Text

Members of the commission shall serve without compensation, but may be reimbursed for travel expenses and receive a per diem as appropriate and in compliance with Section 12638 of Title 42 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 30. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024.)
