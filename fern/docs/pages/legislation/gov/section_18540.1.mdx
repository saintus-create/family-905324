# GOV § 18540.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18540.1

---

## Text

“National emergency” as used in this part means any period in which the United States is at war prior to the declaration by the Governor of a state military emergency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 808.)
