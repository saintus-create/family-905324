# GOV § 14032.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 14032.6

---

## Text

The department shall, within existing resources, collect, analyze, and summarize highway congestion data and make it available upon request to California regional transportation planning agencies, congestion management agencies, and transit agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 392, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
