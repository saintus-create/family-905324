# GOV § 61142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 61142

---

## Text

A board of directors may change the boundaries of a zone or dissolve a zone by following the procedures in Sections 61140 and 61141.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
