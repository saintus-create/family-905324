# GOV § 53501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Pledge and Use of Revenues to Pay and Secure General Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53501

---

## Text

This article applies to all local agencies which have the power to issue general obligation bonds to acquire, construct or finance a revenue-producing facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1157.)
