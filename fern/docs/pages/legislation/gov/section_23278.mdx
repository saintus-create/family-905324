# GOV § 23278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23278

---

## Text

The board of the transferring county shall appoint as election officers three representatives who reside in the affected county represented.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
