# GOV § 9742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 3. Legislative Manual

**Citation:** GOV § 9742

---

## Text

The manual shall be distributed as follows:

(a) To each Senator and Assemblyman as determined by the Rules Committee of the respective houses.

(b) Each elective state officer, one copy.

(c) The State Library, five copies.

(d) Each free public library in the state, one copy.

(e) The Congressional Library at Washington, D.C., five copies.
