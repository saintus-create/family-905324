# GOV § 69948.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 9. Official Reporters Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 69948.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 69948, in Modoc County, the board of supervisors may, by ordinance, prescribe a higher rate of compensation for superior court reporters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 230, Sec. 1.)
