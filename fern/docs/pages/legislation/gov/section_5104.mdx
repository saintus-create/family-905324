# GOV § 5104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Protection of Bond Guarantors

**Citation:** GOV § 5104

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “order” means declaration, proclamation, or order of any officer or court having jurisdiction to make the declaration, proclamation, or order.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
