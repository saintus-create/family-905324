# GOV § 37464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37464

---

## Text

The legislative body may purchase, lease, construct, furnish, or repair such buildings. It may provide all necessary employees and supplies for the proper maintenance of the buildings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
