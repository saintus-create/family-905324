# GOV § 8524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. The Milton Marks Commission on California State Government Organization and Economy > Article: 2. Purposes and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8524

---

## Text

The commission may devise a basic test to determine what, if any, governmental activities should be granted continuing or permanent appropriations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 2038.)
