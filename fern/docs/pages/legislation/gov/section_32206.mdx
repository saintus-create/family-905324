# GOV § 32206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 32206

---

## Text

“Board” and “board of retirement” mean the County Foresters, Firewardens, and County Fire Protection District Firemen’s Retirement Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
