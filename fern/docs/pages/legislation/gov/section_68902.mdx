# GOV § 68902

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 3. The Supreme Court > Article: 3. The Reporter of Decisions of the Supreme Court and the Courts of Appeal

**Citation:** GOV § 68902

---

## Text

Such opinions of the Supreme Court, of the courts of appeal, and of the appellate divisions of the superior courts as the Supreme Court may deem expedient shall be published in the official reports. The reports shall be published under the general supervision of the Supreme Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 248. Effective September 28, 1998.)
