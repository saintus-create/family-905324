# GOV § 52106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 5. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 52106

---

## Text

An authority may, subject to the direction of the governing body, do all of the following:

(a) Conduct public hearings for all energy projects.

(b) Hold public hearings to review and approve all projects being developed within the community that involve a substantial use of energy and may impact the economic stability of the local economy, with particular attention to the impact on low- and moderate-income households.

(c) Exempt specific projects from the review process.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
