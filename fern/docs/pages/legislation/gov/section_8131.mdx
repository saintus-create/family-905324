# GOV § 8131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.6. California-Nevada Interstate Compact Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8131

## Text

As used in this chapter “commission” means California-Nevada Interstate Compact Commission.
