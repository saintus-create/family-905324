# GOV § 60250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. District Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 60250

---

## Text

In this chapter, “district” means any district created by, or pursuant to, law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1036.)
