# GOV § 23000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 23000

---

## Text

A county is the largest political division of the State having corporate powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
