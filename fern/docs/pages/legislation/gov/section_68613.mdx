# GOV § 68613

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 5. The Trial Court Delay Reduction Act

**Citation:** GOV § 68613

---

## Text

The Judicial Council may receive and expend on the programs established by this article any funds available from county, state, or federal government or other sources which may be available for such purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1232, Sec. 3.)
