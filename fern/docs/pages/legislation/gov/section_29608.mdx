# GOV § 29608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Charges > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29608

---

## Text

The contingent expenses necessarily incurred for the use and benefit of the county are county charges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
