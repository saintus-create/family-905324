# GOV § 20810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Employer Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 20810

---

## Text

The state shall make the increased contributions required on account of liability for benefits provided by Section 20415 from social security contributions which would have been payable by the employer had Section 20415 not been enacted up to an amount necessary to fully fund the cost of those benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
