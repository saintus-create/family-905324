# GOV § 9602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Operation of Statutes and Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9602

---

## Text

Every concurrent and joint resolution takes effect upon the filing of it with the Secretary of State.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 123.)
