# GOV § 29300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29300

---

## Text

The income and revenue paid into the county treasury shall be at once appropriated to and kept in separate funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
