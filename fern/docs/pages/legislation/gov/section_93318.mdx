# GOV § 93318

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.5. CENTRAL CALIFORNIA RAILROAD AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of the Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 93318

---

## Text

Agencies forming the joint powers agreement implementing the authority shall be liable for all debts and obligations of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 234, Sec. 1. (SB 325) Effective January 1, 2012.)
