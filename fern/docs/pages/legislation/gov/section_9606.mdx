# GOV § 9606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Operation of Statutes and Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9606

---

## Text

Any statute may be repealed at any time, except when vested rights would be impaired. Persons acting under any statute act in contemplation of this power of repeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
