# GOV § 25845.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 8. Health and Safety > Article: 3. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 25845.5

---

## Text

The board of supervisors, by ordinance, may provide that upon entry of a second or subsequent civil or criminal judgment within a two-year period finding that an owner of property is responsible for a condition that may be abated in accordance with an ordinance enacted pursuant to Section 25845, except for conditions abated pursuant to Section 17980 of the Health and Safety Code, the court may order the owner to pay treble the costs of the abatement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 114, Sec. 1.)
