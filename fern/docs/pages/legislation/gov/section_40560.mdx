# GOV § 40560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 40560

---

## Text

An authority shall be managed under the provisions of this chapter and may exercise the powers therein expressly granted or necessarily implied therefrom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
