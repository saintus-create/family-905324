# GOV § 25123.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 7. Ordinances

**Citation:** GOV § 25123.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of Section 25123, that portion of any ordinance which changes supervisorial salaries shall become effective 60 days after its adoption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 556.)
