# GOV § 51912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51912

---

## Text

“Other cities” mean the cities in Sacramento County with lesser population than the largest city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 972.)
