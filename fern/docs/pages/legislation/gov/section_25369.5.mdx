# GOV § 25369.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25369.5

---

## Text

In any county having a department under the management and control of a county forester and fire warden, the board may authorize rescue or first aid service, or both, as a function of that department and may appoint agents and employees and acquire needed property and equipment for such purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 568.)
