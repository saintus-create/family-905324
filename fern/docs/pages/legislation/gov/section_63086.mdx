# GOV § 63086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 63086

---

## Text

The bank may employ financial consultants, advisers, and accountants, as may be necessary in its judgment, in connection with the issuance and sale of any bonds of the bank. Payment for these services may be made out of the proceeds of the sale of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 4, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1999.)
