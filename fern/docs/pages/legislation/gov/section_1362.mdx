# GOV § 1362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Appointments, Nominations, Commissions, and Oaths > Article: 4. Oath of Office

**Citation:** GOV § 1362

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided, the oath may be taken before any officer authorized to administer oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
