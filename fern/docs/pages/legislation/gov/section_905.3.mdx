# GOV § 905.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 905.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law to the contrary, no claim shall be submitted by a local agency or school district, nor shall a claim be considered by the Department of General Services pursuant to Section 905.2, if that claim is eligible for consideration by the Commission on State Mandates pursuant to Article 1 (commencing with Section 17550) of Chapter 4 of Part 7 of Division 4 of Title 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 36. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
