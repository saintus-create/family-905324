# GOV § 51290.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 6. Eminent Domain or Other Acquisition

**Citation:** GOV § 51290.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public improvement” means facilities or interests in real property, including easements, rights-of-way, and interests in fee title, owned by a public agency or person, as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 51291.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 690, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
