# GOV § 54036

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 2. Automobile Parking Areas

**Citation:** GOV § 54036

---

## Text

Any and all acts authorizing or providing methods for the improvement or acquisition of real property by a local agency for street, highway, library, school, playground, or other purposes may be used to provide methods for improving or acquiring any property to be acquired or improved for the use authorized in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
