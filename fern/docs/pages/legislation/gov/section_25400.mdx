# GOV § 25400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 3. Cement Manufacturing Plants

**Citation:** GOV § 25400

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may purchase, lease, construct, or otherwise acquire, own, operate, manage, and control, in any county in the State, a cement manufacturing plant, and may purchase, lease, or otherwise acquire real or personal property to be used in connection with the plant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
