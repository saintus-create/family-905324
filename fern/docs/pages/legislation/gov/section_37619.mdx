# GOV § 37619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 7. Hospitals Managed by Board of Hospital Trustees

**Citation:** GOV § 37619

---

## Text

The revenue derived from the tax and money otherwise acquired for hospital purposes shall be placed in the hospital fund and expended for the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
