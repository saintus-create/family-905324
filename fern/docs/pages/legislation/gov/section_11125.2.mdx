# GOV § 11125.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 11125.2

---

## Text

Any state body shall report publicly at a subsequent public meeting any action taken, and any rollcall vote thereon, to appoint, employ, or dismiss a public employee arising out of any closed session of the state body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 968, Sec. 10.3.)
