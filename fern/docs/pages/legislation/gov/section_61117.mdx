# GOV § 61117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Finance

**Citation:** GOV § 61117

---

## Text

The board of directors may establish a revolving fund pursuant to Article 15 (commencing with Section 53950) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
