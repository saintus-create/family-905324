# GOV § 31698.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.9. Vision Care

**Citation:** GOV § 31698.2

---

## Text

Each retired member that elects to participate in the program shall be solely responsible for the payment of premiums.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 331, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
