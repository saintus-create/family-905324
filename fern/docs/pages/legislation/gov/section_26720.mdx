# GOV § 26720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26720

---

## Text

For services performed by them sheriffs shall charge and collect the fees fixed in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
