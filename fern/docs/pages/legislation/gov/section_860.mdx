# GOV § 860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 6. Administration of Tax Laws

**Citation:** GOV § 860

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “tax” includes a tax, assessment, fee or charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
