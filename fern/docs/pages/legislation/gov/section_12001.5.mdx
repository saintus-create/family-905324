# GOV § 12001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 12001.5

---

## Text

The Governor shall appoint a person to represent the state in Washington, D.C. to identify revenue sources available to the state in connection with the closure and conversion of military bases in the state. The representative shall be appointed or selected by, and report to, the Governor or a member of the Governor’s cabinet, as decided by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 3, Sec. 1. Effective September 27, 1993. Inoperative, by Sec. 4 of Ch. 3, upon determination by the Governor that a representative is no longer needed.)
