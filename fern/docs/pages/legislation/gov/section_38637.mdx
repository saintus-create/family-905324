# GOV § 38637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 2. Police Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 38637

---

## Text

A deduction shall not be made from the pay of any police officer granted leave of absence pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
