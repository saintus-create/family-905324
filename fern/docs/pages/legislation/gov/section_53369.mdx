# GOV § 53369

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.6. Infrastructure and Revitalization Financing Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 53369

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature in enacting this chapter to establish a long-term permanent program that provides local governments with tools and resources for specified purposes, including, but not limited to, public infrastructure, affordable housing, economic development and job creation, and environmental protection and remediation, in a manner that encourages local cooperation and includes appropriate protections for state and local taxpayers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 775, Sec. 1. (AB 229) Effective January 1, 2015.)
