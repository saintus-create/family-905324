# GOV § 8670.37.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 5.5. Financial Responsibility

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.37.52

---

## Text

The certificate of financial responsibility shall be conclusive evidence that the person or entity holding the certificate is the party responsible for the specified vessel, facility, or oil for purposes of determining liability pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 33. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014.)
