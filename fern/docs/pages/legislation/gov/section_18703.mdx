# GOV § 18703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 3. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 18703

---

## Text

The board shall provide for dismissals, demotions, suspensions, and other adverse action for or in the state civil service in accordance with Article VII of the Constitution and this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 478, Sec. 6.)
