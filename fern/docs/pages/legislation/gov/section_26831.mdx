# GOV § 26831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26831

---

## Text

The county clerk may charge a reasonable fee to cover the cost of preparing copies of any record, proceeding, or paper on file in his or her office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 75, Sec. 72. Effective July 19, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 156 of Ch. 75.)
