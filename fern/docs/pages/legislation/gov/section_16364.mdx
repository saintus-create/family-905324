# GOV § 16364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 1.5. Federal Trust Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16364

---

## Text

The State Controller and all state agencies receiving money from the United States shall keep an accurate account of all money deposited in, transferred to or from, or disbursed from, the Federal Trust Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1284.)
