# GOV § 88005.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 8. Ballot Pamphlet

**Citation:** GOV § 88005.5

---

## Text

The Legislative Counsel shall prepare and proofread the texts of all measures and the provisions which are repealed or revised.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
