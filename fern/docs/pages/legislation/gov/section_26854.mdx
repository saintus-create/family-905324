# GOV § 26854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26854

---

## Text

The fee for searching records or files is five dollars ($5) for each file.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 651, Sec. 1.)
