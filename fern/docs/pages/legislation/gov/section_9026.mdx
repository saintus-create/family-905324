# GOV § 9026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 2. Meeting and Organization of the Legislature

**Citation:** GOV § 9026

---

## Text

All standing committees of either the Senate or Assembly shall be appointed by the presiding officer of their respective house if the house by resolution or its rules does not direct otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
