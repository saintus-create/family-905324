# GOV § 20023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 20023

---

## Text

“County peace officer service” shall include service rendered in the sheriff’s office of a city and county in positions that were subsequently reclassified as positions within the definition of “county peace officer.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
