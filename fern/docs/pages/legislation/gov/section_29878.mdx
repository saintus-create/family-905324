# GOV § 29878

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 4. Purchase by State of County Warrants for Aid

**Citation:** GOV § 29878

---

## Text

All registered warrants issued to the State pursuant to this article are subject in every respect to Article 2.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
