# GOV § 54344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 2. Powers of a Local Agency

**Citation:** GOV § 54344

---

## Text

A local agency may prescribe, revise, and collect charges for the services, facilities, or water furnished by the enterprise.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 526.)
