# GOV § 31455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31455

---

## Text

Unless the context otherwise requires, or unless superseded by any provision of the California Public Employees’ Pension Reform Act of 2013, the definitions and general provisions contained in this article govern the construction of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 247, Sec. 5. (AB 1380) Effective January 1, 2014.)
