# GOV § 51281.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 5. Cancellation

**Citation:** GOV § 51281.1

---

## Text

The board or council may require the payment of a reasonable application fee to be made at the time a petition for cancellation is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1120.)
