# GOV § 22960.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.27

---

## Text

“Retirement” means termination of all employment for the employer and completion of all conditions precedent to receiving a distribution for retirement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
