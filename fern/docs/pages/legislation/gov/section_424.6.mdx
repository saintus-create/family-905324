# GOV § 424.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 424.6

---

## Text

Lace lichen (Ramalina menziesii) is the official state lichen.

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 113, Sec. 2. (AB 1528) Effective January 1, 2016.)
