# GOV § 50503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50503

---

## Text

If no protests have been received by the legislative body, it may lease the property pursuant to the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
