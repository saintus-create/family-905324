# GOV § 71671

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 7. Trial Court Employment Protection and Governance Act > Article: 7. Relation to Other Trial Court Statutes

**Citation:** GOV § 71671

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, each trial court employee shall have the protections provided for in Article 5 (commencing with Section 71650) of this chapter, except as otherwise provided by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1010, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2001.)
