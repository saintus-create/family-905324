# GOV § 66425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Maps > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66425

---

## Text

The necessity for tentative, final and parcel maps shall be governed by the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
