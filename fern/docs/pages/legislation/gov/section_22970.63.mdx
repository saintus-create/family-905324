# GOV § 22970.63

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Participation Accounts

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.63

---

## Text

The value of each participant’s account shall be determined at least once annually in a manner prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
