# GOV § 53726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 3.7. Voter Approval of Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53726

---

## Text

Except as set forth in Section 53727, this Article shall not be construed to repeal or affect any statute enacted prior to August 1, 1985 which authorizes the imposition of a special tax.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 4, 1986, by initiative Proposition 62.)
