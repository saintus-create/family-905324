# GOV § 19580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations From Service > Article: 1. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 19580

---

## Text

Either by deposition or at the hearing the employee may be examined and may examine or cause any person to be examined under Section 776 of the Evidence Code.
