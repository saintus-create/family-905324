# GOV § 15421.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15421.1

---

## Text

The State Public Defender is authorized to administer and award grants to improve indigent defense services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 615, Sec. 1. (SB 1117) Effective January 1, 2023.)
