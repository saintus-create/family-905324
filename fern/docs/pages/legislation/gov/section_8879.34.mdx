# GOV § 8879.34

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.49. The Highway Safety, Traffic Reduction, Air Quality, and Port Security Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8879.34

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provisions in the State General Obligation Bond Law, the maximum maturity of any bonds authorized by this chapter shall not exceed 30 years from the date of each respective series. The maturity of each series shall be calculated from the date of each series.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 25, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1B at the November 7, 2006, election.)
