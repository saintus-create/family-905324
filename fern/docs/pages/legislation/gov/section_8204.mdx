# GOV § 8204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Notaries Public > Article: 1. Notaries Public

**Citation:** GOV § 8204

---

## Text

The term of office of a notary public is for four years commencing with the date specified in the commission.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1009.)
