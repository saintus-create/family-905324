# GOV § 12184

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 3. Business Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 12184

---

## Text

(a) The Secretary of State shall charge and collect a fee of one hundred fifty dollars ($150) from an entity for its conversion made pursuant to Chapter 11.5 (commencing with Section 1150) of Division 1 of Title 1 of the Corporations Code.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2005.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 29) and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 480, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 2003. Section operative January 1, 2005, by its own provisions.)
