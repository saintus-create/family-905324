# GOV § 16428.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 9.5. Ratepayer Relief Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16428.7

---

## Text

Nothing in this article affects the allocation of funds from settlements entered into before the effective date of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 228, Sec. 16. Effective August 11, 2003.)
