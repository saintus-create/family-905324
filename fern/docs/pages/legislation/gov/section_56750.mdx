# GOV § 56750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Proceedings for Cities > Article: 3. Annexation and Other Changes of Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 56750

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 56300 and 56301, the commission shall not disapprove a change of organization or reorganization where the reason for disapproval is that the farmland security zone is excluded from the affected territory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 2001.)
