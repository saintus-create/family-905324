# GOV § 57137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Elections

**Citation:** GOV § 57137

---

## Text

On the ballot to the right of or below each question, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 20. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
