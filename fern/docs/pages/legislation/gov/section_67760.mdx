# GOV § 67760

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.85. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL PUBLIC TRANSIT FINANCE > Chapter: 4. Financial Transparency and Review

**Citation:** GOV § 67760

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that financial efficiency and transparency are imperative to build public confidence and support for public transit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 740, Sec. 4. (SB 63) Effective January 1, 2026.)
