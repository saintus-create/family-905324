# GOV § 28002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 28002

---

## Text

On presentation the treasurer shall pay the warrants out of the salary fund of the county treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
