# GOV § 22960.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.38

---

## Text

With regard to the plan, the board shall not engage in any transaction prohibited by Section 503(b) of Title 26 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
