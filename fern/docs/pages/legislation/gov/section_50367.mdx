# GOV § 50367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 3. Issuance of Bonds for the Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** GOV § 50367

---

## Text

The consent of the Legislature is given to the United States to acquire land upon the conditions and for the purposes set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
