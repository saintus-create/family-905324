# GOV § 27009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27009

---

## Text

The treasurer shall give a receipt to each person who deposits money into the county treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 643, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2007.)
