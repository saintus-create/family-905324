# GOV § 15553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 15553

---

## Text

Any entity within the Labor and Workforce Development Agency may share information for research, enforcement, or training with any other entity in the agency without a confidentiality agreement, except as the secretary may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 859, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
