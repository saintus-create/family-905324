# GOV § 15051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 3. Western States Organized Crime Compact

**Citation:** GOV § 15051

---

## Text

No compact proposed pursuant to this article is binding on the State of California until it has been approved by the Legislature of this state and the Legislature of Nevada, Oregon or Arizona, and the Congress of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1795.)
