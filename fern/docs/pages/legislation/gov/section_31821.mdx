# GOV § 31821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 14. Subrogation

**Citation:** GOV § 31821

---

## Text

The retirement association may join with the employer or its compensation insurance carrier in any proceeding under this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
