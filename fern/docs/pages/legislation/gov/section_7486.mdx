# GOV § 7486

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 20. Governmental Access to Financial Records > Article: 5. Penalties and Remedies

**Citation:** GOV § 7486

---

## Text

In any successful action to enforce liability for a violation of the provisions of this chapter, the customer may recover the cost of the action together with reasonable attorney’s fees as determined by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1320.)
