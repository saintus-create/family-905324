# GOV § 38408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38408

---

## Text

If the original dedicator does not protest, the majority of such owners do not protest in writing, and the other protests are overruled, the legislative body has jurisdiction to order the park abandoned.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
