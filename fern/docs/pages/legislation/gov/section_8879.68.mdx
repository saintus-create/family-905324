# GOV § 8879.68

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.491. Implementation of the Highway Safety, Traffic Reduction, Air Quality, and Port Security Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 11. State-Local Partnership Program

**Citation:** GOV § 8879.68

---

## Text

An eligible applicant under the program shall be a local or regional transportation agency that has responsibility for funding, procuring, or constructing transportation improvements within its jurisdiction, and that does either of the following:

(a) Has sought and received voter approval for the imposition of taxes or fees solely dedicated to transportation improvements and administers those taxes or fees.

(b) Has imposed uniform developer fees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 756, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 2008.)
