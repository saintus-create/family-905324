# GOV § 67001

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations of Policy

**Citation:** GOV § 67001

---

## Text

It is further declared that by virtue of the special conditions and circumstances of the natural ecology, developmental pattern, population distribution and human needs in the Lake Tahoe region, the region is experiencing problems of resource use and deficiencies of environmental control.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
