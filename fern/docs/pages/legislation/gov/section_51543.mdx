# GOV § 51543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51543

---

## Text

The county officers designated shall be officers who perform duties of the same character as the city officers whose duties are transferred to them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
