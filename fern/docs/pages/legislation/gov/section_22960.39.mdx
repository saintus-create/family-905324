# GOV § 22960.39

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Administration of the Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.39

---

## Text

The board may require a third-party administrator, recordkeeper, custodian, or investment manager that is contracted with, or appointed by the system, to be subject to the duties set forth in Section 22960.37.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
