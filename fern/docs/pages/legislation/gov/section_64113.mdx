# GOV § 64113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CALIFORNIA TRANSPORTATION FINANCING AUTHORITY > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE

**Citation:** GOV § 64113

---

## Text

A project sponsor of a project imposing tolls may incorporate congestion management mechanisms to regulate usage and increase mobility, accessibility, and environmental benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 474, Sec. 1. (AB 798) Effective January 1, 2010.)
