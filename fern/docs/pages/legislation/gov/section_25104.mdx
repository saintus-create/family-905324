# GOV § 25104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 6. Clerk of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 25104

---

## Text

The books, records, and accounts of the board shall be kept in the custody of the clerk and available for public inspection during normal business hours.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 57, Sec. 1.)
