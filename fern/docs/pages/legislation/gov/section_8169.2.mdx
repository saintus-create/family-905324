# GOV § 8169.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 1. General Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 8169.2

---

## Text

The director shall promulgate regulations for relocation payments and assistance consistent with the requirements of Sections 51063 and 51310 of the Health and Safety Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 144, Sec. 97.)
