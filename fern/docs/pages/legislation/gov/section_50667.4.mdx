# GOV § 50667.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50667.4

---

## Text

The legislative body may provide for events of default and terms upon which the bonds may be declared due before maturity and the terms upon which the declaration and its consequences may be waived.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
