# GOV § 18157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Oaths for State Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 18157

---

## Text

Every appointing power shall notify every new civil service employee immediately following his appointment of the provisions of this chapter that are applicable to the employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
