# GOV § 41607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 6. Chief of Police

**Citation:** GOV § 41607

---

## Text

The chief of police shall perform any license fee and tax collection services prescribed by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
