# GOV § 32270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 3. Board of Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 32270

---

## Text

The board has charge and control of the funds of the system and shall administer them and shall order payments therefrom in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
