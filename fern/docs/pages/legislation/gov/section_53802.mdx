# GOV § 53802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 6.5. Building Certificates

**Citation:** GOV § 53802

---

## Text

Certificates issued pursuant to this article are negotiable instruments and are transferable between local agencies and state agencies or between such agencies and other persons having jurisdiction over special or trust funds at a price mutually agreed upon by the agencies or by such agencies and other persons.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 391.)
