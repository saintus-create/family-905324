# GOV § 34859

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 1. City Manager

**Citation:** GOV § 34859

---

## Text

All ordinances establishing a city manager form of government, enacted prior to September 19, 1947, are confirmed, validated, and declared legally effective.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
