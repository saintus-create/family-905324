# GOV § 8285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8285

---

## Text

The commission may employ and fix the compensation, in accordance with law, of such professional, clerical and other assistants as may be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10305 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1335, Sec. 2.)
