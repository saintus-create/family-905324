# GOV § 50877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 3. Pensions and Death Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 50877

---

## Text

A member retired pursuant to this chapter shall report to the chief of the department from which he was retired on the first Mondays of April, July, October, and January of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
