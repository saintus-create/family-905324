# GOV § 19993.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. State-Owned Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** GOV § 19993.7

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to members of the California Highway Patrol nor to a peace officer defined in subdivision (a) of Section 830.36 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1154, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1996.)
