# GOV § 9357.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 5. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 9357.3

---

## Text

If the membership of a member is terminated, except by death or retirement pursuant to this chapter, he or she shall be paid forthwith all of his or her accumulated contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
