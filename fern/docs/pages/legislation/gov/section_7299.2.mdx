# GOV § 7299.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.5. Use of a Foreign Language in Public Services

**Citation:** GOV § 7299.2

---

## Text

The Department of Human Resources shall be responsible for informing state agencies of their responsibilities under this chapter and providing state agencies with technical assistance, upon request on a reimbursable basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 2. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
