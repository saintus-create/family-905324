# GOV § 15404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 15404

---

## Text

The State Public Defender may issue any regulations and take any actions as may be necessary for proper implementation of this part.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1125.)
