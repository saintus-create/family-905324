# GOV § 53398.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.9. Infrastructure Financing Districts in the Border Development Zone > Article: 3. Division of Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53398.31

---

## Text

All costs incurred by a county in connection with the division of taxes pursuant to Section 53398.30 for a district shall be paid by that district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 773, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
