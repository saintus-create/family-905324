# GOV § 95500

**Hierarchy:** Title: 13. CALIFORNIA SAVINGS AND ASSET PROJECT

**Citation:** GOV § 95500

---

## Text

An individual development account program, to be known as the California Savings and Asset Project, is hereby established. The program shall be administered by the Employment Development Department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1024, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003. Conditionally operative as prescribed in Section 95501.)
