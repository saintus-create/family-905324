# GOV § 17224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 2. Registration

**Citation:** GOV § 17224

---

## Text

If the Controller presents a warrant to the Treasurer for registration, after registration, the Treasurer shall return the warrant to the Controller for distribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 766, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
