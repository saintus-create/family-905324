# GOV § 11080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 5. Advertisements

**Citation:** GOV § 11080

---

## Text

Whenever any state agency is required or authorized by law to prepare and cause the publishing of any notice, advertisement or publication in any newspaper or other medium, it shall properly prepare the notice, advertisement or publication and deliver it to the Department of General Services in due time for publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
