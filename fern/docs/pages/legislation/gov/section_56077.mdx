# GOV § 56077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56077

---

## Text

“Subject agency” means each district or city for which a change of organization or reorganization is proposed or provided in a plan of reorganization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 355, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
