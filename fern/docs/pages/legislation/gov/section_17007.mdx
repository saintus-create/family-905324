# GOV § 17007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Form, Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17007

---

## Text

The Controller may, from time to time, provide that funds shall be designated by name, code number, letter, or combination of number and letter, on warrants issued by him, and in reports, statements, lists or tabulations furnished by him to the Treasurer, or by the Treasurer to him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 382.)
