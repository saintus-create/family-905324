# GOV § 8161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 1. General Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 8161

---

## Text

The department may request the assistance and advice of any state agency in the development of the master plan. Any state agency receiving such a request may render such assistance and advice to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 172.)
