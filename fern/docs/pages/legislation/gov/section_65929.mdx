# GOV § 65929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65929

---

## Text

“Lead agency” means the public agency which has the principal responsibility for carrying out or approving a project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1200.)
