# GOV § 14999.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Commission for Economic Development

**Citation:** GOV § 14999.9

---

## Text

The commission shall make a report of its activities, findings and recommendations to the Governor and the Legislature not later than February 1 of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 121.)
