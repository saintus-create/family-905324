# GOV § 84216.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 2. Filing of Campaign Statements

**Citation:** GOV § 84216.5

---

## Text

A loan of campaign funds, whether or not there is a written contract for the loan, made by a candidate or committee shall be reported as provided in Section 84211.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 853, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
