# GOV § 8880.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 3. Powers and Duties of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.27

---

## Text

Meetings with the Director

The Commission shall meet with the Director not less than once each quarter to make recommendations and set policy, to approve or reject reports of the Director and transact such other business that may be properly brought before it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
