# GOV § 37110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37110

---

## Text

The legislative body may spend money from the general fund for music and promotion, including promotion of sister city and town affiliation programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1195, Sec. 12.3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
