# GOV § 30004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 4. Payment of Unauthorized Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 30004

---

## Text

Each year the board shall levy a tax sufficient to pay the annual interest on the bonds and the principal as it becomes due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
