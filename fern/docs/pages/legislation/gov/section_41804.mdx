# GOV § 41804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 7. City Attorney

**Citation:** GOV § 41804

---

## Text

The city attorney shall receive such compensation as is allowed by the legislative body.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
