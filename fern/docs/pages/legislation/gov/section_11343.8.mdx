# GOV § 11343.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Administrative Regulations and Rulemaking > Article: 3. Filing and Publication

**Citation:** GOV § 11343.8

---

## Text

Upon the request of a state agency, the office may file with the Secretary of State and the office may publish in such manner as it believes proper, any regulation or order of repeal of a regulation not required by this article to be filed with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 11343.9 by Stats. 1981, Ch. 865, Sec. 12.)
