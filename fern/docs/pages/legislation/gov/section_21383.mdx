# GOV § 21383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13. Retirement Benefits > Article: 4. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 21383

---

## Text

The prior service pensions for state members are derived from contributions of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
