# GOV § 19997.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations from Service > Article: 2. Layoff and Demotion

**Citation:** GOV § 19997.1

---

## Text

The duties performed by any employee laid off may be assigned to any other employee or employees in the state agency holding positions in appropriate classes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
