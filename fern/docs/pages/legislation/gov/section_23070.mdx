# GOV § 23070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 1. Terms Defined

**Citation:** GOV § 23070

---

## Text

The terms and definitions contained in this article apply to the boundary descriptions of the several counties, unless otherwise declared.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
