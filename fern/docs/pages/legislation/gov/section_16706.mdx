# GOV § 16706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Duplicate Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 16706

---

## Text

The owner shall pay all costs and expenses in connection with the issuance of any new instrument.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
