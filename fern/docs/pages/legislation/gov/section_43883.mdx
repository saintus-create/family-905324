# GOV § 43883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 7. Registration

**Citation:** GOV § 43883

---

## Text

After any bond has been registered, the principal and interest shall be payable to the registered owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
