# GOV § 14963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. State Architect

**Citation:** GOV § 14963

---

## Text

The duties and functions formerly conducted by the State Fire Marshal that relate to construction, school, plan checking, and construction inspection are hereby transferred to the Office of the State Architect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 865, Sec. 5.)
