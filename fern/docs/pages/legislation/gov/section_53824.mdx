# GOV § 53824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 7. Temporary Borrowing

**Citation:** GOV § 53824

---

## Text

All such notes, tax anticipation warrants, or other evidences of indebtedness shall be issued only after the adoption of a resolution by a four-fifths vote of all members of the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
