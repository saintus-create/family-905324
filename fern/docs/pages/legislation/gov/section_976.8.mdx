# GOV § 976.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Payment of Judgments Against Local Public Entities > Article: 2. Funding Judgments With Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 976.8

---

## Text

If two-thirds or more of the votes cast upon the proposition at the election are in favor of incurring the bonded indebtedness, the board may issue the bonds at the time or times it deems proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
