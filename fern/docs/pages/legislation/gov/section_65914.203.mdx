# GOV § 65914.203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.2.2. Housing Developments Following the 2025 Los Angeles Wildfires

**Citation:** GOV § 65914.203

---

## Text

This chapter offers an optional streamlined, ministerial approval process. This chapter does not affect the availability, applicability, or use of any other exemption from Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000) of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 548, Sec. 3. (SB 625) Effective January 1, 2026.)
