# GOV § 54733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Contracts of Sanitation or Sewerage Enterprises

**Citation:** GOV § 54733

---

## Text

The contracts may be made for any period not exceeding the time when the principal and interest of all bonds of the enterprise have been paid in full.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1249.)
