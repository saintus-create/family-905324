# GOV § 25100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 6. Clerk of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 25100

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided by law, the county clerk is ex officio clerk of the board of supervisors of his county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
