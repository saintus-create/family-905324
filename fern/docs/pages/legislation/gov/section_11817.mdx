# GOV § 11817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8.1. State Government Strategic Planning and Performance Review Act > Article: 3. Strategic Planning and Performance Reviews

**Citation:** GOV § 11817

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that strategic plans developed pursuant to Section 11816 form the basis for conducting performance reviews pursuant to this article or for the implementation of performance budgeting systems pursuant to Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 11800).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 779, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1994.)
