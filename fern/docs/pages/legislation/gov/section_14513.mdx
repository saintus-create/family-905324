# GOV § 14513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14513

---

## Text

The commission may employ its own legal staff or contract with other state agencies for legal services, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 350, Sec. 1.)
