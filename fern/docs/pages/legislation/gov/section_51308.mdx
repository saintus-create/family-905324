# GOV § 51308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of City Functions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 51308

---

## Text

Any person pensioned at the execution of the contract has a vested property right in the pension fund for the payment of his pension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
