# GOV § 22970.61

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Participation Accounts

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.61

---

## Text

In the case of a contribution that is made under a mistake of fact, nothing in this part shall prohibit the return of that contribution to the participant within one year after discovery of the mistake.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
