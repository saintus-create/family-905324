# GOV § 66906.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. Personnel

**Citation:** GOV § 66906.8

---

## Text

The conservancy may select and hire private consultants or contractors to provide services necessary to achieve the purposes of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 15. Effective September 17, 1984.)
