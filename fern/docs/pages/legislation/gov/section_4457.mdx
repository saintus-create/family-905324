# GOV § 4457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Access to Public Buildings by Physically Handicapped Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 4457

---

## Text

On or after January 1, 1986, all portable buildings purchased, leased, or constructed by a school district shall meet the requirements of this chapter, except as provided in subdivision (f) of Section 4451.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 550, Sec. 2.)
