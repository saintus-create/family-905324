# GOV § 12462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 4. Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 12462

---

## Text

Upon request, the Controller shall give information in writing to either house of the Legislature relating to the fiscal affairs of the state or the duties of the Controller’s office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 81. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
