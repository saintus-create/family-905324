# GOV § 87211

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 7. Conflicts of Interest > Article: 2. Disclosure

**Citation:** GOV § 87211

---

## Text

This article does not apply to either of the following of the State Bar of California, who shall file statements under conflict of interest codes developed consistent with Section 87302:

(a) A member of the Board of Trustees.

(b) A designated employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 489, Sec. 3. (SB 1476) Effective January 1, 2025.)
