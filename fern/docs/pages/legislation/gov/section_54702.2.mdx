# GOV § 54702.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 3. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 54702.2

---

## Text

In determining the amount of bonds to be issued, the local agency may include all costs of the issuance of such bonds, bond reserve funds, and bond interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
