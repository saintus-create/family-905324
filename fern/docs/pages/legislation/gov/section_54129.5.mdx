# GOV § 54129.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 5. Earthquake Relief

**Citation:** GOV § 54129.5

---

## Text

In the event that the board loans funds to a local agency, the board may waive any provisions of Section 54127 it deems are not applicable to or feasible for use in connection with such loans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1777.)
