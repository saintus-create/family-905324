# GOV § 16650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16650

---

## Text

As used in this part, “fiscal agent” means any state fiscal agent selected and acting pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 16670).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 193, Sec. 42.)
