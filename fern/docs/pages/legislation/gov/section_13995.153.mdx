# GOV § 13995.153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. California Welcome Centers

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.153

---

## Text

Costs associated with the ongoing maintenance and operation of centers shall be borne by the entity operating a California Welcome Center.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 296, Sec. 1. Effective August 25, 2004.)
