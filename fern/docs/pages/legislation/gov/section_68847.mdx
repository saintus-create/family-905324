# GOV § 68847

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 3. The Supreme Court > Article: 2. The Chief Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 68847

---

## Text

The Clerk/Executive Officer of the Supreme Court shall collect in advance the fees specified in Article 4 (commencing with Section 68926) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 36, Sec. 14. (AB 452) Effective January 1, 2018.)
