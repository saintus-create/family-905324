# GOV § 12948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Discrimination Prohibited > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices, Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 12948

---

## Text

It is an unlawful practice under this part for a person to deny or to aid, incite, or conspire in the denial of the rights created by Section 51, 51.5, 51.7, 51.9, 54, 54.1, or 54.2 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 951, Sec. 3. (SB 224) Effective January 1, 2019.)
