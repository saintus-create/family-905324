# GOV § 8880.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 5. Lottery Game Retailers

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.54

---

## Text

Bonding

The Director may require a bond from any Lottery Game Retailer in an amount specified by regulation or may purchase blanket bonds covering the activities of selected Lottery Game Retailers. These bonds shall be payable upon order of the Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 55, Sec. 16. Effective April 16, 1986. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
