# GOV § 25821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 8. Health and Safety > Article: 2. Disposal Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 25821

---

## Text

The board may permit the use of any dump site, incinerator, or other disposal plant, by lease or otherwise, by municipalities or other governmental agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
