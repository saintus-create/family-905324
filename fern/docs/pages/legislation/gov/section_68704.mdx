# GOV § 68704

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.5. Commission on Judicial Performance > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68704

---

## Text

No act of the commission shall be valid unless concurred in by a majority of its members. The commission shall select one of its members to serve as chairperson.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 79, Sec. 12. (AB 143) Effective July 16, 2021.)
