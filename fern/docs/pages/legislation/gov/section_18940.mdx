# GOV § 18940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Employment Lists > Article: 2. Examinations

**Citation:** GOV § 18940

---

## Text

Any applicant for examination may request reasonable accommodation for a disability or sincerely held religious belief pursuant to the Fair Employment and Housing Act or any other applicable law. The department may prescribe rules governing those requests.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 40. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
