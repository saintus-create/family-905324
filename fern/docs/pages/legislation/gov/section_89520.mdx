# GOV § 89520

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 9.5. Ethics > Article: 4. Campaign Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 89520

---

## Text

The remedies provided in Chapter 11 (commencing with Section 91000) shall not apply to violations of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 84, Sec. 13.)
