# GOV § 82048.8

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82048.8

---

## Text

“Spouse” includes registered domestic partners recognized by state law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 312, Sec. 7. (AB 902) Effective January 1, 2020.)
