# GOV § 27135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 6. County Treasury Oversight Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 27135

---

## Text

The costs of complying with this article shall be county charges and may be included with those charges enumerated under Section 27013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 784, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1996.)
