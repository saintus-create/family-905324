# GOV § 53872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53872

---

## Text

The sale shall be not less than twenty-one days after publication of the notice and shall be at public auction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
