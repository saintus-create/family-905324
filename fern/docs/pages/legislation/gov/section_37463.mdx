# GOV § 37463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37463

---

## Text

The legislative body may furnish sites for such buildings, and may use any portion of a public lot, block, or park for such purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
