# GOV § 37102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37102

---

## Text

The legislative body may use any available funds to provide employment to the city’s destitute or needy unemployed residents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
