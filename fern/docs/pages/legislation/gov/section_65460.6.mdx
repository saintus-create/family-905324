# GOV § 65460.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8.5. Transit Village Development Planning Act of 1994

**Citation:** GOV § 65460.6

---

## Text

An agency responsible for the preparation and adoption of the congestion management program may exclude district impacts from the determination of conformance with level of service standards pursuant to subdivision (c) of Section 65089.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 780, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
