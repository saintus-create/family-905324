# GOV § 3616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. Mediation and Conciliation Service > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Public Transportation Labor Disputes

**Citation:** GOV § 3616

---

## Text

Except as expressly provided by subdivision (b) of Section 3612 and Section 3614, nothing in this chapter shall be construed to grant or deprive employees of a right to strike.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 11. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012.)
