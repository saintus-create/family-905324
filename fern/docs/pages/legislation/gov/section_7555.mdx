# GOV § 7555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 25. Commemorative “California Gold” Medallions

**Citation:** GOV § 7555

---

## Text

After minting, the medallions shall be returned to the producers who, notwithstanding Section 402, shall have sole responsibility for distribution, marketing, and sales.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 826, Sec. 1. Effective September 10, 1982.)
