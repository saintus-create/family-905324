# GOV § 14686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 3. Buildings and Grounds

**Citation:** GOV § 14686

---

## Text

The Department of General Services shall purchase and provide for the display of both the Flag of the United States and the Bear Flag of California in a prominent place outside of each public building of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
