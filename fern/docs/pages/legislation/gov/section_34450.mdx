# GOV § 34450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** GOV § 34450

---

## Text

Any city or city and county may enact, amend, or repeal a charter for its own government according to this article or Article 3 (commencing with Section 9255) of Chapter 3 of Division 9 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 63. Effective January 1, 1995.)
