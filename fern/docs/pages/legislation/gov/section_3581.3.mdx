# GOV § 3581.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 6.5. Supervisors

**Citation:** GOV § 3581.3

---

## Text

The scope of representation for supervisory employees shall include all matters relating to employment conditions and supervisory employee-employer relations including wages, hours, and other terms and conditions of employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 744.)
