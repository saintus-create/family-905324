# GOV § 9353.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 9353.2

---

## Text

The board shall determine who are members of this system and is the sole judge of the conditions under which persons may be admitted to and continue to receive benefits under this system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 879.)
