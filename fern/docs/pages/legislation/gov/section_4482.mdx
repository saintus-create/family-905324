# GOV § 4482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Purchases

**Citation:** GOV § 4482

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to contracts executed prior to the effective date of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1812.)
