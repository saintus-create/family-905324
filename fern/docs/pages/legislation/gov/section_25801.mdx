# GOV § 25801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 8. Health and Safety > Article: 1. Animals

**Citation:** GOV § 25801

---

## Text

The board may enter into agreements with the Director of Agriculture for the purpose of cooperating in the administration and enforcement of those provisions of law subject to the jurisdiction of the Department of Agriculture or county agricultural commissioners, county sealers of weights and measures, and county livestock inspectors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
