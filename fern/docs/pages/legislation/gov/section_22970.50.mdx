# GOV § 22970.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Eligibility

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.50

---

## Text

Any person who is an eligible employee may elect, in a manner prescribed by the board, to participate in the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
