# GOV § 1459

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 1459

---

## Text

After being recorded, the official bond of the county clerk shall be filed in the office of the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
