# GOV § 12002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 12002

---

## Text

Every law of this state relating to the powers and duties of the Governor and to acts and duties to be performed by others toward the Governor extends to the person performing for the time being the duties of Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 3. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
