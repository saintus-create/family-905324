# GOV § 16633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Savings and Loan Association Deposits > Article: 4. Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 16633

---

## Text

The contracts shall be executed by the savings and loan associations and credit unions in triplicate. The Treasurer shall file one copy of each contract with the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1035, Sec. 17.)
