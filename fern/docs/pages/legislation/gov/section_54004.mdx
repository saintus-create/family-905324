# GOV § 54004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 1. Horseback Riding

**Citation:** GOV § 54004

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to remove, deface, or destroy the markers, or to erect fences, barbed wire, or other obstructions on the bridle trails.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
