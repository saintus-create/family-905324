# GOV § 41204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. City Assessor

**Citation:** GOV § 41204

---

## Text

The assessor shall verify the list with an oath and deposit it with the city clerk on or before July 1st.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 47. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
