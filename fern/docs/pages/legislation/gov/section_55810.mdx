# GOV § 55810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Counties Transaction and Use Tax

**Citation:** GOV § 55810

---

## Text

The bonds may be sold as the authority determines by resolution, and the bonds may be sold at a price above or below par, whether by negotiated or public sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 384, Sec. 1. Effective September 9, 1991.)
