# GOV § 19683.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Prohibitions and Offenses > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19683.5

---

## Text

If a state employee is successful in an action brought pursuant to Section 19683, the complaining employee shall be reimbursed for all costs incurred pursuant to Section 995.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1212, Sec. 8.)
