# GOV § 21352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13. Retirement Benefits > Article: 4. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 21352

---

## Text

The service retirement annuity is the sum of the annuities which are the actuarial equivalents of the normal and additional accumulated contributions of a member at the time of his or her retirement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
