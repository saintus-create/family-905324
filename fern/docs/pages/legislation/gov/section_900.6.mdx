# GOV § 900.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 900.6

---

## Text

“State” means the State and any office, officer, department, division, bureau, board, commission or agency of the State claims against which are paid by warrants drawn by the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
