# GOV § 16510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16510

---

## Text

Any State officer or employee who deposits any money belonging to or in the custody of the State in any manner other than as prescribed in this chapter is subject to forfeiture of his office or employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
