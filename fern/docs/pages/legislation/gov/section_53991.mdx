# GOV § 53991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 18. Local Tax Allocation Agreements to Implement Community Facilities Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 53991

---

## Text

No state agency shall, on or after the date that a tax allocation agreement is entered into pursuant to Section 53990 alter, defer, or deny priority or allocation of state funds to a local agency or for state construction projects by virtue of such tax allocation agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 396, Sec. 2. Effective July 27, 1983.)
