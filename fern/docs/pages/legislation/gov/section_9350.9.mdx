# GOV § 9350.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 9350.9

---

## Text

“Accumulated contributions” means the sum of all contributions standing to the credit of a member’s account, and interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 879.)
