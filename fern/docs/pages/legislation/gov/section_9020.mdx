# GOV § 9020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 2. Meeting and Organization of the Legislature

**Citation:** GOV § 9020

---

## Text

The Legislature shall convene in regular session at the City of Sacramento at noon on the first Monday in December of each even-numbered year, and each house shall immediately organize.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 91, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 1996.)
