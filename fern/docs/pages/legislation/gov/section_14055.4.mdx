# GOV § 14055.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.5. Elderly and Disabled Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 14055.4

---

## Text

Prior to approving its program of projects, the commission shall hold not less than one public hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 669, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
