# GOV § 18550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18550

---

## Text

A “full-time” position or appointment is a position or appointment in which the employee is to work the amount of time required for the employee to be compensated at a full-time rate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 387, Sec. 1.)
