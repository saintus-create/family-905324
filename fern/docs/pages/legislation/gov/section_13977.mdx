# GOV § 13977

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 13977

---

## Text

Before entering upon the duties of his office the secretary shall execute an official bond to the state in the penal sum of fifty thousand dollars ($50,000) conditioned upon the faithful performance of his duties.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 138.)
