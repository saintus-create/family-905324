# GOV § 3023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 2. Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3023

---

## Text

When an officer is impeached by the Assembly for a misdemeanor in office, the articles of impeachment shall be delivered to the President of the Senate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
