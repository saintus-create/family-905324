# GOV § 26528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. District Attorney > Article: 2. Other Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 26528

---

## Text

The district attorney may, and when directed by the board of supervisors shall, bring a civil action in the name of the people of the State of California to abate a public nuisance in his county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
