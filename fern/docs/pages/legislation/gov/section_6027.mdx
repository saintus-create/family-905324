# GOV § 6027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 2. Establishing Standing as Newspaper of General Circulation

**Citation:** GOV § 6027

---

## Text

On and after July 1, 1952, a newspaper shall not be in fact or in law a newspaper of general circulation unless it obtains or has theretofore obtained a judicial decree establishing it as having such status pursuant to the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1045.)
