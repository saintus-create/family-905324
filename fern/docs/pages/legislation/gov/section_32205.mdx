# GOV § 32205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 32205

---

## Text

“Terminal salary” means the average salary earnable by any member during (a) the last three years, or (b) the last five years prior to the date of his retirement, whichever is the greater.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
