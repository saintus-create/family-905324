# GOV § 59536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 14. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 59536

---

## Text

The legislative body may appropriate funds from a general fund or from any funds available for any or all of the purposes contemplated in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
