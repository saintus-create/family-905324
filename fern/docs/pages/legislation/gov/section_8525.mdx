# GOV § 8525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. The Milton Marks Commission on California State Government Organization and Economy > Article: 2. Purposes and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8525

---

## Text

The commission may submit to the Legislature and the Governor, at such time, or times, as the commission deems necessary, interim reports of its activities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8524 (as added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1625) by Stats. 1979, Ch. 373.)
