# GOV § 23328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23328

---

## Text

If the clerk certifies a petition to be sufficient, he shall immediately transmit a copy of his certification to the board of supervisors of each affected county and to each of the chief petitioners, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
