# GOV § 1090.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4. Prohibitions Applicable to Specified Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 1090.1

---

## Text

No officer or employee of the State nor any Member of the Legislature shall accept any commission for the placement of insurance on behalf of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 812.)
