# GOV § 8879.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.49. The Highway Safety, Traffic Reduction, Air Quality, and Port Security Bond Act of 2006 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8879.33

---

## Text

The bonds may be refunded in accordance with Article 6 (commencing with Section 16780) of the State General Obligation Bond Law. Approval by the electors of this act shall constitute approval of any refunding bonds issued pursuant to the State General Obligation Bond Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 25, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 1B at the November 7, 2006, election.)
