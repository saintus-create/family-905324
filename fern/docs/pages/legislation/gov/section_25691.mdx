# GOV § 25691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 5. Water System

**Citation:** GOV § 25691

---

## Text

For purposes of this article the board may acquire water rights by purchase or condemnation and may file applications for the appropriation of unappropriated waters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1176.)
