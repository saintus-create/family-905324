# GOV § 29702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Claims > Article: 1. Filing and Approval

**Citation:** GOV § 29702

---

## Text

A claim based upon an expenditure directed to be made by any officer shall be approved by such officer before it is considered by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 29708 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1725.)
