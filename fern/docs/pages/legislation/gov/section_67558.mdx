# GOV § 67558

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67558

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause requiring the department to make all necessary repairs, renewals, and replacements to the regional transit terminal and to keep it at all times in good repair, working order, and condition.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1079.)
