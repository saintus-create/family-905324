# GOV § 67813

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 67813

---

## Text

The purpose of the authority is to plan for, finance, and manage the transition of the military base from military to civilian use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
