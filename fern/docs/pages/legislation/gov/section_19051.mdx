# GOV § 19051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19051

---

## Text

Civil service appointments shall only be made to a class that is appropriate for the duties, functions, and responsibilities that will be performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 53, Sec. 25. (SB 866) Effective June 27, 2018.)
