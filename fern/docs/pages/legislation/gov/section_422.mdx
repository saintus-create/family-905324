# GOV § 422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 422

---

## Text

The California redwood (Sequoia sempervirens, Sequoia gigantea) is the official state tree.
