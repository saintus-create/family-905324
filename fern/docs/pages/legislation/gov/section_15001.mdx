# GOV § 15001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15001

---

## Text

The department is composed of the Office of the Attorney General and those other divisions, bureaus, branches, sections, or other units as the Attorney General may create within the department pursuant to Section 15002.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 176, Sec. 53. Effective August 24, 2007.)
