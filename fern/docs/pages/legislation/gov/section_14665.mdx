# GOV § 14665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. State Property

**Citation:** GOV § 14665

---

## Text

With the consent of the state agency concerned, the director may execute grants to real property belonging to the state in the name and upon behalf of the state to the United States of America in exchange for lands of the latter or for such other considerations, as the director deems are in the best interests of the state.

Real property acquired by the state by exchange pursuant to this section is subject to the laws governing real property of the class to which it belongs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
