# GOV § 3103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Oath or Affirmation of Allegiance for Disaster Service Workers and Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 3103

---

## Text

The oath or affirmation required by this chapter is the oath or affirmation set forth in Section 3 of Article XX of the Constitution of California.
