# GOV § 27728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Hearing Officer

**Citation:** GOV § 27728

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter provide an alternative to, and do not supersede, any other provision of law providing for any matter to be heard or determined by a hearing officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 617, Sec. 9.)
