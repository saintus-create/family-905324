# GOV § 12512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 2. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12512

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall attend the Supreme Court and prosecute or defend all causes to which the state, or any state officer, is a party in the state officer’s official capacity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 91. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
