# GOV § 15570.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Appeals Conferences

**Citation:** GOV § 15570.54

---

## Text

A person requesting relief in an appeals conference may thereafter request a hearing before a tax appeals panel in the Office of Tax Appeals if the department denies the request for relief.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 252, Sec. 3. (AB 131) Effective September 16, 2017.)
