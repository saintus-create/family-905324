# GOV § 59645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59645

---

## Text

Any appropriate changes may be made in the wording of the bonds necessary to make them conform to this chapter and the proceedings taken pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
