# GOV § 26620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 1.1. Sheriff as Ex Officio Director of Emergency Services

**Citation:** GOV § 26620

---

## Text

The office of county director of emergency services shall be held ex officio by the county sheriff.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 38.)
