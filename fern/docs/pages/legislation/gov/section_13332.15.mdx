# GOV § 13332.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2.5. Provisions Applicable to Appropriations of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13332.15

---

## Text

No appropriation may be combined or used in any manner to avoid budgeting the salary or operating expenses of any position or to achieve any purpose which has been denied by any formal action of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 323, Sec. 44. Effective July 1, 1983.)
