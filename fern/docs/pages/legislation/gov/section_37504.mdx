# GOV § 37504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 5. Assembly or Convention Halls

**Citation:** GOV § 37504

---

## Text

The ordinance shall be published in a newspaper published in the city, once a day for five days if in a daily paper, or once a week for three successive weeks, if in a weekly paper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
