# GOV § 11475

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Administrative Adjudication: General Provisions > Article: 16. Administrative Adjudication Code of Ethics

## Text

The rules imposed by this article may be referred to as the Administrative Adjudication Code of Ethics.
