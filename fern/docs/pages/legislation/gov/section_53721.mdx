# GOV § 53721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 3.7. Voter Approval of Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53721

---

## Text

All taxes are either special taxes or general taxes. General taxes are taxes imposed for general governmental purposes. Special taxes are taxes imposed for specific purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 4, 1986, by initiative Proposition 62.)
