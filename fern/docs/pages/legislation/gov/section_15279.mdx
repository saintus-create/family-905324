# GOV § 15279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Public Safety Communications

**Citation:** GOV § 15279

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, unless the context requires otherwise, “director” means the Director of the Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 28, Sec. 22. (SB 71) Effective June 27, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 93 of Ch. 28.)
