# GOV § 22013.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22013.4

---

## Text

“Policeman” as used in this part also includes persons designated by Section 31470.6 as persons whose principal duties consist of “active law enforcement” for the purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 678, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 1999.)
