# GOV § 23272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23272

---

## Text

The election shall be governed and controlled by the general election laws of the state so far as applicable, except as otherwise provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
