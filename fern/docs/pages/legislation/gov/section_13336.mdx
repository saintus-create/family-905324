# GOV § 13336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 3. Revised Budgeting and Accounting System for Fiscal Affairs of State Government

**Citation:** GOV § 13336

---

## Text

The department shall, in cooperation with the Legislature, develop a format for utilization by agencies required to submit a program budget.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1286, Sec. 9.)
