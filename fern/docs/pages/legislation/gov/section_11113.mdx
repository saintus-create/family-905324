# GOV § 11113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 8. Bonds of Licensees and Permittees

**Citation:** GOV § 11113

---

## Text

The Attorney General may consult with private persons, groups, and associations as to bond forms to be approved, modified, or revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1291.)
