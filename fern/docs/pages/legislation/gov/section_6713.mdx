# GOV § 6713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6713

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim the first Sunday in October to be known as “Stepparents Day.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1471, Sec. 1. Effective October 2, 1985.)
