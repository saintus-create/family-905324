# GOV § 40204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 40204

---

## Text

When completed, the census shall be verified before any officer authorized to administer oaths, and filed with the city clerk.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
