# GOV § 7922.500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Request for a Public Record > Article: 1. General Principles

**Citation:** GOV § 7922.500

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to permit an agency to delay or obstruct the inspection or copying of public records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
