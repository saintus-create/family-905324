# GOV § 14892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 5. Compilations

**Citation:** GOV § 14892

---

## Text

The state agency requiring the compilation shall furnish the state agency compiling it such data and information as it has or may be able to obtain conveniently concerning the compilation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
