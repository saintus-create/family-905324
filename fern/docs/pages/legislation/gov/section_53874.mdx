# GOV § 53874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53874

---

## Text

The deed or agreement is prima facie evidence of regularity of all proceedings from the assessment of the property to and including the execution of the deed or agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
