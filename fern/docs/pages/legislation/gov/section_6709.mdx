# GOV § 6709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6709

---

## Text

The Governor shall proclaim January 15 to be known hereafter as Dr. Martin Luther King, Jr., Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1066.)
