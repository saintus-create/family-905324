# GOV § 6593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 4. Local Bond Pooling

**Citation:** GOV § 6593

---

## Text

No member of the governing body of the authority shall be personally liable on the bonds or be subject to any personal liability or accountability by reason of the issuance of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 868, Sec. 6. Effective September 23, 1985.)
