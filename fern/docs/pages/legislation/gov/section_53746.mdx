# GOV § 53746

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4.5. Tort Liability Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53746

---

## Text

The fund shall be used exclusively for the payments of claims, judgments, and legal and investigative costs that may arise under Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1059.)
