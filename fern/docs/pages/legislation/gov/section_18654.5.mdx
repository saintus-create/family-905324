# GOV § 18654.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. The State Personnel Board

**Citation:** GOV § 18654.5

---

## Text

The executive officer shall administer the civil service statutes and rules, subject to the right of appeal to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 15. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
