# GOV § 429.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 429.4

---

## Text

The LGBTQ Veterans Memorial at the Desert Memorial Park in Cathedral City is the official state LGBTQ veterans memorial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 172, Sec. 2. (AB 2439) Effective January 1, 2019.)
