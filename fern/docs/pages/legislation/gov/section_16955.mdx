# GOV § 16955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. The California Pension Restructuring Bond Act of 2004 > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 16955

---

## Text

This chapter, being necessary for the health, welfare, and safety of the state and its residents, shall be liberally construed to effect its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 215, Sec. 4. Effective August 11, 2004.)
