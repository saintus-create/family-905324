# GOV § 20062.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 20062.5

---

## Text

“Risk pool” means the combination of assets and liabilities with respect to one or more contracting agencies for the purpose of pooling actuarial experience and setting the employer contribution rates pursuant to Section 20840.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1133, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
