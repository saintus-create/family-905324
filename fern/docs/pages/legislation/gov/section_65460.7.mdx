# GOV § 65460.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8.5. Transit Village Development Planning Act of 1994

**Citation:** GOV § 65460.7

---

## Text

(a) A transit village plan shall be prepared, adopted, and amended in the same manner as a general plan, except for plans qualified as transit village plans pursuant to Section 65460.11.

(b) A transit village plan may be repealed in the same manner as it is required to be amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 309, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
