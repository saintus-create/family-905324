# GOV § 22951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Part: 6. STATE EMPLOYEES' DENTAL CARE ACT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Recovery of Medical Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 22951

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this part to do all of the following:

(a) Promote increased economy and efficiency in the state service.

(b) Enable the state to attract and retain qualified employees by providing dental care plans similar to those commonly provided in private industry.

(c) Recognize and protect the state’s investment in each permanent employee by promoting and preserving good health among state employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 25. Effective June 24, 2004.)
