# GOV § 9149.34

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 11. Legislative Employee Whistleblower Protection Act

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.34

---

## Text

An individual who intentionally retaliates against a legislative employee for having made a protected disclosure is subject to a fine not to exceed ten thousand dollars ($10,000) and imprisonment in a county jail for a period not to exceed one year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 2, Sec. 1. (AB 403) Effective February 5, 2018.)
