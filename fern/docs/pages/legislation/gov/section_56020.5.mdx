# GOV § 56020.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56020.5

---

## Text

“Certificate of completion” means the document prepared by the executive officer and recorded with the county recorder that confirms the final successful completion of a change of organization or reorganization.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 11. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
