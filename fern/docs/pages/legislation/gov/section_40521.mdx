# GOV § 40521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 3. Surplus Property Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 40521

---

## Text

The authority shall not transact any business or exercise any powers unless and until the legislative body shall by ordinance declare that there is need for the authority to function in the city, city and county, or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
