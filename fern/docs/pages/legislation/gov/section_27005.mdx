# GOV § 27005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27005

---

## Text

The treasurer shall disburse the county money and all other money placed in his or her custody by official authority only on county warrants, checks, or electronic fund transfers issued by the county auditor, except for the making of legal investments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 939, Sec. 8. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative January 1, 1995, by Sec. 29 of Ch. 939.)
