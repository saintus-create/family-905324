# GOV § 15857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 11. PROPERTY ACQUISITION LAW > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 15857

---

## Text

The board may agree with the owner of part or all of the property to be condemned as to the compensation to be paid, and may incorporate such agreement in a stipulation to be filed in the condemnation proceeding.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 989.)
