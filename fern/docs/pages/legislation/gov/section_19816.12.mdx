# GOV § 19816.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Department of Human Resources > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 19816.12

---

## Text

The department shall establish and maintain in suitable form an official roster of all persons holding positions under this part and enter thereupon their names, complete record of state employment, and other facts prescribed by rule.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 108. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
