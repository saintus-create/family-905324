# GOV § 23572.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 5. Establishment of Consolidated Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 23572.5

---

## Text

The maximum tax rate for the consolidated county shall be determined in accordance with Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 2201) of Part 4 of Division 1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
