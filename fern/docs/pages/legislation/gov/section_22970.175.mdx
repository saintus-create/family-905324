# GOV § 22970.175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.175

---

## Text

“Employer” means any city, county, city and county, district, school district, community college district, county superintendent of schools, or other public agency, instrumentality, or political subdivision of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 511, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2008.)
