# GOV § 8283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8283

---

## Text

(a) The commission shall select one of its members chairperson. Five members constitute a quorum of the commission.

(b) The Governor shall select one of the committee members to serve as chairperson. Three members constitute a quorum of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 6. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
