# GOV § 70376

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 6. State Court Facilities Construction Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 70376

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that funding for courthouse alteration, renovation, and construction be funded by money in the State Court Facilities Construction Fund and additional money as necessary from the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1082, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
