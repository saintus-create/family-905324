# GOV § 26452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 15. Revenue Bonds for County Incinerators > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26452

---

## Text

All bonds issued under the provisions of this chapter are negotiable instruments, except when registered in the name of a registered owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 822.)
