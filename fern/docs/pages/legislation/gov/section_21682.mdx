# GOV § 21682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 16. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** GOV § 21682

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be construed to prohibit officers and employees of this system from participating in a tax-preferred retirement savings program, on the same terms as other state employees or participants.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 639, Sec. 18. (SB 1139) Effective January 1, 2011.)
