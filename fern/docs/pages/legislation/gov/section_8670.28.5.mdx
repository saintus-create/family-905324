# GOV § 8670.28.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 5. Contingency Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.28.5

---

## Text

An operator shall maintain a level of readiness that will allow effective implementation of the applicable contingency plans.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 17. Effective September 24, 1990.)
