# GOV § 54099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4.5. Reconstruction of Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 54099

---

## Text

The board may rent all or part of a building constructed or reconstructed pursuant to this article to the local agency within which the building is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
