# GOV § 9375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 8. Survivor’s Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 9375

---

## Text

For the purposes of Section 9374, a person shall be considered to be under the age of 18 if he or she is under the age of 22 and a full-time student.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 93.4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
