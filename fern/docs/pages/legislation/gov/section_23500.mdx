# GOV § 23500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 23500

---

## Text

Two or more counties, each contiguous to the other, or to one of the others, may consolidate into one county pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
