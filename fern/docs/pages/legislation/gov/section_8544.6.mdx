# GOV § 8544.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. California State Auditor > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8544.6

---

## Text

All unreimbursed expenditures of the office are defined as “administrative costs” defined in Section 11270.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 281, Sec. 18. (SB 1395) Effective January 1, 2013.)
