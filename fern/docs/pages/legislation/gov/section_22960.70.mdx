# GOV § 22960.70

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Rights to Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.70

---

## Text

A participant has a vested right to 100 percent of the value of the participant’s account. The right accrues when the person becomes a participant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
