# GOV § 55088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 4. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 55088

---

## Text

An amended or supplemental agreement may be entered into prior to the commencement, during the construction, or after the completion of the outfall sewer and conduit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
