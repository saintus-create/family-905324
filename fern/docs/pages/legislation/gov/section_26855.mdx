# GOV § 26855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26855

---

## Text

The fee for taking acknowledgment of any deed or other instrument, including the certificate, is two dollars and twenty-five cents ($2.25) for each signature.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 327, Sec. 84. Effective June 30, 1982.)
