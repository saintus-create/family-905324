# GOV § 26820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26820

---

## Text

The county clerk shall charge and collect the fees fixed in this article for service performed by the clerk, when not otherwise provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 75, Sec. 49. Effective July 19, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 156 of Ch. 75.)
