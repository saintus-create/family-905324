# GOV § 51296.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 7. Farmland Security Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51296.6

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a school district shall not acquire any land that is within a designated farmland security zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 506, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2001.)
