# GOV § 31904.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31904.5

---

## Text

“County peace officers” also includes detectives and investigators in the office of the district attorney whose duties are substantially similar to those of peace officers as defined in subsection (a) of Section 31904.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 753.)
