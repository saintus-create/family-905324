# GOV § 3119.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8.5. Volunteers > Article: 3. Volunteers

**Citation:** GOV § 3119.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to the Department of Aging’s use of volunteers, pursuant to the provisions of Titles III (42 U.S.C. 3021 and following), V (42 U.S.C. 3041 and following), and VII (42 U.S.C. 3045 and following) of the Older Americans Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1195.)
