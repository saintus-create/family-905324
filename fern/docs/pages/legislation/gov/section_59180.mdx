# GOV § 59180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 2. Authority to Conduct Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 59180

---

## Text

If a district lies entirely within two or more cities, the legislative body of any city in which any of the district lies may conduct the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
