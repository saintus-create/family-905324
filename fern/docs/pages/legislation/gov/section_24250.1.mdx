# GOV § 24250.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Offices and Office Hours

**Citation:** GOV § 24250.1

---

## Text

Sheriffs shall also have offices in each city in which they perform court-related services and a facility of the superior court is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2004.)
