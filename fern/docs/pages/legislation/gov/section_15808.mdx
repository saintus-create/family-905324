# GOV § 15808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15808

---

## Text

When authorized by a separate act or appropriation enacted by the Legislature, the board may acquire and construct public buildings for use by state agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1686.)
