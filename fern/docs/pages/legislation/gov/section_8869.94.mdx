# GOV § 8869.94

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11.8. California Debt Limit Allocation Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8869.94

---

## Text

(a) The committee may adopt, amend, or repeal rules and regulations pursuant to this chapter as emergency regulations in accordance with the rulemaking provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2). The adoption, amendment, or repeal of these regulations is conclusively presumed to be necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health, safety, or general welfare within the meaning of Section 11346.1.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2029.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 1) and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 739, Sec. 2. (AB 346) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative January 1, 2029, by its own provisions.)
