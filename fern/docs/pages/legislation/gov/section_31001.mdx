# GOV § 31001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31001

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county not having a charter which creates the office of county counsel may employ and contract with counsel to assist the district attorney in representing and advising it and all district officers in all matters and questions of law pertaining to their duties and to civil legal questions affecting the county or districts.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1553.)
