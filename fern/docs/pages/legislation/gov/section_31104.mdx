# GOV § 31104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 2. CIVIL SERVICE > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31104

---

## Text

Any county may by ordinance adopt a limited civil service system for any or all county officers and employees, except elective officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
