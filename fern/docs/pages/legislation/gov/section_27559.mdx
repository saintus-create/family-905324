# GOV § 27559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27559

---

## Text

In the preparation of assessors’ maps and block-books the surveyor shall make all investigations and surveys necessary to provide complete and accurate maps.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
