# GOV § 18806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Classification

**Citation:** GOV § 18806

---

## Text

The classification title or a code symbol approved by the board shall be used in all communications relating to personnel and in all budget and financial records.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1141.)
