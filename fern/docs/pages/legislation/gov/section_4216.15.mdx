# GOV § 4216.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3.1. Protection of Underground Infrastructure > Article: 2. Regional Notification Center System

**Citation:** GOV § 4216.15

---

## Text

The board shall meet at least once every three months. The board shall hold meetings in Sacramento and Los Angeles, and in other locations in the state it deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 809, Sec. 14. (SB 661) Effective January 1, 2017.)
