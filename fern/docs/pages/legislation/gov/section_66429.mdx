# GOV § 66429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Maps > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66429

---

## Text

Of the maps required by this division, only final and parcel maps may be filed for record in the office of the county recorder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
