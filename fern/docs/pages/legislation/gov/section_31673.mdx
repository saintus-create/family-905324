# GOV § 31673

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 31673

---

## Text

Upon retirement for service a member is entitled to receive a retirement allowance which shall consist of:

(a) His service retirement annuity.

(b) His current service pension.

(c) His prior service pension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
