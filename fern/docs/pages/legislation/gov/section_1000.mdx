# GOV § 1000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Classification of Officers

## Text

Executive officers are either:

(a) Civil.

(b) Military.
