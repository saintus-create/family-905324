# GOV § 23578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 5. Establishment of Consolidated Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 23578

---

## Text

On or after the date the consolidated county is established, the superior court in the affected counties shall retain jurisdiction in all cases pending in a session of such court immediately prior to consolidation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
