# GOV § 29853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 3. Duplicate Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 29853

---

## Text

A warrant is considered lost if it has been mailed and has not been received by the addressee within 20 days after the date of mailing or within a shorter period of time as the board of supervisors may by ordinance establish.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 939, Sec. 11. Effective September 28, 1994. Operative January 1, 1995, by Sec. 29 of Ch. 939.)
