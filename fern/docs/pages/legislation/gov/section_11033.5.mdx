# GOV § 11033.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 3. Traveling Expenses

**Citation:** GOV § 11033.5

---

## Text

Sections 11032 and 11033 shall not apply to any member of the Commission on Uniform State Laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1335, Sec. 4.)
