# GOV § 26742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26742

## Text

The fee for executing and delivering any other instrument shall be the amount described in Section 26720.9.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 629, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2001.)
