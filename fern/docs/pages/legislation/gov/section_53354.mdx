# GOV § 53354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53354

---

## Text

If the area designated in the resolution adopted pursuant to Section 53351 does not include the entire community facilities district, a separate ballot shall be prepared for the vote upon the proposition to authorize bonds and to levy a special tax for payment of the bonds and only the voters entitled thereto shall be given the ballots.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 670, Sec. 101. Effective January 1, 2008.)
