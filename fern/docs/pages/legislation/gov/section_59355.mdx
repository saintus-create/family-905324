# GOV § 59355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 8. Notice of Assessment Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 59355

---

## Text

The notice shall be posted for not less than 10 days on or near the door of the chamber in which the legislative body conducts its regular public meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
