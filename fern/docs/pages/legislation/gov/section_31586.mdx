# GOV § 31586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 31586

---

## Text

All payments of the county or of any district into the retirement fund, whether made pursuant to this article or made pursuant to law, are obligations of the county or district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
