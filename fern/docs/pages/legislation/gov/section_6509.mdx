# GOV § 6509

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6509

---

## Text

Such power is subject to the restrictions upon the manner of exercising the power of one of the contracting parties, which party shall be designated by the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 84.)
