# GOV § 12001.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 12001.7

---

## Text

The person appointed by the Governor pursuant to Section 12001.5 shall furnish the Secretary of the Senate and the Chief Clerk of the Assembly an annual report of that person’s activities related to the appointed position.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 2. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
