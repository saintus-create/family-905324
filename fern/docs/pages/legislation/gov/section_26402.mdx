# GOV § 26402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 15. Revenue Bonds for County Incinerators > Article: 1. Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 26402

---

## Text

Incinerators may be acquired, constructed, maintained and operated either individually or in groups. Each individual incinerator or group of incinerators shall be designated as a “project” and shall be so referred to in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 822.)
