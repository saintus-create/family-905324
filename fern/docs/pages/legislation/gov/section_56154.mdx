# GOV § 56154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Notice

**Citation:** GOV § 56154

---

## Text

If the published notice is a notice of a hearing, publication of the notice shall be commenced at least 21 days prior to the date specified in the notice for the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 50. Effective January 1, 2001.)
