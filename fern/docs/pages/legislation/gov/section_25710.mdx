# GOV § 25710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 7. Water Resources Management Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 25710

---

## Text

The Legislature finds that local circumstances in the County of San Mateo require the preparation of a comprehensive water resources management plan for such county; that no general law exists which provides procedures for the preparation and adoption of such a plan; and that the procedures prescribed by this article are necessary and desirable in order to enable the preparation of such a plan for the County of San Mateo.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 178.)
