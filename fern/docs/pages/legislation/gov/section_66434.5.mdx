# GOV § 66434.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Maps > Article: 2. Final Maps

**Citation:** GOV § 66434.5

---

## Text

When a soils report, geologic report, or soils and geologic report has been prepared specifically for the subdivision, each report shall be kept on file for public inspection by the city or county having jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 87, Sec. 9. Effective March 1, 1982.)
