# GOV § 55051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 3. Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 55051

---

## Text

The legislative body of the local agency constructing the conduits under permission shall advertise inviting sealed proposals for furnishing the labor and materials for the proposed work before any contract is let.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
