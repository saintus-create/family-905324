# GOV § 56156

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Notice

**Citation:** GOV § 56156

---

## Text

If the mailed notice is notice of a hearing, the notice shall be mailed at least 21 days prior to the date specified in the notice for hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 51. Effective January 1, 2001.)
