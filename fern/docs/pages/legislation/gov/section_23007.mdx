# GOV § 23007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 23007

---

## Text

Except as specified in this chapter, a county shall not, in any manner, give or loan its credit to or in aid of any person or corporation that is not a public bank, as defined in Section 57600. An indebtedness or liability incurred contrary to this chapter is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 442, Sec. 10. (AB 857) Effective January 1, 2020.)
