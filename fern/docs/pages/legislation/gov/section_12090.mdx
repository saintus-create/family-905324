# GOV § 12090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 8. Department of Community Services and Development

**Citation:** GOV § 12090

---

## Text

The Department of Community Services and Development shall have possession and control of all records, papers, offices, equipment, supplies, land, and other property real or personal held for the benefit or use of the Department of Economic Opportunity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 187, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1996.)
