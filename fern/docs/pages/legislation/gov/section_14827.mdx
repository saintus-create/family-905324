# GOV § 14827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. California State Contracts Register

**Citation:** GOV § 14827

---

## Text

State agencies shall develop procedures for assuring that proposed contract opportunities are published in the California State Contracts Register as required by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 823, Sec. 3.5.)
