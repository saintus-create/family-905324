# GOV § 10245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 10245

---

## Text

The Legislative Counsel may cooperate with any educational institution of the State in any manner approved by the Legislative Counsel and such institution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
