# GOV § 51902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 1. Findings and Declarations of Policy

**Citation:** GOV § 51902

---

## Text

It is further found and declared that in order to strengthen and make more efficient and responsive the overall administrative process of local government, administrative and executive authority in Sacramento County should be clearly defined and responsibility established under a single general purpose government, subject to approval of the electorate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 972.)
