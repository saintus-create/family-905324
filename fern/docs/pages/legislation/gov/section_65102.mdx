# GOV § 65102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 1. Local Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 65102

---

## Text

A legislative body may establish for its planning agency any rules, procedures, or standards which do not conflict with state or federal laws.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 690, Sec. 2.)
