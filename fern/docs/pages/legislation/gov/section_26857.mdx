# GOV § 26857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26857

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged by the clerk for service rendered to any municipality or county in the state, or to the state or national government, nor for any service relating thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 75, Sec. 92. Effective July 19, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 156 of Ch. 75.)
