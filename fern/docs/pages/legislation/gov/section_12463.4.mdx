# GOV § 12463.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 4. Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 12463.4

---

## Text

On or before July 1, 2019, the Controller shall publish on the Controller’s Internet Web site a comprehensive list of special districts. The Controller shall update the list every year thereafter. For purposes of this section, the term “special district” means an “independent district” or “independent special district” as those terms are defined in Section 56044.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 334, Sec. 1. (SB 448) Effective January 1, 2018.)
