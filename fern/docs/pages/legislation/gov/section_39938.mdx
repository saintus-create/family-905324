# GOV § 39938

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 15. Rivers, Streams, and Harbors > Article: 2. Establishment of Harbor Lines by Cities Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 39938

---

## Text

Ordinances of a city owning tidelands fronting on a navigable water situated within the city boundaries which were adopted prior to August 10, 1913, and which fix harbor lines upon or adjacent to such tidelands are confirmed and validated unless they extend beyond harbor lines established by the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
