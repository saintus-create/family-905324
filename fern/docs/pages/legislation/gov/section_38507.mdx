# GOV § 38507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 3. Municipal Park Abandonment Law of 1939

**Citation:** GOV § 38507

---

## Text

The legislative body may employ any legal or other assistants that it deems necessary to sell the land for the best advantage of the city, and may fix and pay their compensation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 182.)
