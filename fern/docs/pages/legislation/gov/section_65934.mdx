# GOV § 65934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65934

---

## Text

“State agency” means any agency, board, or commission of state government. For all purposes of this chapter, the term “state agency” shall include an air pollution control district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1200.)
