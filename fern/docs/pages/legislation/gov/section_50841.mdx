# GOV § 50841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 2. Organization, Powers, and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 50841

---

## Text

The board shall issue warrants to persons entitled for the amount of money ordered paid them from the fund by the board. The warrant shall be signed by the president and secretary and shall state the purpose of the payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
