# GOV § 8810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 2. California Public Broadcasting Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8810

---

## Text

The California Public Broadcasting Commission is hereby created in state government as an independent commission in order to encourage the growth and development of public broadcasting services to the people of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
