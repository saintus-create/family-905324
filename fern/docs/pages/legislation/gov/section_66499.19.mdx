# GOV § 66499.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 6. Reversions and Exclusions > Article: 1. Reversion to Acreage

**Citation:** GOV § 66499.19

---

## Text

When a reversion is effective, all fees and deposits shall be returned to the current owner of the property and all improvement security released, except those retained pursuant to Section 66499.17.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 506, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2001.)
