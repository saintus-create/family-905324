# GOV § 58033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 2. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** GOV § 58033

---

## Text

Before publication of the petition, a petitioner may withdraw his signature by filing with the clerk a signed statement of his intention to withdraw his signature from the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
