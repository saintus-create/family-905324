# GOV § 34406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Special Charter Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34406

---

## Text

The abstract shall show the whole number of electors voting at the election, the number of votes cast for reorganization, and the number of votes cast against reorganization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
