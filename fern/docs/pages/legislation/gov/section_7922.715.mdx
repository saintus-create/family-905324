# GOV § 7922.715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Agency Regulations, Guidelines, Systems, and Similar Matters > Article: 3. Catalog of Enterprise Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 7922.715

---

## Text

(a) The catalog of enterprise systems required by Section 7922.710 shall be made publicly available upon request in the office of the person or officer designated by the agency’s legislative body.

(b) If the agency has an internet website, the catalog shall be posted in a prominent location on the agency’s internet website.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
