# GOV § 12324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Treasurer > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12324

---

## Text

The Treasurer shall pay warrants drawn by the Controller, except warrants canceled pursuant to law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1655.)
