# GOV § 51306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of City Functions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 51306

---

## Text

The contract shall provide for the assumption of all city pension rights of the transferred employees by the county, or for their continuation by the city, or by both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
