# GOV § 425.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.12

---

## Text

The pallid bat (Antrozous pallidus) is the official state bat.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 502, Sec. 2. (SB 732) Effective January 1, 2024.)
