# GOV § 12601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 8. Environmental Actions

**Citation:** GOV § 12601

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are not exclusive, and the remedies provided for in this article shall be in addition to any other remedies provided for in any other law or available under common law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1518.)
