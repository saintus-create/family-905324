# GOV § 9361.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 7. Optional Settlements

**Citation:** GOV § 9361.5

---

## Text

Optional Settlement 4 consists of such other benefits as are the actuarial equivalent of his retirement allowance, that he may select subject to the approval of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 879.)
