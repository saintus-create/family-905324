# GOV § 17245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 3. Reimbursement and Refunding Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17245

---

## Text

Registered reimbursement warrants shall be sold by the Controller at public sale to the best bidders or in negotiated sales on the terms and conditions the Controller shall approve, but at no less than face value of the registered reimbursement warrants offered.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 23, Sec. 8. Effective July 28, 2009.)
