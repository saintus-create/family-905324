# GOV § 22005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22005

---

## Text

“Federal agency” means the Department of Health and Human Services or the Secretary of Health and Human Services, or the predecessor or successor in function to that department or officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 609, Sec. 32.)
