# GOV § 56065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56065

---

## Text

“Principal act” means, in the case of a district, the law under which the district was formed and, in the case of a city, the general laws or the city charter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 49. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
