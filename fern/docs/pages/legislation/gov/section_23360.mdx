# GOV § 23360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 4. Election on Creation of the Proposed County

**Citation:** GOV § 23360

---

## Text

The clerk shall also prepare and mail to the qualified electors of each affected county a sample ballot. The sample ballot shall be mailed with and at the same time as the ballot pamphlet.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
