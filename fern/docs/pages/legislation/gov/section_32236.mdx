# GOV § 32236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 2. Retirement System

**Citation:** GOV § 32236

---

## Text

Upon election by any qualified person to come within this chapter, he shall be excluded from any other retirement system established under the laws of this State or any city or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
