# GOV § 55089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 4. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 55089

---

## Text

All contracts for construction pursuant to this article shall be made and entered into by one local agency designated by the legislative bodies of all the local agencies in the manner provided in Article 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
