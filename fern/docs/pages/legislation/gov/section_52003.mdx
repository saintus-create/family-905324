# GOV § 52003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

“Authority” means a community energy authority created pursuant to this chapter.
