# GOV § 66906.9

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 4. Personnel

**Citation:** GOV § 66906.9

---

## Text

The Tahoe Conservancy Fund is hereby created. Any moneys in the fund shall be available for the purposes of this title when appropriated by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 16. Effective September 17, 1984.)
