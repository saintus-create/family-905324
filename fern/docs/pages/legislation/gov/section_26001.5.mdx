# GOV § 26001.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Transportation and Airports > Article: 1. Transportation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 26001.5

---

## Text

The board may deposit funds that it receives from its grant of a franchise to use the public roads in the general fund of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 108, Sec. 1. Effective May 27, 1986.)
