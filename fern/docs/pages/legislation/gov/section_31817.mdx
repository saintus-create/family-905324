# GOV § 31817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 13. Integration With Old Age and Survivors Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31817

---

## Text

This article shall not be applicable to any member subject to Section 31751 except with respect to the member’s service prior to the date such section was made applicable to the member.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 58.)
