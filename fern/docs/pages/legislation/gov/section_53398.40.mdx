# GOV § 53398.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.9. Infrastructure Financing Districts in the Border Development Zone > Article: 4. Tax Increment Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53398.40

---

## Text

The legislative body may, by majority vote, initiate proceedings to issue bonds pursuant to this chapter by adopting a resolution stating its intent to issue the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 773, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
