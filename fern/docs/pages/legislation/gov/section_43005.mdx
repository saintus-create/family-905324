# GOV § 43005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43005

---

## Text

In the case of a sale of property for taxes or special assessments, all proceeds shall be accounted for and distributed as provided in Article 12 (commencing with Section 53925) of Chapter 4 of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 648.)
