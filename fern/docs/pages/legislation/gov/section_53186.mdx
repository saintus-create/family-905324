# GOV § 53186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 3. Purposes and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 53186

---

## Text

The powers of an integrated financing district shall be in addition to those of each financing act, and may be used instead of, in addition to, or in conjunction with, the powers contained in the financing act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
