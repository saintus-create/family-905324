# GOV § 183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Sovereignty of the State > Article: 4. Rights Over Property

**Citation:** GOV § 183

---

## Text

The State may acquire property by taxation in the modes authorized by law.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
