# GOV § 69841

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 7. Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 69841

---

## Text

The clerk of the superior court shall attend each session of the superior court in the county and upon the judges of the court in chambers when required.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 53. Effective January 1, 2004.)
