# GOV § 11110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 8. Bonds of Licensees and Permittees

**Citation:** GOV § 11110

---

## Text

The form of all bonds of licensees, permittees, and all persons other than public officers and employees, furnishing bonds to a State department, office, board, commission or bureau in pursuance of State law shall be on a form which form has been approved as to conformity with applicable law by the Attorney General.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1291.)
