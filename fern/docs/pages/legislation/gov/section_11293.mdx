# GOV § 11293

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 3. Special Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 11293

---

## Text

All money received by the Department of General Services pursuant to paragraph (b) of Section 11290 shall be deposited in the Service Revolving Fund.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 992.)
