# GOV § 15835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 15835

---

## Text

The bond or interim receipt is a negotiable instrument unless there is an express recital on its face that the bond or interim receipt in nonnegotiable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1686.)
