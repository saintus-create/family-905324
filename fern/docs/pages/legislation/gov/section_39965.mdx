# GOV § 39965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 15. Rivers, Streams, and Harbors > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 39965

---

## Text

In the discretion of the legislative bodies of the contracting parties, the ferry may be operated either as a toll or free ferry.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
