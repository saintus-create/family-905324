# GOV § 40533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40533

---

## Text

The commission shall appoint as clerk of the authority, the clerk of the legislative body or other competent person.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
