# GOV § 9125.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 5.6. State Office Building Act of 2018

**Citation:** GOV § 9125.6

---

## Text

It is the intent of the legislative and executive branches to expedite and not unreasonably delay in taking actions needed to advance the expeditious development and construction of the projects authorized by Section 9125 and to collaborate in good faith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 40, Sec. 6. (AB 1826) Effective June 27, 2018.)
