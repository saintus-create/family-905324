# GOV § 9173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees of the Legislature > Article: 2. Permanent Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 9173

---

## Text

Any officer or employee appointed or elected by the Senate or Assembly may at any time be removed in the same manner as is provided for his election or appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
