# GOV § 8669.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 21. California Firefighter Peer Support and Crisis Referral Services Act

**Citation:** GOV § 8669.05

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Firefighter Peer Support and Crisis Referral Services Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 388, Sec. 2. (AB 1116) Effective January 1, 2020.)
