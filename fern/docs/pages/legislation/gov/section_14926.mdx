# GOV § 14926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8.5. Guaranteed Return Trip Demonstration Project

**Citation:** GOV § 14926

---

## Text

The Department of General Services shall establish and administer the Guaranteed Return Trip Demonstration Project in accordance with this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1653, Sec. 3.)
