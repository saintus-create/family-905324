# GOV § 69942

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 9. Official Reporters Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 69942

---

## Text

No person shall be appointed to the position of official reporter of any court unless the person has first obtained a license to practice as a certified shorthand reporter from the Court Reporters Board of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 328. Effective January 1, 2003.)
