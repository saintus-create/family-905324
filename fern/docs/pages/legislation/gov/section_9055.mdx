# GOV § 9055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 3. Crimes Against the Legislative Power

**Citation:** GOV § 9055

---

## Text

Every member of the Legislature convicted of any crime defined in this article, in addition to the punishment prescribed, forfeits his office and is forever disqualified from holding any office in the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
