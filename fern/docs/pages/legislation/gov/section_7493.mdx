# GOV § 7493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 20. Governmental Access to Financial Records > Article: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 7493

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to preclude a state or local agency from obtaining a credit report or consumer credit report from anyone other than a financial institution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1320.)
