# GOV § 4000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Cost Records to be Kept

**Citation:** GOV § 4000

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to maintenance work, work occasioned by emergency, and work costing less than fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000).

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 623.)
