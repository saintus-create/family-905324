# GOV § 26398

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Revenue Bonds for County Improvements > Article: 8. Definition and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 26398

---

## Text

This chapter shall be liberally construed to carry out its objects and purposes and to finance the construction of needed public improvements through the medium of revenue bonds and to protect the holders of such bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 815.)
