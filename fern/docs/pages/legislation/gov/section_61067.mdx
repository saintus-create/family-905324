# GOV § 61067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 61067

---

## Text

A board of directors may provide for any program for the benefit of its employees and members of the board of directors pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 53200) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
