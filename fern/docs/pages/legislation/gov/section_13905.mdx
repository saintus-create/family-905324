# GOV § 13905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13905

---

## Text

The board shall have a seal, bearing the following inscription: “California Victim Compensation Board.” The seal shall be fixed to all writs and authentications of copies of records and to other instruments that the board directs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 105. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
