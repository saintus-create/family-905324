# GOV § 17071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4. Cancellation

**Citation:** GOV § 17071

---

## Text

The Controller shall keep a record of all canceled warrants.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 158, Sec. 1. (SB 1475) Effective January 1, 2017.)
