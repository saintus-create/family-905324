# GOV § 15770.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Public Works Board

**Citation:** GOV § 15770.3

---

## Text

When the Secretary of the Natural Resources Agency is serving as a member of the board, in the case of a vote of the board that results in a tie, the chairperson of the board shall cast the deciding vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 242, Sec. 2. (AB 3133) Effective January 1, 2019.)
