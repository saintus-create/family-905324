# GOV § 58094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58094

---

## Text

If the notice is materially defective, the supervising authority shall reset the hearing and cause new notice to be given.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
