# GOV § 26311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Revenue Bonds for County Improvements > Article: 1. Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 26311

---

## Text

The board may insure against loss of revenue from any cause whatever and the proceeds of such insurance shall be used solely for the payment of the bonds and interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 815.)
