# GOV § 1500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 3. Form and Conditions

**Citation:** GOV § 1500

---

## Text

All official bonds shall be made payable to the State of California in such penalty and with such conditions as required by this article, or the law creating or regulating the duties of the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
