# GOV § 31866

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 16. Alternate Survivors’ Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31866

---

## Text

The board shall fix the additional contributions of members at amounts which it finds will pay one-half of the cost of the survivors’ benefits and lump sum supplemental survivorship benefit provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1591.)
