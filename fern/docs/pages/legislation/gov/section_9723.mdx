# GOV § 9723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 2. Daily Journals

**Citation:** GOV § 9723

---

## Text

One copy of the daily Journal of each house, upon its approval by the house, shall be authenticated as so approved by the chief clerk or secretary of the house.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1976, Ch. 316.)
