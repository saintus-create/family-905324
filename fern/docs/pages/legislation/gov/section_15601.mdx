# GOV § 15601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15601

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the term “secretary” means “executive director.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 7. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017.)
