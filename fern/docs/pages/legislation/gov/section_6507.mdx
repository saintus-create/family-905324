# GOV § 6507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6507

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, the agency is a public entity separate from the parties to the agreement.
