# GOV § 14622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 14622

---

## Text

The director may make available to elected state officials, without charge, state parking facilities for the parking of the privately owned cars of such officials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 13011 by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
