# GOV § 12229

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12229

---

## Text

The Secretary of State may maintain an item in an active file in the Secretary’s office for such time as the Secretary of State deems proper before transferring it to the archives.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 56. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
