# GOV § 68202

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1.5. Compensation of Justices and Judges of Courts of Record

**Citation:** GOV § 68202

---

## Text

Effective January 1, 1985, the annual salary of each of the following judges is the amount indicated opposite the name of the office:

Judge of the superior court, seventy-two thousand seven hundred sixty-three dollars ($72,763).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 213. Effective January 1, 2003.)
