# GOV § 15990.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 14. Tribal Housing Grant Program Fund Advisory Committee > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Implementation

**Citation:** GOV § 15990.3

---

## Text

This part shall only take effect upon sufficient funds appropriated by the Legislature to implement this part through funding made available to the Tribal Housing Grant Program Trust Fund established pursuant to Section 50651.1 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 266, Sec. 1. (AB 1878) Effective January 1, 2025.)
