# GOV § 93304

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.5. CENTRAL CALIFORNIA RAILROAD AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 93304

---

## Text

The authority is a local agency for purposes of the Disaster Assistance Act (Chapter 7.5 (commencing with Section 8680) of Division 1 of Title 2).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 234, Sec. 1. (SB 325) Effective January 1, 2012.)
