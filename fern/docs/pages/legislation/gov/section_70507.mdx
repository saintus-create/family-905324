# GOV § 70507

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 9. Conveyance: Court Facility Property: County of San Diego

**Citation:** GOV § 70507

---

## Text

The demolition project shall be deemed to be a project that is separate and distinct from the development project. The demolition project and development project serve different purposes, have independent utility, and can be implemented independently.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 17, Sec. 23. (AB 103) Effective June 27, 2017.)
