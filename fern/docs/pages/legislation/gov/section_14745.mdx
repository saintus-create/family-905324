# GOV § 14745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. State Records > Article: 2. Administration of State Records

**Citation:** GOV § 14745

---

## Text

The director shall establish and administer in the executive branch of state government a records storage program that will apply efficient and economical records storage methods to the utilization, maintenance, retention, preservation, and disposal of state records.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 32. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
