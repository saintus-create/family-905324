# GOV § 50064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.1. Habitat Maintenance Assessment Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 50064

---

## Text

The clerk of the local agency shall cause notice of the filing of the report prepared pursuant to Section 50061.5, and of a time, date, and place of hearing pursuant to Section 50063.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 262, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
