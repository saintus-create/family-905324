# GOV § 11894

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. The Financial Information System for California (FISCal) > Article: 5. Department of FISCal

**Citation:** GOV § 11894

---

## Text

The Director of FISCal shall be appointed by, and serve at the pleasure of, the Governor, subject to Senate confirmation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 569, Sec. 29. (AB 156) Effective September 27, 2022.)
