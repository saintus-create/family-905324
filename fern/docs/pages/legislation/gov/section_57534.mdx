# GOV § 57534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 9. Mergers and the Establishment of Subsidiary Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 57534

---

## Text

On and after the effective date of an order establishing a district as a subsidiary district of a city, the city council shall be designated, and empowered to act, ex officio as the board of directors of the district. The district shall continue in existence with all of the powers, rights, duties, obligations, and functions provided for by the principal act, except for any provisions relating to the selection or removal of the members of the board of directors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 162, Sec. 72. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2013.)
