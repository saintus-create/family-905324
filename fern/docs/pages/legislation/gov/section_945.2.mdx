# GOV § 945.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Actions Against Public Entities

**Citation:** GOV § 945.2

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the rules of practice in civil actions apply to actions brought against public entities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
