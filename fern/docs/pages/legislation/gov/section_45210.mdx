# GOV § 45210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Service > Article: 6. Prohibitions and Offenses

**Citation:** GOV § 45210

---

## Text

Every person who violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 597.)
