# GOV § 15506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Part: 8. LOCAL AGENCY ALLOCATION LAW > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 15506

---

## Text

This part shall not be construed as affecting or changing any authority or practice existing on May 24, 1949 whereby any state officer or agency is allocating or apportioning money to local agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 170.)
