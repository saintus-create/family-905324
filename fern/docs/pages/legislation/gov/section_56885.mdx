# GOV § 56885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Commission Decision > Article: 2. Terms and Conditions

**Citation:** GOV § 56885

---

## Text

The commission may, at any time, authorize any legislative body holding a hearing pursuant to this division, to continue the hearing to a date or dates extending beyond the dates specified in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 211. Effective January 1, 2001.)
