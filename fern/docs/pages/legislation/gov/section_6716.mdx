# GOV § 6716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6716

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim December 7 as “Pearl Harbor Day.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6714 (as added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 443) by Stats. 1990, Ch. 216, Sec. 33.)
