# GOV § 27279.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 3. Documents to be Recorded

**Citation:** GOV § 27279.3

---

## Text

(a) A requester and addressee for delivery of a recorded image may record a digitized image of a recordable instrument if the requester meets the conditions set forth in paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 27279.1.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 5) and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 463, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999. Section operative January 1, 2000, by its own provisions.)
