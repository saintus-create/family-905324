# GOV § 12742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Community Services Block Grant Program > Article: 4. State Plan and Legislative Hearings

**Citation:** GOV § 12742

---

## Text

The current state plan may be amended by the department at any time during the program year, provided that any proposed amendments, together with the reasons therefor, are distributed to all eligible entities and state legislators for a 30-day comment period commencing at least 45 days prior to their planned date of submission to the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 46, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2008. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 12790.)
