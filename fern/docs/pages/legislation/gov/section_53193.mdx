# GOV § 53193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 4. Reimbursement Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 53193

---

## Text

The reimbursement agreement may provide for the terms and conditions under which the investor may enforce the convenants and duties imposed by the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
