# GOV § 1237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 1237

---

## Text

All state and local agencies with an aggregate spending of fifty million dollars ($50,000,000) or more annually shall consider establishing an ongoing audit function.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 452, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2007.)
