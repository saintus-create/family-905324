# GOV § 12098.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 5. Small Business Advocate

**Citation:** GOV § 12098.1

---

## Text

The following definitions apply to this article, unless otherwise indicated:

(a) “Advocate” means the Small Business Advocate who is also the Director of the Office of Small Business Advocate.

(b) “Director” means the Director of the Office of Small Business Advocate.

(c) “Office” means the Office of Small Business Advocate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 475, Sec. 2. (AB 29) Effective January 1, 2012.)
