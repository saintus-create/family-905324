# GOV § 17615.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 5. State Mandate Apportionments System

**Citation:** GOV § 17615.9

---

## Text

The commission shall periodically review programs funded under the State Mandate Apportionments System to evaluate the effectiveness or continued statewide need for each such mandate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1534, Sec. 4. Effective October 2, 1985.)
