# GOV § 31802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 13. Integration With Old Age and Survivors Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31802

## Text

“Federal agency” means the Secretary of Health, Education and Welfare, or the predecessor or successor in functions to such officer.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 663.)
