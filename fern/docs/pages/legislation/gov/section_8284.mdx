# GOV § 8284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8284

---

## Text

The commission may appoint an executive director and fix the director’s compensation, in accordance with law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 7. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
