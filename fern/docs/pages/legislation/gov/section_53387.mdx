# GOV § 53387

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 5. Senior Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53387

---

## Text

The legislative body may, by majority vote, initiate proceedings to issue bonds pursuant to this chapter by adopting a resolution stating its intent to issue those bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
