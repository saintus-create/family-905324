# GOV § 13971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Indemnification of Citizens Benefiting the Public

**Citation:** GOV § 13971

---

## Text

As used in this article, “private citizen” means any person other than a peace officer, fireman, lifeguard, or person whose employment includes the duty to protect the public safety acting within the course and scope of such employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 569, Sec. 12. (AB 1140) Effective January 1, 2016.)
