# GOV § 62531

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 62531

---

## Text

The authority board shall provide for annual financial reports. The authority board shall make copies of the annual financial reports available to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2025.)
