# GOV § 9721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 2. Daily Journals

**Citation:** GOV § 9721

---

## Text

A sufficient number of copies, with proper repaging, as determined by the Secretary and Chief Clerk, shall also be bound at the end of the session of the Legislature as the Journals of the Senate and Assembly.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
