# GOV § 65303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 5. Authority for and Scope of General Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 65303

---

## Text

The general plan may include any other elements or address any other subjects which, in the judgment of the legislative body, relate to the physical development of the county or city.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1009, Sec. 9.)
