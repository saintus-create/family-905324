# GOV § 25716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 7. Water Resources Management Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 25716

---

## Text

The comprehensive water resources management plan shall comply with the provisions of any federal, state or regional plans which apply to water supply and distribution and to the treatment and disposal of waste water and which are required by statute or intergovernmental agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 178.)
