# GOV § 66540.30

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.30

---

## Text

The authority may contract with any department or agency of the United States, with any state or local governmental agency, or with any person upon those terms and conditions that the authority finds are in its best interests.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
