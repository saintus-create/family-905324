# GOV § 54030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 2. Automobile Parking Areas

**Citation:** GOV § 54030

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Local agency” means city, county, or subdivision of the State.

(b) “Property” means real or personal property, easement, license, or other right in property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
