# GOV § 19056.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19056.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision in this part, if the appointment is to be made from a general reemployment list, the names of the three persons with the highest standing on the list shall be certified to the appointing power.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1, Sec. 8. Effective January 16, 2002.)
