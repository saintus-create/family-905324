# GOV § 12955.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Discrimination Prohibited > Article: 2. Housing Discrimination

**Citation:** GOV § 12955.3

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “disability” includes, but is not limited to, any physical or mental disability as defined in Section 12926.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1049, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2001.)
