# GOV § 53833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 7. Temporary Borrowing

**Citation:** GOV § 53833

---

## Text

The legislative body of each local agency may include in its budget, separately stated, amounts of anticipated disbursement to meet the interest to be paid on any funds borrowed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
