# GOV § 65460.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8.5. Transit Village Development Planning Act of 1994

**Citation:** GOV § 65460.10

---

## Text

A city, county, or city and county may require a developer to enter into a development agreement pursuant to Article 2.5 (commencing with Section 65864) of Chapter 4 to implement a density bonus specified in the transit village plan pursuant to subdivision (g) of Section 65460.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 780, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
