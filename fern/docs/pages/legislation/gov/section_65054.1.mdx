# GOV § 65054.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 5.7. Diverse, Ethnic, and Community Media

**Citation:** GOV § 65054.1

---

## Text

The office shall assist state agencies and departments in marketing, advertising, and outreach to priority populations identified in paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 65054.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 352, Sec. 2. (AB 1511) Effective January 1, 2025. Inoperative July 1, 2028. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 65054.3.)
