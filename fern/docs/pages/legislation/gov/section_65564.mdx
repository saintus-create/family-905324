# GOV § 65564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 10.5. Open-Space Lands

**Citation:** GOV § 65564

---

## Text

Every local open-space plan shall contain an action program consisting of specific programs which the legislative body intends to pursue in implementing its open-space plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1590.)
