# GOV § 23519

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23519

---

## Text

In certifying the sufficiency of a petition, the clerk shall compare the name of each person signing the petition with the registration records of the county in which the person signing the petition resides.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
