# GOV § 51119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 2. Timberland Production Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51119

---

## Text

Any action of the board or council undertaken to zone a parcel as timberland production pursuant to Section 51112 or 51113 is exempt from the requirements of Section 21151 of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 15.)
