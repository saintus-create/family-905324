# GOV § 43430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Street Work Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 43430

---

## Text

The legislative body may reimburse the revolving fund, in whole or in part, by appropriating to, and depositing in, the fund money from any of the funds enumerated in Section 43422.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
