# GOV § 66540.20

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.20

---

## Text

The board is the legislative body of the authority and, consistent with the provisions of this title, shall determine all questions of authority policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
