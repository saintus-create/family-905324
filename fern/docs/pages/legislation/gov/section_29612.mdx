# GOV § 29612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Charges > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29612

---

## Text

The expenses necessarily incurred in searching for and rescuing persons who are lost or in danger of their lives are county charges.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1390.)
