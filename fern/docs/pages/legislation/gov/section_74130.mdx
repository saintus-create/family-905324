# GOV § 74130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 10. County-Specific Provisions > Article: 20. Riverside County

**Citation:** GOV § 74130

---

## Text

This article applies to the municipal courts established in Riverside County.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 443. Effective January 1, 2003. Repealed as of January 1, 2074, pursuant to Section 74146.)
