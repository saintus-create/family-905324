# GOV § 16493.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 6. Local Agency Emergency Loan Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16493.5

---

## Text

There is hereby created in the State Treasury a Local Agency Emergency Loan Fund. Short-term loans shall be available to local agencies for the purpose of meeting operating costs at the end of the 1977–78 fiscal year and during the 1978–79 fiscal year. Such funds shall not be used for capital outlay projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 292.)
