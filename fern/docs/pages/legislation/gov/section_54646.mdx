# GOV § 54646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 10. Remedies of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 54646

---

## Text

A waiver of any default or breach of duty or contract by any bondholder or his trustee does not affect any subsequent default or breach of duty or contract nor impair any rights or remedies on the subsequent default or breach.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
