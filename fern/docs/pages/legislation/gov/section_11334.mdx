# GOV § 11334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 5. Sale or Exchange of Products

**Citation:** GOV § 11334

---

## Text

All money received by a state agency for the sale of products shall be accounted for and reported at the end of each month to the Controller and at the same time remitted to the Treasurer and credited to the support appropriation current at the time the proceeds were received by the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 239.)
