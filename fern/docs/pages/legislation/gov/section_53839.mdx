# GOV § 53839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 7.4. Securitized Limited Obligations Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 53839

---

## Text

A special district shall not issue any securitized limited obligation notes after December 31, 2024, unless a later enacted statute that is enacted before December 31, 2024, deletes or extends that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 213, Sec. 1. (AB 2107) Effective January 1, 2021.)
