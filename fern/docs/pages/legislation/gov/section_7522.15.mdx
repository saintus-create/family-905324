# GOV § 7522.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 21. Public Pension and Retirement Plans > Article: 4. California Public Employees’ Pension Reform Act of 2013

**Citation:** GOV § 7522.15

---

## Text

Except as provided in subdivisions (d) and (e) of Section 7522.02, each public employer and each public retirement system that offers a defined benefit plan shall offer only the defined benefit formulas established pursuant to Sections 7522.20 and 7522.25 to new members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 296, Sec. 15. (AB 340) Effective January 1, 2013.)
