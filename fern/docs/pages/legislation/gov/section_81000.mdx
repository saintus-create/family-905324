# GOV § 81000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 81000

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the “Political Reform Act of 1974.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
