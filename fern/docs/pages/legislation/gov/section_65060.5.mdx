# GOV § 65060.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65060.5

---

## Text

“Official census,” as used in this chapter, means any decennial or special federal census, or an official estimate of the State Department of Finance.
