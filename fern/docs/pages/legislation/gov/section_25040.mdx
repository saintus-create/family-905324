# GOV § 25040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 3. Members

**Citation:** GOV § 25040

---

## Text

Each member of the board of supervisors shall be elected by the district which he represents, and not at large, except in any county in which supervisorial districts have not been established by law or ordinance, and in which supervisors were on May 17, 1907, required to be elected at large, but from particular wards, the members shall be elected at large and without regard to residence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
