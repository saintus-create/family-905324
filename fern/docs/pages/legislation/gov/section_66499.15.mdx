# GOV § 66499.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 6. Reversions and Exclusions > Article: 1. Reversion to Acreage

**Citation:** GOV § 66499.15

---

## Text

A public hearing shall be held on the proposed reversion to acreage. Notice thereof shall be given in the time and manner provided in Section 66451.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
