# GOV § 54408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54408

---

## Text

Pending the preparation of the definitive bonds, interim receipts or temporary bonds, exchangeable for definitive bonds may be issued in the form and with the provisions the legislative body determines.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
