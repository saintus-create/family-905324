# GOV § 37615.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 7. Hospitals Managed by Board of Hospital Trustees

**Citation:** GOV § 37615.8

---

## Text

The board of trustees may, by resolution, change the name of the municipal hospital. The change in the name of the municipal hospital shall be effective upon the filing of a verified copy of the resolution with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 72, Sec. 23. Effective May 28, 1992.)
