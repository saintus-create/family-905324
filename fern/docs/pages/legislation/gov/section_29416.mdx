# GOV § 29416

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 5.5. District Attorney’s Family or Child Support Trust Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29416

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the budget of each county shall indicate the amount appropriated for the local child support agency for child and spousal support enforcement. All such moneys shall be available for expenditure for capital and operational expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 107. Effective September 28, 2000.)
