# GOV § 3028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 2. Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3028

---

## Text

When the defendant appears, he may object in writing to the sufficiency of the articles of impeachment, or he may answer the articles by an oral plea of not guilty.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
