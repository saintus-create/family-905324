# GOV § 14110.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14110.3

---

## Text

The department shall achieve, at a minimum, an overall rate of 25 percent certified small business participation in state-funded contracts and procurements. For purposes of this section, “small business” has the same meaning as set forth in Section 14837.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 95, Sec. 6. (SB 103) Effective July 21, 2017.)
