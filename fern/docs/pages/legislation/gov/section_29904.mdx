# GOV § 29904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29904

---

## Text

Several separate propositions may be submitted at the same election, and any single proposition may include one or more purposes.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 141.)
