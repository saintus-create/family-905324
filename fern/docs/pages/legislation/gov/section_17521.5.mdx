# GOV § 17521.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 17521.5

---

## Text

“Legislatively determined mandate” means the provisions of a statute or executive order that the Legislature, pursuant to Article 1.5, has declared by statute to be a mandate for which reimbursement is required by Section 6 of Article XIIIB of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 329, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
