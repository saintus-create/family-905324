# GOV § 425.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.15

---

## Text

The Dungeness crab (Metacarcinus magister) is the official state crustacean.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 667, Sec. 2. (AB 1797) Effective January 1, 2025.)
