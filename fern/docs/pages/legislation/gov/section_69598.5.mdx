# GOV § 69598.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 3. Number of Judges

**Citation:** GOV § 69598.5

---

## Text

In the County of San Luis Obispo there are 11 judges of the superior court.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 268. Effective January 1, 2003.)
