# GOV § 99017

**Hierarchy:** Title: 17. CALIFORNIA FISCAL RECOVERY FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 99017

---

## Text

This title, being necessary for the health, welfare, and safety of the people of the state, shall be liberally construed to effect its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 13, Sec. 2. Effective October 28, 2003.)
