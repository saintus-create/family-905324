# GOV § 68079

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68079

---

## Text

A court for which the necessary seal has not been provided, or the judges of that court, shall provide it. The expense shall be an item of court operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2004.)
