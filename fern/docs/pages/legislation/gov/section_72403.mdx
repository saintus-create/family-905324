# GOV § 72403

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 8. Superior Courts > Article: 9. Traffic Referees

**Citation:** GOV § 72403

---

## Text

The traffic referee shall have the power of a deputy clerk of the court, and shall perform such other duties as may be assigned by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 390. Effective January 1, 2003.)
