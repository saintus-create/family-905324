# GOV § 6714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6714

---

## Text

The Governor annually shall proclaim April 21st to be “John Muir Day.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 833, Sec. 2.)
