# GOV § 14772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. State Forms Management

**Citation:** GOV § 14772

---

## Text

Each state agency shall appoint a forms management representative and provide necessary assistance to implement the State Forms Management Program within the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 398.)
