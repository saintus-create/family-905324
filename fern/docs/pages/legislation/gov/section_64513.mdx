# GOV § 64513

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE > Chapter: 2. The Bay Area Housing Finance Authority and Governing Board

**Citation:** GOV § 64513

---

## Text

(a) The authority board shall hold its first meeting at a time and place within the San Francisco Bay area fixed by the chair of the authority board.

(b) After the first meeting described in subdivision (a), the authority board shall hold meetings at times and places determined by the authority board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 598, Sec. 1. (AB 1487) Effective January 1, 2020.)
