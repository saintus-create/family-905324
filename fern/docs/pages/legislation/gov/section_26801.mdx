# GOV § 26801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 1. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 26801

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, the county clerk shall act as clerk of the board of supervisors in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 263, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2008.)
