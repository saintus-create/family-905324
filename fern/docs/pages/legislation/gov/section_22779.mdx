# GOV § 22779

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22779

---

## Text

“Out-of-state employee” means an employee permanently assigned to perform his or her duties outside of the state. An employee is permanently assigned out-of-state if the assignment is intended to exceed four months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
