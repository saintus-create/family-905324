# GOV § 12012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12012

---

## Text

The Governor is the sole official organ of communication between the government of this State and the government of any other State or of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
