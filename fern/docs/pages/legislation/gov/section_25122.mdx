# GOV § 25122

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 7. Ordinances

**Citation:** GOV § 25122

---

## Text

On the passage of all ordinances the votes of the several members of the board shall be entered on the minutes, and all ordinances shall be entered at length in the ordinance book.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
