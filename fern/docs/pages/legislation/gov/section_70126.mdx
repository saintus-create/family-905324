# GOV § 70126

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 12.5. Official Reporters in Counties With a Population of 95,000 and Under 120,000

**Citation:** GOV § 70126

---

## Text

A judge of the superior court may appoint a pro tempore official reporter, to serve as the convenience of the court may require, when an official reporter is unavailable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1409.)
