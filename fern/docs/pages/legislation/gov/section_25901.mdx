# GOV § 25901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 9. Agriculture and Fairs > Article: 1. Fairs

**Citation:** GOV § 25901

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may erect and maintain permanent county fair buildings within the county on lands owned by the county or any municipal corporation or body politic.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
