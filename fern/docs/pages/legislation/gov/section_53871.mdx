# GOV § 53871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53871

---

## Text

The notice shall be published at least once in a newspaper of general circulation published in the county where is located any part of the property which, or the lien on which, is to be sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
