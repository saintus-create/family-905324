# GOV § 27010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27010

---

## Text

The treasurer may receive any money constituting gift, bequest, or devise, and pay it out in accordance with the terms thereof, or, if none are fixed, according to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
