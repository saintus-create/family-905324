# GOV § 19996.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations from Service > Article: 1.7. Reduced Worktime for Partial Service Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 19996.30

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the “Partial Service Retirement Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1258, Sec. 1.5. Effective September 30, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 16 of Ch. 1258.)
