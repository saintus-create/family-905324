# GOV § 17309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Part: 5. STATE OF CALIFORNIA NOTES > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 6. Warrant Payment Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 17309

---

## Text

Whenever the Treasurer deems that it will increase the salability of the notes to obtain, prior to or after sale, a legal opinion as to the validity of the notes from attorneys other than the Attorney General, the Treasurer may obtain a legal opinion elsewhere.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 10, Sec. 5. Effective February 17, 1983.)
