# GOV § 25549.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 10. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** GOV § 25549.3

---

## Text

The term of any lease or agreement entered into by a county or city and county pursuant to this article shall not exceed 66 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 341.)
