# GOV § 64522

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE > Chapter: 3. Powers of the Bay Area Housing Finance Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 64522

---

## Text

The executive board and the authority shall not do either of the following:

(a) Regulate or enforce local land use decisions.

(b) Acquire property by eminent domain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 598, Sec. 1. (AB 1487) Effective January 1, 2020.)
