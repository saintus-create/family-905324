# GOV § 25534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 8. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25534

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may at the session, if it deems such action to be for the best public interest, reject any and all bids, either written or oral, and withdraw the property from sale or lease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1800.)
