# GOV § 53237.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 2.4.5. Sexual Harassment Prevention Training and Education

**Citation:** GOV § 53237.5

---

## Text

The requirements of this article are in addition to any other law requiring similar or related training, and nothing in this article shall be construed to supersede the requirements of Section 12940 or 12950.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 816, Sec. 1. (AB 1661) Effective January 1, 2017.)
