# GOV § 36803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 36803

---

## Text

The mayor may make or second any motion and present and discuss any matter as a member of the council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
