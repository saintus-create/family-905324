# GOV § 8880.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 2. California State Lottery Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.18

---

## Text

Compensation and Expenses

Commissioners shall be compensated at the rate of one hundred dollars ($100) for each day they are engaged in commission business. Commission members shall be reimbursed for actual expenses incurred on commission business, including necessary travel expenses as determined by the Department of Human Resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 44. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
