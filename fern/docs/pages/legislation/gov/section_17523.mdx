# GOV § 17523

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 17523

---

## Text

“Deflator” means the Implicit Price Deflator for the Costs of Goods and Services to Governmental Agencies, as determined by the Department of Finance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1534, Sec. 2. Effective October 2, 1985.)
