# GOV § 41610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 6. Chief of Police

**Citation:** GOV § 41610

---

## Text

At least once a month, the chief of police shall file with the city clerk a detailed statement of all money handled in an official capacity during the preceding month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 54. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
