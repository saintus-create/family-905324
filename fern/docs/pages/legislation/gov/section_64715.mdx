# GOV § 64715

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 2. The Los Angeles County Affordable Housing Solutions Agency and Governing Board

**Citation:** GOV § 64715

---

## Text

(a) The board shall hold its first meeting at a time and place within Los Angeles County fixed by the Los Angeles County Clerk, as a ministerial duty.

(b) After the first meeting described in subdivision (a), the board shall hold meetings at times and places determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
