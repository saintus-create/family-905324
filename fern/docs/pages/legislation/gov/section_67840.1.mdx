# GOV § 67840.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 67840.1

---

## Text

After the board has adopted a reuse plan, each county or city with territory occupied by the base shall submit to the board its general plan or amended general plan, which shall be required to satisfy both of the following:

(a) The plan is submitted pursuant to a resolution adopted by the county or city, after a noticed public hearing, that certified that the portion of the general plan or amended general plan applicable to the territory of the base is intended to be carried out in a manner fully in conformity with this title.

(b) The plan contains, in accordance with guidelines established by the board, materials sufficient for a thorough and complete review.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
