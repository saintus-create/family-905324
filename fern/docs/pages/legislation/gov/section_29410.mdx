# GOV § 29410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 5.5. District Attorney’s Family or Child Support Trust Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29410

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of a county may establish a local child support agency’s family or child support trust fund pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 102. Effective September 28, 2000.)
