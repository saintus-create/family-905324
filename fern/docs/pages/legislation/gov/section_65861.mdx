# GOV § 65861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Zoning Regulations > Article: 2. Adoption of Regulations

**Citation:** GOV § 65861

---

## Text

When there is no planning commission, the legislative body of the city or county shall do all things required or authorized by this chapter of the planning commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 686, Sec. 5. Effective October 10, 1995. Operative January 1, 1996, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 686.)
