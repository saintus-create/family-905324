# GOV § 59535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 14. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 59535

---

## Text

The legislative body may pay all or part of the costs and expenses of any proceedings from any fund over which it has jurisdiction and control and which is legally applicable to such payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
