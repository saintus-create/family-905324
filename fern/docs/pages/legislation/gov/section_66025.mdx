# GOV § 66025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 9. Protests, Legal Actions, and Audits

**Citation:** GOV § 66025

## Text

“Local agency,” as used in this chapter, means a local agency as defined in Section 66000.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1572, Sec. 22.)
