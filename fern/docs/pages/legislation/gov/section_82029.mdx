# GOV § 82029

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82029

---

## Text

“Immediate family” means the spouse and dependent children.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1000. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
