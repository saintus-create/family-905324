# GOV § 36515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36515

---

## Text

The compensation of a city council member appointed or elected to fill a vacancy is the same as that payable to the member whose office was vacated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 13. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
