# GOV § 926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Presentation of Claims to State Controller

**Citation:** GOV § 926

---

## Text

If he or she disapproves a claim, the Controller shall file it and a statement of his or her disapproval and his or her reasons with the department as prescribed in the rules and regulations of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 45. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
