# GOV § 58903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Notice of Formation or Change in Boundaries of Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 58903

---

## Text

Upon the filing or other submission of any proposal, the supervising authority shall give notice thereof as required by this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 846.)
