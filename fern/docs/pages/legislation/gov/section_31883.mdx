# GOV § 31883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 17. Integration with Federal Old Age, Survivors, and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** GOV § 31883

---

## Text

“Federal-state agreement” means the agreement or any modification thereof now, heretofore, or hereafter executed by the board pursuant to Section 218 of Title II of the Social Security Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2090.)
