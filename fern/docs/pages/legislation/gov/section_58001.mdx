# GOV § 58001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 58001

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this chapter to make available a procedure for the organization, operation and government of districts.
