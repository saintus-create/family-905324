# GOV § 66540.66

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 5. Employee Benefits and Retirement System

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.66

---

## Text

Contributions shall be in amounts that shall accumulate at retirement a fund sufficient to carry out the promise to pay benefits to the individual on account of his or her service as a member of the system, without further contributions from any source.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
