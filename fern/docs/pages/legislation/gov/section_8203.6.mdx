# GOV § 8203.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Notaries Public > Article: 1. Notaries Public

**Citation:** GOV § 8203.6

---

## Text

No fees shall be collected by such notaries public for service rendered within the reservation in the capacity of a notary public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1360.)
