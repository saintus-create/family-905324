# GOV § 53167.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 12. Public Broadband Services

**Citation:** GOV § 53167.2

---

## Text

Except for reasonable network management, a local agency insofar as it is engaged in the provision of broadband Internet access service shall not block lawful content, applications, services, or nonharmful devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 963, Sec. 2. (AB 1999) Effective January 1, 2019.)
