# GOV § 53395.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.8. Infrastructure Financing Districts > Article: 2. Preparation and Adoption of Infrastructure Financing Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 53395.16

---

## Text

The designated official shall consult with each affected taxing entity, and, at the request of any affected taxing entity, shall meet with representatives of an affected taxing entity. Any affected taxing entity may suggest revisions to the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1575, Sec. 1.)
