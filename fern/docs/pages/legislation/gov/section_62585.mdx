# GOV § 62585

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 3. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 62585

---

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to this article are fully negotiable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2025.)
