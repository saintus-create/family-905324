# GOV § 65930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65930

---

## Text

“Local agency” means any public agency other than a state agency. For purposes of this chapter, a redevelopment agency is a local agency and is not a state agency.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1113.)
