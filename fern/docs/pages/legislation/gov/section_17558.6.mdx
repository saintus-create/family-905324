# GOV § 17558.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Commission Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17558.6

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the Commission on State Mandates review its process by which local agencies may appeal the reduction of reimbursement claims on the basis that the reduction is incorrect in order to provide for a more expeditious and less costly process.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 681, Sec. 3. Effective September 22, 1998.)
