# GOV § 8574.34

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 3.9. Regional Railroad Accident Preparedness and Immediate Response

**Citation:** GOV § 8574.34

---

## Text

Every person who operates a railroad that transports hazardous materials by rail car shall register with the board pursuant to Section 55021 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 25, Sec. 6. (SB 84) Effective June 24, 2015.)
