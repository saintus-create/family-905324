# GOV § 811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL

**Citation:** GOV § 811

---

## Text

“Law” includes not only enactments but also the decisional law applicable within this State as determined and declared from time to time by the courts of this State and of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
