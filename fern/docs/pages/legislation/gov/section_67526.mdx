# GOV § 67526

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 2. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 67526

---

## Text

All members of the authority or their duly designated alternates shall serve without pay, but they may receive their necessary actual expenses incurred in the discharge of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1373.)
