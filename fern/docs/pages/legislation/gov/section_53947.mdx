# GOV § 53947

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 14. Pleasure Riding Tax

**Citation:** GOV § 53947

---

## Text

The taxing agency shall provide to the owner of such animal a record of payment, on durable material, for each animal for which a tax is paid under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1572.)
