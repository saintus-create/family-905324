# GOV § 15570.82

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Inspections and Examinations

**Citation:** GOV § 15570.82

---

## Text

The director may examine the books, accounts, and papers of all persons required to report to him or her, or having knowledge of the affairs of those required to report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 5. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Operative July 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 15570.100.)
