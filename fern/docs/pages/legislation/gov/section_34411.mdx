# GOV § 34411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Special Charter Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34411

---

## Text

Any city organized pursuant to this chapter is deemed for all purposes to be the identical corporation that existed before the reorganization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
