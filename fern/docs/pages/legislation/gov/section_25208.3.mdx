# GOV § 25208.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 25208.3

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of a county may contract for optometric service with an optometrist licensed pursuant to the provisions of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 90.)
