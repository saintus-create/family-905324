# GOV § 22794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 3. The Board of Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 22794

---

## Text

The board shall have all powers reasonably necessary to carry out the authority and responsibilities expressly granted or imposed upon it under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
