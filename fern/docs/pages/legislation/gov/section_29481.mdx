# GOV § 29481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 8. Proceeds of United States Forest Reserve Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29481

---

## Text

The payment made to each county from the receipts of any given forest reserve shall be in the proportion which the area of the forest reserve situated in the county bears to the total area of the reserve in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 26481 by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1353.)
