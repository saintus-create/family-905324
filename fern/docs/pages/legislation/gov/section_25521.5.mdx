# GOV § 25521.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 8. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25521.5

---

## Text

The Board of Supervisors of the County of Riverside may transfer to the Riverside County Regional Park and Open Space District, without a vote of the electors of the county first being taken, any real or personal property belonging to the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1195, Sec. 9.5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
