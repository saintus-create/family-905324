# GOV § 7928.800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 15. Public Entity Spending, Finances, and Oversight > Article: 2. Requirements Specific to Online Access

**Citation:** GOV § 7928.800

---

## Text

In implementing this division, each independent special district shall maintain an internet website in accordance with Section 53087.8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
