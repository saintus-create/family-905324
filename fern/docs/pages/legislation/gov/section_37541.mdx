# GOV § 37541

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 6. Museums

**Citation:** GOV § 37541

---

## Text

By ordinance, the legislative body may establish a public museum of natural and historical objects in a city where there is none.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
