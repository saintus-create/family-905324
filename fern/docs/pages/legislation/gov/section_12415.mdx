# GOV § 12415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12415

---

## Text

The Controller may require any person, presenting an account for settlement, to be sworn before him and to answer orally or in writing as to any facts relating to it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
