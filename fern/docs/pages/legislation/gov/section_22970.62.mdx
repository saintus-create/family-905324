# GOV § 22970.62

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Participation Accounts

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.62

---

## Text

The net earnings of the applicable investment fund option available under the plan shall be allocated to the participant’s account as of each valuation date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 755, Sec. 9. (SB 277) Effective January 1, 2014.)
