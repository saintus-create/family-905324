# GOV § 18900.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Employment Lists > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 18900.5

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, “designated appointing power” means an appointing power designated by the department under Section 18930.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 28. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
