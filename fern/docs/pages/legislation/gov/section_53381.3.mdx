# GOV § 53381.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 4. Powers of a District

**Citation:** GOV § 53381.3

---

## Text

A district may not perform routine maintenance, except when ancillary to, and supportive of, a rehabilitation project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
