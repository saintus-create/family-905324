# GOV § 37607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 7. Hospitals Managed by Board of Hospital Trustees

**Citation:** GOV § 37607

---

## Text

The board shall appoint one of its members president. That member shall serve for one year and until a successor is appointed. In the president’s absence, the board shall select a president pro tempore.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 24. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
