# GOV § 4562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Child Care Facilities for State Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 4562

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the design of new state office buildings, additions, alterations, or repairs of existing state-owned office buildings, where the Public Works Board has approved, prior to the effective date of this chapter, the commencement of the working drawing phase of the new state office building.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 4542 by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 162.)
