# GOV § 27561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27561

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may provide for the sale at not less than cost of copies of maps prepared for the use of the assessor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
