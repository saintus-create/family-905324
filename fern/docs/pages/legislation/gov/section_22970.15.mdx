# GOV § 22970.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.15

---

## Text

“Early retirement age” means the age at which the participant attains age 50 or qualifies for early retirement under Part 3 (commencing with Section 20000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
