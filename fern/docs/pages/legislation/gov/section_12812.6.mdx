# GOV § 12812.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 12812.6

---

## Text

The Secretary for Environmental Protection shall coordinate greenhouse gas emission reductions and climate-change activities in state government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 4. Effective August 16, 2004.)
