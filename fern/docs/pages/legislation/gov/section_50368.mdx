# GOV § 50368

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 3. Issuance of Bonds for the Acquisition of Property

## Text

Title shall be transferred by a deed signed by the chairman of the legislative body and attested by the clerk under seal.
