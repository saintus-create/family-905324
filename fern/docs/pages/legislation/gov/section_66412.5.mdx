# GOV § 66412.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66412.5

---

## Text

When so provided by local ordinance, this division shall be inapplicable to subdivisions of four parcels or less for construction of removable commercial buildings having a floor area of less than 100 square feet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 412.)
