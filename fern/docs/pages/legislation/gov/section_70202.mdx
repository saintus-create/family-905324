# GOV § 70202

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.1. Unification of Municipal and Superior Courts > Article: 1. Unification Voting Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 70202

---

## Text

Unification of the municipal and superior courts in a county shall occur on the earlier of the date specified in the unification vote or 180 days following certification of the vote for unification.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 257. Effective September 28, 1998.)
