# GOV § 5401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Tax or Assessment Levy for Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 5401

---

## Text

“Bonds” as used in this chapter means any bonds or other evidences of indebtedness the principal and interest of which are payable or may be paid from ad valorem taxes or assessments levied by or on behalf of a public body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 333.)
