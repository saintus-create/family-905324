# GOV § 26335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Revenue Bonds for County Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26335

---

## Text

The board shall fix and determine the conditions upon which any trustee shall receive, hold or disburse any or all revenues collected for or on account of the bondholders. The board shall prescribe the duties and powers of such trustee with respect to the payment of principal and interest on the bonds, the redemption of the bonds, the registration and discharge from registration of the bonds, and the management of any sinking fund or other funds provided as security for the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 815.)
