# GOV § 38600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 1. Fire Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 38600

---

## Text

The legislative body of a city may provide fire engines and all other necessary or proper apparatus for the prevention and extinguishment of fires.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 624.)
