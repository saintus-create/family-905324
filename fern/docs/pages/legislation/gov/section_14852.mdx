# GOV § 14852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Printing > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14852

---

## Text

All printing required by the California Exposition and State Fair and district agricultural associations is exempt from Section 14850.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1110, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1997.)
