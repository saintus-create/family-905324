# GOV § 31907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31907

---

## Text

“Member” means any person included in the membership of the system who has not retired and is not receiving any pension or other benefits provided by the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
