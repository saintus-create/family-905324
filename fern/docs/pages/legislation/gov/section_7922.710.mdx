# GOV § 7922.710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Agency Regulations, Guidelines, Systems, and Similar Matters > Article: 3. Catalog of Enterprise Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 7922.710

---

## Text

(a) In implementing this division, each local agency, except a local educational agency, shall create a catalog of enterprise systems.

(b) The local agency shall complete and post the catalog as required by this article by July 1, 2016, and thereafter shall update the catalog annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
