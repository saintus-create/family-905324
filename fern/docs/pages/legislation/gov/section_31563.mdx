# GOV § 31563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 4. Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 31563

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a person ceases to be a member for any portion of his or her service as an elected public officer or as a public employee that is forfeited pursuant to Sections 7522.70, 7522.72, and 7522.74.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 741, Sec. 3. (AB 2474) Effective January 1, 2015.)
