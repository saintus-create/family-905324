# GOV § 62455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. Downtown Revitalization and Economic Recovery Financing Districts > Title: 6. DISTRICTS

**Citation:** GOV § 62455

---

## Text

After adopting the resolution pursuant to Section 62451 or 62451.5, as applicable, the governing body shall send a copy of the resolution to the district board. The district board shall designate and direct the appropriate government official to prepare a downtown revitalization financing plan pursuant to Section 62456.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 642, Sec. 6. (AB 1445) Effective January 1, 2026.)
