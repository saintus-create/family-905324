# GOV § 20192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 6. Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 20192

---

## Text

In addition to the other investments authorized by this article, the board may invest in real estate and leases thereof and improvements thereon for business or residential purposes as an investment for the production of income.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
