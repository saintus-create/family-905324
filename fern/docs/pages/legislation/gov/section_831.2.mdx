# GOV § 831.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Dangerous Conditions of Public Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 831.2

---

## Text

Neither a public entity nor a public employee is liable for an injury caused by a natural condition of any unimproved public property, including but not limited to any natural condition of any lake, stream, bay, river or beach.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
