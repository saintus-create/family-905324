# GOV § 37461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37461

---

## Text

A city may provide and maintain homes for veterans. It may also provide and maintain buildings, memorial halls, and meeting places for veterans’ patriotic, fraternal, and benevolent associations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
