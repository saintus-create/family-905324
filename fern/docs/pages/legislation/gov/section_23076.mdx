# GOV § 23076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 1. Terms Defined

**Citation:** GOV § 23076

---

## Text

The mouth of a creek, river, or slough which empties into another creek, river, or slough, is the point where the middle of the channels intersect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
