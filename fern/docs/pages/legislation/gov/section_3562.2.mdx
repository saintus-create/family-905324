# GOV § 3562.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 3562.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (r) of Section 3562, for purposes of the California State University only, “scope of representation” also means any retirement benefits available to a state member under Part 3 (commencing with Section 20000) of Title 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 793, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
