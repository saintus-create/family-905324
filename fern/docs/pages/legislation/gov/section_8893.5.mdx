# GOV § 8893.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13.4. Hazardous Building Wall Anchorage Law > Article: 2. Adequate Wall Anchorage

**Citation:** GOV § 8893.5

---

## Text

No transfer of title shall be invalidated on the basis of a failure to comply with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 859, Sec. 3.)
