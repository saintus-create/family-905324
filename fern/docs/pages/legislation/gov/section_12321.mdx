# GOV § 12321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Treasurer > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12321

---

## Text

The Treasurer shall not receive money into the Treasury unless it is certified by the Controller as provided for in Section 12414.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1245.)
