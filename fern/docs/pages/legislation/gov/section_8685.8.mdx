# GOV § 8685.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 4. Allocations to Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 8685.8

---

## Text

Under procedures to be prescribed by the director, a local agency may receive an advance of funds to initiate a project. Such advances shall be limited to not more than 90 percent of the estimated state’s share of the project, as determined pursuant to Section 8686.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 195. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
