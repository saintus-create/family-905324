# GOV § 14659.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 1.1. Government Claims Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 14659.04

---

## Text

The Office of Risk and Insurance Management, any state office designated pursuant to Section 14659.02, or their designees shall keep a full and true record of all proceedings, issue all necessary process, writs, warrants, and notices, and perform those other duties described in Section 14659.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 132. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
