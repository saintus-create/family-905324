# GOV § 26025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Transportation and Airports > Article: 2. Airports

**Citation:** GOV § 26025

---

## Text

The city shall use all aid which it receives for the activities described in the resolution and shall return to the county any portion which is not so used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
