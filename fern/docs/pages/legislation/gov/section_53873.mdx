# GOV § 53873

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53873

---

## Text

On the execution of the deed in a sale for cash or on the execution of an agreement for sale in a sale on terms, all rights to redeem the property from delinquency are terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
