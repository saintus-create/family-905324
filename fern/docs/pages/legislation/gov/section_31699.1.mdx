# GOV § 31699.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.10. Post-Employment Health Benefits: San Bernardino County Alternative Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 31699.1

## Text

This article shall apply to a board of retirement established in a county of the seventh class.

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 326, Sec. 1. (SB 11) Effective January 1, 2010.)
