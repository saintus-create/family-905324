# GOV § 43857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 6. Compromise

**Citation:** GOV § 43857

---

## Text

In any action brought upon any of the bonds described in the notice, the judgment of a court of competent jurisdiction is conclusive as to the regularity of all proceedings taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
