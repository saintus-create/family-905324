# GOV § 50302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 1. Extraterritorial Property

**Citation:** GOV § 50302

---

## Text

This article is not applicable to the sale or lease of real property by a local agency to the State or a county, city, district, or political subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
