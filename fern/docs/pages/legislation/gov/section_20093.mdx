# GOV § 20093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 1. The Board of Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 20093

---

## Text

The members of the board shall serve without compensation, but shall be reimbursed for actual and necessary expenses incurred through service on the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
