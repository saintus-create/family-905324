# GOV § 34852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 1. City Manager

**Citation:** GOV § 34852

---

## Text

The ordinance shall define the powers and duties of the city manager and may fix the city manager’s compensation or the minimum amount the city manager is to receive.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 17. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
