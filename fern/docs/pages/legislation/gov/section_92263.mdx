# GOV § 92263

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 5. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 92263

---

## Text

All expenses incurred in carrying out the provisions of the trust agreement or resolution may be treated as a part of the cost of the operation of a project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
