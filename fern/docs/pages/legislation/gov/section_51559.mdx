# GOV § 51559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51559

---

## Text

Upon the repeal of the city charter provision or ordinance the performance of city functions by county officers authorized by it shall cease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
