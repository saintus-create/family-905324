# GOV § 59533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 14. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 59533

---

## Text

If the proceedings for which contributions are made are not consummated, the legislative body may refund any contribution to the person who made it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
