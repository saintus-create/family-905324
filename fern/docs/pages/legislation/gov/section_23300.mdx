# GOV § 23300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 23300

---

## Text

New counties may be formed and created from portions of one or more existing counties solely pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
