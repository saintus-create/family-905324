# GOV § 12945.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Discrimination Prohibited > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices, Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 12945.5

---

## Text

It shall be an unlawful employment practice for an employer to require any employee to be sterilized as a condition of employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 619.)
