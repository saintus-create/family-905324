# GOV § 22970.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.19

---

## Text

“Net earnings” means the income earned, or losses incurred, after asset management fees, on the applicable investment fund options offered under the Supplemental Contributions Program, less the costs of administering the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 755, Sec. 5. (SB 277) Effective January 1, 2014.)
