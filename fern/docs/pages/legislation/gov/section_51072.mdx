# GOV § 51072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.6. Open-Space Easement Act of 1974 > Article: 1. Declaration

**Citation:** GOV § 51072

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby declares that open-space lands, if preserved and maintained, would constitute important physical, social, economic or aesthetic assets to existing or pending urban development.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1003.)
