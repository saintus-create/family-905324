# GOV § 8543.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. California State Auditor > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8543.6

---

## Text

The California State Auditor may be removed for cause at any time by concurrent resolution of the Legislature. A California State Auditor removed pursuant to this section may not be reappointed to that office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 281, Sec. 11. (SB 1395) Effective January 1, 2013.)
