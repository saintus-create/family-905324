# GOV § 56074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56074

---

## Text

“Service” means a specific governmental activity established within, and as a part of, a function of a local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 20, Sec. 1. (AB 1822) Effective January 1, 2020.)
