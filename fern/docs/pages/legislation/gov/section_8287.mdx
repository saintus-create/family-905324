# GOV § 8287

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8287

---

## Text

The Board of Trustees of the State Bar shall assist the commission and the committee in any manner the commission or committee may request within the scope of its powers or duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 9. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
