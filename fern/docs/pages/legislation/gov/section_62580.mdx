# GOV § 62580

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 3. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 62580

---

## Text

The authority board may, by majority vote, initiate proceedings to issue general obligation bonds pursuant to this chapter by adopting a resolution stating its intent to issue the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2025.)
