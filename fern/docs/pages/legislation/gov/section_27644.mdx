# GOV § 27644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 12. County Counsel

**Citation:** GOV § 27644

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall furnish the county counsel with such assistants as will enable him to perform properly the duties of his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
