# GOV § 67073

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 3. California Tahoe Regional Planning Agency > Article: 4. Tahoe Regional Interim Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 67073

---

## Text

Within 90 days after the formation of the agency and after at least one public hearing, the governing body shall review the testimony and recommendations presented at such hearing and shall adopt a regional interim plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
