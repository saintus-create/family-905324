# GOV § 854.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Medical, Hospital and Public Health Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 854.3

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “county psychiatric hospital” means the hospital, ward, or facility provided by the county pursuant to the provisions of Section 7100 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1099.)
