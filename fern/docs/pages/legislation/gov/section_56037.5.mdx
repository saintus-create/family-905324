# GOV § 56037.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56037.5

---

## Text

“Elections official” shall have the same meaning as in Section 320 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001.)
