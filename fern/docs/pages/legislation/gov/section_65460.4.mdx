# GOV § 65460.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8.5. Transit Village Development Planning Act of 1994

**Citation:** GOV § 65460.4

---

## Text

A transit village development district shall include all land within not more than one-half mile of the main entrance of a transit station designated by the legislative body of a city, county, or city and county that has jurisdiction over the station area.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 354, Sec. 3. (AB 987) Effective January 1, 2011.)
