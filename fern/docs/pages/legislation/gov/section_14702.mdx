# GOV § 14702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 4. State Burial Grounds

**Citation:** GOV § 14702

---

## Text

There may be interred in the state burial grounds in the City of Sacramento the remains of any person who:

(a) Was a state officer or a Member of the Senate or Assembly at the time of his death.

(b) At the death of any state officer or Member of the Senate or Assembly, was the spouse of the officer or member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
