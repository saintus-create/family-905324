# GOV § 32020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 6. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 32020

---

## Text

The normal rates of contribution of members shall be based on age at the nearest birthday at the time of entrance into the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
