# GOV § 17208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17208

---

## Text

Whenever such a registered warrant is canceled the Controller shall so notify the Treasurer in writing, specifying the number, date, amount, to whom drawn, fund on which drawn and date of cancellation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
