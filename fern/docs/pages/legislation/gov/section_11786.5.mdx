# GOV § 11786.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.2. Information Technology Innovation Grants Program

**Citation:** GOV § 11786.5

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to the University of California, the California State University, the State Compensation Insurance Fund, community college districts, or the judicial or legislative branches of state government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 608, Sec. 2. Effective September 24, 2000.)
