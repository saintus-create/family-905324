# GOV § 25217.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2.5. County Service Areas > Article: 8. Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 25217.3

---

## Text

A local agency formation commission shall have no power or duty to review and approve or disapprove a proposal to form a zone, a proposal to change the boundaries of a zone, or a proposal to dissolve a zone.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 158, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
