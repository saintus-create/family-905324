# GOV § 87308

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 7. Conflicts of Interest > Article: 3. Conflict of Interest Codes

**Citation:** GOV § 87308

---

## Text

Judicial review of any action of a code reviewing body under this chapter may be sought by the commission, by the agency, by an officer, employee, member or consultant of the agency, or by a resident of the jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 765. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
