# GOV § 59292

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 6. Diagram

**Citation:** GOV § 59292

---

## Text

The diagram shall show each lot in the new district and its dimensions sufficient to identify it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
