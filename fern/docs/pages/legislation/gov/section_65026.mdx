# GOV § 65026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65026

---

## Text

“Functional plan” as used in this chapter, means an intermediate- or short-range plan for the operation of a discrete function of state government.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1534.)
