# GOV § 53733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4. Capital Outlay Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53733

---

## Text

If the local agency is a city or county operating under a charter, the limitations upon the levying of taxes imposed by the charter apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
