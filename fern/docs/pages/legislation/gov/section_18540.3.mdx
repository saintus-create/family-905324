# GOV § 18540.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18540.3

---

## Text

“Recognized military service” means full-time service by a person in the armed forces during the national emergency or a state military emergency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 808.)
