# GOV § 50787

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 6. Contributions and Cooperation

**Citation:** GOV § 50787

---

## Text

The board may enter into any contract, arrangement or agreement with the United States, the State or any other municipal corporation or public agency and may cooperate with the United States, the State or any other municipal corporation or public agency for the development, maintenance, operation or use of any project authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
