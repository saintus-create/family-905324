# GOV § 15103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State’s Teletype System > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15103

---

## Text

The department shall file with the Controller a monthly report of all money received for the use of the system, and at the same time deposit all such money with the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1377.)
