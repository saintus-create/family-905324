# GOV § 22785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22785

---

## Text

“Special district” means a nonprofit, self-governed public agency located within the state, comprised solely of public employees, and performing a governmental function.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 69, Sec. 22. Effective June 24, 2004.)
