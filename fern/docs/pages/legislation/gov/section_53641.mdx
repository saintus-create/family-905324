# GOV § 53641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53641

---

## Text

When money is deposited in a depository, the treasurer or other authorized official shall take and preserve a receipt, certificate of deposit, or other evidence of the deposit as he or she requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 248, Sec. 93.)
