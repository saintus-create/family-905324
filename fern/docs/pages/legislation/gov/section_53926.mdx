# GOV § 53926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 12. Proceeds of Sale for Taxes or Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 53926

---

## Text

There shall first be distributed to the general fund of the agency conducting the sale for its costs, one hundred fifty dollars ($150) for all or any portion of each separately valued parcel of real property, and amounts to reimburse the agency for the costs of advertising the sales.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 648.)
