# GOV § 14774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. State Forms Management

**Citation:** GOV § 14774

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to any state form with provisions specifically authorized and established by statute.
