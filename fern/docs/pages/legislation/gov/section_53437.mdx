# GOV § 53437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Mutilated or Defaced Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53437

---

## Text

The duplicate bond shall be signed by the same officers and as nearly as possible shall be issued in the manner of the original.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
