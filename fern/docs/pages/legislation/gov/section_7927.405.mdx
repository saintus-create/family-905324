# GOV § 7927.405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10. Personal Information and Customer Records

**Citation:** GOV § 7927.405

---

## Text

Nothing in this division requires the disclosure of the residence or mailing address of any person in any record of the Department of Motor Vehicles except in accordance with Section 1808.21 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
