# GOV § 66540.255

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.255

## Text

The authority may accept grants, contributions, and appropriations from any public agency, private foundation, or individual.

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
