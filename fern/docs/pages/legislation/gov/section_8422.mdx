# GOV § 8422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.6. Governor’s Office of Service and Community Engagement > Article: 4. The Office of Community Partnerships and Strategic Communications

**Citation:** GOV § 8422

---

## Text

State agencies and departments collaborating with the office, upon request, shall share relevant data and statistical information that may improve the efficacy of current or future outreach efforts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 30. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024.)
