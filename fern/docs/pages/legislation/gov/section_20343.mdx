# GOV § 20343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Membership in System > Article: 4. Termination of Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 20343

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 21259, a person ceases to be a member for any portion of his or her service as an elected public officer that is forfeited pursuant to Section 1243.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 322, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2006.)
