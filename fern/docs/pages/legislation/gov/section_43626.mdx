# GOV § 43626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43626

---

## Text

If any officer whose signature or countersignature appears on bonds or coupons ceases to be an officer before delivery of the bonds, that officer’s signature is as effective as if the officer had remained in office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 58. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
