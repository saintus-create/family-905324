# GOV § 54660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 11. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54660

---

## Text

A local agency may provide for the issuance, sale, or exchange of refunding bonds to redeem or retire bonds issued by it upon the terms, at the times, and in the manner which its legislative body determines.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
