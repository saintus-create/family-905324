# GOV § 5300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Signatures

**Citation:** GOV § 5300

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “bond” means any bond and the interest coupons attached thereto issued by any public body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 1252.)
