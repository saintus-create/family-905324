# GOV § 20556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Contract Members of System > Article: 4. Alternative Supplemental Service Retirement Plans for Local Miscellaneous Members of Riverside County

**Citation:** GOV § 20556

---

## Text

The employer contribution rate of a contracting agency subject to this article shall not be reduced because of concurrent coverage of social security.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
