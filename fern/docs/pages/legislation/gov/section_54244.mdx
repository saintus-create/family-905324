# GOV § 54244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 9. Public Leasebacks

**Citation:** GOV § 54244

---

## Text

Section 54241 shall not apply to any public leaseback which is executed prior to the effective date of Section 54241.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 304.)
