# GOV § 19591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations From Service > Article: 2. Tenure of Managerial Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 19591

---

## Text

Any employee demoted pursuant to Section 19590 shall, as specified by Section 19140.5, have the right to be reinstated to his or her former civil service position.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 985, Sec. 3.)
