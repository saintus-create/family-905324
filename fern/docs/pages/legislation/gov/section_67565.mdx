# GOV § 67565

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 4. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 67565

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause limiting the power of the commission to issue additional bonds except upon such terms and conditions as are set forth in the indenture.
