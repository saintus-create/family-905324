# GOV § 40230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 2. Special Census

**Citation:** GOV § 40230

---

## Text

For the purpose of determining where county offices shall be established, a city legislative body may establish the population of the city pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2004.)
