# GOV § 95003

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. CALIFORNIA EARLY INTERVENTION SERVICES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 95003

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the State Department of Health Care Services, the State Department of Developmental Services, the State Department of Social Services, and the State Department of Education work together to provide coordinated, interagency services to high-risk and disabled infants and their families.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 945, Sec. 4) by Stats. 2012, Ch. 438, Sec. 5. (AB 1468) Effective September 22, 2012. Operation contingent as provided in Section 95004.)
