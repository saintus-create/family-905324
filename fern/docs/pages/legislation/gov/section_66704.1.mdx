# GOV § 66704.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.25. SAN FRANCISCO BAY RESTORATION AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 5. Powers and Duties of the Authority > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 66704.1

---

## Text

The authority shall not acquire or own real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 690, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009. Repealed as of January 1, 2049, pursuant to Section 66706.)
