# GOV § 25557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Parks and Recreation > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25557

---

## Text

A report showing the disposition of the aid shall be made by the legislative body of the city to the board of supervisors within one year from the time it is received.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
