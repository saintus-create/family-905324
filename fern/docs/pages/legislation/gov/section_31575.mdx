# GOV § 31575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 4.6. Sacramento Area Sewer District Pension Protection Act of 2024

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Sacramento Area Sewer District Pension Protection Act of 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 64, Sec. 1. (AB 2301) Effective July 2, 2024.)
