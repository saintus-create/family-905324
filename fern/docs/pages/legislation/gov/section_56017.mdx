# GOV § 56017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56017

---

## Text

“Annexation” means the inclusion, attachment, or addition of territory to a city or district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 6. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
