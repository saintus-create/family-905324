# GOV § 11893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. The Financial Information System for California (FISCal) > Article: 5. Department of FISCal

**Citation:** GOV § 11893

---

## Text

The administrative costs, as defined in Section 11270, of the Department of FISCal shall be allocated to and recovered from funds in a manner consistent with Section 11274.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 31, Sec. 91. (SB 836) Effective June 27, 2016.)
