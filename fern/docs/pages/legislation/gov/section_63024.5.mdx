# GOV § 63024.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 2. California Infrastructure and Economic Development Bank > Article: 1. Creation of the Bank

**Citation:** GOV § 63024.5

---

## Text

Where a state agency is authorized under state law to request that the bank issue bonds on its behalf, the agency may request, and the bank may issue, the bonds for the purpose authorized by state law and to fund any necessary reserves, capitalized interest, and costs of issuance associated with the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 518, Sec. 2. (AB 1292) Effective January 1, 2012.)
