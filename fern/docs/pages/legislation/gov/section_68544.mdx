# GOV § 68544

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 2. Assignment of Judges

**Citation:** GOV § 68544

---

## Text

When the judge files a claim, the Judicial Council shall draw warrants for the payment of extra compensation or expenses as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 909, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
