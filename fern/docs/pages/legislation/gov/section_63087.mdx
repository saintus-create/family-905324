# GOV § 63087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. THE BERGESON-PEACE INFRASTRUCTURE AND ECONOMIC DEVELOPMENT BANK ACT > Title: 6.7. INFRASTRUCTURE FINANCE > Chapter: 5. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 63087

---

## Text

Section 10295 and Sections 10335 to 10382, inclusive, of the Public Contract Code shall not apply to agreements entered into by the bank in connection with the sale of bonds or notes authorized under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 4, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1999.)
