# GOV § 16554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 3. Custody of Securities

**Citation:** GOV § 16554

---

## Text

The charges of any qualified trust company or of any federal reserve bank or any branch thereof for the handling and safekeeping of such securities are not a charge against the Treasurer but shall be paid by the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 25, Sec. 26. (SB 84) Effective June 24, 2015.)
