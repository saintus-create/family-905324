# GOV § 27132.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 6. County Treasury Oversight Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 27132.4

---

## Text

Committee meetings shall be open to the public and subject to the Ralph M. Brown Act (Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 54950) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 784, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1996.)
