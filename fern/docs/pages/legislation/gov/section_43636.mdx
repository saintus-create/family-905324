# GOV § 43636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43636

---

## Text

When the city and the United States jointly perform work on such municipal improvements, the city may turn over its portion of the cost to the United States for expenditure by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
