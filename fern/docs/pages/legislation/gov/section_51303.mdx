# GOV § 51303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Transfer of City Functions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 51303

---

## Text

The county officers and employees named in the contract shall exercise within the city all of the powers and duties conferred upon the city officers or employees named in the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
