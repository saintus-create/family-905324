# GOV § 59474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 12. Recording of Assessment

**Citation:** GOV § 59474

---

## Text

The lien shall continue until the reassessment and the interest and penalties are paid or until it is discharged of record.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
