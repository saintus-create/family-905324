# GOV § 15133

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State’s Teletype System > Article: 2. Leases of Teletype Facilities and Equipment

**Citation:** GOV § 15133

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall lease facilities and equipment to connect the State Teletype System with the sheriff’s office in the counties of:

(a) Alpine.

(b) Del Norte.

(c) Lake.

(d) Lassen.

(e) Nevada.

(f) Plumas.

(g) Sierra.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 84.)
