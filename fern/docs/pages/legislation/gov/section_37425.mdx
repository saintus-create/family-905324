# GOV § 37425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 3. Sale of Buildings and Sites

**Citation:** GOV § 37425

---

## Text

If no protests are received or the legislative body overrules the protests by a four-fifths vote of its members, it may proceed with the sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
