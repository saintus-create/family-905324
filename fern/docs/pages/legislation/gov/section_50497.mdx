# GOV § 50497

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50497

---

## Text

At the time fixed in the resolution of intention, any owner of property assessed or being assessed to purchase the property to be leased may appear before the legislative body and object to the lease. Failure to object at that time is a waiver of all objections to the proposed leasing and a bar to any subsequent action for the prevention of the leasing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
