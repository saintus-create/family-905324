# GOV § 70398.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 7.1. Superior Court Design-Build Projects

**Citation:** GOV § 70398.2

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall notify the State Public Works Board regarding the method to be used for selecting the design-build entity, prior to advertising the design-build project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 79, Sec. 30. (AB 143) Effective July 16, 2021.)
