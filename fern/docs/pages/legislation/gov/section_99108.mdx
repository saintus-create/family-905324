# GOV § 99108

**Hierarchy:** Title: 19. THE EVERY KID COUNTS (EKC) ACT

**Citation:** GOV § 99108

---

## Text

The commission may use up to 3 percent of any legislative appropriation for this part for administration of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 7, Sec. 30. (AB 108) Effective March 13, 2018.)
