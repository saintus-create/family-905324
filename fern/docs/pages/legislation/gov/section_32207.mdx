# GOV § 32207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

## Text

“Fund” means the County Foresters, Firewardens, and Firemen’s Retirement Fund.
