# GOV § 50270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 11. City Selection Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 50270

---

## Text

In any county in which two or more cities are incorporated there is hereby created a city selection committee the purpose of which shall be to appoint city representatives to boards, commissions, and agencies as required by law. The membership of each such city selection committee shall consist of the mayor of each city within the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1232.)
