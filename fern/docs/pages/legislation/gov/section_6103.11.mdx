# GOV § 6103.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 6103.11

---

## Text

Section 6103 does not apply to any fee or charge for official services required by Title 7.3 (commencing with Section 66700).

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1309.)
