# GOV § 60352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.5. California Special District Consolidation Assistance Program

**Citation:** GOV § 60352

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county may participate in the program created by this chapter by adopting an ordinance implementing the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1392, Sec. 1.)
