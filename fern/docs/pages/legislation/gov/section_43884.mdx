# GOV § 43884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 7. Registration

**Citation:** GOV § 43884

---

## Text

The city treasurer shall keep in his office a book showing at all times what bonds are registered and in whose names.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
