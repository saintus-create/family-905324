# GOV § 17518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 17518

---

## Text

“Local agency” means any city, county, special district, authority, or other political subdivision of the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
