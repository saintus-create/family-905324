# GOV § 59222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 3. Resolution of Intention

**Citation:** GOV § 59222

---

## Text

The time shall be not less than 15 nor more than 60 days after the adoption of the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
