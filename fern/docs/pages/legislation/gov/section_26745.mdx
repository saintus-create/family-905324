# GOV § 26745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26745

---

## Text

The fee for summoning a trial jury of 12 or less is two dollars ($2), and for each additional juror, ten cents ($0.10).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
