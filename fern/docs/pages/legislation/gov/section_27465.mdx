# GOV § 27465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27465

---

## Text

Within 90 days after an inquest upon a dead body the coroner shall deliver to the legal representatives of the deceased any money or other property found upon the body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 77, Sec. 1.)
