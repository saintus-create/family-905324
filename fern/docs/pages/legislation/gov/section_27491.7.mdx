# GOV § 27491.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 2. Inquests

**Citation:** GOV § 27491.7

---

## Text

The coroner, his authorized deputy, or a hearing officer shall conduct the inquest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1220.)
