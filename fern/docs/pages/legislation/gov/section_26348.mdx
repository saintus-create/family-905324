# GOV § 26348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Revenue Bonds for County Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26348

---

## Text

All bonds, and the interest or income therefrom, are exempt from all taxation in this State other than gift, inheritance and estate taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 815.)
