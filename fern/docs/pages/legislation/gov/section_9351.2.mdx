# GOV § 9351.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 9351.2

---

## Text

“Benefit” means the retirement or survivor allowance granted under this chapter or payment of accumulated contributions or lump-sum payments with respect to the death of a member.
