# GOV § 8875.95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.2. Building Earthquake Safety

**Citation:** GOV § 8875.95

---

## Text

No transfer of title shall be invalidated on the basis of a failure to comply with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 941, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
