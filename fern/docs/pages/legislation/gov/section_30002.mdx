# GOV § 30002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 4. Payment of Unauthorized Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 30002

---

## Text

If two-thirds of all the voters voting at the election vote in favor of issuing the bonds, the board shall issue bonds in the sum stated in the notice of election, and payable to the creditors named in the ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
