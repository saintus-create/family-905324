# GOV § 50669.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50669.6

---

## Text

A validating proceeding may be conducted as now, or hereafter, provided in Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
