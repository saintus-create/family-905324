# GOV § 51296.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 7. Agricultural Land > Article: 7. Farmland Security Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51296.7

---

## Text

The board shall not approve any use of land within a designated farmland security zone based on the compatible use provisions contained in subdivision (c) of Section 51238.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 506, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2001.)
