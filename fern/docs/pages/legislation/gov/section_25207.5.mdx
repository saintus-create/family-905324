# GOV § 25207.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 25207.5

---

## Text

A county may disseminate information to the public concerning the rights, duties, properties, and activities of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 605.)
