# GOV § 61048

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Board of Directors

**Citation:** GOV § 61048

---

## Text

A board of directors may appoint one or more advisory committees to advise the board of directors about the district’s finances, policies, programs, or operations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
