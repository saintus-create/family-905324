# GOV § 15771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Public Works Board

**Citation:** GOV § 15771

---

## Text

The members of the board and of the interim committee shall receive no compensation for their services under this part, but shall be reimbursed for their actual and necessary expenses incurred in connection with the performance of their duties prescribed by this part, to be paid out of the money made available to the director for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 170.)
