# GOV § 65072.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2.3. Long-Range Transportation Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 65072.5

---

## Text

Development of the California Transportation Plan shall incorporate the Clean Freight Corridor Efficiency Assessment’s findings and recommendations made pursuant to Section 14517.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 769, Sec. 4. (SB 671) Effective January 1, 2022.)
