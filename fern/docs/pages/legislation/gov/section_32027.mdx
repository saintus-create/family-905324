# GOV § 32027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 6. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 32027

---

## Text

Upon election by any person to come within this chapter any sums due him under any other system shall be paid to him as in the case of separation from the service, except as provided in Section 32028.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
