# GOV § 51506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 51506

---

## Text

The offices of city assessor, tax collector, and treasurer may be abolished by ordinance after their duties have been transferred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
