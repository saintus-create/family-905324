# GOV § 8880.66

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 7. State Lottery Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.66

---

## Text

Intergovernmental Reimbursements for Services

The Commission shall reimburse all other governmental entities for any and all services necessary to effectuate the purpose of this Chapter provided by such governmental entities to the State Lottery Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
