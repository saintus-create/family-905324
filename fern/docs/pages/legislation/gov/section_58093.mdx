# GOV § 58093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58093

---

## Text

The supervising authority is not deprived of jurisdiction to proceed with the hearing, and no action on the petition is invalid, because of clerical error in the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
