# GOV § 26645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 2. Prisoners’ Property

**Citation:** GOV § 26645

---

## Text

For failure to comply with this article, the sheriff is liable on his official bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
