# GOV § 7582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 26.5. Interagency Responsibilities for Providing Services to Children with Disabilities

**Citation:** GOV § 7582

---

## Text

Assessments and therapy treatment services provided under programs of the State Department of Health Care Services, or its designated local agencies, rendered to a child referred by a local education agency for an assessment or a disabled child or youth with an individualized education program, shall be exempt from financial eligibility standards and family repayment requirements for these services when rendered pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 28, Sec. 2. (AB 1494) Effective June 27, 2012. Note: A previous Section 7582 was repealed by Stats. 2012, Ch. 23, effective June 27, 2012.)
