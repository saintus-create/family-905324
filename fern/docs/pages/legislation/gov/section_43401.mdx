# GOV § 43401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43401

---

## Text

All moneys received from sales and use taxes shall be paid into the General Fund, or into such special fund or funds for such purpose or purposes as shall be established by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 767.)
