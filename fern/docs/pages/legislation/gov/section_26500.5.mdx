# GOV § 26500.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. District Attorney > Article: 1. Duties as Public Prosecutor

**Citation:** GOV § 26500.5

---

## Text

The district attorney may sponsor, supervise, or participate in any project or program to improve the administration of justice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 629.)
