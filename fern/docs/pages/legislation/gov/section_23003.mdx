# GOV § 23003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 23003

---

## Text

A county is a body corporate and politic, has the powers specified in this title and such others necessarily implied from those expressed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
