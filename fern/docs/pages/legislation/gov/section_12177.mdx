# GOV § 12177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12177

---

## Text

For services performed in the Secretary’s office, the Secretary of State shall charge and collect the fees fixed in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 49. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
