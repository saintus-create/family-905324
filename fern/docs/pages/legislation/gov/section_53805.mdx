# GOV § 53805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 6.5. Building Certificates

**Citation:** GOV § 53805

---

## Text

The local agency shall be responsible for the maintenance and repair of all public buildings constructed, acquired or operated by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2070.)
