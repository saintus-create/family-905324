# GOV § 75103.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 6. Judges’ Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 75103.1

---

## Text

The Legislature reserves the right to increase the rates of contribution prescribed by Sections 75101 to 75103, inclusive, in such amounts as it may find appropriate.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1363.)
