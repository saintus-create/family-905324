# GOV § 67734

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.85. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL PUBLIC TRANSIT FINANCE > Chapter: 1. Retail Transactions and Use Tax Authorization

**Citation:** GOV § 67734

---

## Text

A tax proposed pursuant to this title may only be placed on the ballot for the November 3, 2026, statewide general election and shall be submitted to the voters of the entire district in accordance with Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 67740).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 740, Sec. 4. (SB 63) Effective January 1, 2026.)
