# GOV § 3123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 9. Travel Insurance of Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 3123

---

## Text

The office of county superintendent of schools may carry a master policy for travel insurance on behalf of the school districts of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 454, Sec. 37.)
