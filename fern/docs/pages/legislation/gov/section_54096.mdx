# GOV § 54096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4.5. Reconstruction of Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 54096

---

## Text

The board may have prepared all necessary plans and specifications for buildings to be constructed or reconstructed pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
