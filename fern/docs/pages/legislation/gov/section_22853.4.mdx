# GOV § 22853.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Public Employees’ Health Benefits > Article: 6. Health Benefit Plans and Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 22853.4

---

## Text

Commencing January 1, 2024, a health benefit plan or contract shall provide coverage for vasectomies and related services consistent with the requirements under Section 1367.255 of the Health and Safety Code and Section 10123.1945 of the Insurance Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 630, Sec. 11. (SB 523) Effective January 1, 2023.)
