# GOV § 9104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 4. Legislative Offices

**Citation:** GOV § 9104

---

## Text

Appropriations for the support of the offices provided for in this article shall be included in the Budget Bill.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 1012.)
