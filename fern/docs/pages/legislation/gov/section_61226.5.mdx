# GOV § 61226.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 61226.5

---

## Text

A district may destroy a record pursuant to Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 60200) of Division 1 of Title 6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 158, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2006.)
