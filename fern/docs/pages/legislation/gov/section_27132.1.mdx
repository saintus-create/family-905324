# GOV § 27132.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Treasurer > Article: 6. County Treasury Oversight Committees

**Citation:** GOV § 27132.1

---

## Text

A member may not be employed by an entity that has (a) contributed to the campaign of a candidate for the office of local treasurer, or (b) contributed to the campaign of a candidate to be a member of a legislative body of any local agency that has deposited funds in the county treasury, in the previous three years or during the period that the employee is a member of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 156, Sec. 3. Effective July 12, 1996.)
