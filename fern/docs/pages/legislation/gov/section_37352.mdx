# GOV § 37352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 1. General

## Text

The legislative body may erect and maintain buildings for municipal purposes.
