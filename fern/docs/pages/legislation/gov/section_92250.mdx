# GOV § 92250

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 5. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 92250

---

## Text

The commission may incur indebtedness and issue securities of any kind or class, and renew them, if all indebtedness is payable solely from revenues of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
