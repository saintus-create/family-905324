# GOV § 27441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 9. Public Administrator

**Citation:** GOV § 27441

---

## Text

The public administrator shall charge and collect such fees as are allowed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
