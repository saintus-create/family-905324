# GOV § 53430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Mutilated or Defaced Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53430

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Bond” includes warrant or other evidence of indebtedness.

(b) “Local agency” means county, city, irrigation district, reclamation district, school district, or other municipal corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
