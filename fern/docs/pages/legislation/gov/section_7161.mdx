# GOV § 7161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14. State Tax Liens > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 7161

## Text

“Security” has the same meaning as defined in Section 8102 of the Commercial Code.
