# GOV § 40578

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 6. Co-operation

**Citation:** GOV § 40578

---

## Text

In any such action all findings of fact or conclusions of the commission upon all matters shall be conclusive unless the action was instituted within six months after the finding or conclusion was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
