# GOV § 8670.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 2. Duties of the Administrator

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.14

---

## Text

The administrator shall coordinate the oil spill prevention and response programs and facility, tank vessel, and nontank vessel safety standards of the state with federal programs as appropriate and to the maximum extent possible.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 35, Sec. 15. (SB 861) Effective June 20, 2014.)
