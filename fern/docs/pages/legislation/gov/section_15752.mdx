# GOV § 15752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15752

---

## Text

As used in this part, “board” means the State Public Works Board, and “director” means Director of General Services.
