# GOV § 93107

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.3. SISKIYOU RAILROAD AUTHORITY

**Citation:** GOV § 93107

---

## Text

The authority may prepare a plan for the acquisition and operation of a railroad line, at no expense to the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 132, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
