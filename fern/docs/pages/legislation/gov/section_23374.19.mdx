# GOV § 23374.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 4.5. Election to Select County Officers and Location of County Seat in the Approved County

**Citation:** GOV § 23374.19

---

## Text

All costs of an election shall first be paid by the principal county. The new county shall then reimburse the principal county for all costs of the election held pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 370.)
