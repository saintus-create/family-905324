# GOV § 20002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 20002

---

## Text

The Public Employees’ Retirement System created by Chapter 700 of the Statutes of 1931, as amended, is continued in existence under this part. This system is a unit of the Government Operations Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 304. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
