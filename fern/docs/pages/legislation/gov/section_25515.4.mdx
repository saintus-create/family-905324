# GOV § 25515.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 7.5. Development of Public Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25515.4

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to tide and submerged lands granted in trust by the Legislature to a city, county, or a city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1136, Sec. 2.)
