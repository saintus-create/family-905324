# GOV § 3075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 3. Removal Other Than by Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3075

---

## Text

In a proceeding under this article, appeal is to the court of appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 178. Effective September 28, 1998.)
