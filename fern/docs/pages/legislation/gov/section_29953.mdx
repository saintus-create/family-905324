# GOV § 29953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 3. Special Improvement Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29953

---

## Text

The interest rate on the bonds need not be the same during their entire term, but different rates may be fixed for one or more interest payments on the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
