# GOV § 55112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 5. Sewage Treatment and Disposal Plants

**Citation:** GOV § 55112

---

## Text

Any local agency may issue bonds to pay all or part of the cost of construction of or ownership in the plant or works, or of purchasing capacity in the plant or works or a right to use them, as provided in the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
