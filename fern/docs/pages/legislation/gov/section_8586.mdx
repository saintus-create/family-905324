# GOV § 8586

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 5. Office of Emergency Services

**Citation:** GOV § 8586

---

## Text

The Governor shall assign all or part of his or her powers and duties under this chapter to the Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 125. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
