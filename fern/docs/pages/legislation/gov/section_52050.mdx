# GOV § 52050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 52050

---

## Text

The members of the governing body shall, ex officio, be the commissioners of the authority except in the case of an area energy authority, as provided by Section 52031.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
