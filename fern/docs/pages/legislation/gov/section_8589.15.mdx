# GOV § 8589.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 5.5. State Assistance for Fire Equipment Act

**Citation:** GOV § 8589.15

---

## Text

The office may contract with the California Correctional Training and Rehabilitation Authority to perform any of the responsibilities or services required or authorized by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 241, Sec. 9. (SB 857) Effective January 1, 2026.)
