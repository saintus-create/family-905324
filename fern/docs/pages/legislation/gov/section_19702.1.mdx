# GOV § 19702.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Prohibitions and Offenses > Article: 2. Discrimination

**Citation:** GOV § 19702.1

---

## Text

Hiring and promotion pursuant to this part shall conform to the Federal Civil Rights Act of 1964.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 915.)
