# GOV § 22970.44

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. The Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 22970.44

---

## Text

The assets of the fund shall be valued annually, and may be valued more frequently as prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 307, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2000.)
