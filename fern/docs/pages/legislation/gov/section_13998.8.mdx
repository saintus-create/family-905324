# GOV § 13998.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Technology Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 13998.8

---

## Text

(a) The agency may obtain scientific and technological expertise as needed to provide advice and input on the program, the establishment of targeted technologies and industries, the review of grant applications, and the review of project performance.

(b) The agency may award funds over a multiyear period to a grantee without requiring the grantee to reapply, so long as the funds in multiple years are utilized for the same project originally funded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 13994.8 by Stats. 2015, Ch. 303, Sec. 204. (AB 731) Effective January 1, 2016.)
