# GOV § 83116.3

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 3. Fair Political Practices Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 83116.3

---

## Text

Whenever the commission rejects the decision of an administrative law judge made pursuant to Section 11517, the commission shall state the reasons in writing for rejecting the decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 297, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
