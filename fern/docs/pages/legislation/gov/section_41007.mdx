# GOV § 41007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. City Treasurer

**Citation:** GOV § 41007

---

## Text

The deputies shall hold office at the pleasure of the city treasurer and receive such compensation as is provided by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
