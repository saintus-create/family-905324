# GOV § 59534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 14. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 59534

---

## Text

If contributions are made exclusively for the payment of the incidental expenses, the legislative body may refund pro rata to the persons making them any balances remaining after the payment of incidental expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
