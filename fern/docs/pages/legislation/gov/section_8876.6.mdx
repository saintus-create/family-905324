# GOV § 8876.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.3. Center for Earthquake Engineering Research

**Citation:** GOV § 8876.6

---

## Text

The governance, administration, and operation of the center shall be established by agreement between the University of California and the National Science Foundation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 966, Sec. 2. Effective September 27, 1996.)
