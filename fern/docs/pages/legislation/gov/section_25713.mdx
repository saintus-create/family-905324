# GOV § 25713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 7. Water Resources Management Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 25713

## Text

The county is hereby authorized to prepare and adopt a comprehensive water resources management plan as prescribed in this article.
