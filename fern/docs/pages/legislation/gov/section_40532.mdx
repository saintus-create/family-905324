# GOV § 40532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40532

---

## Text

The presiding officer of the legislative body, or other member appointed by the commission, shall be the president of the Surplus Property Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
