# GOV § 14998.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Film Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 14998.7

---

## Text

Any funds appropriated to, or for use by, the commission for purposes of this chapter, shall be under the control of the Director of the Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development or his or her designee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 302, Sec. 8. (AB 3256) Effective January 1, 2019.)
