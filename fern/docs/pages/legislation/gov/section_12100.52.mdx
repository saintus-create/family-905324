# GOV § 12100.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 6.7. California Foreign Investment Program

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.52

---

## Text

Any public or private corporation authorized to establish, operate, and maintain a regional center whose application is granted pursuant to the terms of the Immigration and Nationality Act may establish, operate, and maintain the regional center subject to the conditions and restrictions of the EB-5 Investment Program, and any amendments thereto, and any conditions and restrictions established by the director pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6315.2 by Stats. 2017, Ch. 13, Sec. 10. (AB 1727) Effective January 1, 2018.)
