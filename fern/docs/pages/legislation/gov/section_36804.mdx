# GOV § 36804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 36804

---

## Text

If the city clerk is absent, the deputy city clerk shall act. If there is none, the mayor shall appoint one of the council members as city clerk pro tempore.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 16. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
