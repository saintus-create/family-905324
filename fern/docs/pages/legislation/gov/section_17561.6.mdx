# GOV § 17561.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 1. Commission Procedure

**Citation:** GOV § 17561.6

---

## Text

A budget act item or appropriation pursuant to this part for reimbursement of claims shall include an amount necessary to reimburse any interest due pursuant to Section 17561.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 890, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2005.)
