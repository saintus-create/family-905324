# GOV § 13400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. The State Leadership Accountability Act

**Citation:** GOV § 13400

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the State Leadership Accountability Act.
