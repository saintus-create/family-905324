# GOV § 53395.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.8. Infrastructure Financing Districts > Article: 2. Preparation and Adoption of Infrastructure Financing Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 53395.11

---

## Text

The legislative body shall direct the clerk to mail a copy of the resolution of intention to create the district to each owner of land within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1575, Sec. 1.)
