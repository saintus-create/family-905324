# GOV § 31102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 2. CIVIL SERVICE > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31102

---

## Text

It is the intent of this part to enable any county to adopt such a limited civil service system as is adaptable to its size and type.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
