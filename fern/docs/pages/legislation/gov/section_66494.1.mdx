# GOV § 66494.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Requirements > Article: 8. Taxes and Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 66494.1

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may, by resolution, authorize any county officer to perform the duties required of the clerk of the board of supervisors under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 866, Sec. 4.2.)
