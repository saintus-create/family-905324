# GOV § 24356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 8. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 24356

---

## Text

The auditor shall file in his or her office the forms required by this chapter, and may dispose of them after five years in accordance with Section 26907.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 982, Sec. 7.)
