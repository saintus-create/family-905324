# GOV § 22008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22008

---

## Text

“Eligible retirement system employee” for the purposes of any referendum with respect to any retirement system has the meaning given the term “eligible employee” in Section 218(d)(3) of the Social Security Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1441.)
