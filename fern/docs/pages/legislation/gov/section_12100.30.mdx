# GOV § 12100.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 6.6. California Employee Ownership Act

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the California Employee Ownership Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 733, Sec. 1. (SB 1407) Effective January 1, 2023.)
