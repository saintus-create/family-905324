# GOV § 16370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 2. Special Deposit Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16370

---

## Text

The Special Deposit Fund in the treasury is continued in existence. It consists of money which is paid into it in trust pursuant to law. The fund is appropriated to fulfill the purposes for which payments into it are made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 121.)
