# GOV § 39583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 13. Weed and Rubbish Abatement > Article: 2. Alternative Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 39583

---

## Text

Laws relating to the levy, collection, and enforcement of county taxes apply to such special assessment taxes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
