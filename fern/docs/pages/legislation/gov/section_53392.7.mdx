# GOV § 53392.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 6. Bond Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 53392.7

---

## Text

An action may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Part 2 of Title 10 of the Code of Civil Procedure to determine the validity of bonds, proceedings, projects, or tax allocations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
