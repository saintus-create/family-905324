# GOV § 14999.36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Uniform Film Permit

**Citation:** GOV § 14999.36

---

## Text

A city or county may adopt an ordinance or other regulation governing the issuance of permits to engage in the use of property for occasional commercial filming, on location, pursuant to Section 65302.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 687, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
