# GOV § 32234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 2. Retirement System

**Citation:** GOV § 32234

---

## Text

Except as provided in this article, all county foresters, firewardens, and firemen shall be included in and be subject to this chapter on the first day of the calendar month following the expiration of 30 days after it becomes operative in any county or on the first day of the calendar month following the expiration of 30 days after their entrance into the service, whichever is later.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
