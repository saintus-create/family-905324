# GOV § 7921.710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. ACCESS TO PUBLIC RECORDS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. General Rules Governing Disclosure > Article: 3. Disclosure to District Attorney and Related Matters

**Citation:** GOV § 7921.710

---

## Text

Disclosure of records to a district attorney under the provisions of this division shall effect no change in the status of the records under any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 614, Sec. 2. (AB 473) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 7931.000.)
