# GOV § 27289

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 3. Documents to be Recorded

**Citation:** GOV § 27289

---

## Text

Whenever a notice is required by law to be recorded for any purpose, it shall be signed by the person giving notice or his agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 713.)
