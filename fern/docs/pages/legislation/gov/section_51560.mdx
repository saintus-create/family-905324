# GOV § 51560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Transfer of City Tax Functions > Article: 2. Chartered Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 51560

---

## Text

The county officers shall perform all duties relative to the collection of city taxes, including delinquent taxes, levied prior to the repeal, the enforcement of their payment, and the sale of property for nonpayment, and redemption from sale, pursuant to the laws applicable to county taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
