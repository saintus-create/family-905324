# GOV § 50243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 9. Abandoned Excavations

**Citation:** GOV § 50243

---

## Text

Before the superintendent arrives, any property owner may abate the abandoned excavation at his own expense.
