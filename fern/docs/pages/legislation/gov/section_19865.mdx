# GOV § 19865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. Days and Hours of Work > Article: 3. Sick Leave

**Citation:** GOV § 19865

---

## Text

No state employee who is an inmate or member of any state institution and who is employed on a full or part time basis shall be entitled to receive pay for any absence attributable to sickness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
