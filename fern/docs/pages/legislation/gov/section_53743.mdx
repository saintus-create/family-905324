# GOV § 53743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4.5. Tort Liability Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53743

---

## Text

If the local agency is a city or county operating under a charter, any limitations upon the levying of taxes imposed by the charter apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1059.)
