# GOV § 53108.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 6. Local Emergency Telephone Systems

**Citation:** GOV § 53108.5

---

## Text

“Division,” as used in this article, means the Public Safety Communications Division within the Office of Emergency Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 353, Sec. 96. (SB 820) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 129 of Ch. 353.)
