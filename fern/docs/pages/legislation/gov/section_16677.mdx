# GOV § 16677

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Fiscal Agent

**Citation:** GOV § 16677

---

## Text

Any fiscal agent shall promptly notify the remitting officer of the receipt of the remittances and shall promptly cancel paid bonds and coupons and return them to the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2070.)
