# GOV § 56049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56049

---

## Text

“Landowner-voter” means any person entitled to vote in a landowner-voter district, or the legal representative of that person or, in the case of an election, the proxy of that person if authorized by the principal act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 38. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
