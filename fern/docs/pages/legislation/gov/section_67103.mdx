# GOV § 67103

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 4. Agency’s Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 67103

---

## Text

All public works projects shall be reviewed prior to construction and approved by the agency as to the project’s compliance with the adopted regional general plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
