# GOV § 51111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 2. Timberland Production Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51111

---

## Text

On or before October 1, 1976, the board or council shall adopt a list and a detailed description of additional compatible uses for parcels zoned as timberland production.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 678, Sec. 2.)
