# GOV § 95013.2

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. CALIFORNIA EARLY INTERVENTION SERVICES ACT > Chapter: 3.1. Transition to Preschool and Other Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 95013.2

---

## Text

In providing services pursuant to paragraph (2) of subdivision (d) of Section 95024, resources shall be made available by the lead agency or the agency’s contractor or both to families with information on the following areas:

(a) The difference between Part B and Part C services.

(b) Information about local Part B programs and other services in a family’s community.

(c) Options for services for families after their child reaches three years of age.

(d) An overview of the process and timelines for a child’s transition at three years of age.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2026, Ch. 80, Sec. 6. (SB 163) Effective July 13, 2026. Repealed conditionally as prescribed in Section 95003.)
