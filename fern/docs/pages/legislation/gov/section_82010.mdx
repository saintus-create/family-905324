# GOV § 82010

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82010

---

## Text

“Closing date” means the date through which any report or statement filed under this title is required to be complete.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
