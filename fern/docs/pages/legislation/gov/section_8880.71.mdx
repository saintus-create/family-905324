# GOV § 8880.71

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.71

---

## Text

Restrictions

No person shall be selected, appointed or hired to be a Commissioner, Director, deputy director, or Commission employee who has been convicted of a felony or any gambling-related offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
