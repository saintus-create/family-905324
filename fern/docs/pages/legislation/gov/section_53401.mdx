# GOV § 53401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 1. Unsold Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53401

---

## Text

This article does not apply to bonds authorized under a law permitting their sale at a price netting the purchaser more than 6 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
