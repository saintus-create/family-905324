# GOV § 59508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 13. Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 59508

---

## Text

The foreclosure action shall be brought in the name of the local agency whose legislative body levied the reassessment upon the request of any person entitled to any of the money to be derived from the reassessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
