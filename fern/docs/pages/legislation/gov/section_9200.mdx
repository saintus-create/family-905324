# GOV § 9200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees of the Legislature > Article: 4. Legislative Employee Benefits

**Citation:** GOV § 9200

---

## Text

Each employee of the Legislature may elect to receive one or more employee benefits, as prescribed by concurrent resolution heretofore or hereafter adopted, in lieu of a portion of the compensation provided to the employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1077, Sec. 2.)
