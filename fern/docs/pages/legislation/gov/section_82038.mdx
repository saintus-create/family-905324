# GOV § 82038

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82038

---

## Text

“Legislative official” means any employee or consultant of the Legislature whose duties are not solely secretarial, clerical or manual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
