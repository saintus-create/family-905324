# GOV § 8880.34

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 4. Powers and Duties of the Director

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.34

---

## Text

Salary

The Director shall receive the salary provided for by Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 11550 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2. The Director shall devote his or her entire time and attention to the duties of his or her office and shall not be engaged in any other profession or occupation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 220, Sec. 1. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
