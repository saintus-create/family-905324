# GOV § 59654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 18. Bond Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 59654

---

## Text

If Bond Plan B or C is used the reassessment and curative clauses and all other bond and assessment provisions of the Improvement Bond Act of 1915 or the Refunding Assessment Bond Act of 1935, respectively, apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
