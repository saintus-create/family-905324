# GOV § 40203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 40203

---

## Text

The persons taking the census shall enumerate all the inhabitants of the city.
