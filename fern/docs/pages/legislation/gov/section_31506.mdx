# GOV § 31506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 2. Establishment of System

**Citation:** GOV § 31506

---

## Text

All previously retired members of the superseded system shall be paid for their respective lives the full amount of the retirement payments or annuities to which they are entitled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
