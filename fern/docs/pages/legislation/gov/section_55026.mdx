# GOV § 55026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 2. Request by Legislative Body

## Text

The ordinance or resolution shall state any terms and conditions of the permission prescribed by the legislative body.
