# GOV § 67960

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.96. NORTH LAKE TAHOE TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the North Lake Tahoe Transportation Authority Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1044, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
