# GOV § 8296

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8296

---

## Text

The commission and the committee may cooperate with any bar association or other learned, professional, or scientific association, institution, or foundation in any manner suitable for the fulfillment of the purposes of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 25, Sec. 17. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2019.)
