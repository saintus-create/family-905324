# GOV § 56038.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56038.5

---

## Text

“Feasible” means capable of being accomplished in a successful manner within a reasonable period of time, taking into account economic, legal, social, and technological factors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001.)
