# GOV § 91014

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 11. Enforcement

**Citation:** GOV § 91014

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall exempt any person from applicable provisions of any other laws of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
