# GOV § 65452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 8. Specific Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 65452

---

## Text

The specific plan may address any other subjects which in the judgment of the planning agency are necessary or desirable for implementation of the general plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1009, Sec. 18.)
