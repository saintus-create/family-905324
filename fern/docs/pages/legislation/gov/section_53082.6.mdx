# GOV § 53082.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 4. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 53082.6

---

## Text

A local agency may serve as an administrator for the purposes of Section 116686 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 120, Sec. 1. (SB 200) Effective July 24, 2019.)
