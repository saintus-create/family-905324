# GOV § 5062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1.5. Registered Public Obligations

**Citation:** GOV § 5062

---

## Text

This chapter shall be construed in conjunction with the Uniform Commercial Code and the principles of contract law relative to the registration and transfer of obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 59, Sec. 1. Effective June 2, 1983.)
