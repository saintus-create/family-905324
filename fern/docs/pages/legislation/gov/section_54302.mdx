# GOV § 54302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 1. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 54302

---

## Text

The powers conferred by this chapter are in addition to, and the limitations imposed by this chapter do not affect, the powers conferred by any other law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
