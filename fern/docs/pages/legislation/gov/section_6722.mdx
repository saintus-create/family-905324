# GOV § 6722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6722

---

## Text

The Governor annually shall proclaim January 30 as Fred Korematsu Day of Civil Liberties and the Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 241, Sec. 10. (AB 1775) Effective January 1, 2011.)
