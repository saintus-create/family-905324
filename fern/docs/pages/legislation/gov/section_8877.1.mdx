# GOV § 8877.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.4. Disaster Recovery Reconstruction Act of 1986

**Citation:** GOV § 8877.1

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Disaster Recovery Reconstruction Act of 1986.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1470, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1986.)
