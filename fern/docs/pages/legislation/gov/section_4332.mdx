# GOV § 4332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Preference for Materials > Article: 2. California-Made Materials

**Citation:** GOV § 4332

---

## Text

All State, county, or city officers, all bodies, and other persons charged with advertising for supplies shall state in their advertisement that such preferences for California-made supplies will be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
