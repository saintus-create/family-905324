# GOV § 50070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3.1. Habitat Maintenance Assessment Districts

**Citation:** GOV § 50070

---

## Text

This article does not limit or prohibit the levy or collection of any other fee, charge, assessment, or tax for habitat maintenance authorized by any other provision of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1301, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
