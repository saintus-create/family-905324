# GOV § 43426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Street Work Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 43426

---

## Text

It may collect payments of principal and interest upon such bonds or warrants and deposit them in the revolving fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
