# GOV § 21053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 6. Service Credit Election and Cost Calculation

**Citation:** GOV § 21053

---

## Text

All contributions of a member under this article shall be deemed to be and shall be administered as normal contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 489, Sec. 18. Effective January 1, 2001.)
