# GOV § 38900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 11. Sewers Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 38900

---

## Text

A city legislative body may construct, establish, and maintain drains and sewers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
