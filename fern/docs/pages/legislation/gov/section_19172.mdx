# GOV § 19172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 6. Probationary Period

**Citation:** GOV § 19172

---

## Text

During the probationary period the appointing power shall evaluate the work and efficiency of a probationer in the manner and at such periods as the department rules may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 48. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
