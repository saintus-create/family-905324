# GOV § 53803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 6.5. Building Certificates

**Citation:** GOV § 53803

---

## Text

The board has no power at any time or in any manner to pledge the credit or taxing power of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2070.)
