# GOV § 31770.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 11.5. Deferred Retirement Option Program

**Citation:** GOV § 31770.8

---

## Text

The rights of a participant or his or her spouse under the program shall be subject to any applicable provisions of law or court orders relating to dissolution of marriage, division of community property, and child or spousal support.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 897, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
