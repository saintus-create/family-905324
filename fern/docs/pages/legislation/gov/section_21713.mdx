# GOV § 21713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 19. California Employers’ Pension Prefunding Trust Program

**Citation:** GOV § 21713

---

## Text

An employer authorized by the board may elect to participate in the prefunding plan established by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 665, Sec. 1. (SB 1413) Effective January 1, 2019.)
