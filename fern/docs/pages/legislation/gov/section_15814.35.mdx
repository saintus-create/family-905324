# GOV § 15814.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Energy Efficiency in Public Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 15814.35

---

## Text

The energy efficiency provisions of this chapter apply only to those public buildings in which energy costs exceed ten thousand dollars ($10,000) per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 962, Sec. 2.)
