# GOV § 50501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50501

## Text

At the time for hearing protests, evidence may be produced in such order as the legislative body may summarily direct.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
