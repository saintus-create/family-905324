# GOV § 17052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 17052

---

## Text

The indorsements authorized by this article may be accepted by the Treasurer and he may pay such warrants.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
