# GOV § 6708

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6708

---

## Text

The Governor shall proclaim September 28th to be known hereafter as “Cabrillo Day.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 892.)
