# GOV § 8212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Notaries Public > Article: 1. Notaries Public

**Citation:** GOV § 8212

---

## Text

Every person appointed a notary public shall execute an official bond in the sum of fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000). The bond shall be in the form of a bond executed by an admitted surety insurer and not a deposit in lieu of bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 79, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
