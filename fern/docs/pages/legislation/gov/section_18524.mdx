# GOV § 18524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18524

---

## Text

“Appointing authority” or “appointing power” means a person or group having authority to make appointments to positions in the state civil service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 13. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
