# GOV § 75506

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11.5. Judges’ Retirement System II > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 75506

---

## Text

The board shall, annually, send each judge a member statement which shall include information regarding accrued service credit, accrued monetary credits, retirement eligibility dates, and other pertinent information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 879, Sec. 11. Effective September 27, 1994. Operative November 9, 1994, by Sec. 16 of Ch. 879.)
