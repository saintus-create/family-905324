# GOV § 7154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14. State Tax Liens > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 7154

---

## Text

“Deposit account” has the same meaning as defined in paragraph (29) of subdivision (a) of Section 9102 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 45. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
