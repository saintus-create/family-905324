# GOV § 9106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 5. The State Capitol Building Annex

**Citation:** GOV § 9106

---

## Text

The State Capitol Building Annex is intended primarily for the use of the legislative department and, except as otherwise provided in this article, shall be devoted exclusively to such use.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1757, Sec. 3. Effective October 1, 1984.)
