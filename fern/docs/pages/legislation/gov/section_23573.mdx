# GOV § 23573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 5. Establishment of Consolidated Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 23573

---

## Text

The affected counties shall continue to provide necessary service from the date of establishing the consolidated county until service responsibilities and functions are transferred to the consolidated county according to the provisions of the resolution of the board of supervisors of the principal county adopted pursuant to Section 23567.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
