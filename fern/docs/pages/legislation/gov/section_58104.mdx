# GOV § 58104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58104

---

## Text

The supervising authority shall not consider a petition or other proceedings for the formation of the same district for the same project until after one year from the order of termination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
