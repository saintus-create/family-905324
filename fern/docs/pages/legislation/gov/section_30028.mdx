# GOV § 30028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6.3. Local Revenue Fund 2011

**Citation:** GOV § 30028

---

## Text

For fiscal year 2011–12, which includes cash received in July and up to August 15, 2012, funds allocated to the Juvenile Justice Account from the Local Revenue Fund 2011 pursuant to paragraph (4) of subdivision (c) of Section 30027 shall be allocated by the Controller as follows:

(a) Ninety-six and fifteen hundredths of 1 percent to the Youthful Offender Block Grant Subaccount.

(b) Three and eighty-five hundredths of 1 percent to the Juvenile Reentry Grant Subaccount.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 40, Sec. 13. (SB 1020) Effective June 27, 2012.)
