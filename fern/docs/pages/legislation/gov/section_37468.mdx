# GOV § 37468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37468

---

## Text

To accomplish the objects of this article, the city may combine with the county in which it is situated and hold property and expend money jointly with the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
