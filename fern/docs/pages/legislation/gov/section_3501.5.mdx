# GOV § 3501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10. Local Public Employee Organizations

**Citation:** GOV § 3501.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “public agency” does not mean a superior court.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 123. Effective January 1, 2003.)
