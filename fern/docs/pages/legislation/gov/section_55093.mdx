# GOV § 55093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 4. Agreements for the Joint Construction and Maintenance of Sanitation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 55093

---

## Text

When a local agency enters into an agreement pursuant to this article, the proportionate part of the costs required to be paid by it in the agreement may be raised by any legal means including the issuance and sale of the bonds of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
