# GOV § 61009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 61009

---

## Text

Whenever the boundaries of a district or a zone change, the district shall comply with Chapter 8 (commencing with Section 54900) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 249, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
