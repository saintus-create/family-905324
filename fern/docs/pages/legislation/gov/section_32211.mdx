# GOV § 32211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 32211

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may levy a special tax to be used for the payment of pensions and annuities under such pension, retirement, and benefit systems or associations as are established by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
