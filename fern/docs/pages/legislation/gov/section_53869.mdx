# GOV § 53869

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 8. Delinquent Assessment Acquisition and Sales Law of 1915

**Citation:** GOV § 53869

---

## Text

By ordinance or resolution the legislative body may provide the notice and the procedure of sale by which the lien or property may be sold and whether the sale shall be for cash or on terms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
