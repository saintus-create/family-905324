# GOV § 15622

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15622

---

## Text

The board may sell at prices fixed by the board copies of compilations of the revenue laws of the State, maps, and other documents.
