# GOV § 996.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 7. DEFENSE OF PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Procedures Applicable to State and Local Public Entities

**Citation:** GOV § 996.6

---

## Text

The rights of an employee or former employee under this part are in addition to and not in lieu of any rights he may have under any contract or under any other enactment providing for his defense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1683, Sec. 16.)
