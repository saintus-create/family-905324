# GOV § 19772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Military Service > Article: 2. Military Leave of Absence

**Citation:** GOV § 19772

---

## Text

“Short-term military leave” means a leave for six months or less. “Long-term military leave” means a leave of over six months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 446.)
