# GOV § 8298

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8298

---

## Text

The commission may study and recommend revisions to correct technical or minor substantive defects in the statutes of the state without a prior concurrent resolution of the Legislature referring the matter to it for study.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 152, Sec. 1.)
