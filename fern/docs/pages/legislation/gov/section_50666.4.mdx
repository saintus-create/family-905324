# GOV § 50666.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50666.4

---

## Text

If the interest and principal of the bonds and all charges to protect or secure them are paid when due, an amount or amounts for other purposes may be apportioned from the revenues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
