# GOV § 15801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15801

---

## Text

No public building shall be acquired or constructed under this part, unless authorized by a separate act or appropriation enacted by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1686.)
