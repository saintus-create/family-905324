# GOV § 43067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43067

---

## Text

The corrected list for each tax is the assessment roll upon which the tax is levied for that year. The city clerk, acting as clerk of the board of equalization, shall certify the corrected list as being the assessment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
