# GOV § 34414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Special Charter Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34414

## Text

The provisions of Sections 34331 and 34332 apply to cities organized pursuant to this chapter.
