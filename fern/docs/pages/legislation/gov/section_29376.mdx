# GOV § 29376

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 4. County Officers’ Cash Difference Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29376

---

## Text

Each county officer or administrative head of a county department having a cash difference fund shall upon demand of the auditor or the board of supervisors give an account of the cash difference fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 470, Sec. 41. (AB 1529) Effective January 1, 2013.)
