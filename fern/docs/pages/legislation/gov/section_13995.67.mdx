# GOV § 13995.67

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 7. Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.67

---

## Text

Assessments may be used in furtherance of the purposes set forth in Section 13995.41, or to fund the costs pursuant to Section 13995.57. Assessments may be used to fund these costs regardless of whether the work was performed by the office or commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 229, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
