# GOV § 64711.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 2. The Los Angeles County Affordable Housing Solutions Agency and Governing Board

**Citation:** GOV § 64711.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (c) of Section 64711, if a member appointed by the mayor of the City of Los Angeles is not a member of the city council of that city, the person appointed may serve for 60 days without consent of the city council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
