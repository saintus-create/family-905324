# GOV § 25482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 6. Purchase of Personal Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25482

---

## Text

In considering the bids, the board may accept or reject all or any of them, or may accept or reject a part of any bid, preference being given, however, to the lowest responsible bidder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
