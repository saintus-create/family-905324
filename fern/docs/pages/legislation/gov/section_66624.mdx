# GOV § 66624

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66624

---

## Text

The time and place of the first meeting of the commission shall be prescribed by the Governor, but, in no event, shall it be scheduled for a date later than 10 days after the effective date of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1162.)
