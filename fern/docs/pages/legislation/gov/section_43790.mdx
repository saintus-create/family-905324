# GOV § 43790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 43790

---

## Text

Any bond issued pursuant to this article shall not be callable or redeemable prior to its fixed maturity date unless it contains a statement that the bond is callable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
