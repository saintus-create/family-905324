# GOV § 88000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 8. Ballot Pamphlet

**Citation:** GOV § 88000

---

## Text

There shall be a state voter information guide which shall be prepared by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 249, Sec. 9. (AB 1511) Effective January 1, 2026.)
