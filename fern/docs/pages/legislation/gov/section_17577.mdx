# GOV § 17577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Identification and Payment of Costs Mandated by the State > Article: 2. Specific Costs Mandated by the State

**Citation:** GOV § 17577

---

## Text

The estimate required by Section 17576 shall be the amount estimated to be required during the first fiscal year of a bill’s operation in order to reimburse local agencies and school districts for costs mandated by the state by the bill.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1459, Sec. 1.)
