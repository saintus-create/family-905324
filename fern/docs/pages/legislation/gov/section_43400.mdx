# GOV § 43400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43400

---

## Text

All money received from licenses, street poll taxes, fines, penalties, and forfeitures shall be paid into the general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
