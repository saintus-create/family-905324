# GOV § 3020.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 2. Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3020.5

---

## Text

The Senate when sitting as the court of impeachment is a court of record. The officers of the Senate are the officers of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 206.)
