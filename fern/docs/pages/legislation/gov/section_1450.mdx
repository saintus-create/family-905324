# GOV § 1450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 1450

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided, every official bond shall be filed in the proper office within the time prescribed for filing the oath.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
