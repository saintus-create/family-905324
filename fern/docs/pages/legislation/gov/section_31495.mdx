# GOV § 31495

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1.5. Alternative Plan for Counties with Populations in Excess of Six Million

**Citation:** GOV § 31495

---

## Text

Until the completion of the next regularly scheduled actuarial survey of the retirement system, the employer contribution required to finance the plan created by this article shall be established by the board, upon the recommendation of the actuary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 910, Sec. 1. Effective September 28, 1981.)
