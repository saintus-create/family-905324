# GOV § 4529.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.1. [Architectural and Engineering Services]

## Text

If any act of the Legislature conflicts with the provisions of this act, this act shall prevail.
