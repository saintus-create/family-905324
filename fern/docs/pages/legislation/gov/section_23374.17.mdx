# GOV § 23374.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 4.5. Election to Select County Officers and Location of County Seat in the Approved County

**Citation:** GOV § 23374.17

---

## Text

The clerk of the principal county shall immediately make out and deliver to each person elected a certificate of election, authenticated by his signature and the seal of the board of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 370.)
