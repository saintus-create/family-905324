# GOV § 59356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 8. Notice of Assessment Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 59356

---

## Text

Before the date designated for the hearing, the notice shall be published twice in the newspaper in which the resolution of intention was published, if still existing, otherwise in any newspaper designated by the legislative body. The first publication shall be at least 10 days before the day set for hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
