# GOV § 66418.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 66418.1

---

## Text

“Development” means the uses to which the land which is the subject of a map shall be put, the buildings to be constructed on it, and all alterations of the land and construction incident thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1113, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1986, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1113.)
