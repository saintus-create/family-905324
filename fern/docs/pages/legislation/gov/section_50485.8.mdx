# GOV § 50485.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 6.5. Airport Approaches Zoning Law

**Citation:** GOV § 50485.8

---

## Text

No airport zoning regulations adopted under this article shall require the removal, lowering, or other change or alteration of any structure or tree not conforming to the regulations when adopted or amended, or otherwise interfere with the continuance of any nonconforming use, except as provided in Section 50485.10.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1741.)
