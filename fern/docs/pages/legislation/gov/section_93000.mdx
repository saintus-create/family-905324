# GOV § 93000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. GREAT REDWOOD TRAIL AGENCY ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 93000

---

## Text

(a) This title shall be known and may be cited as the Great Redwood Trail Agency Act.

(b) This section shall become operative on March 1, 2022.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 3) and added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 423, Sec. 4. (SB 69) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative March 1, 2022, by its own provisions.)
