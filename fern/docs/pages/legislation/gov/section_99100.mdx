# GOV § 99100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 19. THE EVERY KID COUNTS (EKC) ACT

**Citation:** GOV § 99100

---

## Text

This act shall be known, and may be cited, as the Every Kid Counts (EKC) Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 23, Sec. 23. (SB 85) Effective June 27, 2017.)
