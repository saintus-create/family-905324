# GOV § 15984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. State Review

**Citation:** GOV § 15984

---

## Text

The Department of Transportation shall provide technical assistance to paratransit providers who may wish to explore coordination strategies but lack the capability to develop and implement those strategies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 962, Sec. 4.)
