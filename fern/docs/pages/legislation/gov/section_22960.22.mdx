# GOV § 22960.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.22

---

## Text

“Net earnings” means the income earned, or losses incurred, on the State Peace Officers’ and Firefighters’ Defined Contribution Plan Fund, less the costs of administering the plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
