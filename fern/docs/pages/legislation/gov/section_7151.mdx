# GOV § 7151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14. State Tax Liens > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 7151

---

## Text

“Bona fide purchaser” has the same meaning as “protected purchaser” as defined in Section 8303 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 497, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 1997.)
