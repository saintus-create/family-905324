# GOV § 32251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 3. Board of Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 32251

---

## Text

One member of the board shall be the county treasurer, acting in an ex officio capacity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
