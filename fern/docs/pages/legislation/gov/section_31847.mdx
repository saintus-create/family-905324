# GOV § 31847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 15.5. Survivors’ Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31847

---

## Text

The monthly survivor’s allowance in the case of two or more children entitled thereto shall be divided equally between them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1591.)
