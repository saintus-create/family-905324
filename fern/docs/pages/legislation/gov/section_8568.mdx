# GOV § 8568

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 3. Powers of the Governor

**Citation:** GOV § 8568

---

## Text

The State Emergency Plan shall be in effect in each political subdivision of the state, and the governing body of each political subdivision shall take such action as may be necessary to carry out the provisions thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1454.)
