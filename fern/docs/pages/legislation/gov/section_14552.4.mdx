# GOV § 14552.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Federal Highway Grant Anticipation Notes > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 14552.4

---

## Text

“Federal transportation funds” means any funds apportioned to the state by the United States Department of Transportation, including, but not limited to, funds paid pursuant to the Transportation Equity Act for the 21st Century (Public Law 105-178).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 862, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
