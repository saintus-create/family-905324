# GOV § 23245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23245

---

## Text

In certifying the sufficiency of a petition, the clerk shall compare the name of each person signing the petition with the registration records of the county in which the person signing the petition resides.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
