# GOV § 50235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 9. Abandoned Excavations

**Citation:** GOV § 50235

---

## Text

The heading of the notices shall be “Notice to abate abandoned excavation” in letters not less than one inch in height.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1178.)
