# GOV § 69909

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 8. Officers, Attaches, and Employees Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 69909

---

## Text

(a) In the County of Riverside, in addition to any other compensation and benefits, each judge of the superior court shall receive the county flexible benefits plan.

(b) Subject to approval by the board of supervisors, each judge of the superior court shall receive long-term disability insurance to the same extent as provided by the County of Riverside for other elected county officials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 69908 (as added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1232) by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1091, Sec. 66.)
