# GOV § 3071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 3. Removal Other Than by Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3071

---

## Text

The district attorney and the defendant are each entitled to such process as is necessary to enforce the attendance of witnesses as upon a trial of an indictment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
