# GOV § 15101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State’s Teletype System > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15101

---

## Text

The system shall be used exclusively for the official business of the State, and the official business of any city, county, city and county, or other public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
