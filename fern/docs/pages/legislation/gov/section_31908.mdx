# GOV § 31908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31908

---

## Text

“Beneficiary” means any person in receipt of a pension, or other benefit provided by the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
