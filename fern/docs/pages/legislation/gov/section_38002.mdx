# GOV § 38002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 7. Park and Playground Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 38002

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “improvement” includes a public park, urban open-space lands, playground, or library.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 426.)
