# GOV § 43724

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43724

---

## Text

The bonds shall be serial bonds, and not less than one-fortieth of the principal and interest on all sums unpaid shall be paid each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
