# GOV § 9149.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 9. The Historic State Capitol Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.12

---

## Text

The commission shall use any funds appropriated to its use, or allocated to its use by the Joint Rules Committee, donated to it, or acquired as revenue from any concession operated in the State Capitol, only for purposes of furthering the objectives of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1757, Sec. 8. Effective October 1, 1984.)
