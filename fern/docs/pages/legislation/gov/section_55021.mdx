# GOV § 55021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 2. Request by Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 55021

---

## Text

The resolution shall contain:

(a) A description of the conduits proposed to be constructed and maintained in the other local agency.

(b) A description of the streets or other public places in, across, or along which the conduits are proposed to be constructed and maintained.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
