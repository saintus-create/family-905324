# GOV § 51933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 3. Reorganization Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 51933

---

## Text

After performing their duties, the election officers shall deliver the ballots, tally sheets, and returns to the clerk of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 972.)
