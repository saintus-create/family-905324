# GOV § 27557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27557

---

## Text

The surveyor shall plat, trace, blueprint, or otherwise make all county, road, district, and other maps and, at the request of the assessor, make all assessors’ block-books for the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1187, Sec. 4.5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
