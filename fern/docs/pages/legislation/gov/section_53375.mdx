# GOV § 53375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 2. Formation of District

**Citation:** GOV § 53375

---

## Text

If the legislative body determines to proceed with establishing the district, the legislative body shall adopt an ordinance establishing the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
