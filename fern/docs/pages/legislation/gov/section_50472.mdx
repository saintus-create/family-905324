# GOV § 50472

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 6. Airports

**Citation:** GOV § 50472

---

## Text

By the methods provided by law, a local agency may incur indebtedness and issue bonds for the purposes of Section 50470.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
