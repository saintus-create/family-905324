# GOV § 54737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Contracts of Sanitation or Sewerage Enterprises

**Citation:** GOV § 54737

---

## Text

The affirmative vote of four-fifths of the legislative body is required to approve the contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1249.)
