# GOV § 17277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 4. Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 17277

---

## Text

If it appears to the Controller that, on the maturity date on the face of any registered reimbursement warrant, there will be insufficient unapplied money in the General Fund available for its payment he or she may issue a refunding warrant, with the written approval of the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 766, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
