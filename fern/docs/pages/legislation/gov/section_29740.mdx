# GOV § 29740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Claims > Article: 2. Allowance of Claims by Auditor

**Citation:** GOV § 29740

---

## Text

By resolution the board of supervisors may adopt the procedure for the approval of claims prescribed in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
