# GOV § 82035.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82035.5

---

## Text

“LAFCO proposal” means a proposal, as defined in Section 56069, including a proceeding, as defined by Section 56067.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 113, Sec. 4. (AB 528) Effective January 1, 2010.)
