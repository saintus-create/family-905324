# GOV § 27500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 2. Inquests

**Citation:** GOV § 27500

---

## Text

Any witness served with a subpena who wilfully and without reasonable excuse fails to attend and testify is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
