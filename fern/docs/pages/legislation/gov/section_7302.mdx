# GOV § 7302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.7. Open-Space Land Dedication

**Citation:** GOV § 7302

---

## Text

Any person may offer land included within a transfer described in Section 7301 to the state for preservation as open-space land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1144.)
