# GOV § 26318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 14. Revenue Bonds for County Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26318

---

## Text

An indenture pursuant to which bonds are issued may include such covenants and agreements on the part of the board as the board deems necessary or advisable for the better security of the bonds issued thereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 815.)
