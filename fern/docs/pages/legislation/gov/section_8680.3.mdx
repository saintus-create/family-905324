# GOV § 8680.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8680.3

---

## Text

“Disaster” means a fire, flood, storm, tidal wave, earthquake, terrorism, epidemic, or other similar public calamity that the Governor determines presents a threat to public safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 461, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
