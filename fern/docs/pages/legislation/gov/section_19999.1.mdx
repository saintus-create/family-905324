# GOV § 19999.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Two-Tiered Retirement System

**Citation:** GOV § 19999.1

---

## Text

The Department of Human Resources, in conjunction with the Board of Administration of the Public Employees’ Retirement System, shall develop legislation which will implement a two-tiered retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 106. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
