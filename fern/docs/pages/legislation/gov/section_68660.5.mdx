# GOV § 68660.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.3. California Habeas Corpus Resource Center

**Citation:** GOV § 68660.5

---

## Text

The purposes of this chapter are to qualify the State of California for the handling of federal habeas corpus petitions under Chapter 154 of Title 28 of the United States Code, to expedite the completion of state habeas corpus proceedings in capital cases, and to provide quality representation in state habeas corpus for inmates sentenced to death. This chapter shall be construed and administered consistently with those purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 2016, by initiative Proposition 66, Sec. 13.)
