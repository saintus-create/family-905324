# GOV § 66623

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Conservation and Development Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66623

---

## Text

The Governor shall select, from among public representatives on the commission appointed pursuant to subdivision (j) of Section 66620, a chairperson and a vice chairperson.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 519, Sec. 2.)
