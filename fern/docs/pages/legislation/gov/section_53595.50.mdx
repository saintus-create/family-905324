# GOV § 53595.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 13. Collateralized Borrowing

**Citation:** GOV § 53595.50

---

## Text

Any debt instruments issued under this article, their transfer, and in the income therefrom shall at all times be free from taxation of every kind by the state and by all municipalities and political subdivisions in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 902, Sec. 1. Effective October 8, 1993.)
