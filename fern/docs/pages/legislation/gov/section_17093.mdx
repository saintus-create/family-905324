# GOV § 17093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Lost or Destroyed Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17093

---

## Text

If the application is approved, the Controller shall issue and deliver to the applicant, on demand, a replacement warrant for the full amount of the original warrant. When the Controller issues the replacement, he or she shall notify the Treasurer that a replacement warrant has been issued and identify the warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 43. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
