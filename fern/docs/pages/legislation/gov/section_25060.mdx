# GOV § 25060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 4. Vacancies

**Citation:** GOV § 25060

---

## Text

Whenever a vacancy occurs in a board of supervisors, the Governor shall fill the vacancy. The appointee shall hold office until the election and qualification of his or her successor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 76, Sec. 98. (AB 383) Effective January 1, 2014.)
