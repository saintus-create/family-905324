# GOV § 4007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Cost Records to be Kept

**Citation:** GOV § 4007

---

## Text

Every engineer who wilfully violates any of the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
