# GOV § 15956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 15956

---

## Text

“County transportation commission” means such a commission created pursuant to Division 12 (commencing with Section 130000) of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1120.)
