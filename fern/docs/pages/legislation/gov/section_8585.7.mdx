# GOV § 8585.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 5. Office of Emergency Services

**Citation:** GOV § 8585.7

---

## Text

The office may certify the accredited status of local disaster councils, subject to the requirements of Section 8612.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 124. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
