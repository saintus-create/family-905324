# GOV § 50512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 7. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 50512

---

## Text

If the property is offered for lease at a monthly rental, all bids shall be accompanied by a bond in the amount fixed by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 517, Sec. 255.)
