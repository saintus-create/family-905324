# GOV § 20381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Membership Classifications > Article: 2. Miscellaneous Member Classification

**Citation:** GOV § 20381

---

## Text

“University member” includes all members who are employees of the university.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
