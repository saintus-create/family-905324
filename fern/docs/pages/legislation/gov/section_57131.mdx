# GOV § 57131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Elections

**Citation:** GOV § 57131

---

## Text

The notice of election required to be given by Section 57130 shall contain all of the matters specified in Section 57115.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 260.5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
