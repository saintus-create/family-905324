# GOV § 4476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Purchases

**Citation:** GOV § 4476

---

## Text

“Person,” as used in this chapter means any individual, corporation, association, or any other entity organized for the purpose of conducting business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1812.)
