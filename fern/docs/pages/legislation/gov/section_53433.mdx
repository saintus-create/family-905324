# GOV § 53433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Mutilated or Defaced Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53433

---

## Text

The owner of the bond desiring a duplicate shall make a written application to the legislative body, stating the facts required by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
