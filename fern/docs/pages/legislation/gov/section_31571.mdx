# GOV § 31571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 4.5. Big Bear Fire Agencies Pension Consolidation Act of 2018

## Text

This article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Big Bear Fire Agencies Pension Consolidation Act of 2018.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 72, Sec. 1. (AB 2004) Effective July 9, 2018.)
