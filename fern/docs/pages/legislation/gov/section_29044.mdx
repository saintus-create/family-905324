# GOV § 29044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 2. Budget Request

**Citation:** GOV § 29044

---

## Text

The auditor shall provide to the administrative officer or such other official as the board directs, any financial statements, data, or recommendations, if any, for any changes to the estimated financing sources referenced in Section 29040.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 21. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
