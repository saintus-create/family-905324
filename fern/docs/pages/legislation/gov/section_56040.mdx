# GOV § 56040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56040

---

## Text

“Function” means any power granted by law to a local agency to provide designated governmental or proprietary services or facilities for the use, benefit, or protection of persons or property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 29. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
