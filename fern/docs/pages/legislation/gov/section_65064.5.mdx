# GOV § 65064.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 65064.5

---

## Text

The board shall meet at least six times in each year, and may call additional meetings at its own discretion, or, during periods between meetings, at the discretion of the chairman or of a majority of the executive committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
