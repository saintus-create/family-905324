# GOV § 6728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6728

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim the month of June as LGBTQ+ Pride Month.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 947, Sec. 1. (AB 1432) Effective January 1, 2023.)
