# GOV § 15570.60

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 15570.60

---

## Text

For good cause shown, the department may extend for not more than 30 days the time fixed for filing any report required by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 5. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Operative July 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 15570.100.)
