# GOV § 40102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 16. Advertising

**Citation:** GOV § 40102

---

## Text

The money appropriated may be used in the manner and for the purposes recited in the ordinance making the appropriation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
