# GOV § 13983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 13983

---

## Text

The secretary shall conduct a program relating to the medical aspects of traffic injury and accident control.
