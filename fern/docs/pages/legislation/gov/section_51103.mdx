# GOV § 51103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51103

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature to implement the policies of this chapter by including all qualifying timberland in timberland production zones.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 5.)
