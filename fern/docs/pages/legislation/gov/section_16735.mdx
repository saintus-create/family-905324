# GOV § 16735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. State General Obligation Bonds > Article: 2. Issuance of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 16735

---

## Text

Each bond shall contain a reference to the bond act, and if subject to call, tender, or redemption prior to maturity, a recital to that effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 205, Sec. 4. (SB 826) Effective January 1, 2010.)
