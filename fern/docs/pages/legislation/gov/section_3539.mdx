# GOV § 3539

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.5. Excluded Employees Bill of Rights

**Citation:** GOV § 3539

---

## Text

The enactment of this chapter shall not make Section 923 of the Labor Code applicable to state employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1522, Sec. 17.)
