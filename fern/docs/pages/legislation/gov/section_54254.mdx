# GOV § 54254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 10. The Local Government Privatization Act of 1985

**Citation:** GOV § 54254

---

## Text

A local agency’s compliance with Section 54253 shall constitute compliance with Division 3 (commencing with Section 6001) of the Public Utilities Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1430, Sec. 4.)
