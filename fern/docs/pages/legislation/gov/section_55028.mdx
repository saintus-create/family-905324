# GOV § 55028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 2. Request by Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 55028

---

## Text

The cost may be determined by resolutions of the legislative bodies of both local agencies. The payment shall be made at the times and in the amounts determined by the legislative bodies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
