# GOV § 20898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 20898

---

## Text

In computing the service with which a member is entitled to be credited under this part, time during which the member is excused from working because of holidays, sick leave, vacation, or leave of absence, with compensation, shall be included.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
