# GOV § 19243.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Limited Examination and Appointment Program > Article: 2. Examinations and Appointments

**Citation:** GOV § 19243.2

---

## Text

With the approval of the department, the appointing power may shorten or extend the duration of the job examination period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 60. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
