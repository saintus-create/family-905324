# GOV § 16678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Fiscal Agent

**Citation:** GOV § 16678

---

## Text

Any fiscal agent may redeem state bonds and coupons when duly presented to it by the holder, with or without a certificate making them payable at a fiscal agent.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2070.)
