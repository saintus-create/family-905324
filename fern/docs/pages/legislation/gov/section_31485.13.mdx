# GOV § 31485.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31485.13

---

## Text

In accordance with Section 401(a)(8) of Title 26 of the United States Code, a forfeiture of benefits under this chapter shall not be applied to increase benefits that a member would otherwise receive under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 212, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
