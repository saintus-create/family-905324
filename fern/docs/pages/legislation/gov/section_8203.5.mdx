# GOV § 8203.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Notaries Public > Article: 1. Notaries Public

**Citation:** GOV § 8203.5

---

## Text

In addition to the name of the State, the jurat shall also contain the name of the reservation in which the instrument is executed.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1970.)
