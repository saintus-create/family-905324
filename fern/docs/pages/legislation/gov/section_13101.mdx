# GOV § 13101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. Infrastructure Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 13101

---

## Text

As used in this article, “infrastructure” means real property, including land and improvements to the land, structures and equipment integral to the operation of structures, easements, rights-of-way and other forms of interest in property, roadways, and water conveyances.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 606, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
