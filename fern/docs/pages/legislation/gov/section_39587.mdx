# GOV § 39587

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 13. Weed and Rubbish Abatement > Article: 2. Alternative Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 39587

---

## Text

The proceedings provided by this article are an alternative to any procedure established by ordinance pursuant to Article 1 (commencing with Section 39500).
