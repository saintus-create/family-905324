# GOV § 64635

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 3. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 64635

---

## Text

Bonds issued pursuant to this article are fully negotiable.
