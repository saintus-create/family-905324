# GOV § 29062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 3. Recommended Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29062

---

## Text

The recommended budget shall be submitted to the board by the administrative officer or auditor, as designated by the board, on or before June 30 of each year, as the board directs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 26. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
