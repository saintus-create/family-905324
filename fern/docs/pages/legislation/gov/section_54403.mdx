# GOV § 54403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54403

---

## Text

Any premium payable on the bonds shall be in the amount or amounts specified by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1130, Sec. 6.)
