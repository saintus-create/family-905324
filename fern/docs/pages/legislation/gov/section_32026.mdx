# GOV § 32026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 6. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 32026

---

## Text

Upon election by any person to come within this chapter, any sums due him or to his credit under any other system shall be transferred to this system to his credit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
