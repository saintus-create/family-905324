# GOV § 50669.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50669.4

---

## Text

The duties set forth in this article do not require the local agency to expend any funds other than revenues pledged to secure payment of the principal of or interest on bonds as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
