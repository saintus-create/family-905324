# GOV § 66658

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 5. The San Francisco Bay Plan and Further Reports of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66658

---

## Text

Until the termination of the existence of the commission, it shall have all powers and duties prescribed by Chapters 1 (commencing with Section 66600) to 4 (commencing with Section 66630), inclusive, of this title including, without limitation, the power to continue or make further studies authorized thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 713.)
