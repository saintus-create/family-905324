# GOV § 16100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Appropriation

**Citation:** GOV § 16100

---

## Text

There is hereby continuously appropriated from the State General Fund to the Controller an amount sufficient to pay the subventions required by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1406.)
