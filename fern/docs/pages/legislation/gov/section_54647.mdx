# GOV § 54647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 10. Remedies of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 54647

---

## Text

The delay or omission of any bondholder or his trustee to exercise any right or power accruing upon any default does not impair the right or power and is not a waiver of the default or acquiescence in it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
