# GOV § 12955.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Discrimination Prohibited > Article: 2. Housing Discrimination

**Citation:** GOV § 12955.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall preclude the government from establishing programs to collect information relating to discriminatory housing practices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 182, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1993.)
