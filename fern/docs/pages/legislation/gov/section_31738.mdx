# GOV § 31738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 10. Disability Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 31738

---

## Text

Each payment of a retirement allowance, after the effective date of this section, to a beneficiary who retired prior to September 20, 1947, shall be calculated according to the provisions of this chapter as they existed on September 20, 1947.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 448.)
