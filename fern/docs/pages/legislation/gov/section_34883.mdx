# GOV § 34883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 2. Election of Legislative Body By or From Districts in Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34883

---

## Text

Registered voters signing nomination petitions or voting for a member of the legislative body shall be residents of the geographical area making up the district from which the member is to be elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 745.)
