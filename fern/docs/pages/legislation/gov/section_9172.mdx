# GOV § 9172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees of the Legislature > Article: 2. Permanent Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 9172

---

## Text

All permanent officers and employees of the Senate or Assembly, except the President of the Senate and the Majority and Minority Floor Leaders and Minute Clerk of the Assembly, shall be elected by the house to which they are attached.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1964, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 22.)
