# GOV § 25621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25621

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may appoint a county board of forestry, to consist of no less than three persons who shall serve without compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1019, Sec. 10.)
