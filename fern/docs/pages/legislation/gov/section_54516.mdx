# GOV § 54516

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 6. Duties of Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 54516

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of Section 54516.1, the local agency shall operate, maintain and preserve the enterprise in good repair and working order.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 811.)
