# GOV § 9193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees of the Legislature > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 9193

---

## Text

The Minute Clerk of the Senate and the Minute Clerk of the Assembly shall keep a correct record of the proceedings of their respective houses.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
