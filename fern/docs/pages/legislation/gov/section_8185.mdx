# GOV § 8185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 3. Capitol Area Redevelopment

**Citation:** GOV § 8185

---

## Text

For the purpose of exercising powers of a redevelopment agency, the jurisdiction of the authority shall be the “metropolitan area” as defined in subdivision (a) of Section 8160.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 953.)
