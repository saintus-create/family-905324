# GOV § 66664.4

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 5.5. San Francisco Bay Dredging > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 66664.4

---

## Text

“Dredging” means the extraction of sand, mud, or other materials from San Francisco Bay, its tributaries, the delta, or coastal state waters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 583, Sec. 3.)
