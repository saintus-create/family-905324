# GOV § 8243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.1. Commission on the Status of Women and Girls

**Citation:** GOV § 8243

---

## Text

All meetings of the commission shall be open and public and all persons shall be permitted to attend any meetings of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8223 (as added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 579) by Stats. 1979, Ch. 373.)
