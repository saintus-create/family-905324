# GOV § 68660

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2.3. California Habeas Corpus Resource Center

**Citation:** GOV § 68660

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “center” means the California Habeas Corpus Resource Center, and “board” means the board of directors of the center.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 853, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
