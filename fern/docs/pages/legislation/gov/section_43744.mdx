# GOV § 43744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43744

---

## Text

The treasurer shall keep a record of bonds, warrants, judgments, notes, or other evidences of indebtedness redeemed, and report the redemptions to the city legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
