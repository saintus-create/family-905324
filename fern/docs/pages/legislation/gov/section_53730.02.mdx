# GOV § 53730.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 3.8. Hospital Districts: Special Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53730.02

---

## Text

This article does not apply to any special tax imposed prior to January 1, 1989, nor does this article affect the validity of any special tax imposed by a hospital district prior to January 1, 1989.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1345, Sec. 1.)
