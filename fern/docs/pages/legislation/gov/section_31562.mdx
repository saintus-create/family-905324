# GOV § 31562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 4. Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 31562

---

## Text

Any elective officer who does not declare his intention to become a member of the retirement association as required by this chapter is ineligible to membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
