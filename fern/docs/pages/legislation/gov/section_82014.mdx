# GOV § 82014

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82014

---

## Text

“Conflict of Interest Code” means a set of rules and regulations adopted by an agency pursuant to Chapter 7 of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
