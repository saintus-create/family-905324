# GOV § 68074.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68074.1

---

## Text

The seal of any superior court may be affixed by a seal press or stamp which will print or emboss a seal which will reproduce legibly under photographic methods.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 202. Effective January 1, 2003.)
