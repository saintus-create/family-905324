# GOV § 69101

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 4. The Courts of Appeal > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 69101

---

## Text

The Court of Appeal for the First Appellate District consists of five divisions having four judges each, and shall hold its regular sessions at San Francisco.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 998, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
