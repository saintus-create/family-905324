# GOV § 12179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12179

---

## Text

The fee for attesting each patent for land issued by the Governor is one dollar ($1) for each 160 acres, or fraction thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 1000, Sec. 51. Effective January 1, 2000.)
