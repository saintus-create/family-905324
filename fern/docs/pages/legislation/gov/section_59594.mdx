# GOV § 59594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 16. Acquisition and Cancellation of Bonds of Nonconsenting Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 59594

---

## Text

Any cash collected on reassessments and not required to pay consenting bondholders may also be used to reimburse in whole or in part any local agency, person, or corporation making the advance, and the legislative body may contract to so apply such cash.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
