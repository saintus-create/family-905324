# GOV § 50962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.5. Volunteer Firefighters Length of Service Award Act > Article: 3. Service Credit and Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 50962

---

## Text

When the member’s application for an award is received by the board, an award shall be granted to accrue and become effective pursuant to the terms, conditions, and eligibility requirements established by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 651, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008. Operative March 1, 2008, by Section 50979.)
