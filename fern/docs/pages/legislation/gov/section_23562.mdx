# GOV § 23562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** GOV § 23562

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by this article, the election shall be conducted as other elections in the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
