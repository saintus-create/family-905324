# GOV § 25549.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 10. Joint Occupancy

**Citation:** GOV § 25549.7

---

## Text

Before entering into a lease or agreement pursuant to this article, the board of supervisors shall comply with the provisions of Section 25549.8.

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 341.)
