# GOV § 51191.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.9. Solar-Use Easement > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51191.8

---

## Text

The Department of Conservation may adopt regulations pursuant to the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Division 3 of Title 2) for the implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 596, Sec. 8. (SB 618) Effective January 1, 2012.)
