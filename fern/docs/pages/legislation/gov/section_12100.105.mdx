# GOV § 12100.105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 9.5. California Small Agricultural Business Drought and Flood Relief Grant Program

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.105

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 45, Sec. 33. (AB 127) Effective July 10, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 9.5, commencing with Section 12100.100.)
