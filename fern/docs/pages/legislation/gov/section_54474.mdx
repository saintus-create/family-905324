# GOV § 54474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54474

---

## Text

Any resolution containing such a procedure may also provide for meetings of bondholders or for their written assent without a meeting and the manner of consenting, with or without a meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
