# GOV § 11158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Departments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11158

---

## Text

The sheriffs in the several counties shall execute all lawful orders of a department in their counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 1997.)
