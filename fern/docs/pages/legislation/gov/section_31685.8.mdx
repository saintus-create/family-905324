# GOV § 31685.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8.4. Community Property

**Citation:** GOV § 31685.8

---

## Text

A nonmember entitled to receive a retirement allowance shall receive a retirement allowance based on the service retirement formula applicable to the service credited to the nonmember.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 670, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
