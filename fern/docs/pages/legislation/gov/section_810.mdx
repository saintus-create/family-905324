# GOV § 810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL

**Citation:** GOV § 810

---

## Text

(a) Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions contained in this part govern the construction of this division.

(b) This division may be referred to as the Government Claims Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 759, Sec. 5. (AB 2690) Effective January 1, 2013.)
