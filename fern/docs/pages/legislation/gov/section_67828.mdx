# GOV § 67828

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 3. Organization

**Citation:** GOV § 67828

---

## Text

The board shall elect from its own members a chair and a vice chair at the first board meeting held each year. Each shall serve a term of one year and may be reelected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
