# GOV § 27252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 2. Books

**Citation:** GOV § 27252

---

## Text

The recorder shall keep an index to the certificates of births, deaths and marriages.
