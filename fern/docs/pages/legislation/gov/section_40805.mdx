# GOV § 40805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. City Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 40805

---

## Text

The report shall be published or posted consistent with the timelines established in Section 53891 after the close of the fiscal year for which the report is compiled.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 366, Sec. 10. (SB 974) Effective January 1, 2017.)
