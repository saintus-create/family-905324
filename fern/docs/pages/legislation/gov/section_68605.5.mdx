# GOV § 68605.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 5. The Trial Court Delay Reduction Act

**Citation:** GOV § 68605.5

---

## Text

On and after July 1, 1992, this article shall apply to all actions and proceedings in the superior court in each county, except actions and proceedings subject to subdivision (a) of Section 68608 or subdivision (b) of Section 68609.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1232, Sec. 3.)
