# GOV § 8181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 3. Capitol Area Redevelopment

**Citation:** GOV § 8181

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that:

(a) The project area is blighted and in need of redevelopment in the interest of the health and safety and general welfare of the people of the City of Sacramento and the State of California.

(b) The authority is the appropriate entity to direct the redevelopment of the project area.

(c) The redevelopment plan provides an appropriate plan for the redevelopment of the project area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 953.)
