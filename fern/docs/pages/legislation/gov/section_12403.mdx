# GOV § 12403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 12403

---

## Text

The Controller may designate any one or more of the Controller’s employees to administer oaths in respect to returns, reports, affidavits, or other documents that are required to be filed with the Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 65. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
