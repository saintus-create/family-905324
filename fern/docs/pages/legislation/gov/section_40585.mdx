# GOV § 40585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 7. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 40585

---

## Text

The commission may pledge all or any part of the revenues which shall be derived by it from the sale and leasing of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
