# GOV § 19990.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. Days and Hours of Work > Article: 10. Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 19990.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 18000 and 19990, state officers and employees may render services during their office hours, or hours of work for the state, if compensation for these services may be made pursuant to Section 18000.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1513, Sec. 2. Effective September 30, 1986.)
