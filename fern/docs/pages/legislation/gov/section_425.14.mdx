# GOV § 425.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.14

---

## Text

The shell of the black abalone (Haliotis cracherodii) is the official state seashell.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 669, Sec. 1. (AB 2504) Effective January 1, 2025.)
