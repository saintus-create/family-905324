# GOV § 36904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. Ordinances > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 36904

---

## Text

The legislative body may require persons imprisoned for violation of an ordinance to labor on public property or works within the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
