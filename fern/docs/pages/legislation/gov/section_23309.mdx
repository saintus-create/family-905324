# GOV § 23309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 23309

---

## Text

No boundary line of a proposed county shall pass through or divide the territory of any incorporated city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
