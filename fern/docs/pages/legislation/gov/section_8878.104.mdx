# GOV § 8878.104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.47. Earthquake Safety and Public Buildings Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1990 > Article: 4. Local Government Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 8878.104

---

## Text

Operation and maintenance costs shall be the responsibility of the grantee and may not be considered as part of the project cost. Costs for planning preliminary engineering studies, including preliminary plans, may be reimbursed following the receipt of an allocation of funds, subject to approval by the State Architect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 23, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 122 at the June 5, 1990, election.)
