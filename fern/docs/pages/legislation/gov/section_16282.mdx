# GOV § 16282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Cost of Living Increases

**Citation:** GOV § 16282

---

## Text

For the purpose of this chapter “local public agency” means a city, county, city and county, special district, school district, county board of education, or community college district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 332.)
