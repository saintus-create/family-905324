# GOV § 59510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 13. Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 59510

---

## Text

New bonds shall not issue against public property and the list of unpaid reassessments to be filed with the treasurer shall not include any unpaid reassessments upon public property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
