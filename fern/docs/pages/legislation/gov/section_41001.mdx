# GOV § 41001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. City Treasurer

**Citation:** GOV § 41001

---

## Text

The city treasurer shall receive and safely keep all money the treasurer receives.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 77, Sec. 1. (SB 742) Effective January 1, 2018.)
