# GOV § 810.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL

**Citation:** GOV § 810.2

---

## Text

“Employee” includes an officer, judicial officer as defined in Section 327 of the Elections Code, employee, or servant, whether or not compensated, but does not include an independent contractor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 1995.)
