# GOV § 26880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Controller

**Citation:** GOV § 26880

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may create the office of controller. The office of county controller shall be held ex officio by the county auditor.
