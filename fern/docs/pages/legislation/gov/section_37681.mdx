# GOV § 37681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 9. Cemeteries

**Citation:** GOV § 37681

---

## Text

The city legislative body may acquire and maintain cemeteries, situated outside or inside of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
