# GOV § 15570.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 15570.20

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part, the following terms shall have the following meanings:

(a) “Board” means the State Board of Equalization.

(b) “Department” means the California Department of Tax and Fee Administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 5. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Operative July 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 15570.100.)
