# GOV § 10211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 10211

---

## Text

The Legislative Counsel, or any deputy legislative counsel, may administer and certify oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 575.)
