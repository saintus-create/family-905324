# GOV § 8547.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6.5. California State Auditor > Article: 3. California Whistleblower Protection Act

**Citation:** GOV § 8547.15

---

## Text

An action for damages pursuant to this article shall not be subject to the claims presentation requirements of the Government Claims Act (Division 3.6 (commencing with Section 810) of Title 1).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 781, Sec. 2. (SB 496) Effective January 1, 2014.)
