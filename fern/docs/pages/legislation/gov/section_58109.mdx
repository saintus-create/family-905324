# GOV § 58109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58109

---

## Text

The notice shall describe the land proposed to be included and state the time and place at which objections to inclusion will be heard.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
