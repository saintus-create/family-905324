# GOV § 9078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 3.5. Legislative Open Records Act

**Citation:** GOV § 9078

---

## Text

The court shall award court costs and reasonable attorney fees to the plaintiff should the plaintiff prevail in litigation filed pursuant to Section 9077.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1246.)
