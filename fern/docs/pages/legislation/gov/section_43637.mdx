# GOV § 43637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43637

---

## Text

By resolution, the legislative body may require the treasurer to give additional bonds for the safe custody and care of the public funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
