# GOV § 25402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 3. Cement Manufacturing Plants

**Citation:** GOV § 25402

---

## Text

The notice shall contain a description of the property to be purchased or works to be constructed, a statement of the amount of money to be invested, the terms upon which it is to be invested, and the time when the proposition will come before the board of supervisors to be acted upon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
