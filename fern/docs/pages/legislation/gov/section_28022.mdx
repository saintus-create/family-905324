# GOV § 28022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Classification of Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 28022

---

## Text

Counties containing a population of 4,000,000 and over are counties of the first class.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1961, Ch. 43.)
