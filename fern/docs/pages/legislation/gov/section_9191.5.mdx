# GOV § 9191.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees of the Legislature > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 9191.5

---

## Text

The Secretary of the Senate and Chief Clerk of the Assembly may administer and certify oaths and may provide the proof or acknowledgment of an instrument in accordance with Article 3 (commencing with Section 1180) of Chapter 4 of Part 4 of Division 2 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 20, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
