# GOV § 92303

**Hierarchy:** Title: 11. CALIFORNIA PASSENGER RAIL FINANCING COMMISSION ACT > Chapter: 6. Construction and Lease of Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 92303

---

## Text

Any lease may provide the means or methods by which title shall vest in a participating party upon the termination of the lease and shall contain other terms and conditions that the commission may determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1553, Sec. 2.)
