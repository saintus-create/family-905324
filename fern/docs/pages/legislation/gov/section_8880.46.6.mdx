# GOV § 8880.46.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 4.5. Public And Financial Accountability

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.46.6

---

## Text

The Controller shall conduct quarterly and annual postaudits of all accounts and transactions of the commission and other special postaudits as the Controller deems necessary. The Controller or his or her agents conducting an audit under this chapter shall have access and authority to examine any and all records of the commission, its distributing agencies, lottery contractors, and lottery game retailers. The Controller may issue a public report of any annual postaudit, which shall be posted on the Internet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 3rd Ex. Sess., Ch. 8, Sec. 3. Effective February 20, 2009. Note: This section contains provisions from former Section 8880.67 (repealed by Ch. 8), which was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
