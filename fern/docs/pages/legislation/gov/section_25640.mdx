# GOV § 25640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25640

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may plant shade and ornamental trees on or about the public grounds and buildings of the county and provide for their care. The cost of planting and caring for the trees shall be paid out of the county general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
