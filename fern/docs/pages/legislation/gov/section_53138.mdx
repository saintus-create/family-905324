# GOV § 53138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Federally Mandated Audits of Block Grant Funds Allocated to Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 53138

---

## Text

The Controller, the Department of Finance, the State Auditor’s office, or other state department auditors may perform audit activities pursuant to this article insofar as the standards of independence contained in the “Government Auditing Standards” published by the Comptroller General of the United States are met.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 107, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2004.)
