# GOV § 55303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Joint Projects for Sanitation and Electrical Lines > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 55303

---

## Text

All powers and duties conferred upon the Public Utilities Commission by this chapter are exclusive. Its judgment is conclusive upon all parties to any proceedings taken pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
