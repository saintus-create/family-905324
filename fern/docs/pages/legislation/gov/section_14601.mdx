# GOV § 14601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 14601

---

## Text

There is in the state government, in the Government Operations Agency, the Department of General Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 292. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
