# GOV § 54702.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 3. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 54702.13

---

## Text

This chapter is full authority for the issuance of bonds by a local agency for the purpose of financing construction of rental housing for employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
