# GOV § 9764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 4. Preparation and Printing of Statutes

**Citation:** GOV § 9764

---

## Text

The statutes of each session shall be prepared for printing and publication by the Legislative Counsel, the State Librarian, or such other state agency authorized or required by law to render the service when directed or requested, as the Department of General Services directs or requests in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
