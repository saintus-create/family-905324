# GOV § 16276.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Special Districts > Article: 3. Distribution Process

**Citation:** GOV § 16276.5

---

## Text

The county auditor shall disburse funds to the special district in the same manner as disbursements which are made from the county treasurer’s property tax trust fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 454, Sec. 66.)
