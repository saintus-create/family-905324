# GOV § 18576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 18576

---

## Text

Judicial notice shall be taken of board and department rules, regulations, and amendments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 360, Sec. 16. (SB 1309) Effective January 1, 2013.)
