# GOV § 25524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 8. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25524

---

## Text

Any lease may be made upon such consideration or for such rental, as is authorized by the action of the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1800.)
