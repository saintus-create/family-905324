# GOV § 9932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Legislative Reform > Article: 4. Legislative Funds and Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 9932

---

## Text

Disbursements from Contingent Funds of the Senate and Assembly

No disbursements shall be made from the Contingent Funds of the Senate and Assembly except as provided by vote of the Joint Rules Committee, two thirds of the total membership thereof concurring.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 5, 1984, by initiative Proposition 24.)
