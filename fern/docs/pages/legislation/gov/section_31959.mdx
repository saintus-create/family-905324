# GOV § 31959

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Peace Officers Retirement Law > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** GOV § 31959

---

## Text

The board may appoint a secretary and provide for the payment from the fund of all its necessary expenses, including secretary hire and printing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
