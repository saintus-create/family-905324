# GOV § 6597

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 4. Local Bond Pooling

**Citation:** GOV § 6597

---

## Text

All public capital improvements financed by the authority shall pay interest within a reasonable time after the authority receives revenues or proceeds from bonds as provided under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 868, Sec. 6. Effective September 23, 1985.)
