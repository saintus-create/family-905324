# GOV § 59181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 2. Authority to Conduct Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 59181

---

## Text

If the district lies partly within unincorporated territory of a county and partly within one or more cities, the legislative body of the county, or of any such city may conduct proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
