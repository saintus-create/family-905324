# GOV § 54614

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 9. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 54614

---

## Text

The contributions may consist of money, rights of way, labor, materials, and other property.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
