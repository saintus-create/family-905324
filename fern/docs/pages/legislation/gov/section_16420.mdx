# GOV § 16420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 8. Service Revolving Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16420

---

## Text

There is in the State Treasury the Service Revolving Fund, hereafter referred to as “the fund.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 875.)
