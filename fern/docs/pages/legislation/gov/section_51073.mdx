# GOV § 51073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.6. Open-Space Easement Act of 1974 > Article: 1. Declaration

**Citation:** GOV § 51073

---

## Text

The Legislature further declares that the acquisition of open-space easements is in the public interest and constitutes a public purpose for which public funds may be expended or advanced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1003.)
