# GOV § 18525.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18525.1

---

## Text

“Promotion” means the appointment of an employee to a position in a different class with a higher salary range, unless the appointment is by transfer, as defined by subdivision (b) of Section 18525.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 103, Sec. 1.)
