# GOV § 3524.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.4. Judicial Council Employer-Employee Relations

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Judicial Council Employer-Employee Relations Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 835, Sec. 1. (AB 83) Effective January 1, 2018.)
