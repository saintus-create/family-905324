# GOV § 7221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14.5. Registration of State Tax Liens

**Citation:** GOV § 7221

---

## Text

The notice of state tax lien shall set forth the matters required by subdivision (c) of Section 7171.
