# GOV § 19996.27

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Separations from Service > Article: 1.6. Reduced Worktime Act

**Citation:** GOV § 19996.27

---

## Text

The department shall adopt appropriate rules and guidelines relating to reduced worktime implementation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
