# GOV § 16375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 2. Special Deposit Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 16375

---

## Text

The Controller shall keep an accurate account of all money deposited in the Special Deposit Fund for each respective agency making such a deposit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 121.)
