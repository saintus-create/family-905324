# GOV § 70041.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 11. Official Reporters and Fees in Particular Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 70041.1

---

## Text

Unless otherwise specifically provided the provisions of this article apply in a county with a population of 290,000 or more and under 295,000.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 421.)
