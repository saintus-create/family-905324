# GOV § 12768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 9. Community Services Block Grant Program > Article: 7. Migrant and Seasonal Farmworker Programs

**Citation:** GOV § 12768

---

## Text

Migrant and seasonal farmworker entities funded by the department shall coordinate their plans and activities with other eligible entities funded by the department to avoid duplication of services and to maximize services for all eligible beneficiaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 46, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2008. Repealed conditionally as prescribed by Section 12790.)
