# GOV § 34000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES GENERALLY > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 34000

---

## Text

As used in this title, “legislative body” means board of trustees, city council, or other governing body of a city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
