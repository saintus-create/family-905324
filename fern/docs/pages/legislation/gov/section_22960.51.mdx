# GOV § 22960.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. The Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 22960.51

---

## Text

No part of the assets of the fund may revert to the employer until all liabilities of the plan have been fully satisfied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 820, Sec. 11. Effective September 25, 1998.)
