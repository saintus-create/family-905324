# GOV § 12014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 12014

---

## Text

The Governor may require the Attorney General or the district attorney of any county to inquire into the affairs or management of any corporation existing under the laws of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
