# GOV § 8357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Drug-Free Workplace > Article: 2. State Contractors and Grantees

**Citation:** GOV § 8357

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be construed to require any contractor or grantee to ensure that other businesses with which it subcontracts also provide drug-free workplaces.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1170, Sec. 1.)
