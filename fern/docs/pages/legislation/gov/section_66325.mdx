# GOV § 66325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 13. Accessory Dwelling Units > Article: 2. Accessory Dwelling Unit Approvals

**Citation:** GOV § 66325

---

## Text

(a) Except as provided in subdivision (b), this article shall supersede a conflicting local ordinance.

(b) This article does not limit the authority of local agencies to adopt less restrictive requirements for the creation of an accessory dwelling unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 7, Sec. 20. (SB 477) Effective March 25, 2024.)
