# GOV § 65085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2.5. Transportation Planning and Programming

**Citation:** GOV § 65085

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may designate any county officer who is properly qualified to serve as the county director of transportation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1253.)
