# GOV § 6729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6729

---

## Text

The Governor annually shall proclaim April 10 as Dolores Huerta Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 130, Sec. 3. (AB 2644) Effective January 1, 2019.)
