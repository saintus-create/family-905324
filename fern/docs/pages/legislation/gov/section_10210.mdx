# GOV § 10210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 10210

---

## Text

Subject to Article 3 (commencing with Section 10270), neither the Legislative Counsel nor any employee of the bureau shall oppose or urge legislation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 16. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012.)
