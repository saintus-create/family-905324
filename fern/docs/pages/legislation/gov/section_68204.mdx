# GOV § 68204

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1.5. Compensation of Justices and Judges of Courts of Record

**Citation:** GOV § 68204

---

## Text

The justices and judges named in Sections 68200 to 68202, inclusive, shall not be deemed to be state officers for the purposes of Section 11569.
