# GOV § 29009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29009

---

## Text

In the recommended, adopted, and final budgets the funding sources shall equal the financing uses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 16. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
