# GOV § 52052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Community Energy Authority > Article: 3. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 52052

---

## Text

A majority of the commissioners constitutes a quorum for purposes of conducting the business of the authority. The commissioners shall act by majority vote thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1550, Sec. 1.)
