# GOV § 40548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40548

---

## Text

No ordinance, resolution or motion shall be passed or become effective without the affirmative votes of at least a majority of the members of the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
