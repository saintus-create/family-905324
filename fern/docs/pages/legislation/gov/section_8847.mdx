# GOV § 8847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. The Public Works Project Peer Review Act of 2013

**Citation:** GOV § 8847

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Public Works Project Peer Review Act of 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 252, Sec. 1. (SB 425) Effective January 1, 2014.)
