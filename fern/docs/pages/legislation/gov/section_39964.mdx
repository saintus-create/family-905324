# GOV § 39964

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 15. Rivers, Streams, and Harbors > Article: 3. Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 39964

---

## Text

Each contracting party may pay its proportionate share of the cost of acquiring and operating the ferry out of its general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
