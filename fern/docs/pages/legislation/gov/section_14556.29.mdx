# GOV § 14556.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. The Traffic Congestion Relief Act of 2000 > Article: 4. Administration and Expenditure of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 14556.29

---

## Text

The Controller shall develop a system that provides access to funds allocated by the commission under this article from the Traffic Congestion Relief Fund by electronic transfer of funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 656, Sec. 3. Effective September 26, 2000.)
