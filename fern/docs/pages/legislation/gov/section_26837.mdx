# GOV § 26837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26837

---

## Text

For comparing with the original on file in the office of the county clerk, the copy of any paper, record, or proceeding prepared by another and presented for his or her certificate, the fee is fifty cents ($0.50) a page, in addition to the fee for the certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 75, Sec. 80. Effective July 19, 2005. Operative January 1, 2006, by Sec. 156 of Ch. 75.)
