# GOV § 7061.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12.76. Local Tenant Preferences to Prevent Displacement Act

**Citation:** GOV § 7061.2

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2033, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 660, Sec. 2. (SB 649) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2033, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 12.76, commencing with Sec. 7061.)
