# GOV § 12100.102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 9.5. California Small Agricultural Business Drought and Flood Relief Grant Program

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.102

---

## Text

If the office determines that the grantee has failed to meet the criteria for a qualified small business under Section 12100.101, or for a small farmer or socially disadvantaged farmer under Section 12100.103, as may be applicable, a grant may be recaptured, in whole or in part, in accordance with Article 8 (commencing with Section 19294) of Chapter 5 of Part 10.2 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 570, Sec. 3. (AB 157) Effective September 27, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 12100.105.)
