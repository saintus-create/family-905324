# GOV § 970.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Payment of Judgments Against Local Public Entities > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 970.2

---

## Text

A local public entity shall pay any judgment in the manner provided in this article. A writ of mandate is an appropriate remedy to compel a local public entity to perform any act required by this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 285.)
