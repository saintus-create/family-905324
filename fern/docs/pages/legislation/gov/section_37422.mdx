# GOV § 37422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 3. Sale of Buildings and Sites

**Citation:** GOV § 37422

---

## Text

The resolution shall:

(a) Fix a time for hearing protests to the sale.

(b) Provide for publication of the notice of hearing.

(c) Fix the time final action will be taken.

(d) Contain an accurate description of the property to be sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
