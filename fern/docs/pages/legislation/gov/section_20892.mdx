# GOV § 20892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 20892

---

## Text

Service by a member in two or more school districts having governing boards composed of the same persons, shall be considered as though the service were rendered in one school district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
