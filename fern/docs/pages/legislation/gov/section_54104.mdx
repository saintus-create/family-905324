# GOV § 54104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4.5. Reconstruction of Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 54104

---

## Text

The legislative body may enter into, comply with, and do all things necessary to execute an agreement with the board which is required or authorized by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
