# GOV § 28057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Classification of Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 28057

---

## Text

Counties containing a population of 42,000 and under 44,000 are counties of the 36th class.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1204.)
