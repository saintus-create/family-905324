# GOV § 25006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Organization > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25006

---

## Text

Any member of the board may administer oaths when necessary in the performance of his official duties.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
