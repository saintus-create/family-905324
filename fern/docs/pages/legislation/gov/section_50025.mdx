# GOV § 50025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Legislative Bodies

**Citation:** GOV § 50025

---

## Text

By resolution, a legislative body may withdraw from the association at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
