# GOV § 53396.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.8. Infrastructure Financing Districts > Article: 3. Division of Taxes

**Citation:** GOV § 53396.5

---

## Text

All costs incurred by a county in connection with the division of taxes pursuant to Section 53396 for a district shall be paid by that district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1575, Sec. 1.)
