# GOV § 50366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Public Property > Article: 3. Issuance of Bonds for the Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** GOV § 50366

---

## Text

A local agency may exercise the right of eminent domain to acquire any property necessary or convenient to carry out this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1240.)
