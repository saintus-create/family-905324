# GOV § 23702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Charters > Article: 1. Enacting a Charter

**Citation:** GOV § 23702

---

## Text

Petitions to propose or revise a charter shall be subject to Article 1 (commencing with Section 9100) of Chapter 2 of Division 9 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 47. Effective January 1, 1995.)
