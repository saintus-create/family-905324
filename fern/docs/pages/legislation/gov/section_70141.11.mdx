# GOV § 70141.11

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 13. Court Commissioners

**Citation:** GOV § 70141.11

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 269 of the Code of Civil Procedure, any court reporting functions for the commissioner in Contra Costa County may be by electronic or mechanical means and devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 337. Effective January 1, 2003.)
