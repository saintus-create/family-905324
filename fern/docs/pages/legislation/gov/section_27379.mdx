# GOV § 27379

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27379

---

## Text

The fee for administering and certifying each oath or affirmation is fifty cents ($0.50).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
