# GOV § 11090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 6. Reports and Records

**Citation:** GOV § 11090

---

## Text

On order of the Governor the head of each State agency shall make a report to the Governor giving an account of all matters pertaining to the agency covering the period specified by the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
