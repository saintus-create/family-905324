# GOV § 77000

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 13. State Funding of Trial Courts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 77000

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Brown-Presley Trial Court Funding Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 945, Sec. 10. Effective September 16, 1988. Conditionally inoperative as provided in Section 77400.)
