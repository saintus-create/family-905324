# GOV § 14033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Activities

**Citation:** GOV § 14033

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2018, the department shall update the Highway Design Manual to incorporate the “complete streets” design concept.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 5, Sec. 3. (SB 1) Effective April 28, 2017.)
