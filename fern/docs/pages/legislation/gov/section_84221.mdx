# GOV § 84221

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 2. Filing of Campaign Statements

**Citation:** GOV § 84221

---

## Text

Slate mailer organizations shall terminate their filing obligations in the same manner as applies to committees qualifying under subdivision (a) of Section 82013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 905, Sec. 7.)
