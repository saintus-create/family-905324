# GOV § 29128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 6. Appropriations and Transfers

**Citation:** GOV § 29128

---

## Text

All emergency expenditures shall be paid from any money in the county treasury in any fund from which the expenditure may properly be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 59. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
