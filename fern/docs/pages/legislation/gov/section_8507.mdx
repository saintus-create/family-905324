# GOV § 8507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. The Milton Marks Commission on California State Government Organization and Economy > Article: 1. Creation and Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 8507

---

## Text

The commission shall select from among its members a chairman and vice chairman, who shall not be registered as members of the same political party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 8504 by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1625.)
