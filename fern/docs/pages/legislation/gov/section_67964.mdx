# GOV § 67964

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.96. NORTH LAKE TAHOE TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Creation of the Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 67964

---

## Text

The North Lake Tahoe Transportation Authority may be created upon approval of a resolution calling for the creation of the authority by a majority vote of the membership of the board of supervisors of the County of Placer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 771, Sec. 2. (AB 2920) Effective January 1, 2019.)
