# GOV § 45005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Service > Article: 1. The System

**Citation:** GOV § 45005

---

## Text

The ordinance shall designate the departments, and the appointive officers and employees who shall be included in the merit system or civil service system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
