# GOV § 8669.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. California Emergency Services Act > Article: 22. Law Enforcement Peer Support and Crisis Referral Services Program

**Citation:** GOV § 8669.1

---

## Text

The article shall be known, and may be cited, as the Law Enforcement Peer Support and Crisis Referral Services Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 621, Sec. 1. (AB 1117) Effective January 1, 2020.)
