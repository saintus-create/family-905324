# GOV § 31529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** GOV § 31529

---

## Text

The district attorney, or the county counsel if there is one, is the attorney for the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
