# GOV § 11250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Interagency Services and Transactions > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11250

---

## Text

Whenever a State agency supported from the General Fund renders services or furnishes materials to a State agency not supported from the General Fund, the cost of the services or materials is a charge against the fund from which is derived the support of the State agency receiving the services or materials.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
