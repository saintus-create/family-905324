# GOV § 7280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17. Employment of Veterans

**Citation:** GOV § 7280

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the “Burton-Stull Vietnam Veterans Employment Act.”
