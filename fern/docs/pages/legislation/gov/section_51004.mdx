# GOV § 51004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 51004

---

## Text

The petition for the determination of the boundary line shall be accompanied by documents, maps, or other exhibits as may be essential to a determination of the case.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
