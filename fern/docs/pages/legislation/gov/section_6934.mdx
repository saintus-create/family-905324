# GOV § 6934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 11.5. Governmental Investors

**Citation:** GOV § 6934

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply to contracts entered into, renewed, or extended on and after January 1, 1991.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 709, Sec. 1.)
