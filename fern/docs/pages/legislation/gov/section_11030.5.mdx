# GOV § 11030.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 3. Traveling Expenses

**Citation:** GOV § 11030.5

---

## Text

Any state officer or officer or employee of any state agency may, when traveling on state business, fly in state-owned or leased civil aircraft as a passenger or as part of the crew.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1612.)
