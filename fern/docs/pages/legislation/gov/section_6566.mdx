# GOV § 6566

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 6566

---

## Text

The indenture may provide for the rights, liabilities, powers and duties arising upon the entity’s breach of any covenants, conditions or obligations of the indenture.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 329.)
