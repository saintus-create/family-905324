# GOV § 54126

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 5. Earthquake Relief

**Citation:** GOV § 54126

---

## Text

Upon the application of the legislative body of a local agency the board may loan funds to a local agency for the replacement, repair, or reconstruction of any public building or any public facility of such local agency which has been damaged or destroyed by earthquake after July 1, 1952, as provided in Sections 54129 and 54130, or the board may replace, repair, or reconstruct any public building or any public facility of a local agency which has been damaged or destroyed by earthquake after July 1, 1952, as provided in Sections 54127 and 54128.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1952, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 21.)
