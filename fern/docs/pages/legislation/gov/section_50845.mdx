# GOV § 50845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Police Officers’ Pension Fund and Firemen’s Pension Fund > Article: 2. Organization, Powers, and Duties of the Board

**Citation:** GOV § 50845

---

## Text

Upon proper vouchers, payments pursuant to this chapter shall be made quarterly from the police relief and pension fund and monthly from the firemen’s relief and pension fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
