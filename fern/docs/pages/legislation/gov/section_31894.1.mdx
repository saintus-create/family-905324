# GOV § 31894.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 17.5. Termination of Social Security in Counties with a Population Exceeding 6,000,000

**Citation:** GOV § 31894.1

---

## Text

This article shall apply only in a county with a population exceeding 6,000,000 and shall apply in that county only if the board of supervisors has provided notice of intent to terminate benefits under federal social security.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1567, Sec. 1.)
