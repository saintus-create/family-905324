# GOV § 6101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 6101

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged in proceedings upon habeas corpus.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
