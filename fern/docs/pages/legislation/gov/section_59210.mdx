# GOV § 59210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 3. Resolution of Intention

**Citation:** GOV § 59210

---

## Text

Before ordering the acquisition and cancellation of any bonds, the legislative body shall adopt a resolution declaring its intention to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
