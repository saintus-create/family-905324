# GOV § 53984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 17. Community Facilities Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 53984

---

## Text

Any local agency may adopt a plan which shall include, but not be limited to, a component identifying public facilities needed for development within a defined area and a component describing the method of financing construction of such public facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 396, Sec. 1. Effective July 27, 1983.)
