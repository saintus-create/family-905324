# GOV § 41802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 7. City Attorney

**Citation:** GOV § 41802

---

## Text

The city attorney shall frame an ordinance or resolution required by the legislative body.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 210, Sec. 4. (SB 184) Effective January 1, 2014.)
