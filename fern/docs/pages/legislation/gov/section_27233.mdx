# GOV § 27233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 2. Books

**Citation:** GOV § 27233

---

## Text

The recorder shall keep an index of deeds, labeled “Grantees,” each page divided into three columns, headed respectively: “Names of grantees,” “Names of grantors,” and “Where recorded.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
