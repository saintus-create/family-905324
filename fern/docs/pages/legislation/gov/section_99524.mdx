# GOV § 99524

**Hierarchy:** Title: 20. INTERNATIONAL RELATIONS > Chapter: 2. California-Mexico Border Relations Council

**Citation:** GOV § 99524

---

## Text

Beginning January 1, 2008, the council shall submit a report to the Legislature on the council’s activities annually.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 716, Sec. 3. (AB 690) Effective January 1, 2015.)
