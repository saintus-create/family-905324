# GOV § 6004.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 6004.5

---

## Text

In order to qualify as a newspaper of general circulation the newspaper, if either printed or published in a town or city, shall be both printed and published in one and the same town or city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 937.)
