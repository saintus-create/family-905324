# GOV § 43746

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43746

---

## Text

The report shall be made at least once a month, and shall be accompanied by the bonds, warrants, judgments, or other evidences of indebtedness which have been taken up and canceled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
