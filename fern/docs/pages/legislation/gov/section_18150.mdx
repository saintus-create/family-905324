# GOV § 18150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Oaths for State Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 18150

---

## Text

The oath required by this chapter is the oath set forth in Section 3 of Article XX of the Constitution of California.
