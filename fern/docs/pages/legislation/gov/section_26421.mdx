# GOV § 26421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 15. Revenue Bonds for County Incinerators > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26421

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause relating to the bonds issued thereunder requiring the board to make all needful and necessary repairs, renewals and replacements to any project and to keep it at all times in good repair, working order and condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 822.)
