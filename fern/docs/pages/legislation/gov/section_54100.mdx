# GOV § 54100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 4.5. Reconstruction of Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 54100

---

## Text

The board may fix the rental to be paid by the local agency for such buildings and the terms and manner of the payment of the rental.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
