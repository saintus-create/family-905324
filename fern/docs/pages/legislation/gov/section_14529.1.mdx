# GOV § 14529.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 14529.1

---

## Text

The commission shall establish guidelines for the allocation of funds to an entity for a project to verify that the entity has the resources and capabilities to implement the project in a timely manner and may establish a process for monitoring the progress being made and proper use of funds. The guidelines and process shall be kept to the minimum needed to protect state funds and provide for a timely use of those funds. The commission shall request that the entity receiving funds accept an audit of funds allocated to it by the commission if an audit is deemed necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 622, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1998.)
