# GOV § 29908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29908

---

## Text

If two-thirds of the electors voting on a proposition vote in favor of it, the bonds in an amount not exceeding that specified in said proposition may be issued. When two or more propositions are submitted at the same election, the votes cast for and against each proposition shall be separately counted.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 141.)
