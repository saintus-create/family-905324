# GOV § 21390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13. Retirement Benefits > Article: 4. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 21390

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 21362, 21362.2, 21363, 21363.1, 21369, 21370, and 21389, for local safety members who retire on or after January 1, 2002, and with respect to all local safety service rendered to a contracting agency that is subject to any of those sections, the benefit limit shall be 90 percent of final compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 796, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
