# GOV § 23277

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23277

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by this article, the election shall be conducted as other elections in the transferring county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
