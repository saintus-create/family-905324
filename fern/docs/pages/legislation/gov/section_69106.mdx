# GOV § 69106

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 4. The Courts of Appeal > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 69106

---

## Text

The Court of Appeal for the Sixth Appellate District consists of one division having seven judges and shall hold its regular sessions at San Jose.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 998, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
