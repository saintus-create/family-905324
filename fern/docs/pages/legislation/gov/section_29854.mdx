# GOV § 29854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 3. Duplicate Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 29854

---

## Text

The auditor and treasurer shall each make the proper entries on their respective books, showing that the warrant is lost or destroyed and that a duplicate warrant has been issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
