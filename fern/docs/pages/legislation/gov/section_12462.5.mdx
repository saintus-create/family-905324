# GOV § 12462.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 4. Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 12462.5

---

## Text

The Controller shall include the following information on bank accounts and savings and loan association accounts outside the treasury system in the budgetary-legal basis annual report, submitted pursuant to Section 12460:

(a) The name of the account.

(b) The source of authorization for establishing the account.

(c) The account balance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 230, Sec. 1. (AB 1583) Effective January 1, 2015.)
