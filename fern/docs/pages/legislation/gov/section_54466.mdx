# GOV § 54466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 5. Additional Powers to Secure Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54466

---

## Text

The legislative body may also specifically provide that the proceeds of insurance against loss of revenue shall be used solely for the payment and retirement of the bonds issued for acquisition, construction, or improvement of the enterprise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
