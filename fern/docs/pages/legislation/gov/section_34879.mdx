# GOV § 34879

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. ORGANIZATION AND BOUNDARIES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Alternative Forms of Government > Article: 2. Election of Legislative Body By or From Districts in Cities

**Citation:** GOV § 34879

---

## Text

The term of office of members of the legislative body elected pursuant to the provisions of this article shall be four years, unless otherwise expressly provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 278.)
