# GOV § 13903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 13903

## Text

The Secretary of Government Operations shall serve as chair of the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 248. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
