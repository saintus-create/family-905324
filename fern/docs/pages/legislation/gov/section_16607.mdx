# GOV § 16607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Savings and Loan Association Deposits > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16607

---

## Text

The Treasurer is not responsible for any money deposited in a savings and loan association or credit union pursuant to this chapter, and while it remains so deposited.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1035, Sec. 8.)
