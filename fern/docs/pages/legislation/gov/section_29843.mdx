# GOV § 29843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. Warrants > Article: 2.5. Optional Method—Registering Claims or Orders

**Citation:** GOV § 29843

---

## Text

Warrants shall be issued by the county auditor in payment of registered claims or orders from the first moneys thereafter becoming available in the fund and before any other claims or orders are paid from the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 294, Sec. 2.)
