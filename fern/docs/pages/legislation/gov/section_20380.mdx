# GOV § 20380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Membership Classifications > Article: 2. Miscellaneous Member Classification

**Citation:** GOV § 20380

---

## Text

“State miscellaneous member” includes all members employed by the state and university, except National Guard, industrial, patrol, state peace officer/firefighter, and state safety members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 355, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2008.)
