# GOV § 425.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.16

## Text

Bigberry manzanita (Arctostaphylos glauca) is the official state shrub.
