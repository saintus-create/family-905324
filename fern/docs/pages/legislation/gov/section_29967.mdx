# GOV § 29967

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 3. Special Improvement Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 29967

---

## Text

The board may make any issue of bonds issued pursuant to this article mature at one time, not to exceed 20 years after the date of issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
