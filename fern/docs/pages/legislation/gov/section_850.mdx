# GOV § 850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Fire Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 850

---

## Text

Neither a public entity nor a public employee is liable for failure to establish a fire department or otherwise to provide fire protection service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
