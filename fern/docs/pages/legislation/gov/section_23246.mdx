# GOV § 23246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23246

---

## Text

A resolution to initiate proceedings for a county boundary change shall contain the information required for a petition by Section 23234.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
