# GOV § 12020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 2.5. Budget Reports

**Citation:** GOV § 12020

---

## Text

The budget submitted by the Governor to the Legislature within the first 10 days of each regular session shall contain a statement of accounts payable for the previous fiscal year and an estimate of accounts payable for the current fiscal year and the succeeding fiscal year.

The statements with respect to accounts payable shall be classified to show major expenditure classifications.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 980, Sec. 3.)
