# GOV § 13995.93

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 9. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.93

---

## Text

The approved marketing plan of the commission shall be no less than fifty million dollars ($50,000,000) per fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 320, Sec. 3. (SB 1119) Effective January 1, 2015.)
