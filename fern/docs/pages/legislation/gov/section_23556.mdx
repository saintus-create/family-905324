# GOV § 23556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** GOV § 23556

---

## Text

Candidates for elective offices of the consolidated county shall file nomination papers at the same time and in the same manner as candidates for the governing body of general law counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
