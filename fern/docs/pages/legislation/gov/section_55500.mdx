# GOV § 55500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Joint Cement Projects

**Citation:** GOV § 55500

---

## Text

Counties, cities, and irrigation districts may jointly or severally purchase, lease, or otherwise acquire and operate, manage, and control rock quarries, rock plants, sand pits, cement plants, and other works or projects for the extraction, manufacture, or preparation of rock, sand, cement, and other materials used by them in performing their functions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
