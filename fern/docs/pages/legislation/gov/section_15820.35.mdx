# GOV § 15820.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.7. Libraries and Related Educational Facilities in Public Universities > Article: 2. Library Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 15820.35

---

## Text

The board is authorized to, and may, finance the construction or renovation, and the equipping, of public buildings or facilities on a site or sites within the University of California, the California State University, the California Maritime Academy, and the California Community College districts, as agreed upon with the Regents of the University of California, the Trustees of the California State University, the Board of Governors of the California Maritime Academy, and the California Community College districts, respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 836, Sec. 1.)
