# GOV § 25556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Parks and Recreation > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 25556

---

## Text

The aid shall be used by the city within one year from the time it is extended by the county and any sum or property remaining at the end of the year shall be returned to the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
