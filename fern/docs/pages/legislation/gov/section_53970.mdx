# GOV § 53970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 16. Police and Fire Protection Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53970

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means any city, county, or city and county, or a district, public authority, or any other political subdivision in the state.
