# GOV § 18535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18535

---

## Text

“Departmental reemployment list” means a list established for the reemployment of persons in a particular class in a particular State agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
