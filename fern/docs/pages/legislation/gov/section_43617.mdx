# GOV § 43617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43617

---

## Text

The legislative body shall prescribe the form of the bonds and interest coupons, and fix the date of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
