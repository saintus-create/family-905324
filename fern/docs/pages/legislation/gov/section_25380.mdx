# GOV § 25380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 2. Jails in Other Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 25380

---

## Text

Pursuant to this article, the board of supervisors may purchase, receive by donation, lease, or otherwise acquire real or personal property in another county necessary for the use of the county for a jail.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
