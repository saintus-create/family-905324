# GOV § 14521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 14521

---

## Text

The commission may request and review reports of the department and of other entities which pertain to transportation issues and concerns that the commission determines need special study.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1106.)
