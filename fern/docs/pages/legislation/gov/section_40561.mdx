# GOV § 40561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 5. Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 40561

---

## Text

Each authority has the power generally to perform all acts necessary to carry out fully the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
