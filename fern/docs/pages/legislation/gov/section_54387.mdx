# GOV § 54387

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 3. Authorization of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54387

---

## Text

If the issuance of the bonds is authorized at the election, the legislative body shall provide for their issuance and sale by resolution to take effect upon adoption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
