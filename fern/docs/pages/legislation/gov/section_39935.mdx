# GOV § 39935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 15. Rivers, Streams, and Harbors > Article: 2. Establishment of Harbor Lines by Cities Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 39935

---

## Text

The authority granted by this article may be exercised by a city over, along, or upon the water frontage or tidelands which it owns or which one claimed or possessed by any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
