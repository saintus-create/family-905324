# GOV § 12606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Attorney General > Article: 8. Environmental Actions

**Citation:** GOV § 12606

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall be permitted to intervene in any judicial or administrative proceeding in which facts are alleged concerning pollution or adverse environmental effects which could affect the public generally.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1518.)
