# GOV § 12973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Enforcement and Hearing Procedures > Article: 1. Unlawful Practices

**Citation:** GOV § 12973

---

## Text

Within one year of the effective date of every final order or decision issued pursuant to this part, the department shall conduct a compliance review to determine whether the order or decision has been fully obeyed and implemented.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 52. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012. Operative January 1, 2013, by Sec. 140 of Ch. 46.)
