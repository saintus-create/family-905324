# GOV § 58095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58095

---

## Text

If the supervising authority determines that the petition does not comply with the law, the matter may be dismissed without prejudice to the right to present a new petition on the same matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
