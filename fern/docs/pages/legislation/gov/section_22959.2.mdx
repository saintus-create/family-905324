# GOV § 22959.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Part: 6.1. VISION CARE PROGRAM FOR STATE ANNUITANTS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Recovery of Medical Costs

**Citation:** GOV § 22959.2

## Text

The Vision Care Program for State Annuitants shall be administered by the Department of Human Resources.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 160. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
