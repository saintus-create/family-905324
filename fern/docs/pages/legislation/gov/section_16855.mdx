# GOV § 16855

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Minority and Women Business Participation Goals for Professional Bond Services

**Citation:** GOV § 16855

---

## Text

Beginning July 1, 1989, and on January 1, 1990, and on January 1 of each year thereafter, each awarding department shall report to the Governor and the Legislature on the level of participation by minority and women business enterprises in contracts as identified in this chapter. If the established goals are not met, the awarding department shall report the reasons for its inability to achieve the goals and identify steps it shall take in an effort to achieve the goals.

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 61, Sec. 1.)
