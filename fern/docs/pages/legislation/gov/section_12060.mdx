# GOV § 12060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Governor > Article: 6. Succession to the Office of Governor in the Event of War or Enemy-Caused Disaster

**Citation:** GOV § 12060

---

## Text

As used in this article “disaster” means a war or enemy-caused calamity occurring in the State of California, such as an attack by nuclear weapons, as a result of which the incumbent Governor is either killed, missing, or so seriously injured as to be unable to perform the Governor’s duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 20. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
