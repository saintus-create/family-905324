# GOV § 17204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17204

---

## Text

Any State agency that is authorized to invest funds in the treasury in securities which are legal investments for savings banks may invest the funds in registered warrants of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
