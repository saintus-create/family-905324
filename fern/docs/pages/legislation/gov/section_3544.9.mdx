# GOV § 3544.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.7. Meeting and Negotiating in Public Educational Employment > Article: 5. Employee Organizations: Representation, Recognition, Certification, and Decertification

**Citation:** GOV § 3544.9

---

## Text

The employee organization recognized or certified as the exclusive representative for the purpose of meeting and negotiating shall fairly represent each and every employee in the appropriate unit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 961.)
