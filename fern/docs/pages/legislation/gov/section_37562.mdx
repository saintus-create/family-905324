# GOV § 37562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 6. Museums

**Citation:** GOV § 37562

---

## Text

Subject to rules and regulations of the board, the museum shall be free to the inhabitants and nonresident taxpayers of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
