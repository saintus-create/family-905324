# GOV § 9501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Enactment of Statutes and Adoption of Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9501.5

---

## Text

The enacting clause of every law shall be “The people of the State of California do enact as follows:”.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 161.)
