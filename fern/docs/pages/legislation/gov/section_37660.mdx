# GOV § 37660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 8. Hospitals Managed by City Legislative Body

**Citation:** GOV § 37660

---

## Text

Annually the legislative body shall levy and collect a property tax for:

(a) The maintenance of the hospital.

(b) The payment of interest upon any indebtedness created pursuant to this article.

(c) A sinking fund for the payment of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
