# GOV § 12100.65

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 7. California Small Business Development Technical Assistance Expansion Act of 2018

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.65

---

## Text

Upon appropriation of funds by the Legislature for the purpose of implementing this article, the office shall make grants to small business technical assistance centers, consistent with the requirements of Section 12100.63.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 74, Sec. 13. (SB 151) Effective July 12, 2021.)
