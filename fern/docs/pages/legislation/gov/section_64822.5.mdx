# GOV § 64822.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.9. LOS ANGELES COUNTY AFFORDABLE HOUSING SOLUTIONS AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Revenue > Article: 2. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 64822.5

---

## Text

The agency may issue mortgage revenue bonds pursuant to Part 5 (commencing with Section 52000) of Division 31 of the Health and Safety Code, and other applicable law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 661, Sec. 1. (SB 679) Effective January 1, 2023.)
