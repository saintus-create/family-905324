# GOV § 19815.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Department of Human Resources > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19815.25

---

## Text

The Department of Human Resources, as established on July 1, 2012, is hereby established within the Government Operations Agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 302. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
