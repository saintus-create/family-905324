# GOV § 14507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 14507

---

## Text

The commission shall not form a committee for the purpose of considering budgetary and related fiscal matters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1106.)
