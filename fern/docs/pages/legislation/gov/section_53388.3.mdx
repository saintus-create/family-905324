# GOV § 53388.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 5. Senior Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53388.3

---

## Text

The county shall, if requested by the legislative body of a district created within the boundaries of a city, conduct the election for the district. The district shall pay the county for costs incurred by the county in conducting the election, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
