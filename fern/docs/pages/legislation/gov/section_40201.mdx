# GOV § 40201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 40201

---

## Text

By resolution, the legislative body may declare its intention to cause a census to be taken by suitable persons appointed by it and at the city’s expense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
