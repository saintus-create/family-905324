# GOV § 65060.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65060.8

---

## Text

A regional plan shall be advisory only and shall not have any binding effect on the counties and cities located within the boundaries of the regional planning district for which the regional plan is adopted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
