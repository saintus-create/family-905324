# GOV § 65039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 3. Establishment and Functions of the Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation

**Citation:** GOV § 65039

---

## Text

The Governor may appoint the Director of Land Use and Climate Innovation at a salary that shall be fixed pursuant to Section 12001.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 41, Sec. 48. (SB 164) Effective June 29, 2024.)
