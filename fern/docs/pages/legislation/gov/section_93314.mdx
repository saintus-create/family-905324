# GOV § 93314

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12.5. CENTRAL CALIFORNIA RAILROAD AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of the Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 93314

---

## Text

The authority may prepare a plan for the acquisition and operation of any railroad line specified in Section 93301, at no expense to the state, to achieve the purposes set forth in Section 93302.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 234, Sec. 1. (SB 325) Effective January 1, 2012.)
