# GOV § 54141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 6. Federal Surplus Property

**Citation:** GOV § 54141

---

## Text

As used in this article:

(a) “Local agency” means county, city, municipal corporation, or public district.

(b) “United States” includes any department, board, or agency thereof.

(c) “State” includes any department or agency thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
