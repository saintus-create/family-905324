# GOV § 26743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26743

---

## Text

The fee for subpoenaing a witness, including a copy of the subpoena and any affidavit required to be served therewith, shall be the amount described in Section 26720.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 629, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2001.)
