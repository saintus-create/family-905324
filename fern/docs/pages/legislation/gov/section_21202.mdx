# GOV § 21202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 7. Reinstatement from Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 21202

---

## Text

A person employed in violation of Section 21220 may be reinstated to membership in the category in which, and on the date on which, the unlawful employment occurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 136, Sec. 1. (SB 411) Effective January 1, 2022.)
