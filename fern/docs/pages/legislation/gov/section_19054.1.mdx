# GOV § 19054.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19054.1

---

## Text

When an examination for a managerial position is conducted on an open and promotional basis, the names of eligibles shall be placed on one list and ranked in relative order of the examination scores received.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 53, Sec. 26. (SB 866) Effective June 27, 2018.)
