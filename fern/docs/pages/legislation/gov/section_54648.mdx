# GOV § 54648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 10. Remedies of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 54648

---

## Text

Every substantive right and remedy conferred upon the bondholders or their trustee may be enforced and exercised as often as is deemed expedient.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
