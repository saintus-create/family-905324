# GOV § 25200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 25200

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may divide the county into election, road, and supervisorial districts and change their boundaries as convenience requires.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 454, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2003.)
