# GOV § 31530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 3. Retirement Board

**Citation:** GOV § 31530

---

## Text

The county health officer, either directly or through a duly authorized representative, shall advise the board on medical matters and, if requested by the board, shall attend its meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 186, Sec. 12. (SB 634) Effective January 1, 2022.)
