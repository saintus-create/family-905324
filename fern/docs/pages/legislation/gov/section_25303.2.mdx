# GOV § 25303.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 25303.2

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, negotiable paper means bank checks and drafts and express and post-office money orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 947.)
