# GOV § 4529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PUBLIC WORK AND PUBLIC PURCHASES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10. Contracts with Private Architects, Engineering, Land Surveying, and Construction Project Management Firms

**Citation:** GOV § 4529

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply where the state or local agency head determines that the services needed are more of a technical nature and involve little professional judgment and that requiring bids would be in the public interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1016, Sec. 5.)
