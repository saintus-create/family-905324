# GOV § 50745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50745

---

## Text

Interest on bonds may be paid out of the proceeds of the sale of the bonds during the actual construction of any project for the acquisition, construction or completion of which the bonds have been issued, and for a period of not to exceed two (2) years thereafter as provided for in the indenture.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
