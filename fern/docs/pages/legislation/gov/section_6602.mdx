# GOV § 6602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 6. Benefit Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 6602

---

## Text

If the charge is not paid, a lien to secure the payment of the charge accrues to the State. The lien may be foreclosed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 587.)
