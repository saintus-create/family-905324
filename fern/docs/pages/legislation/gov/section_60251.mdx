# GOV § 60251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. District Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 60251

---

## Text

In this chapter, “assessor” means the assessor of a district, or any other officer who makes assessments for the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1036.)
