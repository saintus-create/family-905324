# GOV § 66478.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4. Requirements > Article: 3.5. Public Access to Public Resources

**Citation:** GOV § 66478.9

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 66478.5 shall apply to the site of electric power generating facilities.

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1536.)
