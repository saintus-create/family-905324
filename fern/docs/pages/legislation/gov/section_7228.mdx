# GOV § 7228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 14.5. Registration of State Tax Liens

**Citation:** GOV § 7228

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “state tax” includes a local tax which is administered or collected by a state agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 915.)
