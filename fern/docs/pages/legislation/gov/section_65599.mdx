# GOV § 65599

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 10.8. Water Conservation in Landscaping

**Citation:** GOV § 65599

---

## Text

Any actions or proceedings to attach, review, set aside, void, or annul the act, decision, or findings of a local agency on the ground of noncompliance with this article shall be brought pursuant to Section 1085 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 559, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
