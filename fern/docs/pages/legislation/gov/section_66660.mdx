# GOV § 66660

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.2. SAN FRANCISCO BAY CONSERVATION AND DEVELOPMENT COMMISSION > Chapter: 5. The San Francisco Bay Plan and Further Reports of the Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 66660

---

## Text

The commission shall make a supplemental report, or reports, containing all of the following:

(a) The results of any continued or further studies made by the commission;

(b) Such other information and recommendations as the commission deems desirable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 713.)
