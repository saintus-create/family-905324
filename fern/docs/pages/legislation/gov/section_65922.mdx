# GOV § 65922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 65922

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not apply to the following:

(a) Activities of the State Energy Resources Development and Conservation Commission established pursuant to Division 15 (commencing with Section 25000) of the Public Resources Code.

(b) Administrative appeals within a state or local agency or to a state or local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 87, Sec. 2. Effective March 1, 1982.)
