# GOV § 54411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54411

---

## Text

Bonds bearing the signature of officers of the local agency in office on the date of the signing are valid, notwithstanding that before delivery the persons signing have ceased to be officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
