# GOV § 26747

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26747

---

## Text

For transporting prisoners to the county jail, the sheriff shall charge the actual cost of such transportation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
