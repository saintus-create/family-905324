# GOV § 3599.63

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12.5. Legislature Employer-Employee Relations

**Citation:** GOV § 3599.63

---

## Text

If an agreement is reached between the employer and the recognized employee organization, the parties shall jointly prepare a written memorandum of understanding reflecting the terms of the agreement, which shall be presented, when appropriate, to the employer for adoption as a resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 313, Sec. 1. (AB 1) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative July 1, 2026, pursuant to Sec. 3599.84.)
