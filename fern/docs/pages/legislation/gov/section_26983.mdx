# GOV § 26983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4.6. Consolidated Office of Director of Finance

**Citation:** GOV § 26983

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of every county having the office of director of finance shall cause an annual, independent audit to be made of such office. The board of supervisors may request that the grand jury select such independent auditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 638.)
