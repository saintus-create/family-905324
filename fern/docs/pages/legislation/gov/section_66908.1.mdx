# GOV § 66908.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.42. CALIFORNIA TAHOE CONSERVANCY > Chapter: 6. Finances

**Citation:** GOV § 66908.1

---

## Text

The conservancy may fix and collect fees for any services rendered by it. The amount of any fee may not exceed the reasonable costs of providing the services rendered by the conservancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1239, Sec. 19. Effective September 17, 1984.)
