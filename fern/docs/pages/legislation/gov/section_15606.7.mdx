# GOV § 15606.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15606.7

---

## Text

Training of assessors and their staffs under Sections 15606 and 15608 shall be provided by the board on a nonreimbursable basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 15606.5 (as added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1167) by Stats. 2013, Ch. 76, Sec. 89. (AB 383) Effective January 1, 2014.)
