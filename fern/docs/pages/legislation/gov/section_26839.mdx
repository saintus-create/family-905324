# GOV § 26839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26839

---

## Text

For an exemplification of a record or other paper on file, the fee is twenty dollars ($20) and the charges allowed for copying or comparing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 158, Sec. 9. Effective July 21, 1993.)
