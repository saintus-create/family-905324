# GOV § 21701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 18. California Public Employees Retirement System School Employees Alternative System

**Citation:** GOV § 21701

---

## Text

The plan shall be designed and implemented to comply with pertinent provisions of the federal Internal Revenue Code and federal Internal Revenue Service regulations and guidelines.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 951, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1998.)
