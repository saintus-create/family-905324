# GOV § 38415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38415

---

## Text

The legislative body may employ and fix the compensation of such legal or other assistants as it deems necessary to sell the land for the best advantage to the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
