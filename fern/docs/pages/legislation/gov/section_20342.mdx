# GOV § 20342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Membership in System > Article: 4. Termination of Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 20342

---

## Text

Until his or her return to state service any member absent on military service may resign from this system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
