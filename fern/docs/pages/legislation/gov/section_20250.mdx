# GOV § 20250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration of System > Article: 8. Subrogation

**Citation:** GOV § 20250

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall be deemed to create a right of subrogation only to amounts paid as disability retirement allowances and special death benefits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
