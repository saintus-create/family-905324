# GOV § 19243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Limited Examination and Appointment Program > Article: 2. Examinations and Appointments

**Citation:** GOV § 19243

---

## Text

Upon successful completion of the job examination period, the candidate shall have qualified in the examination. The appointing power shall appoint the candidate, without further examination, to an appropriate position where permanent status may accumulate. A candidate appointed in this way is not required to serve a probationary period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 53, Sec. 29. (SB 866) Effective June 27, 2018.)
