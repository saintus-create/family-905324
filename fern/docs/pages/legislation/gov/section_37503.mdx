# GOV § 37503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 5. Assembly or Convention Halls

**Citation:** GOV § 37503

---

## Text

In addition to other required recitations, the ordinance calling the election shall recite the rate of interest to be paid on the indebtedness.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
