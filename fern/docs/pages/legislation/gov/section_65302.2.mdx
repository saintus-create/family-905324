# GOV § 65302.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 3. Local Planning > Article: 5. Authority for and Scope of General Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 65302.2

---

## Text

Upon the adoption, or revision, of a city or county’s general plan, on or after January 1, 1996, the city or county shall utilize as a source document any urban water management plan submitted to the city or county by a water agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 881, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
