# GOV § 40809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 2. City Clerk

**Citation:** GOV § 40809

## Text

This article does not prevent the proof of the passage and publication of ordinances in the usual way.
