# GOV § 43735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43735

---

## Text

The ordinance calling the election shall recite the object and purposes for which the bonded indebtedness is to be incurred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
