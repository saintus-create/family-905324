# GOV § 29326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 2. Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29326

---

## Text

The officer shall not be authorized to expend any portion of the revolving fund except for services or material which are a legal charge against the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
