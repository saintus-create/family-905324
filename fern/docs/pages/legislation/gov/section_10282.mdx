# GOV § 10282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Counsel > Article: 3. Commission on Uniform State Laws

**Citation:** GOV § 10282

---

## Text

The commission shall report to the Legislature from time to time as the commission deems desirable and practicable, giving an account of its transactions and its advice and recommendations for legislation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 46, Sec. 17. (SB 1038) Effective June 27, 2012.)
