# GOV § 24002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. County Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 24002

---

## Text

A person is not eligible to the office of district attorney unless he has been admitted to practice in the Supreme Court of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
