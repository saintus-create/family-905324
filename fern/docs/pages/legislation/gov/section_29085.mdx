# GOV § 29085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 4. Adopted Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29085

---

## Text

The budget for each fund may contain nonspendable, restricted, committed, or assigned fund balance classifications in such amounts as the board deems sufficient. General reserves and stabilization arrangements may also be included as part of the restricted, committed, assigned, and unassigned fund balance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 382, Sec. 1.8. (SB 194) Effective January 1, 2012.)
