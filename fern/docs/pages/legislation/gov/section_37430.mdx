# GOV § 37430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 3. Sale of Buildings and Sites

**Citation:** GOV § 37430

---

## Text

If a legislative body sold property prior to May 23, 1945, by a proceeding substantially complying with this article, the sale is as effective as if it had occurred after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
