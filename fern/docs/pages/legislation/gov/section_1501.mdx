# GOV § 1501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 3. Form and Conditions

**Citation:** GOV § 1501

---

## Text

The condition of an official bond shall be that the principal will well, truly, and faithfully perform all official duties then required of him by law, and also all such additional duties as may be imposed on him by any existing law of the State or law enacted subsequently to the execution of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
