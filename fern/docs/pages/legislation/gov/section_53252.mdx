# GOV § 53252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 3. Firemen’s Vacations

**Citation:** GOV § 53252

---

## Text

Deduction shall not be made from the salary or pay of a member of the department granted such a leave of absence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
