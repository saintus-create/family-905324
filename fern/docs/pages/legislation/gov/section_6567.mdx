# GOV § 6567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

## Text

The indenture may provide for a fiscal agent and the deposit of funds therewith.
