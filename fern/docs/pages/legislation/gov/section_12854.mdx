# GOV § 12854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 12854

---

## Text

Whenever a power is granted to the secretary of an agency, the power may be exercised by such officer or employee within the agency as designated in writing by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 138.)
