# GOV § 67844

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.86. THE MILITARY BASE REUSE AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 67844

---

## Text

The board may enter into contracts and agreements as necessary to mitigate any impacts of the reuse of the military base.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1165, Sec. 1. Effective September 30, 1994.)
