# GOV § 16300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16300

---

## Text

The General Fund consists of money received into the treasury and not required by law to be credited to any other fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
