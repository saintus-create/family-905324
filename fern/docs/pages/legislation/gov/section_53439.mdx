# GOV § 53439

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Mutilated or Defaced Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53439

---

## Text

An exchange shall not be made unless the defaced or mutilated bond with any coupons attached is identifiable and is first surrendered by the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
