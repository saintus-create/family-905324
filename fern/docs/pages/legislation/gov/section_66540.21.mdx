# GOV § 66540.21

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.21

---

## Text

The board shall determine what water transportation services facilities should be acquired or constructed for the common benefit of the bay area region as a whole.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
