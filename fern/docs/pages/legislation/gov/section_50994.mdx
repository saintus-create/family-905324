# GOV § 50994

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4.6. The Short-Term Rental Facilitator Act of 2025

**Citation:** GOV § 50994

---

## Text

A short-term rental facilitator shall include in the listing of a short-term rental any applicable local license number associated with the short-term rental and any transient occupancy tax certification issued by a local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 751, Sec. 2. (SB 346) Effective January 1, 2026.)
