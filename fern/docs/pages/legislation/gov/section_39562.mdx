# GOV § 39562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 13. Weed and Rubbish Abatement > Article: 2. Alternative Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 39562

---

## Text

The resolution shall:

(a) Refer to the street by its commonly known name.

(b) Describe the property upon which or in front of which the nuisance exists by giving its lot and block number according to the official or city assessment map.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
