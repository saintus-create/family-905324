# GOV § 50667.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 4. Limited Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50667.2

---

## Text

In the resolution authorizing the bonds, the legislative body may insert any of the provisions authorized by this article, which shall become a part of the contract with the bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 320, Sec. 2.)
