# GOV § 50287

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 12. Historical Property Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 50287

---

## Text

As an alternative to cancellation of the contract for breach of any condition, a landowner that is a party to the contract may bring any action in court necessary to enforce a contract, including, but not limited to, an action to enforce the contract by specific performance or injunction.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 278, Sec. 5. (AB 654) Effective January 1, 2012.)
