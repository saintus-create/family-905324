# GOV § 25216.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2.5. County Service Areas > Article: 7. Capital Financing

**Citation:** GOV § 25216.2

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may finance any enterprise and issue revenue bonds pursuant to the Revenue Bond Law of 1941 (Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 54300) of Part 1 of Division 2 of Title 5), and a county service area shall be deemed a “local agency” for the purposes of that chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 158, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
