# GOV § 25628

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Public Resources > Article: 2. Forestry and Trees

**Citation:** GOV § 25628

---

## Text

The county board of forestry may also employ, or authorize the county forester to employ, a deputy county forester.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
