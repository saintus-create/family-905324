# GOV § 95004

**Hierarchy:** Title: 14. CALIFORNIA EARLY INTERVENTION SERVICES ACT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 95004

## Text

This title shall become operative upon the repeal of Title 14 (commencing with Section 95000) as added by Section 2 of Chapter 945 of the Statutes of 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 945, Sec. 4) by Stats. 1994, Ch. 146, Sec. 86. Effective January 1, 1995.)
