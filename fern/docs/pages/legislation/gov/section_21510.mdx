# GOV § 21510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 14. Death Benefits > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 21510

---

## Text

Any payment of benefits by warrant issued after the date of death of the benefit recipient shall be refunded to the retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 506, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
