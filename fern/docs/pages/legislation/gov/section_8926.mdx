# GOV § 8926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 2. Code of Ethics

**Citation:** GOV § 8926

---

## Text

Every person who knowingly and willfully violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor. Every person who conspires to violate any provision of this article is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 740, Sec. 4.4.)
