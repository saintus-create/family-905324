# GOV § 62523

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.5. REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE AUTHORITIES > Chapter: 3. Authority Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 62523

---

## Text

An authority shall not acquire property by eminent domain.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 767, Sec. 1. (SB 440) Effective January 1, 2025.)
