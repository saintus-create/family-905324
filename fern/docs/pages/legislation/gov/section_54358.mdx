# GOV § 54358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 2. Powers of a Local Agency

**Citation:** GOV § 54358

---

## Text

The local agency may execute all instruments, perform all acts, and do all things necesssary or convenient in the exercise of the powers granted by this chapter or in the performance of its covenants or duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 602.)
