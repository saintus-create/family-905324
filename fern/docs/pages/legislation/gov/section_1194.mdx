# GOV § 1194

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Deputies and Subordinates

**Citation:** GOV § 1194

---

## Text

When not otherwise provided for, each deputy possesses the powers and may perform the duties attached by law to the office of his principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
