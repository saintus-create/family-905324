# GOV § 989

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Part: 6. INSURANCE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Procedures Applicable to State and Local Public Entities

**Citation:** GOV § 989

---

## Text

As used in this part, “local public entity” means any public entity except a state agency covered by Section 11007.4 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1682, Sec. 12.)
