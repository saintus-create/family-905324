# GOV § 37350.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 37350.5

---

## Text

A city may acquire by eminent domain any property necessary to carry out any of its powers or functions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1240.)
