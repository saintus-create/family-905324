# GOV § 55000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 55000

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “local agency” means municipal corporation or sanitary district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
