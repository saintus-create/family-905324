# GOV § 13341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 3. Revised Budgeting and Accounting System for Fiscal Affairs of State Government

**Citation:** GOV § 13341

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the existing budgeting, accounting, and reporting systems of the University of California shall not be substantially modified to comply with the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 1284.)
