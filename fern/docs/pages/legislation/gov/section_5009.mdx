# GOV § 5009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Registration of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 5009

---

## Text

All moneys received by the State Treasurer under this chapter shall be paid monthly into the State Treasury for credit to the General Fund.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
