# GOV § 27365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Recorder > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 27365

---

## Text

The fee for any copy of a birth, death, or marriage certificate, when the copy is made by the recorder, is the same as is payable to a state or local registrar of vital statistics.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
