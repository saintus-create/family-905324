# GOV § 67023

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 67023

## Text

“Governing body” means the governing board of the Tahoe Regional Planning Agency.
