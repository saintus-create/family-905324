# GOV § 26836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26836

---

## Text

For every certificate the fee for which is not otherwise fixed, the fee is one dollar and seventy-five cents ($1.75).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 327, Sec. 76. Effective June 30, 1982.)
