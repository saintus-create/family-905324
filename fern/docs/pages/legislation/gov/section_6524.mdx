# GOV § 6524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6524

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a private, nonprofit children’s hospital in a county of the third class may enter into a joint powers agreement with any public agency, as defined in Section 6500.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 212, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
