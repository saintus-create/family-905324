# GOV § 40558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 19. Surplus United States Property > Article: 4. Surplus Property Commission and Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 40558

---

## Text

The treasurer shall install and maintain a system of auditing and accounting that shall completely and at all times show the financial condition of the authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1227.)
