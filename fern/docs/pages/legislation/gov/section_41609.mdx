# GOV § 41609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 6. Chief of Police

**Citation:** GOV § 41609

---

## Text

The chief of police shall immediately deposit with the city treasurer all money collected by the police department as required by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 53. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
