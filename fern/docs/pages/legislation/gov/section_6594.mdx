# GOV § 6594

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 4. Local Bond Pooling

**Citation:** GOV § 6594

---

## Text

The authority may, out of any funds available therefor, purchase its bonds. The authority may hold, pledge, cancel, or resell the bonds, subject to, and in accordance with, agreements with bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 868, Sec. 6. Effective September 23, 1985.)
