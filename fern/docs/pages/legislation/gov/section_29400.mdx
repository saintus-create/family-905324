# GOV § 29400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 5. District Attorney’s Special Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29400

---

## Text

There is a district attorney’s special appropriation in each county.
