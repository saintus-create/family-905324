# GOV § 67621

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 7. Rights of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 67621

---

## Text

The enumeration of the rights and remedies in Section 67620 does not exclude the exercise or prosecution of any other rights or remedies available to the holders of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1373.)
