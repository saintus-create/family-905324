# GOV § 53398.61

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.99. Enhanced Infrastructure Financing District > Article: 2. Preparation and Adoption of Infrastructure Financing Plan

**Citation:** GOV § 53398.61

---

## Text

The legislative body shall direct the city official or county official, as applicable, selected by the legislative body, to mail or electronically submit a copy of the resolution to each affected taxing entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 599, Sec. 4. (SB 1140) Effective January 1, 2025.)
