# GOV § 57550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 10. Effect of Reorganization

**Citation:** GOV § 57550

---

## Text

On and after the effective date of a reorganization, each change of organization ordered for any city or district shall be given the force and effect pertaining to a change of organization of that type, as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 541, Sec. 3. Effective September 9, 1985. Operative January 1, 1986, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 541.)
