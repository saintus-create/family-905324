# GOV § 27502.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 2. Inquests

**Citation:** GOV § 27502.2

---

## Text

The inquest verdict or decision shall be inadmissible as evidence in any civil or criminal proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1220.)
