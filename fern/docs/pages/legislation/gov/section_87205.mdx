# GOV § 87205

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 7. Conflicts of Interest > Article: 2. Disclosure

**Citation:** GOV § 87205

---

## Text

A person who completes a term of an office specified in Section 87200 and within 45 days begins a term of the same office or another such office of the same jurisdiction is deemed not to assume office or leave office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 200, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2006. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
