# GOV § 6004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Publications and Official Advertising > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 6004

---

## Text

For a newspaper to be “published,” it shall have been issued from the place where it is printed and sold to or circulated among the people and its subscribers during the whole of the one year period.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
