# GOV § 30105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Settlement With the State

**Citation:** GOV § 30105

---

## Text

The auditor shall transmit one copy of the report to the State Controller by mail or express and deliver the other copy to the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
