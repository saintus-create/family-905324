# GOV § 53791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 6. Exceeding the Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 53791

---

## Text

A city, county, or district may make all transfers of money from and to general or special funds necessary to pay expenses incurred pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
