# GOV § 72190.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 8. Superior Courts > Article: 5. Other Officers

**Citation:** GOV § 72190.1

---

## Text

A commissioner may conduct arraignment proceedings on a complaint if directed to perform those duties by the presiding judge of the court, including the issuance and signing of bench warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 375. Effective January 1, 2003.)
