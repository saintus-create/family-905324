# GOV § 11102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 7. Signatures

**Citation:** GOV § 11102

---

## Text

The premiums on forgery insurance are a proper charge against appropriations for the support or maintenance of the officer using the machine or the State agency to which the officer is attached.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
