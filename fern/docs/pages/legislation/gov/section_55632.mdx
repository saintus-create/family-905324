# GOV § 55632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 2. Supplementary Fire or Police Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 55632

---

## Text

The legislative body of any local agency may contract with any other local agency for the furnishing of fire or police protection to such other local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1068.)
