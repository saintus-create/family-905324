# GOV § 16700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Duplicate Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 16700

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “instrument” means bonds, coupons, and other evidences of State indebtedness, except warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 120.)
