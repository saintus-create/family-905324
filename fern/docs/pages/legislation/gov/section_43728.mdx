# GOV § 43728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43728

---

## Text

The proceeds of any sale for cash shall be placed in the city treasury to the credit of the funding fund, and applied only to refunding the indebtedness for which the bonds are issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
