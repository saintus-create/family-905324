# GOV § 56844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 2. Reorganization

**Citation:** GOV § 56844

---

## Text

If the commission orders the discharge of a reorganization committee, the commission may make a study, report, and recommendation upon a plan of reorganization in the place of the reorganization committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 177. Effective January 1, 2001.)
