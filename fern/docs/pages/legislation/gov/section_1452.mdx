# GOV § 1452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3. Official Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 1452

---

## Text

No officer with whom any official bond is required to be filed shall file such bond until approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
