# GOV § 19601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Demonstration Projects

**Citation:** GOV § 19601

---

## Text

No demonstration project may provide for a waiver of any provision of Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 19680).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 957.)
