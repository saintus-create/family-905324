# GOV § 38636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 2. Police Protection

**Citation:** GOV § 38636

---

## Text

Leaves of absence granted for sickness or injuries received in the discharge of duty are not a part of the annual leave of absence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
