# GOV § 19175.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 6. Probationary Period

**Citation:** GOV § 19175.1

---

## Text

The board, upon the written request of a probationer who has been rejected for medical reasons only, may restore the name of the rejected probationer to the employment list from which his name originally was certified if the list is still in existence and the board determines after medical examination that he meets the required medical standards.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 516.)
