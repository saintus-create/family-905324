# GOV § 11121.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 11121.9

---

## Text

Each state body shall provide a copy of this article to each member of the state body upon his or her appointment to membership or assumption of office.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 968, Sec. 7.1.)
