# GOV § 21310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 13. Retirement Benefits > Article: 3. Cost-of-Living Adjustments

**Citation:** GOV § 21310

---

## Text

It is the purpose of the Legislature in enacting this article to provide for the preservation of the purchasing power of benefits under the Public Employees’ Retirement Law through a system of adjustments in benefits based on changes in living costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
