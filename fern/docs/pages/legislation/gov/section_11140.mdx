# GOV § 11140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 10. State Boards and Commissions

**Citation:** GOV § 11140

---

## Text

It is the policy of the State of California that the composition of state boards and commissions shall be broadly reflective of the general public including ethnic minorities and women.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 977.)
