**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Urban Agriculture Incentive Zones

## Text

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 406, Sec. 1. (AB 551) Effective January 1, 2014.)
