# GOV § 43634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43634

---

## Text

These taxes shall be levied and collected as other city taxes, and in addition to all other taxes. They shall be used only for payment of the bonds and interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
