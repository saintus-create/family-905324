# GOV § 19993.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. State-Owned Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** GOV § 19993.5

---

## Text

Any violation by a state officer or employee of this chapter or the rules and regulations adopted pursuant thereto shall constitute misuse of state property under Section 19572 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
