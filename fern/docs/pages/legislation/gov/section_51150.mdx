# GOV § 51150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 6. Eminent Domain or Other Acquisition

**Citation:** GOV § 51150

---

## Text

It is the policy of the state to avoid, whenever practicable, the location of any state or local public improvements and any improvements of public utilities, and the acquisition of land therefor, in timberland production zones.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 23.)
