# GOV § 15650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Access to Public Records

**Citation:** GOV § 15650

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “public record” means any public record as defined in Section 7920.530.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 182. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
