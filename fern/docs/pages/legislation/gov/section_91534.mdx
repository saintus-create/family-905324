# GOV § 91534

**Hierarchy:** Title: 10. CALIFORNIA INDUSTRIAL DEVELOPMENT FINANCING ACT > Chapter: 1. California Industrial Development Financing > Article: 2. Industrial Development Authorities

**Citation:** GOV § 91534

---

## Text

No company shall, by reason of any project agreement, be deemed the agent of an authority in the carrying out of such agreement unless (and in such case only to the extent) such agreement specifically provides otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1358.)
