# GOV § 17205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Registered Warrants > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 17205

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provision of the Uniform Commercial Code, all registered warrants are negotiable instruments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 135, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
