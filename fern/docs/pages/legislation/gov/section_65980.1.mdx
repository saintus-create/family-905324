# GOV § 65980.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.7. School Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 65980.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 69580, for the purposes of Section 65974, interim facilities shall include leased residential dwellings used by the Lodi Unified School District for school purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 82, Sec. 1. Effective June 14, 1983.)
