# GOV § 6206.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 3.1. Address Confidentiality for Victims of Domestic Violence, Sexual Assault, Stalking, Human Trafficking, Child Abduction, and Elder or Dependent Adult Abuse

**Citation:** GOV § 6206.1

---

## Text

Certification as a program participant shall not be evidence that minor children in the participant’s custody are at risk in the participant’s care.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 686, Sec. 5. (AB 1726) Effective January 1, 2023.)
