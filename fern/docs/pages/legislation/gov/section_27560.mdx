# GOV § 27560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Surveyor > Article: 1. Duties Generally

**Citation:** GOV § 27560

---

## Text

All maps which are platted, traced, blueprinted, or otherwise so made for the county and all data obtained by the surveyor or person making them from other sources is the property of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
