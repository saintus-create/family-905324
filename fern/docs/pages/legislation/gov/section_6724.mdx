# GOV § 6724

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6724

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim January 23 as Ed Roberts Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 115, Sec. 10. (SB 1256) Effective January 1, 2011.)
