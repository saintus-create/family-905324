# GOV § 421.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 421.5

---

## Text

(a) West Coast Swing Dance is the official state dance.

(b) The Square Dance is the official state folk dance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1645, Sec. 2.)
