# GOV § 12471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 5. Uniform State Pay Roll System

**Citation:** GOV § 12471

---

## Text

The uniform state pay roll system shall provide adequate accounting procedures to enable each state agency to properly account salary and wage expenditures under the uniform state accounting system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1146.)
