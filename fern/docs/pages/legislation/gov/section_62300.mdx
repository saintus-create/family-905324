# GOV § 62300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. Climate Resilience Districts > Title: 6. DISTRICTS

**Citation:** GOV § 62300

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Climate Resilience Districts Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 266, Sec. 1. (SB 852) Effective January 1, 2023.)
