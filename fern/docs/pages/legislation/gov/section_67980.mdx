# GOV § 67980

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.96. NORTH LAKE TAHOE TRANSPORTATION AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. Powers and Functions of the Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 67980

---

## Text

Expenditure of the revenues derived from the tax imposed pursuant to this chapter, together with other federal, state, and local funds made available to the authority for transportation improvements, shall be in accordance with the transportation expenditure plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1044, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999.)
