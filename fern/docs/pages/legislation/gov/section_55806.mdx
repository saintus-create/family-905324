# GOV § 55806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Counties Transaction and Use Tax

**Citation:** GOV § 55806

---

## Text

The authority may provide for the limited tax bonds to bear a variable or fixed interest rate, for the manner and intervals in which the rate shall vary, and for the dates on which the interest shall be payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 384, Sec. 1. Effective September 9, 1991.)
