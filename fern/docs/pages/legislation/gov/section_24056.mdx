# GOV § 24056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 24056

---

## Text

The board of supervisors upon receiving a certified copy of the record of conviction of any officer for receiving illegal fees shall declare his office vacant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
