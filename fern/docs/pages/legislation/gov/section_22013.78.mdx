# GOV § 22013.78

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22013.78

---

## Text

“Policeman” as used in this part also includes persons currently employed in classifications listed in Sections 20401.5, 20423.6, 31469.2, 45311, and 53217.6 for purposes of Section 218(d)(5)(A) of the Social Security Act (42 U.S.C. Sec. 418(d)(5)(A)).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 519, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2004.)
