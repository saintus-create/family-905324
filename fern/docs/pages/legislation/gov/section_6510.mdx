# GOV § 6510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 1. Joint Powers Agreements

**Citation:** GOV § 6510

---

## Text

The agreement may be continued for a definite term or until rescinded or terminated. The agreement may provide for the method by which it may be rescinded or terminated by any party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 84.)
