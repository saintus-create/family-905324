# GOV § 27401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 7. Tax Collector

**Citation:** GOV § 27401

---

## Text

At least once a month the tax collector shall pay the public money in his hands into the county treasury, taking the receipt of the treasurer therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
