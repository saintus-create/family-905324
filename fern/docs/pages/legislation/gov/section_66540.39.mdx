# GOV § 66540.39

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.10. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA WATER EMERGENCY TRANSPORTATION RESPONSE AND DISASTER RECOVERY ACT > Chapter: 5. Duties and Powers > Article: 3. Facilities, Equipment, and Services

**Citation:** GOV § 66540.39

---

## Text

The air emission standard for new vessels purchased by the authority shall exceed the federal Environmental Protection Agency’s air quality standards for Tier II 2007 marine engines by at least 85 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 734, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
