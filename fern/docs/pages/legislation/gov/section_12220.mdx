# GOV § 12220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12220

---

## Text

As used in this article, “item” includes, but is not limited to, any paper, document, book, map, artifact, or other type of record.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 145, Sec. 1. (AB 1759) Effective January 1, 2024.)
