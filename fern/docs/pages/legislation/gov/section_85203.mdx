# GOV § 85203

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 5. Limitations on Contributions > Article: 2.5. Applicability of the Political Reform Act of 1974

**Citation:** GOV § 85203

---

## Text

“Small contributor committee” means any committee that meets all of the following criteria:

(a) The committee has been in existence for at least six months.

(b) The committee receives contributions from 100 or more persons.

(c) No one person has contributed to the committee more than two hundred dollars ($200) per calendar year.

(d) The committee makes contributions to five or more candidates.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 102, Sec. 20. Approved in Proposition 34 at the November 7, 2000, election. Operative January 1, 2001, by Sec. 83 of Ch. 102, as amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 241, Sec. 18.)
