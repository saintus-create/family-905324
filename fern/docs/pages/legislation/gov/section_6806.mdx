# GOV § 6806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Computation of Time

**Citation:** GOV § 6806

---

## Text

A day is the period of time between any midnight and the midnight following.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
