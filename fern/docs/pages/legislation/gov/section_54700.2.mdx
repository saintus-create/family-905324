# GOV § 54700.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 54700.2

---

## Text

“Bonds” means bonds, notes or other evidence of indebtedness issued by a city pursuant to this chapter and secured by rental or lease revenues derived from rental housing for employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
