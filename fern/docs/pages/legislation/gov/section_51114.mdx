# GOV § 51114

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 2. Timberland Production Zones

**Citation:** GOV § 51114

---

## Text

Parcels zoned as timberland production shall be zoned as such for an initial term of 10 years. On the first and each subsequent anniversary date of the initial zoning, a year shall be added to the initial term of 10 years, unless a notice of rezoning is given as provided in Section 51120.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1489, Sec. 8.)
