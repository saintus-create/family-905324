# GOV § 12100.53

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 6.7. California Foreign Investment Program

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.53

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to EB-5 activities of the state relating to the EB-5 Investment Program occurring on or after January 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6315.3 by Stats. 2017, Ch. 13, Sec. 11. (AB 1727) Effective January 1, 2018.)
