# GOV § 26847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Clerk > Article: 2. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26847

## Text

The fee for making a record of a certificate of revivor is two dollars ($2).
