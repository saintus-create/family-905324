# GOV § 8297

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Law Reform > Article: 2. California Law Revision Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8297

---

## Text

The commission may, with the approval of the Director of General Services, enter into, amend and terminate contracts with colleges, universities, schools of law or other research institutions, or with qualified individuals for the purposes of research.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10340 by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1335, Sec. 2.)
