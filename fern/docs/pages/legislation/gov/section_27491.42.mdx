# GOV § 27491.42

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Coroner > Article: 2. Inquests

**Citation:** GOV § 27491.42

---

## Text

(a) For purposes of this section, “sudden unexplained death in childhood” means the sudden death of a child one year of age or older but under 18 years of age that is unexplained by the history of the child and where a thorough postmortem examination fails to demonstrate an adequate cause of death.

(b) The coroner shall notify the parent or responsible adult of a child described in subdivision (a) about the importance of taking tissue samples.

(c) A coroner shall not be liable for damages in a civil action for any act or omission in compliance with this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 614, Sec. 1. (AB 620) Effective January 1, 2020.)
