# GOV § 26982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4.6. Consolidated Office of Director of Finance

**Citation:** GOV § 26982

---

## Text

The consolidation of the office of director of finance with any elective office shall become effective only upon the expiration of the current term of office of the incumbent of the office so consolidated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 638.)
