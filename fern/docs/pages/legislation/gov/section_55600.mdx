# GOV § 55600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 1. Performance of Fire Protection Functions by County Firewarden or State Forester

**Citation:** GOV § 55600

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means a city, chartered city, or a fire protection district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 465, Sec. 2. Effective August 22, 1988.)
