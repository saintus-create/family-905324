# GOV § 53666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 2. Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53666

---

## Text

The only liability that shall attach to the administrator as the result of the operation of this article is that which would attach as a result of other laws of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1483.)
