# GOV § 3598

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 11. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 3598

---

## Text

No memorandum of understanding shall contravene any federal or state law, including rules and regulations promulgated pursuant to such laws, prohibiting discrimination in employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 744.)
