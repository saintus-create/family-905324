# GOV § 31895.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 18. Adjustment to Fit Other Allowances

**Citation:** GOV § 31895.5

---

## Text

As used in this article, “total benefits” means the retirement allowance or other benefit payable to any beneficiary pursuant to the provisions of this chapter plus any other monetary payments due to the same beneficiary from any public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 511.)
