# GOV § 33008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. County Peace Officers and Fire Service Retirement, Disability and Death Benefit Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 33008

---

## Text

All warrants drawn on said fund shall be on order of the board of administration and shall be signed by the treasurer and at least one other member of the board who shall be designated by the board. Every warrant shall be countersigned, numbered and recorded by the county auditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1479.)
