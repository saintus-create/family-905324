# GOV § 16645.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Prohibition on Use of State Funds and Facilities to Assist, Promote, or Deter Union Organizing

**Citation:** GOV § 16645.3

---

## Text

(a) No state contractor shall assist, promote, or deter union organizing by employees who are performing work on a service contract, including a public works contract, for the state or a state agency.

(b) A state contractor that violates subdivision (a) is liable for a civil penalty of one thousand dollars ($1,000) per employee per violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 872, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
