# GOV § 3539.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.5. Excluded Employees Bill of Rights

**Citation:** GOV § 3539.5

---

## Text

(a) The Department of Human Resources may adopt or amend regulations to implement employee benefits for those state officers and employees excluded from, or not otherwise subject to, the Ralph C. Dills Act (Chapter 10.3 (commencing with Section 3512)).

(b) These regulations shall not be subject to the review and approval of the Office of Administrative Law pursuant to the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2). These regulations shall become effective immediately upon filing with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 665, Sec. 38. (SB 1308) Effective January 1, 2013.)
