# GOV § 37466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 4. Veterans’ Homes

**Citation:** GOV § 37466

---

## Text

The legislative body may establish a fund for the purposes of this article, and transfer money from the general fund to such fund as it deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
