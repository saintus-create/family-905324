# GOV § 17096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Lost or Destroyed Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17096

---

## Text

A replacement warrant is void if not presented to the Treasurer for payment within the same time limit provided by law for the original warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 46. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
