# GOV § 23563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** GOV § 23563

---

## Text

The board of each affected county shall appoint as election officers three representatives who reside in the affected county represented.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
