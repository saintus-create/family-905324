# GOV § 51009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 51009

---

## Text

From the date of filing, the boundary line is established and fixed for all purposes and constitutes the true and official boundary line of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
