# GOV § 23689

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 4. County Seats > Article: 2. Removal of County Seats

**Citation:** GOV § 23689

---

## Text

Unless two-thirds of the votes are cast for some other place than that fixed by law as the former county seat, no second election for the removal of the county seat shall be held within four years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
