# GOV § 20893

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 20893

---

## Text

If a person is employed concurrently by more than one contracting agency or the state and one or more contracting agencies, his or her status under this system is the same as if he or she were employed in more than one state agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
