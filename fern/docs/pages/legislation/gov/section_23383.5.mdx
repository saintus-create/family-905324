# GOV § 23383.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 5. Transfers

**Citation:** GOV § 23383.5

---

## Text

The maximum tax rate for the new county shall be established in accordance with Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 2201) of Part 4 of Division 1 of the Revenue and Taxation Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
