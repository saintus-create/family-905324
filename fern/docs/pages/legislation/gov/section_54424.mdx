# GOV § 54424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 4. Provisions Relating to Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 54424

---

## Text

So long as any bonds or interest thereon are unpaid the revenues and interest thereon shall not be used for any other purpose, except as provided in Sections 54425 and 54426.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 526.)
