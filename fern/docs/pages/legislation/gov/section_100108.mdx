# GOV § 100108

**Hierarchy:** Title: 21.1. CALACCOUNT BLUE RIBBON COMMISSION

**Citation:** GOV § 100108

---

## Text

This title shall remain in effect until January 1, 2032, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 451, Sec. 3. (AB 1177) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Title 21.1, commencing with Section 100100.)
