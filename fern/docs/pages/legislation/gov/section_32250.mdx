# GOV § 32250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 3. Board of Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 32250

---

## Text

The management of the system is vested in a county foresters, fire wardens, and fire protection district foremen’s retirement board, consisting of five members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
