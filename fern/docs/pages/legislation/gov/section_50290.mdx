# GOV § 50290

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 12. Historical Property Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 50290

---

## Text

Local agencies and owners of qualified historical properties may consult with the State Historical Resources Commission for its advice and counsel on matters relevant to historical property contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 965, Sec. 8.)
