# GOV § 65932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.5. Review and Approval of Development Projects > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 65932

---

## Text

“Public agency” means any state agency, any county, city and county, city, regional agency, public district, redevelopment agency, or other political subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1200.)
