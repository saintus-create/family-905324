# GOV § 54061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 3. Coliseum and Stadium Parking

**Citation:** GOV § 54061

---

## Text

A local agency may acquire necessary real and personal property to construct, equip, operate, and maintain parking facilities in connection with any stadium or coliseum maintained by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
