# GOV § 66435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Maps > Article: 2. Final Maps

**Citation:** GOV § 66435

---

## Text

Prior to filing, those certificates, statements, and acknowledgments set forth in this article shall appear on the final map and may be combined where appropriate.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1408, Sec. 5.)
