# GOV § 14076.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5.6. Capitol Corridor

**Citation:** GOV § 14076.8

---

## Text

For the purpose of carrying out its responsibilities pursuant to this article, the board may seek funds from any jurisdiction served by the Capitols passenger rail service for enhanced service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 263, Sec. 10. Effective July 23, 1996.)
