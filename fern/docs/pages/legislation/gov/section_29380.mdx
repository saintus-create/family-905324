# GOV § 29380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 4. County Officers’ Cash Difference Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29380

---

## Text

At the end of the fiscal year the board shall transfer any money in an overage fund to the county general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
