# GOV § 12477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Controller > Article: 5. Uniform State Pay Roll System

**Citation:** GOV § 12477

---

## Text

Rules and regulations adopted under this article or Section 1153 are excepted from the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act and shall be distributed in the form and manner as the Controller determines.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1270, Sec. 26.)
