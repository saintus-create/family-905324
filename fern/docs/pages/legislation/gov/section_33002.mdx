# GOV § 33002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. County Peace Officers and Fire Service Retirement, Disability and Death Benefit Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 33002

---

## Text

This chapter and every retirement, disability and death benefit plan adopted pursuant to its terms, shall be given a liberal interpretation with a view to carrying out its purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1479.)
