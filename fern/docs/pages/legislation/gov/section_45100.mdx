# GOV § 45100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. EMPLOYEES > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Civil Service > Article: 5. Reduction in Personnel

**Citation:** GOV § 45100

---

## Text

If the legislative body determines that a reduction in personnel is necessary for economy reasons, it shall observe the seniority rule in putting the reduction into effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
