# GOV § 50053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 3. Financial Affairs

**Citation:** GOV § 50053

---

## Text

When any such money becomes the property of a local agency and is in a special fund, the legislative body may transfer it to the general fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
