# GOV § 19815.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Department of Human Resources > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19815.5

---

## Text

Subject to the State Civil Service Act, the director shall appoint such other assistants and other employees as are necessary for the administration of the affairs of the department and shall prescribe their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 230, Sec. 55.)
