# GOV § 67102

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 4. Agency’s Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 67102

---

## Text

All ordinances, rules, regulations and policies adopted by the agency shall be enforced by the agency, and by the counties and cities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
