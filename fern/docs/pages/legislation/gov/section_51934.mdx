# GOV § 51934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 3. Reorganization Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 51934

---

## Text

At its next regular meeting following the election, the legislative body of the county shall canvass the returns. The canvass shall be completed at the meeting if practicable, or as soon as possible thereafter, avoiding adjournments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 972.)
