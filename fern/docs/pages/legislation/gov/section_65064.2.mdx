# GOV § 65064.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 65064.2

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the board constitutes a quorum for the transaction of business and may act for the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
