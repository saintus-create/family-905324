# GOV § 59350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 8. Notice of Assessment Hearing

## Text

When the diagram and assessment have been made, they shall be attached together and filed with the clerk.
