# GOV § 68100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 68100

---

## Text

When the court is held at a place appointed, pursuant to Section 68115, every person held to appear at the court shall appear at the place so appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 149, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2004.)
