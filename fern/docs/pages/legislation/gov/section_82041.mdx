# GOV § 82041

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 82041

---

## Text

“Local government agency” means a county, city or district of any kind including school district, or any other local or regional political subdivision, or any department, division, bureau, office, board, commission or other agency of the foregoing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 727, Sec. 4. Operative July 1, 1985, by Sec. 12 of Ch. 727. Note: This section was added on June 4, 1974, by initiative Prop. 9.)
