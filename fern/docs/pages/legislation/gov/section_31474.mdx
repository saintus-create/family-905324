# GOV § 31474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31474

---

## Text

“Retirement association” means an association of all persons who may qualify as annuitants or beneficiaries pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
