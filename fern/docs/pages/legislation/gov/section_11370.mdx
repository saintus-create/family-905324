# GOV § 11370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Office of Administrative Hearings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 11370

---

## Text

Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340), Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 11370), Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 11400), and Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) constitute, and may be cited as, the Administrative Procedure Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 938, Sec. 16.5. Effective January 1, 1996. Operative July 1, 1997, by Sec. 98 of Ch. 938.)
