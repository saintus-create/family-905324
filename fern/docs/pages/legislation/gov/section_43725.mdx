# GOV § 43725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Indebtedness

**Citation:** GOV § 43725

---

## Text

The legislative body may fix a date for the earliest maturity of the principal of the bonds, not more than three years from the date of the issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
