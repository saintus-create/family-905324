# GOV § 43061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 1. Property Tax Assessment, Levy,and Collection > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 43061

---

## Text

Property sold for city taxes is subject to redemption within a redemption period of at least five years upon such terms as the legislative body prescribes by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
