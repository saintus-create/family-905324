# GOV § 66700

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.25. SAN FRANCISCO BAY RESTORATION AUTHORITY ACT > Chapter: 1. Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** GOV § 66700

---

## Text

This title shall be known and may be cited as the San Francisco Bay Restoration Authority Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 690, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009. Repealed as of January 1, 2049, pursuant to Section 66706.)
