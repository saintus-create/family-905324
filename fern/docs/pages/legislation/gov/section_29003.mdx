# GOV § 29003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29003

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specifically provided by law, a majority vote of the total membership of the board is required for the board to take action pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 10. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
