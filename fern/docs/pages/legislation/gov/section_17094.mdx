# GOV § 17094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 5. Lost or Destroyed Warrants

**Citation:** GOV § 17094

---

## Text

The Controller shall make the proper entries on his books, showing the lost or destroyed warrants, and the issuance of replacement warrants in lieu thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 28, Sec. 44. (SB 854) Effective June 20, 2014.)
