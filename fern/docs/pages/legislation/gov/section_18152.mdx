# GOV § 18152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Oaths for State Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 18152

---

## Text

The method and manner of taking, subscribing, and filing the oath by a person appointed to a State position not in the state civil service shall be as provided in Article 4 (commencing with Section 1360) of Chapter 2 of Division 4 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 561, Sec. 83. (AB 1516) Effective January 1, 2018.)
