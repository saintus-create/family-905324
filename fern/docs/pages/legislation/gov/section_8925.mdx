# GOV § 8925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 2. Code of Ethics

**Citation:** GOV § 8925

---

## Text

No person shall induce or seek to induce any Member of the Legislature to violate any provision of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 740, Sec. 4.2.)
