# GOV § 53180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1.5. Integrated Financing District Act > Article: 2. Proceedings to Create an Integrated Financing District

**Citation:** GOV § 53180

---

## Text

Proceedings for the establishment of an integrated financing district may be instituted by the legislative body of any local agency authorized to use any financing act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1512, Sec. 1.)
