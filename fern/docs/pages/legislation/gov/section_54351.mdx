# GOV § 54351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 2. Powers of a Local Agency

**Citation:** GOV § 54351

---

## Text

If the local agency acquires, constructs, or improves a sanitation or garbage removal or disposal system, it may require its inhabitants to use the facilities exclusively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
