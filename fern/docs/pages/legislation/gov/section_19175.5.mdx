# GOV § 19175.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 6. Probationary Period

**Citation:** GOV § 19175.5

---

## Text

Subject to board rule an appointing power may cancel or withdraw a notice of rejection of a probationer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 575.)
