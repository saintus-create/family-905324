# GOV § 3523.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.3. State Employer-Employee Relations

**Citation:** GOV § 3523.5

---

## Text

The enactment of this chapter shall not be construed as making the provisions of Section 923 of the Labor Code applicable to state employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1159.)
