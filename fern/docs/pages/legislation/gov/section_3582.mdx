# GOV § 3582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 7. Organizational Security

**Citation:** GOV § 3582

---

## Text

Subject to the limitations set forth in this section, organizational security shall be within the scope of representation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 744.)
