# GOV § 56017.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56017.1

---

## Text

“Applicant” means a local agency or person or persons that submits an application, as defined by Section 56017.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 7. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
