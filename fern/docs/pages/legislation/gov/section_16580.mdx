# GOV § 16580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.3. The Accounts Receivable Management Act

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Accounts Receivable Management Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1224, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
