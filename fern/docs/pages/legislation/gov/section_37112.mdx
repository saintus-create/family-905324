# GOV § 37112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 37112

---

## Text

In addition to other powers, a legislative body may perform all acts necessary or proper to carry out the provisions of this title.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 37113 by Stats. 1955, Ch. 624.)
