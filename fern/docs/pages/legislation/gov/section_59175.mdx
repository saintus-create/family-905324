# GOV § 59175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 2. Authority to Conduct Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 59175

---

## Text

Authority to conduct proceedings is conferred upon the legislative body of a local agency pursuant to this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
