# GOV § 65040.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 1.5. Office of Land Use and Climate Innovation > Article: 4. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 65040.11

---

## Text

The “State Clearinghouse” shall submit such information acquired by it pursuant to the application of the Intergovernmental Cooperation Act of 1968 (P.L. 90-577) to an agency designated for that purpose by concurrent resolution of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12036 by Stats. 1996, Ch. 872, Sec. 41. Effective January 1, 1997.)
