# GOV § 54615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 9. Contributions

**Citation:** GOV § 54615

---

## Text

The local agency may also make contributions, for any of the purposes specified in this article, of any of the funds referred to in Section 54478.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 526.)
