# GOV § 65919.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 4.4. Interagency Referrals

**Citation:** GOV § 65919.11

## Text

Failure to comply with the procedural requirements of this chapter shall not affect the validity of any proposed action.
