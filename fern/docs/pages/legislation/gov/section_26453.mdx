# GOV § 26453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 15. Revenue Bonds for County Incinerators > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26453

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.
