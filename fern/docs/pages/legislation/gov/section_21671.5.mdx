# GOV § 21671.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 16. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** GOV § 21671.5

---

## Text

The design and administration of a tax-preferred retirement savings program established pursuant to Section 21670 shall conform with the applicable provisions of Title 26 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 639, Sec. 9. (SB 1139) Effective January 1, 2011.)
