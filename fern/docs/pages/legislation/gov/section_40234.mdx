# GOV § 40234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 17. Census > Article: 2. Special Census

**Citation:** GOV § 40234

---

## Text

The city clerk shall prepare a certified copy of the final census report. Thereafter it is the official census of the city for the purposes of this article.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 806.)
