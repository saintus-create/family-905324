# GOV § 8880.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 2. California State Lottery Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.19

---

## Text

Annual Selection of Chairperson

The Commission shall select annually from its membership a chairperson. The chairperson shall have the power to convene special meetings of the Commission upon 48 hours’ written notice to members of the Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 55, Sec. 5. Effective April 16, 1986. Note: This section was added on Nov. 6, 1984, by initiative Prop. 37.)
