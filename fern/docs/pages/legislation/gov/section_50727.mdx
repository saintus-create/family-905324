# GOV § 50727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3.5. Revenue Bonds for Public Improvements > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50727

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause limiting the power of the board to apply the proceeds of the sale of any issue of bonds for the acquiring, constructing, or completing of any improvement or any part thereof, or more than one of such improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2066.)
