# GOV § 8192

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 3. Capitol Area Redevelopment

**Citation:** GOV § 8192

---

## Text

The lessee of any property leased from the authority for residential purposes for a term of five years or less shall receive a credit against future rental payments, or a refund if he is no longer leasing the property, upon submission to the authority that he has paid the taxes imposed on him for his possessory interest in the property. The amount of the credit or refund shall be the amount of the tax actually paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 953.)
