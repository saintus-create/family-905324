# GOV § 15166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. California Law Enforcement Telecommunications System

**Citation:** GOV § 15166

---

## Text

The Director of General Services shall fix the charge to be paid by any state department, officer, board or commission to the Department of Justice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1595.)
