# GOV § 8670.37.57

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.4. Oil Spill Response and Contingency Planning > Article: 5.5. Financial Responsibility

**Citation:** GOV § 8670.37.57

---

## Text

No certificate of financial responsibility shall have a term greater than two years. The administrator may issue certificates for shorter periods where appropriate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1248, Sec. 17. Effective September 24, 1990.)
