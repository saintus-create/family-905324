# GOV § 29924

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29924

---

## Text

When collected the tax shall be paid into the treasury of the county and used solely to pay the interest and principal of the bonds as they become due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
