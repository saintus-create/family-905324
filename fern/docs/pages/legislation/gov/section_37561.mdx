# GOV § 37561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 6. Museums

**Citation:** GOV § 37561

---

## Text

Claims upon the fund shall be presented to the board for allowance. Such claims shall be paid as are other claims against the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
