# GOV § 29916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 29916

---

## Text

Interest on the bonds shall not exceed 8 percent per annum, payable semiannually, except that interest for the first year after the date of the bonds may be made payable at the end of said year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
