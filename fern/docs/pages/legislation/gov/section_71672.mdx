# GOV § 71672

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 7. Trial Court Employment Protection and Governance Act > Article: 7. Relation to Other Trial Court Statutes

**Citation:** GOV § 71672

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, each trial court employee shall be entitled to such benefits as are provided for in Article 2 (commencing with Section 71620) of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1010, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2001.)
