# GOV § 8880.72

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.72

---

## Text

All civil process in any action brought against the Director, the Commission, or the Lottery and any subpoena for the production of Lottery records shall be served upon the Director or his or her designated representative at the Lottery headquarters in Sacramento.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 225, Sec. 1.)
