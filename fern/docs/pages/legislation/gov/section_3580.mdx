# GOV § 3580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 12. Higher Education Employer-Employee Relations > Article: 6.5. Supervisors

**Citation:** GOV § 3580

---

## Text

Except as provided by this article, supervisory employees shall not have the rights, or be covered by, any provision or definition established by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 744.)
