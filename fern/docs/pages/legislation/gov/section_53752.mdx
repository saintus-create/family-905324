# GOV § 53752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4.6. Proposition 218 Omnibus Implementation Act

**Citation:** GOV § 53752

---

## Text

The Department of General Services shall develop compliance standards in the State Administrative Manual (SAM) to inform owners of state property of their duties and responsibilities pursuant to this article and Articles XIIIC and XIIID of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 981, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
