# GOV § 425.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 425.11

---

## Text

California Golden Chanterelle (Cantharellus californicus) is the official state mushroom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 644, Sec. 2. (AB 261) Effective January 1, 2024.)
