# GOV § 50022.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Legislative Bodies

**Citation:** GOV § 50022.9

---

## Text

A city may enact ordinances which adopt by reference county ordinances, codes, or any parts thereof and any amendments thereto by complying with the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 374.)
