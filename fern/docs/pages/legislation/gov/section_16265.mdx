# GOV § 16265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.5. County Revenue Stabilization

**Citation:** GOV § 16265

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Bergeson-Costa-Nielsen County Revenue Stabilization Act of 1987.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 375, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2001.)
