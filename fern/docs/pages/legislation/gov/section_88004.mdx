# GOV § 88004

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 8. Ballot Pamphlet

**Citation:** GOV § 88004

---

## Text

Measures shall be printed in the state voter information guide, so far as possible, in the same order, manner, and form in which they are designated upon the ballot.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 249, Sec. 14. (AB 1511) Effective January 1, 2026.)
