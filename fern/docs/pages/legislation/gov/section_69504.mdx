# GOV § 69504

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 69504

---

## Text

The superior court of each county shall purchase and provide for the installation of the Flag of the United States and the Bear Flag of California in each superior courtroom in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 210, Sec. 22. (AB 1984) Effective January 1, 2021.)
