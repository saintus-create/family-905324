# GOV § 41611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 6. Chief of Police

**Citation:** GOV § 41611

---

## Text

The chief of police shall perform any other services required by general law and the city ordinances.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 467, Sec. 55. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2019.)
