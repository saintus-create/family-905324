# GOV § 25529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Property > Article: 8. Sale or Lease of Real Property

**Citation:** GOV § 25529

---

## Text

Whenever it is proposed to lease real property and the board of supervisors unanimously determines in the resolution that in its opinion, the monthly rental value of the property does not exceed the sum of fifty dollars ($50), the resolution may, before the date of the meeting, be published in the county pursuant to Section 6062.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 357.)
