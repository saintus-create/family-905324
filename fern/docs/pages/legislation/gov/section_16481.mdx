# GOV § 16481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Investments > Article: 4.5. Treasury Pooled Money Investments

**Citation:** GOV § 16481

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, the State Treasurer may enter into security loan agreements pursuant to the provisions of Division 8 (commencing with Section 7600) of Title 1 of the Government Code with respect to securities which he is authorized by law to invest in.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1214.)
