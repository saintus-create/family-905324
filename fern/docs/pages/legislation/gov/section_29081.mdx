# GOV § 29081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 4. Adopted Budget

**Citation:** GOV § 29081

---

## Text

The hearing may be continued from day to day until concluded, but not to exceed a total of 14 calendar days.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 35. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
