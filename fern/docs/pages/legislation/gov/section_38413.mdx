# GOV § 38413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38413

---

## Text

At the time fixed, the legislative body shall pass on the report and any objections that are made to it. The legislative body may confirm, correct, or modify the report. It may adjourn the meeting from time to time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
