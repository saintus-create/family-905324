# GOV § 29144

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 1. Budget and Tax Levy > Article: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 29144

---

## Text

All commitments covered by the restricted, committed, or assigned fund balance encumbrances account at fiscal yearend are appropriated for the succeeding fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 382, Sec. 1.13. (SB 194) Effective January 1, 2012.)
