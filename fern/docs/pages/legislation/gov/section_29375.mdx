# GOV § 29375

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 4. County Officers’ Cash Difference Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29375

---

## Text

If an overage is reported to the county officer or administrative head of a county department involved, the county officer or administrative head shall immediately deposit the overage in the overage fund in the county treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 470, Sec. 40. (AB 1529) Effective January 1, 2013.)
