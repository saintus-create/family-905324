# GOV § 54729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.5. Contracts of Sanitation or Sewerage Enterprises

**Citation:** GOV § 54729

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “bonds” means bonds issued by a local agency payable exclusively from the revenues of the enterprise for which the bonds are issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1249.)
