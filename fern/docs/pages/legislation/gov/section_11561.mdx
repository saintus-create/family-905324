# GOV § 11561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 6. Statutory Salary Equalization Plan > Article: 1. Salaries of Specified Positions

**Citation:** GOV § 11561

---

## Text

An annual salary of twenty-five thousand five hundred dollars ($25,500) shall be paid to each member of the Alcoholic Beverage Control Appeals Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1335, Sec. 3.)
