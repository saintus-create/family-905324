# GOV § 900.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 900.3

---

## Text

A “judicial branch entity” is a public entity and means any superior court, court of appeals, the Supreme Court, the Judicial Council, or the Administrative Office of the Courts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1007, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
