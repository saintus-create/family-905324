# GOV § 5803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PUBLIC BONDS AND OBLIGATIONS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10. Sale of Local Securities

**Citation:** GOV § 5803

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “issuer” means a nonprofit corporation, a joint powers authority or a parking authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 292.)
