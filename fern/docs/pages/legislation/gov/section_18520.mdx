# GOV § 18520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 18520

---

## Text

Unless the context requires otherwise, the definitions hereinafter set forth govern the construction of this part and the rules adopted hereunder.

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
