# GOV § 6151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2.5. Medium of Payment

**Citation:** GOV § 6151

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “negotiable paper” means bank checks and drafts and express and post-office money orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1471.)
