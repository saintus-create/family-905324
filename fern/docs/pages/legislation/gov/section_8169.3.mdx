# GOV § 8169.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.8. Capitol Area Planning > Article: 1. General Planning

**Citation:** GOV § 8169.3

---

## Text

Construction of parking structures in the core area on state-owned property for the use of the state pursuant to Section 14671.5 is hereby authorized by the Legislature.

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1108.)
