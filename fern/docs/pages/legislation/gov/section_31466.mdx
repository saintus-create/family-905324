# GOV § 31466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31466

---

## Text

“Accumulated additional contributions” means the sum of all additional contributions standing to the credit of a member’s individual account and regular interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
