# GOV § 65064.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 65064.7

---

## Text

The provisions for the executive committee set forth in this article shall not be construed to limit the board or the executive committee from setting up any other committees or groups which it may see fit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
