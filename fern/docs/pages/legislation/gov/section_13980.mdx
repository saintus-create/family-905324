# GOV § 13980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Duties and Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 13980

---

## Text

For the purpose of administration, the secretary shall review the organization of the agency and report to the Governor on such changes as he deems necessary properly to segregate and conduct the work of the agency.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 138.)
