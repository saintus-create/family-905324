# GOV § 56864.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 4. Initiation by Petition

**Citation:** GOV § 56864.3

---

## Text

If a person is qualified to sign for two or more of the changes of organization proposed by the petition, that person need sign the petition only once and his or her signature shall be counted as if that person had signed and requested each change of organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 209. Effective January 1, 2001.)
