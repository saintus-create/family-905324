# GOV § 6543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 5. Joint Exercise of Powers > Article: 2. Power to Issue Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 6543

---

## Text

“Governing body” as used in this article means the board or commission provided for by a joint powers agreement, pursuant to Article 1 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 329.)
