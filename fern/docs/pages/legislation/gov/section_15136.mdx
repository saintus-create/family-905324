# GOV § 15136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State’s Teletype System > Article: 2. Leases of Teletype Facilities and Equipment

**Citation:** GOV § 15136

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall lease facilities and equipment to connect the State Teletype System with the police department of each of the following cities:

(a) Paso Robles.

(b) Bishop.

(c) Santa Maria.

(d) Oceanside.

(e) Alameda.

(f) San Jose.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 84.)
