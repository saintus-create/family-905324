# GOV § 53394

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 7. Alternative Revenue Sources

**Citation:** GOV § 53394

---

## Text

The legislative body of a district established within the boundaries of a city shall reimburse the county for any costs incurred by the county auditor on behalf of the district pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 652, Sec. 7.)
