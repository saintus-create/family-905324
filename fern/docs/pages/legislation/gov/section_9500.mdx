# GOV § 9500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Enactment of Statutes and Adoption of Resolutions

**Citation:** GOV § 9500

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “clerk” means the engrossing and enrolling clerk or the employee who performs the duties of engrossing and enrolling clerk when no employee is so designated by name.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
