# GOV § 16351.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Special Funds > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16351.5

---

## Text

The unexpended balance in any appropriation for capital outlay which the Director of Finance, with the approval of the State Public Works Board, determines not to be required for expenditure pursuant to the appropriation, may be transferred on order of the Director of Finance to the unappropriated surplus of the fund from which the appropriation was originally made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 29, Sec. 104. (SB 82) Effective June 27, 2019.)
