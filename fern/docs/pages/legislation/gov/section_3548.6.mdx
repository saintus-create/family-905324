# GOV § 3548.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 10.7. Meeting and Negotiating in Public Educational Employment > Article: 9. Impasse Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 3548.6

---

## Text

If the written agreement does not include procedures authorized by Section 3548.5, both parties to the agreement may agree to submit any disputes involving the interpretation, application, or violation of the agreement to final and binding arbitration pursuant to the rules of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 961.)
