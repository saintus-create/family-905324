# GOV § 56765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Proceedings for Cities > Article: 4. Initiation by Petition

**Citation:** GOV § 56765

---

## Text

A petition for the disincorporation of a city shall be signed by not less than 25 percent of the registered voters residing in the city proposed to be disincorporated as shown on the county register of voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 110. Effective January 1, 2001.)
