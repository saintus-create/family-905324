# GOV § 77400

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 13. State Funding of Trial Courts > Article: 5. Nonseverability

**Citation:** GOV § 77400

---

## Text

If any portion of this chapter is declared to be unconstitutional or void, the entire chapter shall become inoperative.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 945, Sec. 10. Effective September 16, 1988. Note: Termination clause applies to Chapter 13, commencing with Section 77000.)
