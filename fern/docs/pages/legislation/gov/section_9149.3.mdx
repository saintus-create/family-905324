# GOV § 9149.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 9. The Historic State Capitol Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 9149.3

---

## Text

The appointees of both the Speaker of the Assembly and the President pro Tempore of the Senate shall include one person who represents the arts or humanities and one person from the general public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1757, Sec. 8. Effective October 1, 1984.)
