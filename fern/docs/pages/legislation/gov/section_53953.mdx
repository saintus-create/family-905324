# GOV § 53953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 15. District Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 53953

---

## Text

Certified copies of the resolution establishing the revolving fund shall be transmitted to the county auditor and county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 333.)
