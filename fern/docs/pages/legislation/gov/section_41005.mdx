# GOV § 41005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 3. City Treasurer

**Citation:** GOV § 41005

---

## Text

The city treasurer shall perform such duties relative to the collection of city taxes and license fees as are prescribed by ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
