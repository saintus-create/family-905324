# GOV § 15806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 15806

---

## Text

The state office building and garage in the City of Los Angeles, County of Los Angeles, bounded by First Street on the northerly side, Broadway Street on the easterly side, Second Street on the southerly side, and Hill Street on the westerly side, shall be named and shall be known hereafter as the Junípero Serra Building.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1848.)
