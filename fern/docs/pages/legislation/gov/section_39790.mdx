# GOV § 39790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 14. Municipal and Public Utilities > Article: 2. Facilities in Aid of Public Utilities

**Citation:** GOV § 39790

---

## Text

As used in this article, “facility” means any works, road, railroad, tramway, power plant, telegraph or telephone line, or other necessary works or structures.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
