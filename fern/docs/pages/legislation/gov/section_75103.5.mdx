# GOV § 75103.5

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 11. Judges’ Retirement Law > Article: 6. Judges’ Retirement Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 75103.5

---

## Text

No county shall directly or indirectly pay from its funds the member contributions to the Judges’ Retirement Fund required by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1232, Sec. 9. Operative January 9, 1991, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1232.)
