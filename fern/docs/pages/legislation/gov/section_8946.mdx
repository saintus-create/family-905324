# GOV § 8946

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Legislative Organization > Article: 3. Joint Legislative Ethics Committee

**Citation:** GOV § 8946

---

## Text

After the complaint has been filed the respondent shall be entitled to examine and make copies of all evidence in the possession of the committee relating to the complaint.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 163.)
