# GOV § 83120

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 3. Fair Political Practices Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 83120

---

## Text

An interested person may seek judicial review of any action of the Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 4, 1974, by initiative Proposition 9.)
