# GOV § 821.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions Relating to Liability > Article: 3. Liability of Public Employees

**Citation:** GOV § 821.6

---

## Text

A public employee is not liable for injury caused by his instituting or prosecuting any judicial or administrative proceeding within the scope of his employment, even if he acts maliciously and without probable cause.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
