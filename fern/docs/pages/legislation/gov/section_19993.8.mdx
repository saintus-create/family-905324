# GOV § 19993.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. State-Owned Motor Vehicles

**Citation:** GOV § 19993.8

---

## Text

Section 89008 of the Education Code, and not this chapter, shall govern use of state-owned motor vehicles by employees of the California State University.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1040, Sec. 44.)
