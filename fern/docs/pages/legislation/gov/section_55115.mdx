# GOV § 55115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 5. Sewage Treatment and Disposal Plants

**Citation:** GOV § 55115

---

## Text

Bonds issued by a sanitary district shall be issued substantially in the manner provided in the act under which the district is incorporated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
