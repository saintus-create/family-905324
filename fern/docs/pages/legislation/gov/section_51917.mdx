# GOV § 51917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Local Government Reorganization > Article: 2. Definitions and General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 51917

---

## Text

“Reorganize” and “reorganization” means the consolidation of a county with all of the cities within the county pursuant to Article XI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 972.)
