# GOV § 21028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 5. Credit for Public Service

**Citation:** GOV § 21028

---

## Text

“Public service” also means service in temporary, seasonal, limited term, on call, emergency, intermittent, substitute, or other irregular employment in which a person is excluded from membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 83, Sec. 80. Effective January 1, 2000.)
