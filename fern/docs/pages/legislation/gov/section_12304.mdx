# GOV § 12304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Treasurer > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 12304

---

## Text

Peace officers of the Department of the California Highway Patrol shall make routine inspections of the premises of the Treasurer’s office after normal working hours each day and on Saturdays, Sundays, and holidays.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 227, Sec. 3. (SB 1578) Effective January 1, 2013.)
