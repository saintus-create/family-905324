# GOV § 27640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 12. County Counsel

**Citation:** GOV § 27640

---

## Text

In any county a county counsel may be appointed by the board of supervisors.
