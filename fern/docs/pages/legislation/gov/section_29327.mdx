# GOV § 29327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 2. Revolving Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 29327

---

## Text

Any expenditure in excess of one dollar ($1) shall not be made unless a receipt is obtained, setting forth the date, purpose of the expenditure, and the amount expended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
