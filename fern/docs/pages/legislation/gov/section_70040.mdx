# GOV § 70040

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 11. Official Reporters and Fees in Particular Counties

**Citation:** GOV § 70040

---

## Text

Unless otherwise specifically provided, the determination of whether a county has the population prescribed in this article shall be made on the basis of the 1950 federal census.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 206.)
