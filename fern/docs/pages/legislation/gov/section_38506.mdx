# GOV § 38506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 3. Municipal Park Abandonment Law of 1939

**Citation:** GOV § 38506

---

## Text

If all objections are overruled, the legislative body has jurisdiction to order all or any portion of the park abandoned and the land sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 170.)
