# GOV § 245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. SOVEREIGNTY AND PEOPLE OF THE STATE > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. People of the State > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 245

---

## Text

Absence from this state, on business of the state or of the United States, shall not affect the question of residence of any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 789.)
