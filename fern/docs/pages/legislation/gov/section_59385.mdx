# GOV § 59385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Special Assessment and Bond Refunding Law of 1939 > Article: 9. Hearing on Assessments

**Citation:** GOV § 59385

---

## Text

If the resolution of intention is actually published pursuant to this chapter, a court shall not invalidate any assessment, diagram, or proceedings prior to the assessment, for any informality, or other defect.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
