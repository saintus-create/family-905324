# GOV § 66433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SUBDIVISIONS > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Maps > Article: 2. Final Maps

**Citation:** GOV § 66433

## Text

The content and form of final maps shall be governed by the provisions of this article.
