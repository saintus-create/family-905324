# GOV § 20960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 11. Service Credit > Article: 3. Computation of Service Credit

**Citation:** GOV § 20960

---

## Text

Except as provided in Article 4 (commencing with Section 20990), time during which a member is absent from state service without compensation shall not be allowed in computing service.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
