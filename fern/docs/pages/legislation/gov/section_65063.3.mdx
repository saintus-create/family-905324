# GOV § 65063.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 65063.3

---

## Text

The members of the regional planning board appointed by the city selection committee shall be mayors or members of the city councils of the cities.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
