# GOV § 24057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 24057

---

## Text

Every county officer and the officer’s deputies may administer and certify oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 931, Sec. 193. Effective September 28, 1998.)
