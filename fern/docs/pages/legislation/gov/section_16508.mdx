# GOV § 16508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16508

---

## Text

The Treasurer is not responsible for any money deposited in a bank pursuant to this chapter, and while it remains so deposited.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1542.)
