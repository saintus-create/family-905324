# GOV § 23327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23327

---

## Text

If the clerk certifies a petition to be insufficient he shall give mailed notice thereof to each of the chief petitioners, if any, and file the petition as a public record without prejudice to the filing of a new petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1392.)
