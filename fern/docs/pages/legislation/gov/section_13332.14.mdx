# GOV § 13332.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Fiscal Affairs > Article: 2.5. Provisions Applicable to Appropriations of Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 13332.14

---

## Text

No expenditures for park furnishings shall be made unless the Department of Parks and Recreation has made reasonable efforts to acquire the furnishings on a consolidated procurement basis or through California Conservation Corps labor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 323, Sec. 44. Effective July 1, 1983.)
