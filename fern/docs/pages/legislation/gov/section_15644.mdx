# GOV § 15644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Surveys of Local Assessment Procedures

**Citation:** GOV § 15644

---

## Text

The surveys shall incorporate reviews of existing assessment procedures and practices as well as recommendations for their improvement in conformity with the information developed in the surveys as to what is required to afford the most efficient assessment of property for tax purposes in the counties or cities and counties concerned.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1087, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1997.)
