# GOV § 18678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** GOV § 18678

---

## Text

Any person served with a subpena to appear and testify or to produce books or papers issued in the course of any such investigation or hearing who disobeys or neglects to obey such subpena is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 123.)
