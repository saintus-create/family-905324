# GOV § 39794

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 14. Municipal and Public Utilities > Article: 2. Facilities in Aid of Public Utilities

**Citation:** GOV § 39794

---

## Text

This article does not extend or enlarge any limitation prescribed by law or charter upon taxation, expenditure of public funds, or the incurring of indebtedness by a city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 39793 by Stats. 1951, Ch. 330.)
