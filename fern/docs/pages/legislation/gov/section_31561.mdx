# GOV § 31561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 4. Membership

**Citation:** GOV § 31561

---

## Text

Any person employed under contract for temporary services requiring professional or highly technical skill is ineligible for membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
