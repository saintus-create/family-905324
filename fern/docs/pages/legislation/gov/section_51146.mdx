# GOV § 51146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.7. Timberland > Article: 5. Removal from Zone

**Citation:** GOV § 51146

---

## Text

A fee imposed under this article shall be indicated on the assessment roll and when so indicated shall become a lien against the parcel of land in the same manner as county general taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 176.)
