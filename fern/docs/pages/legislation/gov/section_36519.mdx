# GOV § 36519

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36519

## Text

The city council may require bonds of any other officer or employee of the city.
