# GOV § 31005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES

**Citation:** GOV § 31005

---

## Text

The board of supervisors of any county, chartered or otherwise, shall not by ordinance establish any minimum or maximum age limits for any county employment, whether as officer, deputy, or assistant, and age shall not be a minimum qualification for any county employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
