# GOV § 32262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 3. Board of Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 32262

---

## Text

A majority of the members may transact any business of the board provided that the second or third member is present.
