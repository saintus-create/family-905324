# GOV § 37392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 2. Leases

**Citation:** GOV § 37392

---

## Text

A city or any of its departments may lease or sublease land to the state for housing personnel and equipment, or to the State Adjutant General for military or armory purposes, for not to exceed 99 years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 454, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2003.)
