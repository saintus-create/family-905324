# GOV § 53466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 3. Lost or Destroyed Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53466

---

## Text

The undertaking required by this article, duly indorsed as approved, shall be filed in the office of the clerk or the treasurer of the issuing local agency as the legislative body directs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 517, Sec. 259.)
