# GOV § 14178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 4. Guaranteed Return Trip Demonstration Project

**Citation:** GOV § 14178

---

## Text

The amount of any grant shall be matched on a dollar-for-dollar basis by the grant applicant. The funds granted, together with the matching funds, shall be used solely for purposes of the guaranteed return trip program, as specified in Section 14180.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1653, Sec. 2.)
