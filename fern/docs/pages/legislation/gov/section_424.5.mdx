# GOV § 424.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. STATE SEAL, FLAG, AND EMBLEMS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 2. State Flag and Emblems

**Citation:** GOV § 424.5

## Text

The California dog-face butterfly (Zerene eurydice) is the official State Insect.
