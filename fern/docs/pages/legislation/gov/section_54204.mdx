# GOV § 54204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 7. Purchases of Supplies and Equipment by Local Agencies

**Citation:** GOV § 54204

---

## Text

If the local agency is other than a city, county, or city and county, the policies provided for in Section 54202 shall be adopted by means of a written rule or regulation, copies of which shall be available for public distribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1912.)
