# GOV § 53316.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 53316.6

---

## Text

The agreement entered into pursuant to Section 53316.2 may provide for the division of responsibility to provide any of the facilities or services among the entities entering into the agreement. The agreement shall provide for the allocation and distribution of the proceeds of any special tax levy among the parties to the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 269, Sec. 7. Effective July 3, 1984.)
