# GOV § 53934

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 13. Priority of Special Assessment Liens

**Citation:** GOV § 53934

---

## Text

The lien of a reassessment or a refunding assessment shall be the same as the original assessment to which it relates. A supplemental assessment is a new assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1465.)
