# GOV § 6807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Computation of Time

**Citation:** GOV § 6807

---

## Text

“Daytime” is the period of time between sunrise and sunset. “Nighttime” is the period of time between sunset and sunrise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 655.)
