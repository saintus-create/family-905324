# GOV § 16160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. In-Lieu Property Tax Relief for Recipients of Public Assistance

**Citation:** GOV § 16160

---

## Text

Out of the amount appropriated to the Controller by Section 16100, he shall expend the sums required by Section 15204.1 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1406.)
