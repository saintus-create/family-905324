# GOV § 53369.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.6. Infrastructure and Revitalization Financing Districts > Article: 4. Tax Increment Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53369.43

---

## Text

The legislative body shall submit the proposal to issue the bonds to the voters who reside within the district. The election shall be conducted in the same manner as the election to create the district pursuant to Section 53369.20 and the two elections may be consolidated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 775, Sec. 1. (AB 229) Effective January 1, 2015.)
