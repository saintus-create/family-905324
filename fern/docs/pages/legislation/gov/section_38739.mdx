# GOV § 38739

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 10. Health and Safety > Article: 5. Water

**Citation:** GOV § 38739

---

## Text

The total costs of works which exclusively serve one city shall be borne by that city exclusively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
