# GOV § 36501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36501

---

## Text

The government of a general law city is vested in:

(a) A city council of at least five members.

(b) A city clerk.

(c) A city treasurer.

(d) A chief of police.

(e) A fire chief.

(f) Any subordinate officers or employees provided by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 506, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2001.)
