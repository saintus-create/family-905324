# GOV § 9353.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.5. Retirement of Legislators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 9353.5

---

## Text

Each member of this system shall file with the board any information affecting his or her status as a member as the board may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 664, Sec. 89. Effective January 1, 2003.)
