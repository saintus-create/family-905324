# GOV § 16604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4.5. Savings and Loan Association Deposits > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 16604

---

## Text

Deposits in any savings and loan association or credit union shall not exceed the total of its net worth.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1035, Sec. 5.)
