# GOV § 56033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 56033

---

## Text

“Detachment” means the exclusion, deletion, or removal from a city or district of any portion of the territory of that city or district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 300, Sec. 20. (AB 1430) Effective January 1, 2012.)
