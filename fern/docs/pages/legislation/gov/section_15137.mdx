# GOV § 15137

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State’s Teletype System > Article: 2. Leases of Teletype Facilities and Equipment

**Citation:** GOV § 15137

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall lease facilities and equipment to connect the state teletype system with the police department of the City of Lompoc if the city assumes the cost of connecting to the system and the annual cost of operating such extension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1433.)
