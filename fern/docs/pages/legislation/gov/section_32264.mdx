# GOV § 32264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 5. County Fire Service Retirement Law > Article: 3. Board of Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 32264

---

## Text

The board may appoint a secretary and provide for the payment from the fund of all its necessary expenses, including secretary hire and printing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
