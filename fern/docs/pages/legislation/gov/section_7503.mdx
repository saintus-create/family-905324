# GOV § 7503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 21. Public Pension and Retirement Plans > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 7503

---

## Text

All state and local public retirement systems shall prepare an annual report in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1978, Ch. 388.)
