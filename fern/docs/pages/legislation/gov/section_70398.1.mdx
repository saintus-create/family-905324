# GOV § 70398.1

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5.7. Superior Court Facilities > Article: 7.1. Superior Court Design-Build Projects

**Citation:** GOV § 70398.1

---

## Text

(a) Subject to the provisions of this article and Section 13332.19, the Judicial Council may procure design-build contracts for public works projects.

(b) The Judicial Council shall develop guidelines for a standard organizational conflict of interest policy, consistent with applicable law, regarding the ability of a person or entity that performs services for the Judicial Council relating to solicitation of a design-build project to submit a proposal as a design-build entity, or to join a design-build team. This conflict of interest policy shall apply to the Judicial Council entering into a design-build contract authorized pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 79, Sec. 30. (AB 143) Effective July 16, 2021.)
