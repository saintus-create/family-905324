# GOV § 100024

**Hierarchy:** Title: 21. THE CALSAVERS RETIREMENT SAVINGS TRUST ACT

**Citation:** GOV § 100024

---

## Text

The board shall include a notice of the existence of, and the internet website address for, the Retirement Investments Clearinghouse in a notice disseminated to eligible employers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 21, Sec. 8. (AB 102) Effective June 29, 2020.)
