# GOV § 65064.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PLANNING AND ZONING > Title: 7. PLANNING AND LAND USE > Chapter: 2. Regional Planning Districts > Article: 4. Governing Body

**Citation:** GOV § 65064.1

---

## Text

The board is the governing body of the district and shall exercise all of the powers of the district, except as otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1811.)
