# GOV § 23241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Boundaries > Article: 4.5. Alteration of Boundaries

**Citation:** GOV § 23241

---

## Text

All petitions shall be filed with the clerk of the transferring county. All counterparts of a petition shall be filed at the same time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1393.)
