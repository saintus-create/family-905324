# GOV § 53389.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.7. Community Rehabilitation District Law > Article: 5. Senior Obligation Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53389.3

---

## Text

When two or more propositions for incurring indebtedness pursuant to this chapter are submitted at the same election, the votes cast for and against each proposition shall be counted separately.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 906, Sec. 1.)
