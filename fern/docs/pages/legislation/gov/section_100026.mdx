# GOV § 100026

**Hierarchy:** Title: 21. THE CALSAVERS RETIREMENT SAVINGS TRUST ACT

**Citation:** GOV § 100026

---

## Text

A vendor may not charge a fee associated with a registered product that is not disclosed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 734, Sec. 3. (SB 1234) Effective January 1, 2013.)
