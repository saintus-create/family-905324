# GOV § 55055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 3. Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 55055

---

## Text

This chapter does not prohibit the local agency itself from constructing or completing the works and employing the necessary labor without advertisement for proposals or letting of a contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
