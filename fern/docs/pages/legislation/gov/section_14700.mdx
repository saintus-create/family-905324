# GOV § 14700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 4. State Burial Grounds

**Citation:** GOV § 14700

---

## Text

The Department of General Services has control of the state burial grounds in the City of Sacramento, and of the Union Cemetery in San Mateo County accepted by Chapter 1096 of the Statutes of 1947.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 371.)
