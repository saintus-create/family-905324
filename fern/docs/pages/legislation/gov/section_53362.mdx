# GOV § 53362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.5. The Mello-Roos Community Facilities Act of 1982 > Article: 5. Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53362

---

## Text

The legislative body may, by resolution, issue new bonds to refund any or all of the district bonds outstanding or improvement area bonds outstanding that have been issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 269, Sec. 29. Effective July 3, 1984.)
