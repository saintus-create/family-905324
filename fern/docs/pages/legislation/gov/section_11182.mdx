# GOV § 11182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. State Departments > Article: 2. Investigations and Hearings

**Citation:** GOV § 11182

---

## Text

The head of a department may delegate the powers conferred upon him by this article to any officer of the department he authorizes to conduct the investigation or hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
