# GOV § 54038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 2. Automobile Parking Areas

**Citation:** GOV § 54038

---

## Text

This article provides an alternative authorization for the acquisition and use of property by local agencies for the parking of motor vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
