# GOV § 53212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Officers and Employees > Article: 1.1. Deferred Compensation

**Citation:** GOV § 53212

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means a county, city, public district, joint powers agency, or any public or municipal corporation.
