# GOV § 20069.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 20069.1

---

## Text

“Trial court” shall have the same meaning as the term is defined in the Trial Court Employment Protection and Governance Act (Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 71600) of Title 8).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1010, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
