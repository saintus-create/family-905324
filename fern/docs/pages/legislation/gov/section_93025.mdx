# GOV § 93025

**Hierarchy:** Title: 12. GREAT REDWOOD TRAIL AGENCY ACT > Chapter: 3. Powers and Duties of Authority

**Citation:** GOV § 93025

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 93004.5, the state is not liable for any contracts, debts, or other obligations of the North Coast Railroad Authority, its predecessor in interest, or the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 424, Sec. 8. (SB 727) Effective January 1, 2026.)
