# GOV § 14672.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties, Generally > Article: 2. State Property

**Citation:** GOV § 14672.25

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 14670, the director, with the consent of the State Department of Developmental Services, may lease to the City of San Jose for the purpose of constructing and maintaining a fire station for a period not to exceed 75 years, real property of approximately two acres located on the grounds of Agnew State Hospital.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1062, Sec. 1. Effective September 24, 1987.)
