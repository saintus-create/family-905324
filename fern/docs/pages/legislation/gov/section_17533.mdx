# GOV § 17533

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Commission on State Mandates

**Citation:** GOV § 17533

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 11425.10, Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 11400) of Part 1 of Division 3 does not apply to a hearing by the commission under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 938, Sec. 55. Effective January 1, 1996. Operative July 1, 1997, by Sec. 98 of Ch. 938.)
