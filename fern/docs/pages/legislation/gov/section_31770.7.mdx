# GOV § 31770.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 11.5. Deferred Retirement Option Program

**Citation:** GOV § 31770.7

---

## Text

The right of a participant to benefits under the program is not subject to execution or any other process, except to the extent permitted by Section 704.110 of the Code of Civil Procedure, and is unassignable except as specifically provided under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 897, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
