# GOV § 1193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 7. Deputies and Subordinates

**Citation:** GOV § 1193

---

## Text

When the number of deputies or other subordinate officers is not fixed by law, it is limited only by the discretion of the appointing power.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
