# GOV § 64531

**Hierarchy:** Title: 6.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL HOUSING FINANCE > Chapter: 4. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 64531

---

## Text

The authority board shall provide for annual financial reports. The authority board shall make copies of the annual financial reports available to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 598, Sec. 1. (AB 1487) Effective January 1, 2020.)
