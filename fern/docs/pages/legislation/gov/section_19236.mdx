# GOV § 19236

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 9. Hiring of Disabled Persons

**Citation:** GOV § 19236

---

## Text

The department shall provide technical assistance, statewide advocacy, coordination, and monitoring of plans to overcome any underrepresentation determined pursuant to Section 19234.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 405, Sec. 4. (SB 846) Effective September 14, 2018.)
