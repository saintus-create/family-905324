# GOV § 55004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. Joint Sanitation Projects > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 55004

---

## Text

All condemnation actions may be brought by and in the name of one local agency designated by all of the parties to the agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
