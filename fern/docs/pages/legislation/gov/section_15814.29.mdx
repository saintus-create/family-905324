# GOV § 15814.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 2.7. Energy Conservation in Public Buildings

**Citation:** GOV § 15814.29

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (f) of Section 15814.11, for the purposes of this chapter “state agency” also shall include any local government as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 5921.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 15814.25 (as added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 234, Sec. 1) by Stats. 2013, Ch. 76, Sec. 90. (AB 383) Effective January 1, 2014.)
