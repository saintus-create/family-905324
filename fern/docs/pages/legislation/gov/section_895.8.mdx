# GOV § 895.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 21. Tort Liability Under Agreements Between Public Entities

**Citation:** GOV § 895.8

---

## Text

Except for Section 895.6, this chapter applies to any agreement between public entities, whether entered into before or after the effective date of this chapter. Section 895.6 applies to any agreement between public entities entered into, or renewed, modified, or extended, after the effective date of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1681.)
