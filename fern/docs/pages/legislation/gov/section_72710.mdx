# GOV § 72710

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 9. Los Angeles County

**Citation:** GOV § 72710

---

## Text

On order of the court, the per diem fees and benefits of official reporters pro tempore shall be paid from the Reporter’s Salary Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 398. Effective January 1, 2003.)
