# GOV § 8878.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.45. California Earthquake Safety and Housing Rehabilitation Bond Act of 1988 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 8878.33

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that, inasmuch as the proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this chapter are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution, the disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by that article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 27, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 77 at the June 7, 1988, election.)
