# GOV § 18001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Salaries > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 18001

---

## Text

Every employee has the right to inspect personnel records pursuant to Section 1198.5 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 886, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
