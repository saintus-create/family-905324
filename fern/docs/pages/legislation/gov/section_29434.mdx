# GOV § 29434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 6. Sheriff’s Funds

## Text

The population basis is that fixed by the last decennial federal census.
