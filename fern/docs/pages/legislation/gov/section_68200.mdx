# GOV § 68200

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 1.5. Compensation of Justices and Judges of Courts of Record

**Citation:** GOV § 68200

---

## Text

The annual salary of the Chief Justice of California is ninety three thousand one hundred forty dollars ($93,140).
