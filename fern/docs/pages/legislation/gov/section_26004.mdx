# GOV § 26004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 10. Transportation and Airports > Article: 1. Transportation Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 26004

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may install and maintain watering troughs along any state highway subject to agreement as to location with the Department of Transportation, and along any county road or stock trail in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 681, Sec. 16.)
