# GOV § 21765

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Part: 3.4. INTERNAL REVENUE CODE COMPLIANCE AND REPLACEMENT BENEFIT PLAN > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 19. California Employers’ Pension Prefunding Trust Program

**Citation:** GOV § 21765

---

## Text

The Legislature reserves the power and right to amend this part, as needed to effect its purposes. This part shall be controlling over any memorandum of understanding reached between employers and employees pursuant to Chapter 10 (commencing with Section 3500), Chapter 10.3 (commencing with Section 3512), or Chapter 12 (commencing with Section 3560) of Division 4 of Title 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996. Conditionally inoperative as prescribed in Section 21763.)
