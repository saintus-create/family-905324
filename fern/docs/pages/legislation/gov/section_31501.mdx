# GOV § 31501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 2. Establishment of System

**Citation:** GOV § 31501

---

## Text

This chapter becomes operative in any county on either January 1st or July 1st following the adoption of the ordinance, as specified in the ordinance, but not sooner than 60 days after its adoption.

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
