# GOV § 6735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Holidays

**Citation:** GOV § 6735

---

## Text

The Governor shall annually proclaim December 1 as World AIDS Day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 30, Sec. 1. (SB 1278) Effective January 1, 2025.)
