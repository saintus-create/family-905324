# GOV § 22013.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. General Provisions, Definitions, Designation of Special Groups > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** GOV § 22013.5

---

## Text

“Policeman” as used in this part also includes persons employed as members of a state university or state college police department who are peace officers and whose principal duties consist of law enforcement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 703.)
