# GOV § 54953.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 9. Meetings

**Citation:** GOV § 54953.7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, legislative bodies of local agencies may impose requirements upon themselves which allow greater access to their meetings than prescribed by the minimal standards set forth in this chapter. In addition thereto, an elected legislative body of a local agency may impose those requirements on appointed legislative bodies of the local agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 327, Sec. 7. (SB 707) Effective January 1, 2026.)
