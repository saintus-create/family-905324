# GOV § 19062.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5. Appointments > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 19062.5

---

## Text

The department may establish eligibility requirements governing movement of employees between full-time, part-time, and intermittent positions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 427, Sec. 56. (AB 1062) Effective January 1, 2014.)
