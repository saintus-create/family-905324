# GOV § 7298

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 17.5. Use of a Foreign Language in Public Services

**Citation:** GOV § 7298

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are not applicable to school districts, county boards of education, or the office of a county superintendent of schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1182.)
