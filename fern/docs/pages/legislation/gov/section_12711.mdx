# GOV § 12711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. Farmworker Resource Center Grant Program

**Citation:** GOV § 12711

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the following definitions apply:

(a) “Center” means a farmworker resource center.

(b) “Department” means the Department of Community Services and Development.

(c) “Program” means the grant program established and administered by the department pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 203, Sec. 1. (AB 941) Effective January 1, 2022.)
