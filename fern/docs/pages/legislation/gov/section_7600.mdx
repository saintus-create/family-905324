# GOV § 7600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SECURITIES OWNED BY STATE AGENCIES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Investment of Securities Owned by State Agencies > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 7600

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that:

(a) Specified state agencies be authorized to invest marketable securities by entering into security loan agreements;

(b) State agencies charged with such authority exercise prudence in making such agreements;

(c) Sound fiscal management be established with respect to transactions involving security agreements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1214.)
