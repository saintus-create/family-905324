# GOV § 19810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration

**Citation:** GOV § 19810

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “local agency” means any city, county, city and county, district, or other subdivision of the state, or any independent instrumentality thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1283.)
