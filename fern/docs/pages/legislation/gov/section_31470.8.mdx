# GOV § 31470.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 31470.8

---

## Text

In cases of doubt as to whether a person is eligible to become a safety member, the board shall decide.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 31470.6 by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1301.)
