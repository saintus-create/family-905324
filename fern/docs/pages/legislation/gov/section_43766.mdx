# GOV § 43766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 4. Bonds > Article: 4. Investment Bond Act of 1909

**Citation:** GOV § 43766

---

## Text

The interest rate on the bonds need not be the same during the entire term and different rates may be fixed for one or more interest payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
