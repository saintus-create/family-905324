# GOV § 29412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. FINANCIAL PROVISIONS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Funds > Article: 5.5. District Attorney’s Family or Child Support Trust Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 29412

---

## Text

The local child support agency shall use the trust fund solely for the purpose of advancing reimbursement for moneys erroneously attached or intercepted by a government agency for payment of a delinquent support obligation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 808, Sec. 103. Effective September 28, 2000.)
