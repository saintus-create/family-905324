# GOV § 12096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development

**Citation:** GOV § 12096

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Economic Revitalization Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 475, Sec. 2. (AB 29) Effective January 1, 2012.)
