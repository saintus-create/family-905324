# GOV § 21119

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. Retirement from Employment > Article: 4. Reduced Worktime for Partial Service Retirement

**Citation:** GOV § 21119

---

## Text

Article 3 (commencing with Section 21310) of Chapter 13, relating to cost-of-living adjustments, shall not apply to an employee who is participating in reduced worktime for partial service retirement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 379, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
