# GOV § 8880.69

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 8. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.69

---

## Text

Preemption of Local Laws

It is the intent of this Chapter that all matters related to the operation of the Lottery as established hereby be governed solely pursuant to this Chapter and be free from regulation or legislation of local governments, including a city, city and county, or county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
