# GOV § 38303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 8. Parks Outside City Limits

**Citation:** GOV § 38303

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 38302, such franchises shall be granted pursuant to Part 4, Title 4, Division 1, of the Civil Code and Chapter 578 of the Statutes of 1905.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
