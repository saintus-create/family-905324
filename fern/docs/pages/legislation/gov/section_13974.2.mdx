# GOV § 13974.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.5. Indemnification of Citizens Benefiting the Public

**Citation:** GOV § 13974.2

---

## Text

Any reference to Article 2 (commencing with Section 13970) of Chapter 5, as it read on December 31, 2002, shall be construed to refer to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1141, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2003.)
