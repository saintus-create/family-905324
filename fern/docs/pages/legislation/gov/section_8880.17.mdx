# GOV § 8880.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12.5. [California State Lottery Act of 1984] > Article: 2. California State Lottery Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8880.17

---

## Text

Qualifications of Commissioners

At least one of the Commissioners shall have a minimum of five years experience in law enforcement, and at least one of the Commissioners shall be a certified public accountant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 6, 1984, by initiative Proposition 37.)
