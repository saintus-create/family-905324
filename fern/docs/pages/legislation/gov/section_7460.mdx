# GOV § 7460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. MISCELLANEOUS > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 20. Governmental Access to Financial Records > Article: 1. Declaration of Policy

## Text

This chapter shall be known as the “California Right to Financial Privacy Act.”
