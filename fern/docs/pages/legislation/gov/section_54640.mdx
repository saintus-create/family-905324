# GOV § 54640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6. Revenue Bond Law of 1941 > Article: 10. Remedies of Bondholders

**Citation:** GOV § 54640

---

## Text

Subject to any contractual limitations binding upon a bondholder or his trustee, any bondholder or his trustee has the remedies set forth in this article for the equal benefit and protection of all bondholders similarly situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
