# GOV § 58099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. The District Organization Law > Article: 4. Final Hearing

**Citation:** GOV § 58099

---

## Text

Written protests shall be filed with the clerk on or before the time fixed for the final hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 331.)
