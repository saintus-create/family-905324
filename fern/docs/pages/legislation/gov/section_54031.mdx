# GOV § 54031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 2. Automobile Parking Areas

**Citation:** GOV § 54031

---

## Text

By gift, lease, purchase, eminent domain, or other manner a local agency may acquire property for parking motor vehicles.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
