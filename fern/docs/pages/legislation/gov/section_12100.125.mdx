# GOV § 12100.125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.6. Governor’s Office of Business and Economic Development > Article: 11. Local Government Budget Sustainability Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 12100.125

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect until June 30, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 68, Sec. 24. (SB 193) Effective June 30, 2022. Repealed as of June 30, 2028, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 11, commencing with 12100.120.)
