# GOV § 54125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Property > Article: 5. Earthquake Relief

## Text

As used in this article:

“Board” means the State Allocation Board.

“Local agency” means any city, city and county, county, or public district.
