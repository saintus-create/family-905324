# GOV § 33011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. County Peace Officers and Fire Service Retirement, Disability and Death Benefit Plans

**Citation:** GOV § 33011

---

## Text

It shall be competent in any plan adopted under this chapter to provide for the inclusion therein, as members thereof, the city police officers and city firemen of any municipal corporation which elects to bring itself within the operation of such plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1479.)
