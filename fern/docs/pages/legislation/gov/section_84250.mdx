# GOV § 84250

**Hierarchy:** Title: 9. POLITICAL REFORM > Chapter: 4. Campaign Disclosure > Article: 2.5. LAFCO Proposal Requirements

**Citation:** GOV § 84250

---

## Text

All requirements of this title applicable to a measure, as defined in Section 82043, also apply to a LAFCO proposal, as defined in Section 82035.5, except as set forth in Section 84252.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 192, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
