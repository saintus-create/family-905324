# GOV § 19790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. PERSONNEL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 12. State Civil Service Equal Employment Opportunity Program

**Citation:** GOV § 19790

---

## Text

(a) Each state agency is responsible for an effective equal employment opportunity program. The equal opportunity program shall include a reasonable accommodation policy.

(b) The department shall be responsible for statewide advocacy, coordination, enforcement, monitoring of the programs described in subdivision (a), and the development of model policies, including a model policy on reasonable accommodation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 515, Sec. 5. (AB 313) Effective January 1, 2022.)
