# GOV § 100100

**Hierarchy:** Title: 21.1. CALACCOUNT BLUE RIBBON COMMISSION

**Citation:** GOV § 100100

---

## Text

As used in this title:

(a) “CalAccount Program” means the proposed program described in paragraph (1) of subdivision (a) of Section 100104.

(b) “Commission” means the CalAccount Blue Ribbon Commission convened pursuant to Section 100102.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 451, Sec. 3. (AB 1177) Effective January 1, 2022. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 100106. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 100108.)
