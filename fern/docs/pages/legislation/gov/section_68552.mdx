# GOV § 68552

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 2. The Judicial Council > Article: 3. Coordinated Educational Programs for the Judiciary

**Citation:** GOV § 68552

---

## Text

In carrying out its duties under this article, the Judicial Council may publish and distribute manuals, guides, checklists and other materials designed to assist the judiciary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 412.)
