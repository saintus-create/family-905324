# GOV § 9149.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1.5. General > Article: 12. Legislative Discriminatory Harassment Retaliation Prevention Act

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Legislative Discriminatory Harassment Retaliation Prevention Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 952, Sec. 2. (SB 419) Effective September 30, 2018.)
