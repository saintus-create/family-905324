# GOV § 8821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8821

---

## Text

The commission may make grants to public broadcasting stations in order to aid in the improvement of their broadcast operations, programming, and capital facilities and equipment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
