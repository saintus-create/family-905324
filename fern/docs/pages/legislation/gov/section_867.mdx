# GOV § 867

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 8. Activities to Abate an Impending Peril

**Citation:** GOV § 867

---

## Text

An employee of a local public entity is not liable for damages for injury to persons or property resulting from an impending peril or from any action taken to abate such peril pursuant to Section 866.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1119.)
