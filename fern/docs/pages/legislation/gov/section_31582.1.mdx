# GOV § 31582.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 5. Financial Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 31582.1

---

## Text

In any county in which the board of retirement so provides, the county auditor shall not be required to make the certifications required by Section 31582.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1317.)
