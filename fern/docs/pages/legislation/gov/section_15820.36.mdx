# GOV § 15820.36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3.7. Libraries and Related Educational Facilities in Public Universities > Article: 2. Library Facilities

**Citation:** GOV § 15820.36

---

## Text

Any public building or facility which is constructed or renovated and equipped pursuant to this chapter shall be utilized only for library and related educational purposes, as set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 836, Sec. 1.)
