# GOV § 12230

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Secretary of State > Article: 4. State Archives

**Citation:** GOV § 12230

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall establish a Document Laboratory and a Record Processing Program to facilitate the preservation and description of the archives.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 145, Sec. 4. (AB 1759) Effective January 1, 2024.)
