# GOV § 67503

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 2. Definitions

## Text

“Authority” means the San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority created by this title.
