# GOV § 8690.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7.5. California Disaster Assistance Act > Article: 5. Funds

**Citation:** GOV § 8690.25

---

## Text

The Disaster Assistance Fund, referred to as “fund” in this article, is hereby created as a special fund in the State Treasury. This fund and its subsidiary account, the Earthquake Emergency Investigations Account, are continuously appropriated, without regard to fiscal years, for purposes of this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 461, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2003.)
