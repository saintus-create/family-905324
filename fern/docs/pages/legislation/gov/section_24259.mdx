# GOV § 24259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 6. Offices and Office Hours

**Citation:** GOV § 24259

---

## Text

If the officer has a branch office to which a single deputy is assigned, he may close the branch office during the hour from 12 o'clock noon to 1 o'clock p.m.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
