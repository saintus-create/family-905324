# GOV § 38416

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 9. Discontinuance of Parks > Article: 1. Parks Dedicated by Original Town Site Map or Plat

**Citation:** GOV § 38416

---

## Text

The legislative body may determine when and at what price or terms the land or any part of it will be sold.

Credit terms shall not exceed four years, and deferred payments shall bear interest at 7 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
