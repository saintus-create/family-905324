# GOV § 61000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. COMMUNITY SERVICES DISTRICTS > Title: 6. DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Introductory Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 61000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Community Services District Law.
