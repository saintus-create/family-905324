# GOV § 23302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of New Counties > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** GOV § 23302

---

## Text

Any action to determine the validity of the formation of a county pursuant to this chapter shall be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 699, Sec. 4.5. (SB 894) Effective January 1, 2011.)
