# GOV § 26427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 15. Revenue Bonds for County Incinerators > Article: 2. Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 26427

---

## Text

An indenture may include a clause limiting the power of the board to apply the proceeds of the sale of any issue of bonds for the acquiring, constructing, or completing of any project or any part thereof, or more than one of such projects.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 822.)
