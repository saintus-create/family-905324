# GOV § 16527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 4. Bank Deposits > Article: 2. Security for Deposits

**Citation:** GOV § 16527

---

## Text

On demand of the Treasurer, the Insurance Commissioner shall issue a certificate showing the qualifications of any admitted surety insurer as surety for demand or time deposits.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 582.)
