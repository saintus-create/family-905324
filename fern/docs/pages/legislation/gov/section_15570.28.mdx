# GOV § 15570.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 3. Transfer of Duties, Powers, and Responsibilities

**Citation:** GOV § 15570.28

---

## Text

The department shall provide the board with administrative and personnel services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 16, Sec. 5. (AB 102) Effective June 27, 2017. Operative July 1, 2017, pursuant to Section 15570.100.)
