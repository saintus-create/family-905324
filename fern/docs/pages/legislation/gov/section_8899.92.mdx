# GOV § 8899.92

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 17. Geographic Feature and Place Name Prohibitions

**Citation:** GOV § 8899.92

---

## Text

The Natural Resources Agency shall direct the California Advisory Committee on Geographic Names to revise its existing charter and amend it to include the execution of the duties, powers, and responsibilities listed under Section 8899.94.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 479, Sec. 2. (AB 2022) Effective January 1, 2023.)
