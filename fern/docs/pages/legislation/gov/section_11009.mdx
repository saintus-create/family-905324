# GOV § 11009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. State Agencies > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 11009

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided by law, the members of State boards and commissions shall serve without compensation, but shall be allowed necessary expenses incurred in the performance of duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
