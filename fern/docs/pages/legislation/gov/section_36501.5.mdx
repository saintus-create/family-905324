# GOV § 36501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Part: 1. GENERAL > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES

**Citation:** GOV § 36501.5

## Text

Every employee has the right to inspect personnel records pursuant to Section 1198.5 of the Labor Code

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 886, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2001.)
