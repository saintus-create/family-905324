# GOV § 8818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 2. California Public Broadcasting Commission

**Citation:** GOV § 8818

---

## Text

The meetings of the commission shall be open and public in accordance with Article 9 (commencing with Section 11120) of Chapter 1 of Part 1 of Division 3.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 142, Sec. 33.)
