# GOV § 50633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Registration of Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 50633

---

## Text

Upon the direction of the legislative body, the treasurer, having the duty of paying the interest on an issue of ad valorem special assessment district improvement bonds, shall keep a register. Upon presentation of an interest coupon of such bond, he shall enter in the register the name and address of the owner or holder and the number and amount of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81.)
