# GOV § 67545

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.8. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA TRANSPORTATION TERMINAL AUTHORITY > Chapter: 3. San Francisco Bay Area Transportation Terminal Authority > Article: 3. General Powers

**Citation:** GOV § 67545

---

## Text

The authority shall not have power to levy taxes for any purpose whatsoever.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1373.)
