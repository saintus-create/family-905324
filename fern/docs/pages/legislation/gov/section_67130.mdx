# GOV § 67130

**Hierarchy:** Title: 7.5. CALIFORNIA TAHOE REGIONAL PLANNING AGENCY > Chapter: 6. Dissolution and Deactivation

**Citation:** GOV § 67130

---

## Text

The state may dissolve the California Tahoe Regional Planning Agency by enacting a statute repealing this title.

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1589.)
