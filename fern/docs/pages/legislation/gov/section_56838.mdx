# GOV § 56838

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 5. Proceedings for Special Districts > Article: 2. Reorganization

**Citation:** GOV § 56838

---

## Text

The clerk of a subject district shall give immediate notice to the executive officer of all appointments and removals made by the legislative body to a reorganization committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 761, Sec. 159. Effective January 1, 2001.)
