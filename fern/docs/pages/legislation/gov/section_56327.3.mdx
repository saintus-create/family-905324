# GOV § 56327.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. CORTESE-KNOX-HERTZBERG LOCAL GOVERNMENT REORGANIZATION ACT OF 2000 > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2. Formation of Commission and Selection of Commissioners

**Citation:** GOV § 56327.3

---

## Text

In Santa Clara County, the commission shall be enlarged by two members if, pursuant to the provisions of Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 56821), the commission orders representation of special districts upon the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 114, Sec. 6. (AB 1532) Effective January 1, 2016.)
