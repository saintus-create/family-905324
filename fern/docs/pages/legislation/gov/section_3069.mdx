# GOV § 3069

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. PUBLIC OFFICERS AND EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 7. Removal from Office > Article: 3. Removal Other Than by Impeachment

**Citation:** GOV § 3069

---

## Text

If the defendant pleads guilty, or refuses to answer the accusation, the court shall render judgment of conviction against him. If he denies the matters charged, the court shall immediately, or at such time as it appoints, try the accusation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 134.)
