# GOV § 925.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3.6. CLAIMS AND ACTIONS AGAINST PUBLIC ENTITIES AND PUBLIC EMPLOYEES > Title: 1. GENERAL > Chapter: 4. Presentation of Claims to State Controller

**Citation:** GOV § 925.8

---

## Text

If the Controller approves a claim he shall draw his warrant for the amount approved in favor of the claimant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1715.)
