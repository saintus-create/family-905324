# GOV § 70012

**Hierarchy:** Title: 8. THE ORGANIZATION AND GOVERNMENT OF COURTS > Chapter: 5. The Superior Courts > Article: 10.6. Official Reporters in Counties With a Population of 210,000 to 220,000

**Citation:** GOV § 70012

---

## Text

The regular official phonographic reporters and phonographic reporters pro tempore shall be compensated at the same rate as reporters in counties having a population over 2,000,000.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1035.)
