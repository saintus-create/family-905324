# GOV § 8330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 5.1. Citizen Complaint Act of 1997

**Citation:** GOV § 8330

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Citizen Complaint Act of 1997. All state agencies that have Internet websites shall implement this act in a manner that is consistent with the statewide strategy for electronic commerce as established by the Department of Information Technology.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 416, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
