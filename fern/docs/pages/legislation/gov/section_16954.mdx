# GOV § 16954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. FISCAL AFFAIRS > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 8. The California Pension Restructuring Bond Act of 2004 > Article: 2. Issuance of Bonds to Finance the Program

**Citation:** GOV § 16954

---

## Text

The net proceeds of bonds issued and sold pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the Pension Obligation Bond Fund established pursuant to Section 16929.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 215, Sec. 4. Effective August 11, 2004.)
