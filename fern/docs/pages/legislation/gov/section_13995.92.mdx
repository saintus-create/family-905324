# GOV § 13995.92

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. California Tourism Marketing Act > Article: 9. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** GOV § 13995.92

---

## Text

The assessment for the passenger rental car industry rate shall be set by the commission, shall be no more than 3.5 percent, and shall be set at a level that will generate no more than 60 percent of all expenditures by the commission as set forth in Section 13995.45.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 320, Sec. 2. (SB 1119) Effective January 1, 2015.)
