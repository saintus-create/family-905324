# GOV § 53397.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 2.8. Infrastructure Financing Districts > Article: 4. Tax Increment Bonds

**Citation:** GOV § 53397.1

---

## Text

The legislative body may, by majority vote, initiate proceedings to issue bonds pursuant to this chapter by adopting a resolution stating its intent to issue the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1575, Sec. 1.)
