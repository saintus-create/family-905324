# GOV § 53510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 5. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 53510

---

## Text

As used in this article “local agency” means county, city, city and county, public district or any public or municipal corporation, public agency or public authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2118.)
