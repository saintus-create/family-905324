# GOV § 54702.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 6.3. Employee Rental Housing > Article: 3. Bonds and Notes

**Citation:** GOV § 54702.6

---

## Text

The local agency may purchase its bonds from any source of funds available therefor. The local agency may hold, pledge, cancel, or resell such bonds, subject to and in accordance with agreements with the bondholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1092, Sec. 1.)
