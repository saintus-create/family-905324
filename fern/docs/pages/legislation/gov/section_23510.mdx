# GOV § 23510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. COUNTIES GENERALLY > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3.5. Consolidation of Counties > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** GOV § 23510

---

## Text

Proceedings for the consolidation of two or more contiguous counties may be initiated by petition, or by resolution of the board of supervisors of each affected county filed with the board of supervisors of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1391.)
