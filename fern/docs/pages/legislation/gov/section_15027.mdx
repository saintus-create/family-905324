# GOV § 15027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. EXECUTIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 1. Administration > Article: 2. Organized Crime

**Citation:** GOV § 15027

---

## Text

The department’s functions concerning organized crime shall be divided among five programs as follows:

(a) Operations and training.

(b) Intelligence.

(c) Long-range intelligence research.

(d) Investigation.

(e) Prosecution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1795.)
