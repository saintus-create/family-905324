# GOV § 37615.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 7. Hospitals Managed by Board of Hospital Trustees

**Citation:** GOV § 37615.2

---

## Text

The board of trustees may purchase real property, and erect or rent and equip those buildings or building, room or rooms as may be necessary for the hospital.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 72, Sec. 17. Effective May 28, 1992.)
