# GOV § 26102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 11. Advertising

**Citation:** GOV § 26102

---

## Text

The tax authorized by this chapter is in addition to any tax authorized by this part to be levied for fair purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
