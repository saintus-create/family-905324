# GOV § 31674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. EMPLOYEES > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 3. County Employees Retirement Law of 1937 > Article: 8. Retirement for Service

**Citation:** GOV § 31674

---

## Text

The service retirement annuity is an annuity which is the actuarial equivalent of his accumulated contributions at the time of his retirement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 424.)
