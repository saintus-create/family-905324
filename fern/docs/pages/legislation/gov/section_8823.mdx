# GOV § 8823

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 10. Public Broadcasting Act of 1975 > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** GOV § 8823

---

## Text

The commission may, taking into account the advice of the radio committee or the T.V. committee, as appropriate, make grants for the development, production, and acquisition of public broadcast programming.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1227.)
