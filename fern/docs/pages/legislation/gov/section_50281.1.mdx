# GOV § 50281.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. CITIES AND COUNTIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 1. General > Article: 12. Historical Property Contracts

**Citation:** GOV § 50281.1

---

## Text

The legislative body entering into a contract described in this article may require that the property owner, as a condition to entering into the contract, pay a fee that shall not exceed the reasonable cost of providing the service pursuant to this article for which the fee is charged.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 278, Sec. 2. (AB 654) Effective January 1, 2012.)
