# GOV § 55607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Fire and Police Protection > Article: 1. Performance of Fire Protection Functions by County Firewarden or State Forester

**Citation:** GOV § 55607

---

## Text

The Department of Forestry and Fire Protection may contract with the county for the performance by the county fire warden, his or her deputies, and assistants of functions for the prevention or suppression of fires within the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 427, Sec. 69. Effective January 1, 1993.)
