# GOV § 37350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. OFFICERS > Title: 4. GOVERNMENT OF CITIES > Chapter: 5. City Property > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** GOV § 37350

---

## Text

A city may purchase, lease, receive, hold, and enjoy real and personal property, and control and dispose of it for the common benefit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 79.)
