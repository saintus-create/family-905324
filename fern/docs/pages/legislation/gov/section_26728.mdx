# GOV § 26728

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. OFFICERS > Title: 3. GOVERNMENT OF COUNTIES > Chapter: 2. Sheriff > Article: 7. Fees

**Citation:** GOV § 26728

---

## Text

The fee for preparing and posting the initial notice of personal property sale under a writ of attachment, execution, or sale or order of court shall be the amount described in Section 26720.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 629, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001.)
