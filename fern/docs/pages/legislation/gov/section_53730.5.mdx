# GOV § 53730.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CITIES, COUNTIES, AND OTHER AGENCIES > Title: 5. LOCAL AGENCIES > Chapter: 4. Financial Affairs > Article: 4. Capital Outlay Fund

**Citation:** GOV § 53730.5

---

## Text

As used in this article, “local agency” means a city, county, or district empowered to levy and collect assessments or taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 53730 (as added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 81) by Stats. 1987, Ch. 56, Sec. 87.)
