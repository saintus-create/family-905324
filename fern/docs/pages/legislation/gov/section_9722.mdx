# GOV § 9722

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. LEGISLATIVE DEPARTMENT > Title: 2. GOVERNMENT OF THE STATE OF CALIFORNIA > Chapter: 7. Legislative Printing and Publications > Article: 2. Daily Journals

**Citation:** GOV § 9722

---

## Text

Each daily Journal and the Journals of the Senate and Assembly shall be printed by the State Printing Office. Each daily Journal shall be printed in book form separately, and the Journals of the Senate and Assembly shall be bound in book form.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 111.)
