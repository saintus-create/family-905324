# ELEC § 318

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 318

---

## Text

“Election” means any election including a primary that is provided for under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
