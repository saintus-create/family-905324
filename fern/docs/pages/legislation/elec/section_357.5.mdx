# ELEC § 357.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 357.5

---

## Text

“Vote center” means a location established for holding elections that offers the services described in Sections 2170 and 4005.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 479, Sec. 2. (AB 1762) Effective January 1, 2024.)
