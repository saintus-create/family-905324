# ELEC § 11328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 3. Elections in General

**Citation:** ELEC § 11328

---

## Text

A recall election shall be conducted, canvassed, and the results declared in substantially the manner provided by law for a regular election for the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
