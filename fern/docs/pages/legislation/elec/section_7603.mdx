# ELEC § 7603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7603

---

## Text

Each delegate to the state convention shall appoint to membership on this committee one voter of the same sex as the delegate and two voters of the opposite sex. These appointments when so made shall be absolute.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
