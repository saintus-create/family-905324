# ELEC § 11383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 4. Recall Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 11383

---

## Text

If one-half or more of the votes at a recall election are “No”, the officer sought to be recalled shall continue in office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
