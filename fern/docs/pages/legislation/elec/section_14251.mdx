# ELEC § 14251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 3. Challenging a Voter

**Citation:** ELEC § 14251

---

## Text

Any doubt in the interpretation of the law shall be resolved in favor of the challenged voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
