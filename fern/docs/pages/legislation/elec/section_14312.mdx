# ELEC § 14312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 5. Provisional Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14312

---

## Text

This article shall be liberally construed in favor of the provisional voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 175, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
