# ELEC § 7407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7407

---

## Text

A person shall not be eligible for appointment or election to a committee who is not registered as affiliated with this party at the time of his or her appointment or election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
