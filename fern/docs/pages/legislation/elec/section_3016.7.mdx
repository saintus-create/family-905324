# ELEC § 3016.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VOTE BY MAIL VOTING, NEW RESIDENT, AND NEW CITIZEN VOTING > Chapter: 1. Vote by Mail Application and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 3016.7

---

## Text

The county elections official shall permit any voter to cast a ballot using a certified remote accessible vote by mail system, regardless of whether the voter is a voter with disabilities or a military or overseas voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 312, Sec. 3. (AB 37) Effective January 1, 2022.)
