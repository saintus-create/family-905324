# ELEC § 9321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 4. District Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9321

---

## Text

If the provisions of two or more ordinances adopted at the same election conflict, the ordinance receiving the highest number of affirmative votes shall control.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
