# ELEC § 14100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Precinct Supplies

**Citation:** ELEC § 14100

---

## Text

The county elections official, in providing the materials required by this division, shall not be required to utilize the services of the county purchasing agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
