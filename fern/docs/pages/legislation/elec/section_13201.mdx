# ELEC § 13201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 13201

---

## Text

The ballots of each political party’s central committee shall be designed so that each ballot may be easily and clearly distinguished from, and not confused with, a ballot of any other political party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 199, Sec. 43. Effective January 1, 1999.)
