# ELEC § 8064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 4. Circulation and Signatures—Nomination Documents

**Citation:** ELEC § 8064

---

## Text

The declaration of candidacy shall be obtained from, and delivered to, the elections official of the county in which the candidate resides and is a voter in accordance with Section 8028.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
