# ELEC § 7880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 3. General

**Citation:** ELEC § 7880

---

## Text

A committee shall have charge of the party campaign under general direction of the state central committee or of the executive committee selected by the state central committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
