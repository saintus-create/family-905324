# ELEC § 10556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 4. UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10556

---

## Text

No informalities in the conduct of the general district election or any matters related to it shall invalidate the election if fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
