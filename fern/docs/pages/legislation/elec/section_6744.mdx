# ELEC § 6744

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Determination of Number to National Convention

**Citation:** ELEC § 6744

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, the boundary between the northern and southern territories of the state shall be that latitudinal line coinciding with the northern boundaries of San Luis Obispo, Kern, and San Bernardino Counties.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
