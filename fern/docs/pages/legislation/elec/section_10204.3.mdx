# ELEC § 10204.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 1.2. The Congressional Term Limits Act

**Citation:** ELEC § 10204.3

---

## Text

The California Legislature, due to the desire of the People of the State of California to establish term limits on the Congress of the United States, is hereby instructed to make the following application to Congress, pursuant to its power under Article V of the U.S. Constitution:

“We, the People and Legislature of the State of California, due to our desire to establish term limits on the Congress of the United States, hereby make application to Congress, pursuant to our power under Article V of the U.S. Constitution, to call a convention for proposing amendments to the Constitution.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added June 2, 1998, by initiative Proposition 225. Repealed conditionally as prescribed in Section 10204.10.)
