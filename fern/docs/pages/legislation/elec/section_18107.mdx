# ELEC § 18107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Voter Registration

**Citation:** ELEC § 18107

---

## Text

Every person who willfully violates Section 2158 is guilty of an infraction, punishable by a fine not to exceed two hundred dollars ($200).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
