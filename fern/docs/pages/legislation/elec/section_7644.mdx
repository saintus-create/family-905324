# ELEC § 7644

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 4. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7644

---

## Text

A quorum of this committee is a majority of the entire membership, represented either in person or by proxy.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
