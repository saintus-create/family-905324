# ELEC § 8801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Part: 4. WITHDRAWAL OF CANDIDATES:  VACANCIES > Chapter: 3. Term Limits

**Citation:** ELEC § 8801

---

## Text

No candidate nominated at any primary election may withdraw as a candidate at the ensuing general election except those candidates permitted to withdraw by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
