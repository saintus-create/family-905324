# ELEC § 2168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 2. Registration > Article: 4. Forms

**Citation:** ELEC § 2168

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall establish and maintain a statewide system to facilitate the reporting of election results and voter and candidate information, and to otherwise administer and enhance election administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 38. (AB 1020) Effective January 1, 2016. Operative September 26, 2016, when the Secretary of State issued the certification prescribed by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 88.)
