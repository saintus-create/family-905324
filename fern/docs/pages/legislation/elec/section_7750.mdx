# ELEC § 7750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Members of Central Committees > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7750

---

## Text

At each presidential primary election, members of central committees may be elected in each county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 507, Sec. 21. (SB 1272) Effective January 1, 2013.)
