# ELEC § 6700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 6700

---

## Text

This chapter shall be applicable to the presidential primary ballot of the Peace and Freedom Party only.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
