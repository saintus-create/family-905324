# ELEC § 9120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 2. County Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9120

## Text

Article 3 (commencing with Section 9160) shall govern the procedures for submitting arguments for county initiatives.
