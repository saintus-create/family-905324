# ELEC § 9307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 4. District Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9307

---

## Text

Each section of the petition shall have attached thereto the affidavit of the person soliciting the signatures. This affidavit shall be substantially in the same form as set forth in Section 9022.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 278, Sec. 24. (SB 213) Effective January 1, 2014.)
