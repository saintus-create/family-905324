# ELEC § 8042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 3. Form of Nomination Documents

**Citation:** ELEC § 8042

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall establish uniform filing forms for candidates to use when filing a declaration of candidacy pursuant to Section 8040 and nomination papers pursuant to Section 8041.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 148, Sec. 1. (AB 2577) Effective January 1, 2023.)
