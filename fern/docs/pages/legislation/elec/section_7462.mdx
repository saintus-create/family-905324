# ELEC § 7462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 5. District Committees

**Citation:** ELEC § 7462

---

## Text

Each committee shall consist of the persons elected to the county central committee of a single Assembly or supervisorial district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
