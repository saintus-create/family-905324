# ELEC § 10200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 10200

---

## Text

This chapter shall be liberally construed to promote its objects, and no error, omission or irregularity shall invalidate an election if there has been a substantial compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
