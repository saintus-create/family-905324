# ELEC § 15600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 9. Recount > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 15600

---

## Text

Except as provided in this chapter, this chapter applies to all elections. The recount of votes cast for candidates for presidential electors shall be governed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
