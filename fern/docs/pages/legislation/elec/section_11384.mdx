# ELEC § 11384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 4. Recall Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 11384

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 11382, if a majority of the votes on a recall proposal are “Yes”, the officer sought to be recalled shall be removed from office upon the qualification of the officer’s successor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 790, Sec. 5. (AB 2582) Effective January 1, 2023.)
