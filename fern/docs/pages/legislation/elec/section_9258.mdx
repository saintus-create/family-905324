# ELEC § 9258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9258

---

## Text

The petition may be circulated in sections, but each section shall contain a correct copy of the text of the proposed amendment.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
