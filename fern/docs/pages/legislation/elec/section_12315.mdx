# ELEC § 12315

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Precinct Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 12315

---

## Text

If the inspector ceases to act, a majority of the remaining members of the precinct board may appoint a substitute.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
