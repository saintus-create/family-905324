# ELEC § 7400.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7400.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 7400 and 7401, in the County of Placer, the county central committee shall be elected by supervisor districts and shall be composed of seven members elected from each of the supervisor districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 111, Sec. 1. (AB 1148) Effective January 1, 2016.)
