# ELEC § 8604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** ELEC § 8604

---

## Text

No fee or charge shall be required of a write-in candidate except in the case of a candidate for city office, as provided in Section 10228.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
