# ELEC § 19222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 3. Certification of Voting Systems > Article: 2. Voting System Testing Agencies

**Citation:** ELEC § 19222

---

## Text

The person, corporation, or public agency applying for certification of a voting system is responsible for all costs associated with the testing of the voting system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 602, Sec. 35. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2014.)
