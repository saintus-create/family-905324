# ELEC § 7620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 2. Meetings

**Citation:** ELEC § 7620

---

## Text

The committee shall convene in the same community where the state convention is held, at 10 a.m. on the Sunday following the state convention.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 199, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 1999.)
