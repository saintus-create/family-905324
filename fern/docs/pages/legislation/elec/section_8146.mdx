# ELEC § 8146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 8. Nominated Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 8146

---

## Text

Certificates of nomination or election issued by county or municipal officers shall be issued immediately upon the completion of the canvass of the returns at the primary election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
