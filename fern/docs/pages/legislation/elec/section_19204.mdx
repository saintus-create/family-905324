# ELEC § 19204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 3. Certification of Voting Systems > Article: 1. Procedures for Certification of Voting Systems

**Citation:** ELEC § 19204

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall not certify or conditionally approve any voting system that includes features that permit a voter to produce, and leave the polling place with, a copy or facsimile of the ballot cast by the voter at that polling place.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19200.5 by Stats. 2013, Ch. 602, Sec. 11. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2014.)
