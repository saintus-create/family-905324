# ELEC § 12287

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Precincts > Article: 5. Polling Places

**Citation:** ELEC § 12287

---

## Text

A candidate’s residence shall not be designated as a polling place for an election at which that candidate’s name will appear on the ballot.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 29, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
