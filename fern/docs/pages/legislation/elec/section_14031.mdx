# ELEC § 14031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1.5. Rights of Voters

**Citation:** ELEC § 14031

---

## Text

This chapter is enacted to implement the guarantees of Section 7 of Article I and of Section 2 of Article II of the California Constitution.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 129, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
