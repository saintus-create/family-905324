# ELEC § 7871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 2. Meetings

**Citation:** ELEC § 7871

---

## Text

All meetings of the committee shall be held in quarters which shall be accessible to persons with disabilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
