# ELEC § 12221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Precincts > Article: 2. Precinct Formation

**Citation:** ELEC § 12221

---

## Text

In any order establishing precincts, their boundaries shall be defined by reference to exterior descriptions or delineation thereof on a map or maps.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 725, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1997.)
