# ELEC § 16620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 7. Court’s Duties > Article: 2. Primary Elections:  Contests Other than Recount

**Citation:** ELEC § 16620

---

## Text

The court shall meet at the time and place designated in the order setting the contest for trial, and shall have all powers necessary to determine the issues.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
