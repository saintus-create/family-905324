# ELEC § 6406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 6. Arrangement and Examination of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6406

---

## Text

No filing fee is required from any person to be voted for at a presidential primary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
