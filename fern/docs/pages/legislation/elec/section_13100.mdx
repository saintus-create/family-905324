# ELEC § 13100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 2. Forms of Ballots: Ballot Order

**Citation:** ELEC § 13100

---

## Text

All ballots used in all elections shall be governed by this chapter unless otherwise specifically provided.
