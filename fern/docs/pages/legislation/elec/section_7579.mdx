# ELEC § 7579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Convention > Article: 3. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7579

---

## Text

The vote on any issue at the convention, including the election of officers, may be by rollcall or any other method as may be prescribed by the convention.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
