# ELEC § 18361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Election Campaigns > Article: 6. Solicitation of Funds

**Citation:** ELEC § 18361

---

## Text

Upon the complaint of the affected candidate or committee, any person who violates Section 20202 or 20203 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
