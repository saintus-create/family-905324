# ELEC § 6791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6791

---

## Text

Circulators may obtain signatures to the nomination paper of the candidate or group of candidates at any time not more than 120 nor less than 81 days prior to the presidential primary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 149, Sec. 17. (SB 505) Effective July 30, 2019.)
