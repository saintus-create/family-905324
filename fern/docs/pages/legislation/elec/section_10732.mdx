# ELEC § 10732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. Vacancies in Congressional Offices caused by Catastrophe

**Citation:** ELEC § 10732

---

## Text

Within seven calendar days of a catastrophe, the Governor shall issue a proclamation calling a special election pursuant to Section 10703.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 658, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2003.)
