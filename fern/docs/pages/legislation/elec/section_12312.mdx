# ELEC § 12312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Precinct Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 12312

---

## Text

A person shall not be suspended or discharged from any service or employment because of absence while serving as an election officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 37. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
