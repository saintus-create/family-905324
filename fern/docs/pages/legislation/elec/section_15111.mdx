# ELEC § 15111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Vote by Mail Ballot Processing

**Citation:** ELEC § 15111

---

## Text

The elections official shall keep an accurate list of all voters who have received and voted a vote by mail ballot at each election and compare this list with the roster as provided in Section 15278. That list shall include the election precinct of the voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 71. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
