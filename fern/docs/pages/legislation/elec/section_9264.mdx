# ELEC § 9264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9264

---

## Text

A voter may withdraw his or her signature from a petition in the manner prescribed in Section 9602.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
