# ELEC § 14203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 1. Election Day Posting Requirements

**Citation:** ELEC § 14203

---

## Text

The precinct officer shall post the signs required by Section 14106 in plain view within each voting booth or compartment whenever an election has been canceled pursuant to Section 8026.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
