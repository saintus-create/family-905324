# ELEC § 10226.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 2. Nomination of Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 10226.5

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall establish uniform filing forms for candidates to use when filing nomination papers pursuant to Section 10226 and a declaration of candidacy pursuant to Section 10226.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 109, Sec. 10. (AB 2582) Effective January 1, 2025.)
