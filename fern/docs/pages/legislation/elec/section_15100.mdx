# ELEC § 15100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Vote by Mail Ballot Processing

**Citation:** ELEC § 15100

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply to the processing of vote by mail ballots during the 29-day period before any election, during the semifinal official canvass, and during the official canvass.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 508, Sec. 86. Effective January 1, 2008.)
