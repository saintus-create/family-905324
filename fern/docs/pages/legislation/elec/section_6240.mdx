# ELEC § 6240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 13. Write-in Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 6240

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a space shall be provided on the presidential primary ballot for a voter to write in the name of a candidate for nomination by the Democratic Party for President of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 190, Sec. 24. (AB 1689) Effective January 1, 2011.)
