# ELEC § 11005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Procedures: General Provisions and Initial Steps in the Recall > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 11005

---

## Text

The proponents of a recall must be registered voters of the electoral jurisdiction of the officer they seek to recall.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
