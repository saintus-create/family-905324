# ELEC § 9109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 2. County Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9109

---

## Text

Each petition section shall have attached to it an affidavit to be completed by the circulator. The affidavit shall be substantially in the same form as set forth in Section 104.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
