# ELEC § 10243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 3. Conduct of Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 10243

---

## Text

A majority of the members of any precinct board shall be present at the polling place at all times while the polling place is open.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
