# ELEC § 16803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 9. Costs

**Citation:** ELEC § 16803

---

## Text

The provisions relating to costs in contested final elections apply to primary election contests conducted under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
