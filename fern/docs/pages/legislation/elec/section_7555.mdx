# ELEC § 7555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Convention > Article: 1. Delegates

**Citation:** ELEC § 7555

---

## Text

Membership in the state convention shall not be granted to any party nominee unless he or she is registered as being a member of this party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
