# ELEC § 6422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 7. Canvass of Returns. Certificate of Election

**Citation:** ELEC § 6422

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall, not later than the 32nd day after the election, issue a certification to each person selected as a delegate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 55, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
