# ELEC § 13211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 13211

---

## Text

The names of the candidates shall be printed on the ballot, without indentation, in at least 10-point bold type.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 26. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
