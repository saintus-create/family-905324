# ELEC § 325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 325

---

## Text

“Inspector” means the supervisor of the precinct board of which he or she is a member.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 2. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
