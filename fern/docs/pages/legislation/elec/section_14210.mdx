# ELEC § 14210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 2. Election Day Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 14210

---

## Text

The members of each precinct board shall distribute the duties devolving upon the precinct board, which are in addition to their individual duties, in a manner they deem most advantageous.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
