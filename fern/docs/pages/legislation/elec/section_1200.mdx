# ELEC § 1200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ESTABLISHED ELECTION DATES > Chapter: 3. Statewide Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 1200

---

## Text

The statewide general election shall be held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in November of each even-numbered year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1143, Sec. 21. Effective September 30, 1996.)
