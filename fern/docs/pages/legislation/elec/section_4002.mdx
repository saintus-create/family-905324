# ELEC § 4002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MAIL BALLOT ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Conditions for Mail Ballot Election

**Citation:** ELEC § 4002

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 4000, a special district may conduct its elections by mail in accordance with Sections 1500, 4104, 4105, and 4108.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 86, Sec. 1. Effective July 19, 2005.)
