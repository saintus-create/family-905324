# ELEC § 14431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Closing of the Polls > Article: 3. Return of Supplies to the Clerk

**Citation:** ELEC § 14431

---

## Text

The precinct board shall enclose and seal in one or more packages, as determined by the elections official, all voted, spoiled, canceled, or unused ballots.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1999.)
