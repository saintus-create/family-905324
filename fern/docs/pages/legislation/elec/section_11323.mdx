# ELEC § 11323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** ELEC § 11323

---

## Text

A voter shall indicate, by completely selecting the voting target next to either “Yes” or “No,” their vote for or against the recall proposal, respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 13. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
