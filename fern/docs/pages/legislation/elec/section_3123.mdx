# ELEC § 3123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VOTE BY MAIL VOTING, NEW RESIDENT, AND NEW CITIZEN VOTING > Chapter: 2. Military and Overseas Voters Application and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 3123

---

## Text

A court may issue an injunction or grant other equitable relief appropriate to ensure substantial compliance with, or enforce, this chapter on application by either of the following:

(a) A military or overseas voter.

(b) An elections official in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 744, Sec. 26. (AB 1805) Effective January 1, 2013.)
