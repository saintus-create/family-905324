# ELEC § 15110

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Vote by Mail Ballot Processing

**Citation:** ELEC § 15110

---

## Text

Reports to the Secretary of State of the findings of the canvass of vote by mail ballots shall be made by the elections official pursuant to Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 15150) and Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 15300).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 508, Sec. 94. Effective January 1, 2008.)
