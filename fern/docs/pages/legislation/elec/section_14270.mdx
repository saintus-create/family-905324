# ELEC § 14270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 4. Issuing Ballots and Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14270

---

## Text

The procedure at the polls where voting is conducted pursuant to this division shall be the same as at other polling places, except as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
