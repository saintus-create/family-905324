# ELEC § 13212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 13212

---

## Text

Except for a voter-nominated office at a general election, under the designation of each office shall be printed as many blank spaces, defined by light lines, as there are candidates to be nominated or elected to the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 28. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
