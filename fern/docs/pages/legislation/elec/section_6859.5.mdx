# ELEC § 6859.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Preparation, Circulation, and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6859.5

---

## Text

The certificate of the elections official to nomination papers of a candidate shall be in substantially the following form:CERTIFICATE OF COUNTY ELECTIONS OFFICIAL TO NOMINATION PAPERS OF A CANDIDATETo the Secretary of State:I, County Elections Official of the County of ______, hereby certify that I have examined the nomination papers, to which this certificate is attached, of the presidential candidate, and that the number of names which I have not marked “not sufficient” is _______. The candidate named in the nomination papers is comprised of the following:_______________________________________________________Dated this ________ day of ________, 20___.________________________(SEAL)County Elections OfficialBy Deputy

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
