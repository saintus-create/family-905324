# ELEC § 9080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 7. State Voter Information Guide

**Citation:** ELEC § 9080

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 9084 to 9093, inclusive, are a restatement of, and shall be construed in conformity with, Sections 88001 to 88007, inclusive, of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
