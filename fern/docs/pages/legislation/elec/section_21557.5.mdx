# ELEC § 21557.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 6.6. County of Merced Citizens Redistricting Commission

**Citation:** ELEC § 21557.5

---

## Text

(a) A commission member shall not communicate with any individual or organization regarding redistricting matters outside of a public meeting. This subdivision does not restrict a commission member from communicating with the staff of the board regarding administrative matters of the commission.

(b) This subdivision does not restrict a commission member from directly communicating with another commission member, commission staff, legal counsel, or consultants retained by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 730, Sec. 1. (AB 1441) Effective January 1, 2026.)
