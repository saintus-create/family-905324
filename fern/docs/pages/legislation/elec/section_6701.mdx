# ELEC § 6701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 6701

---

## Text

The Peace and Freedom Party presidential primary ballot shall be divided into a presidential preference portion and a national convention delegate selection portion.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
