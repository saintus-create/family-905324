# ELEC § 7906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Members of County Councils

**Citation:** ELEC § 7906

---

## Text

The removal of residence by an elected or appointed member of a county council from the applicable county shall constitute automatic resignation from the county council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 4. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
