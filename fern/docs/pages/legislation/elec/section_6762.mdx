# ELEC § 6762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 4. Qualification of Group of Candidates for National Convention Delegate Portion of Primary Ballot

**Citation:** ELEC § 6762

---

## Text

The number of candidates for delegate grouped on a nomination paper shall be equal to the total number of delegates to be elected by the Peace and Freedom Party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
