# ELEC § 8002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 8002

---

## Text

If a candidate is a candidate for a nonpartisan office, all reference to party affiliation shall be omitted on all forms required to be filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
