# ELEC § 16460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 5. Form of Contest Statement > Article: 4. Contests Involving a Recount

**Citation:** ELEC § 16460

---

## Text

This article applies only to contests on the ground that due to mistake, error, or misconduct the votes in any precinct were so incorrectly counted as to change the result.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
