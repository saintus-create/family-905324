# ELEC § 15504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 7. Duties of the Secretary of State

**Citation:** ELEC § 15504

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall make out and deliver, or transmit by mail, a certificate of election or nomination to each person elected or nominated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
