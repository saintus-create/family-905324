# ELEC § 9053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 5. Ballot Titles

**Citation:** ELEC § 9053

---

## Text

Each measure shall be designated on the ballot by the ballot label certified by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 751, Sec. 6. (AB 1416) Effective January 1, 2023.)
