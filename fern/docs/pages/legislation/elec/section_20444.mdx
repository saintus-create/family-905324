# ELEC § 20444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. ELECTION CAMPAIGNS > Chapter: 5. Fair Campaign Practices > Article: 3. Code of Fair Campaign Practices

**Citation:** ELEC § 20444

---

## Text

In no event shall a candidate for public office be required to subscribe to or endorse the code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
