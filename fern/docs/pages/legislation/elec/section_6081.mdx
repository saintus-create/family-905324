# ELEC § 6081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Steering Committees

**Citation:** ELEC § 6081

---

## Text

The chairperson of a steering committee, at least 82 days prior to the presidential primary, shall file with the Secretary of State a statement containing the names and addresses of the members of the steering committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 791, Sec. 6. Effective October 10, 1999.)
