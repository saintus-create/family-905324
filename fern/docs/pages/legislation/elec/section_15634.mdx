# ELEC § 15634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 9. Recount > Article: 3. Voter-Requested Recounts

**Citation:** ELEC § 15634

---

## Text

This chapter does not:

(a) Authorize the opening or recounting of ballots for any precinct except for the purposes specified in this chapter.

(b) Limit other provisions of law regarding an election contest or recount.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
