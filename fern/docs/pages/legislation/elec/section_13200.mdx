# ELEC § 13200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 13200

---

## Text

Ballots not printed in substantial compliance with this chapter shall not be cast nor counted at any election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 15. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
