# ELEC § 6581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation, and Filing of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6581

---

## Text

Nomination papers properly prepared, circulated, signed, and verified shall be left for examination with the elections official of the county in which they are circulated at least 81 days prior to the presidential primary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 370, Sec. 110. (SB 1371) Effective January 1, 2021.)
