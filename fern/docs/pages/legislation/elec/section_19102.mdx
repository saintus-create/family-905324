# ELEC § 19102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 2. Secretary of State Requirements

**Citation:** ELEC § 19102

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall study the performance of voting systems in use in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 602, Sec. 7. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2014.)
