# ELEC § 9319

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 4. District Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9319

---

## Text

Any number of proposed ordinances may be voted upon at the same election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
