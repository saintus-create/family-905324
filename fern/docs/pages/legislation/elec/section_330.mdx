# ELEC § 330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 330

---

## Text

“Municipal election” means elections in general law cities and where applicable in chartered cities.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
