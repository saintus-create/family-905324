# ELEC § 8080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 5. Verification of Nomination Signatures

**Citation:** ELEC § 8080

---

## Text

No fee or charge shall be made or collected by any officer for verifying any nomination document or circulator’s affidavit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
