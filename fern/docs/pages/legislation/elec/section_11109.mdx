# ELEC § 11109

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Recall of State Officers: Intermediate Steps in the Recall

**Citation:** ELEC § 11109

---

## Text

When the Secretary of State determines that the proponents have collected sufficient signatures, he or she shall certify that fact to the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 714, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1997.)
