# ELEC § 6761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 4. Qualification of Group of Candidates for National Convention Delegate Portion of Primary Ballot

**Citation:** ELEC § 6761

---

## Text

The committee, on or before the 104th day before the presidential primary election, shall file with the Secretary of State a statement containing the name of the committee and the names, residence addresses and telephone numbers of at least five of its members, and its officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
