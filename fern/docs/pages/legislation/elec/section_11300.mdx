# ELEC § 11300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 11300

---

## Text

No insufficiency in a petition against any officer shall bar the later filing of a new petition against that officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
