# ELEC § 6863.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Certification of National Convention Delegates Election and Postelection Proceedings

**Citation:** ELEC § 6863.5

---

## Text

National convention delegates shall be selected as provided for in the bylaws and the rules and procedures of the Green Party and pursuant to the rules of the national political party with which the Green Party is affiliated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
