# ELEC § 7580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Convention > Article: 3. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7580

---

## Text

The proceedings at the convention shall be governed by any manual of parliamentary procedure designated by the convention.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
