# ELEC § 6854.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Preparation, Circulation, and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6854.5

---

## Text

Nomination papers properly prepared, circulated, signed, and verified shall be left, for examination, with the elections official of the county in which they are circulated at least 81 days before the presidential preference primary election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 149, Sec. 20. (SB 505) Effective July 30, 2019.)
