# ELEC § 10540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 4. UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10540

---

## Text

Candidates’ statements of their qualifications submitted in accordance with Section 13307 shall be filed with the county elections official, who shall cause the voters’ pamphlet, if any is required, to be mailed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2003.)
