# ELEC § 6861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 4. Green Party Presidential Primary Ballot

**Citation:** ELEC § 6861

---

## Text

Following the filing of nomination papers, the presidential preference primary candidates shall be printed upon the ballot.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
