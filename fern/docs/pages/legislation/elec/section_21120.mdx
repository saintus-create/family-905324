# ELEC § 21120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 2. Fair And Inclusive Redistricting for Municipalities And Political Subdivisions (FAIR MAPS) Act of 2023

**Citation:** ELEC § 21120

---

## Text

This chapter applies to a county, county office of education, general law city, charter city, school district, community college district, or special district whose legislative body is elected by districts or from districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 343, Sec. 14. (AB 764) Effective January 1, 2024.)
