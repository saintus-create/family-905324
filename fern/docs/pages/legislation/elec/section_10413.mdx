# ELEC § 10413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 3. CONSOLIDATION OF ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10413

---

## Text

When the returns of any elections consolidated pursuant to this part are required to be canvassed by the same body, the elections shall be held in all respects as if there were only one election, and only one form of ballot shall be used.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
