# ELEC § 6401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 6. Arrangement and Examination of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6401

---

## Text

Prior to filing, the sections of a nomination paper for a candidate shall be numbered in order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
