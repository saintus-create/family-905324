# ELEC § 11045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Procedures: General Provisions and Initial Steps in the Recall > Article: 3. Recall Petition

**Citation:** ELEC § 11045

---

## Text

Only registered voters of the electoral jurisdiction of the officer sought to be recalled are qualified to sign a recall petition for that officer. A person who meets the requirements of Section 102 may circulate the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 278, Sec. 27. (SB 213) Effective January 1, 2014.)
