# ELEC § 16703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 8. Court’s Decisions, Judgment, and Determinations > Article: 1. General Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 16703

---

## Text

If in any election contest it appears that another person than the defendant has the highest number of legal votes, the court shall declare that person elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
