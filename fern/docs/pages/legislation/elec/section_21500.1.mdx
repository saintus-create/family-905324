# ELEC § 21500.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 6. Supervisorial Districts

**Citation:** ELEC § 21500.1

---

## Text

This chapter applies to a county that elects members of the county’s board of supervisors by districts or from districts.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 343, Sec. 16. (AB 764) Effective January 1, 2024.)
