# ELEC § 14430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Closing of the Polls > Article: 3. Return of Supplies to the Clerk

**Citation:** ELEC § 14430

---

## Text

The precinct board, as soon after the polls are closed as possible, shall prepare the supplies, including the copies of the voter list or roster posted at or near the polling place, and records of the election for delivery to the elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 69. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
