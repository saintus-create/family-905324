# ELEC § 9403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 5. Bond Issues

**Citation:** ELEC § 9403

---

## Text

Failure to comply with this chapter shall not affect the validity of any bond issue following the sale and delivery of the bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
