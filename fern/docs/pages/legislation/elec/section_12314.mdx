# ELEC § 12314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Precinct Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 12314

---

## Text

The inspector may appoint a voter to replace any precinct board member who ceases to act or becomes incapacitated during the progress of an election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 725, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1997.)
