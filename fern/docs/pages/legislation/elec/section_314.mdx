# ELEC § 314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 314

---

## Text

“County officer” means any elected officer enumerated in Division 2 (commencing with Section 24000) of Title 3 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
