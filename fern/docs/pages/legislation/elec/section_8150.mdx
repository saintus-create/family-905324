# ELEC § 8150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 8. Nominated Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 8150

---

## Text

The certificate of the Secretary of State showing candidates nominated or selected at a primary election, and justices of the Supreme Court and courts of appeal to appear on the general elections ballot, shall be in a form prescribed by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 1, Sec. 30. (SB 6) Effective January 1, 2010. Operative January 1, 2011, pursuant to Sec. 67 of Ch. 1.)
