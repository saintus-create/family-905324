# ELEC § 8407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 3. Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 8407

---

## Text

The affidavit of any circulator obtaining signatures under this chapter shall be verified free of charge by any officer authorized to administer oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
