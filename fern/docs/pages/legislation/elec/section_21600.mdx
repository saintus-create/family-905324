# ELEC § 21600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 7. City Elections > Article: 1. General Law Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 21600

---

## Text

This article applies to a general law city that elects members of the city’s legislative body by districts or from districts, as those terms are defined in Section 34871 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 343, Sec. 29. (AB 764) Effective January 1, 2024.)
