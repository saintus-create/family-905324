# ELEC § 14402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Closing of the Polls > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 14402

---

## Text

Any one who arrives at the polling place after the time provided for closing the polls shall not be entitled to vote, even though the polls are open when the voter arrives.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
