# ELEC § 8800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Part: 4. WITHDRAWAL OF CANDIDATES:  VACANCIES > Chapter: 3. Term Limits

**Citation:** ELEC § 8800

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 8020.5, a candidate whose declaration of candidacy has been filed for any primary election shall not withdraw as a candidate at that primary election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 355, Sec. 8. (AB 1784) Effective January 1, 2025.)
