# ELEC § 14434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Closing of the Polls > Article: 3. Return of Supplies to the Clerk

**Citation:** ELEC § 14434

---

## Text

The sealed packages containing the lists, papers, and ballots shall be delivered by two of its members without delay, unopened, to the elections official or to a receiving station designated by the elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1999.)
