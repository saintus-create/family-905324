# ELEC § 9268

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9268

---

## Text

The conduct of election and publication requirements shall substantially conform with Part 1 (commencing with Section 10000) and Part 2 (commencing with Section 10100) of Division 10.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
