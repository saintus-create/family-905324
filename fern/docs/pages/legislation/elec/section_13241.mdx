# ELEC § 13241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 3. Vote Tabulating Devices

**Citation:** ELEC § 13241

---

## Text

The names of the candidates and the respective offices shall be printed on the ballot in parallel columns at least 2 inches wide.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 57, Sec. 8. (AB 2835) Effective January 1, 2019.)
