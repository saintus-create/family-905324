# ELEC § 9143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 2. County Elections > Article: 2. Referendum

**Citation:** ELEC § 9143

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 9141, that portion of any ordinance that changes supervisorial salaries shall become effective 60 days from the date of its final passage.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
