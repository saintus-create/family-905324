# ELEC § 7226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 2. Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 7226

---

## Text

The elections official, no later than January 31 preceding the presidential primary, shall compute the number of members of the committee allotted to each Assembly district or supervisor district, as the case may be, pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 507, Sec. 3. (SB 1272) Effective January 1, 2013.)
