# ELEC § 12301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Precinct Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 12301

---

## Text

The persons appointed to serve as election officers for each precinct at any election shall constitute the precinct board for that precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
