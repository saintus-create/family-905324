# ELEC § 2200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Cancellation and Voter File Maintenance > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 2200

---

## Text

The registration of a voter is permanent for all purposes during his or her life, unless and until the affidavit of registration is canceled by the Secretary of State or the county elections official for any of the causes specified in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 55. (AB 1020) Effective January 1, 2016. Operative September 26, 2016, when the Secretary of State issued the certification prescribed by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 88.)
