# ELEC § 15321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Official Canvass > Article: 2. Processing Vote by Mail Ballots and Mail Ballot Precinct Ballots

**Citation:** ELEC § 15321

---

## Text

For any statewide election or special election to fill a vacancy in a congressional or legislative office, votes cast by vote by mail ballot and votes cast at the polling place shall be tabulated by precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 508, Sec. 104. Effective January 1, 2008.)
