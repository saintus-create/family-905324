# ELEC § 7700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 7700

---

## Text

This part applies to the organization, operation, and functions of that political party known as the Peace and Freedom Party of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
