# ELEC § 11044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Procedures: General Provisions and Initial Steps in the Recall > Article: 3. Recall Petition

**Citation:** ELEC § 11044

---

## Text

Separate petitions are necessary to propose the recall of each officer.
