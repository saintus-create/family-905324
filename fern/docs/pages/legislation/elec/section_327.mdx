# ELEC § 327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 327

---

## Text

“Judicial officer” means any Justice of the Supreme Court, justice of a court of appeal, or judge of the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 784, Sec. 94. Effective January 1, 2003.)
