# ELEC § 6646

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 7. Postelection Proceedings

**Citation:** ELEC § 6646

---

## Text

If a vacancy exists in the office of the delegate or alternate, the chairperson of the delegation shall designate a person to fill that vacancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
