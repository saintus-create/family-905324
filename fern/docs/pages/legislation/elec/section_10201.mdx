# ELEC § 10201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 10201

---

## Text

A proposition may be submitted at a regular election, or a special election may be called, by ordinance or resolution, for the purpose of voting on a proposition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
