# ELEC § 346

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 346

---

## Text

“Rebuttable presumption” shall be deemed a presumption which affects the burden of producing evidence.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
