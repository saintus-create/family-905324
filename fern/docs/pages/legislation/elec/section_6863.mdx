# ELEC § 6863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Certification of National Convention Delegates Election and Postelection Proceedings

**Citation:** ELEC § 6863

---

## Text

The number of delegates to be selected following the presidential preference primary election shall be the number established by the national political party with which the Green Party is affiliated.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 107. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
