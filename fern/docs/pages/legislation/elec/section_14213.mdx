# ELEC § 14213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 2. Election Day Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 14213

---

## Text

Before the precinct board receives any ballots, it shall proclaim aloud at the place of election that the polls are open.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
