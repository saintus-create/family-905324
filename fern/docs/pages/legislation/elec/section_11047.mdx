# ELEC § 11047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Procedures: General Provisions and Initial Steps in the Recall > Article: 3. Recall Petition

**Citation:** ELEC § 11047

---

## Text

When a petition is circulated in more than one county for the recall of an officer, each section of the petition shall bear the name of the county for which it is circulated, and only registered voters of that county may sign that section.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 156, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2005.)
