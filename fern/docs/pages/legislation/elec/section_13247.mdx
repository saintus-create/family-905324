# ELEC § 13247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 3. Vote Tabulating Devices

**Citation:** ELEC § 13247

---

## Text

The statement of all measures submitted to the voters shall be abbreviated on the ballot in a ballot label as provided for in Section 9051. The ballot label shall be followed by the words, “Yes” and “No”, except for a statewide referendum measure. The ballot label for a statewide referendum measure shall be followed by the words, “Keep the law” and “Overturn the law.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 162, Sec. 9. (AB 421) Effective September 8, 2023.)
