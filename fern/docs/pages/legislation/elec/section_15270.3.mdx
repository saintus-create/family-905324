# ELEC § 15270.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 5. Manual Vote Count in the Precinct

**Citation:** ELEC § 15270.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 15270.1 or subdivision (a) of Section 19207.5, an elections official may conduct a manual vote count in the event of a natural disaster or other state of emergency in which use of a certified voting system is not feasible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 300, Sec. 3. (AB 969) Effective October 4, 2023.)
