# ELEC § 13216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 3. Ballot Printing Specifications > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 13216

---

## Text

A ballot stub may be used for a ballot. The ballots may contain printed and distinguishing marks if secrecy in voting is protected.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 32. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
