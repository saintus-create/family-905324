# ELEC § 1100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ESTABLISHED ELECTION DATES > Chapter: 2. Election Day

**Citation:** ELEC § 1100

---

## Text

No election shall be held on any day other than a Tuesday, nor shall any election be held on the day before, the day of, or the day after, a state holiday.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1143, Sec. 20. Effective September 30, 1996.)
