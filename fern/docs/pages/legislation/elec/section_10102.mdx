# ELEC § 10102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 10102

---

## Text

Section 13107, relating to ballot designations, shall apply to municipal elections, whether held in a general law or chartered city.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
