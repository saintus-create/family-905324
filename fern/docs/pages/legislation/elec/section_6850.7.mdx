# ELEC § 6850.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 6850.7

---

## Text

A filing fee shall not be required from a person to be voted for in a Green Party presidential primary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
