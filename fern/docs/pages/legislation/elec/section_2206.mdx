# ELEC § 2206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Cancellation and Voter File Maintenance > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 2206

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall adopt regulations to facilitate the availability of death statistics from the State Department of Health Services. The data shall be used by the Secretary of State or county elections officials in canceling the affidavit of registration of deceased persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 60. (AB 1020) Effective January 1, 2016. Operative September 26, 2016, when the Secretary of State issued the certification prescribed by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 88.)
