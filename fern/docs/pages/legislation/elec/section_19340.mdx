# ELEC § 19340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 4. Elections Using Voting Machines > Article: 3. Precincts and Board Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 19340

---

## Text

Any member of a precinct board who has not previously attended a training class in the use of the voting machines and the duties of a board member shall be required to do so, unless appointed to fill an emergency vacancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
