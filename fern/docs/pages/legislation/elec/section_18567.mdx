# ELEC § 18567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 6. Corruption of the Voting Process > Article: 4. Corruption of Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 18567

---

## Text

Every person who willfully adds to or subtracts from the votes actually cast at an election, in any official or unofficial returns, or who alters the returns, is punishable by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 of the Penal Code for 16 months or two or three years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 84. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.)
