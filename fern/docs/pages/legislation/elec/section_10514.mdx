# ELEC § 10514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 4. UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10514

---

## Text

The qualifications of a candidate for elective office, and of an elective officer, of a district shall be determined by the principal act of that district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
