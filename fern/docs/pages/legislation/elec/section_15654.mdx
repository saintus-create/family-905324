# ELEC § 15654

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 10. Tie Votes > Article: 1. Elections Other than Primary Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 15654

---

## Text

In case any two or more persons have an equal and highest number of votes for either Governor or Lieutenant Governor, the Legislature shall, by a joint vote of both houses, choose one of the persons to fill the office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
