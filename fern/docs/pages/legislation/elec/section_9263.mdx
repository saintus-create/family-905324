# ELEC § 9263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9263

---

## Text

The sheets comprising each petition section shall be fastened together securely and remain so during circulation and filing.
