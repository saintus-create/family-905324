# ELEC § 6911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1.2. Uniform Faithful Presidential Electors Act

**Citation:** ELEC § 6911

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited as, as the Uniform Faithful Presidential Electors Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 216, Sec. 8. (SB 103) Effective January 1, 2023.)
