# ELEC § 14271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 4. Issuing Ballots and Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14271

---

## Text

After the opening of the polls, the precinct board shall not allow any voter to enter the voting booth until it ascertains that he or she is entitled to vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
