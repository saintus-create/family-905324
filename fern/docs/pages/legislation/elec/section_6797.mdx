# ELEC § 6797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6797

---

## Text

The certificate of the elections official to nomination papers of a candidate or group of candidates shall be in substantially the following form: CERTIFICATE OF COUNTY ELECTIONS OFFICIAL TO NOMINATION PAPERS OF CANDIDATE OR GROUP OF CANDIDATESTo the Secretary of State:I, County Elections Official of the County of ______, hereby certify that I have examined the nomination papers, to which this certificate is attached, of the presidential candidate or group of candidates for delegates at the ensuing presidential primary, and that the number of names which I have not marked “not sufficient” is _______. The candidate or group of candidates named in the nomination papers comprise the following (state names of candidates):etc.etc.etc.Dated this ________ day of ________, 20___.________________________(SEAL)County Elections OfficialBy Deputy
