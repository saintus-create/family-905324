# ELEC § 16444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 5. Form of Contest Statement > Article: 3. Contests Other than Recount

**Citation:** ELEC § 16444

---

## Text

No special appearance, demurrer or objection may be taken other than by the affidavits which shall be considered a general appearance in the contest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
