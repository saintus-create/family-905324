# ELEC § 14279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 4. Issuing Ballots and Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14279

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided by law, no person shall apply for or receive any ballot at any precinct other than that in which the voter is entitled to vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
