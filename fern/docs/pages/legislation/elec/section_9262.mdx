# ELEC § 9262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9262

---

## Text

Each petition section shall consist of sheets of white paper, uniform in size, with dimensions no smaller than 812 by 11 inches or greater than 812 by 14 inches.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
