# ELEC § 14285

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 4. Issuing Ballots and Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14285

---

## Text

To vote in a contest where two or more candidates for the same office are to be elected, a voter shall mark the voting targets next to the names of the candidates of the voter’s choice, up to the number of candidates to be elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 46. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
