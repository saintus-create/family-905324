# ELEC § 19105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 2. Secretary of State Requirements

**Citation:** ELEC § 19105

---

## Text

The Secretary of State may investigate any alleged violation of this code or the Secretary of State’s regulations with the power to subpoena all necessary persons and records.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19102 by Stats. 2013, Ch. 602, Sec. 6.5. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2014.)
