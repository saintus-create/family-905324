# ELEC § 6001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 6001

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the “Alquist Open Presidential Primary Act.”

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6000a by Stats. 2019, Ch. 149, Sec. 3. (SB 505) Effective July 30, 2019.)
