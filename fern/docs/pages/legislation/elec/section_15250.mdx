# ELEC § 15250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 3. Automated Vote Count in Precincts

**Citation:** ELEC § 15250

---

## Text

This article applies to all elections in which votes are counted by means of a voting machine at a polling place or vote center.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 73. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
