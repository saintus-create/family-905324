# ELEC § 14214

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 2. Election Day Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 14214

---

## Text

Voting shall commence as soon as the polls are opened and shall be continued during the time the polls remain open.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
