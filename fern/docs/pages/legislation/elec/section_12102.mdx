# ELEC § 12102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Preelection Notices

**Citation:** ELEC § 12102

---

## Text

The notice of the municipal election shall be substantially in the following form:

Notice is hereby given that a ____ (general or special) municipal election will be held in the ____ of ____ on ____, the ____ day of ____, 20__, for the following officers: (name them).

The polls will be open between the hours of __ m. and __ m.City Elections OfficialDated, _______, ___.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 58, Sec. 13. (AB 3259) Effective January 1, 2019.)
