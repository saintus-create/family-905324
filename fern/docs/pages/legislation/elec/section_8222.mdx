# ELEC § 8222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 2. Judicial Officers > Article: 2. Adoption of Subdivision (d) of Section 16 of Article VI of the California Constitution

**Citation:** ELEC § 8222

---

## Text

The petition shall state the name and address of a person to whom notice of the insufficiency of the petition shall be sent in the event the petition has not the required number of signatures of voters signed thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
