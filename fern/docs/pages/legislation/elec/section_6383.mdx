# ELEC § 6383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Circulators

**Citation:** ELEC § 6383

---

## Text

A county elections official or deputy county elections official may not circulate a nomination paper. Circulators may not obtain signatures within 100 feet of any election booth or polling place.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 790, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2000.)
