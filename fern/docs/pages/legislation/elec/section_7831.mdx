# ELEC § 7831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 3. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7831

---

## Text

The temporary chairpersons shall appoint any committees as the state central committee may direct.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
