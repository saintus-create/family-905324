# ELEC § 6402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 6. Arrangement and Examination of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6402

---

## Text

Nomination papers, properly assembled, may be consolidated and fastened together by counties, but nomination papers signed by voters in different counties shall not be thus fastened together.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
