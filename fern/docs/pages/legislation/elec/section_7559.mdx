# ELEC § 7559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Convention > Article: 1. Delegates

**Citation:** ELEC § 7559

---

## Text

A county central committee may authorize its chairperson to appoint delegates to fill vacancies in the membership of party state conventions that the county central committee has power to fill.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
