# ELEC § 9081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 7. State Voter Information Guide

**Citation:** ELEC § 9081

---

## Text

There shall be a state voter information guide, that the Secretary of State shall prepare.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 422, Sec. 23. (AB 2911) Effective January 1, 2017.)
