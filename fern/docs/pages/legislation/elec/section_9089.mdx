# ELEC § 9089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 7. State Voter Information Guide

**Citation:** ELEC § 9089

---

## Text

Measures shall be printed in the state voter information guide, so far as possible, in the same order, manner and form in which they are designated upon the ballot.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 422, Sec. 34. (AB 2911) Effective January 1, 2017.)
