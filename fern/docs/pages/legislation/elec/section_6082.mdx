# ELEC § 6082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Steering Committees

**Citation:** ELEC § 6082

---

## Text

Each steering committee shall be responsible for the circulation of nomination papers of candidates who seek to qualify under Article 4 (commencing with Section 6060) and groups proposing uncommitted delegations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 190, Sec. 12. (AB 1689) Effective January 1, 2011.)
