# ELEC § 15341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Official Canvass > Article: 3. Processing Write-In Votes

**Citation:** ELEC § 15341

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no name written upon a ballot in any election shall be counted for an office or nomination unless the candidate whose name has been written on the ballot has complied with Part 3 (commencing with Section 8600) of Division 8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 1999.)
