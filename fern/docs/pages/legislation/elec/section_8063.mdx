# ELEC § 8063

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 4. Circulation and Signatures—Nomination Documents

**Citation:** ELEC § 8063

---

## Text

The nomination paper shall be delivered to the elections official of the county in which the signer resides and is a voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
