# ELEC § 9257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9257

---

## Text

The petition signed by registered voters of the city or city and county proposing an amendment to a charter shall set forth in full the text of the proposed amendment, in no less than 10-point type.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
