# ELEC § 338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 338

---

## Text

“Party” means a political party or organization that has qualified for participation in any primary or presidential general election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 511, Sec. 2. (AB 1419) Effective January 1, 2014.)
