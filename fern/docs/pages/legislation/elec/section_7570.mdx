# ELEC § 7570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Convention > Article: 2. Meetings

**Citation:** ELEC § 7570

---

## Text

The state convention shall meet biennially, at a location designated by the state central committee, at 10 a.m. on a Saturday following the direct primary election, but in no event later than August 15.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 199, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1999.)
