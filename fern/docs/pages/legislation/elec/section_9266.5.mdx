# ELEC § 9266.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9266.5

---

## Text

The proponent of a measure proposing to amend a charter may withdraw the charter amendment at any time before the 88th day before the election, whether or not the petition has already been found sufficient by the elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 569, Sec. 3. (SB 681) Effective October 8, 2019.)
