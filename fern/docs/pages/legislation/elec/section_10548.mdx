# ELEC § 10548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 4. UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10548

---

## Text

The canvass shall be made in public and by opening the returns and determining the vote for each person voted for and declaring the results thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
