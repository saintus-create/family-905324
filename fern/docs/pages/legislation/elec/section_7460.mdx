# ELEC § 7460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 5. District Committees

**Citation:** ELEC § 7460

---

## Text

This article applies only to committees established by Assembly districts or supervisorial districts within a county pursuant to the bylaws of the county central committee of that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
