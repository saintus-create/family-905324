# ELEC § 8123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 7. Certified List of Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 8123

---

## Text

The Secretary of State, at the time he or she transmits the list of candidates to the county elections official, shall inform the county elections official of the address of each candidate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
