# ELEC § 8652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 2. Presidential Electors

**Citation:** ELEC § 8652

---

## Text

The declaration of write-in candidacy shall be filed with the Secretary of State no later than the 14th day prior to the general election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
