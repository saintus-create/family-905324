# ELEC § 14314

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 5. Provisional Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14314

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall promulgate regulations establishing guidelines for county elections officials relating to the processing of provisional ballots.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 821, Sec. 2. (AB 1970) Effective January 1, 2017.)
