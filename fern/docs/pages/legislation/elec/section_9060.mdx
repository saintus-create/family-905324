# ELEC § 9060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 6. Arguments Concerning Measures Submitted to Voters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9060

---

## Text

In case either the argument for or the argument against any measure placed on the ballot is not prepared and filed, the Secretary of State shall, by a general press release, request voters to submit arguments.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
