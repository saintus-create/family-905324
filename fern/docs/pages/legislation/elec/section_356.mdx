# ELEC § 356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 356

---

## Text

“Special election” is an election, the specific time for the holding of which is not prescribed by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
