# ELEC § 16200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 3. Contests at General Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 16200

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to elections for the office of state Senator or Member of the Assembly of the California Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
