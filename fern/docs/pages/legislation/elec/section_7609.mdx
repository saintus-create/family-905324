# ELEC § 7609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7609

---

## Text

A person is not eligible for appointment to this committee if he or she is not registered as affiliated with this party at the time of his or her appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
