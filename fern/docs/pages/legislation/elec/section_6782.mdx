# ELEC § 6782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6782

---

## Text

Each signer of a nomination paper for the presidential primary ballot may sign only one paper. The signer shall add her or his printed name and place of residence indicating city and giving the street and number, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
