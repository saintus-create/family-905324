# ELEC § 15272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 5. Manual Vote Count in the Precinct

**Citation:** ELEC § 15272

---

## Text

The count shall be public and shall be continued without adjournment until completed and the result is declared. During the reading and tallying, the ballot read and the tally sheet kept shall be within the clear view of watchers.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
