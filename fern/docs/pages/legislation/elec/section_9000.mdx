# ELEC § 9000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 1. Initiative and Referendum Petitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 9000

---

## Text

This article applies only to initiative and referendum measures affecting the Constitution or laws of the state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
