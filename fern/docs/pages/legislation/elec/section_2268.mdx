# ELEC § 2268

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 4.5. California New Motor Voter Program

**Citation:** ELEC § 2268

---

## Text

A person registered or preregistered to vote under this chapter may cancel the person’s voter registration or preregistration at any time by any method available to any other registered or preregistered voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2266 by Stats. 2021, Ch. 314, Sec. 7. (AB 796) Effective January 1, 2022.)
