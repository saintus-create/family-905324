# ELEC § 3117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VOTE BY MAIL VOTING, NEW RESIDENT, AND NEW CITIZEN VOTING > Chapter: 2. Military and Overseas Voters Application and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 3117

---

## Text

A valid ballot cast shall be counted if it is received by the elections official in accordance with Section 3020.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 618, Sec. 2. (SB 29) Effective January 1, 2015.)
