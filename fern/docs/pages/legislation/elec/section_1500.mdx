# ELEC § 1500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ESTABLISHED ELECTION DATES > Chapter: 6. Mail Ballot Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 1500

---

## Text

The established mailed ballot election dates are as follows:

(a) The first Tuesday after the first Monday in May of each year.

(b) The last Tuesday in August of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 58, Sec. 1. (AB 3259) Effective January 1, 2019.)
