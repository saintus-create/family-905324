# ELEC § 14055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1.7. Voter Participation

**Citation:** ELEC § 14055

---

## Text

A voter who resides in a political subdivision where a violation of subdivision (a) of Section 14052 is alleged may file an action pursuant to that section in the superior court of the county in which the political subdivision is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 235, Sec. 1. (SB 415) Effective January 1, 2016. Section operative January 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 14057.)
