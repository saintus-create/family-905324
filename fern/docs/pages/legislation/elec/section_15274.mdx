# ELEC § 15274

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 5. Manual Vote Count in the Precinct

**Citation:** ELEC § 15274

---

## Text

The members of the precinct board may relieve each other in the duties of counting ballots.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
