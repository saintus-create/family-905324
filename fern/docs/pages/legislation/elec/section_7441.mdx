# ELEC § 7441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 4. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7441

---

## Text

At the first meeting, a committee shall organize by selecting a chairperson, a secretary, and any other officers and committees as it deems necessary for carrying on the affairs of this party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. (AB 334) Effective January 1, 2020.)
