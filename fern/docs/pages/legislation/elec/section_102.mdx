# ELEC § 102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Petitions and Petition Signers

**Citation:** ELEC § 102

---

## Text

A person shall not circulate a state or local initiative, referendum, or recall petition or nominating paper unless the person is 18 years of age or older.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 278, Sec. 1. (SB 213) Effective January 1, 2014.)
