# ELEC § 6561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 4. Qualification of Groups of Candidates for Delegate Selection Portion of Primary Ballot

**Citation:** ELEC § 6561

---

## Text

The committee, on or before the 104th day before the presidential primary election, shall file with the Secretary of State a statement containing the name of the committee and the names of its officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
