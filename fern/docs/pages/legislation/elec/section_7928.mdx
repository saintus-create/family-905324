# ELEC § 7928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. State Coordinating Committee

**Citation:** ELEC § 7928

---

## Text

The state coordinating committee shall communicate in writing to the Secretary of State the identity of the Green Party Liaison to the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 4. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
