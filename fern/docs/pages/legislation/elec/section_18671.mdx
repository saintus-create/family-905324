# ELEC § 18671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 7. Initiative, Referendum, and Recall > Article: 8. Filing Petitions to Defeat an Initiative or Referendum

**Citation:** ELEC § 18671

---

## Text

Any petition, or any section of a petition, filed by any person other than the proponents of an initiative or referendum measure and with an intention of defeating an expression of the public will is null and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
