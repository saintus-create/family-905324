# ELEC § 7820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 2. Meetings

**Citation:** ELEC § 7820

---

## Text

The state central committee shall hold meetings not less than once during each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
