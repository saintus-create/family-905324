# ELEC § 3003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VOTE BY MAIL VOTING, NEW RESIDENT, AND NEW CITIZEN VOTING > Chapter: 1. Vote by Mail Application and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 3003

---

## Text

The vote by mail ballot shall be available to any registered voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 508, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2008.)
