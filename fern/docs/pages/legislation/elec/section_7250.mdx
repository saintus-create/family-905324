# ELEC § 7250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 7250

---

## Text

This part shall apply to the organization, operation, and function of that political party known as the California Republican Party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 60, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
