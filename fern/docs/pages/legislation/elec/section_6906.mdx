# ELEC § 6906

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 6906

---

## Text

The electors, when convened, shall vote by ballot for a person for President and a person for Vice President of the United States, one of whom, at least, is not an inhabitant of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 216, Sec. 4. (SB 103) Effective January 1, 2023.)
