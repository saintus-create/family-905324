# ELEC § 2260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 4.5. California New Motor Voter Program

**Citation:** ELEC § 2260

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California New Motor Voter Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 729, Sec. 3. (AB 1461) Effective January 1, 2016.)
