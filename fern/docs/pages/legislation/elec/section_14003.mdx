# ELEC § 14003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1. Privileges of Voters

**Citation:** ELEC § 14003

---

## Text

Except in time of war or public danger, no voter is obliged to perform militia duty on any election day.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
