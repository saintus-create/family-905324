# ELEC § 19004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 19004

---

## Text

Voting equipment may be loaned or rented for any purposes with the consent of the board of supervisors, if payment for the expenses incident to the use of the machine is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
