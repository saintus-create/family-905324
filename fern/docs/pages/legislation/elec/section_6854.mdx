# ELEC § 6854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 5. Green Party Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Preparation, Circulation, and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6854

---

## Text

This article applies to the nomination of a Green Party candidate for the presidential preference primary ballot.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 100. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
