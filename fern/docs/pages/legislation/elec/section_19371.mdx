# ELEC § 19371

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 4. Elections Using Voting Machines > Article: 5. Closing the Polls

**Citation:** ELEC § 19371

---

## Text

Before adjourning, the precinct board shall do all of the following:

(a) Comply with the requirements of Section 15250.5.

(b) Seal each voting machine with the seal or seals provided.

(c) Lock the voting machines.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 911, Sec. 14. (AB 2631) Effective January 1, 2015.)
