# ELEC § 7152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee

**Citation:** ELEC § 7152

---

## Text

The Democratic Party of California shall post a current copy of its standing rules and bylaws for public inspection on its Internet Web site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 392, Sec. 2. (AB 1396) Effective January 1, 2010.)
