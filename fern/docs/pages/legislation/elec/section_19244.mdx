# ELEC § 19244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 3. Certification of Voting Systems > Article: 4. Accessible Voting Systems

**Citation:** ELEC § 19244

---

## Text

Compliance with this article in regard to voting technology and systems purchased prior to the effective date of this article shall be achieved at the time of procurement of an upgrade or replacement of existing voting equipment or systems.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19228 by Stats. 2013, Ch. 602, Sec. 44. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2014.)
