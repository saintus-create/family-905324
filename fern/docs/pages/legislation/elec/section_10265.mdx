# ELEC § 10265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 4. Canvass of Ballots and Returns

**Citation:** ELEC § 10265

---

## Text

The elections official shall immediately sign and deliver to each person elected a certificate of election. He or she shall also administer to each person elected the oath of office prescribed in the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
