# ELEC § 15153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 15153

---

## Text

During the semifinal official canvass, write-in votes shall be counted in accordance with Article 3 (commencing with Section 15340) of Chapter 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
