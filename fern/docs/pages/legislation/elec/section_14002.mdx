# ELEC § 14002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1. Privileges of Voters

**Citation:** ELEC § 14002

---

## Text

Sections 14000, 14001, and 14004 shall apply to all public agencies and the employees thereof, as well as to employers and employees in private industry.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 223, Sec. 2. (AB 17) Effective January 1, 2020.)
