# ELEC § 6123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 7. Circulators

**Citation:** ELEC § 6123

---

## Text

A county elections official or his or her deputy may not circulate a nomination paper and circulators may not obtain signatures within 100 feet of any election booth or polling place.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 790, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2000.)
