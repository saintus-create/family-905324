# ELEC § 8166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1.5. June 2, 2026, Statewide Direct Primary Election

**Citation:** ELEC § 8166

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 97, Sec. 1. (SB 280) Effective August 21, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 1.5, commencing with Section 8160.)
