# ELEC § 327.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 327.5

---

## Text

“Jurisdiction” means any county, city and county, city, or special district that conducts elections pursuant to this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 605, Sec. 1. (SB 1328) Effective September 25, 2024.)
