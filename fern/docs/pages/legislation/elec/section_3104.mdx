# ELEC § 3104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VOTE BY MAIL VOTING, NEW RESIDENT, AND NEW CITIZEN VOTING > Chapter: 2. Military and Overseas Voters Application and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 3104

---

## Text

Applications for the ballots of military or overseas voters shall be received and, except as provided in Section 3106, the ballots shall be received and canvassed, at the same time and under the same procedure as vote by mail ballots, insofar as that procedure is not inconsistent with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3102 by Stats. 2012, Ch. 744, Sec. 7. (AB 1805) Effective January 1, 2013.)
