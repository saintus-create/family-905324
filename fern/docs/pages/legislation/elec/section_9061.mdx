# ELEC § 9061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 6. Arguments Concerning Measures Submitted to Voters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9061

---

## Text

The press release shall be mailed at least 120 days prior to the date of the election at which a measure is to be voted upon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 345, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
