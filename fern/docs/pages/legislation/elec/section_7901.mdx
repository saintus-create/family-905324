# ELEC § 7901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Members of County Councils

**Citation:** ELEC § 7901

---

## Text

At each presidential preference primary election, members of central committees, which shall be termed “county councils,” shall be elected in each county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 108. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
