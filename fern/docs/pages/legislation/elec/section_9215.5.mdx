# ELEC § 9215.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9215.5

---

## Text

The proponent of an initiative may withdraw the initiative at any time before the 88th day before the election, whether or not the petition has already been found sufficient by the elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 155, Sec. 2. (SB 1153) Effective January 1, 2019.)
