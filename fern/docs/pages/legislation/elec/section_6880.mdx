# ELEC § 6880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 7. Income Tax Return Disclosure Requirements

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Presidential Tax Transparency and Accountability Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 121, Sec. 1. (SB 27) Effective July 30, 2019.)
