# ELEC § 6847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 7. Certification of National Convention Delegates Elected and Postelection Proceedings

**Citation:** ELEC § 6847

---

## Text

The elected delegates may, if they desire, meet or confer prior to attending the convention to select a chairperson of the delegation and to fill any vacancies in the office of delegate or alternate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
