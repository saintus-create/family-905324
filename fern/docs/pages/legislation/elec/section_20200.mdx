# ELEC § 20200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. ELECTION CAMPAIGNS > Chapter: 3. Campaign Funds > Article: 1. Solicitation

**Citation:** ELEC § 20200

---

## Text

As used in this article “person” includes a firm, association, corporation, campaign committee or organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
