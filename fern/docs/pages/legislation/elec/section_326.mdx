# ELEC § 326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 326

---

## Text

“Judicial office” means the office filled by any judicial officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
