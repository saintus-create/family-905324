# ELEC § 6784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation and Filing of Nominating Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6784

---

## Text

Each section shall be prepared with the lines, for signatures numbered, and shall have attached the declaration of the circulator who obtained signatures to it, which shall meet all of the requirements of Section 104. No other declaration is required to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 278, Sec. 10. (SB 213) Effective January 1, 2014.)
