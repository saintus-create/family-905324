# ELEC § 8065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 4. Circulation and Signatures—Nomination Documents

**Citation:** ELEC § 8065

---

## Text

The elections official shall not accept for filing any nomination paper unless all blanks in the certificate are filled.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 790, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 2000.)
