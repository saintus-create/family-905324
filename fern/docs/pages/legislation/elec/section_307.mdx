# ELEC § 307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 307

---

## Text

“Clerk” means the county elections official, registrar of voters, city clerk, or other officer or board charged with the duty of conducting any election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2003.)
