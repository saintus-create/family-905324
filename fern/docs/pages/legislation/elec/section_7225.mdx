# ELEC § 7225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 2. Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 7225

---

## Text

At every presidential primary election, a county central committee may be elected in each county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 507, Sec. 2. (SB 1272) Effective January 1, 2013.)
