# ELEC § 9124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 2. County Elections > Article: 1. Initiative

**Citation:** ELEC § 9124

---

## Text

The enacting clause of an ordinance submitted to the voters of a county shall be substantially in the following form:

“The people of the County of ____ ordain as follows:”

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
