# ELEC § 2022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 1. Voter Qualifications > Article: 2. Determination of Residence and Domicile

**Citation:** ELEC § 2022

---

## Text

If a person moves to another state with the intention of making it his or her domicile, the voter loses his or her domicile in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
