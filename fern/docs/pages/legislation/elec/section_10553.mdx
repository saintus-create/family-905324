# ELEC § 10553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 4. UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10553

---

## Text

The county elections official shall immediately make and deliver to each person elected a certificate of election signed by the county elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
