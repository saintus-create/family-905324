# ELEC § 14295

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 4. Issuing Ballots and Voting

**Citation:** ELEC § 14295

---

## Text

No voter shall deliver to any member of the precinct board any ballot other than the one received from the board member.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
