# ELEC § 329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 329

---

## Text

“Measure” means any constitutional amendment or other proposition submitted to a popular vote at any election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
