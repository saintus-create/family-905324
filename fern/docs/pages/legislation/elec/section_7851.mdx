# ELEC § 7851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7851

---

## Text

A county central committee may require a balance of elected and appointed committee members to create a total membership division of 50 percent women and 50 percent men.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
