# ELEC § 6382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Circulators

**Citation:** ELEC § 6382

---

## Text

Circulators may obtain signatures to the nomination paper of a candidate at any time not more than 120 nor less than 81 days prior to the presidential primary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 149, Sec. 10. (SB 505) Effective July 30, 2019.)
