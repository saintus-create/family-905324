# ELEC § 22001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 8. Special Districts

**Citation:** ELEC § 22001

---

## Text

This chapter and Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 21100) do not apply to special districts in which only landowners vote for directors or whose directors are all elected at large or appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 343, Sec. 52. (AB 764) Effective January 1, 2024.)
