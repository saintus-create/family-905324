# ELEC § 11102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Recall of State Officers: Intermediate Steps in the Recall

**Citation:** ELEC § 11102

---

## Text

Each section of a recall petition shall be filed with the elections official of the county for which it was circulated.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 156, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
