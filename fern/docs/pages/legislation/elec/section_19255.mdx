# ELEC § 19255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 3. Certification of Voting Systems > Article: 5. Voting Modernization Bond Act of 2002 (Shelley-Hertzberg Act)

**Citation:** ELEC § 19255

---

## Text

The Legislature may amend subdivisions (c) and (d) of Section 19254 and Section 19256 by a statute, passed in each house of the Legislature by rollcall vote entered in the respective journals, by not less than two-thirds of the membership in each house concurring, if the statute is consistent with, and furthers the purposes of, this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19234.5 by Stats. 2013, Ch. 602, Sec. 53. (SB 360) Effective January 1, 2014. Note: This section (as originally numbered) was added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 902, and approved in Prop. 41 on March 5, 2002.)
