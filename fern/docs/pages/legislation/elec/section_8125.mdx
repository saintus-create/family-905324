# ELEC § 8125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 7. Certified List of Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 8125

---

## Text

The certified list of candidates sent to each county elections official by the Secretary of State shall be in a form prescribed by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 1, Sec. 26. (SB 6) Effective January 1, 2010. Operative January 1, 2011, pursuant to Sec. 67 of Ch. 1.)
