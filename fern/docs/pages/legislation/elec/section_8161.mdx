# ELEC § 8161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1.5. June 2, 2026, Statewide Direct Primary Election

**Citation:** ELEC § 8161

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “statewide direct primary election” means the June 2, 2026, statewide direct primary election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 97, Sec. 1. (SB 280) Effective August 21, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 8166.)
