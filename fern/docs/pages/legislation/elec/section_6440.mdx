# ELEC § 6440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 8. Write-in Candidates

**Citation:** ELEC § 6440

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a space shall be provided on the presidential primary ballot for an elector to write in the name of a candidate for President of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
