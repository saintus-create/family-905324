# ELEC § 6849

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. Peace and Freedom Party Presidential Primary > Article: 7. Certification of National Convention Delegates Elected and Postelection Proceedings

**Citation:** ELEC § 6849

---

## Text

The Secretary of State shall, as soon as practicable, certify to the chairperson of the state central committee and to the chairperson of each party county central committee of record, the final list of national convention delegates.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
