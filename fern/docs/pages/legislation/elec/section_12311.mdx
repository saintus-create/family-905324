# ELEC § 12311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Precinct Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 12311

---

## Text

No public agency shall be required as the result of any assignment or transfer to pay the stipend of an election officer for services to any person other than the election officer to whom the stipend is due.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 725, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1997.)
