# ELEC § 6222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 12. Canvass of Returns and Certificate of Selection

**Citation:** ELEC § 6222

---

## Text

No fee shall be required of any person as a condition of receiving a certificate of selection as a delegate or an alternate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
