# ELEC § 6564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 4. Qualification of Groups of Candidates for Delegate Selection Portion of Primary Ballot

**Citation:** ELEC § 6564

---

## Text

The number of alternates to be selected shall be no greater than one for each delegate, and the alternates shall be selected in the manner prescribed by Section 6563.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
