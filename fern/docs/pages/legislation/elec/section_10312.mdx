# ELEC § 10312

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10312

---

## Text

Whenever the government of the city is in full operation, the governing body shall enter a resolution in its minutes declaring that fact. The resolution shall be conclusive evidence of that fact, except as against a direct action or proceeding to set aside or annul the government.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
