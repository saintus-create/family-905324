# ELEC § 15278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 5. Manual Vote Count in the Precinct

**Citation:** ELEC § 15278

---

## Text

On completion of the canvass of the returns for each election, the elections official shall compare the vote by mail voters’ list with the roster for each precinct to determine if any voter cast more than one ballot at that election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 76. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
