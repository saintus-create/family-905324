# ELEC § 2165

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 2. Registration > Article: 4. Forms

**Citation:** ELEC § 2165

---

## Text

Affidavits of registration shall be processed immediately.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 34. (AB 1020) Effective January 1, 2016. Operative September 26, 2016, when the Secretary of State issued the certification prescribed by Stats. 2015, Ch. 728, Sec. 88.)
