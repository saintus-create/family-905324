# ELEC § 7833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 3. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7833

---

## Text

The vote on any issue at the convention meeting of the state central committee may be by alphabetical rollcall by county if a majority of its members vote to do so, or any other method prescribed by this committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
