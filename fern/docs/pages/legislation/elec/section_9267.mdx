# ELEC § 9267

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 3. City or City and County Charters

**Citation:** ELEC § 9267

---

## Text

Petitions that do not substantially conform to the form requirements of this article shall not be accepted for filing by the elections official.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
