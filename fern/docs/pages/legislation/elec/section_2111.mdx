# ELEC § 2111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 2. Registration > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 2111

---

## Text

A person may prove he or she is a citizen by his or her certification under penalty of perjury on the affidavit of registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
