# ELEC § 7924

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. State Coordinating Committee

**Citation:** ELEC § 7924

---

## Text

The members of the state coordinating committee shall be elected as provided in the Green Party bylaws and shall have the responsibilities described in those bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 4. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
