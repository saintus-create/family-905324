# ELEC § 19286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 3.5. Certification of Remote Accessible Vote By Mail Systems

**Citation:** ELEC § 19286

---

## Text

The person, corporation, or public agency applying for certification of a remote accessible vote by mail system is responsible for all costs associated with the testing and examination of the remote accessible vote by mail system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 75, Sec. 13. (AB 2252) Effective January 1, 2017.)
