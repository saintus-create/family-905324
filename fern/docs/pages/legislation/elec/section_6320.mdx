# ELEC § 6320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Republican Presidential Primary > Article: 2. Number and Certification of Delegates

**Citation:** ELEC § 6320

---

## Text

The chairperson of the state central committee shall notify the Secretary of State on or before the 120th day preceding the presidential primary as to the number of delegates to represent the state in the next national convention of his or her party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 913, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1999.)
