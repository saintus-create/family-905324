# ELEC § 1002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. ESTABLISHED ELECTION DATES > Chapter: 1. Election Dates

**Citation:** ELEC § 1002

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 1003, notwithstanding any other provisions of law, all state, county, municipal, district, and school district elections shall be held on an established election date.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1143, Sec. 19. Effective September 30, 1996.)
