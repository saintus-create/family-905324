# ELEC § 16702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 8. Court’s Decisions, Judgment, and Determinations > Article: 1. General Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 16702

---

## Text

Whenever an election is annulled or set aside by the judgment of the superior court, and no appeal has been taken within 10 days thereafter, the commission, if any has issued, is void and the office vacant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
