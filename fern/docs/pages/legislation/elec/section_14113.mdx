# ELEC § 14113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Precinct Supplies

**Citation:** ELEC § 14113

---

## Text

The containers of the ballots furnished to the polling places shall provide adequate protection for the ballots during storage and upon removing the ballots.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 806, Sec. 50. (SB 286) Effective January 1, 2018.)
