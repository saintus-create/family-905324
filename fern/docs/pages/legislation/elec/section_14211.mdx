# ELEC § 14211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 2. Election Day Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 14211

---

## Text

The polling places shall be arranged so that neither the ballot containers nor the voting booths or compartments shall be hidden from the view of those present.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
