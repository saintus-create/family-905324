# ELEC § 15202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 2. Automated Count in a Central Location

**Citation:** ELEC § 15202

---

## Text

If the ballots are to be counted at a central counting place, no fewer than two precinct board members shall, following the close of the polls, deliver the ballots, in a sealed container, to the central counting place or a designated receiving station. There may be two or more central counting places.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
