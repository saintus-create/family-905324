# ELEC § 8060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 4. Circulation and Signatures—Nomination Documents

**Citation:** ELEC § 8060

---

## Text

A candidate who declares his or her candidacy shall have registered voters sign his or her nomination papers pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
