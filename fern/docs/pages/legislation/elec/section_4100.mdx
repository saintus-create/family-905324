# ELEC § 4100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. MAIL BALLOT ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Conduct of Mail Ballot Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 4100

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, mail ballot elections shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 3000) of Division 3.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
