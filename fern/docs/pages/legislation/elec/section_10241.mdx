# ELEC § 10241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Regulations Governing Elections in Cities > Article: 3. Conduct of Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 10241

---

## Text

Before opening the polls, the election officers shall sign a declaration to discharge faithfully the duties of an election officer, before the inspector or before any other of themselves.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
