# ELEC § 15670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 10. Tie Votes > Article: 2. At Primary Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 15670

---

## Text

This article applies only to:

(a) Candidates for delegates to a national convention for the nomination of party candidates for President and Vice President of the United States.

(b) Candidates for nomination at the direct primary to offices other than nonpartisan offices or voter-nominated offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 1, Sec. 59. (SB 6) Effective January 1, 2010. Operative January 1, 2011, pursuant to Sec. 67 of Ch. 1.)
