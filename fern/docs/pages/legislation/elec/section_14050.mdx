# ELEC § 14050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1.7. Voter Participation

**Citation:** ELEC § 14050

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Voter Participation Rights Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 235, Sec. 1. (SB 415) Effective January 1, 2016. Section operative January 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 14057.)
