# ELEC § 6592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation, and Filing of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6592

---

## Text

An elections official or deputy elections official may not serve as a circulator and circulators may not obtain signatures within 100 feet of any election booth or polling place.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 790, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2000.)
