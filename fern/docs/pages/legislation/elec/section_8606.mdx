# ELEC § 8606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** ELEC § 8606

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a person may not be a write-in candidate at the general election for a voter-nominated office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 3, Sec. 26. (AB 1413) Effective February 10, 2012.)
