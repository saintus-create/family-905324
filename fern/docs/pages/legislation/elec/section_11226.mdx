# ELEC § 11226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. Recall of Local Officers: Intermediate Steps in the Recall > Article: 2. Recall Petitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 11226

---

## Text

If the certificate shows that the petition is insufficient, no action shall be taken on it, but the petition shall remain on file.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
