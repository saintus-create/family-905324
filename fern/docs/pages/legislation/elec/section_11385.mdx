# ELEC § 11385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 4. Recall Elections

**Citation:** ELEC § 11385

---

## Text

If a state officer is recalled at a recall election, the candidate receiving the highest number of votes for the office shall be declared elected for the unexpired term of the recalled officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 251, Sec. 2. (AB 94) Effective January 1, 2026.)
