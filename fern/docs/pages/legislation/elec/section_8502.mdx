# ELEC § 8502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 5. Arrangement and Examination of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 8502

---

## Text

Nomination papers shall be left with the county elections official for examination and filed by him or her pursuant to the time limitations set forth in Section 8403.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
