# ELEC § 6542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Determination of Number of Delegates to National Convention

**Citation:** ELEC § 6542

---

## Text

If the chairperson of the state central committee fails to file a notice as to the number of delegates, the Secretary of State shall ascertain the number from the call for the national convention issued by the national committee of the party.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
