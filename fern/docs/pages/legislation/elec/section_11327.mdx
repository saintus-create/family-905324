# ELEC § 11327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. General Procedures: Final Steps in the Recall > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** ELEC § 11327

---

## Text

An officer whose recall is being sought may file a statement with the elections official in accordance with Section 13307, to be sent to each voter, together with the voter information guide.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 422, Sec. 59. (AB 2911) Effective January 1, 2017.)
