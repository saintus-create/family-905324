# ELEC § 333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 333

---

## Text

“Nomination documents” means declaration of candidacy and nomination papers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6489 by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1143, Sec. 43. Effective September 30, 1996.)
