# ELEC § 21546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 6.4. County of Riverside Citizens Redistricting Commission

**Citation:** ELEC § 21546

---

## Text

Implementation of this chapter is contingent upon an appropriation in the annual Budget Act or other statute for purposes of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 403, Sec. 1. (AB 1307) Effective January 1, 2023.)
