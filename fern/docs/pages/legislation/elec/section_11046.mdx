# ELEC § 11046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Procedures: General Provisions and Initial Steps in the Recall > Article: 3. Recall Petition

**Citation:** ELEC § 11046

---

## Text

To each section of a petition shall be attached a declaration, signed by the circulator thereof, that complies with Section 104.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 278, Sec. 28. (SB 213) Effective January 1, 2014.)
