# ELEC § 15002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1. Preparation for Canvass

**Citation:** ELEC § 15002

---

## Text

No later than January 1 of each even-numbered year, the Secretary of State shall review, and if necessary amend, administrative procedures for use with each of the voting systems pursuant to Division 19.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1999.)
