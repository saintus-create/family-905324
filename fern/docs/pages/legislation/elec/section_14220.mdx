# ELEC § 14220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Procedures at Polls > Article: 2. Election Day Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 14220

---

## Text

At any election, a majority of the members of any precinct board shall be present at the polling place at all times while the polling place is open.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
