# ELEC § 12320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Precinct Boards > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 12320

---

## Text

No person is eligible to act as an election officer until the declaration required by Section 12321 has been signed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
