# ELEC § 15207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 2. Automated Count in a Central Location

**Citation:** ELEC § 15207

---

## Text

The elections official or authorized deputy shall segregate the persons employed to count the ballots into counting boards. These counting boards shall be deemed to be precinct boards, and are subject to all laws governing precinct boards where ballots are counted at the polling place.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
