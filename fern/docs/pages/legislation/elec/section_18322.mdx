# ELEC § 18322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. PENAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Election Campaigns > Article: 3. Deceptive Online Activities

**Citation:** ELEC § 18322

---

## Text

In addition to any other remedies available under law, a court may order the transfer of a domain name as part of the relief awarded for a violation of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 277, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004.)
