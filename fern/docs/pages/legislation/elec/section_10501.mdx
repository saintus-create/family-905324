# ELEC § 10501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 4. UNIFORM DISTRICT ELECTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10501

---

## Text

It is the purpose of this part to provide a procedure for the election of elective officers of districts. These elections shall be called and conducted and the results canvassed, returned, and declared pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
