# ELEC § 352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 352

---

## Text

“School officer” means the Superintendent of Public Instruction and the superintendent of schools of a county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
