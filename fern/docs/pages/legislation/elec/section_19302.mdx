# ELEC § 19302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 4. Elections Using Voting Machines > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 19302

---

## Text

The labels on voting machines and the way in which candidates’ names are grouped shall conform as nearly as possible to the form of ballot provided for in elections where voting machines are not used.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
