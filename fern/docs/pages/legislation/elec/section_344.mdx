# ELEC § 344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 344

---

## Text

“Punchcard” means a tabulating card on which the voter may record his or her vote by punching, marking, or slotting.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
