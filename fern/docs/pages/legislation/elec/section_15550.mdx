# ELEC § 15550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 8. Disposition of Ballots and Supplies by the Elections Official

**Citation:** ELEC § 15550

---

## Text

The records and supplies of any election when received by the elections official shall be disposed of in the manner set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
