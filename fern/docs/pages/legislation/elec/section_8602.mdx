# ELEC § 8602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. General

**Citation:** ELEC § 8602

---

## Text

The nomination papers for a write-in candidate shall be substantially in the same form as set forth in Section 8041.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 790, Sec. 42. Effective January 1, 2000.)
