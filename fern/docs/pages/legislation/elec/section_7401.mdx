# ELEC § 7401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7401

---

## Text

In each county containing more than four and less than 20 Assembly districts, a county central committee shall be elected from Assembly districts and shall consist of six members elected from each Assembly district.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
