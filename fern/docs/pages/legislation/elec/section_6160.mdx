# ELEC § 6160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 9. Notification of Qualification from Secretary of State

**Citation:** ELEC § 6160

---

## Text

At least 68 days before a presidential primary, the Secretary of State shall notify each steering committee whether or not it has qualified a candidate or uncommitted delegation for placement on the ballot pursuant to Section 6060.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 190, Sec. 21. (AB 1689) Effective January 1, 2011.)
