# ELEC § 6920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1.5. Voting Compact

**Citation:** ELEC § 6920

---

## Text

The Legislature of the State of California hereby ratifies the Agreement Among the States to Elect the President by National Popular Vote as set forth in Section 6921.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 188, Sec. 1. (AB 459) Effective January 1, 2012.)
