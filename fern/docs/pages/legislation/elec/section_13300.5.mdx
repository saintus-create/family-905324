# ELEC § 13300.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 4. State and County Voter Information Guides

**Citation:** ELEC § 13300.5

---

## Text

In order to facilitate the timely production and distribution of county voter information guides, the county elections official may prepare a combined county voter information guide.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 422, Sec. 65. (AB 2911) Effective January 1, 2017.)
