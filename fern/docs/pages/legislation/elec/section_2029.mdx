# ELEC § 2029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 1. Voter Qualifications > Article: 2. Determination of Residence and Domicile

**Citation:** ELEC § 2029

---

## Text

The domicile of one spouse shall not be presumed to be that of the other, but shall be determined independently in accordance with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
