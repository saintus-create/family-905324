# ELEC § 6040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Selection of Candidates by the Secretary of State

**Citation:** ELEC § 6040

---

## Text

This article shall apply to the designation of candidates by the Secretary of State for placement only on the presidential primary ballot.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
