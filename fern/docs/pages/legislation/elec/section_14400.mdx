# ELEC § 14400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Closing of the Polls > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** ELEC § 14400

---

## Text

At any election, all members of the precinct board shall be present at the closing of the polls.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
