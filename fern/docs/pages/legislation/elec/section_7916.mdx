# ELEC § 7916

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. Election of County Councils

**Citation:** ELEC § 7916

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this code, a person may obtain and circulate nomination papers for both nomination to a public office and for election as a member of a county council.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 726, Sec. 4. (AB 477) Effective January 1, 2016.)
