# ELEC § 21454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. STATE AND LOCAL REAPPORTIONMENT > Chapter: 5. Congressional Districts

**Citation:** ELEC § 21454

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative only if Assembly Constitutional Amendment 8 of the 2025–26 Regular Session is approved by the voters and takes effect, and subdivision (b) of Section 4 of Article XXI of the California Constitution, as added by that constitutional amendment, becomes operative.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 96, Sec. 1. (AB 604) Effective August 21, 2025. Conditionally repealed pursuant to Section 21455.)
