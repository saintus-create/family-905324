# ELEC § 3116

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. VOTE BY MAIL VOTING, NEW RESIDENT, AND NEW CITIZEN VOTING > Chapter: 2. Military and Overseas Voters Application and Voting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 3116

---

## Text

A military or overseas voter may use a federal write-in absentee ballot to vote for all offices and ballot measures described in subdivision (a) of Section 3102.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 744, Sec. 19. (AB 1805) Effective January 1, 2013.)
