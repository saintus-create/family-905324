# ELEC § 7803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7803

---

## Text

The state central committee may require a balance of elected and appointed members so that 50 percent of the state central committee members from each county are women and 50 percent are men.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
