# ELEC § 7645

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. State Central Committee > Article: 4. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7645

---

## Text

The proceedings at all meetings of this committee shall be governed by any manual of parliamentary procedures designated by the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
