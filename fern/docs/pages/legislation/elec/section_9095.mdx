# ELEC § 9095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 1. State Elections > Article: 7. State Voter Information Guide

**Citation:** ELEC § 9095

---

## Text

Any costs incurred by a county for mailing the state voter information guides pursuant to the provisions of subdivisions (b) and (c) of Section 9094 shall be reimbursed to the county by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 422, Sec. 40. (AB 2911) Effective January 1, 2017.)
