# ELEC § 8102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. NOMINATIONS > Chapter: 1. Direct Primary > Article: 6. Nomination Papers.  Place of Filing.  Fees

**Citation:** ELEC § 8102

---

## Text

No defect in any nomination document presented shall prevent the filing of another nomination document within the period allowed for presenting the nomination document.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
