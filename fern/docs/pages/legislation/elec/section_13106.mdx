# ELEC § 13106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. BALLOTS, SAMPLE BALLOTS, AND VOTER PAMPHLETS > Chapter: 2. Forms of Ballots: Ballot Order

**Citation:** ELEC § 13106

---

## Text

No title or degree shall appear on the same line on a ballot as a candidate’s name, either before or after the candidate’s name, in the case of any election to any office.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
