# ELEC § 340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** ELEC § 340

---

## Text

“Presidential primary” is the primary election that is held on the first Tuesday after the first Monday in March of any year that is evenly divisible by four.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 111, Sec. 3. (SB 970) Effective January 1, 2021.)
