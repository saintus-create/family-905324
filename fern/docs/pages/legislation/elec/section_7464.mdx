# ELEC § 7464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 5. District Committees

**Citation:** ELEC § 7464

---

## Text

The district committees may elect any officers and undertake any action as the bylaws of the county central committee of which they are a part provide.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
