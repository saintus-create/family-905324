# ELEC § 7577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. State Convention > Article: 3. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7577

---

## Text

The convention, upon election of permanent officers, shall adopt a state platform for this party. This platform shall be made public upon its adoption.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
