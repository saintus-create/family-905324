# ELEC § 10401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. LOCAL, SPECIAL, VACANCY, AND CONSOLIDATED ELECTIONS > Part: 3. CONSOLIDATION OF ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. Emergency Elections in Cities

**Citation:** ELEC § 10401

---

## Text

Where one of the elections to be consolidated is a statewide election, the board of supervisors of the county in which the consolidation is to be effected may order the consolidation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
