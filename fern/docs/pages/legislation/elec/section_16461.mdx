# ELEC § 16461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 5. Form of Contest Statement > Article: 4. Contests Involving a Recount

**Citation:** ELEC § 16461

---

## Text

The superior court of that county in which is located the precinct in which the contestant demands a recount has jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
