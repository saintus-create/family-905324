# ELEC § 6540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 3. Determination of Number of Delegates to National Convention

**Citation:** ELEC § 6540

---

## Text

The Chairperson of the State Central Committee of the American Independent Party shall notify the Secretary of State on or before the 120th day preceding the presidential primary as to the number of delegates to represent the state in the next national convention of his or her party.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 913, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 1999.)
