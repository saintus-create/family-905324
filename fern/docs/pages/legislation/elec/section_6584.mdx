# ELEC § 6584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 3. American Independent Party Presidential Primary > Article: 5. Preparation, Circulation, and Filing of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6584

---

## Text

Each section shall be prepared with the lines for signatures numbered, and shall have attached the affidavit of the circulator who obtained signatures to it, which shall meet all of the requirements of Section 104. No other affidavit is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 278, Sec. 7. (SB 213) Effective January 1, 2014.)
