# ELEC § 16443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 5. Form of Contest Statement > Article: 3. Contests Other than Recount

**Citation:** ELEC § 16443

---

## Text

The defendant, after receipt of the copy of the affidavit, may file an answer and a cross-contest affidavit within five days.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
