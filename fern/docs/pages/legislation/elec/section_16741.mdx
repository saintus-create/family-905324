# ELEC § 16741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. ELECTIONS CONTESTS > Chapter: 8. Court’s Decisions, Judgment, and Determinations > Article: 3. Primary Elections:  Involving a Recount

**Citation:** ELEC § 16741

---

## Text

A certified copy of the judgment shall be served upon the elections official and may be enforced summarily in the same manner as provided in Section 13314.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1143, Sec. 66. Effective September 30, 1996.)
