# ELEC § 7870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 2. Meetings

**Citation:** ELEC § 7870

---

## Text

The Department of General Services shall permit any committee that desires to do so to hold meetings in a state building within the county, at least one of which meetings each month shall be without charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
