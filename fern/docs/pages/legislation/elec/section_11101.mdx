# ELEC § 11101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. RECALL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Recall of State Officers: Intermediate Steps in the Recall

**Citation:** ELEC § 11101

---

## Text

Unless and until it is otherwise proven upon official investigation, it shall be presumed that the petition presented contains the signatures of the requisite number of registered voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 714, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1997.)
