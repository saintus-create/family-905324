# ELEC § 19381

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. CERTIFICATION OF VOTING SYSTEMS > Chapter: 4. Elections Using Voting Machines > Article: 6. Counting Procedures

**Citation:** ELEC § 19381

---

## Text

The precinct board shall immediately transmit unsealed to the elections official a copy of the result of the votes cast at the polling place, the copy shall be signed by the members of the precinct board, and shall be open to public inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19385 by Stats. 2014, Ch. 911, Sec. 20. (AB 2631) Effective January 1, 2015.)
