# ELEC § 14112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Precinct Supplies

**Citation:** ELEC § 14112

---

## Text

All voting equipment shall be transferred to the polling places in charge of an authorized official, who shall certify to their delivery in good order.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
