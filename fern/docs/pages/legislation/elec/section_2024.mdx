# ELEC § 2024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. VOTERS > Chapter: 1. Voter Qualifications > Article: 2. Determination of Residence and Domicile

**Citation:** ELEC § 2024

---

## Text

The mere intention to acquire a new domicile, without the fact of removal avails nothing, neither does the fact of removal without the intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
