# ELEC § 12288

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Precincts > Article: 5. Polling Places

**Citation:** ELEC § 12288

---

## Text

A polling place shall not be accessible by a door, window, or other opening to any place where any alcoholic beverage is sold or dispensed while the polls are open.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 155, Sec. 1. (AB 2037) Effective January 1, 2023.)
