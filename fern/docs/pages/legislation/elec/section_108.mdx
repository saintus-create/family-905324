# ELEC § 108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 0.5. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Petitions and Petition Signers

**Citation:** ELEC § 108

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the pages of a petition may be bound together by any reasonable method, including the use of staples.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 563, Sec. 4. (SB 47) Effective January 1, 2020.)
