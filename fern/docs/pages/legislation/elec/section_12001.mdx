# ELEC § 12001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 1. Proclamations And Election Orders

**Citation:** ELEC § 12001

---

## Text

For a special local election, the governing body of the local agency shall issue a proclamation or a resolution calling the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
