# ELEC § 14443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. ELECTION DAY PROCEDURES > Chapter: 4. Closing of the Polls > Article: 4. Snap Tallies

**Citation:** ELEC § 14443

---

## Text

If ballots are counted by electronic or electromechanical means, the elections official may provide for early tabulation and announcement of the returns in a manner consistent with the use of the tabulating devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 676, Sec. 48. (AB 1219) Effective January 1, 2024.)
