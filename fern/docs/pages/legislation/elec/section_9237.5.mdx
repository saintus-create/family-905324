# ELEC § 9237.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. MEASURES SUBMITTED TO THE VOTERS > Chapter: 3. Municipal Elections > Article: 2. Referendum

**Citation:** ELEC § 9237.5

---

## Text

The provisions of this code relating to the form of petitions, the duties of the county elections official, and the manner of holding elections shall govern the petition procedure and submission of the ordinance to the voters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 312, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2000.)
