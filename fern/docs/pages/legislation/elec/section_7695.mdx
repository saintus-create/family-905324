# ELEC § 7695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 4. General Business

**Citation:** ELEC § 7695

---

## Text

Each committee may establish annual dues not to exceed twenty-four dollars ($24) per year for elected, ex officio and alternate members. The committee may remove members or alternates for nonpayment of dues.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1994, Ch. 920, Sec. 2.)
