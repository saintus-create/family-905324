# ELEC § 7854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. POLITICAL PARTY ORGANIZATION AND CENTRAL COMMITTEE ELECTIONS > Chapter: 4. County Central Committee > Article: 1. Members

**Citation:** ELEC § 7854

---

## Text

The removal of residence by an elected or appointed member of a county central committee from the county from which the member was elected shall constitute the member’s automatic resignation from the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 538, Sec. 150. Effective January 1, 2007.)
