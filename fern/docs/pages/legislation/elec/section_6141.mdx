# ELEC § 6141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. PRESIDENTIAL ELECTIONS > Chapter: 1. Democratic Presidential Primary > Article: 8. Arrangement and Examination of Nomination Papers

**Citation:** ELEC § 6141

---

## Text

Prior to filing, the sections of a nomination paper shall be numbered in order.
