# ELEC § 12113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. PREELECTION PROCEDURES > Chapter: 2. Preelection Notices

**Citation:** ELEC § 12113

---

## Text

For each school or special district, the county elections official immediately shall deliver a copy of all published notices to the district secretary. Each notice shall be posted in the district office.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 725, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
