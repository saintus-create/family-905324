# ELEC § 15206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. SEMIFINAL OFFICIAL CANVASS, OFFICIAL CANVASS, RECOUNT, AND TIE VOTE PROCEDURES > Chapter: 3. Semifinal Official Canvass > Article: 2. Automated Count in a Central Location

**Citation:** ELEC § 15206

---

## Text

The elections official or any deputy authorized by the elections official may excuse or dismiss any person from any counting board and enforce the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1073, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1999.)
