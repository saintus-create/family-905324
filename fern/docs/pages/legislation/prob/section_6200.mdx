# PROB § 6200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 6. California Statutory Will > Article: 1. Definitions and Rules of Construction

**Citation:** PROB § 6200

## Text

Unless the provision or context clearly requires otherwise, these definitions and rules of construction govern the construction of this chapter.
