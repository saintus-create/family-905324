# PROB § 16045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 2.5. Uniform Prudent Investor Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16045

---

## Text

This article, together with subdivision (a) of Section 16002 and Section 16003, constitutes the prudent investor rule and may be cited as the Uniform Prudent Investor Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 63, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
