# PROB § 5692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 5. Contest

**Citation:** PROB § 5692

---

## Text

(a) A contest proceeding pursuant to Section 5690 shall not be commenced before the transferor’s death.

(b) For the purposes of the applicable limitations period, a contest proceeding accrues on the date of the transferor’s death.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 293, Sec. 17. (AB 139) Effective January 1, 2016. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
