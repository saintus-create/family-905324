# PROB § 10559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 3. Administration Under Independent Administration Authority > Article: 4. Powers Exercisable Without Giving Notice of Proposed Action

**Citation:** PROB § 10559

---

## Text

The personal representative has the power to purchase securities or commodities required to perform an incomplete contract of sale where the decedent died having sold but not delivered securities or commodities not owned by the decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
