# PROB § 2032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 8. Interstate Jurisdiction, Transfer, and Recognition: California Conservatorship Jurisdiction Act > Article: 6. Federally Recognized Indian Tribe

**Citation:** PROB § 2032

---

## Text

Article 2 (commencing with Section 1991) does not apply to a proposed conservatee who is a member of an Indian tribe with jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 553, Sec. 20. (SB 940) Effective January 1, 2015. Operative January 1, 2016, by Stats. 2014, Ch. 553, Sec. 29.)
