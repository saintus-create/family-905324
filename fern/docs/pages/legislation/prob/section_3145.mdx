# PROB § 3145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 6. MANAGEMENT OR DISPOSITION OF COMMUNITY PROPERTY WHERE SPOUSE LACKS LEGAL CAPACITY > Chapter: 3. Proceeding for Particular Transaction > Article: 5. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** PROB § 3145

---

## Text

A court determination pursuant to this chapter that a spouse lacks legal capacity for the proposed transaction affects the legal capacity of the spouse for that transaction alone and has no effect on the legal capacity of the spouse for any other purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
