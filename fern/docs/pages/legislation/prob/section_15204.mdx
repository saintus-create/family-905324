# PROB § 15204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 1. Creation and Validity of Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 15204

---

## Text

A trust created for an indefinite or general purpose is not invalid for that reason if it can be determined with reasonable certainty that a particular use of the trust property comes within that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
