# PROB § 16060.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 3. Trustee’s Duty to Report Information and Account to Beneficiaries

**Citation:** PROB § 16060.7

---

## Text

On the request of a beneficiary, the trustee shall provide the terms of the trust to the beneficiary unless the trustee is not required to provide the terms of the trust to the beneficiary in accordance with Section 16069.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 621, Sec. 2. (SB 202) Effective January 1, 2011.)
