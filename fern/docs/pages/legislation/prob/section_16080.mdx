# PROB § 16080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 4. Duties With Regard to Discretionary Powers

**Citation:** PROB § 16080

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 16081, a discretionary power conferred upon a trustee is not left to the trustee’s arbitrary discretion, but shall be exercised reasonably.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
