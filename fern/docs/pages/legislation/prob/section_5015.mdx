# PROB § 5015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 1. PROVISIONS RELATING TO EFFECT OF DEATH > Chapter: 2. Nonprobate Transfers of Community Property > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 5015

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the application of principles of fraud, undue influence, duress, mistake, or other invalidating cause to a written consent to a provision for a nonprobate transfer of community property on death.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 51, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
