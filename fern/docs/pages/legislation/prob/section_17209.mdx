# PROB § 17209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 3. Proceedings Concerning Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 17209

---

## Text

The administration of trusts is intended to proceed expeditiously and free of judicial intervention, subject to the jurisdiction of the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
