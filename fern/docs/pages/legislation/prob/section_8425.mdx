# PROB § 8425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 2. Executors

**Citation:** PROB § 8425

---

## Text

If the court does not appoint all the persons named in the will as executors, those appointed have the same authority to act in every respect as all would have if appointed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
