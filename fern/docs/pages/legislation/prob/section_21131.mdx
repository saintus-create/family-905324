# PROB § 21131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Exoneration; Ademption

**Citation:** PROB § 21131

---

## Text

A specific gift passes the property transferred subject to any mortgage, deed of trust, or other lien existing at the date of death, without right of exoneration, regardless of a general directive to pay debts contained in the instrument.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2003.)
