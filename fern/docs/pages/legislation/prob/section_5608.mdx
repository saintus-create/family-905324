# PROB § 5608

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5608

---

## Text

“Beneficiary” means a person named in a revocable transfer on death deed as transferee of the property. A natural person, trust, or legal entity may be named as a beneficiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 215, Sec. 4. (SB 315) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
