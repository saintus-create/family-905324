# PROB § 18002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 6. RIGHTS OF THIRD PERSONS > Chapter: 1. Liability of Trustee to Third Persons

**Citation:** PROB § 18002

---

## Text

A trustee is personally liable for torts committed in the course of administration of the trust only if the trustee is personally at fault.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
