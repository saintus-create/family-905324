# PROB § 6406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 2. INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Chapter: 1. Intestate Succession Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 6406

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 6451, relatives of the halfblood inherit the same share they would inherit if they were of the whole blood.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 529, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
