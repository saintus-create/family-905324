# PROB § 8440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 3. Administrators With the Will Annexed

**Citation:** PROB § 8440

---

## Text

An administrator with the will annexed shall be appointed as personal representative if no executor is named in the will or if the sole executor or all the executors named in the will have waived the right to appointment or are for any reason unwilling or unable to act.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
