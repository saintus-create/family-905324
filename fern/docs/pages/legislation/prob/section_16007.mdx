# PROB § 16007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 1. Trustee’s Duties in General

**Citation:** PROB § 16007

---

## Text

The trustee has a duty to make the trust property productive under the circumstances and in furtherance of the purposes of the trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
