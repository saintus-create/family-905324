# PROB § 21101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 21101

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, this part applies to a will, trust, deed, and any other instrument.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003.)
