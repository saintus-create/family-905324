# PROB § 4615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 4615

---

## Text

“Health care” means any care, treatment, service, or procedure to maintain, diagnose, or otherwise affect a patient’s physical or mental health condition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 21, Sec. 1. (AB 2288) Effective January 1, 2023.)
