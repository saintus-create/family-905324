# PROB § 17202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 3. Proceedings Concerning Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 17202

---

## Text

The court may dismiss a petition if it appears that the proceeding is not reasonably necessary for the protection of the interests of the trustee or beneficiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
