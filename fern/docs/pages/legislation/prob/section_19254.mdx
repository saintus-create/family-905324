# PROB § 19254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 8. Allowance and Rejection of Claims

**Citation:** PROB § 19254

---

## Text

If within 30 days after a claim is filed the trustee has refused or neglected to act on the claim, the refusal or neglect may, at the option of the claimant, be deemed equivalent to the giving of a notice of rejection on the 30th day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
