# PROB § 6345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 9. Devise Subject to California Uniform Transfers to Minors Act

**Citation:** PROB § 6345

---

## Text

The will may provide for successor or substitute custodians and may specify the standard of compensation of the custodian.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
