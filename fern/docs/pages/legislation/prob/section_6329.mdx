# PROB § 6329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 8. Nonprobate Transfer to Trustee Named in Decedent’s Will

**Citation:** PROB § 6329

---

## Text

Enactment of this chapter does not invalidate trusts, otherwise valid, not made pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
