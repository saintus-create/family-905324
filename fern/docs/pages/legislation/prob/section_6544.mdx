# PROB § 6544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 4. Family Allowance

**Citation:** PROB § 6544

---

## Text

The costs of proceedings under this chapter shall be paid by the estate as expenses of administration.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
