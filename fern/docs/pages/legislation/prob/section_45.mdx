# PROB § 45

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 45

---

## Text

“Instrument” means a will, a document establishing or modifying a trust, a deed, or any other writing that designates a beneficiary or makes a donative transfer of property.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 56, Sec. 1. (SB 308) Effective January 1, 2020.)
