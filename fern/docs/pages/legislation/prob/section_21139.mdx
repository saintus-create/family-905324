# PROB § 21139

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 3. Exoneration; Ademption

**Citation:** PROB § 21139

---

## Text

The rules stated in Sections 21133 to 21135, inclusive, are not exhaustive, and nothing in those sections is intended to increase the incidence of ademption under the law of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 2003.)
