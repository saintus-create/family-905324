# PROB § 15620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 3. TRUSTEES AND BENEFICIARIES > Chapter: 1. Trustees > Article: 2. Cotrustees

**Citation:** PROB § 15620

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in the trust instrument, a power vested in two or more trustees may only be exercised by their unanimous action.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
