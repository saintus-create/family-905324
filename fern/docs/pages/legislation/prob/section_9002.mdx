# PROB § 9002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 9002

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by statute:

(a) All claims shall be filed in the manner and within the time provided in this part.

(b) A claim that is not filed as provided in this part is barred.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
