# PROB § 16201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 16201

---

## Text

This chapter does not affect the power of a court to relieve a trustee from restrictions on the exercise of powers under the trust instrument.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
