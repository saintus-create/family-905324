# PROB § 4100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 2. POWERS OF ATTORNEY GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 4100

---

## Text

This part applies to all powers of attorney under this division, subject to any special rules applicable to statutory form powers of attorney under Part 3 (commencing with Section 4400).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
