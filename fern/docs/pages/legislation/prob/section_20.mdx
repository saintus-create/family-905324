# PROB § 20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 20

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, the definitions in this part govern the construction of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 82, Sec. 1. Effective June 30, 1991. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 31 of Ch. 82.)
