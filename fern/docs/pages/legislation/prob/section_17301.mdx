# PROB § 17301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 4. Testamentary Trusts Subject to Continuing Court Jurisdiction > Article: 1. Administration of Testamentary Trusts Subject to Continuing Court Jurisdiction

**Citation:** PROB § 17301

---

## Text

If a trust described in Section 17300 continues after distribution of the decedent’s estate, the court in which the decedent’s estate was administered retains jurisdiction over the trust for any of the purposes specified in Section 17200.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
