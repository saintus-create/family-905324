# PROB § 21105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 21105

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Sections 641 and 642, a will passes all property the testator owns at death, including property acquired after execution of the will.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2003.)
