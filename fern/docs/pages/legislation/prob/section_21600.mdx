# PROB § 21600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 6. FAMILY PROTECTION: OMITTED SPOUSES AND CHILDREN > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 21600

---

## Text

This part shall apply to property passing by will through a decedent’s estate or by a trust, as defined in Section 82, that becomes irrevocable only on the death of the settlor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 724, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1998.)
