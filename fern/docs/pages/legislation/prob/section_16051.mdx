# PROB § 16051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 2.5. Uniform Prudent Investor Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16051

---

## Text

Compliance with the prudent investor rule is determined in light of the facts and circumstances existing at the time of a trustee’s decision or action and not by hindsight.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 63, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
