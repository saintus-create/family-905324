# PROB § 11641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 10. DISTRIBUTION OF ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Order for Distribution > Article: 3. Final Distribution

**Citation:** PROB § 11641

---

## Text

When an order settling a final account and for final distribution is entered, the personal representative may immediately distribute the property in the estate to the persons entitled to distribution, without further notice or proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
