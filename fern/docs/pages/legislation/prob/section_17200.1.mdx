# PROB § 17200.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 3. Proceedings Concerning Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 17200.1

---

## Text

All proceedings concerning the transfer of property of the trust shall be conducted pursuant to the provisions of Part 19 (commencing with Section 850) of Division 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 49, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2002.)
