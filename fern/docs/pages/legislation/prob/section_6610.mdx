# PROB § 6610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Small Estate Set-Aside

**Citation:** PROB § 6610

---

## Text

Upon becoming final, an order under Section 6609 shall be conclusive on all persons, whether or not they are then in being.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
