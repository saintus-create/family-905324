# PROB § 9700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 3. Deposit of Money and Personal Property With Financial Institutions

**Citation:** PROB § 9700

---

## Text

The personal representative may deposit money of the estate in an insured account in a financial institution in this state. Unless otherwise provided by court order, the money may be withdrawn without order of the court.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
