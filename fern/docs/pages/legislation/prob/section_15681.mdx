# PROB § 15681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 3. TRUSTEES AND BENEFICIARIES > Chapter: 1. Trustees > Article: 5. Compensation and Indemnification of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 15681

---

## Text

If the trust instrument does not specify the trustee’s compensation, the trustee is entitled to reasonable compensation under the circumstances.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
