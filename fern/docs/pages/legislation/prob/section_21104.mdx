# PROB § 21104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 21104

---

## Text

As used in this part, “at-death transfer” means a transfer that is revocable during the lifetime of the transferor, but does not include a joint tenancy or joint account with right of survivorship.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2003.)
