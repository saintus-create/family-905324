# PROB § 1261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 7. Proof of Giving Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 1261

---

## Text

Proof of mailing may be made in the manner prescribed in Section 1013a of the Code of Civil Procedure.
