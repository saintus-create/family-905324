# PROB § 8125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Notice of Hearing > Article: 3. Publication

**Citation:** PROB § 8125

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 8100, after the notice of hearing is published and an affidavit filed, any subsequent publication of the notice ordered by the court may omit the information for creditors and contingent creditors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
