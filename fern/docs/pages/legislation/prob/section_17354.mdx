# PROB § 17354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 4. Testamentary Trusts Subject to Continuing Court Jurisdiction > Article: 2. Removal of Trusts From Continuing Court Jurisdiction

**Citation:** PROB § 17354

---

## Text

After a trust is removed from continuing court jurisdiction pursuant to this article, neither a change in trustees nor any other event causes the trust to be subject to continuing court jurisdiction under Article 1 (commencing with Section 17300).

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
