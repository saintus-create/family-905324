# PROB § 8112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Notice of Hearing > Article: 2. Service of Notice of Hearing

**Citation:** PROB § 8112

---

## Text

A general personal representative shall give notice of administration of the estate of the decedent to creditors under Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 9050), and to public entities under Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 9200), of Part 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
