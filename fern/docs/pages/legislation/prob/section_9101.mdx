# PROB § 9101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 3. Time for Filing Claims

**Citation:** PROB § 9101

---

## Text

A vacancy in the office of the personal representative that occurs before expiration of the time for filing a claim does not extend the time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
