# PROB § 8907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 3. INVENTORY AND APPRAISAL > Chapter: 3. Appraisal > Article: 1. Procedure

**Citation:** PROB § 8907

---

## Text

Neither the personal representative nor the attorney for the personal representative is entitled to receive compensation for extraordinary services by reason of appraising any property in the estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 82, Sec. 24. Effective June 30, 1991. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 31 of Ch. 82.)
