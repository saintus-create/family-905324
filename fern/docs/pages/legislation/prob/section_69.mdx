# PROB § 69

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 69

---

## Text

“Revocable transfer on death deed” or “revocable TOD deed” means a revocable transfer on death deed as described in Section 5614.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 293, Sec. 3. (AB 139) Effective January 1, 2016.)
