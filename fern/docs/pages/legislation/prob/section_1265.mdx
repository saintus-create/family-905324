# PROB § 1265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 7. Proof of Giving Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 1265

---

## Text

Proof of electronic delivery may be made in the manner prescribed in Section 1013b of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 319, Sec. 25. (AB 976) Effective January 1, 2018.)
