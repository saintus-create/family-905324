# PROB § 360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Scope of Part

**Citation:** PROB § 360

---

## Text

This part applies to all fiduciaries and consultants, whether appointed or acting before, on, or after July 1, 1989.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
