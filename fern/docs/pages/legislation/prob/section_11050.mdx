# PROB § 11050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 8. ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 4. Compelling Account

**Citation:** PROB § 11050

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this chapter, if the personal representative does not file a required account, the court shall compel the account by punishment for contempt.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
