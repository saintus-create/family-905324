# PROB § 7641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Public Administrators > Article: 3. Deposit of Money of Estate

**Citation:** PROB § 7641

---

## Text

Money deposited in a financial institution or with the county treasurer under this article may be withdrawn upon the order of the public administrator when required for the purposes of administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
