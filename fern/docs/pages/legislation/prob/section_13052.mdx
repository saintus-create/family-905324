# PROB § 13052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. COLLECTION OR TRANSFER OF SMALL ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 13052

---

## Text

In making an appraisal for the purposes of this part, the probate referee shall use the date of the decedent’s death as the date of valuation of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
