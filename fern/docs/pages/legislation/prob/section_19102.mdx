# PROB § 19102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 5. Time for Filing Claims

**Citation:** PROB § 19102

---

## Text

A claim that is filed before expiration of the time for filing the claim is timely even if acted on by the trustee or the court after expiration of the time for filing claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
