# PROB § 2944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 5. PUBLIC GUARDIAN > Chapter: 4. Administration by Public Guardian

**Citation:** PROB § 2944

---

## Text

The public guardian is not liable for failing to take possession or control of property that is beyond the ability of the public guardian to possess or control.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
