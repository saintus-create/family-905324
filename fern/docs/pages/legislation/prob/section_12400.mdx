# PROB § 12400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 12. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF MISSING PERSONS PRESUMED DEAD

**Citation:** PROB § 12400

---

## Text

Unless the provision or context otherwise requires, as used in this part, “missing person” means a person who is presumed to be dead under Section 12401.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
