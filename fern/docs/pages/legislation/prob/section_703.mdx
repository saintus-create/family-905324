# PROB § 703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 15. DEPOSIT OF ESTATE PLANNING DOCUMENTS WITH ATTORNEY > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 703

---

## Text

“Depositor” means a natural person who deposits the person’s document with an attorney.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 519, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
