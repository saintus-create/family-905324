# PROB § 21386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 3.7. Presumption of Fraud or Undue Influence > Chapter: 2. Operation and Effect of Presumption

**Citation:** PROB § 21386

---

## Text

If a donative transfer fails under this part, the instrument making the donative transfer shall operate as if the beneficiary had predeceased the transferor without spouse, domestic partner, or issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 56, Sec. 3. (SB 153) Effective January 1, 2018.)
