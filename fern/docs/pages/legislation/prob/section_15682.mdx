# PROB § 15682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 3. TRUSTEES AND BENEFICIARIES > Chapter: 1. Trustees > Article: 5. Compensation and Indemnification of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 15682

---

## Text

The court may fix an amount of periodic compensation under Sections 15680 and 15681 to continue for as long as the court determines is proper.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
