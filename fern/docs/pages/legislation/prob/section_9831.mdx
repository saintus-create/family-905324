# PROB § 9831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 9. Compromise of Claims and Actions; Extension, Renewal, or Modification of Obligations

**Citation:** PROB § 9831

---

## Text

Unless the time for filing creditor claims has expired, authorization by order of court is required for a compromise or settlement of a claim, action, or proceeding by or for the benefit of, or against, the decedent, the personal representative, or the estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
