# PROB § 1827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 1. Establishment of Conservatorship > Article: 3. Establishment of Conservatorship

**Citation:** PROB § 1827

---

## Text

The court shall hear and determine the matter of the establishment of the conservatorship according to the law and procedure relating to the trial of civil actions, including trial by jury if demanded by the proposed conservatee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 17, Sec. 4.2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
