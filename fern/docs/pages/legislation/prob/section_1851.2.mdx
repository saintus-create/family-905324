# PROB § 1851.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 2. Periodic Review of Conservatorship

**Citation:** PROB § 1851.2

---

## Text

Each court shall coordinate investigations with the filing of accountings, so that investigators may review accountings before visiting conservatees, if feasible.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 553, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2008.)
