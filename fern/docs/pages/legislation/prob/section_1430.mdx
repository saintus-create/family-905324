# PROB § 1430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

## Text

“Petition” includes an application or request in the nature of a petition.
