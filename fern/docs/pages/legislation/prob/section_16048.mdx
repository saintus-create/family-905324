# PROB § 16048

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 2.5. Uniform Prudent Investor Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16048

---

## Text

In making and implementing investment decisions, the trustee has a duty to diversify the investments of the trust unless, under the circumstances, it is prudent not to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 63, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
