# PROB § 9781

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 6. Abandonment of Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** PROB § 9781

---

## Text

Unless otherwise provided in the will, subject to the requirements of this chapter, the personal representative may exercise the power provided in Section 9780 without court authorization or approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
