# PROB § 4803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 5. ADVANCE HEALTH CARE DIRECTIVE REGISTRY

**Citation:** PROB § 4803

---

## Text

Failure to register with the Secretary of State does not affect the validity of any advance health care directive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
