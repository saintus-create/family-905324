# PROB § 1264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 7. Proof of Giving Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 1264

---

## Text

Proof of notice by personal delivery may be made by the affidavit of the person making the delivery showing the time and place of delivery and the name of the person to whom delivery was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
