# PROB § 11620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 10. DISTRIBUTION OF ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Order for Distribution > Article: 2. Preliminary Distribution

**Citation:** PROB § 11620

---

## Text

A petition for an order for preliminary distribution of all, or a portion of, the share of a decedent’s estate to which a beneficiary is entitled may not be filed unless at least two months have elapsed after letters are first issued to a general personal representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
