# PROB § 26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 26

---

## Text

“Child” means any individual entitled to take as a child under this code by intestate succession from the parent whose relationship is involved.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
