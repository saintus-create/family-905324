# PROB § 730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 15. DEPOSIT OF ESTATE PLANNING DOCUMENTS WITH ATTORNEY > Chapter: 3. Termination of Deposit > Article: 2. Termination by Attorney

**Citation:** PROB § 730

---

## Text

An attorney with whom a document has been deposited, or to whom a document has been transferred pursuant to this article, may terminate the deposit only as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 519, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
