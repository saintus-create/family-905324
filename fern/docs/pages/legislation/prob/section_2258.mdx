# PROB § 2258

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 4. PROVISIONS COMMON TO GUARDIANSHIP AND CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 3. Temporary Guardians and Conservators

**Citation:** PROB § 2258

---

## Text

A temporary guardian or temporary conservator is subject to the provisions of this division governing the suspension, removal, resignation, and discharge of a guardian or conservator.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
