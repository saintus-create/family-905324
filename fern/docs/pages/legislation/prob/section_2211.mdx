# PROB § 2211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 4. PROVISIONS COMMON TO GUARDIANSHIP AND CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 2. Jurisdiction and Venue > Article: 2. Change of Venue

**Citation:** PROB § 2211

---

## Text

The court in which a guardianship or conservatorship proceeding is pending may, upon petition therefor, transfer the proceeding to another county within this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
