# PROB § 10256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 18. Sales > Article: 5. Sale of Personal Property

**Citation:** PROB § 10256

---

## Text

Whether a sale of personal property is private or at public auction, bids shall substantially comply with any terms specified in the notice of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
