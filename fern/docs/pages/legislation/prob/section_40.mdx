# PROB § 40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 40

---

## Text

“Financial institution” means a state or national bank, state or federal savings and loan association or credit union, or like organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
