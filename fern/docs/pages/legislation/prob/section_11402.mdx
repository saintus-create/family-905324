# PROB § 11402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 9. PAYMENT OF DEBTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Preliminary Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 11402

---

## Text

“Wage claim” means a claim for wages, not exceeding two thousand dollars ($2,000), of each employee of the decedent for work done or personal services rendered within 90 days before the death of the decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
