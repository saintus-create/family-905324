# PROB § 4678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 2. UNIFORM HEALTH CARE DECISIONS ACT > Chapter: 1. Advance Health Care Directives > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 4678

---

## Text

Unless otherwise specified in an advance health care directive, a person then authorized to make health care decisions for a patient has the same rights as the patient to request, receive, examine, copy, and consent to the disclosure of medical or any other health care information.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
