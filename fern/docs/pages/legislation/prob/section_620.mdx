# PROB § 620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 14. POWERS OF APPOINTMENT > Chapter: 3. Creation of Powers of Appointment

**Citation:** PROB § 620

---

## Text

A power of appointment can be created only by a donor having the capacity to transfer the interest in property to which the power relates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 30, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
