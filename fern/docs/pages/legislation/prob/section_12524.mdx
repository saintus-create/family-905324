# PROB § 12524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 2. Ancillary Administration > Article: 2. Probate of Nondomiciliary Decedent’s Will Admitted to Probate in Sister State or Foreign Nation

**Citation:** PROB § 12524

---

## Text

A nondomiciliary decedent’s will admitted to probate under this article has the same force and effect as the will of a person who dies while domiciled in this state that is admitted to probate in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
