# PROB § 19403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 12. Distributee Liability

**Citation:** PROB § 19403

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter affects the rights of a purchaser or encumbrancer of property in good faith and for value from a person who is personally liable under this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 280. Effective January 1, 2005.)
