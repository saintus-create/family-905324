# PROB § 11443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 9. PAYMENT OF DEBTS > Chapter: 3. Allocation of Debts Between Estate and Surviving Spouse

**Citation:** PROB § 11443

---

## Text

The petitioner shall give notice of the hearing as provided in Section 1220, together with a copy of the petition and the order to show cause, if any.
