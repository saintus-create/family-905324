# PROB § 11467

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 9. PAYMENT OF DEBTS > Chapter: 4. Debts That are Contingent, Disputed, or Not Due

**Citation:** PROB § 11467

---

## Text

The court may order that the administration of the estate continue until the contingency, dispute, or passage of time of a debt that is contingent, disputed, or not due is resolved.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 31.)
