# PROB § 3303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 8. OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 3303

---

## Text

Nothing in this part limits the provisions of the California Uniform Transfers to Minors Act, Part 9 (commencing with Section 3900).

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
