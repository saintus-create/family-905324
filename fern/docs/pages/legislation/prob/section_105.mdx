# PROB § 105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. EFFECT OF DEATH OF MARRIED PERSON ON COMMUNITY AND QUASI-COMMUNITY PROPERTY

**Citation:** PROB § 105

---

## Text

This part does not apply where the decedent died before January 1, 1985, and the law applicable prior to January 1, 1985, continues to apply where the decedent died before January 1, 1985.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
