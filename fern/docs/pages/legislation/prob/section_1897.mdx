# PROB § 1897

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 4. Legal Capacity of Conservatee > Article: 2. Capacity to Give Informed Consent for Medical Treatment

**Citation:** PROB § 1897

---

## Text

An order of the court under Section 1880 continues in effect until the earliest of the following times:

(1) The time specified in the order, if any.

(2) The time the order is modified or revoked.

(3) The time the conservatorship is terminated.
