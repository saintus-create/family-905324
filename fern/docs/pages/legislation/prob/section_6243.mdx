# PROB § 6243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 6. California Statutory Will > Article: 3. Form and Full Text of Clauses

**Citation:** PROB § 6243

---

## Text

Except as specifically provided in this chapter, the general law of California applies to a California statutory will.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 20.)
