# PROB § 8571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 9. Nonresident Personal Representative

**Citation:** PROB § 8571

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter and notwithstanding a waiver of a bond, the court in its discretion may require a nonresident personal representative to give a bond in an amount determined by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
