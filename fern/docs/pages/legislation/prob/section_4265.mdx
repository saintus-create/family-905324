# PROB § 4265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 2. POWERS OF ATTORNEY GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys-in-Fact > Article: 3. Authority of Attorneys-in-Fact

**Citation:** PROB § 4265

---

## Text

A power of attorney may not authorize an attorney-in-fact to make, publish, declare, amend, or revoke the principal’s will.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 36. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
