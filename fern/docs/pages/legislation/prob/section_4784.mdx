# PROB § 4784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 4. REQUEST REGARDING RESUSCITATIVE MEASURES

**Citation:** PROB § 4784

---

## Text

In the absence of knowledge to the contrary, a health care provider may presume that a request regarding resuscitative measures is valid and unrevoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 266, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2009.)
