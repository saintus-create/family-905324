# PROB § 5512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 3. UNIFORM TOD SECURITY REGISTRATION ACT

**Citation:** PROB § 5512

---

## Text

This part applies to registrations of securities in beneficiary form made before, on, or after January 1, 1999, by decedents dying on or after January 1, 1999.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 242, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
