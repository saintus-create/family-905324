# PROB § 8802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 3. INVENTORY AND APPRAISAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 8802

---

## Text

The inventory and appraisal shall separately list each item and shall state the fair market value of the item at the time of the decedent’s death in monetary terms opposite the item.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
