# PROB § 16442

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Liability of Trustees to Beneficiaries > Article: 3. Measure of Liability for Breach of Trust

**Citation:** PROB § 16442

---

## Text

The provisions in this article for liability of a trustee for breach of trust do not prevent resort to any other remedy available under the statutory or common law.
