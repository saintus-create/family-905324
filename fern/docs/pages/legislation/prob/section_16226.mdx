# PROB § 16226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 2. Specific Powers of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 16226

---

## Text

The trustee has the power to acquire or dispose of property, for cash or on credit, at public or private sale, or by exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
