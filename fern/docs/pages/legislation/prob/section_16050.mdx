# PROB § 16050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 2.5. Uniform Prudent Investor Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16050

---

## Text

In investing and managing trust assets, a trustee may only incur costs that are appropriate and reasonable in relation to the assets, overall investment strategy, purposes, and other circumstances of the trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 63, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
