# PROB § 281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 8. DISCLAIMER OF TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER INTERESTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 281

---

## Text

A disclaimer, when effective, is irrevocable and binding upon the beneficiary and all persons claiming by, through, or under the beneficiary, including creditors of the beneficiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
