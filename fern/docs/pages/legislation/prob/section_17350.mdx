# PROB § 17350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 4. Testamentary Trusts Subject to Continuing Court Jurisdiction > Article: 2. Removal of Trusts From Continuing Court Jurisdiction

**Citation:** PROB § 17350

---

## Text

This article applies only to trusts created by will executed before July 1, 1977, and not incorporated by reference in a will on or after July 1, 1977.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
