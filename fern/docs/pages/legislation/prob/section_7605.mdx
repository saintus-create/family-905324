# PROB § 7605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 4. Public Administrators > Article: 1. Taking Temporary Possession or Control of Property

**Citation:** PROB § 7605

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2010, the public administrator shall comply with the continuing education requirements that are established by the California State Association of Public Administrators, Public Guardians, and Public Conservators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 237, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2009.)
