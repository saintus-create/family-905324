# PROB § 4309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 2. POWERS OF ATTORNEY GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Relations With Third Persons

**Citation:** PROB § 4309

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter requires a third person to engage in any transaction with an attorney-in-fact if the attorney-in-fact has previously breached any agreement with the third person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
