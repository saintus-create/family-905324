# PROB § 11853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 10. DISTRIBUTION OF ESTATE > Chapter: 5. Deposit with County Treasurer

**Citation:** PROB § 11853

---

## Text

If money is deposited or is already on deposit with the county treasurer, the personal representative shall deliver to the county treasurer a certified copy of the order for distribution.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
