# PROB § 9880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 12. Purchase of Claims or Estate Property by Personal Representative or Personal Representative’s Attorney

**Citation:** PROB § 9880

---

## Text

Except as provided in this chapter, neither the personal representative nor the personal representative’s attorney may do any of the following:

(a) Purchase any property of the estate or any claim against the estate, directly or indirectly.

(b) Be interested in any such purchase.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
