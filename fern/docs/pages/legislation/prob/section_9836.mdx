# PROB § 9836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 9. Compromise of Claims and Actions; Extension, Renewal, or Modification of Obligations

**Citation:** PROB § 9836

---

## Text

The court authorization required by this chapter shall be obtained from the court in which the estate is being administered.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
