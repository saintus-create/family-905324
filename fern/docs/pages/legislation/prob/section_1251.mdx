# PROB § 1251

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 6. Request for Special Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 1251

---

## Text

A request for special notice under this chapter may be modified or withdrawn in the same manner as provided for the making of the initial request.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
