# PROB § 1900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 4. Legal Capacity of Conservatee > Article: 3. Capacity of Conservatee to Marry

**Citation:** PROB § 1900

---

## Text

The appointment of a conservator of the person or estate or both does not affect the capacity of the conservatee to marry or to enter into a registered domestic partnership.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 418, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2006.)
