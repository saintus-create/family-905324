# PROB § 870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 20. Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act

**Citation:** PROB § 870

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the Revised Uniform Fiduciary Access to Digital Assets Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 551, Sec. 1. (AB 691) Effective January 1, 2017.)
