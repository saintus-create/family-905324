# PROB § 4767

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 3. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 3. Petitions and Orders

**Citation:** PROB § 4767

---

## Text

A proceeding under this part is commenced by filing a petition stating facts showing that the petition is authorized under this part, the grounds of the petition, and, if known to the petitioner, the terms of any advance health care directive in question.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
