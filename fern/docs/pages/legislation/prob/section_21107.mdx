# PROB § 21107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 21107

---

## Text

If an instrument directs the conversion of real property into money at the transferor’s death, the real property and its proceeds shall be deemed personal property from the time of the transferor’s death.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2003.)
