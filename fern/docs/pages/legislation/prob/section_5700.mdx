# PROB § 5700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 5. GIFTS IN VIEW OF IMPENDING DEATH

**Citation:** PROB § 5700

---

## Text

As used in this part, “gift” means a transfer of personal property made voluntarily and without consideration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 18.)
