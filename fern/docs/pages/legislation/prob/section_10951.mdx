# PROB § 10951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 8. ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 2. When Account Required

**Citation:** PROB § 10951

---

## Text

The personal representative shall file a final account and petition for an order for final distribution of the estate when the estate is in a condition to be closed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
