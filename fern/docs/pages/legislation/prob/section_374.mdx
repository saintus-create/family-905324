# PROB § 374

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 2. Appointment of Substitute Fiduciary

**Citation:** PROB § 374

---

## Text

A substitute fiduciary or an original fiduciary reinstated under Section 373 is not liable for the acts or omissions of the predecessor fiduciary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
