# PROB § 8123

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Notice of Hearing > Article: 3. Publication

**Citation:** PROB § 8123

---

## Text

The caption of a notice under this article shall be in 8-point type or larger and the text shall be in 7-point type or larger.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
