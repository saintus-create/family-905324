# PROB § 84

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 84

---

## Text

“Trustee” includes an original, additional, or successor trustee, whether or not appointed or confirmed by a court.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
