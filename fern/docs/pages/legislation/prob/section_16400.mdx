# PROB § 16400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Liability of Trustees to Beneficiaries > Article: 1. Liability for Breach of Trust

**Citation:** PROB § 16400

---

## Text

A violation by the trustee of any duty that the trustee owes the beneficiary is a breach of trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
