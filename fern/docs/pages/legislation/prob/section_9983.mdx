# PROB § 9983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 17. Option to Purchase Given in Will

**Citation:** PROB § 9983

---

## Text

No omission, error, or irregularity in the proceedings under this chapter shall impair or invalidate the proceedings or the transfer or conveyance made pursuant to an order made under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
