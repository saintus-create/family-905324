# PROB § 9923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 14. Exchange of Property

**Citation:** PROB § 9923

---

## Text

No omission, error, or irregularity in the proceedings under this chapter shall impair or invalidate the proceedings or the exchange made pursuant to an order made under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
