# PROB § 16605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. California Uniform Directed Trust Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16605

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the consent of the public administrator, public guardian, or public conservator is required before they may be appointed to act as a trust director or directed trustee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 721, Sec. 3. (SB 801) Effective January 1, 2024.)
