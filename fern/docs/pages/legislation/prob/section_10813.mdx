# PROB § 10813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 7. COMPENSATION OF PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE AND ATTORNEY FOR THE PERSONAL REPRESENTATIVE > Chapter: 1. Amount of Compensation > Article: 2. Compensation of Attorney For the Personal Representative

**Citation:** PROB § 10813

---

## Text

An agreement between the personal representative and the attorney for higher compensation for the attorney than that provided by this part is void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 82, Sec. 30. Effective June 30, 1991. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 31 of Ch. 82.)
