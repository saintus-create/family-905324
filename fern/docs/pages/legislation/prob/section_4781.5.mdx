# PROB § 4781.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 4. REQUEST REGARDING RESUSCITATIVE MEASURES

**Citation:** PROB § 4781.5

---

## Text

The legally recognized health care decisionmaker shall make health care decisions pursuant to this part in accordance with Sections 4684 and 4714.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 266, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2009.)
