# PROB § 10404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 10404

---

## Text

The personal representative may not be granted authority to administer the estate under this part if the decedent’s will provides that the estate shall not be administered under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
