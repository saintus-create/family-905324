# PROB § 16382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Uniform Fiduciary Income and Principal Act > Article: 8. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 16382

---

## Text

This chapter applies to a trust or estate existing or created on or after the effective date of this chapter, except as otherwise expressly provided in the terms of the trust or this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 28, Sec. 2. (SB 522) Effective January 1, 2024.)
