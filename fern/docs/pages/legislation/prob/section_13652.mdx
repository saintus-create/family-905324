# PROB § 13652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 5. Determination or Confirmation of Property Passing or Belonging to Surviving Spouse

**Citation:** PROB § 13652

---

## Text

If proceedings for the administration of the estate of the deceased spouse are pending, a petition under this chapter shall be filed in those proceedings without the payment of an additional fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
