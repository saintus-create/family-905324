# PROB § 5620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 2. Execution and Revocation > Article: 1. Execution

**Citation:** PROB § 5620

---

## Text

An owner of real property who has the capacity to contract may make a revocable transfer on death deed of the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 293, Sec. 17. (AB 139) Effective January 1, 2016. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
