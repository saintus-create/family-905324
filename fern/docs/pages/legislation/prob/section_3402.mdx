# PROB § 3402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 8. OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 2. Money or Property Belonging to Minor > Article: 1. Total Estate Not in Excess of $5,000

**Citation:** PROB § 3402

---

## Text

The written receipt of the parent giving the written assurance under Section 3401 shall be an acquittance of the person making the payment of money or delivery of other property pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
