# PROB § 21368

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 3.7. Presumption of Fraud or Undue Influence > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 21368

---

## Text

“Domestic partner” has the meaning provided in Section 297 of the Family Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 620, Sec. 7. (SB 105) Effective January 1, 2011.)
