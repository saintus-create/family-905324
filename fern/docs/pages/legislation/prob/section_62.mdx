# PROB § 62

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 62

---

## Text

“Property” means anything that may be the subject of ownership and includes both real and personal property and any interest therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
