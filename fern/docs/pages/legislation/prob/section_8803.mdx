# PROB § 8803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 3. INVENTORY AND APPRAISAL > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 8803

---

## Text

On the filing of an inventory and appraisal or a supplemental inventory and appraisal, the personal representative shall, pursuant to Section 1252, deliver a copy to each person who has requested special notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 319, Sec. 70. (AB 976) Effective January 1, 2018.)
