# PROB § 265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 8. DISCLAIMER OF TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER INTERESTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 265

---

## Text

“Disclaimer” means any writing which declines, refuses, renounces, or disclaims any interest that would otherwise be taken by a beneficiary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
