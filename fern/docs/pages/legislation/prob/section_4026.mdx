# PROB § 4026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 4026

---

## Text

“Principal” means a natural person who executes a power of attorney.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
