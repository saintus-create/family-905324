# PROB § 16624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. California Uniform Directed Trust Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16624

---

## Text

In an action against a trust director for breach of trust, the director may assert the same defenses a trustee in a like position and under similar circumstances could assert in an action for breach of trust against the trustee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 721, Sec. 3. (SB 801) Effective January 1, 2024.)
