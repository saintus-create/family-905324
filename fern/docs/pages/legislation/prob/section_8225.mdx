# PROB § 8225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Probate of Will > Article: 2. Proof of Will

**Citation:** PROB § 8225

---

## Text

When the court admits a will to probate, that fact shall be recorded in the minutes by the clerk and the will shall be filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
