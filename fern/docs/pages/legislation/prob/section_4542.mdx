# PROB § 4542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 4. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Chapter: 3. Petitions, Orders,Appeals

**Citation:** PROB § 4542

---

## Text

A proceeding under this part is commenced by filing a petition stating facts showing that the petition is authorized under this part, the grounds of the petition, and, if known to the petitioner, the terms of the power of attorney.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
