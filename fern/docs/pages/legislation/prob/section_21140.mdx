# PROB § 21140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 4. Effective Dates

## Text

This part applies to all instruments, regardless of when they were executed.
