# PROB § 16227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 2. Specific Powers of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 16227

---

## Text

The trustee has the power to manage, control, divide, develop, improve, exchange, partition, change the character of, or abandon trust property or any interest therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
