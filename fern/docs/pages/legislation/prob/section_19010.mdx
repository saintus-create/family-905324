# PROB § 19010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 19010

---

## Text

Nothing in this part imposes any duty on the trustee to initiate the notice proceeding set forth in Section 19003, and the trustee is not liable for failure to initiate the proceeding under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
