# PROB § 1431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 1431

---

## Text

“Proceedings to establish a limited conservatorship” include proceedings to modify or revoke the powers or duties of a limited conservator.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
