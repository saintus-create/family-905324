# PROB § 1217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 2. Mailing in General

**Citation:** PROB § 1217

---

## Text

If a notice or other paper is required to be served or otherwise given and no other manner of giving the notice or other paper is specified by statute, the notice or other paper shall be delivered pursuant to Section 1215.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 319, Sec. 20. (AB 976) Effective January 1, 2018.)
