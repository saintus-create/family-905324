# PROB § 5130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 2. MULTIPLE-PARTY ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5130

---

## Text

“Joint account” means an account payable on request to one or more of two or more parties whether or not mention is made of any right of survivorship.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
