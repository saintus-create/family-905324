# PROB § 10380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 18. Sales > Article: 9. Damages and Recovery of Property

**Citation:** PROB § 10380

---

## Text

The personal representative is liable to an interested person for damages suffered by the interested person by reason of the neglect or misconduct of the personal representative in the proceedings in relation to a sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
