# PROB § 21521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 5. COMPLIANCE WITH INTERNAL REVENUE CODE > Chapter: 2. Marital Deduction Gifts

**Citation:** PROB § 21521

---

## Text

Sections 21524 and 21526 do not apply to a trust that qualifies for the marital deduction under Section 20.2056(e)-2(b) of the Code of Federal Regulations (commonly referred to as the “estate trust”).

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
