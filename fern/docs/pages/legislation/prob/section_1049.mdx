# PROB § 1049

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Hearings and Orders

**Citation:** PROB § 1049

---

## Text

An order may be enforced as provided in Title 9 (commencing with Section 680.010) of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
