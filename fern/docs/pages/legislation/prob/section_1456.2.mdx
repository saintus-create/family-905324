# PROB § 1456.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 1456.2

---

## Text

On or before January 1, 2010, the public conservator shall comply with the continuing education requirements that are established by the California State Association of Public Administrators, Public Guardians, and Public Conservators.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 237, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2009.)
