# PROB § 1003.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Rules of Practice

**Citation:** PROB § 1003.5

---

## Text

The public guardian shall not be appointed as a guardian ad litem pursuant to Section 1003 unless the court, after reasonable notice and inquiry, finds that no other qualified person is willing to act as a guardian ad litem.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 472, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
