# PROB § 3904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 9. CALIFORNIA UNIFORM TRANSFERS TO MINORS ACT

**Citation:** PROB § 3904

---

## Text

A person may make a transfer by irrevocable gift to, or the irrevocable exercise of a power of appointment in favor of, a custodian for the benefit of a minor pursuant to Section 3909.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
