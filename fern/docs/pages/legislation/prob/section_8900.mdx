# PROB § 8900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 3. INVENTORY AND APPRAISAL > Chapter: 3. Appraisal > Article: 1. Procedure

**Citation:** PROB § 8900

---

## Text

The appraisal of property in the inventory shall be made by the personal representative, probate referee, or independent expert as provided in this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
