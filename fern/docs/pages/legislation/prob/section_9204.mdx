# PROB § 9204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 5. Claims by Public Entities

**Citation:** PROB § 9204

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect the order of priority of claims provided for under other provisions of law.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
