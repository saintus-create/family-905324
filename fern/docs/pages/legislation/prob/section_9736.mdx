# PROB § 9736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 4. Investments and Purchase of Property

**Citation:** PROB § 9736

---

## Text

The personal representative may hold a security in the name of a nominee or in any other form without disclosure of the estate so that title to the security may pass by delivery.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
