# PROB § 38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 38

---

## Text

“Family allowance” means an allowance provided for in Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 6540) of Part 3 of Division 6.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
