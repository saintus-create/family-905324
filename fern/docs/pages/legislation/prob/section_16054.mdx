# PROB § 16054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 2.5. Uniform Prudent Investor Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16054

---

## Text

This article applies to trusts existing on and created after its effective date. As applied to trusts existing on its effective date, this article governs only decisions or actions occurring after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 63, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
