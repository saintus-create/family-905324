# PROB § 4685

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 2. UNIFORM HEALTH CARE DECISIONS ACT > Chapter: 1. Advance Health Care Directives > Article: 2. Powers of Attorney for Health Care

**Citation:** PROB § 4685

---

## Text

Unless the power of attorney for health care provides otherwise, the agent designated in the power of attorney who is known to the health care provider to be reasonably available and willing to make health care decisions has priority over any other person in making health care decisions for the principal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
