# PROB § 365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Procedural Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 365

---

## Text

Proceedings under this part are in the court having jurisdiction over the estate or, if none, any court in which jurisdiction of the estate is proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
