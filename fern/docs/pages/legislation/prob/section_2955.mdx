# PROB § 2955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 5. PUBLIC GUARDIAN > Chapter: 5. Financial Abuse of Mentally Impaired Elders > Article: 2. Estate Protection

**Citation:** PROB § 2955

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit or restrict a public guardian from undertaking any other proceeding authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 813, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
