# PROB § 13113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. COLLECTION OR TRANSFER OF SMALL ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Affidavit Procedure for Collection or Transfer of Personal Property

**Citation:** PROB § 13113

---

## Text

The remedies available under Sections 13109 to 13111, inclusive, are in addition to any remedies available by reason of any fraud or intentional wrongdoing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 29, Sec. 10. (AB 1716) Effective January 1, 2023.)
