# PROB § 9351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 8. Claims in Litigation > Article: 1. Claim Where No Pending Action or Proceeding

**Citation:** PROB § 9351

---

## Text

An action may not be commenced against a decedent’s personal representative on a cause of action against the decedent unless a claim is first filed as provided in this part and the claim is rejected in whole or in part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
