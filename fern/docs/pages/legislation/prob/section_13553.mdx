# PROB § 13553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Liability for Debts of Deceased Spouse

**Citation:** PROB § 13553

---

## Text

The surviving spouse is not liable under this chapter if all the property described in paragraphs (1) and (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 13502 is administered under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
