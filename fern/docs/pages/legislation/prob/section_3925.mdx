# PROB § 3925

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 9. CALIFORNIA UNIFORM TRANSFERS TO MINORS ACT

**Citation:** PROB § 3925

---

## Text

This part shall not be construed as providing an exclusive method for making gifts or other transfers to minors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
