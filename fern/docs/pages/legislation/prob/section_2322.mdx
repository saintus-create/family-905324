# PROB § 2322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 4. PROVISIONS COMMON TO GUARDIANSHIP AND CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 4. Oath, Letters, and Bond > Article: 3. Bonds of Guardians and Conservators

**Citation:** PROB § 2322

---

## Text

One appointed only as guardian of the person or conservator of the person need not file a bond unless required by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
