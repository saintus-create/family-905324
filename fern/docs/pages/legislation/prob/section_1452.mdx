# PROB § 1452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 1452

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specifically provided in this division, there is no right to trial by jury in proceedings under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
