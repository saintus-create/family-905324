# PROB § 16421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Liability of Trustees to Beneficiaries > Article: 2. Remedies for Breach of Trust

**Citation:** PROB § 16421

---

## Text

The remedies of a beneficiary against the trustee are exclusively in equity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
