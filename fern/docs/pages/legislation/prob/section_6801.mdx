# PROB § 6801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 4. ESCHEAT OF DECEDENT'S PROPERTY

**Citation:** PROB § 6801

---

## Text

Real property in this state escheats to this state in accordance with Section 6800.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
