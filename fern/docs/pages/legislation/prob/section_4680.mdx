# PROB § 4680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 2. UNIFORM HEALTH CARE DECISIONS ACT > Chapter: 1. Advance Health Care Directives > Article: 2. Powers of Attorney for Health Care

**Citation:** PROB § 4680

---

## Text

A power of attorney for health care is legally sufficient if it satisfies the requirements of Section 4673.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
