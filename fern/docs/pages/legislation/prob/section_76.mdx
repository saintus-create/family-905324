# PROB § 76

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 76

---

## Text

A “subscribing witness” to a will means a witness who signs the will as provided in Section 6110.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
