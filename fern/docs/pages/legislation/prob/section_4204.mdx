# PROB § 4204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 2. POWERS OF ATTORNEY GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys-in-Fact > Article: 1. Qualifications and Authority of Attorneys-in-Fact

**Citation:** PROB § 4204

---

## Text

An attorney-in-fact is entitled to reasonable compensation for services rendered to the principal as attorney-in-fact and to reimbursement for reasonable expenses incurred as a result of acting as attorney-in-fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
