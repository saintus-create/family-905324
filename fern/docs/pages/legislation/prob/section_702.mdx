# PROB § 702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 15. DEPOSIT OF ESTATE PLANNING DOCUMENTS WITH ATTORNEY > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 702

---

## Text

“Deposit” means delivery of a document by a depositor to an attorney for safekeeping or authorization by a depositor for an attorney to retain a document for safekeeping.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 519, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
