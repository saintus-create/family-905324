# PROB § 18101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 6. RIGHTS OF THIRD PERSONS > Chapter: 2. Protection of Third Persons

**Citation:** PROB § 18101

---

## Text

A third person who acts in good faith is not bound to ensure the proper application of trust property paid or delivered to the trustee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
