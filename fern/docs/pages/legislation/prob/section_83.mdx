# PROB § 83

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 83

---

## Text

“Trust company” means an entity that has qualified to engage in and conduct a trust business in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
