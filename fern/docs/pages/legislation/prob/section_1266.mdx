# PROB § 1266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 7. Proof of Giving Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 1266

---

## Text

Proof of notice, however given, may be made by evidence presented at the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1265 by Stats. 2017, Ch. 319, Sec. 24. (AB 976) Effective January 1, 2018.)
