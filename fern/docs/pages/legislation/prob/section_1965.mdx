# PROB § 1965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 6. Sterilization

**Citation:** PROB § 1965

---

## Text

Any court order made pursuant to this chapter granting authority to consent to sterilization shall be stayed pending a final determination on appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
