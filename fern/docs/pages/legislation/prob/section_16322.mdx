# PROB § 16322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Uniform Fiduciary Income and Principal Act > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 16322

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in the terms of a trust or this chapter, this chapter applies to the following:

(a) A trust or an estate.

(b) A life estate or other term interest in which the interest of one or more persons will be succeeded by the interest of one or more other persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 28, Sec. 2. (SB 522) Effective January 1, 2024.)
