# PROB § 3613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 8. OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 4. Money or Property Paid or Delivered Pursuant to Compromise or Judgment for Minor or Disabled Person > Article: 2. Disposition of Money or Other Property Where No Guardianship or Conservatorship

**Citation:** PROB § 3613

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, a court may not make an order or give a judgment pursuant to Section 3600, 3601, 3602, 3610, or 3611 with respect to an adult who has the capacity within the meaning of Section 812 to consent to the order and who has no conservator of the estate with authority to make that decision , without the express consent of that person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 67, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 2005.)
