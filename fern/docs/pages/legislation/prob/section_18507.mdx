# PROB § 18507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 7. UNIFORM PRUDENT MANAGEMENT OF INSTITUTIONAL FUNDS ACT

**Citation:** PROB § 18507

---

## Text

Compliance with this part is determined in light of the facts and circumstances existing at the time a decision is made or action is taken, and not by hindsight.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 715, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2009.)
