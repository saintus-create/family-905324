# PROB § 9839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 9. Compromise of Claims and Actions; Extension, Renewal, or Modification of Obligations

**Citation:** PROB § 9839

---

## Text

If the personal representative pays a claim for less than its full amount, the personal representative’s accounts may be credited only for the amount actually paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
