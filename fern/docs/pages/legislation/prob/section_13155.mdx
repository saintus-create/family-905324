# PROB § 13155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. COLLECTION OR TRANSFER OF SMALL ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Court Order Determining Succession to Property

**Citation:** PROB § 13155

---

## Text

Upon becoming final, an order under this chapter determining that property is property passing to the petitioner is conclusive on all persons, whether or not they are in being.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 46.)
