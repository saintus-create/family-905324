# PROB § 8460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 4. Administrators

**Citation:** PROB § 8460

---

## Text

(a) If the decedent dies intestate, the court shall appoint an administrator as personal representative.

(b) The court may appoint one or more persons as administrator.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
