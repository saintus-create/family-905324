# PROB § 2311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 4. PROVISIONS COMMON TO GUARDIANSHIP AND CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 4. Oath, Letters, and Bond > Article: 2. Letters

**Citation:** PROB § 2311

---

## Text

Except as otherwise required by the order of appointment, the letters of guardianship or conservatorship shall be in substantially the same form as letters of administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
