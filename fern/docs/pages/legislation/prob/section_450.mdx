# PROB § 450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 12. PROBATE REFEREES > Chapter: 2. Powers of Probate Referee

**Citation:** PROB § 450

---

## Text

Upon designation by the court, the probate referee has all the powers of a referee of the superior court and all other powers provided in this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
