# PROB § 16626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. California Uniform Directed Trust Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16626

---

## Text

(a) By accepting appointment as a trust director of a trust subject to this chapter, the trust director submits to personal jurisdiction of the courts of this state regarding any matter related to a power or duty of the trust director.

(b) This section does not preclude other methods of obtaining jurisdiction over a trust director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 721, Sec. 3. (SB 801) Effective January 1, 2024.)
