# PROB § 6225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 6. California Statutory Will > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 6225

---

## Text

Only the texts of property disposition clauses and the mandatory clauses shall be considered in determining their meaning. Their titles shall be disregarded.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 20.)
