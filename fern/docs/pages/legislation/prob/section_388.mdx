# PROB § 388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Delegation of Powers > Article: 2. Delegation by Consultant

**Citation:** PROB § 388

---

## Text

A consultant who delegates powers under this article is not liable for the acts or omissions of the delegate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
