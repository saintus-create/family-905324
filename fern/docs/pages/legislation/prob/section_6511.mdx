# PROB § 6511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. Setting Aside Exempt Property Other Than Family Dwelling

**Citation:** PROB § 6511

---

## Text

A petition for an order under Section 6510 may be filed by any interested person. Notice of the hearing on the petition shall be given as provided in Section 1220.
