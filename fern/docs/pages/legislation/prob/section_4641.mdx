# PROB § 4641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 4641

---

## Text

“Supervising health care provider” means the primary physician or, if there is no primary physician or the primary physician is not reasonably available, the health care provider who has undertaken primary responsibility for a patient’s health care.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
