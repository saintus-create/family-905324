# PROB § 17006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and Venue

**Citation:** PROB § 17006

---

## Text

There is no right to a jury trial in proceedings under this division concerning the internal affairs of trusts.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
