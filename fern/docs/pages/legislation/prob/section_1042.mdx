# PROB § 1042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Hearings and Orders

**Citation:** PROB § 1042

---

## Text

A hearing under this code shall be on notice unless the statute that provides for the hearing dispenses with notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
