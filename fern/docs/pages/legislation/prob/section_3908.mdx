# PROB § 3908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 9. CALIFORNIA UNIFORM TRANSFERS TO MINORS ACT

**Citation:** PROB § 3908

---

## Text

A written acknowledgment of delivery by a custodian constitutes a sufficient receipt and discharge for custodial property transferred to the custodian pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
