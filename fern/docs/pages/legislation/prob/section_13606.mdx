# PROB § 13606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Collection by Affidavit of Compensation Owed to Deceased Spouse

**Citation:** PROB § 13606

---

## Text

The procedure provided in this chapter is in addition to, and not in lieu of, any other method of collecting compensation owed to a decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
