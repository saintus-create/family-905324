# PROB § 17004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and Venue

**Citation:** PROB § 17004

---

## Text

The court may exercise jurisdiction in proceedings under this division on any basis permitted by Section 410.10 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
