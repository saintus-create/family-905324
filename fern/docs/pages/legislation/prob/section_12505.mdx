# PROB § 12505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 12505

---

## Text

“Nondomiciliary decedent” means a person who dies domiciled in a sister state or foreign nation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
