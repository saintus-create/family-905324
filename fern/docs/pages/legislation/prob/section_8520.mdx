# PROB § 8520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 7. Changes in Administration

**Citation:** PROB § 8520

---

## Text

A vacancy occurs in the office of a personal representative who resigns, dies, or is removed from office under Article 6 (commencing with Section 8500), or whose authority is otherwise terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
