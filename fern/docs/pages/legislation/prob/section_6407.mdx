# PROB § 6407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 2. INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Chapter: 1. Intestate Succession Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 6407

---

## Text

Relatives of the decedent conceived before the decedent’s death but born thereafter inherit as if they had been born in the lifetime of the decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
