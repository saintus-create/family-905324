# PROB § 6386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 11. Uniform International Wills Act

**Citation:** PROB § 6386

---

## Text

The international will is subject to the ordinary rules of revocation of wills.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
