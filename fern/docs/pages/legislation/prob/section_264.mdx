# PROB § 264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 8. DISCLAIMER OF TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER INTERESTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 264

---

## Text

“Disclaimant” means a beneficiary who executes a disclaimer on his or her own behalf or a person who executes a disclaimer on behalf of a beneficiary.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
