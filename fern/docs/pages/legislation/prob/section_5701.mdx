# PROB § 5701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 5. GIFTS IN VIEW OF IMPENDING DEATH

**Citation:** PROB § 5701

---

## Text

Except as provided in this part, a gift in view of impending death is subject to the general law relating to gifts of personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 18.)
