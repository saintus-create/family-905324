# PROB § 10511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 3. Administration Under Independent Administration Authority > Article: 2. Powers Exercisable Only After Giving Notice of Proposed Action

**Citation:** PROB § 10511

---

## Text

The personal representative who has full authority has the power to sell or exchange real property of the estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
