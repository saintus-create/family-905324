# PROB § 9820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 8. Actions and Proceedings by or Against Personal Representative

**Citation:** PROB § 9820

---

## Text

The personal representative may:

(a) Commence and maintain actions and proceedings for the benefit of the estate.

(b) Defend actions and proceedings against the decedent, the personal representative, or the estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
