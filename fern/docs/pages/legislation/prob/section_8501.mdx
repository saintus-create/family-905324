# PROB § 8501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 6. Removal From Office

**Citation:** PROB § 8501

---

## Text

On removal of a personal representative from office, the court shall revoke any letters issued to the personal representative, and the authority of the personal representative ceases.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
