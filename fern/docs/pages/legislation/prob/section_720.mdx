# PROB § 720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 15. DEPOSIT OF ESTATE PLANNING DOCUMENTS WITH ATTORNEY > Chapter: 3. Termination of Deposit > Article: 1. Termination by Depositor

**Citation:** PROB § 720

---

## Text

A depositor may terminate a deposit on demand, in which case the attorney shall deliver the document to the depositor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 519, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
