# PROB § 15308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions on Voluntary and Involuntary Transfers

**Citation:** PROB § 15308

---

## Text

Any order entered by a court under Section 15305, 15306, 15306.5, or 15307 is subject to modification upon petition of an interested person filed in the court where the order was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
