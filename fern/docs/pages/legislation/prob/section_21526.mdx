# PROB § 21526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 5. COMPLIANCE WITH INTERNAL REVENUE CODE > Chapter: 2. Marital Deduction Gifts

**Citation:** PROB § 21526

---

## Text

A fiduciary is not liable for a good faith decision to make any election, or not to make any election, referred to in Section 2056(b)(7) or Section 2523(f) of the Internal Revenue Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
