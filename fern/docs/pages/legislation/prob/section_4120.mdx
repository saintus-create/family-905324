# PROB § 4120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 2. POWERS OF ATTORNEY GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Creation and Effect of Powers of Attorney

**Citation:** PROB § 4120

---

## Text

A natural person having the capacity to contract may execute a power of attorney.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
