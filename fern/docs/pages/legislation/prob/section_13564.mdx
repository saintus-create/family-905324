# PROB § 13564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3.5. Liability for Decedent’s Property

**Citation:** PROB § 13564

---

## Text

The remedies available under Sections 13561 to 13563, inclusive, are in addition to any remedies available by reason of any fraud or intentional wrongdoing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 57.)
