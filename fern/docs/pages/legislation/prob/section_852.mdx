# PROB § 852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 19. CONVEYANCE OR TRANSFER OF PROPERTY CLAIMED TO BELONG TO DECEDENT OR OTHER PERSON

**Citation:** PROB § 852

---

## Text

An interested person may request time for filing a response to the petition for discovery proceedings, or for other preparation for the hearing, and the court shall grant a continuance for a reasonable time for any of these purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 49, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2002.)
