# PROB § 12000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 10. DISTRIBUTION OF ESTATE > Chapter: 8. Interest and Income Accruing During Administration

**Citation:** PROB § 12000

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter apply where the intention of the testator is not otherwise indicated by the will.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
