# PROB § 3300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 8. OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 3300

---

## Text

A parent who receives any money or property belonging to a minor under any provision of this part shall account to the minor for the money or other property when the minor reaches the age of majority.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
