# PROB § 690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 14. POWERS OF APPOINTMENT > Chapter: 7. Rule Against Perpetuities

**Citation:** PROB § 690

---

## Text

The statutory rule against perpetuities provided by Part 2 (commencing with Section 21200) of Division 11 applies to powers of appointment governed by this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 30, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
