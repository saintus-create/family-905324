# PROB § 9253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 6. Allowance and Rejection of Claims

**Citation:** PROB § 9253

---

## Text

A claim barred by the statute of limitations may not be allowed by the personal representative or approved by the court or judge.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
