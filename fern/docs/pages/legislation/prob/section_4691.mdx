# PROB § 4691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 2. UNIFORM HEALTH CARE DECISIONS ACT > Chapter: 1. Advance Health Care Directives > Article: 2. Powers of Attorney for Health Care

**Citation:** PROB § 4691

---

## Text

If directed by the principal in a power of attorney for health care, an attorney-in-fact shall, upon the death of the principal, inform those individuals whose names are provided by the principal to the attorney-in-fact for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 92, Sec. 4. (AB 1085) Effective January 1, 2016.)
