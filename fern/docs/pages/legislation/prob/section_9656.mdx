# PROB § 9656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Estate Management Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 9656

---

## Text

The personal representative may insure the property of the estate against damage or loss and may insure himself or herself against liability to third persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
