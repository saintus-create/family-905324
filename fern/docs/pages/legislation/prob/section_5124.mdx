# PROB § 5124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 2. MULTIPLE-PARTY ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5124

---

## Text

“Agent” means a person who has a present right, subject to request, to payment from an account as an attorney in fact under a power of attorney.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
