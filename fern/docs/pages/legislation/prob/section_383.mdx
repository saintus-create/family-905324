# PROB § 383

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 3. Delegation of Powers > Article: 1. Delegation by Fiduciary

**Citation:** PROB § 383

---

## Text

The original fiduciary is not liable for the acts or omissions of the delegate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
