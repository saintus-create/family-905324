# PROB § 286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 8. DISCLAIMER OF TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER INTERESTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 286

---

## Text

The right to disclaim exists regardless of any limitation imposed on the interest of a beneficiary in the nature of an expressed or implied spendthrift provision or similar restriction.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
