# PROB § 1802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 1. Establishment of Conservatorship > Article: 1. Persons for Whom Conservator May Be Appointed

**Citation:** PROB § 1802

---

## Text

Subject to Section 1800.3, a conservator of the person or estate, or both, may be appointed for a person who voluntarily requests the appointment and who, to the satisfaction of the court, establishes good cause for the appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
