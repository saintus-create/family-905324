# PROB § 17210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 3. Proceedings Concerning Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 17210

---

## Text

In a case involving a charitable trust subject to the jurisdiction of the Attorney General, the Attorney General may petition under this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
