# PROB § 12501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 12501

---

## Text

“Ancillary administration” means proceedings in this state for administration of the estate of a nondomiciliary decedent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
