# PROB § 6601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Small Estate Set-Aside

**Citation:** PROB § 6601

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “minor child” means a child of the decedent who was under the age of 18 at the time of the decedent’s death and who survived the decedent.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
