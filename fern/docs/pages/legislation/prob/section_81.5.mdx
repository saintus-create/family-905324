# PROB § 81.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 81.5

---

## Text

“Transferee” means the beneficiary, donee, or other recipient of an interest transferred by an instrument.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 806, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
