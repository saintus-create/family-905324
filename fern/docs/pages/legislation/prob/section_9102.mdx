# PROB § 9102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 3. Time for Filing Claims

**Citation:** PROB § 9102

---

## Text

A claim that is filed before expiration of the time for filing the claim is timely even if acted on by the personal representative or by the court after expiration of the time for filing claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 159, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2008.)
