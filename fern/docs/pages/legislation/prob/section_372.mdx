# PROB § 372

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 2. Appointment of Substitute Fiduciary

**Citation:** PROB § 372

---

## Text

Except as otherwise ordered by the court, a substitute fiduciary shall give a bond in the manner and to the extent provided by law for the original fiduciary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
