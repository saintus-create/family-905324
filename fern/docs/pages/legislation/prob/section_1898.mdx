# PROB § 1898

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 4. Legal Capacity of Conservatee > Article: 2. Capacity to Give Informed Consent for Medical Treatment

**Citation:** PROB § 1898

---

## Text

An order of the court under Section 1880 may be modified or revoked upon a petition made, noticed, and heard by the court in the manner provided in this article.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
