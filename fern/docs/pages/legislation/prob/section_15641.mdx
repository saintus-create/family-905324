# PROB § 15641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 3. TRUSTEES AND BENEFICIARIES > Chapter: 1. Trustees > Article: 3. Resignation and Removal of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 15641

---

## Text

The liability for acts or omissions of a resigning trustee or of the sureties on the trustee’s bond, if any, is not released or affected in any manner by the trustee’s resignation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
