# PROB § 12204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 11. CLOSING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Time for Closing Estate

**Citation:** PROB § 12204

---

## Text

Failure of the personal representative to comply with an order made under this chapter is grounds for removal from office.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
