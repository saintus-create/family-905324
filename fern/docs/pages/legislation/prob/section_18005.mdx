# PROB § 18005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 6. RIGHTS OF THIRD PERSONS > Chapter: 1. Liability of Trustee to Third Persons

**Citation:** PROB § 18005

---

## Text

The question of liability as between the trust estate and the trustee personally may be determined in a proceeding under Section 17200.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
