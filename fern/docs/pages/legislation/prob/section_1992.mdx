# PROB § 1992

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 8. Interstate Jurisdiction, Transfer, and Recognition: California Conservatorship Jurisdiction Act > Article: 2. Jurisdiction

**Citation:** PROB § 1992

---

## Text

For a conservatorship proceeding governed by this article, this article provides the exclusive basis for determining whether the courts of this state, as opposed to the courts of another state, have jurisdiction to appoint a conservator of the person, a conservator of the estate, or a conservator of the person and estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 553, Sec. 20. (SB 940) Effective January 1, 2015. Operative January 1, 2016, by Stats. 2014, Ch. 553, Sec. 29.)
