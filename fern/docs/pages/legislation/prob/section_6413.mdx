# PROB § 6413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 2. INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Chapter: 1. Intestate Succession Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 6413

---

## Text

A person who is related to the decedent through two lines of relationship is entitled to only a single share based on the relationship which would entitle the person to the larger share.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
