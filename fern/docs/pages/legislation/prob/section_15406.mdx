# PROB § 15406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 3. Modification and Termination of Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 15406

---

## Text

In determining the class of beneficiaries whose consent is necessary to modify or terminate a trust pursuant to Section 15403 or 15404, the presumption of fertility is rebuttable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
