# PROB § 6301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 7. Uniform Testamentary Additions to Trusts Act

**Citation:** PROB § 6301

---

## Text

This chapter does not invalidate any devise made by a will executed prior to September 17, 1965.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
