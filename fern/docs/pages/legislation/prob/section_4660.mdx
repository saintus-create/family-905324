# PROB § 4660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 4660

---

## Text

A copy of a written advance health care directive, revocation of an advance directive, or designation or disqualification of a surrogate has the same effect as the original.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
