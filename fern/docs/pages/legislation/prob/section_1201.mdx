# PROB § 1201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Notice Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 1201

---

## Text

If a person is required to give notice, the person required to give the notice need not give the notice to himself or herself or to any other person who joins in the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
