# PROB § 9051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 2. Notice to Creditors

**Citation:** PROB § 9051

---

## Text

The notice shall be given within the later of:

(a) Four months after the date letters are first issued.

(b) Thirty days after the personal representative first has knowledge of the creditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 862, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1997.)
