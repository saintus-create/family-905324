# PROB § 16249

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 2. Specific Powers of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 16249

---

## Text

The trustee has the power to prosecute or defend actions, claims, or proceedings for the protection of trust property and of the trustee in the performance of the trustee’s duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 49, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2002.)
