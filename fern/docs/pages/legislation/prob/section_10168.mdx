# PROB § 10168

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 18. Sales > Article: 3. Compensation of Agent, Broker, or Auctioneer

**Citation:** PROB § 10168

---

## Text

This article does not supersede any agreement cooperating agents or brokers may have among themselves to divide the compensation payable under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 26. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 48 of Ch. 710.)
