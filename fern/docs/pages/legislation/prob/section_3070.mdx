# PROB § 3070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 6. MANAGEMENT OR DISPOSITION OF COMMUNITY PROPERTY WHERE SPOUSE LACKS LEGAL CAPACITY > Chapter: 2. Management, Control, and Disposition > Article: 2. Substitute for Joinder or Consent Requirements

**Citation:** PROB § 3070

---

## Text

If the requirements of this article are satisfied with respect to a transaction described in Section 3071, the transaction is deemed to satisfy the joinder or consent requirements of the statute referred to in that section.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
