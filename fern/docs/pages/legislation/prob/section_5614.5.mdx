# PROB § 5614.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5614.5

---

## Text

“Stock cooperative” has the same meaning as in Section 4190 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 62, Sec. 3. (AB 288) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
