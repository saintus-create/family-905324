# PROB § 1204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Notice Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 1204

---

## Text

A person, including a guardian ad litem, guardian, conservator, trustee, or other fiduciary, may waive notice by a writing signed by the person or the person’s attorney and filed in the proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
