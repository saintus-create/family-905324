# PROB § 13505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 13505

---

## Text

This part applies whether the deceased spouse died before, on, or after July 1, 1987.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
