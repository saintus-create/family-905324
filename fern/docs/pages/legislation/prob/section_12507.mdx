# PROB § 12507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 12507

---

## Text

“Sister state personal representative” means a personal representative appointed in a sister state.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
