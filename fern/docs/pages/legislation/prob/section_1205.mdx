# PROB § 1205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 1. General Notice Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 1205

---

## Text

If a hearing is continued or postponed, no further notice of the continued or postponed hearing is required unless ordered by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
