# PROB § 4619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 4619

---

## Text

“Health care institution” means an institution, facility, or agency licensed, certified, or otherwise authorized or permitted by law to provide health care in the ordinary course of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
