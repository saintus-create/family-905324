# PROB § 8487

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 5. Bond

**Citation:** PROB § 8487

---

## Text

The provisions of the Bond and Undertaking Law (Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 995.010) of Title 14 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure) apply to a bond given under this division, except to the extent this division is inconsistent.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
