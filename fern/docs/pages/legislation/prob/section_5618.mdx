# PROB § 5618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5618

---

## Text

“Unsecured debts” includes, but is not limited to, a transferor’s funeral expenses, expenses of a transferor’s last illness, and wage claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 215, Sec. 8. (SB 315) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
