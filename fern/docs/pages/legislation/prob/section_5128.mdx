# PROB § 5128

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 2. MULTIPLE-PARTY ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5128

---

## Text

“Financial institution” includes:

(a) A financial institution as defined in Section 40.

(b) An industrial loan company as defined in Section 18003 of the Financial Code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
