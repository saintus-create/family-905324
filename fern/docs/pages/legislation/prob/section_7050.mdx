# PROB § 7050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Jurisdiction and Courts > Article: 1. Jurisdiction and Venue

**Citation:** PROB § 7050

---

## Text

The superior court has jurisdiction of proceedings under this code concerning the administration of the decedent’s estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 806, Sec. 22. Effective January 1, 1995.)
