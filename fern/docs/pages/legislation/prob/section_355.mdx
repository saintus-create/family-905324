# PROB § 355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 11. FIDUCIARIES' WARTIME SUBSTITUTION LAW > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 355

---

## Text

“Original fiduciary” means a fiduciary who is replaced by a substitute fiduciary or who makes a delegation of power under this part.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
