# PROB § 6111.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 2. Execution of Wills

**Citation:** PROB § 6111.5

---

## Text

Extrinsic evidence is admissible to determine whether a document constitutes a will pursuant to Section 6110 or 6111, or to determine the meaning of a will or a portion of a will if the meaning is unclear.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 14. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 48 of Ch. 710.)
