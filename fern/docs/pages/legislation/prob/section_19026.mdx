# PROB § 19026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 2. Petition for Approval and Settlement of Claims Against Deceased Settlor

**Citation:** PROB § 19026

---

## Text

The court may dismiss a petition if it appears that the proceeding is not reasonably necessary for the protection of the interests of the trustee or any beneficiary of the trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
