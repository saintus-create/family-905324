# PROB § 6201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 6. California Statutory Will > Article: 1. Definitions and Rules of Construction

**Citation:** PROB § 6201

---

## Text

“Testator” means a person choosing to adopt a California statutory will.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 20.)
