# PROB § 104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 1. EFFECT OF DEATH OF MARRIED PERSON ON COMMUNITY AND QUASI-COMMUNITY PROPERTY

**Citation:** PROB § 104

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 100, community property held in a revocable trust described in Section 761 of the Family Code is governed by the provisions, if any, in the trust for disposition in the event of death.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 163, Sec. 119. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative January 1, 1994, by Sec. 161 of Ch. 163.)
