# PROB § 19524

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 9. Uniform Trust Decanting Act

**Citation:** PROB § 19524

---

## Text

A reference in this code to a trust instrument or terms of the trust includes a second trust instrument and the terms of the second trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 407, Sec. 1. (SB 909) Effective January 1, 2019.)
