# PROB § 1262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 2. NOTICES AND CITATIONS > Chapter: 7. Proof of Giving Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 1262

---

## Text

Proof of publication may be made by the affidavit of the publisher or printer, or the foreman or principal clerk of the publisher or printer, showing the time and place of publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
