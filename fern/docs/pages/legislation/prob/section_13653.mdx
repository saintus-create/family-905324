# PROB § 13653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 5. Determination or Confirmation of Property Passing or Belonging to Surviving Spouse

**Citation:** PROB § 13653

---

## Text

If proceedings for the administration of the estate of the deceased spouse are not pending, a petition under this chapter may, but need not, be filed with a petition for probate of the will of the deceased spouse or for administration of the estate of the deceased spouse.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
