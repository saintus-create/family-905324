# PROB § 4786

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 4. REQUEST REGARDING RESUSCITATIVE MEASURES

**Citation:** PROB § 4786

---

## Text

This part does not repeal or narrow laws relating to health care decisionmaking.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
