# PROB § 5632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 2. Execution and Revocation > Article: 2. Revocation

**Citation:** PROB § 5632

---

## Text

(a) An instrument revoking a revocable transfer on death deed shall be executed and recorded in the same manner as execution and recordation of a revocable transfer on death deed.

(b) Joinder, consent, or agreement of, or notice to, the beneficiary is not required for revocation of a revocable transfer on death deed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 215, Sec. 12. (SB 315) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
