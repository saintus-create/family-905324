# PROB § 11624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 10. DISTRIBUTION OF ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Order for Distribution > Article: 2. Preliminary Distribution

**Citation:** PROB § 11624

---

## Text

The costs of a proceeding under this article shall be paid by the distributee or the estate in proportions determined by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
