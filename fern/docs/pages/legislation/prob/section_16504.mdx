# PROB § 16504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 5. Notice of Proposed Action by Trustee

**Citation:** PROB § 16504

---

## Text

This chapter does not require a trustee to use these procedures prior to taking any action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 54, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
