# PROB § 6603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 6. Small Estate Set-Aside

**Citation:** PROB § 6603

---

## Text

The petition shall be filed in the superior court of a county in which the estate of the decedent may be administered.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
