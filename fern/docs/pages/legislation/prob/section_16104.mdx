# PROB § 16104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 5. Duties of Trustees of Private Foundations, Charitable Trusts, and Split-Interest Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 16104

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 16101, 16102, and 16103 shall be deemed to be contained in the instrument creating every trust to which this article applies. Any provision of the instrument inconsistent with or contrary to this article is without effect.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
