# PROB § 8466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 4. Administrators

**Citation:** PROB § 8466

---

## Text

If a person whose only priority is that of a creditor claims appointment as administrator, the court in its discretion may deny the appointment and appoint another person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
