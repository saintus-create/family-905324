# PROB § 1892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 4. Legal Capacity of Conservatee > Article: 2. Capacity to Give Informed Consent for Medical Treatment

**Citation:** PROB § 1892

## Text

Notice of the hearing on the petition shall be given for the period and in the manner provided in Chapter 3 (commencing with Section 1460) of Part 1.
