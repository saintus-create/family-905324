# PROB § 21121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 1. RULES FOR INTERPRETATION OF INSTRUMENTS > Chapter: 2. Ascertaining Meaning of Language Used in the Instrument

**Citation:** PROB § 21121

---

## Text

All parts of an instrument are to be construed in relation to each other and so as, if possible, to form a consistent whole. If the meaning of any part of an instrument is ambiguous or doubtful, it may be explained by any reference to or recital of that part in another part of the instrument.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 138, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 2003.)
