# PROB § 12503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 12503

---

## Text

“Foreign nation personal representative” means a personal representative appointed in a jurisdiction other than a state of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
