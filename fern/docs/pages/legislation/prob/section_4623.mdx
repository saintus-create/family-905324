# PROB § 4623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 4623

---

## Text

“Individual health care instruction” or “individual instruction” means a patient’s written or oral direction concerning a health care decision for the patient.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
