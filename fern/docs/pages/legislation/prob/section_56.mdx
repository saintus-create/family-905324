# PROB § 56

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 56

---

## Text

“Person” means an individual, corporation, government or governmental subdivision or agency, business trust, estate, trust, partnership, limited liability company, association, or other entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 202. Effective January 1, 1995.)
