# PROB § 6521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 3. Setting Aside Probate Homestead

**Citation:** PROB § 6521

---

## Text

The probate homestead shall be set apart for the use of one or more of the following persons:

(a) The surviving spouse.

(b) The minor children of the decedent.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
