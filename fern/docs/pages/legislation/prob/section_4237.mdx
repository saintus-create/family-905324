# PROB § 4237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 2. POWERS OF ATTORNEY GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys-in-Fact > Article: 2. Duties of Attorneys-in-Fact

**Citation:** PROB § 4237

---

## Text

An attorney-in-fact with special skills has a duty to apply the full extent of those skills.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
