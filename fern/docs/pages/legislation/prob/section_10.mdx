# PROB § 10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 10

---

## Text

The singular number includes the plural, and the plural, the singular.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
