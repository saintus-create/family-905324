# PROB § 1046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Hearings and Orders

**Citation:** PROB § 1046

---

## Text

The court shall hear and determine any matter at issue and any response or objection presented, consider evidence presented, and make appropriate orders.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
