# PROB § 8485

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 5. Bond

**Citation:** PROB § 8485

---

## Text

A personal representative who petitions for substitution or release of a surety shall file with the petition an account in the form provided in Section 10900. The court shall not order a substitution or release unless the account is approved.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
