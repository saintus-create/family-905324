# PROB § 8570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 9. Nonresident Personal Representative

**Citation:** PROB § 8570

---

## Text

As used in this article, “nonresident personal representative” means a nonresident of this state appointed as personal representative, or a resident of this state appointed as personal representative who later removes from and resides without this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 22.)
