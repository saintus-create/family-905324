# PROB § 1453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 1453

---

## Text

A motion for a new trial may be made only in cases in which, under the provisions of this division, a right to jury trial is expressly granted, whether or not the case was tried by a jury.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
