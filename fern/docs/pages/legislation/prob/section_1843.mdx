# PROB § 1843

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 3. CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 1. Establishment of Conservatorship > Article: 4. Special Provisions Applicable Where Proposed Conservatee Is an Absentee

**Citation:** PROB § 1843

---

## Text

(a) No citation is required under Section 1823 to the proposed conservatee if the proposed conservatee is an absentee.

(b) No notice is required under Section 2002 to the proposed conservatee if the proposed conservatee is an absentee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 553, Sec. 11. (SB 940) Effective January 1, 2015. Operative January 1, 2016, by Stats. 2014, Ch. 553, Sec. 29.)
