# PROB § 16221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 2. Specific Powers of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 16221

---

## Text

The trustee has the power to accept additions to the property of the trust from a settlor or any other person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
