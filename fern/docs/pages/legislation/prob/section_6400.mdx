# PROB § 6400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 2. INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Chapter: 1. Intestate Succession Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 6400

---

## Text

Any part of the estate of a decedent not effectively disposed of by will passes to the decedent’s heirs as prescribed in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
