# PROB § 6501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 3. FAMILY PROTECTION > Chapter: 1. Temporary Possession of Family Dwelling and Exempt Property

**Citation:** PROB § 6501

---

## Text

A petition for an order under Section 6500 may be filed by any interested person. Notice of the hearing on the petition shall be given as provided in Section 1220.
