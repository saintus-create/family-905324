# PROB § 9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 9

---

## Text

The present tense includes the past and future tenses, and the future, the present.
