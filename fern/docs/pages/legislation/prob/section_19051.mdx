# PROB § 19051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 4. Actual Notice to Creditors

**Citation:** PROB § 19051

---

## Text

The notice shall be given before expiration of the later of the following times:

(a) Four months after the first publication of notice under Section 19040.

(b) Thirty days after the trustee first has knowledge of the creditor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 159, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2008.)
