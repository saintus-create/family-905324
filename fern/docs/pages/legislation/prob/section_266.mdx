# PROB § 266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 8. DISCLAIMER OF TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER INTERESTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 266

---

## Text

“Employee benefit plan” includes, but is not limited to, any pension, retirement, death benefit, stock bonus, or profit-sharing plan, system, or trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
