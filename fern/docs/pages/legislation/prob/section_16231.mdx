# PROB § 16231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 2. Specific Powers of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 16231

---

## Text

The trustee has the power to enter into a lease for any purpose as lessor or lessee with or without the option to purchase or renew and for a term within or extending beyond the term of the trust.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
