# PROB § 5202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 2. MULTIPLE-PARTY ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 5202

---

## Text

Nothing in this part affects the law relating to transfers in fraud of creditors.
