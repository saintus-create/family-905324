# PROB § 2620.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 4. PROVISIONS COMMON TO GUARDIANSHIP AND CONSERVATORSHIP > Chapter: 7. Inventory and Accounts > Article: 3. Accounts

**Citation:** PROB § 2620.1

---

## Text

The Judicial Council shall, by January 1, 2009, develop guidelines to assist investigators and examiners in reviewing accountings and detecting fraud.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 553, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2008.)
