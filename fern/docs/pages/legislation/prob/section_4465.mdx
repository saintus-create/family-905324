# PROB § 4465

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 3. UNIFORM STATUTORY FORM POWER OF ATTORNEY > Chapter: 2. Construction of Powers

**Citation:** PROB § 4465

---

## Text

A statutory form power of attorney under this part does not empower the agent to take any of the actions specified in Section 4264 unless the statutory form power of attorney expressly grants that authority to the attorney-in-fact.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 113, Sec. 11. (AB 1082) Effective January 1, 2012.)
