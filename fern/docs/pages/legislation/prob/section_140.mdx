# PROB § 140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 3. CONTRACTUAL ARRANGEMENTS RELATING TO RIGHTS AT DEATH > Chapter: 1. Surviving Spouse’s Waiver of Rights

**Citation:** PROB § 140

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “waiver” means a waiver by the surviving spouse of any of the rights listed in subdivision (a) of Section 141, whether signed before or during marriage.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
