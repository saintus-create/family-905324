# PROB § 16060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 1. Duties of Trustees > Article: 3. Trustee’s Duty to Report Information and Account to Beneficiaries

**Citation:** PROB § 16060

---

## Text

The trustee has a duty to keep the beneficiaries of the trust reasonably informed of the trust and its administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
