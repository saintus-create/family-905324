# PROB § 17302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 4. Testamentary Trusts Subject to Continuing Court Jurisdiction > Article: 1. Administration of Testamentary Trusts Subject to Continuing Court Jurisdiction

**Citation:** PROB § 17302

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, proceedings relating to trusts under continuing court jurisdiction are governed by this part.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
