# PROB § 15208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 1. Creation and Validity of Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 15208

---

## Text

Consideration is not required to create a trust, but a promise to create a trust in the future is enforceable only if the requirements for an enforceable contract are satisfied.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
