# PROB § 12542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 2. Ancillary Administration > Article: 4. Distribution of Property to Sister State Personal Representative

**Citation:** PROB § 12542

---

## Text

If the nondomiciliary decedent’s estate in the sister state where the decedent was domiciled is insolvent, distribution may be made only to the sister state personal representative and not to the beneficiaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
