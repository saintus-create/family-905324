# PROB § 9003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 4. CREDITOR CLAIMS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 9003

---

## Text

A claim that is established under this part shall be included among the debts to be paid in the course of administration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
