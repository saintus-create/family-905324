# PROB § 4403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 3. UNIFORM STATUTORY FORM POWER OF ATTORNEY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 4403

---

## Text

If the line in front of (N) of the statutory form under Section 4401 is initialed, an initial on the line in front of any other power does not limit the powers granted by line (N).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
