# PROB § 9657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 2. Estate Management Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 9657

---

## Text

The personal representative shall not make profit by the increase, nor suffer loss by the decrease or destruction without his or her fault, of any part of the estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
