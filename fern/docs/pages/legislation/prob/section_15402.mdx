# PROB § 15402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 3. Modification and Termination of Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 15402

---

## Text

Unless the trust instrument provides otherwise, if a trust is revocable by the settlor, the settlor may modify the trust by the procedure for revocation.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
