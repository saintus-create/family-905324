# PROB § 17105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 2. Notice

**Citation:** PROB § 17105

---

## Text

A petitioner or other person required to give notice may cause notice to be given to any person interested in the trust without the need for a court order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79, Sec. 14.)
