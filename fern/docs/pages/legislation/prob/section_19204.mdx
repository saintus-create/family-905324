# PROB § 19204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 7. Claims by Public Entities

**Citation:** PROB § 19204

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to affect the order of priority of debts provided for under other provisions of law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
