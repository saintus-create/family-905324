# PROB § 16240

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 2. Powers of Trustees > Article: 2. Specific Powers of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 16240

---

## Text

The trustee has the power to insure the property of the trust against damage or loss and to insure the trustee against liability with respect to third persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
