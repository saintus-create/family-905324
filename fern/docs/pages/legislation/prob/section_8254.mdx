# PROB § 8254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Probate of Will > Article: 3. Contest of Will

**Citation:** PROB § 8254

---

## Text

The court may make appropriate orders, including orders sustaining or denying objections, and shall render judgment either admitting the will to probate or rejecting it, in whole or in part, and appointing a personal representative.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
