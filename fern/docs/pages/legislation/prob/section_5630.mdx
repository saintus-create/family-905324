# PROB § 5630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 4. REVOCABLE TRANSFER ON DEATH DEED > Chapter: 2. Execution and Revocation > Article: 2. Revocation

**Citation:** PROB § 5630

---

## Text

A transferor who has the capacity to contract may revoke a revocable transfer on death deed at any time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 293, Sec. 17. (AB 139) Effective January 1, 2016. Repealed as of January 1, 2032, pursuant to Section 5600.)
