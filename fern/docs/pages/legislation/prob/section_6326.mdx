# PROB § 6326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 8. Nonprobate Transfer to Trustee Named in Decedent’s Will

**Citation:** PROB § 6326

---

## Text

As to matters not specifically provided in Section 6325, the provisions of Division 9 (commencing with Section 15000) apply to the trust.
