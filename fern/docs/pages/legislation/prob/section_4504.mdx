# PROB § 4504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 4. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 4504

---

## Text

There is no right to a jury trial in proceedings under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 37. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
