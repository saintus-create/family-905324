# PROB § 856.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 19. CONVEYANCE OR TRANSFER OF PROPERTY CLAIMED TO BELONG TO DECEDENT OR OTHER PERSON

**Citation:** PROB § 856.5

---

## Text

The court may not grant a petition under this chapter if the court determines that the matter should be determined by a civil action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 417, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
