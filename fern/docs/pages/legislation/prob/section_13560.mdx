# PROB § 13560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 2. PASSAGE OF PROPERTY TO SURVIVING SPOUSE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3.5. Liability for Decedent’s Property

**Citation:** PROB § 13560

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “decedent’s property” means the one-half of the community property that belongs to the decedent under Section 100 and the one-half of the quasi-community property that belongs to the decedent under Section 101.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1055, Sec. 57.)
