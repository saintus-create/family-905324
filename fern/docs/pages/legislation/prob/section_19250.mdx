# PROB § 19250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 8. Allowance and Rejection of Claims

**Citation:** PROB § 19250

---

## Text

When a claim is filed, the trustee shall allow or reject the claim in whole or in part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
