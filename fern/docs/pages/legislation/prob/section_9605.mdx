# PROB § 9605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Duties and Liabilities of Personal Representative

**Citation:** PROB § 9605

---

## Text

Appointment of a person as personal representative does not discharge any claim the decedent has against the person.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
