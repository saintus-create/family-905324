# PROB § 4696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 2. UNIFORM HEALTH CARE DECISIONS ACT > Chapter: 1. Advance Health Care Directives > Article: 3. Revocation of Advance Directives

**Citation:** PROB § 4696

---

## Text

A health care provider, agent, conservator, or surrogate who is informed of a revocation of an advance health care directive shall promptly communicate the fact of the revocation to the supervising health care provider and to any health care institution where the patient is receiving care.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
