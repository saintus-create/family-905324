# PROB § 12572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 13. NONDOMICILIARY DECEDENTS > Chapter: 3. Collection of Personal Property of Small Estate by Sister State Personal Representative Without Ancillary Administration

**Citation:** PROB § 12572

---

## Text

The sister state personal representative may bring an action against a holder of the decedent’s property, and may be awarded attorney’s fees, as provided in subdivision (b) of Section 13105.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
