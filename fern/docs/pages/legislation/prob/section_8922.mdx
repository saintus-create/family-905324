# PROB § 8922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 3. INVENTORY AND APPRAISAL > Chapter: 3. Appraisal > Article: 2. Designation and Removal of Probate Referee

**Citation:** PROB § 8922

---

## Text

The court has authority and discretion not to designate a particular person as probate referee even though appointed by the Controller to act as a probate referee for the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
