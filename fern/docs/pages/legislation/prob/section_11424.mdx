# PROB § 11424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 9. PAYMENT OF DEBTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 11424

---

## Text

The personal representative shall pay a debt to the extent of the order for payment of the debt, and is liable personally and on the bond, if any, for failure to make the payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
