# PROB § 15603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 3. TRUSTEES AND BENEFICIARIES > Chapter: 1. Trustees > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 15603

---

## Text

On application by the trustee, the court clerk shall issue a certificate that the trustee is a duly appointed and acting trustee under the trust if the court file shows the incumbency of the trustee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
