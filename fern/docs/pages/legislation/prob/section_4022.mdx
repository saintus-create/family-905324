# PROB § 4022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.5. POWERS OF ATTORNEY > Part: 1. DEFINITIONS AND GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 4022

---

## Text

“Power of attorney” means a written instrument, however denominated, that is executed by a natural person having the capacity to contract and that grants authority to an attorney-in-fact. A power of attorney may be durable or nondurable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 307, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 1995.)
