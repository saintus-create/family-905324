# PROB § 6322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 1. WILLS > Chapter: 8. Nonprobate Transfer to Trustee Named in Decedent’s Will

**Citation:** PROB § 6322

---

## Text

The designation is ineffective unless the designator’s will contains provisions creating the trust or makes a disposition valid under Section 6300.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
