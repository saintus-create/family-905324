# PROB § 16630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 4. TRUST ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 6. California Uniform Directed Trust Act

**Citation:** PROB § 16630

---

## Text

In applying and construing this chapter, consideration shall be given to the need to promote uniformity of the law with respect to its subject matter among states that enact it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 721, Sec. 3. (SB 801) Effective January 1, 2024.)
