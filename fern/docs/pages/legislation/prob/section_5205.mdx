# PROB § 5205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 2. MULTIPLE-PARTY ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 5205

---

## Text

This part applies to accounts in existence on July 1, 1990, and accounts thereafter established.
