# PROB § 15683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 3. TRUSTEES AND BENEFICIARIES > Chapter: 1. Trustees > Article: 5. Compensation and Indemnification of Trustees

**Citation:** PROB § 15683

---

## Text

Unless the trust instrument otherwise provides or the trustees otherwise agree, if the trust has two or more trustees, the compensation shall be apportioned among the cotrustees according to the services rendered by them.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
