# PROB § 15210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 1. Creation and Validity of Trusts

**Citation:** PROB § 15210

---

## Text

A trust created pursuant to this chapter which relates to real property may be recorded in the office of the county recorder in the county where all or a portion of the real property is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
