# PROB § 88

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 88

---

## Text

“Will” includes codicil and any testamentary instrument which merely appoints an executor or revokes or revises another will.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
