# PROB § 600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 14. POWERS OF APPOINTMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 600

---

## Text

Except to the extent that the common law rules governing powers of appointment are modified by statute, the common law as to powers of appointment is the law of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 30, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
