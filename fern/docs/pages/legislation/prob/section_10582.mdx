# PROB § 10582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 4. Notice of Proposed Action Procedure

**Citation:** PROB § 10582

---

## Text

Notice of proposed action need not be given to any person who consents in writing to the proposed action. The consent may be executed at any time before or after the proposed action is taken.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
