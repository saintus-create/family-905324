# PROB § 81

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 81

---

## Text

“Transferor” means the testator, settlor, grantor, owner, or other person who executes an instrument.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
