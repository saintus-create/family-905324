# PROB § 4741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4.7. HEALTH CARE DECISIONS > Part: 2. UNIFORM HEALTH CARE DECISIONS ACT > Chapter: 5. Immunities and Liabilities

**Citation:** PROB § 4741

---

## Text

A person acting as agent or surrogate under this part is not subject to civil or criminal liability or to discipline for unprofessional conduct for health care decisions made in good faith.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 658, Sec. 39. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2000, by Sec. 43 of Ch. 658.)
