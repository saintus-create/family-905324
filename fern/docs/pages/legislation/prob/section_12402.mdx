# PROB § 12402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 12. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF MISSING PERSONS PRESUMED DEAD

**Citation:** PROB § 12402

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this part, the estate of a missing person may be administered in the manner provided generally for the administration of estates of deceased persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
