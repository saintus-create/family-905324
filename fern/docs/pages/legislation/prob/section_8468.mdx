# PROB § 8468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 2. OPENING ESTATE ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 4. Appointment of Personal Representative > Article: 4. Administrators

**Citation:** PROB § 8468

---

## Text

If persons having priority fail to claim appointment as administrator, the court may appoint any person who claims appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
