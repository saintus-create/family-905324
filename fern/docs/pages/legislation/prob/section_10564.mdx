# PROB § 10564

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 3. Administration Under Independent Administration Authority > Article: 4. Powers Exercisable Without Giving Notice of Proposed Action

**Citation:** PROB § 10564

---

## Text

The personal representative has the power to give a partial satisfaction of a mortgage or to cause a partial reconveyance to be executed by a trustee under a deed of trust held by the estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
