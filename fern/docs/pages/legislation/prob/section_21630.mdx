# PROB § 21630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. CONSTRUCTION OF WILLS, TRUSTS, AND OTHER INSTRUMENTS > Part: 6. FAMILY PROTECTION: OMITTED SPOUSES AND CHILDREN > Chapter: 4. Applicability

**Citation:** PROB § 21630

---

## Text

This part does not apply if the decedent died before January 1, 1998. The law applicable prior to January 1, 1998, applies if the decedent died before January 1, 1998.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 724, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1998.)
