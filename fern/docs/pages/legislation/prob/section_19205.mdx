# PROB § 19205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 8. PAYMENT OF CLAIMS, DEBTS, AND EXPENSES FROM REVOCABLE TRUST OF DECEASED SETTLOR > Chapter: 7. Claims by Public Entities

**Citation:** PROB § 19205

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to liability for the restitution of amounts illegally acquired through the means of a fraudulent, false, or incorrect representation, or a forged or unauthorized endorsement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 992, Sec. 3.)
