# PROB § 10530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 3. Administration Under Independent Administration Authority > Article: 3. Powers the Exercise of Which Requires Giving of Notice of Proposed Action Under Some Circumstances

**Citation:** PROB § 10530

---

## Text

Except to the extent that this article otherwise provides, the personal representative may exercise the powers described in this article without giving notice of proposed action under Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 10580).

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
