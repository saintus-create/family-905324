# PROB § 54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. PRELIMINARY PROVISIONS AND DEFINITIONS > Part: 2. DEFINITIONS

**Citation:** PROB § 54

---

## Text

“Parent” means any individual entitled to take as a parent under this code by intestate succession from the child whose relationship is involved.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
