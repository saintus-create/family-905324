# PROB § 15309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 2. CREATION, VALIDITY, MODIFICATION, AND TERMINATION OF TRUSTS > Chapter: 2. Restrictions on Voluntary and Involuntary Transfers

**Citation:** PROB § 15309

---

## Text

A disclaimer or renunciation by a beneficiary of all or part of his or her interest under a trust shall not be considered a transfer under Section 15300 or 15301.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
