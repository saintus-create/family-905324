# PROB § 283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 8. DISCLAIMER OF TESTAMENTARY AND OTHER INTERESTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 283

---

## Text

A disclaimer is not a voidable transfer by the beneficiary under the Uniform Voidable Transactions Act (Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 3439) of Title 2 of Part 2 of Division 4 of the Civil Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 44, Sec. 26. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2016.)
