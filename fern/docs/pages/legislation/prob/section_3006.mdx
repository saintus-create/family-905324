# PROB § 3006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. GUARDIANSHIP, CONSERVATORSHIP, AND OTHER PROTECTIVE PROCEEDINGS > Part: 6. MANAGEMENT OR DISPOSITION OF COMMUNITY PROPERTY WHERE SPOUSE LACKS LEGAL CAPACITY > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 3006

---

## Text

“Conservatorship estate” includes the guardianship estate of a married minor.
