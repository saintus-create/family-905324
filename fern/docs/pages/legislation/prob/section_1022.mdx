# PROB § 1022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. GENERAL PROVISIONS OF A PROCEDURAL NATURE > Part: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 2. Petitions and Other Papers

**Citation:** PROB § 1022

---

## Text

An affidavit or verified petition shall be received as evidence when offered in an uncontested proceeding under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
