# PROB § 13114.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. DISPOSITION OF ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Part: 1. COLLECTION OR TRANSFER OF SMALL ESTATE WITHOUT ADMINISTRATION > Chapter: 3. Affidavit Procedure for Collection or Transfer of Personal Property

**Citation:** PROB § 13114.5

---

## Text

If transferred property is returned to the estate under Sections 13110.5 or 13111, the value of that property shall be included in the total value of the estate, for all purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 29, Sec. 12. (AB 1716) Effective January 1, 2023.)
