# PROB § 6404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. WILLS AND INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Part: 2. INTESTATE SUCCESSION > Chapter: 1. Intestate Succession Generally

**Citation:** PROB § 6404

---

## Text

Part 4 (commencing with Section 6800) (escheat) applies if there is no taker of the intestate estate under the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
