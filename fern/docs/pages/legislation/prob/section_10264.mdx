# PROB § 10264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 18. Sales > Article: 5. Sale of Personal Property

**Citation:** PROB § 10264

---

## Text

No omission, error, or irregularity in the proceedings under this article shall impair or invalidate the proceedings or the sale pursuant to an order made under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
