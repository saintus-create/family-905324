# PROB § 825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Part: 18. RIGHT TO TRIAL

**Citation:** PROB § 825

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided in this code, there is no right to a jury trial in proceedings under this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 175, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
