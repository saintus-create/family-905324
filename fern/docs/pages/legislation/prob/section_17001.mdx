# PROB § 17001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. TRUST LAW > Part: 5. JUDICIAL PROCEEDINGS CONCERNING TRUSTS > Chapter: 1. Jurisdiction and Venue

**Citation:** PROB § 17001

---

## Text

In proceedings commenced pursuant to this division, the court is a court of general jurisdiction and has all the powers of the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 710, Sec. 44. Operative July 1, 1991, by Sec. 48 of Ch. 710.)
