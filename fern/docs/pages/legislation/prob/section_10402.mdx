# PROB § 10402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 6. INDEPENDENT ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** PROB § 10402

---

## Text

As used in this part, “full authority” means authority to administer the estate under this part that includes all the powers granted under this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
