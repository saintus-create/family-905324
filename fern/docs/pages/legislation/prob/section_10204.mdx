# PROB § 10204

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. ADMINISTRATION OF ESTATES OF DECEDENTS > Part: 5. ESTATE MANAGEMENT > Chapter: 18. Sales > Article: 4. Special Provisions Applicable to Particular Types of Property

**Citation:** PROB § 10204

---

## Text

Property of the estate that consists of a partnership interest or an interest belonging to an estate by virtue of a partnership formerly existing may be sold in the same manner as other personal property.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
