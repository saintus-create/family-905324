# PROB § 5142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. NONPROBATE TRANSFERS > Part: 2. MULTIPLE-PARTY ACCOUNTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Definitions > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** PROB § 5142

---

## Text

“P.O.D. payee” means a person designated on a P.O.D. account as one to whom the account is payable on request after the death of one or more persons.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1990, Ch. 79.)
