# BPC § 18848

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 14. Administrative Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 18848

---

## Text

The license of any promoter or matchmaker found guilty of managing a boxer or martial arts fighter, either directly or indirectly, without written approval from the commission shall be subject to disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 757, Sec. 20.)
