# BPC § 18766

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 9. Martial Arts

**Citation:** BPC § 18766

---

## Text

Every contestant in an amateur kickboxing or martial arts contest under the age of 18 years shall wear protective headgear approved by the commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
