# BPC § 2833.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 7. Nurse Anesthetists

**Citation:** BPC § 2833.5

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 2725 and in this section, the practice of nurse anesthetist does not confer authority to practice medicine or surgery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 696, Sec. 7.)
