# BPC § 19072.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 4. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 19072.6

---

## Text

The manufacturer of chairs and benches and similar articles, using slip seats that are manufactured by himself or herself or purchased from another, is responsible for the labeling of those articles.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 405, Sec. 48. Effective January 1, 2003.)
