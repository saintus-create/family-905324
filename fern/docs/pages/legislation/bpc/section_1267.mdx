# BPC § 1267

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 1267

---

## Text

Any denial, suspension, or revocation of a license under this chapter shall be conducted in compliance with Section 100171 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 220, Sec. 5. Effective August 4, 1997.)
