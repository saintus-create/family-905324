# BPC § 6734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6734

---

## Text

Any person practices civil engineering when he professes to be a civil engineer or is in responsible charge of civil engineering work.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
