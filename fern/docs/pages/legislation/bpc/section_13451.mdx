# BPC § 13451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 6. Standards for Compression-Ignition Engine Fuels, Kerosene, and Fuel Oils

**Citation:** BPC § 13451

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell or deliver to any premises for the purpose of sale, any product as or purported to be “compression-ignition engine fuel,” “diesel fuel,” “kerosene,” or “fuel oil,” unless the product conforms to the specifications of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 521, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
