# BPC § 6720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6720

## Text

Each member of the board shall receive a per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.
