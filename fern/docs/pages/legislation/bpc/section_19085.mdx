# BPC § 19085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 5. Labeling

**Citation:** BPC § 19085

---

## Text

Labels shall contain no advertising matter nor anything that detracts, or is likely to detract from the required statements.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
