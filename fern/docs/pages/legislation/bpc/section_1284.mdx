# BPC § 1284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 5. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 1284

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a duly licensed physician and surgeon, or any person authorized to serve as director under this chapter, to serve only as a nominal director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1285 by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1377.)
