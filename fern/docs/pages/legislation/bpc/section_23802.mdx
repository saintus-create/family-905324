# BPC § 23802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 5. Restrictions on Issuance of Licenses > Article: 1.5. Conditional Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 23802

---

## Text

Such conditions shall be endorsed upon the license and any renewal thereof and shall be binding upon all persons to whom the license is transferred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1351.)
