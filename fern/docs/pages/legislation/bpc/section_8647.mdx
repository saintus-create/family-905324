# BPC § 8647

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 8647

---

## Text

Failure to comply in the sale or use of pesticides with the provisions of Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 12751) of Division 7 of the Food and Agricultural Code is a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 560, Sec. 33. (SB 1244) Effective January 1, 2015.)
