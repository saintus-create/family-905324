# BPC § 4996.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 14. Social Workers > Article: 4. Licensure

**Citation:** BPC § 4996.10

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall be construed only as provisions relating to the examination and licensing of clinical social workers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 820, Sec. 1.)
