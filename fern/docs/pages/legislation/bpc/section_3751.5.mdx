# BPC § 3751.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 5. Suspension, Revocation, and Reinstatement of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 3751.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 489, a person whose application for licensure has been denied for cause may reapply to the board for licensure only after a period of three years has elapsed from the date of the denial.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1274, Sec. 19.5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
