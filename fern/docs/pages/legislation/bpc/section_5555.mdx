# BPC § 5555

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 4. Issuance of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 5555

---

## Text

Licenses to practice architecture remain in full force until revoked or suspended for cause, or until they expire, as provided in this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1223, Sec. 17.)
