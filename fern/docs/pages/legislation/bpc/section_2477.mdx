# BPC § 2477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 22. Podiatric Medicine

**Citation:** BPC § 2477

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter prohibits the manufacture, the recommendation, or the sale of either corrective shoes or appliances for the human feet.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
