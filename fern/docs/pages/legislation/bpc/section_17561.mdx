# BPC § 17561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 3. Motel and Motor Court Rate Signs

**Citation:** BPC § 17561

---

## Text

“Room rates” as used in this article means the rates at which rooms or other accommodations are rented to occupants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 975.)
