# BPC § 4933.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 1. Administration and General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4933.5

---

## Text

The board, by and with the approval of the director, may employ personnel necessary for the administration of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 397, Sec. 4. (SB 1246) Effective January 1, 2015.)
