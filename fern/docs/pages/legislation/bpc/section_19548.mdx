# BPC § 19548

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 6.5. Fairs and Expositions

**Citation:** BPC § 19548

---

## Text

Except as provided in this chapter, no license, tax, or fee shall be assessed against or collected from any fair which, directly and not through any private person, conducts a horse racing meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 60. Effective January 1, 2001.)
