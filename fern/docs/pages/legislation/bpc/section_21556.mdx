# BPC § 21556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 2. Builders’ Tools

**Citation:** BPC § 21556

---

## Text

Any person who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.
