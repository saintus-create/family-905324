# BPC § 22385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 17. Invention Development Services Contracts > Article: 4.5. Remedies and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 22385

---

## Text

Any waiver by the customer of the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed contrary to public policy and shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 967.)
