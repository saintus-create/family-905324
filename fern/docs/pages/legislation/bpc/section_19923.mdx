# BPC § 19923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 9. Conditions of Operation

**Citation:** BPC § 19923

---

## Text

No owner licensee shall operate a gambling enterprise in violation of any governing local ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19917 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 88. Effective January 1, 2003.)
