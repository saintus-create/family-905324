# BPC § 4991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 14. Social Workers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4991

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the Clinical Social Worker Practice Act. It shall be liberally construed to effect its objectives.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 2007.)
