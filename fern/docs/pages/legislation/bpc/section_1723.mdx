# BPC § 1723

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 6. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 1723

---

## Text

All fines, penalties, and forfeitures, including the examination fee, imposed or collected by the board under any provision of this chapter shall be paid to the executive officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 47, Sec. 15. Effective March 21, 1984.)
