# BPC § 6731.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6731.4

---

## Text

If a registered civil engineer provides construction management services pursuant to Section 6731.3, Section 6703.1 shall not limit the responsibility of the engineer for the services actually provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 786, Sec. 2.)
