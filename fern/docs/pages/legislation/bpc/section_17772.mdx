# BPC § 17772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Trading Stamp Company Act

**Citation:** BPC § 17772

---

## Text

Trading stamps issued by a trading stamp company subject to this chapter are exempt from the Corporate Securities Law of 1968 (Division 1 (commencing with Section 25000) of Title 4 of the Corporations Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
