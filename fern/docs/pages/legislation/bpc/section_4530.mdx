# BPC § 4530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 4. Schools for Preparation of Psychiatric Technicians

**Citation:** BPC § 4530

---

## Text

The board shall prepare and maintain a list of approved schools which offer an approved program for psychiatric technicians.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 338, Sec. 21. (SB 539) Effective January 1, 2012.)
