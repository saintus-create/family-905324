# BPC § 1213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 1213

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “school” means any place, establishment, or institution organized and operated to offer training for one or more of the personnel classifications included in this chapter or the regulations pertaining thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1208 (as added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1377) by Stats. 1971, Ch. 438.)
