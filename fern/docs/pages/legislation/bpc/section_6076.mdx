# BPC § 6076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 5. Disciplinary Authority of the Board of Governors

**Citation:** BPC § 6076

---

## Text

With the approval of the Supreme Court, the Board of Trustees may formulate and enforce rules of professional conduct for all licensees of the State Bar.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 55. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
