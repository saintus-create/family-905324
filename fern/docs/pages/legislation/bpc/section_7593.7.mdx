# BPC § 7593.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 7593.7

---

## Text

The chief shall issue a pocket identification card to the owner; any partner, officer, member, or manager active in the licensed business; and qualified manager. The chief shall determine the form and content of the card.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 376, Sec. 25. (AB 830) Effective January 1, 2022.)
