# BPC § 4990.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.7. Board of Behavioral Sciences > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4990.14

---

## Text

The board shall have and use a seal bearing the words “The Board of Behavioral Sciences,” and shall otherwise conform to Section 107.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2007.)
