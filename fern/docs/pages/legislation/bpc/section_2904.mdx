# BPC § 2904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2904

---

## Text

The practice of psychology shall not include prescribing drugs, performing surgery or administering electroconvulsive therapy.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1677.)
