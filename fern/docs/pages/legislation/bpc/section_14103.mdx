# BPC § 14103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 1. Good Will

**Citation:** BPC § 14103

---

## Text

The person transferring the good will of a business may transfer with it the right of using the name under which the business is conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 57.)
