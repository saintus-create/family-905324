# BPC § 11420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 8. Real Estate Appraisers Regulation Fund

**Citation:** BPC § 11420

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 786, Sec. 65. (SB 774) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Ch. 8, commencing with Section 11410.)
