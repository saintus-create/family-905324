# BPC § 6739

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6739

---

## Text

Officers and employees of the United States of America practicing solely as such officers or employees are exempt from registration under the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
