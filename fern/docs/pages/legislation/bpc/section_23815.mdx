# BPC § 23815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 5. Restrictions on Issuance of Licenses > Article: 2. Limitation on Number of Licensed Premises

**Citation:** BPC § 23815

---

## Text

It is hereby determined that the public welfare and morals require that there be a limitation on the number of premises licensed for the sale of distilled spirits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
