# BPC § 3005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3005

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “place of practice” means any location where optometry is practiced.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2005.)
