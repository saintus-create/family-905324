# BPC § 12100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. State Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 12100

---

## Text

Where not otherwise provided by law, the Department of Food and Agriculture has general supervision of the weights and measures and weighing and measuring devices sold or used in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 246, Sec. 3.)
