# BPC § 19266.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3.1. Household Movers Act > Article: 5.4. Subhauling Agreements

**Citation:** BPC § 19266.2

---

## Text

This article does not apply to a subhauling agreement when the subhauler is not otherwise subject to this chapter for activity related to the subhauling agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 8. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018.)
