# BPC § 7622.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7622.2

---

## Text

A person shall not open or maintain a place or establishment at which to engage in or conduct, or hold himself, herself, or itself out as engaging in or conducting, the activities of a funeral director without a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 17. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
