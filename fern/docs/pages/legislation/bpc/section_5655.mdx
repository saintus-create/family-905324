# BPC § 5655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 4. Issuance of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 5655

---

## Text

Licenses to practice landscape architecture shall remain in full force until revoked or suspended for cause, or until they expire, as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 879, Sec. 22.16. Effective January 1, 1999.)
