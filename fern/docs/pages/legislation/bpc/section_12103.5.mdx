# BPC § 12103.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. State Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 12103.5

---

## Text

The duty of enforcing this division and carrying out its provisions and requirements is vested in the secretary and in each sealer acting under the supervision and direction of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 573, Sec. 47. (SB 800) Effective January 1, 2018.)
