# BPC § 18816

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18816

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for a licensed professional trainer, as described in Section 18653, shall be two hundred dollars ($200).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 370, Sec. 14. (SB 309) Effective January 1, 2014.)
