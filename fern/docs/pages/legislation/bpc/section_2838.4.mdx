# BPC § 2838.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 9. Clinical Nurse Specialists

**Citation:** BPC § 2838.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit, revise, or expand the current scope of practice of a registered nurse.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 159, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998. Section operative July 1, 1998, pursuant to Section 2838.3.)
