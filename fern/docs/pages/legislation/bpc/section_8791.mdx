# BPC § 8791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 7. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 8791

---

## Text

It is the duty of the respective officers charged with the enforcement of laws to prosecute all persons charged with the violation of any of the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 41.)
