# BPC § 22948.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 35. Connected Televisions

**Citation:** BPC § 22948.22

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this chapter is contrary to public policy and void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 524, Sec. 1. (AB 1116) Effective January 1, 2016.)
