# BPC § 26229.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 9. Members

**Citation:** BPC § 26229.1

---

## Text

If a member of a nonstock association is other than a natural person, the member may be represented by any individual, associate, officer, or manager or member of it, who is duly authorized in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 107. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
