# BPC § 2850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2850

## Text

Meetings may be held at any time and place by the written consent of all members of the board.
