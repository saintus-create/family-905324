# BPC § 7084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 6. Records

**Citation:** BPC § 7084

---

## Text

The registrar, with the approval of the director may adopt and promulgate the rules and regulations he deems necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 37.)
