# BPC § 2539.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 9. Dispensing Audiologists

**Citation:** BPC § 2539.14

---

## Text

The provisions of subdivisions (b) and (c) of Section 2538.11 and the provisions of Section 2538.12 do not apply to a licensed audiologist who satisfies the requirements of Section 2539.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 12. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
