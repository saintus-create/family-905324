# BPC § 5222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 5222

---

## Text

“660 feet from the edge of the right-of-way” means 660 feet measured from the edge of the right-of-way horizontally along a line normal or perpendicular to the centerline of the highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 991.)
