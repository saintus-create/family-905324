# BPC § 23394.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23394.7

---

## Text

No privileges under an off-sale license shall be exercised by the licensee at any customer-operated checkout stand located on the licensee’s physical premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 726, Sec. 2. (AB 183) Effective January 1, 2012.)
