# BPC § 9889.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 7. Denial, Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.6

---

## Text

Upon the effective date of any order of suspension or revocation of any license governed by this chapter, the licensee shall surrender the license to the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1578.)
