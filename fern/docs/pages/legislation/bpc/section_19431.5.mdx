# BPC § 19431.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19431.5

---

## Text

The board shall publish, on a weekly basis on its internet website, all racehorse fatalities related to racing or training that occur within a licensed inclosure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 252, Sec. 2. (SB 800) Effective January 1, 2021.)
