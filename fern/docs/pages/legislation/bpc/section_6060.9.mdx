# BPC § 6060.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 4. Admission to the Practice of Law

**Citation:** BPC § 6060.9

---

## Text

Approval of any agency or agencies not existing under and by virtue of the laws of this State shall not be made a condition for accreditation of any California law school.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 647.)
