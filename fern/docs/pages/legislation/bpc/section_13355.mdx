# BPC § 13355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 13. Automatic Checkout Systems > Article: 2. Point-Of-Sale System Accuracy Verification

**Citation:** BPC § 13355

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “initial standard inspection” means an inspection made at the customary time interval used by an enforcement agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 529, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2006.)
