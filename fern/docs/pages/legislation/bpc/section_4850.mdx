# BPC § 4850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 3. Issuance of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 4850

---

## Text

Every person holding a license under this chapter shall conspicuously display the license in his or her principal place of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 621, Sec. 80. Effective January 1, 2006.)
