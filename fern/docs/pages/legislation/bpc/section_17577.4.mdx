# BPC § 17577.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 6. Water Treatment Devices

**Citation:** BPC § 17577.4

---

## Text

Any violation of this article is a misdemeanor punishable by imprisonment in the county jail not exceeding six months, or by a fine not exceeding two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500), or by both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1278, Sec. 1.)
