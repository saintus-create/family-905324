# BPC § 2981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 7. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 2981

---

## Text

The money in the Psychology Fund shall be used for the administration of this chapter.
