# BPC § 17023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 17023

---

## Text

“Give” includes giving, offering to give or advertising the intent to give.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
