# BPC § 7712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6.5. Crematories

**Citation:** BPC § 7712

---

## Text

A crematory established, operated, or maintained, may be operated by a corporation, partnership, or natural person, provided that a valid crematory license shall have been issued by the bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 35. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
