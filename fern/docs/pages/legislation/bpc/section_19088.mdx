# BPC § 19088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 5. Labeling

**Citation:** BPC § 19088

---

## Text

It is unlawful to use, in the description of filling material, or in the statement on any label, any misleading term or designation or any term or designation likely to mislead.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
