# BPC § 3718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3718

---

## Text

The board shall issue, deny, suspend, and revoke licenses to practice respiratory care as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1150, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2003.)
