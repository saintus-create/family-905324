# BPC § 3095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3095

---

## Text

In accordance with Section 125.9, the board may establish a system for the issuance of citations, and the assessment of administrative fines, as deemed appropriate by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3135 by Stats. 2005, Ch. 393, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 2006.)
