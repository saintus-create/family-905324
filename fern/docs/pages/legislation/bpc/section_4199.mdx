# BPC § 4199

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 15. Veterinary Food-Animal Drug Retailers

**Citation:** BPC § 4199

---

## Text

(a) Any veterinary food-animal drug dispensed pursuant to a prescription from a licensed veterinarian for food producing animals from a veterinary food-animal drug retailer pursuant to this chapter is subject to the labeling requirements of Sections 4076, 4076.6, and 4077.

(b) All prescriptions filled by a veterinary food-animal drug retailer shall be kept on file and maintained for at least three years in accordance with Section 4333.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 784, Sec. 3. (AB 1073) Effective January 1, 2016.)
