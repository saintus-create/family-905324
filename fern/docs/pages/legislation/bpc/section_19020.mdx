# BPC § 19020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19020

---

## Text

“R-value” means a measure of thermal resistance of material or composite materials. “R-value” is equal to the reciprocal of the conductance of a given material, orR-value =1conductance of the material where the conductance of the material is: Btu (hour × square foot × Fahrenheit degree average temperature difference between two surfaces).

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1456, Sec. 3.)
