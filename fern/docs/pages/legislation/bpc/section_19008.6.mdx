# BPC § 19008.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19008.6

---

## Text

Any article of upholstered furniture or bedding on sales floors in a private residence or room, which is not separated from living quarters, is secondhand.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
