# BPC § 21628.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21628.3

---

## Text

In complying with the requirements of Section 21628, a secondhand dealer shall verify the identification of the seller or pledger for each transaction, not for each item that shall be reported.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 749, Sec. 2. (AB 1182) Effective January 1, 2016.)
