# BPC § 18897.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 2. Professional Athletes and Athlete Agents

**Citation:** BPC § 18897.33

---

## Text

No athlete agent shall knowingly enter into a contract containing any term or condition that, if complied with, would be in violation of law, or attempt to fill an order for help to be employed in violation of the law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 857, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
