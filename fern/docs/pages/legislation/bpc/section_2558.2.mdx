# BPC § 2558.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.5. Registered Dispensing Opticians > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2558.2

---

## Text

Any individual, corporation, or firm operating as a registered dispensing optician under this chapter who demonstrates a willful disregard for the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 630, Sec. 57. (AB 1534) Effective January 1, 2022.)
