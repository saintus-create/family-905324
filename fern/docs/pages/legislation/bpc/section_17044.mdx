# BPC § 17044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 3. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 17044

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person engaged in business within this State to sell or use any article or product as a “loss leader” as defined in Section 17030 of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 334.)
