# BPC § 18897

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 2. Professional Athletes and Athlete Agents

**Citation:** BPC § 18897

---

## Text

Every agent contract shall be in writing and shall include a description of the types of services to be performed and a schedule of the fees to be charged under the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 857, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
