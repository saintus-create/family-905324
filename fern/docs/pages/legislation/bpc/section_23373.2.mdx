# BPC § 23373.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23373.2

---

## Text

A winegrower or brandy manufacturer may be represented by only one California winegrower’s agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 783.)
