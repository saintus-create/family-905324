# BPC § 6531

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 6531

---

## Text

A professional fiduciary shall not operate with an expired, suspended, retired, canceled, or revoked license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 336, Sec. 1. (AB 2024) Effective January 1, 2015.)
