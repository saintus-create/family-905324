# BPC § 6159.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 9.6. Legal Aid Organizations

**Citation:** BPC § 6159.51

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “legal aid organization” means a nonprofit organization that provides civil legal services for the poor without charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 457, Sec. 3. (AB 590) Effective January 1, 2010.)
