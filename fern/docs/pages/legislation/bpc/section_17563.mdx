# BPC § 17563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 3. Motel and Motor Court Rate Signs

**Citation:** BPC § 17563

---

## Text

This article shall apply to operators and owners of motels, motor courts, and like establishments, and to such establishments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 975.)
