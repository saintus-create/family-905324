# BPC § 14270

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Model State Trademark Law > Article: 12. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** BPC § 14270

---

## Text

This chapter shall not affect any suit, proceeding, or appeal pending on January 1, 2008.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
