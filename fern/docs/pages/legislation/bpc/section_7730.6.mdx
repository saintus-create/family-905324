# BPC § 7730.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7730.6

---

## Text

(a) For reinstatement of a license within the fiscal year, the fee shall be fifty dollars ($50).

(b) As used in this section, “reinstatement of a license” means the reissuance of a canceled cemetery broker’s license, or a cemetery salesperson’s license which was canceled during the year for which it was issued upon the salesperson’s withdrawal from the employ of a cemetery broker.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 62. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
