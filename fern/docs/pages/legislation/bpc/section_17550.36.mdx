# BPC § 17550.36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 2.7. Travel Consumer Restitution Plan

**Citation:** BPC § 17550.36

---

## Text

“Participant,” as used in this article, means a seller of travel, as defined in Section 17550.7, who is registered pursuant to Section 17550.20.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1123, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
