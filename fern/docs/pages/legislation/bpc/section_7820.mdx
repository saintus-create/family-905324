# BPC § 7820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7820

---

## Text

The board shall have and use a seal bearing the name “Board for Professional Engineers, Land Surveyors, and Geologists.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 432, Sec. 22. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2012.)
