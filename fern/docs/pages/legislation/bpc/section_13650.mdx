# BPC § 13650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14.5. Service Stations

**Citation:** BPC § 13650

---

## Text

“Service station,” as used in this chapter, means any establishment which offers for sale or sells gasoline or other motor vehicle fuel to the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1561, Sec. 1.)
