# BPC § 23040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23040

---

## Text

“Within this State” means all territory within the boundaries of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
