# BPC § 23430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 4. Club Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 23430

---

## Text

The department may issue one club license to any club as defined in this article.
