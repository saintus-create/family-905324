# BPC § 10222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 4. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 10222

---

## Text

For any examination required under any order issued pursuant to the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act, the fee shall be the same as for a salesperson or broker license examination, as appropriate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 342, Sec. 15) by Stats. 1997, Ch. 232, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1998.)
