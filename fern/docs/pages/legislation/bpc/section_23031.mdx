# BPC § 23031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23031

---

## Text

“Gallon” or “wine gallon” means that liquid measure containing 231 cubic inches.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953. Ch. 152.)
