# BPC § 2475.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 22. Podiatric Medicine

**Citation:** BPC § 2475.2

---

## Text

As used in this article, “podiatric residency” means a program of supervised postgraduate clinical training, one year or more in duration, approved by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 655, Sec. 21.)
