# BPC § 3002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3002

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, a trial frame or test lens is any frame or lens used in testing the eye, which is not sold and is not for sale to clients.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 423.)
