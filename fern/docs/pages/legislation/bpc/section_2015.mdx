# BPC § 2015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2015

---

## Text

The president of the board may call meetings of any duly appointed and created committee or panel of the board at a specified time and place.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 678, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2008.)
