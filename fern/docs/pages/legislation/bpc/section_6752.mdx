# BPC § 6752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 6752

---

## Text

An applicant for registration as a civil engineer must have gained his experience under the direction of a civil engineer legally qualified to practice.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 92.)
