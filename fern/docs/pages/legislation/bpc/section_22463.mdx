# BPC § 22463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 20. Professional Photocopiers

**Citation:** BPC § 22463

---

## Text

A failure to comply with the requirements of this chapter shall be punishable as a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1005, Sec. 1.)
