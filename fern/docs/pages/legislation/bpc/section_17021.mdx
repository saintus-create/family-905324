# BPC § 17021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 17021

---

## Text

“Person” includes any person, firm, association, organization, partnership, business trust, company, corporation or municipal or other public corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
