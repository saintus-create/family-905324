# BPC § 10182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 3. Disciplinary Action

**Citation:** BPC § 10182

---

## Text

As a condition to the reinstatement of a revoked or suspended license, the commissioner may require the applicant to take and pass a qualifying examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1243.)
