# BPC § 7731.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7731.4

---

## Text

(a) All moneys paid into the State Treasury and credited to the Cemetery and Funeral Fund shall be expended in accordance with law for the payment of all actual and necessary expenses incurred in carrying out the provisions of this act.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2016.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 66) and added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 67. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016. Section operative July 1, 2016, by its own provisions.)
