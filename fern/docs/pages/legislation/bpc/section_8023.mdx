# BPC § 8023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Shorthand Reporters > Article: 3. Application, Examination, and Certificate; Requirement and Contents

**Citation:** BPC § 8023

---

## Text

No certificate shall be issued until the applicant has passed the examination prescribed by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 665.)
