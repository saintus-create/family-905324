# BPC § 19008.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19008.5

---

## Text

Any article of upholstered furniture or bedding is secondhand if it contains any secondhand material in whole or in part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
