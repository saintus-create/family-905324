# BPC § 2864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 2864

---

## Text

Every licensee under this chapter may be known as a licensed vocational nurse and may place the letters “L.V.N.” after the licensee’s name.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 632, Sec. 10. (AB 1536) Effective January 1, 2022.)
