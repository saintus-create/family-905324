# BPC § 7414.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 12.5. Tanning Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 7414.6

---

## Text

The board may adopt regulations concerning the operation of tanning facilities in licensed establishments.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 2004.)
