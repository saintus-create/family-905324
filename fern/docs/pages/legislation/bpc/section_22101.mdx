# BPC § 22101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 22101

---

## Text

“Article” means any article of merchandise, and includes each and every portion of any such article whether or not such portion is separable or is a distinct part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 316.)
