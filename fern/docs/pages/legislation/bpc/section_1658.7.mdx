# BPC § 1658.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 3.5. Additional Offices

**Citation:** BPC § 1658.7

## Text

The board shall have the power to carry out the provisions of this article by uniform rules and regulations.
