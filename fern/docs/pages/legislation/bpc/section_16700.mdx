# BPC § 16700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Combinations in Restraint of Trade > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 16700

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are cumulative of each other and of any other provision of law relating to the same subject in effect May 22, 1907.
