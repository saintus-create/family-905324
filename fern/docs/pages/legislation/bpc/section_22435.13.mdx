# BPC § 22435.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 19. Shopping and Laundry Carts > Article: 2. Shopping Cart or Laundry Cart Retrieval Services

**Citation:** BPC § 22435.13

---

## Text

Any person who violates the provisions of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

The provisions of this section are not intended to preclude the application of any other laws relating to prosecution for theft.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 687, Sec. 1.)
