# BPC § 678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Nursing

**Citation:** BPC § 678

---

## Text

The failure to give the notice required by this article shall make any contract entered into between the parties null and void.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1490.)
