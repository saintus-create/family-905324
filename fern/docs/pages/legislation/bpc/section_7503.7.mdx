# BPC § 7503.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 7503.7

---

## Text

The form and content of the license shall be determined by the director in accordance with Section 164.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
