# BPC § 105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 1. The Department

**Citation:** BPC § 105

---

## Text

Members of boards in the department shall take an oath of office as provided in the Constitution and the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 829.)
