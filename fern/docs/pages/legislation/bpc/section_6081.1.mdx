# BPC § 6081.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 5. Disciplinary Authority of the Board of Governors

**Citation:** BPC § 6081.1

---

## Text

Nothing in Sections 6080 and 6081 shall require the State Bar Court to transcribe oral testimony unless ordered by the Supreme Court or requested by a party at the party’s expense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1159, Sec. 8.)
