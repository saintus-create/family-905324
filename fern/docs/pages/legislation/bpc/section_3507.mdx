# BPC § 3507

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3507

---

## Text

The appointing power has power to remove from office any member of the board, as provided in Section 106.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 332, Sec. 35. (SB 1236) Effective January 1, 2013.)
