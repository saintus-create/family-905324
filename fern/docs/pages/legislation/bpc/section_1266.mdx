# BPC § 1266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 1266

---

## Text

The clinical laboratory license and the license or current renewal permit of each person performing tests shall be conspicuously posted in the clinical laboratory.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1377.)
