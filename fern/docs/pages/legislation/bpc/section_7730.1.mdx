# BPC § 7730.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7730.1

---

## Text

The cremated remains disposer registration fee shall be one hundred ninety dollars ($190).

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 57. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
