# BPC § 18767

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 9. Martial Arts

**Citation:** BPC § 18767

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to light and noncontact kickboxing and martial arts, and kickboxing and martial arts instruction and schools.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
