# BPC § 4173

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 12. Prescriber Dispensing

**Citation:** BPC § 4173

---

## Text

This chapter does not prevent the dispensing of drugs or devices by registered nurses functioning pursuant to Section 2725.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
