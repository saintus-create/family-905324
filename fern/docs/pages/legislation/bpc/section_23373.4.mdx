# BPC § 23373.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23373.4

---

## Text

A California winegrower’s agent’s license authorizes the holder to furnish samples, to produce and distribute wine lists, to produce and furnish advertising material, retailers’ advertising specialties and consumer advertising specialties, with respect to the wine or brandy he distributes as an agent for a winegrower or brandy manufacturer so authorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 783.)
