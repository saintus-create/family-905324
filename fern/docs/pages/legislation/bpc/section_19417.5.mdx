# BPC § 19417.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19417.5

---

## Text

“Appaloosa racing” is the form of horse racing in which each participating horse is an Appaloosa horse, is mounted by a jockey, and engages in races on the flat over a distance of not less than one-quarter of a mile or more than four miles.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2001.)
