# BPC § 12009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12009

---

## Text

“Sell,” in any of its variant forms, includes barter, exchange, trade, rent, lease, keep for sale, offer for sale, or expose for sale, in any of their variant forms.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 156, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
