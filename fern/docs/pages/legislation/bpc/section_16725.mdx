# BPC § 16725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Combinations in Restraint of Trade > Article: 2. Prohibited Restraints on Competition

**Citation:** BPC § 16725

---

## Text

It is not unlawful to enter into agreements or form associations or combinations, the purpose and effect of which is to promote, encourage or increase competition in any trade or industry, or which are in furtherance of trade.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
