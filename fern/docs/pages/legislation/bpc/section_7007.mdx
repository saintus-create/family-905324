# BPC § 7007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7007

---

## Text

Eight members constitute a quorum at a board meeting.

Due notice of each meeting and the time and place thereof shall be given each member in the manner provided by the bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1005, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
