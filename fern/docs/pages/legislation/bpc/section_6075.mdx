# BPC § 6075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 5. Disciplinary Authority of the Board of Governors

**Citation:** BPC § 6075

---

## Text

In their relation to the provisions of Article 6, concerning the disciplinary authority of the courts, the provisions of this article provide a complete alternative and cumulative method of hearing and determining accusations against licensees of the State Bar.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 54. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
