# BPC § 3650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 5. Naturopathic Childbirth Attendance

**Citation:** BPC § 3650

---

## Text

A naturopathic doctor may perform naturopathic childbirth attendance if they have completed additional training and have been granted a certificate of specialty practice by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 497, Sec. 42. (SB 1526) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
