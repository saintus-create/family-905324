# BPC § 7159.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 10. Home Improvement Business

**Citation:** BPC § 7159.11

---

## Text

A violation of any provision of Section 7159.10 by a licensee, or a person subject to be licensed under this chapter, or by his or her agent or salesperson, is cause for discipline.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 385, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2006.)
