# BPC § 7711.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7711.10

---

## Text

A renewal cemetery broker’s license is a cemetery license issued to a person who had a cemetery broker’s license unrevoked and unsuspended on June 30 of the fiscal year previous to the fiscal year for which the renewal cemetery license is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 32. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
