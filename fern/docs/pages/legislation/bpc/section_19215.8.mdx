# BPC § 19215.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 9.5. Disciplinary Proceedings Against Nonresidents

**Citation:** BPC § 19215.8

---

## Text

As used in this article “nonresident” means a person who is not a resident of this State at the time he or she engages in business in the State as described in Section 19215.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 405, Sec. 61. Effective January 1, 2003.)
