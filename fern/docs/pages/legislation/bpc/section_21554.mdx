# BPC § 21554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 2. Builders’ Tools

**Citation:** BPC § 21554

---

## Text

The report shall include references to all builders’ tools bought or received as a pledge or shipped or otherwise transmitted since the preceding report.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 50.)
