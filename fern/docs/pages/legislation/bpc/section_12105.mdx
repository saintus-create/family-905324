# BPC § 12105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. State Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 12105

---

## Text

The department shall, at a frequency determined by the secretary, inspect the work of the local sealers and may inspect the weights, measures, balances, or any other weighing or measuring devices of any person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 661, Sec. 24. (SB 1576) Effective January 1, 2013.)
