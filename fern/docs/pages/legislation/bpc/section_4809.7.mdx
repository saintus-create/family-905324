# BPC § 4809.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4809.7

---

## Text

The board shall establish a regular inspection program that will provide for random announced or unannounced inspections of veterinary premises. The board shall make every reasonable effort to ensure veterinary premises are inspected in a timely manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 195, Sec. 3. (AB 1502) Effective January 1, 2026.)
