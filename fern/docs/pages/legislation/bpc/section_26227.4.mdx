# BPC § 26227.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** BPC § 26227.4

---

## Text

An association may establish reserves and invest the funds of the reserves in bonds or in other property as may be provided in the bylaws.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 107. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
