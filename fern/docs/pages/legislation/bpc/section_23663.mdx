# BPC § 23663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 4. Imports

**Citation:** BPC § 23663

---

## Text

Alcoholic beverages which are consigned to a destination within this State shall be presumed to be for delivery or use within this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
