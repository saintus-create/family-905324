# BPC § 470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 8. Dispute Resolution Programs > Article: 6. Funding

**Citation:** BPC § 470

---

## Text

A county may accept and disburse funds from any public or private source for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1313, Sec. 1.)
