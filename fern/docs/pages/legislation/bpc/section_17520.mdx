# BPC § 17520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 1.5. Labeling of Products Made by the Blind

**Citation:** BPC § 17520

---

## Text

Any misrepresentation that goods offered for sale are the products of blind workers is hereby directed to be a special matter of state-wide concern.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 766.)
