# BPC § 12106.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. State Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 12106.5

---

## Text

The secretary may accept the testing and sealing of weighing and measuring devices by another state upon a finding that the requirements of California for testing and sealing such devices have been met.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 661, Sec. 25. (SB 1576) Effective January 1, 2013.)
