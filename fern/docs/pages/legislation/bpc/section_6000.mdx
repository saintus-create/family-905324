# BPC § 6000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6000

---

## Text

This chapter of the Business and Professions Code constitutes the chapter on attorneys. It may be cited as the State Bar Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
