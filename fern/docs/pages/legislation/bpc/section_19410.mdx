# BPC § 19410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19410

---

## Text

“Inclosure” means all areas of the racing association’s or fair’s grounds and locations, as designated by the racing association or fair licensed to conduct a live racing meeting and approved by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 594, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
