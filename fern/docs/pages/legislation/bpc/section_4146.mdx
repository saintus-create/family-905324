# BPC § 4146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 9. Hypodermic Needles and Syringes

**Citation:** BPC § 4146

---

## Text

A pharmacy may accept the return of needles and syringes from the public if contained in a sharps container, as defined in Section 117750 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 307, Sec. 40. (SB 821) Effective January 1, 2010.)
