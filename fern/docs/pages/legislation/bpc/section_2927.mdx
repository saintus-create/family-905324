# BPC § 2927

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2927

---

## Text

Five members of the board shall at all times constitute a quorum.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 2932 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 26, Sec. 69. Effective March 30, 1994.)
