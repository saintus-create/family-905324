# BPC § 19123.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19123.6

---

## Text

Every article of bedding to which any secondhand material has been added in the process of repairing, shall be sanitized in accordance with the provisions of the sanitization regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1448, Sec. 16.)
