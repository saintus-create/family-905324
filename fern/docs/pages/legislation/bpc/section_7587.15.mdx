# BPC § 7587.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7587.15

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the director may assess a fine of up to five thousand dollars ($5,000) per violation against any licensee for a failure to comply with subdivision (d) of Section 7583.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 377, Sec. 33. (SB 609) Effective January 1, 2020.)
