# BPC § 7344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 5. Examinations

**Citation:** BPC § 7344

---

## Text

The board may contract or otherwise arrange for reasonably required physical accommodations and facilities to conduct examinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2004.)
