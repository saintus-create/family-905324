# BPC § 20044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5.5. Franchise Relations > Article: 9. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 20044

---

## Text

A franchisor is prohibited from modifying a franchise agreement, or requiring a general release, in exchange for any assistance related to a declared state or federal emergency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 728, Sec. 4. (AB 676) Effective January 1, 2023.)
