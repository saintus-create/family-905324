# BPC § 22913

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22913

---

## Text

The lien claimant shall provide written notice of the claim of lien to the lien debtor within 10 days of the date of filing the lien with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 712, Sec. 18. Effective October 7, 2005.)
