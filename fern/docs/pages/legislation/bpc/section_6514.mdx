# BPC § 6514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6514

---

## Text

The bureau shall keep a complete record of all its proceedings and all licenses issued, renewed, or revoked, and a detailed statement of receipts and disbursements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 491, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
