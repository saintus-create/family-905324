# BPC § 821

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 12.5. Mental Illness or Physical Illness

**Citation:** BPC § 821

---

## Text

The licentiate’s failure to comply with an order issued under Section 820 shall constitute grounds for the suspension or revocation of the licentiate’s certificate or license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1183, Sec. 1.)
