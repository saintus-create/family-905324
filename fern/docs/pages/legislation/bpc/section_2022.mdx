# BPC § 2022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2022

---

## Text

The directory shall be prima facie evidence of the authority of the persons named therein to practice under this act, unless such authority has been revoked, suspended, or otherwise limited pursuant to this chapter subsequent to the publication of the directory.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
