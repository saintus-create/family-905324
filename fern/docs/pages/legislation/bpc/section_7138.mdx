# BPC § 7138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7138

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a fee paid in connection with a service or application covered by Section 7137 shall accrue to the Contractors License Fund as an earned fee and shall not be refunded.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 312, Sec. 68. (SB 1474) Effective January 1, 2021.)
