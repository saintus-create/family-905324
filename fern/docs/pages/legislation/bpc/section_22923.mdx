# BPC § 22923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22923

---

## Text

The Secretary of State may adopt any regulations necessary to carry out his or her duties pursuant to this chapter, including prescribing necessary forms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1271, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
