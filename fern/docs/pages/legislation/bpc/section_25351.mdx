# BPC § 25351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 14. Seizure and Forfeiture of Property

**Citation:** BPC § 25351

---

## Text

Any person who possesses alcoholic beverages which are subject to seizure under Section 25350 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
