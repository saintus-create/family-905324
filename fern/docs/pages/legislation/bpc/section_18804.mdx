# BPC § 18804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18804

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for a license to promote or conduct a professional contest or exhibition shall be one thousand dollars ($1,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1057, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1994.)
