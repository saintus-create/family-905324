# BPC § 6346.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Law Libraries > Article: 3. Duties and Powers of Boards

**Citation:** BPC § 6346.5

---

## Text

A board of law library trustees may contract with the California Public Employees’ Retirement System, to make all or any of the employees of the law library members of the system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 332, Sec. 1.9. (SB 113) Effective January 1, 2010.)
