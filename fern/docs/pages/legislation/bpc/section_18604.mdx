# BPC § 18604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 18604

## Text

The appointing authority may remove any member of the commission for causes specified in Section 106.
