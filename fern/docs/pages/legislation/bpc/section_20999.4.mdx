# BPC § 20999.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 7.5. Franchises

**Citation:** BPC § 20999.4

---

## Text

At such time as a franchisor intends to withdraw from the marketing of fuel through retail and distribution outlets in the relevant geographic market area, prompt notification, together with a plan describing the schedule and conditions of the withdrawal, shall be provided by the franchisor to the Governor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 90, Sec. 4.)
