# BPC § 7588.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7588.4

---

## Text

Application or license fees shall not be refunded except in accordance with Section 158.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
