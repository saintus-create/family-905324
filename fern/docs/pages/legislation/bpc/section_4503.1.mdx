# BPC § 4503.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 4503.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the repeal of Section 4503 renders the successor entity to the board in Section 4503 subject to review by the appropriate policy committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 586, Sec. 14. (AB 1229) Effective January 1, 2018.)
