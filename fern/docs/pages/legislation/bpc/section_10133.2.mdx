# BPC § 10133.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 1. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 10133.2

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 10016, 10131, 10131.1, and 10132 do not apply to any stenographer, bookkeeper, receptionist, telephone operator, or other clerical help in carrying out their functions as such.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 30. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
