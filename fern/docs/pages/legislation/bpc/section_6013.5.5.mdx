# BPC § 6013.5.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6013.5.5

---

## Text

Subdivision (c) of Section 450 and Sections 450.2 to 450.6, inclusive, shall apply to public members appointed or reappointed on or after January 1, 2012.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 569, Sec. 2. (AB 156) Effective September 27, 2022.)
