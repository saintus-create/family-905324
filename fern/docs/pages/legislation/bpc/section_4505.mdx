# BPC § 4505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 4505

---

## Text

Except as provided by Section 159.5, the board may employ whatever personnel is necessary for the administration of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 376, Sec. 1.)
