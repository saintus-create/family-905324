# BPC § 6401.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5.5. Legal Document Assistants and Unlawful Detainer Assistants > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6401.7

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2030, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 341, Sec. 1. (AB 690) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 5.5, comprising Sections 6400 to 6415.)
