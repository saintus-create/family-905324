# BPC § 2564.73

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.5. Registered Dispensing Opticians > Article: 2.5. Nonresident Ophthalmic Lens Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2564.73

## Text

The board may adopt, amend, or repeal any rules and regulations that are reasonably necessary to carry out this article.
