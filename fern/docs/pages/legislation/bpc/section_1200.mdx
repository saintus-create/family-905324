# BPC § 1200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 1200

## Text

Every provision of this chapter shall be liberally construed to protect the interests of all persons affected.
