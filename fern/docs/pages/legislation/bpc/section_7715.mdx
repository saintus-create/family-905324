# BPC § 7715

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 7. Penal Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 7715

---

## Text

Any person, partnership, association, corporation, or other form of organization, or any agent or representative thereof, who violates any of the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 970, Sec. 83. Effective January 1, 1999.)
