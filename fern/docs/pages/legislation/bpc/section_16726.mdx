# BPC § 16726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Combinations in Restraint of Trade > Article: 2. Prohibited Restraints on Competition

**Citation:** BPC § 16726

---

## Text

Except as provided in this chapter, every trust is unlawful, against public policy and void.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
