# BPC § 5557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 4. Issuance of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 5557

---

## Text

A duplicate license to practice architecture, replacing one which has been lost, destroyed, or mutilated, may be issued subject to the rules and regulations of the board. The duplicate license fee fixed by this chapter shall be charged for that issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1223, Sec. 18.)
