# BPC § 16223

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. State Licensing > Article: 2. Actions

**Citation:** BPC § 16223

---

## Text

Upon application and without bond or affidavit, every officer suing under this chapter is entitled to all or any of provisional remedies provided in the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 61.)
