# BPC § 7673.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 4.5. Cremated Remains Disposer

**Citation:** BPC § 7673.2

---

## Text

A cremated remains disposer shall be subject to and shall be disciplined by the bureau in accordance with Article 6 (commencing with Section 7686). Any violation of this article shall also be grounds for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 20. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
