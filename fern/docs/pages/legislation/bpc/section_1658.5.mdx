# BPC § 1658.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 3.5. Additional Offices

**Citation:** BPC § 1658.5

---

## Text

The transfer of the location of one additional office to a new location within the same county shall not be considered the establishment of a new additional office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 947.)
