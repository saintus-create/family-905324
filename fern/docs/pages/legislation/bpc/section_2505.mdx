# BPC § 2505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 24. Licensed Midwives

**Citation:** BPC § 2505

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Licensed Midwifery Practice Act of 1993.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1280, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
