# BPC § 7582.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7582.12

---

## Text

(a) The license shall at all times be posted in a conspicuous place in the principal place of business of the licensee.

(b) The director may assess a fine of two hundred fifty dollars ($250) per violation of subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 607, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2002.)
