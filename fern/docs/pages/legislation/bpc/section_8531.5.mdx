# BPC § 8531.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8531.5

---

## Text

The board shall prepare minutes of its annual meeting and any special meetings, which shall be permanently maintained at the office of the registrar.
