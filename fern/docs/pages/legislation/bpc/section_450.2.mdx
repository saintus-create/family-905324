# BPC § 450.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 6. Public Members

**Citation:** BPC § 450.2

---

## Text

In order to avoid a potential for a conflict of interest, a public member of a board shall not:

(a) Be a current or past licensee of that board.

(b) Be a close family member of a licensee of that board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1150, Sec. 1.2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
