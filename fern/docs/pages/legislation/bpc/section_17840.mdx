# BPC § 17840

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Fixed Term Installment Contracts

**Citation:** BPC § 17840

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be interpreted to prevent a buyer from paying the full remaining balance of a fixed term installment contract before its maturity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 557, Sec. 1. (AB 483) Effective January 1, 2026.)
