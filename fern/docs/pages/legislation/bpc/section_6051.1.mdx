# BPC § 6051.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 3. Investigations, Examining Committee, Subpoenas, and Other Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 6051.1

---

## Text

A motion to quash a subpoena issued pursuant to Section 6049 shall be brought in the State Bar Court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 453, Sec. 9.)
