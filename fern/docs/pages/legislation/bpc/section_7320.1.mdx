# BPC § 7320.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 2. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 7320.1

---

## Text

When providing a manicure or pedicure, no metal instruments shall be used except those metal instruments necessary for the cutting, trimming, manicuring, or pedicuring of nails or cuticles or for the smoothing and massaging of the hands and feet.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 415, Sec. 26. (SB 1491) Effective January 1, 2011.)
