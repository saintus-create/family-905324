# BPC § 8505.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 8505.4

---

## Text

Fumigation shall be performed in compliance with all applicable state, county, and city laws and ordinances and all applicable laws and regulations of the United States.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 354, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2008.)
