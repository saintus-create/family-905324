# BPC § 22704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 23. Tanning Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 22704

---

## Text

Any tanning device used by a tanning facility shall comply with all applicable federal laws and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 808, Sec. 1.)
