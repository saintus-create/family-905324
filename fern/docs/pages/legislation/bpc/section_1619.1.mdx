# BPC § 1619.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 1619.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 1619, the board need not retain the National Board of Dental Examiners’ examination papers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 872.)
