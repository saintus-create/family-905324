# BPC § 2496

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 22. Podiatric Medicine

**Citation:** BPC § 2496

---

## Text

In order to ensure the continuing competence of persons licensed to practice podiatric medicine, the board shall adopt and administer regulations requiring continuing education of those licensees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 122, Sec. 1. (AB 826) Effective January 1, 2024.)
