# BPC § 3001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3001

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, an ophthalmic lens is any lens which has a spherical, cylindrical, or prismatic power or value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 630, Sec. 75. (AB 1534) Effective January 1, 2022.)
