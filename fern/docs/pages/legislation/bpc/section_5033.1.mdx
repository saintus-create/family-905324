# BPC § 5033.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5033.1

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “license” shall also include “certificate.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1278, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
