# BPC § 6728.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2.3. Professional Engineers Review Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 6728.2

## Text

Each member of a committee shall receive a per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103 of this code.
