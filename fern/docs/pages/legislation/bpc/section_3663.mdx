# BPC § 3663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 6. Offenses and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 3663

---

## Text

(a) The board shall have the responsibility for reviewing the quality of the practice of naturopathic medicine carried out by persons licensed as naturopathic doctors pursuant to this chapter.

(b) The board may discipline a naturopathic doctor for unprofessional conduct. After a hearing conducted in accordance with the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code), the board may deny, suspend, revoke, or place on probation the license of, or reprimand, censure, or otherwise discipline a naturopathic doctor in accordance with Division 1.5 (commencing with Section 475).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 497, Sec. 47. (SB 1526) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
