# BPC § 18734

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 7. Regulation of Contests

**Citation:** BPC § 18734

---

## Text

(a) The commission shall designate the scoring officials for each contest.

(b) The referee may render a decision at the end of a boxing contest if authorized by a representative of the commission.

(c) The commission may require that judges be used in conjunction with the referee at any boxing contest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1095, Sec. 8.)
