# BPC § 5078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 4. Applications, Registrations, Permits Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 5078

---

## Text

In each office of a certified public accountant or public accountant in this state which is not under the personal management of such an accountant, respectively, work shall be supervised by a certified public accountant or public accountant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 92.)
