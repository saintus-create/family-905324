# BPC § 18815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18815

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for a sparring permit shall be twenty-five dollars ($25).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1057, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1994.)
