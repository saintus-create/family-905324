# BPC § 19701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4.5. Mule Racing

**Citation:** BPC § 19701

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a mule racing meeting or mule races may be conducted by any fair.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 872, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2003.)
