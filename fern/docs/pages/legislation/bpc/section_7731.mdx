# BPC § 7731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7731

---

## Text

Upon payment of the charges set forth, the bureau shall issue a renewal of the certificate of authority to the cemetery authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 61. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
