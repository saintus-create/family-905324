# BPC § 11351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 5. Reciprocity of Licenses and Certification

**Citation:** BPC § 11351

---

## Text

Temporary practice is not required under this chapter if the appraiser from another state assists in the performance of the appraisal as provided by Section 11324.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 940, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1994.)
