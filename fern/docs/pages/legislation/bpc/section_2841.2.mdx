# BPC § 2841.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2841.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the repeal of Section 2841 renders the successor entity to the board in Section 2841 subject to review by the appropriate policy committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 586, Sec. 2. (AB 1229) Effective January 1, 2018.)
