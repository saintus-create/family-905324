# BPC § 2983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 7. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 2983

---

## Text

Every person to whom a license is issued shall, as a condition precedent to its issuance, and in addition to any application, examination or other fee, pay the prescribed initial license fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 658, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2006.)
