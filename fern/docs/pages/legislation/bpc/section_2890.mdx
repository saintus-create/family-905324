# BPC § 2890

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 6. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 2890

---

## Text

The Vocational Nursing and Psychiatric Technicians Fund is hereby created in the State Treasury.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 759, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1998.)
