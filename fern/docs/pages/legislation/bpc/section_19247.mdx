# BPC § 19247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3.1. Household Movers Act > Article: 2. Regulation of Household Movers

**Citation:** BPC § 19247

---

## Text

Every household mover shall add a prominent link to its Internet Web site that immediately directs all consumers to the bureau’s Internet Web site.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 8. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018.)
