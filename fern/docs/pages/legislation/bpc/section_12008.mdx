# BPC § 12008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12008

---

## Text

“Sealer,” when used without qualification, includes the State Sealer, county sealers and their deputies and inspectors.
