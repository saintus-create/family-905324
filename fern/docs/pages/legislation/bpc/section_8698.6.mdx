# BPC § 8698.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14.5. Structural Fumigation Enforcement Program

**Citation:** BPC § 8698.6

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 82, Sec. 1. (AB 307) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 14.5, commencing with Section 8698.)
