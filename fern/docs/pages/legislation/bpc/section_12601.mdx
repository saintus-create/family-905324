# BPC § 12601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 6. Fair Packaging and Labeling Act

**Citation:** BPC § 12601

---

## Text

This chapter is designed to protect purchasers of any commodity within its provisions against deception or misrepresentation. Packages and their labels should enable consumers to obtain accurate information as to the quantity of the contents and should facilitate value comparisons.

It is hereby declared to be the policy of the Legislature to assist in attaining these goals.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1309.)
