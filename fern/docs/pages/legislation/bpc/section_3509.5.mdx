# BPC § 3509.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3509.5

---

## Text

The board shall elect annually a president and a vice president from among its members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 426, Sec. 33. (SB 800) Effective January 1, 2016.)
