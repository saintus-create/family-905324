# BPC § 9887.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 5. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 9887.4

---

## Text

It is unlawful to violate any regulation adopted by the director pursuant to Articles 5, 6, and 7 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1578. Conditionally repealed pursuant to Section 9887.5.)
