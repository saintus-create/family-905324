# BPC § 6104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6104

---

## Text

Corruptly or wilfully and without authority appearing as attorney for a party to an action or proceeding constitutes a cause for disbarment or suspension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
