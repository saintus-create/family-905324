# BPC § 22751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 24. Bakery Trays, Bakery Baskets, and Merchandise Pallets

**Citation:** BPC § 22751

---

## Text

Any person who purchases or leases a bakery tray, bakery basket, or merchandise pallet shall retain a copy of the bill of sale or other evidence that supports the purchase.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 456, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
