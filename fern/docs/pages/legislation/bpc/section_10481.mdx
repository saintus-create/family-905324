# BPC § 10481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 6.5. Real Estate Recovery Program

**Citation:** BPC § 10481

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter limits the authority of the commissioner to take disciplinary action against any licensee for a violation of the Real Estate Law, or of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 11000) of Part 2, or of the rules and regulations of the commissioner; nor shall the repayment in full of all obligations to the Consumer Recovery Account by any licensee nullify or modify the effect of any other disciplinary proceeding brought pursuant to the Real Estate Law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 712, Sec. 32. (SB 706) Effective January 1, 2012.)
