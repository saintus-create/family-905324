# BPC § 7320.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 2. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 7320.5

---

## Text

Any licensee who uses a laser in the treatment of any human being is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 253, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
