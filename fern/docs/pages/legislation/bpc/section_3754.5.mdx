# BPC § 3754.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 5. Suspension, Revocation, and Reinstatement of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 3754.5

---

## Text

The board shall initiate action against any licensee who obtains a license by fraud or misrepresentation. The board shall take action against any licensee whose license was issued by mistake.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1274, Sec. 21.7. Effective January 1, 1995.)
