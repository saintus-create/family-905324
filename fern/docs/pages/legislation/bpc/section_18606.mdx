# BPC § 18606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 18606

---

## Text

The members of the commission shall elect one of their number as the chair and another member as the vice-chair. The chair and vice-chair shall be elected at the first meeting of each calendar year or when a vacancy exists.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
