# BPC § 21508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 1. Watches

**Citation:** BPC § 21508

---

## Text

Any person advertising in any manner secondhand watches for sale shall state clearly in such advertising that the watches so advertised are secondhand watches.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 50.)
