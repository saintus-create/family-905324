# BPC § 17702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Premium Coupons

**Citation:** BPC § 17702

---

## Text

Any person violating this chapter is for each offense guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 64.)
