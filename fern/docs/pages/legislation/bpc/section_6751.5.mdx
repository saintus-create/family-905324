# BPC § 6751.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 6751.5

---

## Text

The board shall by rule establish the criteria to be used for approving curricula of schools of engineering.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2084.)
