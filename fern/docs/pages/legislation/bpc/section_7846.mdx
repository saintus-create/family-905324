# BPC § 7846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7846

---

## Text

An applicant failing in an examination may be examined again upon filing a new application and the payment of the examination fee fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 188, Sec. 12. (SB 826) Effective January 1, 2022.)
