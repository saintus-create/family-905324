# BPC § 4990.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.7. Board of Behavioral Sciences > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4990.01

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, the repeal of Section 4990 renders the board subject to review by the appropriate policy committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 787, Sec. 74. (SB 775) Effective January 1, 2026.)
