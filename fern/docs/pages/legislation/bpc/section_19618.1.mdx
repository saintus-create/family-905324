# BPC § 19618.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 9.5. License Fees, Commissions, and Purses

**Citation:** BPC § 19618.1

---

## Text

Subdivisions (a) and (b) of Section 19618 shall not apply to any payment by a licensed harness racing association in the northern zone, or by any fair, in connection with promotional contests or sponsorship contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 127, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
