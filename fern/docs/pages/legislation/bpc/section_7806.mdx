# BPC § 7806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 7806

---

## Text

A subordinate is any person who assists a professional geologist or professional geophysicist in the practice of geology or geophysics without assuming the responsible charge of work.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 657, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2006.)
