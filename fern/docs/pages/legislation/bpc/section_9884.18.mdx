# BPC § 9884.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 3. Registration Procedure

**Citation:** BPC § 9884.18

---

## Text

Nothing in the provisions of this chapter shall prohibit the bringing of a civil action against an automotive repair dealer by an individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1578.)
