# BPC § 1647.37

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2.87. Use of Pediatric Minimal Sedation

**Citation:** BPC § 1647.37

---

## Text

A pediatric minimal sedation permit shall expire on the date specified in Section 1715 that next occurs after its issuance, unless it is renewed as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 483, Sec. 32. (SB 1453) Effective January 1, 2025.)
