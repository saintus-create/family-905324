# BPC § 18811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18811

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for a boxing, kickboxing, and martial arts trainer’s license, chief second’s and second’s license shall be fifty dollars ($50).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1057, Sec. 11. Effective January 1, 1994.)
