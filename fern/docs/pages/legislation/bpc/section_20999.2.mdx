# BPC § 20999.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 7.5. Franchises

**Citation:** BPC § 20999.2

---

## Text

No petroleum distributor shall deny to any gasoline dealer or prospective gasoline dealer the right to be accompanied by counsel or a personal representative to any meeting held between the parties for the purpose of negotiating the terms of a franchise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 706.)
