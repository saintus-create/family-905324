# BPC § 5624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5624

---

## Text

Each member of the landscape architects committee shall receive per diem and expenses, as provided in Section 103.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 879, Sec. 22.5. Effective January 1, 1999.)
