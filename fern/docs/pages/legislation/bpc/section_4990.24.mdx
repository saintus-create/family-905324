# BPC § 4990.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.7. Board of Behavioral Sciences > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4990.24

---

## Text

The powers and duties of the board, as set forth in this chapter, shall be subject to the review required by Division 1.2 (commencing with Section 473).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2007.)
