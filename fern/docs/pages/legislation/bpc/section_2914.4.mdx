# BPC § 2914.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2914.4

---

## Text

In determining its continuing professional development, the board shall consider including a course in menopausal mental health.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 636, Sec. 3. (AB 2270) Effective January 1, 2025.)
