# BPC § 19031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 19031

---

## Text

The duty of enforcing and administering this chapter, Chapter 3.1 (commencing with Section 19225), and Chapter 20 (commencing with Section 9800) of Division 3 is vested in the chief who is responsible to the director therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 188, Sec. 17. (SB 826) Effective January 1, 2022.)
