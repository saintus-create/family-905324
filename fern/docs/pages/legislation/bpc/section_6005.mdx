# BPC § 6005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6005

---

## Text

Inactive licensees are those licensees who have requested that they be enrolled as inactive licensees or who have been enrolled as inactive licensees as set forth in Section 6007.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 10. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
