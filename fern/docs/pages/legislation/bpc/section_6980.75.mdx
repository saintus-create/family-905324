# BPC § 6980.75

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 8. Disciplinary Proceedings and Administrative Citations

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.75

---

## Text

A record of conviction, or a certified copy thereof, shall be conclusive evidence of conviction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
