# BPC § 7622.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7622.5

---

## Text

An applicant for examination under Section 7622 shall pay the funeral director’s examination fee fixed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1338.)
