# BPC § 6180.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 11. Cessation of Law Practice—Jurisdiction of Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6180.13

---

## Text

An order made pursuant to this article is nonappealable, and shall not be stayed by petition for a writ except as ordered by the superior court or the appellate court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 589.)
