# BPC § 6757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 6757

---

## Text

Applicants who profess to be qualified in more than one branch of engineering shall be required to file an application for each branch in which they wish to be registered.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 896.)
