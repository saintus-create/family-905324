# BPC § 22978.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.6. CIGARETTE AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS LICENSING ACT OF 2003 > Chapter: 3. License for Wholesalers and Distributors of Cigarettes and Tobacco Products

**Citation:** BPC § 22978.8

---

## Text

The board shall include on its Web site the name of any wholesaler or distributor whose license has been suspended or revoked.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 82, Sec. 4. Effective June 30, 2004.)
