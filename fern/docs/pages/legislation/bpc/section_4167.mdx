# BPC § 4167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 11. Wholesalers, Third-Party Logistics Providers, and Manufacturers

**Citation:** BPC § 4167

---

## Text

A wholesaler or third-party logistics provider shall not obtain, by purchase or otherwise, any dangerous drugs or dangerous devices that it cannot maintain, in a secure manner, at the place of business licensed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 507, Sec. 25. (AB 2605) Effective January 1, 2015.)
