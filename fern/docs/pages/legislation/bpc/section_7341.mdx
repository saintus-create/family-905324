# BPC § 7341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 5. Examinations

**Citation:** BPC § 7341

---

## Text

The board shall deliver to every person failing any examination provided for in this chapter the total grade received on the examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 422, Sec. 4. (SB 1492) Effective January 1, 2019.)
