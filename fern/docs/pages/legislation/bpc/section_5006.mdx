# BPC § 5006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5006

---

## Text

The officers of the board shall continue in office until their successors are elected and qualify.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1353.)
