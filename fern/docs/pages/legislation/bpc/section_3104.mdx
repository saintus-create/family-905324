# BPC § 3104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3104

---

## Text

The employing of what are known as “cappers” or “steerers” to obtain business constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3100 by Stats. 2005, Ch. 393, Sec. 16. Effective January 1, 2006.)
