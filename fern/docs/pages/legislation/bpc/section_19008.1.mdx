# BPC § 19008.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19008.1

---

## Text

“Used” means furniture or bedding that has been previously owned or used by another individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 354, Sec. 66. Effective January 1, 2008.)
