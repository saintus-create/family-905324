# BPC § 5361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 6. Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 5361

---

## Text

Each permit provided in this chapter shall carry an identification number and shall entitle the holder to place the advertising display described in the application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 991.)
