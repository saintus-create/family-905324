# BPC § 5082.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 5. Certificates, Information and Records

**Citation:** BPC § 5082.2

---

## Text

A candidate who fails an examination provided for in this article shall have the right to reexamination pursuant to the provisions of this article and regulations adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 909, Sec. 19. Effective September 30, 2004.)
