# BPC § 10201.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 4. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 10201.6

---

## Text

Any person who has qualified in an examination for a real estate license shall file the required application and fee for the license within one year thereafter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1489.)
