# BPC § 7519.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7519.4

---

## Text

(a) The Private Investigator Disciplinary Review Committee shall be provided all evidence used by the bureau in reaching its decision prior to hearing an appeal.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 635, Sec. 4. (AB 921) Effective January 1, 2016. Section operative July 1, 2017, by its own provisions.)
