# BPC § 3000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3000

---

## Text

This chapter constitutes the chapter on optometry. It shall be known and may be cited as the Optometry Practice Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
