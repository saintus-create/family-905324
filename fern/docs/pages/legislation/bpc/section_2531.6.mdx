# BPC § 2531.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2531.6

---

## Text

The Governor has power to remove from office any member of the board for neglect of any duty required by this chapter, for incompetency, or for unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 758, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1998.)
