# BPC § 23367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23367

---

## Text

A still license authorizes the person to whom issued to own or possess the number of stills indicated in the license upon the premises for which issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
