# BPC § 19667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 11. Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 19667

---

## Text

No person shall knowingly have in his possession any altered, forged, or counterfeit parimutuel ticket.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 832.)
