# BPC § 2538.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 8. Hearing Aid Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2538.41

---

## Text

Before setting aside the revocation or suspension of any license or modifying the probation of any licensee, the board may require the petitioner to pass the regular examination given for applicants for licenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 9. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
