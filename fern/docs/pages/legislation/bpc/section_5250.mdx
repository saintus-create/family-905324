# BPC § 5250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5250

---

## Text

The director may make orders and regulations for the enforcement of this chapter and may authorize the Department of Transportation to enforce its provisions.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 681, Sec. 1.)
