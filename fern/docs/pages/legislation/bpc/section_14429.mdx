# BPC § 14429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 3. Trade Names and Designations > Article: 2. Container Brands

**Citation:** BPC § 14429

---

## Text

There shall be included as a part of each brand the words, “Registered in California,” or the abbreviation, “Reg. Cal.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 59.)
