# BPC § 1280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 5. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 1280

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to represent himself or act as a licensed individual under this chapter unless he is, in fact, duly licensed under this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1377.)
