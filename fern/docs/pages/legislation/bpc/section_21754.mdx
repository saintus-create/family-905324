# BPC § 21754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 11. Copyrighted Performances of Musical Works

**Citation:** BPC § 21754

---

## Text

A person who wilfully violates any of the provisions of this act is liable for a civil penalty of five thousand dollars ($5,000) per violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 340, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
