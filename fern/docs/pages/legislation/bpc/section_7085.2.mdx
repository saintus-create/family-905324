# BPC § 7085.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 6.2. Arbitration

**Citation:** BPC § 7085.2

---

## Text

An arbitrator may render an award and that award shall be deemed to be an order of the registrar.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1311, Sec. 2. Effective September 28, 1987.)
