# BPC § 19002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19002

---

## Text

“Person” includes any individual, partnership, association, firm, auctioneer, trust, limited liability company, and corporation, and the agents, servants, and employees of any of them.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 508, Sec. 32. (SB 814) Effective January 1, 2024.)
