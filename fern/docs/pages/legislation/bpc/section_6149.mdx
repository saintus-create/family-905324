# BPC § 6149

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 8.5. Fee Agreements

**Citation:** BPC § 6149

---

## Text

A written fee contract shall be deemed to be a confidential communication within the meaning of subdivision (e) of Section 6068 and of Section 952 of the Evidence Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 475, Sec. 8.)
