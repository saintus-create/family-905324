# BPC § 150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 2. The Director of Consumer Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 150

---

## Text

The department is under the control of a civil executive officer who is known as the Director of Consumer Affairs.
