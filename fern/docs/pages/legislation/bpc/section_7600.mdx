# BPC § 7600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7600

---

## Text

This chapter of the Business and Professions Code constitutes the chapter on cemetery and funeral law. It may be cited as the Cemetery and Funeral Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 4. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
