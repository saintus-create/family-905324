# BPC § 1322

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 7. Denial, Revocation and Suspension of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 1322

---

## Text

The proceedings under this chapter for the suspension or revocation of a license or registration shall be conducted in accordance with Section 100171 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 220, Sec. 7. Effective August 4, 1997.)
