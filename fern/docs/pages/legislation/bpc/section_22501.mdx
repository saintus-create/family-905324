# BPC § 22501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 21. Ticket Sellers

**Citation:** BPC § 22501

---

## Text

A ticket seller shall maintain records of ticket sales, deposits, and refunds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 378, Sec. 1.)
