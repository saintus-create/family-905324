# BPC § 7570.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 7. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7570.1

---

## Text

The fee for processing fingerprints for all registrations and licenses is that amount charged the bureau by the Department of Justice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
