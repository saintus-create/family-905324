# BPC § 19407.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19407.5

---

## Text

“Executive director” means the Executive Director of the California Horse Racing Board.
