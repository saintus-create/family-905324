# BPC § 22941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 30. Billing Practices

**Citation:** BPC § 22941

---

## Text

Any violation of this chapter shall be punishable by a civil penalty of up to two hundred fifty dollars ($250) for each violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 343, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
