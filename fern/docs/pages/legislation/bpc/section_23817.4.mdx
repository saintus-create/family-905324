# BPC § 23817.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 5. Restrictions on Issuance of Licenses > Article: 2. Limitation on Number of Licensed Premises

**Citation:** BPC § 23817.4

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the public welfare and morals require that there be a limitation on the number of premises licensed for the off sale of beer and wine.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 564, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1998.)
