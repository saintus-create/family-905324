# BPC § 496

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. DENIAL, SUSPENSION AND REVOCATION OF LICENSES > Chapter: 5. Examination Security

**Citation:** BPC § 496

---

## Text

A board may deny, suspend, revoke, or otherwise restrict a license on the ground that an applicant or licensee has violated Section 123 pertaining to subversion of licensing examinations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1022, Sec. 3.)
