# BPC § 3097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3097

---

## Text

The sending of a solicitor from house to house or the soliciting from house to house by the holder of an optometrist license constitutes a cause to revoke or suspend his or her license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3096 by Stats. 2005, Ch. 393, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2006.)
