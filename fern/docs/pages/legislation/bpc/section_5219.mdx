# BPC § 5219

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 5219

---

## Text

“Person” includes natural person, firm, cooperative, partnership, association, limited liability company, and corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1995.)
