# BPC § 7319.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 2. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 7319.5

---

## Text

Students engaged in performing services on the public while enrolled in a school approved by the board shall not be required to be licensed under this chapter if they perform those services at the approved school in which they are enrolled.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2004.)
