# BPC § 19052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19052

---

## Text

Every custom upholsterer, unless he or she holds a furniture and bedding manufacturer’s license, shall hold a custom upholsterer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 33. Effective January 1, 1997.)
