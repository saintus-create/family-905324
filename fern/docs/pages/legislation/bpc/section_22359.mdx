# BPC § 22359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 16. Process Servers

**Citation:** BPC § 22359

## Text

Any person who violates any of the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.
