# BPC § 4502.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 4502.1

---

## Text

A psychiatric technician, working in a mental health facility or developmental disability facility, when prescribed by a licensed physician and surgeon, may administer medications by hypodermic injection.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 632, Sec. 35. (AB 1536) Effective January 1, 2022.)
