# BPC § 17902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. Fictitious Business Names

**Citation:** BPC § 17902

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” includes individuals, limited liability companies, partnerships and other associations, and corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 1994.)
