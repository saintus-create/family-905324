# BPC § 6753.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 6753.5

---

## Text

All applicants shall be given equal credit for engineering experience in the armed forces of United States as with any other comparable engineering experience.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
