# BPC § 12011.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12011.7

---

## Text

“Pallet” means any portable platform upon which containers or material is placed to facilitate handling.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 344.)
