# BPC § 6980.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 5. Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.40

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the application for a branch office registration, the chief shall issue a “Branch Office Registration.” The registration shall be posted in a conspicuous place at the branch office location.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
