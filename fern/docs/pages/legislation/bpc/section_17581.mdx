# BPC § 17581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 7. Environmental Representations

**Citation:** BPC § 17581

---

## Text

Any violation of this article is a misdemeanor punishable by imprisonment in the county jail not to exceed six months, or by a fine not to exceed two thousand five hundred dollars ($2,500), or by both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1413, Sec. 2.)
