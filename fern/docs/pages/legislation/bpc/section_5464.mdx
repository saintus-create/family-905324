# BPC § 5464

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 9. Crimes and Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 5464

---

## Text

Every person as principal, agent or employee, violating any of the provisions of this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 991.)
