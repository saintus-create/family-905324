# BPC § 6105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6105

---

## Text

Lending his name to be used as attorney by another person who is not an attorney constitutes a cause for disbarment or suspension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
