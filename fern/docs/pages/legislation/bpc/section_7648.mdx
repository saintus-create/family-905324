# BPC § 7648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 3. Embalmers

**Citation:** BPC § 7648

---

## Text

No embalmer’s license is assignable, and only the licensee may engage in the practice of embalming under the license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 39.)
