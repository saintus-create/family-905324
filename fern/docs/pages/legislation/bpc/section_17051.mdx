# BPC § 17051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 3. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 17051

---

## Text

Any contract, express or implied, made by any person, firm, or corporation in violation of this chapter is an illegal contract and no recovery thereon shall be had.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
