# BPC § 7399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 10. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 7399

---

## Text

Under no circumstances shall a temporary license be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1672, Sec. 3. Operative July 1, 1992, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1672.)
