# BPC § 1904

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 9. Dental Hygienists

**Citation:** BPC § 1904

---

## Text

The dental hygiene board shall meet at least two times each calendar year and shall conduct additional meetings in appropriate locations that are necessary to transact its business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 456, Sec. 7. (SB 786) Effective January 1, 2020.)
