# BPC § 25007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 12. Beer Price Posting and Marketing Regulations

**Citation:** BPC § 25007

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 25000.5, no manufacturer, importer, or wholesaler mentioned in this chapter is prohibited the right of choice of customers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 760.)
