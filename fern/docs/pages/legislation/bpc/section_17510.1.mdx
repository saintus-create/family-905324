# BPC § 17510.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 1.3. Charitable Solicitations

**Citation:** BPC § 17510.1

---

## Text

As used in this article, “sale” shall include a gift made with the hope or expectation of monetary compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1113.)
