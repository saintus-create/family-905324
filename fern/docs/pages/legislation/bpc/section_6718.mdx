# BPC § 6718

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6718

---

## Text

Any member of the board may administer oaths and may take testimony and proofs concerning all matters within the board’s jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
