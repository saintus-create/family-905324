# BPC § 11287

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 2. The Vacation Ownership and Time-Share Act of 2004 > Article: 5. Powers, Investigation, and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 11287

---

## Text

Any person who violates Section 11226, 11227, 11234, 11244, 11245, or 11283, is guilty of a public offense punishable by a fine not to exceed ten thousand dollars ($10,000), by imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 of the Penal Code or in a county jail not exceeding one year, or by both the fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 24. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.)
