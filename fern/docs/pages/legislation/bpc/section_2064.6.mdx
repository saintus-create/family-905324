# BPC § 2064.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 3. License Required and Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 2064.6

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (b) of Section 2064.5, the expiration date for any postgraduate training license that expires after June 1, 2023, and before December 31, 2023, shall be extended to March 31, 2024.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 196, Sec. 3. (SB 143) Effective September 13, 2023.)
