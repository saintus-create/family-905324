# BPC § 21714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 10. Self-Service Storage Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 21714

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall only apply to rental agreements entered into, or extended, or renewed after the effective date of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 439, Sec. 1.)
