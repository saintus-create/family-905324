# BPC § 2403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 18. Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 2403

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 2400 do not apply to physicians and surgeons or doctors of podiatric medicine enrolled in approved residency postgraduate training programs or fellowship programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 515, Sec. 16. (SB 304) Effective January 1, 2014.)
