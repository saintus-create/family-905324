# BPC § 4030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 4030

---

## Text

“Intern pharmacist” means a person issued a license pursuant to Section 4208.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 695, Sec. 28. Effective January 1, 2005.)
