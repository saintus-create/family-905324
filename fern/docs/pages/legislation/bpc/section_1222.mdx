# BPC § 1222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 2. Administration and Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 1222

---

## Text

The department may approve schools that are accredited by the National Accrediting Agency for Clinical Laboratory Sciences.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 510, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1996.)
