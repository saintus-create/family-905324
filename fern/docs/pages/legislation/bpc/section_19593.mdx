# BPC § 19593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 9. Wagering

**Citation:** BPC § 19593

---

## Text

No method of betting, pool making, or wagering other than by the parimutuel method shall be permitted or used by any person licensed under this chapter to conduct a horse racing meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
