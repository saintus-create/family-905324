# BPC § 23018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23018

---

## Text

“Exporter” means any person who sells, delivers, or consigns alcoholic beverages located within this State for delivery, use, or sale without the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
