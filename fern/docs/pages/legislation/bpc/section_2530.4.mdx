# BPC § 2530.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2530.4

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as authorizing a speech-language pathologist or audiologist to practice medicine, surgery, or any other form of healing, except that authorized by Section 2530.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 26, Sec. 33. Effective March 30, 1994.)
