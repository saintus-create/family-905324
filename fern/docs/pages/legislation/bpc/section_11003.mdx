# BPC § 11003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Subdivided Lands > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 11003

---

## Text

“Planned development” has the same meaning as specified in Section 4175 or 6562 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 181, Sec. 4) by Stats. 2013, Ch. 605, Sec. 2. (SB 752) Effective January 1, 2014.)
