# BPC § 19082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 5. Labeling

**Citation:** BPC § 19082

---

## Text

A rubber stamp may be used in lieu of a label, on articles with slip seats having a smooth backing on which the imprint can be legibly and indelibly stamped.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
