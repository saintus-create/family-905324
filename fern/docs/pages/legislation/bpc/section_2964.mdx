# BPC § 2964

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 4. Denial, Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** BPC § 2964

---

## Text

Whenever the board revokes a license, it shall report the action to the National Practitioner Data Bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 787, Sec. 35. (SB 775) Effective January 1, 2026.)
