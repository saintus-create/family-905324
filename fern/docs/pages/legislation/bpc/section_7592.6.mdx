# BPC § 7592.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 7592.6

---

## Text

Any person who knowingly falsifies fingerprints or photographs as required for any provisions of this chapter is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
