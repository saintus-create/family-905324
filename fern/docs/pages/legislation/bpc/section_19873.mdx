# BPC § 19873

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19873

---

## Text

No license may be assigned or transferred either in whole or in part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19859 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 2003.)
