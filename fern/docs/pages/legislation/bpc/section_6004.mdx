# BPC § 6004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6004

---

## Text

Every licensee of the State Bar is an active licensee until as in Section 6007 of this code provided or at the licensee’s request, the licensee is enrolled as an inactive licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 9. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
