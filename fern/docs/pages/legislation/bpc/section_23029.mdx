# BPC § 23029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23029

---

## Text

“Case” or “original case” means a standard box or carton as packed by the manufacturer or wine grower in which packages of alcoholic beverages are shipped or transferred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
