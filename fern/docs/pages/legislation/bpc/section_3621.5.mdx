# BPC § 3621.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3621.5

---

## Text

The board shall meet at least two times each calendar year and shall conduct additional meetings in appropriate locations that are necessary to transact its business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 497, Sec. 25. (SB 1526) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
