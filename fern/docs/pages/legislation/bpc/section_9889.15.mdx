# BPC § 9889.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 8. Lamp and Brake Adjustment Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.15

---

## Text

As used in this article, “station,” “licensed station,” and “licensed adjuster” have the same meaning as defined in Article 6 (commencing with Section 9888.1).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1433, Sec. 9. Conditionally repealed pursuant to Section 9889.19.1.)
