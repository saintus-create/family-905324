# BPC § 22971.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.6. CIGARETTE AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS LICENSING ACT OF 2003 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 22971.2

---

## Text

The board shall administer and enforce the provisions of this division and may prescribe, adopt, and enforce rules and regulations relating to the administration and enforcement of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 890, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
