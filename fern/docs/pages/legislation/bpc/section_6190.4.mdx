# BPC § 6190.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 12. Incapacity to Attend to Law Practice—Jurisdiction of Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6190.4

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 11 (commencing with Section 6180) of Chapter 4 of Division 3 of this code shall apply to the proceeding, whenever possible.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 387.)
