# BPC § 2525.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 25. Recommending Medical Cannabis

**Citation:** BPC § 2525.3

---

## Text

Recommending medical cannabis to a patient for a medical purpose without an appropriate prior examination and a medical indication constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 719, Sec. 5. (SB 643) Effective January 1, 2016.)
