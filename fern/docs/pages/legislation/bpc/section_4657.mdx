# BPC § 4657

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10.7. Music Therapy

**Citation:** BPC § 4657

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be construed to require a music therapist currently employed by the State of California to obtain certification as a Board Certified Music Therapist.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 167, Sec. 1. (AB 1540) Effective January 1, 2020.)
