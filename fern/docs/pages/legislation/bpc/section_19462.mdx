# BPC § 19462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 3. Licenses Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 19462

---

## Text

If any license is suspended or revoked, the board shall state publicly its reasons for the suspension or revocation, and shall cause an entry of such reasons to be made on its minute book.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
