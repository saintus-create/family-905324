# BPC § 7331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 3. Qualifications for Examination

**Citation:** BPC § 7331

---

## Text

The board shall grant a license to practice to an applicant if the applicant submits all of the following to the board:

(a) A completed application form and all fees required by the board.

(b) Proof of a current license issued by another state to practice that meets both of the following requirements:

(1) It is not revoked, suspended, or otherwise restricted.

(2) It is in good standing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 648, Sec. 9. (SB 803) Effective January 1, 2022.)
