# BPC § 7803.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 7803.1

---

## Text

“Geophysicist,” as used in this chapter, refers to a person engaged in the practice of geophysics.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1396.)
