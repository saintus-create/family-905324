# BPC § 6980.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 5. Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.41

---

## Text

Every branch office registration issued under this chapter shall be subject to the same renewal provisions which apply to a license as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
