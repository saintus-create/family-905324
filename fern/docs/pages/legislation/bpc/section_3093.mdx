# BPC § 3093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3093

---

## Text

Before setting aside the revocation or suspension of any optometrist license, the board may require the applicant to pass the regular examination given for applicants for an optometrist license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 473, Sec. 11. (SB 821) Effective January 1, 2014.)
