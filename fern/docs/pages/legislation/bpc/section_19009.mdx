# BPC § 19009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19009

---

## Text

“Retailer” means a person who sells any article of upholstered furniture or bedding or filling materials to a consumer or user of the article as purchased.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
