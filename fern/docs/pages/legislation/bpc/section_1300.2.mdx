# BPC § 1300.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 6. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 1300.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this article, the total fees collected under this chapter shall not exceed the costs incurred by the department for licensing, certification, inspection, or other activities relating to the regulation of clinical laboratories and their personnel.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 201, Sec. 5. (SB 744) Effective October 11, 2009.)
