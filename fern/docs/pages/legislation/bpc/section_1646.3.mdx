# BPC § 1646.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2.75. Use of Deep Sedation and General Anesthesia

**Citation:** BPC § 1646.3

---

## Text

(a) A physical evaluation and medical history shall be taken before the administration of deep sedation or general anesthesia.

(b) Medical history, physical evaluation, deep sedation, and general anesthesia records shall be maintained as required by board regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 483, Sec. 15. (SB 1453) Effective January 1, 2025.)
