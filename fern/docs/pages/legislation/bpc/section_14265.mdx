# BPC § 14265

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Model State Trademark Law > Article: 11. Severability

**Citation:** BPC § 14265

---

## Text

If any provision of this chapter, or the application of such provision to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the remainder of this chapter shall not be affected thereby.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
