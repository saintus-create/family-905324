# BPC § 2726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 2726

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided herein, this chapter confers no authority to practice medicine or surgery.
