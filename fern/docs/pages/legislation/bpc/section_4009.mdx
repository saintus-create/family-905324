# BPC § 4009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4009

---

## Text

The board may not adopt or amend any rule or regulation that thereby would conflict with Section 1186 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 190, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
