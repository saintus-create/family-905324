# BPC § 7596.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 6. Firearms Permits and Permits for Other Deadly and Nonlethal Weapons

**Citation:** BPC § 7596.6

---

## Text

A firearms qualification card does not authorize the holder thereof to carry a pistol, revolver, or other firearm capable of being concealed upon the person in a concealed manner pursuant to Section 26150, 26155, 26170, or 26215 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 178, Sec. 10. (SB 1115) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 107 of Ch. 178.)
