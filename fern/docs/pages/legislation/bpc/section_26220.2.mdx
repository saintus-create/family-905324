# BPC § 26220.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 26220.2

---

## Text

“Member” includes members of associations without capital stock and holders of common stock in associations that are organized with shares of stock.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 107. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
