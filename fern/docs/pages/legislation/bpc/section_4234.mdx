# BPC § 4234

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 17. Continuing Education

**Citation:** BPC § 4234

---

## Text

The board may, in accordance with the intent of this article, make exceptions from the requirements of this article in emergency or hardship cases.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
