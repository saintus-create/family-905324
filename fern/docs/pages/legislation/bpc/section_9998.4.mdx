# BPC § 9998.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 21.5. Foreign Labor Contractors

**Citation:** BPC § 9998.4

---

## Text

No foreign labor contractor shall recruit for employment or cause any minor who is a foreign worker to be employed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1450, Sec. 1.)
