# BPC § 2848

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2848

---

## Text

The board for the purpose of transacting its business shall meet at least twice each year, at times and places it designates by resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1275, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1995.)
