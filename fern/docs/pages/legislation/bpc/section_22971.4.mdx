# BPC § 22971.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.6. CIGARETTE AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS LICENSING ACT OF 2003 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 22971.4

---

## Text

No person is subject to the requirements of this division if that person is exempt from regulation under the United States Constitution, the laws of the United States, or the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 822, Sec. 1. Effective September 27, 2004.)
