# BPC § 5032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5032

---

## Text

“State” when not specifically referring to this State, means any state, territory or insular possession of the United States, or the District of Columbia.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 310.)
