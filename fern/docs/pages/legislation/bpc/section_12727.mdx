# BPC § 12727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 7. Weighmasters

**Citation:** BPC § 12727

---

## Text

The secretary may, at any time, require a vehicle to proceed to the nearest vehicle scale for the purpose of weight verification.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 661, Sec. 47. (SB 1576) Effective January 1, 2013.)
