# BPC § 17550.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 2.7. Travel Consumer Restitution Plan

**Citation:** BPC § 17550.51

---

## Text

The Travel Consumer Restitution Corporation shall not be liable for any consequential damages, or for any punitive damages, in connection with the performance of its restitution function.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1123, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
