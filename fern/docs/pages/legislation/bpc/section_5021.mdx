# BPC § 5021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5021

---

## Text

The members of the enforcement advisory and qualifications committees shall hold office for two years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 415, Sec. 2. (SB 1491) Effective January 1, 2011.)
