# BPC § 22908

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22908

---

## Text

The lien created by this act shall be perfected and shall be effective upon the filing of a notice claim of lien with the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 712, Sec. 14. Effective October 7, 2005.)
