# BPC § 23013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23013

---

## Text

“Winegrower” means any person who has facilities and equipment for the conversion of grapes, berries, or other fruit into wine and is engaged in the production of wine.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 28, Sec. 1. Effective June 6, 2008.)
