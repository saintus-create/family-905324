# BPC § 19132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19132

---

## Text

New or sanitized articles of bedding or materials shall at all times be kept separate from any secondhand or used articles or materials that are unsanitized.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 354, Sec. 68. Effective January 1, 2008.)
