# BPC § 2746.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 2.5. Nurse-Midwives

**Citation:** BPC § 2746.3

---

## Text

Midwife’s certificates issued by the Medical Board of California prior to the effective date of this article shall be renewable only by such board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 886, Sec. 53.)
