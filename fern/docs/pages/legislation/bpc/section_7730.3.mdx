# BPC § 7730.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7730.3

---

## Text

For change of name or of address of licensee on the records of the bureau, the fee shall be twenty-five dollars ($25).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 59. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
