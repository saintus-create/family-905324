# BPC § 17511.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 1.4. Telephonic Sellers

**Citation:** BPC § 17511.7

---

## Text

No seller shall make or authorize the making of any references to its compliance with this article to any prospective or actual purchaser.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1009, Sec. 1.)
