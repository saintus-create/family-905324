# BPC § 23437

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 4. Club Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 23437

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this division, no club license issued under this article shall entitle the holder to any off-sale privileges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1070.)
