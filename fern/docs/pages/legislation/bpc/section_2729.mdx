# BPC § 2729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 2729

---

## Text

Nursing services may be rendered by a student when these services are incidental to the course of study of one of the following:

(a) A student enrolled in a board-approved prelicensure program or school of nursing.

(b) A nurse licensed in another state or country taking a board-approved continuing education course or a postlicensure course.
