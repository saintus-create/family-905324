# BPC § 727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 10.5. Unprofessional Conduct

**Citation:** BPC § 727

---

## Text

The provisions of subdivision (2) of Section 1103 of the Evidence Code shall apply in disciplinary proceedings brought against a licensee for acts in violation of Section 726.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 731 by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 4.)
