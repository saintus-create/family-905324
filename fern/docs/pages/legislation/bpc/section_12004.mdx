# BPC § 12004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12004

---

## Text

“State Sealer” means the chief of the division of the department charged with the enforcement of the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 43.)
