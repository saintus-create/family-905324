# BPC § 21636.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21636.5

---

## Text

No secondhand dealer or coin dealer shall promise a seller of tangible property that the seller may repurchase property sold to the secondhand dealer or coin dealer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 884, Sec. 4.)
