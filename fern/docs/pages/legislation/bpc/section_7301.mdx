# BPC § 7301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7301

---

## Text

This chapter constitutes the chapter on hair, skin, nail care, and electrolysis and may be known and cited as the Barbering and Cosmetology Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1148, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
