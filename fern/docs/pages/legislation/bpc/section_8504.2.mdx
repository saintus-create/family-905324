# BPC § 8504.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 8504.2

---

## Text

“Control” means a pest population management system that utilizes techniques to reduce and maintain pest populations at levels below those causing economic or material injury, or to manipulate the populations to prevent causing such injury.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 572, Sec. 1. (SB 1481) Effective January 1, 2019.)
