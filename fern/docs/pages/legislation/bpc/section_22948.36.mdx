# BPC § 22948.36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 35.5. Connected Devices

**Citation:** BPC § 22948.36

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, an entity that is subject to Chapter 6 (commencing with Section 28200) of Division 12 of the Vehicle Code shall not be subject to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 676, Sec. 2. (SB 50) Effective January 1, 2026.)
