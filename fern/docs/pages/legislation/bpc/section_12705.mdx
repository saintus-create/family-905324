# BPC § 12705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 7. Weighmasters

**Citation:** BPC § 12705

---

## Text

Any change in the legal entity of a licensed weighmaster requires the new legal entity to obtain a weighmaster license before operating as a weighmaster.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 646, Sec. 4.)
