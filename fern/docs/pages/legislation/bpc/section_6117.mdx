# BPC § 6117

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6117

---

## Text

During such disbarment or suspension, the attorney shall be precluded from practicing law.

When disbarred, his name shall be stricken from the roll of attorneys.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
