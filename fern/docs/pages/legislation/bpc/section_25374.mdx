# BPC § 25374

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 14. Seizure and Forfeiture of Property

**Citation:** BPC § 25374

---

## Text

Nothing contained in this chapter applies to common carriers or to an employee acting within the scope of his employment under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
