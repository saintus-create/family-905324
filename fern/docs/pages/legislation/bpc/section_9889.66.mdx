# BPC § 9889.66

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 11. Auto Body Repair Study

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.66

---

## Text

The form for registration pursuant to Section 9884 shall contain sufficient information to enable the Bureau of Automotive Repair to identify all registrants performing automotive collision repair work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 479, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
