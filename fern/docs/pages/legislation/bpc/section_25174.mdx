# BPC § 25174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 13. Labels and Containers > Article: 1. Distilled Spirits

**Citation:** BPC § 25174

---

## Text

The department may seize any distilled spirits sold, served, removed, possessed, delivered, or held in any manner in violation of Sections 25170 to 25173, inclusive.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 447.)
