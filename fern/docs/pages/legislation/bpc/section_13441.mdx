# BPC § 13441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 5. Standards for Spark-Ignition Fuels

**Citation:** BPC § 13441

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to sell any product as, or purporting to be, gasoline or automotive spark-ignition engine fuel, unless the product conforms to the specifications of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 167, Sec. 2.5.)
