# BPC § 5037.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5037.1

---

## Text

Nothing in subdivision (k) of Section 17406 of the Financial Code or subdivision (a) of Section 17406.1 of the Financial Code shall be construed to impair or impede a licensee’s rights, duties, and obligations under Section 5037.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 496, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
