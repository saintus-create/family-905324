# BPC § 7818

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7818

---

## Text

The board, pursuant to the provisions contained in Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, may adopt, amend, or repeal rules and regulations to carry out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 86, Sec. 14. (SB 1171) Effective January 1, 2017.)
