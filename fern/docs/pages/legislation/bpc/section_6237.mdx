# BPC § 6237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 15. Attorney Diversion and Assistance Act

**Citation:** BPC § 6237

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that the authorization of an Attorney Diversion and Assistance Program not be construed as limiting or altering the powers of the Supreme Court of this state to disbar or discipline licensees of the State Bar.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 145. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
