# BPC § 23086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1.5. Administration > Article: 4. Appeals From Decisions of the Department

**Citation:** BPC § 23086

---

## Text

In all cases, the board shall enter its order within 60 days after the appeal is submitted for decision.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 294, Sec. 1. (AB 2921) Effective January 1, 2023.)
