# BPC § 653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 6. Unearned Rebates, Refunds and Discounts

**Citation:** BPC § 653

---

## Text

The word “person” as used in this article includes an individual, firm, partnership, association, corporation, limited liability company, or cooperative association.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
