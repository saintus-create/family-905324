# BPC § 22579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 22. Internet Privacy Requirements

**Citation:** BPC § 22579

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on July 1, 2004.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 829, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 22, commencing with Section 22575.)
