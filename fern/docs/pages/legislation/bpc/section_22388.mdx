# BPC § 22388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 17. Invention Development Services Contracts > Article: 4.5. Remedies and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 22388

---

## Text

Failure to make the disclosure required by Article 4 (commencing with Section 22380) shall render any contract subsequently entered into between the customer and the invention developer voidable by the customer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 967.)
