# BPC § 7415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 13. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7415

---

## Text

Licenses issued under this chapter, unless specifically excepted, shall be issued for a two-year period and shall expire at midnight on the last day of the month of issuance by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 73. Effective January 1, 2004.)
