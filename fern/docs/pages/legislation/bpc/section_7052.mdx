# BPC § 7052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 7052

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any person who only furnishes materials or supplies without fabricating them into, or consuming them in the performance of, the work of the contractor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 90.)
