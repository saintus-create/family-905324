# BPC § 10157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 2. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 10157

---

## Text

No real estate license gives authority to do any act specified in this chapter to any person, other than the person to whom the license is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 127.)
