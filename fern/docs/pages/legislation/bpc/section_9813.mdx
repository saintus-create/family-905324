# BPC § 9813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 9813

---

## Text

The director shall have the powers granted to the head of a department by, and shall conform to the provisions of Article 2 (commencing with Section 11180) of Chapter 2 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1492.)
