# BPC § 12010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12010

---

## Text

“Use,” in any of its variant forms includes keep for use in any of its variant forms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 43.)
