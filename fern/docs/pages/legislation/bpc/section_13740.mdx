# BPC § 13740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 15. Automotive Products > Article: 5. Unlawful Acts

**Citation:** BPC § 13740

---

## Text

It is unlawful to sell or distribute any product referred to in this chapter which is adulterated or mislabeled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 698, Sec. 10.)
