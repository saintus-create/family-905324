# BPC § 5035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5035

---

## Text

“Person” includes individual, partnership, firm, association, limited liability company, or corporation, unless otherwise provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1995.)
