# BPC § 6562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 4. Practice Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6562

---

## Text

The annual statement shall be filed with the bureau 60 days prior to the expiration of the license as provided in subdivision (a) of Section 6541.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 491, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
