# BPC § 5222.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 5222.1

---

## Text

“State highway system” means the state highway system as described in Section 300 of the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 991, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1994.)
