# BPC § 22511

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 21. Ticket Sellers

**Citation:** BPC § 22511

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any nonprofit charitable tax-exempt organization selling tickets to an event sponsored by the organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 378, Sec. 1.)
