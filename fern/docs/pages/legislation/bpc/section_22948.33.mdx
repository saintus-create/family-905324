# BPC § 22948.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 35.5. Connected Devices

**Citation:** BPC § 22948.33

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this chapter is contrary to public policy and void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 676, Sec. 2. (SB 50) Effective January 1, 2026.)
