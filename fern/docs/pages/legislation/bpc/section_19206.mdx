# BPC § 19206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 9. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19206

---

## Text

No person shall interfere with, obstruct or otherwise hinder any inspector of the bureau or of the department’s Division of Investigation in the performance of his or her duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1264, Sec. 90. Effective January 1, 1994.)
