# BPC § 7580.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 7580.14

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as entitling any person to practice law in this state unless he or she is an active member of the State Bar of California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
