# BPC § 2039

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2039

---

## Text

All certificates issued by the board shall state the extent and character of the practice which is permitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
