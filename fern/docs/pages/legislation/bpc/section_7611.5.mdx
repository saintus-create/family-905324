# BPC § 7611.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 1.5. Definitions and Regulation of Cemeteries

**Citation:** BPC § 7611.5

---

## Text

A “crematory manager” is a person engaged in or conducting, or holding himself or herself out as engaged in those activities involved in, or incidental to, the maintaining or operating of a crematory licensed under this chapter, and the cremation of human remains.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 13. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
