# BPC § 7819

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7819

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided by law, all records of the board shall be open to inspection by the public during regular office hours.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 942.)
