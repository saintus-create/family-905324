# BPC § 22120

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 2. The Platinum Group

**Citation:** BPC § 22120

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply exclusively to the following metals and to the alloys of each:

(a) Platinum

(b) Iridium

(c) Palladium

(d) Ruthenium

(e) Rhodium

(f) Osmium.
