# BPC § 18679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 4. Applications

**Citation:** BPC § 18679

---

## Text

The commission may issue temporary permits prior to licensure, as provided by regulations of the commission.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
