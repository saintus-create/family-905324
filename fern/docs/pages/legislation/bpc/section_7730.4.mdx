# BPC § 7730.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7730.4

---

## Text

For transfer of a salesperson’s license on change of employer, the fee shall be fifty dollars ($50).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 60. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
