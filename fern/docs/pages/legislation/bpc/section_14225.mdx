# BPC § 14225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Model State Trademark Law > Article: 5. Records

**Citation:** BPC § 14225

---

## Text

The secretary shall keep for public examination a record of all marks registered or renewed under this chapter, as well as a record of all documents recorded pursuant to Section 14220.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
