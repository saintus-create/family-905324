# BPC § 8637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 8637

---

## Text

Misrepresentation of a material fact by the applicant in obtaining a license or company registration is a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1348, Sec. 62. Operative January 1, 1987, by Sec. 97 of Ch. 1348.)
