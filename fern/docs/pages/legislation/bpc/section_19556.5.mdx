# BPC § 19556.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 7. Charity Racing Days

**Citation:** BPC § 19556.5

---

## Text

The board shall designate a nonprofit organization that is dedicated to research and development of improved safety standards for horse racing as a beneficiary qualified to receive a distribution pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 67. Effective January 1, 2001.)
