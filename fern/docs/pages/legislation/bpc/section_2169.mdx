# BPC § 2169

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 8.5. Special Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 2169

---

## Text

A person who holds a special faculty permit shall meet the continuing medical education requirements set forth in Article 10 (commencing with Section 2190).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 308, Sec. 18. (SB 819) Effective January 1, 2010.)
