# BPC § 12714.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 7. Weighmasters

**Citation:** BPC § 12714.5

---

## Text

All information contained on the certificate shall be clear and legible.

Each certificate shall be numbered consecutively.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 646, Sec. 4.)
