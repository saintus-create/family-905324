# BPC § 7826.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 2.1. Technical Advisory Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 7826.1

---

## Text

Each member of each technical advisory committee shall be appointed by the board and shall serve at the pleasure of the board. Each committee shall be composed of no more than five members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 432, Sec. 25. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2012.)
