# BPC § 9855.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 4.5. Service Contractors

**Citation:** BPC § 9855.5

---

## Text

A service contractor shall comply with the provisions of Sections 1794.4 and 1794.41 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1265, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 9855.9.)
