# BPC § 26225.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 5. Bylaws

**Citation:** BPC § 26225.5

---

## Text

The bylaws may prescribe penalties for violations of the bylaws.
