# BPC § 19056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19056

---

## Text

Every bedding renovator shall hold an upholstered-furniture and bedding manufacturer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 38. Effective January 1, 1997.)
