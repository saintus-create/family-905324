# BPC § 5554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 4. Issuance of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 5554

---

## Text

The certificate shall contain the name of the person to whom issued. Proper index and record of each certificate shall be kept by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1405, Sec. 16.)
