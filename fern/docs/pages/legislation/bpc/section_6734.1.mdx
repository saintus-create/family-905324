# BPC § 6734.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6734.1

---

## Text

Any person practices electrical engineering when he professes to be an electrical engineer or is in responsible charge of electrical engineering work.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 526.)
