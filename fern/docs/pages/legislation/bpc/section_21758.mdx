# BPC § 21758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 11. Copyrighted Performances of Musical Works

**Citation:** BPC § 21758

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to conduct engaged in while enforcing Section 653w of the Penal Code.
