# BPC § 6027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6027

---

## Text

Special meetings of the State Bar may be held at such times and places as the board provides.
