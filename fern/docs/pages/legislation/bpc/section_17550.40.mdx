# BPC § 17550.40

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 2.7. Travel Consumer Restitution Plan

**Citation:** BPC § 17550.40

---

## Text

Each participant is required to comply with the provisions of this article and shall abide by the rules and decisions of the Travel Consumer Restitution Corporation adopted in accordance with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1123, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1995.)
