# BPC § 6734.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6734.2

---

## Text

Any person practices mechanical engineering when he professes to be a mechanical engineer or is in responsible charge of mechanical engineering work.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 526.)
