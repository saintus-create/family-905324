# BPC § 3101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3101

---

## Text

It is unlawful to advertise by displaying a sign or otherwise or hold himself or herself out to be an optometrist without having at the time of so doing a valid unrevoked license from the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3128 by Stats. 2005, Ch. 393, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 2006.)
