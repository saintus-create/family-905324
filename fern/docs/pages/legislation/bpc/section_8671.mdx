# BPC § 8671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 8671

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “renewal operator’s license” means a license issued to an individual who had an operator’s license unrevoked and unsuspended on June 30th of the preceding renewal period.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1348, Sec. 79. Operative January 1, 1987, by Sec. 97 of Ch. 1348.)
