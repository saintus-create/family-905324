# BPC § 865.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 15. Sexual Orientation Change Efforts

**Citation:** BPC § 865.1

---

## Text

Under no circumstances shall a mental health provider engage in sexual orientation change efforts with a patient under 18 years of age.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 835, Sec. 2. (SB 1172) Effective January 1, 2013.)
