# BPC § 8764.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 5. Surveying Practice

**Citation:** BPC § 8764.5

---

## Text

Statements shall appear on the map as follows:Surveyor’s StatementThis map correctly represents a survey made by me or under my direction in conformance with the requirements of the Professional Land Surveyors’ Act at the request of Name of Person Authorizing Surveyin _______, 20__.(Signed and sealed) (Date) L.S. (or R.C.E.) No. County Surveyor’s StatementThis map has been examined in accordance with Section 8766 of the Professional Land Surveyors’ Act this ____ day of ____, 20__.(Signed and sealed) County SurveyorL.S. (or R.C.E.) No. Recorder’s StatementFiled this ____ day of ____, 20__, at ____.m. in Book ____ of ____ at page ____, at the request of ____.(Signed) County Recorder

No other statements may appear on the face of the map except those required or authorized by this article.
