# BPC § 4540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 5. Penal Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4540

---

## Text

After January 1, 1970, no person shall perform services described in Section 4502 without a license issued under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1323.)
