# BPC § 6980.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.31

---

## Text

No license issued pursuant to this chapter shall be transferred to another person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
