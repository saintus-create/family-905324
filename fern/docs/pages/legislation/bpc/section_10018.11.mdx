# BPC § 10018.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10018.11

---

## Text

“Dual agent” means an agent acting, either directly or through a salesperson or broker associate, as agent for both the seller and the buyer in a real property transaction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 19. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
