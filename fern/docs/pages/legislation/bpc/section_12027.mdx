# BPC § 12027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12027

---

## Text

The secretary may make rules and regulations as are reasonably necessary for the purpose of carrying out the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 661, Sec. 21. (SB 1576) Effective January 1, 2013.)
