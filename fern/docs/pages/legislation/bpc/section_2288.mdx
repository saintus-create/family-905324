# BPC § 2288

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2288

---

## Text

The impersonation of any applicant or acting as proxy for any applicant in any examination required under this chapter for a certificate constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
