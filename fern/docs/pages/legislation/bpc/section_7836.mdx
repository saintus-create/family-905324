# BPC § 7836

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 3. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 7836

---

## Text

Officers and employees of the United States of America practicing solely as such officers or employees are exempt from registration under the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 942.)
