# BPC § 7687

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7687

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a complaint, the bureau may make or cause to be made such investigation as it deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 135. Effective January 1, 2001.)
