# BPC § 6086

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 5. Disciplinary Authority of the Board of Governors

**Citation:** BPC § 6086

---

## Text

The board of trustees, subject to the provisions of this chapter, may by rule provide the mode of procedure in all cases of complaints against licensees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 67. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
