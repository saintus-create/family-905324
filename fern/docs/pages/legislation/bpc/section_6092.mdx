# BPC § 6092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 5.5. Miscellaneous Disciplinary Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6092

---

## Text

The State Bar may engage the services of consultants and an unpaid volunteer peer review committee and undertake any other steps that may be appropriate for devising methods for determining and improving attorney competence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 80. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
