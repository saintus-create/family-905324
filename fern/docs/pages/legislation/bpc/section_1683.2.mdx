# BPC § 1683.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 4. Suspension and Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 1683.2

---

## Text

A provider of dental services shall not require a patient to sign an agreement that limits the patient’s ability to file a complaint with the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 865, Sec. 44. (AB 1519) Effective January 1, 2020.)
