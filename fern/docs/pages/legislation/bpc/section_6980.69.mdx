# BPC § 6980.69

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 7. Conduct of Business

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.69

---

## Text

No licensee shall fail in any material respect to complete the installation, repair, opening, or modification of a lock for the price stated in the contract for services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
