# BPC § 19666

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 11. Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 19666

---

## Text

A person shall not alter or counterfeit, or attempt to alter or counterfeit, any parimutuel ticket.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 98, Sec. 3. (AB 280) Effective January 1, 2012.)
