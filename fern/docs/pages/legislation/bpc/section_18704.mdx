# BPC § 18704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 7. Regulation of Contests

**Citation:** BPC § 18704

---

## Text

(a) Subject to subdivision (b), a professional fighter licensed under this chapter shall be entitled to a minimum purse of two hundred dollars ($200) per round fought.

(b) The commission may raise the minimum purse amount by regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 486, Sec. 5. (SB 1456) Effective January 1, 2025.)
