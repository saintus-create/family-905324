# BPC § 2964.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 4. Denial, Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** BPC § 2964.5

---

## Text

The board may require any licensee or registrant it places on probation or whose license or registration it suspends to obtain additional continuing professional development, to pass an examination as specified in Section 2941, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 787, Sec. 38. (SB 775) Effective January 1, 2026.)
