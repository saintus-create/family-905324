# BPC § 23009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23009

---

## Text

“Licensee” means any person holding a license, a permit, a certification, or any other authorization issued by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 437, Sec. 1. Effective September 9, 2004.)
