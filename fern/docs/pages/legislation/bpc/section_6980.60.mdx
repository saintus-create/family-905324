# BPC § 6980.60

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 7. Conduct of Business

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.60

---

## Text

No licensee or employee shall conduct business from any location other than the location for which a license or branch office registration was issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 679, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 2009.)
