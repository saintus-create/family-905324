# BPC § 19008.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19008.2

---

## Text

“Antique” means furniture having special value because of its age, especially a work of art or handicraft that is over 100 years old.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 30. Effective January 1, 1997.)
