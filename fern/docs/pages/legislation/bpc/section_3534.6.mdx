# BPC § 3534.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 6.5. Diversion of Impaired Physician Assistants

**Citation:** BPC § 3534.6

---

## Text

In addition to the criteria and causes set forth in Section 3534.4, the board may set forth in its regulations additional criteria for admission to the program or causes for termination from the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 332, Sec. 72. (SB 1236) Effective January 1, 2013.)
