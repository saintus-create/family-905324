# BPC § 7139.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 8.5. The Construction Management Education Sponsorship Act of 1991

**Citation:** BPC § 7139.9

---

## Text

The board may allocate up to fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000) per year from the Construction Management Education Account for the administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1158, Sec. 1.)
