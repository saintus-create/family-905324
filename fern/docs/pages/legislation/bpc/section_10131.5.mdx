# BPC § 10131.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 1. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 10131.5

---

## Text

A nonresident of California may become a real estate broker by conforming to all of the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 455.)
