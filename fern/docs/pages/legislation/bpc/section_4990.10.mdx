# BPC § 4990.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.7. Board of Behavioral Sciences > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4990.10

---

## Text

The board may conduct research in, and make studies of problems involved in, the maintaining of professional standards among those engaged in the professions it licenses and may publish its recommendations thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2007.)
