# BPC § 3108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3108

---

## Text

When the holder is suffering from a contagious or infectious disease, it constitutes a cause to suspend his or her license during the period of continuance of that disease.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 3097 by Stats. 2005, Ch. 393, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2006.)
