# BPC § 10028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10028

---

## Text

“Trust deed” or “deed of trust” as used in this part includes “mortgage.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 886.)
