# BPC § 5355

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 6. Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 5355

---

## Text

An application for a permit to place a display shall contain a description of the display, including its material, size, and subject and the proposed manner of placing it.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 653, Sec. 10.)
