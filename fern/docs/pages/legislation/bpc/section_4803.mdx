# BPC § 4803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4803

---

## Text

The Governor may, in his judgment, remove any member of the board for neglect of duty or other sufficient cause, after due notice and hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 933.)
