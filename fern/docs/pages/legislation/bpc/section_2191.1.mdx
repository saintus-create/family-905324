# BPC § 2191.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 10. Continuing Medical Education

**Citation:** BPC § 2191.1

---

## Text

The Division of Licensing shall encourage every physician and surgeon to take a course in pharmacology and pharmaceuticals as part of his or her continuing education.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1600, Sec. 2.)
