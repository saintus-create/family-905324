# BPC § 7651.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 3.5. Licenses and Certificates of Authority

**Citation:** BPC § 7651.1

---

## Text

The bureau shall not grant an original cemetery broker’s license to any person who is not a resident of this state. Change of residence to another state shall terminate the license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 19. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
