# BPC § 7504.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 5. Examination

**Citation:** BPC § 7504.2

---

## Text

Examinations and reexaminations shall be given at least once every other month at such places and on such specific dates as the chief may from time to time determine and fix.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
