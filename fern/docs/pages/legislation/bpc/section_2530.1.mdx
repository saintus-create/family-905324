# BPC § 2530.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2530.1

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the practice of speech-language pathology and audiology and hearing aid dispensing in California affects the public health, safety, and welfare and there is a necessity for those professions to be subject to regulation and control.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 3. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
