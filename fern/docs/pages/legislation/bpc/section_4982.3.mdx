# BPC § 4982.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13. Licensed Marriage and Family Therapists > Article: 2. Denial, Suspension, and Revocation

**Citation:** BPC § 4982.3

---

## Text

The proceedings conducted under this article shall be held in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1365, Sec. 4.)
