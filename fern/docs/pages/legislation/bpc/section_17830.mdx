# BPC § 17830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Fixed Term Installment Contracts

**Citation:** BPC § 17830

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be interpreted to prohibit a contract from requiring the return of a good if the fixed term installment contract is terminated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 557, Sec. 1. (AB 483) Effective January 1, 2026.)
