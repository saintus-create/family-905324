# BPC § 22175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 3. Gold

**Citation:** BPC § 22175

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply exclusively to gold and to its alloys.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 319.)
