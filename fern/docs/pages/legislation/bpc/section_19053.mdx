# BPC § 19053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19053

---

## Text

Every upholstered-furniture wholesaler, bedding wholesaler, and upholstered-furniture and bedding wholesaler, unless he or she holds an importer’s license, shall hold a wholesale furniture and bedding dealer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1997.)
