# BPC § 18819

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18819

---

## Text

The fee for a simultaneous telecast permit under Section 18831 shall be fifty dollars ($50) per event.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1057, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1994.)
