# BPC § 2837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 8. Nurse Practitioners

**Citation:** BPC § 2837

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit the current scope of practice of a registered nurse authorized pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 439.)
