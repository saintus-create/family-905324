# BPC § 2564.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.5. Registered Dispensing Opticians > Article: 2. Contact Lens Dispensing

**Citation:** BPC § 2564.6

---

## Text

A registered contact lens dispenser shall comply with the applicable provisions of Section 2541.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 788, Sec. 10. (SB 776) Effective January 1, 2026.)
