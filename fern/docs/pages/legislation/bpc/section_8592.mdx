# BPC § 8592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 5. Renewal of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 8592

---

## Text

Any licensee whose license is under suspension may make application for renewal of his or her license as provided in this article, but the board may not renew his or her license until the suspension has been lifted.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 354, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2008.)
