# BPC § 5806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.9. Interior Designers

**Citation:** BPC § 5806

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit interior design or interior decorator services by any person or retail activity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 333, Sec. 1. (SB 308) Effective January 1, 2014. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 5810.)
