# BPC § 4080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 5. Authority of Inspectors

**Citation:** BPC § 4080

---

## Text

All stock of any dangerous drug or dangerous device or of shipments through a customs broker or carrier shall be, at all times during business hours, open to inspection by authorized officers of the law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 549, Sec. 53. Effective January 1, 1998.)
