# BPC § 19030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 19030

---

## Text

Any reference in California law to the Bureau of Home Furnishings or to the Bureau of Home Furnishings and Thermal Insulation shall be deemed to refer to the Bureau of Household Goods and Services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 578, Sec. 29. (SB 1483) Effective January 1, 2019.)
