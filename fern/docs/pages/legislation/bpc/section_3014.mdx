# BPC § 3014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3014

---

## Text

The board shall elect from its membership a president, a vice president, and a secretary who shall hold office for one year or until the election and qualification of a successor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2005.)
