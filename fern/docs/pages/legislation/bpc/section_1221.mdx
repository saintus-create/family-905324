# BPC § 1221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 2. Administration and Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 1221

---

## Text

The department may employ special examiners, and may make regulations for the conduct of examinations under this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1977, Ch. 579.)
