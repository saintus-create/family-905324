# BPC § 6785

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 6. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6785

---

## Text

The board shall have the power, duty, and authority to investigate violations of the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
