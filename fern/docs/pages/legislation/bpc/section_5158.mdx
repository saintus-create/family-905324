# BPC § 5158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 9. Accountancy Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 5158

---

## Text

Each office of an accountancy corporation engaged in the practice of public accountancy in this state shall be managed by a certified public accountant or public accountant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 92.)
