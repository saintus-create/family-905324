# BPC § 9889.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 10.5. Auto Body Repair

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.51

---

## Text

“Auto body repair shop” means a place of business operated by an automotive repair dealer where automotive collision repair or reconstruction of automobile or truck bodies is performed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 445, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
