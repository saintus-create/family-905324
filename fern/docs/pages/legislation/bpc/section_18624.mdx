# BPC § 18624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 18624

## Text

“Committee” means an advisory committee on medical and safety standards created by this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1095, Sec. 2.)
