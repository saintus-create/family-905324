# BPC § 10001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10001

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specified, the definitions in this chapter apply to the provisions of this part only and do not affect any other provisions of this code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 1. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
