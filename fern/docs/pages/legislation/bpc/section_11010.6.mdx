# BPC § 11010.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Subdivided Lands > Article: 2. Investigation, Regulation and Report

**Citation:** BPC § 11010.6

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not be applicable to subdivided land which is offered or proposed to be offered for sale, lease, or financing by a state agency, including the University of California, a local agency, or other public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 148, Sec. 2. Effective April 5, 1982.)
