# BPC § 7741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 9. Preneed Funeral Arrangements

**Citation:** BPC § 7741

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall apply to cemetery property; cemetery commodities; cemetery service; or merchandise that is delivered as soon as paid for.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1414.)
