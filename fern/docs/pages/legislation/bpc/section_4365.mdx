# BPC § 4365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 21. Pharmacists Recovery Program

**Citation:** BPC § 4365

---

## Text

The board shall contract with one or more qualified contractors to administer the pharmacists recovery program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 621, Sec. 70. Effective January 1, 2006.)
