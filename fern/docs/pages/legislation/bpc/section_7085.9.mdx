# BPC § 7085.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 6.2. Arbitration

**Citation:** BPC § 7085.9

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a complaint referred to arbitration pursuant to Section 7085 is not subject to disclosure to the public until such time as an investigation into an alleged violation of Section 7085.6 has been initiated by the registrar.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1035, Sec. 2.)
