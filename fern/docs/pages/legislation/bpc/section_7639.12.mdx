# BPC § 7639.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2.7. Hydrolysis Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 7639.12

---

## Text

A licensed hydrolysis facility shall not prohibit relatives or the responsible party from viewing the hydrolysis process.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 846, Sec. 4. (AB 967) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2020, pursuant to Section 7639.19.)
