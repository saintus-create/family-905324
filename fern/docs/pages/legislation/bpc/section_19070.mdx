# BPC § 19070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 4. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 19070

---

## Text

This chapter applies to upholstered furniture, bedding and filling materials, and insulation sold in California regardless of its point of origin.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1456, Sec. 7.)
