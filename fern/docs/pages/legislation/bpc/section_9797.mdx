# BPC § 9797

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 19.5. Aircraft Repair

**Citation:** BPC § 9797

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provision to the contrary, upon authorization from the customer as to a specific job, a repairperson may work on an aircraft on a time and materials basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1001, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
