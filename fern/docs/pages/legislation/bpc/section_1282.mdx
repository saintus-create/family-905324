# BPC § 1282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 5. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 1282

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to engage in clinical laboratory practice in a clinical laboratory unless he or she is a duly licensed physician and surgeon or is duly authorized to do so under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 195, Sec. 9. Effective July 9, 1990.)
