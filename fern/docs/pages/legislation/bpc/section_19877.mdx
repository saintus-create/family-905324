# BPC § 19877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 4. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19877

---

## Text

The failure of an owner licensee to file an application for renewal before the date specified in this chapter may be deemed a surrender of the license. A license has not been renewed within the meaning of this section until all required renewal fees have been paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19862.5 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 62. Effective January 1, 2003.)
