# BPC § 2033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2033

---

## Text

“Professional” relates to the art and science of medicine and surgery and to such other arts and sciences as may be included within the field of medicine and surgery.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
