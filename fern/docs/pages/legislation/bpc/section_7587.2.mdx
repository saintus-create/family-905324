# BPC § 7587.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7587.2

---

## Text

Any person who knowingly makes a false statement in his or her application for a license or registration as a security guard is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
