# BPC § 7311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7311

---

## Text

The board shall adopt and use a common seal for the authentication of the board’s records.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1148, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
