# BPC § 4842.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 2.5. Registered Veterinary Technicians

**Citation:** BPC § 4842.2

---

## Text

All funds collected by the board under this article shall be deposited in the California Veterinary Medical Board Contingent Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 497, Sec. 61. (SB 1526) Effective January 1, 2025.)
