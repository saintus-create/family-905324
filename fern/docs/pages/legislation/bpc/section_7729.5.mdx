# BPC § 7729.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7729.5

---

## Text

The annual renewal fee for a cemetery broker’s license shall five hundred sixty dollars ($560).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 50. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
