# BPC § 8790

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 7. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 8790

---

## Text

The board shall enforce all of the provisions of this chapter and cause the prosecution of all violations coming to its notice.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 41.)
