# BPC § 25358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 14. Seizure and Forfeiture of Property

**Citation:** BPC § 25358

---

## Text

The department may order the alcoholic beverages or other property seized disposed of, or returned to the petitioner if illegally or erroneously seized.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1955, Ch. 447.)
