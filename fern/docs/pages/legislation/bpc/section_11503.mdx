# BPC § 11503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 2. Certified Common Interest Development Manager

**Citation:** BPC § 11503

---

## Text

A “certified common interest development manager” does not include a common interest development management firm.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1116, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2003.)
