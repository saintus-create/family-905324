# BPC § 5653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 4. Issuance of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 5653

---

## Text

The board may deny or refuse to issue a license to an applicant upon proof of the commission by the applicant of any act or omission which would constitute grounds for disciplinary action under this chapter if committed by a licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 879, Sec. 22.14. Effective January 1, 1999.)
