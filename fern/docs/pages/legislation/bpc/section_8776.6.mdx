# BPC § 8776.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 5.7. Reporting Requirements

**Citation:** BPC § 8776.6

---

## Text

The board may adopt regulations to further define the reporting requirements of Sections 8776, 8776.1, and 8776.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 691, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 2005. Section conditionally operative on January 1, 2008, pursuant to Section 8776.7.)
