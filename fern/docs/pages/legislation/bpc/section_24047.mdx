# BPC § 24047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Transfer of Licenses > Article: 4. Issuance and Renewal of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 24047

---

## Text

Whenever a license certificate is in effect and is lost or destroyed, the department shall issue a duplicate license upon the payment of a fee of twenty-five dollars ($25).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 29, Sec. 60. (SB 82) Effective June 27, 2019.)
