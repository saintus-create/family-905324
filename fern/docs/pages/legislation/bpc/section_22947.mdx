# BPC § 22947

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 32. Consumer Protection Against Computer Spyware Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22947

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known as and may be cited as the Consumer Protection Against Computer Spyware Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 843, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2005.)
