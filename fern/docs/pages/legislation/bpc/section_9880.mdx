# BPC § 9880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 9880

---

## Text

This chapter constitutes the chapter on automotive repair dealers. It may be cited as the Automotive Repair Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1578.)
