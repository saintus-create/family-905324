# BPC § 11008

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Subdivided Lands > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 11008

---

## Text

No provision of this part which makes a violation of this part a crime shall be construed to preclude application of any other criminal provision of the law of this state to an act or omission which constitutes a violation of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 57, Sec. 2.)
