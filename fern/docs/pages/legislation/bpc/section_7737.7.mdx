# BPC § 7737.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 9. Preneed Funeral Arrangements

**Citation:** BPC § 7737.7

---

## Text

A trustee may deposit the corpus of the trust in any credit union which is insured by the National Credit Union Share Insurance Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 764, Sec. 1.)
