# BPC § 5035.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5035.4

---

## Text

“Comparable licensure requirements” means another state requires passage of the examination described in Section 5082 and has education and experience requirements, when considered collectively, that meet or exceed the standards established by the board pursuant to Article 5 (commencing with Section 5080).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 293, Sec. 1. (AB 1175) Effective January 1, 2026.)
