# BPC § 5678.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 5. Discipline

**Citation:** BPC § 5678.3

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a licensee shall not be considered to have violated a confidential settlement agreement or other confidential agreement by providing a report to the board as required by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 564, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2007.)
