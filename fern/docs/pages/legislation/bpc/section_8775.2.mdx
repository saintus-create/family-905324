# BPC § 8775.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 5.5. Photogrammetry

**Citation:** BPC § 8775.2

---

## Text

Maps, documents, or reports prepared by, or under the direction of, a licensed photogrammetric surveyor shall carry his signature and certificate number which will indicate his responsibility for the work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1967.)
