# BPC § 19062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19062

---

## Text

A license issued by the bureau shall be posted in a conspicuous place in the main office or principal place of business of the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 405, Sec. 45. Effective January 1, 2003.)
