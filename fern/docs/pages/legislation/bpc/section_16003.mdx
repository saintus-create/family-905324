# BPC § 16003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Licensing by Cities

**Citation:** BPC § 16003

---

## Text

This article does not repeal any act vesting municipal corporations with power to license for revenue purposes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 61.)
