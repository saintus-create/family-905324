# BPC § 11418.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 8. Real Estate Appraisers Regulation Fund

**Citation:** BPC § 11418.4

---

## Text

Any sums received by the bureau pursuant to any provisions of this chapter shall be deposited in the State Treasury and credited to the Recovery Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 786, Sec. 60. (SB 774) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 11420.)
