# BPC § 8711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8711

---

## Text

The executive officer of the board shall keep a complete record of all applications for license and the board’s action thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 47, Sec. 80. Effective March 21, 1984.)
