# BPC § 23027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23027

---

## Text

“Wholesale sale” or “sale at wholesale” means a sale to any licensee for purposes of resale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
