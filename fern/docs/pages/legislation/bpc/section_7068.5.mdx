# BPC § 7068.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 5. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 7068.5

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person other than the examinee named in the application to take the qualifying examination on behalf of an applicant for a contractor’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 491.)
