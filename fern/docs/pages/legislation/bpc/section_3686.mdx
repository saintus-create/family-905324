# BPC § 3686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 9. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3686

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 414, Sec. 9. (AB 2685) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 8.2, commencing with Section 3610.)
