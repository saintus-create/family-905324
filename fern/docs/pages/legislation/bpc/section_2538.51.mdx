# BPC § 2538.51

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 8. Hearing Aid Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2538.51

---

## Text

It is unlawful to engage in the practice of fitting or selling hearing aids without the licensee having and maintaining an established business address, routinely open for service to their clients.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 510, Sec. 20. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2024.)
