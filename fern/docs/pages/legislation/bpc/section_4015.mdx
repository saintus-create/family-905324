# BPC § 4015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 4015

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the definitions of the terms in this article shall govern the construction of this chapter, unless otherwise indicated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
