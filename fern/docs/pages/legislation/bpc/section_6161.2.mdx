# BPC § 6161.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 10. Law Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 6161.2

---

## Text

All fees for registration and renewal paid pursuant to Sections 6161 and 6161.1 shall be paid into the treasury of the State Bar and shall be used for its regulatory and disciplinary purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 2, Sec. 5. (SB 55) Effective January 25, 2010.)
