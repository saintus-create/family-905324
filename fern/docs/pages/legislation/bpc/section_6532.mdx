# BPC § 6532

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 6532

---

## Text

Only a person who holds a current and active license from the bureau may identify themselves as a “licensed professional fiduciary.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 80, Sec. 9. (SB 1525) Effective January 1, 2025.)
