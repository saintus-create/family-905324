# BPC § 19443

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19443

---

## Text

The Attorney General and every district attorney shall enforce this chapter in their capacities as law enforcement officers.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19442 by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1080.)
