# BPC § 16702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Combinations in Restraint of Trade > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 16702

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “person” or “persons” includes corporations, firms, partnerships and associations existing under or authorized by the laws of this State or any other State, or any foreign country.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
