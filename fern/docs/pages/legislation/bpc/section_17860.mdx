# BPC § 17860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Fixed Term Installment Contracts

**Citation:** BPC § 17860

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this chapter shall be deemed contrary to public policy and shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 557, Sec. 1. (AB 483) Effective January 1, 2026.)
