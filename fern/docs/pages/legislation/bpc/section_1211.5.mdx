# BPC § 1211.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 1211.5

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “cytological slides” shall refer to cellular materials submitted for preliminary cytologic examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 927, Sec. 1.5.)
