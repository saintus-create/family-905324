# BPC § 19201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 9. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19201

---

## Text

The chief or any inspector may determine the fitness of any secondhand or damaged article of bedding or filling material, for sanitization and sale.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1448, Sec. 27.)
