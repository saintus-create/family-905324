# BPC § 1627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2. Admission and Practice

**Citation:** BPC § 1627

---

## Text

The license of any dentist, existing at the time of the passage of this chapter, shall continue in force until it expires or is forfeited in the manner provided by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 380, Sec. 7.)
