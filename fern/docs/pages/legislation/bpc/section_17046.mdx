# BPC § 17046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 3. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 17046

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to use any threat, intimidation, or boycott, to effectuate any violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
