# BPC § 16112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.1. Business License Taxation

**Citation:** BPC § 16112

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that protecting the privacy of a business’s tax payments and gross receipts is a matter of statewide interest and concern.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 388, Sec. 1.)
