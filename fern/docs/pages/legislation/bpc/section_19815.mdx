# BPC § 19815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 19815

---

## Text

(a) The members of the commission shall receive the salary provided for by Section 11553.5 of the Government Code.

(b) The chairperson of the commission shall receive the salary provided for by Section 11553 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19814A by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2003.)
