# BPC § 2814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 6. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 2814

---

## Text

All money in the Board of Registered Nursing Fund shall be available, upon appropriation by the Legislature, to carry out this chapter and the promotion of nursing education in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 520, Sec. 8. (SB 799) Effective January 1, 2018.)
