# BPC § 3534.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 6.5. Diversion of Impaired Physician Assistants

**Citation:** BPC § 3534.7

---

## Text

All board and committee records and records of proceedings and participation of a physician assistant in a program shall be confidential and are not subject to discovery or subpoena.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 332, Sec. 73. (SB 1236) Effective January 1, 2013.)
