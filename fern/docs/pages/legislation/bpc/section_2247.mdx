# BPC § 2247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2247

---

## Text

(a) A licensee shall meet the requirements set forth in subdivision (f) of Section 1031 of the Government Code prior to performing either of the following:

(1) An evaluation of a peace officer applicant’s emotional and mental condition.

(2) An evaluation of a peace officer’s fitness for duty.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2005.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 777, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004. Section operative January 1, 2005, by its own provisions.)
