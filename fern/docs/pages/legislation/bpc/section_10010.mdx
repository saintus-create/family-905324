# BPC § 10010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10010

---

## Text

“Provisions of this part relating to hearings” means the provisions of Article 3 of Chapter 2 of Part 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 177, Sec. 6. (AB 685) Effective January 1, 2017.)
