# BPC § 23033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23033

---

## Text

“Proof gallon” means a gallon of proof spirits or an equivalent amount of alcohol.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
