# BPC § 7652.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 3.5. Licenses and Certificates of Authority

**Citation:** BPC § 7652.10

---

## Text

The bureau may require such proof as it deems advisable concerning the compliance by such applicant to all the laws, rules, regulations, ordinances and orders applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 19. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
