# BPC § 7567

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7567

---

## Text

The bureau may require a licensee who violates any provision of this chapter to do either of the following:

(a) Satisfactorily complete relevant coursework as determined by the bureau.

(b) Retake the licensing examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 309, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
