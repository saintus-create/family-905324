# BPC § 19127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19127

---

## Text

The form, size and color of sanitization labels, and the material of which they are made shall be approved by the chief and as provided in the Label Regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1448, Sec. 19.)
