# BPC § 471.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 8. Dispute Resolution Programs > Article: 7. Rules and Regulations

**Citation:** BPC § 471.3

---

## Text

The rules and regulations adopted by the advisory council pursuant to Section 471 shall be formulated to promote statewide uniformity with the guidelines contained in those rules and regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 28, Sec. 6. Effective May 28, 1987.)
