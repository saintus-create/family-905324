# BPC § 14260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Model State Trademark Law > Article: 10. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 14260

---

## Text

Unless specified by the secretary, the fees payable herein are not refundable.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
