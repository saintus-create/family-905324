# BPC § 7737.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 9. Preneed Funeral Arrangements

**Citation:** BPC § 7737.5

---

## Text

A trustee may deposit the corpus of the trust in any financial institution insured by the Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 970, Sec. 91. Effective January 1, 1999.)
