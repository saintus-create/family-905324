# BPC § 5501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 5501

---

## Text

This chapter constitutes the chapter on professional architects. It shall be known and may be cited as the Architects Practice Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 566, Sec. 2.)
