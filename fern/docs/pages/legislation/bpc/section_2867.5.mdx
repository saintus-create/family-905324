# BPC § 2867.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 2867.5

---

## Text

Every person to whom a license is issued shall, as a condition precedent to its issuance and in addition to any other fee that may be payable, pay the initial license fee prescribed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 586, Sec. 9. (AB 1229) Effective January 1, 2018.)
