# BPC § 3664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 6. Offenses and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 3664

---

## Text

A person who violates Section 3660 or 3661 is guilty of a misdemeanor, and upon conviction shall be punished by a fine of not more than five thousand dollars ($5,000), or by imprisonment of not more than one year in a county jail, or by both that fine and imprisonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 485, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
