# BPC § 26222.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 26222.3

---

## Text

An association that is organized pursuant to this chapter shall not conspire in restraint of trade, or serve as an illegal monopoly, attempt to lessen competition, or to fix prices in violation of law of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 107. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
