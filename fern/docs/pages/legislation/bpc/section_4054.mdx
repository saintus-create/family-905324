# BPC § 4054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 3. Scope of Practice and Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 4054

---

## Text

Section 4051 shall not apply to a manufacturer or wholesaler that provides dialysis drugs and devices directly to patients.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 857, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2005.)
