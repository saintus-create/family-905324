# BPC § 3775.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 7. Fiscal Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3775.5

---

## Text

The fee for an inactive license shall be the same as the renewal fee for the practice of respiratory care as specified in Section 3775.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 830, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1997.)
