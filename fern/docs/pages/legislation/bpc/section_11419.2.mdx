# BPC § 11419.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 8. Real Estate Appraisers Regulation Fund

**Citation:** BPC § 11419.2

---

## Text

The failure of an aggrieved person to comply with this chapter shall constitute a waiver of any rights hereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 786, Sec. 63. (SB 774) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 11420.)
