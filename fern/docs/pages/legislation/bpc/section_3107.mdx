# BPC § 3107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 5. Revocation and Suspension

**Citation:** BPC § 3107

---

## Text

It is unlawful to use or attempt to use any license or certificate issued by the board that has been purchased, fraudulently issued, counterfeited, or issued by mistake, as a valid license or certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 473, Sec. 15. (SB 821) Effective January 1, 2014.)
