# BPC § 4141

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 9. Hypodermic Needles and Syringes

**Citation:** BPC § 4141

---

## Text

No person shall furnish hypodermic needles or syringes, by sale or otherwise, without a license issued by the board, except as otherwise provided by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
