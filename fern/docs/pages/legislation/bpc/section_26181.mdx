# BPC § 26181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 18. License Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 26181

---

## Text

The State Water Resources Control Board, the Department of Fish and Wildlife, and other agencies may establish fees to cover the costs of their cannabis programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 98. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017. Note: This section was added on Nov. 8, 2016, by initiative Prop. 64.)
