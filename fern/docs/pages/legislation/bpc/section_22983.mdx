# BPC § 22983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.6. CIGARETTE AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS LICENSING ACT OF 2003 > Chapter: 5. Inspections, Prohibitions, and Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 22983

---

## Text

The provisions of Chapter 4 (commencing with Section 55121) of Part 30 of Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code apply with respect to the collection of the fees, civil fines, and penalties imposed pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 82, Sec. 8. Effective June 30, 2004.)
