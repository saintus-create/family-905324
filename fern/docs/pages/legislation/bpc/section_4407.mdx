# BPC § 4407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 23. Revenue and Renewal

**Citation:** BPC § 4407

---

## Text

All compensation of members and all other expenses of the board shall be paid out of the examination and registration fees and fines.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
