# BPC § 8011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Shorthand Reporters > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8011

---

## Text

The board shall promulgate, by regulation, a definition of a “full-time student” for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 615, Sec. 11. Effective October 9, 2001.)
