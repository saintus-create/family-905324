# BPC § 22912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22912

---

## Text

The notice of claim of lien shall be filed, indexed, and marked in the office of the Secretary of State in the same manner as a financing statement is filed, indexed, and marked pursuant to Sections 9516 and 9519 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 1.2. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
