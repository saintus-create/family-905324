# BPC § 1647.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2.86. Use of Oral Conscious Sedation for Adult Patients

**Citation:** BPC § 1647.24

---

## Text

Any office in which oral conscious sedation of adult patients is conducted pursuant to this article shall, unless otherwise provided by law, meet the facilities and equipment standards set forth by the board in regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 539, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2006.)
