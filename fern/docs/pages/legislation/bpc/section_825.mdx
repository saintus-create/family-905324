# BPC § 825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 12.5. Mental Illness or Physical Illness

**Citation:** BPC § 825

---

## Text

As used in this article with reference to persons holding licenses as physicians and surgeons, “licensing agency” means a panel of the Division of Medical Quality.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1267, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1994.)
