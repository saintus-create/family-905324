# BPC § 3620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3620

---

## Text

The board shall enforce and administer this chapter and shall be solely responsible for the implementation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 497, Sec. 23. (SB 1526) Effective January 1, 2025. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
