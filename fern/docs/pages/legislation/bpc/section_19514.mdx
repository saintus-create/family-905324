# BPC § 19514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 5. Stewards and Racing Officials

**Citation:** BPC § 19514

---

## Text

Racing officials shall be licensed by the board pursuant to rules and regulations that the board may adopt, and upon payment of a license fee fixed and determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 871, Sec. 8.)
