# BPC § 10018.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10018.13

---

## Text

“Appraiser” means a person licensed or certified under Part 3 (commencing with Section 11300).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 20. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
