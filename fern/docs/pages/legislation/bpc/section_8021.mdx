# BPC § 8021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Shorthand Reporters > Article: 3. Application, Examination, and Certificate; Requirement and Contents

**Citation:** BPC § 8021

---

## Text

Examinations shall be held at least semiannually, and at such times and places as the board may designate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 191.)
