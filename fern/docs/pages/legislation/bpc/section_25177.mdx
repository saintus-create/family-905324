# BPC § 25177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 13. Labels and Containers > Article: 1. Distilled Spirits

**Citation:** BPC § 25177

---

## Text

Every person who sells, offers for sale, or keeps for sale distilled spirits in any package which has been refilled or partly refilled is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
