# BPC § 19820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 19820

---

## Text

The commission may employ not more than eight attorneys. Nothing in this section shall be deemed to exempt the commission from the operation of Section 11040, 11042, or 11043 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19818A by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2003.)
