# BPC § 22757.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 25.1. Transparency in Frontier Artificial Intelligence Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22757.16

---

## Text

The loss of value of equity does not count as damage to or loss of property for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 138, Sec. 2. (SB 53) Effective January 1, 2026.)
