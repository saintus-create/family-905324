# BPC § 1617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 1617

---

## Text

A copy of any part or all of the books of the board duly certified by the executive officer shall be primary evidence in any court of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 47, Sec. 6. Effective March 21, 1984.)
