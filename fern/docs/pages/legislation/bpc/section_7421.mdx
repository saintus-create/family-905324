# BPC § 7421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 13. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7421

---

## Text

The fees shall be set by the board, within the limits set forth in this article, in amounts necessary to cover the expenses of the board in performing its duties under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 75. Effective January 1, 2004.)
