# BPC § 18732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 7. Regulation of Contests

**Citation:** BPC § 18732

---

## Text

Before starting a contest, the referee shall ascertain from each contestant, the name of his or her chief second, and shall hold such chief second responsible for the conduct of assistants during the contest.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
