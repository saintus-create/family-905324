# BPC § 12242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2.1. Fees and Charges

**Citation:** BPC § 12242

---

## Text

The revenue from any fee charged pursuant to Section 12240 shall be deposited in the general fund of the county and used solely for the activities required by Sections 12210 and 12211.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1050, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, pursuant to Section 12246.)
