# BPC § 7068.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 5. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 7068.7

---

## Text

Any person who obtains and provides for another the qualifying examination, or any part thereof, when not authorized to do so, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1013.)
