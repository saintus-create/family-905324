# BPC § 1058

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 2. Chiropractors > Article: 2. Chiropractic Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 1058

## Text

Moneys received by the board pursuant to this article shall be used to carry out the purposes of this article.
