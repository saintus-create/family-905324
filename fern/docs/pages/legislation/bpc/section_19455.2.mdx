# BPC § 19455.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2.5. Backstretch Worker Labor Relations

**Citation:** BPC § 19455.2

---

## Text

(a) The board shall provide for labor agreements under this article to be binding upon every applicable licensee.

(b) No horseman or trainer who has a separate agreement with the exclusive representative labor union shall be required to be a party to a multiemployer collective bargaining agreement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 198, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2002.)
