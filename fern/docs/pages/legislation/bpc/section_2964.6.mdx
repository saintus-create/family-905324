# BPC § 2964.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 4. Denial, Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** BPC § 2964.6

---

## Text

The board may require any licensee or registrant it places on probation to pay the monetary costs associated with probation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 787, Sec. 40. (SB 775) Effective January 1, 2026.)
