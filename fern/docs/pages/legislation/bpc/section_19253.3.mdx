# BPC § 19253.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3.1. Household Movers Act > Article: 4. Rates

**Citation:** BPC § 19253.3

---

## Text

The bureau shall make any rules that are necessary to the application and enforcement of the rates established or approved pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 8. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018.)
