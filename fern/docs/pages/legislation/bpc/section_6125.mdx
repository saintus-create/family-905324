# BPC § 6125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 7. Unlawful Practice of Law

**Citation:** BPC § 6125

---

## Text

No person shall practice law in California unless the person is an active licensee of the State Bar.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 89. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
