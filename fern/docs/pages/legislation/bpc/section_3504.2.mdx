# BPC § 3504.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3504.2

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the repeal of Section 3504 renders the board subject to review by the appropriate policy committee of the Legislature.

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 194, Sec. 11. (AB 1501) Effective January 1, 2026.)
