# BPC § 2018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2018

---

## Text

The board may adopt, amend, or repeal, in accordance with the provisions of the Administrative Procedure Act, those regulations as may be necessary to enable it to carry into effect the provisions of law relating to the practice of medicine.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 678, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2008.)
