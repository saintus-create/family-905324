# BPC § 13535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 12. Price Sign Advertising

**Citation:** BPC § 13535

---

## Text

If any motor vehicle fuel or lubricant is advertised for sale, but not under any brand designation, the words “no brand” shall be used on the advertising medium as a brand designation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 591, Sec. 42. (AB 808) Effective January 1, 2016.)
