# BPC § 19961.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 13. Local Governments

**Citation:** BPC § 19961.05

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 19961 and 19962, a city, county, or city and county may amend its ordinance to increase the operating hours of a gambling establishment to up to 24 hours a day, seven days a week.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 244, Sec. 1. (SB 654) Effective January 1, 2018.)
