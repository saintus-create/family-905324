# BPC § 6106.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6106.1

---

## Text

Advocating the overthrow of the Government of the United States or of this State by force, violence, or other unconstitutional means, constitutes a cause for disbarment or suspension.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 179.)
