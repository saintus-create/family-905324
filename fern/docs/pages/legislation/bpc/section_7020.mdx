# BPC § 7020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7020

## Text

The board shall maintain a computerized enforcement tracking system for consumer complaints.
