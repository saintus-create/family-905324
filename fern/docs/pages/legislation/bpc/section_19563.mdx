# BPC § 19563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 8. Regulation Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 19563

---

## Text

The board may adopt any rules and regulations of the United States Trotting Association, not inconsistent with this chapter, for the regulation of harness racing.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
