# BPC § 18607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 18607

---

## Text

Each member of the commission shall receive a per diem and necessary travel expenses as provided in Section 103.
