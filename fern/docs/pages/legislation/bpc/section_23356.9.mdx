# BPC § 23356.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23356.9

---

## Text

A wine blender’s license does not authorize winetasting activities or the conducting or sponsoring of wine tastings either on or off the wine blender’s licensed premises.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 463, Sec. 4. (AB 1425) Effective January 1, 2014.)
