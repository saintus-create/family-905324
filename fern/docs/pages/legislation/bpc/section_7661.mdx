# BPC § 7661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 4. Apprentices

**Citation:** BPC § 7661

---

## Text

An application for registration as an embalmer’s apprentice shall be made upon a form provided by the bureau, verified by the applicant and accompanied by the fee fixed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 121. Effective January 1, 2001.)
