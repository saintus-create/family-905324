# BPC § 6726.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6726.3

---

## Text

All the members of each technical advisory committee shall serve without compensation but shall receive per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1134, Sec. 1.5.)
