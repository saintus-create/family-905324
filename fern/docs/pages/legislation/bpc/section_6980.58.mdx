# BPC § 6980.58

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 7. Conduct of Business

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.58

---

## Text

A licensee shall at all times be responsible for those actions of his or her employees performed in violation of this chapter, when acting within the course and scope of his or her employment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
