# BPC § 2538.44

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 8. Hearing Aid Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2538.44

---

## Text

It is unlawful to purchase or procure by barter any license issued by the board with intent to use the same as evidence of the holder’s qualification to practice the fitting or selling of hearing aids.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 9. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
