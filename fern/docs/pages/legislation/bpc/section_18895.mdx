# BPC § 18895

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Miller-Ayala Athlete Agents Act.
