# BPC § 9998.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 21.5. Foreign Labor Contractors

**Citation:** BPC § 9998.11

---

## Text

The Labor Commissioner may adopt regulations or policies and procedures to implement the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 711, Sec. 10. (SB 477) Effective January 1, 2015.)
