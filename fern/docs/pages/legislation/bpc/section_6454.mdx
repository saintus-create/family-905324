# BPC § 6454

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5.6. Paralegals

**Citation:** BPC § 6454

---

## Text

The terms “paralegal,” “legal assistant,” “attorney assistant,” “freelance paralegal,” “independent paralegal,” and “contract paralegal” are synonymous for purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 439, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
