# BPC § 7705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7705

---

## Text

Fraud or misrepresentation in obtaining a license constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 39.)
