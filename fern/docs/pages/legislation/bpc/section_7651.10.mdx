# BPC § 7651.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 3.5. Licenses and Certificates of Authority

**Citation:** BPC § 7651.10

---

## Text

A cemetery license does not give authority to do any act specified in this act to any person other than the person to whom the license is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 19. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
