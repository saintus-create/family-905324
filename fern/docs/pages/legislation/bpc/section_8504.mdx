# BPC § 8504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 8504

---

## Text

“Person” includes an individual, firm, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, association or other organization or any combination thereof.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1995.)
