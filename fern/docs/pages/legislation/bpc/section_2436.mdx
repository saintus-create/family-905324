# BPC § 2436

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 20. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 2436

---

## Text

The fees in this article fixed by the board shall be set forth as emergency regulations duly adopted by the Division of Licensing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1267, Sec. 34. Effective January 1, 1994.)
