# BPC § 26306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 25. Interstate Cannabis Agreements > Article: 2. Agreements

**Citation:** BPC § 26306

---

## Text

An agreement shall provide for collection of all applicable taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 396, Sec. 3. (SB 1326) Effective January 1, 2023.)
