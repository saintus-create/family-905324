# BPC § 7085.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 6.2. Arbitration

**Citation:** BPC § 7085.7

---

## Text

A complainant may enforce an arbitrator’s award in accordance with Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 1285) of Title 9 of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 1311, Sec. 8. Effective September 28, 1987.)
