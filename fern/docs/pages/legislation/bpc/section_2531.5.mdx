# BPC § 2531.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2531.5

---

## Text

The board shall issue, suspend, and revoke licenses and approvals to practice speech-language pathology and audiology as authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 758, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1998.)
