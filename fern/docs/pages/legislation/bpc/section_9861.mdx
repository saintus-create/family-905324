# BPC § 9861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 5. Informal Adjustment of Complaints

**Citation:** BPC § 9861

---

## Text

If the complaint does not appear to state any violations of this chapter or of the regulations made pursuant to this chapter, the director shall so advise the complainant and take no further action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1492.)
