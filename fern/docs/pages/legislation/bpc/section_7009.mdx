# BPC § 7009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7009

---

## Text

Any member or committee of the board may administer oaths and may take testimony and proofs concerning all matters within the jurisdiction of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 37.)
