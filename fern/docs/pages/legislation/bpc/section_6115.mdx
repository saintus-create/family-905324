# BPC § 6115

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6115

---

## Text

The court may, in its discretion, order a reference to a committee to take depositions in the matter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
