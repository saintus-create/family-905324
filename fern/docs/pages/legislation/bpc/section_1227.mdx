# BPC § 1227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 2. Administration and Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 1227

---

## Text

Every person or clinical laboratory licensed or registered under this chapter shall report to the department, within 30 days thereof, any change of name or address.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 510, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1996.)
