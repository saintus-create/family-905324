# BPC § 10023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10023

---

## Text

“Mineral, oil, and gas licensee” means a person licensed under Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 10500) of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 177, Sec. 14. (AB 685) Effective January 1, 2017.)
