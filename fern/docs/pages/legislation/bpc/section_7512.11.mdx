# BPC § 7512.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 7512.11

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “employee” means an individual who works for an employer, is listed on the employer’s payroll records, and is under the employer’s direction and control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
