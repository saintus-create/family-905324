# BPC § 7637.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2.5. Cemetery Brokerage Regulations

**Citation:** BPC § 7637.9

---

## Text

Each cemetery broker, other than a cemetery corporation holding a certificate of authority, and each cemetery salesperson shall include in any advertising a statement that he or she is acting as a cemetery broker or cemetery salesperson.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 18. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
