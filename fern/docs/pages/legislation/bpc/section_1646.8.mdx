# BPC § 1646.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2.75. Use of Deep Sedation and General Anesthesia

**Citation:** BPC § 1646.8

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to authorize a dentist to administer or directly supervise the administration of general anesthesia or deep sedation for reasons other than dental treatment, as defined in Section 1625.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 929, Sec. 4. (SB 501) Effective January 1, 2019. Operative January 1, 2022, pursuant to Section 1646.13)
