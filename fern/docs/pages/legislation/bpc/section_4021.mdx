# BPC § 4021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 4021

---

## Text

“Controlled substance” means any substance listed in Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 11053) of Division 10 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 549, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 1998.)
