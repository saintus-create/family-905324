# BPC § 8535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8535

---

## Text

The registrar, and any member of the board, may administer oaths for the purpose of administering this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1163.)
