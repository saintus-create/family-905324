# BPC § 2826.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 7. Nurse Anesthetists

**Citation:** BPC § 2826.5

---

## Text

A nurse anesthetist is authorized to perform anesthesia services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 169, Sec. 2. (AB 876) Effective January 1, 2026.)
