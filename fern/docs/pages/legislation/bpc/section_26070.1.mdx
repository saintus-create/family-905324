# BPC § 26070.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 7. Retailers, Distributors, Microbusinesses, and Combined Activities

**Citation:** BPC § 26070.1

---

## Text

Cannabis or cannabis products purchased by a customer shall not leave a licensed retail premises unless they are placed in an opaque package.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 60. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
