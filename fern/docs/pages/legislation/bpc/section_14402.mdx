# BPC § 14402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 3. Trade Names and Designations > Article: 1. Trade Names

**Citation:** BPC § 14402

---

## Text

Any court of competent jurisdiction may restrain, by injunction, any use of trade names in violation of the rights defined in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 59.)
