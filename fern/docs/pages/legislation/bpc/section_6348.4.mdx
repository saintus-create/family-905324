# BPC § 6348.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Law Libraries > Article: 3. Duties and Powers of Boards

**Citation:** BPC § 6348.4

---

## Text

Real property acquired by a board may be sold, leased, rented, or licensed with the proceeds to be deposited in the law library fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 201, Sec. 2. (SB 1462) Effective January 1, 2015.)
