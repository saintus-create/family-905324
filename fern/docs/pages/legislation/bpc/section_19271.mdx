# BPC § 19271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3.1. Household Movers Act > Article: 7. Suspension and Revocation of Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 19271

---

## Text

The bureau may, at the request of any household mover, suspend the operating permit of the household mover for a definite time during which it is unlawful for the household mover to conduct any operations as a household mover.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 8. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018.)
