# BPC § 8726.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 3. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 8726.2

---

## Text

A licensed land surveyor may also perform land planning in connection with the land surveying activities authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 817, Sec. 2.)
