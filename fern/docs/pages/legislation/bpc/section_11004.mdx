# BPC § 11004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Subdivided Lands > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 11004

---

## Text

“Community apartment project” has the same meaning as specified in Section 4105 of the Civil Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 181, Sec. 6. (AB 806) Effective January 1, 2013. Operative January 1, 2014, by Sec. 86 of Ch. 181.)
