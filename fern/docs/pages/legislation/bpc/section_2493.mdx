# BPC § 2493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 22. Podiatric Medicine

**Citation:** BPC § 2493

---

## Text

An applicant for a certificate to practice podiatric medicine shall pass an examination in the subjects required by Section 2483 in order to ensure a minimum of entry-level competence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 332, Sec. 19. (SB 1236) Effective January 1, 2013.)
