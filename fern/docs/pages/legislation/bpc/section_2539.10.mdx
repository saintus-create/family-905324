# BPC § 2539.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 9. Dispensing Audiologists

**Citation:** BPC § 2539.10

---

## Text

A licensed dispensing audiologist shall, upon the consummation of a sale of a hearing aid, keep and maintain records in the licensed dispensing audiologist’s office or place of business at all times and each such record shall be kept and maintained for a seven-year period. These records shall include:

(a) Results of test techniques as they pertain to fitting of the hearing aid.

(b) A copy of the written notice and the written receipt required by Section 2539.4 and the written recommendation and receipt required by Section 2539.6, when applicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 266, Sec. 4. (AB 435) Effective January 1, 2022.)
