# BPC § 12016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12016

---

## Text

Any person who hinders or obstructs in any way any sealer, in the performance of his official duties, is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 83.)
