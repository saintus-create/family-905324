# BPC § 5003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5003

---

## Text

The officers of the board are a president, vice president and a secretary-treasurer.
