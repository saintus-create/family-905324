# BPC § 8530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8530

---

## Text

The board, with the approval of the director, may establish the main office of the board, and branch offices of the board in any city of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1163.)
