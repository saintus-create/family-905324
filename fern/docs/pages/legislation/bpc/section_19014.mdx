# BPC § 19014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19014

---

## Text

“Branch” means any subordinate establishment situated apart from the parent house, maintaining a separate service to the trade.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
