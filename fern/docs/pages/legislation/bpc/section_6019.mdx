# BPC § 6019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6019

---

## Text

Each place upon the board for which a member is to be appointed shall for the purposes of the appointment be deemed a separate office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 422, Sec. 15. (SB 36) Effective January 1, 2018.)
