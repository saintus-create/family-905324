# BPC § 5254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5254

## Text

The director may enforce the penalties for failure to comply with the provisions of this chapter.
