# BPC § 5357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 6. Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 5357

---

## Text

If the applicant for a permit is engaged in the outdoor advertising business, the application shall contain the number of the license issued by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 991.)
