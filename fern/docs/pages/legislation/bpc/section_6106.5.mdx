# BPC § 6106.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6106.5

---

## Text

It shall constitute cause for disbarment or suspension for an attorney to engage in any conduct prohibited under Section 1871.4 of the Insurance Code or Section 550 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 867, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2001.)
