# BPC § 22178

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 3. Gold

**Citation:** BPC § 22178

---

## Text

A quality mark applied to any article shall indicate the number of twenty-fourth parts by weight of pure gold or its alloy contained in the article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 319.)
