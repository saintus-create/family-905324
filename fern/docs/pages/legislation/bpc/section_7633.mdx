# BPC § 7633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7633

---

## Text

No funeral director shall charge a fee for filing a certificate of death or for providing copies thereof in excess of fees set by statute for filing and providing certified copies of such certificates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 557.)
