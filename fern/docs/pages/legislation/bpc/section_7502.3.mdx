# BPC § 7502.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 7502.3

---

## Text

Any person who knowingly falsifies the fingerprints or photographs required by any provision of this chapter is guilty of a felony.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
