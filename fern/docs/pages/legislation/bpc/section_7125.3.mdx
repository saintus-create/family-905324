# BPC § 7125.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 7.5. Workers’ Compensation Insurance Reports

**Citation:** BPC § 7125.3

---

## Text

A contractor shall be considered duly licensed during all periods in which the registrar is required to accept the certificate prescribed by Section 7125, provided the licensee has otherwise complied with the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 311, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2003.)
