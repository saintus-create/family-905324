# BPC § 1638.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2.4. Oral and Maxillofacial Surgery

**Citation:** BPC § 1638.3

---

## Text

(a) The fee to renew an oral and maxillofacial surgery permit shall be the amount prescribed in Section 1724.

(b) Every provision of this chapter applicable to a person licensed to practice dentistry shall apply to a person to whom a special permit is issued under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 510, Sec. 9. (AB 179) Effective January 1, 2016.)
