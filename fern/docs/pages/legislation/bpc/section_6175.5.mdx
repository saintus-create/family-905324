# BPC § 6175.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 10.5. Provision of Financial Services by Lawyers

**Citation:** BPC § 6175.5

---

## Text

A violation of this article by a licensee shall be cause for discipline by the State Bar.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 125. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
