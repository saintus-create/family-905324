# BPC § 8643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 8643

---

## Text

The negligent handling or use of any pesticide is a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2014, Ch. 560, Sec. 32. (SB 1244) Effective January 1, 2015.)
