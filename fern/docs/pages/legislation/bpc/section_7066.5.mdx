# BPC § 7066.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 5. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 7066.5

---

## Text

Any person may obtain blank license application forms from the board or may cause to be printed forms used by or approved by the Registrar of Contractors.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 432, Sec. 13. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2012.)
