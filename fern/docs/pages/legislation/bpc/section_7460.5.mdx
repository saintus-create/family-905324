# BPC § 7460.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10.5. App-Based Drivers and Services > Article: 5. Antidiscrimination and Public Safety

**Citation:** BPC § 7460.5

---

## Text

A network company shall make continuously and exclusively available to law enforcement a mechanism to submit requests for information to aid in investigations related to emergency situations, exigent circumstances, and critical incidents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 3, 2020, by initiative Proposition 22, Sec. 1. Effective December 16, 2020.)
