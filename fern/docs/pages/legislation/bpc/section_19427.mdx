# BPC § 19427

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19427

---

## Text

The board shall appoint such employees as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.
