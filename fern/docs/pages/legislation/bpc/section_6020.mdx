# BPC § 6020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6020

---

## Text

The officers of the State Bar are a chair, a vice chair, and a secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 19. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
