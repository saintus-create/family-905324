# BPC § 2833.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 7. Nurse Anesthetists

**Citation:** BPC § 2833.3

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to limit a certified nurse anesthetist’s ability to practice nursing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 696, Sec. 7.)
