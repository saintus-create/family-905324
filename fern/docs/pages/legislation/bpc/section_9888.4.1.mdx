# BPC § 9888.4.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 6. Lamp and Brake Adjusting Stations

**Citation:** BPC § 9888.4.1

---

## Text

This article shall remain operative only until six months after the effective date of the regulations adopted by the director pursuant to Article 6.5 (commencing with Section 9888.5) and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 681, Sec. 8. (AB 1263) Effective January 1, 2024. Conditionally repealed by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 6, commencing with Section 9888.1.)
