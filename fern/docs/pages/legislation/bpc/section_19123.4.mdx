# BPC § 19123.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19123.4

---

## Text

Newly manufactured articles of bedding that contain any secondhand filling material shall be sanitized before they are offered or exposed for sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 401, Sec. 60. Effective January 1, 1998.)
