# BPC § 5521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5521

---

## Text

The executive officer shall keep an accurate record of all proceedings of the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 47, Sec. 42. Effective March 21, 1984.)
