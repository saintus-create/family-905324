# BPC § 7018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7018

---

## Text

The board shall maintain the current contractor license check search function on their internet website that permits consumers to search for a licensed contractor by either ZIP Code or geographic location.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 378, Sec. 4. (SB 610) Effective January 1, 2020.)
