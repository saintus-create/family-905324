# BPC § 19059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19059

---

## Text

Every supply dealer, unless he or she holds an upholstered-furniture and bedding manufacturer’s license or an importer’s license, shall hold a supply dealer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 41. Effective January 1, 1997.)
