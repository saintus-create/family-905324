# BPC § 7612.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 1.5. Definitions and Regulation of Cemeteries

**Citation:** BPC § 7612.8

---

## Text

The report shall state the name of the trustee or trustees of the endowment care fund. Any change of trustee shall be reported to the bureau within a period of 30 days after the change is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 13. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
