# BPC § 2737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 2737

---

## Text

An applicant for a license authorizing him to practice nursing in this State under this chapter, upon the filing of his application shall pay the fee required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 807.)
