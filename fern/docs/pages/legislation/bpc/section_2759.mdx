# BPC § 2759

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 3. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 2759

---

## Text

The board shall discipline the holder of any license, whose default has been entered or who has been heard by the board and found guilty, by any of the following methods:

(a) Suspending judgment.

(b) Placing upon them probation.

(c) Suspending their right to practice nursing for a period not exceeding one year.

(d) Revoking their license.

(e) Taking other action in relation to disciplining them as the board in its discretion may deem proper.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 510, Sec. 33. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2024.)
