# BPC § 4403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 23. Revenue and Renewal

**Citation:** BPC § 4403

---

## Text

The board shall not reissue or renew any license without the payment of the fees required by this chapter and the payment of all fees that are delinquent at the time that the application is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 539, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 2004.)
