# BPC § 22384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 17. Invention Development Services Contracts > Article: 4.5. Remedies and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 22384

---

## Text

Any contract for invention development services entered into in reliance upon any willful and false, fraudulent, or misleading representation by the invention developer shall be void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 967.)
