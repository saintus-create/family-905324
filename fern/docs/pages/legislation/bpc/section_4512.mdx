# BPC § 4512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 2. Licensure

**Citation:** BPC § 4512

---

## Text

An applicant for a psychiatric technician’s license shall, upon the filing of the application, pay to the board the application fee prescribed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 632, Sec. 42. (AB 1536) Effective January 1, 2022.)
