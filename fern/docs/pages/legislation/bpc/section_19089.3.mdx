# BPC § 19089.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 5. Labeling

**Citation:** BPC § 19089.3

---

## Text

All bedding with liquid filling material and each component part of such bedding shall be clearly labeled in a manner approved by the chief.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 160, Sec. 1.)
