# BPC § 13303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 13. Automatic Checkout Systems > Article: 1. Point-Of-Sale Displays

**Citation:** BPC § 13303

---

## Text

The secretary may adopt necessary rules and regulations regarding the accuracy of automated systems for retail commodity price charging referred to as “scanners.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12736 by Stats. 2012, Ch. 661, Sec. 49. (SB 1576) Effective January 1, 2013.)
