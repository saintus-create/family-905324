# BPC § 468

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 8. Dispute Resolution Programs > Article: 4. Application Procedures

**Citation:** BPC § 468

---

## Text

All funds available to a county for the purposes of this chapter shall be utilized for projects proposed by eligible programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1313, Sec. 1.)
