# BPC § 4341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 20. Prohibitions and Offenses

**Citation:** BPC § 4341

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, prescription drugs or devices may be advertised if the advertisement conforms with the requirements of Section 651.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 549, Sec. 131. Effective January 1, 1998.)
