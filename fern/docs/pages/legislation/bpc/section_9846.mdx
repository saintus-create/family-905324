# BPC § 9846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 4. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 9846

---

## Text

The use of “guarantee” and words of like import shall conform to the regulations adopted by the director.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1492.)
