# BPC § 6980.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.2

---

## Text

The Governor shall appoint a chief of the bureau at a salary to be fixed in accordance with Section 12080.3 of the Government Code. The chief shall serve under the direction and supervision of the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
