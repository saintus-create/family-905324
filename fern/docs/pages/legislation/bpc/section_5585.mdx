# BPC § 5585

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 5. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 5585

---

## Text

The fact that in the practice of architecture the holder of a license has been guilty of incompetency or recklessness constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 184, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1997.)
