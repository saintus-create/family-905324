# BPC § 19416.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19416.6

---

## Text

“Arabian horse” is any horse (including mare, gelding, colt, and filly) that meets the requirements of and is registered by the Arabian Horse Registry of America, Inc.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2001.)
