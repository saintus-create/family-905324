# BPC § 2746.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 2.5. Nurse-Midwives

**Citation:** BPC § 2746.1

---

## Text

Every applicant for a certificate to practice nurse-midwifery shall comply with all the provisions of this article in addition to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 1407.)
