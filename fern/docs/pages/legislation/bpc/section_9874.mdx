# BPC § 9874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 6. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 9874

---

## Text

All salaries, expenses, or costs incurred or sustained pursuant to this chapter shall be payable only out of the Household Goods and Services Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 508, Sec. 30. (SB 814) Effective January 1, 2024.)
