# BPC § 4991.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 14. Social Workers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4991.2

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter:

(a) “Accredited school of social work” means a school that is accredited by the Commission on Accreditation of the Council on Social Work Education.

(b) “Board” means the Board of Behavioral Sciences.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 640, Sec. 5. (AB 232) Effective January 1, 2024.)
