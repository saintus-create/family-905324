**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 2. The Vacation Ownership and Time-Share Act of 2004 > Article: 5. Powers, Investigation, and Enforcement

## Text

This chapter shall take effect on July 1, 2005.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 697, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2005. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 2, commencing with Section 11210.)
