# BPC § 10018.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10018.04

---

## Text

“Seller’s agent” means a licensee who provides services requiring a real estate license for or on behalf of a seller. A seller’s agent may or may not be a listing agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 12. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
