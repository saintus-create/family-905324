# BPC § 18648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 3. Jurisdiction, Licenses, and Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 18648

---

## Text

The commission shall prescribe standards as necessary, for the licensure of any persons required to be licensed by this chapter or by the commission’s regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
