# BPC § 8715.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 15. Land Surveyors > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8715.3

---

## Text

All members of each technical advisory committee shall serve without compensation but shall receive per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 229, Sec. 11.)
