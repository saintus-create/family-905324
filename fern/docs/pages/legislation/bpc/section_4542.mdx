# BPC § 4542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10. Psychiatric Technicians > Article: 5. Penal Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4542

---

## Text

(a) It is unlawful for any person willfully to make any false representation or impersonate any other person in connection with any examination or application for a license.

(b) It is unlawful for any person to permit or aid any other person in any manner to impersonate that person in connection with any examination or application for a license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 632, Sec. 52. (AB 1536) Effective January 1, 2022.)
