# BPC § 19542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 6.5. Fairs and Expositions

**Citation:** BPC § 19542

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, fairs that conduct live horse racing meetings in the northern zone may allow a joint powers authority to administer and distribute purses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 559, Sec. 7. (AB 2143) Effective January 1, 2025.)
