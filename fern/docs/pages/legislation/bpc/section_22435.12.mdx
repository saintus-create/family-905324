# BPC § 22435.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 19. Shopping and Laundry Carts > Article: 2. Shopping Cart or Laundry Cart Retrieval Services

**Citation:** BPC § 22435.12

---

## Text

Each vehicle employed for the retrieval of shopping carts or laundry carts shall display a sign that clearly identifies the retrieval service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 687, Sec. 1.)
