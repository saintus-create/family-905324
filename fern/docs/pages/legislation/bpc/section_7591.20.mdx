# BPC § 7591.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7591.20

---

## Text

The disciplinary review committee shall be provided all evidence used by the bureau in reaching its decision prior to hearing an appeal.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
