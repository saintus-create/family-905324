# BPC § 22757.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 25. AI Transparency Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22757.5

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any product, service, internet website, or application that provides exclusively non-user-generated video game, television, streaming, movie, or interactive experiences.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 291, Sec. 1. (SB 942) Effective January 1, 2025. Operative August 2, 2026, pursuant to Section 22757.6.)
