# BPC § 7500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 7500

---

## Text

This chapter of the Business and Professions Code constitutes the chapter on repossessors. It may be cited as the “Collateral Recovery Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 505, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
