# BPC § 22948.54

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 36. In-Vehicle Cameras

**Citation:** BPC § 22948.54

---

## Text

Any waiver of the provisions of this chapter is contrary to public policy and void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 864, Sec. 1. (SB 296) Effective January 1, 2024.)
