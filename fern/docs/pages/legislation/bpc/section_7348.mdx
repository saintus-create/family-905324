# BPC § 7348

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 6. Establishments

**Citation:** BPC § 7348

---

## Text

An establishment shall at all times be in the charge of a person licensed pursuant to this chapter except an apprentice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 878, Sec. 48.3. Effective January 1, 1999.)
