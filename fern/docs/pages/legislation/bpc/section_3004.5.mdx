# BPC § 3004.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3004.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” means a natural person, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, or other organization, or any combination thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 788, Sec. 20. (SB 776) Effective January 1, 2026.)
