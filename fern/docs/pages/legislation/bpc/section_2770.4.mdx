# BPC § 2770.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 3.1. Intervention Program

**Citation:** BPC § 2770.4

---

## Text

Three members of a committee shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting. Any action requires a majority vote of the committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 865, Sec. 1.)
