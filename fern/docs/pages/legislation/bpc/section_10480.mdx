# BPC § 10480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 6.5. Real Estate Recovery Program

**Citation:** BPC § 10480

---

## Text

The failure of an aggrieved person to comply with all of the provisions of this chapter shall constitute a waiver of any rights hereunder.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 690, Sec. 3. Operative January 1, 1987, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 690.)
