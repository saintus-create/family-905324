# BPC § 7582.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7582.9

---

## Text

The director may require an applicant or his or her manager, to demonstrate his or her qualifications by a written or oral examination, or a combination of both.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
