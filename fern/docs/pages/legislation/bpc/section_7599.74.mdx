# BPC § 7599.74

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 14. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.74

---

## Text

All money derived from Section 7591.9 shall be used to support the bureau’s enforcement program.
