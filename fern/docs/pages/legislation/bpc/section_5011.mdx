# BPC § 5011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5011

---

## Text

The board shall designate the location of its principal office and may establish branch offices in other locations.

(History: Added by renumbering Section 5016 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 310.)
