# BPC § 7580.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 7580.3

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” includes any individual, firm, company, association, organization, partnership, corporation, and participating tribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 484, Sec. 29. (SB 1454) Effective January 1, 2025.)
