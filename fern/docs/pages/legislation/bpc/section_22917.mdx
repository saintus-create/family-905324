# BPC § 22917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22917

---

## Text

A member of the public may obtain a copy of any notice of an equipment repurchase lien, including notices affecting the notices from the Secretary of State. The fee for these copies shall be the same as that prescribed in Section 9525 of the Commercial Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 1.5. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
