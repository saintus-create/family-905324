# BPC § 10477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 6.5. Real Estate Recovery Program

**Citation:** BPC § 10477

---

## Text

Any sums received by the commissioner pursuant to any provisions of this chapter shall be deposited in the State Treasury and credited to the Consumer Recovery Account.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 712, Sec. 30. (SB 706) Effective January 1, 2012.)
