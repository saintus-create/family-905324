# BPC § 21608.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 3. Junk

**Citation:** BPC § 21608.3

---

## Text

(a) Any unauthorized disclosure of personal identification information collected from a seller by a junk dealer or recycler is prohibited, and any such disclosure shall render the violator liable for a civil fine of up to five thousand dollars ($5,000).

(b) This section shall become operative on December 1, 2008.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 730, Sec. 4. Effective September 30, 2008. Section operative December 1, 2008, by its own provisions.)
