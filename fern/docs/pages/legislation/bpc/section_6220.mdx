# BPC § 6220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 14. Funds for the Provision of Legal Services to Indigent Persons

**Citation:** BPC § 6220

---

## Text

Attorneys in private practice who are providing legal services without charge to indigent persons shall not be disqualified from receiving the services of the qualified support centers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 789, Sec. 1.)
