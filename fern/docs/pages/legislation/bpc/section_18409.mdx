# BPC § 18409

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Automobiles

**Citation:** BPC § 18409

---

## Text

Any contract or agreement in violation of the provisions of this chapter is void and is not enforceable at law or in equity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 44.)
