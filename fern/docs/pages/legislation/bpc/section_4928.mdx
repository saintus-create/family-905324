# BPC § 4928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 1. Administration and General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4928

---

## Text

(a) The Acupuncture Board, which consists of seven members, shall enforce and administer this chapter.

(b) This section shall remain in effect until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 682, Sec. 3. (AB 1264) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, by its own provisions.)
