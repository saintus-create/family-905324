# BPC § 21636.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21636.6

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person, who is required to be licensed under Section 21640, to publicly advertise any matter relating to the business for which the license is required without including the license number.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 647, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
