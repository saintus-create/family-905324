# BPC § 19702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4.5. Mule Racing

**Citation:** BPC § 19702

---

## Text

The board shall administer this chapter and shall issue a license for the conducting of a mule racing meeting or mule races.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 317, Sec. 1. Effective August 22, 1994.)
