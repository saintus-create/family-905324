# BPC § 4978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 7. Acupuncture Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 4978

---

## Text

The name of an acupuncture corporation and any name or names under which it may render professional services shall contain words “acupuncture” or “acupuncturist” and wording or abbreviations denoting corporate existence.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1314, Sec. 2.2. Operative July 1, 1982, by Sec. 19 of Ch. 1314.)
