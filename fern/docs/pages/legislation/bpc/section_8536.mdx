# BPC § 8536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8536

---

## Text

No manufacturer, his or her agents or employees, nor any person who is a consultant of or connected with any manufacturer, shall be appointed to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 238, Sec. 3. (AB 1736) Effective January 1, 2011.)
