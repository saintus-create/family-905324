# BPC § 7713.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6.5. Crematories

**Citation:** BPC § 7713.4

---

## Text

A crematory licensee shall be subject to and shall be disciplined by the bureau in accordance with Article 6 (commencing with Section 7686).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 35. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
