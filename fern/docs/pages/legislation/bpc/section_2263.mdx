# BPC § 2263

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2263

---

## Text

The willful, unauthorized violation of professional confidence constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
