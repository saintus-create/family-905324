# BPC § 7352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 6. Establishments

**Citation:** BPC § 7352

---

## Text

Every establishment shall provide adequate and convenient handwashing facilities, including running water, soap, and towels or air hand dryers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 415, Sec. 27. (SB 1491) Effective January 1, 2011.)
