# BPC § 3514.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 3. Certification and Approval

**Citation:** BPC § 3514.1

---

## Text

The board shall formulate by regulation guidelines for the consideration of applications for licensure as a physician assistant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 194, Sec. 14. (AB 1501) Effective January 1, 2026.)
