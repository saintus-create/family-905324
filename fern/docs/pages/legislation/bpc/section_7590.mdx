# BPC § 7590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 7590

---

## Text

This chapter constitutes the chapter on alarm company operators and alarm agents. It may be cited as the Alarm Company Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
