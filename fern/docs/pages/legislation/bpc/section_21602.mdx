# BPC § 21602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 3. Junk

**Citation:** BPC § 21602

---

## Text

As used in this article, “junk yard” includes any yard, plot, space, inclosure, building or any other place where junk is collected, stored, gathered together and kept.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1078.)
