# BPC § 7021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7021

---

## Text

The board may enter into an interagency agreement with any other state or local agency the board deems to be in possession of any information relevant to its priority to protect the public described in Section 7000.6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 372, Sec. 1. (SB 465) Effective January 1, 2017.)
