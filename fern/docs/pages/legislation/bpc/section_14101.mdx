# BPC § 14101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 1. Good Will

**Citation:** BPC § 14101

---

## Text

The good will of a business does not include a right to use the name of any person from whom the business was acquired.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 57.)
