# BPC § 2820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 6.5. Public Health Nurse Certification

**Citation:** BPC § 2820

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as expanding the scope of practice of a registered nurse beyond that which is authorized under Section 2725.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1135, Sec. 2.8. Effective January 1, 1993.)
