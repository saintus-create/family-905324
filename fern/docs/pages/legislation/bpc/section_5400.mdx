# BPC § 5400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 7. Regulations

**Citation:** BPC § 5400

---

## Text

No advertising structure may be maintained unless the name of the person owning or maintaining it, is plainly displayed thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 991.)
