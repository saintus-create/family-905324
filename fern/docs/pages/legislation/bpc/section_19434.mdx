# BPC § 19434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19434

---

## Text

The board may require that the books and financial or other statements of any person licensed under this chapter shall be kept in any manner which to the board may seem best.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
