# BPC § 9855.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 4.5. Service Contractors

**Citation:** BPC § 9855.15

---

## Text

A service contract administrator who is an obligor on a service contract and is registered as a service contract administrator may perform all the functions permitted by a seller and shall not be required to register separately as a seller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 543, Sec. 3. (AB 2111) Effective January 1, 2011. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 9855.9.)
