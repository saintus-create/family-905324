# BPC § 5252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5252

---

## Text

The director shall prescribe the form of all applications, licenses, permits and other appurtenant written matter.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 991.)
