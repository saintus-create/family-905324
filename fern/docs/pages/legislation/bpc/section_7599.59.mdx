# BPC § 7599.59

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 12. Alarm Company Operators—Conduct of Business, Prohibited Acts and Citations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.59

---

## Text

The director may assess administrative fines of fifty dollars ($50) against any licensee, qualified certificate holder, firearms qualification card holder, or registrant for each violation for failure to notify the bureau within 30 days of any change of residence or business address.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 800, Sec. 74. (SB 1196) Effective January 1, 2017.)
