# BPC § 18813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18813

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for an amateur boxing, kickboxing, and martial arts referee’s and judge’s license shall be seventy-five dollars ($75).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1057, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1994.)
