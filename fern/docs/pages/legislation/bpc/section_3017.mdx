# BPC § 3017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3017

---

## Text

The board shall hold regular meetings every calendar quarter.

Special meetings shall be held upon request of a majority of the members of the board or upon the call of the president.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 2005.)
