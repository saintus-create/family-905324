# BPC § 4863

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 3.5. Wellness Evaluation Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 4863

---

## Text

Three members of a wellness evaluation committee shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting. Any action requires the majority vote of the wellness evaluation committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 631, Sec. 27. (AB 1535) Effective January 1, 2022.)
