# BPC § 7504.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 5. Examination

**Citation:** BPC § 7504.5

---

## Text

All applicants shall be examined concerning their competency, experience, and qualifications by the chief and the chief may take testimony of anyone in regard thereto under oath.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
