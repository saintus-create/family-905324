# BPC § 7596.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 6. Firearms Permits and Permits for Other Deadly and Nonlethal Weapons

**Citation:** BPC § 7596.12

---

## Text

Every person licensed, registered, or designated under this chapter, who in the course of his or her employment carries tear gas, or any other nonlethal chemical agent, shall complete the course required pursuant to Section 22835 of the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 178, Sec. 11. (SB 1115) Effective January 1, 2011. Operative January 1, 2012, by Sec. 107 of Ch. 178.)
