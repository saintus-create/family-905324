# BPC § 4872

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 3.5. Wellness Evaluation Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 4872

---

## Text

The board shall provide for the representation of any persons making reports to a wellness evaluation committee or the board under this article in any action for defamation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 631, Sec. 35. (AB 1535) Effective January 1, 2022.)
