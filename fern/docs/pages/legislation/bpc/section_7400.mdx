# BPC § 7400

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 10. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 7400

---

## Text

Every licensee of the board, except establishments shall, within 30 days after a change of address, notify the board of the new address, and, upon receipt of the notification, the board shall make the necessary changes in the register.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 2004.)
