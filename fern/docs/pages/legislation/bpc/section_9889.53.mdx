# BPC § 9889.53

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 10.5. Auto Body Repair

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.53

---

## Text

A check or draft issued to a repairer pursuant to Section 560 of the Insurance Code shall include the repairer’s registration number or taxpayer identification number.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 445, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
