# BPC § 2034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2034

---

## Text

“Medical licensing authority” refers to any officer, board, commission, or department of another state upon whose certificate a reciprocity certificate may be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
