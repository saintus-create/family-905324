# BPC § 19202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 9. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19202

---

## Text

The bureau may condemn, withhold from sale, seize, or destroy any upholstered furniture or bedding or any filling material or insulation which is found to be in violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1456, Sec. 12.)
