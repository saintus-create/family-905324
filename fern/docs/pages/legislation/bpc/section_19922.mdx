# BPC § 19922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 9. Conditions of Operation

**Citation:** BPC § 19922

---

## Text

No owner licensee shall operate a gambling enterprise in violation of any provision of this chapter or any regulation adopted pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19916 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 87. Effective January 1, 2003.)
