# BPC § 16004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Licensing by Cities

**Citation:** BPC § 16004

---

## Text

Any license issued to a business pursuant to this chapter shall be revoked for a violation of Section 24185 of the Health and Safety Code, relating to human cloning.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 821, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
