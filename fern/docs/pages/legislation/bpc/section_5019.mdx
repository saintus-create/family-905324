# BPC § 5019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5019

---

## Text

Every applicant, when subscribing to an application for certificate or registration, shall acknowledge the fact that the applicant has read and understands the rules of professional conduct adopted by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 661, Sec. 2. (SB 1576) Effective January 1, 2013.)
