# BPC § 4955.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 4. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 4955.2

---

## Text

The board may deny, suspend, revoke, or impose probationary conditions upon the license of any acupuncturist if he or she is guilty of committing any one of the following:

(a) Gross negligence.

(b) Repeated negligent acts.

(c) Incompetence.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 714, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2003.)
