# BPC § 7729.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7729.10

---

## Text

The annual renewal fee for a cemetery salesperson’s license shall be fifty dollars ($50).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 54. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
