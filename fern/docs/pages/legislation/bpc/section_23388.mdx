# BPC § 23388

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23388

---

## Text

A licensed beer manufacturer or a licensed beer wholesaler, in addition to selling beer at their licensed premises, may sell beer from wagons, trailers, or trucks operated by them to licensees authorized to sell beer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 30, Sec. 1. (AB 233) Effective January 1, 2026.)
