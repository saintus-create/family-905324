# BPC § 22910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22910

---

## Text

The notice of claim of lien shall be signed by the lien claimant or by a person authorized by the claimant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 712, Sec. 16. Effective October 7, 2005.)
