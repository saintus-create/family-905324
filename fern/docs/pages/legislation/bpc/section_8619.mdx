# BPC § 8619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 6. Regulation of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 8619

---

## Text

(a) An inspection tag shall be posted whenever an inspection for wood destroying pests or organisms is made.

(b) If the registered company completes any work with respect to wood destroying pests or organisms, it shall post a completion tag next to the inspection tag, unless both the inspection and completion tags are combined on the same form.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 572, Sec. 12. (SB 1481) Effective January 1, 2019.)
