# BPC § 4948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 2. Certification Requirements

**Citation:** BPC § 4948

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall not be construed to make unlawful the activities of persons involved in research pursuant to Section 2075.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 11.5. Section operative July 1, 1982, pursuant to former Section 4974.5.)
