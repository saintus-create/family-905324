# BPC § 6025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6025

---

## Text

Subject to the laws of this state, the board may formulate and declare rules and regulations necessary or expedient for the carrying out of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 698, Sec. 4. (SB 176) Effective January 1, 2020.)
