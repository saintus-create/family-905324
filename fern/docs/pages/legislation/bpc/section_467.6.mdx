# BPC § 467.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 8. Dispute Resolution Programs > Article: 3. Establishment and Administration of Programs

**Citation:** BPC § 467.6

---

## Text

Each program shall maintain those statistical records required by Section 471.5, and as may be required by the county. The records shall maintain the confidentiality and anonymity of the parties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1313, Sec. 1.)
