# BPC § 5535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 5535

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the word “person” includes any individual, firm, partnership, general corporation, professional corporation, or limited liability partnership, as authorized by the Corporations Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 275, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2008.)
