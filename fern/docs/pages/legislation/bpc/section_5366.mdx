# BPC § 5366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 6. Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 5366

---

## Text

The issuance of a permit pursuant to this chapter does not allow any person to erect an advertising display in violation of any ordinance of any city, county, or city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 653, Sec. 13.)
