# BPC § 17534

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 2. Particular Offenses

**Citation:** BPC § 17534

---

## Text

Any person, firm, corporation, partnership or association or any employee or agent thereof who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 63.)
