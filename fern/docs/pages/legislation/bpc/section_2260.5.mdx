# BPC § 2260.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2260.5

---

## Text

A violation of Section 24185 of the Health and Safety Code, relating to human cloning, constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 821, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
