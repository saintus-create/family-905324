# BPC § 24077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Transfer of Licenses > Article: 5. Transfer of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 24077

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no license shall be transferred into any county having a population of 35,000 or less.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 783.)
