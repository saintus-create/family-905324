# BPC § 7016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7016

---

## Text

Each member of the board shall receive a per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.
