# BPC § 22948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 33. Anti-Phishing Act of 2005

**Citation:** BPC § 22948

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Anti-Phishing Act of 2005.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 437, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
