# BPC § 17070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 4. Civil Liability

**Citation:** BPC § 17070

---

## Text

Any person or trade association may bring an action to enjoin and restrain any violation of this chapter and, in addition thereto, for the recovery of damages.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
