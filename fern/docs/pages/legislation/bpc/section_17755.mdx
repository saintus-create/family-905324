# BPC § 17755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Trading Stamp Company Act

**Citation:** BPC § 17755

---

## Text

“Trading stamp company” means any person engaged in any manner in distributing trading stamps for retail issuance by others or in redeeming trading stamps for retailers.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
