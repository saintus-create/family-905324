# BPC § 7506.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 7. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7506.6

---

## Text

Qualified certificate holders who comply with other provisions of this chapter are not required to register under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
