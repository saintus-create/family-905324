# BPC § 10015.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10015.2

---

## Text

“Manager” means a real estate licensee authorized to perform supervisorial services for a responsible broker.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 4. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
