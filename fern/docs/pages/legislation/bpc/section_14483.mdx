# BPC § 14483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 3. Trade Names and Designations > Article: 4. Laundry Supply Designations

**Citation:** BPC § 14483

---

## Text

The registrant shall pay to the Secretary of State for filing each laundry supply designation described and for issuing a certificate of filing a fee as set forth in subdivision (e) of Section 12193 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 506, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
