# BPC § 6003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6003

---

## Text

Licensees of the State Bar are divided into two classes:

(a) Active licensees.

(b) Inactive licensees.
