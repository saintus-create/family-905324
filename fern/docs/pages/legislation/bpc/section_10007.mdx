# BPC § 10007

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10007

---

## Text

“Provisions of this part relating to real estate” means the provisions of Chapters 1, 2, 3, and 6 of Part 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 177, Sec. 3. (AB 685) Effective January 1, 2017.)
