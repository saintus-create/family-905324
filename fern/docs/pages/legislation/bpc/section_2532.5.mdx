# BPC § 2532.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 3. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 2532.5

---

## Text

Every person holding a license under this chapter shall display it conspicuously in their primary place of practice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 510, Sec. 5. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2024.)
