# BPC § 2217

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 11.5. Surgery in Certain Outpatient Settings

**Citation:** BPC § 2217

---

## Text

The Division of Licensing of the Medical Board of California may adopt regulations to implement this article and Chapter 1.3 (commencing with Section 1248) of Division 2 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1276, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
