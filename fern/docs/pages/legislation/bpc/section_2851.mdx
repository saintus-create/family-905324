# BPC § 2851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2851

---

## Text

Six members of the board constitute a quorum for transaction of business at any meeting.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 810, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 2003.)
