# BPC § 4839

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 2.5. Registered Veterinary Technicians

**Citation:** BPC § 4839

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “registered veterinary technician” means a person who has met the requirements set forth in Section 4841.5 and is registered by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 195, Sec. 12. (AB 1502) Effective January 1, 2026.)
