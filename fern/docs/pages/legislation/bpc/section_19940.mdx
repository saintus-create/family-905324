# BPC § 19940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 11. Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 19940

---

## Text

Any person included on the list of persons to be excluded or ejected from a gambling establishment pursuant to this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor if he or she thereafter knowingly enters the premises of a licensed gambling establishment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 19930 by Stats. 2002, Ch. 738, Sec. 97. Effective January 1, 2003.)
