# BPC § 2407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 18. Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 2407

---

## Text

A medical or podiatry corporation shall be subject to the provisions of Sections 2285 and 2415.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1314, Sec. 4.2.)
