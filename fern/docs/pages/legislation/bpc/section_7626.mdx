# BPC § 7626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7626

---

## Text

The bureau shall examine and pass upon the qualifications of the applicant as to ability and experience before passing upon the physical status or plans and specifications of the proposed funeral establishment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 108. Effective January 1, 2001.)
