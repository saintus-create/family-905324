# BPC § 8618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 6. Regulation of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 8618

---

## Text

Documents as specified by regulation must be maintained by all registered companies for three years.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 844, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 1995.)
