# BPC § 19215.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 9.5. Disciplinary Proceedings Against Nonresidents

**Citation:** BPC § 19215.7

---

## Text

The director shall keep a record of all process served upon him or her pursuant to this article that shall show the day and hour of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 405, Sec. 60. Effective January 1, 2003.)
