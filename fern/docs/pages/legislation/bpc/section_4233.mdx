# BPC § 4233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 17. Continuing Education

**Citation:** BPC § 4233

---

## Text

A pharmacist who is recognized as an advanced pharmacist practitioner shall complete 10 hours of continuing education each renewal cycle in addition to the requirements of Section 4231. The subject matter shall be in one or more areas of practice relevant to the pharmacist’s clinical practice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 196, Sec. 45. (AB 1503) Effective January 1, 2026.)
