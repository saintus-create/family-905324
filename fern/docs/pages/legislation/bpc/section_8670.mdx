# BPC § 8670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 8670

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “original operator’s license” means an operator’s license that is not a renewal license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1163.)
