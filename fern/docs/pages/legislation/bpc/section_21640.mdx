# BPC § 21640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21640

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to engage in the business of secondhand dealer, as defined in Section 21626, without being licensed as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 499, Sec. 13.)
