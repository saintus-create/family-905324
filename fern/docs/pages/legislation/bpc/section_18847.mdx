# BPC § 18847

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 14. Administrative Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 18847

---

## Text

The disobedience of a subpoena issued under Section 18846 shall be attended by the same consequences and subject to the same penalties as if such disobedience occurred in an action in the superior court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
