# BPC § 6980.38

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 5. Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.38

---

## Text

An application for a branch office registration under this article shall be on a form prescribed by the director and shall be accompanied by the fee as set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
