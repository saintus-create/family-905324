# BPC § 2537.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 7. Speech-Language Pathology Corporations and Audiology Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 2537.2

---

## Text

Except as provided in Sections 13401.5 and 13403 of the Corporations Code, each shareholder, director and officer of a speech-language pathology corporation or an audiology corporation, except an assistant secretary and an assistant treasurer, shall be a licensed person as defined in Section 13401.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 26, Sec. 40. Effective March 30, 1994.)
