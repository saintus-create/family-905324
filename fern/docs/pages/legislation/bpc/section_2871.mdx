# BPC § 2871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 2871

---

## Text

Any applicant who fails to pass the first examination may take a second examination upon payment of the fee required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 632, Sec. 14. (AB 1536) Effective January 1, 2022.)
