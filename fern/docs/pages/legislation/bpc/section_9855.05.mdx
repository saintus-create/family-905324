# BPC § 9855.05

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 4.5. Service Contractors

**Citation:** BPC § 9855.05

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 2000, for the purposes of this chapter, “service contract” also includes a service contract as described in subdivision (e) of Section 12741 of the Insurance Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1075, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1999. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 9855.9.)
