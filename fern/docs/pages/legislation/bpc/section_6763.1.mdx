# BPC § 6763.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 4. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 6763.1

---

## Text

An applicant to use the title “structural engineer” shall have successfully passed a written examination for structural engineering that is administered by a nationally recognized entity approved by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 448, Sec. 20. (SB 543) Effective January 1, 2012.)
