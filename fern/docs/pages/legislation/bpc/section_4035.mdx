# BPC § 4035

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 4035

---

## Text

“Person” includes, but is not limited to, firm, association, partnership, corporation, limited liability company, state governmental agency, trust, or political subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 484, Sec. 16. (SB 1193) Effective January 1, 2017.)
