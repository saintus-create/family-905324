# BPC § 22679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 22.8. Content Moderation Requirements for Internet Terms of Service

**Citation:** BPC § 22679

---

## Text

(a) The duties and obligations imposed by this chapter are cumulative to any other duties or obligations imposed under local, state, or federal law and shall not be construed to relieve any party from any duties or obligations imposed under law.

(b) The remedies or penalties provided by this chapter are cumulative to each other and to any other remedies or penalties available under local, state, or federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 269, Sec. 2. (AB 587) Effective January 1, 2023.)
