# BPC § 18897.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 2. Professional Athletes and Athlete Agents

**Citation:** BPC § 18897.4

---

## Text

All forms of advertising used by an athlete agent shall include the name and business address of the athlete agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 857, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
