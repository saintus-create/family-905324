# BPC § 1646.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 2.75. Use of Deep Sedation and General Anesthesia

**Citation:** BPC § 1646.12

---

## Text

A pediatric endorsement shall expire on the date specified in Section 1715 that next occurs after its issuance, unless it is renewed as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 483, Sec. 17. (SB 1453) Effective January 1, 2025.)
