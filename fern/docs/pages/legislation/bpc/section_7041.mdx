# BPC § 7041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 3. Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 7041

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to officers of a court when they are acting within the scope of their office.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 37.)
