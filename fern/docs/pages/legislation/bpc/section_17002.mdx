# BPC § 17002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 17002

## Text

This chapter shall be liberally construed that its beneficial purposes may be subserved.
