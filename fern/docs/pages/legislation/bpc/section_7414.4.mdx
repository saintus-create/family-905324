# BPC § 7414.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 12.5. Tanning Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 7414.4

---

## Text

The board, and its authorized representatives, may disseminate information to tanning facilities regarding compliance with the Filante Tanning Facility Act of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 788, Sec. 71. Effective January 1, 2004.)
