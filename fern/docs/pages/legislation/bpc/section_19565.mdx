# BPC § 19565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 8. Regulation Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 19565

---

## Text

Subject to the provisions of this article, the board shall by rule provide for the registration of all California-bred horses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 68. Effective January 1, 2001.)
