# BPC § 22393

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 17. Invention Development Services Contracts > Article: 6. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 22393

---

## Text

No invention developer shall make, or authorize the making of, any reference to compliance by it with this act in any advertisement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 967.)
