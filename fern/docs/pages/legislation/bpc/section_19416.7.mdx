# BPC § 19416.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19416.7

---

## Text

“Paint horse” is any horse, including mare, gelding, colt, and filly, that meets the requirements of and is registered by the American Paint Horse Association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1277, Sec. 1.)
