# BPC § 7599.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 11. Alarm Company Operators—Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.29

---

## Text

Every branch office registration issued under this chapter shall be subject to the same renewal provisions which apply to a license as set forth in Sections 7593.11, 7593.12, 7593.13, and 7593.14.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
