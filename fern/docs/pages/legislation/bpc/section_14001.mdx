# BPC § 14001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS

**Citation:** BPC § 14001

---

## Text

As used in this division, “person” means any person, association, organization, partnership, business trust, limited liability company, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 1995.)
