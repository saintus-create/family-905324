# BPC § 5090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 5. Certificates, Information and Records

**Citation:** BPC § 5090

---

## Text

An applicant for the certified public accountant license shall comply with the education, examination, and experience requirements in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 293, Sec. 9. (AB 1175) Effective January 1, 2026.)
