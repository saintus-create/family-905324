# BPC § 7583.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 4. Private Patrol Operators

**Citation:** BPC § 7583.18

---

## Text

A qualified manager who complies with Section 7582.22 is not required to register under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
