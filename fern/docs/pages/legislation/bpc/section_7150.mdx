# BPC § 7150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 10. Home Improvement Business

**Citation:** BPC § 7150

---

## Text

(a) “Person” as used in this article is limited to natural persons, notwithstanding the definition of person in Section 7025.

(b) “Senior citizen” means an individual who is 65 years of age or older.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2020, Ch. 158, Sec. 1. (AB 2471) Effective January 1, 2021.)
