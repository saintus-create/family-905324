# BPC § 5211

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 5211

---

## Text

“Flashing” is a light or message that changes more than once every four seconds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 787, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
