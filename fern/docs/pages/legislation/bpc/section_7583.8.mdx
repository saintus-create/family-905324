# BPC § 7583.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 4. Private Patrol Operators

**Citation:** BPC § 7583.8

---

## Text

A licensee shall not permit an employee to perform the functions of a security guard without confirming that the person holds a current and valid security guard registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 377, Sec. 22. (SB 609) Effective January 1, 2020.)
