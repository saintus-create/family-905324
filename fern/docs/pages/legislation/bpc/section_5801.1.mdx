# BPC § 5801.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.9. Interior Designers

**Citation:** BPC § 5801.1

---

## Text

The procedure for the issuance of a stamp by the council under Section 5801, including the examinations recognized and required by the council, shall be subject to the occupational analyses and examination validation required by Section 139 every five to seven years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 723, Sec. 24. (SB 816) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 5810.)
