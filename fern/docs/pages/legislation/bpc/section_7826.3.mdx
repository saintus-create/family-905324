# BPC § 7826.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 2.1. Technical Advisory Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 7826.3

---

## Text

All the members of each technical advisory committee shall serve without compensation but shall receive per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 432, Sec. 25. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2012.)
