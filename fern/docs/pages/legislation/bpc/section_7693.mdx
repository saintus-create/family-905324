# BPC § 7693

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7693

---

## Text

False or misleading advertising as a funeral establishment, funeral director, or embalmer constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 970, Sec. 72. Effective January 1, 1999.)
