# BPC § 7414.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 12.5. Tanning Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 7414.5

---

## Text

This article shall be applicable only to those tanning facilities operated in conjunction with, or at the same location as, an establishment licensed under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 521, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
