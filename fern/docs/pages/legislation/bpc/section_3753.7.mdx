# BPC § 3753.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 5. Suspension, Revocation, and Reinstatement of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 3753.7

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, costs of prosecution shall include attorney general or other prosecuting attorney fees, expert witness fees, and other administrative, filing, and service fees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1069, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
