# BPC § 4989.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.5. Licensed Educational Psychologists > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 4989.12

---

## Text

The Board of Behavioral Sciences shall administer and enforce the provisions of this chapter. For the purposes of this chapter it shall be designated as the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007.)
