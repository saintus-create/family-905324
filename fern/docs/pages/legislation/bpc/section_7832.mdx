# BPC § 7832

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 3. Scope of Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 7832

---

## Text

Any person, except as in this chapter specifically exempted, who shall practice or offer to practice geology or geophysics for others in this state is subject to the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1396.)
