# BPC § 2362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 15. Osteopathic Physician and Surgeon Diversion Evaluation Committee

**Citation:** BPC § 2362

---

## Text

One or more diversion evaluation committees are hereby created in the state to be established by the board. The board shall establish criteria and appoint the members of the committee pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 384, Sec. 1.)
