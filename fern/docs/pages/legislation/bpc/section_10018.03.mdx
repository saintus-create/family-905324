# BPC § 10018.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10018.03

---

## Text

“Listing agent” means a licensee who provides services requiring a real estate license for or on behalf of a seller pursuant to a listing agreement. Listing agent includes a seller’s agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 11. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
