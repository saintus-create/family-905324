# BPC § 2471

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 22. Podiatric Medicine

**Citation:** BPC § 2471

---

## Text

Except as provided by Section 159.5, the board may employ, within the limits of the funds received by the board, all personnel necessary to carry out this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 588, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2008.)
