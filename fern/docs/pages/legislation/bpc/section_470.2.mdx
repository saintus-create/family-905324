# BPC § 470.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 8. Dispute Resolution Programs > Article: 6. Funding

**Citation:** BPC § 470.2

---

## Text

A county’s share of the funding pursuant to this chapter shall not exceed 50 percent of the approved estimated cost of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1313, Sec. 1.)
