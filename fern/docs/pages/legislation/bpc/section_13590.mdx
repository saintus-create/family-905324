# BPC § 13590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 16. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 13590

---

## Text

It is the duty of the department acting through the Division of Measurement Standards to enforce the provisions of this chapter, and to appoint and employ inspectors as may be necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 591, Sec. 46. (AB 808) Effective January 1, 2016.)
