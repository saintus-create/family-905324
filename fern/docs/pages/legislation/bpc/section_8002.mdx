# BPC § 8002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 13. Shorthand Reporters > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 8002

## Text

Each member of the board shall receive a per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.
