# BPC § 17528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 1.6. Cyber Piracy

**Citation:** BPC § 17528

---

## Text

Jurisdiction for actions brought pursuant to this article shall be in accordance with Section 410.10 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 218, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
