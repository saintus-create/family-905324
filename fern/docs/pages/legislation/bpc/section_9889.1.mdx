# BPC § 9889.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 7. Denial, Suspension and Revocation

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.1

---

## Text

Any license issued pursuant to Article 6.5 (commencing with Section 9888.5) may be suspended or revoked by the director. The director may refuse to issue a license to any applicant for the reasons set forth in Section 9889.2. The proceedings under this article shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, and the director shall have all the powers granted therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 592, Sec. 24. (SB 861) Effective January 1, 2026.)
