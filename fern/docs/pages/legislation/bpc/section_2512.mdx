# BPC § 2512

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 24. Licensed Midwives

**Citation:** BPC § 2512

---

## Text

The board shall issue a license to practice midwifery to all applicants who meet the requirements of this article and who pay the fee required by Section 2520.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1280, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
