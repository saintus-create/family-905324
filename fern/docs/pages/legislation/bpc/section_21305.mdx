# BPC § 21305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 8.5. Consumer Wheelchair Right to Repair

**Citation:** BPC § 21305

---

## Text

This chapter applies with respect to equipment sold or in use on or after January 1, 2025.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 797, Sec. 1. (SB 1384) Effective January 1, 2025.)
