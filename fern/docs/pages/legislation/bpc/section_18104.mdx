# BPC § 18104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Part: 5. Independent Contractors > Chapter: 6. Bots

**Citation:** BPC § 18104

---

## Text

A waiver of any provision in this part shall be deemed contrary to public policy and is void and unenforceable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 870, Sec. 1. (SB 988) Effective January 1, 2025.)
