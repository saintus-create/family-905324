# BPC § 6106.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 6. Disciplinary Authority of the Courts

**Citation:** BPC § 6106.3

---

## Text

(a) It shall constitute cause for the imposition of discipline of an attorney within the meaning of this chapter for an attorney to engage in any conduct in violation of Section 2944.6 of the Civil Code.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2017.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 630, Sec. 2) by Stats. 2012, Ch. 563, Sec. 2. (SB 980) Effective January 1, 2013. Section operative January 1, 2017, by its own provisions.)
