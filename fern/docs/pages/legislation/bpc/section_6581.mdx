# BPC § 6581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 5. Enforcement and Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 6581

---

## Text

A licensee, or an agent working on behalf of a licensee, including an attorney, shall not bill a client or impose a fee on the estate or trust of a client for responding to a complaint filed with the bureau against the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 681, Sec. 6. (AB 3144) Effective January 1, 2019.)
