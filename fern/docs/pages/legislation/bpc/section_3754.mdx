# BPC § 3754

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 5. Suspension, Revocation, and Reinstatement of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 3754

---

## Text

The board may deny an application for, or issue with terms and conditions, or suspend or revoke, or impose probationary conditions upon, a license in any decision made after a hearing, as provided in Section 3753.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1274, Sec. 21.5. Effective January 1, 1995.)
