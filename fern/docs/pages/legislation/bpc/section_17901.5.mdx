# BPC § 17901.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. Fictitious Business Names

**Citation:** BPC § 17901.5

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “manager” means a manager of a limited liability company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1200, Sec. 3. Effective September 30, 1994.)
