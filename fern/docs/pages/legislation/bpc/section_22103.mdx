# BPC § 22103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 22103

---

## Text

“Apply,” “applies” or “applied” mean imprint, emboss or engrave on the article itself and not otherwise.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1943, Ch. 919.)
