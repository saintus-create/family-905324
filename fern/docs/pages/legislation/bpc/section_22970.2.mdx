# BPC § 22970.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.6. CIGARETTE AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS LICENSING ACT OF 2003 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 22970.2

---

## Text

The board shall administer a statewide program to license manufacturers, importers, distributors, wholesalers, and retailers of cigarettes and tobacco products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 890, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2004.)
