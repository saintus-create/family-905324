# BPC § 18801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18801

---

## Text

The salaries and expenses of the members and employees, and the operating expenses of the commission shall be paid by the Treasurer on the warrant of the Controller from the funds appropriated therefor upon the certification of the executive officer.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
