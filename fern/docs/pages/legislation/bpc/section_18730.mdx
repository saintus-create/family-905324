# BPC § 18730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 7. Regulation of Contests

**Citation:** BPC § 18730

---

## Text

A referee shall be in attendance and direct and control at every boxing contest. Referees shall enforce the rules of the commission at every boxing contest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
