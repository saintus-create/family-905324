# BPC § 6159.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 9.5. Legal Advertising

**Citation:** BPC § 6159.1

---

## Text

A true and correct copy of any advertisement made by a person or licensee shall be retained for one year by the person or licensee who pays for an advertisement soliciting employment of legal services.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 121. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
