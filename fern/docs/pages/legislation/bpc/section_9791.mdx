# BPC § 9791

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 19.5. Aircraft Repair

**Citation:** BPC § 9791

---

## Text

This chapter applies only to work done on a general aviation aircraft with either an estimated cost or an actual cost of one hundred dollars ($100) or more.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1001, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
