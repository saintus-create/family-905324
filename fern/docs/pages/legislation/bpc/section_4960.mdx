# BPC § 4960

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 4. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 4960

---

## Text

Disciplinary proceedings under this article shall be conducted pursuant to the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 11.5. Section operative July 1, 1982, pursuant to former Section 4974.5.)
