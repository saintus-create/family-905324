# BPC § 10580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 7. Mineral, Oil and Gas Brokerage > Article: 5. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 10580

---

## Text

Mineral, oil, and gas brokers are subject to the provisions applicable to real estate brokers contained in Sections 10200, 10207, 10209.5, 10210, 10211, and 10222.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 416, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1994.)
