# BPC § 1224

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 2. Administration and Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 1224

---

## Text

The department may, pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, adopt, amend, or repeal any regulations necessary for the administration or enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 510, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1996.)
