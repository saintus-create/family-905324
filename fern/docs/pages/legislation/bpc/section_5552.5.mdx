# BPC § 5552.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 4. Issuance of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 5552.5

---

## Text

The board may, by regulation, implement an architectural education and training experience or internship program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 376, Sec. 9. (SB 608) Effective January 1, 2020.)
