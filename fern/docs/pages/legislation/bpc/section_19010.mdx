# BPC § 19010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19010

---

## Text

“Bedding renovator” means a person who rebuilds, repairs, makes over, re-covers, restores, renovates or renews bedding.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 401, Sec. 58. Effective January 1, 1998.)
