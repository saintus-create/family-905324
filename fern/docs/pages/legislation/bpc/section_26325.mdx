# BPC § 26325

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 26. Medicinal Cannabis Patients’ Right of Access Act

**Citation:** BPC § 26325

---

## Text

This chapter addresses a matter of statewide concern and not a municipal affair, as that term is used in Section 5 of Article XI of the California Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 395, Sec. 2. (SB 1186) Effective January 1, 2023.)
