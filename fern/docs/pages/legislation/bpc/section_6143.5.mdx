# BPC § 6143.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 6143.5

---

## Text

Any licensee, active or inactive, failing to pay any child support after it becomes due shall be subject to Section 17520 of the Family Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 111. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
