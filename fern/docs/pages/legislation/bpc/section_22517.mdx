# BPC § 22517

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 21.5. Money Exchange Houses

**Citation:** BPC § 22517

---

## Text

It is unlawful for a money exchange house to advertise “no commission” when, in fact, a commission or fee is charged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 433, Sec. 1.)
