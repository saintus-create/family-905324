# BPC § 7540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 4. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 7540

---

## Text

For purposes of this article, “licensee” means a licensed private investigator.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
