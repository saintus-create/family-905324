# BPC § 3019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3019

---

## Text

The board shall keep a record of all prosecutions for violations of this chapter and of all applications for licensure and examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 14. Effective January 1, 2005.)
