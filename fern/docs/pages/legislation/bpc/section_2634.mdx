# BPC § 2634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.7. Physical Therapy > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation and Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 2634

---

## Text

The board may investigate each and every applicant for a license, before a license is issued, in order to determine whether or not the applicant has in fact the qualifications required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 829, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1997.)
