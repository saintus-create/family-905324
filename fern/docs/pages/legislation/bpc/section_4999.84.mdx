# BPC § 4999.84

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 16. Licensed Professional Clinical Counselors > Article: 5. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 4999.84

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that any communication made by a person to a licensed professional clinical counselor in the course of professional services shall be deemed a privileged communication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 619, Sec. 3. (SB 788) Effective January 1, 2010.)
