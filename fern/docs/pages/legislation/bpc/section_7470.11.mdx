# BPC § 7470.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10.7. Transportation Network Company Drivers Labor Relations Act

**Citation:** BPC § 7470.11

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, the term “scope of representation” shall mean TNC driver earnings, benefits, and other terms and conditions of work, including deactivations, which shall be mandatory subjects of bargaining.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 335, Sec. 1. (AB 1340) Effective January 1, 2026.)
