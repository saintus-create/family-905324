# BPC § 2712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2712

---

## Text

Five members of the board constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting.
