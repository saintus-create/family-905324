# BPC § 7507.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 8. Conduct of Business

**Citation:** BPC § 7507.4

---

## Text

A licensed repossession agency or its registrants shall not make demand for payment in lieu of repossession and shall not sell collateral recovered under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 340, Sec. 3. (AB 791) Effective January 1, 2014.)
