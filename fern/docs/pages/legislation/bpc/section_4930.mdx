# BPC § 4930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 1. Administration and General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 4930

## Text

Each member of the board shall be appointed for a term of four years.
