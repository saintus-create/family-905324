# BPC § 10012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10012

---

## Text

“Broker,” when used without modification, means a person licensed as a broker under any of the provisions of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 177, Sec. 8. (AB 685) Effective January 1, 2017.)
