# BPC § 10240.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 7. Real Property Loans

**Citation:** BPC § 10240.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this article, exclusive of the provisions of Section 10240, apply only to loans secured by a dwelling.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1355, Sec. 5.)
