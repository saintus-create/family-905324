# BPC § 5671

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 5. Discipline

**Citation:** BPC § 5671

---

## Text

The fact that, in the practice of landscape architecture, the holder of a license has been guilty of negligence or willful misconduct constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 879, Sec. 22.28. Effective January 1, 1999.)
