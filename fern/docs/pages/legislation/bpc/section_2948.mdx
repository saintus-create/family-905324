# BPC § 2948

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 3. License

**Citation:** BPC § 2948

---

## Text

The board shall issue a license to all applicants who meet the requirements of this chapter and who pay to the board the initial license fee provided in Section 2987.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 758, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 1998.)
