# BPC § 2661.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.7. Physical Therapy > Article: 6. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2661.6

---

## Text

(a) The board shall establish a probation monitoring program to monitor probationary licenses.

(b) The program may employ nonpeace officer staff to perform its probation monitoring.

(c) The program shall be funded with moneys in the Physical Therapy Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1150, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2003.)
