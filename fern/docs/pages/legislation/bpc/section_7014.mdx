# BPC § 7014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7014

---

## Text

The board may procure equipment and records necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 37.)
