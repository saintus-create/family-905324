# BPC § 2929

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2929

## Text

The board shall adopt a seal, which shall be affixed to all licenses issued by the board.
