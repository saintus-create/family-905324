# BPC § 22104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 22104

---

## Text

“Person” includes a firm, partnership, association, limited liability company, and corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 1995.)
