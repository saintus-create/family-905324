# BPC § 17771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Trading Stamp Company Act

**Citation:** BPC § 17771

---

## Text

Any person who willfully violates any provision of this chapter shall upon conviction be fined not more than five thousand dollars ($5,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
