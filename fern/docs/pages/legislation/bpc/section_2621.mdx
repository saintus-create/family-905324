# BPC § 2621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.7. Physical Therapy > Article: 2. Scope of Regulation and Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 2621

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as authorizing a physical therapist to practice medicine, surgery, or any other form of healing except as authorized by Section 2620.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 1284.)
