# BPC § 19124.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19124.5

---

## Text

Every person who sanitizes any bedding or filling material shall affix a sanitization label to the article or material in the manner prescribed by the bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1448, Sec. 18.)
