# BPC § 17539.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 2. Particular Offenses

**Citation:** BPC § 17539.6

---

## Text

Any broadcast or print advertisement or notice that contains a 900 number shall be written or spoken in the same language as the language used in a recorded message or by a live operator of the 900 number call.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 944, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
