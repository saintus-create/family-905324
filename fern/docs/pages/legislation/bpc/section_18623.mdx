# BPC § 18623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 18623

---

## Text

A professional or amateur boxer or martial arts fighter is one who engages in a boxing or martial arts contest and who possesses fundamental skills in his or her respective sport.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1989, Ch. 757, Sec. 3.)
