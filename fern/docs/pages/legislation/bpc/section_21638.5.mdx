# BPC § 21638.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21638.5

---

## Text

Sections 21636, 21636.1, 21637, and 21638, insofar as they apply to holding periods for personal property, are not applicable to personal property pledged to a pawnbroker with respect to the redemption of personal property by the pledgor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 510, Sec. 91. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2024.)
