# BPC § 17101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 6. Penal Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 17101

---

## Text

In the prosecution of any person as officer, director or agent, it is sufficient to allege and prove the unlawful intent of the person, firm or corporation for which he acts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
