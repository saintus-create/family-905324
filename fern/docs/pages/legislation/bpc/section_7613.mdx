# BPC § 7613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 1.5. Definitions and Regulation of Cemeteries

**Citation:** BPC § 7613

---

## Text

The bureau shall examine the reports filed with it as to their compliance with the requirements of the Health and Safety Code as to the amount of endowment care funds collected and as to the manner of investment of those funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 13. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
