# BPC § 22382

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 17. Invention Development Services Contracts > Article: 4.5. Remedies and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 22382

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter are not exclusive and do not relieve the parties or the contract subject thereto from compliance with all other applicable provisions of law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 967.)
