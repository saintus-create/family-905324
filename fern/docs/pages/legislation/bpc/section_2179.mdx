# BPC § 2179

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 9. Examinations

**Citation:** BPC § 2179

---

## Text

The Division of Licensing shall insure that nutrition is included on the examination for a certificate as a physician and surgeon.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 889, Sec. 4.)
