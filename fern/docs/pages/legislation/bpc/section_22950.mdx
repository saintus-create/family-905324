# BPC § 22950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.5. STOP TOBACCO ACCESS TO KIDS ENFORCEMENT ACT

**Citation:** BPC § 22950

---

## Text

This Division shall be known and may be referred to as the Stop Tobacco Access to Kids Enforcement Act or the STAKE Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1009, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
