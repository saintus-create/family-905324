# BPC § 2538.31

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 8. Hearing Aid Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2538.31

---

## Text

Practical examinations shall be held by the board at least twice a year. The time and place of any practical examination shall be fixed by the board at least 45 days prior to the date it is to be held.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 9. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
