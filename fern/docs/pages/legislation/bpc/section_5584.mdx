# BPC § 5584

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 5. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 5584

---

## Text

The fact that, in the practice of architecture, the holder of a license has been guilty of negligence or willful misconduct constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1223, Sec. 33.)
