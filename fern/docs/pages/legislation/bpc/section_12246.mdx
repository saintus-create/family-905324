# BPC § 12246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2.1. Fees and Charges

**Citation:** BPC § 12246

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2029, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 575, Sec. 2. (AB 1304) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2029, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 2.1, commencing with Section 12240.)
