# BPC § 19016.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19016.5

---

## Text

A person engaged exclusively in the manufacture of slip covers shall not be required to have a license under the provisions of this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
