# BPC § 6066

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 4. Admission to the Practice of Law

**Citation:** BPC § 6066

---

## Text

Any person refused certification to the Supreme Court for admission to practice may have the action of the board, or of any committee authorized by the board to make a determination on its behalf, pursuant to the provisions of this chapter, reviewed by the Supreme Court, in accordance with the procedure prescribed by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
