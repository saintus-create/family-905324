# BPC § 3003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3003

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “optometrist” means a natural person who is licensed to practice optometry in this state under the authority of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 788, Sec. 19. (SB 776) Effective January 1, 2026.)
