# BPC § 22945.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 31.5. Drug Safety Policies on Social Media Platforms

**Citation:** BPC § 22945.9

---

## Text

This chapter shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2028, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 824, Sec. 5. (AB 1027) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 22945.9. Note: Repeal affects Chapter 31.5, commencing with Section 22945.)
