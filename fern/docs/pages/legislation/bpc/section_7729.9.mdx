# BPC § 7729.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7729.9

---

## Text

A cemetery salesperson’s license fee is payable on each filing of an application for a cemetery salesperson’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 47. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
