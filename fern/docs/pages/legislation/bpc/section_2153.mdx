# BPC § 2153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 7. Reciprocity and National Board Diplomate Applications

**Citation:** BPC § 2153

---

## Text

In addition to the requirements of Section 164, a certificate issued under this article shall include in its description the basis upon which the certificate was issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
