# BPC § 7501.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7501.5

---

## Text

It shall be the duty of the chief to initiate and conduct investigations into the business and affairs of licensees on the chief’s own motion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
