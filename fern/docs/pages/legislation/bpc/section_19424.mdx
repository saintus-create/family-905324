# BPC § 19424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19424

---

## Text

No board member is disqualified from receiving a share of any purse awarded him as the result of any horse race as an owner of a horse or as a breeder of a California-bred horse.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
