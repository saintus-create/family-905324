# BPC § 14259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 2. Model State Trademark Law > Article: 9. Violations

**Citation:** BPC § 14259

---

## Text

Nothing herein shall adversely affect the rights or the enforcement of rights in marks acquired in good faith at any time within common law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 711, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
