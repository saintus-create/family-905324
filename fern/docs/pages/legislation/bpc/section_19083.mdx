# BPC § 19083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 5. Labeling

**Citation:** BPC § 19083

---

## Text

Labels shall be securely attached to the article or filling material at the factory, in a position where they can be conveniently examined.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 787.)
