# BPC § 2525.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 25. Recommending Medical Cannabis

**Citation:** BPC § 2525.4

---

## Text

It is unprofessional conduct for any attending physician recommending medical cannabis to be employed by, or enter into any other agreement with, any person or entity dispensing medical cannabis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 719, Sec. 5. (SB 643) Effective January 1, 2016.)
