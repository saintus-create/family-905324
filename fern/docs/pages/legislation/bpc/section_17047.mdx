# BPC § 17047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 3. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 17047

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any manufacturer, wholesaler, distributor, jobber, contractor, broker, retailer, or other vendor, or any agent of any such person, to solicit any violation of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
