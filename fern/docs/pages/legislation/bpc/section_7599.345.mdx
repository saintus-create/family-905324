# BPC § 7599.345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 12. Alarm Company Operators—Conduct of Business, Prohibited Acts and Citations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.345

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, commencing January 1, 2030, a licensee shall not conduct business under this chapter as a limited liability company.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 484, Sec. 47. (SB 1454) Effective January 1, 2025.)
