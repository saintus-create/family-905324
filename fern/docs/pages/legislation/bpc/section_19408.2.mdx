# BPC § 19408.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19408.2

---

## Text

“Claiming race” means a race in which any horse entered therein may be claimed in conformity with the rules established by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1043, Sec. 3.1.)
