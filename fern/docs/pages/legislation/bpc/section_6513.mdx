# BPC § 6513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6513

---

## Text

The bureau may employ, subject to civil service and other provisions of law, other employees as may be necessary to carry out the provisions of this chapter under the direction of the chief.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 491, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
