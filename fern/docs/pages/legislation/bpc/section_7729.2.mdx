# BPC § 7729.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7729.2

---

## Text

The delinquent renewal fee for a license, registration, or certificate of authority under this chapter shall be 150 percent of the timely fee, but not less than the renewal fee plus twenty-five dollars ($25).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 40. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
