# BPC § 1201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 1201

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” includes firm, association, partnership, individual, limited liability company, and corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1010, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
