# BPC § 19562

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 8. Regulation Generally

**Citation:** BPC § 19562

---

## Text

The board may prescribe rules, regulations, and conditions, consistent with the provisions of this chapter, under which all horse races with wagering on their results shall be conducted in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
