# BPC § 7599.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 11. Alarm Company Operators—Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.21

---

## Text

Any licensed alarm company operator, who maintains or proposes to maintain a branch office as defined in this article, shall apply and qualify for a branch office registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
