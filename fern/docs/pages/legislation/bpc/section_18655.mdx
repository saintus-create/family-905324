# BPC § 18655

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 3. Jurisdiction, Licenses, and Exemptions

**Citation:** BPC § 18655

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to any contest conducted under the supervision or control of an entity of the United States Government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
