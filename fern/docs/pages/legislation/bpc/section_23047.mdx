# BPC § 23047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23047

---

## Text

“Scheduled flight” means a regularly scheduled and advertised flight of an air common carrier but does not mean each daily operation of airplanes upon such flight.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 23046 by Stats. 1957, Ch. 37.)
