# BPC § 4864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 3.5. Wellness Evaluation Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 4864

---

## Text

Each wellness evaluation committee shall elect from its membership a chairperson and a vice chairperson.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 631, Sec. 28. (AB 1535) Effective January 1, 2022.)
