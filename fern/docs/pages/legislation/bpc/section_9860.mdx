# BPC § 9860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 5. Informal Adjustment of Complaints

**Citation:** BPC § 9860

---

## Text

(a) The director shall establish procedures for accepting complaints from the public against any service dealer.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2028.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 89) by Stats. 2023, Ch. 508, Sec. 21. (SB 814) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative January 1, 2028, by its own provisions.)
