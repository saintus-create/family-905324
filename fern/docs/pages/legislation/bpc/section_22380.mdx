# BPC § 22380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 17. Invention Development Services Contracts > Article: 4. Disclosures Made Prior to Contract

**Citation:** BPC § 22380

---

## Text

Every invention developer who charges a fee or requires any consideration for his invention development services must clearly and conspicuously disclose such fact in every advertisement of such services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 967.)
