# BPC § 7619.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7619.3

---

## Text

No licensed funeral director shall engage in or conduct, or hold himself or herself out as engaging in or conducting, the activities of a funeral director without being employed by, or without being a sole proprietor of, a licensed funeral establishment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1151, Sec. 7.5. Effective January 1, 1997.)
