# BPC § 19073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 4. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 19073

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to upholstered furniture, bedding, or insulation manufactured and sold at wholesale in California for delivery outside the borders of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1456, Sec. 8.)
