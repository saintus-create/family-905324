# BPC § 12700.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 7. Weighmasters

**Citation:** BPC § 12700.5

---

## Text

(a) A weighmaster is not required to provide weighing services to the general public.

(b) Weighing for hire is at the discretion of the weighmaster.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 646, Sec. 4.)
