# BPC § 824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 12.5. Mental Illness or Physical Illness

**Citation:** BPC § 824

---

## Text

The licensing agency may proceed against a licentiate under either Section 820, or 822, or under both sections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1183, Sec. 1.)
