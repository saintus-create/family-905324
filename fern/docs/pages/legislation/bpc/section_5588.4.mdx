# BPC § 5588.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 5. Disciplinary Proceedings

## Text

The board may adopt regulations to further define the reporting requirements of Sections 5588 and 5588.1.
