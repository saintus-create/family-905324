# BPC § 4990.42

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.7. Board of Behavioral Sciences > Article: 2. Disciplinary Actions

**Citation:** BPC § 4990.42

---

## Text

The proceedings under this article shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2007.)
