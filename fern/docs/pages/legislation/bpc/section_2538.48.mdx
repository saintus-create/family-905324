# BPC § 2538.48

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 8. Hearing Aid Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2538.48

---

## Text

It is unlawful to engage in the practice of fitting or selling hearing aids in this state without having at the time of so doing a valid, unrevoked, and unexpired license or temporary license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 9. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
