# BPC § 11013.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. Subdivided Lands > Article: 2. Investigation, Regulation and Report

**Citation:** BPC § 11013.3

---

## Text

Taxes and assessments levied by public authority shall not be considered a blanket encumbrance within the meaning of Section 11013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1863.)
