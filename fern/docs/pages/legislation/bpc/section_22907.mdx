# BPC § 22907

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22907

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this act, the notice of lien shall remain in effect, and no new notice of claim of lien shall be required in order to maintain the lien, as long as the dealer remains unpaid for the amounts secured by the lien.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 712, Sec. 13. Effective October 7, 2005.)
