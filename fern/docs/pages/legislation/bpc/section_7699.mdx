# BPC § 7699

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7699

---

## Text

Aiding or abetting an unlicensed person to practice funeral directing or embalming constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 39.)
