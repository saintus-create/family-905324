# BPC § 17764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Trading Stamp Company Act

**Citation:** BPC § 17764

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prohibit any person from redeeming, pursuant to contract, any trading stamps issued on or before December 31, 1997.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 310, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
