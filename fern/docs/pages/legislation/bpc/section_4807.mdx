# BPC § 4807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4807

---

## Text

Five members of the board constitute a quorum for transaction of business at any meeting of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 538, Sec. 6. (AB 1980) Effective January 1, 2011.)
