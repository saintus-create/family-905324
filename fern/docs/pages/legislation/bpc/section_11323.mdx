# BPC § 11323

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Scope of Practice

**Citation:** BPC § 11323

---

## Text

No licensee shall engage in any appraisal activity if his or her compensation is dependent on or affected by the value conclusion generated by the appraisal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 429, Sec. 63. (SB 547) Effective January 1, 2018.)
