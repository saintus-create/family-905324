# BPC § 22250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 14. Tax Preparers

**Citation:** BPC § 22250

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Tax Preparation Act. Whenever a reference is made to the Tax Preparation Act by any statute, it shall be construed to refer to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 597, Sec. 2. (AB 3143) Effective January 1, 2019. Repealed as of January 1, 2028, pursuant to Section 22259.)
