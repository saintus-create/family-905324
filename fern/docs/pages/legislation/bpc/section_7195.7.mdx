# BPC § 7195.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9.3. Home Inspectors

**Citation:** BPC § 7195.7

---

## Text

A home inspector shall not give an opinion of valuation on a property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 267, Sec. 1. (AB 1018) Effective January 1, 2020.)
