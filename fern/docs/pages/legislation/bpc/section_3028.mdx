# BPC § 3028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3028

---

## Text

The Attorney General shall act as the legal counsel for the board and his or her services shall be a charge against it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 26. Effective January 1, 2005.)
