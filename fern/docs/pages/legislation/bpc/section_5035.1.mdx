# BPC § 5035.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5035.1

---

## Text

“Firm” means a sole proprietorship, a corporation, or a partnership.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 303, Sec. 2.)
