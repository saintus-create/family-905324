# BPC § 5082.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 5. Certificates, Information and Records

**Citation:** BPC § 5082.1

---

## Text

(a) The examination required by the board for the granting of a license as a certified public accountant may be conducted by the board or by a public or private organization specified by the board. The examination may be conducted under a uniform examination system.

(b) The board may make arrangements with a public or private organization for the conduct of the examination, as deemed necessary by the board. The board may contract with a public or private organization for materials or services related to the examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 807, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2004.)
