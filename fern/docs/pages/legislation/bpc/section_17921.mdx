# BPC § 17921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. Fictitious Business Names

**Citation:** BPC § 17921

---

## Text

Notice of the date of expiration shall be on the fictitious business name statement form.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 581, Sec. 3.)
