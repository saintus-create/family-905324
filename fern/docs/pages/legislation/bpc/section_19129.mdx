# BPC § 19129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19129

---

## Text

Secondhand or used bedding and any secondhand or used article that can be used for sleeping purposes shall be sanitized under the provisions of this chapter before being sold.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 354, Sec. 67. Effective January 1, 2008.)
