# BPC § 7599.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 12. Alarm Company Operators—Conduct of Business, Prohibited Acts and Citations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.46

---

## Text

No licensee shall aid and abet an unlicensed alarm company operator in any activity for which a license is required. A violation of this section may result in a fine of one thousand dollars ($1,000) for each violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
