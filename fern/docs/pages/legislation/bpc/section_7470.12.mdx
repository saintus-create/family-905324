# BPC § 7470.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10.7. Transportation Network Company Drivers Labor Relations Act

**Citation:** BPC § 7470.12

---

## Text

Each individual covered TNC shall retain exclusive control over the development, maintenance, design, pricing, and implementation of its product and product features, software, contract terms, algorithm, and operations and areas of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 335, Sec. 1. (AB 1340) Effective January 1, 2026.)
