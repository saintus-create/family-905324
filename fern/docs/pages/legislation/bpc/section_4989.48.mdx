# BPC § 4989.48

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.5. Licensed Educational Psychologists > Article: 4. Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 4989.48

---

## Text

A licensee shall display their license in a conspicuous place in the licensee’s primary place of practice when rendering professional clinical services in person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 160, Sec. 7. (SB 1024) Effective January 1, 2025.)
