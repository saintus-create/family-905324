# BPC § 2538.50

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 8. Hearing Aid Dispensers

**Citation:** BPC § 2538.50

---

## Text

It is unlawful to advertise by displaying a sign or otherwise or hold themselves out to be a person engaged in the practice of fitting or selling hearing aids without having at the time of so doing a valid, unrevoked license or temporary license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 510, Sec. 19. (SB 887) Effective January 1, 2024.)
