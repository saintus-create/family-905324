# BPC § 7199

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9.3. Home Inspectors

**Citation:** BPC § 7199

---

## Text

The time for commencement of a legal action for breach of duty arising from a home inspection report shall not exceed four years from the date of the inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 338, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
