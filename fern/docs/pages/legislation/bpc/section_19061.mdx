# BPC § 19061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19061

---

## Text

Every person doing business at the same address under more than one firm name is subject to the license provisions for each firm name.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 46.)
