# BPC § 10015.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10015.3

---

## Text

“Broker associate” means a broker retained by a responsible broker who has authority to provide services requiring a real estate license on behalf of the responsible broker.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 5. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
