# BPC § 22304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 15. Passage Tickets

**Citation:** BPC § 22304

---

## Text

Any person, firm, corporation, partnership, or association or any employee or agent thereof who violates this chapter is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1224.)
