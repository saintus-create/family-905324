# BPC § 7599.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 11. Alarm Company Operators—Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.24

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the application for a branch office registration, the chief shall issue a “Branch Office Registration.” The registration shall be posted in a conspicuous place at the branch office location.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
