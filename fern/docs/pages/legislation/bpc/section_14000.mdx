# BPC § 14000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS

**Citation:** BPC § 14000

---

## Text

Definitions in this division shall not affect the construction of the same terms in any other division of this code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 56.)
