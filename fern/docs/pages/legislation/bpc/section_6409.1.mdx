# BPC § 6409.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5.5. Legal Document Assistants and Unlawful Detainer Assistants > Article: 3. Conduct of Business and Prohibited Acts

**Citation:** BPC § 6409.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the venue for an action arising out of a dispute between a legal document assistant or unlawful detainer assistant and their client shall be the county in which the client has their primary residence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 128, Sec. 10. (AB 1213) Effective January 1, 2020. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 6401.7.)
