# BPC § 10018.06

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10018.06

---

## Text

“Buyer’s agent” means a licensee who provides services requiring a real estate license for or on behalf of a buyer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 14. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
