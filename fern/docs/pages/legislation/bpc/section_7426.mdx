# BPC § 7426

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 10. Barbering and Cosmetology > Article: 13. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7426

---

## Text

The fee for a duplicate license as provided for in Section 7398 shall be ten dollars ($10).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1672, Sec. 3. Operative July 1, 1992, by Sec. 10 of Ch. 1672.)
