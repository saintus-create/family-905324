# BPC § 13474

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 8. Price Indications on Motor Vehicle Fuel Dispensing Apparatus

**Citation:** BPC § 13474

---

## Text

All letters, figures, or numerals which are part of any sign or price indicator required by this article shall be plainly legible. The color or tint shall contrast with the background and other parts of the sign.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 636, Sec. 5.)
