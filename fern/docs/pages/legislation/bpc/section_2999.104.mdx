# BPC § 2999.104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 10. Psychological Testing Technicians

**Citation:** BPC § 2999.104

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to expand or constrict the scope of practice of a person who is licensed under any other provision of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 622, Sec. 1. (SB 1428) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2024, pursuant to Section 2999.105.)
