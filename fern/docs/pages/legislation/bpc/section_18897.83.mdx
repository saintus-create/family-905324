# BPC § 18897.83

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 4. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 18897.83

---

## Text

Every athlete agent shall maintain an agent for service of process in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 857, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
