# BPC § 7582.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7582.15

---

## Text

A licensee shall at all times be legally responsible for the good conduct in the business of each of his or her employees or agents, including his or her manager.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
