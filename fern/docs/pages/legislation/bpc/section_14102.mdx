# BPC § 14102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 1. Good Will

**Citation:** BPC § 14102

---

## Text

The good will of a business is property and is transferable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 57.)
