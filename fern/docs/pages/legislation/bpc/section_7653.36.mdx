# BPC § 7653.36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 3.5. Licenses and Certificates of Authority

**Citation:** BPC § 7653.36

---

## Text

(a) The bureau shall annually conduct a minimum of one unannounced inspection of each reduction facility and each licensed hydrolysis facility.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2027.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 7) and added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 399, Sec. 8. (AB 351) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative January 1, 2027, by its own provisions.)
