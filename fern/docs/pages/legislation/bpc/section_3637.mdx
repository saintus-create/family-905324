# BPC § 3637

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 3. Licensure

**Citation:** BPC § 3637

---

## Text

Only an individual may be licensed under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 485, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2004. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
