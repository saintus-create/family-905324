# BPC § 13462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 7. Standards for Lubricating Oils and Oils Used to Lubricate Transmissions, Gears, or Axles

**Citation:** BPC § 13462

---

## Text

Any manufacturer or packager of any product subject to this article and sold in this state shall provide, upon request to duly authorized representatives of the department, documentation of any claim made upon their products’ label.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1047, Sec. 9.)
