# BPC § 23429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 4. Club Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 23429

---

## Text

A club as defined in this article is a bona fide club within the meaning of Section 22 of Article XX of the Constitution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
