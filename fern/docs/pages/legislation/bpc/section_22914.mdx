# BPC § 22914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 28. Fair Practices of Equipment Manufacturers, Distributors, Wholesalers, and Dealers Act

**Citation:** BPC § 22914

---

## Text

For the purpose of the Secretary of State’s index pursuant to Sections 9516 and 9519 of the Commercial Code and for the purpose of the issuance of a certificate pursuant to Section 9523 or 9528 of the Commercial Code, the Secretary of State shall identify a notice pursuant to this article as a financing statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 1.3. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991.)
