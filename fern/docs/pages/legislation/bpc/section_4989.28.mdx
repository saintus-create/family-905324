# BPC § 4989.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.5. Licensed Educational Psychologists > Article: 2. Licensure

**Citation:** BPC § 4989.28

---

## Text

The board may deny an application for licensure if the applicant is or has been guilty of unprofessional conduct as described in Section 4989.54.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 659, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2007.)
