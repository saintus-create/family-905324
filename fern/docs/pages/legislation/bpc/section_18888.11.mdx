# BPC § 18888.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 17. Mixed Martial Arts Retirement Benefit Fund

**Citation:** BPC § 18888.11

---

## Text

This article shall not be construed to create a liability on the General Fund, and the General Fund shall not be used to offset or fund any liabilities attributed to the operation of the MMA Fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 466, Sec. 1. (AB 1136) Effective January 1, 2024.)
