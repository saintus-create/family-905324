# BPC § 6047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 3. Investigations, Examining Committee, Subpoenas, and Other Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 6047

---

## Text

Subject to the approval of the board, the examining committee may adopt such reasonable rules and regulations as may be necessary or advisable for the purpose of making effective the qualifications prescribed in Article 4.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 34.)
