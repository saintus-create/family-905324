# BPC § 5034

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5034

---

## Text

“Public accountant” means any person who has registered with the board as a public accountant and who holds a valid permit for the practice of public accountancy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 310.)
