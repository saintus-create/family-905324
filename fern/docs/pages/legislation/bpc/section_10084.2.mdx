# BPC § 10084.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. The Real Estate Commissioner

**Citation:** BPC § 10084.2

---

## Text

(a) As existing resources permit, or as private resources are made available, the Homeowners’ Guide to Environmental Hazards prepared pursuant to Section 10084.1, updated pursuant to Sections 13261, 25417, and 25417.1 of the Health and Safety Code, and as required to be updated pursuant to Section 25417.2 of the Health and Safety Code, shall be updated to include three new sections on wildfires, climate change, and sea level rise.

(b) The Department of Toxic Substances Control shall seek the advice and assistance of departments within the Natural Resources Agency in the writing of the booklet to determine the contents of the booklet prepared pursuant to this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 263, Sec. 2. (AB 455) Effective January 1, 2026.)
