# BPC § 4335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 20. Prohibitions and Offenses

**Citation:** BPC § 4335

---

## Text

Any person who knowingly violates subdivision (b) of Section 4312 is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
