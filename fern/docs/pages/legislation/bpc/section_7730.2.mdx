# BPC § 7730.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7730.2

---

## Text

The renewal fee for a cremated remains disposer registration shall be one hundred dollars ($100).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 625, Sec. 58. (SB 1443) Effective January 1, 2023.)
