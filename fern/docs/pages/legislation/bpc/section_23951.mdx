# BPC § 23951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Transfer of Licenses > Article: 1. Applications for Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 23951

---

## Text

The application shall contain the following information:

(a) The name of the applicant.

(b) For a general partnership, the names of the individual partners.

(c) For a limited partnership, limited liability company, or a corporation, the name of the entity.

(d) The location of the premises for which the license is applied.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 639, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 1999.)
