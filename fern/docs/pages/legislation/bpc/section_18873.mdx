# BPC § 18873

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 15. Crimes and Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 18873

---

## Text

Any person who throws any object at the ring during a contest, match, or exhibition is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
