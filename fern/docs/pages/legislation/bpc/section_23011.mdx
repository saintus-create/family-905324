# BPC § 23011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23011

---

## Text

“Salesman” means any individual who solicits or receives an order for alcoholic beverages from any licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
