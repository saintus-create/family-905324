# BPC § 17606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 9. Automatic Purchase Renewals

**Citation:** BPC § 17606

---

## Text

This article shall become operative on December 1, 2010.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 350, Sec. 1. (SB 340) Effective January 1, 2010. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Article 9, commencing with Section 17600.)
