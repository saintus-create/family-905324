# BPC § 7071.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 5. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 7071.15

---

## Text

If a licensee fails to maintain a sufficient bond required by this article, the license is subject to suspension or revocation pursuant to Section 996.020 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 18, Sec. 1.5. Effective April 21, 1983.)
