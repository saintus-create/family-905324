# BPC § 18854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 14. Administrative Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 18854

---

## Text

Any official who fails to enforce the provisions of this act or the commission’s rules and regulations shall be subject to disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 18855 by Stats. 2015, Ch. 316, Sec. 9. (SB 469) Effective January 1, 2016.)
