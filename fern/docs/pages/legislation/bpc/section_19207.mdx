# BPC § 19207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 9. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19207

---

## Text

Any inspector having knowledge of a violation of any of the provisions of this chapter shall notify the chief of the violation.
