# BPC § 5062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 3.5. Standards of Professional Conduct

**Citation:** BPC § 5062

---

## Text

A licensee shall issue a report which conforms to professional standards upon completion of a compilation, review or audit of financial statements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1393, Sec. 1.3.)
