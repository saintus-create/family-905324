# BPC § 7527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7527

---

## Text

The director may require an applicant or his or her qualified manager, to demonstrate his or her qualifications by a written or oral examination, or a combination of both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 569, Sec. 14. (SB 559) Effective January 1, 2018.)
