# BPC § 7580.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 7580.6

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “licensee” means a person licensed under this chapter and includes, but is not limited to, private patrol operator and armored contract carrier.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
