# BPC § 2940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 3. License

**Citation:** BPC § 2940

---

## Text

To obtain a license from the board, an applicant shall submit any applications and pay any applicable fees as prescribed in Section 2987. These fees shall not be refunded by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 456, Sec. 60. (SB 786) Effective January 1, 2020.)
