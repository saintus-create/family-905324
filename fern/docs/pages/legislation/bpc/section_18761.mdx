# BPC § 18761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 9. Martial Arts

**Citation:** BPC § 18761

---

## Text

Rules and regulations of the commission relating to professional boxing shall apply to kickboxing and martial arts, except where specifically excluded by the commission’s regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
