# BPC § 3025.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3025.6

---

## Text

The board may adopt regulations clarifying the level of training and the level of supervision of assistants.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1150, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2003.)
