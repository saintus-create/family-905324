# BPC § 21634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21634

---

## Text

The chief of police or the sheriff who receives a report on a form filed pursuant to the provisions of this article shall daily submit the original to the Department of Justice.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1377.)
