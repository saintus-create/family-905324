# BPC § 17081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 4. Civil Liability

**Citation:** BPC § 17081

---

## Text

It is not necessary for the plaintiff, in any action under this chapter, to provide or file any undertaking or bond for the issuance of any interim or final injunction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
