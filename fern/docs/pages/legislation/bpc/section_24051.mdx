# BPC § 24051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Transfer of Licenses > Article: 4. Issuance and Renewal of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 24051

---

## Text

The department may issue and renew on-sale beer licenses for fishing party boats on payment of the fees without regard to the provisions of Sections 23985, 23986, 23987, 23988, 24013, 24014, or 24043.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 24052 by Stats. 1961, Ch. 73.)
