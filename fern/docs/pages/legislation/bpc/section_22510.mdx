# BPC § 22510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 21. Ticket Sellers

**Citation:** BPC § 22510

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter prohibits any local agency from imposing any local fees or taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 378, Sec. 1.)
