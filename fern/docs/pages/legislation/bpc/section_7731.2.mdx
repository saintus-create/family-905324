# BPC § 7731.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7731.2

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any cemetery corporation to make any interments without a valid certificate of authority. Each interment shall be a separate violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 63. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
