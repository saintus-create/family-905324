# BPC § 4011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 4011

---

## Text

The board shall administer and enforce this chapter and the Uniform Controlled Substances Act (Division 10 (commencing with Section 11000) of the Health and Safety Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
