# BPC § 2271

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2271

---

## Text

Any advertising in violation of Section 17500, relating to false or misleading advertising, constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1313, Sec. 2.)
