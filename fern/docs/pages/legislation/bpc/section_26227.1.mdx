# BPC § 26227.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 7. Powers

**Citation:** BPC § 26227.1

---

## Text

An association may borrow without limitation as to the amount of corporate indebtedness or liability and may make advances to members.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 107. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
