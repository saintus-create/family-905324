# BPC § 7713.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6.5. Crematories

**Citation:** BPC § 7713.3

---

## Text

It is a misdemeanor for any person, firm, or corporation to cremate human remains or to engage in the disposition thereof without a valid, unexpired crematory license. Each cremation shall be a separate violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 35. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
