# BPC § 6008.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6008.7

---

## Text

The State Bar shall, by January 1, 2019, develop purchasing policies that align with the purchasing policies of other state agencies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 422, Sec. 4. (SB 36) Effective January 1, 2018.)
