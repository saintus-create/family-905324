# BPC § 23056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1.5. Administration > Article: 1. The Department of Alcoholic Beverage Control

**Citation:** BPC § 23056

---

## Text

The department shall send a copy of the information sheet prepared by the Department of the California Highway Patrol pursuant to Section 2426 of the Vehicle Code with each renewal notice to any on-sale licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 838, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
