# BPC § 7613.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 1.5. Definitions and Regulation of Cemeteries

**Citation:** BPC § 7613.6

---

## Text

The bureau may administer oaths and examine under oath any person relative to the endowment care fund. The examination shall be conducted in the principal office of the person or body in charge of the endowment care fund and shall be private.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 13. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
