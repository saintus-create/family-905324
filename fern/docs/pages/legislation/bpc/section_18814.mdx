# BPC § 18814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18814

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for a timekeeper’s license shall be fifty dollars ($50) unless the commission establishes a lower fee by regulation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1997.)
