# BPC § 7593.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 7593.9

---

## Text

Each alarm company operator license, together with the current renewal certificate, if any, shall at all times be conspicuously displayed at the place of business for which the license is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
