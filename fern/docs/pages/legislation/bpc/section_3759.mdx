# BPC § 3759

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 5. Suspension, Revocation, and Reinstatement of Certificates

**Citation:** BPC § 3759

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 43.8 of the Civil Code, no person shall incur any civil penalty as a result of making any report required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 553, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1999.)
