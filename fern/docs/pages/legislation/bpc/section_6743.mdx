# BPC § 6743

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 6743

---

## Text

This chapter does not affect Chapter 15 of Division 3 of this code, relating to surveyors, except insofar as this chapter is expressly made applicable.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
