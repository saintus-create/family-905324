# BPC § 2521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 24. Licensed Midwives

**Citation:** BPC § 2521

---

## Text

Any person who violates this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1280, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
