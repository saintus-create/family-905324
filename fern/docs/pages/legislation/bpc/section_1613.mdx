# BPC § 1613

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 1613

---

## Text

The board shall have and use a seal bearing the name “Dental Board of California.”

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 473, Sec. 1. (SB 821) Effective January 1, 2014.)
