# BPC § 9889.68

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 11. Auto Body Repair Study

**Citation:** BPC § 9889.68

---

## Text

Any auto insurance company check or draft issued to a repairer pursuant to Insurance Code Section 560 shall include the repairer’s registration number or Tax Payer Identification Number.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 479, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
