# BPC § 6713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6713

---

## Text

Each appointing authority shall have the power to remove from office at any time any member of the board appointed by that authority pursuant to Section 106.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 588, Sec. 4. (AB 3253) Effective January 1, 2025.)
