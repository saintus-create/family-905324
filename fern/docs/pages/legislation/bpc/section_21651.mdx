# BPC § 21651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 5. Resale of Goods

**Citation:** BPC § 21651

---

## Text

As used in this article,

(a) “Junk dealer” has the meaning defined in Section 21601; and

(b) “Secondhand dealer” has the meaning defined in Section 21626.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1174.)
