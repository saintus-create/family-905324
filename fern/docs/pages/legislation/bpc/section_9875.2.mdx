# BPC § 9875.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.1. Motor Vehicle Replacement Parts

**Citation:** BPC § 9875.2

## Text

Any violation of this chapter shall be enforced by the penalties provided in Section 790.06 of the Insurance Code.
