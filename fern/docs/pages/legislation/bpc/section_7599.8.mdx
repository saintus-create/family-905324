# BPC § 7599.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 10. Alarm Company Operators—Qualifications and Examination

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.8

---

## Text

All applicants shall be examined concerning their competency, experience, and qualifications by the chief. The chief may take testimony of anyone in regard thereto under oath.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
