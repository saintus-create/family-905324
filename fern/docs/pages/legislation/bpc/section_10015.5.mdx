# BPC § 10015.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 10015.5

---

## Text

“Professional identity” includes “responsible broker’s identity” and the identity under which the licensee is authorized to do business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 285, Sec. 7. (AB 2884) Effective January 1, 2019.)
