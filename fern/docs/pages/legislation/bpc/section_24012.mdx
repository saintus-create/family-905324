# BPC § 24012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Transfer of Licenses > Article: 3. Denial of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 24012

---

## Text

Upon receipt by the department of a petition for a license in proper form, the petition shall be set for hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1034, Sec. 2. Effective September 22, 1983.)
