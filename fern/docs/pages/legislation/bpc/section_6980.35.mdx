# BPC § 6980.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 5. Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.35

---

## Text

A licensee desiring to operate a locksmith business at a location other than the principal place of business as shown on the license shall apply for a branch office registration for each additional location as set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
