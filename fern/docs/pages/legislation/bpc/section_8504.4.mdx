# BPC § 8504.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 8504.4

---

## Text

“Inspection” is the act of a field representative or operator physically performing an onsite assessment of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 572, Sec. 3. (SB 1481) Effective January 1, 2019.)
