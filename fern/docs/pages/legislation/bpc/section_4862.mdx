# BPC § 4862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 3.5. Wellness Evaluation Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 4862

---

## Text

Each member of a wellness evaluation committee shall receive per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 631, Sec. 26. (AB 1535) Effective January 1, 2022.)
