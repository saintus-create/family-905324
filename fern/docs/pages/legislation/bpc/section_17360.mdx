# BPC § 17360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 7. Sales of Telephone and Other Communications Equipment

**Citation:** BPC § 17360

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” includes an individual, partnership, firm, association, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 738, Sec. 2. Effective September 13, 1983. Section operative November 1, 1983, pursuant to Section 17365.)
