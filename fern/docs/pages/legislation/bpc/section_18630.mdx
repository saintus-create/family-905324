# BPC § 18630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 18630

---

## Text

“Proprietary interest” means any membership, coownership, stock ownership, legal or beneficial interest, profit-sharing arrangement, or other proprietary interest, designated arranged or held, directly or indirectly in any form.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
