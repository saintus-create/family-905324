# BPC § 7117.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7117.6

---

## Text

Acting in the capacity of a contractor in a classification other than that currently held by the licensee constitutes a cause for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 891, Sec. 28.)
