# BPC § 17903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. Fictitious Business Names

**Citation:** BPC § 17903

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “registrant” means a person or entity who is filing or has filed a fictitious business name statement, and who is the legal owner of the business.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 716, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
