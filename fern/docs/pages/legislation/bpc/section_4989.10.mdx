# BPC § 4989.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.5. Licensed Educational Psychologists > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 4989.10

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited as, the Educational Psychologist Practice Act.
