# BPC § 6023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6023

---

## Text

The officers of the State Bar shall continue in office until their successors are appointed or selected.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 21. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
