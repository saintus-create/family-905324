# BPC § 2638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.7. Physical Therapy > Article: 3. Qualifications and Requirements for Licensure

**Citation:** BPC § 2638

---

## Text

Any applicant for licensure as a physical therapist or physical therapist assistant who fails to pass the examination required by the board may retake the licensing examination and shall pay the reexamination fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 389, Sec. 36. (SB 198) Effective January 1, 2014.)
