# BPC § 1610

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 1610

---

## Text

Eight members of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business at any meeting.
