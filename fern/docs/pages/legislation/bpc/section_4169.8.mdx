# BPC § 4169.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 11.7. Cancer Medication Collection and Distribution: Registry of Participating Practitioners

**Citation:** BPC § 4169.8

---

## Text

This article shall remain in effect only until January 1, 2027, and as of that date is repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 541, Sec. 1. (SB 310) Effective January 1, 2022. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, by its own provisions. Note: Repeal affects Article 11.7, commencing with Section 4169.7.)
