# BPC § 12009.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12009.5

---

## Text

“Distribute,” in any of its variant forms, includes keep for distribution, offer for distribution, or expose for distribution, in any of their variant forms.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 527.)
