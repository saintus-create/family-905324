# BPC § 12024.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12024.8

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 12024.5 shall not apply to fish not used for human consumption.

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 141.)
