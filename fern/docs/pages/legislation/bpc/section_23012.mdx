# BPC § 23012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23012

---

## Text

“Beer manufacturer” means any person that has facilities and equipment for the purposes of, and is engaged in, the commercial manufacture of beer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 686, Sec. 1. (AB 647) Effective January 1, 2014.)
