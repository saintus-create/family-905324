# BPC § 6175.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 10.5. Provision of Financial Services by Lawyers

**Citation:** BPC § 6175.6

---

## Text

The court shall report the name, address, and professional license number of any person found in violation of this article to the appropriate professional licensing agencies for review and possible disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 454, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
