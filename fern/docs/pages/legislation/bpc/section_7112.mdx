# BPC § 7112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7112

---

## Text

Omission or misrepresentation of a material fact by an applicant or a licensee in obtaining, or renewing a license, or in adding a classification to an existing license constitutes a cause for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 728, Sec. 61. Effective January 1, 2002.)
