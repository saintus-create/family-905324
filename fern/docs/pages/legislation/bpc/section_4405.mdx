# BPC § 4405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 23. Revenue and Renewal

**Citation:** BPC § 4405

---

## Text

All fines recoverable under this chapter shall be paid by the magistrate receiving the same to the board, except where other provision is made in this chapter for the disposition thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
