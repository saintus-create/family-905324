# BPC § 7826.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 2.1. Technical Advisory Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 7826.2

---

## Text

Each member of each technical advisory committee shall be an expert in the area of geology or geophysics within the committee’s jurisdiction and shall be licensed under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 432, Sec. 25. (SB 944) Effective January 1, 2012.)
