# BPC § 19413.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19413.2

---

## Text

“Standardbred breeder” means the owner of the dam of a standardbred foal at the time the foal was conceived.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 595, Sec. 2.)
