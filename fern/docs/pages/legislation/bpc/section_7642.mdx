# BPC § 7642

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 3. Embalmers

**Citation:** BPC § 7642

---

## Text

An application for an embalmer’s license shall be written on a form provided by the bureau, verified by the applicant, and accompanied by the fee fixed by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 115. Effective January 1, 2001.)
