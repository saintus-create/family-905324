# BPC § 1605

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 1605

---

## Text

Each appointing authority has power to remove from office at any time any member of the board appointed by that authority under Section 1603 pursuant to Section 106.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 865, Sec. 10. (AB 1519) Effective January 1, 2020.)
