# BPC § 7599.49

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 12. Alarm Company Operators—Conduct of Business, Prohibited Acts and Citations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.49

---

## Text

No licensee shall abandon the completion of any installation of an alarm system without legal excuse. A violation of this section may result in a fine of one hundred dollars ($100) for the first violation and five hundred dollars ($500) for each subsequent violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
