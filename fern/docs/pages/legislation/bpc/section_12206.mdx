# BPC § 12206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 2. Local Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 12206

---

## Text

The jurisdiction of a county sealer appointed by a county or the secretary extends over the entire territorial limits of the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 573, Sec. 49. (SB 800) Effective January 1, 2018.)
