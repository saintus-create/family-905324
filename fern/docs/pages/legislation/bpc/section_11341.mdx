# BPC § 11341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 4. Licenses and Certification Application

**Citation:** BPC § 11341

---

## Text

A license issued with an effective date of January 1, 2000, or later shall be valid for two years unless otherwise extended or limited by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 974, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
