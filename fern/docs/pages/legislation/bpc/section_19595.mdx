# BPC § 19595

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 9. Wagering

**Citation:** BPC § 19595

---

## Text

Any form of wagering or betting on the result of a horse race other than that permitted by this chapter is illegal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended (as amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 198, Sec. 9) by Stats. 2007, Ch. 613, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2008.)
