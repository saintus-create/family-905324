# BPC § 10167.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 2.3. Prepaid Rental Listing Service

**Citation:** BPC § 10167.15

---

## Text

Any person, including an officer, director, or employee of a corporation who willfully violates any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1051, Sec. 2. Effective September 26, 1980.)
