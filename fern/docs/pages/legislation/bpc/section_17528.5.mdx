# BPC § 17528.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 1.6. Cyber Piracy

**Citation:** BPC § 17528.5

---

## Text

In addition to any other remedies available under law, a court may order the transfer of a domain name as part of the relief awarded for a violation of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 277, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2004.)
