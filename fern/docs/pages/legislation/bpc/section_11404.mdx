# BPC § 11404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 7. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 11404

---

## Text

The fee for an original or renewal real estate appraiser license or appraiser trainee license shall not exceed four hundred fifty dollars ($450).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 683, Sec. 9. (AB 2519) Effective January 1, 2013.)
