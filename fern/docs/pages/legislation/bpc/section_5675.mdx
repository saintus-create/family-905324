# BPC § 5675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 5. Discipline

**Citation:** BPC § 5675

---

## Text

The conviction of a felony in connection with the practice of landscape architecture constitutes a ground for disciplinary action. The record of a conviction shall be conclusive evidence thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1535.)
