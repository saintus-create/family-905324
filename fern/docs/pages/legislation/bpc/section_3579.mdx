# BPC § 3579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.8. Polysomnographic Technologists

**Citation:** BPC § 3579

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall apply to diagnostic electroencephalograms conducted in accordance with the guidelines of the American Clinical Neurophysiology Society.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 635, Sec. 1. (SB 132) Effective October 23, 2009.)
