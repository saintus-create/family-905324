# BPC § 4427.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 25. Automated Drug Delivery System

**Citation:** BPC § 4427.1

---

## Text

An ADDS shall not be installed or operated in California unless it meets the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 666, Sec. 9. (SB 1447) Effective January 1, 2019. Operative July 1, 2019, pursuant to Section 4427.8.)
