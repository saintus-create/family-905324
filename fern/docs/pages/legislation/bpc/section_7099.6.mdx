# BPC § 7099.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7099.6

---

## Text

(a) The failure of a licensee to comply with a citation after it is final is a ground for suspension or revocation of license.

(b) The failure of an applicant for licensure to comply with a citation after it is final is a ground for denial of license.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1205, Sec. 2.)
