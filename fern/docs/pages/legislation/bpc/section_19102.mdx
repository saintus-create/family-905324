# BPC § 19102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 5.5. Juvenile Products, Upholstered Furniture, and Mattresses

**Citation:** BPC § 19102

---

## Text

The director may adopt regulations and rules necessary or appropriate for the implementation and enforcement of this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 924, Sec. 2. (AB 2998) Effective January 1, 2019.)
