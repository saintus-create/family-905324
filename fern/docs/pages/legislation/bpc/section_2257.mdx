# BPC § 2257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 12. Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 2257

---

## Text

The violation of Section 109275 of the Health and Safety Code, relating to informed consent for the treatment of breast cancer, constitutes unprofessional conduct.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1023, Sec. 9. Effective September 29, 1996.)
