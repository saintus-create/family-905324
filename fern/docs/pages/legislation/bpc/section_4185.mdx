# BPC § 4185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 13. Nonprofit or Free Clinics

**Citation:** BPC § 4185

---

## Text

The board shall have the authority to inspect a clinic at any time in order to determine whether a clinic is, or is not, operating in compliance with this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
