# BPC § 18758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 8. Amateur Boxing

**Citation:** BPC § 18758

---

## Text

No amateur boxing contest shall be held in conjunction with vaudeville shows, dances, or entertainments of any sort.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
