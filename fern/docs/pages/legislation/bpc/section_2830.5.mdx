# BPC § 2830.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6. Nursing > Article: 7. Nurse Anesthetists

**Citation:** BPC § 2830.5

---

## Text

Every applicant shall show by evidence satisfactory to the board that he or she has met the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 696, Sec. 7.)
