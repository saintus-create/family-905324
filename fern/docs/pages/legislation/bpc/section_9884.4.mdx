# BPC § 9884.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 3. Registration Procedure

**Citation:** BPC § 9884.4

---

## Text

A registration shall cease to be valid when the director finds that any of the information provided by the form specified in Section 9884, which the director by regulation deems material, ceases to be current.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1578.)
