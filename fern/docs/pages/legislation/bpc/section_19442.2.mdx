# BPC § 19442.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19442.2

---

## Text

The board shall, if possible, designate at least one steward at each track where a horse racing meeting is conducted who is a former jockey or at least one steward at each track where a harness meeting is conducted who is a former driver.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 27. Effective January 1, 2001.)
