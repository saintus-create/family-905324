# BPC § 23000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23000

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the “Alcoholic Beverage Control Act.”
