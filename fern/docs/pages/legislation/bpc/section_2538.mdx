# BPC § 2538

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 7.5. Speech-Language Pathology Assistant

**Citation:** BPC § 2538

---

## Text

A person seeking approval as a speech-language pathology assistant shall make application to the board for that approval.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 1058, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
