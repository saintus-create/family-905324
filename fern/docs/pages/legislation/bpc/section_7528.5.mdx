# BPC § 7528.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7528.5

---

## Text

The license shall at all times be posted in a conspicuous place in the principal place of business of the licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
