# BPC § 5015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5015

---

## Text

The board may employ clerks, examiners and, except as provided by Section 159.5, other assistants in the performance of its duties, and pay salaries and necessary expenses.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 716.)
