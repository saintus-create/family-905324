# BPC § 19124

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 6. Sanitization

**Citation:** BPC § 19124

---

## Text

Every person who receives for sanitization any bedding filling material shall sanitize all those articles and material in accordance with the sanitization regulations.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 47. Effective January 1, 1997.)
