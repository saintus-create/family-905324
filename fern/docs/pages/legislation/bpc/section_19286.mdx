# BPC § 19286

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3.1. Household Movers Act > Article: 9. Household Movers Uniform Business Permit Fee Act

**Citation:** BPC § 19286

---

## Text

An adequate transportation system is essential to the welfare of the state, and an important part of that system is service rendered by household movers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 421, Sec. 8. (SB 19) Effective January 1, 2018.)
