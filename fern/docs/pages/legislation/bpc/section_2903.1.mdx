# BPC § 2903.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2903.1

---

## Text

A psychologist licensed under this chapter may use biofeedback instruments which do not pierce or cut the skin to measure physical and mental functioning.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 734.)
