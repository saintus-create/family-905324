# BPC § 18705.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 7. Regulation of Contests

**Citation:** BPC § 18705.5

## Text

The commission shall adopt regulations detailing the criteria for approval of a licensed physician pursuant to Section 18705.
