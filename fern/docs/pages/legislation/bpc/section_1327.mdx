# BPC § 1327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 7. Denial, Revocation and Suspension of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 1327

---

## Text

The department may enter into agreements with the Secretary of Health and Human Services for the use of the services or facilities of the department for carrying out the provisions of CLIA.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 510, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 1996.)
