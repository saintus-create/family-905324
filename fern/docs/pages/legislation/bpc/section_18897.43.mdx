# BPC § 18897.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 2. Professional Athletes and Athlete Agents

**Citation:** BPC § 18897.43

---

## Text

No athlete agent shall knowingly secure employment for persons in any place where a strike, lockout, or other labor trouble exists, without notifying the person of those conditions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 857, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1997.)
