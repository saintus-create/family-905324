# BPC § 12710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 7. Weighmasters

**Citation:** BPC § 12710

---

## Text

A weighmaster may employ or designate any person to act for the weighmaster as a deputy weighmaster and shall be responsible for all acts performed by that person.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 297, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1993.)
