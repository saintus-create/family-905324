# BPC § 679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 7. Nursing

**Citation:** BPC § 679

---

## Text

Any person, firm, association, partnership, or corporation who violates this article is guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1490.)
