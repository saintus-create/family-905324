# BPC § 18662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 4. Applications

**Citation:** BPC § 18662

---

## Text

Any applicant for a license may be required to appear before the commission as it or the executive officer may deem necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
