# BPC § 2531.95

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2531.95

---

## Text

The board shall from time to time adopt the regulations that may be necessary to effectuate this chapter. In adopting regulations the board shall comply with Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 758, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 1998.)
