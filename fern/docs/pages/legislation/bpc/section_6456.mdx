# BPC § 6456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5.6. Paralegals

**Citation:** BPC § 6456

---

## Text

An individual employed by the state as a paralegal, legal assistant, legal analyst, or similar title, is exempt from the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 439, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
