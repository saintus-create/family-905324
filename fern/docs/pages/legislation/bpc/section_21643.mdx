# BPC § 21643

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 9. Secondhand Goods > Article: 4. Tangible Personal Property

**Citation:** BPC § 21643

---

## Text

No transactions that require reporting under Section 21628, or commencing July 1, 2010, Section 21628.2, shall be engaged in with a minor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 335, Sec. 9. (SB 449) Effective January 1, 2010.)
