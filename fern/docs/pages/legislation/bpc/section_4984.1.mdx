# BPC § 4984.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13. Licensed Marriage and Family Therapists > Article: 4. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 4984.1

---

## Text

A licensee may renew a license at any time within three years after its expiration by completing all of the actions described in subdivision (b) of Section 4984 and paying any delinquency fees.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 588, Sec. 66. Effective January 1, 2008.)
