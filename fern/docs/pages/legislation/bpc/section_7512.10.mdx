# BPC § 7512.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.3. Private Investigators > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 7512.10

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “employer” means a person who employs an individual for wages or salary, lists the individual on the employer’s payroll records, and withholds all legally required deductions and contributions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1285, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1995.)
