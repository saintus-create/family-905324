# BPC § 3612

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.2. Naturopathic Doctors Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3612

---

## Text

The California Board of Naturopathic Medicine is hereby created within the Department of Consumer Affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 414, Sec. 2. (AB 2685) Effective January 1, 2023. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 3686.)
