# BPC § 26324

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 26. Medicinal Cannabis Patients’ Right of Access Act

**Citation:** BPC § 26324

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit or otherwise affect the ability or right of a local jurisdiction to regulate adult-use cannabis pursuant to Section 26200.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 395, Sec. 2. (SB 1186) Effective January 1, 2023.)
