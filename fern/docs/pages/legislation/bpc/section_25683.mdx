# BPC § 25683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 16. Regulatory Provisions > Article: 4. Responsible Beverage Service (RBS) Training Program Act of 2017

**Citation:** BPC § 25683

---

## Text

A person shall not perform duties that include the sale or service of alcoholic beverages for consumption on licensed premises, and shall not manage that person, without a valid alcohol server certification.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 847, Sec. 2. (AB 1221) Effective January 1, 2018.)
