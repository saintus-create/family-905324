# BPC § 313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. DEPARTMENT OF CONSUMER AFFAIRS > Chapter: 4. Consumer Affairs > Article: 3. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** BPC § 313

---

## Text

The director shall provide for the establishment of a comprehensive library of books, documents, studies, and other materials relating to consumers and consumer problems.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1262.)
