# BPC § 17550.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 2.6. Sellers of Travel

**Citation:** BPC § 17550.2

---

## Text

“Advertise” means to make any representation in the solicitation of air or sea transportation, and includes communication with other members of the same partnership, corporation, joint venture, association, organization, group, or other entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1123, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995. Section operative January 1, 1995, pursuant to Section 17550.33 (later repealed).)
