# BPC § 17080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 4. Civil Liability

**Citation:** BPC § 17080

---

## Text

Any injunction against a violation of this chapter, whether interim or final, shall cover every article or product and not merely the particular article or product involved in the action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
