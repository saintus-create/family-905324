# BPC § 4373

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 21. Pharmacists Recovery Program

**Citation:** BPC § 4373

---

## Text

No member of the board shall be liable for any civil damages because of acts or omissions that may occur while acting in good faith pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 621, Sec. 78. Effective January 1, 2006.)
