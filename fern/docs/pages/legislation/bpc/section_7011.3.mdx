# BPC § 7011.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 7011.3

---

## Text

The registrar shall not assess a civil penalty against a licensed contractor who has been assessed a specified civil penalty by the Labor Commissioner under Section 1020 or 1022 of the Labor Code for the same offense.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 327, Sec. 5. Effective June 30, 1982.)
