# BPC § 7807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12.5. Geologists and Geophysicists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 7807

---

## Text

A qualified geologist is a person who possesses all the qualifications specified in Section 7841 for registration except that he is not registered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 942.)
