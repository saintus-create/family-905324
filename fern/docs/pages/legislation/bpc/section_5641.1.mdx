# BPC § 5641.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 3. Application of Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 5641.1

---

## Text

This chapter shall not be deemed to prohibit any person from preparing any plans, drawings, or specifications for any property owned by that person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2004, Ch. 691, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2005.)
