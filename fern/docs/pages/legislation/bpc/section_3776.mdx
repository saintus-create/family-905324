# BPC § 3776

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 7. Fiscal Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3776

---

## Text

(a) Any person who submits to the board a check for fees that is returned unpaid shall pay all subsequent required fees by cashier’s check or money order.

(b) Any person who submits to the board a check for fees that is returned unpaid shall be assessed an additional processing fee as determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 991, Sec. 14.4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
