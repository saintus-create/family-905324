# BPC § 7618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7618

---

## Text

An application for a funeral director’s license shall be written on a form provided by the bureau, verified by the applicant, accompanied by the fee fixed by this chapter and filed at its Sacramento office.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 568, Sec. 104. Effective January 1, 2001.)
