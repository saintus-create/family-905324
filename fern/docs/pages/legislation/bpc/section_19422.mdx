# BPC § 19422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 2. General Administration and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 19422

---

## Text

Each member of the board shall have been a resident of this State for two years next preceding his appointment.
