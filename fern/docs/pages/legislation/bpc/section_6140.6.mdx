# BPC § 6140.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 6140.6

---

## Text

The board may increase the annual license fees fixed by Sections 6140 and 6141 by an additional amount not to exceed twenty-five dollars ($25) to be applied to the costs of the disciplinary system.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 659, Sec. 103. (AB 3249) Effective January 1, 2019.)
