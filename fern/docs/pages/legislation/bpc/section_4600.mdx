# BPC § 4600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10.5. Massage Therapy Act

**Citation:** BPC § 4600

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Massage Therapy Act. Whenever a reference is made to the Massage Therapy Act by any statute, it shall be construed to refer to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 406, Sec. 2. (AB 1147) Effective January 1, 2015. Repealed as of January 1, 2030, pursuant to Section 4621.)
