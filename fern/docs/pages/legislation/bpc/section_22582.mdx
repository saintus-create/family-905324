# BPC § 22582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 22.1. Privacy Rights for California Minors in the Digital World

**Citation:** BPC § 22582

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on January 1, 2015.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 336, Sec. 1. (SB 568) Effective January 1, 2014. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 22.1, commencing with Section 22580.)
