# BPC § 8552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 3. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 8552

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to advertise or represent in any manner that any pest control work, in whole or in part, has been done upon any structure, unless the work has been performed by a registered company, except as otherwise provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 430, Sec. 36. (AB 181) Effective January 1, 2016.)
