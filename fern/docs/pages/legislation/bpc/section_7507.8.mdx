# BPC § 7507.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 8. Conduct of Business

**Citation:** BPC § 7507.8

---

## Text

Every advertisement by a licensee, soliciting or advertising business, shall contain the licensee’s name, address, and license number as they appear in the records of the bureau.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1138, Sec. 11.)
