# BPC § 2857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.5. Vocational Nursing > Article: 1. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 2857

## Text

The board shall issue a license designated as a vocational nurse license.
