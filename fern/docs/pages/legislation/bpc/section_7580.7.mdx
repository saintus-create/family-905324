# BPC § 7580.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.5. Private Security Services > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** BPC § 7580.7

---

## Text

(a) As used in this chapter, “qualified manager” means the individual under whose direction, control, charge, or management the business of a licensee is operated, who has qualified pursuant to Section 7582.22, and who is in possession of a current and valid qualified manager’s certificate issued pursuant to this chapter.

(b) This section shall become operative on January 1, 2025.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 23) and added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 571, Sec. 2. (AB 1244) Effective January 1, 2024. Operative January 1, 2025, by its own provisions.)
