# BPC § 6306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Law Libraries > Article: 1. Boards of Law Library Trustees

**Citation:** BPC § 6306

---

## Text

Each board shall appoint one of its number as president.
