# BPC § 3506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 3506

## Text

Each member of the board shall receive a per diem and expenses as provided in Section 103.
