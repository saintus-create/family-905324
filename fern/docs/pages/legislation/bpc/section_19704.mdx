# BPC § 19704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4.5. Mule Racing

**Citation:** BPC § 19704

---

## Text

The board shall issue a license for owners, trainers, jockeys, and other participants in mule racing. The license shall be limited to mule races only.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1133, Sec. 3.)
