# BPC § 3045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 3. Admission to Practice

**Citation:** BPC § 3045

---

## Text

Applications shall be verified by the oath of the applicant and shall contain information and evidence satisfactory to the board showing the eligibility of the applicant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 426, Sec. 32. Effective January 1, 2005.)
