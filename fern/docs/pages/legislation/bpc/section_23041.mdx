# BPC § 23041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23041

---

## Text

“Without the State” means all territory without the boundaries of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
