# BPC § 5518

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5518

---

## Text

The board shall elect from its members a president, a vice president, and a secretary to hold office for one year, or until their successors are duly elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1223, Sec. 4.)
