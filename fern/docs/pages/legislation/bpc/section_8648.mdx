# BPC § 8648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 8648

---

## Text

Authorizing, directing, conniving at or aiding in the publication, advertisement, distribution or circulation of any material by false statement or representation concerning a registered company’s business is a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1348, Sec. 69. Operative January 1, 1987, by Sec. 97 of Ch. 1348.)
