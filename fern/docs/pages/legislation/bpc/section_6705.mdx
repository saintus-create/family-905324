# BPC § 6705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6705

---

## Text

A subordinate is any person who assists a registered professional engineer in the practice of professional engineering without assuming responsible charge of work.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1709.)
