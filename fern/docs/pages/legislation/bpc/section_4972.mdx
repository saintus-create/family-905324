# BPC § 4972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 12. Acupuncture > Article: 6. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 4972

---

## Text

Fees fixed by the board shall be set forth in regulations duly adopted by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 655, Sec. 80. Effective January 1, 2000.)
