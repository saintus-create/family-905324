# BPC § 7599.60

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 13. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.60

---

## Text

Except as otherwise required to comply with the provisions of this chapter, the proceedings under this article shall be conducted in accordance with Section 7591.19.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
