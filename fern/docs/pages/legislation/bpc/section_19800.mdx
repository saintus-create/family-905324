# BPC § 19800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5. The Gambling Control Act > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19800

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known, and may be cited, as the “Gambling Control Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 867, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
