# BPC § 7711.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7711.9

---

## Text

An original cemetery broker’s license is a cemetery license issued to a person who did not have a cemetery broker’s license on June 30 of the fiscal year previous to the fiscal year for which the license is issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 31. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
