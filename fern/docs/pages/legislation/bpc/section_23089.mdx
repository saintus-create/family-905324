# BPC § 23089

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1.5. Administration > Article: 4. Appeals From Decisions of the Department

**Citation:** BPC § 23089

---

## Text

Final orders of the board may be reviewed by the courts specified in Article 5 (commencing with Section 23090) of this chapter within the time and in the manner therein specified and not otherwise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1525.)
