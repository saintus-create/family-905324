# BPC § 1247.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 3.5. Hemodialysis Training

**Citation:** BPC § 1247.8

---

## Text

Each hemodialysis unit or clinic shall have information available for inspection by the department survey teams which verifies that the hemodialysis technicians employed at the unit or clinic meet the requirements of Section 1247.61.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 559, Sec. 9. (SB 112) Effective October 11, 2009.)
