# BPC § 5035.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 1. Accountants > Article: 2. General Powers and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 5035.3

---

## Text

For purposes of Sections 5054 and 5096.12, “firm” includes any entity that is authorized or permitted to practice public accountancy as a firm under the laws of another state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2024, Ch. 586, Sec. 4. (AB 3251) Effective January 1, 2025.)
