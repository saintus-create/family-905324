# BPC § 6560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 6. Professional Fiduciaries > Article: 4. Practice Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 6560

---

## Text

A licensee shall keep complete and accurate client records, and shall make those records available for audit or review by the bureau upon request.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 511, Sec. 36. (SB 1495) Effective January 1, 2023.)
