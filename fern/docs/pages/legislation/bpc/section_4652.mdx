# BPC § 4652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 10.7. Music Therapy

**Citation:** BPC § 4652

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that this chapter do the following:

(a) Provide a statutory definition of music therapy.

(b) Enable consumers and state and local agencies to more easily identify qualified music therapists.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 167, Sec. 1. (AB 1540) Effective January 1, 2020.)
