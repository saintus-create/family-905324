# BPC § 2533.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.3. Speech-Language Pathologists, Audiologists, and Hearing Aid Dispensers > Article: 4. Denial, Suspension, Revocation, and Probation

**Citation:** BPC § 2533.3

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 2538.42, any person who violates any of the provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a misdemeanor punishable by imprisonment in the county jail not exceeding six months, or by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000), or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 449, Sec. 7. (SB 933) Effective January 1, 2012.)
