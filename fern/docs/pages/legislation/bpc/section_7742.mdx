# BPC § 7742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 9. Preneed Funeral Arrangements

**Citation:** BPC § 7742

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall apply to any arrangement, contract, or plan for the issuance of securities now or hereafter authorized under a permit of the Commissioner of Financial Protection and Innovation of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2022, Ch. 452, Sec. 7. (SB 1498) Effective January 1, 2023.)
