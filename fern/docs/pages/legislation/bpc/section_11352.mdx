# BPC § 11352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 5. Reciprocity of Licenses and Certification

**Citation:** BPC § 11352

---

## Text

The director shall adopt regulations governing the process and procedure of applying for temporary practice, which shall meet, at a minimum, the requirements of the Appraisal Subcommittee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 837, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1995.)
