# BPC § 9884.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20.3. Automotive Repair > Article: 3. Registration Procedure

**Citation:** BPC § 9884.12

---

## Text

All proceedings to deny, suspend, revoke, or place on probation a registration shall be conducted pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 307, Sec. 97. (SB 821) Effective January 1, 2010.)
