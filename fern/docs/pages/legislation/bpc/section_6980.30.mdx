# BPC § 6980.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 4. Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.30

---

## Text

A locksmith whose license has been canceled pursuant to this article, may obtain a new license only upon compliance with all of the provisions of this chapter relating to the issuance of an initial license.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
