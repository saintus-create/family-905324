# BPC § 6344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Law Libraries > Article: 3. Duties and Powers of Boards

**Citation:** BPC § 6344

---

## Text

A board may purchase books, journals, other publications, and other personal property. It may dispose of obsolete or duplicate books, and other unneeded or unusable property.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1113.)
