# BPC § 4032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 4032

---

## Text

“License” means and includes any license, permit, registration, certificate, or exemption issued by the board and includes the process of applying for and renewing the same.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
