# BPC § 5667

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 5. Discipline

**Citation:** BPC § 5667

---

## Text

The fact that the holder of a license has obtained the license by fraud or misrepresentation, or that the person named in the license has obtained it by fraud or misrepresentation constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 879, Sec. 22.24. Effective January 1, 1999.)
