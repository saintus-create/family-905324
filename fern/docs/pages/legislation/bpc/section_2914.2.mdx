# BPC § 2914.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 6.6. Psychologists > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2914.2

---

## Text

The board shall encourage licensed psychologists to take continuing professional development in psychopharmacology and biological basis of behavior.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 484, Sec. 5. (SB 1193) Effective January 1, 2017.)
