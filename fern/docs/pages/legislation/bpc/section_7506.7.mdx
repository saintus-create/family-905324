# BPC § 7506.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11. Repossessors > Article: 7. Registration

**Citation:** BPC § 7506.7

---

## Text

Employees of a licensee who are engaged exclusively in stenographic, typing, filing, clerical, in-office skip tracing, or other office activities are not required to register under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 505, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1996.)
