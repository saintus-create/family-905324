# BPC § 3060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7. Optometry > Article: 3. Admission to Practice

**Citation:** BPC § 3060

---

## Text

The board shall periodically develop and disseminate to all persons licensed to practice optometry information and educational material regarding all of the following:

(a) The detection of child abuse and neglect. The board shall consult with the Office of Child Abuse Prevention in developing the materials distributed pursuant to this subdivision.

(b) The detection of elder abuse and neglect. The board shall consult with the Adult Protective Services Division of the State Department of Social Services in developing the materials distributed pursuant to this subdivision.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 556, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1998.)
