# BPC § 4155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 9. Pharmacy > Article: 10. Pharmacy Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 4155

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed as requiring the applicant or holder of a pharmacy permit pursuant to Section 4110 to be a pharmacy corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 890, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1997.)
