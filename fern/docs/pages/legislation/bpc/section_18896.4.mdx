# BPC § 18896.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2.5. Athlete Agents > Article: 1.5. Public Disclosure

**Citation:** BPC § 18896.4

---

## Text

Sections 18896 and 18896.2 do not require the disclosure of information related solely and exclusively to other businesses of the athlete agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 809, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
