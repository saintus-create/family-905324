# BPC § 2603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.7. Physical Therapy > Article: 1. Administration and General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 2603

---

## Text

The members of the board shall consist of four physical therapists, only one of whom shall be involved in physical therapy education, and three public members.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 389, Sec. 6. (SB 198) Effective January 1, 2014.)
