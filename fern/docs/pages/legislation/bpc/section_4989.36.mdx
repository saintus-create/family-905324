# BPC § 4989.36

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13.5. Licensed Educational Psychologists > Article: 3. Renewal and Continuing Education

**Citation:** BPC § 4989.36

---

## Text

A licensee may renew a license that has expired at any time within three years after its expiration date by taking all of the actions described in Section 4989.32 and by paying the delinquency fee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 647, Sec. 38. (SB 801) Effective January 1, 2022.)
