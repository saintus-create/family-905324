# BPC § 10186.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 3. Disciplinary Action

**Citation:** BPC § 10186.9

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, on and after January 1, 2015, the department shall be subject to review by the appropriate policy committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 712, Sec. 13. (SB 706) Effective January 1, 2012.)
