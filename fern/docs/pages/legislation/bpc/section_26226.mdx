# BPC § 26226

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 22. Cannabis Cooperative Associations > Article: 6. Directors and Management

**Citation:** BPC § 26226

---

## Text

The affairs of the association shall be managed by a board of not less than three directors who are elected by the members or stockholders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 107. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
