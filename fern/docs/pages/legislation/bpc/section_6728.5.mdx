# BPC § 6728.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2.3. Professional Engineers Review Committees

**Citation:** BPC § 6728.5

---

## Text

The board may adopt, amend, or repeal, in accordance with the provisions of Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code, rules and regulations necessary to implement these sections.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 1013, Sec. 54. Effective January 1, 2003.)
