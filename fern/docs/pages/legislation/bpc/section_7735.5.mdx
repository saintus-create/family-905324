# BPC § 7735.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 9. Preneed Funeral Arrangements

**Citation:** BPC § 7735.5

---

## Text

The preneed funeral arrangement contract shall clearly state if benefits are unavailable or limited for any reason.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 715, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2002.)
