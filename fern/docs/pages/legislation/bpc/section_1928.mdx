# BPC § 1928

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 9. Dental Hygienists

**Citation:** BPC § 1928

---

## Text

A registered dental hygienist in alternative practice may submit or allow to be submitted any insurance or third-party claims for patient services performed as authorized pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, Ch. 31, Sec. 47. Effective January 1, 2009. Operative July 1, 2009, by Sec. 55 of Ch. 31.)
