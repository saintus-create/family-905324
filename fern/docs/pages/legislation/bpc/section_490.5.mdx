# BPC § 490.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1.5. DENIAL, SUSPENSION AND REVOCATION OF LICENSES > Chapter: 3. Suspension and Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 490.5

---

## Text

A board may suspend a license pursuant to Section 17520 of the Family Code if a licensee is not in compliance with a child support order or judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 328, Sec. 3. (SB 1330) Effective January 1, 2011.)
