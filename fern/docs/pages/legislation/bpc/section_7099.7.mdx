# BPC § 7099.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7099.7

---

## Text

No order for payment of a civil penalty shall be made against any bond required pursuant to Sections 7071.5 to 7071.8.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 1013, Sec. 24.)
