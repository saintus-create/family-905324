# BPC § 23028

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23028

---

## Text

“Package” means any container or receptacle used for holding alcoholic beverages which is corked or sealed with a stub, stopper, cap, or in any other manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
