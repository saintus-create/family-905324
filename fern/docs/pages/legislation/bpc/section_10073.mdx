# BPC § 10073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 2. Administration > Article: 1. The Real Estate Commissioner

**Citation:** BPC § 10073

---

## Text

The commissioner shall employ deputies, clerks, and employees as needed to discharge in the proper manner the duties imposed upon the commissioner by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 786, Sec. 5. (SB 774) Effective January 1, 2026.)
