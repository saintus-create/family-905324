# BPC § 19012.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 19012.5

---

## Text

“Sanitizer” means a person who sanitizes articles of bedding or filling materials.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1448, Sec. 6.)
