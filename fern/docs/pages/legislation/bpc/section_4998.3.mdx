# BPC § 4998.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 14. Social Workers > Article: 5. Licensed Clinical Social Workers Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 4998.3

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 13403 of the Corporations Code, each director, shareholder, and officer of a licensed clinical social worker corporation shall be a licensed person as defined in the Moscone-Knox Professional Corporation Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 4998.4 by Stats. 1999, Ch. 657, Sec. 21. Effective January 1, 2000.)
