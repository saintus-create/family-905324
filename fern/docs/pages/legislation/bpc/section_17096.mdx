# BPC § 17096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 5. Liability of Agents

**Citation:** BPC § 17096

---

## Text

In any injunction proceeding against any person as officer, director or agent, it is sufficient to allege and prove the unlawful intent of the person, firm or corporation for which he acts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
