# BPC § 17365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 7. Sales of Telephone and Other Communications Equipment

**Citation:** BPC § 17365

---

## Text

This chapter shall become operative on November 1, 1983.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 738, Sec. 2. Effective September 13, 1983. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date for Chapter 7, commencing with Section 17360.)
