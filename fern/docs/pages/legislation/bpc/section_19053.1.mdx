# BPC § 19053.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 19053.1

---

## Text

Every importer shall hold an importer’s license.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1137, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 1997.)
