# BPC § 3705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 3705

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as authorizing a respiratory care practitioner to practice medicine, surgery, or any other form of healing, except as authorized by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1344, Sec. 1. Operative July 1, 1983, by Sec. 3 of Ch. 1344.)
