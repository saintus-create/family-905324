# BPC § 7614.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 1.5. Definitions and Regulation of Cemeteries

**Citation:** BPC § 7614.8

---

## Text

Any security or other evidence of debt if in default as to principal or interest or if not amply secured shall not be valued as an asset of the endowment care fund above its market value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 13. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
