# BPC § 6303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 5. Law Libraries > Article: 1. Boards of Law Library Trustees

**Citation:** BPC § 6303

---

## Text

The office of trustee is honorary, without salary or other compensation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 452.)
