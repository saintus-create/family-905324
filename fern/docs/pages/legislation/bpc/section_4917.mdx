# BPC § 4917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 6. Veterinary Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 4917

---

## Text

Nothing in this article requires an applicant for or a holder of a certificate of registration of veterinary premises described in Section 4853 to be a veterinary corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1578, Sec. 1.)
