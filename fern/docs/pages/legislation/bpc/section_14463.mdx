# BPC § 14463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 3. Trade Names and Designations > Article: 3. Farm Names

**Citation:** BPC § 14463

---

## Text

Registration under this article shall have the same effect as the registration of a trade-mark.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 59.)
