# BPC § 23026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 23026

---

## Text

“Retail sale” or “sale at retail” means the sale by an on- or off-sale licensee for consumption and not for resale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
