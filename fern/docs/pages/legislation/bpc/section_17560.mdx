# BPC § 17560

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 3. Motel and Motor Court Rate Signs

**Citation:** BPC § 17560

---

## Text

“Outdoor sign” or “outside sign” as used in this article means any sign visible to passers-by whether the same shall be located within or without buildings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 975.)
