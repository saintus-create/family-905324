# BPC § 23954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Transfer of Licenses > Article: 1. Applications for Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 23954

---

## Text

The application shall be verified under oath and accompanied by the license fee.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 152.)
