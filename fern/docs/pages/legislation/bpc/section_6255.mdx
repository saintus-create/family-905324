# BPC § 6255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 17. The California Consumer Legal Funding Act

**Citation:** BPC § 6255

---

## Text

The contingent right to receive an amount of the potential proceeds of a legal claim is assignable by a consumer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 565, Sec. 3. (AB 931) Effective January 1, 2026.)
