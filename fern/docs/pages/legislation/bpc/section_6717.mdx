# BPC § 6717

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 7. Professional Engineers > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 6717

---

## Text

The board may, by regulation, define the scope of each branch of professional engineering other than civil, electrical, and mechanical engineering for which registration is provided under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1006, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
