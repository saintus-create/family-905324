# BPC § 22461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 20. Professional Photocopiers

**Citation:** BPC § 22461

---

## Text

A registrant whose certificate of registration is suspended or revoked shall be entitled to challenge the decision in a court of competent jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1005, Sec. 1.)
