# BPC § 1283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 5. Offenses Against the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 1283

---

## Text

It is unlawful for any person to conduct, maintain, or operate a clinical laboratory unless he is a duly licensed physician and surgeon or is duly authorized to do so under the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1284 by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1377.)
