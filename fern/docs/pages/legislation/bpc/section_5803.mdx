# BPC § 5803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.9. Interior Designers

**Citation:** BPC § 5803

---

## Text

A Certified Interior Designer, as defined in this chapter, is exempt from Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 7000) of Division 3 insofar as they are designing systems for work to be performed by a licensed contractor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 723, Sec. 26. (SB 816) Effective January 1, 2024. Repealed as of January 1, 2027, pursuant to Section 5810.)
