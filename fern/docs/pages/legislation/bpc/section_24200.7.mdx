# BPC § 24200.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 7. Suspension and Revocation of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 24200.7

---

## Text

The department shall revoke or suspend any license if the licensee or the agent or employee of the licensee manufactures, distributes, or offers for retail sale powdered alcohol.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 778, Sec. 4. (SB 819) Effective January 1, 2017.)
