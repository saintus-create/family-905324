# BPC § 22177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 12. Precious Metal Marking > Article: 3. Gold

**Citation:** BPC § 22177

---

## Text

A quality mark need not be applied to any article, but if a quality mark is applied to any article it shall be applied in accordance with the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 319.)
