# BPC § 17506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 1. Advertising > Article: 1. False Advertising in General

**Citation:** BPC § 17506

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” includes any individual, partnership, firm, association, or corporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 664.)
