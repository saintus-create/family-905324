# BPC § 7596.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 6. Firearms Permits and Permits for Other Deadly and Nonlethal Weapons

**Citation:** BPC § 7596.5

---

## Text

(a) The firearms qualification card shall be mailed to the applicant at the address that appears on the application.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2018.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 50) and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 429, Sec. 51. (SB 547) Effective January 1, 2018. Section operative July 1, 2018, by its own provisions.)
