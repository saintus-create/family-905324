# BPC § 12019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 12019

---

## Text

A false weight or measure is one which does not conform to the standard established or recognized by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 43.)
