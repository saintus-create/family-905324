# BPC § 5500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 5500

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, architect means a person who is licensed to practice architecture in this state under the authority of this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1223, Sec. 1.)
