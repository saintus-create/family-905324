# BPC § 17025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. GENERAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Unfair Trade Practices > Article: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 17025

---

## Text

“Vendor” includes any person who performs work upon, renovates, alters or improves any personal property belonging to another person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 526.)
