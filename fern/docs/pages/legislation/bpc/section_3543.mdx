# BPC § 3543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 7.7. Physician Assistants > Article: 8. Physicians’ Assistants Corporations

**Citation:** BPC § 3543

---

## Text

The name of a physician assistant corporation and any name or names under which it may render professional services shall contain the words “physician assistant,” and wording or abbreviations denoting corporate existence.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 26, Sec. 123. Effective March 30, 1994.)
