# BPC § 2460.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 22. Podiatric Medicine

**Citation:** BPC § 2460.1

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other law, the repeal of Section 2460 renders the Podiatric Medical Board of California subject to review by the appropriate policy committees of the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 194, Sec. 2. (AB 1501) Effective January 1, 2026.)
