# BPC § 9870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 20. Electronic and Appliance Repair Dealers > Article: 6. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 9870

---

## Text

All fees collected pursuant to this chapter shall be paid into the State Treasury to the credit of the Household Goods and Services Fund, which fund is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 508, Sec. 25. (SB 814) Effective January 1, 2024.)
