# BPC § 5351

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 2. Advertisers > Article: 6. Permits

**Citation:** BPC § 5351

---

## Text

Every person desiring a permit to place any advertising display shall file an application with the director or with his authorized agent.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1003, Sec. 1. Effective September 11, 1984.)
