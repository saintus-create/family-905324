# BPC § 4853.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 11. Veterinary Medicine > Article: 3. Issuance of Licenses

**Citation:** BPC § 4853.1

---

## Text

Each application to register a premises pursuant to Section 4853 shall be made on a form provided by the board.
