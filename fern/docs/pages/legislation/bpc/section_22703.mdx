# BPC § 22703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 23. Tanning Facilities

**Citation:** BPC § 22703

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to a phototherapy device used by or under the direct supervision of a licensed physician who is trained in the use of phototherapy devices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 808, Sec. 1.)
