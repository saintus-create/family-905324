# BPC § 1767

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 4. Dentistry > Article: 7. Dental Auxiliaries

**Citation:** BPC § 1767

## Text

The board shall adopt regulations necessary to implement the provisions of this article.
