# BPC § 7624

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 2. Funeral Establishments and Directors

**Citation:** BPC § 7624

---

## Text

Not more than one person, partnership, association, corporation, or other organization engaged in business as a funeral establishment shall transact business in one specific funeral facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1151, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1997.)
