# BPC § 22680

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 22.8. Content Moderation Requirements for Internet Terms of Service

**Citation:** BPC § 22680

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to a social media company that generated less than one hundred million dollars ($100,000,000) in gross revenue during the preceding calendar year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 269, Sec. 2. (AB 587) Effective January 1, 2023.)
