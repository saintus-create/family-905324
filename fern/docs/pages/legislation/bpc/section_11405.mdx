# BPC § 11405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 7. Fees

**Citation:** BPC § 11405

---

## Text

The fee for an original or renewal certification as a state certified real estate appraiser shall not exceed five hundred twenty-five dollars ($525).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 683, Sec. 10. (AB 2519) Effective January 1, 2013.)
