# BPC § 26056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 5. Licensing

**Citation:** BPC § 26056

---

## Text

The requirements of Sections 13143.9, 13145, and 13146 of the Health and Safety Code shall apply to all licensees.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 44. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017.)
