# BPC § 13477

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 14. Fuels and Lubricants > Article: 8. Price Indications on Motor Vehicle Fuel Dispensing Apparatus

**Citation:** BPC § 13477

---

## Text

The provisions of this article do not apply to the sale of motor vehicle fuel for aircraft through or from any portable dispensing device.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 591, Sec. 30. (AB 808) Effective January 1, 2016.)
