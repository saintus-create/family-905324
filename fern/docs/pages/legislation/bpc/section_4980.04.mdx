# BPC § 4980.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 13. Licensed Marriage and Family Therapists > Article: 1. Regulation

**Citation:** BPC § 4980.04

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Licensed Marriage and Family Therapist Act.
