# BPC § 10249.91

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 3. Real Estate Regulations > Article: 8. Out-of-State Land Promotions

**Citation:** BPC § 10249.91

---

## Text

The term of a registration issued pursuant to this article shall be one year, unless the commissioner by regulation prescribes a longer term.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 723, Sec. 12. Effective January 1, 1996.)
