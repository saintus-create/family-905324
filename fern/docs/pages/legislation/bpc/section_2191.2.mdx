# BPC § 2191.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 10. Continuing Medical Education

**Citation:** BPC § 2191.2

---

## Text

The division shall encourage every physician and surgeon to take a course in geriatric medicine, including geriatric pharmacology, as part of his or her continuing education.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 440, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2001.)
