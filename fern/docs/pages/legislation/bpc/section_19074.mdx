# BPC § 19074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 3. Home Furnishings > Article: 4. Application of the Chapter

**Citation:** BPC § 19074

---

## Text

This chapter does not apply to the manufacturer of insulating window or door glass or products containing insulating window or door glass.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 925, Sec. 10. Effective September 22, 1987.)
