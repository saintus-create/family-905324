# BPC § 7730.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 8. Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 7730.9

---

## Text

The fee for filing a report of a change of designated manager or a request for approval to share a designated cemetery manager shall not exceed fifty dollars ($50).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 395, Sec. 58. (AB 180) Effective January 1, 2016.)
