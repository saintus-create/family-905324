# BPC § 11345.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. REAL ESTATE > Chapter: 4. Licenses and Certification Application

**Citation:** BPC § 11345.1

---

## Text

A certificate of registration as an appraisal management company shall be valid for a period of two years, unless otherwise extended or limited by the director.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 173, Sec. 11. (SB 237) Effective January 1, 2010.)
