# BPC § 2515.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 24. Licensed Midwives

**Citation:** BPC § 2515.5

---

## Text

Each applicant shall show by evidence satisfactory to the board that he or she has met the educational standards established by the board pursuant to this article or the equivalent thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1280, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
