# BPC § 7599.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 11.6. Alarm Companies > Article: 11. Alarm Company Operators—Licensed and Registered Locations

**Citation:** BPC § 7599.22

---

## Text

The application for a branch office registration under this article shall be on a form prescribed by the director and shall be accompanied by the fee as set forth in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1210, Sec. 12.)
