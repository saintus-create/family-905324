# BPC § 8695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 9. Financial Responsibility

**Citation:** BPC § 8695

---

## Text

The violation of any provision of this article is a misdemeanor and shall be grounds for the suspension or revocation by the board of the operator’s license of the owner or qualifying manager or managers of the registered company and of the company registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1348, Sec. 90. Operative January 1, 1987, by Sec. 97 of Ch. 1348.)
