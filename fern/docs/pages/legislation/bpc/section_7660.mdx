# BPC § 7660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 4. Apprentices

**Citation:** BPC § 7660

---

## Text

An apprentice embalmer is a person engaged in the study of embalming under the instruction and supervision of a licensed embalmer who has had at least two years’ practical experience as a California licensed embalmer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1151, Sec. 23. Effective January 1, 1997.)
