# BPC § 3762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 8.3. Respiratory Therapy > Article: 6. Offenses and Enforcement

**Citation:** BPC § 3762

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter is intended to limit, preclude, or otherwise interfere with the practices of other licensed personnel in carrying out authorized and customary duties and functions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1274, Sec. 25. Effective January 1, 1995.)
