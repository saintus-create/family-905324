# BPC § 19662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 11. Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 19662

---

## Text

Any person who bets upon the results of a horse race except by a parimutuel method of wagering conducted by a person licensed under Article 4 of this chapter is punishable as provided in the Penal Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2000, Ch. 1082, Sec. 112. Effective January 1, 2001.)
