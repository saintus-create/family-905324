# BPC § 18807

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 12. Revenue and Fiscal Affairs

**Citation:** BPC § 18807

---

## Text

The application and renewal fee for a boxing, kickboxing, or martial arts assistant matchmaker’s license shall be two hundred dollars ($200).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1057, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
