# BPC § 7704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 12. Cemetery and Funeral Act > Article: 6. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7704

---

## Text

Violation of any state law or municipal or county ordinance or regulation affecting the handling, custody, care or transportation of human remains constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 970, Sec. 78. Effective January 1, 1999.)
