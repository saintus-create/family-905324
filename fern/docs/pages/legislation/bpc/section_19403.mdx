# BPC § 19403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 4. Horse Racing > Article: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 19403

---

## Text

“Association” means any person engaged in the conduct of a recognized horse race meeting.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1828.)
