# BPC § 26065

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. CANNABIS > Chapter: 6. Licensed Cultivation Sites

**Citation:** BPC § 26065

---

## Text

An employee engaged in the cultivation of cannabis under this division shall be subject to Wage Order No. 4-2001 of the Industrial Welfare Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 27, Sec. 55. (SB 94) Effective June 27, 2017. Note: This section was added on Nov. 8, 2016, by initiative Prop. 64.)
