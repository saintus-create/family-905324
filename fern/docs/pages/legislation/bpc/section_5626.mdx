# BPC § 5626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3.5. Landscape Architecture > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** BPC § 5626

---

## Text

The executive officer shall keep an accurate record of all proceedings of the landscape architects committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 879, Sec. 22.6. Effective January 1, 1999.)
