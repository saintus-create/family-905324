# BPC § 20003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 5.5. Franchise Relations > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** BPC § 20003

---

## Text

A “franchisor” is a person who grants or has granted a franchise.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1355, Sec. 1. Effective October 1, 1980. Operative January 1, 1981, by Sec. 6 of Ch. 1355.)
