# BPC § 14100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BUSINESS RIGHTS > Chapter: 1. Good Will

**Citation:** BPC § 14100

---

## Text

The “good will” of a business is the expectation of continued public patronage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 57.)
