# BPC § 1311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 3. Clinical Laboratory Technology > Article: 6.5. Penalties

**Citation:** BPC § 1311

---

## Text

The department shall have three years from the date of a violation of this chapter or of a regulation adopted thereunder to file a civil or administrative action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 322, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 2001.)
