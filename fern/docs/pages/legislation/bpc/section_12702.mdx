# BPC § 12702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. WEIGHTS AND MEASURES > Chapter: 7. Weighmasters

**Citation:** BPC § 12702

---

## Text

“Weighmaster,” when used without qualification, includes a deputy weighmaster.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 646, Sec. 4.)
