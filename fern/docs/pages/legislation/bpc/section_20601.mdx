# BPC § 20601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 6.5. Video Arcades

**Citation:** BPC § 20601

---

## Text

A video arcade shall make available upon request a brochure to consumers that explains the rating system described in Section 20600.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 748, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
