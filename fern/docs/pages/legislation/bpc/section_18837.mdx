# BPC § 18837

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 2. Boxing, Wrestling, and Martial Arts > Article: 13. Closed Circuit Telecast Revenue

**Citation:** BPC § 18837

---

## Text

This article does not apply to a cable television’s system franchised under Section 53066 of the Government Code.

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1092, Sec. 2.)
