# BPC § 6980.68

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 8.5. Locksmiths > Article: 7. Conduct of Business

**Citation:** BPC § 6980.68

---

## Text

No licensee shall willfully or deliberately disregard any building or safety laws of the state or any political subdivision thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1263, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 1994.)
