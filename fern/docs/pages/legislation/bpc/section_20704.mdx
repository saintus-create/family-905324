# BPC § 20704

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. SPECIAL BUSINESS REGULATIONS > Chapter: 7. Industrial Hygienist Definition and Registration Act > Article: 4. Unfair Business Practices

**Citation:** BPC § 20704

---

## Text

It is an unfair business practice for any person to represent themselves as a “certified industrial hygienist” or a “CIH” unless they comply with the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1021, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
