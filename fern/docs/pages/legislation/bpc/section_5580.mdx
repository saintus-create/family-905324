# BPC § 5580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 3. Architecture > Article: 5. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 5580

---

## Text

The fact that the holder of a license is impersonating an architect or former architect of the same or similar name, or is practicing under an assumed, fictitious, or corporate name, constitutes a ground for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 184, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1997.)
