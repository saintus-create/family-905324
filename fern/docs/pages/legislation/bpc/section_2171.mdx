# BPC § 2171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5. Medicine > Article: 9. Examinations

**Citation:** BPC § 2171

---

## Text

All examinations shall be designed to ascertain the applicant’s fitness to practice medicine. Unless otherwise provided the examination shall be in writing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 983, Sec. 3.)
