# BPC § 22990.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8.6. CIGARETTE AND TOBACCO PRODUCTS LICENSING ACT OF 2003 > Chapter: 6. Disposition of Funds

**Citation:** BPC § 22990.7

---

## Text

(a) The board shall report to the Legislature, Governor, and Department of Finance on or before January 1, 2019, and on and before January 1 annually thereafter, regarding the adequacy of funding for the Cigarette and Tobacco Products Licensing Act of 2003. The report shall include data and recommendations about whether the annual licensing fee funding levels are set at an appropriate level to maintain an effective enforcement program.

(b) The report to the Legislature required by subdivision (a) shall be submitted in compliance with Section 9795 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 699, Sec. 6. (AB 2770) Effective January 1, 2017.)
