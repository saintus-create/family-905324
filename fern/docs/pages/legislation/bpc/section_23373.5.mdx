# BPC § 23373.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. ALCOHOLIC BEVERAGES > Chapter: 3. Licenses and Fees > Article: 3. Rights and Obligations of Licensees

**Citation:** BPC § 23373.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall preclude the department from taking disciplinary action against a winegrower or brandy manufacturer for any violation of this division when such violation was committed by the holder of a California winegrower’s agent’s license while acting on behalf of the winegrower or brandy manufacturer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 783.)
