# BPC § 8508

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 14. Structural Pest Control Operators > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** BPC § 8508

---

## Text

“Household” means any structure and its contents that are used for persons and their convenience.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 430, Sec. 33. (AB 181) Effective January 1, 2016.)
