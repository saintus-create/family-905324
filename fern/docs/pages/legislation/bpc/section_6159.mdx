# BPC § 6159

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 4. Attorneys > Article: 9.5. Legal Advertising

**Citation:** BPC § 6159

---

## Text

The court shall report the name, address, and professional license number of any person found in violation of this article to the appropriate professional licensing agency for review and possible disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 6157.5 by Stats. 1994, Ch. 711, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 1995.)
