# BPC § 2570.26

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. HEALING ARTS > Chapter: 5.6. Occupational Therapy

**Citation:** BPC § 2570.26

---

## Text

(a) The board may, after a hearing, deny, suspend, revoke, or place on probation a license, inactive license, or limited permit.

(b) As used in this chapter, “license” includes a license, limited permit, or any other authorization to engage in practice regulated by this chapter.

(c) The proceedings under this section shall be conducted in accordance with Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 11500) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, and the board shall have all the powers granted therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 307, Sec. 25. (SB 821) Effective January 1, 2010.)
