# BPC § 7107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. PROFESSIONS AND VOCATIONS GENERALLY > Chapter: 9. Contractors > Article: 7. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** BPC § 7107

---

## Text

Abandonment without legal excuse of any construction project or operation engaged in or undertaken by the licensee as a contractor constitutes a cause for disciplinary action.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 37.)
