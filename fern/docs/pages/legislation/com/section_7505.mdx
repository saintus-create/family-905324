# COM § 7505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE > Chapter: 5. Warehouse Receipts and Bills of Lading: Negotiation and Transfer

**Citation:** COM § 7505

---

## Text

The indorsement of a tangible document of title issued by a bailee does not make the indorser liable for any default by the bailee or previous endorsers.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 254, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 2007.)
