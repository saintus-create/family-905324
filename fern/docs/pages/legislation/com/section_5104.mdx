# COM § 5104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. LETTERS OF CREDIT

**Citation:** COM § 5104

---

## Text

A letter of credit, confirmation, advice, transfer, amendment, or cancellation may be issued in any form that is a signed record.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 210, Sec. 15. (SB 95) Effective January 1, 2024.)
