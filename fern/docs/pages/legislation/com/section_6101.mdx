# COM § 6101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. BULK SALES

**Citation:** COM § 6101

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as Uniform Commercial Code—Bulk Sales.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1191, Sec. 3.)
