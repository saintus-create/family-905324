# COM § 13103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. EFFECTIVE DATE AND REPEALER

**Citation:** COM § 13103

---

## Text

Except as provided in the following section, all acts and parts of acts inconsistent with this act are hereby repealed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1359, Sec. 8. Operative January 1, 1990, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 1359.)
