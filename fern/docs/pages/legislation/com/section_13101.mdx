# COM § 13101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. EFFECTIVE DATE AND REPEALER

**Citation:** COM § 13101

---

## Text

This code shall become effective on January 1, 1965. It applies to transactions entered into and events occurring after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1359, Sec. 8. Operative January 1, 1990, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 1359.)
