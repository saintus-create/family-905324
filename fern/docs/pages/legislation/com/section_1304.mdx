# COM § 1304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Territorial Applicability and General Rules

**Citation:** COM § 1304

---

## Text

Every contract or duty within this code imposes an obligation of good faith in its performance and enforcement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1203 by Stats. 2006, Ch. 254, Sec. 19. Effective January 1, 2007.)
