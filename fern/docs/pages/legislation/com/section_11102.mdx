# COM § 11102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. FUNDS TRANSFERS > Chapter: 1. Subject Matter and Definitions

**Citation:** COM § 11102

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 11108, this division applies to funds transfers defined in Section 11104.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 125, Sec. 2.)
