# COM § 2800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SALES > Chapter: 8. Retail Sales

**Citation:** COM § 2800

---

## Text

As used in this chapter “goods” means goods used or bought for use primarily for personal, family or household purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 972.)
