# COM § 7506

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE > Chapter: 5. Warehouse Receipts and Bills of Lading: Negotiation and Transfer

**Citation:** COM § 7506

---

## Text

The transferee of a negotiable tangible document of title has a specifically enforceable right to have its transferor supply any necessary indorsement, but the transfer becomes a negotiation only as of the time the indorsement is supplied.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 254, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 2007.)
