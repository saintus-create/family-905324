# COM § 10311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PERSONAL PROPERTY LEASES > Chapter: 3. Effect of Lease Contract

**Citation:** COM § 10311

---

## Text

Nothing in this division prevents subordination by agreement by any person entitled to priority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 111, Sec. 32. Effective July 15, 1991.)
