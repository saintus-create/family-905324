# COM § 8209

**Hierarchy:** Division: 8. INVESTMENT SECURITIES > Chapter: 2. Issue and Issuer

**Citation:** COM § 8209

---

## Text

A lien in favor of an issuer upon a certificated security is valid against a purchaser only if the right of the issuer to the lien is noted conspicuously on the security certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 497, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1997.)
