# COM § 15101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TRANSITION PROVISIONS

**Citation:** COM § 15101

---

## Text

The repeal and addition of Division 8 (commencing with Section 8101) made at the 1984 portion of the 1983–84 Regular Session shall become effective on January 1, 1985.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1359, Sec. 10. Operative January 1, 1990, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 1359.)
