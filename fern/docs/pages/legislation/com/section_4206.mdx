# COM § 4206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BANK DEPOSITS AND COLLECTIONS > Chapter: 2. Collection of Items: Depositary and Collecting Banks

**Citation:** COM § 4206

---

## Text

Any agreed method that identifies the transferor bank is sufficient for the item’s further transfer to another bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 914, Sec. 24. Effective January 1, 1993.)
