# COM § 10101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. PERSONAL PROPERTY LEASES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** COM § 10101

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Uniform Commercial Code—Leases.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1359, Sec. 5. Operative January 1, 1990, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 1359.)
