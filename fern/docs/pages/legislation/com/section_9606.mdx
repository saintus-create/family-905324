# COM § 9606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS > Chapter: 6. Default

**Citation:** COM § 9606

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, a default occurs in connection with an agricultural lien at the time the secured party becomes entitled to enforce the lien in accordance with the statute under which it was created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991 and Section 9701.)
