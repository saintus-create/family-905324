# COM § 2301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. SALES > Chapter: 3. General Obligation and Construction of Contract

**Citation:** COM § 2301

---

## Text

The obligation of the seller is to transfer and deliver and that of the buyer is to accept and pay in accordance with the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1963, Ch. 819.)
