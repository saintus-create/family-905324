# COM § 9302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Perfection and Priority

**Citation:** COM § 9302

---

## Text

While farm products are located in a jurisdiction, the local law of that jurisdiction governs perfection, the effect of perfection or nonperfection, and the priority of an agricultural lien on the farm products.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991 and Section 9701.)
