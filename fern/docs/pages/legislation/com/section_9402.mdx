# COM § 9402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS > Chapter: 4. Rights of Third Parties

**Citation:** COM § 9402

---

## Text

The existence of a security interest, agricultural lien, or authority given to a debtor to dispose of or use collateral, without more, does not subject a secured party to liability in contract or tort for the debtor’s acts or omissions.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991 and Section 9701.)
