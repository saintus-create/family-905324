# COM § 1306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL PROVISIONS > Chapter: 3. Territorial Applicability and General Rules

**Citation:** COM § 1306

---

## Text

A claim or right arising out of an alleged breach may be discharged in whole or in part without consideration by agreement of the aggrieved party in a signed record.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2023, Ch. 210, Sec. 4. (SB 95) Effective January 1, 2024.)
