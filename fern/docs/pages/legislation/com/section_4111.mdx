# COM § 4111

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. BANK DEPOSITS AND COLLECTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** COM § 4111

---

## Text

An action to enforce an obligation, duty, or right arising under this division shall be commenced within three years after the cause of action accrues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 914, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1993.)
