# COM § 7306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. DOCUMENTS OF TITLE > Chapter: 3. Bills of Lading: Special Provisions

**Citation:** COM § 7306

---

## Text

An unauthorized alteration or filling in of a blank in a bill of lading leaves the bill enforceable according to its original tenor.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 254, Sec. 49. Effective January 1, 2007.)
