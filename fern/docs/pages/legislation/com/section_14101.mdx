# COM § 14101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TRANSITION PROVISIONS

**Citation:** COM § 14101

---

## Text

The amendments to this code, as adopted by the Legislature at the 1973–74 Regular Session, shall become effective at 12:01 a.m. on January 1, 1976.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1359, Sec. 9. Operative January 1, 1990, by Sec. 11 of Ch. 1359.)
