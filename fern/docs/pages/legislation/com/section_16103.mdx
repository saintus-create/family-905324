# COM § 16103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. EFFECTIVE DATE AND TRANSITION PROVISIONS

**Citation:** COM § 16103

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall have the effect of (a) reviving a cause of action barred by limitation before January 1, 1993, or (b) applying Section 3118 or 4111 on limitation of actions to an action commenced before January 1, 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 668, Sec. 15. Effective January 1, 1995.)
