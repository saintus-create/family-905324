# COM § 9339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS > Chapter: 3. Perfection and Priority

**Citation:** COM § 9339

---

## Text

This division does not preclude subordination by agreement by a person entitled to priority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991 and Section 9701.)
