# COM § 5105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. LETTERS OF CREDIT

**Citation:** COM § 5105

---

## Text

Consideration is not required to issue, amend, transfer, or cancel a letter of credit, advice, or confirmation.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 176, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1997.)
