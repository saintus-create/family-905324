# COM § 9101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 9. SECURED TRANSACTIONS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** COM § 9101

---

## Text

This division may be cited as the Uniform Commercial Code-Secured Transactions.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 991, Sec. 35. Effective January 1, 2000. Operative July 1, 2001, by Sec. 75 of Ch. 991 and Section 9701.)
