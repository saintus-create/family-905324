# INS § 11703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 1. Deposits by Workers’ Compensation Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11703

---

## Text

An insurer desiring to write workers’ compensation insurance shall maintain or provide occupational safety and health loss control consultation services pursuant to Section 6354.5 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2003, Ch. 13, Sec. 3. Effective May 28, 2003.)
