# INS § 8078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 5. Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8078

---

## Text

The president and secretary of the reinsurer may secure the services of an adjuster to represent it on any loss, subject to confirmation by its executive committee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
