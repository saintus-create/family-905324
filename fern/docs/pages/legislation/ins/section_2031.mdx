# INS § 2031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 1. Change of Risk

**Citation:** INS § 2031

---

## Text

When a contract of fire insurance does not restrict use or condition of insured subject matter, such contract is not affected by an alteration in such use or condition if the alteration does not increase the risk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
