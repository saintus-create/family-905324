# INS § 799.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 6.9. The Equal Insurance HIV Act

**Citation:** INS § 799.04

---

## Text

A life or disability income insurer shall not require an applicant to undergo an HIV test unless the cost of the test is borne by the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2020, Ch. 184, Sec. 3. (SB 1255) Effective January 1, 2021.)
