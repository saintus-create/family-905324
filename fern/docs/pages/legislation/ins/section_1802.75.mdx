# INS § 1802.75

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1802.75

---

## Text

The commissioner shall make an examination of the books and records of the applicant. The costs and expenses of the examination shall be paid by the commissioner out of funds appropriated for support of the Department of Insurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2048.)
