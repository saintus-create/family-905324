# INS § 15030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 15030

---

## Text

Each licensee shall maintain a record containing that information relative to his or her employees as may be prescribed by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
