# INS § 10192.22

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 6. Medicare Supplement Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10192.22

---

## Text

(a) On or before March 1 of each year, an issuer shall report the following information for every individual resident of this state for which the issuer has in force more than one Medicare supplement policy or certificate:

(1) Policy and certificate number.

(2) Date of issuance.

(b) The items set forth above must be grouped by individual policyholder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 706, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
