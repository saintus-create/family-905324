# INS § 12410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 6. Rebates and Commissions

**Citation:** INS § 12410

---

## Text

In enforcing any of the provisions of this article, the commissioner shall be entitled to the remedies provided for in Section 12928.6 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 891.)
