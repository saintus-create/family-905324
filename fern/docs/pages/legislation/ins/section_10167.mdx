# INS § 10167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 3a. Standard Nonforfeiture Law for Life Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10167

---

## Text

Any policy to which this article is applicable which does not contain a paid-up nonforfeiture benefit shall be construed as granting nonparticipating paid-up term insurance as a nonforfeiture benefit under subdivision (a) Section 10160.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 955.)
