# INS § 1752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 15. Travel Insurance Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1752

---

## Text

The commissioner may issue a limited lines travel insurance agent license to any organization engaged in transacting travel insurance through travel retailers not otherwise meeting the conditions set forth in Section 1754.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 257, Sec. 1. (AB 2354) Effective January 1, 2013.)
