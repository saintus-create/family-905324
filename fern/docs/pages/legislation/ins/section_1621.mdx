# INS § 1621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 1621

---

## Text

An insurance agent is a person who transacts insurance, other than life, disability, or health insurance, on behalf of an admitted insurance company. The term “insurance agent” as used in this chapter does not include a life agent as defined in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 400, Sec. 6. (AB 2782) Effective January 1, 2011.)
