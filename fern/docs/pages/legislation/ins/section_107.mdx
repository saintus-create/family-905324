# INS § 107

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 1. Classes of Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 107

---

## Text

Plate glass insurance includes insurance against breakage of glass.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
