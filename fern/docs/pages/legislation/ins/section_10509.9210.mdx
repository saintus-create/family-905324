# INS § 10509.9210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 9.5. Suitability Requirements for Annuity Transactions on or after January 1, 2025

**Citation:** INS § 10509.9210

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to sales or recommendations of annuities made on or after January 1, 2025.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 2, Sec. 6. (SB 263) Effective January 1, 2025.)
