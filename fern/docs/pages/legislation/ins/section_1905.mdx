# INS § 1905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 2. Concealment and Representations—Rules Peculiar to Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1905

---

## Text

The failure of subsequent circumstances to conform to a representation as to expectation does not, in the absence of fraud, avoid marine insurance.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
