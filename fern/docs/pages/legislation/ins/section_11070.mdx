# INS § 11070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 5. Contract

**Citation:** INS § 11070

---

## Text

After September 21, 1954, no action at law or in equity shall be had or maintained on any certificate or contract issued by a society unless commenced within two years after the cause of action shall accrue.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
