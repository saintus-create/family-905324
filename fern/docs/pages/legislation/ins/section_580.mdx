# INS § 580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 6. Loss > Article: 6. Electronic Payment of Claims

**Citation:** INS § 580

---

## Text

Every insurer required to make payment for a loss, may, with the consent of the insured, make payment by an electronic funds transfer. An insurer may not require an insured to consent to payment by an electronic funds transfer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 433, Sec. 6. (AB 328) Effective January 1, 2010.)
