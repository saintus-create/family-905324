# INS § 1350.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 4. Certificate of Authority

**Citation:** INS § 1350.5

---

## Text

In the event of substitution of attorney or a change in any of the matters specified in the certificate of authority pursuant to Section 1350, the attorney shall apply to the commissioner for an amended certificate of authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 495.)
