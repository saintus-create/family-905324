# INS § 779.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.9. Credit Life and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 779.12

---

## Text

Any order or final determination of the commissioner under the provisions of Sections 779.8 to 779.11, both inclusive, shall be subject to judicial review.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1667.)
