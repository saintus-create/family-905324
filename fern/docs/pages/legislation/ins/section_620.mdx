# INS § 620

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 8. Reinsurance

**Citation:** INS § 620

---

## Text

A contract of reinsurance is one by which an insurer procures a third person to insure him against loss or liability by reason of such original insurance.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
