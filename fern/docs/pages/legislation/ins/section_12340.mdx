# INS § 12340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12340

---

## Text

The definitions set forth in this article shall govern the construction of the terms used in this chapter, but shall not affect any other provisions of this code.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1130.)
