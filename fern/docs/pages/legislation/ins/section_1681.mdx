# INS § 1681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 7. License Qualification Examinations

**Citation:** INS § 1681

---

## Text

If an applicant fails the qualifying examination, that applicant may, subject to the provisions of Section 1682, retake a qualifying examination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 11. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
