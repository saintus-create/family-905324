# INS § 447

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 4. Warranties

**Citation:** INS § 447

---

## Text

The violation of a material warranty or other material provision of a policy, on the part of either party thereto, entitles the other to rescind.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
