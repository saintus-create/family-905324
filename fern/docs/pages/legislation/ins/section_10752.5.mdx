# INS § 10752.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 5. Stop-Loss Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10752.5

---

## Text

The commissioner may adopt regulations as may be necessary to carry out the purposes of this article. In adopting regulations, the commissioner shall comply with Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 443, Sec. 1. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2014.)
