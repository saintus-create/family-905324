# INS § 4042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 4. Loans

**Citation:** INS § 4042

---

## Text

The issuance of any document evidencing any such loan shall require the commissioner’s securities permit under Article 8 of Chapter 1, Part 2, Division 1 (commencing with Section 820).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
