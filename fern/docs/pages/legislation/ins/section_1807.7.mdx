# INS § 1807.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1807.7

---

## Text

Commencing on January 1, 2011, all licenses issued pursuant to this article shall be for a license term of two years.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2010, Ch. 400, Sec. 20. (AB 2782) Effective January 1, 2011.)
