# INS § 1982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1982

---

## Text

The acceptance of an abandonment, whether express or implied, is conclusive upon the parties and admits the loss and the sufficiency of the abandonment.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
