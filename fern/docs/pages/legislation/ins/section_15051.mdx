# INS § 15051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 6. Nonresidents

**Citation:** INS § 15051

---

## Text

The presiding officer shall keep a record of all process served upon him or her pursuant to this article, which records shall show the day and hour of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
