# INS § 10509.919

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 9. Suitability Requirements for Annuity Transactions before January 1, 2025

**Citation:** INS § 10509.919

---

## Text

This article shall apply to both of the following:

(a) A recommendation of an annuity made before January 1, 2025, that results in the purchase, exchange, or replacement that was recommended.

(b) A sale of an annuity made before January 1, 2025, that is not based on a recommendation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2024, Ch. 2, Sec. 5. (SB 263) Effective January 1, 2025.)
