# INS § 2053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 2. Measure of Indemnity

**Citation:** INS § 2053

---

## Text

A clause shall be inserted in such a valued policy, stating substantially that the value of the insured’s interest in the insured building or structure has been thus fixed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
