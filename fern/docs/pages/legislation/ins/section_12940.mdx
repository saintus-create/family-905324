# INS § 12940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 2. Review of Commissioner’s Acts

**Citation:** INS § 12940

---

## Text

The acts and orders of the commissioner are subject to such review, or other action by a court of competent jurisdiction, as is permitted or authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
