# INS § 11629.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.5. California Low-Cost Automobile Insurance Program

**Citation:** INS § 11629.8

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the coverage amounts required by Section 16056 of the Vehicle Code, a low-cost automobile policy issued under the program shall satisfy the financial responsibility requirements of Sections 4000.37, 16021, and 16431 of the Vehicle Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 435, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2006.)
