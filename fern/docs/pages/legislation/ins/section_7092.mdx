# INS § 7092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 1. Formation and Organization

**Citation:** INS § 7092

---

## Text

The directors and all of such officers shall hold office for one year from the date of their election, and until their successors are elected and qualified.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
