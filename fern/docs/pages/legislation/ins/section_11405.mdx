# INS § 11405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10A. Firemen's, Policemen's,or Peace Officers’ Benefit and Relief Associations

**Citation:** INS § 11405

---

## Text

The trustees, directors or governing body of such association, by whatever name their office is known or designated, shall be elected by the membership of the association.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 161.)
