# INS § 10233.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.6. Long-Term Care Insurance > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 10233.7

---

## Text

No policy may be advertised, marketed, or offered as long-term care or nursing home insurance unless it complies with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1273, Sec. 12.)
