# INS § 10246

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Burial Contracts

**Citation:** INS § 10246

---

## Text

All funeral insurance contracts shall be subject in all respects to the provisions of Sections 10150 to 10167 inclusive, and of Sections 10478 to 10489.6 inclusive of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 557.)
