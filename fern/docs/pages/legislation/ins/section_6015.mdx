# INS § 6015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 4. The Policy

**Citation:** INS § 6015

---

## Text

The insurer may add to the policy any matter relating to its financial condition, directors, officers, members and history, and the address of its home office and principal office in the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
