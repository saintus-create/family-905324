# INS § 10108.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Small Business Guide to Commercial Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10108.1

---

## Text

(a) As used in this chapter, “policy of commercial property insurance” has the same meaning as defined in Section 675.5.

(b) As used in this chapter, “insurer” has the same meaning as defined in Section 10091.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 316, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1995.)
