# INS § 8070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 5. Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8070

---

## Text

A member of such reinsurer, upon sustaining loss covered by reinsurance, shall immediately notify the president or secretary, stating the amount of loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
