# INS § 10139.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 2.3. Transfers of Structured Settlement Payment Rights

**Citation:** INS § 10139.1

---

## Text

Any subsequent transfer of any additional structured settlement payments between the payee and transferee may be made only after compliance with all of the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 624, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2002.)
