# INS § 12340.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12340.2

---

## Text

“Title policy” means any written instrument or contract by means of which title insurance liability is assumed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1130.)
