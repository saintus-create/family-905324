# INS § 16010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 3. Emergency Disaster Assessment > Article: 2. Emergency Disaster Assessment

**Citation:** INS § 16010

---

## Text

In order to ensure an adequate response to disasters, the Legislature finds it necessary and appropriate to establish insurance disaster assessment teams.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 510, Sec. 1. Effective September 15, 1998.)
