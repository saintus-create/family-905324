# INS § 7041

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 9.5. Merger

**Citation:** INS § 7041

---

## Text

When any such plan and agreement shall have been approved by the commissioner, with changes, if any required by him, the same shall be approved by a vote of not less than two-thirds of the members voting at a meeting of the members of each such insurer involved at which there shall be present in person or by proxy at least 10 percent of the members entitled to vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1963.)
