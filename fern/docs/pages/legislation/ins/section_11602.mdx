# INS § 11602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Capital Requirements of Reserve Basis Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11602

---

## Text

Any such insurer which on July 26, 1919, was authorized by its charter to transact liability insurance may transact workers’ compensation and common carrier liability insurance as though expressly permitted by its charter to do so.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 276.)
