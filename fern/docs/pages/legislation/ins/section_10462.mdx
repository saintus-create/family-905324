# INS § 10462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10462

---

## Text

Should any insurer thus registering policies become insolvent, the commissioner may reinsure all or any part of such registered policies, using the securities thus deposited for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
