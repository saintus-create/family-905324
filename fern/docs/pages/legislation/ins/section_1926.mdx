# INS § 1926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 3. Implied Warranties Peculiar to Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1926

---

## Text

A ship may be seaworthy for the purpose of insurance upon itself and, at the same time, unseaworthy for the purpose of insurance upon the cargo because of unfitness to receive the cargo.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
