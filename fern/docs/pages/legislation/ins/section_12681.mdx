# INS § 12681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.1. HEALTH DISABILITY INSURANCE CONVERSION

**Citation:** INS § 12681

---

## Text

An insurer shall not be required to issue a converted policy providing benefits in excess of those provided under the group policy from which conversion is made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1096.)
