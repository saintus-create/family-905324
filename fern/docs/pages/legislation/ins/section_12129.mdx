# INS § 12129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 4. MISCELLANEOUS CASUALTY INSURANCES > Chapter: 3. Group and Individual Plans for Legal Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 12129

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall be enforced by the commissioner, and he may, after notice and public hearing, promulgate such reasonable rules and regulations as are necessary to administer this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12124 by Stats. 1995, Ch. 91, Sec. 104. Effective January 1, 1996.)
