# INS § 1215.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 4.7. Insurance Holding Company System Regulatory Act

**Citation:** INS § 1215.3

---

## Text

The following shall be violations of this article:

(a) The failure to file the statement required under subdivision (a) of Section 1215.2.

(b) Effectuation or any attempt to effectuate an acquisition or control of, or merger with, a domestic insurer either within the 60-day period referred to in subdivision (d) of Section 1215.2, unless the commissioner has given his approval thereto, or after disapproval of such acquisition of control or merger.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1275.)
