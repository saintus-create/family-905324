# INS § 12157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 12157

---

## Text

Any act by a motor club for the purpose of rendering a service defined in this chapter constitutes such service, whether or not the service as defined is completed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
