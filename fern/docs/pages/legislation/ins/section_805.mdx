# INS § 805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 7. Restrictions on Underwriting

**Citation:** INS § 805

---

## Text

The license of any licensee that is suspended by the Secretary of State shall become inactive. The inactive licensee shall not conduct any activity for which a license, issued by the commissioner, is required until the licensee is no longer suspended by the Secretary of State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 204, Sec. 1. (AB 1140) Effective January 1, 2024.)
