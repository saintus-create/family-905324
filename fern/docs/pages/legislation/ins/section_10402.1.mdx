# INS § 10402.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 8. Penalties

**Citation:** INS § 10402.1

---

## Text

An insurer or plan which accepts or ratifies a contract for alternative rates known by it to have been executed by a provider prior to the insurer’s or plan’s authorization, in reliance on the representation that it was authorized by such insurer or plan, shall be guilty of a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1594, Sec. 13. Effective September 30, 1982. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 82 of Ch. 1594.)
