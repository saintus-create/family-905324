# INS § 14077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 6. Nonresidents

**Citation:** INS § 14077

---

## Text

The presiding officer shall keep a record of all process served upon him or her pursuant to this article, which record shall show the day and hour of service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1190, Sec. 11. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 13 of Ch. 1190.)
