# INS § 449

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 4. Warranties

**Citation:** INS § 449

---

## Text

A breach of warranty without fraud merely exonerates an insurer from the time that it occurs, or where the warranty is broken in its inception, prevents the policy from attaching to the risk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
