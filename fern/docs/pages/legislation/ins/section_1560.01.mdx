# INS § 1560.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 17. Conversion of Certain Reciprocal Insurers to Incorporated Stock Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1560.01

---

## Text

By following the procedure specified in this article, any domestic reciprocal insurer described in Section 1560 may be converted into an incorporated stock insurer. To that end, it may provide and carry out a plan for the conversion by complying with the requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 421, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
