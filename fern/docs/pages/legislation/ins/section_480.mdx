# INS § 480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 5. The Premium

**Citation:** INS § 480

---

## Text

An insurer is entitled to payment of the premium as soon as the subject matter insured is exposed to the peril insured against.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
