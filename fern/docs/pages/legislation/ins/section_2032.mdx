# INS § 2032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 1. Change of Risk

**Citation:** INS § 2032

---

## Text

After the execution of a contract of fire insurance, an act of the insured does not affect the contract unless the act violates policy provisions, even though such act increases the risk and causes a loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
