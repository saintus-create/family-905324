# INS § 15018.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 15018.5

---

## Text

The commissioner may, without hearing, deny an application if the applicant has committed any act or been convicted of a crime constituting grounds for denial of license under Section 1669.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 448, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2006.)
