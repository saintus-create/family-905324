# INS § 1140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** INS § 1140

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this code, incorporated insurers are subject to the provisions of the general corporation law in like manner with other corporations.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
