# INS § 779.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.9. Credit Life and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 779.17

---

## Text

Nothing in this act shall be construed to authorize any charge now prohibited under any statute or rule governing credit transactions, irrespective of whether the same is contained in this code or made pursuant thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1667.)
