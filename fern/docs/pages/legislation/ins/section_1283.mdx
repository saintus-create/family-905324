# INS § 1283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 1283

---

## Text

The provisions of Part 7, Division 2 of the Revenue and Taxation Code shall be applicable to reciprocal or interinsurance exchanges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 112.)
