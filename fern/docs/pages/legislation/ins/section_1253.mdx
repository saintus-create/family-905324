# INS § 1253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 7. Valuation of Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1253

---

## Text

Any such insurer may report such bonds or other evidence of debt at market value or book value, but in no event at an aggregate value exceeding the aggregate of the values calculated according to the rule prescribed by section 1252.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
