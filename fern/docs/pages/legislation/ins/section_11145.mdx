# INS § 11145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 7. Financial Matters

**Citation:** INS § 11145

---

## Text

In any determination of the incompleteness or misleading character of any comparison or statement, it shall be presumed that the insured had no knowledge of any of the contents of the contract involved.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
