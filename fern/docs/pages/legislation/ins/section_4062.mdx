# INS § 4062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 6. Nonassessable Policies

**Citation:** INS § 4062

---

## Text

A foreign or alien mutual insurer may issue nonassessable policies to its members in this State pursuant to its charter and the laws of its domicile.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
