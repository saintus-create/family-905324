# INS § 10455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10455

---

## Text

The commissioner shall give his receipt for the securities and the State shall be responsible for the custody and safe return of any securities so deposited.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
