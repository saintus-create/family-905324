# INS § 1850.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 1. Purpose and Scope of Chapter

**Citation:** INS § 1850.5

---

## Text

In this chapter “wilful” or “wilfully” in relation to an act or omission which constitutes a violation of this chapter means with actual knowledge or belief that such act or omission constitutes such violation and with specific intent to commit such violation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 805.)
