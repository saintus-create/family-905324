# INS § 6010.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 4. The Policy

**Citation:** INS § 6010.5

---

## Text

The policy is not required to be used for reinsurance between insurers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 697.)
