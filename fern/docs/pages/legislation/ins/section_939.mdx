# INS § 939

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 11. Deposit of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 939

---

## Text

Except as otherwise expressly provided, all deposits of securities with the commissioner shall be subject to the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1937, Ch. 732.)
