# INS § 10089.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.6. California Earthquake Authority

**Citation:** INS § 10089.25

---

## Text

Beginning December 31, 1997, and annually thereafter on the 30th of April, the board shall notify each participating insurer of the maximum earthquake loss funding assessment level that it may be required to meet.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 264, Sec. 5. Effective August 6, 1998.)
