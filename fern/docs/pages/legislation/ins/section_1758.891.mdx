# INS § 1758.891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 16.6. Car Rental Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1758.891

---

## Text

Until January 1, 2001, a rental car company or a franchisee of a rental car company shall not be required to obtain a license to offer the insurance products described in Section 1758.85.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 618, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
