# INS § 11751.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 3. Rating and Other Organizations

**Citation:** INS § 11751.4

---

## Text

From and after the taking effect of this act, it shall be the duty of every insurer to be a member of a rating organization. No insurer may at the same time belong to more than one rating organization licensed under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1123.)
