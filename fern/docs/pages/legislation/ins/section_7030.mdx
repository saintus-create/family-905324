# INS § 7030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 9. Reports and Statements

**Citation:** INS § 7030

---

## Text

The secretary shall prepare an annual statement, showing the condition of such insurer on December thirty-first preceding the annual meeting. He shall present the statement at the annual meeting.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
