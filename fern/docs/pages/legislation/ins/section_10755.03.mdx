# INS § 10755.03

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.02. Grandfathered Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 2. Small Employer Carrier Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 10755.03

---

## Text

The commissioner shall have the authority to determine whether a health benefit plan is covered by this chapter, and to determine whether an employer is a small employer within the meaning of Section 10755.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 15. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
