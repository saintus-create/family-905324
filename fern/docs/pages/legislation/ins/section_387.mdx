# INS § 387

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 1. Definition and Scope

**Citation:** INS § 387

---

## Text

When the name of the person intended to be insured is specified in a policy, it can be applied only to his own interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
