# INS § 1716

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 11. License Terms

**Citation:** INS § 1716

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to any certificate of convenience other than a military service certificate of convenience issued to a natural person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
