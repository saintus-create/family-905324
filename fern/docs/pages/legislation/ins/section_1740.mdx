# INS § 1740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 13. Disciplinary Actions

**Citation:** INS § 1740

---

## Text

The certificate of the commissioner certifying any facts found after a hearing held under this chapter shall be prima facie evidence of the facts set forth therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
