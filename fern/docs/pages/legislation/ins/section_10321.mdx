# INS § 10321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 3. Policy Form Generally—Uniform Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 10321

---

## Text

After issuance a disability policy shall not be amended, changed, limited, altered, or restricted by any means other than rider upon a separate piece of paper.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 570.)
