# INS § 8030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 3. Additional Memberships

**Citation:** INS § 8030

---

## Text

Any county mutual fire insurer in this State may become a member of such a reinsurer and entitled to all the rights and privileges appertaining thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
