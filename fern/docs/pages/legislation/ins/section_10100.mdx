# INS § 10100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Basic Property Insurance Inspection and Placement Plan

**Citation:** INS § 10100

---

## Text

The commissioner may require such reports from insurers concerning risks insured under any program approved pursuant to this chapter as he shall deem necessary to effect the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 574.)
