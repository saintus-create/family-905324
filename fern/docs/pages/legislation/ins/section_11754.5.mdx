# INS § 11754.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 3. Rating and Other Organizations

**Citation:** INS § 11754.5

---

## Text

Any finding, determination, rule, ruling, or order made by the commissioner under this article or Article 2 (commencing with Section 11730) shall be subject to review by the courts of the state pursuant to Section 1094.5 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 582, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1996.)
