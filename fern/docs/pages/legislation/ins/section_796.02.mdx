# INS § 796.02

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 6.8. Claims Reviewers

**Citation:** INS § 796.02

---

## Text

Compensation of a person retained by a disability insurer to review claims for health care services shall not be based on either of the following:

(a) A percentage of the amount by which a claim is reduced for payment.

(b) The number of claims or the cost of services for which the person has denied authorization or payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 787, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
