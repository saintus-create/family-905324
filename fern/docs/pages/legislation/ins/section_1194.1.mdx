# INS § 1194.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 4. Property Authorized for Excess Funds Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1194.1

---

## Text

Excess funds investments may be made in bonds issued pursuant to the Improvement Bond Act of 1915.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 659.)
