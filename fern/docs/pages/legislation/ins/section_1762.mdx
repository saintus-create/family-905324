# INS § 1762

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. Surplus Line Brokers

**Citation:** INS § 1762

---

## Text

For purposes of Sections 1764, 1764.1, and 1764.3, the term “certificate” means a surplus line broker certificate as defined in Section 48.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 255, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2000.)
