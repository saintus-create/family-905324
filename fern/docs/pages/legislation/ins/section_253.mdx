# INS § 253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 3. Events Subject to Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 253

---

## Text

On and after January 1, 1986, no insurer shall issue or amend contracts of insurance in this state to provide coverage for the payment of any damages awarded to a person because of Section 1029.8 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 895, Sec. 2.)
