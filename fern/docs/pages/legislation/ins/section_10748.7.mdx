# INS § 10748.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 4. Purchasing Pool for Small Employers

**Citation:** INS § 10748.7

---

## Text

There is in the program a five-member small employer advisory panel to be appointed by the board to provide consultation to the board on program design and implementation. The composition of the panel shall reflect varying sizes of small employers and a variety of occupational categories.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1128, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1128.)
