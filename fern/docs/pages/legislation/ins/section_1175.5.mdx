# INS § 1175.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 3. General Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1175.5

---

## Text

Such insurers may invest in bonds of any county water district operating under Division 12 of the Water Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1653.)
