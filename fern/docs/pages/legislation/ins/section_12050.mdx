# INS § 12050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 4. MISCELLANEOUS CASUALTY INSURANCES > Chapter: 1. Surety Insurers on Reserve Basis; Capital Requirements and Permitted Insurances > Article: 1. Capital and Scope of Business

**Citation:** INS § 12050

---

## Text

An incorporated insurer issuing surety policies on the reserve basis shall be governed by the paid-in capital and surplus requirements of Sections 700. 01 to 700.05, inclusive.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 958.)
