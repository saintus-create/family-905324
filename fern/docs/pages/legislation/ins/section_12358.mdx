# INS § 12358

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 2. Title Insurers; Capital and Guarantee Fund Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12358

---

## Text

The reasonable cost of examining such evidence of title and of making such appraisement, shall be paid by the title insurer making the deposit.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1937, Ch. 732.)
