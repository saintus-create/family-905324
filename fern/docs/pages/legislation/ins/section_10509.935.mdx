# INS § 10509.935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 11. Life Insurance Proceeds Disclosure Act of 2011

**Citation:** INS § 10509.935

---

## Text

If the insurer settles life insurance benefits through a retained asset account, the insurer shall provide the beneficiary with a supplemental contract that clearly discloses the rights of the beneficiary and the obligations of the insurer under the supplemental contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 130, Sec. 1. (SB 713) Effective January 1, 2012.)
