# INS § 12693.982

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 16.1. Healthy Families Bridge Benefits Program

**Citation:** INS § 12693.982

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “Medi-Cal” means the state health care program established pursuant to Chapter 14 (commencing with Section 14000) of Part 3 of Division 9 of the Welfare and Institutions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 171, Sec. 17. Effective August 10, 2001.)
