# INS § 1759.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5A. Administrators

**Citation:** INS § 1759.7

---

## Text

All claims paid by the administrator from funds collected on behalf of the insurer shall be paid only on checks or drafts of, or, with the consent of the insured, by an electronic funds transfer from, and as authorized by the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2009, Ch. 433, Sec. 12. (AB 328) Effective January 1, 2010.)
