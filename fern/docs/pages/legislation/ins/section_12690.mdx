# INS § 12690

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.1. HEALTH DISABILITY INSURANCE CONVERSION

**Citation:** INS § 12690

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall prohibit insurers from establishing one or more pools from which the converted policies provided for on this part may be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1096.)
