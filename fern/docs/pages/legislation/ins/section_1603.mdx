# INS § 1603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Foreign Insurers > Article: 3. Agent for Service of Process

**Citation:** INS § 1603

---

## Text

The person appointed and designated pursuant to Section 1600 shall be deemed in law a general agent, and shall for service of process be the principal agent of the insurer in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1999, Ch. 808, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
