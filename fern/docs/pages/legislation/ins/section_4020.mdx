# INS § 4020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 2. Directors

**Citation:** INS § 4020

---

## Text

The affairs of every domestic mutual insurer shall be managed by a board of directors consisting of not less than 5 directors nor more than 15 directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
