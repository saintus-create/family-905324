# INS § 10202.81

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Group Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10202.81

---

## Text

The State and any political subdivisions thereof and any municipality, may provide for the type of insurance set forth in Section 10202.8 of this code the same as any private employer and may contribute to a fund established under such section the same as any private employer.
