# INS § 1775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. Surplus Line Brokers

**Citation:** INS § 1775

---

## Text

All such reports and statements shall be made on blanks furnished to the surplus line broker by the commissioner on application therefor.
