# INS § 774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.5. Insurance in Connection With Sales and Loans

**Citation:** INS § 774

---

## Text

The commissioner, after hearing upon notice, may issue a cease and desist order to any person if he finds that such person has, in more than one transaction, violated Section 770. The violation of such a cease and desist order is a misdemeanor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 224.)
