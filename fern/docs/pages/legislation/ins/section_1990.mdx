# INS § 1990

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1990

---

## Text

In case of a valued policy of marine insurance on freightage or cargo, if only a part of the subject matter is exposed to risk, the valuation applies only in proportion to such part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
