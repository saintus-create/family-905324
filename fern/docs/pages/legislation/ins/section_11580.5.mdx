# INS § 11580.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 2. Actions on Policies Containing Liability Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 11580.5

---

## Text

No award made in an arbitration proceeding instituted pursuant to Section 11580.2 shall be deemed to be res judicata or collateral estoppel in any court action which may be pending or brought by the insured against the owner or operator of an uninsured motor vehicle.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1654.)
