# INS § 12693.80

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 11. Protection Against Substitution of Benefits

**Citation:** INS § 12693.80

---

## Text

The board shall use due diligence in the creation of participation standards for the program that minimize the incentive for employers or applicants to drop or reduce dependent health coverage.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
