# INS § 1758.99

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 16.7. Credit Insurance Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1758.99

---

## Text

An organization licensed as a credit insurance agent shall prominently display its license number and the department’s toll free consumer hot line telephone number on brochures and information sheets required by this article and on any evidence of insurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 321, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 321.)
