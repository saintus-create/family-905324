# INS § 11470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Change by Assessment Plan Insurer to Reserve Plan > Article: 4. Powers of Insurer After Transformation

**Citation:** INS § 11470

---

## Text

The insurer, after such transformation, shall exercise all the rights and powers and perform all the duties conferred or imposed by law upon insurers writing the classes of insurance written by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
