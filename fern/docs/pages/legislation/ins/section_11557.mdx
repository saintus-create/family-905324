# INS § 11557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 1. Reserves of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11557

---

## Text

Whenever the reserves for outstanding liability or compensation losses and loss expenses of any insurer, computed pursuant to this article, seem inadequate to the commissioner, he shall require such insurer to maintain additional reserves.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 705.)
