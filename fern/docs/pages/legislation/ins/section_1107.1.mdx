# INS § 1107.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 17. Loans and Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1107.1

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require the payment of three hundred seventy-four dollars ($374), as fee for the determination referred to in Section 1107.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 23. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
