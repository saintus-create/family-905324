# INS § 1694

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 8. Certificates of Convenience

**Citation:** INS § 1694

---

## Text

A military service certificate of convenience shall terminate upon the relicensing of the nominating licensee, but in no event shall it remain in force beyond the period set forth in Section 1722.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
