# INS § 13854

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 6. Affordable Housing Insurance Coverage Study

**Citation:** INS § 13854

---

## Text

Implementation of this chapter is contingent upon an appropriation by the Legislature for purposes of this chapter in the annual Budget Act or another statute.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 728, Sec. 1. (AB 1339) Effective January 1, 2026. Repealed as of January 1, 2031, pursuant to Section 13855.)
