# INS § 13554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 8. Insurance Payment Intercept Program

**Citation:** INS § 13554

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “central reporting organization” means a third-party service that automates the claims identifying process or provides interactive lookups.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 439, Sec. 1. (AB 2802) Effective January 1, 2019. Operative January 1, 2020, pursuant to Section 13555.)
