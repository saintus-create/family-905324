# INS § 11556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 1. Reserves of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11556

---

## Text

The reserve for outstanding losses and loss expenses under such liability or compensation insurance shall be determined in accordance with the regulations of the commissioner promulgated pursuant to Section 923.5.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1982, Ch. 454, Sec. 121.)
