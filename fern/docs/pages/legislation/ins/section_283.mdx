# INS § 283

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 4. Insurable Interest: Generally

**Citation:** INS § 283

---

## Text

A mere contingent or expectant interest in anything, not founded on an actual right to the thing, nor upon any valid contract for it, is not insurable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
