# INS § 2055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 2. Measure of Indemnity

**Citation:** INS § 2055

---

## Text

Except as provided by section 2056, the insurer shall not be required to pay more than the amount stated in such a valued policy.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
