# INS § 10233.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.6. Long-Term Care Insurance > Article: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 10233.4

---

## Text

No long-term care insurance benefits may be reduced because of out-of-pocket expenditures by the insured or on behalf of the insured by a family member of the insured or by any other individual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1273, Sec. 9.)
