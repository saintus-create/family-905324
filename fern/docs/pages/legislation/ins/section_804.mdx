# INS § 804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 7. Restrictions on Underwriting

**Citation:** INS § 804

---

## Text

Any insurer willfully violating any provision of this article is guilty of a misdemeanor and is punishable by a fine not exceeding one thousand dollars ($1,000) for each violation thereof, or the commissioner may suspend the certificate of authority of such insurer for the remainder of the term thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1092, Sec. 180. Effective September 27, 1983. Operative January 1, 1984, by Sec. 427 of Ch. 1092.)
