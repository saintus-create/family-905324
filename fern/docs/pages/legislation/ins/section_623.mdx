# INS § 623

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 8. Reinsurance

**Citation:** INS § 623

---

## Text

The original insured has no interest in a contract of reinsurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
