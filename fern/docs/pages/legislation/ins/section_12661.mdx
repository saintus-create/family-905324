# INS § 12661

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 3. Land Value Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 12661

---

## Text

The commissioner may suspend or revoke or may deny any license or certificate issued under any provision of this code in any case where the applicant or licensee or certificate holder has committed an act denounced by section 12660.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 936.)
