# INS § 11532.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 13. Voluntary Mutualization of Incorporated Life and Life and Disability Insurers Having a Capital Stock and Issuing Nonassessable Policies on a Reserve Basis > Article: 2. Execution of Plan

**Citation:** INS § 11532.8

---

## Text

All insurers mutualized under the provisions of this chapter shall be subject to all other applicable provisions of this code and to the provisions of the general corporation law as provided in Section 1140 of this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 379.)
