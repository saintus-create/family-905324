# INS § 6011.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 4. The Policy

**Citation:** INS § 6011.5

---

## Text

In lieu of showing the term of coverage in the form set forth in Section 6010, the standard form policy may show the term in any form which clearly states the period during which the insurance is to continue. The period shall begin and end on specified dates at 12:01 a.m., standard time, at the location of the property involved. An example of permissible method of showing the term is: “ for the term offromAt 12:01 a.m.(Standard Time) toAt 12:01 a.m.(Standard Time)at location of property involved, ”
