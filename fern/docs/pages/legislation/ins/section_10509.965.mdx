# INS § 10509.965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5.5. Life Insurance Policy Illustrations

**Citation:** INS § 10509.965

---

## Text

This chapter shall become effective on and after July 1, 1997, and shall apply to policies sold on or after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 1106, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997. Note: This section prescribes a delayed effective (operative) date of July 1, 1997, for Chapter 5.5, commencing with Section 10509.950.)
