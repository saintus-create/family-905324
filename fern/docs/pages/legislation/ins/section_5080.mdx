# INS § 5080

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** INS § 5080

---

## Text

Such insurer and its directors possess the usual powers, and are subject to the usual duties of corporations and directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
