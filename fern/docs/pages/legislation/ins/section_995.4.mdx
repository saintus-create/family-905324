# INS § 995.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 13.3. Obligations of Automobile Insurers and Their Agents Under Contingent and Retrospective Compensation Arrangements

**Citation:** INS § 995.4

---

## Text

An insurer, notwithstanding the provisions of any contingent or retrospective compensation arrangement with any person, shall maintain as a liability, as part of its unearned premium reserve the unearned portion of all premium charges made to insureds without deduction for uncollected charges.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1818.)
