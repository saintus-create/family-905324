# INS § 1310

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 2. Organization of Exchange

**Citation:** INS § 1310

---

## Text

Such body shall be composed of subscribers or agents of subscribers. Not more than one-third of the members serving on such body shall be agents, employees or shareholders of the attorney.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
