# INS § 12342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12342

---

## Text

For purposes of this chapter, “holders of liens or encumbrances” includes, but is not limited to, the institutional third parties described in Sections 1452 and 1717 of Title 12 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 433, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1996.)
