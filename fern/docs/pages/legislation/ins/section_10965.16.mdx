# INS § 10965.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9.9. Individual Access to Health Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10965.16

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this chapter, this chapter shall be implemented to the extent that it meets or exceeds the requirements set forth in PPACA.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 19. (AB 2 1x) Effective September 30, 2013.)
