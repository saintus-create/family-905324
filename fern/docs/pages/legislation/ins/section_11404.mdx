# INS § 11404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10A. Firemen's, Policemen's,or Peace Officers’ Benefit and Relief Associations

**Citation:** INS § 11404

---

## Text

Such association shall not pay, promise or agree to pay, either directly or indirectly, any consideration of any nature for the solicitation or procuring of members or applications for membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 161.)
