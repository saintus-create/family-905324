# INS § 14011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 2. Administration

**Citation:** INS § 14011

---

## Text

The commissioner may, in accordance with the State Civil Service Act, appoint such inspectors, investigators, and other personnel as may be necessary for the administration and enforcement of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1190, Sec. 11. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 13 of Ch. 1190.)
