# INS § 12353

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 2. Title Insurers; Capital and Guarantee Fund Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12353

---

## Text

Except as provided in section 12355, assets in such deposits in this State may, with the approval of the commissioner, be withdrawn or exchanged from time to time for other assets of like character and value.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1935, Ch. 292.)
