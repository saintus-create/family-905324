# INS § 11629.82

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.5. California Low-Cost Automobile Insurance Program

**Citation:** INS § 11629.82

---

## Text

Nothing in this article is intended to amend or otherwise affect or interpret any provision of Proposition 103, approved by the electors on November 8, 1988, and no provision of that initiative measure applies to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 794, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2000.)
