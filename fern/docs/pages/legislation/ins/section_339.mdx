# INS § 339

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Negotiations Before Execution > Article: 1. Concealment

**Citation:** INS § 339

---

## Text

Neither party to a contract of insurance is bound to communicate, even upon inquiry, information of his own judgment upon the matters in question.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
