# INS § 11025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 3. Organization

**Citation:** INS § 11025

---

## Text

A society may accept general or social members who shall have no voice or vote in the management of its insurance affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
