# INS § 738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 4. Examination by Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 738

---

## Text

The commissioner shall have the same powers and authority to examine the State Compensation Insurance Fund as are conferred upon him by law relative to the examination of other insurers except where the fund is specifically exempted by reference.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 740, Sec. 3.3. Effective January 1, 2007.)
