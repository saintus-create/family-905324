# INS § 1710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 10. Termination of Licenses

**Citation:** INS § 1710

---

## Text

All licenses issued to natural persons terminate upon the death of such person.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
