# INS § 10753.18.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.01. Nongrandfathered Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 2. Small Employer Carrier Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 10753.18.7

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, no provision of this chapter shall be construed to limit the applicability of any other provision of the Insurance Code unless such provision is in conflict with the requirements of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 14. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
