# INS § 1076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 15. Withdrawal of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1076

---

## Text

The withdrawing insurer shall pay to the commissioner a fee of one thousand four hundred ten dollars ($1,410) for all services and expenses in connection with the withdrawal.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 19. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
