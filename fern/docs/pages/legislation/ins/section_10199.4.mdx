# INS § 10199.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1.5. Health Coverage Contract Notification > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 10199.4

---

## Text

Proof of mailing a notice and the reason therefor to the appropriate entity or individual at the last known mailing address shall be sufficient proof of the notice required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 949, Sec. 2.)
