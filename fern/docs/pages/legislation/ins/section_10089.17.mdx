# INS § 10089.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.6. California Earthquake Authority

**Citation:** INS § 10089.17

---

## Text

Notwithstanding subdivision (h) of Section 10089.7, the authority shall be subject to the provisions of the Political Reform Act of 1974 (Title 9 (commencing with Section 81000) of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 967, Sec. 11. Effective September 27, 1996.)
