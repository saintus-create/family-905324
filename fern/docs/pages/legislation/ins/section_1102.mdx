# INS § 1102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 17. Loans and Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1102

---

## Text

The financial obligation of any officer, director, trustee, or other person having authority in the management of an insurer’s funds shall not be guaranteed by such insurer in any capacity, and any such guarantee shall be void.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1937, Ch. 736.)
