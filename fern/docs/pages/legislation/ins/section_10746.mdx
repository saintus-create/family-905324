# INS § 10746

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 4. Purchasing Pool for Small Employers

**Citation:** INS § 10746

---

## Text

Participating carriers shall offer rates to small employers or enrollees in the program that, at a minimum, are consistent with the program regulations and existing statutes and regulations regulating health insurance offerings to small employers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1128, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1128.)
