# INS § 11112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 6. Powers of Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 11112

---

## Text

All decisions and findings of the commissioner made under the provisions of this chapter shall be subject to review by proper proceedings in any court of competent jurisdiction in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
