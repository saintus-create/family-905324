# INS § 1560.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 17. Conversion of Certain Reciprocal Insurers to Incorporated Stock Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1560.17

---

## Text

Upon completion of the act of conversion and issuance of the certificate of authority under Section 1560.10, the Secretary of State shall accept for filing a verified copy of the articles of incorporation of the converted insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 421, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
