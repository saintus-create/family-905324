# INS § 1875.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 12. The Insurance Frauds Prevention Act > Article: 5. Arson Investigations

**Citation:** INS § 1875.5

---

## Text

In any case in which an insurer willfully fails to comply with this article, the authorized agency may petition the superior court in an appropriate county for an order requiring compliance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1119, Sec. 3.)
