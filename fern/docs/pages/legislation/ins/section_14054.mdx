# INS § 14054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 4. Bonds

**Citation:** INS § 14054

---

## Text

Bonds executed and filed with the commissioner pursuant to this article shall remain in force and effect until the surety has terminated future liability by 30-day notice to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1190, Sec. 11. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 13 of Ch. 1190.)
