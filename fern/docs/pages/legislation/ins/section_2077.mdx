# INS § 2077

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 3. California Standard Form Fire Insurance Policy

**Citation:** INS § 2077

---

## Text

The insurer may add to the standard form, in red ink, any provisions required or permitted in its policies by the State or country of its organization, respecting limitation of liability of the insurer, its shareholders or members.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
