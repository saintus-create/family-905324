# INS § 448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 4. Warranties

**Citation:** INS § 448

---

## Text

Unless the policy declares that a violation of specified provisions thereof shall avoid it, the breach of an immaterial provision does not avoid the policy.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
