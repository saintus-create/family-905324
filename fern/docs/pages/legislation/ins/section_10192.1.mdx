# INS § 10192.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 6. Medicare Supplement Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10192.1

---

## Text

All Medicare supplement policies and certificates shall comply with the provisions of subdivision (b) of Section 10291.5 and Chapter 7 (commencing with Section 10600) of Part 2 of Division 2, regardless of the situs of the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 706, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
