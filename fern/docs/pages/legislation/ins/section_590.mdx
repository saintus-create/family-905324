# INS § 590

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 7. Double Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 590

---

## Text

A double insurance exists where the same person is insured by several insurers separately in respect to the same subject and interest.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
