# INS § 11441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Change by Assessment Plan Insurer to Reserve Plan > Article: 2. Capital Adjustments

**Citation:** INS § 11441

---

## Text

All assets belonging before transformation to any such insurer or arising or accruing from policies issued upon the assessment plan, shall be used only for the benefit of the holders of such policies. Such assets shall not be used or considered as any part of the paid in capital provided for by section 11440.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
