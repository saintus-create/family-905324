# INS § 6042

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 5. Risks

**Citation:** INS § 6042

---

## Text

Policies may be executed for any time not exceeding five years and not extending beyond the time limited for the existence of the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
