# INS § 678.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 11. Cancellation and Failure to Renew Certain Property Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 678.2

---

## Text

The provisions of subdivisions (c) and (e) of Section 678.1 which prohibit notice of nonrenewal earlier than 120 days in advance of the end of the policy period shall not apply to professional liability policies issued to health care providers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 103, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
