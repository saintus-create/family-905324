# INS § 10450.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10450.5

---

## Text

An insurer which is not registering its policies pursuant to the provisions of this article on September 30, 1939, shall not thereafter be permitted to register its policies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 933.)
