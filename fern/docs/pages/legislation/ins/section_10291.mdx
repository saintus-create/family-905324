# INS § 10291

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 2. Approval of Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 10291

---

## Text

If the commissioner notifies the insurer, in writing, that the filed form does not comply with the requirements of law, specifying the reasons for his opinion, it is unlawful thereafter for any such insurer to issue any policy in such form.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
