# INS § 10853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. The Private Health Care Voluntary Purchasing Alliance Act > Article: 6. Participating Carriers

**Citation:** INS § 10853

---

## Text

In contracts with participating carriers, the purchasing alliance may establish performance standards for specific contractual elements and penalties for failure to fulfill specific contractual obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 916, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
