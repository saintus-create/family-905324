# INS § 11731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 2. State Rate Supervision

**Citation:** INS § 11731

---

## Text

This article applies to workers’ compensation insurance and employers’ liability insurance written in connection therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 228, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994. Operative January 1, 1995, by Sec. 7 of Ch. 228, as amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1242.)
