# INS § 10295.19

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 2.1. Accelerated Death Benefits

**Citation:** INS § 10295.19

---

## Text

A policy, certificate, rider, or endorsement shall include a provision giving the policyholder or certificate holder the right to appeal to the insurer a decision regarding benefit eligibility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 345, Sec. 7. (SB 281) Effective January 1, 2014.)
