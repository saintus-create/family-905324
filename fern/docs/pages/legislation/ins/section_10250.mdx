# INS § 10250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Burial Contracts

**Citation:** INS § 10250

---

## Text

No funeral insurance contract shall be issued or delivered in this State except by an admitted insurer issuing policies on a legal reserve basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 557.)
