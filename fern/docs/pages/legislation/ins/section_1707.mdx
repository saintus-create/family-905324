# INS § 1707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 9. Issuance of Licenses

**Citation:** INS § 1707

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in Section 1704.5, each notice of appointment or notice of termination of appointment filed pursuant to this article shall be filed on forms prescribed by the commissioner within 15 days of appointment or termination.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2006, Ch. 740, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2007.)
