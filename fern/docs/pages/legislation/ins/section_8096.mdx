# INS § 8096

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 6. Assessments and Coverage of Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8096

---

## Text

The directors of any such reinsurer who wilfully refuse or neglect to perform the duties imposed upon them by law or by the by-laws of the reinsurer are liable in their individual capacity to the reinsurer sustaining loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
