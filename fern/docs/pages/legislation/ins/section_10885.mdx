# INS § 10885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. The Private Health Care Voluntary Purchasing Alliance Act > Article: 10. Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 10885

---

## Text

Purchasing alliances shall be exempt from requirements of licensure as a health care service plan or solicitor under Chapter 2.2 (commencing with Section 1340) of Division 2 of the Health and Safety Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 916, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
