# INS § 10752.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 5. Stop-Loss Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10752.6

---

## Text

A stop-loss insurer that violates the provisions of this article is subject to the remedies and administrative penalties applicable to insurers in Sections 10718 and 10718.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 443, Sec. 1. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2014.)
