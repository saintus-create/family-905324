# INS § 10198.10

**Citation:** INS § 10198.10

## Text

This article shall become operative on January 1, 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 12) and added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 11. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013. Note: This section prescribes a delayed operative date (Jan. 1, 2014) for Article 7, commencing with Section 10198.6.)
