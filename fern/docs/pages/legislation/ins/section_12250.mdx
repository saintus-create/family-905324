# INS § 12250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 3. The Service Contract

**Citation:** INS § 12250

---

## Text

A service contract shall not be executed, issued or delivered in this State until the form thereof is approved in writing by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
