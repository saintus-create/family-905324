# INS § 1587

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Foreign Insurers > Article: 2. Alien Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1587

---

## Text

Securities deposited with the commissioner pursuant to this article shall be specially deposited by him in the State Treasury, in packages marked with the name of the insurer from whom the securities were received.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 51.)
