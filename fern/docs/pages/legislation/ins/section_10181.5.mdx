# INS § 10181.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 4.5. Review of Rate Increases

**Citation:** INS § 10181.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any provision in a contract between a health insurer and a provider, the department may request from a health insurer any information required under this article or PPACA.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2010, Ch. 661, Sec. 7. (SB 1163) Effective January 1, 2011.)
