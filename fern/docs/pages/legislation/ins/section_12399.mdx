# INS § 12399

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 4.5. Business Plans

**Citation:** INS § 12399

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall limit or preclude the consideration or approval of an application for a license, except as expressly provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 344, Sec. 1.)
