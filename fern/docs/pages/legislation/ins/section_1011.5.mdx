# INS § 1011.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14. Proceedings in Cases of Insolvency and Delinquency

**Citation:** INS § 1011.5

---

## Text

The consent described in subdivision (c) of Section 1011 shall be obtained by filing an application with the commissioner in a form to be prescribed by him or her accompanied by that additional information concerning the insurer, its condition and affairs, as the commissioner requires.

A fee of six thousand three hundred fifty dollars ($6,350) shall be paid to the commissioner for the filing of the application.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 18. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
