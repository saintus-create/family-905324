# INS § 11100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 6. Powers of Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 11100

---

## Text

Every society operating under the provisions of this chapter and issuing a policy or certificate of insurance may appoint agents to represent it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
