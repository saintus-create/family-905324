# INS § 1631.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1631.5

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be deemed to affect the current operations of the Healthy Families Program (Part 6.2 (commencing with Section 12693) of Division 2) or the Access for Infants and Mothers Program (Part 6.3 (commencing with Section 12695) of Division 2).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 321, Sec. 3.5. Effective January 1, 2001. Operative January 1, 2002, by Sec. 9 of Ch. 321.)
