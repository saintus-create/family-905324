# INS § 5059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 1. Formation and Organization

**Citation:** INS § 5059

---

## Text

A member may cast his votes in person or by proxy, distributing them among the directors to be elected, or among a less number of the directors, or cumulating them upon one candidate, as he sees fit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
