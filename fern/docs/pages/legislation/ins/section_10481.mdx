# INS § 10481

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 3. Valuation of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10481

---

## Text

Every admitted foreign life insurer shall, upon the written demand of the commissioner, furnish him or her, at such time as he designates, the requisite data for determining the valuation of all its policies then outstanding.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1005, Sec. 4.)
