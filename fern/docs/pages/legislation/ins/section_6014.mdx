# INS § 6014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 4. The Policy

**Citation:** INS § 6014

---

## Text

All county mutual fire insurance policies on subject matter in this State shall be on the county mutual standard form and, except as provided by this article, shall not contain additions thereto. Except as provided in Section 6017, no part of the standard form shall be omitted from the policy.
