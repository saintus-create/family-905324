# INS § 1758.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 16. Variable Contract Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1758.5

---

## Text

The commissioner may, pursuant to Chapter 4.5 (commencing with Section 11371), Part 1, Division 3, Title 2 of the Government Code, adopt reasonable rules and regulations necessary for the convenient administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1707.)
