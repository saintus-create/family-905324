# INS § 1309

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 2. Organization of Exchange

**Citation:** INS § 1309

---

## Text

Such body shall have authority to procure the audit of the accounts and records of the exchange and of the attorney-in-fact, at the expense of the exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
