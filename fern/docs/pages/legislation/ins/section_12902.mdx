# INS § 12902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 1. Appointment, Qualification, and Offices

**Citation:** INS § 12902

---

## Text

The annual salary of the commissioner is provided for by Chapter 6 of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code.
