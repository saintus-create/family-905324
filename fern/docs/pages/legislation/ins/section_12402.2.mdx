# INS § 12402.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 5.7. Advisory Organizations

**Citation:** INS § 12402.2

---

## Text

No bylaw or rule or regulation required by Section 12402.1 shall be effective until filed with the commissioner.

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1130.)
