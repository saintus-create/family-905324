# INS § 1056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14. Proceedings in Cases of Insolvency and Delinquency

**Citation:** INS § 1056

---

## Text

All costs and expenses connected with proceedings for the mutualization of such insurer shall be paid by the commissioner out of the funds of such insurer, whether or not mutualized, subject to the approval of said court.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 291.)
