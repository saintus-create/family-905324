# INS § 16002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 3. Emergency Disaster Assessment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 16002

---

## Text

The purpose and function of the disaster assessment teams is solely to determine the staffing and support needs of insurers in handling anticipated claims.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 510, Sec. 1. Effective September 15, 1998.)
