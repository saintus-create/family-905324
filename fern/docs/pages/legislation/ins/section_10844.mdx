# INS § 10844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. The Private Health Care Voluntary Purchasing Alliance Act > Article: 5. Additional Powers of and Restrictions on Purchasing Alliances

**Citation:** INS § 10844

---

## Text

A purchasing alliance may offer coverage pursuant to Chapter 9.5 (commencing with Section 10900).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 810, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 2001.)
