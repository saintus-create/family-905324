# INS § 9050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 9. Dissolution

**Citation:** INS § 9050

---

## Text

Any such reinsurer may be proceeded against and dissolved in the same manner and upon the same conditions as in the case of other domestic insurers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
