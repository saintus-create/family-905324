# INS § 1759.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5A. Administrators

**Citation:** INS § 1759.8

---

## Text

With respect to any policies where an administrator adjusts or settles claims, the compensation to the administrator with regard to such policies shall in no way be contingent on claim experience.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 998.)
