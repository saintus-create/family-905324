# INS § 10073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Underwriters Fire Patrols > Article: 4. Meetings and Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 10073

---

## Text

A majority of the whole number so represented may decide the question of sustaining the fire patrol organized by the corporation and may fix the maximum amount of expenses to be incurred therefor during the ensuing fiscal year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
