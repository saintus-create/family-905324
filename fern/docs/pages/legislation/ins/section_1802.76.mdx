# INS § 1802.76

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1802.76

---

## Text

Upon failure of the applicant to pay the expense of publication of notice within 30 days after the presentation of the bill therefor, the commissioner shall collect the costs out of the deposited securities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 2048.)
