# INS § 10742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 4. Purchasing Pool for Small Employers

**Citation:** INS § 10742

---

## Text

The board shall establish a mechanism to collect premiums from small employers, including remittance of the share of the premium paid by the enrollee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1128, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1128.)
