# INS § 11624.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 4. Assigned Risk Plans

**Citation:** INS § 11624.3

---

## Text

Any return premium checks due to an insured or to a lender subject to the provisions of subdivision (g) of Section 673 on account of a cancellation or endorsement shall be mailed within 30 days of the effective date of the cancellation or endorsement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 509, Sec. 4.)
