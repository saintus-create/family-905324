# INS § 8053

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 4. Risks

**Citation:** INS § 8053

---

## Text

Such reinsurer shall not at any time write reinsurance, subject to one risk, in excess of five per cent of the total amount of reinsurance on its books at the time of accepting the risk.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
