# INS § 15062

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 8. Expiration and Renewal of Licenses and Cards

**Citation:** INS § 15062

---

## Text

Application or license fees shall not be refunded pursuant to Section 1751.5.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2016, Ch. 833, Sec. 24. (SB 488) Effective January 1, 2017.)
