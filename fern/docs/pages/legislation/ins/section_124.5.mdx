# INS § 124.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 1. Classes of Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 124.5

---

## Text

“Homeowners’ insurance” means insurance covering the risks described in subdivision (a) of Section 675.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 447, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2006.)
