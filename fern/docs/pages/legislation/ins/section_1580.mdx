# INS § 1580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Foreign Insurers > Article: 2. Alien Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1580

---

## Text

As used in this article, the term “alien insurer” means a foreign insurer organized under the laws of any jurisdiction other than a State of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 51.)
