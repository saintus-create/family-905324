# INS § 1781.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 6.5. Reinsurance Intermediaries

**Citation:** INS § 1781.13

---

## Text

No insurer or reinsurer may continue to utilize the services of a reinsurance intermediary on and after January 1, 1992, unless utilization is in compliance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1009, Sec. 1.)
