# INS § 12691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.1. HEALTH DISABILITY INSURANCE CONVERSION

**Citation:** INS § 12691

---

## Text

A converted policy which is delivered in a jurisdiction other than this state may be in a form which could be delivered in such jurisdiction as a converted policy had the group policy been issued in such jurisdiction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1096.)
