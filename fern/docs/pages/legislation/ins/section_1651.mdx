# INS § 1651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1651

---

## Text

The commissioner shall at all times retain full property rights in any document evidencing any license issued pursuant to Chapters 5, 6, 7, and 8 of this part. The commissioner may require the surrender of said document for any proper reason.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 5. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
