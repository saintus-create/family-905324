# INS § 1181

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 3. General Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1181

## Text

Such insurers may also invest in registered warrants of this State, issued pursuant to law.
