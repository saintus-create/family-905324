# INS § 12158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 12158

---

## Text

A service contract is a written agreement whereby any person promises for a consideration to render, furnish or procure motor club service for any other person.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
