# INS § 12152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 12152

---

## Text

Map service is the furnishing by a motor club of road maps without cost to holders of service contracts with any such club.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
