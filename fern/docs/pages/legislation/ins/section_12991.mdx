# INS § 12991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 5. Fee Oversight

**Citation:** INS § 12991

---

## Text

On and after October 1, 1995, the department may not levy any fee or fees under Section 736 or 12979 unless the fees are created in compliance with Sections 12992 and 12993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 965, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
