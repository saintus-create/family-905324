# INS § 1861.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 10. [Reduction and Control of Insurance Rates]

**Citation:** INS § 1861.04

---

## Text

Full Disclosure of Insurance Information. (a) Upon request, and for a reasonable fee to cover costs, the commissioner shall provide consumers with a comparison of the rate in effect for each personal line of insurance for every insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added November 8, 1988, by initiative Proposition 103.)
