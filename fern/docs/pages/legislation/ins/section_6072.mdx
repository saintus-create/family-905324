# INS § 6072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 6. Reinsurance

**Citation:** INS § 6072

---

## Text

The restrictions imposed by this chapter upon any insurer as to original insurance shall apply to reinsurance written by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
