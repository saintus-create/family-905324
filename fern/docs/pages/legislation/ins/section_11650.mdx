# INS § 11650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Workers’ Compensation Policies > Article: 2. Policy Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 11650

---

## Text

Every contract insuring against liability for compensation and every compensation policy is conclusively presumed to contain all of the provisions required by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
