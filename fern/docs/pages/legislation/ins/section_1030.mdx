# INS § 1030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14. Proceedings in Cases of Insolvency and Delinquency

**Citation:** INS § 1030

---

## Text

The value of the security to be credited upon such claim shall be determined by an appraiser appointed by the liquidator and approved by the court. Such claimant shall elect to accept the security or to release it to the liquidator.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1935, Ch. 291.)
