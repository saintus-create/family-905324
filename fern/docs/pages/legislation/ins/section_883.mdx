# INS § 883

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 9. Registration of Insurers’ Names

**Citation:** INS § 883

---

## Text

An underwriter’s policy may be issued under a name thus registered and shall clearly show:

(a) The names of the insurers guaranteeing it.

(b) The severalty of the contract.

(c) The proportion of the premium to be paid to each insurer.

(d) The proportion of liability which each assumes.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
