# INS § 11660

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Workers’ Compensation Policies > Article: 2. Policy Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 11660

---

## Text

Failure to observe the requirements of Sections 11657 and 11659 shall render a policy issued under Section 11657, and not complying therewith, unlimited.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 582, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1996.)
