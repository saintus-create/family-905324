# INS § 12693.326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** INS § 12693.326

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this part, a new subscriber in the program shall be allowed to switch his or her choice of plans once within the first three months of coverage for any reason.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 234, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2005.)
