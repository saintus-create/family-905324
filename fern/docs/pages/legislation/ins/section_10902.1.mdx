# INS § 10902.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9.5. Individual Access to Contracts for Health Care Services

**Citation:** INS § 10902.1

---

## Text

In connection with the offering for sale of any health benefit plan designed to an individual, each carrier shall make a reasonable disclosure, as part of its solicitation and sales materials, of all individual contracts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 810, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2001.)
