# INS § 1749.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 13.5. Prelicensing and Continuing Education

**Citation:** INS § 1749.9

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall require any person exempted from licensure by Section 1634 or 1635 to hold a license as required by Section 1631.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1040, Sec. 17.)
