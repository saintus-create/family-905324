# INS § 12254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 3. The Service Contract

**Citation:** INS § 12254

---

## Text

A club or an officer or agent thereof shall not in any manner misrepresent the terms, benefits or privileges of any service contract issued or to be issued by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
