# INS § 12389.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 3.7. Underwritten Title Companies

**Citation:** INS § 12389.5

---

## Text

Every person engaged in the business of preparing title searches, title examinations, title reports, and certificates of abstracts of title, upon which a title insurer writes title policies, is required to be licensed as an underwritten title company in compliance with the licensing requirements of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 433, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
