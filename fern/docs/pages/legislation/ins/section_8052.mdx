# INS § 8052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 4. Risks

**Citation:** INS § 8052

---

## Text

Such reinsurer may write reinsurance on property which is located in this State and which is insured in any member insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
