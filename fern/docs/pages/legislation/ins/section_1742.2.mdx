# INS § 1742.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 13. Disciplinary Actions

**Citation:** INS § 1742.2

---

## Text

The department shall promulgate regulations necessary to comply with the requirements of Section 1033 of Title 18 of the United States Code no later than January 1, 2001.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 782, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2000.)
