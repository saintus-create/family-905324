# INS § 6016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 4. The Policy

**Citation:** INS § 6016

---

## Text

Clauses may be added to the policy providing for and defining the rights, duties and obligations of mortgagees, assignees, and other parties having an interest in, right to or lien upon the insured subject matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
