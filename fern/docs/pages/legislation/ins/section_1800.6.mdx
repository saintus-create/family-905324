# INS § 1800.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1800.6

---

## Text

This chapter shall not limit the power of any city or county to enact other and further regulations concerning, and not in conflict with, the provisions of this chapter.
