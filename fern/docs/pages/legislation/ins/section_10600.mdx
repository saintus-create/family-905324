# INS § 10600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Disability Insurance Disclosure

**Citation:** INS § 10600

## Text

This part shall be known and may be referred to as the “Health Insurance Disclosure Act of 1974.”

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 936.)
