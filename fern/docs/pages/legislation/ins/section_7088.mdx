# INS § 7088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 1. Formation and Organization

**Citation:** INS § 7088

---

## Text

The annual meeting of the members of the reinsurer shall be held on the third Monday in February of each year.
