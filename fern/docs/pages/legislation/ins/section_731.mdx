# INS § 731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 4. Examination by Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 731

---

## Text

Whenever any foreign insurer applies for admission the commissioner may make, or cause to be made by the insurance authority of the State where the insurer is organized, an examination of its insurance business and affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
