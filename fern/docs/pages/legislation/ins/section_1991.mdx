# INS § 1991

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1991

---

## Text

When profits are valued and insured by marine insurance, a loss of them is conclusively presumed from a loss of the property out of which they were expected to arise, and the valuation fixes their amount.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
