# INS § 941

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 11. Deposit of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 941

---

## Text

Such deposited securities shall not be estimated above their par value nor above their market value, except that preferred stock shall be estimated only at its market value.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1974, Ch. 588.)
