# INS § 11803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** INS § 11803

---

## Text

All claims, costs of doing business, liabilities, expenses, and obligations arising out of or related to the fund’s transaction of insurance pursuant to the United States Longshoremen’s and Harbor Workers’ Compensation Act shall be paid and charged to the income of whatsoever nature derived from its United States Longshoremen’s and Harbor Workers’ Compensation Act insurance business only.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 507.)
