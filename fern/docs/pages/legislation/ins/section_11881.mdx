# INS § 11881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 6. Penalties

**Citation:** INS § 11881

---

## Text

Whenever in Chapter 4, Part 3, Division 2 of the Insurance Code the term “State Industrial Accident Commission” or “Industrial Accident Commission” or “commission” or “director” or similar designation occurs, it means the Board of Directors of the State Compensation Insurance Fund except when such meaning is inconsistent with the intent and context of said chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1431.)
