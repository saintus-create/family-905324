# INS § 11061

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 5. Contract

**Citation:** INS § 11061

---

## Text

All statements purporting to be made by the member shall, in the absence of fraud, be representations and not warranties. Any waiver of this provision shall be void.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
