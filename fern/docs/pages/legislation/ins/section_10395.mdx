# INS § 10395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 7. Exemption

**Citation:** INS § 10395

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 800, 801, 802, 803, and 804 shall not apply to disability insurance.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 676, Sec. 198.)
