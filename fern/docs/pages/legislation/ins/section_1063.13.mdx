# INS § 1063.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14.2. California Insurance Guarantee Association

**Citation:** INS § 1063.13

---

## Text

No member insurer of the association shall engage in the unlawful trade practice defined and condemned in subdivision (g) of Section 790.03.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 1205.)
