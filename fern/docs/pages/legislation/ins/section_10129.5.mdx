# INS § 10129.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 2. Transfer

**Citation:** INS § 10129.5

---

## Text

Sections 10130 and 10131 do not apply to annuity contracts which are within the scope of Section 401(g) of the Internal Revenue Code of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2061.)
