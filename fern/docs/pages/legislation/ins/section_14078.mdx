# INS § 14078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 6. Nonresidents

**Citation:** INS § 14078

---

## Text

As used in this article, “nonresident” means a person who is not a resident of this state at the time of the performance of the act referred to in Section 14071.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1190, Sec. 11. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 13 of Ch. 1190.)
