# INS § 973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 12. Wildfire Safety and Risk Mitigation Program

**Citation:** INS § 973

---

## Text

(a) The department shall create a framework and multiyear plan with available data for the development, demonstration, and deployment of a public wildfire catastrophe model created pursuant to a grant provided by this article.

(b) The framework and plan shall be published on the department’s internet website upon completion.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 541, Sec. 1. (SB 429) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 978.)
