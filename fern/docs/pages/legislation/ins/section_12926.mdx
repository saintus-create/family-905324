# INS § 12926

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 1. Generally

**Citation:** INS § 12926

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require from every insurer a full compliance with all the provisions of this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
