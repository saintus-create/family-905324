# INS § 1057

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14. Proceedings in Cases of Insolvency and Delinquency

**Citation:** INS § 1057

---

## Text

In all proceedings under this article, the commissioner shall be deemed to be a trustee for the benefit of all creditors and other persons interested in the estate of the person against whom the proceedings are pending.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1935, Ch. 291.)
