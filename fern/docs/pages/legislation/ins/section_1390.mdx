# INS § 1390

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 6. Assessment of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1390

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply only to exchanges writing liability, common carrier liability, or workers’ compensation insurance, except that any exchange may apply for and receive the certificate provided for by Section 1401.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 231, Sec. 8. (AB 2045) Effective January 1, 2019.)
