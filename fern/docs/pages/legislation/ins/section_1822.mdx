# INS § 1822

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1822

---

## Text

A licensee or applicant for a license under this chapter shall notify the commissioner, in writing, of any change in the address from which he intends to conduct his business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 671.)
