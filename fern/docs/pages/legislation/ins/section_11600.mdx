# INS § 11600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Capital Requirements of Reserve Basis Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11600

---

## Text

An incorporated insurer issuing policies of liability, workers’ compensation, or common carrier liability insurance, shall be governed by the paid-in capital and surplus requirements of Sections 700.01 to 700.05, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1981, Ch. 714, Sec. 275.)
