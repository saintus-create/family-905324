# INS § 825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 8. Issue of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 825

---

## Text

“Stock agent” means every person employed or appointed by an insurer or broker who, within this state and for a compensation, sells any security.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 558, Sec. 3. (AB 487) Effective January 1, 2026.)
