# INS § 10153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 3. Policy Provisions Required

**Citation:** INS § 10153

---

## Text

No agreement between the insurer and the policy holder or applicant for insurance contrary to the foregoing shall be held to waive any of the provisions of sections 10151 and 10152.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
