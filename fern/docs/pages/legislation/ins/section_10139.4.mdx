# INS § 10139.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 2.3. Transfers of Structured Settlement Payment Rights

**Citation:** INS § 10139.4

---

## Text

A violation of this article by a transferee shall constitute an unfair business practice pursuant to Chapter 5 (commencing with Section 17200) of Part 2 of Division 7 of the Business and Professions Code and shall be subject to the penalties and other remedies of that chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, Ch. 624, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 2002.)
