# INS § 10089.43

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.6. California Earthquake Authority

**Citation:** INS § 10089.43

---

## Text

Upon termination of the authority by the Legislature, its remaining funds shall be transferred to the General Fund unless otherwise directed by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 944, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1996.)
