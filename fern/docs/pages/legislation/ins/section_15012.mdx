# INS § 15012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 15012

---

## Text

The license issued by the commissioner shall include, but not be limited to, adjusting claims for fire and allied coverages, burglary, flood, and all property claims both real and personal, and loss of income.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
