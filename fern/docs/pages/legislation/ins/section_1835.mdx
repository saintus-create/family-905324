# INS § 1835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Life and Disability Insurance Analysts

**Citation:** INS § 1835

---

## Text

The applicant shall answer under oath such interrogatories as the commissioner himself or through his deputies propounds on forms prepared by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
