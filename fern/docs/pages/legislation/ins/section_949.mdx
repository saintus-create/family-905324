# INS § 949

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 11. Deposit of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 949

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require the payment of seventy-two dollars ($72), in advance, as a fee for each certificate issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 17. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
