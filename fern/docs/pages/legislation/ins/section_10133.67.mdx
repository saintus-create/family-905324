# INS § 10133.67

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 2. Transfer

**Citation:** INS § 10133.67

---

## Text

Pursuant to Section 12921, the commissioner may also agree to payment to a health care provider who submitted a claim for health care benefits provided to an insured that are covered under the insured’s health insurance policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 723, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2006.)
