# INS § 11752.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 3. Rating and Other Organizations

**Citation:** INS § 11752.2

---

## Text

The reasonable cost of any examination authorized by this article of any rating or advisory organization shall be paid by the organization examined.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1123.)
