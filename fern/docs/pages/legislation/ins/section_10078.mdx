# INS § 10078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Underwriters Fire Patrols > Article: 4. Meetings and Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 10078

---

## Text

The secretary of such corporation shall, within the prescribed ten days, by written demand signed by him, require the statement from every person assessable under this chapter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
