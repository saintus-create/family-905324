# INS § 11581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 2. Actions on Policies Containing Liability Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 11581

---

## Text

Upon any proceeding supplementary to execution, such judgment debtor may be required to exhibit any policy carried by him, insuring him against the liability for the loss or damage for which judgment was obtained.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
