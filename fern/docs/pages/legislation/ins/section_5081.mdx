# INS § 5081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 2. Powers

**Citation:** INS § 5081

---

## Text

Such insurer may accumulate such surplus as its directors deem desirable for meeting contingencies, to cover the catastrophe hazard, and for general corporate purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 96.)
