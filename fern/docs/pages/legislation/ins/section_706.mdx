# INS § 706

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Certificate of Authority

**Citation:** INS § 706

---

## Text

Prior to admission each insurer shall file with the commissioner a certified copy of its last annual statement or a verified financial statement exhibiting its condition and affairs.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
