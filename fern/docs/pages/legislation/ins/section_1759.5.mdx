# INS § 1759.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5A. Administrators

**Citation:** INS § 1759.5

---

## Text

The agreement shall make provision with respect to the underwriting or other standards pertaining to the business underwritten by such insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 998.)
