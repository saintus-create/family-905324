# INS § 10484

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 3. Valuation of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10484

---

## Text

In the case of insurance issued by a domestic insurer authorized to do business in a foreign country upon the lives of residents of that country, the commissioner may vary the mortality standard to a standard applicable to that country.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
