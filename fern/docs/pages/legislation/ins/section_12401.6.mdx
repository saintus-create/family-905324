# INS § 12401.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 5.5. Rate Filing and Regulation

**Citation:** INS § 12401.6

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be construed to prohibit concert of action between entities under the same general management and control.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1130.)
