# INS § 12693.01

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12693.01

---

## Text

For purposes of this part, the definitions contained in this chapter shall govern the construction of this part, unless the context requires otherwise.

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
