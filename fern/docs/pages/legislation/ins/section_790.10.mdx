# INS § 790.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 6.5. Unfair Practices

**Citation:** INS § 790.10

---

## Text

The commissioner shall, from time to time as conditions warrant, after notice and public hearing, promulgate reasonable rules and regulations, and amendments and additions thereto, as are necessary to administer this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 975.)
