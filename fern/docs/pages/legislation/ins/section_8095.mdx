# INS § 8095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 6. Assessments and Coverage of Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8095

---

## Text

An action may be brought against any member of such reinsurer that neglects or refuses to pay the liabilities due such reinsurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
