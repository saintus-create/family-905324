# INS § 11845

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 3. Policies

**Citation:** INS § 11845

---

## Text

The estimations of the wage values, respectively, of such insured employers and members of their families shall be reasonable and shall be separately stated in and added to the valuation of the pay rolls upon which their premium is computed.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
