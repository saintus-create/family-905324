# INS § 10752.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 5. Stop-Loss Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10752.8

---

## Text

The provisions of this article are severable. If any provision of this article or its application is held invalid, that invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 443, Sec. 1. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2014.)
