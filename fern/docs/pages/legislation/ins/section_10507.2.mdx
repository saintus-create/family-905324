# INS § 10507.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 6. Investment Return Assurance

**Citation:** INS § 10507.2

---

## Text

An investment return assurance policy evidencing such insurance, shall not be issued or delivered in this state until a copy of the form thereof is filed with the commissioner, the fees required by Section 12973.9 are paid, and the commissioner has given written approval of the form.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 142, Sec. 88.)
