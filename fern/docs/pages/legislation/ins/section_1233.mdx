# INS § 1233

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 5.5. Life Insurance Policy Loans

**Citation:** INS § 1233

---

## Text

If the maximum rate of interest is determined pursuant to paragraph (2) of subdivision (a) of Section 1232, the policy shall contain a provision setting forth the frequency at which the rate is to be determined for that policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1351, Sec. 3.)
