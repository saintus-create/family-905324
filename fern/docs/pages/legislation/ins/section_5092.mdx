# INS § 5092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 3. Membership

**Citation:** INS § 5092

---

## Text

The withdrawal may be completed by paying the member’s share, if any, under the terms of his policy, of all claims that exist against the insurer on the day of such completion.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
