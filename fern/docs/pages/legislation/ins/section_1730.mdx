# INS § 1730

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 12. Conduct of Licensee

**Citation:** INS § 1730

---

## Text

A licensee shall not misrepresent the type of license under which that licensee is transacting insurance, nor shall that licensee engage in transactions not authorized by the licenses held by that licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 133, Sec. 20. (SB 272) Effective July 23, 2021.)
