# INS § 359

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 3. Negotiations Before Execution > Article: 2. Representation

**Citation:** INS § 359

---

## Text

If a representation is false in a material point, whether affirmative or promissory, the injured party is entitled to rescind the contract from the time the representation becomes false.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
