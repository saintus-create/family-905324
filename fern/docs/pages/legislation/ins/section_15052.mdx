# INS § 15052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 6. Nonresidents

**Citation:** INS § 15052

---

## Text

As used in this article, “nonresident” means a person who is not a resident of this state at the time of the performance of the act referred to in Section 15045.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
