# INS § 1980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1980

---

## Text

Upon an abandonment, acts done subsequent to the loss and in good faith by persons who at the time were the insured’s agents in respect to the subject matter insured, are at the risk of the insurer, and for his benefit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
