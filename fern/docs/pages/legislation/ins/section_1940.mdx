# INS § 1940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 4. Effect of Course of Voyage

**Citation:** INS § 1940

---

## Text

When the voyage contemplated by marine insurance is described by the places of beginning and ending, the voyage insured is one which conforms to the course of sailing fixed by mercantile usage between those places.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
