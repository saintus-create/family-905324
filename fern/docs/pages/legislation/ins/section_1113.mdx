# INS § 1113

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 18. Multiple Insurer Operations

**Citation:** INS § 1113

---

## Text

For filing application for a permit issued pursuant to this article, the commissioner shall charge and collect the sum of one hundred seven dollars ($107).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 24. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
