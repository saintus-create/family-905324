# INS § 12693.105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12693.105

---

## Text

A health care service plan, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 12693.10, shall include a plan operating as a geographic managed care plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 625, Sec. 2. Effective October 3, 1997.)
