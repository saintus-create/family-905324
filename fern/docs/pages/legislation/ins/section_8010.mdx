# INS § 8010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** INS § 8010

---

## Text

Upon organizing under its articles of incorporation, such reinsurer may carry on a fire reinsurance business as provided by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
