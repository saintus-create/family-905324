# INS § 10602.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Disability Insurance Disclosure

**Citation:** INS § 10602.1

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall prevent an insurer which makes contracts with hospitals from distinguishing between contracting hospitals and noncontracting hospitals.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 936.)
