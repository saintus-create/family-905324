# INS § 11527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 13. Voluntary Mutualization of Incorporated Life and Life and Disability Insurers Having a Capital Stock and Issuing Nonassessable Policies on a Reserve Basis > Article: 1. Authority and Requirements to Mutualize

**Citation:** INS § 11527

---

## Text

The commissioner shall examine the plan submitted under subdivision (c) of Section 11526. The commissioner shall not approve the plan unless in the commissioner’s opinion the rights and interests of the insurer, its policyholders, and shareholders are protected and the commissioner is satisfied that the plan will be fair and equitable in its operation.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 142, Sec. 93.)
