# INS § 886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 9. Registration of Insurers’ Names

**Citation:** INS § 886

---

## Text

Termination of approval and registration of an underwriter’s name, whether through denial of a renewal application or revocation, shall have no effect on the validity or the retention in force to normal expiration of any underwriter’s policies issued on or prior to the date of such termination with an effective date not more than sixty (60) days after its issue date provided that if any such policy is continuous in form it shall not be continued in force beyond its annual renewal date first occurring after such date of termination.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1494.)
