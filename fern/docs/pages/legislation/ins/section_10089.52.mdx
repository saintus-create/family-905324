# INS § 10089.52

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.6. California Earthquake Authority

**Citation:** INS § 10089.52

---

## Text

Nothing in Section 10089.50 or 10089.51 is intended to limit the applicability to the authority of any provision of Section 5450 or subdivision (c) of Section 5922 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 967, Sec. 20. Effective September 27, 1996.)
