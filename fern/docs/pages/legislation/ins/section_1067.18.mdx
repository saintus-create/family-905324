# INS § 1067.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14.7. California Life and Health Insurance Guarantee Association

**Citation:** INS § 1067.18

---

## Text

This article shall not apply to any insurer that was declared to be insolvent or impaired, or as to which delinquency proceedings had been commenced, on or before October 1, 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1993, Ch. 975, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1994.)
