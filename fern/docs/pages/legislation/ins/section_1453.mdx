# INS § 1453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 9. Suits

**Citation:** INS § 1453

---

## Text

A judgment rendered in any case where service of process has been made on the commissioner shall be binding against the subscribers as their interests appear.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
