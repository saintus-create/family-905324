# INS § 1942

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 4. Effect of Course of Voyage

**Citation:** INS § 1942

---

## Text

Deviation is:

(a) A departure from the course of the voyage insured.

(b) An unreasonable delay in pursuing the voyage.

(c) The commencement of an entirely different voyage.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
