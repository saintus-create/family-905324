# INS § 12400.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 5. Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 12400.1

---

## Text

Article 5.6 (commencing with Section 1875.20) of Chapter 12 of Part 2 of Division 1 does not apply to title insurers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 131, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
