# INS § 1396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 6. Assessment of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1396

---

## Text

Notice of all such proposed assessments shall be filed with the commissioner and the assessments shall not take effect until approved by him after such investigation as he deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
