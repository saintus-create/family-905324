# INS § 172

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 2. Parties to Contract: Mortgagor and Mortgagee

**Citation:** INS § 172

---

## Text

If an insurer assents to the transfer of insurance from a mortgagor to a mortgagee, and, at the time of the assent, imposes further obligations on the assignee, the acts of the mortgagor cannot affect the rights of the assignee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
