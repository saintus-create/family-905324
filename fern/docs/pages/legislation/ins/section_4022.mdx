# INS § 4022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 2. Directors

**Citation:** INS § 4022

---

## Text

A director of a mutual insurer shall be a policyholder thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
