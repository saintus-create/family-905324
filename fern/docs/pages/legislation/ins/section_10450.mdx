# INS § 10450

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10450

---

## Text

Any insurer transacting life insurance in this State on September 30, 1939, may register its policies with the commissioner in the manner and subject to the restrictions set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1939, Ch. 933.)
