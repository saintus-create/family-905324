# INS § 9090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Fraternal Fire Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 9090

---

## Text

Such association creates a mutual agreement of its members to participate in each other’s loss by fire.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
