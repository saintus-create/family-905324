# INS § 1756

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 15. Travel Insurance Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1756

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter relating to the appointment and termination of an insurance agent by an insurer or its authorized representative are applicable to licenses issued pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
