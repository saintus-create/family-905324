# INS § 11106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 6. Powers of Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 11106

---

## Text

An application for injunction against, proceedings for the dissolution of, or the appointment of a receiver for, any domestic society or branch thereof shall not be entertained by any court unless made by the Attorney General.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
