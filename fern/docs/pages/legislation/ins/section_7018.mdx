# INS § 7018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 8. Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 7018

---

## Text

A director of any such insurer who wilfully refuses or neglects to perform the duties imposed upon him by law or by the insurer’s by-laws shall be liable in his individual capacity to the person sustaining a loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
