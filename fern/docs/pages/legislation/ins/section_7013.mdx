# INS § 7013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 8. Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 7013

---

## Text

The term of any such loan shall not be greater than twelve months nor shall the date of maturity be in excess of thirty days beyond the date of the next annual meeting of the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
