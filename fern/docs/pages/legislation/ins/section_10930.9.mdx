# INS § 10930.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9.6. Consumer Operated and Oriented Plans

**Citation:** INS § 10930.9

---

## Text

The department may adopt regulations implementing this chapter pursuant to the Administrative Procedure Act (Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 859, Sec. 3. (AB 1846) Effective January 1, 2013.)
