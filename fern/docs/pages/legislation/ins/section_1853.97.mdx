# INS § 1853.97

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 2.5. Making and Use of Rates—Insurance of Properties Being Purchased From Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** INS § 1853.97

---

## Text

Automobile liability insurance and automobile physical damage insurance may, at the option of the insurer, be considered a single line for rate filing purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1113, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
