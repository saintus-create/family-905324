# INS § 11697

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 1. Deposits by Workers’ Compensation Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11697

---

## Text

The payment of a workers’ compensation claim by the commissioner shall constitute a satisfaction of the claim to the extent of the payment made. In the event any judgment is entered on the claim, the commissioner shall file a proportionate satisfaction thereof in the office of the clerk of the court wherein the judgment is entered.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 899, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
