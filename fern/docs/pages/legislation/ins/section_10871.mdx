# INS § 10871

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. The Private Health Care Voluntary Purchasing Alliance Act > Article: 8. Marketing

**Citation:** INS § 10871

---

## Text

Any marketing, advertisement, or educational material for health benefit plans or ancillary benefit plans sold through the purchasing alliance shall be approved by the board prior to its use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 916, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
