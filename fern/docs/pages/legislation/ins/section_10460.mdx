# INS § 10460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10460

---

## Text

Such mortgage may be deposited in the State treasury as part of the securities required by this article. Such mortgage is withdrawable in like manner with other securities in the deposit. The commissioner shall require the mortgage to be recorded before acceptance for deposit.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
