# INS § 2050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 2. Measure of Indemnity

**Citation:** INS § 2050

---

## Text

The effect of a valuation in a fire policy is the same as in a marine policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
