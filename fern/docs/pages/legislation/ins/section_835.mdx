# INS § 835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 8. Issue of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 835

---

## Text

If the applicant is a partnership, unincorporated association, or joint stock company, it shall file with its application a copy of its articles of partnership or association, and all other papers pertaining to its organization.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
