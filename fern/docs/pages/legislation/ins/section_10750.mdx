# INS § 10750

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 4. Purchasing Pool for Small Employers

**Citation:** INS § 10750

---

## Text

This chapter shall not apply to a health benefit plan that is subject to Chapter 8.01 (commencing with Section 10753) or Chapter 8.02 (commencing with Section 10755), except as otherwise provided in those chapters.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 13. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
