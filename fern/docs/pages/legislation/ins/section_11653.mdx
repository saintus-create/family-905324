# INS § 11653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Workers’ Compensation Policies > Article: 2. Policy Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 11653

---

## Text

Every such contract or policy shall contain a clause to the effect that jurisdiction of the employer will, for the purpose of the law imposing liability for compensation, be jurisdiction of the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
