# INS § 11802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** INS § 11802

---

## Text

All premiums, reserves, investment income, and all property of whatsoever kind derived or acquired by the fund from its transaction of insurance pursuant to the United States Longshoremen’s and Harbor Workers’ Compensation Act shall be maintained and identified in separate accounts and records.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 507.)
