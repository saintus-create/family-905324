# INS § 10752.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 5. Stop-Loss Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10752.46

---

## Text

On April 1, 2014, and on April 1 annually thereafter, a stop-loss insurer shall report to the Department of Insurance the number of small employer stop-loss policies it had issued and in effect as of December 31 of the previous year. The information shall include new policies issued and policies reissued or renewed in the previous year for groups that have 1 to 50 employees and 51 to 100 employees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 443, Sec. 1. (SB 161) Effective January 1, 2014.)
