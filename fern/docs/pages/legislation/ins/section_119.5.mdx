# INS § 119.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 1. Classes of Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 119.5

---

## Text

Insolvency insurance includes insurance against loss arising from the failure of an insolvent insurer to discharge its obligations under its insurance policies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1347.)
