# INS § 1857.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 6. Records and Examinations

**Citation:** INS § 1857.4

---

## Text

The reasonable cost of any examination authorized by this article shall be paid by the organization, group, association or insurer to be examined.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 805.)
