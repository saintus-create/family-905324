# INS § 8074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 5. Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8074

---

## Text

If the two arbitrators thus appointed fail to agree upon the amount of such loss, they shall select a third disinterested person to act with them.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
