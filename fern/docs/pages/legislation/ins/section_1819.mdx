# INS § 1819

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1819

---

## Text

The certificate of the commissioner certifying any facts found after hearing held under this chapter shall be prima facie evidence of the facts set forth therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1939, Ch. 361.)
