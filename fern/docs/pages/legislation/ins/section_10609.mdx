# INS § 10609

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Disability Insurance Disclosure

**Citation:** INS § 10609

---

## Text

Beginning on or before January 1, 1976, each insurer shall, to the extent required by the commissioner, file with the commissioner copies of all printed advertising which the insurer proposes to disseminate in the state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 790.025 by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1208.)
