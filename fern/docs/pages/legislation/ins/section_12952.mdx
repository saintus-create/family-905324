# INS § 12952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 3. Procuring Information on Policies

**Citation:** INS § 12952

---

## Text

In such affidavit the insurer shall make a full, true and correct statement of all the desired facts and information in its possession, regardless of the location of its record of such information.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
