# INS § 12354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 2. Title Insurers; Capital and Guarantee Fund Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12354

---

## Text

As long as the depositing insurer continues solvent, it shall receive the interest and dividends on any assets in the deposit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
