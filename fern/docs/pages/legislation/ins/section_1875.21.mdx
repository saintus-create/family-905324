# INS § 1875.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 12. The Insurance Frauds Prevention Act > Article: 5.6. Insurer Fraud Investigation

**Citation:** INS § 1875.21

---

## Text

Insurers may maintain the unit or division required by this article using its employees or by contracting with others for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1222, Sec. 4.)
