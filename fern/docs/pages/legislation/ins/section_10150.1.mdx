# INS § 10150.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 3. Policy Provisions Required

**Citation:** INS § 10150.1

---

## Text

This article is not applicable to life policies issued on or after the operative date as to such policies of Article 3a, Chapter 1, Part 2, Division 2.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 955.)
