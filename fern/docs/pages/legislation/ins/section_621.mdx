# INS § 621

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 8. Reinsurance

**Citation:** INS § 621

---

## Text

A reinsurance is presumed to be a contract of indemnity against liability, and not merely against damage.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
