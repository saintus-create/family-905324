# INS § 985.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 13. Insolvency

**Citation:** INS § 985.6

---

## Text

The costs incurred in investigating and preparing the report required by Section 985.5 shall be an expense of administration within the meaning of paragraph (1) of subdivision (a) of Section 1033.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1562, Sec. 1.)
