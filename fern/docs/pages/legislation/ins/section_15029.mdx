# INS § 15029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 15029

---

## Text

No person shall concurrently hold a license under the provisions of this chapter and the provisions of Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 14000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
