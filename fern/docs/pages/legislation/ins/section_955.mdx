# INS § 955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 11. Deposit of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 955

---

## Text

All appraisal fees collected by the commissioner under the provisions of this article shall be paid into the State treasury in trust and withdrawn as provided by law for withdrawal of trust funds from the State treasury.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
