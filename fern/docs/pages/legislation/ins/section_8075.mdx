# INS § 8075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 5. Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8075

---

## Text

Such arbitrators so appointed may examine witnesses and do all other things necessary to the proper determination of the amount of loss sustained by the claimant.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
