# INS § 1873.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 12. The Insurance Frauds Prevention Act > Article: 3. Insurance Fraud Reporting

**Citation:** INS § 1873.4

---

## Text

Any or all information released or received by an authorized governmental entity pursuant to Section 1873 or 1873.1 shall be provided by that agency to the Fraud Division within 10 days of the agency’s receipt of the information.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 717, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2006.)
