# INS § 11700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 1. Deposits by Workers’ Compensation Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11700

---

## Text

The deposit required pursuant to Section 11691, unless withdrawn by the commissioner, shall be used only for the payment of compensable workers’ compensation claims and expenses as provided in Section 11698.02 as long as there remains unpaid any claim or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 899, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
