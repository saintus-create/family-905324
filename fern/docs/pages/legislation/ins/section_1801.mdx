# INS § 1801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Bail Licenses > Article: 1. Qualification and Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1801

---

## Text

(a) Bail licenses are:

(1) Bail agents’ licenses.

(2) Bail permittees’ licenses.

(3) Bail solicitors’ licenses.

(4) Bail fugitive recovery agent licenses.

(b) This section shall become operative on July 1, 2023.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed (in Sec. 3) and added by Stats. 2022, Ch. 768, Sec. 4. (AB 2043) Effective January 1, 2023. Operative July 1, 2023, by its own provisions.)
