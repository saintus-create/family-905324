# INS § 779.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.9. Credit Life and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 779.1

---

## Text

The purpose of this article is to promote the public welfare by regulating credit life insurance and credit disability insurance. Nothing in this article is intended to prohibit or discourage reasonable competition.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1316, Sec. 1.)
