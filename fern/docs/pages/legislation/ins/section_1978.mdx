# INS § 1978

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1978

---

## Text

An abandonment is equivalent to a transfer to the insurer of the insured’s interest, with all the chances of recovery and indemnity.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
