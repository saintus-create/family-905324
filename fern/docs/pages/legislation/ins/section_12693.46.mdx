# INS § 12693.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** INS § 12693.46

---

## Text

The board may prohibit applicants who drop coverage after enrolling in the pool from reenrollment in the program for up to six months.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
