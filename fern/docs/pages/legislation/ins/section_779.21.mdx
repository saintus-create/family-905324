# INS § 779.21

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.9. Credit Life and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 779.21

---

## Text

The commissioner may adopt, pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, reasonable rules and regulations necessary to carry out this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 32, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1993.)
