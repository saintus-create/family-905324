# INS § 7015.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 8. Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 7015.5

---

## Text

The commissioner shall charge and collect in advance a fee of one hundred seventy-seven dollars ($177) for issuing a certificate under this article, or for reissuing and amending or changing an obsolete certificate at the request of an insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 55. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
