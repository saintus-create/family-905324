# INS § 779.24

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.9. Credit Life and Disability Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 779.24

---

## Text

Any party affected by an order of the commissioner shall be entitled to judicial review in accordance with the provisions of Section 12940.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1667.)
