# INS § 10489.98

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 3a. Standard Valuation Law

**Citation:** INS § 10489.98

---

## Text

A company shall submit mortality, morbidity, policyholder behavior, or expense experience and other data as prescribed in the valuation manual.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 658, Sec. 22. (SB 696) Effective January 1, 2016.)
