# INS § 10295.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 2.1. Accelerated Death Benefits

**Citation:** INS § 10295.17

---

## Text

An insurer that fails to conform to the requirements provided under this article shall be subject to Article 6.5 (commencing with Section 790) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 345, Sec. 7. (SB 281) Effective January 1, 2014.)
