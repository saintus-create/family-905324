# INS § 11631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Workers’ Compensation Policies > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 11631

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, the term “insurer” includes the State Compensation Insurance Fund.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
