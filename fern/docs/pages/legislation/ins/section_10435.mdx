# INS § 10435

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 1. Restrictions on Business

**Citation:** INS § 10435

---

## Text

Whenever any insurer violates Section 10434, the commissioner may revoke its certificate of authority or refuse to issue a certificate of authority to that insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1139, Sec. 2.)
