# INS § 10930.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9.6. Consumer Operated and Oriented Plans

**Citation:** INS § 10930.2

---

## Text

A domestic or foreign insurer admitted as a CO-OP insurer shall be subject to the same “paid-in capital” or “capital paid-in” requirements as are imposed on domestic and foreign mutual insurers pursuant to Sections 36 and 4011.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 859, Sec. 3. (AB 1846) Effective January 1, 2013.)
