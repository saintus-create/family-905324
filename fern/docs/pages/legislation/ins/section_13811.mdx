# INS § 13811

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 5. Slavery Era Insurance Policies

**Citation:** INS § 13811

---

## Text

The commissioner shall obtain the names of any slaveholders or slaves described in those insurance records, and shall make the information available to the public and the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 934, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
