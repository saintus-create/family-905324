# INS § 2082.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 3. California Standard Form Fire Insurance Policy

**Citation:** INS § 2082.5

---

## Text

Where an insurer has no president or secretary in the United States, the facsimile signature on the standard form may be that of its principal executive officer or manager residing within the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 5.)
