# INS § 12693.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 3. Creation of Program and Powers of the Board

**Citation:** INS § 12693.20

---

## Text

The Healthy Families Program is hereby created and shall be administered by the Managed Risk Medical Insurance Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
