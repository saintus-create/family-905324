# INS § 1879.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 12. The Insurance Frauds Prevention Act > Article: 8. Insurance Fraud Prevention

**Citation:** INS § 1879.6

---

## Text

The commissioner may adopt administrative regulations as he or she deems necessary for the effective implementation of this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1008, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
