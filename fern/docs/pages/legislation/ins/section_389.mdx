# INS § 389

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 1. Definition and Scope

**Citation:** INS § 389

---

## Text

To render an insurance effected by one partner or part-owner applicable to the interest of his copartners, or of other part-owners, it is necessary that the terms of the policy should be such as are applicable to the joint or common interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
