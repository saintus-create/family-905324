# INS § 412

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 2. Types of Policies

**Citation:** INS § 412

---

## Text

A valued policy is one which expresses on its face an agreement that the thing insured shall be valued at a specified sum.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
