# INS § 12105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 4. MISCELLANEOUS CASUALTY INSURANCES > Chapter: 1. Surety Insurers on Reserve Basis; Capital Requirements and Permitted Insurances > Article: 5. Financial Guaranty Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 12105

---

## Text

The filing fee for a certificate of authority or amended certificate of authority to transact financial guaranty insurance shall be seven thousand four hundred seventy-two dollars ($7,472).

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 73. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
