# INS § 10295.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 2.1. Accelerated Death Benefits

**Citation:** INS § 10295.18

---

## Text

Accelerated death benefits shall not limit or exclude coverage by type of illness, treatment, medical condition, or accident, except under the circumstances described in paragraphs (1) to (4), inclusive, of subdivision (g) of Section 10271.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 345, Sec. 7. (SB 281) Effective January 1, 2014.)
