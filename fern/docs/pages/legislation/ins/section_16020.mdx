# INS § 16020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 3. Emergency Disaster Assessment > Article: 3. Adjuster Identification

**Citation:** INS § 16020

---

## Text

The commissioner, in consultation with the Office of Emergency Services and other emergency service agencies, shall establish a method for identification of representatives of insurers.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 352, Sec. 398. (AB 1317) Effective September 26, 2013. Operative July 1, 2013, by Sec. 543 of Ch. 352.)
