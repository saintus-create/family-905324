# INS § 1846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Life and Disability Insurance Analysts

**Citation:** INS § 1846

---

## Text

A licensee or applicant for a license under this article shall notify the commissioner, in writing, of any changes in the address from which he intends to conduct his business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 4.)
