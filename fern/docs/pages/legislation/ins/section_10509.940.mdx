# INS § 10509.940

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 12. Unclaimed Life Insurance and Annuities Act

**Citation:** INS § 10509.940

## Text

This act shall be known and may be cited as the Unclaimed Life Insurance and Annuities Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 286, Sec. 1. (SB 740) Effective January 1, 2020.)
