# INS § 1853.96

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 2.5. Making and Use of Rates—Insurance of Properties Being Purchased From Department of Veterans Affairs

**Citation:** INS § 1853.96

---

## Text

The use of such rates and forms by insurers pursuant to such agreements is hereby expressly permitted, and the provisions of Section 1852 are not applicable thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1950, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 19.)
