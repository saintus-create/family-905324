# INS § 11161

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 8. Violations and Penalties

**Citation:** INS § 11161

---

## Text

Any person violating Section 11160 is guilty of a felony and punishable by a fine not exceeding ten thousand dollars ($10,000) or imprisonment pursuant to subdivision (h) of Section 1170 of the Penal Code, or both.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 15, Sec. 214. (AB 109) Effective April 4, 2011. Operative October 1, 2011, by Sec. 636 of Ch. 15, as amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 39, Sec. 68.)
