# INS § 2010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 6. Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 2010

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 481, 800, 801, 802, 803, and 804 shall not apply to ocean marine insurance as defined in Section 481.
