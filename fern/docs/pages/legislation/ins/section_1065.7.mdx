# INS § 1065.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14.5. Stop Order Power of the Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 1065.7

---

## Text

Any order or notice of the commissioner hereunder may be served on any person, in the same manner and with the same effect as provided for in civil actions in a superior court of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1579.)
