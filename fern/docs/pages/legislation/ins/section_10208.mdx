# INS § 10208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Group Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10208

---

## Text

The policy shall contain a provision for the equitable adjustment of the premium or the amount of insurance payable in the event of a misstatement of the age of an employee.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
