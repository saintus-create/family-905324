# INS § 1757.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 15.5. Cargo Shippers’ Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1757.3

---

## Text

The commissioner may make reasonable rules and regulations necessary for the convenient administration of the provisions of this article respecting the licensing and renewal of licenses of cargo shippers’ agents.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 515, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1994.)
