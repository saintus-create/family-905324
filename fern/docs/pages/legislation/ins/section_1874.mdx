# INS § 1874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 12. The Insurance Frauds Prevention Act > Article: 4. Motor Vehicle Theft and Motor Vehicle Insurance Fraud Reporting

**Citation:** INS § 1874

---

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the Motor Vehicle Theft and Motor Vehicle Insurance Fraud Reporting Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 1119, Sec. 3.)
