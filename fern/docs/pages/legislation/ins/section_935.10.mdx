# INS § 935.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 10.6. Own Risk and Solvency Assessment

**Citation:** INS § 935.10

---

## Text

If any provision of this article, or the application thereof to any person or circumstance, is held invalid, that determination shall not affect the provisions or applications of this article that can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and to that end the provisions of this article are severable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2013, Ch. 238, Sec. 1. (AB 584) Effective January 1, 2014. Section operative January 1, 2015, pursuant to Section 935.11.)
