# INS § 7050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 10. Dissolution

**Citation:** INS § 7050

---

## Text

Any such insurer may be proceeded against and dissolved in the same manner and upon the same conditions as in the case of other domestic incorporated insurers.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
