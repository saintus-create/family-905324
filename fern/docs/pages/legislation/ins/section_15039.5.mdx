# INS § 15039.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 5. Disciplinary Proceedings

**Citation:** INS § 15039.5

---

## Text

The commissioner may, without hearing, suspend or revoke a license issued under this chapter if he or she determines that the licensee has committed any act or crime constituting grounds for denial of license under Section 15018.5.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 448, Sec. 20. Effective January 1, 2006.)
