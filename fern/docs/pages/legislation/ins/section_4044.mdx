# INS § 4044

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 4. Loans

**Citation:** INS § 4044

---

## Text

This section shall not apply to loans obtained by the insurer in ordinary course of business from banks and other financial institutions, nor to loans secured by pledge or mortgage of assets.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
