# INS § 445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 4. Warranties

**Citation:** INS § 445

---

## Text

A statement in a policy, which imports that there is an intention to do or not to do a thing which materially affects the risk, is a warranty that such act or omission will take place.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
