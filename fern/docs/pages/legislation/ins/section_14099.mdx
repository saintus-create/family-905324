# INS § 14099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 9. Revenue

**Citation:** INS § 14099

---

## Text

Application or license fee shall not be refunded except in accordance with Section 158 of the Business and Professions Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 1190, Sec. 11. Operative July 1, 1981, by Sec. 13 of Ch. 1190.)
