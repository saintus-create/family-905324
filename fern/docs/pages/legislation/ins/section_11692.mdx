# INS § 11692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 1. Deposits by Workers’ Compensation Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11692

---

## Text

A certificate of authority to transact workers’ compensation insurance in this state shall not be issued nor renewed to any insurer until the deposit required pursuant to Section 11691 is approved by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2005, Ch. 415, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2006.)
