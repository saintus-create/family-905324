# INS § 1171

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 3. General Investments

**Citation:** INS § 1171

---

## Text

Such insurers may invest in obligations of the United States or obligations for which the faith and credit of the United States are pledged for payment of principal and interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
