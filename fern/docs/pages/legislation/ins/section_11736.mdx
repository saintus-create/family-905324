# INS § 11736

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 2. State Rate Supervision

**Citation:** INS § 11736

---

## Text

An experience rating plan shall contain reasonable eligibility standards, provide adequate incentives for loss prevention, and shall provide for sufficient premium differentials so as to encourage safety.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1997, Ch. 748, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
