# INS § 1067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14.7. California Life and Health Insurance Guarantee Association

**Citation:** INS § 1067

## Text

This article shall be known and may be cited as the California Life and Health Insurance Guarantee Association Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 974, Sec. 1.7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
