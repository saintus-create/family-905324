# INS § 11885.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 7. Transfer of Ownership

**Citation:** INS § 11885.3

---

## Text

In order to accomplish the purpose of this article, the State Compensation Insurance Fund and its board of directors shall participate fully in good faith with the Director of Finance, and the Director of Finance shall act in good faith in carrying out the duties prescribed by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 4th Ex. Sess., Ch. 12, Sec. 21. Effective July 28, 2009.)
