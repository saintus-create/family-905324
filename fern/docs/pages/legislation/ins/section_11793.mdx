# INS § 11793

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** INS § 11793

---

## Text

Expenditures made by the State Compensation Insurance Fund are exempted from the provisions of Part 3 (commencing with Section 900) of Division 3.6 of Title 1 of the Government Code.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 738.)
