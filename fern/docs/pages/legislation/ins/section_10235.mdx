# INS § 10235

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.6. Long-Term Care Insurance > Article: 4. Implementation

**Citation:** INS § 10235

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 10235.95, this article applies to all long-term care insurance policies delivered or issued for delivery in this state on or after January 1, 1990.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 171, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2009.)
