# INS § 10369.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 5. Optional Standard Provisions and Special Provisions—Uniform Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 10369.3

---

## Text

A disability policy may contain a provision in the form set forth herein.

Misstatement of Age: If the age of the insured has been misstated, all amounts payable under this policy shall be such as the premium paid would have purchased at the correct age.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 570.)
