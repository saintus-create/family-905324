# INS § 1850.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 1. Purpose and Scope of Chapter

**Citation:** INS § 1850.4

---

## Text

In this chapter “casualty insurance” means all classes of insurance to which the provisions of this chapter are applicable and which are included within Sections 105, 107, 108, 110, 112, 113, 115, and, when written by insurers not admitted to transact fire or marine insurance, Sections 111, 114, 116, 118 and 120.
