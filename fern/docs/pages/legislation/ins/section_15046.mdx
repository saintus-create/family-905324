# INS § 15046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 6. Nonresidents

**Citation:** INS § 15046

---

## Text

The acceptance of such rights and privileges as evidenced by such act shall signify the agreement of the licensee that any such process which is served against him or her in the manner provided in this article shall be of the same legal force and validity as if serviced upon him or her personally in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
