# INS § 10456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10456

---

## Text

Such deposit shall be maintained in an amount equal to the full net value of all policies registered up to the time of making the deposit, less the amount loaned on such registered policies.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
