# INS § 900.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 10. Financial Statements of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 900.8

---

## Text

The commissioner may decline to grant or renew or may suspend or revoke a certificate of authority of an insurer that knowingly files with the department a false financial statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 395.)
