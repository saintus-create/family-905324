# INS § 11099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 6. Powers of Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 11099

---

## Text

Article 14 (commencing with Section 1010) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 1 applies to any society, except as otherwise provided in this chapter.
