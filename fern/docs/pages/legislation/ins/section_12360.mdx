# INS § 12360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 2. Title Insurers; Capital and Guarantee Fund Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12360

---

## Text

An insurer which anywhere in the United States transacts any class of insurance other than title insurance is not eligible for the issuance of a certificate of authority to transact title insurance in this State nor for the renewal thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 542.)
