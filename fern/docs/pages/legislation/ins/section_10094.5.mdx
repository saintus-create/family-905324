# INS § 10094.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Basic Property Insurance Inspection and Placement Plan

**Citation:** INS § 10094.5

---

## Text

Within 90 days following the effective date of this section, the association shall file a new or amended rate application with the commissioner consistent with paragraph (1) of subdivision (c) of Section 10091.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2021, Ch. 128, Sec. 3. (SB 11) Effective July 23, 2021.)
