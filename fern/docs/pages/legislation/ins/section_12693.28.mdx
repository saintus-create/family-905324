# INS § 12693.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** INS § 12693.28

---

## Text

The program shall be administered without regard to gender, gender identity, gender expression, race, creed, color, sexual orientation, health status, disability, or occupation.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 719, Sec. 28. (AB 887) Effective January 1, 2012.)
