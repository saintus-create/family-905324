# INS § 486

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 5. The Premium

**Citation:** INS § 486

---

## Text

When an overinsurance is effected by simultaneous policies, the insurers contribute to the premium to be returned in proportion to the amount insured by their respective policies.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
