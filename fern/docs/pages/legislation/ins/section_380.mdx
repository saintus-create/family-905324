# INS § 380

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 1. Definition and Scope

**Citation:** INS § 380

---

## Text

The written instrument, in which a contract of insurance is set forth, is the policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
