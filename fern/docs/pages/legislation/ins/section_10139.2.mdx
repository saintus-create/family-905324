# INS § 10139.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 2.3. Transfers of Structured Settlement Payment Rights

**Citation:** INS § 10139.2

---

## Text

Any notice required by this article shall be deemed to have been given if addressed to the recipient’s last known address and deposited, first class postage paid, in the United States mail not less than five calendar days prior to the date on which notice is required.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 10141 (as added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 742) by Stats. 2000, Ch. 135, Sec. 115. Effective January 1, 2001.)
