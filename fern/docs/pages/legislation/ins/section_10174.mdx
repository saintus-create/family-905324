# INS § 10174

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 4. Payment and Proceeds

**Citation:** INS § 10174

---

## Text

Policies of disability insurance, as defined in Section 106, that provide for death benefits, shall, as to those death benefits, be subject to Sections 10172, 10172.5, and 10173.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 601, Sec. 5. Effective January 1, 2005.)
