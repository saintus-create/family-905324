# INS § 150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 1. Parties to the Contract: Generally

**Citation:** INS § 150

---

## Text

Any person capable of making a contract may be an insurer, subject to the restrictions imposed by this code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
