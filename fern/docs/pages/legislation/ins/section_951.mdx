# INS § 951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 11. Deposit of Securities

**Citation:** INS § 951

---

## Text

Pending such examination the securities requested to be withdrawn may continue subject to withdrawal and substitution as provided by section 943.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
