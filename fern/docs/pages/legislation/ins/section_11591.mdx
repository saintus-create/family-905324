# INS § 11591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 2.5. Personal Liability Insurance Providing Workers’ Compensation Coverage for Household Employees

**Citation:** INS § 11591

---

## Text

The requirements of Section 11590 shall be inapplicable to any such policy of insurance or endorsement where the services of such employee are in connection with the trade, business, profession, or occupation, as such terms are defined in Sections 3355 and 3356 of the Labor Code, of the insured.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 17.)
