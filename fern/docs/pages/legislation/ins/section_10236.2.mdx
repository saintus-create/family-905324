# INS § 10236.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.6. Long-Term Care Insurance > Article: 4. Implementation

**Citation:** INS § 10236.2

---

## Text

Except where the provisions of a group contract provide otherwise, the provisions of subdivisions (d) and (e) of Section 10236.1 shall apply to all group long-term care insurance policies issued before the approval of premium rate schedules under Section 10236.11.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 627, Sec. 4. (AB 999) Effective January 1, 2013.)
