# INS § 1215.17

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 4.7. Insurance Holding Company System Regulatory Act

**Citation:** INS § 1215.17

---

## Text

If any provision of this article or the application thereof to any person or circumstance is held invalid, the invalidity shall not affect other provisions or applications of this article which can be given effect without the invalid provision or application, and for this purpose the provisions of this article are severable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1215.15 by Stats. 2012, Ch. 282, Sec. 17. (SB 1448) Effective January 1, 2013.)
