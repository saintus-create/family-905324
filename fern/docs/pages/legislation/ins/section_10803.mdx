# INS § 10803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. The Private Health Care Voluntary Purchasing Alliance Act > Article: 1. General

**Citation:** INS § 10803

---

## Text

It is envisioned that a purchasing alliance will contract with qualified group carriers to provide a meaningful choice of carriers providing health benefit plans or ancillary benefit plans to purchasing alliance participants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 916, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
