# INS § 281

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 4. Insurable Interest: Generally

**Citation:** INS § 281

---

## Text

Every interest in property, or any relation thereto, or liability in respect thereof, of such a nature that a contemplated peril might directly damnify the insured, is an insurable interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
