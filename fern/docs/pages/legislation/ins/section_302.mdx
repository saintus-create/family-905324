# INS § 302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 5. Insurable Interest: Effect of Transfer

**Citation:** INS § 302

---

## Text

A change of interest in one or more of several distinct subjects, separately insured by one policy, does not avoid the insurance as to the others.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
