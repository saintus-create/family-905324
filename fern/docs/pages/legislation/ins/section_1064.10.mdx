# INS § 1064.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14.3. Uniform Liquidation Act

**Citation:** INS § 1064.10

---

## Text

The domiciliary receiver of an insurer domiciled in a reciprocal state may sue in this state to recover any assets of that insurer to which he or she may be entitled under the laws of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1466, Sec. 1.)
