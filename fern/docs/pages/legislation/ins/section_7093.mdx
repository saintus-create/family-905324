# INS § 7093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 1. Formation and Organization

**Citation:** INS § 7093

---

## Text

The treasurer and secretary shall give bonds to the reinsurer in such amounts as are prescribed by the board of directors, conditioned upon the faithful performance of their duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
