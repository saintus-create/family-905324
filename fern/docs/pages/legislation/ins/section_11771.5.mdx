# INS § 11771.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** INS § 11771.5

---

## Text

Any advertising of the State Compensation Insurance Fund shall include the following disclaimer: “The State Compensation Insurance Fund is not a branch of the State of California.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 6, Sec. 8. Effective January 1, 2003.)
