# INS § 12417

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 7. Annual Renewal Fees

**Citation:** INS § 12417

---

## Text

The annual renewal fee provided by this article is not a tax but a charge for services to be rendered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 1415.)
