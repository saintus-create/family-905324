# INS § 14023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 14023

---

## Text

An application for a license under this chapter shall be on a form prescribed by the commissioner and accompanied by the application fee provided by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2019, Ch. 502, Sec. 5. (SB 240) Effective October 3, 2019.)
