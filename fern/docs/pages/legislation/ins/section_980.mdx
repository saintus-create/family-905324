# INS § 980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 13. Insolvency

**Citation:** INS § 980

---

## Text

As used in this article, “liability” includes liability for losses reported, expenses, taxes, and all other indebtedness not included in those categories.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
