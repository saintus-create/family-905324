# INS § 1327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 3. Filing of Documents

**Citation:** INS § 1327

---

## Text

The attorney’s bond may be sued upon in one and the same action either by any subscriber or any number of subscribers suffering loss through a violation of its conditions or by the receiver or trustee in liquidation of the exchange. Liability thereunder may be enforced by any one or more of such parties.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
