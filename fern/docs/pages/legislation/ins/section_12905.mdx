# INS § 12905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 1. Appointment, Qualification, and Offices

**Citation:** INS § 12905

---

## Text

The commissioner shall maintain offices in Sacramento, Los Angeles, San Diego, and the San Francisco Bay area.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 37, Sec. 44. (AB 1817) Effective June 27, 2018.)
