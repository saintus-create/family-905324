# INS § 1881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 1. Insurable Interests Peculiar to Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1881

---

## Text

The insurable interest of the owner of a ship hypothecated by bottomry is only the excess of its value over the amount secured by bottomry.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
