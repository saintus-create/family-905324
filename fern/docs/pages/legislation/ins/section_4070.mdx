# INS § 4070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 7. Insolvency

**Citation:** INS § 4070

---

## Text

Such an insurer is insolvent if its surplus becomes less than the amount of paid-in capital required of a capital stock company to qualify to transact the same classes of insurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
