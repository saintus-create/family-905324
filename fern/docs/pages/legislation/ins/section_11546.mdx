# INS § 11546

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 14. Conversion of an Incorporated Mutual Life or Life and Disability Insurer into an Incorporated Stock Life Insurer Issuing Policies on a Reserve Basis > Article: 1. Authority and Requirements to Convert

**Citation:** INS § 11546

---

## Text

Upon completion of the act of conversion and issuance of the certificate of authority under Section 11542, the Secretary of State shall accept for filing a verified copy of the amended articles of incorporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 988, Sec. 1.)
