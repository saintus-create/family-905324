# INS § 5094

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 3. Membership

**Citation:** INS § 5094

---

## Text

In case of cancellation by the insurer it shall also notify in the same manner any holder of a mortgage whose name appears, either on the signed application which is a part of the policy, or otherwise upon the policy and any party to whom loss, if any, is payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
