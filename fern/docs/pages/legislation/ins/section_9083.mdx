# INS § 9083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Fraternal Fire Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 9083

---

## Text

The officers of the association shall be members of the association, having property insured therein.
