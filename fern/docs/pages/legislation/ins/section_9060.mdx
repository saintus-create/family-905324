# INS § 9060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 10. Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 9060

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 980, 981, 982, 983, 984, 985, 986, 987, 988, 989, 990, 991, 992, and 993 shall not apply to county mutual fire reinsurers.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 34.)
