# INS § 8076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 5. Loss

**Citation:** INS § 8076

---

## Text

The arbitrators shall make their award in writing to the president of the reinsurer and to the member. Such award shall be final as to the amount of the loss sustained.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
