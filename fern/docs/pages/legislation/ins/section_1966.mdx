# INS § 1966

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1966

---

## Text

In addition to the liability mentioned in section 1965, a marine insurer is liable, up to the amount insured, for damages, expenses of discharging, storage, reshipment, extra freightage, and all other expenses incurred in saving cargo reshipped pursuant to section 1965.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
