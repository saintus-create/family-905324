# INS § 802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 7. Restrictions on Underwriting

**Citation:** INS § 802

---

## Text

Any admitted insurer may, by means of temporary binders, execute contracts of insurance at offices outside this state upon subject matter located in this state if policies therefor are thereafter issued by it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 805 by Stats. 1969, Ch. 506.)
