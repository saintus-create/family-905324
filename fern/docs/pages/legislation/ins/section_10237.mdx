# INS § 10237

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.6. Long-Term Care Insurance > Article: 5. Inflation Escalator and Benefit Increases

**Citation:** INS § 10237

---

## Text

This article applies to all long-term care insurance policies delivered or issued for delivery in this state on or after January 1, 1991.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 530, Sec. 3.)
