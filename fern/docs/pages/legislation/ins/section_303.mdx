# INS § 303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 5. Insurable Interest: Effect of Transfer

**Citation:** INS § 303

---

## Text

A change of interest by will or succession, on the death of the insured, does not avoid insurance; and his interest in the insurance passes to the person taking his interest in the subject matter insured.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
