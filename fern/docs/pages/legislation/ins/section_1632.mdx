# INS § 1632

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1632

---

## Text

Unless prohibited in this article, a person otherwise eligible for a license may be authorized to act in one or more of the capacities specified in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1420, Sec. 8. Operative January 1, 1992, by Sec. 77 of Ch. 1420.)
