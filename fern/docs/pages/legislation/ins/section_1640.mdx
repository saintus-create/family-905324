# INS § 1640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 3. Licensing

**Citation:** INS § 1640

---

## Text

A person authorized to act as an insurance solicitor is not eligible at the same time to act as an insurance agent or broker, and a person authorized to act as either an insurance agent or broker is not eligible at the same time to act as an insurance solicitor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1420, Sec. 12. Operative January 1, 1992, by Sec. 77 of Ch. 1420.)
