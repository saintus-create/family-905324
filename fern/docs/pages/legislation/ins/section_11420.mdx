# INS § 11420

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 11. Change by Assessment Plan Insurer to Reserve Plan > Article: 1. Authorization and Effect

**Citation:** INS § 11420

---

## Text

Any domestic insurer providing life insurance upon the assessment plan may transform itself into an incorporated life insurer issuing policies on a reserve basis with such name as its directors or trustees determine. If incorporated, such insurer may make the transformation by amendment of its articles and by-laws, either by a majority vote of its directors or trustees or in any other lawful manner. If unincorporated it may make the transformation by incorporating.
