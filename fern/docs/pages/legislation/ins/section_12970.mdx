# INS § 12970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 4. Fees

**Citation:** INS § 12970

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require the payment in advance of a fee for copying microfilm records in the amount of thirty cents ($0.30) per page.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1985, Ch. 770, Sec. 27.5.)
