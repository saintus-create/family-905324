# INS § 10753.09

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8.01. Nongrandfathered Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 2. Small Employer Carrier Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 10753.09

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as prohibiting a carrier from restricting enrollment of late enrollees to open enrollment periods provided under Section 10753.05 as authorized under Section 2702 of the federal Public Health Service Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 852, Sec. 14. (AB 1083) Effective January 1, 2013.)
