# INS § 679.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 11. Cancellation and Failure to Renew Certain Property Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 679.5

---

## Text

Proof of mailing of a notice of cancellation and the reasons therefor or of intention not to renew to the named insured at the address shown in the policy shall be sufficient proof of the notice required by this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 313.)
