# INS § 742.425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 4.7. Multiple Employer Welfare Arrangements

**Citation:** INS § 742.425

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to multiple employer welfare arrangements as defined in Section 1144(b)(6)(D) of Title 29 of the United States Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1994, Ch. 1082, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
