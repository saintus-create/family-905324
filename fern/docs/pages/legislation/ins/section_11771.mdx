# INS § 11771

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** INS § 11771

---

## Text

The State shall not be liable beyond the assets of the State Compensation Insurance Fund for any obligations in connection therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
