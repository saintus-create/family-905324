# INS § 1313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 2. Organization of Exchange

**Citation:** INS § 1313

---

## Text

Nothing in Chapter 504 of the Statutes of 1965 shall limit or affect the right of an admitted foreign reciprocal or interinsurance exchange to accept reinsurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1805.)
