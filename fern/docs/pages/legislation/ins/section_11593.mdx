# INS § 11593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 2.5. Personal Liability Insurance Providing Workers’ Compensation Coverage for Household Employees

**Citation:** INS § 11593

---

## Text

The premium charge for the coverage required by Section 11590 shall not be separately stated from that charged for other coverage under the policy in the insured’s copy of the following: premium notice, policy, endorsement or memorandum of insurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 765.)
