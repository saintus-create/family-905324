# INS § 12757

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 7. HOME PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12757

---

## Text

The provisions of Article 14.2 (commencing with Section 1063) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 1, shall not be applicable to home protection companies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 820, Sec. 10.)
