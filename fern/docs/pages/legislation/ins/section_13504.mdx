# INS § 13504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 7. Suspension of Privilege of Appearing or Practicing Before the Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 13504

---

## Text

Any proceeding brought under any section of this article shall not preclude a proceeding under any other section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1328, Sec. 7.)
