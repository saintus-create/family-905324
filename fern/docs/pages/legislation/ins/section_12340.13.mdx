# INS § 12340.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12340.13

---

## Text

“Business location” means a facility or other place of business in this state where an underwritten title company or controlled escrow company engages in the business of conducting escrow services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2015, Ch. 370, Sec. 1. (AB 704) Effective January 1, 2016.)
