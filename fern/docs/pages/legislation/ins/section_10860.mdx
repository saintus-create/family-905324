# INS § 10860

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. The Private Health Care Voluntary Purchasing Alliance Act > Article: 7. Contracts with Employers, Small Employers, and Participating Carriers

**Citation:** INS § 10860

---

## Text

Contracts between the purchasing alliance and participating carriers shall specify how all premiums will be transmitted, and penalties and grace periods for payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 916, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1997.)
