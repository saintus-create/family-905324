# INS § 11777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 1. Organization and Powers

**Citation:** INS § 11777

---

## Text

Such cash dividend or credit is to be in an amount which the board of directors in its discretion considers to be the employer’s proportion of divisible surplus.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 520.)
