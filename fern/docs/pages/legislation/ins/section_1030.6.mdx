# INS § 1030.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14. Proceedings in Cases of Insolvency and Delinquency

**Citation:** INS § 1030.6

---

## Text

In any proceeding under this article, no agent shall be liable to the liquidator or conservator for unearned premiums uncollected by the agent, or unearned commissions uncollected by the agent, arising from an insolvency of an insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 672, Sec. 1.)
