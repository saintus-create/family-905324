# INS § 1451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 9. Suits

**Citation:** INS § 1451

---

## Text

No action shall lie against any subscriber, upon any obligation made or incurred in the name of the exchange, until a final judgment has been obtained against the exchange and remains unsatisfied for thirty days.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
