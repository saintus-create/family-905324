# INS § 1858.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 7. Hearings, Procedure and Judicial Review

**Citation:** INS § 1858.7

---

## Text

Whenever the commissioner determines that a rate is excessive or not excessive, he shall, upon the written request of any complainant, disclose the basis upon which such rate was determined to be excessive or not excessive in writing to the complainant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1978, Ch. 180.)
