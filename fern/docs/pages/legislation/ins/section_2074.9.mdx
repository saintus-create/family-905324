# INS § 2074.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 3. California Standard Form Fire Insurance Policy

**Citation:** INS § 2074.9

---

## Text

During such time as countersignatures are not required by law, the provisions of Section 2071 requiring policies to make provision for countersignatures shall not be in effect and an insurer may omit such provisions from its policies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 506.)
