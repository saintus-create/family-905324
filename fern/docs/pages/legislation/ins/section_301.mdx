# INS § 301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 5. Insurable Interest: Effect of Transfer

**Citation:** INS § 301

---

## Text

A change of interest in a subject insured, after the occurrence of an injury which results in a loss, does not affect the right of the insured to indemnity for the loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
