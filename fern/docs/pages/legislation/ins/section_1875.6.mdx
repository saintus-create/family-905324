# INS § 1875.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 12. The Insurance Frauds Prevention Act > Article: 5. Arson Investigations

**Citation:** INS § 1875.6

---

## Text

Any authorized agency that receives any information furnished as required by this article shall not make the information public until the time that its release is required in connection with a criminal or civil proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 602, Sec. 4.)
