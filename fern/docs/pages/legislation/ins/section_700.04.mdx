# INS § 700.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Certificate of Authority

**Citation:** INS § 700.04

---

## Text

Paid-in capital for life insurers is governed by Section 10510 of this code, for title insurers by Section 12359, and for mortgage guaranty insurers by Section 12640.03.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2012, Ch. 786, Sec. 6. (AB 2303) Effective January 1, 2013.)
