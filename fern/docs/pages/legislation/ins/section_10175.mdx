# INS § 10175

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 4. Payment and Proceeds

**Citation:** INS § 10175

---

## Text

Nothing contained in Sections 10172, 10173 or 10174 shall affect any claim or right to any policy or the proceeds thereof, or payments thereunder, as between all persons other than the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 272.)
