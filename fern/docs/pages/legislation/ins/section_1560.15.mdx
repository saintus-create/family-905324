# INS § 1560.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 17. Conversion of Certain Reciprocal Insurers to Incorporated Stock Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1560.15

---

## Text

The offer or sale of securities issued pursuant to the plan of conversion developed and approved in accordance with the provisions of this article, shall be exempt from Article 8 (commencing with Section 820) of Chapter 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 421, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1999.)
