# INS § 1833

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Life and Disability Insurance Analysts

**Citation:** INS § 1833

---

## Text

A license to act as life insurance analyst shall not be issued to any person not residing in this state, nor to any person who is under 18 years of age at the time of application.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1748.)
