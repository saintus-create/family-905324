# INS § 1970

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1970

---

## Text

Abandonment, in marine insurance, is the act of the insured by which, after a constructive total loss, he declares the relinquishment to the insurer of the insured’s interest in the subject matter.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
