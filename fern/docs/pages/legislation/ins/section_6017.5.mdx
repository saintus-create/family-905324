# INS § 6017.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 4. The Policy

**Citation:** INS § 6017.5

---

## Text

Insurers authorized to eliminate their assessment liability in accordance with the terms of this chapter may use the California standard form insurance policy in lieu of the standard form prescribed in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1553.)
