# INS § 10181.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 4.5. Review of Rate Increases

**Citation:** INS § 10181.8

---

## Text

A health insurance policy subject to Section 10181.3 or 10181.4 shall file a separate schedule documenting the cost savings associated with Section 10176.11 and the impact on rates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 862, Sec. 6. (AB 290) Effective January 1, 2020.)
