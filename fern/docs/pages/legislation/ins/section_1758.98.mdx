# INS § 1758.98

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 16.7. Credit Insurance Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1758.98

---

## Text

Under the authority of the credit insurance agent license, a credit insurance agent shall not do any of the following:

(a) Offer to sell insurance except in conjunction with, and incidental to, a loan or extension of credit.

(b) Advertise, represent, or otherwise portray itself or its employees, agents, or endorsees as licensed insurers, life agents, property broker-agents, or casualty broker-agents.

(c) Pay any unlicensed person any compensation, fee, or commission dependent on the placement of insurance under the agent’s license. Nothing in this subdivision shall prohibit production payments or incentive payments to an endorsee.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 411, Sec. 51. (AB 1416) Effective January 1, 2012.)
