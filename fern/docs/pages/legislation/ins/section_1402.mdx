# INS § 1402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 6. Assessment of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1402

---

## Text

All funds of such exchange and the proceeds of the contingent liability of its subscribers shall be available for the payment of any liability of the exchange.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
