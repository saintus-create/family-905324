# INS § 10478

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 3. Valuation of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10478

---

## Text

This article is applicable only to policies and contracts issued prior to the operative date as to such policies or contracts of Article 3a, Chapter 1, Part 2, Division 2.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 955.)
