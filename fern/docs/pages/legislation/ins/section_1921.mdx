# INS § 1921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 3. Implied Warranties Peculiar to Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1921

---

## Text

A ship is seaworthy when reasonably fit to perform the services and encounter the ordinary perils of the voyage contemplated by the parties to the policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
