# INS § 4010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** INS § 4010

---

## Text

A “mutual” insurer is an insurance corporation without capital stock owned by its policyholders collectively, who have the right to vote in the election of its directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
