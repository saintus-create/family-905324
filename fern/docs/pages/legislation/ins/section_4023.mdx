# INS § 4023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 2. Directors

**Citation:** INS § 4023

---

## Text

As to an insurer operating as an authorized insurer only in the State of California, a majority of the members of the insurer’s board of directors shall be citizens of and shall actually reside in this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1772.)
