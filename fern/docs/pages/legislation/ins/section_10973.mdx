# INS § 10973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 1. Scope of Chapter

**Citation:** INS § 10973

---

## Text

The commissioner may require from any society such information as will enable him to determine whether or not such society is exempt from the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
