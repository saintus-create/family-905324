# INS § 11874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 5. Coverage of Public Employers

**Citation:** INS § 11874

---

## Text

On the effective date of this act the Controller shall draw his or her warrant in favor of the State Compensation Insurance Fund for the total amount of the funds in the custody of the Treasurer belonging to the State Compensation Insurance Fund, and the Treasurer shall pay that warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 426, Sec. 11. (SB 712) Effective January 1, 2012.)
