# INS § 12052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 4. MISCELLANEOUS CASUALTY INSURANCES > Chapter: 1. Surety Insurers on Reserve Basis; Capital Requirements and Permitted Insurances > Article: 1. Capital and Scope of Business

**Citation:** INS § 12052

---

## Text

After such three-year period, investments of such insurers are subject only to the provisions of this code regulating generally the investments of other incorporated insurers issuing policies on a reserve basis.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
