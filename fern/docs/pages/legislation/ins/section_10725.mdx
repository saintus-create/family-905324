# INS § 10725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Small Employer Health Insurance > Article: 3. Voluntary Reinsurance Mechanism

**Citation:** INS § 10725

---

## Text

The fund’s board of directors shall establish the rules, conditions, and procedures pertaining to the reinsurance of members’ risks by the fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1128, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1993. Operative July 1, 1993, by Sec. 15 of Ch. 1128.)
