# INS § 10072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Underwriters Fire Patrols > Article: 4. Meetings and Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 10072

---

## Text

At such meeting each insurer or insurance agent, doing a fire insurance business in the municipality, whether a member of the corporation or not, has a right to be represented and is entitled to one vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
