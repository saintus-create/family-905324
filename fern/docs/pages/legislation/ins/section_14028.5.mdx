# INS § 14028.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 1. Insurance Adjuster Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 14028.5

---

## Text

The commissioner may, without hearing, deny a license if the applicant has committed any act or crime constituting grounds for denial of license under Section 1669.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 448, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 2006.)
