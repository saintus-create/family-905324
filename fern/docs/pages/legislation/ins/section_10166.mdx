# INS § 10166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 3a. Standard Nonforfeiture Law for Life Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10166

---

## Text

No agreement between the insurer and the policyholder or applicant for insurance contrary to this article shall be held to waive any of the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 955.)
