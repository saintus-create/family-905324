# INS § 1306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 2. Organization of Exchange

**Citation:** INS § 1306

---

## Text

The principal office of the attorney shall be maintained at a place designated by the subscribers in the power of attorney.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
