# INS § 1984

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 5. Loss in Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1984

---

## Text

On an accepted abandonment of a ship, freightage earned previous to the loss belongs to the insurer of the freightage; but freightage subsequently earned belongs to the insurer of the ship.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
