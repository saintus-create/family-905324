# INS § 1599

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Foreign Insurers > Article: 2. Alien Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1599

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require the payment of twenty-nine dollars ($29), in advance, as a fee for filing each certificate of a trustee required to be filed by Section 1592.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 33. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
