# INS § 12414.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 6.9. Examinations, Penalties and Miscellaneous

**Citation:** INS § 12414.20

---

## Text

The commissioner may, as often as may be reasonable and necessary, make or cause to be made an examination of any advisory organization for the business of title insurance in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1130.)
