# INS § 885

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 9. Registration of Insurers’ Names

**Citation:** INS § 885

---

## Text

The commissioner may at any time institute proceedings for the revocation of approval and registration of an underwriter’s name upon any of the grounds set forth in Section 884 by following the procedure prescribed in that section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1494.)
