# INS § 12408.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 6. Rebates and Commissions

**Citation:** INS § 12408.1

---

## Text

Whenever a title insurer terminates its underwriting agreement with any underwritten title company, it shall at the same time give notice of the termination to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 578.)
