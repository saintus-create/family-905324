# INS § 925.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 10. Financial Statements of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 925.2

---

## Text

The commissioner may prescribe the subject matter and form of reporting supplemental information and the subject matter of opinions.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 1328, Sec. 3.)
