# INS § 9088

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 7. Fraternal Fire Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 9088

---

## Text

Such association shall provide in its by-laws for the ascertainment of loss or damage by fire, and for the payment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
