# INS § 12693.25

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 4. Administration

**Citation:** INS § 12693.25

---

## Text

The board may use a purchasing pool model, issuance of purchasing credits, supplemental coverage, or other means as appropriate to meet the purposes of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
