# INS § 922.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 10. Financial Statements of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 922.9

---

## Text

Sections 922.4 and 922.5 shall apply to all cessions on and after January 1, 1997, under reinsurance contracts that have had an inception, anniversary, or renewal date not less than six months after that date.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 840, Sec. 17. Effective January 1, 1997.)
