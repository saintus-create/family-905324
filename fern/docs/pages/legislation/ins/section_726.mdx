# INS § 726

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Certificate of Authority

**Citation:** INS § 726

---

## Text

The commissioner shall notify the Secretary of State of any refusal to issue a certificate of authority to transact insurance to an applicant therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 737.)
