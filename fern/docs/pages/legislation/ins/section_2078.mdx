# INS § 2078

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 3. California Standard Form Fire Insurance Policy

**Citation:** INS § 2078

---

## Text

There may be added to the standard form, clauses providing for and defining the rights, duties and obligations of mortgagees, assignees and other parties having or acquiring an interest in, right to, or lien upon the insured subject matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
