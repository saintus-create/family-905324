# INS § 1861.07

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 9. Rates and Rating and Other Organizations > Article: 10. [Reduction and Control of Insurance Rates]

**Citation:** INS § 1861.07

---

## Text

All information provided to the commissioner pursuant to this article shall be available for public inspection, and the provisions of Section 7929.000 of the Government Code and Section 1857.9 of the Insurance Code shall not apply thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 615, Sec. 306. (AB 474) Effective January 1, 2022. Operative January 1, 2023, pursuant to Sec. 463 of Stats. 2021, Ch. 615.)
