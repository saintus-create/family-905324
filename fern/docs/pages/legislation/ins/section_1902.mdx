# INS § 1902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Marine Contract > Article: 2. Concealment and Representations—Rules Peculiar to Marine Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 1902

---

## Text

A person insured by a contract of marine insurance is presumed to have, at the time of insuring, knowledge of a prior loss, if the information might possibly have reached him in the usual mode of transmission and at the usual rate of communication.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
