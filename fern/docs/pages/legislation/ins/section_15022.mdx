# INS § 15022

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. INSURANCE ADJUSTERS > Chapter: 2. Public Insurance Adjusters Act > Article: 3. Regulation, Licensing, and Registration

**Citation:** INS § 15022

---

## Text

Upon the issuance of a license, a pocket card of that size, design, and content as may be determined by the commissioner shall be issued without charge to each licensee.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1202, Sec. 1.)
