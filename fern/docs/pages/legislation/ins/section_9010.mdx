# INS § 9010

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 7. Cancellation of Membership

**Citation:** INS § 9010

---

## Text

Any member of such reinsurer may cancel any policy of reinsurance at any time while the reinsurer continues its reinsurance business.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
