# INS § 11629.88

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.5. California Low-Cost Automobile Insurance Program

**Citation:** INS § 11629.88

---

## Text

An applicant shall not be required to provide a copy of the applicant’s or other household member’s driver’s license, if the Department of Insurance finds that there is another suitable means of verifying the driver’s licensure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 401, Sec. 3. (AB 1024) Effective January 1, 2012.)
