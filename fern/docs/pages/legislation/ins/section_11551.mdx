# INS § 11551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 1. Reserves of Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11551

---

## Text

As used in this article, the term “compensation” means workers’ compensation insurance.
