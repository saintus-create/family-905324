# INS § 1215.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 4.7. Insurance Holding Company System Regulatory Act

**Citation:** INS § 1215.9

---

## Text

The commissioner may, upon notice and opportunity for all interested persons to be heard, issue such rules, regulations, and orders as shall be necessary to carry out the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 1215.8 by Stats. 2012, Ch. 282, Sec. 9. (SB 1448) Effective January 1, 2013.)
