# INS § 554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 6. Loss > Article: 3. Notice and Proofs of Loss

**Citation:** INS § 554

---

## Text

Delay in the presentation to an insurer of notice or proof of loss is waived, if caused by an act of his, or if he omits to make objection promptly and specifically upon that ground.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
