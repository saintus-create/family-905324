# INS § 11844

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. The State Compensation Insurance Fund > Article: 3. Policies

**Citation:** INS § 11844

---

## Text

Such policies covering employers shall insure to such employers and working members of their families the same compensations provided for their employees, and at the same rates.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
