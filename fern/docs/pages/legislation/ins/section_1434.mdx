# INS § 1434

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 8. Reports and Examination

**Citation:** INS § 1434

---

## Text

Where the principal office of the attorney is located in another State, the commissioner, in lieu of examination may accept a certified copy of the report of examination made by the insurance authority of another State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
