# INS § 10509.911

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 9. Suitability Requirements for Annuity Transactions before January 1, 2025

**Citation:** INS § 10509.911

---

## Text

(a) This article shall apply to any recommendation to purchase, exchange, or replace an annuity made to a consumer that results in the purchase, exchange, or replacement that was recommended.

(b) Nothing in this act shall be interpreted to preclude, preempt, or otherwise interfere with the application of any other laws of this state that may apply in any matter involving the sale of an annuity that is subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 295, Sec. 2. (AB 689) Effective January 1, 2012.)
