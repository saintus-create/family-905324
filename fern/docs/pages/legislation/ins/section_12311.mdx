# INS § 12311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 5. Penalties

**Citation:** INS § 12311

---

## Text

Any person violating any provisions of this part is guilty of a misdemeanor.
