# INS § 10480

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 3. Valuation of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10480

---

## Text

On or before the first day of March of each year every domestic incorporated life insurer shall furnish the commissioner the necessary data for determining the valuation of all its policies outstanding on the last preceding thirty-first of December.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1005, Sec. 3.)
