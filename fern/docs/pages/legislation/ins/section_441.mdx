# INS § 441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 4. Warranties

**Citation:** INS § 441

---

## Text

A statement in a policy of a matter relating to the person or thing insured, or to the risk, as a fact, is an express warranty thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
