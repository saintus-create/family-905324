# INS § 685.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 1.5. Retaliation

**Citation:** INS § 685.4

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall also be applicable to reciprocals or interinsurance exchanges and fraternal benefit societies.

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 2120.)
