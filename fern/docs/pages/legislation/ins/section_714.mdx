# INS § 714

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Certificate of Authority

**Citation:** INS § 714

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require the payment of seventy-two dollars ($72), in advance, as a fee for filing papers required under Section 713, on account of change or changes made at one time.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2017, Ch. 534, Sec. 7. (AB 1699) Effective January 1, 2018.)
