# INS § 12352

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 2. Title Insurers; Capital and Guarantee Fund Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12352

---

## Text

If the deposit is made in this state, it shall first be approved by the commissioner who shall make a special deposit thereof in the State Treasury, for the purpose specified in Section 12350. The Treasurer shall give his or her receipt therefor, to the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2011, Ch. 426, Sec. 12. (SB 712) Effective January 1, 2012.)
