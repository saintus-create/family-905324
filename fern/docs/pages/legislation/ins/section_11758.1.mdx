# INS § 11758.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Regulation of Business of Workers’ Compensation Insurance > Article: 3. Rating and Other Organizations

**Citation:** INS § 11758.1

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to the workers’ compensation insurance covering those persons defined as employees by subdivision (d) of Section 3351 of the Labor Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 17.)
