# INS § 4052

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 5. Dividends to Policyholders

**Citation:** INS § 4052

---

## Text

The payment of any such dividends on a workers’ compensation policy shall be subject to the provisions of Section 11738 and for that purpose a mutual policy shall be considered to be a participating policy.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 231, Sec. 13. (AB 2045) Effective January 1, 2019.)
