# INS § 10021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Underwriters Fire Patrols > Article: 1. Scope of Chapter and Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 10021

---

## Text

This chapter shall not impair or interfere with the powers or duties of the regular fire department of any municipality.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
