# INS § 1321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 3. Filing of Documents

**Citation:** INS § 1321

---

## Text

If the name of the exchange does not contain either the words “interinsurance,” “reciprocal” or “exchange,” then such forms shall have printed under such name the words “an interinsurance exchange” in a prominent place on each of such forms.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
