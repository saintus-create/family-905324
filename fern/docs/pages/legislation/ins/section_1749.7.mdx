# INS § 1749.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 13.5. Prelicensing and Continuing Education

**Citation:** INS § 1749.7

---

## Text

The commissioner may, pursuant to Chapter 3.5 (commencing with Section 11340) of Part 1 of Division 3 of Title 2 of the Government Code, adopt reasonable rules and regulations necessary for the convenient administration of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1420, Sec. 65. Operative January 1, 1992, by Sec. 77 of Ch. 1420.)
