# INS § 13505

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 7. Suspension of Privilege of Appearing or Practicing Before the Commissioner

**Citation:** INS § 13505

---

## Text

All hearings held under this article shall be closed to the public, unless the commissioner on the commissioner’s own motion, or at the request of a party, otherwise directs.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 260. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
