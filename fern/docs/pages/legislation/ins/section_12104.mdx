# INS § 12104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 4. MISCELLANEOUS CASUALTY INSURANCES > Chapter: 1. Surety Insurers on Reserve Basis; Capital Requirements and Permitted Insurances > Article: 5. Financial Guaranty Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 12104

---

## Text

An admitted financial guaranty insurance corporation shall be subject to all of the provisions of this code applicable to property and casualty insurers to the extent that the provisions are not inconsistent with the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1032, Sec. 6.)
