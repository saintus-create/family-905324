# INS § 1067.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 14.7. California Life and Health Insurance Guarantee Association

**Citation:** INS § 1067.14

---

## Text

The association shall be exempt from payment of all fees and all taxes levied by this state or any of its subdivisions, except taxes levied on real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 974, Sec. 1.7. Effective January 1, 1994.)
