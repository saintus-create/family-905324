# INS § 10225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 2.5. Blanket Life Insurance

**Citation:** INS § 10225

---

## Text

A policy of blanket insurance shall not be delivered or issued for delivery in this State until a copy of the form of the policy is filed with and approved by the commissioner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 367.)
