# INS § 10491

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 4. Qualifications and Exemptions

**Citation:** INS § 10491

---

## Text

Persons possessing a valid and unrevoked certificate of authority to transact life or disability or life and disability insurance issued by the commissioner are not subject to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1941, Ch. 1060.)
