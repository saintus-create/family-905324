# INS § 7017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 8. Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 7017

---

## Text

The insurer may bring an action against any member who neglects or refuses to pay an assessment made upon him under the provisions of this chapter, or to pay other liabilities due the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
