# INS § 4097.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 10. Conversion of an Incorporated Medical Malpractice  Mutual Insurer into an Incorporated Stock Insurer

**Citation:** INS § 4097.18

---

## Text

Upon completion of the act of conversion and issuance of the certificate of authority under Section 4097.11, the Secretary of State shall accept for filing a verified copy of the amended articles of incorporation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 421, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
