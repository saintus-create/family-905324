# INS § 1550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 16. Merger of Certain Reciprocal Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 1550

---

## Text

This article shall apply only to domestic reciprocal insurers organized after 1974 to provide medical malpractice insurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 728, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
