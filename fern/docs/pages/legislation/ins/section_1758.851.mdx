# INS § 1758.851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. Production Agencies > Article: 16.6. Car Rental Agents

**Citation:** INS § 1758.851

---

## Text

The insurance products listed in Section 1758.85 that are sold in conjunction with a vehicle rental are not transferable and do not apply to any vehicle not listed in the rental contract issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 618, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2000.)
