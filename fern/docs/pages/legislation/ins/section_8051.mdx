# INS § 8051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. County Mutual Fire Reinsurers > Article: 4. Risks

**Citation:** INS § 8051

---

## Text

All such reinsurers shall classify the reinsured subject matter at the time of issuing policies thereon, under rates corresponding as nearly as practicable to the several kinds of subject matter.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
