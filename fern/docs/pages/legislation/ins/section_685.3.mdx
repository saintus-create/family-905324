# INS § 685.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 1.5. Retaliation

**Citation:** INS § 685.3

---

## Text

To the extent permitted by the California Constitution there is hereby imposed upon the commissioner the duty to enforce Section 685. It shall be the duty of the commissioner to initiate the enforcement and execution of Section 685 in respect to all matters which by the California Constitution or laws of this state require further or final action by other officials of this state. In respect to any matter initiated by the commissioner, it shall be the duty of the Board of Equalization and the Controller to complete the enforcement and execution of Section 685 in respect to that matter within the scope of the duties and powers of each as set forth in the California Constitution and laws of this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 320, Sec. 29. Effective January 1, 1997.)
