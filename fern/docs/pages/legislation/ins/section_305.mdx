# INS § 305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 5. Insurable Interest: Effect of Transfer

**Citation:** INS § 305

---

## Text

The mere transfer of subject matter insured does not transfer the insurance, but suspends it until the same person becomes the owner of both the insurance and the subject matter insured.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
