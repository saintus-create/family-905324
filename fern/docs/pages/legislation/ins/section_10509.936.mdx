# INS § 10509.936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 11. Life Insurance Proceeds Disclosure Act of 2011

**Citation:** INS § 10509.936

---

## Text

If the life insurance benefits are placed in a retained asset account, the insurer shall send the beneficiary at least one statement per quarter, and a statement for any month in which there has been account activity other than just the crediting of interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2011, Ch. 130, Sec. 1. (SB 713) Effective January 1, 2012.)
