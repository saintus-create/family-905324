# INS § 12693.13

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12693.13

---

## Text

“Purchasing credit member” means an applicant 18 years of age or a child who is eligible for and participates in the purchasing credit component of the program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
