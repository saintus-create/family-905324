# INS § 10190

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. The Contract > Article: 5. Exemptions and Miscellaneous Provisions Relating to Insurance Contracts

**Citation:** INS § 10190

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 800, 801, 802, 803, and 804 shall not apply to life insurance.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 676, Sec. 189.)
