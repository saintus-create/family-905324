# INS § 1303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 3. Reciprocal Insurers > Article: 2. Organization of Exchange

**Citation:** INS § 1303

---

## Text

The organization under which such subscribers so exchange contracts is termed a reciprocal or interinsurance exchange, and shall be deemed the insurer while each subscriber shall be deemed an insured.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1649.)
