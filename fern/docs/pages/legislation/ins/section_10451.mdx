# INS § 10451

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 2. Registration of Life Policies

**Citation:** INS § 10451

---

## Text

When an insurer elects to register any of its policies, it shall register every policy thereafter issued by it until it discontinues registration.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
