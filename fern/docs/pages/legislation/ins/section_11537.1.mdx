# INS § 11537.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 14. Conversion of an Incorporated Mutual Life or Life and Disability Insurer into an Incorporated Stock Life Insurer Issuing Policies on a Reserve Basis > Article: 1. Authority and Requirements to Convert

**Citation:** INS § 11537.1

---

## Text

For the conversion of a mutual life insurer, the plan of conversion shall provide for either a mutual holding company in compliance with Section 11537.2 or for consideration in compliance with Section 11537.3. For the conversion of a mutual holding company, the plan of conversion shall provide for consideration in compliance with Section 11537.3.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 406, Sec. 6. Effective August 19, 1996.)
