# INS § 11601

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 3. LIABILITY, WORKERS' COMPENSATION, AND COMMON CARRIER LIABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 3. Capital Requirements of Reserve Basis Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 11601

---

## Text

Except as restricted by its charter, such an incorporated insurer, having such a paid-in capital, may transact all three of such insurances.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
