# INS § 12401.10

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6. INSURANCE COVERING LAND > Chapter: 1. Title Insurance > Article: 5.5. Rate Filing and Regulation

**Citation:** INS § 12401.10

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall require the filing of rates by title insurers for reinsurance contracts or agreements or policies of excess coinsurance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1130.)
