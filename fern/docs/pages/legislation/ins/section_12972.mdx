# INS § 12972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 2. Powers and Duties > Article: 4. Fees

**Citation:** INS § 12972

---

## Text

The commissioner shall require the payment of one dollar ($1), in advance, as a fee for attaching the commissioner’s seal of office to any paper or document not specified in this code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2021, Ch. 50, Sec. 257. (AB 378) Effective January 1, 2022.)
