# INS § 4097.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. General Mutual Insurers > Article: 10. Conversion of an Incorporated Medical Malpractice  Mutual Insurer into an Incorporated Stock Insurer

**Citation:** INS § 4097.16

---

## Text

The offer or sale of securities, including any debt securities, issued pursuant to the plan of conversion developed and approved in accordance with the provisions of this article, shall be exempt from Article 8 (commencing with Section 820) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 421, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1999.)
