# INS § 10275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 4. Standard Provisions in Disability Policies > Article: 1. Scope of Chapter and Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 10275

---

## Text

The term “policy of accident and sickness insurance” as used in this chapter includes any policy or contract covering the kind or kinds of insurance described in Section 106.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 570.)
