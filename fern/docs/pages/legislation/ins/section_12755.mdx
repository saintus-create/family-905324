# INS § 12755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 7. HOME PROTECTION > Chapter: 2. Fiscal Requirements

**Citation:** INS § 12755

---

## Text

A home protection company shall be deemed insolvent whenever its net worth is reduced below 50 percent of the amount required by Section 12750.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 820, Sec. 8.)
