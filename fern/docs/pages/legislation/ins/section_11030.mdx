# INS § 11030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 3. Organization

**Citation:** INS § 11030

---

## Text

Printed copies of the constitution or laws of any domestic or foreign society as amended, certified by the secretary or corresponding officer of the society shall be prima facie evidence of the legal adoption thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
