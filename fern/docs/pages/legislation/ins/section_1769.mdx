# INS § 1769

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 6. Surplus Line Brokers

**Citation:** INS § 1769

---

## Text

Whenever required so to do by the commissioner, such surplus line broker shall furnish to the commissioner a list of the admitted insurers from which the entire amount of insurance desired was not obtainable.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 980, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1995.)
