# INS § 12255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 5. MOTOR CLUBS > Chapter: 3. The Service Contract

**Citation:** INS § 12255

---

## Text

Any service contract made, issued or delivered contrary to any provision of this part shall nevertheless be valid and binding on the club.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
