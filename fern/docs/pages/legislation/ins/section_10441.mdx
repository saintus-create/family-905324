# INS § 10441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. General Regulation of Life Insurers > Article: 1a. Regulation of Internal Affairs of Mutual Insurers

**Citation:** INS § 10441

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 10440 shall not apply to any of the transactions, meetings, or votes described in Sections 1001, 1103, and 1900 of the Corporations Code.
