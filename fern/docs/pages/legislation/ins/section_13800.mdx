# INS § 13800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. THE INSURANCE COMMISSIONER > Chapter: 4. Holocaust Era Insurance Registry

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Holocaust Victim Insurance Relief Act of 1999.

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 827, Sec. 2. Effective October 10, 1999.)
