# INS § 10075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 8. Underwriters Fire Patrols > Article: 4. Meetings and Assessments

**Citation:** INS § 10075

---

## Text

Such assessment is collectible by and in the name of the corporation, in any court of competent jurisdiction, in such manner and at such time or times as the corporation determines.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
