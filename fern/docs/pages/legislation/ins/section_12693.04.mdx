# INS § 12693.04

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.2. HEALTHY FAMILIES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** INS § 12693.04

---

## Text

“Child” means a person who is under 19 years of age who is eligible for the program pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 12693.70).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1997, Ch. 623, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 1998.)
