# INS § 284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 2. Parties, Events, and Interests > Article: 4. Insurable Interest: Generally

**Citation:** INS § 284

---

## Text

Except in the case of a property held by the insured as a carrier or depositary, the measure of an insurable interest in property is the extent to which the insured might be damnified by loss or injury thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
