# INS § 1216.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. Incorporated Insurers > Article: 4.8. Business Transacted with Producer Controlled Insurer

**Citation:** INS § 1216.6

---

## Text

Controlled insurers and controlling producers who are not in compliance with Section 1216.3 of this act on its effective date shall have 60 days to do so and shall comply with Section 1216.4 beginning with all policies written or renewed on or after March 1, 1993.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 614, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 1993.)
