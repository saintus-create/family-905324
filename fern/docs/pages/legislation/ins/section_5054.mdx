# INS § 5054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 5. County Mutual Fire Insurers > Article: 1. Formation and Organization

**Citation:** INS § 5054

---

## Text

The articles of incorporation and certificate obtained by any county mutual fire insurer operating under the provisions of this chapter are subject to control and modification by the Legislature of this State.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
