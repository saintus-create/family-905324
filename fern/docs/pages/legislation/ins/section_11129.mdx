# INS § 11129

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 2. LIFE AND DISABILITY INSURANCE > Chapter: 10. Fraternal Benefit Societies > Article: 7. Financial Matters

**Citation:** INS § 11129

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 1596 to 1598, inclusive, shall apply to such society specified in Section 11127 and to the matters set forth in Sections 11127 and 11128.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1193.)
