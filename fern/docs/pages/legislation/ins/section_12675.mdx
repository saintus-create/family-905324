# INS § 12675

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 6.1. HEALTH DISABILITY INSURANCE CONVERSION

**Citation:** INS § 12675

---

## Text

The premium for the conversion coverage shall be determined in accordance with the insurer’s rates applicable to the age and class of risk of each person to be covered and to the type and amount of coverage provided.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 1096, Sec. 2. Operative January 1, 1983, by Sec. 5 of Ch. 1096.)
