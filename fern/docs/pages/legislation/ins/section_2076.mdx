# INS § 2076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. CLASSES OF INSURANCE > Part: 1. FIRE AND MARINE INSURANCE > Chapter: 2. The Fire Insurance Contract > Article: 3. California Standard Form Fire Insurance Policy

**Citation:** INS § 2076

---

## Text

The insurer may add to the standard form any matter relating to its financial condition, directors, officers, shareholders and history, and the address of its home office and principal office in the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
