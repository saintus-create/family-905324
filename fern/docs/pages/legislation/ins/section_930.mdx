# INS § 930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 10.5. National Association of Insurance Commissioners

**Citation:** INS § 930

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall apply to all domestic, foreign, and alien insurers doing business in this state.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 986, Sec. 1.)
