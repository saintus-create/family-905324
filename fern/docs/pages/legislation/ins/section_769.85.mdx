# INS § 769.85

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 5.4. Managing General Agents

**Citation:** INS § 769.85

---

## Text

The acts of the MGA are considered to be the acts of the insurer on whose behalf it is acting. An MGA may be examined as if it were the insurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 686, Sec. 1.)
