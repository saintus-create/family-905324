# INS § 974

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 12. Wildfire Safety and Risk Mitigation Program

**Citation:** INS § 974

---

## Text

The Wildfire Safety and Risk Mitigation Account is hereby created within the Insurance Fund. Funds in the account shall fund the development, demonstration, and deployment of public wildfire catastrophe modeling.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 541, Sec. 1. (SB 429) Effective January 1, 2026. Conditionally operative pursuant to Section 978.)
