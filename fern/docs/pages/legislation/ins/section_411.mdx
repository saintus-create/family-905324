# INS § 411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 4. The Policy > Article: 2. Types of Policies

**Citation:** INS § 411

---

## Text

An open policy is one in which the value of the subject matter is not agreed upon, but is left to be ascertained in case of loss.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1935, Ch. 145.)
