# INS § 572

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 1. THE CONTRACT > Chapter: 6. Loss > Article: 5. Certain Insurance Covering Real Property

**Citation:** INS § 572

---

## Text

As used in this article, the term “loss payee” shall include, but not be limited to, any mortgagee of the insured real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 281.)
