# INS § 881.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL RULES GOVERNING INSURANCE > Part: 2. THE BUSINESS OF INSURANCE > Chapter: 1. General Regulations > Article: 9. Registration of Insurers’ Names

**Citation:** INS § 881.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 5652 of the Financial Code, use of the term “savings bank” in a name or title may be approved for use by the commissioner if the remaining words in the name or title show that the insurer is engaged in the business of insurance and is not a savings bank.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2006, Ch. 740, Sec. 3.8. Effective January 1, 2007.)
