# WAT § 71302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71302

---

## Text

The board may fix the time and place or places at which its regular meetings will be held, and shall provide for the calling and holding of special meetings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
