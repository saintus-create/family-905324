# WAT § 24630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 2. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 24630

---

## Text

Coupons payable to bearer in a form and signed as prescribed by the board may be attached to warrants payable at a future date to evidence their interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
