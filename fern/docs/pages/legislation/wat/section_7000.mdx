**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 1. Joint Use of Wells, PumpingPlants and Conduits > Article: 1. Definitions

## Text

As used in this chapter “conduit” includes ditch, pipe line, and flume.
