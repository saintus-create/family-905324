# WAT § 11709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11709

---

## Text

The enumeration of rights and remedies in the next preceding section does not exclude the exercise or prosecution of any other rights or remedies by holders of bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
