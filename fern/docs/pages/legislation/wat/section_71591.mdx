# WAT § 71591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 71591

---

## Text

A district may:

(a) Have perpetual succession.

(b) Adopt a seal and alter it at pleasure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
