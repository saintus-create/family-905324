# WAT § 26650

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Procedure for Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 26650

---

## Text

Where the district has elected to transfer the duties of collector to the county tax collector, the delivery of duplicate assessment rolls shall not be required, but the county auditor shall furnish to the directors the statement of assessed values as provided in Section 26627.
