# WAT § 6152

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 5. Action and Procedure to Restrain Violations

**Citation:** WAT § 6152

---

## Text

Any action or proceeding under this article shall be commenced in the superior court in and for the county in which (a) the cause or some part thereof arose, (b) the owner or person complained of has its principal place of business, or (c) the person complained of resides.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
