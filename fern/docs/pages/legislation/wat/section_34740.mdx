# WAT § 34740

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 3. Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 34740

---

## Text

The bylaws shall fix the compensation to be paid to all officers. The compensation received by directors shall not exeed that specified in Section 34741.
