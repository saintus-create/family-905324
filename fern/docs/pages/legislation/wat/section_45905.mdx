# WAT § 45905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 1. Project and Government Contract Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 45905

---

## Text

Direct assessment warrants may be made payable to any of the following:

(a) Bearer.

(b) Persons furnishing work, labor or material.

(c) The contractor if the work on the project is to be done under contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
