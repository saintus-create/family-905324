# WAT § 75130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75130

---

## Text

At the hearing, any person interested, including persons owning property within the proposed special improvement district, may appear and present any matters material to the matters set forth in the resolution of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
