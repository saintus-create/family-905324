# WAT § 25803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 1. Regular Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25803

---

## Text

Limited assessments shall be computed and entered by the secretary and collected as a part of the annual assessment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
