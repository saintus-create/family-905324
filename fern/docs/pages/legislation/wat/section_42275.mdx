# WAT § 42275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 4. Filing the Report

**Citation:** WAT § 42275

---

## Text

Upon completion of the examination and study of the proposed project by the board, the board shall prepare and file a report with the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 51. Effective January 1, 1993.)
