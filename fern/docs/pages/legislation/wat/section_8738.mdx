# WAT § 8738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Hearings, Depositions and Notices

**Citation:** WAT § 8738

---

## Text

The deposition shall be taken before a notary public or other officer or person authorized by law or by this part to administer oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
