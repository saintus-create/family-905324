# WAT § 74521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 74521

---

## Text

A district may appropriate, acquire, and conserve water and water rights for any useful purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
