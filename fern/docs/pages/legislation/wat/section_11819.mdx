# WAT § 11819

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 3. Revenue Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 11819

---

## Text

All appropriations and contributions received from the United States, the State, any State agency, any political subdivision, or from any other source for the purpose of maintaining or operating the project, or borrowed therefrom for such purposes, shall be received by the State Treasurer and deposited in and credited to the revenue fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
