# WAT § 70031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 70031

---

## Text

The formation petition shall describe the boundaries of the proposed district and shall pray that it be incorporated into a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
