# WAT § 34850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. The Secretary

**Citation:** WAT § 34850

---

## Text

The secretary shall keep a record of all the proceedings had at meetings of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
