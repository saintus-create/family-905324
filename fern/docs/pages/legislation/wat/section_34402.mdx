# WAT § 34402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 1. Calling the Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34402

---

## Text

Notice of the election shall be published once a week for at least three weeks previous to the election in a newspaper of general circulation published in each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
