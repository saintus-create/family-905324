# WAT § 31579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 31579

---

## Text

All powers and duties of the district may be exercised on behalf of or within any improvement district formed pursuant to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 874.)
