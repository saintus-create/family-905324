# WAT § 48031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 2. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 48031

---

## Text

No exclusion of land from any district impairs its existence, its rights, including those in or to property, or its obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
