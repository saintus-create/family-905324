# WAT § 7003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 1. Joint Use of Wells, PumpingPlants and Conduits > Article: 3. Contribution of Expenses of Jointly Used Wells, Pumping Plants or Conduits

**Citation:** WAT § 7003

---

## Text

The action authorized by this article may be brought by any or all of the parties who have contributed more than his or their just proportion of the expenses, and the plaintiff may recover, as costs, reasonable counsel fees to be fixed by the court.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
