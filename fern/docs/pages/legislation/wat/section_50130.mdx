# WAT § 50130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 3. Public Lands

**Citation:** WAT § 50130

---

## Text

No credit shall be given, or agreed to be given to any landowner who has previously received such credit.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
