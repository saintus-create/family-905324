# WAT § 23865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Further Assessments and Inclusion > Article: 2. Assessment for New Purposes

**Citation:** WAT § 23865

---

## Text

Whenever it is desired to do additional work or acquire additional property in or for an improvement district, upon the petition of two-thirds in number of the holders of title to the land in the improvement district an additional assessment may be levied substantially in the same manner as the original assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
