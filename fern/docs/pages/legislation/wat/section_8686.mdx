# WAT § 8686

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8686

---

## Text

The plans and specifications for the work shall be prepared and approved by the board and delivered to the department with a request that the work be done by the department.
