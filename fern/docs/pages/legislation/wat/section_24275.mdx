# WAT § 24275

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Settlements and Statements

**Citation:** WAT § 24275

---

## Text

Notification that the annual financial statement is available for inspection and review shall be made at least once a week for two weeks in a newspaper published in the county in which the principal office of the district is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 771, Sec. 6.5.)
