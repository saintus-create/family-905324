# WAT § 53093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 5. Warrants Drawn on Irrigation Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 53093

---

## Text

All the provisions of Article 2 of this chapter and Chapter 2, Article 2 of this part relating to the payment or renewal of warrants apply to a warrant drawn on the irrigation fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
