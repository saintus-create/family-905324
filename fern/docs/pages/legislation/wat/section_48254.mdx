# WAT § 48254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 3. Inclusion Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 48254

---

## Text

The notice of election shall state that the election is for the purpose of determining whether certain land, describing its boundaries, shall or shall not be included in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
