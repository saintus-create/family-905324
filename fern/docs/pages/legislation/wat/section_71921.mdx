# WAT § 71921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation of Uninhabited Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 1. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 71921

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter the portion of a district formed into an uninhabited improvement district shall be deemed uninhabited if less than 12 voters reside therein at the time of the formation thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
