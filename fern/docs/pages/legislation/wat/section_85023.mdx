# WAT § 85023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Chapter: 2. Delta Policy

**Citation:** WAT § 85023

---

## Text

The longstanding constitutional principle of reasonable use and the public trust doctrine shall be the foundation of state water management policy and are particularly important and applicable to the Delta.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 39. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
