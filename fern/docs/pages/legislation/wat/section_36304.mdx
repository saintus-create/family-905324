# WAT § 36304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 1. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 36304

---

## Text

Revenue bonds issued subject to call and redemption prior to maturity shall contain a recital to that effect on the face of the revenue bonds, and no revenue bond shall be subject to call or redemption prior to its fixed maturity date unless it contains such recital.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
