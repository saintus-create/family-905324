# WAT § 71308

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71308

---

## Text

The board may by ordinance delegate and redelegate to the officers of the district the power to employ clerical, legal, and engineering assistants and labor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
