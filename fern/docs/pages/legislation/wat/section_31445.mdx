# WAT § 31445

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 5. Refunding

**Citation:** WAT § 31445

---

## Text

The face value of refunding bonds exchanged for original bonds shall not exceed the face value of the original bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
