# WAT § 47806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 12. Cancellation and Refund of Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 47806

---

## Text

When a resolution to cancel is adopted, a copy thereof, certified by the secretary, shall be attached to the assessment roll by the county treasurer of each county in which the assessment roll is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 128. Effective January 1, 1993.)
