# WAT § 74017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 74017

---

## Text

“President” means the president of the board of directors of a district and “secretary” means the secretary of the board of directors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
