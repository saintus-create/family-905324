# WAT § 36340

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36340

---

## Text

Revenue bonds and interest coupons attached thereto shall be in such form as may be prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
