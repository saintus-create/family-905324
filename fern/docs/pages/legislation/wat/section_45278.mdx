# WAT § 45278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Bond Election > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 45278

---

## Text

Except as in this part specifically provided, the provisions of this division with reference to all matters pertaining to elections shall govern bond elections.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
