# WAT § 11732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 4. Bond Form and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 11732

---

## Text

It is not necessary that all bonds of the same authorized issue bear the same interest rate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
