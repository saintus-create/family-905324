# WAT § 8957

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8957

---

## Text

Drafts of the board may be presented to the Controller and warrants drawn against the funds to be raised by an assessment as soon as the board has passed its order or resolution for the levy of the assessment and appointed the assessors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
