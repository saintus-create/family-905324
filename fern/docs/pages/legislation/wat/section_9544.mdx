# WAT § 9544

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 1. Notice and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 9544

---

## Text

The county treasurer may if directed by the board postpone the delinquent sale from time to time for not less than 10 nor more than 30 days by a written notice posted at the place of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
