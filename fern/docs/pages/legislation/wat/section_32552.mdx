# WAT § 32552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Inclusion in Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 32552

---

## Text

If all the owners of land within the territory proposed to be annexed have given their written assent to such annexation, the board may by resolution order such an annexation (a) without notice and hearing by the board, (b) without an election, or (c) without notice and hearing by the board and without an election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 41.)
