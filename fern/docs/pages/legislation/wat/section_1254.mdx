# WAT § 1254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 1254

---

## Text

In acting upon applications to appropriate water the board shall be guided by the policy that domestic use is the highest use and irrigation is the next highest use of water.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
