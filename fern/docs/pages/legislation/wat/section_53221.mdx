# WAT § 53221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cancellation and Reissuance > Article: 2. Mandatory

**Citation:** WAT § 53221

---

## Text

Warrants issued for interest pursuant to this article shall not bear interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
