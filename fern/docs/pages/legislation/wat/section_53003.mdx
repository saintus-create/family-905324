# WAT § 53003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 53003

---

## Text

Time warrants are warrants, based upon an operation and maintenance assessment, payable at a specified future date expressed in the warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
