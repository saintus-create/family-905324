# WAT § 8862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8862

---

## Text

When the installment is delinquent, the board, when it deems it advisable, shall publish in each county where the delinquency exists, a list in one notice of all delinquencies once a week for two weeks successively in some newspaper of general circulation published in the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
