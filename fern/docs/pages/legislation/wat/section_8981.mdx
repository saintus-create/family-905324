# WAT § 8981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8981

---

## Text

The warrants shall, unless sold or exchanged, be held by the Department of Finance until paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
