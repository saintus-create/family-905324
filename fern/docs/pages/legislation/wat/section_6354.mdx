# WAT § 6354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 1. New or Enlarged Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6354

---

## Text

As soon as practicable the completed dam or reservoir shall be inspected by the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
