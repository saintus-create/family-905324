# WAT § 9336

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9336

---

## Text

The authorization shall be in writing and certified to by the secretary or clerk of the district or public agency and attested by its seal duly acknowledged and filed with the board of election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
