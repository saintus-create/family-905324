# WAT § 11653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Contracts > Article: 2. Contracts with State Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 11653

---

## Text

All officers of any State agency charged with the collection of the taxes or assessments levied by the State agency shall enforce and collect all taxes or assessments levied or assessed for the purpose of providing payment of the money due or to become due under a contract as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
