# WAT § 35881

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 35881

---

## Text

A proposal to enter into a contract with the United States for any purpose other than obtaining a water supply shall be authorized at an election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
