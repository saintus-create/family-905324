# WAT § 11625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Contracts > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11625

---

## Text

Any state agency, mutual water company, political subdivision, or other entity or organization may enter into contracts with the department for the purchase or for the use of water, water flow, water storage, electric power, or other resources and facilities made available by the project.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
