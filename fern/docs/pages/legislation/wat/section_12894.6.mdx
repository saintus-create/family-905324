# WAT § 12894.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5.5. Local Agency Bond Payments

**Citation:** WAT § 12894.6

---

## Text

In making loans pursuant to Section 12894.3, the department shall impose such terms and conditions as are necessary to protect the state’s investment and carry out the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1078.)
