# WAT § 51302

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 2. Additional Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51302

---

## Text

An assessment levied pursuant to this article shall be levied and collected in the same manner as the original assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
