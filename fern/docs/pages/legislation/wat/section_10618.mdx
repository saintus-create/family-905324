# WAT § 10618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 10618

---

## Text

“Water supply and demand assessment” means a method that looks at current year and one or more dry year supplies and demands for determining water shortage risks, as described in Section 10632.1.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2018, Ch. 14, Sec. 23. (SB 606) Effective January 1, 2019.)
