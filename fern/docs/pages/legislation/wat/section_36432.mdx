# WAT § 36432

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.9. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 36432

---

## Text

As an alternative procedure for the annexation of land to an improvement district, additional lands may be included within any improvement district by resolution of the board declaring the territory annexed after receipt by the board of written consent of the owners of all of the land to be annexed thereto which consent shall include the terms and conditions of annexation, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 357.)
