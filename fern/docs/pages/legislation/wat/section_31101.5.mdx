# WAT § 31101.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 6. Sewer Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 31101.5

---

## Text

A district may supply sewage and waste services to property not subject to district taxes at special rates, terms, and conditions as are determined by the board for the services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 254, Sec. 1.)
