# WAT § 23606

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23606

---

## Text

A petition to form an improvement district shall be filed with the secretary in the office of the district and may be inspected by all persons interested.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
