# WAT § 26676

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Collection, Payment in Lieu of Assessment and Distribution of Water by Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26676

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided herein the contract between the district and the public agency may include such terms and conditions as may be agreed upon between the district and the public agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 603.)
