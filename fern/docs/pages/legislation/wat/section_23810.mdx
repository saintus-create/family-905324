# WAT § 23810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2A. Revenue Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23810

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 23811, the improvement district warrants shall be made payable over not to exceed a period of 10 years and shall specify a rate of interest fixed at the time of their issuance not exceeding 8 percent per year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
