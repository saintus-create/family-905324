# WAT § 12529

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Commissioner

**Citation:** WAT § 12529

---

## Text

The commissioner shall exercise on behalf of the State every right and power granted to the State or to any representative of it by Section 16 of the act of Congress designated the “Boulder Canyon Project Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
