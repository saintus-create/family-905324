# WAT § 75165.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 6. Alternative Provisions for Government of Special Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 75165.7

---

## Text

The four persons receiving, respectively, the highest number of votes for trustee in each division, plus the one person receiving the highest number of votes for trustee at large, shall be declared to be duly elected to such offices.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1651.)
