# WAT § 10611.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 10611.5

---

## Text

“Demand management” means those water conservation measures, programs, and incentives that prevent the waste of water and promote the reasonable and efficient use and reuse of available supplies.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1995, Ch. 854, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
