# WAT § 20721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 2. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 20721

---

## Text

A petition for the formation of a district may request any of the following:

(a) Three divisions in the proposed district.

(b) Election of the directors of the proposed district by the district at large.

(c) Consolidation of any offices allowed to be consolidated.
