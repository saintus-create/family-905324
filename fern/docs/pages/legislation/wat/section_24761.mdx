# WAT § 24761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Applicable to Bonds and Warrants > Article: 2. Modification of Bonds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24761

---

## Text

The rate of interest specified in the agreement or plan shall not exceed 8 percent per year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
