# WAT § 72707

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 72707

---

## Text

If the board finds and determines that either the area proposed to be annexed to the improvement district will not be benefited thereby or that the improvement district to which the area is proposed to be annexed will not be benefited thereby and will be injured thereby, the board shall by resolution disapprove such annexation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
