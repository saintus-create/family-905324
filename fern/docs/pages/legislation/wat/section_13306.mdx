# WAT § 13306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 5. Enforcement and Implementation > Article: 1. Administrative Enforcement and Remedies

**Citation:** WAT § 13306

---

## Text

A majority vote of the entire membership of a regional board shall be required to adopt, rescind, or modify any enforcement action authorized by Section 13301.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1165.)
