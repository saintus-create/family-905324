# WAT § 25332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 6. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 25332

---

## Text

In case no award is made, the board thereafter may either readvertise the bonds or any part of them for sale or sell them at private sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
