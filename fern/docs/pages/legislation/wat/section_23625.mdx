# WAT § 23625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 2. Plans and Estimate of Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23625

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a petition to form an improvement district the board shall cause a survey to be made of the proposed improvements, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
