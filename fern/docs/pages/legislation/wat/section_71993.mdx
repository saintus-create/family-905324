# WAT § 71993

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Formation of Improvement Districts for Issuance of Revenue Bonds > Article: 4. Calling of Revenue Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71993

---

## Text

If the issuance of the revenue bonds is authorized at the election, the board shall provide for their issuance, sale, authentication, form and redemption in the manner provided in the Revenue Bond Law of 1941.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 229.)
