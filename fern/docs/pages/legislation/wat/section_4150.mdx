# WAT § 4150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Distribution of Water in Service Areas > Article: 1. Procedure of Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 4150

---

## Text

The department may make such reasonable regulations to secure distribution of water in accordance with determined rights as may be needed.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
