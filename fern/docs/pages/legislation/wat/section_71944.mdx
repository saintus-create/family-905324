# WAT § 71944

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71944

---

## Text

The secretary, as soon as the result of the bond election is declared, shall enter in the records of the board a statement of such results.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
