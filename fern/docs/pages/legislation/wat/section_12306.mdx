# WAT § 12306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1.5. Environmental Mitigation and Protection Requirements

**Citation:** WAT § 12306

---

## Text

This chapter applies to special flood control projects subject to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 12310) and to the payment of delta levee subventions under Part 9 (commencing with Section 12980).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1140, Sec. 1.)
