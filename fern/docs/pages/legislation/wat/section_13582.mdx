# WAT § 13582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7.5. Water Recycling Act of 1991

**Citation:** WAT § 13582

---

## Text

This chapter is not intended to alter either of the following:

(a) Any rights, remedies, or obligations which may exist pursuant to Article 1.5 (commencing with Section 1210) of Chapter 1 of Part 2 of Division 2 of this code or Chapter 8.5 (commencing with Section 1501) of Part 1 of Division 1 of the Public Utilities Code.

(b) Any rates established or contracts entered into prior to January 1, 1999.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1998, Ch. 753, Sec. 9. Effective January 1, 1999.)
