# WAT § 37853

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 37853

---

## Text

All actions and proceedings commenced after the merger, either on account of the affairs of the water storage district or affairs of the California water district, shall be prosecuted by or against the surviving district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
