# WAT § 21556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Divisions and Methods of Electing Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 21556

---

## Text

Each proposal shall be stated on the ballot, and the method of voting on it shall be as nearly as practicable in conformity with the provisions relating to bond elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
