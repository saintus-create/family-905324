# WAT § 8964

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8964

---

## Text

The notice shall be published for 10 days in one newspaper published in the City of Stockton and one published in the City of Sacramento.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
