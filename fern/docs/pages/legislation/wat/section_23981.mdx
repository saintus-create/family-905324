# WAT § 23981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 23981

---

## Text

Any surplus funds and any money held by a district in a sinking or depreciation fund may in the discretion of its board be invested in the warrants of any improvement district within the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1947, Ch. 536.)
