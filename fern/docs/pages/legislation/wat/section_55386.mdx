# WAT § 55386

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Use of the Improvement Act of 1911 > Article: 1. Authorization to Use Improvement Act of 1911

**Citation:** WAT § 55386

---

## Text

The district shall be formed, the work or improvement done, and the cost thereof assessed and collected in accordance with the provisions of the Improvement Act of 1911.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 452.)
