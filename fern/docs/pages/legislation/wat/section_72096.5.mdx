# WAT § 72096.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Taxes Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 72096.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provisions of law, a mobilehome which occupies a rental space and is not permanently attached to the land is not property subject to ad valorem property taxes for debt service of the district or of any improvement district therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 109, Sec. 1.)
