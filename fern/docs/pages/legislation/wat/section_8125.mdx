# WAT § 8125

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Flood Control in Counties > Article: 4. Improvement of Non-Navigable Streams

**Citation:** WAT § 8125

---

## Text

“Non-navigable streams,” as used in this article, means streams and washes in a county which are not declared by law to be navigable and which are not in fact navigable for commercial purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
