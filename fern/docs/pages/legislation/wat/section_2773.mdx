# WAT § 2773

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2773

---

## Text

The decree is conclusive as to the rights of all existing claimants upon the stream system lawfully embraced in the determination.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
