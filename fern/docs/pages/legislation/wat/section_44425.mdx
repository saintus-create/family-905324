# WAT § 44425

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Statements and Reports

**Citation:** WAT § 44425

---

## Text

The county treasurer of each of the several counties shall report all transactions of delinquencies on district assessments and sales of land within the district to the treasurer of the district who shall keep a record thereof in the office of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
