# WAT § 20568.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 20568.1

---

## Text

No person may sign for the owner of property or the owner of an undivided interest therein without the authorization of the owner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1083.)
