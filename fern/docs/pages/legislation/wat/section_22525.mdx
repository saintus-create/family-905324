# WAT § 22525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 5. Lease of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 22525

---

## Text

This article provides an independent method of leasing and its provisions apply only to leases made pursuant to it.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
