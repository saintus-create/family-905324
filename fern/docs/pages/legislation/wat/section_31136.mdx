# WAT § 31136

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9. Sanitation Service

**Citation:** WAT § 31136

---

## Text

The district may prescribe, revise, and collect rates or other charges for the services furnished pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1981.)
