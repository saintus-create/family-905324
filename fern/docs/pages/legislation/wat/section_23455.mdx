# WAT § 23455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Cooperation With Other Districts > Article: 2. Cooperation with County Water Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23455

---

## Text

In any contract between districts they may contract to compromise and settle any controversies existing between them as to the extent or priority of their respective rights and claims to the use of water and agree upon the limitation and definition of the areas within them respectively upon which such water shall be beneficially used.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
