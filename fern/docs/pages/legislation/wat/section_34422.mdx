# WAT § 34422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34422

---

## Text

At the election the last equalized assessment roll of each affected county shall be used in lieu of the district assessment book.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
