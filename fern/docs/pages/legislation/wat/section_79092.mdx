# WAT § 79092

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 6. Watershed Protection Program > Article: 3. Water and Watershed Education Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79092

---

## Text

Three million dollars ($3,000,000) in the subaccount, upon appropriation by the Legislature to the department, may be used by the department for allocation to California State University, Fresno for the purposes of establishing and furthering the purposes of the California Water Institute at that campus.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 606, Sec. 13. Effective October 9, 2001. Note: This section was added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, and approved in Prop. 13 on March 7, 2000.)
