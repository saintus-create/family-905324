# WAT § 12894.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5.5. Local Agency Bond Payments

**Citation:** WAT § 12894.5

---

## Text

Annual payments on the loan, including both principal repayment and interest, shall be in substantially equal amounts over the term of the loan, excluding the initial deferment, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1078.)
