# WAT § 6500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Part: 2. FISHWAYS OVER DAMS > Chapter: 10. Dams and Reservoirs Under State Supervision Through 1965 Revisions of Part 1 > Article: 3. Fees for Dams or Reservoirs Under Construction Before 1965 Revisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6500

---

## Text

Whenever an application for approval of plans and specifications for a new dam, or for the enlargement of any dam, in any stream in this State, is filed pursuant to Part 1 of this division, a copy of the application shall be filed with the Fish and Game Commission as required by the Fish and Game Code.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
