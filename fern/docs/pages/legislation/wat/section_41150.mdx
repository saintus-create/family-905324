# WAT § 41150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redivision and Establishing of Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41150

---

## Text

The board shall establish a convenient number of election precincts in the district and define the boundaries thereof. At least one precinct must be established for each division of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
