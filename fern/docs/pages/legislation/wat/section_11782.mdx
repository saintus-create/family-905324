# WAT § 11782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 8. Retirement of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 11782

---

## Text

The proceedings authorizing the issuance of bonds may provide limitations and conditions upon the time and manner of applying such surplus to the purchase and call of outstanding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
