# WAT § 50932

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property

**Citation:** WAT § 50932

---

## Text

A district may construct, maintain and operate such drains, canals, sluices, bulkheads, water gates, levees, embankments, pumping plants, dams, diversion works, or irrigation works, and all things reasonably necessary or convenient for accomplishing the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
