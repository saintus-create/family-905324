# WAT § 22097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Drainage

**Citation:** WAT § 22097

---

## Text

The officers, agents, and employees of districts have the same powers, duties, and liabilities respecting drainage and construction, operation, and control relating to it as they have respecting irrigation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
