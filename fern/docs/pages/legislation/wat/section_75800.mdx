# WAT § 75800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 4. Recordation of Change of Boundaries and Filing of Certificate

**Citation:** WAT § 75800

---

## Text

If the board excludes any lands from the district, it shall make an entry in the minutes of the board describing the boundaries of the district, if the exclusion of the lands from the district changes the boundaries of the district, and for that purpose the board may cause a survey to be made of such portions of the district as the board deems necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
