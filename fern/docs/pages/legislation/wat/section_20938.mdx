# WAT § 20938

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 3. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20938

---

## Text

The appeal shall be heard and determined within 60 days from the time of filing the notice of appeal.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
