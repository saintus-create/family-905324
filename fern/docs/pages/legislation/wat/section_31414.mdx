# WAT § 31414

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 31414

---

## Text

No informalities in the conduct of the election shall invalidate the election if fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
