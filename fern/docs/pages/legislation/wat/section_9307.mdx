# WAT § 9307

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 9307

---

## Text

Each county treasurer shall indorse on the assessment list the time to the hour and minute when it was so filed in his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
