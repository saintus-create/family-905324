# WAT § 26256

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 2. Partial Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26256

---

## Text

The decision of the board shall be final, and the collector shall conform with it.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
