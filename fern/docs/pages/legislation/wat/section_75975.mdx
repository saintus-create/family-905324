# WAT § 75975

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Annexation of Districts > Article: 3. Procedure in Annexing District

**Citation:** WAT § 75975

---

## Text

Upon the annexation of a district as provided in this chapter, the annexation order and the annexation petition shall be recorded.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
