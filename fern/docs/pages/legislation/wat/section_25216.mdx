# WAT § 25216

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25216

---

## Text

Each bond shall be signed by the president and secretary then in office at any time between the date of the bond and its delivery to a purchaser from the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
