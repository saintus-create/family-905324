# WAT § 12929.30

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 4. Water Quality Program

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.30

---

## Text

The Water Quality Program includes projects or programs of public agencies throughout the state that will either (1) reduce the amounts of agricultural drainage water or (2), improve the quality of agricultural drainage water with the result of improving the quality of water used for agricultural, municipal, or industrial purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 715, Sec. 1.)
