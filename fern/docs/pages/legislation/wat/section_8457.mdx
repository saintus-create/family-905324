# WAT § 8457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Maintenance Costs of Vegetation on Levees

**Citation:** WAT § 8457

---

## Text

State expenditures for the purposes of this chapter shall not exceed the amount of two hundred thousand dollars ($200,000) per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 995.)
