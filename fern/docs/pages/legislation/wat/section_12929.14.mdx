# WAT § 12929.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 2. Environmental Water Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.14

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that nine million dollars ($9,000,000) per year be appropriated in the annual Budget Act for the 1994–95 to 1997–98 fiscal years, inclusive, to pay for programs and projects included in the environmental water program.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 626, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1995.)
