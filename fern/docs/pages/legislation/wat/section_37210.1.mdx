# WAT § 37210.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 7.5. ALTERNATIVE PROVISIONS FOR LEVY, COLLECTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF DISTRICT ASSESSMENTS BY THE COUNTY > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37210.1

---

## Text

In levying a standby charge, the board of any district which has elected, pursuant to Section 37203, to proceed under this part shall comply with the notice, protest, and hearing procedures in Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 27, Sec. 31. Effective January 1, 2008.)
