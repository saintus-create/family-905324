# WAT § 9385

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 4. Canvass and Contest

**Citation:** WAT § 9385

---

## Text

The polls at each polling place for the election shall be kept open from 9 o'clock in the forenoon until 5 o'clock in the afternoon of the day appointed for the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
