# WAT § 81046

**Hierarchy:** Division: 30. CALCONSERVE WATER USE EFFICIENCY REVOLVING LOAN PROGRAM > Part: 3. Implementation

**Citation:** WAT § 81046

---

## Text

A local agency may implement water use efficiency loan programs pursuant to this division through on-bill financing.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2015, Ch. 2, Sec. 10. (AB 92) Effective March 27, 2015.)
