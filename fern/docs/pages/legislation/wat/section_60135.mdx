# WAT § 60135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 60135

---

## Text

In the case of all districts (except as to the election of the first board) the elections of directors shall be held at the time of the general election and shall be consolidated therewith.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 7, Sec. 1.5. Effective February 8, 1984.)
