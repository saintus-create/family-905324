# WAT § 25260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 3. Allocation of Revenue to Bond Payment

**Citation:** WAT § 25260

---

## Text

Any designated revenue may by resolution of the board be allocated to the payment of the whole or any portion of the principal or interest or both of any bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
