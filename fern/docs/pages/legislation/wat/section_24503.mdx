# WAT § 24503

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Payment and Purchase of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24503

---

## Text

If the principal or interest of any bonds is payable from any special or sinking fund, it shall be payable from that fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
