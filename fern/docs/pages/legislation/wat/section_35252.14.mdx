# WAT § 35252.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.3. Method of Election for the Riverview Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35252.14

---

## Text

This chapter does not in any way invalidate any act of the district, its directors, or its officers and employees made or performed while the district was operating as a landowner voting district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 833, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
