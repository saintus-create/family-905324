# WAT § 50956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Personnel

**Citation:** WAT § 50956

---

## Text

The board, upon presentation of a proper claim, shall draw semiannually a warrant on the district funds for such clerk hire.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
