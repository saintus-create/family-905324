# WAT § 26255

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 2. Partial Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26255

---

## Text

The board may either:

(a) Confirm, modify, or set aside the valuation of the assessor.

(b) Refuse to authorize the separate redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
