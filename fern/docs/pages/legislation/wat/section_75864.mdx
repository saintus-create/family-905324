# WAT § 75864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 2. Petition and Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 75864

---

## Text

The petitioners shall advance to the secretary sufficient money to pay for the publication of the notice of the filing of the petition for the inclusion of lands, which shall be refunded to the petitioners if the petition is granted in whole or in part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
