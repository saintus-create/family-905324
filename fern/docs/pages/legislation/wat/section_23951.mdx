# WAT § 23951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 1. Improvement District Management

**Citation:** WAT § 23951

---

## Text

The board may also hold property used or acquired in connection with the improvement in the name of the directors and their successors in office as trustees for the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
