# WAT § 31301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31301

---

## Text

The district may issue warrants to pay its formation expenses bearing interest at 6 percent per year from date of issue until funds are available to pay the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
