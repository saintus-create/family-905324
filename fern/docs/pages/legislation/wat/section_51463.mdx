# WAT § 51463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 9. Warrant Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51463

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall prevent the board from making other arrangements with holders of district warrants.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
