# WAT § 24956

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Construction Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24956

---

## Text

The estimate shall include the estimated cost of inspection required by law of works in course of construction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
