# WAT § 25828

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 2. Special Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25828

---

## Text

The board shall set a time and place for the hearing of a petition for a special rate of assessment or of all the petitions if more than one has been filed and shall give notice of it in a newspaper published in the office county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
