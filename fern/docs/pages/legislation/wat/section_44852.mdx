# WAT § 44852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 4. Alternative Provisions for the Deposit of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 44852

---

## Text

Any money belonging to a district may be deposited by the treasurer pursuant to the general laws governing the deposit of public money.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 695.)
