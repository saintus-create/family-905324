# WAT § 36460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 36460

---

## Text

Land within a water district, which need not be contiguous, may be formed into a distribution district for the purpose of contracting with the United States, pursuant to federal reclamation laws, or for the purpose of contracting with the district for the construction of a distribution system separate from or supplemental to the works of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
