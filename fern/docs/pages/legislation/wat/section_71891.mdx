# WAT § 71891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Formation of Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 3. Formation

## Text

The determinations made in the resolution of formation shall be final and conclusive.
