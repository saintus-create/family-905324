# WAT § 1745.06

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 4. Water Supplier Contracts

**Citation:** WAT § 1745.06

---

## Text

A water supplier may transfer water pursuant to Section 1745.04 whether or not the water proposed to be transferred is surplus to the needs within the service area of the water supplier.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 481, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
