# WAT § 23752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Alternative Procedure for Formation > Article: 1. Proposal by Board

**Citation:** WAT § 23752

---

## Text

No petition is required for the formation of an improvement district pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
