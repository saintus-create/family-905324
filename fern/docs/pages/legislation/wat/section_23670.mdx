# WAT § 23670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 4. Formation and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 23670

---

## Text

Except as provided in Section 23670.1, the assessment may be made payable in not more than 10 annual installments.
