# WAT § 74131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74131

---

## Text

The directors elected at the formation election shall be made parties defendant in the election contest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
