# WAT § 74101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74101

---

## Text

The ballots shall contain instructions that the voters shall completely select the voting target next to the words that indicate the voter’s choice.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 65. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
