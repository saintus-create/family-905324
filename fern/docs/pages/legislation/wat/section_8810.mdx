# WAT § 8810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8810

---

## Text

The assessors shall make a separate list of the lands assessed in each county pursuant to the provisions of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
