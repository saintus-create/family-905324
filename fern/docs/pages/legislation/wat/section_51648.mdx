# WAT § 51648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51648

---

## Text

The deed shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of the matters therein recited, and of ownership by the grantee of the lands therein described.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
