# WAT § 2603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 5. Report and Preliminary Order of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2603

---

## Text

The report shall include a preliminary order of determination determining and establishing the several rights to the water of the stream system, giving appropriate consideration to claims at variance with the measurements and determinations of the representatives of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
