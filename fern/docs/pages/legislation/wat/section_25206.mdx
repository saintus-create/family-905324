# WAT § 25206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25206

---

## Text

The date of issue of any bond shall be deemed to be the date of the bond appearing on its face.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
