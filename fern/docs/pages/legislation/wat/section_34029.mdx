# WAT § 34029

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 34029

---

## Text

“Irrigation” includes subirrigation, percolation, underground storage, and well replenishment.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
