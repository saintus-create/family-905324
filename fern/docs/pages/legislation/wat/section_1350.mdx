# WAT § 1350

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Proceedings on Application > Article: 2. Action on Application

**Citation:** WAT § 1350

---

## Text

The board may grant, or refuse to grant a permit and may reject any application, after hearing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
