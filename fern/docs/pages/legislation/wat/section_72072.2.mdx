# WAT § 72072.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 6. Powers—Utility Tax

**Citation:** WAT § 72072.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law to the contrary, persons subject to the jurisdiction of the Public Utilities Commission need not obtain an authorization from the commission to comply with the provisions of this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 291.)
