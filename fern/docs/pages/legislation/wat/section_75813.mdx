# WAT § 75813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 5. Bondholders’ Assent to Exclusion

**Citation:** WAT § 75813

---

## Text

If the holders of outstanding bonds of the district which are payable from assessments upon real property give their assent, as provided in Section 75810, to the exclusion of lands from the district, the assent shall release all real property which is excluded from the district by virtue of the exclusion of such lands from the lien of such outstanding bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
