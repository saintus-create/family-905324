# WAT § 45554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 45554

---

## Text

Coupons for each installment of interest shall be attached to the bonds and shall bear the facsimile signature of the treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
