# WAT § 14917

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 23. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Act > Article: 2. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14917

---

## Text

In carrying out this chapter, the department shall consider the effects of purchases of property pursuant to this chapter on the overall economy of the local communities, including the impact on job opportunities and businesses.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 959, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993. Section operative July 1, 1993, pursuant to Section 14920.)
