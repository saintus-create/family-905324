# WAT § 6407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 6. Inspection During Progress of Work

**Citation:** WAT § 6407

---

## Text

A failure to comply with the approval and approved plans and specifications as originally approved or as revised shall render the approval subject to revocation by the department, if compliance is not made in accordance therewith after notice and order from the department as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
