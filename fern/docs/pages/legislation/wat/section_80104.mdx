# WAT § 80104

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. PURCHASE AND SALE OF ELECTRIC POWER > Chapter: 2. Power Program > Article: 1. Powers of the Department

**Citation:** WAT § 80104

---

## Text

Upon the delivery of power to them, the retail end use customers shall be deemed to have purchased that power from the department. Payment for any sale shall be a direct obligation of the retail end use customer to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 4, Sec. 4. Effective February 1, 2001.)
