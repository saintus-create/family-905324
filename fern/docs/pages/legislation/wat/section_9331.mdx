# WAT § 9331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9331

---

## Text

The unpaid amount shall be entered by the board in its records and stated by the board in its order for the election and the order for the election shall be entered upon the minutes of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
