# WAT § 6252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 3. Dams Constructed Prior to August 14, 1929

**Citation:** WAT § 6252

---

## Text

The department shall give notice to file to owners who have failed to do so as required by this article, and a failure to file within 30 days after such notice shall be punishable as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
