# WAT § 21930

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bond Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 21930

---

## Text

Bond election ballots shall include instructions to voters that are consistent with the requirements of Section 13204 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 40. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
