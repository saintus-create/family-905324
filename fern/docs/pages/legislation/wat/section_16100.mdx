# WAT § 16100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 27. California Watershed Improvement Act of 2009

**Citation:** WAT § 16100

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the California Watershed Improvement Act of 2009.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, Ch. 577, Sec. 1. (SB 310) Effective January 1, 2010.)
