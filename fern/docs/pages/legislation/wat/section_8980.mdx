# WAT § 8980

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8980

---

## Text

The warrants shall be payable with interest in their proper order of registration, as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
