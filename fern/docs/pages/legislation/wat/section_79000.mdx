# WAT § 79000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** WAT § 79000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Costa-Machado Water Act of 2000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
