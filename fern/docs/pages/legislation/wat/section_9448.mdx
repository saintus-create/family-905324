# WAT § 9448

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 4. Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 9448

---

## Text

In making payments in bonds, the board shall draw orders upon the State Treasurer payable in bonds to the amount therein named.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
