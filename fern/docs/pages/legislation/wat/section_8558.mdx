# WAT § 8558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8558

---

## Text

The regular meetings of the board shall be held at times fixed by the board.
