# WAT § 8841

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8841

---

## Text

The approved map shall be filed for record in the office of the county recorder of the county wherein the land indicated on the map is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
