# WAT § 70072

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. The Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 70072

## Text

Any vacancies in the offices of the directors shall be filled pursuant to Section 1780 of the Government Code.
