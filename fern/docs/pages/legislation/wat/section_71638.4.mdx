# WAT § 71638.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water > Article: 2.5. Alternative Provisions for Standby Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 71638.4

## Text

The levy and collection of such assessment or charge shall be made as provided in Sections 71634 through 71637, inclusive.
