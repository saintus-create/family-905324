# WAT § 53245

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cancellation and Reissuance > Article: 3. Lost, Destroyed, Mutilated or Defaced Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53245

---

## Text

The holder of a mutilated or defaced warrant shall, at the time of filing the application for a duplicate, deliver the mutilated or defaced warrant to the board for cancellation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
