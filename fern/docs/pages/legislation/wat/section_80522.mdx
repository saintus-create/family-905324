# WAT § 80522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND RECOVERY ACT OF 2019 > Chapter: 2. Powers of the Department

**Citation:** WAT § 80522

---

## Text

The commission may issue rules regulating the enforcement of the agency functions pursuant this division, including collection and payment to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 79, Sec. 22. (AB 1054) Effective July 12, 2019.)
