# WAT § 74227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 74227

---

## Text

A majority of the members of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
