# WAT § 23772

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Alternative Procedure for Formation > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23772

---

## Text

Owners of the property proposed to be included within the improvement district may be heard either in opposition to or in support of the formation of the improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
