# WAT § 8692

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8692

---

## Text

The board may furnish to the department any of its dredges, machines, appliances, tools, apparatus or other property which may be necessary or convenient for performing work delegated to the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
