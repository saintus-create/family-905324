# WAT § 74433

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Relocation of Division Boundaries

**Citation:** WAT § 74433

---

## Text

If land is excluded from a district pursuant to Chapter 1 (commencing with Section 75750) of Part 10 of this division, the board not less than 30 days before any election in the district, may reestablish the boundaries of the divisions and election precincts within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
