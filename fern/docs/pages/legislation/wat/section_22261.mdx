# WAT § 22261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 2. Water Distribution

**Citation:** WAT § 22261

---

## Text

Nothing in this article authorizes the sale of any water right.
