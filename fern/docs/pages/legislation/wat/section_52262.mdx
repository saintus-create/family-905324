# WAT § 52262

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 4. Exchange

**Citation:** WAT § 52262

---

## Text

Bonds deposited for exchange shall be canceled by the county treasurer and the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
