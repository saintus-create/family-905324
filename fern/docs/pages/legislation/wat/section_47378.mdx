# WAT § 47378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 8. Assessments Upon Which Bonds Have Been Issued > Article: 4. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 47378

---

## Text

No parcel sold and conveyed to the district shall thereafter be subject to sale by the county treasurer for delinquent installments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
