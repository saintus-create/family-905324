# WAT § 72145

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Chino Basin Production Assessments > Article: 6. Remedies

**Citation:** WAT § 72145

---

## Text

Any assessing district may bring a suit in the court having jurisdiction against any producer for the collection of any delinquent production assessments, interest, or penalties. The court having jurisdiction of the suit may, in addition award interest, costs, and attorney’s fees on any judgment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 165.)
