# WAT § 60153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 60153

---

## Text

A majority of the board shall constitute a quorum for the transaction of business.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
