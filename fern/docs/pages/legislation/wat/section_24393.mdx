# WAT § 24393

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Deposits > Article: 3. Special Accounts

**Citation:** WAT § 24393

---

## Text

No payment shall be made from any special account except upon the written order of a person designated for the purpose by the board, which order shall indicate the purpose for which and the person to whom payment is to be made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
