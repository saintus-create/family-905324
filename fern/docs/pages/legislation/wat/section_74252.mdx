# WAT § 74252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Officers

**Citation:** WAT § 74252

---

## Text

The salary of the secretary and the amount of the bond to be given for the faithful performance of his duties shall be fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
