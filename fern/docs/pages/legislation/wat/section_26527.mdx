# WAT § 26527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 2. Collection by County

**Citation:** WAT § 26527

---

## Text

As to money collected by the county tax collector and paid to the county treasurer, the county treasurer shall perform the duties ordinarily imposed on the treasurer of a district and be accountable therefor upon his official bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
