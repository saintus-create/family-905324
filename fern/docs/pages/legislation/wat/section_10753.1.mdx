# WAT § 10753.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Groundwater Management Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 10753.1

---

## Text

Nothing in this part, or in any groundwater management plan adopted pursuant to this part, affects surface water rights or the procedures under common law or local groundwater authority, or any provision of law other than this part that determines or grants surface water rights.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 603, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2003.)
