# WAT § 23604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23604

---

## Text

The petition, all proceedings in reference to it, the improvement district, and the land in it shall be designated by a number.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
