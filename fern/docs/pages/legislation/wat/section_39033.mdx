# WAT § 39033

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39033

---

## Text

“Other district” means any district or other public agency formed under any law of the state.
