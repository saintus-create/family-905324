# WAT § 30201

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30201

---

## Text

The petition for formation shall describe the proposed boundaries of the proposed district and shall pray that it be incorporated into a district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
