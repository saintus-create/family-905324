# WAT § 74520

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 74520

---

## Text

A district may make surveys and investigations of the water supply and resources of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
