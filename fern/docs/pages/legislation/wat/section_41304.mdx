# WAT § 41304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 1. Preliminary Procedure and Nominations

**Citation:** WAT § 41304

---

## Text

The board shall, by order made more than 24 days before the time for election, designate a convenient place within each precinct as the polling place of the precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
