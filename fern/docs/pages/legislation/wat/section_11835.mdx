# WAT § 11835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 5. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 11835

---

## Text

Warrants for payments to be made on account of bonds shall be drawn by the State Controller upon request of the State Treasurer whenever the drawing of warrants is required in order to make the payments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
