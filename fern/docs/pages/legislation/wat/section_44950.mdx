# WAT § 44950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 44950

---

## Text

A district may issue revenue warrants pursuant to this chapter to obtain funds for any lawful purpose of the district, including the repayment of indebtedness of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 36.)
