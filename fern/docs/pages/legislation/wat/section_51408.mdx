# WAT § 51408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 6. Supplemental Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51408

---

## Text

Notice of the supplemental assessment and of the call thereon shall be served upon each owner of land in the district in the manner prescribed in Section 51519.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
