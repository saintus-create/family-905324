# WAT § 41153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Redivision and Establishing of Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41153

---

## Text

The redivision order of the board shall be filed in the office of the secretary and a copy certified by the secretary shall be filed with the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 47. Effective January 1, 1993.)
