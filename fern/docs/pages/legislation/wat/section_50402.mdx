# WAT § 50402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Districts Formed to Operate Without a Board

**Citation:** WAT § 50402

---

## Text

The existence of a district formed to operate without a board shall not prevent the owners of the land included therein from forming a district to operate with a board by presenting to the board of supervisors the petition provided for in Chapter 1 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
