# WAT § 52759

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 52759

---

## Text

The first publication of the notice shall be not less than 30 days nor more than 90 days prior to the date fixed in the refunding bonds for the redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
