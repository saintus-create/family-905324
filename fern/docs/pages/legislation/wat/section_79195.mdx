# WAT § 79195

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 9. Water Supply, Reliability, and Infrastructure Program > Article: 3. Bay-Delta Multipurpose Water Management Program

## Text

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
