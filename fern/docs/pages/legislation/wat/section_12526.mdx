# WAT § 12526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Commissioner

**Citation:** WAT § 12526

---

## Text

The commissioner shall serve without compensation, but he shall be reimbursed for traveling and other expenses actually and necessarily incurred in the performance of his duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
