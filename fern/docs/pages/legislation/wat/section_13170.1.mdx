# WAT § 13170.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 3. State Water Quality Control > Article: 4. Other Powers and Duties of the State Board

**Citation:** WAT § 13170.1

---

## Text

The state board shall consider all relevant management agency agreements, which are intended to protect a specific beneficial use of water, prior to adopting all water quality control plans pursuant to Section 13170.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 578, Sec. 1.)
