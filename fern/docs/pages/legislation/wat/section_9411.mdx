# WAT § 9411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 2. Form of Bond

**Citation:** WAT § 9411

---

## Text

The interest coupons may be substantially in the following form: No.$The Treasurer of the State of California will pay to the holder hereof on the ____ day of ____, 19__, at his office in the City of Sacramento, State of California, the sum of ____ dollars ($____) in lawful money of the United States, out of the funds of the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District applicable thereto, for interest on bond of the district numbered ____.Attest:State Controller.
