# WAT § 6357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 1.5. Certificates of Approval

**Citation:** WAT § 6357

---

## Text

Each certificate of approval issued by the department under this part may contain such terms and conditions as the department may prescribe.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
