# WAT § 11656

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Contracts > Article: 2. Contracts with State Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 11656

---

## Text

The use by the department of any remedy specified in this article for the enforcement of any contract made with it is not exclusive and shall not deprive the department of, or limit the application of, any other remedy provided by law.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
