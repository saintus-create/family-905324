# WAT § 12928.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.6. Groundwater Recharge Facilities Financing > Article: 4. Loans

**Citation:** WAT § 12928.2

---

## Text

A contract for a loan pursuant to Section 12928 shall require the prior approval of the California Water Commission.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 1034, Sec. 1.)
