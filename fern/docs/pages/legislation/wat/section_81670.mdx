# WAT § 81670

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 81670

---

## Text

Membership of any public entity in the authority does not affect the identity or legal existence, nor impair the powers, of that public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 849, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
