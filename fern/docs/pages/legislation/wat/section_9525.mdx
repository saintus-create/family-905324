# WAT § 9525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 9. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 9525

---

## Text

The refunding bonds or any part thereof when sold may be called and redeemed by the board in the manner provided by Chapter 6 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
