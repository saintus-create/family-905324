# WAT § 10813

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 10813

---

## Text

“Customer” means a purchaser of water from a water supplier who uses water for agricultural purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 4, Sec. 4. (SB 7 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
