# WAT § 36365

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 4. Action to Secure Payment of Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36365

---

## Text

The jurisdiction of the court over the affairs of the district shall continue until the default for which the receiver was appointed has been discharged.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
