# WAT § 1097

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Witnesses and Production of Evidence > Article: 3. Compelling Attendance

**Citation:** WAT § 1097

---

## Text

Upon failure to obey the order the witness shall be dealt with as for contempt of court.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
