# WAT § 72700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by the Board

**Citation:** WAT § 72700

---

## Text

The board, by resolution, may initiate proceedings for the annexation of territory within the district whether contiguous or not to an improvement district to such improvement district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 132.)
