# WAT § 72679

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 72679

---

## Text

Within 10 days after the filing of any supplemental petition or petitions, the secretary shall examine them and certify to the result of such examination as provided in Sections 72675 to 72677, inclusive.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
