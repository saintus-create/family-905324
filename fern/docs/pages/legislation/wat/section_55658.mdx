# WAT § 55658

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 6. Zones

**Citation:** WAT § 55658

---

## Text

The date set for the hearing shall be at least 13 days after the date of the posting and the date of last publication of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
