# WAT § 36343

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36343

---

## Text

Each issue of revenue bonds shall be numbered consecutively and the bonds of each issue shall be numbered consecutively from lower to higher as they mature and shall bear such date as may be prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
