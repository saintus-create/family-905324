# WAT § 12831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Financing of Projects > Article: 2. Extent and Method of Allocations

**Citation:** WAT § 12831

---

## Text

The department shall submit to the Controller all approved local agency claims for reimbursement allocations for flood control projects within 90 days of receipt of the claim.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1378, Sec. 1. Effective September 26, 1984.)
