# WAT § 1342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Proceedings on Application > Article: 1. Notice of Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 1342

---

## Text

If a hearing is held on an unprotested application, notice of hearing shall be given by mailing notice not less than 20 days before the date of hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
