# WAT § 6205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 1. New Dams and Reservoirs or Enlargements of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6205

---

## Text

The application shall set forth the purpose for which the impounded or diverted water is to be used.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
