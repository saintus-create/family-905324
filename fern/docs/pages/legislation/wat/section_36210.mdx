# WAT § 36210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 4. Destruction and Redemption of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36210

---

## Text

Whenever a district has any of its unsold general obligation bonds which it is not necessary to sell for the purpose of raising funds, the board may, at a general election, or at a special election called for the purpose, submit the proposition of whether or not the bonds should be destroyed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
