# WAT § 30504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30504

---

## Text

All vacancies occurring in the office of director shall be filled pursuant to Section 1780 of the Government Code.
