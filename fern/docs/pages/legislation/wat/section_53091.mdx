# WAT § 53091

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 5. Warrants Drawn on Irrigation Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 53091

---

## Text

A warrant drawn on the irrigation fund shall be presented for payment to the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
