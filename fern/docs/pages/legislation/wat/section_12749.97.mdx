# WAT § 12749.97

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Approved Projects > Article: 3. Projects in Other Watersheds

**Citation:** WAT § 12749.97

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Sections 12749.93 and 12749.94, it is the intent of the Legislature that no funds be appropriated for state cooperation in the Murrieta Creek Flood Control Project before July 1, 2013.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 12749.95 (as added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 730) by Stats. 2004, Ch. 183, Sec. 359. Effective January 1, 2005.)
