# WAT § 13206

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 1. Organization and Membership of Regional Boards

**Citation:** WAT § 13206

---

## Text

Public officers associated with any area of government, including planning or water, and whether elected or appointed, may be appointed to, and may serve contemporaneously as members of, a regional board.
