# WAT § 6162

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 6. Inundation Maps and Emergency Planning

**Citation:** WAT § 6162

---

## Text

Any regulation promulgated pursuant to this article shall be deemed to be an emergency and necessary for the immediate preservation of the public peace, health and safety, or general welfare.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 88. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
