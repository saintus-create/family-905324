# WAT § 78537

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 4. Delta Improvement Program > Article: 3. Bay-Delta Agreement Program

**Citation:** WAT § 78537

---

## Text

The state shall, to the greatest extent possible, secure federal and nonfederal matching funds to implement this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
