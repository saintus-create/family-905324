# WAT § 8961

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8961

---

## Text

The warrants shall, at any time within the period of four years, be received in payment of any assessment for work or expenses for which the warrants were issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
