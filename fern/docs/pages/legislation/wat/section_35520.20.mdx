# WAT § 35520.20

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.2. Provisions Pertaining Only to the Lost Hills Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35520.20

---

## Text

The assessment roll as approved by the board shall be conclusive evidence before any court or body that the assessment has been made and levied according to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1983, Ch. 57, Sec. 1. Effective May 31, 1983.)
