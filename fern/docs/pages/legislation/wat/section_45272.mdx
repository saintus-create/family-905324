# WAT § 45272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Bond Election > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 45272

---

## Text

A list of the ballots cast shall be made by the board of election, containing the name of each voter who has voted at the election, and if the ballot was cast by proxy also the name of the person casting it, and the number of votes cast by each voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
