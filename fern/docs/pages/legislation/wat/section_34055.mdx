# WAT § 34055

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 34055

---

## Text

Districts governed by this division are subject to the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.
