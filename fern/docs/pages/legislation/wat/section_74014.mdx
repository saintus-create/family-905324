# WAT § 74014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 74014

---

## Text

“Principal county” means the county in which the lands within a district or a proposed district, or the greater portion thereof, are situated, and is the county in which the petition for the formation of a district is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
