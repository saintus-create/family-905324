# WAT § 35185

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Method of Elections for the Moulton-Niguel Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35185

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall apply only to the Moulton-Niguel Water District. For purposes of this chapter “district” means the Moulton-Niguel Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 713, Sec. 1.)
