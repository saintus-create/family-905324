# WAT § 5200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Part: 5.2. Groundwater Extraction Reporting for Probationary Basins and Basins Without a Groundwater Sustainability Agency > Chapter: 9. Alternative Distribution Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 5200

---

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that this part establishes groundwater reporting requirements for the purposes of subdivision (b) of Section 10724 and Chapter 11 (commencing with Section 10735) of Part 2.74 of Division 6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 347, Sec. 10. (AB 1739) Effective January 1, 2015.)
