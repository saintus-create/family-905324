# WAT § 74000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Short Title and General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 74000

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Water Conservation District Law of 1931.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
