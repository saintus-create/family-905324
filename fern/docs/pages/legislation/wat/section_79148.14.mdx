# WAT § 79148.14

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 7. Clean Water and Water Recycling Program > Article: 5. Coastal Nonpoint Source Control Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79148.14

---

## Text

No project shall receive funds under this article if it receives funds pursuant to Article 2 (commencing with Section 79110).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
