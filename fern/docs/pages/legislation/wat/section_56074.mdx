# WAT § 56074

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. DRAINAGE > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 56074

---

## Text

Only voters registered in the district are eligible to vote at the bond election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1232.)
