# WAT § 20742

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 3. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 20742

---

## Text

No defect in the form or contents of the published notice, title to it, or petition, nor failure to publish or error in publishing any signature to the petition shall vitiate the proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
