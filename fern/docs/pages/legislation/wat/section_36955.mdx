# WAT § 36955

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 36955

---

## Text

Thereupon the collector shall, as to each parcel sold, make an entry “Sold to the district” in the assessment book, and the date of the sale.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1958, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 94.)
