# WAT § 20561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 20561

---

## Text

Nothing in this division impairs the validity of any district formed prior to the effective date of this division, its rights, or obligations.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
