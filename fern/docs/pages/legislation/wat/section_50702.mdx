# WAT § 50702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 50702

---

## Text

The certified roll furnished to the election board shall be used by the election board in determining the number of votes each voter may cast.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 1109, Sec. 1.2. Effective September 28, 1983.)
