# WAT § 40653

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Board Meetings and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 40653

---

## Text

Special meetings of the board may be held as required for the proper transaction of the business of the district, but shall be ordered by a majority of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
