# WAT § 55580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 55580

---

## Text

The board may issue refunding bonds of the district for the purpose of refunding any or all outstanding bonds of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
