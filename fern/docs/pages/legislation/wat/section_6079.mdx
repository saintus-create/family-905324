# WAT § 6079

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 1. Powers in General

**Citation:** WAT § 6079

---

## Text

In carrying out the provisions of this part the department may cooperate with the United States or any of its agencies.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
