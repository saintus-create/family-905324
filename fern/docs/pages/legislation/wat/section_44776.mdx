# WAT § 44776

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 2. Proceeds of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 44776

---

## Text

Money collected upon any assessment upon which bonds have been issued shall be used exclusively for the payment of principal and interest of the bonds issued on the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
