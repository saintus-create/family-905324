# WAT § 8826

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8826

---

## Text

At the hearing, the board shall hear such evidence as may be offered touching the correctness of the assessment or the manner of its apportionment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
