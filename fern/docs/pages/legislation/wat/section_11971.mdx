# WAT § 11971

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 11. Water Conservation and Reclamation Projects > Article: 3. Conservation in the State Water Resources Development System

**Citation:** WAT § 11971

---

## Text

As used in this article, “sponsoring contractor” means a contractor or contractors, who have a long-term water supply contract with the department and who agree to reduce demands for project water from the State Water Resources Development System in accordance with Section 11970.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 938, Sec. 1.)
