# WAT § 36411

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.9. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 36411

---

## Text

Improvement districts consisting of contiguous or noncontiguous portions of the territory of a district may be formed within a district and bonds and warrants of the district may be issued for such improvement districts as provided in this chapter.
