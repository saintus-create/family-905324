# WAT § 8536

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 8536

---

## Text

The board has no power, jurisdiction, authority or control over the construction, operation or maintenance of the Central Valley Project or any part of it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
