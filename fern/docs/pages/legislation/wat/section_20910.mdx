# WAT § 20910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20910

---

## Text

For the purposes of the formation election the board of supervisors shall establish a convenient number of election precincts in the proposed district and define their boundaries.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
