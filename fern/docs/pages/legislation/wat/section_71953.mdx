# WAT § 71953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Sale of Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 71953

---

## Text

The bonds shall bear interest at a rate or rates not to exceed 8 percent per year, payable semiannually, except that interest for the first year may be payable at the end of that year.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
