# WAT § 22970.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 7. Runoff Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 22970.15

---

## Text

The voters qualified to vote for the elective offices at the preceding general district election shall be the only voters qualified for the runoff election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 268.)
