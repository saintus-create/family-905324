# WAT § 55526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 55526

---

## Text

The interest coupons of the bonds shall be numbered consecutively and signed by the auditor of the county by his engraved or lithographed signature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
