# WAT § 40333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 2. Official Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 40333

---

## Text

The bonds of the first directors of any district may be approved by a judge of the superior court of any affected county and recorded in the office of the recorder of such county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
