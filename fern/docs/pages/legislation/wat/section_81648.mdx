# WAT § 81648

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Authority > Article: 1. Powers

## Text

The authority may apply for and receive state and federal grants, loans, and other financial assistance.
