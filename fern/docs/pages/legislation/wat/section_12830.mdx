# WAT § 12830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Financing of Projects > Article: 2. Extent and Method of Allocations

**Citation:** WAT § 12830

---

## Text

The department may refuse to make any order for the reallocation of any of the funds if the provisions of this chapter are violated.

(History: Amended by Stats. 2001, Ch. 745, Sec. 230.7. Effective October 12, 2001.)
