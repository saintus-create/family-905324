# WAT § 24482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24482

---

## Text

The proceeds from the sale of any bonds in which any part of the sinking fund was invested shall be deposited in the sinking fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
