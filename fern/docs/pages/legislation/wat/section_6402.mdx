# WAT § 6402

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 6. Inspection During Progress of Work

**Citation:** WAT § 6402

---

## Text

If conditions are revealed which will not permit the construction of a safe dam or reservoir the approval may be revoked.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
