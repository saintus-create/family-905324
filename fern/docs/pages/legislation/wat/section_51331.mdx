# WAT § 51331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51331

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given in the same manner and for the same time as the notice of the hearing of objections to an original assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
