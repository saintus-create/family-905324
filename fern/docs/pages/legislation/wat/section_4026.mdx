# WAT § 4026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Creation of Watermaster Service Areas

**Citation:** WAT § 4026

---

## Text

Service areas shall be created from time to time as rights to water are ascertained and determined.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
