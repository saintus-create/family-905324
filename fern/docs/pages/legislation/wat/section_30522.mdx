# WAT § 30522

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 30522

---

## Text

At its organization meeting the board may transact any business of the district.
