# WAT § 36470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 2. Plans and Estimate of Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 36470

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a petition to form a distribution district the board shall cause a survey to be made of the proposed distribution system.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
