# WAT § 9332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9332

---

## Text

At the election the owner of each tract of land assessed in and by the assessment, upon which the assessment has not been paid as provided in Chapter 4 of this part, may, in person or by proxy, cast one vote for each one cent ($0.01) of the amount for which the tract is assessed by the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
