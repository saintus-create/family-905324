# WAT § 85084

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Part: 2. EARLY ACTIONS > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 85084

---

## Text

The council shall develop an interim plan that includes recommendations for early actions, projects, and programs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 39. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
