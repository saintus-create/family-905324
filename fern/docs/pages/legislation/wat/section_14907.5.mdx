# WAT § 14907.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 23. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Act > Article: 2. The San Joaquin Valley Drainage Relief Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14907.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of this chapter, the possessor of the water right determines the final disposition of the water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 959, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993. Section operative July 1, 1993, pursuant to Section 14920.)
