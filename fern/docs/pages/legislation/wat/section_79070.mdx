# WAT § 79070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 6. Watershed Protection Program > Article: 1. Watershed Protection Account

**Citation:** WAT § 79070

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “account” means the Watershed Protection Account created by Section 79071.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
