# WAT § 74015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 74015

---

## Text

“Affected county” means any county in which the land of a district is situated.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
