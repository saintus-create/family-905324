# WAT § 31025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 31025

---

## Text

The district shall fix and through the general manager collect the water rates.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
