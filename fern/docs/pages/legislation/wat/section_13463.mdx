# WAT § 13463

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6.1. Water Conservation and Water Quality Bond Law of 1986

**Citation:** WAT § 13463

---

## Text

Upon request of the department or the board, the committee shall determine whether or not it is necessary or desirable to issue bonds authorized under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 6, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 44 at the June 3, 1986, election.)
