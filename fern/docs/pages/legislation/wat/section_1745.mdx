# WAT § 1745

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 4. Water Supplier Contracts

**Citation:** WAT § 1745

---

## Text

As used in this article, the following terms have the following meanings:

(a) “Person” includes a public agency.

(b) “Water supplier” means a local public agency or private company supplying or storing water, or a mutual water company.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 481, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
