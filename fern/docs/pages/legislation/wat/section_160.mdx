# WAT § 160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2. State Administration Generally > Article: 2. California Water Commission

**Citation:** WAT § 160

---

## Text

The members of the California Water Commission shall select one of the members to serve as chairman, who shall hold office as chairman at the pleasure of the commission.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1941.)
