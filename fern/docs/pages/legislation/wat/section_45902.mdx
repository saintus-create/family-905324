# WAT § 45902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 1. Project and Government Contract Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 45902

---

## Text

Direct assessment warrants shall be made payable in amounts and at the times corresponding substantially to the amounts and times of payment of the installments of the direct assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
