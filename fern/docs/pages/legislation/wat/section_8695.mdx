# WAT § 8695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8695

---

## Text

The department may at its option appoint the chief engineer of the board to act also as its assistant in the performance of the duties required of it by this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
