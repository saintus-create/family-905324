# WAT § 51328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51328

---

## Text

Operation and maintenance assessment rolls adopted by any district prior to September 19, 1939, may be used for any or all of the purposes provided for in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
