# WAT § 37208

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 7.5. ALTERNATIVE PROVISIONS FOR LEVY, COLLECTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF DISTRICT ASSESSMENTS BY THE COUNTY > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37208

---

## Text

Said ad valorem assessment shall be levied and collected at the same time and in the same manner and by the same officers and with the same penalties and interest as general county taxes and when collected shall be paid to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 36508 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 614.)
