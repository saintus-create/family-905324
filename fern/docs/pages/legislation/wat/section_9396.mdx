# WAT § 9396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9396

---

## Text

The bonds shall be of the denomination of not less than one hundred dollars ($100) nor more than one thousand dollars ($1,000) each.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
