# WAT § 22225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 22225

---

## Text

Each district has the power generally to perform all acts necessary to carry out fully the provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
