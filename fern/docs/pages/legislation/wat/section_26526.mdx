# WAT § 26526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 2. Collection by County

**Citation:** WAT § 26526

---

## Text

When any county tax collector collects any assessments for any district, he shall pay the proceeds to the county treasurer of the office county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
