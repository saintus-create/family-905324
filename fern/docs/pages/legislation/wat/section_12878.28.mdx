# WAT § 12878.28

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 4. Assessments for Maintenance and Operation

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.28

---

## Text

Following the first day of April of each year, the board or department shall hold a hearing at Sacramento on the matter of fixing the estimates and assessments for all units to be operated or maintained by the department pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1800.)
