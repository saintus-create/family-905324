# WAT § 52222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 2. Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 52222

---

## Text

The county treasurer may accept in full or partial payment for bonds, outstanding warrants of the district at face value plus the accrued interest thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
