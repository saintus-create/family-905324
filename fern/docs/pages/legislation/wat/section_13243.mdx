# WAT § 13243

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 3. Regional Water Quality Control Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 13243

---

## Text

A regional board, in a water quality control plan or in waste discharge requirements, may specify certain conditions or areas where the discharge of waste, or certain types of waste, will not be permitted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
