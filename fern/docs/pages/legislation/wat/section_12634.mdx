# WAT § 12634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. Investigation of Projects > Article: 5. Investigation of Water Development Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 12634

---

## Text

Notice given as provided in Sections 12632 and 12633 shall be deemed full and sufficient notice to all parties in interest of the hearing or investigation.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 196.)
