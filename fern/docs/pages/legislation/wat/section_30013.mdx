# WAT § 30013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 30013

---

## Text

“District” means a county water district formed under the provisions of the County Water District Act or this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
