# WAT § 14038

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 16. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 3. Fiscal Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 14038

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby finds and declares that, inasmuch as the proceeds from the sale of bonds authorized by this chapter are not “proceeds of taxes” as that term is used in Article XIIIB of the California Constitution disbursement of these proceeds is not subject to the limitations imposed by that article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 45, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 81 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
