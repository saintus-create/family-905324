# WAT § 1732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 1. Temporary Changes

**Citation:** WAT § 1732

---

## Text

The petitioner shall not initiate or increase the use of groundwater to replace surface water transferred pursuant to this article, except in compliance with Sections 1745.10 and 1745.11.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 938, Sec. 13. Effective January 1, 2000.)
