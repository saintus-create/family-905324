# WAT § 1264

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 2. Contents of Applications

**Citation:** WAT § 1264

---

## Text

If for municipal water supply the application shall state the present population to be served, and, as near as may be, the future requirements of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
