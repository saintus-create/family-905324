# WAT § 25328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 6. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 25328

---

## Text

The notice shall state that sealed proposals will be received by the board at the district office for the purchase of bonds until the day and hour specified in the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
