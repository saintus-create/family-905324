# WAT § 25205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25205

---

## Text

The date of any bond shall be subsequent to the date of the election at which its issuance was authorized and prior to that of its delivery to a purchaser from the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
