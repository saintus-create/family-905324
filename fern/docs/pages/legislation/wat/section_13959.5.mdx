# WAT § 13959.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 12.5. Clean Water and Water Conservation Bond Law of 1978

**Citation:** WAT § 13959.5

---

## Text

There is in the State Treasury the State Clean Water and Water Conservation Fund, which fund is hereby created.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1160. Approved in Proposition 2 at the June 6, 1978, election.)
