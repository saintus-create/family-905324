# WAT § 75160

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 5. Inclusion of Territory

**Citation:** WAT § 75160

---

## Text

Territory may be added to a special improvement district in the manner provided in this chapter for its formation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
