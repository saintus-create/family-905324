# WAT § 12252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. Feather River Recreation

**Citation:** WAT § 12252

---

## Text

Construction of the recreation project authorized pursuant to Section 12250 shall be contingent upon a favorable environmental impact determination, and upon the execution of an agreement with a local public agency under which such public agency agrees to fund any construction costs exceeding one million two hundred thousand dollars ($1,200,000), and agrees to pay all costs of maintaining the project.
