# WAT § 41903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Contest of Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 41903

---

## Text

The contest shall be brought within 20 days after the declaration of the result.
