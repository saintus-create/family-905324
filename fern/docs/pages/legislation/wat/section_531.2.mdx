# WAT § 531.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Measurement > Article: 4.3. Agricultural and Urban Water Use Reporting

**Citation:** WAT § 531.2

---

## Text

The department, the board, and the State Department of Public Health shall coordinate the collection, management, and use of agricultural and urban water measurement information provided to each agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 675, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
