# WAT § 46761

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 5. Sale for Nonpayment

**Citation:** WAT § 46761

---

## Text

A certificate of sale shall be executed by the county treasurer to the purchaser, and this certificate of sale shall be recorded in the office of the county recorder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
