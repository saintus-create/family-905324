# WAT § 36320

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 2. Lien of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36320

---

## Text

The payment of both principal and interest of revenue bonds shall be secured only by the revenues pledged therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 6, Sec. 10. Effective February 4, 1980.)
