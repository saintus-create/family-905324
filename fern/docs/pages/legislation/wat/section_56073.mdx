# WAT § 56073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 17. DRAINAGE > Chapter: 6. Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 56073

---

## Text

An election board consisting of one inspector, one judge, and one clerk shall be appointed by the district board for each precinct.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1232.)
