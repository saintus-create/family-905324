# WAT § 13569

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7.3. Potable Reuse

**Citation:** WAT § 13569

---

## Text

The department may accept funds from nonstate sources and may expend these funds, upon appropriation by the Legislature, for the purposes of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 637, Sec. 4. (SB 322) Effective January 1, 2014.)
