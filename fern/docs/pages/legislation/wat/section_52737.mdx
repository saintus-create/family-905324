# WAT § 52737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 2. Sinking Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 52737

---

## Text

The proceeds of sale of any bonds in which any part of the sinking fund is invested shall be deposited in the sinking fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
