# WAT § 81327

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 2. Formation of Agency > Article: 3. Establishment of Agency

**Citation:** WAT § 81327

---

## Text

The Secretary of State, not later than 10 days from the date of the receipt of the certificate described in Section 81326, shall issue a certificate of formation reciting that the agency has been formed pursuant to this division and identifying the public entities of which the agency is comprised. The Secretary of State shall transmit a copy of the certificate to each public entity that is a member of the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
