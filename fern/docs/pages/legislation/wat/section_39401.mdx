# WAT § 39401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 1. Petitioners and Land

**Citation:** WAT § 39401

---

## Text

The proposed district may include land situated in other distinctive district agencies of the State, including other water storage districts having different plans and purposes and the object of which is not the same.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
