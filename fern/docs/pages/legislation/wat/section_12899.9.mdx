# WAT § 12899.9

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 6.5. State Water Resources Development System Rights-Of-Way

**Citation:** WAT § 12899.9

---

## Text

The department may adopt regulations to implement this chapter, including regulations that provide for the filing of an application for a permit, related administrative review and inspection, the imposition of permit fees and permit terms and conditions, an administrative appeal process, and a process for administrative review and regulation of existing encroachments in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2005, Ch. 263, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2006.)
