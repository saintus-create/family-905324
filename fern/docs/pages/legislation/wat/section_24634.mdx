# WAT § 24634

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 2. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 24634

---

## Text

Notice of the election shall be given and the election shall be held and the result determined as nearly as practicable in the manner provided for bond elections.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
