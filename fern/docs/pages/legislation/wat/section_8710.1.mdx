# WAT § 8710.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8710.1

---

## Text

The provisions of Section 8710 shall not apply to any work of interior irrigation or drainage upon reclaimed land, which work is not actually on, through, or adjoining any flood control works under the jurisdiction of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1707.)
