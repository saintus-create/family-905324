# WAT § 50805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Special Elections > Article: 1. Calling

**Citation:** WAT § 50805

---

## Text

An affidavit of the publication and posting of the notice shall be filed with the county elections official, with a copy of the order calling the election which is certified by the president.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 174. Effective January 1, 2003.)
