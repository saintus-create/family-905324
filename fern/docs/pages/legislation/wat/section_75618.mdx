# WAT § 75618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 4. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 75618

---

## Text

Upon good cause shown, an amended statement of water production may be filed or a correction of the records may be made at any time prior to the final date for filing the next semiannual water production statement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
