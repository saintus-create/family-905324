# WAT § 8689

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8689

---

## Text

Any contract let by the department for any work under this article shall be approved by the board before becoming effective.
