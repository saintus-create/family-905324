# WAT § 40651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Board Meetings and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 40651

---

## Text

The board shall hold a regular meeting each month at the place selected as the district office.
