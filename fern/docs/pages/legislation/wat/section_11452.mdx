# WAT § 11452

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Department of Water Resources > Article: 2. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 11452

---

## Text

The department shall proceed with the construction of the project immediately upon funds being available therefor and shall carry such work to completion as rapidly as possible.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
