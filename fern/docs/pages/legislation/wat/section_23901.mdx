# WAT § 23901

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3.5. Alternative Provisions for the Formation of Improvement Districts and the Issuance of Bonds Therefor

**Citation:** WAT § 23901

---

## Text

Improvement districts consisting of a portion of the territory of a district may be formed within a district and, notwithstanding any other provision in this division, bonds of the district may be issued for such improvement districts as provided in this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 580.)
