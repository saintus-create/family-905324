# WAT § 80500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND RECOVERY ACT OF 2019 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 80500

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed to reduce or modify an electrical corporation’s obligation to serve. The commission shall issue orders it determines are necessary to carry out this section.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 80502 by Stats. 2019, Ch. 396, Sec. 53. (AB 1513) Effective January 1, 2020.)
