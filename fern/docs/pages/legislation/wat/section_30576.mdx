# WAT § 30576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board and Officer Activities

**Citation:** WAT § 30576

---

## Text

The powers of districts enumerated in this division shall, except as therein otherwise provided, be exercised by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
