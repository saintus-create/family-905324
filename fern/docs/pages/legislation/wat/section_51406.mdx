# WAT § 51406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 6. Supplemental Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51406

---

## Text

At least 30 days shall elapse between the date of call and the date of delinquency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
