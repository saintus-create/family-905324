# WAT § 8635

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 3. Levees

**Citation:** WAT § 8635

## Text

The giving of the notice by publication shall be complete on the twenty-first day after the first publication.
