# WAT § 25528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 2. Land Escaping Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 25528

---

## Text

The additional assessments shall be payable at the same times as the current assessment, and if not paid, shall be subject to like penalties and proceedings to enforce collection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
