# WAT § 51242

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51242

---

## Text

The list of charges assessed, when completed, shall be filed with the clerk of the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
