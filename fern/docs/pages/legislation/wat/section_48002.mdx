# WAT § 48002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 1. Petition and Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 48002

---

## Text

The petition shall be acknowledged, and the acknowledgment shall have the same force and effect as evidence as the acknowledgment of a conveyance of real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
