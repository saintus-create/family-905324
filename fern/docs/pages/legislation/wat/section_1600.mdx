# WAT § 1600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 9. Licenses > Article: 1. Report of Completion

**Citation:** WAT § 1600

---

## Text

Immediately upon completion of the construction of works and application of the water to beneficial use the permittee shall report the completion to the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 289.)
