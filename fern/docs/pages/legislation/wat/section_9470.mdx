# WAT § 9470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9470

---

## Text

Any bonds purchased pursuant to the provisions of this article, may be delivered to the State Treasurer for cancellation and the bonds shall thereupon be canceled by the State Treasurer and after cancellation shall cease to be an indebtedness or obligation of the drainage district and the assessment, or either, for any purpose whatsoever.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
