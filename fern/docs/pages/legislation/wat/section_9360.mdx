# WAT § 9360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 2. Polling Places, Notices and Board of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 9360

---

## Text

The board of election shall consist of three owners of land assessed in and by the assessment and situated within the voting district where the polling place is located.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
