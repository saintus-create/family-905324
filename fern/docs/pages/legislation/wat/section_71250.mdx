# WAT § 71250

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 71250

---

## Text

The board of directors shall consist of five members. Each director shall be a resident of the division from which he is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
