# WAT § 6391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 5. Complaints as to Unsafe Conditions

**Citation:** WAT § 6391

---

## Text

If the complainant insists upon an inspection and deposits with the department a sum estimated by it to be sufficient to cover costs of an inspection, the department shall cause an inspection to be made despite its finding as to the sufficiency of its records to determine the alleged danger.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
