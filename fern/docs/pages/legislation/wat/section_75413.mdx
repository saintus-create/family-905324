# WAT § 75413

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 1. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 75413

---

## Text

The assessment shall be payable in not to exceed 40 annual installments, with interest at an annual rate not to exceed 7 percent.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
