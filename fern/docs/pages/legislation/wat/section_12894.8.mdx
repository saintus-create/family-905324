# WAT § 12894.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5.5. Local Agency Bond Payments

**Citation:** WAT § 12894.8

---

## Text

Prior approval by the voters of an eligible district of a project described in Section 12894.3 and bonds therefor shall constitute approval of a loan pursuant to Section 12894.3 to the extent that the loan proceeds will be utilized to construct the project in lieu of the approved bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 1078.)
