# WAT § 9047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 5. Land Subject to Lien of Assessment No. 6

**Citation:** WAT § 9047

---

## Text

The trustees shall not return the warrants to the board for cancellation, without the consent of the board, if an assessment is then in process of being levied to pay the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
