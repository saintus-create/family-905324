# WAT § 10530

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** WAT § 10530

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Integrated Regional Water Management Planning Act.
