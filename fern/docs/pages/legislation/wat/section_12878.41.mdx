# WAT § 12878.41

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 5. Administration of Maintenance Area Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.41

---

## Text

All money deposited in the Water Resources Revolving Fund pursuant to Section 12878.39, and all money transferred to the Water Resources Revolving Fund from the Flood Control Project Maintenance Revolving Fund, or appropriated for flood project maintenance under this chapter, is appropriated for the purposes of this chapter without regard to fiscal years and shall not be subject to the provisions of Section 16304 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1800.)
