# WAT § 71170

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71170

---

## Text

One director shall be elected for each division by the voters of the division. Each director shall be a resident of the division for which he is elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
