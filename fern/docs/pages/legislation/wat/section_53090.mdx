# WAT § 53090

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 5. Warrants Drawn on Irrigation Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 53090

---

## Text

A warrant drawn on the irrigation fund shall designate that it shall be paid therefrom, and is not required to be approved by the board of supervisors nor be in the form prescribed by Article 2 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
