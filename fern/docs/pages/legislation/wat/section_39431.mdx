# WAT § 39431

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 2. Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 39431

---

## Text

Signatures to the petition may be withdrawn at any time before its publication is commenced by filing a declaration signed and acknowledged by the petitioner with the department stating that it is the intention of the petitioner to withdraw from the petition.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
