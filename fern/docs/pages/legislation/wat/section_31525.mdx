# WAT § 31525

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 31525

---

## Text

“Council chambers” refers to the place designated by the board of directors of the county water district for holding its regular meetings.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
