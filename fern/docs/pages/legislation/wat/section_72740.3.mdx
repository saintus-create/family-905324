# WAT § 72740.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Consolidation of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 72740.3

---

## Text

At the time and place fixed in the resolution of intention, or at any time or place to which the hearing is adjourned, the board shall proceed with the hearing. At the hearing, any person interested, including any person owning property in each improvement district or in the proposed consolidated improvement district, may appear and present any matters material to the questions set forth in the resolution of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 555, Sec. 4. Effective August 22, 1986.)
