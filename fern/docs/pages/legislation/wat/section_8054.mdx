# WAT § 8054

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 5. Powers of City Council

**Citation:** WAT § 8054

---

## Text

The city council may, by resolution, require the treasurer of the city to give additional bonds for the safe custody and care of public funds derived under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
