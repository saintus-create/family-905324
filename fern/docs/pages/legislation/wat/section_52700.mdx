# WAT § 52700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Forms

**Citation:** WAT § 52700

---

## Text

The board may cause refunding bonds to be issued as provided in this chapter for the purpose of refunding any outstanding bonds, including original issues and refunding issues.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
