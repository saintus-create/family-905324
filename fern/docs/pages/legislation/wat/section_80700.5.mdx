# WAT § 80700.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 29. ELECTRICITY SUPPLY STRATEGIC RELIABILITY RESERVE PROGRAM > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 80700.5

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Electricity Supply Strategic Reliability Reserve Program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 53, Sec. 14. (SB 124) Effective July 10, 2023.)
