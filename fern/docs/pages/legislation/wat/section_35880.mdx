# WAT § 35880

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 35880

---

## Text

In any contract made between a district and the United States pursuant to this chapter, the land which may be charged with any taxes or assessments under the contract shall be designated and described.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 991.)
