# WAT § 50316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 2. Approval

**Citation:** WAT § 50316

---

## Text

The board of supervisors may further continue the hearing from time to time, by order entered upon its minutes, to the end that a full hearing may be had.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
