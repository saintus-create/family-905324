# WAT § 4001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 4001

---

## Text

As used in this part, “conduit” includes ditches, pipe lines, and other means of diversion.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
