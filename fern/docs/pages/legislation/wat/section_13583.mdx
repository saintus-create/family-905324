# WAT § 13583

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7.5. Water Recycling Act of 1991

**Citation:** WAT § 13583

---

## Text

(a) If a retail water supplier that is a public agency does not comply with this chapter, the customer may petition a court for a writ of mandate pursuant to Chapter 2 (commencing with Section 1084) of Title 1 of Part 3 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

(b) If a retail water supplier is regulated by the Public Utilities Commission and does not comply with this chapter, the Public Utilities Commission may order the retailer to comply with this chapter after receiving a petition from the customer specifying the provisions of this chapter with which the retailer has failed to comply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1998, Ch. 753, Sec. 10. Effective January 1, 1999.)
