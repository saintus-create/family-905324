# WAT § 22080.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1.5. Watermaster Service

**Citation:** WAT § 22080.5

---

## Text

Any duty or authority vested in a watermaster appointed by a district may be exercised by the watermaster directly or through the agency of a deputy watermaster if there is one.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 780, Sec. 1.)
