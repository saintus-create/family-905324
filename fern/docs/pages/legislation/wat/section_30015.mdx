# WAT § 30015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 30015

---

## Text

“Land” except in Chapter 2 of Part 8 means land in the district or proposed district involved.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
