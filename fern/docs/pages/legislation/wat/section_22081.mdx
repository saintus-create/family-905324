# WAT § 22081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1.5. Watermaster Service

**Citation:** WAT § 22081

---

## Text

A district, as defined in subdivision (b) of Section 22080, may appoint one or more watermasters to regulate the use of water within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 780, Sec. 1.)
