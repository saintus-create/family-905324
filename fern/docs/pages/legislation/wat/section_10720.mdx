# WAT § 10720

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10720

---

## Text

This part shall be known, and may be cited, as the “Sustainable Groundwater Management Act.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 346, Sec. 3. (SB 1168) Effective January 1, 2015.)
