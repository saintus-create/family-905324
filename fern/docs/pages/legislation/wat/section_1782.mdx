# WAT § 1782

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 2. Joint Occupancy and Use

**Citation:** WAT § 1782

---

## Text

The applicant shall also pay a proper pro rata share, based as above provided in this article, of the cost of maintaining the works, on and after beginning the occupancy and use thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
