# WAT § 22970.35

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Pertaining Only to 500,000-Acre Districts > Article: 7. Runoff Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 22970.35

---

## Text

In all other respects a runoff election shall be conducted in the same manner as the general election for that office held pursuant to the Uniform District Election Law, insofar as practicable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1968, Ch. 268.)
