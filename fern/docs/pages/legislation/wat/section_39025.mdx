# WAT § 39025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39025

---

## Text

“Dispose” includes sell, contract to lease, contract to sell, and the making of any instrument necessary therefor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
