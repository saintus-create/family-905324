# WAT § 5006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Part: 5. RECORDATION OF WATER EXTRACTIONS AND DIVERSIONS > Chapter: 9. Alternative Distribution Procedure

## Text

Each notice shall be sworn to and shall be accompanied by a filing fee which shall be fixed by the board pursuant to Section 1529.
