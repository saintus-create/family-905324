# WAT § 75064

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75064

---

## Text

Immediately after the bond election, the election officers shall publicly count the votes and make and transmit a report of the result of the election to the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
