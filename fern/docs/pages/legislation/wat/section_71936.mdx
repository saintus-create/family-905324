# WAT § 71936

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation of Uninhabited Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 3. Advance of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 71936

---

## Text

The board may advance general funds of the district to accomplish the purposes of an improvement district formed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1151.)
