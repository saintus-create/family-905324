# WAT § 46356

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 9. Reassessment of Cost

**Citation:** WAT § 46356

---

## Text

The reassessment of costs of the project shall be made in the same manner as provided in this chapter for the original assessment of costs.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
