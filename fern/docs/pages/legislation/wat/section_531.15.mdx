# WAT § 531.15

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Measurement > Article: 4.3. Agricultural and Urban Water Use Reporting

**Citation:** WAT § 531.15

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of the law, state agencies shall carry out the duties described in this article only to the extent that funds are made available for the purposes of implementing those duties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2007, Ch. 675, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2008.)
