# WAT § 21266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Selecting Officers in Certain Cases > Article: 1. Appointments to Fill Vacancies

**Citation:** WAT § 21266

---

## Text

A board shall fill by appointment vacancies in the offices of assessor, collector, and treasurer. If the board fails to make an appointment within 40 days, the board of supervisors of the office county shall make the appointment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
