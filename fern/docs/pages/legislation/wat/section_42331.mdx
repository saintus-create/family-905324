# WAT § 42331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Recommendation of Abandonment of Project > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42331

---

## Text

The qualifications of voters at the election shall be the same as prescribed for the formation election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
