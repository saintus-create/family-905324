# WAT § 74502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Duties Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 74502

---

## Text

A district may appoint and employ such engineer or engineers and such attorney or attorneys as the board deems necessary or advisable to accomplish the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
