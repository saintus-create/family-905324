# WAT § 13953.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 12.2. San Joaquin Valley Agricultural Drain

**Citation:** WAT § 13953.1

---

## Text

There shall be no discharge from a San Joaquin Valley drain into Monterey Bay or tributaries draining into Monterey Bay.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 33, Sec. 1.)
