# WAT § 52751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 52751

---

## Text

The notice shall state the amount available for the redemption of bonds or refunding bonds and shall specify when and where the proposals will be opened.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
