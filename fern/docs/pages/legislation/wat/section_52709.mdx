# WAT § 52709

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Forms

**Citation:** WAT § 52709

---

## Text

Refunding bonds issued pursuant to this chapter may be sold from time to time in the same manner as other bonds of the district, or may be exchanged for other bonds of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 133. Effective January 1, 1993.)
