# WAT § 13291.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4.5. Onsite Sewage Treatment Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 13291.7

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to limit the land use authority of any city, county, or city and county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 781, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2001.)
