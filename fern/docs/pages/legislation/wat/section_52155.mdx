# WAT § 52155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Sale of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 52155

---

## Text

A record of the sale shall be made upon the books of the county treasurer.
