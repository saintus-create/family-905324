# WAT § 25215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25215

---

## Text

When bonds are made callable, a statement to that effect shall be set forth on the face of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
