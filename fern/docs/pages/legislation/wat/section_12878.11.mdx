# WAT § 12878.11

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 1. Formation of Maintenance Areas

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.11

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication in at least one newspaper of general circulation within the proposed boundaries of the maintenance area for at least once a week for two successive weeks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1800.)
