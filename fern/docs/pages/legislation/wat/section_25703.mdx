# WAT § 25703

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 3. Particular Purpose Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25703

---

## Text

To the right of or below each question on the ballot, the words “Yes” and “No” shall be printed on separate lines, with voting targets.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 50. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
