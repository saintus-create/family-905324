# WAT § 52205

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 52205

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall place the bonds to the credit of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
