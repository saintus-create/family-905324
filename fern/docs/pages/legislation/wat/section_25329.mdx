# WAT § 25329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 6. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 25329

---

## Text

At the time specified the board shall open the proposals and award the purchase of the bonds or any portion of them to the highest responsible bidder or bidders.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
