# WAT § 31321

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 2. Settlements and Statements

**Citation:** WAT § 31321

---

## Text

The collector shall be credited in his settlement with the secretary with the amount due to the district on property he sells to the district for delinquent taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
