# WAT § 25212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25212

---

## Text

Each bond shall be made payable at a given time for its full face value and not for a percentage thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
