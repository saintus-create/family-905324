# WAT § 36215

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 4. Destruction and Redemption of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36215

---

## Text

The provisions of Sections 36303, 36304, 36305 and 36348 shall also be applicable to general obligation bonds.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1329.)
