# WAT § 75460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 75460

---

## Text

The project assessment, or any installment thereof, shall be and remain a lien on the lands in the same manner as, and shall be part of, the annual district assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
