# WAT § 36830.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 36830.2

---

## Text

As used herein, “negotiable paper” means bank checks and drafts and express and post office money orders payable on demand.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 438.)
