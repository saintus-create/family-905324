# WAT § 79205.12

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 9. Water Supply, Reliability, and Infrastructure Program > Article: 4. Interim Water Reliable Supply and Water Quality Infrastructure and Management Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79205.12

## Text

The state, to the greatest extent possible, shall seek matching federal funds to implement this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
