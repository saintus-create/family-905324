# WAT § 8696

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8696

---

## Text

The compensation of the chief engineer while acting also as assistant to the department shall be apportioned as may be agreed upon between the board and the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
