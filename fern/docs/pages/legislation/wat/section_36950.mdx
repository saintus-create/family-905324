# WAT § 36950

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 36950

---

## Text

Unpaid assessments are delinquent unless paid within six calendar months after they become due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
