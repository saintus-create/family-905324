# WAT § 60182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 60182

---

## Text

The president and secretary in addition to the respective duties imposed on them by law shall perform such duties as may be imposed on them by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
