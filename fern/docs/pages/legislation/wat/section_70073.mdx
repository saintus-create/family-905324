# WAT § 70073

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Internal Organization > Article: 1. The Board of Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 70073

---

## Text

Each director shall, before entering upon the duties of his office, take an oath of office and file the same with the county clerk. The directors shall, at their first meeting after taking office, elect a chairman and a clerk from their own membership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
