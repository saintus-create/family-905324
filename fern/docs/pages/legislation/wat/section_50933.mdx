# WAT § 50933

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property

**Citation:** WAT § 50933

---

## Text

A district may construct, maintain, and operate ferry boats, bridges, road systems, and related facilities to provide access to the district’s levee, drainage, and irrigation systems, and to provide access to the lands within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 268, Sec. 1. Effective August 2, 1989.)
