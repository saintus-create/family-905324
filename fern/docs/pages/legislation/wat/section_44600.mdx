# WAT § 44600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Warrants > Article: 1. Execution

**Citation:** WAT § 44600

---

## Text

Warrants drawn by the department shall be drawn upon the treasurer of the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 91. Effective January 1, 1993.)
