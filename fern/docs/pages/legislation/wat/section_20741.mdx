# WAT § 20741

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 3. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 20741

---

## Text

When the petition consists of a number of separate instruments, only one need be published, but the names attached to all of the instruments shall appear in the publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
