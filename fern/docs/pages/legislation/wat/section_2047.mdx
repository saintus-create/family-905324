# WAT § 2047

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Reference by Courts of This State > Article: 3. Expenses of Reference

**Citation:** WAT § 2047

---

## Text

If no objection is filed with the court within the prescribed period, the court upon ex parte application of the board shall enter a judgment against the parties in favor of the board in the amounts apportioned to them.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 329.)
