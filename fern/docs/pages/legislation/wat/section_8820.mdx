# WAT § 8820

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8820

---

## Text

The assessment lists when completed shall be filed with the secretary of the board and the secretary shall forward to the county treasurer of each county in which any land so assessed is situated, the assessment list for the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
