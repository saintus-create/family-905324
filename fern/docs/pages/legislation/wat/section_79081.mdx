# WAT § 79081

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 6. Watershed Protection Program > Article: 2. Watershed Protection Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79081

---

## Text

A grant recipient shall obtain written permission from the landowners of the parcel of land upon which the project is proposed to be carried out. The written permission shall expressly consent to the actions described in the grant application.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
