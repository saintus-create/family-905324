# WAT § 1215.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1.7. Areas of Origin

**Citation:** WAT § 1215.6

---

## Text

For the purposes of this article, “water user or users” within a protected area means an appropriator or appropriators, a riparian user or users, or a groundwater user or users of water on land owned or controlled by them within a protected area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1655, Sec. 2.)
