# WAT § 8801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 3. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8801

---

## Text

As many tracts may be embraced in one application as applicant is the owner of, or interested in.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
