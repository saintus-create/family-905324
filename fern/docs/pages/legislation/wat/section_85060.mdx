# WAT § 85060

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 85060

---

## Text

“Delta watershed” means the Sacramento River Hydrologic Region and the San Joaquin River Hydrologic Region as described in the department’s Bulletin No. 160-05.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 39. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
