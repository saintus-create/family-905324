# WAT § 31405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 31405

---

## Text

The board shall fix a date when an election shall be held to authorize the bonded indebtedness described in the resolution adopted pursuant to Section 31391.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
