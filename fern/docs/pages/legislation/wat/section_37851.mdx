# WAT § 37851

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 37851

---

## Text

All actions and proceedings pending by or against the water storage district at the time of the merger may be prosecuted to final judgment for or against the water storage district or the surviving district may be substituted as a party.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
