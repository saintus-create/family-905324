# WAT § 31461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 6. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 31461

---

## Text

When bonds are made callable, a statement to that effect shall be set forth on the face of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
