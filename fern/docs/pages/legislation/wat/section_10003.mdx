# WAT § 10003

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 1. ADOPTION OF STATE WATER PLAN

**Citation:** WAT § 10003

---

## Text

Notwithstanding anything contained in this part, all applications heretofore filed by the Department of Finance under Part 2 of Division 6 shall remain valid and shall retain and have the status and priority accorded to such applications as now or hereafter provided in said Part 2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1945, Ch. 1157.)
