# WAT § 46231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 4. Hearings Before Adjustment Board

**Citation:** WAT § 46231

---

## Text

At the hearings, the adjustment board shall hear such evidence as may be offered touching the correctness of the assessment, and may modify, amend, or approve the assessment in any particular and may reapportion the whole or any part of it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
