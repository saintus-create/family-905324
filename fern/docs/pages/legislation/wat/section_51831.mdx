# WAT § 51831

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 2. Calls on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51831

---

## Text

A certified copy of the resolution shall be filed in the office of the county treasurer, and the county treasurer shall make the necessary entry in the records of his office to evidence the cancellation of the call.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
