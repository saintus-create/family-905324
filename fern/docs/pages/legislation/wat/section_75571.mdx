# WAT § 75571

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 2. Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 75571

---

## Text

The secretary shall publish a notice of the receipt of the engineering investigation and report and of a public hearing thereon to be held in April. The notice shall be published, pursuant to Section 6061 of the Government Code, in a newspaper of general circulation printed and published within the district, at least 10 days prior to the date at which the public hearing is held.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 255, Sec. 2.)
