# WAT § 75638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 6. United Water Conservation District

**Citation:** WAT § 75638

---

## Text

The remedies specified in this article are cumulative and the district may pursue them alternatively or use them concurrently as determined by the board of directors of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 294, Sec. 1. (AB 552) Effective January 1, 2018.)
