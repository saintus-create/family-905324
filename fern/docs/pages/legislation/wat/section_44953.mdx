# WAT § 44953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 44953

---

## Text

Revenue warrants shall mature at such time or times as the board may prescribe, but not more than five years from the date of issuance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 36.)
