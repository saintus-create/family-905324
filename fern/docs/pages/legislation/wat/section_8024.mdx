# WAT § 8024

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 8024

---

## Text

The special election shall be held in the manner provided by law for holding elections in the city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
