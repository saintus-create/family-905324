# WAT § 9466

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9466

---

## Text

Whenever the drainage district has any money in the bond fund of any assessment which money is not immediately required for the purpose of paying principal or interest on bonds of the assessment, all or any part of it may be invested in bonds of the drainage district based upon and secured by the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
