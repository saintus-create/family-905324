# WAT § 45903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 1. Project and Government Contract Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 45903

---

## Text

Direct assessment warrants shall bear interest at a rate to be fixed by the board at the time of sale thereof, not to exceed 7 percent per year, which interest may be made payable semiannually.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1086.)
