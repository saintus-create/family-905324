# WAT § 9570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 3. Redemption and Conveyancing of Lands

**Citation:** WAT § 9570

---

## Text

Every deed by the board purporting to be executed under this chapter shall be prima facie evidence of the truth of the matters therein recited and of ownership by the grantee of the lands therein described.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
