# WAT § 20220

**Hierarchy:** Division: 10. FINANCIAL SUPERVISION OF DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Water District Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 20220

---

## Text

(a) Notwithstanding any other provision of law, a mobilehome used primarily for residential purposes and located on rental spaces within a mobilehome park is not property subject to ad valorem property taxes for debt service of any water district or of any improvement district therein.

(b) As used in this chapter, “water district” means any district or other political subdivision, other than a city or county, a primary function of which is the irrigation, reclamation, or drainage of land or the diversion, storage, or distribution of water primarily for domestic, municipal, agricultural, industrial, recreation, fish and wildlife enhancement, flood control, or power production purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 56, Sec. 179.)
