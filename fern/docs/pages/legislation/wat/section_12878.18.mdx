# WAT § 12878.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 1. Formation of Maintenance Areas

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.18

---

## Text

Land benefited by more than one unit may be included within as many maintenance areas as there are units by which it is benefited.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1800.)
