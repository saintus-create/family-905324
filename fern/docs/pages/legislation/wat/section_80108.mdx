# WAT § 80108

**Hierarchy:** Division: 27. PURCHASE AND SALE OF ELECTRIC POWER > Chapter: 2. Power Program > Article: 1. Powers of the Department

**Citation:** WAT § 80108

---

## Text

The commission may issue rules regulating the enforcement of the agency function pursuant this division, including collection and payment to the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2001, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 4, Sec. 4. Effective February 1, 2001.)
