# WAT § 30777

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Election of Directors > Article: 3. Preparation for Election

**Citation:** WAT § 30777

---

## Text

The order of candidates names on the ballot shall be as provided by the Elections Code.
