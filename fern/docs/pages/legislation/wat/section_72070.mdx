# WAT § 72070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance of Bonds and Payment of Debt Service by the Pomona Valley Municipal Water District > Article: 5. Powers—Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 72070

---

## Text

The board may advance general funds of the district to accomplish the purposes of an improvement district performed pursuant to this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 291.)
