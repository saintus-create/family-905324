# WAT § 78550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 4. Delta Improvement Program > Article: 5. South Delta Barriers Program

**Citation:** WAT § 78550

---

## Text

(a) There is hereby created in the account the South Delta Barriers Subaccount.

(b) For the purposes of this article, “subaccount” means the South Delta Barriers Subaccount created by subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
