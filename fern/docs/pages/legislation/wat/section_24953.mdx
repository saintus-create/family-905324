# WAT § 24953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Construction Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24953

---

## Text

The surveys, examinations, and drawings shall be made under the direction of a competent engineer and shall be certified by him.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
