# WAT § 30579

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board and Officer Activities

**Citation:** WAT § 30579

---

## Text

The secretary shall:

(a) Countersign all contracts on behalf of the district.

(b) Perform other duties imposed by the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
