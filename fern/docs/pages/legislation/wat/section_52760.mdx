# WAT § 52760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 52760

---

## Text

Interest on refunding bonds declared to be callable shall cease after the date fixed for redemption if the district has provided funds for the payment of the principal and interest thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
