# WAT § 39082

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Department of Water Resources and State Treasurer > Article: 1. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 39082

---

## Text

Whenever the department deems it in the public interest that preliminary surveys and field investigations of proposed district projects shall be made at the expense of the State the department shall make the surveys and investigation and prepare a report thereof which shall be kept on file in its office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
