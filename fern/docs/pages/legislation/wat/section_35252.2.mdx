# WAT § 35252.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.3. Method of Election for the Riverview Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35252.2

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, or the bylaws of the district, all elections within the district, including elections for director and elections that are required by law to authorize any special taxes or bonded indebtedness, shall be conducted as resident voter elections in accordance with this chapter. Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to require resident voter approval of bonds where voter approval is not required under the statutory or constitutional provisions in effect prior to January 1, 1996.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 833, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
