# WAT § 1316

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 2. Publication of Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1316

---

## Text

Proof of publication shall be by copy of the notice as published attached to and made a part of the affidavit of the publisher or foreman of the newspaper publishing the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
