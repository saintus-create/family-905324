# WAT § 12879

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.7. Water Conservation Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12879

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Water Conservation Bond Law of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 46, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 82 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
