# WAT § 13203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 1. Organization and Membership of Regional Boards

**Citation:** WAT § 13203

---

## Text

The official designation of each regional board shall be: California Regional Water Quality Control Board, (region name).
