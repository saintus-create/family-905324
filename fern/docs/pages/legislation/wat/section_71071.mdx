# WAT § 71071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Territory > Article: 2. Inclusion of Cities

**Citation:** WAT § 71071

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided in this article, if an application includes any portion of a city, the entire territory of the city, at the time of filing the application, shall be included within the boundaries of the proposed district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1971, Ch. 310.)
