# WAT § 80510

**Hierarchy:** Division: 28. WILDFIRE PREVENTION AND RECOVERY ACT OF 2019 > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 80510

---

## Text

All state agencies and other official state organizations, and all persons connected with those agencies and organizations, shall, at the request of the department, give the department assistance or other cooperation in carrying out the purposes of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2019, Ch. 79, Sec. 22. (AB 1054) Effective July 12, 2019.)
