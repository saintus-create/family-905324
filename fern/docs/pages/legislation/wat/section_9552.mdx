# WAT § 9552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 2. Purchase and Sale of Land by the Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 9552

---

## Text

Payment for the land purchased under the next preceding section may be made by the purchaser either in cash or matured bonds and coupons issued upon assessment taken at their face value.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
