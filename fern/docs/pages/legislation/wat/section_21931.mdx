# WAT § 21931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bond Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 21931

---

## Text

The voters shall vote for or against any proposal at a bond election by completely selecting the voting target next to either “Yes” or “No,” respectively.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 303, Sec. 41. (AB 1512) Effective January 1, 2026.)
