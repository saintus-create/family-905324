# WAT § 2856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 13. Expenses of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2856

---

## Text

The clerk of the court shall, at least 10 days prior to the date of hearing, give notice thereof by mail to all parties.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
