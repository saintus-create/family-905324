# WAT § 25983

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 3. Segregation Before Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 25983

---

## Text

The board may either:

(a) Confirm, modify, or set aside the same.

(b) Refuse to authorize the segregation, separate valuation, and assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
