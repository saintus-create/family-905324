# WAT § 134.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2. State Administration Generally > Article: 1. Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 134.5

---

## Text

The Director of Finance shall administratively establish positions necessary to implement activities funded by the appropriations made in Division 33 (commencing with Section 83000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 3. Effective March 1, 2009.)
