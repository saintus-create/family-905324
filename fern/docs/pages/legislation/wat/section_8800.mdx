# WAT § 8800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 3. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8800

---

## Text

The notice need not describe the land with particularity, but it is sufficient to describe the tract or tracts of land subdivided as a tract or tracts assessed to the owner or owners giving his name or other names upon the assessment list.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
