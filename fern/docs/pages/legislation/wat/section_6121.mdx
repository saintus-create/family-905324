# WAT § 6121

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 4. Investigations and Studies

**Citation:** WAT § 6121

---

## Text

The department shall also make or cause to be made such watershed investigations and studies as may facilitate its decisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
