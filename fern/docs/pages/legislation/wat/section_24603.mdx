# WAT § 24603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 1. Payment of Claims

**Citation:** WAT § 24603

---

## Text

The officer or employee presenting any claim provided for in Section 24601 shall verify it before the secretary, who is authorized to administer oaths for that purpose, or before any person authorized by law to administer oaths.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
