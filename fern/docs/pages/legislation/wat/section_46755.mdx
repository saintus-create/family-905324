# WAT § 46755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 5. Sale for Nonpayment

**Citation:** WAT § 46755

---

## Text

The date of the sale of assessed property shall not be less than 10 days after the date of the last publication of the notice of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
