# WAT § 35953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Provisions Relating to All Bonds > Article: 1. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 35953

---

## Text

When the treasurer receives the bonds from the board, he shall place them to the credit of the district and keep a record of the bonds, the payment thereof, and the interest thereon, in a book provided for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
