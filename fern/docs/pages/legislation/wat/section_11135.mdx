# WAT § 11135

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Aid From State Agencies or Other Sources

**Citation:** WAT § 11135

---

## Text

Any State agency may advance or contribute money, rights of way, labor, materials, and any other property for the construction, operation, or maintenance of the project or any unit thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
