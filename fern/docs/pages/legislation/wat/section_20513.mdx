# WAT § 20513

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20513

---

## Text

“District” means any irrigation district formed pursuant to any law of this State or to this division except the Palo Verde Irrigation District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
