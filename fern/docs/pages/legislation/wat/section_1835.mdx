# WAT § 1835

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 12. Enforcement of Water Rights > Article: 2. Cease and Desist Orders

**Citation:** WAT § 1835

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “person” includes any city, county, district, the state, or any department or agency thereof, and the United States to the extent authorized by law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 933, Sec. 13.)
