# WAT § 39461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 4. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 39461

---

## Text

The notice shall not be published until five days after the filing of the petition and undertaking in the office of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
