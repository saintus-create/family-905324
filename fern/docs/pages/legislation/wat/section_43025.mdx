# WAT § 43025

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Water and Electricity > Article: 2. Electricity

**Citation:** WAT § 43025

---

## Text

The board may provide for the generation and distribution of hydroelectric energy incidental to water storage and distribution.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
