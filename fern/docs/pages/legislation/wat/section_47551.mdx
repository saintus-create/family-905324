# WAT § 47551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 10. Assessment of Included Land

**Citation:** WAT § 47551

---

## Text

The board shall appoint commissioners to make the assessment in the number and manner provided in Article 1 (commencing with Section 46150) of Chapter 2 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 126. Effective January 1, 1993.)
