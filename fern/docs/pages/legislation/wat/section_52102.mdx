# WAT § 52102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52102

---

## Text

Bonds shall be signed by the president and attested by the county auditor of the principal county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
