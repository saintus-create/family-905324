# WAT § 50140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 4. Damage to Reclamation Works

**Citation:** WAT § 50140

---

## Text

Any person who cuts, injures, or destroys reclamation works in any district, is responsible for all damages occasioned thereby to such works.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
