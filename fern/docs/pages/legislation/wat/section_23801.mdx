# WAT § 23801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2A. Revenue Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23801

---

## Text

Unless otherwise expressly provided in this chapter the provisions of all sections of Part 7 of this division shall be applicable to an improvement district formed pursuant to this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 717.)
