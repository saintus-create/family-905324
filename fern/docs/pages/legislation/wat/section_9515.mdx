# WAT § 9515

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 9. Refunding Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 9515

---

## Text

The refunding bonds, together with interest thereon, shall be payable at the office of the State Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
