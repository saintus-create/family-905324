# WAT § 36392

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4.3. Revenue Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 36392

---

## Text

Revenue warrants shall not be issued under this chapter by any district in any one fiscal year in excess of four million dollars ($4,000,000).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1977, Ch. 1117.)
