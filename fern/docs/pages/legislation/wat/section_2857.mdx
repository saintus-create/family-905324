# WAT § 2857

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 13. Expenses of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2857

---

## Text

If no objection is filed with the court within the prescribed periods the court upon ex parte application of the board shall enter a judgment against the parties in favor of the board in the amounts apportioned to them.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 329.)
