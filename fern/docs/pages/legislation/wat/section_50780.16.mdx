# WAT § 50780.16

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Reclamation District No. 1000

**Citation:** WAT § 50780.16

---

## Text

At the 1995 district election, the board shall designate the seats of the three trustees whose terms expire as “parcel seats.”

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 842, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
