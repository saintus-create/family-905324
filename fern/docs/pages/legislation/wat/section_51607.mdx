# WAT § 51607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 1. Delinquency Sales on Unbonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51607

---

## Text

The owner of any parcel offered for sale to pay a delinquent assessment or installment thereof may designate in writing to the county treasurer prior to the sale the portion he or she wishes first sold if less than the whole.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 322, Sec. 3. Effective August 22, 1994.)
