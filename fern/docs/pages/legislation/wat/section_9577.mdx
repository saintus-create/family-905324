# WAT § 9577

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 4. Disposition of Proceeds

**Citation:** WAT § 9577

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall pay to the owner of the property any surplus remaining after the payment to the State Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
