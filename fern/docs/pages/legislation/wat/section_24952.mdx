# WAT § 24952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Construction Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 24952

---

## Text

The board shall cause any surveys, examinations, and drawings, to be made which will furnish the proper basis for making an estimate of the amount of money necessary to be raised.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
