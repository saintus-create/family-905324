# WAT § 12878.29

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 4. Assessments for Maintenance and Operation

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.29

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be given by publication in at least one newspaper of general circulation in each maintenance area affected for at least once a week for two successive weeks.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1800.)
