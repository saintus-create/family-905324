# WAT § 1778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 2. Joint Occupancy and Use

**Citation:** WAT § 1778

---

## Text

If the water is used or to be used for irrigation or domestic purposes, the pro rata portion of the cost shall be based upon the proportion of the water used by the original and the subsequent users of the works.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
