# WAT § 24000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 24000

---

## Text

At any time before improvement district warrants are issued, the amount of any improvement district assessment on any land, exclusive of interest and the 10 per cent added for anticipated delinquencies, may be paid in money.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
