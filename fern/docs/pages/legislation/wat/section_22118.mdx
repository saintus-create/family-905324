# WAT § 22118

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 3. Electric Power

**Citation:** WAT § 22118

---

## Text

The board and the officers, agents, and employees of districts shall do all necessary and proper acts for the construction and operation of its electric power works.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
