# WAT § 2768

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2768

---

## Text

After the hearing, the court shall enter a decree determining the right of all persons involved in the proceeding.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
