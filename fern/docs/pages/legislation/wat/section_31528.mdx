# WAT § 31528

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 31528

---

## Text

“Right of way” refers to any parcel of land through which a right of way has been granted to the county water district for any purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
