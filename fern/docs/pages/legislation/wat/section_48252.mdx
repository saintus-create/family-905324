# WAT § 48252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 3. Inclusion Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 48252

---

## Text

The board shall fix the time at which the election shall be held and cause notice thereof to be published.
