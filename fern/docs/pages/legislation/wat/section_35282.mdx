# WAT § 35282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Method of Elections for Ramirez Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35282

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed as requiring the board to act by a four-fifths vote on any matter except as expressly provided in this chapter.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1996, Ch. 124, Sec. 157. Effective January 1, 1997.)
