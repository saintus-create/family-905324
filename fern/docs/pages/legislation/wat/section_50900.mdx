# WAT § 50900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 50900

---

## Text

A district may do all things necessary or convenient for accomplishing the purposes for which it was formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
