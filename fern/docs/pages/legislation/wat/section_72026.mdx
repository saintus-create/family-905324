# WAT § 72026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 72026

---

## Text

No irregularities or informalities in conducting the special election shall invalidate it, if the election has otherwise been fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
