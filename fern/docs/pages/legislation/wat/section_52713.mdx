# WAT § 52713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Forms

**Citation:** WAT § 52713

---

## Text

The board may make expenditures, or incur indebtedness, and issue warrants therefor to pay the cost and expenses incident to any refunding plan or in connection with the refunding of its bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 134. Effective January 1, 1993.)
