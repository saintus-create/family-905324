# WAT § 30830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Initiative; Referendum; Recall

**Citation:** WAT § 30830

---

## Text

Ordinances may be passed by voters in accordance with Article 1 (commencing with Section 9100) of Chapter 2 of Division 9 of the Elections Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1994, Ch. 923, Sec. 239. Effective January 1, 1995.)
