# WAT § 31652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31652

---

## Text

A district may cause taxes to be levied to pay the expenses of its formation, including fees of attorneys and others employed to conduct the formation proceedings.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
