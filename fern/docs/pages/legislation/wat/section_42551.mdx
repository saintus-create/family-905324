# WAT § 42551

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Recommendation of Completion of Project > Article: 3. Proceedings After Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42551

---

## Text

The board shall carry out the project of the district in accordance with the plans and specifications of the approved and adopted report of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
