# WAT § 20535

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20535

---

## Text

“Assessee” means the person to whom property is assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
