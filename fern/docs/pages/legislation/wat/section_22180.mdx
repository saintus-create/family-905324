# WAT § 22180

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 6. Sewage Disposal

**Citation:** WAT § 22180

---

## Text

The board and the officers, agents, and employees of districts shall do all necessary and proper acts for the construction and operation of its sewage collection and disposal system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 420.)
