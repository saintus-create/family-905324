# WAT § 9012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 2. Application of Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 9012

---

## Text

When the compensation applied pursuant to the provisions of the next preceding section exceeds the total amount of such credits upon the assessments upon the lands in the district, the excess shall be paid to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
