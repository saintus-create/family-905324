# WAT § 26100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 2. Publication of List of Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26100

---

## Text

When assessments are not payable in installments, the collector shall commence to publish the list of delinquencies on or before the first day of February.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
