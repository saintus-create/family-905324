# WAT § 51424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 7. Calling Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51424

---

## Text

No action contesting the validity of any call of an installment of an assessment pursuant to this article may be commenced, unless commenced within six months from the due date of the call.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 681.)
