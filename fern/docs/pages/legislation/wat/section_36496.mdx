# WAT § 36496

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 4. Formation and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 36496

---

## Text

All lands of the distribution district shall remain jointly liable for all obligations accruing to the United States or accruing to the district under any contract between the distribution district and the United States or any contract between the distribution district and the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
