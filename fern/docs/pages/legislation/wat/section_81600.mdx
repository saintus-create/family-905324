# WAT § 81600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31.7. SAN FRANCISCO BAY AREA REGIONAL WATER SYSTEM FINANCING AUTHORITY > Chapter: 1. General Provisions and Definitions > Article: 1. General Provisions

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the San Francisco Bay Area Regional Water System Financing Authority Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 849, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
