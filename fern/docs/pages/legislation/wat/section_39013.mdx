# WAT § 39013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39013

---

## Text

“Include,” except when used in relation to the inclusion of land into a district, does not necessarily exclude matters not enumerated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
