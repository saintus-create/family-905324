# WAT § 51810

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 1. Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51810

---

## Text

No assessment or part thereof shall be canceled pursuant to this article if bonds based thereon and secured thereby have been issued and remain unpaid and provision has not been made for the payment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
