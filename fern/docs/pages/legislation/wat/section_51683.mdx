# WAT § 51683

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 2. Sale After One Year After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51683

---

## Text

It shall be sufficient to describe the parcel to be sold in the notice by reference number as set forth in the assessment lists to which reference is made, by the date and time of filing for further particulars.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
