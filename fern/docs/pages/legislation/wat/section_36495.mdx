# WAT § 36495

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 4. Formation and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 36495

---

## Text

The assessment, and each installment of it, shall be and remain a lien on the land in the distribution district in the same manner as, and be a part of, the annual assessment of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
