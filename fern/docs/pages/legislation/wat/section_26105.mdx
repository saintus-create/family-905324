# WAT § 26105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 2. Publication of List of Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 26105

---

## Text

The list of delinquencies and notice shall be published once a week for three successive weeks in a newspaper published in the county in which the delinquent property is situated but only information pertaining to and descriptions of land situated in the county need be set forth in the publication in that county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
