# WAT § 50153

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 5. Actions Against Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 50153

---

## Text

Any landowner may maintain an action on behalf of his district in the superior court of the county in which any part of the district is situated against a member of the board and his sureties upon his bond for any improper disbursement from the revolving fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
