# WAT § 45405

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 45405

---

## Text

The treasurer, upon written request of a majority of the directors, shall reject any or all bids.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
