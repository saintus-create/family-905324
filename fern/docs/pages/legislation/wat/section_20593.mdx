# WAT § 20593

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. State Public Land

**Citation:** WAT § 20593

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter creates any obligation against the State to pay any assessments or charges.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
