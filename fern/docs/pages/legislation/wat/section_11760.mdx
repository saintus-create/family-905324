# WAT § 11760

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 6. Disposition of Proceeds from Bond Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 11760

---

## Text

The proceeds from the sale of bonds shall be paid to the Treasurer or the designee of the Treasurer to the credit of the construction fund.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 5. Effective June 6, 1991.)
