# WAT § 37155.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and its Termination

**Citation:** WAT § 37155.3

---

## Text

Upon first publication of the notice required by Section 37155.1, there shall be added to the amount to redeem as provided in Section 37150 the sum of the following:

(a) The cost of publication of the notice required by Section 37155.1.

(b) The cost of securing the lot book guarantee required by Section 37155.2.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 560, Sec. 6. Effective September 11, 1987.)
