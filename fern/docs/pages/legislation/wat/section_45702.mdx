# WAT § 45702

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Additional Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 45702

---

## Text

All provisions of this part relative to the original issue of bonds shall apply to any additional bonds.
