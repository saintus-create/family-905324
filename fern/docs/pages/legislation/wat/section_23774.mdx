# WAT § 23774

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Alternative Procedure for Formation > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23774

---

## Text

Upon the conclusion of the hearing to form an improvement district an order shall be made by the board either dismissing the proceedings or ordering the improvement to proceed in the same manner and with the same effect as provided in Article 4 of Chapter 1 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
