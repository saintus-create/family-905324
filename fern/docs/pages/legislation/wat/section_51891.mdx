# WAT § 51891

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 5. Reapportioning Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51891

---

## Text

If the application for reapportionment is signed by all of the landowners, the reapportionment is final upon entry of the minute order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
