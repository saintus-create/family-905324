# WAT § 4200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 4200

---

## Text

The department may incur such expense and make such expenditures as may be necessary to provide for the administration of any service area and the distribution of water therein.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
