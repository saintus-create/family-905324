# WAT § 25834

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 2. Special Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25834

---

## Text

If a determination that any land is entitled to a special rate of assessment is made in any year too late for the land to be separately described in the assessment book but before the annual assessment has been levied, the board shall direct the secretary to make entries in the assessment book that will enable him to compute the assessments at the special rates determined by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
