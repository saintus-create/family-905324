# WAT § 6006

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 6006

---

## Text

“Alterations,” “repairs,” or either of them, mean only such alterations or repairs as may affect the safety of the dam or reservoir.
