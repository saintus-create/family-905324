# WAT § 20806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 1. Preliminary Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20806

---

## Text

If the board of supervisors determines that the requirements for the formation petition and notice have been complied with, the resolution so determining shall be known as the preliminary formation resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
