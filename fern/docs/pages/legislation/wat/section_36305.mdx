# WAT § 36305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 1. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 36305

---

## Text

The board in its resolution authorizing the issuance of revenue bonds shall fix the method of giving notice of redemption to the holders of revenue bonds to be redeemed and the price or prices at which the revenue bonds shall be subject to redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
