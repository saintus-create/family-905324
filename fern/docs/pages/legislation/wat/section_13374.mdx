# WAT § 13374

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 5.5. Compliance With the Provisions of the Federal Water Pollution Control Act as Amended in 1972

**Citation:** WAT § 13374

---

## Text

The term “waste discharge requirements” as referred to in this division is the equivalent of the term “permits” as used in the Federal Water Pollution Control Act, as amended.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 1256.)
