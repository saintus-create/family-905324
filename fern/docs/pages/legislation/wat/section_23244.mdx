# WAT § 23244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 4. Federal Contract Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 23244

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter relieves any district from its obligation to pay as a district in case of the default of any land unless so provided by the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
