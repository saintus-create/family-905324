# WAT § 45922

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 2. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 45922

---

## Text

Any landowner who desires at any time to lessen or remove the lien upon his land of any direct assessment may deliver to the district treasurer for cancellation warrants payable out of the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
