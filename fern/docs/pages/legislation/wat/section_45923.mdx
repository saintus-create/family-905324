# WAT § 45923

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 2. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 45923

---

## Text

The board may require warrants delivered to lessen or remove an assessment lien to be substantially of the average maturities of the issue of warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
