# WAT § 74526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 74526

---

## Text

A district may sell, deliver, distribute, or otherwise dispose of any water that may be stored or appropriated, owned, or controlled by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
