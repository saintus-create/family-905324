# WAT § 74016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 74016

---

## Text

“Board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of the principal county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
