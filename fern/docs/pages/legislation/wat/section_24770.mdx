# WAT § 24770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Applicable to Bonds and Warrants > Article: 2. Modification of Bonds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24770

---

## Text

Each bond or warrant presented and indorsed shall continue as an obligation of the district and shall not become due until the date specified in the agreement or plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
