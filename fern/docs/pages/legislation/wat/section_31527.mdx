# WAT § 31527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 31527

---

## Text

“Superintendent of streets” or “street superintendent” and “city engineer” refer to the engineer of the county water district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
