# WAT § 53200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cancellation and Reissuance > Article: 1. Permissive

**Citation:** WAT § 53200

---

## Text

The board, at the request of the holder of any warrant, may cancel it and issue two or more warrants in lieu thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
