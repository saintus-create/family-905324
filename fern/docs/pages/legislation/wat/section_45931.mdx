# WAT § 45931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 45931

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of any assessment or warrants may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
