# WAT § 50335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 3. Filing and Recording

**Citation:** WAT § 50335

---

## Text

Districts organized before May 28, 1868, may retain their respective numbers.
