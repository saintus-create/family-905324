# WAT § 13896.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.7. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1986

**Citation:** WAT § 13896.6

---

## Text

First priority for loans shall be given to suppliers with the most critical public health problems. Priority for loans shall also be given to suppliers which have a lesser capability to reasonably finance system improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 410, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 55 at the November 4, 1986, election.)
