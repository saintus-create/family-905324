# WAT § 45801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 6. Callable Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 45801

---

## Text

Bonds may be made subject to redemption prior to their fixed maturity at such times and prices, and upon such terms and conditions as the board may determine and set forth in the resolution.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1967, Ch. 36.)
