# WAT § 37200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 7.5. ALTERNATIVE PROVISIONS FOR LEVY, COLLECTION AND ENFORCEMENT OF DISTRICT ASSESSMENTS BY THE COUNTY > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37200

---

## Text

This part provides an alternative method for the levy, collection and enforcement of district assessments and the provisions of this part shall not apply to or affect any other provisions of this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by renumbering Section 36500 by Stats. 1959, Ch. 614.)
