# WAT § 22338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 5. Supervision by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 22338

---

## Text

The department may at any time make an examination of the affairs of any district or call upon the authorities of the district for any information it desires and make any report thereon it deems advisable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
