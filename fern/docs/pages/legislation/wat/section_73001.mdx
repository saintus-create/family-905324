# WAT § 73001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 12. REPEALS > Chapter: 3. Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 73001

---

## Text

The repeal effected by this part shall not be construed to deprive any district or person or any other entity of any substantial right which would have existed or hereafter exists had such repeal not been effected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
