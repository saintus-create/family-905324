# WAT § 45404

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 45404

---

## Text

At the time appointed, the treasurer shall open the bids and award the bonds to the highest responsible bidder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
