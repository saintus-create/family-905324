# WAT § 51542

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51542

---

## Text

When the operation and maintenance assessment levy is to be paid in more than one installment the secretary shall give notice for 60 days immediately preceding the due date of each installment in substantially the form prescribed in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
