# WAT § 75132

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75132

---

## Text

Lands not included in the original resolution of intention shall not be included within the proposed special improvement district except at a hearing held by the board on the inclusion thereof, after notice of such hearing has been duly given in the manner provided for the principal hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
