# WAT § 22231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 22231

---

## Text

A district by contract may acquire and operate any needed or desirable equipment to put water under its control to any beneficial use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
