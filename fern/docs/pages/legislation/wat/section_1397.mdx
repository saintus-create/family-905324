# WAT § 1397

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 6. Permits > Article: 4. Application to Beneficial Use and Construction of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 1397

---

## Text

The work shall be completed and the water applied to beneficial use in accordance with this division, the rules and regulations of the board, and the terms of the permit and within the period specified in the permit.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
