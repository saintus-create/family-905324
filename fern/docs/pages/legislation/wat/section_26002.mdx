# WAT § 26002

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 4. Modification and Refund of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 26002

---

## Text

No order for a refund under this article shall be made except upon a claim both:

(a) Verified by the person who paid the assessments, penalties, or costs, his guardian, executor, or administrator.

(b) Filed within three years after the making of the payment sought to be refunded.
