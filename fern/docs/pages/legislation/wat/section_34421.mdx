# WAT § 34421

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34421

---

## Text

For the convenience of voters the board may divide the proposed district into election precincts and establish the boundaries of the precincts. In such case the board shall appoint a board of election for each precinct and designate a polling place in each precinct.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
