# WAT § 34580

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Madera Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 34580

---

## Text

The Madera Water District created pursuant to the Madera Water District Act (Chapter 735 of the Statutes of 1987) is hereby continued as a district under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 300, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
