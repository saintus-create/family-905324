# WAT § 52105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52105

---

## Text

Coupons for each installment of interest shall be attached to the bonds and shall bear the facsimile signature of the county auditor of the principal county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
