# WAT § 21575

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 2. Abolition and Reestablishment of Divisions

**Citation:** WAT § 21575

---

## Text

Any district having less than 2,500 acres within its boundaries, having less than 100 electors residing within it, and having only three divisions may abolish the divisions in it pursuant to this article.

Any district having not more than 15 freeholders who are voters shall have no divisions.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1986, Ch. 454, Sec. 2.)
