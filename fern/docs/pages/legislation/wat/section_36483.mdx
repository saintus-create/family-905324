# WAT § 36483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 3. Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 36483

---

## Text

At the hearing the board shall make any changes in reference to matters set forth in Section 36482 as it considers proper. The board may exclude any part of the land described in the petition from the proposed distribution district and may include additional land.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
