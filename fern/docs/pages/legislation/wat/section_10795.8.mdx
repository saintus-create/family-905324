# WAT § 10795.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.78. LOCAL GROUNDWATER MANAGEMENT ASSISTANCE ACT OF 2000 > Chapter: 5. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10795.8

---

## Text

The department may enter into contracts and may adopt regulations subject to the advice and review of the Technical Advisory Panel, to carry out this part. Any grant contract entered into pursuant to this part may include provisions that the department determines are necessary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 708, Sec. 2. Effective January 1, 2001.)
