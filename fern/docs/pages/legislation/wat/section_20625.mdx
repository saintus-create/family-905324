# WAT § 20625

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Information by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 20625

---

## Text

The department shall give information so far as it may be practicable to persons contemplating the formation of districts.
