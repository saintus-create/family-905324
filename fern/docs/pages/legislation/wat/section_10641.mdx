# WAT § 10641

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Urban Water Management Plans > Article: 3. Adoption and Implementation of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 10641

---

## Text

An urban water supplier required to prepare a plan or a water shortage contingency plan may consult with, and obtain comments from, any public agency or state agency or any person who has special expertise with respect to water demand management methods and techniques.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2018, Ch. 14, Sec. 38. (SB 606) Effective January 1, 2019.)
