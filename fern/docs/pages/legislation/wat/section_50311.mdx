# WAT § 50311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition > Article: 2. Approval

**Citation:** WAT § 50311

---

## Text

If it is shown that any land is improperly included in the proposed district, the board of supervisors shall, in its order, exclude the same therefrom.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
