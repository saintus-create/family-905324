# WAT § 51725

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 3. Subdivision and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 51725

---

## Text

The board shall set forth in the reapportionment list the date to which the apportioned interest was computed.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
