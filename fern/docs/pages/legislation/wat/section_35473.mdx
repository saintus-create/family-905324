# WAT § 35473

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 35473

---

## Text

The charges to pay revenue bonds and interest thereon when due shall be fixed by the board before April 1st of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
