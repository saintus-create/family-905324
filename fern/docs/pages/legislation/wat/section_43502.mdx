# WAT § 43502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 43502

---

## Text

No bonds shall be given in payment for property or rights at a valuation less than 90 percent of their face value and accrued interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
