# WAT § 6056

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 3. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6056

---

## Text

The department shall retain a board of three consultants who shall make an independent report to the director upon the issuance, modification, or renewal of any certificate of approval for any dam owned by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 284.)
