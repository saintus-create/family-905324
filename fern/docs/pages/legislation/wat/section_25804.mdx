# WAT § 25804

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 1. Regular Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25804

---

## Text

Annual installments of an improvement district assessment shall be entered in the assessment book by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
