# WAT § 8884

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Sale of Land for Delinquent Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8884

---

## Text

All deeds required by this chapter to be executed by the board may be executed by the president and secretary thereof on behalf of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
