# WAT § 1460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Priorities > Article: 3. Preferred Priorities of Municipalities

**Citation:** WAT § 1460

---

## Text

The application for a permit by a municipality for the use of water for the municipality or the inhabitants thereof for domestic purposes shall be considered first in right, irrespective of whether it is first in time.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
