# WAT § 4329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 6. Alternative Method of Payment by Owners

**Citation:** WAT § 4329

---

## Text

The association shall pay the total amount apportioned to it to the department on or before the first day of December next succeeding the receipt of the statement.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1411.)
