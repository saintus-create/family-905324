# WAT § 48083

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 4. Liability of Excluded Land

**Citation:** WAT § 48083

---

## Text

In case land is excluded from any district, the board may, not less than 60 days before any election in the district, re-establish the boundaries of the divisions within the district.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1963, Ch. 1607.)
