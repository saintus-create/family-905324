# WAT § 72770

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Part: 11.6. Central Basin Municipal Water District > Chapter: 3. Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 72770

---

## Text

Notwithstanding Section 71274, no ordinance, motion, or resolution relating to the ethics, compensation, or benefits of the members of the Central Basin Municipal Water District board of directors shall be passed or become effective without the affirmative votes of two-thirds of the members of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 426, Sec. 2. (SB 953) Effective January 1, 2017.)
