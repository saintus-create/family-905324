# WAT § 51254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51254

---

## Text

The assessment list shall be prepared in duplicate, and one original shall be retained in the office of the district, and the second original shall be filed with the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
