# WAT § 103

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 1. General State Policy

**Citation:** WAT § 103

---

## Text

In the enactment of this code the Legislature does not intend thereby to effect any change in the law relating to water rights.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
