# WAT § 25040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 1. General Refunding Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 25040

---

## Text

Any issue of refunding bonds may in the discretion of the board mature serially or at one time.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
