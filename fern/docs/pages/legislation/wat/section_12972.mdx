# WAT § 12972

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 8. WATER AGENCY RECREATIONAL FACILITIES > Chapter: 9. Saline Water Conversion > Article: 3. State Participation

**Citation:** WAT § 12972

---

## Text

A water agency may expend funds on such recreational facilities and may fix and assess reasonable charges for the use of such recreational facilities by members of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 214.)
