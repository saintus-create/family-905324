# WAT § 71013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 71013

---

## Text

“District” means a municipal water district formed pursuant to the Municipal Water District Act of 1911 or this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
