# WAT § 30527

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 30527

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances passed by the board shall be: “Be it ordained by the board of directors of __________ County Water District as follows:”

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
