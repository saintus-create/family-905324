# WAT § 34403

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 1. Calling the Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34403

---

## Text

The board shall at the time of calling the formation election for the proposed district, prescribe the procedure for nominating candidates for the offices to be filled at that election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1676.)
