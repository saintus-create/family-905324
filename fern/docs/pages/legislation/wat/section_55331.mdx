# WAT § 55331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 55331

---

## Text

The district may do all things necessary or proper to accomplish the purposes of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
