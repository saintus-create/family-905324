# WAT § 31611

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Formation and Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 31611

---

## Text

After the formation of an improvement district, additional lands may be included therein upon the written petition of the owners thereof and a resolution of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
