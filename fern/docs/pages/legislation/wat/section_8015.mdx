# WAT § 8015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 2. Preliminary Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 8015

---

## Text

The city council, by the same resolution or ordinance, shall determine, if so advised, that the cost of the works will be too great to be paid out of the ordinary income or revenue of the city.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
