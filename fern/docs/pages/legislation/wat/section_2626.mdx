# WAT § 2626

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 6. Objections to the Report

**Citation:** WAT § 2626

---

## Text

A representative of the board shall be present at the time and place designated in the notice, and shall permit, during the period of inspection, any person interested to inspect such evidence and proofs of claim as have been filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
