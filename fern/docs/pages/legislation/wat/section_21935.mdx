# WAT § 21935

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Bond Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 21935

---

## Text

No informalities in the conduct of the election shall invalidate the election if fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
