# WAT § 410

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 4. Weather Resources Management > Article: 2. Notice of Intention

**Citation:** WAT § 410

---

## Text

Before commencing a weather resources management project, the project operator shall file with the department and cause to be published a notice of intention.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 950, Sec. 2.)
