# WAT § 72301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Inclusion > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 72301

---

## Text

The cities need not be contiguous, and the unincorporated territory, inhabited or uninhabited, may consist of one or more parcels which need not be contiguous to each other or to any such cities. The annexing area or areas need not be contiguous to the district.
