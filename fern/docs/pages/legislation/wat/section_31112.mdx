# WAT § 31112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 6.5. Sewer and Garbage Facilities in the Calaveras County Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 31112

---

## Text

The Board of Directors of the Calaveras County Water District may levy and collect taxes in the manner provided in this division for the purpose of the maintenance and operation of any facilities constructed pursuant to Section 31110.

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 440.)
