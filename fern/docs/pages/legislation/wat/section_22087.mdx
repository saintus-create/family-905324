# WAT § 22087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1.5. Watermaster Service

**Citation:** WAT § 22087

---

## Text

Any person who is injured by the action of any watermaster may apply to the superior court of the county in which the injury takes place for an injunction.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 780, Sec. 1.)
