# WAT § 11780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 8. Retirement of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 11780

---

## Text

Any surplus which exists in the construction fund shall be applied to the retirement of bonds issued for construction of the project by purchase or call.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
