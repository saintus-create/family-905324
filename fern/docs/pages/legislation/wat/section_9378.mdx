# WAT § 9378

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 3. Voting Lists and Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 9378

---

## Text

A ballot cast by proxy shall contain the name of the landowner for whom the ballot is cast and the signature of the person casting the vote as proxy.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
