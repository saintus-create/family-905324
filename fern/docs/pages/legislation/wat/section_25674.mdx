# WAT § 25674

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 2. Completion Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25674

---

## Text

The notice of the election shall specify both:

(a) The day, hours, and polling places in each precinct for holding the election.

(b) The amount of assessment proposed to be levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
