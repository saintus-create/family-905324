# WAT § 30203

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30203

---

## Text

But, if one or more municipal corporations or part thereof is included in the proposed district, the petition shall be signed by at least 10 percent of the voters of each municipal corporation or part thereof and of the unincorporated territory included in the proposed district voting at the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
