# WAT § 75973

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Annexation of Districts > Article: 3. Procedure in Annexing District

**Citation:** WAT § 75973

---

## Text

If a majority of all votes cast at the election are against the proposed annexation, the annexation petition shall be denied. If a majority of all votes cast are in favor of the proposed annexation, the board of the annexing district shall thereupon adopt an order annexing the petitioning district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
