# WAT § 71638.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water > Article: 2.5. Alternative Provisions for Standby Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 71638.2

---

## Text

The resolution fixing a standby assessment or availability charge may establish schedules varying the charges according to land uses, water uses, and degree of water availability, subject to the maximum charges provided in Section 71631.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 139.)
