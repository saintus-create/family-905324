# WAT § 11591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 6. Acquisition and Disposal of Property > Article: 3. Acquiring Facilities of Public Utilities and State Agencies

## Text

The expense of the department in complying with the requirements of this article is part of the cost of constructing the project.
