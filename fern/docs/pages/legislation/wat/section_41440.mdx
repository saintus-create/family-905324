# WAT § 41440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 6. Results of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 41440

---

## Text

The person receiving the highest number of votes for each office to be filled at the election shall be elected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
