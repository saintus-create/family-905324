# WAT § 11842

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 6. Investment and Use of Surplus Money

**Citation:** WAT § 11842

---

## Text

If the proceedings authorizing the issuance of bonds do not require surplus revenues to be held or applied in any particular manner, they shall be allocated and used for such other purposes incidental to the construction, operation, and maintenance of the project and for making necessary replacements thereto as the department may determine.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
