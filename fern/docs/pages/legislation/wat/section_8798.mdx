# WAT § 8798

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 3. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 8798

---

## Text

Before the reapportionment becomes final, the board shall appoint a time when it will meet for the purpose of hearing objections to the reapportionment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
