# WAT § 12929.46

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7.7. Environmental Water Act of 1989 > Article: 5. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12929.46

---

## Text

All loan repayments received pursuant to this chapter shall be deposited in the fund from which the loan was made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1989, Ch. 715, Sec. 1.)
