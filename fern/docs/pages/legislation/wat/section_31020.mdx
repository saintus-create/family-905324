# WAT § 31020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 31020

---

## Text

A district may do any act necessary to furnish sufficient water in the district for any present or future beneficial use.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
