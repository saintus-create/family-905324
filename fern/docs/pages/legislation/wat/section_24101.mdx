# WAT § 24101

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Dissolution of Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 24101

---

## Text

A hearing on dissolution shall be had in the same manner and after the same notice as is required for the formation of an improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
