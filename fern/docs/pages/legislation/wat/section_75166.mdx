# WAT § 75166

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 6. Alternative Provisions for Government of Special Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 75166

---

## Text

The salary of the secretary and the amount of the bond to be given for the faithful performance of his duties shall be fixed by the board of trustees.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1651.)
