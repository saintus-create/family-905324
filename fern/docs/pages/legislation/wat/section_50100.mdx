# WAT § 50100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 1. Applicability of Division

**Citation:** WAT § 50100

---

## Text

Nothing in this division impairs the validity of any district formed prior to the effective date of this division, its rights, or obligations.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
