# WAT § 11419

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Department of Water Resources > Article: 1. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11419

---

## Text

The department shall keep full and complete accounts concerning all matters and things relating to the project and annually shall prepare balance sheet and income and profit and loss statements showing the financial condition of the project.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
