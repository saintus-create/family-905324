# WAT § 10952

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.12. Recycled Water Usage by Car Washes > Chapter: 3. Groundwater Monitoring Program

**Citation:** WAT § 10952

---

## Text

This part shall not apply to a self-service car wash.

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 545, Sec. 2. (AB 2230) Effective January 1, 2013.)
