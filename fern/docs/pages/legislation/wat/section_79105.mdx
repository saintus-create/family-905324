# WAT § 79105

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 7. Clean Water and Water Recycling Program > Article: 1. Clean Water and Water Recycling Account

**Citation:** WAT § 79105

---

## Text

For the purposes of this chapter, “account” means the Clean Water and Water Recycling Account created by Section 79106.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
