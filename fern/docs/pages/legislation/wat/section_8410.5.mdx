# WAT § 8410.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Cobey-Alquist Flood Plain Management Act > Article: 4. Establishment of Flood Plain Regulations

**Citation:** WAT § 8410.5

---

## Text

All public agencies shall comply with flood plain regulations established to meet the requirements of Section 8411. For the purposes of this section, “public agencies” includes the state or any department or agency thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 410.)
