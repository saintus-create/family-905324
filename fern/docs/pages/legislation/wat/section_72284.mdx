# WAT § 72284

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Automatic Exclusion of Cities

**Citation:** WAT § 72284

---

## Text

From and after the date of the Secretary of State’s certificate, the area described therein is excluded from, and no longer forms a part of, the municipal water district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
