# WAT § 12953

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 7. PROJECT COSTS FOR LOCAL GOVERNMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9. Saline Water Conversion > Article: 3. State Participation

**Citation:** WAT § 12953

---

## Text

For the purposes of this part the Department of Water Resources shall designate the site of principal construction of every water construction project undertaken by the State, and the date construction was started.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 464.)
