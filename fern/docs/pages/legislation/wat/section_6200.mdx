# WAT § 6200

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 1. New Dams and Reservoirs or Enlargements of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6200

---

## Text

Construction of any new dam or reservoir or the enlargement of any dam or reservoir shall not be commenced until the owner has applied for and obtained from the department written approval of plans and specifications.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
