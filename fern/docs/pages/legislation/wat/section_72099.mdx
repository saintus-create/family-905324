# WAT § 72099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Taxes Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 72099

---

## Text

Liens for district taxes, whether for payment of a bonded indebtedness and the interest thereon or for other purposes, shall be of the same force and effect as other liens for taxes, and their collection may be enforced by the same means as provided for the enforcement of liens for state and county taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
