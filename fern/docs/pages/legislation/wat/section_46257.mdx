# WAT § 46257

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 5. Approval of Assessment Roll

**Citation:** WAT § 46257

---

## Text

The assessment roll as approved by the adjustment board shall be conclusive evidence before any court or tribunal that the assessment has been made and levied according to law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
