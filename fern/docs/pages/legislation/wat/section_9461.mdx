# WAT § 9461

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 5. Payment of Principal, Interest and Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 9461

---

## Text

The indorsed bond or coupon shall bear interest at the rate expressed in the bond until paid or until funds have been provided in the State Treasury applicable to its payment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
