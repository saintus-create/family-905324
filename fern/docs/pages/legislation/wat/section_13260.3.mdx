# WAT § 13260.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 4. Regional Water Quality Control > Article: 4. Waste Discharge Requirements

**Citation:** WAT § 13260.3

---

## Text

On or before January 1 of each year, the state board shall report to the Governor and the Legislature on the expenditure of annual fees collected pursuant to Section 13260.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 5. Effective October 28, 2003.)
