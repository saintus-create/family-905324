# WAT § 11662

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Contracts > Article: 3. Additional Provisions Relating to Contracts with State Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 11662

---

## Text

Any State agency may comply with the terms, provisions, and conditions of any contract entered into by it pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
