# WAT § 14018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 16. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 2. California Safe Drinking Water Program

**Citation:** WAT § 14018

---

## Text

First priority for loans shall be given to suppliers with the most critical public health problems. Priority for loans shall also be given to suppliers which have a lesser capability to reasonably finance system improvements.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 45, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 81 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
