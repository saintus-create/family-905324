# WAT § 47552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 10. Assessment of Included Land

**Citation:** WAT § 47552

---

## Text

The assessment shall be made in the same manner and with the same incidents as an original assessment under Chapter 2 of this part, and all of the provisions of this division as to assessments shall, so far as they are applicable, apply to such assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
