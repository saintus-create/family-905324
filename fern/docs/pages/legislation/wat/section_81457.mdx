# WAT § 81457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 7. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 81457

---

## Text

Membership of a public entity in the agency does not affect the identity or legal existence, nor impair the powers, of that public entity.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
