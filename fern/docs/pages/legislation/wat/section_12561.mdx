# WAT § 12561

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. Colorado River Management Program

**Citation:** WAT § 12561

---

## Text

There is hereby created the Colorado River Management Account in the General Fund. Moneys in the account are available, upon appropriation by the Legislature, for use in accordance with this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2008, Ch. 760, Sec. 22. Effective September 30, 2008.)
