# WAT § 1221

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1.7. Areas of Origin

**Citation:** WAT § 1221

---

## Text

This article shall not be construed to authorize the board to regulate groundwater in any manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1655, Sec. 2.)
