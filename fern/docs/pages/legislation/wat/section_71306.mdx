# WAT § 71306

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71306

---

## Text

The board may require and fix the amount of all official bonds necessary for the protection of the funds and property of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
