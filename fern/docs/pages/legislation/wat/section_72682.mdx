# WAT § 72682

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Inclusion of Territory > Article: 1. Inclusion Proceedings Initiated by Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 72682

---

## Text

No more than five of the names attached to the petition for annexation need appear in the publication of the petition and notice, but the number of signers shall be stated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
