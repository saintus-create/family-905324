# WAT § 21146

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 3. Official Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 21146

---

## Text

Each officer herein named shall be liable upon his bond for his wilful violation of any express duty.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
