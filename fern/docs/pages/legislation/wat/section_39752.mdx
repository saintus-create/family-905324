# WAT § 39752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Hearing on Formation > Article: 1. Preliminary Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 39752

---

## Text

The hearing may be adjourned from time to time, not exceeding 30 days in all.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
