# WAT § 40330

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 2. Official Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 40330

---

## Text

The amount of the bond to be given by the secretary for the faithful performance of his duties shall be fixed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
