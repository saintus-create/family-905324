# WAT § 12910

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. California Water Fund > Article: 2. The California Water Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12910

---

## Text

The California Water Fund is created as a special fund in the State Treasury, the moneys, securities, and increment of which shall be held by the State Treasurer and shall be expended upon projects when appropriated by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 140.)
