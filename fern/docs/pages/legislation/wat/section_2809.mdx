# WAT § 2809

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 11. Proceedings when Appropriation Incomplete at Time of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2809

---

## Text

The clerk of the court shall post notice of hearing of the motion at the courthouse where the hearing is to be held and at least 10 days prior to the hearing.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
