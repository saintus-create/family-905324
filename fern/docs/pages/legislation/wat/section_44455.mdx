# WAT § 44455

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 3. Payment of Claims

**Citation:** WAT § 44455

---

## Text

All claims against the district shall be paid by warrants of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
