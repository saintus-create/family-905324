# WAT § 75636

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 6. United Water Conservation District

**Citation:** WAT § 75636

---

## Text

In accordance with Section 75614, when an operator provides notice to the district of the abandonment of a water-producing facility or a water-producing facility is in fact abandoned, the district may affix seals to the facility.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 294, Sec. 1. (AB 552) Effective January 1, 2018.)
