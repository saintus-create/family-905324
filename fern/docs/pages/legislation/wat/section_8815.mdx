# WAT § 8815

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 4. Assessment Lists

**Citation:** WAT § 8815

---

## Text

No mistake in the name of the owner, or supposed owner, of any real estate invalidates the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
