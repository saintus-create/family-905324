# WAT § 12886.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 7. State Participation in Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 12886.6

---

## Text

The department is authorized to make loans and grants to public agencies pursuant to the provisions of this chapter for projects in which the department participates under this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 27.)
