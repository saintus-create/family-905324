# WAT § 74554

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Property

**Citation:** WAT § 74554

---

## Text

The right of eminent domain shall not extend to any property used for, or dedicated to, cemetery purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
