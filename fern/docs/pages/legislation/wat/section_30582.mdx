# WAT § 30582

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board and Officer Activities

**Citation:** WAT § 30582

---

## Text

If the board appoints an auditor or treasurer pursuant to Section 30540, the auditor or treasurer shall install and maintain a system of auditing and accounting that shall completely and at all times show the financial condition of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 454, Sec. 8.)
