# WAT § 8864

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8864

---

## Text

The notice shall also state that each of the parcels will be sold at public auction by the county treasurer in front of the courthouse of the county at a specified day and hour, to pay the installment with accrued interest and the penalty, together with its proportion of the total cost of publication of the notice of sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
