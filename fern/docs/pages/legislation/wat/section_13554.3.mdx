# WAT § 13554.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 7. Water Reclamation > Article: 7. Water Reuse

**Citation:** WAT § 13554.3

---

## Text

The State Water Resources Control Board may establish a reasonable schedule of fees by which it is reimbursed for the costs it incurs pursuant to Sections 13553 and 13554.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1991, Ch. 723, Sec. 4.)
