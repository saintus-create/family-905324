# WAT § 8965

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8965

---

## Text

After the last publication of the notice the warrants therein mentioned cease to bear interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
