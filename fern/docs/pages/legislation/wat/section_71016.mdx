# WAT § 71016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 71016

---

## Text

“Affected county” means any county in which the land of a district, or proposed district, is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
