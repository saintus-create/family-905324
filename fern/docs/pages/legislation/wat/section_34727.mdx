# WAT § 34727

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 2. Consolidation and Segregation

**Citation:** WAT § 34727

---

## Text

The board may, at least 90 days before a general district election, segregate any offices which have previously been consolidated.
