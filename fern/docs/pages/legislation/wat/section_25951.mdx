# WAT § 25951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 2. Installments

**Citation:** WAT § 25951

---

## Text

The two installments shall be equal unless the resolution specifies different percentages to be paid in each installment, in which case the installments shall be payable as specified in the resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
