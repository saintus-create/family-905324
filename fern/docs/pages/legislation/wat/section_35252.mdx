# WAT § 35252

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9.3. Method of Election for the Riverview Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35252

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply only to the Riverview Water District. For purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Riverview Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 833, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 1996.)
