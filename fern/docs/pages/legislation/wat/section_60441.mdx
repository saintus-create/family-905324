# WAT § 60441

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60441

---

## Text

Twenty days from and after the holding of the election, in case a majority of said votes were cast in favor of said disincorporation, said district shall be forever disincorporated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
