# WAT § 53244

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cancellation and Reissuance > Article: 3. Lost, Destroyed, Mutilated or Defaced Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53244

---

## Text

The bond of indemnification shall be executed jointly and severally by the holder and a corporate surety approved by the board and duly authorized to do business, and doing business, in the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
