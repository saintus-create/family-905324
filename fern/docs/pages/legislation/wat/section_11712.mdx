# WAT § 11712

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11712

---

## Text

The purchase price of bonds shall be paid for the account of the department to the Treasurer or the designee of the Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 3. Effective June 6, 1991.)
