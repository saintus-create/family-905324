# WAT § 8360

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Maintenance and Operation at Sacramento River Flood Control Project > Article: 2. Powers and Duties of Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8360

---

## Text

On behalf of the State the department has supervisory powers over the maintenance and operation of the flood control works of the Sacramento River Flood Control Project.
