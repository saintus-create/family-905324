# WAT § 41332

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 41332

---

## Text

Each ballot cast shall contain the number of votes it represents, which number shall be written or stamped upon it by an election officer and initialed by him when handed to the voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
