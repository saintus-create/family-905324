# WAT § 71670.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Other Functions > Article: 2. Sewage, Waste, and Storm Water Disposal

**Citation:** WAT § 71670.5

---

## Text

A district may supply sewage and waste services to property not subject to district taxes at special rates, terms and conditions as are determined by the board for such services.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1864.)
