# WAT § 51329

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51329

---

## Text

Upon receipt of the operation and maintenance assessment roll the board of supervisors shall appoint a time when it will meet for the purpose of hearing objections thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
