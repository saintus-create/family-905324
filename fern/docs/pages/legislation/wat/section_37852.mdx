# WAT § 37852

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Operation of Surviving District

**Citation:** WAT § 37852

---

## Text

After the merger, no action or proceeding shall be brought for or against the water storage district or its directors or officers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 746.)
