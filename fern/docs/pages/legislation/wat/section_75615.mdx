# WAT § 75615

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Procedure for Levy and Collection > Article: 4. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 75615

---

## Text

If any operator of a water-producing facility fails to pay the ground water charge when due, the district shall charge interest at the rate of one percent (1%) each month on the delinquent amount of the ground water charge.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
