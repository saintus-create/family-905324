# WAT § 24738

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Provisions Applicable to Bonds and Warrants > Article: 1. Surrender of Bonds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 24738

---

## Text

This article does not restrict any other method of releasing obligations of a district to it.
