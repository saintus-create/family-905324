# WAT § 9019

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 2. Application of Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 9019

---

## Text

The county treasurer, or other officer having the custody of the funds of the district shall comply with such directions and transfer to the proper fund the necessary amount therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
