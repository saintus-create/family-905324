# WAT § 35048

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Elections Generally > Article: 3. Alternative Method of Conducting Elections

**Citation:** WAT § 35048

---

## Text

The petition shall be filed with the county elections official of the principal county at least six months prior to the date of the next general district election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2002, Ch. 221, Sec. 163. Effective January 1, 2003.)
