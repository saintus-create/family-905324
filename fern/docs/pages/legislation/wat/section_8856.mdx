# WAT § 8856

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8856

---

## Text

All assessments not paid in full within the period of 30 days shall bear interest at the rate of 7 per cent per annum from and after the time when the assessment list is filed in the office of the county treasurer for collection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
