# WAT § 60186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Officers and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 60186

---

## Text

The attorney shall be the legal adviser of the district and shall perform such other duties as may be prescribed by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
