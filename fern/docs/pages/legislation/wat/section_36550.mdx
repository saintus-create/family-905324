# WAT § 36550

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Annual Estimate

**Citation:** WAT § 36550

---

## Text

As used in this part “board of supervisors” means the board of supervisors of the principal county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
