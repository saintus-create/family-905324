# WAT § 1729

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 1. Temporary Changes

**Citation:** WAT § 1729

---

## Text

A proposed temporary change under this article shall be exempt from the requirements of Division 13 (commencing with Section 21000) of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1145, Sec. 3.)
