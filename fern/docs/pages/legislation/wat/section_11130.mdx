# WAT § 11130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 3. Use of State Property

**Citation:** WAT § 11130

---

## Text

The State hereby consents to the use and occupation of any real property now or hereafter owned by it, and not dedicated to public use, necessary for the construction, operation, or maintenance of the project, including land of the State lying beneath any navigable waters of the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
