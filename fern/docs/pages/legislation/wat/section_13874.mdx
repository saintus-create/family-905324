# WAT § 13874

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 10.5. California Safe Drinking Water Bond Law of 1976

**Citation:** WAT § 13874

---

## Text

The committee may authorize the State Treasurer to sell all or any part of the bonds herein authorized at such time or times as may be fixed by the State Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1008. Approved in Proposition 3 at the June 8, 1976, election.)
