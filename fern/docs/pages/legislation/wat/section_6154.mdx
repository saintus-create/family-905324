# WAT § 6154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 5. Action and Procedure to Restrain Violations

**Citation:** WAT § 6154

---

## Text

The court shall specify a time, not exceeding 20 days after the service of the copy of the petition, within which the owner or person complained of shall answer the petition, and in the meantime the owner or person may be restrained.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
