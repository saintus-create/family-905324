# WAT § 39021

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39021

---

## Text

“Property,” except in Part 9, embraces all real and personal property, including water, water rights, works, franchises, concessions and rights.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
