# WAT § 6266

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 4. Approval of Applications

**Citation:** WAT § 6266

---

## Text

The department may, upon written application and for good cause shown, extend the time for commencing construction.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
