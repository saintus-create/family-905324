# WAT § 36502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion and Exclusion of Land > Article: 1. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 36502

---

## Text

The same proceedings shall be had on the distribution district inclusion petition as upon the original petition for the formation of a distribution district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
