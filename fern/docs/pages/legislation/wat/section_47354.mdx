# WAT § 47354

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 8. Assessments Upon Which Bonds Have Been Issued > Article: 3. Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 47354

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall execute to each purchaser, including himself as trustee, a certificate of sale, and shall record a duplicate in the county recorder’s office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
