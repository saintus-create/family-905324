# WAT § 23877

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Further Assessments and Inclusion > Article: 3. Inclusion of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 23877

---

## Text

The same proceedings shall be had on the improvement district inclusion petition as upon the original petition for the formation of an improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
