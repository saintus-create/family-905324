# WAT § 20931

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 3. Contest of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20931

---

## Text

No contest shall be brought more than 20 days after the canvass of the vote of the election by the board of supervisors.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
