# WAT § 75131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Special Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 75131

---

## Text

The board may exclude from the proposed special improvement district any territory that, in its opinion, would not be benefited by being in the proposed special improvement district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
