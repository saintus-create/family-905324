# WAT § 34018

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 34018

---

## Text

Except as otherwise provided, “board” means the board of directors of a district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
