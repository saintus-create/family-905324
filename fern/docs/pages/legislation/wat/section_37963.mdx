# WAT § 37963

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Groundwater Extraction Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 37963

---

## Text

All moneys collected by the district pursuant to this act shall be available for expenditure by the district to carry out its groundwater management functions pursuant to this act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 350, Sec. 1. (AB 2453) Effective January 1, 2015.)
