# WAT § 75861

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 2. Petition and Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 75861

---

## Text

Each signature to the petition for inclusion of lands shall be acknowledged or proved as provided by law for signatures to an instrument to entitle it to be recorded.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
