# WAT § 6227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 5. Applications > Article: 2. Repairs, Alterations or Removals

**Citation:** WAT § 6227

---

## Text

The application shall state the proposed time of commencement and of completion of construction.
