# WAT § 1341

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 5. Proceedings on Application > Article: 1. Notice of Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 1341

---

## Text

The notice of hearing on a protested application shall state the names of the applicant and protestant, the time and place fixed for the hearing, and such other appropriate information as may be deemed advisable by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
