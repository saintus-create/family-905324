# WAT § 25556

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 3. Equalization

**Citation:** WAT § 25556

---

## Text

The board acting as a board of equalization shall continue in session from time to time as long as may be necessary but not to exceed 10 days exclusive of Sundays.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
