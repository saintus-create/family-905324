# WAT § 51870

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 4. Erroneous Assessments and Invalid Calls

**Citation:** WAT § 51870

---

## Text

Any assessment erroneously made by reason of inadvertence or clerical mistake may be refunded upon order of the board at any time after payment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
