# WAT § 75862

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 2. Petition and Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 75862

---

## Text

The secretary shall cause a notice of the filing of the petition for inclusion of lands to be given and published in the same manner and for the same time as notices of elections for the issuance of bonds are required to be given and published.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
