# WAT § 31155

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation > Article: 1. Cooperation Under Irrigation District Federal Cooperation Law

**Citation:** WAT § 31155

---

## Text

In any contract made between a county water district and the United States pursuant to this article, the land which may be charged with any taxes or assessments under the contract shall be designated and described.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
