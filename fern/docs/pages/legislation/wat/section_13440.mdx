# WAT § 13440

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 6. State Financial Assistance > Article: 3. State Water Pollution Cleanup and Abatement Account

**Citation:** WAT § 13440

---

## Text

There is in the State Water Quality Control Fund the State Water Pollution Cleanup and Abatement Account (hereinafter called the “account”), to be administered by the state board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
