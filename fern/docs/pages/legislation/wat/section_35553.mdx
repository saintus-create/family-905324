# WAT § 35553

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.75. Provisions Pertaining Only to Lost Hills Water District > Article: 1. Plan of Water Allocation

**Citation:** WAT § 35553

---

## Text

In the event the plan is approved, either according to the provisions of Section 35550 or Section 35552, a certified copy of the board’s resolution approving the plan shall be recorded in the office of the County Recorder of the County of Kern following which recording such plan shall be effective for all purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 63.)
