# WAT § 75212

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Benefit Assessment Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Assessment and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 75212

---

## Text

The improvement district assessment, or any installment thereof, shall be and remain a lien on the lands in the same manner as, and shall be a part of, the annual district assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
