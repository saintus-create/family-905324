# WAT § 23241

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 4. Federal Contract Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 23241

---

## Text

The district by its board shall annually levy an assessment sufficient to raise the money necessary to meet all payments when due as provided in the contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
