# WAT § 22085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1.5. Watermaster Service

**Citation:** WAT § 22085

---

## Text

The district may make such reasonable regulations to secure distribution of water in accordance with determined rights as may be needed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 780, Sec. 1.)
