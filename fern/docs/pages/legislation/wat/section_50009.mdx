# WAT § 50009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 50009

---

## Text

“Principal county” means the county in which the whole or the greater portion of the land of a district is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
