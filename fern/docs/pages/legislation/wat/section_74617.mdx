# WAT § 74617

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts With Other Agencies > Article: 3. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 74617

---

## Text

A contract entered into between a district and the United States may provide that the district shall not be dissolved, nor shall the boundaries be changed except upon the written consent of an official of the United States filed with the official records of the district. If such consent is given and the lands excluded, the area excluded shall be free from all liens and charges for payments to become due to the United States under any such contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
