# WAT § 31106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 6. Sewer Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 31106

---

## Text

From and after the posting or publication of any ordinance as provided in Section 31105 above, it shall be a misdemeanor for any person to violate any ordinance of the district adopted pursuant to Section 31105.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1970, Ch. 930.)
