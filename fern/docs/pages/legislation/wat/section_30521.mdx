# WAT § 30521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 30521

---

## Text

The board shall:

(a) Provide for the time and place of holding its regular meetings.

(b) Provide for the manner of calling special meetings.
