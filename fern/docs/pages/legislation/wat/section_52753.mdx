# WAT § 52753

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 52753

---

## Text

Any or all proposals may be rejected by the county treasurer, and shall be rejected by him if requested to do so in writing by a majority of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
