# WAT § 48087

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 4. Liability of Excluded Land

**Citation:** WAT § 48087

---

## Text

The provisions of this article shall not apply to any outstanding bonds, the holders of which have assented to the exclusion of the land from the district, as provided in Article 3 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
