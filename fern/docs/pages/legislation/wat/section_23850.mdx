# WAT § 23850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Further Assessments and Inclusion > Article: 1. Supplemental Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 23850

---

## Text

If the assessments levied upon land in an improvement district are insufficient to pay the cost of improvements or the warrants issued for the improvements, a supplemental assessment shall be levied upon all of the land in the improvement district sufficient to pay the cost or the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
