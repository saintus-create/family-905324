# WAT § 25067

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 2. Refunding Bond Sinking Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 25067

---

## Text

No proposal to sell bonds to the district pursuant to this article at a price in excess of their par value shall be accepted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
