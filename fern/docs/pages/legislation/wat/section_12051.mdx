# WAT § 12051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 4.3. WASHOE PROJECT WATER SUPPLY CONTRACTS > Chapter: 11. Water Conservation and Reclamation Projects > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** WAT § 12051

---

## Text

The Legislature hereby declares that the purpose of this part is to enable the acquisition of water supplies from the Washoe Project on the Truckee and Carson Rivers or their tributaries for beneficial use in California.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 42.)
