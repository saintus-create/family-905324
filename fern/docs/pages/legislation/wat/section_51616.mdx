# WAT § 51616

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 1. Delinquency Sales on Unbonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51616

---

## Text

The deed shall convey the parcel free of all liens and encumbrances excepting any prior or subsequent district assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
