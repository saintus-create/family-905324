# WAT § 41415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 5. Canvass

**Citation:** WAT § 41415

---

## Text

The board shall meet at its usual place of meeting on the first Monday after each election to canvass the returns.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
