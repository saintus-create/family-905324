# WAT § 8663

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 4. Lease and Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 8663

---

## Text

The board, before ordering the lease of any property pursuant to the next preceding section, shall, in a regular open meeting, adopt a resolution declaring their intention to lease the property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
