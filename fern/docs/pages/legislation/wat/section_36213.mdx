# WAT § 36213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 4. Destruction and Redemption of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36213

---

## Text

The amount of the general obligation bonds destroyed shall be deducted from the total amount authorized to be issued and no part of the bonds destroyed shall thereafter be reprinted or reissued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
