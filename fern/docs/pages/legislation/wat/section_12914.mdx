# WAT § 12914

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 7. California Water Fund > Article: 2. The California Water Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 12914

---

## Text

All of the assets in the fund shall be retained in the State Treasury in the fund and shall not be expended except upon appropriations made by the Legislature.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 140.)
