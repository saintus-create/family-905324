# WAT § 71303

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 2. Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 71303

---

## Text

The board may fix the location of the principal place of business of the district and the location of all offices and departments maintained under this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
