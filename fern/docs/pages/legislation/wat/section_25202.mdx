# WAT § 25202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25202

---

## Text

An issue of bonds means all of the bonds issued in accordance with a single proposal approved at an election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
