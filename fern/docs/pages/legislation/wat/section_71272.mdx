# WAT § 71272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71272

---

## Text

At its first meeting, the board shall provide for the time and place of holding its meetings and the manner in which its special meetings may be called.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
