# WAT § 12619

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. Investigation of Projects > Article: 5. Investigation of Water Development Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 12619

---

## Text

Reports and recommendations shall be submitted by the department to each county, city, state agency, or public district which has appeared at any hearing held pursuant to Section 12623.
