# WAT § 22651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 22651

---

## Text

In all actions or proceedings the board may sue, appear, and defend in person or by attorneys and in the name of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
