# WAT § 26276

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 3. Deed on Termination of Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26276

---

## Text

Collector’s deeds shall contain all of the following:

(a) Date of the sale.

(b) Statement that no person redeemed the property during the time allowed for its redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
