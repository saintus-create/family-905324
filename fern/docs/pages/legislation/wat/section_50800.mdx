# WAT § 50800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Special Elections > Article: 1. Calling

**Citation:** WAT § 50800

---

## Text

A special election is an election called pursuant to the provisions of this part to submit to the landowners the question of whether or not bonds or refunding bonds shall be issued.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
