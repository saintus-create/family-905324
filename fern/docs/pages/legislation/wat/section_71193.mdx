# WAT § 71193

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Establishment of District

**Citation:** WAT § 71193

---

## Text

A copy of the Secretary of State’s certificate shall be transmitted to and filed with the county clerk of each affected county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
