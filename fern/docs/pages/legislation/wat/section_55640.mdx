# WAT § 55640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 5.5. Bonds as Legal Investments

**Citation:** WAT § 55640

---

## Text

The bonds of the district or those issued for any zone pursuant to this act are entitled to the same force or value or use as bonds issued by any municipality.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 406.)
