# WAT § 31444

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 5. Refunding

**Citation:** WAT § 31444

---

## Text

Refunding bonds may, if the holders of bonds of an original issue and the board so agree, be exchanged for original bonds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
