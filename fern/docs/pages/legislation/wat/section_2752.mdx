# WAT § 2752

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2752

---

## Text

The clerk of the court shall immediately furnish the board with a certified copy of the order setting the time for hearing.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
