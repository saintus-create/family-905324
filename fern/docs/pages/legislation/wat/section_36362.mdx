# WAT § 36362

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 4. Action to Secure Payment of Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36362

---

## Text

The summons in the proceeding shall be served by publishing a copy thereof once a week for four weeks in a newspaper of general circulation published in each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
