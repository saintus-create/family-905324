# WAT § 23808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2A. Revenue Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23808

---

## Text

Until the payment or retirement of the warrants for the benefit of which an allocation was made, the allocation shall be irrevocable and the revenue allocated shall be applied solely to the payment of the obligations specified in the allocating order.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 717.)
