# WAT § 21384

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 21384

---

## Text

A smaller number of directors than a quorum may adjourn from day to day.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
