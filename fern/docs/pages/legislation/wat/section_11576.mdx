# WAT § 11576

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 6. Acquisition and Disposal of Property > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11576

---

## Text

The cost of contruction of the project includes the cost and expense of acquiring property and any such cost and expense is an integral part of the capital cost of the project.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
