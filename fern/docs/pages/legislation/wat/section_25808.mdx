# WAT § 25808

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Assessment Rate > Article: 1. Regular Rate

**Citation:** WAT § 25808

---

## Text

On or before the first day of November the secretary shall deliver the assessment book to the collector.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
