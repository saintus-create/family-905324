# WAT § 33106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 33106

---

## Text

Nothing in this part shall impair or adversely affect any right of any bondholder or creditor of the storm water district or the county water district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
