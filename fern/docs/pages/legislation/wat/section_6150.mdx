# WAT § 6150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 5. Action and Procedure to Restrain Violations

**Citation:** WAT § 6150

---

## Text

The department may commence an action or proceeding under this article, either by mandamus or injunction, for the purpose of stopping or preventing violations or threatened violations.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
