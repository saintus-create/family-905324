# WAT § 11733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 4. Bond Form and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 11733

---

## Text

Principal and interest on bonds shall be payable at such place or places as may be fixed and determined by the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
