# WAT § 2651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 7. Hearing of Objections

**Citation:** WAT § 2651

---

## Text

The board may adjourn hearings of objections from time to time upon reasonable notice to all parties in interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 545.)
