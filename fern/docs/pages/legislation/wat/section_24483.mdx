# WAT § 24483

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 1. Funds Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 24483

---

## Text

The proceeds of the annual assessment shall be paid into the district treasury and be apportioned to the several proper funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
