# WAT § 51802

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 1. Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51802

---

## Text

The board of supervisors, upon receipt of the request, shall appoint a time when it will meet and hear any objections to the request.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
