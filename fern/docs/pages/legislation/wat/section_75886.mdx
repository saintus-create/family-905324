# WAT § 75886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 3. Hearing and Order

**Citation:** WAT § 75886

---

## Text

Any public land of the United States may be included within a district, except as may be provided otherwise by federal law.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
