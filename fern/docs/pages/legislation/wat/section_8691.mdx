# WAT § 8691

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 5. Cooperation Between Board and Department of Water Resources

**Citation:** WAT § 8691

---

## Text

Any forfeiture, as soon as received or recovered, shall be paid to the State Treasurer and placed to the credit of the assessment out of which the cost of the work is to be paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
