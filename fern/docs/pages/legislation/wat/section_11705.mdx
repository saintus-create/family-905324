# WAT § 11705

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 2. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11705

---

## Text

Bonds shall be issued in the name of the department and shall constitute obligations only of the department and shall be identified as Central Valley Project revenue bonds.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
