# WAT § 72300

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Inclusion > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 72300

---

## Text

Any portion of a county, or any city situated within such county, or both, may be added to a district. Such portion or city need not be within a county in which the existing district is situated.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
