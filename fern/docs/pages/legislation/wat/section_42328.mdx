# WAT § 42328

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Recommendation of Abandonment of Project > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42328

---

## Text

For the purposes of the special election, the board shall establish a convenient number of election precincts and define their boundaries.
