# WAT § 71732

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts > Article: 2. Central Basin Municipal Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 71732

---

## Text

The district shall rebid a contract if the district significantly changes the scope of work of the contract. Significant changes include, but are not limited to, changes to the nature of the services or work products.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 426, Sec. 1. (SB 953) Effective January 1, 2017.)
