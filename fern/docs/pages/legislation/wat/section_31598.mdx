# WAT § 31598

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Formation and Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 31598

---

## Text

The notice shall contain a copy of the resolution and a designation of a public place within the district where a copy of a map of the proposed improvement district may be seen by any interested person.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
