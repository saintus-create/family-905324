# WAT § 1001

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Interpretation of Division

**Citation:** WAT § 1001

---

## Text

Nothing in this division shall be construed as giving or confirming any right, title, or interest to or in the corpus of any water.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
