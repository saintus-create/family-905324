# WAT § 25501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 25501

---

## Text

As used in this part “improvements” includes trees, vines, alfalfa, all growing crops, and all buildings and structures.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
