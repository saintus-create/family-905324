# WAT § 80824

**Hierarchy:** Division: 29.5. ELIGIBLE ENERGY RESOURCE CENTRAL PROCUREMENT > Chapter: 3. Department Powers

**Citation:** WAT § 80824

---

## Text

The commission may issue rules regulating the enforcement of the agency function pursuant to Section 80823, including the collection of nonbypassable charges and payment into the fund on behalf of the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 367, Sec. 15. (AB 1373) Effective October 7, 2023.)
