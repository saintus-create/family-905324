# WAT § 36345

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36345

---

## Text

Revenue bonds shall be signed by the president and attested by the secretary, and interest coupons appertaining thereto shall be signed by the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
