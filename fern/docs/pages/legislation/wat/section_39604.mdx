# WAT § 39604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Investigation by Department

**Citation:** WAT § 39604

---

## Text

Provision may be made for payment of warrants issued pursuant to this article in any bond issue authorized for the purposes of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
