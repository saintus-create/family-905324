# WAT § 10603

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.5. SOUTH FORK AMERICAN RIVER > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10603

---

## Text

In undertaking studies pursuant to this part, the department shall take into consideration the potential necessity to produce hydroelectric energy to pay for future water supplies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1982, Ch. 1472, Sec. 1.)
