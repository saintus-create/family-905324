# WAT § 7045

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 4. Maintenance of Flow in Streams

**Citation:** WAT § 7045

---

## Text

No acts authorized by this chapter shall limit the powers of the Department of Water Resources to accomplish in its own way the purposes of this chapter nor interfere with the construction of any flood control works in accordance with any plan of flood control adopted by the Reclamation Board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
