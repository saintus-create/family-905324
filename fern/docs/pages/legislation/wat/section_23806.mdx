# WAT § 23806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2A. Revenue Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 23806

---

## Text

The notice provided for in Section 23645 shall also state that the hearing is called to determine the allocation of revenue to pay the costs of the proposed improvement.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1947, Ch. 717.)
