# WAT § 11905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 10. Fish and Wildlife and Recreation in Connection With State Water Projects > Article: 3. Application

**Citation:** WAT § 11905

---

## Text

The provision of this chapter shall apply to the Central Valley Project and every other project constructed by the State itself or by the State in co-operation with the United States, including, but not limited to, the State Water Resources Development System.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 867.)
