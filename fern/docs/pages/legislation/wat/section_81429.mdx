# WAT § 81429

**Hierarchy:** Division: 31. BAY AREA WATER SUPPLY AND CONSERVATION AGENCY > Chapter: 4. Powers and Functions of Agency > Article: 2. Financial Matters

**Citation:** WAT § 81429

---

## Text

The agency may issue bonds for the purpose of refunding any revenue bonds of the agency, whether due or not due.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2002, Ch. 844, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 2003.)
