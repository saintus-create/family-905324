# WAT § 6406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 6. Inspection During Progress of Work

**Citation:** WAT § 6406

---

## Text

The department may order that no further work be done until such compliance has been effected and approved by the department.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
