# WAT § 8521

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Definitions and General Provisions > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 8521

---

## Text

“Board” means the Central Valley Flood Protection Board. Any reference to the Reclamation Board in this or any other code means the Central Valley Flood Protection Board.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 366, Sec. 7. Effective January 1, 2008.)
