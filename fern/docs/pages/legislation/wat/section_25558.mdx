# WAT § 25558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 3. Equalization

**Citation:** WAT § 25558

---

## Text

The secretary shall be present during the equalization proceedings and shall make all changes ordered in the assessment book.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
