# WAT § 8730.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Hearings, Depositions and Notices

**Citation:** WAT § 8730.2

---

## Text

Applicants shall be informed of the staff recommendations concerning the application at least seven days prior to being acted upon.

This section shall not apply to emergency permits.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 141.)
