# WAT § 13902

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 11. Discharges From Houseboats on or in the Waters of the State

**Citation:** WAT § 13902

---

## Text

Each regional board shall investigate its region to determine areas in which discharges of waste from houseboats are inadequately regulated by local ordinance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
