# WAT § 79700

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26.7. WATER QUALITY, SUPPLY, AND INFRASTRUCTURE IMPROVEMENT ACT OF 2014 > Chapter: 1. Short Title

**Citation:** WAT § 79700

---

## Text

This division shall be known, and may be cited, as the Water Quality, Supply, and Infrastructure Improvement Act of 2014.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2014, Ch. 188, Sec. 8. (AB 1471) Approved in Proposition 1 at the November 4, 2014, election.)
