# WAT § 305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2.5. Miscellaneous Powers of Department > Article: 4. Waste from Artesian Wells

**Citation:** WAT § 305

---

## Text

Any artesian well which is not capped or equipped with a mechanical appliance which will readily and effectively arrest and prevent the flow of any water from the well is a public nuisance.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
