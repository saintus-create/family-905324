# WAT § 36514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion and Exclusion of Land > Article: 2. Exclusion of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 36514

---

## Text

All expenses incurred in connection with a petition to exclude lands from a distribution district shall be paid by the petitioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
