# WAT § 22456

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 2. Eminent Domain

**Citation:** WAT § 22456

---

## Text

The district may exercise the right of eminent domain to take any property necessary to carry out its purposes.
