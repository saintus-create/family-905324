# WAT § 71592

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 71592

---

## Text

A district may make contracts, employ labor, and do all acts necessary for the full exercise of its powers.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
