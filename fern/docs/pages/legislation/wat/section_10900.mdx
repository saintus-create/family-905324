# WAT § 10900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.9. EFFICIENT WATER MANAGEMENT PRACTICES FOR AGRICULTURAL WATER SUPPLIERS > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10900

---

## Text

This part shall be known and may be cited as the Agricultural Water Suppliers Efficient Water Management Practices Act.

(History: Added by Stats. 1990, Ch. 739, Sec. 1.)
