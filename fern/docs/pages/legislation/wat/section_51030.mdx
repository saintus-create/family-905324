# WAT § 51030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Approval > Article: 1. Districts Within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 51030

---

## Text

No city or county shall proceed with the reclamation of any lands located within the Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District until the provisions of this article have been complied with.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 2122.)
