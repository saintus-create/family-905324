# WAT § 47981

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 13. Alternate Provisions for Collection and Enforcement of District Assessments by the County > Article: 4. Collection of Tolls and Charges by the County

**Citation:** WAT § 47981

---

## Text

The county auditor shall thereafter add to the tax bills for each assessee and assessee parcel as so certified, in addition to the other charges, such tolls and charges of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 1086.)
