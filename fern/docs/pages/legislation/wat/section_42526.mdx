# WAT § 42526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Recommendation of Completion of Project > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 42526

---

## Text

The election shall be noticed, held, and conducted and the result determined and declared in all respects as nearly as possible as provided in Article 2 (commencing with Section 42325) of Chapter 2 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 63. Effective January 1, 1993.)
