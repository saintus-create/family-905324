# WAT § 47182

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 47182

---

## Text

In case any tolls or charges remain unpaid for a period of 30 days after they become payable, they become delinquent and a penalty of 10 percent shall be added thereto, and they bear interest at the rate of 12 percent per year.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
