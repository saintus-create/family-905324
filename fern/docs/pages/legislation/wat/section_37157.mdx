# WAT § 37157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and its Termination

**Citation:** WAT § 37157

---

## Text

A collector’s deed to a district shall be recorded in the usual manner of recording conveyances of real property.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 51.)
