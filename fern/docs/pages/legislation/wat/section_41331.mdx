# WAT § 41331

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 41331

---

## Text

One of the judges of the election shall deliver to each of the voters one ballot for the votes to which the voter is entitled by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 1593, Sec. 10. Effective September 30, 1990.)
