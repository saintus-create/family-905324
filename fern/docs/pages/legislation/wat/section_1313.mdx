# WAT § 1313

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 2. Publication of Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1313

---

## Text

In case there is no newspaper published within the appropriate county publication shall be made in a newspaper having a general circulation within the county.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
