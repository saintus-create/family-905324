# WAT § 11784

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 8. Bonds > Article: 8. Retirement of Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 11784

---

## Text

All bonds retired by purchase or call shall be immediately canceled.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
