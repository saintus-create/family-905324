# WAT § 36482

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Formation > Article: 3. Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 36482

---

## Text

At the hearing the board shall hear any objections coming before it to any of the following:

(a) The petition.

(b) The formation of the distribution district.

(c) The land to be included within the distribution district.

(d) The plans and specifications.

(e) The estimate of cost.

(f) The proposed assessment.

(g) The apportionment of the assessment.
