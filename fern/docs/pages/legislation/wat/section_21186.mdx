# WAT § 21186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 5. Deputies and Employees

**Citation:** WAT § 21186

---

## Text

The board shall:

(a) Approve and reject as to number deputies of elective and appointive officers.

(b) Remove any deputies for good cause or for lack of work or funds.

(c) Fix and alter the salaries of all deputies.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
