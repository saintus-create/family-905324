# WAT § 6457

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 10. Dams and Reservoirs Under State Supervision Through 1965 Revisions of Part 1 > Article: 1. Dams and Reservoirs Completed Before 1965 Revisions

**Citation:** WAT § 6457

---

## Text

The department shall give notice to file an application to owners of such dams or reservoirs who have failed to do so as required by this article, and a failure to file within 30 days after such notice shall be punishable as provided in this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
