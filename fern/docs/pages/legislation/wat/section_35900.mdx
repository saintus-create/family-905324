# WAT § 35900

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 35900

---

## Text

The treasurer shall receive to the credit of the district and in trust for its use and benefit all the money belonging to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
