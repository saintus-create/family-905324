# WAT § 20849

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Hearings on Formation > Article: 3. Final Hearing

**Citation:** WAT § 20849

## Text

The order on final hearing shall be entered in full upon the minutes of the board of supervisors.
