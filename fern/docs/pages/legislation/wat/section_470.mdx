# WAT § 470

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 7. Water Transfer > Article: 1. Short Title

## Text

This chapter shall be known as and may be cited as the Costa-Isenberg Water Transfer Act of 1986.

(History: Added by Stats. 1986, Ch. 970, Sec. 1.)
