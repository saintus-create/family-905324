# WAT § 51407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 6. Supplemental Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51407

---

## Text

The supplemental assessment shall not bear interest and shall be payable only in lawful money of the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
