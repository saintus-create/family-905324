# WAT § 8737

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Hearings, Depositions and Notices

**Citation:** WAT § 8737

---

## Text

The board may provide for the taking at any place of the deposition under oath of any witness for or against any application pending before the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
