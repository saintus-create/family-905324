# WAT § 36558

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Annual Estimate

**Citation:** WAT § 36558

---

## Text

The board may from time to time by resolution provide for a district fiscal year to commence and end at the dates specified in such resolution.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 730.)
