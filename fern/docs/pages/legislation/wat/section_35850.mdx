# WAT § 35850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts With Other Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 35850

---

## Text

A district may for a valuable consideration enter into any contract with any irrigation or drainage district formed under the laws of the State as the board deems proper or advisable in the interests of the district, or to carry out or execute any of the purposes authorized by this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
