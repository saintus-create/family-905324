# WAT § 1085

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Witnesses and Production of Evidence > Article: 2. Attendance and Fees

**Citation:** WAT § 1085

---

## Text

All fees and mileage to which any witness is entitled under the provisions of this chapter may be collected by action therefor instituted by the person to whom they are payable.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
