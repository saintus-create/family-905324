# WAT § 22282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 3. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 22282

---

## Text

Whenever any charges for any service provided for by this division have been fixed, they may be made payable in advance.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
