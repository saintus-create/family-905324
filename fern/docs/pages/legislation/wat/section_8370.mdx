# WAT § 8370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Maintenance and Operation at Sacramento River Flood Control Project > Article: 3. Duties and Liabilities of Districts and Public Agencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8370

---

## Text

It is the responsibility, liability and duty of the reclamation districts, levee districts, protection districts, drainage districts, municipalities, and other public agencies within the Sacramento River Flood Control Project limits, to maintain and operate the works of the project within the boundaries or jurisdiction of such agencies, excepting only those works enumerated in Section 8361 and those for which provision for maintenance and operation is made by Federal law.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
