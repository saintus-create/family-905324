# WAT § 2764

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2764

---

## Text

All proceedings on the hearing shall be as nearly as may be in accordance with the rules governing civil actions.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
