# WAT § 12827

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Financing of Projects > Article: 2. Extent and Method of Allocations

**Citation:** WAT § 12827

---

## Text

As to projects other than those approved and authorized by the Legislature, the department shall make its determination in the manner provided by Chapters 1 and 2 of this part.
