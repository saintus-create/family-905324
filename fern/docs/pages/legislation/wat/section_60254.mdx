# WAT § 60254

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 60254

---

## Text

Taxes for other purposes of the district shall be levied on all real property and improvements thereon, but not on personal property in the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
