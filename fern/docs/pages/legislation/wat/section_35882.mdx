# WAT § 35882

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 35882

---

## Text

Proceedings at the election shall be had insofar as applicable in the manner provided in the case of issuance of general obligation bonds.

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1935.)
