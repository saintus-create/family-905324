# WAT § 71167

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71167

---

## Text

Within seven days after the formation election the board of supervisors shall canvass the vote.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
