# WAT § 1735

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 10.5. Change of Point of Diversion, Place of Use, or Purpose of Use, Involving the Transfer of Water > Article: 2. Long-Term Transfers

**Citation:** WAT § 1735

---

## Text

The board may consider a petition for a long-term transfer of water or water rights involving a change of point of diversion, place of use, or purpose of use. A long-term transfer shall be for any period in excess of one year.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 1145, Sec. 3.)
