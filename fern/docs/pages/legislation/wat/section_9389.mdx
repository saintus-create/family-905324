# WAT § 9389

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 4. Canvass and Contest

**Citation:** WAT § 9389

---

## Text

If no contest is commenced within the time mentioned in the next preceding section, the declaration of the result by the board is final and conclusive.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
