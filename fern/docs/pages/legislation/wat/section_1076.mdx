# WAT § 1076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Witnesses and Production of Evidence > Article: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 1076

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, “evidence” means any paper, book, map, account, or document.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
