# WAT § 11225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 2. Description of the Project > Article: 5. Friant Dam

**Citation:** WAT § 11225

---

## Text

The unit designated as Friant Dam consists of a dam, reservoir, and one or more hydroelectric power plants to be located on the San Joaquin River at or near Friant.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
