# WAT § 9280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 9280

---

## Text

A certified copy of the judgment referring to the assessment lists shall be filed in the office of the board.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1536.)
