# WAT § 35604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition and Disposition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 35604

---

## Text

A district may for a valuable consideration lease, sell, or contract for the sale of any property of the district whenever it may be necessary, advisable, or for the best interests of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
