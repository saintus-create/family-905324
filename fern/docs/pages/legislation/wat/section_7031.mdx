# WAT § 7031

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 3. Bridges and Conduits on or Near Highways

**Citation:** WAT § 7031

---

## Text

As used in this chapter, except in Section 7034, “highway” includes both state and county highways as defined by or identified in the Streets and Highways Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1786.)
