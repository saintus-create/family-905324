# WAT § 1627

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 9. Licenses > Article: 5. Contents and Conditions of License

**Citation:** WAT § 1627

---

## Text

A license shall be effective for such time as the water actually appropriated under it is used for a useful and beneficial purpose in conformity with this division but no longer.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
