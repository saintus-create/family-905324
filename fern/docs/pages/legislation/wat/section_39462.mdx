# WAT § 39462

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 4. Publication

**Citation:** WAT § 39462

---

## Text

When the petition consists of a number of separate instruments, only one copy need be published, but the names attached to all of the instruments shall appear in the publication.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
