# WAT § 9017

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 2. Application of Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 9017

---

## Text

Any reclamation district, levee district, or drainage district, to which compensation is made, shall apply the same, or the proceeds thereof, to the payment of outstanding warrants of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
