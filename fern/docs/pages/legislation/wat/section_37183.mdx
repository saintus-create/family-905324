# WAT § 37183

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Optional Provisions for Installment Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 37183

---

## Text

Payments under this chapter are not a redemption or partial redemption and do not affect the district’s interest in the real estate. The payments are compensation for the use of the real estate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 50.)
