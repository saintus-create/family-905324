# WAT § 31131

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 8. Recreational Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 31131

---

## Text

A district may prescribe reasonable rules and regulations to govern the use of such facilities, including regulations imposing reasonable charges for the use thereof. A district is not required to charge for the use of such facilities.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 574.)
