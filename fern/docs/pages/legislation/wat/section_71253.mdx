# WAT § 71253

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 71253

---

## Text

Except for the directors elected at a formation election, directors elected to office shall take office at noon on the first Friday in December succeeding their election.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2013, Ch. 8, Sec. 1. (AB 72) Effective January 1, 2014.)
