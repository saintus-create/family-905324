# WAT § 47305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 8. Assessments Upon Which Bonds Have Been Issued > Article: 1. Lien and Additional Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 47305

---

## Text

The word installment as used in this chapter shall be construed as applying to interest as well as to principal, as the case may be.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
