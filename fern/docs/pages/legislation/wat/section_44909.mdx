# WAT § 44909

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 44909

---

## Text

No warrants issued pursuant to this article made payable more than five years from the date of issuance shall be valid unless their issuance is authorized by a majority of the voters voting at an election called by the board for the purpose of determining whether or not the warrants shall be authorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1367.)
