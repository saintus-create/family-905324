# WAT § 30581

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board and Officer Activities

**Citation:** WAT § 30581

---

## Text

The general manager shall also:

(a) Perform other duties imposed by the board.

(b) Report to the board in accordance with the rules and regulations as it adopts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
