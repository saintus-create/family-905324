# WAT § 35501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 5. Sewers

**Citation:** WAT § 35501

---

## Text

The district may prescribe, revise, and collect rates or other charges for the services and facilities furnished pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 351.)
