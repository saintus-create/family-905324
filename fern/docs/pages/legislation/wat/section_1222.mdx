# WAT § 1222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1.7. Areas of Origin

**Citation:** WAT § 1222

---

## Text

Nothing in this article shall be deemed to diminish the rights and protections to watersheds of origin contained in existing law including, but not limited to, Part 4.5 (commencing with Section 12200) of Division 6.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1655, Sec. 2.)
