# WAT § 60009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60009

---

## Text

“Special water district election” means any district election other than a general water replenishment district election.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
