# WAT § 51640

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51640

---

## Text

Any bond or coupon received in payment for a parcel purchased at the delinquency sale shall be canceled forthwith by the county treasurer and filed in his office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
