# WAT § 8846

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 5. Maps

**Citation:** WAT § 8846

---

## Text

No fee shall be charged by any county recorder for the filing for record of any map as provided in this article.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
