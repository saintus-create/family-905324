# WAT § 51207

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 51207

---

## Text

Any costs associated with notices, public hearings, or filing charges with the board required pursuant to this division shall be recovered through charges, fees, or assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 38, Sec. 4. Effective January 1, 1993.)
