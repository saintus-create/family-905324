# WAT § 53222

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cancellation and Reissuance > Article: 2. Mandatory

**Citation:** WAT § 53222

---

## Text

Upon drawing an interest warrant the board shall endorse on the reverse of the old warrant the fact that interest has been paid to the date of drawing the interest warrant.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
