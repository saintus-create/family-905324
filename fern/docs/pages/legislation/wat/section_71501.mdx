# WAT § 71501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Election of Directors > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71501

---

## Text

One director, who shall be a resident of the division and shall be elected by the voters thereof, shall be elected for each of the five divisions of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
