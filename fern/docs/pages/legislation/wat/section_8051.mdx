# WAT § 8051

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 5. Powers of City Council

**Citation:** WAT § 8051

---

## Text

All contracts for the works shall be let, in such parcels as the city council determines, to the lowest responsible bidder, after notice inviting sealed proposals has been published.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
