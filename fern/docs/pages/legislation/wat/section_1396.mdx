# WAT § 1396

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 6. Permits > Article: 4. Application to Beneficial Use and Construction of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 1396

---

## Text

The construction of the work thereafter and the utilization of water for beneficial purposes shall be prosecuted with due diligence in accordance with this division, the terms of the permit, and the rules and regulations of the board.
