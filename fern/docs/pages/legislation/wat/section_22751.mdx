# WAT § 22751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Controversies > Article: 5. Settlement of Damages to Water Rights of State Land

**Citation:** WAT § 22751

---

## Text

The damages claimed in a controversy defined by this article are those occurring prior to the execution of the tax deed to the State.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
