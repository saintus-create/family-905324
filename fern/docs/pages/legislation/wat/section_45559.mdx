# WAT § 45559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 45559

---

## Text

When bonds are made callable, a statement to that effect shall be set forth on the face of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 1137.)
