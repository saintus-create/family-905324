# WAT § 9557

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 2. Purchase and Sale of Land by the Drainage District

**Citation:** WAT § 9557

---

## Text

The board shall execute to the purchaser a conveyance of the land free of incumbrances except State, county and municipal taxes, and assessments levied or assessed by statutory authority.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
