# WAT § 30232

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Notice of Hearing on Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30232

---

## Text

No defect in the contents of the petition, the title to or form of the notice, signatures, or lack of signatures thereto shall vitiate the proceedings if the petition has a sufficient number of qualified signatures attached thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
