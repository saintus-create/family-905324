# WAT § 52106

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 1. Form

**Citation:** WAT § 52106

---

## Text

The principal of bonds shall be made payable on the first day of July, or the first day of January in such years as the board prescribes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
