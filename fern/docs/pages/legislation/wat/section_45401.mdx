# WAT § 45401

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 45401

---

## Text

The treasurer shall place the proceeds of sale of any bonds sold to the credit of the district.
