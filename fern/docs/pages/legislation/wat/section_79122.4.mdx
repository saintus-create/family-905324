# WAT § 79122.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 7. Clean Water and Water Recycling Program > Article: 3. Clean Water Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79122.4

---

## Text

The board may transfer unallocated funds from the State Revolving Fund Loan Subaccount to the State Water Pollution Control Revolving Fund created pursuant to Section 13477 for the purposes of meeting federal requirements for state matching funds to provide loans in accordance with the Clean Water Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
