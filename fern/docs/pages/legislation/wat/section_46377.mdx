# WAT § 46377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 10. Interim Project Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46377

---

## Text

The board may by resolution levy an interim project assessment, if in its judgment the levy is in the best interests of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 125. Effective January 1, 1993.)
