# WAT § 75415

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Project Assessments > Article: 1. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 75415

---

## Text

The resolution, plans, specifications, estimate, and proposed assessment shall be filed in the district office and shall be subject to public inspection.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
