# WAT § 53020

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 2. Demand Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 53020

---

## Text

Demand warrants may be in substantially the following form: FaceNo. ________$ ________Office of the Board of Trustees of Reclamation District No. _________The Treasurer of ____ County will pay to the order of ____ out of Reclamation District No. ____ fund the sum of ____ dollars ($____) for ____ allowed by the board of trustees of said Reclamation District No. ____.Dated _____, 19__.TrusteesAttest:SecretaryReverseWhen registered this warrant bears interest at the rate of __ percent per year, computed from the date of registration hereof.This warrant will outlaw and cannot legally be paid four years after date, unless it is payable by its terms at a future date.Pay to ____ or order ____.Presented for payment but not paid for want of funds, this ____ day of ____, 19__.Treasurer of ________ County
