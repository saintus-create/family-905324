# WAT § 40500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Appointments to Fill Vacancies

**Citation:** WAT § 40500

---

## Text

A vacancy in the office of director shall be filled pursuant to Section 1780 of the Government Code.
