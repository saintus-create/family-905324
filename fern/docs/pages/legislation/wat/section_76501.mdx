# WAT § 76501

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Part: 11. REPEALS > Chapter: 5. Dissolution > Article: 4. Vesting of Property Upon Dissolution

**Citation:** WAT § 76501

---

## Text

The repeals effected by this part shall not be construed to deprive any person or entity of any substantial right which would have existed or hereafter exist had such repeal not been effected.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
