# WAT § 9478

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 6. Funds and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9478

---

## Text

No warrant issued pursuant to any of the provisions of this part or of Part 4 of this division shall be accepted or received by the county treasurer in payment of all or any part of any assessment upon which bonds have been authorized.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
