# WAT § 4100

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 4. Construction and Maintenance of Works and Measuring Devices > Article: 1. By Owners of Conduits

**Citation:** WAT § 4100

---

## Text

The owner of every conduit subject to regulation by a watermaster shall construct and maintain to the satisfaction of the department a substantial and serviceable diversion dam or works in the channel of the stream from which the water is diverted.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
