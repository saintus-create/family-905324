# WAT § 22099

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Drainage

**Citation:** WAT § 22099

---

## Text

No drainage need be provided for land as to which the district is relieved from liability for seepage or flooding by reason of grant, waiver, prescription, statute, decree, or condemnation.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
