# WAT § 45920

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 2. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 45920

---

## Text

At any time before direct assessment warrants are issued, the amount of any assessment on any land, exclusive of interest and the 10 percent added for anticipated delinquencies, may be paid in money.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
