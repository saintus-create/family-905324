# WAT § 74093

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 74093

---

## Text

The formation election shall be held on the next established election date not less than 74 days after the date of filing the formation petition.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1973, Ch. 1146.)
