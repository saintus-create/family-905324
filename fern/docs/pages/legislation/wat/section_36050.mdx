# WAT § 36050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Provisions Relating to All Bonds > Article: 5. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 36050

---

## Text

An action to determine the validity of bonds may be brought pursuant to Chapter 9 (commencing with Section 860) of Title 10 of Part 2 of the Code of Civil Procedure.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1486.)
