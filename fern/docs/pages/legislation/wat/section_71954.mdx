# WAT § 71954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Issuance and Sale of Bonds > Article: 1. Issuance and Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 71954

---

## Text

The board may provide for the call and redemption of bonds prior to maturity at such times and prices and upon such other terms as it may specify. A bond shall not be subject to call or redemption prior to maturity unless it contains a recital to that effect or unless a statement to that effect is printed thereon.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
