# WAT § 13631

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 9. Waste Water Treatment Plant Classification and Operator Certification

**Citation:** WAT § 13631

---

## Text

Prior to adopting or amending any regulations or approving any courses for operator training, the state board shall appoint an advisory committee to assist it in carrying out its responsibilities under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 420, Sec. 3. Effective July 30, 1985.)
