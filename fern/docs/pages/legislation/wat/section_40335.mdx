# WAT § 40335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 2. Official Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 40335

---

## Text

All bonds shall be made payable to the district and be in the form prescribed for the official bonds of county officers. Premiums may be paid by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
