# WAT § 35407

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 35407

---

## Text

A district may commence and maintain any actions and proceedings to carry out its purposes or protect its interests and may defend any action or proceeding brought against it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
