# WAT § 70272

**Hierarchy:** Division: 19. LEVEE DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Reorganization

**Citation:** WAT § 70272

---

## Text

This reorganization shall in no manner impair any obligation heretofore legally incurred by the reorganized levee district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 370.)
