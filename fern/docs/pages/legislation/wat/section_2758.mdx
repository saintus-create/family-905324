# WAT § 2758

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 9. Hearing and Decree of Court

**Citation:** WAT § 2758

---

## Text

The notice of exceptions shall state briefly the exceptions taken, the reasons therefor, and the prayer for relief.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
