# WAT § 31408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Bonds > Article: 3. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 31408

---

## Text

Every voter residing within the voting precincts, but no others, may vote at the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
