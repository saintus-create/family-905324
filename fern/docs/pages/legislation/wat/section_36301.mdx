# WAT § 36301

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 1. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 36301

---

## Text

Revenue bonds shall be issued in the name of the district and shall be obligations of the district, limited, however, to the payment or redemption of the revenue bonds and the payment of interest thereon from the revenues of the district, or such portion thereof as may be prescribed by the district by order or by contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
