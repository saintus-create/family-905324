# WAT § 20573

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 20573

---

## Text

Districts governed by this division are subject to the provisions of the Uniform District Election Law.
