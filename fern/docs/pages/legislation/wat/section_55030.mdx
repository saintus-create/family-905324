# WAT § 55030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Applicability of Division

**Citation:** WAT § 55030

---

## Text

This division shall not affect any other law relating to the same or a similar subject, but is intended to provide an alternative method of procedure governing the subject to which it relates. When proceeding under the provisions of this division its provisions and none other shall apply.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1953, Ch. 155.)
