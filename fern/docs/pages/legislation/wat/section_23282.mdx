# WAT § 23282

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 6. Borrowing from United States

**Citation:** WAT § 23282

---

## Text

Any district, upon being authorized so to do as provided by Article 3 of this chapter as modified in this article, may as evidence of the loan execute a contract with the United States.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
