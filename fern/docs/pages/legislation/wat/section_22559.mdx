# WAT § 22559

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 6. Water Leases for Mechanical Purposes

**Citation:** WAT § 22559

---

## Text

All money collected on a lease shall be paid into the district treasury and be apportioned to any funds the board deems advisable.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
