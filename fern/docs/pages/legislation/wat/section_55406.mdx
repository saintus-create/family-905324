# WAT § 55406

**Hierarchy:** Division: 16. COUNTY WATERWORKS DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Use of the Improvement Act of 1911 > Article: 2. Terms and Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 55406

---

## Text

“Superintendent of streets” or “streets superintendent” means and includes the county surveyor.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 452.)
