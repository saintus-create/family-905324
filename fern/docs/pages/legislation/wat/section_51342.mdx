# WAT § 51342

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51342

---

## Text

The rate of interest on unpaid operation and maintenance assessments is 112 percent per month commencing from the date of delinquency unless the board, by resolution, adopts a lesser rate and files a certified copy of the resolution with the county treasurer for insertion in the operation and maintenance assessment roll.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1990, Ch. 417, Sec. 1.)
