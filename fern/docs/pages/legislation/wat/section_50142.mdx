# WAT § 50142

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. General Provisions > Article: 4. Damage to Reclamation Works

**Citation:** WAT § 50142

---

## Text

Damages recovered in the action shall be paid to the treasurer of the county in which the action is brought, who shall credit the amount to the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
