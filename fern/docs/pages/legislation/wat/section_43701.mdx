# WAT § 43701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Controversies > Article: 1. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 43701

---

## Text

In all actions or proceedings the board may sue, appear, and defend in person or by attorneys and in the name of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
