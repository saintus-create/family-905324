# WAT § 25504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 25504

---

## Text

The assessor shall prepare an assessment book with appropriate headings, in which shall be listed all of the land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
