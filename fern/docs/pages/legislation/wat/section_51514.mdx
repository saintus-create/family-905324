# WAT § 51514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 2. Original and Additional Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51514

---

## Text

After the period of 30 days, all unpaid original assessments shall bear interest at the rate of 112 percent per month unless the board, by resolution, adopts a lesser rate and files a certified copy of the resolution with the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 38, Sec. 6. Effective January 1, 1993.)
