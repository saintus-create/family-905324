# WAT § 9334

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9334

---

## Text

Guardians, conservators, executors, administrators and other persons holding land in a trust capacity under appointment of court may vote at the election without obtaining special authority therefor.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1979, Ch. 730.)
