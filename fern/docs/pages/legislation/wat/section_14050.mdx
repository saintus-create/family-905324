# WAT § 14050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 17. Clean Water and Water Reclamation Bond Law of 1988 > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 14050

---

## Text

This chapter shall be known and may be cited as the Clean Water and Water Reclamation Bond Law of 1988.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1988, Ch. 47, Sec. 2. Approved in Proposition 83 at the November 8, 1988, election.)
