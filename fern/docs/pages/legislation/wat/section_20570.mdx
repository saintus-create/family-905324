# WAT § 20570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 20570

---

## Text

It is reaffirmed that districts are state agencies formed and existing for governmental purposes.

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 1366.)
