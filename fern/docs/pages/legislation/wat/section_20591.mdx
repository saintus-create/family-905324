# WAT § 20591

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. State Public Land

**Citation:** WAT § 20591

---

## Text

Public land is subject to all of the provisions of law relating to districts to the same extent and in the same manner as land in private ownership.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
