# WAT § 51806

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cancellation, Refund and Reapportionment of Assessments > Article: 1. Unnecessary Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51806

---

## Text

When an order is made approving, rejecting, modifying or amending the request, a certified copy of the order shall be attached to the assessment roll by the county treasurer in whose office the assessment roll is filed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
