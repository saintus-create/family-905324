# WAT § 1004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Definitions and Interpretation of Division

**Citation:** WAT § 1004

---

## Text

As used in this division, “useful or beneficial purposes” shall not be construed to mean the use in any one year of more than 212 acre-feet of water per acre in the irrigation of uncultivated areas of land not devoted to cultivated crops.

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
