# WAT § 10574

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.4. Rainwater Capture Act of 2012 > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10574

---

## Text

Use of rainwater collected from rooftops does not require a water right permit pursuant to Section 1201.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2012, Ch. 537, Sec. 2. (AB 1750) Effective January 1, 2013.)
