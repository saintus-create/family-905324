# WAT § 53070

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 4. Warrants Drawn on Maintenance Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 53070

---

## Text

A demand warrant on the maintenance fund pursuant to Section 51546 shall designate that it is so drawn and is to be paid therefrom, and is not required to be in the form specified in Article 2 of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
