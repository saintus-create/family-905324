# WAT § 22282.1

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 3. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 22282.1

---

## Text

A district may refuse service to any land if outstanding charges for services already rendered such land have not been paid within a reasonable time.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 673.)
