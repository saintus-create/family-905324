# WAT § 6370

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 7. Inspection and Approval > Article: 3. Removal of Dams and Reservoirs

**Citation:** WAT § 6370

---

## Text

Upon completion of the removal of a dam or reservoir such evidence as to the manner in which the work was performed and as to the conditions obtaining after the removal as the department requires shall be filed with the department.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1225.)
