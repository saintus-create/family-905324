# WAT § 8962

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Finances > Article: 6. Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 8962

---

## Text

The board may, at its option, at any time before payment of any warrant renew it upon application of the owner or holder thereof by an indorsement thereon of the fact and date of the renewal and notice thereof to the State Treasurer and Controller.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
