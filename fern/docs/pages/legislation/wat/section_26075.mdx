# WAT § 26075

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Delinquency > Article: 1. Date and Penalties for Delinquency

**Citation:** WAT § 26075

---

## Text

Unpaid assessments not payable in installments are delinquent at 5 p.m. on December 20th of the year in which they are levied.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 995.)
