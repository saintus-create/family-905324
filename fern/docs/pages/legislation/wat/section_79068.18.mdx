# WAT § 79068.18

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 5. Flood Protection Program > Article: 8. Yuba Feather Flood Protection Program

**Citation:** WAT § 79068.18

---

## Text

Not more than 5 percent of the total amount deposited in the subaccount may be used to pay the costs incurred in connection with the administration of this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
