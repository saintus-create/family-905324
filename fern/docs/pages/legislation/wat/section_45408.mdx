# WAT § 45408

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Bond Issuance

**Citation:** WAT § 45408

---

## Text

The bonds of any series of an authorized issue shall be equally and ratably secured with the bonds of any other series of such authorized issue.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 829.)
