# WAT § 41361

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 3. Conduct at Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41361

---

## Text

The inspector is chairman of the election board. He may administer all oaths required in the process of an election and appoint judges if, during the progress of the election, any judge ceases to act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
