# WAT § 75905

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Inclusion > Article: 4. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 75905

---

## Text

The ballots cast at the election shall contain the words “For change of boundaries,” or “Against change of boundaries,” or words equivalent thereto.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
