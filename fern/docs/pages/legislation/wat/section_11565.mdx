# WAT § 11565

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Construction of Project by the Department of Water Resources > Article: 3. Sequence of Units

**Citation:** WAT § 11565

---

## Text

The construction of all or any part of the San Joaquin Pumping System may be deferred until such time as the department determines that the construction of it is required and is economically justified.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
