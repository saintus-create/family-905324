# WAT § 51638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Delinquency Sales > Article: 2. Delinquency Sales on Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51638

---

## Text

Payment for parcels purchased at the sale shall be made in cash or in bonds or interest coupons of the district issued on the assessment and then matured or to mature within 60 days after the sale.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
