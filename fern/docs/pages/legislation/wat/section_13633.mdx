# WAT § 13633

**Hierarchy:** Division: 7. WATER QUALITY > Chapter: 9. Waste Water Treatment Plant Classification and Operator Certification

**Citation:** WAT § 13633

---

## Text

The advisory committee shall review all proposed regulations and make recommendations to the state board prior to adoption of any regulations or amendments thereto.

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 420, Sec. 3. Effective July 30, 1985.)
