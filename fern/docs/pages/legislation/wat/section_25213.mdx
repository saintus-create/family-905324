# WAT § 25213

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Bond Provisions > Article: 1. Bond Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 25213

---

## Text

The principal on bonds shall be payable on the first day of January or the first day of July of the years designated by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
