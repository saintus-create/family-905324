# WAT § 31032.7

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Water

**Citation:** WAT § 31032.7

---

## Text

In the event a district does not elect to collect such assessments with the general taxes of the district, it may issue separate bills payable in the time and manner designated by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 2005.)
