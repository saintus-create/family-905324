# WAT § 72127

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payments by Public Agencies in Lieu of Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 72127

---

## Text

On or before the first day of September, in any year the governing body of a public agency located wholly or partially within a district may declare its intention to utilize its funds to pay the whole or a portion of the taxes to be next paid to such district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 550.)
