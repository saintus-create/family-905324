# WAT § 2825

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 12. Service of Copy of Decree

**Citation:** WAT § 2825

---

## Text

The board shall furnish to each claimant whose rights are decreed a copy of the decree and a notice of the entry thereof.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
