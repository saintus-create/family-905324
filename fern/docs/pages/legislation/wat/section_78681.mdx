# WAT § 78681

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 6. Water Supply Reliability > Article: 5. Sacramento Valley Water Management and Habitat Protection Measures

**Citation:** WAT § 78681

---

## Text

(a) There is hereby created in the account the Sacramento Valley Water Management and Habitat Protection Subaccount.

(b) For the purposes of this article, “subaccount” means the Sacramento Valley Water Management and Habitat Protection Subaccount created by subdivision (a).

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
