# WAT § 8013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 2. Preliminary Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 8013

---

## Text

The general plans and estimates shall, after adoption, be filed in the office of the clerk of the city, and shall be substantially adhered to thereafter in proceedings under this chapter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
