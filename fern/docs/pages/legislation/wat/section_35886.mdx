# WAT § 35886

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Cooperation With the United States

**Citation:** WAT § 35886

---

## Text

The board may execute the contract on behalf of the district if two-thirds of the votes cast at the election favor the contract.
