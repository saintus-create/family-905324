# WAT § 8778

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 2. Assessors and Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 8778

---

## Text

The compensation of assessors shall be fixed and allowed by the board.
