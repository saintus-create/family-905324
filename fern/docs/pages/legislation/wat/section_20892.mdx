# WAT § 20892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 1. Calling of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20892

---

## Text

Notice of the formation election shall be published once a week for three weeks previous to the election, in a newspaper published in each affected county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
