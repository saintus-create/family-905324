# WAT § 10713

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.7. GROUNDWATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 10713

---

## Text

If a local agency annexes land subject to a groundwater management program of another local agency, the local agency annexing the land shall continue to comply with the groundwater management program for the annexed property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1987, Ch. 472, Sec. 1.)
