# WAT § 2865

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 13. Expenses of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2865

---

## Text

During the pendency of any proceedings under this chapter, the board, after at least 20 days’ notice to the parties, may order interim or partial payments of the expense to be made by the parties as the board deems proper and equitable under the circumstances.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2003, Ch. 741, Sec. 88. Effective January 1, 2004.)
