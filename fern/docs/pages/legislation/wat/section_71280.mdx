# WAT § 71280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71280

---

## Text

The enacting clause of all ordinances passed by the board shall be: “Be it ordained by the Board of Directors of ____ Municipal Water District as follows:”.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
