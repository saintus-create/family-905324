# WAT § 60040

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 60040

---

## Text

Districts, regardless of the date of formation, are subject to the provisions of this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1955, Ch. 1514.)
