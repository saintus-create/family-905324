# WAT § 12886.2

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 7. State Participation in Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 12886.2

---

## Text

In participating in a project under this article, the department may perform all or part of the planning, designing, construction, operation or maintenance of the project on terms agreed upon with the agency.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 27.)
