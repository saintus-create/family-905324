# WAT § 25526

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 2. Land Escaping Assessment

**Citation:** WAT § 25526

---

## Text

The additional assessments on land escaping the payment of an assessment shall be equalized as provided in this part for current assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
