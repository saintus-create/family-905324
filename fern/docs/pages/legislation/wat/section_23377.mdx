# WAT § 23377

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Cooperation With Other Districts > Article: 1. Cooperation With Districts in Other States

**Citation:** WAT § 23377

---

## Text

Agreements pursuant to this article may be evidenced by written contracts executed on behalf of the board of directors or trustees of each respective district or by resolutions entered upon their respective minutes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
