# WAT § 53071

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Issuance, Registration and Payment > Article: 4. Warrants Drawn on Maintenance Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 53071

---

## Text

A demand warrant drawn on the maintenance fund shall be presented to the county treasurer and, if there is insufficient money in the maintenance fund to pay it, endorsement of that fact shall be made on the warrant by the county treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
