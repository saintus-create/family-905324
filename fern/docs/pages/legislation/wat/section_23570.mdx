# WAT § 23570

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Functioning of Distribution Districts > Article: 3. Actions and Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 23570

---

## Text

All acts, proceedings, conclusions, and findings of fact, including the levy of assessments or charges, by a board of a district concerning a distribution district therein shall be conclusive except in an action or proceeding instituted within six months after the acts, proceedings, conclusions, or findings were had or made.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 169.)
