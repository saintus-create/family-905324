# WAT § 9493

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 6. Bonds > Article: 7. Annual Installments

**Citation:** WAT § 9493

## Text

The list shall contain appropriate columns for the entry of payments, sales and redemptions.
