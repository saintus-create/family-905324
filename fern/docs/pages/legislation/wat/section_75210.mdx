# WAT § 75210

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Benefit Assessment Improvement Districts > Article: 3. Assessment and Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 75210

---

## Text

The improvement district assessment shall include a sum that equals interest on any deferred payments at a rate not to exceed 8 percent per year, and 10 percent additional for anticipated delinquencies.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1975, Ch. 130.)
