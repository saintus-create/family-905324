# WAT § 21265.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Selecting Officers in Certain Cases > Article: 1. Appointments to Fill Vacancies

**Citation:** WAT § 21265.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, all vacancies on the board of directors elected pursuant to Section 21551 shall be filled pursuant to Section 1780 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1975, Ch. 1059.)
