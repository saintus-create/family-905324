# WAT § 2600

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 5. Report and Preliminary Order of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2600

---

## Text

After all investigations of water use have been completed and the time for filing proofs of claims has expired, the board shall prepare a report describing the water supply and abstracting the claim of water right of each claimant.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2025, Ch. 422, Sec. 8. (SB 697) Effective January 1, 2026.)
