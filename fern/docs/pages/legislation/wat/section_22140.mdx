# WAT § 22140

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 4. Pension and Retirement Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 22140

---

## Text

The board may establish or modify any existing plan or system, so as to provide for a pension or retirement plan or system for the benefit of officers, including directors, and employees of the district and therein provide for the payment of annuities, pensions, retirement benefits, disability payments and death benefits or any one or more of them.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 726.)
