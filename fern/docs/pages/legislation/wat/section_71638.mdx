# WAT § 71638

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Water > Article: 2.5. Alternative Provisions for Standby Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 71638

---

## Text

If the procedures set forth in this article as it read at the time a standby charge was established were followed, the district or improvement district may, by resolution, continue the charge pursuant to this article in successive years at the same rate. If new, increased, or extended assessments are proposed, the district or improvement district shall comply with the notice, protest, and hearing procedures in Section 53753 of the Government Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2007, Ch. 27, Sec. 40. Effective January 1, 2008.)
