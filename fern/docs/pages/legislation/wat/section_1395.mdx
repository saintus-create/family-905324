# WAT § 1395

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 6. Permits > Article: 4. Application to Beneficial Use and Construction of Works

**Citation:** WAT § 1395

---

## Text

Actual construction work upon any project shall begin within the time specified in the permit, which time shall not be less than 60 days from the date of the permit.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
