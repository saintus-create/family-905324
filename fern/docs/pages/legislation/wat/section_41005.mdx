# WAT § 41005

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Voters and Qualifications

**Citation:** WAT § 41005

---

## Text

No person shall vote by proxy unless his authority to cast such vote is evidenced by an instrument acknowledged and filed with the board of election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
