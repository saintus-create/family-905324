# WAT § 7032

**Hierarchy:** Division: 4. WELLS, PUMPING PLANTS, CONDUITS AND STREAMS > Chapter: 3. Bridges and Conduits on or Near Highways

**Citation:** WAT § 7032

---

## Text

No conduit shall be laid, constructed, or maintained so as to obstruct any highway.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1786.)
