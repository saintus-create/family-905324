# WAT § 20912

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 20912

---

## Text

The board of supervisors shall act in place of the board, and the clerk of the board of supervisors shall act in place of the secretary.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
