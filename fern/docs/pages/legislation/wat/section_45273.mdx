# WAT § 45273

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Bond Election > Article: 2. Election

**Citation:** WAT § 45273

---

## Text

At the close of the polls the board of election shall at once proceed to canvass the votes and declare the result.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
