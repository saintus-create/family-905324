# WAT § 71733

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Contracts > Article: 2. Central Basin Municipal Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 71733

---

## Text

The general manager of the district shall submit a quarterly report to the district’s board detailing all of the district’s contracts, contract amendments, and contract and amendment dollar amounts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 426, Sec. 1. (SB 953) Effective January 1, 2017.)
