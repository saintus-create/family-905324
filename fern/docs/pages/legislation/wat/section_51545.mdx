# WAT § 51545

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51545

---

## Text

The money collected on operation and maintenance assessments shall be placed by the county treasurer to the credit of the district in a separate fund designated “maintenance fund” of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
