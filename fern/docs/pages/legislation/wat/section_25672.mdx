# WAT § 25672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 2. Completion Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25672

---

## Text

Before a completion assessment proposal is submitted to the voters, an order of submission shall be entered in the minutes of the board, stating:

(a) The amount to be levied.

(b) The purpose of the levy.

(c) The day of the election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
