# WAT § 24602

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 1. Payment of Claims

**Citation:** WAT § 24602

---

## Text

Claims set forth in Section 24601 shall be itemized in detail to show:

(a) Date of each expenditure.

(b) Place where the expenditure was made.

(c) Purpose for which any money claimed was expended.

(d) Miles traveled and purposes of trips for which mileage is claimed.

(e) Any other matters the board may require.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
