# WAT § 39059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 39059

---

## Text

The powers herein conferred upon the department and board are police and regulatory powers and are necessary to the accomplishment of a purpose that is indispensable to the public interest.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1992, Ch. 1020, Sec. 44. Effective January 1, 1993.)
