# WAT § 22143

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 4. Pension and Retirement Systems

**Citation:** WAT § 22143

---

## Text

This article does not affect the authority of any district to participate in the State Employees’ Retirement System or any county employees’ retirement system.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 726.)
