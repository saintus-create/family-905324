# WAT § 11817

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 9. Finances > Article: 3. Revenue Fund

**Citation:** WAT § 11817

---

## Text

The State Treasurer shall deposit the revenues to the credit of the revenue fund as demand deposits in a depositary authorized by law to receive deposits of State funds.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
