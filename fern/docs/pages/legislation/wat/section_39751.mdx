# WAT § 39751

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Hearing on Formation > Article: 1. Preliminary Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 39751

---

## Text

The department shall hear all competent and relevant testimony offered in support of or in opposition to the sufficiency of the petition and the publication of the notice.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
