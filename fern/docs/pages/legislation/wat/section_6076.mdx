# WAT § 6076

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 4. Powers of the Department > Article: 1. Powers in General

**Citation:** WAT § 6076

---

## Text

All dams and reservoirs in the state are under the jurisdiction of the department.
