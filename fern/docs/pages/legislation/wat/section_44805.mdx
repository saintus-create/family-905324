# WAT § 44805

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. Funds > Article: 3. Proceeds of Sale of Land

**Citation:** WAT § 44805

---

## Text

The county treasurer shall deposit the proceeds of the sale in the treasury of the county to the credit of the bond fund of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
