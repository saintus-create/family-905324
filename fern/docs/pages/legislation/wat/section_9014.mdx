# WAT § 9014

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 2. Application of Compensation

**Citation:** WAT § 9014

---

## Text

The compensation, or the proceeds thereof, when received by any reclamation district shall be paid or delivered by the board of trustees thereof to the county treasurer having the custody of the funds of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
