# WAT § 1259

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2. Applications to Appropriate Water > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 1259

---

## Text

In acting upon applications to appropriate water, the board shall consider the state goal of providing a decent home and suitable living environment for every Californian.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1979, Ch. 947.)
