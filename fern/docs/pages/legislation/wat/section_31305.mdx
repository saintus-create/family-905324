# WAT § 31305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 31305

---

## Text

The board may designate the county treasurer the depositary to have the custody of all, or a portion of, the district’s money.
