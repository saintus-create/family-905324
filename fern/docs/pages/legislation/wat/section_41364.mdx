# WAT § 41364

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 3. Conduct at Precincts

**Citation:** WAT § 41364

---

## Text

Unless the board fixes other hours, the polls shall be opened at 6 a.m. on the morning of the election and closed at 7 p.m.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
