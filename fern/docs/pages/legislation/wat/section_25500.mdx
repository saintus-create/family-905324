# WAT § 25500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Assessment > Article: 1. Assessment Book

**Citation:** WAT § 25500

---

## Text

As used in this part “land” includes city and town lots and excepts improvements.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
