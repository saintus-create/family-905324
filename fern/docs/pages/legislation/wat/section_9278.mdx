# WAT § 9278

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Validating Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 9278

---

## Text

Unless the aggregate amount of the whole of the assessment is modified or amended by the judgment in the proceedings so as to cause a difference of more than 212 percent greater or less than the original total amount of the assessment, the assessment shall not be deemed to have been substantially modified and no necessity shall exist for a reapportionment thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 1536.)
