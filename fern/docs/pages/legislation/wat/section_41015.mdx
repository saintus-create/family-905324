# WAT § 41015

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Voters and Qualifications

**Citation:** WAT § 41015

---

## Text

The lists shall contain the names of the persons entitled to possessory rights and the quantity of land held by each.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
