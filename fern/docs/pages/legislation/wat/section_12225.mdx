# WAT § 12225

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta Levees > Article: 1. Plan for Improvement

**Citation:** WAT § 12225

---

## Text

The plan for improvement of the Sacramento-San Joaquin Delta levees, as set forth in Bulletin No. 192 of the Department of Water Resources, dated May 1975, is approved as a conceptual plan to guide the formulation of projects to preserve the integrity of the delta levee system.

(History: Added by Stats. 1976, Ch. 1302.)
