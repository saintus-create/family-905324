# WAT § 74469

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Redivision of a District Within Ventura County > Article: 2. Redivision Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 74469

---

## Text

Following approval by the voters of a redivision of the district, the directors shall, to the extent necessary, classify themselves in the manner provided by Section 74202.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1836.)
