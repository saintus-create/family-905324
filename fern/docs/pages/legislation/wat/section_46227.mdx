# WAT § 46227

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 4. Hearings Before Adjustment Board

**Citation:** WAT § 46227

---

## Text

Notice of the hearing shall be published at least once a week for two successive weeks in each affected county.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
