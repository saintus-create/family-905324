# WAT § 20514

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 20514

---

## Text

“Improvement district” means an improvement district formed pursuant to the Irrigation District Improvement Act or to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
