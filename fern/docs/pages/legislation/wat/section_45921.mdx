# WAT § 45921

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Direct Assessment Warrants > Article: 2. Advance Payment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 45921

---

## Text

Land on which the amount of the assessment has been paid pursuant to Section 45920 shall not be subject to the annual installments of the assessments levied for the purposes of the project or contract, but it shall be and remain liable for any assessments levied for operation and for any supplemental or additional assessments levied.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1961, Ch. 976.)
