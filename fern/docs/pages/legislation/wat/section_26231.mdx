# WAT § 26231

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 1. Redemption Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 26231

---

## Text

When a certificate of sale has been assigned, the collector shall hold the redemption money for and on demand pay it to the holder of the certificate.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
