# WAT § 31138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 9. Sanitation Service

**Citation:** WAT § 31138

---

## Text

The district may determine that sanitary service may not be necessary or feasible for all residents or properties within the district and shall thereupon make a finding as to which type of customer or area shall be excluded from service.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1981.)
