# WAT § 10001.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 1. ADOPTION OF STATE WATER PLAN

**Citation:** WAT § 10001.3

---

## Text

The American River Development, as described in Public Law 356 of the Eighty-first Congress, First Session, and as constructed by the Federal Government, is part of the State Water Plan.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1121.)
