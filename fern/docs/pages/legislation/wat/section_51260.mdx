# WAT § 51260

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 1. Original Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51260

---

## Text

If the action is not commenced within 30 days, no action or defense shall thereafter be maintained attacking the legality of the assessment.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
