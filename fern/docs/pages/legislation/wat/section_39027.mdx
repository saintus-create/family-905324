# WAT § 39027

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39027

---

## Text

“General election” is the election required to be held in districts in each odd-numbered year.
