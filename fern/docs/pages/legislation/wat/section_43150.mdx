# WAT § 43150

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. General Powers and Functions

**Citation:** WAT § 43150

---

## Text

The board has all powers and authority necessary to enable it to fully perform the duties imposed upon it by this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
