# WAT § 75830

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Exclusion > Article: 6. Effect of Exclusion

**Citation:** WAT § 75830

---

## Text

A district, notwithstanding the exclusion of lands therefrom, shall be and remain a district as fully, to every intent and purpose, as it would be had no change been made in the boundaries of the district, or had the lands excluded therefrom never constituted a portion of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
