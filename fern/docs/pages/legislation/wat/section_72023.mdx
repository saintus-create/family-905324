# WAT § 72023

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Formation Election

**Citation:** WAT § 72023

---

## Text

The returns of the special election shall be made, the votes canvassed by the board within seven days following the election, and the results thereof ascertained and declared in accordance with the provisions of the Elections Code, so far as they may be applicable, except as otherwise provided in this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
