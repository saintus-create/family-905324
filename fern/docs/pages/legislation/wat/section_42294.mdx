# WAT § 42294

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. The Project > Article: 5. Improvement Districts

**Citation:** WAT § 42294

---

## Text

The determination of the board establishing an improvement district shall be final and conclusive.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 755.)
