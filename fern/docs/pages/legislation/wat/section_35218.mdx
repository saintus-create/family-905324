# WAT § 35218

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Method of Elections for the El Toro Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35218

---

## Text

The provisions of this chapter shall in no way invalidate any act of the district, its directors, or its officers and employees made or performed while the district was operating as a landowner voting district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1981, Ch. 234, Sec. 2. Effective July 20, 1981.)
