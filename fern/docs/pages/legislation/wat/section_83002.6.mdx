# WAT § 83002.6

**Hierarchy:** Division: 33. INTEGRATED WATER SUPPLY AND FLOOD PROTECTION PLANNING, DESIGN, AND IMPLEMENTATION

**Citation:** WAT § 83002.6

---

## Text

Up to 5 percent of the funds appropriated by this division may be expended to pay the costs incurred in the administration of that program.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2008, 2nd Ex. Sess., Ch. 1, Sec. 6. Effective March 1, 2009.)
