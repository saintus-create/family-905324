# WAT § 12951

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 7. PROJECT COSTS FOR LOCAL GOVERNMENTAL SERVICES > Chapter: 9. Saline Water Conversion > Article: 3. State Participation

**Citation:** WAT § 12951

---

## Text

State expenditures and loans for aid to local governments provided in accordance with the provisions of this part are a part of the project cost of the project giving rise to the creation of the impact area.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 464.)
