# WAT § 51664

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 1. Sale After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51664

---

## Text

After the lapse of one year from the expiration of the redemption period the county treasurer may sell the parcel pursuant to Article 2 of this chapter in lieu of selling the parcel as provided in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
