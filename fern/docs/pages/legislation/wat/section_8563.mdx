# WAT § 8563

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 2. Administrative Provisions > Article: 1. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 8563

---

## Text

The board shall keep full and correct minutes of all proceedings and transactions of all meetings of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
