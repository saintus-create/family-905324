# WAT § 9026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 8. Districts and Persons Within Sacramento and San Joaquin Drainage District > Article: 3. Exchange of Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 9026

---

## Text

Any person, owning and holding any warrants of a reclamation district may apply to the board of trustees of the reclamation district for an exchange of the warrants.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
