# WAT § 39012

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 39012

---

## Text

“District” means any water storage district formed pursuant to the California Water Storage District Act or this division.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
