# WAT § 71932.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Formation of Uninhabited Improvement Districts for Issuance of Bonds > Article: 2. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71932.5

---

## Text

Within 10 days after the date on which all ballots must be received by the district, the board shall canvass the votes and declare the result.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1974, Ch. 385.)
