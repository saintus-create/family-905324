# WAT § 24628.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Warrants > Article: 2. Warrants Payable at Future Times

**Citation:** WAT § 24628.5

---

## Text

Warrants payable at a future time or times may also be issued to obtain funds or property for any lawful purpose of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1991, Ch. 1226, Sec. 35.)
