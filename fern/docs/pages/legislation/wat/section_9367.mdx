# WAT § 9367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 2. Polling Places, Notices and Board of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 9367

---

## Text

The notice shall specify the time and place of holding the election, the aggregate face value of bonds proposed to be issued, and the names of the persons appointed to act as the board of election.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
