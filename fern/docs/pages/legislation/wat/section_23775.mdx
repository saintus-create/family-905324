# WAT § 23775

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Alternative Procedure for Formation > Article: 2. Hearing and Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 23775

---

## Text

If a formation order is made pursuant to this chapter, the land formed into the improvement district shall, for a period not to exceed five years, be assessed in an amount not to exceed ten dollars ($10) per acre per year in addition to the regular assessments levied on the same land annually for district purposes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
