# WAT § 36367

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 4. Action to Secure Payment of Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36367

---

## Text

No court has jurisdiction to enter any order or decree requiring or permitting a receiver to sell, assign, mortgage, or otherwise dispose of or encumber the district or any part thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
