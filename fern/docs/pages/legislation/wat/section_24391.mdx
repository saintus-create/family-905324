# WAT § 24391

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Deposits > Article: 3. Special Accounts

**Citation:** WAT § 24391

---

## Text

Special accounts from the general fund shall severally be supplied from the general fund by warrant in the same manner as in the payment of any claim against the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
