# WAT § 41333

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 3. General Elections > Article: 2. Ballots

**Citation:** WAT § 41333

---

## Text

Each ballot shall have a perforated tab which shall be marked with the initials of a member of the board of election of the precinct immediately before being handed to the voter.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
