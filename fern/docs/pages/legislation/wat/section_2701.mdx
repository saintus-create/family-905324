# WAT § 2701

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Statutory Adjudications > Article: 8. Order of Determination

**Citation:** WAT § 2701

---

## Text

A notice of adoption of the order of determination and a copy of the order shall be sent by registered mail to each claimant and to each person whose rights are determined in the order.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1984, Ch. 1654, Sec. 3.)
