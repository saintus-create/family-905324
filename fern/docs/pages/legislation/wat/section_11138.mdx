# WAT § 11138

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 1. General Provisions > Article: 4. Aid From State Agencies or Other Sources

**Citation:** WAT § 11138

---

## Text

Money or property advanced or contributed may be immediately transferred or delivered to the department to be used for the purpose for which the advance or contribution was made.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
