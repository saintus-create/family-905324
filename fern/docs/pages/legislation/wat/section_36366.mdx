# WAT § 36366

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 4. Action to Secure Payment of Revenue Bonds

**Citation:** WAT § 36366

---

## Text

A receiver has no power to dispose of any part of the property of the district except in the ordinary course of business. The authority of a receiver is limited to the operation and maintenance of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
