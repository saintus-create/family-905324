# WAT § 35280

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 11. Method of Elections for Ramirez Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35280

---

## Text

This chapter shall apply only to the Ramirez Water District. For purposes of this chapter, “district” means the Ramirez Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1995, Ch. 699, Sec. 1. Effective October 10, 1995.)
