# WAT § 43158

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. General Powers and Functions

**Citation:** WAT § 43158

---

## Text

All waters and water rights belonging to this State within the district are given, dedicated, and set apart for the uses and purposes of the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
