# WAT § 10607

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 2.5. SOUTH FORK AMERICAN RIVER > Chapter: 6. Miscellaneous

**Citation:** WAT § 10607

## Text

The Legislature finds and declares that the circumstances which resulted in the enactment of this part are unique and therefore this part shall not be considered a precedent.
