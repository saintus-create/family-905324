# WAT § 36344

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Provisions Relating to Revenue Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36344

---

## Text

The date appearing upon the face of a revenue bond shall be deemed to be the date of issuance irrespective of the subsequent delivery of the bond.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
