# WAT § 34424

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34424

---

## Text

The election shall be held and the result determined and declared as nearly as may be pursuant to Chapters 1 and 2 of Part 4 of this division and the Uniform District Election Law.
