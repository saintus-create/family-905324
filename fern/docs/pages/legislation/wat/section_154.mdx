# WAT § 154

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2. State Administration Generally > Article: 2. California Water Commission

**Citation:** WAT § 154

---

## Text

Appointments shall be for terms of four years. Vacancies shall be immediately filled by the Governor for the unexpired portion of the terms in which they occur.

## Legislative History

(History: Repealed and added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
