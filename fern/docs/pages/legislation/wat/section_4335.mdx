# WAT § 4335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 7. Expenses of Distribution > Article: 6. Alternative Method of Payment by Owners

**Citation:** WAT § 4335

---

## Text

If, at the time of creation of a service area, all the owners have formed an association by agreement in writing and have filed a certified copy of the agreement as provided in this article, the department need not file a certified copy of the order creating the service area or a description of the land within the service area in the office of any county recorder.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
