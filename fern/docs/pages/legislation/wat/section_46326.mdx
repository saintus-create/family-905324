# WAT § 46326

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 2. Original Assessments to Finance Projects > Article: 8. Reapportionment of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 46326

---

## Text

Supplementary assessment rolls showing the reapportionments shall be made separately for land lying within different counties.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
