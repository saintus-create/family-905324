# WAT § 8731

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 4. Hearings, Depositions and Notices

**Citation:** WAT § 8731

---

## Text

The board may meet at any place in the State for a hearing or partial hearing of any application coming before it.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
