# WAT § 1651

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 9. Licenses > Article: 6. Filing of Licenses and Orders with County Recorder

**Citation:** WAT § 1651

---

## Text

Whenever an order revoking a license in whole or in part becomes final a true copy of the order shall be recorded promptly by the board in the office or offices in which a copy of the license affected is recorded.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1959, Ch. 51.)
