# WAT § 35476

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Powers > Article: 4. Charges

**Citation:** WAT § 35476

---

## Text

The charges to pay revenue bonds and interest thereon may be made payable in advance before service of water is made to the land.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
