# WAT § 79149.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 26. SAFE DRINKING WATER, CLEAN WATER, WATERSHED PROTECTION, AND FLOOD PROTECTION ACT > Chapter: 7. Clean Water and Water Recycling Program > Article: 6. Seawater Intrusion Control

**Citation:** WAT § 79149.4

## Text

The board may enter into contracts to make loans to local agencies for the purposes set forth in this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1999, Ch. 725, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 13 at the March 7, 2000, election.)
