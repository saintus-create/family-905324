# WAT § 34801

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board

**Citation:** WAT § 34801

---

## Text

The board shall select and maintain an office for the district, at a fixed place which place need not be within the district.
