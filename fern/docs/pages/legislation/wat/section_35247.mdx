# WAT § 35247

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 9. Method of Election for the Santa Margarita Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35247

---

## Text

This chapter does not in any way invalidate any act of the district, its directors, or its officers and employees made or performed while the district was operating as a landowner voting district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1993, Ch. 1047, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1994.)
