# WAT § 26552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 3. Supervision by District Attorney and Attorney General

**Citation:** WAT § 26552

---

## Text

The district attorney shall give notice to other officials and take any action necessary to secure the performance in their proper sequence of subsequent duties relating to the levying and collection of assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
