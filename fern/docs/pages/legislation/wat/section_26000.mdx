# WAT § 26000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Collection > Article: 4. Modification and Refund of Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 26000

---

## Text

A board shall order the collector to cancel or modify, as may be proper, an assessment when it finds that any property has been either:

(a) Assessed in any year more than once.

(b) Assessed by reason of a clerical error for more than its full cash value.

(c) Computed for assessment on an excessive acreage.

(d) Assessed while not in the district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
