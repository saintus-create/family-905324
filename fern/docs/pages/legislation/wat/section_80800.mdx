# WAT § 80800

**Hierarchy:** Division: 29.5. ELIGIBLE ENERGY RESOURCE CENTRAL PROCUREMENT > Chapter: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 80800

---

## Text

This division does not reduce or modify an electrical corporation’s obligation to serve.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2023, Ch. 367, Sec. 15. (AB 1373) Effective October 7, 2023.)
