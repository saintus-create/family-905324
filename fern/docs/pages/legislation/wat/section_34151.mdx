# WAT § 34151

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation > Article: 1. Petitions and Presentation of Petition

**Citation:** WAT § 34151

---

## Text

As used in this part, “petition” means a petition for the formation of a district.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
