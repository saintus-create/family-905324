# WAT § 36456.8

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 2. Definition of Improvement Act Terms

**Citation:** WAT § 36456.8

---

## Text

“Right-of-way” refers to any parcel of land through which a right-of-way has been granted to the water district for any purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 268.)
