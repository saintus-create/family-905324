# WAT § 74803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Bonds > Article: 2. Initiation of Proceedings

**Citation:** WAT § 74803

---

## Text

The resolution shall further provide for submitting the question of the incurring of the indebtedness to the qualified electors of the district at an election called for that purpose.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 75.)
