# WAT § 34013

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 34013

---

## Text

“District” except as otherwise provided means a water district formed pursuant to the California Water District Act or to this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
