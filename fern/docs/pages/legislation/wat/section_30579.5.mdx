# WAT § 30579.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Board and Officer Activities

**Citation:** WAT § 30579.5

---

## Text

Notwithstanding the provisions of subdivision (a) of Section 30578 and subdivision (a) of Section 30579, the board may alternatively authorize, subject to such limitations and conditions as it may determine, the execution of contracts on behalf of the district by another officer or officers of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1973, Ch. 241.)
