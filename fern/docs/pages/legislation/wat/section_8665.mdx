# WAT § 8665

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 4. Lease and Sale of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 8665

---

## Text

The meeting shall be held at the board’s regular place of meeting, at which meeting sealed proposals to lease the property will be received and considered.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
