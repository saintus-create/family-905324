# WAT § 22186

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 7. Recreational Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 22186

---

## Text

A district may fix and assess reasonable charges for the use of the recreational facilities by members of the public.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 533.)
