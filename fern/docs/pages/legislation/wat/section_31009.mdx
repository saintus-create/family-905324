# WAT § 31009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31009

---

## Text

In any district authorized to provide fire protection services, the district board is authorized to enforce the provisions of Sections 4291 to 4296, inclusive, of the Public Resources Code.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1013.)
