# WAT § 36457.3

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Adoption of Improvement Acts > Article: 3. Engineer of Work

**Citation:** WAT § 36457.3

---

## Text

The contract shall be entered into by the engineer, and the assessment and warrant, when confirmed, shall be recorded in the office of the engineer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 268.)
