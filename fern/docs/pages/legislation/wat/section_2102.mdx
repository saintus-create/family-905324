# WAT § 2102

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 2.5. Adjudications to Protect the Quality of Ground Water

**Citation:** WAT § 2102

---

## Text

As used in this article, “minor quantities of water” refers to the extraction by any person of not to exceed 10 acre-feet of ground water annually.

(History: Added by Stats. 1969, Ch. 482.)
