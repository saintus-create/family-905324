# WAT § 72030

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Advance of Funds

**Citation:** WAT § 72030

---

## Text

The board may advance general funds of the district to accomplish the purposes of an improvement district formed pursuant to this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
