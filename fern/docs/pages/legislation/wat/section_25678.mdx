# WAT § 25678

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy > Article: 2. Completion Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 25678

---

## Text

If a majority of the votes cast are “Assessment—No,” the result of the election shall be entered of record.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
