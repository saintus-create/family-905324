# WAT § 9357

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 2. Polling Places, Notices and Board of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 9357

---

## Text

The board shall designate and provide one polling place within each voting district at which shall be cast the votes of the owners of land within the voting district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
