# WAT § 177

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2. State Administration Generally > Article: 3. State Water Resources Control Board

**Citation:** WAT § 177

---

## Text

All members of the board shall be appointed for terms of four years. Vacancies shall be immediately filled by the Governor for the unexpired portion of the terms in which they occur.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1983, Ch. 40, Sec. 2. Effective May 26, 1983.)
