# WAT § 12050

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Part: 4.3. WASHOE PROJECT WATER SUPPLY CONTRACTS > Chapter: 11. Water Conservation and Reclamation Projects > Article: 4. Administration

**Citation:** WAT § 12050

---

## Text

The following definitions shall govern the construction of this part:

(a) “Public agency” includes any city, county, district, or other political subdivision of the State of California.

(b) “Department” means the Department of Water Resources.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1966, 1st Ex. Sess., Ch. 42.)
