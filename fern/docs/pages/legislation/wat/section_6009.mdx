# WAT § 6009

**Hierarchy:** Division: 3. DAMS AND RESERVOIRS > Chapter: 1. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 6009

---

## Text

“State jurisdictional dam” means a dam not wholly under the control of the federal government.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2017, Ch. 26, Sec. 85. (SB 92) Effective June 27, 2017.)
