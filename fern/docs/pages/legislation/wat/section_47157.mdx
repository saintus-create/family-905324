# WAT § 47157

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 7. Supplementary Assessments for Maintenance > Article: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 47157

---

## Text

All provisions of this division with respect to the levy and collection of assessments shall, so far as appropriate, be applicable to supplementary assessments.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
