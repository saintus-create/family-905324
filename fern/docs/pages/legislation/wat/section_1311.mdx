# WAT § 1311

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 3. Notice of Application > Article: 2. Publication of Notice

**Citation:** WAT § 1311

---

## Text

Upon receipt of notice of an application coming under this article, the applicant shall cause it to be published as directed by the board.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
