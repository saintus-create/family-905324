# WAT § 31011

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 1. Powers Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 31011

---

## Text

A district may disseminate information to the public concerning the rights, properties, and activities of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 330.)
