# WAT § 26652

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 8. Alternative Procedure for Assessment and Collection by County > Article: 3. Collection

**Citation:** WAT § 26652

---

## Text

The county auditor shall compute and enter in the assessment book the district assessment on all lands assessed as being in the district using the rates fixed by the district’s board of directors and the assessed value of the lands in the assessment book at the time and in the manner he computes and enters county taxes.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1938.)
