# WAT § 9540

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 7. Sale of Land for Delinquent Installments > Article: 1. Notice and Sale

**Citation:** WAT § 9540

---

## Text

Any bonds or coupons so received in payment shall be canceled by the county treasurer and transmitted to the State Treasurer.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
