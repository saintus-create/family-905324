# WAT § 40305

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualification and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 40305

---

## Text

The board may elect a vice president and appoint an assistant secretary to serve during the pleasure of the board.

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
