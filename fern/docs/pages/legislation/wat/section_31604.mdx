# WAT § 31604

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Alternative Procedure for Formation of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Formation and Projects

**Citation:** WAT § 31604

---

## Text

At the conclusion of the hearing if proceedings relating to the proposed improvement district have not been terminated in the manner set forth in Section 31600 the board may, by resolution, declare that the proposed improvement district is formed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1959, Ch. 442.)
