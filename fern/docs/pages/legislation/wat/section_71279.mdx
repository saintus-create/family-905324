# WAT § 71279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. The Board > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 71279

---

## Text

Votes of the members of the board shall not be cast or exercised by proxy.
