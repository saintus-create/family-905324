# WAT § 1814

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 11. Joint Use and Development > Article: 4. Joint Use of Capacity in Water Conveyance Facilities

**Citation:** WAT § 1814

## Text

This article shall apply to only 70 percent of the unused capacity.
