# WAT § 36197

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Provisions Relating to General Obligation Bonds Only > Article: 3. Miscellaneous Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 36197

---

## Text

A general obligation bond shall be payable when due upon presentation and surrender thereof to the treasurer by the holder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 1756.)
