# WAT § 8711

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8711

---

## Text

Any such plan is void until approved by the board, and no work shall be done without the permission of the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
