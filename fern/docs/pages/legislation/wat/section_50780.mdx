# WAT § 50780

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2.5. Reclamation District No. 1000

**Citation:** WAT § 50780

---

## Text

Notwithstanding any other provision of law, this chapter applies to the manner in which elections are conducted in the Reclamation District No. 1000.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1992, Ch. 842, Sec. 1. Effective January 1, 1993.)
