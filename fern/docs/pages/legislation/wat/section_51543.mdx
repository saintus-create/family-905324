# WAT § 51543

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payment and Collection > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51543

---

## Text

Except as otherwise specifically provided, unpaid assessments for operation and maintenance levied pursuant to this article shall be collected in the manner provided for the payment and collection of original and additional assessments.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
