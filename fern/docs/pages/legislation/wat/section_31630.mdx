# WAT § 31630

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 7. Coachella Valley Water District Water Replenishment Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 31630

---

## Text

The Coachella Valley Water District shall have the power to levy and collect water replenishment assessments, as provided in this chapter, for the purpose of replenishing ground water supplies within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1980, Ch. 226, Sec. 2.)
