# WAT § 43026

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 1. Water and Electricity > Article: 2. Electricity

**Citation:** WAT § 43026

---

## Text

The board may sell, distribute, or otherwise dispose of hydroelectric energy not necessary for the uses and purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
