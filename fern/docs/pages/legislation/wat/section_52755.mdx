# WAT § 52755

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Alternate Method for Refunding Bonds > Article: 3. Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 52755

---

## Text

No proposal to sell bonds to the district at a price in excess of the par value thereof shall be accepted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
