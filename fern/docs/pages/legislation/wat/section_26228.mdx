# WAT § 26228

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 1. Redemption Generally

**Citation:** WAT § 26228

---

## Text

In case of a redemption of part of any property described in a certificate of sale, the part redeemed shall be described in the certificate of redemption.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
