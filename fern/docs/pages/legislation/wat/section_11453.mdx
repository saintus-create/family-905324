# WAT § 11453

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 3. The Department of Water Resources > Article: 2. General Powers and Duties

**Citation:** WAT § 11453

---

## Text

The collection of rates, charges, and revenues shall be continued as provided in this part until all bonds issued are fully redeemed and paid.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 370.)
