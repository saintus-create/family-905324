# WAT § 78500

**Hierarchy:** Division: 24. SAFE, CLEAN, RELIABLE WATER SUPPLY ACT > Chapter: 1. Short Title and Findings and Declarations

**Citation:** WAT § 78500

---

## Text

This division shall be known and may be cited as the Safe, Clean, Reliable Water Supply Act.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1996, Ch. 135, Sec. 1. Approved in Proposition 204 at the November 5, 1996, election.)
