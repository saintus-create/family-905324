# WAT § 35188

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Method of Elections for the Moulton-Niguel Water District

**Citation:** WAT § 35188

---

## Text

All directors elected after the effective date of this chapter shall be residents of the district and shall be registered voters at the time of their election and during their term of office.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1980, Ch. 713, Sec. 1.)
