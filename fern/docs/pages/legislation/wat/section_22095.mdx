# WAT § 22095

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Powers and Purposes Generally > Article: 2. Drainage

**Citation:** WAT § 22095

---

## Text

A district may provide for any and all drainage made necessary by the irrigation provided for by the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
