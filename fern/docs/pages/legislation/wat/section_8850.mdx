# WAT § 8850

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Assessments > Article: 6. Collection and Delinquencies

**Citation:** WAT § 8850

---

## Text

After the board has held the hearings in each county pursuant to this chapter and the assesssment lists have been made to conform with its decision, the lists shall be certified by the secretary of the board to be correct, and shall thereupon be filed in the offices of the county treasurers, respectively, of the several counties in which are situated any of the lands assessed.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
