# WAT § 20980.5

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Change of Name

**Citation:** WAT § 20980.5

---

## Text

The Palmdale Irrigation District may change its name pursuant to this chapter to the Palmdale Water District.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1972, Ch. 143.)
