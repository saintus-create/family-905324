# WAT § 11552

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. Construction of Project by the Department of Water Resources > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 11552

---

## Text

The department may construct the works or any portion thereof or may contract with the United States that the works or any portion thereof may be constructed by and through the department under the supervision and subject to the approval of the United States.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1957, Ch. 1932.)
