# WAT § 85059

**Hierarchy:** Division: 35. SACRAMENTO-SAN JOAQUIN DELTA REFORM ACT OF 2009 > Chapter: 4. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 85059

---

## Text

“Delta Plan” means the comprehensive, long-term management plan for the Delta as adopted by the council in accordance with this division.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2009, 7th Ex. Sess., Ch. 5, Sec. 39. (SB 1 7x) Effective February 3, 2010.)
