# WAT § 8004

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Flood Control by Cities > Article: 1. General Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 8004

---

## Text

Every publication required by this chapter shall be made in some newspaper published in the city.

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
