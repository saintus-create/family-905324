# WAT § 8504

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 1. Creation and Boundaries

**Citation:** WAT § 8504

---

## Text

The district may acquire, own, hold, use, and enjoy any and all properties necessary for the purposes of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
