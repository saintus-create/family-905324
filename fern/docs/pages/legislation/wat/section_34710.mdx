# WAT § 34710

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers > Article: 1. Qualifications and Tenure

**Citation:** WAT § 34710

---

## Text

Deputies shall hold office at the pleasure of the appointing officer and shall receive such compensation as may be provided by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 694.)
