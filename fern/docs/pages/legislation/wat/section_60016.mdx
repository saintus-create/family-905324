# WAT § 60016

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Definitions

**Citation:** WAT § 60016

---

## Text

“Nonsaline water” means water which has less than 1,000 parts of chlorides to 1,000,000 parts of water.
