# WAT § 46672

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 5. Collection of Original Assessments > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 46672

---

## Text

All unpaid assessments and accrued interest shall be collected by the county treasurers when and as called. The county treasurers shall hold the money collected to the credit of the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
