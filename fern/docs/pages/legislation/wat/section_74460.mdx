# WAT § 74460

**Hierarchy:** Division: 21. WATER CONSERVATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Redivision of a District Within Ventura County > Article: 2. Redivision Procedure

**Citation:** WAT § 74460

---

## Text

The number of members of the board and the basis for dividing a district into divisions for the election of directors may be changed in the manner prescribed in this chapter.

(History: Added by Stats. 1965, Ch. 1836.)
