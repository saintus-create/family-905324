# WAT § 11954

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 11. Water Conservation and Reclamation Projects > Article: 1. Policy

**Citation:** WAT § 11954

---

## Text

Nothing in this chapter shall be construed to alter or impair any existing rights, including rights to divert water from the Colorado River and rights to the distribution or use of that water.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1985, Ch. 938, Sec. 1.)
