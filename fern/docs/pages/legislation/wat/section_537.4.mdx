# WAT § 537.4

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 8. Water Measurement > Article: 5. Multiunit Structures

**Citation:** WAT § 537.4

---

## Text

It is the intent of the Legislature that this article should not be construed to impose costs on any local government agency, except to the extent that the local government agency is a water purveyor.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2016, Ch. 623, Sec. 5. (SB 7) Effective January 1, 2017. Section operative January 1, 2018, pursuant to Section 537.5.)
