# WAT § 26279

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 6. Redemption and Its Termination > Article: 3. Deed on Termination of Redemption

**Citation:** WAT § 26279

---

## Text

In case partial redemption has been made, the form of the collector’s deed shall be modified to conform to the facts.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
