# WAT § 31338

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. General Financial Provisions > Article: 3. Investments

**Citation:** WAT § 31338

---

## Text

Any bonds or registered warrants purchased pursuant to this article and held may, from time to time, be sold and the proceeds reinvested pursuant to this article.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1949, Ch. 274.)
