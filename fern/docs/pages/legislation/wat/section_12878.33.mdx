# WAT § 12878.33

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 4.5. Maintenance Areas > Article: 4. Assessments for Maintenance and Operation

**Citation:** WAT § 12878.33

---

## Text

If any area or zone lies within more than one county, the department shall divide the amount of the estimate in the proportion of the acreage in each county, as modified to reflect any zones of benefit, as described in Section 12878.9.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 2004, Ch. 230, Sec. 35. Effective August 16, 2004.)
