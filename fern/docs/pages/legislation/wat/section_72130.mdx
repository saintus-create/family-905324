# WAT § 72130

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Payments by Public Agencies in Lieu of Taxes

**Citation:** WAT § 72130

---

## Text

Upon receipt of a certified copy of such declaration, the board of directors of the district may reject such declaration whenever the public agency has, in the preceding five-year period, declared its intention to utilize its funds in lieu of taxes and has failed to meet its obligations thereunder in a timely manner.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1971, Ch. 550.)
