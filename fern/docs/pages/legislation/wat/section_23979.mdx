# WAT § 23979

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Functioning of Improvement Districts > Article: 2. Improvement District Warrants

**Citation:** WAT § 23979

---

## Text

Improvement district warrants may be made payable to any of the following:

(a) Bearer.

(b) Persons furnishing work, labor, or material.

(c) The contractor if the work of improvement is to be done under contract.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
