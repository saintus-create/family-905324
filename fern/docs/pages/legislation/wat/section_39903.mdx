# WAT § 39903

**Hierarchy:** Division: 14. CALIFORNIA WATER STORAGE DISTRICT LAW > Chapter: 4. Election on Formation > Article: 1. Calling of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 39903

---

## Text

In the order calling the election, the department shall designate voting places and appoint three holders of title to land to act as a board of election at each voting place.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 391.)
