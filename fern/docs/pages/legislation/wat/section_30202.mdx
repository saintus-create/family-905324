# WAT § 30202

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Petition for Formation

**Citation:** WAT § 30202

---

## Text

The petition shall be signed by voters equal in number to at least 10 percent of the voters registered within the boundaries of the proposed district 30 days prior to the date the petition is filed.
