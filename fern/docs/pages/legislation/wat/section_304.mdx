# WAT § 304

**Hierarchy:** Division: 1. GENERAL STATE POWERS OVER WATER > Chapter: 2.5. Miscellaneous Powers of Department > Article: 4. Waste from Artesian Wells

**Citation:** WAT § 304

---

## Text

A beneficial use under the next preceding section shall not exceed one tenth of one miner’s inch of water per acre, perpetual flow, but the person using the water may cumulate that amount within any period of each year.

## Legislative History

(History: Enacted by Stats. 1943, Ch. 368.)
