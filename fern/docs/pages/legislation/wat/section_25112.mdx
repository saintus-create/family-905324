# WAT § 25112

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Refunding Bonds > Article: 4. Unauthorized Refunding Modification

**Citation:** WAT § 25112

---

## Text

The approval of the modification by the district shall be given by a resolution of its board and by its voters at an election called by the board.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
