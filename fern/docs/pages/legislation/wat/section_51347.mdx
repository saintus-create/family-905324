# WAT § 51347

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 3. Operation and Maintenance Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51347

---

## Text

Valuation assessment commissioners appointed to prepare a new operation and maintenance assessment roll shall have the same qualifications and take the same oath as the original assessment commissioners.

## Legislative History

(History: Amended by Stats. 1951, Ch. 681.)
