# WAT § 53803

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 4. Transfer of Districts From One County to Another

**Citation:** WAT § 53803

---

## Text

After a district is transferred to another county, all assessments shall be collected and paid into the treasury of the county to which the district is transferred.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
