# WAT § 22430

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Property > Article: 1. Acquisition of Property

**Citation:** WAT § 22430

---

## Text

There is given, dedicated, and set apart for the uses and purposes of each district all water and water rights belonging to this State within the district.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
