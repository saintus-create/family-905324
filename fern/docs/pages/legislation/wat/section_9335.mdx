# WAT § 9335

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 5. Bond Elections > Article: 1. In General

**Citation:** WAT § 9335

---

## Text

The vote of any public or private corporation or of any reclamation district, levee district, drainage district or other public agency entitled to vote at the election, may be cast by any person authorized by the board of directors or trustees or other managing body thereof.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
