# WAT § 34423

**Hierarchy:** Division: 13. CALIFORNIA WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 3. Election on Formation > Article: 2. Conduct of Election

**Citation:** WAT § 34423

---

## Text

At the election the elective officers shall be elected but only one person shall be elected to fill the offices consolidated, if any.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 390.)
