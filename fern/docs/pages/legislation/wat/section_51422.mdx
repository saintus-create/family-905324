# WAT § 51422

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Levy of Assessments > Article: 7. Calling Bonded Assessments

**Citation:** WAT § 51422

---

## Text

If no newspaper is published in any county in which the district is situated, the publication shall be made in a newspaper published in an adjoining county.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
