# WAT § 60618

**Hierarchy:** Division: 18. WATER REPLENISHMENT DISTRICTS > Part: 8. COMPETITIVE BIDDING > Chapter: 3. Disincorporation

**Citation:** WAT § 60618

---

## Text

Upon receipt of an offer for a less price than that specified in the lowest rejected bid, the board may enter into a contract for the furnishing of the materials with the person who has offered them at the lowest price.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 2000, Ch. 888, Sec. 3. Effective January 1, 2001.)
