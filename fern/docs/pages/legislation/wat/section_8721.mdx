# WAT § 8721

**Hierarchy:** Division: 5. FLOOD CONTROL > Chapter: 3. Powers, Duties and Jurisdiction > Article: 7. Approval of Plans

**Citation:** WAT § 8721

---

## Text

Any plans or specifications adopted or approved by the board for any work to be done pursuant to this part may be changed or altered, with the consent of the board, at any time before commencement or during progress of the work.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 369.)
