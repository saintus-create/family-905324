# WAT § 30502

**Hierarchy:** Division: 12. COUNTY WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 1. Officers and Employees Generally > Article: 1. Directors

**Citation:** WAT § 30502

---

## Text

The term of office of each director other than directors first elected or directors appointed to fill an unexpired term shall be four years.

(History: Amended by Stats. 1953, Ch. 1565.)
