# WAT § 51695

**Hierarchy:** Division: 15. RECLAMATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Unredeemed Property > Article: 2. Sale After One Year After Expiration of Redemption Period

**Citation:** WAT § 51695

---

## Text

If part of any parcel is sold pursuant to this article the assessments thereon shall be reapportioned as provided in Chapter 6, Article 5 of this part.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1951, Ch. 336.)
