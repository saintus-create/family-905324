# WAT § 12892

**Hierarchy:** Division: 6. CONSERVATION, DEVELOPMENT, AND UTILIZATION OF STATE WATER RESOURCES > Chapter: 5. State Financial Assistance for Local Projects > Article: 10. Administrative Provisions

**Citation:** WAT § 12892

---

## Text

No member of the California Water Commission shall participate in the action of the commission in considering for approval, or approving, a loan or grant under this chapter to a public agency of which he is an officer, employee, agent, consultant, accountant, engineer, or legal counsel or in which he owns real property.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1967, Ch. 27.)
