# WAT § 71945

**Hierarchy:** Division: 20. MUNICIPAL WATER DISTRICTS > Chapter: 5. Bond Election

**Citation:** WAT § 71945

---

## Text

No irregularities or informalities in conducting the bond election shall invalidate it, if the election has otherwise been fairly conducted.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1963, Ch. 156.)
