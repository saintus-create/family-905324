# WAT § 23261

**Hierarchy:** Division: 11. IRRIGATION DISTRICTS > Chapter: 2. Cooperation With United States > Article: 5. Construction of Works for United States

**Citation:** WAT § 23261

---

## Text

If a district is the successful bidder for the construction of the works or any part of them, the district may execute at its expense a bond for the faithful performance of the work and do any and all things required by the United States as would be required of any other successful bidder.

## Legislative History

(History: Added by Stats. 1943, Ch. 372.)
