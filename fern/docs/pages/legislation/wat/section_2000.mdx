# WAT § 2000

**Hierarchy:** Division: 2. WATER > Chapter: 1. Reference by Courts of This State > Article: 1. Subjects of Reference

**Citation:** WAT § 2000

---

## Text

In any suit brought in any court of competent jurisdiction in this State for determination of rights to water, the court may order a reference to the board, as referee, of any or all issues involved in the suit.
